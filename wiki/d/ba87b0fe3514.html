RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarization of Biomedical Texts

Yuelyu Ji, Zhuochun Li, Rui Meng, Sonish Sivarajkumar, Yanshan Wang, Zeshui Yu, Hui Ji, Yushui Han, Hanyu Zeng, Daqing He · 2024-08-16 · 10 sections
"RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarization of Biomedical Texts" by Yuelyu Ji, Zhuochun Li, Rui Meng, Sonish Sivarajkumar, Yanshan Wang, Zeshui Yu, Hui Ji, Yushui Han, Hanyu Zeng, Daqing He (2024), licensed CC BY. Shown here as extracted text, with facts drawn from it. https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.bionlp-1.75

This paper presents RAG-RLRC-LaySum, a system for turning complex biomedical papers into lay summaries for non-specialists. It combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Reinforcement Learning for Readability Control. The system processes whole papers, uses an initial lay summary to retrieve Wikipedia descriptions through Pyserini, and applies ColBERT or BGE-v2 neural rerankers to select relevant background information. Gemini-1.0-pro paraphrases initial generations or directly produces summaries while seeking to retain input keywords. RLRC uses PPO and a Gaussian-based reward tied to Flesch Reading Ease to make outputs clearer than approaches guided mainly by fine-tuning and ROUGE. Training uses the Longformer Encoder-Decoder. Experiments on PLOS and eLife, whose technical abstracts have expert lay summaries, assess relevance, readability, and factuality with ROUGE, BERTScore, FKGL, DCRS, LENS, Summac, and AlignScore. Retrieval and reranking generally improve relevance over Plain LED, while some Gemini and ChatGPT variants improve readability despite lower ROUGE-L. RAG-RLRC improves factuality, with reported Summac results including a 66.68 average and 72.97 on PLOS; another reported PLOS comparison gives 78.45 versus 73.44 for Plain LED. The framework surpassed Plain Gemini and ranked 11th in BioLaySumm, but faces source bias, computational cost, imperfect readability measures, uncertain cross-domain performance, and reliance on automated evaluation.

Introduction body

The unit concerns biomedical research and the development of automated lay summaries that make complex studies understandable to non-specialists. It presents RAG-RLRC-LaySum, a framework that retrieves trustworthy external explanations, including from Wikipedia, using Retrieval-Augmented Generation and a neural re-ranker. The framework also uses a reward-based approach with readability metrics to improve clarity beyond what traditional fine-tuning and ROUGE scores provide. During training, it uses the Longformer Encoder-Decoder, and the method ranked 11th in the BioLaySumm challenge.

Methodology .. 2.3 body

The methodology describes a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework for processing entire scientific papers and generating lay summaries with relevant background information. RAG uses an initial lay summary as a retrieval query, retrieves Wikipedia descriptions with Pyserini, and applies ColBERT or BGE-v2 neural rerankers. Reinforcement Learning for Readability Control (RLRC) uses PPO and a Gaussian-based reward related to the Flesch Reading Ease score to adjust summary readability. Large Language Models, implemented with Gemini-1.0-pro, paraphrase initial generations and directly produce layman summaries while aiming to include input keywords.

4 body

This unit reviews related work on automatic summarization in the biomedical domain, focusing on simplifying scientific content for laypersons while preserving factuality. It describes approaches that use external knowledge, Wikipedia, and trained re-rankers to improve summary accuracy and relevance. The RAG-RLRC-LaySum framework is reported to improve readability and factual accuracy through knowledge retrieval and readability optimization. Its limitations include source bias, high computational complexity, imperfect readability metrics, uncertain cross-domain generalizability, and reliance on automated evaluation.
