The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917 · 28 sections

The document is a fantasy adventure in which Princess Ozma, ruler of Oz, vanishes along with crucial magical objects: her Magic Picture, Glinda the Good’s Great Book of Records, and the Wizard of Oz’s magic tools. Dorothy, Betsy Bobbin, Trot, Scraps, the Wizard, and many friends organize searches across Oz, while Cayke the Cookie Cook and the Frogman separately pursue Cayke’s stolen diamond-studded gold dishpan, a family heirloom that helps its owner cook well. Dorothy’s party travels through the Winkie Country and dangerous unknown lands, crossing the Merry-Go-Round Mountains, entering the illusory City of Thi, passing the Great Orchard, and visiting Herku. There they learn that Ugu the Shoemaker, a powerful magician in a wickerwork castle, may be responsible. Meanwhile, Cayke and the Frogman follow clues through the Winkie Country and Bear Center. The Lavender Bear King and the truth-telling Little Pink Bear identify Ugu as the thief and join their expedition. Near Ugu’s castle, the two parties unite. Ugu has used Cayke’s dishpan and inherited magic to steal the magical possessions and abduct Ozma, intending to become master of Oz. The rescuers overcome his defenses, a ring of fire, a steel wall, and illusory girl-soldiers, and enter the castle. Ugu traps them in an upside-down rotating hall, but Dorothy uses the Magic Belt’s daily wish to free them. When Ugu threatens them, Dorothy transforms him into a dove; he escapes briefly in Cayke’s dishpan. The Magic Picture initially suggests that Ozma has become a black spot, but the Little Pink Bear reveals that she is hidden as a golden peach pit in Button-Bright’s pocket. The Wizard opens the pit, freeing Ozma. The party returns to the Emerald City, Toto regains his growl, and Ozma restores the stolen items. Ugu, still a dove, repents and is forgiven by Dorothy. Finally, Cayke receives her jeweled dishpan back from Ozma.

front matter .. The Lost Princess of Oz front matter

CHAPTER 1 body

Princess Ozma, the girl ruler of the Land of Oz, disappears from her royal palace without telling anyone where she is going. Dorothy discovers that Ozma is missing and searches the palace and grounds with Jellia Jamb and Scraps, while questioning several of Ozma’s friends. Dorothy, Betsy Bobbin, and Trot then try to use the Magic Picture to locate Ozma, but they discover that the Magic Picture itself is gone.

CHAPTER 2 body

Glinda the Good discovers that her Great Book of Records and all her magical instruments and chemical compounds have been stolen from her castle. The theft leaves her unable to use the book or prepare a charm to identify the robber, although she retains her knowledge of magic. The Wizard of Oz arrives to report that Ozma has disappeared from the Emerald City and that Ozma's Magic Picture is also missing. Glinda tells him that the Great Book of Records cannot be consulted because it has disappeared as well.

CHAPTER 3 body

Cayke the Cookie Cook discovers that her diamond-studded gold dishpan has been stolen in the Yip Country. The Yips take her to the Frogman, who first gives unhelpful advice and then concludes that a stranger from the world below took the dishpan. Cayke, the Frogman, and nine Yips begin a journey down the mountain to recover it, but the Yips turn back at a deep gulf. The Frogman carries Cayke across the gulf with a great leap, and they continue into the lower world.

CHAPTER 4 body

The Frogman and Cayke the Cookie Cook arrive in the far-west Winkie Country after descending from the mountain of the Yips. They meet the Winkie farmer Wiljon and his wife Nellary, who are unimpressed by the Frogman and do not know where Cayke’s diamond-studded gold dishpan is. Cayke explains that the dishpan is a family heirloom with magic powers that make its owner a good cook, and the Frogman agrees that they must find it.

CHAPTER 5 body

Dorothy, Betsy, Trot, and Scraps discover that Ozma has disappeared from the Emerald City and that her Magic Picture, Glinda the Good’s Book of Records, and the Wizard of Oz’s black bag of magic tools have also been stolen. The Wizard suspects that an enemy abducted Ozma and removed the magical objects to prevent anyone from finding or rescuing her. Glinda and Ozma’s other friends decide to divide into parties and search the Land of Oz, with Dorothy leading a party into the Winkie Country and the Wizard accompanying her.

CHAPTER 6 body

After Ozma is stolen, Glinda, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, Tik-Tok, the Shaggy Man, Jack Pumpkinhead, Ojo, Unc Nunkie, Dr. Pipt, Dorothy, the Wizard, and others organize searches in different parts of Oz. Dorothy and the Wizard lead a party through the Winkie Country in the Red Wagon, joined by Betsy Bobbin, Trot, Button-Bright, Scraps, the Woozy, the Cowardly Lion, and later Toto. The travelers cross the Winkie River and enter the Rolling Prairie, where no one has news of Ozma and they spend the night with a shepherd. Although the shepherd warns them about the dangerous country ahead, the party decides to continue into the unknown territory in search of Ozma.

CHAPTER 7 body

Dorothy and her companions reach the Merry-Go-Round Mountains, a series of cone-shaped peaks spinning in opposite directions across a deep gulf. They first believe the gulf and mountains are impossible to cross, but Scraps devises a plan using the Sawhorse's harness. By joining the harness straps into one long strap, the travelers swing across the gulf and are bounced from mountain to mountain until they land safely on a green meadow. Everyone crosses, including the Wizard, who wraps blankets around himself to soften the bumps.

CHAPTER 8 body

Dorothy, Trot, Button-Bright, the Wizard, the Cowardly Lion, the Woozy, the Patchwork Girl, Betsy, Hank, and the Wooden Sawhorse travel west after bouncing across the Merry-Go-Round Mountains. They discover a walled city that repeatedly disappears and reappears in different directions, while music from it remains audible. A field of sharp thistles blocks the way, but the travelers cross it using blankets and by riding the Woozy and the Sawhorse. After reaching the wall, the Patchwork Girl walks through the apparently solid stone and disappears.

CHAPTER 9 body

Scraps leads the Wizard, Dorothy, Button-Bright, Trot, Betsy, and their animal companions through the Wall of Illusion into the City of Thi. They meet the Thists, diamond-headed, heart-bodied people who eat thistles and claim to govern themselves, and are introduced to the High Coco-Lorum. The High Coco-Lorum secretly admits that he rules the city by making laws to suit himself, but says Royal Ozma is not there and warns them about the Herkus. After seeing the city's jeweled mechanical auto-dragon and learning that the only available food is thistles, the travelers leave Thi to continue searching for Royal Ozma.

CHAPTER 10 body

The travelers pass through the turning-lands and camp in a stony country after eating yellow berries. Toto realizes that he has lost his growl and discusses the loss with the Lion, Hank the Mule, the Woozy, and the Sawhorse. The animals debate beauty and individuality, while the Wizard worries about their dangerous adventure and whether they will find Ozma. Toto suspects that whoever stole his growl also stole Ozma and hopes the thief will be punished.

CHAPTER 11 body

The Patchwork Girl discovers a vast orchard and leads the travelers there for breakfast. Button-Bright wanders away, eats the lone peach from an enchanted peach tree, and learns from a Bluefinch and a White Rabbit that Ugu the Shoemaker enchanted it. Scraps finds Button-Bright and leads him back to Dorothy and the others, who continue their search for Ozma. The travelers camp at the orchard’s edge, while Toto worries about losing his growl.

CHAPTER 12 body

Trot, Scraps, Dorothy, the Wizard, Button-Bright, and their companions travel from the Great Orchard to the City of Herku while searching for Ozma. They meet Vig the Czarover, learn that the thin people of Herku gain tremendous strength from zosozo, and see that the giants of Herku are enslaved. Vig tells them that Ugu the Shoemaker, a powerful magician living in a wickerwork castle in the mountains west of Herku, may have stolen Ozma and other magical objects. The travelers leave Herku and head west toward Ugu’s castle despite Vig’s warnings.

CHAPTER 13 body

The Frogman and Cayke the Cookie Cook continue searching for the stolen diamond-studded dishpan after spending the night in a grove. The Frogman finds the Truth Pond, bathes in it, and becomes unable to lie about his supposed wisdom. He confesses to Cayke that he is not wise at all and urges her to bathe in the pond as well. Cayke refuses because she fears that telling the truth would hurt the Frogman’s feelings.

CHAPTER 14 body

Cayke and the Frogman travel east through the Winkie Country seeking the diamond-studded gold dishpan that was stolen from Cayke. A Winkie woman advises them to continue east toward the Emerald City, where Ozma may be able to identify the thief. They spend the night with a ferryman who has been punished by the Tin Woodman so he cannot communicate with animals, birds, or fishes. The ferryman carries them across the Winkie River, and they continue east through scrubland into a forest.

CHAPTER 15 body

Cayke and the Frogman are stopped by Corporal Waddle, a small brown stuffed bear guarding the way to Bear Center. They agree to go peacefully after learning that the city is ruled by the sorcerer known as the Lavender Bear, whom Cayke suspects may know about her missing jeweled dishpan. Bear Center is revealed to be a clearing surrounded by hollow trees filled with hundreds of stuffed bears. The Lavender King arrives and faces the captured travelers.

CHAPTER 16 body

Cayke the Cookie Cook and the Frogman reach Bear Center while searching for Cayke’s stolen jeweled gold dishpan. The Bear King uses magic to show Cayke an image of the dishpan and learns from the Little Pink Bear that Ugu the Shoemaker has it in a wickerwork castle nineteen miles and three furlongs northeast of Bear Center. The Bear King agrees to accompany them and carry the Little Pink Bear, which can truthfully reveal what has happened. Cayke, the Frogman, and the Bear King then leave Bear Center for Ugu’s castle, while the King formally condemns Cayke and the Frogman to death ten years later for trespassing and asking questions about the theft.

CHAPTER 17 body

Frogman and Scraps meet on a hill near the wicker castle of Ugu the Shoemaker and discover that both their parties are pursuing Ugu for stolen possessions. Dorothy, the Wizard of Oz, Cayke, the bears, and their companions unite to search for Ugu, Ozma, the stolen magic, and Cayke's magic dishpan. The Lavender Bear King uses his wand to reveal Ugu watching the travelers through Ozma's Magic Picture, while the travelers can also see Ugu and his room.

CHAPTER 18 body

The Wizard holds a conference with the travelers to plan how to reach Ugu the Shoemaker, rescue Ozma, and recover the stolen magical objects. The Little Pink Bear confirms that Ugu stole Ozma and shut her in a dark place, believed to be a dungeon cell. The travelers cannot devise a way to defeat Ugu, so they decide to approach his wicker castle and look for an idea. Before they leave, Button-Bright is lost again, and the others proceed toward the castle without finding him.

CHAPTER 19 body

Ugu the Shoemaker, a descendant of famous magicians, studies his ancestors’ magic and plans to become master of the Land of Oz. He steals the Magic Dishpan from Cayke the Cookie Cook and uses it to plunder Glinda the Good, the Wizard of Oz, and Ozma of Oz of their magical possessions. Ugu also captures Ozma and takes her to the Wicker Castle, where he hides her and arranges his stolen treasures. The Great Book of Records and the Magic Picture reveal that expeditions led by the Wizard and Dorothy and by Cayke and the Frogman are coming to stop him.

CHAPTER 20 body

Dorothy, the Wizard, and their companions approach Ugu the Shoemaker’s wicker castle while continuing to search for Ozma of Oz and Button-Bright. The little Pink Bear leads them to a deep hole, where they find and rescue Button-Bright, but Ozma is not visibly there. The Pink Bear gives contradictory answers about Ozma’s location, eventually saying that she is among the group. Unable to solve the mystery, the companions decide to question Ugu at his castle.

CHAPTER 21 body

The Wizard leads Dorothy and their companions toward Ugu the Shoemaker’s wicker castle, overcoming a circle of fire and a spiked steel wall with simple magical counters. The Frogman leaps over the steel wall and uses a pin to make it disappear. Ugu then sends out an apparently enormous army of girl-soldiers, but Scraps discovers that the soldiers are an optical illusion, allowing everyone to pass through them. The group enters the wicker castle through its open gates.

CHAPTER 22 body

The Wizard of Oz and his followers enter the wicker castle to find and rescue Ozma of Oz, but Ugu the Shoemaker traps them inside its central hall. Ugu turns the entire room upside down, leaving the group imprisoned while he remains safe in a golden cage. Their attempts to reach the stolen magic fail, until Dorothy uses the Magic Belt's daily wish to return the room to its proper position. The group escapes the rotating-room trap, but Ozma remains hidden and Ugu is not yet conquered or punished.

CHAPTER 23 body

Dorothy uses the Magic Belt to transform Ugu the Shoemaker into a dove after he threatens her and the others. The enormous dove attacks Dorothy, but the Frogman fights it until Dorothy makes it as small as a canary bird. Ugu escapes in Cayke the Cookie Cook's golden jeweled dishpan to the northeast corner of Quadling Country. The Magic Picture locates Ugu and the dishpan, but shows Ozma only as a round black spot, suggesting that she may have been transformed into a chunk of pitch.

CHAPTER 24 body

The Wizard and his companions question the little Pink Bear about Ozma's location after seeing a black spot in the Magic Picture. The Pink Bear says Ozma is in Button-Bright's left-hand jacket pocket, where the group finds a golden peach pit. When the Wizard opens the pit, Ozma of Oz emerges from a pink haze, freed from her enchantment. The Bear King triumphantly declares that the little Pink Bear never makes a mistake.

CHAPTER 25 body

Ozma is freed from the golden peach pit, where Ugu the Shoemaker had hidden her, and the party returns with her to the Emerald City. Toto recovers his growl, while Ozma thanks the Frogman and the little Pink Bear for helping her rescuers. Ozma confiscates Ugu’s magical possessions and keeps him transformed into a dove until he reforms. Her safe return is celebrated with a royal procession, a week of festivities, and the restoration of stolen magical items, although Cayke still longs for her jeweled dishpan.

CHAPTER 26 body

Ugu the Shoemaker, transformed into a gray dove, asks Dorothy to forgive him for stealing Ozma and the magic belonging to her and others. Ugu says that quiet thought has taught him that only things acquired honestly can make someone content, and he prefers life as a dove to becoming a man again. Dorothy forgives Ugu, and he flies away. After Ozma is found, the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman return Cayke’s dishpan to the Emerald City, where Ozma gives it back to Cayke the Cookie Cook.
