Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912 · 34 sections

Abstract written by a model

Sky Island is L. Frank Baum’s fairyland adventure about Trot, Cap’n Bill, and Button-Bright, continuing Trot and Cap’n Bill’s earlier adventures. Button-Bright arrives beside the Pacific Ocean from Philadelphia after traveling by his family’s Magic Umbrella, which flies to any named destination but cannot be redirected or stopped. Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap’n Bill test it, then fly toward Sky Island and become stranded there when the umbrella is seized. On the Blue side of the island, the travelers are captured by Quitey Righty Boolooroo, ruler of the Blueskins. Trot is made servant to the Six Snubnosed Princesses, Cap’n Bill is assigned as Royal Nectar Mixer, and Button-Bright as Royal Bootblue. With help from Ghip-Ghisizzle, who believes the Boolooroo has illegally prolonged his rule by hiding the Royal Record Book, they seek the umbrella. Button-Bright retrieves the Record Book but not the umbrella, and the three escape through the Great Fog Bank into the sunny Pink Country. The Pinkies treat them more comfortably but debate whether outsiders must be thrown from Sky Island. Rosalie the Witch confirms their story yet supports expelling them. At the island’s edge, Polychrome, the Rainbow’s Daughter, arrives and interprets the laws to make Trot the rightful Queen of the Pink Country. Trot, Cap’n Bill, and Button-Bright resolve to recover the umbrella, while Rosalie proposes an army to confront the Boolooroo. Cap’n Bill commands the Pinkies’ march through the fog against the Blues. During the conflict he is captured, and Trot uses Rosalie’s invisibility ring to enter the Blue City. She disrupts the planned patching punishment, helps Ghip-Ghisizzle escape, and ultimately saves Cap’n Bill. The Boolooroo is defeated by the very punishment intended for Cap’n Bill, aided by Trot and a billygoat. Trot takes control of both countries, ends the war, makes Ghip-Ghisizzle the new Boolooroo, banishes the former Boolooroo and the Princesses, and destroys the Great Knife. After finding the umbrella on a rubbish heap, the party returns through the Fog Bank, where the umbrella briefly becomes an elephant. Trot reforms Pink Country rule, appoints Rosalie Queen, and then flies home with Cap’n Bill and Button-Bright. Button-Bright safely reaches Philadelphia, and his father locks the Magic Umbrella away.

A LITTLE TALK TO MY READERS front matter

L. Frank Baum explains that Sky Island continues the adventures of Trot and Cap'n Bill, who were introduced in The Sea Fairies. Button-Bright and his Magic Umbrella are essential to the story, and Trot and Button-Bright meet Polychrome, the Rainbow's Daughter, in the sky. Baum describes the sky country as a remarkable fairyland but says that Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are fortunate to return to Earth. He gives his address at Ozcot in Hollywood, California, and invites readers to write to him about Sky Island.

A MYSTERIOUS ARRIVAL body

Trot meets a mysterious boy named Button-Bright beside the Pacific Ocean and learns that he is from Philadelphia. Button-Bright says he arrived by dropping from the sky with his umbrella, though he has not yet explained how he got into the air. Trot introduces him to Cap'n Bill, and the three walk to Trot's home so Button-Bright can tell his story. Cap'n Bill offers to help and advise the strange young traveler.

THE MAGIC UMBRELLA body

Button-Bright arrives at Mrs. Griffith's frame cottage with Trot and explains that his family's old umbrella is magical. He says the umbrella can carry him through the air to places he names, including Buffalo, Chicago, Denver, and the Pacific Ocean, but it cannot provide food or change course after flight begins. Mrs. Griffith and Cap'n Bill invite him to stay, while Button-Bright promises to take Trot flying to the village the next morning. Cap'n Bill worries about Trot's safety and secretly prepares for the possibility that the umbrella can fly.

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE body

Trot and Button-Bright use the Magic Umbrella and a rope swing seat to fly from Trot's house to town and back. Cap'n Bill builds an additional seat, overcomes his fear, and joins them on a trial air-ride to Smuggler's Cove. After the successful trial, the three set out over the Pacific toward Sky Island with a basket of lunch. The Magic Umbrella cannot be stopped or redirected once it has been given a destination.

THE ISLAND IN THE SKY body

Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright travel by Magic Umbrella toward an island they mistakenly call Sky Island. Because Button-Bright gives the umbrella the destination “Sky Island,” it carries them past the first island and out over the sea. They pass through rain clouds and fantastical cloud shapes before descending toward a real island in the sky. The landing is rough, and an angry unseen person objects when Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright land on their feet.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES body

Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright arrive in Sky Island and encounter Quitey Righty Boolooroo, the blue ruler of the Blues. The Boolooroo accuses them of trespassing and damaging him, but Cap'n Bill challenges him and prevents him from taking the Magic Umbrella. The travelers learn that Blueskins live exactly six hundred years and that Sky Island calls itself a republic whose Boolooroo is elected for three hundred years. When the travelers try to leave, blue cords coil around them and the Magic Umbrella, capturing them.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES body

The Boolooroo has the captured Magic Umbrella taken to his Royal Treasury, then releases Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill because he believes they can no longer escape. The Six Snubnosed Princesses claim Trot as their slave and drag her into the Blue Palace. The Boolooroo assigns Cap'n Bill the duty of Royal Nectar Mixer and Button-Bright the duty of Royal Bootblue, sending both to the palace servants' quarters. The prisoners are threatened with being patched if they disobey.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY body

Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill are brought into the Royal Palace and introduced to its blue-clad servants. They meet Jimfred Jonesjinks, a patched Blueskin, and learn that patching is a severe punishment in Sky Island. Ghip-Ghisizzle tells them that he is intended to become the next Boolooroo and secretly suspects that the current Boolooroo has extended his reign by concealing the Royal Book of Records. He assigns Cap'n Bill to deliver royal nectar and Button-Bright to serve as the Royal Bootblue, then takes them to see the Blue City.

THE BLUE CITY body

Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, and Ghip-Ghisizzle tour the Blue City and learn about the two divisions of Sky Island. Ghip-Ghisizzle explains that the Blue Country has no sun, while the Pink Country lies beyond the impassable Great Fog Bank and is known only through the Book of Records. Button-Bright decides to recover his Magic Umbrella from the Treasure Chamber, while Ghip-Ghisizzle asks him to retrieve the Book of Records. The group returns to the palace for dinner, where Trot's absence begins to worry Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright.

THE TRIBULATION OF TROT body

Trot is taken to the luxurious apartments of the Six Snubnosed Princesses in Sky Island and is ordered to serve them as a slave. After the Princesses shake and slap her for refusing, they command her to feed their unusual blue pets. Trot treats the animals kindly, and the pets defend her when Princess Indigo tries to drive her away. The Princesses argue over how to punish Trot, while Trot and the pets remain in the apartments and later go to the servants' quarters for dinner.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER body

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are reunited in the Boolooroo's palace but remain threatened by the Blueskins and the Boolooroo. The Boolooroo plans to force them through the Arch of Phinis into the Great Blue Grotto and threatens them with patching, while Ghip-Ghisizzle tries to protect them. Button-Bright secretly takes the Treasure Chamber key from the sleeping Boolooroo and disguises himself as the guard of the Royal Treasure Chamber. He enters the chamber with a pillow to confront the Blue Wolf and recover the Magic Umbrella, which the prisoners believe is necessary for their escape to Earth.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF body

Button-Bright enters the Treasure Chamber, defeats the Blue Wolf with a sofa pillow, and searches unsuccessfully for the Magic Umbrella. He takes the Royal Record Book, then escapes the palace with Cap'n Bill and Trot after the Boolooroo raises an alarm about his stolen golden Key. They flee the Blue city, evade a Blueskin guard, and head toward the Fog Bank with the blue parrot, while the pursuing Blueskins stop at its edge in fear.

THROUGH THE FOG BANK body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, and the parrot travel through the moist Fog Bank while trying to escape the Blueskins and reach the Pink Country. A common frog tells them that the Pinkies are decent neighbors and offers to guide them to the border. Along the way, they encounter a giant lizard, the constellation crab Cancer, and a sleeping turtle. The frog carries them on its back in a powerful leap, sending them out of the Fog Bank and into a sunny meadow.

THE PINK COUNTRY body

Trot, Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and the parrot emerge from the Fog Bank into the entirely pink Pink Country. Four Pinkies mistake the travelers for possible enemies, then escort them as prisoners to the Pink City to appear before Queen Tourmaline. The travelers explain that the Boolooroo stole their Magic Umbrella and enslaved them in the Blue Country, prompting their escape through the Fog Bank. The procession reaches the center of the Pink City, where the Pink citizens gather to watch them.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN body

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are brought before Tourmaline, the young Queen of the Pink Country, after escaping the Blueskins through the Fog Bank. Tourmaline explains that Pink Country rulers have absolute power to enforce the laws but possess no wealth or special status, and that her only reward will be a statue after death. She searches the Great Book for a law concerning outsiders, since the Pinkies have no place for the strangers and have never before received people from beyond their borders. Until the next day, Coralie takes the travelers to her home, while Tourmaline considers whether to seek help from the reluctant witch Rosalie.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE body

Trot, Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and the parrot remain prisoners but are treated comfortably by Coralie in the Pink Country. They learn that the Pinkies are divided into the Sunrise Tribe and the Sunset Tribe, whose rivalry concerns which side the sun favors. Tourmaline convenes a Council of twelve to decide whether the strangers may stay or must be thrown from Sky Island. The Council’s two proposed outcomes are to honor the strangers as guests or cast them over the island’s edge into the sky.

ROSALIE THE WITCH body

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are brought before Tourmaline and the twelve Counselors, who are evenly divided over whether the strangers may remain in the Pink Country. Tourmaline summons Rosalie the Witch, who uses a vision to verify the Earth people's account of their adventures. Rosalie votes to have them thrown from the island, but explains that fairies will probably protect them because fairy power enchants the Magic Umbrella. The Pinkies escort the prisoners toward the island's edge through heavy rain.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME body

Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright are led to the edge of the Pink Country to be thrown over the gulf because they do not fit the Pinkies' color scheme. The Daughters of the Rainbow appear on a Rainbow, and Polychrome descends to protect the travelers. Polychrome interprets the Great Book of Laws to show that the travelers are entitled to protection and that Trot is the rightful ruler because she has the lightest skin. Tourmaline gives Trot her circlet, and the Pinkies accept Trot as their new Queen.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY body

Trot is made Queen of the Pink Country after Polychrome rescues her, and the Pinkies enthusiastically accept her as their ruler. Polychrome departs on the Rainbow after promising to watch over Button-Bright, while Rosalie the Witch pledges to protect and advise Trot. Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill decide that they must recover Button-Bright's Magic Umbrella from the Boolooroo in the Blue Country so they can return home. Rosalie proposes raising an army from the Pink Country, marching through the Fog Bank, and forcing the Boolooroo to surrender the umbrella.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES body

The Pinkies agree to join an expedition through the Fog Bank to fight the Blueskins, with Cap'n Bill as commander. Rosalie the Witch arranges for the King of the Giant Frogs and the giant frogs to help transport the army across the Fog Bank. The Army of Conquest reaches the Blue Country, where the Pinkies prepare to march on the Blue City after Blueskins flee toward it. Cap'n Bill believes the Pinkies can conquer the Blueskins and plans to ask them to surrender.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME body

The Boolooroo learns that the Book of Records is missing and prepares to have Ghip-Ghisizzle patched with Tiggle, but a Pinkie invasion interrupts the operation. Cap'n Bill's army reaches the City and forces the Blueskins to defend it while the Boolooroo closes the gates. The Six Snubnosed Princesses free Ghip-Ghisizzle from the Room of the Great Knife, but they imprison him elsewhere until he agrees to marry one of them.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL body

Cap'n Bill leads the Pinkies in an attack against the Blueskins, but he is captured after pursuing the fleeing Blueskins alone. Rosalie the Witch enchants the Pinkies' weapons, allowing them to defeat the Blueskins' longer lances, but her magic cannot be used again for three days. Trot, Button-Bright, and Rosalie make a rope ladder, and Rosalie gives Trot a magic ring that makes her invisible. Trot uses the ladder and ring to enter the Blue City secretly in search of Cap'n Bill.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE body

Trot enters the Boolooroo's palace at night to search for Cap'n Bill, who is being held for patching. She frees Tiggle from the Room of the Great Knife so the Boolooroo will have no one to patch Cap'n Bill to. Trot then accidentally enables Ghip-Ghisizzle to escape through a window and across the palace and City walls. Ghip-Ghisizzle reaches the Pinkies' camp, where Captain Coralie captures him and takes him to Rosalie the Witch.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO body

Trot uses the ring of Rosalie the Witch to enter the Boolooroo's apartments unseen and free Cap'n Bill, but Cap'n Bill is recaptured while trying to escape over the Blue City's wall. Trot fails to find the Magic Umbrella and remains in the palace after Cap'n Bill is taken back. Button-Bright gives Ghip-Ghisizzle the Royal Record Book, which proves that the Boolooroo's rule has unlawfully continued past its permitted term. Ghip-Ghisizzle, Rosalie the Witch, Button-Bright, and the Pinkies hold a council of war and plan to take the Blue City and capture the Boolooroo.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES body

The Boolooroo recaptures Cap'n Bill and takes him to the Room of the Great Knife to be patched. When Tiggle cannot be found, the Boolooroo orders the first living creature encountered to be brought in, and the soldiers bring a billygoat. Invisible Trot uses a rope to pull Cap'n Bill's frame away from the falling Great Knife, while the billygoat attacks the Boolooroo, soldiers, and Six Snubnosed Princesses. Trot and Cap'n Bill then bind the Boolooroo in the frame beneath the Great Knife, leaving him threatened with the punishment he intended for Cap'n Bill.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND body

Trot removes Rosalie's ring and declares herself Queen of the Pinkies and Boss of the Blues, with Cap'n Bill as her General and manager. She forces the Captain of the Guards and the soldiers to obey her, has the Six Snubnosed Princesses locked in their rooms, and leaves the Boolooroo bound under the goat's guard. Trot and Cap'n Bill search the palace for the Magic Umbrella but do not find it. When told that the Pinkies are preparing to attack the City, Trot orders the Blue City to welcome them and hold a grand celebration.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY body

Trot celebrates the end of the war after conquering the Blue Country and becoming Queen of Sky Island. The Pinkies and Blueskins enter the Blue City peacefully, where they feast and celebrate for three days. Trot makes Ghip-Ghisizzle the new Boolooroo, sends the former Boolooroo and the Six Snubnosed Princesses away, and orders the Great Knife destroyed. Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, Rosalie the Witch, and their companions plan to return home once Button-Bright's umbrella is found.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA body

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill search the palace and surrounding area for the missing Magic Umbrella but learn that Indigo knows nothing about it. Tiggle recalls throwing an umbrella-like object onto a rubbish heap, where Cap'n Bill uncovers the Magic Umbrella and its attached equipment. Trot decides not to return home immediately because she must arrange matters in Sky Island, including helping the Pinkies return home safely and establishing a new queen for them. The Pinkies then depart the Blue City with Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, Rosalie, and their companions.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE body

Trot, Button-Bright, Rosalie the Witch, Cap'n Bill, and the Pinkies enter the Fog Bank to reach the Pink Country with the help of the frogs. The Frog King refuses to let them pass while Button-Bright carries his umbrella, but the umbrella transforms into a huge elephant and frightens the frogs away. The elephant leads the travelers through the fog and changes back into the Magic Umbrella at the edge of the Pink Country.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES body

Trot and her companions return with the Pinkies to the Pink Country, where the people welcome them. Tourmaline refuses to become Queen again, so Trot creates a new law granting the Queen the same food, clothing, housing, money, and leisure as everyone else, but no more. Trot appoints Rosalie the Witch as Queen of the Pinkies and orders a new palace and statues for Rosalie, Tourmaline, and herself. The Pinkies give Trot the royal band and jewel, and she announces that she and her companions will soon fly back to Earth.

THE JOURNEY HOME body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright leave Sky Island in the Magic Umbrella and travel through the sky toward Earth. They pass clouds, a Rainbow with Polychrome and the Daughters of the Rainbow, stars, and a huge bird before landing near Trot's house. Trot returns home to her mother, while Button-Bright stays overnight and then flies to Philadelphia. Several days later, Trot learns that Button-Bright arrived safely and that his father locked the Magic Umbrella in an attic chest.
