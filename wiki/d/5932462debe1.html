The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · 23 sections

This document is a fantasy adventure centered on the Hungry Tiger of Oz, Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and Prince Evered, called Reddy, of Rash. It begins when Pasha Irashi of Rash, seeking a way to deal with his overcrowded prison, sends Ippty to Oz to obtain the Hungry Tiger as a jailer who will eat prisoners. Although tempted by the prospect of eating live people, the Tiger refuses to harm anyone and secretly shelters prisoners instead. Meanwhile, Betsy meets Carter Green, a Vegetable Man, and both are swept from Oz across the Deadly Desert into Rash. Irashi imprisons them for the Tiger, but the Tiger protects them. They learn from Fizzenpop that Irashi stole Rash from Prince Evered and scattered the kingdom’s three protective magic rubies. The group escapes through an underground route into Down, where they find the first ruby, then travel through the Gnome Kingdom, where Carter steals a second ruby from Kaliko and Guph. Protected by the rubies, they escape fire and continue toward Immense City. Giants capture the Hungry Tiger, while Reddy is carried into the city by pigeons. He discovers that the Tiger is being treated as Princess Elma’s kitten. Reddy frees him and learns that the apparent Giants are ordinary-sized people made huge by magical big wigs. He uses Elma’s wig to rescue the Tiger and reunite with Betsy and Carter. In a parallel journey, Ozma is abducted by Atmos Fere, an airman seeking proof of life near the earth. After she escapes and Atmos is repaired by Rusty Ore, they eventually meet Betsy’s party. Atmos possesses the third Rash ruby, completing Reddy’s protection. The companions return to Rash, where Reddy uses the rubies and giant wig to defeat Irashi’s forces, capture Irashi and Ippty, and reclaim his crown as the Scarlet Prince. Atmos then carries the captives away, while Ozma, Betsy, Carter, Reddy, and the Hungry Tiger return safely to the Emerald City by hurry-canes. Reddy keeps the rubies and gives the wig to Fizzenpop. The story ends with a celebration in Oz and the Hungry Tiger realizing that heart-ache is worse than stomach-ache, curing him of any wish to eat a living person.

The Hungry Tiger of Oz front matter

CHAPTER 1 body

The Pasha of Rash orders the cook punished after a burnt pudding causes a furious outburst. When Fizzenpop warns that the Rash prison is full, Ippty proposes feeding prisoners to a wild animal, and the Pasha decides to obtain the Hungry Tiger from Oz. Ippty travels toward the Emerald City of Oz by hurry-cane to fetch the tiger. Fizzenpop researches the plan and learns that the Hungry Tiger has never been known to devour a living person.

CHAPTER 2 body

Betsy Bobbin celebrates her birthday in the Palace garden of the Emerald City of Oz with Ozma, Dorothy, Trot, and many famous residents of Oz. The Hungry Tiger, worried about the Wizard's suggestions that he be stuffed, is tempted by Ippty's offer to eat prisoners in Rash. Ippty uses a hurry-cane to transport himself and the Hungry Tiger away toward Rash. Dorothy and Ozma see the flash of their departure but mistake it for lightning.

CHAPTER 3 body

The Hungry Tiger travels with Ippty to Rash and is appointed its future jailer, though Irashi secretly locks him in the prison courtyard. Hungry and troubled by his conscience, the Hungry Tiger refuses to eat a sad singer who has been condemned under Rash law. Instead, he hides the singer in an underground chamber and accepts food thrown over the wall. Irashi mistakenly believes the singer has been eaten and promises to provide more prisoners.

CHAPTER 4 body

Betsy Bobbin meets Carter Green, a Vegetable Man, in the garden of the Emerald City and buys strawberries from him with an emerald ring. Carter explains that he became vegetable-like by eating leftover vegetables, and he and Betsy are carried away when a winding road tears loose and disappears. In a desert, Betsy finds Quick Sandals for Carter, but they make him rush uncontrollably across the quicksand toward the Deadly Desert. Betsy fears destruction as the cart reaches the edge of the Deadly Desert.

CHAPTER 5 body

Betsy and Carter Green are carried across the Deadly Desert by the quick sandals and enter the country of Rash. After Carter tries to sell vegetables to Pasha Irashi, the Pasha orders the Rash Guardsmen to capture them and throw them into prison for the tiger. The prisoners discover that the tiger is actually the Hungry Tiger of Oz, an old friend of Betsy, though Carter fears this will make their situation worse.

CHAPTER 6 body

The Hungry Tiger protects Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, the Rash Barber, and other prisoners in the Rash courtyard instead of eating them. Fizzenpop brings the hidden Prince Evered of Rash to the courtyard and explains that Irashi seized the throne, stole the Rash scepter, and scattered the three magic rubies of Rash. The Hungry Tiger lowers Evered and then enters the underground cavern, after which the tiger, the Prince, the barber, and the other prisoners mysteriously disappear. Fizzenpop tells Irashi that the Hungry Tiger escaped, and Irashi assumes that the tiger ate the Prince.

CHAPTER 7 body

Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, the Rash barber, and the Rash singer escape from Rash through a hidden tunnel after the barber pounds on the cavern wall. The tunnel carries them through a wild series of slides and loops into a field of down beneath a square green moon. The Hungry Tiger and Prince Evered of Rash soon follow them through the tunnel, and the group discovers a strange city nearby. Betsy and Prince Evered become friendly, while everyone except Carter Green falls asleep in the field of down.

CHAPTER 8 body

Betsy Bobbin, the Hungry Tiger, Carter Green, Prince Evered of Rash, the Rash Barber, and the sad singer travel across Down toward Down Town. Carter discovers that a square ruby marked with an "R," which he found in a potato, is one of the Rash rubies, and Prince Evered takes possession of it. In Down Town, the Hungry Tiger leads the group into a restaurant, where they eat until the proprietor and the Down Officers arrest them. The prisoners are taken to the roof of a bank to face Dad, the King of Down Town, and a strange lady identified as the Queen.

CHAPTER 9 body

King Dad and Fi Nance, the Queen of Down Town, demand payment of a food bill for the Hungry Tiger of Oz and his companions, who have eaten nearly one hundred dollars' worth of food. Since the travelers have no money, Fi Nance assigns Betsy Bobbin to be a cash girl and Prince Evered of Rash to be an office boy, while the barber and sad singer choose to remain Down Town. In the public square, the travelers find the Indus Tree, pick tools and weapons, and discover a subway marked for Up Town. The subway leads them to Cave Inn, where they fall into darkness after the door opens.

CHAPTER 10 body

Kaliko, King of the gnomes, uses the exspectacles to watch Betsy Bobbin, the Hungry Tiger of Oz, Carter Green, and Prince Evered traveling through the Lost Labyrinth. He invites them to lunch and welcomes them to the Gnome Kingdom, while Guph guides them from the labyrinth to the underground castle. During the meal, Betsy explains their adventures and Prince Evered's stolen Rash rubies, prompting Kaliko and Guph to show sudden interest. Afterward, Carter overhears Guph discussing how to obtain another ruby.

CHAPTER 11 body

Carter Green overhears Guph and Kaliko discussing the protective power of a second Rash ruby and steals the ruby ring from the throne. Guph and the Gnome army attack Carter, Betsy Bobbin, Prince Evered, and the Hungry Tiger, but the Rash rubies protect them from harm and they escape. The Hungry Tiger carries the travelers over a cliff and down a fire-fall, where the ruby protects them from the flames, although Carter loses most of his ears. They reach the country beyond the Gnome King's Kingdom and begin traveling through farming lands.

CHAPTER 12 body

Carter Green returns with new corn ears, and he, Betsy, and Prince Evered continue traveling with the Hungry Tiger while hoping to find the last ruby and return to Oz. They encounter Giants, who seize the Hungry Tiger and carry him toward Immense City, prompting the others to follow. At the walled city, they cannot enter, so Carter finds a giant apple for supper and prepares beds outside the walls while the others sleep. Carter remains awake, determined to find a way into the city and rescue the Hungry Tiger.

CHAPTER 13 body

Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green wait outside the walls of Immense City, hoping to enter and find Reddy and the Hungry Tiger. Giant pigeons mistake Reddy for food and carry him over the wall, leaving Betsy and Carter alone. Carter plans to grow over the wall with Betsy, but the city door suddenly opens and a giant foot appears while a Big Wig calls for Betsy.

CHAPTER 14 body

Princess Ozma is carried from her castle into the sky by Atmos Fere, an airman who wants to use her as proof that life exists near the earth. Ozma worries about Betsy Bobbin's disappearance and what may happen to Oz during her absence. To escape, Ozma punctures Atmos with an emerald pin, causing them to fall back to earth. Rusty Ore, an ornamental iron worker, finds them and carries Atmos and Ozma to his shop so he can try to restore the airman.

CHAPTER 15 body

Ozma and Atmos leave Rusty's shop after Rusty repairs and inflates Atmos and makes him iron boots. Atmos carries Ozma south toward the Emerald City, but they are delayed by his curiosity, a storm, a magically produced dog house, and a furious dog. They then enter the great rolling country of the east, where waves of earth and mud carry them along and threaten their journey. Ozma refuses Atmos's suggestion that he remove the boots and fly away, hoping that something will turn up.

CHAPTER 16 body

Reddy is carried by a giant pigeon to the castle of Immense City, where he discovers that the Hungry Tiger is being kept as Princess Elma's kitten. Using the Rash rubies, he safely reaches the dining hall floor and follows a giant maid to Elma's playroom. Reddy watches Princess Elma mistreat the Hungry Tiger, then waits in her nursery until he finds and frees him from the doll clothes. Reddy and the Hungry Tiger plan to escape the Giants' castle that night, but Princess Elma returns with a plate of chicken and a doll bed before they can leave.

CHAPTER 17 body

Reddy discovers that the supposed Giants of Immense City become ordinary-sized when they remove their big wigs. He takes Princess Elma's big wig, grows into a Big Wig, escapes the Giant Castle with the Hungry Tiger, and returns to Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green. After demonstrating the wig's power, Reddy removes it and rejoins his friends, who now possess two Rash rubies and the magic wig. The four adventurers settle down to rest while Carter keeps watch for Giants.

CHAPTER 18 body

Reddy, Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and the Hungry Tiger meet Ozma and Atmos on the road while searching for a way back to the Emerald City of Oz. Reddy explains their adventures, and Ozma says they should consult the Wizard of Oz about restoring Reddy to the throne of Rash. Atmos reveals that he has the third lost ruby, which completes Reddy's set and protects him from danger in the air. The group then heads east toward the Deadly Desert, with Atmos offering to carry them across it.

CHAPTER 19 body

Reddy returns to Rash with Carter Green, Atmos, Betsy Bobbin, Ozma, and the Hungry Tiger, determined to conquer Irashi himself. Using the Rash rubies and the giant's wig, Reddy frightens away the Rash army, captures Irashi and Ippty, and takes back the crown of Rash. Fizzenpop proclaims that Evered, the Scarlet Prince, has returned and that Irashi's rule is over. During the victory feast, Reddy is restored as ruler of Rash, while Atmos takes the bound Irashi and Ippty away before the others realize he is leaving.

CHAPTER 20 body

Atmos Fere takes Ippty and Irashi into the sky to use them as proof that people live at the bottom of the air, intending to abandon them on a lonely island after his lecture. Ozma and her companions must find another way across the Deadly Desert, and Fizzenpop provides Rash hurry-canes that carry them safely back to the Emerald City. Reddy joins the travellers, keeping the Rash rubies while giving the giant wig to Fizzenpop. The travellers receive a joyful welcome in the Emerald City, while the Hungry Tiger learns that heart-ache is worse than a stomach-ache and loses his desire to eat a live man.
