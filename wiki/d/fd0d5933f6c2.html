Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929 · 23 sections

Abstract written by a model

This is an Oz adventure centered on Peter, a boy who returns to Oz after finding a gold piece in a pirate’s sack. He meets Jack Pumpkinhead and travels toward the Emerald City, but their route becomes a chain of strange dangers: Chimneyville, the Goody Shop, a hostile Christmas Tree, Scare City, and its imprisoned Iffin, Snif. After Peter frees Snif, the flying creature joins Peter and Jack, and they meet Belfaygor of Bourne, whose endlessly growing beard prevents him from rescuing Princess Shirley Sunshine from Baron Mogodore the Mighty. Peter, Jack, Snif, and Belfaygor undertake the rescue at Baffleburg, using Belfaygor’s beard to cross a chasm and enter the city. Mogodore imprisons them, beheads Jack, takes Peter’s pirate sack, and sets out to conquer the Emerald City, marry either Shirley or Ozma, and become King of Oz. Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor escape, recover Jack and their magical possessions, and retrieve the forbidden flagon from a labyrinth beneath Mogodore’s castle. Their pursuit is disrupted by Swing City and by the pirate sack, which traps Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor; Jack is carried to the Red Jinn, who repairs his leg. Meanwhile, Mogodore captures the Emerald City and Ozma’s palace, binds Ozma, Dorothy, Scraps, and others, and uses the magic belt to turn Scraps into a bird. Scraps escapes to seek Glinda the Good. Jack arrives with the forbidden flagon, breaks it on Mogodore, and uses its power to shrink Mogodore and his followers. Glinda frees the captives, and Jack releases Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor from the sack, though Scares emerge first and must be sent back to Scare City by Ozma. The rescuers are honored, Belfaygor marries Shirley Sunshine, and Glinda explains that Mogodore’s people were reddies enlarged by the flagon’s red liquid. Ozma returns them to their tiny city. Peter finally asks to go home to Philadelphia, where he tells his grandfather of Oz and values him as his best chum and bravest adventurer.

front matter .. Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz front matter

CHAPTER 1 body

Peter, stuck indoors by rain, finds a gold piece in one of the pirate's sacks and wishes to return to Oz. The coin transports him to a pumpkin-shaped cottage, where he meets Jack Pumpkinhead and learns more about Jack's history and Ozma's transformation from Tip. Peter and Jack set out for the Emerald City, but a red brick wall blocks their expected route. Peter opens a black door marked "Enter Here" and steps through it.

CHAPTER 2 body

Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead fall down a chimney into Chimneyville, where the Smokies and Chimney-villains attack them with smoke. Peter uses a candle end inside Jack's head as a lantern, allowing them to escape through another chimney into a quiet wood. They continue toward the Emerald City and visit the Goody Shop, where Peter receives bird seed labeled a good breakfast and Jack receives advice to keep his mouth shut.

CHAPTER 3 body

Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead encounter a walking Christmas Tree in the Quadling Country, which attacks them with grasping hands and thrown ornaments. They escape through a hidden rock door and discover that a silver bell summons a magically appearing dinner. After eating, they continue through a frightening corridor and arrive at the gates of Scare City.

CHAPTER 4 body

Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead enter Scare City, where the Chief Scarer and the Scares try to frighten them into trembling, running, or screaming. Jack uses the magic dinner bell, allowing Peter to throw dinners and dishes at the attacking Scares. When the pirate sack opens, it swallows Harum Scarum, the Scares, and the Fraid Cats, but Peter and Jack remain trapped inside the city. A red stone griffin then comes to life and asks them, "Who? What? Whither? Why?"

CHAPTER 5 body

Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead meet Snif, an Iffin who was formerly a griffin imprisoned in Scare City. After Peter frees Snif from his chain, Snif briefly flies away but returns and agrees to carry them toward the Emerald City. Snif explains that he was held captive for five years and offers to serve Peter and Jack for seven years. He then flies with them over the wall into the Land of the Barons and lands in a peaceful valley.

CHAPTER 6 body

Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif travel from Scare City through the Quadling Country toward the Emerald City. They meet Belfaygor of Bourne, whose beard has grown uncontrollably after he was mesmerized and now prevents him from rescuing Princess Shirley Sunshine. Belfaygor explains that Mogodore the Mighty has stolen Shirley Sunshine, and Peter and Snif offer to help recover her.

CHAPTER 7 body

Belfaygor tells Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif how a beard-growing enchantment caused his red beard to grow uncontrollably while Mogodore abducted Shirley Sunshine. Peter, Jack, and Snif agree to help rescue Shirley Sunshine from Baffleburg, using the pirate's sack and the magic dinner bell. They fly to the chasm outside Baffleburg and plan to enter Mogodore's castle at night, but Snif accidentally eats shrinking violets and becomes no bigger than a squirrel.

CHAPTER 8 body

Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, Snif, and Belfaygor must find a way to cross the chasm and reach Mogodore's city to rescue Shirley Sunshine. Snif, who has shrunk after eating violets, proposes using Belfaygor's beard as a bridge, with Snif flying it across and fastening it to a tree. The group plans to climb to the city gates in daylight, where the Baffleburghers may capture them and take them to Mogodore. They spend the night in a cave, using cut lengths of Belfaygor's beard as mattresses while Jack stands guard.

CHAPTER 9 body

In Baffleburg, Baron Mogodore the Mighty worries about the secret of the forbidden flagon while preparing to marry the captive Princess Shirley Sunshine. Shirley refuses to marry him but manipulates his vanity by suggesting he should become a king, prompting him to plan an invasion of Oz and the Emerald City. Wagarag warns that Ozma and the Wizard may defeat him, but Mogodore orders preparations for the expedition and intends to open the flagon once he is king.

CHAPTER 10 body

Peter, Snif, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Belfaygor travel toward Baffleburg to confront Mogodore. They cross a deep chasm and scale a steep cliff using Belfaygor's growing beard, then pass through a treacherous tunnel into a courtyard outside the city gates. Baffleburg's tilting towers fire golden spears at them, but Belfaygor enters a tower and lowers his beard so the others can climb inside. The adventurers begin descending a spiral stairway within the tower.

CHAPTER 11 body

Peter, Snif, Belfaygor of Bourne, and Jack Pumpkinhead enter Baffleburg pretending to be magicians so they can be captured and learn where Princess Shirley Sunshine is held. Mogodore recognizes Belfaygor, declares that Shirley will marry him, and orders the travelers imprisoned after their attempted magical tricks fail. Jack Pumpkinhead is beheaded, Peter loses the pirate's sack, and Belfaygor and Peter are locked in separate dungeons. Mogodore then marches toward the Emerald City intending to conquer Oz, marry Shirley, and become its king.

CHAPTER 12 body

Peter and Snif escape their dungeon when the shrinking violets wear off and Snif returns to his full size, freeing Belfaygor from the tower as well. They find Jack's head and the pirate's sack in Mogodore's castle, but Jack tells them about a forbidden flagon guarded beneath the castle. Believing the flagon may destroy Mogodore's power, Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor enter the lost labyrinth while Jack remains behind. They plan to find the enchanted cavern and obtain the flagon before Mogodore reaches the Emerald City and captures it from Ozma.

CHAPTER 13 body

Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor follow the labyrinth to an enchanted cavern, where magic mirrors make them believe it is filled with Ugly Muglies. They find the Forbidden Flagon in a fire fountain and retrieve it using a piece of Belfaygor's beard, after which they return through the labyrinth. Back at Mogodore's palace, they reunite Jack's Pumpkinhead with his body and prepare to leave with the flagon, the pirate's sack, the dinner bell, and the sauce box. They then depart toward the Emerald City on Snif's back.

CHAPTER 14 body

Peter, Belfaygor, Snif, and Jack Pumpkinhead travel toward the Emerald City on Snif while carrying the pirate's sack, the forbidden flagon, and Mogodore's magic bell. They are intercepted in Swing City, where Hi-Swinger, Tip Toppsy the Tenth, and the Swingers toss them among the swings and trapezes. Mogodore's sauce box falls from Peter's pocket and frightens the Swingers into dropping them into a safety net. The travelers escape to the ground with the sack, flagon, and bell still in their possession, then continue pursuing Mogodore.

CHAPTER 15 body

Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, Belfaygor, and Snif pursue Mogodore and his army after escaping Swing City's net. When Peter tries to open the pirate's sack over Mogodore, a gust of wind carries it upward and then sends it falling onto the rescuers, capturing Peter and Belfaygor while Snif disappears into it. Jack escapes the sack's fall with the flagon but breaks his wooden leg, retrieves the sack, and tries to summon help with the Red Jinn's bell. The summoned slave vanishes, and Jack disappears with him, leaving the pink hay field empty.

CHAPTER 16 body

Jack Pumpkinhead arrives at the glittering red glass Palace of the Red Jinn after being carried by the magic dinner bell. The Red Jinn laughs at Jack's appearance but repairs his broken leg and listens to his account of the pirate's sack, which contains the little boy, the baron, and the flying red monster. The Red Jinn begins devising a rescue and return to Oz, but Jack suddenly vanishes through another magic before the plan can be carried out.

CHAPTER 17 body

Mogodore arrives in the Emerald City with his army and captures the city and Ozma's palace while Ozma and her companions are playing blind-man's buff. His soldiers bind Ozma, Dorothy, Scraps, and the other celebrities and courtiers, while Mogodore declares himself the future King of Oz and announces his intention to marry Ozma. Betsy Bobbin hides in a butterfly bush and considers reaching the magic belt, but Mogodore finds it first. When Mogodore uses the belt to transform Scraps into a bird, Scraps escapes with Ozma's crown and flies away to spread the alarm.

CHAPTER 18 body

Mogodore forces Ozma to join him at a feast and declares that he intends to marry her and rule Oz. While the prisoners remain guarded outside, Mogodore tries to use the magic belt to bring the forbidden flagon to the castle. Jack Pumpkinhead arrives with the flagon, breaks it against Mogodore's nose, and releases its thin red liquid. Ozma hopes that Scraps and Glinda will come to her aid.

CHAPTER 19 body

Jack Pumpkinhead returns to the banquet hall and uses the forbidden flagon to shrink Mogodore the Mighty and his midget followers. Toto drives the frightened invaders into a corner while Ozma, Jack, and Shirley Sunshine realize the others imprisoned in the garden must be freed. Scraps reaches Glinda the Good, who releases the captives, and the company returns to the hall. Ozma is restored to her place, the prisoners are guarded, and Jack prepares to explain what happened and release three enchanted comrades.

CHAPTER 20 body

Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor arrive at Ozma's banquet after Jack Pumpkinhead shakes the pirate sack, but the sack first releases a horde of Scares that attack the banquet. Ozma commands the Scares to return to Scare City, and Jack explains how he, Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor escaped their adventures involving the forbidden flagon, Baffleburg, and the sack. The rescuers are celebrated for saving the Kingdom of Oz, while Mogodore and his tiny army are captured. The company then holds a wedding feast for Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine, and celebrates until daybreak.

CHAPTER 21 body

Peter spends the day in Oz before asking Ozma to send him back to Philadelphia. Glinda explains that Mogodore and his followers were reddies whose great size had been sustained by the red liquid in the forbidden flagon, and Ozma sends them back to their now-tiny city. Peter returns to his grandfather, tells him about his adventures in Oz, and becomes convinced that his grandfather is his best chum and bravest adventurer. His grandfather agrees that they should try flying together.
