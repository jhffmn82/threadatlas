Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893 · 11 sections

This document is the three-act play Dandy Dick. It centers on Augustin Jedd, Dean of St. Marvells, whose efforts to preserve dignity, fund the restoration of the Minister Spire, and control his household are undone by racing, betting, disguises, and misunderstandings. His daughters, Salome and Sheba Jedd, want to attend a masked ball with Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey, but their expensive dresses expose the family’s lack of ready money. The Dean has committed £1,000 to the Restoration Fund and must retrench. His widowed sister, Georgiana Tidman—formerly the racehorse owner George Tidd—arrives and proves a disruptive force, co-owning the racehorse Dandy Dick with Sir Tristram Mardon and secretly planning to bet on him. Georgiana agrees to stake Salome’s and Sheba’s money on Dandy Dick. At the Deanery, the Dean’s public offer brings seven wealthy donors to promise £1,000 each, making his own obligation urgent. When the Swan Inn burns, Dandy Dick is rescued and hidden in the Deanery stables. Georgiana persuades the reluctant Dean to bet £50 on the horse for the Restoration Fund. Edward Blore, sent to place the wager, secretly puts strychnine in Dandy Dick’s bolus in an attempt to keep him from racing, while mistakenly betting the Dean’s money on Bonny Betsy. Meanwhile, the girls return from the cancelled ball with Tarver and Darbey, who have arrived disguised as shabby Cavaliers. The confusion culminates when the Dean is arrested after being found administering medicine to Dandy Dick. At the police station, Hannah Topping recognizes him as her former master and tries to help him, but Noah Topping repeatedly mistakes events and adds charges against him. In the final return to the Deanery, the Dean is rescued and the various secrets emerge. Salome and Sheba have won fifty pounds at the races and accepted Tarver and Darbey; Georgiana has won £1,500 on the Handicap. The strychnine is traced to Blore, whose confession clears the Dean of poisoning the horse, though it reveals the Dean’s own concealed betting. Noah’s final accusation also collapses. Georgiana concludes that the Dean’s deceptions have damaged his authority, but offers help for the Spire through George Tidd. The play ends with the household accepting friendly laughter at the Dean’s folly.

DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT. body

Salome Jedd and Sheba Jedd plan to attend the Fancy Dress Masked Ball at Durnstone secretly with Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey, but they are alarmed by the expensive account for their fancy dresses. Dr. Jedd discovers their financial request and explains that the family must retrench because he has no spare cash and has committed £1,000 to the restoration of the Minister Spire. Dr. Jedd also announces that his widowed sister, Georgiana Tidman, is coming to live at the Deanery of St. Marvells, and she unexpectedly arrives after staying at Durnstone.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters. body

Georgiana Tidman arrives at the Deanery and reveals herself as a former racehorse owner known as George Tidd, to Augustin's embarrassment. Sir Tristram Mardon visits to discuss the races at St. Marvells and reveals that he co-owns Dandy Dick with Georgiana, who secretly intends to bet on the horse. Salome and Sheba disclose that they owe forty pounds, nineteen shillings and ask Georgiana to help them win the money. Georgiana agrees to place their petticoats on Dandy Dick, ending the act with the girls frightened by the risk.

THE SECOND ACT. body

After dinner at the Deanery, Sheba and Salome plan to leave secretly while Georgiana jokes about the meal, the guests, and horse racing. Major Tarver fails to sing because his music is missing, while Mr. Darbey plays the violin with Sheba accompanying him. The Dean learns that his thousand-pound Restoration Fund offer has prompted seven wealthy donors to give a thousand pounds each, leaving him responsible for the offer. When the Swan Inn catches fire, Dandy Dick is rescued and Georgiana insists on bringing him into the Deanery stables, before Mr. Darbey briefly returns for his violin and provokes the Dean further.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window. body

Georgiana and Sir Tristram shelter Dandy Dick at the Deanery after the fire at the Swan, while Hatcham fears the fire may have been deliberate and aimed at the horse. Georgiana urges the Dean to raise fifty pounds by betting on Dandy Dick in the handicap race, and the Dean eventually gives Edward Blore the money to place the bet. Blore admits that he has gambled at races, including on commission for the kitchen, and secretly adds strychnine to the bolus the Dean prepares for Dandy Dick. The Dean, Georgiana, and Sir Tristram each become anxious about Dandy Dick during the storm, while unidentified people approach the Deanery garden.

The curtains at the window are pushed aside, and SALOME and SHEBA enter; both in body

Salome and Sheba return from the cancelled ball furious and complain about the Military, Nugent Darbey, and Gerald Tarver. Darbey and Tarver, disguised and shabby as Cavaliers, apologize and explain that the Military were summoned to St. Marvells, while Georgiana reprimands them for taking the girls out without permission. Salome and Sheba help Darbey and Tarver escape when Georgiana calls Sir Tristram to remove them. Hatcham then reports that the villain who set fire to the Swan Inn has been arrested while administering a dose to Dandy, and Blore identifies the prisoner as the Dean.

THE THIRD ACT. body

At the St. Marvells police station, Hannah Topping learns that the prisoner Noah Topping arrested is the Dean, her former master from the Deanery. She frees the Dean from the prisoner's cell and feeds him while proposing an escape during the journey to Durnstone, but Noah returns unexpectedly and discovers them together. Noah locks the Dean back in the cell and adds charges involving Hannah and his midday meal to the existing allegations.

Georgiana Tidman and Sir Tristram visit the police-station to report that Augustin Jedd, the Dean of St. Marvells, has disappeared. Noah initially theorizes that the Dean committed suicide, but Georgiana recognizes him as the man arrested for dosing Dandy Dick and discovers him in the cell. The Dean says he was treating a suffering horse for chills, while Hannah secretly arranges his escape and warns that another charge threatens him. Noah takes the Dean toward Durnstone, but Hannah discovers that Noah has put Kitty in the cart and left Old Nick behind.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again. body

Georgiana and Sir Tristram rescue the Dean after he is pursued and forcibly brought home from the Hill, while Major Tarver and Mr. Darbey secure Salome's and Sheba's acceptances. The Dean discovers that Salome and Sheba attended a masked ball at Durnstone and won fifty pounds at the Races, while Georgiana won fifteen hundred pounds over the Handicap. The analysis of the bolus reports sixteen grains of strychnine, but Blore confesses that he added it while trying to remove Dandy Dick from the race and mistakenly wagered the Dean's money on Bonny Betsy. The Dean admits that he instructed Blore to back Dandy Dick for the Restoration Fund, and the unit ends with Salome and Sheba confronting him about his deception and proposed marriages.

Directly they have disappeared, NOAH TOPPING, looking dishevelled, rushes in at body

Noah Topping rushes into the Deanery with Hannah, accusing the Dean of being the unknown man who escaped his custody after allegedly alienating Hannah's affections. The Dean establishes that he is not the culprit and invites Noah and Hannah to dine in the kitchen. Georgiana tells the Dean that his own deception has caused his family influence and dignity to collapse, while promising that George Tidd may lend money for the poor old Spire. The company agrees that laughter at the Dean's expense is acceptable when it is directed at folly rather than virtue and comes from friends.
