Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916 · 28 sections

The document tells the adventure of Prince Inga of Pingaree, a prosperous pearl-trading island ruled by King Kitticut and Queen Garee. Pingaree possesses three hereditary magic pearls: the Blue Pearl gives strength, the Pink Pearl protects from danger, and the White Pearl offers wise guidance. King Rinkitink of Rinkitink, accompanied by his talking goat Bilbil, visits just before warriors from Regos and Coregos conquer Pingaree, destroy it, and carry away its people, including Inga’s parents, as slaves. Inga, hidden during the attack, rescues Rinkitink from a well, finds the pearls, and follows the White Pearl’s advice to sail with Rinkitink and Bilbil toward the Twin Islands. Using the Blue and Pink Pearls, Inga defeats King Gos’s forces and takes Regos, but loses his shoes containing the Pink Pearl. The shoes pass to Nikobob and his daughter Zella, who unknowingly benefit from their magic. Queen Cor captures Inga and Rinkitink through deceit, but Zella’s arrival enables Inga to recover the shoes, free the women and children of Pingaree, and begin liberating the other captives. Bilbil escapes King Gos, and Inga later frees King Kitticut and the male slaves from the mines, though Gos and Cor flee with Kitticut and Queen Garee as hostages. Inga sends his people home, appoints Nikobob to help rebuild Pingaree, and pursues his parents to the Nome King Kaliko’s caverns. Kaliko imprisons Inga and tests Rinkitink with magic, but the pearls protect them; Inga survives the dangerous Trick Caverns through his strength and courage. Dorothy and the Wizard of Oz arrive from Oz after learning of their plight, and Dorothy forces Kaliko to release Kitticut and Garee by threatening him with eggs. The Wizard and Glinda discover that Bilbil is Prince Bobo of Boboland under enchantment and restore him. After celebrations in the Emerald City, the party returns to a rebuilt Pingaree, where the pearl fisheries, homes, palace, and treasury have been restored. Finally, a fleet from Gilgad comes for Rinkitink. Though he prefers leisure, he accepts Kitticut’s reminder of his duty and leaves Pingaree with Prince Bobo to rule his own kingdom.

Chapter One body

Pingaree is a small, wealthy island in the Nonestic Ocean whose people gain their prosperity by harvesting and trading pearls with the Kingdom of Rinkitink. King Kitticut rules Pingaree with Queen Garee and their son, Prince Inga, while fearing another attack from the fierce peoples of Regos and Coregos. Kitticut reveals to Inga that three magical pearls, inherited by the Kings of Pingaree, helped secure victory in the earlier Great Fight. The blue pearl grants immense strength, the pink pearl protects its owner from danger, and the white pearl speaks wise and helpful words.

Chapter Two body

King Rinkitink arrives at Pingaree in a large boat with twenty rowers, his talking goat Bilbil, gifts, and a parchment entitled "How to be Good." King Kitticut welcomes him and escorts him to the palace, where Rinkitink gives presents to the royal family and court. Rinkitink explains that he secretly left his kingdom because his subjects feared he would get into mischief. During dinner, he reads parts of the parchment, which gives humorous advice about being good.

Chapter Three body

King Rinkitink remains on Pingaree while Prince Inga studies in a tree near King Kitticut's palace. When a fog lifts, hundreds of boats carrying warriors from Regos and Coregos land on the island and seize the royal household and inhabitants. King Kitticut, Queen Garee, and the people of Pingaree are taken prisoner, while Prince Inga hides in a tree and watches the palace and island being destroyed. The Northmen depart with their captives, boats, plunder, and pearls, leaving Pingaree completely conquered.

Chapter Four body

After the invaders leave Pingaree with Prince Inga's parents and countrymen, Inga comes down from his hiding place and finds the island ruined and apparently empty. He discovers King Rinkitink trapped in the well and, with Bilbil's help, pulls him out. Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil search the island, find a small supply of food, and confirm that no other inhabitants or boats remain. They spend the night beneath a cassa tree, facing an uncertain future together.

Chapter Five body

Prince Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil search the ruins of Pingaree for food, shelter, and a way to escape the island. Inga and his companions move marble blocks in the royal banquet hall and uncover the hiding place of the three magical pearls. The Blue Pearl promises strength, the Pink Pearl protection from danger, and the White Pearl advises Inga to travel to the Islands of Regos and Coregos to rescue his parents from slavery. A storm arrives as predicted, and Inga expects a boat to strand on the island by morning.

Chapter Six body

Prince Inga finds the magical boat promised by the White Pearl and agrees with King Rinkitink to row it toward Regos and Coregos rather than Gilgad. Rinkitink cannot move the boat, but Inga rows it effortlessly because of the Blue Pearl's magic. During the three-day voyage, Inga plans how to rescue his parents and the people of Pingaree while Rinkitink and Bilbil exchange jokes and songs. On the fourth morning, they reach the shores of Regos and Coregos.

Chapter Seven body

Regos and Coregos are ruled by the cruel King Gos and Queen Cor, who enslave the people of Pingaree after raiding their island. Prince Inga arrives at Regos with King Rinkitink and Bilbil, using the Pink Pearl's protection and the Blue Pearl's strength to defeat the warriors and break through the city gates. Captain Buzzub and the warriors flee, followed by King Gos, who abandons the City of Regos and crosses to Coregos. The oppressed people of Regos rejoice at the fall of their masters.

Chapter Eight body

Inga and King Rinkitink take possession of the palace and city of Regos after defeating King Gos, while Bilbil receives some credit for the victory. Rinkitink accidentally throws Inga's left shoe, containing the Pink Pearl, at a cat, and the remaining shoe is later discarded by an old woman, leaving Inga apparently powerless. The Voice of the Pearl advises Inga to conceal the loss, so he pretends to retain his magical strength and begins ruling Regos kindly. Inga postpones rescuing his parents and the people of Pingaree while King Gos and his followers remain on Coregos and plot against him.

Chapter Nine body

Nikobob finds Inga's lost shoes near King Gos's palace and takes them home as a present for his daughter, Zella. While carrying the shoes, he unknowingly gains the strength and protection of their magic pearls and destroys the feared beast Choggenmugger. Zella discovers that the shoes protect her from bees while she gathers honey. Her parents then decide that she should leave the next morning to carry two pails of honey to Queen Cor in Coregos.

Chapter Ten body

Queen Cor, tired of housing King Gos and his warriors, devises a plan to defeat Prince Inga through cunning rather than force. She visits Regos, pretends to honor Inga, and suddenly captures him and King Rinkitink with the help of four men. Queen Cor takes them to Coregos and declares that Inga will be her page while she considers how to use Rinkitink. King Gos and his warriors return to Regos and celebrate, although the nonwarrior people there mourn Inga's capture.

Chapter Eleven body

Zella leaves Nikobob and her mother’s forest cabin to carry two pails of honey to Queen Cor in Coregos. Although the journey is dangerous because of King Gos’s warriors and wild animals, Zella discovers that she has extraordinary strength and uses it to clear obstacles and defeat a wild boar. She crosses the bridge of boats safely and reaches the back door of Queen Cor’s palace without entering Regos.

Chapter Twelve body

Bilbil is left alone and shut in an upstairs room of the palace of King Gos, where he grows hungry and angry after the servants stop bringing him food. When he sees King Gos and his warriors entering the palace, Bilbil breaks down his door and attacks them on the staircase, causing them to tumble into a heap. He then escapes the palace and runs across the bridge of boats to the palace of Queen Cor.

Chapter Thirteen body

Queen Cor orders the women slaves from Pingaree to be whipped, but Prince Inga regains the magical power of his shoes after recognizing them on Zella. Inga defeats Queen Cor's attacks, takes the keys from her slave driver, and frees the women and children of Pingaree. Queen Cor flees toward Regos, collides with Bilbil, and apparently takes Queen Garee with her after removing the bridge of boats. Inga, Rinkitink, Bilbil, and Zella remain in Queen Cor's palace while preparing to rescue Garee and return the people to Pingaree.

Chapter Fourteen body

Inga, Rinkitink, Bilbil, and Zella recover the boat from Pingaree and sail to Regos to rescue King Kitticut and the men from Pingaree. They find that King Gos had removed King Kitticut from the mines, but Inga breaks the chains of all the slaves and organizes them to march toward the City of Regos. At the city, Captain Buzzub tells Inga that King Gos and Queen Cor have fled together by boat, taking King Kitticut and the Queen of Pingaree with them. Inga's attempt to rescue his father therefore remains unfinished, while the liberated slaves become an army to support him.

Chapter Fifteen body

Queen Cor flees from Pingaree to Regos with Queen Garee and warns King Gos that Prince Ingato is powerful enough to conquer both islands. Cor and Gos devise a plan to take King Kitticut and Queen Garee to the Nome Kingdom as hostages and threaten to kill them unless Ingato leaves their islands alone. They escape by boat with the captives, gold, jewels, and forty oarsmen. When Ingato learns that his parents have been taken, he declares that he will follow them and search the world for them if necessary.

Chapter Sixteen body

Zella is reunited with her parents, Nikobob and his wife, at Regos after returning in Prince Inga's silver-lined boat. Inga offers Nikobob the kingship of the Twin Islands and wealth, but Nikobob refuses both, explaining that poverty and obscurity protect him from danger. Nikobob later agrees to move with his family to Pingaree and is appointed to manage its rebuilding and pearl fisheries. Inga sends the people of Pingaree home with recovered goods, then follows King Gos's boat after the great fleet sails for Pingaree.

Chapter Seventeen body

King Gos and Queen Cor take the captive King Kitticut and Queen Garee to the Nome King's underground caverns after landing on the coast of the Wheelers. They offer King Kaliko gold and jewels in exchange for keeping the prisoners confined. Kaliko recognizes that Gos's account is false but accepts the bargain because he prefers to support the stronger king and fears offending Ozma of Oz. Kitticut and Garee are placed under guard in the caverns, while Gos and Cor leave after feasting with Kaliko.

Chapter Eighteen body

Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil travel to the Nome Country to rescue Inga's parents, King Kitticut and Queen Garee, who are held captive by King Kaliko after being taken by King Gos and Queen Cor. Inga gives the Pink Pearl to Rinkitink for protection, while the White Pearl guides their actions. King Kaliko displays his armies, receives the travelers as guarded guests, and then secretly separates them. Inga becomes trapped in a series of caverns and corridors and is finally chained inside an underground prison.

Inga escapes his chains using the Blue Pearl and enters the third cave, where a giant challenges him to wrestle. Instead, he retrieves an iron post and strikes the giant, defeating it. The floor of the cavern collapses into an abyss, but Inga avoids falling and uses the Blue Pearl's strength to leap across the gulf into the opposite passageway.

Prince Inga reaches the last cavern of the three and finds it transformed into a furnace filled with glowing coals, an obstacle placed by the Nome King. Unable to turn back or leap across the cavern, Inga tears loose rocks from the crooked corridor and makes stepping-stones over the coals. He crosses quickly, avoids being burned, and reaches a passage where a rock door opens. Inga then enters one of the Nome King's inhabited caverns, where he rejoins King Kaliko, Klik, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil.

Chapter Nineteen body

Rinkitink safely endures several of Kaliko's magical attacks, including falling rocks, a golden net, a Bottomless Gulf pit, and flying knives. The Pink Pearl protects Rinkitink and Bilbil whenever they are in contact, while Bilbil carries Rinkitink through the royal cavern. Kaliko admits that Rinkitink's protective magic defeats all of his tested arts, but he reveals that Inga has been facing the more dangerous trials of the Three Trick Caverns. Rinkitink and Kaliko then go to see whether Inga has survived, and they find him still alive.

Chapter Twenty body

Dorothy learns from Glinda's Great Book of Records and Ozma's Magic Picture that Inga is trying to rescue his parents and people after the destruction of Pingaree. Concerned about Inga and King Rinkitink's danger in the Nome King's caverns, Dorothy asks Ozma to let her help them. Dorothy and the Wizard of Oz cross the Deadly Desert on Ozma's Magic Carpet and reach the entrance to the caverns of the nomes. Dorothy carries eggs because contact with one can destroy a nome's immortality.

Chapter Twenty-One body

Dorothy and the Wizard of Oz arrive in the Nome King's caverns and help Inga secure the release of his parents, King Kitticut and Queen Garee. Dorothy frightens Kaliko into sending for the captives by threatening to show him eggs. The Wizard discovers that Bilbil is actually Prince Bobo of Boboland, transformed into a talking goat by an enchantment. The group returns over the Magic Carpet to the Land of Oz so Glinda may try to restore Bilbil's proper form.

Chapter Twenty Two body

Ozma prepares a splendid banquet in the Emerald City for the returning party from the Nome King's caverns and invites her friends. Glinda and the Wizard of Oz transform Bilbil through several animal and human forms until he is restored as Prince Bobo of Boboland. At the banquet, King Rinkitink enjoys Ozma's guests, sings songs, and celebrates the party's victories and planned return to Pingaree.

Chapter Twenty Three body

Rinkitink, Inga, King Kitticut, Queen Garee, Prince Bobo, and their companions return from Oz to Pingaree, crossing the Deadly Desert safely on the Magic Carpet. They find the Pearl Kingdom rebuilt, with a new palace, restored homes, renewed pearl fisheries, and pearls in the royal treasury. Nikobob is rewarded with a high office for directing the rebuilding and managing the kingdom’s business. Rinkitink remains in Pingaree, where he sings about the sudden changes and reversals of life.

Chapter Twenty-Four body

A fleet from Gilgad arrives at Pingaree after searching for Rinkitink, and Lord Pinkerbloo tells him that he must return to rule his kingdom. Rinkitink protests that he prefers a quiet life of eating, sleeping, and singing, but King Kitticut advises him that it is his duty to reign. Rinkitink agrees to leave after three days of feasting and merriment with Kitticut. He departs for Gilgad in a golden throne, accompanied by Prince Bobo, and sings a farewell song to Pingaree.
