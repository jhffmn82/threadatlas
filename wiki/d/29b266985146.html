The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923 · 25 sections

Abstract written by a model

The Cowardly Lion of Oz, by Ruth Plumly Thompson and illustrated by John R. Neill, continues the Oz stories credited to L. Frank Baum. It centers on Mustafa of Mudge, a blue-whiskered ruler who owns 9,999 and a half lions and becomes determined to acquire the Cowardly Lion of Oz after reading Famous Lions of Oz. Although Mudgers are forbidden to leave Mudge, Mustafa compels the traveling clown Notta Bit More and the orphan Bob Up to fetch the Lion, threatening them with his lion enclosure. Notta and Bob, who reached Mudge through a magic rhyme after a circus mishap, set out for the Emerald City but are delayed by the Kingdom of Doorways. Meanwhile, the Cowardly Lion leaves the Emerald City seeking a brave person to swallow, believing courage might be gained that way. He meets Notta and Bob, who pretend to travel with him while intending to capture him for Mustafa. Their journey carries them through Un, where hostile Featherheads try to expel them from the skyle. They escape in the Flyaboutabus with Nickadoodle, endure storms, the Preservatory, and a crash, and eventually divide: Notta, Bob, and Nick go to the Emerald City for help, while the Lion travels toward Mudge with Crunch, a Stone Man. At the Emerald City, Dorothy initially mistakes Notta’s disguise for a witch, but the Wizard of Oz reveals the truth. Glinda reports through the Magic Record Book that the Lion is in danger, and Notta’s magic verse transports Ozma, Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Bob, and others to Mudge. There, Mustafa has imprisoned the Lion among his thousands of lions. Crunch betrays the Lion, using seven magic words to turn all the lions to stone and attempting to take the Cowardly Lion away. Glinda and the Wizard stop Crunch, Ozma takes Mustafa’s ring, and Crunch is left as a monument. Magic cannot revive the Lion, but Dorothy’s tears and those of the others restore him; he decides he would rather be cowardly than a stone lion. In the end, Ozma settles Un’s troubles by summoning Unselfish to rule it, the Wizard reunites a half lion with its missing half, and Notta, Bob Up, and Snorer remain in Oz, with Notta providing for Bob’s education.

The Cowardly Lion of Oz front matter

The Cowardly Lion of Oz is a book by Ruth Plumly Thompson, founded on and continuing the Oz stories by L. Frank Baum. It is mostly about the Cowardly Lion and the people hunting him. The introduction describes Mustafa of Mudge, who has blue whiskers, as the one daring to hunt the Lion. The book is set in and around Oz and includes twenty-two chapters concerning the Lion's adventures and peril.

Chapter 1 body

Mustafa of Mudge demands another lion despite already possessing 9,999 and a half lions, but Tazzywaller explains that Mudge has no lions left and cannot afford more. Panapee finds a book called Famous Lions of Oz, which introduces Mustafa to the Cowardly Lion of Oz, whom he immediately decides to obtain. Panapee and Tazzywaller argue over who should undertake the dangerous journey, since the blue book of Mudge forbids Mudgers from leaving their country. Before a plan is settled, fifteen Mudgers report seeing a strange lion under a palm tree in Mustafa's garden.

Chapter 2 body

The circus in Stumptown is struggling to entertain its frightened, rain-soaked audience on its last day. The chief clown pulls a red-headed orphan boy into a trick and accidentally uses an old Oz formula, causing the boy and then the clown to disappear. The ringmaster deceives the crowd while the circus hurriedly departs, and the clown and orphan land together in a strange, flat, silvery desert.

Chapter 3 body

Notta Bit More and Bobbie Downs arrive in Mudge after being carried there by a strange rhyme and head toward a large blue-striped tent. Notta explains his four rules for danger and disguises himself as a lion when a blue-whiskered figure alerts the inhabitants to their presence. The white-robed company captures Notta and brings him before Mustafa of Mudge, who orders him locked up with ten thousand other lions. Bobbie follows them into the royal tent and reveals that the supposed lion is actually Notta.

Chapter 4 body

Notta Bit More reveals that he is not a lion and explains that he and Bob Up reached Mudge through a magic rhyme. Mustafa orders Notta to travel to the Emerald City and bring back the Cowardly Lion, threatening to send him and Bob to the lions of Mudge if he refuses. Tazzywaller helps persuade Mustafa to use Notta as a messenger instead of harming him. Notta and Bob leave Mudge through the lion enclosure and begin their journey north toward the Emerald City.

Chapter 5 body

Notta and Bob Up travel through a forest toward the Emerald City, following Mustafa's orders while trying to avoid the Cowardly Lion. They eat food from Mustafa, sleep safely under the protection of a fairy's red lantern, and continue their journey the next morning. Notta uses marshmallows to restore his white clown face and plans to disguise himself as a bear. At the end of the unit, they reach the Kingdom of Doorways, where seven doors stand in a wall and only one is correct.

Chapter 6 body

Notta and Bob Up investigate seven doors while searching for Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion. After being frightened by a baby dragon, thrown from Door Six, and pulled through the Queen's door, they reach a throne room occupied by Queen Adora the First and King Theodore the Third. Notta uses buttons as supposed titles and claims membership in the Order of Bachelors, but the Queen accuses him of stealing door jam. When the doormen move to punish him, Bob reveals that the bear is actually Notta.

Chapter 7 body

Notta and Bob Up enter the Kingdom of Doorways and face King Theodore and Queen Adora, who demand that Notta amuse them before allowing passage. Notta fails to make the rulers laugh but escapes with Bob through a door marked "Out." Outside, they meet a half lion who asks them to tell a wizard in the Emerald City about its separation from its other half. Notta and Bob continue north toward the Emerald City through the curious country of U.

Chapter 8 body

The Cowardly Lion longs to feel brave even though he always fights bravely when danger approaches. After the Patchwork Girl suggests that eating a brave person might give him courage, he decides to leave the Emerald City and find a brave woodcutter to swallow. He travels south through a forest toward the Munchkin Country, hoping to return with courage. The Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary discuss whether courage depends on feelings or actions.

Chapter 9 body

The Cowardly Lion searches for a brave man to swallow so he can gain courage, but he cannot eat the polite woodcutters and discovers that the supposed huntsman is actually Notta Bit More, with Bob Up. Notta and Bob travel with the Cowardly Lion toward the Emerald City, while secretly planning to capture him for Mustafa. The travelers learn about Oz and its countries from the lion, but their journey is interrupted when a knitting tree numbered twenty captures them in a net and sends them sailing through the air.

Chapter 10 body

Notta, Bob Up, and the Cowardly Lion of Oz are carried by a balloon to the rocky skyle of Un, where the Featherheads of Un capture them. They are taken to I-wish-I-was, King of Un, who declares each traveler unish and orders that they be pushed off the skyle at the first opportunity. The King gives them fishing rods and reels under the pretense of sending them to fish, but Notta realizes that the Uns intend to use the outing to expel them. The travelers decide to risk fishing while the Cowardly Lion watches for an attack.

Chapter 11 body

Notta, Bob Up, and the Cowardly Lion fish from the edge of the Skyland while trying to think of a way to escape the Uns. They catch a cooked goose, a skye terrier, and packages of dreams, which put Notta and Bob to sleep. The Uns attack during the night, but the Cowardly Lion holds them off until Notta uses the magic verse to send many of them to Mudge. By morning, the Cowardly Lion discovers that Notta and Bob are missing.

Chapter 12 body

The Cowardly Lion saves Notta and Bob after the Uns push them from the skyle, and the three travelers take shelter until they meet Nickadoodle. Nick explains that the Uns spend their days wishing, fishing, and fighting, and that the only escape is the royal Flyaboutabus. The travelers steal the vehicle while the Uns are wishing, with Nick cutting its rope and guiding it upward. During their escape, the travelers struggle with newly grown feathers and do not yet know how to bring the Flyaboutabus down.

Chapter 13 body

Notta, Bob, Nickadoodle, and the Cowardly Lion escape Un in the Flyaboutabus and land in the Munchkin Country. They find the Travelers' Tree, which provides them with a plentiful breakfast, then plan to travel to the Emerald City. Mustafa activates his magic ring, turning Notta and Bob blue and freezing their movements because they have not captured the Cowardly Lion. Nickadoodle uses a rope to make the Cowardly Lion count as captured, restoring Notta and Bob to normal.

Chapter 14 body

Bob Up, Notta, the Cowardly Lion, and Nick attempt to fly toward the Emerald City in the Flyaboutabus, but a violent storm fills the bus with water and forces them to crash near a woodcutter's hut. They recover at the hut, discover that their feathers have disappeared, and determine that flying straight ahead will lead toward Mudge. After drying the Flyaboutabus, they head toward a strange city, with Notta disguised as a huge fish. The city appears unlike any place they have seen as they pass through its glittering glass gates.

Chapter 15 body

Mustafa keeps constant watch over his magic ring after Bob and Notta's departure, hoping to see the Cowardly Lion appear in Mudge. He has twenty Uns imprisoned in a cage, while Mixtuppa keeps them alive for their colorful feathers and they teach Mustafa Unish. Mustafa orders a jeweled collar and heavy gold chain prepared so the Cowardly Lion cannot leave once he arrives. Tazzywaller is relieved that Panapee now suffers Mustafa's anger instead of him.

Chapter 16 body

Notta Bit More, the Cowardly Lion, Nick, and Bob Up enter the Preservatory, where the jarred Preserves arrest them after the Flyaboutabus breaks one of their glass jars. Preserva the Great orders them pickled, canned, minced, and potted, but the Imperial Squawmos reveals that she preserves the city's inhabitants so they will not have to work. Notta presses the only undamaged upward button, sending the Flyaboutabus into the sky, where it crashes into a small day star and throws the travelers out. Nick saves Bob, and a stone man catches Notta and the Cowardly Lion by the rope binding them together.

Chapter 17 body

Notta, Bob Up, Nick, and the Cowardly Lion are caught by Crunch, a Stone Man brought to life by the wizard Wam. Crunch joins the Cowardly Lion to travel toward Mudge and help defeat Mustafa, while Notta, Bob, and Nick head toward the Emerald City to seek help from Ozma, Dorothy, and the Wizard of Oz. After separating, the travelers eat eggs, descend into a forest, and discover that it is the Fiddlestick Forest of Oz, where the trees play enchanting music.

Chapter 18 body

Bob Up wakes in the Fiddlestick Forest to find Notta and Nick gone, discovers the Fiddlebow Boat, and soon learns that Notta has disguised himself as a witch. Notta, Bob, and Nick travel by boat and then across a meadow and the yellow brick road to the Emerald City of Oz. Dorothy mistakes Notta for a wicked witch and drenches him with water before the guards capture and march all three to the palace. Ozma raises her scepter to silence the shouting as the group enters the throne room.

Chapter 19 body

Notta Bit More, disguised as a witch, is brought before Ozma and the people of the Emerald City with Bob Up and Nickadoodle. The Wizard of Oz proves that Notta is not a witch, and Notta introduces himself, Bob, and Nickadoodle before beginning to tell of their journey through Oz. Glinda the Good Sorceress arrives with news from the Magic Record Book that the Cowardly Lion is in grave danger in Mudge. Notta uses his magic verse to transport a rescue party, including Ozma, to Mudge.

Chapter 20 body

Crunch travels with the Cowardly Lion toward Mudge and secretly plans to use a magic trick to make him courageous and keep him forever. Instead of waiting, Crunch carries the sleeping lion at great speed to Mustafa's Kingdom, where Mustafa has him captured and taken to the lion enclosure. Mustafa's 9,999 lions are about to attack the Cowardly Lion when Notta Bit More, Bob, Ozma, the Scarecrow, Dorothy, and others arrive. Crunch then reaches the enclosure and raises his arm to speak seven magic words.

Chapter 21 body

Crunch uses seven magic words to turn Mustafa's 9,999 lions, including the Cowardly Lion, into stone and tries to carry the Cowardly Lion away. Glinda and the Wizard of Oz stop Crunch by depriving him of the power to move, and Ozma orders Mustafa to surrender his ring and declares that Crunch will remain as a monument in Mudge. Back in the Emerald City, magic cannot restore the Cowardly Lion, but Dorothy's tears and the tears of the others bring him back to life. The Cowardly Lion says he would rather remain cowardly than become a stone lion.

Chapter 22 body

Notta tells Ozma, Dorothy, and the others about his adventures with Bob Up in Oz, including the Uns, the Flyaboutabus, and the Fiddlebow Boat. Ozma and her companions use the Magic Picture to observe a battle among the Uns, then summon Unselfish to rule the skyle wisely. Ozma invites Notta, Bob Up, and Snorer to remain in Oz, and Notta accepts, settling in a tent near the Emerald City. The Wizard reunites the half lion with its missing half, while Notta saves enough money to pay for Bob's education.
