Part of Oz series

Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920 · 27 sections

This document is a twenty-four-chapter Oz adventure about Princess Ozma and Princess Dorothy’s effort to stop a war between the Skeezers of the Magic Isle and the Flatheads of a nearby mountain, and the larger rescue and political settlement that follows. Glinda the Good first learns through her Great Book of Records that the two hidden peoples are at war. Ozma, accompanied by Dorothy and protected by magic, travels to investigate. Their journey includes escape from the Spider King’s web, passage over a mist valley with the Mist Maidens, and entry into Flathead Mountain. The Flathead ruler, the Su-dic, claims Queen Coo-ee-oh of the Skeezers turned his wife Rora into a Golden Pig. He rejects Ozma’s peace proposal and tries to imprison the girls, but they escape invisibly and reach the Skeezer island. Coo-ee-oh, a powerful Krumbic Witch, also imprisons them. Lady Aurex explains that Coo-ee-oh stole the magic of three Adepts by changing them into gold, silver, and bronze fishes; the struggle over the lake and these fishes drives the conflict. When the Flatheads attack, the Skeezer island submerges beneath the lake. The Su-dic changes Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan, but loses his poison for the fishes. Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers are trapped under the Great Dome because Coo-ee-oh alone knew how to raise the island. Glinda learns of their danger and leads a rescue expedition with the Wizard of Oz and others. Meanwhile, Ervic the Skeezer follows the enchanted fishes’ directions to Reera the Red, who restores them as the Adepts Audah, Aurah, and Aujah. The Adepts join Glinda and the Wizard. They lower the lake, enter the Dome, and search Coo-ee-oh’s magical rooms. Dorothy realizes that Coo-ee-oh’s name contains the three needed magic words: “Coo” frees boats, “ee” extends the bridge, and “oh” raises the island. The island is restored and the prisoners freed. Ozma brings the Skeezers into the Land of Oz, makes Lady Aurex their Queen and Ervic Prime Minister, and restores the lake. At Flathead Mountain, the Adepts regain authority, the Su-dic is imprisoned, and Glinda gives the Flatheads round heads and access to their brains; they become Mountaineers. Ozma and her companions return to the Emerald City after establishing peace.

Glinda of Oz front matter

Chapter One body

Glinda, the good Sorceress of Oz, learns from Glinda's Great Book of Records that the Skeezers have declared war on the Flatheads, two previously unknown peoples in the Land of Oz. Glinda discovers that the Skeezers live on the Magic Isle in a lake and that the Flatheads inhabit a hollow-topped mountain nearby, but she cannot learn much more because both peoples use magic to shield themselves. Ozma, Ruler of Oz, decides that it is her duty to visit them and settle their dispute, and Princess Dorothy agrees to accompany her. Glinda gives Dorothy a protective alarm ring, and Ozma and Dorothy return to the Emerald City to prepare for their journey.

Chapter Two body

Ozma and Dorothy leave the Emerald City in the Red Wagon with the Sawhorse to travel toward the Skeezer Country, while the Scarecrow temporarily rules the Land of Oz. They are trapped by a strong purple web woven by enormous purple spiders led by the Spider King, who intends to make them servants. Dorothy's Magic Belt protects her from the Spider King's attack, and Ozma's Silver Wand repels him. A green crab agrees to cut the web after Ozma changes its color to white, allowing Ozma and Dorothy to escape.

Chapter Three body

Ozma and Dorothy reach a valley filled with dense, smoke-like mist and consider how to cross it. Ozma calls for the Mist Maidens, believing they live in the Mist Valley and will answer because they are fairies. The Mist Maidens carry Ozma and Dorothy safely over the mist to the opposite hillside, then disappear back into the mist.

Chapter Four body

Dorothy and Ozma cross a flower-filled plain toward the flat mountain where the Flatheads live, but stop to camp when night overtakes them. Ozma uses her silver wand and fairy magic to create a striped tent filled with food, beds, and other comforts. Dorothy reflects that everyone having such magic might remove work and worry, but Ozma explains that striving, helping others, and having things to marvel at make life worthwhile. After dinner, the table and its contents disappear by magic, and Dorothy and Ozma sleep in the tent.

Chapter Five body

Dorothy and Ozma travel from their vanished fairy tent to the flat mountain, hoping to reach its summit and meet the Flatheads. They discover an invisible stone wall guarding the mountain entrance and find a way around it. Inside, they climb a winding stairway and passage designed to warn the Flatheads of intruders. At the summit, Dorothy and Ozma encounter a large group of armed Flatheads, whose unusual appearance and metal clothing are described.

Chapter Six body

Princess Ozma and Dorothy enter Flathead Mountain, where the Flatheads bring them before the Supreme Dictator. The Su-dic explains the Flatheads' dispute with the Skeezers over fishing in the Lake of the Skeezers and describes how Queen Coo-ee-oh transformed his wife, Rora Flathead, into a Golden Pig. Ozma proposes a settlement, but the Su-dic rejects it, declares war, and orders the girls imprisoned. Before the Flatheads can seize them, Ozma and Dorothy vanish.

Chapter Seven body

Ozma uses her silver wand to make herself and Dorothy invisible, allowing them to escape the Su-dic and the Flatheads. The Su-dic and his soldiers pursue them but are delayed when the Golden Pig trips them. After reaching safety, Ozma and Dorothy decide to find the Lake of the Skeezers and warn its people about the Su-dic’s planned attack. They discover the lake and a beautiful island containing a large glass palace, but see no way to reach it.

Chapter Eight body

Princess Ozma and Princess Dorothy reach Skeezer island by crossing a retractable steel bridge into the glass-domed village. Queen Coo-ee-oh receives them suspiciously, rejects Ozma's authority, and boasts that she is a powerful Krumbic Witch. Ozma tries to explain her rule and prevent war between the Skeezers and Flatheads, but Coo-ee-oh orders the girls kept as prisoners until the expected Flathead attack. Lady Aurex takes Ozma and Dorothy to her house, where they are allowed to wander under the Great Dome.

Chapter Nine body

Lady Aurex shelters Ozma and Dorothy in her house and explains why the Skeezers are unhappy under Queen Coo-ee-oh. She recounts how Coo-ee-oh stole the magic of three Adepts in Sorcery by transforming them into a gold fish, a silver fish, and a bronze fish, causing enmity between the Skeezers and the Flatheads. The coming war concerns control of the lake and the fishes, whose destruction would deprive Coo-ee-oh of her magic. Ozma promises to help, while Dorothy urges Ozma to remove the cruel rulers from power.

Chapter Ten body

Dorothy and Ozma remain prisoners in Lady Aurex's house while Queen Coo-ee-oh prepares the Skeezers' island for a Flathead attack. At daybreak, Coo-ee-oh submerges the island and its Great Dome beneath the lake, revealing the Skeezers' underwater defenses. The Su-dic arrives with armed Flatheads, two copper vessels, and poison intended to destroy the fish in the lake. Coo-ee-oh sends a submarine toward the Flatheads, but the Su-dic throws the contents of a copper vessel into her face before she can use her magic rope.

Chapter Eleven body

Queen Coo-ee-oh falls into the lake and is transformed into a Diamond Swan by the Su-dic, who claims this avenges Rora's transformation into a Golden Pig. The Su-dic loses the poison needed to kill the fishes and cannot make more because Rora has forgotten her magic. The Skeezers are left helpless in their submarine and unable to return to their village, while the Flatheads return to their mountain believing they have won the war. The Su-dic celebrates the conquest of the Skeezers with a feast.

Chapter Twelve body

Coo-ee-oh is transformed into a Diamond Swan by Su-dic's magic and abandons the Skeezers, who remain trapped with their island beneath the Lake of the Skeezers. Ozma, Dorothy, and Lady Aurex recognize that only Coo-ee-oh knew how to raise the island, operate the under-water boats, and open the basement doors. The Skeezers accept Ozma as their Ruler, and she searches Coo-ee-oh's palace for a way to save them. Dorothy begins searching the lake for the three fishes that were once the Adept Witches, hoping Ozma can restore them and obtain their help.

Chapter Thirteen body

Glinda, the Good, learns through her Great Book of Records and alarm bell that Ozma and Dorothy are prisoners in the submerged great Dome of the Isle of the Skeezers. Queen Coo-ee-oh, the only one able to raise the island, has been transformed into a Diamond Swan by the Flathead Su-dic. Glinda cannot find or invent magic for raising the sunken island, so she travels to the Emerald City to seek help. She and the Scarecrow arrange for a Council of State to decide how to rescue Ozma and Dorothy.

Chapter Fourteen body

Princess Ozma's unusual council gathers in the Emerald City after Glinda explains the plight of Ozma and Dorothy. The counsellors decide that they must travel to the Skeezer country to investigate the sunken island and determine how to rescue them. Glinda the Good and the Wizard of Oz will lead the expedition because magic must meet magic, and the group plans to leave at sunrise. Glinda will send her stork chariot back to her castle, so the others will travel together on foot.

Chapter Fifteen body

The Wizard's rescue expedition travels from the Emerald City through the Winkie Country and into the Great Gillikin Forest to search for Ozma. When Button Bright becomes separated from the party, Glinda uses her sorcery and a small magic mirror to locate him asleep between a tiger and a wolf. Glinda magically immobilizes the beasts, and the Glass Cat finds Button Bright and brings him back. Glinda then warns Button Bright not to get lost again.

Chapter Sixteen body

Ervic and three other Skeezers are left drifting in the iron boat after Queen Coo-ee-oh becomes a Diamond Swan and abandons them. Three Adepts, transformed into a goldfish, silverfish, and bronzefish, approach Ervic and promise to help restore themselves and save the Skeezers if he obeys them. Following their instructions, Ervic uses Coo-ee-oh's silver cord to help bring the boat ashore, cleanses the copper kettle, and carries the three fishes along a path. Near dawn, he reaches a lonely purple cottage with white trimmings and asks the fishes what to do next.

Chapter Seventeen body

Glinda the Good and the Rescue Expedition reach the Lake of the Skeezers after deciding to postpone confronting the Flathead Su-dic. They learn that the Magic Isle has been submerged, imprisoning Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers beneath the Great Dome. The transformed Queen Coo-ce-oh, now the Diamond Swan, has forgotten the witchcraft needed to raise the island. Glinda plans to summon the three enchanted fishes, former Adepts at Magic, to discover how the island can be restored.

Chapter Eighteen body

Ervic the Skeezer enters Reera the Red's cottage carrying a copper kettle containing three fishes and refuses to leave despite threats from magical creatures. Reera, a powerful Yookoohoo, changes her own form and transforms the cottage's creatures while Ervic observes that she uses a closed right hand and touches the creatures. During a meal, Ervic cleverly suggests that the fishes be transformed into three magically adept girls, while insisting that they remain fishes, hoping to prompt Reera to make the transformation.

Chapter Nineteen body

Reera the Red transforms the three fish into three beautiful young women and Adepts at Magic after Ervic persuades her to accept their consent. The newly restored Adepts explain how they became fishes and agree to help save the Skeezer people by raising their island to the surface of the lake. Reera then assumes her natural form, reveals her red hair, and asks the Adepts and Ervic never to return to or reveal her cottage. They thank her and depart.

Chapter Twenty body

Glinda the Good and the Wizard of Oz enchant the abandoned submarine so that it obeys their commands, but the submarine cannot pass the marble door of the sunken island because they do not know its magic word. They return to camp, establish a comfortable encampment, and spend several days seeking a way to rescue Ozma and Dorothy and raise the island. Glinda and the Wizard continue studying magic and signaling to Ozma and Dorothy through the Great Dome, while the imprisoned girls remain safe. At the end of the unit, three stately visiting maidens and a young Skeezer arrive seeking Glinda.

Chapter Twenty-One body

Glinda learns that Audah, Aurah, and Aujah are the three Adepts at Magic, whom Coo-ee-oh had transformed into fishes after betraying them. The Adepts explain that Coo-ee-oh used the magical formulas and arts she stole from them, and they believe those secrets could help rescue Princess Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers. The Patchwork Girl suggests drying up the lake, leading Glinda, the Wizard of Oz, and the Adepts to plan lowering the lake enough to expose the top of the Great Dome while leaving water for the fishes.

Chapter Twenty-Two body

Glinda, the Wizard, and the three Adepts use an incantation to lower the lake and expose the Dome of the submerged island. The Wizard, Glinda, Ervic, and the Adepts enter the Dome and reunite with Ozma, Dorothy, Lady Aurex, and the Skeezers. They investigate the island's basement and learn that its machinery controls the submarines and bridge, not the island itself. Glinda and the others then go to Coo-ee-oh's room of magical instruments to search for the secret of raising the island.

Chapter Twenty-Three body

Glinda, Aujah the Adept, Ozma, Dorothy, the Wizard, and the other characters investigate the Room of Magic and discover Gaulau, a rare mineral powder used by Coo-ee-oh. Dorothy suggests that Coo-ee-oh's three magic words are the syllables of her name: Coo, ee, and oh. Coo releases the boats, oh raises the island, and ee extends the steel bridge to the mainland. The Skeezers cheer when the island rises, and the company prepares to join them and the Rescue Expedition.

Chapter Twenty-Four body

Princess Ozma establishes the Skeezers as loyal subjects of the Land of Oz, appointing Lady Aurex as their Queen and Ervic as Prime Minister. Glinda, the Wizard, and the three Adepts at Magic restore the lake, while the Skeezers celebrate their liberation from the lake and from their former Queen's cruelty. At the Flatheads' Enchanted Mountain, the three Adepts regain authority, the Su-dic is imprisoned, and Glinda transforms the Flatheads by giving them round heads and access to their brains. The former Flatheads become Mountaineers, and Ozma and her companions return to the Emerald City after securing peace between the peoples.
