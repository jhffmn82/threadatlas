The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928 · 23 sections

Abstract written by a model

The Giant Horse of Oz, by Ruth Plumly Thompson, continues the Oz stories founded by L. Frank Baum. It follows several converging quests caused by Mombi’s old enchantments and the threat of Quiberon, a monster who imprisons King Cheeriobed’s Ozure Isles in Lost Lake of Orizon. Quiberon demands a mortal maiden and threatens to destroy the islands. Cheeriobed’s son, Prince Philador, sets out to seek the Good Witch of the North, while the councillor Akbad goes after Dorothy, Betsy, or Trot; he abducts Trot with stolen magic wings. Meanwhile, Danny accidentally brings a stone Public Benefactor to life in Boston. Called Benny, he falls into Oz, meets the Scarecrow and Trot, and hopes to become a real person. Akbad carries them to the Ozure Isles, but they escape Quiberon’s cave into Cave City and then flee toward the Emerald City. Their route takes them through the Round-abouties’ house and Shutter Town. Philador finds the Good Witch’s home deserted, frees Herby the Medicine Man from a bottle, and travels with him toward Ozma. Joe King and Queen Hyacinth help them, and Joe’s giant horse, High Boy, becomes their swift carrier. Philador, Herby, High Boy, Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow eventually unite and reach the Emerald City. There they tell Ozma and the Wizard about Quiberon, the missing Queen Orin, Tattypoo’s disappearance, and Benny’s wish. Ozma and the Wizard go to Sapphire City, where the Wizard petrifies Quiberon. Queen Orin returns and reveals that Mombi had abducted her and transformed her into Tattypoo; Agnes helped break the spell. Orin is reunited with Cheeriobed and Philador, while Akbad’s lies are exposed. He is pardoned, banished to a cottage, and stripped of the golden wings. The ending restores the Ozure Isles’ family and prosperity: sea horses are returned, Cheeriobed and Orin become King and Queen of the East with Sapphire City as capital, Trot becomes Princess of the Ozure Isles, and Joe and Hyacinth are made rulers of the North. Benny chooses to remain stone and live in the Emerald City, and Herby becomes Court Physician.

The Giant Horse of Oz body

The unit introduces Ruth Plumly Thompson’s book The Giant Horse of Oz, founded on and continuing the Oz stories by L. Frank Baum. It presents a poem in which Thompson says the story concerns the Medicine Man and Ben, as well as a high horse. The Prince of the Ozure Isles and Trot are said to gallop away on the high horse. The book’s chapter list previews adventures involving the Ozure Isles, Tattypoo, Cave City, and other places and characters.

CHAPTER 1 body

The Ozure Isles of Oz lie in the Lost Lake of Orizon and are ruled by the kind King Cheeriobed, whose people have been trapped there by Mombi's monster, Quiberon. Quiberon demands that a mortal maiden be sent to serve him and threatens to destroy the islands within three days. Cheeriobed, his son Philador, and their subjects struggle to find a way to escape or obtain help from the Good Witch of the North. Philador hopes to cross the lake, and a blue gull tells him to return at moonrise because she has thought of a way to help him.

CHAPTER 2 body

Cheeriobed and his councillors cannot find a way to appease Quiberon, who threatens to destroy the Ozure Isles unless given a mortal maiden. Akbad decides to travel to the Emerald City of Oz to obtain Dorothy, Betsy, or Trot, while Philador secretly leaves the islands to seek the Good Witch of the North. The Mo-gull carries Philador away, and Akbad plucks the golden pear from the emerald tree. Instead of transporting him normally, the pear becomes golden wings that carry Akbad toward the Emerald City of Oz.

CHAPTER 3 body

The Public Benefactor, later called Benny, comes to life after Danny reads strange words from a little black book. He leaves his Boston pedestal, causes panic by trying to behave like an ordinary person, and falls through the earth into the fairy Kingdom of Oz. There he meets the Scarecrow, who explains that Oz accepts living beings of all kinds and offers to take Benny to the Emerald City to meet the Queen. Benny wishes to become a real person, and he and the Scarecrow begin traveling along the yellow brick highway.

CHAPTER 4 body

Benny, the Scarecrow, and Trot travel toward the Emerald City after meeting on the road, and Benny learns about the Scarecrow's history and life in Oz. Benny hopes to become a real person, while Trot promises she would dance with him despite his stone body. Akbad suddenly attacks them, identifies Trot as the maiden he seeks, and abducts her with his magic wings. Benny tries to resist, but Akbad carries Trot away toward the Ozure Isles.

CHAPTER 5 body

Akbad brings Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow to the Ozure Isles and leaves Trot to serve the monster Quiberon. Quiberon chases them through a jeweled cave, but Benny protects his companions and helps them escape through a waterfall and narrowing passages. Quiberon becomes stuck in the passage, while the three travelers reach the entrance to Cave City. They open the door by rocking with laughter and are admitted by an old mer-man.

CHAPTER 6 body

Akbad returns to the Ozure Isles and falsely claims that he saved Philador by carrying him to the Emerald City and left a mortal maiden in Quiberon's cave. King Cheeriobed sends himself and Guards to rescue the maiden, but they find neither Quiberon nor Trot. Akbad discovers that the golden wings will not detach and that he cannot fly beyond the island's shore. Cheeriobed prepares to welcome Ozma and Philador, unaware that Akbad's story is false.

CHAPTER 7 body

Tattypoo lives happily in the good witch's hut while ruling the Gillikens and helping anyone who seeks her advice. Agnes urges her to investigate her forgotten past and consider youth and beauty, so they look through the witch's window. Tattypoo sees something shocking through the blue pane and jumps out, and Agnes follows while trying to help her. Philador then arrives at the empty hut, where the cat with two tails escapes through a window.

CHAPTER 8 body

Philador discovers that Tattypoo has disappeared and learns from the magic slate that he must go to the Emerald City to seek help from Ozma of Oz. While searching the good witch's cottage for useful magic, he breaks an enormous bottle and releases Herby, a medicine man who has been trapped inside for thirty years. Herby joins Philador, and they take the thinking cap and a jumping rope before setting out through the forest toward the Emerald City. They leave after the thinking cap reveals the direction to the capital.

CHAPTER 9 body

Philador and Herby travel through the forest, cross a rushing river with the help of the jumping rope, and begin climbing the purple mountains toward the Emerald City. They meet Joe King, the King of the Uplanders, who offers to help them and summons his giant horse, High Boy. High Boy carries Philador, Herby, and Joe King to the gates of the purple city at the mountain top. Philador hopes to reach the Emerald City so Ozma can help save his father's kingdom in the Ozure Isles from a monster.

CHAPTER 10 body

Philador and Herby, the Medicine Man of Oz, are welcomed by Joe King and Queen Hyacinth in Up Town after arriving on High Boy. They enjoy a royal feast, then are separated from the castle when a sudden storm carries them down Joe King's mountain in their umbrellas. High Boy finds them and carries them toward the Emerald City, explaining that it lies two mountains and a couple of countries to the south.

CHAPTER 11 body

Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny are taken through Cave City by the mer-man Orpah and confronted by its shadow-like cave men. Orpah explains that Mombi abducted the Queen of the Ozure Isles and that Quiberon attacked the Islanders before Orpah escaped to Cave City, where he has been imprisoned for twenty years. King Silly the Second orders the travelers to be transformed into shadows, but when Benny steps before the blue ray, its flash and roar interrupt the process.

CHAPTER 12 body

Trot, Orpah, Benny, and the Scarecrow escape from Quiberon's cave when the blue ray explodes and carries them across Lake Orizon to the mainland. Orpah returns to the lake to tell Cheeriobed the news, while Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow begin traveling toward the Emerald City to seek Ozma's help in finding the Queen of the Ozure Isles. Along the way, they enter a narrow blue road marked "Roundabout Way" and arrive at a huge round house with a revolving door.

CHAPTER 13 body

Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny enter the round house and are forced to spin through several circles of Round-abouties before boarding a merry-go-round. The ring leader demands that they make him laugh and orders the Round-abouties to keep them going around, so Trot leads her companions up a tower stairway to escape. Benny carries Trot and helps the group reach the tower roof, from which they slide across the forest and land safely in the Munchkin Country. They then find a road that the Scarecrow believes leads to Jinjur's door.

CHAPTER 14 body

Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny meet High Boy, Prince Philador, and the medicine man Herby while traveling toward the Emerald City. Trot and Philador exchange accounts of Quiberon's demand for a mortal maiden, the disappearance of Philador's mother, and their recent adventures. High Boy agrees to carry the group to the Emerald City, where they intend to seek Ozma's help and report the good witch's disappearance. At a deserted village, the travelers encounter an Out Keeper who refuses to let them enter, and the chapter ends when the Keeper reveals his frightening appearance.

CHAPTER 15 body

The travelers pass through Shutter Town, where the Out Keeper, Tighty, and the other Shutter Faces hide behind blue shutters and try to shut themselves away from everyone. High Boy steps over the town wall, drops Tighty into pickle bushes, and carries the travelers safely across a river. As night falls, Herby's pills keep High Boy, Trot, and Philador awake and make High Boy race toward the Emerald City. The travelers reach the Emerald City, which shimmers like a jeweled capital.

CHAPTER 16 body

Cheeriobed learns from Orpah that Trot and her companions escaped Quiberon, who is trapped in a cavern, and that Ozma is expected to come to the Ozure Isles. Queen Orin unexpectedly returns by boat, but Quiberon attacks her before she can reach shore. Akbad overcomes his fear and uses the magic wings to rescue Queen Orin and carry her to the sapphire castle. Quiberon then attacks the castle, leaving its occupants fearing destruction.

CHAPTER 17 body

Trot, the Scarecrow, Prince Philador, Herby, Benny, High Boy, and their companions arrive in the Emerald City and tell Ozma and the Wizard of Oz about Mombi's enchantments, Quiberon, and their adventures. The Wizard lists the problems to solve, including finding Queen Orin and Tattypoo, undoing Mombi's mischief, and changing Benny into a real person. Using the magic picture and wishing pills, Ozma and the Wizard transport the group to the Sapphire City, where the Wizard petrifies Quiberon before he can attack the Castle of Cheeriobed. Philador is reunited with King Cheeriobed and Queen Orin, who reveals that she is the Good Witch of the North, leaving the company waiting to hear her story.

CHAPTER 18 body

Queen Orin recounts how Mombi abducted her from the Ozure Isles and transformed her into a witch named Tattypoo. As Tattypoo, Orin defeated Mombi and ruled the North until Agnes helped her break the spell by jumping through a witch's window. Orin returned to Cheeriobed and Philador, while the company discovered that Akbad had falsely claimed credit for rescuing her and Philador. Akbad was pardoned, banished to a cottage, and freed from his stolen golden wings before the company celebrated with a feast.

CHAPTER 19 body

Cheeriobed leads the royal party through Sapphire City, which they find to be one of the fairest cities in Oz. Ozma and the Wizard secretly restore the herd of white sea horses, delighting Cheeriobed, Orpah, Orin, and the little Prince. The Wizard moves Quiberon's great figure to the mouth of his cave and explains how it escaped. Ozma, the Wizard, and the others then travel with Cheeriobed's family to the Emerald City using wishing pills.

CHAPTER 20 body

Benny, Trot, Herby, Cheeriobed, Orin, Philador, and High Boy return to Oz and are welcomed at a grand banquet in the Emerald City. Benny decides to remain a stone man and is invited to live at the capital, while Herby is appointed Court Physician. Ozma declares Cheeriobed and Orin King and Queen of the East, with Sapphire City as their capital, and Cheeriobed makes Trot a Princess of the Ozure Isles. Ozma also appoints Joe and Hyacinth rulers of the North, prompting High Boy to leave to share the news with Joe.
