The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911 · 25 sections

Abstract written by a model

The document is an illustrated fantasy adventure about Trot and Cap’n Bill’s journey beneath the ocean. It begins beneath an acacia tree overlooking the Pacific, where Cap’n Bill warns Trot that mermaids lure people to drown, while Trot hopes to see one and survive. In Giant’s Cave, mermaids invite them to visit their deep-sea kingdom and magically transform them so they can breathe and swim underwater. Princess Clia, Merla, and Queen Aquareine welcome them to coral palaces, while Trot’s mother is kept asleep and their home protected. The visitors tour sea gardens and communities, meet creatures including King Anko, the ancient and friendly ruler of the ocean, and learn of the dangerous magician Zog. Aquareine takes Trot and Cap’n Bill on wider travels, including a hidden island inhabited by seals and turtles. Sea devils then capture the party, Trot, Cap’n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia, and carry them to Zog’s enchanted castle. Zog imprisons them to gain revenge on Anko and experiments unsuccessfully with attacks: a Yell-Maker, falling marble, heated water, and freezing ice. In the castle, Cap’n Bill discovers his long-lost brother, Cap’n Joe Weedles, among Zog’s slaves, and the captives receive help and warnings from Prince Sacho. Aquareine prepares an enchanted golden sword, and the four escape through a broken window toward the dome above the castle. Zog pursues them in an eel-like form after sending wolf-fish, destroys Aquareine’s sword, and knocks away her wand. King Anko arrives, crushes Zog, and rescues them. Afterward, Anko recounts his third pain and orders Aquareine and Clia to return Trot and Cap’n Bill home. The group revisits Zog’s castle, where the freed slaves elect Cap’n Joe as king and Sacho as his attendant. Finally, Aquareine gives Trot a ring to summon the mermaids, Clia restores Trot and Cap’n Bill to their natural forms at Giant’s Cave, and they return happily to their ordinary world.

front matter .. The Sea Fairies front matter

[Illustration: TROT AND CAP'N BILL body

Trot and Cap'n Bill sit beneath a giant acacia tree overlooking the Pacific Ocean and discuss mermaids. Cap'n Bill claims that anyone who sees a mermaid is lured into the water and drowns, though Trot points out that this account seems contradictory. He describes mermaids as beautiful, fish-tailed fairies with enchanting songs, and Trot says she would like to see one and survive. Their conversation ends when Trot's mother calls them into the house for supper.

[Illustration: _Chap 2._ body

Trot and Cap'n Bill take Trot's rowboat into Giant's Cave, where they discover a glowing blue underwater cavern. Mermaids appear around the boat and explain that they are water fairies who wish to please people and can transform visitors so they can live underwater. Trot accepts an invitation to visit the mermaids' deep-sea kingdom, and Cap'n Bill reluctantly follows to look after her. Trot takes Clia's hand, falls into the water, and calls cheerfully for Cap'n Bill to join her.

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._ body

Cap'n Bill joins Trot and the mermaids in the water and discovers that he has become a merman with a powerful green-scaled tail. Princess Clia and Merla guide Cap'n Bill and Trot through the ocean, protecting them from possible dangers such as sharks and devilfish. The mermaids explain that sea serpents are ancient, harmless, and kind, and that the mermaid palaces lie hundreds of miles away in the deepest part of the ocean. Using fairy power, the group travels swiftly toward the palaces.

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE body

Trot and Cap'n Bill descend into the ocean with Princess Clia and the mermaids and discover that they can breathe, swim, and remain dry through magic. They pass through colorful gardens guarded by swordfishes and arrive at the mermaids' coral palaces, where Queen Aquareine welcomes them. Queen Aquareine shows them that Trot's mother is asleep under an enchantment and promises to protect the house while the visitors remain below the sea. The queen explains the magical air space around mermaids and visitors, the use of fins, and the nature of mirages.

[Illustration: _Chap 5._ body

Trot and Cap'n Bill meet King Anko, the ancient sea serpent who rules the ocean but not the mermaids. Queen Aquareine explains that Anko is friendly and that the visitors can understand the language of every sea inhabitant. Anko describes his immense age, extraordinary length, past pains, and two shorter brothers, then leaves to return home for dinner. Trot and Cap'n Bill realize afterward that he forgot to explain his third pain.

[Illustration: _Chap. 6._ body

Queen Aquareine hosts Trot and Cap'n Bill in her underwater palace, where they rest, answer questions, and share a large midday dinner with the mermaids. Afterward, Merla leads them on an afternoon swim through the sea gardens and into the ocean to see jellyfish, singing barnacles, and snapping eels. Cap'n Bill discovers that he can light and smoke his pipe underwater because an air space surrounds them. The travelers encounter playful snapping eels but are not harmed.

[Illustration: _Chap. 7._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Merla explore the sea and visit codfish, Crabville, several kinds of crabs, mackerel, and balloonfish. The codfish are described as haughty, while the fiddler crabs play unpleasant music, the clown crabs perform repeated tricks, and the hermit crabs hide in shells and act unsociably. A mackerel named Flippity is caught by a hook, which the other mackerel mistakenly believe has taken him to glory. Merla explains various aspects of sea life, including why fish are caught and eaten, before leading Trot and Cap'n Bill back to the palace.

[Illustration: _Chap. 8._ body

Trot and Cap'n Bill are welcomed to the mermaid palaces, where they are given rooms, dressed for the occasion, and served a lavish meal. They attend an underwater reception with mermaid music and dancing before sleeping in comfortable sponge-covered couches. The next morning, Queen Aquareine announces that she will take them to the ocean's most interesting regions to see the largest sea creatures and visit King Anko. Before they leave, she protects both guests with the Magic Circle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._ body

Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, Trot, and Cap'n Bill swim through the sea to visit unusual creatures and plants. They meet a bashful octopus, who is distressed that humans compare octopuses to wicked and deceitful organizations. The party is attacked unsuccessfully by sawfishes because the Magic Circle protects them, then descends into darker waters and observes phosphorescent fish and argonauts. Queen Aquareine also explains the danger posed by sea devils and the imprisoned magician Zog, who fears King Anko.

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Clia travel through the ocean and discover an unnamed island that has never been seen by humans. Beneath the water, they observe colorful weeds, starfishes, sea horses, and sea spiders before reaching the island's surface. The island is inhabited by seals and turtles and visited by sea-gulls. Chief Muffruff's teasing of a turtle leads to a quarrel in which the turtles drive the seals from the island.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia are driven through the ocean by cuttlefish ink toward the Devil Caves and the hidden castle of Zog the Terrible. Sea devils surround them, using their crimson feelers to form a cage that carries the group forward. The mermaids' fairy circles prevent the sea devils from touching them, but the group is nevertheless trapped and unable to escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia escape the sea devils and enter a silver passage leading to a vast sea cavern and the Palace of Zog. Aquareine suspects they are in the hidden dominions of Zog and decides to face the magician rather than retreat. At the palace they meet Prince Sacho of Sacharhineolaland, now a slave, who leads them through the castle and a magical maze to Zog's presence. The group passes through an illusory wall into a magnificent chamber.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._ body

Zog explains that he captured Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, Trot, and Cap'n Bill to destroy them as revenge against King Anko, whom he hates. He says the prisoners will remain in his enchanted castle while he experiments with ways to overcome the mermaids' fairy power and destroy them. Zog assigns the mermaids and Trot to the Rose Chamber and Cap'n Bill to the Peony Room, allowing them to roam the castle but not leave it. Sacho guides them to their rooms and offers to help with their comfort.

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._ body

Cap'n Bill and Trot explore their rooms in Zog's enchanted castle, where they discover that Cap'n Bill's quarters contain a seemingly identical wooden-legged sailor. The sailor is Cap'n Joe Weedles, Cap'n Bill's long-lost brother, whom Zog transformed with gills after rescuing him from a wreck. Cap'n Joe explains that he serves Zog by sewing buttons onto the magician's clothes and warns that escaping Zog will be extremely difficult. He says Sacho may be able to help the prisoners, while Trot and Cap'n Bill discuss Joe's life beneath the sea.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia are held captive in Zog's castle and discuss escaping through an opening in its dome. Chief cook Tom Atto provides them with a meal, while Zog's slaves appear cheerful despite their servitude. Zog subjects the four captives to a first trial of strength by setting the Yell-Maker upon them, but Queen Aquareine's magic protects them. Aquareine destroys the Yell-Maker, and Zog then causes the marble ceiling to collapse over the captives, without harming them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._ body

Zog abandons his attempt to crush Cap'n Bill, Trot, Aquareine, and Clia with marble blocks and sends them back to their rooms, while Sacho warns that Zog may be planning to kill them. Aquareine decides they must try to escape, but learns that the great dome's opening is guarded by the prince of the sea devils and that every entrance is watched. The prisoners discover that Zog's enchanted castle has constant magical light, with no day or night, and that Zog supposedly has not slept for hundreds of years. Unable to escape immediately, they eat a fine dinner and sleep while preparing for a future attempt.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia endure another attack by Zog, who makes the water in their rooms dangerously hot. They discuss escaping Zog's castle, but Aquareine decides they need a weapon to overcome the guards and obtains a golden sword from the castle's goldsmiths. With Cap'n Bill's traded tools as payment, a goldsmith forges the sword, and Aquareine later charms it with fairy powers. The group returns to the Rose Chamber and goes to sleep while Aquareine prepares the weapon for their planned escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._ body

Zog freezes the water in the castle, trapping Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia in ice. Queen Aquareine uses her fairy wand to melt the ice and free them, then restores Cap'n Bill's warmth. She decides they must escape Zog's castle and uses the enchanted golden sword to break a window. The group swims toward the opening in the vast dome, where Aquareine intends to attack the prince of the sea devils and escape into the ocean.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._ body

Zog discovers that Aquareine, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Princess are escaping through the broken window and sends wolf-fish after them. Aquareine kills the attackers, but Zog reveals his terrible eel-like form and pursues the four fugitives, destroying her sword and knocking away her wand. King Anko arrives through the dome opening, captures Zog in his coils, and crushes him into lifeless pulp. King Anko then tells the rescued fugitives that they may return home through the dome.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._ body

Trot and Cap'n Bill visit King Anko's alabaster palace with Aquareine and Clia after Zog's destruction. Anko explains how he found Zog's castle, tells the story of his third pain, and claims that the law of the ocean gives him ownership of those whose lives he saves. He orders Aquareine and Clia to take Trot and Cap'n Bill home and restore them to their natural forms. Before they leave, he shows them the immense coils of his body beneath the palace.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._ body

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Princess Clia return to Zog's former castle to see what has become of his slaves before continuing home. Sacho proposes that the former slaves govern themselves under a king, and Cap'n Joe is elected to rule them while Sacho serves as his attendant. The visitors say goodbye to Sacho and Cap'n Joe, then swim through the dome and continue their return journey toward their mermaid home.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._ body

Queen Aquareine gives Trot a pearl-set gold ring that will summon the mermaids if she calls “Aquareine” at the ocean’s edge. The mermaids carry Trot and Cap’n Bill across the ocean to the Giant’s Cave, where Princess Clia restores them to their regular shapes. They reunite with their boat, say goodbye to Queen Aquareine and Princess Clia, and leave the cave. Trot and Cap’n Bill are glad to return to their everyday world, though they are pleased to have visited the mermaids.
