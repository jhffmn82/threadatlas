Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · 24 sections

The document is a fantastical Oz adventure centered on Prince Tatters of Ragbad, his headless father King Fumbo, and several intertwined searches for a missing head, a princess, and a fortune. A storm devastates Ragbad and blows off Fumbo’s head, though he remains alive. Tatters leaves with Grampa, carrying Ragbad’s flag and a red umbrella, to seek the Emerald City, recover Fumbo’s head, and perhaps marry a princess with a fortune. Their journey gathers unusual companions: Bill, a living iron weather cock; Urtha, a flower maiden awakened in Gorba’s enchanted garden; and eventually Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere, the Forgetful Poet. Tatters and Grampa escape bandits, survive Gorba’s garden and its winding stairway, pass through an underground lake, Fire Island, a volcano, and the icy island of Isa Poso. Grampa defeats the dragon Enorma, but King Chin Chilly deceives the group and strands them on an iceberg. A magical mishap turns them into crows, and after they return toward Oz, a storm takes them into the clouds. There they find Fumbo’s head among many floating heads and bring it back in Maribella’s knitting bag. In a parallel journey, Peer Haps of Perhaps City fears a prophecy that a monster will marry his daughter, the Princess. The prophet Abrog disappears with her, and Percy sets out to find them. Dorothy, separated from her route to the Emerald City, joins Percy; they escape a runaway road, wild wash women, and imprisonment on Monday Mountain. The parties unite beneath a tulip tree and continue toward Perhaps City. Tatters is mistakenly arranged to marry the cloaked missing Princess, whom Abrog calls a two-headed monster. The group exposes the truth: Urtha is the lost Princess of Perhaps City, transformed into a flower fairy by Abrog’s failed magic. Dorothy interprets the prophecy to mean Tatters, not a monster, and Grampa turns Abrog into a mouse. Urtha is restored, Tatters receives a yellow hen that produces a fortune of gold bricks, and Ozma transports everyone to Ragbad. Fumbo’s head is permanently restored, Ragbad prospers, Mrs Sew-and-Sew becomes Queen again, and Tatters and Urtha remain happy.

front matter .. Grampa in Oz front matter

CHAPTER 1 body

King Fumbo of Ragbad panics when a violent storm strikes the kingdom, damaging roofs, the red mill, cottages, and the rag crop. The storm also causes Fumbo to lose his head, though he remains alive and active because Ragbad is in Oz. Grampa brings the headless King back to Mrs Sew-and-Sew, Prince Tatters, and Pudge, and ties him to a pillar after he causes further chaos. The group leaves Fumbo kicking his heels while they go to the kitchen for coffee.

CHAPTER 2 body

Prince Tatters is told to search Oz for his father King Fumbo’s missing head and to seek his fortune, possibly by marrying a Princess with a fortune. Pudge advises that Tatters and Grampa should leave the next morning, while the people of Ragbad help by giving Fumbo a baked dough head. Grampa and Tatters prepare for their journey, and they depart from the castle with a huge red umbrella, supplies, and the patched flag of Ragbad. They begin marching down the red highway into the dangerous lands of Oz.

CHAPTER 3 body

Prince Tatters and Grampa decide to travel north from Ragbad to the Emerald City to seek help restoring Fumbo’s head and finding the Princess and the fortune. They enter the Blue Forest of Oz, where they meet Bill, a living iron weather cock created by a storm and brought to Oz. Bill joins them, but bandits attack their camp, and the three companions are eventually overpowered, bound, and dragged to the bandits’ camp.

CHAPTER 4 body

Grampa and Prince Tatters are captured by Vaga and his bandits, but Grampa tricks the outlaws by pretending to join them and telling a long story that puts them to sleep. Grampa recovers their possessions, takes a blue pouch and a bottle of patent medicine, and escapes with Tatters and Bill. Bill’s crowing alerts the bandits, forcing the fugitives to hide in a hollow tree. The tree has no bottom, and Grampa, Tatters, and Bill fall down into it.

CHAPTER 5 body

Prince Tatters, Grampa, and Bill fall down a hollow tree and land in a small park surrounded by an impossibly high hedge. Grampa uses the wizard's patent medicine to recover from the fall, while Bill reveals that he stole a tiny golden key from the robber chief. The key leads them to a golden gate and the beautiful Garden of Gorba, which Grampa suspects belongs to a wizard. The three enter the garden and realize that it may be connected to the wizard mentioned by the bandit.

CHAPTER 6 body

Tatters, Grampa, and Bill enter Gorba’s enchanted garden while searching for King Fumbo’s head, the Prince’s fortune, and a Princess. They encounter magical stepping stones, animal-shaped bushes, and a flower maiden named Urtha, whom Tatters brings to life with water from a gold watering can. Urtha explains that she was made of earth and has known only the garden, so Grampa accepts responsibility for her and invites her to join their journey. The unusual little army leaves the garden together, with Urtha dancing happily among the flowers.

CHAPTER 7 body

Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, and Bill remain trapped in the wizard’s garden until Grampa eats a house plant and grows a chimney and bay window. Tatters uses the magic medicine bottle to cure Grampa, and flower messages warn that Gorba will return at midnight and that anyone who stays all night will become a lantern tree forever. The group finds Gorba’s Winding Stairway, but Grampa turns its handle the wrong way, sending them whirling downward.

CHAPTER 8 body

In Perhaps City, Peer Haps learns from Abrog that a monster will marry his daughter, the Princess, in four days. Abrog suggests marrying the Princess himself, but Peer Haps decides to marry her to the young Perix instead. Before this can happen, the Princess disappears, and Abrog vanishes from his tower. When no one else will descend Maybe Mountain to find her, Percy Vere bravely sets out after the Princess and the prophet.

CHAPTER 9 body

Dorothy becomes separated from the route to the Emerald City after chasing a baconfly with Toto and meets Percy Vere, the Forgetful Poet. Percy tells Dorothy that the Princess of Perhaps City and Abrog, the Prophet, have disappeared after strange events involving a prophecy about a monster. Dorothy offers to use Ozma's magic picture to find the lost Princess, but she and Percy are captured by a runaway road headed for a pepper mine. They escape by grabbing a tree branch and then continue into a dark forest.

CHAPTER 10 body

Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, and Bill fall through a strange stairway into an underground lake and are swept toward Fire Island. The wizard’s medicine saves them from drowning and protects them from the lake’s heat and Fire Island’s flames. On Fire Island they meet Prince Forge John the First and the Fire Islanders, who cannot help them find King Fumbo’s head or return to Oz. Fearing that the medicine will wear off and they will burn up, the travelers are sent by boat toward Blazes, the keeper of a volcano.

CHAPTER 11 body

Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, and Bill are sent from Fire Island through a dark tower and into a volcano. Blazes erupts them from the volcano, and Gorba's medicine protects them from the boiling lava as it forms a flying island beneath them. They land in the Nonestic Ocean, spend the night on the floating island, and reach a snowy shore marked as the Illustrious Island of Isa Poso. A sign warns them to beware of the dragon.

CHAPTER 12 body

Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, and Bill travel across the frozen island of Isa Poso to find and slay the dragon Enorma, hoping to claim half the kingdom and Princess Poso. Grampa defeats Enorma by throwing snuff in her face, causing her to sneeze, lose her teeth, and flee into an icy stream, where she is put out. The travelers find Enorma’s cave, eat her stored meat, and then encounter King Chin Chilly the Third and his snow-men subjects. Grampa claims the reward for Prince Tatters of Ragbad, and Chin Chilly leads them toward the island’s kingdom, while Tatters begins to worry about the princess.

CHAPTER 13 body

Prince Tatters and his companions are stranded on an iceberg after Chin Chilly tricks them with the Princess of Isa Poso and cuts away the connecting land. The wizard's medicine protects Tatters and Grampa from the cold, and Urtha's flowers make the iceberg cheerful as it drifts east toward Oz. Grampa later smokes tobacco from a pouch, causing Bill to vanish and Tatters, Urtha, and Grampa to transform into crows. The adventurers then begin flying east toward Oz, led by the invisible Bill.

CHAPTER 14 body

Dorothy, Percy Vere, and Toto spend the night in the forest and continue toward the Emerald City. They find a woodcutter’s cabin, eat breakfast, and leave a poem and Dorothy’s gold ring as payment. While climbing a mountain, they are attacked by wild wash women and discover that it is Monday Mountain. The Tubbies capture Dorothy and Percy after Percy refuses to marry the Princess of Monday Mountain.

CHAPTER 15 body

Grampa, Urtha, Tatters, and Invisi-Bill cross the Nonestic Ocean, Ev, and the great desert to reach the Winkie country, but their magical crow transformation ends before they reach the Emerald City. A storm carries them and the red umbrella into the sky, where they meet Maribella and find King Fumbo's head among nearly a hundred heads in the clouds. They place King Fumbo's head in Maribella's pink knitting bag and return by traveling down a rainbow with Polychrome. The search for the Princess and the fortune remains unfinished.

CHAPTER 16 body

Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere are held prisoner on Monday Mountain and forced by the Queen of the Tubbies to work at the wash tubs. Percy Vere discovers the word that stops the running white fence and helps Dorothy and Toto escape through its gate. The Tubbies overturn their wash tubs, sending hot soapy water down the mountain and causing the prisoners to slide to the bottom. After fleeing, they meet Bill, who hears Percy call Dorothy a princess and flies away shouting about the princess.

CHAPTER 17 body

Dorothy and Percy Vere meet Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, Bill, and the disembodied head of King Fumbo beneath a tulip tree. The travelers recognize Dorothy as a Princess of Oz and learn that Tatters is the Prince of Ragbad, while both groups explain their journeys and their searches for missing princesses. Grampa decides that the combined party should search for the Princess of Perhaps City and the prophet Abrog. They make camp, eat supper, share stories, and spend the evening together before resting.

CHAPTER 18 body

Grampa's army continues its march toward the Emerald City but is diverted into the play grounds of the Play Fellows. The Play Fellows forcibly make Grampa, Percy Vere, Dorothy, Tatters, and Urtha participate in exhausting games, while Toto hides and Bill watches helplessly. Urtha escapes through a wicket gate, and Grampa escapes using a croquet mallet after discovering that his game leg has been stolen. Tatters follows Urtha's trail of daisies and forget-me-nots to search for her.

CHAPTER 19 body

Peer Haps finds his missing daughter, the Princess of Perhaps City, climbing Maybe Mountain, but she is cloaked and apparently bewitched. He hastily arranges for the arriving Prince of Ragbad to marry her, believing the marriage will protect her from the prophesied monster. Tatters is confused and distressed, while the Forgetful Poet, Percy Vere, Dorothy, and Grampa arrive and expose the mix-up. Abrog then declares that Tatters has married a two-headed monster, but Tatters seizes Abrog and removes the cloak, revealing the frightened Princess herself.

CHAPTER 20 body

Dorothy discovers that the flower maiden is Urtha, the lost Princess of Perhaps City, and that Abrog is responsible for bewitching her. Abrog confesses that he intended to turn Urtha into mud so the monster would not marry her, but his magic transformed her into a flower fairy instead. Dorothy interprets the prophecy as describing Tatters rather than a monster. Grampa uses the wizard's medicine to punish Abrog, transforming him into a mouse, but the group then realizes that no one knows how to restore Urtha.

CHAPTER 21 body

Urtha is cured of her enchantment and transformed from a little flower fairy back into a lovely Princess. Peer Haps learns the adventurers' full story and welcomes Fumbo as the King of Ragbad, while Grampa recovers his lost leg with Bill's help. Peer Haps gives Tatters the yellow hen so he can receive a fortune of a thousand gold bricks. Just as everyone celebrates, a blinding flash makes everyone in the throne room disappear.

CHAPTER 22 body

Ozma uses her magic belt to transport Dorothy, Peer Haps, the Forgetful Poet, and their companions to Ragbad, where they reunite with Mrs Sew-and-Sew, Tatters, Urtha, Fumbo, Pudge, and Grampa. Fumbo’s head is permanently restored, and everyone celebrates with a feast and the Ragbad Quadrille. Dorothy, Toto, Peer Haps, and the Forgetful Poet later return home, while Ragbad becomes prosperous through the gold bricks produced by the yellow hen. Mrs Sew-and-Sew becomes Queen of Ragbad again, and Tatters and Urtha remain happy.
