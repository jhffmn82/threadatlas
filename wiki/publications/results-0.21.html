The arms

Arm What the reader gets
plain RAG (flat- on LongMemEval, plain-rag- on GraphRAG-Bench) raw text by vector search, packed to the budget: turns on LongMemEval, 256-token chunks of the source on GraphRAG-Bench
plain RAG, top 5 (plain-rag) the benchmark's own five chunks
chunks through hybrid search (chunks-hybrid-) the store's chunks only, found by ThreadAtlas's fused keyword and vector search; no fact, cell, abstract or parent; no walk
graph and chunks, hop 0, 1, 2 (hop<n>-) ThreadAtlas 0.21: facts, cells, abstracts, parents and chunks, found by the same fused search, then a walk of 0, 1 or 2 hops, packed as whole bundles
full context LongMemEval only: the question's whole history
no context GraphRAG-Bench only: the question alone

Budgets are 2,000 and 12,000 tokens. "Filtered" searches each question's own history (LongMemEval) or its own set's documents (GraphRAG-Bench). "Whole store" searches all 24,071 documents: chats, books and papers together. Medical has no whole-store run.

Plain RAG to chunks through hybrid search isolates the search. Chunks through hybrid search to graph and chunks isolates the graph.

Commentary

  • Over the whole store the graph wins at every setting, by 5.8 to 11.8 points (best per budget: +11.8 at 2,000, +8.8 at 12,000; every graph arm p 0.01 or below). Hybrid search over chunks alone does not: it is within error of plain RAG at both budgets. On LongMemEval the gain over the whole store is the graph's, not the search's.
  • The graph loses less when the haystack becomes the library. From each question's own history to the whole store, plain RAG falls 36.8 points at 2,000 tokens and 31.2 at 12,000; the best graph arm falls 28.8 and 26.2.
  • Filtered, the graph is ahead at every setting by 1.6 to 3.8 points. One setting, hop 1 at 12,000, is below p 0.05 before any correction for the number of comparisons. Chunks through hybrid search score below plain RAG filtered: a turn of a chat is a better unit than a 256-token chunk that cuts across turns.
  • The graph uses fewer tokens at 12,000 filtered (9,390 at hop 0), because it packs whole bundles and stops when the next does not fit.
  • Full context, 60.6, is below every filtered arm, all of which use 12,000 tokens or fewer against its 115,123.
  • 0.20 against 0.21, filtered: 0.20's best was 73.8 at hop 2, 12,000, tying plain RAG at 73.8.

Commentary

  • At 12,000 tokens the graph ties plain RAG. On its own set every hop is within one standard error, with 8,175 to 10,946 tokens against plain RAG's 11,901. Over the whole store hops 0 and 1 tie (-0.42, -0.40); hop 2 trails by 1.32 (se 0.63), and hybrid search alone by 1.53 (se 0.60).
  • At 2,000 tokens on its own set the graph beats plain RAG by about 2 points, and so does hybrid search over chunks alone (+2.44). There the gain is the search's.
  • At 2,000 tokens over the whole store the graph beats plain RAG by 1.6 (about 2.3 se) and hybrid search alone does not (-0.37). There the gain is the graph's, as on LongMemEval.
  • From own set to whole store, hybrid search over chunks falls 4.7 points at 2,000 tokens; the graph falls about 2. The question sets differ slightly (1,968 and 1,954).
  • 0.20, the graph without chunks (the retrieval 0.20 Novel run, 1,931 questions): hop 0 at 12,000 scored 61.14 against plain RAG's 64.12, -5.07 per question (se 0.68). With chunks as records, 0.21 closes that gap.

Set Whole store Own set, 2,000 Own set, 12,000

The graph's best setting never scores below plain RAG beyond its standard error. It gains most where the store is large and mixed, and where the material is a person's conversations; on one book set or one medical corpus it ties, usually with fewer tokens. Where hybrid search over chunks matches the graph (Novel own set, 2,000 tokens), the gain is the search's; where it does not (LongMemEval and Novel over the whole store), the gain is the graph's.
