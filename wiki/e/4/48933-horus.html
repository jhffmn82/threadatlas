Abstract written by a model

In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Horus appears in several forms, including a hawk-headed man, Horus the child, and the god of silence, and is worshipped at Edfoo. In Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa, Horus, Osiris’s son, ultimately defeats Typho after Typho killed and dismembered Osiris.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVII

Horus was the son of Osiris and appeared in several forms, including a hawk-headed man and Horus the child. He was the local deity of Edfoo, and in one form he was represented as the god of silence.
