Horus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Horus, son of Osiris, appears in several forms, including a hawk-headed man, Horus the child, and the god of silence, and is worshipped at Edfoo.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVII

Horus was the son of Osiris and appeared in several forms, including a hawk-headed man and Horus the child. He was the local deity of Edfoo, and in one form he was represented as the god of silence.
