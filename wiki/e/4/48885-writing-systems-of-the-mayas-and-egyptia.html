writing systems of the Mayas and Egyptians in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

The writing systems of the Mayas and Egyptians are described as combining phonetic, ideographic, and symbolic characters. They are read generally right to left in vertical columns and horizontal lines. The Mayas are said to use straight-lined signs enclosed in square or oblong figures, and their inscriptions are claimed to resemble Egyptian, Chaldee, and Etruscan characters.
