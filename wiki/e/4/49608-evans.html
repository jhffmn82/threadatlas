Evans in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Evans, an educated convict sent away by boat, joins a mutiny but refuses to sanction killings. He escapes, is rescued by the Hotspur, adopts a new name in Sydney, becomes a wealthy colonial with Beddoes, and later fears Hudson’s threats after recognizing him.

“‘One evening, about the third week after our start, the doctor

Evans participates in the mutiny but refuses, with seven others, to approve the cold-blooded killing of the surviving officers and warders. He escapes in a boat, is picked up by the brig Hotspur, changes his name in Sydney, and later becomes wealthy with Beddoes. He recognizes Hudson as the sailor rescued from the wreck and fears Hudson's threats.
