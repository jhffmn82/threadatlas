Mr. Melas in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

X. The Greek Interpreter

Mr. Melas is a Greek-born interpreter who is chiefly associated with the Greek language and works in courts and hotels. Mr. Latimer abducts him by carriage and forces him to question Kratides, but he secretly inserts questions that reveal details about Kratides’s identity and captivity.

“I cannot tell you the loathing and horror with which this

Mr. Melas is abducted for a second time by a small, bespectacled man who threatens him with a life-preserver. He is taken to The Myrtles, forced to interpret further threats against Paul Kratides, then struck unconscious and left bound in a room filled with charcoal fumes. Sherlock Holmes and the others rescue him, and he recovers with ammonia and brandy.
