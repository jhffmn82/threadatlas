reincarnation in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Reincarnation is believed in by Mayas and Egyptians. Maya belief describes souls returning to earth after suffering and bliss to animate bodies; workers removing the Chaacmol statue saw it as a warrior’s future body and the narrator as a disenchanted great man returned.

The text says that Maya belief held that souls returned to earth after periods of suffering and bliss. The workers who removed the Chaacmol statue believed that it would serve as the warrior’s future body, and they identified the narrator as a great man who had been disenchanted and returned.
