reincarnation

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: live again on earth, return to earth, transmigration

Abstract written by a model

Reincarnation is believed in by Mayas and Egyptians. Maya belief describes souls returning to earth after suffering and bliss to animate bodies; workers removing the Chaacmol statue saw it as a warrior’s future body and the narrator as a disenchanted great man returned.

reincarnation is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
