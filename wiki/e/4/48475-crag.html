Abstract written by a model

In LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering, CRAG is an advanced RAG baseline reproduced with LangGraph within LangChain; it evaluates chunks individually, misses connections, and struggles with complex multi-hop long-context questions. In Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, CRAG iteratively uses a small evaluator model and relies on GPT-3.5 for query rewriting.
