The Boolooroo's reign is supposed to last three hundred years, but Ghip-Ghisizzle suspects that the current Boolooroo has already ruled for three hundred years and claims it has lasted only two hundred. If so, the Boolooroo is five hundred years old and is trying to gain another hundred years of rule.
