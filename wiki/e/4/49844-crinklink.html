Crinklink in Little Wizard Stories of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913

Crinklink is a fictional, changeable-sized monster presented as a terrifying giant. He carries Dorothy and Toto across a black-water lake to his log castle, threatens punishment and dishwashing, and may enslave captives, before being revealed as the Wizard of Oz in disguise.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

LITTLE DOROTHY AND TOTO

Crinklink is presented as a terrifying giant who carries Dorothy and Toto across the black-water lake and takes them to his log castle. He threatens Dorothy with punishment and orders her to wash his dishes, but he is ultimately revealed not to exist. The supposed Crinklink was the Wizard of Oz in disguise.
