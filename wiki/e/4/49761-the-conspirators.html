the conspirators in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The conspirators, Harold Latimer and Wilson Kemp aided by a coachman and his wife, imprison Paul Kratides and attempt to coerce him through cruelty and starvation, hold Sophy at The Myrtles, abduct Mr. Melas as interpreter, then flee with Sophy after exposure.

The conspirators are Harold Latimer and Wilson Kemp, aided by the coachman and his wife, who serve as their tools. They imprison Paul Kratides, conceal his identity, abduct Mr. Melas to use as an interpreter, and flee with Sophy Kratides after their secret is discovered.
