QA performance in RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering

Zihan Zhang, Meng Fang, Ling Chen · 2024

QA performance generally improves as retrieval occurs more frequently on the evaluated questions. The unit also notes that effective use of retrieved context matters, as shown by GPT-4's high accuracy despite retrieving for only 67.6% of questions with Vanilla prompting. QA performance may be harmed when retrieved documents provide noisy context or lack the information needed to answer questions.

QA performance may be harmed when retrieved documents provide noisy context or lack the information needed to answer questions.
