the Liquid of Petrifaction

Also called: Liquid of Petrifaction, the Liquid, the bottle of Liquid of Petrifaction, the magic spell of the Liquid of Petrifaction, the powerful Liquid of Petrifaction

Abstract written by a model

The Liquid of Petrifaction is a liquid that turns whatever it touches into solid marble. Unlawfully kept on Dr. Pipt's shelf, it is accidentally knocked down and immediately petrifies Margolotte and Unc Nunkie. Their transformation prompts Ojo to search for materials to reverse its effect.

Chapter Five

The Liquid of Petrifaction is accidentally knocked from its shelf and spills on Margolotte and Unc Nunkie. It works immediately, turning both of them into stiff marble statues.

Chapter Sixteen

The Liquid of Petrifaction accidentally turns Unc Nunkie and Margolotte to marble. Ojo begins a search for the materials needed to reverse its effect.

Chapter Eighteen

The Liquid of Petrifaction was unlawfully kept by Dr. Pipt and caused the accident that left Margolotte and Unc Nunkie as marble statues.
