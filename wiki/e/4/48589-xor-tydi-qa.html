XOR-Tydi QA in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where XOR-Tydi QA first appears

Abstract written by a model

XOR-Tydi QA is a multilingual question answering dataset used in evaluation with MKQA. It contains 40K context-independent, information-seeking questions in seven languages and 3K validation questions. Questions have universal answers and are grounded in Wikipedia in the question’s language or English. Reported examples cover Arabic, Finnish, Japanese, Korean, and Russian; results find user-language Wikipedia retrieval more beneficial.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

XOR-TyDi QA is used for evaluation together with MKQA. The unit reports example counts and median question and answer lengths for Arabic, Finnish, Japanese, Korean, and Russian.

opening

XOR-TyDi QA comprises 40K information-seeking questions in seven languages, with 3K validation questions used in the experiments. It grounds questions in Wikipedia in the question's language or in English, and its questions are context-independent with universal answers.

Results and discussion .. opening

XOR TyDi QA is one of the datasets used in the multilingual evaluation. Because its questions are grounded in both English and user languages, retrieval from Wikipedia in the user language is more beneficial.
