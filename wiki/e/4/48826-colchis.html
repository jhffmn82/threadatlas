Also called: Aea, Aeaean, Colchian land, Colchians, Colchisque, Colchos, kingdom of Colchis, native land, the city, the city among the Colchians, the city of the Colchians, Κολχικῆς, Κόλχοις, Κόλχους
  • In Heroides and Amores, Colchis is Medea's native land and the realm of her father, Aeetes; Jason and the Greek crew arrive there seeking the golden fleece, and Medea later leaves Colchis with Jason.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Colchis is a kingdom ruled by Aeetes, containing Mars's temple and the Golden Fleece; Jason and the Argonauts arrive, take it, defeat pursuing Colchians, and depart.
  • In The Argonautica, Colchis is the land of Aeetes and the Colchians and the destination of the heroes’ quest for the golden fleece; the Argonauts arrive, encounter its people and customs, become involved with Medea and Chalciope as help is discussed for the stranger, and then depart by water with the fleece while Aeetes and the Colchians pursue them.
  • In Medea, Colchis is Aietes’ distant land and Medea’s homeland, Jason’s destination for the Golden Ram’s fleece and Phrixus’s soul, before Medea betrays and leaves it.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Colchis is Æetes’s land, a refuge for Phryxus and the destination of Jason and the Argonauts seeking the Golden Fleece; its people witness Jason’s labors, and with Medea’s aid Jason enters, succeeds, and removes Æetes’s treasures.
  • In The Library, Colchis is a realm of Aeetes containing the Phasis River and a grove of Ares where the Golden Fleece hangs from an oak, guarded by a sleepless dragon; the Argonauts arrive, Jason asks Aeetes for the fleece and is sent to obtain it, and later Medea comes there unknown after leaving Athens.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Colchis is a kingdom on the eastern shore of the Black Sea ruled by Aeetes, the place where Phryxus landed safely and where the Golden Fleece was kept; Jason and the Argonauts reached it after passing through the dangerous Symplegades.

Colchis in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Colchis is the land of Aeetes and the Colchians, the destination of the heroes’ quest for the golden fleece. The Argonauts arrive there, encounter its people and customs, and become involved with Medea and Chalciope as help is discussed for the stranger. Medea then flees her home, and the heroes depart Colchis by water with the fleece while Aeetes and the Colchians pursue them.
