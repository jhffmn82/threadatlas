war between the Pinkies and Blueskins in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The war begins when the Pinkies enter the Blue Country to fight the Blueskins, believing themselves braver and better armed. It ends after the Boolooroo's defeat, when the Blueskins peacefully receive the Pinkies and Trot calls for sociability and happiness.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Pinkies agree to fight the Blueskins, believing they are braver and better armed. The conflict begins with the Pinkies entering the Blue Country and preparing to march against the Blueskins.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The war ends with the defeat of the Boolooroo and the peaceful reception of the Pinkies by the Blueskins. Trot declares that everyone must become sociable and happy rather than continue fighting.
