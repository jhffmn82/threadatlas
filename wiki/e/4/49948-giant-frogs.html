THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The giant frogs form a barrier across the Fog Bank and carry the Pinkies on their backs. They leap together across the fog, transporting the army to the Blue Country.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The frogs appear to help carry the travelers across the Fog Bank, but they refuse to advance with the umbrella present. They flee and hide in the moist banks when the umbrella transforms into a huge elephant.
