Abstract written by a model

The cottage near Grant Munro's house is a two-storied, old-fashioned cottage with a porch and honeysuckle in Norbury. Empty for eight months, it gains new residents on a Monday: a tall hostile Northern woman, Lucy, and the nurse. Grant sees a pale, yellow, livid face at its upper window and finds Effie secretly visiting, while she begs Jack not to enter. Though it first seems deserted, its comfortable upper room holds Effie's photograph and reveals her hidden child and secret.
