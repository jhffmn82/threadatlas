Hea

deity · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: HEA OR HOA, Hoa, Oannes

Abstract written by a model

Hea or Hoa is a fish god, half man and half fish, linked with serpent and knowledge. Coming from the Persian Gulf, he teaches astronomy and letters to the first settlers on the Euphrates and Tigris; his name is interpreted as a house or residence on the water.

Hea is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
