Abstract written by a model

Alonzo Phelps, convicted of murder and sentenced to hang in Vicksburg, had committed his first murder in Europe and confessed to hiding Lasco family papers. On the day set for execution, he escaped jail, was pursued, and was shot dead by an officer.

Alonzo Phelps is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
