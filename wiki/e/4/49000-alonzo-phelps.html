Alonzo Phelps in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Alonzo Phelps, convicted of murder and sentenced to hang in Vicksburg, had committed his first murder in Europe and confessed to hiding Lasco family papers. On the day set for execution, he escaped jail, was pursued, and was shot dead by an officer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

Alonzo Phelps was sentenced to hang for murder in Vicksburg. He escaped from jail on the day of his scheduled execution but was pursued and shot dead by an officer of the law; the narrator learned that Phelps had written a confession.
