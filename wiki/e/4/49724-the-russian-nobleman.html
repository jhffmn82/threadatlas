the Russian nobleman in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

A supposed Russian nobleman living in England seeks Trevelyan’s help for longstanding catalepsy, arrives with his claimed son, vanishes during an apparent attack, then returns saying he awoke in a strange room and left dazed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX. The Resident Patient

The Russian nobleman is introduced through an undated letter as a patient seeking Trevelyan's expertise in catalepsy. He arrives with his supposed son, experiences an apparent cataleptic attack during the consultation, disappears while Trevelyan retrieves medicine, and returns the next evening with an explanation that he had awakened in a strange room and left in a dazed state.
