4.1 .. 4.2

The immediate answer is generated by feeding the question directly into a smaller model such as Mistral-7B. The student model is trained to revise incorrect predictions in this answer.

Limitations

The initial answer is generated in the first step of the framework and may be highly dependent on the task. For some tasks, it may not be meaningful or useful.
