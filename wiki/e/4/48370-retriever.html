retriever in Reducing hallucination in structured outputs via Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Patrice Béchard · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where retriever first appears

Abstract written by a model

Retriever is a small, well-trained retriever encoder and crucial ingredient of Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is trained on pairs of user queries and corresponding steps or tables, with negative sampling. During initialization, it builds an index. For a natural-language query, it encodes a unit-norm embedding, uses cosine similarity, and maps the query to existing step and database-table names, retrieving up to K related structured JSON objects. Its output becomes LLM input. Used with RAG in workflow generation, a very small retriever can enable a smaller Large Language Model without loss in performance, reducing deployment resources.

retriever encoder in Reducing hallucination in structured outputs via Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Patrice Béchard · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where retriever encoder first appears

Abstract written by a model

Retriever encoder is a fine-tuned model that aligns natural language with structured JSON objects and application-specific terminology, producing normalized fixed-length vectors for queries, steps, and tables. It is trained on about 15,000 step-name and 1,500 table-name pairs, then evaluated on Human Eval against off-the-shelf GTR-T5 models.

The retriever encoder is fine-tuned to align natural language with structured JSON objects and to represent application-specific terminology. It produces normalized fixed-length vectors for user queries, steps, and tables.

The retriever encoder is trained on approximately 15,000 step-name pairs and 1,500 table-name pairs derived from extracted and deterministic samples. Its quality is evaluated on Human Eval, and the study compares a fine-tuned encoder with off-the-shelf GTR-T5 models of different sizes.
