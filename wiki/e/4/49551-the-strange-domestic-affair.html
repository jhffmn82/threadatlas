III. The Yellow Face

The strange domestic affair concerns the sudden barrier between Grant Munro and Effie Munro after three years of happiness. Its known features include Effie's unexplained request for money, secret nighttime visits to the nearby cottage, apparent lies, and Grant's sighting of an unnatural face there.
