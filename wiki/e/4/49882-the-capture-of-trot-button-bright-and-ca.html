the capture of Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: You are my prisoners, capture, found themselves absolutely helpless, suddenness of her capture, the travelers' capture by the Blueskins

Abstract written by a model

The Blueskins capture Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright in a garden, creeping behind their bench and throwing thin, weighted blue cords around them and the Magic Umbrella. The cords pin each traveler's arms to their sides, leaving the three prisoners helpless under the Boolooroo and his people. After arranging for the Magic Umbrella to be secured, the Boolooroo releases them.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

The Blueskins capture Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright when thin blue cords shoot out and wind around the Magic Umbrella and each traveler. The cords pin their arms to their sides and leave them absolutely helpless.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

The three companions are seized suddenly in the garden by Blueskins who have crept behind their bench and thrown weighted cords around them. The Boolooroo later releases them after arranging for the Magic Umbrella to be secured.
