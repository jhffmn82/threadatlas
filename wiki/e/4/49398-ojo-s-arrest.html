Ojo's arrest

Abstract written by a model

Ojo's arrest is his detention at the Emerald City gate for allegedly breaking the Law of Oz by picking a six-leaved clover. Acting under Ozma of Oz's order, the Soldier with the Green Whiskers handcuffs Ojo, promises him a fair trial and an opportunity to prove innocence, and takes him to the Emerald City prison. His imprisonment is comfortable and nonviolent. The arrest leads to Ojo's trial and judgment before Ozma of Oz, with freedom possible if innocence is proved.

Ojo's arrest is in one work on this site, The Patchwork Girl of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

Ojo's arrest occurs at the Emerald City gate after the Soldier with the Green Whiskers receives Ozma's order to detain him. The Soldier says Ojo will receive a fair trial and a chance to prove his innocence, but first obeys the order and handcuffs him.

Chapter Fifteen

Ojo's arrest begins when the Soldier with the Green Whiskers takes him into custody and leads him to the Emerald City prison. His imprisonment is comfortable and nonviolent, and he is treated as an unfortunate person who should be helped to become strong and brave.

Chapter Sixteen

The six-leaved-clover arrest concerns Ojo's imprisonment by the Soldier with the Green Whiskers after he is accused of picking a six-leaved clover. Dorothy says Ozma of Oz ordered the arrest but agrees that Ojo should be freed if his innocence can be proved.
