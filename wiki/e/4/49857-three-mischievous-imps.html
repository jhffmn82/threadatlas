OZMA AND THE LITTLE WIZARD

The three Imps are small, lively creatures with big round ears, flat noses, wide grinning mouths, and black hair shaped like horns. They jeer at travelers, call them names, push them, throw stones, and later attack Ozma and the Wizard even after being transformed.
