the creation of the Powder of Life in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The creation of the Powder of Life is Dr. Pipt's nearly completed process for Margolotte's purpose. Over nearly six years, he stirs a compound in four kettles with both feet and hands. When the contents boil away, he and Margolotte collect, mix, cool, and lock the remaining grains in a gold bottle.

Chapter Two

Dr. Pipt's process takes several years and involves preparing a compound in four kettles. He is nearly finished with the new batch, which he is making for Margolotte's purpose.

Chapter Three

Dr. Pipt creates the Powder of Life by stirring four kettles for nearly six years. After the contents boil away, he and Margolotte collect the remaining grains, mix them in a golden dish, cool the powder, and place it in a locked gold bottle.
