Chapter Twenty-Seven

The Tin Woodman considers yellow butterflies beautiful and sensitive to pain, and he says that tearing off a wing would cause exquisite torture and death. He refuses to let anyone harm them, and the Winkie Country is the only place in Oz where they can be found.
