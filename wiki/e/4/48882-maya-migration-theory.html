Maya migration theory

Abstract written by a model

proposes_migration_from American continent [westward across the Pacific toward Asia and Egypt] The Maya migration theory proposes that the Mayas or mound builders originated in the American continent and traveled westward across the Pacific through Asia toward Egypt. It is supported in the text by alleged similarities in architecture, customs, religious beliefs, personal appearance, language, place-names, and traditions.

Maya migration theory is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
