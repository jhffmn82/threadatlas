L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Patchwork Girl's brains are stored and sewn inside her head before animation. Margolotte chooses Obedience, Amiability, Truth, and Cleverness; Ojo secretly adds powders granting many other qualities.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

Margolotte initially selects Obedience, Amiability, and Truth for the Patchwork Girl's brains and considers adding a little Cleverness. Ojo secretly adds some powder from every labeled bottle on the Brain Furniture shelf, including qualities such as Judgment, Courage, Ingenuity, Learning, Poesy, and Self Reliance, before Margolotte adds Cleverness.
