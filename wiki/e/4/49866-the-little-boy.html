the little boy in Little Wizard Stories of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913

The little boy is a lost, hungry child in the western forest with the little girl. After they eat squirrels' stored nuts, squirrels bind him to a tree, but he explains their hunger and is returned to his friends.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

JACK PUMPKINHEAD AND THE SAWHORSE

The little boy becomes lost in the western forest with the little girl and is bound to a tree by the squirrels after eating nuts from their storehouse. He explains that they ate the nuts because they were hungry, and he is later returned to his friends.
