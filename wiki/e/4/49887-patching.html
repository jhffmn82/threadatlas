patching

Abstract written by a model

Patching is the Boolooroo’s severe punishment in Sky Island and the Blue Country. Using the Great Knife or slicing machine, prisoners are cut in two and mismatched halves are joined, causing the resulting person to lose individuality and potentially have conflicting desires. The Boolooroo threatens Ghip-Ghisizzle, guards, and strangers with patching, including a proposed head-rearranging variant. Ghip-Ghisizzle is to be joined with Tiggle but is rescued before it happens. After his escape, Cap’n Bill is scheduled for patching with Tiggle, and later the Boolooroo plans to join him to the billygoat. Trot prevents these punishments, turns the threat back on the Boolooroo, and finally forbids any further patching, choosing exile and loss of status instead.

patching in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912 · the work’s page · read it where patching first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

Patching involves slicing two people in half and joining mismatched halves together. It is said to be a severe punishment because the resulting person loses individuality and may have conflicting desires with the other half.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

Patching is a punishment used by the Boolooroo against those who displease him. The Boolooroo threatens Ghip-Ghisizzle and the guards with patching, and he threatens to use a new form of punishment involving chopping off and rearranging the strangers' heads.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

Patching is the operation by which prisoners are sliced in two with the Great Knife and their halves are joined to other prisoners. Ghip-Ghisizzle is scheduled to be patched with Tiggle, but the operation does not occur before the Princesses rescue him.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

The patching is scheduled for the following morning after breakfast. The Boolooroo intends to patch Cap'n Bill to Tiggle because Ghip-Ghisizzle has escaped.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

Patching is the Boolooroo's punishment of cutting a person in two with the Great Knife, sometimes with the stated intention of joining that person to another creature. The Boolooroo intends to patch Cap'n Bill to the billygoat, but Trot prevents it and instead helps place the Boolooroo in the frame for the same punishment.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Trot forbids any more patching in the Blue Country, rejecting Ghip-Ghisizzle's request to patch the Six Snubnosed Princesses and the former Boolooroo. She decides that exile and loss of status are sufficient punishments.
