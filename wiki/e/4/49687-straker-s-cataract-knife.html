Abstract written by a model

Straker’s cataract knife is a delicate, inflexible surgical instrument made by Weiss & Co., London. John Straker picked it up from his dressing-table, cork-protected its tip, and carried it on a rough expedition; it was later found bloodstained in his hand after his death. Though an unusual weapon, Holmes concludes Straker meant to use it to nick Silver Blaze’s tendons beneath the skin and lame the horse.
