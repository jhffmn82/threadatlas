Abstract written by a model

The burglar is a middle-sized man in dark clothing, described as peculiar and experienced. He allegedly forced entry into Cunningham’s house, struggled with and shot William Kirwan, then escaped across the garden and hedge. Suspected in the Acton burglary, he takes stolen odds and ends, including a ball of string and a letter-weight; Holmes questions the account of his entry.

VII. The Reigate Squires

The burglar forced entry at Cunningham's house, struggled with William Kirwan, shot him, and escaped across the garden and over a hedge. He was described as a middle-sized man wearing dark clothing, and he is suspected of also committing the Acton burglary.

We passed the pretty cottage where the murdered man had lived,

The burglar is described as an unusual and experienced criminal who allegedly forced the Cunningham house door. Holmes notes that such a burglar might have taken odd objects, as in the earlier burglary at Acton’s, and questions whether he entered the house before William Kirwan arrived.
