the rope (Blessington's bedroom) in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

A large coil of escape rope kept under Blessington’s bed for fire emergencies. When Blessington was murdered, the killers cut rope from it and used it to hang him, avoiding the need to assemble separate gallows.

The rope used to hang Blessington was cut from a large coil kept under his bed. Dr. Trevelyan says Blessington kept the coil nearby so he could escape through the window if the stairs caught fire, and Holmes infers that the murderers used it instead of assembling a separate gallows.
