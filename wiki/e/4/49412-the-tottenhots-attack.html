the Tottenhots' attack in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Tottenhots attack the adventurers by roughly tossing the Scarecrow and Patchwork Girl and trying to toss Ojo, until Dorothy and Toto stop them and they retreat into circular houses.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

The Tottenhots' attack consists of tossing the Scarecrow and the Patchwork Girl through the air and attempting to toss Ojo. Dorothy and Toto stop the attack, after which the Tottenhots retreat into their circular houses.
