fire

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Fire, Hot splendour of the shaft of God, Lightning's eye, fire that sleeps, flame, flame from Semele's enhallowed sod, shaft of God, the fire from heaven, the fire on Semele's tomb

Abstract written by a model

In Hippolytus and The Bacchae, it is identified with the blaze that came upon Semele, rising from her tomb and burning against Pentheus's house when Dionysus calls upon it. (Hippolytus; The Bacchae) In The Bacchae of Euripides, it leaps up on Semele's tomb at the Voice's command and is recognized as the splendour of the heavenly shaft that caused Semele's death.
