The training setup for GenGround includes instructional grounding distillation of Mistral-7B on 50,000 synthetic examples. Training uses DeepSpeed ZeRO, a learning rate of 5e-5, weight decay of 0.01, and three NVIDIA A100-PCIE-80GB GPUs for 18 hours.
