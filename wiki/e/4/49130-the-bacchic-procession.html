Abstract written by a model

The Bacchic procession, led by Dionysus toward the hills, carries Theban women from homes and looms; imagined as joyful, music-filled ecstatic dancing, it moves from mountains into streets, towns, and towers, summoning Dionysus.

CHORUS

The Bacchic procession is imagined as a joyful movement of maidens from the mountains into streets, towns, and towers, accompanied by timbrels, drums, pipes, Phrygian music, and cries of 'Come, O ye Bacchae.' Its participants follow Dionysus in ecstatic dancing and summon the god into the city.
