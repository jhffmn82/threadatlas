Abstract written by a model

The Tottenhots are tiny, dusky, scarlet-haired people who live in circular pot-like houses, avoid sunlight, and play at night. Mischievous and fun-loving, they toss the Scarecrow and Patchwork Girl and restrain Ojo, then release the travelers to play outside before asking them to leave at daylight.

Chapter Nineteen

The Tottenhots are tiny, dusky people with brilliant scarlet hair who dislike daylight and play at night. They initially toss the Scarecrow and the Patchwork Girl and restrain Ojo, but agree to leave the travelers alone in exchange for being allowed to play outside. At daylight, one Tottenhot asks the travelers to leave his house.
