Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

The Tottenhots are tiny, dusky people with brilliant scarlet hair who dislike daylight and play at night. They initially toss the Scarecrow and the Patchwork Girl and restrain Ojo, but agree to leave the travelers alone in exchange for being allowed to play outside. At daylight, one Tottenhot asks the travelers to leave his house.
