monstrous plants in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Monstrous plants grow in rows beside the yellow brick road, twice as high as the Patchwork Girl’s head, with swaying broad leaves that change color and bend down to fold around, capture, and imprison travelers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

The monstrous plants grow in rows along both sides of the yellow brick road and have tall, broad leaves that continually sway without wind. Their leaves display changing colors and can bend down to seize and imprison travelers.
