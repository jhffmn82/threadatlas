THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Sunset Tribe lives in the western half of the Pink Country and claims importance because the sun comes to it after leaving the Sunrise Tribe. Its members gather to watch the setting of the sun, and six of them, including Coralie, sit on the Council.
