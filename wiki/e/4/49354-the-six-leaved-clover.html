the six-leaved clover in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The six-leaved clover is a very scarce tiny green plant, a clover with six spreading leaves, found in the green country and fields around the Emerald City. Though neglected and trampled under foot, it is protected by the Law of Oz: picking it is forbidden without Ozma’s consent. It is one of five required things for the restoring compound, needed to restore the petrified victims and to restore Unc Nunkie to life. Ojo searches for it, discovers one beside the road of yellow bricks, plucks it, and hides it in his basket. Suspected of breaking the law, he gives it to Scraps, who hides it in a golden vase so it cannot prove his offense. Before the court it grows from the vase, and Ozma forgives Ojo and permits him to keep it.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

A six-leaved clover is required for the restoring compound. Dr. Pipt says it can be found in the green country around the Emerald City and is very scarce there.

Chapter Eleven

The six-leaved clover is one of the five things required by the Crooked Magician. The Shaggy Man says it may be found in the fields around the Emerald City, although a law forbids picking it.

Chapter Fourteen

Ojo discovers a six-leaved clover beside the road of yellow bricks after searching for one. Believing it may help restore Unc Nunkie to life, he plucks it and hides it in his basket.

Chapter Fifteen

The six-leaved clover is the small green plant Ojo picked, thereby breaking a Law of Oz. Ojo thinks it is harmless because it grows neglected and is trampled underfoot.

Chapter Sixteen

The six-leaved clover is forbidden by the Law of Oz. Ojo is suspected of picking one, and Scraps removes it from his basket and hides it in a golden vase so it cannot be used as proof against him.

Chapter Eighteen

Ojo picked the six-leaved clover despite knowing it was forbidden and gave it to Scraps, who hid it in a golden vase. The clover grows from the vase before the court, and Ozma allows Ojo to take and keep it after forgiving his offense.
