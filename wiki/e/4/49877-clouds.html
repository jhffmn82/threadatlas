THE ISLAND IN THE SKY

The travelers pass through a thick black rain cloud, a white misty cloud bank, and clouds shaped like ships, trees, castles, and palaces. Trot and Button-Bright also see fanciful forms resembling dragons and giants before the cloudland gives way to clear blue sky.
