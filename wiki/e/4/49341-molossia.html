Molossia

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Molossia’s land, Μολοσσίᾳ

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, Molossia is the land where Thetis orders Andromache and her young son to live, and whose future kings are descended from the son. In The Library, Molossia is the country to which Neoptolemus traveled with Helenus, where Helenus founded a city and Neoptolemus ruled the Molossians as king.
