Abstract written by a model

Arthur Harry Pinner is a dark-haired, dark-eyed, black-bearded financial agent who presents himself as the managing director of the Franco-Midland Hardware Company, Limited. In London he recruits Hall Pycroft as business manager, pays him a £100 advance, obtains an agreement, and sends him to Birmingham to meet a supposed brother, Harry Pinner. He resembles Harry in figure, voice, and distinctive gold-filled tooth, though his hair and beard differ; he is also Beddington’s brother. At the temporary offices, terrified by a newspaper report, he tries to hang himself with his braces but is rescued by Sherlock Holmes, Dr. John Watson, and Pycroft.

IV. The Stockbroker’s Clerk

Arthur Pinner visits Hall Pycroft in London and praises his financial ability, offering him the position of business manager of the Franco-Midland Hardware Company, Limited, at a salary of £500 plus commission. He gives Pycroft a £100 advance, obtains a written agreement, and directs him to meet his supposed brother in Birmingham.

Arthur Harry Pinner is found in a state of extreme terror at the temporary company offices and asks to be left alone. He enters an inner room and attempts to hang himself with his braces, but Sherlock Holmes, Dr. John Watson, and Hall Pycroft rescue him; he later explains that the newspaper report is the reason for his action.
