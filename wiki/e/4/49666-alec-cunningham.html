Abstract written by a model

Alec Cunningham, Mr. Cunningham’s son, is a young man living in the Cunningham house and the ringleader in a plot involving William Kirwan. He says he heard Kirwan call for help, ran downstairs, and saw a shooting outside the open back door. He later denies that clues exist and disputes Holmes’s theory while joining his father in the search. He is identified as the younger writer of a note and likely instigator of the burglary and killing, having lied, torn the note from Kirwan’s hand, and hidden it. When exposed, he attacks Holmes and tries to cock a revolver before being disarmed.

Alec Cunningham is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VII. The Reigate Squires

Alec Cunningham was smoking in his dressing-gown when he heard William Kirwan calling for help. He ran downstairs, saw two men wrestling outside the open back door, and witnessed one of them shoot the other before fleeing.

We passed the pretty cottage where the murdered man had lived,

Alec Cunningham claims that there is no clue to the crime and argues against Holmes’s theory that the burglar entered the house before William Kirwan was attacked. He accompanies his father during the search and attacks Holmes when Holmes is discovered. After Holmes’s accusation, Alec displays apparent ferocity and attempts to cock a revolver before the Inspector disarms him.
