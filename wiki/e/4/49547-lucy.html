Lucy in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Lucy, Effie and John Hebron’s biological daughter, is a little girl of African descent, cared for by a former servant in a Norbury cottage. Her face is concealed from neighbours because of her racial identity. She later dies of yellow fever in Atlanta.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Lucy is Effie’s daughter by John Hebron and has been living in the cottage under the care of a former servant. She is a little black girl dressed in a red frock and white gloves, and her face has been covered to conceal her racial identity from the neighbours.
