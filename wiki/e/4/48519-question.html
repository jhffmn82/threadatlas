A single-hop question in the NQ dataset, formulated by GenGround as a simpler subquestion of a multi-hop question. It has high quality, is paired with ground-truth and noise documents, receives a directly generated immediate answer, and includes a detailed revision trajectory before grounding.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

A single-hop question is formulated by GenGround as a simpler subquestion of a multi-hop question. The model directly generates an answer to it before grounding the question-answer pair in retrieved documents.

4.1 .. 4.2

Each question is paired with a corresponding ground-truth document and noise documents. It is supplemented with an immediate answer and a detailed revision trajectory.
