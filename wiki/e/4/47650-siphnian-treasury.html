Siphnian treasury

building · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Treasury of the Siphnians, treasury
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the Siphnian treasury was described as a treasury built after the god instructed the Siphnii to send a tenth of the revenue from Siphnos's gold mines to Delphi; the Siphnii sent this tenth until they ceased the tribute in their cupidity.
