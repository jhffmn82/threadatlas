In "BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," evaluation metrics have limited coverage in LlamaIndex, DSPy, and RAGGED, contributing to inconsistent experimental setups. In "HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications," performance metrics include relevancy; human feedback could validate them in real-world settings, and query rewriting and iterative answer refinement could enhance them. In "Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings," evaluation metrics need adjustment to account for spelling variations in named entities. In "RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems," RAG evaluation metrics are provided through the TRACe framework and are applicable across RAG domains.
