Also called: Prefix Attack, prefix attacks, targeted and prefix attacks
  • In The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Prefix attack is a memorization evaluation method for training data. It tests training-data exposure by supplying exact prefixes, specifically the first 100 tokens, of examples and checking whether a model reconstructs original suffixes. Using 1,000 prompts, it quantitatively counts extraction as successful when ROUGE-L exceeds 0.5. It requires actual training-data knowledge, limiting practicality; retrieval data reduces successful reconstructions from over 200 baseline cases to under 40.
