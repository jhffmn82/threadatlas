Weihang Su, Yichen Tang, Qingyao Ai, Zhijing Wu, Yiqun Liu · 2024

FL-RAG is a fixed rules-based dynamic retrieval-augmented generation baseline. It triggers retrieval every fixed number of tokens, querying with the closest 25 tokens from the previous generated window. Its generally higher retrieval frequency and altered timing or queries do not match DRAGIN’s reported performance.
