Abstract written by a model

Multi-source Knowledge-grounded Dialogue System generates dialogue from plain text, tables, and knowledge graphs, using MSKE-Dialog and an encoder-decoder framework with varied attention weights; unlike M-RAG, it focuses specifically on dialogue generation.
