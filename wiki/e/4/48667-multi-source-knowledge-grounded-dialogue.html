Related Work

Multi-source Knowledge-grounded Dialogue System uses multi-source heterogeneous data, including plain text, tables, and knowledge graphs, for dialogue generation. It employs an encoder-decoder framework with varied attention weights and focuses specifically on dialogue generation, unlike M-RAG.
