the burial of Hercules’ children

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Inter, bury, covered them, funereal pomp, pious office
  • In Hecuba and other plays: Amphitryon is instructed to consign the children’s bodies to the tomb and shed the required tears. Hercules says that their mother should embrace them in death, while he will later return to take Amphitryon to Athens.
