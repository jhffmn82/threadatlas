Abstract written by a model

Witchcraft is widely believed in by Mayas and their descendants from remote antiquity. Belief leads some, such as a sick man at X-Kanchacan, to attribute illness to spells and reject treatment; in Equatorial Africa, sorcery belief produces fear, suspicion, illness, and many murders.
