Retrieval performance depends strongly on knowledge-base size: CLIP performs much better on InfoSeek's 100k-item collection than on Encyclopedic-VQA's 2M-item collection. Oracle entity retrieval substantially outperforms CLIP-based retrieval, demonstrating that entity identification is a major bottleneck.
