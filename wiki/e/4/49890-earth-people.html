Earth people in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Earth travelers, wearing Boolooroo uniforms, are enslaved by Blueskins in Blue Country but escape through the Fog Bank. In Pink Country, Pinkies capture them as suspected enemies. After they tell the truth about their adventures, Tourmaline and the Counselors judge them; despite support for honoring them, the order is to throw them into the sky.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE PINK COUNTRY

The travelers emerge safely from the Fog Bank into the Pink Country and rest on the pink grass. The Pinkies treat them as possible enemies, escort them through the Pink City under guard, and take them toward Tourmaline for judgment.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The strangers are judged by Tourmaline and the Counselors after arriving in the Pink Country. Although six Counselors favor treating them as honored guests, the official order is to throw them over the bushes into the sky.

Earth people in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Earth people are strangers in the Pink Country: Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill, whose truth Rosalie confirms before concluding fairies protect them unknowingly.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The Earth people are identified as Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill. Rosalie's vision confirms that they have told the truth, and she concludes that they are protected by fairies even though they do not know it themselves.
