Aunt Katy Demitt

Abstract written by a model

Aunt Katy Demitt is a nearest neighbor and first benefactress of the Fairfield sisters, an economical, industrious woman in Port William. After learning of the children’s plight, she orders Joe and Dick to bring them from the cabin, shelters them, claims eleven-year-old Suza in the settlers’ council, and becomes Suza’s foster mother. She supports their household by knitting stockings, keeps private savings and a will, and later reveals that Demitt relations sought Suza’s removal. She confirms that she has bequeathed her possessions to Suza.

Aunt Katy Demitt is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
