capture by the monstrous plants in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Monstrous plants capture Ojo, Scraps, Glass Cat, and Woozy by enclosing them in leaves, holding them in darkness until the Shaggy Man intervenes and enables their escape.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

The monstrous plants capture Scraps, the Glass Cat, the Woozy, and Ojo by folding their leaves around them. The captives are held in darkness and cannot escape until the Shaggy Man intervenes.
