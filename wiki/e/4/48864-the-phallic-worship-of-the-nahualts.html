the phallic worship of the Nahualts

Abstract written by a model

The phallic worship of the Nahualts is an ancient worship whose remnants were first discovered at Uxmal; its symbols are frequent in northern Yucatan and Nahualt-influenced regions, but absent from Chichen-Itza.
