SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

The federal army invades Missouri and is placed under the command of General Lyon. It fights Price’s forces at Springfield and Wilson’s Creek, retreats after Lyon’s death, and later occupies the fortified position at Lexington before its capture.

The federals had fortified Helena, a point three hundred miles above Vicks burg

The federals fortified Helena on the west bank of the river, three hundred miles above Vicksburg. They had about four thousand men, three forts, and a gunboat in the river.
