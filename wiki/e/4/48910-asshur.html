Asshur in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Asshur is the supreme god of the Assyrians, called “my lord” when his name is pronounced. Interpreted through the Maya form Axul as “thy end,” Asshur is associated with the beginning and end of all things.
