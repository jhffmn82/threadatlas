Asshur

Abstract written by a model

Asshur is the supreme god of the Assyrians, called “my lord” when his name is pronounced. Interpreted through the Maya form Axul as “thy end,” Asshur is associated with the beginning and end of all things.

Asshur is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
