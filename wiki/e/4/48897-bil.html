Abstract written by a model

Bil, said to mean Lord and associated with NIPRU, is Maya-interpreted as way or road, and therefore as origin, father, and procreator.

Bil is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
