InfoSeek

dataset · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

InfoSeek is a dataset and benchmark for knowledge-based visual question answering and evaluating MLLMs with external data. It contains 1.3M image-question-answer triplets on about 11k entities, derives images from OVEN, uses Wikidata-filled templates, and evaluates retrieval on validation data.

InfoSeek is in one work on this site, Wiki-LLaVA: Hierarchical Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multimodal LLMs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

4. Experiments / 4.1. Datasets .. 4.5. Limitations and Future Works

InfoSeek contains 1.3M image-question-answer triplets concerning around 11k entities, with evaluation performed on its validation split because test answers and entities were unavailable. Its experiments use a 100k-entity knowledge-base subset and show stronger retrieval and larger gains from retrieved context than Encyclopedic-VQA.
