The Horners' radium mine lies under their mountain, where they spend their time digging radium. Diksey says that a dark well is located in his radium mine, and he leads the group there to investigate.

The mine is reached through a dark, slanting hole in the great cave. Its smooth tunnel leads downward to a vast cave containing the dark well.
