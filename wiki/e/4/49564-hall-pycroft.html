Hall Pycroft in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Hall Pycroft is a young stockbroker’s clerk and accountant with five years at Coxon & Woodhouse, whose failure leaves him among twenty-seven dismissed clerks. While unemployed, he follows market quotations and secures a £200 position at Mawson & Williams. Arthur Pinner then lures him to the purported Franco-Midland Hardware Company with a higher-paying business-manager role, a £100 advance, and orders to report in Birmingham. There Pycroft meets the supposed Harry Pinner and grows suspicious. He brings Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson to Arthur Harry Pinner, discovers that his handwriting enabled another man to impersonate him at Mawson & Williams, and is sent to contact the police after the robbery is revealed.

IV. The Stockbroker’s Clerk

Hall Pycroft is a former clerk of Coxon & Woodhouse who is offered a position at Mawson & Williams after the collapse of his former firm. He is persuaded by Arthur Pinner to accept a much higher-paying position with the Franco-Midland Hardware Company, Limited, receives £100, and is sent to Birmingham, where he becomes suspicious after meeting the supposed Harry Pinner.
