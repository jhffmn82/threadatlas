Ake monument

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: structure, three immense superposed platforms

Abstract written by a model

The Ake monument consists of three immense superposed platforms supporting a structure with thirty-six katun columns. The columns record 5,760 years from the laying of the first stone to the final capping stone, and it may have been completed around the Spanish conquest.

Ake monument is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
