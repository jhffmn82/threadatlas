Thor in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Thor, Odin's eldest son, is a god, thunderer, and the strongest of gods and men. Bearing Miolnir, a strength belt, and iron gloves, he destroys a mountain giant artificer. Traveling with Thialfi and Loki, he confronts giants, is deceived by Utgard-Loki's illusions yet proves astonishingly strong, and finally kills Hrungnir with his mallet.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

Thor is Odin's eldest son, the strongest of gods and men, and the thunderer. He possesses Miolnir, the belt of strength, and iron gloves, and he destroys the mountain giant artificer after the artificer fails to complete the gods' fortress.

Chapter XXXII

Thor travels with Thialfi and Loki toward the giants' country. He strikes Skrymir three times with his mallet without defeating him, then fails Utgard-Loki's contests involving drinking, lifting a cat, and wrestling Elli, though Utgard-Loki reveals that Thor's feats were astonishing because they were tested against illusions. Later, Thor kills the giant Hrungnir with his mallet.
