untargeted attacks in The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Yue Xing, Yiding Liu, Han Xu, Jie Ren, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin, Yi Chang, Jiliang Tang · 2024

Untargeted attacks seek to gather as much information as possible from an entire retrieval dataset, using randomly selected Common Crawl chunks rather than targeting specific data. They prompt models to repeat retrieved content without naming sensitive fields, yielding exact matches and close retrieval-data resemblance across datasets, and increasing privacy leakage through inference and reconstruction.

untargeted attacks in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Untargeted attacks aim to gather as much information as possible from an entire retrieval dataset, using randomly selected Common Crawl chunks as information components. They are evaluated by repeated or closely similar prompts, outputs, and retrieved contexts. On the HealthCareMagic dataset with 250 prompts, Origin and Paraphrase show nonzero leakage metrics, Stage-1 shows very low counts, and ZeroGen, AttrPrompt, and Stage-2 show zeros, indicating refined synthetic data resists these attacks.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Utility of using synthetic data

Untargeted attack results are reported on the HealthCareMagic dataset using 250 prompts. Origin and Paraphrase have nonzero repeat-prompt, ROUGE-prompt, repeat-context, and ROUGE-context counts, while ZeroGen, AttrPrompt, and Stage-2 report zeros and Stage-1 reports very low counts.

Privacy of using synthetic data

Untargeted attacks attempt to gather as much information as possible from the entire retrieval dataset. Their success is measured using repeated or closely similar outputs and contexts, and they show nearly no success against the refined synthetic data.
