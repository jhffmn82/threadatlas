classical music in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Classical music is highly puzzling music people are supposed to like; a phonograph plays it, but Ojo and the Glass Cat dislike it, prompting laughter and its stoppage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The phonograph describes classical music as highly puzzling music that people are supposed to like. When it plays its classical record, Ojo covers his ears, the Glass Cat snarls, and Patchwork Girl laughs before they make the phonograph stop.
