Chiss in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Chiss is an overgrown giant porcupine in Oz, regarded as an evil spirit because sharp arrowlike quills cover its body and it throws them in any direction. Dangerous to road travelers and said to resist ordinary killing, it attacks the travelers until the Shaggy Man imprisons it and Ojo and Scraps remove all its quills.

Chapter Twelve

Chiss is a giant porcupine regarded in Oz as an evil spirit because it can throw its quills in any direction. After attacking the travelers, it is held prisoner by the Shaggy Man while Ojo and Scraps collect all its quills, leaving it unable to hurt anyone.
