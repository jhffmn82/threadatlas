Abstract written by a model

Chiss is an overgrown giant porcupine in Oz, regarded as an evil spirit because sharp arrowlike quills cover its body and it throws them in any direction. Dangerous to road travelers and said to resist ordinary killing, it attacks the travelers until the Shaggy Man imprisons it and Ojo and Scraps remove all its quills.

Chiss is in one work on this site, The Patchwork Girl of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
