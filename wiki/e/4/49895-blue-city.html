Blue City in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Blue City is a blue city in Blue Country, inhabited by Blueskins and extending a half mile in every direction from its center. Broad blue-marble streets run among blue buildings, castles, shops, spires, and turrets. It contains the Royal Palace, the Boolooroo's palace, and the Room of the Great Knife; its city wall surrounds it, connects with the palace-garden wall, and has a principal gate. Outer walls protect it against the country beyond. At first, Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill tour the city with Ghip-Ghisizzle. Later, fugitives find it deserted, but an alarm brings lights, shouts, angry citizens, guards, and pursuers. As the Pinkies approach, Blueskins rush inside, the Boolooroo closes and locks the gates, and Cap'n Bill's army halts outside the wall. The Blueskins capture Cap'n Bill within the city, while Trot secretly climbs over the wall. At night the city is quiet and blue-lit but joins the pursuit of Ghip-Ghisizzle; finally it wakes as Cap'n Bill tries to escape and soldiers pursue him along the wall, while Ghip-Ghisizzle plans an attack on it.

Sky Island

The Blue City is named in the contents as the subject of chapter 8.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

The Blue City is the city outside the Royal Palace. Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill had not seen it during their rapid descent, but they begin touring it with Ghip-Ghisizzle at the end of the unit.

THE BLUE CITY

The Blue City is extensive, with broad blue-marble streets, blue buildings, castles, shops, spires, and turrets. It extends a half mile in all directions from its center and is surrounded by outer country extending another half mile.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Blue city is deserted when the fugitives run through it, but it soon fills with lights, shouts, and angry activity after the alarm. Its citizens and pursuers are presented as a danger to the fugitives.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

Several Blueskins rush toward the Blue City when the Pinkies arrive. Cap'n Bill intends to march the Pinkies to the Blue City and ask the Blueskins to surrender.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Pinkies march toward the City, prompting the Boolooroo to close and lock all its gates. Cap'n Bill's army reaches the City wall but must halt until it can find a way inside.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

The Blue City is enclosed by a wall and gate, and the Blueskins lock themselves inside it after the Pinkies arrive. The Blueskins capture Cap'n Bill within the City, which Trot secretly enters by climbing over its wall.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

The City is quiet and blue-lit at night, with guards and residents joining the pursuit of Ghip-Ghisizzle. Its wall connects with the wall around the palace gardens.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Blue City begins waking as Cap'n Bill attempts to escape, and its soldiers pursue him along the wall. The Blue City is the intended target of Ghip-Ghisizzle's planned attack.
