The return of Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill to Earth is planned after they leave the Pink Country. Although the recovered Magic Umbrella allows them to go home whenever they wish, Trot delays until fulfilling duties in Sky Island and helping the Pinkies, then announces their departure after arranging a new Queen.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The recovered Magic Umbrella makes it possible for Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill to return home whenever they wish. Trot postpones the return until she has fulfilled her duties in Sky Island and helped the Pinkies get home.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Trot announces that she and her friends will leave the Pink Country and return to Earth. She tells the Pinkies that they may gather after dinner to watch the group fly away through the sky.
