Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Three

Radium is a translucent, frosted-silver metal that gives off a soft light and is used by the Horners to decorate their homes and furniture. The Horners have more radium than they can use, and Chief Horner says it is also a medicine that prevents sickness for those living near it.
