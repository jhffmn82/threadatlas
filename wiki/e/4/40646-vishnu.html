Vishnu in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Vishnu personifies preservation as one power of the supreme deity, protects the world through Avatars including Krishna, and will as Kalki destroy vice and restore virtue at the present age’s end.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXX

Vishnu is the preserving principle in the Hindu triad and protects the world through successive incarnations. His most celebrated human form is Krishna, and his tenth Avatar, Kalki, is expected to destroy vice and restore virtue at the end of the present age.
