Egyptian calendar

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: sideral or sothic year, solar year

Abstract written by a model

The Egyptian solar year is described as having twelve months of thirty days, followed by five additional days called Epact. These five days were considered unlucky, and the Egyptians are also said to have used a sideral or sothic year of 365 days and 6 hours.
