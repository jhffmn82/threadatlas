fine-tuning

Also called: LLaVA fine-tuning, MLLM fine-tuning, fine-tuning phase

Abstract written by a model

Fine-tuning is a classical approach that augments multimodal large language models by training on natural-language question–answer pairs requiring external knowledge, so models exploit retrieved passages. It uses low-rank adapters to reduce trainable parameters and is performed separately for two datasets with total batch size 512. It can degrade LLaVA-1.5 benchmarks; adding LLaVA-Instruct samples improves InfoSeek and better preserves benchmark performance.
