Abstract written by a model

Pink City is a city in Pink Country, enclosed by a strong high pink marble wall and entered through pink metal bars. Its circular street winds past round domed houses toward central Tourmaline, alongside the Pinkies' humble, uncomfortable palace and Court of the Statues.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Pink City is surrounded by a high wall of pink marble and entered through a gate of pink metal bars. It contains round, domed houses and one circular street that winds toward the center, where Tourmaline lives.
