Hecuba’s accusation of Helen

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the foul deeds, thy adulterous wife

Abstract written by a model

Hecuba’s accusation states that Helen willingly chose Paris because of his beauty and Troy’s wealth, remained in Paris’s household by choice, and followed Fortune rather than virtue. Hecuba says that Helen refused repeated advice to flee and shows no remorse for the war.
