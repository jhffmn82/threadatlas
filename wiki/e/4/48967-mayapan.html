Also called: ancient city of Mayapan

Abstract written by a model

Mayapan is a ruined site near three or four aguadas, where Augustus Le Plongeon found a gnomon, presented as evidence of Maya mathematical and astronomical knowledge.

Mayapan is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
