Introduction

The LongRAG paper describes a fine-tuning data construction pipeline and a multi-task training strategy using multi-length long-context data. It reports that LongRAG significantly surpassed long-context LLMs, mainstream advanced RAG, and Vanilla RAG, and states that the approach has robustness and transferability across different fine-tuned LLMs and domains.
