1 section; each heading opens the text

“We examined the room also. There is no possibility of a secret

The room containing Phelps’s desk was examined for a secret door, windows, a trap-door, a fireplace, and other traces of an intruder. No physical clue was found, and Phelps maintained that the thief could only have entered through the door.
