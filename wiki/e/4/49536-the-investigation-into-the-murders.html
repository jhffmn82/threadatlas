the investigation into the murders in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

A police investigation assisted by Sherlock Holmes begins with physical evidence in a packet and research for Holmes’s ear analysis. Holmes traces links to the Cushing sisters and the Browners through inquiries by Lestrade and Algar. Lestrade arrests James Browner aboard the S.S. May Day, where Browner voluntarily confesses.

The investigation proceeds from physical evidence in the packet to information about the Cushing sisters and the Browners. Holmes sends inquiries through Lestrade and Algar of the Liverpool force, and expects the remaining details to be established when Browner arrives.
