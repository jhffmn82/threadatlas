Maya hieroglyphic writing

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Maya writing, Maya writing system, archaic writings, characters, characters used by them in expressing their language in writing, hieratic form of writing, hieroglyphic characters, hieroglyphic writings, ideographic writings, inscriptions, monumental inscriptions, their mode of expressing their thoughts on paper

Abstract written by a model

Maya hieroglyphic writing is presented as a system of letters proper, monograms, and pictorial characters, including straight-line signs enclosed in square or oblong figures. The records emphasize claimed similarities between Maya and Egyptian writing: Maya words are used to explain Egyptian and place names, U LUUMIL KIN at Uxmal is compared with an Egyptian land-and-sun sign, and a sieve sign for a lord or master is treated as possible evidence of a connection between MAYAB and rulers of Egypt.

Maya hieroglyphic writing is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
