1 section; each heading opens the text

[A HENCHMAN _enters in haste_.]

The Henchman reports Hippolytus's destruction in detail, including the Sea-Bull's emergence, the horses' terror, and the chariot's wreck. He insists that Hippolytus was innocent and refuses to say that he died in sin.
