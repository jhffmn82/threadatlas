Harry Pinner

Abstract written by a model

Harry Pinner is presented as promoter and prospective managing director of the Franco-Midland Hardware Company. In Birmingham, he confirms Hall Pycroft’s engagement and assigns him Paris-directory work identifying hardware sellers and furniture shops. His gold-filled tooth matches Arthur Pinner’s, leading Holmes and Pycroft to conclude he is Arthur in disguise. After meeting Holmes, Watson, and Pycroft at the temporary offices, Arthur Harry Pinner attempts to hang himself with his braces but is revived.

Harry Pinner is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

IV. The Stockbroker’s Clerk

Harry Pinner supposedly meets Hall Pycroft at 126B Corporation Street in Birmingham and confirms his engagement with the Franco-Midland Hardware Company, Limited. He assigns Pycroft the task of marking hardware sellers in a Paris directory and later asks him to list furniture shops; Holmes and Pycroft conclude that Harry is actually Arthur Pinner in disguise.
