Abstract written by a model

The capital charge against Mrs. Barclay arises when Colonel Barclay is found dead beside her in a locked room, leading to possible murder trial and Holmes's warning. It ends when the inquest finds that James Barclay died of apoplexy, and the accusation is dropped.
