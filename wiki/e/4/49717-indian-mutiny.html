Indian Mutiny

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Mutiny, the outbreak of the Mutiny, the suppression of the Mutiny

Abstract written by a model

In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, the Mutiny begins while Henry Wood and his regiment are shut up in Bhurtee, leaving the settlement surrounded and short of water and prompting Wood to seek General Neill's column for help. In Dr. Elsie Inglis, the outbreak of the Indian Mutiny in 1857 prompted John Inglis to return to India, where he served in its suppression, including the action at Najibabad, the recapture of Bareilly, and the pacification of Rohilcund.
