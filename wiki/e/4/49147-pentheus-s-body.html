Pentheus's body

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: His body, body, the bier, the body, this body

Abstract written by a model

In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, Pentheus's body is found by Cadmus after being torn limb from limb in Kithaeron's woodland, and its remains are carried back to Thebes on a bier. In The Bacchae of Euripides, Pentheus's murdered body is torn limb from limb, scattered across Kithaeron, then gathered by Cadmus and returned on a bier.

Pentheus's body in The Bacchae of Euripides

Euripides · 406 to 405 BC · the work’s page · read it where Pentheus's body first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pentheus's murdered body is torn limb from limb after the dismemberment, scattered across Kithaeron's precipice, glens, and tangled wildwood as Maenads toss its flesh. Finding it is difficult until Cadmus gathers the pieces, returns them on a bier, and shows them to Agave.

Pentheus's body is left far from the scene, divided among the precipice and the tangled woodland. The Messenger says that finding all of it will be difficult, while the Maenads toss pieces of his flesh in their frenzy.

CHORUS.

Cadmus finds Pentheus's body torn limb from limb and scattered through the forest of Kithaeron. He gathers it, brings it back on a bier, and shows it to Agave after she recognizes the severed head.
