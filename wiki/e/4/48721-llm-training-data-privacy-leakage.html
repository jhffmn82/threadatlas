LLM training-data privacy leakage in The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Yue Xing, Yiding Liu, Han Xu, Jie Ren, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin, Yi Chang, Jiliang Tang · 2024

LLM training-data privacy leakage is a privacy investigation concerning LLMs used in RAG systems. It compares exposure of targeted training-corpus subsets with and without retrieval augmentation on the same large language model, using targeted and prefix attacks.

3 / Method / 3.1 / Background and Threat Model .. 3.3 / Privacy Leakage on LLM Training Data

LLM training-data privacy leakage is investigated by comparing exposure of training data with and without retrieval augmentation while attacking the same large language model. The investigation focuses on targeted subsets of the training corpus using targeted attacks and prefix attacks.
