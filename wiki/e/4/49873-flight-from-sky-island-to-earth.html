The flight home begins at Sky Island, passes clouds and Polychrome, and gently lands on a bluff near Trot's house on Earth.

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

The air-ride carries Trot and Button-Bright quickly and comfortably to town and back. Cap'n Bill first fears it but enjoys the trial ride to Smuggler's Cove and agrees to travel with the others to Sky Island.

THE JOURNEY HOME

The flight begins when the Magic Umbrella obeys the instruction to take the travelers to Trot's house on Earth. It moves through clouds and clear sky, passes near Polychrome and the Daughters of the Rainbow, and ends with a gentle landing on a high bluff.
