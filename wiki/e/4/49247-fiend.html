IRIS, A FIEND, CHORUS.

The Fiend says that she is the daughter of Night and that she would dissuade Iris and Juno from attacking Hercules, whom she recognizes as a benefactor of the gods. Forced to obey, she enters Hercules’ house, vows to unleash terrible madness, and causes the slaughter before Athena checks Hercules’ violence.
