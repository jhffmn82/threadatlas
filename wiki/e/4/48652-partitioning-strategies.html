Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Experimental Results

Partitioning strategies are evaluated across XSum, Es→En translation, and DailyDialog. Retrieval from the entire database generally fails to achieve optimal performance, and increasing the number of partitions can slightly reduce performance because smaller partitions may recall memories that do not align with language-model preferences.
