Rosalie the Witch in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912 · the work’s page · read it where Rosalie the Witch first appears

Abstract written by a model

Rosalie the Witch is a famous, wise, honest, rosy-pink witch, somewhat taller than most Pinkies, with a slender staff tipped by a lustrous pink jewel. Though not very obliging and prone to headaches from witchcraft, she has useful but limited powers: only one magic charm every three days. She has no power over frogs, keeps Ghip-Ghisizzle prisoner in her tent, and is friends with giant frogs. Summoned as the wise and honest deciding voter, she uses a vision to confirm the Earth people's account, votes to throw them from the island, and expects fairies to protect them through the Magic Umbrella. She advises the Army of Conquest's size, secures the frogs' help across the Fog Bank, sees events in the Blue City, enchants the Pinkies' sticks, and helps the rescue effort with a rope ladder and the invisibility ring she lends Trot. After victory, she urges the group to return before conflict between Pinkies and Blueskins. She refuses queenship under an old law that would make its ruler poor and miserable, but after Trot changes it, accepts appointment as Queen of the Pinkies. Loved and respected, she rules and protects them, cares for a parrot given by Trot, and remains on Sky Island with Coralie and Tourmaline when Trot leaves because they have no Magic Umbrella.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

ROSALIE THE WITCH

Rosalie the Witch is summoned to cast the deciding vote because she is considered wise and honest. She creates a vision that confirms the Earth people's story, votes to have them thrown into the sky, and explains that fairies will likely protect them through the Magic Umbrella.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

Rosalie the Witch advises that the Army of Conquest consist of one hundred Sunsets and one hundred Sunrises. She speaks with the King of the Giant Frogs and arranges for the frogs to help the Pinkies cross the Fog Bank.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

Rosalie sees through a vision what is happening inside the Blue City and enchants the Pinkies' sharp sticks. Because she can use only one magic charm every three days, she cannot immediately rescue Cap'n Bill, but she makes a rope ladder and gives Trot an invisibility ring to aid the rescue.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

Rosalie the Witch previously lent Trot the ring that makes her invisible. She awaits Button-Bright, Coralie, and the others at her tent, where they hold a council of war.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Rosalie the Witch arrives with the Pinkies, congratulates Trot, and receives her magic ring back from her. She suggests that the group return to their own country soon because conflict might arise between the Pinkies and Blueskins.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

Rosalie the Witch signals for the frogs to help the travelers cross the Fog Bank. She cannot make the frogs allow the umbrella through and says that the fairies controlling it must be very powerful.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Rosalie the Witch refuses to become Queen while the old law would make the ruler poor and miserable. After Trot changes the law, Trot appoints Rosalie Queen of the Pinkies because she is sensible, loved, respected, and able to use her powers to protect the people.

THE JOURNEY HOME

Rosalie accepts the parrot from Trot and says goodbye when Trot leaves. She believes that she, Coralie, and Tourmaline will remain on Sky Island because they have no Magic Umbrella.
