Also called: the gnomon

Abstract written by a model

An astronomical instrument discovered in the ruined city of Mayapan, the gnomon is said to show Maya scholars’ latitude calculations, solstitial-point and solar-declination identification, and a construction representing the sacred year through its stela centers’ joining line.
