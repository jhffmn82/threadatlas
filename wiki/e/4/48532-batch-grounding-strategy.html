batch grounding strategy

technique · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Batch Knowledge Ground, batch grounding

Abstract written by a model

Batch grounding strategy is a grounding strategy for retrieved documents that divides lengthy reference lists into batches, examines them sequentially, and revises an answer when relevant evidence can be cited. It stops once a batch provides evidence; if none does, it outputs the original answer. It handles irrelevant information and improves F1 and accuracy.
