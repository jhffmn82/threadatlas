the secret held over the men by Hudson

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: one of sin and shame, the guilty secret, the past of this Trevor, this secret, what this secret was

Abstract written by a model

A guilty secret from the shared past of Mr. Trevor, Hudson, and Mr. Beddoes is known by Hudson and held over two wealthy, respected men. It threatens disgrace and is feared by Victor Trevor as sin and shame. Holmes believes Hudson may threaten exposure, or Beddoes may warn Mr. Trevor of it; its actual nature remains unrevealed.
