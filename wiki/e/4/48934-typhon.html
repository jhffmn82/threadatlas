Typhon

person · 3 works · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Seth, Seth of the monuments, TYPHO, Typho, Typlion
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Typhon is Osiris's wicked brother who kills and dismembers him before being avenged by Isis and Orus.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Typhon is a fire-breathing giant and the envious brother of Osiris who conspires against, kills, and dismembers him.
  • In Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa, Typho is the jealous brother of Osiris and Isis who murders Osiris, scatters his remains, and is defeated by Horus.

Typhon in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Typhon, Osiris’s wicked brother, kills and dismembers him, distributes the pieces to confederates, and is later avenged upon by Isis and Orus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

Typhon, the wicked brother of Osiris, murders him and cuts his body into six-and-twenty pieces, distributing them among his confederates. Isis and Orus later avenge Osiris's death upon Typhon and his accomplices.

Typhon in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Typhon, identified with Seth of the monuments and brother of Osiris, is a fire-breathing giant. Envying Osiris and seeking his throne, he organizes a seventy-two-member conspiracy, kills Osiris by casting him in a chest into the Nile, then cuts and disperses his body into fourteen pieces.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MYTH OF OSIRIS AND ISIS

Typhon, identified with Seth of the monuments, envied Osiris and tried to usurp his throne while Osiris was away. He organized a conspiracy, murdered Osiris by shutting him in a chest, later found the concealed coffin, and cut Osiris's body into fourteen pieces.

Typho in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Typho, brother of Osiris and Isis, murders Osiris from jealousy and a desire for the throne. He later steals Osiris’s remains from Isis, cuts them into pieces, and scatters them over the earth. His course ends when Horus defeats him.
