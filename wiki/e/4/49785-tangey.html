Tangey, a Foreign Office employee, keeps an overnight lodge at the foot of the stairs and makes coffee for late workers; during a theft, he sleeps beside a boiling kettle, hears Phelps's bell, and joins the search.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI. The Naval Treaty

The commissionnaire keeps an overnight lodge at the foot of the Foreign Office stairs and ordinarily makes coffee for officials working late. During the theft he falls asleep beside a boiling kettle, hears the bell from Phelps's room, and follows Phelps in searching for the thief.
