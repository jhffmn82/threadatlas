LongRAG in LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering

Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, Jie Tang · 2024

LongRAG is a retrieval-augmented generation system paradigm for long-context question answering. It uses a dual information perspective to address incomplete collection of long-context information and difficulty identifying factual information amid noise. It can use small parameter-size LLMs in plug-and-play components, low-cost local deployment instead of expensive online API resources, and an automated instruction-data fine-tuning pipeline. Evaluations in fine-tuned and non-fine-tuned configurations across HotpotQA, 2WikiMQA, and MusiQue show robust gains: LongRAG-GLM-4 without SFT reaches a 52.56% average score and LongRAG outperforms long-context LLMs, advanced RAG methods, Vanilla RAG, and GPT-3.5-Turbo.

LongRAG in LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering

Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, Jie Tang · 2024

LongRAG is a general, dual-perspective, robust, plug-and-play LLM-based retrieval-augmented generation system paradigm for long-context question answering. Authored by Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, and Jie Tang, it aims to improve understanding of complex long-context knowledge by combining global information with factual details. It follows the structure of Vanilla RAG, but after retrieval adds a Long-Context Extractor and a CoT-guided Filter. Its Hybrid Retriever obtains relevant chunks; the LLM-augmented Information Extractor maps these chunks to source paragraphs to extract global information; the filter identifies factual details; and the LLM-augmented Generator uses the resulting inputs to answer questions. The paradigm can adapt to various domains and LLMs, and is evaluated with fine-tuned and non-fine-tuned LLMs on HotpotQA, 2Wiki-MultiHopQA, and MusiQue. Using 200-word chunks and top-7 retrieval by default, it generally surpasses long-context LLM, advanced RAG, and Vanilla RAG baselines; its Extractor & Filter strategy usually produces the strongest results while sending fewer tokens to the generator. Reported gains include up to 6.94% over long-context LLMs, 6.16% over Self-RAG on average, and up to 17.25% over Vanilla RAG. Its effectiveness may be limited by one-time retrieval quality and annotation biases in self-generated fine-tuning datasets.

LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering

LongRAG is the title of a work authored by Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, and Jie Tang. It concerns a dual-perspective retrieval-augmented generation paradigm for long-context question answering.

Abstract

LongRAG is proposed as a general, dual-perspective, robust, and plug-and-play LLM-based RAG system paradigm for LCQA. It is designed to improve understanding of global information and factual details in complex long-context knowledge, can adapt to various domains and LLMs, and outperforms advanced RAG by up to 6.16% and Vanilla RAG by up to 17.25%.

Introduction

LongRAG is a general, dual-perspective, and robust RAG system paradigm for long-context question answering. It combines a hybrid retriever, an LLM-augmented information extractor, a CoT-guided filter, and an LLM-augmented generator to mine global information and identify factual details. Its reported results show improvements over long-context LLMs, advanced RAG methods, and Vanilla RAG.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.2

LongRAG follows the structure of Vanilla RAG while adding a Long-Context Extractor E and a CoT-guided Filter F after retrieval. Its Hybrid Retriever obtains relevant chunks, the extractor maps them to source paragraphs to extract global information, the filter identifies factual details, and the generator combines these inputs to answer the question.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.5

The LongRAG System consists of a Hybrid Retriever, an LLM-augmented Information Extractor, a CoT-guided Filter, and an LLM-augmented Generator. It is evaluated with both fine-tuned and non-fine-tuned LLMs on three multi-hop datasets.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

LongRAG is evaluated on HotpotQA, 2Wiki-MultiHopQA, and MusiQue with four retrieval configurations, using 200-word chunks and top-7 retrieval by default. It generally outperforms long-context LLM, advanced RAG, and vanilla RAG baselines, and its Extractor & Filter strategy usually gives the strongest results with fewer tokens sent to the generator.

LongRAG is presented as a general-purpose, corpus-level retrieval-augmented generation system paradigm for long-context question answering. Its effectiveness may be limited by one-time retrieval quality and annotation biases in self-generated fine-tuning datasets.
