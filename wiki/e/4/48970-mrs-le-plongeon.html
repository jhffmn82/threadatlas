Mrs. Le Plongeon

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Le Plongeon arrived at Progresso in 1873, worked with Augustus Le Plongeon studying Maya monuments, and described Uxmal, Ake, and Mayapan in letters to the New York World.

Mrs. Le Plongeon is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
