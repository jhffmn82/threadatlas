Saturnalia in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Saturnalia is an annual winter feast held in memory of Saturn's beneficent dominion. During it, public business stops, war declarations and executions are postponed, friends exchange presents, and slaves receive liberties and a feast served by their masters.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

Saturnalia was held every year in winter in memory of Saturn's beneficent dominion. Public business was suspended, declarations of war and criminal executions were postponed, friends exchanged presents, and slaves were given great liberties, including a feast at which their masters served them.
