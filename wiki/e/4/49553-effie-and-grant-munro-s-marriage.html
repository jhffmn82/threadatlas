Abstract written by a model

Effie and Grant Munro’s marriage is a loving union that had been happy for three years until Effie’s secret life, unexplained money, nighttime departures, cottage visits, and apparent falsehoods, damages their trust. Her concealment of Lucy, driven by fear of Grant’s rejection amid racial prejudice, creates their first serious shadow. Once the truth emerges, Grant accepts Effie and Lucy and proposes discussing it at home.

III. The Yellow Face

Grant Munro says that he and Effie Munro had loved each other and lived happily for three years without any disagreement. Their trust is damaged when Effie develops a secret life involving unexplained money, nighttime departures, visits to the cottage, and statements Grant believes are false, although he insists that she still loves him.

“For two days after this I stayed at home, and my wife appeared

Effie’s concealment of Lucy creates the first serious shadow between her and Grant. After the truth is revealed, Grant indicates that he will accept Effie and Lucy and discuss the matter more comfortably at home.
