In Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering, the open source corpus, specifically Wikipedia, supports the retrieval system by providing publicly accessible and freely available data. In RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models, the corpus is a carefully controlled English Wikipedia corpus whose contents influence what is deemed unsafe and which safety violations answering may trigger.

Abstract written by a model

Corpus is a carefully controlled English Wikipedia corpus in a common RAG scenario. It cannot be modified by red-teaming methods under the threat model. Its contents influence what is deemed unsafe and which safety violations answering may trigger, including in domains such as legal statutes.

Related Work

The unit focuses on a common RAG scenario in which the corpus is carefully controlled.

Limitations

The corpus can influence what is deemed unsafe and which safety violations are triggered. For example, a corpus consisting of legal statutes may affect whether answering legal questions violates unauthorized practice of law.
