Odin

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Alfadur, All-father, Wodin

Abstract written by a model

Odin is a god and son of an unnamed god. With two brothers he helps create the world and humanity, regulates celestial time, rules from Valhalla, and oversees heaven and earth. He invents Runic characters, fathers Baldur, seeks knowledge of Baldur’s fate, and at Ragnarok fights Fenris, who kills him.

Odin is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Odin in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855 · the work’s page · read it where Odin first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

Odin was one of the three brothers who helped create the world and humanity, and he regulated day, night, and the seasons. He rules from Valhalla, is attended by the ravens Hugin and Munin and the wolves Geri and Freki, invented the Runic characters, and sends the Valkyrior to choose warriors for Valhalla.

Chapter XXXIII

Odin seeks knowledge of Baldur's threatened fate by consulting the dead prophetess Angerbode in Hela's dominions. At Ragnarok, he fights Fenris and is killed by the wolf.
