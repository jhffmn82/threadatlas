BGE-m3 in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where BGE-m3 first appears

Abstract written by a model

BGE-m3 is a publicly available multilingual retrieval, ranking, and reranking model whose training includes English and all languages considered. Selected for multilingual RAG experiments, it retrieves from English, user-language, and concatenated multilingual Wikipedia, dynamically selecting passages across languages. Used as both retriever and reranker, it enables reliable cross-lingual retrieval and outperforms SPLADE with translated queries.
