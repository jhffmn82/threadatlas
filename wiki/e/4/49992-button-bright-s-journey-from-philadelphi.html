A MYSTERIOUS ARRIVAL

Button-Bright has traveled from Philadelphia to the Pacific coast, but he does not say how he made the journey. He denies arriving by railroad, wagon, automobile, boat, or airship, and says he dropped from the sky with his umbrella. He promises to explain his journey in a story.
