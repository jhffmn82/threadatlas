Abstract written by a model

Diksey is a good-natured Horner man and expert guide in the Horners' radium mine. He jokes with the Hoppers and helps Ojo and Dorothy rescue the Scarecrow by bringing a ladder. He tells Ojo of the mine's dark well, then leads Ojo and companions safely through the cave and tunnel to it and back.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Three

Diksey explains to the Hoppers that his remark about their understanding was a wordplay joke about their having one leg instead of two. He brings a ladder so Ojo and Dorothy can rescue the Scarecrow, then makes another joke about mending the Scarecrow's ways by mending his ripped leg. Diksey also tells Ojo about a dark well in his radium mine.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Diksey leads Ojo and his companions through the great cave to the mine containing the dark well. He warns them about the well and guides them safely back through the tunnel.
