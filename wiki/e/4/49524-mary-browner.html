Mary Browner

Abstract written by a model

Mary Browner, younger sister of Susan and Sarah Cushing and wife of James Browner, is initially described as loving, trusting, and innocent. She stops writing to Susan, leaving her family uncertain. James says Sarah’s influence and Alec Fairbairn’s appearance turn her trust to suspicion; believing Mary involved with Fairbairn, he kills her in a boat after attacking them.

Mary Browner is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
