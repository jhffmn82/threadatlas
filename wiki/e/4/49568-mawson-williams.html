Abstract written by a model

Mawson & Williams is a large, wealthy and famous London stockbroking firm in Lombard Street, holding securities worth considerably over a million sterling. It employs an armed watchman and offers Hall Pycroft a £200-a-year clerkship, but Beddington, posing as Pycroft, obtains the position while Arthur Pinner tries to keep Pycroft from resigning. Beddington uses the access to prepare an attempted robbery, during which the watchman is murdered.

Mawson & Williams is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
