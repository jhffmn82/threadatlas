Reichenbach Falls in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

A dangerous, fearful Swiss waterfall reached by a path beside the torrent, Reichenbach Falls becomes the site of Holmes's final confrontation with Moriarty after Watson leaves.

the Reichenbach Fall in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The Reichenbach Fall was the site where Professor Moriarty fell to his death and Sherlock Holmes appeared to die. Holmes secretly climbed to a ledge, evaded an accomplice who tried to kill him with rocks, and escaped over the mountains. The Reichenbach Fall is where Colonel Sebastian Moran previously attacked Holmes by dropping rocks over the cliff. Holmes refers to Moran’s attentions there when confronting him.
