  • In Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain, token F1 scores measure answer quality, correlate reasonably with human subjects for extractive tasks, and support model comparisons and quality displays.

  • In SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, F1 (correct) is a metric that, using DeepSeek, strongly agrees with human judgments and declines under conflict attack.
  • In SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, F1 (incorrect) scores obtained using DeepSeek demonstrate strong agreement with human judgments, with values reported for four attack types.
  • In SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, F1 score is used as an evaluation metric by RAG Bench and PoisonedRAG, with SafeRAG reporting correct, incorrect, and average responses.
  • In HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation, F1 score measures answer and retrieval quality alongside exact match; average answer F1 is 66.76 with GPT-3.5-turbo and 66.40 with GPT-4o.
  • In Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study, F1-score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall and is used as a balanced metric for embedding-model and large-language-model comparisons.
  • In Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, F1 score evaluates response quality against ground-truth answers and retrieval quality against referenced facts.
  • In Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, F1 measures token-level overlap between predictions and references through separate answer, supporting-fact, and joint versions.
