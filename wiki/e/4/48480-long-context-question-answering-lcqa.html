Long-Context Question Answering (LCQA) in LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering

Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, Jie Tang · 2024

Long-Context Question Answering (LCQA) is a challenging, complex task of reasoning over a long document or multiple long-context documents to produce accurate answers to questions. It requires methods that scale the limited context length of existing LLMs and is affected by the “lost in the middle” problem, in which long-context LLMs overlook crucial factual details. LongRAG presents a dual-perspective retrieval-augmented generation paradigm for LCQA. Its fine-tuned components use data retaining supporting and some distracting paragraphs to extract global information, filter factual details, and generate answers. The approach is evaluated on three multi-hop datasets, where it is presented as improving RAG performance by extracting and filtering relevant information.
