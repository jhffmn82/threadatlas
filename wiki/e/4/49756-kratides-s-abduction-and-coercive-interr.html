Kratides's abduction and coercive interrogation in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Paul Kratides comes from Athens after Sophy flees with Harold Latimer. Latimer and Wilson Kemp abduct, imprison, disguise, and isolate him because he cannot speak English. In an unknown furnished house, starvation, plaster restraints, threats, and separation from Sophy coerce written answers and a signature; he refuses unless he sees her married by a Greek priest.

X. The Greek Interpreter

Kratides is confined in an unknown richly furnished house, weakened by starvation, and questioned about whether he will sign papers. The men use threats and his separation from Sophy to pressure him, but he repeatedly refuses and demands to see her married before a Greek priest.
