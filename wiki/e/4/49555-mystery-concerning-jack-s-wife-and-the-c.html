mystery concerning Jack's wife and the cottage

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: all, mystery, secret, strange business, the secret of the cottage, this mystery, truth

Abstract written by a model

The mystery concerns Jack's wife, the cottage, and Lucy, Effie's concealed daughter. It begins with Jack's wife's secret nightly visits and suspicion about the creature inside. Jack temporarily stops demanding an explanation but seeks the truth. After Grant Munro enters the cottage, Lucy's identity is revealed: Effie hid her there with a nurse, fearing Grant would reject her because of her African ancestry.
