Abstract written by a model

The wood-cutters are neighborhood representatives who supply passing steamboats with wood. Ranging from men of sterling integrity to floating drinkers and card players, they settle personal quarrels under early boatmen's law through supervised unarmed contests.

SCENE SECOND.

The wood-cutters are the representative men of the neighborhood and supply the wood used by passing steamboats. Some are described as men of sterling integrity, while others belong to the floating population and gather at headquarters for drinking, cards, and entertainment. Their customary method of settling personal quarrels is an unarmed physical contest supervised by chosen seconds.
