the Blue Wolf in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Blue Wolf is a monstrous, ravenous animal in the Blue City, terrible and fearful in aspect, with a lion-sized head, powerful shoulders and front legs, flashing blue eyes, and needle-sharp foot-long teeth. Crouching near the Treasure Chamber entrance, it guards the Royal Treasure Chamber. It attacks Button-Bright, who jams a sofa pillow in its jaws; the wolf cannot free its teeth and struggles to remove it with its claws.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BLUE CITY

The Blue Wolf guards the inside of the Treasure Chamber. It is described as a ravenous creature with teeth a foot long and as sharp as needles.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Blue Wolf attacks Button-Bright in the Treasure Chamber, but Button-Bright jams a sofa pillow into its jaws. The wolf is unable to free its teeth from the pillow and later struggles to pull it out with its claws.
