Abstract written by a model

The Blue Wolf is a monstrous, ravenous animal in the Blue City, terrible and fearful in aspect, with a lion-sized head, powerful shoulders and front legs, flashing blue eyes, and needle-sharp foot-long teeth. Crouching near the Treasure Chamber entrance, it guards the Royal Treasure Chamber. It attacks Button-Bright, who jams a sofa pillow in its jaws; the wolf cannot free its teeth and struggles to remove it with its claws.
