the Foolish Owl in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Foolish Owl, an unusually foolish public adviser, sits on a shelf in a blue sunbonnet and composes rhyming nonsense poems about Scraps, Ojo's charm search, and the travelers' uncertain journey; the Wise Donkey admires her unusual foolishness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eight

The Foolish Owl sits on a shelf wearing a blue sunbonnet and speaks in rhyming, nonsensical verses. Her poems describe Scraps, Ojo's search for a charm, and the travelers' uncertain journey. The Wise Donkey says she is extremely foolish but admires her for being unusual.
