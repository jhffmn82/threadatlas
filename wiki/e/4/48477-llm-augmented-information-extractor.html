LLM-augmented Information Extractor

component · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Information Extractor, LLM-augmented information extractor, information extractor, long-context extractor

Abstract written by a model

The LLM-augmented Information Extractor is a plug-and-play LongRAG component evaluated in a one-time retrieval scenario. It maps retrieved chunks to source long-context paragraphs, keeps the paragraph tied to the most similar chunk when duplicates occur, and concatenates them. Using zero-shot ICL, it turns these paragraphs into global information with background and structural knowledge, extending semantic space and reducing poor responses from redundant long passages before final generation.

Introduction

The LLM-augmented information extractor is a plug-and-play LongRAG component that explores global long-context information. Its long-context extractor uses a mapping strategy to extend the semantic space of retrieved chunks into a higher-dimensional long-context semantic space, then refines global information and contextual structure among chunks.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.5

The LLM-augmented Information Extractor maps retrieved chunks back to their source long-context paragraphs, retaining the paragraph associated with the most similar chunk when duplicates occur. It concatenates the mapped paragraphs and uses zero-shot ICL to produce global information containing background and structural knowledge.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

The information extractor is described as a component that obtains global information from mapped source long paragraphs before final generation. It helps mitigate poor responses caused by feeding redundant long passages directly into the LLM.
