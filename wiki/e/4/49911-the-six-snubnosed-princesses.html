Abstract written by a model

The Six Snubnosed Princesses are six sister princesses of Sky Island, daughters of the Boolooroo and Queen, and royal daughters of the Revered and Resplendent Royal Ruler of the Blues. They are gorgeously dressed Blueskin girls with blue gowns and hair, huge towers of hair, long thin necks, and snub noses regarded as high breeding. Haughty, cruel, and vindictive, they habitually mistreat their favored animal and bird pets. They claim Trot as their maid, slave, and living pincushion, fasten six ribbons to her, and drag her into the Blue Palace, where they order her to serve them and seek harsh punishment for her after their pets rebel. They also quarrel over marrying Ghip-Ghisizzle to become Queen, free him from the Room of the Great Knife, and confine him until he chooses one of them. When the billygoat attacks during Cap'n Bill's patching, they are thrown into the corridor in terror. Later they reject Trot's rule and each demands the kingdom, but Trot has them locked under guard, making them meek and mild. After Trot's victory, they and their parents are expelled from the palace and escorted, screaming and hysterical, to Ghip-Ghisizzle's small cabin.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

The Six Snubnosed Princesses are gorgeously dressed Blueskin girls with blue hair, long thin necks, and snub noses that are considered marks of high breeding in Sky Island. They choose Trot as their maid and slave, attach six ribbons to her, and drag her into the Blue Palace.

THE TRIBULATION OF TROT

The Six Snubnosed Princesses occupy magnificent blue-decorated apartments and order Trot to serve them. They mistreat their pets and quarrel over how Trot should be punished after the pets rebel against them. They eventually leave for the Royal Banquet Hall while Trot remains in the apartments.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Six Snubnosed Princesses bitterly denounce Trot during dinner and urge the Boolooroo to invent a terrible punishment for her. Their favored pets follow Trot, and Button-Bright later falsely claims that the Boolooroo ordered Jimfred to guard their apartments.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Six Snubnosed Princesses quarrel over which of them will marry Ghip-Ghisizzle and become Queen. They free him from the Room of the Great Knife, then lock him in a vacant room until he chooses one of them.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The Six Snubnosed Princesses enter the Room of the Great Knife in magnificent clothing to watch Cap'n Bill be patched. The billygoat attacks them, overturning their chairs and sending them tangled into the corridor in terror.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Six Snubnosed Princesses object to Trot's rule and each claims the right to rule the kingdom. Trot has them seized and locked in their rooms under guard, after which they become meek and mild.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Six Snubnosed Princesses are expelled from the palace with their father and mother after Trot's victory. They are escorted by soldiers to Ghip-Ghisizzle's small cabin, screaming and suffering hysterics over the decree.
