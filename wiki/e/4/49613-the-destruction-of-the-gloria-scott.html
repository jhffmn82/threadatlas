Abstract written by a model

The destruction of the Gloria Scott occurs on November 6, 1855, at N. lat. 15º 20’, W. long. 25º 14’, during a confrontation with the first mate. After escaping his bonds, he hides beside an open powder barrel and threatens to destroy the ship. An explosion, possibly from a convict’s misdirected bullet, destroys the vessel and kills all aboard except Hudson.
