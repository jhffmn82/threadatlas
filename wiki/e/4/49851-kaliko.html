Abstract written by a model

In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, Kaliko serves as the Nome King’s Chief Steward and mechanic, repairs Tiktok, and is rewarded with higher wages. In Tik-Tok of Oz, Kaliko serves as Ruggedo’s Royal Chamberlain and High Chamberlain, advises him, assists the adventurers, and becomes the new King of the Nome Kingdom. In Tik-Tok of Oz, King Kaliko becomes the new Nome King, rules the Nome Kingdom, appoints Guph as Royal Chamberlain, and grants freedom to the Ugly One. In The Gnome King of Oz, Kaliko becomes King of the Gnomes, but yields the crown to Ruggedo and resumes serving as Royal Chamberlain.

Kaliko in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where Kaliko first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kaliko is a highly intelligent nome who begins as Ruggedo's loyal Royal Chamberlain and High Chamberlain, serving as an official and carrying out orders even when he foresees that Ruggedo's choices will ruin him. He uses the Magic Spyglass to watch the approaching adventurers, warns Ruggedo about Tititi-Hoochoo and the forbidden Hollow Tube, and advises releasing the Shaggy Man's brother. Though he sometimes acts covertly, kicking Tik-Tok's knee-joints, he also protects and assists others: he shelters Betsy Bobbin and Hank in his furnished room, feeds Betsy, and organizes nomes for Ruggedo's plans. He repeatedly offers prudent counsel, including that Tik-Tok is valuable, but Ruggedo disregards or threatens him. After the rescue, Kaliko becomes the new King of the Nome Kingdom, ruling Nome Country and responsible for its subjects. He leads the group back through the Metal Forest, provides refreshments, and permits the formerly served Ruggedo to remain only if he behaves, with exile promised for further misconduct.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Kaliko serves as Ruggedo's High Chamberlain and uses a magic spyglass to observe the approaching adventurers. He warns Ruggedo about Tititi-Hoochoo, obeys the order to redirect the path to the Hollow Tube, and privately writes letters of recommendation because he expects Ruggedo's decision to ruin him.

Chapter Fourteen

Kaliko explains that Tititi-Hoochoo would not allow the invaders to remain in his kingdom and warns Ruggedo that throwing them into the Hollow Tube was forbidden. He uses the Magic Spyglass to see a dragon carrying the invaders through the Tube and advises Ruggedo to release the Shaggy Man's brother. He narrowly avoids Ruggedo's thrown sceptre by falling flat on the floor.

Chapter Sixteen

Kaliko secretly kicks Tik-Tok's knee-joints and causes him to fall. He then leads Betsy Bobbin and Hank to his own furnished room, gives Betsy food, and instructs her to admit people only after three knocks.

Chapter Seventeen

Kaliko advises Ruggedo to bind Shaggy Man's arms and gathers nomes to carry out the plan. He warns Ruggedo that Tik-Tok is a valuable machine, but Ruggedo threatens to put him in the furnace and later orders him to search for escaped prisoners.

Chapter Twenty-Two

King Kaliko leads the group back through the Metal Forest and toward the passage to the outer world. He permits Queen Ann and the Army officers to gather jewels and later helps Shaggy raise Ruggedo.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Kaliko orders refreshments for the party and allows Ruggedo to stay in the Nome Kingdom as long as he behaves. He warns that he will drive Ruggedo out if the old nome acts badly again, as Tititi-Hoochoo commanded.

King Kaliko in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where King Kaliko first appears

Abstract written by a model

King Kaliko is a Metal Monarch and former Royal Chamberlain who becomes the new Nome King after Ruggedo flees and falls. Kind to Betsy Bobbin during her captivity and friendly to the officers of Oogaboo, he is praised by Betsy, made King by Quox, and takes Ruggedo’s throne, ruby crown, and sceptre. He appoints Guph as Royal Chamberlain, rules the Nome Kingdom and its cavern, and directs the nomes to seek a passage to the Metal Forest and pursue Ruggedo. Although he plans to expel Ruggedo after permitting him treasure, Kaliko assures Queen Ann of friendship, grants freedom to the Ugly One, and cannot undo the enchantment of his appearance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

Kaliko brings Betsy Bobbin and Hank from his private room after Ruggedo flees and is praised by Betsy for having treated her kindly. Quox makes him King of the Nomes, and he takes Ruggedo's throne, ruby crown, and sceptre before appointing Guph as Royal Chamberlain.

Chapter Twenty

Kaliko becomes King after Ruggedo's fall and instructs the nomes to find a passage to the Metal Forest. He leads the search party after Ruggedo and plans to have the nomes throw Ruggedo out after allowing him to take treasure from the Forest.

Chapter Twenty-One

Kaliko is identified as the new Nome King and assures Queen Ann that he is a friend. He grants Ruggedo permission to fill his pockets with jewels and confirms the freedom of Shaggy Man's brother, though he cannot break the enchantment that changed the Ugly One's appearance.
