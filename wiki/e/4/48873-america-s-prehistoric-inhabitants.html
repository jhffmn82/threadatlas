When, on January 28, 1873, I had the honor of reading a paper before the New Yor

America's prehistoric inhabitants are compared with the peoples of Asia and Egypt through similarities in monuments, customs, religious rites, and traditions. The mound builders are said possibly to have followed a route from America toward Asia and the Nile valley.
