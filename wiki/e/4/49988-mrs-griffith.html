Mrs. Griffith in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Mrs. Griffith, Trot’s mother, lives in a frame cottage and is motherly and good, welcoming and feeding Button-Bright while often scolding Cap’n Bill and Trot.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Mrs. Griffith welcomes Button-Bright, gives him food, and invites him to stay at the cottage. She is motherly and kind toward the children but frequently scolds Cap'n Bill and Trot, including for spilled tea and the wrong-colored yarn.
