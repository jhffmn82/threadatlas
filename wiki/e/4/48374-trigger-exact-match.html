Trigger Exact Match

Abstract written by a model

Trigger Exact Match is an evaluation metric ranging from 0 to 1 that measures whether a generated JSON or workflow trigger exactly matches the ground truth, including a required table name. It evaluates performance across settings; with RAG, larger StarCoderBase models generally perform better.
