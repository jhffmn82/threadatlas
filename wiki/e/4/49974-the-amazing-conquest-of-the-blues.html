Trot, supported by the Pinkies and aided by the billygoat, completes the conquest of the Blue Country, becoming Queen of the Blues; Ghip-Ghisizzle becomes Boolooroo and is ordered to rule kindly and avoid trouble.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The conquest of the Blue Country is achieved by Trot with help from the billygoat. It makes Trot Queen of the Blues and leads to Ghip-Ghisizzle becoming Boolooroo.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Trot's conquest of the Blues results in Ghip-Ghisizzle becoming Boolooroo of the Blue Country. Trot orders him to rule kindly and avoid making trouble while she is away.
