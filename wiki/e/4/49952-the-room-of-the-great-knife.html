the Room of the Great Knife in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Room of the Great Knife is a large palace execution room centered on the Great Knife, with spectator benches, a royal platform, and a cutting machine used to patch prisoners. It holds bound prisoners including Ghip-Ghisizzle and Tiggle, and later receives Cap'n Bill for punishment. During the conquest, attackers drive out the soldiers and royal family. After the former Boolooroo is removed, its knife and terrible furnishings are destroyed, and Trot orders the room converted into a skating rink.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

Ghip-Ghisizzle and Tiggle are bound and locked in the Room of the Great Knife while the Boolooroo deals with the Pinkie invasion. The Six Snubnosed Princesses unlock the room and remove Ghip-Ghisizzle, leaving Tiggle behind.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The Room of the Great Knife is a large chamber with spectator benches, a royal platform, and a cutting machine used to patch prisoners. Cap'n Bill is brought there for punishment, but the billygoat's attack drives out the soldiers and royal family, leaving Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the injured Boolooroo inside.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Room of the Great Knife is cleared after the former Boolooroo is removed. Its frames are split, its benches are chopped into kindling, and the room's terrible furnishings are destroyed so it can be turned into a skating rink.
