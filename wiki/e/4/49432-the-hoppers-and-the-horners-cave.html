Chapter Twenty-Two

The great cave is vast enough to reach nearly to the top of the mountain and is illuminated by soft, invisible light. Its marble walls have delicate veins of color, and it contains the villages of the Hoppers and Horners.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The great cave contains the Horner city and several round, dark holes leading into the ground. Beyond the Horner city, one of these holes leads to the mine.
