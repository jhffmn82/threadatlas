the woodchopper in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The woodchopper is a Munchkin man with bushy blue whiskers who lives alone in a small leaf-covered house at the forest’s edge. He advises Scraps about illegal magic, explains dangers ahead, tells of Dorothy and the Tin Woodman, and offers lodging.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The woodchopper lives alone in a small leaf-covered house at the edge of the forest. He laughs at Scraps, warns that the Magician’s creations may be arrested near the Emerald City, and explains that unauthorized magic is illegal. He tells them about Dorothy and the Tin Woodman and warns that their route to the Winkies will be long and dangerous before inviting them to stay the night.
