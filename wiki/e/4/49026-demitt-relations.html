Demitt relations in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

The Demitt relations plot to obtain Aunt Katy's money, believing Suza Fairfield may inherit; they hire Tom Ditamus to take Suza to the Cumberland Mountains and isolate her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE FIFTH.

The Demitt relations speculate about Aunt Katy's money and believe that Suza Fairfield may inherit it. They arrange for Tom Ditamus to take Suza away so that Aunt Katy will not hear from her and the money may eventually pass to them.
