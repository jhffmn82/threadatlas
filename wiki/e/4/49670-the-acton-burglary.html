the Acton burglary in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The Acton burglary occurred at Acton's house on the Monday before Holmes and Watson arrived at Colonel Hayter's house. Burglars extensively searched the house, stealing plated candlesticks, an ivory letter-weight, an oak barometer, and a ball of twine; police found no clue.

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII. The Reigate Squires

The Acton burglary occurred on the Monday before Holmes and Watson arrived at Colonel Hayter's house. The house was extensively searched, but the burglars took only an unusual assortment of objects, and the police had found no clue.
