Time-Aware Adaptive REtrieval in RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering

Zihan Zhang, Meng Fang, Ling Chen · 2024

Time-Aware Adaptive REtrieval (TA-ARE) is a simple, model-based, time-aware adaptive retrieval method that helps large language models decide whether retrieval is necessary. It improves adaptive retrieval-augmented generation through in-context learning without calibration or additional training. Its instruction includes “Today is current_date()”, and it uses four demonstrations: two Yes examples for long-tail knowledge, selected with SimCSE as semantically closest long-tail questions, and two No examples of manually created questions requiring no extra information for most LLMs. This improves retrieval decisions for new world and long-tail questions, with average gains of 14.9% in retrieval accuracy and 6.7% in QA accuracy.
