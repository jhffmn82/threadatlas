Inspector Forrester

Also called: Inspector, the Inspector, the official

Abstract written by a model

Inspector Forrester is a young, keen-faced police inspector and official investigating the Acton and Cunningham burglaries, William Kirwan’s murder, and the attempted burglary. He brings Holmes the paper fragment found in Kirwan’s hand, examines the scene, interviews connected people, searches the house, and summons constables. His initial belief that the Cunninghams, as county magnates, could not be involved leads him to overlook the paper’s significance and nearly reveal it to them. After Holmes accuses the Cunninghams, Forrester helps secure their arrest when Alec Cunningham’s revolver is knocked away.

Inspector Forrester is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VII. The Reigate Squires

Inspector Forrester investigates the Acton burglary and the Cunningham burglary and murder. He tells Holmes that the burglar was seen fleeing after shooting William Kirwan, shows Holmes the fragment of paper found in Kirwan's hand, and joins Holmes in examining the crime scene and interviewing people connected with it.
