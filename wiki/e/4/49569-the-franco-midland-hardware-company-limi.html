IV. The Stockbroker’s Clerk

The Franco-Midland Hardware Company, Limited, is presented to Hall Pycroft as a company with 134 branches in France, plus one in Brussels and one in San Remo. Its supposed temporary offices in Birmingham are sparsely furnished and have no company name displayed.
