Abstract written by a model

Visual question answering with external data is a task setting evaluated on Encyclopedic-VQA and InfoSeek, whose questions concern specific entities and often require external knowledge. The approach retrieves an entity and relevant passages, then supplies them to a multimodal large language model for answer generation. Accuracy is measured with official scripts; relevant retrieved context improves results, especially with two or three textual chunks on InfoSeek, while oracle retrieval performs substantially better.
