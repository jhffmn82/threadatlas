Abstract written by a model

Roxie Daymon, only daughter of Roxie Fairfield, is raised and educated among Chicago’s moneyed aristocracy and reaches womanhood. After losing every property case, she becomes destitute. Traveling with a pleasure party to New Orleans, she becomes engaged to young Simon and, after his death, inherits his whole estate under his will.
