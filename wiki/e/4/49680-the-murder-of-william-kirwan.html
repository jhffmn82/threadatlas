the murder of William Kirwan in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The murder of William Kirwan occurs at the Cunningham house, where he is found wounded after a staged alleged struggle with a burglar. Alec Cunningham commits the killing to eliminate a feared blackmailer, with Mr. Cunningham involved. Holmes exposes the false burglary story, proves Alec lied, and the Inspector arrests both men.

The murder concerns William Kirwan, who was found wounded after an alleged struggle with a burglar near the Cunningham house. Holmes ultimately accuses Mr. Cunningham and Alec Cunningham of murdering him, and the Inspector arrests them.

The killing was staged to resemble a burglary-related attack by an unknown man. Holmes determines that William was shot from more than four yards away, that Alec lied about a struggle, and that no unknown assailant escaped across the ditch.
