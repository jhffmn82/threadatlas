Poverty Queen in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Poverty Queen is Trot’s regulation of the Queen of the Pinkies: she receives the same food, dresses, good times, housing, and spending money as the people, cannot take more, and faces being pushed off the edge if she does.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Trot has the Royal Scribbler record a law stating that the Queen shall have the same food, dresses, good times, housing, and spending money as the people, but no more than her share. If the Queen seeks more than her share, the law provides that she will be taken to the edge and pushed off.
