the Pink Country in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Pink Country, also called the Sunset Country, is a pink country forming one half of Sky Island, separated from the Blue Country by the Fog Bank. About two square miles in area, it borders the fog and has a dangerous outer edge dropping into the gulf below. It is a land of sunrises and sunsets, with pink grass, trees, paths, sky, clouds, sunlight, marble public spaces, and round buildings without corners or angles; its neat, garden-like landscape ranges from faint rose to deep pink. It contains the City and Tourmaline's palace, and is inhabited by several hundred Pinkies, including the eastern Sunrise Tribe and western Sunset Tribe. Its prosperity, color uniformity, and laws matter greatly. Under Tourmaline, the Pinkies initially have no established place for outsiders and consider expelling Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill after they emerge from the Fog Bank. Polychrome interprets the laws to protect the travelers and make Trot Queen. Tourmaline is relieved, Trot is received as the new Queen, and the customs require a poor plain dress and little pleasure. After Trot and her companions return from the Blue Country, the country welcomes them; Trot leaves Rosalie the Witch to rule under the new law.

Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

The Pink Country is named in the contents. Chapters include "The Pink Country," "Tourmaline the Poverty Queen," and "Mayre, Queen of the Pink Country."

THE BLUE CITY

The Pink Country is the other half of Sky Island and is said to be pink instead of blue. It is also called the Sunset Country, whose evenings reportedly display orange, crimson, golden-yellow, and red colors.

THROUGH THE FOG BANK

The Pink Country is the destination Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, and the parrot seek after escaping the Blueskins. The common frog says that the Pinkies are very decent neighbors. The travelers approach it as the Fog Bank becomes lighter and rose-colored.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Pink Country has pink grass, trees, buildings, paths, sky, clouds, and sunlight, with many shades ranging from faint rose to deep pink. Its landscape is neat and garden-like, and its buildings are round rather than angular.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Pink Country is a prosperous land inhabited by the Pinkies and ruled by Tourmaline. It has pink marble public spaces, statues of former rulers, and no established place for outsiders such as Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Pink Country occupies one half of Sky Island and is about two miles square with several hundred inhabitants. It contains the eastern Sunrise Tribe and western Sunset Tribe, and its people are governed by Tourmaline.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The Pink Country is where the three Earth people land after passing through the Fog Bank. Its people are concerned with maintaining a uniform color scheme and initially consider expelling the strangers.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

The Pink Country is a rainy land with sticky pink mud, and its people avoid the dangerous edge where the land drops into a gulf. Its laws are interpreted by Polychrome to require protection for the travelers and to make Trot its Queen.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Pink Country receives Trot as its new Queen after Tourmaline is relieved of the position. Its customs require the Queen to wear a poor, plain dress and to live without much pleasure.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The Pink Country is the travelers' destination beyond the Fog Bank. The group reaches its edge after following the elephant through the fog.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

The Pink Country welcomes Trot and her companions after their return from the Blue Country. Trot leaves Rosalie the Witch to rule it under the new law.
