Abstract written by a model

Māui is a great primordial hero in Maori and other Polynesian traditions who obtains fire for earth from Mahuika, sometimes as a bird, and in one Hawaiian account learns fire-making from an alae bird.

IIJ.—Myrus oF THE ORIGIN OF FIRE

Māui is the great primordial hero who procures fire for earth in Maori and other Polynesian traditions. He obtains it from Mahuika, sometimes assumes the form of a bird, and is also said in one Hawaiian account to learn fire-making from an alae bird.
