Abstract written by a model

Rosalie the Witch’s witchcraft treasure is a magic ring worn by Trot. It makes its wearer invisible to Blueskins, Pinkies, and Earth people, allowing Trot to enter the Blue City unseen. Touching another person or living creature makes Trot visible while contact lasts, and Rosalie requires the ring’s return.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

Rosalie's magic ring makes its wearer invisible to Blueskins, Pinkies, and Earth people. Trot wears it to enter the Blue City unseen, but Rosalie warns that touching another person would make Trot visible and that the ring must be returned.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The ring of Rosalie the Witch makes Trot invisible unless she touches a living creature. Contact with the blue dog, the blue lamb, or Cap'n Bill makes her visible while the contact lasts.
