An imagined light, four-wheeled road vehicle carries six passengers in its passenger box, follows the golden rule, and has a pilot, rear engineer and fireman, brass boiler, and phosphorus-and-tinder heat source.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE SIXTH.

The steam carriage is imagined as a light, four-wheeled vehicle carrying six passengers. It has a pilot on top, an engineer and fireman at the rear, a polished brass boiler, and a chemical heat source made from phosphorus and tinder.
