Maya calendar

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: civil year, sacred year

Abstract written by a model

Maya calendar distinguishes a civil year of eighteen twenty-day months plus five unlucky supplementary days from a sacred or sideral year of 365 days and 6 hours.

Maya calendar is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Maya civil year is described as having eighteen months of twenty days, followed by five supplementary days considered unlucky. The Maya sacred or sideral year is said to have consisted of 365 days and 6 hours.
