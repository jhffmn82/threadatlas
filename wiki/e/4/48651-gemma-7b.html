Abstract written by a model

In “M-RAG: Reinforcing Large Language Model Performance through Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Multiple Partitions,” Gemma 7B was compared using Selfmem and M-RAG, with M-RAG improving its summarization, translation, and dialogue results. In “TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” Gemma-7B served as a reader model for evaluating TRACE and baselines. In “RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models,” Gemma-7b-It was assessed in RAG and appeared safer largely because of poor extraction and summarization and frequent harmless-question refusals. In “SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” Gemma-7B served as a baseline for evaluating SafetyRAG’s handling of unsafe prompts.

Gemma-7B in SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Sihem Omri, Manel Abdelkader, and Mohamed Hamdi · 2025-02-17 · the work’s page · read it where Gemma-7B first appears

Abstract written by a model

Gemma-7B is a lightweight LLM from Google, aligned with responsible behavior through fine-tuning and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback. Used as a baseline to evaluate SafetyRAG, it initially blocked 39% of direct unsafe prompts and 1% of indirect unsafe prompts with prompt injection. After SafetyRAG, it blocked 100% of unsafe prompts without injection and 88% of injected indirect prompts.
