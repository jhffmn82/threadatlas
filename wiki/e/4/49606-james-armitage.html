James Armitage in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

James Armitage, later known as Trevor, was convicted in his younger days after using money not his own to pay a debt of honour, and was sentenced to transportation aboard the Gloria Scott.

James Armitage says that he formerly used the name Trevor. He was convicted of breaking the law after using money that was not his own to pay a debt of honour, and he was sentenced to transportation aboard the Gloria Scott.
