Wilson

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: chaplain, chaplain of the Gloria Scott, sham chaplain, the sham chaplain

Abstract written by a model

Wilson is a conspirator disguised as a chaplain and Prendergast’s partner. Arriving aboard the Gloria Scott with proper papers, money, and a black bag, he purchases the loyalty of the crew, two warders, and the second mate, helping organize the mutiny and entering the convicts’ cells.

Wilson is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
