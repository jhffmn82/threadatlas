the accident involving Margolotte and Unc Nunkie in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A magical accident petrifies Unc Nunkie and Margolotte. It begins when the Patchwork Girl knocks the Powder of Life from Dr. Pipt's hand, startling them; Unc Nunkie's movement upsets the Liquid of Petrifaction, which falls on both and turns them into marble statues. The accident is later linked to Dr. Pipt's unlawful keeping of the liquid. Ojo undertakes a quest for ingredients, including three tail hairs, for a magic charm to restore them to life, accompanied or aided by Scraps, the Woozy, and the Glass Cat. Ozma later has the statues brought to the palace.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

The terrible accident begins when the Patchwork Girl knocks the Powder of Life from Dr. Pipt's hand, startling Unc Nunkie and Margolotte. Unc Nunkie's movement upsets the Liquid of Petrifaction, which falls on him and Margolotte, while the Powder of Life lands on the phonograph.

Chapter Nine

Ojo tells the Woozy that an accident caused Unc Nunkie and Margolotte's condition and that the three tail hairs are needed to help restore them to life.

Chapter Sixteen

The petrification of Unc Nunkie and Margolotte occurs accidentally when they are turned to marble by the Liquid of Petrification. Ojo searches for the ingredients of a magic charm to restore them, while Scraps, the Woozy, and the Glass Cat accompany or discuss the effort.

Chapter Eighteen

The petrification accident occurred because Dr. Pipt unlawfully kept the Liquid of Petrifaction on his shelf. It transformed his wife Margolotte and Unc Nunkie into marble statues.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

The accident involving Margolotte and Unc Nunkie is discussed as part of the story of Ojo's quest. It left Margolotte and Unc Nunkie as marble statues, which Ozma later has brought to the palace.
