the burglars in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The burglars targeted Acton’s and Cunningham’s houses. At Acton’s house, they ransacked rooms and stole several items; they remained at large and are suspected of the Cunningham incident.

VII. The Reigate Squires

The burglars who entered Acton's house ransacked the library, drawers, and presses before taking an odd volume of Pope's 'Homer,' two plated candlesticks, an ivory letter-weight, a small oak barometer, and a ball of twine. They remained at large, and the same party is suspected in the Cunningham incident.
