L. Frank Baum · 1912

The travelers escape the Blue Country after the Boolooroo makes them slaves. Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill cross the wet, sticky Fog Bank with a big frog's help, reach the Pink Country, and are captured by the Pinkies and taken to Tourmaline.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE PINK COUNTRY

The travelers escape from the Blue Country after being made slaves by the Boolooroo. They pass through the Fog Bank and reach the Pink Country, where the Pinkies capture them and take them to Tourmaline.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The escape through the Fog Bank carries Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill from the Blue Country into the Pink Country. Although the journey is wet and sticky, a big frog helps them get through safely.
