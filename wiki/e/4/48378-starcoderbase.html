4 / 4.1 .. 4.3

StarCoderBase is fine-tuned in 1B, 3B, 7B, and 15.5B parameter variants to measure the impact of model size on final metrics. It is selected because it was pretrained on JSON and multiple programming languages.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

StarCoderBase models from 1B to 15.5B parameters are evaluated with and without a retriever. Larger StarCoderBase models generally improve RAG performance, with StarCoderBase-7B providing the best trade-off because its performance is close to that of the 15.5B model.
