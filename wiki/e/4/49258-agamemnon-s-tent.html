Agamemnon's tent

Also called: Agamemnon’s tent, rich king Agamemnon's tent, tent, tents, the tent Of Agamemnon, the tent of our captivity, the tents of our imperious lords, these tents, yon roof

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, Agamemnon’s tent stands in the Greek camp near Troy and serves as the place where Hecuba and the captive women are held; Talthybius comes from it to announce the captives’ assignments. In The Fall of Troy, Agamemnon's tent is the site of a nighttime feast after the Argives return to the ships, where Achilles and the other mighty warriors remain until dawn.
