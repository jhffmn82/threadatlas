Mister Yoop in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Mister Yoop is a twenty-one-foot-tall, heavy, four-hundred-year-old giant in captivity, fierce and ravenous for meat people and orange marmalade, dressed in pink. In Chapter Twenty, he tries to seize and eat the travelers through bars, but can only grab and throw the Scarecrow and Scraps.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty

Mister Yoop is a twenty-one-foot-tall, very heavy, four-hundred-year-old giant with a fierce temper and a ravenous appetite for meat people and orange marmalade. He tries to seize and eat the travelers, but he can reach only between the bars and throws the Scarecrow and Scraps after grabbing them.

Mr. Yoop in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mr. Yoop is a terrible giant with a bad temper who lived in Yoop Castle, ate taken cows and sheep, damaged houses when angry, and mistreated Mrs. Yoop. Little folks captured him and confined him in a mountain cage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Mr. Yoop was a terrible giant who lived in Yoop Castle and had a bad temper. He took cows and sheep for food and sometimes knocked over houses when angry, so little folks captured him and carried him to a cage somewhere in the mountains. Mrs. Yoop says that he also treated her badly and kicked her shins.
