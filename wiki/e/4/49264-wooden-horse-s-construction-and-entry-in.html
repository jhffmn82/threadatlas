Wooden Horse's construction and entry into Troy

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 4 connections
Also called: Wooden Horse stratagem, Wooden Horse was fashioned, and brought into Troy by her people, fraud of Greece, great work, latent snare, semblance, stratagem, the wooden horse deception, treachery, work for which ye crave

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, it is the deception that presents the horse as a holy image and pledge of peace, concealing Greek armed men inside while the Trojans bring it into the city as a sacred offering, causing the fall of Troy. In The Fall of Troy, it is a warfare and strategy deception in which Achaeans hide warriors inside a seemingly sacred offering, withdraw to Tenedos, and use Sinon's deception to bring the horse into Troy before a signal summons the fleet and releases the warriors.
