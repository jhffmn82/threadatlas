Wooden Horse's construction and entry into Troy

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 4 connections
Also called: Wooden Horse stratagem, Wooden Horse was fashioned, and brought into Troy by her people, fraud of Greece, great work, latent snare, semblance, stratagem, the wooden horse deception, treachery, work for which ye crave
  • In Hecuba and other plays, it is the deception that presents the horse as a holy image and pledge of peace, concealing Greek armed men inside while the Trojans bring it into the city as a sacred offering, causing the fall of Troy.
  • In The Fall of Troy, it is a warfare and strategy deception in which Achaeans hide warriors inside a seemingly sacred offering, withdraw to Tenedos, and use Sinon's deception to bring the horse into Troy before a signal summons the fleet and releases the warriors.

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

The Wooden Horse is a warfare and strategy deception to capture Troy. Achaeans hide warriors inside a seemingly sacred offering, withdraw to Tenedos, and use Sinon's deception to persuade the people of Troy to bring it into their city; a signal then summons the fleet and releases the warriors.

The Wooden Horse's construction and entry into Troy are the subject of Book XII. The contents state that it was fashioned and brought into Troy by the people of Troy.

BOOK XII

The Wooden Horse stratagem requires the Achaeans to hide warriors inside a seemingly sacred offering, withdraw to Tenedos, and persuade the Trojans to bring the Horse into their city. Sinon provides the deception, while a signal will summon the fleet and release the warriors from the Horse.
