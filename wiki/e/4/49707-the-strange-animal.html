Abstract written by a model

An unidentified carnivorous creature, larger than weasels and stoats, has a long body and very short legs inferred from five-padded, long-nailed tracks. It leaves prints, climbs a curtain toward a canary cage, and is carried in Henry Wood’s box.

The strange animal leaves small five-padded footprints with long nails and appears to have climbed the curtain toward a canary cage. Its prints indicate a long body, very short legs, and carnivorous behavior. It travels in Henry Wood’s box, but its species remains unknown.
