Abstract written by a model

Sky Island is a book, following Trot and Cap'n Bill's visit to The Sea Fairies, and is dedicated to Mary Louise Brewster. Its story begins when Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright are carried by the Magic Umbrella toward an island suspended in the sky. The island is surrounded by sky rather than water; its edges are guarded in places by bushes or fences because anyone who steps beyond them tumbles away. It has trees, ponds, houses, and three countries: the Blue Country, the Fog Country, and the Pink Country. The travelers first reach the blue part, where gardens, buildings, vegetation, paths, and water are blue. The Blue Country is ruled by the Boolooroo, whose palace adjoins their landing place. Though he calls the government a republic, he directs elections and punishes disobedience. He and his court call Sky Island the Center of the Universe. The island is a fairy country whose people do not visibly age and cannot die before six hundred years. The Great Fog Bank separates the equal-sized Blue and Pink Countries. Trot and her companions, initially seeking to escape, become involved in the conflicts of both lands. The narrative proceeds through the Pink Country, its queens and tribes, the arrival of Polychrome, war between Pinks and Blues, Cap'n Bill's capture, Trot's invisible adventure, and the conquest of the Blues. After victory, Trot becomes Queen of Sky Island except the Fog Bank, while the Blue and Pink Countries retain separate rulers. Before leaving, she regulates the Pinkies by leaving Rosalie to rule the Pink Country. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright then say goodbye to their friends and fly upward from Sky Island in the Magic Umbrella to return home.

Sky Island

Sky Island is the title of the work. The contents include chapters titled "The Island in the Sky" and "The Ruler of Sky Island."

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

Sky Island is a distant island in the Pacific that Trot wants to visit for a picnic. It is said to have woods, pastures, clear-water springs, cows, goats, and a few people who fish for a living.

THE ISLAND IN THE SKY

Sky Island is revealed to be a real destination different from the first island. The travelers eventually see an island suspended among the sky, with trees, ponds, and houses, and the Magic Umbrella carries them toward its blue part.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

Sky Island is a blue place where the garden, walls, trees, grass, flowers, pebbles, seats, fountain, and water are all blue. The travelers land in the garden adjoining the Boolooroo's palace, which has six hundred tall towers and turrets. Its government is called a republic, although the Boolooroo tells voters whom to elect and punishes those who disobey.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

Sky Island is described by the Boolooroo as the Center of the Universe. Its people believe that a snub nose is a sign of high breeding, and its scientists have supposedly claimed that the Earth is uninhabited.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

Sky Island is a fairy country where people do not show their age in their appearance and cannot die until they have lived six hundred years. Its ruler can impose patching as punishment, and its royal servants and buildings are associated with the color blue.

THE BLUE CITY

Sky Island is divided into the Blue Country and the Pink Country, which are said to be equal in size. The two parts are separated by the Great Fog Bank.

THE TRIBULATION OF TROT

Sky Island is the domain Trot and her companions entered by mistake and from which they originally wanted to leave. Trot says that the Royal Ruler of the Blues has prevented them from departing.

THE PINK COUNTRY

Sky Island contains three countries: the Blue Country, the Fog Country, and the Pink Country. The Pinkies consider the Pink Country the most important.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

Sky Island is the place to which the Magic Umbrella carried Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill. Cap'n Bill says it is not very large and offers few places where the travelers can safely stay.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

Sky Island has sky all around it, and anyone who steps beyond its edge will tumble away and never be heard from again. It contains the Blue Country and Pink Country, with bushes or fences protecting some of its edges.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Trot declares herself Queen of all Sky Island except the Fog Bank. The Pink and Blue Countries remain under separate rulers while Trot retains overall sovereignty.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Sky Island is the realm over which Trot is Queen. Before returning home, Trot says she must straighten out affairs there, including ensuring that the Pinkies have a new queen.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Trot identifies herself as the Queen of Sky Island when announcing that she will leave Rosalie to rule the Pink Country while she returns to Earth.

THE JOURNEY HOME

Sky Island is where Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright say goodbye to Rosalie, Coralie, Tourmaline, the Pinkies, and the Pink Band. They leave it by flying upward in the Magic Umbrella.
