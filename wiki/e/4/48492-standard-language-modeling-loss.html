Abstract written by a model

optimizes student model [using the collected revision trajectory] The standard language modeling loss is applied to the detailed revision trajectory. It optimizes the student model by maximizing the probability of each trajectory token given its preceding tokens, the instruction, and the ground-truth and noise documents.
