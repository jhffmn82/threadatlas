Tourmaline in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Tourmaline is the young, beautiful, light-pink Queen of the Pinkies and sole ruler of the Pink Country. About seventeen, slender and rosy-complexioned, she wears a plain coarse pink robe and a rose-gold brow band bearing a luminous pink jewel. Though her word is law and she holds absolute authority, including the power to enforce laws and decree death when punishment is merited, she is modest, plain, and poor by principle. Living in a plain stone palace, she darns her own stockings and believes a ruler should be a public servant, not rich or falsely admired. As queen, Tourmaline consults the Great Book of Laws when strangers arrive, convenes the twelve Counselors, and seeks a decision on whether the travelers should remain as guests or be thrown over the island’s edge. When the Council divides evenly, she will not cast the deciding vote because she finds it impolite to oppose Counselors, instead asking Rosalie the Witch to decide. She orders the strangers cast away because they do not harmonize with the Pink Country’s color scheme, pressing for immediate execution even in rain. Later, she accepts Polychrome’s reading of the protection law and yields her circlet when Trot is identified as the rightful Queen. After losing the throne, Tourmaline appears in fine robes but declines restoration, saying queenship brought want and misery and that she prefers freedom. Trot orders a pink marble statue of her placed in the Court of the Statues after her death.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

Tourmaline is named in the contents as the Poverty Queen.

THE PINK COUNTRY

Tourmaline is the Queen of the Pinkies and the sole ruler of the Pink Country. Her word is the Law of the Land, and the Pinkies take the travelers to her so she can decide their fate.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

Tourmaline is the young Queen of the Pink Country, about seventeen years old, who lives simply and darns her own stockings. She explains that a ruler is a public servant with power to enforce laws, adjust grievances, and maintain order, but should have no riches, high station, or false adulation. She agrees to search the Great Book for a law concerning the strangers and tells them to return the next morning so she can decree their fate.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

Tourmaline is the Queen acknowledged by both Pink tribes. She consults the Great Book of Laws, summons the Council of twelve, and presents the alternatives of keeping the strangers as honored guests or throwing them over the island’s edge.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

Tourmaline presides over the twelve Counselors' evenly divided vote and refuses to cast the deciding vote herself. She summons Rosalie the Witch, announces the order to throw the three strangers from the island, and insists that it be carried out at once despite the rain.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

Tourmaline leads the travelers through the Pink Country and orders that Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill be thrown over the edge because they do not harmonize with the country's color scheme. She initially argues that the Great Book of Laws permits only Pinkies to remain, but accepts Polychrome's interpretation of the protection law. Tourmaline then relinquishes her circlet when Polychrome identifies Trot as the rightful Queen.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Tourmaline arrives dressed in fine robes and earnestly refuses Trot's offer to make her Queen again, explaining that she is enjoying freedom from the poverty and hardships of the position. Trot orders a pink statue of Tourmaline to be placed in the Court of the Statues.
