May Day in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

May Day is a steamer calling at Belfast, Dublin, and Waterford; Jim Browner leaves aboard it, and Holmes expects it to reach the Thames the next night for Lestrade to meet him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“The case,” said Sherlock Holmes as we chatted over our cigars

The May Day is the steamer aboard which Jim Browner left. Holmes calculates that it will arrive in the Thames the following night, where Lestrade is expected to meet Browner.
