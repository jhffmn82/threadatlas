Abstract written by a model

A multi-agent reinforcement learning training process uses Deep Q-Network to optimize two collaborative policies, repeatedly sampling text pairs, selecting and refining candidates, storing experiences, and converging on validation.

Methodology .. 3.4 / The M-RAG Framework

The training process samples text pairs, constructs states, selects partitions, generates hypotheses, refines memories through repeated candidate selection, and stores agent experiences. It optimizes both agent policies until convergence on a validation set.
