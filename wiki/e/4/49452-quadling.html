Quadling in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A Quadling man living beside the river with his wife and children, he is called the laziest man in all Oz. Though he owns an axe and complains of overexertion, he helps make a raft for six Square Meal Tablets by chopping fallen trees and binding logs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Five

The Quadling man lives beside the river with his wife and children and is described as extremely lazy. He agrees to help make a raft in exchange for six Square Meal Tablets, chops fallen trees into logs, and helps bind them together, though he complains that the work has overexerted him.
