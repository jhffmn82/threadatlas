Prompt translation faces semantic errors, register choices, specialized-term complications, and missing direct equivalents for “You are a helpful assistant”; it resolves these issues by choosing informal style for all cases.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes

Prompt translation raises issues involving incorrect semantics for the assistant’s task, choices between formal and informal register, complications with field-specific terms such as “named entities,” and the absence of a direct translation of “You are a helpful assistant” in some languages. Informal style was chosen for all cases.
