Pollux

mythological figure · 3 works · 9 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: POLLUX, Πολυδεύκης, Πολυδεύκους
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Pollux is Zeus’s divine twin son of Leda and Helen’s brother, who appears with Castor, calms Theoclymenus, and foretells Helen’s return and divine honors.
  • In The Library, Pollux is a boxer and one of the Dioscuri with Castor, who helps recover Helen, marries a daughter of Leucippus, fathers Mnesileus, kills Lynceus, is carried to heaven by Zeus, and refuses immortality while Castor is dead.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Pollux is included with Castor in Simonides’s poem for Scopas and is understood to have summoned Simonides away before the banquet hall collapsed.

Pollux in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Pollux, Zeus’s divine twin son of Leda and Helen’s brother, appears with Castor, calms Theoclymenus, and foretells Helen’s return and divine honors.

Swift bark of Sidon, by whose dashing oars

Pollux appears with Castor and restrains Theoclymenus’s anger through an explanation of fate and divine command. He declares that Helen will return to her husband and later receive divine honors.

Pollux in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Pollux, son of Leda and said also to have been born to Zeus and Leda, was a boxer and one of the Dioscuri with Castor. With Castor he recovered Helen, married a daughter of Leucippus, and had Mnesileus by Phoebe. He killed Lynceus with a spear, was carried to heaven by Zeus, and refused immortality while Castor was dead.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Εἰσὶ δὲ οἱ λέγοντες ᾿Αφαρέα μὲν καὶ Λεύκιππον

Pollux was said to have been born to Zeus and Leda. He practiced boxing, and his bravery with Castor led both brothers to be called the Dioscuri. He and Castor recovered Helen from Aphidnae after Theseus carried her away.

Of the sons born to Leda Castor practised the art

Pollux practised boxing and, with Castor, was called one of the Dioscuri. He carried off and married a daughter of Leucippus and had Mnesileus by Phoebe. He killed Lynceus with a spear, was later taken up to heaven by Zeus, and refused to accept immortality while Castor was dead.
