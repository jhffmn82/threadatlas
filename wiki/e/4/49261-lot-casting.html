lot-casting in The Trojan Women of Euripides

Euripides · -0414

determines the Trojan women's destinations [after the fall of Troy] Talthybius announces that the lots have been cast to determine the women's destinations. The lots separate them among Agamemnon, Pyrrhus, Odysseus, and the place where Polyxena is kept.

lottery assigning the captives in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

The lottery assigning the captives determines separate Greek masters for the Trojan women. Cassandra goes to Agamemnon, Andromache to Achilles’ son, Hecuba to Ulysses, and Polyxena to Achilles’ tomb.
