lot-casting

Also called: The lots are cast, chance distributes, lottery assigning the captives, portion out, the lot, the lots are cast, what lot hath any

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, it determines separate Greek masters for the Trojan women. In The Trojan Women of Euripides, it determines the Trojan women's destinations after the fall of Troy.
