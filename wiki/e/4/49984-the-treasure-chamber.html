the Treasure Chamber in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Treasure Chamber is a dark, crowded, guarded room in the palace, holding the Boolooroo's accumulated riches, the Book of Records, and the Magic Umbrella. The Boolooroo keeps its key, Jimfred Jinksjones guards outside, and the Blue Wolf guards inside. Jimfred guards the entrance until Button-Bright deceives him into leaving; Button-Bright then unlocks and enters the chamber. Among gold, jewels, ornaments, cloths, furniture, vases, and bric-a-brac, he searches for the Magic Umbrella but does not find it.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BLUE CITY

The Treasure Chamber is in the palace and contains the Book of Records and Button-Bright's Magic Umbrella. It is guarded outside by Jimfred Jinksjones and inside by the Blue Wolf, while the Boolooroo keeps its key in his pocket.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Royal Treasure Chamber is a guarded room in the palace containing the Magic Umbrella and a ferocious Blue Wolf. Jimfred guards its entrance until Button-Bright deceives him into leaving, after which Button-Bright unlocks the chamber and enters.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Treasure Chamber is dark and filled with the Boolooroo's accumulated riches, including gold, jewels, ornaments, cloths, furniture, vases, and bric-a-brac. Button-Bright searches it for the Magic Umbrella but does not find it.
