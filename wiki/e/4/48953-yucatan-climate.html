Yucatan climate is mild, comparatively healthy tropical, averaging about 80°F with little variation; it reaches about 96°F in July and August and seldom falls below 65°F in December. A dry season runs January–June; rainy moisture and poor drainage contribute to disease.

Vestiges of the Mayas

Yucatan has a mild, comparatively healthy tropical climate with moisture-laden air and an average temperature of about 80 degrees Fahrenheit. Temperatures may reach 96 degrees in July and August and rarely fall below 65 degrees in December, while rainy-season moisture and poor drainage contribute to disease.
