the Moriarty criminal organization in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

A powerful, carefully protected criminal syndicate, it uses numerous organized agents to commit forgery, robbery, murder, theft, and other planned crimes while Moriarty remains hidden.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII. The Final Problem

The Moriarty criminal organization is described as a powerful, carefully protected network with numerous agents who carry out crimes planned by Professor Moriarty. It is linked to forgery, robbery, murder, theft, and other offenses, while Moriarty remains hidden behind the activities of his agents.
