Abstract written by a model

In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, the Moriarty criminal organization uses numerous organized agents to commit forgery, robbery, murder, theft, and other planned crimes while Moriarty remains hidden. In The Valley of Fear, Moriarty's criminal organization unites pickpockets, blackmailers, and card sharpers under death-enforced discipline, using formidable machinery and extravagant resources to locate and destroy John Douglas.

XII. The Final Problem

The Moriarty criminal organization is described as a powerful, carefully protected network with numerous agents who carry out crimes planned by Professor Moriarty. It is linked to forgery, robbery, murder, theft, and other offenses, while Moriarty remains hidden behind the activities of his agents.
