drop of oil from a live man's body in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A drop of oil from a live man's body is one of five ingredients required by the Crooked Magician's magic charm. Though the Shaggy Man considers it improbable to find, Ojo keeps searching and captures a drop from the Tin Woodman's knee joint in a crystal vial, fulfilling the requirement.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

A drop of oil from a live man's body is one of the five things required by the Crooked Magician. The Shaggy Man doubts that such oil exists, but Ojo continues searching because the Crooked Magician said the recipe would not require something that could not be found.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

Ojo catches a drop of oil from the Tin Woodman's knee joint in a crystal vial. This fulfills one of the ingredients required for the magic charm.
