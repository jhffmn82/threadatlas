Abstract written by a model

The Gloria Scott is an old, heavy-bowed, broad-beamed five-hundred-ton barque, formerly serving the Chinese tea trade and later transporting prisoners. It leaves Falmouth bound for Australia with thirty-eight convicts, twenty-six crewmen, eighteen soldiers, officers, a doctor, a chaplain, and four warders. About three weeks into the voyage, a violent mutiny captures the ship. After an escape boat departs, the first mate threatens to use a powder barrel, and an explosion in the after-hold destroys the vessel and nearly everyone aboard. The Admiralty records it as lost at sea, unaware of its true fate.
