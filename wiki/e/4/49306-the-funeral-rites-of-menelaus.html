Abstract written by a model

Menelaus's funeral rites are a claimed Greek ceremony for one drowned: an empty garment is buried and offerings cast into the sea, including blood, animals, arms, flowers, a bier, ship, and sailors.

Helen and Menelaus claim that a drowned Greek is honored by burying an empty garment and casting offerings into the sea. Theoclymenus agrees to provide blood, animals, weapons, flowers, a bier, a ship, and sailors for these supposed rites.
