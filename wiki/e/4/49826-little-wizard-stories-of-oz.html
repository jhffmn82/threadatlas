Abstract written by a model

This document is a sequence of six Oz adventures centered on familiar inhabitants of the Emerald City and the wider Land of Oz. It begins with the Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger setting out to behave ferociously, only to rescue a lost baby and injured mother; her praise makes them reject cruelty. Dorothy and Toto then face the giant Crinklink while traveling alone, but Crinklink proves to be the Wizard of Oz in disguise, teaching Dorothy to return safely to the Emerald City. Tiktok seeks repairs from the Nome King, is smashed after insulting him, and is restored by Kaliko; Tiktok’s apparent return as a phantom frightens the King into apologizing and sending jewels to Princess Ozma. Ozma and the Wizard next tour the Land of Oz to ensure that people are happy and wrongs are corrected. They stop three Imps from tormenting travelers and a family, ultimately confining their spirits in buttons until they repent. Ozma later uses the Magic Picture to discover two lost children, and Jack Pumpkinhead and the Sawhorse rescue them from the King of all the Squirrels. When Jack’s head is smashed, the little Wizard saves the children and gives Jack a new pumpkin head. Finally, the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman suffer a river accident, are trapped by crows, and are rescued by the Wizard, who restores the Tin Woodman with oil. Across the episodes, Oz’s friends repeatedly turn danger into rescue, correction, repair, and safe return.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

THE COWARDLY LION AND THE HUNGRY TIGER

In the Emerald City, the Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger leave the Throne Room intending to frighten people by tearing apart a person and eating a fat baby. They encounter a lost baby and its injured mother, but instead comfort and rescue them. The mother thanks the beasts and says they have never had an evil thought, which shames them. The Lion and Tiger conclude that it is better to be cowardly or hungry than to do wrong or be cruel.

LITTLE DOROTHY AND TOTO

Dorothy and Toto travel through the wild wooded hills at the southeast of Oz and meet a tiny ferryman who claims he can carry them across a black-water lake. The ferryman becomes the giant Crinklink, carries them to Crinklink's log castle, and orders Dorothy to wash a huge pile of dishes. While Dorothy works, Toto attacks the sleeping Crinklink, who is revealed to be the Wizard of Oz in disguise. The Wizard explains that he staged the danger to teach Dorothy not to travel alone through the Land of Oz, and Dorothy decides to return to the Emerald City of Oz.

TIKTOK AND THE NOME KING

Tiktok visits the Nome King to request new springs and a cog-wheel for his thought and speech machinery. When Tiktok insults the Nome King, the angry King smashes him into scattered pieces with a mace. Kaliko repairs Tiktok, who then frightens the King by appearing to be a phantom. The Nome King apologizes by giving Kaliko a raise and sending Tiktok back to the Land of Oz with jewels for Princess Ozma of Oz.

OZMA AND THE LITTLE WIZARD

Princess Ozma and the Wizard of Oz travel from the Emerald City through the Land of Oz to learn whether its people are happy and to correct any wrongs. In a valley in the mountains, they meet three mischievous Imps who torment travelers and a nearby family. The Wizard successively transforms the Imps into thorny bushes, white pigs, blue doves, and finally three buttons containing their spirits. Ozma and the Wizard plan to keep the buttons until the Imps repent and can be restored as good citizens.

JACK PUMPKINHEAD AND THE SAWHORSE

Ozma sees through the Magic Picture that a little girl and a little boy are lost in a western forest, so she sends Jack Pumpkinhead to rescue them. Jack rides the Sawhorse to the forest, finds the children bound by the King of all the Squirrels, and frees them after learning they took nuts because they were hungry. A falling limb smashes Jack's pumpkin head, leaving him helpless, but the Sawhorse carries him and the children until the little Wizard arrives. The little Wizard returns the children to their friends and gives Jack a new pumpkin head at his home.

THE SCARECROW AND THE TIN WOODMAN

The Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman take the Scarecrow's corncob boat up a forest river and collide with a rock, sending the Tin Woodman to the bottom. The Scarecrow tries to rescue him, but the crows pull both friends into a tall dead tree and leave them hanging. After the Scarecrow drops the anchor and both fall, the Wizard of Oz arrives, frees the Scarecrow, and helps restore the Tin Woodman's movement with the oil-can. The friends return safely to the Scarecrow's palace, and the Tin Woodman is careful not to stand in the boat again.
