Tiggle in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Tiggle is a servant and prisoner of the Boolooroo who works in the royal pantry mixing royal nectar. Bound captive in the Room of the Great Knife, he is meant to be patched to Cap'n Bill, but Ghip-Ghisizzle replaces him and Trot frees him. Tiggle hides in a cellar of a deserted house to evade the Boolooroo's soldiers. A former servant of the former Boolooroo, he later returns after hearing the new Boolooroo wants him and recalls discarding the Magic Umbrella behind the palace.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

Tiggle is bound in the Room of the Great Knife, but Trot frees him. He promises to hide in a house where the Boolooroo's soldiers cannot find him, leaving the Boolooroo without the intended mate for Cap'n Bill.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Tiggle, a former servant of the old Boolooroo, had escaped and hidden in the cellar of a deserted house. After hearing that the new Boolooroo wanted him, he returns and recalls throwing the umbrella onto a rubbish heap in an alley behind the palace.
