Coralie

Also called: Captain Coralie, the Pinky woman, the woman, their hostess

Abstract written by a model

Coralie is a prominent Pinkie woman of the Sunset Tribe who serves as captain, Commander in Chief, and spokesperson. She hosts Trot and other prisoners kindly, argues to the Council that they are harmless and deserve a chance to return home, and later captures Ghip-Ghisizzle after his flight from the City.

Coralie is in one work on this site, Sky Island. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

Coralie, a prominent woman of the Pinkies and a member of the Sunset Tribe, hosts the prisoners in her comfortable home. She argues before the Council that the strangers are harmless, have done no wrong, and might eventually find a way home.
