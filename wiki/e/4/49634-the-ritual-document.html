VI. The Musgrave Ritual

The ritual document is a copy of the questions and answers used in the Musgrave family’s ancient observance. Brunton secretly removes it from a bureau and studies it in the library, prompting Reginald Musgrave to dismiss him; Holmes asks to examine it.
