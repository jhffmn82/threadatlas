Abstract written by a model

In "LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering," QASPER is an English dataset of lengthy contexts included in LRGinstruction construction to help LLMs learn long-context question-answering styles. In "Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?," Qasper is one of the seven LongBench datasets used to evaluate RAG and long-context prompting, included among the English, real, query-based tasks. In "Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Qasper is a dataset of human-written question-answer pairs based on NLP papers, used with NaturalQuestions to evaluate real-instruction chunking.
