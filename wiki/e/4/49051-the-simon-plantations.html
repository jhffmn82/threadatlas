the Simon plantations in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

S. S. Simon's four one-thousand-acre plantations organize enslaved labor by an early bell before daylight; President Lincoln's proclamation of freedom abolishes the enslaved people's monetary value, transforming what the plantations contain.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

SCENE SIXTH.

S. S. Simon's plantations consist of four plantations of one thousand acres each. Their labor is organized through an early morning bell that calls the slaves to work before daylight.

The federals had fortified Helena, a point three hundred miles above Vicks burg

The Simon plantations were affected by President Lincoln's proclamation of freedom, which removed the monetary value of the slaves held there.
