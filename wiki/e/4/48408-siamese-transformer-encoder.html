siamese transformer encoder in Reducing hallucination in structured outputs via Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Patrice Béchard · 2024

The siamese transformer encoder is a fine-tuned 110M-parameter encoder for retrieval. It encodes natural-language queries and step or table JSON objects with mean pooling, then normalizes embeddings to norm 1. It retrieves the top 15 steps and top 10 tables, evaluated by Recall@15 and Recall@10 against ground-truth workflow JSON. Scaling an off-the-shelf encoder helps little; fine-tuning matters most and supports GPU or CPU deployment.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

3 .. 3.2

The siamese transformer encoder encodes user queries and step or table JSON objects using mean pooling. A normalization layer produces embeddings with norm 1.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.3

The retriever retrieves the top 15 steps and top 10 tables from their respective indices for a natural language requirement. Its coverage is evaluated by checking whether the retrieved items include the steps and required table in the ground-truth workflow JSON document.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

The retriever encoder shows limited improvement when scaled as an off-the-shelf encoder, but fine-tuning substantially improves retrieval. The system uses a fine-tuned 110M-parameter encoder for deployment, and its small size permits deployment on the same GPU as the LLM or on CPU.
