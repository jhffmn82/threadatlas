In An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation, noise from retrieval includes toxic content and can influence generated content. In BlendFilter: Advancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models via Query Generation Blending and Knowledge Filtering, noise in retrieved knowledge documents is irrelevant or misleading information among the top-K results that can cause LLM inaccuracies. In Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain, retrieval noise reduces answer-generation quality, and models are generally sensitive to noisy documents in the retrieved list. In RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering, noisy context from retrieved documents may interfere with LLMs and hurt question-answering performance. In MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation, noisy documents are irrelevant or misleading documents retrieved during the retrieval stage of a RAG system and are targets for removal by MAIN-RAG. In MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation, noisy document retrieval is the challenge addressed by MAIN-RAG, which aims to enhance recall of relevant information while minimizing irrelevant content. In Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability, irrelevant documents are examined in a TQA filtering ablation, where filtering reduces correct answers and hallucinations while increasing IDK responses.

noisy documents in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where noisy documents first appears

Abstract written by a model

Noisy documents are irrelevant or misleading documents retrieved during the retrieval stage of a RAG system. They can mislead language models, produce incorrect answers, reduce response accuracy, degrade performance, increase computational overhead, and undermine reliability. MAIN-RAG targets them for removal while maintaining high recall of relevant documents. It filters them using relevance scores and an adaptive judge bar. Compared with relevant documents, their scores are more uniformly distributed and have a larger standard deviation, reflecting lower LLM confidence, including misjudgment by Mistral-7B. Their score distribution is considered when adapting the judge bar across queries.

irrelevant documents in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where irrelevant documents first appears

Abstract written by a model

For irrelevant documents in a TQA filtering ablation on Llama3-8B-Instruct, filtering lowers correct answers from 26.07% to 4.16% and hallucinations from 22.89% to 4.53%, while raising IDK from 50.92% to 91.19%, replacing ungrounded responses with uncertainty.

C

Irrelevant documents often lead LLMs to produce correct or hallucinated responses that are not grounded in the retrieved documents. Filtering reduces these misaligned responses and replaces them with IDK responses.
