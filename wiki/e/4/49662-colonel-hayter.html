Abstract written by a model

Colonel Hayter is an old, experienced soldier who owns a bachelor house near Reigate, including a smoking-room. He hosts Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson, discusses the Acton burglary, the Cunningham burglary and murder, and a lawsuit, accompanies their visit to the Cunninghams, then admires Holmes’s explanation and methods.

VII. The Reigate Squires

Colonel Hayter hosts Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson at his bachelor house near Reigate. He tells them about the Acton burglary, discusses the Cunningham burglary and murder, and agrees to accompany Holmes, Watson, and Inspector Forrester to Cunningham's house.
