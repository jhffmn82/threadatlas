Differential Privacy

topic · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: DP, deferentially private, differential privacy

Abstract written by a model

In "The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)," differential privacy is identified as a potential, effective strategy for protecting retrieval data and is cited with Feyisetan et al., Utpala et al., and Carvalho et al. In "Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data," Differential Privacy is a rigorous method integrated with synthetic data for in-context demonstrations, aiming to make each data item indistinguishable, providing protection stronger than the targeted and untargeted attacks considered, and generating private demonstrations and differentially private data.

Related Works

Differential privacy is addressed by Tang et al. (2023), who generate private in-context demonstrations from a private dataset and provide a differential privacy guarantee. Xie et al. introduce a private evolution algorithm to generate differentially private data.

Limitations

Differential Privacy is described as a rigorous method that aims to make each data item indistinguishable. It provides stronger protection than the targeted and untargeted attacks considered in the research.
