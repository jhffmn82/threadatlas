the vessel in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

A wine-filled drinking vessel, kept privately by Polyphemus, is brought from the cave by Ulysses and served to Polyphemus after he agrees not to share it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ULYSSES, CHORUS.

Ulysses brings the vessel of wine from the cave and serves it to Polyphemus. Polyphemus first asks whether he must share it with his brothers, but Ulysses persuades him to keep it for himself.
