Aldershot in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Aldershot is the setting for the first battalion of the Royal Mallows, the north camp, Lachine, and soldiers including Henry Wood. Colonel Barclay dies there, prompting Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson to investigate. They travel there, find Wood lodging and meet him and Major Murphy; after the inquest, Holmes decides they are no longer needed.
