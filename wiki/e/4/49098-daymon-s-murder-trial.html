Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

At Daymon's murder trial, the state presented a shoe identified as belonging to his wife and a body from Beargrass Creek wearing a dress identified as hers. Daymon had no defense counsel until the judge appointed attorneys, and the jury found him guilty before his wife entered the courtroom alive.
