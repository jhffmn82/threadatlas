SOLAR-10.7B in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where SOLAR-10.7B first appears

Abstract written by a model

SOLAR-10.7B is a state-of-the-art, instruction-tuned, English-centric generator model selected for multilingual generation experiments. Evaluated across system prompts and retrieval settings, it often replies in English by default. Advanced prompts improve language behavior: it performs strongly for French and Russian, and Korean improves when the response language and translated user-language prompt are explicit, though Korean Correct Language Rate remains below the desired level.

opening .. opening

SOLAR-10.7B is evaluated across system prompts and retrieval settings. It often performs strongly for French and Russian, while Korean performance improves markedly when the response language and translated user-language prompt are explicitly specified.

Results and discussion .. opening

SOLAR-10.7B often replies in English with the default system prompt, but advanced prompting improves its language behavior. It performs strongly for French and Russian, while its English recall exceeds that of Command-R-35B and its Korean Correct Language Rate remains below the desired level.
