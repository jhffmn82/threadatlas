Quetzalcoatl

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Kukulcan, the winged serpent

Abstract written by a model

Quetzalcoatl is an Aztec and Maya culture hero, identified with the winged serpent and with Kukulcan. Worshipped by Mexicans as Guetzalcoalt and by Quiches as Cucumatz, he may have been Chaacmol's father or ancestor and became associated with feather-covered serpent emblems. His traditions are compared with the civilizing deeds of Osiris and Kukulean.

Quetzalcoatl is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
