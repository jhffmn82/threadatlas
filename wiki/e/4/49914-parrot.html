BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The blue parrot escapes from the palace and follows Trot. It finds her, lands on her shoulder, speaks in rhyme, barks like a dog, and is described by Cap'n Bill as an unusually bright bird.
