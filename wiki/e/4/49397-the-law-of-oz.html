Abstract written by a model

The Law of Oz is a body of rules meant to protect the people and guard their welfare, with willful violation risking arrest. It includes a prohibition on picking six-leaved clovers. Ojo the Unlucky is accused of breaking the Law by picking one, despite Scraps's objection, after being warned by the Shaggy Man. He is arrested and is to be tried and required to make amends if guilty. Ozma says even seemingly foolish laws should not be disobeyed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

The Laws of Oz are presented as rules whose willful violation can result in arrest. The Soldier with the Green Whiskers says Ojo has broken one of them, while Scraps insists that he has broken no Law.

Chapter Fifteen

The Law of Oz is broken by Ojo when he picks the six-leaved clover. The Soldier identifies Ojo's offense only as breaking a Law, and Tollydiggle says Ojo will be tried and required to make amends if found guilty.

Chapter Sixteen

The Law of Oz forbids picking a six-leaved clover. The Shaggy Man warned Ojo about this prohibition, and Ojo's alleged violation leads to his arrest.

Chapter Eighteen

The Laws of Oz are presented as rules intended to protect the people and guard their welfare. Ozma says that disobeying a law is wrong, even when the law initially seems foolish to a stranger.
