Cyclops in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Cyclops are one-eyed sons of Neptune, shepherds living in caves without cities or shared government. They eat milk, cheese, sheep, and strangers, dwelling in Polyphemus’s cave, rejecting mutual rule and democracy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE CYCLOPS.

The Cyclops are one-eyed sons of Neptune who inhabit the caves of Mount Ætna. They live as shepherds without cities or shared government, eat milk, cheese, and sheep, and are said to regard the flesh of strangers as delicious food.

Cyclopes in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Cyclopes are inhuman people who live in mountain-top caves. They neither plant nor plough, instead living on wild wheat, barley, and grapes. They have no laws, assemblies, ships, shipwrights, or travel between cities or overseas; each governs his own family. They receive prophecies from Telemus until he grows old. When Polyphemus cries out, neighboring Cyclopes come but leave after misunderstanding him.

the Cyclops in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

The Cyclops are a huge, lawless pastoral race in Sicily, living in mountain caves from spontaneous produce and flocks. They have one central forehead eye, are associated with Vulcan as workmen, and include the fierce, violent Polyphemus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE VII. [XIII.719-897]

The Cyclops are described as a race of huge, lawless, pastoral beings who live in mountain caves and have one eye in the middle of the forehead. Polyphemus is presented as one of them, feared for his fierceness and violence.

Cyclopes in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Cyclopes are one-eyed human giants, sole possessors of an island near Sicily, living in caves as shepherds on wild produce and flock yields. They answer Polyphemus's cries, then leave when told Noman hurt him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXII

The Cyclopes are one-eyed giants who live in caves, shepherd flocks, and feed on the island's wild products. They answer Polyphemus's cries but leave after hearing that Noman has hurt him.
