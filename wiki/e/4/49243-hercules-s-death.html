Hercules's death

event · 4 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Hercules’ death, consumed him, death, death of Hercules, died at Trachyn, dying, end his life, expires in the flames, the death of Hercules, torn to death
  • In Heroides and Amores, Hercules's death is caused by a poisoned cloak, whose poison comes from Nessus's blood; Deianira imagines him being torn apart on Oeta and fears surviving as the cause of his death.
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Hercules's death occurs in the underworld after he enters Pluto's mansion, where the Chorus mourns his fate and describes his friends as dismayed.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hercules's death occurs on Œta after the poisoned garment causes unbearable agony; flames consume his mortal part while Jupiter preserves and receives his immortal part.
  • In The Library, Hercules's death follows the corrosion of his body by Hydra's poison in the tunic; he is taken to a pyre on Mount Oeta, and a cloud with thunder carries him to heaven.
