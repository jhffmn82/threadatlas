3 / 3.1 .. 3.2

LRGinstruction is a small, high-quality instruction-following dataset created with ChatGLM3-32B-128k as the teacher LLM. It is constructed from training sets of HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MusiQue, and QASPER to support supervised fine-tuning of LongRAG components. The text reports that 2600 samples are sufficient to fine-tune the component LLMs effectively for long-context question-answering tasks.
