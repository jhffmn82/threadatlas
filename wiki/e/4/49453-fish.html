Chapter Twenty-Six

Ojo catches a great fish with a gold nail bent into a hook and baited with bread. The fish pulls the raft forward strongly enough to overcome the reversed current, but it later grows weak and drags the raft toward shore. Ojo cuts the clothesline, freeing the fish before the raft grounds.
