Multilingual RAG pipeline

The multilingual setting includes both monolingual and cross-lingual retrieval, depending on whether the query language matches the collection language. The work uses multilingual retrievers and rerankers and also evaluates a baseline that translates queries with NLLB-600M.

Results and discussion .. opening

Cross-lingual retrieval is evaluated with English documents and non-English user queries. BGE-m3 enables reliable retrieval in this setting and outperforms SPLADE coupled with query translation, although performance under multilingual domain shift remains unexplored.
