Abstract written by a model

Silver Blaze is Colonel Ross’s five-year-old racehorse, descended from Isonomy stock, a celebrated and reliable favourite for the Wessex Cup. Known by his white forehead and mottled off-foreleg, he has won prizes for Colonel Ross and is favoured by the racing public. After John Straker’s murder, Silver Blaze disappears from King’s Pyland, having been seen near the place where Straker died. Sherlock Holmes follows his tracks to Mapleton stables, where Silas Brown conceals him and his white markings are disguised. Holmes ensures that Silver Blaze starts on Tuesday in Colonel Ross’s black cap and red jacket. Recognized despite the disguise, he wins the Wessex Plate by six lengths. He is then revealed to have killed Straker by kicking him in self-defence.

Silver Blaze is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

I. Silver Blaze

Silver Blaze is Colonel Ross’s five-year-old racehorse from the Isonomy stock and the leading favourite for the Wessex Cup. He disappears from King’s Pyland after being seen in the hollow where John Straker was killed, and no one has located him.

It was evening before we reached the little town of Tavistock,

Silver Blaze is the missing favorite for the Cup. Sherlock Holmes follows his tracks to Mapleton stables and concludes that Silas Brown is hiding him there.

“My friend and I return to town by the night-express,” said

Sherlock Holmes expresses confidence that the horse will start on Tuesday. Colonel Ross would prefer possession of the horse rather than Holmes’s assurance that it will run.

Four days later Holmes and I were again in the train, bound for

Silver Blaze is disguised as a powerful bay horse by having his white markings concealed, but Holmes recognizes him and enters him in the Wessex Plate. He wins the race by six lengths and is revealed to have killed John Straker by kicking him in self-defence.
