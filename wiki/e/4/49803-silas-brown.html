Silas Brown in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Silas Brown, trainer at the Mapleton stables, holds large Cup bets and has Silver Blaze concealed at Mapleton. After finding the horse wandering on the moor, he hides it; Holmes discovers this and compels him to keep it safe.

Silas Brown, the trainer at Mapleton stables, has a financial interest in Silver Blaze’s disappearance and was no friend to John Straker. Holmes concludes that Brown found Silver Blaze wandering on the moor, concealed him at Mapleton, and compels Brown to keep the horse safe.
