Abstract written by a model

Silas Brown, trainer at the Mapleton stables, holds large Cup bets and has Silver Blaze concealed at Mapleton. After finding the horse wandering on the moor, he hides it; Holmes discovers this and compels him to keep it safe.

Silas Brown is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
