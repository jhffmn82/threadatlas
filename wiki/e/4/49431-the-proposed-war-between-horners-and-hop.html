the proposed war between Horners and Hoppers in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A dangerous war between the Hoppers and Horners is proposed after the Hoppers misunderstand a Horner joke criticizing their one leg as an insult. Though Hip Hopper expects victory, both sides fear fighting, especially the Horners' sharp horns. Dorothy persuades the Hoppers to laugh at Diksey's joke and ask him not to repeat it, preventing the war.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The Hoppers and Horners are at war because a Horner insulted the Hoppers' understanding by criticizing their having only one leg. Hip Hopper expects the Hoppers to conquer the Horners that afternoon.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The war begins because the Hoppers consider a Horner’s joke about their one leg an insult. Both sides would prefer to avoid fighting because the Hoppers fear the Horners’ sharp horns and the Horners do not want a dangerous war.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The Hoppers declare war because they misunderstand and are angered by Diksey's joke. Dorothy helps prevent the conflict by persuading the Hoppers to laugh at the joke and ask Diksey not to repeat it.
