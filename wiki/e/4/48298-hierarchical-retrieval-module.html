hierarchical retrieval module

technology · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: hierarchical two-step search, retrieval module

Abstract written by a model

The hierarchical retrieval module is an augmentation component for a multimodal large language model. It supplies appropriate external document knowledge, supporting diverse responses and assessment of retrieved content’s importance. It proceeds in two searches: image-to-title similarity selects documents, then the question selects passages within them.

1. Introduction

The retrieval module retrieves appropriate information from an external knowledge base of documents and supplies that information to an MLLM. It is designed to help the model use diverse information in its responses and learn to assess the relative importance of retrieved content.
