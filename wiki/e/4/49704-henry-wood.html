Henry Wood

Also called: Corporal Henry Wood, Harry Wood, Mr. Henry Wood, Wood, the crippled wretch, the fellow, this man

Abstract written by a model

Henry Wood is a physically deformed former corporal of the 117th Foot, now a conjurer and travelling performer living in Hudson Street and recently in Aldershot. He carries a box containing Teddy, a strange animal used in his tricks, and uses a stick for walking. Before the Mutiny he loved Nancy Devoy Barclay, but James Barclay betrayed him, leading to his capture by Indian rebels, torture, and permanent deformity. On meeting Nancy again, Wood followed her and Miss Morrison, watched the Barclays quarrel, and saw James Barclay die after seeing him. Wood entered the morning-room, took Nancy Barclay's key, dropped his stick, and fled.

Henry Wood is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Henry Wood is a deformed conjurer and performer who has recently arrived in Aldershot. He encounters Mrs. Barclay in Hudson Street, carries a box containing an unusual creature, and is later traced by Holmes to lodgings there. Holmes believes Wood followed Mrs. Barclay and Miss Morrison, watched the Barclay quarrel, entered the morning-room, and removed the key.

Henry Wood, formerly a corporal in the 117th Foot, tells Holmes that James Barclay betrayed him during the Mutiny, causing his capture, torture, and permanent deformity. He says he encountered Nancy Barclay again, followed her home, and witnessed Barclay die after seeing him; he then took the door key, dropped his stick, and fled.
