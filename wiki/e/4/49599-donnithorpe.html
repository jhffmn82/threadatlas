Donnithorpe in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Donnithorpe is a Norfolk home in the Broads near Langmere, where Holmes vacationed with the Trevors before Hudson abusively lived there.

V. The “_Gloria Scott_”

Donnithorpe was the Norfolk home of Mr. Trevor and Victor Trevor, where Sherlock Holmes spent part of the long vacation. Hudson later lived and acted abusively within the household there.
