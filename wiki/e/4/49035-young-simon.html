Young Simon in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Young Simon, the only son of S. S. Simon by a second wife, is a wealthy cotton planter who inherits four thousand acres of plantations, more than six hundred slaves, and an estate worth more than a million dollars. His mother is dead, and he has poor health and little family knowledge. In the last stage of consumption, he travels toward New Orleans and the West Indies for relief, learns Roxie Daymon’s family mystery, becomes engaged to her, returns to Chicago, dictates a will in her favor, and dies shortly afterward.
