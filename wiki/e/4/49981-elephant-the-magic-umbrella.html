THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The elephant grows from the Magic Umbrella after its carved head's eyes glow, its trunk sways, and the umbrella swells. It charges the frogs, leads the travelers through the fog, and then shrinks back into the umbrella.
