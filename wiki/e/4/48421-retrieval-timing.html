retrieval timing

topic · 3 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Timing Selection, When to Retrieve, determining whether to retrieve, retrieval timing judgement, retrieval timing judgements, retrieval trigger timing, timing of retrieval, timing of retrieval augmentation
  • In Unified Active Retrieval for Retrieval Augmented Generation, retrieval timing judgement is the task of deciding when retrieval should be applied, and Unified Active Retrieval improves it compared with existing work.
  • In DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models, retrieval timing strongly affects dynamic RAG performance, and DRAGIN’s timing method outperforms FLARE, FL-RAG, and FS-RAG on IIRC when query formulation is held constant.
  • In DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation, retrieval timing concerns when to activate retrieval during or before language-model generation; DioR uses early and real-time detection to select timing instead of static rules.

Introduction

Retrieval timing concerns the decision about when to activate retrieval during or before language-model generation. Existing Dynamic RAG methods often use static rules, such as a low token-generation probability, while DioR uses early and real-time detection to select timing.
