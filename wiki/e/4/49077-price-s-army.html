1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

Price’s army is an unpaid and poorly equipped volunteer force made up of people from many occupations and ages. It survives on irregular provisions, captures weapons and equipment from the Federals, fights across Missouri, and later crosses the Mississippi River.
