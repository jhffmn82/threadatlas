In Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa, the American continent is the starting point for a proposed westward route toward Asia and the valley of the Nile; its mounds and monuments are described as early or comparatively rude architectural examples, and its ruined tropical cities may predate Egypt’s oldest monuments. In The Valley of Fear, America is the place where Mr. Douglas lived before his marriage, and where Mrs. Douglas believes events left him in danger from powerful enemies.

Abstract written by a model

The American continent is presented as the starting point for a proposed westward route followed by the mound builders toward Asia and the valley of the Nile. Its mounds and monuments are described as early or comparatively rude examples of architectural forms later developed elsewhere. America is described as the western continent where the Mayas were indigenous. The text says that the ruined cities of tropical America may be older than the most ancient monuments of Egypt.
