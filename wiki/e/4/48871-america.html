American continent in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

The American continent is presented as the starting point for a proposed westward route followed by the mound builders toward Asia and the valley of the Nile. Its mounds and monuments are described as early or comparatively rude examples of architectural forms later developed elsewhere. America is described as the western continent where the Mayas were indigenous. The text says that the ruined cities of tropical America may be older than the most ancient monuments of Egypt.
