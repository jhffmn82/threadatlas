Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The prisoners plan to leave through the street door of Cap'n Bill's room and open the Magic Umbrella so they can travel home. Button-Bright begins the attempt by stealing the Treasure Chamber key, deceiving Jimfred, and entering the guarded chamber while Trot and Cap'n Bill wait for him.
