King’s Pyland

Abstract written by a model

King’s Pyland is Colonel Ross’s small Devonshire training-stable and Silver Blaze’s home, containing four horses and guarded by stable lads. A stranger’s visit precedes Silver Blaze’s disappearance and John Straker’s murder on the surrounding moor. Holmes finds Silver Blaze is not there, then reconstructs that Straker drugged the stable-boy, removed the horse, and tried to injure it.

King’s Pyland is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

I. Silver Blaze

King’s Pyland is Colonel Ross’s training-stable, containing four horses and guarded at night by stable lads. A stranger visits its stable, Silver Blaze disappears from it, and John Straker’s murder is connected to the surrounding moor.

It was evening before we reached the little town of Tavistock,

King’s Pyland is the stable from which Silver Blaze disappeared and where John Straker was employed. Holmes reasons that a loose horse would likely return there or go to Mapleton, but establishes that Silver Blaze is not at King’s Pyland.
