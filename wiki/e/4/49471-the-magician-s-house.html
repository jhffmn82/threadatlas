A large, round, blue house in a mountain clearing in the Country of the Gillikins, surrounded by forest; Dr. Pipt and Margolotte live there amid an abundant garden and a workshop with bubbling kettles.

Narrative, section by section

Chapter Two

Dr. Pipt's house is a large, round, blue house in a mountain clearing surrounded by forest. Its garden contains blue vegetables, flowers, bun-trees, cake-trees, cream-puff bushes, buttercups, and chocolate-caramel plants, while its workshop has a domed hall, windows, a fireplace, and four bubbling kettles.
