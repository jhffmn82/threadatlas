Osiris mysteries

religious mysteries · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Osiris Mysteries, The mysteries of Osiris, the former, the greater and less mysteries, the higher mysteries, the latter

Abstract written by a model

Osiris mysteries were mysteries divided into lesser and greater grades. Candidates progressed through the lesser grades, demonstrated a virtuous life, and met further requirements before higher admission, regarded as a great honor. According to Wilkinson, they were the source of Eleusis mysteries.
