Abstract written by a model

has_customs_resembling Yucatan [in many customs of its inhabitants] Equatorial Africa is presented as the region whose inhabitants share many customs with the Mayas. These customs include tooth filing, belief in sorcery, fermented honey-and-bark beverages, grave offerings and sacrifices, abandonment of places associated with death, large drums, fetish houses, native dogs, and protective leopard-skin charms.
