Also called: LLAMA2-13B, LLAMA2-13B as the generator, LLaMA-2-13B, LLaMA2-13B, Llama 2 (13B), Llama 2 13B, Llama-13b, Llama-2-13B, Llama2, Llama2 models, Llama2-13B, Llama2-13b, Llama2-13b-ch, Llama2-2-13B
  • In “Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain,” it is evaluated as an answer generation model in base and chat forms, ranking below GPT-3.5-turbo but above the other listed Llama models in overall head-to-head win rate; its outputs may copy source sentences, run up to three times long, and barely produce required parseable JSON.
  • In “PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers,” it is an open model used to evaluate IterRAG-LM and PlanRAG-LM, with both methods solving no problems and scoring 0% across the listed settings.
  • In “An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” it is used as the generator in open-domain question-answering experiments, achieving higher EM and F1 scores with the proposed SFT and DPO filters while using shorter compressed evidence.
  • In “Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” it is improved by INFO-RAG fine-tuning with LoRA, raising its overall score from 36.86 to 41.04 and particularly improving reported long-form, dialogue, language-modeling, and code-generation measures.
  • In “M-RAG: Reinforcing Large Language Model Performance through Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Multiple Partitions,” it is evaluated with M-RAG on summarization, machine translation, and dialogue generation, with reported scores generally below those of Mixtral 8×7B with M-RAG.
  • In “SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs,” it is an open LLM configuration used with HuggingFace transformers for answer generation and inference, performing poorly with two examples and generally below Llama2-13b-chat and ClimateGPT-13b.
  • In “SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” it is used as a baseline LLM model from Meta AI’s Llama 2 family and, after pretraining and alignment fine-tuning, in an evaluation where LLMs showed improved ability to decline unsafe requests.
