marriage of Neoptolemus and Andromache in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Neoptolemus and Andromache form an alliance through marriage; she is bestowed as a reward for his valor, then Menelaus challenges her place while Peleus defends it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PELEUS, MENELAUS, ANDROMACHE, MOLOSSUS, CHORUS.

Andromache is described as having been bestowed upon Peleus's grandson as a reward for his valor. Menelaus disputes her place in Neoptolemus's household, while Peleus defends the marriage and condemns violence against her.
