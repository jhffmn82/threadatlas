Unc Nunkie in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Unc Nunkie is an old Munchkin man, called the Silent One, with a long beard, kindly eyes, a solemn manner, and a habit of speaking only as many words as necessary. He wears the blue Munchkin costume of boots and a blue coat with gold braid, is descended from former Munchkin kings, and lives with his baby nephew Ojo in a solitary nook of the Blue Forest. Though quiet, he is Ojo's uncle, friend, and protector. He objects when Ojo is too little to leave home, stays awake thinking by the fire after Ojo sleeps, and later leads Ojo to the house of his former friend Dr. Pipt. There he approves Margolotte's plan for the Patchwork Girl but argues that she should receive a little Cleverness rather than poor brains. While Dr. Pipt prepares the Powder of Life, Unc Nunkie accidentally knocks over the Liquid of Petrifaction. The liquid turns him and Margolotte into cold, hard marble statues. Ojo then makes restoring his dear uncle his central purpose, seeking the things required for the Crooked Magician's charm, including three hairs from the Woozy's tail, a six-leaved clover, and the left wing of a yellow butterfly. Even after Ojo fails to obtain the butterfly wing, Unc Nunkie's statue is brought to the Emerald City palace. At last the Wonderful Wizard of Oz restores him to life. Unc Nunkie joyfully embraces Ojo, and Ozma gives him a nice home outside the Emerald City under her protection.

Narrative, section by section

14 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Unc Nunkie is Ojo's old, long-bearded uncle, who speaks very few words and usually answers with brief statements. He objects that Ojo is too little to leave home and remains awake by the fire thinking after Ojo falls asleep.

Chapter Two

Unc Nunkie awakens Ojo, takes him from their home, and leads him up the mountain to Dr. Pipt's house. He reunites with his former friend Dr. Pipt and approves of Margolotte's plan for making the Patchwork Girl a servant.

Chapter Three

Unc Nunkie objects when Margolotte says the Patchwork Girl should not have very good brains and suggests giving her a little Cleverness. He later sits with Ojo, Margolotte, and Dr. Pipt while they admire the Powder of Life and talk.

Chapter Four

Unc Nunkie is introduced as a descendant of the former kings of the Munchkins. He has lived alone in the forest with Ojo, says little, and watches as Dr. Pipt prepares to awaken the Patchwork Girl.

Chapter Five

Unc Nunkie accidentally knocks a bottle from a shelf, causing the Liquid of Petrifaction to spill on him and Margolotte. He is turned into a cold, hard marble statue, and Ojo promises to try to save him.

Chapter Nine

Unc Nunkie is one of the people Ojo is trying to restore to life after an accident. The Crooked Magician requires the Woozy's three tail hairs as part of the magic charm intended to restore him.

Chapter Eleven

Unc Nunkie was terribly injured in the accident at the Crooked Magician's house and has been turned into a marble figure. Ojo is searching for the Crooked Magician's required things to help restore him to life.

Chapter Thirteen

Unc Nunkie remains transformed into a marble statue. Ojo continues searching for the ingredients needed for the Crooked Magician's charm to restore him, including three hairs from the Woozy's tail.

Chapter Fourteen

Unc Nunkie is the person Ojo is trying to restore to life. Ojo believes that finding a six-leaved clover is one of the important things needed for that restoration.

Chapter Fifteen

Unc Nunkie is Ojo's dear companion, whom Ojo disobeyed the Law of Oz in an attempt to restore to life. Ojo says he lived all his life with Unc Nunkie in a lonely forest.

Chapter Sixteen

Unc Nunkie is accidentally turned to marble along with Margolotte by the Liquid of Petrifaction. Ojo seeks the ingredients for a magic charm intended to restore him to life.

Chapter Eighteen

Unc Nunkie remains a petrified victim of the accident involving Dr. Pipt's Liquid of Petrifaction. Ojo's main purpose in seeking the magical ingredients is to restore him to life.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

Unc Nunkie remains a marble statue after Ojo fails to obtain the left wing of a yellow butterfly. Ozma says that his marble statue has been brought to the Emerald City palace.

Chapter Twenty-Eight

Unc Nunkie is restored to life by the Wonderful Wizard of Oz after being turned to stone. He joyfully embraces Ojo, and he is given a home outside the Emerald City under Ozma's protection.
