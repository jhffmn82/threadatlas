the Musgrave Ritual

Abstract written by a model

The Musgrave Ritual is an ancient private family ceremony and formula, expressed as a catechism and performed by every Musgrave on coming of age. Handed down through generations and not hidden from others, it was initially thought to have no practical use. Its questions and directions invoke an oak, elm, measurements, cardinal directions, an old door, and a passage. Holmes interprets its opening as concerning a crown of Charles I and Charles II and uses its instructions to locate a chamber in the Hurlstone cellar.

VI. The Musgrave Ritual

The Musgrave Ritual is a private family observance performed by each Musgrave on coming of age. It is considered ancient and of possible archaeological interest, though Reginald Musgrave initially regards it as having no practical use.
