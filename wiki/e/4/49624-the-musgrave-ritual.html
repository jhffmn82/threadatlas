VI. The Musgrave Ritual

The Musgrave Ritual is a private family observance performed by each Musgrave on coming of age. It is considered ancient and of possible archaeological interest, though Reginald Musgrave initially regards it as having no practical use.

The Musgrave Ritual gives directions involving an oak, an elm, measurements, cardinal directions, and a location under the old door and passage. Holmes uses it to find the cellar chamber and interprets its opening questions as referring to a crown belonging first to the dead Charles I and then to the returning Charles II.
