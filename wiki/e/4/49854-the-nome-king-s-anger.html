TIKTOK AND THE NOME KING

The Nome King's anger is caused partly by a painful bitten tongue and later by Tiktok's insulting remarks. It leads him to destroy Tiktok, an act he regrets and recognizes as a result of his unreasoning temper. He resolves never to become angry again unless something annoys him.
