Also called: TinyLlama-1.1B, TinyLlama-1.1B-chat

Abstract written by a model

In "BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," TinyLlama-1.1B, also called TinyLlama-1.1B-chat, is evaluated with and without oracle retrieval and gains from retrieval; after QLoRa fine-tuning on NQ, it reaches 0.77 LLMeval, a gain of 0.41 over its zero-shot result, the largest reported fine-tuning gain. In "RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering," TinyLlama is evaluated as a 1.1B-parameter instruction-tuned model; with TA-ARE, its retrieval accuracy rises from 39.1 to 54.1 and its match accuracy rises from 14.7 to 19.0.
