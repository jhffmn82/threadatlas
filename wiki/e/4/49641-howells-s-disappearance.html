Howells's disappearance

Abstract written by a model

Howells disappears from her room on the third night after Brunton vanishes, despite a nurse watching her. Her open window and footprints lead across the lawn toward the mere, where no body is found. Holmes believes she fled after the cellar events, taking the treasure and leaving England haunted by Brunton’s death.
