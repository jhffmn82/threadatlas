In RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, parametric knowledge supplements low-confidence retrieved contexts and sometimes answers queries directly, with its usefulness varying across models, datasets, and questions. In Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?, model internal knowledge is used to answer questions; prompting answers based only on the provided passage is intended to exclude it and lowers Gemini-1.5-Pro’s average long-context performance, while stronger control and tracing methods remain future work. In RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering, parametric knowledge is knowledge encoded in a model’s parameters through pre-training; adaptive retrieval methods rely on it when external retrieval is unnecessary, while RetrievalQA ensures required answers are absent from it. In RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models, internal knowledge is used in unsafe responses and competes with retrieved documents, potentially introducing unsafe content from otherwise safe documents.

parametric knowledge in RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kiseung Kim and Jay-Yoon Lee · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where parametric knowledge first appears

Abstract written by a model

Parametric knowledge in large language models can supplement low-confidence retrieved contexts and sometimes answer queries directly. It is more abundant in some models and datasets than others, benefiting TriviaQA more than Natural Questions, especially where Llama 2 has scarce knowledge; future work should assess it before deeming queries unanswerable.

Parametric knowledge is more abundant in large language models and can be used when retrieved contexts have low confidence. Its usefulness varies by dataset: it generally benefits TriviaQA, while its scarcity limits gains on Natural Questions, especially for Llama 2.

7

model internal knowledge in Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?

Zhuowan Li · 2024-11-12 to 2024-11-16 · the work’s page · read it where model internal knowledge first appears

Abstract written by a model

Model internal knowledge is knowledge used to answer questions. Prompting answers “based only on the provided passage” is intended to exclude it and lowers Gemini-1.5-Pro’s average LC performance from 50.57 to 45.53. After removing context-free questions, overall trends remain; stronger control and tracing methods are future work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening .. Exclusion of LLM’s internal knowledge

Model internal knowledge is addressed by prompting models to answer 'based only on the provided passage,' which lowers Gemini-1.5-Pro’s average LC performance from 50.57 to 45.53. Removing questions that can be answered without context leaves the overall LC, RAG, and SELF-ROUTE trends broadly intact. The text notes that more comprehensive methods for controlling and tracing internal knowledge remain future work.

notes

Internal knowledge is used by models to answer questions, and differences in that knowledge may lead to different numbers of non-commonsense questions.
