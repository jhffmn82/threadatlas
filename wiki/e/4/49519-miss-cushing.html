Abstract written by a model

Miss Susan Cushing is a respectable, placid maiden lady of fifty who lives a retired life at Cross Street in Croydon, with few acquaintances or correspondents. The oldest of three sisters, she is sister to Sarah and to Mary Browner, formerly Mary Cushing. She receives by parcel post a cardboard packet addressed to “Miss S. Cushing” containing two human ears, reports it to the police, and maintains it was probably sent to her by mistake. She tells Holmes of her sisters, Mary’s husband James Browner, and former medical-student lodgers. Holmes notes that her ear closely matches the female ear in the box, indicating the female victim was likely a close blood relation.

Miss Cushing is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
