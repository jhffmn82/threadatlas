the police theory in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The police theory attributes Silver Blaze's disappearance and John Straker's murder to Fitzroy Simpson: he drugged Ned Hunter, obtained a duplicate key, stole the horse, killed Straker with his weighted stick, then hid it or caused it to bolt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

I. Silver Blaze

The police theory is that Fitzroy Simpson drugged Ned Hunter, obtained a duplicate key, stole Silver Blaze, and was confronted by John Straker while leading the horse away. It proposes that Simpson killed Straker with his weighted stick and either hid the horse or caused it to bolt, though Holmes considers the explanation improbable.
