LITTLE DOROTHY AND TOTO

Washing the dishes is the task Crinklink assigns to Dorothy as a supposed captive. Dorothy performs it cautiously for hours without breaking anything, while Crinklink grows sleepy; the task ends when Toto knocks the dishes down during his attack.
