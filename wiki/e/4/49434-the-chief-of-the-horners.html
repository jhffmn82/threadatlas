Abstract written by a model

Jak, the Chief of the Horners, is a respected Horner who lives in the Chief Horner's dwelling, wears a jeweled star above his horn, and has nineteen daughters. During a dispute with the Hoppers, he insists the Horners are safe behind a locked gate, explains the joke involved, and waits for Diksey. After the joke is explained, he ends the war and restores peace.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Chief of the Horners is Jak, a respected Horner who wears a jeweled star in his hair above his horn. He says the Horners are not afraid while the gate is locked, explains the joke that caused the dispute, and agrees to wait for Diksey so the joke may be explained to the Hoppers.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Chief Horner shows Scraps his beautiful radium-lined home and explains that Horners care more about the inside of their houses than their exteriors. He introduces his nineteen daughters and says they are raised according to rules written by a leading bachelor. After Diksey explains the joke, he declares that the war is over and peace has been restored.
