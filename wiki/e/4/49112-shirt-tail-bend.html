Abstract written by a model

Shirt-Tail Bend is a Mississippi River locality, named for woodmen who ran out in shirt-tails to supply steamboats at night. Initially settled by wood-cutters, it has wood yards, Sundown Hill cabin, and no schoolhouse, church, or post-office. It is associated with Don Carlo, S. S. Simon, and Brindle Bill. After Don Carlo's victory, planters arrive, Simon becomes prominent, and small towns develop.

Shirt-Tail Bend is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SCENE SECOND.

Shirt-Tail Bend is a Mississippi River locality known for its wood yards and for woodmen running out in their shirt-tails to supply steamboats at night. It has little social infrastructure, with no schoolhouse, church, or post-office at first. After Don Carlo's victory, planters settle there and small towns begin to develop.

SCENE FOURTH--ROXIE DAYMON AND ROSE SIMON.

Shirt-Tail Bend is where S. S. Simon lives and where Brindle Bill returns to pursue the Lasco inheritance scheme. Simon later becomes a prominent planter in the neighborhood.

SCENE FIFTH.

Don Carlo had been associated with Shirt-Tail Bend and had sold out there before returning toward his childhood home. He is later identified as the hero of Shirt-Tail Bend.
