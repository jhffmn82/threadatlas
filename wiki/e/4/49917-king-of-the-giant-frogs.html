Abstract written by a model

King of the Giant Frogs is a king of the frogs in the Fog Bank, about ten times larger than a common frog. He leads giant frogs, rules Frog King's kingdom, and has frogs as subjects. He helps Rosalie the Witch's party cross the Fog Bank, but later bars travelers over an umbrella and flees when it becomes an elephant.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The King of the Giant Frogs agrees to help Rosalie the Witch and the Pinkies pass through the Fog Bank. He carries Trot, her parrot, Rosalie, Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and the two army captains, slowing at the edge so they can dismount comfortably.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The Frog King refuses to let the travelers pass while Button-Bright carries the umbrella, calling it insulting to his dominions. When the umbrella becomes an elephant and charges, he flees before the other frogs.
