Square Meal Tablets in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

contain a complete meal [each tablet] Square Meal Tablets contain the equivalent of several foods, including soup, fried fish, mutton pot-pie, lobster salad, charlotte russe, and lemon jelly. The Scarecrow uses six tablets to persuade the lazy Quadling man to help build the raft and gives him two additional tablets afterward.

Square-Meal Tablet in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Square-Meal Tablet is a tiny tablet containing condensed soup, fried fish, roast, salad, and dessert, providing a full meal’s nourishment. Professor H. M. Wogglebug fed it to students instead of ordinary food, but they refused it for lack of meal taste.

21. The College of Athletic Arts

The Square-Meal Tablet is a tiny tablet containing the condensed equivalent of soup, fried fish, a roast, a salad, and a dessert, with the nourishment of a full meal. Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E., fed it to his students instead of ordinary food, but they refused it because they wanted to enjoy the taste of their meals.
