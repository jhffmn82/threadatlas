Abstract written by a model

Moo, Queen of Chichen, is Chaacmol's wife and sister. Represented as a macaw, she is worshiped at Izamal as Kinich-Kakmo and portrayed in murals protected by a serpent and warned by a baboon.

Moo is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
