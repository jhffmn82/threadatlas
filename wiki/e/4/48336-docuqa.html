Abstract written by a model

DocuQA is a dataset for application-based document question-answering systems that process document inputs. It contains 20 diverse documents, journal articles, news reports, financial documents, and tutorials, and 100 questions with corresponding ground-truth answers. It is used for performance testing and to evaluate proposed document QA systems and other document QA applications, including alongside the Stanford Question Answering Dataset. On DocuQA, the proposed QA system achieved 96% accuracy, with reported ROUGE-L, BERTScore, BLEU, and Jaccard Similarity results; its precision, recall, and F1-Score were reported as superior to other tested applications.

DocuQA is in one work on this site, Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach: Document Question Answering using Large Language Model. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
