Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII. The Final Problem

Sherlock Holmes reports that Moriarty’s agents have tried to kill or injure him by driving a two-horse van at him, dropping a brick from a roof, and sending a man armed with a bludgeon to assault him. Holmes avoids the first two attacks and knocks down the assailant in the third, injuring his own knuckles in the struggle.
