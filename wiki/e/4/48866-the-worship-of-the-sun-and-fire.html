the worship of the sun and fire in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

The worship of the sun and fire was practiced by the Cans alongside worship of the mastodon's head. They represented sun and fire with the same hieroglyph used by Egyptians for the sun, resembled Chaldeans and Hindoos in fire worship, and differed from Egyptians, who venerated no fire.

The Cans worshipped the sun and fire and represented them with the same hieroglyph that the narrator says the Egyptians used for the sun. He compares their fire worship with that of the Chaldeans and Hindoos, while distinguishing it from Egyptian attitudes toward fire.
