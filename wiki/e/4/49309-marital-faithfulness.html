In Hecuba and other plays, marital faithfulness is exemplified by Helen. In The Trojan Women of Euripides, marital faithfulness is violated by Helen.

Abstract written by a model

is_exemplified_by Helen [her continued love for Menelaus] Helen presents herself as faithful to Menelaus, saying that she retains the memory of their marriage and will honor him even after his supposed death.
