Joseph Harrison

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Joseph, Master Joseph, Mr. Harrison, Mr. Joseph Harrison, brother Joseph, her brother, the plump young man, the stout young man

Abstract written by a model

Joseph Harrison, Annie Harrison’s brother and Percy Phelps’s prospective brother-in-law, is present in Woking during Phelps’s illness and helps investigate signs of an attempted night intrusion. Beneath this helpful appearance, he is the thief of the stolen State papers. After heavy stock-speculation losses, he becomes willing to do anything to improve his fortunes, hides the papers beneath a removable board under the carpet in Phelps’s bedroom, and later returns at night to retrieve them. Armed with a knife, he attacks Holmes during the confrontation, but Holmes overpowers him and recovers the papers. He is characterized as selfish, financially desperate, and dangerous.

Joseph Harrison is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

XI. The Naval Treaty

Joseph Harrison is Annie Harrison's brother and is engaged to become Percy Phelps's relation by marriage. He is in Woking during Phelps's illness and is mentioned as someone Phelps hoped to meet after returning by the eleven o'clock train.

“Where are we going to now?” I asked, as we left the office.

Joseph Harrison helps rouse the household after Percy Phelps reports the attempted night intrusion. He and the groom find marks on the bed outside the window and identify a cracked section of the fence, though the evidence does not establish when or how it was damaged.

It was seven o’clock when I awoke, and I set off at once for

Joseph Harrison is revealed to be the thief who stole the State papers and hid them beneath a removable floorboard in Percy Phelps’s room. He returns at night to recover them, attacks Holmes with a knife, and gives up the papers after Holmes overpowers him. Holmes believes Joseph is selfish, financially desperate from stock speculation, and dangerous.
