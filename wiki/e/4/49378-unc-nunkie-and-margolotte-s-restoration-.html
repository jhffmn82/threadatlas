The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, using magic taught by Glinda the Good, ultimately frees both from the Liquid of Petrifaction and returns them to life.

Chapter Nine

Ojo and his companions are seeking materials for the restoration of Unc Nunkie and Margolotte to life. Ojo fears that without the Woozy's three tail hairs, the other things he finds will be useless.

Chapter Fourteen

Ojo is gathering important things that he believes will restore Unc Nunkie to life. The six-leaved clover is one such item, but the unit does not establish whether the restoration will succeed.

Chapter Twenty-Eight

The restoration is accomplished through a method known to Glinda the Good and performed by the Wonderful Wizard of Oz. Both Unc Nunkie and Margolotte are freed from the Liquid of Petrifaction and return to life.
