Abstract written by a model

is_a mythological history [of Osiris in Egypt] The Osiris myth concerns Osiris's life on Earth, his efforts to civilize the world, his killing and dismemberment by Typho, Isis's search for his body, and Horus's defeat of Typho. The text compares parts of this myth with traditions of the Maya culture heroes Kukulean and Quetzalcoatl.
