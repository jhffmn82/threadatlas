Hip Hopper

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Champion, the Champion, the Hopper, the man, the poor fellow

Abstract written by a model

Hip Hopper is a round, fat, one-legged Hopper and self-described Champion wrestler who leads the Hoppers and may be the strongest man in his country. He travels swiftly and gracefully by hopping on a broad, flat foot. After surrendering to Dorothy when Toto overcomes him, he explains the Hopper-Horner war and a route toward Horner Country, leads the group, and unsuccessfully tries to throw the Scarecrow over the dividing fence, leaving him trapped on a picket.

Chapter Twenty-One

Hip Hopper is a one-legged Hopper who hops quickly on a broad, flat foot. He calls himself the Champion wrestler and surrenders after Toto overcomes him. He tells the travelers about the Hopper and Horner countries, the war between them, and a path leading toward the Horner Country, then leads the group toward the Hopper city.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Champion is a strong Hopper who has surrendered to Dorothy and her companions. He explains the Hoppers’ conflict with the Horners and tries to throw the Scarecrow over the dividing fence, but the attempt leaves the Scarecrow trapped on a picket.
