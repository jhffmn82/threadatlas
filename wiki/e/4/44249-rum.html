Abstract written by a model

Rum is a distilled liquor made from molasses and containing nearly or about one-half alcohol. It may follow weaker alcoholic drinks. Sailors once received it daily, with extra during great storms, but a captain found it did not make them stronger.

CHAPTER VII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Rum is nearly one half alcohol. Drinking rum is presented as a possible later stage after drinking weaker alcoholic drinks.

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Rum is distilled from molasses and contains about one half alcohol. Sailors were once given rum every day and were given twice as much during great storms, but a captain found that it did not make them stronger.
