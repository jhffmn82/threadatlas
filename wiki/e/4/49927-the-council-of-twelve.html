Abstract written by a model

The Council of twelve is a council summoned by Tourmaline to advise on the strangers' fate in the Pink Country. After examining and conferring, it must choose whether to honor them as guests or throw them over the island’s edge. Its vote ends evenly divided, six to six.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Council of twelve is summoned by Tourmaline to decide whether the strangers may remain in the Pink Country. After examining them and conferring, it chooses between honoring them as guests and throwing them over the island’s edge.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The twelve Counselors vote six to allow the strangers to remain and six to throw them over the bushes. Some argue that the strangers have done no harm, while others object that they disrupt the Pinkies' color scheme.
