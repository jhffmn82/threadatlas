Abstract written by a model

is_planned_by Helen [as a deception to obtain a ship] The false funeral is intended to persuade the king that Menelaus has died in the sea. Helen will cut her hair, put on mourning clothes, feign grief, request a ship for the funeral rites, and use the ceremony to escape with Menelaus.
