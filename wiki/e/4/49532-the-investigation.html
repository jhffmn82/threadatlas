Abstract written by a model

The investigation is a case concerning a packet containing human ears and the double murder of Mrs. Browner and a man presumed to be seafaring. Beginning with Inspector Lestrade’s inquiry and the medical-student theory, Holmes examines evidence, questions Susan, investigates Sarah’s address, and receives a telegram. Reasoning backward from effects to causes, he concludes that Jim Browner committed the crime, though one victim remains unidentified.
