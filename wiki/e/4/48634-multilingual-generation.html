multilingual generation in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where multilingual generation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Multilingual generation is a research area and the second stage of the multilingual RAG pipeline. It generates responses from a user query and retrieved context with a generator LLM, testing prompting strategies, greedy decoding, a 128-new-token limit, and an int4-quantized model. Experiments compare English-centric LLaMA-2-7B-chat and SOLAR-10.7B with multilingual Mixtral-8x7B and Command-R-35B. Evaluation uses correct language rate and character 3-gram recall. Multilingual-by-design models are essential for broad non-English output, though prompting can partly improve English-centric models. It is the pipeline's weakest part, especially for mixed-language context, motivating mixed-language instruction-tuning examples or decoding strategies.

Multilingual RAG pipeline

Generation is the second stage of the multilingual RAG pipeline. It produces a response from the user query and retrieved context using a generator LLM and experiments with various prompting strategies.

opening

Multilingual generation is discussed in relation to the limited language coverage of many current state-of-the-art models and the possible effects of the curse of multilinguality. The experiments compare English-centric and multilingual instruction-tuned models.

4

Generation uses greedy decoding, limits output to a maximum of 128 new tokens, and runs experiments with an int4-quantized model.

opening .. opening

Multilingual generation is evaluated through correct language rate and character 3-gram recall. The results indicate that multilingual-by-design models are essential for broad non-English generation, while prompting can partially improve English-centric models.

Conclusion

Multilingual generation is identified as the weakest part of the mRAG pipeline, particularly with mixed-language context. The text suggests improving it through mixed-language examples in instruction tuning or specific decoding strategies.
