Encyclopedic-VQA in Wiki-LLaVA: Hierarchical Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multimodal LLMs

Davide Caffagni · 2024

Encyclopedic-VQA is a benchmark dataset for evaluating MLLM capabilities with external data and knowledge-based visual question answering. It contains about 221k question-answer pairs for 16.7k fine-grained entities, over 1M image-question-answer triplets, and a 2M-article Wikipedia knowledge base. Training supports LLaVA fine-tuning; evaluation uses 4,750 filtered test triplets and finds CLIP retrieval difficult at this scale.

4. Experiments / 4.1. Datasets .. 4.5. Limitations and Future Works

Encyclopedic-VQA contains around 221k question-answer pairs associated with 16.7k fine-grained entities and more than 1M image-question-answer triplets, supported by a 2M-article Wikipedia knowledge base. The experiments use its training split for LLaVA fine-tuning and evaluate on 4,750 filtered test triplets, reporting accuracy and showing that CLIP retrieval is difficult at this knowledge-base scale.
