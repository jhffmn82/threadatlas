Abstract written by a model

In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the Blue Forest is a solitary, forgotten thick forest on the mountainside along the path into Munchkin Country, crossed and left near sundown. In Grampa in Oz, the Blue Forest is a deep, dark, forbidding forest at the beginning of the Munchkin country where Tatters, Grampa, and Bill camp before bandits capture them and Grampa and Tatters escape.
