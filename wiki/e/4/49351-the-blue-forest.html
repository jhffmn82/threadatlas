the Blue Forest in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A solitary, forgotten thick forest on the mountainside along the path into Munchkin Country, gloomy to Ojo but lovely to Scraps, is crossed and left near sundown.

Chapter Six

The forest lies along the path from the mountainside into Munchkin Country. Ojo finds it gloomy and sad, while Scraps admires its trees, ferns, wildflowers, and mosses. The travelers leave it near sundown and later pass through darkness beyond its edge.

the Blue Forest of Oz in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The Blue Forest of Oz is a deep, dark, forbidding forest at the beginning of the Munchkin country, filled with blue trees, heavy underbrush, gnarled trees, and crows from magical tobacco. Tatters, Grampa, and Bill camp there; bandits capture them, then Grampa and Tatters escape as pursuit begins.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The Blue Forest of Oz is a deep blue forest at the beginning of the Munchkin country. Prince Tatters, Grampa, and Bill camp there before bandits surround and capture them.

CHAPTER 4

The blue forest is dark and forbidding, with heavy underbrush, gnarled trees, and many crows. Grampa and Tatters escape through it, but the bandits follow after Bill sounds the alarm.
