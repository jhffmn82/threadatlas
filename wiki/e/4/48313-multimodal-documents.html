Also called: document, documents, retrieved documents

Abstract written by a model

Documents are Wiki-LLaVA’s external knowledge and memory. They are retrieved by similarity between an input image and document titles, divided into chunks, and filtered for relevant passages that are included in the MLLM input context.

Abstract

Multimodal documents constitute the external knowledge source integrated by Wiki-LLaVA.

3. Proposed Method .. 3.2. Training

Documents form the external memory and are initially retrieved using the similarity between the input image and their titles. Each selected document is divided into chunks from which relevant passages are identified.

5. Conclusion

Documents serve as the external knowledge used by Wiki-LLaVA. The architecture retrieves documents and selects parts of them for inclusion in the MLLM input context.
