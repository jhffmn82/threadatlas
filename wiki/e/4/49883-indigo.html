Indigo in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Princess Indigo, one of the Six Snubnosed Princesses and the Boolooroo’s daughter, owns a blue dog. She declares Trot a slave and mistreats her, but Trot’s pets drive Indigo away; rescued by her sisters, she avoids her room because Trot is inside.

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE TRIBULATION OF TROT

Princess Indigo asserts that Trot is a slave and shakes her when Trot speaks impertinently. She later tries to remove Trot from the blue rabbit's boudoir, but the pets attack and frighten her, biting, scratching, pecking, butting, and tripping her. Her sisters rescue her, and she subsequently avoids entering her own room because Trot is there.
