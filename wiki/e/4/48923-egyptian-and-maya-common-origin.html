Abstract written by a model

The topic argues that Egyptians and Mayas shared common descent or intimate remote-age communication. It presents alleged correspondences in deities, myths, names, royal relationships, rites, symbols, priestly practices, writing, dress, architecture, and scientific knowledge, culminating in identifications of Chaacmol with Osiris, Moo with Isis, and Aac with Typho and comparisons of animal and Sun imagery.
