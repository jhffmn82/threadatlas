contributes_to Tom Fairfield's death [through intoxication and exposure] Tom Fairfield became too fond of whisky after repeatedly winning bottles in Kentucky wedding races and associating with dissipated boatmen. At Port William he was drinking late at night when Dave Deminish threw him out after he heaved at a glass of spoiled beer. His drinking contributed to the belief that he became intoxicated, froze, and died in the snow.
