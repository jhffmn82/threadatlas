FS-RAG in DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models

Weihang Su, Yichen Tang, Qingyao Ai, Zhijing Wu, Yiqun Liu · 2024

FS-RAG is a dynamic retrieval-augmented generation framework and fixed rules-based baseline. It triggers retrieval after every generated sentence, using the last sentence as the query. Its retrieval-call count reflects average LLM sentences, but it does not consistently beat single-round retrieval and trails DRAGIN in reported experiments.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.5

FS-RAG triggers retrieval after every sentence. It uses the last generated sentence as the query.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

FS-RAG is a fixed rules-based retrieval baseline whose reported number of retrieval calls also indicates the average number of sentences produced by the LLM. It does not consistently outperform single-round retrieval, and it performs below DRAGIN in the reported timing and query formulation experiments.
