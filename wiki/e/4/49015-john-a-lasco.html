John A. Lasco

Abstract written by a model

John A. Lasco, an English passenger on the Brandywine and Susan Lasco’s older brother, searches America for her for three years. Badly injured in the Brandywine’s burning, he briefly survives, dictates bequests to Susan, her descendants, and his caregiver, then dies.

John A. Lasco is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SCENE FOURTH--ROXIE DAYMON AND ROSE SIMON.

John A. Lasco searches for his sister Susan Lasco in America for three years. After surviving the burning of the Brandywine only briefly, he dictates a statement leaving twenty thousand dollars to Susan Lasco and her descendants and one hundred ten dollars to the woman caring for him.
