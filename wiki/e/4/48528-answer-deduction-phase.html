answer deduction phase in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024

Answer deduction is a phase for deducing intricate knowledge in multi-hop questions. It uses LLMs’ stored parametric knowledge to supplement retrieved knowledge, repeatedly breaking a complex input question into fine-grained single-hop sub-questions and answering each from current context until the deduction is complete.

3 .. 3.3

Answer deduction breaks a complex question into single-hop sub-questions and prompts the LLMs to answer each one. It uses the current context and the input question to formulate the next sub-question.

6 / 6.1 .. 6.3

The answer deduction phase uses LLMs' parametric knowledge to supplement retrieved knowledge and deduce intricate knowledge for multi-hop questions. Removing it causes absolute Acc decreases of 6 points on HotpotQA and 10 points on SQA.
