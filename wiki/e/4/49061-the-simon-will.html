the Simon will

Also called: Young Simon’s will, last will and testament, the will, will

Abstract written by a model

The Simon will bequeaths Young Simon’s entire estate to Roxie Daymon, dictated to Governor Morock. After Simon’s death, cousin Cæsar contests it through Morock, alleging Simon lacked capacity; Morock proposes using money to arrange testimony about Simon’s mental faculties.

SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

The Simon will is threatened with a legal challenge by Cousin Cæsar and Roxie Daymon. Governor Morock claims that money could break it and proposes arranging testimony concerning Simon’s mental faculties.
