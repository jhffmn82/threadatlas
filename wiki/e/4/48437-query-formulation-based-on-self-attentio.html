Query Formulation based on Self-attention

method · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: QFS, QFS approach, QFS method, QFS module, QFS: Query Formulation based on Self-attention, “what to retrieval” (i.e., QFS)

Abstract written by a model

Query Formulation based on Self-attention is DRAGIN’s proposed query formulation method for dynamic retrieval augmentation. At a position identified by Real-time Information Needs Detection, it examines the entire context through a Transformer-based LLM’s self-attention to determine information needs. It selects the top n preceding tokens by attention score, restores their original text order, and constructs a retrieval query from their corresponding words. It provides precise query formulation; experiments on HotpotQA show it outperforms the query methods used by FLARE, FL-RAG, FS-RAG, and full-context querying.
