Croydon is the town containing Cross Street and the police investigation site. Holmes and Watson travel there from London to examine the packet and question Susan Cushing. The address on the packet is written as “Cross Street, Croydon,” although Holmes observes that “Croydon” was first spelled with an “i” and then corrected to a “y.”
