Dark Well

place · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: The well, a dark well, a pitch-dark well, a very dark well, dark well, dark wells, it, such a thing, the dark well

Abstract written by a model

Dark Well is a naturally dark, underground well whose water receives no daylight and has never seen the light of day. Its most important role is supplying a gill of unseen water for the restoring compound or charm needed to restore petrified victims; the water must be put into a gold bottle without light reaching it. At first, neither Ojo nor the Shaggy Man knows where to find it, and the companions prepare to search. Jack Pumpkinhead directs them away from the flat country toward the mountains. While Dorothy and Ojo investigate a tunnel, Hip Hopper says the Hopper city has only well-lighted wells and suggests Horner Country. The search leads to Diksey's radium mine, where Diksey helps determine whether the well has water. In a dark corner of the vast cave beneath the mine, Ojo finds the well and collects its water.

Dark Well is in one work on this site, The Patchwork Girl of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

The restoring compound requires a gill of water from a dark well. The well must receive no daylight, and the water must be placed in a gold bottle without light reaching it.

Chapter Eleven

A gill of water from a dark well is one of the five things required by the Crooked Magician. Neither Ojo nor the Shaggy Man knows where such a well can be found, so the Shaggy Man suggests asking the Scarecrow.

Chapter Eighteen

A gill of water from a dark well is one of the ingredients needed for Dr. Pipt's restoring charm. No one knows where such a well is, so Ojo and his companions prepare to search for it.

Chapter Nineteen

The dark well must be naturally dark, and its water must never have seen the light of day. Jack Pumpkinhead says such a well is unlikely to be found in the flat country and directs the travelers toward the mountains.

Chapter Twenty-One

The dark well is what Dorothy and Ojo are searching for. They suspect the tunnel may lead to one, but Hip Hopper says the Hopper city has only well-lighted wells and suggests that a very dark well may be in the Horner Country.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The dark well is located in Diksey's radium mine. Diksey does not know whether it contains water, so he agrees to help find out.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The dark well is located in a dark corner of the vast cave beneath the mine. It contains water that Ojo collects.
