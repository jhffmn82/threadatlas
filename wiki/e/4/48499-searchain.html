Abstract written by a model

In "Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering," SearChain is a generation-with-retrieval, retrieval-then-read method that dynamically uses retrieval to verify and correct answers, and GenGround outperforms it by 4–6 accuracy points across datasets. In "Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," SearChain is an open-retrieval method using LLaMA-2-13B-chat, and INFO-RAG improves its results on HotpotQA, Musique, T-REx, and ZS over SearChain alone and listed previous state-of-the-art.

SearChain in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where SearChain first appears

Abstract written by a model

SearChain is a generation-with-retrieval, retrieval-then-read method that dynamically uses retrieval to verify and correct answers. It generates long-form answers against short-form ground truth, so evaluation uses Accuracy and semantic accuracy; GenGround outperforms it by 4–6 accuracy points across datasets.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

SearChain dynamically interacts with the retriever to verify and correct generated answers. Because it prompts the language model to generate a long-form answer while the ground truth is short-form, it is evaluated only with Accuracy and semantic accuracy.

6 / 6.1 .. 6.3

SearChain is evaluated as a retrieval-then-read baseline. GenGround significantly outperforms it, with the text reporting a 4-6 point increase in accuracy metrics across datasets.
