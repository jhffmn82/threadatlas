Three buttons, tin, brass, and lead, contain the spirits of the mischievous Imps Olite, Udent, and Ertinent after the Wizard transforms blue doves. He boxes them, intending to sew them onto his coat until repentance changes them into silver, gold, and aluminum and restores the Imps.

OZMA AND THE LITTLE WIZARD

The Wizard transforms the blue doves into a tin button for Imp Olite, a brass button for Imp Udent, and a lead button for Imp Ertinent. He places the buttons in a box and plans to sew them onto his coat until the Imps repent; then the buttons will become silver, gold, and aluminum and the Imps can be restored to good forms.
