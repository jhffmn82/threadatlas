Abstract written by a model

The Cunningham house is a fine, old Queen Anne house, dated “Malplaquet,” with a forced side door, oak-lined avenue, dog, and family-room lamps. During an investigation, Mr. Cunningham and Alec Cunningham say their front-facing rooms were lit; Holmes searches its rooms and stairways and finds part of a sheet of paper.

The Cunningham house is a Queen Anne house with a forced side door, an oak-lined avenue, and several rooms and stairways relevant to the investigation. Mr. Cunningham’s and Alec Cunningham’s rooms overlook the front, and both men say their lamps were lit during the incident. Holmes searches the house and finds the remainder of a sheet of paper.
