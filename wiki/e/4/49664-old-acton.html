Old Acton in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Old Acton, a county magnate who owns Acton’s house, claims half the Cunninghams’ estate. After a burglary at his house, he attends Holmes’s explanation and says the thieves sought a document that could weaken his lawsuit.

Mr. Acton attends Holmes's explanation because his house was the scene of the original burglary. He explains that he had a claim to half of the Cunninghams' estate and that they sought a document that could have weakened his case.
