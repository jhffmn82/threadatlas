the Bacchic hunt

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: hunting, the breaking of dawn, the chase, the hunt on Kithaeron, the taking, this chase, this deed
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, the Bacchic hunt takes place on Kithaeron as Agave and the other daughters of Cadmus pursue Pentheus under divine madness, with Agave celebrating it before its true victim is revealed.
  • In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, the hunt on Kithaeron is presented by Agave as the triumphant capture of a lion, but is actually the divinely directed pursuit leading to Pentheus’s death and dismemberment.

CHORUS.

The Bacchic hunt takes place on Kithaeron, where Agave and the other daughters of Cadmus pursue Pentheus while under divine madness. Agave celebrates the hunt as a feat achieved without nets or weapons, until its true victim is revealed.

the hunt on Kithaeron in Hippolytus; The Bacchae

Euripides · -0427

The hunt on Kithaeron is presented by Agave as a triumphant capture of a lion, though the supposed prey is Pentheus. It is actually the divinely directed pursuit that leads to his death and dismemberment.
