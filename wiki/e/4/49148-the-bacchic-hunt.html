the Bacchic hunt

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: hunting, the breaking of dawn, the chase, the hunt on Kithaeron, the taking, this chase, this deed

Abstract written by a model

In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, the hunt on Kithaeron is presented by Agave as the triumphant capture of a lion, but is actually the divinely directed pursuit leading to Pentheus’s death and dismemberment. In The Bacchae of Euripides, the Bacchic hunt takes place on Kithaeron as Agave and the other daughters of Cadmus pursue Pentheus under divine madness, with Agave celebrating it before its true victim is revealed.
