information extraction attack in The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Yue Xing, Yiding Liu, Han Xu, Jie Ren, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin, Yi Chang, Jiliang Tang · 2024

Information extraction attacks target retrieval datasets to uncover private information and investigate memorization in LLMs. They ask for general disease information or suggestions, then append a command prompt intended to reveal retrieved contexts. In studied models, this produces high rates of verbatim or highly similar record extraction, extending data extraction attacks that expose training-data recall.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 / Introduction

Information extraction is carried out by asking for general information or suggestions related to a disease and appending a command prompt intended to make the model reveal retrieved contexts. The unit reports high extraction rates for verbatim or highly similar records in the studied models.

2 / Related Work / 2.1 / Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) .. 2.2 / Privacy Risk of Large Language Models

Carlini et al. (2021) pioneered research on data extraction attacks against large language models. These attacks revealed the models’ tendency to recall and reproduce segments of their training data.
