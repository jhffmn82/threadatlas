Mistral-7B

model · 8 works · 8 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Mistral, Mistral 7B, Mistral- 7B, Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2, Mistral-7B-v0.1, Mistral-V0.2, Mistral7B, RadioRAG+Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2, open source model

Abstract written by a model

In "Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering," Mistral-7B is used for comparison and as the backbone in GenGround retrieval, ablation, and experimental analyses; direct prompting outperforms baselines, instructional grounding distillation further improves performance, and accuracy with randomly sampled examples declines slightly with less data, reaching 42.08 at 45k and 40.12 at 20k examples. In "M-RAG: Reinforcing Large Language Model Performance through Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Multiple Partitions," Mistral 7B is compared using Selfmem and M-RAG, with M-RAG improving its summarization, Es→En translation, and dialogue scores, including translation BLEU from 26.26 to 32.65. In "RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering," Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 is evaluated with and without RadioRAG, showing no improvement: 50% accuracy in both conditions and notable incorrect-response rates. In "Reducing hallucination in structured outputs via Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Mistral-7B-v0.1 is fine-tuned as another alternative 7B-parameter pretrained LLM and evaluated using the same datasets and hyperparameters as the other LLMs; despite RAG fine-tuning, it performs worse than StarCoderBase. In "TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Mistral-7B-v0.1 is used as a reader to evaluate TRACE and baselines on HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, with results showing that TRACE's reasoning chains generalize effectively. In "Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study," Mistral-7B supplies the pre-training parameters for the locally constructed LLaMA-based large language model. In "MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Mistral 7B is used as a pre-trained baseline and the default backbone for all three MAIN-RAG agents; its retrieval baseline scores 69.4% on TriviaQA and 55.5% on PopQA, while MAIN-RAG-Mistral7B improves these to 71.0% and 58.9%. In "RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models," Mistral-V0.2 has unsafe-response rates of 8.7% without RAG, 19.9% with safe documents, and 60.3% with unsafe documents; its RAG-task accuracy is 65.4% and its random-document accuracy is 12.3%.

Mistral-7B in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Mistral-7B first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mistral-7B is a smaller open source model used for comparison and as the backbone in GenGround retrieval, ablation, and experimental analyses. It is evaluated in vanilla prompted and other-method settings; direct prompting outperforms baselines, and instructional grounding distillation improves performance further. When trained with randomly sampled examples for that method, its accuracy declines slightly with less data, reaching 42.08 at 45k and 40.12 at 20k examples.
