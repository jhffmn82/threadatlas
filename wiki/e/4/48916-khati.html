Khati in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Khati was a powerful nation inhabiting Cale-Syria and the Orontes valley, also known in Scripture as the Hittites. It resisted Egyptian and Assyrian conquest, developed military arts, and became prominent in trade through Katish, its sacred city, and Carchemish. Rameses the Great defeated it, as recorded at Thebes.

Khati is described as a powerful nation inhabiting Caele-Syria and the valley of the Orontes, known in Scripture as the Hittites. They opposed the Assyrians and Egyptians, placed obstacles in the path of conquest, developed military arts, and became prominent merchants centered at Carchemish and Katish.
