Holmes's farewell note

Abstract written by a model

A three-page note to Dr. John Watson, written at Reichenbach Falls, recounts Holmes’s final confrontation with Moriarty, identifies evidence to convict the gang, directs Inspector Patterson to it, gives property to Mycroft, and sends greetings to Mrs. Watson.

Holmes's farewell note is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
