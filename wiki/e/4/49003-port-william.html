Port William in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Port William is the first settlement and a village at the mouth of the Kentucky River, where it meets the Ohio, on Ohio River bottom land. Its landing draws keel boats, boatmen, idle citizens, and whisky sellers, sustaining a corn-whisky trade through groceries and saloons. It is where Rose Simon and her sisters are separated, where Aunt Katy and Suza Fairfield live, and where Suza returns and later rides with Don Carlo.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Port William was the first settlement at the mouth of the Kentucky River, where the river meets the Ohio. Its landing attracted keel boats, boatmen, idle citizens, and whisky sellers, including Dave Deminish and Redhead Sam Sims.

SCENE FOURTH--ROXIE DAYMON AND ROSE SIMON.

Port William, at the mouth of the Kentucky River, is identified as the place where Rose Simon and her sisters were separated.

SCENE FIFTH.

Port William is the village where Aunt Katy and Suza Fairfield live. Suza returns there after her escape, and it is later the home of the belle Suza and the place toward which she and Don Carlo ride after the camp meeting.
