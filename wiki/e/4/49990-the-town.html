1 section; each heading opens the text

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

The Magic Umbrella carries Trot and Button-Bright to the town and sets them down in front of the store. Trot changes the yarn inside the store while Button-Bright remains outside, attracting the attention of a wondering crowd.
