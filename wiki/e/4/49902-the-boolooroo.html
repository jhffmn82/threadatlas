  • In Sky Island: The Boolooroo, Quitey Righty, is the sky-blue king and elected monarch of Sky Island, also called the Blue Country and the country of the Blueskins. He has a round body and long neck, blue skin, eyes, hair, and clothing, and wears a star-shaped jewel with blue stones. Though elected for a three-hundred-year term, he claims to have reigned not quite two hundred years; the Royal Book of Records shows that he has completed, and exceeded, his lawful term. He keeps that book and the Royal Treasury locked up, with the key in his pocket, apparently to conceal his fraud and retain power. What matters most is his cruel, deceitful rule. He steals Button-Bright's Magic Umbrella, enslaves Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright, and assigns them work in Sky Island. He threatens punishment by “patching,” a punishment he invented, and uses the Great Knife against prisoners. He is feared, hated, and friendless; he hates everyone, treats the strangers cruelly, and is afraid of the Pinkies. He is father of the Six Snubnosed Princesses and married to the Queen, but even his household brings him anger and demands. As the travelers seek escape and the Book of Records threatens his position, he suspects Ghip-Ghisizzle, orders him bound and scheduled for patching, and tries to hold his rule during the Pinkies' invasion. He imprisons Cap'n Bill and repeatedly orders him patched, eventually proposing to join him to a billygoat. When he tries to operate the Great Knife, the goat knocks him down, and Trot and Cap'n Bill bind him beneath it. Trot takes control, keeps him locked in the Room of the Great Knife, and removes him from rule because he has ruled beyond his lawful term. He loses the palace and treasure and must leave with the Six Snubnosed Princesses, saying he will reform and become a model private citizen.

Narrative, section by section

17 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

The Boolooroo of the Blues is named in the contents as the subject of chapter 5.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

Quitey Righty Boolooroo is the blue ruler of Sky Island, with a round body, long neck, blue skin, blue eyes, blue hair, and blue clothing. He threatens to punish the travelers for entering his garden and injuring him, but Cap'n Bill shakes him until he drops the Magic Umbrella. He says that Blueskins live exactly six hundred years, that he has reigned for nearly two hundred years of his three-hundred-year term, and that the Boolooroo is elected in Sky Island's system of government.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

The Boolooroo orders the Magic Umbrella locked in his Royal Treasury and releases the prisoners because he believes they are now unable to escape. He assigns Trot to the Six Snubnosed Princesses, Cap'n Bill to be the Royal Nectar Mixer, and Button-Bright to be the Royal Bootblue, threatening disobedient prisoners with being patched.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

The Boolooroo rules Sky Island and is described as cruel, with the power to punish people by having them patched. He keeps the Royal Book of Records locked in the Royal Treasury and may be falsely claiming that he has ruled for only two hundred years.

THE BLUE CITY

The Boolooroo is said to be misrepresenting the length of his reign and trying to steal a hundred years. The Book of Records would reportedly prove that he has reigned three hundred years and would require him to resign the throne to Ghip-Ghisizzle.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Boolooroo becomes angry after his daughters denounce Trot and the Queen asks him for gold. He cannot make the Magic Umbrella work and threatens Ghip-Ghisizzle unless he learns its secret. He plans to send the three strangers through the Arch of Phinis and threatens to chop off their heads and put the wrong heads on them if they survive.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Boolooroo raises an alarm, shouting that his golden Key has been stolen. He becomes furious as soldiers, guards, and servants search the palace and pursue the fugitives.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Boolooroo stole the travelers' Magic Umbrella in the Blue Country and made them slaves. The travelers say that he wanted to patch them.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Boolooroo is described as the ruler of the Blueskins, whom the travelers fear and dislike. He stole the Magic Umbrella and prevented Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill from returning home, causing the parrot to repeatedly condemn him.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Boolooroo is in possession of Button-Bright's Magic Umbrella and is expected to resist giving it up. The Royal Record Book reportedly proves that he has already completed his lawful three-hundred-year reign, making Ghip-Ghisizzle a possible successor.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Boolooroo suspects Ghip-Ghisizzle of stealing the Book of Records and orders him bound. He plans to have Ghip-Ghisizzle patched, but abandons the operation temporarily when the Pinkies invade and orders the City gates closed.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

The Boolooroo is nervous about the Pinkies and orders the Blueskins to attack them. He flees back to the City during the first attack, orders longer sharp sticks to be made, and later stops fleeing to command that the fallen Cap'n Bill be captured.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

The Boolooroo keeps Cap'n Bill hidden until morning so he can be patched to Tiggle. He has ordered the patching to take place after breakfast and has soldiers guarding the palace.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Boolooroo sleeps while Cap'n Bill is held in the adjoining room, and he has devised a cord arrangement to be awakened if the sailor tries to escape. The Royal Record Book shows that he has ruled the Blue Country beyond the lawful term and has no right to rule.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The Boolooroo discovers that Cap'n Bill has escaped, recaptures him, and orders him to be patched. After Tiggle cannot be found, he orders the first living creature encountered to be patched to Cap'n Bill, laughs at the prospect of joining Cap'n Bill to a billygoat, and tries to operate the Great Knife. The billygoat knocks him among the benches, and Trot and Cap'n Bill later tie him into the frame beneath the knife.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Boolooroo is bound in the place where Cap'n Bill had been and is threatened with being sliced if anyone enters without Trot's permission. After Trot takes control, he remains locked in the Room of the Great Knife with the goat guarding him.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The former Boolooroo is removed from the Room of the Great Knife and told that he is no longer ruler. He loses the palace and its treasure, must leave with the Six Snubnosed Princesses, and says he will reform and live as a model private citizen.
