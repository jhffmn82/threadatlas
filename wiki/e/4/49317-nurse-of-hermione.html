THE NURSE OF HERMIONE, CHORUS.

The Nurse tries to prevent Hermione from killing herself and urges her to return inside. She reassures Hermione that Achilles’ son may pardon her and that Menelaus will not abandon her, then warns her not to appear outside the mansion.
