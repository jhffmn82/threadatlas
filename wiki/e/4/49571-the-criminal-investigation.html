Abstract written by a model

The King’s Pyland criminal investigation concerns Silver Blaze’s disappearance, John Straker’s murder, and Ned Hunter’s drugging. Colonel Ross and Inspector Gregory enlist Sherlock Holmes while Fitzroy Simpson is held as the principal suspect. Holmes finds the missing horse, postpononing further work on Straker’s murder.
