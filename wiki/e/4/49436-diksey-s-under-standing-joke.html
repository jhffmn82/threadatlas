Diksey's under-standing joke

Abstract written by a model

Diksey Horner's joke compares the Hoppers' one leg with the Horners' two, saying the Hoppers have less “under-standing” or “standundering,” while denying it means less understanding. Jak praises it, but the Hoppers misunderstand it and pretend to laugh to prevent war, causing tension between the groups.

Diksey's under-standing joke is in one work on this site, The Patchwork Girl of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Diksey Horner joked that the Hoppers have less “under-standing” than the Horners because the Hoppers have only one leg. The joke refers to legs being underneath the body, and Jak considers it the best joke he has heard.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Diksey's joke claims that the Hoppers have less "standundering" because they have one leg while the Horners have two, although he says he does not mean that they have less understanding. The Hoppers do not understand the joke, but pretend to laugh at it to prevent war.
