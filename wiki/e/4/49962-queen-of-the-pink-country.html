Queen of the Pink Country in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Queen of the Pinkies is an office regulated by Trot under a new law: its holder has the same food, dresses, good times, money, and no better home than the people. Trot holds it temporarily, then appoints Rosalie the Witch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Under the new law, the Queen of the Pinkies will no longer be required to endure poverty and hardship, but will receive the same conditions as the people and no more. Trot first holds the position temporarily and then appoints Rosalie the Witch to it.
