Abstract written by a model

The magic charm is a scarce, hard-to-get object that Ojo seeks to make to undo the Liquid of Petrifaction’s marble transformation and restore Unc Nunkie and Margolotte to life. Dr. Pipt says the compound can restore them instantly if its five ingredients are obtained: a six-leaved clover, the left wing of a yellow butterfly, water from a dark well, three hairs from the tip of a Woozy’s tail, and one drop of oil from a live man’s body. The charm also contains a flesh-wound charm. Ozma permits Dr. Pipt to make it, on condition that his magic powers are removed afterward. By the end, the Crooked Magician has sent for the ingredients and Ojo has obtained four, but cannot obtain the yellow butterfly’s left wing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Dr. Pipt says that a compound can destroy the spell of the Liquid of Petrifaction and restore Margolotte and Unc Nunkie instantly. Its ingredients include a six-leaved clover, the left wing of a yellow butterfly, water from a dark well, three hairs from a Woozy's tail, and a drop of oil from a live man's body.

Chapter Eight

The Foolish Owl's poem says that Ojo is searching for a charm and that charms are scarce and hard to get.

Chapter Nine

The magic charm is intended to help restore Unc Nunkie and Margolotte to life. The Woozy's three tail hairs are one part of the charm.

Chapter Sixteen

The magic charm is the object Ojo seeks to make from the needed ingredients. It is intended to restore Unc Nunkie and Margolotte to life after they are turned to marble.

Chapter Eighteen

The magic charm is intended to restore Margolotte and Unc Nunkie from their marble forms to life. Ozma agrees that Dr. Pipt may make it, with the condition that his magic powers will be removed afterward.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

The magic charm requires five ingredients sent for by the Crooked Magician. Ojo obtains four of them, including the drop of oil from a live man's body, but cannot obtain the left wing of a yellow butterfly.
