Abstract written by a model

Valhalla is Odin's beautiful great hall and residence in Asgard. Heroes who fall bravely in battle feast, drink mead, and recover there nightly from daily fighting wounds. Its eastern side contains mistletoe that can harm Baldur, and it has a golden roof.

Valhalla is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter XXXI

Valhalla is Odin's beautiful hall and residence in Asgard. It is where heroes who fall bravely in battle feast, drink mead, and recover each night from the wounds received in their daily fighting.

Chapter XXXIII

Valhalla is mentioned as the place whose eastern side contains the mistletoe that can harm Baldur. It is also described as having a golden roof in Matthew Arnold's poem about Baldur's death.
