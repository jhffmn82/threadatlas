William Kirwan's letter in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

VII. The Reigate Squires

William Kirwan received a letter by the afternoon post on the day he was killed, but the envelope was destroyed. Holmes reasons that the letter may have contained the message bringing Kirwan out at the hour of his death and asks about the missing remainder of the sheet.

Sherlock Holmes was as good as his word, for about one o’clock he

The letter was written by two people alternating words, with the stronger hand writing first and leaving spaces for the weaker hand. Holmes concludes that the two writers were the younger and older Cunninghams and that the stronger-handed writer planned the affair.
