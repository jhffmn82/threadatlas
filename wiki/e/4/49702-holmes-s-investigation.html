VIII. The Crooked Man

Sherlock Holmes's investigation focuses on the supposed murder of Colonel James Barclay at Lachine in Aldershot. He has gathered information from Major Murphy and other officers about the Barclays, their marriage, Colonel Barclay's character, and the regiment, and he recruits Dr. John Watson to help complete the inquiry.
