Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The attempted recovery of the papers is conducted by Sherlock Holmes, who lets Joseph Harrison make repeated efforts to take papers from Percy Phelps’s room, then watches outside and confronts him after the final nighttime attempt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

It was seven o’clock when I awoke, and I set off at once for

Joseph Harrison makes repeated efforts to recover the papers from Percy Phelps’s room, including a nighttime attempt when he believes the room is unguarded. Holmes prevents the final attempt by keeping watch outside the window and confronting Joseph as he leaves with the papers.
