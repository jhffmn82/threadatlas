Also called: 'Sizzle, Blueskin, Boolooroo, GHIP-GHISIZZLE, Ghip, Ghip Ghisizzle, Ghip-'Sizzle, Majordomo, Sizzle, friend Sizzle, new Boolooroo, the Blueskin, the Majordomo, the Majordomo and Master of Ceremonies
and 4 morethe officer, the rightful Ruler of the Blues, the young officer, their guide
  • In Sky Island: Ghip-Ghisizzle is a Blueskin royal officer, Majordomo, and Master of Ceremonies in the Blue Country, marked by a blue-gold chain and expected to become the Boolooroo. He is friendly and protective toward Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, Trot, and the other strangers, guiding them through the Blue City, explaining its customs, giving them rooms, and trying to shield them from hostile servants, soldiers, and the Boolooroo's plan to send them through the Arch of Phinis. He sympathizes with the prisoners because he disapproves of their treatment. His central concern is the succession. Though the current Boolooroo dislikes him and conceals the length of his reign, Ghip-Ghisizzle believes the Royal Record Book can establish whether he is the lawful ruler. After the Book disappears, he denies taking it and suspects Button-Bright. The Boolooroo has him bound and schedules him to be patched with Tiggle. The Six Snubnosed Princesses rescue him only to confine him and demand that he marry one of them; he refuses. He escapes over the City Wall, reaches the Pinkies' camp, and is captured by Captain Coralie. With the Royal Record Book, Ghip-Ghisizzle learns that the Boolooroo has ruled beyond the lawful three hundred years and that he has been the rightful ruler since Thursday. He seeks the former Boolooroo's resignation and agrees to help Button-Bright and the Pinkies recover the Magic Umbrella if they help him. Trot appoints him Boolooroo, and he becomes ruler of the Blue Country. He opposes patching, agrees to destroy the Great Knife and care for the billygoat, offers his little cabin to the former royal family, refuses to marry the former Princesses because he judges them wicked, and is ordered by Trot to treat the people of the Blue City nicely while she is away.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

Ghip-Ghisizzle is named in the contents. Chapters state that Ghip-Ghisizzle proves friendly and later has a bad time.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

Ghip-Ghisizzle is a young Blueskin officer who treats Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill kindly and instructs them in their duties. He secretly says that he is to become the next Boolooroo and suspects that the current ruler is concealing the true length of his reign.

THE BLUE CITY

Ghip-Ghisizzle guides Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright through the Blue City and protects them from hostile Blueskins. He explains the customs and history of Sky Island and asks Button-Bright to retrieve the Book of Records from the Treasure Chamber.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

Ghip-Ghisizzle permits Trot's animal companions to remain in the servants' hall and advises the prisoners to stay safely in a small room. He tells the Boolooroo that the strangers cannot legally be sent through the Arch of Phinis before they have lived six hundred years, but he cannot stop the king's planned experiment. He gives Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright rooms in the palace and tries to protect them from the servants and soldiers.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

Ghip-Ghisizzle denies stealing the Book of Records and secretly suspects that Button-Bright took it. He is bound and scheduled to be patched with Tiggle, but the Six Snubnosed Princesses rescue him and imprison him until he agrees to marry one of them.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

Ghip-Ghisizzle escapes from the Room of the Great Knife and is hidden in a chamber by the Six Snubnosed Princesses. When Trot enters, he escapes through the window, runs along the palace and City walls, and reaches the Pinkies' camp, where Captain Coralie captures him.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

Ghip-Ghisizzle learns from the Royal Record Book that he has been the rightful Ruler of the Blues since Thursday. He plans to force the Boolooroo to resign and agrees to let Button-Bright and the Pinkies recover the Magic Umbrella and return home if they help him.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Ghip-Ghisizzle explains that the Book of Royal Records proves the Boolooroo has ruled beyond his lawful three hundred years, making Ghip-Ghisizzle the lawful Boolooroo since the previous Thursday. Trot appoints him Boolooroo of the Blues, and he agrees to destroy the Great Knife, care for the billygoat, and govern the Blueskins.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Ghip-Ghisizzle refuses to marry Indigo in exchange for information about the Magic Umbrella because he considers the former Princesses to have wicked hearts and evil thoughts. As the new Boolooroo, he is left in charge of the Blue Country while Trot is away.
