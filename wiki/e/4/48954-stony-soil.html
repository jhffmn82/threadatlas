is_a arable loam [a few inches thick and formed from stone detritus and decomposed vegetable matter] The stony soil of Yucatan consists mainly of limestone with only a few inches of arable loam formed from stone detritus and decomposed vegetation. Despite its thinness, it can be highly fertile, and it supports large trees as well as the hennequen plant.

The stony soil of Yucatan consists mainly of limestone with only a few inches of arable loam formed from stone detritus and decomposed vegetation. Despite its thinness, it can be highly fertile, and it supports large trees as well as the hennequen plant.
