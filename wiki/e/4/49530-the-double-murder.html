the double murder in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The double murder is believed to have killed Mrs. Browner and a seafaring man, probably on Wednesday, Tuesday, or earlier. Holmes treats two ears as evidence, infers jealousy as the motive, and suspects Jim Browner as the sender and murderer, who sent the packet to announce the deed or cause pain.
