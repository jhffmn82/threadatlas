THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are threatened with being thrown over the edge of the Pink Country because Tourmaline claims they do not harmonize with its color scheme. Polychrome prevents the execution by invoking the Pink Country protection law and then makes Trot Queen.
