Methodology .. 3.4 / The M-RAG Framework

The selection and refinement procedures are formulated as Markov Decision Processes involving states, actions, and rewards. Agent-S operates in a bandit-like setting, while Agent-R receives transition rewards based on changes in hypothesis quality.
