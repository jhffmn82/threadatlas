Helen’s escape from Egypt

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Helen and Menelaus’s escape, Helen hath departed from this realm, carries off your sailors, escape, escape yourselves, our common good, our safety, saved ourselves, the planned escape, the project, this scheme

Abstract written by a model

Helen’s escape from Egypt is a planned flight with Menelaus. It depends on concealing Menelaus, falsely reporting that he died at sea, and obtaining permission and a vessel for his funeral rites. Helen and Menelaus board the funeral ship with surviving sailors, who arm themselves; Menelaus leads it, they defeat Theoclymenus’s sailors, seize control, and flee Egypt.

CHOR. My sentiments on prophets well accord

The planned escape depends on concealing Menelaus’s presence, reporting that he died in the sea, obtaining the king’s permission for a funeral, and securing a ship. Menelaus and the surviving sailors will then arm themselves, board the vessel, and flee with Helen.

CHORUS.

Helen and Menelaus plan to use the funeral voyage to flee Egypt. Menelaus is to lead the ship, and Helen intends to go with him despite Theoclymenus’s request that she remain behind.

Swift bark of Sidon, by whose dashing oars

Helen’s escape from Egypt is accomplished through Menelaus’s false report of his death and the staging of a funeral voyage. She boards the ship, and the Greeks seize control after defeating Theoclymenus’s sailors.
