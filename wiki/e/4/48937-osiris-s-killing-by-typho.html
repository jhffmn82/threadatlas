Osiris's killing by Typho

event · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Osiris's death, cruel murder, death of Osiris, his death, his killing by his brother Typho, murder, murder of Osiris, the death of Osiris, the manner of his killing by his brother Typho
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Osiris's death occurs when Typhon murders him, cuts his body into twenty-six pieces, and gives them to his confederates.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the murder of Osiris is carried out by Typhon and his conspirators, who trap him in a chest, throw it into the Nile, and later dismember and scatter his body.
  • In Vestiges of the Mayas, Osiris's killing by Typho is followed by the disposal and scattering of his remains. (Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa)

Osiris's death in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Osiris is murdered by his brother Typhon while ruling Egypt justly. Typhon cuts his body into six-and-twenty pieces and gives the pieces to his confederates so that they share in the guilt.

murder of Osiris in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The murder of Osiris was carried out by Typhon and his conspirators, who trapped him in a fitted chest and threw it into the Nile. Typhon later dismembered Osiris's body and scattered the pieces.
