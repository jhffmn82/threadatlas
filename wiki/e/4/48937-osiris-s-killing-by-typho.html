Osiris's killing by Typho

event · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Osiris's death, cruel murder, death of Osiris, his death, his killing by his brother Typho, murder, murder of Osiris, the death of Osiris, the manner of his killing by his brother Typho

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Osiris's death occurs when Typhon murders him, cuts his body into twenty-six pieces, and gives them to his confederates. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the murder of Osiris is carried out by Typhon and his conspirators, who trap him in a chest, throw it into the Nile, and later dismember and scatter his body. In Vestiges of the Mayas, Osiris's killing by Typho is followed by the disposal and scattering of his remains. (Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa)
