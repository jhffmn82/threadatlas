Royal Record Book in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Royal Record Book is a small light-blue leather book kept locked in the Treasure Chamber by the Boolooroo. It records the Pink Country as the Sunset Country and contains the terms of the Boolooroo’s rule and Ghip-Ghisizzle’s lawful succession. The Boolooroo hides it because it proves his permitted reign has expired. When it goes missing, he fears exposure. Button-Bright has taken it; Ghip-Ghisizzle reads it, learns the reign ended on Thursday, and recovers evidence that establishes his succession.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BLUE CITY

The Book of Records contains information about the Pink Country and calls it the Sunset Country. The Boolooroo keeps it locked in the Treasure Chamber because it would reveal the true length of his reign.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Book of Records is discovered to be missing from the Treasure Chamber. Its loss frightens the Boolooroo because it may expose his fraud on the people.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

Button-Bright has kept the Royal Record Book since taking it from the Boolooroo's Treasure Chamber. Ghip-Ghisizzle reads it and discovers that the Boolooroo's lawful reign ended on Thursday, giving Ghip-Ghisizzle evidence against him.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Book of Royal Records is recovered from the Treasure Chamber by Ghip-Ghisizzle. It reveals that the Boolooroo has reigned longer than the permitted three hundred years and establishes Ghip-Ghisizzle's lawful succession.
