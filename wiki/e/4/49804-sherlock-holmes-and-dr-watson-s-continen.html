XII. The Final Problem

Sherlock Holmes proposes that Dr. John Watson accompany him on a temporary journey to the Continent while the police prepare to act against Professor Moriarty’s organization. Holmes gives Watson elaborate instructions involving luggage, separate cabs, the Lowther Arcade, a specially marked brougham, and a reserved first-class carriage at Victoria station.
