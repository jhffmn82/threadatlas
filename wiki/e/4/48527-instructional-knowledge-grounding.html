instructional knowledge grounding

method · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Instructional knowledge Grounding, grounding phase, instructional knowledge grounding method

Abstract written by a model

Instructional knowledge grounding is a method and setting for grounding LLMs in evidence. It helps LLMs find the most relevant evidence in a document list and reduces noise in retrieved documents. LLMs support answers with retrieved evidence, correct wrong predictions, and revise answers. Each revised answer and its cited evidence are added to the context for the next iteration; this setting also collects LLM trajectories using selected single-hop training questions.

instructional knowledge grounding is in one work on this site, Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

Instructional knowledge grounding is proposed to enable large language models to find the most relevant evidence from a document list. It is intended to reduce the influence of noise in retrieved documents.

3 .. 3.3

Instructional knowledge grounding guides LLMs to support generated answers with evidence from retrieved documents and to correct wrong predictions. Its revised answer and cited evidence are added to the context for the next iteration.

4.1 .. 4.2

Instructional knowledge grounding is the setting in which LLM trajectories are collected. The questions used for training are selected because their single-hop nature is similar to this setting.
