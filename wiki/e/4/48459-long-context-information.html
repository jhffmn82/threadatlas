Abstract

Long-context knowledge includes both global information and factual details that LongRAG is designed to understand more effectively. The unit describes this knowledge as complex and difficult for standard RAG approaches because chunking can disrupt its global structure and retrieval can introduce noise.

6

Long-context information is incompletely collected by existing methods, identified as one of the two main issues LongRAG addresses.
