Army of Conquest

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

The Army of Conquest is a Pinkie army formed to fight the Blueskins and support Ghip-Ghisizzle in his struggle for rule of the Blue Country. Rosalie proposes gathering it and marching through the Fog Bank against the Boolooroo. It consists of two hundred Pinkies, one hundred Sunsets and one hundred Sunrises, with men and women equally divided, and is commanded and led by Cap'n Bill. Expected to be ready within days, it relies on sharp sticks as an advantage over Blueskin weapons. The army rides giant frogs across the Fog Bank, reaches the City wall, and must halt before its locked gates. Its aid depends on return of the Magic Umbrella and permission for the adventurers to fly home.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

Rosalie proposes assembling an army from the Pinkies and marching it through the Fog Bank against the Boolooroo. The army is expected to be ready in two or three days, and Cap'n Bill believes its sharp sticks will give it an advantage over the Blueskins' weapons.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Army of Conquest consists of one hundred Sunset warriors and one hundred Sunrise warriors, equally divided between men and women. It is placed under Cap'n Bill's command and reaches the Blue Country after riding across the Fog Bank on giant frogs.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

Cap'n Bill's army reaches the wall of the City after the Pinkies break through the Fog Bank. It is forced to halt outside because the City gates are closed and locked.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Pink Army is offered as a force that will fight for Ghip-Ghisizzle against the Boolooroo. Its support is conditional on Ghip-Ghisizzle returning the Magic Umbrella and allowing the adventurers to fly home.
