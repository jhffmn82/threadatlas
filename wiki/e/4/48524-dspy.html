Also called: Dspy

Abstract written by a model

In BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, DSPy is a programming-based, declarative language-model pipeline framework that creates compositional modules and compiles calls into self-improving pipelines, but lacks research flexibility. In Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering, DSPy is a language-model-empowered programming framework evaluated as generation with retrieval and a retrieval-then-read baseline; it scores 42.43, 13.40, and 43.43 on three datasets, then serves as a baseline below GenGround in BM25 and Google Search settings.
