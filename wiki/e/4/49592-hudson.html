Hudson

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Head-keeper Hudson, the man who had been picked off the wreck, the sailor, the sailor Hudson, the seaman, this accursed Hudson, this animal Hudson

Abstract written by a model

Hudson is a sailor and former shipmate of Mr. Trevor and Mr. Beddoes. As a young seaman, he survives the destruction of the Gloria Scott by clinging to wreckage and being rescued by Evans and others. Roughly thirty years later, he returns to Mr. Trevor’s house, serving first as gardener and then butler, while behaving abusively. He uses knowledge of the past to control Mr. Trevor and apparently Mr. Beddoes, living on their fear of exposure. He tracks down Evans, threatens Beddoes with exposure and violence, and is identified as having “told all.” Hudson and Beddoes eventually disappear.

Hudson is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
