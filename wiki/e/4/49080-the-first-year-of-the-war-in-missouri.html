SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

The first year of the war in Missouri is portrayed as an unusually harsh campaign in which Price’s army marches more than eight hundred miles, often lacks food and equipment, and fights frequent engagements. Despite these hardships, the army remains cheerful and is said never to lose a battle on Missouri soil during that year.
