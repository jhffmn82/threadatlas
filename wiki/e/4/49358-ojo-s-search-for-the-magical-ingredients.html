Ojo's search for the magical ingredients in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Ojo's search for magical ingredients is a rescue mission to save Unc Nunkie and Margolotte by obtaining Dr. Pipt's restoring compound ingredients. Traveling with the Patchwork Girl and Bungle, he gathers three Woozy-tail hairs, a six-leaved clover, water from a dark well, and oil from a live man's body. The journey leads from the Emerald City toward the Quadling Country, but it ends unresolved when the Tin Woodman refuses to allow harm to a yellow butterfly for its left wing.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Ojo's rescue mission is to collect the ingredients for Dr. Pipt's restoring compound and return as quickly as possible. He undertakes the journey to save Unc Nunkie and Margolotte, traveling with the Patchwork Girl and Bungle.

Chapter Eighteen

Ojo already has the Woozy, whose tail contains the required three hairs, and receives permission to keep the six-leaved clover. He still needs a gill of water from a dark well and is willing to search for years to save Unc Nunkie.

Chapter Nineteen

The journey to the dark well takes the adventurers from the Emerald City to Jack Pumpkinhead's home and then south toward the Quadling Country. They travel for two days across fields and sandy plains before reaching palm trees and the rocks leading toward the mountains.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

Ojo's quest requires five ingredients: three hairs from a Woozy's tail, a six-leaved clover, water from a dark well, oil from a live man's body, and the left wing of a yellow butterfly. He obtains the first four but cannot obtain the final ingredient because the Tin Woodman refuses to permit a butterfly to be harmed.
