Abstract written by a model

Tom Ditamus, a lazy, cowardly man with a wife, two dirty children, and an ox-drawn covered wagon, agrees to transport Suza Fairfield for ten dollars and captures her for the Demitt relations. Suza escapes while his family sleeps, and he continues traveling after discovering she is gone.
