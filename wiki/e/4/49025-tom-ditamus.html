Tom Ditamus in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Tom Ditamus, a lazy, cowardly man with a wife, two dirty children, and an ox-drawn covered wagon, agrees to transport Suza Fairfield for ten dollars and captures her for the Demitt relations. Suza escapes while his family sleeps, and he continues traveling after discovering she is gone.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE FIFTH.

Tom Ditamus agrees to take Suza Fairfield to the backwoods of the Cumberland Mountains for ten dollars. He captures her, but she tricks him and escapes while he and his family sleep; Tom then continues traveling after discovering her absence.
