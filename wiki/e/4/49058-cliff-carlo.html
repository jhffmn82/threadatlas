Cliff Carlo in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Cliff Carlo, oldest son and representative man of the Carlo family descended from Don Carlo and Suza Fairfield, is a prosperous Kentucky farmer and subscriber to THE ROUGH DIAMOND. During the Simon estate dispute, he consults Cousin Cæsar but stays noncommittal because Roxie Daymon is a near relative, writes Governor Morock, then joins the Southern armies and survives the war unharmed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

Cliff Carlo counsels with Cousin Cæsar about the expenses of contesting the Simon estate, but remains non-committal because Roxie Daymon is a near relative. He later writes to Governor Morock for information about the estate and joins the Southern armies, passing through the war without sickness or wounds.
