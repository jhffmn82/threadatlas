Abstract written by a model

Hurlstone is the Musgrave family estate in western Sussex, centered on an old L-shaped building with a 1607 inscription, older-looking fabric, and a cellar beneath a stone passage. Its grounds include a park, oak, former elm, avenue, and lake. Brunton secretly examines the ritual there, then disappears; Rachel Howells also vanishes.

Hurlstone is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VI. The Musgrave Ritual

Hurlstone is the Musgrave family estate in western Sussex. Strange events there include Brunton’s secret examination of the ritual document, his disappearance, and Rachel Howells’s disappearance.

Hurlstone is an old L-shaped building with an ancient nucleus dated by an inscription to 1607, though its beams and stonework are considered older. Its old section contains a cellar beneath a stone passage, and the estate includes the oak, the former elm, and a lake or mere.
