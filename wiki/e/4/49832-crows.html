THE SCARECROW AND THE TIN WOODMAN

The crows laugh at the Scarecrow after he loses one painted eye. Their King suggests using a string to pull the Tin Woodman from the river, and the crows lift both friends into the air. Instead of landing them safely, they carry them to a tall dead tree, leave them suspended there, and fly away laughing.
