Abstract written by a model

The colossal statue of Chaacmol is an effigy discovered by the narrator at Chichen-Itza and named from the circumstances of its discovery. Found in an excavation, it was placed on rollers for removal, but workers first tried to rebury it face downward, fearing desecration of the future body of a great warrior and king. After the men were disarmed, the narrator left it wrapped in oilcloth beneath a palm-leaf hut. Its head, found near urns in its burial monument, was taken aboard the gunboat Libertad to the National Museum of Mexico without the narrator’s consent or compensation. Its identity remains disputed.
