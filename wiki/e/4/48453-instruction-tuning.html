  • In LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering, Instruction-Tuning uses 2,600 high-quality LRGinstruction examples to fine-tune ChatGLM, Qwen1.5, Vicuna, Llama2, and Llama3 models, producing a model that supports the extractor, filter, and generator.
  • In BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, instruction tuning is represented by work on training language models to follow instructions with human feedback and by a survey of instruction tuning for large language models.
  • In Ask in Any Modality, Instruction Tuning fine-tunes or instruct-tunes generation components for specific applications, improving visual feature extraction, prompt generation, adaptive retrieval, evidence identification, ranking, image-difference description, knowledge balancing, and generation quality.
  • In SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains, instruction fine-tuning computes loss only on assistant-response tokens and is used in Stage-I with general instruction, context-aware QA, and retrieval-related data.
  • In SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation, instruction tuning is used as a first-line method for aligning LLMs and limiting harmful behavior, but it fails to address malicious fine-tuning and prompt injection.
