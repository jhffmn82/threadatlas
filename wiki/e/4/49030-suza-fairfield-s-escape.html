Suza Fairfield escapes at night after persuading Tom Ditamus that she hates Aunt Katy and would go anywhere to leave her. She follows the Kentucky River, crosses White's Creek at a ford, rejoins the road, and reaches Aunt Katy's house in Port William by dawn.
