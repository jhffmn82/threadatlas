Abstract written by a model

Egyptian mythology is characterized as a transformed or mythical version of historical traditions that the author believes originated among the Mayas. The unit specifically compares its stories of Osiris, Isis, Typho, Aroeris, Nephthis, and Thoth with Maya figures, events, symbols, and monuments.
