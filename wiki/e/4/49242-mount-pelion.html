Mount Pelion

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Pelion, Pelion’s cave, mountain-top, topmost heights

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, Pelion is the cave to which Peleus says he will go after burying his grandson. In The Argonautica, Mount Pelion overlooks the departure route, while its nymphs watch the heroes wielding the oars. In The Library, Pelion hosted Acastus and Peleus’s hunt, where Peleus was abandoned and later rescued by Chiron.
