Lord Holdhurst in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Lord Holdhurst is a prominent Conservative politician, statesman, nobleman, Cabinet member, and England’s foreign minister, regarded as a future premier despite limited means. Percy Phelps’s maternal uncle, he gives Phelps the original secret treaty between England and Italy and orders it copied securely at the Foreign Office. Later, in his Downing Street chambers, he courteously receives Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson, confirms the instructions, denies sharing the plan, and warns that disclosure could have grave consequences.

XI. The Naval Treaty

Lord Holdhurst is Percy Phelps's maternal uncle and a prominent Conservative politician who becomes foreign minister. He gives Phelps the original secret treaty between England and Italy and instructs him to copy it securely at the Foreign Office.

“Where are we going to now?” I asked, as we left the office.

Lord Holdhurst receives Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson courteously in his Downing Street chambers. He confirms that he gave instructions there for the naval treaty to be copied, denies telling anyone about the plan, and says that the treaty becoming known could have very grave results. Holmes observes that Lord Holdhurst is not rich and that his boots have been resoled.
