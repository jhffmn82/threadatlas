flight of the Magic Umbrella

Abstract written by a model

The flight of the Magic Umbrella is a swift, unfaltering air journey triggered when Button-Bright holds the umbrella open and speaks a destination or wishes to travel. It carries Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and Trot faster than railroad trains, amazing villagers because it flies without visible power. On a trial trip it passes over Smuggler's Cove, circles, and returns without stopping for a drifting boat. Later it crosses the Pacific toward Sky Island, through clouds and down in an arching descent.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The umbrella's flying begins when Button-Bright holds it open and speaks a destination or expresses a wish to travel there. It carries him quickly through the air, but the journeys can be long and tiring, and it does not stop or reverse simply because he changes his mind.

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

The flight of the Magic Umbrella amazes Cap'n Bill and the villagers because the umbrella carries people through the air without visible power. During the trial flight it passes over Smuggler's Cove, circles, and returns, despite the passengers' inability to make it stop and help a drifting boat.

THE ISLAND IN THE SKY

The Magic Umbrella's flight is swift and unfaltering, rising higher as it moves away from the first island. It travels over the sea, passes through clouds, then follows a gradual arched descent toward the real Sky Island.
