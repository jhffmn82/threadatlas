Foreign Office

Also called: The Foreign Office, his department of the Foreign Office, the Foreign Office, the office, the office (where Holmes and Watson began), these offices

Abstract written by a model

In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, the Foreign Office was an organization on Charles Street with a servants’ side door; it employed Percy Phelps in a trusted role and kept the Naval Treaty in a secure room, from which the original disappeared while he investigated delayed coffee, with examination seeming to exclude ordinary entry or concealment. In Dr. Elsie Inglis, the Foreign Office was responsible for the safety of Scottish Women’s Hospitals’ British outpost hospitals, cooperating with Dr. Elsie Inglis to equip and convey units overseas, agreeing stipulations that did not hinder her work, and undertaking extensive care and diplomatic work for the hospitals.

XI. The Naval Treaty

The Foreign Office employs Percy Phelps in a position of trust and houses the secure room where he copies the Naval Treaty. The original treaty is stolen from a desk there while Phelps is away investigating a delay in receiving coffee.

“We examined the room also. There is no possibility of a secret

The Foreign Office is where Percy Phelps worked and where the important treaty was kept. The treaty disappeared from a room there under circumstances that appeared to rule out ordinary entrances and hiding places.
