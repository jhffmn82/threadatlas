IX. The Resident Patient

After the second visit by the supposed Russian patients, Blessington finds several fresh, unusually large footprints on the light carpet in his room. Nothing has been taken, but the footprints indicate that one of the visitors apparently went upstairs while Trevelyan was occupied.
