” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

The early settlers in Kentucky endured Indian attacks and lived through a period of violent conflict. They were described as religious, and their preachers helped elevate and improve backwoods society. Settlers within ten miles of the Fairfield cabin gathered to decide how the orphaned sisters would be cared for.
