Command-R-35B in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Command-R-35B first appears

Abstract written by a model

Command-R-35B is a recent instruction-tuned multilingual generator model selected for multilingual generation experiments. It is multilingual by design and usually replies in the user language, though it sometimes replies in English and can be distracted by English Wikipedia context. It achieves very high Correct Language Rate across Korean, French, and Russian, including 100% in several configurations. With advanced prompts it consistently has the highest recall across considered languages; its highlighted baseline uses BGE-m3, translated language-specific prompts, and concatenated multilingual Wikipedia retrieval. Its English recall is lower than SOLAR-10.7B's, possibly from multilinguality.
