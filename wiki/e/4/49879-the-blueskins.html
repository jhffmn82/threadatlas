the Blueskins in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Blueskins are a people and citizens of the Blue Country, especially the Blue City, and inhabitants of Sky Island. Blue-skinned, tall, lanky, large compared with the Pinkies, and described as ugly and fierce, they have egg-shaped heads, long thin necks, short round bodies, and exceptionally long legs. They wear blue silk liveries as palace servants and use long winding-up cords weighted at the ends as weapons. Every man and woman is a voter under the government of Sky Island. Each is allowed exactly six hundred years of life; at that term's end they pass away rather than die and enter the Great Blue Grotto. Jealous of strangers and possible royal favor, the Blueskins dislike, threaten, and often pursue unfamiliar visitors, including Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright. They particularly hate the Six Snubnosed Princesses and are the Pinkies' natural enemies. They fear the Fog Bank above all else: no Blueskin has entered it, and pursuers stop when the travelers reach it. Their conflict with the Pinkies includes capturing prisoners in weighted cords, patching captured Pinkies, defending the City, and fighting with their cords, although several flee toward the Blue City when the Pinkies arrive. Many resent the Boolooroo, believing his treatment of the Earth people brought on the invasion. During the events, Blueskin soldiers capture the prisoners, carry out the Boolooroo's orders, chase escaping travelers, and later recapture Cap'n Bill. After Trot's conquest and the Boolooroo's defeat, they rejoice that the war is over, welcome Trot and the Pinkies, celebrate the destruction of the Great Knife, and receive Ghip-Ghisizzle as their new Boolooroo. Trot orders them to stop quarreling with the Pinkies and welcome them to the Blue City; she is also identified as their queen. Under Ghip-Ghisizzle after the conquest, they are secretly glad when Trot, the Pinkies, and the other strangers depart, and remain sullen during the departure.

Narrative, section by section

14 sections; each heading opens the text

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

Several Blueskins capture the prisoners with weighted cords, which wind around their bodies and hold them fast. They appear to be soldiers, and they later carry out the Boolooroo's orders concerning Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright.

THE BLUE CITY

The Blueskins have egg-shaped heads, long thin necks, short round bodies, and exceptionally long legs. They are nervous and restless, dislike Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright because of their strange appearance, and threaten to attack them, although Ghip-Ghisizzle's protection restrains them.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Blueskins in the servants' hall are amazed to see the Princesses' pets following Trot. Some insist that the animals and birds be removed, and the servants and soldiers generally dislike and threaten the strangers because they are jealous of possible royal favor.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

Blueskin guards and pursuers chase Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and Trot after their escape. The pursuing Blueskins stop at the Fog Bank because they regard it as dreadful and no Blueskin has ever entered it.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Blueskins are the Pinkies' natural enemies. The Pinkies initially mistake the travelers for Blueskins because they wear the Boolooroo's blue uniforms, although their skin is not blue.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Blueskins are the people of the Blue Country, who hate Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill. The travelers escape from them after the Boolooroo takes the Magic Umbrella.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Blueskins are described as larger than the Pinkies but as cowards. Their weapons are winding-up cords with weights on the ends, in contrast to the Pinkies' sharp sticks.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Blueskins are described as tall, lanky, ugly, and fierce. They fight with long cords weighted at the ends, and several Blueskins flee toward the Blue City when the Pinkies arrive.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Blueskins prepare to defend the City from the Pinkies with weighted cords. Many of them resent the Boolooroo and believe his treatment of the Earth people caused the invasion.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

Most of the Blueskins are asleep while a few soldiers guard the palace and pursue Ghip-Ghisizzle. They search for Ghip-Ghisizzle after seeing him run along the walls, but abandon the chase when he reaches the direction of the Pinkies' camp.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Blueskins awaken when a soldier sees Cap'n Bill crossing the wall. They pursue him, use Trot's rope ladder to head him off, recapture him, and carry him back into the Blue City.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Blueskins are described as hating the Six Snubnosed Princesses. Under Trot's orders, they are to stop quarreling with the Pinkies and welcome them to the Blue City.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Blueskins rejoice that the war is over and that the wicked Boolooroo has been defeated. They welcome Trot and the Pinkies, celebrate the destruction of the Great Knife, and receive Ghip-Ghisizzle as their new Boolooroo.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The Blues are ruled by Ghip-Ghisizzle after Trot's conquest. The people are secretly glad when Trot, the Pinkies, and the other strangers leave, and they remain sullen during the departure.

the Blueskins in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Blueskins are blue, long-legged people of the Blue City and soldiers of the Boolooroo, serving under the Captain of the Guards. Their only arms are cords until they use bluewood lances. They attack the Pinkies, retreat from sharp sticks, are defeated by enchanted weapons, and capture Cap'n Bill. After returning cautiously, they obey Trot, imprison the princesses, and prepare the Blue City for the Pinkies' arrival.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

The Blues are named in the contents in connection with the Boolooroo of the Blues, the Blue City, and the war with the Pinks.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

The Blueskins first attack the Pinkies with cord-and-weights but retreat when the Pinkies use their sharp sticks. Armed with longer bluewood lances, they are defeated by the enchanted Pinkies' weapons, then capture Cap'n Bill after he falls and carry him into the Blue City.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The soldiers return cautiously after recovering their courage. They obey Trot rather than the princesses, lock the princesses in their rooms, and are ordered to help prepare the Blue City for the Pinkies' arrival.
