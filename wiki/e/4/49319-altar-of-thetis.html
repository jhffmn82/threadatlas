The altar of Thetis is a hallowed sanctuary where Andromache and Molossus seek asylum. Though it offers protection, Hermione says neither altar nor temple will save Andromache, and Menelaus forces her from it before ordering her seizure.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

ANDROMACHE.

The altar of Thetis is part of the sanctuary where Andromache seeks protection. Hermione declares that neither the altar nor the temple will save Andromache from the threatened attack.

MENELAUS, MOLOSSUS, ANDROMACHE, CHORUS.

Thetis’s altar serves as the sanctuary where Andromache and Molossus seek protection. Menelaus claims that Andromache’s reliance on the altar is a craft and forces her to leave it before ordering her seizure.
