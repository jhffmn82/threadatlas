THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill are captured with weighted cords and then released when the Boolooroo decides they cannot escape without the Magic Umbrella. They are condemned to severe punishment and threatened with being patched, and each is assigned a different form of slavery.
