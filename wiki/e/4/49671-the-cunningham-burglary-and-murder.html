Abstract written by a model

The Cunningham burglary at Cunningham's house occurs about midnight when an intruder enters through a forced back door or pantry window. William Kirwan confronts the intruder and is shot through the heart with a revolver. Mr. Cunningham and Alec Cunningham report seeing the fleeing man, but Holmes investigates the scene, tests their account through deceptions, finds evidence, and exposes the Cunninghams.

VII. The Reigate Squires

The Cunningham burglary occurred at about midnight after the burglar forced the back-door lock or entered through the pantry window. William Kirwan confronted the intruder and was shot, while Mr. Cunningham and Alec Cunningham saw the fleeing man; Inspector Forrester believes the incident was connected to the Acton burglary.

We passed the pretty cottage where the murdered man had lived,

The investigation examines the forced door, the positions of the witnesses, the lighting in the house, and the possibility that the burglar took unusual objects rather than valuables. Holmes uses a staged illness, a false mistake in the reward notice, and a staged accident to uncover evidence and expose the Cunninghams.
