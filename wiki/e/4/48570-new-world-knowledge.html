Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Dataset Construction

New world knowledge consists of information outside LLMs' pre-training corpora, including changing events and topics such as politics, business, and entertainment. The dataset includes questions selected to make this knowledge novel to most LLMs.

RetrievalQA Challenges Adaptive RAG / 3.1 .. 3.4

new world knowledge questions are recognized more successfully as requiring retrieval than long-tail knowledge questions. For these questions, the experiments use top-five documents returned by Google search.

Improving Adaptive RAG Prompting / Method .. 4.3 / Conclusion

New world knowledge questions may contain time-sensitive information such as "last week" or "recent." TA-ARE improves performance on most new world questions and uses time awareness to support retrieval decisions for them.
