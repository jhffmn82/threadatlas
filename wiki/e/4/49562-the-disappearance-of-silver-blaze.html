I. Silver Blaze

Silver Blaze's disappearance occurs after a stranger appears at the King’s Pyland stables and stable-boy Ned Hunter is drugged with opium. The horse’s tracks are found in the hollow where John Straker died, but the horse remains missing despite a large reward and an extensive search.

It was evening before we reached the little town of Tavistock,

The disappearance of Silver Blaze is traced through horse tracks across Dartmoor to Mapleton stables. Holmes concludes that the horse wandered from the scene and was then taken and concealed by Silas Brown.
