Abstract

The "lost in the middle" issue affects existing long-context large language models and makes it difficult for them to handle information in long contexts effectively.

Introduction

The “lost in the middle” issue occurs when relevant context appears in the middle of a document rather than at its beginning or end. In this situation, long-context LLMs are prone to producing sub-optimal or incorrect responses.
