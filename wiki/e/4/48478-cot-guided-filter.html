CoT-guided filter

component · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: CoT-guided Filter, filtering stage, filters

Abstract written by a model

The CoT-guided filter is a two-stage LongRAG component evaluated in a one-time retrieval scenario. It uses Chain of Thought from all retrieved chunks to supply global clues, then evaluates question-chunk pairs and assigns relevance labels. By retaining answer-supporting chunks and filtering irrelevant ones, it improves evidence density and yields low-redundancy factual details for complex contexts.

Introduction

The CoT-guided filter uses Chain of Thought to provide global clues from all retrieved chunks. It instructs LLMs to review factual details carefully and filter out irrelevant chunks, thereby improving evidence density and understanding of complex, lengthy contexts.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.5

The CoT-guided Filter first generates a chain of thought from the retrieved chunks and then evaluates each chunk against the question using those global clues. It retains chunks labeled as supporting the answer, producing a low-redundancy set of factual details.
