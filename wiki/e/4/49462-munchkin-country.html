Abstract written by a model

In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Munchkin Country is a blue part of the Land of Oz with fertile fruits and flowers, jolly people, the Blue Forest, broad fields, and blue-domed houses; Ojo and Unc Nunkie travel from its remote forest through mountains toward the Gillikins. In The Lost Princess of Oz, Munchkin Country is one of Ozma’s four great countries in the Land of Oz, called its prettiest by the Scarecrow; Betsy Bobbin proposes visiting it with Dorothy and Trot. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, the Land of the Munchkins still contains unexpected inhabitants, including the tiny household of Mrs. Squealina Swyne and Professor Grunter Swyne. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Munchkin Country is a blue-tinted, well-cultivated eastern region of Oz, east of Winkie Country with Emerald City between them; Nimmie Amee lives there, and Dorothy’s house falls on the Wicked Witch there. In The Magic of Oz, Munchkin Country is a northeastern part of the Land of Oz near Gillikin Country, with a hidden river island containing the Magic Flower in its sparsely traveled north. In The Cowardly Lion of Oz, Munchkin Country contains the Emerald City; travelers arrive in a blue city, find the Travelers' Tree, and continue toward it. In Grampa in Oz, the Munchkin country is west of Oz and blue on maps, beginning at its edge in a deep blue forest reached by Grampa and Prince Tatters. In The Giant Horse of Oz, Munchkin Country contains Lost Lake of Orizon and a blue farm house; Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny land there and head toward a road to Jinjur's door.
