Abstract written by a model

The Enron Email dataset is a collection of 500,000 employee emails, partitioned into individual email data pieces and containing private personally identifiable information. It is a subset of GPT-Neo-1.3B pre-training data and is used in memorization experiments. Evaluations examine extraction and reproduction of private email content: 116 of 250 GPT-3.5-turbo prompts exactly matched content, while 121 were closely related; further experiments compare retrieval conditions.
