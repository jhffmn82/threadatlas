Beddington in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Beddington, a recently released forger and cracksman, impersonates Hall Pycroft to join Mawson & Williams. Identified by the Evening Standard, he gathers lock mouldings and strong-room knowledge, murders the watchman, steals securities, and is captured leaving with a carpet bag.

Beddington is identified in the Evening Standard as the criminal who used the false name Hall Pycroft to obtain employment at Mawson & Williams. He obtained mouldings of locks and knowledge of the strong room, murdered the watchman, stole securities, and was captured while leaving with a carpet bag.
