Hindostan

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Hindoostan

Abstract written by a model

Hindostan is a place containing highly elaborate, imposing temples and said to contain megalithic monuments and stepped temples. It is associated with Maya traditions, religious ideas, and altered surviving customs, including carrying children astride hips, red hand imprints, elephant worship, and accounts of Maya, Hema, Rama, and Maya’s death.

Hindostan is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
