Mrs. Tangey in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Mrs. Tangey, a commissionnaire’s wife with a bad reputation who drinks alcohol and has an eldest daughter, is suspected of taking a treaty, arrested and searched, but no papers are found.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Mrs. Tangey, the commissionnaire’s wife, hurried away from the Foreign Office and was suspected of taking the treaty. She was found at home, arrested, taken to Scotland Yard, and searched, but no papers were found; she gave explanations for her haste and conduct.
