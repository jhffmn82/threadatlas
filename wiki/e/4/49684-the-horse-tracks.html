the horse tracks in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Horse tracks matched to Silver Blaze’s horseshoe are found away from the original crime scene, leading toward Mapleton, then alongside a man’s track and back to the gates of Mapleton stables.

The horse tracks are found in soft ground away from the original crime scene and are matched by Silver Blaze’s horseshoe. They first show the horse moving toward Mapleton, then reveal a man accompanying it and a return route ending at the gates of Mapleton stables.
