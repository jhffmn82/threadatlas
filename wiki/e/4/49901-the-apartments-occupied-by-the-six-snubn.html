THE TRIBULATION OF TROT

The apartments are exceptionally magnificent, with a broad reception room, six bedchambers, marble baths, boudoirs, dressing rooms, and lavish blue-and-gold furnishings. Trot is kept there by the Princesses, befriends their pets there, and later occupies Princess Indigo's room.
