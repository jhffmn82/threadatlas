Abstract

Multilingual retrievers are described as high-quality off-the-shelf components available for multilingual retrieval-augmented generation. Their availability does not eliminate the need for task-specific prompt engineering.

Related Work

Multilingual retrievers are identified as individual RAG pipeline components that are rather well developed for multilingual settings.

Conclusion

Multilingual retrievers are predominantly trained on Wikipedia-based data. This training focus could limit their applicability to domains beyond Wikipedia.
