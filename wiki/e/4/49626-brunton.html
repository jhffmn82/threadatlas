Brunton

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Brunton the butler, Richard Brunton, butler, butler Brunton, faithless lover, missing butler, the butler

Abstract written by a model

Brunton is Hurlstone’s long-serving butler to Reginald Musgrave, serving for twenty years and noted for first-rate intelligence, languages, and musical ability. Engaged to Rachel Howells before taking up with Janet Tregellis, he secretly studies the Musgrave Ritual in the library, apparently having seen the catechism and comparing it with a map or chart. After Musgrave catches and dismisses him, Brunton disappears, leaving his clothes, watch, money, and boots. He asks about the former elm’s height, locates a concealed chamber, and apparently enters it with Howells. He is found dead there beside an opened box, without wound or bruise.

Brunton is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VI. The Musgrave Ritual

Brunton is the long-serving butler at Hurlstone, known for his intelligence, languages, musical ability, and womanizing. Reginald Musgrave catches him secretly studying a copy of the Musgrave Ritual and dismisses him, after which Brunton disappears from the house, leaving his clothes, watch, and money behind.

Holmes believes Brunton had previously seen the Musgrave catechism and consulted it on the night Musgrave caught him. Brunton apparently compared it with some map or chart, which he put into his pocket when Musgrave appeared.
