THE SCARECROW AND THE TIN WOODMAN

The Scarecrow's boat is made from a hollowed, pointed corncob, decorated with jewels and fitted with a purple silk sail. It carries the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman up the river until it bumps into a rock. After the crows lift the friends away, it floats in midstream until the Wizard of Oz magically draws it to shore.
