Victor Trevor in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Victor Trevor is Sherlock Holmes’s only college friend and the only son of Mr. Trevor. A college student, he lives with his father at Donnithorpe and owns a bull terrier. He invites Holmes to Donnithorpe, then later summons him when his father is dying. Hudson’s hold over the household leaves Victor afraid of disgrace and danger. After finding his father’s statement in the Japanese cabinet, Victor, too distressed to read it, asks Holmes to do so. The statement reveals that Victor’s father had used the name James Armitage and had been convicted.
