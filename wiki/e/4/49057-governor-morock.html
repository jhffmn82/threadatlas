Governor Morock

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Gov. Morock, Governor, Governor Mo-rock, the Governor

Abstract written by a model

Governor Morock is a lawyer associated with the Simon will, operating from the law office at No. 77 Strait street and described as fashionable in Chicago. He exploits Roxie Daymon’s destitution by offering, for fifty dollars, to arrange a legal loophole or “shadow” marriage. He writes young Simon’s will for ten dollars, then takes one thousand dollars in advance from Cousin Cæsar to challenge it, seeking further fees. During the war, he promises money and testimony may break the will and tries to obtain cotton through a federal officer, but gains nothing despite spending nearly all of Cæsar’s money. He ultimately retires, or is retired, from law and survives by cheaply selling his bad reputation for dirty work.

Governor Morock is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Gov. Morock tells Roxie that she is destitute and offers, for fifty dollars, to arrange a legal loophole or “shadow” to marry her off. Later, he writes young Simon’s will for ten dollars and agrees to challenge it for cousin Cæsar, taking one thousand dollars in advance and planning to charge additional sums.

SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

Governor Morock tells Roxie Daymon that money can help break the Simon will and offers to arrange testimony. He proposes to obtain cotton from the Simon plantations through a federal officer, but his agents secure no crumbs despite his use of nearly all of the thousand dollars obtained from Cousin Cæsar.
