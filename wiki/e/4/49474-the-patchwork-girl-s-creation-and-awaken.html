the Patchwork Girl's creation and awakening in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Patchwork Girl is a servant intended for Dame Margolotte. Assembled from scraps and fitted with brains, she lies lifeless on a bench after being brought into Dr. Pipt's workshop. Dr. Pipt prepares music and sprinkles Powder of Life over her; the records do not state the result.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

Margolotte plans to bring the Patchwork Girl to life by having Dr. Pipt sprinkle the Powder of Life over her. This has not yet happened in the unit; the Patchwork Girl is only brought into the workshop and shown to Ojo and Unc Nunkie.

Chapter Four

The Patchwork Girl was assembled from scraps, fitted with brains, and left lying lifeless on a bench. Dr. Pipt prepares her awakening with music and the Powder of Life, then sprinkles the powder over her, but the text ends before describing whether she becomes alive.
