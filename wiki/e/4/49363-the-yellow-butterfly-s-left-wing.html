the yellow butterfly's left wing in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The yellow butterfly's left wing is an ingredient Ojo seeks for a magic charm in the Country of the Winkies, but the Tin Woodman forbids taking it because doing so would torture and kill the butterfly, preventing Ojo from saving Unc Nunkie.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The yellow butterfly's left wing is the object Ojo and his companions intend to obtain in the Country of the Winkies.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

The left wing of a yellow butterfly is the fifth ingredient Ojo needs for the magic charm. The Tin Woodman refuses to let Ojo obtain it because removing it would torture and kill the butterfly, leaving Ojo convinced that he cannot save Unc Nunkie.
