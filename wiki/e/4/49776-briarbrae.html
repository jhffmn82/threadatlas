Abstract written by a model

Briarbrae is a large detached house in Woking, near the station, where Percy Phelps recuperates after brain-fever while Annie Harrison cares for him and Holmes and Watson hear his account. It contains stolen State papers hidden beneath a removable floorboard in Phelps’s bedroom; Holmes watches from the grounds and intercepts Joseph Harrison outside the window.
