Abstract written by a model

Steps are workflow components, represented as a JSON object and sometimes requiring a database table. They are indexed and retrieved from queries to existing names to reduce hallucination. Evaluation uses Recall@15, Bag of Steps, and hallucinated-step metrics; retrieval reduces hallucinations but can miss complex steps and produce structural errors with logic steps.

3 .. 3.2

Steps are indexed and retrieved as possible components of a workflow. The retriever is trained to map queries to existing step names because step names are a major source of potential hallucination.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

Retrieved and generated steps are evaluated with Recall@15, Bag of Steps, and hallucinated-step metrics. The system hallucinates fewer steps with a retriever, but complex or infrequently used steps may be missed, and logic steps such as IF, TRY, and FOREACH can cause structural errors.
