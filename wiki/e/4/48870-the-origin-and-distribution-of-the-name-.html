the origin and distribution of the name Maya in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

The origin and distribution of Maya centers on the claim that the Mayas probably took their name from Mayab, their country, and that Maya, with the same meaning wherever found, extends from Central America across ancient civilized regions of the Old World.

The narrator says that the Mayas probably took their name from Mayab, the name of their country. He claims that the name Maya, with the same meaning wherever it occurs, is distributed across the ancient civilized world as well as Central America.
