the origin and distribution of the name Maya

Abstract written by a model

The origin and distribution of Maya centers on the claim that the Mayas probably took their name from Mayab, their country, and that Maya, with the same meaning wherever found, extends from Central America across ancient civilized regions of the Old World.

the origin and distribution of the name Maya is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
