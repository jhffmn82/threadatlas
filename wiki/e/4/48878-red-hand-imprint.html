red hand imprint in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

The red hand imprint is a religious and property symbol found on monuments, sacred buildings, rocks, caves, and among peoples in Yucatan, North America, Hindostan, the South Sea islands, and Australia. It is interpreted as marking vows, prayers, blessings, possession, or a building’s opening for use.

1 section; each heading opens the text

When, on January 28, 1873, I had the honor of reading a paper before the New Yor

The red hand imprint is described as a symbol found on sacred buildings and rocks in Yucatan, among some North American aborigines, in Hindostan, and in the South Sea islands and Australia. The text interprets it as a mark associated with vows, prayers, blessings, possession, or the opening of a building for use.
