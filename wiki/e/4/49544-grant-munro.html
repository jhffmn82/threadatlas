Grant Munro in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

III. The Yellow Face

Grant Munro is a married hop merchant whose previously happy life has been disrupted by a secret involving his wife, Effie. He reports that Effie obtained one hundred pounds without explaining why, visited the nearby cottage at night, lied about where she had been, and was later seen leaving the cottage. When he threatens to enter, Effie pulls him back with convulsive strength and begs him not to do so.

Jack is persuaded by his wife's desperation not to enter the cottage. He agrees to trust her only if she promises that the mystery will end and that there will be no more secret nightly visits or concealed actions. Although he leaves with her, he knows he will not be at ease until he solves the mystery.

Grant Munro becomes convinced that his wife is concealing a serious secret in the cottage at Norbury. He forces his way inside with Holmes and Watson, learns that the supposed strange occupant is his wife’s daughter, Lucy, and responds by kissing and carrying the child home while offering Effie his hand.
