The Myrtles is a large, dark house in Beckenham standing back from the road in its own grounds. It is the furnished house where Paul Kratides is imprisoned and where Mr. Melas is taken for the second interview. The investigators find it apparently deserted, then discover Paul and Melas in an upstairs room filled with charcoal fumes.
