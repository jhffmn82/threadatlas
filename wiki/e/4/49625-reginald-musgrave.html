Abstract written by a model

Reginald Musgrave is a bachelor, a scion and member of the Musgrave family, owner of the old Hurlstone estate, and a former college acquaintance and client of Sherlock Holmes. He employs the household staff, including butler Brunton. When Brunton and Rachel Howells disappear, Musgrave asks Holmes to investigate. He provides the paper containing the Ritual, though he first doubts its value, then takes Holmes to Hurlstone and explains its landmarks. He helps open the cellar, recognizes Brunton’s belongings and body, identifies coins of Charles I, and learns that the hidden relic is an ancient crown.

VI. The Musgrave Ritual

Reginald Musgrave is a former college acquaintance of Sherlock Holmes and a member of an old family established at Hurlstone in western Sussex. He asks Holmes to investigate strange events at Hurlstone after Brunton and Rachel Howells disappear.

“He handed me the very paper which I have here, Watson, and this

Musgrave gives Watson the paper containing the catechism and reads its questions and answers. He says the original has no date but uses the spelling of the middle of the seventeenth century, and he initially doubts that it can help solve the mystery.

“The same afternoon saw us both at Hurlstone. Possibly you have

Reginald Musgrave brings Holmes to Hurlstone and supplies information about the oak, the former elm, and the Ritual. He helps open the cellar, recognizes Brunton’s muffler and body, identifies the coins as belonging to Charles I, and learns from Holmes that the hidden relic is the ancient crown.
