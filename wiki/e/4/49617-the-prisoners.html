Abstract written by a model

The prisoners are thirty-eight convicts transported aboard the Gloria Scott. Nearly all join a weapons-backed conspiracy, except one considered mentally weak and one with jaundice. They overpower guards and soldiers and seize the ship; some join Prendergast's killings, while five, including Evans, flee. Others confront the first mate by powder, causing destruction.

The convicts overpower the guards and soldiers, seize weapons, and take control of the Gloria Scott. Some convicts join Prendergast in killing the remaining officers, while five convicts, including Evans, reject the executions and escape. A group of convicts later confronts the first mate beside an open powder barrel, triggering the ship's destruction.
