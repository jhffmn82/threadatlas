the prisoners in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The prisoners are thirty-eight convicts transported aboard the Gloria Scott. Nearly all join a weapons-backed conspiracy, except one considered mentally weak and one with jaundice. They overpower guards and soldiers and seize the ship; some join Prendergast's killings, while five, including Evans, flee. Others confront the first mate by powder, causing destruction.

Some convicts join Prendergast in killing the remaining officers, while five convicts, including Evans, reject the executions and escape.
