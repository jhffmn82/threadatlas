5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

GRG with decomposition reads answers from retrieved documents and contextual documents generated by language models, while applying decomposition to sub-questions. It obtains HotpotQA, MuSiQue, and 2Wikimultihopqa F1 scores of 50.21, 24.87, and 40.42, respectively.

7 / 7.1 .. 7.3

GRG w/ decomposition is used as a baseline for comparing token consumption on HotpotQA. The framework spends fewer tokens than it does.
