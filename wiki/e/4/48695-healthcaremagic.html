HealthCareMagic in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

HealthCareMagic is a dataset of 200k doctor-patient medical dialogues, including HealthcareMagic-101 for medical dialog monitoring retrieval. It supports utility and privacy experiments: BLEU-1 and ROUGE-L compare outputs with ground truth, while 250 prompts assess targeted disease-information and untargeted attacks. Across evaluations, refined synthetic data and proposed methods, especially ZeroGen, AttrPrompt, and Stage-2, show zero or near-zero attack-related counts and nearly no attack success.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Experiment

HealthcareMagic-101 is used as the retrieval dataset for a medical dialog monitoring scenario. It contains 200k doctor-patient medical dialogues.

Utility of using synthetic data

The HealthCareMagic dataset is used for targeted and untargeted attack evaluations with 250 prompts. The proposed methods, especially ZeroGen, AttrPrompt, and Stage-2, show zero or near-zero attack-related counts in the reported tables.

Privacy of using synthetic data

HealthCareMagic is evaluated under targeted and untargeted attacks. The targeted evaluation seeks diagnosed cases for specific diseases, while the untargeted results show that refined synthetic data has nearly no attack success.

Ablation Studies

HealthCareMagic is the dataset used for the utility and targeted attack experiments. BLEU-1 and ROUGE-L are compared with ground truth for utility evaluation.
