Brunton's death in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Brunton’s death occurs in a concealed Hurlstone cellar chamber when confinement beneath a flagstone leaves him trapped. After several days, his body is found crouched beside an opened box, uninjured; Holmes concludes the flagstone closed after Howells assisted or abandoned him.

Brunton’s body is found crouched beside the opened box in the concealed chamber after he has been dead for several days. No wound or bruise is found, and Holmes concludes that the flagstone probably closed over him after Howells assisted or abandoned him.
