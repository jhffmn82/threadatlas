Abstract written by a model

The supposed murder of Colonel Barclay is a local Aldershot tragedy on the Monday night before Sherlock Holmes's visit. After a violent quarrel with Mrs. Barclay in the locked morning-room, Barclay suffers a fatal head injury, possibly from a carved wooden club or from falling in fright onto the fender. At Major Murphy’s request, Holmes investigates.
