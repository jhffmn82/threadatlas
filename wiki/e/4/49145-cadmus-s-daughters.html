Cadmus's daughters

group · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Cadmus' daughters, Cadmus' royal brood, The daughters, daughters, sisters twain, the daughters, thy sisters twain

Abstract written by a model

In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, Cadmus' daughters are driven into a Dionysian frenzy on Kithaeron, seize Pentheus, and participate in his dismemberment. In The Bacchae of Euripides, Cadmus's daughters participate in the Bacchic hunt on Kithaeron and together kill Pentheus while driven mad by Dionysus.
