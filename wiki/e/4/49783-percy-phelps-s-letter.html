XI. The Naval Treaty

Percy Phelps's letter asks Dr. John Watson to bring Sherlock Holmes to Briarbrae and explains that Phelps has recently recovered from brain-fever. Holmes studies the handwriting and determines that it was written by a woman of exceptional character, rather than by Phelps himself.
