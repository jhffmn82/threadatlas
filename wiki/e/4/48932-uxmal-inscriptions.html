Uxmal inscriptions

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: inscriptions of Uxmal

Abstract written by a model

Uxmal inscriptions are Maya-language inscriptions arranged in squares or parallelograms, combining phonetic, symbolic, and ideographic signs. They include characters resembling Chaldee, Egyptian, and Etruscan signs and were used to identify Uxmal’s founders.

Uxmal inscriptions is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
