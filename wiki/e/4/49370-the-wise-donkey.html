Abstract written by a model

The Wise Donkey is a wise public adviser, born in the Land of Mo and obliged to remain in the Land of Oz after it was shut off. Dressed in a blue apron and cap, he partners with the Foolish Owl. He welcomes travelers, offers advice, and directs them along the yellow brick road toward the Emerald City.

Chapter Eight

The Wise Donkey welcomes the travelers and offers advice. He says the yellow brick road leads directly to the Emerald City and advises the travelers to keep following their path and move on. He explains that he was born in the Land of Mo and has remained in the Land of Oz since it was shut off from the rest of the world.
