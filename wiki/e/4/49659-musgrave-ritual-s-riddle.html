contains_clue crown of the Kings of England [through the first two questions] The Ritual’s riddle includes the questions “Whose was it?” and “Who shall have it?” with the answers “His who is gone” and “He who will come.” Holmes interprets these as references to Charles I after his execution and Charles II before his return.
