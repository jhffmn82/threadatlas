Abstract written by a model

The Pinkies are the several hundred short, round, chubby, wholly pink people of Pink Country, with pink hair, eyes, cheeks, complexions, clothing, and a pink color scheme. Wealthy, polite, strong, and devoted to their laws, they regard wealth and honor as their due. Their society includes the Sunrise and Sunset Tribes, treats women as important as men in work and war, and uses long sharp-pointed sticks as weapons. They are ruled first by Tourmaline, whom they acknowledge as Queen, and regard the Blueskins as natural enemies. At first suspicious of outsiders, Pinkies capture and escort the travelers to Tourmaline, fearing enemies, magicians, or sorcerers. Although they do not hate the strangers, they have never admitted outsiders and support an order to execute them. Rosalie's explanation and Polychrome's interpretation of the laws change their response: they kneel to Trot, recognize her as Queen, and spread news of her rule. Eager for relief from monotonous lives, they form a two-hundred-warrior army and invade Blue Country across the Fog Bank on giant frogs. They camp outside Blue City, repel an attack, and, after Rosalie enchants their sticks, destroy the Blueskins' longer lances. They suffer setbacks in attempts to save Cap'n Bill, capture Ghip-Ghisizzle, and prepare to assault the City. Instead, Trot orders peace and they enter Blue City as welcomed conquerors. They celebrate but refuse to dance with the Blues and quarrel with them. Anxious to return home, the Pinkies travel back through the Fog Bank. They welcome Trot's return, accept her new law granting the Queen equal conditions as just, accept Rosalie as Queen, and promise her a palace and statues. They give Trot the royal pink-gold band with its pink jewel. At the end, their Pink Band plays while they gather in the Court of the Statues to bid Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright farewell.

Sky Island

The Pinkies are named in the contents in connection with Trot regulating them.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Pinkies are short, round, chubby inhabitants with pink hair, eyes, cheeks, and complexions. Four Pinkies armed with sharp-pointed rosewood sticks capture and escort the travelers to Tourmaline because they suspect them of being enemies, magicians, or sorcerers.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Pinkies are the people of the Pink Country, shown as wealthy, polite, and devoted to their laws. They do not hate the strangers, but they have never before admitted anyone from outside their borders and do not want the travelers to remain in their country.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Pinkies inhabit the Pink Country, where women are as important as men and are expected to work and fight in wars. They are divided into the Sunrise Tribe and Sunset Tribe, use sharp sticks in conflicts, and are uneasy about the strangers’ unfamiliar appearances and Cap'n Bill’s strength.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The Pinkies accept Rosalie's summoning and listen to her vision and explanation of the strangers' protection. They prepare to escort the prisoners to the island's edge, wearing gossamer raincoats and carrying sharp sticks.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

The Pinkies escort the travelers through the muddy Pink Country, with some pulling Cap'n Bill from the mud. They initially support Tourmaline's order to execute the travelers but accept Polychrome's interpretation of the laws. They kneel before Trot, greet her as Queen, and spread the news of her rule.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Pinkies enthusiastically accept Trot as their new Queen, kneel before her, and kiss her hand. They are described as a great nation pledged to obey her commands and possessing sharp sticks, and they are expected to provide the army for the planned campaign.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Pinkies eagerly agree to undertake the war because their lives have been monotonous and uninteresting. They form an army of two hundred warriors, travel across the Fog Bank on giant frogs, and enter the Blue Country.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Pinkies break through the Fog Bank and march toward the City in a great army. They cause the Boolooroo to halt the patching operation and prepare the Blueskins for defense.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

The Pinkies encamp before the principal gate of the Blue City and successfully repel the Blueskins' first attack. Rosalie enchants their sharp sticks, enabling them to destroy the Blueskins' longer lances, but they fail to rescue Cap'n Bill after his capture and become despondent.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

The Pinkies are camped outside the City and are treated by Ghip-Ghisizzle as preferable to his own people and their feared Boolooroo. Their forces take Ghip-Ghisizzle prisoner after he reaches their camp.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Pinkies march toward the Blue City wall to assist Cap'n Bill's escape after Button-Bright alerts Coralie. They arrive too late and return to camp after the Blueskins recapture him.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Pinkies are enemies of the Blueskins and are preparing to attack the City. Trot declares herself their queen and orders the Blueskins to welcome their army instead of fighting it.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Pinkies arrive intending to rescue Trot and Cap'n Bill and conquer the Boolooroo, but they are instead welcomed by Trot and the Blueskins. They celebrate in the Blue City but refuse to dance with the Blues and quarrel with them during the ball.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The Pinkies helped Trot and her companions conquer the Blues and are anxious to return home. Trot arranges to escort them safely and leads them out of the Blue City toward the Fog Bank.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The Pinkies put on raincoats before entering the Fog Bank and follow the elephant through it. By the time they near the Pink Country, they are dripping wet and their legs are tired and aching.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

The Pinkies joyfully welcome Trot and her companions back to their land and accept the new law as just. They accept Rosalie as Queen, agree to build her a new palace and erect statues, and give Trot the royal band of pink gold with its pink jewel.

THE JOURNEY HOME

After the Pinkies are dismissed, they reunite in the Court of the Statues with their Pink Band to see Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright depart. They play music and throw kisses as the travelers disappear.
