Abstract written by a model

Jimfred Jonesjinks is a patched Blueskin and patched man, one half of a mismatched paired body with Fredjim Jinksjones. Joined from two people’s halves, he has mismatched features and a limp, guards the palace entrance, and is more intelligent than Fredjim. He says patching destroys individuality while Fredjim guards the Treasure Chamber.
