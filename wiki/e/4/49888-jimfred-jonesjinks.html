GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

Jimfred Jonesjinks is a patched Blueskin whose body is joined from halves of two people, giving him mismatched features and a limp. He explains that his partner, Fredjim Jinksjones, is guarding the Treasure Chamber and that patching destroys a person's individuality.
