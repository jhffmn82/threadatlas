Abstract written by a model

A living man of respectable parentage, educated in a log schoolhouse, he gained early experience navigating western rivers and observing character. Using fancy names and young surveying assistants, he recounts witnessed or traditional events rather than his own history, including a perilous Helena journey and observations of Alonzo Phelps and Daymon's trial.

The narrator presents the work as an account of events he witnessed or learned by tradition rather than his own history. He describes his early life, travels on the Mississippi River and its tributaries, a dangerous journey to Helena, Arkansas, and his observations of Alonzo Phelps and Daymon's trial.
