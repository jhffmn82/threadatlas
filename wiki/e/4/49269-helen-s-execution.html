Helen's execution

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Helen’s execution, death, her life, her ruin, kill thee, slay her, slay thee

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, Helen's execution is planned by Menelaus after her transport to Argos. In The Trojan Women of Euripides, Helen's execution is intended by Menelaus as a future punishment in Argos, but he does not carry it out in the document.
