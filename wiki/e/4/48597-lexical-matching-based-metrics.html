lexical matching-based metrics in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024

Lexical matching metrics evaluate whether ground-truth tokens appear in generated answers; valued for transparency and interpretability, they begin with word-level containment but require adjustment for equivalent named-entity transliterations.

Lexical matching metrics are used to evaluate whether the ground truth or its tokens are contained in the generated answer. The unit notes that word-level matching can miss equivalent transliterations of named entities, motivating an adjustment to the metric.
