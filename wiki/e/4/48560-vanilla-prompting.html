Vanilla prompting

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Vanilla, vanilla prompting

Abstract written by a model

Vanilla prompting is a method that asks a large language model a yes/no question about whether retrieval is needed, then performs retrieval only after Yes. It is inadequate and insufficient for reliable retrieval decisions: retrieval accuracy varies across models and does not scale consistently with model size.

Vanilla prompting is in one work on this site, RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
