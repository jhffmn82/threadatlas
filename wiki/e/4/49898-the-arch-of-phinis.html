Abstract written by a model

The Arch of Phinis is a grim blue-marble archway at the entrance to the Great Blue Grotto outside the Blue City. Marked "Phinis," it is the required passage for Blueskins when they reach six hundred years, though the Boolooroo plans to send three strangers through early. Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and Trot consider it for refuge but do not enter.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

THE BLUE CITY

The Arch of Phinis is the entrance to the Great Blue Grotto, located just outside the gates of the Blue City. Blueskins are required by law to pass through it when their six hundred years end.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Arch of Phinis is the passage into the Great Blue Grotto. Ghip-Ghisizzle explains that only people who have lived six hundred years may officially march through it, but the Boolooroo plans to send the three strangers through it anyway.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Arch of Phinis is a grim blue-marble archway marked with the word "Phinis." Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and Trot consider using it as a refuge but decide not to enter.
