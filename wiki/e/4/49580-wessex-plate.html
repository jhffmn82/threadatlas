The Wessex Plate is a one-mile-and-five-furlong horse race with six entries: The Negro, Pugilist, Desborough, Silver Blaze, Iris, and Rasper. Silver Blaze, owned by Colonel Ross, is the favourite, while Desborough is second in the betting.
