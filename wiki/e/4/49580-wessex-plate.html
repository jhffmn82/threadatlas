Abstract written by a model

The Wessex Plate is a one-mile-and-five-furlong horse race with six entries: The Negro, Pugilist, Desborough, Silver Blaze, Iris, and Rasper. Silver Blaze, owned by Colonel Ross, is the favourite, while Desborough is second in the betting. Colonel Ross considers withdrawing Silver Blaze, but Sherlock Holmes urges him to keep the horse entered and hopes it will start on Tuesday. Four days later, Silver Blaze wins by six lengths; Desborough finishes second and Iris third.
