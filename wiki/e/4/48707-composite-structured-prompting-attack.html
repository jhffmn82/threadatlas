Also called: composite structured prompting, composite structured prompting attack method, prompt structure, structured prompting attack

Abstract written by a model

In The Good and The Bad: Exploring Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), composite structured prompting attack is described as a prompting method for extracting retrieval data from RAG systems that combines information steering context retrieval toward particular data with a command instructing the language model to reproduce or include retrieved contexts in its response. In Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data, composite structured prompting attack is described as combining information guiding retrieval with commands instructing the language model to include retrieved information in responses.
