Shaggy Man's rescue in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Shaggy Man rescues Ojo, then frees Scraps and the Glass Cat by cutting down their enclosing leaves, and frees the Woozy by slicing off the leaf’s stem.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

The Shaggy Man rescues Ojo by cutting down the leaf that holds him. He then cuts down the leaves holding Scraps, the Glass Cat, and the Woozy, freeing all of Ojo's companions.
