Abstract written by a model

The murder of John Straker, Silver Blaze’s trainer, occurs at the Dartmoor training-stables on Monday night. After Straker fails to return from checking the horses, he is found in a hollow near the stables with a shattered head, a cut thigh, a bloodied knife, and a red-and-black cravat. Holmes initially leaves the killing unresolved after finding Silver Blaze, then concludes that Straker was accidentally killed in self-defence when Silver Blaze kicked him as he tried to lame the horse for race fraud.
