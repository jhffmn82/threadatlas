James Browner

person · 1 work · 20 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

James Browner is a sailor and steward, serving on a South American line, a Liverpool boat, and the S.S. May Day. He is Mary Browner’s husband and Sarah Cushing’s brother-in-law. A big, powerful, clean-shaven, very swarthy man of strong passions, he leaves a superior berth to be nearer Mary. After breaking his blue ribbon, he returns to drinking; alcohol makes him violently deranged, and he has occasional hard-drinking fits and quarrels with Susan and Sarah. He departs on the May Day, carrying a heavy oak stick and a big sharp knife. Holmes suspects him of the murders. Found aboard the May Day, withdrawn and remorseful, Browner is taken into custody. He confesses to killing Mary Browner and Alec Fairbairn, sinking their bodies in a boat, and returning to his ship; afterward he suffers insomnia and remorse.
