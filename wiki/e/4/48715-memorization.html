memorization

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: LLMs’ tendency to reproduce memorized information, memorizing, model memorization

Abstract written by a model

Memorization in large language models is the model's reproduction of information from training data. It increases with model size, data duplication, and prompt length, varies by fine-tuning task, and can rise more when fine-tuning heads than smaller adapter modules. Retrieval integration substantially decreases observed reproduction of memorized personal identifying information and text.
