Beddoes

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Mr. Beddoes, him

Abstract written by a model

Beddoes is a wealthy, respected Hampshire man and former colonial convict. After joining a mutiny, he reaches Sydney with Evans, changes his name, and lives peacefully for more than twenty years. He later invites Victor Trevor’s father to shoot over his preserves. When Hudson threatens to expose their past, Beddoes receives a cipher warning, is identified as its likely sender, and disappears, probably after killing Hudson and fleeing with money.

Beddoes is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
