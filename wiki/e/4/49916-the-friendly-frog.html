the friendly frog in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The friendly frog is a common, very small frog living in the Fog Bank who hopes to grow larger. It meets Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright, dislikes the Blueskins, and considers the Pinkies decent neighbors. Swiftly guiding the travelers with grunts, croaks, and enormous leaps, it helps them escape by carrying each on its back in a final leap to the Fog Bank border before attending the King's Court.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THROUGH THE FOG BANK

The common frog encounters the travelers in the Fog Bank and says that it dislikes the Blueskins and that the Pinkies are decent neighbors. It offers to lead them to the border, making enormous leaps and guiding them with grunts and croaks. Because it must attend the King's Court, it carries Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright on its back in one final leap, which takes them out of the Fog Bank.
