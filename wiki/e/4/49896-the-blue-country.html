the Blue Country in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Blue Country is the blue half of Sky Island, beyond the Fog Bank. It has no sun, but moonlight every night; its fields are blue, and it contains Blue City, a mountain, the Great Blue Grotto, the Arch of Phinis, and plentiful blue goats. It is inhabited by nervous, restless Blueskins and is less delightful and less comfortable than the Pink Country. For three hundred years it was ruled by Boolooroo, whose cruel treatment of Earth people helped bring the Pinkies through the Fog Bank to threaten and conquer the country. Earlier, travelers were enslaved after Boolooroo stole their Magic Umbrella, but escaped through the Fog Bank; they also could not remain because the people hated them. After Trot and Cap'n Bill overpower Boolooroo, Trot takes the Blue Country into her kingdom, appoints Cap'n Bill Royal Manager, removes the former ruling family, and appoints Ghip-Ghisizzle Boolooroo. She plans peace with the Pinkies and a celebration in Blue City.

Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BLUE CITY

The Blue Country is the half of Sky Island where the moon shines every night but the sun is never seen. It contains the Blue City, a mountain, the Great Blue Grotto, and the Arch of Phinis.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Blue Country is where the travelers were made slaves by the Boolooroo after he stole their Magic Umbrella. They escape from it by passing through the Fog Bank.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Blue Country is the land of the Blueskins, where the travelers cannot remain because everyone there hates them. They leave it after the Boolooroo steals the Magic Umbrella.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Blue Country is the less comfortable part of Sky Island, where the great Fog hides the sun and only the moon is visible. Cap'n Bill says he would rather remain with the Pinkies than return there.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Blue Country is where the Boolooroo and the Blueskins live. The planned route to it leads through the Fog Bank.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Blue Country lies beyond the Fog Bank and has blue fields where the Pinkies land after their ride. Once there, the Pinkies remove their raincoats and prepare for battle.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Blue Country is threatened by the Pinkies after they break through the Fog Bank. Its Blueskins become fearful and blame the Boolooroo's cruel treatment of the Earth people for bringing about the invasion.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Royal Record Book shows that the Boolooroo had already ruled the Blue Country for three hundred years by the previous Thursday. Ghip-Ghisizzle claims the right to rule the Blue Country and plans to replace him.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The Blue Country is ruled by the Boolooroo, who says that everyone in the country hates him. After Trot and Cap'n Bill overpower him, Trot says that she and Cap'n Bill may be able to run the Blue Country themselves.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Blue Country comes under Trot's intended rule, with Cap'n Bill appointed its Royal Manager. Trot plans to end its conflict with the Pinkies and hold a celebration in the Blue City.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Blue Country becomes part of Trot's kingdom after the conquest. Ghip-Ghisizzle is appointed its Boolooroo, while the former ruling family is removed from power.
