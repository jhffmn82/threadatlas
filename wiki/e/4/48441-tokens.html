In DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models, a token is evaluated using its vocabulary-distribution entropy, its maximum attention from subsequent tokens, and a semantic indicator that excludes stopwords; a token whose combined score exceeds the predefined threshold causes retrieval to activate.
