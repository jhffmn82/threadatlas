Abstract written by a model

Helena, Arkansas, is a point on the west bank of the river, three hundred miles above Vicksburg. Near its former town seat was race ground where Billy Horner and Henry Mooney raced ponies. Fortified by federals with three forts, Helena was attacked by General Holmes’s forces on July 4, 1863.

Helena, Arkansas is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

Near Helena, Arkansas, old Billy Horner and Henry Mooney held a quarter-mile race between two ponies. The narrator traveled there from Bush Bayou to obtain the Governor of Arkansas's signature on a land grant and title.
