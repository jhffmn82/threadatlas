SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

The battle of Wilson’s Creek, also called the battle of Oak Hill or Springfield, takes place on August 10, 1861. General Lyon is killed while leading a charge, the Federals retreat, and Cousin Cæsar receives a painful gunshot wound in the right leg.
