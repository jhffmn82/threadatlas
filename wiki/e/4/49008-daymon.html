Daymon, a young laborer and boat hand, conducted boats across Louisville’s falls, abused his wife while intoxicated, and was arrested and tried for allegedly drowning her in Beargrass Creek, pleading not guilty.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

Daymon was a young laborer or boat hand who helped conduct boats across the falls at Louisville, Kentucky. He was dissipated and frequently abused his wife while intoxicated, and he was arrested and tried for allegedly drowning her in Beargrass Creek; despite circumstantial evidence, he pleaded not guilty.
