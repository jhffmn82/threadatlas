Abstract written by a model

The yellow face appears at an upper window of the newly occupied cottage near Grant Munro’s house. Grant sees it watching, but cannot tell whether it is a man or woman; it looks livid, chalky white, rigid, and unnatural, and vanishes whenever he approaches. It is later explained as Lucy’s face under a mask, worn to conceal that she was a black child.

III. The Yellow Face

The yellow face is seen by Grant Munro watching from an upper window of the newly occupied cottage. He cannot determine whether it belongs to a man or a woman, but describes it as livid, chalky white, rigid, and shockingly unnatural.

“For two days after this I stayed at home, and my wife appeared

The face at the window appears unnaturally coloured and rigid, vanishing with a jerk whenever Grant approaches. It is later explained as Lucy’s masked face, deliberately covered so that neighbours would not see that she was a black child.
