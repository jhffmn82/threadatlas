Brindle Bill

Also called: Bill, Brindle Devil

Abstract written by a model

Brindle Bill is a five-foot-eight, square-shouldered man, strong as a buffalo, a little-working wood-chopper, former three-card-monte gambler, and bully of the settlement. A floating-population member raised in Port William, he leads the party favoring degradation and is Dan Carlo’s fast friend, yet joins a scheme against him. After fighting Don Carlo and leaving the new settlement, he conspires over the Lasco inheritance, pressures S. S. Simon, and is imprisoned for Lasco inheritance fraud.

Brindle Bill is in one work on this site, Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SCENE SECOND.

Brindle Bill is a strong wood-chopper and the bully of the settlement who leads one of its social parties. He joins the scheme against Dan Carlo and claims the money when Don Carlo interrupts the game. He fights Don Carlo but is defeated when the fire at headquarters ends the contest, after which he leaves the new settlement.

SCENE FOURTH--ROXIE DAYMON AND ROSE SIMON.

Brindle Bill recognizes Susan Lasco's name in John A. Lasco's dying statement and connects it to Rose Simon's mother. He conspires with S. S. Simon and Sundown Hill to obtain the Lasco inheritance by falsely claiming that Rose's sisters are dead, and later pressures Simon for additional money.
