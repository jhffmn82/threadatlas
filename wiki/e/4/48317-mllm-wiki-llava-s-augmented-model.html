Abstract written by a model

MLLM is augmented by Wiki-LLaVA with retrieved Wikipedia passages for visual questions. It improves question and dialogue effectiveness and can raise accuracy when retrieval finds relevant entities and documents, while maintaining proficiency across tasks.
