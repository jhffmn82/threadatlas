Abstract written by a model

Mr. Trevor, Victor Trevor’s only son’s widowed father, is a wealthy landed proprietor and Justice of the Peace, valued for countryside kindness and charity. Formerly a traveler and gold-digger, he tries to erase J. A. initials and fears Hudson’s power over his past. A coded message brings a fatal stroke.
