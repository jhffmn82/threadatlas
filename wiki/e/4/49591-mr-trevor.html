V. The “_Gloria Scott_”

Mr. Trevor was Victor Trevor's father, a wealthy landowner, Justice of the Peace, and former traveler and gold-digger. He had a half-effaced tattoo bearing the initials J. A., feared a past connection, and fell under Hudson's power before suffering a fatal stroke after receiving a coded message.
