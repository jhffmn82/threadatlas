Abstract written by a model

The marble dividing fence separates Hopper Country from Horner Country and the Hoppers from the Horners before peace. Very high, with a locked central gate marked “WAR IS DECLARED,” it later holds the Scarecrow atop a picket until Diksey, Ojo, and Dorothy rescue him.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The marble dividing fence is very high and separates the Hopper and Horner parts of the cave. Its central gate is locked on both sides and bears a sign reading “WAR IS DECLARED.”

Chapter Twenty-Three

The marble picket fence separates the Horners and the Hoppers during their dispute. The Scarecrow is stuck on top of one of its pickets until Diksey brings a ladder and Ojo and Dorothy rescue him.
