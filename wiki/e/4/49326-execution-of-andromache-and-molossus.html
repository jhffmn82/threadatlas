Abstract written by a model

The execution of Andromache and Molossus is a death sentence imposed by Menelaus and Hermione: Menelaus condemns Andromache and Hermione condemns Molossus. Menelaus orders them sent together to the underworld while Neoptolemus is absent, but Peleus frees and shelters them, preventing the killings; Hermione later confesses and grieves.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

MENELAUS, MOLOSSUS, ANDROMACHE, CHORUS.

Menelaus orders Andromache and Molossus to go together to the underworld. He says that Andromache’s life is his sentence and that Hermione’s sentence takes Molossus’s life.

PELEUS, MENELAUS, ANDROMACHE, MOLOSSUS, CHORUS.

Menelaus's followers are taking Andromache and Molossus to be killed, and Andromache says that Menelaus intends to slay them while Neoptolemus is absent. Peleus prevents the immediate killing by freeing them and sheltering them.

THE NURSE OF HERMIONE, CHORUS.

Hermione and Menelaus attempted to kill Andromache and her son. Peleus prevented the killing, and Hermione later confesses and grieves over the attempt.
