headquarters in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Headquarters is an old abandoned cabin at the edge of a small cane-brake, distant from the river. Used by Brindle Bill and others for gambling, drinking, cards, and a scheme against Dan, it holds the jug, admits Don Carlo through the chimney, and is later burned by Simon’s wife.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE SECOND.

Headquarters is an abandoned cabin in the edge of a cane-brake where the jug is kept and men gather for drinking and cards. Brindle Bill, S. S. Simon, Sundown Hill, and Dan Carlo use it for their scheme against Dan. Don Carlo enters through the chimney, and the cabin is later set on fire by Simon's wife.
