VI. The Musgrave Ritual

The Musgrave Ritual case is one of Sherlock Holmes’s early investigations and is described as a singular chain of events with unusually large issues at stake. It concerns the ritual document, Brunton’s disappearance, Rachel Howells’s disappearance, and the objects recovered from the mere.
