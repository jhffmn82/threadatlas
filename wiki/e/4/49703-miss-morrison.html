Miss Morrison accompanies Mrs. Barclay to the Guild meeting and witnesses her encounter with Henry Wood in Hudson Street. She initially withholds the information but later tells Holmes that Wood called Mrs. Barclay Nancy and that Mrs. Barclay identified him as Henry, whom she had believed dead for thirty years.
