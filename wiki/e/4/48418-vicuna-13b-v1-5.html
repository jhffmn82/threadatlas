Vicuna-13B-v1.5 is selected as an LLM for evaluating DRAGIN and other RAG baselines. It is described as an open-source chatbot fine-tuned from LLaMA using user-shared conversations gathered from ShareGPT.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

Vicuna-13b-v1.5 is evaluated with the same baseline and DRAGIN configurations. DRAGIN achieves the highest listed results across all four benchmarks and substantially outperforms the other dynamic RAG methods in the IIRC timing comparison.
