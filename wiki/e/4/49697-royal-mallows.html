Royal Mallows is a famous Irish regiment in the British army, formerly the 117th and called the old 117th. Its first battalion served with distinction in the Crimea and Mutiny, was stationed at Aldershot, and was commanded by Colonel James Barclay until Monday night’s reported tragedy.
