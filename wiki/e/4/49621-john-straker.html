John Straker in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

John Straker, also called Derbyshire, was Colonel Ross’s servant, former jockey, and trainer of Ross’s horses at the Dartmoor stables. He had served Ross for twelve years, five as jockey and seven as trainer, and was married to Mrs. Straker. Secretly burdened by debts for his mistress’s expensive dresses, he drugged the stable-boy and led Silver Blaze onto the moor at night to lame the horse with a surgical cataract knife. Silver Blaze was frightened by Straker’s light and, acting in self-defence, kicked him in the forehead; Straker also cut his own thigh. He was found dead in a hollow near King’s Pyland, holding the bloodied knife and Fitzroy Simpson’s cravat.

I. Silver Blaze

John Straker is Colonel Ross’s trainer and a former jockey who had served Ross for twelve years. He leaves home during the night to check on the horses and is later found dead in a hollow on the moor, with his head shattered and a cut on his thigh; his hands hold a small bloodied knife and Fitzroy Simpson’s cravat.
