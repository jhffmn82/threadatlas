Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

The Blue Palace contains the Boolooroo's Royal Treasury and entrances to the areas where the palace servants live. Trot is dragged into it by the Six Snubnosed Princesses, while Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright are taken to its servants' quarters.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE PROVES FRIENDLY

The Royal Palace is a magnificent building with large, lofty rooms, superb blue furnishings, broad corridors, and many passages. Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill are brought there, instructed in their duties, and led through it to the streets of the Blue City.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The palace contains the servants' hall, the royal apartments, the Royal Treasure Chamber, and rooms given to the three prisoners. Ghip-Ghisizzle says the prisoners are safer there because the servants and soldiers fear injuring them without the Boolooroo's permission. Button-Bright moves through the palace at night to steal the Treasure Chamber key and reach the chamber.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The palace contains the Treasure Chamber and is filled with guards, soldiers, and servants after the Boolooroo's alarm. Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and Trot escape from it through a little door and flee into the city.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

The palace is guarded at night, but Trot enters through the doorway while the guards are distracted. It contains the Room of the Great Knife, Cap'n Bill's hidden prison location, and the chamber from which Ghip-Ghisizzle escapes.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The palace hosts the royal feast, Trot's account of her adventures, and a grand ball for important Pinkies and Blueskins. After the conquest, its treasure and authority pass to Ghip-Ghisizzle, while the former Boolooroo and his family are expelled.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The palace is searched carefully for the Magic Umbrella, but no trace is found there. Tiggle later identifies the umbrella as something he had swept from the King's rooms and discarded behind the palace.
