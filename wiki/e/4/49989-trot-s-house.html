Trot's house in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Trot's house, home of Trot and Cap'n Bill, is a white cottage near a giant acacia on a high bluff, with a shed. Trot breakfasts there before flying, returns from town by Magic Umbrella, and comes home at dusk to find her mother cooking.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill have breakfast at Trot's house before the first flight. The Magic Umbrella carries Trot and Button-Bright back there from town and later departs from the nearby bluff for the trip to Sky Island.

THE JOURNEY HOME

Trot's house is a white cottage near the giant acacia on the high bluff. Trot returns there at dusk and finds her mother busy in the kitchen.
