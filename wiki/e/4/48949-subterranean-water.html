subterranean water

Also called: currents of water, springs, subterranean stream, underground current

Abstract written by a model

Subterranean water supplies the caves, senotes, and rare surface aguadas of Yucatan. Since the peninsula has no surface rivers and almost no lakes, plants, animals, and inhabitants depend heavily on underground water and seasonal rain.
