Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where online misinformation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Online misinformation is a growing presence in social-media discussions of trending topics and poses significant threats to the public interest, with delayed intervention associated with outcomes such as vaccine hesitancy. It is detected through content- and evidence-based approaches and commonly countered through social correction; newer language-based methods generate counter-responses or explanations. The records present it as an insufficiently studied setting for retrieval augmented methods. RARG addresses it by retrieving supporting evidence and generating evidence-based responses that explicitly refute inaccurate claims and aim to reduce misinformation’s spread.

misinformation in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where misinformation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Misinformation is false information spread through unreliable or AI-generated sources and a risk for retrieval-augmented generation. It is modeled as distinct incorrect answers and documents assigned to sources by truthfulness, then used in benchmarks. It degrades several retrieval systems in adversarial settings, while source reliability estimation and robust retrieval approaches aim to combat it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Misinformation is presented as a problem that can be spread when retrieval systems use unreliable or AI-generated sources. Source reliability estimation is identified as an important step in combating it.

Related Works

Misinformation is a risk in retrieval-augmented generation because retrieved documents can be irrelevant or unreliable. The discussed approaches seek to improve robustness against misinformation after or during retrieval.

Experiments / Experimental setups .. Ablation studies and analysis

Misinformation is generated in the benchmark to simulate unreliable source content. It causes MV, Vanilla RAG, Robust RAG, and Self-RAG to degrade more severely than RA-RAG in adversarial settings.
