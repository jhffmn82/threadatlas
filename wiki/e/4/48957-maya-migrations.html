Maya migrations

Abstract written by a model

Maya migrations move from Mayab into Hindostan, Afghanistan, Ethiopia, the Persian Gulf, Chaldea, Asia Minor, and Egypt, reaching Afghanistan and the Euphrates and spreading Maya names, languages, customs, traditions, religious ideas, and artistic practices.
