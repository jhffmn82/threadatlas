being patched in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Being patched is a punishment and means of control in which the Boolooroo uses the Great Knife to split two people and join them into a strange combination. Jimfred and Fredjim found it less painful than expected but confusing. The Boolooroo plans to patch Cap'n Bill with Tiggle, though Tiggle is hiding.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

Being patched involves splitting people in two with the Great Knife and joining them into a strange combination. Jimfred and Fredjim say it did not hurt as much as expected, but left them confused until they became accustomed to it.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Boolooroo intends to patch Cap'n Bill after breakfast, pairing him with Tiggle. Trot says that Tiggle has been set free and is hiding, so the Boolooroo may need to find someone else.
