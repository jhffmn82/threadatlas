Maya architecture

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: buildings, manner of construction, sacred edifices

Abstract written by a model

Maya architecture uses stone in Yucatan to form mounds, cones, or pyramids of superposed platforms, often oriented toward cardinal points. Generally seven platforms rise by exterior staircases to the summit. Temples have thick walls, flat roofs, triangular-arched long narrow chambers, squared recesses, and an inner Sanctum Sanctorum.

Maya architecture is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa.
