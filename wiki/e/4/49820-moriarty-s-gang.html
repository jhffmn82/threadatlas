Moriarty's gang

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Moriarty gang, the gang, the whole gang, their organization

Abstract written by a model

In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Moriarty's gang is a criminal organization exposed by Holmes's evidence; London police capture all but Professor Moriarty, and the gang is heavily punished in subsequent proceedings. In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, the Moriarty gang, led by Professor Moriarty and including Colonel Sebastian Moran, is investigated after Moriarty's death; its dangerous members remain free, forcing Sherlock Holmes to conceal his survival until only one enemy remains in London.
