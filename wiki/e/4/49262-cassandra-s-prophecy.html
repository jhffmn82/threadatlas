Cassandra's prophecy

topic · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Bodings, Cassandra’s prophecy, God's word, omens, oracles Apollo gave, the wisdoms
  • In The Trojan Women of Euripides, Cassandra's prophecy declares that she will kill Agamemnon and destroy his house, foretells Odysseus's long, lonely suffering, and presents the Greeks' apparent victory as a source of future grief.
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Cassandra's prophecy foretells Agamemnon's destruction, the fall of the Atreus house, her own death, and Ulysses' prolonged and perilous journey home, and predicts that the Greeks will suffer more deeply than the Trojans despite their victory.
