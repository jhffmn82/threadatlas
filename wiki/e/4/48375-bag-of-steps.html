Bag of Steps measures the order-agnostic overlap between generated JSON steps and ground-truth steps, similarly to a bag-of-words measure. It ranges from 0 to 1.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.5

Bag of Steps measures the generated step set without focusing on ordering. RAG improves or maintains this metric across models and OOD splits, with StarCoderBase-7B achieving 0.672 on Human Eval and 0.714 on the weighted OOD average.
