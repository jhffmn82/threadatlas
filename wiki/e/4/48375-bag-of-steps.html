Bag of Steps

Also called: Bag of Steps (BofS), BofS

Abstract written by a model

Bag of Steps is an evaluation metric measuring order-agnostic overlap between generated JSON steps and ground-truth steps from 0 to 1. It ignores ordering; RAG improves or maintains it across models and OOD splits, with StarCoderBase-7B reaching 0.672 and 0.714.
