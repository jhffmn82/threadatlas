Abstract written by a model

The Maya religious system is presented as a changing body of beliefs and rites shaped by invasions and outside influences. It may have begun among the Cans with worship of a divine essence, then developed deified rulers, natural phenomena, images, and idolatrous rites. Its practices included sun worship, sistrum use, divination, oracles, priestesses, temples, offerings, reverence, and honors for deceased rulers, heroes, and eminent women and men, customs said to have spread with Maya emigrants.
