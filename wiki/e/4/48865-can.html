Can

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: CAN, CAN'S, mastodon head, the mastodon's head

Abstract written by a model

Can, meaning serpent, is presented as the founder or head and family name of Chaacmol’s family, associated with principal cities of Mayab. Can and his family worshipped Deity beneath a mastodon-head symbol displayed on buildings, while the family’s feathered-serpent totem surrounded Chaacmol’s mausoleum and was said to influence Uxmal and Chichen.

Can is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
