Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

IX. The Resident Patient

The supposed son accompanies the Russian nobleman and presents himself as deeply concerned about his father's health. He leaves the consultation room, later claims that he mistook his father's departure for the end of the consultation, and is one of the two men suspected of entering Blessington's room.
