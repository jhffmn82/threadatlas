III. .. 5) The Jaccard similarity quantifies the similarity

Jaccard Similarity measures the similarity between two sets by dividing the size of their intersection by the size of their union. Its value ranges from 0 to 1, where 1 indicates complete identity and 0 indicates no common elements.
