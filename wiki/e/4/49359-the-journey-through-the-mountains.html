the journey through the mountains in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The journey through the mountains is an adventure undertaken by Ojo, Scraps, and the Glass Cat toward the Emerald City and the Country of the Winkies. After leaving the advisers, they cross lonely routes, rivers, and dark forests, spend a night at a roadside house, seek directions from the Foolish Owl and Wise Donkey, leave the Tottenhot, climb rough rocks, and pass Mister Yoop’s cave through a narrow passage.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The journey takes Ojo, Scraps, and the Glass Cat from the forest toward the Emerald City and the Country of the Winkies. They are warned that the route includes lonely parts of Oz, rivers, and dark forests, and they spend the first night at a roadside house.

Chapter Eight

The travelers stop at the Foolish Owl and Wise Donkey's house during their journey. After receiving directions and hearing the owl's final hint, they say goodbye and resume traveling toward the Emerald City.

Chapter Twenty

The travelers leave the Tottenhot and climb through rough, sharp rocks toward the mountains. After escaping past Mister Yoop’s cave, they resume their journey along the passage in high spirits.
