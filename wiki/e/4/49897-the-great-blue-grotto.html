The Great Blue Grotto is a fatal place at the foot of the mountain in the Blue Country, beyond the Arch of Phinis outside the City gates. When Blueskins' six hundred years end, they march into it and never return; the Boolooroo expects it to destroy three strangers sent there.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Great Blue Grotto lies beyond the Arch of Phinis, and no one who has entered it is known to have come out alive. The Boolooroo suspects that sending the three strangers there will destroy them.
