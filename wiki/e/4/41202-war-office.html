War Office in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

At the beginning of the war, the War Office rejects women’s offers of military medical service, including Elsie Maud Inglis’s expertise, and rejects Cargilfield as an auxiliary hospital. It operates military hospitals for military medical care; its position on women may change before the war ends.

The War Office initially rejected women’s offers of wartime service, including Elsie Maud Inglis’s offer of medical expertise. It regarded women as unable to fight and therefore as having little use in wartime, although the text notes that its position might change before the end of the war.
