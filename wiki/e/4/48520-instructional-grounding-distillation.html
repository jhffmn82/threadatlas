  • In Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering: Instructional grounding distillation is a method for generalizing GenGround and enabling smaller, open-source models to use generate-then-ground. Its central aim is to give smaller models grounding capability because they initially struggle to cite relevant evidence. It begins with Natural Questions examples containing ground-truth and noise documents. ChatGPT generates and adjusts answers, producing trajectories during instructional knowledge grounding. These trajectories are collected to construct a synthetic training dataset, and ChatGPT's output process is distilled into a smaller student model through instruction tuning with a language modeling objective. The method trains Mistral-7B with 50,000 synthetic examples and is compared with a vanilla prompted version in retrieval analysis. It improves Mistral-7B's overall performance, reaching 42.08 Acc on HotpotQA and 14.37 Acc on MusiqueQA; with 45k training examples it has 42.08 accuracy, versus 40.12 with 20k. Across data scales, it can achieve performance comparable to, and sometimes better than, strong baselines using ChatGPT as the backbone.

instructional grounding distillation in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024

Instructional grounding distillation is a method for generalizing GenGround and enabling smaller, open-source models to use generate-then-ground. Its central aim is to give smaller models grounding capability because they initially struggle to cite relevant evidence. It begins with Natural Questions examples containing ground-truth and noise documents. ChatGPT generates and adjusts answers, producing trajectories during instructional knowledge grounding. These trajectories are collected to construct a synthetic training dataset, and ChatGPT's output process is distilled into a smaller student model through instruction tuning with a language modeling objective. The method trains Mistral-7B with 50,000 synthetic examples and is compared with a vanilla prompted version in retrieval analysis. It improves Mistral-7B's overall performance, reaching 42.08 Acc on HotpotQA and 14.37 Acc on MusiqueQA; with 45k training examples it has 42.08 accuracy, versus 40.12 with 20k. Across data scales, it can achieve performance comparable to, and sometimes better than, strong baselines using ChatGPT as the backbone.
