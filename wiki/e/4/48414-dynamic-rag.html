dynamic RAG in DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models

Weihang Su, Yichen Tang, Qingyao Ai, Zhijing Wu, Yiqun Liu · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where dynamic RAG first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dynamic RAG is a multi-round retrieval-augmented generation paradigm and method for Large Language Models. It performs multiple retrievals during generation, using a retrieval module and query to decide when external knowledge is needed and what to retrieve. Its central challenge is selecting an optimal activation moment and crafting a query, avoiding static timing and narrow recent-context approaches that can add noise and cost or miss broader needs. In DRAGIN, token-level information-need scores trigger retrieval, which can recur later with newly retrieved documents.

The dynamic retrieval augmented generation (RAG) paradigm decides when and what to retrieve during the text generation process of Large Language Models.

Dynamic RAG performs retrieval multiple times during language-model generation. Dynamic RAG in DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Hanghui Guo, Jia Zhu, Shimin Di, Weijie Shi, Zhangze Chen, Jiajie Xu · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where Dynamic RAG first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dynamic RAG is a method that improves language-model outputs and mitigates hallucinations through multi-turn retrieval during generation. It unfolds by selecting when to trigger retrieval and then constructing a query, with existing approaches including fixed-length, fixed-sentence, and uncertainty- or relevance-based retrieval. Its central limitations are ineffective trigger control, insufficient scrutiny of retrieved content, reliance on static rules, lack of capability prediction before generation, and limited retrieval optimization. DioR is presented as a dynamic RAG method that improves retrieval timing and generated-content accuracy through adaptive cognitive detection and post-retrieval processing.
