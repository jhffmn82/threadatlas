Chaacmol's funeral chamber

Also called: the chamber, the funeral chamber of Chaacmol
  • In Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa: contains mural paintings [including scenes of Chaacmol's death and cremation] Chaacmol’s funeral chamber is located on the eastern wall of the gymnasium at Chichen-Itza. Its mural paintings depict Chaacmol in battle, his corpse prepared for cremation, and funerary scenes involving the extraction and preservation of the heart and other organs.
