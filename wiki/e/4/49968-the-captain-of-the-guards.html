THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Captain of the Guards returns with his soldiers after Trot threatens the Boolooroo. He chooses to obey Trot, escorts the Six Snubnosed Princesses to their rooms, guards the palace, and reports that the Pinkies are preparing to attack the City.
