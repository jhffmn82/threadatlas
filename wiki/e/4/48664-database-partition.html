Abstract written by a model

A database partition is a basic entity for RAG execution and retrieval in M-RAG. It supports partition selection for more specific memories, index construction and maintenance, distributed architectures, and privacy protection through access-controlled storage. M-RAG creates multiple homogeneous partitions from a single-source homogeneous dataset and studies partitioning strategies and partition numbers.
