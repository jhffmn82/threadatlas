Mycroft Holmes in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Mycroft Holmes is Sherlock Holmes’s older brother, seven years his senior, with even greater powers of observation and deduction. Despite this, he has little ambition or energy, treats deduction as a hobby, audits government department books, lives in Pall Mall, walks only between Whitehall, and spends much of his time at the Diogenes Club, which he helped found. He brings Mr. Melas’s Greek case to Sherlock, then advertises for information about Paul Kratides and Sophy. After J. Davenport identifies Sophy’s location, Mycroft joins Sherlock, Dr. Watson, and Inspector Gregson at The Myrtles and helps rescue the poisoned captives.

Mycroft Holmes in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mycroft Holmes is Sherlock Holmes’s brother and a subordinate British government official earning four hundred and fifty pounds a year. Indispensable as a coordinator and information specialist, he has an exceptionally orderly fact-storing mind, knows of foreign spies and international agents in England, and regularly resides at Pall Mall and frequents the Diogenes Club. When Arthur Cadogan West dies and the Bruce-Partington plans are stolen, he treats the matter as a national crisis, asks Sherlock to recover the missing plans, offers the State’s assistance, and presses Colonel Valentine Walter to help restore them.
