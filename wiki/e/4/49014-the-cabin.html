” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Tom Fairfield built a cabin for his family on land under the Henderson claim, but that claim was later set aside by Virginia. After Tom disappeared, the cabin contained Roxie, Rose, and Suza with little wood and food. Roxie left it to seek help, and the sisters were rescued from the freezing conditions.
