the cabin in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Tom Fairfield built the cabin for his family on land whose Henderson claim Virginia later set aside. During Tom’s absence, Roxie, Rose, and Suza endured freezing conditions with scant wood and food until Roxie sought help and they were rescued.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Tom Fairfield built a cabin for his family on land under the Henderson claim, but that claim was later set aside by Virginia. After Tom disappeared, the cabin contained Roxie, Rose, and Suza with little wood and food. Roxie left it to seek help, and the sisters were rescued from the freezing conditions.
