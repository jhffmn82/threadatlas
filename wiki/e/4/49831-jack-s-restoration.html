Jack's restoration in Little Wizard Stories of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913

At Jack's pumpkin field, the little Wizard brings Jack Pumpkinhead home, carves and attaches a new pumpkin head, restoring him to good condition.

JACK PUMPKINHEAD AND THE SAWHORSE

Jack's restoration takes place at his pumpkin field after the little Wizard brings him home. The Wizard selects and carves a new pumpkin, attaches it to Jack's neck, and restores Jack to good condition.
