Holmes's career in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Holmes says that he has not lived wholly in vain, having used his powers on the right side in more than a thousand cases. He regards capturing or eliminating Moriarty as the culmination of his career and says that no conclusion could be more congenial to him.

Holmes's career in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Holmes's career began with A Study in Scarlet and The Sign of Four, published between 1887 and 1889, then expanded into a long short-story series beginning with A Scandal in Bohemia in 1891. Across a broken thirty-six-year run, it spans the later Victorian Era, Edward's reign, and afterward, comprising fifty-six stories in four collections plus twelve in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes.

PREFACE

Holmes's career began with A Study in Scarlet and The Sign of Four, published between 1887 and 1889, followed by the first short story, A Scandal in Bohemia, in 1891. By this preface, the adventures comprise fifty-six stories in four collections, with twelve additional stories collected in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes; the career spans the later Victorian Era, the reign of Edward, and the years afterward.
