ANDROMACHE.

Thetis’ temple stands between Phthia and Pharsalia and commemorates Thetis’ marriage to Peleus. Andromache uses it as an asylum, believing that Peleus and his descendants hold it in religious awe, while Hermione threatens to drive her out.
