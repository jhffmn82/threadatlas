The federal government calls for five hundred thousand men through Congress, sends forces into Missouri after Fort Sumter, then during the first year will not interfere with loyal men's slaves.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SCENE SEVENTH--WAR BETWEEN THE STATES.

The federal government calls for troops after Fort Sumter and sends federal forces into Missouri. During the first year of the war, it announces that it will not interfere with the slaves of loyal men.
