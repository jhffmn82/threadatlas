Abstract written by a model

A blue billygoat is obstinate and dangerous. First encountered by soldiers, it attacks the Boolooroo, soldiers, and Six Snubnosed Princesses, clearing the Room of the Great Knife; Trot ties it to a doorway pillar. Later released and locked with the Boolooroo, it serves as a feared guard. It helps Trot defeat and dethrone the Boolooroo, becoming an honored hero promised a fenced yard, food, and lifelong honor from the Blueskins.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The soldiers bring in a great blue billygoat as the first living creature they encounter. It attacks the Boolooroo, soldiers, and Six Snubnosed Princesses, clearing the Room of the Great Knife, and Trot later ties it by the horns to a doorway pillar.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The goat is released in the Room of the Great Knife and locked inside with the Boolooroo. Cap'n Bill considers it an effective guard because everyone is afraid of it.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The billygoat helps Trot defeat the Boolooroo and is treated as a hero by the Blueskins. Ghip-Ghisizzle promises to give it a fenced yard with blue grass and other food and to have the people honor it for life.
