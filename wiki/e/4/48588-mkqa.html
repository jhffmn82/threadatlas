MKQA in BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

David Rau, Hervé Déjean, Nadezhda Chirkova, Thibault Formal, Shuai Wang, Stéphane Clinchant, Vassilina Nikoulina · 2024

MKQA is a linguistically diverse, parallel multilingual open-domain question answering benchmark containing 10,000 Natural Questions examples translated into 25 languages, grounded primarily in English Wikipedia; experiments use a 2.7k-sample KILT NQ-overlapping subset and favor English Wikipedia retrieval.

MKQA in Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024

MKQA is a multilingual question-answering dataset for evaluation, parallel across 25 languages and primarily grounded in English Wikipedia. It originates from Natural Questions, translating questions initially written from English Wikipedia; experiments use about 2.7K overlapping KILT NQ samples, while statistics report 2,827 examples for 13 languages. With median question and answer lengths of 43 and 11 Unicode characters, it is used to compare multilingual RAG, where English-Wikipedia retrieval is especially beneficial.

MKQA is a multilingual dataset parallel across 25 languages and primarily grounded in English Wikipedia. The experiments use a subset of 2.7K samples that overlap with the KILT NQ dataset, allowing relevant document information to be recovered from KILT NQ.

Results and discussion .. opening

MKQA is one of the datasets used to compare multilingual RAG performance. Retrieval from English Wikipedia is more beneficial on MKQA because its questions were initially written using English Wikipedia and then translated into other languages.
