Abstract written by a model

Model-based methods directly prompt large language models to decide whether retrieval is needed, avoiding a predefined calibration threshold. They are evaluated with several instruction-tuned models, though their effectiveness remains ambiguous because earlier methods were assessed separately and within limited scopes.
