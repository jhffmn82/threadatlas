the Great Knife

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Great Knife, dreadful instrument, immense sharp knife, knife, the immense blade, the knife, the terrible knife

Abstract written by a model

The Great Knife is a dreadful, immense cutting machine and instrument of punishment, a huge blade mounted in a derrick-like framework. Used to patch prisoners, it is released by a cord to split a person in two before halves are joined. It is prepared for Ghip-Ghisizzle and Tiggle, then twice misses Cap'n Bill when Trot pulls his frame away. Afterward, it is broken into pieces and burned, and the Blueskins celebrate its destruction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Great Knife is prepared for the patching of Ghip-Ghisizzle and Tiggle. The Boolooroo expects the operation to make the two prisoners look ridiculous when their halves are joined.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

The Great Knife is a huge blade mounted in a framework reaching to the ceiling. When the Boolooroo pulls its cord, it drops to cut in two the person secured beneath it, but Trot pulls Cap'n Bill's frame away twice so that the blade strikes only the air.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Great Knife is broken into pieces and burned with the other furnishings from the Room of the Great Knife. Its destruction is celebrated by the Blueskins because it had caused them great unhappiness.
