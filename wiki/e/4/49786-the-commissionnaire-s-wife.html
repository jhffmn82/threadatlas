Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI. The Naval Treaty

The commissionnaire's wife is a large, elderly woman who works as a charwoman at the Foreign Office. She answers Phelps's summons and receives his order for coffee; a policeman later reports seeing a tall, elderly woman in a Paisley shawl leaving Charles Street, while the commissionnaire insists she is uninvolved.
