the investigation in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Holmes’s principal inquiry concerns the theft of the naval treaty and a related attempted night intrusion. After interviewing Lord Holdhurst, investigating the missing treaty, and examining the alleged Woking intrusion, he finds no reply to his advertisement and little evidence, remaining in Woking as Phelps and Watson return to London.

The investigation includes Sherlock Holmes's interview with Lord Holdhurst, inquiries concerning the missing naval treaty, and examination of the alleged intrusion at Woking. Holmes receives no answer to his advertisement, finds little useful evidence at the window and fence, and continues working in Woking while Phelps and Dr. John Watson return to London.
