Suza Fairfield in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Suza Fairfield, the youngest Fairfield sister, begins as a shivering baby in an unheated cabin and is taken in, raised, and educated by Aunt Katy Demitt until she can leave as a free woman. At eleven, an orphan, she bravely escapes Tom Ditamus after he abducts her, returning through the woods to Port William. She becomes Port William’s belle, attracts suitors but stays with Aunt Katy, meets and rescues Don Carlo from the Red Stone wreck, and is reportedly engaged to him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Suza Fairfield was the baby of the family and was shivering and crying in the unheated cabin. Aunt Katy became attached to her and declared that Suza would remain in her house until she left it as a free woman.

SCENE FIFTH.

Suza Fairfield, an eleven-year-old orphan raised by Aunt Katy Demitt, is abducted by Tom Ditamus but escapes by deceiving him and traveling through the woods back to Port William. She later becomes the belle of Port William and meets Don Carlo at a camp meeting.
