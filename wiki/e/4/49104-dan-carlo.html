Abstract written by a model

Dan Carlo, Don Carlo's brother and equal wood-business partner, receives half the sales money but spends it and repeatedly fails to reform. As Shirt-Tail Bend improves, he feels out of place. He goes to headquarters with Brindle Bill, S. S. Simon, and Sundown Hill amid plans to swindle him, then is found dead in a saloon.

Simon, and Sundown Hill, where they plan to swindle him. Shortly after these events, he is found dead in a saloon.
