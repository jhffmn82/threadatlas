Neoptolemus’s death at Delphi

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Neoptolemus’s murder at Delphi, The murder of Neoptolemus at Delphi, the blood, the murderous violence, this murder
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Neoptolemus’s murder at Delphi is carried out by a Delphian with the help of a multitude after Orestes turns the citizens against him.
  • In The Library, Neoptolemus’s death at Delphi is attributed mainly to Orestes, though another account attributes it to Machaereus after Neoptolemus’s offenses against Apollo’s temple.

Neoptolemus’s death at Delphi in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Neoptolemus was killed at Delphi, according to the main account, by Orestes. Another account says that Machaereus killed him after he demanded satisfaction from Apollo, plundered the votive offerings, and burned the temple.
