Neoptolemus’s death at Delphi

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Neoptolemus’s murder at Delphi, The murder of Neoptolemus at Delphi, the blood, the murderous violence, this murder

Abstract written by a model

In Hecuba and other plays, Neoptolemus’s murder at Delphi is carried out by a Delphian with the help of a multitude after Orestes turns the citizens against him. In The Library, Neoptolemus’s death at Delphi is attributed mainly to Orestes, though another account attributes it to Machaereus after Neoptolemus’s offenses against Apollo’s temple.
