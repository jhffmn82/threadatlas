V. The “_Gloria Scott_”

The coded message appeared to discuss game, a head-keeper, fly-paper, and a hen-pheasant's life. Sherlock Holmes realizes that reading every third word, beginning with the first, reveals a threatening message capable of explaining Mr. Trevor's terror.

The warning says, "The game is up. Hudson has told all. Fly for your life." It uses a prearranged cipher in which the writer fills spaces with two additional words, including the sporting terms "head-keepers" and "hen pheasants."
