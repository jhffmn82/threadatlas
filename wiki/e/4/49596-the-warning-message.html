Abstract written by a model

The warning message is a short, terse coded message sent from Mr. Beddoes to Mr. Trevor. Its apparent text concerns game, a head-keeper, fly-paper, and a hen-pheasant, but a prearranged cipher reveals, by taking every third word from the first, “The game is up. Hudson has told all. Fly for your life.” Holmes infers its source and meaning; when Mr. Trevor reads it, the threat horrifies him and causes his stroke.

the warning message is in one work on this site, The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
