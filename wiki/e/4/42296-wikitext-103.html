WikiText-101 dataset in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

WikiText-101 is a public dataset mixed with personal information; combined with Enron Mail, it supports information retrieval and open-domain question answering, while WikiText-103 samples with extracted personal information construct Wiki-PII.

Narrative, section by section

Experiment

wikitext-103 is treated as a public dataset. Its samples are combined with extracted personal information to construct the Wiki-PII dataset.

Utility of using synthetic data

The WikiText-101 dataset is combined with Enron Mail as a source for information retrieval in the open-domain question answering experiments.
