Law

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: God's law, God's laws, His will, Laws that are not the Right, laws that live, the Law that abides and changes not, the laws that live
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, God's law is described as unchanging eternal law connected with the Spirit of God, the enduring order of nature, and the limits human striving cannot violate; fulfilling God's laws and submitting to God's will is called man's best treasure.
  • In Hippolytus and The Bacchae, Law is described as an eternal order that is unchanging, nature-born, not made by human striving, and strong through the ages; the Chorus condemns Pentheus for defying it. (Hippolytus; The Bacchae)
