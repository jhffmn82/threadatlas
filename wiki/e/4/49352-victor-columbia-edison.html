In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, it is a talking machine attached to the Magician's workshop table, brought to life by the Powder of Life, and it plays music, quarrels with Dr. Pipt, escapes, joins the travelers, and is sent toward a distant Munchkin village.

Victor Columbia Edison in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01 · the work’s page · read it where Victor Columbia Edison first appears

Abstract written by a model

Victor Columbia Edison, or Vic, is a phonograph made living and mobile by Powder of Life. It names itself when asked by Patchwork Girl. Later, its varnished wooden case is damaged by thrown objects; it plays “My Lulu,” angers the Shaggy Man, and runs away to avoid being taken apart.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

Victor Columbia Edison is the name the phonograph gives when Patchwork Girl asks what it is called. Patchwork Girl shortens the name to Vic.

Chapter Eleven

The live phonograph returns with its wooden case marred, dented, and scratched after having objects thrown at it. It plays the popular song 'My Lulu,' angering the Shaggy Man, and then runs away before he can take it apart.

Phonograph in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01 · the work’s page · read it where Phonograph first appears

Abstract written by a model

Victor Columbia Edison is a talking machine attached to the Magician's workshop table, with a gold horn and one phonograph record. Brought to life by the Crooked Magician's Powder of Life, it plays march music during the Patchwork Girl's awakening, quarrels with Dr. Pipt, escapes, joins the travelers, plays music, and is sent toward a distant Munchkin village.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The phonograph, brought to life by the Crooked Magician's Powder of Life, runs away from Dr. Pipt after their quarrel. It catches up with Ojo, the Glass Cat, and Patchwork Girl, plays classical music and rag-time, and is sent toward a distant Munchkin village because the travelers consider it a nuisance.
