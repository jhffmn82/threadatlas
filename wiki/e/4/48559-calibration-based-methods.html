calibration-based methods in RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering

Zihan Zhang, Meng Fang, Ling Chen · 2024

Calibration-based methods are adaptive retrieval-augmented generation methods that use a predefined hyperparameter to trigger retrieval only when a metric exceeds a threshold. They are effective but suboptimal because thresholds require tuning across datasets and models. The process begins with threshold selection, then metric comparison, retrieval when surpassed, and trade-off balancing between task performance and retrieval efficiency.
