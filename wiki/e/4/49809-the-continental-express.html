the Continental express in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The train is preparing to leave Victoria Station when Holmes joins Watson in disguise. It gathers speed and clears the station before Moriarty can stop it.

“Have you seen the morning paper, Watson?”

The express stops at Canterbury and runs in connection with the boat. Holmes and Watson leave it there, while Moriarty follows in another carriage and engine that rushes into the station.
