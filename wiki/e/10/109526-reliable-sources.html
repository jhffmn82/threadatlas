Introduction

Reliable sources are prioritized by Reliability-Aware RAG when they also contain relevant documents. Their information receives greater influence during Weighted Majority Voting.

Limitations

Reliable sources may suddenly disseminate large volumes of unreliable information, including when their accounts are hacked. Although uncommon, this possibility creates a need for more responsive systems.
