Chok Chung

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Chinese merchant, a fat merchant, my man, Chok Chung, shop-keeper, the Christian, the fat shop-keeper, the fugitive, the merchant, the plump Christian merchant, the prisoner, the swimmer

Abstract written by a model

Chok Chung is an extremely honest, mild Christian convert and merchant who owns a Chinese merchant’s hemp. In town, Au-yoeng kills him in his shop, alarming Dr. Earle because Chok Chung is one of his converts. In another account of his persecution, he is tried and questioned for following the Lord Jesus; after affirming his faith, his teeth are beaten out and he is thrown into prison, wearing soaked prison rags. Later he swims desperately toward a house while an armed boat pursues him, crying, “I am Jesus Christ!” He is shot in the shallows, sinks, and dies.

Chok Chung is in one work on this site, Dragon's Blood. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
