Experimental Setup / Tasks and Datasets .. Implementation Details

The scientific domain evaluation includes the scientific subsets of MMLU, ARC-challenge, and SciQ. Wikipedia is used as an unlabeled corpus, and top-10 passages are fetched by Google Search for evaluation on datasets outside the medical domain.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

In the scientific domain, SimRAG outperforms the evaluated baselines, with an average performance gain of 6.37% over the Llama variant and 3.50% over the Gemma variant.
