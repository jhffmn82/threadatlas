video retrieval in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Soyeong Jeong · 2025

Video retrieval is a module for video corpora that identifies query-relevant videos. It represents the query and each video with a large video language model, compares representations through similarity such as cosine similarity, and returns the top-k videos. It is critical to RAG performance: oracle retrieval is strongest, suggesting retrieval can improve. Textual features consistently outperform visual features, though combining them helps. Its corpus may contain problematic examples that support biased or harmful responses.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Method .. 2.4 / Auxiliary Text Generation

Video retrieval identifies query-relevant videos from the video corpus. It obtains representations for the query and each video with a large video language model, compares them using representation-level similarity such as cosine similarity, and selects the top-k videos.

opening .. Case Study

Video retrieval is identified as a critical factor in RAG performance. Oracle retrieval produces the strongest results, indicating room for improvement in retrieval mechanisms.

Ethics Statement

The retrieval process inherently depends on the video corpus. Problematic examples in the corpus may lead it to support responses that reflect bias or harm.
