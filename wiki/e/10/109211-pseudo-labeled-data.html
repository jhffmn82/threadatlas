Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Pseudo-labeled data is generated by prompting the same language model with unlabeled specialized-domain corpora. SimRAG uses the resulting data for continued fine-tuning toward domain-specific question answering.

Related Work

Pseudo-labeled tuples are generated by extracting candidate answers from corpus documents and then generating candidate questions conditioned on the documents and answers. SimRAG filters these tuples with round-trip consistency before using them for further fine-tuning.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

SimRAG generates pseudo-labeled data through question generation and answer generation after Stage-I. Question filtering improves overall performance and accelerates training, while diverse pseudo-labeled examples across multiple question types provide the strongest results.
