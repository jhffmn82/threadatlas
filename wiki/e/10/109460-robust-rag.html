Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

Robust RAG is a certifiably robust defense framework and accuracy-comparison-baseline method for retrieval-augmented generation. It aggregates keywords from independent passages assuming most documents are trustworthy, but struggles with varying reliability and degrades as adversaries increase.

Robust RAG aggregates keywords from independent passages under the assumption that most retrieved documents are trustworthy. It struggles when source reliability varies and performs worse than MV under the heterogeneous-reliability setting.
