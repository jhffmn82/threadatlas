attack tasks

topic · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: attack task

Abstract written by a model

Attack tasks are four security evaluation tasks: silver noise, inter-context conflict, soft ad, and white Denial-of-Service (white DoS). They generate malicious attack contexts/texts that are combined with golden contexts for dataset construction. Even apparent attacks can bypass retrievers, filters, and advanced large language models, degrading RAG service quality. In generator and evaluator analysis, related settings include noise, conflict, toxicity, and DoS; Baichuan 13B leads in multiple tasks, especially DoS.
