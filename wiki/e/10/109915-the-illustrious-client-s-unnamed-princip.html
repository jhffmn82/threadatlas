THE ADVENTURE OF THE ILLUSTRIOUS CLIENT

The unnamed principal is an old friend of General de Merville who has taken a paternal interest in Violet since childhood. He asks Sir James Damery to engage Holmes to prevent the marriage but insists on remaining personally uninvolved and anonymous.
