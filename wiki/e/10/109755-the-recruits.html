the recruits in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The recruits are about a dozen novices of the brotherhood who enter the lodge disguised in coolie clothes, then undergo initiation by swearing to the Fragrant Ones and sharing blood offerings and a ritual drink.

Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER XI WHITE LOTUS He was swarming up, quiet as a thief, when his fingers clawed the bare plaster.

A group of about a dozen recruits crawls into the lodge in coolie clothes with their queues un-plaited. They swear oaths, offer blood from their wrists, and drink from the goblet after it is mixed with the blood of the white cock.
