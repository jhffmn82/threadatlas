Electryon in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Electryon ruled Mycenae, fathered Alcmena and Licymnius, entrusted the kingdom and Alcmena to Amphitryon before war against the Teleboans, and was accidentally killed by Amphitryon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, 1]. 1v. 2-3

Electryon ruled Mycenae and committed the kingdom and Alcmena to Amphitryon while planning war against the Teleboans. Amphitryon accidentally killed him when a club rebounded from a cow’s horns and struck his head.
