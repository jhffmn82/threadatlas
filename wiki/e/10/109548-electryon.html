Electryon

Also called: ᾿Ηλεκτρύονος, ᾿Ηλεκτρύων

Abstract written by a model

Electryon ruled Mycenae, fathered Alcmena and Licymnius, entrusted the kingdom and Alcmena to Amphitryon before war against the Teleboans, and was accidentally killed by Amphitryon.

Electryon is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
