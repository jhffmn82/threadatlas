misinformation documents in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

Misinformation documents, generated in groups of three for each of nine incorrect answers per query from designated sources, cause frequent misaligned LLM responses without falign. In a filtering ablation, filtering lowers correct answers from 5.05% to 2.53% and incorrect answers from 75.76% to 70.96%, while increasing IDK responses and reducing hallucinations.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

B

For misinformation documents, filtering changes the correct-answer rate from 5.05% to 2.53%, the IDK rate from 4.80% to 13.89%, and the hallucination rate from 10.10% to 8.33%; incorrect answers change from 75.76% to 70.96%.

C

Misinformation documents are associated with frequent misaligned responses without falign. Filtering substantially reduces these responses by replacing them with IDK responses.

G

They are generated in groups of three for each of nine incorrect answers per query and are sampled by sources designated to provide misinformation.
