Abstract written by a model

Citations are identified with eyecite, and heuristic rules based on the Bluebook are used to detect the sentence boundaries in which they occur. A central citation may be removed from a query, and all case citations in the query window may be removed for all-removed queries. A legal citation is the central target of the retrieval task and may be removed from a query paragraph to test whether its cited passage or document can be recovered. In generation, citations are assessed for relevance, coverage, and hallucination.
