Dolores in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Dolores is a tall, slim, brown-faced South American maid and servant in the Ferguson household, a longtime friend of Ferguson's Peruvian wife. On a foggy November evening, she cares for the ill wife, fears being alone without a doctor, brings Dr. Watson to her, admits Holmes and Ferguson, and hears Holmes explain the case.

It was evening of a dull, foggy November day when, having left our bags

Dolores, a tall, slim, brown-faced girl, cares for Ferguson's ill wife and fears being left alone with her without a doctor. She takes Dr. Watson to the wife's room, later admits Holmes and Ferguson, and remains present while Holmes explains the case.
