Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V. When Hercules heard that, he went to Tiryns

The Cerynitian hind has golden horns and is sacred to Artemis. Hercules pursues it for a year, captures it alive near the river Ladon, appeases Artemis by blaming Eurystheus, and carries it to Mycenae.
