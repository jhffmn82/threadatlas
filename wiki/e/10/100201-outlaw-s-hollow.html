outlaw's hollow in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

A small secluded valley enclosed by heights and buckeye-covered hills, outlaw's hollow is a secure isolated retreat, inaccessible from the stage road by a sheer cliff and guarded by difficult approaches.

II The robbery furnished the usual amount of copy for the local press.

The outlaw's hollow is a small, secluded valley enclosed by surrounding heights and little buckeye-covered hills. It is inaccessible from the stage road's side because of a sheer cliff and is protected by difficult approaches.
