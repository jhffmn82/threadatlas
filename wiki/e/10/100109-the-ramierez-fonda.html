the Ramierez fonda in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The Ramierez fonda is an inn on the outskirts of the town, formerly an old hacienda. Its patio is a stable-yard for carts, one wing is an open-veranda corridor, and the opposite wing is Ramierez's tienda. It hosts gambling and sells aguardiente. Richards visits while investigating attacks on people leaving with winnings and sees the Ramierez daughter. Later, the fonda is linked with Cota, her family, attacks on Colonel Starbottle and Richards, and a scuffle outside its stable door.
