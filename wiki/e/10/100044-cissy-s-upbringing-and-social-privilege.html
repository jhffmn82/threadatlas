" A BELLE OF CANADA CITY Cissy was tying her hat under her round chin before a small glass at her window.

Cissy has grown up as Montagu Trixit's daughter in the most prominent house in Canada City, which has an unusually luxurious double drawing-room and a carefully landscaped garden. Since her mother's death, she has treated her father's wealth and authority as constant and natural, without understanding his business responsibilities. Her social privilege makes her confident that the town will defer to her and that her father can satisfy her wishes.
