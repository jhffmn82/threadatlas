Abstract written by a model

Flounce is a small fox-terrier, affectionately called Foreign Dog by Miss Drake. On a voyage she first recognizes Heywood, barks at a hostile approaching boat and draws gunfire through the sail, then whimpers and joyfully leaps at Heywood when he returns aboard; he says she alone knew he was alive.

Flounce is in one work on this site, Dragon's Blood. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Flounce barks at the approaching hostile boat, provoking a gunshot that tears through the matting sail. Later, when Heywood's boat approaches, she whimpers as though recognizing something, then leaps joyfully at Heywood when he comes aboard. Heywood calls her the only one who knew he was alive.
