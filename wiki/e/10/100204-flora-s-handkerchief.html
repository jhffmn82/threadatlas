III In twenty-four hours Edward Brice was in San Francisco.

Flora returns Edward Brice's handkerchief by mail after having its bullet-rent carefully mended so that it nearly resembles embroidery. Its return, accompanied by Tarbox's printed address, gives Brice a clue that Flora has not forgotten him.
