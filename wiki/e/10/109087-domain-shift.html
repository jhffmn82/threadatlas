Also called: distribution shifts

Abstract written by a model

Domain shift between standard or general retrieval training data and legal text reduces zero-shot legal citation retrieval performance in the CLERC task. It contributes to poor results, but fine-tuning on CLERC’s longer queries and legal material alleviates the problem, showing the importance of legal-data training.
