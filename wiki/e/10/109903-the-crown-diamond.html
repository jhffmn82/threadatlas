the Crown diamond in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The Crown diamond, also called the Mazarin stone, is a great missing yellow jewel valued at one hundred thousand pounds. Count Negretto Sylvius keeps it in a secret pocket, plans to send it out of England and cut it into four pieces in Amsterdam. Holmes links Sylvius and Sam Merton to its theft, takes the diamond, and shows it to Lord Cantlemere.
