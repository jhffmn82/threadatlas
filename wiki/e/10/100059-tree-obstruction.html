Abstract written by a model

The tree obstruction is a fallen tree lying across and blocking the road. It jolts and stops the stagecoach. Yuba Bill finds the coach’s regulation axe ineffective, obtains a larger axe and ropes, and leads its removal. The newspaper describes it as possibly preliminary work by the robber gang. With Hiram Tarbox’s assistance it is removed, and its location helps Brice find the mountain trail.
