Colonel Emsworth in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Colonel Emsworth is a former army officer, Crimean V.C., former martinet, father of his only son Godfrey, and owner of the Emsworth mansion, where he lives with his wife. Strict and intimidating, he conceals Godfrey and the family mystery. He tells James M. Dodd that Godfrey is traveling for his health, refuses details, later confronts a watcher at the detached lodge, and orders Dodd and Holmes away. When Holmes writes “Leprosy,” Emsworth admits the secret and allows them to see Godfrey, explaining that he hid him to prevent compulsory segregation.
