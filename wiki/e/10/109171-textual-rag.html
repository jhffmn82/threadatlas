Textual RAG

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: textual, textual RAG, textual retrieval

Abstract written by a model

Textual RAG retrieves relevant text chunks with BGE-1.5 and a seven-item context window, excelling at linguistic reasoning but potentially missing visual elements. It generally trails Visual RAG on visually rich datasets, though its disadvantage narrows when figures have detailed text and captions.

Textual RAG is in one work on this site, VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Results / 7.1 .. 7.3

Textual RAG generally performs below Visual RAG on the visually rich datasets. Its relative disadvantage is smaller on SciGraphQA and SPIQA, whose scientific figures are accompanied by detailed text and captions; Gemini often performs better in the textual modality than in the visual modality.
