Abstract written by a model

The Ming saucer is a delicate deep-blue piece of genuine egg-shell pottery from the Ming dynasty, one of six corresponding pieces and worth a king's ransom as a complete set. Sherlock Holmes uses it to support Dr. John Watson's false collector identity and entice Baron Adelbert Gruner into receiving him.
