the placards

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: placards, the big characters, the long red scrolls, their placards

Abstract written by a model

Placards posted on both nunnery gate pillars and in the West Quarter warn of agitation. They promote anti-foreign action, accusing the “Red-Bristled Ghosts” of attacks on ancestral worship, mind-darkening pills, and taking babies’ eyes for silver. They call for missionaries’ expulsion, Tao-tais to assemble people, and patriots to join.

" CHAPTER XIV OFF DUTY The last of the sunlight streamed level through a gap in the western ridges.

The placards accuse the 'Red-Bristled Ghosts' of teaching people to break ancestral tablets and worship a picture of a naked infant. They claim that foreigners give converts mind-darkening pills and dig out babies' eyes for silver, and they end by urging local officials and patriots to drive the foreigners beyond the oceans.
