Proetus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Proetus, twin brother of Acrisius, returns from Lycia and becomes king of Tiryns. Restored by his father-in-law, he fathers children, accepts Melampus’s cure terms, and later seeks Bellerophon’s death through Iobates.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, 11. 1. 5—u. 1

Proetus was restored to his land by his father-in-law and occupied Tiryns, which the Cyclopes had fortified. He had three daughters by Stheneboea, agreed to Melampus's terms for curing the women, and later sent Bellerophon to Iobates with a letter ordering Bellerophon's death.
