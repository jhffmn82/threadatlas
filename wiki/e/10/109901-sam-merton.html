Sam Merton in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Sam Merton is a professional boxer and criminal accomplice of Count Negretto Sylvius in the Crown diamond burglary. Described as a great, silly, bull-headed gudgeon, he waits outside Baker Street, fears Holmes may be listening, and joins Sylvius's attempt to deceive him. Merton suggests killing Holmes, but after Holmes reveals himself, takes the stone, and arrests them, Merton accepts it as a fair cop.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE MAZARIN STONE

Sam Merton is identified as Count Negretto Sylvius's partner in the Crown diamond burglary. He waits outside Baker Street, is brought into the meeting by Billy, and is told that he may face imprisonment unless the stone is surrendered.

Holmes withdrew, picking up his violin from the corner as he passed. A

Sam Merton agrees with Count Sylvius's plan to deceive Holmes and suggests killing him, though Count Sylvius rejects the idea. He is surprised when Holmes emerges from the dummy's chair and takes the stone, then acknowledges the arrest as a fair cop.
