the narrow mountain trail in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The narrow mountain trail is a rocky, uneven, slowly ascending trail, less than a foot wide in places. Hidden from the road by brush but overlooking it, it runs between the mountain wall and river-side thicket, climbs from the road up the mountain to a ridge, then descends through wilder ground toward the North Fork canyon and outlaw's hollow. Flora leads Brice along it to the fallen pine.
