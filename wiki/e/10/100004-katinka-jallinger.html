Abstract written by a model

Katinka Jallinger, also called Tinka, is a seventeen- or eighteen-year-old young woman living in the isolated Jallinger cabin with her father, Henry Boone Jallinger, and cared for by Mammy. Initially mistaken for a child because of oversized clothes and a sunbonnet, she lends Jack Fleming a bread pan for his gold specimen ring. Though her father dislikes miners, she sends Fleming gold flakes and a letter, admits she salted his pan to bring him back, leads him to gold-bearing quartz at the Run, and becomes engaged to him.

Katinka Jallinger is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

" A TREASURE OF THE REDWOODS PART I Mr.

Katinka Jallinger, also called Tinka, lives at an isolated cabin with her father, Henry Boone Jallinger. She initially appears to Fleming to be a child because she wears oversized clothing and a large sunbonnet, but she is revealed to be a young woman of seventeen or eighteen. She lends Fleming the bread pan in exchange for his gold specimen ring and tells him that her father dislikes miners.

PART II Mr.

Katinka Jallinger sends Fleming gold flakes and a letter to bring him back, but later admits that she salted his pan because she wanted him to return. She leads Fleming to the Run, where they find gold-bearing quartz, and becomes engaged to him.
