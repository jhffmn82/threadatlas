Miss Piney Tibbs in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Miss Piney Tibbs is a pretty girl, the hotel-keeper John Tibbs’s daughter and Cissy Trixit’s protégée and bosom friend. Though conscious of their unequal social position, she remains loyal to Cissy, admires the young engineer, and accompanies Cissy through town before leaving her at the parsonage. Later, Piney writes that her father will not let her come to Cissy, but she will try to slip out late that night.
