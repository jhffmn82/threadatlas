HippoRAG2 in CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Nengbo Wang, Xiaotian Han, Jagdip Singh, Jing Ma, Vipin Chaudhary · 2025

HippoRAG2 is a recent competitive RAG method using an entity-centric knowledge graph: it extracts entities and passage nodes, selects seed nodes, applies PageRank with deeper passage integration, ranks filtered triples for generation, and reports faithfulness 67.36, precision 73.72, recall 47.22.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Experiment / Experimental Setup .. Parameter Study

HippoRAG2 selects seed nodes and ranks filtered triples for generation, using entity extraction and passage nodes. It achieves answer faithfulness of 67.36, context precision of 73.72, and context recall of 47.22, but can leave context-rich sentences unlinked to the query.
