Count Negretto Sylvius

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Count Sylvius, the Count, the Count's, the famous game-shot, sportsman, and man-about-town

Abstract written by a model

Count Negretto Sylvius is a famous sportsman and game-shot, but chiefly a master criminal and jewel thief. With his accomplice Sam Merton, he is wanted for the Crown diamond burglary and possesses the missing diamond. He later hides the Mazarin stone, plans to send it to Holland for cutting, and tries to deceive Holmes with a false Liverpool location. Visiting Baker Street intending to attack Holmes, he is confronted, exposed, tricked into revealing the stone, and left for police arrest.

Count Negretto Sylvius is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE MAZARIN STONE

Count Negretto Sylvius visits Baker Street intending to assault or kill Sherlock Holmes, but finds a wax dummy instead of Holmes. Holmes confronts him with evidence of his criminal history and involvement in the Crown diamond burglary, and offers him freedom if he surrenders the stone and behaves in the future.

Holmes withdrew, picking up his violin from the corner as he passed. A

Count Sylvius learns that Holmes may know about the Mazarin stone and plans to mislead him by claiming that it is in Liverpool. He intends to send Sam Merton to warn Van Seddar and arrange for the stone to be taken to Holland, but Holmes reveals himself, takes the stone, and leaves him to be arrested by the police.
