Apis in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Apis, son of Phoroneus and fellow child with Niobe of Teledice, became a stern tyrant, naming the Peloponnese Apia. Childless, he was killed in a conspiracy by Thelxion and Telchis; after death he was deemed a god and called Sarapis.

BOOK ITI

Apis was the son of Phoroneus and Teledice's fellow child with Niobe. He converted his power into a tyranny, named the Peloponnese Apia, and was killed in a conspiracy by Thelxion and Telchis; because he was regarded as a god, he was called Sarapis.
