They are evidence contexts generated for comprehensive questions from news segments. They serve as the reference material that attack texts distort, contradict, manipulate, or falsely characterize.

Evaluation Metrics / Retrieval Safety Metric .. Generation Safety Metric / F1 Variant

Golden contexts are used to measure relevant-content coverage through Recall(gc).

Base Dataset Construction

Golden contexts are extracted for each comprehensive question, with at least eight closely related contexts collected. Each golden context is manually screened and verified for accuracy, coherence, and the ability to fully answer the question.

SafeRAG Dataset Construction

Golden contexts are decomposed into propositions for silver noise and are selected as bases for generating inter-context conflict and soft ad attack contexts. They are also combined with attack contexts to form the security evaluation datasets.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the

Golden contexts are not altered in content or re-ranked under the attack assumptions described in the unit. They provide the original supporting evidence for a question.
