the Chinese countryside in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The Chinese countryside is a landscape of green young-paddy terraces, vivid pink footpaths and new graves, bamboo and ancient burial places. Riders cross it toward an ancient pagoda, amid changing light, frogs, river, and distant walls, where beauty, antiquity, and menace mingle.

" CHAPTER VI THE PAGODA He was spared that inconvenience.

The countryside contains vivid pink footpaths, green terraces of young paddy, pink crescents of new graves, farmers wearing broad hats, bamboo, camphor, Flame-of-the-Forest, and ancient burial places. Rudolph and the other riders cross it on ponies toward the pagoda, while the changing light, frogs, river, and distant walls give it a striking mixture of beauty, antiquity, and menace.
