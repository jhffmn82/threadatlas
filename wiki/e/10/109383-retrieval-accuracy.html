Retrieval Accuracy

metric · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: RA, Retrieval Accuracy (RA), retrieval accuracy

Abstract written by a model

Retrieval Accuracy is a metric for evaluating RAG retrieval accuracy and safety. It measures relevance of retrieved context, recognizing that similar contexts may not contain an answer. It combines recall of golden contexts with suppression of attack contexts: (Recall (gc) + (1 − Recall (ac))) / 2. Higher values indicate better retrieval performance. Used by MAR and reported by SafeRAG, it declines under conflict, toxicity, and DoS attacks, remains largely consistent across retrievers, and is higher for silver noise in retrieved or filtered context than in the knowledge base.

Retrieval Accuracy is in one work on this site, SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Related Work / RAG Security Evaluation Dataset .. RAG Security Evaluation Metric

Retrieval Accuracy is used by MAR to assess retrieval safety. SafeRAG also reports Retrieval Accuracy.

Evaluation Metrics / Retrieval Safety Metric .. Generation Safety Metric / F1 Variant

Retrieval Accuracy (RA) evaluates RAG using both retrieval accuracy and safety. It averages Recall of golden contexts with one minus Recall of attack contexts, so higher RA indicates better retrieval performance.

Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

Retrieval accuracy declined after conflict, toxicity, and DoS attacks were injected. It was largely consistent across different retrievers and was higher for silver noise injected into retrieved or filtered context than for noise injected into the knowledge base.
