Abstract written by a model

Flora Dimwood, also called Flo and Flora Tarbox, is Snapshot Harry’s niece, Henry Dimwood’s niece, and cousin to Hiram and Sophy. A bold, audacious, wonderfully pretty young woman, she lives around her uncle’s cabin and home in the bush, though loneliness leads her to wander beyond the hollow. After her mother’s death, she attends a Catholic convent school as a refuge. Flora’s chief importance is her independent knowledge of the mountain country and her loyalty amid the robbery’s aftermath. She has explored the valley on foot, discovered a narrow trail unknown to the gang, and uses it to guide Edward Brice safely through the terrain while shielding him from the gang’s questions. Henry protects her within the gang’s territory, yet Flora acts for herself: she defends Hiram and Sophy before the stage company, serves passengers, playfully unsettles the expressman, and jokes and flirts with Brice. She gives Brice her embroidered handkerchief, keeps his as a souvenir, wears Henry’s hat, and helps Brice locate the stolen money. She urges him not to return it to uncle Harry and directs him toward a route beyond the cabin. Afterward, Flora sends Brice’s repaired handkerchief through Hiram Tarbox as a clue to Hiram’s address, keeps the hollow’s events secret, and later reunites with Brice. She tells him that she buried the actual thief and burned the empty belt.

Flora Dimwood is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Flo is Snapshot Harry’s niece and a young woman with an audacious, attractive manner. She openly acknowledges her uncle’s identity, defends Hiram and Sophy against the stage company’s treatment, serves whiskey and coffee to the passengers, and playfully unsettles the expressman.

II The robbery furnished the usual amount of copy for the local press.

Flora Tarbox has left the Tarbox cabin at sun-up and gone to her uncle's home. Her footprints mark parts of the difficult trail Brice follows, and Hiram's wife says Flora sometimes wanders outside the hollow when she becomes lonesome.

Flora accompanies Brice as his guide and protection from the gang's questions. She jokes and flirts with him, gives him her handkerchief while offering to mend his, and reveals that Henry is protective of her because of his outlaw reputation.

"I am going to be a real guide to you now," she said suddenly.

Flora Dimwood reveals a hidden route up the mountain that she has discovered while exploring the valley. She helps Brice find the stolen money, argues against taking it back to uncle Harry, and directs Brice up the fallen pine toward a trail leading beyond the cabin.

III In twenty-four hours Edward Brice was in San Francisco.

Flora sends Edward Brice's repaired handkerchief as a clue to Hiram Tarbox's address and remains loyal by keeping the events in the hollow secret. She is placed in a Catholic convent school, reunites with Brice, and tells him that she buried the actual thief and burned the empty belt.
