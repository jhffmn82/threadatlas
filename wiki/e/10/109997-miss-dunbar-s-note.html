Miss Dunbar's note

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a note, the note, the short note

Abstract written by a model

Miss Dunbar's note, addressed to Mrs. Gibson, arranges a nine o'clock appointment at Thor Bridge and is signed “G. Dunbar.” Mrs. Gibson is found clutching it in her left hand at death. The note becomes direct evidence against the governess, though the clutching suggests it was not planted after death.

Miss Dunbar's note is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

The note found on Neil Gibson's dead wife arranged an appointment at Thor Bridge and was signed by Miss Dunbar. It is treated as direct evidence against the governess.

There was some delay in the official pass, and instead of reaching

Miss Dunbar's note read, "I will be at Thor Bridge at nine o'clock.--G. Dunbar." Mrs. Gibson was found tightly clutching it in her left hand, and Holmes considers that detail important because it suggests the note was not placed there after her death.
