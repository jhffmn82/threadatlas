young Masterton in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24 · the work’s page · read it where young Masterton first appears

Abstract written by a model

Young Masterton, Judge Masterton’s well-connected, well-educated son, becomes engineer of the mill works in Canada City. Though Cissy finds him conceited and disrespectful, Piney admires his intelligence and appearance. He works beside miners and townsmen to understand the failed old works, criticizes the city’s mushroom prosperity, and later greets Cissy with unusual gravity.

Young Masterton is the new engineer of the mill works and the son of Judge Masterton, with wealthy relations. He works alongside common laborers to understand how the mill operates and why the old works failed. Cissy considers him conceited and disrespectful, while Piney regards him as smart, well educated, and attractive; he later greets Cissy with unusual gravity.

Dick Masterton in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24 · the work’s page · read it where Dick Masterton first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dick Masterton, the New Mill's engineer and a former deputy sheriff, is sent by the directors to find Montagu Trixit after Trixit's bank fails and to seek a business interview. Though disgusted by the assignment and unwilling to question Trixit's daughter, whom he knows, he accepts it. He travels by coach, then takes a mustang to follow a Chinese messenger toward a ruined prospector's hut. The horse falls through snow, leaving Masterton bruised, shaken, unconscious, and nearly frozen. Cissy Trixit rescues him, saving his life. He tells her he acted under the directors' orders and follows her to Sacramento, where she says Montagu Trixit will be.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

The excitement over the failure of Montagu Trixit's bank did not burn itself out

Dick Masterton was the New Mill's engineer and was hurriedly summoned to the directors' meeting. Though disgusted by the purpose of the assignment, he accepted the adventurous mission to find Trixit and possibly obtain an interview. He rejected the suggestion that he seek information from Trixit's daughter because he was acquainted with her.

A few hours later he was on the coach.

Mr. Masterton travels by mountain wagon toward the next station while reflecting uneasily on his mission and on Cissy's father's disgrace. After hearing that Montagu Trixit may be nearby, he takes a saddled mustang and follows a Chinese messenger toward the prospector's ruined hut, but his horse plunges through the snow and he is knocked unconscious.

How long he lay there thus he never knew.

Masterton is rescued after lying unconscious and nearly frozen in a prospector's hole. He learns that Cissy saved his life, admits that he came under orders from his directors to see Montagu Trixit, and agrees to go to Sacramento after Cissy tells him he will be there.
