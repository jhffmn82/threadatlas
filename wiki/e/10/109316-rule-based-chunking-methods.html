Abstract

Rule-based chunking methods are widely used but usually produce suboptimal splits. Their overly large chunks can contain irrelevant information, while their small chunks can lack semantic coherence.

4 / Experimental Settings .. 4.4 / Implementation Details

Rule-based chunking is evaluated through sentence-by-sentence, paragraph-by-paragraph, and fixed-size window segmentation. The fixed-size method creates 100-word chunks while preserving sentence boundaries.
