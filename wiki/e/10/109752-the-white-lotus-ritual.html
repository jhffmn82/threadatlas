the White Lotus ritual in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The White Lotus ritual, said to ape Triad ritual, begins with a white-lime circle, right foot placement, and a declaration of unity; participants drink from a designated cup, may include blood drinking, answer symbolic questions, and are admitted through successive doors.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER X THREE PORTALS Not till after dinner, that evening, did Rudolph rouse from his stupor.

The White Lotus ritual includes a circle of white lime, placing the right foot within it, and declaring, "We are all in-the-circle men." It also involves drinking from a designated cup and answering symbolic questions before being admitted through successive doors.
