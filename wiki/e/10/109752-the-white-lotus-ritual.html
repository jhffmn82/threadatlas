the White Lotus ritual

Abstract written by a model

The White Lotus ritual, said to ape Triad ritual, begins with a white-lime circle, right foot placement, and a declaration of unity; participants drink from a designated cup, may include blood drinking, answer symbolic questions, and are admitted through successive doors.
