daughter's mustang in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Cota's favored mustang is a half-broken pinto mare, calico and mottled in lavender and pink, with Arabian proportions and greenish-gray eyes. When Grey mounts her, she repeatedly tries to throw him until his spurs stop her, leaving tracks on her sides. Later she attacks Colonel Starbottle and Richards with her teeth. Richards shoots her twice in the neck, killing her, and Cota becomes furious.

PART II The next afternoon he went to the fonda.

Cota's mustang is a half-broken pinto, or calico horse, mottled in lavender and pink, Arabian in proportions, and similar to Cota in its unusual appearance and expression. When Grey mounts it, the mustang repeatedly leaps in an attempt to throw him, but his spurs force it to stop, leaving scarifying tracks along its sides.
