the Black Dog

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Black Dog, The Black Dog, a bronze cannon, cannon, the Black Dog from Rotterdam, the Hak Kau

Abstract written by a model

The Black Dog, or Hak Kau, is a nine-foot bronze cannon cast at Rotterdam in 1607 and used by the attacking Chinese forces. Identified first as a cannon whose bark is meant to signal the Fragrant Ones' action, it is reportedly seen in a shed in the town, then missing the next day while attackers await ammunition. During the attack, its heavy sound is recognized as having barked earlier than expected. In the assault it fires repeatedly from nearby, sending copper cash clinking along the wall; its report serves as a signal rather than battering the masonry with round-shot.
