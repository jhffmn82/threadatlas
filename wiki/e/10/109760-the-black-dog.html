the Black Dog in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The Black Dog, or Hak Kau, is a nine-foot bronze cannon cast at Rotterdam in 1607 and used by the attacking Chinese forces. Identified first as a cannon whose bark is meant to signal the Fragrant Ones' action, it is reportedly seen in a shed in the town, then missing the next day while attackers await ammunition. During the attack, its heavy sound is recognized as having barked earlier than expected. In the assault it fires repeatedly from nearby, sending copper cash clinking along the wall; its report serves as a signal rather than battering the masonry with round-shot.

The Black Dog is identified by Wutzler as a cannon. Its firing is intended to serve as the signal for the Fragrant Ones' action.

" CHAPTER XII THE WAR BOARD "Rigmarole?

The Hak Kau, described as the attackers' Black Dog, is identified as a bronze cannon nine feet long and cast at Rotterdam in 1607. O.W. reportedly saw it in a shed one night, but it was gone the next day, and the attackers are waiting for more ammunition.

CHAPTER XV KAU FAI At the top of the nunnery stairs, Rudolph met them with awkward ceremony, and with that smiling air of encouragement which a nurse might use in trying cheerfully to deceive a sick man.

The Black Dog is identified by Heywood as the source of the heavy sound heard during the attack. Heywood says that it has barked earlier than expected.

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The Black Dog from Rotterdam fires repeatedly during the assault, sending a shower of copper cash clinking along the wall. Its heavy report signals that it is not far away, but it does not batter the masonry with round-shot.
