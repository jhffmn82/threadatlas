Gemma2-27B-Instruct in SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains

Ran Xu, Hui Liu, Sreyashi Nag, Zhenwei Dai, Yaochen Xie, Xianfeng Tang, Chen Luo, Yang Li, Joyce C. Ho, Carl Yang, Qi He · 2025

Gemma2-27B-Instruct is a general-domain LLM used as a backbone and baseline for the proposed method. Fine-tuned with LoRA under resource constraints, it scores 59.40 medical, 76.28 scientific, and 58.08 overall in computer science; SimRAG 27B improves on these results.

Experimental Setup / Tasks and Datasets .. Implementation Details

Gemma2-27B-Instruct is used as another backbone for the proposed method and corresponding baseline implementations. Because of resource constraints, LoRA with r=32 and α=32 is used during fine-tuning; the backbone scores 59.40 on the medical-domain average and 76.28 on the scientific-domain average, while SimRAG 27B reaches 65.17 and 81.64.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

Gemma2-27B-Instruct is used as a backbone and baseline, scoring 58.08 overall in the computer science results. SimRAG 27B improves this score to 60.96 and achieves performance close to GPT-4 across the three domains.
