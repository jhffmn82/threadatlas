Introduction

Synthetic data is used to address the scarcity of high-quality fine-tuning data for retrieval-augmented generation. SimRAG generates synthetic examples from unlabeled specialized-domain corpora, while prior approaches are described as costly, inefficient, and lacking explicit quality control.
