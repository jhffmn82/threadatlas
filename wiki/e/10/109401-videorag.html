VideoRAG

framework · 1 work · 38 quoted facts · 15 connections
Also called: our VideoRAG, our proposed VideoRAG, proposed VideoRAG

Abstract written by a model

VideoRAG is a novel retrieval-augmented generation framework and video-based RAG approach that uses a large video corpus as an external knowledge source for answering user queries. It dynamically retrieves query-relevant videos, then grounds response generation in those videos by jointly using visual information, video frames, and textual information. Powered by Large Video Language Models, it processes video content within a unified framework rather than relying solely on frame-based or text-conversion approaches. Its pipeline combines video retrieval, adaptive frame selection, auxiliary text generation, and video-augmented response generation. Adaptive frame selection retains only the most informative subset of frames, improving processing effectiveness and efficiency. When subtitles are unavailable, automatic speech recognition generates textual transcripts; the VT variant jointly uses frames and transcripts. The framework requires triples of queries, relevant videos, and ground-truth answers for evaluation, and datasets are constructed by associating WikiHowQA with HowTo100M, including an automatically collected synthetic dataset. VideoRAG is evaluated on question-answering tasks and reported RAG benchmarks. Its variants outperform conventional textual, text-image, and text-video RAG baselines, including TEXTVIDEORAG, and results indicate gains from stronger video retrieval, adaptive frame selection, and larger LVLMs. The work presents itself as among the first to operationalize a full RAG pipeline over a video corpus. It also identifies future opportunities to improve effectiveness and efficiency. Its use may carry risks from problematic corpus examples and biases in LVLMs.

VideoRAG in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

VideoRAG dynamically retrieves videos based on their relevance to queries and uses both visual and textual information for response generation. It includes a video frame selection mechanism and a strategy for extracting textual information from videos without subtitles. Experiments show that VideoRAG is superior to relevant baselines.

Introduction

VideoRAG retrieves relevant videos from a large video corpus and integrates visual and textual elements into answer generation. It uses a frame selection model and automatically generated transcripts to make video retrieval and processing more effective.

Method .. 2.4 / Auxiliary Text Generation

VideoRAG extends retrieval-augmented generation by retrieving relevant videos from a video corpus and using them to generate grounded responses. It combines video retrieval, frame selection, and video-augmented response generation.

opening .. Case Study

VideoRAG is evaluated as a video-based retrieval-augmented generation approach for question answering. Its variants outperform conventional textual, text-image, and text-video RAG baselines, and its performance improves with better video retrieval, adaptive frame selection, and larger LVLMs.

4 / Retrieval-Augmented Generation .. Large Video Language Models

VideoRAG is proposed to dynamically retrieve and holistically use video content in RAG. It is powered by Large Video Language Models and is intended to address the limitations of frame-based and text-conversion approaches.

5

VideoRAG is presented as a framework that expands retrieval-augmented generation by using a video corpus as an external knowledge source. It retrieves videos based on query relevance, integrates their visual and textual elements, and uses adaptive frame selection to retain the most informative frames. Experimental results validate its superiority over existing RAG methods.

Limitations

VideoRAG operationalizes a full pipeline of RAG over a video corpus, including dynamic retrieval of query-relevant videos and answer generation grounded in those videos. Its frame selection strategy improves video-processing efficiency and effectiveness.

Ethics Statement

VideoRAG is designed to offer answers to user queries by retrieving query-relevant videos from a large video corpus. Its use may involve risks arising from problematic corpus examples and biases in LVLMs.
