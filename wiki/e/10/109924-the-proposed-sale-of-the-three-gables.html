THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

A supposed house agent offers Mrs. Maberley five hundred pounds more than she paid for The Three Gables and agrees to buy the furniture for an additional large sum. The buyer insists on acquiring everything and on checking anything removed from the house, causing Mrs. Maberley to reject the agreement.
