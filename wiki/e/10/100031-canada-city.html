Canada City

Abstract written by a model

Canada City is a new, glaringly unpicturesque mining town among mountains, canyons, and pines, with fierce, unregenerate youth. Main Street is its principal thoroughfare; it is home to miners and townsmen, served by Canada City Bank, and contains the new church where Masterton first saw Cissy Trixit. Cissy and Piney become a civic spectacle on Main Street. The town is closely bound to Montagu Trixit and his bank: a branch used withdrawn securities, and even enemies say Canada City was the bank and the bank was Trixit. After the bank's failure, crowds gather at the bank and newspaper office, news changes the town's mood, and townspeople reject Cissy over her father's alleged crime. Cissy leaves to find him and says she will never return.

Canada City is a new, glaringly unpicturesque mining town set among mountains, canyons, and pines. Its residents treat Cissy and Piney as a civic spectacle when they walk down Main Street. By Cissy's return, groups of townsmen are preoccupied, crowds surround the bank and newspaper office, and an extra carries news that visibly alters the town's mood.
