Parkhurst in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Parkhurst, a florid Texan and one of Eureka Mining Company’s five partners, is a woman-hating miner who believes gold lies near water. At the spring he discovers the gold find and gold-bearing quartz, explains his theory to Bray, and misreads Eugenia’s note to Jack and its reference to Jill as whiskey.

Parkhurst attributes Neworth's failure to visit the claim to the women accompanying him and later discovers gold-bearing quartz near the spring. He finds Eugenia's note addressed to "Jack," interprets "Jill" as a measure of whiskey, and gives the note and a laurel sprig to Bray after explaining his quartz riddle.
