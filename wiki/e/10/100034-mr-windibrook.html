Mr. Windibrook in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Mr. Windibrook, a reverend gentleman in Canada City, publicly praised Cissy's father, received a requested salary raise from him, and supported a proposed church organ. Visiting Cissy, he urges favorable presentation of her father's donation, offers refuge, then denounces him; Cissy exposes his hypocrisy and expels him.

Mr. Windibrook visits Cissy and urges her to present her father's church donation and liberality favorably if questioned. He offers to have her stay with Mrs. Windibrook, then angrily calls Cissy's father a thief and felon; Cissy denounces him as a hypocrite and drives him away.
