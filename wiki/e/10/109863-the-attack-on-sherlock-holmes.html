the attack on Sherlock Holmes in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The attack on Sherlock Holmes occurs outside the Café Royal on Regent Street, where two attackers with sticks inflict scalp wounds and bruises. Holmes exploits the apparent near-fatal injuries, requiring stitches, to make Baron Adelbert Gruner lower his precautions while he prepares a counterplot.

And it did. Their blow fell--or his blow rather, for never could I

The attack on Sherlock Holmes leaves him with two lacerated scalp wounds, serious bruises, and several stitches. Holmes uses the attack to create the impression that he is near death while preparing a counterplot against Baron Adelbert Gruner.
