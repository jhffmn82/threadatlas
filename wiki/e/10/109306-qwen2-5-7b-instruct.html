Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is one of the two LLMs used to evaluate end-to-end question-answering performance. Answer generation uses greedy sampling with a maximum length of 512.

5 / Main Results / 5.1 / Retrieval Performance .. 5.2 / End-to-end QA Performance

With Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as the generation model, PIC achieves the best QA results, improving the average by 2 points across six datasets and by 5.7 points on EQ. Under the top-5 setting, PIC declines by 8.5 points from retrieval to QA performance, compared with larger declines for Proposition and Semantic chunking.
