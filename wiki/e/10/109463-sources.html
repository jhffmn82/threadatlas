front matter .. Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability / Abstract

Sources in external databases can have heterogeneous reliability. Reliability-Aware RAG cross-checks information across sources, selects the top-κ reliable and relevant sources, and shows improved performance as the number of sources increases.

Introduction

Sources have heterogeneous reliability, and information from different sources may conflict. Reliability-Aware RAG estimates source reliability and selectively combines information from multiple sources.

Problem Formulation / Standard RAG .. Multi-source RAG with source reliability

A source contributes documents to the database and may be defined at different granularities, such as an individual social media account, a specific individual, or a news website. Each source is assigned an estimated reliability value for weighted aggregation.

Method: RA-RAG .. Iterative reliability estimation

Sources provide documents from which responses are generated for user queries and fact-checking queries. Their weights are initialized equally, updated according to agreement with estimated answers, and then used to prioritize responses during aggregation.

Limitations

The reliability estimation method is effective at estimating the reliability of real-world sources, particularly when they concern news-related claims with abundant fact-checking sources.

B

Each source has a reliability score and is processed in descending reliability-score order by κ-RRSS.
