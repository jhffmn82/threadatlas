Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V IN TOWN Night, in that maze of alleys, was but a more sinister day.

Au-yoeng was a cormorant-fisher whose birds died, whose luck failed, and who nearly starved. He stole about a hundred catties of Fuh-kien hemp, endured continued pressure from his neighbors, blamed the merchant and the Jesus religion for his troubles, and drunkenly killed Chok Chung before dying by his own hand.
