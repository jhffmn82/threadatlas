Abstract written by a model

This entity is pseudo-labeled training samples: synthetic domain-relevant question-answer examples containing a generated query, retrieved or source context, and a generated answer. They are generated from unlabeled specialized-domain corpora by SimRAG using a model fine-tuned after Stage-I. Samples span multiple task types and are filtered or denoised through quality checks, including retaining cases whose answers appear in top-k retrieved documents. Used for continuous Stage-II domain-adaptive fine-tuning, they improve domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation tasks. From generation through retrieval-based filtering to training, SimRAG’s samples are more relevant on average than examples from the backbone model or off-the-shelf T5-Large.

Abstract

Synthetic examples are generated by the same large language model from unlabeled corpora and are filtered to retain high-quality instances. SimRAG leverages these examples to improve performance on domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation tasks.

Introduction

Pseudo-labeled QA pairs consist of generated question, passage, and answer tuples grounded in specialized-domain contexts. SimRAG continuously fine-tunes on these pairs, which are diversified through multiple task types and denoised with round-trip consistency filtering.

Methodology / Problem Setup .. Stage-II: Domain Adaptive Fine-tuning

Pseudo-labeled training samples consist of generated queries, retrieved or source documents, and generated answers. Samples are retained when the answer appears in the top-k documents retrieved for the generated query.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

Synthetic question-answer pairs are generated from unlabeled domain-relevant corpora and used for Stage-II training. Pairs generated by the fine-tuned model are more relevant on average than those generated directly by Llama3-8B-it or by an off-the-shelf T5-Large question-generation model.
