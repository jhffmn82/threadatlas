Mountain Clarion in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The Mountain Clarion is a country newspaper, printed on a hand-press under strict economy and edited by Mr. Grey. It reports Colonel Starbottle’s accident and warns against half-broken Spanish mustangs, but withholds both speculation about his secret and the real account of the attacks.
