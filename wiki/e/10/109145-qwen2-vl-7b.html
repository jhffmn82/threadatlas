Qwen2-VL-7B in VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution

Xueguang Ma, Shengyao Zhuang, Bevan Koopman, Guido Zuccon, Wenhu Chen, Jimmy Lin · 2025

Qwen2-VL-7B is a model evaluated on Wiki-VISA and Paper-VISA. Fine-tuned, it reaches 54.2% bounding-box and 65.2% answer accuracy on Wiki-VISA, and 68.2% and 43.8% on Paper-VISA, in single-candidate setups. It handles long-context inputs better than the smaller model; with three candidate documents, it reaches 32.3% bounding-box, 41.8% answer accuracy, and indicates 82.2% of No Answer cases.
