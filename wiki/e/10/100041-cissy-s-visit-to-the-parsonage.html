Cissy makes a social call at Reverend Mr. Windibrook's Pastorage. Mr. Windibrook boasts of Montagu Trixit's financial success, while Mrs. Windibrook gossips about local people and Canada City's rough society. Cissy partly enjoys herself, plays with the younger Windibrooks, climbs a tree, has tea, and leaves.

" A BELLE OF CANADA CITY Cissy was tying her hat under her round chin before a small glass at her window.

Cissy visits the Reverend Mr. Windibrook and his family at the dwelling known as the Pastorage. Mr. Windibrook boasts about Montagu Trixit's financial success, while Mrs. Windibrook discusses the Secamp girls, young Masterton, and the supposed dangers of Canada City's rough society. Cissy enjoys the visit somewhat, plays with the younger Windibrooks, climbs a tree, and leaves after tea.
