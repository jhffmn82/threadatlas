Port Said in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Port Said is a crowded, noisy meeting place at the Suez Canal, containing Bar la Poste and filled with ships, travelers, soldiers, laborers, and many nationalities. It unfolds as Rudolph moves through its streets, visits a German shipping-office, and boards a German mail-boat.
