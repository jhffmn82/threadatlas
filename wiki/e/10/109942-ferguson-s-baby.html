Ferguson's baby in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Ferguson's baby is a dear, beautiful child under one year old, the child of Robert Ferguson and Ferguson's wife. In a foggy November evening, Ferguson fondles the dark-eyed, golden-haired baby protectively. The child is targeted in an attempted poisoning and suffers a small puncture on the throat from a poisoned arrow, but survives after the poison is sucked out. Mrs. Mason guards the baby day and night.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

The baby is the Peruvian wife's child and is under one year old. A nurse and the unnamed husband each reportedly saw the wife beside the baby with blood on or near its neck, and Mrs. Mason now guards the child continuously.

It was evening of a dull, foggy November day when, having left our bags

Ferguson's baby is a beautiful, dark-eyed, golden-haired child whom Ferguson fondles protectively. The baby has a small puckered wound on the neck, caused by the poisoned arrow, but survives because the poison was sucked out.
