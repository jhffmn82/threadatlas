Soft ad attack is a novel attack strategy and attack task, classified as a special type of implicit toxicity and malicious soft ad. It presents promotional content as seemingly professional and harmless text, designed to blend naturally into an attack-targeted or original golden context and potentially linked to authoritative entities. It uses manually generated attack keywords and proceeds through either direct insertion, embedding advertising text without significantly changing context, or indirect insertion, subtly modifying context to incorporate the promotion. The resulting attack context can be combined with two other golden contexts to construct DSA. As a SafeRAG attack-task category, it can evade LLM detection and be inserted into a generator’s response, exposing gaps in existing defenses.
