the marriage of Mr. Bilson and Frida in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Mr. Bilson and Frida Jansen plan to marry at once in San Francisco after their relationship becomes known at Buckeye Hill. Mr. Bilson tells Miss Trotter that they will then travel abroad for a year or two. The news disappoints Chris but causes little scandal. During Mr. Bilson’s absence, Miss Trotter temporarily controls the Summit Hotel; the couple later return after her resignation.

Mr. Bilson informs Miss Trotter that he and Frida will marry at once in San Francisco and then travel abroad for a year or two. Their marriage is presented as the outcome of Frida's relationship with Mr. Bilson and the cause of Chris's disappointment.

III When the news of the marriage reached Buckeye Hill, it did not, however, make much scandal, owing, possibly, to the scant number of the sex who are apt to disseminate it, and to many the name of Miss Jansen was unknown.

The marriage of Mr. Bilson and Frida produces little scandal at Buckeye Hill. Mr. Bilson’s absence leads to Miss Trotter’s temporary control of the Summit Hotel, and the couple later return after her resignation.
