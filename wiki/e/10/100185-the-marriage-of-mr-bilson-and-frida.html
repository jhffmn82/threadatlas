Abstract written by a model

Mr. Bilson and Frida Jansen plan to marry at once in San Francisco after their relationship becomes known at Buckeye Hill. Mr. Bilson tells Miss Trotter that they will then travel abroad for a year or two. The news disappoints Chris but causes little scandal. During Mr. Bilson’s absence, Miss Trotter temporarily controls the Summit Hotel; the couple later return after her resignation.
