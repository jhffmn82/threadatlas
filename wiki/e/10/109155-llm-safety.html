LLM safety

topic · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: safety, safety behavior, safety behavior of language models, safety behavior of models, safety concerns, safety profile, safety profiles, unsafe behaviors
  • In “VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” LLM safety concerns potential risks and mitigation strategies and directs users to relevant 2024 work by Kumar et al., Cui et al., and Luu et al.
  • In “SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” LLM safety is addressed by proposing SafetyRAG, which uses retrieved safety facts to improve safe behavior and refusal of unsafe requests.
  • In “RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models,” safety concerns expose service providers to legal risks and undermine public trust, prompting safety fine-tuning, guardrails, and red teaming.
  • In “RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models,” the safety behavior of language models does not necessarily generalize from non-RAG to RAG settings, as models may prioritize helpfulness and document synthesis over safety when relevant context is supplied.
  • In “RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models,” safety profiles are assessed for eleven popular large language models on more than 5,000 harmful questions, finding that RAG introduces unsafe behaviors across nearly every safety category.
