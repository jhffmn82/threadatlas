Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Tarbox is an old woman who lives in a cabin with and is married to Hiram Tarbox. As Hiram’s wife or close household companion, she helps prepare coffee, complains that the stage company’s suspicion and persecution have made life difficult, and affectionately rebukes Flo for causing bother and discredit.
