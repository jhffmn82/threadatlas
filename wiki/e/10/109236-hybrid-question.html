hybrid question in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

Hybrid questions are questions in semi-structured knowledge bases that require both textual and relational information. This combined requirement complicates retrieval and motivates a hybrid retrieval method. The description opens with hybrid question answering and an example involving nanofluid heat transfer papers published by John Smith, then reiterates the textual-relational definition.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Hybrid questions require both textual and relational information from a semi-structured knowledge base. Their requirements complicate retrieval and motivate the need for a hybrid retrieval method.

opening

Hybrid question answering requires both relational and textual information to answer questions correctly. The unit describes it in the context of semi-structured knowledge bases and gives the example of identifying nanofluid heat transfer papers published by John Smith.
