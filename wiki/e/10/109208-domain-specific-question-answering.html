Abstract

Domain-specific RAG tasks are the target of SimRAG's adaptation process. Experiments across 11 datasets, two backbone sizes, and three domains show that SimRAG improves performance over baselines by 1.2%–8.6%.

Introduction

Domain-specific QA is the target application of SimRAG. The method uses domain corpora to generate grounded question-answer examples and adapts models to this task through continued fine-tuning.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

The experiments cover domain-specific question answering across 11 datasets in the medical, scientific, and computer science domains. SimRAG improves performance on diverse formats including multiple-choice, assertion, fill-in-the-blank, open-ended generation, short-span question answering, and claim verification.
