A

The misalignment filtering threshold is denoted by τ and is set to 0.1 in all experiments. This value follows Lei et al. (2023), which identifies it as optimal for a real-world dataset comprising CNN and Daily Mail articles; an ablation study on τ is conducted in Section L.
