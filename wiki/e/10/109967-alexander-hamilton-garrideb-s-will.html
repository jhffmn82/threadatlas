THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GARRIDEBS

Alexander Hamilton Garrideb's will divides his property into three parts and makes John Garrideb's share conditional on finding two other Garrideb men to share the remainder. The will requires three adult men named Garrideb to stand together before the inheritance can be claimed.
