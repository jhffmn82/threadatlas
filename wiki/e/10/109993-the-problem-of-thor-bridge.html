Abstract written by a model

The Problem of Thor Bridge concerns Sherlock Holmes’s investigation of Mrs. Gibson’s apparent murder. Suspicion falls on Neil Gibson and Miss Dunbar, while the missing weapon, Dunbar’s connection to the scene, and concealed relationships complicate the case. Holmes finds inconsistencies suggesting planted evidence, uses a chip in the bridge’s fresh stonework to trace disposal of the weapon, identifies Mrs. Gibson’s motive, and resolves the attempt to make Miss Dunbar appear guilty.
