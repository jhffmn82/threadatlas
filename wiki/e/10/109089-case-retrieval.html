Abstract written by a model

Case retrieval is a CLERC task that helps legal professionals support legal analysis by finding relevant case documents or cited passages. It has four query categories combining two query kinds and two data views, and treats the passage cited by a query’s central citation as relevant. Given a paragraph with a central citation sentence and context, systems retrieve cited passages or documents. Evaluation uses Recall@10, Recall@100, Recall@1000, and nDCG@10; reported models often struggle with relevance, though fine-tuned LegalBERT DPR exceeds BM25 at Recall@1K.

Introduction

Case retrieval is formulated in CLERC as a task intended to help legal professionals find relevant cases. The evaluation includes retrieval models for documents and passages, and reports that current information retrieval models often struggle with relevance.

Related Work .. 2.3

Citation retrieval is presented as a core component of the benchmark. The benchmark evaluates the retrieval of citations in long-form legal analysis.

3 / 3.1 .. Input Preparation

Case retrieval is modeled as the task of finding case documents that support a legal analysis. The relevant passage for a query is defined as the passage cited by the query's central citation.

4 .. 4.2.2

Legal citation retrieval is formulated as retrieving relevant cited passages or documents from a collection, given a query paragraph containing a central citation sentence and surrounding context. BM25 achieves 48.3% Recall@1K, while fine-tuned LegalBERT DPR achieves 68.5%.
