Seriphus

place · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the island of Seriphus, Σέριφον, Σερίφον, Σερίφου

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Seriphus is where the chest came ashore, Polydectes ruled, and Dictys raised Perseus. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Seriphus is the island where Dicte finds Danae and Perseus after their chest drifts at sea; Perseus grows up there and later returns to confront Polydectes.
