SKR

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: SKR filter, compressor SKR

Abstract written by a model

SKR is a filtering method and compression-based filter evaluated in retrieval and generation safety comparisons. It compresses information, which can lose details and reduce F1 (avg). It is less secure for conflict attacks because conflict details may be removed, but is safe for toxicity and DoS attacks by compressing soft ads and warnings.

SKR is in one work on this site, SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

SKR was less secure for conflict attacks because compression could remove conflict details and lower F1 (correct). It was considered safe for toxicity and DoS attacks because it could compress soft ads and warnings, although compression-based filtering could lose detailed information and decrease F1 (avg).
