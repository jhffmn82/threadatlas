" A BELLE OF CANADA CITY Cissy was tying her hat under her round chin before a small glass at her window.

Cissy and Piney's walk through Canada City resembles a civic procession because residents stop to watch and honor them. Men remove their hats and obstructions, miners and workers pause, riders perform displays, and Vaquero Billy rears his mustang before them. Cissy enjoys the attention and assumes a slightly haughty air, while Piney shares in the reflected glory.
