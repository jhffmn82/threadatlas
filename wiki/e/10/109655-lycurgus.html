Lycurgus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Lycurgus, son of Dryas, is king and ruler of the Edonians beside the Strymon. He has spouses named Cleophyle, Eurydice, or Eurynome in differing accounts and several children. After insulting and expelling Dionysus, he is driven mad, kills a son, is bound on Mount Pangaeum, and destroyed by horses.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V. Διόνυσος δὲ εὑρετὴς ἀμπέλου γενόμενος,

Lycurgus, son of Dryas and king of the Edonians beside the Strymon, insulted and expelled Dionysus. Dionysus drove him mad, and after Lycurgus killed his son while mistaking him for a vine branch, Lycurgus was bound on Mount Pangaeum and killed by horses at Dionysus's will.
