HowTo100M in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Soyeong Jeong · 2025

HowTo100M is a dataset and instruction video corpus sourced from YouTube. It supplies unanswered query-video pairs, associated with WikiHowQA through search results, for evaluating VideoRAG and constructing RAG-over-videos datasets. It also supports automatically generated synthetic query-answer pairs for evaluation; its stated limitation reiterates the WikiHowQA-linked query-video setup.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

HowTo100M provides query-video pairs without answers for the evaluation of VideoRAG. The datasets are used to automatically collect a dataset for RAG over videos.

opening .. Case Study

HowTo100M supplies instruction videos from YouTube and is associated with WikiHow queries through search results. It is also used to generate synthetic query-answer pairs for evaluation.

Limitations

HowTo100M provides pairs of queries and videos that are associated with WikiHowQA to construct a dataset for evaluating RAG over videos.
