GraphRAG-Global summarizes graph communities for broader context understanding, achieving higher recall than GraphRAG-Local but lower precision and faithfulness; peripheral information can enter its summaries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Experiment / Experimental Setup .. Parameter Study

GraphRAG-Global summarizes graph communities to support broader context understanding. It obtains higher context recall than GraphRAG-Local but has lower context precision and answer faithfulness, and its community summaries can introduce peripheral information.
