Abstract written by a model

The wax dummy of Sherlock Holmes is a lifelike effigy made by the French modeller Tavernier, dressed like Holmes and seated by the Baker Street window. Used by Holmes as a decoy behind a curtain, it convinces watchers and Sam Merton that Holmes is present. Count Negretto Sylvius approaches to strike it, whereupon the real Holmes reveals himself, springs from the chair, and seizes the stone.

The wax dummy of Sherlock Holmes is placed in the Baker Street window behind a curtain to make watchers believe Holmes is sitting there. Count Negretto Sylvius approaches it intending to strike Holmes, allowing the real Holmes to reveal himself and confront him.

The dummy is an effigy seated in the window and resembles Holmes so closely that Sam Merton mistakes it for a living figure. Holmes has occupied it secretly and springs from its chair to seize the stone.
