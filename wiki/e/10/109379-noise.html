Noise

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: noise, noise (RAG attack task), noise attack

Abstract written by a model

Noise is an attack task that exploits retrieval limitations by injecting extensive distracting texts to dilute useful knowledge. It uses relevant or irrelevant noise, including superficially relevant contexts that lack answers, semantically varying irrelevant contexts, and silver noise containing partial but incomplete answers. Such noise can evade simple safety filters. It is covered by SafeRAG and identified there as one of four critical attack tasks.

Noise is in one work on this site, SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Introduction

A noise attack exploits retrieval limitations by injecting extensive noisy texts that dilute useful knowledge. Existing noise often consists of superficially relevant contexts that are either irrelevant or do not contain answers, making it vulnerable to simple safety filters.

Related Work / RAG Security Evaluation Dataset .. RAG Security Evaluation Metric

Noise attacks introduce relevant or irrelevant distracting contexts. RGB separates golden contexts from relevant noise, RAG Bench adds irrelevant noise, and LRII categorizes irrelevant noise as semantically unrelated, partially related, or related to questions.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the / Meta Data Collection and Pre-processing .. Generation of White Denial-of-Service

It is one of the attack categories covered by the SafeRAG dataset. Silver noise is used to represent partial but incomplete answers.

Conclusion

Noise is identified as one of the four critical attack tasks evaluated by SafeRAG.
