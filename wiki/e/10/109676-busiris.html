Also called: Βούσιρις

Abstract written by a model

Busiris, son of Poseidon and Lysianassa, institutes annual sacrifices of strangers to Zeus on an oracle’s advice during Egypt’s dearth, but Hercules kills him after being captured.

Busiris is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE LIBRARY, 11. v. 11

Busiris sacrifices strangers to Zeus because an oracle says this will end Egypt's nine-year dearth. He captures Hercules for sacrifice, but Hercules breaks his bonds and kills Busiris and his son Amphidamas.
