Manan Suri, Puneet Mathur, Franck Dernoncourt, Kanika Gowswami, Ryan A. Rossi, and Dinesh Manocha · 2025

Multi-document question answering is a document QA task in which systems must localize relevant context and identify the document containing an answer across large collections of source documents. It is scarce in datasets compared with single-document QA. Existing benchmarks are largely text-centric; VisDoMBench expands the task to text, tables, charts, and slides, while several other benchmarks remain single-document.

VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Multi-document question answering is the task addressed by the work introduced in this unit.

Introduction

Multi-document QA requires systems to identify the document containing a relevant answer within a large collection of sources. Existing benchmarks are described as scarce and predominantly textual, often overlooking tables, charts, slides, and other visual content.

Related Work

Multi-document question answering is supported by LongBench and Loong, although these benchmarks remain text-centric. VisDoMBench extends multi-document question answering across text, tables, charts, and slides, while MPDocVQA, UDA, and MMLONGBENCH-DOC are limited to single-document question answering.
