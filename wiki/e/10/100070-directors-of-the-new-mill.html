Abstract written by a model

The directors of the New Mill react excitedly to the failure of Montagu Trixit’s bank. They summon Dick Masterton, choose him to find Trixit, seek an interview, and recover securities held for the mill, while wanting a public search to satisfy stockholders. Masterton learns they used methods associated with Cissy’s disgraced father. The directors later receive terms for returning the securities and must send renewed notes to Sacramento within three days.

The excitement over the failure of Montagu Trixit's bank did not burn itself out

The directors of the New Mill held an excited meeting and summoned Dick Masterton. They selected him to locate Trixit, seek an interview if possible, and recover the securities Trixit held for the mill.

A few hours later he was on the coach.

Mr. Masterton learns that his own directors used the methods associated with Cissy's disgraced father. The directors are also described as wanting a public search for Montagu Trixit to satisfy the stockholders.

How long he lay there thus he never knew.

The directors ordered Masterton to pursue a business interview with Montagu Trixit. They are told to send renewed notes to Sacramento if they want the securities returned.
