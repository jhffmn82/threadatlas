General Retrieval-related Data

Abstract written by a model

General Retrieval-related Data is retrieval-related data used to improve answer and query generation, developing answer-generation from grounding documents and query-generation from answers and documents for high-quality pseudo-labeled samples.

General Retrieval-related Data is in one work on this site, SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Methodology / Problem Setup .. Stage-II: Domain Adaptive Fine-tuning

General Retrieval-related Data develops answer-generation and query-generation skills needed to create high-quality pseudo-labeled samples. Its answer-generation component uses grounding documents to produce candidate answer spans, while its query-generation component uses answers and documents to produce queries.
