computational overhead

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Computational Overhead, Computational overhead, computation, engineering overhead, overhead

Abstract written by a model

In "A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models," computational overhead is described as increasing with retrieved-text length and the processing of graph elements, with prompt processing time scaling quadratically. In "Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," computational overhead is described as a low-latency limitation that grows with subquery generation and candidate reranking and can be reduced with smaller instruction models and lightweight rerankers. In "Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability," computational overhead is reported to be significantly reduced by κ-RRSS while model performance is preserved.

computational overhead in Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Paul J. L. Ammann · 2025-07-28 to 2025-07-29 · the work’s page · read it where computational overhead first appears

Abstract written by a model

Computational overhead is a low-latency limitation for real-time deployments. It grows when M subqueries are generated and M × k candidate passages reranked, increasing latency and GPU requirements. It may be reduced through smaller instruction models for question decomposition and lightweight rerankers.

Question decomposition and reranking both increase computational overhead, which may be prohibitive in low-latency or real-time deployments. Proposed efficiency-oriented solutions include smaller instruction models for question decomposition and lightweight rerankers.

Limitations
