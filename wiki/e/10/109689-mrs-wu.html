Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Wu, formerly the Pretty Lily, is a capable Chinese boatwoman who owns and captains a sampan with her husband. She ferries Heywood and Rudolph, bargains and jokes with them, and is Rudolph’s friend. By the end, she takes control of a hostile craft’s steering and commands it.

Mrs. Wu is in one work on this site, Dragon's Blood. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Mrs. Wu, formerly known as the Pretty Lily, ferries Heywood and Rudolph in her sampan. She is described as a capable, humorous woman who captains the boat, bargains with the passengers, and argues and jokes with Heywood while her husband works the sweep.
