the stage road in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The stage road is a coach route near a cabin and a former coach-robbery site. During the coach journey, a fallen tree blocks it; Yuba Bill and helpers clear the obstruction, and a stranger warns that the coach might otherwise have gone down a canyon. Beyond the place where the tree was thrown down, the road runs on a mountain shelf above the North Fork canyon. A thorn- and brush-hidden trail parallels part of it, and the road eventually crosses the canyon on a high bridge. Flora's hidden route reaches the road, from which a dead traveler apparently fell while escaping.
