Eugenia Neworth in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Eugenia Neworth is a tall, handsome, plucky girl and lady with gray eyes, living with the Neworths in San Francisco. She is the daughter of Eugenia's father and Neworth's daughter, sister of Harry Neworth, and niece of Mrs. Dobbs. While traveling by the Pioneer Coach with her companions, she leaves the coach to walk uphill beside the stage road, slips, and falls into a ravine near a spring. Edmund Bray rescues her and helps her back, though her family dismisses him and she thanks him formally. Later, she writes a note to Parkhurst, signing it “Jill,” praising his honest, manly calling and asking him to remember her if he becomes rich. She also writes to Bray, arranges delivery through a schoolboy, explains her expectations, and invites him to call properly. After resolving their misunderstanding, Eugenia and Bray become romantic partners in a new partnership.
