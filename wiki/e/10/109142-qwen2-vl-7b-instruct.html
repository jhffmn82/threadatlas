In VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution, Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct is used as an open-source initialization for fine-tuning vision-language models with VISA. In VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct is included as an open-source LLM with visual and long-context capabilities, with average scores of 12.09 with Long Context, 31.65 with Text RAG, 39.06 with Visual RAG, and 39.94 with VisDoMRAG.
