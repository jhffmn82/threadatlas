the prophecy about Acrisius

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: prophecy concerning Acrisius, the ancient prophecy, the oracle how he should get male children, the story of his birth and parentage
  • In The Library, it foretells that Danae will bear Perseus, who will kill Acrisius, prompting him to confine Danae.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it states that Danae's child will kill Acrisius and is fulfilled when Perseus accidentally strikes Acrisius with a discus at Larissa.

the prophecy about Acrisius in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

A prophecy concerning Acrisius foretells that Danae will bear Perseus, who will kill him; fearing it, Acrisius confines Danae.
