the prophecy about Acrisius

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: prophecy concerning Acrisius, the ancient prophecy, the oracle how he should get male children, the story of his birth and parentage

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, it foretells that Danae will bear Perseus, who will kill Acrisius, prompting him to confine Danae. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it states that Danae's child will kill Acrisius and is fulfilled when Perseus accidentally strikes Acrisius with a discus at Larissa.
