the Europeans in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The Europeans are foreign, white expatriate residents who attend the club and ride through the Chinese countryside after passing the inland wall. In “Passage at Arms,” they watch Heywood and Doctor Chantel fence, are attacked by neighboring hamlet men, pursue the rioters, and return exhausted after driving them away. Later, Rudolph and Wutzler hide among oil-jars, cross a battle zone, and return to the defended compound. During the siege, the defenders hold the northeast corner and water gate through attack, repair, and guard duty.

The Europeans watch Heywood and Doctor Chantel fence, then come under attack from the neighboring hamlet men. They pursue the rioters and return exhausted after driving them away.

CHAPTER XVII LAMP OF HEAVEN The white men peered from among the oil-jars, like two of the Forty Thieves.

The white men, Rudolph and Wutzler, hide among the oil-jars while observing a motionless figure near the hollow. They then attempt to cross the battle zone and return to the defended compound.

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The defenders hold the northeast corner and the water gate through a night attack and continued intermittent firing. They sleep by turns, build screens against arrows, and continue repairing and guarding the defenses during the siege.
