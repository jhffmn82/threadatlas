Abstract written by a model

Uncle Hiram's cabin is an unpainted four-roomed redwood building beside the road, inhabited by Hiram and Sophy. It has a fire but little comfort or light. After a coach stops, Hiram, Sophy, and Flo shelter visiting passengers with coffee and whiskey while the road is cleared.
