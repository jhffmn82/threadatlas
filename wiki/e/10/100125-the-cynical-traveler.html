the cynical traveler in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The cynical traveler is a thief and the robber of a coach. Armed and masked, he confronts the coach, escapes with greenbacks, and wears the stolen money in a treasure belt beneath his torn shirt. After his accomplice is caught and confesses, he is trapped on the stage road between Tarbox's cabin and the bridge while Henry's men watch the road and trail. He is ultimately identified as the dead traveler after a fatal fall from the road, still carrying the money.
