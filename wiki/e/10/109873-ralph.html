Abstract written by a model

Ralph is an elderly butler serving at Tuxbury Old Park and the Emsworth mansion. Married and foster-father to Godfrey Emsworth, he remains among the household’s few servants, carrying food to the detached house. His disinfectant-impregnated brown gloves draw Holmes’s attention when Ralph admits Holmes and Dodd.
