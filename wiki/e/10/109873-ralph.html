Ralph in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Ralph is an elderly butler serving at Tuxbury Old Park and the Emsworth mansion. Married and foster-father to Godfrey Emsworth, he remains among the household’s few servants, carrying food to the detached house. His disinfectant-impregnated brown gloves draw Holmes’s attention when Ralph admits Holmes and Dodd.

Ralph, the elderly butler, is one of the few servants in the Emsworth household and carries food toward the detached house. He wears brown leather gloves, which he removes on admitting Holmes and Dodd. Holmes detects a tarry disinfectant odour coming from the gloves.
