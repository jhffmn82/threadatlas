Benchmark of Multi-source RAG in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

Benchmark of Multi-source RAG is a question-answering benchmark for multi-source retrieval-augmented generation under heterogeneous source reliability. Constructed from Natural Questions, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA, it combines diverse factual and misleading documents, assigns sources reliability and coverage parameters, and uses closed-ended fact-checking queries. It includes 1,600 queries per dataset: 200 for reliability estimation and 1,400 for testing. Examples compare Multi-Source Outputs, majority-vote answers, and RA-RAG against ground truth, with RA-RAG often correct when majority vote is not.

Experiments / Experimental setups .. Ablation studies and analysis

The multi-source RAG benchmark is constructed from Natural Questions, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA with diverse factual documents and misinformation. It assigns each source reliability and coverage parameters and uses 1,600 queries per dataset, including 200 for reliability estimation and 1,400 for testing.

G

It evaluates multi-source retrieval-augmented generation with heterogeneous source reliability using factual and misleading documents generated from three question-answering datasets.

H / H.1 .. H.2

The Multi-Source Benchmark includes examples comparing Multi-Source Outputs, majority-vote answers, and RA-RAG answers against ground-truth answers. The examples show RA-RAG producing the ground-truth answer in several cases where the majority-vote answer is incorrect.
