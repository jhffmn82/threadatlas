Abstract written by a model

Thor Bridge is a broad, balustraded stone bridge carrying the drive across the narrowest part of Thor Mere, a long reedy pool in the grounds of Thor Place. It matters as the site where Mrs. Gibson meets Miss Dunbar, where Mrs. Gibson's body is found at the bridge's mouth, and where Miss Dunbar is seen near the time of the shooting. A fresh, forceful chip in its parapet leads Holmes to demonstrate a string-dragged, stone-weighted pistol striking the bridge and falling into the water.

Thor Bridge is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

Thor Bridge is a broad stone bridge with balustrades carrying the drive over the narrowest part of Thor Mere. Maria Gibson's body was found at the mouth of the bridge, and Miss Dunbar was seen near it around the time of the shooting.
