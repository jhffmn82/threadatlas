Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

The unnamed husband married the Peruvian wife after knowing her for only a few weeks and has a fifteen-year-old son from an earlier marriage. After seeing blood on the baby's neck and around his wife's lips, he confronted her, confined her to her room, and became so distressed that he sought Ferguson's help.
