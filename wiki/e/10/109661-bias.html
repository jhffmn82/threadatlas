Bias in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Bias, son of Amythaon and brother of Melampus, sought Pero; after Melampus obtained Phylacus’s kine, Bias married Pero and became father of Talaus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Now Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus and Alcidice,

Bias, son of Amythaon, wooed Pero, daughter of Neleus. Because Neleus required a suitor to bring him the kine of Phylacus, Bias asked his brother Melampus for help. After Melampus obtained the kine, Bias received Pero and later had a son, Talaus, with her.
