Asteria in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Asteria, daughter of Coeus, escapes Zeus’s amorous advances as a quail by throwing herself into the sea; the island once called Asteria is later called Delos.

IV. Τῶν δὲ Κοίου θυγατέρων ᾿Αστερία μὲν

Asteria took the form of a quail and threw herself into the sea to escape Zeus's amorous advances. The island formerly called Asteria was later called Delos.
