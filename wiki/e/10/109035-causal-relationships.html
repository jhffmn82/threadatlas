Abstract

Causal relationships are constructed and traced by CausalRAG. They help preserve contextual continuity and improve retrieval precision.

opening

Causal relationships within external knowledge are explicitly identified by CausalRAG. Their identification helps preserve contextual coherence and supports retrieval of causally grounded documents.

Methodology .. Retrieving Context Causally

Causal relationships are identified and estimated within the retrieved nodes and edges. They are used to prioritize relevant information and filter out semantically related but causally irrelevant content.
