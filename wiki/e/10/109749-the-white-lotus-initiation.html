the White Lotus initiation

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: set the day, swear in recruits, the White Lotus initiation ritual, the draught of blood

Abstract written by a model

The White Lotus initiation is a nighttime ceremony for recruits. It begins with invocations and displays of sacred or symbolic objects, then recruits swear oaths beneath crossed swords, enforced by threats of death. A white cock is sacrificed, blood is mixed and drunk, recruits enter the army as brothers, and the day of planned violence is set.
