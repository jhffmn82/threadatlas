drawing of lots for the cities

Also called: cast lots for the cities, the drawing of the lots, the lot

Abstract written by a model

After conquering the Peloponnese, the leaders sacrifice and draw lots: Temenus receives Argos first, the sons of Aristodemus Lacedaemon second, and Cresphontes obtains Messene third through a dissolving clod.

THE LIBRARY, II. vir. 2

After conquering the Peloponnese, the leaders sacrifice at three altars and draw lots for the cities. Temenus and the sons of Aristodemus throw stones into a water-filled pitcher, while Cresphontes throws in a clod that dissolves.
