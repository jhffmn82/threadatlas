Abstract written by a model

Retrieval units are smaller pieces created when long documents are split before storage in a knowledge base. They may have sentence, paragraph, or document granularity and be created by fixed size, punctuation, phrase structure, or semantic relationships; relevant units are retrieved by user’s instructions.
