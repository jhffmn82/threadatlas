Barney Stockdale's gang in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Barney Stockdale's gang is hired by Isadora Klein to recover Douglas Maberley’s manuscript, burglarizes The Three Gables, chloroforms Mrs. Maberley, searches unsuccessfully, then flees when she and the maid raise the alarm and police pursue.
