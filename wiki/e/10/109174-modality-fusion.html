Modality Fusion in VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Manan Suri, Puneet Mathur, Franck Dernoncourt, Kanika Gowswami, Ryan A. Rossi, and Dinesh Manocha · 2025

Modality Fusion is a key contribution of VisDoMRAG that integrates curated evidence, reasoning chains, and generated answers from textual and visual pipelines. It combines and evaluates their reasoning chains under a consistency constraint, uses LLM-based ensembling to identify discrepancies and gaps, reconciles differences by re-evaluating evidence or adjusting steps, and generates a coherent final answer.

Introduction

Modality fusion integrates the outputs of VisDoMRAG's textual and visual pipelines. It imposes a consistency constraint on their reasoning chains and uses LLM-based ensembling to produce the final answer.
