This is a structured descriptive dataset about the 114 surahs of the Qur’an, derived from a book about those surahs rather than from the Qur’an text itself. It records each surah’s number of verses, name meaning and reason, general objective, provenance or reason for revelation when available, historical context, virtues, and relationships between surahs and their parts. It therefore supplies contextual and interpretative details beyond the actual text. A preliminary study selected 20 surahs. The material was digitized, structured, validated, vectorized into text embeddings with NLP models, and stored in a vector database for efficient retrieval. Its central role is to provide curated external domain-specific knowledge for a Retrieval-Augmented Generation architecture: models retrieve pertinent information before responding, supporting descriptive Quranic-studies tasks, evaluation of responses, contextual correctness, and credible, relevant answers.

Abstract—Accurate and contextually faithful responses are

The descriptive dataset of Quranic surahs contains information on the meanings, historical context, and qualities of all 114 surahs. It is used with Retrieval-Augmented Generation so models can retrieve relevant knowledge before responding.

I.

The dataset is derived from a book about the 114 surahs of the Qur’an rather than from the Qur’an text itself. It provides information about each surah, including its meaning, provenance of revelation, and historical context, and is used to support descriptive and relevant answers about Quranic studies.

II.

The Qur’anic surah descriptions dataset comes from a descriptive book studying the 114 surahs of the Qur’an, with 20 surahs selected for this preliminary study. It contains fields such as verse count, name meaning, reason for the name, general objective, revelation context, virtues, and relationships, and it was digitized, structured, validated, vectorized, and stored in a vector database.

III.

The descriptive dataset of Quranic surahs provides the external knowledge used by the RAG system to evaluate model responses.

IV.

The descriptive Qur’anic dataset provides external domain-specific knowledge for the Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework. Its use helps models retrieve pertinent information, ground responses in credible data, and improve contextual correctness.
