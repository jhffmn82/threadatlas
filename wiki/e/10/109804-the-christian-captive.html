the Christian captive in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A haggard, imprisoned Christian captive is seized in Rudolph's go-down, threatened with death and compelled to recant or die. Maintaining his faith, he chooses martyrdom, refuses demanded words, and is tortured with burning flour and oil until rifle fire interrupts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII LAMP OF HEAVEN The white men peered from among the oil-jars, like two of the Forty Thieves.

The Christian captive is seized in Rudolph's go-down and forced to choose between recanting and dying. He refuses to speak as demanded, answers that he will die, and is crowned with a burning mass of flour and oil until rifle fire interrupts the torment.
