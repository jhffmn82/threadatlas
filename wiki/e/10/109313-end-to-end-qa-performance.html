Abstract

PIC significantly improves end-to-end QA performance, measured by Exact Match, without any additional training.

4 / Experimental Settings .. 4.4 / Implementation Details

End-to-end QA performance evaluates how chunking affects the question-answering abilities of the RAG system. It is measured with Exact Match using Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct.

5 / Main Results / 5.1 / Retrieval Performance .. 5.2 / End-to-end QA Performance

End-to-end question-answering performance is consistently best for PIC across experiments with two different LLMs. Compared with retrieval performance, QA performance is substantially lower, but PIC shows the smallest performance decline among the evaluated methods.

6 / Further Analysis / 6.1 / Impacts of the Pseudo-Instruction .. 6.4 / Chunk Analysis

Question-answering performance follows the same general pattern as retrieval performance: summary-based PIC and a threshold at the mean produce the strongest results. Real-instruction chunking achieves the highest reported question-answering scores on NaturalQuestions and Qasper.

7 / Conclusions / Limitations

PIC significantly improves end-to-end QA performance compared with existing approaches across six QA benchmarks.
