the Summit Hotel

Also called: Summit Hotel, Summit House, establishment, hotel, house, the Summit House, the hotel, this house

Abstract written by a model

The Summit Hotel is a hotel in Buckeye Hill. It employs Frida Jansen as a servant and hotel girl and Miss Euphemia Trotter as housekeeper, with Trotter supervising the girls’ duties. After Chris Calton is injured, he stays there as a patient under medical supervision and Trotter’s care before returning to Roanoke Ledge. Following Mr. Bilson’s marriage to Frida and their travel abroad, Trotter gains control of the establishment, later resigning when Mr. Bilson resumes charge.

Mr. Bilson's Housekeeper

The Summit House employs Miss Euphemia Trotter as housekeeper and receives Chris Calton as a patient after his injury. Calton convalesces there under medical supervision and Trotter’s care before returning to Roanoke Ledge.
