Abstract written by a model

Dirce, wife of Lycus, mistreats imprisoned Antiope until Zethus and Amphion bind her to a bull, kill her, and name a spring for her.

Ὁ δὲ Κάδμος pera ‘Appovias Θήβας ἐκλιπὼν

Dirce is the wife of Lycus and mistreats the imprisoned Antiope. Zethus and Amphion bind her to a bull and throw her dead body into a spring that is thereafter called Dirce.
