STARK-PRIME in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where STARK-PRIME first appears

Abstract written by a model

STARK-PRIME is a STARK benchmark variant and Hybrid Question Answering STARK dataset for retrieval evaluation. It evaluates HYBGRAG, which reports 55% relative Hit@1 improvement over the second-best baseline and Hit@1 0.2856, Hit@5 0.4138, Recall@20 0.4358, and MRR 0.3449. It then provides examples of question-routing refinement and critic-feedback entity and relation extraction under context, in-context learning, and oracle settings.
