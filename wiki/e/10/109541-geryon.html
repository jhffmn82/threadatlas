Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Geryon is the three-bodied owner of the red kine who attacks Hercules and is shot dead. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Geryon is a three-bodied monster and reputed king of Erytheia.

Abstract written by a model

Geryon, son of Chrysaor and Callirrhoe, inhabits Erythia, has three joined male bodies, and owns red kine; when Hercules takes them, Geryon attacks beside Anthemus and is shot dead.

Geryon in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855 · the work’s page · read it where Geryon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Geryon, a three-bodied monster, dwelt on Erytheia, an island interpreted as Spain, where he was said to be king.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XII

Geryon was a monster with three bodies who dwelt on the western island of Erytheia. The description is thought to apply to Spain, where Geryon was said to be king.
