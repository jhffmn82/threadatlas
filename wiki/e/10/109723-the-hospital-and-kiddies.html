the hospital and kiddies in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The hospital, more crowded and underfunded than ever, receives Rudolph’s rescued infant as Miss Drake and a native woman try to save her; a local leper accuses its workers of stealing children for magic medicine.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII IPHIGENIA "Don't chop off a hen's head with a battle-axe.

The hospital is described as more crowded and more out of funds than ever. Miss Drake and a native woman receive Rudolph's rescued infant there and attempt to save her. A local leper accuses the hospital workers of stealing children to puncture their eyes for magic medicine.
