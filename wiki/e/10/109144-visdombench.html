VisDoMBench

dataset · 1 work · 39 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: VisDoMBench (Ours), VisDoMBench corpus

Abstract written by a model

VisDoMBench is a comprehensive, first-of-its-kind multi-document, multimodal document question-answering benchmark and QA dataset for visually rich content. It evaluates systems that must answer questions across document sets containing text, tables, charts, and presentation slides, requiring both textual and visual comprehension. The benchmark contains 2,271 queries and 1,277 documents, spans multiple domains, includes diverse question types and annotated grounded evidence, and represents each data point as a question, a document set, and a ground-truth answer. It combines PaperTab, FeTaTab, SciGraphQA, SPIQA, and SlideVQA. Its construction uses publicly accessible source documents, removes overlap between PaperTab and SPIQA, deduplicates QA pairs and similar questions across collections, adds distracting documents to simulate realistic multi-document settings, and augments queries with specific question variations generated with GPT-4o. VisDoMBench provides a more comprehensive multi-domain evaluation framework for multimodal retrieval and end-to-end QA. In experiments, it benchmarks large language models and compares retrieval and end-to-end question answering across its component datasets. Retrieval is evaluated with ANLCS and document-retrieval accuracy, while end-to-end results use Word Overlap F1 and compare multiple RAG and long-context approaches. It is introduced and concluded as a benchmark for multi-document systems incorporating rich visual elements.

Introduction

Related Work

VisDoMBench / 4.1

VisDoMBench is a multi-document QA benchmark whose data points consist of a question, a document set, and a ground-truth answer. It combines PaperTab, FeTaTab, SciGraphQA, SPIQA, and SlideVQA, covering text, tables, charts, and slides, and applies de-duplication, document augmentation, and query augmentation to support grounded evaluation.
