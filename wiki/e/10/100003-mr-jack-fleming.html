Mr. Jack Fleming in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Jack Fleming is a young, inexperienced prospector and miner working a claim with two partners near a mining town. After becoming lost in the redwood forest while returning from town, he finds a spring and quartz outcrop, borrows a bread pan from Katinka Jallinger, and initially recovers only tiny gold flakes. He returns the pan, retrieves his gold specimen ring, and helps Katinka with her clothes. Later, prompted by flakes and Katinka's letter, he learns the pan was salted, finds gold-bearing quartz at the Run, and becomes engaged to Katinka two weeks after the strike.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" A TREASURE OF THE REDWOODS PART I Mr.

Jack Fleming becomes lost in the redwood forest after leaving the path while returning from a mining town. He discovers a spring and quartz outcrop, borrows a bread pan from Katinka Jallinger, and finds only a few tiny flakes of gold while prospecting. He returns the pan, retrieves his gold specimen ring, and helps Katinka adjust her ill-fitting clothes.

PART II Mr.

Mr. Jack Fleming works a mining claim with two partners but is described as not much of a miner. After receiving gold flakes and a letter from Katinka Jallinger, he investigates the spring and outcrop, discovers that the pan had been salted, and then finds gold-bearing quartz in the Run. He becomes engaged to Katinka Jallinger.
