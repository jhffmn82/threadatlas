Abstract written by a model

The assault on Richards and Colonel Starbottle is an unexplained series of encounters initially suspected to be a lasso attack involving a black silk neck-handkerchief and unidentified assailants. Some believe Starbottle was dragged from his horse and choked, though the text leaves an attack uncertain; he admits choking. Grey investigates at the fonda. Later, Cota's pinto horse is identified as the apparent assailant, attacking Starbottle from behind and nearly strangling him before later attacking Richards.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Some people believe Colonel Starbottle was dragged from his horse and choked rather than merely thrown. The text does not determine whether an attack occurred, though the colonel admits that he experienced a sensation of choking.

PART II The next afternoon he went to the fonda.

The assault on Richards and Colonel Starbottle remains unexplained in this unit. Mr. Grey's visit to the fonda is intended in part to discover who attacked them.

He was still more concerned a week later, on returning unexpectedly one afternoo

The pinto horse's attack on Starbottle is described as an assault from behind that nearly results in strangulation. Richards identifies it as the same horse that later attacks him.
