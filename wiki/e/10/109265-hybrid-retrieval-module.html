3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

The hybrid retrieval module uses identified topic entities and useful relations to retrieve entities in an ego-graph, then ranks documents associated with the extracted entities using VSS between the question and documents. It combines relational and textual information.

4 .. 4.1.2

The hybrid retrieval module combines textual and relational information. It is the second-best method in the STARK retrieval comparison, while HYBGRAG improves further by correcting initially incorrect entity and relation extraction.
