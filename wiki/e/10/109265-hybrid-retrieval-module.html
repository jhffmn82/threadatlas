hybrid retrieval module

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Hybrid RM, Hybrid retrieval module, hybrid retrieval

Abstract written by a model

The hybrid retrieval module takes identified topic entities and useful relations, retrieves ego-graph entities, and ranks their associated documents with VSS between question and documents. Combining relational and textual information, it is second-best in STARK; HYBGRAG improves by correcting extraction errors.
