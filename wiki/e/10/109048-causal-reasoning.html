3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Causal reasoning is presented as the grounding principle of CausalRAG's retrieval process. The findings suggest that grounding retrieval in causal reasoning is promising for knowledge-intensive tasks.

Experiment / Experimental Setup .. Parameter Study

Causal reasoning improves retrieval by explicitly tracing relationships and preserving causal pathways. The experiments associate it with better alignment between retrieved evidence and the mechanisms needed to answer a question.

Conclusion and Future Work

Causal reasoning is integrated into CausalRAG to make retrieved context causally grounded rather than merely semantically relevant. Identifying causal paths during inference requires additional LLM calls, which introduces computational costs.
