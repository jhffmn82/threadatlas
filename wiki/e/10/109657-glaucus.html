Abstract written by a model

Glaucus, son of Minos by Pasiphae, dies after falling into a jar of honey while chasing a mouse, then Polyidus restores him with an herb brought by a serpent.

THE LIBRARY, 111. 10

Glaucus fell into a jar of honey while chasing a mouse and died. A serpent revived another serpent with an herb, and Polyidus used the same herb to restore Glaucus to life.
