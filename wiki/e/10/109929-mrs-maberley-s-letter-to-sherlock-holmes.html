THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Mrs. Maberley writes to Holmes about strange incidents connected with The Three Gables and asks for his advice. The letter bears a 10 p.m. postmark, yet Susan apparently informed Barney Stockdale about it quickly enough for Holmes to be threatened by the following morning.
