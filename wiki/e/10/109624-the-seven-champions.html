Abstract written by a model

The Seven Champions are Adrastus’s seven leaders for war against Thebes. With variant membership, they march via Nemea, bury the slain Opheltes, and send Tydeus to demand that Eteocles yield the kingdom.

VI. ᾿Ἑτεοκλῆς δὲ καὶ ἸΠολυνείκης περὶ τῆς

The Seven Champions are assembled by Adrastus for the war against Thebes. They include Adrastus, Amphiaraus, Capaneus, Hippomedon, Polynices, Tydeus, and Parthenopaeus, although another account replaces Polynices and Tydeus with Eteoclus and Mecisteus. They travel to Nemea, bury Opheltes after the child is killed, and later send Tydeus to demand that Eteocles yield the kingdom.
