VisDoMRAG in VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Manan Suri, Puneet Mathur, Franck Dernoncourt, Kanika Gowswami, Ryan A. Rossi, and Dinesh Manocha · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where VisDoMRAG first appears

Abstract written by a model

VisDoMRAG is a proposed multimodal retrieval-augmented generation system and approach for multi-document, visually rich document question answering. Its central design is to run visual RAG and textual RAG concurrently rather than relying on only one modality or a long context. First, Evidence-driven Parallel Unimodal RAG retrieves relevant pages and chunks from a multi-document source through parallel visual and textual pipelines. Within each modality, it performs evidence curation and chain-of-thought reasoning to generate modality-specific answers. Second, Modality Fusion ensembles these outputs: at inference time, a consistency-constrained modality-fusion mechanism evaluates reasoning-chain consistency, aligns reasoning across modalities, and produces the final answer. This fusion is intended to improve answer verifiability through implicit context attribution while constraining the context processed by the LLM, helping maintain performance as page counts rise. Across evaluated datasets and LLM settings, VisDoMRAG outperforms Visual RAG, Textual RAG, and Long Context baselines; reported end-to-end improvements are 12%–20%, while gains in the results range from 0.24% to 52.16%. It achieves the highest average score for each listed LLM and an overall average of 50.01 with ChatGPT-4o; prompt ablation reduces the average to 45.98. The work focuses on multimodal multi-document RAG and plans to extend to end-to-end trained models for reasoning, especially in low-resource settings.

Abstract

VisDoMRAG is proposed as a multimodal Retrieval Augmented Generation approach that simultaneously uses visual and textual retrieval. It performs evidence curation and chain-of-thought reasoning across concurrent visual and textual pipelines, and applies consistency-constrained modality fusion to align their reasoning at inference time. Experiments show that it outperforms unimodal and long-context large language model baselines by 12–20% for end-to-end multimodal document question answering.

Introduction

VisDoMRAG is introduced as a multimodal RAG approach that runs parallel textual and visual RAG pipelines and integrates their outputs through modality fusion. The authors report that it improves end-to-end QA performance by 12%–20% over strong baselines across various open- and closed-source LLM settings.

VisDoMBench / 4.1

VisDoMRAG processes a multi-document source through parallel visual and textual retrieval pipelines. It retrieves relevant pages and chunks, uses evidence curation and chain-of-thought reasoning to produce modality-specific answers, and applies modality fusion with reasoning-chain consistency analysis to generate a final answer.

VisDoMRAG / 5.1 .. 5.2

VisDoMRAG performs visually rich document question answering through parallel textual and visual RAG pipelines followed by Modality Fusion. It uses evidence curation, Chain-of-Thought reasoning, answer generation, and consistency evaluation to produce a final answer.

Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

VisDoMRAG is evaluated as the authors' approach for end-to-end QA. It achieves the highest average score for each listed LLM and reaches an overall average of 50.01 with ChatGPT-4o.

Results / 7.1 .. 7.3

VisDoMRAG consistently outperforms Visual RAG, Textual RAG, and Long Context baselines across the evaluated datasets, with gains ranging from 0.24% to 52.16%. It maintains relatively consistent performance as the number of pages increases and achieves an average ablation score of 50.01, while prompt ablation reduces this to 45.98.

Conclusion and Future Work

VisDoMRAG is introduced as a multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation approach that fuses visual and textual pipelines using consistency-constrained modality fusion. It demonstrates a 12–20% improvement over traditional long-context, textual, and visual RAG.
