Abstract written by a model

The prospecting pan is the family's battered, leaky bread pan, lent by Katinka to Fleming for washing dirt for gold. Despite its damaged bottom, Fleming tests soil at the spring and quartz outcrop and finds little gold. Later, its break reveals gold flakes while Katinka mixes dough, and she uses another pan to search for their source.

" A TREASURE OF THE REDWOODS PART I Mr.

Katinka lends Fleming the family's battered bread pan so he can wash soil for gold. The pan is leaky, with a damaged bottom, but Fleming uses it at the spring for two tests of soil from the quartz outcrop. He returns it to Katinka after finding little gold.

PART II Mr.

A broken prospecting pan brings gold flakes to light when Katinka uses it to mix bread dough. Fleming later borrows another pan to test the spring and outcrop, and Katinka uses the pan he brought to the Run while searching for the real source of the gold.
