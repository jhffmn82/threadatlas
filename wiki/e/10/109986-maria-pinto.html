Abstract written by a model

Maria Pinto, a Brazilian and daughter of a Manaos government official, was Neil Gibson's devoted wife. Once exceptionally beautiful and passionate, she was found dead near Thor Bridge in her dinner dress, fatally shot through the brain.

Maria Pinto is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
