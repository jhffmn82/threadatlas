Maria Pinto in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Maria Pinto, a Brazilian and daughter of a Manaos government official, was Neil Gibson's devoted wife. Once exceptionally beautiful and passionate, she was found dead near Thor Bridge in her dinner dress, fatally shot through the brain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

Maria Pinto was Neil Gibson's Brazilian wife and the victim of the tragedy. Gibson says that she was once exceptionally beautiful, passionate, and devoted to him, even after his love for her had faded.
