Abstract written by a model

The Sword-Pen is Fang, a scholar and strict Confucian known as a clever, notorious mischief-maker and one of the district’s two most dangerous men. Tall, stooping, and dressed in a long robe or bright robes, he could have become a mandarin but prefers to live as a country gentleman. He hates the Jesus religion and foreigners, and Dr. Earle fears he will use Chok Chung’s killing for his purposes. After a stabbing, he is seen leaving the edge of a hostile crowd, and Heywood suspects he stirred the crowd against him. Fang is said to have brought the suit against Chok Chung and is identified as head of the White Lotus lodge. Through a black shrine image, he asks when the foreign monsters will perish and how the lodge will learn the appointed hour and day. Later, while seated on a white pony near a bier, he is attacked by Heywood with a halberd and knocked down in one blow.
