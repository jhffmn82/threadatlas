Cissy Trixit in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Cissy Trixit is the acknowledged belle of Canada City, daughter of Montagu Trixit, its principal banker, and protector and patron of Miss Piney Tibbs. A country belle proud of her father’s position, she is frankly vain, socially imperious, and initially unaware of his business and its responsibilities. Her new hat and confidence give way to alarm when townspeople avoid her, crowds gather at the bank and newspaper office, and a newspaper extra reports her father’s absconding and the bank crisis. She rejects the accusations as lies, angrily condemns Mr. Windibrook’s judgment, and refuses to leave the family house unless her father tells her to go. When the house passes into the hands of the law, she takes private contracts and some of her father’s money, secretly leaves with Ah Fe to find Hop Li, and pursues her father alone. Disguised successfully as a Chinaman, she travels without attracting white observers, rescues Masterton after his fall with another Chinaman’s help, arranges his care, and accompanies Montagu toward Sacramento. Her courage and loyalty transform the vain, childlike belle into an active defender and rescuer of her father and Masterton.
