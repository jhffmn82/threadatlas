Personalized PageRank in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where Personalized PageRank first appears

Abstract written by a model

Personalized PageRank is a retriever using only relational information. It performs random walks from topic entities identified by Claude 3 Sonnet, ranks neighboring entities by knowledge-graph connectivity, extracts useful-relation ego-graphs, and uses their intersection when multiple graphs are extracted; on STARK-MAG, it achieves Hit@1 0.2533 and Hit@5 0.5523.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

Personalized PageRank uses only relational information, performing random walks from topic entities identified by Claude 3 Sonnet and ranking neighboring entities by their connectivity in the knowledge graph. It achieves Hit@1 of 0.2533 and Hit@5 of 0.5523 on STARK-MAG.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

The graph retriever extracts entities in the ego-graph of the identified topic entities, connected by the useful relations. If multiple ego-graphs are extracted, their intersection is used as the final result.
