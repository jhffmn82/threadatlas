native attackers in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Native attackers are an armed, numerous group that first attack an uprising and move through darkness in crowds, fighting Rudolph amid burning houses while crying “Sha, sha.” They possess powder stored underground and shift from ineffective sniping to digging beneath the defenders, preparing an underground assault intended to blow up the defending position in one sweep.

CHAPTER XVI THE GUNWALE Rudolph's mission began quietly, with a glimpse which he afterward recalled as incredibly peaceful.

Native attackers move through the darkness in crowds, fight Rudolph on the road, and continue shooting and attacking amid the burning houses. Rudolph sees them as a disordered mass carrying torches and hears their repeated cry of "Sha, sha."

CHAPTER XIX BROTHER MOLES This new danger, working below in the solid earth, had thrown Rudolph into a state of sullen resignation.

The beasts are understood to be preparing an underground attack rather than relying on their ineffective sniping. Heywood believes they intend to make one clean sweep and blow up the defending position.
