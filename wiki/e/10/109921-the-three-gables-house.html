Abstract written by a model

The Three Gables is Mrs. Maberley’s poorly situated but well-furnished brick-and-timber villa in Harrow Weald, standing on its own acre near Weald Station. A suspicious buyer seeks the house, its furniture, and everything inside, prompting Holmes’s protection. During the night it is burgled by men searching Douglas Maberley’s belongings; police examine it and find a crumpled manuscript fragment.
