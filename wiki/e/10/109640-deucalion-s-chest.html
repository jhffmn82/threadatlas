VII. Προμηθεὺς δὲ ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ γῆς ἀνθρώ-

Deucalion constructs the chest on Prometheus's advice and stores provisions in it. He and Pyrrha enter it and float over the sea for nine days and nights during the Flood.
