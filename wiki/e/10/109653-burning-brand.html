burning brand

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a brand then burning upon the hearth, brand, fatal brand, the brand, the fatal brand, the fatal wood, δαλὸς, τὸν δαλὸν
  • In The Library, the burning brand determines Meleager’s life: Althaea rescues it from the fire and stores it in a chest, then later burns it, causing his immediate death.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the fatal brand is linked by the Destinies to the length of Meleager’s life; Althea quenches and preserves it when he is born, but later throws it onto a fire, causing Meleager to suffer and die as it burns.

A burning hearth brand determines Meleager’s life: Althaea rescues it from fire and stores it in a chest, then later burns it, causing his immediate death.
