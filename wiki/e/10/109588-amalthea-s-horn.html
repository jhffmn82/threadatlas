Abstract written by a model

produces whatever is asked for [bestowed by Zeus on the nymphs who reared him] Amalthea’s horn was given by Achelous in exchange for the horn Hercules had broken off. According to Pherecydes, it could supply whatever meat or drink was desired in abundance.
