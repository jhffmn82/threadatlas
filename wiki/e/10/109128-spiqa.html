VisDoMBench / 4.1

SPIQA is a scientific-paper QA dataset involving tables and charts, contributing 586 queries and 117 documents to VisDoMBench. It was sourced from a chart-and-table QA dataset system based on QASPER, and overlapping samples with PaperTab were removed.

Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

SPIQA is evaluated for retrieval performance and end-to-end QA. Its source-document identification scores range from 61.06 with MiniLM to 98.43 with BGE-1.5 at k = 5.
