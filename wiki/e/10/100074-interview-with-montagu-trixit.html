interview with Montagu Trixit in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

An interview with Montagu Trixit is a directors-ordered business mission undertaken by Mr. Masterton, though unwelcome to him. After setting out by coach, Masterton faces a storm, suspected Chinese spies, and Jake Poole, which may let him reach Trixit first. He later explains the interview could have benefited them both.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

A few hours later he was on the coach.

Mr. Masterton's mission is to reach Montagu Trixit, though he finds its nature unpleasant and feels some self-reproach about it. The storm, the suspected Chinese spies, and Jake Poole complicate the mission, but also appear to give him an opportunity to reach Trixit first.

How long he lay there thus he never knew.

Masterton came to see Montagu on the order of his directors. He says the intended interview was a business matter that might have been as much in Montagu's interest as his own.
