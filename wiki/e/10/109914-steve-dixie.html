Steve Dixie in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Steve Dixie is a bruiser belonging to the Spencer John gang and allegedly involved in killing young Perkins. Ordered by Barney Stockdale, he enters Holmes's rooms to warn him off the Harrow Weald case, but Holmes intimidates him and extends protection to Mrs. Maberley and her house.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Steve Dixie enters Holmes's rooms to warn him not to interfere in the Harrow Weald case, claiming that his friend and employer's associate has ordered the warning. Holmes identifies him as a bruiser connected with the Spencer John gang and frightens him by mentioning the killing of young Perkins. Steve later admits that Barney Stockdale gives him orders and is warned that Mrs. Maberley and everything in the house are under Holmes's protection.
