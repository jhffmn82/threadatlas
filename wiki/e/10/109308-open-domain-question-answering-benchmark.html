Open-domain question-answering benchmarks are a set of six benchmarks, Natural Questions, TriviaQA, WebQuestions, SQuAD, EntityQuestions, and PopQA, used to evaluate PIC and chunking methods on retrieval and end-to-end answering. They assess reading comprehension and natural-language queries with Wikipedia or structured knowledge bases; experiments conclude that PIC significantly improves retrieval accuracy and QA performance.

Abstract

Experiments on multiple open-domain question-answering benchmarks are used to evaluate PIC.

1 / Introduction

Open-domain question-answering benchmarks are used to evaluate different chunking methods. PIC outperforms existing approaches on retrieval performance measured by Hits@k and end-to-end question-answering performance measured by Exact Match.

4 / Experimental Settings .. 4.4 / Implementation Details

The OpenQA benchmarks used are Natural Questions, TriviaQA, WebQuestions, SQuAD, EntityQuestions, and PopQA. They evaluate reading comprehension and answering natural-language queries using Wikipedia or structured knowledge bases.

7 / Conclusions / Limitations

Experiments across six QA benchmarks show that PIC achieves significant improvements in retrieval accuracy and end-to-end QA performance.
