North Fork canyon in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

North Fork canyon lies beneath the stage road and bridge, enclosed by narrow, precipitous walls. Brice reaches it, crosses a faint ledge of driftwood and cliff debris, and emerges near an isolated hollow. Flora later pretends to lead him through it, then turns into a buckeye thicket instead.

II The robbery furnished the usual amount of copy for the local press.

The North Fork canyon is enclosed by narrow, precipitous walls, with the stage road and its bridge hundreds of feet above. Brice reaches the canyon, crosses along a faint ledge formed by driftwood and cliff debris, and emerges near the isolated hollow.

"I am going to be a real guide to you now," she said suddenly.

Flora pretends to lead Brice through the canyon so that anyone watching them will believe they have taken that route. Instead, she turns into the buckeye thicket before reaching it.
