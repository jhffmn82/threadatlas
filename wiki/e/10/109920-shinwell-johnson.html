Shinwell Johnson in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Shinwell Johnson, a former criminal who served two terms at Parkhurst, now works as Sherlock Holmes’s agent in London’s criminal underworld. Sent to find damaging information about Adelbert Gruner, he quickly locates his old mate Kitty Winter, tells her of Gruner’s pursuit of another woman, brings her to Holmes, and is tasked with taking her to a quiet suburb for protection.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ILLUSTRIOUS CLIENT

Shinwell Johnson is a former dangerous villain who now works as Holmes's agent in London's criminal underworld. Holmes sends him to search for damaging information about Baron Gruner.

We found him sure enough, a huge, coarse, red-faced, scorbutic man,

Shinwell Johnson brings Kitty Winter to Sherlock Holmes within an hour of receiving Holmes's message. He has been telling Kitty about Gruner's pursuit of another woman and confirms that Gruner's fancy crockery would not interest burglars.
