Llama3.2:3b

Also called: Llama 3.2 3B, Llama3.2:3b model, a small model

Abstract written by a model

Llama3.2:3b is a small LLM and small model evaluated for context relevance, answer faithfulness, and answer relevance. It begins as a small-model entry but emerges as an outlier in the experimental results: it scores 0.508 on context relevance, 4.619 on answer faithfulness, and 4.857 on answer relevance, with a Kappa value of 0.90. Its faithfulness and relevance scores are the highest reported and outperform medium models and a number of large models. In these aspects it performs comparably to larger models, suggesting that architectural improvements, pre-training quality, data efficiency, and architectural optimizations can partly offset smaller parameter size.

Llama3.2:3b is in one work on this site, Investigating Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Quranic Studies: A Study of 13 Open-Source Large Language Models. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
