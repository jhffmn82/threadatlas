the detached lodge in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The detached lodge is a sizable building at the end of the garden path near the Emsworth mansion, fit for a gardener or gamekeeper. Its shuttered, heavily curtained windows conceal a bright, warm room with a lamp and fire, though a little bearded man and another figure are seen inside. It houses Godfrey Emsworth in seclusion with Mr. Kent; he may leave after dark, and Holmes, Dodd, Colonel Emsworth, and Kent later enter to speak with him.
