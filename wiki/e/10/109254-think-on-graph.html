Abstract written by a model

Think-on-Graph is a graph retrieval baseline implemented with provided code and Claude 3 Sonnet. Because full experiments exceed a week in latency and cost, it is tested on 10% of questions, reporting lower retrieval scores than HYBGRAG on both STARK datasets.

Think-on-Graph is in one work on this site, HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
