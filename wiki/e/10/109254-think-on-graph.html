Think-on-Graph is evaluated as a graph retrieval baseline on 10% of the testing questions because of latency and cost. It reports lower retrieval scores than HYBGRAG on both STARK datasets.

appendix

Think-on-Graph is implemented with its provided code using Claude 3 Sonnet as the base model. Because a full experiment takes more than a week, it is evaluated on only 10% of the testing data.
