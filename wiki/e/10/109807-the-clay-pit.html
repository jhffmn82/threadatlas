the clay-pit in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The clay-pit is a place during the siege, connected by a mine tunnel to the surface. Rudolph first sees coolies working there; later he and Heywood hear digging and recognize the attackers’ underground danger. A shaft from the mound opens into its passage, enabling their descent, return with powder, and escape before the explosion.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The clay-pit is where Rudolph notices coolies working during the first night. Later, Rudolph and Heywood hear digging there and conclude that the attackers are working beneath the defenses.

CHAPTER XIX BROTHER MOLES This new danger, working below in the solid earth, had thrown Rudolph into a state of sullen resignation.

The shaft opens from the mound into the underground passage. Heywood and Rudolph descend through it, return to the surface with powder, and later use it to escape the tunnel before the explosion.
