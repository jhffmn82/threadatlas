Abstract written by a model

The landscape from the pagoda includes an eastern sapphire ocean, glowing ancient walls, fields, hillocks, bamboo, pink paths, and a merchant on a white pony. Its timeless continuity, accompanied by bells and frogs, oppresses Rudolph.

" CHAPTER VI THE PAGODA He was spared that inconvenience.

From the pagoda tower, Rudolph and Mrs. Forrester see the ocean as a sapphire band, ancient city walls glowing on a ridge, green fields and hillocks, bamboo, pink footpaths, and a merchant riding a white pony. The timeless landscape and the sounds of bells and frogs make Rudolph feel oppressed by the aged continuity of life.
