the family house in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Cissy's father's house contains a veranda, drawing-room, kitchen, basement staircase, and garden. It is unusually still when Cissy arrives: no servant answers, the kitchen is empty, and the back door stands open while fires burn and dishes remain on the table. Seized and in the hands of the law, it is guarded by a sheriff's officer; Cissy leaves by the back way.

The house is unusually still, and no servant answers the doorbell or Cissy's calls. The kitchen is empty, although the back door is open, the fires are burning, and dishes are on the table.

The family house is seized because it is in the hands of the law, and a sheriff's officer is stationed there. Cissy realizes it is no longer hers and leaves it through the back way.
