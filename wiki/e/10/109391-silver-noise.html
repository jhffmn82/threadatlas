SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation / Abstract

Silver noise is one of the four attack-task categories used in the SafeRAG security evaluation.

Introduction

Silver noise is evidence that partially contains the answer. It is proposed as an improved noise attack because it can circumvent most safety filters and undermine RAG diversity.

It consists of partial but incomplete answers. To create it, golden contexts are decomposed into fine-grained propositions, the proposition most semantically similar to the question is selected, and diverse contexts are generated from it.

Evaluation Metrics / Retrieval Safety Metric .. Generation Safety Metric / F1 Variant

Silver noise is evaluated by constructing multiple-choice questions from fine-grained propositions derived from golden contexts, including deliberately distorted incorrect options.

Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

The silver noise task uses a default retrieval window of top K = 6 and a default attack injection ratio of 3/6. Increasing silver noise reduced F1 (avg), and noise injected into retrieved or filtered context achieved higher retrieval accuracy than noise injected into the knowledge base.

Conclusion

Silver noise is proposed as a novel attack strategy that exposes gaps in existing defenses.
