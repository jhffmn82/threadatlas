2 sections; each heading opens the text

VisDoMBench / 4.1

SciGraphQA supplies chart-based questions from scientific papers and contains 407 queries and 319 documents in VisDoMBench. Trivial layout and document-metadata questions were filtered out, and questions were sampled from the top 50th percentile by length; full document PDFs were extracted using the ArXiv API.

Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

SciGraphQA is evaluated for retrieval performance and end-to-end QA. Its source-document identification scores range from 72.73 with BM25 to 95.58 with ColQwen2 at k = 5.
