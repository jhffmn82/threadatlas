Quranic studies is the sensitive, domain-specific application area examined in the study. The models answer queries related to the meanings, historical context, and qualities of Quranic surahs.

I.

Quranic studies is presented as a domain in which natural language processing and large language models can support question answering and knowledge retrieval. The unit focuses on improving the accuracy, contextual relevance, and trustworthiness of AI-generated responses about the Qur’an and its descriptive material.

II.

Islamic scholarship is used as a criterion for assessing the dataset’s authenticity and for excluding speculative interpretations. Experts in Islamic studies reviewed the digitized and structured content for accuracy and alignment with the original source.
