Related Work

In visual question answering, one approach addresses open-domain challenges by using object detection, image captioning, and optical character recognition to transform target images into textual data. MuRAG retrieves text and image data and incorporates images as visual tokens, while RAMM retrieves and encodes similar biomedical images and their captions through distinct networks.
