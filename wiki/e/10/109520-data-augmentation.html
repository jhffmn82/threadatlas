data augmentation in VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution

Xueguang Ma, Shengyao Zhuang, Bevan Koopman, Guido Zuccon, Wenhu Chen, Jimmy Lin · 2025

Data augmentation with FineWeb-VISA raises Wiki-VISA bounding-box accuracy, especially for multiple passages, while not significantly improving Paper-VISA in-domain results. Combined with Paper-VISA training, it substantially improves zero-shot transfer to Wiki-VISA.

6 / 6.1 .. 6.4

Data augmentation with FineWeb-VISA improves Wiki-VISA bounding box accuracy from 54.2% to 58.2% and improves performance on cases with more than one passage from 50.1% to 61.7%. It does not significantly improve Paper-VISA in-domain performance but raises zero-shot performance on Wiki-VISA when combined with Paper-VISA training.
