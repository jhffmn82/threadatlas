Abstract written by a model

In “Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability,” data augmentation is the setting under which reliability estimation experiments for Politician A, Politician B, and User A are conducted. In “VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution,” data augmentation with FineWeb-VISA raises Wiki-VISA bounding-box accuracy, especially for multiple passages, does not significantly improve Paper-VISA in-domain results, and combined with Paper-VISA training substantially improves zero-shot transfer to Wiki-VISA.

Data augmentation with FineWeb-VISA improves Wiki-VISA bounding box accuracy from 54.2% to 58.2% and improves performance on cases with more than one passage from 50.1% to 61.7%.
