Prescott's counterfeiting outfit in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Prescott's counterfeiting outfit, a printing press and materials used by Rodger Prescott, was hidden beneath Nathan Garrideb's apartment and discovered after Prescott's death, pleasing police who had failed to locate it.

Prescott's counterfeiting outfit was concealed in the cellar beneath Nathan Garrideb's apartment. It included a printing press and related materials, and its discovery pleased the Yard because the police had known the outfit existed but could not locate it after Prescott's death.
