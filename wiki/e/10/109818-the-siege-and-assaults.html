the siege and assaults in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A prolonged, furious military encirclement targets the nunnery over nights and days, alternating pauses with outbreaks. Attackers use gunfire, river boats, battering-rams against both gates, dummies to draw fire, and eventually dig beneath the defenses. Defenders beat back the assaults but become exhausted and nearly out of ammunition as the threat escalates.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The siege continues through alternating attacks and pauses, with the defenders receiving no immediate explanation for the warnings sent by arrow. The Chinese forces eventually begin digging beneath the defenses, suggesting a new threat.

CHAPTER XX THE HAKKA BOAT "Pretty fair," Captain Kneebone said.

The siege includes sustained gunfire, attacks by river boats, battering-rams at both gates, and attempts to draw the defenders' fire with dummies. The assaults are beaten off, but they leave the defenders exhausted and nearly out of ammunition.
