the siege and assaults

Also called: Siege, an attack of prolonged and furious exasperation, battering-rams, later assaults, the siege

Abstract written by a model

A prolonged, furious military encirclement targets the nunnery over nights and days, alternating pauses with outbreaks. Attackers use gunfire, river boats, battering-rams against both gates, dummies to draw fire, and eventually dig beneath the defenses. Defenders beat back the assaults but become exhausted and nearly out of ammunition as the threat escalates.
