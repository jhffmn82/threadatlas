Killer Evans in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Killer Evans, a forty-four-year-old Chicago native and dangerous armed criminal, shot three men, including Rodger Prescott over cards. Under police supervision since 1901, he posed as John Garrideb to reach Prescott's hidden counterfeiting operation, fired at Watson, and was subdued by Holmes and handed to authorities.
