1 section; each heading opens the text

IX. ᾿Αρκάδος δὲ καὶ Λεανείρας τῆς ᾿Αμύκλου

Auge hid her baby in Athena's precinct, where she held the priesthood. When the land became barren, oracles declared that an impiety existed there, leading to the discovery of the baby and Auge's punishment.
