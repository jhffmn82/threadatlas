Abstract written by a model

The Eureka Mining Company is a five-member mining company working on a Sierran foothill, with a foreman and members including Ned Bray and Parkhurst. The partners work a burning ledge beside the stage road, shelter beneath a pine tree during the hottest hour, and use dice to choose who fetches water from a spring four or five times daily. As the sun leaves the ledge, they move their prospecting efforts toward the spring.
