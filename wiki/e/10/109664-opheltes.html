1 section; each heading opens the text

VI. ᾿Ἑτεοκλῆς δὲ καὶ ἸΠολυνείκης περὶ τῆς

Opheltes is the infant son of Eurydice and Lycurgus, and Hypsipyle nurses him. After Hypsipyle leaves him to show the champions the spring, a serpent kills him; the champions bury him and call him Archemorus.
