Kempner's death in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Kempner dies during the Chinese assault while defending the northeast corner, after throwing a burning stink-pot cylinder away from the defenders. His action helps end the assault; his weapon goes to Gilly Forrester and his body is buried at the nunnery.

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

Kempner's death occurs when he throws the burning stink-pot cylinder away from the defenders. His action helps end the assault, and Heywood and Rudolph carry his body to the nunnery for burial.
