the banquet in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A celebratory, lavish dinner replaces a planned meal when Captain Kneebone brings stores from Saigong. The banquet unfolds with abundant food, excellent wine, candles, conversation, and laughter, until an attack interrupts it.

Captain Kneebone replaces the planned dinner with a banquet supplied by the stores he brings from Saigong. It is marked by abundant food, excellent wine, candles, conversation, and laughter before the attack interrupts it.
