James Winter in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

James Winter, also known as Morecroft and Killer Evans, is a forty-four-year-old Chicago native listed by Scotland Yard after shooting three men in the United States and coming to London.

James Winter is listed in Scotland Yard's records as an alias of Morecroft and Killer Evans. He is aged forty-four, a native of Chicago, and is known to have shot three men in the United States before coming to London.
