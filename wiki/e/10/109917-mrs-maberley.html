Mrs. Maberley

Also called: Mary Maberley, the lady, your mistress

Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Maberley is an elderly, refined woman living at and owning The Three Gables, where she employs Susan and keeps the unpacked luggage of her dead son, Douglas. Planning long-dreamed-of travel, possibly to Madeira or around the world, she asks Sherlock Holmes to investigate strange events tied to a proposed sale of her house and contents. After ignoring Holmes's protection advice, she is chloroformed and injured in a burglary, but Holmes obtains £5,000 from Isadora Klein for her travels.

Mrs. Maberley is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Mrs. Maberley asks Holmes to investigate strange incidents at The Three Gables. She explains the proposed sale of the house and its contents, identifies Douglas as her dead son, and agrees to have his recently arrived luggage examined.

When I met my friend in his room early next morning, I was conscious

Mrs. Maberley is chloroformed and attacked during the burglary at The Three Gables after disregarding Holmes's advice to have someone stay in the house. She tries to seize one of the burglars but is knocked unconscious, and Holmes later secures £5,000 from Isadora Klein to send her around the world.
