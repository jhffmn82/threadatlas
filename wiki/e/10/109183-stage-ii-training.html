Stage-II training in SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains

Ran Xu, Hui Liu, Sreyashi Nag, Zhenwei Dai, Yaochen Xie, Xianfeng Tang, Chen Luo, Yang Li, Joyce C. Ho, Carl Yang, Qi He · 2025

Stage-II training is a domain adaptation stage that adapts the Stage-I model to specialized domains. It generates pseudo-labeled, self-synthesized training samples from unlabeled domain corpora, filters them for high-quality answerable QA pairs, and combines them with general instruction and context-aware QA data for continued fine-tuning. It uses batch size 64, gradient accumulation 8, and one epoch, with learning rates of 2e-7 for Llama3 and 5e-7 for Gemma. Diverse question types perform best; removing short-span QA hurts most. It further improves results after Stage-I, while removing it lowers medical and scientific performance.

Methodology / Problem Setup .. Stage-II: Domain Adaptive Fine-tuning

Stage-II domain adaptive fine-tuning adapts the Stage-I model to specialized domains using pseudo-labeled samples generated from unlabeled domain corpora. The synthetic samples are combined with general instruction and context-aware QA data for continued fine-tuning.

Experimental Setup / Tasks and Datasets .. Implementation Details

Stage-II training uses a learning rate of 2e-7 with the Llama3 backbone and 5e-7 with the Gemma backbone, while retaining a global batch size of 64, gradient accumulation of 8, and one epoch. It uses pseudo-labeled samples generated from domain corpora, and removing Stage-II training lowers SimRAG performance in both the medical and scientific evaluations.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

Stage-II fine-tunes language models on self-synthesized training tuples and produces further improvements after Stage-I, with average increases of 2.21% for Llama and 3.50% for Gemma. Including multiple-choice, short-span, and claim-verification data gives the best performance, while removing short-span question answering causes the largest performance drop.
