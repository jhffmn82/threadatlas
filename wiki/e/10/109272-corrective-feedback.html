Abstract written by a model

Corrective feedback, from a critic module and grounded in verified training experiences, guides HYBGRAG’s router to refine question routing. When retrieval or evaluation is wrong, it identifies faulty or missing entities, relations, intersections, domains, inputs, or modules, enabling revised retrieval decisions.

corrective feedback is in one work on this site, HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Corrective feedback gives the router explicit guidance for refining question routing. It can identify incorrect, missing, or absent entities and relations, intersection problems, or an incorrect retrieval module.

appendix .. C.1.2

Corrective feedback is provided when retrieval or evaluation is incorrect, indicating that the retrieval input, extracted entity, relation, domain, or selected module may be wrong. HYBGRAG uses this feedback to revise its retrieval decisions.
