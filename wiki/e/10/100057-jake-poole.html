Jake Poole in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Jake Poole is a deputy sheriff acting for the law. He takes possession of the family house, where the sheriff’s officer says the house and contents are in the hands of the law, allows Cissy to save keepsakes, and prepares for inventory and sealing. Later, Poole travels with Mr. Masterton under orders, pretending to search seriously for Montagu Trixit and approaching a Chinese group.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Then, in a revulsion of feeling lest any one should find her there, she ran upst

The sheriff's officer on the veranda represents the authority taking possession of the house and its contents. He explains that the house is in the hands of the law and later permits Cissy to set aside personal keepsakes before an inventory and sealing are completed.

A few hours later he was on the coach.

Jake Poole is identified as a deputy sheriff traveling with Mr. Masterton under the pretense of hunting Montagu Trixit. He appears to be acting under orders and is seen approaching the Chinese group, causing one of them to act as a messenger.
