HQA in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

HQA, or hybrid question answering, is a question answering task over a semi-structured knowledge base. It answers hybrid questions by retrieving documents associated with entities that meet both relational and textual requirements. It is the central task addressed by HYBGRAG and studied in STARK. Existing ODQA, KBQA, RAG, Graph RAG, and self-reflective LLM approaches may struggle because they lack suitable retrieval tools or guidance for refining retrieval. HYBGRAG addresses HQA’s textual, relational, and hybrid questions through a retriever bank and critic module, and is reported to outperform existing RAG and GRAG methods.
