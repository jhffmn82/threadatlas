Aloads

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: ᾿Αλωάδας

Abstract written by a model

Aloads were Otus and Ephialtes, sons of Poseidon and Iphimedia. Growing extraordinarily fast, they became enormous, opposed the gods, and piled Pelion on Ossa toward Olympus to reach heaven. They threatened sea and land, imprisoned Ares, and Artemis killed them by deception.

Aloads is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Aloads were Otus and Ephialtes, sons of Poseidon and Iphimedia. They grew rapidly, attempted to pile mountains to reach heaven, threatened to reshape the sea and land, imprisoned Ares, and were killed by Artemis through deception.
