Abstract written by a model

Teuthras, prince and ruler of Mysia and Teuthrania, marries Auge, adopts her son Telephus, and is succeeded as ruler of Mysia by Telephus after his death.

Teuthras is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

IX. ᾿Αρκάδος δὲ καὶ Λεανείρας τῆς ᾿Αμύκλου

Teuthras was the ruler of Mysia. He received and married Auge, and later adopted her son Telephus, who succeeded him as ruler when he died.
