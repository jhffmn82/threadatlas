Introduction

The visual and textual modalities are jointly used to represent videos and support their retrieval. VideoRAG obtains textual information through transcripts when descriptions such as subtitles are unavailable.

5

The inclusion of visual features, textual features, or their combination improves retrieval and generation performance. Textual features are described as critical for retrieval quality but absent from some videos.
