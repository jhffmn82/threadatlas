visual and textual modalities in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Soyeong Jeong · 2025

VideoRAG jointly uses visual and textual modalities to represent videos for retrieval and generation, obtaining transcripts when subtitles are unavailable; visual, textual, or combined features improve performance, with text critical for retrieval quality despite being absent in some videos.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The visual and textual modalities are jointly used to represent videos and support their retrieval. VideoRAG obtains textual information through transcripts when descriptions such as subtitles are unavailable.

5

The inclusion of visual features, textual features, or their combination improves retrieval and generation performance. Textual features are described as critical for retrieval quality but absent from some videos.
