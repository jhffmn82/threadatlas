Abstract

PIC computes the semantic similarity of sentences to a document summary and uses the results to group sentences dynamically into chunks.

3 / Methodology .. 3.2 / Chunking Based on Pseudo-Instructions

Sentences in document D are encoded with an embedding model and compared individually with the document summary. Adjacent sentences with similarity scores on the same side of the threshold are grouped together.
