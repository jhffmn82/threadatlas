Reliability-Aware RAG counters unreliable sources that dominate or exploit retrieval by prioritizing sources estimated more reliable.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Unreliable sources may contain incorrect information or highly relevant but false documents. Standard retrieval can allow them to dominate results, whereas Reliability-Aware RAG prioritizes sources estimated to be more reliable.
