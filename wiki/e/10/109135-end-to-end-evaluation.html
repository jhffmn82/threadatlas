Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

End-to-end QA uses the best text and visual retrieval models identified in the retrieval experiments. It evaluates Long Context, TextualRAG, VisualRAG, and VisDoMRAG with Qwen2-VL, Gemini-1.5-Flash, and ChatGPT-4o.

Results / 7.1 .. 7.3

The end-to-end evaluation compares VisDoMRAG with Visual RAG, Textual RAG, and Long Context methods using Qwen2VL (7B), Gemini Flash, and GPT-4. VisDoMRAG achieves superior performance across the evaluated datasets, and larger models generally perform better within each baseline method.
