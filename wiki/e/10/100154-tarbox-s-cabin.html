Brice had left the coach and gone to a settler's cabin before the robbery. He believes the treasure was taken during his absence. Tarbox's cabin is connected with the events surrounding the robbery because the two conspirators got a scout to cut down the tree by falsely claiming it was part of Henry's orders. The road from Tarbox's cabin to the bridge remains watched by Henry's men, trapping the escaped thief.
