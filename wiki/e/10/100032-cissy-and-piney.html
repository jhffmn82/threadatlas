Abstract written by a model

Cissy and Piney, young women regarded by Canada City as fresh and innocent, walk together down Main Street in Cissy's new hat to see the town's reaction and visit the parsonage. Miners and townsmen honor them; Piney leaves Cissy there and returns home.

" A BELLE OF CANADA CITY Cissy was tying her hat under her round chin before a small glass at her window.

Cissy and Piney go out together in Cissy's new hat, intending to walk down Main Street, view the town's reaction, and visit the parsonage. They receive bows, removed obstacles, and other signs of public attention as they pass through Canada City. Piney leaves Cissy at the parsonage and returns home.
