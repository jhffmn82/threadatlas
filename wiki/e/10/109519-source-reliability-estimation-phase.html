source reliability estimation phase in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

The source reliability estimation phase is a one-time preprocessing step in database construction with practical computational overhead. Its wall-clock estimation time rises from 4.65 minutes for 5 sources to 14.81 hours for 1,000, while iterative estimation takes under 0.2 seconds at 1,000 sources.

J / Inference phase / Reliablity estimation phase

The source reliability estimation phase is described as having practical computational overhead and serving as a one-time preprocessing step during database construction. Its wall-clock time increases from 4.65 minutes for 5 sources to 14.81 hours for 1,000 sources.
