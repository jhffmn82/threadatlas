Montagu Trixit in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Montagu Trixit is a man, Cissy’s father, Canada City’s principal banker, bank owner, and manager, as well as a managing director of the Excelsior Bank. He is reputed to be a fearless, highly successful speculator: he made a fifty-thousand-dollar profit in a Comstock deal and contributed one thousand dollars toward a new organ. Through finance and public credit, he builds Canada City. His standing collapses when his bank fails and a newspaper reports that he has absconded. He is accused of stealing money from the Canada City bank and fleeing, bringing disgrace upon Cissy, who had not known the details of his business. From hiding, he sends her a key and directs her to obtain an envelope marked “Private Contracts” for its bearer. He flees eastward across the Sierras, is thought to have been stopped by frontier police, and may be hiding near the Summit. He also holds New Mill securities for money privately advanced to the mill. The scandal leaves him disgraced and changes how others regard Cissy.

Montagu Trixit in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Montagu Trixit, father of Cissy Trixit, is sought as a fugitive and is believed to be hiding near mountain stations or a prospector’s ruined hut. Reports say he knows dangerous information and has hidden papers with a friend. At the hut he meets Masterton, travels toward Sacramento with securities, and offers their return for renewed notes within three days. The proceedings against him are later stopped.
