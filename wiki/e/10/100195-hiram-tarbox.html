Hiram Tarbox

Abstract written by a model

Hiram Tarbox is Sophy’s husband, a cabin occupant and resident of the vicinity, later living at the Tarbox residence in San Francisco as a land and timber agent. His connection to Snapshot Harry brings constables, detectives, newspaper men, and other strangers to the cabin. Though apathetic while receiving delayed passengers, he helps remove a tree obstruction, provides hospitality, and takes pleasure in the newspaper’s praise. He directs Brice toward Harry’s hidden trail but warns him to protect himself. In San Francisco, Hiram admits destroying Brice’s letter and refusing correspondence. Having broken with Snapshot Harry, he offered to take Flora Dimwood with him, builds a land-and-timber business, and seeks to persuade Harry to sell his valley and water-power rights.

Hiram Tarbox is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BILSON'S HOUSEKEEPER FROM SAND HILL TO PINE A NIECE OF SNAPSHOT HARRY'S I There was a slight jarring though the whole frame of the coach, a grinding and hissing from the brakes, and then a sudden jolt as the vehicle ran upon and recoiled from the taut pole-straps of the now arrested horses.

Hiram is one of the cabin’s occupants and is treated as an old cousin by Flo. He and Sophy have endured visits from constables, detectives, newspaper men, and other strangers because of their connection to Snapshot Harry, and he helps receive the passengers with an apathetic manner.

II The robbery furnished the usual amount of copy for the local press.

Hiram Tarbox is delighted to read a newspaper account naming him as an esquire who helped remove the obstruction and provided hospitality to delayed passengers. He gives Brice directions to the hidden trail leading toward Harry's home, while warning him to look out for himself.

III In twenty-four hours Edward Brice was in San Francisco.

Hiram Tarbox admits that he destroyed Brice's enclosed letter and initially refused to correspond, but explains that he had broken with Snapshot Harry and offered to take Flora with him. He has become a land and timber agent in San Francisco, is building a business, and is trying to persuade Harry to sell his valley and water-power rights.
