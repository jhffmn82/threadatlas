semantic search in CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Nengbo Wang, Xiaotian Han, Jagdip Singh, Jing Ma, Vipin Chaudhary · 2025

Semantic search retrieves documents or passages from a vector database by query similarity. It can introduce bias by favoring contextually irrelevant yet similar material and missing causally relevant information; in a case study, it finds influence tactics but not how they lead to contract wins.

semantic-based retrieval in Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Xiangrong Zhu · 2025-04-29/2025-05-04

Semantic-based retrieval is an approach for retrieving isolated, relevant chunks and preparing seed chunks for subsequent knowledge graph-guided processing. It transfers a user query and chunks into high-dimensional representations with an embedding model, computes cosine similarity between the query and every chunk, and selects the top-k highest-similarity chunks as retrieved seed chunks.

Abstract

Semantic-based retrieval is used to retrieve relevant chunks and, in KG2RAG, to provide seed chunks for subsequent knowledge graph-guided processing.
