the village in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The village is a pastoral, happy place near the town, hidden under great trees and favored of the Influences. Its market-place lies on the route through it. At evening it is peaceful, prosperous, and active, until an old man warns Miss Drake and Heywood against the evil-minded West Quarter.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER XIV OFF DUTY The last of the sunlight streamed level through a gap in the western ridges.

The village is portrayed as peaceful, prosperous, and active at evening, with laborers, shops, shrines, children playing, and villagers offering warnings and neighborly conversation. An old man with a lark warns Miss Drake and Heywood not to pass through the West Quarter because it is full of evil-minded persons and its placards are posted.
