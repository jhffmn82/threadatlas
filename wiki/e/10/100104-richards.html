Abstract written by a model

Richards is the Mountain Clarion's foreman and acting pressman, a faithful henchman of Mr. Grey who works with the printing press. Suspecting that Colonel Starbottle was attacked, he investigates at the Ramierez fonda, where he is seized and nearly strangled, yet chooses not to call the sheriff. Later he invites Cota Ramierez to the composing-room and sincerely loves her, but reveals that Cota's pinto horse attacked him and that he killed it; Cota then rejects him.

Richards is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Richards, the Mountain Clarion's foreman and acting pressman, suspects that Colonel Starbottle was attacked rather than injured in an accident. While investigating the Ramierez fonda, Richards is seized from behind and nearly strangled, then resolves to handle the matter himself rather than call the sheriff.
