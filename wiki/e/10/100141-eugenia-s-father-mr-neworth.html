Abstract written by a model

Mr. Neworth, Eugenia's father, is a San Francisco capitalist and well-known investor interested in the Eureka ledge mines. He visits from Red Dog to inspect the Eureka Company's claim for possible investment, but Eugenia's fall interrupts the visit and sends him on to Sacramento without examining it. Later, regretting his brief acquaintance with Edmund Bray, he invites Bray to call at the Neworths' residence.
