Abstract written by a model

Frida Pauline Jansen is a pretty, yellow-haired Swedish chambermaid and hotel servant at the Summit House, described as a foreigner. She is seen embracing an unfamiliar young man and later emerging from the woods with Joshua Bilson, behavior that damages her reputation and suggests divided attachments while she is engaged to or courted by Chris Calton. She tends Chris in his room with gentleness and sympathy, earning his admiration and apparently gifts, and is also seen entering the gentlemen’s card-room. After Chris’s apparent courtship ends, she marries Bilson; later, at a foreign Spa, she is known as Mrs. Calton after marrying Chris. Baroness X. calls her a former peasant, widow, and remarried woman.

Frida Pauline Jansen is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
