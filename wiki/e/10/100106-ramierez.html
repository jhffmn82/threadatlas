Abstract written by a model

Ramierez is a Mexican innkeeper and round-whiskered proprietor of a fonda and tienda on a small estate, run with Senora Ramierez and associated with gambling. Suspicions connect him or his associates to nearby violence, but nothing is established. When Mr. Grey arrives, Ramierez offers perfunctory courtesy and warns that Cota’s mustang invitation is a trick.

Ramierez is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Ramierez keeps a fonda or hostelry on a small estate where gambling takes place. Richards and others suspect that Ramierez or people connected with him may have been involved in violence near the fonda, although the text does not establish this.

PART II The next afternoon he went to the fonda.

Ramierez, the round-whiskered proprietor of the fonda and tienda, welcomes Mr. Grey with perfunctory urbanity. He warns Grey that Cota's invitation to ride the mustang is a trick and says that the old vaquero is dangerous only to bulls and wild horses.
