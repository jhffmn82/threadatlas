Barney Stockdale is the astute leader of a gang, and Steve Dixie’s immediate superior. Hired by Isadora Klein to recover Douglas Maberley’s manuscript, he carries information about Mrs. Maberley’s letter; he, Susan, and his associates are pursued by police after the burglary.
