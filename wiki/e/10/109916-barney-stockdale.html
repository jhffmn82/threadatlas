Barney Stockdale in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Barney Stockdale is the astute leader of a gang, and Steve Dixie’s immediate superior. Hired by Isadora Klein to recover Douglas Maberley’s manuscript, he carries information about Mrs. Maberley’s letter; he, Susan, and his associates are pursued by police after the burglary.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Barney Stockdale is Steve Dixie's immediate superior and is described as more astute than Steve. Susan is caught speaking to him, and Holmes infers that Barney carried information about Mrs. Maberley's letter to the unknown person behind the scheme.

When I met my friend in his room early next morning, I was conscious

Barney Stockdale leads the gang that Isadora Klein hires to recover Douglas Maberley's manuscript. He and his wife Susan are said to know Klein's identity as the employer, and the police are pursuing him and his associates for the burglary.
