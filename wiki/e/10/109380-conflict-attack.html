3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

A conflict attack injects texts that conflict with other knowledge sources, potentially causing an LLM to produce vague or incorrect responses. Existing conflict attacks primarily examine context-memory conflict, which current adaptive retrievers can mitigate.

Related Work / RAG Security Evaluation Dataset .. RAG Security Evaluation Metric

Conflict attacks provide misleading or contradictory information. Existing studies often generate counterfactual perturbations with large language models, while manually constructed conflicts are described as more reliable because automated methods may alter key facts incorrectly.

Conclusion

Conflict is identified as one of the four critical attack tasks evaluated by SafeRAG.
