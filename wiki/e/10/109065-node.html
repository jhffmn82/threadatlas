Abstract written by a model

In A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models, a node represents an entity, concept, topic, or other unit of knowledge in a graph, and graph retrieval can retrieve and traverse nodes, add new nodes when knowledge is updated, and use topic nodes or anchor entities to guide retrieval. In CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation, nodes represent key entities or concepts identified in uploaded documents, with the k nodes having the smallest embedding distances to the user query selected as initial nodes and then expanded through graph edges.
