Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Douglas Maberley was Mrs. Maberley's son, a former attaché at Rome whom Holmes remembered as exceptionally energetic and impressive. He died of pneumonia the previous month after becoming cynical and morose following a damaging love affair.

When I met my friend in his room early next morning, I was conscious

Douglas Maberley had been Isadora Klein's lover and wanted to marry her. After Klein rejected him and had him beaten, he wrote a manuscript describing their relationship under different names, intending it as a threat to her reputation.
