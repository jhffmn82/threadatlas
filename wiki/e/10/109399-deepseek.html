DeepSeek in Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology

Rong Chen, Siyun Zhang, Yiyi Zheng, Qiuhua Yu and Chuhuai Wang · 2025-05-14

DeepSeek is an innovative AI model used as a comparison model in an evaluation of treatment decision-making for low back pain. Because of interface limitations, it was assessed through manual questioning and recording, then evaluated alongside CLBP-GPT, GPT-4.0, and ERNIE Bot, receiving accuracy scores of 3.81 and 3.83.
