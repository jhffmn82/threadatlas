the Peruvian wife

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The lady, a Peruvian lady, the one woman, the wife, your wife

Abstract written by a model

A beautiful Peruvian merchant’s daughter, loving and apparently devoted as a wife, has a fiercely jealous nature and curious traits. After twice assaulting Jack and being seen apparently biting her baby, she refuses to explain or speak to her husband. Confined to her room, she is cared for by her longtime maid and friend, Dolores.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

The Peruvian wife is described as beautiful, loving, devoted, and intensely jealous, but she has twice assaulted Jack and was twice seen apparently biting her own baby. She refuses to explain the alleged attacks or speak to her husband, and remains locked in her room with Dolores bringing her food.
