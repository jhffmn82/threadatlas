Abstract written by a model

Nathan Garrideb is a reclusive collector living at 136 Little Ryder Street, where he keeps a museum-like collection of geological, anatomical, historical, archaeological, and other curious specimens. Rarely leaving except for Sotheby's or Christie's, he seeks the Garrideb fortune to finance further additions. As a required adult Garrideb, he joins the inheritance search, confirms John's story, and agrees to go to Birmingham for proof of Howard Garrideb. Deceived by the three Garridebs scheme, he permits Sherlock Holmes to inspect and enter his apartment. The collapse of the inheritance hope leaves him unrecovered; he is last heard of in a Brixton nursing-home.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GARRIDEBS

Nathan Garrideb is a reclusive collector who lives at 136 Little Ryder Street and rarely leaves his rooms. He confirms John's story and hopes to receive five million dollars, which he plans to spend on specimens for his collection. Nathan agrees to travel to Birmingham to obtain proof of Howard Garrideb's existence.
