the water gate

Abstract written by a model

The water gate is a defensive position of the nunnery opening onto the river. During the siege, Gilly Forrester, converts, and others defend it against arrows and gunfire from the marsh; a fire signal is proposed but the route is judged covered. Later it is guarded, opened, and used for escape to the Hakka boat.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The water gate is defended by Gilly Forrester, converts, and others against arrows and later gunfire from the marsh. A coded message instructs the defenders to light a fire on its steps to signal the Hakka boat, but Heywood concludes that the route is covered.

CHAPTER XX THE HAKKA BOAT "Pretty fair," Captain Kneebone said.

Heywood and Gilbert Forrester guard the water gate while the escape is organized. Supplies are carried there, Captain Kneebone opens it, and the fugitives use the nearby river steps to board the Hakka boat.
