IDK

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 6 connections
Also called: IDK answer, IDK answers, IDK responses, “I don’t know"

Abstract written by a model

IDK is a replacement response for misaligned answers. Under falign filtering, unsupported responses are replaced with IDK rather than added to the selected set, raising IDK rates from 4.80% to 13.89% for misinformation and from 50.92% to 91.19% for irrelevant documents.

IDK is in one work on this site, Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

B

κ-RRSS does not add an answer to its selected set when falign labels it IDK. Filtering increases the IDK rate for misinformation documents from 4.80% to 13.89% and for irrelevant documents from 50.92% to 91.19%.

C

IDK responses increase after falign filtering because misaligned responses are replaced with IDK responses. They serve as the highlighted replacement category for responses unsupported by the retrieved documents.
