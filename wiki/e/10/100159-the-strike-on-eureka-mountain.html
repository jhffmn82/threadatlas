the strike on Eureka mountain

Also called: Parkhurst's discovery, discovery, special Providence, strike, the first strike on Eureka mountain, the gold discovery

Abstract written by a model

The strike on Eureka mountain begins when Eugenia Neworth, Edmund Bray's declared new partner, makes the first strike near the spring in decomposed quartz. Parkhurst then finds gold after widening a crack with his clasp knife. The partners prospect the area and determine it is a major, or “big,” strike.
