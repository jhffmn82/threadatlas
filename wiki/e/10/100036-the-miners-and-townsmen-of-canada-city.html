show_homage_to Cissy and Piney [spontaneously in the streets] The miners and townsmen of Canada City enthusiastically honor Cissy and Piney as they pass, removing obstructions, taking off their hats, and interrupting their work or horseplay to watch them. Later, groups of men gather around the bank, newspaper office, and a newspaper extra, becoming so preoccupied that they avoid noticing Cissy.
