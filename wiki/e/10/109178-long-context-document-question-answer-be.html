Related Work

Long context document question-answer benchmarks differ in content types, multi-document capabilities, and domains. L-Eval, Marathon, and LooGLE primarily use text-based, multi-domain content without multi-document inputs; LongBench and Loong support multi-document settings but remain text-centric. MPDocVQA, UDA, and MMLONGBENCH-DOC include tables, charts, and slides but are limited to single-document question answering.
