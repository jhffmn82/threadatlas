response aggregation

process · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: aggregation, aggregation process

Abstract written by a model

Response aggregation combines source-specific responses through weighted majority voting. It addresses hallucinations, document misalignment, and paraphrased equivalents by filtering insufficiently aligned responses to IDK, clustering semantically equivalent responses, choosing the cluster with greatest total source weight, and returning that cluster’s first response.

Problem Formulation / Standard RAG .. Multi-source RAG with source reliability

Response aggregation combines the source-specific responses for weighted majority voting. It is complicated by LLM hallucinations, answers that are misaligned with retrieved documents, and semantically identical responses expressed through paraphrasing.

Method: RA-RAG .. Iterative reliability estimation

The aggregation process filters responses that are not sufficiently aligned with the query and retrieved documents, clusters semantically equivalent responses, and selects the cluster with the greatest total source weight. The first response in the selected cluster is used as the final response.
