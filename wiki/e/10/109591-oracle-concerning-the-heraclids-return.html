Abstract written by a model

Oracle concerning the Heraclids' return warns against a premature return, marked by plague in Peloponnese and Hippotes' ten-year banishment; it requires the third crop, explained as the third generation, and identifies the narrows as the broad-bellied sea right of the Isthmus.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, II. vir. 2

The oracle first says that the Heraclids should await the third crop. It later explains that this means the third generation rather than three years, and that the narrows refer to the broad-bellied sea on the right of the Isthmus.
