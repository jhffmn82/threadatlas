F1 (avg)

metric · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: F1(avg)

Abstract written by a model

F1(avg) is a generation safety metric for RAG in the inter-context conflict task, calculated as the average of F1(correct) and F1(incorrect). Higher values mean better distinction of correct and incorrect options and stronger security; it declines with more noise, DoS attacks, and SKR’s information loss.

F1 (avg) is in one work on this site, SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
