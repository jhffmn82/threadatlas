Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

The Peruvian wife has assaulted Jack twice without an apparent provocation, once striking him with a stick and once beating him with her hands. She has said that she hates him, but neither Jack nor the husband provides a clear explanation for the attacks.
