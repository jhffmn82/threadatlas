Abstract written by a model

The Triad is a secret band or society with protective signs and ritual. Its Heaven-and-Earth sign, including a marked leg, identifies members in the streets. Wutzler’s wife’s cousin is a Grass Sandal; members claim peaceful intent, though some oppose the White Lotus and admit Heywood through the first gate.
