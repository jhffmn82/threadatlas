Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The video frame selection mechanism extracts the most informative subset of frames from videos. It is introduced because Large Video Language Models may not have sufficient context size for all frames in extremely long videos and because some frames are more important than others.

Introduction

The frame selection model is introduced to extract the most informative subset of frames from long and redundant videos. It is trained to maximize retrieval and generation performance while reducing the amount of video content processed by Large Video Language Models.

Method .. 2.4 / Auxiliary Text Generation

Adaptive frame selection extracts an informative and computationally feasible subset of frames instead of processing every frame or uniformly sampling them. It selects the subset with the highest score, using query-guided selection during generation.

5

Adaptive frame selection enables VideoRAG to use only the most informative subset of full frames, improving effectiveness and efficiency.

Limitations

The frame selection strategy narrows the frames of a given video to a small subset, greatly improving the efficiency of video processing for retrieval and generation as well as its effectiveness. Further improving its efficacy by maximizing effectiveness and efficiency is proposed as future work.
