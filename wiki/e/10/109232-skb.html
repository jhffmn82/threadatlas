SKB in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

SKB is a semi-structured knowledge base that combines a structured knowledge graph, including entities, relations, and types, with unstructured text documents associated with graph entities. Its documents are interconnected by relations and provide textual information alongside graph information. SKB serves as the knowledge source for hybrid question answering, where questions may require both relational and textual evidence. HYBGRAG operates over SKB by retrieving references from both the graph and documents for hybrid retrieval, or from documents alone for text retrieval, distinguishing this setting from prior Graph RAG approaches.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The semi-structured knowledge base contains text documents interconnected by relations. It serves as the knowledge source for questions that may require both textual and relational information.

opening

A semi-structured knowledge base consists of a structured knowledge base, such as a knowledge graph, and unstructured text documents associated with knowledge-graph entities. HYBGRAG is designed to answer hybrid questions over this combination of data.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

A semi-structured knowledge base consists of a knowledge graph G = (E, R), entity-associated text documents, and entity and relation types. It is the setting in which hybrid question answering combines relational and textual information.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

SKB contains graph and document information used by HYBGRAG. The selected retrieval module retrieves references from both G and D in SKB for hybrid retrieval, or only D for text retrieval.

5

The paper addresses HQA in SKB, distinguishing its setting from prior Graph RAG methods that focus on KBQA, ODQA, or answering questions from a given subgraph.
