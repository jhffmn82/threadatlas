Also called: Κρηθέα, Κρηθέως, Κρηθεὺς, Κρηθεῖ

Abstract written by a model

Brother of Salmoneus, Cretheus raised and married Tyro, founded Pherae and Iolcus, and fathered Aeson, Amythaon, and Pheres.

Cretheus is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
