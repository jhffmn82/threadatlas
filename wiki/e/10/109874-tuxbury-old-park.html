Tuxbury Old Park is the remote Emsworth family property near Bedford, about five miles from the nearest station. Identified in Dodd's letter, it is where Dodd and Holmes investigate Godfrey Emsworth's disappearance and concealment, finding the Emsworth mansion and the detached house where Godfrey lives.
