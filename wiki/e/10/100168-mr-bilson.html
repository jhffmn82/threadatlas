Mr. Bilson in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Mr. Bilson is a hotel landlord and proprietor who owns or manages Summit House and normally manages the Summit Hotel. After losing his wife, he hires Miss Euphemia Trotter as housekeeper to manage the hotel with him, treating her with awe, respect, and reverence. He is later discovered privately with Frida Pauline Jansen, first offering an embarrassed explanation and then telling Miss Trotter that he will marry Frida in San Francisco. He promises that the marriage will elevate Miss Trotter to full control of the establishment while he and Frida go abroad. He marries Frida, remains absent for a year, and returns to take charge after Miss Trotter resigns; she sends him a letter addressed to Paris before his return.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Mr. Bilson's Housekeeper

Joshua Bilson, of the Summit House, loses his wife and hires Miss Euphemia Trotter to manage the hotel with him. He stands in awe of Trotter, speaks of her with respect and reverence, and later is found in the card-room with Frida Pauline Jansen, offering an embarrassed and unconvincing explanation.

II One afternoon Miss Trotter received a message that Mr.

Mr. Bilson is the proprietor of the hotel and is found privately with Frida in the Summit woods. He tells Miss Trotter that he intends to marry Frida in San Francisco and that their marriage will not diminish Miss Trotter's position, but will promote her to entire control of the establishment. He and Frida plan to go abroad after the wedding.

III When the news of the marriage reached Buckeye Hill, it did not, however, make much scandal, owing, possibly, to the scant number of the sex who are apt to disseminate it, and to many the name of Miss Jansen was unknown.

Mr. Bilson marries Frida and is absent from the Summit Hotel for a year. He later returns to take charge of the hotel after Miss Trotter resigns, and Miss Trotter sends him a letter addressed to Paris before that return.
