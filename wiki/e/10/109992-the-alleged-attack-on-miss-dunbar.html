the alleged attack on Miss Dunbar in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The alleged attack on Miss Dunbar is the case leading to her murder charge and referral to the Assizes at Winchester. Coroner's-jury and police-court proceedings found the evidence persuasive, including a revolver hidden in her wardrobe and a note left at the scene. Miss Dunbar reserves her defence and is represented by Joyce Cummings. Neil Gibson hires Holmes, whose reconstruction suggests the Gold King's wife threatened Miss Dunbar, a scuffle followed, and the shooting was accidental.

THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

The case has been referred to the Assizes at Winchester after a coroner's jury and police-court proceedings found the evidence against Miss Dunbar persuasive. Neil Gibson hires Holmes to seek new facts that might establish her innocence.

To my surprise the Gold King took the reproof with equanimity.

The alleged attack may have involved the Gold King's wife threatening Miss Dunbar with a gun, followed by a scuffle and an accidental shooting. The Gold King's wife denies the attack, while Mr. Holmes says her denial is not conclusive.

Miss Dunbar faces a murder charge, with the available evidence apparently against her, including the pistol hidden in her wardrobe and the note she left at the scene. Holmes's reconstruction is intended to secure her vindication.
