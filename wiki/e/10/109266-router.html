Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

The router is an LLM that performs question routing for HYBGRAG. It identifies topic entities and useful relations, selects the text or hybrid retrieval module, and refines these decisions using critic feedback when retrieval is rejected.

opening .. 4.3

The router selects and refines retrieval decisions with feedback from the critic module. In the STARK-MAG example, it initially misidentifies “optical TALU implementations in electronic circuits” as a relational topic entity and then addresses the critic's feedback by revising that decision.

C.2

The router extracts entities and relations, chooses between the knowledge graph and text documents, and classifies CRAG questions by type, dynamism, and domain. Its reflection prompts ask it to reconsider incorrect predictions or references.
