the spring in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

A spring in the redwood forest near a quartz outcrop, visible in its depths, where Fleming washes soil pans yielding only scant color and no substantial discovery.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART II Mr.

The spring is near the outcrop and is used by Fleming to wash several pans of soil. It yields only a few pin-points of color and does not confirm a substantial discovery.
