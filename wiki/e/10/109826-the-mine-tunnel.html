Abstract written by a model

The mine tunnel is a damp, narrow underground passage through wet earth from the shaft toward the clay-pit. It contains a chamber with powder sacks and burden-carriers’ niches; Heywood and Rudolph use it to reach the enemy’s position amid danger.

CHAPTER XIX BROTHER MOLES This new danger, working below in the solid earth, had thrown Rudolph into a state of sullen resignation.

The mine tunnel is a damp, narrow passage running through wet earth from the shaft toward the clay-pit. It contains a chamber, powder sacks, and niches for those carrying burdens, and it is used by Heywood and Rudolph to reach the enemy's position.
