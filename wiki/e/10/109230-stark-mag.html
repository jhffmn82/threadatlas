STARK-MAG in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

STARK-MAG is a STARK dataset with an academic knowledge graph, unstructured documents for some entity types, and a testing set of 2665 questions. It includes a retriever-bank router and interacting critic module; their examples show an interpretable, chain-of-thought-like refinement path. HYBGRAG performs better on STARK-MAG than on STARK-Prime, and its academic domain is less complex than the medicinal domain.

STARK-MAG provides examples of interactions between the router in the retriever bank and the critic module. Its refinement path is described as similar to chain-of-thought, making the process interpretable and easy for users to understand.

Limitations

HYBGRAG performs better on STARK-MAG than on STARK-Prime. The academic domain represented by STARK-MAG is described as generally less complex than the medicinal domain.

STARK-MAG in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

STARK-MAG is a STARK benchmark evaluation subset and Hybrid Question Answering dataset. It is used to compare textual and graph retrievers, measure subgraph extraction, and evaluate retrieval. Optimal routing reaches Hit@1 0.4522 and Hit@5 0.7463; HYBGRAG reports 47% relative Hit@1 improvement and strong retrieval metrics.
