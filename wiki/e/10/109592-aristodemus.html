Aristodemus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Aristodemus marries Argia, fathers twin sons Eurysthenes and Procles, and is killed by a thunderbolt at Naupactus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, II. vir. 2

Aristodemus is killed by a thunderbolt while the army is at Naupactus. He leaves twin sons, Eurysthenes and Procles, by Argia.
