filter in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The filter processes retrieved texts before they are synthesized into a response. Simple safety filters can remove some existing noise and refusal signals, but the proposed silver noise and white DoS attacks are designed to bypass such defenses.

Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

Filters did not effectively resist silver noise when it was injected into retrieved or filtered context. NLI filtering was generally ineffective for toxicity and DoS attacks, whereas SKR was less secure for conflict attacks but safe for toxicity and DoS attacks.

Preliminaries and Definitions / RAG Pipeline

The filter refines the retrieved contexts by selecting or compressing them into contexts that are highly relevant to the query. It produces m contexts, where m is less than or equal to k.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the

The filter can be attacked by inserting malicious contexts into the retriever's output. The filter may treat the injected texts as part of the retrieved contexts.
