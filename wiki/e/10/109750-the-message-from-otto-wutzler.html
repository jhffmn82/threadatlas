the message from Otto Wutzler in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A serious coded message, signed O. W. and carried by arrow during a siege, directs defenders to light a fire at the water-gate steps to summon a waiting Hakka boat. Its later fragment urges them to listen, work quickly, use many bags, and keep watch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

The coded message is carried on an arrow and signed with marks identified as O. W. It says that a Hakka boat waits across the river, gives the signal for summoning it, and later another fragment warns the defenders to listen, work fast, use many bags, and watch closely.
