text-based graph in CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Nengbo Wang, Xiaotian Han, Jagdip Singh, Jing Ma, Vipin Chaudhary · 2025

A structured text-based graph comprises nodes and edges extracted from uploaded documents: entities or concepts become nodes and inferred relationships become edges. It is embedded and stored in a vector database for similarity retrieval. In the case study, expert validation supports a causal summary from influence tactics through buyer attention to contract award; CausalRAG uses such graphs, with scalability remaining future work.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology .. Retrieving Context Causally

The text-based graph is constructed from unstructured uploaded documents by identifying key entities or concepts as nodes and inferring relationships as edges. It is embedded and stored in a vector database for similarity-based retrieval.

Experiment / Experimental Setup .. Parameter Study

A causal graph summary preserves major causal pathways for retrieval. In the case study, it represents the pathway from influence tactics through buyer attention to contract award.

Conclusion and Future Work

Causal graphs are used by CausalRAG to retrieve causally grounded context. Future work would need scalable methods for constructing and processing numerous high-dimensional graphs derived from large-scale documents.
