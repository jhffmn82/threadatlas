Abstract written by a model

Thestius, son of Demonice and Ares, fathered daughters Althaea, Leda, and Hypermnestra and sons Iphiclus, Evippus, Plexippus, and Eurypylus by Eurythemis; after his sons claimed a boar skin, Meleager killed them.

Thestius is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Thestius had daughters named Althaea, Leda, and Hypermnestra, and sons named Iphiclus, Evippus, Plexippus, and Eurypylus by Eurythemis. His sons later claimed the boar skin, and Meleager killed them after they took it from Atalanta.
