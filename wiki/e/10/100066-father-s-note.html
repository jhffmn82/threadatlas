father's note in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

A handwritten note from Cissy’s father instructs Cissy Trixit to retrieve his “Private Contracts” envelope and deliver it to the bearer; it reaches her through Ah Fe after Chinese messengers pass it along.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then, in a revulsion of feeling lest any one should find her there, she ran upst

The note tells Cissy to take the envelope marked "Private Contracts" from the second drawer of her father's desk and give it to the bearer. It reaches her through Ah Fe after being passed among Chinese messengers.
