round-trip consistency filtering

method · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Round-trip Consistency, round-trip consistency, round-trip consistency filtering technique

Abstract written by a model

Round-trip consistency filtering improves synthetic question-answer quality by retaining generated pairs or pseudo-labeled tuples only when retrieval returns the original context among top results; retained tuples include a question, documents, and an answer for further fine-tuning.
