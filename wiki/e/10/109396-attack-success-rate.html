Attack Success Rate in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xun Liang, Simin Niu, Zhiyu Li, Sensen Zhang, Hanyu Wang, Feiyu Xiong, Jason Zhaoxin Fan, Bo Tang, Jihao Zhao, Jiawei Yang, Shichao Song, Mengwei Wang · 2025

Attack Success Rate is a generator safety metric for conflict, toxicity, and DoS tasks, used by PoisonedRAG and SafeRAG. It rises as attack keywords make up more of a response, and it has high consistency with human judgments. In the reported comparison, it is 100 for Soft Ad, while Silver Noise has no reported value.
