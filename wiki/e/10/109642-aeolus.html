Aeolus

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Αἰόλου, Αἴολος

Abstract written by a model

Aeolus, son of Hellen and Orseis, ruled the regions around Thessaly and named their inhabitants Aeolians. With his wife Enarete, he had seven sons, Cretheus, Sisyphus, Athamas, Salmoneus, Deion, Magnes, and Perieres, and five daughters: Canace, Alcyone, Pisidice, Calyce, and Perimede.

Aeolus is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
