intelligence systems in CLERC: A Dataset for U. S. Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Analysis Generation

Abe Bohan Hou · 2025

Intelligence systems assist legal professionals in writing documents and could provide major benefits. To be useful, they must locate, summarize, and reason over salient precedents, making them challenging to design. Evaluated systems, including fine-tuned ones, are not currently well suited to these tasks. Built on case-law data, they also risk propagating historical biases and ideologies.
