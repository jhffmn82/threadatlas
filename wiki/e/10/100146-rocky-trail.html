rocky trail in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Rocky trail is a narrow, precipitous route descending from the mining camp toward the stage road and continuing along the mountainside to the spring. Its lower section risks a fall into pine trees below. Bray recklessly hurries down it for water, then laboriously climbs back carrying a pail.

The rocky trail descends from the mining camp toward the stage road and continues along the mountainside to the spring. Its lower section is narrow and precipitous, with a misstep threatening a fall into the pine trees far below.
