retriever bank in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

The retriever bank is a retrieval system component in HYBGRAG that simultaneously leverages textual and relational information for textual, relational, and hybrid questions. Composed of multiple retrieval modules and a router, it addresses Challenge 1 by selecting a strategy, routing questions to hybrid or text retrieval, and producing top-K references. It also supplies resources for ReAct and Corrective RAG; in STARK-MAG, its router works with the critic module to refine decisions.

opening

HYBGRAG uses a retriever bank that leverages textual and relational information simultaneously. The retriever bank supports a unified approach to textual, relational, and hybrid questions.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

The retriever bank addresses Challenge 1 and is composed of multiple retrieval modules and a router. It selects a retrieval strategy and produces top-K references for a question.

4 .. 4.1.2

The retriever bank routes each question to either the hybrid retrieval module or the text retrieval module. It is one of the mechanisms HYBGRAG uses to address different retrieval needs.

opening .. 4.3

The retriever bank supplies retrieval modules used by HYBGRAG and by the ReAct and Corrective RAG baselines. In STARK-MAG, its router interacts with the critic module to refine retrieval decisions.
