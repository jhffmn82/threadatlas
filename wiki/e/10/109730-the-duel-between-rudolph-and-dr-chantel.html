the duel between Rudolph and Dr. Chantel in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The planned sword duel follows Rudolph’s quarrel with Dr. Chantel and Rudolph’s strike. Chantel is expected to choose swords; Heywood calls it plain murder because Chantel is a remarkable swordsman and Rudolph admits no knowledge of swordsmanship, though Rudolph sees possible escape.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII THE HOT NIGHT Rudolph paced his long chamber like a wolf,--a wolf in summer, with too thick a coat.

The duel is proposed after Rudolph strikes Dr. Chantel, with Chantel expected to choose the weapons. Heywood says Chantel will choose swords because he is a remarkable swordsman, while Rudolph reveals that he has no knowledge of swordsmanship and sees the duel as a possible escape.
