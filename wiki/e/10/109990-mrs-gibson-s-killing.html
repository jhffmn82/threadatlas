Mrs. Gibson's killing in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Mrs. Gibson's killing is a tragedy at Thor Bridge involving the death of Neil Gibson's wife, Maria Pinto. Found late at night in dinner dress and a shawl, she had been shot behind the right temple at close range; no weapon or signs of struggle were present. Although staged as a murder to implicate Miss Dunbar, the death is determined to have been a carefully planned suicide: after confronting Miss Dunbar, Mrs. Gibson fatally shot herself and disposed of the pistol in the water beside the bridge.
