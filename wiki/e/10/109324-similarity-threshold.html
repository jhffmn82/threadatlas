Also called: chunking threshold, different thresholds, dynamic threshold, fixed one, threshold, τ
  • In Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation: The similarity threshold is a dynamic chunking condition set to the mean similarity between a document’s sentences and its summary. It controls chunk relevance: higher thresholds select fewer, shorter relevant chunks. Experiments from two standard deviations below to above the mean show the mean performs best for retrieval and question answering; overly high or low thresholds mix relevance or approach no chunking.

The similarity threshold is a dynamic chunking condition set to the mean similarity between a document’s sentences and its summary. It controls chunk relevance: higher thresholds select fewer, shorter relevant chunks. 3 / Methodology .. 3.2 / Chunking Based on Pseudo-Instructions

The similarity threshold controls the chunking condition. It is dynamically set to the mean similarity between all sentences in document D and its summary, rather than being fixed across documents.

6 / Further Analysis / 6.1 / Impacts of the Pseudo-Instruction .. 6.4 / Chunk Analysis

The chunking threshold is based on the mean similarity between sentences and the summary, with experiments ranging from the mean minus two standard deviations to the mean plus two standard deviations. The mean threshold performs best, while thresholds that are too high or too low mix relevant and irrelevant sentences more or reduce the method toward no chunking.
