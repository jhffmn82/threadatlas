question generation in SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains

Ran Xu, Hui Liu, Sreyashi Nag, Zhenwei Dai, Yaochen Xie, Xianfeng Tang, Chen Luo, Yang Li, Joyce C. Ho, Carl Yang, Qi He · 2025

Question generation is a SimRAG capability complementary to question answering: prompted with unlabeled, domain-specific context, it produces diverse grounded questions, reinforcing context-based answering.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Question generation is a capability that SimRAG develops alongside question answering. The model is prompted to generate diverse, domain-relevant questions from unlabeled corpora.

Introduction

Question generation is one of the two complementary tasks performed by the SimRAG model. The model generates questions grounded in domain-specific context, and this capability reinforces its ability to answer questions using context.
