Large Video Language Models in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Soyeong Jeong · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where Large Video Language Models first appears

Abstract written by a model

Large Video Language Models are multimodal language models built on Large Language Models that directly process video content. With vision and text encoders, they jointly represent visual frames and associated textual data, including temporal, spatial, and multimodal information. They power VideoRAG by representing videos for retrieval and integrating retrieved videos with queries during response generation. Their development extends from object and action detection to spatio-temporal analysis, event-sequence prediction, causal inference, and context-aware descriptions.

LVLMs in VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus

Soyeong Jeong · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where LVLMs first appears

Abstract written by a model

LVLMs directly process video and handle action detection, spatio-temporal analysis, event-sequence prediction, causal inference, and context-aware descriptions, including in zero-shot settings. They power VideoRAG’s generation process, though their potential in RAG had not been fully explored. Trained on vast multimodal datasets, they may inherit and amplify training-data biases.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4 / Retrieval-Augmented Generation .. Large Video Language Models

LVLMs can directly process video content and perform increasingly challenging video-understanding tasks, including spatio-temporal analysis, event-sequence prediction, causal inference, and context-aware description generation. Their potential in RAG had not been fully explored, motivating VideoRAG.

Ethics Statement

LVLMs power the generation process for VideoRAG. Because they are trained on vast multimodal datasets, their responses may inherit and amplify biases present in their training data.
