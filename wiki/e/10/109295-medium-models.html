Abstract written by a model

Medium models are large language models including Gemma2:9b, Llama3:8b, and Llama3.1:8b. They strongly deliver answer faithfulness and relevance relative to size, balancing efficiency and response quality for moderately complex tasks, though with lower context relevance than large models.

IV.

Medium models, including Gemma2:9b, Llama3:8b, and Llama3.1:8b, perform strongly relative to their parameter sizes, especially in answer faithfulness and answer relevance. They offer a compromise between response quality and computational efficiency, although their context relevance is somewhat lower than that of large models.

V.

Medium-sized models demonstrated a balance between efficiency and performance, making them suitable for moderately complex tasks.
