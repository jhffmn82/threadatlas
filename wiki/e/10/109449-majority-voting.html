majority voting

method · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: MV, final decision voting, majority vote

Abstract written by a model

In MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health, majority voting is applied to binary yes/no decisions extracted from candidate answers, retaining responses matching the dominant label before clustering and synthesizing the final answer. In Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability, MV is an accuracy-comparison baseline and response-aggregation method for multi-source queries, giving all sources equal weight and selecting the response with the most consensus.

MV in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where MV first appears

Abstract written by a model

MV is an accuracy-comparison baseline and response-aggregation method for multi-source queries. It gives all sources equal weight and selects the response with the most consensus. Across evaluation and ablation, it fails when correct answers are less frequent than incorrect ones: misinformation can win, and accuracy declines as adversarial sources outnumber trustworthy sources.

Experiments / Experimental setups .. Ablation studies and analysis

MV assigns equal weight to all sources and disregards source reliability. It can select misinformation when incorrect answers dominate by frequency, and its performance significantly degrades when adversarial sources outnumber trustworthy sources.
