the stagecoach

vehicle · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: The huge vehicle, coach, stage, stage road, stagecoach, that coach, the coach, the stage, the up coach, the vehicle

Abstract written by a model

The stagecoach is a large coach that carries passengers, express cargo, an express box, and greenbacks along the road, including a bridge high above the North Fork canyon. Associated with Yuba Bill as his old coach, it had been held up near a cabin six months earlier. On a rainy night, a fallen tree stops it near that earlier robbery site; passengers and road workers clear the obstruction, allowing it to continue through a narrow opening in the split pine. Masked robbers then take the express box, after which Yuba Bill drives it furiously three miles to the first station and it later leaves with its passengers. Accounts also say two passenger-robbers first rob it for the greenbacks, then Henry's gang stops it, making it robbed twice within half an hour. A newspaper reports that Hiram Tarbox's warning may have prevented a crash into the tree.
