Montagu Trixit's bank

Also called: Canada City Bank, Canada City bank, HIS OWN bank, her father's bank, the Bank, the bank, the masther's bank

Abstract written by a model

Montagu Trixit's bank is a bank owned or run by Trixit, identified as the Canada City Bank and closely associated with him as its principal banker. A crowd gathers around it as concern grows, and a newspaper extra announces a run during the father's absence. Depositors, including Norah, withdraw funds amid accusations that Cissy's father stole money and fled. The bank fails amid reported defalcations, whose amount may have been exaggerated. Its most consequential unresolved issue is whether Trixit will declare securities he holds for the New Mill as bank assets.

Montagu Trixit's bank is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
