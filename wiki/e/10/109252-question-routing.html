Question routing is a retrieval-module selection process that identifies a question’s textual and relational aspects, including topic entities and useful relations, to determine which module to use and how. It distinguishes textual topics from relations and selects text or hybrid retrieval. In STARK-Prime, HYBGRAG refines routing through self-reflection and validation feedback, changing text retrieval to hybrid retrieval when needed.

opening

Question routing is used to identify the textual and relational aspects of a question. The unit explains that incorrect routing can confuse the textual aspect, such as nanofluid heat transfer papers, with the relational aspect, such as by John Smith; HYBGRAG refines this routing through self-reflection.

Question routing is the process of determining which retrieval module to use and how to use it for a question. It includes identifying topic entities and useful relations before selecting a text or hybrid retrieval module.
