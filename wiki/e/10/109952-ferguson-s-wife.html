Ferguson's wife is found feverish, distressed, and unwilling to see her husband after he accused or suspected her. She had sucked poison from the baby's wound to save the child, but concealed the truth because she feared the effect on Ferguson. She confirms Holmes's explanation and is cleared of the apparent attempt to harm the baby.
