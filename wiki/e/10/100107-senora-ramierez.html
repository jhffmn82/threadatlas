Abstract written by a model

Senora Ramierez, wife of Ramierez, is an innkeeper at the Ramierez fonda and mother of Cota. She is described as coquettish and charming, offers Colonel Starbottle Cota's mustang, then welcomes Mr. Grey and introduces him to Cota, newly returned from four years at the convent in San Jose.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Senora Ramierez is described as a woman with coquettish behavior and considerable physical charms. Colonel Starbottle says that after playful badinage she offered him the use of her daughter's mustang.

PART II The next afternoon he went to the fonda.

Senora Ramierez welcomes Mr. Grey and tells him that Cota has recently returned from four years at the convent in San Jose. She explains that Cota cares chiefly for riding and leads Grey outside to see her daughter and the horse.
