Abstract written by a model

John Garrideb presents himself to Holmes as an American counsellor at law from Moorville, Kansas, organizing a search for two other adult Garridebs so they can claim Alexander Hamilton Garrideb's inheritance. He advertises for Howard Garrideb and persuades Nathan Garrideb to go to Birmingham. Holmes notes his English clothing, London accent, and false details, concludes that he is an American criminal using a fraudulent identity, and identifies him as Killer Evans. The inheritance scheme is revealed as a device to remove Nathan from his apartment; John’s ultimate purpose is investigated.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GARRIDEBS

John Garrideb, who presents himself as an American counsellor at law from Moorville, Kansas, tells Holmes that he must find two other adult Garridebs to obtain Alexander Hamilton Garrideb's inheritance. Holmes observes that John is wearing English clothing, has a smoothed London accent, and has lied about several details. John produces an advertisement for Howard Garrideb and persuades Nathan to travel to Birmingham to meet him.
