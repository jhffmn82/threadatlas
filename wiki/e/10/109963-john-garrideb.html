THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GARRIDEBS

John Garrideb, who presents himself as an American counsellor at law from Moorville, Kansas, tells Holmes that he must find two other adult Garridebs to obtain Alexander Hamilton Garrideb's inheritance. Holmes observes that John is wearing English clothing, has a smoothed London accent, and has lied about several details. John produces an advertisement for Howard Garrideb and persuades Nathan to travel to Birmingham to meet him.
