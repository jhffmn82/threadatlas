the Jallinger cabin in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The Jallinger cabin is a rambling log dwelling with sheds in an isolated green valley near the Run, spring, and rocky outcrop. Surrounded by a clearing and rough fenced cultivation, it displays drying animal skins and bird wings. Fleming visits for a pan and road information, then worries that a permanent claim nearby may offend Katinka's father, who plans to move farther into the mountains.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" A TREASURE OF THE REDWOODS PART I Mr.

The Jallinger cabin stands in an isolated green valley surrounded by a clearing and a roughly fenced cultivated area. Its exterior and fence are covered with the drying skins of animals and displayed wings of birds. Fleming visits it to borrow a pan and inquire about the road.

PART II Mr.

The Jallinger cabin is near the spring and outcrop, and Fleming worries that locating a permanent claim there might offend Katinka's father. Katinka's father later plans to move farther into the mountains.
