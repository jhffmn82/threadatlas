Dr. Earle in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where Dr. Earle first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dr. Earle, identified as the Reverend James Earle, is a white-haired doctor, padre, and clergyman married to Mrs. Earle. An English-speaking expatriate at the club, he leads a Christian flock of native converts and serves at a nunnery; his hospital work is praised by the magistrate. Heywood calls him one of the district’s two most dangerous men. As danger threatens his flock and Chok Chung, Earle agonizes over whom to protect. During the siege, he recognizes a scroll message, helps Heywood decipher it with the Triad cipher, and learns that a Hakka boat waits across the river. He stays with a dying convert, buries him, and permits the others to leave.

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV THE SWORD-PEN "Wutzler was missing last night," said Heywood, lazily.

Heywood identifies the Reverend James Earle as the other most dangerous man in the district. He and Rudolph intend to find Earle that night and report to him.

" CHAPTER XII THE WAR BOARD "Rigmarole?

Dr. Earle listens as Heywood reports that the magistrate spoke highly of his hospital work. He is deeply troubled by the danger facing his flock and Chok Chung, and asks what he must choose between protecting his people and protecting the others.

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

Dr. Earle examines a scroll attached to a blunt arrow and recognizes it as a serious message. With Heywood, he deciphers it using a Triad cipher and determines that a Hakka boat is waiting across the river.

CHAPTER XX THE HAKKA BOAT "Pretty fair," Captain Kneebone said.

The padre remains with a wounded convert who is dying and cannot be ferried away. After the convert dies, he conducts the burial, then tells the others that they may leave.
