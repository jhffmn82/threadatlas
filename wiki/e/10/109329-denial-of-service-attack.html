Denial-of-Service attack in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xun Liang, Simin Niu, Zhiyu Li, Sensen Zhang, Hanyu Wang, Feiyu Xiong, Jason Zhaoxin Fan, Bo Tang, Jihao Zhao, Jiawei Yang, Shichao Song, Mengwei Wang · 2025

Denial-of-Service attack is an attack task and one of SafeRAG’s four attack-task categories. It aims to make an LLM refuse to answer despite available evidence, preventing useful responses. Unlike traditional approaches that insert refusal signals that may be filtered or ignored, the white form targets the original comprehensive question, adds rule-based refusal contexts, and fabricates a safety warning that falsely says retrieved evidence contains distorted facts. This accusation is used to induce refusal to answer.

DoS in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xun Liang, Simin Niu, Zhiyu Li, Sensen Zhang, Hanyu Wang, Feiyu Xiong, Jason Zhaoxin Fan, Bo Tang, Jihao Zhao, Jiawei Yang, Shichao Song, Mengwei Wang · 2025

DoS is a SafeRAG attack category and one of four critical tasks. Injected into accessible texts in a RAG pipeline, its White Denial-of-Service construction presents a false safety warning to induce refusal. In evaluation, it reduces average F1 and severely reduces generation diversity; DPR is more vulnerable than other retrievers, SKR is considered safe, and the NLI filter is generally ineffective. Baichuan 13B particularly excels on this task.

White DoS in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xun Liang, Simin Niu, Zhiyu Li, Sensen Zhang, Hanyu Wang, Feiyu Xiong, Jason Zhaoxin Fan, Bo Tang, Jihao Zhao, Jiawei Yang, Shichao Song, Mengwei Wang · 2025

White DoS is a novel denial-of-service attack strategy, task, and SafeRAG attack type. It disguises a refusal signal as a white safety warning that falsely claims evidence contains distorted facts, aiming to bypass filters or generators and cause refusal. It generates a warning-based attack context from a question, combines it with complete golden contexts to construct DWDoS, and is evaluated with proposition-based multiple-choice questions containing correct and deliberately distorted options. It exposes gaps in existing defenses.
