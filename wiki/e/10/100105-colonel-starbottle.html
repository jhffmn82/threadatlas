Abstract written by a model

Colonel Starbottle, a war-horse of the Democracy, carries a Derringer and is a dead shot. After being thrown from a bolting, buck-jumping mustang, he recovers at the Palmetto Hotel and denies attack. Later, it emerges he was attacked near the Ramierez fonda and narrowly escaped strangling, with Cota’s pinto identified as the attacker.
