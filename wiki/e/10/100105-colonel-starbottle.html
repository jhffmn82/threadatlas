Colonel Starbottle in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Colonel Starbottle, a war-horse of the Democracy, carries a Derringer and is a dead shot. After being thrown from a bolting, buck-jumping mustang, he recovers at the Palmetto Hotel and denies attack. Later, it emerges he was attacked near the Ramierez fonda and narrowly escaped strangling, with Cota’s pinto identified as the attacker.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Colonel Starbottle says he was thrown when a mustang bolted and buck-jumped after he left the Ramierez rancho. He denies being attacked, but becomes uneasy when Mr. Grey mentions that some people believe he was strangled with a lasso.

Colonel Starbottle was previously attacked near the Ramierez fonda and narrowly escaped being strangled. Richards identifies his attacker as Cota's pinto horse.
