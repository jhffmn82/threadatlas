Mrs. Mason in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Mrs. Mason is Ferguson's baby's devoted nurse. She knows of the attempted poisoning, claims to have seen the Peruvian wife apparently bite the child, tells Ferguson, and refuses to leave the baby night or day. During the child's separation from Ferguson's wife, she continues caring for and protecting it after being told to watch it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

Mrs. Mason is the baby's nurse and claims to have seen the Peruvian wife apparently bite the child. She tells the husband about the incident and swears that she will not leave the baby, whom she considers herself responsible for protecting.

It was evening of a dull, foggy November day when, having left our bags

Mrs. Mason cares for Ferguson's baby while the child remains away from Ferguson's wife. Holmes learns that she knew about the danger and had been told to watch the child.
