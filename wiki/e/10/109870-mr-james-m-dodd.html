Mr. James M. Dodd in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

James M. Dodd is a big, fresh, sunburned, upstanding Briton, a Throgmorton Street stockbroker and former Imperial Yeoman of the Middlesex Corps during the Boer War. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLANCHED SOLDIER

James M. Dodd, a former Imperial Yeoman of the Middlesex Corps, visits Holmes to seek an explanation for Godfrey Emsworth's disappearance. He had served with Godfrey in South Africa, visited Tuxbury Old Hall, challenged Colonel Emsworth, and resolved to discover what had happened to his friend. While staying at the Hall, he hears Ralph imply that Godfrey is dead or worse, then sees Godfrey suddenly standing before him.

My client had paused as one in deep emotion.

The client recounts seeing Godfrey outside his window and pursuing him through the garden after Godfrey fled. He investigates a detached lodge, sees a figure he believes to be Godfrey inside, and then leaves for London to ask Sherlock Holmes for advice and assistance.

Mr. James M. Dodd asks Holmes to investigate his old comrade Godfrey Emsworth, whom he saw looking pale and altered at a window. He accompanies Holmes to Tuxbury Old Park and insists that he must hear from Godfrey himself that he is under no restraint. Dodd learns that Godfrey's confinement was intended to conceal a serious-looking skin condition rather than a crime or imprisonment.
