Abstract written by a model

A comprehensive question is generated for each selected paragraph from its title and segment, asking what, why, or how about its topic. In SafeRAG, it is linked to golden contexts to guide proposition selection and white denial-of-service context generation; in that attack, the original question becomes the target.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the / Meta Data Collection and Pre-processing .. Generation of White Denial-of-Service

It is generated from a news title and segment and is designed to ask a comprehensive what, why, or how question. In the White Denial-of-Service attack, the original comprehensive question is used directly as the attack target.

Base Dataset Construction

A comprehensive question is generated for each selected paragraph. It is designed to ask about what, why, or how aspects of the paragraph's specific topic.

SafeRAG Dataset Construction

Questions are associated with sets of golden contexts in the SafeRAG dataset. They guide proposition selection for silver noise and the generation of white DoS attack contexts.
