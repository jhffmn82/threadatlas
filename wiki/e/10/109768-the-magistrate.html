the magistrate in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A local magistrate stationed at a neglected, dangerous post lacks means or recourse to defend the settlement. Reporting the war threat indirectly but honestly, he formally visits Heywood and privately warns that precious resources should not be wasted and the settlement must act quickly to protect itself.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER XII THE WAR BOARD "Rigmarole?

The magistrate visits Heywood in a formal procession and holds a private conference with him. He says indirectly that precious things should not be knowingly thrown away and that there is merit in dispatch; Heywood understands him to be warning that the settlement must look after itself because the magistrate cannot provide help.
