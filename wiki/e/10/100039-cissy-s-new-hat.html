Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" A BELLE OF CANADA CITY Cissy was tying her hat under her round chin before a small glass at her window.

Cissy's new hat comes from the best milliner in San Francisco and is an object of pride and vanity to her. She expects it to impress Piney, the Secamp girls, and the townspeople, and uses it as a reason to parade down Main Street. On her return, she briefly suspects that the hat may be the cause of the town's strange reaction.
