Abstract written by a model

The Phorcides, Enyo, Pephredo, and Dino, are daughters of Phorcus and Ceto and sisters of the Gorgons; sharing one eye and tooth, they guide Perseus to the nymphs after he takes them.

The Phorcides were Enyo, Pephredo, and Dino, daughters of Phorcus and Ceto and sisters of the Gorgons. They shared one eye and one tooth, and showed Perseus the way to the nymphs after he took possession of them.
