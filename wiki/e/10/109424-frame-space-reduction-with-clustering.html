Method .. 2.4 / Auxiliary Text Generation

Frame space reduction with clustering uses k-means++ to divide all video frames into groups and selects the frame closest to each cluster centroid. Frame selection then operates on this reduced representative set, substantially reducing the search space while preserving frame diversity.
