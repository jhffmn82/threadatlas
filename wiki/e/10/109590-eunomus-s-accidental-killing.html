Abstract written by a model

At a feast with Oeneus, Hercules accidentally struck and killed Eunomus while he poured water on Hercules’s hands, leading Hercules to undergo exile.

THE LIBRARY, II. vir. 6

Hercules accidentally killed Eunomus while Eunomus was pouring water on his hands during a feast with Oeneus. Because the killing was an accident, it was treated as a matter for which Hercules would undergo exile.
