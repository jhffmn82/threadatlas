weighted majority voting in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

Weighted Majority Voting (WMV) is an aggregation method that combines information, candidate responses, and crowdsourced labels using estimated source reliability. It gives greater influence to more trustworthy sources, filters and semantically clusters responses, then selects the clustered response with the largest sum of source-reliability weights. If all sources have equal reliability, it becomes majority voting. In RA-RAG, WMV supports iterative reliability estimation and response aggregation for more accurate answers.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Weighted Majority Voting aggregates information from multiple sources according to their estimated reliability. In Reliability-Aware RAG, it supports more accurate answers by giving greater influence to reliable sources.

Problem Formulation / Standard RAG .. Multi-source RAG with source reliability

Weighted majority voting selects the candidate response with the greatest sum of the reliability weights of sources producing that response. When all sources have equal reliability, it reduces to majority voting.

Method: RA-RAG .. Iterative reliability estimation

WMV aggregates candidate responses by selecting the semantically clustered response with the greatest sum of source weights. RA-RAG extends WMV from crowdsourced label aggregation to source reliability estimation and response aggregation.

F

WMV is used by RA-RAG to aggregate information from multiple sources.
