the mystery of Godfrey Emsworth's disappearance

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Godfrey's case, the case, the case in point, the case of the concealed man, the family mystery, the matter, the mystery, the problem, the secret, the situation, the whole thing

Abstract written by a model

The mystery concerns Godfrey Emsworth's unexplained silence and disappearance from public view after he is wounded in South Africa. His family appears to conceal him: Colonel Emsworth says he is travelling, Ralph wishes him dead, and he is secretly confined in an outhouse or detached house, though he later appears before Dodd. Holmes considers criminal concealment, insanity, and disease, initially infers leprosy, and then obtains a medical review identifying pseudo-leprosy or ichthyosis.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLANCHED SOLDIER

The mystery concerns Godfrey's silence after he was wounded in South Africa and the conflicting signs that his family is concealing what happened to him. Colonel Emsworth claims Godfrey is travelling, while Ralph says he wishes Godfrey were dead; the mystery deepens when Godfrey suddenly appears before Dodd.

The case concerns the secret confinement of Godfrey Emsworth in an outhouse or detached house on his family's property. Holmes considers three explanations, concealment after a crime, insanity, and disease, and concludes that the evidence most strongly supports leprosy before obtaining a medical review that identifies pseudo-leprosy or ichthyosis.
