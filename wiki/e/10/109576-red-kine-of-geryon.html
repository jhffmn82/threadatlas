red kine of Geryon in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Geryon's red cattle, guarded, are taken by Hercules from Erythia, scatter and are recovered en route, then sacrificed to Hera by Eurystheus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, II. v. 9

The red kine belong to Geryon and are guarded by Eurytion and Orthus. Hercules takes them from Erythia, drives them across many lands, recovers some after they scatter, and delivers them to Eurystheus.
