Eureka ledge

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Eureka Company claim, old ledge, their claim

Abstract written by a model

Eureka ledge is a mining location identified with Eureka Company and containing mines that greatly interest Eugenia’s father. Neworth visits its claim to inspect it for possible investment. After the spring is found not to lie on the claim, the partners must formally locate the newly discovered ground; Bray’s mining affairs include machinery for a connected new claim.

Eureka ledge is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
