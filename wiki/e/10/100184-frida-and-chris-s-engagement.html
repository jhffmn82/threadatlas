Frida and Chris's engagement in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Frida and Chris's engagement begins with Chris Calton, faces Jim's opposition over Frida's suitability and motives, and ends when Chris discovers Frida's faithlessness after seeing her with Mr. Bilson.

Jim opposes Frida and Chris's engagement because he considers Frida unsuitable and suspects that she wants Chris's wealth. Chris asks Miss Trotter to help him maintain the engagement, but the engagement effectively collapses after he sees Frida with Mr. Bilson.
