Abstract written by a model

A mustang, left saddled for Montagu Trixit’s escape, is taken by Mr. Masterton to pursue the Chinese messenger and dies breaking its neck in Masterton’s fall.

A mustang is found tethered, saddled, and bridled in the station's shed, apparently prepared for Montagu Trixit's escape. Mr. Masterton takes it and rides after the Chinese messenger, but it crashes through the snow crust and plunges into a hidden depth.
