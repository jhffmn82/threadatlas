In "SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains," RAG approaches with the same backbone models provide the comparison under which SimRAG does not increase inference time complexity. In "TableRAG: A Retrieval Augmented Generation Framework for Heterogeneous Document Reasoning," existing RAG approaches flatten and chunk tables in heterogeneous documents, compromising structural integrity and causing information loss that degrades performance on global, multi-hop reasoning tasks.
