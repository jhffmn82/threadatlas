the Garrideb fortune in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The Garrideb fortune is an approximately five-million-dollar-per-share inheritance controlled by Alexander Hamilton Garrideb’s will. It can be claimed only after the required Garridebs are found, prompting Nathan’s plans to expand his collection.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GARRIDEBS

The fortune is described as approximately five million dollars for each of the three Garridebs. Nathan expects to use his share to buy valuable specimens and expand his collection, but the money cannot be claimed until the required Garridebs are found.
