Iasus

Also called: Ἴασος, ᾿Ιάσου

Abstract written by a model

Iasus, spouse of Clymene, is presented as Atalanta’s father, though other accounts name Schoeneus; one tradition also makes him Io’s father.

Iasus is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
