Chris Calton

Abstract written by a model

Chris Calton, a twenty-six-year-old partner in the Roanoke Ledge and younger brother of Jim Calton, is brought to Summit House as a hotel guest after a fall fractures his arm and collar-bone. Despondent and in pain, he receives rest and medical care and becomes grateful for Frida Pauline Jansen’s gentleness and beauty. After returning to Roanoke Ledge, he asks Miss Trotter to protect his relationship with Frida, but reaches Frida first, finds her with Mr. Bilson, and silently accepts the loss without telling Jim. Later, weakened by illness and relapse, with a changed, pain-marked face and recurring arm injury, he returns to the Summit Hotel. He tells Miss Trotter his interest in Frida was fabricated to gain her attention, confesses his love, and later marries her.

Mr. Bilson's Housekeeper

Chris Calton, a twenty-six-year-old partner in the Roanoke Ledge, is brought to the Summit House after fracturing his arm and collar-bone. He is emotional and initially despondent, but becomes especially appreciative of Frida Pauline Jansen’s gentleness and beauty. After recovering, he returns to Roanoke Ledge and sends Miss Trotter a watch and chain with a note of gratitude.

II One afternoon Miss Trotter received a message that Mr.

Chris asks Miss Trotter to help preserve his relationship with Frida and promises not to see Frida until Miss Trotter has spoken with her. He goes to Frida before Miss Trotter can do so, discovers her with Mr. Bilson, and returns pale and changed, releasing Miss Trotter from her promise. He accepts his loss quietly and does not tell Jim what he has learned.

III When the news of the marriage reached Buckeye Hill, it did not, however, make much scandal, owing, possibly, to the scant number of the sex who are apt to disseminate it, and to many the name of Miss Jansen was unknown.

Chris Calton returns to the Summit Hotel physically weakened after an illness and relapse. He explains to Miss Trotter that he never loved Frida, fabricated his apparent interest in her to gain Miss Trotter’s attention, and finally tells Miss Trotter that he loves her.
