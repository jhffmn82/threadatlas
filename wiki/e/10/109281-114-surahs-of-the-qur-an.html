Abstract—Accurate and contextually faithful responses are

The 114 Quranic surahs are represented through their meanings, historical context, and qualities. This information supports the models in answering related queries.

II.

The surahs of the Qur’an are represented in the source book through descriptive information about their names, objectives, revelation contexts, virtues, and relationships. Twenty of the total 114 surahs were selected and tested in the study.
