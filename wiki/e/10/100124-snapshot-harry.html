Snapshot Harry in From Sand Hill to Pine

Henry Dimwood, also known as Snapshot Harry, explains the circumstances of the two robberies and says the thief is trapped along the road. He listens to Brice's recovery plan, agrees to sound out the gang, and sends Flora to guide Brice safely away.

"I am going to be a real guide to you now," she said suddenly.

Uncle Harry is associated with the guards and the gang controlling the hollow, and Flora says he must do what the gang says. Brice initially intends to return the recovered money to him, but Flora warns that the money may not remain safe in his possession.

III In twenty-four hours Edward Brice was in San Francisco.

Snapshot Harry is identified as Flora's uncle and as a former road agent whose niece has been separated from him. Tarbox is trying to persuade him to leave the valley and sell his rights in the valley and the water power on the Fork.
