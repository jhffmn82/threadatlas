Abstract written by a model

The robbery of greenbacks is committed by the cynical traveler and two accomplices posing as passengers. Assigned to verify money aboard and report to Henry's gang, they create a false obstruction, steal the money amid confusion, and flee. The cynical traveler is identified as the coach robber, and the money is recovered from his body.
