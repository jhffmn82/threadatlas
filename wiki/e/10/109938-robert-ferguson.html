Abstract written by a model

Robert Ferguson is a former Richmond rugby player and tea broker with Ferguson and Muirhead, living at Cheeseman's in Lamberley. Married with a baby and a son, Jacky, he loves his wife deeply and seeks Holmes's help when he believes she has harmed the baby. Holmes discovers she was protecting the child from poison and that jealous Jacky attacked the baby, devastating Ferguson.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

Robert Ferguson, a former Richmond rugby player, asks Holmes to help a friend whose wife allegedly drinks their baby's blood and assaults Jack. He explains the household's circumstances, says the wife is confined to her room, and arranges for Holmes and Watson to visit Cheeseman's in Lamberley.

It was evening of a dull, foggy November day when, having left our bags

Robert Ferguson believes that his wife may be a would-be murderer after seeing her rise from beside the baby's cot with blood on her lips. Holmes reveals that she was saving the baby from poison and that Jacky Ferguson had attacked the child. Ferguson is devastated to learn of Jacky's jealousy and hatred.
