Introduction

Evidence curation is a multi-step component of VisDoMRAG's textual and visual pipelines. It supports answer verifiability by building context attribution into the approach.

VisDoMRAG / 5.1 .. 5.2

Evidence Curation prompts the LLM to isolate relevant paragraphs, tables, and figure details from retrieved context and verbalize them in a structured form. It filters irrelevant or distracting information and helps mitigate hallucinations.
