Abe Bohan Hou · 2025

Federal case law in U.S. history consists of judge-written precedents explaining court reasoning in resolving legal disputes under facts and laws. These prior decisions are cited in legal analyses and are foundational in Common Law and some Civil Law jurisdictions. The recorded dataset’s raw collection covers all such federal case law through September 21, 2021.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Precedents are previous case decisions that legal analyses cite. Systems assisting legal professionals need to help locate, summarize, and reason over salient precedents.

Introduction

Case law consists of precedents written by judges to explain courts' reasoning in resolving legal disputes based on facts and laws. It is described as foundational for legal analyses in Common Law and some Civil Law jurisdictions.

The raw collection used for the dataset includes all federal case law in U.S. history up to September 21, 2021.
