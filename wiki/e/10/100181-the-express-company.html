Abstract written by a model

The express company owns the lost greenbacks. After robbers are introduced as passengers, Brice proposes restoring the money and delivering the thief to win its favor. The company assumes the loss, repays shippers without publicizing recovery, thanks Brice with a check, and promotes him to its San Francisco office.
