Colonel Starbottle's accident in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Colonel Starbottle's accident is reported as a runaway-horse incident on a public road near the fonda. He says his horse bolted and buck-jumped, throwing and injuring him, though marks on his neck, nose, and ear raise doubts. A week later, Richards explains that Cota's pinto horse caused both his and Starbottle's attacks.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Colonel Starbottle reports that his horse bolted and buck-jumped, throwing him and causing his injuries. His account is questioned because his neck, nose, and ear reportedly showed signs that could be consistent with a struggle.

He was still more concerned a week later, on returning unexpectedly one afternoo

The mysterious attacks on Colonel Starbottle and Richards initially appear to be the work of an unknown human or supernatural assailant. Richards explains that both attacks were carried out by Cota's pinto horse.
