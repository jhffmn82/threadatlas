Abstract written by a model

Henry Boone Jallinger is a Kentucky religious, reclusive hunter and Katinka Jallinger’s father. Though hostile to miners, mining towns, and gold prospecting, he secretly prospects the Jallinger gold strike. He later reportedly makes a major strike at Lone Run, disputed by Fleming’s partners.

Henry Boone Jallinger is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PART II Mr.

Mr. Jallinger is Katinka Jallinger's father, a religious hunter who dislikes miners and mining camps. He is said to have secretly prospected the lode and later makes a big strike at Lone Run, although the account is disputed by Fleming's partners.
