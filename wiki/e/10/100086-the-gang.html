the gang in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

A criminal group of about twenty-five men shares in the greenbacks and patrols every foot of the stage road before and after a stage is stopped. Two members secretly arrange an initial robbery and betray the others by removing the treasure. The rest twice stop the coach within half an hour, using a fallen tree and hidden guns to take the express box, unaware of the betrayal.

The robbers stop the coach with a fallen tree, cover the passengers with hidden guns, and take the express box. The text suggests that other robbers or traitors had already removed the treasure before the main group stopped the coach.

The gang includes about twenty-five men, each of whom has a share in the greenbacks. Two gang members secretly arranged the first robbery, while the rest later stopped the coach without knowing that those two had betrayed them.
