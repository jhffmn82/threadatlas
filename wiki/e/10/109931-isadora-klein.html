Isadora Klein

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Isadora, madame

Abstract written by a model

Isadora Klein is a celebrated Spanish beauty, widow of the German sugar king Klein, planning to marry the Duke of Lomond despite their age difference. Formerly Douglas Maberley’s lover, she hires Barney Stockdale’s gang to seize his manuscript, is exposed as their employer, burns her copy when challenged, and pays £5,000 for Mrs. Maberley’s travels.

Isadora Klein is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
