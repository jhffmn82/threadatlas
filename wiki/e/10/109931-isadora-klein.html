Isadora Klein in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Isadora Klein is a celebrated Spanish beauty, widow of the German sugar king Klein, planning to marry the Duke of Lomond despite their age difference. Formerly Douglas Maberley’s lover, she hires Barney Stockdale’s gang to seize his manuscript, is exposed as their employer, burns her copy when challenged, and pays £5,000 for Mrs. Maberley’s travels.

When I met my friend in his room early next morning, I was conscious

Isadora Klein is revealed as the employer behind the burglary. She admits that she hired Barney Stockdale and his gang to recover Douglas Maberley's manuscript, burns the manuscript when Holmes demands its return, and agrees to provide £5,000 for Mrs. Maberley's travels.
