Jim Calton (Chris's elder brother) in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Jim Calton, Chris Calton’s elder brother and a partner in Roanoke Ledge, opposes Chris’s engagement to Frida, whom he distrusts as an ignorant foreign servant interested in Chris’s money. He asks Miss Trotter to investigate Frida and regulate the girls’ conduct, but her reasoning unsettles him. After the marriage, he credits Miss Trotter with changing Chris and urges her to visit Chris during his illness, awkwardly suggesting Chris may love her.

II One afternoon Miss Trotter received a message that Mr.

Jim visits Miss Trotter to oppose Chris's engagement to Frida, whom he considers an ignorant foreign servant seeking Chris's money. He believes Miss Trotter should control the girls' conduct and asks her to question Frida about what she expects from the marriage. Miss Trotter's reasoning leaves him discomfited, though he admires her clear-sightedness.

III When the news of the marriage reached Buckeye Hill, it did not, however, make much scandal, owing, possibly, to the scant number of the sex who are apt to disseminate it, and to many the name of Miss Jansen was unknown.

James Calton tells Miss Trotter that he believes her influence changed Chris and suggests that she see Chris while he is ill. He mistakenly implies that Chris may have transferred his affections to her, prompting Miss Trotter’s embarrassment.
