the attack on the nunnery in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The attack on the nunnery is signaled by the Black Dog’s barking and approaches through darkness. Stones strike the open windows, followed by shouts, gongs, conches, and gunfire. Heywood orders shutters closed, directs the men to guns, and plans to hold the nunnery while evacuating the women.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV KAU FAI At the top of the nunnery stairs, Rudolph met them with awkward ceremony, and with that smiling air of encouragement which a nurse might use in trying cheerfully to deceive a sick man.

The attack begins with stones thrown through or against the open windows, followed by shouts, gongs, conches, and a sputtering fusillade. Heywood orders the shutters closed, tells the men where the guns are, and says the nunnery can be held while the women are evacuated.
