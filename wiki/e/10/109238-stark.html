STARK in HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases

Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, Christos Faloutsos · 2025

STARK is a hybrid question answering benchmark and primary retrieval-evaluation setting for HYBGRAG and baseline methods. It includes STARK-MAG and STARK-PRIME, whose test sets contain 2,665 and 2,801 questions. Evaluation uses Hit@1, Hit@5, Recall@20, and mean reciprocal rank. It provides training examples and default entity and relation types; HYBGRAG extracts egographs with radius no more than two and performs four self-reflection iterations. Same-name entities are resolved through an answer-containing one-hop neighborhood. HYBGRAG reports a 51% average relative Hit@1 improvement, including 47% on STARK-MAG and 55% on STARK-PRIME.

Abstract

The STARK benchmark is used to evaluate HYBGRAG. On this benchmark, HYBGRAG achieves an average relative improvement of 51% in Hit@1.

STARK is identified as a hybrid question answering benchmark used to evaluate HYBGRAG and baseline methods. HYBGRAG outperforms the second-best baseline on STARK, with the unit reporting relative Hit@1 improvements of 47% on STARK-MAG and 55% on STARK-PRIME.

4 .. 4.1.2

STARK serves as the primary retrieval evaluation benchmark. Its reported metrics are Hit@1, Hit@5, Recall@20, and mean reciprocal rank.

A / A.1 .. A.2

The STARK benchmark includes the STARK-MAG and STARK-PRIME datasets. Its testing sets are used for evaluation, containing 2665 questions for STARK-MAG and 2801 questions for STARK-PRIME.

C / C.1 .. CRAG

STARK provides the training examples and default entity and relation types used in the HYBGRAG implementation. When multiple knowledge-base entities share a name, the implementation selects the entity with the answer in its one-hop neighborhood; this issue occurs in 3.83% of STARK-MAG questions and 0.07% of STARK-PRIME questions.
