Buckeye Hill in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Buckeye Hill is a practical business community centered on the Summit Hotel. It expects Joshua Bilson to marry his housekeeper, but is startled by his choice of the serious, capable Miss Euphemia Trotter. Though Bilson’s marriage causes little scandal, the community respects and fears Trotter’s hotel management, values her social standing, and later speculates about her marrying Judge Boompointer or Judge Fletcher.

Mr. Bilson's Housekeeper

Buckeye Hill expects Joshua Bilson’s housekeeper to become his next wife, but is startled when he selects the serious, middle-aged, highly competent Miss Euphemia Trotter instead. The community respects and fears Trotter’s management of the hotel.

III When the news of the marriage reached Buckeye Hill, it did not, however, make much scandal, owing, possibly, to the scant number of the sex who are apt to disseminate it, and to many the name of Miss Jansen was unknown.

Buckeye Hill reacts with little scandal to Mr. Bilson’s marriage, but its practical business community is stirred by Miss Trotter’s appointment to control the Summit Hotel. Its residents admire Miss Trotter’s social standing and later speculate about her possible marriage to Judge Boompointer or Judge Fletcher.
