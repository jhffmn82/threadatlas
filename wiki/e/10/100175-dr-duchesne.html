Dr. Duchesne in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Dr. Duchesne is a doctor directing Chris Calton’s treatment, nursing, and convalescence. He asks Miss Trotter to choose Frida and oversee Calton’s diet, rest, hope, and immobility. He praises Trotter’s nursing as central to Calton’s improvement, while mistakenly believing Calton loves her.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Mr. Bilson's Housekeeper

Dr. Duchesne directs Chris Calton’s treatment and asks Miss Euphemia Trotter to ensure that Calton eats properly, avoids excitement and worry, remains hopeful, and keeps his injured parts immobile. He praises Trotter’s nursing ability and believes she is largely responsible for Calton’s improvement, though he mistakenly interprets her look as suggesting that Calton is in love with her.
