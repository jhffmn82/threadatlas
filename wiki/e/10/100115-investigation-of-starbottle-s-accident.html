Investigation of Starbottle's accident is conducted by Mr. Grey to learn the truth and prevent rumors. Grey questions Colonel Starbottle, who says his injuries came from falling off a mustang, and questions Ramierez without results. Richards investigates the fonda and is nearly strangled, but no assailant is identified; Grey ends empty-handed.

WHAT HAPPENED AT THE FONDA PART I "Well!

Mr. Grey questions Colonel Starbottle directly and learns that the colonel attributes his injuries to a fall from the mustang. Richards independently investigates the Ramierez fonda, but his near-strangulation produces no clear identification of the person responsible.

PART II The next afternoon he went to the fonda.

Mr. Grey questions Ramierez about suspicious loiterers and possible disturbances at the fonda, but Ramierez knows of none. The search produces no result, and Grey rides home empty of information.
