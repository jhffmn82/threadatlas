securities held by Montagu Trixit for the New Mill in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Montagu Trixit privately advances money to the New Mill and holds its securities, absent from the mill’s books. Amid concern that they might be claimed as bank assets or lost through escape or suicide, Montagu shows Masterton they are safe and unseen. He promises to return them in Sacramento within three days when the directors provide renewed notes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The excitement over the failure of Montagu Trixit's bank did not burn itself out

Trixit held the New Mill's securities for money he had advanced to the mill privately. The securities did not appear on the New Mill's books, and the mill feared that Trixit might declare them as assets of his bank or that they would be lost if he escaped or committed suicide.

How long he lay there thus he never knew.

Montagu shows Masterton the securities and says they are safe in his hands and have not been seen by anyone else. He agrees to return them after receiving the directors' renewed notes in Sacramento within three days.
