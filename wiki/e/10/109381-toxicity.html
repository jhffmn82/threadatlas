Toxicity in SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xun Liang, Simin Niu, Zhiyu Li, Sensen Zhang, Hanyu Wang, Feiyu Xiong, Jason Zhaoxin Fan, Bo Tang, Jihao Zhao, Jiawei Yang, Shichao Song, Mengwei Wang · 2025

Toxicity is an attack task in SafeRAG that uses malicious or toxic texts, mainly through direct prompt injection against large language models. These texts can enter accessible RAG-pipeline content and induce generators to produce toxic responses, although advanced generators can detect and avoid several explicit and implicit forms of toxicity. Research also includes implicit toxic attacks intended to bypass retrievers, filters, and generators. In evaluation, toxicity consistently degraded attack failure rate across retrievers; the NLI filter was generally ineffective, while SKR was considered safe because it could compress soft ads and warnings. It concludes as one of four critical SafeRAG attack tasks.
