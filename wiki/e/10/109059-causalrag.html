CausalRAG

framework · 1 work · 40 quoted facts · 17 connections
Also called: Causal-RAG, causality-driven pipeline, causality-oriented approach, our proposed framework, CausalRAG

Abstract written by a model

CausalRAG is a novel RAG framework and proposed system that integrates retrieval-augmented generation with causality. It constructs text-based causal graphs from uploaded documents, relying on LLMs for graph construction and causal-relationship identification. By identifying cause–effect dependencies and tracing causal paths among nodes, it preserves contextual coherence and integrity while guiding retrieval. It expands causal paths to combine entities with their underlying causal explanations, producing a causal summary and targeted evidence that are relevant and causally grounded. This approach addresses limitations of regular and graph-based RAG, improves alignment between user queries and retrieved content, enhances generation quality and reasoning performance, and mitigates hallucinations. In evaluation across diverse domains and varying context lengths, it outperforms regular RAG and improves context recall, context precision, and answer faithfulness. In the main comparison with k = s = 3, it leads in answer faithfulness at 78.00 and context precision at 92.86, achieves context recall of 49.46, and has an aggregate score of 91.69 on the full paper. Its inference requires causal paths.

Abstract

CausalRAG is a framework that incorporates causal graphs into the retrieval process. It constructs and traces causal relationships to preserve contextual continuity and improve retrieval precision, and it is reported to outperform regular RAG and graph-based RAG approaches across several metrics.

opening

CausalRAG is a framework that integrates causal graphs to improve retrieval accuracy and reasoning performance. It explicitly identifies causal relationships in external knowledge, preserves contextual coherence, captures cause–effect dependencies, and retrieves documents that are relevant and causally grounded. Its evaluation reports improvements in answer faithfulness, context recall, and context precision, along with reduced hallucinations and greater interpretability.

Related Work / Retrieval-Augmented Generation .. Rethinking Graph-based RAGs

CausalRAG is introduced as a proposed framework for addressing the limitations of regular and graph-based RAG through causally grounded retrieval.

Methodology .. Retrieving Context Causally

CausalRAG integrates retrieval-augmented generation with causality by constructing text-based graphs from uploaded documents and retrieving information along causally linked paths. It uses a causal summary as retrieval context to improve contextual coherence, precision, and grounding.

Experiment / Experimental Setup .. Parameter Study

CausalRAG retrieves information by tracing causal paths and integrating entities with their underlying causal explanations. It achieves the highest answer faithfulness (78.00) and context precision (92.86), with competitive context recall (49.46), and performs strongly across documents of different lengths.

Conclusion and Future Work

CausalRAG integrates causal reasoning into retrieval-augmented generation. It uses causal graphs to retrieve context that is both relevant and causally grounded, improving generation quality, reducing hallucinations, and improving alignment between user queries and retrieved content.
