Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

Noise injection into the knowledge base, retrieved context, or filtered context caused F1 (avg) to decline as the noise ratio increased. Injection into retrieved and filtered context produced higher retrieval accuracy than injection into the knowledge base.
