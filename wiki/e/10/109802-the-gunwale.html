CHAPTER XVI THE GUNWALE Rudolph's mission began quietly, with a glimpse which he afterward recalled as incredibly peaceful.

The boat's polished gunwale becomes for Rudolph a vivid boundary between life aboard the boat and death ashore. He leans against it while Bertha clings to him, then wrenches his hands away and falls into the mud after choosing not to cross it.
