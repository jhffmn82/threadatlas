Nesbit in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Nesbit is a Cockney clerk and Heywood’s second. At a dinner, he proposes and helps stage an accident to stop a duel. During a riot, he recovers early, fights attackers with bottles, leads the Europeans into the breach, and suffers a forehead cut. Later he jokes and boasts about his part.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER IX PASSAGE AT ARMS "Boy.

Nesbit suggests arranging an accident to stop the duel and helps carry out the plan. During the riot, he is among the first to recover, fights the attackers with bottles, leads the Europeans into the breach, and receives an ugly cut on his forehead.

" CHAPTER X THREE PORTALS Not till after dinner, that evening, did Rudolph rouse from his stupor.

Nesbit jokes about having taken part in the events and boasts that his accident trick helped produce the outcome. Heywood calls him an idiot.
