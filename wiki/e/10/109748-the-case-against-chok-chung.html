the case against Chok Chung in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The case against Chok Chung, a Christian merchant, is forced on the magistrate as a trap to test whether his padre will interfere; Chok Chung is questioned about following foreigners, beaten, and imprisoned.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER X THREE PORTALS Not till after dinner, that evening, did Rudolph rouse from his stupor.

The trial of Chok Chung is described as a trap meant to test whether his padre will interfere with the law. The proceedings involve questioning Chok Chung about whether he follows the foreign dogs and goats, followed by his beating and imprisonment.
