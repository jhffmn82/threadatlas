Rudolph's rescue of the infant in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Rudolph rescues an abandoned infant from an island's muddy depression, carrying her through sludge and river, wrapping her in his jacket, bringing her to land, and taking her to the hospital for treatment.

CHAPTER VII IPHIGENIA "Don't chop off a hen's head with a battle-axe.

Rudolph enters the island's muddy depression, picks up the feebly squirming infant, and carries her through the sludge and river. He wraps her in his jacket, brings her to land, and takes her to the hospital for treatment.
