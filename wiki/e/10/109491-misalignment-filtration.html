misalignment filtration

process · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Misalignment Filtration, misalignment filtering process

Abstract written by a model

Misalignment filtration converts outputs into declarative sentences, evaluates alignment with relevant context using Alignscore, retains sufficiently scored outputs, replaces others with IDK, and studies the threshold.

misalignment filtration is in one work on this site, Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
