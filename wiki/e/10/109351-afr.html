AFR

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: attack failure, attack failure rate

Abstract written by a model

AFR is an evaluation metric reported before and after attacks alongside RA or F1(avg). It has high consistency with human judgments, with values of 95.65 for Inter-context Conflict and 100 for White DoS. Its attack failure rate declines after conflict, toxicity, and DoS attacks, degrading substantially for Hybrid-Rerank under conflict, DPR under DoS, and retrievers under toxicity.

AFR is in one work on this site, SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
