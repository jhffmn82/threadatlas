Abstract written by a model

Comaetho, daughter of Pterelaus and lover of Amphitryon, removes her father’s golden lock; after Pterelaus dies, Amphitryon kills her.

THE LIBRARY, II. tv. 6-7

Comaetho, the daughter of Pterelaus, fell in love with Amphitryon and removed the golden lock from her father’s head. After Pterelaus died, Amphitryon killed Comaetho.
