the swords in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A pair of bell-hilted swords, originally belonging to Chantel's father, is chosen for the duel between Rudolph and Dr. Chantel because Chantel may choose and excels at fencing. Chantel prefers swords through vanity and skill, while Rudolph cannot tell point from handle. Sturgeon brings the pair; Rudolph is cut in practice, and Chantel breaks one sword in the rubble, with no replacement nearby.

CHAPTER VIII THE HOT NIGHT Rudolph paced his long chamber like a wolf,--a wolf in summer, with too thick a coat.

Swords are identified as the likely weapons for the duel because Chantel will have the choice and is a highly skilled fencer. Rudolph admits that he cannot distinguish a sword's point from its handle, making the proposed contest dangerously unequal.
