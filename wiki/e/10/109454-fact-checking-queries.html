Method: RA-RAG .. Iterative reliability estimation

Fact-checking queries are generated from statements in source documents to verify those documents across sources. For each query, RA-RAG retrieves documents from every source and uses the resulting responses to estimate an answer and update source reliability.

Real-world Application / Setup .. Experimental results

Fact-checking queries are generated as yes/no questions for each collected claim, such as “Is it true that {claim}?” They are primarily designed for Politician A, Politician B, and User A, which results in more precise reliability estimates for those target sources.
