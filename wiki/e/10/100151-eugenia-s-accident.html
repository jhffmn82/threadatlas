Abstract written by a model

Eugenia's accident occurs near the spring by the stage road, when she leaves the coach, walks uphill, and slips near the bank. She falls into the ravine, lands on Bray, and nearly slides over a ledge. Though shocked and with her dust-cloak damaged, she is not seriously injured. The fall delays Neworth, and its track later helps Parkhurst find disturbed ground containing quartz and gold.

Eugenia's accident is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
