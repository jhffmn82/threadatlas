the defending party in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A defending party occupies a nunnery under siege, resisting enemy mining and gunfire. As the enemy digs beneath them, Heywood and Rudolph enter the tunnel and sabotage its powder. After enduring heat, fighting, exhaustion, flies, and dwindling ammunition, the fugitives evacuate by Hakka boat while Heywood stays behind after attacking Fang.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX BROTHER MOLES This new danger, working below in the solid earth, had thrown Rudolph into a state of sullen resignation.

The defending party watches and fights while the enemy digs beneath its position. Heywood and Rudolph act on its behalf by entering the mine tunnel and sabotaging the enemy's powder.

CHAPTER XX THE HAKKA BOAT "Pretty fair," Captain Kneebone said.

The fugitives endure days of heat, battle, exhaustion, flies, and dwindling ammunition inside the nunnery. They prepare to evacuate, wait for the padre to finish burying the dying convert, and finally board the Hakka boat, with Heywood remaining behind on foot after attacking Fang.
