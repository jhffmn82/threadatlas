ColQwen2 in VisDoM: Multi-Document QA with Visually Rich Elements Using Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Manan Suri, Puneet Mathur, Franck Dernoncourt, Kanika Gowswami, Ryan A. Rossi, and Dinesh Manocha · 2025

ColQwen2 is a late-interaction, multi-vector visual retrieval model built on the Qwen2 LLM backbone. In evaluation across PaperTab, FetaTab, SciGraphQA, SPIQA, and SlideVQA, it attains the highest reported source-document identification results, averaging 96.94 at k=5. It outperforms retrieval baselines, including BGE-1.5 by 4.5%, attributed to its strong Qwen2 backbone.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

ColQwen2 is evaluated as a late-interaction multi-vector visual retrieval model built on Qwen2. It achieves the highest reported average source-document identification score at k = 5, with 97.61 on PaperTab, 96.86 on FetaTab, 95.58 on SciGraphQA, 96.85 on SPIQA, 97.82 on SlideVQA, and 96.94 on average.

Results / 7.1 .. 7.3

ColQwen2 outperforms the other retrieval baselines across datasets, which the unit attributes to its strong Qwen2 LLM backbone. At k=5, it is 4.5% better than the next closest BGE1.5 model in identifying the correct source document.
