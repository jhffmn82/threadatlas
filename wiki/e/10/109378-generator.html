generator

software · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: G, Generator, generators
  • In SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation: The generator synthesizes processed evidence into a final response. It concatenates the instruction, query, and filtered contexts into a unified prompt, feeds that prompt to an LLM, and produces an answer. It is evaluated for proposition coverage, correct and incorrect option identification, correct-context use, and resistance to attack effects. Selection relies on cumulative positive RAG generation safety metrics across attack tasks; Baichuan 13B leads multiple tasks, while lighter models are described as safer than the GPT series and DeepSeek. Advanced generators may avoid explicit and implicit toxicity and ignore refusal signals, but malicious or conflicting contexts and soft ad content can mislead them.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The generator synthesizes processed evidence into a response. Advanced generators can detect and avoid several forms of explicit and implicit toxicity and may ignore refusal signals mixed into evidence, but they can still be misled by inter-context conflict or soft ad content.

Evaluation Metrics / Retrieval Safety Metric .. Generation Safety Metric / F1 Variant

The generator is evaluated on whether it can cover facts in propositions, identify correct and incorrect options, use correct context, and resist attack effects.

Analysis of Generator and Evaluator / Selection of Generator .. Selection of Evaluator

The generator is selected based on cumulative positive RAG generation safety metrics across different attack tasks. Baichuan 13B maintains a leading position in multiple tasks, while lighter models are described as safer than stronger models such as the GPT series and DeepSeek.

Preliminaries and Definitions / RAG Pipeline

The generator combines the instruction, query, and filtered contexts by concatenating them into a unified prompt. It feeds this prompt into the LLM to generate the final answer.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the

The generator can be attacked by inserting malicious contexts into the filtered results. The injected contexts directly distort the information provided to the generator.
