Kempner in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

Kempner, a defender at the northeast corner with long, thin arms, doubts the position can hold amid uproar, catches and returns a burning stink-pot cylinder, is killed, and is buried quietly beside the pit near the former chapel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

Kempner catches the burning stink-pot cylinder and throws it back among the attacking Chinese forces. He is killed in the effort, and the defenders bury him near the former chapel in the nunnery compound.
