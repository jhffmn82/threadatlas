CHAPTER XVIII SIEGE He never believed that they could hold the northeast corner for a minute, so loud and unceasing was the uproar.

Kempner catches the burning stink-pot cylinder and throws it back among the attacking Chinese forces. He is killed in the effort, and the defenders bury him near the former chapel in the nunnery compound.
