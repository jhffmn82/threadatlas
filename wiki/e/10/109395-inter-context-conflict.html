SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation / Abstract

Inter-context conflict is one of the four attack-task categories used in the SafeRAG security evaluation.

Introduction

Inter-context conflict is a more hazardous conflict type than the widely studied context-memory conflict. Because LLMs lack sufficient parametric knowledge to resolve external conflicts, they are more susceptible to being misled by tampered texts.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the / Meta Data Collection and Pre-processing .. Generation of White Denial-of-Service

It is generated by manually modifying a golden context so that it contradicts or can be confused with the original context. The construction emphasizes minimal perturbation, realistic rewriting, and preservation of key facts relevant to the context and query.

Experiments / Settings .. Results on Conflict, Toxicity, and DoS

Conflict attack reduced retrieval accuracy and attack failure rate across RAG stages and increased the likelihood of incorrect options being judged correct, lowering F1 (correct). Hybrid-Rerank was more susceptible to conflict attack, and SKR was less secure because it could compress conflict details.

Conclusion

Inter-context conflict is proposed as a novel attack strategy that exposes gaps in existing defenses.

SafeRAG Dataset Construction

Inter-context conflict uses a golden context to generate an attack context that clearly contradicts it, then combines the conflicting pair with another randomly selected golden context. This process constructs DICC.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the

Inter Context Conflict includes attack contexts that contradict factual information in the original contexts. The unit gives an example that changes the Federal Reserve rate-cut probabilities to implausible values.
