the revolver in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

A revolver with one discharged chamber was found on the floor of Miss Dunbar's wardrobe, a circumstance said to incriminate her. Its calibre matched the bullet that killed Neil Gibson's wife. The Gold King proposes that his wife carried it to frighten Miss Dunbar, that it discharged in a scuffle, and that she later hid or discarded it among her clothes.
