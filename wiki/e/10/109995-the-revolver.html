the revolver

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a revolver, revolver, the gun, this revolver

Abstract written by a model

A revolver with one discharged chamber was found on the floor of Miss Dunbar's wardrobe, a circumstance said to incriminate her. Its calibre matched the bullet that killed Neil Gibson's wife. The Gold King proposes that his wife carried it to frighten Miss Dunbar, that it discharged in a scuffle, and that she later hid or discarded it among her clothes. Neil Gibson also keeps numerous firearms and sleeps beside a loaded revolver.

THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

A revolver with one discharged chamber was found on the floor of Miss Dunbar's wardrobe. Its calibre corresponded with the bullet that killed Neil Gibson's wife.

To my surprise the Gold King took the reproof with equanimity.

The Gold King suggests that his wife may have carried a revolver to frighten Miss Dunbar and that it may have discharged during a scuffle. Mr. Holmes notes that she might have returned home with it and hidden or discarded it among her clothes.
