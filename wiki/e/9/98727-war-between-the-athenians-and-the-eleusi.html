war between the Athenians and the Eleusinians in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

War between Athenians and Eleusinians begins when Eleusinians summon Eumolpus and a Thracian force; Erechtheus kills Eumolpus, then Poseidon destroys Erechtheus and his household.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XV. Πανδίονος δὲ ἀποθανόντος οἱ παῖδες τὰ

A war arose between the Athenians and the Eleusinians, who called on Eumolpus to fight for them with a large Thracian force. The conflict ended with Eumolpus killed by Erechtheus, followed by Poseidon’s destruction of Erechtheus and his household.
