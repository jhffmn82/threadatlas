CHAPTER 16

The escape from Monday Mountain begins when Percy Vere tells Dorothy how to stop the running white fence and they dash through its gate with Toto. The Tubbies cannot follow immediately because the fence starts spinning again, and the fugitives slide down the mountain before running away.
