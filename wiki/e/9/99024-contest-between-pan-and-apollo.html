contest between Pan and Apollo

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: award, challenge, contest, judgment, music contest between Pan and Apollo, trial of skill, unequal contest
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is a musical contest arbitrated and judged by Tmolus that Apollo wins, while Midas alone condemns the decision as unjust.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a musical trial in which Pan challenges Apollo, Tmolus judges Apollo's lyre superior to Pan's pipes, and Apollo wins despite Midas's disagreement.

music contest between Pan and Apollo in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Pan challenges Apollo to a musical trial; Tmolus judges Apollo's lyre superior to Pan's pipes, awarding Apollo victory despite Midas's disagreement.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

The music contest was held after Pan challenged Apollo to a trial of skill. Tmolus judged Apollo's lyre superior to Pan's rustic pipes, though Midas disagreed with the award.
