contest between Pan and Apollo

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: award, challenge, contest, judgment, music contest between Pan and Apollo, trial of skill, unequal contest

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is a musical contest arbitrated and judged by Tmolus that Apollo wins, while Midas alone condemns the decision as unjust. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a musical trial in which Pan challenges Apollo, Tmolus judges Apollo's lyre superior to Pan's pipes, and Apollo wins despite Midas's disagreement.
