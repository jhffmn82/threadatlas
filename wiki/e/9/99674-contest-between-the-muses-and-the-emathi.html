contest between the Muses and the Emathian sisters

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a contest, a dispute, contest, singing contest between the Muses and Pierides, the contest

Abstract written by a model

A contest of words, voice, and skill pits the Pierides, or Emathian sisters, against the Muses before Nymph judges. After a Pierides sings of the Gods’ flight from Typhoeus, the Muses answer; the Nymphs award victory to the Goddesses of Helicon, and the abusive losers are punished.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The Pierides challenged the Muses to sing, with the Nymphs serving as judges and the contestants agreeing to withdraw from their lands if defeated. One of the Pierides first sang about the Gods’ flight from Typhoeus, after which the Muses were called upon to answer.

opening

The contest ends with the Nymphs declaring that the Goddesses of Helicon have won. The defeated Emathian sisters respond with abuse and are punished.
