1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

The son survives after sailors rescue and repair him. As an adult he unknowingly kills his father, marries his mother, kills her after discovering who she is, and undergoes penance involving an apple tree and a well.
