Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the storm stirs the sea, intensifies overnight with rain, thunder, lightning, and waves, wrecks a ship, and buries Ceyx beneath the water. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the storm's violent roaring winds, towering waves, rain, thunder, and lightning overwhelm sailors, destroy Ceyx's ship, and cause Ceyx to drown.

FABLE VII. [XI.410-748]

The storm grows during the night as fierce winds, rain, thunder, lightning, and enormous waves overwhelm the sailors’ efforts. It damages the ship, breaks its mast and helm, and drives a wave over Ceyx that buries him beneath the water.
