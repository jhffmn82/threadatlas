storm in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

A storm caused by winds stirs the sea, intensifies overnight with rain, thunder, lightning, and waves, wrecks a ship, and buries Ceyx beneath the water.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE VII. [XI.410-748]

The storm grows during the night as fierce winds, rain, thunder, lightning, and enormous waves overwhelm the sailors’ efforts. It damages the ship, breaks its mast and helm, and drives a wave over Ceyx that buries him beneath the water.
