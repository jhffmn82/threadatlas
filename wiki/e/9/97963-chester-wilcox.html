Chester Wilcox in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Chester Wilcox is a scarred former colour sergeant and chief foreman of the Iron Dike Company, known for being armed and a quick, accurate shot. Targeted by the Scowrers and the Society of Freemen after shooting Jim Carnaway, he survives two earlier attempts on his life. Ordered attacked by Jack McMurdo at his isolated home, he moves his family to safer, less-known quarters under police protection, where he is later reportedly fired upon from ambush.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

Chester Wilcox is the chief foreman of the Iron Dike Company and a scarred former colour sergeant who is armed and reputed to shoot quickly and accurately. The Scowrers have already made two unsuccessful attempts to kill him, and Boss McGinty orders Jack McMurdo to attack him at his isolated home.

McMurdo thought long and deeply over the commission which had been so

Chester Wilcox was marked for destruction because of the Society of Freemen's campaign of violence. He moved himself and his family to safer quarters under police protection the day before McMurdo's attack, and he was later reported to have been shot at from an ambuscade.
