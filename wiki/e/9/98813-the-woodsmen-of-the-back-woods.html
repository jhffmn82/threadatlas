CHAPTER 11

The woodsmen of the Back Woods warn Dorothy and Humpy to go away and threaten them with axes. After Dorothy writes that she is Princess Dorothy and a friend of Ozma of Oz, they agree to let the travelers pass, but then pursue them until the back wind forces them back.
