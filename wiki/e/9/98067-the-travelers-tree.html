the Travelers' Tree in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

The Travelers' Tree, planted by the Wizard Wam in 1120 O. Z., bears breakfast provisions. Notta, Bob, Nickadoodle, and the Cowardly Lion obtain and eat breakfast from it, then bury the dishes as directed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The Travelers' Tree, planted by the Wizard Wam in the year 1120 O. Z., provides cups, hot coffee, cocoa, tea, prepared dishes, napkins, and hard-boiled eggs. Notta, Bob, Nickadoodle, and the Cowardly Lion use it to obtain and eat breakfast, then bury the dishes as its sign directs.
