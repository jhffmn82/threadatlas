Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

FABLE VIII. [X.298-518]

Myrrha, the daughter of Cinyras and Cenchris, conceives an incestuous passion for her father and recognizes the criminality of her desire. She attempts to hang herself, but her nurse saves her; later she enters her father's chamber in disguise, becomes pregnant by him, flees when discovered, and is transformed into a tree.

[Footnote 42: _The Panchæan land._--Ver. 309. Panchæa was a region

Myrrha is described in the explanation as the daughter-in-law of Cinyras and the wife of Ammon. After seeing Cinyras violate the rules of decency while drunk, she tells Ammon, and Cinyras responds by cursing her and Adonis. She later retires to Arabia, where poets said she gave birth to Adonis and was transformed into a myrrh tree.
