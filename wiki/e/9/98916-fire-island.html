Fire Island in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Fire Island is a red-hot island of fire in green, sulphurous waters, inhabited by flame families and containing fireplaces, gardens, flowers, flies, and fire-making works. Its rowers leave Grampa and companions at the dark tower, then return.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

Fire Island is a red-hot island surrounded by green, sulphurous waters. It contains flame families, open fireplaces, fire gardens, fire flowers, and works where fires and fireworks are manufactured.

CHAPTER 11

Fire Island is visible from the dark tower, whose moss soaks up its rays. The rowers from Fire Island leave Grampa and his companions at the tower and row back to the island.
