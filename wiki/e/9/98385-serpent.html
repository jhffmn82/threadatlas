serpent in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Serpent is a reptile believed in Armenia to know magical life-giving herbs and springs. In resurrection stories, it revives a dead or wounded serpent with a herb, teaching humans plants' healing, renewing, and potentially immortal power.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.—THE RESURRECTION OF GLAUCUS

Serpents repeatedly serve as the source from which humans learn the power of life-giving plants. In the stories, a serpent applies a herb to a dead or wounded serpent, and in Armenian belief serpents know springs and flowers that heal them, renew their youth, and may confer immortality.
