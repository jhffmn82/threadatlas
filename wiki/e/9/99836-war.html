Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, War emerges with destructive iron and gold, using violence and brandishing clattering weapons in blood-stained hands. In The Fall of Troy, War, a divine force embodied in battle, drives Argives into combat as fighting, bloodshed, and deaths continue among Greeks and Trojans.
