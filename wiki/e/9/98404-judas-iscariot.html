1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

In a medieval version of the Oedipus story, Judas Iscarioth is abandoned at sea, kills his unknown father, and marries his unknown mother. After learning the truth, he becomes a disciple of Christ but later returns to evil through his control of the money bag.
