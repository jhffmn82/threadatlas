Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the golden apples are thrown by Hippomenes during the race, delaying Atalanta until he overtakes her. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the golden apples are given by Venus to Hippomenes, who throws them during the race and reaches the goal first.
