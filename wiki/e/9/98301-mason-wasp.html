Mason-Wasp in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Mason-Wasp, common in Central Africa and called Ngongwa, is a winged, yellow-abdomened, black-and-orange-legged creature regarded as the Ba-ila’s Prometheus, bringing fire from God. It builds and seals mud cells on fireplaces, houses, walls, books, and pictures, lays eggs with grubs, and its young eat insects benumbed by stinging. Ngongwa is supposed to change into it.

The Mason-Wasp builds mud cells on fireplaces, walls, books, and pictures. It lays eggs in the cells with a caterpillar or grub, seals them, and its young eat insects benumbed by the parent. The Ba-ila account supposes that Ngongwa changes into a Mason-Wasp.
