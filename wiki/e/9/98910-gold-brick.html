CHAPTER 21

The yellow hen lays a gold brick upon the table after Bill questions whether it can provide a fortune. The brick astonishes Bill and delights the others.

CHAPTER 22

The gold bricks are laid by the yellow hen and sold by the twenty-four rustic laborers. Their sale supplies Ragbad with enough money to buy supplies and care for the linens and lawns.
