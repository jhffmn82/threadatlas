Abstract written by a model

The Seven Doors are seven heavy oak doors in the wall of the Kingdom of Doorways, each with a brass plate. Only one is right. Their plates give varied warnings, instructions, and identities, including a talking seventh door. Notta and Bob are unsure, try several, and finally enter through the Queen's door.

Chapter 5

The seven doors are heavy oak doors set into the wall of the Kingdom of Doorways. A sign says that only one of them is the right door, leaving Notta and Bob Up uncertain which to choose.

Chapter 6

The Seven Doors present different warnings, instructions, and identities: one says to keep out, one warns not to waken the baby, one concerns the Dorms, one names King Theodore the Third, one belongs to the Queen, one says "Push!", and the seventh talks. Notta and Bob try several of them before entering through the Queen's door.
