3 .. 3.2

Single-step evaluations test models after one inference call using naive prompts, BM25-retrieved documents, or an Oracle Prompt. For Gemini-Pro-1.5-0514, accuracy increases from 0.408 with naive prompting to 0.474 with four BM25-retrieved documents and 0.729 with oracle information.
