Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Polyidus is a seer who learns a magical herb’s power from a serpent and uses it to restore the dead Glaucus to life. In The Library, Polyidus, son of Coeranus and a diviner, identifies Minos’s cow, finds and restores dead Glaucus with an herb, then is compelled to teach him divination.

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.—THE RESURRECTION OF GLAUCUS

Polyidus restored the dead Glaucus to life by laying on him a magical herb. He had learned of the herb’s power by seeing a serpent apply it to a dead serpent.
