Polyidus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Polyidus, a seer, learns a magical herb’s power from a serpent and uses it to restore the dead Glaucus to life.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.—THE RESURRECTION OF GLAUCUS

Polyidus restored the dead Glaucus to life by laying on him a magical herb. He had learned of the herb’s power by seeing a serpent apply it to a dead serpent.
