Also called: Whiffen, Whiffenpuff, general, the General

Abstract written by a model

General Whiffenpuff, the King's body guard and a general of Kimbaloo, loves eating above all. After finding the pantry, dinner, goose, and Mombi gone and Snip missing, he takes his gun and searches night and day. He reaches the palace with the Invisible Cook, finds Snip safe, and leaves for Kimbaloo with them and Kabumpo.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

General Whiffenpuff checks the pantry and reports that the dinner, goose, and Mombi are gone. He is upset by Snip's disappearance, then takes his gun and boldly leaves to search for Mombi, the goose, Snip, and an invisible cook.

CHAPTER 21

General Whiffenpuff arrives at the palace with the Invisible Cook after traveling night and day in search of Mombi and Snip. He is delighted to find Snip safe and later leaves for Kimbaloo with Kabumpo, the Invisible Cook, and Snip.
