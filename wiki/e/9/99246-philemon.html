Philemon in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Philemon, a pious poor old man married to Baucis, welcomes Jupiter and Mercury, serves them, seeks temple service, and is transformed with Baucis into a tree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

Philemon, an old and poor man, lives with Baucis in their small cottage. He welcomes Jupiter and Mercury, helps prepare their meal, joins Baucis in requesting service in the gods' temple, and is transformed with her into a tree.
