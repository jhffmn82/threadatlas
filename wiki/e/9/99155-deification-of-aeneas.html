Abstract written by a model

Venus asks Jupiter to grant Aeneas godhead after his heroic deeds, and Jupiter assents with Juno’s approval. Numicius washes away Aeneas’s mortality, Venus anoints him with divine substances, and he becomes a god called Indiges.
