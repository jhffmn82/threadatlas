Birlstone in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Birlstone is a small, ancient Sussex village on the county’s northern border, at the extreme fringe of the Weald forest and ten or twelve miles west of Tunbridge Wells. Picturesque and quiet, it has grown with well-to-do residents and shops. It contains the Manor House, Birlstone house, and the Westville Arms. Jack Douglas rented a secluded house there with Mrs. Douglas, apparently because of danger. After Douglas is murdered, Holmes, Watson, MacDonald, Mason, and the narrator travel to Birlstone to investigate.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

Birlstone is the Sussex location where John Douglas has been murdered. Sherlock Holmes, Inspector MacDonald, and the narrator travel there to investigate the case’s extraordinary features.

Chapter III

Birlstone is a small, ancient village on the northern border of Sussex, about ten or twelve miles west of Tunbridge Wells. Its picturesque setting and growing population have attracted well-to-do residents and led to the development of shops.

Chapter IV

Birlstone is the Sussex location where the murder occurs and where Holmes, Dr. Watson, White Mason, and Inspector MacDonald meet. The village contains the Manor House and the Westville Arms.

Chapter V

Birlstone is the quiet English place where Jack Douglas rented a house and lived with Mrs. Douglas. Barker believed Douglas's choice of such a secluded place was connected with the danger hanging over him.
