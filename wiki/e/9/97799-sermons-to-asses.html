“Sermons to Asses” in Laurence Sterne in Germany

A work mistakenly associated with Sterne in German publications and translations, actually written by Rev. James Murray, attacking the established church, taxation, and coercive power.

“Sermons to Asses” was mistakenly associated with Sterne in some German publications and translations. The work was actually written by Rev. James Murray and directed against the established church, taxation, and coercive power.
