is_a act of divine vengeance [carried out through Tisiphone] Juno's poisoning of Athamas and Ino is carried out by Tisiphone, who throws a mixture of poison, Cerberus's foam, Echidna's venom, rage, tears, crime, and the love of murder into their breasts. The poison attacks their minds rather than their limbs and produces their direful actions.
