Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Kinda Jolly is the kind and jolly King of Kimbaloo, where he made a fortune in buttons. He lives in the button-covered castle with Rosa Merry, oversees the button boys, and is seen calmly walking under the button trees after Mombi attempts a transformation.

CHAPTER 2

King Kinda Jolly is identified as Ozma's father and the rightful ruler of Oz. Mombi says she has forgotten what she did with him, while Pajuka insists that he is still alive in some transformed shape and has been searching for him.

CHAPTER 3

Kinda Jolly happily discusses dinner with Rosa Merry, expecting to eat a large roast goose that cost a thousand gold buttons. He becomes distressed when Mombi, the goose, and Snip are found to be gone, but agrees to send for an invisible cook and later laughs with Hah Hoh.

CHAPTER 9

The King of Oz is identified as Ozma's father. A note on the royal robe says that he has been preserved by Fairy Lurline and can be restored to his proper shape if the robe is placed on his shoulders with the specified incantation.

CHAPTER 13

Snip tells Tora that Mombi transformed and hid the lost King and plans to find and restore him to the throne. Snip hopes to reach the Emerald City and warn Ozma so she can help find the lost King.

CHAPTER 14

The King of Oz is someone Snip and Tora intend to find after locating Pajuka. Snip wonders what shape Mombi changed the King into.

CHAPTER 15

The King of Oz is described as lost but alive. Snip and Pajuka are searching for him, while Dorothy, Snip, Tora, Humpy, and Kabumpo travel toward the Emerald City to help find him and warn Ozma.

CHAPTER 18

The golden goose feather announces that the King of Oz is in the palace. Ozma and her companions hurry home expecting to find him, but the unit does not show him.
