Abstract written by a model

The dead-ass episode from The Sentimental Journey is a central model for Timme’s satire of sentimental responses to animals. Frau Kurt and Pankraz repeatedly compare or reproduce its emotional effect, but their sympathy becomes absurd because it displaces concern for living people and other immediate obligations.
