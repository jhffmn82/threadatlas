Iphis's suicide

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: I die, a hapless burden, hanging, he fastened a rope to the gate-post, his death, my death, the deed
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Iphis's suicide occurs before Anaxarete's door after her prolonged rejection has made his love unbearable; he fastens a halter to the door, places his head in the noose, and hangs himself while turned toward her, causing Anaxarete's transformation into stone after she witnesses his funeral.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Iphis's suicide follows his announcement that he will die to end Anaxarete's rejection and prove that his love leaves him only with life; he fastens a rope to her gate-post, places his head in the noose, and dies with his neck broken, causing Anaxarete's transformation into stone through her witnessing his funeral.
