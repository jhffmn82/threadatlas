Abstract written by a model

Proteus’s shape-shifting spans appearances from a young man and animals through stone and tree to river and fire, encompassing solid forms, flowing water, and its opposite.

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

Proteus can appear as a young man, lion, boar, serpent, bull, stone, tree, river, or fire. His transformations include both solid forms and forms resembling flowing water and its opposite.
