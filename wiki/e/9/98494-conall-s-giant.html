Conall's giant

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a great giant, the brute, the giant

Abstract written by a model

Conall's giant is a one-eyed giant who owns goats, their leading buck, and a talking ring. He keeps goats in a cave and intends to eat Conall, but Conall blinds him, escapes in the buck's skin, and causes him to drown pursuing the ring.

Conall's giant is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
