Nisus

person · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: King Nisus, father, her father, my father

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Nisus is Pandion’s son and king of Megara, bearing a fated purple hair whose removal means death; when his daughter Scylla pulls it out in treachery, he dies. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Nisus is king of Alcathoë, protected by a purple lock until Scylla cuts it; transformed into a sea eagle, he pursues her. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Nisus is king and ruler of Megara, protected by a purple lock; after Scylla cuts it, he becomes a sea-eagle and attacks her.
