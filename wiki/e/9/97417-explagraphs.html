ExplaGraphs in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where ExplaGraphs first appears

Abstract written by a model

ExplaGraphs is a common-sense reasoning dataset for predicting positions in debates. It averages 2,766 graphs, 5.17 nodes, 4.25 edges, and 1,396 tokens, and is evaluated by Accuracy across retrieval settings. Small graphs limit gains beyond top-5; GRAG reaches 0.9223 without fine-tuning and 0.4540 after WebQSP transfer.

ExplaGraphs is a common-sense reasoning dataset focused on predicting positions in debates, averaging 2,766 graphs, 5.17 nodes, 4.25 edges, and 1,396 tokens. GRAG achieves 0.9223 accuracy without fine-tuning, and a GRAG model trained on WebQSP reaches 0.4540 accuracy when transferred to ExplaGraphs.

ExplaGraphs is evaluated with Accuracy using top-3, top-5, and top-10 retrieval settings. Its smaller graph sizes lead to minimal performance changes beyond top-5 for most retrievers.
