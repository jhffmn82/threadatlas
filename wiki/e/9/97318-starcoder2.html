Canonical RACG / 3.1 .. 3.4

StarCoder2 is included as a code-specific generation model, with StarCoder2-7B used in the experiments. It is a weaker generator that benefits substantially from retrieved contexts on several tasks, especially MBPP and RepoEval.

RACG with Open Retrieval

StarCoder2 is used to study open RACG with a weak language model alongside the three top retrievers. StarCoder2-7B is also evaluated on RepoEval with local, open, and combined contexts.
