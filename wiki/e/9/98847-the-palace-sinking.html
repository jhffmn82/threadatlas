the palace sinking in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

The palace sinking is a predicted disaster in the Palace of Emeralds. After Mombi’s command and incorrect magic formula, the palace suddenly descends through earth and mud, trapping the company in darkness, throwing people into confusion, and leaving those outside clinging to the roof.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 17

The palace begins sinking suddenly after Mombi commands Humpy to assume his proper form. It descends through earth and mud until the windows and doorways are blocked and the company is lost in darkness.

CHAPTER 19

The castle sinks suddenly into the earth after Mombi uses her magic formula incorrectly. The people inside are thrown down and confused, while those outside later find themselves clinging to the roof.
