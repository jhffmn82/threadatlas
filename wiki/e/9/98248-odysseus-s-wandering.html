Odysseus's wandering

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: wandered about, wandering, ἐπλανᾶτο

Abstract written by a model

Odysseus wanders from Ilium through Ismarus, the land of the Lotus-eaters, the land of the Cyclopes, Aeolia, the land of the Laestrygones, and the Aeaean isle. The text also says that some accounts place his wandering in Libya, Sicily, the Ocean, or the Tyrrhenian Sea.
