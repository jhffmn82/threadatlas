balanced retrieval in Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Jiwoong Sohn · 2025

Balanced retrieval is a method for gathering medical evidence evenly from PubMed, PMC, clinical guidelines, and medical textbooks. It addresses dense-retriever bias toward larger or heavily represented corpora by extracting equal numbers of documents from each source, improving representation of smaller corpora. The documents are then reranked for relevance. The method consistently outperforms MedRAG and is presented as mitigating retriever bias.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Balanced retrieval obtains equal amounts of evidence snippets from PubMed, PMC, textbooks, and clinical guidelines. The strategy is intended to reduce dense-retriever bias toward larger or more heavily represented corpora.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.4

Balanced retrieval extracts an equal number of documents from each corpus so that smaller corpora are represented more evenly. The retrieved documents are then refined by reranking them for relevance.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

Balanced retrieval extracts information in a balanced manner from each corpus. It consistently outperforms MedRAG and is presented as a way to mitigate retriever bias.
