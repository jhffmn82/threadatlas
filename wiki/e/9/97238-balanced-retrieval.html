balanced retrieval

method · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Balanced retrieval, balanced retrieval stage, balanced retrieval strategy, our balanced retrieval, retrieval component

Abstract written by a model

Balanced retrieval is a method for gathering medical evidence evenly from PubMed, PMC, clinical guidelines, and medical textbooks. It addresses dense-retriever bias toward larger or heavily represented corpora by extracting equal numbers of documents from each source, improving representation of smaller corpora. The documents are then reranked for relevance. The method consistently outperforms MedRAG and is presented as mitigating retriever bias.
