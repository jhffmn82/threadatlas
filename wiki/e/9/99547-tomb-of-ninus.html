tomb of Ninus

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: busta, one sepulchre, one tomb, sepulchre of Ninus, the monument, the tomb, the tomb of Ninus, tombs
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the tomb of Ninus was the agreed meeting place of Pyramus and Thisbe, beneath the shade of a lofty mulberry tree near a cold spring.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the tomb of Ninus was the agreed meeting place outside the city's bounds where Pyramus and Thisbe planned to meet secretly; Thisbe went there first and waited beneath the appointed tree.
