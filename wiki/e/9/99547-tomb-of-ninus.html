Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the tomb of Ninus was the agreed meeting place of Pyramus and Thisbe, beneath the shade of a lofty mulberry tree near a cold spring. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the tomb of Ninus was the agreed meeting place outside the city's bounds where Pyramus and Thisbe planned to meet secretly; Thisbe went there first and waited beneath the appointed tree.
