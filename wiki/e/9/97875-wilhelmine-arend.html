Wilhelmine Arend is the leading character in Wezel’s novel and is presented as someone seized by sentimentalism as if by a pestilential disease. Her excessive sympathy for animals and inability to accept reasonable human relations lead to estrangement, increasing mental aberration, seclusion, and death.
