Henry Thomas Riley in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Henry Thomas Riley (1816–1878) was a translator of Ovid’s Metamorphoses. He earned a B.A. in 1840 and an M.A. in 1859, then produced a literal English translation of Books I–VII with notes and explanations, chiefly using the Delphin edition while consulting Burmann and Gierig.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

front matter

Henry T. Riley produced the literal English translation of Books I-VII and supplied notes and explanations. He generally adopted the text of the Delphin edition while consulting the editions of Burmann and Gierig.
