XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

Dolopathos is a mediaeval collection of tales written in Latin by John of Haute-Seille. It contains a version of the Polyphemus story in which a famous robber defeats a giant through deception, and its account diverges in notable ways from the Odyssey.
