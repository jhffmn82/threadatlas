Chapter 16

Preserva the Great is the jellied Queen of the Preservatory. She orders the boy pickled, the fish canned, the lion minced, and the fowl potted, then considers preserving the travelers whole for a royal museum. She repeatedly removes and replaces her jar lid while calling for help.
