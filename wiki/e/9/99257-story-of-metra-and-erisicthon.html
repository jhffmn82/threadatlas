EXPLANATION.

The story concerns Erisicthon’s punishment for impiety and Metra’s efforts to sustain him after his luxury and extravagance had ruined him. Its explanation suggests that Metra’s transformations may represent her ability to turn to different kinds of useful work, service, or other means of earning food for her father.
