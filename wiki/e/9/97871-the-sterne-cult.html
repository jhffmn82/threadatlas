the Sterne cult in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

The Sterne cult was a German movement of admiration for Laurence Sterne, shaped by German distortions of his work. After an initially delayed reception, it grew rapidly when A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy was published and fostered its spread in Germany. Johann Joachim Christoph Bode promoted it through extracts, a complete translation, and his portrayal of Sterne as an authority on the human heart. Translation and imitation then extended the cult into sentimental behavior and literature. By the late 1770s and early 1780s, its excesses brought growing satire and critical protest, though enthusiasm was strong enough for Lange to market Zückert’s defective translation as new.
