the magical crow transformation in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The magical crow transformation is caused when Grampa smokes three puffs of Yaga's magic tobacco. Bill becomes an unseen cock's crow, Tatters a great black crow, Urtha a vari-colored crow, and Grampa an old crow with a game leg. After three hours, they change back over the Winkie country.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The crow transformation occurs when Grampa smokes the tobacco. Bill becomes an unseen cock's crow, Tatters becomes a great black crow, Urtha becomes a crow with vari-colored feathers, and Grampa becomes an old crow with a game leg.

CHAPTER 15

The magical crow transformation changes Grampa, Urtha, and Tatters into crows for three hours. It ends at the minute because Grampa smoked three puffs of the magic tobacco, and the company changes back while descending over the Winkie country.
