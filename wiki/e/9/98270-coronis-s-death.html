Coronis's death

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Coronis’s death, deprived of life, he killed Coronis, poured forth her life, we two shall die in one, αὐτὴν δὲ ἀπέκτεινε
  • In The Library, it is the event in which Apollo kills Coronis after she chooses to live with Ischys, while he rescues her child from the pyre.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is the event in which Apollo's arrow pierces Coronis's breast, after which she bleeds, speaks final words, and dies.

Coronis's death in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Coronis’s death occurs when Apollo’s arrow pierces her breast; she removes it, bleeds, speaks final words, and dies in coldness.
