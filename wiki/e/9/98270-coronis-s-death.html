Coronis's death

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Coronis’s death, deprived of life, he killed Coronis, poured forth her life, we two shall die in one, αὐτὴν δὲ ἀπέκτεινε

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, it is the event in which Apollo kills Coronis after she chooses to live with Ischys, while he rescues her child from the pyre. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is the event in which Apollo's arrow pierces Coronis's breast, after which she bleeds, speaks final words, and dies.
