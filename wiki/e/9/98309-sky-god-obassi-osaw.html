Sky God Obassi Osaw

deity · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Obassi Osaw, Sky God, his master, the Sky God, the deity, the god

Abstract written by a model

Obassi Osaw, a Sky God in the Ekoi account, made everything at the world's beginning and lived in a sky house, keeping fire with his wives. He withheld fire from earth until the Lame Boy stole it, then sent his eldest son.

Sky God Obassi Osaw is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
