(8) Another Italian version of the story, recorded at Pisa,

The giant offers employment to the man of Florence, the curate, and the workman, but kills the curate and workman while pretending to give them their jobs. The man of Florence blinds him with boiling oil, and the giant pursues him while carrying a marble table and later gives him a ring that turns his finger to marble.
