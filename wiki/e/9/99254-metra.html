1 section; each heading opens the text

EXPLANATION.

Metra, the daughter of Erisicthon and wife of Autolycus, asked Neptune for the power to transform herself so that she could help satisfy her father’s hunger. She escaped a master by changing into a male fisherman, and later changed into various animals whenever Erisicthon sold her, providing him with dishonest means of support.
