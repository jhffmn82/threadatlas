Abstract written by a model

Myrrha's aged foster-mother prevents her suicide, draws out her secret despair, then promises and arranges help for her incestuous passion by enabling meetings with Cinyras during Cenchris's absence; Antoninus Liberalis names her Hippolyte.

FABLE VIII. [X.298-518]

Myrrha's nurse prevents her from hanging herself and persistently urges her to reveal the cause of her despair. After learning of the incestuous passion, the nurse promises assistance and arranges for Myrrha to meet Cinyras while Cenchris is away.
