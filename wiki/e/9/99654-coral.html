coral

Abstract written by a model

Coral is a sea plant that becomes stone and hardens in air after leaving the sea; its production may be explained by the defeat of the Gorgons.

Coral becomes hard when exposed to air. It retains the nature of a sea plant that turns to stone outside the sea after contact with Medusa's head.
