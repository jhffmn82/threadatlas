Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Hermaphroditus is the son of Mercury and the Cytherean Goddess, and his face shows the features of both parents. He rejects Salmacis, but after she seizes him in the spring, he is united with her and becomes neither wholly male nor wholly female; he then asks his parents to make men who enter the spring become half male.
