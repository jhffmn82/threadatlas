CHAPTER 19

The Lord High Humpus, who is also Chief Justice and handles state weddings, helps Peer Haps seek a groom for the Princess. He notices Tatters at the city gates, helps bring him into the palace, and mistakenly congratulates him on his marriage. He flees when Fumbo's disembodied voice frightens him.
