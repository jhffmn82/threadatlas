Ovid · 0008~

A treaty concerning Proserpine divides her residence between Ceres on earth and Pluto in the Infernal Regions: Apollodorus assigns nine months to Ceres and three to Pluto, while other writers give each six months.

notes .. notes

The treaty concerning Proserpine required her to remain part of the year with Ceres on earth and part with Pluto in the Infernal Regions. Apollodorus assigns nine months to Ceres and three to Pluto, while other writers divide the time equally.
