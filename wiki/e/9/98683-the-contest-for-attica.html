the contest for Attica in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

The contest for Attica was between Poseidon and Athena; Zeus appointed twelve gods, who awarded it to Athena after Cecrops testified she first planted the olive-tree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV. Κέκροψ αὐτόχθων, συμφυὲς ἔχων σῶμα

The contest for Attica was between Poseidon and Athena. Zeus appointed the twelve gods as judges, and they awarded the country to Athena because Cecrops testified that she had first planted the olive-tree.
