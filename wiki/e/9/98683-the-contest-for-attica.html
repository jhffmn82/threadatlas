Abstract written by a model

In The Library, it was the contest between Poseidon and Athena for Attica, awarded to Athena after Cecrops testified that she first planted the olive-tree. In Description of Greece, it is depicted behind the Parthenon, with Athene showing the olive tree and Poseidon showing water.

The contest for Attica was between Poseidon and Athena. Zeus appointed the twelve gods as judges, and they awarded the country to Athena because Cecrops testified that she had first planted the olive-tree.
