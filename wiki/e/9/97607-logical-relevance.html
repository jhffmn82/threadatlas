logical relevance in HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Hao Liu, Zhengren Wang, Xi Chen, Zhiyu Li, Feiyu Xiong, Qinhan Yu, Wentao Zhang · 2025

Logical relevance is an ultimate information-retrieval goal beyond lexical and semantic similarity. Current retrievers lack logic-aware mechanisms, causing distracting indirect retrievals and missed answer-supporting passages; HopRAG augments retrieval to better account for it.

Abstract

Logical relevance is identified as a dimension that traditional retrievers may overlook when they focus on lexical or semantic similarity. HopRAG augments retrieval to better account for logical relevance.

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

Logical relevance is presented as a retrieval goal beyond lexical and semantic similarity. Its absence causes systems to retrieve indirectly relevant or distracting passages and to miss passages needed to answer a query.
