Blanks in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

The Blanks are invisible inhabitants of Blankenburg whose visible clothes, hats, shoes, and gloves reflect an all-consuming obsession with dress. Once vain, haughty people devoted to splendid clothing, they became invisible after bathing in magic water and outlawed having a face. They pursue Snip for showing his face, quarrel over his buttons, and search Tora's shop before leaving confused by her pretended misunderstanding.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The Blanks are invisible people whose clothes, hats, shoes, and gloves can be seen. They chase Snip for showing his face in Blankenburg, quarrel over the buttons he throws, and search Tora's shop before leaving when Tora pretends not to understand them.

CHAPTER 14

The Blanks were once vain and haughty people who spent their time and money on splendid clothing. After bathing in the magic water, they became invisible, and they made it against the law to have a face in Blankenburg.
