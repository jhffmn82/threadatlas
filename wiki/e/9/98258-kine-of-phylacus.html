  • In The Library, the kine of Phylacus are originally Tyro's, are stolen by Iphiclus or Phylacus, and are recovered by Melampus and brought to Neleus as Pero's bridal gift.
  • In The Library, the kine of Phylacus are guarded by a dog that no person or beast can approach; Melampus steals them for Bias, is imprisoned for a year when detected, and drives them to Pylus so that Bias can receive Pero.

IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

The kine originally belonged to Tyro, the mother of Neleus, but were stolen and kept by Iphiclus or Phylacus depending on the account. Melampus recovered them and brought them to Neleus as Pero's bridal gift.
