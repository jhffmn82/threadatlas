Zückert translation of Tristram Shandy in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905 · the work’s page · read it where Zückert translation of Tristram Shandy first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zückert’s translation of the first six parts of Tristram Shandy was the first attempt to bring Sterne’s work into German. Published by Gottlieb August Lange under the Lange, Berlin und Stralsund imprint, it was repeatedly expanded, reissued, and marketed, even using Wieland’s name without authorization. Though weak, inaccurate, and sometimes nonsensical, it mattered as a first step in the German Shandy cult, despite limiting appreciation of Sterne.
