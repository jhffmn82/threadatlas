Sciron

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Sciron, the Corinthian, Σκείρωνα, Σκειρωνίδας

Abstract written by a model

Sciron is a Corinthian man, said to be son of Pelops or, in some accounts, Poseidon. At the Scironian Rocks in the Megarian region, he forces passersby to wash his feet, then throws them into the sea for his enormous turtle. Theseus ultimately seizes Sciron by the feet and throws him into the sea.

Sciron is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

APOLLODORI BIBLIOTHECA

Sciron was a Corinthian who was described as a son of Pelops, though some said he was a son of Poseidon. At the Scironian Rocks in the Megaric region, he forced passersby to wash his feet and then threw them into the depths as food for an enormous turtle; Theseus seized him by the feet and threw him into the sea.
