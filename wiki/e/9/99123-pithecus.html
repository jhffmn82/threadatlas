Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE FOURTEENTH.

Pithecusæ are barren, hill-situated islands encountered by Æneas’s ship. They are associated with the transformed Cercopes and are said to receive their name from the word signifying an ape.
