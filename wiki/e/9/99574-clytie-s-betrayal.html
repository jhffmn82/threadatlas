Clytie's betrayal in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Clytie, resentful of Leucothoë, reveals an affair to Leucothoë’s father; her betrayal leads to Leucothoë’s burial alive, though grief and love might have excused it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Clytie's resentment toward Leucothoë leads her to publish the affair and report it to Leucothoë's father. Her betrayal results in Leucothoë's burial alive, although the text says that love might have excused Clytie's grief and her grief the betrayal.
