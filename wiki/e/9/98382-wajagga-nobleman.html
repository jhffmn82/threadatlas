Abstract written by a model

The Wajagga nobleman undergoes the boiling a nobleman ceremony to make himself a name and prolong his life. He lies in a covered trench beneath a fire and boiling pot, sweats throughout the day, and receives the wizard’s blessing of long life and protection.
