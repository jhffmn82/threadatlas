Abstract written by a model

The Valley of Fear story is John Douglas’s written account, prepared during confinement and given to Dr. Watson. It recounts Douglas’s pursuit by enemies from America, Ted Baldwin’s attack, Baldwin’s accidental death, and Douglas’s plan to conceal himself by presenting Baldwin as the victim, while insisting he has told the truth.
