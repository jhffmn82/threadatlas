Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Ceres searches for Proserpina throughout all lands and seas, finding no rest and traveling from the rising to the setting of the sun. She carries burning pine torches through the darkness, becomes thirsty from her labor, and reaches a thatched cottage where an old woman gives her barley liquor.

opening

Ceres searches through the whole earth and across the seas, finding no immediate answer. Her search brings her to Sicily, where Cyane’s girdle and Arethusa’s testimony reveal Proserpine’s location.

notes .. notes

Ceres searched for Proserpine and received information about her from Arethusa. The Sirens were also said to have sought permission to become birds so that they could search for Proserpine, possibly reflecting a tradition that they equipped a ship for this purpose.
