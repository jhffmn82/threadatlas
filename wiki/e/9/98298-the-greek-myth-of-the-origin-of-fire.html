IIJ.—Myrus oF THE ORIGIN OF FIRE

The Greek myth explains the arrival of fire among men through Prometheus’s theft. The fire is variously said to come from Zeus himself or from the workshop of Hephaestus and Athena, and it may also be associated with Hephaestus’s fall from heaven to Lemnos.
