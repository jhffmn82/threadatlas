publication of Sterne’s letters in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

The publication of Sterne’s letters began in 1775 through Letters from Yorick to Eliza, Sterne’s Letters to His Friends, and Lydia Sterne de Medalle’s three-volume collection. That collection included Fragment in the manner of Rabelais and a June dedication to Garrick. Some letters’ authenticity remained uncertain, though others were accepted or standardly edited.

Sterne’s letters began appearing in print in 1775, including Letters from Yorick to Eliza, Sterne’s Letters to His Friends, and Lydia Sterne de Medalle’s three-volume collection. The authenticity of several letters was uncertain, although some were accepted as genuine or included in standard editions.
