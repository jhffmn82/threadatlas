Abstract written by a model

The publication of Sterne’s letters began in 1775 through Letters from Yorick to Eliza, Sterne’s Letters to His Friends, and Lydia Sterne de Medalle’s three-volume collection. That collection included Fragment in the manner of Rabelais and a June dedication to Garrick. Some letters’ authenticity remained uncertain, though others were accepted or standardly edited.
