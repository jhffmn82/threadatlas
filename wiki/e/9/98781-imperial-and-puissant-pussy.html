Imperial and Puissant Pussy in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

A cat queen and Queen who rules Catty Corners, the Imperial and Puissant Pussy receives Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi beneath a catsup tree, welcomes Mombi but condemns the others, bars Mombi's departure, plans their punishments, eats her pudding, and is lifted aloft with the cats.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

The Imperial and Puissant Pussy is the ruler whom the cat guard brings Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi to meet. She lies under a catsup tree attended by kittens and guards, then welcomes Mombi while condemning Snip and Pajuka.

CHAPTER 7

The Catty Queen refuses to let Mombi leave and plans to have Snip mauled and Pajuka plucked and eaten. She eats Mombi's rice cream pudding and is lifted into the air with the other cats.
