Metra's transformations in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Metra transforms from a male fisherman to mare, bird, cow, and stag, escaping repeated sales and supporting her starving father through deception and useful work.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

EXPLANATION.

Metra’s transformations include becoming a male fisherman to deceive one master and later becoming a mare, bird, cow, and stag. These changes enable her to escape repeated sales and provide food or other support for her starving father.
