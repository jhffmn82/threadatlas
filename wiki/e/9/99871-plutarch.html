Plutarch

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Plutarch is a writer and interpreter who authored How to read the Poets. He explains ritual head coverings and interprets the Muses’ encounter with Pyreneus as hostility to learning, while also describing Phaëton as a Molossian king and astronomy student and locating his tomb by the Po.

Plutarch is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Plutarch interpreted the adventure of the Muses with Pyreneus, and their asking the Gods for wings, as a metaphor for Pyreneus’s hostility to learning. According to this interpretation, Pyreneus destroyed institutions where learning was taught and was said to have violated the Muses before losing his life while pursuing them.
