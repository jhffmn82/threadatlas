Chapter 16

The Preserves are inhabitants of the Preservatory who are enclosed in glass jars, with their arms and legs passing through sealed openings. They become angry when the Flyaboutabus breaks one of their jars and pursue the travelers. They preserve themselves so they will not need to eat, drink, or work.
