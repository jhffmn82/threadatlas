Sun's palace in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The Sun’s palace stands on radiant columns, richly decorated with gold, carbuncle, ivory, silver, and Mulciber’s carvings of sea, Earth, Heaven, deities, cities, animals, and Zodiac signs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE SECOND.

The Sun’s palace is raised on radiant columns and decorated with gold, carbuncle, ivory, and silver. Its doors and carvings depict the sea, Earth, the heavens, deities, cities, animals, and the signs of the Zodiac.

Sun's palace is a palace (belonging to the Sun)1 quote
  1. “The palace of the Sun was raised high, on stately columns, bright with radiant gold, and carbuncle that rivals the flames; polished ivory covered its highest top, {and} double folding doors shone with the brightness of silver.”The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII · BOOK THE SECOND.
Sun's palace contains carvings of sea, Earth, and Heaven (created by Mulciber)1 quote
  1. “The workmanship {even} exceeded the material; for there Mulciber had carved the sea circling round the encompassed Earth; and the orb of the Earth, and the Heavens which hang over that orb.”The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII · BOOK THE SECOND.
Sun's palace contains Zodiac signs (six on each door)1 quote
  1. “Over these is placed the figure of the shining Heaven, and there are six Signs {of the Zodiac} on the right door, and as many on the left.”The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII · BOOK THE SECOND.

the Sun's palace in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Sun's palace, richly ornamented and created by Vulcan, stands on gold columns; its ivory ceilings and silver doors depict earth, sea, skies, zodiac, and inhabitants.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

LATONA AND THE RUSTICS

The Sun's palace stood on glittering gold columns with precious stones, ivory ceilings, and silver doors. Vulcan's workmanship depicted the earth, sea, skies, zodiac signs, and their inhabitants.
