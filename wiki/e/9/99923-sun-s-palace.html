Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Sun's palace stands on radiant columns and is richly decorated with gold, carbuncle, ivory, silver, and Mulciber's carvings of the sea, Earth, Heaven, deities, cities, animals, and Zodiac signs. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Sun's palace, richly ornamented and created by Vulcan, stands on gold columns, with ivory ceilings and silver doors depicting the earth, sea, skies, zodiac, and inhabitants.
