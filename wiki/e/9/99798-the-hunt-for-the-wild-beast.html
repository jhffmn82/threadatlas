Ovid · 0008~

A hunt by neighboring youths around Thebes seeks a wild beast sent by Themis. Nets and barriers fail as it leaps free; Lælaps pursues it across the plain while Cephalus watches and prepares his javelin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Neighboring youths surround the extensive fields with nets to hunt the wild beast sent against Thebes, but it leaps over the nets and barriers. Lælaps is released and pursues it in circles across the plain, while Cephalus watches from a hill and prepares his javelin.
