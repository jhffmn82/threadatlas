Abstract written by a model

Battus betrays Mercury's theft for an additional reward, so Mercury turns his perjured breast to stone, creating the Touchstone marked by an old disgrace.

opening

After Battus reveals Mercury's theft for an additional reward, Mercury turns his perjured breast into a hard stone. The transformed Battus is called the Touchstone, and the text says that an old disgrace is attached to the stone even though the stone itself deserves none.
