VI.—THE RENEWAL OF YOUTH

The learned Brahmin offered to cure Pra T’hong Rat Koma by killing him and placing him in a cauldron of boiling medicine. He demonstrated the medicine on a dog and later entered the cauldron himself, but the king threw all three drugs in at once, changing the Brahmin into a stone statue.
