Chapter IV

McMurdo is arrested at home by Captain Marvin while Boss McGinty is visiting him. He spends the night in a common cell with Baldwin and other accused men, but lodge members provide whisky and cards, and he is released after the next day’s hearing.

Chapter V

Jack McMurdo’s arrest and acquittal, occurring on the very night he joined the lodge, make him more popular among his fellow Scowrers. The event gives him a reputation for boldness and strengthens their belief that he is suited for violent work.
