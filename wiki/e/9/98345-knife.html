IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

Phylacus used the knife while pursuing Iphiclus and then stuck it into a wild pear-tree. According to one account, the knife accidentally touched Iphiclus's genitals, and its rust was later used to cure his childlessness.
