Abstract written by a model

The birth of Adonis occurs when the child conceived by Myrrha and Cinyras emerges through cracks in Myrrha’s transformed myrrh-tree; Lucina aids delivery, and Naiads anoint him with tears.

FABLE VIII. [X.298-518]

The child conceived by Myrrha and Cinyras grows within the transformed tree. The tree opens through cracks in its bark, and the child is delivered with the help of Lucina; the Naiads lay him on the grass and anoint him with his mother's tears.
