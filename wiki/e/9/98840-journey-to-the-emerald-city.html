Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15

Dorothy, Humpy, Tora, and Snip begin the journey to the Emerald City with Kabumpo carrying them. Kabumpo travels swiftly across the wheat, trumpeting as he clears a path, because they hope to arrive before Mombi.

CHAPTER 18

Ozma and her faithful followers make a weary journey north from the Quadling Country toward the Emerald City. After the golden goose feather reports that the King of Oz is in the palace, they hurry onward and reach the capital hot, tired, and dusty.
