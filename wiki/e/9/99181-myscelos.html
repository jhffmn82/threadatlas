Myscelos

Abstract written by a model

Myscelos, an Argive son of Alemon favoured by the Gods, is commanded by Hercules in a dream to leave Argos for land by the Æsar. Though tried under Argive law forbidding departure, Hercules aids his acquittal; he sails to Italy and founds Crotona.

Myscelos is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK THE FIFTEENTH.

Myscelos, the son of Alemon and an Argive, is warned by Hercules to leave his native country and settle by the Æsar in Italy. After being accused of violating Argive law, he is miraculously acquitted and sails to Italy, where he founds Crotona.
