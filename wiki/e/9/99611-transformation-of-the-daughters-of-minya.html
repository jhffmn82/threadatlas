Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The transformation occurs as the daughters hide from fires and light. A membrane stretches over their small limbs, covers their arms with light wings, and leaves them with diminutive squeaking voices; they then fly by night and derive their name from the late evening.
