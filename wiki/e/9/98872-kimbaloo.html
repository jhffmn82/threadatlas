Abstract written by a model

Kimbaloo is a cheerful, merry kingdom in the Gilliken country, with about five hundred inhabitants who live comfortably by selling buttons and bouquets. Its palace is served by 249 button boys and 250 flower girls, and it has a Town Crier and Town Laugher. Everyone is happy except Mombi, who fears exposure as a witch; she leaves sadly with Snip and Pajuka.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Kimbaloo is a cheerful kingdom in the Gilliken country with about five hundred inhabitants. Its people live comfortably through the sale of buttons and bouquets, and everyone is happy except Mombi.

CHAPTER 2

Kimbaloo is a kingdom in the Gilliken country of northern Oz. Mombi enjoys special privileges there as a cook, but she fears being exposed as a witch and expelled. Snip leaves Kimbaloo sadly with Mombi and Pajuka.

CHAPTER 3

Kimbaloo is a merry Kingdom with both a Town Crier and a Town Laugher, because its people do not ordinarily shed tears. It has two hundred and forty-nine little button boys and two hundred and fifty little flower girls serving around the palace.
