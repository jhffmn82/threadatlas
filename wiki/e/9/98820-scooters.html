Scooters

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Scooter, Scoots, playfellows, rivermen

Abstract written by a model

Scooters are tall, angular rivermen with narrow boat- or ski-like feet and bright yellow sails from ankles to fingertips. They skim, turn, and race over water, eating water cress, water melons, and fish, which they believe grows sails and feet. They carry Dorothy and Humpy across a river and invite Dorothy to learn scooting.

Scooters is in one work on this site, The Lost King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 12

The Scooters are tall, angular creatures with ski-like feet and bright yellow sails extending from their ankles to their fingertips. They skim, turn, and race across the water, eat water cress, water melons, and fish, and believe that fish help their sails and feet grow. They carry Dorothy and Humpy across the river and invite Dorothy to stay and learn to scoot.
