Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Pygmies are a supposedly diminutive people described by ancient writers, variously placed in Ethiopia, India, Scythia, and parts of Africa, and traditionally said to wage continual warfare with Cranes; later explanations seek a historical or natural basis for this story. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Pygmies are a nation of dwarfs about a cubit tall, said to live near the Nile, defending cornfields from cranes and once attacking sleeping Hercules.

The Pygmies are described as a supposedly diminutive people whom ancient writers placed in various regions, including Ethiopia, India, Scythia, and parts of Africa. They were traditionally said to be in continual warfare with the Cranes, and several later explanations attempt to identify the historical or natural basis of the story.
