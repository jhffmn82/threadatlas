Abstract written by a model

is_a proposed explanation [for the serpent transformation story] Cadmus's Phoenician origin was proposed as a basis for the story that he and Hermione became serpents. Bochart connected the name Cadmus with Cadmonia, the eastern part of Phoenicia.
