object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections

  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Midas's ass ears were given to him by Apollo as punishment; he hid them under a purple turban, but his barber's whispered secret was revealed by reeds.

Narrative, section by section

FABLE III. [XI.146-193]

Apollo gives Midas ears that are long, covered with grey hairs, unsteady at the lower part, and capable of movement. Midas hides them under a purple turban, but the secret is eventually revealed by reeds growing from the place where his barber whispered it.
