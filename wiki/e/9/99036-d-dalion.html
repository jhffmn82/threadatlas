Abstract written by a model

Dædalion, Lucifer’s active, warlike, violent son and Chione’s father, grieves her death, attempts suicide from Parnassus, and Apollo transforms him into a hawk.

FABLES V. AND VI. [XI.221-409]

Dædalion is described as active, warlike, and violent, unlike his peaceful brother Ceyx. After Chione's death, he grieves intensely, attempts to throw himself from Parnassus, and is transformed by Apollo into a hawk.
