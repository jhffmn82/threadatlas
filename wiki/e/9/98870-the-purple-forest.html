Abstract written by a model

In The Lost King of Oz, the Purple Forest is where Mombi, Pajuka, and Snip stop for the night; it has purple pines, lengthening shadows, and a grim, forbidding appearance in the twilight. In The Giant Horse of Oz, the purple forest is a deep, impenetrable yet beautiful purple-toned place around the good witch's cottage, where Philador and Herby enter and emerge at a rushing torrent.
