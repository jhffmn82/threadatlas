the purple forest in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The purple forest is a deep, impenetrable yet beautiful purple-toned place around the good witch's cottage, rich in roots, herbs, vines, flowers, streams, and lavender fragrance; Philador and Herby enter it and emerge at a rushing torrent.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

A deep, impenetrable purple forest surrounds the good witch's cottage. Philador and Herby enter it while following the thinking cap's directions to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 9

The forest is grim but richly colored and beautiful, with purple-tinged trees, vines, flowers, streams, and the fragrance of lavender. Philador and Herby pass through it and emerge at a rushing torrent.
