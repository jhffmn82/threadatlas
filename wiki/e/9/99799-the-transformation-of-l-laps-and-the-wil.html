the transformation of Lælaps and the wild beast in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

A divine transformation ends Lælaps and the wild beast’s evenly matched speed contest: as Cephalus looks away to prepare his javelin, both become marble statues in the plain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

As Lælaps and the wild beast remain evenly matched in speed, Cephalus looks away while preparing to throw his javelin. When he looks back, both have become marble statues standing in the middle of the plain, apparently frozen in their contest.
