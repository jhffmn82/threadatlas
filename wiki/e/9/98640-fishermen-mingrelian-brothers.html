fishermen (Mingrelian brothers)

Abstract written by a model

Mingrelian fishermen spend months at sea, send catches home weekly, and carry honey and wax after leaving fish. Towed to a rocky shore, they are captured by a one-eyed giant; surviving brothers blind him after he devours most.
