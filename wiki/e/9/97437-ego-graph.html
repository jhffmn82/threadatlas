ego-graphs in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

An ego-graph is a K-hop neighborhood centered on a node, uniquely identified, encoded, indexed, and retrieved by GRAG for queries. It differs from a tree through additional within-level or cross-level edges: Breadth-First Search extracts its rooted tree part, Tg, while Eg holds non-tree edges. GRAG searches these graphs; 2-hop versions outperform 1-hop ones in the reported WebQSP study, whereas larger subgraphs may cost more time, oversmooth, and add irrelevant information.

ego-graph in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025-07-12

A K-hop ego-graph is a neighborhood centered on an ego node, assigned a unique identifier, encoded as a graph embedding, and retrieved to constrain search space. Unlike a tree, it can contain additional intra-level or cross-level edges. For textual representation, it is divided into a BFS-derived tree rooted at the ego node and a remaining edge set, then described hierarchically. It supports divide-and-conquer textual subgraph retrieval; on WebQSP, retrieved 2-hop ego-graphs outperform 1-hop ones at equal retrieval counts, though too many may introduce irrelevant information. Larger-than-3-hop ego-graphs are subject to oversmoothing.
