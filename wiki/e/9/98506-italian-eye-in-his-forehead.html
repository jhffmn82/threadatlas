Abstract written by a model

Italian Eye-in-his-forehead is a one-eyed giant with an enchanted ring who eats Big Brother and plans to eat Little Brother, until Little Brother blinds him, escapes, and cuts off the ring-bearing finger.

Italian Eye-in-his-forehead is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
