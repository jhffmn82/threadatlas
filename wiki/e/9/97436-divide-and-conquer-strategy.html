method · 2 works · 15 quoted facts · 5 connections
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the divide-and-conquer strategy retrieves relevant K-hop ego-graphs around top-ranked nodes, approximates textual relevance by summing neighborhood scores, and uses soft pruning to refine their union toward the optimal textual subgraph.

divide-and-conquer strategy in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

The divide-and-conquer strategy retrieves the most relevant K-hop ego-graphs around top-ranked important nodes, treating their union as the subgraph. It approximates textual relevance by summing neighborhood scores, then uses soft pruning to refine and union these partial neighborhoods toward the optimal textual subgraph.

divide-and-conquer strategy in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025-07-12

The divide-and-conquer strategy is a retrieval strategy for efficient textual subgraph retrieval. It treats a retrieved subgraph as a union of partial neighborhoods around important nodes, searches for important ego-graphs, ranks them to select top-ranked key nodes, and retrieves relevant ego-graphs first. It then uses soft pruning to refine and union K-hop ego-graphs, approximating an optimal subgraph in linear time.
