Abstract written by a model

The stork, king of the storks and Floria’s grateful helper, leads the birds to seek the mountains holding the fountains of life and death. An old, maimed stork finds them, fills the two bottles, and escapes after the mountains tear off his tail.

V.—Tue CriasHine Rocks

The stork, who is king of the storks, gathers the birds to ask whether any have seen the mountains containing the fountains of life and death. An old, maimed stork undertakes the journey, follows the swallow’s instructions, fills bottles with the two waters, and escapes after the mountains catch and tear off his tail.
