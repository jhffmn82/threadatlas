death of Procris in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Procris’s death, caused by a javelin wound, followed Cephalus’s mistaken belief that noise from her concealment in a thicket was a wild beast. Resentment was believed to have contributed. Before dying, she learned Aura was only the breeze and became more contented and carefree.

Procris's death resulted from Cephalus’s mistaken belief that the noise she made while concealed in the thicket came from a wild beast. Before dying, she learned that Aura was only the breeze and appeared more contented and free from care.
