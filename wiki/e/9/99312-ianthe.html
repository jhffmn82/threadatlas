Abstract written by a model

Ianthe, beautiful daughter of Telestes, shares instruction and love with Iphis, believing Iphis male; after Iphis’s transformation, she marries Iphis.

Ianthe is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Ianthe is a beautiful virgin of Phæstus and the daughter of the Dictæan Telestes. She receives the same early instruction and shares an equal love with Iphis, believing Iphis to be male, and ultimately becomes Iphis's wife.
