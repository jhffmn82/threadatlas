Anaxarete's transformation into stone

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Anaxarete's transformation into stone is a mythic transformation explained as a metaphor for extreme insensibility. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Anaxarete's transformation into stone is memorialized at the temple of Venus at Salamis by a statue in her exact form.
