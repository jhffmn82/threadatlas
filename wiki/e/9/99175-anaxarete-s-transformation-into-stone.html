Anaxarete's transformation into stone

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: all her limbs became stony like her heart, changed into a rock, the statue, the statue under the form of the maiden, the stone
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Anaxarete's transformation into stone is a mythic transformation explained as a metaphor for extreme insensibility.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Anaxarete's transformation into stone is memorialized at the temple of Venus at Salamis by a statue in her exact form.
