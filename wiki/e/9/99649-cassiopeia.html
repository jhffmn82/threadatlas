Cassiopeia

person · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Cassiope, his queen, mother, that starred Aethiop queen, the mother, Κασσιεπείας

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, she is Andromeda's mother who boasts of her beauty against the Nereids, suffers punishment, mourns at the rock, and rejoices when Perseus rescues Andromeda. In The Library, she is the wife of Cepheus and mother of Andromeda who claims to be more beautiful than the Nereids, angering them. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, she is Aethiopia’s queen and Andromeda’s mother who compares her beauty to the Sea-Nymphs’, provoking anger and a sea-monster, and becomes an upside-down constellation after death.
