the four elements

Also called: four elementary bodies, the elements

Abstract written by a model

The four elements are everlasting elementary bodies: earth and water, heavy, move downward; air and fire, light, seek higher regions.

FABLES II. AND III. [XV.60-478]

The four elements are earth, water, air, and fire. Earth and water are heavy and move downward, while air and fire are lighter and seek the higher regions.
