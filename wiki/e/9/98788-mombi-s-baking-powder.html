Mombi's baking powder in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Mombi's marvelous magic baking powder, collected and refined for twenty years, is powerful enough to raise an army. Used in pudding, it makes the Catty Queen and cats rise into the air. By varying amount and method, it raises hair, roofs, rents, castles, or cities; an emptied, watered can restores a castle.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The baking powder in Mombi's purple can is powerful enough to raise an army. Mombi has collected and refined it for twenty years, and she uses it in the pudding to make the Catty Queen and the cats rise into the air.

CHAPTER 19

The magical baking powder can raise hair, roofs, rents, castles, or cities according to the amount and method used. Snip empties a can onto the desired spot, adds water, and raises the castle back to its foundations.
