  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the query is encoded with text attributes by the same pre-trained language model used for subgraphs, measures semantic relevance through cosine comparison with K-hop ego-graph embeddings, selects top-N subgraphs, scales soft pruning of nodes and edges, forms a hard prompt with hierarchical description, and conditions generation.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the query is provided over a textual graph and encoded by a pre-trained language model as a query embedding, measures semantic relevance to K-hop ego-graphs through cosine similarity, serves as the reference for pruning distant entities, and, combined with an optimal textual subgraph, guides retrieval and generation and appears in the hard prompt with the hierarchical description.
