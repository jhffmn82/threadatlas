Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE III. [X.106-142]

The eagle was Jupiter’s chosen form for carrying off Ganymede because it could carry his bolts. It was also described as Jupiter’s attendant bird and as a creature able to fly higher than other birds and gaze at the sun.
