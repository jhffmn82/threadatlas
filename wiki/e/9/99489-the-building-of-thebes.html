the building of Thebes

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: building the city, built the city, city of Thebes, found a city, foundation of Thebes
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the building of Thebes is ordered by the oracle of Phoebus and carried out by Cadmus with the five surviving earth-born warriors as his associates.
  • In The Library, the foundation of Thebes begins when Cadmus follows a cow according to the oracle at Delphi, kills the dragon guarding the spring of Ares, and establishes the city there.
