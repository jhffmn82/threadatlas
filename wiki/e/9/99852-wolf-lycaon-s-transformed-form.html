wolf (Lycaon's transformed form)

Abstract written by a model

The wolf is Lycaon's transformed form after Jupiter's punishment. Garments become hair and arms become legs, yet vestiges of Lycaon remain: hoariness, violent features, bright eyes, and ferocity. It continues to desire slaughter and blood, attacking sheep.
