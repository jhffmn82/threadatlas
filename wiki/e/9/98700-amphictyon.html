Amphictyon in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Amphictyon is a king who expels Cranaus and rules in his place for twelve years. Accounts differ on his origin, calling him either a son of Deucalion or autochthonous, a son of the soil. His reign ends when Erichthonius expels him.
