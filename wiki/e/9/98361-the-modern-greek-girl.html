V.—Tue CriasHine Rocks

The modern Greek girl enters a mountain that opens briefly at noon to fetch the water of life. When the mountain catches her dress as it closes, she cuts the skirt with her sword and escapes, later using the water to restore her two brothers, who had been turned to stone.
