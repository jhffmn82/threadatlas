In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, graph embedding represents each indexed candidate subgraph by mean-pooling pre-trained language-model embeddings of node and edge text attributes, producing a d-dimensional vector stored for retrieval. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, graph embedding, denoted as zg, represents a subgraph and is obtained by mean pooling pre-trained language model embeddings of its node and edge text attributes into dimension d.
