Tantalus's punishment in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

A punishment in Hades perpetually confines Tantalus in a lake beneath an overhanging stone: water reaching his jaws dries when he tries to drink, while winds lift nearby fruit trees to the clouds when he tries to eat.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II. Ὅτι ὁ Τάνταλος ἐν “Atdov? κολάζεται,

A stone hangs above Tantalus, the water of the lake reaches his jaws but dries up when he tries to drink, and the fruit-bearing trees are lifted by winds as high as the clouds when he tries to eat.
