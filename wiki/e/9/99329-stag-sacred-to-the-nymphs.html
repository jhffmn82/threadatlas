A tame, fearless stag sacred to the Nymphs of the Carthæan fields, adorned with golden horns and jewelled ornaments, was Cyparissus’s favorite; he tended it at pasture and water, then accidentally killed it with a javelin.

FABLE III. [X.106-142]

The stag sacred to the Nymphs of the Carthæan fields had golden horns, jeweled ornaments, and no fear of people. It was especially beloved by Cyparissus, who led it to pasture and water and accidentally killed it with a javelin.
