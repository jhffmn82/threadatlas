Cranaus

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Kpavads, Kpavaov, Κραναὸς

Abstract written by a model

Cranaus, an autochthonous son of the soil, became king at Athens after Cecrops. He married Pedias and had children including Cranaichme, Cranae, Menaechme, and Atthis. During his reign Deucalion's flood occurred. After Atthis died unmarried, he named the country Atthis; Amphictyon later expelled him.
