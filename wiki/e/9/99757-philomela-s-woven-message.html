Philomela's woven message

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: piece of cloth, tale, web, web of Barbarian design

Abstract written by a model

A white and purple cloth bears Philomela’s purple woven account of Tereus’s rape, carried by her attendant to Progne, who reads it.

Philomela's woven message is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Philomela weaves purple marks into a white and purple cloth to disclose Tereus's crime. She gives it to an attendant, who carries it to Progne, and Progne reads the mournful account.
