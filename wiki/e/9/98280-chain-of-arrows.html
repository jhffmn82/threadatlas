Abstract written by a model

A chain of arrows forms as each arrow sticks in the preceding arrow’s notch, creating a ladder from earth to sky. In one account it is shaken into a rope. It can break or be knocked down, stranding beings above or making them fall.

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

The chain of arrows is formed when each arrow sticks in the notch of the preceding one, creating a ladder between earth and sky. It breaks or is knocked down in several versions, leaving some beings stranded above or causing others to fall.

XI.—PHAETHON AND THE CHARIOT OF THE SUN

In the Bella Coola and Kwakiutl stories, arrows shot upward strike one another and form a means of reaching heaven. In the Kwakiutl account, the arrows are shaken into a rope by Born-to-be-the-Sun’s mother.
