Rumanian giant in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

A giant captures three brothers and eats two, then hunts the youngest after being blinded; a revealing ring betrays the youth until it is thrown into water, drowning the giant.

(2) All the distinctive features which we have just remarked

The Rumanian giant captures three brothers, eats the eldest and second brother, and tries to catch the youngest. After being blinded, he pursues the youngest because a ring reveals the youth’s location, but he drowns when the severed finger and ring are thrown into the water.
