the Clashing Rocks in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Listed as the fifth appendix heading, the Clashing Rocks are Greek-mythology rock formations and a folk-tale motif: two cliffs clash and separate, barely letting a hero or messenger pass, regarded as story-teller’s invention rather than solar myth or icebergs.

V.—Tue CriasHine Rocks

The Clashing Rocks are presented as a folk-tale motif in which two huge cliffs clash together and separate again, barely allowing a hero or messenger to pass. The text regards the episode as more probably a story-teller’s invention than a solar myth or a memory of icebergs.
