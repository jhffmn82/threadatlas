Abstract written by a model

Graph context is incorporated into generation through textual and topological information represented in text and graph views. GRAG uses this context to enhance generation and support multi-hop reasoning on textual graphs. Graph context is incorporated into both the retrieval and generation stages of GRAG. It is represented through hierarchical text descriptions and graph-based soft prompts so that LLMs can use relationships and topology alongside textual information.
