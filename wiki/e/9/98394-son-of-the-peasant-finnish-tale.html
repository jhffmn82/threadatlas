Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

The son survives being wounded and cast into the sea, is raised at a monastery, and later kills his employer while guarding a field. He marries the employer’s widow, discovers that she is his mother, kills several men while seeking penance, and eventually receives reassurance that no further expiation is required.
