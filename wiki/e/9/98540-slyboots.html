Slyboots in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Slyboots, a Lapp youngest-son figure, defeats a Stalo by blinding him with molten lead, cooks his dog, frees sheep, disguises himself in a ram’s skin, and escapes the fold.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

(10) A Russian story, which belongs to the same class,

Slyboots persuades the Stalo that molten lead will make him sharp-sighted, then pours it into both of the Stalo’s eyes. He cooks the Stalo’s dog for him, releases the sheep, disguises himself in the big ram’s skin, and crawls out of the fold.
