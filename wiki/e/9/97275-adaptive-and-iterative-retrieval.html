3.3

Adaptive and Iterative Retrieval dynamically adjusts retrieval according to query complexity, required modality, granularity, relevance, and feedback from earlier steps. It includes methods that route queries, determine retrieval hops, filter irrelevant content, decompose multimodal queries, update queries, and progressively refine results.
