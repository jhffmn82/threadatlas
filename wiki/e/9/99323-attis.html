Attis

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Athis, Cybeleïan Attis, shepherd, young shepherd

Abstract written by a model

Attis, a shepherd or priest loved by Cybele, is associated with her worship and ritual mutilation; in an account referred to by Ovid, he loses human form and becomes a pine tree pleasing to Cybele.

Attis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
