Wegener’s Raritäten, ein hinterlassenes Werk des Küsters von Rummelsberg in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

A novel dedicated in its first volume to Sebaldus Nothanker, it deliberately imitates Laurence Sterne’s tone, style, and typography. It unfolds through abrupt, conversational, affectedly merry prose and verse, reproducing ribaldry without charm or grace and exemplifying perverted literary taste.

Wegener’s Raritäten is presented as an example of the pernicious influence of Sterne, consisting of abrupt, conversational, affectedly merry prose and verse that reproduce Sterne’s ribaldry without his charm and grace. Its author explicitly describes trying to absorb Sterne’s spirit, while the work’s success is cited as evidence of perverted German literary taste.
