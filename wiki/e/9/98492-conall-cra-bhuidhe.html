Abstract written by a model

Conall Cra Bhuidhe is a man and father of Conall's sons. As a young lad he hunts on his father's land. Later he and his sons try to steal the King of Lochlann's brown horse, are caught, and he saves their lives by recounting adventures against a one-eyed giant, marked by his severed finger.
