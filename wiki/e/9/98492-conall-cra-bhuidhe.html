Conall Cra Bhuidhe tries to steal the brown horse of the King of Lochlann with his sons. After being caught, he saves their lives by telling of his adventures, including how he blinded a one-eyed giant, escaped in a buck’s skin, and defeated the giant with a ring and a severed finger.
