Swiss translation of Yorick’s Sermons in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

A Swiss translation of Yorick’s Sermons began publication in 1766, reordered the sermons, and aimed to interest readers in religious discourse; its preface omitted Sterne and Tristram Shandy while addressing cultured readers apart from instruction.

The Swiss translation of Yorick’s Sermons began publication in 1766 and was undertaken because its translator expected interest in the sermons as religious discourses. Its preface gave no information about Sterne or Tristram Shandy and stated that such sermons might benefit cultured readers who stood apart from religious instruction.
