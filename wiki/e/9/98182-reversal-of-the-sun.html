reversal of the sun

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: backward motion of the sun, sun reversed his course in the sky, the sun set in the east

Abstract written by a model

The reversal of the sun’s course occurs after Thyestes agrees to make it the condition for Atreus’s rule. It serves as a divine indication that Thyestes has acted improperly and that Atreus should receive the kingdom.
