Ligdus

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: her husband, the father

Abstract written by a model

Ligdus is a poor but honorable freeborn man of the Phæstian land, married to Telethusa. Fearing he cannot support a daughter, he orders a female infant killed, yet unknowingly accepts his daughter Iphis as a boy.

Ligdus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
