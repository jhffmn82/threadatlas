Ligdus is a poor but honorable freeborn man of the Phæstian land, married to Telethusa. Fearing he cannot support a daughter, he orders a female infant killed, yet unknowingly accepts his daughter Iphis as a boy.

Ligdus is a poor but honorable freeborn man of the Phæstian land. During Telethusa's pregnancy, he commands her to put a female child to death because he lacks the means to raise a daughter, but he unknowingly accepts Iphis as a boy.
