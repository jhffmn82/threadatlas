red umbrella in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The huge red umbrella, saved by King Fumbo for a rainy day, belongs to Tatters and protects the journey. In a storm it is lifted skyward carrying Grampa, Urtha, and Tatters, then Tatters carries it down the rainbow.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

The huge red umbrella is the only thing King Fumbo had saved for a rainy day. Prince Tatters carries it with him to protect against danger during the journey.

CHAPTER 15

The red umbrella belongs to Tatters and is carried during the journey. The storm lifts it into the sky with Grampa, Urtha, and Tatters clinging to it, and Tatters later carries it down the rainbow.
