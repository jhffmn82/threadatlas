Orpheus's death

event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Orpheus died, Orpheus was slain, he dies, his blood, murder of Orpheus, punish Orpheus, the death of Orpheus, the destruction of the bard, the murder of Orpheus, the women who slew Orpheus, this crime, tore him limb from limb
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the murder of Orpheus is punished by the transformation of the Bacchanals into trees; the women of Thrace attack him, scatter his limbs, and carry away his head and lyre on the river Hebrus.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the death of Orpheus is attributed by different traditions to Thracian women, a lightning strike from the god, or Orpheus's suicide from grief after losing Eurydice; the Macedonians of Pieria say that women killed him there.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Orpheus's death is caused by Thracian maidens who attack him during the rites of Bacchus, tear him limb from limb, and throw his head and lyre into the river Hebrus.

is_caused_by Thracian maidens [who tear him limb from limb] Orpheus's death occurred when the Thracian maidens, enraged by his rejection of them, attacked him during the rites of Bacchus.
