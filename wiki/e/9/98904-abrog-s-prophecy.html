Abrog’s prophecy in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Abrog’s prophecy is a prophecy about the Princess of Perhaps City, written on a prophecy parchment. It foretells that in four days a supposed monster will marry the Princess, alarming Peer Haps and prompting thoughts of an immediate marriage for her. It becomes one of the city’s strange events and is linked to the disappearances of the Princess and Abrog. Its fuller wording reveals that the “monster” is Tatters, a youth in an old bear skin with two heads and a red umbrella.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Abrog’s prophecy states that in four days a monster will marry the Princess. The prophecy alarms Peer Haps and leads him to consider arranging the Princess’s immediate marriage to prevent the monster from taking her.

CHAPTER 9

The prophecy about the monster is one of the strange events in Perhaps City that Percy Vere recounts to Dorothy. The unit does not reveal the prophecy's exact contents.

CHAPTER 17

Abrog's prophecy concerned a monster and was connected to the disappearance of the Princess of Perhaps City and Abrog himself. Grampa distrusts the prophet and suspects that Abrog ran away with the Princess.

CHAPTER 20

The prophecy says that a youth wrapped in the skin of an old bear, with two heads upon his shoulders and carrying a red umbrella, will marry the Princess of Perhaps City. Dorothy and the others determine that it describes Tatters, not a monster.
