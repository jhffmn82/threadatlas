Mustafa in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Mustafa of Mudge is the blue-whiskered, cross-eyed, wicked monarch and ruler of Mudge. Wealthy from selling stone lions, he keeps 9,999 and a half lions in a royal reservation and cares for them more than for his subjects; they are also the only thing that makes him laugh. Bored by kingship, he becomes determined to add the rarer Cowardly Lion to his collection after seeing him in a lion book. He intends to imprison the Lion in Mudge, fitting him with a jeweled collar and heavy gold chain and using him as a fighter against his other lions. Mustafa governs through orders and the dreadful power of his magic ring. He commands Notta and Bob Up to travel toward the Emerald City and capture the Cowardly Lion, providing food and directions while threatening to turn them blue and immobilize them if they disobey. When they fail, he activates the ring and does so. He watches the ring almost constantly, and his steady squinting leaves him permanently cross-eyed. He also enchants Notta and the Cowardly Lion, binding them by rope and making the ring summon them if they travel wrongly. When the Cowardly Lion reaches Mudge, Mustafa welcomes him, has Guards collar and chain him, and orders him into the lion enclosure to fight 9,999 lions. Yet Crunch attacks the enclosure, and Mustafa is petrified with terror. He loses the Cowardly Lion, his other lions, and his ring; at Ozma's command he surrenders the ring and shuffles mournfully back to his tent.

Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Mustafa of Mudge is identified as the person daring to hunt the Cowardly Lion. He is described as having blue whiskers, and the first chapter and several later chapters concern him and his actions.

Chapter 1

Mustafa rules Mudge and keeps 9,999 and a half lions in a royal reservation. He demands another, rarer lion and decides that he wants the Cowardly Lion of Oz after seeing him in Famous Lions of Oz.

Chapter 3

Mustafa of Mudge is the ruler of Mudge. He examines Notta in his lion disguise, decides that he is a cowardly lion but not the Cowardly Lion, and orders him taken to the other lions.

Chapter 4

Mustafa, the Monarch of Mudge, is furious when Notta reveals that he is not a lion but decides to send him after the Cowardly Lion. He gives Notta provisions and directions, and warns that his magic ring will turn him and Bob blue and immobilize them if they disobey.

Chapter 5

Mustafa has ordered Notta and Bob Up to travel toward the Emerald City and warned them about the Cowardly Lion. He has also supplied packets containing sandwiches, figs, honey, and blue tea.

Chapter 13

Mustafa has ordered Notta and Bob to capture the Cowardly Lion and threatened them with his magic ring if they disobeyed. When they fail to capture the lion, he activates the ring, causing Notta and Bob to turn blue and become immobile.

Chapter 15

Mustafa sits on his blue throne and watches his magic ring almost constantly, growing fearful from squinting. He wants the Cowardly Lion to appear in Mudge and is becoming unhappier and more unpleasant as the Uns teach him Unish. He orders a jeweled collar and heavy gold chain prepared to keep the Cowardly Lion from leaving.

Chapter 17

Mustafa of Mudge has enchanted Notta and the Cowardly Lion, forcing them to remain connected by a rope and causing Mustafa's ring to summon them if they move in the wrong direction. The Cowardly Lion and Crunch travel toward Mudge in the hope of confronting him.

Chapter 20

Mustafa is eager to capture the Cowardly Lion and welcomes him into Mudge while failing at first to notice Crunch. He has Guards place a gold collar and chain on the lion and orders him taken into the royal enclosure to fight the 9,999 lions.

Chapter 21

Mustafa is petrified with terror while Crunch attacks the lion enclosure. After losing the Cowardly Lion, his other lions, and his ring, he obeys Ozma's command to surrender the ring and shuffles mournfully to his tent.
