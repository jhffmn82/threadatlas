Anius’s daughters

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Anius’s four daughters receive extraordinary gifts from Liber, transforming things by touch into corn, wine, and Minerva’s oil. Compelled by the son of Atreus to provision the Argive fleet, they seek Bacchus’s help and end as snow-white doves.

FABLES V. AND VI. [XIII.623-718]

Anius’s daughters received gifts from Liber that enabled them to transform things into corn, wine, and the berry of Minerva. The son of Atreus compelled them to provision the Argive fleet; after they sought help from Bacchus, they lost their human forms and became snow-white doves.
