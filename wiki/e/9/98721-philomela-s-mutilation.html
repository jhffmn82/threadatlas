Philomela's mutilation in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

involved tongue [Tereus cut it out] After violating Philomela and telling her that Procne was dead, Tereus cut out her tongue and concealed her. Philomela then conveyed her suffering to Procne by weaving letters into a garment.
