Philomela's mutilation

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: cut away, cut out her tongue, this crime, τὴν γλῶσσαν ἐξέτεμεν αὐτῆς

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, it is the event in which Tereus cut out Philomela's tongue and concealed her. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is the event in which Tereus cuts out Philomela's tongue with his sword after she threatens to expose his crime.
