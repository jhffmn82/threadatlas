Hercules' rescue of Hesione

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Alcides delivers her, Hercules delivers her, Hercules offered to deliver her, rescue of Hesione

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hercules delivers Hesione from the sea monster and demands the horses that Laomedon promised as his reward. In The Library, Hercules undertakes Hesione's rescue for Tros's horses and secures it by killing the sea monster, paralleling Perseus and Andromeda's maiden-rescue pattern.
