retrieval-then-pruning approach in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

A retrieval-then-pruning approach retrieves promising ego-graph neighborhoods, merges top-N, then soft-prunes redundant nodes and edges, limiting search to |V| ego-graphs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

4 .. 4.1

The retrieval-then-pruning approach limits the retrieval space to |V| ego-graphs rather than the full direct-search space of 2|V|+|E|. It retrieves promising neighborhoods first and then reduces redundant nodes and edges through pruning.
