Abstract written by a model

In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the retrieval-then-pruning approach retrieves promising ego-graph neighborhoods, merges the top-N, and soft-prunes redundant nodes and edges while limiting search to |V| ego-graphs. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the retrieval-then-pruning approach restricts search to |V| ego-graphs, retrieves and ranks promising candidates, retains the top ones, and prunes redundant nodes and edges to yield an approximately optimal subgraph structure.
