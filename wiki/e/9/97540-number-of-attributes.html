The number of attributes, denoted m, affects quality and privacy leakage: too few miss essential information; increases improve performance until gains may stop; smaller numbers reduce exposure.

Ablation Studies

A very small number of attributes can produce poor performance because essential information is missed. Increasing the number of attributes improves performance until it no longer necessarily continues to increase, while smaller numbers reduce privacy exposure.
