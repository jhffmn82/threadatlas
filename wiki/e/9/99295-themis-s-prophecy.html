FABLE IV. [IX.324-425]

Themis prophesies the Theban civil war and several connected acts of violence, including Amphiaraüs's death, Alcmæon's vengeance against a parent, and his later murder by the Phegeïan sword. She also foretells that Calirrhoë will ask Jupiter to make her infant sons men, and that Jupiter will grant the request through Hebe's gift.
