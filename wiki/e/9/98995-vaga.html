CHAPTER 4

Vaga is the robber chief who captures Grampa and Tatters and complains that they possess little of value. He decides to make them outlaws after Grampa pretends to welcome the idea, but Grampa’s long story puts Vaga and the other bandits to sleep. Grampa then takes back his possessions and removes a blue pouch and a bottle of patent medicine.
