Abstract written by a model

M . . . R . . ., published at Eisenach in 1772 by E. A. A. von Göchhausen, is a well-received Sterne imitation that later had several editions. It uses brief named chapters, sentimental incidents, digressions, reader interruptions, eccentric typography, and defenses against theft. It includes the Hamster episode, The Monolog, Die Nacht, and Utopian moon fancy. It begins closely dependent on Sterne, but that dependence diminishes as it progresses.

M . . . R . . . is an unusually well-received work inspired by Sterne and was judged worthy of several later editions. It imitates the Sentimental Journey in its brief titled chapters, sentimental incidents, digressions, reader interruptions, eccentric typography, and self-conscious defenses against accusations of theft, but its dependence on Sterne diminishes as it progresses.
