CHAPTER 5

The journey to the Emerald City takes Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi through the Gilliken forest and countryside. They face a dangerous weenix and the Hoopers, then continue along a zig-zag path toward the Corners.

CHAPTER 7

Mombi leads Snip and the sleeping Pajuka out of Catty Corners and through a dark forest toward the Emerald City. During the journey, she becomes increasingly determined to find a way to destroy Snip before he can reveal her plans.
