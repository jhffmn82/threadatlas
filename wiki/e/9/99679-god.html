God in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

God is a deity, architect, Contriver, and Creator associated with ordering and forming the universe. With Nature, God ends Chaos’s primordial discord by dividing it into four Elements, separating earth, sea, heaven, and atmosphere, assigning proper places and inhabitants, and bringing the elements into harmonious regularity. God shapes the world and its sea, rivers, landforms, climates, air, winds, stars, and living creatures, commands plains with ease and infinite power, and forms man from earth, water, and divine elements, giving man an upward-looking countenance and dominion. Some ancient philosophers describe God not as creator but as organizer of pre-existent matter. Seeing mankind’s great wickedness, God resolves to punish earth and destroy mankind and other living creatures by a deluge, while promising that Deucalion’s deluge will be the last.

6 sections; each heading opens the text

front matter

The Deity divides Chaos into four Elements, assigns inhabitants to them, and creates man from earth and water.

opening

God, with bounteous Nature, ends the discord in Chaos by separating the earth from the heavens, the waters from the earth, and the clear heavens from the gross atmosphere. He combines the separated elements in harmonious unison and assigns each to its proper place.

notes .. notes

God is presented in the explanation as the powerful cause whose invisible agency reduces Chaos to order. The ancient system described in the unit does not treat God as the Creator, but as the one who organizes pre-existent matter.

opening

God, or the Artificer of all things, divides the mass into distinct members, shapes it into a globe, and orders the sea, rivers, landforms, climates, air, winds, stars, and living creatures. He gives Man a countenance that looks upward toward the heavens.

notes .. notes

God is presented as the Creator who formed man from divine elements and gave him dominion over the other living works of creation. The unit compares Ovid’s account of God breathing life into man with the account in Genesis.

notes .. notes

God sees that mankind's wickedness is great and decides to destroy man and other living creatures from the earth.
