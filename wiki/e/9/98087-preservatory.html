Preservatory in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

The Preservatory is a strange city in Oz beyond a fringe of trees, entered through glittering glass gates. Unlike any city or town the travelers have seen, it has glass streets, steps, buildings, and three-storied glass houses. It contains jarred inhabitants called Preserves and is ruled by the Imperial Squawmos.

Chapter 14

The strange city lies beyond a fringe of trees and has glittering glass gates. It appears unlike any city or town the travelers have previously seen, even to the experienced Cowardly Lion.

Chapter 16

The Preservatory is a city whose glass-fronted houses contain jarred inhabitants called Preserves. Its streets, steps, and buildings are made of or covered with glass, and its ruler is the Imperial Squawmos.
