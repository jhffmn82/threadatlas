This study is a comprehensive review of multimodal RAG systems. It categorizes key advances in retrieval, multimodal fusion, augmentation, generation, training strategies, and agents, then examines task-specific applications, datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation methods. Drawing primarily on recent work curated from major venues and arXiv, it concisely describes methodologies, identifies open challenges, and outlines promising future directions intended to inspire research. It does not compare model performance and may miss emerging or domain-specific contributions; it plans continuing updates to the survey and repository.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Conclusion

The study provides a comprehensive review of multimodal RAG, covering advancements, task-specific applications, datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation methods. It highlights open challenges and promising future directions.

Limitations

The study curates research from major venues and arXiv, focusing primarily on recent advancements, but its selection may overlook emerging or domain-specific work. It plans to continuously monitor developments and update the survey and corresponding repository.
