popularity selective retrieval policy in Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation

Shijie Wang, Wenqi Fan, Yue Feng, Shanru Lin, Xinyu Ma, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin · 2025

Popularity selective retrieval policy determines whether items in LLM-based recommender systems are augmented with retrieved knowledge, using item popularity. It retrieves items below a predefined popularity threshold, helping cold-start items with limited knowledge while reducing retrieval time. In the main experiments, the threshold is 50%. As the threshold increases, recommendation performance initially improves but later declines, and inference time rises almost linearly. The policy therefore balances retrieval efficiency, knowledge augmentation, and performance; reinforcement learning may later update it dynamically.

The popularity selective retrieval policy determines whether an item should be augmented from the knowledge graph. An item is retrieved when its popularity is below a predefined threshold, reducing retrieval time while addressing the limited knowledge often available for cold-start items.

The popularity selective retrieval policy controls which items receive retrieved knowledge based on a threshold p, set to 50% in the main experiments. Increasing p initially improves recommendation performance but eventually reduces it, while inference time increases almost linearly with p.
