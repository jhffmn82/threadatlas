2

A few works explore using RAG with language models for recommendations, including leveraging knowledge from movie or book datasets. Retrieving faithful structured knowledge from knowledge graphs for recommendations is described as under-explored and promising.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.8

The recommendation task asks the model to select a user’s preferred item from a candidate set containing one positive sample and multiple negative samples. K-RagRec augments this task with knowledge sub-graphs retrieved from a knowledge graph.
