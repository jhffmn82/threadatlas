Abstract written by a model

Recommendations use RAG with language models and book or movie knowledge. An under-explored, promising direction is retrieving faithful structured knowledge from knowledge graphs. K-RagRec retrieves knowledge sub-graphs to help select a user’s preferred item from candidates containing one positive and multiple negative samples.
