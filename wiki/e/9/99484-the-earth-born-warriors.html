  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII: The earth-born warriors are a race of armed men, brothers newly sprung from the earth. They arise from the dragon's teeth as a crop, become maddened, and kill one another in civil war, leaving five survivors: Chthonius, Udeus, Hyperenor, Pelor, and Echion. In the concluding interpretation, they are people of the country or soldiers won by Cadmus, who assist him in conquering enemies and building Thebes's citadel.

the earth-born warriors in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The earth-born warriors are a race of armed men, brothers newly sprung from the earth. They arise from the dragon's teeth as a crop, become maddened, and kill one another in civil war, leaving five survivors: Chthonius, Udeus, Hyperenor, Pelor, and Echion. In the concluding interpretation, they are people of the country or soldiers won by Cadmus, who assist him in conquering enemies and building Thebes's citadel.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The earth-born warriors arise from the dragon's teeth as a crop of armed men. They become maddened and kill one another in civil war, leaving only five survivors.

notes .. notes

The earth-born men were said to emerge after Cadmus sowed the dragon’s teeth. The explanation interprets them as local people or soldiers whom Cadmus won to his side; they helped him conquer his enemies and build the citadel of Thebes.
