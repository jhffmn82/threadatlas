The third volume’s concluding self-criticism is presented as an unusually candid condemnation of Schummel’s own extensive work. Schummel acknowledges imitation, bad taste, indelicacy, failed attempts at whimsical mannerisms, and the inadequacy of his effort to characterize the German people as Sterne characterized the English and French.
