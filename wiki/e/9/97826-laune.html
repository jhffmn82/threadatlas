Laune in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Laune originates in Aristophanes, encompasses Laurence Sterne among English writers, and animates Sterne’s pages; German writers and reviewers debated Sterne’s humor, praising Tristram Shandy while Mendelssohn initially struggled to understand it.

The first mention of Sterne in the _Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen_ is in the nu

German writers and reviewers repeatedly discussed Sterne in relation to humor. Tristram Shandy was praised as an original and exceptionally strange work of wit and humor, while Mendelssohn initially struggled to grasp its humor and compared Sterne with Liscow.
