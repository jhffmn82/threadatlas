Nicolai in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Nicolai writes a Berlinische Monatsschrift résumé of Ferriar’s findings on Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings. He nonetheless defends Sterne as refined, ingenious, humorous, sensitive, and inventive, arguing that creative additions and distinctive qualities sustain his reputation. 1 section; each heading opens the text

Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings, his high-handed and extensive appropriation

Friedrich Nicolai summarized Ferriar’s findings in the February 1795 issue of the Berlinische Monatsschrift. He defended Sterne as a refined, ingenious, and humorous author whose creative additions and distinctive qualities justified his reputation, and he maintained that source investigations should explain rather than depreciate him.
