Ocyrrhoe's transformation into a mare

Abstract written by a model

Ocyrrhoe’s divine transformation into the mare Enippe is a wondrous change, possibly explaining her exceptional riding and horse-handling skill. Desiring grass and open plains, her human form withdraws: fingers become a hoof, face and neck lengthen, hair becomes tail and mane, and her voice and shape change.

opening

Ocyrrhoe’s human shape withdraws as she desires grass and wishes to range over the plains. Her fingers join into a hoof, her face and neck lengthen, much of her hair becomes a tail, and the remaining hair becomes a mane; her voice and shape change, and she receives the name Enippe.

notes .. notes

The transformation of Ocyrrhoë into a mare may have been a legendary explanation of her exceptional skill in riding and handling horses. The story may also reflect the belief that horsemen of her age were mistaken for half-human, half-horse monsters.
