Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE NINTH.

The combat takes place because both Hercules and Acheloüs seek to marry Deïanira. They wrestle fiercely, and Acheloüs uses multiple forms in an effort to gain the advantage, but Hercules prevails.
