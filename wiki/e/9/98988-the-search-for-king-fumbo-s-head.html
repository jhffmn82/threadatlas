the search for King Fumbo’s head in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The search for King Fumbo’s head is a dangerous undertaking begun when Pudge sends Prince Tatters, accompanied by Grampa, to recover the missing head. During the journey, Tatters, Grampa, and Bill pass through Gorba’s garden, find no head there, and leave with Urtha to continue searching.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

Head hunting is the dangerous task Prince Tatters undertakes to find King Fumbo’s missing head. Pudge sends Tatters to perform it with Grampa as his companion.

CHAPTER 6

Tatters, Grampa, and Bill are traveling through Gorba’s garden as part of their search for King Fumbo’s lost head, the Prince’s fortune, and a Princess. Although they do not find King Fumbo’s head in this unit, they bring Urtha with them when they leave the garden and continue the journey.
