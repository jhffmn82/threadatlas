siege of Megara

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The siege, siege, siege of Alcathoë, the war, this sad war
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the siege is part of the war between Minos and Nisus and ends when Scylla removes the purple lock, allowing Minos to control the city.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the siege lasts six months because fate decrees that Megara cannot be taken while Nisus's purple lock remains, until Scylla cuts it off.
