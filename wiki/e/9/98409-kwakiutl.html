Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

The Kwakiutl believed that twins were salmon who had assumed human form and could bring salmon. In one story, a twin woman called Salmon-Maker produced salmon for her husband and his people, but withdrew them after her husband treated her and the salmon disrespectfully.

XI.—PHAETHON AND THE CHARIOT OF THE SUN

The Kwakiutl Indians tell of Born-to-be-the-Sun, whose mother becomes pregnant when sunlight shines on her back. Their story describes his ascent to the Sun, his attempt to take the Sun’s place, the resulting destruction, and his being thrown back to earth.
