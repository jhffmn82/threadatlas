Chin Chilly the Third

Abstract written by a model

Chin Chilly the Third is the snow King who rules Isa Poso and is father of its Princess. He offers half his kingdom and the Princess to whoever slays Enorma, rejects then accepts news of Tatters’s victory, and leads others toward Isa Poso. Keeping his word, he brings the Princess to Tatters, then severs her hand and isolates Isa Poso.

CHAPTER 12

Chin Chilly the Third is the King of Isa Poso and offers half the kingdom and Princess Poso to whoever slays Enorma. He initially objects to Urtha’s flowers and footprints, but becomes pleased when he learns that Tatters has slain the dragon. He then leads Grampa and the others toward the kingdom.

CHAPTER 13

Chin Chilly brings the Princess of Isa Poso to Tatters and insists on keeping his word. He snaps off his daughter's hand, drags her back to Isa Poso, and has the snow men chop away the land connecting the iceberg to the island.
