Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE FOURTH.

Pyramus and Thisbe live in adjoining houses and fall deeply in love, though their fathers forbid their marriage. They communicate through a small crack in the party-wall and arrange to meet secretly outside the city.
