Hercules's apotheosis

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: apotheosis, apotheosis of Hercules, assumption of the hero into Olympus, placed in the number of the Gods, translates him to the heavens, worshipped as a Demigod, ἀναπέμψαι

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hercules's apotheosis occurs after the flames consume what he derived from his mother; Jupiter bears his surviving immortal nature aloft in a chariot drawn by four horses and places him among the stars as a god. In The Library, the apotheosis of Hercules occurs after the pyre burns: Hercules is carried to heaven in a cloud accompanied by thunder, receives immortality, and is reconciled with Hera.
