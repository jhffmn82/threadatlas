Icarius in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Icarius, a father of Penelope and husband of Periboea, is exiled from Lacedaemon with Tyndareus by Hippocoon and his sons, then returns after Heracles kills them. Dionysus teaches him wine-making; shepherds kill him after undiluted wine makes them think they are bewitched, and Maera helps Erigone find his body.

Εἰσὶ δὲ οἱ λέγοντες ᾿Αφαρέα μὲν καὶ Λεύκιππον

Icarius was expelled from Lacedaemon by Hippocoon together with Tyndareus. He later returned after Heracles killed Hippocoon and his sons. With Periboea, he had five sons and a daughter, Penelope, whom Odysseus married.

When Cecrops died, Cranaus came to the throne?!;

Icarius received a vine branch from Dionysus and learned wine-making. He gave the drink to shepherds, who, believing themselves bewitched after drinking it undiluted, killed him; his dog Maera later helped Erigone find his body.
