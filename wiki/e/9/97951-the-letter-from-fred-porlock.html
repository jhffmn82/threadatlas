the letter from Fred Porlock in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

A letter signed by Fred Porlock warns that Professor Moriarty suspects his betrayal, says Porlock will go no further, and asks Sherlock Holmes to burn an unusable cipher message lacking its key.

PART I / Chapter I

The letter from Fred Porlock says that Professor Moriarty suspects Porlock and that Porlock will go no further in the matter. It asks Sherlock Holmes to burn the cipher message, which Porlock believes can no longer be useful.
