Arion

Abstract written by a model

Arion is a famous musician favored by Periander. After winning a Sicilian contest, sailors threaten him for his treasure; he sings, leaps into the sea, and a dolphin rescues him. He reaches shore safely and exposes the sailors to Periander.

Arion is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XVIII

Arion was a famous musician favored by Periander, king of Corinth. After winning a musical contest in Sicily, he was threatened by sailors who wanted his treasure, but he sang before leaping into the sea, where a dolphin carried him safely to shore; he later exposed the sailors to Periander.
