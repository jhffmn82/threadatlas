Abstract written by a model

Siegwart, published in 1776, is a popular sentimental novel that renewed and redirected sentimentalism by offering an outlet for emotions linked to Sterne’s influence. It became a favorite of Frau Kurt and a target of Timme’s satire, especially through its followers and imitators; Timme distinguishes the work itself from their excesses and from reading that ignores people in need.
