destruction of Niobe's children

Abstract written by a model

A divine vengeance for Niobe’s contempt toward Latona, possibly reflecting a plague at Thebes. Apollo and Diana kill Niobe’s fourteen children: the sons first by arrows, then the daughters despite Niobe’s plea for the youngest. The deaths are attributed to Apollo and Diana, and the children remain unburied because people fear contagion.

notes .. notes

The destruction of Niobe's children is presented as possibly based on the historical fact of a plague at Thebes. The deaths were mythically attributed to Apollo and Diana, while the account also says that the children remained unburied because people feared contagion.
