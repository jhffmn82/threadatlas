Ovid's exile

Abstract written by a model

takes_place_in Tomi [on the shore of the Euxine Sea] Ovid’s exile began when Augustus ordered him to leave Rome for Tomi around 8 A.D. Its exact cause remained unclear, though it was probably connected to an objectionable composition and scandals involving members of Augustus’s family. Ovid never returned to Rome and died in banishment at Tomi.
