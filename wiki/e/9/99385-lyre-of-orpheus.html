lyre of Orpheus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

is_placed_among stars [by Jupiter after Orpheus's death] The lyre of Orpheus was given to him by Apollo and enabled him to produce music that charmed living creatures, trees, and rocks. It protected him briefly from the weapons and stones of the Thracian maidens, but it was thrown into the river Hebrus with his head after his death and was later placed among the stars by Jupiter.

Chapter XVII

The lyre of Orpheus was given to him by Apollo and enabled him to produce music that charmed living creatures, trees, and rocks. It protected him briefly from the weapons and stones of the Thracian maidens, but it was thrown into the river Hebrus with his head after his death and was later placed among the stars by Jupiter.
