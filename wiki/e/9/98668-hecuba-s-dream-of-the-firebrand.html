Ἱστορία δὲ! ἡ περὶ τοῦ παλλαδίου τοιάδε

Hecuba’s dream showed her giving birth to a fiery firebrand that spread through the entire city and burned it. Aesacus interpreted the dream as meaning that the child would bring ruin to the country.
