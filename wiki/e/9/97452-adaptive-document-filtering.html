Abstract written by a model

Adaptive document filtering uses τq to remove irrelevant or noisy retrieved documents before answer generation. The case studies show that it can retain useful evidence for questions about Montxu Miranda and Gmina Czorsztyn, although a noisy filtered set leads to an incorrect answer about Arcangelo Ghisleri's occupation.
