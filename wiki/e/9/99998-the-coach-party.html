the coach party

Also called: the party, the passengers, the stage party

Abstract written by a model

The coach party, including Ned Brice and Frank Frenshaw, is awakened when the stagecoach stops near an obstruction by the site of a former robbery. Uneasy passengers shelter in a cabin; Flo’s humor eases the tension, and they hurry back to the coach without questioning Yuba Bill.

The coach party is awakened when the stagecoach stops and becomes uneasy after learning that the obstruction is near the site of a former robbery. Several passengers shelter in the cabin, where Flo’s humor breaks the tension, and they later return hurriedly to the coach without questioning Yuba Bill as planned.
