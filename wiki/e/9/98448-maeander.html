Abstract written by a model

Maeander, son of Cercaphus and Anaxibia, wars against Pessinus and vows the Mother of the Gods the first person to congratulate him if victorious. He sacrifices Archelaus, his wife, and daughter in fulfilment, then throws himself into the river that bears his name.

XII.—TuE Vow or [DOMENEUS

Maeander, son of Cercaphus and Anaxibia, vowed to the Mother of the Gods that he would sacrifice the first person to congratulate him if he won a war against Pessinus. His son Archelaus, his wife, and his daughter met him, and he sacrificed them before throwing himself into the river that thereafter bore his name.
