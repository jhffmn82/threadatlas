Eleusinians

group · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: people of Eleusis

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, the Eleusinians were at war with the Athenians, calling on Eumolpus and his Thracian force to fight on their side. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Eleusinians fought against Erectheus, and their conflict ended with Eumolpus and Erectheus killed in battle. In Description of Greece, the Eleusinians retained private management of the Eleusinian Mysteries under the peace settlement.
