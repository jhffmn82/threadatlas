royal robe of the King of Oz in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

The royal robe of the King of Oz is preserved by Fairy Lurline in a silver casket at the castle of Morrow. Its label says it can restore the King with Incantation No. 986. It fits several wearers without effect, but after fitting Tora, it helps restore Pastoria and reveal his true identity.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The royal robe of the King of Oz is found inside the silver casket in the castle of Morrow. Its label says that it was preserved by Fairy Lurline and that it can restore the King to his proper shape when used with Incantation No. 986.

CHAPTER 20

The King's robe fits Humpy perfectly but does not reveal him as the King. It also fits Sir Hokus and the Soldier with the Green Whiskers without effect, but when Tora wears it, the robe helps restore Pastoria.
