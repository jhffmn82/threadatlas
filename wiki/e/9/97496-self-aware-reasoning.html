Self-aware Reasoning in SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zijun Yao · 2025

Self-aware Reasoning is an adaptive integration strategy in SEAKR for complex questions. It supports iterative retrieval, gathering multiple knowledge pieces, then synthesizes all retrieved knowledge with previously generated rationales after retrieval stops. It compares reasoning using rationales with reasoning using retrieved knowledge and selects the answer with lower generation uncertainty. Its combined, ensemble-learning approach matters most: rationale-only or knowledge-only replacements perform worse.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Self-aware reasoning supports iterative knowledge retrieval to gather multiple pieces of knowledge for complex questions. SEAKR compares direct generation and comprehensive reasoning strategies and selects the one producing the least generation uncertainty.

3 .. 3.4

Self-aware reasoning synthesizes previously generated rationales and retrieved knowledge after retrieval halts. SEAKR generates answers using reasoning with generated rationales and reasoning with retrieved knowledge, then uses the strategy with the lower uncertainty.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

Self-aware Reasoning integrates previously retrieved knowledge and functions as ensemble learning. Replacing it with rationale-only or knowledge-only strategies produces inferior results, and it is applied only to complex QA in the reported ablations.
