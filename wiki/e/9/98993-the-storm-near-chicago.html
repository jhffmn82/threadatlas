The storm near Chicago is forecast by Invisi-Bill, then arrives with strong wind, thunder, and rain, scattering camp materials, lifting an umbrella and several people skyward, animating Bill, enabling a rainbow journey, and explaining King Fumbo's lost head.

CHAPTER 15

A storm is forecast by Invisi-Bill and soon arrives with strong wind, thunder, rain, and scattered camp materials. Its gale lifts the red umbrella and carries Grampa, Urtha, Tatters, and Invisi-Bill above the storm area into the sky. The storm also explains how King Fumbo's head was blown away.
