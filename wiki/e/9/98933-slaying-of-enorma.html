slaying of Enorma in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Tatters, helped by Grampa, slays Enorma after snuff makes her lose her teeth; she flees into an icy stream, and they moor her body before claiming a reward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

The slaying of Enorma is accomplished when Grampa uses snuff to make her sneeze and lose her teeth, after which she flees and plunges into an icy stream. Grampa and Tatters moor her body to a tree as proof of their valor before claiming the reward.
