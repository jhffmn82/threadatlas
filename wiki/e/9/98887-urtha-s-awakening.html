Urtha’s awakening in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Urtha’s awakening is the moment gold watering can contents bring the earth-made flower maiden to life in Gorba’s garden; Tatters sprays her, then Urtha rises, laughs, dances, speaks, and names herself.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Tatters sprays Urtha from head to toe with the gold watering can. After the last drop falls, the flower maiden rises from her bed, laughs, dances with Tatters and Grampa, and begins speaking. She explains that she is named Urtha because she is made of earth.
