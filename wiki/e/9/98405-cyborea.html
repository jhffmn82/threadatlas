Cyborea

Abstract written by a model

Cyborea, married to Ruben Simeon and mother of Judas Iscarioth, dreams her son will destroy the family, abandons him at sea, then unknowingly marries him after he kills Ruben Simeon and reveals their bond.

VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

Cyborea dreams that her son will be fatal to the family and agrees with her husband to cast the newborn child into the sea. Later, unknowingly widowed by her son and given to him in marriage, she reveals the truth that leads Judas Iscarioth to recognize their relationship.
