Baucis and Philemon's transformation into trees

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Philemon and Baucis's transformation into trees, changing in like manner, made of their two bodies, made out of the two good old people, shooting into leaf, two trees
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the divine end-of-life transformation that preserves Philemon and Baucis as adjoining trees after temple service, as they farewell each other before the sacred steps while branches cover their faces.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is the event in which Baucis and Philemon grow old, transform into trees before the temple, exchange farewell, and stand side by side.

Philemon and Baucis's transformation into trees in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

A divine end-of-life transformation preserves Philemon and Baucis as adjoining trees after temple service; before sacred steps, they farewell each other as branches cover their faces.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

After a long life serving the temple, Philemon and Baucis begin to grow into trees while standing before its sacred steps. They exchange farewell as long as they can speak, and branches cover their faces at the same moment.

Baucis and Philemon's transformation into trees in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Baucis and Philemon grow old, transform into trees before the temple, exchange farewell, and stand side by side.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

When Baucis and Philemon had grown very old, they began changing into trees while standing before the temple. They exchanged a final farewell and became two trees standing side by side.
