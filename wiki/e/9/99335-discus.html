Abstract written by a model

Discus is a broad throwing implement made of brass, iron, stone, or wood; Apollo throws one, and its rebound from hard ground strikes Hyacinthus’s face.

FABLE III. [X.106-142]

Apollo and Hyacinthus played with a broad discus. After Apollo threw it, Hyacinthus ran to retrieve it, but the hard ground sent it rebounding into the air and against his face.
