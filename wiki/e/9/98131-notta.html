Also called: Notta Bit More, a brave man, chalk-faced wizard, chief clown, clown, handsome wizard, old Notta, that person, the clown, the kindly clown, the poor clown, their jolly companion, wizard
  • In The Cowardly Lion of Oz: Notta Bit More is a former circus chief clown from the United States, a traveler, performer, and companion of Bob Up. Known for his white face, many jokes, quick disguises, tricks, and rules of disguise, politeness, joke, and running, he is naturally nice and harmless despite often relying on comic evasions. After he and Bob arrive in Mudge through a magic rhyme, Mustafa orders Notta, under threat of execution, to capture the Cowardly Lion. Notta begins the journey intending to comply, but increasingly likes and protects the Lion, eventually refusing to betray him even when Mustafa's magic ring turns Notta blue and immobilizes him. With Bob, the Lion, and later Nickadoodle, he travels through the Seven Doors, Un, the Preservatory, forests, and toward the Emerald City, using disguises, humor, magic verse, and the Flyaboutabus while facing repeated captures, crashes, and dangers. At the Emerald City, disguised as a witch, he gains help from Ozma and the Wizard and carries a rescue party to Mudge. After the danger is resolved, he tells of the journey and chooses to remain in Oz rather than return to earn money in America. He lives in a tent on the outskirts of the Emerald City with Snorer and Bob, performs shows with Oz's inhabitants, and saves enough for Bob's entire education.

Notta in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Notta Bit More is a former circus chief clown from the United States, a traveler, performer, and companion of Bob Up. Known for his white face, many jokes, quick disguises, tricks, and rules of disguise, politeness, joke, and running, he is naturally nice and harmless despite often relying on comic evasions. After he and Bob arrive in Mudge through a magic rhyme, Mustafa orders Notta, under threat of execution, to capture the Cowardly Lion. Notta begins the journey intending to comply, but increasingly likes and protects the Lion, eventually refusing to betray him even when Mustafa's magic ring turns Notta blue and immobilizes him. With Bob, the Lion, and later Nickadoodle, he travels through the Seven Doors, Un, the Preservatory, forests, and toward the Emerald City, using disguises, humor, magic verse, and the Flyaboutabus while facing repeated captures, crashes, and dangers. At the Emerald City, disguised as a witch, he gains help from Ozma and the Wizard and carries a rescue party to Mudge. After the danger is resolved, he tells of the journey and chooses to remain in Oz rather than return to earn money in America. He lives in a tent on the outskirts of the Emerald City with Snorer and Bob, performs shows with Oz's inhabitants, and saves enough for Bob's entire education.

Narrative, section by section

19 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The chief clown tries many stunts and jokes to make the unresponsive audience laugh. He tosses the red-headed orphan boy while reciting a rhyme, causing the boy to disappear, and then disappears himself after repeating the magic words.

Chapter 3

Notta introduces himself to Bobbie Downs and explains that his name came from his parents' disagreement over naming him. He disguises himself as a lion, but the Mudgers capture him and bring him before Mustafa of Mudge; when he is ordered taken to the lions, Bobbie reveals his identity.

Chapter 4

Notta Bit More explains that he is a clown, not a lion or wizard, and that he and Bob Up came to Mudge through a magic rhyme. Mustafa orders him to capture the Cowardly Lion and bring him back to Mudge, so Notta agrees to go and leaves with Bob.

Chapter 5

Notta decides that he and Bob Up must hunt the Cowardly Lion before the lion finds them. He comforts Bob, sings him to sleep, uses marshmallows as clown face powder, and plans to use his bear disguise. He travels with Bob toward the Emerald City and becomes anxious when they reach the seven doors.

Chapter 6

Notta examines the Seven Doors with Bob Up while searching for Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion. He is frightened by the baby dragon, is thrown into blueberry bushes by Door Six, and is dragged through the Queen's door by its knob. Disguised as a bear, he uses buttons as supposed badges of rank and is accused by Queen Adora the First of stealing door jam until Bob reveals his identity.

Chapter 7

Notta tells King Theodore and Queen Adora about his circus life, his flight to Mudge, and Mustafa's demand that he capture the Cowardly Lion. He attempts to amuse the rulers with jokes, acrobatics, and tricks, then escapes with Bob Up through a door marked "Out." After meeting the half lion, he promises to tell a wizard in the Emerald City about the lion's condition and continues north with Bob.

Chapter 9

Notta Bit More disguises himself as a huntsman while traveling with Bob Up, hoping to frighten away wild animals. After the Cowardly Lion attacks him, Notta explains that he and Bob came from Mudge and are traveling to the Emerald City to seek Dorothy's help in returning to the United States. He secretly intends to capture the Cowardly Lion for Mustafa, although he has begun to like him.

Chapter 10

Notta travels with Bob Up and the Cowardly Lion of Oz in the balloon and tries to calm the situation by being extremely polite to the strangers on Un. He learns that the Uns plan to push the travelers off the skyle and leads Bob and the Cowardly Lion away from the palace with fishing rods and reels.

Chapter 11

Notta fishes with Bob Up from the edge of the Skyland and helps pull up a cooked goose. He finds water, sings to cheer Bob, releases the skye terrier, and later falls asleep after opening a package labeled "Dreams for a big boy." When the Uns attack, he wakes and uses the magic verse to send them to Mudge, saving himself and the Cowardly Lion.

Chapter 12

Notta is saved by the Cowardly Lion after being pushed from the skyle by the Uns. He meets Nickadoodle, learns about the Isle of Un and the Flyaboutabus, and helps bring the vehicle low enough for the Cowardly Lion to board. He grows green feathers and escapes aboard the Flyaboutabus with Bob and the Cowardly Lion.

Chapter 13

Notta helps operate and land the Flyaboutabus after escaping Un, then finds food at the Travelers' Tree. He refuses to betray the Cowardly Lion to Mustafa and is turned blue and immobilized when Mustafa uses the magic ring. After Nickadoodle ties a rope to the Cowardly Lion and puts the end in Notta's hand, Notta becomes mobile and returns to normal.

Chapter 14

Notta pilots the Flyaboutabus during the flight, but accidentally makes it spin when he presses the button marked "Turn" during the lightning storm. After recovering from the crash, he helps determine that straight ahead leads toward Mudge and disguises himself as a huge fish before driving toward the strange city.

Chapter 15

Notta has departed with Bob and continues to affect Mustafa through the magic ring. When Notta determined not to betray his faithful four-footed friend, the ring turned black and Mustafa attacked Panapee in anger.

Chapter 16

Notta drives the Flyaboutabus through the Preservatory while disguised as a fish. After the Preserves arrest him, he tries to answer them humorously, then questions the Imperial Squawmos about her preservation system. He presses the only undamaged upward button, escapes into the sky, survives the crash into a day star, and is finally caught by a stone man.

Chapter 17

Notta is caught by Crunch along with Bob Up, Nick, and the Cowardly Lion. He explains their situation with Mustafa's enchantment and decides that he, Bob, and Nick should go to the Emerald City for help while the Cowardly Lion travels toward Mudge. After reaching the forest, he prepares a bed for Bob and discovers the trees' magical music.

Chapter 18

Notta disguises himself as a witch so that Princess Ozma and the Wizard of Oz will supposedly pay attention to him. He travels with Bob Up and Nick in the Fiddlebow Boat, is drenched twice by Dorothy, and is taken to the palace after the guards capture him.

Chapter 19

Notta Bit More is mistaken for a witch because he arrives wearing a black cloak and disguise. The Wizard of Oz proves that he is not a witch, after which he removes the disguise and explains his rules of disguise, politeness, joke, and running. He introduces Bob Up and Nickadoodle, begins telling his story, and then transports a rescue party to Mudge.

Chapter 20

Notta Bit More is described as being in danger, which motivates the Cowardly Lion to continue toward Mudge. He later arrives at the lion enclosure with Bob and the others just before Mustafa's lions attack.

Chapter 22

Notta tells Ozma and the others about his adventures, including Doorways, Un, Preserva the Great, the Flyaboutabus, and the Fiddlebow Boat. Although he initially says he must return to America to earn money for Bob's education and his old age, he accepts Ozma's offer to stay in Oz and later saves enough to pay for Bob's entire education.
