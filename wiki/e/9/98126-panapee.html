Panapee in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Panapee is a tall, thin Mudger who serves Mustafa of Mudge as royal chamberlain and purse bearer. Envious of rival Tazzywaller, he becomes chief chamberlain by proposing a rare lion, consults a book of lions, and has Tazzywaller pursue the Cowardly Lion. He later captures disguised Notta and, on Mustafa's order, imprisons him with other lions.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Panapee is Mustafa's purse bearer and an envious rival of Tazzywaller. He becomes chief chamberlain after suggesting that Mustafa obtain a rare lion from another country, produces Famous Lions of Oz, and proposes that Tazzywaller capture the Cowardly Lion of Oz.

Chapter 3

Panapee seizes Notta by the ear and presents the disguised clown to Mustafa as the lion the ruler wanted. Mustafa orders Panapee to take Notta away and lock him up with the other lions.
