King Fumbo

person · 1 work · 21 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Fumbo, Fumbo’s, King of Ragbad, King’s, father, father’s, head of Fumbo, monster, poor Fumbo, the King, the King’s head, your Majesty

Abstract written by a model

King Fumbo is the king of Ragbad, husband of Mrs Sew-and-Sew, and father of Prince Tatters. A great reader who knows Perhaps City, he spends so heavily on books and tobacco that he empties the treasury. During a storm, he panics, falls down ten flights of steps, and loses his head, yet continues living until Grampa ties him to a red pillar. A baker’s dough head, without eyelids, is placed on his shoulders so he can return calmly to his throne. His real head, carried separately and later found on pink clouds, explains that his wing collar carried it there. Tatters carries the talking head in a knitting bag; Fumbo recognizes Dorothy and supports finding the Princess of Perhaps City for Tatters. Ozma eventually restores his head, and Fumbo welcomes the travelers to Ragbad and joins the feast and dance.

King Fumbo is in one work on this site, Grampa in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

King Fumbo panics during the storm, falls down ten flights of steps, and runs into the garden without his umbrella. The storm blows off his head, but he continues living and running around without it until Grampa ties him to a red pillar.

CHAPTER 2

King Fumbo remains in the castle after losing his head. A baker’s dough head is placed on his shoulders, allowing him to walk calmly to his throne, though he cannot understand the farewell excitement.

CHAPTER 15

King Fumbo's head is found among the heads resting on the pink clouds. He explains that a storm blew off his head while he wore a wing collar, which carried him to the clouds. He agrees to return after Tatters promises to tell him about the company's adventures, although he complains about being carried in a bag and thrown about.

CHAPTER 17

King Fumbo appears as a talking head that Tatters carries in a knitting bag because his head blew off from his body. He recognizes Dorothy, confirms Tatters's princely identity, hears both groups' stories, and supports finding the Princess of Perhaps City as a suitable match for his son.

CHAPTER 22

Fumbo welcomes Dorothy, Peer Haps, and the Forgetful Poet to Ragbad after Ozma restores his head. He joins the feast and dance with Pudge and the others.
