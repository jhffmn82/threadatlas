The first mention of Sterne in the _Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen_ is in the nu

The Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen first mentioned Sterne on November 15, 1764, in a review of the fifth edition of Yorick’s Sermons. A longer review in 1767 still had to explain who Yorick was, but reviews of the sermons in 1770 referred familiarly to Shandy and The Sentimental Journey.
