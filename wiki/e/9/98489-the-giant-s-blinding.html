Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

The giant's blinding was achieved after the robber pretended to be a doctor and promised to cure his aching eyes. The robber poured boiling oil mixed with lime, salt, sulphur, arsenic, and other harmful substances over the giant's head, leaving him completely blind.
