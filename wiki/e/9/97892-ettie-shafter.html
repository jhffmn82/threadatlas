Ettie Shafter in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Ettie Shafter is Jacob Shafter’s nineteen-year-old, blonde, fair-haired daughter, a young woman of singular beauty who runs the Shafter boarding house after her mother’s death. Her life is darkened by fear of the Scowrers and of Teddy Baldwin, to whom she is bound only by a half-promise that she rejects. Although Baldwin and McMurdo contend over her, she is recognized as free to choose. Ettie loves Jack McMurdo deeply and becomes secretly, increasingly intimate with him, while fearing that his apparent criminal life and association with the lodge will destroy them. She repeatedly urges McMurdo to flee with her and her father, proposing other cities, the West, or Europe; he initially refuses, then promises eventual departure. When he later signals, she agrees to the escape, receives a sealed note from Scanlan, and leaves the Valley of Fear by special train with her muffled lover. Ten days later she marries Edwards in Chicago, witnessed by Jacob Shafter. Later she is identified as John Douglas’s wife: she initially knows little of his past, helps Douglas and Barker conceal his survival after Baldwin’s death, learns the full story, and is someone Douglas hopes to rejoin after escaping.

Douglas's wife initially knew little about his past but helped him and Barker conceal the truth after Baldwin's death. She learned the full story after the investigators had seen her, and Douglas says he hoped to reunite with her after escaping.

PART II / Chapter I

Ettie Shafter is Jacob Shafter’s young, beautiful daughter, and she runs the household because her mother is dead. She answers the door when McMurdo arrives, speaks with him, and directs him to wait for her father.

Chapter II

Ettie is loved by McMurdo but has an existing understanding with Teddy Baldwin, whom she says she loathes and fears. She urges McMurdo to flee with her and her father because she believes Baldwin, McGinty, and the Scowrers will endanger his life, but McMurdo refuses to leave.

The young lady is the subject of the dispute between McMurdo and Ted Baldwin. McMurdo and Baldwin are treated as having a choice to make because she is considered free to choose for herself, and McGinty says the matter lies outside a Bodymaster's jurisdiction.

Chapter V

Ettie Shafter remains deeply in love with Jack McMurdo but fears marrying a man regarded as a criminal. She begs him to abandon the lodge and proposes that they flee with her father to another city, the West, or Europe. McMurdo refuses to leave immediately but promises to depart the valley within a year, which gives her some hope.

Chapter VI

Ettie Shafter meets McMurdo when he visits her secretly and recognizes that he is in danger. She agrees to leave the valley with him whenever he sends her a signal.

Chapter VII

Ettie Shafter receives a sealed note from Scanlan and boards a special train with her much-muffled lover to leave the Valley of Fear. Ten days later, she marries Edwards in Chicago, with Jacob Shafter as a witness.
