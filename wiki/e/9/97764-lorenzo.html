Abstract written by a model

Lorenzo, a Franciscan monk, is honored by Jacobi and his companions as a model of gentleness, contentment, patience, and forgiveness; they esteem him above calendar saints, believe he deserves canonization, and link his memory with the Lorenzodosen.
