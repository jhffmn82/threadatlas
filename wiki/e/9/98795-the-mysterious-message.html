the mysterious message in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

A gold-written message on the marble walk begins with “Danger!” and directs travel to Morrow to-day; its unusual wording confuses characters until the Scarecrow identifies time and destination.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The message first appears as "Danger! Go to Morrow to-day!" and leaves the characters confused because of its unusual wording. The Scarecrow eventually interprets one part as a time and the other as a place, suggesting that Morrow is the destination.
