Also called: Tphiclus

Abstract written by a model

Iphiclus, son of Phylacus and father of Podarces, in one version detained kine and imprisoned Melampus for a year before releasing him. Fright from Phylacus's knife caused childlessness; ten days of rusted-knife wine restored virility.

IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

Iphiclus possessed or detained the kine in one version of the story and released Melampus after a year of imprisonment. His childlessness was attributed to the fright caused when Phylacus pursued him with a knife, and he recovered his virility after drinking wine containing rust from that knife for ten days.
