Jacob Shafter in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Jacob Shafter is an honest boarding-house keeper on Sheridan Street in Vermissa and Ettie Shafter’s father. He takes McMurdo as a paying boarder, warns him that the Freemen are the Scowrers, and orders him out, fearing danger amid Baldwin’s courtship of Ettie. Later he saves money, feeds McMurdo, and witnesses Ettie’s marriage to Birdy Edwards in Chicago.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

PART II / Chapter I

Jacob Shafter runs a boarding house on Sheridan Street in Vermissa. He accepts McMurdo as a boarder for seven dollars a week paid in advance.

Chapter II

Mr. Shafter, McMurdo's German host and Ettie's father, tells McMurdo that the local Freemen are the Scowrers, a murder society. He gives McMurdo notice to leave the boarding house because he fears having a Freeman as a boarder, especially while Baldwin is courting Ettie.
