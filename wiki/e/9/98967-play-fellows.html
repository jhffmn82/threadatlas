The Play Fellows are a large, mischievous company of Pierrettes and Pierrots who invite Grampa’s army into their park, split the travelers among amusements, force relentless noisy play, treat Urtha roughly, and steal Grampa’s game leg.

CHAPTER 18

The Play Fellows are a large company of Pierrettes and Pierrots who invite Grampa's army into their park and then carry the travelers away to different amusements. They force the visitors to play noisily and relentlessly, treat Urtha roughly, and steal Grampa's game leg while he sleeps.
