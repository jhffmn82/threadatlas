Die Witzlinge in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Die Witzlinge derives directly from Bury Fair, not Tristram Shandy; although once thought to model characters on Sterne’s Trim and Eugenius, the interpretation is rejected, and Lessing’s Trim differs from Corporal Trim.

Die Witzlinge was once thought to contain characters modeled on Sterne’s Trim and Eugenius. The unit rejects that interpretation, explaining that its plan derives from Shadwell’s Bury Fair and that Lessing’s Trim has nothing in common with Sterne’s Corporal Trim.
