Jupiter Asterius is identified as the figure associated with the abduction of Europa and is said to have reigned about B.C. 1400. According to the account attributed to Diodorus Siculus, he was the son of Teutamus and was connected with the Pelasgian settlement of Crete, where he was its first king.
