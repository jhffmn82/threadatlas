Text View of Textual Graphs

topic · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: text view, text view of textual graphs
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, text view converts textual graphs, including retrieved textual ego-graphs, into hierarchical descriptions used as hard prompts with queries, preserving narrated connections and textual and topological information for the large language model.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Text View of Textual Graphs is a complementary view of a textual graph that converts retrieved textual subgraphs into hierarchical text descriptions through graph and tree traversals, retaining narrated connections as well as textual and topological information, and concatenates the query with the hierarchical description as a hard prompt for the language model.
