Mustafa's magic ring in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Mustafa's magic ring is a magical object with dreadful power, used by Mustafa to monitor and affect Notta and Bob. When activated, it can turn them blue and unable to move; its effects end when Nickadoodle captures the Cowardly Lion with a rope. It turns black when Notta thwarts Mustafa or affects his plans, is tied to the enchantment binding Notta to the Cowardly Lion, and may summon them if they move toward the Emerald City while connected. After Bob warns Ozma, Ozma orders Mustafa to surrender it, and he obeys.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Mustafa's magic ring can turn Notta and Bob blue and make them unable to move when Mustafa activates it. Its effects end when Nickadoodle uses a rope to make the Cowardly Lion count as captured.

Chapter 15

Mustafa stares steadily at the magic ring to monitor Notta's actions. The ring turns black when Notta does something that affects Mustafa's plans, and Mustafa once removed it with alarming consequences for Bob and Notta.

Chapter 17

Mustafa's ring is connected to the enchantment binding Notta to the Cowardly Lion. Notta believes it will summon them again if they take a step toward the Emerald City while still connected.

Chapter 21

Mustafa's ring is taken from him after Bob warns Ozma about its dreadful power. Ozma commands Mustafa to hand it over, and he tremblingly obeys.
