Thisbe's veil

object · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Thisby's mantle, her veil, own garment, the veil, the veil of Thisbe, thin covering, veil, well-known garment
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Thisbe's veil was dropped as she fled from the lioness; the lioness tore and stained it with blood, and Pyramus later found it and mistook it for evidence that Thisbe had been killed.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Thisbe's veil fell as she fled from the lioness; the lioness tore and bloodied it, and Pyramus found it and mistook it as proof of Thisbe's death.
