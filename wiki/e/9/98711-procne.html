Also called: nightingale, ἀηδών

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Procne marries Tereus, bears Itys, kills and serves Itys to Tereus after learning of Philomela's suffering, and becomes a nightingale fleeing with Philomela. In The Library, the nightingale is the transformed form of Procne, a Daulian bird that continued to utter the cry associated with her transformation and was said to flee while calling plaintively for Itys.
