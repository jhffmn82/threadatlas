opening

Juno’s bird receives Argus’s eyes as starry gems on its feathers, filling its tail.

notes .. notes

The peacock’s tail was said to contain Argus’s eyes after Juno placed them there. Its spots may have been associated with eyes because of their resemblance to human eyes.
