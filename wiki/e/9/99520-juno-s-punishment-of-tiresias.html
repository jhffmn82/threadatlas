Juno's punishment of Tiresias

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: condemned, punishment
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII: Juno punishes Tiresias for confirming Jupiter's claim by condemning his eyes to eternal darkness. Jupiter cannot reverse the act of another deity, so he alleviates the punishment by giving Tiresias prophetic knowledge.
