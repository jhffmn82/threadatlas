Abstract written by a model

Bob Up is a solemn, red-headed, seven-year-old orphan boy from Philadelphia in the United States, living in an orphan asylum and described as small, bright, brave, and without unkind behavior. Though he has never had an adventure, he is taken from a group of orphans by a clown's trick, disappears, and lands in a strange land beside Notta Bit More. He becomes Notta's close companion on the journey toward the Emerald City, carrying marshmallows, seeking the wizard, Dorothy, and the Scarecrow, and repeatedly defending the fact that Notta is not a lion or a witch. Frightened at many dangers but consistently brave and helpful, Bob travels through Oz with Notta, the Cowardly Lion, and later Nick. He faces Mudgers, a baby dragon, a net cast by the twentieth tree, the Uns, storms, a crash of the Flyaboutabus, Crunch, and guards at the Emerald City. He shows kindness by releasing a skye terrier because another little boy would miss it, and he forms trust and friendship with the Cowardly Lion. Strange events make him grow red feathers, turn blue, and freeze temporarily, but he is restored and continues onward. He helps steer the Flyaboutabus, starts a fire, identifies the Emerald City from its green lights, and worries about the Cowardly Lion. At the Emerald City, Bob is called the bravest and best little boy in America and is transported with the rescue party to Mudge. In the final conflict, he remembers and whispers the name Unselfish to Ozma, helping end the battle among the Uns. He remains in Oz with Notta and Snorer, becomes friends with everyone, and is especially close to Button Bright. Notta funds his education.

Bob Up is in one work on this site, The Cowardly Lion of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

18 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The red-headed orphan boy is taken from a group of orphans by the clown for a trick. The clown's rhyme makes him disappear, and he later lands beside the clown on a soft dune of sand in a strange land.

Chapter 3

Bobbie Downs is a solemn seven-year-old orphan who has never had an adventure. He follows Notta into the royal tent and bravely protests when the Mudgers try to take Notta away, declaring that Notta is not a lion.

Chapter 4

Bob Up remains frightened while the Mudgers discuss lions and while Mustafa threatens Notta and him. He leaves Mudge holding Notta's hand and begins the journey toward the Emerald City.

Chapter 5

Bob Up follows Notta through the forest toward the Emerald City and helps eat the food Mustafa provided. He falls asleep in Notta's lap, gives Notta his bag of marshmallows, and suggests that D on the sign might stand for Dorothy. He is astonished when the sign post speaks and counts the seven doors.

Chapter 6

Bob Up accompanies Notta past the Seven Doors and helps search for Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion. He is frightened by the baby dragon and thrown into blueberry bushes with Notta by Door Six. In the throne room, he finally tells the Queen that the bear is actually Notta.

Chapter 7

Bob Up accompanies Notta before King Theodore and Queen Adora and objects mildly to some of the King's ideas. He helps Notta escape from Doorways and bravely pats the half lion's mane. He then continues with Notta toward the Emerald City.

Chapter 9

Bob Up helps Notta Bit More while they travel through Oz and is frightened when the Cowardly Lion attacks the disguised clown. He quickly becomes fond of the Cowardly Lion and eagerly wants to see Dorothy and the Scarecrow. While traveling toward the Emerald City, he is captured with Notta and the lion by the twentieth tree's net.

Chapter 10

Bob Up travels with Notta and the Cowardly Lion of Oz and is amazed by the Featherheads of Un. He correctly explains what an isle is, wants to go fishing, and wistfully considers the fishing rods despite the danger.

Chapter 11

Bob Up fishes with Notta and catches the cooked goose and the skye terrier. He releases the terrier because he understands that another little boy would miss it. Bob falls asleep after opening a package labeled "Dreams for a little boy" and is missing along with Notta by morning.

Chapter 12

Bob is pulled safely back to the skyle after the Uns push him from the edge, though he remains frightened and dizzy. He grows red feathers on his head and laughs at the Cowardly Lion and Nickadoodle before realizing that he, too, is becoming unusual. He helps Notta lower the Flyaboutabus and escapes aboard it.

Chapter 13

Bob helps steer the Flyaboutabus and joins the others in eating breakfast from the Travelers' Tree. Mustafa's magic turns him blue and freezes him with one foot in the air. He is restored when Nickadoodle makes the Cowardly Lion count as captured.

Chapter 14

Bob helps endure the storm, starts a fire in the woodcutter's hut, and helps Notta remove his disguises. He and Notta turn blue when the Cowardly Lion moves away from the road to Mudge, and he remains curious and excited about their adventures.

Chapter 16

Bob Up travels with Notta, the Cowardly Lion, and Nick through the Preservatory and reacts with wonder to the preserved people and animals. He is carried safely by Nick after the Flyaboutabus crashes and is set on a huge boulder.

Chapter 17

Bob Up is caught by Crunch and helps identify the Emerald City from Crunch's description of its green lights. He travels with Notta and Nick toward the city, shares the eggs they carried, and worries affectionately about the Cowardly Lion. He spends the night with Notta in the Fiddlestick Forest of Oz.

Chapter 18

Bob Up wakes in the forest, finds the Fiddlebow Boat, and joins Notta and Nick on the journey to the Emerald City. He is excited to meet Dorothy and tries to explain that Notta is not really a witch, but his voice is drowned out when the guards surround them.

Chapter 19

Bob Up insists that Notta is not a witch and identifies him as Notta. He is introduced as an orphan from Philadelphia and as the bravest and best little boy in America. Notta touches him and transports him with the rescue party to Mudge.

Chapter 20

Bob is mentioned as one of the Cowardly Lion's friends who must reach Mudge in time to help him. He arrives at the lion enclosure with Notta Bit More and the company from the Emerald City.

Chapter 22

Bob Up remembers the name of the good Un, Unselfish, and whispers it to Ozma, helping her end the battle among the Uns. He stays in Oz with Notta and Snorer, becomes friends with everyone, and is especially close to Button Bright.
