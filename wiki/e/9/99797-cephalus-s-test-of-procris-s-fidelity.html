Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Cephalus returns to Athens disguised and offers Procris increasingly large sums for one night, hoping to discover whether she has been faithful. She resists his advances and says she is reserved for her husband, but he finally reveals himself and accuses her, causing her to flee in shame.
