Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is found at Narcissus's funeral site after his body disappears, bears his name, and commonly grows around graves. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it appears where Narcissus's body cannot be found after his death, purple within and surrounded by white leaves, preserving his name and memory.
