Narcissus flower

flower · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a flower, a yellow flower, that flower, the Narcissus, the flower (Narcissus), the flower which bears his name
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is found at Narcissus's funeral site after his body disappears, bears his name, and commonly grows around graves.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it appears where Narcissus's body cannot be found after his death, purple within and surrounded by white leaves, preserving his name and memory.
