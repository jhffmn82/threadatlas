sea-maiden (Cretan tale)

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: beautiful shape, darling, sea-maiden, unknown woman, wife

Abstract written by a model

A sea-maiden changes into a dog, serpent, camel, and fire while a young man holds her. After the cock crows she marries him, remains silent for a year, and bears a son. When he threatens the child, she speaks, leaves him, and settles at a spring after the Nereids reject her.

X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The sea-maiden changes into a dog, a serpent, a camel, and fire while the young man holds her by the hair. She becomes his silent wife and bears him a son, but speaks when he threatens their child and then vanishes, settling at a spring near the sea-nymphs' cave.
