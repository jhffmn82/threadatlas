X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The sea-maiden changes into a dog, a serpent, a camel, and fire while the young man holds her by the hair. She becomes his silent wife and bears him a son, but speaks when he threatens their child and then vanishes, settling at a spring near the sea-nymphs' cave.
