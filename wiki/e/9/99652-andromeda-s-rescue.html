Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Perseus offers to save Andromeda in exchange for marriage, attacks the sea monster from the air, kills it, and releases Andromeda from her chains. In The Library, Andromeda’s rescue begins when Perseus promises Cepheus to kill the sea monster for marriage to Andromeda; he kills it, frees her, and faces Phineus’s plot. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Perseus finds Andromeda chained to a rock, offers to save her in exchange for marriage, and kills the sea-monster by striking it repeatedly with his sword.
