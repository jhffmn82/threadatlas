Abstract written by a model

Phaethon, son of Clymene and the Sun and father of Astynous, asks to drive the Sun’s chariot for a day. A helpless, imprudent charioteer, he loses control, burns the earth, and is struck by Jove’s thunderbolt, falling into Eridanus.

He is named in the title “Phaethon and the chariot of the Sun.”

XI.—PHAETHON AND THE CHARIOT OF THE SUN

Phaethon, the son of Clymene and the Sun, asks to drive his father’s chariot for one day. He loses control of the horses, causes the earth to burn, and is killed by Jupiter’s thunderbolt before falling into the Eridanus.
