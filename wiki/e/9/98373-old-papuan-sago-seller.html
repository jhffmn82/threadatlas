An old Papuan sago seller earns a living selling intoxicating sago-palm juice. After catching the Morning Star stealing from his vessels, he receives a magical wand, then throws himself into an iron-wood fire to please his wife and emerges rejuvenated.

VI.—THE RENEWAL OF YOUTH

The old Papuan sago seller caught the Morning Star stealing the juice from his vessels and received a magical wand in ransom. To please his wife, he later threw himself into a great fire of iron-wood, emerged as a handsome young man, and returned adorned with ornaments formed from his old skin and scabs.
