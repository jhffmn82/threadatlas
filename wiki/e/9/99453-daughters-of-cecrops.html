daughters of Cecrops

Also called: these daughters of Cecrops

Abstract written by a model

The daughters of Cecrops hesitated to encourage Minerva’s worship, probably when it was introduced into Attica; portrayed as punished for impiety, they went mad and threw themselves from a tower.

According to Pausanias, the daughters of Cecrops went mad and threw themselves from the top of a tower. The explanation suggests that they may have hesitated to encourage the introduction of Pallas or Minerva's worship, and that their punishment was portrayed as resulting from impiety.
