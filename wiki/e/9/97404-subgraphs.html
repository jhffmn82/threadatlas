In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, subgraphs are ranked by cosine similarity, with the top N forming SN(G), whose nodes and edges receive adaptive scaling to suppress irrelevant entities. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, subgraphs retrieved from graph G are filtered to select the N most semantically relevant, then provide hierarchical descriptions and graph representations during generation, with their text serving as a hard prompt and their structure as a soft prompt.
