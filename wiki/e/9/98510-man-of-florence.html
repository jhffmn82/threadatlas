(8) Another Italian version of the story, recorded at Pisa,

The man of Florence travels with a curate and a workman and accepts employment at a giant’s house. He discovers that the giant kills his workers, blinds the giant with boiling oil, escapes after cutting off a finger trapped by a ring, and returns exhausted to Florence, where he no longer wishes to travel and recount his adventures.
