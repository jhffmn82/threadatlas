training-free RAG in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025

Training-free RAG is a simpler, efficient plug-and-play RAG approach that combines retrieval components with pre-trained language models without extensive retraining. It begins with retrieved documents and language models, but its effectiveness depends on document quality and it can be sensitive to prompts or struggle with noise robustness because it relies on static pre-trained data.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 / Introduction

Training-free RAG approaches are described as simpler and more efficient plug-and-play methods than training-based approaches. However, they remain dependent on the quality of retrieved documents.
