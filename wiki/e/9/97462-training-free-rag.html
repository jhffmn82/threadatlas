training-free RAG

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: training-free RAG approaches, training-free approaches, training-free nature

Abstract written by a model

Training-free RAG is a simpler, efficient plug-and-play RAG approach that combines retrieval components with pre-trained language models without extensive retraining. It begins with retrieved documents and language models, but its effectiveness depends on document quality and it can be sensitive to prompts or struggle with noise robustness because it relies on static pre-trained data.
