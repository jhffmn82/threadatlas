Calliope

deity · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Muse, Muse (the greatest of us), The greatest of us, one of our number, Καλλιόπην

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Calliope, the greatest Muse, led the Muses in a singing contest with the Pierides, tuned her lyre, sang the Muses' song, and finished it. In The Library, Calliope, first listed among the Muses, bore Linus and Orpheus to Oeagrus or nominally Apollo, and was said by some to be the mother of Rhesus.
