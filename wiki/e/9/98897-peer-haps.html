Peer Haps in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Peer Haps is the sixty-year-old, three-hundred-pound cheerful monarch of Perhaps City, an old mountain monarch of Oz and father of Urtha, his only daughter. He rules and manages the city, favors Percy Vere as his chief companion, and deeply fears a prophecy that a monster will marry his daughter. Seeking her safety, he considers Perix, offers a thousand gold bricks and her hand to her rescuer, and waits anxiously as Percy enters Maybe Mountain. When Urtha returns, he tries to arrange her immediate marriage and, while cloaked and apparently bewitched, mistakenly marries her to the Prince of Ragbad. He is surprised to recognize the flower maiden as Urtha, denounces Abrog after the monster proves nonexistent, rejoices when Urtha is restored, welcomes Fumbo as King of Ragbad, rewards Tatters, and later visits Ragbad with Dorothy and the Forgetful Poet before returning home.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Peer Haps is the cheerful ruler of Perhaps City and deeply values his daughter, the Princess. After hearing the prophecy about a monster marrying her, he considers marrying her to Perix, offers a thousand gold bricks and her hand in marriage to whoever rescues her, and finally watches Percy descend Maybe Mountain when no one else volunteers.

CHAPTER 19

Peer Haps anxiously watches Maybe Mountain while waiting for news of the Forgetful Poet and fearing the monster. When he recognizes his lost daughter, he carries her to the palace and tries to arrange her immediate marriage to protect her. He mistakenly marries her, while cloaked and apparently bewitched, to the arriving Prince of Ragbad.

CHAPTER 20

Peer Haps is astonished to learn that the flower maiden is Urtha, although he initially insists that his daughter's name is Pretty Good. He warns Abrog that Dorothy can have him punished and later points angrily at Abrog when the supposed monster is shown not to exist.

CHAPTER 21

Peer Haps is relieved and happy when Urtha is restored, and he listens to the full account of the adventurers' experiences. He welcomes Fumbo as the King of Ragbad and gives Tatters the yellow hen to provide a fortune.

CHAPTER 22

Peer Haps is transported to Ragbad with Dorothy and the Forgetful Poet so he can meet Mrs Sew-and-Sew and Tatters’s other friends. He attends the feast and dance before being transported home with Dorothy and the Forgetful Poet.
