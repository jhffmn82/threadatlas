Leucothoë's burial alive

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: being buried alive, buried alive

Abstract written by a model

Leucothoë's burial alive is a story event attributed to her father Orchamus, who punishes her after learning of her affair with the Sun. He buries her deep in the ground under heavy sand as she reaches for light and says the Sun forced himself on her. It has no ascertained historical basis.

Leucothoë's burial alive is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
