the fountain of Salmacis

place · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

The fountain is a clear, walled pool in Lycia or neighboring Caria, edged by green turf and ever-verdant grass without reeds or rushes. Salmacis dwells there. Its well-known but unexplained, enervating waters are later tainted with ambiguous drugs; it civilizes barbarians and gains a reputation for changing men into women, perhaps through nearby disgraceful adventures.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

opening

The fountain is a clear pool in Lycia or the neighboring Carians, with no fenny reeds, barren sedge, or sharp-pointed rushes, and with green turf and ever-verdant grass around its edges. Salmacis dwells there, and its waters are later tainted with drugs of ambiguous quality.

notes .. notes

The fountain near Halicarnassus in Caria was instrumental in civilizing the barbarians and acquired a reputation for changing men into women. The text suggests that its bad reputation may also have arisen from disgraceful adventures occurring near it.
