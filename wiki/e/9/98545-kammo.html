Kammo

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: the monster, the old rock-spirit, the rock-spirit, the spirit

Abstract written by a model

Kammo is an old rock-spirit with a great horn, a single dim eyelashed eye, and an iron poker for the fire; he smells human flesh until Gylpho blinds his eye with a red-hot poker and cuts off his head.

Kammo is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
