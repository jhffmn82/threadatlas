Kammo in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Kammo is an old rock-spirit with a great horn, a single dim eyelashed eye, and an iron poker for the fire; he smells human flesh until Gylpho blinds his eye with a red-hot poker and cuts off his head.

(14) A Finnish tale of the same general type, but lacking

Kammo is an old rock-spirit with a great horn and one eye in the middle of his forehead. His dim eye is blinded by Gylpho with a red-hot poker, after which Gylpho cuts off his head.
