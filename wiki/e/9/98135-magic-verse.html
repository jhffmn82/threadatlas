Abstract written by a model

A magic rhyme and Mudge's private transformation formula, beginning "Udge! Budge! Go to Mudge!" It transports people to Mudge when recited while touching them. In Chapter 2, the clown's recitation makes the orphan disappear, then makes the clown disappear after he changes the ending. Later Notta uses it on attacking Uns, sending each touched Un to Mudge and transporting a rescue party.

magic verse is in one work on this site, The Cowardly Lion of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The magic rhyme begins, "Udge! Budge! Go to Mudge! Udger budger, You're a Mudger!" After the orphan disappears, the clown changes the ending to "I'm a Mudger!" and disappears as well.

Chapter 4

The magic transformation formula is the private formula of Mudge, expressed in the rhyme beginning "Udge! Budge!" It transports people who recite it, but it cannot transport the Cowardly Lion because the person being transported must be touched.

Chapter 11

The magic verse used by Notta is: "Udge! Budge! / Go to Mudge! / Udger budger, / You're a Mudger!" He recites it while touching the attacking Uns, causing each touched Un to disappear and go to Mudge.
