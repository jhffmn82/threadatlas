training-based RAG in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025

Training-based RAG is a RAG approach that integrates retrieval into language-model training, enabling access to external information during generation. It has strong performance and includes Self-RAG, but generally requires dedicated training procedures, corresponding hardware, and substantial computational resources.

1 section; each heading opens the text

2 / Preliminaries / 2.1 / Notations and Objectives .. 2.3 / Related Work

Training-based RAG integrates retrieval mechanisms into language-model training so that the model can access external information during generation. The unit notes that methods such as those of Lewis et al. (2020), Guu et al. (2020), and Self-RAG are effective but require dedicated training procedures and hardware.
