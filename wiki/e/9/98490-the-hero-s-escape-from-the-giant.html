XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

The hero first hid from the blinded giant by climbing to the roof and hanging from a beam. He then disguised himself in a ram's fleece, passed among the flock while the giant counted the sheep, and was driven outside; the magic ring afterward forced him to sacrifice a finger before he could escape completely.
