FABLES XII. AND XIII. [XIV.698-851]

The Roman-Sabine war begins when Tatius and the Sabine fathers attack Rome after entering through an opened gate. It ends when Roman and Sabine blood has been shed and the two sides agree to peace, shared sovereignty, and common laws.
