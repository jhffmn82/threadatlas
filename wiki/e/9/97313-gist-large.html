GIST-large

Also called: GIST-large (1024)

Abstract written by a model

GIST-large is a 1024-dimensional dense retrieval model that excels at software tutorials, consistently surpasses GIST-base, reaches 61.7 average NDCG@10, and is selected for full RACG experiments.

GIST-large is in one work on this site, CODERAG-BENCH: Can Retrieval Augment Code Generation?. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
