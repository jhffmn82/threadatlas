the Valley of Fear in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The Valley of Fear is Mr. Douglas’s name for a dangerous part of his American past, identified by Sherlock Holmes with Vermissa Valley, a coal and iron valley in the United States. It was long controlled and haunted by the violent Scowrers, leaving its people without hope despite the coming of spring; its terror was especially dark in early summer 1875. Edwards exposes the organization, leading to arrests and prosecutions and freeing the valley from its control, but surviving Scowrers later pursue him for revenge.

Vermissa Valley in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Vermissa Valley is a desolate coal and iron valley in the United States, containing the Vermissa coal and iron district, mining and iron-working settlements, furnaces, slag heaps, smoke, and crowded worker housing. Its defining reality is the Scowrers’ criminal control: their lodges, including Lodge 341, enforce fear and violence across Vermissa and the surrounding thirty-mile valley. Residents are said to know criminals’ identities but fear testifying, while murder threatens the population from dusk until dawn. Boss McGinty is feared throughout the region, and the lodge’s power is used against mine owners and others who resist it. As McMurdo travels through this grim, snowy setting, joins its orbit, and faces growing danger, Birdy Edwards investigates the Scowrers, while McMurdo expects the violence may force him and Ettie to leave.
