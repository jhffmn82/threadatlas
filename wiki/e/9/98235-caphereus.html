Caphereus

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Caphereus' cliffs, Caphereus' heights, Mount Caphareus, Mount Caphereus, Καφηρέα
  • In The Library, Caphereus was the mountain also called Xylophagus where Nauplius lit a beacon to deceive returning Greek ships into approaching the shore as though it were a harbour.
  • In The Fall of Troy, Caphereus was a cliff-bound coastal height whose cliffs and heights became the focus of Athena’s storm and the destruction of the Achaean ships.
