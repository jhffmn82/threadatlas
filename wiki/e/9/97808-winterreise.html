Winterreise in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Winterreise is a sentimental journey narrative by Johann Georg Jacobi, published in Düsseldorf in June 1769. Alternating prose and verse through short, unnumbered chapters, it unfolds as sentimental travel episodes emphasizing fancy, feeling, moral instruction, sympathy for animals, and humane religious encounters.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] CHAPTER VI IMITATORS OF STERNE Among the disciples of Sterne in Germany whose literary imitation may be regarded as typical of their master’s influence, Johann Georg Jacobi is perhaps the best known.

Winterreise was published in Düsseldorf in June 1769. It alternates prose and verse in a manner associated externally with Chapelle, but also uses short unnumbered chapters and sentimental travel episodes reminiscent of A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy; it emphasizes fancy, sentiment, moral instruction, animal sympathy, and humane religious encounters.
