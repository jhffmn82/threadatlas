Agent-2 in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025

Agent-2 is a Judge LLM agent in Multi-Agent Filtering RAG. It evaluates each Document-Query-Answer Triplet for information relevant to the query and answer, returns a Yes/No judgment, and uses those token log probabilities to compute relevance scores that filter and order retrieved documents.
