Abstract written by a model

Lyncus, king of Scythia, hosts Triptolemus, then envies and tries to kill him; for this cruelty, Ceres transforms him into a lynx.

Lyncus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
