X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The Swan Maiden appears as a beautiful maiden after removing her plumage. When a young man hides the bird-skin, she marries him and bears a child, but she eventually finds the skin, resumes her wings, and flies away.
