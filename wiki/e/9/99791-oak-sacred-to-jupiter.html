An oak, sacred to Jupiter and from Dodona seed, trembles for Æacus, then in his dream scatters grain-bearing ants that grow into men.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The oak is sacred to Jupiter and comes from seed from Dodona. It trembles and makes noise without a breeze when Æacus asks for citizens, and in his dream it scatters the grain-bearing ants over the fields as they grow into men.
