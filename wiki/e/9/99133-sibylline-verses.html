Sibylline verses

Abstract written by a model

The Sibylline verses, attributed to the Sibyls, were used by early Christian writers to support Christianity; surviving verses openly contain Christian mysteries, Jesus Christ, and the Virgin Mary, so are now generally considered spurious or heavily interpolated.

FABLE III. [XIV.101-153]

The Sibylline verses were attributed to the Sibyls and were used by early Christian writers, including Justin and St. Augustin, to support Christianity. The explanation says that the surviving collection is generally regarded as spurious or heavily interpolated, partly because it refers openly to Christian mysteries, Jesus Christ, and the Virgin Mary.
