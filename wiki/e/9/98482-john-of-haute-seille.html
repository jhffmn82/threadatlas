XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

John of Haute-Seille was a monk of the Cistercian Abbey of Haute-Seille in Lorraine who wrote Dolopathos in or soon after 1184. He appears to have known the Odyssey, but his account of Polyphemus is inferred to have followed oral tradition rather than the Homeric narrative.
