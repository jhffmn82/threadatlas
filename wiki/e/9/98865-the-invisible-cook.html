Abstract written by a model

The Invisible Cook is a famous invisible cook. Proposed as Mombi's replacement while Rosa Merry cooks temporarily, she is sent for by Kinda Jolly. She later arrives with General Whiffenpuff after searching for Mombi and Snip, repeatedly treading on his toes; he wants to return her to Kimbaloo.

CHAPTER 3

An invisible cook who reportedly lives near a sixteenth cousin of Hah Hoh's is proposed as a replacement for Mombi. Kinda Jolly decides to send for her, while Rosa Merry offers to cook in the meantime.

CHAPTER 21

The Invisible Cook arrives with General Whiffenpuff after they search for Mombi and Snip. Although she is invisible, she has a bad habit of stepping on the general's toes, so he wants to return her to Kimbaloo and turn her over to Kinda Jolly.
