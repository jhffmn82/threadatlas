Dercetis

Also called: Babylonian Dercetis, Derceto

Abstract written by a model

Dercetis, also called Atergatis, is a Syrian goddess. Said to be Semiramis’s mother, she was represented as woman above and fish below; after drowning near Ascalon, she was said to become a fish, and a nearby temple marked the place.

Dercetis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Dercetis, also called Derceto or Atergatis, was represented in some accounts as a woman from the waist upward and a fish below. She was said to have drowned herself near Ascalon and to have been changed into a fish, and she was worshipped by the Syrians.
