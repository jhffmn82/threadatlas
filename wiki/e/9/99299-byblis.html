Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE V. [IX.426-665]

Byblis falls in love with her brother Caunus, first confusing her passion with natural affection and later recognizing it as forbidden desire. After Caunus rejects her, she repeatedly pursues him through foreign lands, becomes delirious with grief, and is finally changed into a fountain.
