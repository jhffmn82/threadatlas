Phryxus in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Phryxus, child of Nephele and Athamas and brother of Helle, flees Ino's designs with Helle by ship. After Helle dies, he reaches Colchis, where Æetes receives him kindly and he marries Chalciope. Some years later Æetes kills him; his death prompts the Greek expedition.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

Phryxus was one of Nephele's children and was warned about Ino's designs. He escaped with Helle by ship and reached Colchis, where Æetes received him kindly.

notes .. notes

Phryxus, the son of Athamas and Nephele, flees with his sister Helle after Ino plots against them. He reaches Colchis, marries Chalciope, and is later assassinated by Æetes, whose death prompts the Greek expedition.
