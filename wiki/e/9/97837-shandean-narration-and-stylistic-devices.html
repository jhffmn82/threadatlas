Abstract written by a model

is_imitated_by Schummel [through digressions, typography, coinages and mock-scientific procedures] Shandean narration and stylistic devices in the second volume include backward jumps in the narrative, numerous digressions, promised chapters on extraordinary subjects, black page margins, an upside-down line, irregular printing, dashes and exclamation points, mathematical calculations, mock-scientific descriptions, and coined words. The unit generally treats Schummel’s use of these devices as weak, awkward, or witless imitations.
