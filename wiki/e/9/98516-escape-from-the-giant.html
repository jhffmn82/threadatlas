(8) Another Italian version of the story, recorded at Pisa,

The man of Florence escapes after blinding the giant, but a ring thrown by the giant turns his finger to marble and prevents him from moving. He cuts off the finger with a knife and escapes while the giant, burdened by the marble table, cannot catch him.
