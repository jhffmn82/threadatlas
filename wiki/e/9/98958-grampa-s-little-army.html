Grampa's little army in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Grampa's army travels with Grampa toward Emerald City to consult Ozma's magic picture about Abrog and the lost Princess, endures separating forced games in play grounds, escapes, then loses Tatters to Urtha's search.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 18

Grampa's army sets out toward the Emerald City to use Ozma's magic picture to find Abrog and the lost Princess of Perhaps City. The army enters the play grounds and is separated and exhausted by forced games before escaping through the exits; afterward, Tatters leaves to search for Urtha.
