Also called: Calydonian, lands of Œneus, over Calydon, the files of Calydon, Καλυδωνίου, Καλυδῶνα
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Calydon was Œneus’s kingdom, devastated by Diana’s boar and later prostrate in mourning after Meleager’s death.
  • In The Library, Calydon was where Hercules wooed and married Deianira, and its people later campaigned with Hercules against Thesprotous.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Calydon was an inhabited place with priests of Dionysus, where Coresus served and Callirhoe died at a harbour well; Augustus later depopulated it to populate Nicopolis, and Patræ received its spoils.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Calydon was ruled by Oeneus and devastated by Diana’s enormous boar, which Meleager and heroes from Greece hunted to protect its fields, vines, olive trees, flocks, and herds.

Calydon in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Calydon, Œneus’s kingdom, is devastated by Diana’s boar until heroes confront it, then mourns prostrate after Meleager’s death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE IV. [VIII.260-546]

Calydon is ravaged by Diana’s boar, which destroys crops, vines, olive branches, and livestock until Meleager and the assembled heroes confront it. After Meleager dies, Calydon lies prostrate in mourning.

Calydon in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Calydon was once an inhabited place with priests of Dionysus; Coresus served there, and Callirhoe died at its harbour well, later named for her. It was later depopulated by Augustus to populate Nicopolis, while Patræ received its spoils.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII.

Calydon was depopulated by Augustus along with the rest of Ætolia so that Nicopolis could be populated. The people of Patræ received spoils from Calydon, including the statue of Laphrian Artemis.

CHAPTER XXI.

Calydon was once inhabited and had priests of Dionysus, including Coresus. Callirhoe killed herself at a well in Calydon near the harbour, and the well has since been called Callirhoe.
