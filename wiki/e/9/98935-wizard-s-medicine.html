wizard's medicine in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Wizard's medicine is a bottle, labeled “Gorba,” with a green label. It cures frost, sorcery, and enchantment. After Urtha recalls it, Grampa gives Prince Tatters four drops on snow, making them warm and frost proof on the iceberg. Later Abrog fears it; its directions call for breaking a saucer over a sorcerer’s head. After Grampa uses nearly all on Abrog, three drops remain for Urtha.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The wizard's medicine is found in the bottle after Urtha reminds the party about it. Grampa gives four drops to Tatters, and the mixture makes them warm and frost proof, allowing them to remain cheerful on the iceberg.

CHAPTER 20

The wizard's medicine is kept in a bottle labeled “Gorba” and causes Abrog to become frightened and desperate when he sees it. Its instructions prescribe breaking a saucer of the mixture over a sorcerer's head.

CHAPTER 21

The wizard's bottle contains a cure for enchantment, though Grampa has poured nearly all of it over Abrog. He shakes out three remaining drops onto Urtha's head.
