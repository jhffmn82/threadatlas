Abstract written by a model

In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Textual Graph Augmented Generation is an approach for providing LLMs with complementary graph views; it provides large language models with complementary text and graph views of a textual graph and combines hierarchical text descriptions with graph embeddings during generation. In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Textual Graph Augmented Generation provides large language models with two complementary views of a textual graph: a text view and a graph view.
