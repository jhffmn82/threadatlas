Tunbridge Wells in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Tunbridge Wells is a place in Kent tied to the events before a murder. Mr. Douglas was there before appearing disturbed and saw Ted Baldwin, whom he recognized as a dangerous enemy. Hargrave stayed there two days before the murder, and a bicyclist may have come from it; clothes in the moat were concluded to be his.
