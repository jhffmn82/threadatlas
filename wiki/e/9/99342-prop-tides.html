Propœtides

Abstract written by a model

The Propœtides are females of Cyprus known for dissolute conduct. They deny Venus is a goddess and are reported as the first women to practice prostitution; after losing shame and hardening in face, Venus transforms them into rocks.

Propœtides is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE VI. [X.220-242]

The Propœtides are women of Cyprus notorious for dissolute conduct. They deny that Venus is a goddess and are reported to have been the first women to prostitute their bodies; when their shame is gone and their faces harden, Venus transforms them into rocks.
