Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE VI. [X.220-242]

The Propœtides are women of Cyprus notorious for dissolute conduct. They deny that Venus is a goddess and are reported to have been the first women to prostitute their bodies; when their shame is gone and their faces harden, Venus transforms them into rocks.
