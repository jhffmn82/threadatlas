Diana bathing

Abstract written by a model

Diana bathing is an accidental encounter and bathing scene in Diana’s bathing grotto. It begins when Actæon wanders unknowingly into the place and sees Diana without her garments; curiosity draws him nearer, and Diana’s punishment transforms him.

Diana bathing is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Actæon enters the unknown wood with uncertain steps and inadvertently reaches Diana's bathing place. The text states that his fault is accidental rather than criminal, because his sight of Diana without her garments results from mistaken wandering.

notes .. notes

Diana was bathing at the time Actæon saw her. Pausanias says that Actæon’s curiosity was aroused by the novelty of the sight and led him to approach nearer.
