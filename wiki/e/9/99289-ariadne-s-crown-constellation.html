Ariadne's crown constellation

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Constellation, a constellation, crown constellation, shape of the crown, the stars

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is a constellation formed from Ariadne's crown to immortalize her name, placed between Hercules and Ophiuchus. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is fixed in the heavens after Bacchus places Ariadne's crown in the sky, lies between Hercules and the man who holds the serpent, and preserves the crown's form.
