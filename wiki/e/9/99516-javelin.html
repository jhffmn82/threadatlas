Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, javelin is a magical weapon given by Cynthia to Procris and then by Procris to Cephalus, which always strikes its target and returns bloodstained, ultimately killing Procris. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Procris's javelin is a weapon that never fails to hit its mark; after Procris gives it to Cephalus, he accidentally kills her with it.

javelin in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · about AD 8 · the work’s page · read it where javelin first appears

Abstract written by a model

Javelin is a beautiful weapon of unknown wood with a golden point. Given by Cynthia to Procris and then by Procris to Cephalus, it always strikes what it is aimed at, cannot be escaped, and returns to its thrower bloodstained without retrieval. During a hunt, Cephalus throws it at what he believes is a wild beast; it strikes and kills Procris, marking their reconciliation.
