The CODERAG-BENCH .. 2.4

Online tutorials are collected from GeeksforGeeks, W3Schools, tutorialspoint, and Towards Data Science through raw HTML pages from ClueWeb22. Their pages contain code snippets and textual explanations covering basic programming techniques and advanced library usage.

RACG with Open Retrieval

Software tutorials can improve HumanEval by 2.1 points when retrieved with GIST. GIST-large is particularly effective at retrieving them, while reranking tutorial chunks degrades results.
