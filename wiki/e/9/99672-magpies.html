Abstract written by a model

Magpies are nine birds, transformed from the Pierides or Emathian sisters after defeat in a dispute. In woods and groves, they perch on lofty branches or hang in the air, lamenting their fate while retaining garrulity, chattering, and imitation with distinct human-like voices.
