Sermons of Mr. Yorick in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Sermons of Mr. Yorick is a collection of sermons attributed to Laurence Sterne, including a sermon on conscience also published in Tristram Shandy. It was less popular than Sterne’s novels and Sentimental Journey, yet received early German notices, translations, and reviews. Initial reviewers treated the sermons independently; later commentary linked them to Yorick, Tristram Shandy, and The Sentimental Journey, sometimes adding inaccurate information.

Yorick’s Sermons received early notices in German periodicals, including the Göttingische Gelehrte Anzeigen, the Bremisches Magazin, and the Allgemeine deutsche Bibliothek. Early reviews often treated the sermons independently of Sterne’s fame, while later reviews associated them familiarly with Yorick, Tristram Shandy, and The Sentimental Journey.

Naturally Sterne’s letters found readers in Germany, the Yorick-Eliza correspond

Sterne’s sermons were translated and reviewed in Germany but were less popular than his novels. A Leipzig translation appeared in 1770, and later commentary praised the sermons while sometimes attaching inaccurate information about Sterne and the English church to them.
