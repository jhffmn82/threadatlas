Abstract written by a model

Pankraz, a former acquaintance of Frau Kurt and Lotte’s betrothed, owns The Sentimental Journey and imitates Yorick and Sterne’s situations as rules for life. His sentimental performances include mourning animals and releasing monkeys, but he causes his cat Riepel’s death and ignores an injured old man. A practical joke burlesques him; he ends absurd, inhuman, and unsympathetic.

A former acquaintance of Frau Kurt, whose favorite reading was Shandy, Wieland’s

Pankraz repeatedly imitates situations from Sterne and treats fancied parallels with Yorick’s experiences as guides to his own conduct. He mourns both the dead dog and the ass in The Sentimental Journey, causes the death of his cat Riepel while absorbed in sentimental emotion, ignores an old man injured by his horse, releases monkeys after recalling Sterne, and eventually becomes an absurd and inhuman figure who loses the reader’s sympathy.
