Bode’s translation of the third and fourth volumes of Yorick’s Journey,[38] that

The translation of the third and fourth volumes was published without explanatory comment from Bode. It preserves some of the original at the beginning but then introduces extensive material of Bode’s own, with more than fifty pages of additions in the second volume.
