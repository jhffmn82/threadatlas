heathen poets

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: poets

Abstract written by a model

Heathen poets probably learned traditions of the first parents’ peaceful innocence, Eden’s abundance, and the fall’s effects; they recast this happier time as a Golden Age in their own countries, disfiguring the original story through fanciful marvels.
