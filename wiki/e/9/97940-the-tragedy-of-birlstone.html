Abstract written by a model

The Tragedy of Birlstone is a murder at Birlstone Manor House, revealed after Sherlock Holmes decodes a warning concerning Douglas. Douglas is reported horribly murdered in the study, with puzzling evidence: the bell rang violently, no shot was heard by Ames or Mrs. Allen, and Barker claimed the murderer escaped through the study window. MacDonald, Barker, Sergeant Wilson, Dr. Wood, Ames, Mrs. Allen, and the household are drawn into the investigation. The apparent murder is ultimately exposed as a staging: Ted Baldwin died accidentally while attacking Douglas, and Douglas, Barker, and Douglas’s wife made Baldwin’s body appear to be Douglas’s.

The Tragedy of Birlstone is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
