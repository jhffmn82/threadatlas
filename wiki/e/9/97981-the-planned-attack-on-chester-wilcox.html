Abstract written by a model

A planned house bombing targets Chester Wilcox at the isolated Iron Dike crossroads. Boss McGinty assigns Jack McMurdo, Manders, and Reilly to kill him with blasting powder, despite the danger to Wilcox’s wife, three children, and hired help. The men detonate quarry powder at the house, but the attack is foiled because Wilcox had already relocated.

Chapter V

Boss McGinty assigns Jack McMurdo, with Manders and Reilly, to kill Chester Wilcox at the isolated Iron Dike crossroads. McGinty suggests placing blasting powder at the front door, a plan that would also kill Wilcox’s wife, three children, and hired help.

McMurdo thought long and deeply over the commission which had been so

McMurdo organized the attempt with Manders and Reilly, approaching at night with pistols and a sack of quarry powder. They set the powder against Wilcox's house and detonated it, but the attempt failed because Wilcox and his family had already moved away.
