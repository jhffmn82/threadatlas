Abstract written by a model

uses knowledge sub-graph [as a soft prompt for the large language model] Knowledge-augmented recommendation encodes the re-ranked knowledge sub-graphs with a graph neural network and maps their representations into the large language model’s embedding space. The resulting embedding is appended as a soft prompt before the input token embeddings.
