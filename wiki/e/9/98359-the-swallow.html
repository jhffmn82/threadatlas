the swallow in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

The swallow flies from the mountain’s heart to warn the stork not to enter before noon, then directs him to descend, fill bottles from the ledge between the waters, and escape without touching the mountain or pebbles.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V.—Tue CriasHine Rocks

The swallow flies from the heart of the mountain to warn the stork not to enter until noon, when the mountains rest for half an hour. It instructs him to descend from high in the air, fill the bottles from the ledge between the waters, and avoid touching the mountain walls or a pebble while escaping.
