multi-step search

method · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Multi-step Search, multi-step retrieval

Abstract written by a model

uses_retrieval_component BM25 [over an index of Wikipedia pages] Multi-step search repeatedly generates queries, retrieves top-ranked Wikipedia documents with BM25, and adds new documents to the context before final answering. Increasing the number of steps and queries improves vanilla accuracy from approximately 0.45 to 0.52, although the process requires multiple non-parallelizable inference calls.
