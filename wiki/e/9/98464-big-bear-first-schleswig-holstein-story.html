Abstract written by a model

Big bear guards golden jingle-jangles on a great tall tree and trades one to a king for what first meets him at his castle. Rejecting substitutes, he accepts the youngest daughter; when she treads on his paw, the spell breaks and he becomes a rich, handsome prince.
