boiling a nobleman ceremony in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Nduwmo woika ndu nnini is a Wajagga custom performed for a nobleman to make a name and prolong his life. A nobleman and female companion sweat in a covered trench beneath hearthstones while food boils, then are released and blessed by a wizard.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI.—THE RENEWAL OF YOUTH

The boiling a nobleman ceremony involves placing a nobleman and his female companion in a covered trench, setting hearthstones above their heads, and boiling food over a fire until evening. The participants are later released after sweating profusely, and the wizard pronounces protection and long life upon them.
