King Fumbo's head in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · the work’s page · read it where King Fumbo's head first appears

Abstract written by a model

King Fumbo’s head, separated from his body and lost in the storm in Ragbad, is the object of Tatters and Grampa’s search. Grampa says magic may be needed to restore it, so the travelers seek the Wizard of Oz or Glinda and continue after Forge John has not seen it. It is found among nearly a hundred heads in the sky, talking, then placed in Maribella’s pink knitting bag for return, where it complains of being jostled and possibly cracked.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

The King’s head has gone missing, and Pudge says that Tatters must search for it. Grampa and Tatters leave Ragbad to look for it.

CHAPTER 3

Fumbo’s head was lost during the storm in Ragbad. Grampa believes it can be restored only by magic, so he and Prince Tatters seek help from the Wizard of Oz or Glinda.

CHAPTER 10

King Fumbo’s head remains missing. Prince Forge John the First has not seen it, and finding it is one of the reasons the travelers continue their journey.

CHAPTER 15

King Fumbo's head is discovered almost first among nearly a hundred heads in the sky, where it is talking with the head of an inventor of market baskets with legs. Grampa puts it into Maribella's pink knitting bag so it can be taken back to its body. During the rainbow journey it is jostled inside the bag and complains that it may be cracked.
