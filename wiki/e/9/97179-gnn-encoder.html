GNN Encoder

technology · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: GNN, GNN encoder, GNNEncoding, GNNIndexing, GNNs, graph neural network, two GNNs

Abstract written by a model

GNN Encoder is a technology in K-RagRec that aggregates neighbor information into hop-level knowledge sub-graph representations, encodes retrieved and re-ranked sub-graphs, and is trained with a second graph neural network. A projector aligns representations with the LLM’s semantic space for knowledge-augmented recommendation.

GNN Encoder is in one work on this site, Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
