sea monster

Also called: a sea monster, monster, sea monster (Hesione's captor), the monster, κῆτος
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it was the creature for which Laomedon's daughter was demanded as an appeasement to Neptune.
  • In The Library, it snatched away the people of the plain after Poseidon sent it there.
