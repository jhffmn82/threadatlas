Crocus in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Crocus is a youth associated with Smilax in accounts of their transformation into little flowers. One account makes them constantly and happily married; another has Crocus reject Smilax’s advances. In both, their chaste, innocent, or otherwise linked lives end with both changed into flowers.
