Abstract written by a model

Animals include grass-fed horses, sheep, and herds, and blood-feeding tigers, lions, bears, and wolves. They may suffer human slaughter, serve humans in life, receive transmigrating souls, and be used for sacrifice despite prohibition.

FABLES II. AND III. [XV.60-478]

Animals are presented as beings that can suffer from human slaughter and as possible dwelling places for transmigrating souls. Horses, sheep, and herds live on grass, while tigers, lions, bears, and wolves feed on blood; sheep and oxen are praised for serving humans during life.
