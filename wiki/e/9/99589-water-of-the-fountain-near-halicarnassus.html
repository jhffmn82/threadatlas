Abstract written by a model

Clear, pleasantly tempered fountain water becomes, after Hermaphroditus's parents grant his request, enfeebling: men entering emerge half men and become effeminate when touched. Displaced barbarians use it, while its unusual effects may reflect a peculiar chemical quality.

opening

The waters are clear and pleasant in temperature. After Hermaphroditus's parents grant his request, they are made to cause a man who enters them to emerge half a man and to become effeminate when touched.

notes .. notes

The water of the fountain was used by the displaced barbarians, who went there to obtain it. Its unusual effects were possibly attributed to a peculiar chemical quality.
