Dorothy's sudden growth is a painful growth in the United States after she leaves Oz, caused by losing Oz's magical influence; she grows by inches until wishing herself back stops it and she disappears with Humpy.

CHAPTER 10

After Dorothy is removed from Oz’s magical influence, she begins growing taller by inches, causing severe pain, tight shoes, and buttons to fly from her dress. She stops the process by wishing herself back, after which she and Humpy disappear from the ravine.
