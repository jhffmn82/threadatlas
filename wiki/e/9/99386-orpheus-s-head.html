Orpheus's head

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: head, the head thrown up on the foreign sands

Abstract written by a model

After his murder, Orpheus's head is carried by the river Hebrus to Lesbos, where a serpent attacks it until Apollo drives it away and turns its jaws to stone.

Orpheus's head is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
