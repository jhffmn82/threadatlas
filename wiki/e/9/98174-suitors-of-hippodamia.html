suitors of Hippodamia in The Library

Apollodorus · AD 1 to 200 · the work’s page · read it where suitors of Hippodamia first appears

Abstract written by a model

Suitors of Hippodamia enter a marriage contest, carrying her by chariot toward the Isthmus of Corinth as Oenomaus pursues; he kills those overtaken, reportedly twelve, displaying their severed heads.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

II. Ὅτι ὁ Τάνταλος ἐν “Atdov? κολάζεται,

The suitors had to take Hippodamia into their own chariots and flee to the Isthmus of Corinth while Oenomaus pursued them. Oenomaus killed those he overtook, and some say he killed twelve suitors; he cut off their heads and nailed them to his house.

are_buried_in tomb of the suitors of Hippodamia [in a common sepulchre made by Pelops] The suitors of Hippodamia were killed by Oinomaus before Pelops defeated him. The suitors of Hippodamia were killed by Oinomaus before Pelops defeated him.
