suitors of Hippodamia in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Suitors of Hippodamia enter a marriage contest, carrying her by chariot toward the Isthmus of Corinth as Oenomaus pursues; he kills those overtaken, reportedly twelve, displaying their severed heads.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II. Ὅτι ὁ Τάνταλος ἐν “Atdov? κολάζεται,

The suitors had to take Hippodamia into their own chariots and flee to the Isthmus of Corinth while Oenomaus pursued them. Oenomaus killed those he overtook, and some say he killed twelve suitors; he cut off their heads and nailed them to his house.

suitors of Hippodamia in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

are_buried_in tomb of the suitors of Hippodamia [in a common sepulchre made by Pelops] The suitors of Hippodamia were killed by Oinomaus before Pelops defeated him. Pelops gave them a common sepulchre, and the text names many of them, including Alcathous, Euryalus, Eurymachus, Crotalus, Acrias, Capetus, Lycurgus, Lasius, Chalcodon, Tricolonus, Aristomachus, Prias, Pelagon, Aëolius, and Cronius.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

The suitors of Hippodamia were killed by Oinomaus before Pelops defeated him. Pelops gave them a common sepulchre, and the text names many of them, including Alcathous, Euryalus, Eurymachus, Crotalus, Acrias, Capetus, Lycurgus, Lasius, Chalcodon, Tricolonus, Aristomachus, Prias, Pelagon, Aëolius, and Cronius.
