  • In MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation: The adaptive judge bar τq is a query-specific threshold for adaptive document filtering in MAIN-RAG. Computed from relevance-score distributions, it approximates a query-specific optimal threshold by averaging relevant scores across retrieved candidate documents. It retains documents whose scores are at least the bar, filtering noisy documents while preserving relevant ones. Its threshold can be relaxed by subtracting n times the score standard deviation; n is MAIN-RAG’s only hyperparameter. Across different queries, τq dynamically adjusts to the distributions of relevant and noisy-document scores. Its default form generally performs well, ranking at least second-best across three benchmarks and two LLM backbones, though lowering it by standard-deviation multiples can sometimes improve results. Future work proposes a more fine-grained adaptive judge bar.

adaptive judge bar τq in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025

The adaptive judge bar τq is a query-specific threshold for adaptive document filtering in MAIN-RAG. Computed from relevance-score distributions, it approximates a query-specific optimal threshold by averaging relevant scores across retrieved candidate documents. It retains documents whose scores are at least the bar, filtering noisy documents while preserving relevant ones. Its threshold can be relaxed by subtracting n times the score standard deviation; n is MAIN-RAG’s only hyperparameter. Across different queries, τq dynamically adjusts to the distributions of relevant and noisy-document scores. Its default form generally performs well, ranking at least second-best across three benchmarks and two LLM backbones, though lowering it by standard-deviation multiples can sometimes improve results. Future work proposes a more fine-grained adaptive judge bar.

The adaptive judge bar is a query-specific threshold computed from the distribution of relevance scores. Documents with relevance scores at least as high as this threshold are retained.

3 / Multi-Agent Filtering RAG (MAIN-RAG) .. 3.3 / Adaptive Judge Bar τq

The adaptive judge bar is a query-specific threshold for filtering documents. It is based on the average relevant score of the candidate documents and can be relaxed by subtracting n times their standard deviation.

4 / Experiments .. 4.6 / Case Studies of Different Adaptive Judge

The adaptive judge bar τq approximates a query-specific optimal threshold by averaging relevance scores over retrieved documents. Its default form generally performs well, consistently ranking at least second-best across three benchmarks and two LLM backbones, while lowering it by multiples of the standard deviation sometimes improves results.

5 / Conclusion and Future Work

The adaptive judge bar dynamically adjusts according to the score distribution of relevant and noisy documents for different queries. A more fine-grained adaptive judge bar is identified as a potential direction for future work.
