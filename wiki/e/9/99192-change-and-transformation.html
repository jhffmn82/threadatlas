change and transformation

Abstract written by a model

Change and transformation are continual and universal in the physical world. Across time, heavens, seasons, bodies, landscapes, waters, cities, nations, and souls, nothing wholly perishes: forms cease to be what they were and assume new appearances, from tadpoles and coral to fallen cities and migrating souls.

FABLES II. AND III. [XV.60-478]

Change and transformation affect time, the heavens, the seasons, human bodies, landscapes, waters, and all natural forms. Nothing wholly perishes; instead, things cease to be what they were and assume different appearances.
