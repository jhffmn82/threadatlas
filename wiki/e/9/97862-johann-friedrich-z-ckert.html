Abstract written by a model

Johann Friedrich Zückert was a Berlin physician, writer, and German translator of Tristram Shandy. Born December 19, 1739, he translated its first six parts in 1763 and later supplied parts VII and VIII, but not the genuine ninth part. He showed interest in Walter Shandy’s theories through passions, moods, and temperaments, and may not have made later careless revisions. He died in Berlin on May 1, 1778.

Johann Friedrich Zückert was a Berlin physician and writer who translated the first six parts of Tristram Shandy into German. His translation appeared in 1763, and he later supplied parts VII and VIII, but he did not translate the genuine ninth part. The text suggests that he may have had little or nothing to do with the careless revisions issued in later editions.
