Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

Bill is a living iron weather cock that came to life during a storm after being charged by a live wire near Chicago. He joins Prince Tatters and Grampa, helps fight the bandits by falling on their heads, and is captured and carried head down to the bandits’ camp.

CHAPTER 4

Bill is muffled in a bandit’s cloak and placed under a rock after repeatedly crowing. Grampa and Tatters rescue him during their escape, but Bill’s loud crowing alerts the bandits. He falls through the hollow tree with Grampa and Prince Tatters.

CHAPTER 5

Bill's crowing betrays the travelers during their fall, causing Grampa to threaten to discharge him, but Grampa soon relents. Bill reveals that he stole a tiny golden key from the robber chief and discovers the golden gate leading into the Garden of Gorba.

CHAPTER 6

Bill warns that he will peck the wizard in the eye but is ordered not to peck without Grampa’s command. He discovers the flower maiden and repeatedly calls her a Princess, though she identifies herself as Urtha instead. Bill loses interest when she is not a Princess, urges the others to continue searching, and hides in an oleander bush after Urtha blows him kisses.

CHAPTER 10

Bill falls while clinging to Tatters and the red umbrella, struggles in the underground lake, and receives medicine dashed over his head because he cannot swallow it. He remains lively on Fire Island, repeatedly announces the fortune, and flies into the boat’s bow as the party travels toward Blazes.

CHAPTER 11

Bill rides on Grampa's shoulder, survives the volcanic eruption, and keeps watch while the others sleep. At sunrise he crows loudly, announces that land is ahead, and flies after the others to the ice-covered shore.

CHAPTER 13

Bill arrives just after the party is stranded and asks about the golden ship and the diamonds. When Grampa smokes the tobacco, Bill vanishes and remains only as an unseen cock's crow. He leads the others east by crowing and fluttering his invisible iron wings.

CHAPTER 15

Invisi-Bill flies with the company across the ocean, Ev, and the desert, calling out directions and warning them when the magical crow transformation is ending. He predicts the storm and suggests that King Fumbo's head might be found in a storm because it was lost in one. He identifies Fumbo's head when the company finds it and travels down the rainbow balanced on Grampa's shoulder.

CHAPTER 17

Bill identifies Dorothy as the Princess when Grampa's party arrives, though Grampa initially dismisses his claim. He explains that the group followed directions on a bottle and traveled by falling, swimming, exploding, sailing, and flying, and he continues to insist that they must find the Princess and the fortune.

CHAPTER 18

Bill jokes about his name while marching and warns the company about trouble ahead. He avoids being captured by the Play Fellows, circles anxiously over the park, helps locate Grampa, and directs the others toward an exit.

CHAPTER 21

Bill returns from the playground carrying Grampa's lost leg, which he rescued from King Capers and two Pierrettes. He is celebrated by everyone and is present when the yellow hen produces a gold brick and when the throne room disappears in a flash.
