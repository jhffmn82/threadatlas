Abstract written by a model

Snorer, also known as Nickadoodle or Nick, is an unusual huge, ridiculous pink bird and crow from the Isle of Un. His long curved blue beak, curly nose, and radio ear let him hear his own explosive, habitual snoring, which wakes him and often makes communication difficult. A faithful, devoted companion of Notta, Bob Up, and Nickadoodle’s friends, he lives with Notta and Bob in Notta’s tent and loves Notta. He knows that the Flyaboutabus is the means of escape from Un. He joins Notta, Bob, and the Cowardly Lion, cuts the vehicle’s rope, pilots and guides it, finds food, and helps restore Notta and Bob to normal. Through crashes, storms, falls, and mountain travel, he repeatedly protects or carries Bob and tries to save Notta, though exhaustion makes him sleep and snore. He helps reach the Emerald City, where Tik Tok seizes him. Later he wants to return to America to patent his nose and teach people to snore properly, but remains in Oz with Notta and Bob Up, helping with their surprising shows.

Snorer is in one work on this site, The Cowardly Lion of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

Nickadoodle is a huge, ridiculous pink bird whose curled blue beak lets him hear himself snore and wake up. He joins Notta, Bob, and the Cowardly Lion, explains life on the Isle of Un, and tells them that the Flyaboutabus is their only escape. He cuts the vehicle's rope during the escape and guides it with its lever.

Chapter 13

Nickadoodle pilots the Flyaboutabus, searches for food, and brings eggs down from the Travelers' Tree. He understands that Mustafa's magic ring has been activated because the Cowardly Lion was not captured, then uses strategy by tying a rope around the lion and placing the end in Notta's hand. This causes Notta and Bob to return to normal.

Chapter 14

Nick's loud snoring makes communication difficult and repeatedly wakes him, but he helps rescue Notta from the water during the storm. He steadies Notta on the Cowardly Lion, stops the Flyaboutabus after the crash, and suggests going into the nearby city to ask where they are.

Chapter 16

Nick puts a wing around Bob in the Preservatory and comments on the canned animals. When the Flyaboutabus crashes into a day star, he catches Bob and carries him safely to a huge boulder, then tries to break Notta's fall with his body.

Chapter 17

Nick flies with Bob Up and Notta after Crunch catches them, and he investigates the view from the mountain to confirm that the Emerald City is nearby. He separates from the Cowardly Lion with Notta and Bob, helps them travel down the mountain, and repeatedly falls asleep or snores from exhaustion.

Chapter 18

Nick returns after breakfast and is startled by Notta's witch disguise, causing his nose to fall off its hook. He travels with Notta and Bob Up, repeatedly checks that his curly nose is secure, flies into the Emerald City gardens, and is seized by Tik Tok.

Chapter 22

Snorer says that he wants to return to America to patent his nose and teach people how to snore properly. He remains in Oz with Notta and Bob Up, sharing Notta's tent and helping with the surprising shows there.
