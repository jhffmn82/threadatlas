A purification prescribed by Bacchus: Midas follows the Pactolus upstream, plunges into its spring, and his golden-touch power leaves his body for the river.

FABLE II. [XI.85-145]

Bacchus instructs Midas to follow the Pactolus upstream to its source and plunge his head beneath the bubbling spring. Midas obeys, and the golden virtue leaves his body and passes into the river.
