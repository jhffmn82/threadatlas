Bode’s translation of the third and fourth volumes of Yorick’s Journey,[38] that

The third and fourth volumes were published in England in the spring of 1769 and met with generally unfavorable criticism and little genuine popularity. Bode’s translation diverged extensively from the original, especially in the fourth volume.
