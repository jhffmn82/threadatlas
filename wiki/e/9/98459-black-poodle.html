Abstract written by a model

In the Hanoverian story, an enchanted prince disguised as a black poodle promises a king a tinkling lion-leaf for what first meets him, carries off the youngest daughter, and is revealed when she burns its dog-skin.

XII.—TuE Vow or [DOMENEUS

The black poodle promises a king a tinkling lion-leaf in exchange for whatever first comes out of the king's house to meet him. It carries off the king's youngest daughter after she is identified as the promised person, but is revealed to be an enchanted prince when she burns its dog-skin.
