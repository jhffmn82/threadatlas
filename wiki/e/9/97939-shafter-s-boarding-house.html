Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

PART II / Chapter I

The Shafter boarding house stands back from Sheridan Street in Vermissa and is run by Jacob Shafter with the help of Ettie. It becomes McMurdo’s new lodging after he agrees to pay seven dollars a week in advance.

Chapter II

Shafter's boarding house contains ten or a dozen boarders, among them McMurdo, who quickly becomes its most prominent resident. Mr. Shafter orders McMurdo to sleep there only one more night because he fears the consequences of housing a Freeman.
