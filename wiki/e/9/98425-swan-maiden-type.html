X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The Swan Maiden type features supernatural females who temporarily assume human form, are constrained into marriage by the concealment of their skins, and later recover those skins and return to their original life. The marriage of Peleus and Thetis is presented as belonging to this familiar type.
