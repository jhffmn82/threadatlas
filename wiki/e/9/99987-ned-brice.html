Ned Brice

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Ned, The expressman, expressman, the express messenger, the expressman, the young expressman

Abstract written by a model

Ned Brice is an expressman for Adams & Co. and express messenger guarding treasure on a coach. When a tree blocks the road, he reports it, worries about his duty and the key to the treasure compartment, accompanies passengers to a cabin, and explains Yuba Bill’s roughness by citing a recent robbery. Later, when masked attackers stop the coach, he reaches for his rifle but is restrained; finding the express box forced and its greenbacks gone, he hands it over.

Ned Brice is in one work on this site, From Sand Hill to Pine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
