anemone

Also called: Anemone, a flower of bloody hue, flower, wind Flower, wind-flower

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, anemone springs from Adonis’s blood as a crimson wind-flower, opens when winds blow, and is short-lived because winds beat it down. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, anemone is a short-lived, bloody-hued flower formed when Venus sprinkles nectar on Adonis's blood, and its opened petals are blown away by wind.
