XII.—TuE Vow or [DOMENEUS

The white wolf in the Lorraine story owns a castle and its talking roses. It spares a man on condition that he bring the first person he meets at home, receives the man's youngest daughter, and is revealed as a fairy condemned to appear as a wolf by day; after the daughter reveals his secret, he falls dead.
