Tartaro

Also called: the Tartaro

Abstract written by a model

Tartaro is a one-eyed colossus, shepherd, and hunter of people, with an old witch for a mother. He eats one sheep daily before human victims. A young man blinds him, escapes in a sheep’s skin, and defeats his pursuit by cutting off the finger bearing Tartaro’s ring.

Tartaro is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
