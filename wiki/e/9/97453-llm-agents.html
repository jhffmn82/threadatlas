Abstract

LLM agents collaboratively filter and score retrieved documents within MAIN-RAG. Their inter-agent consensus supports robust document selection.

1 / Introduction

LLM agents collaborate in MAIN-RAG to process retrieved documents. Agent-1 predicts answers for each query, Agent-2 judges document support and assigns relevance scores, and Agent-3 produces the final answer from the filtered document list.
