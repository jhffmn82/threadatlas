Venus's revenge on the Sun

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: disturbs him with the like passion, exacts satisfaction of the Sun

Abstract written by a model

Venus, driven by the discovery of her affair with Mars, revenges herself on the Sun for exposing it by making him love Leucothoë, disrupting his gaze, course, winter hours, and light.

Venus's revenge on the Sun is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Venus takes revenge on the Sun for revealing her secret affair with Mars by causing him to experience the same disruptive passion. His love for Leucothoë makes him gaze at her instead of the universe, alters his rising and setting, lengthens the winter hours, and affects his light.
