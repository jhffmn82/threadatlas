Ted Baldwin in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Ted Baldwin is a violent Scowrer and higher official of Lodge 341, marked on the forearm with the lodge’s branded circle-and-triangle symbol. A boss and enforcer in Vermissa, he claims Ettie Shafter as his woman and becomes McMurdo’s enemy over her. Though he challenges McGinty’s favor toward McMurdo, McGinty forces him to reconcile publicly; Baldwin remains resentful as McMurdo rises. He eagerly leads the assault on James Stanger, beating him until McMurdo stops him, and later leads the party that murders William Hales, for which the lodge honors him. Baldwin is identified by John Douglas as the worst among his pursuers. He secretly enters Birlstone Manor to attack Douglas with a knife and buckshot gun, but during their struggle the gun fires into Baldwin’s face and kills him. His body is then disguised as Douglas’s.

Ted Baldwin, identified by Douglas as his worst enemy, secretly entered Birlstone Manor and attacked him with a knife and a buckshot gun. During their struggle, the gun discharged into Baldwin's face, killing him, after which his body was disguised as Douglas's.

Chapter II

Teddy Baldwin is described as a boss of the Scowrers and as the man to whom Ettie is attached. He confronts McMurdo, claims Ettie as his own, threatens to punish her and harm McMurdo, and displays a branded circle-and-triangle sign on his forearm.

The bar of McGinty’s saloon was crowded as usual; for it was the

Ted Baldwin confronts McGinty and McMurdo over a young lady, claiming that McGinty has favored a newcomer over a man who has supported him for five years. McGinty overpowers and threatens him when he challenges the ruling, then compels him to reconcile with McMurdo through the lodge's quarrelling toast. Baldwin submits outwardly but remains visibly sullen.

When the drinks which followed the ceremony of initiation had all been

Ted Baldwin complains that workers and local employers are resisting the lodge and eagerly accepts McGinty's order to attack James Stanger. He leads the group into the Vermissa Herald office and repeatedly strikes Stanger after the editor has fallen. Baldwin tries to continue the beating until McMurdo pushes him back and threatens him with a pistol.

Chapter V

Ted Baldwin resents McMurdo’s rapid rise but avoids confronting him because McMurdo is ready to fight. Baldwin leads the party that murders William Hales of Stake Royal and is honored by the lodge for the deed.
