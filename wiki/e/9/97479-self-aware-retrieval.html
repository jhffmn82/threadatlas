Self-aware Retrieval in SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation

3 .. 3.4

Self-aware retrieval uses the self-aware uncertainty estimator to decide whether external knowledge is needed for rationale generation. Retrieval is triggered when the uncertainty of the rationale-generation context exceeds an empirical threshold, and the large language model generates a pseudo-rationale whose high-uncertainty, low-probability tokens are removed to form the search query.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

Self-aware Retrieval dynamically determines when to retrieve knowledge. Removing this capability reduces performance because retrieving knowledge without the LLM supplementing it can mislead generation.
