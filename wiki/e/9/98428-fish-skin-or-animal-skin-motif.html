causes fairy wife’s departure [when the wife discovers and resumes the cast skin] The fish-skin or animal-skin motif involves a supernatural spouse who removes a fish, bird, beast, or other skin to live as a human. When the hidden skin is discovered, the spouse resumes its original form and returns to its former home, often after an act of unkindness or a cruel taunt.
