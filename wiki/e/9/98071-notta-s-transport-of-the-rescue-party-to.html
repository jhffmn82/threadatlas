Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

Notta touches those he believes will be useful in a battle, including Sir Hokus of Pokes, the Tin Woodman, Tik Tok, Glinda, the Wizard of Oz, Dorothy, Bob Up, Snorer, Ozma, and the Scarecrow. He recites his magic verse, and everyone he has touched is transported instantly from the Emerald City to Mudge.
