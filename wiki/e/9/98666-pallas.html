Pallas in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Pallas, Triton’s daughter raised by him, trained in war with Athena and fathered fifty sons; startled by Zeus’s aegis while attacking Athena, she was wounded by Athena.

Ἱστορία δὲ! ἡ περὶ τοῦ παλλαδίου τοιάδε

Pallas was Triton’s daughter and practiced the arts of war with Athena. When she was about to strike Athena, Zeus interposed the aegis; startled, Pallas looked up and was wounded by Athena.
