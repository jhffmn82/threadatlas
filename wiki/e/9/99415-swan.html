Abstract written by a model

Swan is a bird and Cycnus’s new form, taken through grief and attachment to Phaëton. Avoiding the heavens and air after fire from there, it chooses streams, pools, and wide lakes; none were reported on the Po.

opening

The swan is the new form taken by Cycnus. It avoids the heavens and the air because of the fire sent from there and chooses streams, pools, and wide lakes instead.

notes .. notes

Cycnus was said to have been changed into a swan because of his grief and attachment to Phaëton. Lucian reported that no such swans had been seen on the banks of the Po.
