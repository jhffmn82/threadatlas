XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

After the robber escaped, the blind giant offered him a gold ring as a guest's reward. The ring bewitched the robber, forcing him to shout that he was there; he cut off the finger bearing it and threw the finger and ring to the giant in order to escape.
