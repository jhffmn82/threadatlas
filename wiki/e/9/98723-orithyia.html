Orithyia

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: ᾿Ωρείθυιαν

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Orithyia is carried off by Boreas while playing by the Ilissus and becomes the mother of Cleopatra, Chione, Zetes, and Calais. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Orithyia is loved by Boreas, who seizes her and carries her off after failing to woo her gently.

XV. Πανδίονος δὲ ἀποθανόντος οἱ παῖδες τὰ

Orithyia was a daughter of Erechtheus and was carried off by Boreas while she was playing by the Ilissus. She bore Boreas the daughters Cleopatra and Chione and the winged sons Zetes and Calais.
