Orithyia in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Orithyia, daughter of Erechtheus, is carried off by Boreas while playing by the Ilissus and becomes mother of Cleopatra, Chione, Zetes, and Calais.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XV. Πανδίονος δὲ ἀποθανόντος οἱ παῖδες τὰ

Orithyia was a daughter of Erechtheus and was carried off by Boreas while she was playing by the Ilissus. She bore Boreas the daughters Cleopatra and Chione and the winged sons Zetes and Calais.
