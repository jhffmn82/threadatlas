Bode’s translation of the third and fourth volumes of Yorick’s Journey,[38] that

Das Hündchen is an episode added by Bode in which a boy grieves over a dead dog. Critics praised it as a fine addition to Yorick’s adventures, but the text considers its straightforward pathos unlike Sterne’s combination of sentiment and whimsical narration.
