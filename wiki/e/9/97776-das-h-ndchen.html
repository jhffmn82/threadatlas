Abstract written by a model

Das Hündchen is a brief episode added by Bode to his translation of the third and fourth volumes of Yorick’s Journey, replacing the Rue Tireboudin episode. A boy grieves for his dead dog and rejects promises of others; critics praised its pathos, though it differs from Sterne’s sentiment and whimsy.
