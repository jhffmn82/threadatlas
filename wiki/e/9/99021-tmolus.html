Tmolus

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: aged umpire, king of Lydia, mountain God, mountain-god, sacred Tmolus, umpire

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Tmolus was a mountain deity and king of Lydia who judged the contest between Pan and Apollo, favoring Apollo and displeasing Midas. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Tmolus was the mountain-god umpire of the contest between Pan and Apollo, awarding victory to Apollo after hearing his lyre.

Abstract written by a model

Tmolus is a mountain deity and, according to Clitophon, king of Lydia; as umpire between Pan and Apollo, he hears both performances and favors Apollo, displeasing only Midas.
