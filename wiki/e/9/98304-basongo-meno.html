Basongo Meno

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Basongo Meno are a group of tribes in the Congo basin, north of the Sankuru and Kasai rivers. They make fishing-traps from Raphia palm ribs and say fire was first produced while a man made one.

Basongo Meno is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
