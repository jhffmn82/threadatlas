Abstract written by a model

serve Ino [as attendants] The Sidonian attendants follow Ino's footprints to the edge of the rock and, believing Ino and Melicerta dead, lament the house of Cadmus and reproach Juno. Juno transforms them according to their positions: some become fixed in stone and others become birds that skim over the sea.
