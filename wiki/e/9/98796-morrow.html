Morrow in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Morrow is a kingdom in Oz, named in a golden message as the destination; Ozma recognizes it, and the Wizard transports company there, where an overgrown castle was once her hunting lodge.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Morrow is named in the golden message as the place to go. Ozma recognizes the name and says she feels sure that Morrow is a kingdom in Oz.

CHAPTER 9

Morrow is the kingdom where the Wizard transports the company. It contains the ancient, overgrown castle that Ozma remembers as a hunting lodge.
