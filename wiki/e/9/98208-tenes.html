Tenes in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Tenes, son of Cycnus and Proclia, though some called him Apollo’s son, was banished by Cycnus in a chest. He settled on Leucophrys, renamed it Tenedos, ruled there, and was killed by Achilles while defending the island.

Tenes was the son of Cycnus and Proclia, though some said he was the son of Apollo. After being cast adrift in a chest by Cycnus, he settled on Leucophrys, renamed it Tenedos, and ruled there; Achilles later killed him while he defended the island.

Tennes in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tennes, son of Cycnus and Proclea and brother of Hemithea, was falsely accused by Phylonome, cast into the sea, reached Leucophrys, rejected Cycnus’s forgiveness, became associated with Tenedos, and was killed by Achilles defending it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIV.

Tennes was the son of Cycnus and Proclea and the brother of Hemithea. After being falsely accused by Phylonome, he was cast into the sea with his sister, reached Leucophrys, cut the ropes of Cycnus’s ship when his father sought forgiveness, and was later said to have been killed by Achilles while defending Tenedos.
