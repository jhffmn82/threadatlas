Also called: Raven’s daughter, younger sister

Abstract written by a model

Raven’s younger daughter, longing to join a bright star, is taken to the sky and finds a feeble old man. She borrows Spider’s rope to descend, but it falls short of earth, leaving her suspended above her father’s house until she dies.

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

Raven’s younger daughter wishes to be with a bright star and is taken to the sky, where she finds that her star is a feeble old man. She tries to descend using a rope borrowed from Spider, but the rope is too short, and she dies suspended above her father’s house.
