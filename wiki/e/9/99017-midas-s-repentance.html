Abstract written by a model

Midas's repentance begins when his golden-touch power turns food and drink to gold; he confesses wrongdoing and begs Bacchus, who restores him and directs him to the Pactolus.

FABLE II. [XI.85-145]

After discovering that his food and drink have become gold, Midas recognizes that he has done wrong and begs Bacchus to deliver him from the calamity. Bacchus accepts his confession, restores him to his former state, and directs him to the Pactolus.
