Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLES V. AND VI. [XII.536-628]

Periclymenus, the brother of Nestor and son of Neleus, possesses from Neptune the power to assume different shapes. In combat with Hercules, he changes into an eagle, but Hercules shoots him where the wing joins the side, and the arrow's continued pressure drives it into his neck.
