Abstract written by a model

Periclymenus, son of Neleus and brother of Nestor, receives from Neptune the power to change shape. During combat with Hercules, he becomes an eagle; Hercules shoots him with an arrow, which enters his neck and kills him.

Periclymenus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLES V. AND VI. [XII.536-628]

Periclymenus, the brother of Nestor and son of Neleus, possesses from Neptune the power to assume different shapes. In combat with Hercules, he changes into an eagle, but Hercules shoots him where the wing joins the side, and the arrow's continued pressure drives it into his neck.
