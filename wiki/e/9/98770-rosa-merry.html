Rosa Merry in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Rosa Merry is the short, pretty, merry Queen of Kimbaloo, wearing a gold crown and keeping her castle flower-filled and spick and spandy. Rich in bouquets, she tends the bouquet bush for flower girls. Fond of riddles, she sews for and comforts Kinda Jolly, then helps seek their vanished dinner and invisible cook.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Rosa Merry is the quaint, merry Queen of Kimbaloo and keeps the castle spick and spandy with flowers. She manages the bouquet bush and gives bouquets to the flower girls to sell.

CHAPTER 3

Rosa Merry sews a button onto Kinda Jolly's suspenders and guesses at the riddle about dinner. After Mombi and the dinner disappear, she comforts Kinda, sends for the Town Crier, and offers to cook while they seek an invisible cook.
