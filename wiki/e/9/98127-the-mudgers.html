Abstract written by a model

The Mudgers are short-tempered, long-legged, blue-whiskered inhabitants of Mudge, wearing white robes and turbans against heat and desert sands. Forbidden to leave Mudge after earlier raids on neighboring countries, they steal from one another, quarrel, and hunt lions for Mustafa. Before Mustafa’s throne they capture Notta in a lion disguise, restrain him, and try to take him to the lions. Later they accuse Notta of wizardry, threaten him, then retreat and escort Notta and Bob to the enclosure.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The Mudgers are short-tempered, long-legged inhabitants of Mudge who wear white robes and turbans and have bright blue whiskers. They once raided neighboring countries, but after being forbidden to leave Mudge they stole from one another, quarreled, and hunted lions for Mustafa.

Chapter 3

The Mudgers are the inhabitants who shout and move about before Mustafa's throne. They capture Notta in his lion disguise, restrain him with a net and rope, and try to take him to the lions.

Chapter 4

The Mudgers initially gape at Notta, accuse him of being a wizard, and threaten him. They retreat from the royal tent when Mustafa orders them to do so, and the Mudger Guard surrounds the tent and escorts Notta and Bob to the enclosure.
