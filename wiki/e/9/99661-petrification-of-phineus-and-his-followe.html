Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE FIFTH.

Perseus petrifies Phineus’s followers by making them behold the Gorgon head; two hundred remaining bodies become rigid stone. Phineus recognizes his petrified friends, begs Perseus to remove the head, and is then turned into a stone statue himself.
