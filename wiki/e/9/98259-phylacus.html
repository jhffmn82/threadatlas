Phylacus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Phylacus, son of Deion by Diomede and father of Iphiclus, is associated in some accounts with possession of kine and reporting Melampus’s escape. When Iphiclus was a child, Phylacus pursued him with a knife and lodged it in a wild pear-tree, said to cause Iphiclus’s childlessness.

IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE ΟΕ PHYLACUS

The kine of Phylacus are named in the title “Melampus and the kine of Phylacus.”

IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

Phylacus was the father of Iphiclus and, in some accounts, the possessor of the kine and the person who reported Melampus's escape. When Iphiclus was a child, Phylacus pursued him with a knife and stuck it into a wild pear-tree; this event was said to have caused Iphiclus's childlessness.
