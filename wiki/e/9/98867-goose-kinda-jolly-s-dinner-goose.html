Abstract written by a model

Kinda Jolly's dinner goose is a talking white goose bought in a wooden crate for a thousand gold buttons. At the castle, he recognizes Mombi and demands to know what she has done with the King. Though called the finest and fattest goose, he disappears before dinner, prompting General Whiffenpuff's search. Later identified as Pajuka, he is chosen by the Maltese cat queen as prisoner and dinner; despite protesting, he is dragged away with Snip.

CHAPTER 1

The goose bought by Kinda Jolly for dinner is a talking white bird brought to the castle in a wooden crate. It recognizes Mombi and demands to know what she has done with the King.

CHAPTER 3

Kinda Jolly's dinner goose is described as the finest and fattest goose he has ever seen and as having cost a thousand gold buttons. It disappears before it can be served, and General Whiffenpuff searches for it.

CHAPTER 6

The Maltese cat queen chooses Pajuka as a prisoner and dinner, asking how old and tender he is. Pajuka protests that he is an old goose and warns that eating him will give the cats fits. The guards nevertheless drag him away with Snip while the cats howl with delight.
