Reise von Johann depicts Johann and his master traveling through southern Germany. It imitates Yorick-like travel and includes adventures with maids, found papers, a death-bed scene, a village funeral, a whimsical Furth Catechismus, and a disturbing story found among a ragged woman’s papers. The work was associated with von Thümmel by Ebeling without sufficient reason and was considered by Schiller and Goethe as a possible subject for the Xenien, though no such use followed.
