the Princess's enchantment in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The Princess's enchantment is a magical transformation affecting Urtha, Princess of Perhaps City. Abrog takes her to a hidden garden intending to turn her into mud, but instead changes her into a flower fairy and erases her memory of her former life. She appears bewitched and altered until three drops of a cure on her head remove the enchantment and reveal the Princess.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 19

The Princess appears changed and is described by Peer Haps as bewitched, so he keeps her covered and says she cannot yet be seen. The text does not explain the enchantment, but removing the cloak reveals her as the Princess herself.

CHAPTER 20

Abrog carried Urtha to the hidden garden to bewitch her and change her into mud. Because Urtha was sweet, lovely, and good, the spell instead transformed her into a bewitching little flower fairy.

CHAPTER 21

Urtha's enchantment has caused her to lose all memory of her former existence as Princess of Perhaps City. It is removed when three drops of the cure are placed on her head.
