Hercules's shooting of Nessus

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Heracles's shooting of Nessus, pierces him through the back, wound, ἐτόξευσεν
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hercules shoots Nessus through the back with an arrow carrying the venom of Lernæan poison.
  • In The Library, Heracles shoots Nessus in the heart with an arrow after hearing Deianeira cry out during Nessus's attempted assault.

Hercules's shooting of Nessus in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

uses Lernæan poison [the arrow's venom] Hercules shoots Nessus through the back with an arrow as the Centaur flees with Deïanira. The arrow's barbed steel carries the venom of the Lernæan poison, which enters Nessus's wounds.
