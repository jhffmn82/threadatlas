Hercules's shooting of Nessus

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Heracles's shooting of Nessus, pierces him through the back, wound, ἐτόξευσεν

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hercules shoots Nessus through the back with an arrow carrying the venom of Lernæan poison. In The Library, Heracles shoots Nessus in the heart with an arrow after hearing Deianeira cry out during Nessus's attempted assault.
