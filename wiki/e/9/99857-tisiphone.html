Tisiphone in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Tisiphone, a Fury and daughter of Night, serves Juno’s vengeance. Leaving the infernal realms with gore-stained torch, blood-red cloak, snakes, and poison, she infects Athamas and Ino with madness, rage, and murderous intent.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Tisiphone agrees to Juno's command and leaves the infernal realms carrying a bloody torch and wearing a cloak red with blood. She brings snakes and a poisonous mixture to Athamas and Ino, infecting their minds with madness, rage, and murderous intent.
