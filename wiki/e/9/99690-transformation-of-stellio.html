transformation of Stellio in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Stellio mocks Ceres while she drinks; she sprinkles him with barley and liquor, transforming him into a small, spotted newt-like creature, possibly a poet’s fancy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

When Stellio mocks Ceres as she drinks, she sprinkles him with barley mixed with liquor. His face changes, legs replace his arms, a tail appears, and his body contracts into a small, spotted creature resembling a newt.
