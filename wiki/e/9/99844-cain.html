Cain in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Cain is a first murderer, possibly original to Lycaon; he murders Abel during sacrifice, is called to account by Almighty, and is sentenced to be a fugitive and wanderer on earth.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

Cain is proposed as a possible original for the story of Lycaon. Both are described as first murderers, and Cain killed Abel while Abel was offering sacrifice; Cain was also personally called to account by the Almighty and sentenced to wander the earth.
