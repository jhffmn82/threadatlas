concerns Athens [over giving the city its name] The dispute concerns the right to name or claim patronage over Athens. Minerva’s olive tree was judged more useful than Neptune’s gift, which was variously understood as a fountain, a horse, or possibly a ship; later explanations connect the myth with the transition from piracy to agriculture and civilized life.
