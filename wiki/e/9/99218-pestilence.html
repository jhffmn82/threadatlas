Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE VII. [XV.622-744]

The pestilence infects the Latian air and causes bodies to waste away as their blood dries up. It continues despite mortal efforts and medical skill, but ends when Æsculapius comes to Rome.
