Chapter 16

The Imperial Squawmos is a towering cookywitch who rules the Preservatory and has preserved its inhabitants. She wants to put Notta, Bob, the Cowardly Lion, and Nick into preserving kettles, but explains that the inhabitants preserve themselves to avoid working. She crushes the Flyaboutabus's steering gear with an iron spoon before Notta launches it upward.
