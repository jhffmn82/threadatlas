Abstract written by a model

Periphetes, son of Hephaestus and Anticlia, was a weak-legged roadside evildoer called the Clubman or club-bearer for his iron club. He used it to kill passers-by until Theseus wrested it from him and killed him.

Periphetes is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

XVI. Aethra bore to Aegeus a son Theseus, and

Periphetes was the son of Hephaestus and Anticlia and was surnamed the Clubman. He was crazy on his legs and used an iron club to kill passers-by, but Theseus wrested the club from him and killed him.
