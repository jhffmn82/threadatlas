  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV: explains myth of Cinyras and Myrrha [using accounts by Lucian, Phurnutus, and others] Le Clerc explains the story of Cinyras and Myrrha by drawing on Lucian, Phurnutus, and other authors. He interprets the tale as a distorted account involving Cinyras’s drunken indecency, Myrrha’s report of it, and Cinyras’s resulting curse against Myrrha and Adonis.
