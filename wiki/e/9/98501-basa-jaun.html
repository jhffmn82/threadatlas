Basa-Jaun

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The Basa-Jaun, the blinded monster, the stranger

Abstract written by a model

Basa-Jaun is a hairy, one-eyed giant who owns sheep and eats one soldier. After trying to catch the other, he is blinded; the survivor escapes in a sheep’s skin, cuts off his talking ring-bearing finger, and causes him to drown.

Basa-Jaun is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
