Abstract

Ten retrievers are evaluated on CODERAG-BENCH. They often struggle to fetch useful contexts for code generation.

RACG with Open Retrieval

The study uses three top retrievers along with StarCoder2 and OpenAI retrieval with GPT-4o generation. Retriever performance varies by source, with GIST-large particularly effective for tutorials and BM25 and OpenAI embeddings finding higher-quality programming solutions.
