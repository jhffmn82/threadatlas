Melicerta

Abstract written by a model

Melicerta, son of Ino and Athamas, flees Athamas carried by Ino and leaps with her into the sea. Neptune removes his mortality, transforming him into Palæmon, a sea deity worshipped in Greece and Rome as Palæmon and by Romans as Portunus.

Melicerta is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Melicerta is carried by Ino as she flees from Athamas and leaps into the sea. Neptune removes his mortality and transforms him into the deity Palæmon.

notes .. notes

Melicerta was the son of Ino and Athamas and the foster-child of Ino's sister Semele's son, Bacchus. He fled with Ino and was carried with her into the sea, after which he was identified with the sea deity Palæmon and worshipped in Greece and Rome.
