FABLES IV. V. AND VI. [XV.479-621]

Hippolytus was restored to life through the powerful remedies of Apollo's son, potent herbs, and Pæonian aid, despite Pluto's opposition. Diana then concealed him with clouds, changed his appearance, and gave him the name Virbius.
