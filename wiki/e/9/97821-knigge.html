Abstract written by a model

Knigge is a writer only remotely connected with Laurence Sterne. Reise nach Braunschweig contains Schottenius and resembles Fielding and Smollett more than Sterne, despite readerly conversation and a possible hobby-horse motif. He also authored Peter Claus and the epistolary love story Der Roman meines Lebens, neither a Sterne imitation.

Adolph von Knigge is only remotely connected with Sterne. His Reise nach Braunschweig resembles Fielding and Smollett more than Sterne, although it includes conversation with the reader and perhaps a Sternean hobby-horse motif; his novels Peter Claus and Der Roman meines Lebens are not Sterne imitations, though both mention Sterne or quote ideas from him.
