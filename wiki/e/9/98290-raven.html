Raven

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: raven, the raven

Abstract written by a model

Raven is a fire-bringer who steals fire: among the Chilcotin Indians, he lights pitchwood shavings in his hair and kindles fires throughout the country; among the Haida Indians, he takes a burning brand and spreads fire in flight; and he returns from an island with a half-burnt bill. He can wear or remove feathers and appear as a spruce fir leaf. As father of two daughters, he gathers people and animals to attack Sky people after one dies descending.

Raven is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

Raven is the father of two daughters who go to the sky in the Quinault version. After the younger daughter dies while attempting to descend, Raven helps gather the people and animals to attack the Sky people and recover the elder daughter.

In a myth told by the Thompson Indians, who inhabit the

Among the Chilcotin Indians, Raven steals fire by igniting pitchwood shavings tied in his hair and uses it to kindle fires throughout the country. Among the Haida Indians, Raven steals a burning brand from a chief or deity and spreads fire wherever he flies; the Tlingit Indians also tell of Raven bringing fire from an island.
