Percy Vere in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Percy Vere is a young, forgetful poet from Perhaps City who has lived on Maybe Mountain all his life. A prime favorite and son of Peer Haps, he attends to guess work, habitually produces Forgetful poetry, and must remember why he is unhappy. When the Princess of Perhaps City and Abrog disappear, he defends his bravery against Perix, descends Maybe Mountain to seek them, falls into Oz, and, scratched, hungry, and friendless, meets Dorothy. He tells her of the disappearances and travels with her and Toto toward the Emerald City. Captured by a runaway road, he helps Dorothy escape by catching a tree branch. In a woodcutter’s cabin he makes breakfast and leaves a poem as payment. On Monday Mountain, after being struck by soap and fighting the wild wash women, he refuses the Princess’s marriage proposal and is seized by the Tubbies. Forced to wash clothes, he cheers Dorothy with rhymes, solves the word that stops the running white fence, and leads Dorothy and Toto through its gate. Later, as companion to Dorothy, Toto, and Grampa, he recounts Abrog’s prophecy and his journey, helps gather wood, marches with Grampa’s army, and makes riddles, puns, and poems. After escaping the Play Fellows’ games, he reaches Peer Haps’s palace and reassures Tatters that marrying the Princess fulfills Tatters’s search for a princess and fortune.

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Percy Vere is a forgetful poet who attends to the guess work in Perhaps City and is a favorite of Peer Haps. He helps discover that the Princess and Abrog are missing, defends his courage when Perix mocks him, and descends Maybe Mountain to find them.

CHAPTER 9

Percy Vere, the Forgetful Poet, reaches Oz after falling down the steep sides of Maybe Mountain and wanders through the Winkle land, scratched, hungry, and without friends. He tells Dorothy that the Princess of Perhaps City and Abrog, the Prophet, have disappeared, and agrees to go with her to the Emerald City. He is captured with Dorothy by the runaway road and helps her escape by grabbing a tree branch.

CHAPTER 14

Percy Vere spends the night in the forest, makes breakfast in a woodcutter’s cabin, and leaves a poem as payment. He climbs the mountain with Dorothy and is struck by a cake of soap before fighting back against the wild wash women. The Princess of Monday Mountain asks to marry him, but he refuses, and the Tubbies seize him.

CHAPTER 16

Percy Vere is forced to wash clothes on Monday Mountain and tries to keep Dorothy cheerful with rhymes. He figures out the word that stops the running white fence, opens its gate, and leads Dorothy and Toto to escape.

CHAPTER 17

Percy Vere initially mistakes Tatters for a ragged non-Prince and recites a mocking verse about him. He tells the party about Abrog's prophecy, the disappearance of the Princess of Perhaps City and Abrog, and his adventures with Dorothy, then helps Tatters gather wood for camp.

CHAPTER 18

Percy Vere marches with Grampa's army and entertains himself by making riddles, puns, and poems. The Play Fellows drag him through games, including running him around the mulberry bush, until he escapes and rolls under a tree, exhausted.

CHAPTER 19

Percy Vere arrives at Peer Haps's palace with the Forgetful Poet, Dorothy, and Grampa after following the trails. He tells Tatters that marrying the Princess fulfills the purpose for which Tatters had been searching for a princess and fortune, and he tries to reassure him.

Forgetful Poet in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Forgetful Poet, a one-legged old soldier and poet who forgot his words, lives in Perhaps City near Maybe Mountain. He follows a flower trail and Tatters's footprints with companions, reaches familiar ground, drops Fumbo's head, visits Ragbad for a feast and quadrille, then returns home.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 19

The Forgetful Poet follows the flower trail and Tatters's footprints to Maybe Mountain with Percy Vere, Dorothy, and Grampa. He is delighted to reach familiar ground and warmly greets Peer Haps, but his excitement causes him to drop Fumbo's head.

CHAPTER 22

The Forgetful Poet is taken to Ragbad with Dorothy and Peer Haps to meet Mrs Sew-and-Sew and the others. He joins the feast and Ragbad Quadrille, then returns home with Dorothy and Peer Haps.
