Chapter 18

The Fiddlebow Boat is a smooth, garnet-colored boat shaped like a huge hollowed-out fiddle. It carries Notta, Bob Up, and Nick down a rapid stream toward the Emerald City, then is passed from tree to tree after the travelers leave it.
