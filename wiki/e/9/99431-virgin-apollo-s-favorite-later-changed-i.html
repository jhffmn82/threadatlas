virgin (Apollo's favorite; later changed into a crow) in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

A royal virgin, daughter of Coroneus and Apollo’s favorite, watches three virgins guarding Ericthonius. She reports Aglauros’s discovery of the basket’s secret to Minerva; her fidelity brings ruin, and she is changed into a crow, then assigned as Minerva’s faultless companion.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The virgin watches the three virgins entrusted with Ericthonius and reports Aglauros's discovery to Minerva. She says that she is Apollo's favorite, that she was the daughter of Coroneus, and that she was a virgin of royal birth before being changed into a crow.

notes .. notes

The crow is the form into which Coronis was transformed for making too faithful a report. Lucian and other writers fabled that Æsculapius was produced from the egg of this bird.
