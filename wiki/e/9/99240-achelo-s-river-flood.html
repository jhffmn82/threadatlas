Abstract written by a model

Acheloüs river inundation, caused by rains and dissolved mountain snows, delays Theseus, roaring as it carries away beams, stones, flocks, people, woods, fields, and Naiads into the sea.

Acheloüs river flood is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

The flood delays Theseus and is described as strong enough to carry away beams, stones, folds, flocks, and people. In Acheloüs's account, a great flood also tears woods and fields from their places and carries the Naiads into the sea.
