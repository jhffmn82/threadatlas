G-Retriever

method · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: G-retriever, G-retriever (He et al., 2024)

Abstract written by a model

In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," G-Retriever retrieves relevant nodes and edges, constructs a relevant subgraph with a Prize-Collecting Steiner Tree method, and aggregates topology as soft prompts; it beats tested text-only retrievers but, without topology during retrieval, trails GRAG on both datasets. In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," G-Retriever aggregates topological information as soft prompts and outperforms comparison retrievers, but omits topology during retrieval; GRAG performs better by directly retrieving subgraphs. In "Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation," G-Retriever retrieves subgraphs from various types of graphs as a prompt-tuning retrieval baseline; it is competitive with GraphToken w/ RAG but is generally outperformed by K-RagRec and requires 5.86 seconds per inference on MovieLens-1M with Llama-2-7b.
