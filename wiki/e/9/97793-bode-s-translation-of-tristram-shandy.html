Abstract written by a model

Bode’s translation of Tristram Shandy was completed in summer 1774. Praised as a masterly achievement for approximating Sterne’s style, it generally preserved the original’s spirit with fidelity. Its subscription list recorded upwards of 650 subscribers. It later served as the basis for Benzler’s revised edition and for another revision issued in 1831.
