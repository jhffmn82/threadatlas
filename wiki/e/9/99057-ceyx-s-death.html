Ceyx's death

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: I am dead, dead, death, fatal waves, funeral rites
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, death surrounds the crew as the storm destroys the ship; Ceyx calls for Halcyone before a wave covers his head and sinks him beneath the sea.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Ceyx's death is revealed by the dream of Ceyx sent to Halcyone; he dies by drowning after the storm destroys his ship and prays that the waves will carry his body to Halcyone.
