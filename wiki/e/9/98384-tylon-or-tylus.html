Tylon or Tylus

Also called: Lydian legendary hero, Tylon, Tylus

Abstract written by a model

Tylon or Tylus is a Lydian legendary hero killed by a serpent beside the Hermus. His sister Moire restores him with the flower of Zeus after seeing it revive a dead serpent; he is associated with Sardes through coin allusions.

Tylon or Tylus is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VII.—THE RESURRECTION OF GLAUCUS

Tylon or Tylus was a Lydian legendary hero who was killed by a serpent while walking beside the Hermus. His sister Moire restored him to life with the herb called the “flower of Zeus,” after seeing the herb revive a dead serpent.
