Uns in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923 · the work’s page · read it where Uns first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Uns are a company of feathered creatures with round eyes, beaklike noses, and inward-turning feet that make them hop. They first reject and fear the travelers after the Cowardly Lion roars. Later, led by I-wish-I-was, they attack at night; the Lion defeats many, Notta sends others to Mudge, and the survivors flee to the woods.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The Featherheads of Un have feathers instead of hair, round eyes, beaklike noses, and inward-turning feet that make them hop. They initially reject the travelers, but the Cowardly Lion's roar makes their feathers stand on end and frightens them.

Chapter 11

The Uns initially do not follow Notta, Bob Up, and the Cowardly Lion to the edge of the Skyland, but later attack them at night under I-wish-I-was's leadership. The Cowardly Lion defeats many, while Notta sends others to Mudge with the magic verse. The surviving Uns flee to the woods in fear.

the Uns in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923 · the work’s page · read it where the Uns first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Uns are inhabitants of Un and the Isle of Un, the only people allowed on Un’s skyle. Feather-haired, they live in rough boxlike tree houses, do not work, and follow a daily pattern: three morning hours of wishing under I-wish-I-was, afternoon fishing mostly for birds, and evening fighting. Merciless toward captives, they take travelers to the palace and join the King’s plan to send them fishing and push them from the skyle. They push Notta and Bob off, then chase the escaping travelers but cannot stop the royal Flyaboutabus from rising.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The Uns live on the skyle of Un and are the only people allowed there. They take the travelers to the palace, describe themselves with unish qualities, and follow the King's plan to send the travelers fishing so they can be pushed off the skyle.

Chapter 12

The Uns push Notta and Bob from the skyle while the Cowardly Lion sleeps, then flee without noticing the travelers' ropes. They spend each morning shouting wishes, each afternoon fishing, and each evening fighting, and they are led by I-wish-I-was. They chase the travelers when they try to take the royal Flyaboutabus, but cannot stop it from rising.
