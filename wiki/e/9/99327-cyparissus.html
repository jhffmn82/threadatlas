Cyparissus

Abstract written by a model

Cyparissus is a beautiful youth from Cea, beloved by Apollo and devoted to a sacred stag. After accidentally killing it with a javelin, he asks to mourn forever and is transformed into a cypress, a funeral symbol.

Cyparissus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE III. [X.106-142]

Cyparissus, a beautiful youth of Cea, loved and cared for a stag sacred to the Nymphs. After accidentally killing it with a javelin, he wished to die and asked to mourn forever; Apollo then transformed him into a cypress.
