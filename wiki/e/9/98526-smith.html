(10) A Russian story, which belongs to the same class,

The smith sets out to seek evil and meets a tailor who joins him. At Likho’s cottage, he tricks the one-eyed woman into being tied up, attacks her eye, escapes disguised as a sheep, and later cuts off his own hand when it becomes stuck to a golden-handled hatchet.
