Abstract written by a model

Scanlan is a small, sharp-faced, nervous black-eyed man, a Lodge 341 Scowrer and fellow member of the Eminent Order of Freemen who lives in Hobson’s Patch. Though secretly horrified by lodge violence, he urges McMurdo to report at once to Boss McGinty. McMurdo’s original train acquaintance, Scanlan moves in at Widow MacNamara’s, lodges with him, leads him home drunk, and is told to put liquor on the lodge table.

Scanlan is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

Mike Scanlan visits McMurdo and urges him to report immediately to Boss McGinty. He warns that McMurdo must not encounter McGinty without doing so and suggests that McMurdo's hostility toward the police will please the Bodymaster.

Chapter III

Scanlan, McMurdo’s original acquaintance aboard the train, moves into Vermissa and lodges with him at the Widow MacNamara’s. He later leads McMurdo home after McMurdo becomes drunk while being celebrated by the bar crowd, and he is instructed to put liquor on the lodge table.
