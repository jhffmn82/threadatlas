Abstract written by a model

The old red castle is a shabby castle on Red Mountain, with the throne room and the King’s best bed chamber. It is home and shelter to King Fumbo, Mrs Sew-and-Sew, and others: it is battered by a storm as Fumbo falls through its doorway, sees Tatters and Grampa depart, and ends with the reunited group’s feast and dancing in its ballroom.

CHAPTER 1

The old red castle stands on a hill and is the home of King Fumbo and Mrs Sew-and-Sew. During the storm, Fumbo falls through its doorway and down ten flights of steps, while the castle is battered by thunder and wind.

CHAPTER 2

The castle shelters King Fumbo, Prince Tatters, Pudge, Grampa, and Mrs Sew-and-Sew. Prince Tatters and Grampa leave it through the palace gates at eight o’clock in the morning.

CHAPTER 22

The old red castle is where the reunited group gathers in its shabby ballroom. Mrs Sew-and-Sew holds a great feast there, followed by dancing that lasts into the night.
