Calisto

Abstract written by a model

Calisto is a virgin of Nonacris, Lycaon’s daughter, and Diana’s most beloved Nymph, a chaste hunter armed with bow and light javelin. Jupiter seduces and assaults her, and her pregnancy is exposed among Diana’s company. Juno transforms her into a bear while she retains understanding. Unrecognized by her son Arcas, she is finally placed among the heavens by Jupiter as the Great Bear.

Calisto is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Calisto is a virgin of Nonacris and a favored follower of Diana, skilled in hunting and devoted to chastity. After Jupiter assaults her, she returns ashamed and later has her pregnancy exposed when Diana’s company bathes.

notes .. notes

Calisto is transformed by Juno from a human into a bear, while retaining her former understanding. She later encounters her son Arcas, who does not recognize her, and is placed among the stars by Jupiter.
