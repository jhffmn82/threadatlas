1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

In some societies twins were exposed or killed because their birth was believed to bring misfortune. In Nias, one or both twins were sometimes left to perish, while in Ngura twins were ordered to be killed and thrown into water to prevent droughts, famines, floods, or other afflictions.
