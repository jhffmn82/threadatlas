boar skin

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: badge of his victory, boar's head and hide, head, present, skin, spoil
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the boar's head and hide was presented by Meleager to Atalanta as the spoil of victory, then taken from her by the sons of Thestius, prompting Meleager's attack on them.
  • In The Library, the boar skin was promised by Oeneus as the prize for killing the beast, given by Meleager to Atalanta, seized by the sons of Thestius, and restored to Atalanta after Meleager killed them.

Ovid · 0008~

After the boar is killed, Meleager presents its head and hide, still thick with stiff bristles and marked by huge tusks, to Atalanta as the spoil of victory. The sons of Thestius take the spoil from her, prompting Meleager’s attack on them.

belongs_to Atalanta [according to Meleager's award] The boar skin was promised by Oeneus as the prize for killing the beast. Meleager gave it to Atalanta, but the sons of Thestius seized it, claiming it belonged to them by right of birth; Meleager killed them and restored the skin to Atalanta.
