boar skin

Also called: badge of his victory, boar's head and hide, head, present, skin, spoil

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the boar's head and hide was presented by Meleager to Atalanta as the spoil of victory, then taken from her by the sons of Thestius, prompting Meleager's attack on them. In The Library, the boar skin was promised by Oeneus as the prize for killing the beast, given by Meleager to Atalanta, seized by the sons of Thestius, and restored to Atalanta after Meleager killed them.
