Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the Horn of Plenty, also called Cornucopia, is a horn Hercules breaks from Acheloüs that Naiads fill with Autumn fruits and flowers, making it a symbol of abundance. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Cornucopia is Achelous’s severed horn, consecrated and flower-filled by Naiades, adopted and named by Plenty, and able to fill with whatever its possessor wished.
