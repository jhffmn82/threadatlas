opening

Andromeda is bound to a hard rock and exposed to the approaching sea monster as punishment for her mother's boast. Her parents can bring her only tears, lamentations, and embraces until Perseus intervenes.

notes .. notes

The exposure of Andromeda is presented as a response to Cassiope’s comparison of her beauty with that of the Nereids. One proposed interpretation is that it reflects Andromeda’s forced betrothal to a fierce pirate prince in exchange for the safety of Cepheus’s realms.
