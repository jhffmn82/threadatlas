Notta's six disguises in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Notta's six disguises are multiple costumes concealed in his apparel and used as padding while tumbling. After a storm leaves them heavy, disfiguring lumps, they dry in the woodcutter's hut; Notta later wears a huge goggle-eyed fish costume.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

Notta's disguises become heavy, disfiguring lumps after being soaked in the storm, so they are hung up to dry in the woodcutter's hut. Notta later puts on a huge fish disguise, expecting the people in the strange city to obey him when they see a fish walking on land.
