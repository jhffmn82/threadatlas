the Naiads

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Naiads, Nymphs, The Naiads, the Nymphs, the daughters of Acheloüs, the fountain Deities

Abstract written by a model

The five Naiads, daughters of Acheloüs, celebrate with sacrifices to local gods but neglect Acheloüs. His rage and flood carry them into the sea, dividing the land around them and transforming them into Echinades. They also consecrate his broken horn with Autumn’s fruits and fragrant flowers.

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

Five Naiads hold a joyful festival after sacrificing twice five bullocks to the local gods, while neglecting Acheloüs. His rage and flood carry them into the sea, where the land is divided around them.

BOOK THE NINTH.

The Naiads, described as the daughters of Acheloüs, take up the horn broken from him. They consecrate it by filling it with fruits and fragrant flowers from Autumn.
