Fable VI in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

A mythological narrative recounts Ligdus, Telethusa, Iphis, Ianthe, and Isis: Iphis's sex is concealed, love for Ianthe prompts Isis's intervention, transformation, and marriage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Footnote 46: _Every God has._--Ver. 425-6. ‘Cui studeat, Deus

Fable VI recounts the story of Ligdus, Telethusa, Iphis, Ianthe, and Isis. Its central events are the concealment of Iphis's sex, Iphis's love for Ianthe, Isis's intervention, and Iphis's transformation before marriage.
