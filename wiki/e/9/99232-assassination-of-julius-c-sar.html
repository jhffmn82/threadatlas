The assassination of Julius Cæsar is foretold by ominous signs, including strange sounds in the heavens, bloodlike rain, weeping statues, unfavorable entrails, ghosts, and earthquakes. The warnings cannot prevent the conspirators from murdering him in the Senate-house.
