Abstract written by a model

Sanchoniatho is a heathen writer said to have lived before the Trojan War and written originally in Phoenician. He claimed Jerombaal as his source on the world’s original construction; his works survive in translation through Philo Judaeus and are considered spurious by many.

Sanchoniatho is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sanchoniatho is said to have lived before the Trojan war and to have written in the Phœnician language. He claimed to have received information about the original construction of the world from a priest of Jehovah named Jerombaal, and the Greeks may have borrowed their ideas about Chaos from him.
