the didapper

animal · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a didapper, didapper, the diver

Abstract written by a model

The didapper is a diving sea bird with feathers, drawn-up legs, and a wide throat; born when Tethys prevents Æsacus’s suicide, it repeatedly plunges headlong into and cuts through the sea.

FABLE VIII. [XI.749-795]

The didapper is a sea bird that cuts through the water with its legs drawn up and has a wide throat. Æsacus becomes one after Tethys prevents his attempted suicide, and its name is linked to its plunging into the sea.
