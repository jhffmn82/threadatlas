Nephele

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Νεφέλης

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Nephele is Athamas’s wife and the mother of Phryxus and Helle, is divorced, and afterward Ino seeks to destroy her children. In The Library, Nephele receives a golden-fleeced ram from Hermes, rescues her children from the altar, and sends them through the sky on it.
