3.2

Attention-Based Mechanisms dynamically modulate interactions between modalities using cross-attention, co-attention, memory retrieval, gated attention, and mutual-guided alignment. They support fine-grained multimodal reasoning but can require task-specific attention heads, aligned supervision, or substantial computation.
