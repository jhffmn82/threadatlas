Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Snippets are selectively augmented when they are informative and filtered out when they are distractors. RAG2 retrieves snippets evenly from four biomedical corpora and uses rationales as queries to improve their utility.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

A clinical document snippet containing information about tension pneumothorax and needle decompression is retrieved for the COPD exacerbation case. Because the snippet is irrelevant, it causes an incorrect recommendation until rationale-guided filtering removes it.

Limitation

Each snippet was evaluated individually when creating perplexity-based labels. The Flan-T5 model can filter only one snippet at a time.
