Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Methods

Essential attributes are identified from few-shot examples within the dataset. They define which aspects of each sample should guide key-information extraction and synthetic-data generation.

Ablation Studies

Attributes carry information used in synthetic data generation. Using too few attributes misses essential and private information, while increasing them can improve utility but may increase privacy exposure.
