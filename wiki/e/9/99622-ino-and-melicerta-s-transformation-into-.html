A divine transformation in which, after Ino and Melicerta fall from a rock into the sea, Venus intercedes and Neptune removes their mortality, changes their forms and names to Leucothoë and Palæmon, and makes them sea deities, consoling their relatives after their deaths.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Ino and Melicerta's transformation into sea deities follows their fall from the rock into the sea. At Venus's intercession, Neptune removes their mortality, changes their forms and names, and makes Ino Leucothoë and Melicerta Palæmon.

notes .. notes

The transformation of Ino and Melicerta was said to have changed them into sea deities named Leucothoë and Palæmon. The explanation presents this story as a way of consoling their relatives after Ino and Melicerta died.
