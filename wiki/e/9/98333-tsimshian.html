Tsimshian

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Tsimshian Indians, the Tsimshian

Abstract written by a model

The Tsimshian are an adjacent coastal tribe in British Columbia who believe twins’ wishes are fulfilled; twins can call salmon and olachen, are called Sewthan, “making plentiful,” and are feared for harming enemies.

Tsimshian is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
