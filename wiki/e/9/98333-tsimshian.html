Tsimshian in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

The Tsimshian are an adjacent coastal tribe in British Columbia who believe twins’ wishes are fulfilled; twins can call salmon and olachen, are called Sewthan, “making plentiful,” and are feared for harming enemies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

The Tsimshian believed that twins' wishes were fulfilled and that twins could call salmon and olachen, earning them the name Sewthan, meaning "making plentiful."
