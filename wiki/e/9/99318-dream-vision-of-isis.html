dream vision of Isis

Also called: under the form of a vision

Abstract written by a model

A divine revelation received by Telethusa at night: Isis appears with companions and symbols, tells her to defy Ligdus’s command, raise the child regardless of sex, and trust Isis’s protection.

dream vision of Isis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The dream vision shows Isis before Telethusa's bed accompanied by Anubis, Bubastis, Apis, Harpocrates, Osiris, a foreign serpent, sistra, and other symbols. Isis tells Telethusa to disregard Ligdus's command, raise the child, and trust in her protection.
