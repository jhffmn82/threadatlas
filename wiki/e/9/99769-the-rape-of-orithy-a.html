Abstract written by a model

The rape of Orithyïa is an event in which Boreas, after prayers and blandishments fail, forcibly abducts Orithyïa, carries her in his tawny wings through the air to the Ciconians; Plato allegorized it as her wind-driven drowning.

opening

After his prayers and blandishments fail, Boreas abducts Orithyïa by force. He wraps her in his tawny wings and carries her through the air to the people and walls of the Ciconians.

notes .. notes

Plato interpreted the story allegorically, as meaning that Orithyïa was accidentally blown by the wind into the sea and drowned. Apollodorus and Pausanias treated it as based on historical events involving Boreas's seizure of Orithyïa.
