relevance scaling factors

object · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a scaling factor, relevance scaling factors (α), scalar, scaling factor, scaling factors, α, αu, αuv
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, relevance scaling factors adaptively mask node and edge subgraphs, scale features and attributes during Graph Neural Network message passing, and are learned from query relevance to control the graph encoder.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, relevance scaling factors are learned by two Multilayer Perceptrons from distances between nodes, edges, and a query, controlling graph-encoder message passing by scaling node features and edge attributes and adaptively masking tokens.
