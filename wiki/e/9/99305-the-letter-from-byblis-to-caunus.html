Abstract written by a model

A wax-tablet confession in which Byblis hesitantly writes, revises, and sends her love to Caunus, who reads it, angrily rejects it, and threatens the servant.

FABLE V. [IX.426-665]

Byblis writes a hesitant confession of her love on wax tablets, repeatedly composing, correcting, and erasing her words before sending them through a servant. Caunus reads part of the letter, rejects it angrily, and threatens the servant who delivered it.
