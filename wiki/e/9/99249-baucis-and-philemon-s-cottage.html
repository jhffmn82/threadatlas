Baucis and Philemon's cottage

place · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: cottage of Philemon and Baucis, house, humble door, humble mansion, little abode, old cottage, old house, small one, small thatched cottage
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the small, thatched dwelling where Philemon and Baucis live in poverty, and it is transformed into a temple after the gods spare it from the village's destruction.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it shelters weary Jupiter and Mercury, survives the village flood, and becomes a columned, marble-floored, gold-adorned temple.

Baucis and Philemon's cottage in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

A humble thatched home of Baucis and Philemon shelters weary Jupiter and Mercury after others refuse them; when the village floods, it alone remains and becomes a columned, marble-floored, gold-adorned temple.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

Baucis and Philemon's small thatched cottage sheltered Jupiter and Mercury when other houses refused them. After the village was flooded, the cottage alone remained and was transformed into a temple with columns, marble floors, and gold ornaments.
