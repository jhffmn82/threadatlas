Baucis and Philemon's cottage

place · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: cottage of Philemon and Baucis, house, humble door, humble mansion, little abode, old cottage, old house, small one, small thatched cottage

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the small, thatched dwelling where Philemon and Baucis live in poverty, and it is transformed into a temple after the gods spare it from the village's destruction. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it shelters weary Jupiter and Mercury, survives the village flood, and becomes a columned, marble-floored, gold-adorned temple.

A humble thatched home of Baucis and Philemon shelters weary Jupiter and Mercury after others refuse them; when the village floods, it alone remains and becomes a columned, marble-floored, gold-adorned temple.
