Abstract written by a model

Æsculapius cult enters Rome after a serpent reaches the Island of the Tiber, becoming a refuge for disease, especially pestilence.

FABLE VII. [XV.622-744]

The worship of Æsculapius is introduced into Rome after the serpent reaches the Island of the Tiber. The Romans thereafter have recourse to him in cases of disease and especially during pestilence.
