LLM-based recommender systems in Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation

Shijie Wang, Wenqi Fan, Yue Feng, Shanru Lin, Xinyu Ma, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin · 2025

LLM-based recommender systems are a new recommendation paradigm that uses Large Language Models’ language understanding and generalization to capture nuanced user preferences and generate recommendations. Their central challenge is that they can hallucinate or produce fictional recommendations and lack up-to-date, domain-specific knowledge, partly because recommendation-oriented training corpora are limited. Knowledge-graph retrieval-augmented generation, including K-RagRec, is proposed and studied to address these limitations through knowledge-graph retrieval.

LLM-based recommender systems in Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation

Shijie Wang, Wenqi Fan, Yue Feng, Shanru Lin, Xinyu Ma, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin · 2025

LLM-based recommender systems model user preferences from historical interactions and item side information, then use a frozen large language model to select preferred items from candidate sets. Their central challenge is intelligent selective retrieval; popularity-based retrieval improves efficiency, while future work targets policies that update as interests change.
