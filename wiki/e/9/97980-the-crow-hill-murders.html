Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The Crow Hill murders occur at the Crow Hill mine: Andrews and another assassin shoot manager Josiah H. Dunn, kill engineer Menzies after he attacks them, and escape unidentified through the mist.

Chapter V

The Crow Hill murders occur when Andrews shoots manager Josiah H. Dunn and another assassin shoots him again as he tries to escape. Engineer Menzies attacks the killers with an iron spanner and is shot twice in the face; the gang then escapes through the mist without being identified.
