In “GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” common-sense reasoning is evaluated on ExplaGraphs using accuracy, with GRAG reaching 0.9223 compared with 0.3394 for the LLM-only baseline and 0.8927 for the LoRA fine-tuned LLM. In “GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” common-sense reasoning is identified as a task on ExplaGraphs and is substantially improved by GRAG, including when its graph encoding capabilities are transferred from WebQSP.
