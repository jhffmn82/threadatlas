Riedel in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Riedel is a writer and discerning reader of Tristram Shandy. Before Sentimental Journey appeared, he closely appreciated the novel, quoted and translated its Dr. Slop and Obadiah passage, used it to discuss humor and human psychology, wrote Theorie der schönen Künste und Wissenschaften and Ueber das Publikum, and reportedly planned to continue Yorick’s Journey.

Riedel showed a detailed and discriminating appreciation of Tristram Shandy before the publication of the Sentimental Journey. In his writings he quoted and translated a passage about Dr. Slop and Obadiah, referred to Shandy in discussions of humor, and described the novel as a contribution to the understanding of the human heart and psychology.
