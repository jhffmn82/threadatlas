In The Hungry Tiger of Oz, the sky contains Cloud Country and the Earth as its bottom, is Atmos Fere's home, and is inhabited by airmen who drift, float, swim, or fly without tiring.

The sky is Atmos's home, where there are no deserts or storms like those on the Earth. Atmos describes its inhabitants as able to drift, float, swim, or fly without becoming tired.
