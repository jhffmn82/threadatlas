the sky in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The sky contains Cloud Country and the Earth as its bottom. It is Atmos Fere's home, free of deserts and storms, inhabited by airmen who drift, float, swim, or fly without tiring. Atmos moves through its changing day and night and plans to bring Ozma there for a lecture and banquet.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

The sky is inhabited by airmen such as Atmos Fere, who swim through the air and move rapidly through changing day and night. Atmos intends to take Ozma there and keep her for a lecture and banquet.

CHAPTER 15

The sky is Atmos's home, where there are no deserts or storms like those on the Earth. Atmos describes its inhabitants as able to drift, float, swim, or fly without becoming tired.
