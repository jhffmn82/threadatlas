Tatius

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Tatius, a Sabine king, wages war against Romulus with the Sabine fathers; after losses bring peace, he shares sovereignty with Romulus until his death.

Tatius is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Tatius and the Sabine fathers wage war against Romulus and the Romans. After the fighting causes Roman and Sabine losses, Tatius shares sovereignty with Romulus until his death.
