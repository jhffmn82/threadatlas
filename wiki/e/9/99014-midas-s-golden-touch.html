Midas's golden touch

power · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Midas's golden-touch power, gift, gold-creating power, his misfortune, newly acquired power, power, the hurtful favour, the power, this power, this specious calamity, whatever he might touch should be changed into GOLD
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Midas's golden-touch power is a harmful favour granted by Bacchus that makes whatever Midas touches turn to gold, makes food and drink inedible, and is annulled by Bacchus after Midas repents.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Midas's golden touch changes whatever he touches into gold, makes bread inedible, turns wine in his throat into something like melted gold, and is removed by Bacchus when Midas begs for help.

Midas's golden-touch power in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

A harmful favour granted by Bacchus lets whatever Midas touches turn to gold, but makes food and drink inedible until Bacchus annuls it after repentance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE II. [XI.85-145]

Bacchus grants Midas's request that everything he touches with his body turn into gold. The power proves harmful because it makes food and drink inedible, and Bacchus later annuls it when Midas repents.
