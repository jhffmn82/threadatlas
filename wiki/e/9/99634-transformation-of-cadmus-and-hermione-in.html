Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Cadmus's body lengthens, develops scales and azure spots, and loses its human form as he becomes a serpent. Hermione then undergoes a similar transformation after asking the Gods for it, and the two enter a nearby grove together.

notes

The transformation was presented as a story invented after Cadmus's death. It may have arisen from the Phoenician word Chiva, possibly meaning serpent, and from the Greeks' association of the Illyrians with serpents or basilisks.
