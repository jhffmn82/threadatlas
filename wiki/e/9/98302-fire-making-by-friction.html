Fire-making by friction is a method of producing fire with wood by boring or rubbing. Fire-making is learned in several stories through observation or instruction, especially by rubbing two sticks, wood, a bamboo rope, or a bowstring together. In other accounts, people learn the process by watching gods, spirits, birds, or insects make fire.
