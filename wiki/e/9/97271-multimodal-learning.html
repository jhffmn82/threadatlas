Introduction & Background

Multimodal learning enables systems to integrate and reason over heterogeneous data sources for more comprehensive representations. CLIP aligned visual and textual modalities through contrastive learning, inspiring subsequent models and supporting applications including sentiment analysis and biomedical tasks. The field facilitates joint processing of text, images, audio, and video and is presented as a critical enabler of progress toward artificial general intelligence.
