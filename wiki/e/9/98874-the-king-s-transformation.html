the King's transformation in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

The King's transformation is an enchantment caused by Mombi, likely using green magic, in a little wood near the Emerald City. Mombi turns the King of Oz into Humpy, a stuffed man, and changes Pajuka into a goose, then forgets the magic and hiding place. Pajuka's memories help identify the cause and location. The travelers take Mombi to the green country, where the enchantment can be broken and the King restored.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

Mombi cannot remember what kind of magic she used or where she hid the King after transforming him. Pajuka's recollection that the transformation occurred near the Emerald City leads Mombi to identify green magic as the likely cause.

CHAPTER 5

Pajuka says that Mombi transformed the King of Oz in a little wood. The travelers hope to find the King and undo or remedy the transformation, but the King has not yet been found.

CHAPTER 16

Mombi is revealed as the one who transformed the King into Humpy, a stuffed man, and changed Pajuka into a goose. The enchantment was made near the Emerald City and can only be broken in the green country, so the group takes Mombi there to restore the King.
