Eurypylus's death

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: death, death of Eurypylus, was slain, ἀπέκτεινεν

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, the death of Eurypylus is described as his being killed by Neoptolemus after arriving with a large Mysian force to aid the Trojans. In The Fall of Troy, Eurypylus's death comes when Neoptolemus's Pelian lance pierces his throat.
