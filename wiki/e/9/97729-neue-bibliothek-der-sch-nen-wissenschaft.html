Abstract written by a model

Neue Bibliothek der schönen Wissenschaften, a periodical, first mentioned Laurence Sterne in its last 1766 number in an article on humor. In 1767 it announced the ninth volume of Tristram Shandy as exceptionally strange wit and humor, while treating Sterne’s German importance condescendingly.

The Neue Bibliothek der schönen Wissenschaften first mentioned Sterne in an article on humor in its last number for 1766. In 1767 it announced the ninth volume of Tristram Shandy as an exceptionally strange production of wit and humor, while adopting a somewhat condescending tone toward Sterne’s importance in Germany.
