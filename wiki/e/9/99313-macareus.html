Abstract written by a model

Macareus, a Lapith companion of Ulysses from Neritos, meets and recognizes Achæmenides at Caieta, recounts Ulysses’ perils, and warns Æneas to avoid Circe’s shores.

Macareus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE IV. [XIV.154-247]

Macareus, a companion of Ulysses, meets Achæmenides at Caieta and asks how he survived and where the Trojan ship is going. He recounts Ulysses’ encounter with Æolus, the release of the winds, the attack of the Læstrygons, and the danger posed by Circe.
