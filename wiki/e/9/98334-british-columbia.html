British Columbia in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

British Columbia is a region associated with Bella Coola and Kwakiutl Indians and the interior home of Thompson Indians. It is also the setting for fire myths of Lillooet, Okanaken, and Chilcotin Indians and Sun-story versions told by several groups.
