  • In Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation: RAG2, or RAtionale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, is a novel proposed framework and retrieval-augmented generation method for improving reliability and performance in biomedical question answering. It begins by using LLM-generated rationales as queries. It then conducts balanced retrieval, retrieving snippets evenly from a comprehensive set of four biomedical corpora to mitigate retriever bias, and includes reranking. A small rationale-filtering model performs rationale-guided filtering, refining and optimizing the overall RAG pipeline. Across three medical question-answering benchmarks, RAG2 improves state-of-the-art large language models of varying sizes: average accuracy improves by 6.1% on Llama-3-8B-Instruct, 3.8% on Meerkat-7B, and 0.9% on GPT-4o. It reports highest average scores of 66.3%, 72.4%, and 86.9% for those backbones, respectively, and MedQA accuracies of 64.6, 75.6, and 91.1. It consistently outperforms MedRAG across evaluated top-k values, including by 5.6% with Llama-3-8B-Instruct, supporting more reliable AI-assisted medical decision-making.

RAG2 in Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Jiwoong Sohn · 2025

RAG2, or RAtionale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, is a novel proposed framework and retrieval-augmented generation method for improving reliability and performance in biomedical question answering. It begins by using LLM-generated rationales as queries. It then conducts balanced retrieval, retrieving snippets evenly from a comprehensive set of four biomedical corpora to mitigate retriever bias, and includes reranking. A small rationale-filtering model performs rationale-guided filtering, refining and optimizing the overall RAG pipeline. Across three medical question-answering benchmarks, RAG2 improves state-of-the-art large language models of varying sizes: average accuracy improves by 6.1% on Llama-3-8B-Instruct, 3.8% on Meerkat-7B, and 0.9% on GPT-4o. It reports highest average scores of 66.3%, 72.4%, and 86.9% for those backbones, respectively, and MedQA accuracies of 64.6, 75.6, and 91.1. It consistently outperforms MedRAG across evaluated top-k values, including by 5.6% with Llama-3-8B-Instruct, supporting more reliable AI-assisted medical decision-making.

Abstract

RAG2 is a framework that enhances the reliability of retrieval-augmented generation in biomedical contexts. It uses a small rationale-filtering model, LLM-generated rationale queries, and balanced retrieval from four biomedical corpora, improving state-of-the-art large language models by up to 6.1%.

Introduction

RAG2, or RAtionale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, is a framework for improving the reliability of biomedical retrieval-augmented generation. It combines rationale-based query formulation, balanced retrieval, reranking, and rationale-guided filtering, and improves performance on three medical question-answering benchmarks.
