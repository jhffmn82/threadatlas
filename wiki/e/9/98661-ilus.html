Narrative, section by section

XII. Ἠλέκτρας δὲ τῆς "Ἄτλαντος καὶ Διὸς

Ilus was a son of Dardanus and became the founder of Ilium. He went to Phrygia, won a wrestling contest, followed a dappled cow as instructed, founded Ilium where it lay down, and received the Palladium after praying to Zeus.
