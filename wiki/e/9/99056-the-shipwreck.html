the shipwreck in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

The shipwreck follows a storm that tears open the ship’s seams, breaks its equipment, and sends it down. Much of the crew drowns; others grasp wreckage. Ceyx clings to debris but is overwhelmed, dies, and later washes ashore, confirming Morpheus’s vision to Halcyone.

FABLE VII. [XI.410-748]

The shipwreck occurs after the storm tears open the ship’s seams, breaks its equipment, and sends it to the bottom. Many of the crew drown, while others seize broken pieces of the vessel; Ceyx clings to wreckage before being overwhelmed.

The shipwreck kills Ceyx and leaves his body drifting in the sea. Halcyone first learns of it through Morpheus’s vision and later confirms it when his corpse reaches the shore.
