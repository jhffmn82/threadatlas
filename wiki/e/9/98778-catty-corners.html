Catty Corners in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Catty Corners is a cat kingdom city for its cats, excluding boys, enclosed by enormous gray triangular walls with gates. Inside are fences, enclosures, catnip beds, pussy willow trees, cream fountains, fish ponds, and the Queen's garden. Its cats capture Snip and Pajuka and welcome Mombi, who later leaves with prisoners through iron doors into another forest.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Catty Corners is a city surrounded by enormous gray walls shaped into a huge triangle. It contains a network of fences, little enclosures, catnip beds, pussy willow trees, fountains of cream, ponds of fish, and the Queen's garden. Its cats capture Snip and Pajuka while welcoming Mombi.

CHAPTER 7

Catty Corners contains the Queen's garden, the guarded summer house, cream fountains, and a kitchen with a stone fireplace and iron crane. Mombi and the two prisoners leave it through great iron doors and enter another forest.
