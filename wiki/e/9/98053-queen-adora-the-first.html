Queen Adora the First in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Queen Adora the First is a large, handsome, haughty queen who wears eyeglasses and a crown of gold door keys, rules from a throne, and has recurring Queen's fury. Curious about the clown, she first angrily accuses Notta and orders punishment, then delays removing Notta and Bob Up to learn about them, rejects Notta's amusements, and leaves as they escape.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Queen Adora the First rules from a throne wearing a crown made of gold door keys. She demands titles, becomes convinced that Notta stole the door jam, orders him to put out his tongue, and commands Slammer to carry out the punishment when he cannot do so in his bear disguise.

Chapter 7

Queen Adora decides to postpone having Notta and Bob Up removed so she can learn about them. She demands that Notta amuse her and repeatedly refuses to regard his jokes and performances as funny. She leaves the throne room before Notta and Bob escape.
