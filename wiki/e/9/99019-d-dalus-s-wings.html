Abstract written by a model

Dædalus's wings are feathered, thread- and wax-bound imitations of bird wings, fitted to Dædalus and Icarus and used to fly from Crete.

FABLE III. [VIII.183-259]

Dædalus makes wings by arranging feathers from shortest to longest, binding them with thread and wax, and bending them to imitate bird wings. He fits them to his and Icarus's shoulders, and they use them to fly from Crete.
