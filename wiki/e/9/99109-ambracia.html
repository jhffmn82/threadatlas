FABLES V. AND VI. [XIII.623-718]

Ambracia is a city in Epirus that Æneas and his companions behold during their voyage. It was the subject of a dispute among Apollo, Diana, and Hercules, and its judge was transformed into a stone after deciding in favor of Hercules.
