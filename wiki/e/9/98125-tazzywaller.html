Abstract written by a model

Tazzywaller is a round, fat chief chamberlain of Mustafa in Mudge, once valued for good advice. When Mustafa threatens to replace him with Panapee and assign him to feed lions, Tazzywaller says Mudge has none left because he accidentally cut the last old lion in half while defending himself. Jealous when another lion is captured, he faces Mustafa's threat of feeding ten thousand lions. He stops Mudgers attacking Notta with a gong and proposes sending Notta to bring back the Cowardly Lion. He ultimately loses his chamberlain position and feeds Mudge's lions.

Tazzywaller is in one work on this site, The Cowardly Lion of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Tazzywaller is Mustafa's chief chamberlain until Mustafa threatens to replace him with Panapee and make him feed the lions. He explains that there are no more lions in Mudge and reveals that he accidentally cut the last old lion in half while defending himself.

Chapter 3

Tazzywaller is jealous that another lion has been captured after he said there were no more. Mustafa tells him that he will have ten thousand lions to feed if the others do not kill Notta.

Chapter 4

Tazzywaller uses a gong to stop the Mudgers from attacking Notta and proposes that Notta be compelled to serve Mustafa by bringing back the Cowardly Lion. He later remains with Mustafa as Notta and Bob prepare to leave.
