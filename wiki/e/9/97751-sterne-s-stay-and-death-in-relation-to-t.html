Sterne died on March 18, 1768, only a few months before Sturz’s stay in England, which lasted from May 6, 1768, to January 14, 1769. Because England was beginning to reassess Sterne after his death, Sturz encountered an early reaction against the reputation and obscenities that had accompanied the Sterne cult.
