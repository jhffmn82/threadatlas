Abstract written by a model

The Tubbies are a huge, fat, armed army and wild wash women tribe living on Monday Mountain. They attack and capture Dorothy and Percy Vere over their shabby clothes, carry them to the summit, and later, after Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere escape, overturn their surrounding wash tubs to send hot soapy water down the mountain.

CHAPTER 14

The wild wash women attack Dorothy and Percy Vere with soap, blueing, clothes props, wash boards, tubs, and baskets. They complain about the travelers’ shabby clothes and threaten to wash their faces, wring their necks, and hang them up to dry. They capture Dorothy and Percy and carry them to the top of Monday Mountain.

CHAPTER 16

The Tubbies are a wild wash women tribe whose wash tubs surround the top of Monday Mountain. After Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere escape, the Tubbies overturn the tubs and send hot soapy water cascading down the mountain.
