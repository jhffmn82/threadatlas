Mittelstedt

Also called: Pastor Mittelstedt, the anonymous translator

Abstract written by a model

Mittelstedt, a pastor and initially anonymous translator, produced a competing German rendering of A Sentimental Journey in Braunschweig. Published in editions dated 1769, following the work’s 1768 London publication, the translation was among its early German versions and became involved in a brief controversy with Bode.

Pastor Mittelstedt, an anonymous translator initially, produced a competing German rendering of the Sentimental Journey in Braunschweig in 1768. His translation appeared in a first edition dated 1769 and a second edition in the same year, and it became involved in a brief controversy with Bode.
