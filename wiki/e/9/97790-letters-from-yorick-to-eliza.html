“Letters from Yorick to Eliza” in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Letters from Yorick to Eliza is a brief, anonymously published collection of letters, issued early in 1775 and presented as faithfully copied with Eliza’s permission. The text did not question its authenticity. Its English edition was reviewed in German journals and translated in Hamburg in 1775; reviewers emphasized its tenderness, moral character, and authenticity, and particularly praised Bode’s translation.
