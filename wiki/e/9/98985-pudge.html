Pudge in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Pudge is an old, fat Wise Man of Ragbad living in an old red castle, fond of his tower room and coffee. Thoughtful before speaking, he advises Prince Tatters to seek his father’s head and fortune, beginning an investigation the next morning.

CHAPTER 2

Pudge, the Wise Man of Ragbad, advises Prince Tatters to search for his father’s head and his fortune. He declares that the time has come to investigate what the King saved for a rainy day and sets the departure for the next morning.
