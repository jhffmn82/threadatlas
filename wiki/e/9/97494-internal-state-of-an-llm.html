Introduction

Internal states of large language models are used to discern their uncertainty about generated content. SEAKR extracts self-aware uncertainty from the feed-forward network of each layer corresponding to the last generated token.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

The internal state of an LLM is reflected in its hidden representations and can provide information for detecting hallucinated or non-factual generations. The unit presents internal-state analysis as more substantial than output-level detection because decoding continuous internal states into discrete tokens inevitably causes information loss.
