internal state of an LLM

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Internal State, internal state level, internal states, internal states of LLMs, internal states-level

Abstract written by a model

The internal state of an LLM, reflected in hidden representations, can reveal uncertainty and detect hallucinated or non-factual generation. SEAKR extracts self-aware uncertainty from feed-forward-network states at each layer for the last generated token, offering a more substantial basis for adaptive retrieval than output-level detection, which loses information during decoding.

Introduction

Internal states of large language models are used to discern their uncertainty about generated content. SEAKR extracts self-aware uncertainty from the feed-forward network of each layer corresponding to the last generated token.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

The internal state of an LLM is reflected in its hidden representations and can provide information for detecting hallucinated or non-factual generations. The unit presents internal-state analysis as more substantial than output-level detection because decoding continuous internal states into discrete tokens inevitably causes information loss.
