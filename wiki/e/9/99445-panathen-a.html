Also called: festival of the Panathenæa

Abstract written by a model

Panathenæa was a festival instituted at Athens by Ericthonius, celebrated there with games won by Androgeus, and in time adopted by the whole of Greece.

Panathenæa is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
