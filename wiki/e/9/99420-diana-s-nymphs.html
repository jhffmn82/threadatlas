Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Nymphs notice Calisto’s injured honor, perceive her fault, and witness Diana expose her pregnancy while bathing. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Diana's Nymphs assist Diana with her equipment, clothing, hair, and water, then shriek and surround her to cover her bodies when Actæon arrives. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the nymphs of Diana's train bathe with Diana and attempt to hide her from Actaeon; Crocale arranges Diana's hair, while Nephele, Hyale, and the others draw water in urns.

Diana's Nymphs assist her while she bathes: they carry her hunting equipment, remove her sandals and mantle, arrange her hair, and fetch and pour water. When Actæon enters, they shriek, strike their breasts, and surround Diana to cover her bodies.
