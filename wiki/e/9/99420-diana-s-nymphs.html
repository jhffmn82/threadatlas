Diana's Nymphs in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Diana's Nymphs are bathing attendants who assist Diana with her equipment, clothing, hair, and water, then shriek and surround her to cover her bodies when Actæon arrives.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Diana's Nymphs assist her while she bathes: they carry her hunting equipment, remove her sandals and mantle, arrange her hair, and fetch and pour water. When Actæon enters, they shriek, strike their breasts, and surround Diana to cover her bodies.
