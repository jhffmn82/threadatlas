Wren in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Wren, the smallest bird in the Shuswap version, seems too small and weak, yet shoots arrows into the sky; successive arrows form a chain for Earth people to ascend.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

Wren is considered too small and weak to succeed, but he shoots an arrow that reaches and sticks in the sky. In several versions, his successive arrows form the chain by which the Earth people ascend.
