Stage-1 in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Stage-1 is attribute-based synthetic-data generation. It uses an information extractor to identify important attributes and related key information from original data, then a data generator produces synthetic data conditioned on that information for retrieval. Its outputs retain most essential information, including reported HealthcareMagic BLEU-1 of 0.114 and ROUGE-L of 0.0956. Privacy risks can rise as more documents are retrieved, especially without agent-based refinement.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Methods

Stage-1 generates data containing essential information from the original data. It identifies important attributes, extracts related key information, and generates synthetic data conditioned on that information.

Experiment

Attributes-based generation produces the outputs reported as Stage-1. On the HealthcareMagic dataset, Stage-1 obtains BLEU-1 of 0.114 and ROUGE-L of 0.0956.

Utility of using synthetic data

Stage-1 is a synthetic-data generation stage whose outputs retain most of the essential key information according to the text. It achieves strong utility results and has low attack counts in the reported tables.

Ablation Studies

Stage 1 uses an information extractor and data generator to produce data for retrieval. Its privacy risks can increase when more documents are retrieved, especially without agent-based refinement.
