FABLE VIII. [XI.749-795]

Æsacus is transformed into a feathered didapper after Tethys receives him in the sea. His new form has wings, a long neck, a distant head, and elongated spaces between the joints of his legs; he continues diving because the transformation does not satisfy his desire for death.
