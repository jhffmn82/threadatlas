Nauplius in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Nauplius, son of Clymene and father of Palamedes, seeks revenge after Palamedes is killed: denied satisfaction by the Greeks, he arranges adultery among their wives and lights a Mount Caphareus beacon to wreck returning Greeks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ἔμελλον ἀποπλεῖν πορθήσαντες Τροίαν, ὑ ὑπὸ Κάλ-

Nauplius was the son of Clymene and the father of Palamedes. After Palamedes was killed, Nauplius unsuccessfully demanded satisfaction from the Greeks, arranged for Greek wives to be unfaithful, and later lit a beacon fire on Mount Caphareus to lure returning Greeks to destruction.
