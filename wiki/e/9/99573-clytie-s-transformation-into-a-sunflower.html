Clytie's transformation into a sunflower is a story element, not an ascertained historical fact. After the Sun ends intercourse with her, she pines without food or water, survives on dew and tears, becomes rooted and pale, and changes into a flower that continually turns toward the Sun.

Narrative, section by section

opening

After the Sun ends his intercourse with Clytie, she pines away without food or water, surviving on dew and her tears. Her limbs become rooted, her color fades like that of a bloodless plant, and she becomes a flower that continually turns toward the Sun.

notes .. notes

Clytie's metamorphosis is described as her transformation into a sunflower after her jealousy of Leucothoë. The event may reflect a supposed natural relationship in which the sunflower kills the frankincense tree.
