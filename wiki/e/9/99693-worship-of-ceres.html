worship of Ceres in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The worship of Ceres was highly esteemed at Henna, whose inhabitants were regarded as her priests. Probably imported from Egypt as a mysterious worship, it later became connected with agriculture, the Earth, and civil order, while Triptolemus carried it to different countries.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

The worship of Ceres was especially prominent at Henna, where the inhabitants were regarded as her priests. It was later connected with agriculture, the Earth, civil order, and the worship brought from Egypt to Greece.

notes .. notes

Triptolemus carried the worship of Ceres to different countries during his voyages. The unit identifies this spread of worship as a likely subject of the story about Triptolemus being nursed and purified by Ceres.
