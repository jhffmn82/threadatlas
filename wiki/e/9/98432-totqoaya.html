Abstract written by a model

Totqoaya, the Sun’s son in the Bella Coola story, is mocked by other children, ascends to heaven, takes the Sun’s torches and burns the earth, then is thrown down by the Sun and transformed into a mink.

XI.—PHAETHON AND THE CHARIOT OF THE SUN

Totqoaya is the son of the Sun in the Bella Coola story. After other children mock his claim that the Sun is his father, he ascends to heaven, takes the Sun’s torches, overheats the earth, and is thrown down and changed into a mink.
