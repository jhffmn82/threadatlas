Adonis's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: his death, the death, ἀπέθανε

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Adonis's death is caused by the boar after Adonis is struck while hunting. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Adonis's death is memorialized by Adonia annually.
