Abstract written by a model

The swallow is Progne’s form after fleeing Tereus, frequenting human dwellings and symbolizing her restless, futile search for the son she murdered.

The swallow frequents human dwellings, symbolizing Progne’s restlessness as she seeks in vain for the son whom she has murdered. In the alternative account of Anacreon and Apollodorus, Philomela is transformed into the swallow instead.
