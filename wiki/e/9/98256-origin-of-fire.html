Origin of fire is an appendix entry accounted for by Mason-Wasp in the Ba-ila tale and including fire theft as a recurring mythic pattern. It begins with the Ba-ila link between domestic fire and Mason-Wasp construction near fireplaces, includes the Basongo Meno account of fire produced by boring a Raphia-palm rib, and ranges across accounts of humans obtaining fire from gods, animals, birds, or underground guardians.

The unit records many accounts of the origin of fire, including stories in which humans steal it from gods, birds, animals, or guarded underground places.
