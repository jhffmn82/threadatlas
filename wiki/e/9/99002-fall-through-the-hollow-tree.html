fall through the hollow tree in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

A fall through a bottomless hollow tree is experienced by Prince Tatters, Grampa, and Bill. While Tatters holds Bill and a red umbrella, they drop amid falling objects for about a mile and twenty minutes, ending with Tatters in pink bushes and Grampa injured on a cinder path.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

When Grampa, Prince Tatters, and Bill hide in the hollow tree, they discover that it has no bottom. They drop down through it, with Tatters holding Bill and the red umbrella as everything falls around him.

CHAPTER 5

The fall down the hollow tree lasts about a mile and twenty minutes before Prince Tatters, Grampa, and Bill reach the bottom. Prince Tatters lands in pink bushes, while Grampa lands hard on a cinder path and needs the wizard's patent medicine.
