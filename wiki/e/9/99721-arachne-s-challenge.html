Abstract written by a model

is_based_on competition [over the merit of inventing textile arts] Arachne’s challenge is explained as a competition over the merit of inventing or mastering textile arts, especially the working of silk and wool. The contest allows Ovid to contrast Arachne’s human skill with Minerva’s divine status.
