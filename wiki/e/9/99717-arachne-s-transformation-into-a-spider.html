Abstract written by a model

Arachne’s transformation into a spider is a divine punishment inflicted by Minerva after Arachne hangs herself. Minerva keeps her alive, decrees that she and her descendants will hang, and sprinkles her with a noxious herb. Arachne’s features vanish, her body shrinks, her fingers become legs, and she produces thread and weaves as a spider.

BOOK THE SIXTH.

After Arachne hangs herself, Minerva supports her and decrees that she and her descendants shall continue to hang. Minerva sprinkles her with a noxious Hecatean herb, causing her hair, nose, and ears to disappear, her body to become small, and her fingers to become legs. Arachne’s belly takes over the rest of her body, and she begins producing thread and weaving as a spider.

notes .. notes

Arachne’s alleged transformation is linked to the account that she hanged herself in despair after the contest. The explanation suggests that the story may have arisen from the similarity between her name and the Greek word for a spider, or possibly from the Hebrew word “arag,” meaning to spin.
