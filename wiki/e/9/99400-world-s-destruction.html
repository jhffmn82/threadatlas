Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE SECOND.

The world suffers widespread destruction as nations, cities, forests, mountains, rivers, and seas are scorched or dried up. The heavens and Earth are endangered, the sea contracts, Tartarus is exposed through cracks in the ground, and the threat of universal chaos prompts Earth’s plea for rescue.
