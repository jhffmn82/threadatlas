Illyrian war

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: attacked by the Illyrians, their war, war between the Enchelians and Illyrians, πολεμουμένοις

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the war is fought by the Enchelians against the Illyrians, whom Cadmus helps defeat; they give Cadmus the crown to obtain peace. In The Library, the Illyrian war is fought when the Illyrians attack the Encheleans, who defeat them under Cadmus and Harmonia.
