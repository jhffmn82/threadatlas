Abstract written by a model

A clear, silver-like spring with unsullied water remains untouched by shepherds, animals, birds, and falling branches, shaded by grass and wood, until it reflects Narcissus's form.

opening

The clear spring has unsullied, silver-like water untouched by shepherds, animals, birds, or falling branches. Grass and a surrounding wood shade it from the sun, and its water reflects Narcissus's form.
