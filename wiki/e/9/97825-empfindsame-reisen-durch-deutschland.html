Abstract written by a model

Empfindsame Reisen durch Deutschland is Johann Gottlieb Schummel’s three-volume acknowledged imitation of Yorick, published by Zimmermann in Wittenberg and Zerbst from Easter 1771 to spring 1772. A typical, well-known German disciple work, it contributes to sentimentalism as document and example. It draws most constantly on A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy, while adapting ideas, motifs, expression, and method from Tristram Shandy.

Empfindsame Reisen durch Deutschland by Johann Gottlieb Schummel is described as one of the most typical and best-known acknowledged imitations of Yorick. Published in three volumes between Easter 1771 and spring 1772, it draws more constantly on A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy than on Tristram Shandy, while also adapting many features of both works.
