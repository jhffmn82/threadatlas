Aglauros’s transformation is a deadly change into a bloodless stone statue, beginning with heaviness, numbness, stiffening knees, and pallid veins, then spreading until her whole form becomes stone.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Aglauros’s transformation begins when her seated body becomes too heavy and numb to rise, while her knees stiffen and her veins grow pallid. A deadly chill spreads through her body until her neck, face, and whole form become stone, though the stone is stained by her mind.
