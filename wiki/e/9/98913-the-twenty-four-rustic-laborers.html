the twenty-four rustic laborers in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The twenty-four rustic laborers, after leaving Ragbad to seek livelihoods elsewhere and becoming rag-pickers when textile work ends, are summoned by Pudge for the Ragbad Quadrille, then sell the yellow hen’s gold bricks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 22

The twenty-four rustic laborers are summoned by Pudge to join the gathering and fill in the lines for the Ragbad Quadrille. Afterward, they sell the gold bricks laid by the yellow hen as fast as they are produced.
