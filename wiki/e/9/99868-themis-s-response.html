Abstract written by a model

An instruction from Themis directs Deucalion and Pyrrha to prepare and throw their great mother’s bones behind them, later understood as casting earth’s stones.

Themis's response is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Themis's response instructs Deucalion and Pyrrha to depart, cover their heads, loosen their garments, and throw behind them the bones of their great mother. Though initially obscure, it is interpreted as an order to cast stones from the earth.
