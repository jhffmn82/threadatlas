the Naiads

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Naiads, her sisters, watery sisters
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Naiads, Syrinx’s watery sisters, answer her plea when Ladon stops her flight by changing her into reeds; they are possibly associated with an altar.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Naiads are nymphs who preside over brooks and fountains and are described as immortal.
