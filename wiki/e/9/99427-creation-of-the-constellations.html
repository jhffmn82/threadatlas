creation of the constellations

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: exalted to the heavens, forming the Constellation of the Bear, placed among the stars, placed them in the heavens, placement of Calisto and Arcas among the stars, received into heaven

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is Jupiter carrying Calisto and Arcas into the heavens and making them neighboring constellations. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is Jupiter placing Callisto and Arcas in the heavens as the Great and Little Bear, where they circle without setting beneath Oceanus.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

Jupiter creates the constellations of the Great and Little Bear by rescuing Callisto and her son Arcas and placing them in the heavens. Because Juno persuades Tethys and Oceanus to forbid them from entering their waters, the two constellations circle the heavens without setting beneath the ocean.
