English influence in Germany

Abstract written by a model

English influence in Germany predominates in later eighteenth-century literary culture, combats French influence through admired English ideals, and, via periodicals, poetry, journals, translations, and authors, shapes German tastes and fosters greater independence.

NEW YORK, May 1, 1905.

English influence in Germany spread through moral weeklies, religious and didactic poetry, literary journals, translations, and the popularity of English authors. It helped shape German tastes while allowing more independence than the earlier dominance of French models.
