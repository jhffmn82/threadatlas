Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The rivers gather at Neptune's command, open the mouths of their fountains, and flow unobstructed into the ocean. They break across the plains and carry away crops, groves, flocks, people, houses, temples, and sacred utensils.
