the danger from Mr. Douglas's American past in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The danger from Mr. Douglas's American past, associated by him with the Valley of Fear, was a long-persisting feud. Beginning at least eleven years earlier, it shadowed his life through threatening men in California and powerful enemies he believed were pursuing him. He concealed it from Mrs. Douglas, though his precautions, fear of strangers, and references revealed it.

Chapter V

The feud against Douglas was believed by Barker to have begun at least eleven years earlier and to have continued with great persistence. Barker thought it had shadowed Douglas's whole life and was connected with the threatening men who sought him in California.

Inspector MacDonald had sent up a note to the effect that he would wait

The danger from Mr. Douglas's American past was something he concealed from Mrs. Douglas to prevent her alarm. She inferred its existence from his precautions, his reactions to unexpected strangers, his references to the Valley of Fear, and his belief that powerful enemies were following him.
