FABLES V. AND VI. [XIII.623-718]

Anius is king of Delos and priest of Apollo. He receives Æneas, Anchises, and Ascanius into his temple and house, recounts the fate of his daughters, consults Apollo’s oracle with them, and gives gifts to the departing Trojans.
