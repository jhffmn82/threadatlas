FABLES V. AND VI. [XI.221-409]

Chione, Dædalion's daughter, is highly beautiful and attracts many suitors. She bears Autolycus to Mercury and Philammon to Apollo, but Diana kills her with an arrow after Chione boasts that she is more beautiful than the Goddess.
