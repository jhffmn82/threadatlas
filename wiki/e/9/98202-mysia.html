Mysia

Also called: Μυσίαν, Μυσίας, Μυσίᾳ

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Mysia is ruled by Telephus, is mistaken for Troy by the Greeks, and is ravaged by them; Telephus defends Mysia and drives the Greeks back to their ships. In The Library, Mysia is a land visited by the Argonauts after Lemnos and before the Bebrycian land; after Cyzicus was buried, they stopped there, while Hercules and Polyphemus remained searching for Hylas, and Polyphemus founded and ruled Cius. Teuthras ruled Mysia before Telephus succeeded him.
