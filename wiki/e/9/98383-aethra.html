Aethra in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Aethra lay with Aegeus and Poseidon after Aegeus was made drunk. Aegeus instructed her to rear a child without saying whose he was, send him with sword, sandals when he moved a rock. She had Theseus; captive of Thracians.

Aethra in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Aethra, daughter of Pittheus, wife of Aegeus, and mother of Theseus, was formerly queen in Troezen. Fleeing war and fire, she is found by Demophoon and Acamas, identifies her lineage, and is promised return to Hellas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then also Menelaus in Helen's bower

Aethra, the mother of Theseus, is found by Demophoon and Acamas while fleeing the war and fire. She explains her Danaan lineage and asks to be brought to the sons of Theseus, who recognize her and promise to return her to Greece.
