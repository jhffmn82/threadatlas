Aethra

person · 2 works · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Theseus' mother Aethra, me, my, Αἴθραν, Αἴθρας

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Aethra lies with Aegeus and Poseidon after Aegeus is made drunk, has Theseus, follows Aegeus's instructions to rear him without revealing his father, and is later captive of Thracians. In The Fall of Troy, Aethra is the daughter of Pittheus, wife of Aegeus, and mother of Theseus, formerly queen in Troezen; fleeing war and fire, she is found by Demophoon and Acamas, identifies her lineage, and is promised return to Hellas.

Aethra in The Library

Apollodorus · AD 1 to 200 · the work’s page · read it where Aethra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Aethra lay with Aegeus and Poseidon after Aegeus was made drunk. Aegeus instructed her to rear a child without saying whose he was, send him with sword, sandals when he moved a rock. She had Theseus; captive of Thracians.

Aethra in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · about AD 200 to 299 · the work’s page · read it where Aethra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Aethra, daughter of Pittheus, wife of Aegeus, and mother of Theseus, was formerly queen in Troezen. Fleeing war and fire, she is found by Demophoon and Acamas, identifies her lineage, and is promised return to Hellas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then also Menelaus in Helen's bower

Aethra, the mother of Theseus, is found by Demophoon and Acamas while fleeing the war and fire. She explains her Danaan lineage and asks to be brought to the sons of Theseus, who recognize her and promise to return her to Greece.
