Orithyïa in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Orithyïa, daughter of Erectheus and princess of Athens, is seized by Boreas while fearful near the river Ilissus and carried to Thrace. She becomes Boreas’s wife and the mother of Calaïs and Zethes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Orithyïa is the daughter of Erectheus. Boreas carries her away while she trembles with fear, and she becomes his wife and the mother of Calaïs and Zethes.

notes .. notes

Orithyïa was the daughter of Erectheus, king of Athens, and the sister of Procris. According to Apollodorus and Pausanias, Boreas seized her as she was passing the river Ilissus and carried her to Thrace, where she became the mother of Calaïs and Zethes.
