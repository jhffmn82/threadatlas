Abstract written by a model

Un is a rocky, barren skyle: a small island entirely surrounded by clear air and drifting clouds. Restricted to the Uns, it has trees, tree houses, sky wells, and a palace, and is ruled by I-wish-I-was. The Uns live there, grow feathers when something unish happens, and do not work, instead wishing, fishing, and fighting. Visitors fish from its edge until the Uns attack them at night. A good Un hides in a cave after unkind treatment.

Un is in one work on this site, The Cowardly Lion of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Un is a skyle, meaning a small body of land entirely surrounded by air. It is rocky and barren, has many trees and tree houses, and is ruled by I-wish-I-was.

Chapter 11

The Skyland is a small island or skyle surrounded by clear air and drifting clouds. Notta, Bob Up, and the Cowardly Lion fish from its edge, where they catch a cooked goose, a skye terrier, and dreams. The Uns attack them there during the night.

Chapter 12

The Isle of Un is a skyland where the Uns live in tree houses and grow feathers whenever something unish happens to them. Its inhabitants do not work; they wish, fish, and fight instead. A good Un is said to be hiding in a cave because the others treated him unkindly.
