Palaemon

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Divinity, Palæmon, Portumnus

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Palæmon, formerly Melicerta, becomes a sea deity after Neptune transforms him following a fall into the sea, then establishes the Isthmian games. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Palaemon becomes a sea god after leaping into the sea with Ino; sailors invoke him, he rides a dolphin, oversees ports and shores, and receives Isthmian games.

Chapter XV

Palaemon is the god into whom Melicertes is changed after leaping into the sea with Ino. He is usually represented riding a dolphin, is invoked by sailors, receives the Isthmian games in his honor, and is called Portumnus by the Romans.
