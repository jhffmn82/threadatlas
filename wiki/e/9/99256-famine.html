Famine in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Famine, an emaciated, starving deity from desolate Scythia, punishes Erisicthon’s sacrilege by entering his bedchamber and giving him insatiable hunger.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

EXPLANATION.

Famine lived in a desolate region of Scythia and was portrayed as emaciated and starving. She entered Erisicthon’s bedchamber while he slept, breathed hunger into him, and caused his appetite to remain unsatisfied no matter how much he ate.

Famine in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Famine, dwelling in sterile, ice-clad Scythia, enters Erisichthon at Ceres’s command, gives him insatiable hunger, and returns home.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XV

Famine dwells in a sterile, ice-clad region of Scythia. At Ceres’s command, she enters Erisichthon’s bed-chamber, breathes herself into him, and gives him an insatiable hunger before returning to her own haunts.
