Iphis's transformation in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Iphis's transformation is a divine reward for Telethusa's piety: after her prayer in Isis's temple, Iphis becomes male in body and identity and offers Isis previously vowed gifts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Footnote 46: _Every God has._--Ver. 425-6. ‘Cui studeat, Deus

Iphis's transformation occurs in Isis's temple after Telethusa prays for assistance. Iphis leaves the temple with greater strength, more masculine features, shorter hair, and the body and identity of a male, then offers Isis the gifts previously vowed as a female.
