mulberry tree

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: O tree, a white mulberry tree, mulberry, the mulberries, the mulberries o'erload the bending boughs, the tree, tree, white mulberry tree
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the mulberry tree's fruit changes from snow-white to dark purple after Pyramus's blood reaches it, and it later shades the bodies of both lovers.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the white mulberry tree's white berries are stained red by Pyramus's blood and later become purple as a memorial to the lovers.
