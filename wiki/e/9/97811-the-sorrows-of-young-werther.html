The Sorrows of Young Werther in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

The Sorrows of Young Werther is a work built on sentimentalism, on groundwork Goethe said Yorick helped prepare. Though no direct Sterne influence is demonstrable, it reflects a mental and emotional condition Sterne contributed to. Alongside Siegwart and Sterne, it was a principal target of Timme’s satire amid a sentimental frenzy.

[37] Herder’s early acquaintance with Sterne has been already treated.

The Sorrows of Young Werther contains no direct influence from Laurence Sterne that the unit can demonstrate, but it reflects a mental and emotional condition to which Sterne contributed. Johann Wolfgang Goethe later said that Yorick helped prepare the sentimental groundwork on which Werther was built.

The name of Helfreich Peter Sturz may well be coupled with that of Lichtenberg,

Werther is identified as one of the three principal targets of Timme’s satire. Timme presents Werther, along with Siegwart and Sterne, as a source whose followers contributed to the sentimental frenzy.
