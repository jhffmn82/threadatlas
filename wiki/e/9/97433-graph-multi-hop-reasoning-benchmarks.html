Introduction

Graph multi-hop reasoning benchmarks are used to evaluate GRAG. The reported experiments show that GRAG significantly outperforms current RAG methods and LLM baselines, while a frozen LLM with GRAG outperforms a fine-tuned LLM on all tasks.
