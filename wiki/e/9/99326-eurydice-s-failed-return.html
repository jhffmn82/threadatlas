Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE TENTH.

Eurydice's return fails when Orpheus, fearing that she is falling behind and longing to see her, turns his eyes toward her before they leave the underworld. She sinks back, dies a second time, and is carried away beyond recovery.
