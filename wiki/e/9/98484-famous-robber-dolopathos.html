XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

The famous robber had accumulated great riches through robbery before resolving in old age to practice virtue. He recounts that he and other robbers were captured by a giant, that he blinded the giant with a harmful boiling salve, escaped among the sheep, and finally cut off a finger bearing the giant's magic ring.
