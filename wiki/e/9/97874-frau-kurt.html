Frau Kurt in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Frau Kurt is a sentimental reader absorbed in emotion. She values a dog’s mournful death and a goat’s plight over people’s urgent suffering, repulsing a begging woman and ignoring a village fire and its homeless victims.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

A former acquaintance of Frau Kurt, whose favorite reading was Shandy, Wieland’s

Frau Kurt remains absorbed in sentimental reading and emotion while refusing to help people in immediate need. She elevates a dog’s mournful death above other suffering, rudely repulses a woman begging for aid, and later becomes passionately sympathetic toward a goat while ignoring a village fire and the homeless people it creates.
