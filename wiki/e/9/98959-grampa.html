Grampa in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · the work’s page · read it where Grampa first appears

Abstract written by a model

Grampa is an old soldier, bachelor, and retainer of King Fumbo of Ragbad. Discharged from active service but still tending the gardens, he has fought nine hundred and eighty battles, lost a leg in battle, and relies on a game leg. He is a durable, spirited leader: he carries a gun, sword, drum, knapsack, pipes, medals, snuff, and wizard’s medicine, and remains brave, argumentative, and resourceful through repeated dangers. After finding the headless King Fumbo and returning him to the castle, Grampa accompanies Prince Tatters in seeking the King’s head. He chooses the route toward the Emerald City, recruits Bill, resists and escapes Vaga’s bandits by talking them to sleep, and survives a fall through a hollow tree with the medicine. In Gorba’s garden he helps bring Urtha to life, accepts responsibility for her, and is temporarily changed into a chimney and bay window before being restored. Grampa leads the companions through dangerous water, Fire Island, a volcano, and Isa Poso, where he defeats Enorma with snuff and claims the promised reward. Tobacco later changes him into an old crow with a game leg; he flies on, loses his pipe, helps recover Fumbo’s head, and continues the search for the Princess and fortune. He reaches the Emerald City, recounts the journey, leads an army, loses his game leg to the Play Fellows, and uses a croquet mallet as a crutch. He defends Tatters, helps reveal that Urtha is the lost Princess and expose Abrog, then uses the medicine to punish Abrog. At the end, he plays for the Ragbad Quadrille and, with Tatters, achieves record crops after Ragbad can obtain supplies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

17 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Grampa is an old soldier with a game leg who has fought in nine hundred and eighty Ragbad battles. He hides briefly from the storm, finds the headless King, drags him back to the castle, ties him to a pillar, and tries to comfort Mrs Sew-and-Sew.

CHAPTER 2

Grampa decides to accompany Prince Tatters on the journey. He helps prepare for the departure, carries his gun, sword, knapsack, and drum, and leaves the castle with Tatters.

CHAPTER 3

Grampa uses a four-pence to choose the northern route to the Emerald City, believing that magic is needed to restore Fumbo’s head. He recruits Bill, fights the bandits with his drumsticks, and is ultimately bound and taken to their camp.

CHAPTER 4

Grampa refuses to be frightened by Vaga and insults the bandits. He persuades Vaga to make him and Tatters outlaws, then tells such a long account of his battles that the bandits fall asleep. He recovers his possessions, takes a blue pouch and a bottle of patent medicine, and escapes with Tatters and Bill before falling through a hollow tree.

CHAPTER 5

Grampa falls down the hollow tree and lands painfully on a cinder path. He uses the wizard's patent medicine to recover, initially threatens to discharge Bill, and later enters the Garden of Gorba with Prince Tatters and Bill.

CHAPTER 6

Grampa leads Tatters and Bill through Gorba’s garden and is drenched when the enchanted stepping stones carry them beneath a fountain. He admires the peach trees and animal-shaped bushes, helps bring Urtha to life, and decides that they are responsible for looking after her. He invites Urtha to join their search and leaves the garden with the others.

CHAPTER 7

Grampa grows a chimney and a bay window after eating a flower from a bush marked "House plants." Tatters uses two drops of the magic medicine on him, restoring him to normal, and Grampa then leads the group through the dark garden toward a way out.

CHAPTER 10

Grampa falls with Tatters, Urtha, and Bill into an underground lake, rescues Tatters with the wizard’s medicine, and uses the medicine on the whole group when the water becomes dangerously hot. He reaches Fire Island, meets Prince Forge John the First, worries that they will burn up, and is sent toward Blazes by boat.

CHAPTER 11

Grampa and his companions are taken through the dark tower and sent up through a volcano. He survives the eruption because of Gorba's medicine, helps care for Urtha, eats toasted bread and butter, keeps his spirits up, and leads the group ashore on the Illustrious Island of Isa Poso.

CHAPTER 12

Grampa decides to slay Enorma so that Tatters can claim half the kingdom and Princess Poso. He defeats the dragon with snuff, helps secure her body as proof of their valor, finds food and shelter in her cave, and then claims the reward before King Chin Chilly the Third.

CHAPTER 13

Grampa is horrified by the Princess of Isa Poso and angered when Chin Chilly tricks the party. He takes the wizard's medicine, helps keep the others cheerful, and later smokes the tobacco that transforms him into an old crow with a game leg. He flies after Bill toward Oz.

CHAPTER 15

Grampa leads the journey across the Nonestic Ocean, Ev, and the great desert toward the Emerald City. He discovers why the company became crows, loses his pipe, helps find King Fumbo's head, and carries on the search for the Princess and the fortune. He travels down the rainbow with the others and decides that he needs an anchor because he is tired of flying and falling.

CHAPTER 17

Grampa initially insists that Dorothy is only a country girl and not a Princess, but he is embarrassed when King Fumbo identifies her. He tells the story of his party's adventures, decides to join the search for the Princess of Perhaps City, and orders the group to make camp for the night.

CHAPTER 18

Grampa leads the army toward the Emerald City and tries to control the march while Percy Vere makes jokes and poems. The Play Fellows force him through repeated amusements, and after he falls asleep under a checker bush, they run off with his game leg. He escapes by using a long-handled croquet mallet as a crutch, with Percy Vere and Dorothy helping him.

CHAPTER 19

Grampa reaches the throne room with the others and angrily questions Tatters about marrying a Princess he has never seen. He insists on seeing the Princess, tries to defend Tatters when Abrog calls him a monster, and seizes Abrog by the beard.

CHAPTER 20

Grampa learns that the flower maiden is Urtha, the lost Princess, and helps expose Abrog's deception. He uses the wizard's medicine to determine Abrog's punishment and breaks a saucer of the mixture over Abrog's head.

CHAPTER 22

Grampa plays the spirited music for the Ragbad Quadrille. He and Tatters achieve record crops after Ragbad gains the means to obtain supplies.
