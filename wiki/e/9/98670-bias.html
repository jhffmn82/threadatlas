IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

Bias, son of Talaus, agreed to obtain the kine in order to marry Pero and persuaded his brother Melampus to undertake the task. After Melampus brought the kine to Neleus, Bias married Pero.
