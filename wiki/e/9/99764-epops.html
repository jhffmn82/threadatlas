Abstract written by a model

Epops, also called lapwing, is a bird transformed from Tereus, marked by a crested head, prolonged bill, armed-looking face, and delight in impurity.

epops is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
