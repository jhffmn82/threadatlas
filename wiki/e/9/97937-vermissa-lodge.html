Vermissa Lodge

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Union, Vermissa lodge, lodge, organization, society, this lodge

Abstract written by a model

Vermissa Lodge is a lodge of the Eminent Order of Freemen and a criminal association in the coal and iron district, under Jack McGinty’s authority, with Ted Baldwin among its members. It exchanges violent services with other lodges, extorts local firms, supports families of members killed on lodge work, and pursues a murder campaign through discussed assassinations, arson, intimidation, and attacks against those judged by its lodges.

Vermissa Lodge is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PART II / Chapter I

Vermissa Lodge is a lodge of the Eminent Order of Freemen. Its Bodymaster is J. H. Scott according to McMurdo’s identification of his Chicago lodge, while Vermissa’s lodge is under the authority of Jack McGinty.

When the drinks which followed the ceremony of initiation had all been

The Vermissa lodge operates as a criminal organization that exchanges violent services with other lodges, extorts local firms, and supports members' families when they are killed carrying out lodge work. Its members discuss assassinations, arson, intimidation, and attacks while maintaining that their organization is beyond the reach of the law.
