Danaë’s seduction by Jupiter

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Danae’s seduction by Zeus, The story of the seduction of Danaë, the mother of Perseus, by Jupiter, had intercourse with her

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Danaë’s seduction by Jupiter is described as Jupiter reaching Danaë as a shower of gold, with a rationalizing account attributing access to Prœtus bribing guards. In The Library, Danae’s seduction by Zeus is attributed to Zeus becoming gold and pouring into her lap, producing Perseus; some instead say Proetus seduced her.

The myth says that Jupiter seduced Danaë in the form of a shower of gold. One ancient rationalization instead identifies Prœtus, Danaë’s uncle, as the man who gained access to her tower by corrupting the guards with gold.
