the transformation of Salmacis and Hermaphroditus

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: become one body, the mingled bodies of the two are united, their bodies meet together

Abstract written by a model

The Gods unite Salmacis and Hermaphroditus after her prayer, transforming their two bodies into one human shape retaining both sexes, both and neither woman nor boy.

opening

The Gods unite Salmacis and Hermaphroditus after she prays that no time separate them. Their two bodies become one human shape with a twofold form, so that they can be called neither woman nor boy, and seem both and neither.
