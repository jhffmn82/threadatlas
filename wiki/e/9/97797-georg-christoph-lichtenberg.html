Georg Christoph Lichtenberg

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Georg Christopher Lichtenberg, Kästner, Lichtenberg, Lichtenstein

Abstract written by a model

A Göttingen professor, he shared something of Sterne’s inner nature, lived in England shortly after Sterne’s death, and associated with London through Sterne’s intimate friends. He became the chief opponent of Sterne, Sterne’s disciples, and sentimentalism, condemning Sterne’s alleged selfishness, hypocrisy, and manipulation of sympathy while satirizing sentimental writers, fashions, and German authors’ lack of originality.

] CHAPTER VII OPPOSITION TO STERNE AND HIS TYPE OF SENTIMENTALISM Sterne’s influence in Germany lived its own life, and gradually and imperceptibly died out of letters, as an actuating principle.

Georg Christopher Lichtenberg was presented as the chief opponent of the sentimental tendency and of Sterne’s disciples. He condemned Sterne’s alleged selfishness and artificial manipulation of emotion, questioned the sincerity of Sterne’s noble feelings, and satirized sentimental writers, eccentric literary fashions, and the lack of originality among German authors.
