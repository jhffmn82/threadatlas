Abstract written by a model

Alcmena’s labor lasts seven tormented days and nights, prolonged by Ilithyïa’s spell as Cadmeian matrons attend, vow, encourage, and witness her exhaustion and despair.

FABLE III. [IX.273-323]

Alcmena’s labor is prolonged and agonizing, leaving her exhausted and desperate. Ilithyïa increases her pains and impedes the delivery, while the Cadmeian matrons attend her, offer vows, and encourage her.
