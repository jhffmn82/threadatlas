Lotis

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Nymph Lotis, the Nymph Lotis, the plant

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Nymph Lotis flees Priapus and is transformed into a lotus near a lake, preserving her identity in its name. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Lotis is changed into a lotus plant; Dryope unknowingly plucks its flowers, and blood falls from the stems.
