tradition

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: prevailing tradition, the history of the universal deluge, the tradition of the universal deluge, universal flood tradition

Abstract written by a model

Tradition preserves memories of the first parents’ peaceful innocence, a divine decision to punish the earth by a deluge, and one just man surviving universal corruption. Transmitted to heathen poets and preserved by Greeks, Romans, and all nations, it unfolds in Ovid’s Deucalion account, where sea waters join those from heaven; Deucalion corresponds to Noah in Scripture.

notes .. notes

Tradition is said to preserve the account of a divine decision to punish the earth by a deluge and to preserve the fact that at least one just man remained amid universal corruption. It attributes this just man's merit to Deucalion, corresponding to Noah in Scripture.

notes .. notes

The universal flood tradition is described as a story preserved by all nations and adopted by Ovid in his account of Deucalion. It includes the joining of the sea’s waters with those falling from heaven and is compared with the account of Noah’s flood.
