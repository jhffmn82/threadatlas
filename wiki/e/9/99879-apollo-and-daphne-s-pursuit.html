Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Apollo pursues Daphne because Cupid’s golden arrow has made him love her, while Cupid’s leaden arrow makes her flee from the very idea of love. Apollo follows her through the woods, comparing their chase to animals pursuing or escaping one another, until she reaches the waters of Peneus.

notes .. notes

Apollo pursues Daphne as she flees, and the chase is compared with a greyhound pursuing a hare. The comparison emphasizes Apollo’s eagerness and Daphne’s anxiety as she tries to escape.
