Abstract

Rationales are used to train a small filtering model with perplexity-based labels and are generated by large language models as queries. They help select informative snippets and improve the utility of retrieved snippets.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.4

Model-generated rationales are produced through a chain-of-thought prompt that asks the LLM to summarize available information, explain its reasoning, and provide a final answer. They are used as queries to search for relevant information.
