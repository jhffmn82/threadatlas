Also called: Broteas, a hunter

Abstract written by a model

Broteas is a hunter who does not honor Artemis, claims fire cannot harm him, becomes mad, and dies by throwing himself into fire.

Broteas is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
