Meerkat-7B in Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Jiwoong Sohn · 2025

Meerkat-7B is a medical large language model initialized from Mistral-7B weights, instruction-tuned with GPT-4-generated rationales, and fine-tuned on MedQA and MedMCQA with a 2K context limit. In RAG2 evaluation, accuracy rises from 68.6% to 72.4% and MMLU-Med from 73.8% to 78.7%. It benefits from rationale queries and balanced retrieval, peaks with more retrieved snippets, then declines.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Meerkat-7B is one of the language models of varying sizes used in the evaluation of RAG2. RAG2 significantly enhances the average accuracy of the evaluated models.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.3

Meerkat-7B is initialized from Mistral-7B weights and instruction-tuned with GPT-4-generated rationales before being fine-tuned on MedQA and MedMCQA. Its average accuracy is 68.6% without RAG and 72.4% with RAG2, while its MMLU-Med accuracy increases from 73.8% to 78.7%.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

Meerkat-7B reaches a peak as more snippets are retrieved and then declines, likely because it is instruction-tuned with a 2K maximum context length. It benefits from rationale queries and balanced retrieval, and its rationale queries produce stronger MedQA performance than those generated by Llama-3-8B-Instruct.
