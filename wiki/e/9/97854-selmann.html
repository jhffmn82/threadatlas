Selmann is a compound of Yorick and the elder Shandy, with satiric exaggeration directed at the thirst for originals and overwrought sentimentalism. He is generous and sensitive to human pain, but also devotes himself to Shandean speculation about trivial relationships and repeatedly pursues supposedly sentimental or original experiences that prove to be jokes or deceptions.
