Abstract written by a model

The journey to the Emerald City is a northward expedition from Ragbad into dangerous lands of Oz, seeking the lost Princess’s whereabouts through Ozma’s magic picture. Tatters and Grampa prepare overnight, depart with supplies and Ragbad’s flag, cross the Quadling country and enter the Blue Forest, where bandits capture them. Later, Grampa’s army resumes marching but is interrupted at fenced play grounds.

CHAPTER 2

Tatters and Grampa prepare overnight and depart from the castle at eight o’clock. They carry clothing, food, weapons, a drum, the huge red umbrella, and Ragbad’s patched flag as they begin traveling into Oz.

CHAPTER 3

The journey to the Emerald City begins when Grampa’s coin toss selects the northern route. Prince Tatters and Grampa leave Ragbad, cross the Quadling country, enter the Blue Forest of Oz, and are captured by bandits before reaching the city.

CHAPTER 18

Grampa's army begins another march to the Emerald City so they can learn from Ozma's magic picture where Abrog and the lost Princess of Perhaps City are. The march is interrupted when the company enters the fenced play grounds.
