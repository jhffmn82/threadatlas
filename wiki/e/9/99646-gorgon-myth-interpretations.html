Gorgon myth interpretations

Abstract written by a model

The Gorgon myths are interpreted as accounts of female warriors, savage women, wealthy sisters with a shared minister, beautiful women whose appearance caused astonishment, or a deadly animal. The sisters’ single eye may represent their shared minister, while Medusa’s petrifying head may represent a statue, terror, or the fame of Perseus’s exploits.
