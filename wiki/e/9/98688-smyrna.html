Smyrna in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Smyrna, Thias’s daughter, is driven by Aphrodite’s wrath to desire and unknowingly sleep with her father for twelve nights; when pursued by him, she is transformed by the gods into a myrrh tree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

βασιλέως ᾿Ασσυρίων, ὃ ὃς ἔσχε θυγατέρα Σμύρναν.

Smyrna is the daughter of Thias. Because of Aphrodite's anger, she conceives a passion for her father, shares his bed for twelve nights with her nurse's help, is pursued by him, and is transformed into a myrrh tree by the gods.
