  • In Laurence Sterne in Germany: reviews Sermons of Mr. Yorick [under the caption “London”] CHAPTER II STERNE IN GERMANY BEFORE THE PUBLICATION OF THE SENTIMENTAL JOURNEY It is no exaggeration to assert that the works of Yorick obtained and still retain a relatively more substantial position of serious consideration and recognized merit in France and Germany than in the countries where Sterne’s own tongue is spoken.] The Jenaische Zeitungen von gelehrten Sachen gave the first treatment of Sterne’s work in a distinctively literary journal in March 1765. Its review introduced Tristram Shandy as a new and difficult book, apparently without knowing or identifying Sterne as its author. In June 1766, the journal reviewed the Sermons of Mr. Yorick and supplied information about Sterne’s authorship.
