Abstract written by a model

Cadmus's companions are Tyrian men who go to the fountain of Mars for water, where its guarding dragon attacks, devours, and kills them.

opening

Cadmus's companions, men from the Tyrian nation, go to the fountain of Mars to fetch water. The dragon attacks and devours them, killing some with its sting, some with its coils, and some with its poisonous breath.
