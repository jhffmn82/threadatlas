Chapter VII

The trial of the Scowrers is held far from the Valley of Fear so their supporters cannot intimidate the guardians of the law. Edwards gives an unshaken account of their organization and crimes; McGinty and eight chief followers are executed, while more than fifty others receive prison sentences.
