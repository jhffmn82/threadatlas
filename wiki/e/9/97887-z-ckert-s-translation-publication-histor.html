Zückert’s translation publication history in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Zückert’s translation publication history began with parts I–VI in 1763, expanded with parts VII–VIII in 1765, and added a ninth part in 1767 translated from a spurious English original. A revised, advertised “nach einer neuen Uebersetzung” edition followed in 1769–1772 but retained serious errors and the spurious ninth volume. In 1773 or 1774, Lange reissued it under a title invoking Wieland’s advice, which Wieland denied.

Zückert’s translation publication history begins with parts I–VI in 1763, followed by parts VII–VIII in 1765 and a spurious ninth part in 1767. A revised edition appeared in parts from 1769 through 1772, retaining many serious errors and still using a translation of the spurious ninth volume. In 1773 or 1774, Lange reissued the translation under a title invoking Wieland’s advice, although Wieland denied any connection with it.
