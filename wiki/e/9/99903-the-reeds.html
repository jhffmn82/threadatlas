the reeds in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The reeds Pan seizes, believing he caught Syrinx, murmur in wind and become panpipes when unequal reeds are joined with wax.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The reeds are what Pan seizes when he believes he has caught Syrinx. Wind moving among them produces a murmuring sound, and Pan joins unequal reeds with wax to make an instrument.
