Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE X. [X.708-739]

A wild boar is roused by Adonis’s dogs and is pierced by Adonis’s hunting-spear. It strikes the spear away, pursues the fleeing Adonis, and kills him by lodging its tusks in his groin.
