the study in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The study is the murder scene where Barker finds Mr. Douglas dead on the floor after an apparent gunshot. It contains a candle and lamp, the missing dumb-bell before it is put in the moat, and later a sodden bundle beneath the writing table. Its changing light, view over the moat, and preserved scene draw Holmes to spend an evening there seeking clues.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

The study was where Barker found Douglas and where Ames and Barker later found everything as the police had seen it. The study had a candle on the table when Barker entered, but the candle was later out and the lamp was lit.

Chapter VI

The study is where Mr. Douglas’s bloodstained body was found and where the gunshot was apparently fired. Holmes plans to spend an evening alone there, hoping that the room’s atmosphere or further observation will help him solve the case.

Chapter VII

The study is watched from outside because it has a visible light and overlooks the moat. Cecil Barker is present there when the investigators enter, and the sodden bundle is found beneath its writing table.
