Kimbles are 250 happy, light-hearted girls and boys living in tiny cottages beneath Kimbaloo’s trees; girls work for Rosa Merry and boys for Kinda Jolly.

CHAPTER 1

The Kimbles are the two hundred and fifty girls and boys who live in tiny cottages beneath the trees of Kimbaloo. The girls work for Rosa Merry and the boys work for Kinda Jolly.
