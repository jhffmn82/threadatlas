Abstract written by a model

Galanthis is Alcmena’s active, beloved yellow-haired hand-maid. Seeing Ilithyïa hinder Alcmena’s labor, she lies that the birth has occurred, enabling it; punished for deceiving Ilithyïa, she is transformed into a weasel.

Galanthis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE III. [IX.273-323]

Galanthis is an active and beloved hand-maid of Alcmena who notices Ilithyïa’s interference. She falsely announces that Alcmena has already given birth, causing Ilithyïa to release her joined hands and allowing the birth to occur; Ilithyïa then punishes her by transforming her.
