Abstract written by a model

REVEAL is a model that injects retrieval scores into attention layers and minimizes L2-norm differences between query and knowledge embeddings. It integrates objectives: Prefix Language Modeling, Contrastive, Disentangled Regularization, and Alignment Regularization losses in pretraining, then cross-entropy for downstream fine-tuning.
