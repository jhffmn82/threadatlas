Theodor Gottlieb von Hippel wrote Lebensläufe nach aufsteigender Linie and Kreuz und Querzüge des Ritters A. bis Z., which are described as purely Shandean products. Their Sterne-derived humor struggles with pedagogical seriousness, and a detailed study of Hippel in relation to Sterne and Jean Paul is proposed.
