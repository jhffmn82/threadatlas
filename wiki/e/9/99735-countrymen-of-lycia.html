Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the countrymen of Lycia deny Latona water, threaten and mock her, muddy the lake, and are transformed into frogs. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Rustics of Lycia refuse, mock, threaten, and deny Latona water, muddying her pond before she transforms them into frogs.

countrymen of Lycia in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · about AD 8 · the work’s page · read it where countrymen of Lycia first appears

Abstract written by a model

The countrymen of Lycia work near a fertile lake, gathering plants. They brutally deny Latona water, threaten her, and muddy the lake; after her curse, they are transformed into frogs.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The countrymen are working near a lake in fertile Lycia, gathering osiers, bulrushes, and sedge. They refuse Latona's request for water, threaten and abuse her, and muddy the lake by stirring its bottom with their feet and hands. Latona curses them, and they are transformed into frogs.
