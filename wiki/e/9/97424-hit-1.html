Also called: Hits@1, retrieval Hits@1
  • In HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases, Hit@1 is the default STARK retrieval evaluation metric, with HYBGRAG scoring 0.6540/0.2856 and improving it by 51% on average.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Hit@1 is a primary WebQSP retrieval metric assessing whether the top result is correct; GRAG scores 0.7236 without fine-tuning and 0.7275 with LoRA, exceeding the 0.4148 LLM-only score.
  • In SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Hits@1 measures question-answering retrieval performance on MetaQA, PathQuestions, and WorldCup2014.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Hit@1 is one of the primary WebQSP evaluation metrics, with GRAG raising it from 0.4148 for the LLM-only baseline to 0.7236 and GRAGLoRA reaching 0.7275.
