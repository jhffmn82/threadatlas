Abstract written by a model

King Theodore the Third is the small, nervous, entirely bald king of the Kingdom of Doorways. He rules while wearing a crown of china door knobs on gold bars, likes rhyme, breaks into singular verse, and believes books grow on a vine. In the throne room with Queen Adora the First, he questions Notta, suspects Notta stole jam, and grants Notta and Bob Up three trials. Startled and angered, he finally fixates on rhyming “toboggan” as they escape.

Chapter 6

King Theodore the Third sits on a throne beside Queen Adora the First in the throne room. He wears a crown made entirely of china door knobs mounted on gold bars, speaks in rhymes, questions Notta's claim about the Order of Bachelors, and initially agrees that Notta has stolen the jam.

Chapter 7

King Theodore questions Notta and Bob Up after they enter the throne room and allows them three trials to make him and Queen Adora laugh. He is startled by Notta's antics, becomes angry when he thinks Notta is joking about his baldness, and becomes absorbed in finding a rhyme for "toboggan." While his eyes are closed, Notta and Bob escape through the door marked "Out."
