Wiki-PII

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Wiki-PII dataset

Abstract written by a model

Wiki-PII is a dataset constructed by mixing personal information pieces from Enron Mail with wikitext-103 samples, creating a setting where private information appears in public data. It focuses on retrieving personally identifiable information using frequent leading phrases such as "My phone number is." It is used for utility and attack-related evaluations, including targeted extraction evaluations alongside HealthCareMagic.

Wiki-PII is in one work on this site, Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Experiment

Wiki-PII is constructed by mixing personal information pieces from Enron Mail with samples from wikitext-103. It is used in a scenario where private information is mixed with a public dataset.

Utility of using synthetic data

The Wiki-PII dataset is used in utility and attack-related evaluations. Table 2 reports utility results on it, while Table 3 reports targeted attack results on it and the HealthCareMagic dataset.

Privacy of using synthetic data

Wiki-PII is evaluated for targeted extraction of personal identifiable information from data that includes a mix of Enron Mail. Frequently used phrases such as "My phone number is" are used as the information element.
