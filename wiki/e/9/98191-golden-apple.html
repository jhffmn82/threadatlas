Also called: a prize of beauty, an apple, apple, apple of Discord, μῆλον

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, the golden apple is thrown by Strife as a prize of beauty for Hera, Athena, and Aphrodite to contest before Alexander. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the golden apple is thrown among the gods by Eris with the inscription “For the most beautiful,” and Paris awards it to Venus.
