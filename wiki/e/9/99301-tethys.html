Tethys in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Tethys is a deity married to Ocean. With Oceanus she purifies Glaucus, then pities falling Æsacus, receives him in the sea, covers him with feathers, and prevents his sought death through transformation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE VIII. [XI.749-795]

Tethys pities Æsacus as he falls from the rock and receives him softly. She covers him with feathers as he swims, preventing him from obtaining the death he seeks and enabling his transformation into a didapper.
