Blankenburg in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Blankenburg is a strange city inhabited by invisible Blanks, who became invisible after bathing in magic water. Its laws prohibit faces and showing one’s face. Tora is imprisoned there and made to work. The city’s national emblem is a bright pink blanket; entry is through the Well-come and exit through the Fare-well.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

Blankenburg is the strange city where the invisible Blanks live and where Tora has been imprisoned and made to work. Its national emblem is a bright pink blanket, and people enter it through the Well-come and leave through the Fare-well.

CHAPTER 14

Blankenburg is the city where the Blanks live and where Tora was imprisoned. Its people are invisible because they bathed in magic water, and its laws forbid having a face.
