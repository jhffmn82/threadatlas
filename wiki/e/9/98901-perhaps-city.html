Perhaps City in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Perhaps City is a tall, towered city of gold high in the Maybe Mountains of Oz, where steep, craggy peaks keep its contented inhabitants from descending to the valleys. Managed entertainingly by Peer Haps, it contains his castle or palace and is home to Princess Urtha. Percy Vere recounts there the prophecy about a monster, Abrog the Prophet's strange conduct, and the Princess's disappearance. Ruled by Peer Haps, the city becomes the site of the missing Princess's search: noblemen hide after she mysteriously changes, the Prince of Ragbad reaches its gates seeking the fairy, and the prophecy concerns the Princess's marriage.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Perhaps City is a tall, towered city of gold set high in the Maybe Mountains of Oz. Its inhabitants do not descend into the valleys, and its entertaining, well-managed life has made them content to remain there.

CHAPTER 9

Perhaps City is the place where Percy Vere says the prophecy about the monster, the strange conduct of Abrog, the Prophet, and the disappearance of the Princess occurred. Percy tells Dorothy about these events while they sit beneath a jelly tree.

CHAPTER 17

Perhaps City is the place ruled by Peer Haps and is the home of the missing Princess whom Percy Vere seeks. Its Princess disappeared at the same time as Abrog.

CHAPTER 19

Perhaps City is where Peer Haps's palace stands and where the young and single noblemen hide after hearing that the Princess has mysteriously changed. The Prince of Ragbad reaches its gates while searching for the fairy.

CHAPTER 20

Perhaps City is identified as the home of Urtha, its Princess. The prophecy concerns the marriage of its Princess.
