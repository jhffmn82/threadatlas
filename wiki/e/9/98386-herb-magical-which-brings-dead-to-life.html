VII.—THE RESURRECTION OF GLAUCUS

The magical life-giving herb revives dead creatures by contact and can heal severed or wounded bodies. It is variously called the “flower of Zeus,” a red flower, a white root, or an unknown herb, and its power is learned by observing animals use it.
