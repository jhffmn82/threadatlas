Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Baucis hospitably receives Jupiter and Mercury, asks to serve them, and becomes an adjoining tree with Philemon. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Baucis welcomes Jupiter and Mercury in her humble cottage and later becomes a tree with Philemon.

FABLE V. [VIII.547-610]

Baucis, an old woman, receives Jupiter and Mercury hospitably when the surrounding houses refuse them. She prepares their meal, joins Philemon in requesting that they serve the gods, and becomes an adjoining tree with him.
