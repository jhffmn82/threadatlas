Abstract written by a model

Editor Stanger, an elderly and respected Vermissa newspaper editor of the Herald and Daily Herald, writes “Law and Order!” against lodge violence. McGinty orders a severe warning, and Baldwin with other members beats Stanger in his office, though McMurdo stops Baldwin from killing him. Stanger survives without immediate danger of death and later testifies that he can identify only a moustached first attacker.

Editor Stanger is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

When the drinks which followed the ceremony of initiation had all been

James Stanger is an elderly editor of the Herald who has written against the lodge's violence in an article headed “Law and Order!”. McGinty orders the lodge to give him a severe warning. Baldwin and several members attack Stanger in his office, leaving him on the floor with blood on his white hair, but McMurdo prevents Baldwin from killing him.

Chapter IV

Editor Stanger is severely beaten during an attack at the Herald office but is reported to be in no immediate danger of death. He later testifies that he cannot identify the attackers beyond saying that the first man who struck him wore a moustache.
