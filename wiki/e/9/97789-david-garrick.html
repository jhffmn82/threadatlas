David Garrick in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

David Garrick, an intimate friend of Laurence Sterne, told Helfreich Peter Sturz that Sterne was morally and sexually loose, recounting obscenities that drove ladies away and damaged Sterne’s London reputation.

The name of Helfreich Peter Sturz may well be coupled with that of Lichtenberg,

Garrick, described as one of Sterne’s intimate friends, told Sturz that Sterne had been a lewd companion whose obscenities drove ladies away. Garrick also contributed to Sturz’s understanding of Sterne’s personal reputation and its deterioration in London.
