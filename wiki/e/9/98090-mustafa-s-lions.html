Mustafa's lions in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923 · the work’s page · read it where Mustafa's lions first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mustafa's lions are 9,999 lions in Mustafa's enclosure in Oz. They watch the Cowardly Lion approach as an enemy and begin attacking him, but Crunch stops them. Crunch then uses seven magic words to transform all 9,999 lions into stone statues and tosses them aside while searching for the Cowardly Lion.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 20

Mustafa's 9,999 lions press their noses against the enclosure bars and watch the Cowardly Lion approach as an enemy. They spring toward him with growls, roars, and rumbles, but Crunch arrives and raises his arm before the attack is completed.

Chapter 21

All 9,999 of Mustafa's lions are transformed into stone statues by Crunch's seven magic words. Crunch tosses the stone lions aside while searching for the Cowardly Lion.

the lions of Mudge in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923 · the work’s page · read it where the lions of Mudge first appears

Abstract written by a model

The lions of Mudge are a group of speaking, unkillable lions kept by Mustafa in a great iron royal reservation. Mustafa collected them to distract his subjects and protect the kingdom. Their number is given as 9,999 and a half, although Mustafa calls it ten thousand; the half is the front half of an old lion cut in two by Tazzywaller and escaped. Mustafa plans to confine Notta with them and threatens Bob, while the lions roar for Bob and demand him as food.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The lions of Mudge were collected by Mustafa to distract his subjects and protect the kingdom. There are 9,999 and a half in the royal reservation; the half resulted when Tazzywaller cut the last old lion in two, after which the front half escaped.

Chapter 3

The lions are kept by Mustafa of Mudge, and Mustafa says there are ten thousand of them for Tazzywaller to feed. Notta is supposed to be locked up with them, though Mustafa expects that they may chew him up.

Chapter 4

The lions of Mudge live in a great iron enclosure and can speak. Mustafa threatens to throw Notta and Bob among them, while the lions roar for the boy and demand that he be given to them.
