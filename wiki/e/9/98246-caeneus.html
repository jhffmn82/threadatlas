Abstract written by a model

Caeneus, formerly a woman, became an invulnerable man after intercourse with Poseidon. In battle with centaurs, he killed many and ignored wounds until the survivors surrounded him and buried him in the earth with fir trees.

ES 16

Caeneus had formerly been a woman, but after intercourse with Poseidon he asked to become an invulnerable man. During the battle with the centaurs, he killed many of them while disregarding wounds, but the remaining centaurs surrounded him and buried him in the earth by striking him with fir trees.
