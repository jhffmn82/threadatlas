parents of twins

Also called: father and mother of the twins, father of twins, mother of twins

Abstract written by a model

After twins’ birth, parents remain secluded and observe rituals for two years, avoiding fresh salmon. They are credited with fertilizing or abundance-producing power, attracting salmon among the Nootkas and receiving blessing-seeking visits or offerings among the Baganda and Bateso.

1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

Parents of twins are sometimes credited with the same fertilizing or abundance-producing power as the twins. Among the Nootkas they perform rituals to attract salmon, while among the Baganda and Bateso they receive visits or offerings because their presence is believed to bring blessing and increase.
