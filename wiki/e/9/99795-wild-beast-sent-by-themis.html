Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Themis sends the wild beast against Aonian Thebes after the son of Laius solves the riddle of the mysterious propounder. It ravages cattle and people, evades the hunters and their nets, and is finally transformed into a marble statue while being pursued by Lælaps.
