Urysmag

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Urysmag, a figure in Ossete and Mingrelian tales, is captured by a one-eyed giant after trying to seize a ram and placed on a roasting spit. He frees himself, blinds the giant, kills the giant’s son, and escapes wearing a ram’s skin.

Urysmag is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

(33) A version of the tale which presents the main features

Urysmag is seized by an Ossete one-eyed giant and placed on a roasting spit. Because the spit passes between his body and clothes, he frees himself, blinds the giant with it, kills the giant’s son, and escapes disguised in the skin of a ram.
