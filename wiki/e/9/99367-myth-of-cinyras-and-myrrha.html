myth of Cinyras and Myrrha in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

A myth possibly explained as deriving from a Phoenician tradition about Noah and the curse on Ham; under Le Clerc’s interpretation, Myrrha’s transformation into a tree reflects the Arabic meaning of her name, myrrh.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Footnote 42: _The Panchæan land._--Ver. 309. Panchæa was a region

The myth is explained as possibly deriving from a Phoenician tradition concerning Noah and the curse brought upon Ham by his conduct toward his father. Le Clerc further attributes Myrrha’s transformation into a tree to the Arabic meaning of her name, “Mor,” or myrrh.
