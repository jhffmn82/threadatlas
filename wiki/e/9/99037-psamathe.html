Psamathe in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Psamathe, a Nereid and mother of Phocus, avenges his death by sending a wolf, then after refusing Peleus yields to Thetis and petrifies it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLES V. AND VI. [XI.221-409]

Psamathe sends a wolf to avenge the death of Phocus and initially refuses Peleus's entreaties to stop it. After Thetis intercedes, Psamathe turns the wolf into stone.
