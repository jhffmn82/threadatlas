Abstract written by a model

The information extractor is an LLM-based component that constructs attribute-key information pairs for each data sample. Evaluated with GPT-4, GPT-3.5, and Llama3-Chat-8b under a fixed GPT-3.5 generator, weak extraction harms performance; GPT-4 lowers privacy leakage versus GPT-3.5.

Methods

The information extractor is an LLM-based component that extracts key information related to the essential attributes for each data sample. It constructs attribute-key information pairs.

Ablation Studies

The information extractor is evaluated with GPT-4, GPT-3.5, and Llama3-Chat-8b while the data generator is fixed as GPT-3.5. Weak extraction produces poor overall performance, and GPT-4 has lower privacy leakage than GPT-3.5.
