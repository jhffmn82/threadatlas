In “Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability,” GPT-4o-mini paraphrases factual documents, generates incorrect answers and misinformation documents, and is evaluated in several retrieval-augmented generation experiments.

GPT-4o-mini in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where GPT-4o-mini first appears

Abstract written by a model

GPT-4o-mini is a language model used in experiments and fact-checking setups. It paraphrases factual documents into nine versions per query and generates nine distinct incorrect answers with three misinformation documents for each. Although used to generate plausible misinformation, it often resists doing so and may reinsert correct answers, requiring careful prompts and manual post-processing. It is evaluated in beta-prior, NQ, TQA, and HotpotQA experiments, including RA-RAG and κ-RRSS accuracy and efficiency analyses.
