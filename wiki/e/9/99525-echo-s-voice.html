Echo's voice in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Echo's voice can only repeat others' last words, answering Narcissus at first; after Echo's body wastes away and her bones become stones, it alone remains alive, heard but unseen in mountains and woods.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Echo's voice could repeat the last words spoken by others, and she used it to answer Narcissus when he called out. After her body wasted away and her bones became stones, her voice alone remained alive and continued to be heard in the mountains and woods.
