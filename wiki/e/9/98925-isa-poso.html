Isa Poso in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Isa Poso is a cold, frozen island, snow-covered and ice-bound, marked at its entrance with a warning about a dragon. Ruled by Chin Chilly the Third and inhabited by its islanders and towering snow men, it contains Enorma’s fire-lit cave and a sought palace or kingdom. It provides bear steaks before Chin Chilly and the Princess are cut off as the adventurers drift away on an iceberg.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 11

The Illustrious Island of Isa Poso is a cold, ice-bound land with snowy shores and ice-covered trees. A sign identifies it and warns visitors to beware of the dragon.

CHAPTER 12

Isa Poso is a frozen island ruled by Chin Chilly the Third. Its snow-covered land is inhabited by towering snow men, and Enorma’s presence makes the surrounding people melt. The island contains Enorma’s fire-lit cave and a palace or kingdom that Grampa and the others seek.

CHAPTER 13

Isa Poso is the island from which Chin Chilly and the Princess are separated from the adventurers. The connecting strip of land is chopped away, leaving the party marooned on the iceberg and moving away from Isa Poso.
