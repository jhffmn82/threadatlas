Born-to-be-the-Sun

Also called: Mink, the Born-to-be-the-Sun

Abstract written by a model

Born-to-be-the-Sun, the Sun’s Kwakiutl child, is mocked, taught to follow the Sun’s path, then takes the Sun’s place. He sweeps away clouds and burns the world before the Sun pursues him and throws him down through the sky.

XI.—PHAETHON AND THE CHARIOT OF THE SUN

Born-to-be-the-Sun is the Kwakiutl child of the Sun. He climbs to his father’s house by means of arrows formed into a rope, takes the Sun’s place, causes mountains, seas, and trees to burn, and is thrown down through the sky.
