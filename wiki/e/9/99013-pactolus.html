Pactolus

Also called: river, river Pactolus, the river, the river adjoining to great Sardis, the stream

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Pactolus is the river adjoining great Sardis where Midas plunges into its bubbling source, transferring his golden power to the stream and leaving gold in its sands. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the river Pactolus is where Bacchus instructs Midas to wash away his golden power and punishment, causing gold to pass into its waters and sands.
