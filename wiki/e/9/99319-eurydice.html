Eurydice

person · 4 works · 9 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: her, his own Eurydice, his wife, his wife Eurydice, newmade bride, she, wife, Εὐρυδίκην, Εὐρυδίκης
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Eurydice is Orpheus’s wife who is bitten by a serpent and dies; summoned from the newly arrived shades, she returns to the Infernal Regions when Orpheus looks back, and later walks with him in the underworld’s fields of the blessed.
  • In The Library, Eurydice is Lacedaemon’s daughter and Orpheus’s wife who dies from a snake bite and returns to Hades when Orpheus looks back.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Eurydice is described according to Lescheos and the author of the Cyprian Poems as Æneas’s wife; in another account, she dies before Orpheus, who consults an oracle of the dead about her and loses her when he turns back to look at her.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Eurydice is Orpheus’s wife who dies from a snakebite while fleeing Aristaeus, enters the underworld, briefly may leave with Orpheus, is taken back when he looks back, and reunites with him in Tartarus after his death.

Eurydice in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Eurydice, wife of Orpheus, is bitten by a serpent while walking with Naiads and dies. Summoned from newly arrived shades, she returns to the Infernal Regions when Orpheus looks back. Later, in the underworld’s fields of the blessed, Orpheus finds her and they walk together.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE TENTH.

Eurydice is bitten by a serpent while walking with Naiads and dies. She is summoned from among the newly arrived shades, but she is taken back to the underworld when Orpheus turns to look at her.

BOOK THE ELEVENTH.

Eurydice is found by Orpheus in the fields of the blessed after his ghost descends under the earth. She walks with him side by side, sometimes following him and sometimes preceding him.

Eurydice in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Orpheus’s wife dies from a snakebite while fleeing Aristaeus, enters the underworld, briefly may leave with Orpheus but is taken back when he looks back, and reunites with him in Tartarus after his death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVII

Eurydice was Orpheus's wife who died after fleeing from Aristaeus and being bitten by a snake. Pluto and Proserpine allowed her to leave the underworld with Orpheus, but she was taken away again when he looked back. After Orpheus's death, she reunited with him in Tartarus.
