Thesprotians

group · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Thesprotous, Θεσπρωτούς, Θεσπρωτοὺς, Θεσπρωτῶν

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, the Thesprotians receive Ulysses after Epirus; led by Odysseus against the Brygians, their queen Callidice offers him the kingdom. In The Library, Thesprotous is the place against which Hercules campaigns with the Calydonians; he captures a city ruled by Phylas, remains there, and sends sons to Thebes and Sardinia for colonization.
