Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The bandits surround Prince Tatters, Grampa, and Bill at their camp in the Blue Forest of Oz. Although several are knocked down, they overpower the travelers, bind them with heavy ropes, and drag them to their camp.

CHAPTER 4

The bandits capture Grampa and Tatters, shake them, and take their possessions. They accept the two as new outlaws and listen to Grampa’s account of his battles until they fall asleep. They pursue Grampa, Tatters, and Bill after Bill’s crowing, crashing through the forest like a herd of elephants.
