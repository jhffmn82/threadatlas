Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the transformation into birds is the divine transformation of Halcyone and Ceyx after the Gods pity them, preserving their love and conjugal bond. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the transformation into birds occurs when the pitying gods transform Halcyone and Ceyx into birds, allowing them to remain together and produce young.
