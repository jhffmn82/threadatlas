Tatters in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · the work’s page · read it where Tatters first appears

Abstract written by a model

Tatters is the young Prince of Ragbad and son of King Fumbo. After returning from a picnic to find his father headless, he agrees to search for Fumbo’s head, seek his own fortune, and find a Princess. Wearing a patched, many-colored rag suit and a thread-bear skin, and carrying his father’s huge red umbrella, he leaves Ragbad with Grampa. Their journey toward the Emerald City gathers Bill and later Urtha. Tatters fights bandits, is captured, escapes, falls through a hollow tree, and enters Gorba’s garden, where he helps awaken Urtha with a gold watering can. Though reluctant to leave her, he is glad when she joins the company. He helps Grampa with the magic medicine, survives falls, an underground lake, Fire Island, a volcano, and the frozen land of Isa Poso. He comforts Urtha, becomes frost-proof after taking the wizard’s medicine, and is transformed by Yaga’s tobacco into a great black crow before traveling east toward Oz. After becoming human again, Tatters meets Maribella and recognizes King Fumbo as his father. He begs Fumbo to return and carries his father’s head and the red umbrella down the rainbow. Later, Dorothy identifies him as the youth in a prophecy: Tatters, rather than a monster, is to marry the Princess of Perhaps City. He receives the yellow hen to obtain his fortune, returns to Ragbad, reunites with Mrs Sew-and-Sew, celebrates and dances with Urtha, and remains happy with her while he and Grampa have record crops.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

15 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Prince Tatters is the young Prince of Ragbad. He is away on a picnic during the storm and returns afterward, distressed and asking what will happen to the headless King.

CHAPTER 2

Prince Tatters agrees to search for King Fumbo’s head and seek his fortune. He prepares for the adventure, receives a thread-bear skin and a huge red umbrella, and leaves Ragbad with Grampa.

CHAPTER 3

Prince Tatters travels with Grampa toward the Emerald City to find a way to restore his father’s head and to seek the Princess and the fortune. He welcomes Bill into the company and fights the bandits with his red umbrella, but is eventually overpowered, bound, and dragged to their camp.

CHAPTER 4

Prince Tatters is captured with Grampa and is frightened by the bandits’ appearance, though he laughs at Grampa’s defiance. He pretends to join the outlaws, escapes with Grampa and Bill, and falls through the bottomless hollow tree while holding Bill and the red umbrella.

CHAPTER 5

Prince Tatters falls down the hollow tree with Grampa and Bill, landing unharmed in a clump of pink bushes. He helps search for a way out of the hedge, learns about Bill's golden key, and enters the Garden of Gorba with Grampa and Bill.

CHAPTER 6

Tatters enters Gorba’s garden with Grampa and Bill while seeking King Fumbo’s head, the Prince’s fortune, and a Princess. He explores the magical garden, helps free the gold watering can, and sprays Urtha with it, bringing her to life. Tatters is reluctant to leave Urtha behind and is pleased when Grampa lets her join their journey.

CHAPTER 7

Tatters helps Grampa by lighting his pipe and reading the label on the magic medicine bottle. He administers two drops to Grampa and later follows him onto Gorba’s Winding Stairway with the red umbrella.

CHAPTER 10

Tatters falls while clinging to Bill and his father’s umbrella, nearly drowns in the underground lake, and is saved when Grampa gives him the wizard’s medicine. He is frightened by the danger of burning up on Fire Island and travels with Grampa, Urtha, and Bill toward Blazes.

CHAPTER 11

Tatters travels with Grampa, Urtha, and Bill through the dark tower and volcano. He survives the eruption, explains the stars to Urtha, helps make her a pillow from the thread bear's skin, and reaches the snowy shore with the others.

CHAPTER 12

Tatters travels with Grampa, Urtha, and Bill across the frozen land of Isa Poso. He helps pursue Enorma, comforts Urtha after the dragon is put out, and shares the meat from her cave. When Grampa claims the reward for him, Tatters begins to have misgivings about the Princess of Isa Poso.

CHAPTER 13

Prince Tatters is stranded with Grampa, Urtha, and Bill on an iceberg after Chin Chilly cuts away its connection to Isa Poso. He takes the wizard's medicine, becomes protected from the cold, dances with Urtha, and later transforms into a great black crow. He joins the flight east toward Oz.

CHAPTER 15

Tatters becomes partly human and partly crow before the transformation ends, then eats plums and dried bear steak with Grampa. He meets Maribella, asks about the stars, and recognizes King Fumbo as his father. Tatters begs Fumbo to return and later carries his father's head and the red umbrella down the rainbow.

CHAPTER 20

Tatters confirms Urtha's identity as Urtha, and Dorothy identifies him as the youth described in the prophecy. The prophecy therefore indicates that Tatters, rather than a monster, will marry the Princess of Perhaps City.

CHAPTER 21

Tatters whispers cozily with Urtha about Ragbad and other important matters. He receives the yellow hen so that he can obtain its fortune.

CHAPTER 22

Tatters is reunited with Mrs Sew-and-Sew in Ragbad and dances with Urtha during the celebrations. He and Grampa later have record crops, and he remains happy with Urtha.

Prince of Ragbad in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · the work’s page · read it where Prince of Ragbad first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Prince of Ragbad, identified as Tatters despite his ragged look, is King Fumbo’s son and Ragbad’s prince. He travels with Urtha, whom he marries, while seeking his father’s detached head and carrying it in a knitting bag. He helps Dorothy and Percy Vere rescue the Princess, then marches with the company carrying the head and a red umbrella. After the Play Fellows repeatedly force him down a slide and Urtha disappears, he drops both items to search for her. He is later celebrated and happy with Urtha.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 17

Tatters is identified as the Prince of Ragbad, although his ragged appearance and untidy hair initially cause Percy Vere to doubt his identity. He carries King Fumbo's detached head in a knitting bag and agrees to help Dorothy and Percy Vere rescue the Princess.

CHAPTER 18

The Prince of Ragbad marches with the company while carrying his father's head and the red umbrella. The Play Fellows force him onto the high wooden slide repeatedly, leaving him black and blue; after Urtha disappears, he drops the head and umbrella and sets out to search for her.

CHAPTER 22

The Prince of Ragbad is celebrated with the Princess during the party, which ends with a hundred cheers for them. The text identifies Tatters as happy with Urtha but does not explicitly name him as the Prince.
