  • In The Cowardly Lion of Oz: Crunch is a roughly hewn Stone Man, also called the Stone Giant, about three times the size of an ordinary man and made from a huge block of granite. Created in the stone age to decorate a cave, he was buried there for centuries after a landslide until Wam excavated him and gave him life, brains, and magic through magic powder. He lives near Emerald City on an isolated mountain and rescues Notta Bit More and the Cowardly Lion after their fall. Crunch joins the Cowardly Lion’s journey to Mudge, grows fond of him, and promises to help him against Mustafa. Because he is stone, he never tires, travels three times as fast as an ordinary flesh-and-bone man, and cannot break his promise. He carries the lion to Mudge while intending to use a secret transformation trick to make him courageous and keep him forever. At Mudge, he allows Mustafa’s Guards to capture the lion, confronts Mustafa’s attacking lions, and turns 9,999 of them to stone. He enters the enclosure, throws aside stone lions to find the Cowardly Lion, and tries to take him away. However, after petrifying the Cowardly Lion, Glinda and the Wizard of Oz strip Crunch of his power to move, leaving him a statue.

Crunch in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Crunch is a roughly hewn Stone Man, also called the Stone Giant, about three times the size of an ordinary man and made from a huge block of granite. At Mudge, he allows Mustafa’s Guards to capture the lion, confronts Mustafa’s attacking lions, and turns 9,999 of them to stone. He enters the enclosure, throws aside stone lions to find the Cowardly Lion, and tries to take him away. However, after petrifying the Cowardly Lion, Glinda and the Wizard of Oz strip Crunch of his power to move, leaving him a statue.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The stone man is a roughly hewn man of stone who steps down from a rocky ledge after the travelers fall. He catches the rope binding Notta and the Cowardly Lion and carries them in one hand toward Nick and Bob.

Chapter 17

Crunch is a Stone Man about three times the size of an ordinary man, carved from granite by a primitive Oz man to decorate a cave. The wizard Wam brought him to life with magic powder after Crunch had been buried for centuries. Crunch decides to help the Cowardly Lion and joins him on the journey toward Mudge.

Chapter 20

Crunch develops a fondness for the Cowardly Lion but plans to use a secret magic trick to make him courageous and keep him forever. He carries the sleeping lion rapidly to Mudge, lets Mustafa's Guards capture him, and finally arrives as Mustafa's lions attack.

Chapter 21

Crunch turns the 9,999 lions to stone, enters the enclosure, and tosses the stone lions aside until he finds the Cowardly Lion. He tries to carry the Cowardly Lion away, but Glinda and the Wizard of Oz deprive him of all power to move.
