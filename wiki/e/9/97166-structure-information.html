Abstract written by a model

In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, topological information in graph-structured data is incorporated alongside textual information to help models better capture structural relationships within the graph. In Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation, structure information from an external knowledge graph is retrieved to augment recommendations and incorporated into the recommendation generation process.
