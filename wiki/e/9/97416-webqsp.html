WebQSP in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

WebQSP is a large-scale, multi-hop knowledge graph question answering dataset averaging 4,700 graphs, 1,370.89 nodes, 4,252.37 edges, and 100,627 tokens. It is evaluated with F1 Score, Hit@1, and Recall; GRAG reports 0.5022 F1, 0.7236 Hit@1, and 0.5099 Recall without LLM fine-tuning. Hit@1 is tested across increasing retrieval settings.

A. Open-Source Projects and Applications

WebQSP contains 4,737 natural language questions operating over a knowledge graph with 164.6 million facts and 24.9 million entities. The unit states that 30% of its questions require two-hop aggregation, 7% involve constraint reasoning, and 63% require single-fact retrieval; it is also used by ToG, ToG 2.0, RoG, SubgraphRAG, R-DP, and GAIL.
