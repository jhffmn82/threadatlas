Myscelos's trial

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: accusation, charge, judgment, sentence

Abstract written by a model

Myscelos is accused of holding the laws of Argos in contempt when he prepares to depart. Although every pebble initially cast into the urn is black, Hercules changes them all to white, and Myscelos is acquitted.
