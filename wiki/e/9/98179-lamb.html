Lamb in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

A golden lamb, born in a flock, appears after a vow, is hidden then given away, and determines Mycenae’s kingdom through Thyestes’ claim.

ιῖιο Ὅτι viol Πέλοπος Πιτθεὺς ᾿Ατρεὺς Θυέστης

A golden lamb appears in Atreus’s flock after his vow to Artemis. Atreus hides it in a box, but Aerope gives it to Thyestes, who uses it in the contest for the kingdom.
