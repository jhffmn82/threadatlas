Fred Porlock

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: FRED PORLOCK, Friend Porlock, Porlock, the man Porlock, the so-called Porlock

Abstract written by a model

Fred Porlock is an assumed name used by a pseudonymous correspondent and subordinate in Professor Moriarty’s criminal organization, described as its only known flawed link. He anonymously warns Sherlock Holmes that danger threatens Douglas, then sends a cipher message from which he withholds the key because Moriarty suspects him and asks Holmes to burn it. Within an hour of the warning, John Douglas is murdered. Holmes does not trace Porlock, keeping his promise of faith.

Fred Porlock is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

PART I / Chapter I

Fred Porlock sends Sherlock Holmes a cipher message warning of danger to Douglas at Birlstone. In a letter, he says that Professor Moriarty suspects him, refuses to provide the cipher key, and asks Holmes to burn the message.

Chapter II

Porlock is the assumed name of the person who sent Sherlock Holmes an anonymous warning that danger threatened a particular person. Porlock’s warning was followed within an hour by the murder of John Douglas, but Holmes has not attempted to trace Porlock because he promised to keep faith.
