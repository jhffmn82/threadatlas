1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

The third monk tells the repentant sinner that every sin can be atoned for through repentance. He prescribes digging a well in rock while the sinner’s mother holds a black sheep until it turns white, and later declares that no further expiation is needed after the sinner kills a gentleman.
