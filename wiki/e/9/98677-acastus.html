Acastus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Acastus, king of Iolcus and husband of Astydamia, purified Peleus, then after Astydamia accused Peleus, abandoned him hunting on Pelion and hid his sword; he is also reported parent of Sthenelus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII. Peleus fled to Phthia to the court of Eurytion,

Acastus purified Peleus at Iolcus after Peleus fled from Phthia. After Astydamia accused Peleus of attempting her virtue, Acastus took Peleus hunting on Pelion rather than killing the man he had purified, abandoned him while he slept, and hid his sword in cows’ dung.
