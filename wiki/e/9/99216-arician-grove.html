Arician grove

place · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: grove of Aricia

Abstract written by a model

The Arician grove was the place where Numa was said to retire in order to consult the Nymph Egeria. The Romans later searched the grove for Egeria and found only a fountain at the spot Numa had frequented.
