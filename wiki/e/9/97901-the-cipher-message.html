the cipher message in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

A secret book-cipher communication, numerically inscribed through references to words on a page, is decoded by Sherlock Holmes as warning Douglas at Birlstone of imminent danger.

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART I / Chapter I

The cipher message consists of numbered references, including the words Douglas and Birlstone. Sherlock Holmes decodes it as a warning that danger may come very soon to a rich man named Douglas at Birlstone.
