Pierus

Abstract written by a model

Pierus is a very bad poet whose injurious, idle, tiresome, and unmeaning narratives include a work on the war of the Gods with the Giants; he is father of the Pierides, whose transformation into magpies represents his narratives.

Pierus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
