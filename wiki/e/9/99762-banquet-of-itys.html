Abstract written by a model

A private banquet prepared by Progne and Philomela for Tereus serves cooked Itys; when Tereus asks for Itys, Progne reveals he is within him and Philomela throws Itys’s head.

Progne prepares a private banquet for Tereus and serves him the cooked remains of Itys. When Tereus asks for Itys, Progne tells him that what he seeks is within him, and Philomela throws Itys’s bloody head into his face.
