Latona's transformation of the rustics

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The wish of the Goddess comes to pass, the countrymen's transformation into frogs, the wonder happened, transforms them into frogs
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the countrymen's transformation into frogs occurred after they denied Latona water, insulted her, and muddied the lake; they became frogs and remained associated with the muddy stream.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Latona's transformation changed the rustics into frogs after they denied her water and polluted the pond; they retained harsh voices and lived submerged or swimming in the slimy pool.
