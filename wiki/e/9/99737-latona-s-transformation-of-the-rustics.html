Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the countrymen's transformation into frogs occurred after they denied Latona water, insulted her, and muddied the lake; they became frogs and remained associated with the muddy stream. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Latona's transformation changed the rustics into frogs after they denied her water and polluted the pond; they retained harsh voices and lived submerged or swimming in the slimy pool.
