the magic tobacco in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The magic tobacco, taken from Vaga and kept in a blue pouch, is accidentally smoked by Grampa after being forgotten in his game leg. Its smoke makes Bill vanish and turns captives into crows; each puff lasts one hour, so three puffs keep the company transformed for three hours.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The tobacco is in the blue pouch that Grampa took from Vaga and had forgotten inside his game leg. Grampa smokes it without realizing its source, and its magic causes Bill to vanish and the others to transform.

CHAPTER 15

The magic tobacco turns a company of captives into crows, with each puff lasting for one hour. Grampa accidentally smokes it instead of his own tobacco, using three puffs and causing the company to remain crows for three hours.
