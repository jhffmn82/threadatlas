  • In Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data: Synthetic data is a high-utility, privacy-preserving version of original retrieval data for retrieval-augmented generation. It is generated from important attributes and key information, then refined, including agent-based refinement, to retain essential properties and relationships while removing privacy information. Produced offline once, it is returned during inference when corresponding original data is identified, rather than exposing the original data to language models. Across evaluated representative scenarios such as healthcare, it achieves comparable performance to original retrieval context or RAG data while substantially reducing information leakage, privacy risks, and breach risks. Its effectiveness depends on generation quality: weak models can produce poor-quality data that fails to preserve original information, whereas refined data shows minimal privacy leakage even with more retrieved documents. Overall, it replaces sensitive retrieval data while balancing utility with privacy preservation.

synthetic data in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Synthetic data is a high-utility, privacy-preserving version of original retrieval data for retrieval-augmented generation. It is generated from important attributes and key information, then refined, including agent-based refinement, to retain essential properties and relationships while removing privacy information. Produced offline once, it is returned during inference when corresponding original data is identified, rather than exposing the original data to language models. Across evaluated representative scenarios such as healthcare, it achieves comparable performance to original retrieval context or RAG data while substantially reducing information leakage, privacy risks, and breach risks. Its effectiveness depends on generation quality: weak models can produce poor-quality data that fails to preserve original information, whereas refined data shows minimal privacy leakage even with more retrieved documents. Overall, it replaces sensitive retrieval data while balancing utility with privacy preservation.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Synthetic data is proposed as a privacy-preserving alternative to original retrieval data. The experiments show that using it as retrieval context achieves comparable performance to using original data while substantially reducing privacy risks.

Introduction

Synthetic data is proposed as a privacy-preserving version of original data for use in RAG. The unit describes generating it from important attributes and key information, then refining it to remove privacy information while retaining utility.

Methods

Synthetic retrieval data is generated offline and only needs to be produced once. During inference, it is returned when the corresponding original data is identified.

Ablation Studies

Synthetic data generated with weak models has poor quality and may fail to preserve information from the original data. Data after agent-based refinement shows minimal privacy leakage even when more documents are retrieved.

Conclusions

Synthetic data is investigated as a form of retrieval-augmented generation data for mitigating privacy concerns. The generated synthetic data achieves comparable performance to original data as RAG data while mitigating associated privacy issues.

Limitations

Synthetic data is investigated for use in retrieval-augmented generation. The research evaluates its effectiveness and safety in representative scenarios such as healthcare, with an emphasis on high utility and privacy preservation.

Ethic Statement

Synthetic data is generated to replace sensitive data during Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Its use is intended to mitigate privacy risks and reduce privacy breach risks.
