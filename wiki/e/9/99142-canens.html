Abstract written by a model

Canens is a Nymph, daughter of Janus and Venilia, famed for beauty and extraordinarily powerful singing. Faithful to Picus, she searches six days and nights after his disappearance, then grieves beside the Tiber until she dissolves into light air and sound.

Canens is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE VI. [XIV.320-440]

Canens is a Nymph born to Venilia and Janus, renowned for her beauty and especially for her singing, which can move woods and rocks, tame wild beasts, stop rivers, and detain birds. After Picus disappears, she searches for him for six days and nights, then grieves beside the Tiber until she pines away into light air.
