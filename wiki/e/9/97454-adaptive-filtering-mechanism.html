Abstract

The adaptive filtering mechanism dynamically adjusts the relevance filtering threshold based on score distributions. It is designed to minimize noise while maintaining high recall of relevant documents.

1 / Introduction

The adaptive filtering mechanism dynamically adjusts the relevance threshold according to the score distribution of retrieved documents. It is intended to handle diverse queries, reduce noise, and preserve relevant documents without additional training or fine-tuning.
