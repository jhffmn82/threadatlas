Abstract written by a model

The adaptive filtering mechanism dynamically adjusts the relevance threshold from retrieved-document score distributions. It handles diverse queries without additional training or fine-tuning, reducing noise while preserving high recall of relevant documents.

Abstract

1 / Introduction

The adaptive filtering mechanism dynamically adjusts the relevance threshold according to the score distribution of retrieved documents. It is intended to handle diverse queries, reduce noise, and preserve relevant documents without additional training or fine-tuning.
