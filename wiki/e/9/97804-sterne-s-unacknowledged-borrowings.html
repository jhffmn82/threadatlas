Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings, his high-handed and extensive appropriation

Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings were noted in Germany as a result of George Ferriar’s investigations in England, but they did not seriously damage Sterne’s standing among his admirers. The subject remained an unavoidable element of English discussions of Sterne and his place in literature.
