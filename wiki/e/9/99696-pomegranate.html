Abstract written by a model

A pomegranate grows on a bending tree in a finely cultivated garden, enclosing seeds within a pale rind. Proserpine plucks it and eats seven grains, causing her partial stay in the Infernal Regions; it is thereafter avoided during Ceres’ rites.
