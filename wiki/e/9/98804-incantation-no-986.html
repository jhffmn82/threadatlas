Incantation No. 986 in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

An instruction from the Green Book of Magic: used while placing the royal robe on the King's shoulders, it enables his restoration; without the robe, it causes great disaster.

CHAPTER 9

Incantation No. 986 is an instruction from the Green Book of Magic. According to the robe's label, it must be used while placing the robe on the King's shoulders, and using it without the robe would cause a great disaster.
