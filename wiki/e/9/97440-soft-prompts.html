soft prompts

object · 2 works · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: prompt embeddings, soft prompt
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, soft prompts are task-adapted prompt embeddings formed from soft-pruned subgraph representations that preserve graph connections, control message passing, and inform generation.
  • In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, soft prompts are graph-token representations of retrieved textual subgraphs generated by graph encoders to preserve textual connections and original parameters while enabling task-specific adaptations.

Introduction

Soft prompts provide graph-token representations of retrieved textual subgraphs. They are generated with graph encoders that encode graph topology directly while representing text information as node and edge attributes.
