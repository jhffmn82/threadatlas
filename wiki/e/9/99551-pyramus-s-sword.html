Pyramus's sword

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: his sword, sword, the scabbard, the sword

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Pyramus uses the sword with which he is girt to plunge into his bowels, and after he draws it from the wound, Thisbe uses the same bloodied sword to kill herself. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Pyramus uses the sword to stab himself after he believes Thisbe dead, and Thisbe later uses the same sword to kill herself.
