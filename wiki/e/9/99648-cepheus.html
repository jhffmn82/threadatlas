Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Cepheus is Andromeda’s father and king of Joppa, mourns her danger, accepts Perseus’s condition, promises a kingdom as dowry, welcomes Perseus as son-in-law and preserver of his house, and rebukes Phineus’s attack on Perseus. In The Library, Cepheus has twenty sons, rules Ethiopia, is the father of Andromeda and husband of Cassiopeia, and is forced by the Ethiopians to expose Andromeda to the sea monster. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Cepheus is king and ruler of Aethiopia and father of Andromeda, and helplessly consents to Perseus marrying her during her threat.

Cepheus in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · about AD 8 · the work’s page · read it where Cepheus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Cepheus, son of Phœnix, is Andromeda’s father and king of Joppa. With Cassiope he mourns her danger, accepts Perseus’s condition, promises a kingdom as dowry, and welcomes Perseus as son-in-law and preserver of their house. He later rebukes Phineus’s attack on Perseus, invoking justice, good faith, and hospitality; his memory and Phineus’s are honored at Joppa.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Cepheus is Andromeda's father. He and Cassiope mourn Andromeda's danger, agree to Perseus's marriage condition, promise a kingdom as her dowry, and welcome Perseus as their son-in-law and the preserver of their house.

BOOK THE FIFTH.

Cepheus rebukes Phineus for attacking Perseus and argues that Perseus earned Andromeda by saving her life. He calls the disturbance contrary to his will and invokes justice, good faith, and the gods of hospitality before leaving the house.

notes .. notes

Cepheus is identified as the father of Andromeda and, according to Pomponius Mela, as king of Joppa. The memory of Cepheus and his brother Phineus was reportedly honored at Joppa with religious services.
