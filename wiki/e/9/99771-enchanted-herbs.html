enchanted herbs

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: drug, herbs, herbs of Lethæan juice, φαρμάκῳ, φαρμακίς
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the enchanted herbs are given by Medea to Jason, whose use of them they support against the bulls’ flames and the dragon guarding the Golden Fleece.
  • In The Library, the drug is a magical substance Medea gives Jason, which protects him for one day from fire and iron when he anoints his shield, spear, and body with it.

Ovid · 0008~

Medea gives Jason enchanted herbs, teaches their use, protects him from bulls’ flames, and helps him induce sleep in the dragon guarding the Golden Fleece.
