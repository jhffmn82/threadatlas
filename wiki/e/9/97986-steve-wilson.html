Steve Wilson in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Steve Wilson is Birdy Edwards’s undercover alias, lodging at Hobson’s Patch, posing as a reporter, gathering Scowrers information from McMurdo, and sending coded telegraphs.

Chapter VI

Steve Wilson is the name under which Birdy Edwards is operating. He met McMurdo on the cars, claimed to be a newspaper reporter, questioned him about the Scowrers, paid him for information, and sent coded material through the telegraph bureau.
