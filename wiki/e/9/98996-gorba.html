Gorba in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Gorba is a wizard, also called Abrog, who owns the enchanted Garden of Gorba and illegally practices magic there. His name appears on the garden sign, a winding stairway, and gold-lettered patent medicine. While he is away, Tatters, Grampa, and Bill encounter his magical garden, including stepping stones, animal-shaped bushes, Urtha, and a gold watering can. The garden predicts his return at twelve, and later Gorba’s name reveals Abrog’s identity.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

Gorba is named on a sign identifying the Garden of Gorba. The name also appears in gold letters on the wizard's patent medicine beside the list of ailments.

CHAPTER 6

Gorba is identified as the wizard who owns the enchanted garden and practices magic against the law. His garden contains magical stepping stones, animal-shaped bushes, and the flower maiden Urtha. He is apparently away when Tatters, Grampa, and Bill find Urtha and the gold watering can.

CHAPTER 7

The flower bed announces that Gorba will return to the garden at twelve o’clock. Gorba’s name also appears on the winding stairway found by Grampa.

CHAPTER 20

Gorba is revealed to be Abrog's name spelled backwards. The label on the wizard's medicine bears the name “Gorba,” helping Dorothy identify Abrog as the wizard.
