Stage-2 in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Stage-2 is an agent-based interactive refinement process in a novel two-stage synthetic pipeline. After Stage-1 produces synthetic data, Stage-2 iteratively evaluates and rewrites it to remove privacy risks while maintaining utility, including eliminating structured personally identifiable information to mitigate targeted attacks. Its final outputs are described as containing pure useful information, with zero or near-zero attack counts and performance comparable to or better than original data on several ODQA datasets. In evaluation on HealthcareMagic, it has BLEU-1 of 0.113 and ROUGE-L of 0.0943. The pipeline combines attribute-based generation with this refinement, but needs validation across wider domains and datasets.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Methods

Stage-2 refines Stage-1 synthetic data to mitigate privacy concerns. It uses iterative evaluation and rewriting to remove privacy risks while maintaining utility.

Experiment

The complete SAGE pipeline produces the outputs reported as Stage-2. On the HealthcareMagic dataset, Stage-2 obtains BLEU-1 of 0.113 and ROUGE-L of 0.0943.

Utility of using synthetic data

Stage-2 further refines the data produced in the pipeline, yielding outputs described as containing more purely useful information. It achieves performance comparable to or better than original data on several ODQA datasets and has zero or near-zero attack counts.

Conclusions

The two-stage synthetic pipeline combines attribute-based data generation with iterative agent-based refinement. It is proposed to maintain key information while further enhancing data privacy.

Limitations

The pipeline generates synthetic data intended to have high utility and preserve privacy. Its effectiveness and safety are verified in representative scenarios, while broader validation across domains and datasets is left for future work.

agent-based iterative refinement process in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Agent-based iterative refinement is the second component of a proposed two-stage synthetic pipeline. A privacy agent and a re-writing agent collaborate iteratively until the privacy agent determines the synthetic data is safe. It further reduces potentially privacy-violating information and privacy leakage, producing high-safety data; untargeted attack success is nearly absent, targeted success decreases to nearly zero, and privacy remains consistently minimal as retrieved documents increase.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Methods

Agent-based interactive refinement uses a privacy agent and a re-writing agent that collaborate iteratively. The process continues until the privacy agent determines that the synthetic data is safe.

Privacy of using synthetic data

The agent-based refinement process further reduces potentially privacy-violating information in the synthetic data. After refinement, untargeted attack success is nearly absent and targeted attack success decreases to nearly zero.

Ablation Studies

Agent-based refinement reduces privacy leakage in the retrieved data. Its privacy performance remains consistently minimal as the number of retrieved documents increases.

Conclusions

Iterative agent-based refinement is the second component of the proposed two-stage synthetic pipeline. It further enhances the privacy of the data.
