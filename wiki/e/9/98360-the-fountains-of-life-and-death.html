Also called: the fountains of the water of life and the water of death, the two waters

Abstract written by a model

The fountains of life and death, two waters at the bottom of mountains that knock together, are reached by the stork during noontide rest and bottled.

The fountains of life and death lie at the bottom of the mountains that knock against one another. The stork reaches them during the mountains’ noontide rest and fills two bottles.
