the dummy's awakening in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Humpy, a dummy awakened after Dorothy wishes him alive, realizes he can move independently, rises and walks, then becomes curious and uneasy about what to do as a living dummy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

The dummy first pretends to be dead, but Dorothy’s rescue and conversation cause him to realize that he is alive. He pinches himself, rises, walks with a stick, and becomes curious and uneasy about what he should do as a living dummy.
