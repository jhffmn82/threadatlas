Phaëton's fall

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Phaëton's fate, appear to fall, falls headlong, fate, his fate, is hurled headlong

Abstract written by a model

Phaëton is hurled headlong and carried in a long tract through the air, like a star that appears to fall from a clear sky. Eridanus receives him in a distant part of the world. Phaëton’s fate caused his mother and sisters great grief. Their grief was said to have led to their deaths and transformation.
