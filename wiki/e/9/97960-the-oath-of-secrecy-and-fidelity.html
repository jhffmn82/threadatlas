the oath of secrecy and fidelity in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

An oath binds Jack McMurdo to secrecy about lodge business, threatening instant inevitable death for breach; he later tells Ettie Shafter it forbids disclosure, abandonment of comrades, or leaving with secrets.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

McMurdo tells Ettie Shafter that he is under oath not to reveal the lodge’s business, even to her. He says that breaking the oath would mean breaking his word and deserting his comrades, and he argues that the lodge would not let a man leave with its secrets.
