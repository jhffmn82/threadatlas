Atalanta's race

event · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Atalanta and Hippomenes's race, Atalanta's Race, attempt, course, race, that race, the contest, the contest of speed, the last course, the race, the usual race, this unreasonable race, δρόμον
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the race is a footrace between Atalanta and her suitors in which Hippomenes uses Venus's golden apples to win and marry her.
  • In The Library, Atalanta's race is a contest in which wooers race against Atalanta for the right to marry her; Melanion wins by using golden apples.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Atalanta and Hippomenes's race is a contest for marriage that Hippomenes wins with three golden apples, though the lovers are later transformed into lions after offending Cybele.

the race in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

The race is a footrace between Atalanta and her suitors: a suitor must outrun her to win marriage, while a slower contestant dies. Hippomenes competes, and Venus causes Atalanta to stop repeatedly for golden apples, allowing him to win and marry her.

FABLE IX. [X.519-707]

The race requires Atalanta's suitor to outrun her in order to win her as a wife, while the slower contestant receives death. Hippomenes wins after Venus causes Atalanta to stop repeatedly for the golden apples.

requires wooers [to race against Atalanta for the right to marry her] Atalanta's wooers raced from a starting point while she pursued them in armor. A wooer who was caught was to die, while one who escaped was to marry her; after many had died, Melanion won by using golden apples.

Atalanta and Hippomenes's race in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

is_a contest for marriage [the winner receives Atalanta and losers die] Atalanta and Hippomenes's race determines whether Hippomenes can marry Atalanta, since defeated suitors must die. Hippomenes wins by using three golden apples, but after the lovers fail to honor Venus, they offend Cybele and are transformed into lions.
