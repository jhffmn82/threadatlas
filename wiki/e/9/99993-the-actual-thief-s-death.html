Abstract written by a model

The actual thief dies miserably while trying to escape along the road above, falling down the mountain. His death, linked to recovery of the money, leaves Edward Brice able to reclaim it without contradiction. Brice credits guards and uncle Harry, but Flora disputes this and says she buried the body to hide it from Snapshot Harry's gang.
