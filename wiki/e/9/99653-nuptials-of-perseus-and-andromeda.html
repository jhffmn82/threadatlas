Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the nuptials follow Perseus's rescue of Andromeda without a dowry and are celebrated with Hymenaeus, Cupid, music, perfumes, garlands, and a feast. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the wedding feast celebrates Perseus and Andromeda but is interrupted by Phineus and his armed followers, whom Perseus turns to stone with Medusa's head.
