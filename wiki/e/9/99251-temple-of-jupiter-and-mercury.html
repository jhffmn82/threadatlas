  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it was the transformed cottage that became a temple cared for by Philemon and Baucis, who served as its priest and priestess for the rest of their lives.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it was formed from Baucis and Philemon's cottage after the village was punished, and they became its priests, guardians, and keepers for the rest of their lives.
