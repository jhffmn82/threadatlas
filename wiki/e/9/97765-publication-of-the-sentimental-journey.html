Abstract written by a model

The publication of the Sentimental Journey was an event in London on February 27, 1768, less than three weeks before Laurence Sterne’s death. It rapidly reached Germany, where competing translations, extensive discussion, wide popularity, and recognition of Sterne as a literary leader followed. The publication connected Sterne with German literary impulses and emotional experiences and renewed German curiosity about the previously incomprehensible Tristram Shandy. Its influence also appeared in Pankraz’s sentimental journey, which repeats Sterne-derived motifs before turning into a practical joke against him as Pankraz slavishly treats his own disasters as parallels to Yorick’s experiences.
