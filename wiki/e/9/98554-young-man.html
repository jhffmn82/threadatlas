young man in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

A young man returns from La Vendée wealthy, enters a one-eyed giant’s hut, blinds him, escapes hidden under a sheep, then sells it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

(14) A Finnish tale of the same general type, but lacking

The young man in the Breton version encounters a one-eyed giant who keeps human flesh in his hut. He shoots out the giant’s eye, hides among the sheep, and escapes beneath one of them before selling the sheep in the market.
