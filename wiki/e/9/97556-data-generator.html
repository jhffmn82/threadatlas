Abstract written by a model

An LLM-based data generator receives attribute-information pairs and creates synthetic data expected to retain extracted key information. With GPT-3.5 fixed for extraction, it is evaluated using GPT-4, GPT-3.5, and Llama3-Chat-8b; Llama3-Chat-8b preserves little information, yielding poor utility and no privacy leakage.

Methods

The data generator is an LLM-based component that receives attribute-information pairs and generates new synthetic data. The generated data is expected to include the extracted key information.

Ablation Studies

The data generator is evaluated with GPT-4, GPT-3.5, and Llama3-Chat-8b while the information extractor is fixed as GPT-3.5. Llama3-Chat-8b produces poor utility and no privacy leakage because its generated data fails to preserve information from the original data.
