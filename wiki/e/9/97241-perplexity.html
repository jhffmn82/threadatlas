Also called: PPL, perplexity differentials

Abstract written by a model

Perplexity measures model confidence, with lower values generally linked to higher accuracy. It compares rationales generated with and without retrieved documents; reduced perplexity suggests snippets improve answers and confidence. Differences quantify document impact, and the top 25% are used to label training data for rationale-guided filtering.

Introduction

Perplexity differences between rationales generated with and without retrieved documents are used to create labels for training the rationale-guided filtering model. Reduced perplexity indicates that incorporating retrieved snippets may help the model answer correctly and with higher confidence.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.4

Perplexity is used to measure how retrieved documents affect the model’s confidence. The document impact is calculated as the difference between perplexity without the document and perplexity with the document, with the top 25% of differentials used for labeling.
