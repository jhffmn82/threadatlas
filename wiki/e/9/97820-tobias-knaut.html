Tobias Knaut

Abstract written by a model

Tobias Knaut is a humorous eighteenth-century novel, probably the best-known German novel to adopt the style of Tristram Shandy. Beginning ab ovo, it unfolds more extensively through episodes and digressions, readerly addresses, blank pages, mock scholarship, fragments, and typographical play. It treats great events as fortuitously dependent on insignificant details, reflects Henry Fielding and Laurence Sterne, satirizes Empfindsamkeit and originality, and includes Original, whose poems are lost documents.

[69] Probably the best known novel which adopts in considerable measure the styl

Tobias Knaut, or Lebensgeschichte Tobias Knauts des Weisen sonst Stammler genannt, aus Familiennachrichten gesammelt, is described as probably the best-known German novel adopting the style of Tristram Shandy. It imitates Sterne in its digressions, readerly addresses, blank pages, mock scholarship, interrupted fragments, typographical play, and treatment of accidental events, while also satirizing Empfindsamkeit and the cult of originality.
