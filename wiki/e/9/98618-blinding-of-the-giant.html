(22) If the Homeric story of Ulysses and Polyphemus

It is one of the most characteristic incidents of the story type. The giants or Cyclopes are commonly blinded with a heated iron, stake, spit, firebrand, or coal, although the Psara version omits it and the Syrian version restores the giant’s sight instead.

(32) A story resembling the Homeric tale of Ulysses and

The blinding of the giant occurs on the third evening, when one of the travelers thrusts a red-hot spit into his eye. The blinded giant shrieks while the travelers run toward the sea.
