CHAPTER 15

Maribella is a sky shepherdess who tends the baby stars and keeps them from falling out of the Milky Way. She tells the company that there are many heads in the clouds and leads them to King Fumbo's head. She lends them her pink knitting bag and shows them the rainbow they can use to descend.
