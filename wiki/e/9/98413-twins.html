twins

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a twin, the twins

Abstract written by a model

Twins are associated in the unit with powers of fertility, abundance, and attraction, especially over animals, salmon, plants, crops, and seed. In other traditions they are treated as dangerous or unlucky, and their birth can be believed to cause drought, famine, crop failure, disease, or cattle deaths.
