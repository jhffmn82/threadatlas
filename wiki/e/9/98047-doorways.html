Doorways in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

The Kingdom of Doorways is a singular place enclosed by high, wide gray walls on a high hill. Its seven heavy oak doors include an Out door, and travelers are warned to use the right one. During Notta's journey to Oz, Notta and Bob Up escape through Out, slide down a dark passageway, and reach a dusty road.
