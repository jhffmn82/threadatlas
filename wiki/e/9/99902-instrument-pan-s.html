instrument (Pan's)

Also called: an instrument with seven pipes, instrument, the panpipes, this mode of converse

Abstract written by a model

Pan's instrument has seven unequal reeds, joined with wax, named after Syrinx; Pan makes it after wind murmurs sweetly among reeds, possibly from Ladon's banks.

instrument (Pan's) is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

The panpipes are made from unequal reeds joined together with wax. Pan makes them after hearing the wind produce a murmuring, sweet sound among the reeds, and they retain Syrinx's name.
