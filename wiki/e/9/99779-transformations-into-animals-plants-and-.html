Abstract written by a model

This topic concerns explanatory accounts of transformations into animals, plants, and natural features. It links them chiefly to escape, cave hiding, grief, getting lost, name resemblance, and historical events, ranging across many forms and ending with Medea’s dragons possibly deriving from her ship’s name, the Dragon.

The notes connect many transformations with escapes from danger, hiding in caves, excessive grief, losing oneself in a wood, resemblance of names, or historical events. They mention people or beings transformed into a scarabæus, bird, dragon or serpent, stag, dog, cows, rocks, lake, eagle, osprey, kingfisher, dove, daw, fox, swan, crow, horned beetle, bitch, and linden-tree. They also explain that Medea’s dragons may derive from the name of her ship, called the Dragon.
