Abstract written by a model

Flyaboutabus is the royal flying bus and the only way to leave Un. An immense hollowed-out goose-shaped machine, it has seats, feather wheels, a steering wheel that turns the goose head, a front lever, and buttons controlling speed, direction, altitude, stopping, and landing. Tied to a tree near the palace, it is seized by Notta, Bob, the Cowardly Lion, and Nickadoodle; when its rope is cut, it rises, and Nick uses the loose lever to steady it though they do not know how to descend. It later lands in a field, then flies over the Munchkin country until a storm fills it with water, slows its wheels, and causes a crash. Drained and dried, it continues toward the strange city. In the Preservatory it breaks a Preserve's jar, its controls are crushed by Imperial Squawmos, and only the Up! button remains usable. It rises, strikes a small day star, and hangs from it by a feather wheel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

The royal Flyaboutabus is tied to a tree near the palace and is the only way for the travelers to leave Un. Notta, Bob, and the Cowardly Lion seize it while the Uns are wishing, and Nickadoodle cuts its rope so it rises into the air. Nick steadies it with a loose lever, but the travelers still do not know how to bring it down.

Chapter 13

The Flyaboutabus is an immense hollowed-out goose-shaped flying machine with seats, feather wheels, steering controls, and buttons for adjusting its speed, altitude, and landing. Notta and Nickadoodle operate it during their escape from Un, land it in a field, and tie it to a tree before searching for breakfast.

Chapter 14

The Flyaboutabus flies over the Munchkin country, but the storm fills it with water and slows its feather wheels until it crashes to the ground. After the travelers turn it on its side to drain and dry it, it carries them toward the strange city, bouncing because its wheels cannot stay on the ground.

Chapter 16

The Flyaboutabus carries the travelers into the Preservatory, where it accidentally breaks a Preserve's glass jar. Its steering gear and most of its controls are crushed by the Imperial Squawmos, but its upward button still works. It rises into the sky, crashes into a small day star, and hangs there by its feather wheel.
