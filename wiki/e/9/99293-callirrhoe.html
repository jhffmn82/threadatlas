Callirrhoe

Also called: Calirrhoë, Calirrhoë, or Arsinoë, Calirrhoë, the daughter of Acheloüs, Καλλερρόην, Καλλιρρόη
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Calirrhoë, daughter of Acheloüs and mother of Amphiterus and Acarnanus, begs Jupiter to mature her infant sons; Themis foretells that Hebe’s gift will make them men while still children.
  • In The Library, Callirrhoe, daughter of Achelous, becomes Alcmaeon’s wife after his purification by Achelous, demands the necklace and robe, and after Alcmaeon’s death asks that her sons by Alcmaeon become full-grown so that they can avenge him.

Calirrhoë, daughter of Acheloüs and mother of Amphiterus and Acarnanus, begs Jupiter to mature her infant sons; Themis foretells Hebe’s gift will make them men while still children.

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE IV. [IX.324-425]

Calirrhoë, the daughter of Acheloüs, will suppliantly ask mighty Jupiter to give years of maturity to her infant sons. Themis says that Jupiter will prescribe Hebe's gift for them and make them men while they are still children.
