Abstract written by a model

Lorenzodosen are horn snuff-boxes associated with Yorick and Franciscan Lorenzo. Marked “Pater Lorenzo” outside and “Yorick” on the open lid, they commemorate an emotional response to Sterne’s story and express a sentimental creed: gentleness and restraint of anger. Manufactured and sold commercially, they became a fad among teary sentimental enthusiasts, circulating in Germany and beyond. One Lorenzodose, sent by J . . . to the author of M . . . R . . ., repeatedly restores the author’s sentimental focus and prompts reflections on conduct toward the postman. Lichtenberg’s writings make it an object of ridicule.

Lorenzodosen is in one work on this site, Laurence Sterne in Germany. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
