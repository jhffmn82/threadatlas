Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Lorenzodosen are horn snuff-boxes associated with Yorick and Franciscan Lorenzo. Marked “Pater Lorenzo” outside and “Yorick” on the open lid, they commemorate an emotional response to Sterne’s story and express a sentimental creed: gentleness and restraint of anger. Manufactured and sold commercially, they became a fad among teary sentimental enthusiasts, circulating in Germany and beyond. One Lorenzodose, sent by J . . . to the author of M . . . ] CHAPTER V STERNE’S INFLUENCE IN GERMANY Thus in manifold ways Sterne was introduced into German life and letters.

Lorenzodosen were horn snuff-boxes bearing “Pater Lorenzo” on the outside and “Yorick” within, created to commemorate the emotional response to Sterne’s story of Franciscan Lorenzo. They became a widespread fad, were manufactured and sold commercially, and were associated with an oath to practice gentleness and restrain anger.

[54] Another work in which sentimentalism has dwindled away to a grinning shade,

The Lorenzodose is a snuff-box sent to the author by J . . . on June 29th. It repeatedly restores the author’s sentimental focus, bears the letters of the word Yorick on its lid, and is linked to the author’s reflections on his conduct toward the postman.
