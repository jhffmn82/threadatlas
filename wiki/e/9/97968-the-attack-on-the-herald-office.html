the attack on the Herald office in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The attack on the Herald office is a lodge-ordered warning against James Stanger for criticizing the lodge. At McGinty's direction, Ted Baldwin organizes several members to beat Stanger in the Vermissa Herald office. McMurdo prevents Baldwin from killing him, and the attackers escape using an alibi at the Union Bar. Stanger suffers severe head injuries; arrests follow, but uncertain witnesses prevent identification.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

When the drinks which followed the ceremony of initiation had all been

The attack on James Stanger is organized by Ted Baldwin at McGinty's direction after the lodge discusses Stanger's criticism. The attackers beat Stanger in the Vermissa Herald office, and McMurdo intervenes when Baldwin appears likely to kill him. The group then escapes through the streets, relying on an alibi that places them in the Union Bar.

Chapter IV

The attack on the Herald office seriously injures Editor Stanger and leads to the arrest of McMurdo and several other suspected Scowrers. The prosecution cannot establish their identities because the witnesses were confused and the lighting was uncertain.
