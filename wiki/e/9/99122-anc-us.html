Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE IV. [VIII.260-546]

Ancæus boasts that men’s weapons are superior to women’s and attacks the boar with a battle-axe. The boar tears open his groin and kills him, and Meleager later regards Ancæus’s wounds as a happier death than his own.
