Abstract written by a model

Ancæus, a boastful Arcadian hunter, claims men’s weapons surpass women’s, attacks the Calydonian boar with a battle-axe, and is fatally gored through the groin.

Ancæus is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FABLE IV. [VIII.260-546]

Ancæus boasts that men’s weapons are superior to women’s and attacks the boar with a battle-axe. The boar tears open his groin and kills him, and Meleager later regards Ancæus’s wounds as a happier death than his own.
