the cats of Catty Corners in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Catty Corners' cats, called Maltesers by the Catty Queen, serve as guards in her garden and at its summer-house prison. They seize Pajuka, Mombi, and Snip, carry them through the city to the queen, and confine Snip and Pajuka. While preventing escape, they eat Mombi's pudding, fly upward, and fall with rain.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Cat guards seize Pajuka, Mombi, and Snip when they enter Catty Corners. They carry the prisoners through the city along fences and bring them before the cat queen. After her command, they drag Snip and Pajuka away to be kept until wanted.

CHAPTER 7

The cats guard the summer house and surround Pajuka and Snip so they cannot escape. They eat Mombi's rice cream pudding and are sent sailing into the air until rain brings them down.
