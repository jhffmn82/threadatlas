Midas's barber

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Midas's hairdresser, barber, hairdresser, servant, servant, who was wont to cut his hair

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Midas's barber is the servant who cuts Midas's hair, discovers his ass's ears, and whispers the secret into a covered hole. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Midas's hairdresser learns the secret of the king's ass ears, is threatened with severe punishment if he reveals it, and whispers it into a hole in a meadow before covering it.

FABLE III. [XI.146-193]

Midas's barber discovers the ass's ears while cutting Midas's hair. Unable either to reveal the secret openly or keep it to himself, he whispers it into a hole in the ground and covers it again.
