graph traversal

process · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: graph search, traversal

Abstract written by a model

Graph traversal uses LLM reasoning and pruning across multiple hops, moving from indirectly relevant passages toward truly relevant ones; more hops improve retrieval but raise LLM calls, cost, and latency, with four hops sufficient to explore local graph areas.
