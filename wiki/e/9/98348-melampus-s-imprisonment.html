Melampus's imprisonment in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Melampus's imprisonment at Phylacus's house lasts nearly a year, during which he is kept in bonds with two attendants, one kind and one cruel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IV.—MELAMPUS AND THE KINE OF PHYLACUS

Melampus was kept in bonds for nearly a year with two attendants, a man and a woman. The man treated him kindly, while the woman treated him badly.
