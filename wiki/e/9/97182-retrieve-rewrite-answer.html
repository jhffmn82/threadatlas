Abstract written by a model

Retrieve-Rewrite-Answer is an inference-only method proposed by Wu et al. that retrieves knowledge-graph sub-graphs, converts them into text for recommendation, and has limited accuracy and slower reported efficiency than K-RagRec.

Retrieve-Rewrite-Answer is in one work on this site, Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
