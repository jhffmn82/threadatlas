Gram determinant in SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zijun Yao · 2025

A regularized Gram determinant measures consistency among hidden-state representations. Computed through 20 pseudo-generations, with 10–25 generations best, SEAKR uses its value as self-aware uncertainty to guide retrieval, knowledge selection, and final-answer strategy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3 .. 3.4

The regularized Gram determinant is used by SEAKR as a self-aware uncertainty measure. It evaluates the consistency of sampled internal representations, with the resulting value used to decide retrieval, select knowledge, and choose a final-answer strategy.
