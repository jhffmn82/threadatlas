Gram determinant

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: regularized Gram determinant

Abstract written by a model

A regularized Gram determinant measures consistency among hidden-state representations. Computed through 20 pseudo-generations, with 10–25 generations best, SEAKR uses its value as self-aware uncertainty to guide retrieval, knowledge selection, and final-answer strategy.
