Bodymaster McGinty is a name Mr. Douglas repeatedly spoke with anger and horror while delirious from fever. Mrs. Douglas believes there is a connection between him and the Valley of Fear.

PART II / Chapter I

Jack McGinty, also called Black Jack McGinty, is the Bodymaster of Vermissa Lodge and the boss of the Union House. McMurdo is told that nothing can happen in the area unless McGinty wants it and that he should seek McGinty if he is in trouble. A miner warns McMurdo not to mention McGinty in connection with the Scowrers.

Chapter II

Boss McGinty is the head of the local lodge and is called the Bodymaster. Scanlan warns McMurdo that McGinty will hate him if he does not report, and Ettie urges McMurdo to make McGinty his friend before the Scowrers act against him.

Boss McGinty is the feared owner of McGinty's saloon, a municipal councillor, a commissioner of roads, and the Bodymaster of Lodge 341. He tests McMurdo at gunpoint, accepts his criminal past and counterfeit dollars as evidence of his usefulness, and prevents Ted Baldwin from continuing their quarrel. He orders McMurdo to attend Lodge 341's Saturday meeting and warns him that lodge discipline is severe.

Chapter III

McGinty runs the saloon where McMurdo becomes acquainted with the gang and serves as the Bodymaster of Lodge 341, Vermissa. He disputes Captain Marvin’s role as a representative of the mine owners and conducts McMurdo’s lodge initiation.

When the drinks which followed the ceremony of initiation had all been

McGinty presides over the lodge meeting and assigns volunteers to carry out a violent job for Merton County Lodge. He defends the lodge's power, threatens Brother Morris, and orders Ted Baldwin to give James Stanger a severe warning rather than kill him. He also claims that the lodge can put two hundred men into the town and that the police and courts cannot effectively oppose it.

Chapter IV

Boss McGinty visits McMurdo to question him about his meeting with Brother Morris and warns him not to associate with Morris, whom the lodge considers disloyal. He provides McMurdo with an alibi through six citizens, including himself, and promises to arrange his legal defense.

Chapter V

Boss McGinty views McMurdo as an unusually capable instrument of the lodge and assigns him to important violent work. He celebrates the Crow Hill and Gilmerton killings with the lodge and then orders McMurdo to kill Chester Wilcox, chief foreman of the Iron Dike Company.

Chapter VI

McGinty remains Bodymaster and dismisses reports that the Scowrers are threatened. At the lodge meeting, he accepts McMurdo’s warning about Birdy Edwards and agrees to the plan to bring Edwards to Widow MacNamara’s house.

Chapter VII

John McGinty helps plan the capture and killing of the supposed detective, whom he believes is McMurdo’s informant. He is trapped with the other Scowrers, arrested by Captain Marvin, and later executed on the scaffold after the trial.
