Abstract written by a model

Ragbad is a small, shabby kingdom in Oz’s southwestern Quadling country, ruled by King Fumbo and home to Prince Tatters. Under Fumbo, its treasury, population, army, nobility, and textile production decline until only twenty-seven families and a few retainers remain. Its old red castle, surrounding red country, pigeons, Redsmith, Pudge’s tower, and Mrs Sew-and-Sew’s garden matter to Tatters, who leaves with Grampa and longs to return. The people gather at the castle to offer help and farewell them. During their travels, Grampa seeks a reward in Tatters’s name to aid their return, while Fumbo promises Bill a perch on Ragbad’s highest tower. Later Dorothy and her companions are transported there. Gold bricks produced by the yellow hen and sold by twenty-four rustic laborers make Ragbad prosperous, and Mrs Sew-and-Sew becomes Queen of Ragbad again.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Ragbad is a small kingdom in Oz, located in the southwestern corner of the Quadling country. Its treasury, population, army, nobility, and textile production have declined badly under King Fumbo, leaving only twenty-seven families and a few retainers.

CHAPTER 2

Ragbad’s people come to the castle to offer help after learning of King Fumbo’s plight. Twenty-seven good men and the children of Ragbad farewell Prince Tatters and Grampa as they leave.

CHAPTER 3

Ragbad is Prince Tatters’s shabby little kingdom and the place from which he and Grampa begin their journey. Its national air is shouted by Grampa, and its red national color appears in its surrounding country.

CHAPTER 12

Ragbad is the kingdom associated with Prince Tatters. Grampa claims the reward in Tatters’s name and hopes that the reward will help them return to Ragbad after finding the King’s head.

CHAPTER 13

Ragbad is Tatters' home, which he misses while stranded on the iceberg. He talks to Urtha about its pigeons, the Redsmith, Pudge's tower, the old castle, and Mrs Sew-and-Sew's garden, and hopes to return there with Grampa.

CHAPTER 17

Ragbad is the country associated with Tatters and King Fumbo. Grampa's party traveled from Ragbad, and King Fumbo promises that Bill will receive a perch on Ragbad's highest tower.

CHAPTER 21

Ragbad is mentioned while Tatters and Urtha whisper about important matters. Peer Haps welcomes Fumbo as the King of Ragbad.

CHAPTER 22

Ragbad receives Dorothy and her companions after Ozma transports them there. The kingdom becomes prosperous when the twenty-four rustic laborers sell the gold bricks produced by the yellow hen, and Mrs Sew-and-Sew becomes Queen of Ragbad again.
