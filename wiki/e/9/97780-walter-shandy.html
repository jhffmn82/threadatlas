Abstract written by a model

Walter Shandy is a visitor to M’lle Gillet in Bode’s alteration of Vendredi-Saint and an advocate of the favorite contention that great events depend fortuitously on insignificant details. He is represented through this theory and the importance of Christian names; these ideas were adopted in German fiction, echoed in Tobias Knaut, and later cited in Der Roman meines Lebens.

Walter Shandy is represented through theories about the dependence of great events on trifles and the importance of Christian names. Tobias Knaut echoes these ideas in stories involving a volume of Tacitus, Tobias’s hat, and the later citation of Walter Shandy’s theory in Adolph von Knigge’s Der Roman meines Lebens.
