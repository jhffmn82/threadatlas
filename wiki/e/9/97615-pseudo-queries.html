pseudo-queries in HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Hao Liu, Zhengren Wang, Xi Chen, Zhiyu Li, Feiyu Xiong, Qinhan Yu, Wentao Zhang · 2025

Pseudo-queries are generated by query simulation and edge merging to connect passages in HopRAG's index. For each passage, they belong to outgoing or incoming groups; their keywords and semantic vectors match passages, construct edges, link raiser and solver passages, and guide logical retrieval hops.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

Pseudo-queries are generated through query simulation and edge merging operations to connect passages in HopRAG's index. They serve as questions or pathways that link raiser and solver passages and guide logical hops during retrieval.

3 / Method .. Algorithm

A pseudo-query is generated for a passage and belongs to either an out-coming or in-coming group. Its keywords and semantic vector are extracted for matching passages and constructing edges.
