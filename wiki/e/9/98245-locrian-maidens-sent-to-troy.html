Locrian maidens sent to Troy

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: suppliants, δύο παρθένους, ἱκέτιδας

Abstract written by a model

Locrian maidens were sent to Troy in pairs as suppliants to serve and remain within Athena’s sanctuary at Ilium. Beginning with Periboea and Cleopatra, replacements followed after deaths; later infants with nurses were sent, until the practice ended after a thousand years.

The first two maidens were Periboea and Cleopatra, chosen by lot and sent to Troy as suppliants for a thousand years. After the first maidens died, others were sent; later, infants with nurses were sent, and the practice ended after a thousand years following the Phocian War.
