In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, knowledge graph question answering is evaluated primarily with WebQSP using F1 Score, Hit@1, and Recall; GRAG achieves the strongest reported performance among the compared methods without LLM fine-tuning. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, knowledge graph question answering is represented by the WebQSP dataset as one of the graph-related tasks to which LLMs are applied, with F1 Score, Hit@1, and Recall measuring performance. In SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Knowledge Graph Question Answering is evaluated using MetaQA, PathQuestions, and WorldCup2014 across one-hop, two-hop, and three-hop queries, with comparisons among supervised methods, pre-trained LLMs, and KG-driven retrieval-augmented generation approaches.
