Bode’s additions and alterations

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Bode’s direct additions, the divergences from the original, the verbal changes

Abstract written by a model

Bode’s additions and alterations are a divergence from the original whose exact extent is unstated. They include substantial fiction, added episodes and digressions, explanations, and other material, occupying about thirty-three of 142 first-volume pages and over fifty second-volume pages. They also alter or omit passages to reduce indecency and shift M’lle Laborde’s visit from Yorick to Walter Shandy.

Bode’s additions and alterations is in one work on this site, Laurence Sterne in Germany. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
