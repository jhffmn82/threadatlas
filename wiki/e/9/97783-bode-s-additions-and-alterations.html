Bode’s additions and alterations in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Bode’s additions and alterations are a divergence from the original whose exact extent is unstated. They include substantial fiction, added episodes and digressions, explanations, and other material, occupying about thirty-three of 142 first-volume pages and over fifty second-volume pages. They also alter or omit passages to reduce indecency and shift M’lle Laborde’s visit from Yorick to Walter Shandy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Bode’s translation of the third and fourth volumes of Yorick’s Journey,[38] that

Bode’s additions and alterations include Das Mündel, Das Hündchen, Die Moral, Die Erklärung, Vom Gesundheitstrinken, and an episode concerning Yorick’s shooting of a sparrow. He also changed or omitted passages to reduce indecency and transferred the visit to M’lle Laborde from Yorick to Walter Shandy.
