Der Empfindsame in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Der Empfindsame is a four-volume novel, anonymously published in Erfurt from 1781 to 1783 and later acknowledged by Christian Friedrich Timme. It unfolds as an extensive but uneven satire of followers and imitators of Werther, Siegwart, and Sterne.

Der Empfindsame is a four-volume novel published anonymously in Erfurt from 1781 to 1783 and later acknowledged by Christian Friedrich Timme. It satirizes the followers and imitators of Werther, Siegwart, and Sterne, although its great length and Timme’s narrative interests weaken the consistency of the satire.
