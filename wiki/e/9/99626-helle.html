Helle

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: the young princess, Ἕλλη, Ἕλλην

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Helle, Nephele's daughter and Phryxus's sister, travels with him by ship toward Colchis, falls overboard or dies during passage, and gives the Hellespont its name. In The Library, Helle, daughter of Athamas and Nephele, is carried by a golden-fleeced ram over the sea, slips off, drowns, and dies.
