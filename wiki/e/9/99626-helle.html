Helle in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Nephele's daughter and Phryxus's sister travels with him by ship toward Colchis, falls overboard or dies during passage, and gives the Hellespont its name.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

Helle was Nephele's daughter and Phryxus's companion in the voyage to Colchis. She fell overboard and was drowned, or died naturally during the passage, and the Hellespont was said to have received its name from her.

Helle in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Helle, daughter of Athamas and Nephele, is carried by a golden-fleeced ram over the sea, slips off, drowns, and dies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX. Τῶν δὲ Αἰόλον παίδων ᾿Αθάμας, Βοιωτίας

Helle is Athamas and Nephele’s daughter. While being carried by the golden-fleeced ram over the sea between Sigeum and the Chersonese, she slips into the deep and dies.
