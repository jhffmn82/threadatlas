Campe’s distinction treats Empfindsamkeit as morally grounded susceptibility to feeling when balanced with reason, while Empfindelei is small-minded, irrational, ridiculous, and misplaced sensitivity. He also described exaggerated feeling as überspannte Empfindsamkeit and proposed Empfindsamlichkeit for superficial or hypocritical emotion.
