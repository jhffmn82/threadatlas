Cocalus’s ruse with the shell in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Cocalus receives Minos’s spiral shell and gives it to Daedalus to thread; Daedalus uses an ant and thread, completing the task but revealing his presence to Minos.

On being apprized of the flight of Theseus and

Cocalus received the spiral shell from Minos after promising to thread it and gave it to Daedalus. Daedalus used an ant and a thread to pass through the shell, but the completed task allowed Minos to identify Daedalus's presence.
