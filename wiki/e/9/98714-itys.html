Itys

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Itys,, grandson, son, ἤϊτυν

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Itys is killed, cooked, and served by Procne to Tereus without his knowledge. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Itys is affectionately approached by Progne, then murdered, mangled, cooked, and served to Tereus by Progne and Philomela.
