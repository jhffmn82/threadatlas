Itys in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Itys, son of Progne and Tereus, affectionately approaches Progne; because he resembles Tereus, Progne and Philomela murder, mangle, cook, and serve him to Tereus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Itys comes to his mother and shows her affection, but Progne decides to kill him because he resembles Tereus. Progne and Philomela murder him, mangle his body, cook it, and serve it to Tereus.
