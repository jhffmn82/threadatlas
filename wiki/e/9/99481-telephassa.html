Telephassa

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: her mother, mother, Τηλέφασσα, Τηλέφασσαν

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Telephassa was the wife of Agenor and mother of Cadmus, Phœnix, Cilix, and Europa, accompanied Cadmus in Thrace, and died before Delphi. In The Library, Telephassa married Agenor, bore Europa, Cadmus, Phoenix, and Cilix, and accompanied the search for Europa.
