  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Telephassa was the wife of Agenor and mother of Cadmus, Phœnix, Cilix, and Europa, accompanied Cadmus in Thrace, and died before Delphi.
  • In The Library, Telephassa married Agenor, bore Europa, Cadmus, Phoenix, and Cilix, and accompanied the search for Europa.

Telephassa in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Telephassa, wife of Agenor and mother of Cadmus, Phœnix, Cilix, and Europa, accompanied Cadmus in Thrace before dying before Delphi.

notes .. notes

Telephassa accompanied Cadmus while he lived in Thrace and died before he proceeded to Delphi. She was the wife of Agenor and the mother of Cadmus, Phœnix, Cilix, and Europa according to Apollodorus.
