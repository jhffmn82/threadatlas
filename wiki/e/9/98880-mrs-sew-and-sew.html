Mrs Sew-and-Sew in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Mrs Sew-and-Sew is a seamstress, rag-rug maker, and Queen of Ragbad who sets aside her crown to work at keeping the failing kingdom together. She owns and rescues her patchwork quilt, cares for King Fumbo, and helps Tatters by mending his clothes, advising him, and worrying over his danger. After falling ill and fainting at Fumbo’s loss, she joyfully reunites with Tatters and Urtha, prepares a feast, and resumes being Queen.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Mrs Sew-and-Sew is Queen of Ragbad, though she has stopped wearing her crown and works to keep the failing kingdom together. During the storm she rescues her patchwork quilt, catches a frightful cold, and later faints when she sees that King Fumbo has lost his head.

CHAPTER 2

Mrs Sew-and-Sew worries about Tatters facing danger with only an umbrella, then helps prepare him by mending his clothes and giving advice. She also removes the cabbage from Fumbo’s shoulders and helps steady him during the farewell.

CHAPTER 22

Mrs Sew-and-Sew is overjoyed to find Tatters and Urtha reunited with her and repeatedly kisses them to make sure they are real. She prepares a lavish feast, later removes her thimble and becomes Queen of Ragbad again.
