Abstract written by a model

E. A. A. von Göchhausen wrote M . . . R . . . (1772), a repeatedly reissued work that uses Sterne-like reader interposition and sarcastic repudiation of imitation to disarm criticism. It moves from acknowledged dependence on Sterne toward satire of critics and philosophers and a lunar Utopian fantasy.

E. A. A. von Göchhausen wrote M . . . R . . ., published at Eisenach in 1772 and later issued in several editions. He openly and repeatedly engages with his dependence on Sterne, while gradually moving from imitation toward his own satire of critics and philosophers and a Utopian fantasy of life on the moon.
