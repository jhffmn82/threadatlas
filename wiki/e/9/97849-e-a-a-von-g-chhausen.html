[54] Another work in which sentimentalism has dwindled away to a grinning shade,

E. A. A. von Göchhausen wrote M . . . R . . ., published at Eisenach in 1772 and later issued in several editions. He openly and repeatedly engages with his dependence on Sterne, while gradually moving from imitation toward his own satire of critics and philosophers and a Utopian fantasy of life on the moon.
