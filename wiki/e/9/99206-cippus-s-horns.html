Abstract written by a model

Cippus’s horns appeared on his forehead, were confirmed in water, foretold kingship if he entered the city, and were later carved on Rome’s brass-decorated doorposts.

FABLES IV. V. AND VI. [XV.479-621]

Cippus's horns appeared on his forehead and were confirmed when he touched them after seeing their reflection in water. They signified that he would become king if he entered the city, and their wondrous form was later carved on brass-decorated doorposts.
