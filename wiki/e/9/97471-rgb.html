RGB in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where RGB first appears

Abstract written by a model

RGB is a bilingual document question-answering benchmark and evaluation setting using Chinese and English data. It evaluates systems with five retrieved documents across noise ratios from 0.2 to 0.8. Results compare Naïve RAG and TurboRAG variants, including TTFT; fine-tuned variants stay within 1% accuracy of Naïve RAG, while TurboRAG-reordered outperforms TurboRAG-composite.
