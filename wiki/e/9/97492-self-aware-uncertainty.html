self-aware uncertainty

concept · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: self-aware uncertainty score, their self-aware uncertainty, uncertainty score

Abstract written by a model

Self-aware uncertainty is an uncertainty score extracted from the internal states of large language models, specifically feed-forward-network states associated with the last generated token. Computed through consistency across multiple generations to the same prompt, using a regularized Gram determinant, it measures uncertainty for a context. It matters because SEAKR uses it to judge whether knowledge is sufficient, trigger retrieval when uncertainty is high, rank knowledge snippets by how much they reduce uncertainty, and choose between final reasoning strategies. Across the account, it moves from internal-state extraction and score computation to comparisons across contexts, then to retrieval and knowledge-integration decisions, including a case where the most uncertainty-reducing item supplies critical information.

Abstract

Self-aware uncertainty is extracted from the internal states of large language models. Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval uses it to trigger retrieval, re-rank knowledge snippets, and select reasoning strategies.

Introduction

Self-aware uncertainty is extracted from the internal states in the feed-forward network of each layer corresponding to the last generated token. Its score is computed from consistency across multiple generations to the same prompt and is used for retrieval decisions and knowledge integration.

3 .. 3.4

The uncertainty score measures the large language model's uncertainty for a context using the regularized Gram determinant. SEAKR compares these scores across contexts containing different knowledge snippets and across its two final reasoning strategies.

4 / 4.1 / 4.1.1 .. 4.2.2

Self-aware uncertainty is used by SEAKR to determine when retrieval is needed. The text attributes part of SEAKR's advantage over FLARE and DRAGIN to this approach rather than relying superficially on output probability.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

Self-aware uncertainty is used to determine whether the LLM has sufficient knowledge and to rank retrieved knowledge. In the case study, a high uncertainty value triggers retrieval, and the retrieved item that reduces uncertainty the most contains critical information.

6

SEAKR extracts self-aware uncertainty from LLMs’ internal states and uses it as an indicator for invoking knowledge retrieval.
