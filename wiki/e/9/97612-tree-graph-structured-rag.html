Tree&Graph-structured RAG

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: graph- structured RAG, tree-structured RAG

Abstract written by a model

Tree&Graph-structured RAG uses trees and graphs to model logical relations through hierarchical summarization, tree walking, similarity and relatedness trees, mindmaps, knowledge-graph subgraphs, and hierarchical graph indices; tree logic is single-document, while graph construction is costly and time-consuming and returns triplets.
