Lodge 341

group · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Lodge 341, Vermissa, lodge, the lodge, the lodge in these parts

Abstract written by a model

Lodge 341 is a lodge of the Ancient Order of Freemen in Vermissa Valley and the organized body of the Scowrers. Led by Bodymaster Boss McGinty, it has about sixty members, meets on Saturday nights in a reserved room at the Union House, and is guarded by ranks, coded signs, and secret tests. Its local initiation requires courage, endurance of pain, secrecy, fidelity, and branding, while McGinty enforces discipline heavily. McMurdo is ordered to attend and become affiliated. Later, the lodge hears his warning about Birdy Edwards, appoints a committee, and approves an ambush plan.

Lodge 341 is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Lodge 341 in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09 to 1915-05 · the work’s page · read it where Lodge 341 first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Lodge 341 is the lodge in Vermissa Valley whose Bodymaster is Boss McGinty. Its members use secret tests, ceremonies, and disciplinary authority, and McGinty says McMurdo must attend its Saturday meeting to become affiliated and be made free of the Vermissa Valley.

Chapter III

Lodge 341, Vermissa, meets at the Union House and has about sixty members, though it forms part of a wider network of lodges containing at least five hundred men across the coal district. Its initiation ceremony tests McMurdo’s courage and ability to endure pain, requires secrecy and fidelity, and marks him with a lodge symbol.

Chapter VI

The lodge is the Scowrers’ organized body and meets on Saturday evenings under strict guard and coded signs. It hears McMurdo’s warning about Birdy Edwards, appoints a committee to consider the matter, and approves the plan to ambush him.
