semantic indexing

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Semantic Indexing, indexing, semantic index- ing

Abstract written by a model

Semantic indexing uses a pre-trained language model and graph neural network to represent knowledge sub-graphs, stores them in a knowledge vector database, and enables coarse and fine-grained retrieval.
