chariot-race for the suitors of Hippodamia

Also called: marriage contest for Hippodamia, race, wooing and winning of Hippodamia

Abstract written by a model

A chariot race in which a suitor flees with Hippodamia while Oenomaus pursues in armor; reaching the Corinthian Isthmus wins Hippodamia’s hand in marriage.

II. Ὅτι ὁ Τάνταλος ἐν “Atdov? κολάζεται,

Oenomaus offered Hippodamia in marriage to a suitor who could escape him in a chariot race to the Isthmus of Corinth. The suitor began with Hippodamia in his chariot, while Oenomaus immediately pursued him in full armor.
