Abstract written by a model

The raven, once a white bird praised for beautiful feathers, reports Coronis's infidelity to Apollo. Apollo blames and punishes it for its garrulous report, forbidding it among the white birds. It changes from white to black, becoming a bird with black wings.

The raven was formerly white and was praised for its beautiful feathers. Because of its garrulous tongue and the report it carried, it is changed to a bird with black wings.

opening

The raven reports to Apollo that Coronis has been unfaithful. Apollo blames the raven for revealing the cause of his sorrow and forbids it to remain among the white birds.
