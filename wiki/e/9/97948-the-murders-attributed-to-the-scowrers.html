the murders attributed to the Scowrers in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The murders attributed to the Scowrers include Milman, Van Shorst, the Nicholson family, Mr. Hyam, and Billy James. Presented as common knowledge, they reveal a murder society that silences witnesses and protects accused men with false alibis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

Mr. Shafter cites the murders of Milman and Van Shorst, the Nicholson family, old Mr. Hyam, little Billy James, and others as proof that the Scowrers are a murder society. He says the crimes are common knowledge in the valley, while Ettie explains that witnesses are silenced and accused men are protected by false alibis.
