Abstract written by a model

Erisicthon, son of Triopas, neglects the gods and profanes Ceres’s grove. Punished with insatiable hunger, he consumes his estate, repeatedly sells Metra for food, and finally devours his own limbs.

EXPLANATION.

Erisicthon, son of Triopas, despised the gods, neglected their altars, and cut down a sacred oak belonging to Ceres. Ceres punished him with insatiable hunger; after consuming his estate and repeatedly selling Metra, he began to tear apart and eat his own limbs.
