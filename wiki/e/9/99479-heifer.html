heifer in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The heifer, a cow never yoked or marked for servitude, has no keeper and is free from the crooked plough; she leads Cadmus across fields and the Cephisus, then lies down.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE THIRD.

The heifer has never borne the yoke and shows no mark of servitude on her neck. She leads Cadmus through the fields, stops after crossing the Cephisus and the fields of Panope, and lies down on the grass.
