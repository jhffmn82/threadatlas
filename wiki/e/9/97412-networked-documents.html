front matter .. GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation / Abstract

Networked documents occur in applications such as citation graphs, social media, and knowledge graphs. They are difficult for Naive Retrieval-Augmented Generation because retrieval focuses on individual documents.

Introduction

Documents such as social media postings, research papers, knowledge items, and product reviews are described as networked rather than isolated. Their relationships can be important for retrieving comprehensive information and for generating answers that reflect the surrounding context.
