Abstract written by a model

Networked documents are textual data linked by relationships, appearing in citation graphs, social media, and knowledge graphs. Unlike isolated documents, their connections matter for comprehensive retrieval and context-aware answers. The account begins with their real-world applications, then explains why Naive Retrieval-Augmented Generation, focused on individual documents, is difficult.

Introduction

Documents such as social media postings, research papers, knowledge items, and product reviews are described as networked rather than isolated. Their relationships can be important for retrieving comprehensive information and for generating answers that reflect the surrounding context.
