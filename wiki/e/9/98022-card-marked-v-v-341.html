card marked V.V. 341 in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

A rough cardboard card, rudely inscribed in thick ink “V.V. 341,” is found beside John Douglas’s body. Wilson cannot interpret it; Barker says he had not seen it and believes the murderer left it. Holmes links it, with a branded mark, to a possible secret society.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

A card bearing the inked inscription “V.V.” and “341” was found beside John Douglas’s body. Sergeant Wilson could not interpret it, and Barker said he had not noticed it before and believed the murderer had left it.

Chapter IV

The rough cardboard card marked V.V. 341 is found beside the dead man. Holmes determines that it was not printed in the room and suggests that, along with the branded mark, it may be connected with a secret society.
