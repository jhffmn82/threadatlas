Abstract written by a model

Die Grosse Begebenheit aus kleinen Ursachen is Schummel’s three-volume work: it begins by imitating Sterne’s two novels, embedding Sterne elements in another narrative fabric, grows increasingly contradictory toward that model, and ends with an admission of its faults and few original beauties.

Schummel’s three-volume work begins as an imitation of Sterne’s two novels but develops an increasingly contradictory relationship to that model. Its third volume contains Schummel’s admission that the imitation has few or none of the original’s beauties and many faults of its own.
