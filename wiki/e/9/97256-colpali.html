Key Innovations and Methodologies / 3.1

ColPali performs end-to-end document image retrieval by embedding page patches with a vision-language backbone and bypassing OCR. It is also described as using consensus-based filtering and multi-vector mapping to remove low-similarity candidates.

ColPali is a late-interaction multi-vector visual retrieval model built on PaliGemma, evaluated across six datasets and achieving 96.15 average source-document identification at k=5.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Experiments / 6.1 .. 6.2

ColPali is evaluated as a late-interaction multi-vector visual retrieval model built on PaliGemma. It scores 96.93 on PaperTab, 97.71 on FetaTab, 95.28 on SciGraphQA, 93.17 on SPIQA, 97.64 on SlideVQA, and 96.15 on average for source-document identification at k = 5.
