Abstract written by a model

In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, two Multilayer Perceptrons learn scaling factors for nodes and edges: one processes the distance between node and query representations, while the other processes the distance between edge and query representations. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, two Multilayer Perceptrons learn scaling factors from the element-wise distance between node or edge representations and the query representation: one processes node representations and the other processes edge representations.
