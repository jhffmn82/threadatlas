Abstract written by a model

The Garden of Gorba is an enchanted, mysterious garden, apparently belonging to the wizard, entered through a golden gate. It is filled with flowering vines, silver paths and fountain, lantern lanes, peach trees, animal-shaped bushes, a golden bench, magical stepping stones, and a hedge-surrounded flower bed. Its magic matters most: plants produce effects, flowers give warnings, and the stepping stones move according to a person’s stance. Urtha lies in the flower bed before awakening. Grampa, Tatters, Bill, and Urtha become unable to find a way out during the day, encounter Gorba’s Winding Stairway, and eventually leave together.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

The Garden of Gorba is a beautiful magical garden filled with flowering vines, silvery paths, crystal fountains, and trees glowing with magic lanterns. A sign calls it a place of mystery and magic, and Grampa suspects it belongs to the wizard mentioned by the bandit.

CHAPTER 6

Gorba’s garden has silver paths, lantern lanes, peach trees, a silver fountain, and magical stepping stones that move according to how a person stands on them. It also contains bushes shaped like animals and a hedge-surrounded flower bed where Urtha lies. Tatters, Grampa, Bill, and Urtha eventually leave the garden together.

CHAPTER 7

The wizard’s garden keeps Grampa, Tatters, Urtha, and Bill from finding a way out during the day. It contains magical plants, warning flower messages, lanterns, and Gorba’s Winding Stairway.
