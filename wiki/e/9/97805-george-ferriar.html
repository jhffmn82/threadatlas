George Ferriar in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

George Ferriar, an investigator in England, examined Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings, citing Burton passages as theft; his findings were favorably presented by Friedrich Nicolai and prompted attacks by Johann Friedrich Böttiger and Lichtenberg.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings, his high-handed and extensive appropriation

George Ferriar investigated Sterne’s borrowings and presented passages from Burton’s Anatomy of Melancholy as evidence of Sterne’s theft. Friedrich Nicolai gave his findings a favorable and measured presentation in Germany, while Lichtenberg attacked Sterne more spitefully in connection with Ferriar’s essay.
