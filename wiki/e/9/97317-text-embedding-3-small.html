Abstract written by a model

In “CODERAG-BENCH: Can Retrieval Augment Code Generation?,” openai-text-embedding-small-03 is included as a cost-effective proprietary embedding API; its corresponding OpenAI-03 retriever achieves an average NDCG@10 of 61.7 and is used in full RACG experiments, including a reranked variant. In “OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models,” text-embedding-3-small is used as the sentence embedding function across retrieval methods and as the embedding model for the RAGAS metrics.
