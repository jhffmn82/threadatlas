Whitaker’s Almanac in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Whitaker’s Almanac is a standardized common reference book printed in double columns. Holmes first tests its current edition for a cipher without success, then an older edition yields a coded warning of danger to Douglas at Birlstone.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART I / Chapter I

Sherlock Holmes tests the cipher against the current edition of Whitaker’s Almanac but obtains meaningless words. He then uses an older edition and finds that its numbered words produce the warning about danger to Douglas at Birlstone.
