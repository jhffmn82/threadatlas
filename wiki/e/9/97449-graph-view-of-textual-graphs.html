In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, it is the graph view that learns textual-graph representations as soft prompts, preserving textual connections by encoding topology with a Graph Neural Network and controlling message passing through relevance-scaled soft pruning. Graph View of Textual Graphs in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025-07-12 · the work’s page · read it where Graph View of Textual Graphs first appears

Abstract written by a model

Graph View of Textual Graphs is a complementary view of a textual graph in GRAG. It learns graph representations as soft prompts to preserve connections among texts. Beginning with a soft pruned subgraph, it uses a Graph Neural Network and learned relevance scaling factors to control message passing, encode topology, and align graph embeddings with the language model’s token space.
