Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

PART I / Chapter I

Birlstone Manor House is identified as the residence of Mr. Douglas in the decoded warning. It is associated with the reported murder of Mr. Douglas.

Chapter III

Birlstone Manor House is an ancient, many-gabled building surrounded by an inner moat and approached only by a drawbridge. John Douglas and Mrs. Douglas took possession of it after it had stood untenanted and begun to decay, and John Douglas was murdered there on the night of January 6th.

Chapter IV

The Manor House is an old Jacobean house surrounded by a moat and approached by a drawbridge. Mr. Douglas is found murdered there, with clues including an open window, mud-stained footprints, a blood mark, a hammer, a shotgun, a card, and a missing wedding ring.

Chapter V

The house has a pantry and kitchens at the back, servants' rooms at the extreme rear, and a study at the front. Its drawbridge was raised at night according to the old custom of the house.

Chapter VI

Sherlock Holmes spends the afternoon at the Manor House consulting with his two colleagues. The murder scene and the study are associated with the Manor House.

It was nightfall before Inspector MacDonald and White Mason came back

MacDonald theorizes that Hargrave approached the house at twilight, entered the first room he saw, and hid behind a curtain. Holmes instead argues that Mrs. Douglas and Barker reached the room before the murderer escaped and fabricated evidence that he had fled through the window.

Chapter VII

Birlstone Manor remains under observation while Sherlock Holmes and the others hide in a shrubbery opposite its main door and drawbridge. Holmes also studies an account of the ancient house and spends time alone in its study.

Birlstone Manor contained a secret hiding place suitable for concealing Douglas and provided the setting for Baldwin's attack and death. Douglas, Barker, and Douglas's wife used the manor to stage Baldwin's body as Douglas's and to imply that a murderer had escaped.
