Humpy in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Humpy is a stuffed moving-picture dummy dressed as a king: a jewel-crowned figure in regal robes, numbered 202-B-E-10-B-47 on the back of his neck. He regards himself as a dummy rather than having a name, has hair instead of brains, does not eat, and was used to take dangerous falls as Dorothy's double and as a double of Pastoria. After revolutionists throw him over a cliff, Dorothy pulls him from a brook and wishes him alive. He awakens, learns to walk, and travels back to Oz with her, fond of the journey despite his hair-brained panic. He promises to take hard falls for Dorothy, notices approaching rivermen, throws his crown at them, plays catch with the Scooters, crosses the river with their help, and is thrown about by Kabumpo after striking him with a stick. Along the way he jokes that the adventure is a moving picture and asks to fall for the lost King. Though he proves not to be the lost King, he is Pastoria's image and becomes his double. He finally accepts belonging in a tailor shop as a tailor's dummy, requesting another suit from Pastoria.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

Humpy is a moving-picture dummy dressed as a king who has been thrown over a cliff by revolutionists. After Dorothy pulls him from the brook, he awakens, learns to walk, and discusses his life as a dummy before vanishing with Dorothy.

CHAPTER 11

Humpy is alive and returns to Oz with Dorothy, although he remains a stuffed dummy with only hair for brains. He helps Dorothy notice the approaching rivermen, promises to take the hard falls for her, and becomes fond of traveling with her. At the purple river, he panics and throws his crown at the approaching rivermen.

CHAPTER 12

Humpy plays catch with the Scooters and insists that he is Dorothy's double. The Scooters carry him across the river, and he later strikes Kabumpo with his stick, causing the elephant to seize him and throw him into the air. Humpy then rides uneasily on Kabumpo's back.

CHAPTER 15

Humpy is brought back by Dorothy and is astonished by Tora's flying ears and by the breakfast bush. He jokes about the adventure being a moving picture and asks whether he can fall for the lost King of Oz. Kabumpo tosses him onto his back as the group begins traveling to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 20

Humpy is shown not to be the lost King, despite being the image of Pastoria. He becomes Pastoria's double and later accepts becoming a tailor's dummy, asking Pastoria to make him another suit.
