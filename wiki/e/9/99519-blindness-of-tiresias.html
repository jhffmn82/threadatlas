blindness of Tiresias

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Tiresias's blindness, blinded, blindness, condemned the eyes of the umpire to eternal darkness, loss of sight, τυφλὸς τὰς ὁράσεις, τῆς πηρώσεως

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Tiresias's blindness is caused by Juno's punishment after he confirms Jupiter's opinion in the dispute over sexual pleasure; Jupiter later grants him knowledge of things to come. In The Library, different accounts explain Tiresias's blindness: Athena blinds him after he sees her naked, or Hera blinds him after he answers her dispute with Zeus about sexual pleasure.
