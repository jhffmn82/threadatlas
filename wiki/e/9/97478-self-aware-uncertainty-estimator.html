Self-aware Uncertainty Estimator

technology · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: U(c), U(·), self-aware uncertainty estimator, uncertainty estimator

Abstract written by a model

The Self-aware Uncertainty Estimator quantifies a large language model’s uncertainty for an input context by sampling multiple generations, extracting EOS hidden representations, and computing a regularized Gram determinant with INSIDE. Its score guides retrieval, re-ranking, and final-answer selection. It is contrasted with prompting-, perplexity-, multi-perplexity, LN-Entropy-, and energy-based alternatives, which generally reduce SEAKR’s performance. It may also be misused as an adversarial training signal to improve deception about uncertain information.

The self-aware uncertainty estimator quantifies the uncertainty of the large language model when generating for an input context. It samples multiple generations, extracts their EOS hidden representations, computes a regularized Gram determinant, and uses the result to guide retrieval, re-ranking, and final-answer selection.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

The Self-aware Uncertainty Estimator is ablated by replacing it with prompting-based, perplexity-based, multi-perplexity, LN-Entropy, or energy-based methods. These alternatives generally reduce SEAKR's performance, with the text stating that the estimator extracts uncertainty from the LLM.

Ethical Considerations

The self-aware uncertainty estimator can be misused as an adversarial signal for model training, potentially making models better at deceiving humans with uncertain information.
