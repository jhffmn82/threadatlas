Bode’s translation of the Sentimental Journey

Abstract written by a model

Bode’s translation of the Sentimental Journey was published by Cramer in Hamburg and Bremen, probably in September or October 1768, with its volumes printed at Bode’s own press. Lessing read the whole translation, and Ebert aided it. Its preface declared an early Sterne cult, discussed translating “sentimental,” and sketched Sterne’s life and works; a second edition followed in 1769.

Bode’s translation of the Sentimental Journey was published probably in September or October 1768 by Cramer in Hamburg and Bremen, with printing carried out at Bode’s own press. Its preface presented an early declaration of the Sterne cult, discussed the translation of “sentimental,” and included a sketch of Sterne’s life and works; a second edition appeared in 1769.
