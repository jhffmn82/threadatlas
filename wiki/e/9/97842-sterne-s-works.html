Sterne’s works in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Sterne’s works are writings by Laurence Sterne. They hold relatively greater serious consideration and recognized merit in France and Germany than in English-speaking countries. In Germany, their reception unfolds through the wider influence of British literature and the work of translators, reviewers, and imitators.
