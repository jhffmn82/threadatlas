Die Tagereise

Abstract written by a model

Published in 1770 and reissued in 1775 as Die Geschichte eines empfundenen Tages, Die Tagereise is a prose journey interspersed with verse. It draws partly directly from Sterne and indirectly through Jacobi, adapting sentimental-journey episodes and attitudes associated with Yorick. Critics largely judged it an unsuccessful or tiresome imitation, though it has some genuine originality.

Die Tagereise, published by Johann Christian Bock in 1770 and reissued in 1775 as Die Geschichte eines empfundenen Tages, presents a journey in prose varied by verse. It is inspired partly by Sterne and partly through Jacobi, especially by the Sentimental Journey, and adapts several episodes and attitudes associated with Yorick. Contemporary critics generally regarded it as an unsuccessful, insufficient, or tiresome imitation, despite elements of genuine originality.
