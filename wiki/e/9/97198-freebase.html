Shijie Wang, Wenqi Fan, Yue Feng, Shanru Lin, Xinyu Ma, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin · 2025

Freebase is an external knowledge graph used for recommendation tasks. It supplies filtered triples to reconstruct knowledge graphs for MovieLens-1M, MovieLens-20M, and Amazon Book experiments, and is the study’s only external source before proposed use of YAGO, DBpedia, and Wikipedia.

Freebase supplies knowledge-graph triples related to the MovieLens-1M, MovieLens-20M, and Amazon Book datasets. The triples are filtered to reconstruct a knowledge graph for the experiments.

Freebase was the only external knowledge graph used in the study because it is commonly used for recommendation tasks. Future work aims to adopt YAGO, DBpedia, and Wikipedia to examine how different knowledge sources affect performance.
