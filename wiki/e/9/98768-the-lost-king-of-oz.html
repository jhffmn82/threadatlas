The Lost King of Oz in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Narrative, section by section

21 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Kinda Jolly and Rosa Merry rule the cheerful kingdom of Kimbaloo from a cozy button-covered castle in the Gilliken country of Oz. The Kimbles earn their living by selling bouquets and buttons gathered from the bouquet bush and button wood. Mombi, an old Gilliken witch who lost her magic powers, serves as the castle cook while secretly attempting to recover her magic. After Mombi tries to transform Kinda Jolly into a collar button, a talking goose accuses her of having done something to the King.

CHAPTER 2

Snip overhears Mombi and Pajuka reveal that Pajuka is a transformed goose and that Mombi has forgotten what she did with King Kinda Jolly. Mombi tries to silence Snip, but Pajuka protects him and persuades Mombi to search for the missing King. After learning that Mombi is the witch who transformed Ozma and Pajuka, Snip is forced to accompany them into the forest. Mombi makes Snip carry their supplies as they leave Kimbaloo.

CHAPTER 3

Kinda Jolly and Rosa Merry learn that Mombi, the goose intended for dinner, and Snip have disappeared from Kimbaloo. General Whiffenpuff leaves to search for them, while the palace mourns and then settles down to a picnic supper. The King decides to send for an invisible cook, and Rosa offers to cook in the meantime. Hah Hoh comforts Kinda Jolly by revealing his "funny bone."

CHAPTER 4

Mombi drags Snip through the Purple Forest while Pajuka helps watch him, and Snip decides to stay with them rather than face the forest alone. Pajuka explains that Mombi changed him into a goose and that they are searching for the King, whom Mombi cannot remember where she hid. Mombi concludes that green magic transformed the King and plans to go to the Emerald City, steal Ozma's magic belt, restore the King, and take revenge. After supper, Snip and Pajuka fall asleep while Mombi unsuccessfully experiments with her black frying pan.

CHAPTER 5

Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi travel southwest through the Gilliken country toward the Emerald City. They survive an attack by a weenix using Mombi's purple pepper, then meet the Hoopers, who roll over them in their park. Snip defeats several Hoopers with Mombi's crooked stick, angering their king, Rollo the Royal. When Mombi is identified as a witch, Rollo and the Hoopers flee, and the travelers continue toward the Corners.

CHAPTER 6

Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi reach an inland sea, which Mombi jellies with a purple can of gelatin so they can cross it. They then enter Catty Corners, a city inhabited by cats, where the cats welcome Mombi but capture Snip and Pajuka. The Maltese cat queen, called the Imperial and Puissant Pussy, declares that Mombi may stay, Snip will be chased and scratched, and Pajuka will be eaten. Snip tries to protect Pajuka, but cat guards take them away as a threatened dinner and prisoner.

CHAPTER 7

Pajuka and Snip are imprisoned in a summer house in Catty Corners, guarded by cats and threatened with being eaten or mauled. Mombi tricks the Catty Queen and the cats by putting powerful baking powder into rice cream pudding, causing them to rise into the air. Mombi frees Pajuka and Snip and begins leading them toward the Emerald City, while secretly considering how to get rid of Snip. Mombi hopes Pajuka will help her recognize the old King and regain her magic powers so she can rule Oz.

CHAPTER 8

In the Emerald City, Scraps teases Hank while Ozma, Betsy Bobbin, and Trot await Dorothy's return. A golden goose feather writes the puzzling message, "Danger! Go to Morrow to-day!" and leaves everyone uncertain about its meaning. The Wizard of Oz and Tik Tok examine the message, but the Scarecrow realizes that "to-morrow" indicates a time and "to-day" may indicate a place. Ozma confirms that Morrow sounds like a kingdom in Oz, and the group prepares to consult a map.

CHAPTER 9

The Wizard of Oz uses a wishing pill to transport himself, Ozma, Scarecrow, Betsy Bobbin, Trot, Scraps, and Sir Hokus of Pokes to the abandoned castle of Morrow. Inside, they discover a silver casket containing a royal robe said to restore the King of Oz to his proper shape when used with Incantation No. 986 from the Green Book of Magic. Ozma realizes that the castle was a hunting lodge where she and her father once hid from Mombi, and she learns that her father may still be alive. Because the Wizard has no second wishing pill and no one knows the castle's location, the company begins walking toward the Emerald City to use the Magic Picture, stopping briefly at a small red house without finding anyone.

CHAPTER 10

Dorothy leaves Perhaps City and is mysteriously swept from the Winkie Country to a road near Hollywood in California. She discovers that green-clad revolutionists have thrown a stuffed king over a cliff and rescues the moving-picture dummy, who comes alive and is eventually named Humpy. Dorothy explains Oz and her life as a princess, but she suddenly begins growing up outside Oz’s magical influence. Her painful growth ends when she wishes herself back to Oz, causing both her and Humpy to vanish from the ravine.

CHAPTER 11

Dorothy and Humpy return to Oz after Dorothy uses wishing sand to wish herself back, and Dorothy discovers that Humpy has also become alive. They travel through the Gilliken Country toward the Emerald City but are stopped by the backward-speaking woodsmen of the Back Woods, whom they evade by running backward forward. At a purple river, they encounter strange rivermen skating across the water, and Humpy throws his crown at them in alarm.

CHAPTER 12

Dorothy and Humpy meet the Scooters, tall water-skimming creatures with sails extending from their ankles to their fingertips. The Scooters help carry Dorothy and Humpy across the river and tell Dorothy that a water bridge can shoot travelers across. Afterward, Dorothy and Humpy encounter Kabumpo, who throws Humpy into the air after Humpy strikes him, then agrees to take them toward the Emerald City. Kabumpo carries both travelers on his back as they continue their journey.

CHAPTER 13

Mombi throws Snip into a well, but he lands in a padded bucket that carries him through an underground passage to Blankenburg. The invisible Blanks pursue Snip because he has a visible face, but he escapes into the shop of Tora, a tailor whose butterfly ears can fly away and hear distant events. Tora explains that the Well-come brings people into Blankenburg and the Fare-well is the only known exit, then Snip helps Tora escape the invisible force imprisoning him. A golden feather from Pajuka's wing flies toward the Emerald City, while Snip and Tora head for the Fare-well.

CHAPTER 14

Snip helps Tora escape from the Blanks through the underground passage and well, and the two begin traveling toward the Emerald City. Tora explains that the Blanks became invisible after Queen Vanette discovered a pool of magic water and ordered everyone in Blankenburg to bathe in it. Snip finds food for himself and Tora, but they encounter a kingly stranger seeking food for a Princess. The stranger mistakes Tora's temporarily removed ears for being mislaid and goes to call Dorothy.

CHAPTER 15

Dorothy and Humpy reunite with Snip and Tora after Kabumpo carries them near the tailor's location, and they share breakfast while explaining their situations. Snip tells Dorothy that Mombi is seeking the lost King of Oz and plans to turn Ozma into a piano, so they must reach the Emerald City quickly. Kabumpo initially insults Snip and Tora but joins the group after learning about the royal danger. Kabumpo then carries Dorothy, Humpy, Tora, and Snip rapidly toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 16

Kabumpo and his companions are stopped by a steep mountain, which turns out to be only a shadow mountain. Mombi and Pajuka appear, and Pajuka recognizes Humpy as the lost King of Oz while Mombi confirms it by checking the number on his collar. Mombi tries to steal Humpy and restore him in exchange for her magic powers and half the kingdom, but Kabumpo rescues him and captures Mombi. The group proceeds toward the Emerald City with Mombi, intending to have the transformation reversed there.

CHAPTER 17

The little company reaches the Emerald City with Humpy, Mombi, and the stuffed King after traveling toward Ozma's palace. Mombi uses the forgotten green magic formula after Humpy sits on the royal throne, ordering him to assume his proper form. Instead of transforming Humpy, her magic causes the Palace of Emeralds to sink rapidly underground, plunging everyone into darkness.

CHAPTER 18

Ozma and her faithful followers journey north through the Quadling Country toward the Emerald City after learning that the King of Oz is in the palace. The golden goose feather writes the King's message on Sir Hokus's shield, prompting Ozma and the others to hurry home. When they reach the palace grounds, they find the royal castle missing and Ozma breaks down in grief. The castle then erupts from underground, carrying the group into the air and leaving the Scarecrow dangling from a spire.

CHAPTER 19

Mombi's use of her magic formula without the King's robe causes the palace to sink into the earth, leaving Dorothy, Kabumpo, Pajuka, Humpy, Tora, and Snip inside. Snip finds Mombi's magical baking powder and uses it to raise the castle back to its foundations. Ozma and her companions arrive, but the Wizard's attempt to restore Humpy and Pajuka fails because he misreads Humpy's collar number as an incantation. Humpy remains a dummy, and the King and Pajuka are still transformed.

CHAPTER 20

Dorothy and her companions discover that Tora the tailor is actually Pastoria, the lost King of Oz, after the King's robe and the Wizard of Oz's magic restore his natural form. Pastoria is reunited with Ozma, while Pajuka is released from his goose enchantment, and Humpy becomes his double rather than the King himself. Pastoria explains that Snip's kindness weakened the enchantment and that Lurline gave him his flying ears when she could not fully disenchant him. Pastoria then abdicates in favor of Ozma and decides to open a tailoring shop in the Emerald City, with Snip as his assistant.

CHAPTER 21

A grand procession travels through the Emerald City to honor the long-lost King of Oz, with Ozma, Dorothy, Snip, Pajuka, and many others riding or marching in it. Afterward, Ozma decides that Mombi should be punished, and Mombi is washed away with water, leaving only her buckled shoes. General Whiffenpuff arrives with the Invisible Cook and is delighted to find Snip safe, but soon departs with Kabumpo for Kimbaloo. The King of Oz and Snip later work together in a tailoring shop beside the palace.
