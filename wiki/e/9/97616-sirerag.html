Abstract written by a model

SiReRAG is a tree-structured RAG method that constructs similarity and relatedness trees; evaluated as a baseline, it slightly beats HopRAG once, while HopRAG has a small overall advantage without its aggregate nodes.

SiReRAG is in one work on this site, HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
