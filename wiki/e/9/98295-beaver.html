Beaver in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Beaver is an animal among the Earth People and a thief of fire in several versions. After Robin Redbreast, Dog, and Wildcat fail, Beaver obtains fire for the Earth People from the Sky People. In other tellings, Eagle, sometimes Weasel, assists Beaver, who steals glowing coals by feigning death in a fish trap and escapes with fire in a clam-shell or hidden under his skin.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

Beaver obtains fire for the Earth people in the Quinault version after Robin Redbreast, Dog, and Wildcat fail. He also steals glowing coals in the Sanpoil version by pretending to be dead in a fish trap and escaping while the Sky people are distracted.

In a myth told by the Thompson Indians, who inhabit the

In several versions told by the Thompson, Lillooet, and Okanaken Indians, Beaver is the thief of fire, assisted by Eagle or by Eagle and Weasel. Beaver conveys the fire away in a clam-shell or hides it under his skin.
