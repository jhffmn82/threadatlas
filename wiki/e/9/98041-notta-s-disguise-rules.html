Chapter 3

Notta's four rules are to disguise himself, be extremely polite, tell a joke, and finally shout something no one can understand and run. He tells Bobbie to follow these rules in danger and to run whenever he runs, but the first rule fails when the Mudgers capture his lion disguise.
