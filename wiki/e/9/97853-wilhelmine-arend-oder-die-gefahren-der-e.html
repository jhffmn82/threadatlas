Wilhelmine Arend, oder die Gefahren der Empfindsamkeit is Wezel’s two-volume novel of 1782, written to attack Empfindsamkeit on its dangerous rather than comic side. It traces Wilhelmine’s sentimental disorder through increasingly serious troubles to a tragic conclusion.
