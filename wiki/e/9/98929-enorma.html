Enorma in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

On Isa Poso, Enorma is a mighty female, fire-breathing dragon and huntress, feared enough to prompt a warning. She lives in a cave with a large fire and frozen meat. Grampa's snuff makes her sneeze, reveals her toothlessness, and drives her into an icy stream, where she is put out, floats smoking, and is defeated.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 11

A sign at the Illustrious Island of Isa Poso warns visitors to beware of the dragon. Urtha does not know what a dragon is when she asks about it.

CHAPTER 12

Enorma is a fire-breathing dragon who lives on Isa Poso and keeps a large fire and stores of frozen meat in her cave. Grampa throws snuff in her face, causing violent sneezing and revealing that she is toothless. She flees and plunges into an icy stream, where she is put out and floats like a log with only a puff of smoke remaining.
