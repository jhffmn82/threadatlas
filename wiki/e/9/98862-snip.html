Abstract written by a model

Snip is a little button boy from Kimbaloo, Kinda Jolly’s brightest boy and best button picker, who loves her and knows Oz history thoroughly. He carries small treasures in his pockets and becomes an unwilling companion of Mombi after overhearing her and Pajuka discussing the lost King. When Mombi catches him, she threatens him, makes him carry her basket, and drags him into the forest. His disappearance deeply upsets Kinda Jolly and the palace. During the journey toward the Emerald City, Snip befriends Pajuka and learns that Mombi seeks the King and plans to transform Ozma. Though frightened and younger than Mombi, he repeatedly shows courage: he helps fend off Hoopers, tries to protect Pajuka from the cats of Catty Corners, survives imprisonment, and carries Pajuka when the goose is asleep. He plans to escape Mombi and warn Ozma. Mombi throws Snip into a well, but he survives, reaches Blankenburg, escapes the Blanks with Tora, and tells Tora, Dorothy, and others about Mombi’s treachery. Traveling toward the Emerald City, he reunites joyfully with Pajuka and helps bring the warning to the palace. There he finds magical baking powder in Mombi’s basket and uses it to raise the castle, while warning that the wrong directions may cause another bad result. Snip also helps restore Pastoria: his kindness weakens the enchantment, and he insists that Tora try on the King’s robe, helping reveal the King’s identity. After the celebration, he agrees to work in Pastoria’s tailoring shop, but first returns toward Kimbaloo to tell Kinda Jolly where he has been, promising to return.

Snip is in one work on this site, The Lost King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

14 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

Snip hides on a narrow window sill and overhears Mombi and Pajuka discussing the King. After falling into Mombi's arms, he is threatened with being put into the soup kettle, but Pajuka protects him. Mombi then forces Snip to carry a basket and drags him into the forest.

CHAPTER 3

Snip disappears from the palace. Kinda Jolly calls him the brightest boy in Kimbaloo and his best button picker, and the palace is deeply upset by his loss.

CHAPTER 4

Snip is dragged through the Purple Forest by Mombi and helps gather wood for a fire. He becomes friendly with Pajuka, learns about the search for the King, and sleeps beside the fire after watching Mombi's failed magical experiments.

CHAPTER 5

Snip travels with Pajuka and Mombi toward the Emerald City, planning to escape from Mombi there and tell Ozma the whole story. He helps drive off the Hoopers by striking them with Mombi's crooked stick and then continues along the path.

CHAPTER 6

Snip travels with Pajuka and Mombi, encounters laughing willows, and reaches an inland sea. He crosses the jellied sea, enters Catty Corners, and is seized by cat guards. The cat queen orders that he be publicly chased and scratched, but Snip tries to stop the cats from eating Pajuka.

CHAPTER 7

Snip is imprisoned with Pajuka and fears being scratched and mauled by the cats. He carries the sleeping Pajuka after Mombi frees them, drinks from a cream fountain, and follows Mombi toward the Emerald City while becoming uneasy about her intentions.

CHAPTER 13

Snip is thrown into a well by Mombi but survives when he lands in a padded bucket that carries him through an underground tunnel to Blankenburg. He escapes the pursuing Blanks by hiding in Tora's shop, tells Tora about Mombi, Pajuka, and the lost King, and then helps Tora pass through the gate and run toward the Fare-well.

CHAPTER 14

Snip helps Tora escape from the Blanks by guiding him through the well. He travels with Tora toward the Emerald City, worries about finding Pajuka and the King, and fetches two steaming breakfast dishes from a breakfast bush.

CHAPTER 15

Snip gives Dorothy breakfast and explains that Mombi is seeking the lost King of Oz and intends to turn Ozma into a piano. He recounts how he learned of Mombi's plans, encountered the Blanks, and escaped with Tora. After initially objecting to Kabumpo's rudeness, he accepts a ride toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 16

Snip helps Tora after the sudden stop and worries about crossing the rocky mountain. He is frightened to see Mombi but happily embraces Pajuka when the goose recognizes him. Snip learns that Humpy may be the lost King of Oz and is relieved when Pajuka flies through the apparent mountain.

CHAPTER 17

Snip wonders how the King fell into California and how the others will be changed. He is delighted by the sight of the Emerald City and helps Dorothy ring the palace bell.

CHAPTER 19

Snip finds a can of Mombi's magical baking powder in her basket and follows its directions to raise the castle. He explains what he has done to Kabumpo and warns the Wizard that another awful result may follow from using the wrong instructions.

CHAPTER 20

Snip helps reveal the King's identity by insisting that the King's robe be tried on Tora, and his kindness had earlier weakened Pastoria's enchantment. He agrees to work in Pastoria's tailoring shop after first returning to Kimbaloo to tell Kinda Jolly.

CHAPTER 21

Snip rides with the Cowardly Lion and Dorothy in the procession. The King measures him for a suit, and Snip later leaves for Kimbaloo with General Whiffenpuff, the Invisible Cook, and Kabumpo, promising to return.
