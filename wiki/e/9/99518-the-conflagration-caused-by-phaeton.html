the conflagration caused by Phaeton

Also called: Phaëton’s conflagration, conflagration, conflagration of the earth, excessive heat, flames, such a fate, the devouring flame, the world on fire

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Phaëton’s conflagration ravages countries with flames caused by Phaëton’s fall and intensifies the fires of Ætna. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the conflagration caused by Phaeton burns Earth, scorching the heavens and earth, destroying cities and nations, drying rivers and seas, and forcing aquatic inhabitants into deeper waters.
