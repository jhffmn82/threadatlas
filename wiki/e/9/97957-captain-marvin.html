Captain Marvin

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Captain, Captain Marvin of the Mine Police, Marvin, Teddy Marvin, the police captain, the police officer

Abstract written by a model

Captain Marvin, formerly an ordinary civil police officer in Chicago, is captain of the Mine Police. He identifies Jack McMurdo through the Chicago shooting of Jonas Pinto, then leads police to McMurdo’s house, disarms and arrests him over the Herald office attack and searches unsuccessfully for a coining plant. Later, assisting Birdy Edwards, he surrounds Edwards’s house, stops McGinty, disarms the Scowrers, and takes them into custody.

Chapter IV

Captain Marvin leads police into McMurdo’s house, disarms him, and arrests him for involvement in the beating of Editor Stanger. He searches the house for the concealed coining plant but apparently finds nothing.

Chapter VII

Captain Marvin assists Birdy Edwards in the operation against the Scowrers. He leads the armed Mine Police surrounding Edwards’s house, prevents McGinty from escaping, disarms the gang, and takes them into custody.
