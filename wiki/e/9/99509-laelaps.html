Lælaps in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Lælaps is a dog given by Procris to Cephalus after their reconciliation, originally a celebrated gift said to have been made by Vulcan and passed through several owners. It surpasses other dogs in running and cannot be outpaced by any wild beast. Freed during a hunt, it pursues the beast with extraordinary speed until both are transformed into stone.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Lælaps is the dog that Procris gives Cephalus after their reconciliation, a gift once presented to her by Cynthia. He breaks free from his couples and pursues the wild beast with extraordinary speed, but is ultimately transformed into a marble statue.

notes .. notes

The dog was one of the celebrated gifts associated with Procris and Cephalus. The poets say it was made by Vulcan, presented to Jupiter, passed to Europa and then Minos, and finally given to Procris; it was later said to have been changed into stone with the monster it pursued.

Lelaps in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Lelaps, Cephalus's dog, could outrun every rival; given by Diana to Procris and then Cephalus, it chased the fox until heavenly powers turned both animals to stone.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PYTHON

Lelaps was Cephalus's dog, given to Procris by Diana and then given by Procris to Cephalus. It could outrun every rival, but when it chased the fox, both animals were turned to stone because the heavenly powers would not allow either to win.
