Erichthonius in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Erichthonius is described in differing accounts as son of Dardanus, succeeding the childless Ilus and fathering Tros by Astyoche; or as son of Hephaestus with Atthis or Athena. Raised by Athena and intended for immortality, he expels Amphictyon, becomes king of Athens, establishes Athena’s wooden image and the Panathenaea, marries Praxithea, fathers Pandion, and is buried in Athena’s precinct.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

XII. Ἠλέκτρας δὲ τῆς "Ἄτλαντος καὶ Διὸς

Erichthonius was a son of Dardanus. He succeeded to the kingdom after Ilus died childless and had Tros by Astyoche, daughter of Simoeis.

Κέκροπος δὲ ἀποθανόντος Κραναὸς «ἐβασί-

Erichthonius expels Amphictyon after Amphictyon has ruled for twelve years. He is said either to be the son of Hephaestus and Atthis or of Hephaestus and Athena.

Erichthonius was variously said to be the son of Hephaestus and Atthis, or of Hephaestus and Athena. Athena raised him, and he later expelled Amphictyon, became king of Athens, established the Panathenaea, and married Praxithea, with whom he had Pandion.
