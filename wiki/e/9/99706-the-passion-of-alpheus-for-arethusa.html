Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Alpheus pursues Arethusa after she flees from the stream where she was bathing. He follows her over distant regions until Diana hides her, and he continues searching around the concealing cloud.

notes .. notes

The passion of Alpheus for Arethusa was a poetic story based on the similarity between the names of the river and the fountain. It describes Alpheus pursuing Arethusa across many countries and beneath the sea, though Pausanias regarded the story as a mere fable.
