Abstract written by a model

This third volume is a collection of distinct, loosely connected parts linked by a conventional introductory thread and bearing little relation to Laurence Sterne beyond its title. It begins with the story of Caroline and Rosenfeld, then includes the hosts’ ballad, a play, and an honest preacher’s journal. It returns to sentimental adventures, vicarious begging for a maimed soldier-beggar and a child-murderess visit marked by a blank page, before ending with extended commentary on Schummel’s own work.
