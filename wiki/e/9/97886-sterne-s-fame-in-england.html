Sterne’s fame in England

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Sterne’s fame was immediate, his fame, his personal triumph

Abstract written by a model

Sterne’s fame in England became immediate after the first two volumes of Tristram Shandy appeared in 1760, bringing rapid renown and a major personal and literary triumph. English enthusiasm later declined into criticism or forgetfulness, before German fame reached its fullest strength and Sterne-worship followed.
