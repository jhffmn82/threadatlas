The Sun discovers Venus and Mars’s affair, reveals their meeting place to Vulcan, and prompts Vulcan to bind and publicly expose them before the gods, who laugh. A historical version has Helius obtain proof of a court lady’s intrigue and punish the courtier.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The Sun discovers Venus and Mars’s affair and reveals the place of their intrigue to Vulcan. This leads Vulcan to bind and publicly expose them before the gods, who laugh at them.

notes .. notes

The intrigue is discovered by the Sun. Palæphatus gives a historical version in which Helius learns of a court lady’s intrigue with a courtier, obtains ocular proof, and has the courtier severely punished.
