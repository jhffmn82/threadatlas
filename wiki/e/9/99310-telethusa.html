Telethusa

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: his wife, the mother

Abstract written by a model

Telethusa, a Cretan matron and Ligdus’s pregnant wife, bears Iphis, conceals her daughter’s sex and raises her as a boy after Isis’s dream instruction, then worships and seeks Isis’s protection as Iphis’s marriage nears.

Telethusa is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Telethusa is Ligdus's pregnant wife and the mother of Iphis. After Isis instructs her in a dream to disobey Ligdus, she raises her daughter as a boy, keeps the truth known only to the nurse, and later prays to Isis for help when Iphis's marriage approaches.
