Trachyn

place · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Herculean Trachyn, Trachinian land, city of Trachyn, the Hæmonian city

Abstract written by a model

Trachyn is a Herculean land and city ruled by Ceyx. It receives the banished Peleus after Phocus’s killing, though Fate bars his stay; later Halcyone is there when Morpheus, shaped as Ceyx, appears.

Trachyn is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
