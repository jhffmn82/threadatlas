Tiresias’s prophecy in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Tiresias’s prophecy warns that refusing Bacchus a temple will bring Bacchus to Greece and cause Pentheus’s dismemberment, ultimately fulfilled by Pentheus’s mother and Bacchantes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Tiresias prophesies that Pentheus will be scattered and torn apart if he refuses to honor Bacchus. The prophecy is fulfilled when Pentheus is dismembered by his mother and the other Bacchantes on Mount Cithæron.
