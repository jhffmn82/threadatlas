Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

The unit describes a belief that twins and sometimes their parents possess a power that quickens or multiplies living things. This power may attract or increase salmon, livestock, children, plantains, crops, and seed, although the text says that such beliefs are rare and exceptional rather than universal.
