the symbolic transformations of Philomela, Progne, and Tereus

Also called: changed, symbolical changes of Philomela, Progne, and Tereus, transformations, turned into a bird

Abstract written by a model

A symbolic account of transformations after Tereus pursues the sisters. Progne becomes a swallow, Philomela a nightingale, and Tereus the crested, long-billed epops or lapwing. These changes depict their differing characters and actions, though Anacreon and Apollodorus reverse the women’s transformations.

opening

After Tereus pursues the sisters, Progne is transformed into a swallow and Philomela into a nightingale. Tereus is transformed into a crested bird with a long bill, the epops or lapwing.

notes .. notes

The transformations are presented as symbolic depictions of the characters’ different natures and actions. The principal account associates Tereus with the lapwing, Philomela with the nightingale, and Progne with the swallow, although Anacreon and Apollodorus reverse the transformations of the two women.
