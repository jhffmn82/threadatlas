Abstract

Private data can be used in the retrieval process of RAG systems, but its use may create risks of sensitive-information leakage.

Introduction

Private data can improve the response quality of RAG systems but may expose sensitive information during retrieval and generation. The unit discusses this risk in contexts such as medical chatbots using patients’ historical diagnosis cases.
