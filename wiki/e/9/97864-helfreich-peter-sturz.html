Helfreich Peter Sturz in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Helfreich Peter Sturz, a master of English, accompanied King Christian VII of Denmark to France and England. In England in 1768–1769 he met David Garrick and learned of Sterne’s obscenities and declining reputation. Drawing on English sources, he opposed the Sterne cult, German distortions, and Yorick’s fashionable influence in Deutsches Museum writings.

Helfreich Peter Sturz opposed the Sterne cult and its German distortions, drawing his information and perspective directly from English sources. During his stay in England from 1768 to 1769, he met Garrick and learned of Sterne’s obscenities and declining moral reputation; he later criticized Yorick’s influence in writings for the Deutsches Museum, including the poem “Die Mode.”
