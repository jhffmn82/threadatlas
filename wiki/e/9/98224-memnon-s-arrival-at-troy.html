Memnon's arrival at Troy

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: arrival of Memnon at Troy, he cometh, the coming of that godlike man, παραγενόμενον ἐν Τροίᾳ

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Memnon arrives at Troy with a great force of Ethiopians to fight against the Greeks, kills many Greeks including Antilochus, and is then killed by Achilles. In The Fall of Troy, Memnon arrives at Troy with countless Aethiopians after Priam's urgent summons, is joyfully welcomed with gifts and a feast, and is hoped to save the city.
