1X.—APOLLO AND THE KINE oF ADMETUS

The Nootkas observed extensive regulations after the birth of twins, including parental seclusion, bathing, painted faces, ritual songs, and restrictions on eating or touching fresh food, especially salmon. They believed that the twins and their father could attract salmon, and that the birth of twins indicated a good salmon year.
