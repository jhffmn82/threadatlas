fishing for birds in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Fishing for birds is the Uns' main livelihood and an activity from Skyland's edge. The Uns direct travelers to drop lines over the skyle because the air is full of birds; Notta and Bob Up catch a cooked goose, a skye terrier, and packages of dreams.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The Uns say that they fish mostly for birds because the air is full of them. They direct the travelers to fish by going to the edge of the skyle and dropping their lines over it.

Chapter 11

Fishing for birds is practiced by Notta and Bob Up from the edge of the Skyland. Their lines first catch a cooked goose, then a skye terrier, and later packages of dreams.
