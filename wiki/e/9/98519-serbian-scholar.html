(8) Another Italian version of the story, recorded at Pisa,

The Serbian scholar travels with the priest and is forced by the giant to take a mouthful of the roasted priest’s flesh, which he secretly spits out. He blinds the sleeping giant with a sharpened stick, escapes in a ram’s skin among the sheep, and cuts off a finger trapped by the giant’s staff.
