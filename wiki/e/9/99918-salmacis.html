Salmacis

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Naiad, the Nymph

Abstract written by a model

Salmacis is a Naiad nymph who dwells at a pool, avoids hunting and archery, and bathes, arranges her hair, rests, and gathers flowers. After seeing Hermaphroditus, she loves and pursues him despite his resistance, clinging to him in water until their bodies are united.

Salmacis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Salmacis is a Naiad who does not hunt or practice archery and who spends her time bathing, arranging her hair, resting, and gathering flowers. She falls in love with Hermaphroditus, pursues him despite his resistance, and clings to him in the water until their bodies are united.
