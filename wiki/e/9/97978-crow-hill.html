Crow Hill in The Valley of Fear

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

Crow Hill is a large mine whose New England manager, Josiah H. Dunn, has maintained order and discipline despite the surrounding reign of terror. Lawler, Andrews, and several accomplices attack there and murder Dunn and the engineer Menzies.
