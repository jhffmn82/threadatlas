Abstract written by a model

HopRAG is a graph-structured RAG knowledge base built during indexing. It represents passages as vertices and directed logical relations as edges, using pseudo-queries to support flexible logical modeling, cross-document organization, and efficient construction and updating.

The graph-structured index G = (V, E) contains vertices storing passages and directed edges established from logical relations between passages.
