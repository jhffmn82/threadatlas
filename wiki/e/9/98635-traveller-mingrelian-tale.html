traveller (Mingrelian tale)

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: poor man, traveller, wanderer

Abstract written by a model

A traveller journeys from Redut-Kale toward Anaklia, is overtaken by night and attacked by wolves, and uses sticks on his horse’s tail and a sword to keep them at bay. A light leads him to a lonely house, where a host shelters him and recounts the seven fishermen.
