Sterne’s unacknowledged borrowings, his high-handed and extensive appropriation

English imitations of Sterne reached Germany and were often grouped indiscriminately with the broader mass of Yorick production and influence. They included Samuel Paterson’s Another Traveller, Peter Pennyless, continuations of the Sentimental Journey, A Sentimental Journey intended as a sequel, and Fragments in the manner of Sterne, which were translated, adapted, or reprinted in German.
