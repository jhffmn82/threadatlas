Abstract written by a model

Tora is an old, gentle, shabby tailor of Oz who has long lived as a prisoner in Blankenburg. Invisible Blanks force him to make their clothes, while an invisible force bars him from the gate. He carries tailoring tools, silk, beeswax, thread, scissors, shears, a pin cushion, and three tape measures; his spectacles attach to detachable butterfly ears that can fly away, observe events, and report back. After the Blanks leave him with no memory of where he lived, he hides Snip, learns of Snip's trouble, and escapes through the gate when Snip pulls him through it. Traveling with Snip, Dorothy, Humpy, Kabumpo, and the others, he seeks a new shop in Emerald City, mends Dorothy's dress, plans clothes for Dorothy and Snip, and becomes Humpy's tailor. He is concerned by Kabumpo's dangerous travel and urges Humpy not to punish Mombi, since she alone can restore the King. In the throne room, Tora proposes finding whoever fits the King's robe. The robe and the Wizard of Oz's magic reveal that Tora is Pastoria under enchantment.

Tora is in one work on this site, The Lost King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

Tora is an old tailor forced to make clothes for the invisible Blanks and prevented from leaving his house by an invisible force. He hides Snip from the Blanks, learns of his troubles, and then escapes with him after Snip pulls him through the gate.

CHAPTER 14

Tora escapes Blankenburg with Snip's help and decides to travel with him while looking for a new tailoring shop. He explains the history of the Blanks and reveals that he removes his butterfly ears after breakfast so they can rest.

CHAPTER 15

Tora shares breakfast with Dorothy, Humpy, and Snip and listens to Dorothy's account. His ears can fly off, move independently, and tell him occasional odds and ends; he removes them and puts them in his pocket after Kabumpo's rude remarks. Tora mends Dorothy's dress and joins the journey to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 16

Tora lands in gooseberry bushes when Kabumpo stops and later expresses concern about the elephant's dangerous manner of travel. He advises Humpy not to punish Mombi because she is the only one who can restore the King. Tora continues toward the Emerald City with the others.

CHAPTER 17

Tora plans new clothes for Snip and Dorothy and has taken a great fancy to Dorothy. He enters the throne room with the others, where Humpy declares that Tora is his tailor and invites him to sit beside him.

CHAPTER 20

Tora uses his tailor's knowledge to suggest finding the person who fits the King's robe. He is revealed to be Pastoria when the robe and the Wizard of Oz's magic restore him.
