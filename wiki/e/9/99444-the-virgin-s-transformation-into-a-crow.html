opening

As the virgin raises her arms toward heaven, they grow black with light feathers, and her garments become feathers rooted in her skin. Her hands and breast change, she runs without being slowed by the sand, and she is lifted into the air and assigned to Minerva as a faultless companion.

notes .. notes

The Fable says that Coronis was transformed into a crow as punishment for making too faithful a report. The explanation states that history offers no insight into the foundation of this story.
