Retrieve-Reason-Prune pipeline

method · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: retrieve- reason-prune mechanism, retrieve-reason-prune mechanism

Abstract written by a model

Retrieve-Reason-Prune is the central retrieval process in HopRAG. It begins with a retrieval phase that finds lexically or semantically similar passages, then uses pseudo-queries and LLM reasoning to explore multi-hop graph neighbors. In its final pruning phase, it uses Helpfulness to remove candidates and identify truly relevant passages. It reports 36.25% higher answer metric and 20.97% higher retrieval F1 than conventional information retrieval approaches.

Retrieve-Reason-Prune pipeline is in one work on this site, HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
