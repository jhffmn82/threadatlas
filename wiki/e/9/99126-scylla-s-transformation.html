Scylla's transformation

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Scylla’s monster transformation, a brood of serpents and barking monsters, her loins grow hideous with barking monsters, transformed into a rock, turned into a rock

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Scylla’s transformation begins while she bathes, as Circe’s herbs and poisons replace her lower body with Cerberean jaws and savage barking monsters. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Scylla’s transformation is caused by Circe’s poisonous plants and incantations, turning her into a monstrous creature and later a rock that threatens sailors.
