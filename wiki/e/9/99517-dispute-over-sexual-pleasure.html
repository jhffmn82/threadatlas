dispute over sexual pleasure

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: dispute, pleasure, pleasure of you, {females}, that which falls to the lot of {us} males
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII: Jupiter claims that females receive greater sexual pleasure than males, while Juno denies it. Tiresias, who has experienced both sexes, is asked to decide the dispute and confirms Jupiter's claim.
