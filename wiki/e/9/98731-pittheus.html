Pittheus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Pittheus is associated with a three-seat monument at Troezen where he and two others were said to judge. He built the oldest-known temple of Thearian Apollo, taught language, erected an altar to the Muses, and united Hyperea and Anthea into Troezen.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIX.

Pittheus was associated with the monument bearing three seats where he and two others were said to give judgments. He was also credited with building the temple of Thearian Apollo, teaching the art of language, erecting an altar to the Muses, and joining Hyperea and Anthea into the city of Troezen.
