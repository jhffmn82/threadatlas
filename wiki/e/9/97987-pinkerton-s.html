Pinkerton’s in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

Pinkerton’s is a detective organization employed by corporations and railroads to investigate and prosecute the Scowrers. It assigns its best investigator, Birdy Edwards, to secretly infiltrate and dismantle the group, with only Captain Marvin and employers knowing his identity.
