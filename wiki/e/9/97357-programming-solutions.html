The CODERAG-BENCH .. 2.4

Programming solutions form one retrieval source and contain one document for each HumanEval and MBPP problem with a canonical solution. Each document concatenates the natural-language problem and its program solution, and the source contains about 1.1 thousand documents.

RACG with Open Retrieval

Programming solutions are the most helpful open source for ODEX, producing gains of 3.8–4.3 points. They can exemplify correct library usage, and BM25 and OpenAI embeddings retrieve higher-quality solutions than GIST-large.
