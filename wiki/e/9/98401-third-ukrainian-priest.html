VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

The third Ukrainian priest offers the sinner a penance: planting and watering an apple-wood staff until it bears ripe apples. He later places the sinner in a locked well, and after the sinner dies, declares him saved when the keys are recovered from a fish.
