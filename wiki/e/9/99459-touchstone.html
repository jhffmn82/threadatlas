Abstract written by a model

The Touchstone is a hard stone bearing undeserved old disgrace, formed when Mercury turns Battus into stone after Battus betrays him.

Touchstone is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
