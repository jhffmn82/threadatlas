Touchstone in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The Touchstone is a hard stone bearing undeserved old disgrace, formed when Mercury turns Battus into stone after Battus betrays him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Battus first points to a stone while promising that it will reveal Mercury's theft before he does. After Battus betrays Mercury, Mercury turns Battus into a hard stone that is called the Touchstone.
