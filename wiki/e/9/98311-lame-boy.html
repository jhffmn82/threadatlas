Lame Boy

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Lame Boy, the boy, the lad

Abstract written by a model

Lame Boy is a boy who first asks Obassi Osaw for fire and is refused. He serves the Sky God, steals a fire-brand from the house of the god's wives, wraps it in plantain leaves and his cloth, and brings fire to earth. Akpan punishes him by making him unable to walk, and he becomes lame.

Lame Boy is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
