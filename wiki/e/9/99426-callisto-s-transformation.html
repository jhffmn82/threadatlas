Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is described as a punishment inflicted by Juno in which Calisto is transformed into a bear. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is the event in which Juno changes Callisto into a bear.
