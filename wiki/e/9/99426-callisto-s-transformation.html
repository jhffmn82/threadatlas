Callisto's transformation in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

changes Callisto [into a bear] Juno changes Callisto into a bear, causing her hands to become rounded feet with claws, her mouth to become jaws, and her voice to become a growl. Callisto's former disposition remains despite her animal form, and she is later placed among the stars with her son.
