Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

When Daphne prays for aid, a heavy torpor seizes her limbs and her body changes into a laurel. Bark covers her breasts, her hair becomes leaves, her arms become branches, and her feet take root, while her former elegance remains.

notes .. notes

The explanation treats Daphne’s transformation as possibly arising from laurels growing near the place where she died or from the meaning of her name, Daphne, which is Greek for laurel. It also records alternative accounts in which Daphne is Manto or in which Apollo exposes Leucippus’s disguise, after which Daphne and her companions kill him.
