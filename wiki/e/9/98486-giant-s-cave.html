XIII.—Untyssts AND PoLYPHEMUS

The giant's cave was the robbers' prison and was strongly enclosed by masonry, with an iron-barred door as its only exit. The famous robber escaped from it by hiding among the giant's sheep and passing through the doorway with the flock.
