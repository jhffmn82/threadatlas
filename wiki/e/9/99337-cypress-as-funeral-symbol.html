cypress as funeral symbol

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Roman custom of placing branches of Cypress, the symbol of sorrow, used at funerals
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV: Apollo’s words explain the custom of placing cypress branches before houses where a dead body lay and using the tree at funerals and near graves. The cypress became a symbol of sorrow, possibly because of its melancholy appearance and its association with death.
