Chapter 16

The Prime Preserve is an important green, pickled official who writes messages on a pad because the Preserves' voices do not carry well through their lids. He arrests the travelers, advises preserving them whole for a royal museum, and tries to warn the Imperial Squawmos when Notta reaches the upward button.
