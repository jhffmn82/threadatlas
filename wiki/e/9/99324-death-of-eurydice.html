Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the death of Eurydice occurs when a serpent bites her ankle as she strolls with the Naiads, causing Orpheus to mourn and descend to the Infernal Regions. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the death of Eurydice is caused by a venomous snakebite while she flees from Aristaeus.
