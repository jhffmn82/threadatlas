BOOK THE TENTH.

The death of Eurydice occurs when a serpent bites her ankle while she is strolling in the fields with Naiads. Her death causes Orpheus to mourn and undertake his descent to the Infernal Regions.

is_caused_by snake that killed Eurydice [venomous bite] The death of Eurydice occurred shortly after her marriage to Orpheus. While fleeing from Aristaeus, she stepped on a snake in the grass, was bitten in the foot, and died.

Chapter XVII

The death of Eurydice occurred shortly after her marriage to Orpheus. While fleeing from Aristaeus, she stepped on a snake in the grass, was bitten in the foot, and died.
