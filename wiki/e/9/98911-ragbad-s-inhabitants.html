Ragbad's inhabitants in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Ragbad’s inhabitants once comprised workers, servants, nobles, and soldiers; poverty drove most away, leaving twenty-seven families: Redsmith, Miller, Baker, and twenty-four rag-picking laborers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Ragbad's inhabitants once included many workers, servants, nobles, and soldiers, but most left because of the kingdom's poverty. Only twenty-seven families remain, including the Redsmith, the Miller, the Baker, and twenty-four rustic laborers who work as rag-pickers.
