Abstract written by a model

Enhanced retrieval processes select graph information relevant to a query, including nodes, edges, triples, paths, or communities, while incorporating textual and topological information. Retrieval is necessary because providing all graph text yields suboptimal LLM performance. Selecting subgraphs and using topology during generation mitigates redundant graph information and improves performance.
