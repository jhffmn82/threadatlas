Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

The window immediately to the right of the drawbridge is open as it was found after the murder. It is narrow but large enough for a person to squeeze through, and investigators consider it the suspected killer’s route of escape.

Chapter V

The study window was regarded by Barker as the route by which the murderer escaped. He based this belief on the bloodstain and the fact that the drawbridge was raised.
