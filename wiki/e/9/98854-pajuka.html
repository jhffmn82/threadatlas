Mombi transforms him into a great white goose, while transforming the King into a stuffed man. Though he sometimes notes advantages in being a goose, he misses his own self, pockets, slippers, castle, hunting lodge, pipes, smoking jacket, and human company. He crosses the jellied sea, is captured in Catty Corners, and faces being cooked for the cat queen, fearing death but warning that eating him would harm the cats. Pajuka demands that Mombi restore both himself and the King, but Mombi and the Wizard initially fail to end his enchantment. When Tora is restored as Pastoria, Pajuka recognizes both Pastoria and the golden feather as Pastoria's magic quill; Pastoria's restoration ends Pajuka's goose form. He stays close to Pastoria as prime minister. At the end, he rides in a procession smoking two pipes, grieves Snip's departure, and, forgetting he is no longer a goose, dives fully dressed into a pond.

Pajuka in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Pajuka is the loyal Prime Minister of Oz, formerly serving King Kinda Jolly, also identified as Humpy and later Pastoria. Mombi transforms him into a great white goose, while transforming the King into a stuffed man. Though he sometimes notes advantages in being a goose, he misses his own self, pockets, slippers, castle, hunting lodge, pipes, smoking jacket, and human company. His central purpose is faithful service to the lost King: he searches throughout Oz for both the King and Mombi, says he can recognize the King in any shape, and remains deeply attached to his former master. During the search, Pajuka protects Snip from Mombi, brings him food, travels with Snip and Mombi toward the Emerald City, and describes the King as kind, gentle, tall, and absent-minded. He crosses the jellied sea, is captured in Catty Corners, and faces being cooked for the cat queen, fearing death but warning that eating him would harm the cats. After separation from Snip, he is reunited with him and recognizes Humpy as the King. He supports Humpy's authority, helps reveal that an apparent mountain is passable, and urges caution against freeing Mombi. Pajuka demands that Mombi restore both himself and the King, but Mombi and the Wizard initially fail to end his enchantment. When Tora is restored as Pastoria, Pajuka recognizes both Pastoria and the golden feather as Pastoria's magic quill; Pastoria's restoration ends Pajuka's goose form. He stays close to Pastoria as prime minister. At the end, he rides in a procession smoking two pipes, grieves Snip's departure, and, forgetting he is no longer a goose, dives fully dressed into a pond.

Narrative, section by section

13 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

Pajuka is a Prime Minister who has been transformed into a great white goose by Mombi. He resists being cooked, protects Snip from Mombi, and persuades her to help find King Kinda Jolly. He claims that he could recognize the King in any shape.

CHAPTER 4

Pajuka helps Mombi keep watch over Snip and tells him that he has searched all over Oz for Mombi and the King. He explains that Mombi changed him into a goose and says that, despite some advantages of being a goose, he most misses having pockets and being himself.

CHAPTER 5

Pajuka brings Snip grapes and a sugar bun for breakfast and travels with him and Mombi toward the Emerald City. He explains that the King of Oz was kind, gentle, tall, and absent-minded, and that he misses him greatly.

CHAPTER 6

Pajuka travels with Snip and Mombi while searching for the King, and he says he is homesick for his slippers, castle, and pipe. He crosses the jellied sea, is captured by cat guards in Catty Corners, and is selected as the cat queen's dinner. He warns that he is bad for cats and will give them fits if they eat him.

CHAPTER 7

Pajuka fears that the cats will cook and eat him, and he asks Snip to save some of his feathers for Ozma if he dies. He falls asleep despite Snip's attempts to keep him awake, and Snip carries him out of the summer house after Mombi defeats the cats.

CHAPTER 13

Pajuka awakens when Mombi throws Snip into the well and tries to catch him, but Snip only manages to seize some of his feathers. Snip tells Tora that Pajuka is the Prime Minister and is in danger of being roasted, eaten, or lost.

CHAPTER 14

Pajuka is a good goose whom Snip hopes to find. Snip wonders whether Ozma can change Pajuka back from a goose into his own form.

CHAPTER 15

Pajuka is a goose who serves as Prime Minister and is trying to find the lost King of Oz. Snip worries that Mombi will get rid of Pajuka once she finds the King.

CHAPTER 16

Pajuka recognizes Snip and joyfully reunites with him after Mombi brings him through the mountain. He identifies Humpy as his former master, the King, and explains that Mombi transformed the King into a stuffed man, turned Pajuka into a goose, and threw Snip down a well. Pajuka supports Humpy's authority and flies through the apparent mountain to show that it is not solid.

CHAPTER 17

Pajuka worries whether he and the King will ever return to their former selves and longs for his hunting lodge, pipes, and smoking jacket. At the Palace of Emeralds, he warns the others not to release Mombi and is terrified when she begins counting and casting her spell.

CHAPTER 19

Pajuka demands that Mombi restore his own shape and the King, while explaining that everyone has been bewitched. He identifies Humpy as the King of Oz, but remains a goose after both Mombi's and the Wizard's attempts fail.

CHAPTER 20

Pajuka identifies the golden feather as the magic quill Pastoria gave him and recognizes Pastoria when Tora is restored. His enchantment as a goose ends with Pastoria's restoration, and he remains close to Pastoria as prime minister.

CHAPTER 21

Pajuka rides the Comfortable Camel in the procession while smoking two pipes and creating clouds of smoke. He is sad when Snip departs and dives fully dressed into the pond after forgetting that he is no longer a goose.
