Narcissus's reflection

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Narcissus's image, a shadow, a thing that has no substance, his own image, that which I may not touch, the fallacious image, the flying image, the form, the reflection of his own form

Abstract written by a model

A shadow without substance, Narcissus's reflection appears in a clear spring as a beautiful body, mirrors him, deceives his eyes, and retreats or distorts whenever the water is disturbed.

Narcissus's reflection is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Narcissus's reflection appears to him as a beautiful body, although it has no substance and is only a shadow. It mirrors his gestures, smiles, tears, and words, but disappears or becomes distorted when the water is disturbed.
