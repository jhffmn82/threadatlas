Narcissus's reflection in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

A shadow without substance, Narcissus's reflection appears in a clear spring as a beautiful body, mirrors him, deceives his eyes, and retreats or distorts whenever the water is disturbed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Narcissus's reflection appears to him as a beautiful body, although it has no substance and is only a shadow. It mirrors his gestures, smiles, tears, and words, but disappears or becomes distorted when the water is disturbed.
