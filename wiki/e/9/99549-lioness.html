lioness

animal · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a lioness, lions, savage lioness, the lion

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the lioness drinks at a spring after slaughtering oxen and tears Thisbe's veil as Thisbe flees. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the lioness approaches a spring with blood on its jaws, frightens Thisbe away, and tears her discarded veil, causing Pyramus to believe she has been killed.
