lioness

Also called: a lioness, lions, savage lioness, the lion
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the lioness drinks at a spring after slaughtering oxen and tears Thisbe's veil as Thisbe flees.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the lioness approaches a spring with blood on its jaws, frightens Thisbe away, and tears her discarded veil, causing Pyramus to believe she has been killed.
