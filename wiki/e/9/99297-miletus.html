FABLE V. [IX.426-665]

Miletus, the son of Deione and Phœbus, is a youthful rival whom the aged Minos fears may seek his kingdom. Miletus flees across the Ægean and founds a city in Asia; with Cyane, he fathers Byblis and Caunus.

Miletus (the Cretan leader) in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Miletus, a Cretan leader, fled Minos with a force, led Cretans to Anactoria, and gave his name to the renamed town and country of Miletus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Miletus led an expedition of Cretans to the country formerly called Anactoria, after which the town and country took the name Miletus. He and his force had fled Crete from Minos, the son of Europa.
