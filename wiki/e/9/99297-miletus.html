Miletus

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Miletus (the Cretan leader), her father, the Mæandrian youth, the son of Deione

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Miletus, son of Deione and Phœbus, fled Minos across the Ægean, founded a city in Asia, and with Cyane fathered Byblis and Caunus. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Miletus fled Minos with a force, led Cretans to Anactoria, and gave his name to the renamed town and country of Miletus.

CHAPTER II.

Miletus led an expedition of Cretans to the country formerly called Anactoria, after which the town and country took the name Miletus. He and his force had fled Crete from Minos, the son of Europa.
