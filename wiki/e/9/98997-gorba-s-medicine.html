Gorba's medicine in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Gorba's medicine is a green, labeled bottle presented as a sure cure for everything, with specific directions for different ailments. After a fall, Grampa takes a spoonful and is immediately restored from injuries and bad humor. Two drops on the head cure poison, including Grampa’s chimney and bay window. Mouthfuls save Tatters and Grampa from drowning, and the medicine temporarily protects travelers from burns, scalds, heat stroke, and boiling lava as they cross Fire Island and endure its eruption.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

The wizard's patent medicine is labeled as a sure cure for everything, with directions to follow the instructions on the bottle. Grampa takes a dose after the fall and is immediately restored from his injuries and bad humor.

CHAPTER 7

The magic medicine bottle claims to have a cure for everything, including poison of any nature. Its label directs that two drops be placed on the head for poison, and the drops cure Grampa’s chimney and bay window.

CHAPTER 10

The wizard’s medicine saves Tatters and Grampa from drowning when each takes a mouthful. It also protects the travelers from burns, scalds, and heat stroke, allowing them to cross Fire Island safely for a time.

CHAPTER 11

Gorba's medicine keeps Grampa and his companions safe from the boiling lava during the eruption. Without it, they would have been blown clear out of the story.
