occurs_at Aulis [during a sacrifice to Jupiter] The serpent-and-birds omen occurs at Aulis during a Greek sacrifice to Jupiter. An azure serpent devours eight birds and their mother, and Calchas interprets the event as predicting victory after nine years of war.
