Medea's rejuvenation of Aeson

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: become young again, rejuvenation, renewed, restore, restored, the recovery of the golden Fleece, the same operation, what Medea had done for AESON

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it is the rejuvenation performed on Æson through Medea's rites and magical mixture, while the transformation of the ram falsely serves as proof that Pelias can also be rejuvenated. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is Medea's restoration of Aeson to youthful vigor through nine nights of plants, sacrifices, prayers, sleep, and a magical caldron.
