Abstract written by a model

The Naiads of Ausonia are nymphs associated with Janus’s fountain; answering Venus, they heat its springs with sulphur, fire, and pitch, blocking the Sabines and protecting the Romans.

FABLES XII. AND XIII. [XIV.698-851]

The Naiads of Ausonia respond to Venus's request for aid. They release the springs near Janus and, with sulphur, fire, and pitch, heat the fountain until its water blocks the gate against the Sabines.
