Abstract written by a model

Celenæ is a Phrygian town near the river Marsyas, where the river fell from a precipice and Marsyas's skin, suspended by Apollo, was reportedly still visible.

Celenæ is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Celenæ was the Phrygian town near which the river Marsyas fell from a precipice. Apollo was said to have suspended Marsyas's skin there, and Herodotus reported that the skin could still be seen in the town.
