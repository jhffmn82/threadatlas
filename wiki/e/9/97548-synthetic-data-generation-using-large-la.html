Related Works

Synthetic data generation using large language models includes zero-shot prompting, noise-robust re-weighting, mixed soft prompts, and control over attributes such as length and style. Tang et al. (2023) and Xie et al. propose privacy-aware generation methods, but the resulting synthetic data is not guaranteed to include contextual information from the original data, limiting its fit for RAG systems.
