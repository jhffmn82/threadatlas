flooding of the village

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: all the country sunk in a lake, chastisement, flooding and punishment of the village, the other parts sinking in a morass, this calamity

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the flooding and punishment of the village overwhelmed the impious village as its houses and surrounding ground sank into a morass, while Philemon and Baucis escaped to the mountain and their cottage alone remained. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the flooding of the village caused the country around Baucis and Philemon's home to sink into a lake, leaving only their cottage standing before it was changed into a temple.
