the anonymous warning letter

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: an anonymous communication, the coded warning, the inclosed official report, the letter, the strange message, the warning received through the man Porlock, this communication

Abstract written by a model

An anonymous communication sent from Camberwell under the name Porlock warns Sherlock Holmes that Mr. Douglas of Birlstone Manor House faces imminent danger. Its coded message is recovered through numbered words in an older Whitaker’s Almanac, and the warning is followed by the death of John Douglas.

the anonymous warning letter is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PART I / Chapter I

The coded warning concerns imminent danger to Douglas, a rich country gentleman at Birlstone. Its meaning is recovered by using numbered words from an older edition of Whitaker’s Almanac.

Chapter II

The anonymous warning letter was sent to Sherlock Holmes from Camberwell and warned that danger threatened a certain person. It was associated with a cipher and was followed by the actual death of John Douglas; the letter was sent by someone using the name Porlock.
