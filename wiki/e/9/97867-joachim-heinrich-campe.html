Joachim Heinrich Campe criticized the degeneration of Empfindsamkeit into Empfindelei in works published in 1779 and later. He argued that genuine sensibility is natural, private, serious, and expressed through deeds, whereas affected sentimentality is fantastic, theatrical, voluble, and fruitless.
