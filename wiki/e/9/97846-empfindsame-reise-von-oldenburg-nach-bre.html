Abstract written by a model

Empfindsame Reise von Oldenburg nach Bremen, written by Hanoverian army officer H. J. C. Hedemann, satirizes the sentimentalism of earlier travelers rather than offering conventional tearful sympathy. It proceeds through Sterne-like whimsy, self-conscious writing commentary, mock promises, and self-examination, ending with Das Ständchen and its double entendre.

Empfindsame Reise von Oldenburg nach Bremen was written by a Hanoverian army officer and is not a conventional sentimental journey filled with tearful sympathy toward people and animals. Nevertheless, it adopts Sterne-like whimsy, self-conscious discussion of writing, mock promises, formal self-examination, and satire of sentimental travel conventions. Its closing story, Das Ständchen, is told without much whim or satire but includes a Sternian double entendre.
