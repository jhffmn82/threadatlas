the Hoopers in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

The Hoopers are ten-foot-high, thin, flat people living together in the Hoopers' park. They bend to grasp their toes and roll like hoops, attacking Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi until Snip drives several away with Mombi's crooked stick and they flee with Rollo the Royal.

CHAPTER 5

The Hoopers are tall, thin, flat people who can bend over, seize their toes, and roll like hoops. They attack Snip, Pajuka, and Mombi, but Snip knocks several of them away with Mombi's crooked stick before they flee with Rollo the Royal.
