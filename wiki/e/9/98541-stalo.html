Stalo

Also called: This Stalo, a Stalo, the Stalo

Abstract written by a model

Stalo is a householder who owns a house, kitchen, sheep, and old dog, and keeps Slyboots to fatten and eat. Slyboots blinds Stalo with molten lead, deceives him with dog meat, and escapes in a ram’s skin.

Stalo is in one work on this site, The Library. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
