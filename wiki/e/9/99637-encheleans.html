Encheleans

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Enchelians, the Enchelians, ᾿Εγχελέας
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Enchelians wage war against the Illyrians with Cadmus's assistance, and after the Illyrians are defeated, give the crown to Cadmus.
  • In The Library, the Encheleans are attacked by Illyrians, receive an oracle, appoint Cadmus and Harmonia as leaders, and defeat the Illyrians.
