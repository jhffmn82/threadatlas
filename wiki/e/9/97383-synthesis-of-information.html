Synthesis of information is one of the capabilities that FRAMES evaluates alongside factuality and retrieval. Systems are expected to combine information from diverse documents and produce coherent responses. Synthesis is one of the dimensions evaluated by FRAMES. The benchmark assesses how information is integrated into coherent responses.
