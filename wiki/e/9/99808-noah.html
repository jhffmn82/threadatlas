Noah

Abstract written by a model

Noah is a just man included in histories related by Scriptures, identified by some writers with Prometheus and compared with Deucalion. Saved with his family for virtuous conduct, he leaves the ark and offers solemn sacrifices to God.

Noah is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
