Noah in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Noah is a just man included in histories related by Scriptures, identified by some writers with Prometheus and compared with Deucalion. Saved with his family for virtuous conduct, he leaves the ark and offers solemn sacrifices to God.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

Noah is identified as the just man whose merit corresponds to the merit attributed to Deucalion in the tradition described.

notes .. notes

Noah and his family are presented as counterparts to Deucalion and Pyrrha. Noah was saved for his virtuous conduct and offered solemn sacrifices to God after leaving the ark.
