Abstract written by a model

Monday Mountain is a steep blue mountain covered by vapor, home to Tubbies and grazing clothes horses. The Tubbies take Dorothy and Percy to its top; later Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere are imprisoned there to wash all day and eat potatoes and cabbage, before escaping down its side.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

Monday Mountain is a steep, blue mountain covered by clouds of vapor. It is inhabited by wild wash women, the Tubbies, and herds of clothes horses. The Tubbies take Dorothy and Percy to its top.

CHAPTER 16

Monday Mountain is where Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere are held prisoner, with washing required from morning until night and potatoes and cabbage served for dinner. The three escape by sliding down its side after the Tubbies overturn their wash tubs.
