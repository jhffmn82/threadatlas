the gunshot in The Valley of Fear

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The gunshot, apparently causing Mr. Douglas’s murder, was probably fired at a quarter to eleven. Mrs. Allen heard what seemed to be a slamming door, while Mrs. Douglas heard the shooting, came downstairs in her dressing gown, and was stopped by Cecil Barker, who said she could do nothing.

Chapter VI

Holmes concludes that the gunshot was probably fired at a quarter to eleven, when Mrs. Allen heard what she thought was a door slamming. He notes that a shotgun fired at close range would be muffled but should still have been heard in the house.
