Abstract written by a model

NDCG@10 is a retrieval performance metric and the primary percentage metric in CODERAG-BENCH. It is reported across benchmark tasks, where SFR-Embedding-Mistral averages 67.0 and BM25 57.7.

NDCG@10 is in one work on this site, CODERAG-BENCH: Can Retrieval Augment Code Generation?. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
