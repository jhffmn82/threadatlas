RAPTOR in OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models

Kartik Sharma · 2025-11-04/2025-11-09

RAPTOR is a graph-based, retrieval-based method that clusters similar chunks, summarizes them through an LLM, and contributes a semantic-similarity structure. Evaluated as a baseline on Soybean, Wheat, and News, it is compared on retrieval, answer quality, efficiency, and factual deduction; it matches OG-RAG’s News context recall but is slower and inaccurate on Soybean deduction with GPT-4o.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Related Work

RAPTOR is a graph-based retrieval approach that contributes a semantic similarity structure. SiReRAG combines this structure with GraphRAG's entity-driven structure.

Experimental Setup

RAPTOR is a retrieval-based baseline that clusters similar chunks and summarizes them through an LLM. Its Context Recall and Context Entity Recall are 0.54 and 0.19 for Soybean, 0.85 and 0.29 for Wheat, and 0.82 and 0.46 for News.

Experiments / Query answering .. Ontology sensitivity

RAPTOR is compared with OG-RAG, RAG, and GraphRAG on retrieval, answer quality, efficiency, and factual deduction. It matches OG-RAG's context recall on the News dataset, but it is slower and produces an unexpectedly inaccurate deductive answer for Soybean with GPT-4o.
