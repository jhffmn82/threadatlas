Ariadne's crown

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a golden crown, crown, crown of Ariadne, crown taken from off her head, her crown, the crown which Ariadne wore
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Bacchus casts Ariadne's crown skyward; its jewels become heavenly fires, forming a crown constellation and immortalizing Ariadne's name.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Ariadne's crown is a golden wedding present from Bacchus, enriched with gems; after Ariadne dies, Bacchus throws it into the sky, where its gems become stars while it retains its original form and becomes Ariadne's crown constellation.
