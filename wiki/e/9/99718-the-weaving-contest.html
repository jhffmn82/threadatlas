the weaving contest

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: contest, contest of skill, this contest, two webs

Abstract written by a model

is_a contest of skill [in weaving and working wool] Minerva and Arachne weave separate webs using fine warp, woof, shuttles, and vivid colors. Minerva depicts the contest between Neptune and herself for the city and adds examples of mortals punished for challenging the gods. Arachne depicts Europa and numerous divine transformations and deceptions, producing work that Minerva cannot blame for its craftsmanship.
