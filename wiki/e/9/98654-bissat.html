(33) A version of the tale which presents the main features

Bissat sets out to save the Oghuz and avenge his brother, who was killed by Depé Ghoz. He escapes from the giant’s ox-hide boot, blinds him, gets out disguised in a ram’s skin, and ultimately kills him.
