Emathian sisters in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The Emathian sisters lose a contest to the Muses, then abuse their opponents and despise threatening language. Punished after their defeat, they transform as feathers grow from their nails and arms, their faces become hard beaks, and they become magpies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The Emathian sisters are defeated, abuse their opponents, and despise the threatening language directed at them. As they try to speak and gesture, feathers grow from their nails, their arms become covered with feathers, their faces form hard beaks, and they become birds.
