Abstract written by a model

Vermissa is a town in the United States, the central township at the head of Vermissa Valley and the largest town on the railroad line. A bleak, dirty mining settlement of wooden houses, stores, saloons, gaming houses, mines, and factories, it includes the Union House, McGinty’s saloon, Widow MacNamara’s lodging house, and Lodge 341. The lodge operates from the Union House and, with the Scowrers and the Freeman’s society, dominates the coal district through violence, extortion, murder, and retaliation. Small owners are driven out, police cannot secure convictions, and residents fear both the organization and possible intervention by authorities. By the end, citizens begin to unite against the terror amid rumors of secret meetings and firearms.

Vermissa is in one work on this site, The Valley of Fear. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

PART II / Chapter I

Vermissa is the central township at the head of Vermissa Valley and the largest town on the railroad line. It is portrayed as a bleak, dirty settlement of wooden houses, stores, saloons, and gaming houses, dominated by fear of the Scowrers.

Chapter III

Vermissa contains the Widow MacNamara’s lodging house, McGinty’s saloon, and Lodge 341 at the Union House. The town is part of a coal district terrorized by organized criminal violence and has a lodge with about sixty assembled members.

Vermissa is the town in which the lodge operates and where the attack on the Herald takes place. The lodge's violence and extortion have made the district notorious, driven out small owners, and caused fear of eventual intervention by the authorities.

Chapter IV

Vermissa is a grimy mining town surrounded by mines and factories, where the Freeman’s society exercises powerful influence. Its residents live under fear of murder and retaliation, while the police struggle to secure convictions against the Scowrers.

Chapter VI

Vermissa is under the Scowrers’ reign of terror, though its citizens are beginning to band together against them. Rumors also circulate there about secret meetings and the distribution of firearms.
