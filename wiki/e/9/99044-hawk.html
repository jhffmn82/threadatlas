Hawk, formerly Dædalion, is a predatory bird that hunts and attacks other birds, grieving itself while causing them grief.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLES V. AND VI. [XI.221-409]

The hawk was formerly Dædalion. It is hostile to other birds, attacks them without kindness, and becomes a source of grief to them while grieving itself.
