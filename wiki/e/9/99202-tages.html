Tages in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Tages is a diviner and first teacher of Etruscan divination. He arose from a fate-revealing clod moving in a field, took human form, announced future destinies, and taught the Etrurian nation to foretell events; his unknown origin led to his being called son of the earth.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLES IV. V. AND VI. [XV.479-621]

Tages arose when a fate-revealing clod of earth moved in a field, took on human form, and opened its newly made mouth to announce future destinies. The natives called him Tages, and he was the first to teach the Etrurian nation to foretell future events.
