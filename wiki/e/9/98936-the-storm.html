the storm in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

A violent storm rolls over Red Mountain and Ragbad with wind, lightning, thunder, and rain, damaging roofs, the red mill, cottages, the castle, a quilt, and the rag crop and textile industry. It causes King Fumbo to lose his head; afterward Prince Tatters tells Bill, who links it to his storm-born origin.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The storm rolls over Red Mountain with violent wind, lightning, thunder, and rain. It blows off ten roofs and the wings of the red mill, shakes the cottages, damages the castle, scatters Mrs Sew-and-Sew's quilt, and threatens the rag crop.

CHAPTER 3

The storm in Ragbad caused Fumbo’s head to be lost. Prince Tatters tells Bill about it, and Bill connects it to his own origin because he found life during a different storm.
