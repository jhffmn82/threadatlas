Of the sons born to Leda Castor practised the art

Cattle booty was driven from Arcadia by Castor, Pollux, Idas, and Lynceus. Idas divided it into four parts, consumed his own share and his brother’s, and drove the cattle to Messene; the Dioscuri later recovered that booty along with much other spoil.
