Abstract written by a model

is_a state of bodily decline [associated with infirmity and loss of vigor] Old age has nearly brought Æson to death and afflicts Pelias. Medea claims that her arts can remove it, and she restores Æson’s youthful appearance and vigor while deceiving the daughters of Pelias into believing Pelias can receive the same benefit.
