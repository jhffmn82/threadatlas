describes Numa Pompilius [as ruling in peace and promoting piety, justice, and good laws] Dionysius of Halicarnassus states that Crotona was founded only in the fourth year of Numa Pompilius’s reign. He records Roman traditions about Numa’s peaceful reign and alleged consultations with Egeria or a Muse, while also reporting the view that Numa feigned divine conversations to strengthen his laws.
