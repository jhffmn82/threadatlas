Also called: Maybe Mountains of Oz, the mountain

Abstract written by a model

Maybe Mountain is a steep, craggy mountain associated with the City of Gold, topped by Peer Haps’s castle and covered in wild flowers. Percy Vere falls down it into the Winkle land; later the Princess returns by it, the Prince of Ragbad follows its flower trail, and Tatters loses the fairy’s tracks at its summit.

Maybe Mountain is in one work on this site, Grampa in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 9

Maybe Mountain is associated with the City of Gold and has steep, craggy sides. Percy Vere falls down the mountain and arrives at the bottom scratched and bruised before wandering into the Winkle land.

CHAPTER 19

Maybe Mountain is the route by which the Princess returns to Peer Haps's castle and the place where the Prince of Ragbad follows the flower trail. Its summit is covered with wild flowers, making it impossible for Tatters to trace the fairy's footsteps farther.
