graph-based reasoning in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

Graph-based reasoning is identified as a task on which Large Language Models have demonstrated capabilities. The unit evaluates GRAG in graph reasoning scenarios and reports that it outperforms RAG-based retrievers and LLM baselines. LLM contextual reasoning capabilities benefit graph reasoning. Some graph retrieval problems can be simplified into reasoning chains that retrieve the path between a question and a target entity.
