authenticity of Sterne’s letters in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

A topic of repeated German debate, especially over the Yorick-Eliza letters: some reviewers accepted them as genuine, others suspected fabricated letters or collections; Frankfurter Gelehrte Anzeigen defended authenticity vigorously.

Naturally Sterne’s letters found readers in Germany, the Yorick-Eliza correspond

The authenticity of Sterne’s letters was repeatedly debated in Germany. Some reviewers accepted the Yorick-Eliza letters as genuine, while others suspected that particular letters or collections were fabricated, including letters later identified by a confessedly spurious 1780 collection as imitations.
