synthetic data in Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data

Shenglai Zeng, Jiankun Zhang, Pengfei He, Jie Ren, Tianqi Zheng, Hanqing Lu, Han Xu, Hui Liu, Yue Xing, Jiliang Tang · 2025

Synthetic data is retrieval data generated for RAG through a two-stage pipeline. Stage-1 uses attribute-information pairs containing key information extracted from original data, but the resulting data may contain personally identifiable or linkable personal information. Stage-2 refines it for privacy. The generated data is used as retrieval data and preserves utility: it performs comparably to original data in medical dialog and open-domain question answering, surpasses Paraphrase and ZeroGen in medical dialog, and can outperform original data on NQ and WQ. It avoids highly similar sentences and exact copies because it is newly generated. Privacy evaluations show lower targeted and untargeted attack success than original data and simple paraphrasing, with agent-based refinement reducing leakage to nearly zero.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Methods

Synthetic data is generated from attribute-information pairs containing key information extracted from original data. It may initially contain personally identifiable information or personal information that can be linked to individuals, so Stage-2 refines it for privacy.

Utility of using synthetic data

Synthetic data is evaluated as retrieval data and achieves performance comparable to, and sometimes better than, original data in medical dialog and open-domain question answering. The text attributes some gains to preserving key information in Stage-1 and producing more purely useful information in Stage-2.

Privacy of using synthetic data

Synthetic data is evaluated as retrieval data for privacy leakage. Attribute-based generated data has lower targeted and untargeted attack success than original data and simple paraphrasing, and agent-based refinement further reduces leakage to nearly zero.

Conclusions

RAG data is generated synthetically through a two-stage pipeline. The generated RAG data achieves comparable performance to original data while mitigating associated privacy issues.
