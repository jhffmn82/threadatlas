Las

Abstract written by a model

Las is a town ten stades from the sea and forty from Gythium, between Ilium, Asia, and Cnacadium. Its sights include temples of Athene, Carnean Apollo, Dionysus, and Æsculapius, Galaco’s milky water, and the tomb and statue of its founder Las, killed by Patroclus.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Las lies ten stades from the sea and forty from Gythium, between the mountains Ilium, Asia, and Cnacadium. Its sights include temples of Athene, Carnean Apollo, Dionysus, and Æsculapius, and it contains the tomb and statue of its founder Las.
