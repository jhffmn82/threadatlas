Abstract written by a model

Roman Otilius, a Roman commander, was ordered to protect Athenians and Ætolians against Philip. He otherwise obeyed, but captured and razed Hestiæa and Anticyra despite their necessary submission to Philip, then was superseded by Flaminius.

CHAPTER VII.

Otilius, the best-known name of the Roman commander, was ordered to protect the Athenians and Ætolians against Philip. Although he otherwise obeyed his orders, he captured and razed Hestiæa and Anticyra, which had submitted to Philip out of necessity, and was then superseded by Flaminius.
