The temple of Hercules called Champion is dedicated to Hercules and contains white-stone and wooden statues, including works attributed to Xenocritus, Eubius, and Daedalus, later receiving colossal Pentelican-marble statues of Athene and Hercules.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The temple of Hercules called Champion contains a white-stone statue of Hercules by Xenocritus and Eubius, both Thebans, as well as an old wooden statue believed to be by Daedalus. Colossal statues of Athene and Hercules in Pentelican marble were also offered to this temple.
