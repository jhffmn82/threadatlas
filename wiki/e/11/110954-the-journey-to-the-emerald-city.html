The journey to the Emerald City is an event undertaken to save the Queen. It begins on a short foot-path in a gloomy forest, where Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper cling to the railing and each other as the path carries them rapidly onward. They learn the path runs straight to the Emerald City and reach its gates in less than a minute.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

During the Emerald City foot-path journey, Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper cling to the railing and to each other as the path carries them rapidly through the gloomy forest. After reading that the path runs straight to the Emerald City, they learn that they are not going to pieces and arrive at the city's gates in less than a minute.

CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy continue their journey through the pleasant land of the Winkies, following roads and traveling under banana trees. Their route is interrupted when they slide into the soap village, but they resume their escape by crossing its lake.
