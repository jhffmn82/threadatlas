Abstract written by a model

The chapel crypt is an ancient family burial place beneath the old ruined chapel, dark, damp, lonely, and feared at night. It contains family coffins, including an ancestral coffin, and newly found mummy remains concealed in a corner. Sir Robert visits after midnight and meets an unknown man; he and Mr. Norlett later use the crypt to conceal Lady Beatrice’s body after removing the ancestral remains.
