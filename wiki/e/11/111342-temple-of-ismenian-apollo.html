CHAPTER X.

The Temple of Ismenian Apollo has stone statues of Athene and Hermes at its entrance, called gods of the Vestibule; the Hermes is by Phidias and the Athene by Scopas. Inside is a cedarwood statue of Apollo attributed to Canachus, and nearby is Manto’s seat, the stone where Manto is said to have sat, along with statues of Henioche and Pyrrha.
