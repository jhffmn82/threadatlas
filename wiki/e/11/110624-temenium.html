Temenium in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Temenium is a fortified place belonging to the Argives, named for Temenus, who occupied and fortified it fighting with the Dorians against Tisamenus and the Achæans; Phrixus reaches the sea there, beside temples and Temenus’s monument.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXV.

Temenium belongs to the Argives and is named after Temenus, who occupied and fortified it while fighting with the Dorians against Tisamenus and the Achæans. The river Phrixus reaches the sea there, and the place has temples of Poseidon and Aphrodite as well as a monument of Temenus.
