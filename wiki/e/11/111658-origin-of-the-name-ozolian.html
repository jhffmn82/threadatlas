origin of the name Ozolian

Abstract written by a model

Ozolian is a name whose origin is explained by competing traditions. One tells of Orestheus burying a piece of wood born from a bitch, from which a vine grew and gave the Locrians their name; others invoke Nessus, river smell, asphodel, Aborigines, or untanned hides.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

One tradition says that Orestheus, son of Deucalion, buried a piece of wood born from a bitch, from which a vine grew the next spring; the Locrians supposedly took their name from its branches. Other traditions connect the name with the rotting body of Nessus, the unpleasant smell of a river, the smell of abundant asphodel, or the odor of untanned hides worn by the first inhabitants.
