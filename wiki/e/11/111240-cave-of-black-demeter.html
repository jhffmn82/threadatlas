Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLII.

The cave of Black Demeter is on Mount Elaion near Phigalia and was considered sacred because Demeter had stayed there. It has a grove of oak trees around it and a spring where warm water bubbles up.
