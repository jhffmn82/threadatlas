Sir Robert Norberton

Abstract written by a model

Sir Robert Norberton is an unmarried baronet, prominent and daring horse racer, and owner of Shoscombe Prince. He lives with and depends financially on his widowed sister, Lady Beatrice Falder, while holding only a life interest in Shoscombe Old Place and facing deep gambling debts and creditors. His fortune is staked on Shoscombe Prince winning the Derby, and his obsession with the horse appears mentally destabilizing. As Lady Beatrice’s death would bring financial ruin and seizure of the estate, he stops visiting her, gives away her spaniel, and visits the crypt at night. Suspected of murdering her, he ultimately admits concealing her body in the chapel crypt and arranging Mr. Norlett’s personation of her until the Derby’s result.

THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

Sir Robert Norberton is described as a dangerous, reckless rider who has staked his finances on Shoscombe Prince winning the Derby. He has stopped visiting Lady Beatrice, given away her beloved spaniel, visited the haunted crypt at night with an unknown man, and allegedly may have killed his sister to protect his financial position.

In the morning Holmes discovered that we had come without our

Sir Robert owns Shoscombe Old Place and is financially dependent on Lady Beatrice while heavily indebted. After Holmes discovers Lady Beatrice's body, he admits arranging for Mr. Norlett to personate her and hiding her body in the chapel crypt until the Derby was decided.
