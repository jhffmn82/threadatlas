Cepheus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Cepheus, son of Aleus, gives Caphyæ its name and allows inhabitants fleeing Athens to settle there after their supplication.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Cepheus is the son of Aleus and is said to have given Caphyæ its name. The inhabitants of Caphyæ reportedly supplicated him to let them settle there after they fled from Athens.
