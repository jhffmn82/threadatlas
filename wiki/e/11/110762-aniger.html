Aniger

Also called: river Aniger

Abstract written by a model

Aniger is a river rising in the Arcadian mountain Lapithus and flowing to the sea near Samicum. Its wind-blocked, foul-smelling waters were fishless before receiving Acidas and yielded inedible fish afterward. Traditions explained the smell through washed wounds, poison, or purifications; nearby, leprosy sufferers prayed, sacrificed, and bathed at its Nymphs’ cave for healing.

CHAPTER V.

The Aniger flowed into the sea near Samicum, and its flow was often blocked by sand driven inland by violent winds. It rose in the Arcadian mountain Lapithus and had an unpleasant smell; before receiving the Acidas it contained no fish, and afterward its fish were inedible. Traditions attributed its smell to wounds washed in it by Chiron or Pylenor, to Hydra poison, or to the purifications of the daughters of Prœtus. A cave of the Nymphs of the Aniger stood nearby, where sufferers from leprosy prayed, sacrificed, and bathed in the river in the hope of being cured.
