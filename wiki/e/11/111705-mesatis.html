Mesatis in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Mesatis, a city between Anthea and Aroe, was linked to Dionysus’ rearing and danger from the Titans; Patreus barred Achæan settlement, and its inhabitants were later scattered after Patræ’s reverses.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII.

Mesatis was built between Anthea and Aroe. The people of Patræ said that Dionysus was reared there and was endangered by a plot of the Titans, and that its name had a corresponding explanation. Patreus later forbade the Achæans to settle there, and its inhabitants were among those scattered after Patræ suffered reverses.
