Abstract written by a model

Piecer is the Prime Piecer, Minister of Patch, and chief adviser to the Queen of Patch, a Quiltie and one of the Quilty statesmen. He carries beeswax, the royal dust-pan, and a rag-bag, and travels with Chief Scrapper under the Royal Spool of Succession after the Queen goes to pieces. Though he repeatedly wants to turn back, he enters the Emerald City, helps kidnap the Patchwork Girl, and proclaims her Queen Scraps. He then enforces her royal duties, even proposing the Scissor Bird cut off her head if she refuses to clean.

Piecer is in one work on this site, The Gnome King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The Prime Piecer rushes to the Queen after hearing that she has gone to pieces, then helps prepare for the search for a new ruler. He carries the Royal dust-pan and a large rag-bag, follows the Royal Spool of Succession with Chief Scrapper, and repeatedly urges that they return when the journey becomes difficult.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

Piecer follows Scrapper into the Emerald City and is delighted by the Patchwork Girl. He helps kidnap Scraps, carries the rag bag, and announces her as Queen Scraps of Patch before handing her into the royal rolling chair.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Piecer reads Scraps the long list of the Queen’s duties, including preparing breakfast, cleaning, sorting patches, feeding the Scissor Bird, and scolding the Patch-workers. He brings in the Scissor Bird and suggests that it cut off Scraps’s head if she refuses to clean the castle.
