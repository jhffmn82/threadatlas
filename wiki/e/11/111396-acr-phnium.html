Acræphnium in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Acræphnium, originally a city in the district of Thebes, stands on Mount Ptoum, has a notable temple and statue of Dionysus, and received Theban refugees after Thebes’ destruction.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Acræphnium was originally said to have been a city in the district of Thebes. It is situated on Mount Ptoum and contains a temple and statue of Dionysus that are described as worth seeing.
