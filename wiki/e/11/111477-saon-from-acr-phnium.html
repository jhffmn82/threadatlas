Abstract written by a model

Saon, oldest of the Bœotian messengers, follows bees underground to discover Trophonius’s oracle at Lebadea, then learns its ritual and routine from Trophonius.

CHAPTER XL.

Saon, the oldest of the Bœotian messengers, followed a swarm of bees and thereby discovered the oracle of Trophonius at Lebadea. Trophonius instructed him in the oracle’s ritual and routine.
