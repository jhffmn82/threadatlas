Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XL.

Saon, the oldest of the Bœotian messengers, followed a swarm of bees and thereby discovered the oracle of Trophonius at Lebadea. Trophonius instructed him in the oracle’s ritual and routine.
