temple of Æsculapius at Titane

place · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: a temple of Æsculapius at Titane

Abstract written by a model

At Titane, the temple of Æsculapius, built by Alexanor, son of Machaon and grandson of Æsculapius, stood in a precinct with old cypresses and mostly suppliant inhabitants. Its gables, porch, and interior contained figures of gods, heroes, and Æsculapius; partially concealed statues of Æsculapius and Hygiea, Alexanor, Euamerion, and Coronis also stood there. Sacred dragons lived within the precinct.

CHAPTER XI.

The temple was built by Alexanor, son of Machaon and grandson of Æsculapius, and its precinct contained a few inhabitants, mostly suppliants of the god, along with old cypress trees. It held partially concealed statues of Æsculapius and Hygiea, statues of Alexanor and Euamerion, a wooden statue of Coronis, and other wooden and stone figures; sacred dragons also lived within the precinct.
