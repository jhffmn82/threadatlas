1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXII.

The river Stymphelus flows from the spring, soaks into the ground, and reemerges in Argolis under the name Erasinus. A tree once blocked the place where it went underground, turning the plain into a marsh for 400 stades until a fleeing deer and a pursuing hunter entered the swamp and, according to the story, opened a way for the water.
