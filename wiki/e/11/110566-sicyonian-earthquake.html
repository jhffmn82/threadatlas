destroys Sicyon (city) [leaving it almost bare of men and destroying works of art] An earthquake struck Sicyon when the city's fortunes were already poor. It made the city almost bare of inhabitants and destroyed many works of art, while also damaging cities in Caria and Lycia and especially Rhodes.
