CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

The band begins a lively march that makes the population of Tune Town dance and carry out daily activities to music. When Peter speaks against the law, its members rush at the travelers with their instruments, but they flee after the travelers produce a discordant “Ah.”
