THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

Edith Presbury is Professor Presbury's only daughter and is engaged to Trevor Bennett. She reports that her father has seemed like an empty outward shell and says she saw his face pressed against the window of her second-floor room during the night.

Monday morning found us on our way to the famous University town--an

Miss Presbury is regarded by Mr. Bennett as being in danger from her father. Holmes advises that she prolong her visit away from home until the danger has passed.
