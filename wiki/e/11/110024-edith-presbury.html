Abstract written by a model

Edith Presbury, Professor Presbury’s only daughter and devoted to him, is engaged to Trevor Bennett. During a prolonged visit away from home, she reports her father seems an empty shell and saw him at her second-floor window. Bennett fears she is in danger; Holmes advises remaining away until it passes.
