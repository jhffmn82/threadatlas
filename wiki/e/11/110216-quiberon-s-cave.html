Abstract written by a model

Quiberon’s cave is a great cave between jeweled cliffs, with sapphire formations, jeweled rocks, a stream, and a waterfall leading to further, incompletely known caves. Akbad brings Trot there to serve Quiberon and says he carried a mortal maiden there; searchers find neither her nor Quiberon. Trot and companions later escape by an explosion and reach the mainland.

Quiberon's cave is in one work on this site, The Giant Horse of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 5

The travelers are brought to the mouth of a great cave between jeweled cliffs, where Akbad leaves Trot to serve Quiberon. The cave contains sapphire formations, jeweled rocks, a stream, and a waterfall leading into another cave.

CHAPTER 6

Akbad claims that he carried a mortal maiden to the cave of Quiberon. Cheeriobed and his Guards search the cave but find neither the maiden nor Quiberon.

CHAPTER 14
