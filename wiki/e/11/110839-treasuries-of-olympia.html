CHAPTER XVII.

The treasuries of Olympia stand on a tufa-stone base north of the temple of Hera, with Mount Cronos behind them. The unit describes treasuries belonging to the Sicyonians, Carthaginians, Epidamnus, Sybaris, Cyrene, Metapontum, Megara, and Gela, together with their dedications and inscriptions.
