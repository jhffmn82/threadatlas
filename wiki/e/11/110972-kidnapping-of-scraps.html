CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

The kidnapping of Scraps occurs when Scrapper and Piecer seize her in the palace garden, cover her with the rag bag, and carry her toward the gates. The foot-path enables them to escape the Emerald City and transport her to Patch.
