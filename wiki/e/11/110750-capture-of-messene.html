CHAPTER XXIX.

The attempted capture of Messene occurred when Demetrius, son of Philip, led Macedonian troops into the town before dawn. The inhabitants first mistook them for Lacedæmonians, then attacked them after recognizing their Macedonian arms and language; the Macedonians were defeated and many died among the rocks of Ithome.
