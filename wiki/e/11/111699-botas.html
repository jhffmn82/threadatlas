Abstract written by a model

Œbotas, son of Œnias and a native of Dyme, won the foot race at the 6th Olympiad, first among the Achæans. Denied reward, he cursed them against Olympic victories. After consultation and honors, Sostratus won; thereafter Achæans sacrificed to Œbotas and crowned his statue when victorious.

CHAPTER XVII.

Œbotas, the son of Œnias and a native of Dyme, won the foot race at the 6th Olympiad but received no special reward from the Achæans. He cursed the Achæans so that none would thereafter win at Olympia, but after they consulted Delphi and honored him, Sostratus of Pellene won the boys’ race; Achæans continued to sacrifice to Œbotas before competing and crown his statue when victorious.
