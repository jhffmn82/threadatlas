Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

In the battle, the Mantineans held the right wing, the other Arcadians the left, and Aratus with the Sicyonians and Achæans the centre. Aratus deliberately fell back in a crescent formation, allowing the Arcadians to attack the Lacedæmonians’ flanks; the Lacedæmonians were surrounded, lost most of their men, and Agis fell.
