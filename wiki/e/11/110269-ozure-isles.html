Ozure Isles in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928 · the work’s page · read it where Ozure Isles first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Ozure Isles are a kingdom ruled by King Cheeriobed, with Sapphire City as their radiant capital. They include Philador’s father’s kingdom and are home to the Queen, Prince Philador, and the Ozure Islanders. Their blue waters, Orizon, jeweled caverns, sea horses, and past mainland riders are notable. The kingdom faces almost certain destruction when Quiberon demands a mortal maiden and threatens to destroy it. Cheeriobed refuses to harm a mortal, while Akbad seeks a solution and Philador travels to save his mother and his father’s kingdom. Mombi had abducted the Queen, attacked the sea horses, imprisoned Orpah, and earlier carried Orin away; Orin later returned after recovering her identity. As destruction nears, Cheeriobed and his advisors await Ozma. The royal family eventually returns from the Emerald City to Sapphire City, where Cheeriobed makes Trot a Princess of the Ozure Isles.

the Ozure Isles in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928 · the work’s page · read it where the Ozure Isles first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Ozure Isles are five jeweled islands in the center of the Lost Lake of Orizon, west of the mainland. Their beaches are strewn with precious stones, and they contain the Kingdom of the Ozure Isles, its capital, the Sapphire City of Cheeriobed, and the royal castle. Ruled by King Cheeriobed and home to Prince Philador, the islands are inhabited by one thousand and seven Ozurians. Quiberon threatens to destroy the kingdom, attacks its royal castle, and controls the surrounding waters so that the islanders are virtually imprisoned. Philador secretly escapes with the Mo-gull, while Akbad travels toward the Emerald City using golden wings. Akbad later brings Trot to the isles, saying she has been chosen to save them; Cheeriobed expects Ozma’s help. After Tattypoo disappears, Philador worries over their rescue. The group ultimately travels to the isles to rescue the royal household and confront the mystery of the missing Queen Orin.

The Ozure Isles are five jeweled islands in the center of the Lost Lake of Orizon. They are inhabited by one thousand and seven Ozurians and include the Sapphire City of Cheeriobed; Quiberon's attacks and control of the surrounding waters have left the islanders virtually imprisoned.

The Ozure Isles are threatened with destruction by Quiberon. Philador secretly escapes from them by flying with the Mo-gull, while Akbad departs toward the Emerald City of Oz using the golden wings.

CHAPTER 4

The Ozure Isles are the place Akbad commands his magic wings to carry him and Trot.

CHAPTER 5

The Ozure Isles are reached by Akbad after the flight from the Emerald City. Akbad says that Trot has been chosen to save them.

CHAPTER 6

The Ozure Isles are said by Akbad to be threatened by Quiberon. Cheeriobed expects Ozma to come and save the islands.

CHAPTER 8

Philador is worried about saving the Ozure Isles after learning that Tattypoo has disappeared.

CHAPTER 12

The Ozure Isles lie west of the mainland across Lake Orizon. Trot wants to return to the Sapphire City, and Orpah plans to tell Cheeriobed that they have escaped.

CHAPTER 17

The Ozure Isles are Prince Philador's homeland, where Quiberon attacks the royal castle and where the group travels to rescue the royal household. The islands are also connected to the mystery of the missing Queen Orin.
