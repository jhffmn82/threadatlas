Abstract written by a model

Caprus of Elis, son of Pythagoras, became the first recorded athlete to win Olympia’s wrestling and pancratium prizes on one day, defeating Pæanius and Clitomachus; two statues honored him there.

CHAPTER XIII.

Caprus of Elis won wrestling and pancratium prizes on the same day, the first recorded athlete to do so. He defeated Clitomachus in the pancratium and Pæanius in wrestling, and two statues of him stood at Olympia.
