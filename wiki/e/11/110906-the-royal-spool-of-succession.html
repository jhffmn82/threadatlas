Abstract written by a model

The royal Spool of Succession is a golden spool that grows on the central tree in Patch and magically unwinds to identify the next ruler. After the former queen goes to pieces, Chief Scrapper cuts it free and commands it to unwind. It rolls beyond Patch, guides Scrapper and Piecer through difficult terrain and the Emerald City, and reaches the palace garden. There it stops at Scraps’s feet, choosing her as ruler of Patch.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The Royal Spool of Succession grows as one golden spool on the central tree in Patch and is meant to lead to the next ruler. After Chief Scrapper cuts it free and commands it to unwind, it rolls far beyond Patch, leads the ministers through difficult terrain, enters the Emerald City, and continues ahead of them.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

The Spool of Succession leads Scrapper and Piecer through the Emerald City to the palace garden, where it stops at Scraps's feet. It helps them locate the Patchwork Girl they believe should rule Patch.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

The royal Spool of Succession chose Scraps to rule Patch. Scrapper says that the golden spool led him and Piecer to the Emerald City in search of a new queen after the former queen went to pieces.
