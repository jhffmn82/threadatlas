Helisson

Abstract written by a model

Helisson is a river named after Helisson. Rising in a village of the same name, it flows through Dipæa, Lycæatæ, and Megalopolis, dividing Megalopolis in two, receives water from Mount Scolitas, and falls into the Alpheus about 30 stades away.

Helisson is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXX.

Helisson rises in a village of the same name, flows through Dipæa, Lycæatæ, and Megalopolis, and falls into the Alpheus about 30 stades from Megalopolis. It divides Megalopolis into two parts, and water from a spring on Mount Scolitas flows into it.
