CHAPTER V.

Scillus was a town of Triphylia that allied itself with Pisa in the war against Elis, after which the people of Elis dispossessed its inhabitants. The Lacedæmonians later took Scillus from Elis and gave it to Xenophon. The district had wild animals suitable for hunting and was crossed by the river Selinus.
