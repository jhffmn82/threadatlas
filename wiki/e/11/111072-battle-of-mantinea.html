battle of Mantinea

Also called: battle, battle against Epaminondas and the Thebans

Abstract written by a model

The battle of Mantinea was fought between the Achæans and Lacedæmonians. Lacedæmonian light troops defeated Achæan light troops, but Philopœmen’s infantry square routed the Lacedæmonian hoplites and killed Machanidas. Other accounts commemorate bravery against Epaminondas and the Thebans.

CHAPTER IX.

A painting in Mantinea imitates the painting at Ceramicus of the action of the Athenians at Mantinea. The Mantineans also refer to the battle against Epaminondas and the Thebans, in which they say Gryllus the son of Xenophon, Cephisodorus of Marathon, and Podares were the leading examples of bravery.

CHAPTER L.

At the battle of Mantinea, the light-armed Lacedæmonians defeated the light-armed Achæan troops. Philopœmen formed his infantry into a square, routed the Lacedæmonian hoplites, and slew Machanidas as he returned from the pursuit.
