------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

“ASPIRATION” compares the violet, lily, rose, and other flowers with a soul that longs for beauty and fulfillment. The poem presents flowers as transforming heavenly light, fragrance, and aspiring thought into earthly beauty, and ends by anticipating the fulfillment of infinite desire in holiness and beauty.
