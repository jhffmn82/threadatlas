ASPIRATION in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

“ASPIRATION” is a poem expressing Angeline Hall through a speaker’s infinite desire for beauty and holiness, comparing flowers’ transformation of heavenly light and fragrance into earthly beauty with a soul longing for fulfillment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

“ASPIRATION” compares the violet, lily, rose, and other flowers with a soul that longs for beauty and fulfillment. The poem presents flowers as transforming heavenly light, fragrance, and aspiring thought into earthly beauty, and ends by anticipating the fulfillment of infinite desire in holiness and beauty.
