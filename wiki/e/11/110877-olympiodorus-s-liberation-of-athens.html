CHAPTER XXV.

Olympiodorus led old men and young men against the Macedonians and defeated them in battle. He captured the Museum, thereby freeing Athens from the Macedonians, and later recovered the Piraeus and Munychia and defeated Macedonian forces at Eleusis.
