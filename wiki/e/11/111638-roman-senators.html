Roman Senators in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Ten Roman Senators, sent after Perseus and the Macedonians were defeated, settled Macedonian affairs; one, influenced by Callicrates, urged accusations against alleged supporters and sent them to Rome.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Ten Roman Senators were sent to settle affairs in Macedonia after Perseus and the Macedonians were defeated. One Senator, influenced by Callicrates, urged the Achæans to condemn alleged supporters of Perseus and then sent those accused to Rome.
