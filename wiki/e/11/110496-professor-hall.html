Abstract written by a model

Professor Hall is an astronomer, husband to Angeline Hall, and father living with her and their sons in the Gay Street home, where he played whist and taught the boys cards. In 1877 he searched for a satellite of Mars with the Clark refractor. Angeline’s encouragement, lunches, and eager attention to nightly results sustained the work. The account closes by portraying him as Science’s groom: like Orion hunting stars, he found the moons of Mars and gave them to the mother.

Professor Hall is in one work on this site, An Astronomer's Wife. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

Professor Hall shared the Gay Street home with Angeline Hall and their sons. He played whist with the boys and taught them to play cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XV.

Professor Hall pursued astronomical research and searched for a satellite of Mars with a large Clark refractor in 1877. He credited Angeline Hall’s encouragement with helping him continue the search, and she supported his work by sending him nourishing lunches to the observatory and awaiting the nightly results with enthusiasm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XVI.

The unit presents the astronomer’s wife through Angeline Hall’s personal letters rather than through a formal biography. She appears as a wife, mother, correspondent, household manager, traveler, reader, and aspiring writer who engages with scientific and ethical ideas.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ EPILOGUE.

The mother's husband is portrayed as Science's groom. Like Orion hunting down the stars, he found and gave the mother the moons of Mars.
