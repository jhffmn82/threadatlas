Phormis’s votive offerings

Abstract written by a model

Phormis’s votive offerings at Olympia consisted of two horses, two charioteers, and a fighting image; though Lycortas dedicated the image, Greeks counted it among Phormis’s offerings.

CHAPTER XXVI.

They stood among the votive offerings at Olympia and included two horses, two charioteers, and an image of Phormis fighting one, two, and even three enemies. The Greeks called Lycortas’s fighting image part of Phormis’s offerings, although its inscription identified Lycortas as the dedicator.
