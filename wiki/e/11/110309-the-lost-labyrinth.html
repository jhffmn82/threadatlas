CHAPTER 10

The Lost Labyrinth lies at the southern end of the Gnome King's dominions and contains winding, slippery, moss-covered corridors beneath glittering green stone ceilings. Betsy and her companions become lost there until Guph opens a door in a rock wall and leads them out.
