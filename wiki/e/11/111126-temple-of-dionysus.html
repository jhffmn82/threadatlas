temple of Dionysus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A temple for Dionysus, associated with Cynætha and Tanagra, was struck by lightning two generations before the narrator, leaving vestiges. It contains statues of Dionysus and Triton; at a winter festival a bull is sacrificed there. It is called Cynætha’s most memorable feature.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The temple of Dionysus is the most memorable feature of Cynætha. During the god's winter festival, a bull is brought there and offered in sacrifice.

CHAPTER XX.

The temple of Dionysus at Tanagra contains a statue of Dionysus by Calamis in Parian stone. It also contains a statue of Triton that is described as even more wonderful.
