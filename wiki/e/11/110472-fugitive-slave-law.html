------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VII.

Angeline Stickney condemned the fugitive slave law as detestable, abominable, infamous, tyrannical, and unjust. She objected to the provision paying a commissioner ten dollars for deciding in favor of slavery but only five dollars for deciding in favor of freedom, and contrasted the law with Deuteronomy 23:15–16.
