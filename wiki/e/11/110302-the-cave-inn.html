Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

Cave Inn is a door in the rocky cavern, marked with instructions to knock three times. After the Hungry Tiger knocks with his head, the door opens, the cave floor tilts, and the travelers fall inside.
