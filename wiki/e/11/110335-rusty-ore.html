Abstract written by a model

Rusty Ore is a man and ornamental iron worker who owns an ironworking shop and bellows. He finds Princess Ozma and the collapsed Atmos Fere, carries them to his shop, feeds Ozma, restores Atmos with bellows and a patch, makes him iron boots, and warns Ozma about the Gnome King's dominions and the Deadly Desert.

Rusty Ore is in one work on this site, The Hungry Tiger of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 14

Rusty Ore, an ornamental iron worker, finds Princess Ozma and the collapsed Atmos Fere in a field. He offers to use a bellows to restore Atmos and carries him and Ozma to his shop.

CHAPTER 15

Rusty gives Ozma food, makes Atmos a pair of iron boots, inflates and patches him, and helps him prepare to travel. He warns Ozma that his shop is near the Gnome King's dominions and that the Deadly Desert lies ahead.
