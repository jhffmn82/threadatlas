Bœotians

group · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: The Bœotians, the Bœotians

Abstract written by a model

The Bœotians, originally in Thessaly and called Æolians, say their name came from Bœotus. Living about Mount Helicon, they have an approach to Phocis at Panopeus, traditions about prophecy at Holy Well (Hysiæ), Idæan Hercules, and Cerberus at Mount Laphystium. They meet at the temple of Itonian Athene; after drought, they consult Delphi and are sent to Trophonius at Lebadea.

Bœotians is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

The Bœotians hold their general meeting at the temple of Itonian Athene. They also say that Hercules came up from the lower world with Cerberus at Mount Laphystium.

CHAPTER XL.

After two years without rain, the Bœotians sent messengers from every city to consult the oracle at Delphi. They were directed to go to Trophonius at Lebadea to obtain a cure for the drought.
