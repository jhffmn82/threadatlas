plain Argum in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Argum is an unfruitful plain under Nestane: mountain rain makes it swampy, but its water vanishes into a ground cavity and reappears at Dine; a nearby mountain holds ruins of Philip’s camp and Nestane.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The plain Argum is called unfruitful because rain from the mountains makes it swampy, though the water disappears into a cavity in the ground and reappears at Dine. A mountain beside it contains ruins of Philip’s camp and of the village Nestane.
