Dædala in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Dædala was a festival commemorating the reconciliation of Hera and Zeus, named from dædala, an old term for statues; it unfolded as a smaller Platæan celebration and a great festival for all Bœotia.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Dædala was the festival commemorating the reconciliation of Hera and Zeus. Its name was connected with the old use of dædala for statues, and it included a smaller Platæan celebration as well as a great festival for all Bœotia.
