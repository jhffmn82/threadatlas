Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

Arion was a swift, dark-maned horse associated with Poseidon and Demeter, although Antimachus said that he was born from the earth near the grove of Oncean Apollo. Hercules reportedly rode Arion against the people of Elis after obtaining the horse from Oncus, and later gave him to Adrastus, who was said to have been the third to break him in.
