Also called: Thelpusian Arion, foal Arion, godlike Arion, horse, swift courser of Adrastus

Abstract written by a model

Arion is a swift, dark-maned foal of disputed divine or earthly origin, associated with Poseidon and Demeter or born from earth near Oncean Apollo’s grove. Hercules obtained and rode him against Elis, gave him to Adrastus, and Adrastus, his third breaker, drove him.

Arion is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXV.

Arion was a swift, dark-maned horse associated with Poseidon and Demeter, although Antimachus said that he was born from the earth near the grove of Oncean Apollo. Hercules reportedly rode Arion against the people of Elis after obtaining the horse from Oncus, and later gave him to Adrastus, who was said to have been the third to break him in.
