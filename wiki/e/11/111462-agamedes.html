Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVII.

Agamedes was the son of Erginus and brother of Trophonius. He became a skilled builder, helped construct temples and palaces, and was trapped while robbing Hyrieus's treasury; Trophonius cut off his head.
