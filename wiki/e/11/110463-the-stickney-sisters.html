the Stickney sisters in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

The Stickney sisters pursue college education on very limited means, living together on one dollar a week for board and lodging, sometimes preparing food, and practicing economical self-provisioning; Ruth later tries to support Angeline when she cannot remain in college.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VI.

The Stickney sisters lived very economically while attending college, spending only one dollar a week together for board and lodging and sometimes preparing their own food. They sought education despite limited money, and Ruth Stickney tried to support Angeline Stickney after she could no longer remain in college.
