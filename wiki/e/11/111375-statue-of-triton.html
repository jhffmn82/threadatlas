Abstract written by a model

The statue of Triton in the temple of Dionysus is especially wonderful, even more wonderful than Dionysus’s statue; it lacks a head, said to have been struck off by a man of Tanagra with an axe.

CHAPTER XX.

The statue of Triton at the temple of Dionysus is considered especially wonderful. It has no head because, according to one legend, a man of Tanagra struck Triton's head off with an axe.
