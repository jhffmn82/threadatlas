Cave City in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Cave City is a dim, damp underground cavern beyond a narrowing sapphire passage, entered by paying three rocks and making its door open with laughter. Its shadow inhabitants imprison Orpah and take the travelers to King Silly the Second. Orpah escapes it, and the travelers are carried out after an explosion in Quiberon’s cave.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

Cave City lies beyond a narrowing sapphire passage and requires three rocks for admittance. The door opens when Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow rock with laughter, and an old mer-man admits them.

CHAPTER 11

Cave City is a dim, damp underground place with corridors, a muddy stream, and sapphire rocks. Its shadow inhabitants imprison Orpah and bring the travelers to King Silly the Second.

CHAPTER 12

Cave City is the place from which Orpah escaped. The Scarecrow and the others leave it after the explosion carries them out of Quiberon's cave.
