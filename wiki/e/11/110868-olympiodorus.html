Olympiodorus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Olympiodorus is an Athenian general celebrated for restoring Athenian hopes. Elected amid remembered ancestral fame, he defeated Macedonians, captured the Museum, recovered Piraeus and Munychia, won at Eleusis, gained Aetolian aid against Cassander, and helped Phocians at Elatea. Athens, Eleusis, and the Phocians honored him with memorials.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

Olympiodorus was honored for reviving Athenian hopes after repeated failures and was elected general when the Athenians remembered the fame of their ancestors. He defeated the Macedonians, captured the Museum, recovered the Piraeus and Munychia, and defeated Macedonian forces at Eleusis; he also secured Aetolian help against Cassander and aided the Phocians at Elatea. Athens honored him in the Acropolis and Prytaneum, Eleusis displayed a painting of him, and the Phocians erected a bronze statue to him at Delphi.
