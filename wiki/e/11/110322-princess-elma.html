Princess Elma

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Elma, Giantess, girl, great girl, little Elma, little girl, that awful girl, that dreadful girl, the Giantess, the Princess, the little Giant Princess, the little girl Giant

Abstract written by a model

Princess Elma, the Giant King and Queen’s child, is presented as the smallest little girl Giant and owns a nursery and toys. Wearing a big wig to appear gigantic, she overtakes fleeing travelers and captures the Hungry Tiger, whom she adores as a kitten. She searches for him, feeds and dresses him, carries him in a doll coach, and tucks him into a doll bed. When she removes her wig, she shrinks to ordinary size, revealing she is not truly a Giant; Reddy takes the wig while she sleeps.

Princess Elma is in one work on this site, The Hungry Tiger of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

The little girl Giant gains on the fleeing travelers despite the Hungry Tiger's speed. She calls the Hungry Tiger a darling little kitten before he is seized and carried away.

CHAPTER 16

Princess Elma is the Giant King's daughter and dotes on the Hungry Tiger as her kitten. She searches for him, feeds him cream, dresses him in doll clothes, takes him away in a doll coach, and later returns carrying him, a plate of chicken, and a doll bed.

CHAPTER 17

Princess Elma treats the Hungry Tiger as a kitten and tucks him into a doll bed. When she removes her big wig, she shrinks to ordinary size, revealing that she is not truly a Giant. Reddy takes the wig while she sleeps, leaving her without it.
