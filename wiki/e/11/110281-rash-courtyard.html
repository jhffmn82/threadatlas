CHAPTER 6

The Rash courtyard holds the Hungry Tiger, Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and prisoners thrown over its wall by the guards. It contains a water tub, an overturned vegetable cart, and a pink paving stone concealing the entrance to the underground cavern.
