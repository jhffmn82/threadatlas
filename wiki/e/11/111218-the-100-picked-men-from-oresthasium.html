Abstract written by a model

The 100 picked men from Oresthasium volunteered for the expedition to Phigalia, fought bravely to the death, fulfilled the oracle, and drove out the Lacedæmonian garrison.

CHAPTER XXXIX.

The 100 picked men from Oresthasium joined the expedition to Phigalia and engaged the Lacedæmonian garrison. They all died fighting bravely, fulfilled the oracle, and drove out the Spartans.
