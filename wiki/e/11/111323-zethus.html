Zethus

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Ζήθου, Ζῆθος
  • In The Library, Zethus is exposed and raised by a neatherd, tends cattle, reunites with Antiope, kills Lycus and Dirce, expels Laius, fortifies and succeeds to Thebes with Amphion, and marries Thebe.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Zethus is Amphion's brother, is buried with Amphion in a low mound of earth, and has his sepulchre involved in a ritual intended to improve the fertility of land.

Zethus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Zethus, son of Antiope and Zeus, is exposed and raised by a neatherd, tends cattle, reunites with Antiope, kills Lycus and Dirce, expels Laius, fortifies and succeeds to Thebes with Amphion, and marries Thebe.
