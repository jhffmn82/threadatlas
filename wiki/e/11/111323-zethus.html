Zethus

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Ζήθου, Ζῆθος

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Zethus is exposed and raised by a neatherd, tends cattle, reunites with Antiope, kills Lycus and Dirce, expels Laius, fortifies and succeeds to Thebes with Amphion, and marries Thebe. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Zethus is Amphion's brother, is buried with Amphion in a low mound of earth, and has his sepulchre involved in a ritual intended to improve the fertility of land.
