CHAPTER XIX.

Comætho was a very beautiful maiden and the priestess of Artemis Triclaria. She loved Melanippus, and they consummated their love in the temple of Artemis and made it their bridal-chamber; the resulting divine anger led to famine, sickness, and human sacrifice.
