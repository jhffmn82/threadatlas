An Astronomer's Wife

Moses Stickney was a Revolutionary soldier and the paternal grandfather of Angeline Stickney. He lived in Jaffrey beside Mount Monadnock, was known as a crack shot, reportedly carried his musket at Bunker Hill, and yoked his oxen on the day he turned one hundred.
