Abstract written by a model

Moses Stickney was a Revolutionary soldier, crack shot, husband of Mary Hastings, father of Mary Stickney, and paternal grandfather of Angeline Hall. Living in Jaffrey beside Mount Monadnock, he reportedly carried his musket at Bunker Hill and yoked oxen on his hundredth birthday.
