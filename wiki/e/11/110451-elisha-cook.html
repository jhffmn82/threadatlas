An Astronomer's Wife

Elisha Cook left Ellington, Connecticut, with his wife Huldah and their children in January 1806 to seek a home in the wilderness beyond New York’s Dutch settlements. He settled in the Black River Country, where his daughter Electa later married Theophilus Stickney.

Elisha Cook was Angeline’s grandfather and a kindly protector of the Stickney household, living half a mile up the road. He died in the summer of 1841.
