Phlius in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Phlius, chief town of Phliasia near Titane and Sicyon, was taken by Rhegnidas after the Heraclidæ returned; its citadel, sanctuaries, monuments, and public buildings preserve its traditions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Phlius is at most 40 stades from Titane, with a straight road from Sicyon. Its traditions, sanctuaries, monuments, and public buildings include the citadel, the temple and grove of Hebe, precincts of Demeter, temples of Æsculapius, Dionysus, Apollo, and Isis, and the house associated with Amphiaraus.
