Oropians

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Oropians appealed, the Oropians

Abstract written by a model

The Oropians appealed to the Roman Senate after Athenians plundered Oropus. Though they accepted an arrangement with an Athenian garrison and hostages, an outrage led them to demand both hostages’ return and the garrison’s removal. They promised Menalcidas ten talents for Achaean assistance, then paid despite receiving none.

Oropians is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XI.

The Oropians appealed to the Roman Senate after the Athenians plundered Oropus. They agreed to an arrangement involving an Athenian garrison and hostages, but later demanded the return of the hostages and removal of the garrison after an outrage. They promised ten talents to Menalcidas to obtain Achaean assistance.
