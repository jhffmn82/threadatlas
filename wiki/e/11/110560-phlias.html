Phlias in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Phlias, son of Dionysus and Aræthyrea, is said to give his name to the land, sail in Argo, marry Chthonophyle, and have Andromedas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Phlias is said to have given his name to the land and to have been the son of Dionysus and Aræthyrea. He was regarded as one of those who sailed in the Argo, and he was married to Chthonophyle, by whom he had Andromedas.
