the Gay Street house in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

The Gay Street house began as a two-story brick house with white stucco, a shingled roof, porch, and separate parlor, sitting room, dining room, and kitchen. Its garden held trees, shrubs, walks, and flower beds. In 1876 it became a three-story house with a flat tin roof, bay windows, longer porch, furnace heat, kitchen range, and coal fires; its rooms held distinctive furnishings and artwork.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

The Gay Street house began as a two-story brick house with white stucco, a shingled roof, a porch, and separate parlor, sitting room, dining room, and kitchen. In 1876 it was enlarged to three stories with a flat tin roof, three bay windows, a longer porch, furnace heat, a kitchen range, and open coal fires in the dining and sitting rooms.
