Lady Beatrice Falder in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Lady Beatrice Falder is Sir Robert Norberton’s widowed sister, an invalid with weak heart trouble and long-standing dropsy. Through her late husband’s estate she holds a life interest in Shoscombe Old Place, making her the source of Sir Robert’s financial security. Devoted to him despite his difficult conduct, she lives with maid Carrie Evans, keeps prized Shoscombe spaniels, and withdraws to her room, drinking heavily and seeming brooding after a supposed quarrel. She dies, reportedly of dropsy, and her body is concealed while another person publicly impersonates her.

Lady Beatrice Falder is Sir Robert's widowed sister and the owner of Shoscombe Old Place under a life interest. She has heart trouble and dropsy, has withdrawn from the horses, remains in her room, drinks heavily, and is described as brooding and sulky after the supposed quarrel with Sir Robert.
