CHAPTER XXX.

Libethra was a city on Olympus where, according to an account heard at Larissa, Orpheus’s tomb was located nearby. Its people received an oracle that the city would be destroyed when the Sun saw Orpheus’s bones, and Libethra was later destroyed after his urn was broken and his remains were exposed.
