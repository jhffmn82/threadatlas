Gerenia in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Gerenia, formerly called Enope by Homer and Chœrian dell in the author's time, is a Messenian city whose people join the Eleutherolacones' council. Traditions place Nestor's upbringing or flight there after Hercules took Pylos; it holds Machaon's tomb, temple, and bronze statue.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Gerenia is the city formerly called Enope by Homer and is inhabited by Messenians who join the council of the Eleutherolacones. Some said Nestor was brought up there, while others said he fled there after Hercules took Pylos; Gerenia contains the tomb and temple of Machaon.
