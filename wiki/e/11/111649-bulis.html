Bulis

Also called: the town of Bulis

Abstract written by a model

Bulis is a town bordering Phocis, founded by Bulon and colonized from ancient Doris. On high ground, it lay 80 stades from Thisbe and about seven by land from its port. Its people were linked to Philomelus and the Phocians’ impiety; many caught shellfish for purple dye. It had temples, a god, and Saunion well.

Bulis is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXVII.

Bulis is a town bordering Phocis, founded by Bulon and colonized from the towns of ancient Doris. Its inhabitants were said to have shared in the impiety of Philomelus and the Phocians, and more than half lived by catching shellfish for purple dye. Bulis had temples of Artemis and Dionysus, worshipped a god called Supreme, and possessed a well called Saunion.
