CHAPTER 4

The winding road suddenly tears loose and rushes along, carrying Betsy and Carter through twisting turns past trees, lakes, and forests. It then disappears, leaving them in a sandy wilderness; Carter explains that such roads come and go in Oz and can carry travelers to unknown places.
