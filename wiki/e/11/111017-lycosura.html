Lycosura in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Lycosura is a town on Mount Lycæus, founded by Lycaon. Though it has walls and few inhabitants, it is described as earth’s oldest town, first seen by the sun, and a model for mankind’s town building.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Lycosura was founded by Lycaon on Mount Lycæus.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

Lycosura has a circuit of walls and a few inhabitants. It is described as the oldest of the towns on earth, either on the mainland or on islands, and as the first town seen by the sun and the model for all mankind's town building.
