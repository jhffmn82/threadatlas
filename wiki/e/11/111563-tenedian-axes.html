CHAPTER XIV.

The Tenedian axes are connected with the legend of Tennes, who cut the ropes of his father Cycnus’s ship with an axe when Cycnus came seeking his forgiveness. This act gave rise to a proverb for someone who obstinately refuses discussion and cuts a matter short.
