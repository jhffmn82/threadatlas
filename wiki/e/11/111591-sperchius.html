Sperchius is a river crossed by the Galatians. Greeks destroy its bridges and guard it to resist them, but Galatian troops cross elsewhere, forcing the Greek detachment back.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XX.

The Greeks send about 1,000 picked light-armed soldiers and a troop of horse to the Sperchius to prevent the Galatians from crossing without a struggle. They destroy its bridges and camp nearby, but Galatian troops cross elsewhere, causing the Greek detachment to return to the main army.
