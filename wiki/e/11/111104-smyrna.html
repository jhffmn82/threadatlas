Smyrna

Abstract written by a model

Smyrna is a city, first one of the twelve Æolian cities at the old city. Taken by Ionians from Colophon, it was admitted to the Pan-Ionic league. After an oracle at Claros approved, its inhabitants moved to the modern city on Mount Pagus. It especially worships two Nemeses, has a temple of Æsculapius, and practises divination by omens.

Smyrna is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER V.

Smyrna was one of the twelve cities of the Æolians on the site called the old city, but it was taken by Ionians from Colophon and later admitted to the Pan-Ionic league. Its inhabitants were relocated to the modern city after an oracle at Claros approved the move, and the people of Smyrna worship two Nemeses. In Pausanias's time, Smyrna also had a temple of Æsculapius between Mount Coryphe and the sea.
