Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Horse races begin with staggered rope releases from the starting-place so horses reach the beak together; charioteers and horses display skill and speed while facing Taraxippus-caused fear at the course outlet.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The horse races began by releasing the ropes for the horses positioned along the starting-place in sequence until all could start together at the beak. Charioteers and horses then demonstrated their skill and speed, but they were endangered by the fear caused by Taraxippus.
