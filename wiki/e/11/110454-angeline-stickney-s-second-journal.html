Abstract written by a model

Angeline Stickney’s second and last journal is a handmade stitched booklet with thin brown wrapping-paper covers. It follows her travel between towns, efforts to organize painting classes, expenses, teaching and discouragements, and progress through junior and senior years at New York Central College.

Angeline Stickney's second journal is a handmade booklet of writing paper stitched together with brown wrapping-paper covers. It records her travel between towns, attempts to form painting classes, boarding and travel expenses, teaching experiences, discouragements, and her progress into the junior and senior years at New York Central College.
