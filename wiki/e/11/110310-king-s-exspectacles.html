Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

The exspectacles are pink spectacles made by the gnome wizard that allow their wearer to see events before they occur. Kaliko uses them to watch the travelers in the Lost Labyrinth, and the Hungry Tiger uses them to see that lunch is ready.
