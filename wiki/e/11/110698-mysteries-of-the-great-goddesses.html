BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

The mysteries of the Great Goddesses were brought from Eleusis to Messenia by Caucon and later reformed or taught by Lycus and Methapus. They were associated with Andania, where they continued to be celebrated, and Sybotas required annual rites before them.
