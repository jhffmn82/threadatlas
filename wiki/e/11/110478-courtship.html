------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER IX.

The courtship of Angeline Stickney and Asaph Hall began through his course in geometry taught by her, after which admiration displaced his attempt to challenge her with difficult questions. Their relationship continued through long separations, affectionate letters, health concerns, and discussions of religion, work, and marriage.
