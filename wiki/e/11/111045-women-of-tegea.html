Abstract written by a model

The women of Tegea armed themselves, ambushed under Phylactris during Charillus’s first invasion, fought the Lacedæmonians under Marpessa the Widow, helped rout them, and privately sacrificed to Ares without sharing the victim’s flesh with men.

CHAPTER XLVIII.

The women of Tegea took up arms and lay in ambush under the hill called Phylactris during the first invasion of Charillus. They appeared during the battle, helped cause the rout of the Lacedæmonians, and sacrificed privately to Ares without sharing the victim’s flesh with the men.
