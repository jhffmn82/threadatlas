Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVIII.

The women of Tegea took up arms and lay in ambush under the hill called Phylactris during the first invasion of Charillus. They appeared during the battle, helped cause the rout of the Lacedæmonians, and sacrificed privately to Ares without sharing the victim’s flesh with the men.
