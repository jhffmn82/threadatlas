CHAPTER XVII.

The Sardinians offered a brazen statue of Sardus, from whom the island was said to have taken its name. They called the Corsi by the name of their homeland and continued to honour Iolaus. Their island was eventually conquered by the Carthaginians except for the Ilians and Corsi.
