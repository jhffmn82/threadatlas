Sardinians in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Sardinians, inhabitants of Sardinia, offered a brazen statue of Sardus and continued honouring Iolaus; their island was eventually conquered by the Carthaginians, except for the Ilians and Corsi.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The Sardinians offered a brazen statue of Sardus, from whom the island was said to have taken its name. They called the Corsi by the name of their homeland and continued to honour Iolaus. Their island was eventually conquered by the Carthaginians except for the Ilians and Corsi.
