Abstract written by a model

Maud Bellamy, Tom Bellamy’s beautiful, strong-minded daughter and William’s sister, was secretly engaged to Fitzroy McPherson before his death; afterward she helps Holmes and suggests multiple attackers.

Maud Bellamy is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE LION'S MANE

Maud Bellamy is the daughter of Tom Bellamy and is described as exceptionally beautiful and strong-minded. She confirms that she and McPherson were engaged, offers Holmes her help, and says that more than one person may have been needed to inflict his injuries.
