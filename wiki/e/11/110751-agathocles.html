Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Agathocles, the son of Lysimachus, was captured by the Getæ during a campaign against Dromichetes. He later had children by Lysandra but was killed after Arsinoe conspired against him, causing Lysandra and others to flee to Seleucus.
