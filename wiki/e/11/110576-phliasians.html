Phliasians in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Phliasians are a people who say Aras first inhabited their land and founded Arantia. Originally Argives, they became Dorians after the Heraclidæ returned, preserve local traditions and Celeæ mysteries, honor Hebe, and are not connected with Arcadians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

The Phliasians say that Aras was the first person to live in their land and that he founded the city formerly called Arantia. They preserve traditions about Aras, Aoris, Aræthyrea, Phlias, Dysaules, and the mysteries at Celeæ, and they honor Hebe by granting immunity to slaves who seek refuge in her precinct.
