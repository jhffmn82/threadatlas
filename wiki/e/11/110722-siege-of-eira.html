CHAPTER XVI.

The siege of Eira lasts eleven years after the Messenian defeat at the Great Trench. During it, the Messenians conduct raids, while the Lacedæmonians attempt to take the stronghold and receive assistance from Corinthian troops and Cretan bowmen. The siege approaches its conclusion when a deserter informs Emperamus how the abandoned guard posts can be exploited.
