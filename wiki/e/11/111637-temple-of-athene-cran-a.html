1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

The temple of Athene Cranæa stands on the mostly precipitous crest of a hill reached by an easy uphill road from Elatea. It has porticoes and chambers for those who minister to the goddess, especially the priest, who is chosen from the youths, serves for five continuous years, resides with the goddess, and bathes in tubs according to the ancient manner.
