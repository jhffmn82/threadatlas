Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXI.

The temple of Proserpine stands to the right of the temple of the Great Goddesses and contains a stone statue of Proserpine about 8 feet high, with fillets throughout the base. Women may enter it at all times, but men may enter only once a year.
