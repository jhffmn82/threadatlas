Abstract written by a model

begins_with Vesta [in the order of Elis sacrifices] The altar ritual at Olympia includes sacrifices in a prescribed sequence, beginning with Vesta and continuing with Olympian Zeus, Hermes, Artemis, Athena, Alpheus, and other deities. At the great altar, victims are sacrificed at the lower level and their thigh bones are burned at the top; seers annually renew the altar with debris mixed with water from the Alpheus.
