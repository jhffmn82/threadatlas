In the morning Mr. Bennett slipped round to bring us the latest report.

The treatment given to Professor Presbury involves serum administered by hypodermic syringe according to directions regulating a nine-day cycle. It produces increased energy and vitality but also causes Presbury to display crawling, climbing, and other animal-like behavior, and Lowenstein warns that it is dangerous.
