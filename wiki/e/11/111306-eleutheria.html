Also called: the festival called Eleutheria

Abstract written by a model

Eleutheria is a festival at Platæa celebrated every fifth year, culminating in competitors running in heavy armor before the altar of Zeus Eleutherius for chief prizes.

Eleutheria is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER II.

Eleutheria is a festival celebrated at Platæa every fifth year. Its chief prizes are for running, with competitors running in heavy armor before the altar of Zeus Eleutherius.
