Wumbo in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Wumbo is a lovable, loyal citizen of Oz and an accomplished wizard, also called Wonder Worker. Retired and secretive, he lives alone by preference in his crystal cavern with magic furniture, studying harmless magic through the Book of Chants and Enchantments. Father of Kuma Party, he cannot master long-distance magic, and his spells work only when present. He exposes Ruggedo and tries to imprison him, but sleeps and lets him escape; afterward, he warns Ozma through Kuma Party’s hand and resumes magical comforts.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15 Wumbo, Wonder Worker, At Home

Wumbo lives comfortably in a crystal cavern and secretly studies sorcery. He uses chants to speak with his furniture, makes Ruggedo visible, and tries to keep him prisoner until he can warn Ozma, but he falls asleep and Ruggedo escapes.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

Wumbo awakens near noon and realizes that Ruggedo has escaped. He sends Kuma Party's left hand a message ordering an arm to go to the Emerald City, warn Ozma, and hold Ruggedo until Ozma can overpower him. He then uses magic to summon breakfast and turn pens and pencils into a five-piece orchestra.
