BOOK X.--PHOCIS. / CHAPTER I.

The oracle said that a mortal and an immortal would be put together in combat, with victory given to both but the greater victory to the mortal. It became clear to the Greeks after the Phocians won, because the Thessalian word was Itonian Athene and the Phocian word was Phocus.
