Abstract written by a model

Temple of Cretan Dionysus at Argos, dedicated after Dionysus was honoured by the Argives, was named for Ariadne’s burial; restoration reportedly revealed her ashes in an earthenware cinerary urn.

CHAPTER XXIII.

The temple of Cretan Dionysus at Argos was said to have received its name because Ariadne was buried there. Lyceas reported that an earthenware cinerary urn containing Ariadne’s ashes was found when the temple was restored.
