CHAPTER XXIII.

Cythera lies opposite Bœæ, and its promontory of Platanistus is about four stades from the mainland promontory called Ass’ jaw-bone. It has a harbour called Scandea, about ten stades from the town, and contains the temple of Celestial Aphrodite.
