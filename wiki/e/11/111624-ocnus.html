Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIX.

Ocnus is represented seated and making rope while a she-ass eats the rope as quickly as he makes it. He is said to have been industrious but married to an extravagant wife who quickly spent whatever he gathered, and his name is used for a person laboring hard to no profit.
