Electa Cook, daughter of Elisha Cook and Huldah Pratt, married Theophilus Stickney in 1816 and became mother of Angeline, Chloe, and other Stickney girls. After Theophilus’s death, she married Milton Woodward and brought her unmarried daughters into his household.

An Astronomer's Wife

Electa Cook was the daughter of Elisha Cook and Huldah Pratt. She married Theophilus Stickney on Washington’s birthday in 1816 and became the mother of Chloe Angeline Stickney.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Electa Cook was the mother of Angeline and her sisters. After Theophilus Stickney’s death, she married Milton Woodward and brought her unmarried daughters into his household, where Angeline became her chief assistant.
