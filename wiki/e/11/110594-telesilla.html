Telesilla in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Telesilla is a poetess honored by the Argives for poetic gifts and defending Argos. After Cleomenes and the Lacedæmonians devastated Argos, she armed unfit slaves and women, led resistance, and compelled their withdrawal.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

When Cleomenes and the Lacedæmonians had devastated Argos, Telesilla armed the slaves considered unfit for military service and the women in the prime of life. She led them in resisting the Lacedæmonians, who withdrew rather than kill the women or risk being defeated by them.
