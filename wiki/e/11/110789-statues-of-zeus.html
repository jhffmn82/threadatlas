CHAPTER XXII.

The statues of Zeus at Olympia vary in size, appearance, attributes, and dedication. They include offerings by the people of Cynætha, Cleolas of Phlius, Apollonia, Metapontum, Phlius, Leontini, the Greeks who fought at Platæa, the Megarians, Hybla, Cleitor, Corinth, Thessaly, Psophidius, the Lacedæmonians, Mummius, Elis, the Elaitæ, and the Chersonese in Cnidus.
