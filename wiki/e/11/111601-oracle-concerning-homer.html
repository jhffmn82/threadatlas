Abstract written by a model

The oracle concerning Homer foretells mixed fortune, identifies Ios as his motherland and receiver of his remains, and warns against young boys’ riddle; it ends with Homer’s grief and death after failing to solve fisher boys’ riddle.

CHAPTER XXIV.

The oracle concerning Homer says that he was born to both fortunate and unfortunate destinies, had no fatherland but a motherland, and that Ios would receive his remains. It warns him against the riddle of young boys, which is explained as a tradition that Homer died of grief after failing to solve a riddle posed by fisher boys.
