Sellasia

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
  • In Description of Greece, Sellasia was the site of a battle where Antigonus and the Achæans defeated Cleomenes, after which it was enslaved and Lacedæmon was captured.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Sellasia was the site of a battle in which Philopœmen fought against Cleomenes and the Lacedæmonians, opposed by the Achæans, Arcadians from all the cities, and Antigonus with an army from Macedonia.

Sellasia in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Sellasia, site of battle, saw Antigonus and the Achæans defeat Cleomenes, after which it was enslaved and Lacedæmon captured.

Sellasia was the place where Antigonus and the Achæans defeated Cleomenes. After the victory, Sellasia was enslaved and Lacedæmon was captured.

Sellasia in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Sellasia was the site of a battle in which Philopœmen fought against Cleomenes and the Lacedæmonians. The forces opposing Cleomenes included the Achæans, Arcadians from all the cities, and Antigonus with an army from Macedonia.
