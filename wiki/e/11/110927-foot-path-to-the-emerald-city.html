Abstract written by a model

The foot-path to the Emerald City is a magical, mischievous, mobile piece of property owned by the Wizard of Oz, with a hundred broad feet. Normally tied up, it rises and rushes through the forest, carries two ministers to the city gates, and coils beneath a lime drop tree. Left untied, it runs wild, carries Scrapper, Piecer, and Scraps toward Patch, then its forty-ninth foot kicks Piecer before it departs.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The foot-path to the Emerald City has a hundred broad feet and suddenly rises and rushes through the forest. A notice says that it runs straight to the Emerald City, belongs to the Wizard of Oz, and forbids stamping or kicking; it carries the two ministers to the city gates in less than a minute before coiling up beneath a lime drop tree.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

The foot-path is a mischievous piece of property that has been left untied and is running wild. It carries Scrapper and Piecer out of the Emerald City with Scraps in the rag bag, follows Scrapper's written direction to Patch, then kicks Piecer and departs.
