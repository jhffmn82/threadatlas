yearly tax for the murder of Clymenus

object · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a yearly tax

Abstract written by a model

The yearly tax was imposed on the Thebans after Erginus and his brothers defeated Thebes in retaliation for Clymenus's murder. Hercules later had the tax remitted.
