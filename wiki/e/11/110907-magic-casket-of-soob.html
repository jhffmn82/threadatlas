The magic casket of Soob was taken by Polacky, the Plunderer, from the Island of Ashangabad and hidden beneath a raised plank in the treasure room. It contains the Flying Cloak of Invisibility, a small uncut emerald, and an ivory box containing two onion-like objects with instructions to plant them in extreme danger.
