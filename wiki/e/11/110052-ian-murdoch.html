Ian Murdoch

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Ian Murdoch is a tall, dark, thin, taciturn mathematical coach and teacher at The Gables, subordinate to Stackhurst. Though aloof and thought to have outlandish blood, he is McPherson’s friend and supports McPherson’s relationship with Miss Bellamy despite loving her himself, acting as their go-between. After McPherson collapses, Murdoch denies being with him on the beach and is dismissed after an angry exchange with Stackhurst. Suspected because of his temper, quarrel, and love for Miss Bellamy, he is later attacked by Cyanea capillata and survives, clearing him.

Ian Murdoch is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE LION'S MANE

Ian Murdoch, the mathematical coach at The Gables, arrives after McPherson collapses and denies being with him on the beach. He had held the students back with an algebra demonstration, had visited The Haven, and is dismissed by Stackhurst after an angry exchange.

A week passed. The inquest had thrown no light upon the matter and had

Ian Murdoch is suspected of killing Fitzroy McPherson because of his temper, his past quarrel with McPherson, and his feelings for Miss Bellamy. He is attacked by Cyanea capillata at the same beach and survives after suffering severe pain, thereby clearing himself of suspicion.
