Nileus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Nileus, eldest son of Codrus, refused Medon’s rule, led Ionians to Asia Minor after an oracle favored Medon, conquered Miletus, and was buried near Didymi.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Nileus refused to submit to Medon because Medon was lame in one foot. After the oracle assigned the kingdom to Medon, Nileus led a contingent to Asia Minor and later conquered Miletus.
