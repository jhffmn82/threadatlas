Abstract written by a model

Zarax, about one hundred stades from Epidaurus Limera, has a convenient harbour. Cleonymus especially ravaged it among Eleutherolacones towns; its chief remaining sight is a harbour-end temple of Apollo with a lyre-holding statue.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Zarax lies about one hundred stades from Epidaurus Limera and has a convenient harbour. It was especially ravaged among the towns of the Eleutherolacones by Cleonymus, and its chief remaining sight is a temple of Apollo with a statue of the god holding a lyre.
