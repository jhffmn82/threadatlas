Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Zarax lies about one hundred stades from Epidaurus Limera and has a convenient harbour. It was especially ravaged among the towns of the Eleutherolacones by Cleonymus, and its chief remaining sight is a temple of Apollo with a statue of the god holding a lyre.
