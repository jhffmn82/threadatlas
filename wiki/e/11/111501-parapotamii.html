Parapotamii in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Parapotamii was an ancient town in Phocis, renowned in ancient times. Xerxes burned and destroyed it; it was not restored by the Athenians and Boeotians. Its impoverished inhabitants were distributed among other towns, and by the author's day no ruins remained and its site was unknown.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Parapotamii was one of the Phocian towns burned by Xerxes. It was not restored by the Athenians and Bœotians, and its inhabitants were distributed among other towns because of its poverty and lack of money; no ruins remained and its exact site was unknown.
