1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

The sacred precincts of Apollo are very large, lie in the upper part of Delphi, and have several entrances. They contain numerous votive offerings, including statues dedicated by the Corcyræans, Tegeans, and Lacedæmonians.
