Patreus

Also called: his own name, the son of Preugenes

Abstract written by a model

Patreus, son of Preugenes and grandson of Agenor, forbids Achæan settlement in Anthea and Mesatis; his tomb and golden effigy stand near the market-place, where annual rites honor him with Preugenes.

Patreus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XX.

The tomb of Patreus stands in front of a statue of Athene in the market-place. A gate near the market-place bears a golden effigy of Patreus, and he receives annual funereal rites along with Preugenes at the festival of Artemis Limnatis.
