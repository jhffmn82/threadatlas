Abstract written by a model

The statue of Laphrian Artemis, made of ivory and gold by Menæchmus and Soidas, depicts a huntress and was brought from Calydon to Patræ after Augustus depopulated Calydon.

CHAPTER XVIII.

The statue of Laphrian Artemis was brought from Calydon to Patræ after Augustus depopulated Calydon. It depicts the goddess as a huntress, is made of ivory and gold, and was made by Menæchmus and Soidas.
