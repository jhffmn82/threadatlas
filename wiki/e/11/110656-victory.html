Abstract written by a model

Victory is a monument on a pillar at Olympia, designed by Mendæan Pæonius and apparently made from enemy spoils; the Messenians said it commemorated their share with the Athenians at Sphacteria.

Victory is in one work on this site, Description of Greece. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXVI.

It was a Victory erected on a pillar at Olympia, designed by the Mendæan Pæonius and made from spoils taken from an enemy, according to the narrator’s impression. The Messenians said it commemorated their share with the Athenians in the action at Sphacteria and did not name the enemy.
