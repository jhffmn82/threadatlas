Tattypoo's disappearance leaves Philador distressed and uncertain how to save the Kingdom. The magic slate provides his next course of action by directing him to the Emerald City.

The disappearance of the good witch alarms and distresses Ozma and her advisers. They plan to use the magic picture to search for Tattypoo, whom they believe may be the missing good witch.
