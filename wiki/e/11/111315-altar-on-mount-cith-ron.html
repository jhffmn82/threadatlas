Abstract written by a model

An altar on the top of Mount Cithæron, built of square wooden pieces and firewood, receives sacrifices to Hera and Zeus; smaller private offerings, images, and the altar itself are consumed in a very large fire.

CHAPTER III.

The altar was made from square wooden pieces piled up like a stone building and raised with firewood. Chief magistrates sacrificed a cow to Hera and a bull to Zeus, while private people offered smaller animals; the sacrifices, wooden images, and altar were all consumed by a very large fire.
