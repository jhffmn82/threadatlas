Abstract written by a model

A hurry-cane is a hair-raising mode of long-distance travel and a chief product of Rash. It enables instant journeys when directions are placed inside: Ippty uses one to leave a castle for the Emerald City, then takes the Hungry Tiger away in fire and smoke. Its triply guaranteed patent action protects travellers crossing the Deadly Desert, and five hurry-canes return Ozma, Reddy, and others to the Emerald City in five furious flashes.

hurry-cane in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where hurry-cane first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

A hurry-cane is a product of Rash used for long journeys. Ippty places a note reading "Carry me to the Emerald City" inside one, and it carries him out of the castle toward Oz.

CHAPTER 2

The hurry-cane brings Ippty directly to the royal gardens of the Emerald City of Oz and can transport travelers rapidly. Ippty explains its mechanism to the Hungry Tiger, places return directions inside it, and uses it to take them away in a flash of fire and smoke.

CHAPTER 20

The Rash hurry-canes are described as having a patent action that is triply guaranteed to protect travellers while crossing the Deadly Desert. Five of them carry Ozma, Reddy, and the others from Rash to the Emerald City in five furious flashes.
