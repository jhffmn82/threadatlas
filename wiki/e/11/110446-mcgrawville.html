McGrawville in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

McGrawville is a college location offering education through an advertised manual-labor system. It enabled students to live cheaply while studying: Angeline Stickney began her college course there, arrived with Ruth in April 1852 so they could attend together, and later returned for her junior year. Asaph Hall 3rd came in 1854 to earn a living while studying and quickly solved a problem that had baffled students and teachers.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER IV.

McGrawville is mentioned as the place where Angeline Stickney began her college course. In a later letter, she compared the hill near her school with the hills of McGrawville.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Illustration: AN OLD DAGUERREOTYPE] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER V.

McGrawville is chosen as the place where Angeline Stickney and Ruth can attend college together while living very cheaply. Angeline and Ruth arrive there in April 1852, and Angeline later returns to enter the junior year at New York Central College.

opening

McGrawville was the place where Asaph Hall 3rd began serious study. He had hardly begun his studies there when he solved a problem that had previously baffled students and teachers. In 1854 he traveled there because its advertised manual-labor system seemed to offer a way to earn his living while studying.
