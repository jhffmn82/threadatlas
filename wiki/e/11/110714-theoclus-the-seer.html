Theoclus the seer

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Theoclus, the seer, the seer Theoclus

Abstract written by a model

Theoclus is a Messenian seer descended from Eumantis and father of Manticlus. After consulting Delphi with Aristomenes, he interprets the Pythian oracle and signs in nature as foretelling Messene's end, warns Aristomenes, and nevertheless helps defend Eira. He encourages the Messenians, fights the Lacedæmonians, kills several, and dies of wounds during Eira's capture.

CHAPTER XVI.

Theoclus serves as the Messenian seer and warns Aristomenes that Castor and Pollux are seated in the wild pear tree. After observing a fig tree touching the Neda, he interprets the oracle about a he-goat drinking from the river as a sign that Messene's safety has ended. He and Aristomenes had previously consulted Delphi after the disaster at the Great Trench.

CHAPTER XXI.

Theoclus helped defend Eira and encouraged the Messenians by recalling the people of Smyrna’s resistance to Gyges and the Lydians. He told Aristomenes that Messene was destined to perish, then attacked the Lacedæmonians, killed several of them, and died from his wounds.
