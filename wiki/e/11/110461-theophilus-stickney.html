Abstract written by a model

Theophilus Stickney, Moses Stickney’s son, was a skilled carpenter and Electa Cook’s husband. After unpaid building work cost him his home and farm, he moved his family, returned to the Black River Country, became a drunkard, and left before Chloe Angeline was three. Absent from his daughters’ lives, he died in Rochester hospital on February 14, 1842, leaving them doubly orphaned.

An Astronomer's Wife

Theophilus Stickney was Moses Stickney’s son, a skilled carpenter, and the husband of Electa Cook. After losing his home and farm when payment for a building contract failed to arrive, he moved with his family, later returned to the Black River Country, became a drunkard, left home before Chloe Angeline was three, and died in a Rochester hospital in 1842.
