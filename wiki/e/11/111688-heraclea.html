Heraclea in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Heraclea is a city compelled into the Ætolian League, protected by the Ætolians after the Galati ravaged its country without taking it, then blockaded by Critolaus and the Achæans for refusing the Achæan League.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Heraclea was blockaded by Critolaus and the Achæans because it would not join the Achæan League.

CHAPTER XXI.

Heraclea was protected by the Ætolians after its people had been compelled to join the Ætolian League the previous year. The Galati ravaged its surrounding country but did not capture the city.
