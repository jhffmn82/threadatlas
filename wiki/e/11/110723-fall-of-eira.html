CHAPTER XVI.

The fall of Eira is foretold by the oracle concerning the he-goat drinking from the Neda and is brought nearer when a herdsman learns that the Messenian guards have left their posts during heavy rain. The herdsman deserts to the Lacedæmonians and tells Emperamus how the stronghold can be captured.
