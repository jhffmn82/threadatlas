temple to Arean Athene

Also called: a temple to Arean Athene, the temple

Abstract written by a model

The temple to Arean Athene at Platæa, built from spoil given by the Athenians after the Battle of Marathon, contains the goddess's statue. Its vestibule holds paintings by Polygnotus and Onatas, while the statue's base bears an effigy of Arimnestus.

The temple to Arean Athene at Platæa was built from spoil given to the Platæans by the Athenians after the Battle of Marathon. Its vestibule contains paintings by Polygnotus and Onatas, and the base of the goddess's statue has an effigy of Arimnestus.
