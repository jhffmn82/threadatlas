Abstract written by a model

Cardamyle is a city Homer named among Agamemnon’s promised gifts; detached from Messenia by Augustus, it became subject to Sparta and has a Nereus-daughters grove and temples of Athene and Carnean Apollo.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Cardamyle is the city Homer called Cardamyle in Agamemnon’s promises of gifts. It is subject to Sparta after Emperor Augustus detached it from Messenia, lies eight stades from the sea, and contains a grove sacred to the daughters of Nereus and temples of Athene and Carnean Apollo.
