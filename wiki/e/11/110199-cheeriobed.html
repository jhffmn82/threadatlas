Abstract written by a model

Cheeriobed is the kind, gay, soft-hearted ruler of the Ozure Isles, living in a castle and father of Philador. He is associated with Queen Orin, Orpah, the Sapphire City, the Munchkins, and the East, and is described as king of the Ozure Isles, Sapphire City, and the East. When Quiberon’s monster traps his people and destroys their sea horses, Cheeriobed stays calm despite distress. He refuses Quiberon’s demand for a mortal maiden, searches unsuccessfully for another solution, and worries about Philador, Ozma, and the kingdom’s safety. He leads Guards to Quiberon’s cave, prepares to receive Ozma and Philador, and calls a conference when Quiberon threatens destruction. He anxiously welcomes the return of Orin and Philador, initially shuts the castle against danger, then admits the rescuers after Quiberon is petrified. His past includes rejecting Mombi’s marriage proposal, marrying Orin, and becoming King of the Ozure Isles. He first seeks punishment for Akbad but pardons him. He rejoices when the sea horses are restored, travels to the Emerald City, returns to Oz, and appoints Trot a Princess of the Ozure Isles after Ozma declares Cheeriobed Queen of the East.

Cheeriobed is in one work on this site, The Giant Horse of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Cheeriobed is the kind and cheerful King of the Ozure Isles and the father of Philador. Although Quiberon's monster has trapped his people and destroyed their sea horses, Cheeriobed remains calm and often manages to forget his troubles. He is distressed when Quiberon demands a mortal maiden and worries that no one can cross the lake to seek help.

CHAPTER 2

Cheeriobed searches with his councillors for a way to appease Quiberon but finds no plan. He refuses to sacrifice a mortal maiden to save himself or the Kingdom, though he later considers commanding Philador to pick the golden pear if no other solution appears.

CHAPTER 6

King Cheeriobed anxiously searches for Philador and then leads Guards to rescue the mortal maiden from Quiberon's cave. Finding neither Quiberon nor the maiden, he accepts Akbad's story and prepares a grand reception for Ozma and Philador.

CHAPTER 16

Cheeriobed anxiously awaits Ozma and Philador, then rejoices when Orpah tells him that Quiberon is trapped and Trot has escaped. He calls a conference to discuss defending the kingdom from Quiberon's threatened destruction. When Queen Orin returns, he rushes to the shore to greet her but is held back from diving into the lake and later fears that Quiberon will destroy the castle.

CHAPTER 17

King Cheeriobed initially refuses to open the Castle of Cheeriobed because he expects Quiberon outside. After High Boy announces the visitors, Cheeriobed admits the rescuers, is relieved to see Quiberon petrified, and welcomes Philador and Queen Orin.

CHAPTER 18

Cheeriobed refused Mombi's marriage proposal when she appeared as a young maiden, after which she threatened him and his family. He married Orin, became King of the Ozure Isles, welcomed her return, and initially wanted Akbad punished before finally pardoning him.

CHAPTER 19

Cheeriobed conducts the royal party through Sapphire City and wishes that the sea horses would return. He is delighted when the whole herd is restored, and he later travels with the group to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 20

Cheeriobed returns to Oz and is welcomed at the banquet. Ozma declares Cheeriobed Queen of the East, and Cheeriobed makes Trot a Princess of the Ozure Isles.
