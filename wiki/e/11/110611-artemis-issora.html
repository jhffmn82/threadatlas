Abstract written by a model

Britomartis, daughter of Zeus and Carme, loved racing and hunting and befriended Artemis. Fleeing Minos, she fell into nets; Artemis made her a goddess, worshipped as Aphæa in Ægina and Dictynna in Crete.

CHAPTER XXIX.

Britomartis was said to be the daughter of Zeus and Carme, and she enjoyed racing and hunting and was a close friend of Artemis. Fleeing from Minos, she fell into fishing nets, after which Artemis made her a goddess; she was worshipped as Aphæa in Ægina and Dictynna in Crete.
