CHAPTER XXXIX.

Before descending, the consulter purifies himself, avoids warm baths, bathes in the River Hercyna, and receives animal food from various victims. Sacrifices are made to Trophonius and his sons, Apollo, Cronos, Zeus the King, Hera the Chariot-driver, and Demeter called Europa, with a seer inspecting the entrails; on the night of descent, a ram is sacrificed in a ditch while Agamedes is invoked, and its entrails must be favorable.
