Mycalessus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Mycalessus, a depopulated town near Harma and the sea, was named for the cow that led Cadmus and his army to Thebes and lowed there; it has a temple of Mycalessian Demeter.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

Mycalessus was said to have received its name because the cow leading Cadmus and his army to Thebes lowed there. It was previously described as having been depopulated, and near the sea it has a temple of Mycalessian Demeter.
