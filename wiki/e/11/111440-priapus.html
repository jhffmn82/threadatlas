Priapus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Priapus is represented by a statue at Mount Helicon, considered well worth seeing. He is especially honoured where sheep, goats, or bees abound, and the people of Lampsacus honour him more than all gods, saying Dionysus and Aphrodite are his parents.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXI.

Priapus has a statue at Helicon that is considered well worth seeing. He is especially honoured in places with flocks of sheep or goats or swarms of bees, and the people of Lampsacus honour him more than all the gods, saying that he is the son of Dionysus and Aphrodite.
