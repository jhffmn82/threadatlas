Dorieus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Dorieus, son of Anaxandrides and half-brother of Cleomenes, Leonidas, and Cleombrotus, was judged superior in counsel and war but denied kingship; refusing subjection to Cleomenes, he led a colonizing expedition to Sicily.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III.--LACONIA.

Dorieus was the son of Anaxandrides and the half-brother of Cleomenes, Leonidas, and Cleombrotus. Although regarded as superior to Cleomenes in council and war, he was passed over for the kingship and was sent to found a colony because he would not remain subject to Cleomenes.
