the Gold King's wife’s jealousy in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The Gold King's wife’s jealousy is bitter, soul-jealous, and possibly frantic; it arose because Miss Dunbar influenced the Gold King's mind and actions, and may have led to threats.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

To my surprise the Gold King took the reproof with equanimity.

The Gold King says that his wife was bitterly jealous because Miss Dunbar influenced his mind and actions in a way that she never had. He believes her jealousy may have driven her to threaten Miss Dunbar.
