King Dad in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

King Dad, kind and care-worn ruler of Down Town, depends on Fi Nance for decisions. After the group is arrested for robbing a restaurant, he receives them on a bank roof, hears the charge and food bill, directs them to earn payment, assigns positions to Betsy Bobbin and Prince Evered of Rash, and sends the group to the public square.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Dad is the King of Down Town. After the Down Officers arrest the group for robbing the restaurant, they take the prisoners to the roof of a bank, where Dad sits in a swivel chair reading a paper.

CHAPTER 9

King Dad hears the charge against the Hungry Tiger and reads the food bill aloud. He suggests that the travelers make money to pay it, assigns Betsy Bobbin and Prince Evered of Rash positions, and orders the officers to take the group to the public square.
