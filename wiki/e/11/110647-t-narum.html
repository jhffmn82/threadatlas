Tænarum in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tænarum is a promontory with the harbours Achilleus and Psamathus, a cave-like temple of Poseidon, and a bronze Arion riding a dolphin. Its fountain once revealed harbours and ships, but lost this power after dirty linen was washed in it; Cænepolis was formerly called Tænarum.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Tænarum is a promontory with the harbours Achilleus and Psamathus, a cave-like temple of Poseidon, and works of art including a statue of Arion riding a dolphin. It also had a fountain once believed to reveal harbours and ships, though its power ended after a woman washed dirty linen in it; Cænepolis was formerly called Tænarum.
