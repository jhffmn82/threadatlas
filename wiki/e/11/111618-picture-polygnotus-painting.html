picture (Polygnotus’ painting)

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The other part of the painting, that on the left, painting, picture, the left part of the painting

Abstract written by a model

The picture represents Odysseus descending to Hades to consult Tiresias about returning home. Its left part contains Acheron, with reeds, ghostlike fishes, Charon, and his boat, alongside Tellis, Cleobœa, and figures suffering or honored. It then shows Ocnus, Ariadne and Phædra, Odysseus performing a rite, Theseus and Pirithous, and numerous other figures.

picture (Polygnotus’ painting) is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

The left part of the painting represents Odysseus descending to Hades to consult Tiresias about his return home. It contains Acheron, Charon and his boat, Tellis, Cleobœa, and various figures suffering or receiving honor in Hades.

CHAPTER XXIX.

The picture shows Ocnus making rope, Ariadne watching Phædra, Odysseus performing a rite beside a ditch, and Theseus and Pirithous seated on an enchanted rock. It also includes numerous other mythological figures, including Perimedes, Eurylochus, Tityus, Chloris, Thyia, Procris, Clymene, Megara, Eriphyle, Elpenor, Tiresias, and Anticlea.
