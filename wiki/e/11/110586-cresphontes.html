Cresphontes

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Cresphontes is married to Merope, uses a clod of earth instead of a stone to receive Messene in the lots, and is murdered with two of his sons after a short reign. In Description of Greece, Cresphontes gains and rules Messenia after a disputed lot, establishes Stenyclerus, and is killed by wealthy opponents along with all but one of his sons.

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Cresphontes obtained Messenia through a rigged-looking lot when the clay lot of the sons of Aristodemus dissolved in water. He made Stenyclerus his royal residence, but wealthy opponents killed him and all his sons except Æpytus.
