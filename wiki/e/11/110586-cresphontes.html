Cresphontes in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Son of Aristomachus, Cresphontes gains and rules Messenia after a disputed lot, establishes Stenyclerus, then wealthy opponents kill him and all but one son.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Cresphontes obtained Messenia through a rigged-looking lot when the clay lot of the sons of Aristodemus dissolved in water. He made Stenyclerus his royal residence, but wealthy opponents killed him and all his sons except Æpytus.
