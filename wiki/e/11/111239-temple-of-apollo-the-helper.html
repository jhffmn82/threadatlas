The temple of Apollo the Helper stands at Bassae on Cotilius, with a stone roof. Noted for its beautiful stone and neat structure, it ranks among the finest Peloponnesian temples after Tegea and was built by Ictinus.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLI.

The temple of Apollo the Helper stands at Bassae on Cotilius and has a stone roof. It is described as one of the finest temples in the Peloponnese, except for the temple at Tegea, and was built by Ictinus, who was also the architect of the Parthenon at Athens.
