marriage of Angeline Stickney and Asaph Hall in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

The marriage of Angeline Stickney and Asaph Hall began on a Monday morning in early April 1856, after Hall visited her family and joined her in Wisconsin. They traveled to Ann Arbor and settled there while Hall studied astronomy and French. Though they faced poverty, Angeline’s frail health, illness, and Hall’s severe fever, the marriage was fortunate, marked by happiness, mutual care, encouragement, and support.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER IX.

Angeline Stickney and Asaph Hall were married on a Monday morning in early April 1856 after Hall visited her family and later joined her in Wisconsin. They went to Ann Arbor, where they settled while Hall studied astronomy under Brünnow and French under Fasquelle; despite poverty and Angeline’s frail health, their marriage was described as fortunate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER X.

Asaph and Angeline supported and cared for each other during illness and financial hardship. Angeline wrote that they were very happy and that Asaph took the best care of her possible, while she cared for him during a severe fever and encouraged his religious convictions.
