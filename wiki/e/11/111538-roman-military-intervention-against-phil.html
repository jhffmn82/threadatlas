Roman military intervention against Philip

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: sent an army, sent some soldiers

Abstract written by a model

The Romans first sent soldiers to assist the Ætolians nominally while investigating Macedonian intentions, then sent an army under Otilius to protect the Athenians and Ætolians against Philip. Otilius's destruction of Hestiæa and Anticyra displeased the Roman Senate, which replaced him with Flaminius.
