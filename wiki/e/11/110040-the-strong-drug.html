the strong drug in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The strong drug causes Professor Presbury's outbursts, temporarily poisoning him and intensifying his violent nature. He learned it in Prague and receives it in London through a Bohemian intermediary.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Monday morning found us on our way to the famous University town--an

Holmes provisionally identifies the strong drug as a substance that produces a temporary but highly poisonous effect and intensifies Presbury's naturally violent nature. He believes Presbury learned to use it in Prague and receives it through a Bohemian intermediary in London.
