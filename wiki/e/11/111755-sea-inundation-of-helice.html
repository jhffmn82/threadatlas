sea inundation of Helice

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The sea encroached over much of the district, the inundation

Abstract written by a model

In winter, the sea encroached over much of the district and, together with an earthquake, completely flooded and swept away Helice and its population. It submerged Poseidon’s grove, leaving only tree tops visible.

CHAPTER XXIV.

The sea encroached over much of the district in winter and completely flooded Helice. It submerged the grove of Poseidon so that only the tops of the trees remained visible and helped sweep away the city and its population.
