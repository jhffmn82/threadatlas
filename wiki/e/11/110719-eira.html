Eira in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Eira is a mountain stronghold and refuge of the Messenians after defeat at the Great Trench. Besieged by the Lacedæmonians for eleven years, it is defended despite improvised fortifications and raids. During a violent rainstorm, abandoned guards and a herdsman's disclosure enable Emperamus and the Spartan force to scale its citadel. Its capture ends the second war; inhabitants become Helots.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVI.

Eira becomes the refuge of the Messenians after the defeat at the Great Trench and is besieged by the Lacedæmonians for eleven years. Its inhabitants raid enemy territory while defending the mountain stronghold, but its improvised fortifications lack battlements and turrets. During a violent rainstorm the guards abandon their posts, enabling a herdsman to reveal how the Lacedæmonians can capture it.

CHAPTER XXI.

Eira was captured when Emperamus and the Spartan force entered its citadel by scaling the walls. Its fall ended the second war between the Lacedæmonians and the Messenians, and its captured inhabitants and property were dealt with by the Lacedæmonians.

CHAPTER XXIV.

Eira was captured by the Lacedæmonians, after which its Messenian inhabitants were incorporated among the Helots. Bacis was said to have foretold how Eira would be taken, and the Messenians returned to the Peloponnese 287 years after its capture.
