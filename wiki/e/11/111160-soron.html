1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Soron is an oak-coppice between Argeathæ, Lycuntes, and Scotane, on the road to Psophis. Its oak-coppices shelter wild animals such as boars and bears, as well as immense tortoises whose shells could make very large lyres; the ruins of Paus lie at its end.
