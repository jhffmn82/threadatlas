Abstract written by a model

Damoxenus, a Syracusan boxer, breaks his one-strike agreement with Creugas, kills him by tearing out his bowels, and is driven from the course.

CHAPTER XL.

Damoxenus was a Syracusan boxer who violated his agreement with Creugas to strike only once in turn. He was driven off the course after killing Creugas with a blow that tore out his bowels.
