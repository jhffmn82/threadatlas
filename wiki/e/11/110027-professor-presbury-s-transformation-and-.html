Professor Presbury's transformation and strange behavior

Abstract written by a model

Professor Presbury's transformation begins after his Prague journey: though his lectures remain brilliant, he becomes furtive, sly, sinister, and forgetful on certain dates. His behavior escalates into crawling on hands and feet, fierce conduct toward Edith, and unexplained appearances at her second-floor window.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

After returning from his journey, Professor Presbury becomes furtive and sly, behaves in a sinister and unexpected manner, and sometimes appears not to remember what he has done. He is seen crawling through the passage on his hands and feet, becomes fierce toward Edith, and is seen at her second-floor window despite the lack of any apparent means of reaching it.
