the case in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The case concerns Ronder's death and Eugenia Ronder's mutilation at Abbas Parva. Once unresolved by Sherlock Holmes, it is explained by Eugenia's confession; after Leonardo's death and the likely loss of the five-clawed club, Holmes declares it closed.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

The case concerns the unexplained circumstances surrounding Ronder's death and Eugenia Ronder's mutilation at Abbas Parva. Sherlock Holmes had once been unable to resolve it, but Eugenia's confession supplies the missing explanation.

We sat in silence for some time after the unhappy woman had told her

Sherlock Holmes declares the case closed after hearing that Leonardo is dead and that the five-clawed club may have been lost in the chalk-pit pool. The unnamed woman repeats that the case is closed.
