Abstract written by a model

Good Witch's hut is a small, plainly and poorly furnished home, always unbolted and welcoming seekers of help or advice. Tattypoo lives there with Agnes and the two-tailed cat; Orin occupies it while under Mombi's spell. After Tattypoo and Agnes disappear, Philador stays, searches, and finds its shed workshop and enormous bottle.

CHAPTER 7

The good witch's hut is a small, plainly and poorly furnished home where Tattypoo lives contentedly with Agnes and the cat with two tails. Its door is never bolted, and people seeking help or advice are welcomed. Philador enters it after Tattypoo and Agnes disappear.

CHAPTER 8

Philador stays overnight in the good witch's cottage after Tattypoo disappears. He searches its rooms and shed for magic objects and finds the workshop where the enormous bottle is kept.
