Aras in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Aras, an Autochthon and first inhabitant of Phliasian land, founded Arantia on the Arantine hill, named its land and city, fathered Aoris and Aræthyrea, and was buried at Celeæ.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Aras is described as an Autochthon and the first person to live in the land of Phlius. He founded a city on the Arantine hill, gave his name to the land and city called Arantia, and was the father of Aoris and Aræthyrea; his tomb is at Celeæ.
