Ethiopian bulls

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Ethiopian bulls are rhinoceroses, named for one projecting nose horn and a smaller horn beneath it; they have no horns on their heads.

CHAPTER XXI.

Ethiopian bulls have a horn projecting from the nose and a smaller horn beneath it, but no horns on the head. They are called rhinoceroses because of these nose horns.
