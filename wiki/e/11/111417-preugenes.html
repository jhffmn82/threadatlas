Preugenes

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Preugenes, son of Agenor, was a Lacedæmonian leader sharing authority with Achaean rulers. During Dorian rule of Lacedæmon and Argos, he followed a dream and, aided by a trusted slave, took Artemis Limnatis’s statue from Sparta. He was later commemorated by effigy, tomb, and annual rites.

Preugenes is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XX.

Preugenes is shown on a gate near the market-place in a golden effigy with Patreus and Atherion. While the Dorians held Lacedæmon and Argos, he obeyed a dream, took the statue of Artemis Limnatis from Sparta, and was assisted by his most trustworthy slave. His tomb stands before the temple of Athene in the precinct of Artemis Limnatis, and he receives annual funereal rites at that goddess's festival.
