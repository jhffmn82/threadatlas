Abstract written by a model

Pelarge, daughter of Potneus and wife of Isthmiades, taught and reestablished the Cabirian mysteries, extending their celebration beyond ancient boundaries; after an oracle from Dodona, she received honors and a pregnant sacrificial victim.

CHAPTER XXV.

Pelarge, the daughter of Potneus, and her husband Isthmiades reestablished the Cabirian mysteries after they had ceased to be celebrated. She celebrated them beyond the ancient boundaries, and, following an oracle from Dodona, received various honours and was assigned a pregnant victim for sacrifice.
