Thasos in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Thasos is the home of Theagenes and citizens who cast his statue into the sea. After famine, it sends envoys to Delphi, consults the Pythian Priestess again, recalls exiles, recovers and restores the statue, then sacrifices to it as a god.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Thasos was the home of Theagenes and of the citizens who first punished his statue by throwing it into the sea. When the land yielded no fruit, the Thasians consulted Delphi, recalled some exiles, recovered the statue, and restored it to its original site, after which they sacrificed to it as to a god.
