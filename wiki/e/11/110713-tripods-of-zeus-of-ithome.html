CHAPTER XI.

A Delphic oracle promised the Messenian land to those who first set up one hundred tripods at the altar of Zeus of Ithome. The Messenians prepared wooden tripods, while Œbalus secretly placed one hundred clay tripods there for Sparta; the Messenians then placed their wooden tripods at the altar as well.
