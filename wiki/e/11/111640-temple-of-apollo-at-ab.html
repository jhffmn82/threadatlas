Abstract written by a model

The ancient temple of Apollo at Abæ was burnt by Xerxes’ army and later by the Thebans during the Phocian War, when Phocian fugitives sheltered there. Most ruinous among fire-damaged buildings, it stood near Adrian’s smaller temple with bronze statues.

CHAPTER XXXV.

The ancient temple of Apollo at Abæ was burnt by Xerxes’ army and later set on fire by the Thebans during the Phocian War while Phocian fugitives had taken refuge there. It was therefore described as the most ruinous of the fire-damaged buildings; a smaller temple built by the Emperor Adrian stood nearby, containing ancient bronze statues of Apollo, Leto, and Artemis dedicated by the people of Abæ.
