love-cure in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Love-cure is a traditionally believed water treatment: bathing in the Selemnus makes men and women forget love, a remedy the narrator says would surpass great wealth if true.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

The water of the Selemnus was traditionally believed to cure love in both men and women by making those who bathed in it forget their love. The narrator says that, if the tradition were true, the water would be more valuable than great wealth.
