Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Ozwold’s wife’s favorite egg, carried safely by Peter on the journey despite Ozwold’s worry and a fall onto soft sand. During a banquet it hatches into a baby oztrich, steps awkwardly from its shell, wabbles off a chair, and ends the egg’s protective power.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Ozwold’s egg child is his wife’s favorite egg. Peter carries it during the journey, and Ozwold worries that it might hatch before they reach the Emerald City; it remains safe after falling onto soft sand.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The baby oztrich hatches from the egg during the banquet. It steps awkwardly from its shell, wabbles to the edge of the chair, and falls off, causing the egg’s protective power to end.
