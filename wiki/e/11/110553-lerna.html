Abstract written by a model

Lerna is a seaside place about forty stades from Argos, bordered by the Pontinus and Amymone, with sacred places and rites for several deities and associations with descents to Hades.

CHAPTER XXXV.

Lerna lies by the sea about forty stades from Argos and contains rites and sacred places dedicated to Demeter, Demeter Prosymne, Dionysus, Aphrodite, and Athene. It is bordered by the rivers Pontinus and Amymone and is associated with the descent of Pluto and the descent of Dionysus to Hades.
