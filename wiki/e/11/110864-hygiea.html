Hygiea in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Hygiea is a deity worshipped by those seeking propitiation. Her statues include one seated near Æsculapius at Argos and a hard-to-see image at Æsculapius’s temple at Titane, concealed by women’s shorn hair and a Babylonish garment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Hygiea had a statue at the temple of Æsculapius at Titane that was difficult to see because women’s shorn hair and the folds of a Babylonish garment concealed it. Whoever wished to propitiate either Hygiea or Æsculapius was instructed to worship Hygiea.
