bandmaster in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

A bandmaster in uniform governs Tune Town, explains its piano-key gates admitted the travelers through opening chords, requires them to sing and keep step, tests their tune, then permits them to go where they wish.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

The bandmaster explains that Tune Town’s gates are locked with piano keys and that the travelers entered when they struck the opening chords. He warns them to sing rather than speak, tests whether they are in tune, and tells them they may go where they wish as long as they keep step and sing.
