CHAPTER XXI.

The people of Zancle were defeated by Anaxilas at sea and by the Messenians on land. After their fort was taken, they sought refuge at altars and temples; Gorgus and Manticlus persuaded the others not to kill or enslave them, and they subsequently lived together as one people.
