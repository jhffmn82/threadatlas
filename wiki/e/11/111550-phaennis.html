Phaennis

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Phaennis is a divinely inspired prophetess, daughter of the Chaones’ king, contemporary with Antiochus. A generation before the Celts passed into Asia, she foretold their advance and that Attalus, the bull-horned helper, would destroy the Galati.

Phaennis is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XV.

Phaennis foretold, a generation in advance, that the army of the Celts would pass from Europe to Asia. She also foretold that a bull-horned helper, identified as Attalus, would destroy the Galati.
