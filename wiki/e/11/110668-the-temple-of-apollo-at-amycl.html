CHAPTER XIX.

The temple of Apollo at Amyclæ contains a throne with several partitions and intervals, the largest of which is in the middle and supports Apollo’s statue. The base of the statue is like an altar and is said to contain the burial of Hyacinthus; the altar is decorated with figures of numerous gods and mythological figures, including Hyacinthus and Polybœa being carried to heaven.
