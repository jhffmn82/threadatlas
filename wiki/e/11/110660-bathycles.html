Bathycles in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Bathycles of Magnesia was an artist who, aided by Magnesians, made Amyclæan Apollo’s throne, carving Graces and Artemis Leucophryene amid extensive scenes of gods, heroes, myths, contests, battles, animals, and exploits.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Bathycles of Magnesia made the throne of Amyclæan Apollo and carved Graces on it, along with a statue of Artemis Leucophryene. The throne contained extensive representations of gods, heroes, myths, contests, battles, animals, and divine or heroic exploits, including scenes involving Hercules, Theseus, Apollo, Artemis, Poseidon, Zeus, and many others.
