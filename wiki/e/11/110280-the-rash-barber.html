CHAPTER 7

The Rash barber celebrates upon learning that Prince Evered is the last prisoner by throwing his shaving mug and prancing on the sad singer's shins. He pounds on the cavern wall with his razor, apparently touches a secret spring, and causes the cavern side to fall away. He survives the tunnel journey, loses his razor temporarily, and wonders where they have landed.
