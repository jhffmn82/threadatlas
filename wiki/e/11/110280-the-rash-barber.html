the Rash Barber in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The Rash barber, a barber who owns a shaving mug and razor, celebrates Prince Evered being the last prisoner, opens a cavern passage with his razor, survives a tunnel journey, temporarily loses the razor, and wonders where they have landed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The Rash barber celebrates upon learning that Prince Evered is the last prisoner by throwing his shaving mug and prancing on the sad singer's shins. He pounds on the cavern wall with his razor, apparently touches a secret spring, and causes the cavern side to fall away. He survives the tunnel journey, loses his razor temporarily, and wonders where they have landed.
