Abstract written by a model

disables Thessalian cavalry [by laming horses and causing riders to be killed or unhorsed] The Phocians buried earthenware pots in the ground at the place where they expected the Thessalian cavalry to attack. The pots lamed some horses and caused some Thessalian riders to be killed or thrown from their horses.
