CHAPTER XVIII.

The Heraclidæ claimed Argos on the ground that Tisamenus was descended from Pelops, whereas they descended from Perseus. They further argued that Hercules had killed Hippocoon and his sons and restored the country to Tyndareus, supporting their claim to the region.
