Abstract written by a model

Teumessus is a place on the high-road, said to have hidden Europa by Zeus; its fox harmed Thebans until it and its pursuing dog became stone, and it has an unstatueed temple of Athene Telchinia.

CHAPTER XIX.

Teumessus is the place where Europa was said to have been hidden by Zeus. Its fox was brought up to harm the Thebans through the wrath of Dionysus, but it and the dog that was about to catch it were turned into stone. Teumessus also has a temple of Athene Telchinia without a statue.
