Abstract written by a model

The planned marriage of Baron Adelbert Gruner and Violet de Merville becomes the central danger Holmes and his associates seek to prevent. Gruner intends to marry Violet the following month, although he is believed to have murdered his first wife and committed other wrongdoing. Violet refuses warnings, while Holmes fears she will learn Gruner’s character only after marriage and Miss Winter predicts her death. Holmes opposes the union and, after an incriminating book is used against Gruner, the marriage is prevented. Three days later, The Morning Post announces that it will not take place.
