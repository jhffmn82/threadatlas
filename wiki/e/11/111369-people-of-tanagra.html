1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XX.

The people of Tanagra say that Pœmander was their founder and that Tanagra was his wife. They also say that people living nearby called Tanagra Graia because she lived to a very advanced age, and that the city was eventually called Graia before regaining the name Tanagra.
