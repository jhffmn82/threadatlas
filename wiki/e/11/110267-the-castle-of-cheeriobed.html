the Castle of Cheeriobed in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The Castle of Cheeriobed is a blue sapphire castle on the Ozure Isles and the royal residence of Cheeriobed and Queen Orin. After Akbad brings Orin there, Quiberon repeatedly attacks until the Wizard petrifies it in the gardens, and Cheeriobed admits Ozma’s party.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

Akbad carries Queen Orin to the sapphire castle after rescuing her from Quiberon. The Islanders secure themselves inside, but Quiberon crashes through the garden and strikes the castle until its walls and roof shake and splinter.

CHAPTER 17

The Castle of Cheeriobed is attacked by Quiberon until the Wizard petrifies the monster in its gardens. King Cheeriobed then opens the castle to Ozma and the rescuing party.
