Thyia in Description of Greece

Thyia is a Dionysus festival celebrated by the people of Elis about eight stades from the city, where sealed empty flagons are found full of wine next day.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

The Thyia is a festival of Dionysus celebrated by the people of Elis about 8 stades from the city. During it, three empty flagons are sealed in a chapel, and the next day they are found full of wine.
