Inspector MacKinnon in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Inspector MacKinnon, a police inspector, investigates the murders, objects to Holmes’s irregular methods, accepts his evidence, orders a search, and receives newspaper credit for Holmes’s deductions.

Inspector MacKinnon represented the police and objected that Holmes's irregular methods had taken credit from the official investigation. He accepted Holmes's evidence, arranged a search, and was credited by the newspaper with deductions and discoveries that Holmes had actually made.
