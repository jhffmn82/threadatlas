agreement between the Athenians and Oropians in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Agreement between the Athenians and Oropians provides for an Athenian garrison at Oropus and Oropian hostages held by the Athenians, following settlement by promises and gifts. If the Oropians bring further charges, the Athenians must withdraw the garrison and return the hostages.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The agreement provided that an Athenian garrison would occupy Oropus and that the Athenians would hold hostages from the Oropians. If the Oropians brought further charges against the Athenians, the Athenians were to withdraw the garrison and return the hostages.
