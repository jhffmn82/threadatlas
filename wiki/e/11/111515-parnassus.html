Parnassus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Parnassus, a hero and son of the Nymph Cleodora, is said to have Poseidon as divine father and Cleopompus as human father. He built Lycorea, discovered bird-flight omens, and gave his name to Mount Parnassus and dell Parnassus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Parnassus is said to have been the son of the Nymph Cleodora and to have had Poseidon as his divine father and Cleopompus as his human father. He supposedly built the most ancient town there, discovered omens from the flight of birds, and gave his name to Mount Parnassus and the dell Parnassus.
