Eurynome in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Eurynome is identified by the Phigalians as a title of Artemis, while Antiquarians describe her as Oceanus’s daughter who joined Thetis in receiving Hephæstus; her statue is woman above and fish below, chained in gold.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLI.

The Phigalians believe Eurynome to be a title of Artemis, while their Antiquarians say she was the daughter of Oceanus who joined Thetis in receiving Hephaestus. Her statue is said to be a woman down to the waist and a fish below, with gold chains around it.
