Xuthus

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Ἐξούθου, Ἐξοῦθος

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Xuthus was the son of Hellen and Orseis, received the Peloponnese, and fathered Achaeus and Ion by Creusa. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Xuthus was exiled from Thessaly, married Erechtheus's daughter, became the father of Achæus and Ion, and later lived and died in Ægialus.

Xuthus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · about AD 155 to 175 · the work’s page · read it where Xuthus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Xuthus, exiled from Thessaly by brothers over their father's money, fled to Athens, married Erechtheus's daughter, became father of Achæus and Ion, and later lived and died in Ægialus.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Description of Greece, Volume II / BOOK VII.--ACHAIA. / CHAPTER I.

Xuthus was driven out of Thessaly by his brothers, who accused him of secretly taking their father's money. He fled to Athens, married a daughter of Erechtheus, and had two sons, Achæus and Ion; he later lived and died in Ægialus.
