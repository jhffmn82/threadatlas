Xuthus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Xuthus, exiled from Thessaly by brothers over their father's money, fled to Athens, married Erechtheus's daughter, became father of Achæus and Ion, and later lived and died in Ægialus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Description of Greece, Volume II / BOOK VII.--ACHAIA. / CHAPTER I.

Xuthus was driven out of Thessaly by his brothers, who accused him of secretly taking their father's money. He fled to Athens, married a daughter of Erechtheus, and had two sons, Achæus and Ion; he later lived and died in Ægialus.
