Flying Cloak of Invisibility in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

The Flying Cloak of Invisibility is a magical garment that, when worn, makes its wearer invisible and can carry the wearer anywhere desired. Ruggedo seizes it from Peter and tries to use it to reach the Emerald City, but its large hole in the back and rent from collar to hem prevent it from working. Gnome magic cannot mend the damage; it must be properly repaired in the Kingdom of Patch. Ruggedo brings it to Patch’s castle by claiming it needs mending. After an old Quilty woman repairs it, he wears it, becomes invisible except for its blue patch, and then disappears completely. He later uses the cloak to escape Wumbo’s arm-chair, travel to Ozma’s palace, move unseen, and attack and hide from palace residents. Its invisibility ends when the Wizard’s powder breaks the cloak’s spell and reveals Ruggedo.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

The Flying Cloak of Invisibility is described as making its wearer invisible and taking the wearer wherever desired. Ruggedo seizes it from Peter and attempts to travel to the Emerald City, but it does nothing because a large hole and a long tear run through it.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

The magic cloak is carried by Ruggedo after he returns to the Kingdom of Gnomes. Potaroo determines that gnome magic cannot mend it and says it must be repaired in the Kingdom of Patch.

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Ruggedo waves a cloak over his head when he sees Patch’s castle and boasts that he will soon rule the realm. He later tells Scrapper that he has a cloak to be mended, using this claim to enter the castle.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

The magic cloak is brought to the Quilty castle to be repaired because it can take its wearer wherever he wants to go. Once the old Quilty woman mends it, Ruggedo puts it on and becomes invisible except for a blue patch before disappearing completely.

CHAPTER 15 Wumbo, Wonder Worker, At Home

Ruggedo wears a magic cloak that makes him invisible. When Wumbo’s chant wears off, Ruggedo whispers a command and the cloak lifts him out of the arm-chair’s grasp and carries him away.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Ruggedo uses the invisible cloak to travel to Ozma’s palace and move around unseen. It allows him to pinch and strike the palace residents and hide beneath the banquet table while he waits for Ozma.

CHAPTER 19 The Wizard Makes The Gnome King Visible

The magic cloak keeps Ruggedo invisible. The Wizard's powder breaks its spell and reveals Ruggedo to his enemies.
