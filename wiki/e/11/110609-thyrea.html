Thyrea in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Thyrea is a place of battle contested by 300 Argives and 300 Lacedæmonians; the Lacedæmonians secured it, gave it to the Æginetans, and it was later inhabited and claimed as recovered by the Argives.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXV.

At Thyrea, 300 picked Argives fought 300 picked Lacedæmonians for possession of the land. After nearly all were killed, the Lacedæmonians secured the district, later gave it to the Æginetans, and the Argives eventually inhabited it and claimed to have recovered it by conquest.
