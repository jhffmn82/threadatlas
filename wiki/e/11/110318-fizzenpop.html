Fizzenpop

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Grand Vizier, Grand Vizier of Rash, old Fizzenpop, old rascal, old statesman, the Grand Vizier of Rash

Abstract written by a model

Fizzenpop is a wise, thin, bald Rasher and Grand Vizier of Rash who remains in office to serve his people despite wishing to retire. Loyal to Prince Evered, he risks himself to protect him: he warns of the overcrowded prison, investigates the Hungry Tiger, recognizes Evered, explains Irashi's seizure of power, and deceives Irashi after Evered disappears. He secretly searches the Rash garden for the scattered magic rubies. When the rubies are recovered, he welcomes Reddy, arranges Evered's restoration proclamation from the pink balcony, and provides a feast. He creates Rash hurry-canes, uses them to cross the Deadly Desert, and later watches for Ippty and Irashi with a telescope.

Fizzenpop is in one work on this site, The Hungry Tiger of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Fizzenpop in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Fizzenpop first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Fizzenpop warns that the Rash prison is overcrowded and urges the Pasha to set prisoners free. He is unable to stop the plan to obtain the Hungry Tiger, so he consults Professor Wogglebug's Encyclopedia of Oz and learns that the tiger has never been known to devour a living person.

CHAPTER 6

Fizzenpop identifies the child as Prince Evered and explains the history of Irashi's seizure of power and the scattered rubies. After Evered and the Hungry Tiger disappear, Fizzenpop falsely tells Irashi that the tiger escaped so he can protect the Prince and his country.

CHAPTER 19

Fizzenpop comes from the castle to investigate the battle and is frightened by the giant's appearance. After learning what happened and seeing the Rash rubies, he welcomes Reddy, arranges the proclamation of his restoration, and provides a feast.

CHAPTER 20

Fizzenpop proposes using the marvelous Rash hurry-canes to cross the Deadly Desert safely. He brings five hurry-canes from the umbrella stand, and in Rash he later uses a telescope to look for Ippty and Irashi on clear nights.
