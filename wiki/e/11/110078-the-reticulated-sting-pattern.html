Abstract written by a model

is_a red inflamed lines [criss-crossed and reticulated] The reticulated sting pattern consists of criss-crossed red, inflamed lines with uneven intensity and distinct dots of extravasated blood. Sherlock Holmes sees the same pattern on Fitzroy McPherson and Ian Murdoch and recognizes it as the mark left by the jellyfish's filaments.
