Iasius in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Iasius, son of Eleuther, won a horse-race at Olympia when Hercules the Theban established the Olympian games. He is commemorated by a pillar statue in the Tegea market-place, touching a horse with his left hand and holding a palm branch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVIII.

Iasius is represented on a pillar in the market-place, with his left hand on a horse and a branch of palm in his right hand. He is said to have won the horserace at Olympia when Hercules the Theban established the Olympian games.
