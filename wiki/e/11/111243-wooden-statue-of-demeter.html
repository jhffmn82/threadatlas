wooden statue of Demeter

object · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a wooden statue of the goddess, ancient statue, the old one, the statue

Abstract written by a model

A wooden statue of Demeter, made by the people of Phigalia and placed in a cave, showed her seated on a rock with a woman’s body, mare’s head and mane, and monsters around her head. Called Black Demeter for its black dress, it wore a foot-length tunic and held a dolphin and dove. It later burned; its maker and circumstances were unrecorded.

CHAPTER XLII.

The people of Phigalia placed a wooden statue of Demeter in the cave. It portrayed her seated on a rock with a mare’s head and mane, dragons and other monsters around her head, a tunic reaching to her feet, a dolphin in one hand, and a dove in the other; the statue later caught fire, and no one recorded its maker or the circumstances.
