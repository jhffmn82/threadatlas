Acrocorinth in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Acrocorinth is the summit of a hill above Corinth. Briareus awarded it to the Sun after litigation with Poseidon, and the Sun gave it to Aphrodite. It contains temples to many gods, including Aphrodite, and a fountain associated with Asopus or Pirene.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK II.--CORINTH.

Acrocorinth was the summit above Corinth, which the Corinthians said Briareus awarded to the Sun and the Sun gave to Aphrodite. It contained temples of Isis, Serapis, the Sun, Necessity and Force, the Mother of the Gods, the Fates, Demeter, Proserpine, and Bunæan Hera, as well as a temple of Aphrodite and a fountain associated with Asopus or Pirene.
