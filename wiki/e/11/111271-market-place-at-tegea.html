CHAPTER XLVIII.

The market-place is shaped very much like a brick. It contains the temple and statue of the Brick Aphrodite, pillars commemorating legislators and Iasius, a statue of Ares Gynæcothœnas, an altar and statue of Adult Zeus, the tombs of Tegeates and Mæra, and the temple of Ilithyia.
