Umpires of Elis in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Umpires of Elis began with Iphitus alone, followed by the posterity of Oxylus. Their number changed with Elis’s organization and tribes: two, nine, ten, twelve, eight after territory loss, and finally ten. They swore over a boar’s entrails to judge honestly without bribes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII.

The games originally had Iphitus as their sole umpire, followed by the posterity of Oxylus. The number later increased from two to nine, then ten, twelve, eight, and finally ten again as the organization and tribal divisions of Elis changed.
