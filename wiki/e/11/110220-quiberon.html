Quiberon in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Quiberon is Mombi's gigantic sea monster: a dragon-headed fear-fish with dragon scales and talons, fiery nostrils, horns, a silver lock of hair, and eight red tusks. He occupies the Lake of Orizon and its cave, where his head rises above the water. Sent by Mombi, he pitilessly destroys Orizon's sea horses, terrifies the Ozure Islanders, prevents them from leaving, and demands a mortal maiden to serve him. He threatens the Ozure Isles with destruction unless they comply, and his menace is also linked to the threatened destruction of Philador's father's Kingdom. He imprisons Orpah after Orpah defends the sea horses and seeks to make Trot his servant, assigning her tasks in his cave. While chasing Trot and her companions, he loses his tusks biting Benny and becomes trapped in a narrow passageway. He later escapes or reaches the lake, attacks Queen Orin's boat, pursues Akbad, repeatedly assaults the sapphire castle, and is transported to the gardens of the Castle of Cheeriobed. There, before he can attack again, the Wizard uses black magic to petrify him into a glittering dragon of silver and bronze. His great figure is finally moved to the mouth of his cave, remaining there for all to see.

Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Quiberon is Mombi's monster, with the head, scales, and talons of a dragon and the body of a giant fear-fish. He destroys the sea horses, shakes the islands, terrifies the Ozurians, and demands a mortal maiden to wait upon him. After issuing his threat, he dives beneath the waves.

CHAPTER 2

Quiberon threatens the destruction of the Ozure Isles and will allow no one to leave the islands. He demands a mortal maiden, and his horrid head rises from the waters of Orizon.

CHAPTER 5

Quiberon is a giant fear-fish or dragon with fiery nostrils, smoke, and red tusks. He wants Trot as a servant to polish his scales, comb his hair, sweep the cave, and tell him stories, but he chases the travelers, loses his tusks biting Benny, and becomes stuck in a narrow passage.

CHAPTER 6

Quiberon is believed by Akbad to have possibly devoured Philador. When King Cheeriobed and his Guards search the monster's cave, Quiberon is absent.

CHAPTER 8

Quiberon threatens to destroy Philador's father's Kingdom, leaving Philador only two days to find help.

CHAPTER 9

The monster threatening the Ozure Isles is said to be capable of utterly destroying Philador's father's kingdom. No further details about the monster are given in this unit.

CHAPTER 11

Quiberon came roaring across the lake after the Queen was abducted and devoured the sea horses on which the Ozure Islanders rode to the mainland. When Orpah tried to defend the sea horses, Quiberon seized him and thrust him into his cave.

CHAPTER 14

Quiberon demands a mortal maiden and threatens to destroy the Ozure Isles' Kingdom within three days. Trot's account leads Philador to connect Quiberon's demand with the mysterious events surrounding his own quest.

CHAPTER 16

Quiberon is trapped in a narrow passageway after Trot and her companions outwit him, but he later escapes or reaches the area around the lake. He attacks Queen Orin's boat, pursues Akbad after the rescue, and repeatedly strikes the sapphire castle.

CHAPTER 17

Quiberon is shown attacking the sapphire castle and is transported to the gardens of the Castle of Cheeriobed. Before he can strike again, the Wizard petrifies him into a glittering dragon of silver and bronze.

CHAPTER 19

Quiberon's great figure is moved by the Wizard to the mouth of its cave, where it remains for all to see. The Wizard determines that fire from its nostrils probably ate away the passageway and enabled it to escape.
