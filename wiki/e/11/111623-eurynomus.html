Eurynomus is a demon in Hades, as described by the Antiquarians at Delphi. It eats dead flesh clean to the bones and, in a painting, appears blueish-black, fang-showing, and seated on a vulture’s skin; it is absent from named poems.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Eurynomus is described by the Antiquarians at Delphi as a demon in Hades who eats the flesh of the dead down to their bones. In the painting he is blueish-black, shows his fangs, and sits on a vulture’s skin; he is not mentioned in the Odyssey, the Minyad, or The Return from Ilium.
