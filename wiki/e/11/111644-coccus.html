CHAPTER XXXVI.

Coccus grows in the plain between Stiris and Ambrosus, though less plentifully than vines. Its berry is gathered while it contains a grub, because the grub's blood is useful for dyeing wool; the Ionians and Greeks call it coccus, while the Galati above Phrygia call it Hys.
