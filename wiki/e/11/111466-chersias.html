Chersias in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Chersias, an Orchomenian, wrote lost verses on Orchomenus, described Aspledon’s parentage and birth, and was said to have composed an epitaph on Hesiod.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

Chersias, an Orchomenian, wrote verses stating that Aspledon was the son of Poseidon and the Nymph Midea and was born in the large city. Callippus cited these verses, and the Orchomenians say that Chersias composed the epitaph on Hesiod.
