CHAPTER XXI.

The gymnasium of Elis is an old training complex where athletes prepare before going to Olympia. It contains the Colonnade, racecourses, the Plethrium for testing athletes, altars and statues, the Square for wrestling, Maltho for the lads, and the Lalichmium council chamber for rhetorical practice and public criticism of writings.
