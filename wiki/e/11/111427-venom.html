Venom belongs to snakes on Helicon and is generally deadly to men and animals when from wild snakes; its strength varies with snakes’ pastures, being weaker in Helicon, stronger in mountainous Phœnicia, and milder near Arabian balsam trees.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

The strength of venom is said to depend greatly on the pastures where snakes live. In Helicon it is less potent, while roots in mountainous Phœnicia supposedly make viper venom more formidable; venom from vipers near balsam trees in Arabia is described as milder.
