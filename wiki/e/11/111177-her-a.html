Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

Heræa was founded by Heræus son of Lycaon and lies mostly on a gentle eminence on the right of the Alpheus, with part of the town extending to the river. It has race-courses, baths, temples of Dionysus, a building for the orgies of Dionysus, a temple of Pan, and ruins of a temple of Hera. Damaretus of Heræa was the foremost of the Arcadian athletes and the first victor at Olympia in the race in heavy armour.
