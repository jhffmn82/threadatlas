Tegeates

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Tegeates the son of Lycaon

Abstract written by a model

Tegeates, son of Lycaon, founded Tegea and was father of Scephrus. After Limon killed Scephrus, Tegeates and Mera sacrificed to Apollo and Artemis; his other sons migrated to Crete. Tegeates has a tomb in Tegea’s market-place.

Tegeates is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
