Tegeates in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tegeates, son of Lycaon, founded Tegea and was father of Scephrus. After Limon killed Scephrus, Tegeates and Mera sacrificed to Apollo and Artemis; his other sons migrated to Crete. Tegeates has a tomb in Tegea’s market-place.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVIII.

Tegeates, the son of Lycaon, has a tomb in the market-place at Tegea.

CHAPTER LIII.

Tegeates and Mera sacrificed to Apollo and Artemis after Limon killed Tegeates’ son Scephrus. Tegeates’ other sons are said by the people of Tegea to have migrated to Crete.
