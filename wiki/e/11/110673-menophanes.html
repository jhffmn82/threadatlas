Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Menophanes, a general of Mithridates, attacked and destroyed Delos, killing inhabitants and robbing merchants and temples. Afterward, merchants who had escaped lay in wait for him, sank his vessel, and killed him.
