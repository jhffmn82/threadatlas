Abstract written by a model

The beach below the chalk cliffs contains a lagoon, swimming-pools, caves, pebbles, shingle, and a secluded bathing-pool. Though it seems deserted except for distant figures, it becomes the site of McPherson's death, his dog’s tracks and towel, and Ian Murdoch’s attack, caused by a deadly jellyfish concealed in the pool.
