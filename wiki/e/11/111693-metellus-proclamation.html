Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Metellus’ proclamation urged the Lacedæmonians and other cities named by the Romans to leave the Achæan League and promised no Roman anger over earlier disobedience. Later, at the Isthmus, he issued another proclamation inviting the Achæans to peace and harmony.
