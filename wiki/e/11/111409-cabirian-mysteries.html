Cabirian mysteries

Abstract written by a model

The Cabirian mysteries, a gift from Demeter to the Cabiri, ceased after the Argives expelled them, then Pelarge and Isthmiades restored them; their rites and originating deposit remain undisclosed.

CHAPTER XXV.

The text declines to disclose the rites of the Cabirian mysteries or the nature of the deposit associated with their origin. The mysteries were a gift of Demeter to the Cabiri, ceased for a time after the Argives expelled them, and were later restored by Pelarge and Isthmiades.
