Alea

place · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: town

Abstract written by a model

Alea is a town in the Argolic league next to Stymphelus, traditionally founded by Aleus; it has temples of Ephesian Artemis and Alean Athene, Dionysus’ temple and statue, and annually celebrates Scieria.

Alea is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Alea is a town in the Argolic league, said to have been founded by Aleus the son of Aphidas. It has temples of Ephesian Artemis and Alean Athene, and a temple and statue of Dionysus.
