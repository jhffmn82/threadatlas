Dinocrates in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Dinocrates, a prominent Messenian newly elected ruler, forced Lycortas and the Achæan army from Messenia, then occupied routes toward Arcadia to protect Messenians and neighboring districts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIX.

Dinocrates, a prominent Messenian recently elected ruler, forced Lycortas and the Achæan army to withdraw from Messenia without achieving their purpose. He then occupied the byroads between Messenia and Arcadia to protect the Messenians and their neighboring districts.

Dinocrates in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Dinocrates, leader of the wealthy Messenians, urged and used poison to kill Philopœmen, then committed suicide after Lycortas and the popular party punished those responsible.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER LI.

Dinocrates and the wealthy Messenians urged that Philopœmen be put to death. He nevertheless had Philopœmen killed by poison and later committed suicide when Lycortas and the popular party acted against those responsible.
