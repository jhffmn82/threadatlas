Dinocrates

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a prominent man among the people

Abstract written by a model

In Description of Greece, Dinocrates, a prominent Messenian newly elected ruler, forced Lycortas and the Achæan army from Messenia, then occupied routes toward Arcadia to protect Messenians and neighboring districts. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Dinocrates, leader of the wealthy Messenians, urged and used poison to kill Philopœmen, then committed suicide after Lycortas and the popular party punished those responsible.

CHAPTER XXIX.

Dinocrates, a prominent Messenian recently elected ruler, forced Lycortas and the Achæan army to withdraw from Messenia without achieving their purpose. He then occupied the byroads between Messenia and Arcadia to protect the Messenians and their neighboring districts.
