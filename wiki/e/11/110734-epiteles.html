CHAPTER XXIV.

Epiteles, whom the Argives chose as their General and the restorer of Messene, was directed in a dream to dig between an ivy and myrtle tree at Ithome. He found a brass cinerary urn containing the written Mysteries of the Great Goddesses and took it to Epaminondas.
