Oracle concerning the Phigalians’ return to Phigalia: it denies hope if they return alone, but promises success if they take 100 picked men from Oresthasium, who must be slain in battle.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIX.

The oracle declared that the Phigalians could not hope to return to Phigalia by themselves. It promised their return if they took 100 picked men from Oresthasium and those men were slain in battle.
