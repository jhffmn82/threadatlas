the Down Officers in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The Down Officers, twenty tall officers serving Dad as the King’s officers, answer a proprietor’s alarm, net and hold Hungry Tiger’s group, then take them through Down Town to Dad.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The Down Officers respond to the restaurant proprietor's alarm by capturing the Hungry Tiger and the others with a large net and by holding the individual prisoners. They take the entire group through Down Town and bring them to Dad on the roof of a bank.
