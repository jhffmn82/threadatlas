CHAPTER XVII.

The temple contains a statue of Artemis Euclea by Scopas and is said to contain the burials of Androclea and Alcis, the daughters of Antipœnus. A stone lion in front of it was reportedly dedicated by Hercules after his victory over the Orchomenians and their king Erginus.
