temple of Artemis Euclea in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Temple containing a statue of Artemis Euclea by Scopas and the burials of Androclea and Alcis; a lion reportedly dedicated by Hercules stands before it, with statues of Apollo Boedromius and Hermes Agoræus nearby.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The temple contains a statue of Artemis Euclea by Scopas and is said to contain the burials of Androclea and Alcis, the daughters of Antipœnus. A stone lion in front of it was reportedly dedicated by Hercules after his victory over the Orchomenians and their king Erginus.
