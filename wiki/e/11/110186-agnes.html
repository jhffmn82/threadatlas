Agnes in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Agnes is Tattypoo’s small, amiable, good-tempered pet dragon and Orin’s maid-in-waiting, living with Tattypoo since Mombi’s conquest. Though non-destructive, she longs for grandeur, beauty, and a better home; she persuades Tattypoo to use a witch’s window, then falls after her.

CHAPTER 7

Agnes is Tattypoo's good-tempered pet dragon, but she longs for grandeur, beauty, and a more impressive home. She persuades Tattypoo to look through the witch's window and falls out after her when Tattypoo suddenly jumps through it.
