the Valkyrior in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Valkyrior are mounted, armed warlike virgins who serve as Odin's messengers, choosing slain warriors in battle and bringing the bravest to Valhalla.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

The Valkyrior are warlike virgins mounted on horses and armed with helmets, shields, and spears. As Odin's messengers, they choose warriors to be slain in battle and bring the bravest of them to Valhalla.
