Reddy in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Reddy first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reddy is a little boy and Prince of Rash, its rightful ruler, described as brave, resourceful, and later wise and brave as ruler. He possesses the Rash rubies and is an old friend of Hungry Tiger; Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and Vegetable Man are travelling companions or associates. While waiting outside Immense City with Betsy and Carter, Reddy is seized by a giant pigeon and carried over the wall. Dropped on a window ledge in Giant Castle, he uses the rubies to survive a jump into a water pitcher and float to the floor. He reaches Princess Elma's playroom, helps free Hungry Tiger from doll clothes, and learns that Giants become ordinary-sized without their wigs. Reddy takes Princess Elma's big wig, transforms into a Big Wig, rescues Hungry Tiger, and escapes. After Betsy and Carter initially flee from him, he removes the wig and reunites with them. Traveling toward the Emerald City of Oz, he uses the wig to see for miles, learns Atmos holds the last ruby, and recovers all three. Believing the rubies let him confront Irashi and reclaim his throne, he later rules Rash peacefully after Ippty and Irashi are removed. He keeps the rubies, gives the giant wig to Fizzenpop, and promises to return to Rash in a month.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

Reddy waits with Betsy and Carter outside Immense City. A giant pigeon mistakes him for a crumb or bug, snaps him up, and carries him over the wall.

CHAPTER 16

Reddy is dropped by a giant pigeon onto a high window ledge in the Giants' castle. He uses the Rash rubies to survive a jump into a water pitcher and to float safely from the sideboard to the floor. He follows a giant maid to Princess Elma's playroom, waits for the Hungry Tiger, and helps remove the doll cap and coat from him before planning an escape.

CHAPTER 17

Reddy hides beneath a chair in the Giant Castle and learns that the Giants are ordinary-sized when they remove their wigs. He puts on Princess Elma's big wig, grows into a Big Wig, carries the Hungry Tiger out of the castle, and escapes through the gates. After Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green flee from him, Reddy removes the wig and is reunited with them.

CHAPTER 18

Reddy travels with Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and the Hungry Tiger toward the Emerald City of Oz. Using Elma's wig, he sees Ozma and Atmos approaching, and he later learns that Atmos has the last ruby. With all three rubies, Reddy believes he can safely confront Irashi and reclaim his throne.

CHAPTER 20

Reddy repeatedly urges Ozma and the others to stay in Rash, but eventually joins them on their journey to the Emerald City. He keeps the Rash rubies, gives the giant wig to Fizzenpop, and promises to return to Rash in a month.

Prince Evered of Rash in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Prince Evered of Rash first appears

Abstract written by a model

Prince Evered of Rash, also called Reddy and the Scarlet Prince, is Asha's son, Irashi the Rough's young nephew, and the rightful ruler of Rash. After Irashi usurps the throne, Evered is hidden away and becomes Rash's last prisoner, though magic rubies protect the rightful rulers. He is a pleasant, freckle-faced boy with kingly bearing despite ragged clothes. Fizzenpop brings him to a courtyard, and the Hungry Tiger helps him escape through a tunnel, where he meets Betsy Bobbin and her companions. Evered explains that Irashi stole the three Rash rubies and made himself Pasha. Carter gives him a ruby, which protects him from water and later saves him from Guph's pick-axe. Hopeful of recovering his throne, he promises rewards, rejects an office-boy position, acquires swords, survives underground dangers, and seeks the remaining rubies. He insists that he, rather than Ozma by magic, must conquer Rash. With his companions, he captures Irashi, takes Rash's crown, terrifies and defeats the Rash army using a giant's wig, completes Reddy's conquest of Rash, and is proclaimed Evered, the Scarlet Prince and rightful ruler of Rash.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Prince Evered of Rash is Irashi's young nephew and the rightful prince, whom Irashi had hidden away. Fizzenpop brings him to the courtyard, where the Hungry Tiger protects him before lowering him into the underground cavern.

CHAPTER 7

Prince Evered of Rash is introduced as the last prisoner in Rash and is called the Scarlet Prince. He escapes through the tunnel with the Hungry Tiger, meets Betsy Bobbin, likes her immediately, and becomes sleepy after hearing the singer's song.

CHAPTER 8

Prince Evered of Rash, whom Betsy calls Reddy, explains that Irashi stole the Rash rubies and made himself Pasha of Rash. After Carter identifies his ruby, Evered takes it and becomes hopeful that he can regain his throne. He promises rewards to his companions if he becomes Pasha, then is arrested with them in Down Town and taken to Dad.

CHAPTER 9

Prince Evered of Rash refuses the proposed office-boy position and hopes to return to Rash after his kingdom is restored. He picks a sword from the Indus Tree, follows the group into the subway, and falls into Cave Inn.

CHAPTER 10

Prince Evered falls with the others into the underground pool and complains that he is nearly drowned. He retains the ruby that protects him from water, explains that he was deprived of his throne and robbed of the three Rash rubies, and accompanies the others to Kaliko's castle.

CHAPTER 11

Prince Evered is the target of Guph's pick-axe attack, but the blow does not harm him. He draws his sword and knocks Guph flat before escaping with the others down the fire-fall.

CHAPTER 12

Prince Evered hopes to find the last ruby, which he believes protects against danger in the air. He uses his sword to cut the giant apple into slices and follows Betsy and Carter Green toward Immense City after the Hungry Tiger is taken.

CHAPTER 19

Reddy refuses to let Ozma conquer Rash by magic and insists that a prince should conquer his own enemies. He captures Irashi, takes the crown of Rash, uses the giant's wig to rout the Rash army, and is proclaimed Evered, the Scarlet Prince and rightful ruler of Rash.
