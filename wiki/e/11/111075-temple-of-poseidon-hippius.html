Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

The temple of Poseidon Hippius was rebuilt in the writer’s day by the Emperor Adrian, who ordered the new temple to be constructed around the old one and forbade anyone to inspect or disturb the ancient ruins. The older temple was said to have been built by Agamedes and Trophonius, and a woollen thread was once stretched across its entrance. Tradition held that seawater sprang up inside it.
