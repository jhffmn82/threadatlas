is_a spring [formed by the horse of Bellerophon striking the earth with its hoof, according to tradition] Hippocrene is a spring reached after ascending twenty stades from the grove at Helicon. It is said to have been formed when the horse of Bellerophon struck the earth with its hoof, and lead bearing The Works and Days was reportedly found near it.
