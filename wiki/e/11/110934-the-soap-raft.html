CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

The soap raft is made from a large slab of soap taken from an abandoned soap building. Peter pushes it into the lake with a bar of green soap as a pole, and he, Scraps, and Grumpy float away from the Sultan of Suds.
