the second war between the Lacedæmonians and the Messenians in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The second war between the Messenians and Lacedæmonians began thirty-nine years after Ithome’s capture, when Messenian Helots revolted after suppliants were killed at Tænarum. The rebels occupied Ithome, fought an undecided battle at Deræ, and assembled allies for Boar’s Memorial, while the Lacedæmonians sought allied aid. It ended with Eira’s capture in the first year of the 28th Olympiad.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The second Messenian War began thirty-nine years after the capture of Ithome, in the fourth year of the 23rd Olympiad. The Messenians rose against Spartan rule, fought an undecided battle at Deræ, and prepared for a larger engagement with allies at Boar’s Memorial.

CHAPTER XXI.

The second war ended with the capture of Eira in the archonship of Autosthenes, in the first year of the 28th Olympiad, when Chionis the Laconian was victor.

CHAPTER XXIV.

The Second Messenian revolt began when Messenian Helots rose against the Lacedæmonians after the killing of suppliants at Tænarum. The rebels went to Ithome, and the Lacedæmonians sought assistance from allies including Cimon and the Athenians.
