Sosipolis in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Sosipolis is the tutelary deity of the people of Elis, honored in the inaccessible temple of Ilithyia and near Fortune. Maidens and women offer hymns and incense, without wine. In the story, a baby offered to aid the Eleans became a dragon, drove away the Arcadians, and secured an Elean victory.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

Sosipolis was the tutelary deity of the people of Elis and was honored inside the temple of Ilithyia. According to the story, he appeared as a dragon from a baby offered to aid the Eleans, causing the Arcadians to flee and enabling the Eleans to win a notable victory.
