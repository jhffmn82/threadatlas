Narrative, section by section

14 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

Carter Green is a Winkie who became a vegetable-like figure after eating leftover vegetables from his cart. He sells produce, takes Betsy along after the winding road strands them, and puts on the Quick Sandals, which make him leap and race uncontrollably toward the Deadly Desert.

CHAPTER 5

Carter Green is carried across the Deadly Desert by the quick sandals and then travels with Betsy into Rash to sell vegetables. After offering Pasha Irashi a cabbage, he is rooted to the ground, captured by the Rash Guardsmen, and thrown into prison, where he is terrified to find the Hungry Tiger.

CHAPTER 6

Carter Green emerges from the Hungry Tiger's drinking cup after using it as a bath. He supplies vegetables and fruit to the Hungry Tiger and helps keep the prisoners fed and cheerful.

CHAPTER 7

Carter discovers that the cavern floor is stone and listens through the crevice with his corn ears. He escapes through the tunnel with Betsy, but his vegetables are scattered and squashed during the journey. Since becoming a vegetable, Carter does not need rest and spends the night pacing through the field of down while watching over his companions.

CHAPTER 8

Carter Green travels beside the Hungry Tiger and helps Prince Evered understand that the group may be able to recover the Rash rubies. He recognizes that the square ruby he found in a potato has an "R" cut into it and therefore matches Evered's description of a Rash ruby. He gives the ruby to Evered before the group enters Down Town and is later arrested with the others.

CHAPTER 9

Carter Green observes that Down Town is controlled by Daddies and that they make money from many kinds of businesses. He picks a wheel-barrow and a small spade from the Indus Tree, pushes Betsy Bobbin through the subway, and enters Cave Inn with the others.

CHAPTER 10

Carter Green is pulled from the underground pool and continues through the Lost Labyrinth while stubbornly pushing his wheel-barrow. A thin blue mold begins forming on his nose, and he worries that he will spoil if he cannot escape soon. At Kaliko's castle, he notices Kaliko hiding a ring and later overhears Guph discussing another ruby.

CHAPTER 11

Carter Green overhears Guph and Kaliko discussing the second Rash ruby and sees Guph apparently preparing to attack Prince Evered. He takes the ruby ring from the throne and helps fight the Gnome army with a spade, but his ears are burned and popped during the descent down the fire-fall.

CHAPTER 12

Carter Green receives new corn ears after his previous pair popped or wore out. He worries that millions of ants could feast on him, finds a giant apple for supper, and makes beds outside Immense City while staying awake through the night.

CHAPTER 13

Carter Green watches the door of Immense City and tries to reassure Betsy after Reddy is carried away. He plans to grow up the wall with Betsy and bend down on the other side, but the city door opens before he begins.

CHAPTER 17

Carter Green is with Betsy Bobbin when Reddy returns from Immense City wearing the big wig. He initially flees with Betsy, then embraces Reddy and the Hungry Tiger after Reddy becomes boy-sized again. Carter keeps watch for Giants while the others sleep.

CHAPTER 18

Carter Green helps Betsy and Reddy recount their adventures to Ozma and Atmos. He identifies Atmos's ruby as the one Irashi tossed into the air and celebrates the coincidence involving all three rubies. He continues traveling with the group toward the Deadly Desert.

CHAPTER 19

Carter Green agrees to help Reddy conquer Rash, armed with a stout stick and relying on the Rash rubies. He fights the Rash army, fetches rope to bind Irashi and Ippty, and suggests that Ozma and Reddy decide their fate.

CHAPTER 20

Carter Green suggests that a Rash ruby might protect the travellers from the burning sands, recalling that he had possessed it when they first crossed the desert. In the Emerald City, he is so frequently invited and sought after that he could not have taken root even if he had wanted to.
