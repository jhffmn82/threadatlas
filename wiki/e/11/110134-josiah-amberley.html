THE ADVENTURE OF THE RETIRED COLOURMAN

Josiah Amberley, a retired junior partner of Brickfall and Amberley, lives at The Haven after retiring with a fortune and marrying a younger woman. He claims that Mrs. Amberley and Dr. Ray Ernest left together with his deed-box and about seven thousand pounds' worth of cash and securities. He appears physically burdened and emotionally devastated, and is described as a miserly, harsh, and exacting husband.

Josiah Amberley was a miser, a jealous chess player, and the husband of the missing woman. Holmes concluded that Amberley had murdered his wife and Dr. Ray Ernest by trapping them in the strong-room and flooding it with gas; Amberley was arrested after trying to swallow a white pellet.
