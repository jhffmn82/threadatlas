CHAPTER IX.

The votive offerings at Delphi include Phayllus of Croton, a bronze bull dedicated by the Corcyræans, statues commemorating Tegean spoils from the Lacedæmonians, and statues dedicated by the Lacedæmonians after defeating the Athenians. Other offerings commemorate the Spartans and their allies at Ægos-potamoi, as well as the Argives’ claimed victory beyond Thyrea.
