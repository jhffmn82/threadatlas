The Haven in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The Haven begins as the Bellamy family home in Fulworth, where Ian Murdoch leaves as Holmes and Stackhurst arrive. It is also recorded as Tom Bellamy's poorly maintained Lewisham property and, finally, Josiah Amberley's neglected, walled house with a strong-room.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE LION'S MANE

The Haven is the Bellamy family home in Fulworth. Ian Murdoch is seen leaving it when Holmes and Stackhurst arrive to speak with the Bellamys.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE RETIRED COLOURMAN

The Haven is Josiah Amberley's neglected house in Lewisham, surrounded by a high brick wall. It contains a strong-room with an iron door and shutter, as well as the rooms and passage Amberley has been painting.
