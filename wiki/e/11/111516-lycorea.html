Lycorea

Abstract written by a model

Lycorea, a town later called Delphi and Pytho, was supposedly founded by survivors of Deucalion’s flood who followed wolves to Mount Parnassus; its name meant Wolf-town, though another tradition named it for Lycorus.

Lycorea is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER VI.

Lycorea was supposedly founded by the survivors of Deucalion’s flood after they followed wolves to Mount Parnassus, and its name was associated with this circumstance as Wolf-town. Another tradition says that Lycorea was named after Lycorus, who was the son of Apollo and the Nymph Corycia.
