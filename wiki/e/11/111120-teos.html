Teos in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Teos, a place, was colonized by Minyæ from Orchomenus with Athamas; Carians mixed with Greeks, then Ionians led by Apœcus and settlers from Attica and Bœotia were received.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Teos was colonized by Minyæ from Orchomenus who came with Athamas, and Carians were mixed with the Greeks there. The Ionians were led to Teos by Apœcus, and later settlers from Attica and Bœotia were hospitably received.
