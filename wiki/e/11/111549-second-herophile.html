second Herophile

person · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: Herophile, vocal maiden

Abstract written by a model

The second Herophile is a Sibyl who lived before the Trojan War and foretold Helen’s upbringing in Sparta, the ruin of Asia Minor and Europe, and the Greek capture of Ilium. In verses spoken while possessed, she called herself Artemis and described herself as Apollo’s wife and sibling. Her origins were variously linked to a mortal sire, Nymphs, Mount Ida, Marpessus, Corycus, and Theodorus; Erythræ strongly claimed her. She lived chiefly at Samos, chanted at Delphi, and was associated with Clarus and Delos. After death in the Troad, she was buried in the grove of Sminthian Apollo, whose sacristan she was said to be.

CHAPTER XII.

The second Herophile was younger than the first but lived before the Trojan War. She foretold that Helen would be raised in Sparta, causing the ruin of Asia Minor and Europe, and that Ilium would be captured by the Greeks; she was also associated with Samos, Clarus, Delos, and Delphi, and was said to have died in the Troad.
