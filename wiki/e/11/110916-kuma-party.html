Kuma Party in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927 · the work’s page · read it where Kuma Party first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kuma Party is a pleasant old Winkie who lives in a cottage near Patch in Winkie Country, plays checkers in the evening, and owns the cottage. Since early childhood, he has had come-apartishness: he can detach and control his hands, arms, feet, head, and other parts, though his hand acts only on his instructions. He is described as the son of a wizard whose father practiced magic in Zamagoochie Country, and also as Wumbo’s son. He warns Peter about traveling with Ruggedo and helps them reach Patch. Later he sends his right arm and hand to aid Peter’s escape and his left arm to Wumbo; a hand carries a warning for Ozma in the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Kuma Party is a pleasant old Winkie who can separate and control his hands, feet, head, arms, and other body parts. He says that his father was a wizard in the Zamagoochie Country and that he has possessed this gift since childhood. He warns Peter about traveling with a gnome and helps Peter and Ruggedo reach Patch.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

Kuma Party learns that a small mortal boy and a gnome came to his cottage. He sends his right arm and hand to help the boy escape from the Kingdom of Patch and sends his left arm to Wumbo for instructions.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Kuma Party is associated with the hand that flies into the banquet hall while carrying a warning for Ozma. The hand searches the palace for Ozma, but Ruggedo hides beneath the table and avoids it.
