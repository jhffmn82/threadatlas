Ægialeus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ægialeus is an Autochthon said by the people of Sicyon to have first dwelt in their region. Ægialus was named after him; he founded Ægialea, whose citadel site later held Apollo’s temple, and fathered Europs.

And as you go from Corinth along the coast in the direction of Sicyon

Ægialeus is said by the people of Sicyon to have been an Autochthon who first dwelt in their region. Ægialus in the Peloponnese was reportedly named after him, and he founded Ægialea in the plain; the site of the temple of Apollo was said to have been its citadel.
