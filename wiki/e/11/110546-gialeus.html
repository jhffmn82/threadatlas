Ægialeus

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Ægialeus the Autochthon

Abstract written by a model

Ægialeus is an Autochthon said by the people of Sicyon to have first dwelt in their region. Ægialus was named after him; he founded Ægialea, whose citadel site later held Apollo’s temple, and fathered Europs.

Ægialeus is in one work on this site, Description of Greece. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
