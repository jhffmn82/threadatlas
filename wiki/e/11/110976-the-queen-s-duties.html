Abstract written by a model

The Queen’s duties are domestic and patchwork-related tasks: she prepares breakfast, cleans and maintains the household, sorts patches and counts cotton spools, readies old clothes for mending, feeds the Scissor Bird, helps pick tomato pin-cushions, and scolds Patch-workers.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

The Queen’s duties include preparing breakfast, making beds, sweeping floors, dusting furniture, scrubbing steps, washing windows, sorting patches, counting cotton spools, preparing old clothes for mending, feeding the Scissor Bird, helping pick tomato pin-cushions, and scolding the Patch-workers.
