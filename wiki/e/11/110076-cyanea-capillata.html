Cyanea capillata

Abstract written by a model

Cyanea capillata is a fearful stinging jellyfish, a tawny-and-silver lion’s-mane-like mass in the deepest bathing-pool, possibly brought by the South-West gale. Its nearly invisible fifty-foot filaments leave reticulated red marks, causing extreme pain, cardiac distress, and possible death, until Holmes and Stackhurst crush it beneath a boulder.

Cyanea capillata is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
