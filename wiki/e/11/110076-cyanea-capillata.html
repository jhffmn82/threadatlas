Cyanea capillata is a jellyfish resembling a tangled mass of tawny and silver lion's mane. It radiates nearly invisible filaments over a wide area, causing red, dotted marks, extreme pain, cardiac distress, and potentially death; Sherlock Holmes and Stackhurst crush it beneath a boulder.
