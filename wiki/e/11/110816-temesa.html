Temesa in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Temesa is an Italian town troubled by a sailor’s ghost, the Hero of Temesa, which killed inhabitants after violating a maiden. It honored him with a grove temple and annual bride until Euthymus defeated him, freeing the still-inhabited town.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Temesa was the Italian town where the ghost of a sailor killed for violating a maiden had long slain its inhabitants. After Euthymus defeated the Hero associated with the ghost, Temesa was freed from the trouble, and the text says that it was still inhabited.
