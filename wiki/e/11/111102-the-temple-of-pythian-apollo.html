Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The temple of Pythian Apollo lies about 15 stades from Pheneus toward Pellene and Aegira, but only its ruins and a large white-stone altar remain. The people of Pheneus continue to sacrifice there to Apollo and Artemis, and tombs of heroes associated with Hercules’ expedition against Elis are nearby.
