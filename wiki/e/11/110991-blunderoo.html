Abstract written by a model

Blunderoo is a battered, old pirate ship, its name displayed in raised gold letters. Wrecked and drifting at sea beside a green sea cavern, it contains pirate treasure in its hold and is said to have sunk in connection with the pirates’ disappearance. Peter identifies it, and he and Ruggedo board it seeking useful things and treasure. The current carries them through its wrecked interior until it reaches Ev, grounds on a shallow beach, and stops. Gnomes unload its treasure, which Ruggedo later says should go to Ozma.

Blunderoo is in one work on this site, The Gnome King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

Blunderoo is identified by Peter as the name of the battered old ship resting beside a green sea cavern. Peter and Ruggedo board it after seeing its pirate markings and hoping to find useful things and treasure.

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

The Blunderoo carries Peter and Ruggedo with the current while they explore its wrecked interior and find the pirate treasure. It eventually drifts toward a shallow beach on the coast of Ev and is likely to go aground.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

The battered old Blunderoo reaches the shore of Ev, grounds its nose in the sand, and comes to a creaky stop. Its treasure is later unloaded by the gnomes.

CHAPTER 15 Wumbo, Wonder Worker, At Home

Ruggedo says he is going to the Emerald City to report the discovery of a treasure ship. He claims that he will give its jewels and gold pieces to Ozma.
