Quiberon's threatened destruction of the Ozure Isles in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Quiberon threatens to destroy the Kingdom and Ozure Isles within three days unless he receives a mortal maiden, while preventing anyone from leaving. The threat terrifies the castle and islands and drives Philador, Cheeriobed, and Akbad to consider aid, the golden pear, the Good Witch of the North, or finding a maiden. With two days left, Philador begins toward the Emerald City; on the final day, Quiberon attacks Queen Orin and the sapphire castle.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Quiberon declares that he will destroy the Kingdom utterly in three days unless a mortal maiden is sent to him. His threat causes the castle and islands to tremble with fear and prompts Philador to consider seeking help.

CHAPTER 2

The threat of destruction comes from Quiberon, who demands a mortal maiden and prevents anyone from leaving the islands. Cheeriobed, Akbad, and Philador respond differently by considering the golden pear, seeking the Good Witch of the North, or trying to obtain a mortal maiden.

CHAPTER 8

Quiberon has threatened to destroy Philador's father's Kingdom, and only two days remain before the threat is to be carried out. This deadline motivates Philador to begin the journey to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 16

Cheeriobed recalls that Quiberon has threatened to destroy the Ozure Isles the following day. The threat becomes immediate when Quiberon attacks Queen Orin and then assaults the sapphire castle.
