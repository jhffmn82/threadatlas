Museum in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Museum, a hill within Athens’ old walls opposite the Acropolis and associated with Musaeus, was fortified by Demetrius and served Macedonians as a refuge until Olympiodorus captured it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

The Museum stood on a hill opposite the Acropolis, within the old city walls, at a place associated with Musaeus. Demetrius son of Antigonus fortified it, and it became a refuge for the Macedonians until Olympiodorus captured it after defeating them.
