Zeus the God of Oaths is the most terrifying statue of Zeus, in the Council Chamber. Holding a thunderbolt in each hand, it receives oaths from athletes, their relatives, trainers, and umpires.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXII.

The statue of Zeus in the Council Chamber is called Zeus the God of Oaths and is described as the most frightening of the statues of Zeus to wicked men. It holds a thunderbolt in each hand, and athletes, their relatives and trainers, and the umpires swear before it.
