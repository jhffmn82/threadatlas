Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XX.

Leucippus, son of Œnomaus, fell in love with Daphne and pursued her despite her dislike of males. He disguised himself as a girl, gained Daphne's friendship, and was later discovered and killed by Daphne and the other maidens.
