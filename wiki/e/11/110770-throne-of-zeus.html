throne of Zeus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The throne is a richly adorned throne at Olympia, made with gold, precious stones, ebony, ivory, painted animals, and worked figures. Four feet, pillars, Victories, Theban boys, and enclosing panels shape it. Its decoration unfolds through painted panels and divisions showing Hercules, Amazons, and other contests, battles, and figures.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The throne is richly adorned with gold, precious stones, ebony, ivory, painted animals, and worked figures. It has four feet, supporting pillars, Victories, scenes of contests and mythic battles, and panels painted by Panænus; the panels include Atlas, Hercules, Theseus and Pirithous, Salamis, Prometheus, Penthesilea, Achilles, and the Hesperides.
