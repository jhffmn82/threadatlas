Balder

deity · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Baldr, Baldur, Baldur, the Good, god of the dead day, gold-haired Balder

Abstract written by a model

Balder, Odin's son and Nanna's husband, becomes protected from nearly everything after Frigga's oaths, except mistletoe. Loki guides Hodur to kill him with it; denied return from Hel, Balder is burned on Hringham with Nanna and his horse.

Balder is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXXIII

Baldur is protected from almost everything after Frigga obtains oaths from living and lifeless things, but mistletoe is exempted. Loki guides Hodur in throwing mistletoe at him, killing him; Baldur remains in Hel because Thaukt refuses to weep.
