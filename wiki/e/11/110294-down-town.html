Abstract written by a model

Down Town is an underground city and place ruled by Dad as King and the Queen of Down Town. It has gates, stores, hotels, streets, a restaurant, bank, shops, offices, and a public square, but no motors or wagons. Reserved for money-making Daddies and excluding women, it frightens its citizens into flight; the travelers eat, are arrested, charged, and seek escape rather than work.

Down Town is in one work on this site, The Hungry Tiger of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Down Town is a city with gates, stores, hotels, streets, and a restaurant, and it resembles a typical town except that it has no motors or wagons. Its citizens flee when the Hungry Tiger and the group arrive, and the group is arrested after eating in a restaurant. The city's King is Dad, and a Queen sits beside him on the bank roof.

CHAPTER 9

Down Town is a place dominated by money-making Daddies, with a bank, shops, offices, and a public square. The travelers are charged for food there and seek a way out rather than remaining to work.
