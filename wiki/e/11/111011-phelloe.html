Phelloe in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Phelloe is a place about 40 stades from Ægira by a hilly mountain road. Unimportant and uninhabited during Ionian occupation, it has a vine-, wildlife-, and water-rich neighbourhood and temples to Dionysus and Artemis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

Phelloe lies about 40 stades from Ægira by a hilly road over the mountains. It was not very important and was uninhabited when the Ionians occupied the land, but its neighborhood was suitable for vines, wildlife, and abundant water. Phelloe had temples to Dionysus and Artemis.
