Woodward household, a large difficult home at Dry Hill, employs Chloe Angeline Stickney as chief assistant and houseworker; for four years she performs extensive domestic labor while attending school.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

The Woodward household was a large, difficult home where Angeline lived for four years after Electa Cook’s second marriage. She performed extensive domestic labor there, including baking, mopping floors, milking cows, and often doing most of the housework while continuing to attend school.
