THE ADVENTURE OF THE RETIRED COLOURMAN

This unit introduces Sherlock Holmes's investigation of Josiah Amberley's report that his wife and Dr. Ray Ernest disappeared after a theft. It follows Dr. John Watson's visit to The Haven and Holmes's growing suspicion that the apparently simple case has a more complicated aspect.

The case concerned the disappearance of Amberley's wife and Dr. Ray Ernest, along with securities that Amberley said had been taken. It culminated in Amberley's arrest and the discovery of evidence that the disappearances were murders rather than an elopement.
