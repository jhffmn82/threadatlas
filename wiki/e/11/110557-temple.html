temple in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A burnt-down temple near Corinth, on the coastal road toward Sicyon. Its identity and destruction are disputed: one account calls it Apollo’s temple, destroyed by Pyrrhus, son of Achilles; another says Corinthians dedicated it to Olympian Zeus and that accidental fire destroyed it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And as you go from Corinth along the coast in the direction of Sicyon

The temple stands not far from Corinth on the left side of the road toward Sicyon and has been burnt down. One account identifies it as a temple of Apollo destroyed by Pyrrhus, the son of Achilles; another says that the Corinthians dedicated it to Olympian Zeus and that an accidental fire destroyed it.
