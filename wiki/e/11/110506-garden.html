Angelo Hall · 1908

The Gay Street property included cherry, crab-apple, damson plum, peach, walnut, box, evergreen, and other trees, along with flower beds, a hot-bed, vines, and many cultivated plants. Angeline Hall also brought wild flowers from the woods near Georgetown and helped them take root in the yard.
