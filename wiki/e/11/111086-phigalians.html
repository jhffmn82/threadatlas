Phigalians in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Phigalians were defeated and expelled from Phigalia by the Lacedæmonians. After consulting Delphi, they obtained 100 picked men from Oresthasium, who drove out the Spartan garrison and died, enabling the Phigalians to recover their town. They commemorate these men with a mortuary chapel in the market-place and annual hero sacrifices. They annually sacrifice to Eurynome, whom they identify as Artemis. They believe Demeter bore Despoena after an amour with Poseidon, hold the cave of Black Demeter sacred, and, after neglecting Demeter when her wooden statue burned, followed an oracle by commissioning Onatas of Ægina to make a brazen statue and renewing her worship.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIX.

The Phigalians were defeated and expelled from Phigalia by the Lacedæmonians. Those who escaped consulted Delphi and followed the oracle by obtaining 100 picked men from Oresthasium; after the men drove out the Spartan garrison and died, the Phigalians recovered their town.

CHAPTER XLI.

The Phigalians have a mortuary chapel in their market-place for 100 picked men from Oresthasium and annually offer them funeral sacrifices as heroes. They identify Eurynome as a title of Artemis and annually open her temple for public and private sacrifices.

CHAPTER XLII.

The people of Phigalia believed that Demeter gave birth to Despoena after her amour with Poseidon. They placed a wooden statue of Demeter in the cave, later neglected her rites after it was destroyed, and then obtained a brazen statue from Onatas and renewed their worship.
