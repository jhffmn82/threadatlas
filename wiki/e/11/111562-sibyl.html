Sibyl in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Sibyl, the first Herophile, is an extremely ancient woman and first chanter of oracles. Said by Greeks to be daughter of Zeus and Lamia and called Sibyl by Libyans, she foretells Macedonian power’s rise and fall under the first and last Philip.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

The first Herophile, also called the Sibyl, was regarded as extremely ancient and as the first woman to chant oracles. The Greeks said she was the daughter of Zeus and Lamia, the daughter of Poseidon, while the Libyans called her Sibyl.
