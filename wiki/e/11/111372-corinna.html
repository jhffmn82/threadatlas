Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER XXII.

Corinna is described as the only poetess of Tanagra. She has a tomb in a conspicuous place in the town and a painting in the gymnasium, showing her with a fillet because of her victory over Pindar at Thebes; the text attributes her victory partly to her Aeolic dialect and her beauty.
