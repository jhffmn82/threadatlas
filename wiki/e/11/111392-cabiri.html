CHAPTER XXV.

The Cabiri were said to have been the inhabitants of a former town at the site of the Cabirian sanctuary. The Argives drove them out after the Epigoni captured Thebes, but Telondes and the other Cabiri who had left Cabiræa later returned when the mysteries were reestablished.
