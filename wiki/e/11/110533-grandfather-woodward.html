------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Milton Woodward was a strong-willed widower with five sons and five daughters. He married Electa Cook, opposed her bringing her unmarried daughters to his house, was an ardent abolitionist, and sometimes abused Electa in a violent temper.
