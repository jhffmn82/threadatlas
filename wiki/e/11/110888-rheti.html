Rheti in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Rheti are river-like seawater channels, possibly flowing underground from the Euripus near Chalcis; sacred to Proserpine and Demeter, priest-only fishing waters, they once marked the Eleusinian-Athenian boundary.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

The Rheti flow like rivers but contain seawater, and they were thought possibly to run underground from the Euripus near Chalcis. They were sacred to Proserpine and Demeter, and only their priests could catch fish in them; in earlier times they marked the boundary between the Eleusinians and Athenians.
