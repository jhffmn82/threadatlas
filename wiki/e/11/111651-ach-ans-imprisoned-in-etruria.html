Achæans imprisoned in Etruria

Abstract written by a model

More than 1,000 Achæans, held in various towns in Etruria and treated as condemned, were ignored despite repeated embassies and supplications; seventeen years later Romans released about 300 or fewer surviving prisoners as sufficiently punished.

CHAPTER X.

The Achæans imprisoned in Etruria were treated by the Romans as though they had already been condemned. The Romans ignored repeated Achæan embassies and supplications, then released about 300 or fewer of them 17 years later, considering that they had been punished sufficiently.
