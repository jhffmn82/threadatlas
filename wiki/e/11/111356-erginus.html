Erginus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Erginus, eldest son of Clymenus and grandson of Presbon, ruled Orchomenus after Clymenus. He led the Minyæ against Thebes and secured a yearly tax, but made peace with Hercules when the citizens were reduced to extremities. Unmarried and childless until old age, he consulted Delphi, married a young wife, and fathered Trophonius and Agamedes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVII.

Erginus, the eldest son of Clymenus, led his brothers in war against Thebes and secured a yearly tax from the Thebans. After making peace with Hercules, he remained unmarried and childless until old age, then married a young wife according to an oracle and became the father of Trophonius and Agamedes.
