CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

The pirate ship Blunderoo has water-soaked, rotten planks and is covered with rust, decay, sea moss, and barnacles. After the sea quake throws it into the air, it strikes the water, rocks violently, and rights itself, but its rudder, masts, and sails are gone.
