temple of Hera in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Scillus built this large Doric temple for Hera about eight years after Oxylus became king at Elis; 63 feet long, it contains statues and Cypselus's chest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Hera’s temple was said to have been built by the people of Scillus in Triphylia about eight years after Oxylus became king at Elis. It is Doric, measures 63 feet in length, and contains statues and the chest of Cypselus.
