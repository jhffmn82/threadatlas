Ser in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ser is an insect called Ser by the Greeks, about twice the largest beetle, spider-like and eight-footed. It lives in domiciles through summer and winter, spins slender thread around its feet, eats grain four years then green reed in its fifth, and dies after five years.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

The Greeks call the insect that produces the Seres' thread the Ser, while the Seres use another name. It is described as about twice the size of the largest beetle, resembling a web-weaving spider, and having eight feet.
