Hall boys in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Under Angeline Hall’s leadership, the Hall boys do household and yard work, enjoy swimming and skating, and all four later graduate with distinction from Harvard College.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

The Hall boys performed nearly every kind of household and yard work, including sweeping, dusting, making beds, washing dishes, gardening, chopping wood, running errands, and mending fences. They also had time for swimming, skating, and other diversions, and all four later graduated with distinction from Harvard College.
