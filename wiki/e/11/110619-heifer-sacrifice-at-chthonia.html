Heifer sacrifice is performed by priestesses of Demeter: a full-grown heifer is driven into the temple and killed with sickles, then three more are killed; completion requires all four fall on the first heifer’s side.

CHAPTER XXXV.

A full-grown heifer is driven into the temple, where four old women kill it with sickles; three more heifers are brought in and killed in the same way. The sacrifices are considered complete only when all four heifers fall on the same side as the first.
