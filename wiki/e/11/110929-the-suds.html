CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

The Suds are soap villagers who skate through their village and bring Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy to Shampoozle. They are made of soap, wear turbans and robes of Turkish toweling, and come in pink, green, white, and violet.
