Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLIII.

The Mauri war with Rome began when the Mauri commenced hostilities against Rome. The elder Antonine responded by driving them from their territory and compelling them to withdraw toward Mount Atlas and the area around it.
