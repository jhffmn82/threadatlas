CHAPTER XIV.

After hearing the Roman decision, the Achæans attacked all Lacedæmonians residing at Corinth, identifying some by their hair, boots, dress, or names. They plundered suspected Lacedæmonians, dragged some from Orestes’ headquarters, and later imprisoned those they had arrested.
