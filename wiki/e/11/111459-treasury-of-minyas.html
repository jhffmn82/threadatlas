Treasury of Minyas

Also called: Treasury for the reception of his money, treasury, treasury of Minyas

Abstract written by a model

The Treasury of Minyas is a remarkable circular stone treasury at Orchomenus, built to receive Minyas’s money. It has a not very pointed top, whose highest stone is said to hold the whole building together. Though Greek historians omitted it, it is called a marvel inferior to nothing in Greece or elsewhere.

Treasury of Minyas is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXVI.

The Treasury of Minyas was built to receive Minyas’s money. It is described as a remarkable structure that Greek historians failed to mention despite describing the Pyramids of Egypt.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

The treasury of Minyas at Orchomenus is described as a circular stone building with a not very pointed top. Its highest stone is said to hold the whole structure together, and it is called a marvel inferior to nothing in Greece or elsewhere.
