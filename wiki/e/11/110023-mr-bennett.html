Mr. Bennett

Abstract written by a model

Mr. Bennett is Professor Presbury’s trusted professional assistant and secretary, living in the Professor’s household and engaged to Edith Presbury. He handles Presbury’s papers and correspondence, including correspondence with Dorak, and has a medical degree. As Presbury grows sinister despite seeming mentally clear, Bennett and Edith become alarmed. Bennett reports the secret correspondence, the Professor’s anger over a wooden box, Roy’s attacks, and Presbury crawling through a passage at night to Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson. He supplies Dorak’s address, helps observe and follow Presbury, rescues him after Roy’s attack, dresses his wound, and provides the box key.

Mr. Bennett is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

Trevor Bennett is Professor Presbury's professional assistant, secretary, live-in assistant, and Edith Presbury's fiancé. He reports Presbury's secret correspondence, his anger over the wooden box, Roy's attacks, and seeing Presbury crawl through the passage at night.

Monday morning found us on our way to the famous University town--an

Mr. Bennett confirms that he handles Professor Presbury's correspondence and has no record of a note sent to Holmes. He apologizes for Presbury's conduct, says that Presbury is becoming more sinister while remaining mentally clear, gives Holmes Dorak's address from blotting-paper, and warns that Presbury's daughter and he are alarmed.

In the morning Mr. Bennett slipped round to bring us the latest report.

Mr. Bennett reports on Professor Presbury's behavior and helps Holmes and Dr. Watson keep the Professor under observation. He follows Presbury from the house, helps rescue him after Roy's attack, dresses his wound, and provides the key to the Professor's box.
