Agapenor in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Agapenor, son of Ancæus and grandson of Lycurgus, succeeded Echemus, led the Arcadians to Ilium, then after its capture was storm-carried to Cyprus, where he founded Paphos, built Aphrodite’s temple, and dwelt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Agapenor, son of Ancæus and grandson of Lycurgus, succeeded Echemus and led the Arcadians to Ilium. After Ilium was captured, a storm carried him and the Arcadian fleet to Cyprus, where he founded Paphos and erected a temple of Aphrodite.
