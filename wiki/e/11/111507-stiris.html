Stiris

place · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Stiris is a town on high, rocky ground, said to have been founded by Athenians who came from Attica with Peteus after his expulsion from Athens, and named after Stiria. Its few wells give poor water, so it uses a rock-hewn spring below. It has a temple and ancient statue of Demeter and begins the road to Ambrosus.

Stiris is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXV.

Stiris was said by its inhabitants to have been founded by Athenians who came with Peteus, son of Orneus, after his expulsion from Athens by Ægeus. It stood on high, rocky ground with poor water supplies, and contained a temple of Demeter Stiritis and another ancient statue dedicated to Demeter.

CHAPTER XXXVI.

Stiris is the starting point of the road to Ambrosus, which is about 60 stades away.
