1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Cypselus was king of Arcadia when the Dorians returned to the Peloponnese by sea. He gave his daughter in marriage to Cresphontes, thereby gaining Cresphontes' support and securing the Arcadians against danger.
