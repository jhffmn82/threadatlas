Abstract written by a model

Tricrena is a group of mountains near Geronteum in Pheneus, containing three wells associated with Hermes’s washing after his birth.

Tricrena is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
