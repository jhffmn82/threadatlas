CHAPTER VIII.

The temple of Athene Pronoia was the fourth of four temples encountered on entering Delphi. Its ante-chapel contained a large brass statue offered by the Massaliotes, while the golden shield offered by Crœsus had been removed, according to the Delphians, by Philomelus.
