Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Myus was colonized by Cyaretus, the son of Codrus, and the Ionians drove the Carians from it. Its inhabitants later abandoned the town for Miletus after the nearby bay became a mosquito-filled marsh, carrying away what they could and the statues of the gods; in the narrator's time, only a white-marble temple of Dionysus remained.
