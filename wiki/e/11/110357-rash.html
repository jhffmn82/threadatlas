Rash in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Its city, towers, palace, cottages, stores, trees, walks, clothing, and landscape are pink or peppery-pink. Rash has a court, army, guardsmen, prison and prison courtyard, a cavern, a palace, and laws that permit criminals to be thrown to a tiger. His fearful guards enforce punishments, while prisoners, including Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, the Rash barber, the Rash singer, Prince Evered, and the Hungry Tiger, are confined and then escape. Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Rash is described as a small pink Kingdom in the southwestern country of Ev, across the Deadly Desert from the Fairyland of Oz. Its people are hasty and hot-tempered but also warm-hearted and generous, while the country suffers constant unrest because Irasha seized the throne and rules unjustly.

CHAPTER 2

Rash is a small kingdom in the southwestern corner of Ev and has a court ruled by Irasha the Rough. Ippty seeks to bring the Hungry Tiger there to serve by eating criminals held in the kingdom.

CHAPTER 3

Rash is a pink-colored country whose palace, cottages, stores, trees, walks, and clothing are described in shades of pink. Its ruler, Irashi, expects criminals to be eaten and keeps a prison resembling a wing of the palace.

CHAPTER 5

Rash is a pink country with flowering trees, a pink city, and a palace ruled by Pasha Irashi. Its laws allow prisoners to be thrown to a tiger, and its Guardsmen fear being punished if they disobey the Pasha.

CHAPTER 6

Rash is ruled by Irashi after he seizes the throne from the rightful royal line. Its prisoners are confined in a courtyard, while the kingdom's three protective rubies have been scattered.

CHAPTER 7

Rash is the place from which Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, the Rash barber, the Rash singer, Prince Evered, and the Hungry Tiger escape. Betsy declares that any place is better than Rash after their difficult confinement there.

CHAPTER 8

Rash is the realm of which Prince Evered is the rightful ruler. Irashi is said to have stolen Rash's rubies and made himself Pasha, while Evered hopes to regain the throne.

CHAPTER 10

Rash is the kingdom associated with Prince Evered, who is called the Prince of Rash. Betsy tells Kaliko that Evered was deprived of his throne and robbed of the three magical Rash rubies.

CHAPTER 18

Rash is the kingdom whose throne Irashi stole. Reddy is identified as its rightful ruler, and the discovery of all three rubies gives him protection and a chance to conquer Irashi.

CHAPTER 19

Rash is invaded by Reddy and his companions after its guards and citizens flee from the Hungry Tiger and the apparent giant. The city celebrates when Irashi's rule ends and Reddy is restored as its rightful ruler.

CHAPTER 20

Rash is left peaceful under Reddy's rule after Ippty and Irashi are removed. Its pink palace displays Atmos Fere's iron boots, and its people have the Rash hurry-canes and rubies as notable magical possessions.
