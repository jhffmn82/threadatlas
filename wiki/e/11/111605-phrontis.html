CHAPTER XXV.

Phrontis, the son of Onetor, is depicted as the pilot of Menelaus’s ship, standing in its middle with two punting poles. Homer describes him as highly skilled, and says that he died while sailing past Sunium in Attica; Menelaus stayed behind to perform his funeral rites.
