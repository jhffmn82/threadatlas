the wishing pills in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Wishing pills are pills that instantly and imperceptibly transport people: the Wizard uses two to move a hand-holding group to the Sapphire City, and they later carry the royal party over hills and valleys to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 17

The Wizard swallows two wishing pills and uses them to transport the entire hand-holding group instantly to the Sapphire City. The transport occurs without any noticeable motion or sensation.
