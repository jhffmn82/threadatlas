Abstract written by a model

Votive offerings at Delphi, sent to Apollo by Lydian kings, include the surviving iron base of Alyattes’ bowl and other dedications commemorating myths, victories, prosperity, and warfare.

CHAPTER XVI.

Votive offerings at Delphi include the surviving iron base of the bowl of Alyattes, statues, a brazen goat, a brazen ox, and representations of generals and gods. They were dedicated by various peoples and individuals to commemorate myths, victories, prosperity, or successful warfare.
