An immense, splendid carrousel stands at the exact center of the round house, with wooden zebra, tiger, and elephant riders and a round table in its hollow center. Its motion is scarcely noticeable until the ring leader’s bell stops it so Round-abouties can change riders.

CHAPTER 13

The merry-go-round stands in the exact center of the round house and is large enough that its motion becomes less noticeable. Trot rides a wooden zebra, the Scarecrow rides a wooden tiger, and Benny stands beside them after failing to mount a wooden elephant. It stops when the ring leader rings his bell, allowing the Round-abouties to change riders.
