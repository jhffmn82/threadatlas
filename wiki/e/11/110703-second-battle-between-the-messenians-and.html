CHAPTER VI.

The second battle is fought after both Lacedæmonian kings lead an army against the Messenians. Euphaes’s division routs Theopompus’s troops, Pytharatus is killed on the Messenian side, and Euphaes is mortally wounded before the battle ends at nightfall.
