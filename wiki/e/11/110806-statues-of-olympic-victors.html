Abstract written by a model

Statues at Olympia commemorate selected Olympic victors and related figures, including athletes, horses, kings, soldiers, and political figures. They are not a complete catalogue: some victors lacked statues, while greater renown or artistic quality could merit one. The records identify subjects, achievements, origins, patrons, artists, inscriptions, and unusual events.

BOOK VI.--ELIS. PART II.

Not every Olympic victor had a statue, and Pausanias says that some received statues because of greater renown or superior artistic quality rather than simply because they won. The statues record athletic achievements, origins, patrons, artists, inscriptions, and sometimes unusual events surrounding the victories.

CHAPTER XIII.

The unit describes many statues at Olympia, identifying their subjects, dedicators, and sculptors when known. The statues commemorate athletes, rulers, soldiers, horses, and political figures, and some inscriptions preserve athletic records or other information about the people represented.
