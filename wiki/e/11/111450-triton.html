Triton in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A small torrent near Alalcomenae, named for Athene’s upbringing beside a river called Triton, is distinguished from Libya’s Triton flowing from Lake Tritonis into the Libyan sea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIII.

The small torrent near Alalcomenae was called Triton because Athene was said to have been brought up near a river of that name. The author distinguishes this local stream from the Triton in Libya, which flows from Lake Tritonis into the Libyan sea.
