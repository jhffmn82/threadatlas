Kuma Party's assistance in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Kuma Party is a helping hand available whenever needed. He aids Peter and Ruggedo’s journey to Patch with shelter, supper, directions, an umbrella, lantern, and a detached guiding arm. Later, his summoned hand enables their escape from Patch by unlocking the castle, defending them, clearing the way, and carrying Peter and Grumpy to safety.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Kuma Party offers Peter and Ruggedo shelter, supper, directions, an umbrella, and a lantern. His detached arm protects them from the rain and guides them toward Patch, while his hand later pats Peter approvingly, gives him a small note, and warns Ruggedo before departing.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Kuma's assistance comes through the summoned hand, which unlocks the castle, protects the fugitives with a club, clears their path, and carries Peter and Grumpy when they tire. After bringing them safely beyond Patch, the hand departs without leaving another note.
