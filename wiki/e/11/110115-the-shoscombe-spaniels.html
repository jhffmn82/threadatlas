THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

The Shoscombe spaniels are described as an exclusive and famous breed, especially prized by Lady Beatrice. Sir Robert gives away her favorite spaniel after becoming angry with it, and the dog is later kept at the Green Dragon.
