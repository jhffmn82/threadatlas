CHAPTER 13

The round house has a sawdust-covered floor, a revolving door, several moving circles, and a merry-go-round at its center. A round tower rises from its floor to its ceiling, with a spiral stairway leading to a trap door on the roof. Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny escape by sliding from the roof across a forest.
