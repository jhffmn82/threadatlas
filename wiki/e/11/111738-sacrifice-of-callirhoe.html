CHAPTER XXI.

The sacrifice of Callirhoe was prescribed by the oracle at Dodona as a means of ending Dionysus's wrath and the Calydonians' affliction. After the required preliminary rites, Callirhoe was led to the altar, but Coresus killed himself in her place.
