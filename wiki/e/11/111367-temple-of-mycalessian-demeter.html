Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The temple of Mycalessian Demeter near the sea is said to be shut and opened every night by Hercules, who is identified as one of the Idæan Dactyli. Fruits of Autumn placed before the statue inside it remain fresh all year.
