Abstract written by a model

Thersander, Polynices' son, leads Epigoni to take Thebes, then Telephus kills him sailing toward Troy; his tomb is at Elaea.

Thersander is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
