Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

Cynætha is inhabited by people of Arcadian race and lies about 40 stades from the temple of Artemis. Its market-place has altars of the gods and a statue of the Emperor Adrian, and its most memorable feature is a temple of Dionysus. A nearby well called Alyssus is said to cure people who drink its water after being bitten by a mad dog or receiving another injury.
