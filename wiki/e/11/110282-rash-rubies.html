Rash rubies in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The Rash rubies are three precious, square magical gems belonging to Rash and to Reddy, Prince Evered, as part of his royal inheritance. Each bears a cut letter R. Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

The three magic rubies of Rash protect the rightful rulers from danger in water, injury in the air, and harm on or under the earth. Irashi scatters them by throwing one from the palace turret, one into the Rash River, and burying one in the garden.

CHAPTER 8

The Rash rubies are precious magical gems stolen by Irashi. Each is square and has an "R" cut into it, and different rubies are said to protect Prince Evered from danger on earth, under the earth, in the air, or in water.

CHAPTER 10

The three Rash rubies possess magical powers and were stolen from Prince Evered when he was deprived of his throne. One of the lost rubies is in Carter Green's possession, and Kaliko and Guph show sudden interest when Betsy explains this.

CHAPTER 12

The Rash rubies are believed to protect the travelers from danger, including the fall of about a quarter of a mile after the Giants seize the Hungry Tiger. Prince Evered and the others rely on them for courage while following the Giants.

CHAPTER 13

The Rash rubies remain with Reddy after the pigeon carries him away. Carter believes they will protect Reddy if the Giants catch him and help Betsy and the others escape.

CHAPTER 16

The Rash rubies protect Reddy from danger by water and allow him to float safely downward. Reddy uses them to escape the water pitcher and reach the floor, and he plans to rely on them during his escape with the Hungry Tiger.

CHAPTER 17

Reddy plans to use the Rash rubies to protect himself and the Hungry Tiger while escaping down the Giant Castle's stairs. After the reunion, Carter Green notes that they have two of the lost rubies as well as the magic wig.

CHAPTER 18

The three lost rubies belonged to Reddy's royal inheritance and were scattered by Irashi. By the end of the unit, all three have been found: one by Carter Green, one by a fisherman, and one by Atmos.

CHAPTER 19

The Rash rubies protect Reddy when Irashi's scimiter strikes him and when Ippty's coffee pot is thrown at his head. Reddy displays the three rubies to Fizzenpop, who recognizes their importance with great delight and astonishment.

CHAPTER 20

The Rash rubies are discussed as possible protection from the burning sands, though it is uncertain whether they would work on the desert. Reddy keeps the rubies when he travels to the Emerald City, while the giant wig is given to Fizzenpop.
