Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVI.

The custom of carrying off votive offerings and statues from conquered nations was described as long-established and practiced by both Greeks and barbarians. The text gives examples involving the Greeks after the capture of Ilium, Antiphemus, Xerxes, Seleucus, the Argives at Tiryns, and the people of Cyzicus and Proconnesus.
