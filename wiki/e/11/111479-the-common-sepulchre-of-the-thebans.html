Abstract written by a model

The common sepulchre at Chæronea held Thebans killed against Philip; uninscribed, it bore a lion monument, perhaps signifying their bravery.

CHAPTER XL.

The common sepulchre of the Thebans who fell in the battle against Philip stood as Chæronea was approached. It had no inscription but displayed a lion, which may have indicated their bravery.
