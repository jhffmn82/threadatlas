Lusi, once a town on the borders of Clitor, was where Melampus brought and cured the daughters of Prœtus, with its temple of Artemis linked to the cure; by the described present, no ruins remained.

CHAPTER XVIII.

Lusi was the place to which Melampus brought and cured the daughters of Prœtus. It was formerly a town on the borders of Clitor, but in the present time described in the text no ruins of it remain; its temple of Artemis was associated with the cure.
