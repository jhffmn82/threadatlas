Abstract written by a model

In The Gnome King of Oz, Peter is a nine-year-old Philadelphia baseball pitcher and captain who escapes Ruggedo’s dangers, warns Ozma, helps save the Emerald City, declines a princely title, and returns home with pirate gold. In Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz, Peter is a Philadelphia baseball pitcher and captain who returns to Oz, helps Jack and his companions oppose Mogodore and rescue Shirley Sunshine, and is celebrated as a rescuer before returning home.

Peter in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927 · the work’s page · read it where Peter first appears

Abstract written by a model

Peter is a nine-year-old boy from Philadelphia, a Fifth-B student, pitcher, and captain of the A. P. Baseball Team. After buying a green balloon, he is carried away by a balloon bird, escapes by falling into the Nonestic Ocean, and meets Ruggedo. Although Ruggedo repeatedly endangers or uses him, Peter travels with him through the exposed sea bottom, onto the pirate ship Blunderoo, and into Ev and Oz. On the Blunderoo Peter finds pirate gold, the Pirate Chief’s diary, and the magic casket of Soob, keeping emeralds and other objects while Ruggedo takes the Flying Cloak of Invisibility. Peter’s central aim becomes reaching the Emerald City to warn Ozma of Ruggedo’s danger and eventually return home in time for baseball. In Patch, Ruggedo has Peter offered as a slave, but Peter joins Scraps and Grumpy, uses Kuma’s note and Kuma Party’s helping hand to escape, and travels through the Suds, the Bewilderness, Tune Town, and toward the Emerald City while carrying Ozwold’s egg. Lost and threatened at times, he receives help but also acts courageously, including protecting Ozma with the egg when Ruggedo attacks. When Ruggedo becomes invisible and steals the magic belt, Peter throws dishes and other objects until the Silence Stone strikes Ruggedo, stopping his command and saving the Emerald City. Celebrated as a hero and offered the title Prince Peter the First, Peter declines out of loyalty to his baseball teammates and grandfather. He returns to Philadelphia at his own request with two bags of pirate gold.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

15 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

Peter buys a green balloon in Philadelphia and is carried high into the sky when it transforms into a balloon bird. He learns that he is supposedly being taken to Balloon Island as an airrend boy for Queen Luna, but he lets go of the bird, falls into the ocean, swims to a small island, and meets Ruggedo. Peter decides to try to catch fish and later discusses building a boat or raft to escape.

CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

Peter sees the sea bottom exposed when the sea turns upside down and follows Ruggedo across it. He helps discover and board the pirate ship Blunderoo, cooks three small fish for dinner, and later falls asleep while drifting with Ruggedo.

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

Peter discovers the pirate treasure aboard the Blunderoo and imagines using the gold to build a clubhouse, buy vehicles and animals, and help his grandfather. He finds the pirate chief's diary and the magic casket of Soob, but Ruggedo takes the Flying Cloak of Invisibility from him. Peter realizes that the cloak is torn and plans to use it to warn Ozma if he reaches Ev and the cloak is repaired.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

Peter helps Ruggedo swim from the Blunderoo to the shores of Ev and follows him into the Kingdom of Gnomes. He plans to take the repaired magic cloak and fly to the Emerald City, but instead uses the magic bulbs with Ruggedo to cross the Deadly Desert. He arrives in Oz, enjoys the peach orchard, and decides to tell the Wizard of Oz about the elevator plants.

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Peter meets Kuma Party and is astonished and frightened by his separate-moving body parts. He wants to stay and ask Kuma more questions, but follows Ruggedo toward Patch; along the way he objects when Ruggedo suggests stealing Kuma’s hand and is excited by the hand’s assistance.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Peter tries to conceal the truth about himself and Ruggedo because he needs the magic cloak repaired so he can warn Ozma. Ruggedo causes him to be offered as a slave, but Scraps assures him that she will help. Peter unsuccessfully tries to seize the cloak before Ruggedo disappears, then tells Scraps and Grumpy how he came to Oz and resolves to escape with Scraps.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Peter reads Kuma's note and realizes that Kuma will lend them a hand. He helps prepare breakfast, discovers the Sandman's Nap Sack with Scraps, summons Kuma's hand, and escapes Patch with Scraps and Grumpy.

CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

Peter travels with Scraps and Grumpy toward the Emerald City, hoping Ozma can send him back to Philadelphia in time to pitch in his game. He is captured by the Suds, escapes from Shampoozle's palace by jumping from a window, and discovers that soap floats, allowing the group to cross the lake on a soap raft.

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Peter crosses the sudsy lake with Scraps and Grumpy, then becomes lost with them in the Bewilderness. He helps find Ozwold, carries Ozwold's egg during the journey, and considers threatening Ruggedo with it. He reaches the Emerald City with the others after Scraps opens a passage through the yellow wall.

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Peter enters Tune Town with Scraps, Grumpy, and Ozwold while carrying the oztrich egg. He struggles with the law against speaking, becomes hungry at the Viol Inn, and helps produce the discord that gets the travelers out of Tune Town.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Peter travels with Grumpy, Scraps, and Ozwold toward the Emerald City. He eats an umbrella melon, carries Ozwold’s egg child, helps the bookman after Ozwold runs him over, and continues onward when Scraps finds the yellow brick road.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

The small mortal boy is lost in the Kingdom of Patch and is threatened by a gnome who evidently means mischief. He sends for help from Kuma Party and receives the aid of Kuma Party's right arm and hand.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

Peter protects Ozma by forcing the oztrich egg into her arms when Ruggedo attacks. He helps expose the danger, watches for the invisible gnome, tells Sir Hokus about his adventures, and tries to reach the magic belt after Ruggedo takes it.

CHAPTER 19 The Wizard Makes The Gnome King Visible

Peter throws dishes and other objects at the invisible Ruggedo, finally striking him with the Silence Stone. His actions stop Ruggedo's command and save the realm, earning him praise from the Ozites.

CHAPTER 20 Peter Is Made A Prince Of Oz

Peter is celebrated as a hero throughout the Emerald City and is offered the chance to become Prince Peter the First. He declines because he is a pitcher and feels loyal to his teammates and grandfather, then returns to Philadelphia with two bags of the pirate’s gold.

Peter in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929 · the work’s page · read it where Peter first appears

Abstract written by a model

Peter is a little boy from Philadelphia, a baseball pitcher and captain who lives with his grandfather. Though he prefers baseball to Oz, he is an experienced Oz adventurer: on an earlier visit he met Scarecrow and the Wonderful Wizard, discovered a pirate ship and its treasure, opposed the Gnome King, and saved the Emerald City. After Ozma returned him and the pirate sacks to Philadelphia, Peter and his grandfather spent the pirate gold, while Peter kept a magic pirate sack. When rain cancels baseball practice, Peter finds a magic coin in a pirate sack, wishes to return to Oz, and arrives in a pumpkin field. He joins Jack Pumpkinhead on the way to the Emerald City. He uses a candle end to help them escape Chimneyville, survives encounters including a Christmas Tree and Scare City, and uses the magic dinner bell and pirate sack to defeat the Scares. In escaping Scare City, he frees Snif and gains him as a companion. Learning of Mogodore's threat and Shirley Sunshine's captivity, Peter promises to help Belfaygor. He devises a nighttime plan to enter Baffleburg, leads his companions through dangerous crossings, and deliberately enters the castle to reach the Princess, but is captured and confined in the North tower. After Snif frees him, Peter helps recover Jack, the pirate sack, and the Forbidden Flagon. He continues toward the Emerald City with Jack, Snif, and Belfaygor, though the pirate sack ultimately traps Peter and Belfaygor while attempting to capture Mogodore's army. Peter later emerges, identifies the Scares, and is celebrated with his companions as one of the rescuers of the Kingdom of Oz. He then chooses to return to Philadelphia, where he tells his grandfather about his journey.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

16 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Peter becomes frustrated when rain cancels baseball practice, then finds a small gold piece in one of the pirate's sacks. After wishing to return to Oz, he is transported to a pumpkin field, meets Jack Pumpkinhead, and follows him toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 2

Peter falls with Jack Pumpkinhead into Chimneyville and is surrounded and nearly suffocated by the Smokies. He lights the candle end inside Jack's head, uses the lantern light to escape, and then continues toward the Emerald City. At the Goody Shop, he receives bird seed labeled a good breakfast and jokes about Jack's advice.

CHAPTER 3

Peter travels with Jack Pumpkinhead and encounters an aggressive Christmas Tree. He escapes the tree, eats the dinner summoned by the Red Djinn's dinner bell, and proceeds with Jack to Scare City.

CHAPTER 4

Peter is insulted by the Chief Scarer and responds by scowling and sticking out his tongue. He tries not to run or scream, throws food and dishes from the magic dinner bell at the Scares, and uses the pirate sack when the food runs out. After the sack swallows the Scares, Peter ties it shut and searches unsuccessfully for a way out of Scare City.

CHAPTER 5

Peter meets Snif, learns that he is an Iffin, and frees him from his chain with a gold key. He and Jack ride on Snif's back toward the Emerald City, and Peter is pleased that their escape from Scare City led to their meeting with Snif.

CHAPTER 6

Peter rests and eats wild strawberries while traveling with Jack Pumpkinhead and Snif. He praises Snif's abilities, learns about Belfaygor's troubles, and asks the baron to explain who Mogodore the Mighty is and what happened to Shirley Sunshine.

CHAPTER 7

Peter promises to help Belfaygor rescue Shirley Sunshine and proposes flying into Baffleburg at night, dropping into Mogodore's castle courtyard, and using the pirate's sack against Mogodore and his fighting men. After Snif shrinks, Peter tries to comfort him.

CHAPTER 8

Peter reassures Snif that the Wizard of Oz can make him large again and helps plan how to reach the Emerald City. He agrees to Snif's beard-crossing plan and spends the night asleep in the cave with Snif curled in his arm.

CHAPTER 10

Peter encourages Snif by reminding him that he can think and is free. He crosses the chasm and helps Jack Pumpkinhead survive the dangerous crossing, then leads the group through the tilting tunnel and into the courtyard outside Baffleburg. After climbing Belfaygor's beard into a tower, Peter eagerly begins descending its spiral stairway.

CHAPTER 11

Peter enters Baffleburg with Snif, Belfaygor, and Jack Pumpkinhead, intending to get captured so they can reach Princess Shirley Sunshine. He tries to use the pirate's sack, but a spearman seizes him before he can open it; he is imprisoned in the North tower after dropping the sack. Peter worries that Mogodore will conquer Oz and wishes he could escape to warn Ozma.

CHAPTER 12

Peter escapes the dungeon when Snif expands to his former size and tears away the bars. He frees Belfaygor, helps recover Jack's head and the pirate's sack, and reluctantly agrees to search the lost labyrinth for the forbidden flagon before going to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 13

Peter follows Belfaygor through the labyrinth and helps discover that the apparent Ugly Muglies are reflections in magic mirrors. He retrieves the Forbidden Flagon by using a piece of Belfaygor's beard to knock it from the fire fountain, then carries it back to Mogodore's palace. He reunites Jack's Pumpkinhead with his body and prepares to travel toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 14

Peter travels with Belfaygor, Snif, and Jack Pumpkinhead toward the Emerald City. In Swing City, the Swingers toss him between swings and trapezes until Mogodore's sauce box falls from his pocket and causes them to drop him into a net. He helps Jack recover and continues the pursuit of Mogodore with the pirate's sack, flagon, and magic bell.

CHAPTER 15

Peter travels with Jack Pumpkinhead, Belfaygor, and Snif to intercept Mogodore. He unties the pirate's sack and tries to hold it over Mogodore's army, but a gust of wind carries it away before it captures the enemy. The falling sack captures Peter and Belfaygor.

CHAPTER 20

Peter emerges from the pirate sack with Snif and Belfaygor and identifies the intruders as Scares. He helps the Oz folk understand what has happened and is celebrated as one of the four rescuers who saved the Kingdom of Oz.

CHAPTER 21

Peter says goodbye to his friends in Oz and asks Ozma to send him back to Philadelphia because his companions and grandfather depend on him. After returning home, he excitedly tells his grandfather about flying and all that happened in Baffleburg and elsewhere in Oz.
