Abstract written by a model

Iron boots are made by Rusty for Atmos Fere, fitted to his inflated feet to keep him on Earth and protect him from the dog. Before soaring away, Atmos removes them and drops one; afterward, the boots are displayed in Rash’s pink palace as proof he visited.

CHAPTER 15

Rusty makes the iron boots for Atmos and fits them onto his inflated feet. They keep Atmos from rising into the sky and protect him from being punctured when the dog attacks his leg.

CHAPTER 20

Atmos Fere removes his iron boots before soaring into the air, dropping one boot with a crash as he departs. The boots remain on display in the pink palace of Rash as proof that an airman visited the country.
