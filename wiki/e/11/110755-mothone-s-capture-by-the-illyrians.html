CHAPTER XXXIV.

The Illyrians first anchored at Mothone as if they intended friendly commerce and gradually encouraged the inhabitants to come aboard their ships. When the traffic had grown, they seized many men and more women, put them on board, and sailed away toward the Ionian sea, leaving the town partly stripped of its population.
