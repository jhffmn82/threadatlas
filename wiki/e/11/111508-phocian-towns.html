Phocian towns in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Phocian towns, including Lilæa, Hyampolis, Anticyra, Parapotamii, Panopeus, and Daulis, were taken and razed after war, turned into villages except Abæ, then rebuilt and repopulated, though some remained weak for lack of money.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Many Phocian towns were taken and razed to the ground, including Lilæa, Hyampolis, Anticyra, Parapotamii, Panopeus, and Daulis. The towns were later rebuilt and repopulated from the villages, except for places that remained weak because of a lack of money.
