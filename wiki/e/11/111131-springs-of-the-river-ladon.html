Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XX.

The springs are reached about 50 stades from Lycuria. It is said that water from the marsh at Pheneus, after falling into pits beneath the mountains, reappears there and forms the springs, though the text does not confirm whether this is true.
