Glisas in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Glisas is ruins beyond and left of Teumessus, where the Epigoni and Thebans fought a later battle ending in Theban defeat; above it stands Highest with Highest Zeus’s temple and altar, beside Thermodon.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

Glisas was the place where the Epigoni and the Thebans fought their later battle. The Thebans were defeated there.

CHAPTER XIX.

Glisas is represented by ruins located about seven stades beyond Teumessus. Above it is a mountain called Highest, which bears the temple and altar of Highest Zeus, and the torrent there is called Thermodon.
