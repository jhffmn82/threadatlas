Immense City in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Immense City is a Giant City containing Giants and Giant Castle, enclosed by a high, grim gray wall with yellow lanterns and a huge locked, bolted stone door. Voices, wheels, music, and the smell of giant dinners come from within. Travelers, including Betsy and Carter, wait outside seeking entry and hoping to find Reddy and the Hungry Tiger. Later, Reddy’s changed size makes the city seem ordinary, and he runs through it past Big Wig Guards to its gates.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Immense City is a Giant City surrounded by a grim gray wall with yellow lanterns and a locked stone door barred with huge poles. Sounds of voices, wheels, and music, along with the smell of giant dinners, come from inside, while the travelers remain outside and plan to find a way in.

CHAPTER 13

Immense City is surrounded by a high wall and has a large barred door. Betsy and Carter wait outside it, hoping to enter and find Reddy and the Hungry Tiger.

CHAPTER 17

Immense City seems enormous to Reddy while he is small, but it looks ordinary-sized after he puts on the big wig. Reddy runs through the city, passes Big Wig Guards, and reaches its gates without being stopped.
