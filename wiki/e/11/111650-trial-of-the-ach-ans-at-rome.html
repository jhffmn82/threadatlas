CHAPTER X.

The trial of the Achæans at Rome followed accusations by Callicrates and a Roman Senator that prominent Achæans had supported Perseus. More than 1,000 Achæans were sent to Rome, despite the earlier objection that they should not be condemned before their alleged actions were identified.
