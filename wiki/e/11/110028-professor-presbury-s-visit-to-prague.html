Abstract written by a model

Professor Presbury secretly leaves home, spends a fortnight away, and returns travel-worn without naming his destination. A fellow-student's letter reveals he was in Prague. The visit occurs before his strange symptoms and is connected by Holmes to secret correspondence with a Bohemian dealer and H. Lowenstein's serum treatment.

Professor Presbury leaves home without saying where he is going, remains away for a fortnight, and returns travel-worn without explaining his destination. A letter from a fellow-student in Prague reveals that Presbury had been seen there.
