THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

Professor Presbury leaves home without saying where he is going, remains away for a fortnight, and returns travel-worn without explaining his destination. A letter from a fellow-student in Prague reveals that Presbury had been seen there.

In the morning Mr. Bennett slipped round to bring us the latest report.

Professor Presbury's visit to Prague precedes the beginning of his strange symptoms. Holmes connects the visit with Presbury's secret correspondence with a Bohemian dealer in London and with H. Lowenstein's serum treatment.
