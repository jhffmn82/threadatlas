Polydorus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Polydorus, son and successor of Alcamenes, was king of Sparta during the first war with the Messenians; valued for moderation and mercy, he was murdered by Polemarchus and afterward honored by the Lacedæmonians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III.--LACONIA.

Polydorus succeeded Alcamenes and ruled during the first war with the Messenians. He was popular for his moderation and mercy but was murdered by Polemarchus, after which the Lacedæmonians honored him.
