Damophon in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Damophon, identified as a Messenian, made statues of Ilithyia, Hygiea, Æsculapius, the Great Goddesses, and Aphrodite; his daughters were reportedly represented as two flower-basket-bearing maidens.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Damophon, identified as a Messenian, made the statue of Ilithyia at Ægium. An iambic inscription also attributes the statues of Hygiea and Æsculapius in the nearby sacred enclosure to him.

CHAPTER XXXI.

Damophon made the statues of the Great Goddesses and the statues in the temple of the Great Goddesses and of Aphrodite. The daughters of Damophon are said to be represented by two maidens carrying flower-filled baskets.
