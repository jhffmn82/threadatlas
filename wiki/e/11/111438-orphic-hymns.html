Abstract written by a model

The Hymns of Orpheus are very short, few compositions known and chanted by the Lycomidae at the Mysteries; prized chiefly for religious spirit, they rank second to the Hymns of Homer in composition.

CHAPTER XXX.

The Hymns of Orpheus are described as very short and not numerous. The Lycomidae know them and chant them at the Mysteries, and they are valued for their religious spirit and considered second only to the Hymns of Homer in composition.
