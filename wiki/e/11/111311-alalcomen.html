Abstract written by a model

Alalcomenae is a small village at a low mountain’s foot, said named for Alalcomeneus or Alalcomenia; its plain holds Athene’s temple and the small torrent Triton.

Alalcomenæ is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Alalcomenae is a small village at the foot of a low mountain. It was said to have been named after Alalcomeneus, an Autochthon who reared Athene, or after Alalcomenia, one of the daughters of Ogygus.
