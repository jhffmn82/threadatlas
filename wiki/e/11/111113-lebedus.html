Abstract written by a model

Lebedus, originally inhabited by Carians, was taken by Andræmon and Ionians, noted for numerous agreeable warm sea baths, then dispeopled by Lysimachus to enlarge Ephesus.

Lebedus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER III.

Lebedus was dispeopled by Lysimachus to add to the population of Ephesus. It had been inhabited by the Carians before Andræmon and the Ionians drove them out, and it was noted for its numerous agreeable warm baths near the sea.
