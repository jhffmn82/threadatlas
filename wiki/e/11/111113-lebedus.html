Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Lebedus was dispeopled by Lysimachus to add to the population of Ephesus. It had been inhabited by the Carians before Andræmon and the Ionians drove them out, and it was noted for its numerous agreeable warm baths near the sea.
