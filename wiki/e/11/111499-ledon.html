Ledon in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ledon was formerly a town, named in local tradition after the Autochthon Ledon. Destroyed because of Philomelus's impiety and later deserted for weakness, it survived only in name: about 80 inhabitants lived by the Cephisus in a settlement called Ledon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Ledon was formerly considered a town, but in the author's time it had been deserted by its inhabitants because of its weakness. About 80 inhabitants lived near the Cephisus and called their settlement Ledon; the former town was said to have received its name from an Autochthon named Ledon, and its destruction was attributed to the impiety of Philomelus.
