Abstract written by a model

An oracle consultation and descent requires a ram sacrifice with favourable entrails. Two thirteen-year-old Hermæ attendants prepare the consulter, who drinks Lethe to forget and Memory to remember, worships a statue, and enters at night. Inside, the future is communicated by sight or hearing; afterward priests use the Seat of Memory, question the consulter, and record the account on a tablet.

CHAPTER XXXIX.

Two thirteen-year-old citizen lads called Hermæ wash and otherwise attend the consulter, who then drinks from the waters of Lethe and Memory, worships a statue said to have been made by Dædalus, and approaches the oracle at night in a linen tunic, fillets, and local shoes. Inside, the future is communicated to some through their eyes and to others through their ears; after returning, the priests seat the consulter on the Seat of Memory, ask what was seen or heard, and have the account written on a tablet.
