Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Description of Greece, Volume II / BOOK VII.--ACHAIA. / CHAPTER I.

Achæus was the son of Xuthus and a descendant of Hellen. He took an army from Ægialus and Athens, returned to Thessaly, and recovered his ancestral throne; his sons Archander and Architeles later became connected with Argos and Lacedæmon.
