Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

The market-place of Elis, also called the Hippodrome, is built in an antique style with porticoes and regular streets, and people exercise horses there. It includes the Umpires' Hall, the Doric South Portico, the Corcyræan Portico, altars, temples and statues of Apollo, the Sun, the Moon, the Graces, Eros, Silenus, Fortune, and Pluto, as well as a room where sixteen matrons weave Hera's shawl.
