Festival of Isis at Tithorea

Abstract written by a model

The Festival of Isis is held twice yearly, in Spring and Autumn. Before it, the shrine is secretly purified and old remains buried. Traders sell slaves and goods; at noon worshippers sacrifice oxen and deer, but not swine, sheep, or goats. Wrapped victims are processed and burned, tents burn, and participants depart.

CHAPTER XXXII.

The Festival of Isis is celebrated twice each year, in Spring and Autumn. Before each festival the shrine is secretly purified and remains from the previous sacrifices are buried; traders then set up tents, sell slaves, animals, clothing, silver, and gold, and the sacrifice is held at noon. The victims are wrapped in linen or flax, carried in a solemn procession, and burned in or before the shrine, after which the tents are burned and the participants depart quickly.
