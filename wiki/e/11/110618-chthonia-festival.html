Abstract written by a model

The Chthonia is Hermione’s annual summer festival honouring Demeter Chthonia, unfolding through processions led by priests and town authorities with officials, women, men, and garlanded boys in white.

CHAPTER XXXV.

The Chthonia is the annual summer festival held in honour of Demeter Chthonia at Hermione. Priests, officials, women, men, and boys take part in processions, and the boys wear white clothing and garlands.
