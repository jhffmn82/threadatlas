Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXV.

The Chthonia is the annual summer festival held in honour of Demeter Chthonia at Hermione. Priests, officials, women, men, and boys take part in processions, and the boys wear white clothing and garlands.
