CHAPTER V.

Alexander's dream occurred after he returned from hunting on Mount Pagus and slept beneath a plane-tree growing in the water before the temple of Nemesis. The goddesses of Nemesis appeared in the dream and told him to build a town there and move Smyrna's people from the old city.
