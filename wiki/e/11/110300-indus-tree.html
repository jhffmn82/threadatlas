CHAPTER 9

The Indus Tree stands in the center of the public square and bears tools and objects representing trades, businesses, and professions. The barber takes a razor, the sad singer receives a harp, Carter Green takes a wheel-barrow and spade, and Prince Evered of Rash takes a sword.
