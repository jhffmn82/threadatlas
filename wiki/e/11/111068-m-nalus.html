CHAPTER XXXVI.

A road from Megalopolis leads to Mænalus by the river Helisson. Near Mænalus are the ruins of the town of Mænalus, traces of a temple of Athene, and courses for athletic contests and horseraces. Mount Mænalium is considered sacred to Pan, and nearby inhabitants say they hear Pan playing his pipes.
