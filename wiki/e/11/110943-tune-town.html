Tune Town in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Tune Town is a place governed by principle laws with piano-key gates, where all sing and dance amid music-shaped life until travelers escape through terrible discord.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Tune Town requires its inhabitants and visitors to sing instead of talking and dance instead of walking. Its dwellings, streets, trees, and activities are marked or shaped around music, and the travelers eventually escape it by creating a terrible discord.
