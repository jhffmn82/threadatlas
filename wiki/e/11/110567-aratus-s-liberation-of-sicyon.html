Aratus's liberation of Sicyon in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Aratus entered Sicyon by night with refugees and Argive mercenaries, overcame the garrison and drove Nicocles out, then restored isonomy and property with compensation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Aratus entered Sicyon at night with Sicyonian refugees and mercenaries from Argos, overcame the garrison, and forced Nicocles to flee. He restored isonomy and returned the refugees' houses and goods with compensation to those who had purchased them.
