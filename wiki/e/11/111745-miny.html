Minyæ in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Minyæ, subjects of Minyas, took their name from him and remained distinct under Orchomenus. After Hercules remitted the yearly tax, they suffered reverses against Thebans, then were led to Troy by Ascalaphus and Ialmenus.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVI.

The Minyæ took their name from Minyas. Under Orchomenus they continued to be called Minyæ in order to distinguish them from the Orchomenians in Arcadia.

CHAPTER XXXVII.

The Minyæ suffered great reverses in the war after Hercules had the yearly tax remitted. They were later led to Troy by Ascalaphus and Ialmenus.
