the chest containing the statue of Dionysus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A chest containing a statue of Dionysus and Æsymnetes is said to have been made by Hephæstus and given by Zeus to Dardanus. After Ilium’s capture, Eurypylus received it; traditions say Æneas left it or Cassandra discarded it. Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

After the capture of Ilium, Eurypylus received a chest containing a statue of Dionysus, said by some to have been made by Hephæstus and given by Zeus to Dardanus. Other traditions say that Æneas left it behind or that Cassandra threw it away; when Eurypylus opened it, the sight of its contents drove him out of his mind.

CHAPTER XX.

The priest takes the chest outside the temple on one night during the festival.
