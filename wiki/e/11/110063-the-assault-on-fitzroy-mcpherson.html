Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

An assault apparently using a scourge inflicts fatal injuries on McPherson, leaving curved red marks; tortured, he bites through his lip, crawls from the beach, and dies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE LION'S MANE

The assault leaves McPherson’s back covered with dark red, curved lines like marks from a thin wire scourge. He appears to have been tortured until he bit through his lower lip, then managed only to crawl away from the beach before dying.
