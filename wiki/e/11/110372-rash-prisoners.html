Abstract written by a model

The Rash prisoners, about half the population according to Fizzenpop, are dangerous criminals held in an overcrowded underground cavern beneath the Rash courtyard. The Pasha and Ippty plan to dispose of them by feeding them to the Hungry Tiger, at one or more each day. Although thrown into the courtyard, they are protected by the tiger and Fizzenpop, then disappear from the cavern with the tiger and Prince Evered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The Rash prisoners are held in such numbers that the prison has no room for another person. The Pasha and Ippty plan to dispose of them by having the Hungry Tiger eat them.

CHAPTER 2

The prisoners of Rash are described by Ippty as villains, thieves, robbers, and dangerous rogues who threaten the country. He promises the Hungry Tiger that he can eat at least one prisoner a day and sometimes as many as ten.

CHAPTER 6

Rash prisoners are thrown into the courtyard to be eaten by the Hungry Tiger. Instead, the Hungry Tiger hides and protects them, and they later disappear from the underground cavern along with the tiger and Prince Evered.
