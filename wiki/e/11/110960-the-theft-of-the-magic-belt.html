CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The theft occurs when the oztrich egg hatches and can no longer protect Ozma’s treasures. Ruggedo then becomes visible as a blue patch, pounces on the magic belt, and takes it along with the wishing pills and magic box.
