THE ADVENTURE OF THE ILLUSTRIOUS CLIENT

The investigation begins when Sir James Damery asks Holmes to stop Gruner's marriage to Violet. Holmes studies Gruner, confronts him at Vernon Lodge, and uses Shinwell Johnson to seek further evidence through London's criminal underworld.

And it did. Their blow fell--or his blow rather, for never could I

Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson protect Kitty Winter, conceal the extent of Holmes's recovery, and arrange for Dr. John Watson to approach Baron Adelbert Gruner under a false identity. Holmes's staged illness and the Ming saucer lure Gruner into a meeting where Kitty Winter can attack him with vitriol.
