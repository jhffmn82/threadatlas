Seres in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Seres breed Ser insects to produce garment thread, feeding them grain for four years and green reed in the fifth before collecting thread after death; traditions describe them as Ethiopian or mixed Scythian and Indian.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

The Seres produce the thread used for their garments from the Ser insect rather than from a plant. They breed the insects, feed them for four years on grain and in the fifth year on green reed, and then obtain the thread from the insects after they die.
