Abstract written by a model

Thor Place is Mr. Neil Gibson’s estate in Hampshire, with a half-timbered house combining Tudor and Georgian features, grounds, pheasant preserves, and Gibson’s firearms. After a delay and a night at Winchester, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson travel there, inspect the weapons after examining Thor Bridge, and then proceed toward the crime scene.
