Abstract written by a model

is_spartan Sparta [cattle-keeper for Polychares] Euæphnus was a Spartan entrusted by Polychares with cattle but sold them to merchants and falsely claimed that pirates had taken them. After being exposed, he lured and killed Polychares’ son, becoming the central figure in the dispute over whether Polychares should be surrendered.
