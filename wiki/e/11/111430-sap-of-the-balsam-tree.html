Abstract written by a model

Sap of the balsam tree is a sweet-scented ointment and vipers’ preferred food; as nearby vipers feed on it, their venom is said to become milder.

CHAPTER XXVIII.

The sap of the balsam tree is described as the food most agreeable to the vipers that live near the trees. Because the vipers feed on its sweet-scented substance, their venom is said to lose its deadly properties and become milder.
