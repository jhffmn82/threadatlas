------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER X.

Teaching at Shalersville was not financially profitable, and Asaph Hall decided that he preferred carpentry. Nevertheless, the work was successful in other respects: the pupils were apt, the couple was well liked, and the townspeople gave them sixty dollars to help support them during the final term.
