CHAPTER XXIX.

Demetrius, son of Philip, led a Macedonian force sent by Philip to the Peloponnese in search of money. He landed in Argolis, marched secretly to Messene, entered the town before dawn, and was defeated when the Messenians and the citadel's garrison attacked him.
