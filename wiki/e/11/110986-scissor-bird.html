Scissor Bird in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

The Scissor Bird is a talking bird with a dangerous scissor bill that lives on a perch by the window in the Queen's chamber and eats calico scraps for breakfast. When the Queen goes to pieces, it cries for help and flies out to announce the news. It later relentlessly forces Scraps from task to task, snipping off yarn hair and pursuing her. During Peter and Scraps's escape, Peter covers it with the Sandman's Nap Sack, and it falls asleep snoring.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The Scissor Bird sleeps on a perch in the Queen's chamber until the Queen goes to pieces. It cries out for help and then flies out of the window to announce the news to the patchworking population.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

The Scissor Bird threatens Scraps by swooping at her and snipping off an inch of her yarn hair. It relentlessly pursues her from one task to another throughout the day and ignores her complaints.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

The Scissor Bird supervises Peter and Scraps, drives Scraps toward the kitchen, and repeatedly snips at them with its bill. Peter throws the Sandman's Nap Sack over its head, causing it to fall asleep and snore loudly.
