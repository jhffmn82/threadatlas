capture of Phigalia in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The capture of Phigalia occurred in the second year of the 30th Olympiad after the Lacedæmonians defeated the Phigalians and besieged their town.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIX.

The capture of Phigalia occurred when the Lacedæmonians defeated the Phigalians in battle and besieged the town. It took place when Miltiades was chief magistrate at Athens, in the second year of the 30th Olympiad.
