Abstract written by a model

contains Spartan Senate [a council chamber for the Senate] The market-place of Sparta contained the council chamber, public buildings for the Ephors, guardians of the laws, and Bidiæi, and the notable Persian Portico built from spoils taken from the Medes. It also held statues of Persian figures, Artemisia, Pythæan Apollo, Artemis, Leto, Agias, and the People of Sparta, along with temples of several gods and monuments connected with Orestes, Polydorus, and other figures. The text further describes the Scias and several roads and shrines leading from the market-place.
