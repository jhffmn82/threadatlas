Celeæ in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Celeæ, about five stades from Phlius, hosts the Phliasians’ fourth-year mysteries of Demeter and contains Aras’s and Dysaules’s tombs.

CHAPTER XII.

Celeæ lies about five stades from Phlius. The Phliasians hold their mysteries of Demeter there every fourth year, and the place contains the tombs of Aras and Dysaules.
