Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Pelagos is an oak plantation near the temple of Poseidon, along the road from Mantinea to Tegea and later along the road from Mantinea to Pallantium. The boundary between Mantinea and Tegea is marked by a round altar on the highroad, and the cavalry of the Mantineans and Athenians fought the Bœotian cavalry at Pelagos.
