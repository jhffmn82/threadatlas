Abstract written by a model

Pelagos is an oak plantation near the temple of Poseidon, along roads from Mantinea to Tegea and later Pallantium; marked by a boundary altar, it was the site where Mantinean and Athenian cavalry fought Bœotian cavalry.

Pelagos is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XI.

Pelagos is an oak plantation near the temple of Poseidon, along the road from Mantinea to Tegea and later along the road from Mantinea to Pallantium. The boundary between Mantinea and Tegea is marked by a round altar on the highroad, and the cavalry of the Mantineans and Athenians fought the Bœotian cavalry at Pelagos.
