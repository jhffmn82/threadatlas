Oresthasium

Abstract written by a model

Oresthasium, founded by Orestheus, eagerly provides 100 picked men after an oracle; it has ruins, and pillars of Artemis's temple remain.

Oresthasium is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
