Taraxippus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Taraxippus is a circular altar or place at the Hippodrome’s outlet that terrifies horses, causing panic, wrecked chariots, and injured charioteers. Greeks offered several identifications; it is most plausibly identified with Poseidon Hippius.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

Taraxippus was a circular altar or place at the outlet of the horse-racing course that suddenly terrified horses, often causing chariots to break apart and charioteers to be injured. The Greeks offered several explanations for it, including that it was connected with Olenius, Dameon, Myrtilus, Oenomaus, Alcathous, or Poseidon Hippius; the narrator considered the last explanation most plausible.
