Kuma Party’s hand in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Kuma Party’s right arm and hand is magical assistance sent to prisoners. It smashes through a castle door, opens gates and removes obstacles during an escape, striking pursuing Quilties with its stout club and carrying tired Peter and Grumpy to a maple grove. Later it flies over banquet guests, carrying a warning note for Ozma and searching the palace, but fails to find her because Ruggedo hides beneath a table.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Kuma's right arm and hand smashes through the castle door, unlocks the way out, and uses a club to strike the Quilties pursuing the fugitives. It opens gates, removes obstacles, carries Peter and Grumpy when they become tired, and then leaves them in a maple grove several miles from Patch.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Kuma Party’s hand flies over the guests during the banquet preparations, frightening everyone. It carries a note of warning for Ozma and searches the palace for her, but it does not find her because Ruggedo hides beneath the table.
