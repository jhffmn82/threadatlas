A seafight between the Liparæans and Tyrrhenians begins after Pythian Priestess instructs a small fleet; five Liparæan triremes capture twenty Tyrrhenian ships.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVI.

In the seafight between the Liparæans and Tyrrhenians, the Pythian Priestess instructed the Liparæans to fight with as small a fleet as possible. The Liparæans sailed with five triremes and captured the Tyrrhenians’ first five ships and three further groups of five.
