Abstract written by a model

Sapphire City is a city in Oz and the capital of the East, with glittering spires, jeweled sands and rocks, blue waters, and the Castle of Cheeriobed. The Wizard brings Ozma, Philador, Trot, and others there, where Quiberon is petrified. Cheeriobed and the royal party tour it, and King Orin and Queen Cheeriobed declare it the eastern capital.

Sapphire City is in one work on this site, The Giant Horse of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 17

The Wizard transports Ozma, Philador, Trot, and the others to the Sapphire City and the Castle of Cheeriobed. The city contains the blue palace and its gardens, where Quiberon is petrified.

CHAPTER 19

Sapphire City is toured by Cheeriobed and the royal party. Its glittering spires, jeweled sands and rocks, and blue waters make it one of the fairest cities in Oz next to the capital.

CHAPTER 20

Sapphire City is declared the capital of the East under King Orin and Queen Cheeriobed. Trot is given the privilege of living there at any time and for as long as she wants.
