the amour of Poseidon with Demeter in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The amour of Poseidon with Demeter is a tradition of their union at Onceum after Demeter became a mare and Poseidon a horse. It produces Arion and an unnamed daughter, while Phigalian tradition instead says Demeter bore Despoena rather than a foal.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

Demeter’s union with Poseidon occurred after she changed into a mare and Poseidon changed into a horse at Onceum. The tradition says that Demeter bore a daughter whose name was not revealed to the uninitiated and the foal Arion.

CHAPTER XLII.

The people of Phigalia believed the traditions about the amour of Poseidon with Demeter that were also told by the people of Thelpusa. They differed by saying that Demeter gave birth to Despoena rather than to a foal.
