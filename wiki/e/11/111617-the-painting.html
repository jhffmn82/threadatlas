the painting in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The painting depicts scenes in Hades, including Odysseus’ descent, Charon’s boat on Acheron, and punishments for people who mistreated fathers or committed sacrilege. In its upper part are dice and Ajax the son of Oileus, looking at dice players, with Meleager above. Its lower part includes Hector, Thamyris, Memnon, and Sarpedon, with Paris and Penthesilea above. Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

The painting depicts scenes in Hades, including Odysseus’ descent, Charon’s boat on Acheron, the punishment of people who mistreated fathers or committed sacrilege, and several named figures. It also includes Eurynomus, Auge, and Iphimedea.

CHAPTER XXXI.

The painting contains scenes and figures from several myths, including Actæon, the two Ajaxes, Palamedes, Thersites, Meleager, Hector, Memnon, Sarpedon, Paris, Penthesilea, Callisto, Sisyphus, and Tantalus. It also shows people carrying water, a winejar, birds on Memnon’s cloak, and figures apparently making a mock of the Eleusinian mysteries.
