Sergeant Coventry in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Sergeant Coventry, a tall, thin, secretive local police investigator, first examines the affair. Decent and honest though out of his depth, he guides Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson after an official delay and reports Mrs. Gibson's shooting, note, and Mr. Neil Gibson's summons.
