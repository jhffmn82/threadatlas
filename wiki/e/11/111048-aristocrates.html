Aristocrates

Abstract written by a model

Aristocrates, son of Æchmis, committed sacrilege by violating the priestess of Artemis Hymnia at her altar. The Arcadians stoned him to death. His tomb stood beside the wells called Teneæ on the road from Orchomenus toward Pheneus.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Aristocrates, son of Æchmis, committed sacrilege by defiling the priestess of Artemis Hymnia at the altar near her statue, and the Arcadians stoned him to death. A later Aristocrates, grandson of Hicetas and bearing the same name, was also stoned to death after accepting bribes from Lacedæmon and betraying the Messenians at the Great Trench.

CHAPTER XIII.

Aristocrates violated the priestess of Artemis Hymnia. His tomb stood on the road from Orchomenus toward Pheneus, beside the wells called Teneæ.
