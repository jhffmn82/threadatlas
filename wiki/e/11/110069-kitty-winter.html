Kitty Winter

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Miss Kitty Winter, Miss W., Miss Winter, his last mistress, my companion, that girl, the avenger, the fiery Miss W., the girl, the she-devil, this young lady, visitor, young woman

Abstract written by a model

Kitty Winter is a young, fiery woman and Baron Adelbert Gruner’s last former mistress. She says Gruner tempted, used, and ruined her, made her what she is, and may have murdered others; she intensely hates him and offers unpaid help to bring him down as firsthand evidence of his abuse. She joins Holmes in warning Violet de Merville, Gruner’s intended bride, that he ruins women and will cause Violet’s death, though rage nearly drives Kitty to attack Violet. After Holmes is attacked, Kitty is moved for protection, hides near Gruner’s house, and throws vitriol through his window, severely disfiguring him. She is thereafter targeted by two attackers and faces a grave vitriol-throwing prosecution.

Kitty Winter is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

We found him sure enough, a huge, coarse, red-faced, scorbutic man,

Kitty Winter declares intense hatred for Adelbert Gruner, whom she says made her what she is, and offers to help put him where he belongs. She says Gruner used and ruined her, may have been responsible for other murders, and kept a book documenting the women he had ruined. She agrees to return to Holmes and to confront Gruner's intended bride if arranged.

I did not see Holmes again until the following evening, when we dined

Miss Winter accompanies Holmes to warn Violet de Merville about Baron Gruner. She declares that she was one of his former mistresses, accuses him of tempting, using, and ruining women, and warns Violet that he will cause her death; in rage, she nearly attacks Violet before Holmes restrains her.
