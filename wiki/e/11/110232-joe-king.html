Abstract written by a model

Joe King is King of the Uplanders and Up Town, ruling a mountain-top kingdom. Though he came down from ruling to work and have fun, using his ax in the mountains, he owns the horse High Boy. He aids Philador and Herby, welcomes them to Up Town, tells Queen Hyacinth their story, hosts a feast, gives umbrellas, and seeks High Boy to speed their journey to the capital.

CHAPTER 9

Joe King is the King of the Uplanders, whose kingdom is on the mountain top. He says he came down from kinging it to work and have fun, offers Philador half of his kingdom, and agrees to help the travelers by summoning High Boy.

CHAPTER 10

Joe King welcomes Philador and Herby to Up Town and tells Queen Hyacinth about their adventures. He hosts them at a royal feast, gives them umbrellas, and searches for High Boy so the travelers can reach the capital quickly.
