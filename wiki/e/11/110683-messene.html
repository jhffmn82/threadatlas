BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Messene was the proud Argive daughter of Triopas and wife of Polycaon. She helped invade and name Messenia, lived with Polycaon at Andania, and was said to have established games for the Great Goddesses.

CHAPTER XXIV.

Messene, the daughter of Triopas, was invoked with other heroes to return and dwell with the restored Messenians. The priests and leaders called especially upon her during the sacrifices connected with the rebuilding of the Messenian capital.
