Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

The temple of the Eumenides at Cerynea was said to have been built by Orestes. Anyone stained with blood or another defilement who entered to look around was immediately driven frantic by fear, so admission was not granted indiscriminately.
