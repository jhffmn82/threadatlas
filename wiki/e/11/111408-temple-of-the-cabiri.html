Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

The temple of the Cabiri stood about seven stades from the grove and was considered exceptionally holy. Those who entered it with hostile or profane intent were destroyed: some of Xerxes’ army went mad and perished, and Macedonians who entered during Alexander’s attack on Thebes were killed by lightning and thunderbolts.

CHAPTER XXVI.

The temple of the Cabiri serves as a reference point for the plain of Tenerus, the mountain associated with the Sphinx, the ruins of Onchestus, and the route to Thespia.
