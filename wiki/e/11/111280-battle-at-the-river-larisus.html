CHAPTER XLIX.

At the battle at the river Larisus, the Achæans and their allies fought the men of Elis and an Ætolian force supporting Elis because of kinship. Philopœmen personally killed Demophantus, the enemy cavalry commander, and then routed the Ætolian and Elean cavalry.
