Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Merrilow, a South Brixton landlady, owns a private house and lodges Eugenia Ronder for seven years. Having seen Eugenia’s face once and heard her cry about murder, she asks Sherlock Holmes to intervene while fearing the valuable lodger may leave.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

Mrs. Merrilow has rented a private room to Eugenia Ronder for seven years and has seen her face only once. She tells Sherlock Holmes that Eugenia cries out about murder and asks him to come, while chiefly fearing that anything he does might cause her valuable lodger to leave.
