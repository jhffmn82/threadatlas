Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

The mummy remains consist of a human head and a few bones found hidden beneath a board in one corner of the haunted crypt. John Mason believes they may be a thousand years old and says they were not present there before.
