Ozma’s party in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Ozma’s party is a banquet at Ozma’s palace welcoming Ozma. Dorothy prepares speeches, games, dancing, food, music, decorations, and guests. Kuma Party’s hand, Scraps, and the oztrich interrupt it; later Ruggedo’s invisible attacks and warnings disturb it. Guests resume celebrating, but after an egg hatches, Ruggedo steals the magic treasures, including the magic belt.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Dorothy organizes Ozma’s party with a welcoming speech, games, dancing, Ozcream, decorations, refreshments, music, and invited guests. The preparations continue until Ozma returns, but the celebration is interrupted by Kuma Party’s hand and the arrival of Scraps and the oztrich.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The party is Ozma’s banquet, disrupted by the oztrich’s roars, Ruggedo’s invisible attacks, and the warning delivered by Kuma’s hand. After hearing Scraps’s story, the guests resume eating and celebrating until the egg hatches and Ruggedo steals the magic treasures.
