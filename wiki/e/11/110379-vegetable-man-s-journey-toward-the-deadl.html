Vegetable Man's journey toward the Deadly Desert in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

A journey unintentionally directed by the Quick Sandals begins when Carter Green and Betsy Bobbin are stranded in a desert, travels south by cart, crosses quicksand without stopping, and ends at the Deadly Desert's edge.

CHAPTER 4

The Vegetable Man's journey toward the Deadly Desert begins after a winding road strands Carter and Betsy in a desert. They travel south in the cart, encounter quicksand, and cross it when the Quick Sandals drive Carter forward, finally reaching the edge of the Deadly Desert.
