1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER LIII.

Scephrus, the son of Tegeates, privately conversed with Apollo when Apollo and Artemis came to Tegea. Limon mistakenly thought he was accusing him and killed him, after which the oracle at Delphi instructed the people to mourn for Scephrus.
