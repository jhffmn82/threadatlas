Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Chthonia, daughter of Colontas and sister of Clymenus, is brought to Hermione by Demeter, builds Demeter’s temple, and becomes Demeter’s epithet there.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXV.

Chthonia is said to have been the daughter of Colontas and the sister of Clymenus. After Demeter brought her to Hermione, she built the temple there to Demeter, who is consequently called Chthonia.
