CHAPTER VI.

The passes into Arcadia from Argolis ran by Hysiæ and across Parthenium toward Tegea, and two others led through Mantinea by Holm-Oak and Ladder. Ladder was the broadest and had steps cut into it; beyond it lay Melangea and, about seven stades farther, the well of the Meliastæ. Another route crossed Artemisium and was much narrower than Ladder-pass.
