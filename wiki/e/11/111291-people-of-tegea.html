Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER LIII.

The people of Tegea say that Apollo and Artemis punished those who neglected Leto in Arcadia, and that Tegeates’ sons Cydon, Archedius, and Gortys migrated to Crete. They maintain rites for Scephrus, possess four tribal statues of Apollo Aguieus, and preserve traditions about victories over the Lacedæmonians.
