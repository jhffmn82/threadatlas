Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The sons of Codrus disputed or received leadership after Medon was assigned the Athenian kingdom. Nileus and the other sons were sent to found a colony, while Androclus led the Ionians who sailed to Ephesus and Cyaretus colonized Myus.
