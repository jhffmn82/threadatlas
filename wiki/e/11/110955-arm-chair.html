arm-chair in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Wumbo’s animated magic arm-chair has real arms, speaks English stuffily, and can hug or tightly clasp Ruggedo. It holds and scolds the invisible Ruggedo, squeezing him when he struggles or uses its rungs, until its enchantment ends after four hours.
