bridal-bed of Alcmena

Also called: bridal-bed

Abstract written by a model

A bridal-bed made by Trophonius and Agamedes for Amphitryon’s marriage to Alcmena, later shown among his house’s ruins.

CHAPTER XI.

The bridal-bed of Alcmena is shown among the ruins of Amphitryon’s house. It was made for Amphitryon by Trophonius and Agamedes, according to an inscription.
