uses projection [principles of projection] Asaph Hall 3rd showed his fellow carpenters how to construct a house roof scientifically. Instead of relying on their customary cut-and-try method, he worked out the problem by the principles of projection, allowing the timbers to be sawed correctly the first time and fit perfectly. The achievement made him realize that he was more than a carpenter and awakened his ambition to become an architect.
