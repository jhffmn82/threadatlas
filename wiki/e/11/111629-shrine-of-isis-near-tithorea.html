Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXII.

The shrine of Isis is about 40 stades from the temple of Æsculapius and is described as the holiest of the Greek shrines to the Egyptian goddess. The people of Tithorea do not live near it, and no one may approach unless Isis has first invited that person in dreams.
