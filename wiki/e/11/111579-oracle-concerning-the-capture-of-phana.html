oracle concerning the capture of Phana

Also called: the meaning of the oracle, the oracle, the response

Abstract written by a model

An oracle delivered at Delphi to the Achæans directs them to inspect Phana’s water supply and consumption. Though initially misunderstood, it is later applied when the Achæans foul the town’s water.

CHAPTER XVIII.

The oracle told the inhabitants of the land of Pelops and Achaia to observe how much water was daily given to Phana’s inhabitants and how much the town had already drunk. The Achæans initially failed to understand it, but later applied it by fouling the town’s water.
