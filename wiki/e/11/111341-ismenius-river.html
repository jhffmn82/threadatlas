Ismenius River

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Ladon, the river, the river that flows by

Abstract written by a model

Ismenius is a river flowing by Apollo’s sacred hill, sharing its name with the hill and god; before Apollo’s son Ismenius was born, it may have been called Ladon.

Ismenius River is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER X.

The river flowing by the hill sacred to Apollo is called Ismenius, as are the hill and the god. It may previously have been called Ladon before the birth of Apollo’s son Ismenius.
