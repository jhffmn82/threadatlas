Abstract written by a model

Vipers in mountainous Phœnicia are said to have especially dangerous venom. In Arabia, vipers live and lodge beneath balsam trees, rejoice in their shade, and, feeding on sweet-scented sap, have nondeadly venom despite steel-like bites.

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Vipers in mountainous Phœnicia are said to have especially dangerous venom. In Arabia, vipers living near balsam trees are said to shelter beneath them and to have bites resembling wounds from steel, though their venom is not considered deadly because they feed on sweet-scented sap.
