Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Lophis was the son of a ruler who had gone to Delphi for instructions about obtaining water. The ruler killed him because the Pythian Priestess had ordered him to slay the first person he met on returning; Lophis, still alive, ran around, and water sprang up wherever his blood flowed.
