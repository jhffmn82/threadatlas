The note from Mrs. Gibson asks Miss Dunbar to meet her at Thor Bridge and to leave a reply on the garden sundial. Mrs. Gibson's request that it be destroyed, followed by her retaining Miss Dunbar's reply in her hand, forms part of the plan to make Miss Dunbar appear connected to the crime.
