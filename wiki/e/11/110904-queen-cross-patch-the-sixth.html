Queen Cross Patch, the Sixth in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Queen Cross Patch, the Sixth, rules Patch from her castle, watching subjects work. Crossest and patchiest among the Quilties, she works and sews so finely she strains an eye, wears an eye patch, and finally goes to pieces sorting patches as former queen.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

Queen Cross Patch, the Sixth, rules Patch and watches her subjects work from her castle window. She is extremely cross and patchy, has a patch over one eye from fine sewing, and goes to pieces while sorting patches at her sewing table.
