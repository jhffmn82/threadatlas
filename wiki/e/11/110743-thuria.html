CHAPTER XXIX.

Thuria, which was said to be Homer's Anthea, was given by Augustus to the Spartans. Its inhabitants moved from the ancient city on a height to the plain, where the river Aris flowed by the town, while ruins and a temple of the Syrian goddess remained above.
