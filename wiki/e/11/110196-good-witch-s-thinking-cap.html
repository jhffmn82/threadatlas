Good Witch's thinking cap in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The Good Witch of the North's thinking cap is an object that reveals the direction to the Emerald City when asked. Philador and Herby take it and use it to travel through the forest and toward the mountains, though they forget to consult it at the river. A storm carries it away, leaving Herby to mourn it and resolve to think for themselves.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Philador and Herby take the good witch's thinking cap from a peg. When Herby asks it for the way to the Emerald City, it guides him straight ahead through the forest.

CHAPTER 9

The thinking cap guides Herby and Philador in the direction of the Emerald City and later indicates that the way lies over the mountains. They forget to use it while deciding how to cross the river.

CHAPTER 10

The Good Witch's thinking cap is lost when the storm carries Philador and Herby away. Herby mourns its loss and says they will have to do their own thinking thereafter.
