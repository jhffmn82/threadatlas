Abstract written by a model

Shoscombe Prince is a racehorse and leading Derby colt, Sir Robert Norberton's concealed decisive wager and principal hope for financial recovery. Faster than his half-brother, he is hidden behind that substitute in public training and entered as a dark horse. He wins the Derby, allowing Sir Robert to pay creditors and retain enough money to re-establish himself.

THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

Shoscombe Prince is described as the best colt in England and Sir Robert's principal hope for financial recovery. Sir Robert has secretly backed him heavily for the Derby and uses the colt's half-brother in public training spins to conceal his ability.
