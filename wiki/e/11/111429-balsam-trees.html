Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Balsam trees are described as being about the size of myrtle bushes, with leaves like those of marjoram. Vipers in Arabia lodge beneath them because they enjoy their shade and feed on their sap.
