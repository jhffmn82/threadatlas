Quick Sandals

Abstract written by a model

Quick Sandals are large white-leather magical footwear with silver buckles, found by Betsy in a newspaper-wrapped package. Fitting Carter Green, they prevent him from taking root but make him jump and run uncontrollably. They carry Betsy and Carter across quicksand and the Deadly Desert, then leave Carter after reaching Rash.

CHAPTER 4

The Quick Sandals are large white-leather sandals with silver buckles that Betsy finds in a newspaper-wrapped package. They fit Carter's feet and prevent him from taking root, but they also make him spring into the air and race uncontrollably.

CHAPTER 5

The quick sandals carry Betsy and Carter safely across the quicksand and the Deadly Desert. After reaching Rash, they remove themselves from Carter's feet and run away.
