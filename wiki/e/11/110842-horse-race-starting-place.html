Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The horse-race starting-place was shaped like the prow of a ship, with its beak turned toward the course, and each side was more than 400 feet long. It had buildings assigned by lot to entrants, ropes that were released in sequence, a brazen dolphin, and an altar bearing a mechanical brazen eagle that rose when the start was signaled.
