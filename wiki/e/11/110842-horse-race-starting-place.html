horse-race starting-place

Abstract written by a model

The horse-race starting-place, shaped like a ship’s prow facing the course, extended more than 400 feet on each side. It assigned buildings to entrants by lot and staged the start through sequentially released ropes, a brazen dolphin, and a mechanical brazen eagle rising from an unbaked-brick altar.

CHAPTER XVII.

The horse-race starting-place was shaped like the prow of a ship, with its beak turned toward the course, and each side was more than 400 feet long. It had buildings assigned by lot to entrants, ropes that were released in sequence, a brazen dolphin, and an altar bearing a mechanical brazen eagle that rose when the start was signaled.
