Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

Ronder was a famous but declining wild-beast showman who drank, bullied people, abused Eugenia, and treated animals cruelly. Leonardo killed him with a club designed to leave wounds resembling a lion's paw, after which the death was officially treated as misadventure.
