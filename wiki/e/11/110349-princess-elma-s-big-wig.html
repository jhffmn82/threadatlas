Abstract written by a model

Princess Elma's big wig is a magic wig that makes its wearer giant-sized. Reddy takes it from beside her bed, uses it to escape the Giant Castle and show its power to Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green, then wears it against the Rash army and to prevent Irashi and Ippty from escaping. He keeps it as a reminder of the Big Wigs.

CHAPTER 17

Princess Elma's big wig makes its wearer grow to the size of a Big Wig. Reddy takes it from beside her bed, uses it to escape the Giant Castle, and later demonstrates its power to Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green. He decides to keep it as a reminder of the Big Wigs.

CHAPTER 19

Reddy puts on the giant's wig when the Rash army rushes at him, causing him to shoot skyward and terrifying the soldiers. He later puts it on again to ensure that Irashi and Ippty cannot escape while they are being bound.
