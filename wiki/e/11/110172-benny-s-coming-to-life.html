Abstract written by a model

Benny’s coming to life is caused by Danny reading strange, mysterious words from the little black book, changing the Public Benefactor from a statue into a living being. Benny leaves his pedestal, frightens and confuses people in Boston, then falls into Oz and gradually learns to behave like a person.

CHAPTER 3

Benny comes to life when Danny reads strange words from the little black book. He immediately leaves his pedestal, but his attempts to behave like a person cause fear and confusion among the people in Boston.

CHAPTER 4

The Scarecrow tells Trot that Benny was once a stone statue who came to life and fell into Oz. Benny is learning how to behave as a living being and is beginning to act more like a real person.
