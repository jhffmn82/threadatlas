Sir Robert's concealment of the death

Abstract written by a model

Sir Robert concealed Lady Beatrice's death for roughly three weeks so that Shoscombe Prince could run in the Derby and his bets could succeed. He arranged the public personation, hid the body in the chapel crypt, and delayed registering the death, receiving only mild censure afterward.
