Laphria

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: festival called Laphria

Abstract written by a model

Laphria is an annual festival held by the people of Patræ for Artemis. It begins with a splendid procession, ending with a virgin priestess riding in a chariot drawn by stags, and continues the following day with sacrificial rites burning live birds, animals, and tree fruit on a large altar.

Laphria is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XVIII.

Laphria was the annual festival held by the people of Patræ for Artemis. It included a procession led by a virgin priestess in a chariot drawn by stags, followed by sacrifices in which live birds, domestic and wild animals, and tree fruit were burned on a large altar.
