Abstract written by a model

John Mason is head trainer at the Shoscombe training quarters, working for Sir Robert Norberton and training Shoscombe Prince. Concerned by Sir Robert’s strange, possibly criminal behavior, he asks Sherlock Holmes to investigate, reporting household changes, crypt visits, an unknown man, mummy remains, and charred bone. He later guides Holmes and Watson to the chapel crypt, then departs before their examination.
