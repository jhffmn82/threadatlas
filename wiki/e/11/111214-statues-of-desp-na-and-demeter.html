Abstract written by a model

The statues of Despœna and Demeter, with their throne and footstool, were made by Damophon from one stone block found within the temple precincts after a dream vision; each was about the size of the statue of the Mother at Athens.

CHAPTER XXXVII.

The statues of Despœna and Demeter, along with their throne and footstool, were made from one block of stone by Damophon. The stone was said to have been found and dug up within the temple precincts after a vision in a dream. The statues were about the size of the statue of the Mother at Athens.
