Charillus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Charillus, king of the Lacedæmonians, led their first invasion of Tegea, was captured by women of Tegea, and released without ransom after swearing never to lead an army there again; he later violated that oath.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVIII.

Charillus, king of the Lacedæmonians, led the first invasion of Tegea in the Laconian war. He was captured and released without ransom after swearing that he would never again lead a Lacedæmonian army to Tegea, though he later violated the oath.
