ring leader in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The ring leader is a Round-abouty leading the Round-abouties, marked by merry-go-round rings and a large silver bell. Around a round table, he orders Trot to make him laugh and the travelers kept circling; after Trot escapes, he rings the bell to direct pursuit.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The ring leader is a Round-abouty who sits with others around a round table and carries a large silver bell. He tells Trot that her mission is to make him laugh and orders the Round-abouties to keep the travelers going around. When Trot escapes toward the tower, he begins directing the pursuit by ringing the bell.
