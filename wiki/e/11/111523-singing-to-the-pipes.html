Abstract written by a model

Singing to the pipes became a Pythian contest in the third year of the 48th Olympiad, when Echembrotus of Arcadia won; it was discontinued in the second Pythiad as gloomy music associated with elegies and dirges.

CHAPTER VII.

Singing to the pipes was added as a Pythian contest in the third year of the 48th Olympiad, with Echembrotus of Arcadia named as victor. It was discontinued in the second Pythiad because the music was considered the most gloomy kind, and because elegies and dirges were sung in that manner.
