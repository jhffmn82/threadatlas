the naming of Achaia

Abstract written by a model

The country between Elis and Sicyonia was called Achaia in Pausanias's time because of its inhabitants. In earlier times it was called Ægialus, and its inhabitants were called Ægialians or, after Ion, Ionian Ægialians.
