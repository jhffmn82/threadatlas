Phialo in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Phialo, daughter of Alcimedon and mother of Æchmagoras, had an intrigue with Hercules, was exposed with her newborn son, and was later saved by Hercules.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Phialo was the daughter of Alcimedon and had an intrigue with Hercules. After Alcimedon discovered that she had become a mother, he exposed her and her newborn son, but Hercules later released and saved her.
