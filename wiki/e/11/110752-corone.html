Abstract written by a model

Corone is a coastal town under Mathia, 80 stades from the Pamisus and 40 from Colonides. Formerly called Æpea, it was renamed Coronea after the restoration of the Messenians and later called Corone. It has temples of Artemis, Dionysus, and Æsculapius, and brazen statues of Zeus Soter and Athene.

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Corone is a coastal town under Mount Mathia, about 80 stades from the Pamisus and about 40 stades from Colonides. It was formerly called Aepæ, and its name was later said to have been changed to Coronea and then mispronounced as Corone. It contains temples of Artemis, Dionysus, and Æsculapius, a statue of Zeus Soter in the market-place, and a statue of Athene holding a crow in the citadel.
