murder of Philopœmen in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The murder of Philopœmen was carried out by Dinocrates and accomplices through poison. Afterward, the Achæans were thought to banish involved Messenians; Lycortas marched against Messenia, accomplices were executed, and Dinocrates committed suicide.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

The Messenians involved in the death of Philopœmen were thought to have been banished by the Achæans for that reason. The restored Lacedæmonians encouraged them and other Achæan exiles to appeal to Rome.

CHAPTER LI.

The murder of Philopœmen was carried out when Dinocrates took him off by poison. After Lycortas marched against Messenia, those who were privy to the murder were put to death, except Dinocrates, who committed suicide.
