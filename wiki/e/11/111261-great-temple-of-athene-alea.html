great temple of Athene Alea

Abstract written by a model

The great temple of Athene Alea at Tegea surpassed Peloponnese temples in beauty and size. Built by Scopas the Parian, it combined Doric first-row, Corinthian second-row, and Ionic outer pillars. Its gables showed the Calydonian boar hunt and the combat between Telephus and Achilles.

CHAPTER XLV.

The great temple of Athene Alea at Tegea surpassed all temples in the Peloponnese in beauty and size. Its first row of pillars was Doric, its second row Corinthian, and its outer pillars Ionic; its gables depicted the Calydonian boar hunt and the single combat between Telephus and Achilles.
