Epidaurus Limera

place · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

Epidaurus Limera, adjacent to Bœæ and about two hundred stades from Epidelium, was said to be founded by Epidaurians from Argolis rather than Lacedæmonians. Its notable sights unfold through temples, an Æsculapius altar and stone statue, the water of Ino, harbour-side Zeus Soter, citadel Athene, and sea-jutting Minoa.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Epidaurus Limera lies about two hundred stades from Epidelium and was said to have been founded by Epidaurians from Argolis rather than by the Lacedæmonians. Its notable sights include temples of Aphrodite, Æsculapius, Athene, and Zeus Soter, as well as the water of Ino and the promontory of Minoa.
