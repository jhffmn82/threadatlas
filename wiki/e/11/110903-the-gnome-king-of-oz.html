Narrative, section by section

20 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

Queen Cross Patch, the Sixth, goes to pieces while ruling the patch-work Kingdom of Patch in Oz. Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper put her scraps away and begin the required search for a new ruler, using the Royal Spool of Succession. The spool leads them out of Patch, through the Winkie Country, and eventually to the Emerald City. A magical foot-path carries the two Quilties rapidly to the city gates, where they continue following the spool.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

Scrapper and Piecer follow the Spool of Succession into the Emerald City and find the Patchwork Girl, Scraps, whom they mistake as the future ruler of Patch. They kidnap Scraps in a rag bag and use the foot-path to carry her back to the Quilty Kingdom. The Quilties enthusiastically accept Scraps as their queen, and she is crowned Queen of Patch with a sewing basket as her coronet and old spools as her crown jewels.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Scraps, newly made Queen of Patch, refuses to perform the extensive household and administrative duties assigned to her. Piecer and Scrapper use the Scissor Bird to threaten her, forcing her to work all day while the bird pursues her. That night, Scraps discovers Grumpy, a small brown bear who had been the late Queen Cross Patch’s pet, and tells him she intends to escape and return to the Emerald City. A violent disturbance at the palace door ends the chapter as Grumpy hides in a chest.

CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

Peter leaves his Philadelphia home to buy a balloon and is carried into the sky by a green balloon that transforms into a talking balloon bird. The bird tells Peter that he is being taken to Balloon Island as a present for Queen Luna from Sandaroo, but Peter lets go and falls into the Nonestic Ocean. He swims to a small island, where he meets Ruggedo, the exiled Gnome King, who claims to rule five hundred thousand gnomes. As Peter and Ruggedo discuss escaping the island, the Nonestic Ocean suddenly boils and rises into a terrifying wave.

CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

Peter and Ruggedo flee Ruggedo’s island when a sea quake turns the sea upside down and exposes the sea bottom. They cross the strange exposed seabed, find the wrecked pirate ship Blunderoo, and climb aboard just before another quake sends the seabed back underwater and throws the ship into the air. The ship survives but has no rudder, usable masts, or sails, so they drift on the Nonestic Ocean while Ruggedo plans to regain his kingdom and Peter hopes to return to Philadelphia. Peter feeds himself with small fish, and Ruggedo tells him about his magic belt, Ozma, and his past banishment.

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

Peter awakens aboard the Blunderoo and discovers a pirate treasure hold containing gold, silver, jewels, and a diary. The diary leads him to the magic casket of Soob, which contains the Flying Cloak of Invisibility, an emerald, and two mysterious onions. Ruggedo tries to use the cloak to reach the Emerald City, but it fails because it is torn, so he plans to have it mended and steal back his magic belt from Ozma. The Blunderoo reaches the shores of Ev, which Ruggedo identifies as his country, while Peter continues hoping to return to Philadelphia.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

Peter and Ruggedo reach the shores of Ev aboard the battered Blunderoo and return to the underground Kingdom of Gnomes. Ruggedo reclaims his crown from Kaliko, resumes rule, and orders the treasure unloaded, but learns that gnome magic cannot repair the magic cloak. They discover that the cloak must be mended in the Kingdom of Patch, then use the magic bulbs to cross the Deadly Desert and arrive in a peach orchard in Oz. Ruggedo and Peter head toward a yellow cottage to ask the way to Patch while planning to reach the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Peter and Ruggedo visit Kuma Party, a Winkie whose hands, feet, head, and arms can separate from his body. Kuma explains his come-apartishness and offers them shelter, but Ruggedo insists they continue toward Patch. Kuma lends them an umbrella, lantern, and guiding hand, which leads them through rain, woods, and a cotton field to Patch’s castle. At the castle, Kuma’s hand leaves Peter a note and escapes, while Ruggedo claims that his cloak needs mending so they can enter.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Scraps is astonished to find Ruggedo, the former King of the Gnomes, in the Patchwork castle seeking to have his magic cloak mended. Scrapper allows the cloak to be repaired and accepts Peter as payment after Ruggedo falsely identifies him as a slave. Once the cloak is mended, Ruggedo uses it to vanish, despite Peter’s attempt to send him to Zamagoochie. Scraps, Peter, and Grumpy then discuss Ruggedo’s threat to Ozma and the Emerald City and begin considering how to escape from the castle.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy plan to escape the castle in Patch after Peter reads Kuma's note promising help. They discover the Sandman's Nap Sack, use it to put the Scissor Bird to sleep, and summon Kuma's hand to unlock the doors and fight off the Quilties. Kuma's hand carries them out of Patch and helps them reach a maple grove, where it leaves them to continue toward the Emerald City. Scraps declares that she will no longer be Queen of Patch, even though Grumpy says the kingdom will fall apart in four days without her.

CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy continue their journey to the Emerald City and accidentally slide down Soap Mountain into a village made entirely of soap. The Suds take them to their ruler, Shampoozle, the Sultan of Suds, who plans to turn them into soap products. They escape by jumping from a window and run along the shore of a lake. Peter discovers that soap floats, so they build a raft from a large soap slab and sail away.

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy cross the Sudsy lake and become lost in the Bewilderness, where trees, rocks, bushes, and sand move around them. They meet Ozwold, an Oztrich guarding his enormous egg, and persuade him to carry them toward the Emerald City while they transport the egg. Peter considers using the egg to frighten Ruggedo, but promises to keep it safe. At the Emerald City’s yellow wall, Scraps plays an upright piano blocking the opening, causing it to fall backward and then snap upright after they pass through.

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Scraps, Peter, Grumpy, and Ozwold fall into Tune Town, where the bandmaster explains that everyone must sing, dance, and obey the town’s musical laws. They meet Queen Jazzma, perform songs, and visit the Viol Inn, but find that its food consists of leather music rolls rather than dinner. Scraps discovers that the travelers can escape by singing out of tune, so their discordant “Ah” causes the inhabitants to flee and sends them outside the town. The travelers then choose the center road and continue onward, still hungry.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Peter, Grumpy, Scraps, and Ozwold travel rapidly toward the Emerald City after learning that Ruggedo has escaped and plans to steal all the Oz magic and rule Oz. Along the way, Peter and Grumpy eat umbrella melons, and Ozwold worries about protecting his egg child. Ozwold runs over a bookman, who offers them stories but does not know the way to the Emerald City. Scraps eventually spots the yellow brick road and directs Ozwold toward the Emerald City’s golden gate.

CHAPTER 15 Wumbo, Wonder Worker, At Home

Wumbo the Wonder Worker lives in a crystal cavern in the Zamagoochie Country and secretly studies magic after Ozma forbids its practice. He uses a speech-giving chant on his furniture and discovers that an invisible Ruggedo is trapped in his arm-chair. Wumbo makes Ruggedo visible and learns that he plans to go to the Emerald City, though Ruggedo claims he is going there to report a treasure ship. Wumbo falls asleep before he can warn Ozma, allowing Ruggedo to use his magic cloak and escape toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

Wumbo awakens to discover that Ruggedo has escaped and sends a message to Kuma Party for help. Kuma Party dispatches his right arm and hand to aid the small mortal boy in escaping the Kingdom of Patch, while sending his left arm to Wumbo for instructions. Wumbo directs Kuma Party's arm to go to the Emerald City, warn Ozma, and capture Ruggedo, who is wearing a cloak of invisibility. After sending the message, Wumbo comforts himself with a magically produced meal and musical instruments.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Dorothy organizes a party to welcome Ozma back to the Emerald City after Ozma spends the night with Glinda. Ruggedo hides in the palace with his invisible cloak, planning to seize Ozma’s magic belt and destroy the city and its inhabitants. His invisible mischief causes Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Cowardly Lion, and Sir Hokus to quarrel, while preparations for the party continue. When Ozma returns, Kuma Party’s hand appears with a warning, and Scraps arrives on an oztrich warning everyone about the Gnome King.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

Ruggedo infiltrates Ozma’s banquet party while invisible and tries to steal her magic belt, but the oztrich egg initially prevents him from touching it. Scraps tells Ozma and the others about her and Peter’s adventures, and the Wizard of Oz warns everyone to guard the magic treasures. When the oztrich egg hatches, its protective power ends, allowing Ruggedo to seize the magic belt, wishing pills, and magic box. Ruggedo then threatens to send everyone to the bottom of the Nonestic Ocean in revenge.

CHAPTER 19 The Wizard Makes The Gnome King Visible

Ruggedo threatens to use the magic belt to transport the people, but Peter throws objects at him and finally hits him with the Silence Stone. The Wizard of Oz uses a powder to break the magic cloak's spell, making Ruggedo visible, while the Silence Stone prevents him from speaking for seven years. The Wizard removes the magic belt and returns the box of mixed magic and the wishing pills to Ozma, leaving Ruggedo harmless. Peter is praised for saving the day, and the celebration at the Emerald Palace continues through the night.

CHAPTER 20 Peter Is Made A Prince Of Oz

Peter is celebrated throughout the Emerald City after helping rescue it from Ruggedo and is offered a position as a prince by Ozma. He declines because he is a pitcher and feels loyal to his teammates and grandfather, asking instead to be transported to Philadelphia. Ozma sends him there with two bags of the pirate’s gold, which turn out to be real, and Peter is welcomed by cheering boys. Back in Oz, Ruggedo is captured, Kaliko resumes ruling the Gnomes, and Ruggedo is later freed after being made to forget his past wickedness.
