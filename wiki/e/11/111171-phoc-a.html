Phocæa

Abstract written by a model

Phocæa, a place, had no city before the Ionians came to Asia Minor. Its people came from Phocis with Philogenes and Damon, gained territory by agreement with Cyme rather than war, then accepted kings from Codrus’s descendants to join the Pan-Ionic confederacy.

Phocæa is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER III.

Phocæa had no city before the Ionians came to Asia Minor. Its people came from the country under Mount Parnassus with Philogenes and Damon, obtained territory by agreement with the people of Cyme, and accepted kings from the descendants of Codrus in order to join the Pan-Ionic confederacy.
