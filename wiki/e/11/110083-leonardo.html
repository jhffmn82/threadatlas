Leonardo

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: my lover, the strongman, this man Leonardo

Abstract written by a model

Leonardo, a professional-acrobat strongman in Ronder's show and Eugenia Ronder's lover, devises a plot to murder Ronder and makes and uses a spiked club against him. When Sahara King emerges, he flees, abandoning Eugenia under the lion's claws. She never hears from him again; he later drowns while bathing near Margate.

Leonardo is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

Leonardo was the strongman in Ronder's show and Eugenia Ronder's lover. He devised the murder plan and struck Ronder with the spiked club, but lost his nerve and fled when Sahara King came out of the cage.

We sat in silence for some time after the unhappy woman had told her

Leonardo deserted the unnamed woman after leaving her under the lion's claws, and she says she never saw or heard from him again. He was drowned last month while bathing near Margate.
