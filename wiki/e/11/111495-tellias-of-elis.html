BOOK X.--PHOCIS. / CHAPTER I.

Tellias of Elis was the Phocians' seer and commander in chief, and their hopes for safety rested on him. He helped secure the Phocian victory and later devised the night attack in which the Phocians smeared themselves and their armour with plaster.
