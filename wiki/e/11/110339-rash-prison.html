Rash prison in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Rash prison is an overcrowded prison with golden gates, high walls, and a marble-paved courtyard. Hasha is sent to share a cell with the Pasha's grand uncle, prompting concern over prisoner disposal. Later, its concealed loose paving block reveals a damp underground chamber where the sad singer hides.

Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER 1

The Rash prison is already overcrowded, with no room for another prisoner. Hasha is sent there to share a cell with the Pasha's grand uncle, and Fizzenpop warns that another way must be found to dispose of prisoners.

CHAPTER 3

The Rash prison has golden gates, high walls, and a marble-paved courtyard without an obvious escape route. A loose paving block conceals a small damp underground chamber where the sad singer hides.
