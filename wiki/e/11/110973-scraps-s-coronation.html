Scraps's coronation in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Scraps's coronation follows the Quilties acclaiming her ruler at the patched palace, where she receives a round sewing-basket coronet and old-spool crown jewels, then enters the castle amid cheers.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

Scraps's coronation takes place at the patched palace after the Quilties welcome her as their ruler. She receives a coronet made from a round sewing basket and crown jewels made from a string of old spools, then enters the castle amid cheers.
