CHAPTER XX.

In one account, the people of Tanagra filled a bowl with wine for Triton, and he drank it, fell asleep, and tumbled down the rocks. A man of Tanagra then struck off his head with an axe; because Triton was captured while drunk, they thought he was killed by Dionysus.
