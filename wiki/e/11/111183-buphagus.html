Abstract written by a model

The Buphagus rises at Buphagium, forms the boundary between Megalopolis and Heræa at its sources, and flows into the Alpheus.

Buphagus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
