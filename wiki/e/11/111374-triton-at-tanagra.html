Triton at Tanagra

Abstract written by a model

Triton at Tanagra is a figure represented by a notable statue at the temple of Dionysus. Said to attack women, ambush herds, and plunder vessels, he is defeated after drinking wine, falling asleep, and being beheaded by a man of Tanagra.

Triton at Tanagra is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XX.

Triton is represented by a notable statue at the temple of Dionysus. One tradition says he attacked the women of Tanagra and was defeated by Dionysus; another says he ambushed herds, plundered small vessels, drank wine given to him, fell asleep, and was killed after a man of Tanagra struck off his head with an axe.
