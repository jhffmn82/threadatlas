CHAPTER XXXIV.

The Antiquarians of the Peloponnese said that this pursuit occurred before Orestes’s trial before the Areopagus. They said that Perilaus, rather than the deceased Tyndareus, accused Orestes and sought vengeance for the murder of his kinswoman Clytæmnestra.
