Neda, a notably winding river, rises on Cerausius, receives Lymax near Eurynome, and flows to the sea, navigable there for small craft.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLI.

The Neda rises on Mount Cerausius, part of Mount Lycaeus, and flows into the sea, where it is navigable for small craft. The Lymax joins it near the temple of Eurynome.
