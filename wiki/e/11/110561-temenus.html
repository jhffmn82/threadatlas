Temenus

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Temenus consulted the oracle, prepared the army, built ships at Naupactus, received Argos in the division of the Peloponnese, and was murdered by his sons. In Description of Greece, Temenus occupied and fortified Temenium, appointed Deiphontes his general and associate, gave him his daughter Hyrnetho, and was killed by his sons.

Abstract written by a model

builds Naupactus ships [in Locris] Temenus and his brothers consult the oracle about their return. He blames the god for their earlier misfortunes, then prepares the army, builds ships at Naupactus, and later receives Argos in the division of the conquered Peloponnese. His sons arrange his murder because he favors Hyrnetho and Deiphontes.

Temenus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · about AD 155 to 175 · the work’s page · read it where Temenus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Temenus, son of Aristomachus, occupied and fortified Temenium against Tisamenus and the Achæans. He made Deiphontes his general and associate, gave him his beloved daughter Hyrnetho, and seemed likely to name them heirs. His sons killed him over that suspicion.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

Temenus appointed Deiphontes general in his battles, made him his associate in all things, and gave him his daughter Hyrnetho in marriage. Because he seemed likely to make Hyrnetho and Deiphontes his heirs, his sons killed him.
