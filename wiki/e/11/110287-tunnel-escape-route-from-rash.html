CHAPTER 7

The tunnel opens when the cavern wall gives way after the Rash barber pounds on it. It is dark, damp, slippery, and filled with steep slides, loops, bends, and curves before releasing the escapees into the open. The Hungry Tiger and Prince Evered later emerge from it as well.
