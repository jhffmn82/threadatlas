Dryopes in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Dryopes were neighbours of the Lycoritæ near Mount Parnassus, named for their founder; defeated by Hercules, their descendants settled in the Peloponnese and Styra, while Asinæi retained their name, temples, and mysteries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

The Dryopes were originally neighbours of the Lycoritæ near Mount Parnassus and were named after their founder. After their defeat by Hercules, different groups of their descendants settled in the Peloponnese and at Styra in Eubœa. The people of Asine proudly retain the name Dryopes and preserve temples and mysteries connected with Dryops.
