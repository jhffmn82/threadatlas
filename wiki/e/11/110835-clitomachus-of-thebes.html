Clitomachus of Thebes in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Clitomachus of Thebes, son of Hermocrates, was a pancratium victor and athlete. He won wrestling, boxing, and pancratium at the Isthmian games and three Pythian pancratium victories. After Olympic defeats to Theagenes, he won pancratium in the 141st Olympiad, then lost to Caprus after requesting the contest precede boxing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII.

Clitomachus won wrestling, boxing, and pancratium prizes at the Isthmian games and three pancratium victories at the Pythian games. At Olympia he was second to Theagenes of Thasos in boxing and the pancratium before winning the pancratium in the 141st Olympiad; he later lost that contest to Caprus after asking that it be held before boxing battered him.
