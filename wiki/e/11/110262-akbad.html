Akbad

person · 1 work · 25 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Akbad the Soothsayer, I, Akbad the Soothsayer, Soothsayer, old Islander, the Court Soothsayer, the Soothsayer, the great ugly bird-man, the wily Soothsayer

Abstract written by a model

Akbad is a court soothsayer from the Ozure Isles who possesses magic wings and a descriptionary. Rejecting Cheeriobed's inaction, he decides to obtain a mortal maiden for Quiberon. He plucks the golden pear from the sacred tree, which becomes golden wings that carry him to the Emerald City of Oz. There, appearing as a great ugly bird-man, he uses a picture to identify Trot, seizes her, and carries her away despite Benny's resistance. He transports Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow to the Ozure Isles, tells Trot she was chosen to save the islands by serving Quiberon, and leaves. Back in the King's garden, he lies about carrying Philador and leaving a maiden for Quiberon. The wings cannot be removed and allow him to fly only as far as the shore. Later, hiding because he cannot explain Ozma's absence and the Prince's disappearance, he overcomes fear when Quiberon attacks Queen Orin, rescues her, and carries her to the sapphire castle. Philador exposes Akbad's lies; Akbad is dismissed from court, pardoned, and given a cottage.

Akbad is in one work on this site, The Giant Horse of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

Akbad rejects Cheeriobed's inaction and decides to obtain a mortal maiden for Quiberon. He plans to use the golden pear to travel to the Emerald City of Oz, plucks it from the sacred tree, and is carried south by the golden wings it becomes.

CHAPTER 4

Akbad arrives as a great ugly bird-man and identifies Trot as the maiden he is seeking. He seizes Trot, spreads his golden wings, and carries her into the air despite Benny's attempt to hold him back.

CHAPTER 5

Akbad carries Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow from the Emerald City to the Ozure Isles. He tells Trot that she has been chosen to save the Ozure Isles by serving Quiberon, then flies away.

CHAPTER 6

Akbad returns to the King's garden wearing the golden wings and lies that he carried Philador to the Emerald City and left a mortal maiden in the cave of Quiberon. He later discovers that the wings will not come off and that he cannot fly beyond the shore of the island.

CHAPTER 16

Akbad hides from Cheeriobed because he cannot explain Ozma's failure to appear or the disappearance of the little Prince. When Quiberon attacks Queen Orin, Akbad overcomes his fear, flies at the monster, rescues the Queen, and carries her to the sapphire castle.

CHAPTER 18

Akbad is identified as the Soothsayer who claimed to have picked the golden pear, carried Philador to the Emerald City, and summoned Ozma's help. Philador's account shows that Akbad did not actually carry him to the capital, and Akbad is dismissed from court and given a cottage after being pardoned.
