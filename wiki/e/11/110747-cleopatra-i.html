Cleopatra I in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Cleopatra I, mother of Ptolemy VI Philometor and Alexander, opposed her eldest son's succession, promoted Alexander through a fabricated plot, and was eventually killed by him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Cleopatra I disliked her eldest son Ptolemy VI Philometor and sought to have Alexander, his younger brother, chosen king. She fabricated a plot against her by mutilating eunuchs, but was eventually killed by Alexander.
