Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

The Ozolian Locrians occupied the region around Amphissa, Myonia, Œanthea, and Naupactus. The text gives several traditions for their name, and says that the inhabitants of Amphissa joined themselves to the Ætolians because they were ashamed of the title Ozolian.
