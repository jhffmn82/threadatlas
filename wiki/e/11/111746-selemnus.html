Selemnus is a river near Argyra flowing into the sea, transformed by Aphrodite from a love-stricken figure and granted forgetfulness; its water cures love.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Selemnus is a river flowing into the sea near Argyra. The local tradition says that Aphrodite transformed Selemnus, who was dying for love of Argyra, into a river and later granted him forgetfulness of her; its water was also said to cure love.
