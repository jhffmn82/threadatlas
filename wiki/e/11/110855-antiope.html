Antiope in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Antiope, Nycteus’s daughter and reputedly linked to Asopus, was renowned for beauty. Taken or sought in marriage by Epopeus, she was taken to Thebes, bore Zethus and Amphion near Eleutheræ, and became a statue at Aphrodite’s Sicyon temple.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Antiope, the daughter of Nycteus and reputedly connected with the river Asopus, was renowned for her beauty. Epopeus took or sought to marry her, and while being taken to Thebes she gave birth near Eleutheræ to Zethus and Amphion.
