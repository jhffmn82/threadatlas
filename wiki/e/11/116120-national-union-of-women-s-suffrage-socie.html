Abstract written by a model

The National Union of Women’s Suffrage Societies adopted the Scottish Women’s Hospitals plan on 15 October 1914. Through its London Branch, it helped raise money for new units, supported Elsie Maud Inglis’s mission to Serbia, and organized a Scottish Women’s Hospitals unit for the Serbian Army.
