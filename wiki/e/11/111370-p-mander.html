Abstract written by a model

Pœmander, son of Chæresilaus, founded Tanagra and married Tanagra; his lineage extends through Iasius and Eleuther to Apollo and Æthusa.

Pœmander is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XX.

Pœmander is identified as the founder of Tanagra. He was the son of Chæresilaus, the son of Iasius, the son of Eleuther, who was the son of Apollo by Æthusa, the daughter of Poseidon; Pœmander married Tanagra.
