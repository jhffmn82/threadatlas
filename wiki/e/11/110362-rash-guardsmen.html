Rash army in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Rash army, guardsmen serving Rash, advances at Irashi's whistle but is terrified by Reddy's giant appearance and companions' attacks, flees, surrenders swords, and is defeated.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 19

The Rash army advances in response to Irashi's whistle. Reddy's giant appearance, together with the attacks of his companions, terrifies the soldiers into fleeing, and they surrender their swords as they escape.

Rash Guardsmen in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Rash Guardsmen, guards of Rash, overpower Carter and Betsy on Pasha Irashi's order, carry them across the lawn, and imprison them by throwing them over the prison wall.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

The Rash Guardsmen overpower Carter and Betsy after Pasha Irashi orders their arrest. They drag Carter loose from the ground, carry the prisoners across the lawn, and throw them over the prison wall.
