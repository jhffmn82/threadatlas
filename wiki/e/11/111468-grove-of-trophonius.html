Grove of Trophonius in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Grove of Trophonius is a place some distance from Lebadea. Its notable features include the temple of Trophonius, Demeter Europa’s temple, Zeus Hyetius, Proserpine’s Chase, Zeus the King’s temple, temples of Cronos, Hera, Zeus, and Apollo, and Arcesilaus’s tomb.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIX.

The grove of Trophonius lies some distance from Lebadea and contains the temple of Trophonius, a temple of Demeter called Europa, a statue of Zeus Hyetius, Proserpine’s Chase, a temple of Zeus the King, temples of Cronos, Hera, Zeus, and Apollo, and the tomb of Arcesilaus.
