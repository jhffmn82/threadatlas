Ilissus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A river of Attica, sacred to the Muses and several gods, receives Eridanus, marks Orithyia’s abduction by the North Wind, and borders Herodes’s white-marble stadium.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The Ilissus was one of the rivers of Attica and was said to be the place where Orithyia was carried off by the North Wind. The Athenians considered it sacred to several gods, and the river bordered the white-marble stadium built by Herodes.
