Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The sixteen matrons preside over the Hera Festival, weave Hera’s shawl, and conduct two dances called Physcoa’s dance and Hippodamia’s. They were originally selected from sixteen cities to arbitrate disputes between Elis and Pisa, and in the narrator’s time two are chosen from each of the eight tribes.
