Kuma's note

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Kuma’s note, crumpled letter, letter, note, note of warning, note of warning for Ozma, paper, warning note for Ozma
  • In The Gnome King of Oz, Kuma's note is carried by Kuma Party, lets Peter write directions and toss it into the air for help, and is used by Peter to request escape from Patch's palace before disappearing before Kuma arrives.
  • In The Gnome King of Oz, the warning note for Ozma is tightly clasped in Kuma Party's hand, which carries it through the palace while searching for Ozma, who is not present when it first arrives.

Kuma's note in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Kuma's note, carried by Kuma Party, lets Peter write directions and toss it into the air for help; Peter uses it to request escape from Patch's palace, and it disappears before Kuma arrives.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Kuma's note says that Peter can write directions on it and toss it into the air whenever he needs a helping hand. Peter writes a request for help escaping the palace of Patch, and the note disappears before Kuma's hand arrives.
