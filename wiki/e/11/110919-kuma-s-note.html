Kuma's note

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Kuma’s note, crumpled letter, letter, note, note of warning, note of warning for Ozma, paper, warning note for Ozma

Abstract written by a model

In The Gnome King of Oz, Kuma's note is carried by Kuma Party, lets Peter write directions and toss it into the air for help, and is used by Peter to request escape from Patch's palace before disappearing before Kuma arrives. In The Gnome King of Oz, the warning note for Ozma is tightly clasped in Kuma Party's hand, which carries it through the palace while searching for Ozma, who is not present when it first arrives.
