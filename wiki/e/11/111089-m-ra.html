1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLVIII.

Mæra, the wife of Tegeates, has a tomb in the market-place at Tegea. She is said to have been the daughter of Atlas and is mentioned by Homer in Odysseus’ account of the souls he saw in Hades.
