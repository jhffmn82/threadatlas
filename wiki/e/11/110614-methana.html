CHAPTER XXXII.

Methana was a small town on an isthmus jutting into the sea, with temples of Isis and statues of Hermes and Hercules. About thirty stades away were warm, very salty baths that appeared after fire bubbled from the ground during the reign of Antigonus, son of Demetrius.
