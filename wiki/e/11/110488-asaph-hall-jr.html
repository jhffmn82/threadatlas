Asaph Hall, Jr. in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Asaph Hall, Jr., son of Asaph Hall and Angeline Hall, was the eldest listed son and one of four boys raised at 18 Gay Street. A favorite child, he was precocious, active, talkative, helpful, and exceptionally diligent; he later attended college.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XII.

Asaph Hall, Jr. was a favorite child who was described as precocious, active, talkative, and eager to help with housework. He enjoyed clam bakes and oranges, and once wandered toward Boston to buy oranges before being found.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

Asaph Hall was one of the four boys raised at 18 Gay Street. When he was away at college, Percival said that there were five boys in the family counting Asaph.
