The Ilians were Trojans who fled the Libyans to Sardinia’s rocky mountains, where protection from Carthaginians preserved their freedom; they remained Ilians and resembled Libyans in appearance, armour, and life.

CHAPTER XVII.

The Ilians were Trojans who occupied Sardinia’s rocky and difficult-to-access mountains after fleeing the Libyans. They were still called Ilians in the author’s time and resembled the Libyans in appearance, armour, and way of life. The Carthaginians did not reduce them to slavery because the mountains protected them.
