the pistol hidden in Miss Dunbar's wardrobe

Abstract written by a model

A pistol, one of Mr. Neil Gibson’s pair, is found beneath Miss Dunbar’s dresses during a police search. Though she denies ever seeing it, its conspicuous concealment and the missing matching weapon lead Holmes to conclude that Mrs. Gibson placed it in the wardrobe after firing one barrel, intending to incriminate Miss Dunbar.
