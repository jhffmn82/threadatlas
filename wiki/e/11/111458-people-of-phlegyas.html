Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVI.

The people of Phlegyas separated from the other Orchomenians, attracted neighboring peoples, and led an army against Delphi to plunder its temple. They were defeated after Philammon and some Argives came against them, and they were later overthrown by lightning, earthquakes, and an epidemic, with only a few escaping to Phocis.
