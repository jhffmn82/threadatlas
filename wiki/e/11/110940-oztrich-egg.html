oztrich egg in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

An enormous egg containing a baby oztrich before hatching, it lies beside Ozwold, is carried safely on his back, and protects Ozma’s magic treasures by deterring Ruggedo. Its protective power ends when it hatches.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Ozwold's egg is enormous and lies beside him in the sand. Peter carefully carries it on Ozwold's back, and he and Scraps discuss using it to frighten Ruggedo, though Peter decides to keep it safe for Ozwold.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The oztrich egg protects Ozma’s magic treasures because Ruggedo dares not touch them while it is near. Its power ends when the egg hatches.
