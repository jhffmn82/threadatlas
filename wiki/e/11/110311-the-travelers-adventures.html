The travelers' adventures are a journey whose goal is Reddy's return to the throne. Betsy, Reddy, Carter Green, and the Hungry Tiger travel to Rash, meet Kaliko, face Irashi's wrongdoing, fire-fall, Immense City, and Giants, while Prince Evered loses his throne and rubies; they later recount these events to Ozma and Atmos.
