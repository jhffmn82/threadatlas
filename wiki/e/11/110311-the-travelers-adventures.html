the travelers' adventures in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The travelers' adventures are a journey whose goal is Reddy's return to the throne. Betsy, Reddy, Carter Green, and the Hungry Tiger travel to Rash, meet Kaliko, face Irashi's wrongdoing, fire-fall, Immense City, and Giants, while Prince Evered loses his throne and rubies; they later recount these events to Ozma and Atmos.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

The travelers' adventures include the Hungry Tiger's being carried to Rash, Betsy's meeting with Carter Green, their arrival in the same kingdom, and Prince Evered's loss of his throne and rubies. Betsy recounts these events to Kaliko after the group reaches his castle.

CHAPTER 18

Betsy, Reddy, Carter Green, and the Hungry Tiger recount adventures involving their journey to Rash, the discovery of the Hungry Tiger, Irashi's wrongdoing, Down Town, Kaliko, the fire-fall, Immense City, and the Giants. Ozma and Atmos listen with astonishment.
