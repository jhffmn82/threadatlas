Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXX.

Iaseus was very friendly with Phocus and offered him various gifts, including a stone signet-ring set in gold. In Polygnotus’ painting, he is shown trying to take the ring from Phocus’ left hand as a memorial of their friendship.
