Lycortas in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Lycortas, an Arcadian second to none in merit and friend of Philopœmen, was an Achæan general. He defended Achæan claims before the council while covertly criticizing the Romans, then led Arcadian and Achæan forces against Messenia; its popular party joined him, and Philopœmen’s murderers were executed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

Lycortas, described as second to none among the Arcadians and as friendly with Philopœmen, defended the just claims of the Achæans before the council. In doing so, he also covertly criticized the Romans.

CHAPTER LI.

Lycortas led an Achæan army to ravage Messenia and later marched against it with forces from Arcadia and Achaia. The popular party in Messene joined him, and those involved in Philopœmen’s murder were put to death.
