Charlotte Ingalls in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Charlotte Ingalls, formerly Charlotte Stickney, was Angeline’s sister who married Edwin Ingalls, lived in Wilna, and became a capable, thrifty tavern hostess by 1842.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Charlotte Ingalls, formerly Charlotte Stickney, married young and later lived with Angeline in Wilna. She kept tavern with Edwin Ingalls and was described as a capable, thrifty hostess who cooked meat before a great open fire.
