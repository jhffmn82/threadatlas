Gorgus the son of Aristomenes in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Gorgus, son of Aristomenes, was a uniquely four-time Olympic pentathlon victor who also won the double course and heavy-armour race. He defended Eira, led retreating Messenians, women, and children, and later led their Sicilian settlement.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

Gorgus was among the first defenders to reach the fighting at Eira. Aristomenes appointed him, together with Manticlus, to lead the Messenians, women, and children during their retreat, and later he became one of the leaders of the Messenian settlement in Sicily.
