Eugenia Ronder

Abstract written by a model

Eugenia Ronder, formerly Mrs. Ronder, is a former circus girl and lodger of Mrs. Merrilow who lives veiled and secluded in South Brixton after Sahara King permanently mutilated her face. Wasting away in poor health, she sends Sherlock Holmes a blue bottle and confesses her past. Abused by Ronder, she loved Leonardo and helped plan Ronder’s killing, but kept silent to protect Leonardo and her reputation. Leonardo deserted her after the attack, yet she could not condemn him. She considers suicide, then sends prussic acid while indicating she will follow Holmes’s advice.

Eugenia Ronder is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

Eugenia Ronder, formerly Mrs. Ronder, has lived veiled and isolated for seven years because Sahara King tore apart her face. She confesses that she helped Leonardo plan Ronder's killing, but concealed the truth because Leonardo's fate and her own reputation depended on her silence.

We sat in silence for some time after the unhappy woman had told her

The unnamed woman says that Leonardo deserted her after the lion attacked her, though she could not bring herself to send him to the gallows. She reveals that the lion left her face horribly disfigured and says she may have lost the five-clawed club in a chalk-pit pool. After Holmes urges her not to take her own life, she sends him a bottle of prussic acid with a message indicating that she will follow his advice.
