inheritance law favoring the Emperor in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A law governing Roman citizens whose sons were reckoned as Greeks directed inheritances away from sons toward unrelated people or the Emperor; Antonine later allowed property to be left to those sons.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLIII.

The inheritance law favoring the Emperor required certain Roman citizens whose sons were reckoned as Greeks either to leave their money to unrelated people or to let it increase the Emperor's wealth. Antonine allowed them to leave their property to their sons instead.
