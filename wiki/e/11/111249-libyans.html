Libyans in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Libyans are described as early colonists of Sardinia under Sardus, coexisting with its original inhabitants and lacking cities. They later returned in greater force, defeated Greeks, drove Trojans into the mountains, and, after disputes with Carthaginians over spoil, settled in mountainous areas as Balari.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The Libyans were said to have been the first people to cross into Sardinia under Sardus, though they joined rather than expelled the original inhabitants. They later crossed into the island again with a larger force, fought the Greeks, destroyed all but a remnant, and drove the Trojans into the mountains. Libyans or Iberes allied with the Carthaginians later quarrelled over the spoil and settled in the mountainous parts, where they were called Balari.
