Mnesibulus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Mnesibulus was a victorious defender and runner: after winning several contests, including the stadium and double course carrying his shield, he led picked men against the Costoboci at Elatea, killed many, and died in battle; a brazen statue commemorated him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Mnesibulus gathered an army of picked men when the Costoboci came to Elatea, slew many of the barbarians, and died in the battle. He had won several victories and, in the 235th Olympiad, won both the stadium and the double course while carrying his shield; a brazen statue of him stood near the race-course.
