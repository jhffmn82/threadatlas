Lusius

Also called: Gortynius

Abstract written by a model

Lusius is a river flowing by Gortys, locally named from a tradition that Zeus was washed there after his birth. Distant people call it Gortynius. Noted for extreme summer coldness, it rises at Thisoa and joins Alpheus at Rhæteæ.

Lusius is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Lusius is the local name for the river flowing by Gortys, named from the tradition that Zeus was washed there after his birth. People living farther away call it Gortynius, and it is described as one of the coldest streams, rising at Thisoa and joining the Alpheus at Rhæteæ.
