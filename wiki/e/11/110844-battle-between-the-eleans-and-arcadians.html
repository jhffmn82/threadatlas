Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

During the battle between the Eleans and Arcadians, a woman offered her baby to help Elis according to a dream. The child became a dragon, frightening the Arcadians and allowing the Eleans to pursue them and win a notable victory; a monument to the Arcadians slain in the battle stood on a hill west of the Cladeus.
