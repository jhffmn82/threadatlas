Abstract written by a model

Andreus, son of the river Peneus, first dwells at Orchomenus, whose land is called Andreis; urged by Athamas, he marries Euippe and has Eteocles, and is represented by an armored statue.

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Andreus, the son of the river Peneus, was said to have been the first to dwell at Orchomenus, whose country was then called Andreis. He married Euippe, the daughter of Leucon, at Athamas’s urging, and had a son, Eteocles.
