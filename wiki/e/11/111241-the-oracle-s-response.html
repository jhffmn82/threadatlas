Also called: oracular response, the following response

Abstract written by a model

The oracle's response identifies Black Demeter’s recess and directs the people of Phigalia to seek relief from famine there, warning of cannibalism unless they appease Demeter through public libations and restored divine honours.

CHAPTER XLII.

The oracle's response told the Arcadians to go to the secret cave of the horse-bearing Demeter and seek relief from famine. It warned that they would soon eat one another and their children unless they appeased Demeter with public libations and restored divine honours to the cave.
