Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLII.

The oracle's response told the Arcadians to go to the secret cave of the horse-bearing Demeter and seek relief from famine. It warned that they would soon eat one another and their children unless they appeased Demeter with public libations and restored divine honours to the cave.
