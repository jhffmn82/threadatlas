CHAPTER XVI.

The Roman army under Mummius numbered 3,500 cavalry and 22,000 infantry, with Cretan bowmen and troops brought from Attalus. It defeated the Achæans after Roman cavalry routed the opposing cavalry and 1,000 picked Roman infantry attacked the Achæan infantry from the flank.
