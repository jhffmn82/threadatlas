CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Kuma Party’s come-apartishness allows his body parts to move independently and perform tasks while he rests. His hands can gather fruit, his head can seek news, and his parts can assist others; his arm carries an umbrella and lantern for Peter and Ruggedo and guides them to Patch.
