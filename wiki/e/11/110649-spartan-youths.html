CHAPTER XIV.

The Spartan youths practise running at Dromus and conduct their fights at Platanistas. Before the fights, each division sacrifices a puppy dog to Enyalius, then pits tame boars against one another; the victorious boar’s party is generally victorious at Platanistas.
