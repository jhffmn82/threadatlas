Abstract written by a model

Tricoloni, named after Tricolonus, was formerly a town founded by the sons of Lycaon; it retains Poseidon's temple, statue, and grove on a hill, linked by roads to ruined settlements.

Tricoloni is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXV.

Tricoloni was formerly a town founded by the sons of Lycaon. It still has a temple and a square statue of Poseidon on its hill, with a grove of trees around the temple, and it is connected by roads with several ruined settlements.
