Abstract written by a model

The statue of Athene is a full-length ivory-and-gold object. It wears a foot-length tunic and Medusa’s ivory-worked head on its breast, holds a four-cubit Victory and a spear, has a shield and possible dragon Erichthonius, and stands on a base depicting Pandora’s birth.

CHAPTER XXIV.

The statue of Athene is full length and made of ivory and gold. It has a tunic reaching to the feet, Medusa’s head on the breast, a four-cubit Victory in one hand, a spear in the other, a shield at the feet, and a dragon near the spear that may be Erichthonius; its base depicts Pandora’s birth.
