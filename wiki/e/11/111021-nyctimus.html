Nyctimus

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Νυκτίμου, Νύκτιμον
  • In The Library, Nyctimus was the youngest of Lycaon’s sons, was spared when Zeus destroyed Lycaon and the other sons, succeeded to the kingdom, and reigned during the flood of Deucalion.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Nyctimus was Lycaon’s eldest son, succeeded to all his power, and gave money and men to his brother Œnotrus, enabling him to sail to Italy.
