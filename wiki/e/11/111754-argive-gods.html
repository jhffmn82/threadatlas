Argive gods

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

The Argive gods were brazen statues of Poseidon, Hercules, Zeus, and Athene at Ægium, receiving daily sacrifices; made at Argos, they were deposited at Ægium and remained after the Argives could not repay sacrificial costs.

CHAPTER XXIII.

At Ægium, brazen statues of Poseidon, Hercules, Zeus, and Athene were called the Argive gods. The Argive tradition said they had been made at Argos, while the people of Ægium said the Argives had deposited them there; the statues remained at Ægium after the Argives could not repay the sacrifices made for them.
