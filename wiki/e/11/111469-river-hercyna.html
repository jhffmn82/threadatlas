CHAPTER XXXIX.

The River Hercyna is said to have sprung up when Proserpine took a stone from a cave while retrieving a goose. Its sources are in the cave, and a temple of Hercyna with the effigy of a maiden holding a goose stands on its banks; those preparing for the oracle bathe in the river.
