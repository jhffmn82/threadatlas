CHAPTER XXXII.

Creusis is a haven of the Thespians with no public monuments, but a private house contains a plaster statue of Dionysus decorated with a painting. The sea voyage from the Peloponnese to Creusis is circuitous and rough because of projecting promontories and strong winds from the mountains.
