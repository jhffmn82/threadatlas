THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

Impersonation of Lady Beatrice is proposed by Holmes as part of a hypothetical scheme following her possible murder. He suggests that Carrie Evans could help arrange for another woman to appear in Lady Beatrice's place while Sir Robert remained at the estate until after the Derby.

After Lady Beatrice's death, Mr. Norlett appeared as her for several weeks, mainly by riding daily in her carriage while Mrs. Norlett controlled access to her room. The black spaniel exposed the deception by reacting with fury when it encountered the disguised person.
