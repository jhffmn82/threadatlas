Abstract written by a model

Creugas, a boxer from Epidamnus, died when Damoxenus struck under his ribs and tore out his bowels; afterward Argives awarded him Nemea victory and erected a statue in Argos.

CHAPTER XL.

Creugas was a boxer from Epidamnus who died after Damoxenus struck him under the ribs and tore out his bowels. The Argives nevertheless awarded him victory at Nemea and erected a statue to him in Argos.
