Abstract written by a model

Percival Hall, born September 16, 1872, at 18 Gay Street, was the fourth and last child. Described as a real nice little boy, he lost and loosened front teeth in a street-car collision while coasting; Angeline found a tooth and the family dentist restored it. He learned most letters as a young child and later attended college with Angelo.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

Percival Hall was born at 18 Gay Street on September 16, 1872, as the fourth and last child. As a boy he collided with a street car while coasting, losing and loosening front teeth; Angeline Hall found the missing tooth and had the family dentist restore it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XVI.

Percival Hall is described as a young child who has learned most of his letters and later attends college with Angelo. Angeline writes to him about travel, packing a tent, retrieving belongings, furniture, bedclothes, college events, and invitations to friends.
