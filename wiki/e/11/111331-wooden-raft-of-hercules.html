CHAPTER V.

The wooden raft of Hercules was said to have sailed from Tyre and to have reached the Ionian sea after being moored at the promontory called Mid. The people of Erythræ and Chios struggled to bring it ashore until a rope made from the hair of Thracian women enabled them to do so.
