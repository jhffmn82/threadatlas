Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Marpessus was described as the second Herophile’s native place and as sacred to her mother. Its ruins remained on Trojan Ida, with about sixty inhabitants, and it was said to be 240 stades from Alexandria in the Troad.
