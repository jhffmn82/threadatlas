Stymphelus

Abstract written by a model

Stymphelus is a town whose people chose membership in the Argolic League despite Arcadian ancestry; it has a spring, an ancient temple of Stymphelian Artemis, and traditions of man-eating birds.

Stymphelus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXII.

Stymphelus is no longer ranked as Arcadian but belongs to the Argolic League by its own choice. Homer and the ancestry of its founder, Stymphelus, are said to testify that its people are Arcadian by race. The town has a spring, an ancient temple of Stymphelian Artemis, and traditions about man-eating birds.
