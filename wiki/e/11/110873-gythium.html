Gythium in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Gythium is a seaside town of the Eleutherolacones, one of their 18 cities, about 30 stades from Ægiæ, with Las on its right. Local tradition says Apollo and Hercules jointly built it after contesting the tripod. Its market-place has their statues, and it has sanctuaries of Dionysus, Carnean Apollo, Ammon, Æsculapius, Demeter, Poseidon, and Athene; nearby maritime Laconia supplies purple-dye shellfish.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

Gythium lies near the sea and is inhabited by the Eleutherolacones. Its people say that Hercules and Apollo jointly built the town after their contest for the tripod; the market-place contains statues of Apollo and Hercules, and the town also has sanctuaries of Dionysus, Carnean Apollo, Ammon, Æsculapius, Demeter, Poseidon, and Athene. Gythium is about 30 stades from Ægiæ and is one of the 18 cities of the Eleutherolacones.
