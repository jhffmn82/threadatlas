the golden pear

Also called: The Golden Pear, golden pear, pear, the golden fruit, the magic pear, the pear, the sacred bough, the stealing of the golden pear

Abstract written by a model

The golden pear is the single golden fruit on the emerald, enchanted tree in the King's garden, reserved for Prince Philador in extreme danger. When plucked, it can transport a prince wherever he desires. As Quiberon threatens the Kingdom, Toddledy proposes using it, but Cheeriobed hesitates. Akbad instead picks it while claiming to save Philador and the Ozure Isles; it becomes golden wings, and Cheeriobed delays punishing him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The golden pear is reserved for Prince Philador and may be picked only in times of extreme danger. Toddledy suggests it when Quiberon threatens the Kingdom, but Cheeriobed hesitates to use it.

CHAPTER 2

The golden pear is the single golden fruit borne by the emerald tree. It is meant to be plucked by the Prince in times of extreme danger and to transport him wherever he wishes, but when Akbad plucks it, it transforms into golden wings.

CHAPTER 6

Akbad admits that he picked the golden pear while trying to save Philador and the Ozure Isles. Cheeriobed decides not to punish him immediately for stealing it.
