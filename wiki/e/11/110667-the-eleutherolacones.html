the Eleutherolacones in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Eleutherolacones were a group inhabiting maritime Laconia, holding 18 cities. Emperor Augustus liberated them from rule imposed by the Lacedæmonians of Sparta. Their towns included Zarax and Brasiæ; Las joined their council despite Messenian inhabitants, and Zarax was ravaged after Cleonymus destroyed it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The Eleutherolacones inhabit the maritime parts of Laconia and possess 18 cities, including Gythium, Teuthrone, Las, Pyrrhichus, Cænepolis, Œtylus, Leuctra, Thalamæ, Alagonia, Gerenia, Asopus, Acriæ, Bœæ, Zarax, Epidaurus Limera, Brasiæ, Geronthræ, and Marius. The Emperor Augustus liberated them from the rule imposed by the Lacedæmonians of Sparta. Geronthræ and Marius are specifically said to belong to them.

CHAPTER XXIII.

The Eleutherolacones included towns such as Zarax and Brasiæ, and Las joined their council although its inhabitants were Messenians. Zarax was described as the most ravaged of their towns after Cleonymus destroyed it.
