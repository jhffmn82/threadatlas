Pisirodus, son of Callipatira and grandson of Diagoras, won the Olympic boys’ boxing prize; after his mother brought him disguised as a trainer, he received a statue near his maternal grandfather’s at Altis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Pisirodus, the son of Callipatira and grandson of Diagoras, won the Olympic boxing prize among boys. His mother disguised herself as a trainer and dressed him as a man to bring him to the games, and he had a statue near his maternal grandfather's statue at Olympia.
