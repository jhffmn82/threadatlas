Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

Menestratus was the lover of Cleostratus. He devised a contrivance by making Cleostratus a brazen breastplate whose plates had inward-bent hooks, enabling Cleostratus to kill the dragon.
