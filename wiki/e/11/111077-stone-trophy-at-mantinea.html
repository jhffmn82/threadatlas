Abstract written by a model

Mantineans erected a stone trophy after defeating the Lacedæmonians and Agis, dedicating it to Poseidon Hippius for appearing to help them.

CHAPTER X.

The Mantineans erected a stone trophy after their victory over the Lacedæmonians and Agis. They said it was a votive offering to Poseidon because he had appeared to help them.
