Abstract written by a model

Pyrrhichus is a town in the heart of the country, about forty stades inland from the river Scyras. Said to be named for Pyrrhus or the god of the Curetes, it preserves traditions of Silenus and temples of Artemis and Apollo Amazonius.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Pyrrhichus lies about forty stades inland from the river Scyras. It was said to have been named either after Pyrrhus, the son of Achilles, or after the god of the Curetes, and it also had traditions connecting it with Silenus; its temples included those of Artemis and Apollo Amazonius.
