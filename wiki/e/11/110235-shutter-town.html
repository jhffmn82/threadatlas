Shutter Town in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Shutter Town is a gloomy place of dingy blue-shuttered houses, box hedges, and stiff gardens; as High Boy passes through, hedges and flowers shut their lids, and the travelers see no Shutter Face.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15

Shutter Town consists of gloomy, dingy houses with blue-shuttered windows and doors, surrounded by box hedges and stiff little gardens. As High Boy passes through, the hedges and flowers slam their lids shut, and the travelers leave the town without seeing another Shutter Face.
