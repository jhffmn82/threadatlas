CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

In Tune Town, singing replaces speech and dancing replaces walking, and the inhabitants perform ordinary tasks to the music. Scraps, Peter, Grumpy, and Ozwold attempt to follow these rules until their deliberately discordant singing provides a way out.
