CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Queen Jazzma arrives dancing with a tom-tom cat and questions the travelers. She invites Scraps to become her maid in waiting, permits the travelers to eat at the Viol Inn, and takes part in the musical life of Tune Town.
