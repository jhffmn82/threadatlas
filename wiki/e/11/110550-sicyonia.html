Sicyonia

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: what is now Sicyonia
  • In Description of Greece, Sicyonia received its name after Sicyon became king, replacing the earlier name Ægialea, and later became part of Argolis after the Sicyonians became Dorians.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Sicyonia borders Achaia on the east, and its inhabitants attributed the ancient name Ægialus to Ægialeus, king of what was then Sicyonia.
