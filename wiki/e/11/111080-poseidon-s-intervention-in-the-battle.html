Poseidon’s intervention in the battle in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Poseidon reportedly appears to aid Mantinea against the Lacedæmonians and Agis; the Mantineans credit him with helping secure victory, likened to Apollo’s wartime assistance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

The Mantineans said that Poseidon appeared and helped them defeat the Lacedæmonians and Agis. The account considers that the victory may have been largely due to Poseidon, comparing it with traditions of divine beings assisting in other battles.
