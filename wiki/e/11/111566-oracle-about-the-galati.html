oracle about the Galati in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

An oracle foretells that the Galati will cross the Hellespont, devastate Asia, and afflict coastal people, before Cronion raises Attalus as a helper who destroys them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The oracle says that the Galati would cross the Hellespont, devastate Asia, and afflict those living near the sea. It also says that Cronion would raise up a helper, identified as Attalus, who would bring destruction to the Galati.
