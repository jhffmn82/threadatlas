Abstract written by a model

Clepsydra is a spring and well beneath Mount Ithome near Ithome’s gates, supplying Zeus’s temple and the Arsinoe conduit; Messenians said Ithome and Neda washed infant Zeus there.

CHAPTER XXIX.

Clepsydra was a well beneath Mount Ithome whose underground water fed the Arsinoe conduit in Messene. At the spring, the Messenians said, Ithome and Neda washed the infant Zeus after the Curetes had stolen him from Cronos.
