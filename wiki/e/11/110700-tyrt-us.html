Tyrtæus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tyrtæus is a schoolmaster sent from Athens to Sparta, who composed and recited elegiac verses and anapæsts about the former war; his verses later dated Ithome’s evacuation and described twenty years of the first Messenian War.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Tyrtæus’s verses were used to date the evacuation of Ithome and to describe the twenty years of the first Messenian War. During the second Messenian War, the Athenians sent him to Sparta in response to a Delphic oracle, and he recited elegiac verses and anapæsts to the Spartan authorities and people.
