Abstract written by a model

The Rash Singer is a sad singer and prisoner of Rash, condemned after singing beneath the palace balcony. Dropped for the Hungry Tiger, his mournful song spares him, and he is hidden underground with bananas and cabbage. He later escapes through a tunnel, sings of being out of Rash, remains frightened by the square green moon, welcomes Prince Evered sleepily, and falls asleep.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The sad singer is condemned after singing beneath the palace balcony and is dropped into the prison courtyard for the Hungry Tiger to eat. His mournful song causes the Hungry Tiger to reject him, and he is hidden in an underground chamber with bananas and cabbage.

CHAPTER 7

The Rash singer is heard crooning sadly in the cavern and later escapes through the tunnel with the others. After landing in the field of down, he sings about being out of Rash and remains frightened by the square green moon. He welcomes Prince Evered with a sleepy song before falling asleep.
