Akbad's punishment and pardon in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Akbad's punishment and pardon is an event in which Cheeriobed confronts Akbad's deceit, threatens punishment, then pardons and dismisses him after Ozma, Trot, and Orin plead, giving him a cottage while the Wizard removes his heavy golden wings.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 18

Akbad is dragged before Cheeriobed, who is angry at his deceit and threatens punishment. Ozma, Trot, and Orin plead for him, and Cheeriobed pardons him, dismisses him from court, and gives him a cottage; the Wizard then removes his heavy golden wings.
