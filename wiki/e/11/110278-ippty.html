Abstract written by a model

Ippty is the cruel, merciless Chief Scribe of Rash, Irashi, and the Realm, a rogue and villain who serves Irashi the Rough. His most distinctive feature is his unusual writing-tool hands: a fountain-pen finger and other pen, pencil, eraser, sealing-wax, candle, and pen-knife fingers. He carries a hurry-cane and uses it to travel. At the Pasha's court, he records remarks and broken dishes, favors punishing Hasha, and proposes using a wild animal against prisoners. He travels to the Emerald City, persuades the Hungry Tiger to accompany him by promising prisoners to eat, and brings the tiger to Rash, where he helps confine it and reports that it has eaten prisoners. He repeatedly urges Irashi to punish others, including Carter, and suggests making the Vegetable Man into soup. As Irashi's assistant, Ippty helps him rule Rash and steal the scepter. When Atmos appears, Ippty flees into a tree and unsuccessfully throws a coffee pot at Reddy. Atmos captures him, binds his fingers, and carries him with Irashi into the sky as proof that people live at the bottom of the air, intending to abandon him on a lonely island after a lecture.

Ippty is in one work on this site, The Hungry Tiger of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Ippty in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Ippty first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Ippty records the Pasha's remarks and broken dishes in his notebook, supports punishing Hasha, and proposes using a wild animal to destroy prisoners. He identifies the Hungry Tiger as a suitable animal, then travels toward Oz by hurry-cane after writing instructions for the cane to carry him to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 2

Ippty is the Chief Scribe of Rash and arrives in the Palace garden using the hurry-cane. He persuades the Hungry Tiger to come to Rash by promising him prisoners to eat, then rides away with him and secretly intends to keep him there.

CHAPTER 3

Ippty brings the Hungry Tiger to the pink palace and recommends him to Irashi as a serviceable but dangerous creature who will require watching. He helps Irashi lock the Hungry Tiger in the prison courtyard and later leaves with him after Irashi believes the singer has been eaten.

CHAPTER 5

Ippty, Chief Scribe of Rash, warns Carter and Betsy to leave and notices that Carter has corn ears and a turnip nose. He urges Pasha Irashi to punish Carter and suggests that the Vegetable Man would make excellent soup.

CHAPTER 6

Ippty helps Irashi seize and retain control of Rash and is described as having several unusual fingers, including a fountain-pen finger, pencil, sealing-wax finger, eraser, candle thumb, and pen-knife. He helps bring the Hungry Tiger to Rash and tells Irashi that the tiger has eaten the prisoners.

CHAPTER 19

Ippty flees into a tree when he sees Atmos and throws a coffee pot at Reddy, but it bounces harmlessly off his head. He is captured, has his pen and pencily fingers bound, and is carried away with Irashi by Atmos.

CHAPTER 20

Ippty is carried into the sky by Atmos Fere as a specimen to support his claim about people living at the bottom of the air. Atmos Fere plans to abandon Ippty on a lonely island after his lecture.
