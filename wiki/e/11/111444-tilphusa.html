Tilphusa in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Tilphusa is a fountain about 50 stades from Haliartus, near Mount Tilphusium. Tiresias drank from it while being taken to Apollo at Delphi and then died and was buried there.
