CHAPTER XXIX.

Orontes is a river in Syria that runs through precipitous rocks rather than across a level plain. The Roman Emperor diverted it into a navigable canal, and when the old channel dried, a more than eleven-cubit earthenware coffin containing a perfectly formed giant corpse was found there.
