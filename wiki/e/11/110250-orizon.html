Orizon in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Orizon is a remote, deep, salt, turbulent inland sea in the Munchkin Mountains, surrounding the Ozure Isles and forgotten on modern Oz maps. After an explosion carries Trot, Benny, the Scarecrow, and Orpah across its bottom, they emerge from its waters. Later it lies beside the palace, carries Queen Orin’s return route, and becomes the setting for Orpah’s crossing and Quiberon’s attack.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The Lost Lake of Orizon lies in the Munchkin Country, enclosed by the Munchkin Mountains. Its deep, salt, turbulent waters surround the Ozure Isles, and its remote location has caused it to be forgotten on modern Oz maps.

CHAPTER 12

Lake Orizon receives Trot, Benny, the Scarecrow, and Orpah after the explosion in Quiberon's cave carries them across its bottom. Trot reaches the surface first, and Benny later walks out of the water carrying the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 16

Lake Orizon is the body of water beside the palace and the route by which Queen Orin returns. Orpah swims across it toward her boat, and Quiberon comes around Opal Point to attack.
