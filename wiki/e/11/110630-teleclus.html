BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Teleclus was the Lacedæmonian king who, according to the Lacedæmonian account, was killed while trying to prevent the violation of Lacedæmonian maidens at Artemis Limnas. The Messenian account instead says that he plotted against prominent Messenians by disguising armed Spartan youths as maidens, and was killed with them.
