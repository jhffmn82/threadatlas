vegetable cart in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Carter Green's vegetable cart holds fresh produce, including greens, strawberries, cabbages, and apples. It carries Betsy and Carter across the desert and through Rash, spills its contents on a wild road, is taken with the prisoners, and falls over the prison wall onto the Hungry Tiger.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

The Vegetable Man's cart is piled with fresh greens and strawberries and later carries Betsy across the desert with cabbages and apples. Its contents spill out while the winding road carries it wildly, and it continues rattling across the quicksand toward the Deadly Desert.

CHAPTER 5

The vegetable cart carries Betsy and Carter through Rash and is brought to the palace while Carter calls out his wares. The Rash Guardsmen take it along with the prisoners, and it falls over the prison wall onto the Hungry Tiger's head.
