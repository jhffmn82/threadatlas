CHAPTER 16

Reddy and the Hungry Tiger decide to try escaping from the Giants' castle at night while the Giants are asleep. They have not yet escaped when Princess Elma returns and interrupts them.

CHAPTER 17

Reddy and the Hungry Tiger wait until midnight before attempting to escape from the Giant Castle. Reddy uses Princess Elma's big wig to become a Big Wig, carries the Hungry Tiger, unbolts the doors, and leaves the castle before passing through the city gates.
