Androclus

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Androclus, son of Codrus, was appointed king of the Ionians who sailed to Ephesus. He drove Leleges and Lydians from its upper part, took Samos from the Samians, later helped Priene against the Carians, and died in that battle; his tomb lay on the road to the Magnesian gates.

Androclus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
