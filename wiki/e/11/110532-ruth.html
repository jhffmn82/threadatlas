Abstract written by a model

Ruth is Angeline Stickney’s sister and a briefly enrolled student at New York Central College. She joins Angeline’s plan for college at McGrawville, where they live on one dollar a week, have daguerreotypes taken in January 1852, and Ruth later attends college for short periods, including two months in 1854. Poor health forces her home and ends further study, but she financially supports Angeline and brings her home after Angeline breaks down before graduation. Ruth preserves a poem from memory, feels deeply lonely without Angeline, and later endures recurrent paralysis that threatens her husband Asaph’s prospects.

Ruth is in one work on this site, An Astronomer's Wife. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Illustration: AN OLD DAGUERREOTYPE] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER V.

Ruth accompanies Angeline Stickney in the plan to attend college at McGrawville, where the sisters live on one dollar a week. She and Angeline have their daguerreotypes taken in January 1852, and Ruth later joins Angeline in McGrawville.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VI.

Ruth Stickney attended New York Central College for short periods but returned home after poor health prevented her from continuing. She helped Angeline Stickney financially after giving up further college study and took Angeline home when Angeline broke down shortly before graduation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER X.

Ruth was Angeline’s sister, and Angeline planned for Ruth and her husband to move to Michigan so that Asaph could build them a house. Ruth wrote that she was very lonely and had never found anyone so willing to share her grief and joy as Angeline. Ruth later suffered repeated spells of paralysis and feared that her illness would destroy Asaph’s prospects.
