CHAPTER XXX.

The market-place of Megalopolis lies to the north, on the right of the Helisson’s course. It contains precincts and a stone temple to Lycæan Zeus, Philip’s portico, Hermes Acacesius’ temple, public offices, the Myropolis and Aristandreum porticoes, a temple of Zeus Soter, and the statue of Polybius.
