Hero of Temesa in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Hero of Temesa, called Lycas, was the ghost of a sailor stoned after violating a maiden. Temesa yearly offered him its handsomest girl until Euthymus defeated him; he dived into the sea and vanished.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

The Hero of Temesa was the ghost of a sailor stoned to death after violating a maiden. The people of Temesa annually offered him the handsomest girl in the town until Euthymus fought and defeated him; the Hero then dived into the sea and was never seen again. A painting showed him as a dark, formidable figure in a wolfskin and named him Lycas.
