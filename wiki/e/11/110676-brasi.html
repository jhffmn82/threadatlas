Brasiæ in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Brasiæ, formerly Oreatæ, was the last Eleutherolaconian place on that coast, about two hundred stades from Cyphanta. Its people said Semele and Dionysus arrived in a chest; Dionysus was reared in Ino’s cave, inspiring its renamed identity, garden, and temples to Æsculapius and Achilles.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Brasiæ was the last place belonging to the Eleutherolacones in that part of the coast and was about two hundred stades from Cyphanta. Its inhabitants said that Semele and Dionysus arrived there in a chest, that Dionysus was reared there, and that the city’s former name, Oreatæ, was changed to Brasiæ because of the landing.
