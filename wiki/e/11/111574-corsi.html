CHAPTER XVII.

The Corsi came from Cyrnus, which the Greeks called Corsica, after a large contingent crossed to Sardinia during factional conflict. They settled in part of Sardinia’s mountainous district and were named after their homeland. Like the Ilians, they avoided enslavement by the Carthaginians because of the security provided by the mountains.
