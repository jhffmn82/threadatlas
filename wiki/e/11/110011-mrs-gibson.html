Mrs. Gibson

Also called: her rival, the poor lady who has passed, this poor creature, this unhappy woman, this vindictive woman

Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Gibson, who loved Neil Gibson vividly and physically and intensely hated Miss Dunbar, arranged a secret meeting at Thor Bridge. She planned her own death to implicate Miss Dunbar, using Miss Dunbar’s note, a concealed pistol, and a second pistol disposed of with a string and weight. She was found dead on her back near Thor Bridge, shot at close range behind the right temple, with the note clutched in her left hand.

Mrs. Gibson is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
