the transformation into a real person in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

This topic is Benny's wish to become a real person. After becoming alive, he is told that acting like a real person can make him one. He wants laughter, song, dance, and happiness despite the hardships of a different form. He expects the Wizard of Oz to help, but begins finding happiness by helping everyone.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

Benny wishes to become a real person now that he is alive. The Scarecrow tells him that acting like a real person is the way to become one.

CHAPTER 4

Benny wants to become a real person so he can laugh, sing, dance, and be happy. The Scarecrow points out that remaining stone would let him avoid tiredness, hunger, pain, clothing, and wear, but Benny continues to wish for a different form.

CHAPTER 12

Benny believes he will become a real person after seeing the Wizard of Oz, while Orpah says he is already a real person. Benny is beginning to feel happy in his present state because he is helping everyone.
