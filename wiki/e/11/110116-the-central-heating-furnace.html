THE ADVENTURE OF SHOSCOMBE OLD PLACE

The central heating furnace in the cellar beneath Lady Beatrice's room had been unused until Sir Robert had it restarted because he complained of the cold. A fragment of burned human bone is found among its cinders, and the furnace can be reached from outside or through a passage near Lady Beatrice's room.
