BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Antiochus, one of the sons of Phintas, was king of Messenia alongside his brother Androcles. He opposed surrendering Polychares, argued for submitting the case to arbitration, and became sole king after Androcles was killed; he later died before the war began openly.
