Antiochus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Antiochus, son of Phintas, ruled Messenia with Androcles, opposed surrendering Polychares and urged arbitration, became sole king after Androcles' death, dedicated a veil at Olympia, and died before open war.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Antiochus, one of the sons of Phintas, was king of Messenia alongside his brother Androcles. He opposed surrendering Polychares, argued for submitting the case to arbitration, and became sole king after Androcles was killed; he later died before the war began openly.
