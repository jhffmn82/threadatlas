Danny reads the little black book, and the statue strangely comes alive. Unable to bend over and read the pedestal, he does not know his name until the Scarecrow calls him Benny. Though alive, tireless, terribly strong, unharmed by water, and able to dance, he remains a stone man rather than a real person. He protects his companions from Quiberon, breaks the monster's tusks by stepping into its jaws, and carries the Scarecrow while helping Trot reach Cave City. He threatens the cave men, volunteers to face the blue ray, emerges from Lake Orizon carrying the Scarecrow, and continues toward the Emerald City hoping the Wizard of Oz will make him a real person. At Ozma's residence, Benny is introduced as alive but not a real person, and the Wizard considers transforming him in reward for his services to Philador and Trot. After returning to Oz, Benny joyfully dances in the gardens. When the Wizard offers to make him a real man, Benny chooses to remain a stone man at Trot's request. Ozma invites him to live at the Emerald City, where his journey ends as a subject of Ozma alongside Trot and the Scarecrow.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The Public Benefactor is a stone statue who comes to life after Danny reads from the little black book. He leaves his pedestal, struggles to understand human behavior, survives a truck's impact, flees frightened people, and falls through the earth into Oz, where he is called Benny by the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 4

Benny accidentally runs into Trot while fleeing with the Scarecrow from a horriblus bird. He explains that he wishes to become a real person, but when Akbad seizes Trot, Benny grabs Akbad's ankles and tries to stop him; Akbad's magic wings lift them both into the air.

CHAPTER 5

Benny protects Trot and the Scarecrow from Quiberon and argues with the monster about his fire and smoke. He steps into Quiberon's jaws, causing the monster's red tusks to break, then carries the Scarecrow and helps Trot escape to Cave City.

CHAPTER 11

Benny accompanies Trot and the Scarecrow through Cave City, throws a boulder at a cave man, and threatens to fight the cave men. He volunteers to be first before the blue ray, but the passage ends as the ray is turned upon him.

CHAPTER 12

Benny emerges from Lake Orizon carrying the Scarecrow and helps carry both the Scarecrow and Trot onto the beach. He travels toward the Emerald City with Trot and the Scarecrow, hoping to become a real person after seeing the Wizard of Oz, although Orpah says he is already a real person.

CHAPTER 13

Benny loses his balance in the round house and cannot walk normally through its spinning motion. He is pulled through the circles, tries unsuccessfully to mount a wooden elephant, and then helps Trot and the Scarecrow escape. Because he never grows tired or loses his breath, he carries Trot up the tower, drags the Scarecrow through the trap door, and slides them all from the roof.

CHAPTER 14

Benny listens to Trot's story and helps explain that the travelers are seeking a Queen. He becomes puzzled about Philador's age after learning that Mombi stole Philador's mother when Philador was two, and he rides High Boy with the others toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 17

Benny, a stone man from Boston, accompanies Trot to Ozma's residence and is introduced as alive but not a real person. The Wizard lists changing Benny into a real person as one of the problems to solve, in reward for his services to Philador and Trot.

CHAPTER 20

Benny returns to Oz with the others and joyfully dances in the gardens. After the Wizard of Oz offers to change him into a real man, Benny decides to remain a stone man at Trot's request and is invited by Ozma to live at the capital.
