Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A brazen chariot at Olympia, made by Onatas the Æginetan, commemorates Hiero’s Olympian victories; with horses and boys made by Calamis, it was offered to the god by Dinomenes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

The brazen chariot commemorating Hiero the son of Dinomenes stood at Olympia with race-horses and boys on the horses at each side. Onatas the Æginetan made the chariot, while Calamis made the horses and the boys; Dinomenes the son of Hiero offered the monument to the god.
