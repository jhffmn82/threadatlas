Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Caanthus is said to have been the brother of Melia and the son of Oceanus. Sent by his father to find Melia after she was carried off, he found her with Apollo, set fire to the grove of Ismenian Apollo, and was killed by Apollo with an arrow; his tomb is near the spring sacred to Ares.
