------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

At fourteen, Angeline wrote compositions on the sugar maple and slavery for her teacher, George Waldo. The first explained the tree’s many uses, while the second condemned slavery and expressed hope that it would end; her teacher praised her writing and urged her to write more.
