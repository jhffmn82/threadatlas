Angeline’s compositions in An Astronomer's Wife

At fourteen, Angeline wrote compositions on the sugar maple’s uses and against slavery, winning George Waldo’s praise and encouragement to continue writing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

At fourteen, Angeline wrote compositions on the sugar maple and slavery for her teacher, George Waldo. The first explained the tree’s many uses, while the second condemned slavery and expressed hope that it would end; her teacher praised her writing and urged her to write more.
