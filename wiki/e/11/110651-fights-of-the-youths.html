fights of the youths

Also called: the fights of the youths, their fights

Abstract written by a model

The fights of the youths take place at Platanistas after sacrifices to Enyalius at the temple of Phœbus. The divisions approach by routes assigned by lot on the previous night, and the youths fight with hands and feet, bite and tear at one another, and push one another into the water.
