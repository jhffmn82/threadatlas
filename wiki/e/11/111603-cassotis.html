CHAPTER XXIV.

Cassotis is a walled fountain reached by an ascent through the wall near the temple at Delphi. Its water is said to run underground and inspire the women in the sanctuary of Apollo with prophetic powers, and its name is attributed to one of the Nymphs of Parnassus.
