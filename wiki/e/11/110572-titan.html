CHAPTER XI.

Titan was said by the natives to have first dwelt on the mountain-top at Titane and to have given the place its name. He was regarded as the Sun’s brother because he was thought to have been exceptionally skilled in observing the seasons when the Sun fructified and ripened seeds and fruit.
