------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Angeline’s education began in district school at age three and continued through instruction from Edwin Ingalls and study at the Rodman Union Seminary. She enjoyed history, natural philosophy, grammar, arithmetic, spelling, singing, and composition, and she made notable progress despite extensive work and difficult travel.
