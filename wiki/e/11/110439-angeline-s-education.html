Angeline’s education in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Angeline’s education began in district school at age three, continued with Edwin Ingalls and Rodman Union Seminary, and included favored history, natural philosophy, grammar, arithmetic, spelling, singing, and composition despite work and difficult travel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Angeline’s education began in district school at age three and continued through instruction from Edwin Ingalls and study at the Rodman Union Seminary. She enjoyed history, natural philosophy, grammar, arithmetic, spelling, singing, and composition, and she made notable progress despite extensive work and difficult travel.
