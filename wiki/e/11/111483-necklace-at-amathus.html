Also called: the necklace in the temple of Adonis at Amathus

Abstract written by a model

The necklace at Amathus, in the ancient temple of Adonis and Aphrodite, is made of emeralds set in gold. Though said to be Harmonia’s and later Eriphyle’s necklace, the text doubts this because Eriphyle’s was entirely gold.

CHAPTER XLI.

The necklace at Amathus is said to be the necklace originally given to Harmonia and later called Eriphyle’s necklace. The text doubts that it is the same necklace, because the one at Amathus consists of emeralds set in gold, whereas Eriphyle’s is said by Homer to have been entirely gold.
