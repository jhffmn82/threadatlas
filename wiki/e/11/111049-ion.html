Ion in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ion, son of Xuthus, married Helice, became Selinus's adopted heir, and ruled the Ægialians. He founded Helice and gave the Ægialians the additional name Ionians. Later, while commander for Athens, he died in Attica and was buried at Potamos.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Description of Greece, Volume II / BOOK VII.--ACHAIA. / CHAPTER I.

Ion was the son of Xuthus and became the adopted son and heir of Selinus after marrying Selinus's daughter Helice. He ruled the Ægialians, founded Helice, gave the Ægialians the additional name Ionians, and later died in Attica while serving as commander for Athens.
