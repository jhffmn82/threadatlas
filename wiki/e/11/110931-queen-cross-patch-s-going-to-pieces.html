Queen Cross Patch's going to pieces in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Queen Cross Patch's going to pieces is a customary Quilty occurrence at the busiest season. It begins with her shrill scream, flying patches, and fall from a three-legged throne, then she breaks into tiny pieces that Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper sweep up and hang in a closet.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

Queen Cross Patch's going to pieces begins with a shrill scream as she flings patches into the air, topples from her three-legged throne, and breaks into extremely small pieces. Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper sweep up the scraps and hang them in a closet, treating the event as a normal consequence of a Quilty wearing out.
