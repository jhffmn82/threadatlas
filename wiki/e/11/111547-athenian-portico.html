Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The Athenian portico was built with money the Athenians obtained in war from the Peloponnesians and their Greek allies. Nearby were votive figure-heads of captured ships and brazen shields bearing an inscription naming the cities from which the spoil came.
