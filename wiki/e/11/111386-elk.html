Abstract written by a model

The elk is a wild animal found among the Celts, resembling both a stag and a camel. It scents men from far away and hides in rocks and cave recesses, making it hard to hunt or see at a distance. It is occasionally captured when a large net encloses its area.

CHAPTER XXI.

The elk is a wild animal found among the Celts and described as something between a stag and a camel. It is difficult to hunt or see at a distance because it scents humans from far away and hides among rocks and caves; hunters usually capture it only when it is enclosed accidentally within a large net.
