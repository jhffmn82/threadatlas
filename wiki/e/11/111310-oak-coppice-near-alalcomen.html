An oak-coppice near Alalcomenæ contains Bœotian oaks reputed for the finest roots; at the smaller Dædala, Platæans used a crow-selected tree to carve a wooden image.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

The oak-coppice near Alalcomenæ contained oaks said to have the finest roots in Bœotia. At the smaller Dædala, the Platæans placed boiled meat there, watched the crows, and used the tree selected by a crow for carving the wooden image.
