Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVII.

The temple stands within a sacred enclosure reached from the temple of Artemis the Leader. It contains statues of Despœna and Demeter, their throne and footstool, an altar to Demeter, an altar to Despœna, and an altar to the Great Mother. A mirror beside the exit shows the statues and throne clearly but shows a viewer only obscurely or not at all.
