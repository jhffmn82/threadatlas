Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Piety to the gods is illustrated by the Athenians’ treatment of the temple of Olympian Zeus at Syracuse and by Datis’ conduct toward the Delians and a statue of Apollo. Polygnotus included the sacrilegious wretch’s punishment because people in earlier times honored the deity.
