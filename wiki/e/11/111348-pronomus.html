Pronomus

Abstract written by a model

Pronomus is a highly attractive flute-player who discovered a single flute usable for every kind of measure and first played different measures on it. His appearance and movements delighted theatre audiences; his processional song remained in use among Chalcis dwellers at Delos, and the Thebans erected his statue.

CHAPTER XII.

Pronomus was a highly attractive flute-player who discovered that flutes could be used for every kind of measure and was the first to play different measures on the same flute. His appearance and bodily movements delighted theatre audiences, and a processional song of his remained in use among the people of Chalcis near the Euripus who had come to Delos; the Thebans erected a statue of him.
