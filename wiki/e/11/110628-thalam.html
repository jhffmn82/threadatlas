Thalamæ

Abstract written by a model

Thalamæ is a place about eighty stades by road from Œtylus, centered on a temple and dream oracle of Ino, with bronze statues, including Pasiphae and the Sun, and the Moon’s sacred spring.

Thalamæ is in one work on this site, Description of Greece. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Thalamæ lies about eighty stades by road from Œtylus and has a temple and oracle of Ino where answers are received through dreams. The temple also contains statues of Pasiphae and the Sun, and a sacred spring called the water of the Moon.
