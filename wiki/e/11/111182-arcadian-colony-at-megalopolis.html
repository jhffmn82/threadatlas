Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVII.

The colonization gathered numerous Arcadian towns and their inhabitants into Megalopolis so that Arcadia would be stronger against the Lacedæmonians. Epaminondas the Theban promoted the plan, Thebans provided military protection, and most of Arcadia joined, although some communities resisted and suffered expulsion or forced relocation.
