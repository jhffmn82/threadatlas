Arcadian colony at Megalopolis

Also called: Megalopolis colonization, colonization, the Arcadian colony

Abstract written by a model

A year and months after Leuctra, during the 102nd Olympiad's second year, Arcadian towns colonized Megalopolis to strengthen Arcadia against Lacedæmonians, with Theban support and resistance met by expulsion or relocation.

CHAPTER XXVII.

The colonization gathered numerous Arcadian towns and their inhabitants into Megalopolis so that Arcadia would be stronger against the Lacedæmonians. Epaminondas the Theban promoted the plan, Thebans provided military protection, and most of Arcadia joined, although some communities resisted and suffered expulsion or forced relocation.
