CHAPTER 7

The witch's window is a dormer window in the attic of Mombi's former hut, with one blue pane and one pink pane. Looking through the blue pane reveals the past, while looking through the pink pane reveals the future. Tattypoo looks through it, then suddenly jumps out, followed by Agnes.
