the witch's window in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The witch's window is a dormer in the attic of the good witch's hut, with blue and pink leaded panes showing past and future; Tattypoo looks through it, jumps out, and Agnes follows.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The witch's window is a dormer window in the attic of Mombi's former hut, with one blue pane and one pink pane. Looking through the blue pane reveals the past, while looking through the pink pane reveals the future. Tattypoo looks through it, then suddenly jumps out, followed by Agnes.
