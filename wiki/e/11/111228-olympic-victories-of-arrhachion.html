Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XL.

Arrhachion had two victories at Olympia in the two Olympiads before the 54th, attributed to the equity of the umpires and his own merit. In his last contest, the people of Elis awarded him victory after his death.
