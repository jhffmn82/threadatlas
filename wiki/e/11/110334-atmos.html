Atmos in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Atmos Fere is a giant-sized skyman, airman, explorer, and citizen of the air from the Cloud Country, where he lives above Earth with a balloon-like body filled with air and a floating rib. He wears iron boots to travel through air and water and to descend and walk on Earth, and he possesses a sparkling square last ruby, received from a sky-lark, as protection from danger in the air. Engaged to Heiress and associated with Zeph, he is preparing a lecture for the Cloud Country Gentlemen. To prove that people live near the bottom of the air, he captures Princess Ozma. Ozma punctures him with her emerald pin; he loses air, falls to Earth, and is repaired, inflated, and refitted with boots by Rusty. After Ozma explains Earth, Atmos admits that taking her was wrong. He travels with and carries her, marveling at Earth while struggling with its walking, storm, and rolling ground, and offers to carry the group across the Deadly Desert. He aids Reddy against the Rash army by carrying the little girls, capturing Irashi, and helping drive away the soldiers. Finally, he takes the bound Irashi and Ippty into the sky as further proof for his lecture, intending to leave them on a lonely island before disappearing above the clouds.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

Atmos Fere is a skyman who captures Princess Ozma to prove to the Cloud Country Gentlemen that life exists near the bottom of the air. When Ozma punctures him with her emerald pin, he loses air and falls with her to earth. Rusty Ore believes he can blow Atmos up again with a bellows.

CHAPTER 15

Atmos learns about the Earth from Ozma and agrees that taking her away was wrong. Rusty repairs and inflates him and fits him with iron boots, after which Atmos carries Ozma south; he is delighted by the Earth's objects but struggles with walking, the storm, and the rolling ground.

CHAPTER 18

Atmos travels with Ozma after falling from the clouds and carrying her through the air; they eventually reach the ground by way of a lake. He reveals that he has a sparkling square ruby, which he received from a sky-lark after finding it near his air castle. Atmos offers to carry the group across the Deadly Desert.

CHAPTER 19

Atmos helps Reddy by placing the little girls behind him and preparing to tread on the Rash army's toes. He captures Irashi, helps drive away the Rash soldiers, and later carries off the bound Irashi and Ippty as he leaves for the sky.

CHAPTER 20

Atmos Fere takes Ippty and Irashi into the sky, planning to use them as proof that people live at the bottom of the air. He says he will drop them on a lonely island after his lecture and then disappears above the clouds in his iron boots.
