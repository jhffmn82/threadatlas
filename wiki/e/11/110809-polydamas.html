Polydamas

Abstract written by a model

Polydamas, son of Nicias and native of Scotusa, was described as the largest man of his time. Renowned for pancratium crowns and extraordinary strength, he slew lions unarmed, seized a bull, stopped a chariot, and killed three Persian Immortals at Susa after Darius attracted him with rewards. He died when a cave roof collapsed as he tried to hold up its falling rock.

CHAPTER V.

Polydamas, the son of Nicias and a native of Scotusa, is described as the largest man of his time. He won notable victories in the pancratium, slew a lion unarmed, seized a powerful bull, stopped a speeding chariot, and killed three Persian Immortals in single combat at Susa. He died after remaining beneath the cracking roof of a cave and attempting to hold up the falling rock.
