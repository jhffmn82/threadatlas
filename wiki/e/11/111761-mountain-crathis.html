Arcadian mountain source of the river Crathis, containing Pyronian Artemis's temple, whose shrine formerly supplied Argives fire for Lerne.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Mount Crathis is the source of the river Crathis. It also contains a temple to Pyronian Artemis, from whose shrine the Argives formerly brought fire into the district around Lerne.
