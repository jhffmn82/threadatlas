Aristion in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Aristion, Mithridates’ ambassador, persuaded Athenians to prefer Mithridates over Romans; after Roman victory, he fled to Athene’s temple, where Sulla seized and killed him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

Aristion, employed by Mithridates as an ambassador, persuaded the Athenian democracy and fiercer spirits to prefer Mithridates’ friendship to that of the Romans. After the Roman victory he fled to the temple of Athene, but Sulla tore him away and put him to death.
