CHAPTER XIX.

Aristion, employed by Mithridates as an ambassador, persuaded the Athenian democracy and fiercer spirits to prefer Mithridates’ friendship to that of the Romans. After the Roman victory he fled to the temple of Athene, but Sulla tore him away and put him to death.
