Philador in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Philador is a little prince of the Ozure Isles and prince of his father's Kingdom. When Tattypoo disappears, he follows a magic slate's advice to seek Ozma of Oz's help, frees Herby from an enormous bottle, and travels toward the Emerald City carrying a thinking cap and a magic jumping rope. He is welcomed in Up Town, survives a storm that carries him down a mountain, and reunites with High Boy. He helps the travelers cross a river with his rope, shares food and pills, and continues guiding the journey. At the Emerald City, he is sad to part from his friend Trot, returns with the King, Queen, and little Prince, and invites Trot to the Ozure Isles, offering sea-horse rides, his crown, and a request that she become a Princess.

CHAPTER 8

Philador spends the night in the good witch's cottage, then learns that Tattypoo has disappeared. He follows the magic slate's advice to seek help from Ozma of Oz, releases Herby from an enormous bottle, and prepares to travel with him to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 10

Philador visits Up Town with Herby and is warmly welcomed by Joe King and Queen Hyacinth. After the storm carries him down the mountain, he is reunited with High Boy and rides toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 15

Philador observes the Out Keeper's shutters and later offers the travelers a magic jumping rope for crossing the river. He hugs High Boy after the horse carries them across, shares sandwiches, takes Herby's wakefulness pills, and helps guide the journey toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 19

Philador is reluctant to say goodbye to Trot. He returns with the King, Queen, and little Prince to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 20

Philador sits beside Trot at the banquet and invites her to visit the Ozure Isles. He offers her rides on his sea horse and the use of his crown, and says he will ask his father to make her a Princess.

Philador in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Philador is a young, cheerful, handsome, companionable prince of the Ozure Isles, son of King Cheeriobed and Queen Orin. Ten by choice in Oz, he is kind and gentle to the land and sea birds. His central purpose is to find his royal mother, save his father’s Kingdom, and oppose Quiberon. About two years after Mombi carried Orin away, Philador secretly leaves the castle to escape Quiberon’s danger. A blue gull carries him from the Ozure Isles toward the Good Witch of the North, but he finds no one when he calls for Tattypoo. He then travels with Herby, crosses a rushing river by jumping rope, climbs the purple mountains, meets Joe King, and rides High Boy. After explaining his quest, he joins Trot’s journey to the Emerald City. There he tells Ozma and her advisers of Mombi’s enchantments, Quiberon’s cruelty, his flight, and his meeting with Tattypoo. He returns with the group to the Ozure Isles and is reunited with Cheeriobed and Orin. He later corrects Akbad’s false account that Akbad took him to the Emerald City, saying the Grand Mogul brought him to the good witch’s hut before his travels with Herby, Trot, Benny, and High Boy.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

The Giant Horse of Oz

The Prince of the Ozure Isles gallops away with Trot on a real high horse.

CHAPTER 1

Philador is Cheeriobed's son, a cheerful and handsome little Prince of the Ozure Isles. He wants to find his mother and destroy Quiberon, and he hopes to cross the lake to seek help from the Good Witch of the North. A blue gull tells him to return when the moon is high because she has thought of a way to help him.

CHAPTER 2

Philador secretly leaves the castle because he wants to escape the danger posed by Quiberon. He rides the Mo-gull away from the Ozure Isles after asking to be carried to the Good Witch of the North.

CHAPTER 6

Akbad falsely claims that he carried Philador to the Emerald City and placed him in Ozma's care. Cheeriobed believes this account and waits for Philador's return.

CHAPTER 7

Philador, the Prince of the Ozure Isles, is brought by the blue gull to the good witch's door. He enters the hut and calls for Tattypoo, but finds no one there.

CHAPTER 9

Philador travels with Herby toward the Emerald City, crosses the rushing river using the jumping rope, and climbs the purple mountains. He meets Joe King, explains that his father and mother are in the Ozure Isles, and rides High Boy toward the mountain-top city.

CHAPTER 14

Philador identifies himself as the Prince of the Ozure Isles and explains that he is seeking his royal mother and trying to save his father's Kingdom. After hearing Trot's account, he connects Quiberon's demand with the events surrounding his quest and joins the journey to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 17

Prince Philador tells Ozma and her advisers about Mombi's enchantments, Quiberon's cruelty, his flight on the blue gull, and his meeting with Tattypoo. He travels with the group to the Ozure Isles and is reunited with King Cheeriobed and Queen Orin.

CHAPTER 18

Philador was about two years old when Mombi carried Orin away from the Ozure Isles. He later corrected the claim that Akbad had rescued him, explaining that the Grand Mogul took him to the good witch's hut and that he then traveled with Herby, Trot, Benny, and High Boy.
