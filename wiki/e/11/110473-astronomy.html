Abstract written by a model

Astronomy studies the sun, planets, and the motions, scale, and measurement of the earth and other worlds. It teaches earth’s rotation, revolution, and smallness, inspiring exalted views of the Creator, awe, reverence, love, and worship. For Asaph Hall, it became a career involving observations, calculations, reading, and recognition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VII.

Angeline Stickney wrote that the study of Astronomy gives exalted views of the Creator and expands the soul. She described the motions and scale of the earth, sun, planets, stars, and systems of worlds as leading to awe, reverence, love, and worship.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XI.

Astronomy became the basis of Asaph Hall’s career and livelihood. He observed moon-culminations, computed cometary orbits and almanacs, read advanced works in astronomy and mathematics, and gained recognition for his ability.
