Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

The great festival of Dædala was held for all Bœotia every sixth year, according to the stated interval after the period when the Platæans were in exile. Communities provided fourteen wooden statues each year for the smaller festival, decorated the images, carried them from the Asopus to Mount Cithæron, and burned them with animal sacrifices on the altar.
