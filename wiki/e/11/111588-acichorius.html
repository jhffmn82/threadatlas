Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Acichorius’s division followed Brennus and was delayed by Ætolian attacks on its rearguard, baggage, and stragglers. It joined Brennus’s fleeing forces only the night before the final retreat, while a detachment remained at Heraclea to guard the camp’s treasure.
