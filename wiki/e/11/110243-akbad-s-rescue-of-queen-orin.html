Akbad's rescue of Queen Orin in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Akbad’s rescue of Queen Orin is a rescue from Quiberon’s attack. When Quiberon lunges at Orin’s boat, Akbad flies at the monster, snatches her from its jaws, and carries her beyond the islands with his magic wings. Cheeriobed credits him with the rescue, though another account about seeking help is unconfirmed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

When Quiberon lunges at Queen Orin's boat, Akbad flies directly at the monster and snatches the Queen from its jaws. His magic wings carry him beyond the islands because he acts without thinking of himself.

CHAPTER 18

Cheeriobed initially credits Akbad with rescuing Queen Orin by picking the golden pear and bringing Philador for help. The chapter does not confirm that account; Philador says the Grand Mogul took him to the good witch's hut, while Akbad's role is only described as rescuing Orin from Quiberon.
