Phormis

Also called: Mænalian Phormis, Phormis himself

Abstract written by a model

Phormis was a Mænalian from Mænalus who went to Sicily, served Gelon and then Hiero, showed great valor, and became wealthy. He was honored through offerings at Olympia and Delphi, including images of horses, charioteers, and himself fighting enemies, dedicated by Lycortas.

CHAPTER XXVI.

He was a Mænalian from Mænalus who went to Sicily to serve Gelon and later Hiero, displaying great valor and becoming wealthy. He dedicated offerings at Olympia and Delphi, including two horses with charioteers, and an image of himself fighting several enemies; the latter was actually dedicated by the Syracusan Lycortas out of affection for him.
