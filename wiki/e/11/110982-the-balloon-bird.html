CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

The green balloon changes into a plump, talking balloon bird with a stiff leg that carries Peter through the sky. It says Peter is being taken to Balloon Island and warns that Queen Luna plans to cut a hole in his back and blow him up so he can float. Peter lets go of its leg while it is passing through a black cloud.
