Cleomenes attacks and defeats the Achæans at Dyme, then violates agreements and expels Megalopolis’s inhabitants, compelling Aratus to seek Antigonus’s aid.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Cleomenes attacked the Achæans and defeated them at Dyme while Aratus was their general. His subsequent violation of agreements, including the expulsion of the inhabitants of Megalopolis, led Aratus to seek Antigonus's aid against him.
