CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

The bookman is a living book with a round, jolly face, floppy dog ears, thin limbs, and a book body. Ozwold runs him over, but he is not seriously hurt and offers the travelers animal tales, verse, and other stories. He cannot give directions to the Emerald City because he is not a guide book.
