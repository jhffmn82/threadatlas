Siphnii in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Siphnii built a treasury and sent mine revenue to Delphi, but ceased tribute in cupidity; the sea then swept away their gold mines.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The Siphnii possessed gold mines on Siphnos and were instructed to send a tenth of their revenue to Delphi. After they stopped paying the tribute, the sea encroached and swept away their mines.
