Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XX.

The market-place contains a temple of Olympian Zeus, a statue of Athene in the open air, and the tomb of Patreus. Near it are a temple of Hera, a temple of Apollo, an outlet with a gate bearing golden effigies, and the Odeum.
