Abstract written by a model

The bronze wolf, a Delphian votive offering near the great altar, commemorates a wolf that killed a thief of the god’s treasures and led the Delphians to hidden sacred gold.

CHAPTER XIV.

The bronze wolf stands near the great altar and was dedicated by the Delphians themselves. It commemorates a wolf that killed a man who had stolen the god’s treasures and then led the Delphians to the hidden sacred gold.
