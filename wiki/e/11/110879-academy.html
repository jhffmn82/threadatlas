Academy in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Academy, once owned privately, became a gymnasium. Its surroundings held precincts, altars, and Plato’s tomb; Prometheus’ altar began torch races, while the road contained tombs of Athenian soldiers and notable men.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

The Academy had once belonged to a private man but had become a gymnasium. Near it were precincts of Artemis, an altar of Eros, an altar of Prometheus from which runners carried lighted torches, altars of the Muses, Hermes, Athene, and Hercules, and the tomb of Plato. The road to the Academy also contained many tombs of Athenian soldiers and notable men.
