Podares

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Podares, honored with a hero-chapel in Mantinea’s marketplace, commanded the Mantinean right wing and fell fighting Epaminondas and the Thebans. Though his tomb inscription was later altered for a descendant and namesake, Mantineans continued honoring the older Podares.

Podares is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER IX.

Podares has a hero-chapel in the market-place, and the Mantineans say that he fell in the battle against Epaminondas and the Thebans. Three generations before the narrator’s time, they altered the inscription on his tomb to suit a descendant and namesake, but continued to honor the older Podares.
