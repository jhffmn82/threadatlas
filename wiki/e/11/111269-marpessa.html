Abstract written by a model

Marpessa, the Widow from Tegea and once priestess of Athene, excelled Tegean women in daring against the Lacedæmonians.

Marpessa is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
