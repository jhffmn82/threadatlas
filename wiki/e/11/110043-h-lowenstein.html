H. Lowenstein in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

H. Lowenstein is a Prague-based scientist developing the Serum of Anthropoid as a rejuvenation treatment. Using black-faced Langur because a specimen was accessible, he writes to Professor Presbury through his England agent, A. Dorak, warning that the treatment may be dangerous and that Serum of Anthropoid might have been better.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In the morning Mr. Bennett slipped round to bring us the latest report.

H. Lowenstein writes from Prague to Professor Presbury about the treatment and warns that it may be dangerous. He explains that he used black-faced Langur because a specimen was available, while noting that the Serum of Anthropoid might have been better, and states that Dorak is his agent in England.
