Abstract written by a model

Escape from Patch is the effort planned by Scraps and Peter to flee Patch and reach the Emerald City before Ruggedo. It begins with Scraps, locked in the palace sitting-room, declaring that she will escape despite the Scissor Bird's threat to cut off her head. After Peter and Scraps decide escape is urgent and Scraps plans, Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy break out with Kuma's hand, evade or defeat pursuers, and reach Patch's edge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Scraps declares that she intends to escape from Patch and return to the Emerald City, whether or not Ozma finds her first. She remains locked inside the palace sitting-room, and Grumpy warns that the Scissor Bird would cut off her head if she tried to get away.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Peter and Scraps decide that they must escape from the castle and try to reach the Emerald City ahead of Ruggedo. Grumpy points out that escaping is their immediate problem, while Scraps begins planning after the candle goes out and Peter falls asleep.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy escape from Patch after Peter summons Kuma's hand. Kuma's hand helps them break out of the castle, defeat or evade their pursuers, and reach the edge of the kingdom.
