Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

The statue of Arean Athene at Platæa is wooden but gilt over, with a head, fingers, and toes of Pentelican marble. It is nearly as large as the brazen statue in the Acropolis and is the work of Phidias.
