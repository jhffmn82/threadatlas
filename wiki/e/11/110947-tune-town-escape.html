CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

The travelers escape Tune Town after Scraps has them all scream “Ah” in different keys. The resulting discord frightens the band, Queen Jazzma, and the Tunesters away and leaves the four travelers outside the town without knowing exactly how they crossed the wall.
