Abstract written by a model

Carrie Evans is Lady Beatrice Falder’s longtime confidential maid, serving her for five years and described as devoted, though her loyalty may lie with Lady Beatrice or Sir Robert. Later, as Mrs. Norlett, formerly Evans, she helps arrange for her husband to appear publicly as Lady Beatrice and protests Sir Robert’s planned explanation.
