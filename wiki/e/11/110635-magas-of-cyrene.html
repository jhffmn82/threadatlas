Magas of Cyrene in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Magas, Berenice’s son and Ptolemy I Soter’s uterine brother, governed Cyrene, led its revolt and march toward Egypt, turned back amid a Marmaridæ revolt, and persuaded Antiochus I to attack Egypt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Magas, the uterine brother of Ptolemy I Soter, was appointed governor of Cyrene by his mother Berenice. He persuaded Cyrene to revolt, marched toward Egypt, and was forced to turn back when the Marmaridæ revolted; as the husband of Apame, he also persuaded Antiochus I to attack Egypt.
