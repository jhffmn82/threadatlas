Theopompus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Theopompus, son of Nicander and king of the Lacedæmonians, succeeded Nicander, befriended the Phocians, and led the right wing against the Messenians, suffering defeat before fighting Euphaes again.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Theopompus, son of Nicander and king of the Lacedæmonians, leads the right wing against the Messenians. He is routed by Euphaes in the first major battle and later fights Euphaes again in the second battle.
