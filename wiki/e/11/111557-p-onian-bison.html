Pæonian bison

Abstract written by a model

Pæonian bison is a beast, most difficult to capture alive. Hunters drive it into a palisaded hollow, make it slip on hides, weaken it by hunger, attract and tame it with husked pinenuts, then bind and lead it away; Dropion sent its brazen head to Delphi.

Pæonian bison is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XIII.

Pæonian bisons are described as exceptionally difficult to capture alive because ordinary nets cannot hold them. Hunters drive them into a palisaded hollow, make them slip down a slope covered with hides, weaken them through hunger, and finally tame them with husked pinenuts before binding and leading them away.
