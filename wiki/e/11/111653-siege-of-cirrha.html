Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVII.

During the siege, the people of Cirrha continued to hold out by drinking rainwater and water from wells. After Solon’s manipulation of the river Plistus caused them to suffer incessant diarrhoea, they could no longer man the walls, and the Amphictyones captured the town.
