Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Singing a Hymn in honour of Apollo was said to be the most ancient contest at Delphi and the first contest for which a prize was given. Chrysothemis the Cretan was its first reported victor, followed by Philammon and then his son Thamyris.
