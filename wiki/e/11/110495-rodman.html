Rodman in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Rodman is a town in Jefferson County, with productive surrounding soil, abundant grain, and early distilleries. It is where Chloe Angeline Stickney was born, educated, and later taught in Heath Hollow. Its Cook and Stickney homes, district and singing schools, and Rodman Union Seminary shaped her early life and patient teaching.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

An Astronomer's Wife

Rodman, in Jefferson County, was the town where Chloe Angeline Stickney was born. The surrounding soil is described as productive, with abundant grain and many distilleries in the early period described.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER II.

Rodman was the community where Angeline was born and initially educated. It contained the Cook and Stickney homes, district schools, singing schools, and the Rodman Union Seminary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER IV.

Rodman was the location of the district school in Heath Hollow where Angeline Stickney began teaching for a dollar and a quarter a week and board. She and her sister Ruth also attended the seminary at Rodman, and a former pupil from Rodman later recalled Angeline’s patient instruction.
