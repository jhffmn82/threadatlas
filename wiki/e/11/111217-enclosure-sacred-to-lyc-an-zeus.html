enclosure sacred to Lycæan Zeus

Abstract written by a model

The sacred enclosure on Mount Lycæus forbids entry to men; violators are said not to live more than a year. Throughout the year, men and beasts within it cast no shadows, so hunters cannot see an entering beast’s shadow.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

People may not enter the enclosure sacred to Lycæan Zeus, and anyone who violates the prohibition is said not to live more than a year. Men and beasts inside it are said to have no shadow at any time of year, so a hunter outside cannot see the shadow of a beast that enters it.
