statue of Theagenes

Also called: His statue in Altis, a statue, the statue of Theagenes

Abstract written by a model

A statue of Theagenes, made by Glaucias the Aeginetan and located in the Altis, fell on and killed an enemy who scourged it nightly after Theagenes died. Prosecuted and cast into the sea, it was recovered by fishermen, restored by the Thasians, and sacrificed to as a god.

CHAPTER XI.

After Theagenes died, one of his enemies scourged his statue nightly, and the statue eventually fell on and killed him. The Thasians prosecuted the statue for murder and threw it into the sea, but fishermen later recovered it; the Thasians restored it and sacrificed to it as to a god. The statue in the Altis was made by the Aeginetan Glaucias.
