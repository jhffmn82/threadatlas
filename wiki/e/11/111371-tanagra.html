CHAPTER XX.

Tanagra was said by the people of Tanagra to be the daughter of Æolus and the wife of Pœmander, while Corinna said she was the daughter of Asopus. Because she lived to a very advanced age, she was called Graia, and the city later bore that name before recovering the name Tanagra.
