Clazomenæ

place · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Clazomenæ into a peninsula, people of Clazomenæ

Abstract written by a model

Clazomenæ began with no city before the Ionians came to Asia Minor. Ionians from Colophonia built first under Mount Ida, returned to Ionia, and occupied its territory. Most inhabitants came from Cleonæ and Phlius. Fearing the Persians, they moved to the opposite island, which Alexander son of Philip made a peninsula by embankment.

Clazomenæ is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER III.

Clazomenæ had no city before the Ionians came to Asia Minor. Its settlers first built a city under Mount Ida, later returned to Ionia, and then occupied the territory of Clazomenæ; they subsequently moved to the opposite island because of the Persians, and Alexander later connected the island to the mainland by an embankment.
