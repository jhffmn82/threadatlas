An antique stone statue of Arrhachion, depicted as a pancratiast, stands in the market-place of Phigalia; it has close-set feet and hands near the buttocks, while its inscription has been obliterated.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XL.

The statue of Arrhachion stands in the market-place of Phigalia. It is an antique stone statue with feet not very widely separated and hands by the sides near the buttocks; its inscription has been obliterated.
