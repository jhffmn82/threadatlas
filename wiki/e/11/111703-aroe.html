Aroe

Abstract written by a model

Aroe is a town, named for tilling the soil and built by Eumelus under Triptolemus’s instruction in sowing corn. Its Ionians shared Artemis Triclaria’s grove and temple; Eurypylus landed nearby during a sacrifice, and Patreus later enclosed it within Patræ.

Aroe is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII.

Aroe was built by Eumelus under the instruction of Triptolemus, who taught him to sow corn. Patreus later enlarged the walls near Aroe to include the town within Patræ.

CHAPTER XIX.

Aroe was one of the settlements whose Ionians shared the grove and temple of Artemis Triclaria. Eurypylus was driven by the wind to the sea near Aroe and came ashore there while a lad and maiden were being led to the altar for sacrifice.
