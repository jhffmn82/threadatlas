Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

Chelydorea is a mountain next to Cyllene, lying west of Pheneus. It is said to be the place where Hermes found the tortoise, and it forms the boundary between Pheneus and Pellene; the Achæans graze their flocks over most of it.
