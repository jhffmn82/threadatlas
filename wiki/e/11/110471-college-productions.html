------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VII.

The college productions include prose essays, arguments about public worship, reflections on poetry and Astronomy, and several poems. They are preserved in worn and carefully written manuscripts, and their quality varies from sentimental and religiously morbid to passages the narrator considers true and rather good poetry.
