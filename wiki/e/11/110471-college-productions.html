college productions in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

College productions are Angeline Stickney's worn manuscript essays and poems, ranging from worship, poetry, and Astronomy reflections to sentimental religious morbidity and some true, rather good poetry.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER VII.

The college productions include prose essays, arguments about public worship, reflections on poetry and Astronomy, and several poems. They are preserved in worn and carefully written manuscripts, and their quality varies from sentimental and religiously morbid to passages the narrator considers true and rather good poetry.
