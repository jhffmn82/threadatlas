------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIII.

The U.S. Naval Observatory lost workers during the Civil War as some resigned to go South and others were ordered elsewhere. Asaph Hall joined its staff in 1862, became a professor there in 1863, and later showed Abraham Lincoln astronomical objects through its telescope.
