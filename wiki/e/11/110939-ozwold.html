Oztrich in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Oztrich is a rare, exceptional ostrich-like green bird with blue eyes that cannot sing a single note. Guarding an enormous egg, it agrees to carry Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy toward the Emerald City if they transport the egg. In Tune Town it is told to keep quiet and dance, then joins the others in singing out of tune. It later bursts into the banquet hall carrying the three riders, causes confusion, and warns Ozma and the guests of the Gnome King. Its egg keeps Ruggedo from the magic treasures briefly, but Oztrich rushes to its newly hatched child and Ruggedo takes the magic belt and other objects.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

An Oztrich resembles an ostrich but has green feathers and blue eyes. The Oztrich encountered by Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy is guarding an enormous egg and agrees to carry them toward the Emerald City if they transport the egg.

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Ozwold says that he cannot sing despite being a bird, so the bandmaster advises him to keep quiet and dance. He dances through Tune Town, makes a song about running away, rejects the leather music rolls, and joins the others in singing out of tune.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

The oztrich bursts into the banquet hall through the French windows carrying Scraps, Peter, and Grumpy. It knocks over Tik Tok and three footmen, rushes toward Ozma, and loudly warns everyone to beware the Gnome King.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The oztrich’s roars cause confusion at the party, and its egg temporarily prevents Ruggedo from touching the magic treasures. It rushes to its newly hatched child, after which Ruggedo is able to seize the belt and other magical objects.

Ozwold in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Ozwold is a great green Oztrich, husband and parent of an egg and a baby oztrich. He eats rocks and cobblestones and runs exceptionally fast across trees, fences, farms, and hills. In the Bewilderness, he guards his egg until Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy offer to carry it, then carries them rapidly toward the Emerald City. Alarmed by news of Ruggedo’s escape, he rushes on, accidentally runs over the bookman, and follows Scraps onto the yellow brick road. After hiding his head in a flower pot, he gives Ozma a tail plume, receives an emerald necklace, and leaves with his baby secured on his back.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Ozwold is the Oztrich whom Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy meet in the Bewilderness. He initially stays to guard his egg, but agrees to carry the travelers after they offer to take the egg with them. He carries them rapidly out of the Bewilderness and to the yellow wall of the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Ozwold carries Peter, Grumpy, and Scraps toward the Emerald City and eats cobblestones along the way. After hearing about Ruggedo’s escape and plan, he becomes alarmed and races forward, accidentally running over the bookman. He is impatient with the group’s delay and then follows Scraps onto the yellow brick road.

CHAPTER 19 The Wizard Makes The Gnome King Visible

Ozwold emerges after hiding his head in a flower pot and asks to leave because the excitement is bad for his child. He gives Ozma a plume, receives an emerald necklace, and leaves the banquet hall with the baby oztrich secured on his back.
