Bœæ in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Bœæ, founded by Bœus, stands at the head of the Bay of Bœæ opposite Cythera, including a mainland promontory near Platanistus. It worships Artemis Saviour, contains a temple of Apollo and Nymphæum, and has a road to the temple of Artemis Limnas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Bœæ is opposite Cythera and includes the mainland promontory near Platanistus. The places of Epidelium and Epidaurus Limera are described in relation to its borders, and it contains Nymphæum and the road to the temple of Artemis Limnas.
