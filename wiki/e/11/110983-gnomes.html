Gnomes in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Gnomes are underground elves who mine precious stones and treasure in caverns. Thousands work with pick axes to dig jewels. Ruggedo says he rules five hundred thousand but is separated from them while imprisoned; after returning, they accept him, unload treasure, and serve him and Peter.
