Abstract written by a model

Chæronea unguents, made by boiling lilies, roses, narcissuses, and irises, relieve pain; rose unguent also preserves anointed wooden statues from rottenness.

CHAPTER XLI.

The unguents made at Chæronea are said to relieve pain. When an unguent made from roses is applied to wooden statues, it preserves them from rotting.
