Cleobœa is a virgin depicted in Charon’s boat holding a cist used in Demeter’s worship; she came from Paros and first brought Demeter’s mysteries to Thasos.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Cleobœa appears in Charon’s boat as a virgin, holding a cist used in the worship of Demeter. She is said to have first introduced the mysteries of Demeter from Paros to Thasos.
