iron base of the bowl of Alyattes

Abstract written by a model

The iron base of the bowl of Alyattes is a votive offering made by Glaucus of Chios. The only remaining part of offerings sent by Lydian kings to Apollo, it was welded from iron into a turret-like structure with ladder-like girders and outward-bending bars supporting the bowl.

iron base of the bowl of Alyattes is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XVI.

The iron base of the bowl of Alyattes is the only remaining part of the votive offerings that the Lydian kings sent to Apollo. Glaucus of Chios made it by welding iron; its structure rises like a turret, has ladder-like iron girders, and supports the bowl at its outward-bending bars.
