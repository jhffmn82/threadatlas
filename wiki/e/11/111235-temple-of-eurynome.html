Abstract written by a model

The temple of Eurynome stands at the Lymax-Neda confluence in rough, cypress-surrounded country. Difficult to access and holy since remote antiquity, it stays shut except its annual festival day, when Phigalians sacrifice publicly and privately.

CHAPTER XLI.

The temple of Eurynome stands where the Lymax and Neda join, in rough and difficult country surrounded by many cypresses. It remains shut except on one day each year, when the Phigalians perform public and private sacrifices.
