Teuthis in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Teuthis is a village near Thisoa, formerly a town and, in some accounts, also called Ornytus. Its leader quarreled with Agamemnon at Aulis, wounded Athene, and led Arcadians home; afterward disease afflicted him and the land became unfruitful. It contains temples of Aphrodite and Artemis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Teuthis is a village near Thisoa and was formerly a town. Its associated leader, also named Teuthis, quarreled with Agamemnon, wounded Athene in the thigh while she appeared in the semblance of Melas, and returned with his Arcadian detachment; afterward, a wasting disease afflicted him and his land became unfruitful. The village has temples of Aphrodite and Artemis.
