Phalanthus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

A Spartan, son of Agelaus and grandson of Stymphelus, led the Lacedæmonian colonization of Tarentum under an oracle. After failure, he interpreted his wife’s tears and captured Tarentum; he also survived a shipwreck by reaching shore on a dolphin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Phalanthus led the Lacedæmonian colonization of Tarentum under an oracle from Delphi. After initially failing to capture a city or gain land, he interpreted the oracle through his wife Æthra’s tears and captured Tarentum on the following night.
