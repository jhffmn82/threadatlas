A reservoir in the plain of Caphyæ holds back water from Orchomenus to protect a fertile district; within it, another body of water nearly as large as a river is absorbed into the ground.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

The reservoir in the plain of Caphyæ holds back water from the territory of Orchomenus so that it will not damage the fertile district. Within it, another body of water nearly as large as a river is absorbed into the ground.
