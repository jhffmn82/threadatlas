Dr. Watson's revolver in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Dr. Watson's revolver, short, handy, and serviceable, is used by Holmes to reproduce the crime pistol: altered with a cartridge removed, tied to a string and weighted stone, it strikes a bridge parapet and makes a matching chip.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We were compelled to spend the night at Winchester, as the formalities

Dr. Watson's revolver is used by Holmes as a reproduction of the pistol involved in the crime. With one cartridge removed and the weapon tied to a string and weighted stone, it strikes the bridge parapet and creates a second chip matching the original.
