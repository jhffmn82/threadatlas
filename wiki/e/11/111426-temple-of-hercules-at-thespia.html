Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVII.

The temple has a priestess who must remain a virgin for life. One tradition connects this requirement with Hercules sparing one daughter of Thestius after sleeping with the others, but the account suggests that the temple may be too ancient for Hercules son of Amphitryon and may instead have been founded for Hercules of the Idæan Dactyli.
