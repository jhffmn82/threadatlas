CHAPTER X.

These included Athene, Apollo, Miltiades, Erechtheus, Cecrops, Pandion, Leos, Antiochus, Ægeus, Acamas, Codrus, Theseus, and Phyleus. Antigonus, Demetrius, and Ptolemy were sent to Delphi later, with Ptolemy sent through goodwill and the Macedonians through fear.
