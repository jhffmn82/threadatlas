the Gold King's wife in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The Gold King’s wife is marked by jealousy and hatred toward Miss Dunbar and may have planned an alleged gun threat to drive her away. She denies attacking Miss Dunbar, remains in the house after assurances of safety, and hopes Miss Dunbar will beneficially influence the Gold King.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

To my surprise the Gold King took the reproof with equanimity.

The Gold King's wife remained in the house after he swore that she would not be molested again, partly because others depended on her and partly because she wanted to influence him through Miss Dunbar's beneficial effect on him. She denies attacking Miss Dunbar.
