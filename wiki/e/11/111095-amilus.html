Abstract written by a model

Amilus, about seven stades beyond the wells called Teneæ, was formerly a town; there its road divided, with branches toward Stymphelus and Pheneus.

CHAPTER XIII.

Amilus was a place about seven stades beyond the wells called Teneæ. The people said that it had formerly been a town, and the road there branched toward Stymphelus and Pheneus.
