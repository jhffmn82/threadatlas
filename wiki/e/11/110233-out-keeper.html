Out Keeper in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Out Keeper is Shutter Town’s gate keeper, refusing entry while hiding a pale, flat face behind bolted blue shutters. He hears but cannot see through their slats. High Boy carries him over the wall and drops him in pickle bushes; afterward he warns the town about the travelers and flees, shouting for everyone to shut up.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15

The Out Keeper hides his face behind blue shutters fastened with bolts and says he can hear through the slats but cannot see through them. High Boy seizes him by the seat of his pantaloons, carries him over the wall, and drops him in pickle bushes; Tighty then reveals his pale, flat face, warns the town about the travelers, and runs away shouting for everyone to shut up.
