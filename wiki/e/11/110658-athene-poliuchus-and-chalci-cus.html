1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The temple of Athene Poliuchus and Chalciœcus stood on Sparta’s citadel and was begun, according to tradition, by Tyndareus before being completed by the Lacedæmonians. Gitiadas of Sparta made its bronze statue and decorated the temple with scenes including the Labors of Hercules, the deeds of Castor and Pollux, the birth of Athene, and the figures of Amphitrite and Poseidon. Nearby stood temples of Athene the Worker and Martial Aphrodite, the temple of the Muses, statues of Lysander’s victories, and figures of Pausanias.
