Abstract written by a model

The journey to the Emerald City is undertaken by Philador with Herby. After preparing provisions and using a thinking cap to find the direction, they travel through a forest, cross a rushing river with a jumping rope, are separated from the castle by a storm, and are carried over purple mountains toward the capital by High Boy, joined by Joe King.
