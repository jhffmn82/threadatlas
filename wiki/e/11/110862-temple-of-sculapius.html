CHAPTER XIX.

The temple of Æsculapius contained statues of the god and his sons, paintings, and a spring in which Halirrhothius was said to have drowned. It also displayed a Sarmatic coat of mail, prompting an account of Sarmatian armour made from horse hoofs.
