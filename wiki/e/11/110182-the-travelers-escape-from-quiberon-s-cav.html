the travelers' escape from Quiberon's cave in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

An escape through a hidden waterfall passage: Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny flee Quiberon’s cave while he pursues. Benny stops him by breaking his tusks, and Quiberon becomes wedged in the narrowing passage.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

Trot, Benny, and the Scarecrow escape through a hidden passage behind the waterfall while Quiberon follows. Benny breaks Quiberon's tusks and then helps the others flee until Quiberon becomes jammed in the narrowing passage.

CHAPTER 11

Trot, the Scarecrow, and Benny escaped from Quiberon's cave through a waterfall. Quiberon pursued them but became wedged in a narrow passageway.
