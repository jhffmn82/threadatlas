Ptolemy VI Philometor

Abstract written by a model

Ptolemy VI Philometor, an eighth-generation descendant of Ptolemy I Soter and eldest son of Cleopatra I, was hated by her, banished to Cyprus, returned after Alexander fled and Cleopatra I was killed, regained Egypt, suppressed the Theban revolt, and received an Athenian bronze statue with Berenice.

CHAPTER VII.

Ptolemy VI Philometor was banished to Cyprus by his father at the urging of Cleopatra I, but returned after Alexander, his younger brother, fled and his mother was killed. He regained Egypt, suppressed the revolt of the Thebans, and was honored at Athens with a bronze statue alongside Berenice.
