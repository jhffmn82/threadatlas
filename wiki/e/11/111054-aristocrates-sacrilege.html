Aristocrates' sacrilege

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: defiled her there, his great impiety to the gods

Abstract written by a model

Aristocrates' sacrilege was his attempted seduction and defilement of Artemis Hymnia's maiden priestess at her altar; after the Arcadians learned of it, they stoned him to death and changed the priesthood.

Aristocrates' sacrilege is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER V.

Aristocrates attempted to seduce the maiden priestess of Artemis Hymnia and defiled her at the altar near the goddess's statue when she fled there for refuge. The Arcadians stoned him to death and thereafter appointed a woman rather than a maiden as priestess.
