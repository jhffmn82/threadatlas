Bond cottage in An Astronomer's Wife

Bond cottage stood atop Observatory Hill, occupied by the Halls from spring 1859 through their Cambridge stay; behind it they kept a vegetable garden, and Asaph Hall, Jr. was born there.

Narrative, section by section

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XII.

The Bond cottage stood at the top of Observatory Hill and was occupied by the Halls for three years, from the spring of 1859 until the end of their stay in Cambridge. The family kept a vegetable garden behind it, and Asaph Hall, Jr. was born there.
