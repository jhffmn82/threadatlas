Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK II.--CORINTH.

Cenchreae was one of Corinth's seaports, said to have been named after Cenchrias, the reputed son of Poseidon and Pirene. It contained a temple of Artemis, a shrine of Aphrodite, a brazen statue of Poseidon, and temples of Æsculapius and Isis.
