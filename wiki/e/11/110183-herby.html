Herby in The Giant Horse of Oz

The Giant Horse of Oz

The Medicine Man is named as one of the subjects of the story.

CHAPTER 8

Herby is a Gilliken medicine man who has studied cures and remedies for troubles such as impatience, bad tempers, and rudeness. After being trapped in a bottle by Mombi for thirty years, he is released by Philador and joins him on the journey to the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 9

Herby guides Philador through the forest with the thinking cap and uses pills and laugh lozenges from his medicine chest. He crosses the river with Philador on the jumping rope, meets Joe King and High Boy, and rides with them toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 14

Herby, the medicine man, travels with Philador and helps him on his quest. His medicine chest repeatedly spills its contents when he dismounts, but Trot helps restore and secure the chest with her belt before he continues riding High Boy.

CHAPTER 15

Herby is interested in the Out Keeper's unusual appearance and carries a medicine chest containing Keep Awake pills and yawn lozenges. He gives the remedies to High Boy, Trot, and Philador, making them alert and preventing their yawning.

CHAPTER 17

Herby insists that he prefers remaining a medicine chest rather than being restored to his former state. He gives High Boy pills intended to dull the horse's hunger.

CHAPTER 20

Herby returns to Oz and gives a lengthy speech of thanks after Ozma appoints him Court Physician. His pill boxes jingle whenever he comes near.
