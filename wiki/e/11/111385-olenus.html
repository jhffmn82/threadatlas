Abstract written by a model

Olenus was a small Achaean town near the river Pirus; weakened over time, it was abandoned, and its people moved to Piræ and Euryteæ.

Olenus is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XVIII.

Olenus was an Achaean town near the river Pirus. It was originally small, but its people later abandoned it because of its weakness and moved to Piræ and Euryteæ.
