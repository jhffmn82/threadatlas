Liparæans in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Liparæans, following the Pythian Priestess’s instruction, fight the Tyrrhenians with five triremes, capture four groups of five ships, and dedicate matching Apollo statues at Delphi.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVI.

The Liparæans followed the Pythian Priestess’s instruction to fight the Tyrrhenians with only five triremes. They captured four groups of five Tyrrhenian ships and dedicated at Delphi as many statues of Apollo as they had captured ships.
