Cyllene

Abstract written by a model

Cyllene is the arsenal and dockyard of Elis, about 120 stades away, with a fine harbor facing Sicily and temples of Æsculapius and Aphrodite. After Eira fell, maritime people and exiled Messenians took refuge there, wintered with Elis’s support, and planned a new home.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

Cyllene was the arsenal of Elis where the people of Pylos, Mothone, and other maritime towns took refuge after Eira fell. The exiled Messenians gathered there, wintered there with support from Elis, and considered where to establish a new home.

CHAPTER XXVI.

Cyllene lies about 120 stades from Elis, faces Sicily, and has a fine harbor for ships. Its dockyard belongs to the people of Elis, and the town has temples of Æsculapius and Aphrodite and an ithyphallic statue of Hermes.
