CHAPTER XXII.

The Messenians at the Sicilian Strait traditionally sent thirty-five boys, a choir-master, and a piper to Rhegium for a national feast. After all of them died in a sea disaster, they placed brazen effigies of the boys, the choir-master, and the piper at Olympia.
