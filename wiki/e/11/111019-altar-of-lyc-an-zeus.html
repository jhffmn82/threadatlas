Abstract written by a model

The altar of Lycæan Zeus is an earth mound on Mount Lycæus’s highest ridge, overlooking most of the Peloponnese. In front, two east-facing pillars bear very ancient golden eagles; secret sacrifices to Lycæan Zeus are performed there.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

The altar of Lycæan Zeus is a mound of earth on the highest ridge of Mount Lycæus, from which most of the Peloponnese is visible. Two east-facing pillars and ancient golden eagles stand in front of it, and sacrifices to Lycæan Zeus are performed there secretly.
