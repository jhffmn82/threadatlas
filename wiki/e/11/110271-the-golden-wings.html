the golden wings in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The golden wings emerge when the golden pear bursts open, fasten to Akbad's shoulders, and carry him swiftly south toward the capital. They later lift Akbad, Trot, and Benny, despite Benny's great stone weight. After Akbad picks the pear, they attach again but refuse his commands or removal, allowing flight only to the island's shore.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

The golden wings emerge when the golden pear bursts into a dazzling transformation. They fasten themselves to Akbad's shoulders and carry him swiftly and silently toward the south.

CHAPTER 4

The magic wings carry Akbad steadily toward the capital and later lift him, Trot, and Benny into the air. They are strong enough to carry Benny's great stone weight.

CHAPTER 6

The golden wings attach themselves to Akbad's shoulders after he picks the golden pear. They later refuse to obey his commands or detach from him, and they allow him to fly only as far as the island's shore.
