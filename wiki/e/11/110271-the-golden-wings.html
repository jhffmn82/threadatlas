Abstract written by a model

The golden wings emerge when the golden pear bursts open, fasten to Akbad's shoulders, and carry him swiftly south toward the capital. They later lift Akbad, Trot, and Benny, despite Benny's great stone weight. After Akbad picks the pear, they attach again but refuse his commands or removal, allowing flight only to the island's shore.
