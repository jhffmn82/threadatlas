Arene in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Arene, built by Aphareus and named for Œbalus’s daughter, housed Aphareus and his sons before Lycus arrived fleeing Athens.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Arene was built by Aphareus and named after the daughter of Œbalus, who was his wife and uterine sister. Aphareus and his sons lived there, and Lycus came there after fleeing from Athens.
