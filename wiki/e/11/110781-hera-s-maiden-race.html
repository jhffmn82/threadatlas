Abstract written by a model

Hera’s maiden race at Olympia is a maiden race on a course shortened one-sixth of a stade; maidens run hair down in short tunics, and victors receive olive crowns.

CHAPTER XV.

Hera’s maiden race is held at the regular race-course at Olympia but is one-sixth of a stade shorter. Maidens run with their hair down, wearing a short tunic below the knee with the right shoulder bare to the breast.
