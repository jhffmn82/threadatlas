Abstract written by a model

is_a fenced village Phana was a fenced village in Ætolia whose inhabitants depended on a small, measured supply of water from a well near the walls. The Achæans captured it after making that water undrinkable.

Phana is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
