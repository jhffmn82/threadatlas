Abstract written by a model

Corœbus becomes first victor at Olympia when Iphitus restores the discontinued games, with racing the only prize event, and is buried in his tomb.

CHAPTER XXVI.

Corœbus was victor when Iphitus restored the Olympian games after they had long been discontinued, when prizes were offered only for racing. An inscription on his tomb calls him the first victor at Olympia.
