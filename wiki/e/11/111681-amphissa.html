Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

Amphissa was about 120 stades from Delphi and was the largest and most famous town of the Ozolian Locrians. It contained the tombs of Amphissa and Andræmon, a temple and statue of Athene in its citadel, and celebrated rites for youths called Anactes. The inhabitants later joined themselves to the Ætolians, and many Ætolians may also have migrated there after Augustus established Nicopolis.
