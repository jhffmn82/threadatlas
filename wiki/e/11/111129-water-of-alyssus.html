Also called: the water at Cynætha, this water

Abstract written by a model

The water of Alyssus is cold and beneficial for man’s cure: when an afflicted person drinks it, it is said to cure hurt, including the bite of a mad dog; unlike Styx near Pheneus, it is not harmful.

CHAPTER XIX.

The water of Alyssus is cold and is said to cure people who drink it after being bitten by a mad dog or receiving another hurt. It is contrasted with the water called Styx near Pheneus, which is harmful to people.
