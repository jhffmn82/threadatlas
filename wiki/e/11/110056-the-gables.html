Abstract written by a model

The Gables is Harold Stackhurst’s coaching establishment, housing young men preparing for professions. Stackhurst, McPherson, and Murdoch work there. After McPherson’s body is brought there, students report finding his dog; Stackhurst is found in his study, then leaves to bring the stricken Murdoch to Sherlock Holmes and later dismisses him.

The Gables is in one work on this site, The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
