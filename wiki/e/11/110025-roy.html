Abstract written by a model

Roy is Professor Presbury's faithful wolf-hound, kept at the household stables. He repeatedly attacks only Presbury, reacting violently to Presbury's transformed behavior and special appearances. After Presbury torments him, Roy's collar slips and he bites deeply into Presbury's throat; he is then banished and chained near the stables, barking at Edith's window.
