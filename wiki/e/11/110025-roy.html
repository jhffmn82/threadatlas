THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

Roy is Professor Presbury's faithful wolf-hound. He attacks Presbury on several occasions while attacking no one else, is eventually banished to the stables and chained near them, and barks furiously when Presbury appears at Edith's window.
