Abstract written by a model

Patch is a busy, prosperous but unpleasant little kingdom in the Winkie Country of Oz, the seven hundred and fifth smaller country of Oz. Located below the Kingdom of Ann of Oogaboo and near Kuma Party’s cottage, it has cross roads, patched shabby houses, a castle, and cotton fields divided into separate colored patches for its subjects. Its people grow cotton, make and repair quilts, and its rulers are chosen by succession. A queen must be cross, scold the Patch-workers, and do the palace work. Without a ruler, Patch will go to pieces within four days. Scrapper and Piecer return there with Scraps, who is crowned Queen of the Quilties. Later, Ruggedo and Peter travel toward Patch because a magic cloak can be properly repaired nowhere else; they reach its castle after crossing a wood and broad cotton field. Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy are then imprisoned and forced to work, but escape, leaving Patch again threatened without Scraps. It is later where a small mortal boy is lost before Kuma Party’s hand helps him escape.

Patch is in one work on this site, The Gnome King of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

Patch is a busy but unpleasant little Kingdom in the Winkie Country of Oz. Its people grow and work different-colored cotton patches, make and repair quilts, and live among cross roads and patched, shabby houses; it will go to pieces if no new ruler is found within four days.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

Patch is the seven hundred and fifth smaller country among the countries of Oz. It is the Quilty Kingdom to which Scrapper and Piecer return with Scraps, who is crowned its queen.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Patch is the country ruled by the Queen of Patch. Its queens are expected to be cross, scold the Patch-workers, and perform all the work in the palace.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

The Kingdom of Patch is in the Winkie Country of Oz, just below the Kingdom of Ann of Oogaboo. Potaroo says the magic cloak cannot be properly repaired anywhere else, so Ruggedo and Peter travel toward it.

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Patch is the place Peter asks how to reach, and Kuma says it is near his cottage, just below the Kingdom of Queen Ann of Oogaboo. Peter and Ruggedo eventually reach a castle there after crossing a wood and a broad cotton field.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Patch is the little kingdom where Peter, Scraps, and Grumpy are held as prisoners and forced to work. After their escape, Grumpy says that Patch will go to pieces in four days without Scraps as its Queen.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

The Kingdom of Patch is the place where the small mortal boy is lost and from which Kuma Party's right arm and hand are sent to help him escape.
