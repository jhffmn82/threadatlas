Mysæum in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Mysæum is a site about 60 stades from Pellene, containing the temple of Mysian Demeter, said to have been built by Mysius of Argos. It has a tree-filled grove and abundant springs. Its seven-day festival culminates in women’s rites, followed by joking and banter when men return.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVII.

Mysæum lies about 60 stades from Pellene and contains the temple of Mysian Demeter, which was said to have been built by Mysius of Argos. Its seven-day festival includes a night when men, male dogs, and other males are excluded while women perform the customary rites; when the men return, the women and men jest and banter with one another. The site has a grove of many kinds of trees and abundant springs.
