Euthymus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Euthymus was a boxer from Locri near Zephyrium, reputed locally to be son of the River Cæcinus rather than Astycles. He won Olympic boxing in the 74th, 76th, and 77th Olympiads, defeated the Hero of Temesa, married the offered maiden, and reportedly lived long without dying like other men.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Euthymus was a boxer from Locri near the promontory of Zephyrium, reputed by local people to be the son of the River Cæcinus rather than Astycles. He won Olympic boxing victories in the 74th, 76th, and 77th Olympiads, defeated the Hero of Temesa, married the maiden offered to the Hero, and was said to have lived to an advanced age without dying like other men.
