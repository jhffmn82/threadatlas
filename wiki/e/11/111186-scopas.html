Scopas in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Scopas was a Greek sculptor born at Paros and active in the first half of the fourth century. He decorated the temple of Athena at Tegea and the Mausoleum. His art emphasized violent attitudes, pathos, and tragic action; the Niobe group was attributed to him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In Athens, after Pheidias, the greatest sculptures were those

Scopas was born at Paros and lived during the first half of the fourth century. He did decorative work at the temple of Athena at Tegea and at the Mausoleum, and his art was associated with violent attitudes, pathos, tragic action, and the Niobe group and Raging Bacchante.
