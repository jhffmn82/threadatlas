Orpah in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Orpah is an old mer-man from the Ozure Isles, formerly the faithful keeper of King Cheeriobed’s sea horses. Made lame and given golden crutches by Cheeriobed, he was captured after escaping Quiberon’s cave and spent nearly twenty years imprisoned and enslaved in Cave City, where he guarded its entrance. He guides travelers through the city, recounts Mombi’s wickedness and the attacks on his people, then rescues Trot and aids Benny and the Scarecrow after the blue-ray explosion. Escaping at last, he returns to Cheeriobed as a messenger with news of the travelers and swims toward Queen Orin’s boat.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 11

Orpah, an old mer-man and former keeper of Cheeriobed's sea horses, guides the travelers through Cave City. He explains that Mombi abducted the Queen of the Ozure Isles, Quiberon attacked the Islanders, and he escaped Quiberon's cave before becoming a prisoner and slave in Cave City.

CHAPTER 12

Orpah rescues Trot from under Lake Orizon and helps Benny and the Scarecrow reach the shore. He explains that the blue ray caused the explosion and carried them across the lake, then returns to the Ozure Isles to tell Cheeriobed the news.

CHAPTER 16

Orpah returns to Cheeriobed after nearly twenty years of imprisonment in Cave City. He tells the King how Trot and her companions escaped Quiberon and crossed the lake, then goes swimming toward Queen Orin's boat when she returns.
