The selection of a new ruler of Patch is an immediate succession process required after a ruler goes to pieces. Following the Imperial Scrap Book, Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper cut and follow the golden Royal Spool of Succession; a ruler must be found within four days.

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The selection of a new ruler of Patch is required whenever a ruler goes to pieces and must follow the method in the Imperial Scrap Book. Prime Piecer and Chief Scrapper cut the golden spool from the royal spool-cotton tree and follow it because Patch will go to pieces if no ruler is found within four days.
