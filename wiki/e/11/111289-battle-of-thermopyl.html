Abstract written by a model

The Battle of Thermopylæ was a Greek victory over the Galati: narrow, rocky terrain worsened the poorly armoured, inexperienced Galati’s defeat and disorderly retreat, causing many deaths; 40 Greeks were killed.

CHAPTER XXI.

In the Battle of Thermopylæ, the Greeks defeated the Galati, whose inferior armour and lack of experience were worsened by the narrow and rocky terrain. The Galati retreated in disorder, and many were trampled or lost in the marsh; 40 Greeks were killed, while the number of barbarian dead could not be accurately determined.
