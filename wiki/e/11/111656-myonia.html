Myonia

Abstract written by a model

Myonia is a town above Amphissa, about 30 stades away and facing the mainland. It honors Zeus with a shield at Olympia and has high-ground sanctuaries: a grove and altar of the Mild Deities for nightly sacrifices, and Poseidonium.

Myonia is in one work on this site, Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

Myonia lay above Amphissa, about 30 stades away, facing the mainland. Its inhabitants presented a shield to Zeus at Olympia, and the town had a grove and altar of the Mild Deities where nightly sacrifices were performed, as well as a grove and temple of Poseidon called Poseidonium.
