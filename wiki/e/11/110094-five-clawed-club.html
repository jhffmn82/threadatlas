Abstract written by a model

A singular, ingenious spiked club made by Leonardo has a leaden head and five long steel nails arranged like a lion's paw. It delivers the fatal blow to Ronder while concealing the murder's true cause. Afterwards, its fate is unknown; the woman suggests it may lie in a deep green pool at a chalk-pit's base.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE VEILED LODGER

The spiked club was made by Leonardo with a leaden head and five long steel nails arranged to resemble a lion's paw. It was used to deliver the fatal blow to Ronder while concealing the murder's true cause.

We sat in silence for some time after the unhappy woman had told her

The five-clawed club is described as the most singular and ingenious part of the woman's story. She does not know what Leonardo did with it and suggests that it may lie in the deep green pool at the base of a chalk-pit.
