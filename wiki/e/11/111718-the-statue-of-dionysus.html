the statue of Dionysus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The statue of Dionysus, said to be made by Hephæstus and gifted by Zeus to Dardanus, was in Eurypylus’s chest after Ilium’s capture; its sight maddened him and its arrival ended sacrifice to Artemis Triclaria.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The statue of Dionysus was inside the chest received by Eurypylus after the capture of Ilium. Seeing it caused Eurypylus to lose his mind, while its arrival with him ultimately coincided with the ending of the human sacrifice to Artemis Triclaria.
