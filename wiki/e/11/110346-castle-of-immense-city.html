castle of Immense City in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

The castle of Immense City is the Big Wigs’ royal Giant residence, with a giant dining hall, kitchen, Princess Elma’s playroom and bedroom, and a nursery. Reddy enters seeking the Hungry Tiger, discovers its secret, takes Elma’s big wig, opens the doors, and escapes with the tiger.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

The castle of Immense City contains an enormous dining hall, a kitchen, Princess Elma's vast playroom, and her bedroom. Reddy enters it after being dropped on a high window ledge and searches through it for the Hungry Tiger.

CHAPTER 17

The Giant Castle contains a nursery where Princess Elma keeps the Hungry Tiger and where the Big Wigs sleep without their wigs. Reddy discovers the castle's secret, takes Princess Elma's big wig, unbolts the doors, and escapes with the Hungry Tiger.
