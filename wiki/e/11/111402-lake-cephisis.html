lake Cephisis

Also called: Lake Cephisis, the lake Cephisis

Abstract written by a model

Lake Cephisis, also called Copais by some, receives the rivers Cephisus and Melas and covers much of the Orchomenian district. In winter, when the South Wind generally prevails, it spreads over most of the country. Nearby towns, including Athenæ and Eleusis, were said by the Bœotians to have been swept away by a winter flood. Its eels are especially fine to eat.

CHAPTER XXIV.

Lake Cephisis, also called Copais by some, receives the river Cephisus. Several small towns formerly stood near it, including Athenæ and Eleusis, but the Bœotians say that a winter flood swept them away. Its fish resemble those of other lakes, while its eels are especially fine and good to eat.

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

The lake Cephisis receives the river Melas and covers a large part of the Orchomenian district. In winter, when the South Wind generally prevails, its water spreads over most of the country; Homer knows of the lake but not as a creation of Hercules.
