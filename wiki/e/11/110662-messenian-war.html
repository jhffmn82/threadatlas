Messenian War in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Messenian war, involving Lacedæmonians with allies and Messenians with mercenaries, is named for Messenian disasters and runs from Amphea’s capture to Aristodemus’s death and beyond.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

The war is called the Messenian war because of the disasters that befell the Messenians, rather than because of the attacking force. Myron describes events from the capture of Amphea to the death of Aristodemus, while Rhianus begins with events after the battle at the great trench.
