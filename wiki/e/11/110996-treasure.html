treasure in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

The pirate treasure consists of old chests, boxes, and rotting sacks holding gold, silver, and state jewels taken from islanders. It fills the Blunderoo’s hold, where Peter finds it and Polacky’s diary. When the ship reaches Ev, Ruggedo orders Kaliko to unload it into the silver grotto.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

The pirate treasure fills the Blunderoo's hold with old sea chests, boxes, and rotting sacks containing gold and silver coins and jewels. Peter finds the treasure and the diary of Polacky, the Plunderer, while Ruggedo focuses on the jewels.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

The treasure is aboard the Blunderoo when it reaches Ev. Ruggedo orders Kaliko to have the treasure unloaded and stored in the silver grotto.
