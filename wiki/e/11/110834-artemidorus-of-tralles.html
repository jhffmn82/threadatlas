Artemidorus of Tralles in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Artemidorus of Tralles, a pancratium victor, was initially too young to win at Olympia; he later defeated boys, unbearded competitors, and leading men at Smyrna, then won Olympia among men.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII.

Artemidorus of Tralles was too young to win the pancratium among the boys at Olympia, but later defeated the boys, the unbearded competitors, and the best men at Smyrna on the same day. He won the Olympic pancratium among men in the 212th Olympiad.
