CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

Shampoozle is the green-soap Sultan of Suds, ruling from an ivory soap throne. He orders Scraps to be salaamed and plans to turn Grumpy into a bath mat, Scraps into washcloths, and Peter into soap fat, but the three escape through a window.
