Pephnos in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Pephnos is a seaside place about twenty stades from Thalamæ, with a small same-named island. Thalamæ’s people say Castor and Pollux were born there; small open-air bronze statues of them withstand winter waves unmoved.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Pephnos lies by the sea about twenty stades from Thalamæ and has a small island of the same name in front of it. The people of Thalamæ say that Castor and Pollux were born there, and the island contains small bronze statues of them that remain unmoved despite winter waves.
