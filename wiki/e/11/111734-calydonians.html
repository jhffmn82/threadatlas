Calydonians in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Calydonians, inhabitants of Calydon, became insane and died after Coresus appealed to Dionysus; consulting Dodona, they learned the god’s wrath would persist until Callirhoe or a volunteer was sacrificed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

The Calydonians became insane as if drunk after Coresus appealed to Dionysus, and they died beside themselves. They consulted the oracle at Dodona and learned that Dionysus's wrath would continue until Callirhoe or a volunteer was sacrificed.
