The Presbury case concerns unexplained changes in Professor Presbury's behavior, secret communications, a guarded box, Roy's attacks, nocturnal crawling, and Edith's sighting of Presbury at her window. Holmes considers the case obscure but believes its incidents may be linked to particular dates.
