Chopfyt's creation in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Chopfyt is a body created by Ku-Klip, who uses Magic Glue to join mismatched parts from the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier, adds a heart, legs, an arm, and a selected head, then names the result from Nick Chopper and Captain Fyter.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

Ku-Klip created Chopfyt by using Magic Glue to join mismatched body parts from the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier. He completed the body with a heart, attached one leg from each man, used one arm, and finally selected a head from the cupboard; he named the result Chopfyt by combining parts of Nick Chopper's and Captain Fyter's names.
