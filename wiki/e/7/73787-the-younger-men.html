the younger men

Also called: young men, younger

Abstract written by a model

About six hundred young men reject peace and resolve to surrender their lives and their country’s liberty; later they approve killing the inhabitants and burning the houses, break through the enemy’s camp, and flee to nearby hills.

In the utmost borders of Lycia, the Marmareofies*, who inha*

The younger men initially rejected advice to make peace and resolved to surrender their lives and their country's liberty together. They approved the later counsel to burn the houses, and they broke through the enemy's camp and fled to the nearby hills.
