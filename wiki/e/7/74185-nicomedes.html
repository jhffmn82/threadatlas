Nicomedes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Nicomedes, son of Prusias and commander over all Cyprus, routed and killed Prusias at Jupiter's temple, then gained the kingdom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXI.

Nicomedes routed his father Prusias and forced him to flee for shelter to the temple of Jupiter. He then killed Prusias there and obtained the kingdom through that act.
