Charidemus the Athenian in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Charidemus, an Athenian mercenary commander and former adviser under Philip of Acarnania, urged Darius to appoint an experienced general; after insulting Darius’s advisers, he was bound for execution.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Charidemus advised Darius to remain in Asia and appoint an experienced general, arguing that one hundred thousand men would be sufficient. After calling Darius's advisers cowards, he was bound and delivered to the guards for execution.
