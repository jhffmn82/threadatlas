autofill forms

application · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: AF, autofill forms/user services

Abstract written by a model

Autofill forms is a downstream application for evaluating personalized agents. It uses a user’s memories to generate key entities, such as a mobile number, for completing online flight and hotel booking forms. Evaluated by Exact Match accuracy, GPT-4 with EMG-RAG reaches 92.86%, outperforming M-RAG by 2.2 points.
