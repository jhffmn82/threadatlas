the little cottage in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The little cottage, home to Toto, the blind grandmother, and later Bruin, hosts spring farewells, Bruin’s secret stay, and laughing forest-friend visits.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The little cottage is the place where the friends exchange their spring farewells and where Bruin secretly remains. It continues to be home to Toto, the blind grandmother, and Bruin, and its kitchen is filled with laughter when the forest friends visit and share news.
