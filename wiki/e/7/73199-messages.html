Also called: message, nerve messages, orders

Abstract written by a model

Messages travel along separate nerve threads so they do not become mixed or confused. When a finger touches a hot stove, a message tells the brain that the finger will burn, and the brain sends a message to the muscles to contract, bend the joint, and pull the finger away.
