Abstract written by a model

Ugu carries out a coordinated theft of Oz’s magical possessions, including Ozma’s Magic Picture and other magical things, Glinda’s books, chemical compounds, instruments of sorcery, and the Wizard’s bag of magic tools. The theft appears meant to stop Ozma’s friends from locating or rescuing her. Ugu uses the Magic Dishpan to take the items to the Wicker Castle, where he arranges and stores them.
