Also called: Greek cities, cities, the Greek cities
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Greek cities of Sicily were freed from tyrants by Timoleon, received into his protection as confederates, and became more populated and prosperous under the resulting peace.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Greek cities of Sicily variously opposed or submitted to Agathocles; some were assigned to Carthage by treaty, while others were declared free before many were brought under his control.

The Greek cities of Sicily opposed or submitted to Agathocles at different times. Heraclea, Selinus, and Himera were assigned to Carthage by treaty, while the other cities under Syracusan power were declared free before Agathocles later brought many of them under his control.
