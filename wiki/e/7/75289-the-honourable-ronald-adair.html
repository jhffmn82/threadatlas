the Honourable Ronald Adair

Abstract written by a model

The Honourable Ronald Adair was a young aristocrat, the Earl of Maynooth’s second son, living with his mother and sister Hilda at 427 Park Lane. Formerly engaged to Miss Edith Woodley, he regularly and cautiously played card games at the Baldwin, Cavendish, and Bagatelle Clubs and was usually a winner. He played with Colonel Sebastian Moran and won considerable money; Holmes believed Adair discovered Moran cheating and threatened exposure. On March 30, 1894, Adair returned home at ten and was found fatally shot before 11:20 in his locked second-floor sitting-room. Moran killed him by firing an expanding bullet from an air-gun through the open front window.
