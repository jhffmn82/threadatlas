Greek cities' liberation

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: free Athens, set all the Grecian cities at liberty

Abstract written by a model

Antigonus ordered Demetrius to set the Grecian cities at liberty, beginning with Athens. Demetrius liberated Athens and Megara, and Antigonus later ordered him to convene representatives of the confederate cities to consider the public good of Greece.
