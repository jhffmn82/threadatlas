Abstract written by a model

Lucius Aemilius was a Roman consul and censor who conquered Perseus and was regarded as the bravest Roman of his age. He enriched the Roman treasury with gold from Spain while refusing private use of public wealth, despite his authority in Spain and Macedonia. Compared favourably with Aristides and Epaminondas for abstinence from covetousness, he died amid widespread sorrow, leaving little private wealth.

74. Lucius iEmilius, who conquered Perseus, and was fi consul

Lucius iEmilius conquered Perseus and was consul and censor. He was regarded as the bravest man of his age among the Romans, and his death caused widespread sorrow. Although he controlled great wealth and authority in Spain and Macedonia, he did not convert it to private use; after his death, his adopted sons had to sell land to pay his wife's dowry.
