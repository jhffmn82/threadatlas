Abstract written by a model

A dove is sent ahead to test the Cyanean rocks on Phineus’s instruction; it survives the crossing, losing only the ends of its tail-feathers, so the Argonauts should follow.

There Phineus, son of Agenor, had his home by the sea, Phineus who

Phineus instructs the Argonauts to send a dove between the Cyanean rocks before attempting the passage themselves. If the dove survives, they should follow; if it perishes, they must turn back.
