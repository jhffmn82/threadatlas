CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Gastric juice is prepared by the lining of the stomach and is kept ready for use. It mixes with food and helps change it into a thin, grayish fluid. Alcohol injures the gastric juice so that it cannot mix properly with food.
