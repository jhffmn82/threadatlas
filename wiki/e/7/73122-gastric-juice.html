gastric juice in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Gastric juice is prepared by the lining of the stomach and kept ready for use; it mixes with food, changing it into a thin, grayish fluid, but alcohol injures it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Gastric juice is prepared by the lining of the stomach and is kept ready for use. It mixes with food and helps change it into a thin, grayish fluid. Alcohol injures the gastric juice so that it cannot mix properly with food.
