Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Bones form the body's framework and include long bones, short bones, ribs, shoulder-blades, collar-bones, and the bones of the spine. They contain lime and gristle; children's bones have more gristle, while old people's bones contain more lime and therefore break more easily.
