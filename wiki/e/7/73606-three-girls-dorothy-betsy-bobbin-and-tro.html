three girls (Dorothy, Betsy Bobbin, and Trot) in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Dorothy, Betsy Bobbin, and Trot are close friends living in rooms at Ozma’s royal palace. As travelers with the Wizard and animals, they plan a journey to Munchkin Country and seek Ozma; later, they eat yellow berries and sleep together under a blanket.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Dorothy, Betsy Bobbin, and Trot are close friends and live in rooms in Ozma’s royal palace. They plan a journey to the Munchkin Country and work together to discover where Ozma has gone.

CHAPTER 10

The three girls find delicious yellow berries in the stony country and eat them to relieve their hunger. They sleep together in a row under a blanket.
