Lucius Sylla in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Lucius Sylla was a Roman consul, military leader, and later dictator. He gained fame through brave actions, helped Cneius Pompeius defeat the Italians, compelled neighboring territories and cities to obey Rome, and financed war by robbing temples. He opposed and defeated Marius in civil war, fought Mithridates in Asia, and gained control of Rome’s political affairs.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVIL

Sylla helped Cneius Pompeius defeat the Italians and later compelled neighboring territories and cities to obey the Romans. He opposed Marius in a Roman civil war, prevailed, and became dictator. He later fought in Asia against Mithridates and ultimately gained control over Rome's political affairs.

BOOK XXXVII.

Lucius Sylla gained great fame through brave and distinguished military actions and was regarded as likely to attain the highest glory. He robbed the temples of Apollo at Delphi, Esculapius at Epidaurus, and Jupiter at Olympus to obtain money for war, later claiming that the gods assisted him because they had supplied him with funds.
