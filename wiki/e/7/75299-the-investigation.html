Abstract written by a model

The investigation into Captain Peter Carey’s murder initially makes no progress under Stanley Hopkins. Holmes examines forced entry into Carey’s hut, missing logbook pages, and a notebook referring to valuable securities. Holmes, Watson, and Hopkins stake out the hut, capture Neligan, and treat the bloodstained notebook as significant evidence.
