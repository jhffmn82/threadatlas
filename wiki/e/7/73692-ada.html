Abstract written by a model

Ada, Idrieus’s sister and wife, succeeds him as queen of Caria; after meeting Alexander during his march through Caria, she requests restoration of her ancestral kingdom, which he grants, gaining Grecian cities’ support.

CHAP. It.

Ada, a noble woman of the Carian royal family, met Alexander while he was marching through Caria. After she asked him to restore her ancestral kingdom, Alexander gave it to her and thereby gained the support of the Grecian cities.
