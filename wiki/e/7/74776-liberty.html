liberty in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Liberty is presented as a common good defended by Dinocrates, restored from slavery and tyrants by Dion, Philip, and Timoleon, and valued enough that Uticans refuse surrender; Xenodochus and Dinocrates later claim to defend or restore it against Agathocles's rule.

CHAP. III.

The Syracusans believed that Dion restored their liberty after they had lived as slaves for fifty years. Philip likewise restored the Thessalian cities to liberty after subduing their tyrants.
