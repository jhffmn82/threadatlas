the Temple of the Golden Dragon

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Golden Temple, the House of Gold, the Jewelled Shrine, the Sacred Shrine, the Temple, the Temple itself, the Temple of the Saki-Pan, the great temple of the Golden Dragon

Abstract written by a model

In Toto's Merry Winter, the great temple of the Golden Dragon is a sacred destination where Chop-Chin arrives at sunset, with successive guarded courts and the Temple of the Golden Dragon inside; the Golden Dragon is kept on an altar by four priests, who rejoice when it is returned after being found outside. In Toto's Merry Winter, the Temple of the Golden Dragon is the House of Gold beyond guarded courts, entered through a golden gate, where priests lead Chop-Chin blindfolded to its Jewelled Shrine.
