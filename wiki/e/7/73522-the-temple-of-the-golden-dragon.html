the great temple of the Golden Dragon in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The great temple of the Golden Dragon is a sacred destination where Chop-Chin arrives at sunset. It has successive guarded courts and includes the Temple of the Golden Dragon. The Golden Dragon is kept on an altar by four priests, who rejoice when returning it after it is found outside.
