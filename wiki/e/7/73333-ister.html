Ister in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Ister is a broad, exceedingly deep river rising beyond the north wind in the Rhipaean mountains. Within Thracian and Scythian boundaries, it divides into streams, islands, and mouths, reaching seas and a southern gulf. Its channels around three-cornered Peuee shape the heroes' escape and the Colchians' pursuit.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

Ister is described as a broad and deep river whose sources lie beyond the north wind in the Rhipaean mountains. Its stream later divides, sending one part toward the Ionian sea and another toward a southern gulf, and the heroes sail toward it.

Now some of the Colchians, in a vain search, passed out from Pontus

Ister divides around the three-cornered island of Peuee and reaches the sea through two mouths, called Narex and Fair. The heroes and Colchians use its channels and islands during their pursuit and escape.
