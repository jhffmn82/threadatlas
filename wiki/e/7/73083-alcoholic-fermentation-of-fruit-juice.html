alcoholic fermentation of fruit juice in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Alcoholic fermentation of fruit juice is the change of sugar in grape-juice or apple-juice into alcohol and bubbles of gas. Yeast helps the change; bubbles show it is progressing. Alcohol remains in the juice, while gas rises into the air.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

_] CHAPTER IV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Alcoholic fermentation of fruit juice occurs when the sugar in grape-juice or apple-juice changes into alcohol and bubbles of gas. The alcohol remains in the juice while the gas rises and goes into the air.

] CHAPTER V. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The unit describes yeast helping to change sugar into gas and alcohol. The brewer knows the work is progressing by watching for bubbles of gas, which show that sugar is going and alcohol is coming.
