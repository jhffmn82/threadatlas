alcoholic fermentation of fruit juice

Abstract written by a model

Alcoholic fermentation of fruit juice is the change of sugar in grape-juice or apple-juice into alcohol and bubbles of gas. Yeast helps the change; bubbles show it is progressing. Alcohol remains in the juice, while gas rises into the air.
