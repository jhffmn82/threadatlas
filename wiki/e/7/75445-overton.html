THE ADVENTURE OF THE MISSING THREE-QUARTER

Cyril Overton, captain of the Cambridge University rugby team, asks Sherlock Holmes to find Godfrey Staunton, his indispensable right wing three-quarter. He reports Staunton’s disappearance, explains the team’s dependence on him, and consults the other players after Holmes begins the investigation.
