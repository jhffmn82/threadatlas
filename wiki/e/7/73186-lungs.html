lungs

Also called: THE LUNGS, the lungs

Abstract written by a model

The lungs are useful organs in the chest, on each side of and beside the heart. Containing air-cells, they work steadily day and night: the cells expand to take in air and contract to send it out. They need fresh air and room to expand and work; bending over cramps them. Using fresh air, they purify dark, impure blood, making it bright, pure, and red, and breathe out impurities as waste matter.

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The lungs are useful organs located in the chest.

CHAPTER XV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The lungs are beside the heart in the chest and help change impure, bluish blood into pure, red blood. Their air-cells expand to take in air and contract to send it out. They need fresh air and plenty of room, and they work steadily by night and day.

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The lungs use fresh air in making the dark, impure blood bright and pure again.
