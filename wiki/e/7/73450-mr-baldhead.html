Mr. Baldhead in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Mr. Baldhead is an old hermit from Ireland who lives in Baldhead’s cave with a broken, lame leg. Weak, pale, and nearly starving after more than a week confined without food, he begins to recover when water and food arrive. Though he first faints at Bruin’s presence, he welcomes the bear after learning of his charitable mission. He plays Russian backgammon with Bruin, owns and reads books for amusement, shelters Ger-Falcon for winter, and tells Toto, Bruin, and Pigeon Pretty an Irish fairy story.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII.

Baldhead has been confined to his cave for more than a week with a broken leg and no food. He is weak, pale, and nearly starving, but water and food begin to restore him. He initially faints when he realizes a bear is present, then welcomes Bruin after learning about the bear's charitable mission.

_" CHAPTER XII.

The hermit is recovering from a lame leg in his cave and plays Russian backgammon with Bruin. He feeds broth to Ger-Falcon, promises to support him through the winter, and agrees to tell Toto, Bruin, and Pigeon Pretty an Irish fairy story.
