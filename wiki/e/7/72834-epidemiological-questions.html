Abstract written by a model

Epidemiological questions are natural, free-form questions from specialists’ work, answered using real-world electronic health records and claims databases. They are challenging because of medical terminology and complex SQL. They often unfold through selecting populations, relating events within time frames, aggregating statistics, and calculating basic measures such as ratios. A dataset includes 15 pertinent OMOP query library SQL queries, with RAG improving performance across tested scenarios.

Abstract

Epidemiological questions are answered by querying electronic health records and claims databases. Addressing them is challenging because of intricate medical terminology and the need for complex SQL queries.

2

The epidemiological questions were contributed by specialists based on typical questions from their work. They are written in natural, free-form language and often require selecting populations, relating events within a specific time frame, calculating aggregation statistics, and performing basic mathematical operations.

5

The dataset focuses on answering epidemiological questions using real-world data. Fifteen SQL queries from the OMOP query library that were pertinent to epidemiological research were included, and RAG improved performance across all tested scenarios.
