A large, well-fortified city of the Isaurians, resolutely defended until many citizens fell after two days of assaults; it was burned, and silver and gold were found in its ruins.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Perdiccas and king Philip having overcome Ariarathes, delivered

The city of the Isaurians was large, well fortified, and defended by resolute men. After two days of assaults, its defenders continued resisting until many citizens had fallen; the city was then burned, and Perdiccas's soldiers later found silver and gold in the ruins.
