Abstract written by a model

Mercenaries, recruited by Philomelus, served the Phocians for high pay from sacred treasures; they became a substantial force, suffered and inflicted retaliatory killings, and lost many with Onomarchus in battle.

CHAP. VII.

Mercenaries formed a substantial part of the Phocian army because Philomelus and Onomarchus offered unusually high pay from the sacred treasures. Boeotian forces killed captured Phocian mercenaries, prompting retaliation by the Phocian soldiers. More than six thousand Phocians and mercenaries were killed in the battle in which Onomarchus died.
