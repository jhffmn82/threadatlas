Also called: ball and socket joints, elbow joint, hinge-joints, hinges, joint, jointed

Abstract written by a model

Joints connect bones, allowing the body to bend and move. They include hinge-joints in elbows, knees, fingers, and toes, and ball-and-socket hip-joints. Soft smooth fluid keeps them moist. Two or more muscles, fastened by tendons, move joints as one shortens and another stretches.

Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Joints connect the bones and allow the body to bend and move. Hinge-joints occur in the elbows, knees, fingers, and toes, while ball-and-socket joints occur in the shoulders and hips; a soft fluid keeps them moist and moving easily.

CHAPTER II. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Joints are moved by two or more muscles that work together, with one muscle shortening while another stretches. Muscles are fastened to the bones around the joints by tendons.
