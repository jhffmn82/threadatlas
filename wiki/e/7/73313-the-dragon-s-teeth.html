the dragon’s teeth in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The dragon’s teeth are deadly seed given by Aeetes for Jason’s task in the field of Ares. Jason sows them along furrows as the bulls plough, and armed Earthborn men rise from the earth. Medea advises him to cast a heavy stone among the men so that they kill one another.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Now the heroes apart in ambush, in a back-water of the river, were met

Aeetes gives Jason the task of casting serpent’s teeth into the furrows after ploughing the field of Ares. The teeth are described as seed that produces armed men.

Then did night draw darkness over the earth; and on the sea sailors

Aeetes is to give Jason the deadly teeth from the dragon’s jaws for sowing. Medea tells Jason to cast a heavy stone among the earthborn men that arise from them so that they will kill one another.

Then Aeetes arrayed his breast in the stiff corslet which Ares gave him

Jason sows the dragon’s teeth along the furrows as the bulls plough the field. The teeth produce the armed Earthborn men who rise from the earth.
