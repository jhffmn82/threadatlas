Isaurians

Also called: city of the Lsaurians

Abstract written by a model

The Isaurians, inhabitants of their city, had abundant weapons and defended liberty against Perdiccas and the Macedonians. Facing inevitable defeat, they destroyed their families, wealth, and houses, then remaining defenders chose death in the flames over surrender.
