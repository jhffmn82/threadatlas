crossing the thistle field in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A difficult journey crosses the thistle field: blankets are alternated for the Lion and Hank, while the Woozy and Wooden Sawhorse carry others, ending safely at the city.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The group crosses the thistle field by laying two blankets down alternately for the Cowardly Lion and Hank. The Woozy and the Wooden Sawhorse make trips over the thistles carrying the other travelers, and both the Lion and Hank ultimately reach the city safely.
