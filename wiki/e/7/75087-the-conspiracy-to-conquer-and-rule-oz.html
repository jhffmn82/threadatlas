the conspiracy to conquer and rule Oz in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

A conspiracy by Kiki Aru and Ruggedo to conquer and rule Oz begins with mutual distrust and deception. When Kiki fears the Wizard and Oz people will defeat it, Ruggedo forces his own approach while Kiki independently protects the plot.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4. Conspirators

Kiki Aru and Ruggedo conspire to conquer Oz, but neither trusts the other. Each believes he is deceiving his fellow conspirator.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

Kiki fears that the arrival of the Wizard and the Oz people will defeat the conspiracy to conquer and rule Oz. Ruggedo wants to carry out the plans his own way and makes Kiki obey him, while Kiki acts independently to protect the conspiracy.
