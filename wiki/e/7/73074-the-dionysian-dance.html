The Bacchae

The Bacchic dance is presented as a sacred mountain practice accompanied by timbrels, pipes, singing, ivy, fawn-skins, and thyrsi. Cadmus and Teiresias prepare to perform it despite their age, while the Chorus describes it as a source of joy, release, and communion with Dionysus.
