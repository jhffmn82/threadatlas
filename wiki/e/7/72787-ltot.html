Ltot is a joint training objective combining Lgen with scaled Lre and Ltok; α1 and α2 balance relevance and token losses.

Ltot is the joint training objective defined as Lgen plus scaled versions of Lre and Ltok. The scaling factors α1 and α2 balance the effects of the relevance and token losses.
