Chapter Seventeen

A barrel in the corner of Ku-Klip's workshop once held the flesh parts removed from the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier. The travelers wonder whether those parts are still inside, and the Scarecrow says they may remain unchanged because no part of a living creature can be destroyed in the Land of Oz.
