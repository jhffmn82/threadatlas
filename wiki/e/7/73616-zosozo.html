zosozo in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Zosozo, Vig’s unique pure-energy compound, grants tremendous strength but makes consumers thin; kept in his private laboratory, it is yearly rationed to Herku’s people and six doses given to the Wizard.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Zosozo is Vig’s invention and is described as pure energy that gives tremendous strength while causing those who consume it to become thin. Vig keeps it in his private laboratory, feeds a teaspoonful yearly to the people of Herku, and gives the Wizard a golden vial containing six doses.
