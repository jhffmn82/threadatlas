the Sea Unicorn in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The Sea Unicorn is Captain Peter Carey’s former Dundee steam sealer, commanded by him in 1883. Its voyage logbooks matter because John Hopley Neligan seeks them to learn what happened when his father disappeared in August 1883. Its image and name-marked harpoon remained in Carey’s cabin.

THE ADVENTURE OF BLACK PETER

The Sea Unicorn was a steam sealer from Dundee commanded by Captain Peter Carey in 1883. A picture of the vessel was kept in Carey's cabin, and the harpoon used in the murder was engraved with its name.
