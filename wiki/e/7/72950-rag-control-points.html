INTRODUCTION

The fifteen control points of RAG pipelines are presented as techniques for optimizing individual stages and the overall RAG pipeline. They are intended to help achieve user-acceptable quality in chatbot systems.

The unit identifies fifteen control points in RAG pipelines based on case studies and labels them for discussion in the RAG and RAGOps flows. Each control point can affect accuracy, hallucinations, relevance, security, or operational debugging.
