Alexander's illness in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander’s fatal illness began after heavy drinking at Medius’s banquet, worsened into severe pain beyond physicians’ relief, led to despair and giving his ring to Perdiccas, and ended in death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XII. /

After drinking heavily at Medius’s banquet, Alexander gave a deep sigh and was carried to his bed. His illness worsened, his pain became severe, and the physicians could not provide relief; he eventually despaired of life and gave his ring to Perdiccas.
