Abstract written by a model

Athenaeus is a commander sent by Antigonus with infantry and cavalry to raid the Nabataeans. He captures their stronghold, kills or imprisons defenders, and takes valuable plunder, but returning Arabians destroy his exhausted, unwatched army.

In Asia, Sclcucus after the routing of Demetrius at Gaza in Syria,

Athenaeus was sent by Antigonus with four thousand four hundred foot and six hundred horse to raid the Nabataeans. He captured their stronghold, killed or imprisoned its defenders, and carried away myrrh, frankincense, and five hundred talents of silver, but his exhausted and unwatched army was then destroyed by the returning Arabians.
