Abstract written by a model

The paper enhances Llama Large by integrating a weighted distribution of Retrieval-Augmented Generation, combining retrieval and generation to address traditional large-language-model limitations. Its weighting of retrieved documents improves precision, recall, F1, BLEU, factual accuracy, and contextual relevance while providing a scalable framework. It concludes by opening directions for dynamic context-aware and adaptive weighting, real-time feedback, advanced retrieval, semantic understanding, multilingual applications, and domain-specific corpora.
