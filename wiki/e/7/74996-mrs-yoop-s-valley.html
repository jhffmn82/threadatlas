Mrs. Yoop's Valley in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mrs. Yoop's Valley is a rolling-hilled valley containing Yoop Castle, where no one ever dies. Mrs. Yoop has lived there since Mr. Yoop was captured, and Polychrome slept there after descending from the Rainbow. The group ultimately escapes by crossing its ridge.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

The valley containing Yoop Castle has rolling hills and is described by Mrs. Yoop as a place where no one ever dies. It is where Mrs. Yoop has lived since Mr. Yoop was captured and where Polychrome fell asleep after coming down from the Rainbow.

Chapter Eight

The group escapes Mrs. Yoop's Valley by crossing the ridge leading out of it. Polychrome believes Mrs. Yoop does not dare leave the valley because she fears capture by her enemies.
