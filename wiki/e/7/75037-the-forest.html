the forest in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The forest is a place ruled by Gugu, containing clearings, a hollow tree, monkeys, and a vast assemblage of animals. Kiki's transforming power scatters its inhabitants through the jungles. Ruggedo searches there, conspirators meet, and the Wizard hides. Trees imprison giant soldiers, and the animals later welcome the Wizard and Dorothy as trusted friends.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

The forest is ruled by Gugu and contains a vast gathering of animals. Its inhabitants scatter through the jungles after Kiki demonstrates his power to transform them.

14. The Wizard Learns the Magic Word

The forest is where Ruggedo searches for Kiki Aru, where the conspirators meet near the monkeys, and where the Wizard hides while observing them. It contains the hollow tree and the giant soldiers created from the monkeys.

16. The Glass Cat Finds the Black Bag

The forest trees imprison the six giant soldiers because their bodies fill the spaces between the trees. The forest animals later welcome the Wizard and Dorothy as trusted friends.
