Abstract written by a model

Text-to-SQL is a task for translating natural-language epidemiological questions into executable SQL for EHR and claims databases. The approach combines text-to-SQL generation with retrieval augmented generation and adds a medical coding step, which improves data retrieval, clinical-context comprehension, and performance over simple prompting. It reviews datasets and methods, tests LLM-generated queries against reference-query results, and finds prompting and demonstrations beneficial.

Abstract

Text-to-SQL generation is combined with retrieval augmented generation in the proposed methodology for querying electronic health records and claims data. Its performance is significantly improved when a medical coding step is integrated, compared with simple prompting.

1

The text-to-SQL process is part of the approach for answering epidemiological questions from queried EHR and claims databases. The unit reports that integrating medical coding into this process enhances data retrieval and clinical context comprehension.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.2

The unit evaluates how well large language models generate executable SQL for epidemiological questions. Performance is assessed by comparing data retrieved by generated queries with data retrieved by reference queries.

5

The unit reviews text-to-SQL datasets and methods for EHR and biomedical research. It states that prompting LLMs often outperforms specialized fine-tuned models, and that both in-domain and out-of-domain demonstrations improve performance.
