meteor in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Meteor defies the Comet Master, persuades Short-Tail Comet No. 73 to travel toward the Sun, flees after No. 73 is devoured, and is ordered arrested and taken to Bungo.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The meteor persuades Short-Tail Comet No. 73 to disregard the Comet Master's orders and travel toward the Sun. After No. 73 is devoured, the meteor flees to avoid being sent to the Reform School and is later ordered to be arrested and taken to the planet Bungo.
