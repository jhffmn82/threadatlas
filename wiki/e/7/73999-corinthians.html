Corinthians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Corinthians first appears

Abstract written by a model

Corinthians are a group connected to Syracuse and alliances against the Lacedaemonians. They begin wars with Athens and Megara, suffer defeats involving Athens, send aid to the Syracusans, join a wider alliance whose assembly meets at Corinth, then face violent sedition as Corinth becomes the principal theater of the Corinthian War.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIX.

The Corinthians and Epidaurians began a war against Athens, but the Athenians defeated them. The Corinthians later fought the Megareans over territorial boundaries and were defeated by the Athenians assisting Megara.

CHAP. IX,

The Corinthians joined the Boeotians, Athenians, and Argives against the Lacedaemonians, and Corinth became the meeting place of the general assembly. Corinth later suffered a violent sedition and became the principal theater of the Corinthian War.
