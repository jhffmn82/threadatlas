Embisarus, a neighboring king as powerful as Porus, delayed aid, feared the battle, and was compelled by Alexander to submit.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

Embisarus was a neighboring king whose power was said to be no less than Porus's. Because he had been slow to assist Porus and was frightened after the battle, Alexander compelled him to submit.
