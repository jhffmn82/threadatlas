Lycus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Lycus, lord of the Mariandyni, welcomes the Argonauts, hears Jason’s account, praises Heracles, and thanks them for punishing the Bebrycians who harmed his land. Grieving Heracles’ abandonment, he offers gifts, joins Idmon and Tiphys’ funeral rites, promises the sons of Tyndareus a temple and fields, and sends Dascylus with the expedition.

Lycus, lord of the Mariandyni, welcomed the Argonauts and listened as Jason recounted their journey. He praised Heracles, explained the harm done to his land by the Bebrycians, thanked the Argonauts for punishing them, offered gifts, and sent his son Dascylus with the expedition.

Lycus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Lycus, king of the Mariandynians and Nycteus’s brother, flees after killing Phlegyas, rules Thebes for twenty years, captures Antiope, and is killed by Zethus and Amphion.

Lycus, the brother of Nycteus, rules Thebes while Laius is a child and reigns for twenty years. He had fled with Nycteus after killing Phlegyas, later marched against Sicyon, killed Epopeus, and brought Antiope away captive. Zethus and Amphion kill him.
