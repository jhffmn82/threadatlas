Abstract written by a model

Lord Saltire is the ten-year-old only son and heir of the Duke of Holdernesse, a boy at Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable’s Priory School whose sympathies strongly favor his separated mother, the Duchess. He arrives on May 1 and seems to become content, but after receiving a letter from his father he disappears on May 13. Fully dressed and apparently acting willingly, he leaves through his window with prepared means of escape. A note using the Duchess’s name lures him to the Ragged Shaw, where Reuben Hayes takes and holds him at the Fighting Cock Inn under Mrs. Hayes’s care. He is found safe, though horrified by Heidegger’s death, and is ordered home by carriage.

Lord Saltire is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

Lord Saltire is the ten-year-old only son and heir of the Duke of Holdernesse. He arrives at the Priory School on May 1st, is said to be unhappy about his parents' separation but soon becomes apparently content at school, and disappears on the night of May 13th after receiving a letter from his father.

When the nobleman and his secretary had left, my friend flung

The lost boy left Priory School through his window while fully dressed, apparently of his own free will and with foreknowledge of his plans. Holmes concludes that he had a companion and some swift means of escape, possibly another bicycle.

At eleven o’clock next morning my friend and I were walking up

Lord Saltire was lured to the Ragged Shaw by a note using the Duchess’s name and was taken by Reuben Hayes to the Fighting Cock Inn. He is found safe but horrified by Heidegger’s death, and the Duke orders a carriage to bring him home.
