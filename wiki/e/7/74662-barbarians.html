Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIV.

The barbarians dispersed into several cities rather than face Alexander's army in a body. At Massaga, they joined the mercenary Indians in resisting the Macedonians and were driven back into the city after a fierce engagement.
