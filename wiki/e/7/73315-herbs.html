herbs

object · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: herb, magic herbs, various plants, venomous herbs

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, magic herbs taught to Medea by Hecate are used in her magical art to quench unwearied fire and halt rushing rivers, stars, and the moon’s paths. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, herbs are central to explaining Medea’s powers and may have been used in a restorative potion for Æson or mixed with venom to cause Pelias’s death.
