CHAP. ni.

Menelaus was Ptolemy's chief commander in Cyprus and governed Salamis. He led twelve thousand foot and eight hundred horse against Demetrius, was routed, and withdrew behind Salamis's walls. During the siege, he burned Demetrius's principal engines, but Salamis was ultimately taken after Ptolemy's defeat.
