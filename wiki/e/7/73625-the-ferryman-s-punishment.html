the ferryman’s punishment in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Tin Woodman punishes the ferryman for cruelty by denying communication with beasts, birds, and fishes; their sounds become growls, chirps, or croaks, and each encounter reminds him of his cruelty and causes unhappiness.

CHAPTER 14

The Tin Woodman punished the ferryman by preventing him from communicating with beasts, birds, and fishes. Their speech sounds like growls, chirps, or croaks to him, and encounters with them remind him of his former cruelty and make him unhappy.
