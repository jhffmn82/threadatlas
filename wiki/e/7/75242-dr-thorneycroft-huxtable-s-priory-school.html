Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable's Priory School in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable's Priory School is a selective preparatory school near Mackleton, on the main east-west road, educating its students and employing staff and servants. Huxtable considers it England’s best preparatory school. Lord Saltire, who attends it, disappears from a second-floor room, and Heidegger also vanishes. The school reports this to authorities, but an ineffective local investigation follows a false clue.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable's Priory School is a selective preparatory school near Mackleton attended by Lord Saltire. Lord Saltire disappears from a second-floor room there, and Heidegger also vanishes from the school; the school reports the matter to the authorities but does not preserve an effective local investigation while a false clue is pursued.

When the nobleman and his secretary had left, my friend flung

Priory School is the dark square marked on Holmes’ map and the place from which the boy and Heidegger departed. The main road runs east and west past it, with no side road for a mile in either direction.
