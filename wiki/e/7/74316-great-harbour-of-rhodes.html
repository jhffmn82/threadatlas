Abstract written by a model

is_occupied_by Demetrians [a fortified rampart was seized at its highest point] Demetrius seized the highest rampart of the great harbour, fortified it with a mud wall, stones, and timber, and landed 400 men there. His engines then entered the harbour, damaged its walls and machines, and attacked the shipping, but Rhodian defenses and a later storm prevented him from taking control.
