CHAP. IV.

After receiving an appeal from the distressed citizens, Dion marched swiftly from the Leontines to Syracuse. He entered the city from several places, attacked the scattered plunderers, killed more than four thousand, drove the survivors into the castle, extinguished the fires, repaired the wall, and restored the city's defenses.
