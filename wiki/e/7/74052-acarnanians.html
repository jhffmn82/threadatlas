Acarnanians

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Acamanians, Acer* nanians, Aearoanians, the Acarnanians

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Acarnanians allied with Cassander, invaded Aetolia, and followed his advice to abandon scattered forts and concentrate in stronger cities, chiefly Stratopolis. In Description of Greece, the Acarnanians gathered forces from their towns and besieged the Messenians at Œniadæ until the Messenians escaped, losing many men in the fighting.

The Acarnanians were at war with the Aetolians and accepted Cassander's advice to abandon scattered forts and move into stronger cities. Most moved to Stratopolis, while others went to Saurion and Agrinium, with Lyciscus left to assist them.
