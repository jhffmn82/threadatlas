Acarnanians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Acarnanians, at war with the Aetolians, allied with Cassander and invaded Aetolia, then followed his advice to abandon scattered forts and concentrate in stronger cities, chiefly Stratopolis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now, having related the things done throughout all Asia, we

The Acarnanians were at war with the Aetolians and accepted Cassander's advice to abandon scattered forts and move into stronger cities. Most moved to Stratopolis, while others went to Saurion and Agrinium, with Lyciscus left to assist them.
