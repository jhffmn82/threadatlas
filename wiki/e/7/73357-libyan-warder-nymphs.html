Libyan warder nymphs

Abstract written by a model

Libyan warder nymphs are heroines, daughters of Libya, who find Athena when she emerges from Zeus’s head; later they pity heroes, appear to Jason, and advise rousing comrades and recompensing the ship.

Libyan warder nymphs is in one work on this site, The Argonautica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Now had they left behind the gulf named after the Ambracians, now with

The Libyan warder nymphs pity the heroes in their despair and appear to Jason as three goat-skin-clad goddesses. They tell him to rouse his comrades and recompense their mother, the ship, for her labor in bearing them.
