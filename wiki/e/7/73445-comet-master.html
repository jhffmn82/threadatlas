Comet Master in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Comet Master is master of the comets, directing their assigned journeys and restrictions. Carrying a very long, sharp-pointed stick, he prods and punishes comets that misbehave. He sends Short-Tail Comet No. 73 on a thirteen-week, three-day journey with prohibitions, then orders No. 1 Express to find a runaway meteor and take it to planet Bungo.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The Comet Master directs the comets' journeys and punishes them for misbehavior with a sharp-pointed stick. He orders Short-Tail Comet No. 73 to travel while avoiding the Sun, the Earth, and the planet Bungo, and later orders No. 1 Express to find the runaway meteor and take it to the planet Bungo.
