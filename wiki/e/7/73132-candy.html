candy in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Candy is sugar, though some contains cheaper white earth. It may give children too much sugar, may be poisonous, and is not a complete food alone. Pure sugar candy dissolves completely in water; white earth remains at the bottom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] CHAPTER XI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Candy may provide more sugar than is good for children, and it is sometimes poisonous. Pure sugar candy dissolves completely in water, while candy containing white earth leaves it at the bottom because candy-makers may use the cheaper substance in place of sugar.
