COST ECONOMICS OF CHATBOT

A hybrid and balanced LLM strategy uses smaller and customized models to control expenses while allowing responsible exploration of larger LLMs through an LLM Gateway. The strategy also considers security, cost, accuracy, latency, and innovation.
