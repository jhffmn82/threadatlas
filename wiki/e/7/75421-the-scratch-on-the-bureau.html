  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes: The scratch on the bureau begins on the brasswork beside the keyhole and extends about four inches across the varnish. Sherlock Holmes determines that it is recent because the cut brass shines and flakes of varnish remain beside it; he connects it with someone trying to withdraw or use the key.
