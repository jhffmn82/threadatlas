Pisates in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Pisates claim the traditional right to manage the Olympic games, ally with Arcadians against Elians, defeat them, and manage the games at the 104th Olympiad.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX,

The Pisates claimed the right to convene and manage the Olympic games based on old traditions. With Arcadian assistance, they defeated the Elians and managed the sports at the 104th Olympiad.
