breathing in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Breathing occurs day and night without stopping, with short pauses between breaths that rest the lungs; nose breathing is better because nose hairs catch dust and impurities.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Breathing continues by night and day, with a short pause between breaths that gives the lungs rest. It is better to breathe through the nose than through the mouth because hairs inside the nose catch dust and other impurities.
