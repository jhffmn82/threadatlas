THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

The adventure of the solitary cyclist concerns Violet Smith's repeated pursuit by a cyclist and the investigation Holmes begins after hearing her account. The case is presented as an unusual inquiry marked by suspicious relationships, isolated roads, and an unexpected future tragedy mentioned by Watson's introduction.
