Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Porus commanded an Indian army allied with Embisarus, fought Alexander courageously, was wounded and captured after falling from his elephant, and was restored to his kingdom after recovering. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Porus was a friend of Alexander, received the kingdom of a neighboring prince after Hephastion subdued it, and confirmed the reported strength of the Gandarites when questioned by Alexander.

Abstract written by a model

Porus, a prince commanding an Indian army and allied with Embisarus, fought Alexander with strength and courage, was wounded, fell from his elephant, and was captured. After recovering, he was restored to his kingdom.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

Porus commanded an army containing infantry, horse, chariots, and elephants against Alexander. He fought with great strength and courage, was wounded by many darts and arrows, fell from his elephant, and was captured while still alive. After recovering, he was restored by Alexander to his kingdom.
