Eridanus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Eridanus, a river, receives the Argo after the Ausonian sea; its steaming, foul waters hold Phaethon’s fiery fall and bear amber tears of Helios’s daughters before joining Rhodanus.

Eridanus receives the Argo after it passes through the Ausonian sea. Its waters are associated with the burning fall of Phaethon, emit heavy steam and a foul stench, and carry the tears of the daughters of Helios; the river later joins with Rhodanus.
