Eridanus

place · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: dark lake, deep lake, river Eridanus, the clear river, the great Eridanus, the river Eridanus

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Eridanus receives the Argo after the Ausonian sea; its steaming, foul waters hold Phaethon’s fiery fall and bear amber tears of Helios’s daughters before joining Rhodanus. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Eridanus receives Phaëton's body after his fall, far from his country, and bathes his foaming face.

Eridanus receives the Argo after it passes through the Ausonian sea. Its waters are associated with the burning fall of Phaethon, emit heavy steam and a foul stench, and carry the tears of the daughters of Helios; the river later joins with Rhodanus.
