Book XVIII in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Book XVIII recounts succession after Alexander’s death: Arrhidaeus’s appointment, provincial division, Greek revolt and Lamian War, Perdiccas’s Egypt invasion, Antigonus-Eumenes struggles, and Cassander’s rise.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVII.

It concerns the succession after Alexander’s death, the appointment of Arrhidaeus, and the division of the provinces. It describes the revolt of the Greeks, the Lamian War, Perdiccas’s invasion of Egypt, the struggles of Antigonus and Eumenes, and Cassander’s rise in Macedonia and Greece.
