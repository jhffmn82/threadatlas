High Coco-Lorum in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

High Coco-Lorum is Thi’s judge and a public servant in name, but secretly acts as King. He makes laws to suit himself and control the people. He receives travelers, explains Thi’s customs and history, rides them in his jeweled auto-dragon chariot, says Royal Ozma is absent, and warns them about the Herkus.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The High Coco-Lorum receives the travelers, explains the customs and history of Thi, and gives them a ride in his jeweled mechanical dragon-chariot. Although he presents himself as a public servant, he secretly admits that he is effectively the King because he makes the laws and uses them to control the people; he also says that Royal Ozma is not in Thi and warns the travelers about the Herkus.
