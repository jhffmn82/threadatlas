Tin Woodman's transformation into tin in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Tin Woodman's transformation into tin is the change of a man of flesh, bone, and blood into an all-tin figure. It begins when an enchanted axe cuts off his limbs and other body parts; a skilled mechanic and Ku-Klip replace them with tin, producing his present beautiful form.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Tin Woodman explains that he was originally a man of flesh, bone, and blood. After the Witch's enchanted axe cut off his limbs, a skilled tinsmith replaced them with tin parts, beginning his transformation into the Tin Woodman.

Chapter Fifteen

The Tin Woodman was transformed from a meat-bodied man into an all-tin figure after the enchanted axe cut away different parts of his body. Ku-Klip, the famous tinsmith, made his present beautiful form.
