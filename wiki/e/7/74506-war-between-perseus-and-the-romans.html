war between Perseus and the Romans

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Battle with Perseus, Perseus was at war with the Romans, many battles fought with Perseus, overthrow of Perseus and the Macedonian Empire by the Romans, the war, war

Abstract written by a model

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian describes the Romans fighting many battles against Perseus, overcoming numerous dangers and difficulties, and conquering Macedonia; Perseus’s negligence, refusal to pay the Gauls, and other miscarriages contributed to the ruin of his kingdom. Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II identifies it as the beginning of the Achæans’ troubles, followed by Roman intervention in Macedonia and the eventual subjection of the Achæans. Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II says it was caused by Perseus violating his peace agreement with the Romans by attacking Abrupolis, king of the Sapæans, and that Perseus and the Macedonians were defeated because of this outrage.
