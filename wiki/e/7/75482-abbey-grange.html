Abstract written by a model

Abbey Grange is the Brackenstalls’ large country house in Marsham, Kent, home of Sir Eustace Brackenstall. It has an old central block, a modern wing, and a dining-room whose French window opens onto the lawn. After dinner, Captain Crocker encounters Mary Fraser there, kills Sir Eustace, and helps stage an apparent burglary with a cut bell rope, Mary tied to a chair, and missing silver. Holmes later examines the dining-room evidence.
