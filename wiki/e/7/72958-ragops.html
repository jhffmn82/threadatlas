RAGOps provides operational monitoring, debugging, and evaluation for deployed RAG pipelines. It requires tracing stored chunks and metadata, query rephrasals, retrieved and ranked chunks, final prompts, citations, and original document links; frameworks such as Ragas support automation for rapid improvement cycles.
