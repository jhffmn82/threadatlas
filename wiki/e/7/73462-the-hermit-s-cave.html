_" CHAPTER XII.

The hermit's cave is where the hermit recovers, plays backgammon with Bruin, and receives Pigeon Pretty, Ger-Falcon, Toto, and the crow. It becomes a place of refuge where Ger-Falcon is fed and offered shelter through the winter.
