Assessment of tutoring practices evaluates tutors’ social-emotional learning competencies from tutoring transcripts using five categories: Giving Effective Praise, Supporting a Growth Mindset, Reacting to Errors, Responding to Negative Self-Talk, and Using Motivational Strategies. Based on research-based social-emotional learning and relationship-building principles, it aims to reduce routine reliance on scarce human experts. The study prompts GPT models to generate scores and interpretations, compares multiple prompting strategies, and concludes that Retrieval-Augmented Generation performs accurately while costing less than the other evaluated prompts.
