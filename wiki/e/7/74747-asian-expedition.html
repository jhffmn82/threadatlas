Asian expedition in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Asian expedition is a conquest campaign. Philip prepared an expedition against Persia and assembled promised Greek forces, but died; after quieting Greece and Thebes, Alexander consulted officers, rejected marrying first, and assembled forces to begin it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVI.

Philip planned an expedition against Persia after securing Macedon and defeating neighboring peoples. Although the Greek cities promised forces for the expedition, Philip died during the preparations, leaving Alexander to carry out the conquest with the forces already assembled.

Alexander having quieted all the commotions in Greece, mardied

After the campaign against Thebes, Alexander consulted his officers about an expedition into Asia, including when and how to begin it. He rejected advice to marry and secure an heir before undertaking the expedition, and then assembled his forces.
