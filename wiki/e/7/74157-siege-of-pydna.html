siege of Pydna

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: besieged, blocked up the city, the siege

Abstract written by a model

The siege of Pydna, commanded by Cassander, lasted from winter into spring; he blocked land and sea relief, causing famine until the besieged abandoned the city or surrendered.
