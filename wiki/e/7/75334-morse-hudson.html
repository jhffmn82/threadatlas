Morse Hudson in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Morse Hudson, a small, stout picture-dealer, sold three of the six Napoleon busts, including two to Dr. Barnicot and one to Devine. After another bust was smashed in his shop, he identified Beppo as a former Italian piece-work employee.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Morse Hudson told Holmes and Dr. Watson that he sold two of Dr. Barnicot’s busts and that a third was smashed on his counter. He identified the photograph of Beppo and said Beppo had worked for him as an Italian piece-work man before leaving the previous week.
