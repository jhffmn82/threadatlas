Patrick Cairns in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Patrick Cairns is a powerful Dundee seaman and harpooner with twenty-six voyages, including the 1883 Sea Unicorn as a spare harpooner. After seeking Arctic work, he kills Peter Carey with a harpoon in claimed self-defense, leaves his tobacco-pouch, takes a tin box, is accused, then is identified, lured to Baker Street, and arrested after a struggle.

Patrick Cairns is a powerful Dundee seaman and harpooner who served twenty-six voyages, including service aboard the Sea Unicorn. Holmes identifies him as the man who killed Peter Carey, lures him to Baker Street, and arrests him after a physical struggle. Cairns states that he harpooned Carey in self-defense when Carey attacked him with a knife.
