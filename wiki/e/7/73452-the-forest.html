The forest is a place of deep woods, ferns, mosses, and a spider’s web, containing the Green Man’s oak-tree and toadstool. Eileen gathers sticks there, encounters the tiny Green Man, escapes the doctor’s house into it, and carefully travels to the oak tree and toadstool.
