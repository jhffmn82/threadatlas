the sentinel in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A sentinel at the royal palace’s outer gate, armed with a pike and broadsword, guards the entrance on a dark night, admits Chop-Chin carrying the Celestial Footstool, then lets him leave after a warning against peeping beneath its covering.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Ten o'clock of a dark night.

The sentinel guards the outer gate of the royal palace and allows Chop-Chin to enter after hearing that he carries the Celestial Footstool. Later, he lets Chop-Chin leave, nearly peeping beneath the covering before Chop-Chin warns him that doing so would incur four hundred lashes.
