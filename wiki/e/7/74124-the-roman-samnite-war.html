The Roman-Samnite War was a war between Romans and Samnites for sovereignty over Italy. It unfolded through raids, sieges, and battles at Ferentum, Saticula, and Lautulae; despite a serious Roman defeat at Lautulae, successes under Quintus Fabius and colonies at Luceria and Pontia strengthened Roman attacks and gains.
