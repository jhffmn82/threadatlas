High Cellarer in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The High Cellarer, an officer of the king, secretly steals King Sligo's treasure while feigning investigation; the Useful Coal thwarts him twice, exposes his blackened hands, and leads to his arrest and beheading.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

The High Cellarer is the thief who steals King Sligo's treasure while pretending to search for the culprit. The Useful Coal burns his hand during his first attempt, frightens him away from the king's bedchamber during his second attempt, and helps expose him when his soot-blackened hands reveal his guilt; he is arrested and beheaded.
