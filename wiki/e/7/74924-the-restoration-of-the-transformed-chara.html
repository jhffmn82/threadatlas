Chapter Thirteen

Ozma restores the Scarecrow from a Brown Bear and the Tin Woodman from a Tin Owl. She restores Polychrome from a Canary after changing her through several other forms, but Woot remains a Green Monkey until Ozma can attempt Polychrome's proposed exchange with Mrs. Yoop.
