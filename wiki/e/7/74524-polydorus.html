Abstract written by a model

Polydorus, a Thessalian prince, was reportedly brother of Jason of Pherae and, while drunk, was poisoned to death by his brother Alexander the Pheraean.

Polydorus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
