Abstract written by a model

The Jaguar is a predatory woodland beast that can climb trees. In the forest, it attacks the group and tries to catch Woot for breakfast, but Polychrome conjures magic scrambled eggs on toast. After eating, thanking her, and leaving peacefully, it later tells Woot he is safe and directs him along its trail through the bushes.

Chapter Nine

The Jaguar appears after Woot escapes and tells him that he is safe because the Jaguar has already had breakfast. He says he ate magic scrambled eggs on toast rather than Woot's friends, and he directs Woot along a path through the bushes.
