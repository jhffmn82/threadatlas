Abstract written by a model

Jason of Pherae, a prince skilled in military affairs, aided the Thebans and urged peace amid uncertain war. He later pressed the Thessalians to seek Greek sovereignty, allied with Amyntas of Macedonia, and was assassinated by seven young men or Polydorus.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

CHAP. VL

Jason of Pherae brought auxiliary forces to the Thebans and advised both sides to make peace because the outcome of war was uncertain. He later persuaded the Thessalians to seek the sovereignty of Greece, formed a league with Amyntas of Macedonia, and was assassinated by seven young men or, according to another account, by his brother Polydorus.
