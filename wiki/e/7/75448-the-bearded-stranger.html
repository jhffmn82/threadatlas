THE ADVENTURE OF THE MISSING THREE-QUARTER

The bearded stranger is a grizzled, pale-faced man of about fifty who brings a note to Godfrey Staunton at Bentley’s private hotel. He appears agitated, speaks briefly with Staunton about “time,” and leaves with him in the direction of the Strand.
