implicit knowledge integration

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: implicit, “Implicit knowledge”

Abstract written by a model

Implicit knowledge integration is a method that prepends retrieved entity embeddings to a knowledge-enhanced translator’s encoder hidden states, letting the decoder attend to and fuse retriever and translator latent representations. Rather than increasing source-text length, it intervenes in machine translation internals. It is complex and may be infeasible, but is flexible: decoder input length is controlled by retrieved entities and a hyperparameter, and embeddings may indicate reliance on parametric memory.

Implicit knowledge integration intervenes in the inner workings of the machine translation model rather than increasing the source-text length. It is more complex and may not always be feasible, but it is more flexible because the decoder input length grows only with the number of retrieved entities. The injection of entity embeddings may also indicate when the model should rely on parametric memory.
