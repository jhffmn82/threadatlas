L. Frank Baum · 1919

The magic of the Magic Isle enchants living entrants, including Trot and Cap'n Bill, causing roots to grow from their flesh into the ground. They cannot leave, shrink hour by hour and day by day, and eventually disappear; the Wizard seeks a way to overcome it.

17. A Remarkable Journey

The magic of the Magic Isle causes any living thing that enters it to take root in the soil and become unable to leave. The rooted beings grow smaller hour by hour and day by day until they disappear, and the Wizard says he must experiment to find a way to overcome the enchantment.
