13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The Wizard, Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, and Gugu search the clearing and surrounding forest for the missing black bag. After the Glass Cat arrives, they decide to search for the Goose first, believing he may help them find the Magician and recover the bag.
