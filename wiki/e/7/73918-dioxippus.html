Dioxippus

Abstract written by a model

Dioxippus is an Athenian Greek champion, stronger than Coragus and a winner of many prizes and victories. He defeats the Macedonian Coragus in single combat by breaking his sarissa and knocking him down. Later falsely accused of stealing a golden cup, he writes to Alexander and commits suicide.

Dioxippus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
