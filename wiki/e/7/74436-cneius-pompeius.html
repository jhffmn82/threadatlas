Cneius Pompeius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cneius Pompeius was a Roman consul, general, military commander, and praetor in Sicily. Hardened by austere discipline, he studied military affairs, trained an army, defeated Junius Brutus, and gained recognition. As consul during the Marsian war, he and Sylla repeatedly routed the Italians; in Sicily he administered justice diligently and was admired for gravity and sobriety.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVIL

Cneius Pompeius was made consul and general during the Marsian war. Together with Sylla, who had been deputed by the other consul, he repeatedly routed the Italians and helped reduce their power.

BOOK XXXVII.

Cn. Pompeius trained himself rigorously for military service, enduring a spare diet, little sleep, and constant study of military affairs. He quickly trained and disciplined an army, and after defeating Junius Brutus he became recognized for his military ability.
