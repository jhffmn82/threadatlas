WMT benchmarks in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024

WMT benchmarks comprise English-to-X test sets from WMT17 through WMT21, used to assess general-purpose translation quality; KG-MT is competitive, while KG-MTNLLB improves NLLB-200 scores.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

5

WMT benchmarks are used to test whether KG-MT harms general-purpose translation quality. KG-MT obtains competitive BLEU and COMET scores against MT baselines, and KG-MTNLLB improves over NLLB-200 by 0.8 BLEU points and 1.7 COMET points.
