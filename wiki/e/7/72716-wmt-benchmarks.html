WMT benchmarks are used to test whether KG-MT harms general-purpose translation quality. KG-MT obtains competitive BLEU and COMET scores against MT baselines, and KG-MTNLLB improves over NLLB-200 by 0.8 BLEU points and 1.7 COMET points.
