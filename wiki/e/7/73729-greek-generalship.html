Greek generalship in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Greek generalship was held by Philip with absolute power after Chaeronea, enabling him to demand city quotas for a Persian expedition. Alexander later gained it in stages, through Thessalian, Amphictyonic, and Corinthian recognition as general of all Greece.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIV.

Philip was chosen general of all Greece with absolute power after the victory at Chaeronea. He ordered the cities to provide specified quotas of men for his planned expedition against Persia.

BOOK XVII.

Alexander obtained Greek generalship in stages: the Thessalians first declared him general of Greece, and the Amphictyonic Council and the representatives meeting at Corinth later recognized him as generalissimo or general of all Greece.
