Strophades

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Floating Islands, those isles

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, the Floating Islands are where Zetes and Calais nearly overtake the Harpies; after Iris’s intervention, the heroes turn back from them. In The Aeneid, Strophades are Ionian Sea islands where Aeneas lands, meals are ruined, and Celaeno foretells famine.
