Abstract written by a model

Cassandria, founded and named by Cassander in Pallene, was populated with people from the Chersonesus, Potidaea, neighbouring cities, and surviving Olynthians. Given a large territory, it rapidly grew to become Macedonia's most powerful city.

Cassander founded Cassandria in Pallene and named it after himself. He populated it with inhabitants from the Chersonesus, Potidaea, and neighboring cities, including surviving Olynthians, and gave it a large territory so that it soon surpassed the other cities of Macedonia.
