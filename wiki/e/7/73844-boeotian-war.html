Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Boeotian War is described as a war between the Boeotians and Lacedaemonians that included the Theban victory at Leuctra and helped end Lacedaemonian sovereignty in Greece. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the war between the Lacedæmonians and the Thebans is described as a conflict that included the Theban victory over the Lacedæmonians at Leuctra.

Boeotian War in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Boeotian War first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Boeotian War was a war between the Boeotians and Lacedaemonians, begun after the Lacedaemonians detained the Cadmea, expelled prominent people, and later fought Thebes over Boeotian cities' self-government. It brought back Theban exiles, ended with the Cadmea's siege and surrender, and included land and naval fighting, Agesilaus's Boeotian campaigns, and Theban use of difficult ground. Thebans defeated Lacedaemonians at Leuctra; later disasters at Mantinea helped end Lacedaemonian sovereignty in Greece.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAR III.

The Boeotian War broke out between the Boeotians and the Lacedaemonians after the latter forcibly detained the Cadmea and drove out many people of rank. The conflict led to the return of the Theban exiles, the siege and surrender of the Cadmea, and wider opposition to Lacedaemonian power.

Whilst these things were acting in Greece, Acoris^king of Egypt^

The conflict between Thebes and the Lacedaemonians included Agesilaus's campaigns in Boeotia and the Thebans' defense from difficult ground. The Lacedaemonians later suffered major disasters at Leuctra and Mantinea and lost their sovereignty in Greece.

CHAP. VL

The Lacedaemonians undertook war against Thebes after the Thebans refused to let the cities of Boeotia govern themselves. The campaign culminated in the battle of Leuctra, where the Thebans defeated the Lacedaemonians.
