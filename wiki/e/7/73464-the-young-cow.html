the young cow

animal · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The red cow, This young cow, a young cow, the poor cow, the young red cow

Abstract written by a model

The young cow, mother of and very fond of Bossy, grieves when her calf is taken away, believes he is on the moon, leaps toward it, overshoots it, and vanishes forever.

CHAPTER X.

The young cow grieves after her calf is taken away and becomes convinced that he has been taken to the moon. She runs and leaps toward the moon, overshoots it, and is never seen or heard of again.
