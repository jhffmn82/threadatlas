The blue mud is thick, slimy, dark blue, and paintlike when dry; from stream banks, monkeys smear it over the Glass Cat until it hardens on her body, eyes, and ears, then she washes it off.

20. The Monkeys Have Trouble

The blue mud lies along the banks of a stream and is thick and slimy. The monkeys smear it over the Glass Cat, including her eyes and ears, and it dries hard on her body like paint.
