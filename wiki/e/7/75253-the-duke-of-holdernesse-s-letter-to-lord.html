the Duke of Holdernesse's letter to Lord Saltire in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The Duke of Holdernesse wrote this letter to Lord Saltire the day before the boy disappeared. Saltire received it on the day of his disappearance and carried it away from his room; its contents, according to the Duke, gave no reason for flight, and Wilder says he posted it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

The Duke of Holdernesse's letter to Lord Saltire was written the day before Lord Saltire disappeared and received by him on the day of his disappearance. The boy took the letter with him, and the Duke says it contained nothing that should have unbalanced or induced him to flee; James Wilder says he placed it in the post-bag with the Duke's other letters.
