THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

The Duke of Holdernesse's letter to Lord Saltire was written the day before Lord Saltire disappeared and received by him on the day of his disappearance. The boy took the letter with him, and the Duke says it contained nothing that should have unbalanced or induced him to flee; James Wilder says he placed it in the post-bag with the Duke's other letters.
