Abstract written by a model

Woodley assaults Violet Smith after she rejects his advances, seizing her and demanding a kiss. Mr. Carruthers intervenes and tears him away, but Woodley knocks Carruthers down and cuts his face. Woodley is later tried for assault as well as abduction.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

Woodley's assault on Violet Smith occurs when he seizes her after she rejects his advances and demands that she kiss him. Mr. Carruthers tears Woodley away, and Woodley responds by knocking Carruthers down and cutting his face.

Holmes rose and tossed the end of his cigarette into the grate.

Williamson and Woodley are tried for assault as well as abduction. Mr. Carruthers’s assault is apparently viewed less gravely by the court, and a few months are thought to have been sufficient punishment.
