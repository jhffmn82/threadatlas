Caphaurus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Caphaurus, a shepherd descended from Phoebus and Acacallis, kills Canthus defending his sheep, then the Argonauts avenge Canthus by killing him.

Caphaurus, a shepherd and descendant of Phoebus through Amphithemis, Nasamon, and a Tritonian nymph, kills Canthus with a stone while defending his sheep. The Argonauts later avenge Canthus by killing Caphaurus.
