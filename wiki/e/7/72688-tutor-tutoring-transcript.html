3. Results

The tutor was incorrectly judged by GPT-3.5 as failing to validate the student’s feelings or build the student’s self-efficacy in response to negative self-talk. The transcript instead showed that the student did not engage in negative self-talk, so the tutor had no such statement to respond to.

Appendix A. Prompting Strategies

The tutor is evaluated on specific tutoring practices and competency criteria. The evaluation identifies whether the tutor used a practice correctly and cites the tutor's dialogue as evidence.
