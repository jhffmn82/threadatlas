The battle at Issus resulted in a great overthrow for Darius. Troops escaped from the battle and were later mustered by Darius at Babylon.

CHAP. IV.

After the battle of Issus, Alexander buried notable soldiers from both armies, rewarded his own troops, and allowed them time to rest. The battle also left Persian supporters and commanders attempting further resistance.

BOOK XVII.

It was a memorable battle in which Alexander defeated Darius. Darius’s mother, wife, two daughters, and son were captured, and Alexander treated them nobly.
