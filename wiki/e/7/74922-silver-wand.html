In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Ozma’s silver Wand is used by Ozma to perform magic, transforming Mrs. Yoop and restoring Woot. In Glinda of Oz, Silver Wand is Ozma’s weapon of offense and defense, driving back the Spider King, changing a green crab white, and creating a magic tent and needed supplies.
