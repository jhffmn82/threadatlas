hooked chariots in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Hooked chariots, a vast multitude armed with sharp hooks on lower axle-trees and three-span darts in yokes, were prepared by Darius to terrify and cut down enemy soldiers. As they approached, Alexander ordered infantry to frighten horses and open ranks.

As soon as Darius had intelligence of his coming, he got all his

Darius prepared two hundred hooked chariots fitted with darts, longer blades, and sharp hooks intended to terrify and cut down enemy soldiers. Alexander ordered his infantry to strike their shields with javelins to frighten the horses and to open their ranks if the chariots continued forward.
