The journey to recover the dishpan is undertaken by Cayke the Cookie Cook, the Frogman, and nine Yips toward the lower world. They descend a steep mountainside, camp in a cave, and, after the Yips retreat at a gulf, the Frogman leaps across carrying Cayke.

CHAPTER 3

Cayke, the Frogman, and nine Yips begin descending the steep mountainside toward the lower world. The Yips clear a path through the brambles while Cayke and the Frogman follow, and they spend one night in a cave. At a wide, smooth-sided gulf, the Yips turn back, while the Frogman carries Cayke across by leaping over it.
