Narrative, section by section written by a model

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Dinner may include roast beef, potatoes, tomatoes, squash, bread, butter, salt, water, peaches, bananas, oranges, and grapes. After digestion, it can help make and mend the different parts of the body.
