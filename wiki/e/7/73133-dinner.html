dinner in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Dinner includes roast beef, vegetables, bread, butter, salt, water, and fruits; after digestion, it can help make and mend different parts of the body.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Dinner may include roast beef, potatoes, tomatoes, squash, bread, butter, salt, water, peaches, bananas, oranges, and grapes. After digestion, it can help make and mend the different parts of the body.
