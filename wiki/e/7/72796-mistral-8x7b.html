Abstract written by a model

Mistral 8x7B is an open-source, scalable large language and foundation model using Mixture of Experts. Chosen as the basis for experimentation, it is integrated with Retrieval Augmented Generation to strengthen complex information retrieval and reasoning. The study centers on this MoE-RAG combination, concluding that it improves performance across varied tasks and response generation.

Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning / Abstrac

Mistral 8x7B is described as a Large Language Model that already uses Mixture of Experts. Retrieval Augmented Generation is added to it to improve performance on complex information retrieval and reasoning tasks.

I. INTRODUCTION

Mistral 8x7B is selected as the foundation for integrating Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation. Its open-source availability and scalable architecture motivate its selection for experimentation and enhancement.

VI. CONCLUSION

The Mistral 8x7B LLM with MoE is combined with RAG as the central technical focus of the study. The resulting integration enhances performance across a variety of tasks and improves response generation.
