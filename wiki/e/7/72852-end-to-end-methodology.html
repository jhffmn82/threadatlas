end-to-end methodology

methodology · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Our methodology, methodology, our approach

Abstract written by a model

End-to-end methodology for answering epidemiological questions from electronic health records and claims data. It combines LLM-prompted text-to-SQL generation with retrieval augmented generation, medical coding, placeholder generation, ontology mapping, and optional answer articulation using an OMOP CDM-compliant database. Evaluated in a realistic industry setting, it significantly improves over simple prompting.

Abstract

The end-to-end methodology combines text-to-SQL generation with retrieval augmented generation to answer epidemiological questions using electronic health records and claims data. It integrates a medical coding step into the text-to-SQL process.

3

The methodology employs LLM prompting to translate natural language questions into SQL queries and uses an OMOP CDM-compliant database for retrieval. It includes placeholder generation, ontology mapping, and optional answer articulation.
