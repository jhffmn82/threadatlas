A gold throne, made from the king’s treasury after Eumenes described his dream, stood in the king’s pavilion bearing Alexander’s insignia; captains sacrificed before it and received commands in his name.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

A throne of gold was made from the king's treasury after Eumenes described his dream. The diadem, sceptre, crown, and arms associated with Alexander were placed upon it, and the captains sacrificed before it and received commands in the king's name.
