Eduardo Lucas

Abstract written by a model

Eduardo Lucas is a thirty-four-year-old unmarried man living at 16 Godolphin Street, well known in society as an amateur tenor, linguist, student of international politics, and intimate associate of leading politicians. Beneath this public identity, he is an international spy and secret agent, one of three capable of handling a stolen political letter, and lives a double life as Henri Fournaye in London and Paris. He has superficial relationships with women and possesses Lady Hilda’s indiscreet letter, using it to compel her to take a specified document from her husband’s despatch-box. After she delivers the document at his house, Lucas hides it beneath a drugget in his room. He is then stabbed and murdered there. His papers reveal gossip, letters, and political connections but no clear motive for the killing, leaving him identified chiefly as the victim of the murder of Eduardo Lucas.
