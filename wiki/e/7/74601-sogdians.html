Sogdians

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Sogdian, Sogdians^, Su^diani, barbarians, prisoners of the Sogdians, the Sogdians

Abstract written by a model

The Sogdians, people of Sogdia, repeatedly opposed Alexander. Many joined a defection after Bessos was seized; noble prisoners were spared and reportedly helped keep others obedient. They revolted again, refused Alexander’s governors, occupied forts and castles, fled to a steep rock, were defeated, and its defenders surrendered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

SUP. III.

Many Sogdians joined the defection against Alexander after being solicited by those who had seized Bessos. Some Sogdian prisoners of noble birth impressed Alexander with their courage and were spared; those who returned home reportedly kept their countrymen obedient.

SUP, V,

The Sogdians revolted again, occupied forts and castles, and refused to obey the provincial governors appointed by Alexander. Many later fled to a steep rock, whose defenders surrendered after Macedonians reached its summit.
