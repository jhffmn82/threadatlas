Antigonus and Eumenes fought after their armies encamped within forty furlongs of each other. Antigonus's horse gained the advantage and his troops captured Eumenes's baggage, while Eumenes's Argyraspides defeated the opposing infantry and killed more than five thousand men without losing one of their own.
