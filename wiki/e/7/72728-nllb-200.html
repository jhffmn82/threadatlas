NLLB-200

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

NLLB-200 is a multilingual machine-translation model and backbone supporting translation involving about 200 languages through one model. It records the highest average M-ETA among listed MT baselines, 17.9%, plus 23.3 BLEU and 60.2 COMET on EN-to-XX WMT-17–21 tests. It is used as a comparison baseline, which KG-MT variants surpass.

NLLB-200 is in one work on this site, Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

NLLB-200 is used as a comparison system in the experiments. KG-MT achieves a 129% relative improvement over it.

5

NLLB-200 is evaluated as a multilingual MT baseline and supports translation involving about 200 languages. It achieves the highest average M-ETA among the MT baselines at 17.9%, while KG-MTNLLB reaches 41.1%; KG-MTNLLB also improves over NLLB-200 on WMT benchmarks.
