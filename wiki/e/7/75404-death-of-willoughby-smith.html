death of Willoughby Smith in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

THE ADVENTURE OF THE GOLDEN PINCE-NEZ

The death of Willoughby Smith results from a deep stab wound that divides his carotid artery. The wound is inflicted from behind with a small sealing-wax knife, and Smith dies after briefly identifying the professor and the female assailant.

The investigation treats Willoughby Smith’s death as a possible murder committed by a woman searching the wooden bureau. Holmes’s reconstruction holds that she used the knife only after Smith caught hold of her.
