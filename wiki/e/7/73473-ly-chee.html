Ly-Chee in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Ly-Chee, father of Chop-Chin, is a slightly deaf sweeper who has faithfully kept the Imperial court-yard clean for thirty years to protect the Emperor's passage. He accidentally drenches Emperor Wah-Song with ice-cold water, is condemned and imprisoned, then receives the Golden Dragon's pardon. He gains ducats, jewels, and lifelong appointment as Chief Sweeper, and his family rejoices.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" And without further preface, she began as follows:-- CHAPTER II.

Ly-Chee is a sweeper of the Imperial court-yard and the father of Chop-Chin. Because he is slightly deaf, he does not hear the Emperor emerge and accidentally throws a bucket of ice-cold water onto him.

Ten o'clock of a dark night.

Ly-Chee is imprisoned and expected to die at sunrise because Emperor Wah-Song was angered when his shoes and petticoat became wet. After the Golden Dragon's command, he receives a free pardon, twenty bags of ducats, jewels, and the position of Chief Sweeper, and his family rejoices at his good fortune.
