Mrs. Hilton Cubitt

Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Hilton Cubitt, also called Elsie Cubitt and formerly Elsie Patrick, is an American young lady married to Hilton Cubitt. Her painful past is undisclosed, though she feels no personal shame about it. Terrified by dancing-men messages and their unknown sender, she recognizes the signals but refuses to explain them, collapses when shown one, and tries to stop her husband from confronting the man she fears will harm him. The messages show Abe Slaney appealing to and threatening her. After Hilton Cubitt is found dead, she is discovered gravely wounded near the study window, with a bullet through the front of her brain; Holmes says Slaney's pursuit and threats drove her attempted suicide. She regains consciousness and recovers entirely, becomes Hilton Cubitt's widow, administers his estate, and devotes her life to caring for the poor.

Mrs. Hilton Cubitt is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
