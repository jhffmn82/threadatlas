Athens’s war decree

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: decree

Abstract written by a model

Athens’s war decree assigns Athenians the defense of Greece’s common liberty and the freeing of Greek cities from garrisons. It then calls for forty three-banked and two hundred four-banked ships, mobilizes Athenians under forty, and sends ambassadors to Greek cities.

Athens’s war decree is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Athens’s war decree assigned the Athenians responsibility for defending the common liberty of Greece and freeing Greek cities from their garrisons. It called for a fleet of forty three-banked and two hundred four-banked ships, the mobilization of Athenians under forty, and the dispatch of ambassadors to the Greek cities.
