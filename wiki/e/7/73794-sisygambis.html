Abstract written by a model

Sisygambis, also named Sysigambis, is an honourable queen and mother of Darius. She accompanies Darius’s army toward Cilicia, is captured after Issus, and twice mistakes Hephaestion for Alexander. Alexander responds with compassion: he calls her mother, restores her robes and honours, and provides attendants, servants, furniture, and provisions.

Sisygambis is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

CHAP. III.

Darius's mother accompanied Darius's army toward Cilicia. She was captured after the battle at Issus and became part of the royal family whose condition moved the Macedonians to compassion.

But to return to the kings themselves : Darius with all his army

Sisygambis, the mother of Darius, initially mistook Hephaestion for Alexander because Hephaestion was taller and more comely. After the mistake was revealed, Alexander lifted her up, called her mother, restored her royal robes and honors, and supplied her with attendants, servants, furniture, and additional provisions.

CHAP. XII. /

Darius’s mother once mistakenly prostrated herself before Hephaestion, believing him to be Alexander. Alexander told her not to be troubled because Hephaestion was another Alexander.
