Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy is a local physician, married to Mrs. O'Shaughnessy, who lives in the doctor's house and owns a narrow gig and old pony. He examines Eileen Macarthy and the supposed diamonds and pearls, dismissing them as glass and trash, judges her seriously ill, and takes her home to attempt an experiment as a cure. He then holds Eileen captive, intending that she speak jewels until his best room is full. Driven by greed, he imagines becoming King Michael and claiming Ireland's crown for wealth, power, and royal glory. When Eily disappears and the jewels become reptiles, his ambition collapses: he flees and is pursued by the Green Men across County Kerry for three times three days, finally falling exhausted into a bog.

Dr. O’Shaughnessy listens to the family’s account and examines the diamonds and pearls. He says they are bits of glass and other trash rather than jewels, calls Eileen’s condition serious, and takes her to his house so he can attempt an experiment to cure her.

Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy carries Eileen to his house and imprisons her so that she will speak diamonds and pearls until his best room is full. He greedily plans to use the jewels to become King of Ireland, but the Green Man indicates that this will not happen.

Now, just about this time Dr.

Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy arrives home after a long drive, imagining himself becoming King Michael and receiving Queen Victory's crown. After discovering that Eily is gone and the jewels have become reptiles, he flees the house and is pursued across County Kerry until he collapses exhausted in a bog.
