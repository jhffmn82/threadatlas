punishes the seizure and robbing of the oracle [by overtaking the sacrilegious robbers] Divine vengeance is said to have overtaken all the sacrilegious robbers, from the chief ringleaders to those who had the least part in the offense. It is also shown in the punishments of certain women who wore gold taken from Delphi and in the prosperity granted to Philip for defending the oracle.
