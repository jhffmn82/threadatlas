FACTS framework

framework · 1 work · 22 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: FACTS, FACTS dimensions, FACTS mnemonic

Abstract written by a model

The FACTS framework is an evaluation framework for building enterprise-grade RAG-based chatbots. It organizes delivery around five essential dimensions: fresh content, flexible architectures, cost efficiency and LLM cost management, testing, and security. Introduced as a way to deliver chatbots that are fresh, adaptable, cost-efficient, well tested, and secure, it also summarizes challenges and lessons from building three chatbots. It begins with the challenge of keeping enterprise content fresh, then addresses architecture, costs, planning for testing, and security, concluding that these factors are essential to robust enterprise-grade chatbots.

ABSTRACT

The FACTS framework is introduced for building enterprise-grade RAG-based chatbots. Its five dimensions are content freshness, architectures, cost economics of LLMs, testing, and security.

INTRODUCTION

FACTS summarizes the paper's goals for delivering fresh content with flexible architectures that are cost-efficient, tested well, and secure. The acronym represents fresh content, flexible architectures, cost-efficiency, testing, and security.

CASE STUDY

The FACTS framework summarizes the challenges experienced and lessons learned while building the three chatbots. It begins by addressing the challenge of delivering fresh enterprise content.

RELATED WORK

The FACTS framework organizes RAG quality considerations around freshness, architecture, costs, testing, and security. It is presented as providing a distinctive perspective on building secure enterprise-grade chatbots.

CONCLUSIONS

The FACTS framework emphasizes content freshness, architecture, LLM cost management, planning for testing, and security as essential factors in creating robust, secure, and enterprise-grade chatbots.
