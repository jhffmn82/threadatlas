Also called: Quarrels about a successor to Alexander, great dissentioas aod diidewcest

Abstract written by a model

The succession crisis followed Alexander's death without issue. The infantry supported Aridaeus, while leading nobles, bodyguards, and the Social Horse prepared to oppose the Macedonian phalanx; the dispute was settled by making Aridaeus king as Philip III and Perdiccas executive authority.
