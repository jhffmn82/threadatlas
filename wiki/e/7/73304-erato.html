Erato in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Erato, a goddess sharing Cypris’s power, charms unwedded maidens and is invoked to tell of Jason’s return with Medea’s love-aided help.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III

Erato is invoked to tell how Jason brought the fleece back to Iolcus with the aid of Medea's love. She is associated with Cypris and with charming unwedded maidens through love.
