Charles Augustus Milverton in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Charles Augustus Milverton is a wealthy, cunning blackmailer in London, living at Appledore Towers in Hampstead and judged one of the city’s most dangerous men. He buys compromising letters from treacherous servants and genteel ruffians, stores dossiers and documents in his study safe, and waits for the most profitable moment to threaten exposure. Carrying a revolver and pocketbook of material, he negotiates ruthlessly, demanding seven thousand pounds from Lady Eva and threatening her marriage by sending her letters to the Earl. After keeping such papers to ruin others, he is confronted in his study by an unnamed avenging woman whose husband died after he sent him her letters; she shoots Milverton repeatedly, killing him there.

Charles Augustus Milverton is described as a wealthy, cunning blackmailer who buys compromising letters and uses them to extort money from people of wealth and position. He demands seven thousand pounds from Lady Eva Blackwell and threatens to expose her letters if the money is not paid.
