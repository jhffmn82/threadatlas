Canadian Pacific Railway securities in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Canadian Pacific Railway securities were sold and later some reappeared on the London market after Neligan's father took them to realize and repay creditors, leading to Captain Peter Carey.

Alighting at the small wayside station, we drove for some miles

Neligan's father left with securities, intending to realize them and repay his creditors. Some later reappeared on the London market, and the original seller was traced to Captain Peter Carey.
