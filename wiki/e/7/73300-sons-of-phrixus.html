sons of Phrixus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The sons of Phrixus are four brothers, Cytissorus, Phrontis, Melas, and Argus, descended from Phrixus and Chalciope and grandsons of Aeetes. Ordered by their father, they sail in a Colchian ship from Aea toward Orchomenus to claim Phrixus’s boundless wealth. A storm breaks their ship apart near the island of Ares; they survive by clinging to a beam and reach the island. There they meet Jason and the heroes, with Argus identifying the brothers and explaining their descent. They accompany Jason to Aeetes’ palace and say they seek the fleece’s return to Hellas. Aeetes instead treats them as evildoers, demands a penalty, and sees their return as fulfillment of a prophecy of treachery by his offspring.

The sons of Phrixus sail from Aea toward Orchomenus to claim their father’s wealth, but a storm destroys their Colchian ship near the island of Ares. They cling to a beam, reach the island alive, and meet Jason and the heroes. Argus identifies himself and his brothers as Cytissorus, Phrontis, and Melas, and explains their descent from Phrixus.

The sons of Phrixus accompany Jason to Aeetes’ palace and explain that they reached the island of Enyalius after their ship was destroyed. Argus speaks for them, saying that they and the Argonauts came to seek the return of the fleece to Hellas.

Aeetes says that the sons of Phrixus must pay him a fitting penalty for returning with evildoers and attempting to drive him from his honour and throne. He believes the prophecy concerning treachery by his offspring is being fulfilled through the children of Chalciope.
