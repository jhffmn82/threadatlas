children in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Children have about two hundred bones, with more gristle than older people, so their bones bend easily. As they grow, they need food each day for larger bones, muscles, and skin, while their stomachs should rest rather than eat constantly. They are told to sit and stand erect, avoid twisting limbs, and protect growing bodies from tobacco. They are generally warm inside and warmer than old people because their hearts beat eighty to ninety times a minute; cold fingers may be warmed by the mouth. They are better and happier without liquor.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Children's bones contain more gristle than the bones of older people, so they bend easily. Children are told to sit and stand erect, avoid twisting their limbs, and protect their growing bodies from tobacco.

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Children need food each day to make larger bones, muscles, and skin as their bodies grow. They are advised to let the stomach rest instead of eating constantly.

CHAPTER XVIII. / WHAT MAKES US WARM? .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

A child is warm inside even when the fingers are cold. Children generally feel warmer than old people because their hearts beat faster, usually eighty to ninety times a minute.
