front matter

Alcoholic drinks are among the substances whose effects upon the human system receive special reference in Child's Health Primer.

CHAPTER VII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Alcoholic drinks can become more desirable to a person as the person drinks more. People who habitually take them may care more for them than for anything else, even while knowing that the drinks are ruining them.
