Violet Smith's abduction in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Violet Smith is seized from her dog-cart by Jack Woodley with Williamson’s assistance after Peter is knocked unconscious, taken to Charlington Wood for a forced marriage attempt, and the two men are later tried for abduction and assault.

Violet Smith is taken from her dog-cart while traveling to the station. Her groom Peter is struck and left unconscious, and Violet is carried through the shrubbery to Charlington Wood, where her abductors attempt to force her into marriage.
