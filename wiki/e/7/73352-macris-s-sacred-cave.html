Macris's sacred cave in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Macris's sacred cave, formerly inhabited by Macris, faces the Ionian gulf on an island in the Ceraunian sea. Phaeacians prepare Jason and Medea's bridal couch there; the golden fleece is spread, and it becomes Medea's sacred cave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Fronting the Ionian gulf there lies an island in the Ceraunian sea,

Macris's sacred cave is where the Phaeacians prepare the bridal couch and where Jason and Medea are brought together in marriage. The cave contains the golden fleece spread upon the couch and is thereafter called the sacred cave of Medea.
