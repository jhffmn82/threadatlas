Teribazus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Teribazus, a Persian commander, commanded the Persian fleet and negotiated with Evagoras. Accused of treason by Orontas, he defended himself, was acquitted, and received the highest honors from Artaxerxes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

Teribazus commanded the Persian fleet and negotiated with Evagoras, demanding that Evagoras serve Artaxerxes as a master. Orontas accused him of treason, but Teribazus defended himself at trial and was acquitted, after which Artaxerxes advanced him to the highest honors.
