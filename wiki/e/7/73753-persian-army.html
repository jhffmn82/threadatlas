Persian army in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Persian army was raised and organized by Darius Codomannus for war with Macedonia, with Memnon of Rhodes among its commanders and large Persian infantry and cavalry forces. It first lost at the Granicus, where its cavalry was routed, infantry fled, and many were killed or captured. Later, assembled at Babylon with four hundred thousand foot and one hundred thousand horse, it fought fiercely at Issus but collapsed when Darius fled.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVII.

Darius worked to raise and organize a large Persian army and fleet. Its commanders included Memnon, who conducted operations in the region of Cyzicus and Pitane.

CHAP. It.

The Persian army at the Granicus included more than ten thousand horse and at least one hundred thousand foot. Its cavalry was routed by the Macedonians, after which the infantry fled; more than ten thousand foot were killed, at least two thousand horse were killed, and more than twenty thousand were taken prisoner.

CHAP. III.

Darius's Persian army numbered four hundred thousand foot and one hundred thousand horse when it assembled at Babylon. It fought fiercely at Issus but collapsed after Darius came under intense pressure and fled.
