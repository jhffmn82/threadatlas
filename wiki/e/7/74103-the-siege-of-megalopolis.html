Abstract written by a model

The siege began when Polyperchon approached Megalopolis with Macedonian and allied forces, long ships, and towers. Megalopolitans resisted by fortifying, rebuilding walls, and disabling elephants, repelling assault; Polyperchon then left part of his army to continue.

The siege began when Polyperchon approached Megalopolis with Macedonian and allied forces and wooden towers. The defenders repelled the assault, rebuilt the damaged walls, and rendered the elephants ineffective, after which Polyperchon left part of his army to continue the siege.
