palace of Aeetes in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Aeetes’ residence in Colchis is a grand palace with imposing gates, columns, courts, chambers, galleries, and Hephaestus-made fountains. Jason’s delegation enters, dines, and hears the contest announced; later Argus visits, and Medea dreams there of her parents and a stranger disputing the oxen’s task.

The palace of Aeetes has imposing gates, columns, inner courts, chambers, galleries, and wondrous fountains made by Hephaestus. Jason’s delegation enters it, shares a meal, and hears Aeetes announce the contest.

Argus goes to Aeetes’ palace before Chalciope comes to Medea. The palace is also the setting in Medea’s dream, where her parents and the stranger contend over the outcome of the oxen’s task.
