the murder of the Honourable Ronald Adair in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

In 1894 London, Ronald Adair was found shot dead in a locked room at 427 Park Lane; the unexplained crime led to a willful-murder verdict against unknown persons.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE EMPTY HOUSE

The murder occurred in 1894 and initially led to a verdict of willful murder against an unknown person or persons. The victim was found locked inside a room at 427 Park Lane, killed by an expanding revolver bullet, with no weapon, apparent motive, or signs of escape.
