Argus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Argus is a hero, shipwright, counselor, and helper of Athena and the Argonauts, described as son of Arestor and also as son of Phrixus. Under Athena’s guidance he helps build the swift Argo, then joins the assembled heroes despite Pelias’s wishes, wearing a bull hide and directing them to gird the ship with rope. After a shipwreck he seeks shelter, recounts the sons of Phrixus, and warns of Aeetes, the Colchians, and the sleepless serpent. His counsel guides Jason’s anchoring near the Phasis, is approved by Mopsus and the Argonauts, and ends with Jason sending him hastily to the city.
