includes Cretan submission [surrender of ships, hostages, and tribute] Roman agreements and commands require submission, hostages, weapons, tribute, military assistance, or relocation. The Romans order the Carthaginians to surrender hostages and arms and leave Carthage, impose extensive conditions on the Numantians and Termissians, and command the Cretans to surrender ships, hostages, named individuals, and silver.
