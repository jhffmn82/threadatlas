Check-COVID dataset in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024

Check-COVID is a dataset for dense retriever training and evidence-retrieval evaluation. It begins with about 350 evidence documents and 1,000 input-evidence pairs, expands manually to 5,000 CORD and LitCovid documents, and evaluates retrieval using NDCG and Recall at ranks 1, 3, and 5.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

It provides approximately 350 evidence documents and 1,000 input-evidence pairs for dense retriever training. Its document collection is manually expanded to 5,000 documents using samples from CORD and LitCovid.

Experiments / Experiment Design .. Response Generation Results

The Check-COVID dataset is used to evaluate evidence retrieval with NDCG and Recall at ranks 1, 3, and 5. RARG achieves the best reported results across these metrics.
