Abstract written by a model

is_a fort [small but impregnable] Nora was a small but impregnable fort in Cappadocia, built on a high rock and supplied with provisions for many years. Eumenes and about six hundred followers took refuge there after his defeat, and Antigonus blockaded it with walls, trenches, and earthworks.
