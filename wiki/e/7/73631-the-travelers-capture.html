CHAPTER 15

Cayke and the Frogman are captured by Corporal Waddle for trespassing on the domain of the King of Bear Center and for accusing the bears of theft. They go peacefully to Bear Center, where hundreds of stuffed bears surround them and the Lavender Bear confronts them.
