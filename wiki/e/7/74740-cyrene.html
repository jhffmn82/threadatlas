Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Cyrene resisted Thimbron amid internal conflict and bread shortage, was placed under Ptolemy after Ophelas defeated him, and was controlled by Ophelas as he received Agathocles' request for aid. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Cyrene had fertile soil and abundant agricultural production, suffered a great civil conflict after Aristocrates and others seized it and killed five hundred leading citizens, and later ended its internal conflict by swearing to forget past injuries. In Description of Greece, Cyrene revolted against Ptolemy I Soter under the leadership of Magas.

Cyrene in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Cyrene first appears

Abstract written by a model

Cyrene is described as having fat, very fertile soil. It produces corn, wine, oil, fruit, and pasture, and is watered by many rivers. Cyrene suffered a great civil conflict after Aristocrates and others seized the city and five hundred leading citizens were suddenly killed. Its exiles joined the Messenians in attacking the city, and the Cyrenians later ended their internal conflict by swearing to forget past injuries.
