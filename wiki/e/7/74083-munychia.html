Munychia

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Muayohis, Munyehia, fort Munychia

Abstract written by a model

Munychia is a naturally strong, walled fort. Nicanor held and reinforced it, repeatedly delaying a promised surrender to the Athenians; Alexander later retained it for war. It then remained with Cassander under a peace allowing him to hold it until the war with the kings ended. Governed by Dionysius and defended by a garrison, it fell to Demetrius after a sea-and-land siege and two-day assault, and was demolished.

Munychia is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

While these things were acting, Nicanor (who held Muayohis)

Munychia was held by Nicanor, who secretly brought in enough soldiers to defend it. Nicanor promised Olympias that he would surrender it to the Athenians but continually delayed, and Alexander, the son of Polyperchon, later retained it for the war.

After this, Cassander having got five*and-thirty long ships and

Munychia was held by Nicanor with a defending force and later remained in Cassander’s possession. Under the peace with Athens, Cassander was allowed to hold Munychia until the war with the kings ended.

CHAP. ni.

Munychia was governed by Dionysius and defended against Demetrius by its garrison. After a two-day assault, Demetrius's men broke into it and took the governor prisoner; Demetrius then demolished the place.
