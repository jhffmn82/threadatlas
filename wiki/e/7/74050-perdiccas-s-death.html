Perdiccas's death

Abstract written by a model

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian describes Perdiccas's death as caused by horsemen among the conspirators, who entered his tent and killed him after the Nile disaster. Description of Greece describes the assassination of Perdiccas by his bodyguard after his failed attempt to take Egypt from Ptolemy.
