Perdiccas's death

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: assassination of Perdiccas, his death, the death of Perdiccas, was assassinated
  • The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian describes Perdiccas's death as caused by horsemen among the conspirators, who entered his tent and killed him after the Nile disaster.
  • Description of Greece describes the assassination of Perdiccas by his bodyguard after his failed attempt to take Egypt from Ptolemy.

Perdiccas's death in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

is_caused_by the horse [the conspirators among the cavalry] Perdiccas's death occurred after his failed Egyptian operations and the Nile disaster. Disaffected commanders and soldiers conspired against him, and horsemen entered his tent and killed him; afterward, Python and Aridaeus were appointed protectors of the kings.
