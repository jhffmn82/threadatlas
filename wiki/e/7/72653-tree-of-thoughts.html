Tree of Thoughts in Improving Assessment of Tutoring Practices using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zifei (FeiFei) Han · 2024

Tree of Thoughts is a prompt strategy structured as branches of a tree, reported by Yao et al. (2023), for GPT-based assessment of tutors’ social-emotional competencies. It organizes tutoring-transcript information and evaluates a tutor in a competency area using Social Emotional Learning Principles and criteria. Across three layers, it scores performance, identifies met and unmet criteria, and produces a numbered rubric-based evaluation with supporting evidence, scores, and interpretations. It is examined alongside Zero-shot and Retrieval-Augmented Generator-based prompts.

Abstract .. 1. Introduction

Tree of Thought prompting is one of the four strategies examined for GPT-based assessment of tutors’ social-emotional competencies. It is compared with Zero-shot and Retrieval-Augmented Generator-based prompts.

2. Method / 2.1. Data .. 2.3. Large Language Model Generated Evaluation and Feedback

Tree of Thoughts organizes information in a structured, branching format. In this study, the Tree of Thoughts prompt supplied tutoring transcripts to GPT models, which evaluated the tutor using five principles and generated scores and interpretations.

Appendix A. Prompting Strategies

It evaluates a tutor's performance in a competency area using Social Emotional Learning Principles and criteria. It includes layers for scoring, identifying met and unmet criteria, and providing a numbered evaluation with evidence.
