generator in RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kiseung Kim and Jay-Yoon Lee · 2024

Generator is a sequence-to-sequence model that takes a question and context and produces an answer with probability. Its answer likelihood supplies a joint-training signal to a separate context-relevance estimator. It uses T5 or Flan-T5 components; experiments report small and large generators separately, including Llama2, Llama3, ChatGPT, and Codex.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

The generator takes a question and context as input and produces an answer with probability PG(yi,j|qi,cj). During joint training, its answer likelihood also provides a learning signal for the relevance estimator.

3 .. 3.3

The model generator is reported separately from the extra module that evaluates contexts. Experiments cover small generators of 2B parameters or fewer and large language model generators of at least 7B parameters, including Llama2, Llama3, ChatGPT, and Codex.
