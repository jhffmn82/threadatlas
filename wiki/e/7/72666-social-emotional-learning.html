social-emotional learning in Improving Assessment of Tutoring Practices using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zifei (FeiFei) Han · 2024

Social-emotional learning is a set of competencies including self-awareness, empathy, relationship-building, and critical decision-making. It is crucial for students’ holistic development, nurturing tutor-student relationships, equity, and inclusion; neglect during tutoring is associated with reduced effectiveness. In this study, it begins in lessons completed by human tutors before sessions, then informs principles and rubrics for assessing tutoring transcripts. Those principles are converted into word embeddings for a RAG-based prompt, making social-emotional learning the basis of tutoring-quality evaluations, with planned extension to other competencies.

Abstract .. 1. Introduction

Social-emotional learning includes self-awareness, empathy, relationship-building, and critical decision-making. Neglecting it during tutoring is associated with reduced tutoring effectiveness, while supporting it promotes equity, inclusion, and students’ holistic development.

2. Method / 2.1. Data .. 2.3. Large Language Model Generated Evaluation and Feedback

Social-emotional learning was included in the lessons that human tutors completed before the tutoring sessions. It also informed the principles and rubric used to assess tutoring practices.

opening .. 2.4. Evaluation Metrics

Social-emotional learning provides the principles used to guide the evaluation of tutors' practices in tutoring transcripts. Its principles are converted into word embeddings for use in the RAG-based prompt.

opening .. 4. Conclusion

Social-emotional learning competencies are the basis for the tutoring-quality evaluations in this preliminary study. The researchers plan to extend the assessment to additional tutoring competencies, including building content skills and promoting inclusion.
