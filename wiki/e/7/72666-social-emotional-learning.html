Abstract written by a model

Social-emotional learning is a set of competencies including self-awareness, empathy, relationship-building, and critical decision-making. It is crucial for students’ holistic development, nurturing tutor-student relationships, equity, and inclusion; neglect during tutoring is associated with reduced effectiveness. In this study, it begins in lessons completed by human tutors before sessions, then informs principles and rubrics for assessing tutoring transcripts. Those principles are converted into word embeddings for a RAG-based prompt, making social-emotional learning the basis of tutoring-quality evaluations, with planned extension to other competencies.
