Ugu’s transformation into a dove in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Ugu’s transformation from human form into a gray dove begins when Dorothy uses the Magic Belt; his resistance makes the bird initially enormous. Though Dorothy later recognizes a missing specification, Ugu chooses to remain a dove, feeling prettier, happier, and less cruel.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 23

Dorothy commands the Magic Belt to transform Ugu into a dove. Ugu resists with magic and succeeds only in making the transformed bird initially enormous; Dorothy later realizes she had failed to specify that it should be a Dove of Peace.

CHAPTER 26

Ugu has been transformed from a man into a gray dove. He does not want Dorothy to change him back, saying that he is prettier, more content, and less cruel as a dove.
