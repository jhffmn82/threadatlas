Cussaei in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cussaei were a warlike nation in Media's mountains, never subdued during the Persian empire. Alexander repeatedly defeated and captured many of them, ending their resistance by compelling loyalty and obedience.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XI.

The Cussaei were a very warlike people inhabiting the mountainous parts of Media who had never been subdued during the Persian empire. Alexander defeated them in repeated engagements, captured many of them, and compelled them to submit and promise future loyalty and obedience.
