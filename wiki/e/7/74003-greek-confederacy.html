The Greek confederacy formed after Athenian ambassadors won support for war, uniting Aetolians, most Thessalians, Thebans, and Peloponnesian communities, while some Greeks backed Macedonians or remained neutral.

The Greek confederacy formed after Athenian ambassadors persuaded many cities and countries to join the war. The Aetolians, most Thessalians, several neighboring peoples, and various Peloponnesian communities entered it, while other Greeks supported the Macedonians or remained neutral.
