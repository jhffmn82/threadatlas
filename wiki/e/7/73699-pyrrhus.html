Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Pyrrhus is a Macedonian king who campaigns in Sicily against Carthage after leaving Tarentum. In Description of Greece, Pyrrhus is the king of Epirus who returns from exile, fights in Corcyra and Macedonia, crosses into Italy at Tarentum’s invitation to fight Rome, and leads a Sicilian campaign.

Pyrrhus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Pyrrhus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pyrrhus is a king of Macedonia married to Lanassa, daughter of Agathocles. After leaving Tarentum, he lands in Sicily, is welcomed at Catana, reconciles Sicilian factions, and gains cities, ships, troops, and promises of submission. With a large army and elephants, he defeats Carthaginian positions, capturing Heraclea, Eryx, Panormus, and Ercte and receiving further submissions. He cannot take Lilybaeum after two months, then plans a fleet and an army for Africa. Later, after Aegae is plundered by Gauls he employs, he leaves them unpunished despite criticism.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXII.

Pyrrhus was invited to Sicily by Thynion and Sosistratus and landed there while the Carthaginians besieged Syracuse. He reconciled Thynion, Sosistratus, and the Syracusans, received ships, weapons, soldiers, and support from many cities, and conquered most Carthaginian possessions in Sicily. He failed to take Lilybaeum after a two-month siege and then planned to build a fleet and transport an army into Africa.

FROM THE SAME..

After plundering Aegae, the royal seat of the Macedonian kings, Pyrrhus left a garrison of Gauls there. Although he was criticized when the Gauls opened the royal tombs and divided the treasures, he did not punish them because he used them in his wars.

Pyrrhus, the son of Achilles in Description of Greece

Pausanias · about AD 155 to 175 · the work’s page · read it where Pyrrhus, the son of Achilles first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pyrrhus of Epirus is a king of Epirus who returned from exile with Egyptian aid from Ptolemy I Soter. After fighting in Corcyra, Macedonia, and against Lysimachus, he crossed from Greece into Italy at Tarentum’s invitation to fight Rome, then led a Sicilian campaign after Syracuse invited him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Pyrrhus of Epirus became king after returning from exile with Egyptian aid supplied by Ptolemy I Soter. He fought in Corcyra, Macedonia, and against Lysimachus, then crossed into Italy at the invitation of Tarentum and later Sicily at the invitation of Syracuse.
