Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

The Triballians, suffering from a great scarcity of corn, invaded the territories of their neighbors in Thrace with thirty thousand armed men. After plundering the Abderites, they were attacked and lost more than two thousand men, then returned in force and nearly destroyed the Abderite army.
