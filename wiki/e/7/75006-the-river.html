Abstract written by a model

The river begins narrow and gradually broadens northward until it is as wide as a small lake around the tiny Isle of the Magic Flower at its center. After crossing the forest, travelers follow it upstream to the Magic Isle, where the river separates them from Trot and Cap'n Bill; only the Glass Cat can cross underwater.

9. The Isle of the Magic Flower

The river is initially not very wide, but it gradually broadens as Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Glass Cat follow its bank northward. It eventually becomes as broad as a small lake and surrounds the tiny island containing the Magic Flower.

17. A Remarkable Journey

The travelers reach the river after crossing the forest and follow it upstream until they come opposite the Magic Isle. It separates the travelers from Trot and Cap'n Bill, making communication difficult, although the Glass Cat can cross it underwater.
