Abstract written by a model

A seaside peninsula with tightly packed, lofty houses, it favored Athenians and defied Philip; after he breached its walls, terrain and defenses let inhabitants resist.

CHAP. XII.

Perinthus favored the Athenians and obstructed Philip's designs. Although Philip breached its walls and entered the city, its elevated houses, narrow passages, and defensive barriers enabled the inhabitants to resist effectively.
