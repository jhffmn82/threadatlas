peace treaty with Antiochus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A treaty requiring Antiochus to cede European and Taurus-side territories, surrender elephants and long ships, pay fifteen thousand Euboean talents, deliver named people, and provide Roman-chosen hostages.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVIL

The treaty required Antiochus to surrender Europe and territories on the Roman side of the Taurus, all elephants and long ships, fifteen thousand Euboean talents for war expenses, Hannibal, Thoas, and others, and twenty hostages chosen by the Romans.
