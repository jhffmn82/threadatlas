Abstract written by a model

At Abbey Grange, Sir Eustace Brackenstall is violently killed in the dining-room by a poker blow to the head. Lady Brackenstall blames three burglars, but Holmes finds the burglary story fabricated: one powerful, agile man killed Sir Eustace, and Lady Brackenstall was tied to her chair afterward to stage the scene.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

The murder of Sir Eustace Brackenstall is reported as a violent killing in the dining-room, where his head is struck with a poker. Lady Brackenstall attributes it to three burglars, while Holmes examines the scene for details that might confirm or undermine that account.
