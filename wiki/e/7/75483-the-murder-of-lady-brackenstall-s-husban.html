At Abbey Grange, Sir Eustace Brackenstall is violently killed in the dining-room by a poker blow to the head. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

The murder of Sir Eustace Brackenstall is reported as a violent killing in the dining-room, where his head is struck with a poker. Lady Brackenstall attributes it to three burglars, while Holmes examines the scene for details that might confirm or undermine that account.

The murder is initially attributed to three burglars, but Holmes concludes that a single powerful and agile man committed it. The physical evidence indicates that Lady Brackenstall was tied to the chair after the killing and that the burglary was staged.
