First Servile War in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The First Servile War was a slave rebellion in Sicily lasting almost four years, sparked by masters’ extreme cruelty and failure to provide food or clothing. Rebel forces led by Eunus and Cleon, and represented also as led successively by Salvius, Athenion, and Tryphon, captured cities, raided widely, and defeated Roman commanders before Roman forces under Rupilius or Caius Aquilius defeated them and cleared Sicily.

BOOK XXXIV.

The servile war began after Sicilian masters treated their slaves with extreme cruelty and failed to provide adequate food or clothing. Eunus and Cleon led large slave forces that defeated Roman commanders and captured cities, but Rupilius eventually defeated the rebels and cleared Sicily of them.

The First Servile War is represented in this unit as the Sicilian servants' war led successively by Salvius, Athenion, and Tryphon. It involved the capture of cities, widespread raids, and repeated battles with Roman forces, and ended after Caius Aquilius defeated the rebels; the account says it lasted almost four years.
