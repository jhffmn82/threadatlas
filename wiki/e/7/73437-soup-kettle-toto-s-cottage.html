A black soup-kettle hangs over Toto's cottage hearth, bubbling as Bruin stirs its savory onion stew; its ordinary business is making soup from good ingredients.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Toto's Merry Winter / CHAPTER I.

The black soup-kettle hangs over the fire and bubbles noisily about the good food cooking inside it. Bruin stirs it with a long wooden spoon, and it yields a savory stew made from many good ingredients, including onions.
