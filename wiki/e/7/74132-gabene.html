Gabene in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Gabene, a country three days’ march from the armies, is identified by Eumenes as Antigonus’s objective; untouched and rich in corn and forage, it can supply a large army and is defended by rivers and deep ravines.

Gabene was the destination Eumenes identified as Antigonus's objective. It was three days' march away, untouched, rich in corn and forage, and capable of supplying a large army. It also contained rivers and deep ravines that provided substantial defensive advantages.
