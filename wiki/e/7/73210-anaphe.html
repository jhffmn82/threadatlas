Anaphe in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Anaphe is a bare island of the Sporades, revealed by Phoebus to Argonauts lost in darkness; they leave Argo’s harbour-signs and build him a glorious abode and altar, and its name commemorates the revelation.

There is the harbour of Argo and there are the signs of her stay, and

Anaphe is the island revealed to the Argonauts by Phoebus when they are lost in darkness. They build a glorious abode and altar there for Phoebus, and the island’s name commemorates his revelation to the bewildered men.
