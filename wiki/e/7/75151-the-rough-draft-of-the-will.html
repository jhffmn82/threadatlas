the rough draft of the will in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A rough draft of a will, drawn up by Jonas Oldacre during a train journey, fills several notebook sheets with scribbled writing; its varying legibility leads Holmes to conclude it was written on a moving express between Norwood and London Bridge.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

It was a clang of the bell, followed instantly by heavy steps

The rough draft consists of several sheets covered with Oldacre's scribbled writing. Holmes concludes from the varying clarity of the handwriting that it was written on a moving train, probably an express traveling between Norwood and London Bridge.
