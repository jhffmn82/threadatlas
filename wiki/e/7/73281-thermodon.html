Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Thermodon is the vast river flowing into the Pontus near the bay where the Argonauts come ashore. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Thermodon is the setting of a powerful nation governed by female queens, with Themiscyra built at its mouth.
