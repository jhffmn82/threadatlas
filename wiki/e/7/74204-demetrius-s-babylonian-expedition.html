Demetrius's Babylonian expedition

Abstract written by a model

Demetrius's Babylonian expedition is Antigonus's campaign to recover Babylon and advance to the sea through Mesopotamia. Ordered to march with Macedonian infantry, mercenaries, and cavalry, Demetrius captures one fort, abandons another siege, and returns to Lesser Asia when his deadline expires.

Antigonus orders Demetrius to march to the walls of Babylon, recover the province, and continue toward the sea with Macedonian infantry, mercenaries, and cavalry. Demetrius takes one Babylonian fort, abandons the siege of another under Archelaus, and returns to Lesser Asia when the time set by Antigonus expires.
