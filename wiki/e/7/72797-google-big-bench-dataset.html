Google BIG-Bench dataset in Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning

Xingyu Xiong and Mingliang Zheng · 2024-02-23

Google BIG-Bench is a comprehensive, diverse dataset of challenging tasks used in the study to train and evaluate an enhanced, integrated Mistral model. Its tasks span basic factual recall to complex problem-solving and test reasoning, including logical implications and deductions; information retrieval, including sourcing and integrating external information; and natural language understanding of complex constructs, idioms, and contextual cues. It supplies both quantitative metrics and qualitative assessment of cognitive processes, creativity, and coherent contextual responses, as well as task-specific measures for retrieval integration and reasoning. The dataset serves as the study’s sole evaluation setting and as a benchmark for comparison with existing models. Across the study, it is introduced as the basis for evaluating retrieval and reasoning, incorporated into methodology and results, and used to support reported improved performance on varied tasks. The conclusion and discussion also identify an important limitation: despite being comprehensive, it does not encompass every scenario or task large language models may encounter.

It is used to evaluate an enhanced model and the benefits and limitations of weighted RAG integration.
