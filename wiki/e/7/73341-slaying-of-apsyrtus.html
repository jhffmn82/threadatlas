slaying of Apsyrtus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The slaying of Apsyrtus is a ruthless blood guilt requiring purification. Medea lures Apsyrtus alone to Artemis’s sacred island, where Jason ambushes, kills, dismembers, and buries him. Zeus’s wrath then blocks the voyagers’ safe return, and the ship’s beam orders purification by Circe.
