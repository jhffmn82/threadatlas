CHAP. IX.

The Massaga mercenaries left their city under an agreement and marched eight hundred furlongs without suspecting danger. Alexander then attacked them unexpectedly; they formed a defensive ring around their families and fought until the men and many women were killed, after which the unarmed survivors and remaining women were given to his horsemen.
