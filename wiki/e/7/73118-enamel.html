enamel in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Enamel is a hard, smooth covering over tooth bone; it often breaks from cracking nuts, after which the tooth decays and aches and cannot receive new enamel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Enamel is a hard, smooth covering over the bone of a tooth. If it is broken, the tooth soon decays and aches, and a dentist cannot cover it with new enamel.
