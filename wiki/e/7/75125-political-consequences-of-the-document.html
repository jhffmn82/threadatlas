political consequences of the document in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The political consequences of the document are central to a secret involving Lady Hilda Trelawney Hope and her husband. He withholds politics even from his wife, and Holmes cannot disclose the facts because of professional secrecy. Lady Hilda takes the document without understanding its stakes, but it is recovered before public exposure, allowing Holmes to assure the Prime Minister that no danger remains.
