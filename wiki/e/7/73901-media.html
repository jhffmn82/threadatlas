Media in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Media is a region whose metropolis, Ecbatana, held a provincial royal palace; its hilly and mountainous parts were inhabited by the Cussaei. Alexander marched through it after leaving Susa. Later Antigonus made Media his seat of war, gathering forces and supplies from the king’s treasury, wintering near Ecbatana, and replacing the plotting governor Python with Orontobates after Python’s death. Nicanor subsequently governed Media for Antigonus and marched from it against Seleucus. After Nicanor’s defeat and flight, Media came under Seleucus’s control.

CHAP. XI.

Alexander marched through Media after leaving Susa and later reached Ecbatana, its metropolis. The Cussaei inhabited the hilly and mountainous parts of Media.

CHAP. n.

Media became the seat of war after Antigonus retreated there and decided to gather the forces of the higher provinces. It could be reached by a long, hot mountain road or by a shorter, cooler, and more difficult route through the country of the Cosseans. After reaching Media, Antigonus obtained horsemen, horses, carriages, ammunition, and five hundred talents from the king's treasury.
