Abstract written by a model

Heidegger is a German master and schoolmaster at Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable's Priory School, where he has worked for two years. Silent, morose, and unpopular, he is nevertheless an excellent cyclist. When Lord Saltire flees, Heidegger leaves partly dressed through a second-floor window and pursues the boy on his bicycle across the moor. He follows Reuben Hayes and Lord Saltire after the kidnapping, but Hayes fatally strikes him on the head. His death reveals the scheme's gravity and leads James Wilder to confess to the Duke.

Heidegger is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

Heidegger is the German master at the Priory School and has worked there for two years. He disappears at the same time as Lord Saltire, leaves partly dressed through a second-floor window, and takes his bicycle; no connection between him and Lord Saltire is known.

When the nobleman and his secretary had left, my friend flung

Heidegger, the German master, saw the boy’s flight, took his bicycle, and pursued him across the moor. He was found dead after suffering a severe blow to the head.

At eleven o’clock next morning my friend and I were walking up

Heidegger pursued Reuben Hayes and Lord Saltire after the kidnapping and died when Hayes struck him with a stick. His death exposed the seriousness of the kidnapping scheme and caused James Wilder to confess to the Duke.
