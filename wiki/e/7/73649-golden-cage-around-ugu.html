golden cage (around Ugu) in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A golden-bar cage surrounds and restrains Ugu during the hall’s overturning, holds Cayke’s diamond-studded dishpan, and ends with Ugu opening its floor trap door and climbing through.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 22

The golden cage surrounds Ugu and keeps him in place when the central hall turns upside down. It also contains Cayke's diamond-studded dishpan. Ugu later opens a trap door in the floor of the cage and climbs through it.
