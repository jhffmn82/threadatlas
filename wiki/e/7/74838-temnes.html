In Asia, the Persian king having invaded ligypt some years before

Temnes, king of Sidon, initially fought alongside Mentor and the Sidonians against Persian forces. He later betrayed Sidon by delivering leading citizens to Artaxerxes and persuading the Egyptian mercenaries to admit the king into the city; Artaxerxes then had Temnes's throat cut.
