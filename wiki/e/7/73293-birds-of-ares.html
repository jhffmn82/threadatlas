attack Argo [by shooting sharp feathers] The birds of Ares attack the Argo by shooting sharp feathers from the air. One feather wounds Oileus, while Clytius kills one bird with an arrow; when the heroes reach the island and clash their shields, the birds rise in countless numbers and flee toward the opposite mountains.
