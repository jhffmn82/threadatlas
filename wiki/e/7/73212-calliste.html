Calliste in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Calliste is an island born from Euphemus’s clod cast into the sea; it nurses his descendants, bears Argo’s harbour and signs, and is later renamed Thera by Theras.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

There is the harbour of Argo and there are the signs of her stay, and

Calliste rises from the clod that Euphemus casts into the sea. It becomes the sacred nurse of Euphemus’s descendants, and later Theras renames it Thera after bringing the Sintian Lemnians there.
