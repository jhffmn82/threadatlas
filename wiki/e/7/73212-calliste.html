Calliste

place · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Calliste is an island born from Euphemus’s clod cast into the sea; it nurses his descendants, bears Argo’s harbour and signs, and is later renamed Thera by Theras.

Calliste is in one work on this site, The Argonautica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
