Abstract written by a model

Yoxley Old Place’s study contains a large writing-table with open drawers, a locked cupboard, and a fixed wooden bureau, with a back door toward the garden. A suspected woman allegedly searched the table and bureau; Willoughby Smith interrupted her and was fatally stabbed near the bureau, while the sealing-wax knife lay away.
