Oldacre's staged disappearance

event · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: disappearing

Abstract written by a model

Oldacre's staged disappearance is a revenge plot against McFarlane's mother, intended to implicate her child. Oldacre hides in his house, claims murder, and plants his will, stick, animal remains, and buttons as evidence; he calls it a joke, while Lestrade treats it as conspiracy.

Oldacre's staged disappearance is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
