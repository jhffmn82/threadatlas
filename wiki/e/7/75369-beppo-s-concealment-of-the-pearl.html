Beppo’s concealment of the pearl

Abstract written by a model

occurs_at Gelder & Co. [inside a wet plaster bust at the factory] Beppo concealed the pearl by making a small hole in one of six soft, wet plaster busts, dropping the jewel inside, and covering the opening. Because the pearl adhered to the wet plaster, shaking the busts would not reveal which one contained it.
