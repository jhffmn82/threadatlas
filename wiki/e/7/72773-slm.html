sLM in RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kiseung Kim and Jay-Yoon Lee · 2024

sLM is a small generator with 2B parameters or fewer and scarce parametric knowledge. It trains a relevance estimator, which improves the sLM when fine-tuned together and can later be separated for large language models.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The small generator (sLM) is used to train the relevance estimator (RE). An RE trained with an sLM can improve the sLM fine-tuned together with the RE.

opening

The relevance estimator is trained on a small language model. This training benefits the small language model fine-tuned with the estimator, while the estimator can also be separated and applied to large language models.
