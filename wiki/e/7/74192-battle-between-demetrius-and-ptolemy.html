Abstract written by a model

Battle between Demetrius and Ptolemy in which Seleucians fought alongside the Ptolemaians. Cavalry first fought on both wings, then lances and swords in a sharp engagement. Ptolemy and Seleucus surrounded a wing, neutralized elephants, killed Indian riders, captured elephants, and forced Demetrius’s army to retreat; Gaza was subsequently taken.

The battle began with cavalry fighting on both wings and developed into a sharp engagement involving lances and swords. Ptolemy and Seleucus gained the advantage when their forces surrounded the wing, neutralized the elephants with a palisade and missile fire, and caused Demetrius’s army to retreat. Demetrius’s side suffered more than five thousand dead and over eight thousand captured, while Gaza was subsequently taken by Ptolemy’s troops.
