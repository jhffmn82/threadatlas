Thists in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Thists are the inhabitants of City of Thi, with diamond-shaped heads and heart-shaped bodies. They wear embroidered clothing and sandals, eat thistles through gold-lined throats and stomachs, avoid work, and seem peaceful and self-governing, but the High Coco-Lorum secretly controls them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The Thists have diamond-shaped heads, heart-shaped bodies, gold- or silver-embroidered clothing, and gold-lined throats and stomachs that allow them to eat thistles. They appear peaceful and contented, use ordinary magic, dislike work, and believe they govern themselves, though the High Coco-Lorum secretly controls them.
