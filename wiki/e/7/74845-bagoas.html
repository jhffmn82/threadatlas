Bagoas

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Ba- goas, Bagoa?, Bugoas, this wretch

Abstract written by a model

Bagoas is a Persian eunuch, officer, and viceroy trusted by Artaxerxes. Captured at Bubastis, he was freed through Mentor and became his sworn ally. He commanded forces, ruled higher Asia, and gained power over the kingdom. He poisoned Ochus, installed Arses, killed Arses’s brothers, Arses, and his children, then advanced Darius, whose forced poisoned cup ended him.

Bagoas is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. IX.

Bagoas commanded Persian forces alongside Mentor and was trusted greatly by Artaxerxes. After being captured at Bubastis, he was released through Mentor's intervention and swore an enduring covenant of friendship with him. He later became viceroy over the higher parts of Asia and acquired power over the affairs of the kingdom.

BOOK XVII.

Bagoas, a Persian eunuch and army colonel, poisoned Ochus, placed Arses on the throne, and murdered Arses's brothers and later Arses and his children. He then advanced Darius to the Persian throne and attempted to poison him, but Darius forced him to drink the poisoned cup.
