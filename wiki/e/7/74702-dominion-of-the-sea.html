dominion of the sea

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: sovereign command both at sea and land

Abstract written by a model

Epaminondas urged the Thebans to gain dominion of the sea, arguing that their sovereignty on land would help them become masters at sea. The Thebans decreed the construction of a hundred galleys and docks and sought assistance from Rhodes, Chios, and Byzantium.
