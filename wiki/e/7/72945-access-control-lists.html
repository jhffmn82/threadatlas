Access Control Lists

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: ACLs, Access Control, access control permissions, document access control permissions

Abstract written by a model

Access Control Lists are document access control permissions governing enterprise documents. They are a key security requirement: enterprise chatbots and a selected information retrieval product must honor them when retrieving, presenting, and generating responses from enterprise information. They complicate search and retrieval, requiring careful management to keep data secure and relevant.

Access Control Lists is in one work on this site, FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

INTRODUCTION

ENSURING FRESHNESS OF ENTERPRISE .. 3.1

Document access control permissions complicate search and retrieval. They require careful management so that retrieved enterprise data remains both secure and relevant.

Access Control Lists protect enterprise documents and must be honored by RAG-based chatbots during response generation. The team selected an information retrieval product because it was known to support these document ACLs effectively.
