Abstract written by a model

The butterman impostor, a retired butterman, claims an empty throne, is accepted and rules as king, carries a sceptre, then mistakes the princess’s butter question for recognition, pays her six thousand rubies for silence, and leaves the throne.

" CHAPTER XI.

The butterman impostor claims an empty throne and is accepted by the city as its new king. When the princess asks the price of butter, he believes that she has recognized him, gives her six thousand rubies to keep his identity secret, and leaves the throne.
