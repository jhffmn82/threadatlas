Lady Eva Blackwell in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Lady Eva Blackwell, a celebrated debutante engaged to the Earl of Dovercourt, becomes Holmes's client when Milverton blackmails her with compromising letters to an impecunious young squire, threatening to ruin her impending marriage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF CHARLES AUGUSTUS MILVERTON

Lady Eva Blackwell is the client whose case Holmes handles. She is engaged to the Earl of Dovercourt, but Milverton possesses imprudent letters she wrote to an impecunious young squire and threatens to use them to break off the marriage.
