Abstract written by a model

The golden peach pit is an unusual peach pit from a lonesome orchard peach. Ugu hid Ozma inside it as a captive. Lost Button-Bright found it, carried it, and ate the peach unknowingly; the Wizard opened the pit, releasing pink haze and revealing Ozma.

CHAPTER 24

Button-Bright found the golden peach pit in a lonesome peach that he ate while lost. The Wizard pries it open, releasing a pink haze and revealing Ozma inside.

CHAPTER 25

The golden peach pit served as Ozma’s confinement, where Ugu hid her so that she would not be found or liberated. Button-Bright carried it in his pocket and later ate the peach without knowing Ozma was inside.
