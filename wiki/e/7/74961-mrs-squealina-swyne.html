Mrs. Squealina Swyne in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mrs. Squealina Swyne, a female pig and Professor Grunter Swyne’s wife, lives with him in a tiny blue Munchkin dwelling, warns travelers of his teeth, shelters them, and worries about their nine piglets.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty

Mrs. Squealina Swyne introduces herself as Professor Grunter Swyne's wife and warns the travelers that her husband has sharp filed teeth. She permits them to stay in the garden overnight and anxiously asks whether their nine piglets are happy and whether they ever grew up.
