Chapter Twenty

Mrs. Squealina Swyne introduces herself as Professor Grunter Swyne's wife and warns the travelers that her husband has sharp filed teeth. She permits them to stay in the garden overnight and anxiously asks whether their nine piglets are happy and whether they ever grew up.
