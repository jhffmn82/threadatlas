Abstract written by a model

creates more than six thousand exiles [expelled from Syracuse] The massacre began when Agathocles ordered his soldiers to attack the leaders of the six hundred and seize their property. The violence spread throughout the city, killing more than four thousand people in one day and driving more than six thousand into exile, while houses, streets, and even temples became scenes of violence.
