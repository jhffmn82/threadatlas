Eileen Macarthy in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Eileen Macarthy is a girl of the Macarthy family who lives in the Macarthy cottage with her parents, Honor and Dennis, and her brother Phelim, whom she cares for. A talkative child who loves talking and knows many fairy stories, she pities a grasshopper and later goes into the forest to gather sticks for her mother. There she frees a tiny Green Man from a spider’s web and receives a wish: whenever she speaks, diamonds and pearls fall from her mouth. After she crosses her home’s threshold, the jewels alarm her family. Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy takes her from her parents and holds her prisoner, forcing her to speak continually in his house. Eileen escapes the O'Shaughnessy house by telling Mrs. O'Shaughnessy a long story until she sleeps. She returns to the Green Man and asks that the charm be removed, then goes home with pearls and diamonds in her apron. Her account is believed; she grows into a good, rich woman and marries the young Count of Kilmoggan.
