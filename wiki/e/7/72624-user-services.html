Abstract written by a model

User services are a downstream application evaluated on a real-world business dataset. They provide reminders of recent or important events and times and travel assistance with destination addresses for navigation, with information usable in calendar or map applications. EMG-RAG improves performance, reaching 96.43% for reminders and 91.46% for travel.
