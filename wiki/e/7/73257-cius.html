is_a city among the Mysians [founded by Polyphemus] Cius is associated with the Mysian land near the Arganthonian mount and the outfall of Cius. Glaucus says that Polyphemus is destined to found a city at the mouth of Cius, and the people of Cius later send youths as pledges in the search for Hylas.
