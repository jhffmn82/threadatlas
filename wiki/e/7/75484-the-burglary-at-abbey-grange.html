Abstract written by a model

The Abbey Grange burglary allegedly involves three men entering through the dining-room French window, binding Lady Brackenstall, killing Sir Eustace Brackenstall, taking silver plate, and drinking wine. They close the window on leaving; frozen ground yields no clear tracks. Inspector Stanley Hopkins attributes it to the Lewisham gang.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

The burglary at Abbey Grange allegedly involves three men entering through the dining-room’s French window, binding Lady Brackenstall, killing Sir Eustace Brackenstall, taking several pieces of silver, and drinking wine. The apparent burglars close the window when they leave, and no clear tracks are found because the ground is frozen.
