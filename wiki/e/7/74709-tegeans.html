Tegeans

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Tegcans, The people of Tegea, the Tegeans, the people at Tegea
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Tegeans lead an Arcadian faction, seek Boeotian aid by ambassador, and fight with Epaminondas and the Boeotian alliance against the Mantineans at Mantinea.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the Tegeans share wars against Ilium and the Persians and battle at Dipæa, then become the first Arcadians to defeat and capture most of the opposing Lacedæmonians.

Tegeans in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Tegeans lead an Arcadian faction, seek Boeotian aid by ambassador, and fight with Epaminondas and the Boeotian alliance against Mantineans at Mantinea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

The Tegeans led one of the Arcadian factions and sent an ambassador to the Boeotians for assistance. They were supported by Epaminondas and the Boeotian alliance in the war against the Mantineans.

Tegeans in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tegeans, Arcadians, shared wars against Ilium and Persians and battle at Dipæa, then became first Arcadians to defeat and capture most opposing Lacedæmonians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLV.

The Tegeans shared in the common Arcadian wars against Ilium and the Persians and in the battle with the Lacedæmonians at Dipæa. They were the first Arcadians to defeat Lacedæmonians who fought against them and took most of them captive.
