Tegeans

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Tegcans, The people of Tegea, the Tegeans, the people at Tegea

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Tegeans lead an Arcadian faction, seek Boeotian aid by ambassador, and fight with Epaminondas and the Boeotian alliance against the Mantineans at Mantinea. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the Tegeans share wars against Ilium and the Persians and battle at Dipæa, then become the first Arcadians to defeat and capture most of the opposing Lacedæmonians.
