the Green Man in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The Green Man is a dwindling supernatural fairy at a toadstool beneath an oak-tree. He gives Eileen Macarthy a wish causing diamonds and pearls to fall from her lips, then removes the charm when she returns and teaches that gentle words are pearls and kind deeds diamonds.
