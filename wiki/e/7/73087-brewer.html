brewer in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

A brewer makes malt by sprouting barley in a warm, moist place and stopping to save sugar, then soaks it in water, adds yeast and sometimes hops, and watches bubbles as sugar changes into gas and alcohol to make beer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] CHAPTER V. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The brewer sprouts barley in a warm, moist place and stops the process at the right time to save the sugar. He soaks the malt in water, adds yeast, sometimes adds hops, and watches for bubbles showing that sugar is going and alcohol is coming.
