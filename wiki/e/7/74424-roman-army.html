Roman army in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Roman army fought repeatedly against the rebel slaves in Sicily. Roman forces sometimes suffered defeats, including the loss of arms at the battle near Mount Cyprianus and the defeat near Morgantium, but later campaigns under Lucullus and Caius Aquilius broke the rebellion.
