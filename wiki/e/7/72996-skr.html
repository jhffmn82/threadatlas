SKR in Unified Active Retrieval for Retrieval Augmented Generation

Qinyuan Cheng · 2024

SKR is a method that uses model self-knowledge data and a BERT-based classifier to determine whether questions are known before retrieval, retrieving when unknown; it performs strongly in self-aware and time-aware scenarios but has less precise decisions than UAR.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.7

SKR uses model self-knowledge data and a BERT-based classifier to decide whether retrieval is needed. It performs strongly in self-aware and time-aware benchmark scenarios but poorly in others, and its downstream retrieval decisions are less precise than UAR's.
