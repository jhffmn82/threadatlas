Nimmie Amee's marriage to Chopfyt in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Nimmie Amee's marriage to Chopfyt began after the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier failed to return. Though she sometimes finds him disagreeable, she keeps him because he performs household and garden tasks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Two

Nimmie Amee married Chopfyt after the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier failed to return to marry her. Although she does not consider Chopfyt an agreeable husband at all times, she chooses to keep him because he is trained to perform many household and garden tasks.
