the wedding feast in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The wedding feast offers nuts, acorns, berries, wintergreen leaves, sassafras-bark, spruce-gum, golden corn, and root-beer in edible birch-bark goblets; guests drink and eat, while star-nosed moles overeat until their stars turn purple.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER VI.

The wedding feast includes many kinds of nuts, acorns, berries, wintergreen leaves, sassafras-bark, spruce-gum, and golden corn. Root-beer is served in birch-bark goblets, which the guests eat after drinking, and the star-nosed moles eat so much that their stars turn purple.
