thistles in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Thistles are prickly plants with extremely sharp points, covering the plain near the city and growing abundantly around the City of Thi. They sting the Cowardly Lion and Hank, though some travelers cross safely. The Thists eat them, protected by gold-lined insides.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The thistles cover the plain between the travelers and the city and have extremely sharp points. They hurt the Cowardly Lion and Hank, but the Woozy, Wooden Sawhorse, and Patchwork Girl can cross them without difficulty.

CHAPTER 9

Thistles grow abundantly around the City of Thi and are the only food the High Coco-Lorum knows there. The Thists gather and eat them because their gold-lined insides protect them from the sharp points, but the travelers cannot eat them.
