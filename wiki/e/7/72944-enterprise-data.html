4 sections; each heading opens the text

INTRODUCTION

Enterprise knowledge is used as source content for chatbot responses and must remain relevant and accurate. NVIDIA's chatbot work involves retrieving and synthesizing content from sources such as SharePoint, Google Drive, Slack, Confluence, ServiceNow, Jira, knowledge articles, HR benefits pages, company news, blogs, and SEC filings.

CASE STUDY

Enterprise content includes authoritative sources such as IT help articles, HR resources, and project documentation, as well as employee-generated content from Slack and MS Teams. NVInfo Bot answers questions about approximately 500 million enterprise documents exceeding 7 TB.

ENSURING FRESHNESS OF ENTERPRISE .. 3.1

Enterprise data may be structured, unstructured, semi-structured, and multi-modal, including presentations, diagrams, videos, and meeting recordings. Keeping this data fresh and securely retrievable is central to maintaining accurate enterprise chatbot responses.

COST ECONOMICS OF CHATBOT

Enterprise data requires protection when commercial LLM vendor APIs are used. The unit describes guardrails and an LLM Gateway as measures for preventing leakage, controlling access, and supporting audits.
