Tablets of Learning in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Tablets of Learning, patented in Land of Oz by Professor Wogglebug, instantly teach mathematics, geography, spelling, handwriting, history, mechanics, home cooking, and agriculture, freeing Royal Athletic College students from book study for athletic exercises and sports.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

21. The College of Athletic Arts

The Tablets of Learning instantly teach subjects such as mathematics, geography, spelling, handwriting, history, mechanics, home cooking, and agriculture. They allow students of the Royal Athletic College to spend their time on athletic exercises and sports instead of studying from books.
