Abstract written by a model

The ferryman is a very large Winkie with a very small head, living alone in a little yellow house by the river. Unable to understand animals, birds, or fishes, he aids Cayke and the Frogman with food, shelter, and a crossing.

CHAPTER 14

The ferryman lives alone in a little yellow house beside the Winkie River. He provides Cayke and the Frogman with fruit and bread, allows Cayke to sleep in his house, and rows both travelers across the river while keeping his back to the Frogman.
