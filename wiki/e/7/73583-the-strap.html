the strap in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A very long strap, stronger than any rope, is made from the Sawhorse's harness straps, fastened to a tree limb over the gulf, and lets travelers swing onto the first whirling mountain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The strap is made by joining the Sawhorse's harness straps together. One end is fastened to a tree limb at the gulf's edge, allowing the travelers to swing over the gulf and release themselves onto the first whirling mountain.
