Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, it caused sedition in Rome through rivalry over its command. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, it prompted the Thebans to join Mithridates against Rome and led them to seek renewed friendship with Rome after Sulla's invasion of Bœotia.

Abstract written by a model

causes sedition in Rome [through rivalry over its command] The war against Mithridates became the object of intense competition among Roman nobles because of the rewards and riches expected from it. Caius Julius Caesar and Gaius Marius disputed its command, while Sylla eventually led Roman forces in Asia against Mithridates.
