war against Mithridates in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

causes sedition in Rome [through rivalry over its command] The war against Mithridates became the object of intense competition among Roman nobles because of the rewards and riches expected from it. Caius Julius Caesar and Gaius Marius disputed its command, while Sylla eventually led Roman forces in Asia against Mithridates.

BOOK XXXVIL

The war against Mithridates became the object of intense competition among Roman nobles because of the rewards and riches expected from it. Caius Julius Caesar and Gaius Marius disputed its command, while Sylla eventually led Roman forces in Asia against Mithridates.

war with Mithridates in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Thebans joined Mithridates in his war against Rome, apparently out of friendship for the Athenian people. Sulla's invasion of Bœotia during this war caused the Thebans to panic and seek renewed friendship with Rome.

CHAPTER VII.

The Thebans joined Mithridates in his war against Rome, apparently out of friendship for the Athenian people. Sulla's invasion of Bœotia during this war caused the Thebans to panic and seek renewed friendship with Rome.
