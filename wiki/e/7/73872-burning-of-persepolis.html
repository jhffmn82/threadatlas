Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VU.

The burning of Persepolis was carried out after Thais proposed it during Alexander's feast. Alexander led the procession, Thais threw the first firebrand, and the palace was soon consumed to ashes; the act was presented as revenge for Xerxes's destruction of the citadel of Athens.
