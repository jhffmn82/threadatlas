Abstract written by a model

Willoughby Smith is a young university graduate who serves as Professor Coram’s secretary and assistant, writing from dictation each morning. He is regarded as decent, quiet, hard-working, and estimable, with no known weakness or enemy, and is a lighter smoker than Coram. At Yoxley Old Place, he catches Anna taking documents from a cupboard. During her escape, she strikes him with an object from the table, fatally stabbing his neck with a sealing-wax knife. Found dying in the study clutching golden pince-nez, he says, “The professor, it was she.”

Willoughby Smith is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
