Abstract written by a model

contains the phalanx [Macedonian infantry] After leaving Europe, this army was divided into two bodies: Antipater led one toward Cilicia against Perdiccas, while Craterus led the other against Eumenes. Craterus's portion was defeated in Cappadocia, and after Craterus's death and the breaking of both wings, the surviving horsemen withdrew to the phalanx.
