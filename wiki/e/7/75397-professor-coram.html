Professor Coram

person · 1 work · 22 quoted facts · 13 connections
Also called: Sergius, an elderly man, his employer, our host, the old man, the old professor, the professor

Abstract written by a model

Professor Coram is an old, infirm Russian professor and heavy smoker who owns and lives at Yoxley Old Place. A learned man, he works in his study on a manuscript analyzing documents from Coptic monasteries, keeps the bureau key on his watch-chain, and employs Willoughby Smith as secretary and assistant within a self-contained household. When Smith is killed, Coram is ill in bed, says he heard the cry but knows nothing, and proposes accident or suicide while minimizing Smith’s final words. His large meals and composed conduct contrast with the crime. He is ultimately revealed as Sergius, Anna’s husband: he betrayed Anna and the revolutionaries for his life and a reward, hid her diary and Alexis’s letters, and concealed Anna behind a bookcase. After Anna killed his first secretary, Coram helped hide and feed her, while acknowledging that she controls his fate.

Professor Coram is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE GOLDEN PINCE-NEZ

Professor Coram is an elderly invalid and a learned man who lives at Yoxley Old Place. He employs Willoughby Smith as a secretary, was in bed when the crime occurred, heard the cry, and claims to know nothing about the killing.

The gale had blown itself out next day, but it was a bitter

Professor Coram is found ill in bed in a room filled with books and tobacco smoke. He offers theories that Willoughby Smith may have died by accident or suicide, denies that the last words implicating the professor were meaningful, and says the bureau contains family papers, letters, and diplomas. Mrs. Marker reports that despite smoking heavily and the crime, Professor Coram ate a large breakfast and ordered cutlets for lunch.

Professor Coram is revealed to be Anna’s Russian husband, known as Sergius, who betrayed his wife and fellow revolutionaries to save himself and obtain a reward. He helped Anna hide after she killed his first secretary, supplied her with food, and admits that she holds his fate in her hands.
