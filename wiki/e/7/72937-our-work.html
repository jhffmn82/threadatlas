RELATED WORK

Our work builds on previously identified RAG failure points and pain points and presents 15 control points with specific solutions for each pipeline stage. It also covers complex query handling, testing and evaluation, document security, and reference architectures for agentic systems and a flexible generative-AI based Chatbot platform.
