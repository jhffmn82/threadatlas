Ancaeus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Ancaeus, a son of Poseidon born to Astypalaea, dwells in Tegea and serves as the Argo’s helmsman. Sharing the middle bench with Heracles, he helps sacrifice a steer. Given courage by Hera, he urges the crew to continue and undertakes to guide the Argo.
