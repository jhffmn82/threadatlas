Godfrey Staunton's disappearance in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Godfrey Staunton disappears in London on the eve of the Cambridge–Oxford rugby match after leaving his hotel, prompting an urgent telegram and Sherlock Holmes’s investigation. Holmes considers kidnapping and links Dr. Leslie Armstrong’s journeys and reaction to the case.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

It was already dark when we reached the old university city.

Godfrey Staunton's disappearance is investigated by Sherlock Holmes, who seeks information from Dr. Leslie Armstrong. Holmes believes Armstrong's unexplained journeys and his reaction to the evidence are connected with the disappearance.
