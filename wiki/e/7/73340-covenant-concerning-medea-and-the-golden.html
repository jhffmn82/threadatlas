covenant concerning Medea and the golden fleece in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

An agreement between the Minyae and Apsyrtus’ side concerns possession of the golden fleece and Medea’s custody. The heroes may keep the fleece because Aeetes promised it for contests, while Medea awaits a just king’s judgment; Jason and Medea use the arrangement as a pretext for guile.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Now some of the Colchians, in a vain search, passed out from Pontus

The Minyae and Apsyrtus’s side agree that the heroes may retain the golden fleece because Aeetes had promised it for the contests. They also agree to place Medea in the custody of Leto’s daughter until a just king decides whether she must return to Aeetes or follow the chieftains to Hellas; Jason and Medea then use this arrangement as a pretext for guile.
