rebellion of the Sogdians and Bactrians

Also called: Sogdian revolt, defection, revoked a third titne, revolted, revolters, their rebellion

Abstract written by a model

A defection against Alexander by Sogdians, some Bactrians, and river-bordering barbarians. It began with surprise killings of Macedonian garrisons and fortified cities, expanded through occupied forts and rejected governors, and was reduced by Alexander's forces until remaining defenders surrendered after the rock's assault.

SUP. III.

The rebellion began when barbarians bordering the river surprised the Macedonian garrisons, killed them, and fortified the cities. Many Sogdians and some Bactrians joined the defection, allegedly because they feared Alexander or opposed his planned assembly of provincial governors at Zariaspa.

SUP, V,

The Sogdians' revolt involved occupying forts and castles and refusing the authority of Alexander's provincial governors. Alexander's forces subdued much of the revolt, while the remaining defenders were driven to surrender after the assault on the rock.
