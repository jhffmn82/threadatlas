Abstract written by a model

A united traveling party, formed when Frogman's party allies with Dorothy's, joins forces to seek Ugu the Shoemaker at his castle and rescue Ozma. Traveling by large boat on the Winkie River, it agrees to share its fortunes, defeats its challenge, gathers Ugu's stolen magical things, and returns triumphantly to the Emerald City with Ozma and companions.

CHAPTER 17

The united traveling party agrees to travel together toward Ugu's castle and to share its fortunes. The members hope that their combined strength will help them oppose Ugu and reach Ozma.

CHAPTER 25

The royal party spends three days packing the magical things stolen by Ugu and then travels by land and river toward the Emerald City. It returns with Ozma, the Bear King, the little Pink Bear, the Frogman, Cayke, and the recovered magical items.
