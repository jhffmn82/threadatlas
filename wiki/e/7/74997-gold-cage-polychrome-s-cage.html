The gold, diamond-studded bird-cage confines Polychrome after Mrs. Yoop transforms her into a canary and hangs it in her bedroom to prevent escape. Later, Mrs. Yoop hangs it by the window, and Woot removes and carries it out to free Polychrome.

Chapter Five

The gold cage is studded with diamonds and was used by Mrs. Yoop to confine Polychrome after transforming her into a canary-bird. It hangs in Mrs. Yoop's bedroom so Polychrome cannot fly away.

Chapter Seven

Mrs. Yoop hangs Polychrome's bird-cage on a peg by the bedroom window. Woot moves a chair beneath it, climbs up, removes the cage, and carries it through the open door to free Polychrome.
