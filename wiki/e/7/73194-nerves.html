nerves in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Nerves are little white cords or threads connecting the brain with the rest of the body, many branching from the spinal cord. They carry messages to muscles and provide feeling, including pain and pleasure. They tire from one activity, rest through changed work, and alcohol and tobacco injure or deaden them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Nerves are little white cords or threads connecting the brain with the rest of the body, with many branching from the spinal cord. They carry messages, allow the body to feel pain and pleasure, and become tired from doing one thing too long. Alcohol and tobacco injure or deaden them.
