Amycus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Amycus is the arrogant king and boxing champion of the Bebrycians, son of Poseidon Genethlius and Bithynian Melie. He rules Bebrycia, owns a farm and oxstalls, and forces arriving strangers to box him, killing many neighbours. His challenge ends when Polydeuces defeats him by striking above his ear, breaking bones in his head and killing him; Lycus calls this the Bebrycians’ punishment.

BOOK II

Amycus is the haughty king of the Bebrycians and imposes a rule that no stranger may depart without boxing him. He fights Polydeuces but is defeated when Polydeuces strikes him above the ear, breaking the bones inside his head.
