Tin Soldier in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Tin Soldier, also called Captain Fyter, is a tin person and soldier made by Ku-Klip through successive replacements after an enchanted sword cut away his former meat parts. Entirely tin, with a bright durable body, tin heart, and long sword, he is an exact duplicate and twin of the Tin Woodman in size, joints, and material, though he carries a sword rather than an axe. He was once a meat soldier who loved Nimmie Amee, promised to marry her, and had a wedding day set. Rain rusted his joints in a forest, leaving him helpless and a prisoner of Mr. Rust for a long time until travelers oil him and restore his movement. He recounts his soldierly courage, seeks his former head at Ku-Klip's house, and learns that his cast-off parts, combined with the Tin Woodman's, became Chopfyt. Reunited with Nimmie Amee, he explains why he failed to marry her, recognizes his head on Chopfyt, and initially defends it with his sword. He and the Tin Woodman agree to let Nimmie Amee choose a husband, and he accepts that he has much to be thankful for. He later serves Ozma as a soldier and is sent to the Gillikin Country to keep order.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fifteen

The Tin Soldier is an exact duplicate of the Tin Woodman in size, joints, and shining tin construction, but he carries a long sword instead of an axe. He stands rusted and immovable until the travelers oil his joints, after which he speaks, walks, and recites a poem about a brave soldier who fights enemies but seldom fights friends.

Chapter Sixteen

Captain Fyter reveals that he was once a soldier who loved Nimmie Amee and was transformed into a tin man after a Wicked Witch enchanted his sword. He agrees with the Tin Woodman to let Nimmie Amee choose between them.

Chapter Seventeen

The Tin Soldier says that he does not know where to look for Nimmie Amee because he is almost a stranger to that part of the country. He recalls bringing the parts cut from him by the enchanted sword to Ku-Klip, who put them into a barrel and replaced them with tin parts.

Chapter Eighteen

The Tin Soldier looks through Ku-Klip's cupboards for his own former head but does not find it. He learns that his cast-off parts were used with the Tin Woodman's parts to make Chopfyt, and he travels with the group toward Mount Munch.

Chapter Twenty

Captain Fyter stays outside during the overnight visit and says that, although he may be particular about his associates, he draws the line at pigs.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Tin Soldier tells Nimmie Amee that he is her former sweetheart, Captain Fyter, and explains that he failed to marry her because he rusted. He recognizes his head on Chopfyt and draws his sword when the Tin Woodman suggests taking Chopfyt apart. He ultimately agrees that he has much to be thankful for despite finding his head on Chopfyt's body.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Captain Fyter agrees to serve Ozma as a soldier. After serving her for some time, he is sent into the Gillikin Country to keep order among some of its wild inhabitants.

Captain Fyter in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Captain Fyter is a Tin Soldier and soldier who possesses the Tin Soldier's sword and seeks to marry Nimmie Amee. He becomes invisible, collides with Nick Chopper and bends his leg, then helps the Scarecrow cut hay. He confronts the solid-air wall around Nimmie Amee's house, becomes small to enter, and is later welcomed at Ozma's palace, where Ozma asks him to serve as her soldier.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

Captain Fyter becomes invisible and collides with Nick Chopper, bending one leg so that it is shorter and causing him to limp. After crossing the ditch, Polychrome uses fairy magic to straighten and strengthen his leg, and he cuts hay with his sword for the Scarecrow.

Chapter Twenty-One

Captain Fyter also encounters the solid-air wall and learns that it surrounds Nimmie Amee's house. He agrees to become small and pass through the rabbit's burrow, then is restored to his natural size so he can visit Nimmie Amee.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Tin Soldier is warmly welcomed at Ozma's palace. Ozma decides that he should not go with the Tin Woodman to the Emperor's castle, and she asks him to serve her as a soldier.
