THE ADVENTURE OF THE NORWOOD BUILDER

The investigation into Jonas Oldacre's disappearance begins after his absence from the fire leads to an examination of his house. Inspector Lestrade of Scotland Yard is placed in charge and follows clues including the bedroom evidence, the open French windows, drag marks, and possible charred remains.
