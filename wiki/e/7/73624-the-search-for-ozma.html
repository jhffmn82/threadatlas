Abstract written by a model

The search for Ozma is a mission undertaken after Ozma cannot be found in her rooms and is missing from the palace and grounds. Dorothy, with Jellia Jamb and Scraps, searches and questions the Scarecrow, Tik-Tok, the Shaggy Man, Button-Bright, Cap’n Bill, and the Wizard of Oz, but no one has seen Ozma since the previous evening. Dorothy plans an immediate search, and Glinda the Good directs several parties to ask everywhere in the Munchkin, Quadling, Gillikin, and Winkie Countries. Dorothy travels toward the Winkie Country with the Wizard, Betsy, Trot, Button-Bright, Scraps, and companions. They pursue dangerous unexplored regions despite delays, pass from the Great Orchard to Herku, and learn from Vig that Ugu the Shoemaker may have stolen Ozma. The party goes to Ugu’s castle, first rescuing Button-Bright from a hole but finding no sign of Ozma. Believing Ugu has imprisoned her, the Wizard and his followers enter the castle to rescue her; Ugu refuses to reveal her location, and Ozma is not rescued by the end of the records.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Dorothy searches Ozma’s rooms, the palace, and the palace grounds with Jellia Jamb and Scraps. She also questions the Scarecrow, Tik-Tok, the Shaggy Man, Button-Bright, Cap’n Bill, and the Wizard of Oz, but none of them has seen Ozma since the previous evening.

CHAPTER 5

Dorothy proposes an immediate search, and Glinda divides the searchers into parties assigned to the Munchkin, Quadling, Gillikin, and Winkie Countries. Dorothy’s party includes Betsy, Trot, the Wizard of Oz, and herself, and each group is instructed to ask everywhere for Ozma.

CHAPTER 6

The search for Ozma sends separate groups toward the Quadling, Gillikin, and Munchkin Countries, while Dorothy and the Wizard lead a party toward the Winkie Country. Their party finds no immediate clues and chooses to investigate dangerous unexplored regions rather than turn aside.

CHAPTER 11

The search for Ozma continues as the travelers move through the orchard and prepare to cross the plain beyond it. Dorothy emphasizes that Button-Bright's wandering delays this important search.

CHAPTER 12

The search for Ozma leads Trot, Dorothy, the Wizard, Button-Bright, Scraps, and their companions from the Great Orchard to Herku. After Vig identifies Ugu the Shoemaker as a possible suspect, the party leaves Herku to investigate his castle.

CHAPTER 18

The travelers identify rescuing Ozma as the reason they came and agree that they must free her before dealing fully with Ugu. They do not yet know how the rescue will be accomplished.

CHAPTER 20

The search for Ozma leads Dorothy and her companions away from Ugu's castle to the hole identified by the little Pink Bear. They rescue Button-Bright there but find no visible sign of Ozma, so they continue toward the castle to question Ugu.

CHAPTER 22

The Wizard and his followers enter the castle intending to rescue Ozma, whom they believe Ugu has imprisoned. Ugu refuses to reveal her location, and she is not rescued in this unit.
