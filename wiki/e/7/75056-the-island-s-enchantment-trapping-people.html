Abstract written by a model

The Magic Isle’s enchantment takes root in the feet of people and beasts made of meat, trapping them in the ground and making them shrink continuously. It roots Trot and Cap’n Bill when they walk on the island. Wood and glass, including wooden legs or soles and glass feet, are unaffected; leather shoes and wool stockings fail. The Wizard cannot break the magic or identify its creator.

18. The Magic of the Wizard

The enchantment of the Magic Isle roots Trot and Cap'n Bill to the ground and makes them shrink continuously. The Wizard does not know who created it or what powers that magician possessed, and he is unable to break it with the magic he tries.

19. Dorothy and the Bumble Bees

The island's magic takes root in the feet of people and beasts because they are made of meat and holds them to the ground. Wooden legs, wooden soles, and glass feet are not affected, while leather shoes and wool stockings do not prevent the enchantment.
