Marsian War

event · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Marsian war, the Marsian war, the war, this war

Abstract written by a model

The Marsian War was a war waged by Italians against Rome after dissatisfaction with Roman promises of citizenship and enfranchisement. Samnites, Asculans, Lucanians, Picenians, Nola, and others fought; after abandoning their city for Aesernia, they continued until Romans won after heavy bloodshed.

Marsian War in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Marsian War first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVIL

The Marsian war began after the Italians became dissatisfied with Roman promises of citizenship and enfranchisement. The Samnites, Asculans, Lucanians, Picenians, the people of Nola, and other communities fought against the Romans, but the Romans eventually prevailed. The Italians later abandoned their city and continued the struggle from Aesernia before the war ended.
