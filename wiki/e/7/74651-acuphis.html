Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIII.

Acuphis, the chief man of Nysa, led the delegation that asked Alexander to preserve the city's freedom. He persuaded Alexander not to take a hundred of Nysa's leading men and was appointed governor of Nysaea; his son and his nephew were sent to Alexander.
