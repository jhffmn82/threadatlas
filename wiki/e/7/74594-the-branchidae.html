the Branchidae in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Branchidae were a group removed from Miletus by Persian command to a distant little town after violating the Didymæus temple. Descended from Miletus’s betrayers, they spoke a mixed language and welcomed Alexander, who accepted their surrender, then killed them and destroyed their city for their ancestors’ betrayal.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. I.

The Branchidae inhabited a little town after having been removed from Miletus by the command of Xerxes. They spoke a broken language and welcomed Alexander, giving themselves and their city into his hands. Alexander later ordered them killed, and their city destroyed, because of their ancestors’ betrayal.
