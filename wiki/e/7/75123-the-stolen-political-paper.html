Abstract written by a model

The stolen political paper is an immensely important, politically sensitive document kept in Trelawney Hope’s despatch-box. Its disappearance during the Whitehall Terrace incident threatens terrible public consequences, though Lady Hilda does not understand them and Holmes will not explain. Lady Hilda takes it and gives it to Eduardo Lucas; Holmes recovers it, restores it secretly, and it is found intact.
