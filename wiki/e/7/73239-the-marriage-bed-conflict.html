the marriage-bed conflict

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: the marriage-bed, their lawful wives
  • In The Argonautica: causes the Lemnian massacre [through the men’s rejection of their wives and the women’s retaliation] The marriage-bed conflict began when the Lemnian men rejected their lawful wives and desired captive maids from Thrace. The conflict brought dishonour to lawful children, neglect to unmarried maidens and widowed mothers, and ultimately the Lemnian massacre.
