war between the beasts and the people of Oz in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The war between the beasts and the people of Oz is an alleged conflict said by Ruggedo to be planned by Oz. He uses the claim to urge the Council of Beasts to attack, but Gugu disputes any cause for war, and no decision is reached before Kiki interrupts the animals' debate.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Ruggedo claims that the people of Oz are about to make war against the beasts, although Gugu says there has been no reason for war between them. Ruggedo uses this alleged war to persuade the Council of Beasts to attack Oz.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

The forest animals debate whether to fight the Oz people, and the Wizard's arrival is expected to help prevent such a war. Kiki's transformations interrupt the gathering before the animals can decide what to do.
