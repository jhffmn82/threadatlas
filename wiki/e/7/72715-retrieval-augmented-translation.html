Retrieval-augmented translation is represented by KG-MT, which retrieves relevant entities and uses multilingual knowledge-graph information during translation. This approach produces large M-ETA gains on XC-Translate without degrading general-purpose benchmark performance.

7

Retrieval-augmented translation is used by KG-MT to address texts containing entity names. The authors suggest that it may also help translate cultural references beyond entity names, including idioms and metaphors.
