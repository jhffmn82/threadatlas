Caius Julius Caesar in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Caius Julius Caesar, a Roman ruler surnamed Divus, is praised for humanity, clemency, moderation, eloquence, military skill, birth, and contempt for riches. Moved by Corinth's ruins, he sought to rebuild it. He opposed Gaius Marius, later fought Pompey amid internal slaughter, and ultimately gained unbounded power after consular authority was restricted.
