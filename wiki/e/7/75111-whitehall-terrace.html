Whitehall Terrace

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Whitehall Terrace is the location of Trelawney Hope's house, where a letter was kept overnight in a locked despatch-box in his bedroom. It is central to a political incident Holmes and Watson know about, linked by Holmes to Lucas's murder. After leaving the murder scene, Holmes and Watson go there to bring the matter to a head.

Whitehall Terrace is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
