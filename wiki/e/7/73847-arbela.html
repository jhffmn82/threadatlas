Abstract written by a model

Arbela was a village where Darius encamped and trained his army. A famous battle was fought nearby, in which Alexander routed the Persians. After burying the dead, Alexander entered Arbela and found royal furniture and Persian treasure worth three thousand talents of silver.

Arbela is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Arbela was a village where Darius encamped and trained his army. The battle later fought near this place was called the battle of Arbela.

CHAP. VU.

Arbela was the place where Darius was routed. Alexander entered it after burying those killed in the battle and found rich royal furniture and treasures amounting to three thousand talents of silver.

BOOK XVII.

It was the site of the famous battle where the Persians were routed by Alexander.
