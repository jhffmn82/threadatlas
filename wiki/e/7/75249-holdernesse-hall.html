Holdernesse Hall in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Holdernesse Hall, in Hallamshire beside Lower Gill Moor, is a residence of the Duke of Holdernesse near the Priory School, the Fighting Cock, and routes across the moor. It is first considered as a possible destination for Lord Saltire after his disappearance. Holmes and Watson later visit at the Duke’s invitation, where they discuss Lord Saltire’s kidnapping and Heidegger’s death. The Hall’s museum, containing medieval horseshoes made to counterfeit tracks, becomes especially significant to the investigation.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

Holdernesse Hall is one of the Duke of Holdernesse's residences and lies only a few miles from the Priory School. The school initially checks whether Lord Saltire has returned there, and the Duke later invites Holmes to stay at the Hall.

Holdernesse Hall lies beside Lower Gill Moor and is ten miles from the school by road but only six miles across the moor. The patched-Dunlop bicycle track may have led toward the Hall or toward the village on the Chesterfield high road.

Holdernesse Hall is where Holmes and Watson meet the Duke and discuss Lord Saltire’s kidnapping and Heidegger’s death. The Duke also shows them a museum containing medieval horseshoes designed to counterfeit tracks.
