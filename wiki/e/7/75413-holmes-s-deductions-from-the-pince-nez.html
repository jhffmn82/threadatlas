THE ADVENTURE OF THE GOLDEN PINCE-NEZ

Holmes deduces that the pince-nez belongs to a well-dressed woman with a broad-based nose, closely set eyes, a puckered forehead, a peering expression, and probably rounded shoulders. He also infers that her eyesight is extremely poor and that she has visited an optician at least twice, based on the worn and replaced cork nose pads.
