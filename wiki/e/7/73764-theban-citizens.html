Theban citizens in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Theban citizens unanimously approved resistance and defended Thebes against Macedonian assault despite warnings; after the city fell, many were killed and others enslaved.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Alexander having quieted all the commotions in Greece, mardied

The Theban citizens approved the decision to defend their country's liberty and continued the war despite warnings from alleged prophecies and prodigies. After the city fell, many were killed, while others were carried away into slavery.
