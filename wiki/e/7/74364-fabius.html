Fabius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Fabius is a general and historian of Roman affairs, credited with preserving the Roman commonwealth through valour and policy. In Etruria he raided higher Etruria, defeated Etruscans near Perusia, made peace with communities, captured Castula, and lifted Sutrium's siege. Appointed against Viriathus, he initially saw Viriathus decline, but Viriathus rallied and compelled him to accept dishonourable terms.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Having now gone through the affairs of Africa and Sicily, we

Fabius remained in Etruria while the Roman forces were divided, secretly passed through the enemy's bordering country, and raided higher Etruria. He defeated the Etruscans near Perusia, made peace with several communities, took Castula, and forced the lifting of the siege of Sutrium.

5. The Lusitanians at first not having an expert general, were

Fabius was appointed general against Viriathus, after which Viriathus began to decline. Viriathus later rallied his men and managed the conflict so successfully that he forced Fabius to accept terms dishonourable to the Romans.
