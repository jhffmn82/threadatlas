Andromachus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Andromachus of Taurominium gathers Naxos exiles on Mount Taurus, founding Taurominium, where he and his family remain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

Andromachus of Taurominium, father of the historian Timaeus, gathered the exiles of Naxos and settled them on Mount Taurus. He and his family remained there, and the settlement was called Taurominium.
