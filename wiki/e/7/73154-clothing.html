clothing in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Clothing is worn next to the skin and keeps body heat from escaping while blocking cold air. Tight clothing can restrict lungs; after wear it contains worn-out particles and should be aired. For cold weather, it includes woolen under-garments, thick boots, and stockings.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII. / WHAT MAKES US WARM? .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Clothing keeps existing body heat from escaping and helps prevent cold air from reaching the skin. Woolen under-garments, thick boots, and stockings are recommended for cold weather.
