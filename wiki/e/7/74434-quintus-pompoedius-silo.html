Quintus Pompoedius Silo

Abstract written by a model

Quintus Pompoedius Silo was a Marsian of high standing, a Marsian general, and an Italian consul. He led the Marsi and commanded northern and western Italy, later leading remaining Italians from Aesernia. He marched toward Rome with armed supporters to seek freedom, but Caius Domitius persuaded him to return and petition the Senate. In battle against Romans, he killed opponents but lost more than six thousand men.

BOOK XXXVIL

Quintus Pompoedius Silo, a Marsian of high standing, was chosen as one of the Italian consuls. He commanded the northern and western division of Italy and later led the remaining Italians from Aesernia. In a battle against the Romans, he killed some opponents but lost more than six thousand of his own men.

BOOK XXXVII.

Pompoedius, a general of the Marsi, marched toward Rome with armed men, intending to surround the Senate, demand the city’s freedom, and use fire and sword if necessary. Caius Domitius persuaded him to return home and seek the favor of the Senate through petitions rather than hostility.
