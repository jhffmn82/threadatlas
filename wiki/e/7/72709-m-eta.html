M-ETA, or Manual Entity Translation Accuracy, measures translation quality at the entity level. It checks whether an automatic translation contains one or more manually curated valid names for each entity in the target language.

5

M-ETA measures performance on the translation of challenging entity names in this evaluation. NLLB-200 and GPT-4 obtain the strongest baseline averages at 17.9% and 25.3%, respectively, while KG-MTNLLB reaches 41.1%.

Limitations

M-ETA is a simple metric that measures translation quality at the entity level. It is useful for evaluating KG-MT but cannot alone compare the overall performance of different systems.
