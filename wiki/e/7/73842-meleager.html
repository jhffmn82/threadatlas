Meleager

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: M^le^gcK, Me- Icager, Meh-at:er

Abstract written by a model

Meleager, leading captain of Alexander's phalanx, commanded a squadron and guarded Bactria. He backed the Macedonian foot soldiers' mutiny against the commanders' party, then Perdiccas executed him for betrayal.

Meleager is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK XVIII.

Meleager was the leading figure among the phalanx and encouraged the Macedonian foot soldiers to oppose the commanders' party. He helped carry on the mutiny and was later executed by Perdiccas.
