astrology in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Astrology is presented as a system of exact rules founded by the Thebans, using observed stars and eclipses for prognostication. The Chaldeans especially excel in it, reading stellar motions, risings, settings, and colors to foretell weather, nations, kings, and private persons.

The Thebans claim to have founded the exact rules of astrology. They observed the stars and eclipses and used their observations to make prognostications about future events.

CHAP. III.

The Chaldeans are said to excel all others in astrology and to study the motions and influences of the stars. They use the rising, setting, and colors of stars to foretell events affecting the weather, nations, kings, and private persons.

is_studied_by Egyptian priests [through long-term observation of celestial motions] Egyptian astrologers observe the courses, motions, periods, and stops of the planets and preserve observations of the stars for many years.
