the Prime Minister in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The Prime Minister, Lord Bellinger, is a former twice Premier of Britain and head of government. With Trelawney Hope, he seeks Sherlock Holmes’s help over a missing letter whose disclosure could cause European complications and war. Later, while lunching with Hope, he recognizes the recovered document and congratulates him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SECOND STAIN

Lord Bellinger, twice Premier of Britain, brings Trelawney Hope to Sherlock Holmes after the loss of the letter. He explains that revealing the matter to the police could expose the public to information capable of causing European complications and war.

When we arrived at the residence of the European Secretary it was

The Prime Minister is lunching with Trelawney Hope and remains deeply concerned about the missing document. He urges that the despatch-box be brought in, then recognizes the recovered document and congratulates Trelawney Hope.
