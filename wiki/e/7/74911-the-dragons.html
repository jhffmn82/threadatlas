Chapter Nine

The Dragon Tribe consists of enormous, jewel-adorned Dragons living in an underground cavern. They have been driven from the surface of the earth and are allowed to come out only once in a hundred years to search for food. After Woot disturbs them, they chase him but cannot prevent his escape.
