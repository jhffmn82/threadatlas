the Dragons in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The Dragons are enormous, jewel-adorned creatures with green, purple, and orange pie-plate-sized scales, tail jewel clusters, and mouth flame. Driven underground from the surface, they emerge only once every hundred years to feed. When Woot disturbs them, they chase him but cannot stop his escape.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The Dragon Tribe consists of enormous, jewel-adorned Dragons living in an underground cavern. They have been driven from the surface of the earth and are allowed to come out only once in a hundred years to search for food. After Woot disturbs them, they chase him but cannot prevent his escape.
