CASE STUDY

NVHelp Bot focuses on IT help and HR benefits. It uses approximately 2,000 multimodal documents containing text, tables, images, PDFs, and HTML pages, with a tech stack similar to NVInfo Bot's.

ENSURING FRESHNESS OF ENTERPRISE .. 3.1

NVHelp bot was used for evaluations of answer quality and latency across approximately 245 domain queries. The comparisons included OpenAI's GPT-4 and open-source models, with Llama3-70B showing strong answer quality and acceptable latency in the reported results.

COST ECONOMICS OF CHATBOT

The NVHelp Bot is used in an empirical evaluation showing that open-source models can achieve accuracy close to that of larger commercial models.
