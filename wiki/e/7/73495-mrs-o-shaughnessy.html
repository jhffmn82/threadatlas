Mrs. O'Shaughnessy in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Mrs. O'Shaughnessy, Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy's wife, helps guard Eileen Macarthy on her husband's order and greedily gathers diamonds and pearls. Mrs. O'Shaughnessy helps guard Eileen and eagerly gathers the diamonds and pearls that fall from her speech. On the third day, she is left alone with Eileen, falls asleep while listening to Eileen's deliberately tedious story, and allows Eileen to escape.

Now, just about this time Dr.

Mrs. O'Shaughnessy rushes from the house crying that Eily is gone and blaming witches. Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy accuses her of falling asleep and leaving Eily unguarded, then shakes and flings her aside.
