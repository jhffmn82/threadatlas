Acoris in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Acoris is king of Egypt, ruling Egypt and opposing the Persian king. He allies with and supplies Evagoras, receives Gaos into an alliance against Artaxerxes, raises a well-paid foreign army including many Grecians, and employs Chabrias to command it against the Persians.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

Acoris, king of Egypt, allied with Evagoras and supplied him with men, money, corn, and other necessities. He also received Gaos into an alliance against Artaxerxes.

Whilst these things were acting in Greece, Acoris^king of Egypt^

Acoris, king of Egypt, raised a large army of foreigners, including many Grecians, by offering generous pay. He employed Chabrias to command the army against the Persians.
