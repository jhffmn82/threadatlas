Abstract written by a model

Battle won by Agathocles: Carthaginian chariots and horsemen charged but were repelled; Hanno was killed, Bomilcar’s retreat caused a rout, and about 1,000 Carthaginians and 200 Grecians died.

The battle began with the Carthaginian chariots and horsemen charging Agathocles's forces, but they were repelled and put to flight. Hanno was killed during the infantry engagement, Bomilcar's retreat caused a general rout, and Agathocles won the battle.
