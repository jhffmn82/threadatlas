Abstract written by a model

The examination pencil is an unusually large, soft-leaded, dark blue pencil with silver lettering, seldom stocked by stationers. Only about an inch and a half remains, bearing “NN.” It was used and resharpened while copying a paper; Holmes uses its chips to seek a duplicate from four significant town stationers.
