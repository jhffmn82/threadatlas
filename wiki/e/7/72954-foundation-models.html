Foundation models

technology · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: foundation models, foundational models, large foundation models

Abstract written by a model

In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, foundational models are described as large language models that often lack domain-specific and enterprise-specific knowledge, are frozen after training, may hallucinate on unfamiliar enterprise content, and are difficult to align with human feedback without significant customization. In VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus, large foundation models are described as including large language models and vision extensions that encode vast knowledge from textual and multimodal training, while inaccurate or outdated parametric knowledge can produce incorrect outputs.

Abstract written by a model

Foundational models are large language models that often lack domain-specific and enterprise-specific knowledge. After training they are frozen in time and may hallucinate on unfamiliar enterprise content. Models without customization are difficult to align with human feedback; significant feedback across many areas may make customization possible.

Introduction

Large foundation models, including large language models and their extensions to vision, have become standard for diverse tasks because of their capabilities. Despite encoding extensive knowledge in their parameters, they can generate factually incorrect outputs because their knowledge may be inaccurate or outdated.
