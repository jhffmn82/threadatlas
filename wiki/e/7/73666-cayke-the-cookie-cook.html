Cayke the Cookie Cook

person · 1 work · 38 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Cayke, Cayke’s, Cookie Cook, Cookie cook who cannot cook good cookies, my good Frogman, the Cookie Cook, this lady
  • In The Lost Princess of Oz: Cayke the Cookie Cook is a small Yip woman, a traveler, and a Cookie Cook of the Yips who lives in the Yip Country on a high mountain. Her greatest treasure is a diamond-studded gold Magic Dishpan, inherited from her mother and grandmothers. The dishpan enables her to make her famous, exceptionally good cookies, and without it she cannot make good cookies or be completely happy. When Ugu the Shoemaker steals the dishpan from her house, Cayke loudly reports the loss. The Yips refuse to go with her into the lower world, so she resolves to search alone; the Frogman joins her and becomes her companion. She travels down the mountain with him, persuades him to carry her across a gulf, and continues eastward through the world. She refuses to bathe in the Truth Pond because she fears being forced to tell the Frogman everything she thinks and making him dislike her. After crossing the Winkie River, she and the Frogman are taken prisoner in Bear Center, where she hopes the bears may know of the dishpan. The Bear King’s magic reveals that Ugu possesses it, so Cayke determines to confront him. She joins Dorothy’s party in pursuing Ugu, enters his castle, and sees her dishpan in his golden cage. She helps attempt to reach it through a human pyramid but declines to repeat the effort after the group falls. Ugu escapes with the dishpan, but the Magic Picture and Great Book locate it in the northeast corner of Quadling Country. Cayke is taken to the Emerald City as Ozma’s guest and receives deference, though she remains unhappy until the dishpan is restored. When it is returned, she dances, embraces Ozma, and kisses her gratefully. Her search ends, but she is not eager to leave the Emerald City.

Cayke the Cookie Cook in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Cayke the Cookie Cook is a small Yip woman, a traveler, and a Cookie Cook of the Yips who lives in the Yip Country on a high mountain. Her greatest treasure is a diamond-studded gold Magic Dishpan, inherited from her mother and grandmothers. The dishpan enables her to make her famous, exceptionally good cookies, and without it she cannot make good cookies or be completely happy. When Ugu the Shoemaker steals the dishpan from her house, Cayke loudly reports the loss. The Yips refuse to go with her into the lower world, so she resolves to search alone; the Frogman joins her and becomes her companion. She travels down the mountain with him, persuades him to carry her across a gulf, and continues eastward through the world. She refuses to bathe in the Truth Pond because she fears being forced to tell the Frogman everything she thinks and making him dislike her. After crossing the Winkie River, she and the Frogman are taken prisoner in Bear Center, where she hopes the bears may know of the dishpan. The Bear King’s magic reveals that Ugu possesses it, so Cayke determines to confront him. She joins Dorothy’s party in pursuing Ugu, enters his castle, and sees her dishpan in his golden cage. She helps attempt to reach it through a human pyramid but declines to repeat the effort after the group falls. Ugu escapes with the dishpan, but the Magic Picture and Great Book locate it in the northeast corner of Quadling Country. Cayke is taken to the Emerald City as Ozma’s guest and receives deference, though she remains unhappy until the dishpan is restored. When it is returned, she dances, embraces Ozma, and kisses her gratefully. Her search ends, but she is not eager to leave the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section

12 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

Cayke discovers that her diamond-studded gold dishpan has been stolen and loudly reports the loss. When the Yips refuse to accompany her into the lower world, she resolves to go alone, but the Frogman joins her. She travels with him down the mountain and persuades him to carry her across a gulf.

CHAPTER 4

Cayke the Cookie Cook accompanies the Frogman while searching for her stolen diamond-studded gold dishpan. She says the dishpan belonged to her mother and grandmothers and that she cannot make good cookies without it.

CHAPTER 13

Cayke travels with the Frogman in search of the diamond-studded dishpan. She is shocked when he confesses that he is not wise, but refuses to bathe in the Truth Pond because she fears that telling him the truth would make him dislike her.

CHAPTER 14

Cayke tells a Winkie woman that her diamond-studded gold dishpan was stolen and that she can no longer cook good cookies without it. She and the Frogman travel east, stay overnight with the ferryman, and cross the Winkie River.

CHAPTER 15

Cayke travels with the Frogman and is captured after agreeing to go peacefully to Bear Center. She hopes the bears or their king may know who stole her jeweled dishpan.

CHAPTER 16

Cayke tells the Bear King that she is searching the world for her stolen diamond-studded gold dishpan. She is disappointed when the King’s magic produces only an image of the dishpan, then learns that Ugu the Shoemaker has it and determines to confront him and demand its return.

CHAPTER 17

Cayke joins Dorothy's party in pursuing Ugu and is pleased when the two groups agree to travel together. She explains that the Bear King's magic showed them Ugu and that Ugu had stolen her dishpan.

CHAPTER 19

Cayke the Cookie Cook owns the diamond-studded gold Magic Dishpan. Ugu steals it from her house in the Yip Country, and she later joins the Frogman in an expedition to find him.

CHAPTER 22

Cayke enters the castle with the group and sees her diamond-studded dishpan inside Ugu's golden cage. She longs for the dishpan and joins the attempt to form a human pyramid, but refuses to try the plan again after the group falls.

CHAPTER 23

Cayke is distressed when Ugu escapes with her precious dishpan. She learns through the Magic Picture and the Great Book that the dishpan is in the northeast corner of Quadling Country.

CHAPTER 25

Cayke is promised a journey to the Emerald City so that Ozma can have her cherished dishpan restored. As Ozma’s guest, she receives much deference but repeatedly says that she cannot be quite happy until her jeweled dishpan is found.

CHAPTER 26

Cayke the Cookie Cook is delighted when her lost dishpan is returned and dances, embraces Ozma, and kisses her gratefully. Although her mission is complete, she is in no hurry to leave the Emerald City.
