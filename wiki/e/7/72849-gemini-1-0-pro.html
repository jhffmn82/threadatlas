Gemini-1.0-pro

large language model · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Gemini-pro, GeminiPro, GeminiPro 1.0, gemini-1.0-pro

Abstract written by a model

In "Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Gemini-pro was evaluated using gemini-1.0-pro across the retrieval methods, and its MedGraphRAG results exceeded its no-retrieval, standard RAG, and GraphRAG results on most reported datasets. In "RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarization of Biomedical Texts," Gemini-1.0-pro was used to implement the Large Language Model component and serve as the baseline LLM under default settings. In "Retrieval augmented text-to-SQL generation for epidemiological question answering using electronic health records," GeminiPro 1.0 showed improved executability with the advanced prompt and RAG, but its accuracy did not improve from the simple to the advanced prompt; it reached 38.2% accuracy with RAG-top1 and 67.6% accuracy in the RAG-top1-oracle scenario.
