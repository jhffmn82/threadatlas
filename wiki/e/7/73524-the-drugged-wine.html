the drugged wine in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The drugged wine, sent by Tong-Ki-Tcheng and presented by Chop-Chin, contains a powerful temporary narcotic; guards and priests drink it, sleep deeply, and allow removal of the Golden Dragon.

The drugged wine is presented by Chop-Chin as a drink sent by Tong-Ki-Tcheng. The guards and priests drink it and fall into deep sleep, enabling Chop-Chin to reach and remove the Golden Dragon.
