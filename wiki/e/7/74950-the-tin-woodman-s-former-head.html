Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

The Head identifies itself as Nick Chopper, the name the Tin Woodman had used when he was a meat-bodied woodman. It says that it retains its brains and intellect but has a poor memory, does not remember loving Nimmie Amee, rejects its relationship with the Tin Woodman, and wants to remain undisturbed in the cupboard.
