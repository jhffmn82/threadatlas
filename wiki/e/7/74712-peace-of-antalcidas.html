Also called: Peace of Anialcidiis, articles of the peace, common league, late establishment, league, league made, peace, peace all over Greece, peace league, public league
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Peace of Antalcidas was a peace treaty between the Persians and the Greeks that established city autonomy, oaths, freedom from garrisons, and local laws, bringing peace throughout Greece before Lacedaemonian violations.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Peace of Antalcidas was a peace treaty between Artaxerxes and the Grecians that ended the war between the Lacedaemonians and the Grecians, requiring the Greek cities in Asia to return to obedience while the other Grecians governed their own cities according to their laws.

Peace of Antalcidas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Peace of Antalcidas was a peace treaty between the Persians and the Greeks, establishing city autonomy, oaths, freedom from garrisons, and local laws; it brought peace throughout Greece before Lacedaemonian violations.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

The Peace of Antalcidas had established peace throughout Greece, freed cities from garrisons, and allowed them to govern according to their own laws. The Lacedaemonians soon violated it by interfering in cities and attacking Mantinea.
