BOOK XXXVIL

Pompey was called the Great because of his eminent services to the Romans, accomplished partly under Sylla and partly through his own valor. His conflict with Julius Caesar led to another civil war. After losing his army in a great battle, he was murdered near Alexandria.

98. Ptolemy Physcon, the brother of Philometor, began hb

Pompey besieged Lagnetia and demanded that the Numantians be surrendered. After the Numantians attacked the Lagnetians, Pompey captured the city, put its citizens to the sword, released the Numantian auxiliaries, and razed the city.
