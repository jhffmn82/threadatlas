grape-juice in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Grape-juice is squeezed from ripe grapes, drawn off from skins and seeds, and left in a warm place. Its sugar changes into alcohol and gas, developing bubbles; it contains water and becomes wine, no longer safe as a drink or suitable for making jelly.

Grape-juice is squeezed from ripe grapes and left in a warm place. Its sugar changes into alcohol and a gas, so the juice is no longer suitable for making jelly and becomes wine.
