fgen in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024

fgen is a Llama 2 (7B)-based response generation model and RARG component. It is fine-tuned to generate counter-responses from an input claim and retrieved evidence, then optimized with reinforcement learning; its supervised fine-tuning variant is RARG (S).
