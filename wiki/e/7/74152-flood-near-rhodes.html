Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, m.

A flood near Rhodes occurred after violent spring storms, rain, and exceptionally large hailstones. Water filled the lower parts of the city, killed more than five hundred people, destroyed some houses, and damaged others, but a breach in the wall allowed the flood to drain into the sea.
