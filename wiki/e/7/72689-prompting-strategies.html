Prompting Strategies in Improving Assessment of Tutoring Practices using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zifei (FeiFei) Han · 2024

Prompting Strategies are four prompt types designed for GPT models to evaluate tutor performance and tutoring practices through criteria, scores, and evidence-based interpretation. They comprise Basic Zero-shot Prompt Types I and II, Tree of Thought Prompt, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation Based Prompt. The study begins by defining and designing these strategies, then assesses them for hallucination, correctness, cost, and evaluation accuracy. Results compare their effects: Retrieval-Augmented Generation performs consistently well, while GPT-4’s Zero-shot Prompt Type I is more accurate than other strategies. The conclusion identifies Retrieval-Augmented Generation as the most cost-effective strategy for both GPT models while supporting accurate evaluations.
