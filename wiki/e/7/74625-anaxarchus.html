Abstract written by a model

Anaxarchus, a sophist, argues that Alexander deserves divine honors more than Bacchus or Hercules and should be worshipped while alive, provoking Callisthenes’ rebuke.

SUR IX.

Anaxarchus begins the discussion by arguing that Alexander has more right to be regarded as a god than Bacchus or Hercules. He maintains that Alexander should be worshipped while alive rather than only after death, and Callisthenes rebukes him for advancing this proposal.
