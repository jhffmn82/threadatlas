CHAPTER XVIII. / WHAT MAKES US WARM? .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Cold exposure causes the body to lose heat and can lead to freezing and death. Alcohol may make freezing happen more quickly because it increases heat loss and prevents a person from recognizing the danger.
