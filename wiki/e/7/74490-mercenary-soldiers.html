Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXV.

The mercenary soldiers were denied their pay by the Carthaginians and consequently revolted. They brought Carthage into miserable distress and, under their captains, adopted cruel measures against Carthaginian prisoners and confederates.
