Cappadocian royal genealogy claims descent from Cyrus and Anaphas, tracing rulers through Anaphas, Ditamus, Ariamenes, and Ariarathes to an adopted Ariarathes who recovered the kingdom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXI.

The Cappadocian royal genealogy claims descent from Cyrus and from one of the seven Persians who killed Magus. It traces the succession through Anaphas, Ditamus, Ariamenes, Ariarathes, and later rulers, including the adopted Ariarathes who recovered the kingdom after Macedonian conquest.
