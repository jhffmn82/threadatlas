Abstract written by a model

The princess is beautiful, wise, graceful, and gentle, with black hair and star-like eyes, but can make only three remarks. Pressured by her father to accept a marriage proposal, she leaves to avoid it. Her fixed replies help her expose or outwit a butterman impostor and robbers, gaining rubies and sapphires. She finally accepts marriage with the young king she loves.

" CHAPTER XI.

The princess is beautiful, wise, graceful, and gentle, but can speak only three remarks. She runs away when her father orders her to answer a marriage proposal with “With all my heart!”, then uses her unusual replies to obtain bags of gems from a butterman impostor and robbers before meeting a young king whom she loves.
