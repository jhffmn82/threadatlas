Persianization of Alexander's court in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander’s adoption of Persian customs reshaped his court: he appointed Asiatic officers and Asian noble bodyguards, wore Persian dress, clothed companions and horsemen similarly, and brought concubines.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

Alexander ordered the officers of his court to be Asiatics and made Asian nobles squires of the body, including Oxathres, the brother of Darius. He wore the Persian diadem and other Persian clothing, gave purple gowns to his friends, dressed his horsemen in Persian habit, and brought concubines with him.
