general diet or senate in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A council of representatives from allied cities, established by the Athenians, meets at Athens by common consent, with each city—great or small—sending one representative.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAR III.

The Athenians established a general diet or senate composed of representatives chosen from the allied cities. It was to meet at Athens, with one representative from each city regardless of its size.
