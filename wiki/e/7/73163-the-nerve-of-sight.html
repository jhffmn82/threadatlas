the nerve of sight in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The delicate nerve of sight, behind the pupil, carries accounts of everything seen to the brain, can be harmed by bright light or trying to see in dark, and does not regrow after being killed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The nerve of sight is in a little, round room behind the pupil and carries accounts of what is seen to the brain. It is delicate and can be seriously harmed by bright light or trying to see in the dark.
