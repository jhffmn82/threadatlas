Red Chief of the Rustywhanger in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The gigantic robber chieftain leads Red Chief's band, hides a bag of sapphires in an oak, mistakes a princess's remark for an approaching enemy army, and offers her sapphires and escape.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER XI.

The Red Chief of the Rustywhanger is the gigantic leader of the robbers. He believes the princess's remark signals that an enemy army is near, offers her ten thousand sapphires, and directs her toward a path out of the forest.
