Dr. Huxtable in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Dr. Thorneycroft Huxtable, M.A., Ph.D., is a doctor and founder and principal of the Priory School near Mackleton. Exhausted, he asks Sherlock Holmes to find the missing Lord Saltire, describing the disappearance, Heidegger’s flight, and delayed local inquiry. He supplies a blue cricket-cap clue, reports gipsies arrested, and later receives Holmes’s note after Heidegger’s body is found.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

Thorneycroft Huxtable, M.A., Ph.D., is the founder and principal of the Priory School near Mackleton. He comes to Baker Street in a state of exhaustion to ask Sherlock Holmes to find Lord Saltire, explains the boy's disappearance and Heidegger's flight, and admits that the local investigation was mishandled and delayed.

When the nobleman and his secretary had left, my friend flung

Dr. Huxtable brings Holmes a blue cricket-cap found in a gipsy caravan and reports that the gipsies have been arrested. After Heidegger’s body is discovered, Holmes sends a note to Dr. Huxtable through a peasant.
