Mr. Woodley in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Mr. Woodley is a coarse, puffy-faced, red-moustached visitor from South Africa, a frequent visitor to Charlington Hall, and a dangerous brute and bully. He seeks to marry Violet Smith for her inheritance, first making unwanted advances and assaulting her; Carruthers intervenes and drives him off. After Woodley overhears Holmes making inquiries, he abuses and attacks Holmes, but Holmes defeats him. Woodley later abducts Violet with Williamson, forces a marriage in Charlington Wood, and boasts of it to Carruthers, who shoots him; Woodley survives, disfigured by the wound or accident. He is subsequently tried for abduction and assault and sentenced to ten years.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

Mr. Woodley is a coarse, red-moustached man who returned from South Africa with Mr. Carruthers. He makes unwanted advances to Violet Smith, seizes her, and is knocked down and driven away after Carruthers intervenes; Holmes also considers his connection with Carruthers suspicious.

Mr. Woodley is a frequent week-end visitor to Charlington Hall and is described by the landlord as a notably rough or disreputable person. After overhearing Holmes’s questions at the public-house, he abuses Holmes and strikes at him, but Holmes defeats him in a fight; Woodley leaves in a cart.

The Thursday brought us another letter from our client.

Jack Woodley abducts Violet Smith with Williamson’s assistance and has her forcibly married in Charlington Wood. When Bob Carruthers arrives, Woodley boasts that Violet is his wife, but Carruthers shoots him. Dr. Watson reports that Woodley survives the wound.

Holmes rose and tossed the end of his cigarette into the grate.

Woodley is tried for abduction and assault and receives a sentence of ten years. He is described as having a reputation as a very dangerous ruffian.
