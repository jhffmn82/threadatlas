the battle

Abstract written by a model

The battle near the mountains involved Antigonus and Eumenes, from initial cavalry and infantry charges through Eumenes's early gains and Antigonus's counterattack. Both armies prepared to resume fighting at night, then encamped; it ended with casualties and burial rights sought.

The battle involved Antigonus and Eumenes and continued from the initial charges through fighting between the cavalry and infantry. Eumenes's forces first gained major advantages, Antigonus then counterattacked, and both armies prepared to fight again at night before finally encamping.
