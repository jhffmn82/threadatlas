OMOP-CDM is a common data model that harmonizes observational healthcare data and addresses retrieval variability across databases. Underpinned by standardized vocabularies, it is widely recognized as a standard for RWD analysis and supports conforming claims and electronic health record databases. The released question-SQL pairs dataset adheres to it, enabling applicability across differing RWD data models; conforming databases could provide access to 2.1 billion patient records. It also underlies the OMOP query library, from which fifteen epidemiologically relevant SQL queries were adapted.
