Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, the fleece's theft and return to Hellas is planned to depend on Medea being charmed into helping Jason and has not yet taken place. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Jason’s recovery of the Golden Fleece is achieved with Medea’s aid.
