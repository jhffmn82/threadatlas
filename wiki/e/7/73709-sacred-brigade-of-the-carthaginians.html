Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Sacred Brigade was a two-thousand-five-hundred-strong Carthaginian military unit commanded by Hanno. Supporting his right wing, it fought bravely after his death, was surrounded as others fled, gave way, and all were killed.

The Sacred Brigade supported Hanno on the right wing of the Carthaginian army. It fought bravely after Hanno's death but eventually gave way when most of the army fled and the enemy surrounded it.
