Jugurtha in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Jugurtha is an African king and king of Numidia, brother of Adherbal and father-in-law of Bocchus. After routing the Numidians, he besieges Adherbal at Cirta, ignores Roman orders, starves the city into surrender, kills Adherbal and Italians who aided him. Later Marius summons him deceptively; he is seized and delivered bound to Lucius Sylla.

Jugurtha routed the Numidians in a great battle and besieged his brother Adherbal in Cirta. He ignored Roman orders to raise the siege, forced the city to surrender through famine, killed Adherbal, and scourged to death the Italians who had aided him.

Jugurtha is summoned by Marius under the pretense of discussing matters important to them both. He is seized through this deception and delivered bound to Lucius Sylla.
