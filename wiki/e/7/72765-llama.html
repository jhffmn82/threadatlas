Llama Large Language Model in Achieving Higher Factual Accuracy in Llama LLM with Weighted Distribution of Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zhenhua Gai · 2024-05-21

Llama Large Language Model is a large language model and transformer-based model that advances natural language processing. It is extensively trained on diverse datasets and uses multiple attention layers to generate coherent, contextually relevant language, with strong language understanding and generation and high performance across varied tasks. Its central limitation is factual accuracy: probabilistic generation can yield plausible but incorrect, or hallucinated, information. To address this problem, it is integrated and evaluated with a weighted Retrieval-Augmented Generation mechanism intended to ground outputs in verifiable knowledge. The reported evaluation assesses factual accuracy, contextual relevance, and overall output quality. From the initial challenge of incorrect information, the work moves through weighted RAG integration and experimental assessment to reported improvements in factual accuracy, relevance, fluency, coherence, precision, recall, F1 score, and BLEU score. Human evaluations also support the enhanced model’s practical applicability and reliability.
