Also called: a large toadstool, the toadstool, this toadstool

Abstract written by a model

The Green Man's toadstool is a large forest plant at the foot of the Green Man's oak-tree and serves as his seat. When Eileen seeks to remove a charm, she returns there with a sprig of holly, taps or strikes it three times, and speaks the reversal words, causing the Green Man to appear.
