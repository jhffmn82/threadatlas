Captain Peter Carey

Abstract written by a model

Captain Peter Carey, known as Black Peter, was a daring and successful seal and whale fisher, shipmaster, and former captain of the Dundee steam sealer Sea Unicorn. Born in 1845, he retired from sealing and whaling after successful voyages, travelled for some years, bought Woodman’s Lee, and lived there for six years. In ordinary life he was a strict Puritan but also a violent intermittent drunkard who abused family and neighbours. He lived in and owned a private hut or cabin, where he kept a sealskin tobacco pouch and a tin box. Carey was linked to securities as their original seller and was said to have murdered a landsman at sea for the tin box’s contents. After attacking Cairns with a knife, he was killed by Cairns with a harpoon through the chest.
