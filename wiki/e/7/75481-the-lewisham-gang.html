Abstract written by a model

The Lewisham gang, identified by Inspector Stanley Hopkins as the Randall family, father and two sons, is a burglary gang. After a substantial burglary at Sydenham, it is suspected of the Abbey Grange burglary and killing because it matches Lady Brackenstall’s description. Its arrest in New York proves it could not have committed the Kent murder that night.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

The Lewisham gang consists of the father and two sons, known as the three Randalls. Inspector Stanley Hopkins believes they committed the burglary and the killing because they resemble Lady Brackenstall’s description of the three intruders and had recently committed another burglary at Sydenham.
