Aea is the city and region ruled by Aeetes, beside the river Phasis near the Caucasus and the grove of Ares. It is a distant destination at the edge of Pontus and the world. The sons of Phrixus sail from Aea toward Orchomenus; later Jason, his companions, and the Argonauts reach it under Hera’s protection seeking the Golden Fleece, with a deity to guide their return by another route.
