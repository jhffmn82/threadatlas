Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Demetrians besieged Rhodes by land and sea, mounted its walls, suffered losses, captured commanders, and had 400 men surrender. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Demetrius’s army included artificers, workmen, and troops operating by land and sea, suffered losses during Rhodian fire attacks, and failed to hold the part of the city seized during a night assault. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Demetrius's army comprised a 56,000-man land force that was transported from Greece to Asia and later sent into winter quarters near the mouth of Pontus.

Abstract written by a model

The Demetrians, a besieging army initially superior to Ptolemy in cavalry, attacked Rhodes by land and sea, mounted its walls, but suffered losses, captured commanders, and 400 surrendered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

The Demetrians attacked the walls and harbour from ships and from land, assisted one another with shouts and assaults, and in some cases mounted the walls. The Rhodians killed some, captured wounded commanders, and later forced 400 Demetrians to throw down their arms and submit.
