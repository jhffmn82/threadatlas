Abstract written by a model

Jewish laws, attributed to Moses, organized religious, civic, family, and military life, protecting inherited land, children’s education, and priests’ worship; they differed from other peoples’ laws before becoming obsolete through foreign rule and mixture.

BOOK XXXVIL

Jewish laws are attributed to Moses, who organized religious rites, government, inheritance, education, marriage, funerals, and military training. The laws prohibited the permanent sale of inherited land, encouraged the education of children, and gave priests larger portions so they could attend to worship.
