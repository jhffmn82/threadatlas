Tommy Kwikstep in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Tommy Kwikstep is a young Munchkin who lives in a polished hollow tree with doors at both ends. Named for his fast errand-running, he receives a wish from a fairy or witch while on an errand and becomes twenty-legged, with corns on most of his hundred toes. Carrying a note, he seeks the wish-giver until Polychrome restores him; he then delivers the note and resumes errands.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

Tommy Kwikstep explains that a wish for twenty legs changed him while he was running an errand for a fairy or witch. Polychrome restores him to his former two-legged shape and removes his corns, after which he leaves to deliver a note and run errands again.
