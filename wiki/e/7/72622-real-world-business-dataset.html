The business dataset was collected from a real AI assistant and includes daily conversations with the assistant and users’ app screenshots. It is used to extract personal memories and evaluate personalized agents across multiple downstream applications.

The real-world business dataset contains approximately 11.35 billion raw text items, including conversations and screenshot contents, collected between March 2024 and June 2024. After cleaning, it contains around 0.35 billion memories and is used to sample 2,000 users for training and 500 for testing.
