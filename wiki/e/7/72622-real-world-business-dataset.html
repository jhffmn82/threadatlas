real-world business dataset

Abstract written by a model

A real-world business dataset collected from an AI assistant product, containing daily assistant-user conversations and app screenshots from March through June 2024. Its roughly 11.35 billion raw text items are cleaned into about 0.35 billion memories, used to extract personal memories, evaluate personalized agents, and sample 2,000 training and 500 testing users.

real-world business dataset is in one work on this site, Crafting Personalized Agents through Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Editable Memory Graphs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
