Orontas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Orontas, governor of Armenia, commands the Persian land army in Cyprus and becomes chief commander; after accusing Teribazus before Artaxerxes, Teribazus’s acquittal brings Orontas disgrace as a false accuser.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

Orontas commanded the Persian land army in Cyprus and later became the chief commander there. He accused Teribazus to Artaxerxes, but after Teribazus was acquitted, Orontas was discarded as a false accuser and marked with disgrace.
