Peter Carey's killing in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26 · the work’s page · read it where Peter Carey's killing first appears

Abstract written by a model

Captain Peter Carey is killed in his hut, pinned to the wall with his own harpoon. Inspector Stanley Hopkins initially attributes the killing to John Hopley Neligan after a bloodstained notebook bearing Neligan’s initials is found. Holmes finds Neligan’s involvement impossible because of the strength required; Patrick Cairns admits killing Carey in self-defense after Carey attacked him.

The murder of Captain Peter Carey occurred in his hut, where he was pinned against the wall with one of his own harpoons. The unit does not establish who killed him, though John Hopley Neligan is arrested after a bloodstained notebook bearing his initials is found.

Patrick Cairns admits killing Carey after Carey attacked him, describing the act as self-defense rather than murder.
