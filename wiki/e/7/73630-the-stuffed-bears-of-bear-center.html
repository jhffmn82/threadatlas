the stuffed bears of Bear Center in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The stuffed bears of Bear Center are a regiment of hundreds of toy bears, in many colors and sizes but made alike. Armed with tin swords, popguns, and long spears, they serve the Lavender Bear and obey the Bear King. Summoned from tree holes, they surround Cayke and the Frogman and bow to the Lavender Bear; later they report no sight of Cayke’s golden dishpan, bring the Little Pink Bear, and promise good behavior.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15

The stuffed bears emerge from holes in the surrounding trees after Corporal Waddle summons them. They carry toy weapons, surround Cayke and the Frogman, and bow to the Lavender Bear when he enters.

CHAPTER 16

The toy bears tell the Bear King that none of them has seen Cayke’s golden dishpan and bring the Little Pink Bear from its tree hollow. They promise to behave while the Bear King is away after he threatens to send naughty bears to a girl or boy in America.
