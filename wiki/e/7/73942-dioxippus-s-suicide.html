Dioxippus's suicide

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: destroy himself, he murdered himself, his death

Abstract written by a model

Dioxippus’s suicide followed a false theft accusation after courtiers planted a golden cup under his cushion. He wrote to Alexander protesting that his enemies had plotted against him, entrusted the letter to servants, and killed himself. Alexander was greatly troubled, later regretted the death, and recognized Dioxippus’s honesty and the accusers’ knavery.
