Harpalus had left a vast treasure, part of which the Athenians used to support Leosthenes and the war. His name was also associated with the condemnation and flight of Demosthenes, who was accused of receiving bribes from him.

CHAP. II.

Harpalus had earlier left Asia and arrived in Crete with mercenaries. Thimbron, described as one of his special friends, assassinated him and seized his money and soldiers.
