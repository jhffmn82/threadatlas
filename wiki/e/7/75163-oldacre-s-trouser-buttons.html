Oldacre's trouser buttons

Abstract written by a model

Several discolored metal discs found in the ashes are identified as trouser buttons, including one marked with Oldacre's tailor, Hyams. Mrs. Lexington says they belonged to the clothes Oldacre wore, making them appear to support the theory that he was burned in the wood-pile.
