Abstract written by a model

Ophelas's army was a force of more than 10,000 infantry, 600 cavalry, 100 chariots, and many camp followers, including women and children. It marched eighteen days and suffered two months near the Syrtes from thirst, hunger, beasts, and serpents; after Ophelas's death, Agathocles absorbed its survivors.

Ophelas's army included more than 10,000 infantry, 600 cavalry, 100 chariots, and many additional followers, including women and children. It endured an eighteen-day march followed by two months of hardship in a dry country near the Syrtes, suffering from thirst, hunger, wild beasts, and poisonous serpents. After Ophelas was killed, Agathocles persuaded the survivors to lay down their arms and took the whole army into his service.
