tooth in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Teeth are bone covered with enamel and furnished with nerve. Different kinds do different work: front teeth cut food and back teeth grind it. They need cleanliness, careful brushing, and protection from cracking nuts or biting thread.

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The front teeth cut food, and the back teeth grind it. They should be protected from cracking nuts or biting thread, cleaned carefully, and brushed before breakfast and at night.
