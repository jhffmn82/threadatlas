Abstract written by a model

Lord Mount-James, Godfrey Staunton’s uncle, nearest relative, and presumed heir-giver, is a miser among England’s richest men. He first refuses to fund Staunton’s search, then becomes alarmed when kidnapping is linked to his wealth.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE MISSING THREE-QUARTER

Lord Mount-James is Godfrey Staunton’s nearest relative and presumed heir-giver, as well as one of the richest men in England. He initially refuses financial responsibility for finding Staunton, but becomes alarmed when Holmes suggests that criminals may have kidnapped Staunton to obtain information about his wealth.
