Abstract written by a model

Counter-responses are generated by RARG using retrieved scientific evidence and RLHF-based alignment. They aim to counter misinformation with factual, polite, relevant, coherent, and effective responses, addressing limits of language-based methods that improve text quality without sufficiently incorporating evidence.
