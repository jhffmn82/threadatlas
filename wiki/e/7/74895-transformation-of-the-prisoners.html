transformation of the prisoners in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mrs. Yoop creates permanent, irreversible yookoohoo enchantments that transform prisoners into unusual forms: Nick Chopper becomes the Tin Owl, the Scarecrow a stuffed Brown Bear, and Woot a Green Monkey. The changes do not hurt them but alter feelings so they accept them. Though Ozma can break some enchantments, Woot's requires an exchange, while the Tin Owl seeks release.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Mrs. Yoop permanently transforms Nick Chopper into a Tin Owl, the Scarecrow into a stuffed Brown Bear, and Woot into a Green Monkey. The transformations do not hurt them, but added magic alters their feelings so they can accept their new forms.

Chapter Ten

Mrs. Yoop's enchantment remains the cause of the Tin Owl's altered form. The Tin Owl hopes to break it as soon as possible.

Chapter Thirteen

Mrs. Yoop's enchantments transform people into unusual forms, including a bear, a Tin Owl, a Canary, and a Green Monkey. Ozma can break some of them, but Woot's enchantment is presented as an exception that requires an exchange rather than simple restoration.
