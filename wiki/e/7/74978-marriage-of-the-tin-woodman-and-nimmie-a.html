marriage of the Tin Woodman and Nimmie Amee in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The planned marriage of the Tin Woodman and Nimmie Amee is based on his sense of duty and kindness rather than love. Though Woot questions it, the Tin Woodman intends to ask whether she still wishes to marry; if she agrees, Ozma will arrange a grand Emerald City wedding and she will become Empress of the Winkies.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

The Tin Woodman intends to marry Nimmie Amee because he believes it is his duty, although Woot questions marrying without love. He plans to make her an Empress and dress her in tin finery.

Chapter Fifteen

The Tin Woodman plans to ask Nimmie Amee whether she still wishes to marry him. If she agrees, Ozma will arrange a grand wedding at the Emerald City, after which Nimmie Amee would become Empress of the Winkies.
