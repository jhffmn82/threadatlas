Chapter Three

The Tin Woodman intends to marry Nimmie Amee because he believes it is his duty, although Woot questions marrying without love. He plans to make her an Empress and dress her in tin finery.

Chapter Fifteen

The Tin Woodman plans to ask Nimmie Amee whether she still wishes to marry him. If she agrees, Ozma will arrange a grand wedding at the Emerald City, after which Nimmie Amee would become Empress of the Winkies.
