Also called: Mistral+MoE+RAG model, enhanced Mistral model, enhanced model, integrated model

Abstract written by a model

Mistral+MoE+RAG is an enhanced Mistral model produced by combining the study’s approaches, specialized expertise, and extensive knowledge bases. Evaluated on Google BIG-Bench, it outperforms Mistral+MoE across evaluated metrics and Mistral in accuracy, precision, recall, and F1. It achieved 0.93 accuracy, 0.90 precision, 0.88 recall, and 0.89 F1, producing deeper, more detailed, relevant, contextually rich, accurate, and nuanced responses.

Mistral+MoE+RAG is in one work on this site, Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
