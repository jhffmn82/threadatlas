Mistral+MoE+RAG in Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning

Xingyu Xiong and Mingliang Zheng · 2024-02-23

Mistral+MoE+RAG is an enhanced Mistral model produced by combining the study’s approaches, specialized expertise, and extensive knowledge bases. Evaluated on Google BIG-Bench, it outperforms Mistral+MoE across evaluated metrics and Mistral in accuracy, precision, recall, and F1. It achieved 0.93 accuracy, 0.90 precision, 0.88 recall, and 0.89 F1, producing deeper, more detailed, relevant, contextually rich, accurate, and nuanced responses.

IV. RESULTS / A. Implementation .. C. Qualitative Analysis

Mistral+MoE+RAG is the enhanced Mistral model evaluated on the Google BIG-Bench dataset. It achieved an accuracy of 0.93, precision of 0.90, recall of 0.88, and F1 score of 0.89, and produced responses with greater depth, detail, and relevance than the baseline model.

VI. CONCLUSION

Mistral+MoE+RAG is the enhanced model produced by combining the approaches examined in the study. It demonstrates improved performance and an ability to generate contextually rich, accurate, and nuanced responses.
