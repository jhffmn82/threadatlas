marriages of Perdiccas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Perdiccas married Nicaea to retain Antipater's alliance and also married Cleopatra, seeking through her position and authority to secure the kingdom.

Narrative, section by section

Perdiccas and king Philip having overcome Ariarathes, delivered

Perdiccas married Nicaea, the daughter of Antipater, because he had been allied with Antipater and wished to avoid his opposition. He also married Cleopatra, intending to use her position and authority to secure the kingdom.
