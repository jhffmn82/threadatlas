Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In Asia, the Persian king having invaded ligypt some years before

Artaxerxes prepared a Persian army of 300,000 foot soldiers and 30,000 horsemen, supported by a fleet of 300 galleys, 600 ships of burden, and other transports. He assembled the army at Babylon and marched it through Syria into Phoenicia.
