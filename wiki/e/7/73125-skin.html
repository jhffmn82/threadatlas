skin

object · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: a more delicate kind of skin, lining skin, outside skin, the skin, true skin, your skin

Abstract written by a model

Skin covers the body externally and lines it internally in a more delicate form. It has a thin protective outside layer over true skin full of nerves, pores, and perspiration tubes. These tubes carry away waste matter and water, supporting health, while skin loses body heat to cooler air. Washing with soap and water keeps pores unclogged and skin fresh and healthy.

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The skin covers the body, contains many nerves, and carries away waste matter and water through tiny perspiration tubes. It should be washed with soap and water so its pores do not become clogged and it can remain fresh and healthy.
