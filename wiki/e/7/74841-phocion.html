Phocion

Abstract written by a model

Phocion, son of Phocus and an Athenian associated with oligarchy, commanded Greek mercenaries from Egypt in the Persian expedition against Cyprus. As Nicanor’s friend and agent under Antipater, he urged retaining forts; after democratic restoration, he was condemned and executed.

Phocion is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

While these things were acting, Nicanor (who held Muayohis)

Phocion, a friend and agent of Nicanor and the chief commander under Antipater, urged Alexander to retain the forts rather than restore them to the Athenians. After the democratic restoration, he was condemned for allegedly trying to enslave Athens and abolish its democracy and ancient laws; he was executed by drinking hemlock, and his body was cast out of Attica unburied.
