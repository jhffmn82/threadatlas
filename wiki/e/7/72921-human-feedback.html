Abstract written by a model

In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, human feedback is gathered for RAG-based chatbots and incorporated through systematic mechanisms and error analysis to guide iterative improvements to chatbot performance and adaptability. In HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications, human feedback is integrated into a RAG System Optimisation Feedback Loop to align metrics with user preferences and validate real-world performance.
