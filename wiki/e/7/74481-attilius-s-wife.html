Attilius's wife in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Attilius's wife, mother of his sons, grieves her husband's death, blames young men, and drives cruel punishment of captives, ordering her sons' harshness and imprisoning Amilcar with Bostar's corpse on scant food.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXIV.

Attilius's wife blamed the young men for the death of her husband and urged them to treat the captives cruelly. She confined Amilcar with Bostar's corpse, gave Amilcar only enough food to keep him alive, and refused his pleas for compassion.
