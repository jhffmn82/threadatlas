Larissa in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Larissa is a city with a citadel separate from it. Its Aleuadas sought Macedonian help against Alexander the Pheraean; citizens opened its gates to Alexander, king of Macedonia, who mastered the city and later seized the castle. Later it surrendered to Demetrius, who took the citadel, chained its garrison, and restored ancient laws and liberties.
