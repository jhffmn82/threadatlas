Salvius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Salvius, a fortune-teller chosen king by rebellious fugitive slaves, was also called Tryphon. As rebel king he organized and enlarged their army through raids, divided it into three bodies, besieged Morgantium, defeated a Roman army nearby, and proclaimed liberty to its slaves.

Salvius, a fortune-teller, was chosen king by the rebellious slaves and later took the name Tryphon. He organized the army into three divisions, expanded its cavalry and infantry through raids, defeated a Roman army near Morgantium, and proclaimed liberty to the slaves there.
