Abstract written by a model

Leosthenes was an Athenian and brave-spirited general. After organizing and paying mercenaries at Tenarus, securing arms, and seeking Aetolian support, he led a Greek confederacy, defeated the Boeotians, and besieged Antipater in Lamia. He died three days after a stone struck his head during a Macedonian sally.

CHAP. XI.

Leosthenes, an Athenian described as brave-spirited, was made general of the forces assembled at Tenarus. He convened a council of war, distributed fifty talents to pay the mercenaries, secured arms, and sent ambassadors to the Aetolians seeking their support.
