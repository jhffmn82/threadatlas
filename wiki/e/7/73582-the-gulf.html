the gulf in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A deep, narrow gulf surrounds the Merry-Go-Round Mountains, seemingly bottomless and too wide to jump, but travelers cross it by swinging from a tree limb on a strap.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The gulf is deep and narrow, surrounds the edges of the mountains, and appears to have no visible bottom. It is too wide to jump across, but the travelers cross it by swinging from a tree limb on the strap.
