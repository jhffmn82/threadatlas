the bloodstains in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Bloodstains from the murder of Eduardo Lucas soaked through a drugget onto white wood flooring; their misalignment puzzled Inspector Lestrade until Holmes explained the drugget had been turned around.
