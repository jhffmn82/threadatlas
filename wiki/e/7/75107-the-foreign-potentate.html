THE ADVENTURE OF THE SECOND STAIN

The foreign potentate wrote the missing letter hurriedly and entirely on his own responsibility after being ruffled by recent Colonial developments in Britain. The text says that the potentate's Ministers were unaware of it and that a cipher telegram has been sent to him about its disappearance.
