Prick-ear in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Prick-ear, the woodmouse's eldest son and bridegroom, begins resenting the raccoon for eating his checkerberries and chasing him, then greets and presents him as a friend at his cave wedding.

" CHAPTER VI.

The woodmouse's eldest son, Prick-ear, marries at the cave wedding. He initially regards the raccoon with resentment because the raccoon once ate his checkerberries and chased him, but he later greets him and presents him as a friend.
