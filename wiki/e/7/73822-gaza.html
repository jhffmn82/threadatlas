Gaza in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Gaza is a Persian garrison and a city in Judea. Alexander besieges it for two months and takes it by assault, while Greek ambassadors arrive. Later Ptolemy and Demetrius battle there, and Ptolemy wastes it before returning to Egypt; it also compares another captured fortified city.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

Gaza was a Persian garrison that Alexander besieged for two months and then took by assault. The Greek senate sent ambassadors to congratulate Alexander while he was at the siege.

CHAP. VI.

Gaza is the site of the battle between Ptolemy and Demetrius. Ptolemy later wastes Gaza before returning to Egypt.

SUP. III.

Gaza is used as a comparison for the fortifications of another city that Alexander captured. The prisoners of that city were treated in the same manner as those at Gaza.
