Heidegger's bicycle in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Heidegger’s bicycle, kept in a shed beside the school lawn, has Palmer tires that leave longitudinal stripes. Its tracks cross the morass and lead Holmes to the bloodstained bicycle, with one pedal bent, near Heidegger’s body. Holmes considers whether it was used as a false clue.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

The missing bicycle belonged to Heidegger and was kept in a shed beside the school lawn. Sherlock Holmes questions whether it was used as a false clue, noting that if Heidegger intended to suggest that two people had ridden away, he would more likely have hidden or taken two bicycles.

Heidegger’s bicycle had Palmer tires that left longitudinal stripes in the ground. Holmes follows its tracks across the morass to a bloodstained bicycle with one pedal bent, lying near Heidegger’s body.
