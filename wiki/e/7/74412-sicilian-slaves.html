Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXIV.

The Sicilian slaves were bought in large numbers, branded, used for shepherding and other labor, and harshly beaten while being denied necessities. They rebelled under Eunus and Cleon, killed many masters and other inhabitants, captured cities, and fought Roman forces before being suppressed.
