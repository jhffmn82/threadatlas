Williamson

Abstract written by a model

Williamson is an elderly, white-bearded tenant of Charlington Hall, where he lives alone with servants. He is an unfrocked former clergyman with a reportedly dark career. Partnered with Jack Woodley in a criminal scheme, he conducts the planned forced marriage of Violet Smith and Woodley without valid authority, carrying a revolver and threatening Carruthers before Holmes disarms and detains him. He is later tried for abduction and assault and receives seven years.

Williamson is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

We had ascertained from the lady that she went down upon the

Williamson is identified as the tenant of Charlington Hall and is described by the country landlord as a white-bearded elderly man who lives there with a small staff of servants. There is a rumor that he is or was a clergyman, and a clerical agency confirms that a man of that name had been in orders and had a singularly dark career.

The Thursday brought us another letter from our client.

Williamson, an unfrocked former clergyman, performs the purported wedding between Violet Smith and Jack Woodley. He carries a revolver and threatens to shoot Carruthers, but Holmes disarms and detains him. Holmes states that Williamson’s right to solemnize the marriage is questionable and that the forced marriage constitutes a serious felony.

Holmes rose and tossed the end of his cigarette into the grate.

Williamson is tried for abduction and assault and receives a sentence of seven years.
