conquest and punishment of Ugu in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The conquest and punishment of Ugu is the travelers’ planned response to his misdeeds. After rescuing Ozma, Wizard of Oz’s followers conclude force is necessary and seek a method at Ugu’s castle, but Ugu traps them before the conquest occurs.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 18

The travelers conclude that Ugu must be conquered by force because he is unlikely to listen to reason or apologize. They have not discovered a specific method and hope that examining his castle will guide them.

CHAPTER 22

The Wizard declares that the group will conquer and punish Ugu for his misdeeds. Ugu instead traps them and leaves the hall, so his conquest and punishment have not occurred in this unit.
