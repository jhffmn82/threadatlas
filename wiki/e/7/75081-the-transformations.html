the transformations in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

These are magical transformations, a kind of enchantment that changes a target’s form. They require a Magic Word or secret word spoken properly; a transformed human form that cannot speak it cannot change back, so becoming a tree would be permanent. Kiki Aru performs the transformations one at a time and can change himself and others into birds, beasts, dragons, fishes, and other beings that can talk in Oz, without the tools, passes, and many mystic words Ruggedo once needed. He first changes himself and Ruggedo into eagles, then into Li-Mon-Eags while keeping the word secret, and proves the power by turning Loo the Unicorn into a Gillikin man and back. During the proposed conquest, Kiki changes the Wizard, Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, Gugu, and Ruggedo into different forms, leaving them confused and seeking the black bag for reversal. Finally, the Magic Word restores the Wizard, Dorothy, and the others; it also changes Ruggedo, monkeys, and Kiki into further forms.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Two Bad Ones

Kiki understands how to transform himself and others into birds, beasts, dragons, fishes, and other beings that can talk in Oz. A magic word is required, and a transformation into a tree would be permanent because a tree cannot speak the word to change back.

4. Conspirators

Kiki's secret method can transform people without the tools, passes, and many mystic words Ruggedo once needed. Kiki transforms Ruggedo and himself into eagles, while he plans to keep Ruggedo as a marble statue and Ruggedo plans to turn Kiki into burning faggots.

7. The Forest of Gugu

Kiki Aru uses a secret magic word to transform himself and Ruggedo into creatures with lion heads, monkey bodies, eagle wings, and wild-ass tails ending in golden knobs. He performs the transformation while separated from Ruggedo so that Ruggedo cannot learn the secret word.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Kiki Aru proves the claimed magical power by transforming Loo the Unicorn into a fat, chubby man dressed in Gillikin costume. He later changes Loo back into a Unicorn, while Ruggedo promises that the people of Oz and the beasts will be transformed after the proposed conquest.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

Transformation changes the Wizard into a fox, Dorothy into a lamb, the Cowardly Lion into a Munchkin boy, Ruggedo into a goose, the Hungry Tiger into a rabbit, and Gugu into a fat Gillikin woman. The transformed figures remain in the clearing, confused and annoyed by their new shapes.

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The transformations change the Wizard into a Fox, Dorothy into a Lamb, the Cowardly Lion into a Munchkin boy, the Hungry Tiger into a Rabbit, Gugu the King into a fat Gillikin woman, and Ruggedo into a Goose. The transformed group seeks the black bag so the Wizard can reverse them.

14. The Wizard Learns the Magic Word

The transformations change Ruggedo into a Li-Mon-Eag, monkeys into fifty-foot giants, Kiki Aru into a hickory-nut, and Ruggedo into a walnut. The Wizard also uses the Magic Word to restore himself, Dorothy, and the others to their natural shapes.
