Alexander's remorse

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: he repented

Abstract written by a model

Alexander immediately regretted killing Clitus and reportedly tried to take his own life by setting a spear against a wall. He lay on his bed calling Clitus's name, declared himself the murderer of his friends, and refused food or drink for three days while neglecting himself.
