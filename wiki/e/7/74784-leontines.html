Leontines in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Leontines is a Sicilian colony of Chalcidican and original Athenian descent. Driven out and oppressed by the Syracusans, it appealed to Athens, whose league Gorgias helped secure. During the Athenian expedition, however, Leontines made peace with Syracuse, and the Athenian fleet returned home.

Diotinus executing the place of chief magistrate at Athens, the

The Leontines appealed to Athens for aid against the Syracusans, and Gorgias persuaded the Athenians to form a league with them. During the Athenian campaign, they made peace with Syracuse, after which the Athenian fleet returned home.

CHAR VIIL

The Leontines, driven out by the Syracusans, sought an alliance with Athens on the basis of their claimed descent from the Athenians. They sent ambassadors requesting help for oppressed cities and promising to support Athenian interests in Sicily.
