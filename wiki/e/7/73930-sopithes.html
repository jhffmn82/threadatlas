Sopithes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Sopithes is a king, notably tall, who rules a kingdom and law-governed cities valuing beauty, health, and strength. He submits to Alexander, regains his kingdom, entertains the army, and gives 150 powerful tiger-dog hybrids.

Sopithes ruled cities governed by laws that emphasized physical beauty, health, and strength. He surrendered himself and his kingdom to Alexander, received the kingdom back, entertained Alexander’s army, and presented Alexander with one hundred and fifty exceptionally large and strong dogs.
