Ly-Chee's sentence of death in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Ly-Chee is sentenced by the Emperor to be beheaded at sunrise after soaking the Emperor's clothing and shoes; only the Golden Dragon leaving its altar to beg can revoke the sentence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" And without further preface, she began as follows:-- CHAPTER II.

Ly-Chee is sentenced to have his head removed at sunrise because he has soaked the Emperor's clothing and shoes. The sentence can be revoked only if the Golden Dragon comes down from its altar and begs for his life.
