Abstract written by a model

The Little Pink Bear is a tiny pink toy bear, stuffed with curled hair, animated when the Bear King turns its crank and able to walk slowly and awkwardly when wound on its other side. It is a mechanical, crank-operated magical oracle that speaks true answers about what has already happened, but cannot tell future events. Its guidance is said to be correct in difficulties, and it never makes mistakes. It identifies Ugu the Shoemaker as the thief of Cayke’s dishpan and confirms that Ugu stole Ozma and shut her in a dark place. During the search for Ozma, its answers seem contradictory: it says she is in a hole the group has found, then not there, but among the group. It finally says Ozma is in Button-Bright’s left-hand jacket pocket, inside a golden peach pit; this proves true when Ozma emerges. Ozma gives the bear a chain of emeralds for its wise answers, and it goes with the Bear King to the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

The Little Pink Bear is a tiny toy bear that speaks when the Bear King turns its crank. It can truthfully answer questions about what has happened, revealing that Ugu the Shoemaker has Cayke’s dishpan, but it cannot tell what will happen in the future.

CHAPTER 17

The Little Pink Bear answers the Lavender Bear King's question with a warning that it may be safe for the bears but perhaps not for others. Its magic is described as a correct guide in difficulties, and it previously identified Ugu as the thief of the dishpan.

CHAPTER 18

The Little Pink Bear confirms that Ugu stole Ozma and shut her up in a dark place. The Lavender Bear explains that it can reveal what has already happened, but not what is going to happen.

CHAPTER 20

The little Pink Bear first says that Ozma is in a hole in the ground, then confirms that the hole is the one the group has found. After Dorothy asks whether Ozma is really there, it says no and then declares that she is among the group, leaving everyone puzzled.

CHAPTER 24

The little Pink Bear says that Ozma is in the room, specifically in Button-Bright's left-hand jacket pocket. Its answer proves correct when Ozma emerges from the golden peach pit found in that pocket.

CHAPTER 25

The little Pink Bear had told Dorothy that Ozma was inside the peach pit, though Dorothy did not believe him. Ozma gives him a chain of emeralds in gratitude for his wise answers, and he accompanies the Bear King to the Emerald City.
