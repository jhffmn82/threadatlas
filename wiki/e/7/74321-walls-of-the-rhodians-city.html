Abstract written by a model

The Rhodians built and repaired defensive walls, including an inner half-moon wall and trenches, using theatre stones. Demetrius damaged towers and breached spaces, but defenders prevented his forces from securing the city.

CFIAP. V.

The Rhodians built an inner wall, a third wall shaped like a half moon, and trenches to protect weakened sections. Demetrius’s engines and rams damaged the walls, brought down a tower, and broke through two spaces between towers, but the defenders prevented his forces from securing the city.
