Abstract

RARG retrieves and reranks evidence documents using a database comprising over 1 million academic articles. The documents are used to support the generated responses.

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

They form the evidence collection used by the framework because the study focuses on COVID-19 and restricts evidence to articles from reliable sources. Titles and abstracts are extracted after invalid and repeated items are filtered.

opening

Evidence is retrieved in response to the input claim and provided to the generation model. Evidence-response relevance contributes to the estimated reward.

Experiments / Experiment Design .. Response Generation Results

Evidence documents are collected from scientific articles and supplied to the response generator using the top-three or top-five retrieved documents. They support evidence-based counter-responses.
