Abstract written by a model

Evidence documents are scientific articles retrieved and reranked from CORD and LitCovid for response generation. After invalid and duplicate items are filtered, their titles and abstracts form an evidence collection focused on COVID-19. In response to an input claim, top-three or top-five documents are supplied to a generation model to support evidence-based counter-responses, with evidence-response relevance contributing to estimated reward.
