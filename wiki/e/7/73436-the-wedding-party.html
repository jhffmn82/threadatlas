Abstract written by a model

The wedding party gathers woodmice, field-mice, kangaroo-mice, moles, squirrels, Titmice, Mrs. Shrew and her daughters, and other small creatures. Toto and the raccoon briefly frighten the guests; afterward, they resume greeting, dancing, eating, and drinking.

The wedding party includes woodmice, field-mice, kangaroo-mice, moles, squirrels, Mr. and Mrs. Titmouse, Mrs. Shrew and her daughters, and many other small creatures. The guests are briefly paralyzed with fear when Toto and the raccoon enter, then resume greeting, dancing, eating, and drinking.
