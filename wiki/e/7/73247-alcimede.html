Alcimede

Also called: wretched Alcimede

Abstract written by a model

Alcimede, daughter of Phylacus and wife of Aeson, is an Achaean woman and Jason’s mother. Once admired, she grieves intensely as her only son departs under Pelias’s command, lamenting Phrixus’s flight.

Alcimede is in one work on this site, The Argonautica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Alcimede grieves intensely at Jason’s departure and laments that Pelias’s command has brought her sorrow. She recalls Jason as her only son and mourns that Phrixus’s flight has become a source of woe.
