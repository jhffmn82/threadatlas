CHAP. IX.

Aornos was a strongly fortified rock where surviving inhabitants took refuge; it was about one hundred furlongs around and sixteen furlongs high, with the Indus running below its southern side. Guided by an old man, Alexander blocked its only passage, raised a mount, and forced the defenders to abandon it during the night.
