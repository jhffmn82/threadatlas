Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Polyphemus, son of Eilatus, is a Lapith hero and former fighter against the Centaurs who hears Hylas’s cry near Pegae, searches in fear of beasts or ambushers, meets Heracles, reports Hylas’s disappearance, and is destined to found Cius among the Mysians. In The Library, Polyphemus, son of Elatus, hears Hylas cry out in Mysia, searches with Hercules after mistaking the danger for robbers, is left behind when the Argo departs, and founds Cius in Mysia and reigns there as king.

Abstract written by a model

Now at the hour when from the field some delver or ploughman goes

Polyphemus hears Hylas’s cry near Pegae and searches for him, fearing that wild beasts or ambushers have taken him. He meets Heracles on the path and tells him that Hylas has disappeared. Glaucus says that Polyphemus is destined to found a city at the mouth of Cius among the Mysians.

Polyphemus in The Library

Abstract written by a model

Polyphemus, son of Elatus, hears Hylas cry out in Mysia and searches with Hercules after mistaking the danger for robbers. Left behind when the Argo departs, he founds Cius in Mysia and reigns there as king.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Πελίον θυγατέρα Αλκηστιν. ἐκείνου 1 δὲ δώσειν

Polyphemus heard Hylas cry out in Mysia and pursued him with a drawn sword, thinking robbers were carrying him away. He encountered Hercules and informed him of what had happened, but the Argo departed while they searched for Hylas. Polyphemus remained in Mysia, founded the city of Cius, and became its king.
