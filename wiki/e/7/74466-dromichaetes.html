FROM THE SAME..

Dromichaetes, king of Thrace, captured Lysimachus but argued successfully against killing him. He treated Lysimachus generously, returned his friends and servants, and used a carefully arranged banquet to persuade him that a Macedonian supper was preferable to a Thracian one.
