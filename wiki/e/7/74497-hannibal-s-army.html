Hannibal's army in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Hannibal's army, comprising countrymen, foreigners, and mercenaries of varied temperaments and languages, obeys Hannibal and remains free of mutinies through his prudent conduct. Despite moving very large forces from place to place, it sustains orderly discipline and sufficient money and provisions.

Hannibal's army included soldiers differing greatly in temperament and language, including foreigners and mercenaries. It experienced no mutinies, remained in orderly discipline, and had sufficient money and provisions despite marching from place to place with very large forces.
