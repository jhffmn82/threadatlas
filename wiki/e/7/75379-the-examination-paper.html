1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

The examination paper consists of three proof slips containing half a chapter of Thucydides. While Hilton Soames was away, someone moved the slips, apparently copied from them, and left evidence including pencil shavings, a broken lead tip, a desk cut, and black clay.
