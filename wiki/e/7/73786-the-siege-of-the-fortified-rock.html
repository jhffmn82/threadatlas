the siege of the fortified rock in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A two-day uninterrupted siege targets the fortified rock inhabited by the Marmareofies, who resist, trusting their valor and the place’s strength.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In the utmost borders of Lycia, the Marmareofies*, who inha*

The siege involved two days of uninterrupted assault. The Marmareofies resisted because they trusted in their valor and in the strength of the place.
