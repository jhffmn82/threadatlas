Idmon in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Idmon, Apollo’s prophet and a seer publicly counted as Abas’s son, reads sacrificial signs. He foretells the heroes’ return to Hellas with the fleece after countless trials, while knowing through augury that he will die on Asia’s mainland.

Now when all things had been made ready by the thralls, all things that

Idmon watches the sacrificial flame and interprets Apollo’s will. He says that the heroes are destined to return with the fleece, but that countless trials await them and he himself will die somewhere on the mainland of Asia.
