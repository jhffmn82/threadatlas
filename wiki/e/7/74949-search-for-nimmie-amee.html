search for Nimmie Amee in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The companions search for Nimmie Amee after finding her cottage abandoned. They go to Ku-Klip’s house for information, but he is absent. Seeking for Nimmie Amee to choose between the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier, they follow Ku-Klip’s information toward Mount Munch.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seventeen

After finding Nimmie Amee's cottage abandoned, the travelers begin looking for information about her by going to Ku-Klip's house. They intend to ask Ku-Klip what became of her, but he is not home when they arrive.

Chapter Eighteen

The Tin Woodman and his companions are searching for Nimmie Amee so that she can choose between the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier as her husband. Ku-Klip's information leads them to believe that they must search for her at Mount Munch.
