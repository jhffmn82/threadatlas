swallowing in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Swallowing is a process that moves food down the throat into the stomach through the passage behind the windpipe, safely when the windpipe’s trap-door shuts; speaking while eating may cause choking.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Swallowing sends food safely into the passage leading to the stomach when the trap-door at the top of the windpipe shuts. Speaking while food is in the mouth may allow food to enter the windpipe and cause choking.
