Trelawney Hope in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Trelawney Hope is the Secretary for European Affairs, a rising statesman who lives at Whitehall Terrace with his wife, Lady Hilda Trelawney Hope. He keeps political matters from her and owns a locked despatch-box. A foreign letter vanishes from that box after he sees it there before dinner, threatening his political career and public consequences. Later, returning home while Holmes is with Lady Hilda, he opens the box, finds the document intact, and reacts with relief and astonishment.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SECOND STAIN

Trelawney Hope, Secretary for European Affairs, reports that a foreign letter disappeared from his locked despatch-box at Whitehall Terrace. He says he saw it in the box while dressing for dinner the previous evening and did not tell his wife or the household staff about it.

My friend has so often astonished me in the course of our

Lady Hilda's husband has kept the political matter and the stolen paper from her, saying nothing because the issue concerns politics. She believes his political career and the public consequences may be affected by the incident.

When we arrived at the residence of the European Secretary it was

Trelawney Hope returns home while Holmes is with Lady Hilda and is told that there are hopes of recovering the missing paper. He opens his despatch-box, finds the political document intact, and expresses relief and astonishment at its recovery.
