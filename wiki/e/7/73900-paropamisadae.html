Paropamisadae in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Paropamisadae is a northern country, mostly open plain with many towns, severe snow and cold, and little green vegetation or wildlife. Its people used tightly built houses and winter stores, cultivated protected vines and fruit-trees, and ultimately surrendered after Alexander captured their towns.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX.

The Paropamisadae lay far to the north and were covered with snow, with severe cold that made travel difficult. Their inhabitants lived in tightly built, spired-roofed houses, stored provisions for the long winter, and surrendered after Alexander captured their towns.
