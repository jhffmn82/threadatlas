Abstract written by a model

Nysaeans, a free people inhabiting Nysa, send Acuphis and thirty leaders to Alexander, who lets them remain self-governing under their own laws.

SUP. XIII.

The Nysaeans sent Acuphis and thirty other leading men to ask Alexander to leave their city free. Alexander granted their request and allowed them to continue governing themselves according to their own laws.
