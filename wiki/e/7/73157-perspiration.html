perspiration in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Perspiration is water drops appearing on the skin through pores from inside the body. Produced continually in winter and summer, it carries water and waste matter out through the skin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Perspiration consists of little drops of water that come through tiny holes in the skin called pores. It carries waste matter and water out of the body and is produced continually in both winter and summer.
