Alexander's Persian war in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander's Persian war, undertaken with Greek aid, begins in preparation rather than battle: Greeks authorize support for Alexander, while Darius, abandoning settlement by letters, gathers, rearms, organizes, and summons forces from Babylon and the upper provinces.

BOOK XVII.

The Persian war was being prepared but had not yet reached its principal battles in this unit. The Greeks authorized aid to Alexander against Persia, while Darius raised forces and appointed commanders in Asia.

As for Darius, being now got to Babylon, he mustered up his

After concluding that the matter could not be settled by letters, Darius prepared for war. He armed soldiers who had lost their weapons, raised others, formed them into regiments, and summoned forces left behind in the upper provinces.
