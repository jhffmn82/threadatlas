Archidamus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Archidamus was a Lacedaemonian and Spartan king who ruled Lacedaemon for three-and-twenty years, commanded its land and sea forces, and fathered Agis. He aided Lyctians and Tarentines, defeated Phalaecus in Crete, and died in battle; praised for resolve, he was criticized for helping seize Delphi.

Archidamus, king of Sparta, led land and naval forces to assist the Lyctians and defeated Phalaecus and his mercenaries in Crete. He then assisted the Tarentines in Italy and was killed in battle, though he was praised as a capable and resolute commander and criticized for joining the Phocians in seizing Delphi.
