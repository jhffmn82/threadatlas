the Lace Apron in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The Lace Apron is a magical lace apron that opens the castle’s doors and windows when worn and commanded, and opens the earth when told “Open!” Worn by Mrs. Yoop, it is left on her bedpost and stolen by Woot, who uses it to free prisoners. While fleeing the Jaguar, it trips him and opens the earth beneath him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The Magic Apron is a lace apron worn by Mrs. Yoop, and it opens the enchanted castle's doors and windows when its wearer commands them to move. Mrs. Yoop leaves it on her bedpost, allowing Woot to steal it. Woot uses it to open the bedroom door and the outer hall door so the prisoners can escape.

Chapter Eight

The Lace Apron, which Woot is still wearing, catches around his feet and causes him to fall while fleeing the Jaguar. Its magic responds to Woot's command "Open!" by opening the earth beneath him.
