I. INTRODUCTION

Mixture of Experts dynamically selects specialized sub-models, or experts, to handle different aspects of a task. Its integration with Retrieval Augmented Generation is proposed to improve information processing, retrieval, and reasoning in Large Language Models.

II. RELATED WORK / A. Limitations of LLMs in Information Retrieval and Reason- .. C. RAG

Mixture of Experts distributes computational tasks among specialized sub-models and has been studied as a way to improve task-specific performance, adaptability, and efficiency. Research examines dynamic routing, scalability, domain-specific expert configurations, training stability, expert diversity, and incremental model updating without comprehensive retraining.

III. METHODOLOGY / A. Model Architecture .. D. Dataset and Evaluation Metrics

The Mixture of Experts framework uses multiple specialized expert models and a gating network to dynamically route queries according to their features. Expert outputs are weighted by the gating network and aggregated into a final response, improving task specialization and computational efficiency.

IV. RESULTS / A. Implementation .. C. Qualitative Analysis

MoE was integrated into the Mistral model to support expert specialization and expert selection. Its integration required adjustments to expert selection thresholds and calibration of the gating mechanism.

V. DISCUSSION / A. Enhancements in Model Performance .. B. Implications for AI Application Development

MoE contributes specialized expertise to the hybrid architecture and helps address the trade-off between model generality and specificity. Its integration improves the model's handling of complex queries but adds computational demands.

VI. CONCLUSION

MoE contributes specialized expertise to the integrated model alongside RAG and extensive knowledge bases. The study identifies MoE as an advanced methodology with potential to support more intelligent, adaptable, and efficient AI systems.
