the unnamed avenging woman in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A tall, slim, dark avenger whose life was ruined by Charles Milverton and whose husband died because of him. Visiting Milverton at night with a revolver, she confronts him, kills him in retaliation, confirms his death, and leaves.

The unnamed avenging woman visits Charles Milverton at night, reveals that he ruined her life and caused her husband’s death, and shoots him with a revolver. She leaves immediately after confirming that he is dead.
