Abstract written by a model

Greeks in the upper provinces, a revolting population, endured remote settlement under Alexander. After his death they sought to return to Greece, chose Python, raised over twenty thousand infantry and three thousand cavalry, were defeated, and ultimately killed by Macedonians.

BOOK XVIII.

The Greeks in the upper provinces had endured being sent to the remote parts of Alexander's kingdom while he lived. After his death, they sought to return home, chose Python as their captain, assembled more than twenty thousand infantry and three thousand cavalry, and were ultimately killed by the Macedonians after their defeat.
