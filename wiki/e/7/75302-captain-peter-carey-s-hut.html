Captain Peter Carey's hut in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Captain Peter Carey's hut is a small wooden, shingle-roofed dwelling near the road, enclosed by bushes on three sides. It preserves logbooks, becomes the scene of Captain Peter Carey's murder, and is twice approached or entered at night, ending with John Hopley Neligan caught searching it.

Alighting at the small wayside station, we drove for some miles

Captain Peter Carey's hut is a small wooden dwelling near the road, surrounded on three sides by bushes, and the scene of his murder. Someone tries to force its door and window, and John Hopley Neligan is later caught inside searching it.
