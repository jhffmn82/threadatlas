A secret diplomatic communication from a foreign potentate, addressed to Trelawney Hope and stored in a locked despatch-box. Containing provocative phrases about recent Colonial developments, it disappears by morning; publication could provoke public ferment, European complications, and war.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SECOND STAIN

The letter is from a foreign potentate and contains provocative phrases concerning recent Colonial developments by Britain. Its publication could cause a dangerous public ferment and possibly lead to a great war; it disappears from Trelawney Hope's despatch-box.
