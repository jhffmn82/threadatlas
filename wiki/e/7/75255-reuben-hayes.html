Abstract written by a model

Reuben Hayes is the landlord of the Fighting Cock, an inn with a game-cock sign near the Chesterfield high road, and a former head coachman at Holdernesse Hall. A tenant hostile to the Duke of Holdernesse after being dismissed, he becomes suspicious when Holmes and Watson seek news of the Duke’s lost son. He first feigns helpfulness, then angrily denounces them as spies and orders them away. Hayes is James Wilder’s accomplice in the kidnapping of Lord Saltire, whom he confines in an upper room of the inn. During the pursuit he kills Heidegger by striking him with a metal-headed stick. Holmes’s information leads to Hayes’s arrest at Chesterfield, and he faces the gallows.

When the nobleman and his secretary had left, my friend flung

Reuben Hayes is the suspicious landlord of the inn near the Chesterfield high road. When Holmes says that he brings news of the Duke’s lost son, Hayes becomes more genial and offers to help, though he claims not to have a bicycle.

When we were left alone in the stone-flagged kitchen, it was

Reuben Hayes confronts Holmes and Watson at the smithy, calls them infernal spies, and orders them to pay their score and leave. Holmes and Watson regard him as suspicious, and Hayes watches them with sullen eyes until they depart.

At eleven o’clock next morning my friend and I were walking up

Reuben Hayes carried out James Wilder’s kidnapping plan, held Lord Saltire in an upper room at the Fighting Cock Inn, and struck Heidegger with a stick during the pursuit, causing his death. He is arrested at Chesterfield on Holmes’s information and faces the gallows.
