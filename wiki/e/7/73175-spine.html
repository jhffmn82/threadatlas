Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The spine is made of twenty-four small bones with elastic cushions of gristle between them. These cushions help absorb jolts, but leaning over work can press them out of shape and cause a person to become round-shouldered or hump-backed.
