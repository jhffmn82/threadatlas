spine in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The spine is made of twenty-four small bones separated by elastic gristle cushions that absorb jolts; leaning over work can press the cushions out of shape and make a person round-shouldered or hump-backed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The spine is made of twenty-four small bones with elastic cushions of gristle between them. These cushions help absorb jolts, but leaning over work can press them out of shape and cause a person to become round-shouldered or hump-backed.
