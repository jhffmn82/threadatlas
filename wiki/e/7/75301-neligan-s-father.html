He sailed by yacht to Norway with securities, vanished, and was later suspected by his son of meeting Captain Peter Carey's ship; the securities exonerated him.

Alighting at the small wayside station, we drove for some miles

Neligan's father was involved in the failure of Dawson and Neligan, which was publicly attributed to his theft and disappearance. He left by yacht for Norway carrying securities, vanished, and was later suspected by his son of having encountered Captain Peter Carey's ship.
