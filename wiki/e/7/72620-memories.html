Abstract written by a model

Memories are key, cleaned information units extracted from raw user data, including smartphone conversations, screenshots, emails, calendars, location histories, travel activities, and other apps. They are organized chronologically in an Editable Memory Graph, where they can be inserted, deleted, replaced, and selectively retrieved as they evolve. Personalized agents select relevant memories, sometimes combine multiple memories through a base language model, and use them to ground question answering and enhance downstream applications. Selecting essential memories and modeling their complex relationships improves performance.

Abstract

Memories are user data generated on personal devices, particularly smartphones. The personalized agents use these memories to enhance downstream applications.

1

Personal memories are information generated across smartphone conversations, screenshots, emails, calendars, location histories, travel activities, and other apps. They are organized chronologically and require insertion, deletion, replacement, and selective retrieval because they continuously evolve.

2

Users’ personal memories are managed through the EMG structure. The study uses retrieval-augmented generation to select relevant memories and supports dynamic edits to them.

3

Memories can be selected as relevant to users’ queries by personalized agents. Multiple memories may be combined through a base language model to generate a response.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.4

A memory is a key piece of information extracted from raw user data and denoted by M. Memories are organized in the Editable Memory Graph and used as labels and retrieval units for answering questions.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.2

Memories are cleaned units derived from the real-world business dataset and are used as the personal information source for retrieval and answer generation. The experiments find that selecting essential memories and modeling their complex relationships improves downstream performance.

6

Users' personal memories are leveraged by personalized agents to enhance three downstream applications.
