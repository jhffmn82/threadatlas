the sign in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

An omen: a trembling dove escapes a mighty hawk and falls into Jason’s lap; the hawk is impaled on the ship’s stern-ornament. Mopsus reads it as urging the Argonauts to seek the maiden with the Cyprian goddess’s help.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thus he spake, and the gods in their goodwill gave them a sign. A

The sign consists of a trembling dove escaping a mighty hawk, falling into Jason’s lap, while the hawk falls impaled on the ship’s stern-ornament. Mopsus interprets it as showing that the Argonauts should seek the maiden with the help of the Cyprian goddess.
