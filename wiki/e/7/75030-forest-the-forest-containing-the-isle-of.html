9. The Isle of the Magic Flower

The forest lies beyond the settled parts of the Munchkin Country and contains few or no houses or paths. It is home to Kalidahs and monkeys, and its leaves change from blue-green to purple when the travelers cross from the Munchkin Country into the Gillikin Country.
