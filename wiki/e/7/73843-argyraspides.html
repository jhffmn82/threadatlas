Argyraspides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Argyraspides were an elite Macedonian veteran infantry battalion, also called silver shields, about three thousand strong and glittering in their arms. Though very old, they were Eumenes’s chief strength because of their experience, courage, and skill. After receiving the kings’ orders, they joined Eumenes in Cilicia and supported his command; under their captain Antigenes, they wintered with him in Babylon and rejected efforts by Ptolemy, Seleucus, Python, Antigonus, and others to turn them against him. In battle they defeated opposing infantry, routed Antigonus’s main infantry, killed more than five thousand men without loss, and withdrew in square after their horse abandoned them.
