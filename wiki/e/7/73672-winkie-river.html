Winkie River

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: river, the Winkie River, west branch of the Winkie River

Abstract written by a model

In The Lost Princess of Oz, the Winkie River has a west branch where the travelers stay with its ferryman, who rows Cayke and the Frogman across it at sunrise. In The Yellow Knight of Oz, the Winkie River borders Samandra on the south and winds through the Great Empire of the East to the outskirts of the Emerald City, along which Tuzzle and the slaves travel in the Royal Sampan.
