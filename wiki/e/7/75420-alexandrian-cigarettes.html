Alexandrian cigarettes in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Professor Coram smokes cigarettes specially prepared by Ionides of Alexandria and claims to consume a thousand in about a fortnight. Sherlock Holmes smokes several of them while visiting Professor Coram and later says that the cigarettes may help establish whether he is mistaken.
