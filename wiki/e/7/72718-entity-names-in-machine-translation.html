Abstract

Entity names are the focus of the paper because their cultural references can vary significantly across languages. The text reports that current machine translation systems and large language models struggle to translate text containing entity names.

Introduction

Entities remain difficult to translate, particularly movies, books, food, locations, and sometimes people. Their names may require culturally appropriate translations rather than literal word-for-word translations, because social, geographical, historical, and political contexts can affect how they differ across languages.

Related Work

Research on entity names in machine translation has historically focused mainly on transliteration between different scripts. The unit argues that transcreation between languages using the same script, large-scale benchmarking, and methods that avoid synthetic augmentation and frequent retraining remain insufficiently explored.
