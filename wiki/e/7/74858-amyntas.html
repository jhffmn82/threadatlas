Amyntas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Amyntas, king of Macedonia and brother of Andromenes, was defeated by the Illyrians and granted land to the Olynthians. After recovering his kingdom, he sought its return and allied with the Lacedaemonians when refused. Later exiled, he recruited forces in Cyprus and Peloponnese for Darius, defeated Egyptians near Memphis, and was killed there with his men.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. n.

Amyntas, king of Macedon, gave the Olynthians land when he had been defeated by the Illyrians and despaired of recovering his kingdom. After regaining power, he demanded the land back, and when the Olynthians refused, he allied with the Lacedaemonians against them.

CHAP. IV.

Amyntas, an exile from Macedonia, gathered mercenaries and sailed to Cyprus, Peloponnesus, and Egypt in support of Darius. He defeated the Egyptians near Memphis but was later killed with all his soldiers while they were scattered and plundering outside the city.
