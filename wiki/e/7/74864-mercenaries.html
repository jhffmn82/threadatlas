mercenaries

Also called: all the mercenaries, the mercenaries

Abstract written by a model

Mercenaries participated as sacrilegious robbers in seizing the oracle, served under Archidamus and other leaders, then compelled Phalaecus to abandon his voyage; they were defeated, killed, captured, sold, or executed after aiding Elian exiles.

The mercenaries served under the leaders who robbed the oracle, including Onomarchus and Phalaecus. Some compelled Phalaecus to abandon his voyage, others were defeated or killed in Crete and Peloponnesus, and many were captured, sold, or executed after assisting the Elian exiles.
