the murder of Eduardo Lucas in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The murder of Eduardo Lucas is a wilful killing at Lucas's house, 16 Godolphin Street, on the night before the Baker Street meeting, during the same hours as the Whitehall Terrace political incident. Lucas is stabbed instantly through the heart with a curved Indian dagger in the room where he is killed. A coroner's jury calls it murder. Police attribute it to Mme. Henri Fournaye after a jealous struggle, but Holmes regards the timing and Lucas's possible possession of the letter as evidence of a wider connection and continues investigating.
