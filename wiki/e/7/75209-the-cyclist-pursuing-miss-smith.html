Abstract written by a model

A middle-aged, black-bearded man in a dark suit and cloth cap is a cyclist connected with Charlington Hall. He repeatedly pursues Miss Smith near Charlington Heath, keeping his distance and concealing his features. He disappears at a sharp turn, hides among the shrubbery, flees when she rides toward him, adjusts his necktie among the trees, returns to Charlington Hall, and rides down its drive.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

The solitary cyclist's pursuer appears repeatedly behind Violet Smith on the road near Charlington Heath. He rides a bicycle, wears a dark suit and cloth cap, has a short dark beard, keeps his distance, and disappears near a sharp turn without using any visible side road.

The cyclist is a bearded man in a dark suit who hides near Charlington Heath, follows Miss Smith while concealing his features, and flees when she rides toward him. He later returns to Charlington Hall, appears to adjust his necktie among the trees, and rides down the drive toward the Hall.
