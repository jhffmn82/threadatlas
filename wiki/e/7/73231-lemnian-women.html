Lemnian women in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Lemnian women, responsible for the Lemnian massacre after a marriage-bed conflict, killed their men, including husbands and captive maids’ partners. They took up cattle-tending, agriculture, armour, and warfare, feared the Thracians, assembled under Hypsipyle, welcomed and allied with the Argonauts, then lamented their departure.

Narrative, section by section

Here the whole of the men of the people together had been ruthlessly

The Lemnian women killed the men of their people and thereafter tended cattle, wore bronze armour, and worked the fields. They feared the return of the Thracians, welcomed the Minyae, entertained them in their homes, and lamented when the Argonauts departed.

Lemnian women in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

The Lemnian women, cursed with a noisome smell after dishonoring Aphrodite, kill their fathers and husbands after the men sleep with Thracian captives; Hypsipyle saves her father, and they later receive and sleep with the Argonauts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Πελίον θυγατέρα Αλκηστιν. ἐκείνου 1 δὲ δώσειν

The Lemnian women were afflicted by a noisome smell after failing to honor Aphrodite, and their husbands slept with captive women from Thrace. In response, they killed their fathers and husbands, except that Hypsipyle saved her own father. They later received the Argonauts and had intercourse with them.
