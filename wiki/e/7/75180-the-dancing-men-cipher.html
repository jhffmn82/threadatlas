Abstract written by a model

The dancing-men cipher is a systematic secret writing system of childlike figures representing letters, with flags separating words. Designed by Patrick, Mrs. Hilton Cubitt’s father and a Chicago gang leader, it appears to be a child’s scrawl without its key. Holmes studies the messages, gradually determines their meaning, and deciphers communications involving Elsie and Abe Slaney, including requests to meet. He ultimately uses the cipher to communicate with Slaney, though one account ends before explaining the code.
