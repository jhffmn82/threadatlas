Abstract written by a model

Riding Thorpe Manor is Hilton Cubitt’s Norfolk home and manor house. Dancing-men messages repeatedly appear around it at night, including on a window-sill, tool-house door, and sundial. It ends as the scene of the shooting of Hilton Cubitt and Elsie Cubitt, with evidence of a third person outside.
