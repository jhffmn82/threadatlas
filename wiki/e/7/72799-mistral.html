Mistral model in Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning

Xingyu Xiong and Mingliang Zheng · 2024-02-23 · the work’s page · read it where Mistral model first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mistral is a large language model. Its architecture is extended into a hybrid system integrating Mixture of Experts and Retrieval-Augmented Generation: dynamic routing selects experts for incoming queries, while a retrieval layer accesses an extensive document database. These additions are intended to improve adaptability, accuracy, factual relevance, information retrieval, and complex reasoning. The model is extensively tuned for evaluation on the Google BIG-Bench dataset. Before or alongside the reported enhancements, the baseline achieved 0.85 accuracy, 0.82 precision, 0.80 recall, and 0.81 F1. Overall, its development centers on MoE and RAG integration to advance retrieval and reasoning.

Mistral in Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study

Frontiers in Public Health · 2025-01-29 · the work’s page · read it where Mistral first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mistral is a large language model evaluated for personalized physician recommendations. In baseline experiments, it was compared with GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini on a test dataset, while three triage staff members assessed its recommendations on a 5-point Likert scale. It achieved precision of 0.95, recall of 0.94, and an F1-score of 0.94, with a performance and rationality rating of 4.56. Across the findings and discussion, it was identified as an optimal and most suitable implementation choice, alongside SBERT, because it balanced performance with cost effectiveness, affordability, data security, ease of implementation, and ease of migration.
