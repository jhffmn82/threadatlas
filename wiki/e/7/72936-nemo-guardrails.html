SECURING RAG-BASED CHATBOTS (S) .. 7.1

Nemo Guardrails was used to implement enterprise guardrails during the pre-processing of queries and post-processing of responses. It was adopted after the team found that LLMs did not consistently follow guardrails expressed in prompts.

1 section; each heading opens the text

II. LITERATURE REVIEW / A. Threats of Unsafe Prompts for LLMs .. B. Unsafe Prompt Mitigation

NeMo Guardrails is an open-source toolkit that adds programmable guardrails to LLM-integrated applications. Its moderation rail prompts an LLM to classify a user prompt as offensive or not, but this approach introduces extra costs and latency.
