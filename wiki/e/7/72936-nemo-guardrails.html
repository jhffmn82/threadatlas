NeMo Guardrails

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Nemo Guardrails

Abstract written by a model

In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, Nemo Guardrails implements enterprise guardrails by processing queries before and responses after use, adopted when LLMs inconsistently followed prompt-expressed guardrails. In SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation, NeMo Guardrails is an open-source toolkit for LLM-integrated applications that adds programmable guardrails; its moderation rail classifies prompts as offensive or not, trading safety mitigation for extra cost and latency.

NeMo Guardrails is an open-source toolkit that adds programmable guardrails to LLM-integrated applications. Its moderation rail prompts an LLM to classify a user prompt as offensive or not, but this approach introduces extra costs and latency.
