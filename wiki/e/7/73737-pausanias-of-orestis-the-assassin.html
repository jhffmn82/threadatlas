Pausanias of Orestis, a Macedonian and Philip's bodyguard, angered by abuse of another Pausanias, concealed a sword and stabbed Philip at a theatre entrance. Fleeing toward prepared horses, he fell after catching his shoe on a vine branch and was killed by Perdiccas and others.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, XV.

Pausanias of Orestis was one of Philip's bodyguards and was angered by the abuse suffered by another Pausanias. He concealed a Gallic sword, stabbed Philip through the body at the entrance to the theatre, and fled toward horses he had prepared at the city gates. He fell when a vine branch caught his shoe and was killed by Perdiccas and others.
