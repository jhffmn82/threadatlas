Captain Crocker

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Jack Crocker, Mr. Jack Crocker, a captain, the first officer, the prisoner, the sailor

Abstract written by a model

Captain Crocker is a large, strong, active, sunburned sailor and first officer of the Rock of Gibraltar when he meets Mary Fraser, whom he loves devotedly without return for much of their relationship. On a later voyage he is promoted first officer and is due to depart. After witnessing Mary’s husband assault her, Crocker kills him with a poker. He helps Mary and Theresa Wright disguise the death as a burglary by cutting and tying the bell-rope and disposing of silver in a pond. He confesses fully, refuses to flee without Mary, and is acquitted in Holmes’s informal trial.

Captain Crocker is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
