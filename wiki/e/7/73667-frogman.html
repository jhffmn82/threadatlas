the Frogman in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917 · the work’s page · read it where the Frogman first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Frogman is a large, upright, intelligent frog who dresses and behaves like a person. Originating as a frog in a pool in the Winkie Country, he has lived for many years in the Yip Country, where he is its sole frog, adviser, and unofficial leader because the Yips have no king or queen. He lives beside an ordinary pool, wears a handsome coat and shiny purple hat, and carries a gold-headed cane. Proud of his size, culture, education, and supposed wisdom, he long claimed to be the wisest creature in the world, though he was a humbug who pretended to know more than he did. When Cayke the Cookie Cook's diamond-studded gold dishpan is stolen, the Frogman joins and helps lead the search. He first confidently promises to find it, decides that a stranger from below took it, and travels with Cayke through the Winkie Country, leaping across a gulf with her on his back. After bathing in the Truth Pond, however, he must tell the truth and admits that his superior-wisdom claims were a sham: his wisdom is no greater than Cayke's. Though he acknowledges he is a Freak rather than a Person, he remains honest, unafraid, and devoted to helping her. The search brings him through the Winkie River, Bear Center, and toward Ugu's castle, where he joins Cayke, the Bear King, Scraps, Dorothy's party, and others. His strength, courage, and jumping matter most in the final struggle. He leaps over a high steel wall in three powerful leaps and uses the Wizard's pin to make the barrier vanish. After receiving zosozo, he becomes as strong as Ugu the Shoemaker. He helps attempt to reach the stolen magic, then protects Dorothy by leaping onto Ugu's dove form and fighting it despite damage to his clothes. He assists in rescuing Ozma, receives her thanks, and afterward becomes a favorite at the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The Frogman is an unusually large and intelligent frog who serves as the adviser and unofficial leader of the Yips. He pretends to know more than he does while investigating Cayke's loss, eventually deciding that a stranger from below stole the dishpan. He joins Cayke's journey, leaps across a gulf with her on his back, and continues toward the lower world.

CHAPTER 4

The Frogman travels with Cayke the Cookie Cook through the Winkie Country to search for her stolen diamond-studded gold dishpan. He claims to be the wisest creature in the world, considers himself greater than any wizard, and promises that he can find the dishpan if it is anywhere in the world.

CHAPTER 13

The Frogman searches with Cayke the Cookie Cook for the diamond-studded dishpan. After bathing in the Truth Pond, he admits that he is not wise and that his claims of superior wisdom were a sham.

CHAPTER 14

The Frogman accompanies Cayke in searching for the stolen dishpan and proposes traveling from place to place until they find the thief. The ferryman refuses to let him sleep in the house because his presence makes the ferryman miserable, but later rows him across the Winkie River.

CHAPTER 15

The Frogman challenges Corporal Waddle’s claim that he can capture them, but then agrees to follow him to Bear Center with Cayke. He is amused by the bear’s stiff manner of walking and remains unafraid of being taken to the city.

CHAPTER 16

The Frogman acknowledges that he is a Freak rather than a Person and honestly denies being as wise as others believe. He remains committed to helping Cayke, asks to take the Little Pink Bear with them, and accompanies Cayke and the Bear King toward Ugu’s castle.

CHAPTER 17

The Frogman meets Scraps on a hill and learns that her party is also seeking Ugu the Shoemaker. He tells Dorothy's party about the theft of Cayke's dishpan and agrees to unite the two parties.

CHAPTER 21

The Frogman says he can leap over the high steel wall and successfully does so in three powerful leaps. He thrusts the Wizard’s pin into the far side of the wall, causing the barrier to vanish.

CHAPTER 22

The Frogman enters the castle with the others and is expected to fight if necessary after receiving a dose of zosozo from the Wizard. He helps plan the attempt to reach the stolen magic by bracing himself against the dome and chandelier, but the human pyramid collapses.

CHAPTER 23

The Frogman leaps onto Ugu's dove form to protect Dorothy and fights it. The battle tears his pretty clothes, but his thick, tough skin prevents serious injury, and he receives praise for his courage.

CHAPTER 25

The Frogman assists Dorothy and her companions in rescuing Ozma, for which Ozma thanks him earnestly. He later becomes a favorite at the Emerald City.
