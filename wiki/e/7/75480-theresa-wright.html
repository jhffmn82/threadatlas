Abstract written by a model

Theresa Wright is Lady Brackenstall’s lifelong, devoted maid, nurse, and attendant, who came with her from Australia. Stern and suspicious, she hates Sir Eustace, her late employer, for abusing Lady Brackenstall and having once thrown a decanter at her. She reports finding Lady Brackenstall bound and Sir Eustace dead after seeing three men near the lodge gate, supports her mistress’s account, angrily rejects Holmes’s accusation of lying, and assists Captain Crocker in staging a supposed burglary. She was formerly Mary Fraser’s attendant and protector.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

Theresa Wright is Lady Brackenstall’s long-serving maid, who came with her from Australia and is devoted to her. She says that she saw three men near the lodge gate and later found Lady Brackenstall bound and Sir Eustace dead; Inspector Stanley Hopkins notes that Sir Eustace had previously thrown a decanter at her.

Theresa, the stern Australian nurse, describes Sir Eustace as abusive and says that he had mistreated Lady Brackenstall. She corroborates her mistress’s account but reacts angrily when Holmes says that Lady Brackenstall has lied.
