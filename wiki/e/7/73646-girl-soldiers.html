girl-soldiers in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Girl-soldiers are a magical or illusory army of hundreds, dressed in yellow and black satin uniforms, scarlet jackets, gold ornaments, and armed with spears and battle axes. They march from the wicker castle, circle it fiercely, then vanish after the companions pass through them.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 21

Hundreds of fierce-looking girl-soldiers march from the wicker castle carrying spears and battle axes. They form a circle around the castle, but they are revealed to be an optical illusion and disappear after the group passes through them.
