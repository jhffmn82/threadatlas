Abstract written by a model

Girl-soldiers are a magical or illusory army of hundreds, dressed in yellow and black satin uniforms, scarlet jackets, gold ornaments, and armed with spears and battle axes. They march from the wicker castle, circle it fiercely, then vanish after the companions pass through them.
