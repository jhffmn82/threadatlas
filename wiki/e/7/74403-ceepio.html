Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ceepio, a general against Viriathus, rejected earlier peace terms, often defeated Viriathus, drove him to seek peace, arranged his treacherous assassination, and imposed terms on Tantalus and his army.

5. The Lusitanians at first not having an expert general, were

Ceepio became general against Viriathus and rejected the former conditions as having no effect. He often defeated Viriathus, caused him to sue for peace, arranged his treacherous assassination, and then imposed whatever terms he pleased on Tantalus and his army.
