Abstract written by a model

Ruggedo's transformation plan is a revenge scheme against the Oz people who took his kingdom. He plans to turn the beasts of the Forest of Gugu into men and women and Oz people into beasts, then have the transformed beasts march into and conquer Oz, overcome or destroy its leaders, and use the resulting magic to recover his kingdom.

Ruggedo's transformation plan is in one work on this site, The Magic of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

3. Two Bad Ones

Ruggedo wants revenge on the Oz people who took his kingdom from him. His plan is to conquer Oz, destroy or overcome Glinda, Ozma, the Wizard, Dorothy, and the others, then use the resulting magic to recover his own kingdom.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

Ruggedo proposes using magic to transform all the beasts into men and women and all the Oz people into beasts. He says the transformed beasts would conquer the Land of Oz and enjoy its comforts while the transformed Oz people would suffer in the forest.
