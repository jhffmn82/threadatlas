the cellar in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The cellar, beneath a cottage, contains brown mice and is home to Squeak, Scrabble, and their mother. After a frightening kitchen adventure, the young mice return there, while someone seeks to remove the troublesome, thieving mice without killing them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

The cellar is the home of Squeak and Scrabble and their mother. After their frightening kitchen adventure, the two mice rush back to the cellar and resolve never to leave it again.

CHAPTER IV.

The brown mice come out from under the cellar-door and live in the cellar of the cottage. <DW53> says they are troublesome and thieving and wants them moved without killing them.
