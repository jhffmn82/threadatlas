Punjab in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Punjab was the province to which John Inglis was transferred in 1847 and where he served at Sealkote. He later returned during the Indian Mutiny and worked in the region during its settlement, gaining the confidence and affection of Sikhs, Punjabis, and Rajpoots.
