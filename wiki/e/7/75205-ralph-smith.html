Ralph Smith in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Ralph Smith is Violet Smith’s uncle, an illiterate man who lived in Africa for twenty-five years without contacting Violet or her mother. He died in Johannesburg in great poverty, apparently without making a will. After his death, Carruthers and Jack Woodley believed Violet, as his next of kin, would inherit; Ralph supposedly asked them to find and aid his relations.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

Ralph Smith is Violet Smith's uncle, who went to Africa twenty-five years earlier and had not contacted Violet or her mother. After his death in Johannesburg, he supposedly asked Mr. Carruthers and Mr. Woodley to find his relations and ensure that they were not in want.

The Thursday brought us another letter from our client.

Ralph Smith is Violet Smith’s uncle in South Africa. Holmes says that Carruthers and Jack Woodley believed Ralph would die without making a will, leaving Violet as his next of kin and heir. A telegram reports that Ralph Smith has died.
