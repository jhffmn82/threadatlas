theft of the golden fleece

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: capture of the Golden Fleece, evil deeds, plucking down the fleece, seize and carry off the fleece, those deeds

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, the theft of the golden fleece is carried out at night by Jason and Medea, with Medea charming the guardian serpent and Jason removing the fleece from the oak. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the capture of the Golden Fleece is carried out by the Argonauts, who take down the fleece after Medea leads them to the temple and poisons its guarding dragon.
