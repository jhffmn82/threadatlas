comet in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A comet is a heavenly body with a head and shining tail among the constellations. It travels the sky at great speed, though motion may be unseen, disappears, and sometimes returns only after a thousand years.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

A comet is described as a heavenly body with a head and tail that travels through the sky, sometimes disappearing for a thousand years. The comet seen by Toto and his companions spreads its shining tail among the constellations.
