Abstract written by a model

After tribunes report captives' mistreatment, magistrates summon and threaten the Attilii, blaming their mother; they arrange funerals for Bostar and Lucius iEmilius, send Bostar's ashes home, and free imprisoned Carthaginian general Amilcar.

BOOK XXIV.

The magistrates were informed of the captives' mistreatment by the tribunes of the people. They summoned the Attilii, threatened them with severe punishment, attributed much of the cruelty to their mother, arranged for Bostar's body to be burned, and freed Amilcar.
