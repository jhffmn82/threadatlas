Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Predictions warned Satyrus about Musculus and Eumelus about a moving house; inhabitants later judged them fulfilled by Satyrus’s fatal arm wound and Eumelus’s death under a chariot canopy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

It is reported that there were some predictions concerning the

The predictions warned Satyrus to beware of Musculus and Eumelus to beware of a moving house. The inhabitants considered the prophecies fulfilled when Satyrus was killed by a wound in his arm and Eumelus was understood to have died because of a canopy placed on a chariot.
