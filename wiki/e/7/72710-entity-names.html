entity names in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024

XC-Translate is a benchmark using manually curated valid translations by target language and text context. It selects Wikidata entities with English and target-language names that differ substantially from several European-language names or word-for-word English translations, provides translators contextual information, and may later broaden or tailor selection strategies.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4 .. 4.3

Entity names are central to the benchmark and are selected when they have names in English and the target language and differ substantially from names in French, German, Italian, Spanish, or their word-for-word English translations. The translation process gives translators target-language entity names and related Wikidata and Wikipedia information as context.

Limitations

The entities in XC-Translate are selected from Wikidata and are intended to be challenging for machine translation systems. Future selection strategies may use language pairs that do not involve English and may be tuned to individual language pairs.
