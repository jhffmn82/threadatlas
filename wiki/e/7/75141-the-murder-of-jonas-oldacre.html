Abstract written by a model

The apparent murder of Jonas Oldacre begins when blood, signs of a struggle, and McFarlane’s oaken walking-stick are found in Oldacre’s bedroom, with papers apparently rifled and a body thought dragged to the wood-stack. Lestrade concludes that McFarlane killed Oldacre after a secret visit, supported by a will, the stick, and blood, animal remains, and buttons in the wood-pile. Holmes doubts the explanation. Oldacre is ultimately found alive and admits that the supposed death and burning were a practical joke designed to implicate McFarlane falsely.

The murder of Jonas Oldacre is suspected after blood, signs of a struggle, and an oaken walking-stick are found in his bedroom. The reported police theory is that Oldacre was clubbed to death, his papers were rifled, and his body was dragged to the wood-stack.
