battle at Arbela in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The battle of Arbela was fought near Arbela between Alexander's Macedonians and Darius's Persians. Mazaeus's cavalry inflicted severe losses and forced part of the Macedonian army to retreat, but the Macedonians routed Darius. Darius fled; ninety thousand barbarians and five hundred Macedonians were killed, and Alexander buried the dead before entering Arbela.

The battle of Arbela was fought between Alexander's Macedonians and Darius's Persians after both sides assembled their forces near Arbela. Alexander's forces eventually prevailed after Darius fled, although Mazaeus's cavalry inflicted severe losses and forced part of the Macedonian army to retreat.

CHAP. VU.

The Battle of Arbela ended in Darius's defeat and flight. Afterward, Alexander buried the dead before entering Arbela.
