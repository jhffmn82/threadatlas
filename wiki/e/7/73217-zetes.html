Zetes

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Zetes joins Calais in helping Phineus after securing an oath of safety, pursues the Harpies, and reports that Iris prevented their killing. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Zethes begins without feathers, later grows wings like his father’s, and joins Jason and the Minyæ in the search for the Golden Fleece.
