Zetes in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Zetes, a winged son of Boreas, joins Calais in helping Phineus after securing an oath of safety, pursues the Harpies, and reports that Iris prevented their killing.

Zetes, one of the sons of Boreas, promises to help Phineus after receiving his oath that heaven will not punish him and Calais. He and Calais chase the Harpies, and Zetes later reports to the heroes how Iris stopped them from killing the creatures.

Zethes in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Zethes, twin son of Boreas and Orithyïa, begins without feathers, later grows wings like his father’s, reaches youth, and joins Jason and the Minyæ in the search for the Golden Fleece.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Zethes is born as one of the twin sons of Boreas and Orithyïa. He initially lacks feathers, but wings later grow along his sides; after reaching youth, he joins the Minyæ in seeking the Golden Fleece.
