Ku-Klip's workshop in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Ku-Klip's workshop is a tinsmith's place with a lean-to addition for more room, scraps of tin, tools, workbenches, tables, cupboards, and a barrel. It holds the Tin Woodman's former Head and discarded heads and body parts. The Tin Woodman recognizes it as where his tin body was made; later, travelers ask Ku-Klip about Nimmie Amee and learn of Chopfyt's creation.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seventeen

Ku-Klip's workshop contains scraps of tin, hammers, anvils, soldering irons, a charcoal furnace, other tinsmith's tools, workbenches, a long table, cupboards, and a barrel. The Tin Woodman enters it and recognizes it as the place where Ku-Klip made his tin body.

Chapter Eighteen

Ku-Klip's workshop contains cupboards holding discarded heads and a barrel containing various cast-off body parts from the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier. The travelers visit it to ask Ku-Klip about Nimmie Amee and learn about the creation of Chopfyt.
