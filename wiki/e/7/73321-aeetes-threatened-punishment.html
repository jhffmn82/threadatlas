Aeetes’ threatened punishment

Also called: a fitting penalty, calamity, deadly wrath, terrible things

Abstract written by a model

Aeetes threatens to hew down the oak grove, burn the ship and its crew, and punish the strangers if the oxen destroy the man who undertakes the task. He also threatens to prevent anyone from escaping the resulting calamity.
