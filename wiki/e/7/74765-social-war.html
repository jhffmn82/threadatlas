Social War

Abstract written by a model

The Social War was a war in Greece between the Athenians and the Chians, Rhodians, Coians, Byzantines, and other allied Greek states, caused by their defection from Athens. Athenian forces under Chares and Chabrias fought at Chios, where Chabrias was killed. Iphicrates and Timotheus later joined Chares; after three or four years, peace was agreed following Chares's actions against the Persians.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

The Social War was fought by the Athenians for three years after the defection of the Chians, Rhodians, Coians, and Byzantines. Chares and Chabrias led the Athenian forces against Chios, where Chabrias was killed in a naval engagement.

CHAP. V.

The Social War was fought between the Athenians and the Chians, Rhodians, Coians, and Byzantines. It ended after four years when the Athenians and the revolters agreed to terms of peace.

BOOK XIV.

The Social War was fought between the Athenians and other allied states. Iphicrates and Timotheus were joined as admirals with Chares, and the war ended after Chares's actions against the Persians.
