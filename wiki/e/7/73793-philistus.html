Philistus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Philistus, a valiant commander, general, admiral, and historian, transported cavalry to Syracuse and invaded Leontines, was driven out with Syracusan aid, then fought Syracusans at sea for Dionysius and was surrounded.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

Philistus transported cavalry to Syracuse and invaded the Leontines, but the Syracusans helped drive him out. Later, as Dionysius's lord-high-admiral, he fought the Syracusans at sea and was surrounded.
