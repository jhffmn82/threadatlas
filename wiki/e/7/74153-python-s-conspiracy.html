Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, m.

Python’s conspiracy involved winning over soldiers in their winter quarters and causing a defection in the army. After Python’s execution, his friends and other supporters gathered eight hundred horse, fought in Media, and were ultimately killed or captured, with several leaders dying in combat.
