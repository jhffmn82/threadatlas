the escape from Mrs. Yoop's Valley in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The successful escape begins when Woot steals the Magic Apron and carries Polychrome’s bird-cage from Mrs. Yoop’s bedroom. Guided by the Tin Owl, the group opens the hall’s outer door, reaches the night outside the castle, enters an eastern valley, and continues toward a forest seeking to undo transformations.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The escape begins when Woot steals the Magic Apron and carries Polychrome's bird-cage out of Mrs. Yoop's bedroom. With the Tin Owl's guidance, the group reaches the outer door of the hall, opens it with the apron, and enters the fresh night air outside the castle.

Chapter Eight

Polychrome, Woot, the Tin Owl, and the Little Brown Bear escape from Mrs. Yoop's Valley and reach another valley to the east. They then continue toward a forest while considering how to undo their transformations.
