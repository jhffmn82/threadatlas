Abstract written by a model

The investigation, led by Sherlock Holmes with Inspector Martin, begins when Hilton Cubitt brings dancing-men messages to Holmes. Holmes collects and studies the messages, questions Cubitt, seeks information by telegram, and prepares to go to Norfolk as the danger grows. After the dark conclusion, Holmes and Martin examine testimony, bodies, the study, clues, and messages, leading Holmes to Abe Slaney.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE DANCING MEN

The investigation begins when Hilton Cubitt brings the dancing-men mystery to Sherlock Holmes. Holmes gathers copies of the messages, questions Cubitt about strangers and the sender, studies the figures, seeks information by telegram, and prepares to travel to Norfolk because the threat has become urgent.
