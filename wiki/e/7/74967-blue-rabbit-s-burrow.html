Blue Rabbit's burrow in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Blue Rabbit's burrow is a dry tunnel beneath the Wall of Solid Air, opening toward the broad fields and Nimmie Amee's garden. Too small for the travelers at their natural sizes, it becomes their passage after Polychrome makes them small: they enter the enclosed area through it, then later travel through it to emerge outside the circular wall.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The rabbit's burrow extends beneath the Wall of Solid Air and has openings toward the broad fields and Nimmie Amee's garden. After Polychrome makes them small, the travelers use it as a tunnel to enter the area inside the wall.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Polychrome leads the travelers to the Blue Rabbit's burrow and makes them small before they enter. The tunnel is dry, and it carries them to the other end outside the circular Wall of Solid Air.
