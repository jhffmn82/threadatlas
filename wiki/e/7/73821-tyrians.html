Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

The Tyrians refused Alexander entry, relying on their island's strength, abundant supplies, fleet, and connection with Carthage. They resisted the siege with numerous defensive inventions and fought until the city fell, after which thousands were killed, enslaved, or transported.
