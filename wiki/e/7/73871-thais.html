Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VU.

Thais was an Athenian courtesan present at Alexander's feast at Persepolis. She urged Alexander to burn the palace so that Persia's glory would be destroyed by the hands of women, and she threw the first firebrand into it.
