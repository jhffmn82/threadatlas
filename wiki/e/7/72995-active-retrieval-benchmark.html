It unfolds through four balanced, non-overlapping binary-classification subtasks, intent-aware, knowledge-aware, time-aware, and self-aware, with 8,000 samples each, evaluated by accuracy. Introduction

AR-Bench is an Active Retrieval benchmark curated to evaluate retrieval-timing accuracy. Experiments on AR-Bench are reported to show that UAR significantly outperforms existing work.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.7

The Active Retrieval Benchmark evaluates whether methods correctly determine retrieval timing. It contains intent-aware, knowledge-aware, time-aware, and self-aware binary classification tasks, each with 8,000 balanced samples that do not overlap with UAR training data.

6

The Active Retrieval Benchmark, also called AR-Bench, is curated to assess the retrieval timing accuracy of active retrieval methods across different scenarios. UAR significantly outperforms existing methods on AR-Bench.
