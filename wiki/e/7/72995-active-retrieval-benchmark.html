Active Retrieval Benchmark

object · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: AR-Bench, Active Retrieval benchmark (AR-Bench)

Abstract written by a model

AR-Bench, the Active Retrieval Benchmark, is a benchmark curated to assess whether active retrieval methods correctly determine retrieval timing across different scenarios. It unfolds through four balanced, non-overlapping binary-classification subtasks, intent-aware, knowledge-aware, time-aware, and self-aware, with 8,000 samples each, evaluated by accuracy. Reported experiments show that UAR significantly outperforms existing methods.
