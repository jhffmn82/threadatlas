THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

Hilton Soames, a tutor and Greek examiner at the College of St. Luke’s, asks Sherlock Holmes to investigate the tampering with his examination proofs. He explains that Bannister left a key in the door while Soames was away, and that the papers were found separated, with other traces of an intruder. He identifies Gilchrist, Daulat Ras, and Miles McLaren as the three students using the stair.

Holmes made no further allusion to the matter that day, though he

Soames is deeply agitated because the examination is about to begin and he must decide whether to expose the attempted theft. He assists Holmes in questioning Bannister and Gilchrist and is relieved when the matter is explained.
