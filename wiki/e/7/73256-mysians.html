Abstract written by a model

give_pledges_for the pledges and oath concerning the search for Hylas [to Heracles] The Mysians welcome the heroes to their land and provide them with food, sheep, wine, firewood, bedding, and hospitality. After Heracles threatens their land, they choose noble sons as pledges and swear to continue searching for Hylas. They also send youths from Cius as pledges, whom Heracles settles at Trachis.
