Yoop Castle in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Yoop Castle is a huge purple-stone castle at the center of Mrs. Yoop's cup-shaped valley in the Land of Oz. Formerly Mr. Yoop's home, it belongs to Mrs. Yoop and becomes her prison for travelers. It has enormous self-closing doors, few visible windows, bolted entrances, a hall for prisoners, Mrs. Yoop's bedroom, and magical furnishings including a golden ladder, bedstead, and cage holding Polychrome in Canary-Bird form. At dusk, travelers enter and are trapped when the doors close. Polychrome, Woot, the Tin Owl, and the Brown Bear are confined until Woot uses the Magic Apron to escape. Later, Mrs. Yoop again occupies the hall, weaving a lace apron.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Yoop Castle is a huge purple-stone building in a cup-shaped valley, with exceptionally large doors and very few visible windows. The travelers enter it at dusk, where they find Mrs. Yoop eating in a chamber lined with polished gold plates. The doors close by themselves, trapping the travelers inside.

Chapter Six

Mrs. Yoop's castle is a large stone castle in a secluded Valley where she keeps her prisoners. Its doors are secured by powerful bolts, and the prisoners search unsuccessfully for a hidden way to escape.

Chapter Seven

The enchanted castle confines Polychrome, Woot, the Tin Owl, and the Brown Bear in its hall. Its doors and windows cannot be opened without the Magic Apron, but the group escapes after Woot obtains and uses it.

Chapter Fourteen

Yoop Castle is reflected in Ozma's magical platter, showing Mrs. Yoop seated in its big hall and weaving a new lace apron. Mrs. Yoop has returned to occupying the hall after escaping from her room.
