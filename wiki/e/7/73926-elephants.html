elephants in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Elephants, positioned like towers before Porus’s army, initially crushed Macedonians but, struck by darts, panicked, broke formation, and trampled their own soldiers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

Porus's army used more than a hundred elephants, placing them at equal distances in front and protecting them with armed men. The elephants crushed, trampled, seized, and threw Macedonians, but after being struck by many darts they panicked, broke formation, and trampled their own soldiers.
