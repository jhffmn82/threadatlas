THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

The solitary cyclist's pursuit consists of the same cyclist appearing behind Violet Smith on successive Saturdays and Mondays along the isolated road. When Violet slows, stops, and waits around a bend, the cyclist does not appear, leading Holmes to infer that he used a footpath toward Charlington Hall.
