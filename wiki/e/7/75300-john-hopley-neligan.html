Abstract written by a model

John Hopley Neligan is a frail, thin young man seeking to clear his missing father’s name and recover securities for his father’s creditors. On the day Peter Carey is killed, he stays at Brambletye Hotel and secretly enters Carey’s hut to search logbooks for clues. His initialled, blood-stained notebook leads Hopkins to arrest him, but Holmes concludes Neligan entered after the killing and orders his release.

John Hopley Neligan is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Alighting at the small wayside station, we drove for some miles

John Hopley Neligan secretly enters Captain Peter Carey's hut to search the logbooks for information about his father's fate. He claims innocence in Carey's death, but Hopkins finds his initialed notebook with a bloodstain on its cover, and Neligan is taken into custody.

“Well, Watson, what do you think of it?” asked Holmes, as we

Young Neligan was present near the time of Peter Carey’s death and had a notebook concerning securities that Carey may have possessed. Hopkins wrongly concludes that Neligan killed Carey, but Holmes determines that Neligan merely entered the hut after the killing and tells Hopkins to secure his release.
