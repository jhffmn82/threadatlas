Tarentines in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Tarentines, at war with Lucanians and Romans, sought Spartan aid and Cleonymus as general, supplying ships and money for his army before some revolted and deserted him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In Sicily, Agathocles on a sudden, and without any provocation,

The Tarentines, at war with the Lucanians and Romans, asked Sparta for aid and requested Cleonymus as their general. They supplied ships and money, helped him raise a large army, and later some Tarentines revolted and deserted him.
