In Sicily, Agathocles on a sudden, and without any provocation,

The Tarentines, at war with the Lucanians and Romans, asked Sparta for aid and requested Cleonymus as their general. They supplied ships and money, helped him raise a large army, and later some Tarentines revolted and deserted him.
