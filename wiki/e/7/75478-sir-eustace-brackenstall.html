Abstract written by a model

Sir Eustace Brackenstall, a baronet married to Lady Brackenstall, is a confirmed drunkard whose violent episodes abuse her. In the dining room, he is found dead with a severe head injury after allegedly entering with his blackthorn cudgel and being struck by a burglar with his poker.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

Sir Eustace Brackenstall is found dead on the dining-room hearthrug with his head horribly injured. According to Lady Brackenstall, he entered the room carrying his blackthorn cudgel and was struck with the poker by one of the burglars; Inspector Stanley Hopkins also describes him as a confirmed drunkard who could become violent.
