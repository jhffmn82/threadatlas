Abstract written by a model

Artaxias, king of Armenia, rules while remaining covetous; after Antipater dies, he sends an ambassador to Ariarathes, urging the killing of a brother he holds and the division of Sophene.

Artaxias is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
