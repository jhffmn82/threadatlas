the Park Lane Mystery in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The Park Lane Mystery is the unexplained shooting and death of the Honourable Ronald Adair, investigated after Sherlock Holmes returns to London; an inquest finds willful murder by unknown person or persons, with no known motive.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE EMPTY HOUSE

The Park Lane Mystery concerns the unexplained shooting of the Honourable Ronald Adair. Sherlock Holmes returned to London partly because the case appealed to his investigative abilities and appeared to offer personal opportunities.
