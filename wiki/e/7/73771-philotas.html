Philotas

Also called: Phiiotas, Phtlotas, the son of Parmenio

Abstract written by a model

Philotas, son of Parmenio, commanded Macedonian cavalry and Alexander's army; after delaying a plot report, he was condemned, confessed under torture, and executed with conspirators.

Philotas is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

Philotas was informed by Ceballinus about the plot but failed to report it promptly to Alexander. He denied participation but was condemned by the Macedonians, later confessed the whole plot under torture, and was executed with the other conspirators.
