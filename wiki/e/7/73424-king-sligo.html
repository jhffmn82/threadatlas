CHAPTER V.

King Sligo is a rich and kind-hearted king whose treasure has been repeatedly stolen. Following the Useful Coal's instructions, he exposes the High Cellarer by leaving the treasure-chamber key visible and ordering the court to raise its hands, then has the coal set in sapphires in the front of his crown.
