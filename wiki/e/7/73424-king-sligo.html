King Sligo in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

King Sligo is a rich, kind-hearted king ruling his kingdom. After repeated treasure thefts, he follows the Useful Coal's instructions, exposes the High Cellarer, and sets the coal in sapphires on his crown.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

King Sligo is a rich and kind-hearted king whose treasure has been repeatedly stolen. Following the Useful Coal's instructions, he exposes the High Cellarer by leaving the treasure-chamber key visible and ordering the court to raise its hands, then has the coal set in sapphires in the front of his crown.
