Tanais

Also called: river Tanais

Abstract written by a model

Tanais is the improperly used name for the Iaxartes, a river reached by Asiatic Scythians; it is explicitly distinguished from the Tanais flowing into Lake Maeotis.

Tanais is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SUP. III.

The river called Tanais is presented as the river whose banks the Asiatic Scythians reached. A note explains that this was not the Tanais flowing into Lake Maeotis, but another river called the Iaxartes.
