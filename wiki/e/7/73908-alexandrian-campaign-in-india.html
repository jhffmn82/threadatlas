Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX.

In the Indian campaign, Alexander pursued the Massaga mercenaries, took several opposing cities, captured Aornos, crossed the Indus, and received Indian forces and elephants into his protection. The campaign also brought him into alliance with Mophis, whom he restored to his kingdom.
