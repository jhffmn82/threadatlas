Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CflAP. V.

The oracle of Ammon told Alexander that he was the son of the god and would receive what he desired. It stated that Philip’s murderers had been punished and declared Alexander’s past successes evidence of divine birth and future victory.
