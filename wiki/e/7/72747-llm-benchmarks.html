LLM benchmarks

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Benchmarks, benchmark datasets

Abstract written by a model

LLM benchmarks are evaluation tools that provide standardized datasets and protocols for comparing language models across tasks. They enable fair comparisons using accuracy, F1, and BLEU, supplemented by human evaluations when judgments are subjective.

LLM benchmarks is in one work on this site, Achieving Higher Factual Accuracy in Llama LLM with Weighted Distribution of Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
