Mr. Horace Harker

Abstract written by a model

Horace Harker is a journalist for the Central Press Syndicate living at 131 Pitt Street. He owns Napoleon busts, including one bought from Harding Brothers for his room. After hearing a disturbance and yell at night, he finds an unidentified murdered man outside and discovers his mantelpiece bust destroyed. He reports the events to Lestrade, Holmes, and Watson, then writes a sensational account calling it murder by a madman.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SIX NAPOLEONS

Mr. Horace Harker, a journalist living at 131 Pitt Street, owns a plaster bust of Napoleon that was taken from his mantelpiece during the night. He hears a disturbance and a terrible yell, then finds an unidentified murdered man outside his front door and reports the events to Lestrade, Holmes, and Watson.

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Horace Harker bought one of the three Napoleon busts sold by Harding Brothers. His bust was destroyed, and his sensational newspaper account described the incident as a murder by a madman.
