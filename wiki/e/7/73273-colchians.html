Colchians in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Colchians are a people of Colchis, living beyond the Sapeires and Byzeres near Aea and the Golden Fleece, and subject to their king Aeetes. They practice suspending dead men from trees rather than burning or burying them. They support Aeetes against the Minyae: they assemble to consider his response, accompany him to the contest, stand on the Caucasian heights, and cry out when Jason throws a boulder among the Earthborn men. When Medea’s deeds are known, they arm along the river, launch ships, and pursue the heroes to return Medea to Aeetes. Some search through the Cyanean rocks, while others follow Apsyrtus through the river and its mouths, block island routes, and attack the heroes. After Apsyrtus dies and their host is destroyed, survivors scatter because Hera prevents further pursuit. Some settle the Cronian sea, inhabit the Brygean isles of Artemis, or build the Illyrian river; others, after Alcinous refuses their request, settle on an island opposite Drepane.

the Colchians in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

The Colchians are people sent by Aeetes to find the Argo and Medea under threat of Medea’s punishment. They disperse in search: some settle in Corcyra, near the Ceraunian mountains, or on the Apsyrtides Islands; others reach the Phaeacians, demand Medea, then settle there.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

τῶν ταύρων οὐκ ἐδίδου τὸ δέρας Αἰήτης, ἐβούλετο

Aeetes sends many Colchians to search for the Argo and Medea, threatening them with Medea’s punishment if they fail to bring her back. They separate and search in different places; some settle near the Ceraunian mountains, some colonize the Apsyrtides Islands in Illyria, and others reach the Phaeacians. Those who find the Argo at the Phaeacians demand Medea from Alcinous, but afterward the Colchians settle among the Phaeacians.
