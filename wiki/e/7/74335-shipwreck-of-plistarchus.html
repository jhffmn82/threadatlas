Abstract written by a model

is_a shipwreck [caused by a violent tempest] Plistarchus divided his forces into three parts because he lacked enough shipping at Odessus. One part reached Heraclea, another was defeated by enemy ships at the straits of Pontus, and the part carrying Plistarchus was struck by a violent tempest. Most of the ships and men were lost; Plistarchus survived after reaching shore on a plank, though he had lost most of his army.
