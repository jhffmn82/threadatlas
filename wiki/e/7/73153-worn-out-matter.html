worn-out matter in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Waste matter is carried away from the body through skin and other ways, passes through perspiration tubes, and should be washed off to prevent clogged pores.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Waste matter is carried away from the body through the skin, as well as in other ways. It passes through the perspiration tubes and should be washed off the skin because it can clog the pores.
