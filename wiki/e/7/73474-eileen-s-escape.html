Eileen's escape in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Eileen’s escape begins when Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy leaves and she puts Mrs. O'Shaughnessy to sleep with a monotonous story. She runs across fields into a deep forest, meets robbers after encountering the Red Chief of the Rustywhanger, then follows a ferny path toward open country and the Green Man.
