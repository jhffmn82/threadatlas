Alexander's funeral chariot, made for Alexander's body, was exceptionally costly and magnificent, containing a golden coffin beneath a crowned roof, decorated images and achievement tables, gold-covered wheels, and sixty-four adorned mules.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IIL

Alexander's funeral chariot was constructed with exceptional expense, magnificence, and workmanship. It included a gold coffin and cover, a decorated arch, images, tables portraying Alexander's achievements, gold-covered wheels, and sixty-four richly adorned mules.
