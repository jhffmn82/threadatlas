Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Gandarites are reported to live beyond the Ganges under Xandrames, whose king was said to command twenty thousand horse, two hundred thousand foot, two thousand chariots, and four thousand elephants. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Gandarides are described as a group east of the Ganges, renowned for breeding multitudes of monstrous war elephants and left unconquered by any foreign king because their four thousand elephants deterred Alexander.
