Gandarides

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Gandarite, Gandarites, Gandaritie, GandaritsB, nation of the Gandarides
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Gandarides are described as a group east of the Ganges, renowned for breeding multitudes of monstrous war elephants and left unconquered by any foreign king because their four thousand elephants deterred Alexander.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Gandarites are reported to live beyond the Ganges under Xandrames, whose king was said to command twenty thousand horse, two hundred thousand foot, two thousand chariots, and four thousand elephants.

Gandarites in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Gandarites were reported to live beyond the Ganges and to be ruled by Xandrames. Their king was said to command twenty thousand horse, two hundred thousand foot, two thousand chariots, and four thousand elephants.

Gandarides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Gandarides are a group east of the Ganges, renowned for breeding multitudes of monstrous war elephants; their four thousand elephants deterred Alexander, leaving them unconquered by any foreign king.

The Gandarides lie east of the Ganges and are said to breed multitudes of exceptionally large elephants. No foreign king had conquered them, and Alexander abandoned his planned attack after learning that they had four thousand elephants equipped for war.
