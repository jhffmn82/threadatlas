Abstract written by a model

Ugu the Shoemaker’s wicker castle is his residence, stronghold, and place of magical study, built in the hills on the crest of a low mountain. A good-sized, rather pretty building of woven wicker sides, roofs, and domes, it is protected by magic and reputedly so strong that Vig and the people of Herku could not batter it down. It stands in mountains west of Herku, nineteen miles and three furlongs northeast of Bear Center, and near the hill where two parties meet. Ugu lives there alone, keeps Cayke’s dishpan there, and brings Ozma and stolen magical possessions there, imprisoning Ozma while preparing to resist approaching expeditions. Inside, he sits before Ozma’s Magic Picture. Travelers then decide to inspect the castle before confronting him. They find its wicker gates open and enter despite its magical defenses. Once they are inside, the castle shuts and bars its gates and doors. Its passages lead to a domed central hall with Glinda’s Great Book of Records, Ozma’s Magic Picture, stolen magical instruments and supplies, Ugu’s cage, and a rotating-room trap that can turn the room completely upside down.

CHAPTER 12

Ugu’s wickerwork castle stands in the mountains west of Herku and is said to be so strong that Vig and the people of Herku could not batter it down. Ugu lives there by himself.

CHAPTER 16

Ugu’s wickerwork castle is located on a mountain nineteen miles and three furlongs northeast of Bear Center. It is the place where Ugu the Shoemaker keeps Cayke’s dishpan.

CHAPTER 17

The wicker castle is only a few miles from the hill where the two parties meet. Ugu is seen inside it, seated before Ozma's Magic Picture.

CHAPTER 18

The travelers plan to go to Ugu's wicker castle and examine it before deciding how to defeat him. They proceed toward it after failing to find Button-Bright.

CHAPTER 19

Ugu the Shoemaker builds the Wicker Castle in the hills as his home and place of magical study. He brings Ozma and the stolen magical possessions there, imprisons Ozma, and prepares to defend the castle from approaching expeditions.

CHAPTER 20

Ugu's wicker castle stands on top of a low mountain and is described as a good-sized, rather pretty building with woven wicker sides, roofs, and domes. The Wizard believes magic may make it as strong as stone, and the group proceeds toward it to question Ugu.

CHAPTER 21

The wicker castle stands on the crest of a grassy knoll and is surrounded by magical defenses. Its gates stand open when the group reaches it, and they boldly enter its domain.

CHAPTER 22

The wicker castle closes and bars its gates and doors after the Wizard and his followers enter. Its passages lead them to a domed central hall containing stolen magic, Ozma's Magic Picture, and Ugu's cage. The central room can be turned completely upside down by Ugu.
