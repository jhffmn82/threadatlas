Abstract written by a model

Tryphon, formerly Salvius, became king of rebel slaves, strengthened Tricala as his capital, summoned then seized Athenion, led rebels against Lucullus, fled after defeat near Scirthsea, and died.

The care and management of the business for the reducing and

Tryphon was the name taken by Salvius after he became king. He made Tricala his royal seat, summoned Athenion as his general, later suspected and seized him, and led the rebels against Lucullus; after the Roman victory near Scirthsea, Tryphon fled and later died.
