Abstract written by a model

The Olympian Zeus temple, built by Adrian, contained a colossal ivory-and-gold Zeus and many statues and shrines. Its four-stade precinct, full of monuments, included flood traditions, an offering cavity, and statues of Adrian, colonial cities, Isocrates, and Persians.

CHAPTER XIV.

The Olympian Zeus temple was built by Adrian and contained a colossal statue of Olympian Zeus, numerous statues of Adrian and of the empire's colonial cities, and ancient shrines to Zeus, Cronos, Rhea, and Earth. Its precinct included traditions about Deucalion's flood, a cavity into which offerings are cast, statues of Isocrates and Persians, and other monumental structures.
