mind in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Mind, owned by every person and the best strength, is impaired by alcohol, which aches the head, deadens nerves, and hinders thinking.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The mind, or brain strength, is described as the very best kind of strength. Alcohol makes the head ache and deadens the nerves, preventing the brain from thinking well.
