Bebrycians in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Bebrycians are a people inhabiting Bebrycia, ruled by King Amycus and bound to obey his boxing ordinance. They long war against the Mariandyni, seize much of Lycus’s land, and extend to the Hypius meadows. After Amycus dies, they attack Polydeuces and his comrades, who drive them inland as Lycus and the Mariandyni ravage their lands.
