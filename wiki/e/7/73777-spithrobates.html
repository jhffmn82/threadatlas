Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. It.

Spithrobates, the Persian governor of Ionia and son-in-law of Darius, led a force of horse against the Macedonians. He wounded Alexander with a javelin but was killed by Alexander, who pierced him through the head with a lance.
