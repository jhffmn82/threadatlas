Calaïs in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Calaïs, twin son of Boreas and Orithyïa, is born without feathers; wings like his father’s later grow along his sides, and in youth he joins Jason and the Minyæ seeking the Golden Fleece.

opening

Calaïs is born as one of the twin sons of Boreas and Orithyïa. He initially lacks feathers, but wings later grow along his sides; after reaching youth, he joins the Minyæ in seeking the Golden Fleece.
