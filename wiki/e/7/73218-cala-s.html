Calaïs

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Calais

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Calais is a winged son of Boreas who joins Zetes in driving the Harpies from Phineus and pursues them nearly to the Floating Islands before Iris intervenes. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Calaïs is a son of Boreas and Orithyïa whose wings grow later and who joins Jason and the Minyæ in seeking the Golden Fleece.
