conquest of Oz in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The conquest of Oz is Ruggedo's proposed and forest beasts' planned campaign to overthrow the people of Oz. Urging Kiki Aru, Ruggedo plans to enter Oz as birds, transform into powerful beasts, and recruit consenting savage beasts from Gugu Forest. Beasts that join are to be rewarded by transformation into humans, while Oz people are to be turned into beasts. The campaign would use a giant army: Kiki Aru is to transform fifty monkeys into uniformed giants with sharp swords to capture or destroy the people and make them slaves. The plan is interrupted when the Wizard learns the Magic Word.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Two Bad Ones

Ruggedo proposes entering Oz as birds, changing into powerful beasts, and recruiting savage beasts rather than people for an army. He believes this will allow them to conquer Oz without Glinda discovering their actions.

7. The Forest of Gugu

Ruggedo and Kiki Aru intend to conquer the people of Oz by persuading enough beasts in Gugu Forest to join them. They believe the forest contains enough beasts to make this possible.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Ruggedo proposes that the beasts unite, march against the people of Oz, and capture them before they can supposedly set traps. He says conquest would make the beasts masters and the people their slaves.

14. The Wizard Learns the Magic Word

Ruggedo's plan is to have Kiki Aru transform fifty monkeys into giant men with uniforms and sharp swords. The giants are intended to cut the people of Oz into helpless pieces, but the plan is interrupted after the Wizard learns the Magic Word.
