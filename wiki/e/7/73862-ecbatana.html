Ecbatana

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Ecbataiui, Ecbatanty
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Ecbatana is described as a city on a low, even plain where Semiramis built a palace and supplied abundant water by canal from a mere beyond Mount Orontes.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Ecbatana is described as the metropolis of Media, a city two hundred and fifty furlongs in compass where much treasure was stored and where Alexander rested his army and spent time feasting, drinking, and watching stage-plays.
