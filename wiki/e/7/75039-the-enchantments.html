the enchantments in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Enchantments transform the Wizard of Oz into a Fox and Dorothy into a Lamb. Though the Wizard says they are easy to break with known methods and tools, Kiki's taking the black bag leaves the needed magic tools unavailable.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The enchantments causing the transformations are described by the Wizard as easy to break when their method and the necessary tools are known. The group believes the Wizard's magic tools can break them, but the tools are unavailable because Kiki has taken the black bag.
