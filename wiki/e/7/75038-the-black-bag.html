the black bag in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Wizard’s black bag holds the magic tools needed to break transformations. Kiki Aru steals it, hides it hanging from a tree branch, and it is not found during the search. The Glass Cat later finds it, hides it under leaves, guides the Wizard to it, and enables its recovery.

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The Wizard's black bag contains the magic tools needed to break the transformations. Kiki Aru steals it, hides it in a tree, and leaves it hanging from a branch, while the Wizard and his companions search for it without success.

16. The Glass Cat Finds the Black Bag

The Wizard's black bag is found by the Glass Cat hanging from a tree limb. She carries it down, hides it under leaves, and then directs the Wizard to it, enabling him to recover it.

Magic Tools in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Magic Tools inside the black bag can break enchantments when used properly. Kiki cannot identify their uses; when the Wizard regains them with the bag, they restore his confidence to rescue Trot and Cap'n Bill.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The Wizard's magic tools are inside the black bag and can be used to break the enchantments. Kiki examines them but cannot identify their uses, since he is not a wizard or magician.

16. The Glass Cat Finds the Black Bag

The Wizard regains his Magic Tools when he recovers the black bag. Having them again makes him confident that he can rescue Trot and Cap'n Bill.
