grove of Ammon in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The grove of Ammon is a fifty-furlong square inhabited by Ammoni in villages, with fountains, streams, and many fruit-bearing trees. Though surrounded by desert and bordered by Ethiopians, Numidians, and Nasamenes, it has healthful constant-spring air and a central castle of Ammon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CflAP. V.

The grove of Ammon was described as fifty furlongs broad and long, with pleasant fountains, running streams, and many fruit-bearing trees. Its air was said to have the character of constant spring despite the surrounding desert.
