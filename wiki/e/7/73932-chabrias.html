Chabrias in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Chabrias is an Athenian general and commander of mercenary, land, and naval forces. He served Acoris without Athenian consent until recalled, then led Athenian forces in Euboea, defeated Pollis at Naxos, and helped protect Thebes by ordering mercenaries to stand firm against Agesilaus. He rescued Abdera from the Triballians and strengthened it with a garrison, defended Corinth against the Boeotians, and commanded at Chios. The records give differing endings: assassination after Abdera, or death defending his pierced ship at Chios.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Whilst these things were acting in Greece, Acoris^king of Egypt^

Chabrias served Acoris as commander of the Egyptian army until Athens recalled him at Pharnabazus's request. He later commanded Athenian forces in Euboea and the Athenian fleet at Naxos, where he defeated Pollis. At Thebes, he ordered his mercenaries to hold their position against Agesilaus, a maneuver that helped protect the city.

CHAP. IV.

Chabrias arrived with an army when the Abderites were facing destruction, delivered them, and drove the Triballians out of the country. After strengthening Abdera with a strong garrison, he was basely assassinated by an unknown person.

CHAP. vn.

He commanded the Athenian force sent against the Boeotians and gathered troops from Megara, Pellene, and Corinth. At Corinth, he defended the city, drove back the Boeotians, and held the adjoining hills against their attack.

CHAP. 11.

Chabrias commanded the Athenian fleet during the siege of Chios. His ship was pierced by the enemy, and he was wounded and killed while defending it after the other ships withdrew.
