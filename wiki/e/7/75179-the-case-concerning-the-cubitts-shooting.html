THE ADVENTURE OF THE DANCING MEN

The dancing-men mystery concerns unexplained figures that appear around Riding Thorpe Manor and terrify Elsie Patrick. Holmes determines that the figures are systematic rather than childish drawings and treats the matter as a serious and dangerous case.

The case concerns the shooting of Hilton Cubitt and the serious wounding of Mrs. Hilton Cubitt, with Abe Slaney initially suspected of murder. Holmes resolves the central uncertainty by arresting Slaney and obtaining his account, which clears Mrs. Hilton Cubitt of responsibility and identifies Slaney as the surviving gunman.
