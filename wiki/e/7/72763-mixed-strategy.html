mixed strategy

method · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: strategy

Abstract written by a model

The mixed strategy uses retrieved context at high confidence and otherwise bypasses it for the largest model’s parametric knowledge, producing mainly TriviaQA gains that are larger for smaller models when parametric knowledge is effective.
