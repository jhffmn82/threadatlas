5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

The mixed strategy uses retrieved context when its confidence is high and otherwise bypasses the context in favor of the largest model’s parametric knowledge. It produces gains mainly on TriviaQA, with larger benefits for smaller models when parametric knowledge is effective.
