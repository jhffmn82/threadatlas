Abstract written by a model

Blackmail is Charles Augustus Milverton’s practice of buying compromising papers and threatening exposure for large payments. It ruins lives, including an unnamed woman’s, and makes Milverton dangerous. Holmes views recovering the materials as morally justified despite its technical criminality.

THE ADVENTURE OF CHARLES AUGUSTUS MILVERTON

Blackmail is presented as Charles Augustus Milverton’s method of buying compromising letters and threatening exposure to extract large sums of money. Holmes considers taking back materials used for this illegal purpose morally justifiable, though technically criminal.
