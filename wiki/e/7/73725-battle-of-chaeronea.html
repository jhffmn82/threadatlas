Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Battle of Chaeronea is described as a Macedonian victory over the allied Athenians and Boeotians. In Description of Greece, the battle of Chaeronea is described as the beginning of sorrows for the Greeks, leading to enslavement and Macedonian domination.

Abstract written by a model

The Battle of Chaeronea was fought between Philip II of Macedon’s army and the allied Athenians and Boeotians. After fierce fighting, Alexander broke through one wing, Philip routed the opposition, and the Macedonians won, killing over a thousand Athenians and taking two thousand prisoners.

CHAP. XIV.

The Battle of Chaeronea was fought between Philip's army and the allied Athenians and Boeotians. Alexander broke through one wing, Philip routed the opposing forces, and the Macedonians won, killing more than a thousand Athenians and taking two thousand prisoners.

BOOK XIV.

The Battle of Chaeronea was fought between Philip and the Athenians. Philip won, and Lycurgus, the Athenian general, was put to death.
