little woodmice in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Little woodmice, numerous wedding guests and good people, fear Toto and the raccoon, then recover, dance and feast, make checkerberries and nuts for Toto, and send Bruin regretful apologies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER VI.

The woodmice attend the wedding in large numbers, including relatives of the bride and groom. They are initially terrified by the arrival of Toto and the raccoon, but soon recover and join the dancing and feast.
