Also called: the company, the family, the woodmice, woodmice, woodmouse

Abstract written by a model

Little woodmice, numerous wedding guests and good people, fear Toto and the raccoon, then recover, dance and feast, make checkerberries and nuts for Toto, and send Bruin regretful apologies.
