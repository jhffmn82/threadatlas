Abstract written by a model

Kiki Aru is Bini Aru's Hyup son from Mount Munch in the Land of Oz. Cross, unhappy, and eager to leave home, he secretly finds and copies transformation instructions from his father's private room, hides them in a tin box, and turns himself into a bird to escape. Traveling beyond the Deadly Desert through Noland, Ix, and the Land of Ev, he changes into a hawk, white dove, and magpie. Although glad to see the world, he steals a gold piece from an old man and embraces being wicked. After meeting Ruggedo, Kiki reveals that he can perform transformations but guards the secret Magic Word. He joins Ruggedo's conspiracy to conquer Oz, hoping to become King of Oz, while distrusting Ruggedo and privately intending to transform him permanently. Under coercion he acts as Ruggedo's servant, yet repeatedly keeps control of his unique power, turning them into eagles and then Li-Mon-Eags, changing Loo the Unicorn and other creatures, and plotting against the Nome. When the Oz people and the Wizard arrive, Kiki panics, transforms several of them and Gugu, and steals the Wizard's black bag while in Li-Mon-Eag form. He cannot understand its tools. As he uses the Magic Word near a hollow tree to make further transformations, the Wizard overhears it and changes Kiki into a small hickory-nut. Later Kiki is restored to his natural Hyup-boy form and drinks the Water of Oblivion. Forgetting his name, past, magic, and all else, he becomes an innocent, pleasant, harmless boy who lives in the Land of Oz.

Kiki Aru in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919 · the work’s page · read it where Kiki Aru first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

1. Mount Munch

Kiki Aru is Bini Aru's cross and unhappy son, who longs to leave Mount Munch and see the wider world. He secretly enters his father's room, finds and copies the transformation instructions, hides the copy in a tin box, and uses the method to become a large bird before flying toward the country beyond the desert.

2. The Hawk

Kiki Aru crosses the Deadly Desert and travels through Noland, Ix, and the Land of Ev, changing into a hawk, a white dove, and a magpie. He receives hospitality in Noland but steals a gold piece in the Land of Ev because he has no money, and he says he is glad to be wicked after the sparrow condemns him.

3. Two Bad Ones

Kiki Aru meets Ruggedo after arriving at an inn and admits that he can perform transformations. He refuses to reveal his secret despite Ruggedo's offers of jewels and threats, but he considers helping Ruggedo conquer Oz because he would like to become its ruler.

4. Conspirators

Kiki Aru agrees to conspire with Ruggedo to conquer Oz, but he suspects Ruggedo and plans to transform him into a marble statue permanently. He outwits Ruggedo by transforming them into eagles without saying the magic word in Ruggedo's presence.

7. The Forest of Gugu

Kiki Aru flies into Gugu Forest with Ruggedo and agrees to transform them into creatures with lion heads, monkey bodies, eagle wings, and wild-ass tails with golden knobs. He performs the transformation from a distant tree without revealing the secret word, then flies to Ruggedo and joins him on the ground.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Kiki Aru uses a magic word to transform Loo the Unicorn into a man and later changes him back. He agrees to help Ruggedo, while privately planning to transform the deceitful Nome into a tree because he does not trust him.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

Kiki Aru the Hyup stands below the rock, partly hidden and scarcely noticed. He recognizes that Ruggedo is the better speaker and allows Ruggedo to take the lead.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

Kiki Aru becomes alarmed by the arrival of the Oz people and the Wizard of Oz, fearing that their plans will be discovered. He secretly uses his magic word to transform several arrivals and Gugu, then escapes into a tree after the Hungry Tiger attacks him.

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

Kiki Aru, in the form of the Li-Mon-Eag, hides in a tree and opens the Wizard's black bag, but he cannot understand the uses of its tools. He leaves the bag hanging on a branch, then watches the transformed victims from the lower limbs before disappearing into the forest.

14. The Wizard Learns the Magic Word

Kiki Aru transforms Ruggedo back into a Li-Mon-Eag and agrees to turn monkeys into giant soldiers. While Kiki repeatedly speaks the Magic Word from beside a hollow tree, the Wizard overhears it and transforms Kiki into a small hickory-nut.

23. The Fountain of Oblivion

Kiki Aru is restored from the hickory-nut to his natural form as a Hyup boy. He drinks the Water of Oblivion and forgets his name, history, magic, and everything else he knew, becoming an innocent and pleasant boy whom Ozma decides to keep in the Land of Oz.
