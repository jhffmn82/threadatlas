the Blue Rabbit in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Blue Rabbit, a sky-blue friendly rabbit, explains the solid-air wall, offers travelers his burrow passage, follows Woot through its tunnel, and helps them reach Nimmie Amee's garden despite no especial friendship.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The Blue Rabbit explains the nature of the solid-air wall and offers the travelers use of his burrow beneath it. He follows Woot through the tunnel to see how the travelers get along and helps them reach Nimmie Amee's garden.
