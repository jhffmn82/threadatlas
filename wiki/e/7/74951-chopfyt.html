Chopfyt in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Chopfyt is a man of mixed cast-off parts, assembled and glued together by Ku-Klip from parts discarded by the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier or Captain Fyter. He has the Tin Soldier’s former head and the Tin Woodman’s right arm; after first having one arm, he receives a tin replacement. Awkward, very hungry, and a regular grouch, he begins as Ku-Klip’s assistant and leaves to seek adventures eastward in the Munchkin Country. He later claims his parts as his own, becomes Nimmie Amee’s husband, and performs her household and garden work. In the end, he resides at distant Mount Munch, where Nimmie Amee keeps him prisoner.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

Chopfyt is a man made from mixed cast-off parts of the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier. He had one arm until Ku-Klip later made him a tin replacement, was an awkward and very hungry assistant, and eventually left Ku-Klip to seek adventures eastward in the Munchkin Country.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Chopfyt is identified as the man created by old Ku-Klip from parts discarded by the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier. He has the Tin Soldier's head and the Tin Woodman's right arm, but claims that these parts are his personal property. He is Nimmie Amee's husband and performs household and garden tasks for her, although she sometimes has to chide him.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Chopfyt is described as a misfit man made from castoff parts of Nick Chopper and Captain Fyter. He is kept a prisoner by Nimmie Amee on far-away Mount Munch, and Ozma says no one has just cause to blame Ku-Klip for gluing him together.
