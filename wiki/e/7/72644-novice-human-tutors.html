Novice human tutors teach Grade 6–8 math in one-on-one sessions while managing and circulating among three to five breakout rooms, leaving students when they seem able to work independently. Before tutoring, they complete social-emotional learning lessons through a tutor training platform, yet often prioritize content-specific guidance and neglect social-emotional learning during tutoring.
