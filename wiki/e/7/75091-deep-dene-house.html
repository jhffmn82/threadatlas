Abstract written by a model

Deep Dene House is Jonas Oldacre’s large, brick-built modern villa in its own grounds at the Sydenham end of Deep Dene in Lower Norwood. It is central to Oldacre’s apparent murder: his unused bed, open safe, scattered papers, struggle-marked bedroom, fire, and incriminating evidence are found there. Holmes searches it, compares upper and lower corridors, finds a concealed room behind a lath-and-plaster partition, and Oldacre emerges during a simulated fire.

Deep Dene House is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
