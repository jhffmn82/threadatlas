Before the contest, Jason prepares and makes a nighttime sacrifice; Medea promises charms and gives counsel so that he can survive the bulls and obtain the fleece. Jason completes the yoking, ploughing, sowing, and defeat of the Earthborn men. The outcome enrages Aeetes and enables the heroes to take the golden fleece.

BOOK IV

The contest imposed by Aeetes is described as hateful, and its outcome causes Aeetes fierce anger. Its task is ultimately fulfilled through Medea’s counsels, allowing the heroes to take the golden fleece.
