Italians

group · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Italians, those Italians

Abstract written by a model

The Italians rebelled against Rome after repeated promises of citizenship were not fulfilled. They organized a rival government, divided Italy between their consuls, and called their new city Italy. After suffering repeated defeats and losing allies, they abandoned the city and moved to Aesernia.
