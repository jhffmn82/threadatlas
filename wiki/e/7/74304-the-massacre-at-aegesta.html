Abstract written by a model

At Aegesta, Agathocles, after extorting wealthy inhabitants and learning of a plot against him, massacred the poor, tortured the wealthy for their money, inflicted cruelties on many, and sold young women and children to the Brutii in Italy.

The massacre at Aegesta began after Agathocles extorted much of the wealth of its rich inhabitants and learned that they were plotting against him. He killed the poor, tortured the wealthy to discover their money, and subjected many men and women to elaborate cruelties; young women and children were transported to Italy and sold to the Brutii.
