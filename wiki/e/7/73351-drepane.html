Drepane in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Drepane is a fertile island in the Ceraunian sea, fronting the Ionian gulf, with two harbours and sacred to the Phaeacians; Argonauts and Colchians gather there before Jason and Medea depart on the seventh day.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Fronting the Ionian gulf there lies an island in the Ceraunian sea,

Drepane is an island in the Ceraunian sea, rich in soil and possessing a harbour on both sides. It is called the sacred nurse of the Phaeacians, and the Argonauts and Colchians gather there before Jason and Medea depart on the seventh day.
