the fairy wish

Also called: a fairy wish, the charm, the charm is off, the goose that laid eggs of gold, the invisible cloak, the magic carpet

Abstract written by a model

The fairy wish Eileen chose is a charm that makes diamonds and pearls appear whenever she speaks. The Green Man warns she may want it removed and gives a holly-and-toadstool ritual. Later, he touches her lips with his cap and declares the charm off.
