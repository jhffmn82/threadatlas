the fairy wish in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The fairy wish Eileen chose is a charm that makes diamonds and pearls appear whenever she speaks. The Green Man warns she may want it removed and gives a holly-and-toadstool ritual. Later, he touches her lips with his cap and declares the charm off.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" And settling himself comfortably on his moss-pillows, the hermit began the story of-- CHAPTER XIII.

The fairy wish gives Eileen a diamond and a pearl every time she speaks. The Green Man warns that she may later wish to have the charm removed and tells her to strike the toadstool three times with holly while saying, “Slanegher Banegher, Skeen na Lane!”

The charm compels Eileen's speech to produce diamonds and pearls. The Green Man touches her lips with his cap and declares that the charm is off.
