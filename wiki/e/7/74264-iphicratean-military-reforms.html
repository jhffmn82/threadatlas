Abstract written by a model

changes soldiers' shoes [to make them easier to put on, march in, and remove] Iphicrates replaced heavy shields with moderately sized targets, lengthened the spears and swords, and altered the soldiers' shoes. The lighter equipment produced the class called peltasts, or targeteers, and was praised through experience.
