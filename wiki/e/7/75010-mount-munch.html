Mount Munch

place · 2 works · 10 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: the mountain, the saucer-shaped mountain-top, the top of Mount Munch, this hill

Abstract written by a model

In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Mount Munch is the mountain where Nimmie Amee went to live, and where the travelers seek her. In The Magic of Oz, Mount Munch is a steep hill on the eastern edge of Munchkin Country, with a saucer-shaped top containing fields, streams, trees, houses, and Hyups’ homes.

Mount Munch in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918 · the work’s page · read it where Mount Munch first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mount Munch is a mountain in Munchkin Country where Nimmie Amee went to live with people she knew after leaving the Wicked Witch's forest. The travelers seek her there, first seeing the mountain far away, then continuing toward it through the invisible country and climbing toward it after staying with the Swynes.

Chapter Eighteen

Mount Munch is the place where Nimmie Amee went to live with people she knew after leaving the Wicked Witch's forest. The travelers decide to go there in search of her.

Chapter Nineteen

Mount Munch remains visible in the distance while the travelers pass through the invisible country. The travelers continue toward it after crossing the ditch.

Chapter Twenty

Mount Munch continues to appear larger as the travelers approach it, although they know it is still far away. After spending the night with the Swynes, they climb toward the mountain.

Mount Munch in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919 · the work’s page · read it where Mount Munch first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mount Munch is a big, tall, steep hill on Munchkin Country’s east edge. It touches the Deadly Sandy Desert on one side and Munchkin Country on the other. Its broad, deep saucer-shaped top has fields, streams, trees, houses, and Hyups’ homes; its sides become too steep to climb.

1 section; each heading opens the text

1. Mount Munch

Mount Munch is a tall, steep hill on the east edge of the Land of Oz. Its broad, saucer-shaped summit contains fields, streams, trees, houses, and the homes of the Hyups, while its sides are too steep for the Munchkins or Hyups to easily climb.
