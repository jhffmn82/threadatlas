Timoleon establishes democratic administration, frames laws for it, promotes equality and mutual recompense in private contracts, and creates the yearly office of Amphipolus of Jupiter Olympus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XI.

Timoleon framed laws suited to democratic administration and gave special attention to equality and mutual recompense in private contracts. He also established the yearly office of Amphipolus of Jupiter Olympus, with Callimenes as the first holder.
