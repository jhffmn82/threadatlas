Abstract written by a model

Anna, a severely short-sighted Russian revolutionary and reformer, is married to Professor Coram and imprisoned in Siberia for a limited term. Seeking documents in Coram’s house, she enters through the garden, crosses the corridor, reaches the bureau, and is interrupted by Willoughby Smith. She accidentally kills the first secretary, hides behind the bookcase, gives Holmes a packet intended to save Alexis, recounts her history with Coram, and dies by poison.

Anna is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
