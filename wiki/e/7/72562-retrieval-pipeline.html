retrieval pipeline

method · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Evidence Retrieval, Retrieval Pipeline, evidence retrieval, retrieval, retrieval module

Abstract written by a model

Retrieval pipeline is a two-stage method for response generation that retrieves scientific evidence for an input misinformation claim, then reranks documents. It consists of BM25 and fden; fden scores claim-evidence pairs and also assesses claim-response and evidence-response relevance. Trained to rank annotated pairs highly, it retrieves a relevant subset from a large collection and improves ranking performance on Check-COVID, including with limited subsets.

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

It retrieves a subset of relevant evidence documents from a large collection for use in response generation. The framework trains a retrieval model to assign high relevance scores to annotated claim-evidence pairs.

opening

The retrieval pipeline retrieves evidence documents for response generation. Its fden component is also used to assess claim-response and evidence-response relevance.

Experiments / Experiment Design .. Response Generation Results

The evidence retrieval module consists of BM25 and fden. In the reported experiments, the two-stage retrieval pipeline improves ranking performance, including when the retrieved subset is limited.
