opening .. 2.4. Evaluation Metrics

Correctness measures whether GPT accurately assesses the tutor's use of social-emotional learning principles in a tutoring transcript. It is scored as -1 when no information is generated, 0 when the assessment is incorrect, and 1 when the assessment is correct.

3. Results

Correctness is used with hallucination to measure the quality of GPT models’ evaluations. The desired result is an evaluation that is both free of hallucination and correct.
