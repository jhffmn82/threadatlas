Answer marginalization aggregates context-specific answer probabilities, first weighting them by normalized similarity scores in traditional RAG and later by relevance-estimator scores in RE-RAG.

Answer marginalization aggregates context-specific answer probabilities into a probability for the answer. Traditional RAG weights these probabilities with normalized similarity scores, while RE-RAG weights them with normalized relevance-estimator scores.
