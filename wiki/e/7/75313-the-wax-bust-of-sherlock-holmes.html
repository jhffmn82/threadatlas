Abstract written by a model

A wax bust, or dummy, of Sherlock Holmes, moulded in Grenoble by Monsieur Oscar Meunier, is a perfect, wax-coloured facsimile dressed in Holmes’s old dressing-gown. Placed in Holmes’s Baker Street rooms, Mrs. Hudson repeatedly moves it so its shadow appears to be Holmes. It deceives watchers and draws Colonel Sebastian Moran’s fire, and is shot through the head instead of Holmes.

The wax bust of Sherlock Holmes is made by Monsieur Oscar Meunier of Grenoble and placed in Holmes’s Baker Street rooms. Mrs. Hudson repeatedly changes its position so that its shadow resembles Holmes, deceiving the watchers and drawing Moran’s fire.

Our old chambers had been left unchanged through the supervision

The wax dummy is an exceptionally accurate model of Sherlock Holmes, dressed in one of Holmes's old dressing-gowns and positioned to look real from the street. It is shot through the head by Colonel Sebastian Moran, allowing Holmes to avoid the bullet.
