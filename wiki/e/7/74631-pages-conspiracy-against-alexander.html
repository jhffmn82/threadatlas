Abstract written by a model

Hermolaus, Sostrates, and other royal pages plot to kill Alexander in his sleep after Hermolaus is humiliated. Alexander stays awake, the attempt fails, and Epimenes’s disclosure leads to the conspirators’ arrest, interrogation, and confession.

SUR IX.

The conspiracy is formed after Hermolaus is humiliated by Alexander, with Sostrates and several other pages joining him. They plan to kill Alexander while he sleeps, but the attempt fails when Alexander remains awake drinking, or possibly because a Syrian prophetess persuades him to return to his companions. Epimenes's disclosure leads to the conspirators' arrest, interrogation, and confession.
