tin box in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A tin box from a landsman Peter Carey took aboard the Sea Unicorn and threw overboard; Cairns found it in Carey’s hut after killing him, took it expecting money, found papers, and it is to be returned to young Neligan.

The tin box belonged to a landsman whom Peter Carey took aboard the Sea Unicorn and later threw overboard. Cairns found the box in Carey’s hut after killing him and took it, but discovered that it contained only papers rather than money; Holmes says it should be returned to young Neligan.
