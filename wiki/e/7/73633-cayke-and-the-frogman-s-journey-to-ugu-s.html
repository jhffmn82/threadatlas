Abstract written by a model

Cayke and the Frogman lead a planned expedition to Ugu the Shoemaker’s wickerwork castle to inspect it, recover Cayke’s dishpan and other stolen property, and find a way to victory. The Bear King joins and carries the Little Pink Bear. After preparing, breaking camp, and unsuccessfully searching an hour for Button-Bright, the travelers form a procession and continue directly toward the castle.

CHAPTER 16

Cayke and the Frogman prepare to travel to Ugu the Shoemaker’s wickerwork castle to recover the dishpan. The Bear King joins them and carries the Little Pink Bear so that it will not slow their journey.

CHAPTER 18

The travelers break camp and set out toward Ugu's wicker castle after agreeing to follow the Frogman's advice. They search unsuccessfully for Button-Bright for an hour before forming a procession and continuing.

CHAPTER 19

The expedition led by Cayke and the Frogman sets out to find Ugu the Shoemaker and recover the stolen property. It is one of two important expeditions traveling directly toward the Wicker Castle.
