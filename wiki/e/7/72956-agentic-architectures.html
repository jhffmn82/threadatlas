ENSURING FRESHNESS OF ENTERPRISE .. 3.1

Agentic architectures handle complex questions through query decomposition and orchestration. The authors found that complex agents and multi-agent architectures are needed because information-retrieval systems and LLMs alone are insufficient for some complex queries.

RELATED WORK

Our work presents a reference architecture for one implementation of agentic architectures used for complex query handling. ReAct is also described as an agent widely used to handle complex queries recursively.
