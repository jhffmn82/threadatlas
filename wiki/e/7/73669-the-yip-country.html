Abstract written by a model

The Yip Country is a wee, isolated country on a broad tableland in the far southwestern Winkie Country, reached only by steep, bramble-covered hills. It has houses, fields, trees, paths, the Frogman's home, and Cayke's jeweled gold Magic Dishpan. Its story begins with the first known theft, unfolds as Ugu steals the dishpan at night, and sends the Frogman and companions searching for it.

CHAPTER 3

The Yip Country occupies a broad, isolated tableland in the far southwestern corner of the Winkie Country. It is surrounded by steep hills covered with bramble bushes and has scattered houses, fields, trees, and paths. The first theft known there is Cayke's missing dishpan.

CHAPTER 17

The Yip Country is the Frogman's home. The Frogman and his companions travel from there in search of Cayke's stolen dishpan.

CHAPTER 19

The Yip Country contains the diamond-studded gold Magic Dishpan. Ugu enters the house of Cayke the Cookie Cook there at night and steals it while the Yips are asleep.
