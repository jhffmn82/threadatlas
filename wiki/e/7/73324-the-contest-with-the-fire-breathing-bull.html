the contest with the fire-breathing bulls in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The contest is a deadly trial involving fire-breathing bulls, ploughing, dragon’s teeth, and Earthborn men. Medea gives Jason magical protection and advice. Jason faces the bulls, sows the teeth, defeats the men who arise, ploughs four plough-gates, and returns toward the ship before the day ends.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Then did night draw darkness over the earth; and on the sea sailors

The contest requires Jason to receive dragon’s teeth, sow them in the earth, and face the fire-breathing bulls. Medea gives him magical protection and tells him how to defeat the earthborn men who will arise from the sown teeth.

Then Aeetes arrayed his breast in the stiff corslet which Ares gave him

Jason’s contest lasts until the third part of the day remains from dawn. It ends after he ploughs four plough-gates, defeats the Earthborn men, and returns toward the ship.
