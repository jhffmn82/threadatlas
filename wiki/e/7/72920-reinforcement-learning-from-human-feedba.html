method · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: RLHF, RLHF cycle, RLHF-based LLM alignment, RLHF-based approach, RLHF-tuned, reinforcement learning, reinforcement learning with human feedback

Abstract written by a model

In Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation, reinforcement learning from human feedback aligns large language models for evidence-based response generation, using human-feedback reward estimates to guide PPO updates after supervised fine-tuning and tune RARG toward human-preferred responses. In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, RLHF is an important feedback-cycle method for refining large language models and RAG-based chatbots over time; feedback is being gathered, but continuous learning pipelines have not yet been built. In HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications, RLHF is proposed as a technique for future LLM fine-tuning.

reinforcement learning from human feedback in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where reinforcement learning from human feedback first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reinforcement learning from human feedback is a method for aligning large language models for evidence-based response generation. After supervised fine-tuning, human-feedback-based reward estimates guide PPO updates to the actor model and tune RARG toward responses of human preference. Its reward design promotes use of retrieved evidence while preserving generated-text quality. In response-generation results, RLHF-tuned RARG improves refutation, factuality, and politeness by an average 2.40% over RARG (S).
