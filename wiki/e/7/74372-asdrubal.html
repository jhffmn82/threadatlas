Abstract written by a model

Asdrubal, Amilcar’s son-in-law and successor as general, married a Spanish king’s daughter, defeated King Orissus, took twelve cities, and brought Spain’s cities under his control. After criticism for avoiding battle, he marched to Panormus, where Cecilius defeated his unfortified, drunken army and captured sixty elephants. He was assassinated after nine years as general.

Asdrubal is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK XXIIL

Asdrubal, the Carthaginian general, marched to Panormus after being criticized by his soldiers for not fighting. He encamped close to the walls without fortifying his position, and Cecilius defeated his army while the soldiers were drunk; sixty elephants were captured.

Asdrubal, Amilcar's son-in-law, defeated the forces of King Orissus, took twelve cities, and brought all the cities of Spain under his control. He was later assassinated by one of his household after serving as general for nine years.
