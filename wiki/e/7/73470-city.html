city in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

After the late king dies heirless, the city prepares and accepts the butterman impostor as king, then lets him leave and cheers the departing princess.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER XI.

The city is preparing to crown a new king after its old king dies without an heir. Its people accept the butterman as king, but later allow him to leave and cheer the princess as she departs.
