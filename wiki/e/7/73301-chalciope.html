Chalciope in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Chalciope, sister of Medea and mother of Medea’s sons, seeks to save her sons. She rejoices and embraces them when they return, withdraws to avoid Aeetes’ anger, then, urged by Argus, begs Medea for secret help for the stranger and tells her sons Medea will help.
