saliva in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Saliva is a juice in the mouth. It moistens and mixes food during chewing, helps prepare it before it enters the stomach, and changes starch into sugar before the food can be taken into the blood. When tobacco juice is spit out, needed saliva is also lost.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Saliva helps prepare food in the mouth before it enters the stomach. Tobacco mixes with saliva, and when the tobacco juice is spit out, saliva needed for preparing food is also lost.

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Saliva moistens and mixes food in the mouth. It changes starch into sugar, which must happen before the food can be taken into the blood.
