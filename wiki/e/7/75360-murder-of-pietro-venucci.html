murder of Pietro Venucci in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The murder of Pietro Venucci was a fatal knife stabbing during Beppo’s search for the busts. Inspector Lestrade first attributed it to a struggle between Pietro and another Italian, possibly Beppo. Holmes later concludes that Pietro, Beppo’s confederate, confronted him at Harker’s over the lost pearl, and Beppo stabbed him in the scuffle.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

The murder is attributed by Inspector Lestrade to a struggle involving Pietro Venucci and another Italian, whom he believes may be Beppo. Holmes captures Beppo but indicates that the full relationship between the murder and the destroyed busts still requires explanation.

When we met again next evening, Lestrade was furnished with much

Pietro’s murder occurred during Beppo’s search for the busts. Holmes says Beppo stabbed Pietro in a scuffle at Harker’s after Pietro, whom Holmes regarded as a confederate, followed him and held him responsible for the pearl’s loss.
