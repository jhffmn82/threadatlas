Adherbal, king of Numidia and Rome’s ally, is Jugurtha’s brother; after defeat he flees to besieged Cirta, appeals unsuccessfully to Rome, and is killed with his children after its capture.

After Jugurtha defeated him, Adherbal fled to Cirta and was besieged there. He appealed to Rome for help, but Jugurtha disregarded the Roman embassies, captured the city, and killed him as he was leaving with his children.
