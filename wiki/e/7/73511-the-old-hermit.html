the old hermit in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

An old, forest-dwelling hermit lives lonely in a cavern with a crow and hawk. He arrives at a party bearing both birds and announces melting snow, warmer sun, and approaching spring.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIV.

The old hermit arrives at the party with a crow on one shoulder and a hawk on the other. He tells the company that the snow is melting, the sun is warmer, and the mild weather suggests that spring is near.
