Abstract written by a model

The brain is the body's central telegraph office: it thinks, receives messages from nerves, sends orders to muscles and the rest of the body, and controls breathing and other automatic work. Protected by a strong box of bone, its thinking part needs quiet sleep and good care. It needs much pure, good blood and may remain useful for seventy or eighty years if well cared for; alcohol in the blood harms control.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The brain is the part of the body that thinks and is kept safe in a strong box made of bone. It receives messages from the nerves and sends orders to the muscles; one part also keeps breathing and other automatic work going. The thinking part needs quiet sleep and good care.

CHAPTER XIV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The brain needs a great deal of very pure, good blood to remain strong. Alcohol in the blood goes partly to the brain, affects the nerves, and can make a man lose control over his actions.
