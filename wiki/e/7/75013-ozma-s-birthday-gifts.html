6. Ozma's Birthday Presents

Ozma's friends plan varied birthday presents, including Scraps's song, the Tin Woodman's tin-and-emerald girdle, the Scarecrow's straw slippers, Glinda's emerald-thread gown, and Dorothy and the Wizard's surprise cake. Dorothy is especially concerned with giving Ozma something she does not already possess.

22. Ozma's Birthday Party

Ozma's birthday gifts are placed on a large table in the Banquet Hall. They include the Magic Flower and a marvelous gown woven by Glinda and her maidens from strands drawn from pure emeralds, and Ozma examines them all before thanking the donors.
