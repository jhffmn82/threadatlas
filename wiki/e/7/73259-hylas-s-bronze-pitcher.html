Hylas’s bronze pitcher

object · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: pitcher of bronze, the pitcher, the sounding bronze

Abstract written by a model

Hylas’s bronze pitcher is carried to Pegae so that Hylas can draw water for the evening meal. As he dips it into the spring, the ringing sound of the water against the bronze accompanies the water-nymph’s attack.
