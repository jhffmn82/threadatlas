THE ADVENTURE OF BLACK PETER

Peter Carey's cabin was a locked, single-roomed wooden hut where he slept alone and allowed no one else to enter. It contained a bunk, sea-chest, maps, charts, logbooks, a picture of the Sea Unicorn, and the harpoon-pinned body of Carey.
