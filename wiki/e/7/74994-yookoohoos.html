Abstract written by a model

In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Yookoohoos are described as the world’s cleverest magic-workers, surpassing Witches and possessing transformation magic for strange enchantments. In Glinda of Oz, Yookoohoo is described as a powerful magical creature capable of changing forms and transforming other creatures.
