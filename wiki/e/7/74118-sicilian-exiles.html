The Syracusan exiles living in Agrigentum opposed Agathocles and urged war before he grew stronger. They persuaded the Agrigentines to seek a Lacedaemonian general, then gathered under Dinocrates through love of liberty or fear of Agathocles, forming a large, exile-hardened army that challenged him in Sicily.
