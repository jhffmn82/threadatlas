Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

The Indian army under Porus had more than fifty thousand foot soldiers, three thousand horse, over a thousand chariots, and 130 elephants. It lost more than twelve thousand men, including important commanders, and more than nine thousand soldiers were captured.
