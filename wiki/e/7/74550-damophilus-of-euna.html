Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXIV.

Damophilus was a wealthy, proud, and arrogant landowner of Euna who possessed extensive land and cattle and imitated the luxury and cruelty of Italian slaveholders. He armed and displayed slaves, held lavish entertainments, branded captives, imprisoned some, denied others sufficient clothing and food, and scourged slaves almost every day; his treatment provoked them to revolt.
