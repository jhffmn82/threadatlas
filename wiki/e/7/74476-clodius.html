BOOK XXIV.

Clodius took command of the army besieging Lilybsum and bitterly accused the consuls who had preceded him of mismanaging the war. He rashly attempted to build a mole in the sea to block the harbor, severely punished soldiers, scourged Roman allies, and despised others because of his noble birth and ancestry.
