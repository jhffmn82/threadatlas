Byzantines in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Byzantines are a group that joins Athens in the Social War, maintains peace with Antigonus and Lysimachus, then defects to aid Chians and Rhodians against Athens amid a planned siege.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

The Byzantines defected from Athens, supported the Chians, and assisted them during the siege of Chios.

CHAP. V.

The Byzantines joined the Chians and Rhodians against the Athenians. They were the object of the Athenian generals' planned siege, and their allies prepared to relieve them before the fleets met in the Hellespont.
