Abstract written by a model

The great Munchkin forest is the Tin Woodman's birthplace, where enchantment caused him to lose parts of his meat body and where he rusted while chopping. It contains his cabin, Nimmie Amee's abandoned house, the rusted Tin Soldier, and Captain Fyter, who rusted while traveling to elope with Nimmie Amee. With few inhabitants beyond wild beasts, it ends at Ku-Klip's house on the plains' edge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fifteen

The great Munchkin forest is the Tin Woodman's birthplace and the place where he was enchanted and gradually lost parts of his meat body. It contains the tree where he rusted while chopping and paths leading toward Nimmie Amee's former home. The travelers discover the rusted Tin Soldier there.

Chapter Sixteen

Captain Fyter traveled through the forest on the rainy day he intended to elope with Nimmie Amee. He rusted and remained helpless on a little-used forest path until the others found him.

Chapter Seventeen

The forest contains Nimmie Amee's abandoned cottage and has few inhabitants besides wild beasts. Ku-Klip's house stands at its far edge, where the forest meets the plains of the Munchkin Country.
