Ku-Klip's house in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Ku-Klip's house is a good-sized dark-blue building with lighter-blue trim at the forest's eastern edge, facing broad plains. Blue fence, benches, trees, and lawn surround it. Ku-Klip lives in front; his workshop fills the back and lean-to.

Chapter Seventeen

Ku-Klip's house is a good-sized dark-blue building with lighter-blue trim, a blue fence, blue benches, blue trees, and a blue lawn. Ku-Klip lives in the front part, while his workshop occupies the back part and a lean-to addition.
