the Lemnian massacre

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: massacre of the Lemnian men, murdered their fathers and husbands, the murder of kindred, the murder that had been wrought upon the men, φονεύουσι

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, the Lemnian massacre is the killing by the women of their husbands, captive maids, and all other males after the men rejected their lawful wives; Hypsipyle alone saves Thoas and later conceals the deed from Jason. In The Library, the massacre of the Lemnian men causes Lemnos to be bereft of men; the women kill their fathers and husbands after being dishonored by their husbands' intercourse with captive Thracian women, while Hypsipyle hides and saves her father, Thoas.
