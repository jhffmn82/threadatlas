the Lemnian massacre

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: massacre of the Lemnian men, murdered their fathers and husbands, the murder of kindred, the murder that had been wrought upon the men, φονεύουσι
  • In The Argonautica, the Lemnian massacre is the killing by the women of their husbands, captive maids, and all other males after the men rejected their lawful wives; Hypsipyle alone saves Thoas and later conceals the deed from Jason.
  • In The Library, the massacre of the Lemnian men causes Lemnos to be bereft of men; the women kill their fathers and husbands after being dishonored by their husbands' intercourse with captive Thracian women, while Hypsipyle hides and saves her father, Thoas.

the Lemnian massacre in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Lemnian massacre was carried out by the women after the men rejected their lawful wives for captive maids. The women killed the husbands and captives together with all other males, while Hypsipyle alone saved Thoas; Hypsipyle later concealed this deed from Jason.
