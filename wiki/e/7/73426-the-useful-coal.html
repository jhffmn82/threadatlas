the Useful Coal

Also called: BLACK DIAMOND, Useful Coal, coal, good Coal

Abstract written by a model

The Useful Coal, a speaking, sentient coal, promises to save King Sligo's treasure three times and identify its thief. Burning black and red, it burns and frightens the High Cellarer, reveals him with soot, then dies and becomes a black diamond in the king's crown.

CHAPTER V.

The Useful Coal promises to save King Sligo's treasure three times and identify the thief. It burns the High Cellarer's hand, frightens him away from the king's bedchamber, and enables the king to identify him by rubbing soot on the treasure-chamber door; after dying, it is set in sapphires and placed in the king's crown as a black diamond.
