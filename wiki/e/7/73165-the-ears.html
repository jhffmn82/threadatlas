the ears in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The visible ears are curved pieces of gristle that catch sounds and pass them to the true ears deeper in the head; they require care, including washing with a soft towel and avoiding hard or sharp objects.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The visible ears are pieces of gristle curved to catch sounds and pass them to the true ears deeper in the head. They should be washed with a soft towel and should not have hard or sharp objects put into them.
