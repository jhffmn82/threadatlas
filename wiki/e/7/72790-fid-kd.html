FiD-KD in RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kiseung Kim and Jay-Yoon Lee · 2024

FiD-KD is a retriever and baseline used with large language models, including for zero-shot RALM, reranking, and unanswerable-set classification. It retrieves top-100 contexts, uses knowledge distillation through FiD attention scores, and its base and large variants are evaluated with 25 or 100 contexts. It reaches R@1 recall of 49.4 on Natural Questions and 60.1 on TriviaQA. Its scores support reranking better than traditional RAG answer generation, but RE outperforms it on reranking recall and F1; FiD-KD degrades more on unseen datasets and loses answerable-set accuracy under low-confidence classification.

3 .. 3.3

FiD-KD is used as a baseline and as a retriever for experiments with large language models. Its base and large variants are evaluated with 25 or 100 contexts, while its relevance scores are found to work for reranking but not as well for traditional RAG answer generation.

3.4 / 4 / 4.1 .. 4.2

FiD-KD is used as a comparison retriever and as a baseline for re-ranking and unanswerable set classification. RE performs better than FiD-KD on re-ranking recall and F1 classification, although FiD-KD has higher recall for identifying unanswerable context sets.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

FiD-KD experiences larger performance degradation than the relevance estimator when applied to unseen datasets. It also loses more answerable-set accuracy when low-confidence contexts are classified as unanswerable.
