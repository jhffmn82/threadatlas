Woot the Wanderer in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Woot the Wanderer is a boy from a far corner of the Gillikin Country whose profession is wandering. Curious about other parts of Oz, he arrives at the Tin Woodman's castle after nearly a year of travel, hungry and carrying food and a map of Oz. He hears Nick Chopper's history, argues that Nick's kind heart gives him a duty to return to Nimmie Amee, and joins Nick and the Scarecrow in the search for her. Throughout the journey, Woot is practical, questioning, and helpful. He warns of danger, helps free the party from the Loons, assists their entry into Yoop Castle, and resists Mrs. Yoop's wish to change him. She transforms him into a pea-green Green Monkey; her added magic initially makes him content, but he remains a boy by birth and later dislikes the enchantment. As a monkey, he steals the Magic Apron, rescues Polychrome from her cage, escapes the castle, survives a Jaguar by using the apron, and insists to Jinjur that he is a boy in a monkey's shape. After Ozma restores others but not him, Woot accepts his condition bravely until he regains his natural form. He becomes Ozma's faithful subject, promising to love, revere, and defend her. He continues the search with the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, and Polychrome, carrying supplies, helping the Tin Soldier, proposing ideas about Nimmie Amee, organizing the party in the Invisible Country, carrying the Scarecrow's head, and helping restuff him. He passes barriers and burrows through changes of size, reaches Nimmie Amee, and is ashamed and disappointed when the search seems to have achieved nothing. Finally, he returns to the Emerald City, where Ozma welcomes him and permits him to continue wandering wherever he wishes under her protection.

Narrative, section by section

22 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Woot arrives at the Tin Woodman's castle after wandering for nearly a full year from his former home in a far corner of the Gillikin Country. He asks to meet the Tin Woodman, requests food, and asks the Tin Woodman to tell how he became made of tin.

Chapter Two

Woot listens to Nick Chopper's story and questions how he survived being chopped to pieces. He argues that Nick's Kind Heart should have led him to return to Nimmie Amee, and he joins Nick and the Scarecrow for the journey to find her.

Chapter Three

Woot the Wanderer watches the Scarecrow being restuffed and wonders whether he can travel all the way to the Munchkin Country. He carries food in a knapsack, questions the Tin Woodman about their route and marriage, warns that the Gillikin Country may be dangerous, and travels with his companions.

Chapter Four

Woot argues that the warning sign means danger, but he agrees to travel to Loonville with the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman. After a thorn pricks his leg, he breaks free, uses the thorn to puncture several Loons, frees his companions, and helps them escape.

Chapter Five

Woot the Wanderer travels with the Scarecrow and Nick Chopper to Yoop Castle and helps enter it by standing on Nick Chopper's shoulders to reach the latch. He questions Mrs. Yoop about Mr. Yoop and Polychrome and says that he prefers to remain as he is rather than be transformed. Mrs. Yoop plans to transform him into another form the next morning.

Chapter Six

Woot is frightened by Mrs. Yoop's plans and suspicious of her magic food. Mrs. Yoop transforms him into a Green Monkey, and although he initially becomes angry and tries to attack her, added magic makes him cheerful and content with his new form.

Chapter Seven

Woot plans to steal the Magic Apron and hides under Mrs. Yoop's bed when she enters her bedroom. After she falls asleep, he takes the apron, moves a chair beneath Polychrome's bird-cage, rescues her, and commands the bedroom door to open. He carries the cage into the hall, but becomes lost in the dark until the Tin Owl guides him to the outer door.

Chapter Eight

Woot travels with the group in the form of a Green Monkey and worries that Mrs. Yoop may starve. A Jaguar pursues him, but Woot uses the Lace Apron's command "Open!" and disappears into the earth.

Chapter Eleven

Woot the Wanderer is in the form of a Green Monkey and eats ripe cream-puffs at Jinjur's Ranch. When Jinjur attacks, he hides in the fireplace and becomes blackened with soot. Jinjur scrubs him clean, and he insists that he is a boy in a monkey's shape.

Chapter Twelve

Woot the Wanderer leaves with the Tin Woodman and the Scarecrow carrying a knapsack, apparently beginning a long journey. He is also affected by Mrs. Yoop's transformations, and Ozma and Dorothy hope to help him.

Chapter Thirteen

Woot the Wanderer is still a Green Monkey after Ozma restores the others. He accepts his condition bravely but says that he dislikes being a monkey and would not want another person or Toto to suffer the same fate.

Chapter Fourteen

Woot is seen in the magical platter while Mrs. Yoop is transformed into his form. He later regains his natural form and stands beside Ozma.

Chapter Fifteen

Woot is praised by Ozma and Dorothy as modest, intelligent, and well mannered, and he promises to love, revere, and defend Ozma as a faithful subject. He joins the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, and Polychrome in searching for Nimmie Amee, carries Jinjur's basket of cream-puffs and caramels, and helps oil the Tin Soldier's joints.

Chapter Sixteen

Woot asks whether the strange tin person is really a soldier and suggests that Captain Fyter and the Tin Woodman draw lots for Nimmie Amee. Polychrome rejects this suggestion, saying that Nimmie Amee should choose her own husband.

Chapter Seventeen

Woot suggests that Nimmie Amee would want company and may have gone where other people live. At Ku-Klip's workshop, he wonders whether the Tin Woodman's and Tin Soldier's discarded body parts are still in the barrel, and he points out that tin can become dented or rusted.

Chapter Eighteen

Woot listens to the Head's conversation and questions the value of a cast-off head. He helps tell Ku-Klip about the travelers' search and joins the journey to Mount Munch.

Chapter Nineteen

Woot worries that something will go wrong because their journey toward Mount Munch is proceeding too easily. In the Invisible Country he becomes unable to see himself or the others, helps organize the travelers, and carries the Scarecrow's head after the Scarecrow gives up his straw. He helps restuff the Scarecrow with hay after they cross the ditch.

Chapter Twenty

Woot discovers the Swynes' small house, speaks with them, eats vegetables from their garden, and sleeps beside the straw-stack. He helps Polychrome remove the Scarecrow's old hay and replace it with fresh straw.

Chapter Twenty-One

Woot leads the party toward Nimmie Amee's house but suddenly strikes the invisible wall. He travels through the Blue Rabbit's burrow after Polychrome makes him thumb-high, then chooses to remain with his companions and is restored to his natural size.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Woot the Wanderer tells Nimmie Amee and the others that Chopfyt was made from the Tin men's cast-off parts. He becomes ashamed and disappointed when it appears that the journey has achieved nothing.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Woot leaves Nimmie Amee's house with the others and enters the Blue Rabbit's burrow after being made small. He watches the Rainbow and its maidens so intently that he does not notice himself growing big again, and he is surprised when Polychrome has gone.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Woot the Wanderer returns to the Emerald City and is warmly welcomed at Ozma's palace. Because wandering is his profession, Ozma allows him to wander wherever he wishes and promises to watch over and protect him as well as she can.

Green Monkey in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Green Monkey is Woot, a transformed adventurer made by Mrs. Yoop into a cheerful green-furred monkey with monkey hands and feet, wearing a torn, soiled lace apron. Formerly Jaguar's enemy, it falls into an underground dragon cavern, escapes the Dragon Tribe through a magical roof opening made by shouting “Open!” with its apron, follows Jaguar’s trail to friends, and travels south, asking about Jinjur and Tommy Kwikstep and counting twenty legs.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Green Monkey is Woot after Mrs. Yoop transforms him. It has fine green fur and monkey hands and feet, and it becomes cheerful rather than angry because of the magic added to the transformation.

Chapter Nine

Woot falls into the underground dragon cavern and discovers the Dragon Tribe. He escapes their pursuit by shouting "Open!" with his lace apron, creating a hole in the cavern roof, and then follows the Jaguar's trail to his friends.

Chapter Ten

The Green Monkey travels south with the Scarecrow Bear, Tin Owl, and Polychrome. He asks questions about Jinjur and Tommy Kwikstep and counts Tommy's twenty legs.
