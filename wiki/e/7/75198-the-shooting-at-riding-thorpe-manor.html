The shooting occurs at about three in the morning, killing Hilton Cubitt and seriously wounding Elsie Cubitt. Two shots are heard, but Holmes finds evidence of a third shot fired through the study window, indicating that another person was present. The window was open during the shooting and later shut and fastened from inside.
