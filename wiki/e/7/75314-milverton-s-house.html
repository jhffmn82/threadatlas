Milverton’s house

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: Appledore Towers, the house, the huge house, this big one in its own grounds
  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Milverton’s house is a large property in Hampstead with a garden, veranda, conservatory, and several rooms; Holmes and Watson enter through the greenhouse, hide in the study, and escape over a garden wall while the household pursues them.
  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Appledore Towers is Milverton’s Hampstead home, whose layout Holmes learns before planning a nighttime entry to recover Lady Eva’s letters.
