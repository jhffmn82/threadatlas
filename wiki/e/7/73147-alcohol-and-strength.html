alcohol and strength in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Alcohol opposes strength: it cannot build muscles, bone, nerve, or brain, briefly dulls nerves to create a false feeling, then decreases strength.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Alcohol and strength are presented as opposites: alcohol cannot build muscles, bone, nerve, or brain and therefore cannot give real strength. It may only create a temporary false feeling of strength by dulling the nerves.
