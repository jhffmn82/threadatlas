Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the harbor of Syracuse was watched by thirty Carthaginian ships, but Agathocles drew them away, allowing his fleet and the Etrurian ships to pass out and defeat the pursuing enemy. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Syracuse's great haven became the scene of two major naval battles; its shallows, fortifications, and blocked entrance prevented the Athenians from escaping and contributed to the destruction of their fleet.
