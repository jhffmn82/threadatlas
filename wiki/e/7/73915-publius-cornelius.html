Publius Cornelius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Publius Cornelius is a Roman consul, influenced by philosophy in truth as well as words. Renowned for virtue, birth, piety, prudence, and judgment, he is judged Rome’s best citizen and urges preserving Carthage to restrain pride and maintain peace.

Publius Scipio Nasica was renowned for virtue, noble birth, piety, prudence, and political judgment. The senate judged him the best man among the Roman citizens, and he argued that Carthage should be preserved because its continued existence would restrain Roman pride and preserve peace.
