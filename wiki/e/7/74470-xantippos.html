Xantippos in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Xantippos, a Carthaginian ally, delivers the Carthaginians from immediate danger by routing the recently victorious Romans after great slaughter; his prudence, wisdom, and valour change the course of affairs and win widespread admiration.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXIII.

Xantippos delivered the Carthaginians from their immediate dangers and completely changed the course of affairs. He routed the recently victorious Romans after a great slaughter, and his prudence and wisdom were credited with overcoming the difficulties facing the Carthaginians.
