THE ADVENTURE OF CHARLES AUGUSTUS MILVERTON

Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson plan to burgle Milverton’s house at night and take only the letters used for the blackmail. They intend to leave from Baker Street at eleven, reach Appledore Towers before midnight, and return with the letters if the operation succeeds.
