Abstract written by a model

A large, low cave is engaged for the woodmouse family's wedding because it can hold their relatives. Its bramble-cleared entrance is flanked by light-wood torches; inside, glow-worms and torches provide light, while holly and alder branches decorate the floor, walls, and corners for festivity.
