the cave in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A large, low cave is engaged for the woodmouse family's wedding because it can hold their relatives. Its bramble-cleared entrance is flanked by light-wood torches; inside, glow-worms and torches provide light, while holly and alder branches decorate the floor, walls, and corners for festivity.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

The woodmouse family has engaged the cave for the wedding because it is large enough for their family connection. Toto and <DW53> are invited to attend there.

" CHAPTER VI.

The cave has a low entrance cleared of brambles and vines, with light-wood torches on either side. Inside, it is lit by glow-worms and more torches, and its floor and walls are decorated for the wedding.
