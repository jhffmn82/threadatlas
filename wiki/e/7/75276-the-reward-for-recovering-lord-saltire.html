the reward for recovering Lord Saltire in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A reward offers five thousand pounds for information locating Lord Saltire and one thousand for naming his custodians. By the next morning, Holmes says he has earned the full six thousand pounds and requests a crossed check from the Duke.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

At eleven o’clock next morning my friend and I were walking up

The reward consists of five thousand pounds for information leading to Lord Saltire’s location and another thousand pounds for naming those holding him. Holmes claims to have earned the six thousand pounds and asks the Duke for a crossed check.
