robbery and attack in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A robbery and attack unfolds in a cottage when a robber steals food and tries to take silver teaspoons and a porringer. Miss Mary exposes him; Bruin attacks with teeth and claws, and a kettle pours boiling water on his legs. He escapes, after which Miss Mary and Bruin reconcile and thank each other.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

The robber steals food and attempts to take the cottage's silver teaspoons and porringer. Miss Mary exposes him, the raccoon leaps onto his back and claws, bites, and scratches him, and the kettle adds to the attack by spilling boiling water over his legs. The robber escapes, and Miss Mary and the raccoon afterward reconcile and thank one another.
