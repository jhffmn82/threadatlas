the rescue of Trot and Cap'n Bill in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The rescue of Trot and Cap'n Bill begins when travelers reach the river opposite the Magic Isle. The Wizard promises help, sends the Glass Cat to reassure them, and prepares magic. His attempts fail because cutting or pulling their roots would cause severe pain, leaving them trapped and shrinking.

17. A Remarkable Journey

The rescue of Trot and Cap'n Bill begins when the travelers reach the river opposite the Magic Isle. The Wizard promises to do his best, sends the Glass Cat to reassure them, and starts preparing his magic, but no rescue method has yet been found.

18. The Magic of the Wizard

The rescue of Trot and Cap'n Bill is unsuccessful in this unit. The Wizard tries many methods, but cutting or pulling up their roots would cause severe pain, so the prisoners remain trapped and continue shrinking.
