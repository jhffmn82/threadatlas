India expedition

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Dionysius's Indian expedition, Indian expedition, expedition into India, when he commanded in India

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Dionysius's Indian expedition is described as a conquest of the Indians followed by a return toward the Grecian sea and as leading to the foundation of Nysa as a memorial of his victory. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the India expedition lasts three years, then ends in Boeotia with Dionysius’s return carrying spoils and an Indian elephant, commemorated by recurring festivals.

DIODORUS SICULUS- / BOOK IV.

The India expedition lasted three whole years and was led by Dionysius. His return with spoils and an Indian elephant was commemorated by the Trieterica and other festivals held every third year.
