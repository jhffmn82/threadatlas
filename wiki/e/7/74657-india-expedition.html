India expedition in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Dionysius’s India expedition lasts three years, then ends in Boeotia with his return carrying spoils and an Indian elephant, commemorated by recurring festivals.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS- / BOOK IV.

The India expedition lasted three whole years and was led by Dionysius. His return with spoils and an Indian elephant was commemorated by the Trieterica and other festivals held every third year.
