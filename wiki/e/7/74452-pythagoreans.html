Pythagoreans in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Pythagoreans formed a society pursuing wisdom, sharing property and practicing purity and simplicity. Novices first remained silent, accepted Pythagoras’s authority without proof, and killed no animals because they believed in transmigration of souls.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVII

The Pythagoreans formed a society devoted to the pursuit of wisdom, shared their property, and practiced purity and simplicity. They were initially required to remain silent and accept the authority of Pythagoras, and they killed no animals because of their belief in the transmigration of souls.
