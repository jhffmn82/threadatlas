Thynion in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Thynion, son of Mameus, succeeds Hicetas in Syracuse, commands its island with ten thousand men, wars with Sosistratus, then joins him in summoning Pyrrhus, who reconciles them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXII.

Thynion, son of Mameus, deprived Hicetas of Syracuse after Hicetas had ruled there for nine years. He later controlled the island portion of Syracuse and joined Sosistratus in sending for Pyrrhus; Pyrrhus subsequently reconciled the two rulers.
