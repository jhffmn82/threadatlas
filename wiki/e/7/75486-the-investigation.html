Abstract written by a model

The investigation concerns the murder of Sir Eustace Brackenstall. Inspector Stanley Hopkins asks Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson for help; Holmes examines witnesses, the room, physical evidence, and wine glasses. What first seems clear to Hopkins becomes doubtful, and Holmes delays decisive legal action while seeking more information.
