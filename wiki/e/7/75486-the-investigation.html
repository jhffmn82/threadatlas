THE ADVENTURE OF THE ABBEY GRANGE

The investigation is led locally by Inspector Stanley Hopkins, who asks Sherlock Holmes for assistance. Holmes questions the witnesses and studies the room, the bell-rope, the window, the servants’ circumstances, the body, the poker, and the wine glasses.

The case initially appears clear to Hopkins but becomes doubtful when Holmes examines the wine glasses and physical evidence. Holmes ultimately regards it as one of the most remarkable cases in his collection, though he postpones decisive legal action while seeking more information.
