James Wilder

Abstract written by a model

James Wilder is the Duke of Holdernesse’s young private secretary and illegitimate, publicly unacknowledged son. He tells Thorneycroft Huxtable that Lord Saltire sympathizes with his mother, accompanies the Duke to the Priory School, objects to consulting Holmes, and explains the Duke’s letter was put in the post-bag. Secretly, Wilder manages the Duke’s tenant and visits the Fighting Cock at night, travelling there by bicycle to meet a companion. Driven by persistent hatred of Lord Saltire and a wish to inherit the Holdernesse estate by breaking its entail, he arranges the boy’s kidnapping with Reuben Hayes. After learning of Heidegger’s death, Wilder voluntarily confesses to the Duke, warns Hayes, and gives him money to flee. Wilder then leaves the Duke’s household, destined for Australia to seek his fortune.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE PRIORY SCHOOL

He previously told Huxtable that Lord Saltire sympathized strongly with his mother, comes to the Priory School with the Duke, objects to Huxtable's decision to consult Holmes, and says that the Duke's letter was placed in the post-bag with other letters.

James Wilder, the Duke's secretary, cycles rapidly toward the Fighting Cock with a pale and agitated face. He later waits at the inn for a companion, enters with that person, and remains there while a man in a dog-cart leaves at great speed.

James Wilder arranged Lord Saltire’s abduction with Reuben Hayes because of his hatred of the boy and his desire to inherit the Duke’s estate.
