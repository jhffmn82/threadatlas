Lake Asphaltites in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Lake Asphaltites, also called Lake of Sodom and the Dead Sea, is a lake in Edom, 500 furlongs long and 300 broad. Its bitter, foul water supports no fish; it contains brimstone and yields large floating masses of bitumen and pitch. Demetrius camps nearby after withdrawing from the Nabataean rock.

Lake Asphaltites lies in Edom and is described as about 500 furlongs long and 300 furlongs broad. Its bitter, foul water supports no fish, and it produces floating masses of bitumen and pitch; Demetrius camps nearby after withdrawing from the Nabataean rock.
