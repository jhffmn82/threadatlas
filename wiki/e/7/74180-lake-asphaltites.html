Lake Asphaltites

Also called: Asphaltites, the lake

Abstract written by a model

Lake Asphaltites, also called Lake of Sodom and the Dead Sea, is a lake in Edom, 500 furlongs long and 300 broad. Its bitter, foul water supports no fish; it contains brimstone and yields large floating masses of bitumen and pitch. Demetrius camps nearby after withdrawing from the Nabataean rock.

Lake Asphaltites is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
