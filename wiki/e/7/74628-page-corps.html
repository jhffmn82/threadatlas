Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUR IX.

The page corps consists of sons of Macedonian nobility selected to attend the king as body squires and bedchamber attendants. The pages receive Alexander's horses, help him mount, accompany him hunting, and include the conspirators who plan to kill him while he sleeps.
