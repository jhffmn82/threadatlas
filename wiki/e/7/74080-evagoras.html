Evagoras in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Evagoras was a king of Salamis who commanded Cyprus through many cities and made war against the Persians. He allied with Acoris, received money from Hecatomnus to hire foreigners, and led two hundred sail and six thousand subjects. After defeat at sea, heavy losses, and the siege of Salamis, he left its defense to his son Pythagoras and accepted tribute and subordinate rule. Peace followed, but he was treacherously murdered in Cyprus by an eunuch.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

Evagoras, king of Salamis, formed alliances with Acoris of Egypt, Hecatomnus, the king of Arabia, and other enemies of Persia. He fought the Persians by land and sea, but after his fleet was defeated and Salamis was besieged, he agreed to pay tribute and rule Salamis as one king subject to another.

CHAP. V.

Evagoras, king of Salamis, was treacherously murdered in Cyprus.

BOOK XIV.

Evagoras became king of Salamis in Cyprus and made war against Persia. Peace was eventually concluded with him, but he was later murdered in Cyprus by an eunuch.

Evagoras in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Evagoras, a noble Cypriot prince descended from Salamis’s founders, returned with a small band, expelled Abdemon the Tyrian, became king of Salamis, gained cities by force or promises, and sought dominion over all Cyprus, prompting appeals to Artaxerxes.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XL

Evagoras returned to Salamis with a small band, expelled Abdemon the Tyrian, and became king of the city. He later gained cities by force or promises and sought to bring all Cyprus under his rule, prompting appeals to Artaxerxes for aid against him.
