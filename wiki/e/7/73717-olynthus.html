Olynthus

place · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Olynthians, the city, the town

Abstract written by a model

Olynthus, the greatest city of its region, led resistance against Amyntas and the Lacedaemonians, repeatedly fighting successfully with confederate support. Philip later besieged and captured it through its chief magistrates’ treachery, plundering it and selling its citizens as slaves.

Olynthus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. n.

Olynthus was the center of resistance against Amyntas and the Lacedaemonians. Its forces repeatedly fought successfully and prepared supplies and assistance from their confederates as the war continued.

CHAP. X.

Olynthus, described as the greatest city in its region, was besieged and captured by Philip through the treachery of its chief magistrates. Philip plundered the city and sold its citizens as slaves.
