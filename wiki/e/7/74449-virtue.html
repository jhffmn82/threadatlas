virtue in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Virtue is a set of honorable qualities, including courage, prudence, justice, piety, wisdom, gentleness, sobriety, constancy, patience, frugality, loyalty, purity, affability, and generosity. It brings honor, esteem, lasting reputation, and renown, as shown by Lycurgus, Solon, Cyrus, and Publius Scipio. Learning and disciplined conduct cultivate it; neglect leads to luxury, tyranny, cruelty, and loss of power.

Virtue is associated in the unit with courage, prudence, justice, piety, wisdom, gentleness, and honorable conduct. It brings honor and esteem to figures such as Lycurgus, Solon, Cyrus, and others, while the neglect of virtue is linked with luxury, tyranny, cruelty, and the loss of power.
