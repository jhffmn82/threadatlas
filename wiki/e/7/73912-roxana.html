Roxana in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Roxana, daughter of Oxyartes and Gayares, was judged among Asia’s most beautiful women and married Alexander. She became mother of Alexander IV. After Alexander’s affairs, Cassander imprisoned Roxana and her son at Amphipolis to destroy Alexander’s posterity, then ordered Glaucias to murder them; Roxana was killed and her body concealed.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Since we have treated of the afiisiirs of Asia, we shall now pass over

Roxana was imprisoned with her son in the castle at Amphipolis under the charge of Glaucias. Cassander intended to eliminate her and her son as part of his effort to destroy Alexander’s posterity.

CHAP. VH.

Roxana was imprisoned with her son, Alexander, until Cassander ordered Glaucias to murder them. She was killed and her body was concealed.

SUP. XI.

Roxana was the daughter of Oxyartes and a virgin of marriageable age. Alexander's fellow-soldiers judged her to be the most beautiful lady in Asia after Darius's wife, and Alexander married her.
