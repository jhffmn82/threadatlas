Lucius Asellius, praetor in Sicily, finds the province devastated, appoints Caius Sempronius Longus, and with him and Publius Rutilius Rufus reforms administration, protects vulnerable people, suppresses injuries and false accusers, and restores prosperity.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVI.

Lucius Asellius was sent as praetor to Sicily and found the province greatly devastated. With the assistance of Caius Sempronius Longus and Publius Rutilius Rufus, he reformed its administration, protected the poor and vulnerable, suppressed false accusers and injuries, and restored the province to prosperity.
