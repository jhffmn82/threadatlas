Abstract written by a model

Pearls and diamonds are jewels produced whenever Eileen speaks. They fill her lap, the floor, and by the third day the best room to ankle depth, but vanish before Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy sees them. Eily later carries them home in her apron; the Green Man says kind words and deeds are better jewels.
