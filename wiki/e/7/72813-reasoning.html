Reasoning in Merging Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation for Enhanced Information Retrieval and Reasoning

Xingyu Xiong and Mingliang Zheng · 2024-02-23

Reasoning is a central challenge for Large Language Models, particularly when they must integrate retrieved information, perform logical deduction and problem-solving, and avoid plausible but factually incorrect responses. The study investigates it as a target capability of an architecture combining Mixture of Experts and Retrieval Augmented Generation in the Mistral model. It evaluates reasoning and comprehension qualitatively and with task-specific measures of integration and reasoning over retrieved information. From documented limitations in complex queries, the work moves through architecture and evaluation to reported improvements: the combined approach produces more accurate, nuanced, contextually rich responses and strengthens reasoning for complex queries.
