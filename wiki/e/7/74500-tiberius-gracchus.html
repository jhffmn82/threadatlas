Tiberius Gracchus

Also called: Gracchus

Abstract written by a model

Tiberius Gracchus, a young lieutenant-general, distinguished himself in Spain through courage, prudence, and vigorous command. Later, as ambassador inspecting Syria, he was kindly received by Antiochus III without perceiving the king's hostile designs toward Rome. He was killed by Publius Scipio Nasica while allegedly seeking absolute monarchy; public anger subsided after Nasica admitted the killing and made that claim.

Tiberius Gracchus managed the war in Spain vigorously despite his youth, excelling men of his age in courage and prudence.
