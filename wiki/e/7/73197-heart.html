heart

Also called: The heart, a fatty heart, a muscular heart, the heart, your heart

Abstract written by a model

The heart is a useful organ in the chest, near the lungs, made of very thick, strong muscle. It receives blood purified by the lungs and pumps it through arteries, tubes, and the body, where the blood carries food and removes worn-out matter before returning through veins. It rests briefly between beats and, in children, pumps eighty to ninety times a minute and works faster than in old people. Exercise helps it work well; alcohol can make it beat too fast, tire it, and make it fatty. It is similar to, but smaller than, a beef's heart.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The heart is one of the useful organs located in the chest.

CHAPTER XIV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The heart is in the chest and is made of very thick, strong muscle. It pumps blood through arteries and receives it back through veins. Exercise helps it work well, while alcohol can make it beat too fast, tire it, and become fatty.

CHAPTER XV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The heart receives blood that has been purified by the lungs and pumps it through the body. The heart is near the lungs.

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The heart pumps the blood out so that it can carry food throughout the body and take away worn-out matter.
