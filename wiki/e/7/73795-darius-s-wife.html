Darius's wife in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Darius's wife, renowned as Asia's most beautiful lady, accompanied Darius's army and was captured at Issus with the royal household. Alexander promised to preserve her royal state and prosperity, and, despite her beauty, neither violated nor summoned her, sparing her from reverence and honour.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Darius's wife accompanied the army with Darius. She was captured at Issus along with Darius's mother, children, and the rest of the royal household.

But to return to the kings themselves : Darius with all his army

Alexander declared that he would care for Darius's wife so that she would lack nothing needed to maintain her royal state and former prosperity.

SUP. XI.

Darius's wife was esteemed the most beautiful lady in all Asia. Alexander neither desired her unlawfully nor commanded her to his bed, instead sparing her out of reverence for her person and concern for his honour.
