the three travelers in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The three travelers are Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper. Despite a warning, they follow the path to Loonville, are attacked and taken before King Bal, then escape when Woot uses a thorn. They continue to Yoop Castle, where Mrs. Yoop traps them and plans to transform and keep them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

The three travelers follow the path to Loonville despite the warning sign and are attacked, bound, and taken before King Bal. They escape after Woot uses a thorn against the Loons and then continue their journey.

Chapter Five

The travelers are Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper. They enter Yoop Castle expecting it to be deserted, become trapped inside, and learn that Mrs. Yoop intends to transform them and keep them in the valley.
