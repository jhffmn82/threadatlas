Violet Smith in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Violet Smith is a tall, graceful music teacher, ardent bicyclist, and daughter of James Smith and her mother, living near Farnham. Engaged to Cyril Morton, she teaches Mr. Carruthers’s daughter at Chiltern Grange for a hundred a year, cycling weekly to Farnham Station and returning to her mother on weekends. Her work becomes strained after she refuses her employer’s proposal. While riding near Charlington Heath and Charlington Hall, she is repeatedly followed by a concealed cyclist; she confronts him, but later fears Jack Woodley and plans to leave Bob Carruthers’s employment. Woodley’s return coincides with her abduction, threats, and an attempted forced wedding against her will. Holmes declares the marriage invalid, and Dr. Watson escorts her away while she recovers. As Ralph Smith’s next of kin, she inherits a large fortune and ultimately marries Cyril Morton.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

Violet Smith is a young music teacher who works at Chiltern Grange and travels by bicycle between the house and Farnham Station. She reports that a middle-aged, dark-bearded man repeatedly follows her on a bicycle near Charlington Heath, and she asks Holmes for advice.

We had ascertained from the lady that she went down upon the

Miss Smith travels by bicycle toward Charlington Hall and is followed by a concealed male cyclist. She confronts him by turning and riding directly toward him, causing him to flee, and later tells Holmes that her employer’s marriage proposal has made her position difficult after her refusal.

The Thursday brought us another letter from our client.

Violet Smith writes that she is leaving Bob Carruthers’s employment because of the discomforts of her situation and the return of Jack Woodley. She is abducted, threatened, and subjected to a forced wedding with Woodley, but Holmes declares that the marriage is invalid. She is later escorted from the scene by Dr. Watson.

Holmes rose and tossed the end of his cigarette into the grate.

Miss Violet Smith is recovering after the case, and Holmes intends to have her escorted to her mother’s home. She later inherits a large fortune and marries Cyril Morton.
