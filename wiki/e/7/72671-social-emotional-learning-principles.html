Social Emotional Learning Principles in Improving Assessment of Tutoring Practices using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zifei (FeiFei) Han · 2024

Social Emotional Learning Principles are research-based principles proposed by Chhabra et al. (2022) for evaluating tutoring dialogue and designing prompt strategies. They emphasize effective praise, growth mindset, responses to errors and negative self-talk, and motivation. They are converted into word embeddings for database storage and selective RAG retrieval, structure correctness assessments, and define a Tree of Thought competency area in which tutors earn points for demonstrated criteria or skills.

The research-based principles in social-emotional learning and relationship building were proposed by Chhabra et al. (2022). They informed the evaluation of tutoring dialogue transcripts and the design of prompt strategies.

opening .. 2.4. Evaluation Metrics

Social-emotional learning principles guide the evaluation of tutoring transcripts and the selective retrieval performed by the RAG evaluation engine. They are included in the database as word embeddings and are used to structure correctness assessments.

Appendix A. Prompting Strategies

They define the competency area used in the Tree of Thought Prompt. The tutor receives one point for each listed criterion or skill demonstrated.
