Satibarzanes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Satibarzanes, a former Persian lord-lieutenant of Darius and experienced general, joined Bessus against the Macedonians. He killed soldiers under his command, fled to seek aid, and stirred Aria against Alexander. Leading a strong Arian army, he challenged an enemy commander in single combat, was killed by Erigyus, and his forces surrendered.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

Satibarzanes killed the soldiers placed under his command and joined Bessus in plotting war against the Macedonians. When Alexander approached Chrotacana, Satibarzanes fled with two thousand horse to seek help from Bessus, while his remaining forces were forced to surrender; later, he was reported to have entered the country of the Arians with a large body of cavalry and withdrawn its inhabitants from Alexander's allegiance.

CHAP. IX.

Satibarzanes commanded a strong army in Aria and fought Alexander's commanders. During a general battle he removed his helmet and challenged an enemy commander to single combat, but Erigyus killed him, causing his forces to surrender.
