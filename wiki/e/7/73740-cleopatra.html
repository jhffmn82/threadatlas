Abstract written by a model

Cleopatra was Alexander’s half sister, the daughter of Philip and Amyntas, sister of Alexander the conqueror of the Persians, and wife of Alexander who undertook an expedition into Italy. Perdiccas married her hoping her authority would help him gain the kingdom. Angered at Antigonus, she traveled from Sardis toward Ptolemy, was secretly murdered, and magnificently buried.
