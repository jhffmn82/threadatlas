Ozma's birthday in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Ozma's birthday is a great occasion celebrated throughout the Land of Oz with feasting, rejoicing, decorations, processions, games, plays, and other festivities. It begins with Dorothy, Trot, and Cap'n Bill planning a present, including the Magic Flower, though Ozma has everything she needs. The Wizard trains twelve tiny monkeys, shrinks and hides them in a cake. At the Royal Palace's grand feast, they emerge when Ozma cuts the cake, perform tricks, and are returned to the forest the next day.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

5. A Happy Corner of Oz

Ozma's birthday will take place the following month. Dorothy, Trot, and Cap'n Bill want to remember it with a present even though Ozma already has everything she needs.

6. Ozma's Birthday Presents

Ozma's birthday will include a grand feast at the Royal Palace attended by her friends. Dorothy intends for the trained monkeys to surprise the guests during the banquet by emerging from the cake.

16. The Glass Cat Finds the Black Bag

The Wizard wants twelve monkeys for Ozma's birthday celebration. He plans to shrink them, hide them inside a cake, have them perform tricks when Ozma cuts it, and return them to the forest the next day.

21. The College of Athletic Arts

Ozma's birthday was the great occasion for which the Wizard of Oz trained the twelve tiny monkeys.

22. Ozma's Birthday Party

Ozma's birthday is treated as an occasion for feasting and rejoicing. Celebrations, decorations, processions, games, plays, and other festivities are prepared throughout the Land of Oz.
