peace between Demetrius and the Rhodians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A peace agreement concluded after the siege leaves Rhodes self-governing, without a garrison, and retaining its estates and revenues. It requires alliance with Antigonus against all except Ptolemy and delivery of one hundred citizens, chosen by Demetrius, as hostages.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CFIAP. V.

The peace settlement left Rhodes subject to its own laws and without a garrison, and allowed the Rhodians to retain their estates and revenues. They were to join Antigonus in wars except against Ptolemy and deliver one hundred citizens as hostages chosen by Demetrius.
