peace between Demetrius and the Rhodians

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a peace, pacification, peace, peace with Demetrius

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the peace agreement concluded after the siege left Rhodes self-governing, without a garrison, and retaining its estates and revenues; it required alliance with Antigonus against all except Ptolemy and delivery of one hundred citizens chosen by Demetrius as hostages. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the peace was ratified by Demetrius according to his father’s commands, after which he departed with his whole army.

Abstract written by a model

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CFIAP. V.

The peace settlement left Rhodes subject to its own laws and without a garrison, and allowed the Rhodians to retain their estates and revenues. They were to join Antigonus in wars except against Ptolemy and deliver one hundred citizens as hostages chosen by Demetrius.
