robber in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

An ill-looking thief with a hooked nose, straggling red beard, and green eyes, he enters a cottage with a heavy robber's pack, steals food and searches for valuables, threatens Miss Mary, then is attacked by a raccoon and scalded, fleeing screaming down the road.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

The robber enters the cottage carrying a heavy pack, eats food from the cupboard, and searches the kitchen for valuables. After Miss Mary calls him a thief, he threatens her, but the raccoon attacks him and the kettle pours boiling water over his legs. He flees screaming down the road while carrying the raccoon on his back.
