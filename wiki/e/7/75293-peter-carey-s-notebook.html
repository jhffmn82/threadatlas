THE ADVENTURE OF BLACK PETER

Peter Carey's notebook had the initials J.H.N. and the date 1883 on its first page, followed by C.P.R. and lists of securities associated with Argentina, Costa Rica, and San Paulo. It was found on the floor near the door with a bloodstain on the side facing the boards, suggesting that it was dropped after the crime.
