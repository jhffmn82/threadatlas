Abstract written by a model

Peter Carey’s notebook is a dated 1883 notebook with the initials J.H.N., containing C.P.R., Argentine, Costa Rican, and San Paulo securities, some ticked. Found on the floor near the door with a bloodstain on its floorboard-facing side, it appears to have been dropped after the crime. Hopkins treats it as evidence linking young Neligan to a visit to Carey’s hut to recover securities, while Holmes warns it may mislead the investigation.
