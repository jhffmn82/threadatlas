Arabians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Arabians inhabit open desert country, use camels, hide earthen water vessels, and preserve liberty through customs and desert refuge. They trade frankincense and myrrh to the seaside. After Athenaeus’s raid, they returned to the rock, pursued his exhausted force, and killed nearly all its infantry.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

In Asia, Sclcucus after the routing of Demetrius at Gaza in Syria,

The Arabians lived in open desert country and used the wilderness as a refuge from invading armies. After learning of Athenaeus's raid, they returned to the rock, pursued his exhausted force, and killed nearly all its infantry.
