the Celestial Footstool in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The Celestial Footstool, a sacred object kept covered from profane eyes, is broken when Emperor Wah-Song throws it, repaired by the Chief Cabinet-maker, carried into the palace by Chop-Chin, and used in a false claim of further damage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Ten o'clock of a dark night.

The Celestial Footstool was damaged when Emperor Wah-Song threw it at his Putter-on-of-Slippers, and the Chief Cabinet-maker repaired it. Chop-Chin carries it beneath a covering, uses it to enter the palace, and later pretends that the Emperor broke its leg again by striking him with it.
