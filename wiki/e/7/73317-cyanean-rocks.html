Cyanean rocks in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Cyanean rocks are twin, clashing rocks at the entrance to Pontus, where two seas meet and make passage perilous. Phineus tells the Argonauts to send a dove first. The dove escapes damaged; Athena drives Argo through as the rocks nearly destroy it. After a living man passes between them, the rocks become fixed together forever.
