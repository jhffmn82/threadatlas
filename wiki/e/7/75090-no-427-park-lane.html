No. 427, Park Lane in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

No. 427, Park Lane is a residence on Park Lane with the Honourable Ronald Adair’s second-floor front sitting-room. Adair was found dead there after being shot through its open window; the locked door and lack of visible entry or escape traces made the case remarkable. Holmes attributes the shooting to Colonel Sebastian Moran.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE EMPTY HOUSE

427 Park Lane was the residence where the Honourable Ronald Adair was found dead. Its second-floor room had a locked door, an open window, and no visible traces showing that an attacker had entered or escaped.
