THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

The Greek examination begins the next day and includes a previously unseen passage from Thucydides for translation. Hilton Soames is one of the examiners, and the possibility that a candidate prepared the passage in advance threatens the examination’s fairness.

The examination is allowed to proceed, but Gilchrist is prevented from competing after confessing to the attempted theft and copying.
