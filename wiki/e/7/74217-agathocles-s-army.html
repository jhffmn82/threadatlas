Agathocles's land army in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Agathocles's land army first appears

Abstract written by a model

Agathocles's land army included Greek mercenaries, Samnites, Tyrrhenians, and Celts. It moved to Tunis, captured and destroyed Tunis and another city, then defeated Carthaginian forces in open battle, losing 200 Greeks; a false report claimed its destruction.

Agathocles's army captured Tunis and another city, then destroyed those cities and fought in the open field. It defeated the Carthaginian chariots, horsemen, and infantry, suffering the loss of two hundred Grecians according to the account.
