Short-Tail Comet No. 73 in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A young, short-tailed comet assigned a thirteen-week journey seeks why comets travel, disobeys the Comet Master by speaking to a meteor and approaching the Sun, and is devoured.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Short-Tail Comet No. 73 is a young comet eager to discover why comets travel. He disobeys the Comet Master's orders by speaking to a meteor and approaching the Sun, which devours him.
