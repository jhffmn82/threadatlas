Appian Way, a work of Appius Claudius, was laid from Rome to Capua with hard stones, after he leveled rising ground and filled holes and hollows at great expense.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Having now gone through the affairs of Africa and Sicily, we

The road later called the Appian Way was laid largely with hard stones from Rome to Capua, extending for more than a thousand furlongs. Appius Claudius leveled its rising ground and filled its holes and hollows at great expense.
