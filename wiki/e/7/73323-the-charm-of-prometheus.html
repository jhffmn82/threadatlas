the charm of Prometheus

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a charm, the charm, the charms, this charm

Abstract written by a model

The charm of Prometheus is a magical substance made from Titanian root juice. Medea prepares and gives it to Jason, telling him to bathe and anoint himself and his weapons. Jason steeps it in water and sprinkles it over his body and arms; for one day it grants strength and protects him from bronze weapons, blazing fire, and the bulls’ flames.

Then did night draw darkness over the earth; and on the sea sailors

The charm of Prometheus is a powerful substance that protects a person from bronze weapons and blazing fire for one day. Medea has gathered and prepared it, gives it to Jason, and instructs him to bathe and anoint himself and his weapons with it.

Then Aeetes arrayed his breast in the stiff corslet which Ares gave him

Jason steeps the charm in water and sprinkles it over his shield, spear, sword, and body. It gives him great strength and protects him from the consuming heat and flames of the bulls.
