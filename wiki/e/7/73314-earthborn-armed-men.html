earthborn armed men in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Earthborn armed men are warriors who arise from dragon’s teeth in a ploughed field, equipped with bronze arms. Jason must kill them the day he ploughs; after he casts a stone among them, they fight and slay one another, and he cuts down the survivors.

Now the heroes apart in ambush, in a back-water of the river, were met

The serpent’s teeth grow into earthborn men armed in bronze. Jason is required to kill them with his spear on the same day that he ploughs the field.

Then Aeetes arrayed his breast in the stiff corslet which Ares gave him

The Earthborn men spring up across the ploughed field, armed with shields, spears, and helmets. After Jason throws the boulder among them, they attack and kill one another, and Jason cuts down those who remain.
