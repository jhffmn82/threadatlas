Jim Crow

Abstract written by a model

Jim Crow is Baldhead's faithful, lame crow companion. Sick and unable to find food, he regains strength after Bruin gives him roast chicken, then leads Bruin and Toto to Baldhead's cave and laughs with them. Later, as Toto's guest, he politely introduces himself at the cottage and escorts Miss Mary indoors.

Jim Crow in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887 · the work’s page · read it where Jim Crow first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII.

Jim Crow is Baldhead's faithful crow companion, but he is sick and unable to find suitable food for himself or his master. Bruin gives him a wing of roast chicken, restoring his strength, and Jim Crow leads Bruin and Toto to Baldhead's cave. He later joins the others in laughing after Baldhead welcomes Bruin.

CHAPTER X.

Jim Crow comes to the cottage as Toto's guest and formally introduces himself to the household. He addresses Miss Mary as "Lady Parrot" and courteously offers to lead her indoors.
