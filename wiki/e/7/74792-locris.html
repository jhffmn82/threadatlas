Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Locris was the scene of fighting between the Phocians and their opponents. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Locris was where Zaleuchus was born, and its people chose him to make their laws. In The Library, Locris was afflicted by a plague, after which the Locrians sent maidens to Troy in accordance with an oracle.
