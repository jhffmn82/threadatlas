Abstract written by a model

The Pink Bear's contradictory answers first place Ozma in the hole, then deny that she is there, and finally say that she is among the group. These statements puzzle the Bear King and the others, who cannot determine whether the Bear is mistaken or whether Ozma is hidden, transformed, or otherwise present.
