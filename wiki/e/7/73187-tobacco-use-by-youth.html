tobacco use by youth in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Tobacco use by youth is a great danger because developing bodies and minds are harmed. From ages seven to fourteen, smoking or chewing can stunt growth and prevent full bodily and mental development, leaving less fine body and mind at twenty-one.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Tobacco use by youth is described as a great danger because the body and mind are still developing. A youth from seven to fourteen who begins to smoke or chew will not have as fine a body and mind at twenty-one as if tobacco had never been used.
