Lord Saltire’s kidnapping in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Lord Saltire’s kidnapping was planned by James Wilder, who used a false message involving the Duchess; Reuben Hayes carried it out and transported Saltire to the Fighting Cock Inn to be held.

Lord Saltire’s kidnapping was planned by James Wilder and carried out with Reuben Hayes’s assistance. Wilder deceived him with a false message involving the Duchess, and Hayes transported him to the Fighting Cock Inn to be held there.
