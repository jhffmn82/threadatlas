Abstract written by a model

Exercise and active work, including running, coasting, ball games, and brisk play, strengthen growing muscles; idle habits weaken them. In fresh air, exercise also helps purify the blood, makes the heart work well as it beats faster with rests between beats, and warms the body better than a fire.

exercise in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Exercise and active work help muscles grow strong. Running, coasting, games of ball, and other brisk play and work strengthen muscles, while idle habits make them weak.

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Exercise in the open air helps make the muscles grow and contributes to gaining strength. Pure air taken into the lungs also helps purify the blood.

CHAPTER XIV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Good exercise in the fresh air makes the heart work well and warms the body better than a fire could do. During running, the heart beats faster and gets an instant of rest between beats.
