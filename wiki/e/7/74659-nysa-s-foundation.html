Nysa's foundation in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Nysa's foundation is a city foundation attributed to Bacchus, built for his war-unserviceable soldiers, named after his nurse, and commemorating Dionysius's Indian expedition as a perpetual monument.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIII.

Nysa's foundation was attributed to Bacchus, who supposedly built it for soldiers of his family who were unserviceable for war. The city was said to have been named after Bacchus's nurse, while the surrounding country was called Nysaea.
