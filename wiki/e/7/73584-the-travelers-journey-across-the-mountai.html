the travelers' journey across the mountains in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The travelers' journey across the mountains follows their route from the Rolling Prairie to a swinging strap across the gulf, where spinning mountains repeatedly bump them peak to peak until all reach the far-side meadow safely.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The travelers cross by swinging from the strap and allowing the spinning mountains to bounce them from one peak to the next. Scraps, the Woozy, Button-Bright, Dorothy, Trot, Betsy, the Lion, Hank, the Sawhorse, and the Wizard all reach the meadow safely.
