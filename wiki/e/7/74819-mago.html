Mago

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Mago, called king and serving as the Carthaginian general, leads the Carthaginian forces against Dionysius and is killed while fighting bravely at Cabala. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Mago is a Carthaginian general in Sicily who commands Carthaginians and eighty thousand soldiers, seeks to restore Carthaginian affairs through mild treatment and alliances and by opposing Dionysius, suffers defeat near Abacene, later leads an invasion causing defections, and leaves after peace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX,

Mago, the Carthaginian general, tried to restore Carthaginian affairs in Sicily through mild treatment, alliances, and support for enemies of Dionysius. He marched against Messana but was defeated by Dionysius near Abacene.

CHAP. XL

Mago commanded the Carthaginian invasion of Sicily and advanced through the territories of the Sicilians, causing several cities to defect from Dionysius. Agyrus and the Agyrinenses disrupted his supplies, and after peace was concluded Mago left Sicily.
