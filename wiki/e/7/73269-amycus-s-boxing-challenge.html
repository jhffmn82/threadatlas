Amycus's boxing challenge in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Amycus's challenge forces Bebrycian visitors to box before leaving; Polydeuces accepts as champion and defeats and kills Amycus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK II

Amycus's challenge requires every stranger visiting the Bebrycians to fight him in boxing before departing. Polydeuces willingly accepts it, and the challenge ends with Amycus's defeat and death.
