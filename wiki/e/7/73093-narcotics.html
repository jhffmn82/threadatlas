narcotics in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Narcotics are substances affecting the human system by putting nerves to sleep; the primer gives them special reference and identifies alcohol, tobacco, and opium as narcotics.

front matter

Narcotics are among the substances whose effects upon the human system receive special reference in Child's Health Primer.

CHAPTER IX. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

A narcotic is a substance that has the power to put the nerves to sleep. Alcohol, tobacco, and opium are called narcotics.
