Abstract written by a model

Issus, a city of Cilicia, was seized by Alexander before the battle where Darius’s army was defeated and family and camp captured; survivors then organized resistance before Alexander marched toward Egypt and besieged Tyre.

Issus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. III.

Alexander possessed himself of Issus before the battle. The nearby battle at Issus ended in the defeat of Darius's army and the capture of Darius's family and camp.

CHAP. IV.

Issus is identified as the place of the battle preceding Alexander's march toward Egypt and the siege of Tyre. Survivors of the battle continued to organize resistance in support of Darius.
