Abstract written by a model

Brutii is a commonwealth in Italy, formed by runaway servants and others who captured, plundered, and seized Trojana, Arpinum, Thurium, and other cities.

Brutii is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. IV.

The people who captured Trojana, Arpinum, Thurium, and other cities formed themselves into a commonwealth and assumed the name of Brutii because many of them had been servants.
