Tiphys in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Tiphys, son of Hagnias, is the skilled helmsman and pilot of Argo, urged to join by Athena and entrusted with the ship’s helm. He prepares and launches the vessel, wakes the heroes for departure, advises using a small anchorstone first, and commands its course. He guides the Argonauts safely through the Bosporus and Cyanean rocks amid huge waves, reassures Jason of Argo’s divine strength and easier labors ahead, and later dies of a short sickness far from his native land.
