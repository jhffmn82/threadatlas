Cyzicum in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cyzicum is a place besieged and captured by Mindarus with Pharnabazus’s assistance, then reached by Athenians who trapped the Peloponnesian fleet in a decisive battle that killed Mindarus and routed the Spartans.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

Cyzicum was besieged and captured by Mindarus with assistance from Pharnabazus. The Athenians then sailed there, trapped the Peloponnesian fleet, and fought the decisive battle that led to the death of Mindarus.

CHAP. VI.

Cyzicum was the place associated with the Spartans' rout, news of which prompted them to send ambassadors to Athens.
