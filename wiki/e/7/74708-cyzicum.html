Also called: Cyzicam, Cyzicam:, Cyzicuro, Cyzieum, the city

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Cyzicum is a Greek confederate city that resisted Aridaeus’s siege, secretly armed its inhabitants, obtained help from Byzantium, recovered people trapped outside the walls, and forced Aridaeus to abandon the siege. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Cyzicum is besieged and captured by Mindarus with Pharnabazus’s assistance, then reached by the Athenians, who trapped the Peloponnesian fleet in a decisive battle that killed Mindarus and routed the Spartans.

CHAP. V.

Cyzicum was besieged and captured by Mindarus with assistance from Pharnabazus. The Athenians then sailed there, trapped the Peloponnesian fleet, and fought the decisive battle that led to the death of Mindarus.

CHAP. VI.

Cyzicum was the place associated with the Spartans' rout, news of which prompted them to send ambassadors to Athens.
