drunkards in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

A drunkard is a person who keeps taking increasing amounts of alcohol-containing drinks, progressing from cider, wine, or beer to whiskey, brandy, or rum; alcohol injures the brain and nerves, impairing awareness.
