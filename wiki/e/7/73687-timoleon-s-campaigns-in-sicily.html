Timoleon's campaigns in Sicily in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Timoleon's campaigns in Sicily were an expedition from Corinth that brought honour and benefit to Sicilians: he sailed with mercenaries and ten ships, escaped Carthaginians, landed at Taurominium, defeated Hicetas, and reached Syracuse. He then attacked, besieged, liberated, and won cities, subdued Carthaginians, restored liberty and laws, rebuilt and repopulated devastated Greek cities.

In Sicily, the Syracusans, labouring under intestine seditions, and / ^

In Sicily, Timoleon managed affairs with honour and benefit to the Sicilians. He subdued the Carthaginians, rebuilt destroyed Grecian cities, restored Sicily's liberty, and repopulated Syracuse and the desolated Greek cities.

CHAP. XI.

Timoleon's expedition into Sicily began when he sailed from Corinth with mercenaries and ten ships. He escaped the Carthaginians at Rhegium, landed at Taurominium, defeated Hicetas near Adranum, and proceeded to Syracuse.

CHAP. XII.

Timoleon's campaigns included attacks on the Leontines, the siege of the new city and Engyum, and the expulsion of Leptines. He liberated Apolloniadcs and Engyum, raided Carthaginian territory for funds, captured Entella, and won the submission of many cities while allowing them to retain their own laws.
