Abstract written by a model

Timophanes, Timoleon’s Corinthian brother, is the richest and most daring Corinthian; he gathers armed men, rules as an absolute tyrant, seeks sovereignty, and is killed by Timoleon in the market-place.

Timophanes was Timoleon's brother and the richest and most daring man among the Corinthians. He gathered armed men, acted as an absolute tyrant, and showed ambition to seek sovereignty before Timoleon killed him in the market-place.
