Drepanum

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Drepanam, Drepanum’s unhappy port

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Drepanum receives the Carthaginian cavalry sent away from Lilybaeum, serves as a destination for captured provisions, and is the site near which Claudius suffers a major naval defeat. In The Aeneid, Drepanum receives the weary Trojan fleet after wanderings and storms and becomes Aeneas’s greatest final loss when Anchises dies there.
