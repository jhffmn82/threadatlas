Mrs. Yoop in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mrs. Yoop is a wicked, cruel Giantess and yookoohoo who lives alone in secluded Yoop Castle. She wears six magic hairpins, an invisible-to-all-but-fairies ring, ankle bracelets, and a lace apron, and once used magical arts to obtain food and transform people. She created the Green Monkey enchantment and transformed the group into their current forms. After the group takes her Magic Apron, she remains trapped in her bedroom, though Polychrome notes that her other magic items may free her. While weaving a replacement apron, she is transformed by Ozma into Woot the Wanderer and then permanently into the Green Monkey, losing her magical arts.

Chapter Eight

Mrs. Yoop remains trapped in her bedroom after the group takes away her Magic Apron, but Polychrome says that her magic hairpins, ring, and bracelets may help her escape. She is the Giantess who transformed the group into their current forms.

Mrs. Yoop is described as the Giantess who created the Green Monkey enchantment and other transformations. Polychrome proposes that Mrs. Yoop be punished by receiving Woot's original form, allowing Woot to exchange forms with her.

Chapter Fourteen

Mrs. Yoop is shown in Yoop Castle weaving a new lace apron and appears uneasy, possibly sensing that she is being watched. Ozma transforms her first into Woot the Wanderer and then permanently into the Green Monkey, leaving her unable to perform magical arts.

Mrs. Yoop in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Mrs. Yoop is a giantess, a powerful Yookoohoo, and an artist in transformations who lives secluded in Mrs. Yoop's Valley, in her private castle, and, after Mr. Yoop's capture, in Yoop Castle. Married to the giant Mr. Yoop, she has no friends by policy and is lonely, yet is wicked, cruel, merciless, and feared. She dresses in embroidered skirts, a lace apron, and golden shoes, normally wears the Magic Apron, and owns a giant chair, fiddle, stockings, and bedroom. She practices unauthorized yookoohoo magic: she can create objects, turn unwanted things into food, read thoughts when she chooses, and permanently transform living beings for amusement. She turns Polychrome into a canary-bird and confines her in a gold cage, then plans to keep Woot, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper as long as she lives by changing them. She transforms the travelers and leaves them imprisoned while making her sixteen daily circuits of the castle after breakfast. Her Magic Apron opens the enchanted castle's doors and windows, but after she removes it for bed, Woot hides under her bed, opens the bedroom door, and traps her inside. She pounds on the door and threatens vengeance. She later claims no power can undo her transformations, though Ozma finds her magic peculiar and difficult to understand and the travelers hope Ozma or Glinda can counter it.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Mrs. Yoop is a Giantess who lives in Yoop Castle and claims to be an Artist in Transformations, or more of a Yookoohoo than a Witch. She admits that she transformed Polychrome into a canary-bird and intends to keep Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper as company by transforming them into other shapes. She says that she has lived peacefully since Mr. Yoop was captured and can transform unwanted things into food.

Chapter Six

Mrs. Yoop is a powerful Yookoohoo who lives in a secluded castle and can create objects, transform food, and permanently change living beings into new forms. She transforms Woot, Nick Chopper, and the Scarecrow for her own amusement, has Polychrome in a cage, and leaves the prisoners locked inside while she walks around the castle.

Chapter Seven

Mrs. Yoop keeps Polychrome prisoner and uses the Magic Apron to open the doors and windows of the enchanted castle. She takes the apron off before going to bed and fails to notice Woot hiding under her bed. When Woot opens the bedroom door, she wakes and is trapped inside her own bedroom, where she pounds on the door and threatens vengeance.

Chapter Eleven

Mrs. Yoop is a Giantess in the Gillikin Country and a yookoohoo who transformed the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, Woot, and Polychrome. She claims that no power can alter her transformations, though the travelers hope Glinda or Ozma can counter them.

Chapter Twelve

Mrs. Yoop is a Giantess and the only yookoohoo in Oz. She transforms the Tin Woodman, the Scarecrow, Woot, and another person, and Ozma considers her magic peculiar and difficult to understand.
