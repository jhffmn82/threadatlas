the Tin Soldier's sword in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

An enchanted sword cut the Tin Soldier to pieces. The Wicked Witch refused to let Ku-Klip mend him, so Ku-Klip made him a new tin body; his discarded parts were kept in the workshop and later used in making Chopfyt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

The Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier were cut to pieces by their enchanted weapons, and the Wicked Witch refused to let Ku-Klip mend them. Ku-Klip therefore made new tin bodies for them, while their discarded heads and other parts were kept in his workshop and later used in making Chopfyt.
