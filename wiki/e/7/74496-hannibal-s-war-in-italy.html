Abstract written by a model

Hannibal's seventeen-year war in Italy was a campaign against powerful nations that wasted and harassed much of Italy, destroyed many cities, and depended on disciplined forces and stratagems. It unfolded through Hannibal's campaigns, disputes over captives and ambassadors, and ended with his return to Africa.
