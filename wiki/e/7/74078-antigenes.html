Antigenes

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: his master Antigenes

Abstract written by a model

Antigenes is a prudent, faithful captain and general of the Argyraspides. He commands them, resists Antigonus’s corruption and Seleucus’s and Python’s efforts to turn them against Eumenes, persuades Tautamus to abandon a plot, and argues for Eumenes and for Macedonians’ control of choosing the army’s general.

Antigenes is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. V.

Antigenes, one of the commanders of the Argyraspides, rejected Antigonus's attempt to corrupt him and persuaded the corrupted Tautamus to abandon the plot against Eumenes. He argued that Eumenes was less dangerous to their positions than Antigonus.

Eurydice being queen-regent in Macedonia, as soon as she heard

Antigenes commanded the Argyraspides and refused every attempt by Seleucus and Python to turn them against Eumenes. He later argued that the Macedonians under Alexander should control the election of the army's general.
