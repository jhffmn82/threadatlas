Ozma’s safe return in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Ozma’s safe return is an Emerald City celebration after the dishpan is recovered. Ozma is found, prompting the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman to return and present her the dishpan; her restoration brings rejoicing, a triumphal march, palace welcome, and week of feasting.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 25

Ozma’s safe return causes great rejoicing throughout the Emerald City. It is celebrated by a triumphal march, a palace welcome, and a week of feasting and merriment.

CHAPTER 26

Ozma is found after the dishpan has been carried away, and the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman return to the Emerald City because of the news. Her restoration brings them joy, which they express by presenting the dishpan to her.
