Circe’s purification ritual in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Circe’s purification ritual uses a young sow’s blood on suppliants’ hands, libations to Zeus the Cleanser, removal of defilements, and wine-less atonement-cakes to seek appeasement and remove the murder of Apsyrtus.

Circe’s purification ritual begins with a young sow whose blood she sprinkles on the suppliants’ hands. She adds drink offerings to Zeus the Cleanser, has her attendants carry away the defilements, and burns atonement-cakes without wine while praying that the Furies and Zeus will be appeased.
