Abstract written by a model

The knife is a small old-fashioned sealing-wax knife with an ivory handle and stiff blade, belonging to Professor Coram’s study desk. Found on the carpet beside the wounded Willoughby Smith, it is identified as the weapon. Holmes believes a suspected woman used it to stab Smith; Coram suggests Smith discarded it during a suicide.
