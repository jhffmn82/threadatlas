Taurominium

place · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Tauromin turn, Taurominlum, Tauromiuium, citadel

Abstract written by a model

Taurominium was founded when Andromachus settled the exiles of Naxos on Mount Taurus. The city became wealthy and prosperous, though the text says that in a later age its inhabitants were removed and a Roman colony was established. Taurominium was recovered by Rupilius after a severe siege reduced its defenders to cannibalism. Rupilius captured Comaon, the brother of Cleon, while he was trying to escape, and Sarapion's betrayal of the citadel led to the capture and crucifixion of fugitives.
