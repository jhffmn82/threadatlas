A great, bright hearth fire crackles and leaps, lighting the kitchen and making its objects glitter as Bruin tends it and the group gathers in comfort. Before sleep, Bruin carefully covers it with ashes; its embers retain a dusky glow as mice seek warmth.

Toto's Merry Winter / CHAPTER I.

The fire crackles and leaps on the hearth, making the kitchen glow and causing the brass, tin, and glass objects to glitter. Bruin pokes it while preparing supper, and the group sits around it afterward in comfort.

CHAPTER III.

Bruin rakes ashes over the fire and covers it before going to sleep. Its covered embers continue to give off a dusky glow, and the mice later try to warm themselves beside it.
