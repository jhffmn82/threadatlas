Abstract written by a model

Satyrus, a stage-player, was warned by an oracle to beware of Musculus. He barred people of that name, feared mice, and ordered their killing and holes blocked, yet died from a wound in his arm’s muscles.

It is reported that there were some predictions concerning the

Satyrus was warned by an oracle to beware of Musculus. He excluded people of that name from his dominions, feared house and field mice, and ordered children to kill them and block their holes; despite these precautions, he was killed by a wound in the muscles of his arm.
