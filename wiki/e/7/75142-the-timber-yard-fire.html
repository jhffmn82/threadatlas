the timber-yard fire

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: an alarm, the alarm of fire, the burned wood-pile, the conflagration, the fire

Abstract written by a model

At about midnight, a fire begins in Jonas Oldacre's timber-yard, consuming a dry timber stack with great fury before engines contain it. Initially thought accidental, it destroys the wood-pile and reportedly leaves burned human remains and Oldacre's trouser buttons, linking it to his suspected disappearance and murder. Holmes later recreates the alarm with straw, exposing hidden Oldacre.
