Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Halicarnassus is the greatest city in Caria, containing a royal palace with turrets and citadels. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Halicarnassus is the town near which the reputedly transformative fountain was located.
