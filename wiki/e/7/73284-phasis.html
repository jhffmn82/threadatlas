Phasis in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Phasis is a broad-flowing river from the Cytaean mainland into Pontus, the eastern limit of the sea voyage. Reached at night after the Caucasian mountains, its mouth marks entry into Colchian land. Phineus directs the Argonauts there; the Argo is moored while Jason and his comrades pursue the golden fleece.

river Phasis in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Phasis borders the Caucasus and flows into the Caucasian Sea with the Araxes. It carries the ship into Colchis, between the Caucasus and Aea on one side and the plain and grove of Ares on the other, where Jason offers and anchors.
