Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Phasis is the broad-flowing river flowing from the Cytaean mainland into Pontus, whose mouth marks entry into Colchian land and where the Argo is moored while Jason and his comrades pursue the golden fleece. In The Argonautica, the river Phasis borders the Caucasus, flows into the Caucasian Sea with the Araxes, carries the ship into Colchis, and is where Jason offers and anchors.

Phasis in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · 299 to 200 BC · the work’s page · read it where Phasis first appears

Abstract written by a model

Phasis is a broad-flowing river from the Cytaean mainland into Pontus, the eastern limit of the sea voyage. Reached at night after the Caucasian mountains, its mouth marks entry into Colchian land. Phineus directs the Argonauts there; the Argo is moored while Jason and his comrades pursue the golden fleece.
