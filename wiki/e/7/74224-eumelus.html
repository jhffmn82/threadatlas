Eumelus

Abstract written by a model

Eumelus was king of the Cimmerian Bosphorus. With support from neighboring peoples, he raised an army and fought his brother Satyrus for the kingdom; after Satyrus and Prytanis died, he killed their friends, wives, and children except Parisades's son. He later won popular support by restoring institutions, remitting tributes, aiding Greek cities, suppressing pirates, and enlarging his dominion. Warned to heed a moving house, he avoided his house, yet a chariot canopy was interpreted as fulfilling the prophecy. He ruled six years and was succeeded by his son Spartacus.
