Heraclides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Heraclides, Dion's admiral, was storm-delayed in the Peloponnesus, then arrived with twenty galleys and fifteen hundred soldiers and became Syracusan admiral.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

Heraclides, Dion's admiral, was delayed in the Peloponnesus by storms and contrary winds. He eventually arrived with twenty galleys and fifteen hundred soldiers and was declared admiral by the Syracusans.
