Decius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Decius, a Campanian governor and Roman tribune at Rhegium, joins its treacherous seizure and sells inhabitants’ goods; expelled by Campanian soldiers, he is later blinded by a Rhegium physician in retaliation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FROM THE SAME..

Decius, a Campanian governor of the garrison at Rhegium, participated in the treacherous seizure of the city and sold the goods of its inhabitants. After he was expelled by the Campanian soldiers, a physician from Rhegium later blinded him with an eye treatment made from cantharides.
