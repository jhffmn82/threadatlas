the Green Men

group · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Green Man, Green Men, The Green Men, a fairy, the Green Man, the fairies, the little man

Abstract written by a model

The Green Men are fairies and woodland people who live in the forest. A tiny Green Man, wearing a light green jacket and scarlet cap, grants Eileen Macarthy a wish after she saves him from a spider’s web and sends her good wishes. Later, the Green Men pursue Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy in sport and revenge around County Kerry three times for thrice three days, then let him collapse in a bog and vanish.
