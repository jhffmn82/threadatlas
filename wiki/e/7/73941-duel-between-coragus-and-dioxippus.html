duel between Coragus and Dioxippus

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a duel, battle with Dioxippus, combat, fight a duel

Abstract written by a model

A public single combat between Coragus and Dioxippus, won by Dioxippus. Coragus fought armed, while Dioxippus fought naked with a club; Dioxippus avoided the javelin, broke the sarissa, stopped Coragus drawing his sword, and knocked him down. Alexander then ordered Coragus released.
