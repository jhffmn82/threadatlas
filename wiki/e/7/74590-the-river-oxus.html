the river Oxus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The river Oxus is a destination reached by Alexander after desert marches, providing water for soldiers and those behind; during a later encampment nearby, springs of water and oil appeared near his pavilion.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

SUP. I.

Alexander reached the river Oxus about evening after the march through the desert. His arrival provided a place where the soldiers could refresh themselves and fill vessels with water for those still behind.

SUP, V,

Alexander marched again to the river Oxus. While encamped near it, two springs appeared near his pavilion, one of water and the other of oil.
