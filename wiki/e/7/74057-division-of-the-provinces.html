division of the provinces

event · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a second division of the prbviaces

Abstract written by a model

After the events at Triparadisus, Antipater made a second division of the provinces. Ptolemy retained Egypt; Laomedon received Syria; Seleucus Babylon; and Antigonus Susiana, Greater Phrygia, and Cilicia. Other commanders, including Peucestes, Python, Oxyartes, Nicanor, Cassander, Clitus, and Aridæus, received remaining provinces.
