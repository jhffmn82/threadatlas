  • In Description of Greece, the Eleans are renamed from the Epeans during the reign of Eleus, fight Hercules with help from Pylos and Pisa, and later participate in conflicts involving the Dorians, Persians, Lacedæmonians, Macedonians, and other Greeks.

Elians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Elians rejected a forced unlawful Olympiad, executed oracle-robbing mercenaries, regained liberty and recovered the temple at Olympus, then expelled Arcadians from Lassia but were defeated and contested the Olympic games.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX,

The Elians dispossessed the Arcadians from Lassia under the shelter and colour of the Arcadian fugitives. They were defeated near Lassia and later opposed the Pisates and Arcadians at the Olympic games.

people of Elis in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The people of Elis are a group defined by recurring religious practice: monthly sacrifices with specified offerings, libations to several gods and heroes, votive dedications, and preserved oracle consultations. They have a citadel at Elis and honor Dionysus most of all, celebrating the Thyia, during which they report wine miraculously appearing in sealed flagons.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

The people of Elis sacrifice monthly at the altars, using frankincense, honey cakes, olive branches, and libations. They also pour libations to the god of Libya, Hera of Ammon, Parammon, heroes, and the wives of heroes, and they preserve records of consultations with the oracle at Libya.

CHAPTER XXVI.

The people of Elis honor Dionysus above all other gods. They celebrate the Thyia and report that wine appears miraculously in sealed flagons during the festival.
