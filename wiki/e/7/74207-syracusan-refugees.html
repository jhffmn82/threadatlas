Syracusan refugees in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Syracusan refugees flee to Messana, gather against Agathocles, are expelled by Messanians, then received and led by Dinocrates, becoming Sicily’s main opposition; Agathocles’ agents accuse Dinocrates of blocking restoration of their ancient liberties.

The Syracusan refugees gather at Messana because it remains opposed to Agathocles. The Messanians expel them, after which Dinocrates receives the exiles and commands them in the struggle against Agathocles.

CHAR IV,

The exiles were led by Dinocrates and formed the main opposition to Agathocles in Sicily. Agathocles sent agents to them, accusing Dinocrates of obstructing the restoration of their ancient liberties.
