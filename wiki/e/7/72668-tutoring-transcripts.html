Abstract written by a model

Tutoring transcripts are five randomly selected records from real-world United States middle-school math sessions. Produced by transcribing Zoom recordings with Whisper, they document a tutor guiding five students through basic arithmetic. The transcripts are converted into word embeddings for database storage, integrated with social-emotional learning and relationship-building principles, and used to review GPT-generated assessments. Results evaluate tutor competency, including evidence that a student did not engage in negative self-talk. A RAG-based approach evaluates them accurately without hallucinations; broader real-world transcript coverage is planned.
