Philip's planned conquest of Greece and war against Persia

Abstract written by a model

Philip sought sovereign command over all Greece and planned war against Persia, portraying it as benefiting the Grecians and avenging Persian impiety. Once appointed general of all Greece, he ordered the cities to prepare military quotas; the aims eventually came to pass.

CHAP. X.

Philip had long desired sovereign command over all Greece and intended to make war against the Persians. The text says that these aims eventually came to pass, though their details were reserved for a later account.

CHAP. XIV.

Philip planned a war against Persia and presented it as an undertaking for the advantage of the Grecians and in revenge for Persian impiety. After being appointed general of all Greece, he ordered the cities to prepare their military quotas.
