theft of the dishpan in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The theft of Cayke’s jeweled, valuable magic dishpan is the first theft in the Yip Country and occurs on the morning of Ozma’s disappearance. Cayke discovers it missing, prompting a failed proclamation and suspicion that someone entered from the world below. Cayke leaves to search with the Frogman, later joining Dorothy’s party’s search; Ugu is identified as the thief.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The robbery is reported when Cayke discovers that her jeweled dishpan is gone. The Frogman first proposes a proclamation demanding its return, but when no one responds, he concludes that someone from the world below entered the Yip Country at night and took it.

CHAPTER 14

The theft of the dishpan causes Cayke to leave her home and search for it. Cayke and the Frogman do not yet know who committed the theft and plan to seek answers in the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 17

Ugu stole Cayke's valuable magic dishpan. The theft brings the Frogman and his companions into the same search as Dorothy's party.
