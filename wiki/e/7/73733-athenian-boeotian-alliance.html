The Athenian-Boeotian alliance formed as Boeotians joined Athenians for common liberty, then marched to Chaeronea and fought Philip.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIV.

The Athenian-Boeotian alliance was formed after Demosthenes persuaded the Boeotians to join Athens in defending their common liberty. The allied forces marched to Chaeronea and fought Philip there.
