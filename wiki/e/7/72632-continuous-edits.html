Continuous edits dynamically modify EMG, are incorporated by M-RAG, and over four weeks EMG-RAG supports them while outperforming M-RAG across question answering, autofill forms, and user services.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.2

Continuous edits are evaluated over four weeks using question-answering R-L and Exact Match accuracy for autofill forms and user services. EMG-RAG consistently outperforms M-RAG by approximately 10.6% for question answering, 9.5% for autofill forms, and 9.7% for user services.
