John Hector McFarlane's arrest concerns the suspected wilful murder of Jonas Oldacre. Police anticipate arrest and report a warrant while McFarlane is still free and reaches Sherlock Holmes. Inspector Lestrade then formally arrests him, and two constables escort him to a waiting four-wheeler.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE NORWOOD BUILDER

John Hector McFarlane's arrest is anticipated because the police believe they have evidence linking him to Jonas Oldacre's suspected murder. A warrant is reported to have been issued, although McFarlane remains at liberty when he reaches Mr. Sherlock Holmes.

It was a clang of the bell, followed instantly by heavy steps

Inspector Lestrade formally arrests John Hector McFarlane for the wilful murder of Jonas Oldacre. Two constables escort McFarlane to a waiting four-wheeler while Lestrade remains to discuss the case with Holmes.
