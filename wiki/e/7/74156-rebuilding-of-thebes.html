Abstract written by a model

Cassander undertakes rebuilding Thebes for fame and honor, restoring its renowned city with walls built largely by Athenians and aid from Greek communities, Sicily, and Italy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Cassander undertook the rebuilding of Thebes to gain fame and honor from restoring its renowned city. The Athenians built much of the walls, while other Greek communities and contributors from Sicily and Italy also provided assistance.
