rebuilding of Thebes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cassander undertakes rebuilding Thebes for fame and honor, restoring its renowned city with walls built largely by Athenians and aid from Greek communities, Sicily, and Italy.

Narrative, section by section

Since we have treated of the afiisiirs of Asia, we shall now pass over

Cassander undertook the rebuilding of Thebes to gain fame and honor from restoring its renowned city. The Athenians built much of the walls, while other Greek communities and contributors from Sicily and Italy also provided assistance.

restoration of Thebes in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The restoration of Thebes followed the expulsion of the Thebans by Alexander. Cassander carried it out with assistance from the Athenians, Messenians, and Arcadians of Megalopolis.
