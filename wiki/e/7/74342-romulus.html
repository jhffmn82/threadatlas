Romulus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Romulus, born from a priestess of Trojan blood with divine ancestry, restores his grandsire’s crown, founds and expands Rome as a great prince, reigns on earth, and becomes a god.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Where long extended plains of pleasure lay:

Romulus is shown as a great prince born to restore his injured grandsire’s crown. He will found and expand Rome, and his divine parentage will lead to his own elevation among the gods.

Romulus in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Romulus is Rome’s founder, first king, lawgiver, and ruler of the Romans. Married to Hersilia, he leads Rome’s war against Tatius and the Sabines, then shares sovereignty with Tatius. His spear, thrown from Mount Aventine toward the Capitol, takes root and becomes a tree presaging Rome’s greatness. Finally Gradivus takes him from earth to heaven, and he is deified as Quirinus.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

FABLES XII. AND XIII. [XIV.698-851]

Romulus founds Rome and becomes its king. He leads the Romans in the war against Tatius and the Sabines, later shares sovereignty with Tatius, and is taken to heaven by Gradivus, becoming Quirinus.

FABLES IV. V. AND VI. [XV.479-621]

Romulus once saw his lance, fixed in the Palatine hill, suddenly put forth roots and become a tree with flexible branches that cast an unexpected shade.

EXPLANATION.

Romulus was said to have thrown a spear from Mount Aventine toward the Capitol, where it stuck in the ground and became a leafy tree. The tree was regarded as a presage of Rome’s future greatness and was said to flourish while the Republic flourished.

Romulus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Romulus, twin son of Rhea Silvia and brother of Remus, is exposed, rescued and raised, avenges Amulius, then founds Rome with Remus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Romulus is born to Rhea Silvia and is exposed on the Tiber with his twin Remus. A herdsman rescues and educates him, and he later helps take revenge on Amulius and founds Rome.
