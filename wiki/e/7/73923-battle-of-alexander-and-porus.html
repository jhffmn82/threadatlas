Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Battle involving Alexander and Porus: Alexander's horse broke Indian chariots, elephants hurt Macedonians, then panicked and trampled Indians; Porus charged with forty elephants before being overcome.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

In the battle, Alexander's horse first broke the Indian chariots, while the elephants caused severe losses among the Macedonians. The Macedonians then killed the elephant guards and wounded the elephants until they panicked and trampled Indian soldiers. Porus made a fierce charge with forty elephants before he was overcome.
