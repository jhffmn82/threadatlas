  • In The Magic of Oz: The Magic Isle, also called the Isle of the Magic Flower, is a tiny enchanted island in the middle of a river in the Gillikin Country of the Land of Oz, no more than fifty feet across. It is bare, with no trees, shrubs, grass, weeds, stones, or other growth; its only plant is the Magic Plant in a golden flower-pot at its center. Kalidahs avoid it because of its magic. The island’s chief danger is its enchantment: the Magic Flower lures strangers onto it, then flesh roots grow from living visitors’ feet into the ground, holding them unable to move or lift a foot. Those trapped also shrink day by day until nothing remains. Trot and Cap’n Bill arrive, become rooted prisoners, and begin shrinking. The flower can respond to wishes of people on the island, but it is also described as a trap meant to catch and destroy them. In the end, Cap’n Bill protects his foot with wood and carries the Magic Flower away.

the Magic Isle in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Magic Isle, also called the Isle of the Magic Flower, is a tiny enchanted island in the middle of a river in the Gillikin Country of the Land of Oz, no more than fifty feet across. It is bare, with no trees, shrubs, grass, weeds, stones, or other growth; its only plant is the Magic Plant in a golden flower-pot at its center. Kalidahs avoid it because of its magic. The island’s chief danger is its enchantment: the Magic Flower lures strangers onto it, then flesh roots grow from living visitors’ feet into the ground, holding them unable to move or lift a foot. Those trapped also shrink day by day until nothing remains. Trot and Cap’n Bill arrive, become rooted prisoners, and begin shrinking. The flower can respond to wishes of people on the island, but it is also described as a trap meant to catch and destroy them. In the end, Cap’n Bill protects his foot with wood and carries the Magic Flower away.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

9. The Isle of the Magic Flower

The Isle of the Magic Flower is a tiny island in the middle of the river, measuring no more than fifty feet in either direction. It has no visible tree, shrub, or grass, and Kalidahs avoid going there because they do not care for magic.

10. Stuck Fast

The island is bare ground without a weed, stone, or blade of grass, and the Magic Flower is the only thing growing there. Its magical ground sends roots into Trot's and Cap'n Bill's feet, trapping them when they arrive.

15. The Lonesome Duck

The Magic Isle holds Trot and Cap'n Bill fast by their roots, preventing them from moving or even lifting a foot. The Lonesome Duck says that prisoners usually grow smaller there each day until nothing remains of them. The island is described as a place where magic is common and where the Magic Flower responds to the wishes of those upon it.

17. A Remarkable Journey

The Magic Isle lies in the Gillikin Country across the river and contains the golden flower-pot holding the Magic Plant. Living things that venture onto it become rooted in the soil and cannot leave, as has happened to Trot and Cap'n Bill.

18. The Magic of the Wizard

The Magic Isle holds Trot and Cap'n Bill by roots that have grown from their feet into the ground. It causes them to shrink while also serving as a trap connected to the Magic Flower, which Trot says lures people onto the island and destroys them.

19. Dorothy and the Bumble Bees

The Magic Isle traps Trot and Cap'n Bill by causing their feet to take root in the ground. Cap'n Bill later carries away the Magic Flower after protecting his foot with wood.
