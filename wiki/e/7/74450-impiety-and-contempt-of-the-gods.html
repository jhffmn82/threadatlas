impiety and contempt of the gods in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Impiety and contempt of the gods are shown in figures such as Epopeus, Salmoneus, and Romulus Sylvius, who challenged, dishonored, or mocked the gods. Their conduct is presented as bringing destruction or divine punishment: Epopeus destroyed temples and altars, Salmoneus rejected sacrifices and imitated Jupiter's thunder, and Romulus Sylvius was struck dead by a thunderbolt.
