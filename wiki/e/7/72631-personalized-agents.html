Abstract written by a model

Personalized agents are LLM-powered agents for smartphone AI assistant platforms. Constructed from practical memory data gathered from assistants, including conversations, screenshots, memories, and question-answer pairs, they use and retrieve users’ personal memories to help perform personalized tasks. Their essential capabilities include selecting relevant memories, editability, and selectability in practical use. They support and enhance downstream applications such as question answering, autofill forms, user services, reminders, and travel navigation. The agents are transferred into a real smartphone AI assistant, where they improve usability and user experience by answering questions and providing personalized services.

Abstract

Personalized agents are powered by large language models and use a user’s smartphone memories to provide enhanced downstream applications. They were transferred into a real smartphone AI assistant, where they improved usability.

1

AI assistants, including Apple’s Siri, generate or contain users’ personal memories through everyday conversations and apps. The unit studies personalized agents that enhance AI assistants by using these memories with large language models.

2

User-personalized agents are constructed using practical memory data gathered from smartphone AI assistants. They are used for question answering, autofill forms, and user services.

3

Personalized agents assist users on smartphone AI assistant platforms with personalized tasks. They are designed to support editability and selectability, enabling applications such as question answering, autofill forms, reminders, and travel navigation.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.4

The personalized agent is trained from conversations, screenshots, memories, and question-answer pairs to support users through a smartphone AI assistant. It uses retrieved memories to answer questions and provide other services.

6

The personalized agents are powered by LLMs and use users' personal memories to enhance three downstream applications.
