Harding Brothers in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Harding Brothers is a Kensington picture and sculpture retailer. It obtains three Napoleon busts from Gelder & Co. and sells all three, respectively to Horace Harker, Josiah Brown, and Mr. Sandeford; it also sells Devine’s last Napoleon bust to Sandeford.

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Harding Brothers of Kensington sold three Napoleon busts obtained from Gelder & Co. One was sold to Horace Harker, one to Josiah Brown of Laburnum Lodge, Laburnum Vale, Chiswick, and one to Mr. Sandeford of Lower Grove Road, Reading.
