Also called: Harding Brothers, of Kensington

Abstract written by a model

Harding Brothers is a Kensington picture and sculpture retailer. It obtains three Napoleon busts from Gelder & Co. and sells all three, respectively to Horace Harker, Josiah Brown, and Mr. Sandeford; it also sells Devine’s last Napoleon bust to Sandeford.

Harding Brothers is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
