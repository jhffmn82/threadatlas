Abstract written by a model

The sons of Boreas, Zetes and Calais, are destined to restrain the Harpies, drive them from Phineus, stop after Iris's oath, and later are killed by Heracles in Tenos.

There Phineus, son of Agenor, had his home by the sea, Phineus who

The sons of Boreas, Zetes and Calais, are destined by an oracle to restrain the Harpies. They drive the creatures away from Phineus but return after Iris swears that the Harpies will not come back.
