6. Ozma's Birthday Presents

Dorothy and the Wizard plan to obtain large wild monkeys from the Gillikin Country and use magic to shrink them to about three inches high. The Wizard will train them to dance and distribute pieces of cake before they are hidden inside the birthday cake.
