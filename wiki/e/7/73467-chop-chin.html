Chop-Chin in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Chop-Chin is a good twelve-year-old boy, Ly-Chee’s son and an apprentice to the Chief Cabinet-maker, who hopes to become a sweeper. When he learns his father must die, he undertakes a sacred mission: he drugs guards and priests, steals the Golden Dragon, enters the royal palace with the Celestial Footstool, hides in the Dragon to command Emperor Wah-Song, and escapes carrying the Footstool.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" And without further preface, she began as follows:-- CHAPTER II.

Chop-Chin is a twelve-year-old boy and the son of Ly-Chee. After learning that his father must die, he secretly goes to the Golden Dragon's temple, drugs the guards and priests with wine, steals the Golden Dragon, and escapes with it.

Ten o'clock of a dark night.

Chop-Chin carries the Celestial Footstool into the royal palace at night, using a secret door and staircase to reach a closet near the Celestial Bed-chamber. He hides inside the Golden Dragon and speaks as it to command Emperor Wah-Song, then carries the Footstool back out while pretending that the Emperor struck him with it.
