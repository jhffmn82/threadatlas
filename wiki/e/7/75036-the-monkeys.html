Abstract written by a model

The monkeys are forest-dwelling people and animals led by Rango the Gray Ape as chief of the forest monkey tribes. After fleeing giant soldiers, they return, recognize the giants as transformed friends and relatives, and gather. Twelve intelligent, good-tempered monkeys volunteer to accompany the Wizard of Oz to the Emerald City for Ozma's birthday. Temporarily three inches high and confined in a golden cage, they learn and perform the Wizard's tricks.
