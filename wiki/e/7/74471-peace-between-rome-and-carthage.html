peace between Rome and Carthage in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A peace opportunity honourable to Romans but dishonourable to Carthaginians ends when Attilius Regulus rejects it and demands unreasonable conditions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXIII.

Attilius Regulus could have concluded a peace that would have been honourable and advantageous to the Romans but base and dishonourable to the Carthaginians. He rejected that opportunity and instead demanded unreasonable conditions.
