Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Lucanians made peace with the Tarentines, joined Cleonymus in attacking the Metapontians, and later joined forces with other barbarians against him. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Lucanians invaded and defeated the Thurians, killed more than ten thousand Italians, accepted ransoms for survivors rescued by Leptines, and made peace with the Italians.
