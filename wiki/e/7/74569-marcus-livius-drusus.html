Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVI.

Marcus Livius Drusus was very young but was praised for his physical and mental qualities. He was eloquent, wealthy, faithful to his word, influential among the citizens, courageous, and regarded as the principal patron of the senate.
