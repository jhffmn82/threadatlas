Greek auxiliaries in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Greek auxiliaries were auxiliary soldiers serving in Alexander's army. Praised for valor, they received rewards, arrears, and travel provisions, were discharged, and volunteers to remain received three talents each.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

Alexander praised the Greek auxiliaries for their valor, paid each horseman a talent and each foot soldier ten minas, settled their arrears and travel provisions, and discharged them from further service. Those who chose to remain in the army received three talents each.
