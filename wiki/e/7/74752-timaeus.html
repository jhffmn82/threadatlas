Abstract written by a model

Timaeus, a severe critic of earlier historians, wrote a history that disparaged Agathocles after exile from Sicily, minimizing successes and exaggerating misfortunes; it was condemned as biased and unreliable.

Timaeus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FROM THE SAME..

Timaeus criticized earlier historians but was said to have forged many statements about Agathocles because Agathocles had banished him from Sicily. He minimized Agathocles’s successes, exaggerated his misfortunes, and repeatedly called him cowardly despite Agathocles’s military achievements; the text therefore condemns his history as biased and unreliable.
