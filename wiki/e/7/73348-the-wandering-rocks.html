the Wandering Rocks in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

The Wandering Rocks, also called Planctae and Cyaneae, are huge sea cliffs that clash in winds and block passage. Following Phineus’s advice, the Argonauts send a dove, then row through with Hera’s help; after their ship passes, the rocks stand still.

The Clashing Rocks are enormous rocks that collide under the force of the winds and block the sea passage. Following Phineus’s advice, the Argonauts send a dove through them; after its tail is clipped, they pass through with hard rowing and Hera’s help, and the rocks thereafter stand still.
