entity coverage and selection in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024

Entity coverage and selection uses Wikidata, considers only Wikipedia-linked entities with English names, and is described as aggressive, limited by incomplete Wikidata and authors’ experience.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Limitations

Entity coverage and selection are limited by the incompleteness of Wikidata and by requiring entities to have linked Wikipedia pages and at least an English name. The selection strategy is described as potentially aggressive and based partly on the authors’ experience with Wikidata and Wikipedia.
