The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian presents it through campaigns between Rome and Carthage in Sicily, Africa, and at sea, including the siege of Messana, the capture and destruction of Sicilian cities, the Roman defeat in Africa, and major naval disasters. The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian describes it as a twenty-four-year conflict between the Romans and Carthaginians that included Lilybaeum’s ten-year siege and ended in mutually concluded peace.
