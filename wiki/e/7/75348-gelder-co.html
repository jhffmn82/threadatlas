Gelder & Co.

Also called: Gelder, Gelder & Co., in Church Street, Stepney, Gelder & Co., of Stepney

Abstract written by a model

Gelder & Co. is a Stepney wholesale manufacturer of sculpture works, producing Napoleon busts. It supplied three casts to Morse Hudson and three to Harding Brothers, the two halves of a batch of six made in two plaster moulds. The manager reported no known differences among the casts. Beppo worked there and, while the six busts dried in the passage, concealed the pearl in one of them.

Gelder & Co. is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Gelder & Co. of Stepney supplied Morse Hudson and Harding Brothers with the relevant Napoleon busts. Its manager said the six casts were half of a batch, made in two plaster moulds, and that there was no known reason for them to differ from other casts.

When we met again next evening, Lestrade was furnished with much

Gelder & Co. manufactured the six plaster busts of Napoleon at its Stepney establishment. Beppo worked there, and the busts were drying in the passage when he concealed the pearl in one of them.
