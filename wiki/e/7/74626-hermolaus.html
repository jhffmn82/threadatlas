Hermolaus

Abstract written by a model

Hermolaus, son of Sopolides, is a Macedonian nobleman's son, king's attendant youth, and student of Callisthenes. Disgraced after killing a boar before Alexander, he joins and recruits Sostrates for a conspiracy against Alexander, confesses when captured, and is stoned to death.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUR IX.

Hermolaus is a Macedonian nobleman's son serving among the pages and studying philosophy under Callisthenes. After killing a wild boar before Alexander could do so, he is whipped publicly and deprived of his horse. He then persuades Sostrates to join him in plotting Alexander's assassination, confesses the conspiracy when captured, and is stoned to death with the other seized conspirators.
