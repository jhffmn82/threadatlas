Argus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Argus, elder-born son of Phrixus and son of Chalciope, supports Jason’s quest. He explains the Argonauts’ arrival to Aeetes, presents Jason as kin, and offers service against the Sauromatae. After Aeetes sets a challenge, Argus advises Jason and urges Chalciope to seek Medea’s magical aid, remaining at the palace.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Now the heroes apart in ambush, in a back-water of the river, were met

Argus explains to Aeetes why the sons of Phrixus and the Argonauts have come, presenting Jason as a kinsman and offering service against the Sauromatae. After Aeetes sets the challenge, Argus advises Jason to seek the aid of his mother and Medea, who knows magic taught by Hecate.

But straightway Aeetes held an assembly of the Colchians far aloof from

Argus goes to Aeetes’ palace and urges Chalciope to seek Medea’s aid. He remains in the palace while Chalciope speaks with Medea.
