Abstract written by a model

Gedrosia contains rude, savage whale-eating people and a barren, provisionless wilderness; Alexander marches through it, brings its inhabitants to submission easily, then follows the sea-coast through savage peoples and wilderness.

Gedrosia is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
