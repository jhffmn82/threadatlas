the Loons of Loonville in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The Loons of Loonville are round, light-gray, hairless, purple-eyed inhabitants confined to the clearing. They bounce and float, then attack and bind travelers. Though inflatable and repairable, they are vulnerable to thorns: punctures release their air and leave them helpless until mended and pumped up.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

The Loons are round, gray, puffy creatures with purple eyes who bounce as they move and can float upward. They attack and bind the travelers, but thorns puncture them and release their air, leaving them helpless until they are mended and pumped up again.
