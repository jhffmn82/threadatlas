Lower Gill Moor

Abstract written by a model

Lower Gill Moor is a large, desolate plain north of Priory School, stretching ten miles and sloping upward. It matters to the investigation because its bicycle-suitable paths, watercourse, morasses, and unexplained cow-tracks may hold clues. Holmes identifies it as likely ground, then crosses it with Watson returning to the school.

Lower Gill Moor is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
