Cleombrotus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cleombrotus, brother and successor of Agesipolis, was king of Lacedaemon and the Spartans for nine years. He urged Sphodrias to seize the Piraeus without the Ephors' consent, then, as Lacedaemonian general, marched against Thebes. Wounded many times, he was killed at Leuctra; his death helped cause his army's disorder and flight.

Cleombrotus, king of the Lacedaemonians, urged Sphodrias to attempt the surprise seizure of the Piraeus without the Ephors' consent. He was later killed when the Lacedaemonians were defeated at Leuctra.

CHAP. VL

Cleombrotus was appointed general of the Lacedaemonians and led their army toward Thebes. He was killed at Leuctra after receiving many wounds, and his death contributed to the disorder and flight of the Lacedaemonian army.

Cleombrotus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Spartan king who succeeded Agesipolis, led the Lacedæmonians against the Bœotians at Leuctra, and was killed early in battle.

CHAPTER VI.

Cleombrotus succeeded Agesipolis after Agesipolis died childless and led the Lacedæmonians against the Bœotians at Leuctra. He exposed himself too freely and was killed at the beginning of the battle.
