21. The College of Athletic Arts

The Senior Class of the Royal Athletic College seized Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E., and threw him into a river after he insisted that the students eat Square-Meal Tablets. The class was brought to the Emerald City so Ozma could decide how to punish its rebellion.
