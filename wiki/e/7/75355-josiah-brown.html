Josiah Brown in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Josiah Brown, owner of Laburnum Villa in Chiswick and a Harding Brothers Napoleon bust, receives Sherlock Holmes’s letter, locks his house, and waits inside while Holmes, Dr. Watson, and Inspector Lestrade watch outside.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Josiah Brown bought one of Harding Brothers’ Napoleon busts and lived at Laburnum Villa in Chiswick. After receiving Holmes’s note, he locked the doors of his house and waited for developments while Holmes, Dr. Watson, and Inspector Lestrade watched outside.
