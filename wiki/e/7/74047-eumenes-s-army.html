Eumenes's army

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Eumenes's soldiers, forces of Eumenes, his own army, the army, the enemy's army

Abstract written by a model

Eumenes's army was a force of infantry, cavalry, elephants, Argyraspides, and troops from several regions. It grew rapidly by recruitment to more than ten thousand foot and two thousand horse besides the Argyraspides and original troops, and was later reinforced by Macedonians remaining after Neoptolemus's defeat, giving it about twenty-eight thousand infantry and five thousand horse. Although Antigonus demanded Eumenes's surrender, the army remained obedient to the kings. Eumenes's sickness delayed it and discouraged the soldiers, but it marched toward Gabene and deployed about 35,000 foot, 6,100 horse, and 114 elephants. It routed Python's forces and the opposing phalanx, then suffered a reversal and refused to return to battle, choosing its carriages. In winter quarters it was dispersed, then gathered after a fire-signal stratagem and fought with 36,700 foot, 6,050 horse, and 114 elephants.
