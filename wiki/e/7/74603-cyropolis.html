Cyropolis in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cyropolis, the country’s greatest and largest city, built by Cyrus, had walls higher than the other seven cities and many barbarian soldiers. Alexander’s forces entered through its dry river channel, killed about eight thousand defenders, drove the rest into the castle, and compelled surrender after a day of thirst.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. III.

Cyropolis was the largest city and had been built by Cyrus, with higher walls and many defenders gathered inside. Alexander’s forces entered through the dry channel of the river, killed about eight thousand defenders, drove the remainder into the castle, and forced their surrender after one day because of thirst.
