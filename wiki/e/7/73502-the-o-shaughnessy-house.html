Abstract written by a model

The O'Shaughnessy house is a tall, dark, gloomy house with greenish window-panes and gaunt chimneys. Dr. Michael O'Shaughnessy brings Eileen there and guards her; Eily is held captive there. After Eily disappears, reptiles pursue the doctor through its passage and out.
