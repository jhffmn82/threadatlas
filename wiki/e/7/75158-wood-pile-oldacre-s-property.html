Abstract written by a model

A fire-damaged wood-pile containing charred remains, discolored trouser buttons, Oldacre's trousers, and rabbits is treated as where Oldacre's body was destroyed. Ashes are raked for evidence, but the supposed body is not Oldacre's; the contents may have been planted to explain blood and ashes.
