Alcetas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Alcetas, a Molossian commander and Perdiccas’s brother, fled defeat by Antigonus to Termessus, where betrayal led him to kill himself; its young men later buried his abused body.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

Alcetas was defeated by Antigonus in Pisidia and escaped with his life-guard, children, and Pisidian supporters to Termessus. When the elders of Termessus betrayed him, he killed himself rather than be captured, and the young men of the city later buried his abused body.
