care of the eyes in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Care of the eyes emphasizes avoiding direct bright light and strain, using sufficient light for reading, and placing light on the left while writing; it warns against sun-gazing, twilight reading, and crossing the eyes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Care of the eyes requires avoiding direct bright light, having enough light for reading, and positioning light on the left while writing. The eyes should not be strained by looking at the sun, reading in twilight, or imitating crossed eyes.
