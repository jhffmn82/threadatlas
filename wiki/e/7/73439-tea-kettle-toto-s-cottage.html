tea-kettle (Toto's cottage)

Also called: The kettle, copper nose, golden kettle, kettle, tea-kettle, the kettle, the tea-kettle, thing

Abstract written by a model

A remarkable, brightly burnished copper tea-kettle sits on Toto's cottage hearth, polished by Bruin and proud as it sings, steams, and reflects the fire. The raccoon fears it after it catches his paw and seems alive when its cover lifts. Later it duets with the fire during an attack, may spill boiling water on a robber, and ends broken apart on the floor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Toto's Merry Winter / CHAPTER I.

The brightly polished copper tea-kettle sings and sends out clouds of steam while boiling. The raccoon believes it is alive and fears it, especially when it lifts its cover and releases an extra puff of steam. It previously caught the raccoon's paw when he put it inside.

CHAPTER IX.

The kettle shines brightly because Bruin polished it and performs a duet with the fire. During the attack it may deliberately plunge from the hob and spill boiling water over the robber's legs. It ends up with its head separated from its body and lying on the floor, and the raccoon refuses to put it back together.
