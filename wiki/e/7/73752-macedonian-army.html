Abstract written by a model

The Macedonian army was trained by Alexander to obey him. In Asia it stayed loyal after Attalus was killed under Parmenio’s devotion, but withdrew from Pitane before Memnon. In Europe it was defeated by Greeks, retreated to Lamia, sought reinforcements, and thirty thousand Macedonians returned from Asia.

BOOK XVII.

Alexander trained the Macedonian army and brought it to obey his commands. In Asia, it remained loyal after Attalus was killed because Parmenio was devoted to Alexander, but it was forced to withdraw from the siege of Pitane when Memnon approached.
