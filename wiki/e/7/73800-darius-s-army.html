Darius's army

group · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Darius's army was an army within the Persian military force, including Persian infantry, cavalry, and chariots, drawn from many nations. About eight hundred thousand foot and two hundred thousand horse trained at Arbela, marched from Babylon toward Cilicia after leaving baggage at Damascus, and fought the Macedonians at Issus. It initially gained advantages, then was routed after Darius fled, suffering heavy losses and losing its camp, royal household, and tent spoils.

Darius's army is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. III.

Darius's army marched from Babylon toward Cilicia after leaving its heavy baggage and rabble at Damascus. At Issus it fought the Macedonians, suffered extensive casualties, and lost its camp and royal household.

But to return to the kings themselves : Darius with all his army

Darius's army was routed, causing Darius to flee. Its defeat enabled Alexander to take the rich spoils of Darius's tent.

Darius's army consisted of about eight hundred thousand infantry and two hundred thousand cavalry drawn from many nations. It trained at Arbela, advanced against Alexander, initially gained advantages on parts of the field, and then collapsed after Darius fled.
