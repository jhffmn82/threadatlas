Abstract written by a model

The three students are three men living on successive stories above the tutor’s room, all entered for the examination and possible suspects in the entry into Soames’s room. Holmes investigates their rooms and circumstances, then must choose among them; Daulat Ras is seen pacing, while Gilchrist and Miles McLaren remain unseen.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

The three students using the stair are Gilchrist, Daulat Ras, and Miles McLaren, and all three are entered for the examination. Holmes considers their rooms and circumstances while investigating who could have entered Soames’s room.

When we were out in the darkness of the quadrangle, we again

The three students remain the central set of possible suspects, and Holmes describes the problem as requiring a choice among them. The Indian student is seen pacing his room, while the other two are invisible from the quadrangle.
