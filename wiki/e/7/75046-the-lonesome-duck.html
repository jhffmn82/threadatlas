Abstract written by a model

The Lonesome Duck is the only duck in the Land of Oz, a uniquely colored, immortal bird living alone in its diamond palace a little way up the river, with food supplied by magic. It has no family, relations, friends, or occupation, dislikes everyone it meets, and is lonesome. Its simple magic serves its needs: when it cannot free Trot and Cap'n Bill, it performs an incantation to create each of them a comfortable, sturdy toadstool, then leaves.

15. The Lonesome Duck

The Lonesome Duck is a uniquely colored bird and the only duck in the Land of Oz, where it has a diamond palace and magically supplied food. It says it has no family, relations, or friends and dislikes the company of everyone it meets, though it has lived a long time and will live forever. It cannot free Trot and Cap'n Bill, but it performs an incantation that grows a comfortable, sturdy toadstool for each of them before leaving.
