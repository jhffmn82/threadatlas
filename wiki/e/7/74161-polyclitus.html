Polyclitus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Polyclitus, Seleucus’s lieutenant and fleet commander, commands Ptolemy’s hundred ships to Cyprus, defeats Antigonus’s forces near Cilicia, captures leaders and ships, then returns via Cyprus to Pelusium for reward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now, having related the things done throughout all Asia, we

Polyclitus, Seleucus's lieutenant, defeated Antigonus's land and naval forces near Cilicia. He captured Perilaus and Theodotus, took the enemy ships, and returned to Cyprus and then Pelusium, where Ptolemy rewarded him.
