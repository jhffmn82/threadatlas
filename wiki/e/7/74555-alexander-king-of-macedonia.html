Alexander, king of Macedonia in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander, king of Macedonia, aided the Aleuadas against Alexander the Pheraean by joining deserters and bringing forces to Larissa, captured Larissa and Cranon, promised the Thessalians restoration, then garrisoned and retained their cities.

Alexander, king of Macedonia, joined the deserters and brought his forces to Larissa to assist the Aleuadas against Alexander the Pheraean. He captured Larissa and Cranon, promised to restore the cities to the Thessalians, and then garrisoned and retained them.
