the theft of Glinda's Great Book of Records in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A mysterious theft from Glinda's castle occurs secretly while everyone sleeps: a powerful thief cuts the chains securing the heavy, locked, guarded Great Book of Records and removes it. Glinda expects the thief to be discovered and severely punished.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

The theft occurs secretly while everyone in Glinda's castle sleeps. The chains securing the Great Book of Records are cut with a sharp instrument, and the thief is believed to possess great power because the book was heavy, locked, and carefully guarded. Glinda believes the thief will eventually be discovered and severely punished.
