Corsica in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Corsica is a large, mountainous, wooded island with rivers, abundant box-trees, milk, honey, and flesh. Its sometimes bitter honey reflects its box-trees. More than thirty thousand barbarians inhabit it; they are just and humane, speak a strange language, and have useful bond-slaves.

Corsica is a large, mountainous and wooded island with rivers, abundant milk, honey, and flesh, and more than thirty thousand inhabitants. Its people are described as just and humane toward one another, while its inhabitants have a strange language and its bond-slaves are considered especially useful.
