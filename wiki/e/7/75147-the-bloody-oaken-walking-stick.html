Abstract written by a model

The bloody oaken walking-stick is John Hector McFarlane's walking-stick. He leaves it at Jonas Oldacre's house after Oldacre offers to keep it until his return. It is later found in Oldacre's bedroom with slight blood-stains on its handle. The newspaper identifies it as McFarlane's property, and its presence and marks make it a significant clue in the police theory against him.
