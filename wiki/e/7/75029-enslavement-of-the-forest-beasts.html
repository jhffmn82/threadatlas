Abstract written by a model

Enslavement of the forest beasts is Ruggedo's claimed plan that the people of Oz, including Ozma, will invade the Forest of Gugu, use ropes to capture and beat its beasts, and make them beasts of burden. He presents this threat to urge the beasts to conquer Oz first.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Ruggedo claims that the people of Oz intend to enslave the forest beasts and make them beasts of burden. This claim is used to encourage the beasts to conquer the people of Oz first.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

Ruggedo claims that Ozma and the Oz people plan to invade the Forest of Gugu, capture the beasts with ropes, beat them, and make them willing slaves. He says the Oz people have already started making ropes for this purpose.
