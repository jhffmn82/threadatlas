THE ADVENTURE OF THE SIX NAPOLEONS

Dr. Barnicot purchased two duplicate plaster casts of Devine's famous head of Napoleon, placing one in his Kennington Road hall and the other in his Lower Brixton Road surgery. Both are destroyed during separate nighttime intrusions, and they were made from the same mould as the bust destroyed at Morse Hudson's shop.
