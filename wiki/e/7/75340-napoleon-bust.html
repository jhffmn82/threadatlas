THE ADVENTURE OF THE SIX NAPOLEONS

A plaster Napoleon bust is destroyed at Morse Hudson's shop, and another bust of the same type is taken from Mr. Horace Harker's house after a nighttime intrusion. Harker's bust is later found broken into fragments in the garden of an empty house in Campden House Road.
