hidden room (Oldacre's house) in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A concealed hiding-place behind a partition door, lit by eave slits and stocked with furniture, food, water, books, and papers, enabling Oldacre to remain hidden.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Holmes smiled, and clapped Lestrade upon the shoulder.

The hidden room was concealed by a door in a partition six feet from the end of the passage and was lit by slits beneath the eaves. It contained furniture, food, water, books, and papers, allowing Oldacre to remain hidden.
