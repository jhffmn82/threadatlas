Oxyartes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Oxyartes, Roxana’s father, rebels against Alexander, shelters his wife and daughters in a rock refuge, and sees his young son captured after surrender.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP, V,

Oxyartes had revolted against Alexander and placed his wife and daughters in the rock as an apparently impregnable refuge. His nine-year-old son was also captured there after the defenders surrendered.
