Alexander’s restoration letter in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander’s restoration letter addresses Greek exiles, denying responsibility for their banishment and promising their return to their own countries except for those banished for outrageous crimes. It concludes by ordering Antipater to use force against opponents of the restoration.

Alexander’s restoration letter stated that he was not responsible for the exiles’ banishment but would bring them all back to their countries, except those banished for outrageous crimes. It ordered Antipater to use force against anyone who opposed their restoration.
