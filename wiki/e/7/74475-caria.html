Caria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Caria is a place adjoining Chersonesus. It was associated with Chersonesus and partly ruled by Cyrnus after his settlement, then Triopas built Triopium and gained Chersonesus and adjoining country. Later its coastal Grecian cities revolted, and Cimon stormed remaining cities, bringing Caria under his power.

Nireus, the son of Charops and Aglaia, was, in after times^

Caria was associated with Chersonesus and was partly ruled by Cyrnus after he settled there while searching for Io. Triopas later built Triopium in Caria and gained Chersonesus together with much of the adjoining country.

CHAP. XIII.

The Grecian cities on the coast of Caria revolted from the Persians when Cimon arrived. Cimon stormed the cities that retained their native inhabitants and Persian garrisons, bringing Caria under his power.
