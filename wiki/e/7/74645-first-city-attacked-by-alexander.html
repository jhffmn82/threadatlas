Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XII.

The first city attacked by Alexander resisted his initial advance but was driven back behind its walls. Its outer wall was taken the next day, its inner wall was then scaled, and the defenders fled toward the mountains; the city was subsequently leveled.
