people in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

People once thought alcohol and beer gave strength, but the chapter explains that grain’s food has mostly become alcohol. Spending money on liquor is linked with more sickness, poverty, badness, and wretchedness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

People used to think that alcohol made them strong. Some people also say that beer gives strength, but the chapter explains that the food part of the grain has mostly been turned into alcohol.
