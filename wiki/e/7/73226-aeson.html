  • In The Argonautica, Aeson is Jason’s father, lying in baleful old age and grieving and groaning over Jason’s departure.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Æson is Jason’s decrepit, aged father, near death, whom Medea restores to youth by replacing his old blood with magical juices after putting him into a deep sleep and cutting his throat.
  • In The Library, Aeson is threatened with death by Pelias when Pelias believes the Argonauts will not return; Aeson asks to take his own life and dies while offering a sacrifice and drinking bull’s blood.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Aeson is the king of a neighboring Thessalian kingdom and Jason’s aged, infirm father, rejuvenated by Medea and made youthful after she cuts his throat and fills him with caldron juices.

Æson in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Æson is Jason’s father, decrepit, aged, and near death. When Jason seeks help, Medea puts him into deep sleep, cuts his throat, and replaces his old blood with magical juices. Her magic or potion restores his hair, vigor, blood, youthful appearance, spirits, and strength, returning him to youth.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Æson is near death, worn out by old age, when Jason asks Medea to help him. Medea puts him into a deep sleep, cuts his throat, replaces his old blood with magical juices, and restores his hair, vigor, blood, and youthful appearance.

notes .. notes

Æson is the aged man whose youth Medea restores. The explanation suggests that his renewal may have resulted from a transfusion of youthful blood or from a potion that gave him new spirits and strength.

Aeson in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Aeson, king of a neighboring Thessalian kingdom and Jason’s aged, infirm father, is rejuvenated by Medea, becoming youthful after she cuts his throat and fills him with caldron juices.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MEDEA AND AESON

Aeson is an aged and infirm father whose life Jason wishes to prolong. Medea cuts his throat and fills him with the juices of her caldron, after which his hair, beard, blood, strength, and appearance become youthful.
