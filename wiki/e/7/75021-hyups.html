Hyups in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Hyups are people living atop Mount Munch, growing crops, feeding flocks, and maintaining scattered houses; steep sides keep them and the Munchkins largely apart.

1 section; each heading opens the text

1. Mount Munch

The Hyups live on the saucer-shaped top of Mount Munch, where they grow crops, feed flocks, and maintain scattered houses. They seldom descend the mountain, and the Munchkins seldom climb it because the sides are too steep.
