Also called: Sugar

Abstract written by a model

Sugar is a food and a fat-making food, found in ripe fruit-juices and coming from sugar-cane, maple-trees, beets, and starch. Starch changes into sugar as fruit ripens, when grain sprouts, and when saliva acts on food; then sugar can mix with blood and help feed the body. It feeds sprouting grain through roots and stems, but alone cannot prevent starvation. In standing grape-juice or apple-juice, or with yeast, it changes into alcohol and gas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

_] CHAPTER IV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Sugar is found in ripe fruit-juices. When grape-juice or apple-juice is left standing, its sugar changes into alcohol and bubbles of gas.

] CHAPTER V. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Sugar is formed from the starch in grain when the grain begins to sprout. The brewer preserves the sugar and adds yeast to help change it into gas and alcohol.

] CHAPTER XI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Sugar is a fat-making food, but a person would starve if fed only on sugar. The starch in unripe fruit and other foods must change into sugar before it can mix with the blood and help feed the body.

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Sugar is produced when saliva changes starch in food. A piece of dry cracker tastes sweet after it has been chewed because its starch is being changed into sugar.
