blood

object · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 12 connections
Also called: The blood, bad blood, blood in the veins, bright and pure again, dark, impure blood, gray fluid, hot blood, pure, red blood, the blood

Abstract written by a model

Blood is a body fluid made from good food. It takes prepared food from the intestines or stomach to the heart, which pumps it through the lungs and throughout the body. As it flows through arteries and thousands of little branches, it carries food and body heat to every part, leaving material needed for making and mending body parts. Work inside the body warms it; faster flow brings more heat. Blood also gathers and carries away worn-out matter. After collecting impurities, it becomes dull and bluish and returns by veins to the heart. The lungs use fresh air to purify it, making it bright and pure again, after which it is sent to the heart and pumped out once more. It must be good and pure for the brain, though alcohol or tobacco can poison it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The blood takes prepared food from the intestines and carries it to the heart. The heart pumps it with the blood through the lungs and throughout the body, where it provides material for making and mending body parts.

CHAPTER XIV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Blood carries food from the stomach to every part of the body and leaves each part what it needs. It must be good and pure for the brain, but alcohol or tobacco can poison it.

CHAPTER XV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The blood carries food throughout the body and gathers worn-out matter. After collecting impurities, it becomes dull and bluish in the veins. The lungs prepare it to return to the heart pure and red.

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The blood is pumped out of the heart to carry food to every part of the body and to take away worn-out matter. The lungs use fresh air to make the dark, impure blood bright and pure again.

CHAPTER XVIII. / WHAT MAKES US WARM? .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The blood is warmed by work inside the body and carries heat to every part of it. Faster blood flow brings more heat and makes a person feel warmer.
