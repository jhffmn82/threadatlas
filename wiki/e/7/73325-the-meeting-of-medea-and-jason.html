the meeting of Medea and Jason in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The meeting of Medea and Jason occurs privately near the shrine of Hecate after Argus and Mopsus arrange Jason’s approach. Overwhelmed by love, they move from silence to promises and plans. They part gently, intending to meet again; Jason leaves joyful, while Medea returns home distressed by her counsel’s consequences.

Medea and Jason meet alone near the shrine of Hecate after Argus and Mopsus arrange for Jason to approach her privately. They are initially speechless and overwhelmed by love, then exchange promises and plans before separating.

And now her handmaids, glancing at them from a distance, were grieving

Jason and Medea part after speaking gently and promising to meet again. Jason leaves joyfully, while Medea returns home distressed, grieving, and troubled by the consequences of her counsel.
