Mazaeus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Mazaeus is a Persian cavalry general commanding Persians, Cadusians, and Scythians. Sent to guard the Tigris crossing, he abandons it as impassable and devastates the region. In battle he supports chariots, attacks the Macedonian wing, and raids the enemy camp.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

As soon as Darius had intelligence of his coming, he got all his

Mazaeus was sent to guard the passage over the Tigris but abandoned that duty after judging the river impassable and instead destroyed the surrounding country. In the battle, he commanded Persian cavalry, supported the advance of the chariots, attacked the Macedonian wing, and sent Cadusian and Scythian horsemen around the enemy's wings to raid the Macedonian camp.
