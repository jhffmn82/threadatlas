Panch(a)ia

Also called: Panchaia, Panchaian, Panchaisi

Abstract written by a model

Panch(a)ia is the largest of certain islands in the ocean reached by Evemerus. Its religious inhabitants and mountain-top temple of Jupiter Triphylius matter most; a golden pillar there records Uranus, Saturn, and Jupiter’s deeds.

Panch(a)ia is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
