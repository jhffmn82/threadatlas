Dionysius’s Sicilian war against Carthage

Abstract written by a model

Dionysius’s war in Sicily against Carthage began with a large attack that took Selinus, Entella, and Eryx, stalled at Lilybaeum, and ended with heavy ship losses at Eryx.

CHAP. VIIL

Dionysius attacked Carthaginian territory with thirty thousand foot, three thousand horse, and three hundred ships, taking Selinus, Entella, and Eryx and besieging Lilybaeum. He raised the siege when the garrison resisted, then lost most of the ships stationed at Eryx after the Carthaginians unexpectedly entered the harbor.
