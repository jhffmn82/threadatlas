city's disappearance and reappearance in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A mysterious city's apparent disappearance and reappearance confuses and discourages approaching travelers, yet they persist in following it and gradually draw nearer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The city disappears whenever the travelers approach and reappears to the left, behind them, or at another point of the compass. Although this repeatedly confuses and discourages them, they continue following it and gradually draw nearer.
