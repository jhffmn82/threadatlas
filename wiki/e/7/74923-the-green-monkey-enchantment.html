the Green Monkey enchantment in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The Green Monkey enchantment, placed on Woot, cannot be removed by fairy or yookoohoo magic; its form can be exchanged, permanently forcing any recipient to remain a Green Monkey.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

The Green Monkey enchantment placed on Woot is different from the others and cannot be removed by magic known to fairies or yookoohoos. The form may be transferred or exchanged with another person, but whoever receives it would be forced to remain a Green Monkey.
