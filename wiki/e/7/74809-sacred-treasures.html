sacred treasures in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The sacred treasures in Apollo's temple were initially untouched by Philomelus, who used Delphian estates to fund soldiers. He later rifled them to raise mercenary pay and build a large army. They included gold and silver offerings valued at more than ten thousand talents.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VII.

The sacred treasures were initially left untouched by Philomelus, who financed his soldiers from the estates of leading Delphians. He later rifled the treasures to offer higher pay to mercenaries, enabling him to assemble a large army.

CHAP. X.

The sacred treasures included gold tiles dedicated by Croesus, three hundred golden bowls, a golden lion and woman, and other silver objects. The text estimates the gold at four thousand talents and the silver at more than six thousand talents, making the total exceed ten thousand talents.
