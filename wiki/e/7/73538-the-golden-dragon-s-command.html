Ten o'clock of a dark night.

The Golden Dragon's command requires Emperor Wah-Song to pardon Ly-Chee, give him twenty bags of ducats, appoint him Chief Sweeper for life, and appoint Chop-Chin as Second Sweeper. The Emperor agrees because he is terrified by the Dragon's threats.
