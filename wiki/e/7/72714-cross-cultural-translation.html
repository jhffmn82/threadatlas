cross-cultural translation

Also called: cross-cultural translation of texts containing entity names, the problem of cross-cultural translation of texts containing entity names

Abstract written by a model

Cross-cultural translation is a problem centered on translating texts containing entity names whose culturally specific references can differ across languages, sometimes requiring changes beyond transliteration or through transcreation. It matters because the area lacks large-scale, high-quality benchmarks that expose these challenges. The work addresses the problem through resources and methods: it introduces XC-Translate, a benchmark for short, relatively simple surrounding texts with entity-name differences, and KG-MT, a system intended to capture cross-language and cross-cultural differences. It concludes that challenges vary by language and that broader language coverage is needed for robust, generalizable systems.
