the revolver in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The revolver is found on the study floor between Hilton Cubitt and Elsie Cubitt at Riding Thorpe Manor. Two barrels are discharged and two cartridges remain; its ejector expelled a third cartridge into the garden. A window-sash bullet hole shows it fired three shots.

The revolver is found on the study floor midway between Hilton Cubitt and Elsie Cubitt, with two barrels discharged and two cartridges remaining. Holmes finds the ejected third cartridge in the garden and uses the bullet hole in the window-sash to establish that a third shot was fired.
