RARG

method · 1 work · 27 quoted facts · 16 connections
Also called: RARG retriever, evidence-driven retrieval augmented response generation (RARG), proposed method, retrieval augmented response generation, retrieval augmented response generation for online misinformation

Abstract written by a model

RARG is an evidence-driven retrieval augmented response generation method and framework for countering online misinformation. It begins by identifying misinformation and collecting supporting evidence from scientific sources through a two-stage evidence-retrieval process. It then uses response generation, including reward estimation and PPO-based fine-grained reinforcement learning, with an actor model after reinforcement learning, to produce evidence-backed counter-responses that are factual, polite, and clearly refute the input misinformation. In experiments on COVID-19, including in-domain and cross-domain settings, its retriever outperforms evaluated baselines, including E5 on top-1 retrieval score and BM25 on Recall@5, while its generator outperforms GPT-3.5 and other baseline generation methods overall and especially in refutation. It performs robustly across contrastive-loss hyperparameter values up to 0.5. Limitations are the lack of human evaluation, possible difficulty in substantially different domains, and unreliable evidence sources.

RARG in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where RARG first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Retrieval augmented response generation for online misinformation, or RARG, collects supporting evidence from scientific sources and generates counter-misinformation responses from that evidence. It includes evidence collection and response generation stages and consistently outperforms baselines in experiments.

Introduction

Retrieval augmented response generation is proposed as an evidence-driven framework for generating counter-responses against online misinformation. It combines efficient document retrieval with response generation based on collected supporting facts.

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

It introduces a retrieval-augmented response generation framework with two-stage evidence retrieval and fine-grained reinforcement learning for constructing evidence-based counter-responses against misinformation.

opening

RARG uses evidence retrieval, response generation, reward estimation, and PPO-based reinforcement learning to produce counter-misinformation responses.

Experiments / Experiment Design .. Response Generation Results

RARG is an evidence-driven retrieval-augmented response generation framework for countering online misinformation. Its retriever outperforms the evaluated baselines on all reported Check-COVID metrics, and its response generator achieves strong in-domain and cross-domain results.

Limitations

RARG is introduced for evidence-based counter-response generation against online misinformation. Its performance has not been assessed through human evaluations, and its applicability may be limited when test domains differ substantially from COVID or when evidence sources are unreliable.
