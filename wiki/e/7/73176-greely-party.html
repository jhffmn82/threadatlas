Greely party in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The Greely party was a group of twenty-six men in an Arctic ordeal. After months of starvation and freezing in the Arctic seas, nineteen died; seven were rescued alive, though one soon died.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII. / WHAT MAKES US WARM? .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The Greely party consisted of twenty-six men who were rescued from the Arctic seas after months of starving and freezing. Nineteen died, seven were found alive, and one of those seven died soon afterward.
