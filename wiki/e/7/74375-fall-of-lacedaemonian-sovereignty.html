1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

The fall of Lacedaemonian sovereignty over Greece was foretold, according to the account, by signs including the Fiery Beam comet. The Lacedaemonians soon suffered a great defeat and unexpectedly lost their dominion.
