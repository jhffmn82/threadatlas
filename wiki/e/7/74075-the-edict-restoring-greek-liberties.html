the edict restoring Greek liberties

object · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: This decree, decree, decree in writincr, decree restoring Greek liberties, the edict divulged by himself, the late edict

Abstract written by a model

The decree restores democracy, peace, and Philip’s and Alexander’s governmental order to the Greeks. It recalls exiles and restores their franchises and liberties, while excluding named groups and Megalopolis’s exiles. It restores Samos to the Athenians and assigns Polyperchon management of the decree’s matters and all other matters.

Cassander, though he was excluded from the chief command of

The decree restoring Greek liberties promised the governmental forms previously enjoyed under Philip and Alexander, recalled many exiles, restored estates and civic franchises, and canceled opposing decrees except in specified cases. It also addressed Athens, Oropus, and Samos, and required the Greeks not to take up arms against the kings.
