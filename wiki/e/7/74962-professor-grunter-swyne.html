Professor Grunter Swyne in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Professor Grunter Swyne, a professor of Cabbage Culture and Corn Perfection, lives in a tiny blue Munchkin dwelling with Mrs. Squealina Swyne and has nine piglets. Known as dangerous to butchers, he files his teeth; in Chapter Twenty, he aids harmless travelers and recounts the Wizard educating his piglets.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty

Professor Grunter Swyne is identified as a professor of Cabbage Culture and Corn Perfection. He judges that the travelers are harmless, allows Woot to eat from the garden and the Scarecrow to take straw, and explains that the Wizard of Oz took in and educated his nine piglets.
