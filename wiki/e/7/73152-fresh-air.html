Abstract written by a model

Fresh air enables lungs to work and purify blood; it is needed fresh and plentiful, but waste matter breathed out poisons it, and when it cannot enter a room, people may feel dull, sleepy, or have headache.

CHAPTER XV. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Fresh air enables the lungs to do their work, while the waste matter breathed out by the lungs poisons the air. Close rooms can make a person dull and sleepy or cause a headache, while fresh air can make the person feel better.
