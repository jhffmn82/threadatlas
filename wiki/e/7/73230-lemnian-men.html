Lemnian men in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Lemnian men reject lawful wives for Thracian captive maids; Lemnian women slay them in the massacre, while surviving men are said to dwell on snowy Thracian tilths.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Here the whole of the men of the people together had been ruthlessly

The Lemnian men rejected their lawful wives and took captive maids brought from Thrace. The Lemnian women slew all the men, including those with the captives, and the surviving men are said to dwell on the snowy tilths of Thrace.
