Abstract written by a model

Soldiers belonging to Agathocles's faction, gathered from Morgantium, inland cities, and veterans of war against the Carthaginians, became enraged after accusations against the six hundred, killed their leaders, plundered opponents, and carried out the massacre at Syracuse.

AT the time when Demogenes niled as chief magistrtte at Athens,

Agathocles gathered soldiers from Morgantium, other inland cities, and men who had served with him against the Carthaginians. After he accused the six hundred of plotting against him, the soldiers became enraged, killed their leaders, and plundered the six hundred and their supporters, helping to carry out the massacre at Syracuse.
