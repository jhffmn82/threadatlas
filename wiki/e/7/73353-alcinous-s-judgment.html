Abstract written by a model

is_based_on Medea's marital status [virginity, marriage, or pregnancy] Alcinous's judgment is that Medea must be returned to Aeetes if she is still a maiden, but must remain with her husband if she has entered a marriage bed or is carrying a child. After Jason and Medea marry, he proclaims and firmly upholds this judgment despite the Colchians' requests and Aeetes' possible anger.
