1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIII.

Meros was the mountain overlooking Nysa. It was said to have received its name, meaning a thigh, because Bacchus was fabled to have been cherished in his father's thigh; Alexander later visited it to see its ivy, laurel, and shady groves.
