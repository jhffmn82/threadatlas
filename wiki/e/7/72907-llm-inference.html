Abstract written by a model

In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, LLM inference can be accelerated through GPU optimization and specialized inference libraries, with optimized models processing requests faster than non-optimized models. In MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation, LLM inference is the focus of experiments using retrieved external documents, whose RAG workflow contributes to carbon emissions.
