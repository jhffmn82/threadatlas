Forest of Gugu in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Forest of Gugu, also called Gugu Forest and the Great Forest of Gugu, is a vast, great tangle of trees and the largest forest in Oz. It is described as being in the central western Gillikin Country and in the Munchkin Country, with rugged mountains adjoining its east side. Named for and ruled by Gugu the Leopard King under the Laws of the Forest, it houses many kinds of beasts, including most of Oz's wild and biggest beasts. Its clearing hosts important councils and monkey sentinels watch its edge. Ruggedo and Kiki Aru enter seeking support; troublemakers violate the Laws, leading Gugu to call a council. Later Kiki transforms several visitors and Gugu, while the Glass Cat follows the forest's paths. The Wizard and Dorothy are thereafter welcomed as friends of the beasts.

4 sections; each heading opens the text

7. The Forest of Gugu

Gugu Forest is a vast tangle of trees in the central western part of the Gillikin Country, adjoining rugged mountains to the east. It is home to most of the wild beasts in Oz, and Ruggedo and Kiki Aru enter it to seek support for their plans.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

The Forest of Gugu is the setting for trouble caused by Chipo the Wild Boar, Arx the Giraffe, Tirrip the Kangaroo, a giant porcupine, and a chimpanzee. Its Laws are violated, prompting Gugu the Leopard King to call a council.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

The Forest of Gugu is the home of many kinds of beasts ruled by Gugu the Leopard. Its great clearing is used for important meetings, and its edge is watched by monkey sentinels.

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

The Great Forest of Gugu is the place where Kiki transforms the Wizard, Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, and Gugu the King. The Glass Cat knows the forest's paths and reaches it while searching for the Wizard.

the Great Gillikin Forest in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Great Gillikin Forest, Oz’s nearest wilderness, holds dangerous beasts and unknown areas; in Chapter Fifteen, a rescue expedition enters and follows animal paths under the Lion’s leadership.

Chapter Fifteen

The Great Gillikin Forest is described as the nearest thing to a wilderness in Oz, with many dangerous beasts and areas unknown even to the Cowardly Lion. The rescue expedition enters it and travels along animal paths under the Lion's leadership.
