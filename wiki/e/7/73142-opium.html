opium in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Opium is a popular narcotic poison made from poppy juice. It affects the human body, life, and character, poisoning the body alongside alcohol and tobacco. It is used in medicines and may be put into soothing-syrups; a text addresses it in a separate chapter.
