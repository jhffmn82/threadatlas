Abstract written by a model

Lady Hilda’s theft is an internationally consequential removal of a political document, potentially leading to war. To recover her indiscreet letter and prevent her husband from seeing it, she takes an impression of his key, obtains a duplicate from Eduardo Lucas, opens the despatch-box, and removes the paper between 7:30 and 11:30 that evening. Holmes initially suspects it was quickly passed to an international agent, while the incident is linked to Lucas’s death.
