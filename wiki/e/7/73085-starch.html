Also called: Starch, The starch, starch-box

Abstract written by a model

Starch is a food found in bread and all kinds of grain. It changes into sugar when seeds sprout, supporting beer from barley, and during chewing saliva changes it into sugar before it can enter the blood.

starch is in one work on this site, Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

] CHAPTER V. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Starch is found in all kinds of grain. During sprouting, starch changes into sugar, and the brewer uses this change to make beer from barley.

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Starch in food needs to be changed into sugar before it can be taken into the blood. Saliva changes starch into sugar during chewing.
