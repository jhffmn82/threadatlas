Abstract written by a model

Ptolemy Physcon is king of Egypt for fifteen years. Beginning by executing alleged plotters, banishing others on invented charges, and confiscating estates, he rules cruelly, receives Roman diplomatic gifts, and is hated by Egyptians; eventually Hierax takes over government amid threatened soldier revolt.

98. Ptolemy Physcon, the brother of Philometor, began hb

Ptolemy Physcon began his reign by executing people accused of plotting against him, banishing others on invented charges, and confiscating their estates. He was hated for his cruelty, murders, filthy lusts, childish speeches, intemperance, and deformed body; his general Hierax eventually took over the government when soldiers threatened to revolt.
