CHAPTER V.

The treasure-chamber is protected by a key that King Sligo keeps first in his waistcoat pocket and later under his pillow. The High Cellarer enters it after finding the key on the king's table, steals gold and jewels, and locks the door with the key in his pocket when summoned before the king.
