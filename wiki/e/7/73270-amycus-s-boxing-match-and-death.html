Amycus’s boxing match and death in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Amycus’s boxing match with Polydeuces ends when Polydeuces evades Amycus’s blow and kills him with a strike above the ear. Amycus’s death triggers a Bebrycian attack on the Argonauts, assaults on Bebrycian territory, and helps end Bebrycian oppression of the Mariandyni.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK II

Amycus dies when Polydeuces evades his descending blow and strikes him above the ear, breaking the bones inside his head. His death causes the Bebrycians to attack the Argonauts, while Lycus and the Mariandyni assault Bebrycian territory.
