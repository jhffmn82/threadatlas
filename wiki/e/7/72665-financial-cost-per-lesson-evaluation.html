Abstract written by a model

Financial cost per lesson evaluation measures GPT API expenses from input prompt/transcript tokens and output GPT-generated tokens. The study compares GPT-3.5 Turbo and GPT-4 Turbo across prompting strategies for real-time, large-scale tutor assessment. It begins with token-based calculation, then compares models and strategies, finding GPT-4 Turbo approximately ten times costlier than GPT-3.5 Turbo and the Retrieval-Augmented Generator prompt lowest-cost for both.

Abstract .. 1. Introduction

The study reports on the financial considerations of using GPT models for real-time, large-scale tutor assessment. It compares GPT-3.5 and GPT-4, and finds that the Retrieval-Augmented Generator prompt has lower financial costs than the other evaluated strategies.

opening .. 2.4. Evaluation Metrics

Financial cost was evaluated by recording input tokens from prompts and transcripts and output tokens from GPT-generated text. Costs for GPT-3.5 Turbo and GPT-4 Turbo were calculated using GPT API prices.

opening .. 4. Conclusion

The financial cost per lesson evaluation is compared across GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-4 Turbo, and several prompting strategies. GPT-4 Turbo is approximately 10 times more expensive than GPT-3.5 Turbo, while RAG has the lowest cost for both models.
