Abstract written by a model

Yoxley Old Place is a country house in Kent, seven miles from Chatham and far from the railway. It is Professor Coram’s home and the setting of Willoughby Smith’s murder. The investigation examines its garden path, back door, study, and cocoanut-matted corridors, then reveals a concealed room behind a bookcase, where Anna hides after stealing documents and killing the first secretary until Holmes finds her.
