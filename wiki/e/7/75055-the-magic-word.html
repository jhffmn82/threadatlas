Abstract written by a model

The Magic Word, Pyrzqxgl as pronounced by Kiki Aru, is a powerful but simple magic used in Bini Aru's secret method of transformation. Its effect depends on correct pronunciation: Bini and Kiki can use it, while Ruggedo fails when saying it incorrectly. Kiki learns it from instructions, changes himself into a bird, keeps it hidden from Ruggedo, and repeatedly transforms others, including the Wizard, Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, Gugu, monkeys, and beast magicians. After overhearing and remembering Kiki's pronunciation, the Wizard uses the word to make monkeys giants, change Trot and Cap'n Bill into bumblebees, restore them, and return enchanted nuts to their natural forms. It can transform people and restore transformed beings to their former forms, sizes, or natural shapes.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

1. Mount Munch

Pyrzqxgl is the mystical word used with Bini Aru's transformation method. Bini Aru could pronounce it correctly, and Kiki Aru learned the pronunciation from the instructions and used it to change into a bird.

4. Conspirators

The magic word enables Kiki Aru's transformations. Kiki keeps it secret, preventing Ruggedo from learning it when they become eagles.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

The magic word Pyrzqxgl is whispered by Kiki to cause transformations. He uses it repeatedly to change the Wizard, Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, Ruggedo, the Hungry Tiger, and Gugu.

14. The Wizard Learns the Magic Word

The Magic Word is spoken by Kiki Aru to transform monkeys into giants and is overheard by the Wizard of Oz. The Wizard can use it successfully after remembering Kiki's pronunciation, while Ruggedo fails because he pronounces it incorrectly.

19. Dorothy and the Bumble Bees

The Magic Word transforms people into other forms and restores them to their former shapes and sizes. The Wizard uses it to change Trot and Cap'n Bill into bumblebees and then back again.

23. The Fountain of Oblivion

The Magic Transformation Word is the powerful word used to restore the two enchanted nuts to their natural forms. The Wizard says that its power depends not only on the word itself but also on how it is pronounced, and he uses it in the form "Pyrzqxgl."
