Syracusan democracy

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: democracy, democracy of Syracuse, democratical government, free city, liberty, the democracy
  • The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books describes the democracy of Syracuse as restored after Thrasybulus departed, with peace and prosperity lasting three hundred years until the reign of Dionysius.
  • The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian describes the Syracusan democracy as restored or defended in Agathocles's public claims, later used by him to gain authority before establishing absolute command, and restored after his death.

Syracusan democracy in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The democracy was restored or defended in Agathocles's public claims after the exiles returned to Syracuse. He swore that he would not harm it, declared that he would protect it, and used this promise to gain authority before establishing his own absolute command. The Syracusan democracy was restored after Agathocles's death. The Syracusans confiscated Agathocles's goods and estate and broke down his statues.
