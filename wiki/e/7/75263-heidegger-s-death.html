Abstract written by a model

Heidegger’s death is a killing near the road, caused by a savage blow. He dies after falling or being forced from his bicycle and suffering a severe head injury; blood is found on the track and gorse, though the immediate circumstances are initially unclear. It is later established that Reuben Hayes struck him with a stick while fleeing with Lord Saltire. The death prompts James Wilder’s confession and Holmes’s pursuit of Hayes.

Heidegger's death is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
