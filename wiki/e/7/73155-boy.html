boy in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

A boy is advised not to use tobacco while growing. Smoking first makes boys sick, though some continue because they think it is manly and become used to it. Tobacco weakens body and mind and prevents him from becoming as large and fine-looking as he otherwise could.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

A boy who uses tobacco while growing is said to make every part of his body less strong than it otherwise would be. A boy who smokes cannot become as large and fine-looking a man as he would have become without smoking.

CHAPTER VIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Boys who first try smoking are almost always made sick. They may continue because they think smoking is manly, eventually becoming used to it.
