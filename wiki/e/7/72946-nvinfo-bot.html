NVInfo Bot is an enterprise chatbot in early access testing that answers questions about enterprise content and complements intranet search. It uses enterprise knowledge from multiple repositories, handling about 500 million documents totaling more than 7 TB across diverse formats while enforcing document access controls. Its stack includes LangChain, a vendor vector database, and a custom web UI. It evolved from prompt-based guardrails to Nemo Guardrails for query and response pre- and post-processing, with retrieval-time sensitive-data filtering, classification, and enterprise-policy enforcement.

CASE STUDY

NVInfo Bot answers questions about enterprise content and complements intranet search. It handles approximately 500 million documents exceeding 7 TB, supports diverse data formats, and enforces document access controls.

SECURING RAG-BASED CHATBOTS (S) .. 7.1

NVInfo bot implemented sensitive data guardrails and used sensitive data filtering and classification capabilities from its vector search solution to automatically filter sensitive data during retrieval. It initially used many guardrails in LLM prompts, but later implemented them during query and response pre- and post-processing with Nemo Guardrails.
