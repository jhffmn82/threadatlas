Bactria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Bactria is a country entered by Alexander against Bessus, who had raised an army; after its people were subdued, Alexander left commanders and forces to guard it, enforce obedience, and suppress revolt.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX.

Alexander marched into Bactria after hearing that Bessus had usurped the crown and raised a numerous army.

SUP, V,

Alexander left Polyperchon, Attalus, Gorgias, and Meleager in Bactria to guard the country, keep the subdued people obedient, and subdue those who had fallen away from their allegiance.

SUP. XII.

Alexander left Bactria with his army and left Amyntas there with 3,500 horse and 10,000 foot.

Bactria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Bactria is a strong, wealthy country with many large, populous cities and a principal city of the same name. Oxyartes mustered four hundred thousand men before Ninus subdued the province and seized its wealth. Later Semiramis gathered her army there and sent boats overland toward the Indus.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK IL

Bactria was a strong country with many large and populous cities, including a principal city also called Bactria. Its king Oxyartes raised four hundred thousand men, but Ninus eventually subdued the province and took its wealth.
