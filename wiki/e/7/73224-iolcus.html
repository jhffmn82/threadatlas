Also called: Iolcos, Ioleus, Panachaean land, Pelasgian Iolcus, city, city Aesonis, my city, the city called by my father’s name, ᾿Ιωλκὸν
  • In The Argonautica, Iolcus is a city, the heroes’ homeland, Jason’s home in Haemonia, and a royal center from which the voyage begins and to which Jason is expected to return with the golden fleece.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Iolcos is the Thessalian city whose throne was taken from Æson by his brother Pelias, and from which Pelias seeks to remove Jason through the expedition.
  • In The Library, Iolcus is ruled by Pelias and receives the Argonauts and the fleece; after Pelias dies, Acastus expels Jason and Medea.

Iolcus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Iolcus is a city and the heroes’ homeland, Jason’s home in Haemonia and a royal center. The voyage begins there, with the ship leaving nearby Pagasae. Jason is expected to return to Iolcus with the golden fleece, aided by Medea; she asks to come there, and he promises her honor in Hellas and a shared bridal chamber.

Iolcus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Iolcus, ruled by Pelias, receives the Argonauts and fleece; after Pelias dies, Acastus expels Jason and Medea.

The Argonauts return to Iolcus after completing their voyage in four months. Jason gives the fleece to Pelias there, and later Pelias’s death leads Acastus to expel Jason and Medea from Iolcus.
