Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

technology · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: KG, knowledge graphs, multilingual knowledge graph, multilingual knowledge graphs

Abstract written by a model

Multilingual Knowledge Graphs provide lexical and factual information about millions of entities in many languages, including names, aliases, and descriptions. Easier to edit and frequently updated for real-world changes, they serve as KG-MT’s retrieval source for retrieval-augmented generation. KG-MT retrieves and integrates their information to improve entity-aware translation, especially for texts containing entity names, across backbones and language pairs.

Multilingual Knowledge Graphs is in one work on this site, Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
