Jason’s nocturnal sacrifice in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Jason’s nocturnal sacrifice is a secret rite for Hecate Brimo. He goes to a deserted meadow, bathes in a sacred river, wears Hypsipyle’s robe, offers a ewe with milk and mingled libations, calls the goddess, and retreats in fear at her terrifying arrival.

Jason goes secretly to a deserted meadow, bathes in the sacred river, dons a dark robe, and sacrifices a ewe with milk and mingled libations. He calls on Hecate Brimo, then retreats in fear when the goddess arrives with serpents, torches, and hellhounds.
