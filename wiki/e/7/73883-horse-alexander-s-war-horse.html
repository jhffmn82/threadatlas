Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

Alexander's best horse had been given to him by Demaratus the Corinthian and had carried him in all his battles in Asia. It recognized Alexander, knelt for him to mount, and was captured by the barbarians in the Mardian country before being restored.
