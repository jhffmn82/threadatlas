Abstract written by a model

Bocchus, African king of Libya, rejects war with Rome, seeks pardon and alliance through Marius, and helps seize Jugurtha to secure safety and avoid Roman punishment.

23. Ambassadors came to Rome both from the yoonger and the

Bocchus, king of Libya, rebukes those who urge war against the Romans and sends commissioners to Marius seeking pardon and a league. He helps Marius seize Jugurtha, thereby buying his own safety and escaping Roman punishment.
