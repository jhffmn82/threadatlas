Abstract written by a model

Godolphin Street is an old-fashioned, secluded row of eighteenth-century houses in Westminster, near Whitehall Terrace. It contains Eduardo Lucas’s London house at number 16, including a carpet hiding-place in his room. Lady Hilda brings Lucas a political document there in exchange for her letter, and he hides it when someone approaches. Later Lucas is found murdered there; a woman thought to be Mme. Fournaye is seen watching the house that night.
