19. Dorothy and the Bumble Bees

Trot and Cap'n Bill fly from the Magic Isle to the riverbank after the Wizard transforms them into bumblebees. They avoid being eaten by the Hungry Tiger and the Cowardly Lion, land on Dorothy's shoulder, and speak to her in tiny voices.
