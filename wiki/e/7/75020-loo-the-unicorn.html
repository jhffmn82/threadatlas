8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Loo reacts angrily to the alleged threat from the people of Oz and urges the beasts to fight. Kiki Aru transforms him into a man as proof of the strangers' power, after which Loo enjoys being a man but is changed back into a Unicorn for his safety.
