Pontus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Pontus is a region containing the Cimmerian Bosphorus, with the Temple of the Chalcidonians near its mouth. Eumelus aided Greek inhabitants and protected merchants trading there by fighting the Heniochians, people of Taurus, and Achaians. Later Demetrius occupied and fortified its mouth, leaving infantry and ships; Cassander's forces could not pass the strait.

Pontus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Pontus is a sea entered after the Cyanean rocks; Phineus directs the Argonauts along its shores toward the Colchians and Aea.
