Pontus

place · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Pontic sea, Pontns, Pontu^, sea

Abstract written by a model

In The Argonautica, Pontus is a sea that the Argonauts enter after the Cyanean rocks and follow along its shores toward the Colchians and Aea. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Pontus is a region containing the Cimmerian Bosphorus, where Eumelus protected Greek inhabitants and merchants and Demetrius later occupied and fortified the strait's mouth. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Pontus is a sea inhabited by fierce and barbarous peoples, called Axenus because it is inhospitable, through which the Argonauts pass on their outward and return voyages.

Pontus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Pontus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pontus is a region containing the Cimmerian Bosphorus, with the Temple of the Chalcidonians near its mouth. Eumelus aided Greek inhabitants and protected merchants trading there by fighting the Heniochians, people of Taurus, and Achaians. Later Demetrius occupied and fortified its mouth, leaving infantry and ships; Cassander's forces could not pass the strait.
