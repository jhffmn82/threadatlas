Since we have treated of the afiisiirs of Asia, we shall now pass over

The famine at Pydna became extreme: soldiers received only five choinices of bread-corn per month, elephants were fed sawdust, and horses and draught-beasts were slaughtered. Foreign horsemen and many other soldiers died, some barbarians ate the bodies of the dead, and the city filled with unburied corpses.
