Nysa

Abstract written by a model

Nysa is a city between Cophlas and the Indus, said to have been founded by Bacchus for unfit soldiers descended from his family and named from his nurse. It governed itself aristocratically, supplied Alexander three hundred horsemen, and retained freedom after his grant; ivy uniquely grew there in India.

Nysa is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SUP. XIII.

Nysa was presented as a city founded by Bacchus for soldiers who were unfit for war and descended from his family. It governed itself under an aristocracy until Alexander granted it continued freedom, and it was said to be the only place in India where ivy grew.
