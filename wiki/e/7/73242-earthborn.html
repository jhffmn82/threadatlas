Earthborn

Also called: Earthborn men, Earthborn monsters, dread monsters, these monsters

Abstract written by a model

The Earthborn are fierce, insolent six-handed monsters on the Mount of Bears who attack the Argonauts from above Chytus; Heracles and the other heroes kill them all.

Earthborn is in one work on this site, The Argonautica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
