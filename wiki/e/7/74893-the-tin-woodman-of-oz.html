Narrative, section by section

24 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Woot the Wanderer arrives at the Tin Woodman's splendid castle in the Winkie Country and is welcomed with food and conversation by the Tin Woodman and the Scarecrow of Oz. Woot explains that he left his comfortable home in the Gillikin Country because he wanted to see strange people and new sights. At Woot's request, the Tin Woodman begins telling how he was once a flesh-and-blood woodchopper who fell in love with Nimmie Amee, a slave of the Witch of the East. The Witch enchanted his axe to sever his limbs, while a skilled tinsmith replaced each lost limb with one made of tin.

Chapter Two

Nick Chopper tells Woot the Wanderer how the Wicked Witch and her enchanted axe gradually transformed him into the Tin Woodman. After losing his heart, Nick could no longer love Nimmie Amee, though the Wizard of Oz later gave him a Kind Heart. Woot and the Scarecrow persuade Nick that he has a duty to find Nimmie Amee and make her Empress of the Winkies. Nick, the Scarecrow, and Woot prepare to journey to the Munchkin Country to find her.

Chapter Three

Woot the Wanderer, the Tin Woodman, and the Scarecrow leave the Winkie Country to find Nimmie Amee, whom the Tin Woodman intends to marry. They travel northeast through the Winkie Country and plan to pass through the Gillikin Country so they can avoid the Emerald City before turning south into the Munchkin Country. Woot worries about dangers in the Gillikin Country, but agrees to continue with his companions. During the journey, the Scarecrow is restuffed, and the travelers discuss kindness, love, marriage, and the differences between meat, straw, and tin bodies.

Chapter Four

Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and the Tin Woodman follow a warning sign toward Loonville and enter a forest clearing inhabited by strange, inflatable Loons. The Loons attack, bind, and bring the three travelers before King Bal, who considers condemning and perforating them. After a thorn pricks Woot and helps him escape, he punctures several Loons, freeing himself and his companions. The travelers leave Loonville, deciding that the Loons are harmless unless strangers intrude on their country.

Chapter Five

Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper reach Yoop Castle in a valley and enter, believing it to be deserted. Mrs. Yoop reveals that she lives there, has transformed Polychrome into a canary-bird, and intends to keep the travelers as company. She threatens to transform Woot the Wanderer, the Scarecrow, and Nick Chopper into unrecognizable forms the next morning. Mrs. Yoop also explains that she uses magic transformations to turn unwanted things into food and that no one dies in the valley.

Chapter Six

Mrs. Yoop, a powerful Yookoohoo, holds Woot the Wanderer, Nick Chopper, and the Scarecrow prisoner in her secluded castle. She transforms Nick into a Tin Owl, the Scarecrow into a stuffed Brown Bear, and Woot into a Green Monkey, adding magic that makes them content with their new forms. Mrs. Yoop also reveals that Polychrome has been transformed into a Canary-Bird and kept in a golden cage. The prisoners cannot escape, and Mrs. Yoop leaves them locked inside while she takes her morning walk.

Chapter Seven

Polychrome the Canary explains that the Magic Apron is the only thing that can open doors and windows in the enchanted castle and proposes stealing it from Mrs. Yoop. Woot the Monkey hides in Mrs. Yoop's bedroom, takes the apron while she sleeps, and rescues Polychrome by moving a chair to reach her bird-cage. Woot opens the bedroom door with the apron, trapping Mrs. Yoop inside, and the Tin Owl guides the group to the hall's outer door. The friends use the apron to open the door and escape from the castle into the night.

Chapter Eight

Polychrome the Canary leads Woot, the Tin Owl, and the Little Brown Bear away from Mrs. Yoop's Valley and toward a forest in search of food and a way to reverse their enchantments. In the forest, a Jaguar attacks them, but Woot escapes by using the Lace Apron's command to open the earth beneath him. Polychrome conjures scrambled eggs on toast for the hungry Jaguar, who then leaves them in peace. The others discover that Woot has disappeared into the earth and do not know where he is.

Chapter Nine

Woot falls into an underground cavern inhabited by the Dragon Tribe, who have been awakened and consider punishing or eating him. After the Dragons chase him across the cavern, Woot uses his lace apron to open a hole in the roof and escapes into the sunlight. Outside, Woot encounters the Jaguar, who says he has eaten magic scrambled eggs rather than any of Woot's friends. Woot follows the Jaguar's trail and finds the little Brown Bear, the Tin Owl, and the Canary conferring about his disappearance.

Chapter Ten

The Scarecrow Bear, Green Monkey, Tin Owl, and Polychrome leave Gillikin Country and travel south toward Glinda's castle, hoping Glinda can restore them to their proper forms. In Munchkin Country, they meet Tommy Kwikstep, who was given twenty legs after wishing for them while running an errand for a mysterious fairy or witch. Polychrome transforms Tommy back into a two-legged young man and removes the corns from his toes. Tommy then leaves to deliver a note and resume running errands.

Chapter Eleven

The travelers reach Jinjur's Ranch and find her house empty, with gardens of cream-puffs, macaroons, and caramels. They enter the house, where they discuss their transformations by Mrs. Yoop and their hope that Glinda or Ozma can restore their proper forms. Jinjur returns, mistakes them for wild animals, and attacks them with a broom until they explain who they are. She then welcomes them, scrubs Woot clean after he hides in the sooty fireplace, and offers him heliotrope-flavored caramels.

Chapter Twelve

Ozma and Dorothy watch the adventures of the Tin Woodman, the Scarecrow, and Woot the Wanderer through the Magic Picture. They see Mrs. Yoop transform the travelers and become determined to help restore them. Ozma and Dorothy travel in the Red Wagon, drawn by the Wooden Sawhorse, toward Jinjur's Ranch, where they expect to meet the adventurers. Toto accompanies them, and they arrive just as Jinjur finishes scrubbing the Green Monkey.

Chapter Thirteen

Ozma of Oz restores the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman from their bear and owl forms, and she also breaks Polychrome's Canary enchantment. Woot the Wanderer cannot be restored by known magic because the Green Monkey must continue to exist in the Land of Oz. Polychrome proposes that Mrs. Yoop be given Woot's original form so that Mrs. Yoop and Woot can exchange forms, and Ozma agrees to attempt it.

Chapter Fourteen

Ozma performs a magic ceremony using a silver platter of broth to observe and transform Mrs. Yoop inside Yoop Castle. She changes Mrs. Yoop into Woot the Wanderer, then changes her into the Green Monkey while restoring Woot to his natural form. Mrs. Yoop remains permanently a Green Monkey and loses all ability to perform magical arts. Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and the Tin Woodman discuss how the Green Monkey will live and find food.

Chapter Fifteen

Ozma and Dorothy part from Woot the Wanderer, while the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, Woot, and Polychrome continue through the Munchkin Country to find Nimmie Amee. The Tin Woodman intends to ask Nimmie Amee whether she still wishes to marry him and become Empress of the Winkies. In the great Munchkin forest, the group discovers a tin man who is an exact duplicate of the Tin Woodman but carries a sword instead of an axe. They oil his joints, release him from rust, and learn that he is a Tin Soldier.

Chapter Sixteen

Captain Fyter explains to Woot, the Tin Woodman, and their companions that he was turned into a tin soldier after a Wicked Witch enchanted his sword and caused it to cut off his body parts. He reveals that he was in love with Nimmie Amee, who had previously loved the Tin Woodman, and that he rusted in the forest on the day he intended to elope with her. The Tin Woodman and Captain Fyter agree to go to Nimmie Amee and let her choose which of them she wishes to marry. The Scarecrow tells Captain Fyter that the Wicked Witch has been destroyed.

Chapter Seventeen

The Scarecrow, Woot, Polychrome, the Tin Soldier, and the Tin Woodman find Nimmie Amee's cottage deserted and long abandoned. They decide to ask Ku-Klip what became of Nimmie Amee and travel to his house at the edge of the forest, but he is absent. While waiting, the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier enter Ku-Klip's workshop, where they recall receiving their tin body parts and notice the barrel that may still contain their discarded flesh parts. The group is about to leave the workshop when the Tin Woodman tells them to wait.

Chapter Eighteen

The Tin Woodman discovers his former meat head in a cupboard at Ku-Klip's workshop, but the Head denies any meaningful relationship with his tin self and prefers its secluded life. Ku-Klip explains that he made Chopfyt from the cast-off body parts of the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier, using Magic Glue and a head taken from the cupboard. He also tells the travelers that Nimmie Amee left the Wicked Witch's forest to live with acquaintances on Mount Munch. The Tin Woodman, the Tin Soldier, Woot, the Scarecrow, and Polychrome set out toward Mount Munch to find her.

Chapter Nineteen

Woot, Polychrome, the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, and Captain Fyter travel toward Mount Munch when an invisible enchantment makes them and their belongings unseen. They evade a Hip-po-gy-raf in the Invisible Country, then reach a deep ditch that blocks their way. The Hip-po-gy-raf carries them across in exchange for the Scarecrow's straw, so the Scarecrow is restuffed with hay. Polychrome uses fairy magic to repair the Tin Soldier's bent leg and Nick Chopper's dented body.

Chapter Twenty

Woot and his companions find a tiny blue Munchkin dwelling inhabited by Mrs. Squealina Swyne and Professor Grunter Swyne. The Swynes allow Woot to eat from their garden, let the Scarecrow take straw, and invite the travelers to stay outside overnight. Polychrome and Woot restuff the Scarecrow, while the travelers spend the night near the straw-stack. In the morning, they learn that the Swynes' nine piglets live in the Emerald City under the Wizard of Oz's care, and then continue toward Mount Munch.

Chapter Twenty-One

Woot the Wanderer, Polychrome, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, and Captain Fyter reach the vicinity of Mount Munch and discover an invisible Wall of Solid Air surrounding Nimmie Amee's house. The Blue Rabbit explains that Nimmie Amee created the wall with a magic formula taken from an old Witch, and that she lives happily in the house. Polychrome makes the travelers small enough to pass through the rabbit's burrow beneath the wall, then enlarges them again so they can approach the house at their natural sizes.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Tin Woodman, Tin Soldier, Woot the Wanderer, and their companions find Nimmie Amee living happily in her home. Nimmie Amee is married to Chopfyt, a man made from parts discarded by the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier, including the Tin Soldier's head and the Tin Woodman's right arm. She refuses the Tin men's offer to replace Chopfyt and advises them to forget her. The journey ends with the Tin men accepting that Nimmie Amee is married and happy.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Polychrome, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, the Tin Soldier, and Woot the Wanderer leave Nimmie Amee's house before a storm traps them inside the Wall of Solid Air. Polychrome leads them to the Blue Rabbit's burrow, makes them small, and guides them through the tunnel to safety outside the wall. They wait out the storm in the dry tunnel, and when the rain stops, the Rainbow appears so Polychrome can rejoin her sisters. The companions bid Polychrome farewell as she departs on the Rainbow, while Woot grows big again without noticing.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Tin Soldier, and Woot the Wanderer return to the Emerald City without important incident, avoiding the Invisible Country because the Scarecrow fears the Hip-po-gy-raf. At Ozma's palace, they discuss Nimmie Amee's marriage to Chopfyt, and Ozma decides that Chopfyt need not concern the Tin Men. Ozma arranges for Captain Fyter to serve as a soldier in the Gillikin Country, while Woot is allowed to continue wandering under her protection. The Tin Woodman returns to his tin castle, accompanied part of the way by the Scarecrow.
