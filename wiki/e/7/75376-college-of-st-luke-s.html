College of St. Luke’s in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

College of St. Luke’s is a college in a great university town, with green-baize and oak doors, employing Hilton Soames while preparing for an examination threatened by a potentially scandalous tampered paper.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

The College of St. Luke’s employs Hilton Soames as a tutor and lecturer and is preparing for the Fortescue Scholarship examination. Soames fears that the tampered paper could cause a scandal affecting the college and the university.
