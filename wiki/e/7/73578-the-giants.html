Abstract written by a model

The Giants are enslaved guards in Herku, identified by gold bands. Though larger than Herku’s people, they guard the city and escort travelers to Vig’s palace; Vig withholds zosozo, fearing they would rule the city.

CHAPTER 12

The giants guard the City of Herku and escort the travelers to Vig’s palace while wearing gold bands that identify them as slaves. They are much larger than the people of Herku, but they are not given zosozo because Vig fears they would become the masters of the city.
