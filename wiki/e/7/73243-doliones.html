Doliones in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Doliones are descendants of Poseidon dwelling around an isthmus and plain, protected from the Earthborn. They hospitably welcome the Argonauts, then unknowingly fight them when the Argo returns at night, suffer heavy losses, and mourn Cyzicus.

The Doliones inhabit the isthmus and plain around the Mount of Bears under Cyzicus. They welcome the heroes, then mistakenly fight them when the Argo returns at night, suffering many deaths and later mourning Cyzicus.
