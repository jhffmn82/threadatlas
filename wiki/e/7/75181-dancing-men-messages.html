THE ADVENTURE OF THE DANCING MEN

The dancing-men messages are drawn in pencil or chalk and appear on the manor's window-sill, tool-house door, sundial, and on paper. They progress from requests and declarations involving Elsie to the threatening message, "ELSIE-PREPARE-TO-MEET-THY-GOD."
