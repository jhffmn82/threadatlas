Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Roman consuls entered the country of the Samnites with an army, defeated them at Talius, and later benefited from renewed fighting in which many Samnites were killed and more than 2,200 were captured. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Roman consuls are chosen yearly, with one from the senators and one from the common people, though both may be common people; under the settlement, Marcus Horatius and Lucius Valerius Potitus later served and completed the laws.
