XC-Translate

benchmark · 1 work · 32 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Cross-Culture Translate, XC-Translate annotations, benchmark, our benchmark, the benchmark

Abstract written by a model

XC-Translate is a large-scale, manually created and verified machine-translation evaluation benchmark for cross-cultural translation of text containing potentially culturally nuanced entity names. It covers 10 English-to-X language pairs, with Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Thai, and Turkish as target languages. It contains about 5,000 sentences per language pair, more than 58,000 instances, and over 100,000 references, averaging two translations per sentence. Human annotators selected entity names likely to require treatment beyond transliteration. The benchmark is designed to reveal weaknesses in MT systems and multilingual large language models when entity names vary culturally. Evaluation results show that BLEU and COMET can remain high despite incorrect entity translations, while M-ETA reports substantially lower entity-aware performance. It also evaluates a knowledge retriever, which reaches 85.9% hits@1 and 92.1% hits@3; KG-MT significantly outperforms the state of the art. Although diverse, XC-Translate is not exhaustive: it omits Uralic, Dravidian, and Niger-Congo families, many Indo-European languages, and dialectal and regional variation; its Wikidata-based entity selection may also exclude culturally relevant entities.

XC-Translate is in one work on this site, Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Introduction

XC-Translate is introduced as the first large-scale, manually created benchmark for cross-cultural translation across 10 language pairs involving text with entity names. It exposes limitations of current MT models and LLMs, and the authors release it at the stated GitHub address.

Related Work

XC-Translate is introduced as the first large-scale, manually created benchmark for cross-cultural translation of text containing entity names.

4 .. 4.3

XC-Translate evaluates cross-cultural translation of texts containing entity names through parallel texts in 10 English-to-X language pairs. It is manually created and verified, contains about 5,000 sentences per language pair and more than 58,000 instances, and provides over 100,000 references.

5

XC-Translate is used to evaluate translation systems on texts containing challenging entity names. The results show that conventional BLEU and COMET scores can remain high despite incorrect entity translations, whereas M-ETA reveals much lower entity-aware translation performance.

6

XC-Translate is used to evaluate the knowledge retriever. The retriever achieves 85.9% hits@1 and 92.1% hits@3 on this dataset.

7

XC-Translate is introduced as the first large-scale, manually curated benchmark for cross-cultural translation of texts containing entity names. KG-MT significantly outperforms the state of the art on this benchmark.

Limitations

XC-Translate contains a diverse but non-exhaustive set of languages and omits entire linguistic families, many languages, and dialectal or regional variation. Its entity selection is based on Wikidata and may exclude culturally relevant entities.
