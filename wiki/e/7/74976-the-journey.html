Abstract written by a model

The journey is a duty undertaken by the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, and Woot to find Nimmie Amee and make her Empress of the Winkies. They leave the Emperor's castle, travel northeast through the Winkie Country, avoid the Emerald City by going through Gillikin Country, and head south into Munchkin Country toward Mount Munch. Along the way they pass a tiny blue Munchkin dwelling, investigate Loonville despite warnings, survive the Loons, and face trials and troubles. Crossing a plain, they visit the Swynes' dwelling before continuing to Mount Munch. At Nimmie Amee's home, they learn she is married and happy; Woot calls the journey unsuccessful, but the Tin Woodman is relieved of his anxiety.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

Nick decides to journey to the Munchkin Country to find Nimmie Amee and make her Empress of the Winkies. The Scarecrow and Woot agree to accompany him, and preparations begin at once.

Chapter Three

The journey to Nimmie Amee begins when the Tin Woodman, Scarecrow, and Woot leave the Emperor's castle. They travel northeast through the Winkie Country, intending to pass around the Emerald City through the Gillikin Country and then go south into the Munchkin Country.

Chapter Four

The travelers journey into the Country of the Gillikins after spending the night in a field. They investigate Loonville, survive the encounter with the Loons, and resume their journey after deciding that they had intruded where they were warned not to go.

Chapter Twenty

The travelers come upon the Swynes' dwelling while crossing a broad plain toward Mount Munch. After breakfast and farewells, they climb out of the hollow and resume their journey toward the mountain.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The journey brings the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier to Nimmie Amee's home, where they learn that she is married and happy. Woot the Wanderer considers the journey unsuccessful, but the Tin Woodman is glad that it relieved his anxiety about Nimmie Amee.
