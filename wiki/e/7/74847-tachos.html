Tachos in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Tachos, king of Egypt, builds Leuce, declares war on the Persians, and commands a fleet, infantry, and Greek mercenaries. After Nectanabis takes Egypt, he flees to Artaxerxes, becomes a general, and is restored after Agesilaus defeats Nectanabis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAR XL

Tachos, king of Egypt, declared war against the Persians and assembled a fleet, Egyptian infantry, and Greek mercenaries with Lacedaemonian support. After Nectanabis revolted and took Egypt, Tachos fled to Artaxerxes, who forgave him and made him general against the Egyptians. Tachos was restored to his kingdom after Agesilaus defeated Nectanabis's forces.
