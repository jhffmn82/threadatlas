Athenion, a Cilician slave and skilled astrologer, became leader and then king of a separate rebel force in the territories of Aegesta and Lilybaeum. He later cooperated with Tryphon, was temporarily seized by him, and was released when Lucullus entered Sicily; during the battle near Scirthsea he wounded many Romans but was himself wounded and forced to flee. Caius Aquilius eventually fought and killed him.
