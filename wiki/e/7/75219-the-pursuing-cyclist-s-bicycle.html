the pursuing cyclist's bicycle in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Bob Carruthers uses the bicycle to follow Violet Smith covertly from about two hundred yards away, matching her slowing and stopping and apparently leaving by a footpath. Concealed by a beard, he watches over her; later he rides it rapidly toward the scene when Holmes and Watson encounter him.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SOLITARY CYCLIST

The pursuer's bicycle allows the dark-bearded man to follow Violet Smith while maintaining a distance of about two hundred yards. He slows when Violet slows and stops when she stops, then apparently leaves the road by a footpath.

The Thursday brought us another letter from our client.

Bob Carruthers uses a bicycle to follow Violet Smith and watch over her while keeping his identity concealed with a beard. He is riding toward the scene at speed when Holmes and Watson encounter him in Violet’s dog-cart.
