Hilton Cubitt

Abstract written by a model

Hilton Cubitt is an English Norfolk squire of Riding Thorpe Manor, married to Elsie Patrick, also called Elsie Cubitt and Mrs. Hilton Cubitt. He highly values his family honour and trusts Elsie despite her refusal to disclose her past. When dancing-men figures frighten her, he seeks Holmes's help, copies the messages, watches for their sender, and returns to Norfolk rather than leave her alone. He owns a revolver and confronts Abe Slaney after finding him with Mrs. Hilton Cubitt, firing first. Slaney shoots him through the heart, and Cubitt is found dead in his study; Holmes concludes the fatal shot came from outside the window.

Hilton Cubitt is in one work on this site, The Return of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
