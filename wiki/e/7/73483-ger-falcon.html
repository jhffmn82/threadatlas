Ger-Falcon in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Ger-Falcon, a wild, lawless hawk and hunted forest outlaw, is the family's present representative, descended from its grandfather seventy times removed. Starving, he pursues Pigeon Pretty; Bruin captures him. After broth, he recounts royal service and stays with the hermit through snow and frost.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

_" CHAPTER XII.

Ger-Falcon pursues Pigeon Pretty because he has had nothing to eat for a week, but Bruin captures him before he can seize her. After eating the hermit's broth, he explains his family's former service to kings and agrees to remain with the hermit until the snow and frost are gone.
