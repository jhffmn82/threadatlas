smoking and tobacco use in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Smoking and other tobacco use contain nicotine and make air unfit to breathe through smoke and strong bad smell; they offer no real help and can be unsafe, though cigar smoking does not usually kill immediately.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Smoking and other tobacco use expose people to nicotine. Smoking a cigar does not usually kill a man immediately because he does not swallow the nicotine, but tobacco use provides no real help and can be unsafe.
