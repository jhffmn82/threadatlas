Abstract written by a model

Bear Center is a city and forest domain in a large circular forest clearing, surrounded by hollow trees and inhabited by hundreds of stuffed toy bears of different colors and sizes. Ruled by the Lavender Bear, also called the Bear King and a sorcerer, it serves as the reference point for Ugu’s mountain northeastward.

Bear Center is in one work on this site, The Lost Princess of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
