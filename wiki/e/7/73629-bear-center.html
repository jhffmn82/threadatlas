Bear Center in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Bear Center is a city and forest domain in a large circular forest clearing, surrounded by hollow trees and inhabited by hundreds of stuffed toy bears of different colors and sizes. Ruled by the Lavender Bear, also called the Bear King and a sorcerer, it serves as the reference point for Ugu’s mountain northeastward.

CHAPTER 15

Bear Center is a settlement in a circular forest clearing surrounded by hollow trees. Hundreds of stuffed bears of different colors and sizes live in the trees, and the city is ruled by the Lavender Bear.

CHAPTER 16

Bear Center is the city and forest domain ruled by the Bear King. The Little Pink Bear reveals that Ugu’s mountain is nineteen miles and three furlongs northeast of Bear Center.
