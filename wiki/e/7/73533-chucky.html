Chucky in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Chucky, a hungry, peevish woodchuck, customarily hibernates in winter in Toto's cupboard, where he is trapped in worsted, freed by the wood-pigeon, eats, samples dishes, and joins games.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIV.

Chucky is found trapped in the cupboard, entangled in his worsted covering. After the wood-pigeon frees him, he eats a piece of dried beef and samples the dishes before joining the games.
