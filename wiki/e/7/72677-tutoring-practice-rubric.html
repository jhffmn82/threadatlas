The tutoring-practice rubric is developed from research-based social-emotional learning, relationship-building principles, and five lessons on frequently used tutoring strategies. It assesses tutoring practices across five categories, supplies Layer 3 Tree of Thought Prompt criteria, informs prompt strategies, and returns 1 or 0 evaluations with supporting evidence.
