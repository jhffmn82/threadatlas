Spartan army in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Spartan army, commanded by Agesilaus and including Sciritae, was reorganized into ten divisions with valued troop types, then entered Boeotia with more than eighteen thousand foot and fifteen hundred horse.

Whilst these things were acting in Greece, Acoris^king of Egypt^

The Spartan army was reorganized into ten divisions of cities and soldiers, with heavy-armed troops, lightly armed troops, and horsemen assigned stated relative values. It entered Boeotia under Agesilaus with more than eighteen thousand foot and fifteen hundred horse.
