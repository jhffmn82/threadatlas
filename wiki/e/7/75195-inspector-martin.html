Abstract written by a model

Inspector Martin is a police inspector of the Norfolk Constabulary investigating the shooting at Riding Thorpe Manor. When Holmes arrives, Martin cooperates with him, records evidence, examines the premises, and considers precautions for the suspected prisoner. Worried that Abe Slaney may escape, he helps Holmes arrest and handcuff Slaney, gives the required warning about Slaney’s statement, and takes him to Norwich.
