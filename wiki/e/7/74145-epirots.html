Epirots

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: EpirotB, Epirotes

Abstract written by a model

The Epirots are a people willing to fight for their country and allies. Ruled for a time by Alcetas, they killed him after his rule became severe and tyrannical. They later mutinied, banished Aeacidas, allied with Cassander, were routed, made Alcetas king, and suffered further defeats.

Epirots is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
