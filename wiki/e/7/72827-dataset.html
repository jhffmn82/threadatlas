It contains 306 realistic question-SQL pairs spanning 13 tables and 44 columns. Its free-form questions pair with complex SQL involving nesting, multiple logical conditions, aggregation, and mathematical operations; average nesting is 1.5 levels, with 53 samples above two levels and 19 above three. This is a manually curated, manually annotated dataset for epidemiological research that adheres to the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model. It contains 306 realistic question-SQL pairs spanning 13 tables and 44 columns. Its free-form questions pair with complex SQL involving nesting, multiple logical conditions, aggregation, and mathematical operations; average nesting is 1.5 levels, with 53 samples above two levels and 19 above three. Specialists in epidemiological studies supported its creation. Each pair received two paraphrased question versions, though one version per question was used in subsequent evaluations because of budget limits. It serves as an external knowledge base for RAG by extracting relevant pairs into prompts.

The question-SQL pairs dataset consists of manually annotated pairs designed for epidemiological research. It adheres to the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model.

The dataset was created through manual curation with specialists in epidemiological studies. It contains 306 question/SQL pairs and exhibits complex queries, including nesting, multiple logical conditions, aggregation, and mathematical operations. Two paraphrased versions were added to each question-SQL pair, but one version per question was used for subsequent evaluations.

The dataset is used as an external knowledge base. Relevant question-SQL pairs are extracted from it and incorporated into the prompt.
