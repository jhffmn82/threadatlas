Dorothy and the Wizard's Surprise Cake in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Dorothy and the Wizard's Surprise Cake is a huge frosted birthday cake for Ozma, decorated with candy flowers, candles, and an inscription. Dorothy plans its hollow, frosting-covered center to hide twelve tiny monkeys. When Ozma cuts the cake, the monkeys and their golden band instruments emerge to dance and give cake pieces to guests.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

6. Ozma's Birthday Presents

Dorothy plans to make Ozma a large frosted birthday cake with candles. She will leave a hollow center covered by frosting so that tiny monkeys can break through, dance on the tablecloth, and give pieces of cake to the guests.

22. Ozma's Birthday Party

Dorothy and the Wizard's Surprise Cake is a huge frosted cake decorated with candy flowers and candles, bearing the words "OZMA'S Birthday Cake from Dorothy and the Wizard." When Ozma cuts it, it releases twelve tiny monkeys and their golden band instruments.
