Abstract written by a model

is_a attempt to establish a monarchy [in Carthage] Bomilcar's coup began when he gathered 500 citizens and about 1,000 mercenaries, declared himself sole monarch, and attacked Carthage. The citizens armed themselves, resisted the rebels from the houses around the market-place, and forced them into the New City. The revolt was settled through negotiations with the remaining rebels, but Bomilcar was executed.
