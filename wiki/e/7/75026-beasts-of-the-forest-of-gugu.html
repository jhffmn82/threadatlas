Abstract written by a model

The beasts of the Forest of Gugu live under Gugu the Leopard’s leadership, obey its laws, and include monkey tribes. Warned by Ruggedo that Oz’s people may enslave them, they assemble in the Great Clearing, angrily consider fighting, but divide over his proposed transformation plan.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

The forest beasts are warned that the people of Oz supposedly plan to enslave them. Gugu orders them to assemble in the Great Clearing at sunrise so they can decide whether to fight the people of Oz.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

The beasts gather in the clearing in orderly groups according to their kinds and listen to Ruggedo's warning. They react angrily to the alleged enslavement plan, but they disagree about whether to accept his transformation plan.
