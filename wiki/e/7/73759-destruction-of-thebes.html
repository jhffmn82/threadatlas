destruction of Thebes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The destruction of Thebes was its siege and razing by Alexander under a Greek council decree. After quieting commotions in Greece, Alexander advanced as Thebes fell into terror, then carried out the destruction to make other Greeks fear rebellion.

BOOK XVII.

The chapter heading states that Thebes was besieged by Alexander and razed, and refers to the resulting miseries of Thebes. The supplied narrative reaches Alexander's advance and the city's terror before giving the detailed sack account.

Alexander having quieted all the commotions in Greece, mardied

The Greek council decreed that Thebes should be razed to the ground, and Alexander carried out the decree. The destruction was intended to terrify other Greeks who might rebel.
