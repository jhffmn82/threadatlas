Aristodemus the Milesian

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Aristodemus, Aristodemus's

Abstract written by a model

Aristodemus the Milesian was a general appointed by Antigonus who carried a thousand talents to the Peloponnesus, raised eight thousand soldiers, allied Antigonus with Polyperchon and Alexander, and helped install Polyperchon as general. He then commanded mercenaries against Cassander’s garrisons, relieving Cyllene, freeing Patrae, taking Aegae, and aiding Dymaea.

Aristodemus the Milesian is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. IV,

Aristodemus the Milesian carried a thousand talents from Antigonus to the Peloponnesus. He raised eight thousand soldiers, joined Polyperchon and Alexander in alliance with Antigonus, and helped make Polyperchon general of the forces in the Peloponnesus.

And now, having related the things done throughout all Asia, we

Aristodemus, made general by Antigonus, raised forces in the Peloponnesus and campaigned against Cassander's garrisons. He relieved the siege of Cyllene, freed Patrae, took Aegae, and later assisted the Dymaeans in capturing their citadel.
