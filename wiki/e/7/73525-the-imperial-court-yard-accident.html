the Imperial court-yard accident in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The Imperial court-yard accident occurs when the deaf Ly-Chee throws ice-cold water backward as the Emperor steps outside, drenching his yellow satin clothes and damaging his embroidered shoes.

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Imperial court-yard accident occurs when the deaf Ly-Chee throws a bucket of ice-cold water backward just as the Emperor steps outside. The water drenches the Emperor's yellow satin clothing and damages his embroidered shoes.
