the army of Perdiccas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Perdiccas's army was prepared for war against Ptolemy in Egypt. After Perdiccas and king Philip overcame Ariarathes, it left Pisidia toward Egypt; Perdiccas detached troops under Eumenes to secure Hellespont passes and prevent passage.

Perdiccas and king Philip having overcome Ariarathes, delivered

Perdiccas's army left Pisidia and marched toward Egypt to attack Ptolemy. Eumenes was sent ahead with a considerable force to secure the passes at the Hellespont and prevent passage there.

CHAP. IIL

The army of Perdiccas was being prepared for war against Ptolemy in Egypt. Perdiccas assigned part of it, with officers, to Eumenes for operations at the Hellespont.
