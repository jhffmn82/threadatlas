DIODORUS SICULUS.

Agathocles's invasion of Africa was undertaken after his defeat at Himera to draw the Carthaginians away from Sicily and exploit their supposed lack of military experience. He landed safely, burned his fleet to force his soldiers to fight, and began the campaign by capturing a wealthy city.

Thence he forthwith moved with his army to Tunis*, and gained iImI

During Agathocles's African campaign, he captured and destroyed Tunis and another city, defeated the Carthaginians, and took the spoils of the battlefield. He then blockaded Carthage despite having only a broken and diminished army.
