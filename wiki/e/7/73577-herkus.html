Herkus

Also called: Herku, my people, the Herkus, the inhabitants, the people, the people of Herku, the strongest people in all the world

Abstract written by a model

The Herkus are people living beyond the thistles and twisting lands behind Thi. Though thin and weary-looking, they gain tremendous strength from eating zosozo once a year. They are said to be stronger than giants, enslave giants, and perhaps lift the world; their strength and friendliness can accidentally harm strangers.

Herkus is in one work on this site, The Lost Princess of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Herkus in The Lost Princess of Oz

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The Herkus live beyond the thistles and twisting lands behind Thi. They are said to be stronger than giants, to keep giants as slaves, and to be capable of lifting the world, though the High Coco-Lorum has never seen one himself.

CHAPTER 12

The people of Herku are unusually thin and weary-looking, but Vig says they are the strongest people in the world. They eat zosozo once a year, which gives each person tremendous strength, and they can accidentally injure strangers by shaking hands.
