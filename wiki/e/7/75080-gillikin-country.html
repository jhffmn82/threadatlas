  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Gillikin Country is the purple-tinted northern country of Oz, containing Loonville and described as dangerous and home to unpleasant people.
  • In The Magic of Oz, Gillikin Country is entered after Munchkin Country and has wild forests containing many monkeys; Dorothy and the Wizard plan to travel there to obtain monkeys for the surprise cake.
  • In Glinda of Oz, the Gillikin Country is the least known part of the Land of Oz, containing mountains, valleys, forests, streams, strange bands of people, and the Skeezer Country in its farthest northwestern part.
  • In Kabumpo in Oz, the Gilliken country of Oz is the purple northern part of Oz containing Illumi Nation and Pumperdink, where a scroll threatens Pumperdink’s disappearance unless a royal marriage condition is met.
  • In The Lost King of Oz, Gilliken Country is a country and part of Oz that contains Pumperdink; Dorothy and Humpy arrive there, amaze its farmers, and later travel through it.
  • In The Giant Horse of Oz, North is ruled by Joe and Hyacinth, whom Ozma appoints because the Gillikens are without a sovereign.

Gillikin Country is the purple-tinted northern country of Oz, containing Loonville. It is described as dangerous and home to unpleasant people; travelers enter only far enough to pass around the Emerald City, then leave southward before entering Munchkin Country.
