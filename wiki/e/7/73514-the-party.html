the party in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A real party at the cottage gathers friends including Toto, the grandmother, Bruin, the raccoon, the old hermit, Chucky, Jim Crow, Ger-Falcon, and birds. They eat, tell stories, play old-fashioned games, and dance to Toto’s fiddle at their merriest, final gathering.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIV.

The party is held at the cottage and includes Toto, the grandmother, Bruin, the raccoon, the old hermit, Chucky, and several birds. The guests eat, tell stories, play games, and watch the birds dance to Toto’s fiddle; everyone considers it their merriest gathering yet, though they know it is the last.
