the water-nymph in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

A water-nymph haunting the spring Pegae rises, sees Hylas in moonlight, is overcome by desire, pulls him into the eddy as he draws water, and makes him her husband.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Now at the hour when from the field some delver or ploughman goes

The water-nymph rises from the fair-flowing spring at Pegae and sees Hylas in the moonlight. Cypris makes her heart faint with desire, and she pulls Hylas into the eddy while he is drawing water. Glaucus says that she makes Hylas her husband.
