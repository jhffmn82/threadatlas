lotus

Also called: Lotus, lotus tree (Dryope), the branches, the lotus, the new-formed lotus, the trunk, watery lotus

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is a watery flowering plant. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a lotus tree into which Dryope becomes, retaining her face, voice, tears, and warmth briefly before protecting it from an axe.

Chapter V

The lotus tree is the form Dryope takes after her feet become rooted and woodiness spreads through her body. Her face, voice, tears, and warmth remain briefly, and her family embraces the trunk and kisses its leaves before the transformation is complete.
