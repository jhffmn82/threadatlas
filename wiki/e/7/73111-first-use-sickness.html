first-use sickness in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

First-use sickness is the body's warning after initial smoking or tobacco use: it signals danger and poison, then may disappear as the body becomes used to tobacco.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

First-use sickness is the body's warning that tobacco is dangerous and poisonous. It tries to stop a boy before greater harm is done, but the sickness may disappear as the body becomes used to the tobacco.
