Pagasae in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Pagasae is a beach and harbour where Argo lies and the heroes assemble. The Argonauts come ashore, ready the ship, sacrifice to Apollo, and sail away. After leaving Aegina, they return peacefully to its beach, with no further adventure or opposing tempest.
