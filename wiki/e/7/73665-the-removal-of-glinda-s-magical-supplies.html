the removal of Glinda's magical supplies in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

A robbery removes all of Glinda's magical instruments and rare chemical compounds from the Room of Magic cupboard, preventing her from preparing a charm to identify the thief and prompting her to send messengers throughout Oz for replacements.

CHAPTER 2

All of Glinda's magical instruments and rare chemical compounds are removed from the shelves of her Room of Magic. Their removal prevents her from preparing a charm to identify the thief, so she sends messengers throughout Oz to obtain replacements.
