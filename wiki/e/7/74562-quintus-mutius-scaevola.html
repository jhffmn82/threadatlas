Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVI.

Quintus Mutius Scaevola served as proconsul in Asia and used his own virtuous example to reform corrupt manners. He paid the expenses of himself and his retinue, governed moderately and justly, protected the province from oppression, suppressed false accusations, and punished abusive publicans.
