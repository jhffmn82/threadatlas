Laura E. Richards · 1887

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

Life at the cottage continues happily after the other animals return to the forest. Bruin performs the household and farm work, while Toto, the blind grandmother, and their forest friends spend afternoons together at the cottage or in the forest. They return to the cottage each evening, where stories of the animals' exploits and woodland events fill the kitchen with laughter.
