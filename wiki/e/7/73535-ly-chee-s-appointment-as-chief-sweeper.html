Ten o'clock of a dark night.

The Golden Dragon commands that Ly-Chee become Chief Sweeper for life, with gilded brooms, ivory handles, and scrubbing-shoes as yearly benefits. The appointment is carried out, and Ly-Chee is publicly honored in a procession.
