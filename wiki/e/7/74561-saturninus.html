Saturninus

Abstract written by a model

Saturninus, a quaestor in Ostia, lived extravagantly and mismanaged grain transport to Rome, prompting the Senate to remove him. After becoming sober in conduct, he was chosen tribune of the people and opposed the Senate.

BOOK XXXVI.

Saturninus, described as having lived extravagantly, ordered all the grain from the province of Ostia to be carried to Rome. The Roman Senate rebuked him for maladministration, stripped him of authority, and replaced him, but after becoming sober in conduct he was later chosen tribune of the people.
