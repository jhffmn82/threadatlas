Lilybaeum in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Lilybaeum is a Carthaginian city founded after Dionysius ruined Motya. Strongly fortified, sea-supplied, and possessing a harbour, it resisted Pyrrhus and remained Carthaginian; later Romans besieged it and tried to block its harbour, while Carthaginians moved citizens there and repeatedly supplied and defended it.

BOOK XXII.

Lilybaeum was built by the Carthaginians after Dionysius ruined Motya. It was strongly fortified and supplied by sea, resisted Pyrrhus's assaults and undermining attempts, and remained in Carthaginian possession after he raised the siege.
