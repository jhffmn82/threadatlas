relevance estimator (RE)

component · 1 work · 37 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Extra, RE, RE model, RE module, Relevance Estimator, context RE, context relevance estimator, extra module that evaluates a given context, relevance estimator, relevance evaluator
  • In RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation: The relevance estimator (RE) is a detachable relevance-evaluation component in RE-RAG for retrieval-augmented generation and open-domain QA. It measures precise relative relevance between a question or query and retrieved contexts, estimates confidence that a context is useful for answering, and can be weakly supervised from question-answer data without explicit question-context compatibility labels. Using the same question-context input as a generator, it employs a sequence-to-sequence architecture to generate a “true” or “false” classification token. The normalized probability of “true” is converted to a logit, providing relevance scores for reranking and weighted answer marginalization. In use, RE evaluates an initial set of 100 retrieved contexts, reranks them, and selects the top 25 for inference; it also identifies context sets whose top 25 lack an answer through true-token probabilities. Jointly fine-tuned with a small language model, it improves that generator, and after separation it can be applied to large language models. It improves retrieval-augmented generation reliability, supports confidence-aware decoding, and improves large language models when combined with them. Reported results show stronger reranking than the retriever, FiD-KD, and Recall@k comparisons, better unseen-dataset generalization than FiD-KD, and better unanswerable-set classification F1 than MonoT5, with higher precision than the retriever and balanced precision and recall. When applied to Llama2, it surpasses Self-RAG.

relevance estimator (RE) in RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kiseung Kim and Jay-Yoon Lee · 2024

The relevance estimator (RE) is a detachable relevance-evaluation component in RE-RAG for retrieval-augmented generation and open-domain QA. It measures precise relative relevance between a question or query and retrieved contexts, estimates confidence that a context is useful for answering, and can be weakly supervised from question-answer data without explicit question-context compatibility labels. Using the same question-context input as a generator, it employs a sequence-to-sequence architecture to generate a “true” or “false” classification token. The normalized probability of “true” is converted to a logit, providing relevance scores for reranking and weighted answer marginalization. In use, RE evaluates an initial set of 100 retrieved contexts, reranks them, and selects the top 25 for inference; it also identifies context sets whose top 25 lack an answer through true-token probabilities. Jointly fine-tuned with a small language model, it improves that generator, and after separation it can be applied to large language models. It improves retrieval-augmented generation reliability, supports confidence-aware decoding, and improves large language models when combined with them. Reported results show stronger reranking than the retriever, FiD-KD, and Recall@k comparisons, better unseen-dataset generalization than FiD-KD, and better unanswerable-set classification F1 than MonoT5, with higher precision than the retriever and balanced precision and recall. When applied to Llama2, it surpasses Self-RAG.

RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

The relevance estimator is identified in the title as a component used in retrieval-augmented generation to improve open-domain QA performance and interpretability.

Abstract

The relevance estimator (RE) provides relative relevance between contexts and confidence about whether a context is useful for answering a question. It can be trained weakly supervised using question-answer data without labels for correct contexts.

opening

The relevance estimator measures precise relative relevance between retrieved contexts and a query and evaluates confidence in whether a context is useful for answering. It can produce relevance scores for weighted aggregated answers, act as a reranker, and be trained without explicit question-context compatibility labels.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

The relevance estimator measures the relevance of a question-context pair using a sequence-to-sequence architecture. It generates a classification token, either "true" or "false," and its normalized probability of "true" is converted to a logit for reranking and answer marginalization.

3 .. 3.3

The relevance evaluator is an extra module that evaluates retrieved contexts and selects the top 25 from the top 100 retrieved contexts for inference. It is used in RE-RAG and with several large language model generators.

3.4 / 4 / 4.1 .. 4.2

The relevance estimator improves the reliability of RAG by more accurately measuring relevance between questions and contexts. It performs strongly as a re-ranker and as a classifier for context sets that lack an answer, and when applied to Llama2 it surpasses Self-RAG.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

The relevance estimator ranks contexts, produces relevance scores for answer marginalization, and estimates confidence through the probability of generating the “true” token. On unseen datasets, it shows smaller reranking and answer-performance drops than FiD-KD.

7

The RE module measures the relative relevance and confidence of contexts. As a detachable component, it can be combined with modern large language models to improve their performance and can provide confidence information for decoding strategies.
