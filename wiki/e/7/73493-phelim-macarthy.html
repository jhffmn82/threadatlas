Phelim Macarthy

Abstract written by a model

Phelim Macarthy is a boy in the Macarthy family, son of Dennis and little brother of Eileen. He and Eileen share a trundle-bed, and he depends on her at night. He sees shining objects and calls them marbles, runs for Dennis, and stops Dr. O’Shaughnessy’s carriage so Eileen can be examined and taken away.
