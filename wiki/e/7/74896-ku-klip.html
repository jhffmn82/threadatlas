Ku-Klip in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Ku-Klip is a kind-hearted, merry, agreeable, skillful tinsmith and mechanic who owns a workshop at the far edge of the forest facing the plains of the Munchkin Country. He made the Tin Woodman’s replacement tin body parts, then made Captain Fyter after an enchanted sword cut off Fyter’s original parts. Using Magic Glue from the Wicked Witch’s house and cast-off parts of the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier, he made Chopfyt and gave him a tin arm. Absent when travelers first arrive, Ku-Klip later welcomes them and shares what he knows about Nimmie Amee.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Sixteen

Ku-Klip made Captain Fyter's tin legs, arms, head, and body after the enchanted sword cut off his original parts. Ku-Klip had previously made the Tin Woodman as well.

Chapter Seventeen

Ku-Klip is a tinsmith who lives at the far edge of the forest, facing the plains of the Munchkin Country. He is absent when the travelers arrive, although smoke from his chimney suggests that he will soon return.

Chapter Eighteen

Ku-Klip welcomes the travelers to his workshop and tells them what he knows about Nimmie Amee. He explains how he used Magic Glue and the discarded parts of the Tin Woodman and the Tin Soldier to make Chopfyt, then gave Chopfyt a tin arm before Chopfyt left eastward into the Munchkin Country.
