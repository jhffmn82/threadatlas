Park Lane in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Park Lane is a frequented thoroughfare in London with a nearby cab stand. It is associated with the shooting and murder of the Honourable Ronald Adair, who was shot from the open window of No. 427; no shot was heard and no ground disturbance was found.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE EMPTY HOUSE

Park Lane was the location of the murder and was described as a frequented thoroughfare with a cab stand nearby. No one reported hearing the shot, and the ground below the room's window showed no signs of disturbance.
