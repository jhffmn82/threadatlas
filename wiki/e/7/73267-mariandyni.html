Mariandyni in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

The Mariandyni reside on the Bithynian coast. After the Argonauts pass rivers and a lake to reach their land, they and Lycus welcome and ally with them because Polydeuces killed Amycus amid war with the Bebrycians.

The Argonauts reached the land of the Mariandyni after passing the river Sangarius, Lycus, and the Anthemoeisian lake. The Mariandyni welcomed them with Lycus because Polydeuces had slain Amycus and the people had long been at war with the Bebrycians.
