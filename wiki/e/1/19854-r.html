R. in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Richard Reade, eldest son of Sir Thomas Reade, was a tourist on the tour to the Jereed. He joined Captain Balfour’s expedition, received a hyaena skin and head and a bay horse from the Bey of Tunis, and visited Kairwan. He hunted and observed local customs, reported an insult, then pleaded successfully for the Arab’s protection.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

Richard Reade joined Captain Balfour on the expedition and received the skin and head of a hyaena, along with a fine bay horse, as presents from the Bey. He reported that Sidi Mohammed was an exceptionally accurate shot from horseback.

Rode to Kairwan.

R. visited Kairwan with his party, observed its mosque and religious customs, and met Sidi Amour Abeda. He shot a gazelle, reported an Arab's insult to the Bey, and then generously pleaded for the Arab to be spared further punishment. He also took part in gallops after gazelles and visited the Bey's court, fortress, and hunting activities.
