University of Edinburgh in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The University of Edinburgh, an educational institution, first admitted women to examinations for medical degrees, where Dr. Inglis graduated M.B., C.M. in 1899. After previously refusing women medical students and certain extra-mural mixed classes, it opened its doors to women medical students in 1916 under a new head.

The University of Edinburgh admitted women to its medical degree examinations, and Dr. Inglis graduated M.B., C.M. there in 1899. The University had previously refused to admit women medical students or recognise certain extra-mural mixed classes, but in 1916, under a new head, it opened its doors to women medical students.
