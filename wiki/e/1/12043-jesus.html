Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Christ, in disguise on earth, restores youth, receives confessing Judas as a disciple, entrusts him with money, and then Judas resumes his old evil behavior. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Christ is presented in Milton's Hymn of the Nativity as the one whose coming caused the heathen idols and pagan oracles to lose their power and fall silent. In Impressions of Theophrastus Such, Christ is portrayed as teaching or embodying mercy, compassion, and patriotism toward fellow countrymen, and as approving Jewish martyrs over crusaders who robbed and murdered Jews in his name. In Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa, Jesus of Nazareth is portrayed at his death on the cross, speaking words interpreted as “Helo, Helo, Lamah Zabac Ta Ni,” meaning that he is sinking and darkness covers his face, and ending with “It is finished.”

Abstract written by a model

Jesus of Nazareth is portrayed at his death on the cross, speaking words interpreted as “Helo, Helo, Lamah Zabac Ta Ni,” meaning that he is sinking and darkness covers his face. His final moments are characterized by resignation and fortitude, ending with “It is finished.”

Jesus of Nazareth is said to have uttered words at his death that the text interprets as Maya rather than Hebrew. The proposed meaning is “Now, now I am sinking; darkness covers my face,” which the text presents as consistent with his final statement that it was finished.
