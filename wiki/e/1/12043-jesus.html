Christ is portrayed as having taught or embodied mercy, compassion, and a patriotism that placed fellow countrymen within an inner circle of affection and duty. The text argues that Christ would have approved Jewish martyrs who refused to blaspheme more than crusaders who robbed and murdered Jews in his name.

Jesus of Nazareth in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Jesus of Nazareth is portrayed at his death on the cross, speaking words interpreted as “Helo, Helo, Lamah Zabac Ta Ni,” meaning that he is sinking and darkness covers his face. His final moments are characterized by resignation and fortitude, ending with “It is finished.”

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

If from the names of god and goddesses, we pass to that of places, we will find

Jesus of Nazareth is said to have uttered words at his death that the text interprets as Maya rather than Hebrew. The proposed meaning is “Now, now I am sinking; darkness covers my face,” which the text presents as consistent with his final statement that it was finished.
