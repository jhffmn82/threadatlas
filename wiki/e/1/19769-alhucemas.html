Alhucemas in Travels in Morocco

Alhucemas is a small Spanish island presidio with about five or six hundred inhabitants, commanding its bay at the Wad Nechor mouth near Ed-Housh and the ruined ancient capital of Mezemma.

CHAPTER IV.

Alhucemas is a small Spanish island presidio containing approximately five or six hundred inhabitants. It commands the bay of the same name at the mouth of the Wad Nechor, near the Islet of Ed-Housh; the ruined ancient capital of Mezemma lies nearby.
