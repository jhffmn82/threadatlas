gazelles in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Gazelles are swift small deer, faster than greyhounds and difficult to catch. During marches near Gilma, they were repeatedly encountered and pursued; some were caught by Arabs or dogs, one escaped wounded, and Captain B. caught one.
