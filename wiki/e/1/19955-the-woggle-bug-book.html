Abstract written by a model

The document is the Project Gutenberg edition of L. Frank Baum’s 1905 The Woggle-Bug Book, illustrated by Ike Morgan, with front matter, a story of Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., notes, and the Project Gutenberg license. The Woggle-Bug leaves his lodgings seeking money for a beautiful Wagnerian Plaids gown in a store. After ditch-digging, he repeatedly mistakes each new wearer of the gown for the same beloved woman, wasting his earnings on courtship. His misadventures include a fight at Mickey Schwartz’s ball and an expensive excursion with a Swedish widow and her four children, until she drives him away with insect Exterminator. He continues following the costume as it passes from a wash-lady to a Chinaman, tears off part of it, and flees. A balloon carries him away; he parachutes with his wings, is captured by Arabs who make most of the cloth into a waistcoat and leave him scraps for a necktie, then enters a jungle. There he meets Miss Chim and visits King Weasel, escaping after the king orders him eradicated. Back at his lodgings, he accepts the necktie as his love. Notes connect him with The Marvelous Land of Oz and name the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Animated Saw Horse.

The Unique Adventures of the WOGGLE-BUG

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., leaves his lodgings to find money for a beautiful gown he sees in a fine store. He works as a ditch-digger, but mistakes successive wearers of the gown, called Wagnerian Plaids, for the same beloved woman and spends much of his money courting them. His pursuit leads to a fight at Mickey Schwartz's ball, a costly outing with a Swedish widow and her four children, and finally his retreat after the widow uses insect Exterminator to drive him away.

The Woggle-Bug pursues the Wagnerian costume, which passes from a wash-lady to a Chinaman before he tears away part of it and carries it off. Fleeing the Chinaman, he accidentally escapes in a balloon, descends by using his wings as a parachute, and is captured by Arabs, who turn most of the cloth into a waistcoat and give him the scraps as a necktie. He then reaches a jungle, meets Miss Chim, visits King Weasel in the Inner Jungle, and escapes when the king orders him eradicated. Returning to his lodgings, the Woggle-Bug decides that the necktie is his love and that he can be content with possessing it.
