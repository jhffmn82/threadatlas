white brandy in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

White brandy is a stimulant of immorality and intoxication in Moroccan society, produced and distributed by Moroccan Jews despite punishment, consumed by Moors as not grape juice, and troubling Jewish families on the Sabbath.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

White brandy is described as a stimulant of immorality and intoxication in Moroccan society. Jews are said to produce and distribute it despite government punishment, while Moors drink it on the understanding that it is not grape juice, and Jewish families become excited and troubled by it on the Sabbath.
