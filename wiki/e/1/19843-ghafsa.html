Ghafsa

Abstract written by a model

Ghafsa is a town at the foot of hills beside an extensive plain, surrounded by olive and date trees. Though largely ruined and dirty, it has numerous hot mineral springs, ancient baths, gardens, and a nearly ruined Kasbah built chiefly from ancient remains. Its water irrigates the plain and supports palms and olives; local people make olive oil by hand. On a return march to Tunis, a party reached Ghafsa after rain, hail, high wind, and severe cold; Ghabs was about 130 miles away.

Ghafsa lies at the foot of hills beside an extensive plain and is surrounded by olive and date trees. It is described as largely ruined and dirty, though it has numerous hot and mineral springs, ancient baths, a ruined Kasbah, gardens, and water sufficient to irrigate the plain and support many palms and olive trees. Local people make oil by grinding olives between stones and separating the oil from water by hand.
