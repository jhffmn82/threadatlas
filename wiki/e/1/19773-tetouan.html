Abstract written by a model

Tetouan is a large, prosperous mercantile city in Hasbat, several miles inland from the Mediterranean and southeast of Tangier. Identified with Roman Yagath, it has 9,000–12,000 Moors, Arabs, Jews and Berbers, gardens and plantations, bazaars, a Jewish quarter, strong fortifications, and nearby Marteen port.

CHAPTER IV.

Tetouan, identified with the Roman Yagath, is a large, prosperous, and commercial city of the province of Hasbat, situated several miles inland from the Mediterranean. It has gardens, olive and vine plantations, extensive orange groves, covered bazaars, a Jewish quarter, and a nearby port at Marteen or Marteel. Its population is variously estimated at 9,000 to 12,000, and its fortifications are strong enough mainly to resist hostile Berbers.
