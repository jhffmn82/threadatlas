[5] In the Shereefian lineage of Muley Suleiman, copied for Ali Bey by the Emper

The slave trade is defended in letters from the Sultan to Mr. Hay as a practice accepted since the time of the sons of Adam and confirmed by the Koran and the Sunnat. The correspondence states that Islamic law regulates the treatment, sale, emancipation, and religious transfer of slaves.
