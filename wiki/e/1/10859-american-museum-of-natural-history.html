American Museum of Natural History in Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882

Various · 1881

American Museum of Natural History, supervised by Prof. Bickmore, prepares The Woods of America collection in the Arsenal’s third floor, receiving United States specimens for scientific, educational, architectural, gardening, and forestry use.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] THE WOODS OF AMERICA.

The museum's directors are preparing The Woods of America collection in the third floor of the Arsenal in Central Park. The museum receives specimens from throughout the United States and plans to make the exhibition useful for scientific study, education, architecture, landscape gardening, and forestry.
