Texas is described as a place where rattlesnakes are very common and people camping out are exposed to their bites. Croft conducted his observations there, at Hermanitas Ranch, far from chemical laboratories.

AN INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION.

Texas reported 174,184 farms, compared with 61,125 in 1870.
