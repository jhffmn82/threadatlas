Abstract written by a model

Moroccan Jews historically endured Moorish oppression, humiliation, violence, restrictions, and unequal justice, including slavery and plunder; their condition reportedly improved over the preceding half century as some entered European society, adopting its clothing and habits and gaining manners and respectability.

CHAPTER I.

Moroccan Jews historically endured humiliation, violence, restrictions, and unfavorable treatment before Muslim judges, though their position had reportedly improved during the preceding half century. Some adopted European clothing and habits, while others continued to live under oppression and social constraint.
