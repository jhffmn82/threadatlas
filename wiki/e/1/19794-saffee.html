Saffee in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Saffee, also called Asafee and formerly Soffia or Saffia, is an ancient city in Abda, built by Carthaginians near Cape Pantin. Its massive walls, summer roadstead, and European commerce matter most. With about one thousand inhabitants and uncultivated surroundings, it was captured by Portuguese in 1508 and abandoned in 1641.

Saffee, properly Asafee and formerly Soffia or Saffia, is an ancient city of Abda built by the Carthaginians near Cape Pantin. It has massive walls, a summer roadstead, and a history of European commerce, but its population is about one thousand and the surrounding country is comparatively uncultivated.
