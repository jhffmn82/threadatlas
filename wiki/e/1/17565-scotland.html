Abstract written by a model

In The Library, Scotland's Highlands and islands were described as places where children were passed across hearth fires, carried around fires, or swung through smoke to protect them from evil spirits, evil eyes, or illness; in north-east Scotland and Fife, fire was also associated with testing or removing fairy changelings. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Scotland's western and northern parts were immersed in heathenism when Columba arrived at Iona, and through Columba and the Culdees knowledge of the gospel and Scriptures was disseminated throughout the Highlands and Islands. In Dr. Elsie Inglis, Scotland was the setting for much of Dr. Elsie Inglis's suffrage organising, including her work in the Highlands and with the Scottish Federation of Suffrage Societies; Scottish women later supported her wartime medical work, and Scotland honored her with military recognition and a funeral attended by the chief of the Scottish Command.

Scotland in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Abstract written by a model

Scotland was the setting for much of Dr. Elsie Inglis’s suffrage organising, including her work in the Highlands and with the Scottish Federation of Suffrage Societies. Scottish women’s organised support later helped Inglis establish units and equipment for wartime medical work.
