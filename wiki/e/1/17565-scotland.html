Scotland was the setting for much of Dr. Elsie Inglis’s suffrage organising, including her work in the Highlands and with the Scottish Federation of Suffrage Societies. Scottish women’s organised support later helped Inglis establish units and equipment for wartime medical work. Scotland honored Elsie Inglis with military recognition and a funeral attended by the chief of the Scottish Command.
