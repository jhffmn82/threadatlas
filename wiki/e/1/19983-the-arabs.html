the Arabs in The Woggle-Bug Book

L. Frank Baum · 1905

The Arabs are a tent-dwelling group in an Arab camp, superstitious that killing woggle-bugs brings bad luck. Their children attack the Woggle-Bug, and men bind and threaten him, then release and respectfully treat him after his warning.
