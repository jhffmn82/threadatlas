CHAPTER I.

The French bombardment of Tangier and Mogador brought severe suffering to the Jewish communities. Jews were massacred or buried beneath ruins, their wives were plundered, and survivors wandered naked and starving or hid in the mountains.

APPENDIX.

The French bombardment began on 13 August 1844 after the Moors fired twenty-one guns, although the French did not return fire until 2 P.M. The bombardment lasted about twenty-eight hours and caused relatively few French casualties, while the Moors suffered heavily.
