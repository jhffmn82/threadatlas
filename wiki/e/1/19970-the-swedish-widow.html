She lets the wealthy Woggle-Bug court her, takes him and her children through eating establishments, then drives him from her home with insect Exterminator.

The Unique Adventures of the WOGGLE-BUG

The Swedish widow buys the Wagnerian Plaids for $3.65 and wears them in the park while seeking a new husband. She allows the Woggle-Bug to court her because he has money, leads him and her children through several eating establishments, and later scatters insect Exterminator around her home to make him leave.
