Arzila, or Asila, is an ancient Berber city in the province of Hasbat, formerly called Zilia and Zulia by the Romans. It lies on the naked Atlantic shore, has a small port, a population of about one thousand, and produces good tobacco; its Portuguese fortifications are rapidly decaying under Moorish possession.
