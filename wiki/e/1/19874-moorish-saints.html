Moorish saints in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Moorish saints are numerous in Kairwan, defined by descent from the Prophet and lucky divination; they receive devout obeisance while exploiting poor people.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Rode to Kairwan.

Moorish saints were said to be especially associated with lineal descent from the Prophet and the ability to divine through lucky guesses. They were described as numerous in Kairwan, and as receiving devout obeisance and exploiting poor people.
