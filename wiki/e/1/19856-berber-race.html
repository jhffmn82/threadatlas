Abstract written by a model

The Berber race includes the Amayeegh and Shelouh varieties in Morocco. Chiefly mountain-dwelling, its members may be hard to distinguish from Arabs or Moors in urban life. Shelouhs form a major southern population, inhabiting places including Dimenet, Fronga, and Tagawost and living or trading with Moors and Jews.

CHAPTER III.

The Berber race is described as including the Amayeegh and Shelouh varieties in Morocco. Its members are chiefly located in the mountains and are difficult to distinguish from Arabs or Moors when they adopt urban occupations, dress, or habits.

CHAPTER VI.

Shelouhs are presented as a major Berber population in the southern towns and districts, whose presence is suggested by the frequent use of names beginning or ending with the letter T. They inhabit places such as Dimenet, Fronga, and Tagawost, and often trade or live alongside Moors and Jews.
