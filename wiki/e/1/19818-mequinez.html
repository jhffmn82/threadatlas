Abstract written by a model

Mequinez, or Miknas, is a hilltop royal residence and city in the province of Fez, founded by Meknasab. In a well-watered, pleasant region, it is enclosed by triple walls and has twenty thousand to fifty thousand inhabitants. It contains much of the Imperial guard, royal treasures, a Spanish hospitium, and an elaborately decorated palace; its people are polished, kind, and hospitable.

CHAPTER V.

Mequinez, or Miknas, is a royal residence and city of the province of Fez, situated on a hill in a well-watered and pleasant region. It is surrounded by a triple wall, contains much of the Imperial guard, and holds royal treasures estimated at about fifty million dollars. Its inhabitants are described as polished, kind, and hospitable, while its palace is noted for elaborate stucco Arabesque work.
