Samnites in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Samnites are a group repeatedly at war with the Romans under changing conditions. Though allied with the Romans under a league, they take Ferentum, win support from Nuceria and Sora after Roman garrisons are massacred, fight at Saticula and Lautulae, and gather men for a decisive campaign in Apulia. The conflict continues through incursions, sieges, and camps. They besiege the Iapyges, but Marcius relieves them. Routed at Talius, they seize a hill, retreat, and are defeated again, losing many slain and more than 2,200 prisoners. After twenty-two years and six months of war, they make peace with the Romans.
