Battle of the Three Kings in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

The Battle of the Three Kings was fought at El-Kesar in 1578. It caused the deaths of Portuguese king Don Sebastian, Muley Moluc, and Portuguese nobility and chivalry; Muley Moluc died secretly in a litter to preserve Moorish courage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

The Battle of the Three Kings occurred at El-Kesar in 1578. Don Sebastian, the Portuguese king, and two Moorish princes perished in connection with the battle, while Muley Moluc died secretly in a litter so that the Moorish forces would not lose courage.
