railways in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Railways are proposed for Tunis, alongside water ducts, to distribute water and transport it from Zazwan to the Jereed. The text notes French discussion of an Algiers–Blidah line and argues developed Tunisian resources could make railways profitable.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

On our march back to Tunis, we encamped for two days by the foot of a range of h

The text suggests that railways could be applied to Tunis and notes that the French were already discussing a line from Algiers to Blidah across the Mitidjah. It argues that railways and accompanying water ducts could distribute water across the country and that developed Tunisian resources could make railways profitable.
