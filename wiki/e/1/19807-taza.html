Taza in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Taza, capital of the well-watered district of Haiaina, is a city on a river-surrounded rock. With 10,000 to 12,000 hospitable, commercially active inhabitants, it has spacious streets, a large mosque, good provisions, and commerce with Tlemsen and Fez.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Taza is the capital of the well-watered district of Haiaina and is described as one of the finest cities in Morocco. Built on a rock surrounded by a river, it has spacious streets, a large mosque, good provisions, hospitable inhabitants, and commerce with Tlemsen and Fez.
