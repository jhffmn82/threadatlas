French bombarding squadron in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

French bombarding squadron comprising Gemappes, Suffren, Triton, Belle Poule, Asmodee, Pluton, and brigs; it silenced Mogador's batteries before French forces landed on the isle.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

APPENDIX.

The squadron consisted of the ships of the line Gemappes, Suffren, and Triton; the frigate Belle Poule; the steamers Asmodee and Pluton; and some brigs. It silenced the batteries at Mogador before French forces landed on the isle.
