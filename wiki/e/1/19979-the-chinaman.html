Abstract written by a model

The Chinaman, married to Mattie De Forest, buys a Wagnerian costume and remakes it as a flowing Chinese robe. Mistaken for Grenadine by the Woggle-Bug, he names a price and denies it. After the robe is torn, he pursues the Woggle-Bug with a knife and cuts a balloon’s rope.
