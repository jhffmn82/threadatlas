Moroccan tribal administration in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Moroccan tribal administration rests primarily on tribes, with kaidats as European-style jurisdictions; tribal names shape districts, and the Sultan’s authority is weakest among Berber tribes of the Rif and Garet.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

Moroccan tribal administration is presented as more fundamental than European-style provincial divisions. Districts and mountains are commonly named after their chief tribes, while kaidats provide a jurisdictional structure more comparable to European government. The Sultan’s authority is especially weak among the Berber tribes of the Rif and Garet.
