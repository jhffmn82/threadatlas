Abstract written by a model

The hot springs of Ghafsa are hot, sulphurous, and other mineral waters near ancient bath structures and inscription fragments. Observed at ninety-five degrees, they contain fish resembling gudgeons and small snakes that live together; the snakes feed entirely on the fish.

The hot springs of Ghafsa include hot, sulphurous, and other mineral waters in which fish and small snakes live together. The fish are described as resembling gudgeons or other small fish, while the snakes feed entirely on them. Ancient bath structures and fragments of inscriptions remain near the springs.
