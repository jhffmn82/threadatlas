CHAPTER VIII.

Palm-groves are described as beautiful plantations whose trees are generally planted in a quincunx or at distances of four or five yards apart. They are commonly situated beside streams or rivulets, and abundant water produces healthier trees and finer fruit. The groves may yield very large quantities of dates, with one plantation producing fifteen hundred camels' loads.
