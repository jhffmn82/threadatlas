Muley Hisham in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Muley Hisham, father of eldest son Muley Abd Errahman, disputed succession after Yezeed’s death, was proclaimed Sultan in Morrocco and Sous, retired to sanctuary in 1795, soon died, and was succeeded by Muley Suleiman.

[5] In the Shereefian lineage of Muley Suleiman, copied for Ali Bey by the Emper

Muley Hisham disputed the succession after Yezeed’s death and was proclaimed Sultan in the south at Morrocco and Sous. In 1795 he retired to a sanctuary, where he soon died.
