Abstract written by a model

Elaeonderron Argan is a tree peculiar to southern Morocco, large, bushy, wide-spreading, lofty, branching, and broad-armed, sometimes as large as an oak. It bears olive-like fruit with an almond kernel crushed for rancid oil used instead of olive oil. It shades travelers; one celebrated ancient tree, said to have been planted by Okba, shelters many people and is admired for its grandeur.

CHAPTER VII.

The narrator visits a celebrated, ancient Argan tree said by the people to have been planted by Okba. Its broad branches provide shade for many people, and the narrator compares its grandeur with the enchanted wood described by Tasso.
