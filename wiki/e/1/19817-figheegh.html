Figheegh in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Figheegh, also called Figuiq, is a Saharan agglomeration of twelve villages, including Maiz, El-Wadghir, and Zenega. Governed by Sheikh Kebir, it is prosperous through palm plantations and date production, supplying western province of Algeria and trading with Fez, Tafilett, and Touat. Its gardens grow madder-root, tobacco, and barley; wheat comes from the Teli.

Figheegh, also called Figuiq, is an agglomeration of twelve villages known for palm plantations and date production. It has commercial relations with Fez, Tafilett, and Touat, and its people are generally prosperous.
