CHAPTER IV.

Melilla, an ancient Carthaginian city near the cape called Rusadir by the Romans, is the capital of Garet and is said to contain about 3,000 inhabitants. It lies amid fertile country rich in minerals and honey. A nearby Spanish presidio, also called Melilla, occupies an island connected to the mainland by a drawbridge and is reported to contain 2,244 people by Spanish figures, though other writers give a lower number.
