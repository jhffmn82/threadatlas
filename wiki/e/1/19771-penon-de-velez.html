Penon de Velez in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Penon de Velez is a Spanish presidio-island and convict settlement opposite the mouths of the Gomera River, a strong but largely barren rock with about nine hundred inhabitants that receives fresh water from the mainland part of the year.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

Penon de Velez is a Spanish presidio island and convict settlement situated opposite the mouths of the Gomera River. It is a strong position with a garrison and population of about nine hundred, but it is largely a barren rock and must receive fresh water from the mainland for part of the year.
