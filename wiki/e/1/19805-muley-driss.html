Muley Driss in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Muley Driss, also called Zawiat-Muley-Driss or Muley Edris, is a renowned city in the province of Fez, at the foot of the Terhoun Mountains northwest of Fez. It contains Edris’s sanctuary, lies in fertile country, and may occupy ancient Volubilis.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Muley Driss, also called Zawiat-Muley-Driss or Muley Edris, is a renowned city in the province of Fez at the foot of the mountains of Terhoun. It contains a sanctuary dedicated to Edris, founder of the Edrisiti dynasty, and lies in a fertile country producing many necessities and luxuries.
