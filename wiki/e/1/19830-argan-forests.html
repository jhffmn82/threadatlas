Argan forests in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Argan forests in Morocco's arid country south of Mogador are linked to Moorish legends of enchantresses, magical springs, deceptive fruit, and wicked jinns, ending when a holy man breaks their enchantment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The Argan forests are associated with Moorish legends of enchantresses, magical springs, deceptive fruit, and wicked jinns. According to the tale, a holy man released the forests from enchantment by tying pieces of cork bark bearing the sacred name of the Deity to the trees.
