Abstract written by a model

Kairwan is a clean city with many ruined houses and much of a regiment of the Nitham quartered there. After an expedition crossed sparsely inhabited, water-poor hills, it appeared beyond them beside a boundless plain toward Susa; the party then visited its holy well, fortress, Kaed's house, and Bey's place of justice.

CHAPTER IX.

Kairwan appeared beyond the hills at the edge of a boundless plain that also extended toward Susa. The expedition approached this broad plain after traveling through hill country with little water and few inhabitants.

Rode to Kairwan.
