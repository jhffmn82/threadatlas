Oushda in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Oushda is a northeastern frontier town and oasis in the Angad Desert, with 600 to 1,000 Moors and Arabs. A dilapidated fortress and garden walls protect clay houses, winding streets, and spring-irrigated gardens amid severe aridity.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Oushda is a frontier town in the northeast, protected by a fortress and enclosed by garden walls. Its small population lives among low clay houses and winding streets, while irrigated gardens produce melons, olives, and figs; the surrounding Desert of Angad is extremely arid, especially in summer.
