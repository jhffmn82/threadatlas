Atlas Jews in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Atlas Jews are dispersed through the Atlas ranges, living with quasi-independence under protecting Berber chieftains. They bear arms, speak Shelouh, dress like mountaineers, keep their own sheiks for domestic matters, and rarely go to plains or cities. They communicate through mountain networks, follow Berbers, regard Moroccan Jews as heretics, and are described as Caraaites.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The Atlas Jews live dispersed through the Atlas ranges and enjoy a degree of independence from imperial authority under the protection of Berber chieftains. They bear arms, speak Shelouh, dress like mountaineers, maintain their own sheiks for domestic matters, and rarely descend to the plains or cities.
