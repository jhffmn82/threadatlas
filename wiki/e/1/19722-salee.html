Salee in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Salee, or Sala, is an ancient commercial and fortified city of Beni-Hassan on the right bank of the Bouragrag River near its mouth. Its small port is too shallow for large vessels. Captured by Alphonso the Wise in 1263, it returned to Moorish rule and later became a center of piracy; population estimates varied, with two or three thousand reported by a resident.

Salee, or Sala, is an ancient city on the right bank of the Bouragrag near its mouth and a major commercial and fortified city of Beni-Hassan. It was captured by Alphonso the Wise in 1263, returned to Moorish rule, and later became a center of piracy; its population estimates vary widely, and Christians and Jews were not permitted to reside there.
