Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The religion of the Atlas Jews is described as differing from that of Jews generally: they are said to resemble Caraaites, to be unacquainted with Ezra or Christ, and to call the Supreme Being Shadai. Their written law is said to omit the deluge, describe distinct human species, and recount God's appearance to Ishmael.
