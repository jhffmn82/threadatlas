Abstract written by a model

The Shereefs are a dynasty and family of descendants of the Prophet, profoundly venerated by true Mussulmen. Originating in and inhabiting Tafilett, including a small fortified town where many royal family members live in modest or impoverished conditions, they form a southern African branch distinct from the Shereefs of Fez. They claim the Moorish imperial throne and are presented as Mussulman sovereigns whose rulers exercise inviolable Shereefian privileges. Their authority combines regal and sacerdotal elements, though they are not strictly priests. Having once possessed the throne, they are jealous of the Marabouts; their court also defended slavery through Islamic scripture and tradition.

CHAPTER II.

The Shereefs are presented as descendants of the Prophet’s family and as profoundly venerated by true Mussulmen. Many members of the royal family reside at Tafilett, and the Shereefs there are described as living in a small fortified town in moderate or impoverished circumstances.

[5] In the Shereefian lineage of Muley Suleiman, copied for Ali Bey by the Emper

The Shereefs are presented as the lineage of Mussulman sovereigns claiming descent from the Prophets of God. Their rulers are described as exercising divine or inviolable Shereefian privileges, and the Shereefian Court defended slavery as authorized by Islamic scripture and tradition.

CHAPTER V.

The Shereefs are descendants or family members of the Prophet and inhabit Tafilett, which is called the country of the Shereefs. The ruling family is described as a southern African branch of Shereefs rather than Shereefs of Fez.

notes

The Shereefs are described as descendants of the Prophet who combine regal and sacerdotal authority, corresponding to the type of Melchizedek rather than to a strictly priestly order. Because they once possessed the throne, they are said to be jealous of the Marabouts.
