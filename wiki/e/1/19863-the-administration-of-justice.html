James Richardson · 1860

The Bey heard many plaintiffs in Kairwan, issuing redress orders or referrals to kadys and chiefs, while summarily dismissing many; no defendants appeared.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Rode to Kairwan.

The Bey's administration of justice involved hearing many plaintiffs, though no defendants were brought before him. Some people received orders intended to obtain redress, while others were referred to kadys and chiefs; many plaintiffs were dismissed summarily.
