Sheikh Tahid in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Sheikh Tahid, the leading man at Toser, collects the Jereed’s tribute for the Bey. When he fails to have it ready, the Bey imprisons, bastinadoes, and chains him while searches find no money. His gardens are sold for 15,000 piastres and his wife adds 1,000, securing his release.

Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER X.

Sheikh Tahid is imprisoned because he has not prepared the Bey’s tribute. He is repeatedly bastinadoed, including an additional fifty strokes, and is chained after directing searchers to several supposed hiding places where no money is found; he maintains that he cannot pay.

His gardens were sold for 15,000 piastres, and his wife added 1,000 more. He was then set at liberty.
