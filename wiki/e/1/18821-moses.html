Moses in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Moses, a wise and valiant leader, possesses Judea and leads its colony, builds Jerusalem and its temple, institutes worship and laws, divides the people into twelve tribes, and organizes government and priesthood.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXVIL

Moses is described as the wise and valiant leader of the colony that settled in Judea. He possessed the country, built Jerusalem and its temple, instituted the worship of God, established laws, divided the people into twelve tribes, and organized their government and priesthood.
