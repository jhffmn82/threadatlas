Abstract written by a model

Azamor is an ancient city in Dukaila on the southern bank near the mouth of the Omm-er-Rbia River. Its fertile surroundings and Shebbel salmon trade matter most. Portuguese captured and fortified it in 1513, then abandoned it about a century later.

Passing Tangier after a day's journey, we come to Arzila or Asila, in the provin

Azamor, or Azemmour, is an ancient Berber city of Dukaila whose name means olives in the Berber language. It lies near the mouth of the Omm-er-Rbia, has fertile surroundings and a trade in Shebbel salmon, and was captured and fortified by the Portuguese in 1513 before being abandoned about a century later.
