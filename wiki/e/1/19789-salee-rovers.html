Abstract written by a model

Salee rovers were Moroccan pirates based at Salee and Rabat, forming a nearly independent military republic; bitter enemies of Christians, they intercepted British traders near Lundy Island and ranged as far as the British Channel.

The Salee rovers were Moroccan pirates based at Salee and Rabat who formed a nearly independent military republic. They were described as exceptionally ferocious and courageous and once operated as far away as Lundy Island in the British Channel.
