Graeberg de Hemso in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Consul-general for Sweden and Sardinia at Morocco, Graeberg de Hemso describes Moroccan towns and estimates Morocco's population at 8,500,000, considered exaggerated.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Graeberg supplies population figures and descriptions for many Moroccan towns. The author considers his total estimate of 8,500,000 exaggerated but more moderate than Jackson's, suggesting that deducting two million may produce a more probable figure.
