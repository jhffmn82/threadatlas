Abstract written by a model

Hawks caught hobaras, hares, and other birds during the expedition, although strong wind sometimes prevented them from being flown. Dogs caught hares, gazelles, and a fox, while Arabs and members of the expedition also shot or pursued game. The hawks used in hunting hares had their legs connected by a leather strap to prevent strain when one claw seized the hare and the other caught the ground.
