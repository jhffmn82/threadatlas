Moorish authorities in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Moorish authorities at Rabat held high duties and commerce monopolies, detained European residents during danger, then fled or failed to prevent the city's destruction.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

APPENDIX.

The Moorish government, represented locally by the Governor and Deputy-Governor, detained European residents in the hope that their presence might aid the city's defense or lessen the bombardment. Its authorities later fled or failed to prevent the city's destruction.
