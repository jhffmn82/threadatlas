Abstract written by a model

Dar-el-Beida, or Casa-Blanco, is a small Atlantic town in Shawiya, on the ruins of Anfa or Anafa, which the Portuguese destroyed in 1468 before building the town. It has a small port and spacious bay for large vessels, but only about 200 to 1,000 inhabitants.

Dar-el-Beida, or Casa-Blanco, is a small Atlantic town built by the Portuguese on the ruins of Anafa, which they destroyed in 1468. It has a small port and a spacious bay capable of sheltering large vessels, but its population is estimated at only about one thousand or as few as two hundred.
