Wazen in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Wazen is a small unwalled city in Azgar and the Gharb, residence of the hereditary High Priest or Grand Marabout, whose substantial influence accompanies the district’s exemption from imperial tribute.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Wazen, in the province of Azgar and the region of the Gharb, is a small city without walls and the residence of the hereditary High Priest or Grand Marabout. Its religious ruler exercises substantial influence, and the district pays no tribute to the imperial government.
