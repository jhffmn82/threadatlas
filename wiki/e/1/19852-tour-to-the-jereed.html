CHAPTER VIII.

The Tour to the Jereed is introduced as a narrative extracted from the tourists' notebooks and supplemented with the author's observations. It is intended to provide further details about the region, including Toser, Nefta, and Ghafsa.

CHAPTER IX.

The tour took Captain Balfour and Richard Reade across plains and hills toward Kairwan. Along the route they saw ruined towns, ancient bridges and aqueducts, saints' chapels, Arab villages, camels, gazelles, and varied game, while enduring cold mornings and evenings and very hot midday weather.
