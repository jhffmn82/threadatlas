El Araish

Also called: El-Araish, El-Araish Beai-Arous, Laratsh

Abstract written by a model

El Araish, also called Laratsh, is an Atlantic city and the capital and governor’s seat of Azgar, replacing ancient Lixus. Situated at the mouth of the Cos or Luccos River on a small promontory, it has Spanish-made fortifications and a secure but difficult port. It has about 4,000 people and a 500-troop garrison, was captured by the Spanish in 1610 and recaptured by Muley Ishmael in 1689, and produces cotton and exports cork and other goods.

El Araish is in one work on this site, Travels in Morocco. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

El Araish, also called Laratsh, replaces the ancient city of Lixus and is the capital and seat of the governor of the district of Azgar. It has a population of about four thousand according to one estimate, a garrison of five hundred troops, a difficult port on the river Cos or Luccos, and exports cork, wool, skins, bark, beans, and grain.
