Shereefian dynasty in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

The Shereefian dynasty is Morocco’s reigning southern Shereef dynasty, originating from a family near Medina in Arabia. It gained control through successive usurpations and divided into the Hoseinee and Fileli (El Fileli or Tafilett) branches; the latter became the present ruling line. Its rulers must sometimes favor Fez while facing restless tribes and rival political centers.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The Shereefian dynasty originated from a family near Medina in Arabia and gained control of Morocco through successive usurpations. It is divided into the Hoseinee branch and the El Fileli, or Tafilett, branch, with the latter forming the present ruling line.

CHAPTER V.

The present dynasty belongs to Morocco and is identified as a dynasty of Shereefs from the south. Its rulers must favor Fez at times to maintain the allegiance of the northern country, while the dynasty’s authority is threatened by restless tribes and rival political centers.
