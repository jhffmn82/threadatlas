The Unique Adventures of the WOGGLE-BUG

Bridget receives the Wagnerian Plaids from the young woman's husband as a substitute for wages and wears them to Mickey Schwartz's ball. When the Woggle-Bug embraces her, she steps on his toes, causing him to release her, and later sells the gown to a second-hand dealer for three dollars.
