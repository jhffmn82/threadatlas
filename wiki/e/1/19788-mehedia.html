Mehedia

Also called: Mamora, Nuova Mamora

Abstract written by a model

Mehedia, also called Mamora or Nuova Mamora, is a maritime town in Beni-Hassan on the left bank of the Sebon River at its mouth, near the Mamora plain and forest. Built by Jakob-el-Mansour to defend the mouth, it was captured by Spanish in 1614, recaptured by Moors in 1681, and became a weak settlement with fishermen, a small garrison, and a crumbling castle.
