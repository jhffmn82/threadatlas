Abstract written by a model

El-Sibhah, also called Sibhah-el-Soudeeat, is a vast salt lake or salt plain in El-Jereed. Extending about seventy English miles north-east to south-west, it has seasonally changing dry islands and a hard central salt surface. Its agreeable salt is weaker than sea salt and is neither exported nor commercially used.

CHAPTER VIII.

El-Sibhah, literally the salt plain, is a vast salt lake in El-Jereed. It extends about seventy English miles from north-east to south-west and contains dry areas whose number and extent vary with the season. Its salt is weaker than sea salt, has an agreeable flavour, and is not exported or used commercially.
