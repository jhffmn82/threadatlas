Nefta in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Nefta, ancient Negeta, is a southern frontier town of the Tunisian territories, about fourteen miles from Toser. Villages divided by gardens, supplied by Wad Nefta, contain mosques, schools, markets, saints, merchants, proprietors, and Shereefs. Ruled through its Shereefs, it is the gate of Tunis and an intermediary for Gulf-region caravan trade toward the Sahara.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Nefta, the ancient Negeta, lies about fourteen miles from Toser and is the southern frontier town of the Tunisian territories. It consists of villages separated by gardens, has abundant water, mosques, schools, markets, saints, merchants, landed proprietors, and Shereefs, and serves as an entrepôt called the gate of Tunis for commerce toward the Sahara.
