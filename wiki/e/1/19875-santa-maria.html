Abstract written by a model

Santa Maria is a Frenchman travelling with the Bey’s party, experienced in North Africa and fluent in Arabic. After returning a horse to the Bey and receiving another, he is criticized for delicacy and impudence toward the Bey’s officers. Assigned by the Bey of the Camp to espionage, he leaves for Ghabs with twelve men, returns safely, and is praised as enterprising. He then goes to Beja to report, planning later visits to Tripoli and Ghadames.

Santa Maria is in one work on this site, Travels in Morocco. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER X.

Santa Maria returns a horse to the Bey and receives another in exchange, and he is criticized for his delicate feeling and impudence toward the Bey’s officers. He later leaves for Ghabs with an escort of twelve men, although no reliable route to that place is shown on the charts.

On our march back to Tunis, we encamped for two days by the foot of a range of h

Santa Maria arrived safely from Ghabs and was described as enterprising and worthy of imitation. He had spent many years in North Africa, spoke Arabic fluently, and planned to visit Tripoli and Ghadames the following season. The party took leave of him when he went to Beja to give an account of his espionage.
