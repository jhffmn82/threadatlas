Abstract written by a model

Sunday is a day of rest, Sabbatic peace, and rejoicing. It unfolds first in calm beauty: a cliff walk, morning churchgoing, sunset, and moonlit scenery, before a night storm sharply changes the mood. Later, quiet roads near Land’s End are nearly empty as local people go to church or school; despite guilt about traveling during church hours, the narrator finds deep peace and believes Sunday can be kept outside church doors.

Sunday is described as a peaceful day of rest and rejoicing, marked by a walk on the cliffs, attendance at church, a sunset, and moonlit scenery. Its calm beauty contrasts sharply with the storm that arrives during the night.
