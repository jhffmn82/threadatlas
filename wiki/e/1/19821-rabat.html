Rabat in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Rabat, or Er-Rabat, is a large, densely populated, fortified modern city in Temsna, opposite Salee on the Bouragrag River. Built by Jakob-el-Mansour as Rabat-el-Fatah, it has walls, gardens, plantations, forts, and a mausoleum associated with Almanzor. Its mostly Spain-expelled Moors and three or four thousand Jews support interior and foreign trade.

Rabat, or Er-Rabat, is a large, densely populated, fortified city opposite Salee on the Bouragrag River. It was built by Yakob-el-Mansour as Rabat-el-Fatah, contains wealthy Jewish residents and active merchants, and has gardens, plantations, forts, and a mausoleum associated with Almanzor.
