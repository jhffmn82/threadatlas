Mazagran

Abstract written by a model

Mazagran is an ancient, strong castle on a peninsula at the bottom of a spacious bay. Rebuilt by the Portuguese in 1506 as Castillo Real, it is also called El-Bureeja, meaning the citadel. Its thick walls and small port remain after the Portuguese abandoned it in 1769; it has two or three hundred inhabitants.

Mazagran is an ancient and strong castle on a peninsula at the bottom of a spacious bay, rebuilt by the Portuguese in 1506 as Castillo Real. The Portuguese abandoned it in 1769, making it their last stronghold in Morocco; it has thick walls, a small port, and a population of two or three hundred.
