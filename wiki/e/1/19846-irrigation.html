Irrigation is essential to date-palms: in spring and summer, small channels carry stream water to tree basins, where clay embankments retain it until it soaks into earth; palms tolerate salt, sweet, or nitre-impregnated water.

CHAPTER VIII.

Irrigation is essential to the date-palm and is carried out in spring and throughout the summer. Small channels carry water from a stream to basins formed around individual trees, where clay embankments hold the water until it soaks into the earth. The palms tolerate water that is salt, sweet, or impregnated with nitre.
