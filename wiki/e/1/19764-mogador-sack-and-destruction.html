Mogador sack and destruction in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

The sack and destruction of Mogador unfolded as French forces attacked from outside and plunder began within after Moorish authorities and people fled. Wild tribes, Moorish rabble, Berbers, and Arabs attacked and looted residents, especially Moroccan Jews, their homes, women, possessions, and records, leaving Mogador in ruins.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The plunder of Mogador was carried out from within while French forces attacked from outside. Moorish rabble targeted Jewish houses and women, while Berbers and Arabs later plundered both Jews and Moors who had escaped with booty.

APPENDIX.

The sack began after the Moorish authorities and population fled, when wild tribes broke into houses, attacked residents, looted goods, and burned property and records. Mogador was left a heap of ruins, with scarcely any house standing entire.
