Abstract written by a model

Mogador Jewesses are Jewish women settled in Mogador, prominent in European society as belles attending balls. Exempt from walking barefoot, they cross religious and cultural boundaries through European dress and husbands.

CHAPTER I.

The European Jewesses of Mogador are described as the belles of society and as attending its balls. They are said to forget religious differences more readily than Jewish men, and some native Jewesses adopt European clothing and seek husbands who wear European hats or coats.
