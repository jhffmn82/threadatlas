king David in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

David is a king associated with music: he favours the kinnor, probably a lyre or small triangular harp, maintains a royal private band at Jerusalem, and is depicted playing harp and rotta in manuscripts from the seventh through eleventh centuries.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

David's favorite instrument was probably the kinnor, understood as a lyre or possibly a small triangular harp. He and Solomon appear to have maintained royal bands, while sacred singers and instrumental performers served in the Temple.

CHAPTER VII.

King David is depicted playing the harp in an Anglo-Saxon manuscript from the beginning of the eleventh century. Other manuscripts show him playing the rotta, including representations from the seventh, eighth, and tenth centuries.
