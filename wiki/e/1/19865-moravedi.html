Moravedi in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Moravedi is a Marabout dynasty, a family of priests that rose to consequence about 1050 and formerly ruled Morocco, retaining memory of sovereignty.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes

The Marabouts are described as a family of priests who have not forgotten that they once ruled Morocco. They are distinguished from the Shereefs, who combine princely and sacerdotal authority.
