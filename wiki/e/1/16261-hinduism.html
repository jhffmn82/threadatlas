Abstract written by a model

Brahmanism, a religious tradition associated with the Brahmans, sought to unite worshippers through the Trimurti of Brahma, Vishnu, and Siva. It opposed Buddhism for nine centuries, ultimately expelling it from India, then developed into many sects, polytheism, and idolatry.

Chapter XXX

Brahmanism sought to unite different classes of worshippers through the Trimurti of Brahma, Vishnu, and Siva. It struggled with Buddhism for nine centuries and ultimately expelled Buddhism from India, while later developing into many sects and forms of polytheism and idolatry.

BUDDHA

Hinduism is represented through the Brahmanical Hindus, who hold that the soul's confinement in a human body is a consequence of former frailties and sins. Brahmanical tradition also teaches that some exceptional individuals voluntarily descend to earth to promote human welfare.
