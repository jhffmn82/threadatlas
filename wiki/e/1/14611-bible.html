In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Scriptures relate the corresponding events in the histories of Adam and Noah. In Musical Instruments, the Bible mentions the kinnor, nebel, psanterin, asor, chalil, nekeb, mishrokitha, sumphonia, toph, and several kinds of trumpets, cymbals, and bells; it supplies the names of many Hebrew instruments but does not provide representations sufficient to establish their construction.
