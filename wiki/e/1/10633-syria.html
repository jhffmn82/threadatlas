Syria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Syria is a kingdom and region repeatedly contested and brought low. Antipater assigned it to Laomedon of Mitylene, but Ptolemy captured Laomedon and took all Syria. Antigonus entered Upper Syria, faced demands to yield it to Ptolemy, later recovered it after Ptolemy withdrew, and returned there after abandoning Egypt. After the Romans conquered Perseus, Syria endured disturbances from rival royal plots and popular changes of rulers.
