James Richardson · 1860

Captain Balfour, a tourist on the Jereed tour, prefers Trungah dates; joining Richard Reade, he narrowly avoids a ceremonial-gun wad, hunts, and observes camp life.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

Captain Balfour joined the tour through the Jereed with Richard Reade. A wad from a ceremonial gun nearly struck him on the head, and he later hunted with the Bey's doctor and observed the Turkish Infantry and the camp's daily movements.
