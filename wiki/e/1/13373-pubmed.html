Abstract written by a model

In "Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine," PubMed supplied abstracts as a biomedical retrieval corpus for MEDRAG and was evaluated across MIRAGE. In "Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology," PubMed was used to collect relevant CLBP articles and the latest medical guidelines for the knowledge base. In "MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health," PubMed supplied authoritative public-health evidence, helped curate HealthQuestDB, and contributed abstracts to an external knowledge base. In Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation: PubMed is one of the two large corpora used in RAG2's balanced retrieval strategy. In "Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review," PubMed was searched for studies and served as a major source for datasets and information used in reviewed systems.

PubMed in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where PubMed first appears

Abstract written by a model

PubMed is a biomedical retrieval corpus of 23.9 million documents and snippets, supplying abstracts for MEDRAG. Evaluated across MIRAGE, it is a robust corpus and, besides MedCorp, the only single corpus improving all tasks. It is especially useful for PubMedQA* and BioASQ-Y/N; RRF-2 combining BM25 and MedCPT is recommended.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

PubMed is evaluated as one of the retrieval corpora in MEDRAG. It is described as a robust choice across all MIRAGE tasks, whereas point-of-care articles and textbooks are mainly helpful for examination questions.

The MEDRAG Toolkit

PubMed supplies biomedical abstracts for MEDRAG. Its corpus contains 23.9 million documents and 23.9 million snippets, with an average snippet length of 296.

Results .. Practical Recommendations

PubMed is a 23.9-million-item corpus and the only single corpus besides MedCorp that improves performance on all MIRAGE tasks. It is especially useful for PubMedQA* and BioASQ-Y/N, and RRF-2 combining BM25 and MedCPT is recommended for this corpus.
