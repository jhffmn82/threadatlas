CHAPTER IX.

The 300 Turkish Infantry followed the mounted troops by about two hours and were honored by the Bey when they caught up. They were described as highly irregular, performed sham sword fights and music for the Bey, guarded the camp at night, and repeatedly shouted 'Alleya.'
