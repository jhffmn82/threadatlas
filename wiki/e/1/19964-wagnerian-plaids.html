Abstract written by a model

The Wagnerian Plaids are a gorgeous, brightly colored red checked gown, greatly reduced to $7.93. They win the Woggle-Bug's affection and repeatedly lead him to pursue whoever wears them. The gown passes from a young woman to Bridget, a Swedish widow, and a wash-lady; the wash-lady sells it to a Chinaman, who remakes it as a Chinese robe.

The Unique Adventures of the WOGGLE-BUG

The Wagnerian Plaids are a brightly colored checked gown made from red cloth. They attract the Woggle-Bug's affection, pass from the young woman in the Wagnerian plaids to Bridget and then to the Swedish widow, and repeatedly cause him to pursue their wearer.

The widow trades the Wagnerian costume to a friend who had been a wash-lady for General Funston, receiving a crazy quilt, a nearly new corset, and mismatched silk stockings. The wash-lady wears it while delivering wash, but later sells it to a Chinaman, who remakes it into a Chinese robe.
