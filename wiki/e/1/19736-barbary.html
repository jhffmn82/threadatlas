Barbary in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Barbary, also called the land of Barbary, is a North African region containing Morocco and including Algiers among the Barbary States. Its peoples were called Barbarians or Berbers by Romans and Arabs, giving rise to the term Barbary States; its inhabitants reject human sacrifice and may sacrifice animals to God or tutelar saints.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Barbary is the region in which the aboriginal tribes of North Africa were called Barbarians or Berbers by the Romans and Arabs. The term Barbary States derives from this designation.

notes

Barbary is described as a region whose inhabitants reject human sacrifice and may sacrifice animals to God or tutelar saints. The notes also discuss its cities, peoples, mountains, deserts, agriculture, and political divisions.
