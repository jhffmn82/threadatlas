famous mosque (Kairwan) in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

The famous mosque at Kairwan, inaccessible to R.'s party, was said by Giovanni to contain marble pillars and ornaments taken from Christian churches and Pagan temples.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Rode to Kairwan.

R. and his party were not allowed to enter the famous mosque at Kairwan. They heard from Giovanni that many of its marble pillars and other ornaments had been taken from Christian churches and Pagan temples.
