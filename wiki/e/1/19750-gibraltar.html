Abstract written by a model

Gibraltar is the European Pillar of Hercules, opposite the African pillar and Ceuta across the Straits of Gibraltar. It was conceded to England by the peace of Utrecht, with arrangements for provisioning its garrison. Tangier became its principal trading partner, sending cattle, poultry, eggs, flour, and other provisions; about 3,000 cattle annually went at a fixed duty, with roughly 2,000 more at higher rates. Monkeys were fancifully said to travel beneath the straits.

The peace of Utrecht conceded Gibraltar to England, which the text says more than compensated England for the loss of Tangier. A treaty or arrangement also supplied provisions, especially cattle, to the garrison of Gibraltar.

CHAPTER IV.

Gibraltar is described as lying opposite Ceuta across the Straits of Gibraltar and as the European counterpart of the African Pillars of Hercules. The text also mentions the supposed subterranean passage beneath the straits through which monkeys were fancifully believed to travel between Gibraltar and Africa.

APPENDIX.

Gibraltar was Tangier's principal trading partner and received cattle, poultry, eggs, flour, and other provisions. About 3,000 cattle were annually exported to Gibraltar at a fixed duty, with roughly 2,000 more exported at higher rates.
