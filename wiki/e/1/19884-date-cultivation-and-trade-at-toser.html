date cultivation and trade at Toser in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Toser’s abundantly irrigated date gardens produce prized dates, the inhabitants’ near-exclusive food; stones cover streets, and the Bey taxes each tree at two bunches.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Toser’s date gardens are abundantly irrigated, and its dates are considered among the finest. Dates form almost the only food of the inhabitants, their stones cover the streets, and the tribute imposed on the area is equivalent to two bunches from each date-tree.
