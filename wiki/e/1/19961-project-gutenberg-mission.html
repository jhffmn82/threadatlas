In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the Project Gutenberg mission promotes the free distribution of electronic works and preserves free future access to them. In The Woggle-Bug Book, the Project Gutenberg mission promotes the free distribution of electronic works in formats readable by a wide variety of computers.
