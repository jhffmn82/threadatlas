CHAPTER IX.

The Bey's expedition traveled southward through the Jereed with mounted guards, Turkish Infantry, artillery, camp followers, baggage animals, and attendants. It followed a set routine of striking camp before daylight, marching, saluting successive groups, hunting, and encamping at places such as Beereen, Tfeefleeah, Heelet-el-Gazlen, Ben Sayden, and Jebanah.
