the caste system in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The caste system divided Hindus into hereditary ranked classes: Brahmans, Shatriyas, Vaissyas, Sudras, and Pariahs. Occupations, privileges, restrictions, and Vedas instruction varied, with Sudras excluded and Pariahs facing harsher social and religious restrictions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXX

The caste system divided Hindus into hereditary classes with distinct ranks, occupations, privileges, and restrictions. The first three castes could receive instruction from the Vedas, while the Sudras were excluded from that instruction and the Pariahs were subjected to even harsher social and religious restrictions.
