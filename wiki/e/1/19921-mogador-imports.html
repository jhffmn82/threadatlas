Abstract written by a model

Mogador imports comprised British and foreign goods, including Manchester cottons, foodstuffs, metals, textiles, and mirrors; in 1855 British imports totaled L136,496 7s. 6d., with goods consumed inland to Timbuctoo.

APPENDIX.

Mogador imported Manchester cotton goods, blue salampores, prints, sugar, tea, coffee, iron, steel, spices, drugs, nails, beads, deals, woollen cloth, cotton wool, and mirrors. In 1855 British imports were valued at L136,496 7s. 6d. and foreign imports at L31,222 11s. 5d.
