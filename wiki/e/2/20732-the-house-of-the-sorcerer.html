Abstract written by a model

The House of the Sorcerer is a house in a city under a dome, formerly occupied by the Sorcerer of Mangaboo. Dorothy, Zeb, Jim, and Eureka are ordered there so the Sorcerer can determine the truth about the Rain of Stones. Later, the Wizard chooses it as their lodging: its bare domed hall has high air-reached doorways, furnished glass rooms, and no beds. After the Princess spares them, the Wizard, Zeb, and Dorothy stay peacefully for days while piglets play below with Eureka and Jim.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

The Wizard decides to use the House of the Sorcerer as a place for the travelers to stay. Inside, they find a bare domed hall, high doorways reachable by walking through the air, furnished glass rooms, and no beds.

The Wizard, Zeb, and Dorothy are permitted to occupy the House of the Sorcerer peacefully for several days after the Princess spares them. The nine tiny piglets later wander from the Wizard's room into the dome's lower part, where they play with Eureka beside Jim.
