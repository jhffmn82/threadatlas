learned table relevance

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Learned Table Relevance

Abstract written by a model

Learned table relevance adds a binary token before answers: “yes” for gold tables and “no” for retrieved non-gold tables. During generation, tables led by “yes” are prioritized as reliable sources; confidence scores apply when all candidate tokens are “no”.
