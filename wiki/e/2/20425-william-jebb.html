TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

William Jebb was a fluent and well-informed debater in the old Pioneer composing room and had also been a reporter on the St. Paul Times. After writing about the theft of his prized chickens and ending the report with a papal bull of excommunication, he left reporting.
