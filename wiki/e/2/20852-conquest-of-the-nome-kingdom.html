conquest of the Nome Kingdom in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The Nome King’s defeat begins when the Scarecrow blinds him with Billina’s eggs, causing his warriors to flee. Unable to see or escape, he loses his jeweled belt; afterward the silent Nomes allow Ozma, the Queen mother, the royal children, and companions to return safely toward Ev.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The Nome King’s defeat begins when the Scarecrow blinds him with Billina’s eggs and his warriors flee. The King remains unable to see or escape while Billina directs Dorothy to take his jeweled belt.

19. The King of Ev

The defeat of the Nome King leaves the Nomes silent and respectful instead of laughing at the adventurers. It also allows Ozma, the Queen mother, the royal children, and their companions to return safely toward Ev.
