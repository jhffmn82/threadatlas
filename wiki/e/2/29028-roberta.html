In Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain, the RoBERTa Model was used as an encoder-only answer-span extraction baseline fine-tuned on SQuAD2 and Natural-Questions. In RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems, the RoBERTa model was used as a finetuned model for the RAG evaluation task and outperformed LLM-based RAG evaluation methods in the reported benchmarking.
