contains mixed bloods and white women and children [about 250 captives] The Indian camp at Camp Release contained many Indians who had participated in the massacres and held about 250 mixed bloods and white women and children. The entire camp surrendered after Gen. Sibley avoided a hostile demonstration that he believed might lead to the prisoners' annihilation.
