Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FIREMEN AND FIRES OF PIONEER DAYS.

Mrs. Bartlett Presley was described as a guardian angel of the volunteer fire department. She attended fires in severe weather and personally provided coffee and sandwiches to tired and hungry firemen, earning their lasting gratitude.
