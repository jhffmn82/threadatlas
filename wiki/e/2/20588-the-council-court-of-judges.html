A sacred, sworn tribunal established by Athena on Ares’ Hill, the council-court permanently judges bloodshed, guards Athens, rejects gain, punishes swiftly, and remains watchful.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[_Re-enter Athena, with twelve Athenian citizens_.

Athena establishes the council-court of judges as a permanent tribunal for bloodshed on Ares’ Hill. She declares it sacred, free from the lust of gain, swift to punish, and continually watchful over the country.
