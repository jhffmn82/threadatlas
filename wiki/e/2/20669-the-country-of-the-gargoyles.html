Abstract written by a model

The Country of the Gargoyles is a silent land made entirely of wood: sawdust ground, shaving-leaf trees, splinter grass, and wooden animals, houses, and people. Thickly inhabited by flying Gargoyles, it has underground magic that enables their wings. It stretches for miles beneath prisoners carried through the air, past a wooden city toward a cloud-high rock and possible surface archway.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The Country of the Gargoyles is a silent wooden land where the ground is sawdust, trees have shaving leaves, grass is made of splinters, and birds, cows, houses, and people are wooden. It is thickly inhabited by flying Gargoyles.

12. A Wonderful Escape

The wooden country stretches for miles beneath the prisoners as the Gargoyles carry them through the air. It contains a wooden city and a rock rising into the clouds with an archway that may lead to the surface world.
