Capaneus

person · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: such wight, the giant, Καπανέως, Καπανεὺς
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Capaneus joins Adrastus's expedition against Thebes and is killed while attempting to scale the walls.
  • In Four Plays of Aeschylus, Capaneus is an Argive champion assigned to the Electran gates who boasts that neither gods nor Zeus's warrior maid can stop him from destroying the city; his shield shows a weaponless man carrying a torch with the words "Fire for the city bring I," and Eteocles sets Polyphontes against him.
  • In The Library, Capaneus attacks the Ogygian Gate, climbs its walls by ladder, and Zeus strikes him with a thunderbolt, prompting Argive flight.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Capaneus, the son of Hipponous, makes a violent attack on the walls of Thebes at Electris and is said to have been struck by lightning there.
