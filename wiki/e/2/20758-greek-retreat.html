Greek retreat

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: a runaway, escape, fled to Argolis, flight, flying, the Greek flight, they mean to fly, when the Greek is smitten

Abstract written by a model

In The Rhesus of Euripides, the Greek retreat was an anticipated flight to the Greek ships inferred from the beacons and the apparent movement of the Greek army toward the ships, though Aeneas warned that the signs might conceal a strategy. In Hecuba and other plays, the Greek flight was suspected because fires blazed throughout the Greek camp and near the fleet, though the Chorus and Aeneas noted that the Greeks’ actual intention had not yet been established.
