the Argive decree protecting the maidens

Abstract written by a model

forbids the threatened forced marriage [by prohibiting forcible removal] The decree states that the maidens may remain in the land free from seizure or violation, and that no native or foreign hand may drag them away. Anyone among the countrymen who fails to aid them is threatened with the people’s curse and banishment.
