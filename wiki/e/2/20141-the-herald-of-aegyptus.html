Abstract written by a model

A herald representing Aegyptus’ sons claims the Danaids as property and tries to force them back to Aegyptus’ ship, rejecting local gods; the King of Argos stops him under an Argive decree and sends him away.

Re-enter DANAUS.

The Herald attempts to compel the Danaids to return to Aegyptus’ ship, dismissing the authority of the local gods and threatening to drag the maidens away by their hair. The King of Argos stops him and sends him away after declaring that the Argive decree forbids such force.
