Major Sholto’s death in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Major Sholto’s death, on April 28, 1882, followed a confession to his sons that he had concealed Captain Morstan’s death and kept Morstan’s treasure share. As he began to disclose the treasure’s location, a bearded face appeared at the window; he cried out and died before the narrator arrived. His papers revealed nothing, and Morstan’s daughter soon began receiving yearly valuable presents.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

Major Sholto’s death occurred on April 28, 1882. Holmes notes that within a week of it, Captain Morstan’s daughter began receiving valuable presents that continued yearly.

Chapter IV

Before his death, Major Sholto confessed to his sons that he had concealed Captain Morstan’s death and retained Morstan’s share of the treasure. While he was revealing the treasure’s hiding place, a bearded face appeared at the window, after which Major Sholto cried out and died.
