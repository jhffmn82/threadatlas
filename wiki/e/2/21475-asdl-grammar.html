ASDL provides concise notations for describing the abstract syntax of programming languages. In BertranX, it defines grammatical symbols and supports action sequences for describing ASTs.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.2

The ASDL grammar determines which child nodes are legitimate after the tokens already produced. It is used during retrieval to filter out nodes that are not supported by the grammar.
