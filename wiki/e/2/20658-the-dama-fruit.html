the dama-fruit in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The dama-fruit is a delicious, fragrant fruit that grows on a low bush or plant and resembles a peach in size and appearance. Its enticing perfume tempts people, birds, bears, and Eureka; when eaten, it makes anyone invisible, though the travelers avoid it.

The dama-fruit grows on a low plant and resembles a peach in size and appearance. It is delicious and makes anyone who eats it invisible.
