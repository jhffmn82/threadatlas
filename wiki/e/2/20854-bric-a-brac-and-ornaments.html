Abstract written by a model

Bric-a-brac and ornaments are varied objects made of metals, glass, china, stones, marbles, and precious gems. The Nome King uses them as transformed forms of the Queen of Ev and her ten children throughout his palace, then transforms Ozma into a new ornament.

11. The Nome King

The Nome King uses bric-a-brac and ornaments as transformed forms for the Queen of Ev and her ten children, scattering them throughout his palace. He also threatens to transform Ozma into one if she fails to identify any of the royal family correctly.

12. The Eleven Guesses

The palace contains many ornaments made from metals, glass, china, stones, marbles, and precious gems, among them the transformed members of the royal family of Ev. Ozma becomes a new ornament.
