Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PRINTERS AND EDITORS OF TERRITORIAL DAYS.

Capt. E.Y. Shelly was born in Bucks county, Pa., in 1827, learned printing in Philadelphia, served in the Third United States dragoons during the Mexican War, and arrived in St. Paul in July 1849 as the first printer to permanently locate in Minnesota. He worked for the Register, the Pioneer, and the Daily Democrat, and later organized a company with Major Brackett that served in Brackett's battalion during the Civil War. He was mustered out in 1865 and afterward continued working at printing until within a few years of the account.
