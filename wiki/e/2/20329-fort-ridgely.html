Abstract written by a model

Fort Ridgely was a lightly protected post besieged during the Great Sioux Outbreak in 1862 by an estimated 700 or 800 Sioux warriors. About 150 Fifth Regiment men, fifty Renville Rangers, and civilians defended it for more than a week until relief arrived with the advance of Sibley’s force.

THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

Fort Ridgely was a lightly protected post defended by about 150 Fifth Regiment men, fifty Renville Rangers, and civilians against an estimated 700 or 800 Sioux. Its garrison endured a siege for more than a week before relief arrived under Col. McPhail, the advance of Sibley’s force.
