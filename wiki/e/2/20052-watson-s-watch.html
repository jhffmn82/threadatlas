Watson’s watch in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Watson’s watch, inherited by his elder brother from their father and later in Dr. John Watson’s possession, bears dents, cuts, pawnbroker ticket numbers, and key-hole scratches. The Sign of the Four / Chapter I

Watson’s watch has recently been cleaned, but Holmes examines its case and mechanism with a lens and still draws conclusions about its former owner. Dents, scratches, pawnbrokers’ numbers, and marks around the key-hole lead Holmes to infer carelessness, financial difficulties, intermittent prosperity, and drunkenness.
