Dost Akbar in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Dost Akbar, a Sikh and Abdullah Khan’s foster-brother, knows Achmet’s secret and is expected to guide him to an isolated gate for a share of treasure. He helps carry Achmet’s body into the fort, swears with the conspirators, and joins their bargain with Major Sholto and Captain Morstan.
