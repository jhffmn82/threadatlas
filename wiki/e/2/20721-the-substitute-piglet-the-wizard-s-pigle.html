is_a replacement piglet [substituted for the lost pet] The substitute piglet is supplied by the Wizard and produced by the Tin Woodman to make it appear that Princess Ozma's pet has been found. Eureka points out that it lacks the emerald collar, and Ozma returns it to the Wizard after the real piglet is recovered.
