CoNaLa dataset in Syntax-Aware Retrieval Augmented Code Generation

Xiangyu Zhang, Yu Zhou, Guang Yang, Taolue Chen · 2023

CoNaLa is a publicly available code-generation dataset obtained through data mining. It contains 600,000 natural-language/code pairs from StackOverflow, including 2,879 developer-rewritten natural-language queries, and covers programming-project questions. Its mined content includes irrelevant material that can add retrieval noise. It is used to evaluate BertranX and kNN-TRANX, including exact match, ablations, Krule/Kpri settings, and Figure 5 analyses of datastore size and reductions of syntactic and semantic knowledge using BLEU and CodeBLEU; kNN-TRANX improves exact match by 27.6%.
