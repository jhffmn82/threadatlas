TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

O.G. Miller was a well-known musically inclined printer in St. Paul. He belonged to the Great Western band, sang tenor in several churches, and was a 33d Degree Mason; a midnight funeral service was held for him in Masonic hall after his death.
