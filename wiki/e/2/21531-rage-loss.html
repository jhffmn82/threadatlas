RAGE loss

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: original RAGE loss

Abstract written by a model

RAGE loss is modified for tables by dropping its third term, which penalizes documents without gold answers that do not contribute to successful question-answering. Keeping that term penalizes K−1 documents in most cases and causes retriever collapse during joint training; removing it awards good tables and improves performance by around 1% EM on NQ-TABLES.

RAGE loss is in one work on this site, LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
