John McCloud, a hardware dealer and member of McCloud & Bro., jointly bought and brought the first fire engine introduced in the Northwest to St. Paul with Thompson Ritchie. He was an original member and the only surviving city resident of Pioneer Hook and Ladder Company No. 1's original roll.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FIREMEN AND FIRES OF PIONEER DAYS.

John McCloud and Thompson Ritchie purchased and brought to St. Paul the first fire engine introduced in the Northwest at their own expense. McCloud was a member of McCloud & Bro., was an original member of Pioneer Hook and Ladder Company No. 1, and was identified as the only surviving member of that original roll living in the city at the time of the account.
