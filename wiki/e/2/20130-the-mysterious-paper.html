Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

The mysterious paper was found in Captain Morstan’s pocket-book and is made of native Indian paper that had apparently been pinned to a board. It contains a plan of part of a large building, a red cross, the note “3.37 from left,” and a reference to the sign of the four; Holmes considers it important and tells Miss Morstan to preserve it.
