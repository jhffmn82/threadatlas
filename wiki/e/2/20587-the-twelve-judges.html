Abstract written by a model

The twelve judges, sworn members of the council-court, cast equally divided votes in Orestes’ trial until Athena’s additional vote acquits him.

[_Re-enter Athena, with twelve Athenian citizens_.

The twelve judges cast their votes in two urns to decide Orestes’ case. Their votes are equally divided, so Athena’s additional vote determines the acquittal.
