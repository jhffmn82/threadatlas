NVIDIA in FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots

Rama Akkiraju, Anbang Xu, Deepak Bora, Tan Yu, Lu An, Vishal Seth, Aaditya Shukla, Pritam Gundecha, Hridhay Mehta, Ashwin Jha, Prithvi Raj, Abhinav Balasubramanian, Murali Maram, Guru Muthusamy, Shivakesh Reddy Annepally, Sidney Knowles, Min Du, Nick Burnett, Sean Javiya, Ashok Marannan, Mamta Kumari, Surbhi Jha, Ethan Dereszenski, Anupam Chakraborty, Subhash Ranjan, Amina Terfai, Anoop Surya, Tracey Mercer, Vinodh Kumar Thanigachalam, Tamar Bar, Sanjana Krishnan, Jasmine Jaksic, Nave Algarici, Jacob Liberman, Joey Conway, Sonu Nayyar and Justin Boitano · 2024-07-10

NVIDIA is an organization that builds enterprise chatbots to improve employee productivity. It first created three RAG- and LLM-based bots on its in-house NVBot platform: NVHelp Bot for IT and HR benefits, NVInfo Bot for company financial earnings, and Scout Bot for general enterprise content. Its experience showed that successful conversational assistants require careful engineering, execution, evaluation, security, and cost control. Before deployment, it undertook data governance through an enterprise content security initiative, including document-sensitivity classification and excluding sensitive content from chatbots. As use expanded beyond the initial three bots, NVIDIA developed NVBot to avoid duplicated work, engineered RAG pipelines, and implemented an LLM Gateway for payload logging, subscription and API-cost management, and security audits. Its work also informs a FACTS framework and optimization techniques. NVIDIA is now exploring additional domain-specific chatbots, an enterprise-wide chatbot, and copilots as generative AI changes workplace efficiency and information accessibility.
