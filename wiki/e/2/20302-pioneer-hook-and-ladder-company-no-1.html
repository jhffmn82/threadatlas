Pioneer Hook and Ladder Company No. 1

group · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Pioneer Hook and Ladder company, old hook and ladder company

Abstract written by a model

Pioneer Hook and Ladder Company No. 1 was a volunteer fire company organized under the city charter in November 1854, whose members had to respond promptly to fire alarms. It included foreman Isaac A. Banker, officers, and members Washington M. Stees and Charles H. Williams, later chief and assistant chief engineers. In 1856 its accounts went to St. Paul and it owned fire equipment; it participated in an 1859 parade.

FIREMEN AND FIRES OF PIONEER DAYS.

Pioneer Hook and Ladder Company No. 1 was organized in November 1854 under the city charter, with members required to respond promptly to fire alarms. In 1856 its accounts were turned over to the city, and it possessed a truck, hooks and ladders, and a fire engine with hose; it participated in the 1859 parade.
