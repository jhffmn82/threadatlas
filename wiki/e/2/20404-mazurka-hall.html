Abstract written by a model

Mazurka hall was the principal amusement hall of the city for a time. Built by the Elfelt brothers in 1850, it occupied the second story of their old frame building at Third and Exchange streets. It hosted swell entertainments in the early 1850s, including a ball where J.Q.A. Ward met and soon married Miss Pratt.

The People's theater, an old frame building on the corner of Fourth and St.

Mazurka hall occupied the second story of the Elfelt brothers' old frame building at Third and Exchange streets. It was the principal amusement hall of the city for a time, and many swell entertainments of the early 1850s took place there. J.Q.A. Ward met Miss Pratt at a ball there, left with her, and married her after about twenty minutes.
