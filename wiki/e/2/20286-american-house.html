American house, a city landmark hotel at Third and Exchange streets, was built in 1849 and burned in 1863 before firefighters arrived; guests escaped, but thieves took jewelry and clothing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The American house stood at Third and Exchange streets and was built in 1849. It burned on a bitterly cold December night in 1863 before the fire department arrived, and although guests narrowly escaped, thieves carried away jewelry and clothing.
