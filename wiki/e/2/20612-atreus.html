Atreus in The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies

Aeschylus · -0457

Atreus, ruler and father of Agamemnon, is brother and rival of Thyestes; he feigns a feast, serves Thyestes his children’s flesh, then sends Aegisthus into exile.

Atreus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Atreus, spouse of Aerope, keeps a golden lamb despite vowing it to Artemis. After Zeus reverses the sun, he gains Mycenae and banishes Thyestes, then murders and serves Thyestes’ sons to him. Aegisthus kills Atreus and restores the kingdom to Thyestes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

ιῖιο Ὅτι viol Πέλοπος Πιτθεὺς ᾿Ατρεὺς Θυέστης

Atreus vows to sacrifice the finest of his flock to Artemis but keeps the golden lamb after it appears. After Zeus causes the sun to reverse its course, Atreus receives the kingdom of Mycenae and banishes Thyestes; he later murders Thyestes’ sons and serves them to their father before being killed by Aegisthus.

Tz 15 «Τὸν δ᾽ ᾿Αγαμέμνονα ὃ τροφὸς μετὰ τοῦ Μενελάου

Atreus was killed by Aegisthus, who then restored the kingdom to Thyestes.
