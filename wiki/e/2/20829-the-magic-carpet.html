the magic carpet in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The magic carpet is a green magical carpet used by Ozma to protect and carry travelers. Given by Glinda the Good, it continually unrolls beneath a procession, making a comfortable, safe path over life-destroying desert sands; it rolls up behind them and disappears when they reach the grassy meadows of the Land of Ev. When Ozma throws down a small square of green cloth, it becomes a carpet spanning a deep gulf, protecting travelers from dangers on the ground but not a hammer above. Later it unrolls wide enough for an entire party and carries them safely across the dangerous desert to the Land of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

The magic carpet unrolls across the desert beneath Ozma's procession, providing a safe surface above the life-destroying sands. It rolls itself up behind the procession and disappears after they reach the grassy meadows of the Land of Ev.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Glinda the Good gives Ozma a magic carpet that continually unrolls beneath the travelers' feet, creating a comfortable path across the great desert.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

Ozma throws a small square of green cloth onto the ground, and it becomes a magic carpet that unrolls across a deep gulf. It protects the travelers from dangers beneath their feet but cannot shield them from the hammer above.

20. The Emerald City

Ozma throws down the magic carpet at the edge of the dangerous desert, and it unrolls far enough for the entire party to walk on it without being crowded. It carries them safely across the desert to the Land of Oz.

Magic Carpet in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The Magic Carpet unrolls for Dorothy and the Wizard, protecting them as it swiftly carries them across the Deadly Desert to the Nome King’s border, then transports the entire party from the caverns back to the Land of Oz.
