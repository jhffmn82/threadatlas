First Minnesota battery

group · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: First Minnesota battery, light artillery, First battery

Abstract written by a model

The First Minnesota battery was a light artillery organization organized at Fort Snelling in fall 1861 under first captain Emil Munch. After receiving equipment in St. Louis, it joined Grant's army through Prentiss' division and was transferred there before Pittsburg Landing. The only Minnesota organization engaged in that battle, it helped hold the Hornet's Nest, drove off rebel batteries, suffered losses, and was commended. It later served in the Vicksburg and Atlanta campaigns and the March to the Sea.

The First Minnesota battery, light artillery, was organized at Fort Snelling in the fall of 1861 and sent to Pittsburg Landing after receiving its equipment in St. Louis. At the Battle of Pittsburg Landing, it fought in Prentiss' division, repeatedly drove off rebel batteries, and helped hold the Union lines at the Hornet's Nest. It suffered three killed and six wounded, with two of the wounded dying later, and received warm commendation for its conduct.
