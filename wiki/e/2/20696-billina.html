Billina in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Billina is a female yellow hen, originally named Bill, born at the farm and kept as the little boy’s pet when she was a chick. During a storm, she survives by clinging to the hen-coop and begins speaking human language, correctly though as a beginner; she accepts the name Billina and proves able to talk sensible things. She favors insects, especially bugs and ants, over other food, lays an egg each morning except while moulting or without a snug, quiet nest, and lays daily during the desert journey. After finding a golden key and helping Dorothy search for food and Tiktok’s key, she discovers a hidden path and door and helps Dorothy with Tiktok. In the underground palace, Billina identifies transformations from objects that had never been alive, learns the Nome King’s secret, and cleverly leads the restoration of the Queen of Ev, her children, Ozma, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, and others. Her eggs help defeat the Nome King’s enchantment, and she helps restore the Tin Woodman by touching Evring’s whistle and saying “Ev.” She is publicly honored with a pearl-and-sapphire necklace, chooses the Land of Oz over any other country, and ends the journey at a royal feast on a golden roost.

Billina in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Billina is a new, unusual yellow hen who speaks human language, cannot read, lays eggs wherever she is, and is Dorothy’s feathered friend. Raised in the United States, she accompanies Dorothy during her exploration, warning her about the Wheelers, leading her to the rocky hill, and explaining that they cannot follow among the rocks. At Langwidere’s palace, she is sent to the chicken house, fights a speckled rooster, and emerges victorious but badly injured, with lost feathers, a bleeding comb, and one eye nearly pecked out. Dorothy carries her in the golden chariot as she joins the expedition. In the Nome King’s domain, Billina gathers crumbs, hides and sleeps beneath the rocky throne, then lays an egg there. She bargains for entry to the enchanted palace and uses her claws to guess the Nome King’s enchantments. Her eggs frighten and help defeat the Nome King, saving the people of Ev from his transformations. She cannot identify the Tin Woodman’s transformed form, but urges Dorothy not to return the magic belt and supports threatening the Nome King with transformation.

front matter .. Author's Note

Billina is identified as one of the new and unusual characters in Ozma of Oz. A child who read the story before publication called Billina "REAL OZZY."

3. Letters in the Sand

Billina cannot read but discusses what the warning might mean and suggests the Wheelers could be automobiles. She recognizes the approaching creatures as Wheelers, warns Dorothy to run, leads her to the rocky hill, and helps her understand that the Wheelers cannot follow among the rocks.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Billina praises Tiktok after he drives away the first Wheelers and accompanies Dorothy into the palace. She forces her way past the servant, is found in Langwidere’s work-basket, and is ordered to be put in the chicken-house.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Billina is found in the chicken house fighting a speckled rooster and leaves victorious, though she has lost many feathers, nearly lost an eye, and has a bleeding comb. Dorothy carries her back to the palace, where the Hungry Tiger says she looks appetizing but refuses to eat her because it would be wrong.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Billina agrees to join the expedition and is carried by Dorothy in the golden chariot. She spends part of the palace stay smoothing her feathers and later picks up scraps around the dining room.

13. The Nome King Laughs

Billina gathers the crumbs of cake and then hides in a hollow beneath the Nome King’s rocky throne. She falls asleep there, unnoticed by the others, and is missing when Dorothy later looks for her.

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

Billina lays an egg under the Nome King's throne and bargains for the right to enter the palace and guess the enchantments. She insists on going despite Dorothy's concern and enters the enchanted palace, confident that she may guess correctly.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

Billina saves the captives by using eggs against the Nome King, but she has no clue for identifying the Tin Woodman's transformed form. She warns Dorothy not to return the magic belt and supports threatening the Nome King with transformation.

Billina in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Billina is a yellow, fluffy hen who lives in the Land of Oz because she prefers it to Kansas. Fat and sleek, she wears pearls and emerald bracelets, reunites joyfully with Dorothy, then quarrels with Eureka until Dorothy makes them reconcile.

Billina in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Billina is a yellow hen with fluffy feathers and a pearl necklace, head and parent of ten chicks all named Dorothy. Dorothy is her dearest friend; Billina knows Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger and is sent by Princess Ozma to welcome Dorothy. She reunites with Dorothy, describes her chicks and Tik-Tok, then enters the Throne-Room with the chicks and gathers them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

14. Tik-Tok and Billina

Billina reunites with Dorothy and tells her that she has hatched ten clever chicks, naming all of them Dorothy because they were difficult to tell apart. She explains Tik-Tok's machinery when his thoughts run down and says that Nick Chopper is waiting at his castle for Dorothy.

21. Dorothy Receives the Guests

Billina enters the Throne-Room with her ten fluffy chicks, wearing a pearl necklace while each chick wears a gold chain and locket containing Dorothy's picture. She calls the scattered chicks to her and later says that she is their parent.

Billina in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Billina is a yellow hen who lives in the palace gardens and is Dorothy’s companion and friend. Respected by the Oz people for eggs they like, she eats bugs and insects, rises by daybreak, and is queen and governor of the chicken colony she started. She welcomes Dorothy, Aunt Em, and Uncle Henry to her house, where her protected, well-supplied chickens include nine hens named Dorothys and two roosters named Daniels. During Dorothy’s travels, Billina suggests a School Pill for the zebra, searches for food, warns Dorothy about risking Toto against the Spoon Brigade, and is captured with Dorothy. She is imprisoned in Utensia with Dorothy and Toto but released with them. In Bunbury she finds food, comments on the Graham Gems and Toto, and is accused by a bread pudding. At Bunnybury, she advises Dorothy to return to the signpost, then remains outside with Toto to rest while Dorothy explores.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

7. How Aunt Em Conquered the Lion

Billina welcomes Dorothy, Aunt Em, and Uncle Henry and shows them her house and her chicken family. She is the acknowledged Queen and Governor of every chicken in Oz and has nine living hens named Dorothys and two roosters named Daniels. Her chickens are protected, well supplied, and taught good manners and grammar.

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

Billina suggests that the zebra could take one of the Wizard's School Pills to become learned and wise. The next morning, she searches for food, accompanies Dorothy into the forest, warns her not to risk Toto against the Spoon Brigade, and is captured with Dorothy.

16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

Billina is brought into Utensia as a prisoner alongside Dorothy and Toto. She perches on the unlit range during the proceedings and is released with Dorothy and Toto.

17. How They Came to Bunbury

Billina accompanies Dorothy to Bunbury and finds enough bugs and insects to satisfy her own hunger. She suggests that the Graham Gems might be fresh baked, later watches the commotion from atop a cracker house, and says that the Crumpets dared Toto to eat them. A bread pudding accuses her of pecking out the eyes of a Raisin Bunn.

19. How Bunnybury Welcomed the Strangers

Billina advises Dorothy to return to the signpost and later agrees to remain outside Bunnybury with Toto. She says that she and Toto will rest comfortably while Dorothy explores the town.
