Abstract written by a model

The Gargoyles are a numerous wooden people of the Country of the Gargoyles, or wooden land, in the awful Land of Naught atop Pyramid Mountain. Small and round, with oversized heads, long stout arms, carved faces, and short hinged, detachable wooden wings, they fly swiftly and silently. They communicate only by quick signals of fingers or lips and choose a sleeping period because their country has no night. Though they swarm to attack newcomers, loud shouting and battle cries make them recoil. They draw the Wizard's revolver fire with small groups, capture the defenders when ammunition is exhausted, and carry them to a wooden city. After the prisoners steal wings and escape, the Gargoyles pursue them to a cavern, but fear the unfamiliar fire and are held back.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

Gargoyles live in the awful Land of Naught at the top of Pyramid Mountain. Overman-Anu once fought nine days against many Gargoyles, who feared his battle cry and recoiled from loud noise.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The Gargoyles are small wooden beings with round bodies, oversized heads, long stout arms, hinged wooden wings, and varied carved faces and head designs. They make no sound, communicate with gestures, fly swiftly, and attack the newcomers in swarms, though noise causes them to retreat.

12. A Wonderful Escape

The Gargoyles repeatedly send small groups to draw fire from the Wizard's revolvers, then overwhelm and capture the defenders after the ammunition is gone. They carry the prisoners to a wooden city, remove their own wings when they rest, and pursue the prisoners after their stolen wings enable an escape.

13. The Den of the Dragonettes

The Gargoyles pursue Dorothy, the Wizard, Zeb, and their companions to the cavern. They are frightened and held back by the fire, which is unlike anything they have known, and the travelers believe they will have difficulty following through the rocky passages.
