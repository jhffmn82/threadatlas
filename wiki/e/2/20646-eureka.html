Eureka in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Eureka is Dorothy’s white kitten and pet, a talking, agile creature said to have nine lives. Hungry, outspoken, and often jealous or quarrelsome, she repeatedly seeks milk, meat, fish, and especially piglets, while also showing loyalty and courage. In the Glass City, she escapes an overturned bird-cage, explains that she can walk on air, and joins Dorothy and Zeb. During the journey she complains of hunger, eats fish, plays with nine tiny piglets, and fiercely protects them from thorn branches and the Mangaboos, attacking with teeth and claws. Fleeing the Black Pit, she flies nearly two hundred feet above the earth to reach the House of the Sorcerer and warn Dorothy. She sees well in darkness, walks on a river, catches fish, fights Gargoyles, escapes their prison by climbing down with her claws, discovers how they remove their wings to sleep, and helps guide Zeb to the wings. After Ozma rescues the party, Eureka reaches Oz with Dorothy. There, despite a longstanding desire to eat Ozma’s white piglet, she is accused of murdering it after it disappears. Imprisoned and tried in a cage, she first resists answering, then confesses that she caused the piglet to fall into a vase while intending to eat it. When the piglet is recovered, Eureka is declared innocent, but remains in disgrace, forbidden to roam the palace and confined to Dorothy’s room. She begs to be sent elsewhere, then disappears with Dorothy when they are sent to Kansas.

Narrative, section by section

12 sections; each heading opens the text

2. The Glass City

Eureka escapes from her overturned bird-cage and is revealed to be able to talk. She explains that the air supports her and walks through it without falling. She joins Dorothy and Zeb on their way to the street and complains that she wants milk.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

Eureka rides on Jim and later follows the Wizard, Dorothy, and Zeb through the folk gardens. She complains that she is starving and says she would not eat milk-weeds.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

Eureka complains about the lack of milk or meat and questions whether the Wizard is truly a wizard. She eats the fish he catches, refuses the crackers, and sleeps beside Jim after the group returns to the city.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

Eureka plays with the nine tiny piglets and later tries to protect them from the Mangaboos by snarling, biting, scratching, and attacking the enemies' faces. She helps Jim fight back but is eventually forced toward the mountain.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

Eureka escapes through the remaining opening of the Black Pit while the Mangaboos pursue her. She flies higher and faster than they can, reaches the House of the Sorcerer, and awakens Dorothy to tell her what happened.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

Eureka is frightened when an unseen hand grabs her from the table, but Dorothy explains that the house's inhabitants are invisible. She refuses to eat the dama-fruit after Dorothy warns that losing her to invisibility would be painful, and later walks on the river and catches a fish.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

Eureka can see the nine piglets again and asks to eat one because she is hungry. After Jim threatens to chew her up if she harms them, she agrees to leave the piglets alone.

12. A Wonderful Escape

Eureka attacks the Gargoyles by scratching and biting them, then escapes briefly before being captured. She climbs down the prison house, observes that the Gargoyles remove their wings to sleep, guides Zeb to the wings, and returns to the buggy in Dorothy's lap.

14. Ozma Uses the Magic Belt

Eureka remains with the group in the underground cave and objects to the idea of dying there. Dorothy promises to hold and take the kitten with her when Ozma rescues them. Eureka disappears with Dorothy through the Magic Belt and reaches Oz.

18. The Trial of Eureka the Kitten

Eureka is found under Dorothy’s bed after leaving Ozma’s boudoir and refuses at first to answer questions about the missing piglet. She is arrested, taken to prison, and scheduled to be tried for murder.

19. The Wizard Performs Another Trick

Eureka is placed in a cage and tried for allegedly murdering and eating Princess Ozma's piglet. She challenges the Woggle-Bug's evidence, later reveals that the piglet fell into a vase while she intended to eat it, and is freed after the piglet is recovered.

20. Zeb Returns to the Ranch

Eureka is forbidden to wander around the palace after the trial and is confined to Dorothy's room. She begs Dorothy to send her somewhere else, and she disappears with Dorothy when Ozma sends them to Kansas.
