people of Ev in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The people of Ev are a group restored to natural forms by Billina. They witness the Nome King and his warriors with the people of Oz, then welcome the Queen mother and her children, celebrate Evardo’s proclamation as king, and promise obedience.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The people of Ev are restored from their enchanted forms during Billina’s mission. They appear with the people of Oz in the palace doorway and witness the Nome King and his warriors.

19. The King of Ev

The people of Ev gather to welcome the Queen mother and her children, shouting, cheering, and throwing flowers. They shout their approval fifteen times when Evardo is proclaimed king, and some Wheelers promise to obey him.
