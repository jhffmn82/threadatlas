ORESTES (_approaching suddenly_)

The serpent in Clytemnestra’s dream is born from her womb, swaddled and nursed by her, and draws blood from her breast. Orestes identifies himself with the serpent and reads it as a sign that he will bring about Clytemnestra’s death.
