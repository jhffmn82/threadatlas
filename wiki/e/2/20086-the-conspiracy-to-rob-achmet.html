the conspiracy to rob Achmet in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Abdullah Khan, with Mahomet Singh and Dost Akbar, planned to admit Achmet into the fort, kill him, hide his disappearance, and seize the rajah’s jewels; despite being forced to swear noninterference, the narrator had Achmet taken to the main guard.

The conspiracy involved allowing Achmet into the fort, killing him, concealing his disappearance, and taking the rajah’s jewels for the conspirators. Abdullah Khan forced the narrator to swear that he would not interfere, but the narrator finally directed that Achmet be taken to the main guard.
