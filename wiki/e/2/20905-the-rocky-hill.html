the rocky hill in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The rocky hill, near a grove of trees, is a heap of loose, jagged rocks with a concealed smooth path from base to summit. It shelters Dorothy and Billina from the Wheelers, who surround and trap them but cannot climb or use the hidden path. Its summit rock contains the rocky chamber and Tiktok.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Letters in the Sand

The rocky hill provides refuge for Dorothy and Billina. Its loose, jagged rocks prevent the Wheelers from rolling after them, although the Wheelers surround the hill and trap them there.

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

The rocky hill contains a smooth path winding from its base to its top. Rocks at the bottom conceal the path and prevent the Wheelers from using it, while the top holds the solitary rock containing Tiktok.
