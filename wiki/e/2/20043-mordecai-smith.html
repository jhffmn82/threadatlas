Mordecai Smith in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Mordecai Smith is a boatman, husband of Mrs. Smith, and father of Jim Smith. He owns the steam launch Aurora and hires out boats from Smith’s Wharf and landing-stage. After a wooden-legged man rouses him at night, he leaves with Jim in the Aurora and disappears, prompting Holmes’s advertisement. Holmes later finds him at Jacobson’s Yard; Smith takes two gentlemen aboard, shovels coal during a chase, and finally submits with Jim.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

Smith owns the Aurora and hires out boats at his landing-stage. According to his wife, he left in the steam launch after being roused during the night by a wooden-legged man, and she is worried because he has not returned.

Chapter IX

Mordecai Smith is the boatman who left Smith’s Wharf with his son Jim in the steam launch Aurora. His whereabouts remain unknown, and Holmes’s advertisement seeks information about him, his son, and the launch.

Chapter X

Mordecai Smith is found by Holmes at Jacobson's Yard and announces that he wants the Aurora at eight o'clock for two gentlemen. During the chase, he shovels coal aboard the Aurora, and afterward he and his son sit sullenly in the launch before meekly coming aboard when ordered.
