Brahmans

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Brahman, Brahmans of India, Brahmins, first three castes, the Hindu Scribes and Pharisees, the leading Brahmans, the wisest among them

Abstract written by a model

Brahmans are the priestly caste, said to come from Brahma’s mouth, with hereditary privilege to teach the Vedas. They held political and legal power, lived from contributions, practiced doctrines, austerities, and hermitage, though some later worked productively. They opposed Buddha, whose teachings rejected their austerity as a path to perfection.

Brahmans is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXX

The Brahmans are the priestly caste, said to have come from Brahma's mouth and to possess the privilege of teaching the Vedas. They traditionally held great political and legal power, were protected from punishment beyond banishment, and were expected to live from contributions rather than labor, although some later entered productive occupations.

BUDDHA

Brahmans practiced or taught religious doctrines and austerities, but Buddha found no satisfaction in their teachings and concluded that austerity was not the path to perfection. Leading Brahmans opposed Buddha, while Brahmanical tradition had generally reserved the hermit's life for Brahmans.
