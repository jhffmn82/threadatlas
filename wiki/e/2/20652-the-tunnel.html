Abstract written by a model

A passage through the glass mountain connects the Country of the Mangaboos to the Valley of Voe and new country. It climbs from narrow or street-wide, smooth-floored darkness, then grows lighter and level before opening out.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The tunnel runs upward through the glass mountain and varies from very narrow to as wide as a street. Its floor is usually smooth, and it eventually becomes lighter and more level before opening into the new country.
