Abstract written by a model

The table corpus contains tables and serves as the collection from which systems retrieve a question’s gold table. Its tables are decomposed into single rows and columns for CLTR and T-RAG databases, where hits count gold-table units.

table corpus is in one work on this site, LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
