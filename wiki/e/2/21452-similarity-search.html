In "Syntax-Aware Retrieval Augmented Code Generation," similarity search is required at each time step of inference when extra datastores are incorporated, with only one of the two datastores needing to be retrieved. In "TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text," similarity search compares the encoded user query with stored document embeddings and retrieves the most relevant documents whose KV caches are used to form the online contextual cache.
