Nome King's challenge

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Nome King's offer, kind offer, offer, the risk, this offer

Abstract written by a model

The Nome King’s challenge lets Ozma enter his palace alone and identify transformed members of Ev’s royal family by touching eleven objects and saying “Ev.” Correct choices restore the Queen and children; if none are royal, Ozma is transformed into bric-a-brac or an ornament.

Nome King's challenge is in one work on this site, Ozma of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

11. The Nome King

The Nome King permits Ozma to enter his palace alone and touch eleven different objects while saying the word "Ev." Any objects that are members of the royal family will be restored, but if none is correct, Ozma herself will be transformed into bric-a-brac or an ornament.
