T-RAG in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

T-RAG is a system for end-to-end TableQA. It decomposes tables into individual rows and columns to form a table database, uses DPR to retrieve them, and uses a sequence-to-sequence reader to generate answers. During fine-tuning, RAG loss refines retrieval through implicit signals. On E2E-WTQ, it reported 0.7806 Precision@5; its end-to-end accuracy is called Hit@1.

Related Work

T-RAG uses DPR to retrieve rows and columns by decomposing a table. It generates answers with a sequence-to-sequence reader and applies the RAG loss to refine the retriever through implicit signals during end-to-end TableQA fine-tuning.

CLTR and T-RAG Evaluation

T-RAG decomposed tables into single rows and columns to form its table database. It reported a Precision@5 of 0.7806 on E2E-WTQ by counting a retrieved row or column as a hit when it belonged to the gold table, and its end-to-end TableQA accuracy is compared with CLTR.
