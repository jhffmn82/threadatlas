The Glass City

place · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: city, country, glass city, inner world, the city, the glass city, the town

Abstract written by a model

The Glass City is a city of the inner world, made of clear, transparent glass that grows into houses, streets, towers, roofs, domes, spires, bridges, and a palace. Six colored suns illuminate it. Though the Rain of Stones damages and cracks its structures, they grow together again. It becomes the destination for the new Royal Princess’s palace ceremonies, and later the travelers return to its domed hall and enter the House of the Sorcerer.

The Glass City is in one work on this site, Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

2. The Glass City

The Glass City lies in the inner world and has clear glass houses, buildings, roofs, streets, domes, and spires. It is illuminated by six colored suns and has suffered damage from the Rain of Stones. A glass palace with a central dome and four corner spires stands in an open square.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The glass city has fine streets, towers, and roofs that grow rather than being built. A Rain of Stones can break or crack these structures, which eventually grow together again.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

The people of Mangaboo escort the new Royal Princess toward the glass city for her palace ceremonies. The travelers later return there and enter the domed hall containing the House of the Sorcerer.
