CLTR

Abstract written by a model

CLTR is a research paper on TableQA. It decomposes tables into single rows and columns for a table database, retrieves rows and columns rather than whole tables without a reader, and obtains an answer cell by intersecting the top-scored rows and columns; its end-to-end accuracy is compared with T-RAG.

CLTR is in one work on this site, LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Related Work

CLTR retrieves rows and columns without using a reader. It obtains the answer cell by intersecting the top-scored rows and columns.

CLTR and T-RAG Evaluation

CLTR decomposed tables into single rows and columns to form its table database. Its end-to-end TableQA accuracy is compared with T-RAG, while its retrieval metrics are not directly compared with BM25 and DPR because of differing table-corpus setups.
