the royal family of Ev in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The royal family of Ev is the former king’s wife, the Queen of Ev, and her ten children—five boys and five girls—whose realm is the Land of Ev. It owns the lunch-box trees and dinner-pail trees, but no one rules Ev after the Nome King takes the Queen and children. He transforms them into ornaments, bric-a-brac, and other decorations, scattering them through the palace; they are hidden among royal-purple ornaments. Ozma and her companions prepare to free them, but Ozma initially fails to identify the transformed family members. The Nome King’s transformations are eventually undone, restoring the Queen and her children and reuniting them. They then leave the Nome King’s palace with the rescuing party and travel together toward Ev, despite the Nome King’s claim that they are his slaves.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

The royal family of Ev owns the lunch-box trees and dinner-pail trees. There is no one ruling the Land of Ev because King Evoldo's wife and ten children were transformed by the Nome King.

8. The Hungry Tiger

The royal family of the Land of Ev is described as having endured a long imprisonment. Ozma and the others are preparing to determine how to liberate them.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The royal family of Ev consists of Evoldo's wife and ten children, five boys and five girls. The Nome King has enslaved them, imprisoned them in his underground palace, and transformed them into decorations.

11. The Nome King

The Queen of Ev's ten children were sold with her to the Nome King and are held as his slaves. Like their mother, they were transformed into articles of ornament and bric-a-brac and scattered throughout the Nome King's palace.

12. The Eleven Guesses

The royal family of Ev has been transformed into ornaments in the Nome King's palace. Ozma tries to identify its members but fails.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

The royal family of Ev, including Evring, has been transformed into ornaments by the Nome King. They are all hidden among the ornaments that are royal purple.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The royal family of Ev is restored from the Nome King’s transformations. The Queen and her nine children are reunited, and they prepare to return to their own country.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

The royal family of Ev leaves the Nome King's palace with the rescuing party, although the Nome King had claimed they were his slaves. The Queen of Ev and her children travel together toward Ev.
