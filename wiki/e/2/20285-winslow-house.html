Winslow house in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Winslow house was a four-story brick hotel at Seven Corners, constructed by J.M. Winslow in 1854 and among the city’s largest except Fuller house. In fall 1862, a defective cupola stovepipe caused a fire that entirely destroyed it; proprietors were arrested and discharged.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The Winslow house at Seven Corners was a four-story brick building constructed in 1854 by J.M. Winslow. A defective stovepipe in the cupola caused its destruction in the fall of 1862, and only a few badly damaged pieces of furniture were saved; its proprietors were arrested for incendiarism but discharged after a preliminary examination.
