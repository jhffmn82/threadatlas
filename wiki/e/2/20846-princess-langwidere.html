Princess Langwidere in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Princess Langwidere is a vain princess of Ev, niece of King Evoldo and the Queen of Ev, who lives in the royal palace and rules the Land of Ev. She takes freely from the royal treasury and treasures her collection of thirty interchangeable beautiful heads, wearing one at a time with a simple white costume and the ruby key on her left wrist. Much of her time is spent admiring herself in mirrored rooms. She demands Dorothy Gale’s head for her collection and imprisons Dorothy when she refuses. After initially threatening Ozma and her company, Langwidere eagerly accepts their help to free her aunt and cousins and releases Dorothy. She later keeps Billina out of her drawing-room, recounts Evoldo’s fate, refuses the expedition because of the heat, and returns to change heads.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Princess Langwidere lives in the royal palace of Ev and spends much of her time admiring herself in mirrored rooms. She owns thirty beautiful heads, demands Dorothy’s head for her collection, and orders Dorothy imprisoned when she refuses.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Princess Langwidere imprisons Dorothy because Dorothy will not give her a head for her collection. She initially threatens Ozma and her company, but becomes eager to receive help freeing her aunt and cousins and readily releases Dorothy.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Princess Langwidere tells Dorothy that Billina is in the chicken house because her drawing-room is no place for hens. She sits opposite Tiktok in the drawing-room beside Ozma during the gathering.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Princess Langwidere says that Evoldo was wicked and explains that he sold his family to the Nome King for a long life before drowning himself. She refuses to join the expedition because she cannot bear heat, returns to her cabinet to change heads, and remains at home.
