Ozma's army in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Ozma's army is a twenty-seven-member military force of Oz's finest soldiers, serving as her expeditionary army. It has eight Generals, six Colonels, seven Majors, five Captains, and one private soldier. Ozma assembles, houses, and feeds it for a rescue mission; it marches behind the principal expedition members. It later leaves the Nome King's palace ahead of the royal party, commanded by the Scarecrow while the Tin Woodman is absent.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The Hungry Tiger

The Army of Oz is present in the drawing-room wearing handsome uniforms. It consists of twenty-seven members: eight Generals, six Colonels, seven Majors, five Captains, and one private.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Ozma assembles an Army of twenty-seven from the finest soldiers of Oz for the rescue mission. The army is housed and fed at the palace, then marches in formation behind the principal members of the expedition.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

The army leaves the Nome King's palace ahead of the royal family and the other travelers. The Scarecrow commands it during the journey because the Tin Woodman is absent.
