the Mangaboo life cycle in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The Mangaboo life cycle runs from buds and blossoms on bushes through babies and partly grown people to ripe adults; being picked begins life, which can last about five years, and sprouting requires planting.

1 section; each heading opens the text

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The Mangaboo life cycle begins with buds and blossoms on bushes, followed by babies, partly grown people, and ripe adults. A Mangaboo becomes alive when picked, can live for about five years under good conditions, and must be planted if it begins to sprout.
