open-domain TableQA datasets in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

Open-domain TableQA datasets are used to evaluate models, with combined strategies achieving state-of-the-art performance. They pair each question with one gold table, creating a 1/K table-level Precision@K upper bound. They mainly contain look-up questions, lack multi-cell and complex operations, and need larger, more complex evaluation.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The combined strategies achieve new state-of-the-art performance on them.

Limitations

The open-domain TableQA datasets are limited mainly to look-up questions and do not cover sufficiently complex questions involving multiple cells and operations such as SUM, MAX, MIN, and SUBTRACT. Their larger-scale and more complex evaluation is identified as future work.

CLTR and T-RAG Evaluation

Open-domain TableQA datasets associate each question with only one gold table. This creates a 1/K upper bound for table-level Precision@K.
