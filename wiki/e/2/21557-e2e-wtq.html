E2E-WTQ in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

E2E-WTQ is a relatively small open-domain TableQA benchmark and subset of WikiTableQuestions. It focuses on lookup questions requiring cell selection, excludes aggregation across multiple table cells, and generally contains long tables with diverse information. It has WikiTableQuestions-matching splits to avoid test-sample leakage: 851 training examples, 124 development examples, 241 test examples, and 2,108 tables. It has been used to evaluate CLTR, T-RAG, and LI-RAGE. LI-RAGE is trained for 15 epochs and achieves 62.10 accuracy and 81.85 Recall@5, an 11.45-point improvement over the previously best reported result; LI also exceeds DPR by over 20–30% in Recall@5/1. T-RAG reports 0.7806 Precision@5 under a row- and column-level hit definition.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

E2E-WTQ is one of the two benchmarks on which LI-RAGE achieves state-of-the-art results.

Experimental Setup

E2E-WTQ contains lookup questions requiring cell selection and excludes questions involving aggregations across multiple table cells. It is evaluated with accuracy and Recall@K; LI-RAGE obtains 62.10 accuracy and 81.85@5 retrieval recall.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

E2E-WTQ shows an 11.45-point improvement from LI-RAGE over the previously best reported result. LI outperforms DPR by over 20–30% in Recall@5/1 on this dataset, which contains generally long tables with diverse information.

Limitations

E2E-WTQ is one of the two open-domain TableQA datasets used to test the proposed system. It is relatively small compared with the other dataset.

Dataset Statistics

E2E-WTQ contains 851 training examples, 124 development examples, 241 test examples, and 2,108 tables. LI-RAGE is trained on it for 15 epochs with a reader learning rate of 0.000015.

CLTR and T-RAG Evaluation

E2E-WTQ was used to evaluate CLTR and T-RAG. T-RAG reported a Precision@5 of 0.7806 under a row- and column-level hit definition.
