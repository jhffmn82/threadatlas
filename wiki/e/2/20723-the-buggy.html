the buggy in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The buggy is a nearly new vehicle with a shiny top and side curtains, used by travelers for riding on good roads instead of walking. At Hugson's Siding, Zebediah drives Dorothy Gale in it when an earthquake crack pulls it down. Its top catches air like a parachute or umbrella, slowing the fall before it lands on a glass roof. It carries Dorothy, Eureka, baggage, harness, and Dorothy's wicker suit-case, and Jim pulls it through the Glass City. The Mangaboos push it into the Black Pit, but Zeb later drives it through a mountain tunnel. After a broad-leaved plant is rubbed on its tires, it travels across water; Jim then pulls it toward Pyramid Mountain and up mountain stairs. It is dragged up a narrow stairway, receives a bound crowned Gargoyle, and is taken to a prison house by Gargoyles. Finally, four stolen Gargoyle wings are fastened to it, and it flies toward the rock carrying Dorothy, Zeb, the Wizard, and Eureka.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Earthquake

The buggy is standing near trees at Hugson's Siding and has a shiny top and side curtains. Zebediah drives Dorothy in it until an earth crack opens beneath it and pulls it downward.

2. The Glass City

The buggy slows the fall by catching the air like a parachute or umbrella. It lands on a broad glass roof, and Zeb accidentally kicks over Dorothy's bird-cage while getting out. Dorothy later climbs back into it with Eureka, and Jim pulls it through the Glass City.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The Mangaboos push the buggy into the Black Pit after the prisoners. Zeb later drives it while Jim pulls it through the mountain tunnel.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

The buggy is drawn onto the broad river and floats or rolls across the water after the plant is rubbed on its tires. It later carries Dorothy, the Wizard, and Zeb overland toward Pyramid Mountain while Jim pulls it at a gallop.

10. The Braided Man of Pyramid Mountain

The buggy is drawn up the mountain stairs by Jim. The travelers leave it while they walk alongside or ahead of it.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The buggy is dragged up the narrow stairway with help from Zeb and the Wizard. After Zeb captures a crowned Gargoyle, he tosses the bound creature into the buggy.

12. A Wonderful Escape

The Gargoyles carry the buggy to the prison house because they do not know its purpose or whether it is alive. Four stolen wings are fastened to it, and it carries Dorothy, Zeb, the Wizard, and Eureka during the flight toward the rock.
