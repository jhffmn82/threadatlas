6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

The Garden of the Clinging Vines contains tough green vines that writhe together like great snakes. The vines crush everything they touch, and the Wizard, Zeb, and Dorothy are threatened with being thrown into the garden before the fire test saves them.
