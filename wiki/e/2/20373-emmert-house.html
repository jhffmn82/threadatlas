Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The Emmert house stood on Bench street near Wabasha and had been built in territorial times by Fred Emmert. It was no longer used as a hotel when it burned during the winter of 1868, but a family and boarding-house residents occupied it; two people sick with smallpox were rescued by firemen and later taken to safety by W.L. Wilson.
