The May 1870 Concert Hall fire began beneath the sidewalk, where combustible materials were stored, and rapidly engulfed the building. Charles and August Mueller leaped from an upper-floor window and survived after months of suffering, while McClellan, who operated a dressmaking establishment there, was burned to death.
