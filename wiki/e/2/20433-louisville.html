Louisville

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Louisville, Kentucky, Washington City, the suburbs of Louisville

Abstract written by a model

In the untitled work of 1862, Louisville was the Second Minnesota’s destination, and later the place where Mayor J. M. Dolph presented the regiment with a silk flag. (Novel-10146) In Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life, Louisville was described as a principal point on the early route between Pittsburgh and New Orleans, a place where boats required resident pilots at the falls, and the setting of Daymon’s murder trial.
