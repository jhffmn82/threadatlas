Carpenter house was a hotel at Summit avenue and Ramsey street, built by Warren Carpenter on a picturesque site overlooking the city to attract tourists. The panic of 1857 and Civil war thwarted plans; it later burned to the ground January 26, 1879.

HOTEL FIRES.

The Carpenter house stood at Summit avenue and Ramsey street and was built by Warren Carpenter, who envisioned attracting millions of tourists. The panic of 1857 and the Civil war thwarted his plans, and the hotel burned on January 26, 1879 after having passed beyond his control.
