Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

The Ingersoll block on Bridge Square was constructed in 1860, and its third-floor hall was well attended for several years. It hosted speakers including Edward Everett, Ralph Waldo Emerson, John B. Gough, and Mrs. Scott Siddons, and was used by Company C of the First regiment of Minnesota volunteers for military drills and enlistment meetings. A 1861 social and supper held there raised funds for a library that began with about 350 books.
