Gwig in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Gwig is a thorn-covered vegetable sorcerer of the Mangaboos, grown from the Sorcerer’s bush. He is said to be able to discover whether Dorothy, Zeb, and their companions caused the Rain of Stones, and insists his sorcery is always correct. He contests Oz’s arrival, loses a magical contest, and tries to stop Oz’s breath. Oz cuts him in two; declared dead, Gwig is planted so his bush may sprout and produce more sorcerers.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

2. The Glass City

The Sorcerer is said to be able to discover the truth about whether Dorothy, Zeb, and their companions caused the Rain of Stones.

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

Gwig is the thorn-covered Sorcerer of the Mangaboos. He insists that his sorcery is always correct and argues that Oz's arrival does not disprove his prediction because only one person descended from the sky. After losing the magical contest, he attempts to stop Oz's breath, but Oz cuts him in two; Gwig is declared dead and is to be planted so other Sorcerers can grow from his bush.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The two halves of the Sorcerer are carried to the planting-ground, placed in a hole, covered with earth, and watered. The Prince expects him to sprout into a large bush that will produce several sorcerers.
