Abstract written by a model

Don Carlos Buell commanded the Army of the Cumberland and was criticized for taking nineteen days to move approximately eighty-five miles to the Tennessee River. His troops, including divisions under Nelson and Thomas, reinforced Grant during the battle; Grant did not believe Buell was guilty of the charges later made against him.
