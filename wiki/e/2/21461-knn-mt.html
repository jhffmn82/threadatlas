kNN-MT

method · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: kNN-MT model, kNN-MT paradigm

Abstract written by a model

kNN-MT is a non-parametric machine-translation approach and translation mechanism that boosts standard generation models with a token-level translation retriever. It builds a datastore from decoder representations as keys and corresponding target characters as values. During inference, it uses contextual representations to retrieve the top k datastore neighbors and combines their target-token probabilities by distance and temperature. Later work improves retrieval weighting and robustness, while direct code-generation use can retrieve noisy tokens, yield syntactically invalid code, and require datastore-wide similarity search at each step.

kNN-MT is in one work on this site, Syntax-Aware Retrieval Augmented Code Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

kNN-MT equips generation models with a token-level retriever and can improve their performance. When applied directly to code generation, it may retrieve noisy tokens, produce syntactically invalid code, and require a similarity search over the entire datastore at every inference step.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

kNN-MT is a translation mechanism that enhances model generation by incorporating an additional translation retriever. During inference, it retrieves datastore entries using the contextual representation and combines the retrieved target-token probabilities according to distance and temperature.

5

Khandelwal et al. (2021) propose kNN-MT, which constructs a datastore using decoder representations as keys and corresponding target characters as values. Generation retrieves the top k neighbors, and later work improves the paradigm's retrieval weighting and robustness.
