THE GREAT BATTLE OF PITTSBURG LANDING.

Pittsburg Landing was the principal Union position and the site of the April 6–7 battle. Troops and reinforcements arrived there by steamer, and the Union army used the landing and nearby river positions during the fighting.

Pittsburg Landing was the location where the First Minnesota battery arrived in late February 1862 and where it fought on April 6 and 7. The site was also where William Henry Acker was killed, and his remains were later recovered and returned to St. Paul.
