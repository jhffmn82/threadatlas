Abstract written by a model

Pittsburg Landing, on the Tennessee River, was the principal Union position and battlefield vicinity in the April 6–7 battle. Union troops and reinforcements arrived by steamer and used the landing and nearby river positions during the fighting. The First Minnesota battery arrived in late February 1862, fought there, and William Henry Acker was killed there.
