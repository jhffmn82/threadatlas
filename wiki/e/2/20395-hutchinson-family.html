Hutchinson family

Also called: Hutchinsons, family, tribe

Abstract written by a model

The Hutchinson family was a patriotic concert-giving family that generally sang its own music. It frequently visited St. Paul, and Hutchinson in McLeod county was named after it. During the Civil War, Gen. McClellan removed it from the Army of the Potomac for abolitionist songs. In 1902, John W. Hutchinson was identified as its last surviving member.

THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

The Hutchinson family frequently visited St. Paul and performed its own music, and the town of Hutchinson, McLeod county, was named after the family. During the Civil War, the family was ordered out of the Army of the Potomac by Gen. McClellan because of abolitionist sentiments expressed in its songs. John W. Hutchinson was identified in 1902 as the last surviving member of the famous concert-giving family.
