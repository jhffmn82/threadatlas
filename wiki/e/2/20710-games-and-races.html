games and races in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The games and races are public afternoon athletic events in a great field outside the city gates, featuring running, jumping, wrestling, Zeb’s contests, and a Sawhorse–Cab-horse race before Princess Ozma and guests.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

17. The Nine Tiny Piglets

The games and races take place in a field outside the city gates, where people run, jump, and wrestle before Princess Ozma and her guests. They include Zeb’s contests with a Munchkin and the race between the Sawhorse and the Cab-horse.
