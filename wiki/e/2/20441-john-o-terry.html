TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

John O. Terry was the first hand pressman in St. Paul and partnered with Col. Owens in publishing the Minnesotian. He later served for a long time as assistant postmaster of St. Paul and held other positions of trust.
