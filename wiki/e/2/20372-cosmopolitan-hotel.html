Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The Cosmopolitan hotel occupied a three-story brick building at Third and Franklin streets that Mackubin & Edgerton had erected in 1856. Bechtner & Kottman remodeled it as a European-plan hotel, but it later burned while full of lodgers, many of whom lost all they possessed; its remaining walls later collapsed onto Dr. Schell’s nearby building.
