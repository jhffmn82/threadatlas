21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

The magic belt has magical powers only while it is in a fairy country such as the Land of Oz or the Land of Ev. It can fulfill a wish to transport Dorothy to Australia, but it would be lost when she arrived there and could not bring her back. By remaining with Ozma in Oz, it can later be used to return Dorothy to the Land of Oz.
