Abstract written by a model

In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, libations are funereal offerings poured at Agamemnon’s grave: Clytemnestra sends them and Electra pours them while questioning how they should be dedicated. In The Seven Plays in English Verse, libations are offerings Orestes and Pylades plan to pour at Agamemnon’s tomb on Apollo’s command, while Electra rejects Clytemnestra’s libations as insincere and tells Chrysothemis not to let them reach the tomb.
