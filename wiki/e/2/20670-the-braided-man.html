the braided man in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The braided man is a very old, bent inventor with white hair and beard braided and tied with colored ribbons. On Earth he made holes for cheese, plasters, doughnuts, and buttons, then invented an Adjustable Post-hole and fell through it into a cavern. There he lives and works halfway up Pyramid Mountain, making Assorted Flutters and Rustles. Dorothy gives him a treasured blue ribbon, and he gives the travelers boxes of his products.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. The Braided Man of Pyramid Mountain

The braided man is a very old inventor with long white hair and a beard arranged in many braids tied with colored ribbons. He lives and works in a cavern halfway up Pyramid Mountain, where he manufactures Assorted Flutters and Rustles. He receives a blue hair-ribbon from Dorothy and gives the travelers boxes of his products.
