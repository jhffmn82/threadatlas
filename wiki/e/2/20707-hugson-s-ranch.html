Hugson's Ranch in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Hugson's Ranch is Uncle Hugson's ranch, where Uncle Henry visits and Zebediah works; Zebediah brings Dorothy from Hugson's Siding, and later Ozma sends Zeb and Jim home there.

1. The Earthquake

Hugson's Ranch is where Uncle Henry is visiting Uncle Hugson and where Zebediah works for Uncle Hugson. Zebediah takes Dorothy there from Hugson's Siding.

20. Zeb Returns to the Ranch

Hugson's Ranch is Zeb and Jim's home. Ozma sends them there, and Zeb finds himself on the road just ahead of the ranch gates.
