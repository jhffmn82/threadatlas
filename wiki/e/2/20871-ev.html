Ev in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Ev is a fairy country and land, separated from the Land of Oz by a broad desert. It includes Evna, where manufacturing works made Tiktok and where the royal palace stands; it also contains the Wheelers, a beautiful vale of farms, roads, fruit trees, and fields, the palace, and a tower prison. A great mountain at its northern end blocks a valley, with the Nome King's palace said to lie beneath it. Its central trouble is the loss of its royal family. After King Evoldo disappeared, the family was transformed; later accounts say the Queen of Ev and her nine children were sold to the Nome King, transformed into ornaments, and hidden in his palace. With the family imprisoned, Ev is said to have no ruler or current government, though Princess Langwidere occupies part of the palace, uses the royal treasury, and rules while finding the burden unwelcome. Ozma's party stays at the palace and travels through Ev toward the Nome King's domain to recover the family. Evring identifies himself as a prince of Ev, and Evardo, the eldest prince, is designated to inherit the throne. After the party escapes the underground cavern and returns across the gulf through Ev's countryside, its people celebrate the Queen mother and royal children. They accept Evardo as their new ruler, King Evardo Fifteenth.

Ev in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Ev is a kingdom north-west of Oz, beyond or across the impassable Deadly Desert, near Ruggedo’s old home. It is where Ruggedo is found and where a terrible three-legged giant lives on a tall purple mountain. The giant holds Princess Ozma prisoner and has carried off Ozma’s palace and its people. After the Question Box identifies Ev as Ruggedo’s location, Peg Amy, Wag, Kabumpo, and Prince Pompadore travel there. Glinda also flies to Ev to oppose the giant, and the Runaway Country carries the travelers across the desert into Ev.

Ev in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Ev is Ruggedo’s country beyond the Nonestic Ocean and across the Deadly Desert from Oz. Its barren shores are covered with sand and cactus, with hills near Ruggedo’s caverns. Beneath its surface lie Ruggedo’s vast dominions of underground caverns, caves, and labyrinths. The Blunderoo approaches its shore; Peter and Ruggedo land there and travel toward the Deadly Desert.
