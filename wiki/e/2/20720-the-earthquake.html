the earthquake in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The earthquake is an awful event that repeatedly and violently shakes California during a train journey, frightening Dorothy, Zebediah, and Jim. It opens a great crack beneath the horse and buggy, causing Dorothy, Zeb, Jim, the buggy, and other travelers to fall into a pit; it also causes a Rain of Stones and Oz’s fall into the crack before the earth closes over him. Uncle Henry and Aunt Em believe Dorothy was killed and mourn her, until Dorothy later returns.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Earthquake

The earthquake repeatedly shakes the ground during the train journey and later causes the buggy to sway and the earth to split open. It frightens Dorothy, Zebediah, and Jim and ultimately sends them into a pit.

2. The Glass City

The earthquake opens a large crack in the earth through which Dorothy, Zeb, Jim, and the buggy fall. Zeb and Dorothy explain that the earthquake, rather than them, caused the stones to come loose.

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

The earthquake causes a large crack in the earth. Oz falls into the crack after descending in his balloon, and the earth closes over him, carrying him down to the Land of the Mangaboos.

20. Zeb Returns to the Ranch

The earthquake caused Uncle Henry and Aunt Em to believe that Dorothy had been killed. Their mourning is shown to Dorothy through the enchanted picture.
