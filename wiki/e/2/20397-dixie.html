Dixie in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Dixie, an air written by Dan Emmet with Bryant's Minstrels in New York City, was first sung west at Munger Bros, spread through minstrel programs, became the Confederate States' national air, and Lincoln called it national property by capture.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

"Dixie" was written by Dan Emmet while he was connected with Bryant's Minstrels in New York City. It quickly became popular, appeared on minstrel programs throughout the country, and became the national air of the Confederate States. The text says that Abraham Lincoln regarded it as national property by capture.
