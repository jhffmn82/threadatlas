The Great Battle of Pittsburg Landing in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

The Great Battle of Pittsburg Landing, also called Shiloh, was a two-day Civil War battle at Pittsburg Landing on April 6–7, 1862. Confederate forces attacked the Union Army of the Tennessee; the Army of the Cumberland under Buell reinforced it. The First Minnesota battery fought advancing Confederate forces for hours, notably at the Hornet's Nest, helping keep Union lines from being forced toward the Tennessee River. After Buell arrived, it was held in reserve on April 7. Union forces ultimately drove the Confederates from the field amid heavy casualties on both sides.

THE GREAT BATTLE OF PITTSBURG LANDING.

The battle was fought on April 6–7, 1862, and was described as one of the greatest and most sanguinary battles of the Civil War. Union forces ultimately drove the Confederate forces from the field, though both sides suffered heavy casualties.

The Battle of Pittsburg Landing began for the First Minnesota battery on the morning of April 6, when it was sent against the advancing rebel forces. The battery fought for hours, especially at the Hornet's Nest, and helped prevent the Union lines from being forced back toward the Tennessee River. On April 7, after General Buell arrived, the battery was held in reserve and did not participate in the battle.
