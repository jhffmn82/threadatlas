THE DEATH OF PRESIDENT LINCOLN.

Lincoln’s death occurred when an assassin’s bullet ended his life shortly after the fall of Richmond and Lee’s surrender. His death plunged the country into mourning and was commemorated in churches, including the House of Hope church.
