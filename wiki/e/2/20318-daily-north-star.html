The Daily North Star was a short-lived Democratic paper established in St. Paul during the 1860 presidential campaign to support John C. Breckinridge. H. H. Young, editor of the Henderson Independent, was brought to the state capital to start it. It published regularly through the election but collapsed when officeholders were no longer expected to support it.
