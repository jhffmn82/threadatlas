the Nine Tiny Piglets in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The Nine Tiny Piglets are a group of nine small, white, talking piglets from the Island of Teenty-Weent, where everything is small. They are the Wizard of Oz’s pets, belong to him, and are usually carried safely in his inside pocket; they are also presented as his only proof of being a wizard. Oz produces them through a magic performance, repeatedly seeming to pull one piglet apart to make two, though he explains that their separation and joining are a trick. They talk with the Wizard, play with Eureka, eat Dorothy’s crackers, and fear Eureka because she wants to eat them. During the journey, they exercise, wander into the dome, and are driven by the Mangaboos toward the mountain. With Jim, they are forced into the Black Pit and trapped behind a glass-rock barrier, until the Wizard retrieves all nine. In the Valley of Voices they eagerly eat strange fruit and become invisible, though still audible and touchable; beyond the Valley of Voe they become plainly visible again. In a cave they ask about escape and ask Dorothy to take them with her, but the Wizard pockets them before traveling to Oz with Zeb. At the end, Ozma keeps one piglet with an emerald collar, leaving the Wizard to perform his trick with eight.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

The Wizard reveals that the tiny piglets have been in his inside pocket and explains that their apparent separation and joining were a trick. They can talk, fear Eureka, eat Dorothy's crackers, and are returned to the Wizard's pocket to sleep.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

The Wizard produces the nine tiny piglets as one of his magic tricks and later lets them exercise in his room. They wander into the dome, play with Eureka beside Jim, and are driven toward the mountain by the Mangaboos.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The piglets are driven into the Black Pit with Jim and are trapped behind the glass-rock barrier. The Wizard later picks up all nine piglets and places them carefully in his inside pocket.

8. The Valley of Voices

The nine piglets eagerly eat a strange fruit and immediately become invisible, although they can still be heard and touched. The Wizard gathers them and puts them safely in his pocket.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The nine piglets become plainly visible after the travelers pass beyond the magical Valley of Voe. They object when Eureka suggests eating one of them, and the Wizard later puts them safely back into his pocket.

14. Ozma Uses the Magic Belt

The nine tiny piglets remain in the Wizard's pocket until he lets them run around in the cave. They question the Wizard about escaping and ask Dorothy to take them with her. The Wizard puts them back in his pocket before they are transported with him and Zeb to Oz.

17. The Nine Tiny Piglets

The nine tiny piglets appear as the Wizard repeatedly pretends to pull one piglet apart to make two. They run around lively after emerging, and Ozma keeps one of them with an emerald collar; afterward, the Wizard performs the trick with only eight piglets.
