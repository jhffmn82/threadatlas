Narrative, section by section

HOTEL FIRES.

Concert Hall was a three-story building in front and six or seven stories on the river side, located on Third street near Market. Built by J.W. McClung in 1857, it contained one of the city’s largest halls and was destroyed in May 1870.

THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

Concert hall was built in 1857 by J.W. McClung and held approximately 400 or 500 people. Although its basement location made it somewhat inaccessible and limited demand, it hosted speeches by Horatio Seymour and Tom Marshall, as well as musical gatherings including an election-night performance in 1860.
