THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

The Athenaeum was constructed in 1859 by the German Reading society and for several years was St. Paul's only amusement hall with a stage and drop curtain. It hosted performances by Peter and Caroline Richings, wartime benefits for soldiers, German and English dramatic performances, and Plunkett's Dramatic company with Susan Denin. During the Sioux massacre, it also sheltered maimed refugees brought to the city.
