Abstract written by a model

The royal palace of Ev, located in Evna in the Land of Ev near the desert, stands in a beautiful vale amid gardens, fountains, walks, and marble statuary. It contains Princess Langwidere's cabinet and is her residence. Dorothy, Tiktok, and Billina enter; Ozma's company demands Dorothy's release; later the returning royal party is welcomed there and Evardo is proclaimed king from its balcony.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

The royal palace of Ev stands in the center of a beautiful vale and is surrounded by gardens, fountains, walks, and marble statuary. Dorothy, Tiktok, and Billina enter its grounds and are brought to a drawing-room before Dorothy is taken to the North Tower.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

The palace is Princess Langwidere's residence, where Ozma and her company enter without invitation to demand Dorothy's release. Ozma, Langwidere, and Dorothy later meet there after Dorothy is freed.

19. The King of Ev

The palace of Ev comes into view as the royal party returns, and a large crowd gathers there to welcome the Queen mother and her ten children. Princess Langwidere is found in her mirrored chamber, and Evardo is proclaimed king from a balcony overlooking the subjects.
