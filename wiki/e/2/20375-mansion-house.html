The Mansion house was a three-story frame hotel built by Nicholas Pottgieser for $12,000 at Wabasha and Fifth streets, a popular resort where, in 1866, guest Hawkes accidentally killed his wife and was acquitted.

HOTEL FIRES.

The Mansion house stood at Wabasha and Fifth streets on the site of the old post office. Nicholas Pottgieser built the three-story frame hotel in early days at a cost of $12,000, and it was a popular resort; in 1866 guest Hawkes accidentally shot and killed his wife there and was later acquitted of murder.
