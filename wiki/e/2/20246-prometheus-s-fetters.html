Prometheus’s fetters in Four Plays of Aeschylus

Aeschylus · -0471

Prometheus’s fetters are adamant chains made by Hephaestus that fasten Prometheus to a rocky cleft by Zeus’s command, expressing Zeus’s power until Prometheus is released and they yield.

PROMETHEUS BOUND

The chains are forged from adamant and fasten Prometheus to the mountain. They represent Zeus’s power over him, although Prometheus claims that they will eventually yield when Zeus’s purpose is changed.

Enter IO, horned like a cow.

Prometheus’s fetters hold him fast to a rocky cleft by Zeus’s command and Hephaestus’s hand. Prometheus remains unable to escape until he is released.
