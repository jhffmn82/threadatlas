the wooden city in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

A city made entirely of wood, with weather-worn square, six-sided, and eight-sided tower-like houses, where wooden inhabitants move and rest under constant light instead of night.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

12. A Wonderful Escape

The wooden city contains strong, weather-worn, tower-like houses with square, six-sided, or eight-sided shapes. Its wooden inhabitants move about their dwellings and rest at a selected time because the city has constant light rather than night.
