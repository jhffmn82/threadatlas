Sherlock Holmes's launch in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

A very fast police-boat, its green identifying lamp removed, carries Holmes, Watson, Jones, police workers, and the Sholtos' treasure. It pursues the Aurora down the Thames, catches it near marshes, frees it from a bank, and tows it upstream.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The launch carrying Holmes, Watson, Jones, and several police workers is a fast craft with its green police lamp removed. It pursues the Aurora down the Thames, catches it near the marshes, hauls it off the bank, and tows it upstream.
