Pioneer Press · 1862~

George W. Armstrong, known as the Beau Brummel of early territorial printers for wearing kid gloves while making up forms at the Pioneer, was elected state treasurer on the Democratic ticket in 1857 and later entered real estate.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

George W. Armstrong was known as the Beau Brummel of the early printers and wore kid gloves while making up forms at the old Pioneer. He was elected state treasurer on the Democratic ticket in 1857 and later entered the real estate business.
