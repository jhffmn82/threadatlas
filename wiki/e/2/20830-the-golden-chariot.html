the golden chariot in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907 · the work’s page · read it where the golden chariot first appears

Abstract written by a model

The golden chariot is a vehicle with room for eight riders. Drawn by the Cowardly Lion and the Tiger, it carries Ozma across the desert as she stands holding ribbons and an ivory wand. Later, harnessed to the Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger, it leads a departing procession carrying Ozma, Dorothy, and Billina.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

The golden chariot carries Ozma across the desert and is drawn by a Lion and a Tiger. Ozma stands inside it holding the ribbons and an ivory wand marked with diamond letters O and Z.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The golden chariot carries Ozma, Dorothy, and Billina at the front of the departing procession. The Cowardly Lion and the Hungry Tiger are harnessed to it.
