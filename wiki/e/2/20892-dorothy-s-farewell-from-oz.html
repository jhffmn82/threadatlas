21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

Dorothy's friends in the Land of Oz gather to bid her sorrowful good-bye and wish her long life and happiness. She kisses Ozma, says she is sorry to leave her many friends, and hands Ozma the Nome King's magic belt before departing.
