Abstract written by a model

Confederate forces were a military force commanded overall by Albert Sidney Johnston and also by P. G. T. Beauregard. They held Beech Grove, advanced near Logan's Cross Roads, and were driven off with about 600 casualties. They later attacked Grant's army, failed to destroy it or stop a counterattack, and retreated toward Corinth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE DECISIVE BATTLE OF MILL SPRINGS.

Confederate forces held an entrenched camp at Beech Grove and advanced against the Union pickets near Logan's Cross Roads. They were driven from the field and suffered about 600 killed, wounded, and prisoners.

THE GREAT BATTLE OF PITTSBURG LANDING.

The Confederate forces under Albert Sidney Johnston and P. G. T. Beauregard attacked Grant's army on April 6, expecting to destroy it before reinforcements arrived. They fought fiercely but failed to reach the Tennessee River or prevent the Union counterattack, and retreated toward Corinth on April 7.
