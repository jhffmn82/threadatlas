The chicken-coop is a wooden, slatted-sided chicken keeping place with a thick-board bottom, lashed to a ship. Wind tears its ropes, carrying it into the sea with Dorothy clinging on; its bottom floats like a raft, letting her sleep inside.

1. The Girl in the Chicken Coop

The chicken-coop is lashed to the ship's deck until the wind tears away its ropes and carries it into the sea with Dorothy clinging to it. Its thick wooden bottom supports Dorothy like a raft, and it later floats gently enough for her to sleep inside it.
