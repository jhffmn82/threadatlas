prophecy

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: prescience, prophet-robe, prophetic fame, the prophetic gift, the prophet’s path, μαντικὴν
  • In The House of Atreus, the prophetic gift is given to Cassandra by Apollo, enabling her to perceive the house’s past crimes, Agamemnon’s murder, her own death, and the vengeance to come; she says it brought her mockery and disbelief and destroys the prophetic wands and wreaths before entering the palace. (The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies)
  • In The Library, Cassandra learns prophecy from Apollo, but he deprives it of the power to persuade after she refuses his favours.
