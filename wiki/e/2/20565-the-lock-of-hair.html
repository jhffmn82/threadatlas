the lock of hair

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Orestes' lock of hair, a curl Of hair new shorn, a curl new shorn, lock, locks of hair, one, ringlet, the hair, the token of Orestes, these twin locks, this dumb hair, this lock, this ornament
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Orestes’ lock of hair is found by Chrysothemis beside the tomb and taken as proof of Orestes’ return, while Electra says it belongs among offerings commemorating his death.
  • In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, the lock of hair is a relic of Orestes: he brings two locks after returning from banishment, dedicates one to Inachus, and lays one on Agamemnon’s grave; Electra finds a newly shorn lock there and suspects it is Orestes’.
