Abstract written by a model

In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, the lock of hair is a relic of Orestes: he brings two locks after returning from banishment, dedicates one to Inachus, and lays one on Agamemnon’s grave; Electra finds a newly shorn lock there and suspects it is Orestes’. In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Orestes’ lock of hair is found by Chrysothemis beside the tomb and taken as proof of Orestes’ return, while Electra says it belongs among offerings commemorating his death.
