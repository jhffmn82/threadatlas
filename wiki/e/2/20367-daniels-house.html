HOTEL FIRES.

The Daniels house stood on Eagle street near Seven Corners and was destroyed in 1852 shortly after it had been finished and furnished. Strong wind prevented the small group of firemen from saving the structure, though Rev. D.D. Neill, Isaac Markley, Bartlett Presley, and W.M. Stees helped save furniture.
