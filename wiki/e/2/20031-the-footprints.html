the footprints in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Footprints and impressions include a heavy boot print, circular wooden-stump marks near the window and inside, and many small naked-foot prints in the secret room, tracing intruders’ movements.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter VI

A heavy boot print and a circular wooden-stump mark appear near the window, while the secret room contains many clear prints from a naked foot about half the size of an ordinary man’s. Holmes interprets these traces as evidence of the intruders’ movements.
