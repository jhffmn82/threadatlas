Abstract written by a model

The Aurora is Mordecai Smith’s black steam launch, marked by two red streaks or stripes and a black funnel with a white band. Known as a fast “clipper” and one of the river’s fastest launches, it is engaged from old Smith to carry Jonathan Small and Tonga to their ship as an escape vessel. Smith, his son Jim, Small, and the Islander take it down the River Thames after it leaves Smith’s Wharf early Tuesday, prompting a missing-launch investigation and Holmes’s search. A wooden-legged man briefly places it at Jacobson’s Yard for a rudder alteration. It then departs at high speed, making pursuit difficult, but eventually runs aground and is captured by Holmes’s party.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

The Aurora is Mordecai Smith’s steam launch, described as black with two red streaks and a black funnel with a white band. Smith and the wooden-legged man take it down the river, and Holmes sends the Irregulars to locate it.

Chapter IX

The steam launch Aurora is described as black with two red stripes and a black funnel with a white band. It left Smith’s Wharf at about three o’clock on Tuesday morning with Mordecai Smith and his son Jim, and its whereabouts remain unknown.

Chapter X

The Aurora is handed over to Jacobson's Yard by a wooden-legged man for a trivial alteration to its rudder. It leaves the yard at high speed with Jonathan Small, the Islander, and the Smiths aboard, but eventually runs aground and is captured by Holmes's party.

Chapter XI

The Aurora is a fast launch used by Jonathan Small and Tonga because Small had heard that it was a flier. Its speed makes the pursuit difficult, although Holmes and the police eventually overhaul it.
