Prince Evring

Abstract written by a model

Prince Evring is the little, shy prince of Ev and son of the King of Ev. He had been transformed into the image of a purple kitten until Dorothy guesses his hiding place and restores him. He accompanies Dorothy to the throne room, offers Dorothy and Billina a home in the Land of Ev, and waits beside Dorothy during the Nome warriors’ entrance. Though shy around the Scarecrow, he speaks with dignity and grows fond of Dorothy after her rescue. Riding with her on the Sawhorse, he reveals that a tin whistle he found in the Nome King’s palace accidentally contains the Tin Woodman.

Prince Evring is in one work on this site, Ozma of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Prince Evring in Ozma of Oz

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

Evring is a prince of Ev who had been transformed into the image of a purple kitten. Dorothy correctly guesses his hiding place and restores him, after which he accompanies her back to the throne room.

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

Prince Evring returns with Dorothy and offers Billina and Dorothy a home in the Land of Ev. He is shy around the Scarecrow but speaks with dignity when he makes his offer.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

Prince Evring waits with Dorothy for Billina’s mission to succeed. When the Nome warriors enter, he remains beside Dorothy while she holds his hand.

19. The King of Ev

Evring rides with Dorothy on the Sawhorse and has become fond of her after she rescued him. He reveals that he picked up a tin whistle in the Nome King's palace and put it in his pocket, unintentionally carrying the Tin Woodman inside it.
