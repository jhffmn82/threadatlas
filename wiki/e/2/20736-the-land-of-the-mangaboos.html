Abstract written by a model

The Land of the Mangaboos is a beneath-the-earth fairy country with weak gravitation, six colored suns, no night, and shifting lights that change the apparent colors of plants. It contains a glass city, a glass palace with a lofty domed hall and glass pavement, broad garden plains, brooks, glass bridges, planting-grounds, folk gardens, the House of the Sorcerer, the Garden of the Clinging Vines, and a mountain whose prisoners’ side is the Black Pit. Its vegetable people, the Mangaboos, are described as dreadful and dangerous; a Sorcerer can be grown from a bush. The travelers arrive and observe that its people can walk through the air, while the Wizard links this to weak gravitation. They face danger, are walled up, escape through the mountain tunnel and away from the land, and finally lose sight of its colored suns.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

The Land of the Mangaboos contains a glass palace with a lofty domed hall and glass pavement. It is located beneath the earth and is inhabited by vegetable Mangaboos, including a Sorcerer who can be grown from a bush.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The Land of the Mangaboos contains the glass city, broad garden plains, brooks, glass bridges, planting-grounds, and folk gardens. Its people move mainly on the ground even though they can walk through the air.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

Dorothy identifies the land as a fairy country because it has vegetable people and allows Eureka and Jim to talk. The Wizard suggests that its weak gravitational attraction may explain why they can walk in the air.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

The Land of the Mangaboos has six colored suns and no night, so periods between sleep are used to describe days. It contains the city, gardens, the House of the Sorcerer, the Garden of the Clinging Vines, and the mountain with the Black Pit.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The travelers begin their escape far from the Land of the Mangaboos and steadily slant away from it through the mountain. The colored suns of that land are eventually shut out behind them.

8. The Valley of Voices

The travelers have escaped the Country of the Mangaboos through a tunnel in a mountain after the Mangaboos walled them up. The people of Voe describe the Mangaboos as dreadful creatures.
