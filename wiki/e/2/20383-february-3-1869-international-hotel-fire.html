HOTEL FIRES.

The February 3, 1869 International hotel fire began in the basement, probably from a laundry lamp, during an intensely cold night with a strong northwest gale. More than two hundred guests escaped, aided by night watchman Pete O’Brien, and no lives were lost, although the hotel and four other buildings were destroyed.
