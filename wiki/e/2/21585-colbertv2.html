Abstract written by a model

In "LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering," ColBERTv2 is used as a table retriever with the PLAID search engine, integrated into training, fine-tuned per dataset, and used in joint retriever-reader training. In "ARES: An Automated Evaluation Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems," ColBERTv2 is used to construct RAG systems and is identified as the best-performing model tested. In "BlendFilter: Advancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models via Query Generation Blending and Knowledge Filtering," ColBERT v2 is used as a state-of-the-art efficient dense retrieval method over Wikipedia abstracts, generally outperforming BM25. In "Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering," ColBERTv2 is the main retriever, retrieving the top 10 relevant documents for each question and supporting retrieval analysis with Mistral-7B. In "CLERC: A Dataset for U. S. Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Analysis Generation," ColBERTv2 is evaluated at the passage level and performs worst among the evaluated retrieval approaches, achieving 2.2% Recall@10, 7.1% Recall@100, 17.6% Recall@1K, and 1.05 nDCG@10.
