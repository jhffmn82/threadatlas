THE SEVEN AGAINST THEBES

The seven warriors are described as powerful Argive chiefs who sacrifice a bull’s blood and swear either to raze Cadmus’ walls or die in the attempt. They cast lots to determine which gate each will attack and lead their companies toward the city.
