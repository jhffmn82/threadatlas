Ozma's rescue mission brings her, Dorothy, and their companions to the Nome King's dominions to free the Queen of Ev and her ten children. She abandons the idea of fighting the Nome King's army and seeks a peaceful way to restore the royal family. The royal reception celebrates Ozma's successful rescue mission to save the royal family of a neighboring kingdom. Jack Pumpkinhead reads an address congratulating her for its success.
