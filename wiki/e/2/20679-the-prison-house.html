Abstract written by a model

The prison house is a doorless, windowless house with a broad high opening and a platform holding strangers. Its empty lower rooms and upper prison confine Gargoyles whose wings were removed and captured earth people.

12. A Wonderful Escape

The prison house has no doors or windows in its upper section, only a broad opening high beneath the roof and a platform where the prisoners are left. Its lower stories contain empty rooms, and it is used to confine Gargoyles whose wings have been removed as well as the captured earth people.
