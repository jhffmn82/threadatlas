Abstract written by a model

In Ozma of Oz, the underground kingdom of the Nomes is Roquat of the Rocks’s subterranean realm, containing furnaces, forges, great mineral wealth, thousands of doors, and a large Nome force. In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Nome Kingdom is the underground world ruled first by Ruggedo and later by Kaliko, containing the nomes, their treasures, the royal caverns, the Great Domed Cavern, and the Metal Forest. In Tik-Tok of Oz, the underground caverns of the Nome King shelter the nomes and their treasure, receive Kaliko’s visitors, and contain a hidden passage to the Metal Forest’s treasure. In Rinkitink in Oz, Nome King's caverns are the vast connected underground home of the Nome King and his people, containing royal chambers, workshops, treasure rooms, barracks, kitchens, and prisons where King Kitticut and Queen Garee are confined. In Rinkitink in Oz, Nome Kingdom is the mysterious country of the nomes, separated from Oz by the Deadly Desert and containing underground caverns where Inga and his companions seek the captive King Kitticut and Queen Garee. In The Magic of Oz, the Kingdom of the Nomes is the realm that Ruggedo once ruled before being forced to abdicate, and which he hopes to regain through his plans in Oz. In The Hungry Tiger of Oz, the Gnome King's dominions contain the Lost Labyrinth, Kaliko’s underground castle, dangerous caverns, and the kingdom’s highest fire-fall, through which Betsy and her companions travel before escaping. In The Gnome King of Oz, the Kingdom of Gnomes is the underground realm beneath Ev where Ruggedo returns, reclaims rule, and directs the gnomes’ mining and unloading of treasure.

the Nome Kingdom in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where the Nome Kingdom first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Nome Kingdom is an underground world beneath the mountains, home to countless nomes and rich in metals and precious stones. It contains the Nome King's royal cavern, Ruggedo's jeweled cavern, the Great Domed Cavern, and the Metal Forest. At first Ruggedo rules it with absolute sway, guarding it against Shaggy, the Army of Oogaboo, Queen Ann's forces, and other approaching adventurers; its gloomy entrance passage has traps and secret ways, and the invaders are captured or scattered. Ruggedo offers Polychrome a place there, but she refuses. The Jinjin's decree drives Ruggedo out, and eggs prevent his return. Quox appoints Kaliko King, whom the nomes prefer. Later Ruggedo may remain only under Kaliko's supervision as the companions return.
