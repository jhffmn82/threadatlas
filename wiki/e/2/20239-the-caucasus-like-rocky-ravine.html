Abstract written by a model

The rocky ravine in the mountains of Scythia contains the steep rock to which Prometheus is fastened. It is a desolate prison exposed to sun, wind, cold, and solitude. The rocky ravine is the place where Prometheus is bound and confronted by Hermes. Zeus’s thunder and lightning rend its crags, and the shattered rocks fall to bury Prometheus.
