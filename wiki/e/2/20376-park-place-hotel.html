Abstract written by a model

Park Place hotel was a four-story frame family hotel at Summit avenue and St. Peter street, formerly fashionable but unoccupied when it burned to the ground on May 19, 1878. The suspected incendiary fire seriously injured four firemen, who recovered.

HOTEL FIRES.

The Park Place hotel stood at Summit avenue and St. Peter street and was a four-story frame building intended as a family hotel. It was unoccupied when it burned on May 19, 1878, in a fire thought to have been caused by incendiaries; four firemen were seriously injured but recovered.
