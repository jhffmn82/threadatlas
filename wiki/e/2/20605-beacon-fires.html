Abstract written by a model

Beacon fires, kindled by Hephaestus, relay Troy’s news via Mount Ida to Mycenae and the Palace of Atreus, bringing celebrated light, song, hope, and day to Argos.

AGAMEMNON

Beacon fires are kindled on altars and across a chain of watchpoints to transmit news from Troy to the Palace of Atreus. Their light is celebrated as bringing day, song, and hope to Argos.
