Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

And others, far beneath the Ilian wall,

The herald approaches from the shore wearing an olive wreath. His shadowed, dust-covered appearance shows that he has traveled far, and his voice is expected to confirm whether all is well.

_Enter_ HERALD

The Herald announces the Greek victory and the return of the surviving army. He describes the hardships of the war and the storm that destroyed many ships, reports that Menelaus was separated from the fleet, and says that Menelaus may still be alive.
