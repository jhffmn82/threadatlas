Abstract written by a model

The Great Sioux Outbreak in 1862 was Indian warfare in western Minnesota during the Civil War. Beginning without warning on August 18, it caused a Sioux massacre across the reservation, spread rapidly, killed people, destroyed property, and rendered 30,000 homeless within a week. After the Wood Lake battle, Gen. Pope declared the war over; troops remained despite calls to remove them.

THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

The Great Sioux Outbreak in 1862 occurred during a critical period of the Civil War and began with an attack on August 18. It spread rapidly across western Minnesota, killing people, destroying property, and driving thousands of settlers from their homes.

As soon as Gen.

After the Wood Lake battle, Gen. Pope telegraphed that the Indian war was over and asked how to dispose of the troops. Minnesota's people opposed removing the troops, and they remained until making two long marches through the Dakotas and to Montana.
