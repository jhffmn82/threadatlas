Abstract written by a model

The strangers, originating on the earth’s surface, journey through the glass mountain to the Valley of Voe, where invisible inhabitants offer food and directions. They then climb the spiral stairway to the Country of the Gargoyles, face hostile wooden creatures, and are captured and held in the prison house.

8. The Valley of Voices

The travelers reach a lovely valley after passing through the glass mountain from the Country of the Mangaboos. They find the valley apparently deserted, discover invisible inhabitants in a cottage, and are offered food and directions.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The adventurers climb through a dark, narrowing mountain stairway and emerge onto a broad platform in the Country of the Gargoyles. They are surrounded by hostile wooden creatures and defend themselves rather than retreating.
