AN ALLEGORICAL HOROSCOPE IN TWO CHAPTERS.

The dusky maiden gives Robert an astrological forecast and administers an elixir said to grant lasting life and power over his enemies. She advises him to avoid definite convictions, withdraw from disputes when danger arises, and reserve offices for the Okons and their followers.
