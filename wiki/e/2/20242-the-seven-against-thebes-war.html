the Argive invasion of Thebes in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

The Argive invasion of Thebes is a war and battle between Polynices’s Argive army and the Thebans, caused by Polynices’s attempt to recover the rule and inheritance of Thebes after Eteocles breaks their agreement. Though Polynices undertakes it unwillingly against his own city, the Argives surround the seven-gated walls and assault every gate. Eteocles organizes the defense, while Tiresias foresees corpses and mourning unless a sacrifice is made. Parthenopaeus and Capaneus die, both sides suffer heavily, and, after the brothers’ duel, the Thebans drive the unarmed Argives from the field and win.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

THE PHŒNICIAN VIRGINS.

The battle for Thebes is the threatened conflict between Polynices's Argive army and the Thebans defending the city. The approaching forces assemble before the seven-gated walls, causing the Chorus to fear destruction and enslavement.

POLYNICES, CHORUS.

The Argive invasion arises from Polynices's effort to regain the rule of Thebes after Eteocles breaks their agreement. Polynices says that he raises the spear unwillingly against his own city, while Eteocles presents the invasion as a reason not to yield the sceptre.

ETEOCLES, CHORUS, MESSENGER.

The war begins as the Argive army surrounds Thebes and prepares to attack its seven gates. Eteocles organizes the Theban defense, while the chorus laments that Mars has driven the Argives against the descendants of Cadmus. Tiresias predicts that the conflict will bring many corpses and bitter mourning unless the required sacrifice is made.

MESSENGER, JOCASTA, CHORUS.

The battle includes assaults on all seven gates, the deaths of Parthenopaeus and Capaneus, and heavy losses on both sides. After the brothers' duel, the Thebans attack the Argives while they are unarmed and drive them from the field.

the Seven against Thebes war in Four Plays of Aeschylus

Aeschylus · -0471

The Seven against Thebes war is a conflict between Argos and Thebes, in which Polynices and Argive allies assault the Cadmea. Seven champions and an Argive army besiege the city with cavalry, shields, spears, stones, and axes; Theban defenders are chosen for each gate as the Chorus prays. The attack is repulsed, but Eteocles and Polynices kill each other, ending the male line of Laius.
