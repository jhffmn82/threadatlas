  • In Ozma of Oz, the Nome King is the ruler who transforms Evoldo’s family into ornaments, is defeated by eggs, and loses his magic belt.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, the Nome King is the vengeful tyrant who leads an invasion of Oz but forgets his plans after being thrown into the Forbidden Fountain.
  • In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the Nome King is the bad-tempered ruler who smashes Tiktok, regrets it, and promises jewels as compensation.
  • In Tik-Tok of Oz, Ruggedo is the immortal Nome King who loses his magic and throne, is reduced to a wanderer, and reforms.
  • In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Nome King is the underground ruler who traps Queen Ann’s group, orders Tik-Tok to mine gold, and is flattened by Hank.
  • In Rinkitink in Oz, the Nome King is the cautious ruler who confines King Kitticut and Queen Garee and places glowing coals between Inga and safety.
  • In The Magic of Oz, Ruggedo is the former Nome King who conspires to conquer Oz, is transformed several times, and becomes harmless after drinking the Water of Oblivion.
  • In Kabumpo in Oz, Ruggedo is the banished former Gnome King who secretly plots conquest, uses Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic, and becomes a giant carrying Ozma’s palace.
  • In Kabumpo in Oz, Ruggedo is the magician revealed as the giant carrying Ozma’s palace, and he is imprisoned and banished to Ruggedo’s Island.
  • In The Gnome King of Oz, Ruggedo is the banished former Gnome King who recovers the Magic Belt, invades Oz, and ultimately forgets his wickedness.

Nome King in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Nome King, also called Roquat of the Rocks, is the cunning, all-powerful ruler of the Nomes and the Underground World. From his underground palace beneath the mountain at the north end of Ev, he rules warriors and a vast army, sits on a jeweled boulder throne, possesses a magic belt, and uses enchantments to turn captives into ornaments and objects. He bought Evoldo's wife and ten children, imprisoned them, and transformed them into decorations and bric-a-brac, leaving Ev without a ruler. He delights in such transformations, keeps enchanted visitors as a collection of ornaments, and refuses Ozma's demand to free the royal family. Instead, he offers rescuers a guessing challenge: they may identify the transformed prisoners, but failed guesses add more people to his ornaments. He laughs at victims, threatens his officers, and plans further transformations, including Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and the machine man. His power falters because he is terrified of eggs, which are poisonous to Nomes. Billina uses this fear to gain guesses, identify and reverse the transformations, and break his enchantments. As his magic is undone, he becomes enraged, summons his warriors, and tries unsuccessfully to seize or transform the group. Broken eggs blind him, his warriors flee, and he loses the magic belt. Helpless and defeated, he agrees to search for the Tin Woodman, though he later sends warriors after the escaping group. By the end, the Nome King has been defeated and the Nomes no longer laugh at the returning adventurers.

Nome King, also called Roquat the Red and Roquat, is the powerful but persistently angry, very bad king and tyrant of the Nomes and Nome Kingdom. Ruggedo in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Ruggedo, also called the Rough, is an old gnome and former Gnome King, or Metal Monarch, who claims to rule five hundred thousand gnomes and vast underground dominions of Ev. He is banished by Ozma to a small island, where he lives alone for five years after losing his magic belt. Ambitious, greedy, cruel, and repeatedly described as wicked and untrustworthy, he chiefly seeks to recover the belt, regain his throne, revenge himself on Ozma and Dorothy, and rule or destroy Oz and the Emerald City. When Peter reaches the island, Ruggedo alternately threatens him, offers him riches and rank, and uses him in his plans. After the sea exposes a route, Ruggedo finds pirate treasure, claims its jewels, returns to the Kingdom of Gnomes, takes the crown from Kaliko, and has his gnomes repair a Flying Cloak of Invisibility. Unable to cross the Deadly Desert by his own magic, he travels with Peter, seeks further magical advantages, and deceives Scraps so that his cloak can be mended. Invisible in the Emerald City, Ruggedo waits for Ozma’s return, torments palace residents, and tries to steal her magic belt. Once he takes it, he threatens to send everyone to the bottom of the Nonestic Ocean. Peter disrupts him; the Wizard makes him visible; Kuma holds him; and he cannot command the belt. Captured and deprived of speech for seven years, Ruggedo is then freed after being dipped in the fountain of Oblivion, which makes him forget his past wickedness.

CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

Ruggedo, the Gnome King, lives alone in a cave on a small island and angrily objects when Peter sits on his chimney. He says he is the Metal Monarch, ruler of five hundred thousand gnomes, and that he has been banished from his kingdom for trying to recover his magic belt from Ozma of Oz. Although he initially threatens and insults Peter, he eventually agrees to consider helping him escape and offers to make him the richest boy in the world.

CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

Ruggedo urges Peter to cross the exposed seabed, hoping it may lead to Ev. After boarding Blunderoo, he plans to regain his kingdom, recover his magic belt, take revenge on Ozma and Dorothy, and destroy the Emerald City of Oz; he also promises to make Peter a general.

CHAPTER 6 Ruggedo Discovers Pirate’s Treasure

Ruggedo discovers the pirate jewels and greedily claims them, while allowing Peter to have the gold pieces. He tries to use the Flying Cloak of Invisibility to reach the Emerald City, but it does not work because it is torn. Ruggedo plans to have his gnomes mend it, steal back his magic belt, and imprison Ozma and others in cages.

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

Ruggedo returns to the Kingdom of Gnomes, takes back his crown from Kaliko, and resumes his position as Gnome King. He orders lunch, new suits, the unloading of the treasure, and the repair of the magic cloak, but admits that he lacks enough magic to cross the desert. He urges Peter to plant the magic bulbs, crosses the Deadly Desert with him, and heads toward a cottage in Oz.

CHAPTER 8 Peter Meets Kuma Party

Ruggedo is kicked into a gooseberry bush by Kuma Party’s foot and is frightened by Kuma’s detached parts. He refuses Kuma’s hospitality, leads Peter onward, and plans to seize Kuma’s guiding hand so that the magic belt can make it work for him. At Patch’s castle, he claims that his cloak needs mending in order to gain entry.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Ruggedo arrives with Peter to have his magic cloak mended and is disconcerted to discover that Scraps is the Queen of Patch. He falsely agrees that Peter is his slave so that Scrapper will accept the boy as payment, then puts on the repaired cloak and vanishes. Scraps fears that he will use the cloak to reach and threaten the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Ruggedo is the person Peter and his companions are trying to catch. Peter believes that if Ruggedo is afraid of hen's eggs, Ozwold's much larger egg might frighten him.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Ruggedo has escaped and has a flying cloak. Ozwold fears that Ruggedo may already have reached the Emerald City and captured everyone.

CHAPTER 15 Wumbo, Wonder Worker, At Home

Ruggedo is revealed as the invisible visitor trapped in Wumbo’s arm-chair. He says he wants to reach the Emerald City to report a treasure ship, but Wumbo considers him wicked and untrustworthy; after four hours, Ruggedo uses his magic cloak to escape.

CHAPTER 16 Kuma’s Hand Is At Work Again

Ruggedo has escaped from Wumbo's study and gone to the Emerald City wearing a cloak of invisibility. Wumbo says that Ruggedo means to make trouble and orders Kuma Party's arm to find and hold him.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Ruggedo reaches Ozma’s palace in his invisible cloak and finds that Ozma, her magic belt, and her magic appliances are absent. He plans to seize the belt when Ozma returns, destroy the Emerald City and its inhabitants, transport his gnomes there, and take over the party. While waiting, he invisibly pinches and strikes the palace residents, then hides beneath the banquet table and sits in Ozma’s chair.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

Ruggedo uses his magic cloak to remain invisible while attempting to steal Ozma’s magic belt. After the oztrich egg hatches and loses its power over him, he grabs the belt and threatens to send everyone to the bottom of the Nonestic Ocean.

CHAPTER 19 The Wizard Makes The Gnome King Visible

Ruggedo threatens to use the magic belt to transport the people, but Peter interrupts him by throwing objects at him. After the Wizard of Oz makes him visible, Ruggedo struggles against Kuma and tries unsuccessfully to speak to the magic belt.

CHAPTER 20 Peter Is Made A Prince Of Oz

Ruggedo is captured, and his loss of speech is judged to be sufficient punishment for seven years. Ozma grants him freedom after he is dipped in the fountain of Oblivion, causing him to forget his past wickedness.
