AN ALLEGORICAL HOROSCOPE IN TWO CHAPTERS.

A solitary voyageur travels along the Mississippi River on a peaceful mission to the red men. More than eighty years later, he returns, witnesses the city’s transformation and the political dominance of the Okons, and quietly departs in anguish.
