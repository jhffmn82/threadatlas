Clytemnestra’s dream

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Clytemnestra's dream, Oracular thro’ visions, dread vision, dream, dream-land Fear, her vision, night-wandering terror, rede, tale, the dream, the oracle dream, vision, visions

Abstract written by a model

In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, Clytemnestra’s dream depicts her birthing and nursing a full-fanged serpent that wounds her breast, foretelling the serpent’s revenge and the planned murder of Clytemnestra and Aegisthus. In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Clytemnestra’s dream foretells Orestes’s return through the growth of Agamemnon’s sceptre, prompting her to send Chrysothemis to offer libations at Agamemnon’s tomb.

ORESTES (_approaching suddenly_)

Clytemnestra’s dream shows her giving birth to a full-fanged serpent, nursing it, and suffering a bloody wound when it bites her breast. Orestes interprets the dream as a prophecy that he, born from the same womb, will kill her.
