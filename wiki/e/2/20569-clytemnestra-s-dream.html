foretells Orestes's return [through the growth of Agamemnon's sceptre] Clytemnestra dreams that Agamemnon returns, plants his former sceptre in the hearth, and causes a shoot to grow and shadow all Mycenae. Frightened by the dream, she sends Chrysothemis to offer libations at Agamemnon's tomb.

Clytemnestra’s dream in The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies

Aeschylus · -0457

Clytemnestra’s dream depicts her birthing and nursing a full-fanged serpent that wounds her breast; it foretells the serpent’s revenge and the planned murder of Clytemnestra and Aegisthus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ORESTES (_approaching suddenly_)

Clytemnestra’s dream shows her giving birth to a full-fanged serpent, nursing it, and suffering a bloody wound when it bites her breast. Orestes interprets the dream as a prophecy that he, born from the same womb, will kill her.
