Pioneer Press · 1862~

George W. Moore was a printer who came to St. Paul in 1850 and briefly served as foreman of the Pioneer office. He became part owner and manager of the Daily Minnesotian, publishing it with John P. Owens until selling his interest to Dr. Foster in 1860. Appointed collector of the port of St. Paul by Abraham Lincoln in 1861, he held that post for more than twenty years. He was Frank Moore's brother.

Geo. W. Moore was the unnamed narrator's brother and was part owner and manager of the Daily Minnesotian in 1858.

TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

George W. Moore came to St. Paul in 1850 and briefly served as foreman for Mr. Goodhue. He later partnered with John P. Owens in publishing the Minnesotian, sold his interest to Dr. Foster in 1860, and served as collector of the port of St. Paul for more than twenty years after President Lincoln appointed him in 1861.
