Chapter VIII

Holmes consults a gazetteer and learns that the Andaman Islands lie north of Sumatra in the Bay of Bengal. The book describes their aborigines as exceptionally small, fierce people with remarkably small feet who use stone-headed clubs and poisoned arrows.
