guessing challenge in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The guessing challenge is a hopeless task set by the Nome King: entrants must touch palace ornaments and say “Ev” to identify and restore transformed members of Ev’s royal family. Wrong guesses turn participants into enslaved ornaments. The officers, private, and Tiktok fail; the Nome King delays the remaining guests, until Dorothy persists through several wrong guesses and correctly identifies Evring.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

12. The Eleven Guesses

The challenge requires a person to touch an ornament and say the word "Ev" in an attempt to restore a transformed member of the royal family. A wrong guess results in the guesser becoming an ornament and being enslaved.

13. The Nome King Laughs

The guessing challenge requires each entrant to make guesses inside the Nome King’s palace, with failure resulting in transformation into an ornament. The officers and private fail, while the Nome King postpones dealing with the remaining guests until the next day.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

The guessing task requires the visitors to identify which palace ornaments are the transformed people of Ev. Tiktok fails on his final guess, while Dorothy makes several unsuccessful guesses before correctly identifying Evring.
