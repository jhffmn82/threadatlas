The decisive battle of Mill Springs in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

The decisive battle of Mill Springs was a battle in Kentucky, part of the American Civil War and the first important Union victory in the Southwest after its outbreak. Led against Confederate commander Zollicoffer, with the Second Minnesota Infantry Regiment as a Union hero, it inflicted about 600 Confederate losses, cost 248 Union losses, captured twelve cannon, opened a route to East Tennessee, and forced Albert Sidney Johnston’s withdrawal.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE DECISIVE BATTLE OF MILL SPRINGS.

The decisive battle of Mill Springs was the first important Union victory in the Southwest after the outbreak of the Civil War. It resulted in Confederate losses of about 600 killed, wounded, and captured, Union losses of 248 killed and wounded, and the capture of twelve cannon and large quantities of equipment.
