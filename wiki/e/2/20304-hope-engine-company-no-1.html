Abstract written by a model

Hope Engine Company No. 1, organized in 1857, petitioned for hose and included foreman Michael Leroy with assistants R.C. Wiley and Joseph S. Herey. It received a double-deck hand engine in 1858, moved from Franklin and Fourth streets to Third street, and disbanded over the first steamer’s engineer.

FIREMEN AND FIRES OF PIONEER DAYS.

Hope Engine Company No. 1 was organized in 1857 and petitioned the common council for 500 feet of hose. It received one of the new hand engines in the fall of 1858, was housed first on Franklin and Fourth streets and later on Third street, and disbanded after objecting to the engineer selected for the first steamer.
