Prague in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Prague is a city and university town central to Professor Presbury’s case. He visited it before his symptoms began, and Holmes believes he learned to use a strong drug there. Dorak’s Bohemian nationality and H. Lowenstein’s correspondence about the treatment connect Prague to the serum Holmes considers central.

Prague is identified as the place Professor Presbury visited before his symptoms began and as the location from which H. Lowenstein sends correspondence about the treatment. Holmes considers the connection between Prague and the serum central to the case.
