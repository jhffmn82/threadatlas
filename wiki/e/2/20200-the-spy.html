THE SPY in Four Plays of Aeschylus

Aeschylus · -0471

The Spy is a scout and messenger sent to observe the Argive camp. He personally sees seven warriors swear to destroy Cadmus’ city or die, warns Eteocles of their armed advance, and reports each champion assigned to a gate, including their boasts, shields, and threats. After the assault, he announces that the city, its walls, and gates are safe, but that Eteocles and Polynices killed each other at the seventh gate.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE SEVEN AGAINST THEBES

The Spy reports that he personally observed seven Argive warriors swear a blood oath to destroy Cadmus’ city or die before it. He warns Eteocles that the Argive forces are advancing fully armed and urges him to rank the bravest defenders at the gates.

Enter on one side THE SPY; on the other ETEOCLES and the SIX

The Spy returns from scouting the enemy and reports which champion has been assigned to each gate. He describes the champions, their boasts, their shield devices, and the threats they pose to the city.

Enter THE SPY.

THE SPY announces that the city stands safe after the assault, with its walls and gates secure. He reports that Eteocles and Polynices have been killed by each other at the seventh gate.
