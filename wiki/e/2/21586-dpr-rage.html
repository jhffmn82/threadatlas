Abstract written by a model

DPR-RAGE is a system using a DPR retriever, TaPEx answer generator, and RAGE-based joint training. It uses one-dimensional embeddings, achieves 84.35@5 recall on NQ-TABLES, and reports NQ-TABLES Token F1/EM of 49.68/43.02 and E2E-WTQ accuracy of 48.79, alongside specified training and inference costs.

Experimental Setup

DPR-RAGE uses a DPR retriever, TaPEx as the answer generator, and RAGE-based joint training. It obtains 49.68 Token F1 and 43.02 EM on NQ-TABLES, and 48.79 accuracy on E2E-WTQ.

CLTR and T-RAG Evaluation

DPR-RAGE has a reported training speed of 2.1 iterations per second, batch size 1, and training times of 300 minutes on NQ and 35 minutes on WTQ. Its inference speed is 1.22 seconds per iteration with inference batch size 4.

Computational Cost

DPR-RAGE is referenced as using one-dimensional embeddings in comparison with the larger multi-dimensional embeddings used by LI-RAGE.
