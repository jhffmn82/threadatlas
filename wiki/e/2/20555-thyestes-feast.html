Thyestes’ feast in The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies

Aeschylus · -0457

Thyestes’ feast is a crime of inherited horror and kinship violence: Atreus welcomes Thyestes, serves him the flesh of his own children, and Thyestes eats unknowingly before discovering the deed. It is later invoked as an example the Chorus recognizes but cannot fully interpret.

Thyestes’ feast is invoked as an example of the house’s inherited horror and kinship crimes. The Chorus recognizes Cassandra’s reference to it but admits that it cannot understand the rest of her prophecy.

At the feast of Thyestes, Atreus served Thyestes the flesh of his own children while pretending to welcome him. Thyestes unknowingly ate the food before discovering what Atreus had done.
