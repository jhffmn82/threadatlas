Abstract written by a model

Dorothy picks a bright, pretty dinner-pail with a stout handle from the dinner-pail tree and carries it toward the seashore. The Wheelers call it stolen under their law and threaten her over it. It holds lemonade and a large meal, bears the royal E of Ev, and later serves as Tiktok’s weapon, frightening or knocking down Wheelers and soldiers until his action runs down.

3. Letters in the Sand

Dorothy picks a bright, pretty dinner-pail with a stout handle and carries it toward the seashore. The Wheelers accuse her of stealing it and use it as the reason for threatening her.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

The dinner pail contains lemonade, turkey, cold tongue, lobster salad, bread and butter, custard pie, an orange, strawberries, nuts, and raisins. Its bottom bears the royal E of the royal family of Ev, and Dorothy packs the unused food back into it.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

The dinner-pail serves as Tiktok’s weapon against the Wheelers and Langwidere’s soldiers. Its loud blows frighten or knock down his opponents, although Tiktok eventually stops when his action runs down.
