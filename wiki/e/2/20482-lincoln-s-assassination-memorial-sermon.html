THE DEATH OF PRESIDENT LINCOLN.

Lincoln’s assassination memorial sermon was delivered by Frederick A. Noble at the House of Hope church on the Sunday after the assassination. It recounted Lincoln’s early history and presidential achievements, condemned the killing, and eulogized Secretary of State Seward.
