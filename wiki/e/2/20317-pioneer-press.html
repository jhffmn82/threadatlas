Pioneer Press · 1862~

Pioneer Press, a newspaper, absorbed the Minnesotian in 1861 and united with the Pioneer in 1874. By 1908, Frank Moore had served it and its antecedents for fifty years, heading its composing room; it employed George H. Colgrave.

St. Paul Press in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

St. Paul Press, also called the St. Paul Daily Press, was founded in 1861 by W. R. Marshall and Thomas F. Slaughter using the Times office and material leased from Thomas M. Newson. Its competition led Dr. Foster to sell the Minnesotian plant, ending their rivalry. It employed foremen, moved in 1869, and united with Pioneer in 1874.

Pioneer Press in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Pioneer Press is a daily newspaper that also publishes a Sunday edition. During the war, while the Pioneer was moving its plant, the Press had the field exclusively on July 4, 1863. It delayed an extra until it received the fullest possible details of Union victories, then sold papers to a large waiting crowd. Its Sunday edition illustrates the labor of large nineteenth-century printing, and its composing room later used the Minnesotian proof press. Louis E. Fisher and Richard Bradley served for many years.
