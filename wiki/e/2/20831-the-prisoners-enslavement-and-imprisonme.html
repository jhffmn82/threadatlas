the prisoners' enslavement and imprisonment in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The imprisonment and enslavement begin when Evoldo bargains with the Nome King and sells the Queen of Ev and her ten children into his power. In the Nome King’s underground palace, they are held captive and transformed into ornaments and bric-a-brac. Ozma offers to free them, but the guessing challenge ends with her own enslavement after her final incorrect guess.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

The royal family's imprisonment concerns the Queen of Ev and her ten royal children, whom the Nome King is holding captive. Ozma offers to seek a way to free them, and Langwidere welcomes the possibility because their restoration would relieve her of ruling the kingdom.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The queen of Ev and her ten children have been enslaved and held prisoner by the Nome King in his underground palace. They have also been transformed into ornaments and bric-a-brac.

12. The Eleven Guesses

The challenge threatens to enslave anyone who guesses incorrectly. Ozma risks her freedom and is no longer free after her final guess.
