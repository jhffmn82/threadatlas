model canonical incremental adaptation

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: canonical incremental adaptation

Abstract written by a model

Model canonical incremental adaptation is an approach for updating the code generation method kNN-TRANX by switching or updating its datastore with external data rather than adapting model parameters. It aims to lower incremental-learning cost and avoid catastrophic forgetting. It achieves performance comparable to fully fine-tuned models with 7,000 times fewer trainable parameters. Across 10k, 50k, and 95k external-data updates, larger datastore updates generally improve BLEU, CodeBLEU, and EM scores.
