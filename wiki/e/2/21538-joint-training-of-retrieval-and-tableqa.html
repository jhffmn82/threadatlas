joint training of retrieval and TableQA in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

Joint training of retrieval and TableQA jointly trains a retriever and TableQA reader using explicit table-level signals. After DPR or ColBERTv2 fine-tuning, it applies a compositional loss that combines answer prediction with table relevance, adjusting retrieval from gold tables that enable correct answer generation. Unlike ablations that freeze the retriever, joint training with RAGE improves retrieval and end-to-end TableQA: end-to-end gains are about 0.6–2.83%, and NQ-TABLES recall rises from 85.21 to 87.90. LI-RAGE training takes longer because of dynamic retrieval and larger multi-dimensional embeddings.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

It jointly trains the retriever and reader with explicit table-level signals.

Methodology / Table Retrieval .. Learned Table Relevance

Joint training uses a compositional loss combining answer prediction and table relevance. It adjusts the retriever using retrieved tables that are gold tables and from which the answer generator successfully produces the correct answer.

Experimental Setup

Joint retriever-reader training is applied after fine-tuning the DPR or ColBERTv2 retriever on each dataset. Ablations that disable joint training leave the retriever unchanged during the later training stage and generally reduce end-to-end performance.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

Joint training with RAGE improves both retrieval and end-to-end TableQA performance compared with a frozen system. It improves end-to-end performance by approximately 0.6–2.83% and particularly increases NQ-TABLES recall from 85.21 to 87.90.

Computational Cost

Training time for LI is not significantly higher than for DPR because both models use contrastive learning. LI-RAGE's end-to-end training takes longer due to slower dynamic retrieval and larger multi-dimensional embeddings.
