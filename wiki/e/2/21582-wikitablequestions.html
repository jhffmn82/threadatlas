WikiTableQuestions in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

WikiTableQuestions is the supers2026-10-28et of E2E-WTQ used to additionally fine-tune TaPEx because E2E-WTQ is too small for learning a robust TableQA model. Its dataset splits are the same as those of E2E-WTQ, so the text states that no test samples are leaked. WikiTableQuestions is the dataset used for tapex-large fine-tuning for E2E-WTQ. The listed setup uses 40 epochs, a learning rate of 0.00002 with linear decay, AdamW, eight GPUs, per-device batch size 1, gradient accumulation of 4 steps, weight decay 0.01, and label smoothing 0.1.

WikiTableQuestion is a table question-answering benchmark covering diverse domains and requiring operations such as comparison and aggregation. TableRAG obtains scores of 84.62 with Claude-3.5, 80.40 with DeepSeek-V3, and 78.00 with Qwen-2.5-72B. TableRAG's total latency on WikiTQ is reported as 13.50 seconds.
