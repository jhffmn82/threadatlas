emerald grasshopper in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

After Ozma’s unsuccessful guesses, her final guess transforms Ozma of Oz into a pretty grasshopper formed from a single emerald. The hard, lifeless object rests on a palace table, is briefly mistaken for food by Billina, then is restored by Billina, revealing Ozma of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

12. The Eleven Guesses

Ozma's final guess transforms her into a pretty grasshopper that seems to have been formed from a single emerald. It rests on the edge of a small round table in the palace.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The emerald grasshopper is a hard, lifeless transformation that Billina briefly mistakes for food. After the Queen suggests it may be the object Billina seeks, Billina restores it and reveals Ozma of Oz.
