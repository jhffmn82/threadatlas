Billina's eggs in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Billina's eggs are fresh eggs poisonous and believed fatal to Nomes. One is laid under the Nome King's throne and frightens him before the Scarecrow pockets it. Later the Scarecrow throws two eggs at the Nome King, blinding him and driving the warriors away. Billina uses eggs against pursuing warriors; Dorothy transforms those warriors into eggs, blocking a passage and frightening the rest away.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

Billina's egg is laid under the Nome King's throne, where it frightens him because eggs are poisonous to Nomes. The Scarecrow removes it and places it in a pocket.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

Billina’s eggs are treated by the Nome warriors as deadly poison. The Scarecrow throws two of them at the Nome King, blinding him and causing the warriors to flee.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

Eggs are poisonous to Nomes and blind the Nome King when Billina uses them against him. Dorothy later transforms pursuing warriors into eggs, which block the passage and frighten the remaining warriors away.

the eggs in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Eggs are the objects most feared by the nomes because their insides are fatal when they touch them. Possibly enchanted by Tititi-Hoochoo, they do not come through the Hollow Tube at first, then roll from a jeweled locket, pursue Ruggedo, drive him out, and bar the Nome Kingdom.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

Eggs are the objects most feared by the nomes because they belong on the earth's surface and can destroy a nome if their insides touch him. Ruggedo asks whether any eggs are coming through the Hollow Tube, but Kaliko sees none.

Chapter Eighteen

The eggs roll out of the great jeweled locket and steadily pursue Ruggedo. They terrify him, drive him from his cavern, and remain at the entrance to bar his return.
