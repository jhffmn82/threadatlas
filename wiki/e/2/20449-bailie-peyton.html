THE DECISIVE BATTLE OF MILL SPRINGS.

Bailie Peyton was a Confederate lieutenant and the son of a prominent Union man in Tennessee. After refusing to surrender, he shot Lieutenant Stout through the body and was immediately killed; Lieutenant Tuttle captured his sword, which had previously been presented to him by citizens of New Orleans.
