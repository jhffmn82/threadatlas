Abstract written by a model

The broad spiral stairway, cut in the rock inside Pyramid Mountain, begins at an arched opening and circles upward like a corkscrew. Its low, broad steps let Jim draw the buggy, and its landings show views inside and outside the mountain. After a long ascent, it leads to the Country of the Gargoyles, where the travelers emerge onto its broad platform.

10. The Braided Man of Pyramid Mountain

The stairs begin at an arched opening in Pyramid Mountain and circle upward like a corkscrew. They are broad and initially low enough for Jim to draw the buggy, with landings that open onto views inside and outside the mountain.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The spiral stairway leads from the mountain's interior to the broad platform of the Country of the Gargoyles. The travelers emerge from it into the silent wooden land, where the Gargoyles immediately observe them.
