Atossa

Abstract written by a model

Atossa is the Persian queen-mother, widow and former consort of Darius, mother and royal patron of Xerxes, and resident of the Palace of Susa. In The Persians, she fears that Persia may collapse despite its wealth and numbers after dreams and omens warn of danger in Xerxes’ expedition. She consults the Persian Elders, questions a Messenger closely about the defeat, survivors, commanders, Greek ships, and battle, and responds with mourning, libations, prayers, and concern for Xerxes’ return. Through ritual mourning she summons Darius and gravely recounts Persia’s fall near Athens, Xerxes’ land-and-sea campaign, and his return from the Hellespont with only a remnant. After Darius departs, she fetches royal clothing to meet and comfort Xerxes in humiliation.

Atossa is in one work on this site, Four Plays of Aeschylus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

INTRODUCTION

Atossa is the Persian queen-mother in The Persians. She gravely and courageously recounts the catastrophe to the ghost of Darius and contributes to the play's dignified portrayal of Persia despite Xerxes' lack of manliness.

THE PERSIANS

Atossa, widow of Darius and mother of Xerxes, fears that the Persian state may collapse despite its wealth and numbers. She recounts dreams and omens suggesting danger to Xerxes and consults the Chorus of Persian Elders. She plans to offer libations and prayers to the gods and to the dead, especially Darius.

Enter a MESSENGER.

Atossa questions the MESSENGER about the survivors, the commanders, the Greek ships, and the course of the battle. After hearing of the defeat, she brings libations for Darius, orders comfort for Xerxes if he returns, and asks the gods to summon Darius from the dead.

The GHOST OF DARIUS rises.

Atossa tells Darius that Persian power has fallen through the defeat near Athens and that Xerxes led the expedition by land and sea. She explains that Xerxes reached the bridge over the Hellespont but returned with only a remnant. After Darius departs, she goes to fetch a suitable royal robe and meet her son in his humiliation.
