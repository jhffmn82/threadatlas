King of Ev in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907 · the work’s page · read it where King of Ev first appears

Abstract written by a model

King of Ev, named Evoldo, is a cruel ruler who beat servants to death. He bought Tiktok and bargained with the Nome King, selling his lovely wife, the Queen of Ev, and their ten children for a long life. Unable to recover them, he jumped into the sea and drowned, destroying that long life.

The King of Ev is described as a cruel ruler who beat his servants until they died. He had a lovely wife and ten children, whom he sold to the Nome King, and later tried unsuccessfully to recover them.

11. The Nome King

The King of Ev exchanged the Queen of Ev and their ten children to the Nome King for a long life. He then jumped into the sea and was drowned, which the Nome King says destroyed the long life he had provided.

King Evoldo in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907 · the work’s page · read it where King Evoldo first appears

Abstract written by a model

King Evoldo was the cruel king of Ev, ruler of the Land of Ev and uncle of Princess Langwidere. He had a wife and ten children and used a whip to keep the Wheelers in order. In anger, he sold his wife and children, the royal family of Ev, to the Nome King, Roquat of the Rocks, for a long life. Regretting the sale, he locked Tiktok inside a rock, threw the key into the ocean, jumped into the sea after it, and drowned, leaving Ev without a royal family to rule.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

Evoldo is the name Tiktok gives for the cruel King of Ev. After regretting the sale of his family, he locked Tiktok inside the rock, threw the key into the ocean, jumped in after it, and drowned.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

King Evoldo ruled the Land of Ev and used to carry a whip to keep the Wheelers in order. He jumped into the sea, leaving no royal family to rule the land.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Evoldo, the former King of Ev, sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King in exchange for a long life. After realizing what he had done, he jumped into the sea and drowned himself.
