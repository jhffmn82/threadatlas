Abstract written by a model

The Black Pit is a dark, yawning cave halfway up the mountain, beyond the colored suns' rays. After the Princess spares three humans, the Mangaboos drive Jim, Eureka, and the other animals there and seal the entrance with glass rocks. Inside, a tunnel leads through the mountain and provides a way out.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

The Black Pit is in the mountain and is designated as the place where the Mangaboos intend to send Jim and Eureka, along with the other animals. The Mangaboos begin driving the animals toward it after the Princess spares the three humans.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The Black Pit is a dark cave halfway up the mountain, beyond the reach of the colored suns' rays. The Mangaboos use glass rocks to seal Jim and the piglets inside, but its interior contains a tunnel leading through the mountain.
