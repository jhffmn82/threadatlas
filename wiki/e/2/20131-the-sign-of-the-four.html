the sign of the four in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

The sign of the four is an inscription and identifying mark used by Jonathan Small with Mahomet Singh, Abdullah Khan, and Dost Akbar. It first appears as a hieroglyphic of four touching crosses on a mysterious paper and on Captain Morstan’s chart. Later, its words are scrawled on torn note-paper beside Bartholomew Sholto’s body, where Holmes treats it as significant murder evidence.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

The sign of the four appears on the mysterious paper as a hieroglyphic resembling four touching crosses. It is accompanied by the names Jonathan Small, Mahomet Singh, Abdullah Khan, and Dost Akbar.

Chapter V

The words “The sign of the four” are scrawled on a torn sheet of note-paper beside Bartholomew Sholto’s body. Holmes treats the message as significant evidence in the murder.

Chapter VII

The Sign of the Four is the name Jonathan Small gave to the mark representing himself and his associates on Captain Morstan’s chart.
