Abstract written by a model

The Sholtos’ treasure is a massive, solid iron chest of Indian workmanship, made of Benares metal-work and associated with the rajah’s and Agra treasures: precious stones and choice pearls. Conspirators open it with a key hung by a silken cord from its carved handle, inventory the jewels, replace them, and hide the chest beneath bricks in a sound wall of the Agra fort. Later it is carried by Achmet in a shawl-wrapped bundle, said to contain only family matters, though others believe it holds the rajah’s jewels. The box is taken from a chemical laboratory, and eventually found aboard the Aurora. Its key has been thrown into the Thames, so Dr. Watson forces it open for Mary Morstan; despite its thick iron construction, it is empty.

The Sholtos' treasure is found in a solid iron chest of Indian workmanship aboard the Aurora. The chest has no key but is heavy, so the pursuers transfer it carefully to their cabin.
