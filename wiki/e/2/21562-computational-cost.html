Computational cost is a distinct advantage of RAG: processing fewer input tokens makes it substantially cheaper than long-context processing. SELF-ROUTE further reduces cost while preserving performance comparable to long-context processing.

Abstract

Computation cost is significantly lower for RAG than for long-context processing. SELF-ROUTE reduces computation cost while preserving performance comparable to long-context processing.

opening

Computational cost is substantially lower for RAG because it decreases the number of input tokens processed by the LLM. SELF-ROUTE lowers cost relative to long-context processing while maintaining comparable performance.

conclusion

LC has higher computational cost than RAG. The proposed dynamic routing method achieves LC-comparable performance at significantly reduced cost.
