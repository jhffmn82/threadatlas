Abstract written by a model

The Islander is Jonathan Small’s able, efficient ally and Andaman islander: a small black dwarf with a greatly misshapen head and features marked by bestiality and cruelty. Savage and violent, he carries a stone-headed wooden mace, a blowpipe, and poisoned darts or poison spines. He enters through a roof trap-door and helps Jonathan Small enter and leave a secret room by managing a rope, hook, and window. Able to reach a lofty garret inaccessible to Jonathan Small, he enters Bartholomew Sholto’s room, kills him with poison, and leaves creosote-marked bare footprints. During pursuit, wrapped in a dark ulster or blanket, he threatens the pursuers with a short wooden weapon, falls overboard, and dies in the Thames.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter VI

The mysterious ally helps Jonathan Small enter and leave the room by lowering and drawing up the rope, securing it to the wall hook, and closing the window from inside. Holmes believes this ally entered through the roof trap-door and considers him an unusual figure in the history of crime.

Chapter VII

The unnamed associate of Jonathan Small could reach the lofty garret that Jonathan Small could not reach because of his wooden leg. He entered Bartholomew Sholto’s room, killed him with poison, and left a trail marked by a naked foot dipped in creasote.

Chapter VIII

Holmes infers that the fugitives’ second companion is an Andaman islander from his diminutive bare footprints, stone-headed wooden mace, and poisoned blow-pipe darts. Holmes describes the Andaman islanders as fierce and dangerous, with a reputation for attacking shipwrecked crews.

Chapter X

The Islander is the small, distorted companion of Jonathan Small, wrapped in a dark ulster or blanket. He threatens the pursuers with a short wooden weapon and falls into the Thames after Holmes and Watson fire at him.
