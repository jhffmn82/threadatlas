Robert A. Smith in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Robert A. Smith is portrayed allegorically as a young paleface foretold by a dusky maiden to become mighty chief of the great city. The later scene identifies him as St. Paul’s 1902 mayoral candidate, ruling with Okon support and dispensing offices.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

AN ALLEGORICAL HOROSCOPE IN TWO CHAPTERS.

As a young paleface, Robert is told by the dusky maiden that he will become the mighty chief of the future great city and reign for a long time. In the later scene, a banner identifies him as the candidate for mayor in 1902, and he rules from the magnificent building with the support of the Okons.
