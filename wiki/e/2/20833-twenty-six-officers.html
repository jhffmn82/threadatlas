twenty-six officers in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The twenty-six army officers, who command a private, claim courage but fear the hammer; the Cowardly Lion and the Tiger carry them across in pairs, and wrong guesses later make them an ornament.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. The Giant with the Hammer

The twenty-six officers are too frightened to walk or run beneath the hammer, despite their claimed courage in battle. The Cowardly Lion and the Tiger carry them across in pairs, and they insist that the private must also be rescued so they will have someone to command.
