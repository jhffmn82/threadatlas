INTRODUCTION

The fourth-century archaistic theory holds that Rhesus was written by an anonymous poet who deliberately imitated an earlier tragic style. It explains the play's early-seeming verse and diction as an archaizing reaction associated with the period around Plato or Aristotle, but the introduction notes that direct evidence for such a school is lacking.
