TaPEx in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

TaPEx is a BART-based pretrained model for tabular data understanding and state-of-the-art TableQA, autoregressively predicting answers; it warm-starts TableQA, is fine-tuned on WikiTableQuestions for small E2E-WTQ, and is published in the International Conference on Learning Representations.

Related Work

TaPEx is identified as a recent state-of-the-art TableQA model that directly predicts answers through autoregressive generation.

Experimental Setup

TaPEx is a state-of-the-art pretrained model for tabular data understanding based on BART and is used to warm-start the TableQA model. It is additionally fine-tuned on WikiTableQuestions for E2E-WTQ.
