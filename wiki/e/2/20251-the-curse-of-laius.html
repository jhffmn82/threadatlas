The curse of Laius begins when Laius violates Apollo’s warning to die childless by fathering Oedipus. It brings ruin to his house: Oedipus kills Laius and marries Iokaste, their relationship is revealed, and their sons enter a fatal conflict.

INTRODUCTION

The Laius-Oedipus family curse begins with Apollo's warning to Laius that a child would bring ruin to his house. Its consequences include Oedipus's killing of Laius, marriage to Iokaste, the revelation of their relationship, and the fatal conflict between their sons.

Enter on one side THE SPY; on the other ETEOCLES and the SIX

The curse associated with Laius begins with Apollo's command that Laius die childless, but Laius fathers Oedipus instead. The Chorus traces the later disasters of the house back to this rejected divine warning.
