Bartholomew Sholto in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Bartholomew Sholto is the clever twin brother of Thaddeus Sholto and one of Major John Sholto’s two sons. Favoured by his father according to Thaddeus, he is a master of Pondicherry Lodge and has a chamber fitted as a chemical laboratory. Angry with Thaddeus for contacting Miss Morstan, he discovers the hidden treasure by noticing four unaccounted-for feet in the house’s height and finding a sealed garret. He possesses the stolen Indian gems inherited from his father and keeps the recovered treasure in his laboratory. There, after the treasure’s discovery, an unnamed associate of Jonathan Small enters through the roof; Tonga kills Bartholomew during the theft. He is found many hours dead in the locked laboratory, seated stiff and cold with contracted limbs, a distorted face and fixed smile, and a poisoned thorn above his ear. The poison is a powerful vegetable alkaloid delivered through a ceiling hole.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

Bartholomew Sholto is Thaddeus Sholto’s twin brother and is described as clever but angry with Thaddeus over his decision to contact Miss Morstan. He discovered the hidden treasure by calculating that four feet of the house’s height were unaccounted for and locating a sealed garret above the highest room.

Chapter V

Bartholomew Sholto is found dead in his locked chemical laboratory, seated in a chair with his head on his shoulder and a ghastly fixed smile on his face. His limbs appear twisted, and a poisoned thorn has been stuck into the skin above his ear.

Chapter VI

Bartholomew Sholto is found dead in the locked room with severely contracted muscles and a distorted face. Holmes concludes that a poisoned thorn was driven or shot into his scalp through the hole in the ceiling.

Chapter VII

Bartholomew Sholto was murdered in the garret after discovering the Agra treasure. Sherlock Holmes believes the unnamed associate of Jonathan Small entered the room and killed him with poison.
