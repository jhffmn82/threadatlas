The Sons of Malta were a secret order organized in the early 1850s that expanded rapidly throughout the West. After receiving a charter from Pittsburgh in 1858, Pioneer editorial staff established a successful St. Paul lodge at Empire Hall, drawing merchants, lawyers, doctors, printers, and many local men. The Sons of Malta were organized in the early 1850s and expanded rapidly throughout the West. Members of the Pioneer editorial staff obtained a charter in 1858 and established a highly successful lodge in St. Paul. The order used elaborate initiations, including public confessions, frightening ceremonies, and bouncing candidates into the air; its membership and reputation declined after Rev. John Penman published an illustrated exposure of its secret work in Harper's Weekly.
