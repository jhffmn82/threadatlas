The journey to the Nome King’s palace is an expedition by Ozma, Dorothy, their companions, and the Army of twenty-seven to rescue Ev’s royal family. Beginning at daybreak from the Land of Ev, it progresses toward the valley, then crosses a gulf and follows a rocky, narrowing mountain path, requiring abandonment of the chariot.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The journey is undertaken by Ozma, Dorothy, their companions, and the Army of twenty-seven to rescue Ev's royal family. The procession leaves the palace at daybreak and makes good progress toward the valley leading to the Nome King's domain.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

The journey through the mountains takes the procession over a gulf and along an increasingly rocky, constricted path. The travelers must abandon the chariot and use the backs of the Cowardly Lion and the Tiger, as well as the other available means, to continue.
