15. Old Friends are Reunited

Princess Ozma summons Dorothy's old friends to welcome her. The Scarecrow, Billina, Nick Chopper, and H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E. arrive, while Oz is also reunited with the Scarecrow and Nick Chopper. The gathering continues in cheerful conversation until bedtime.
