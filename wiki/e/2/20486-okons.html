AN ALLEGORICAL HOROSCOPE IN TWO CHAPTERS.

The Okons are described as descendants of the sea kings from the bogs of Tipperary who will aid Robert when the city becomes too large for him to manage alone. In the later city, they and their followers control every office within Robert’s gift.
