the jury in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The jury is a nine-member body of animals, animated dummies, and people: the Cowardly Lion, Hungry Tiger, Jim the Cab-horse, Yellow Hen, Scarecrow, Wizard, Tik-tok the Machine Man, Sawhorse, and Zeb of Hugson’s Ranch. Ozma says it meets the law’s requirement, with animals included because they understand one another better than people do. After hearing arguments in Eureka’s trial, it finds her guilty of eating the piglet and recommends death, but the judgment proves mistaken.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

18. The Trial of Eureka the Kitten

The jury is to consist of the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, Jim the Cab-horse, the Yellow Hen, the Scarecrow, the Wizard, Tik-tok the Machine Man, the Sawhorse, and Zeb of Hugson’s Ranch. Ozma says these nine members satisfy the law’s requirement, and several are animals because animals understand one another better than people do.

19. The Wizard Performs Another Trick

The jury consists of animals, animated dummies, and people. After considering the arguments, it finds Eureka guilty of eating the piglet and recommends that she be put to death, but this judgment is shown to be mistaken.
