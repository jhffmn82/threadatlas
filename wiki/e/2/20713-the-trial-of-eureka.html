Abstract written by a model

The trial of Eureka is a planned fair trial in the Throne Room, judged by Ozma, over whether Eureka murdered and ate Ozma's piglet. Ozma says Eureka must die if guilty. A jury condemns her, but a substitute piglet and Eureka's explanation reveal the accusation's weakness.

18. The Trial of Eureka the Kitten

The trial is planned for the Throne Room, with Ozma serving as judge and the people of Oz admitted to hear the testimony. If Eureka is found guilty, Ozma says the kitten must die.

19. The Wizard Performs Another Trick

The trial concerns whether Eureka murdered and ate Princess Ozma's piglet. The jury condemns her, but the proceedings expose the weakness of the accusation when a substitute piglet is produced and Eureka explains what actually happened.
