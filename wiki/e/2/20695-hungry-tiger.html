Abstract written by a model

In Ozma of Oz, Hungry Tiger joins the adventurers, helps pull Ozma’s golden chariot, guards Dorothy, and frightens Nome warriors. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, the Hungry Tiger welcomes Dorothy, refuses to eat Jim, draws Ozma’s chariot, and tackles the Sawhorse. In The Road to Oz, Hungry Tiger draws Ozma’s royal chariot and accompanies the Cowardly Lion. In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, Hungry Tiger acts as one of Ozma’s chief guardians and comforts and carries a lost baby instead of eating it. In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Hungry Tiger helps the Cowardly Lion defend Dorothy and becomes reconciled with Hank. In The Magic of Oz, the Hungry Tiger protects Dorothy and the Wizard, carries companions, attacks Kiki, and is transformed into a Rabbit. In The Hungry Tiger of Oz, Hungry Tiger serves as a protector of prisoners and companions while seeking to return to Oz.

Hungry Tiger in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907 · the work’s page · read it where Hungry Tiger first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Hungry Tiger is a savage, hungry tiger with an exceptionally large appetite for living creatures, from chipmunks to fat babies, but a conscience prevents cruel deeds. Calling himself a good beast and a bad tiger, he leaves the forest and joins the Cowardly Lion and a little band of adventurers. He accompanies the Lion, frightens servants without harming them, helps pull a golden chariot, guards Dorothy, and snarls at Nome warriors. During the royal feast, he loses his appetite.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The Hungry Tiger

The Hungry Tiger explains that he is constantly hungry and craves living creatures, including fat babies, but refuses to eat them because his conscience tells him it is wrong. He says this makes him a good beast but a disgracefully bad tiger, and he has joined the Cowardly Lion after leaving the forest.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The Hungry Tiger accompanies the Cowardly Lion and frightens the palace servants without harming anyone. He jokes with Nanda about eating her, explains that his appetite is too large for his body, and later helps pull the chariot.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The Tiger crouches on Dorothy’s other side and threatens the Nome warriors with fierce snarls and bared teeth. It later stands beside the Sawhorse when the restored people enter.

the Hungry Tiger in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908 · the work’s page · read it where the Hungry Tiger first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Hungry Tiger is a large, purple-striped tiger living in the Land of Oz, a faithful royal beast and friend of the Cowardly Lion and Dorothy. His conscience keeps him from devouring babies. He visits the Emerald City with the Cowardly Lion to welcome Dorothy, refuses to eat Jim, then draws Princess Ozma’s chariot and tackles Jim after he damages the Sawhorse.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

16. Jim, The Cab-Horse

The Hungry Tiger visits the Emerald City with the Cowardly Lion to welcome Dorothy. He says that Jim is dreadfully tough and that his conscience would not permit him to eat him, then calmly responds to Jim's threat to crush his skull. He jokes that if he could eat grass, nothing would tempt him to devour babies and lambs.

17. The Nine Tiny Piglets

The Hungry Tiger draws Princess Ozma’s chariot with the Cowardly Lion and wears a large blue bow. After Jim damages the Sawhorse, the Hungry Tiger tackles Jim and warns him through his threatening presence.

Hungry Tiger in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where Hungry Tiger first appears

Abstract written by a model

Hungry Tiger is a large, striped tiger with a conscience that rules him like a tyrant. Sent by Ozma and harnessed with the Cowardly Lion to her royal chariot, he meets Dorothy near the Emerald City, jokes about eating fat babies and Polychrome, but cannot act on such desires. He then greets Dorothy's companions and draws the chariot to the Royal Palace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

17. The Royal Chariot Arrives

The Hungry Tiger meets Dorothy near the Emerald City and jokes about wanting to eat fat babies, although his conscience prevents him from doing so. He warns Dorothy not to ruin his reputation and teasingly says that Polychrome would make a fine variegated breakfast.

18. The Emerald City

The Hungry Tiger greets Dorothy's companions and politely shakes paws with Toto. He joins the Cowardly Lion in drawing Ozma's royal chariot through the Emerald City to the Royal Palace.

Hungry Tiger is an enormous beast and one of Ozma's chief guardians, born in the jungle with the Cowardly Lion and continually hungry. He leaves the palace to prove power, longs to eat fat babies, but comforts and carries a lost baby, choosing hunger over cruelty.

the Hungry Tiger in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919 · the work’s page · read it where the Hungry Tiger first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Hungry Tiger is a monstrous, powerful but friendly champion who lives in the Emerald City with the Cowardly Lion and protects Dorothy and the Wizard of Oz. Always hungry for fat babies, he does not eat them because of his tender conscience. During the journey he carries the Wizard of Oz, a golden cage, and Dorothy and Trot. He eats a bee when the bees are the only food available, unaware that Trot and Cap'n Bill had become bees. After recognizing Kiki as responsible for transformations, he attacks him and is transformed into a Rabbit. He then stays with the transformed companions while they seek the Wizard's black bag and later bends cage bars while striking at the Glass Cat.

20. The Monkeys Have Trouble

The Hungry Tiger carries Dorothy and Trot during the journey. When the Glass Cat disturbs the sleeping monkeys, he strikes at her, misses, and bends two bars of the monkeys' cage.

Hungry Tiger in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Hungry Tiger first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Hungry Tiger is a wild yet educated, tame tiger of Oz who lives in the Emerald City and has a tender conscience. Although he has a frightful, persistent appetite and is continually hungry, he has never been known to devour a live person and refuses to eat people because of his conscience. Tempted by Ippty's offer of prisoners to eat, he goes to Rash to become its jailer, but Irashi imprisons him in the courtyard and expects him to eat criminals. Instead, he hides and protects prisoners, surviving on food thrown over the wall. After reuniting with Betsy Bobbin and meeting Carter Green, he protects them, Prince Evered, and others during their escape. He helps open their route, seeks food in Down Town, is captured after eating heavily at a restaurant, and continues traveling toward the Emerald City. He carries companions through the Lost Labyrinth and while fleeing the Gnome army, surviving a fall because of the Rash ruby. Though afraid of flying, he is seized by a Giant and taken to Immense City, where Princess Elma humiliates him by keeping him as a kitten, dressing him in doll clothes, and making him drink cream. With Reddy's help, he escapes and rejoins Betsy and Carter. Still eager to return to Oz, regular meals, and life as a tiger, he travels with Ozma and the group. He reluctantly returns to Rash, helps defeat its defenders, guards Irashi and Ippty, and receives a large meal. By the end, his heart-ache over missing his friends in Rash has cured even his desire to eat a live man, and he is relieved not to be a kitten.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

17 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The Hungry Tiger is described as an educated, tame animal living in the Emerald City of Oz with a tender conscience. According to Professor Wogglebug's Encyclopedia of Oz, it has never been known to devour a live man, fairy, or person.

CHAPTER 2

The Hungry Tiger eats three roast ducks and all the plum pudding but remains hungry, explaining that ordinary food fills him without satisfying him. After the Wizard suggests stuffing him, Ippty offers him a position in Rash where he can eat prisoners; tempted by the offer, the Hungry Tiger agrees to go for a few days and disappears with Ippty in the hurry-cane.

CHAPTER 3

The Hungry Tiger travels to Rash with Ippty and is presented as the future jailer of Rash. Irashi locks him in the prison courtyard, where he is expected to eat criminals, but he hides the sad singer instead and survives on food thrown over the wall.

CHAPTER 5

The Hungry Tiger is struck on the head by the vegetable cart and its vegetables, causing him to growl and charge toward Betsy. He recognizes Betsy as a friend from Oz, comforts her by licking away her tears, and frightens Carter when Carter realizes who he is.

CHAPTER 6

The Hungry Tiger explains that Irashi brought him to Rash to eat the prisoners, but he has refused to eat them and has hidden the Rash Singer instead. He protects Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, the barber, and Prince Evered, then enters the underground cavern with Evered and mysteriously disappears.

CHAPTER 7

The Hungry Tiger pushes back the pink block before the escape. He later comes sailing out of the tunnel with Prince Evered, and he decides not to investigate the nearby strange city until morning.

CHAPTER 8

The Hungry Tiger is extremely hungry after the group's journey and leads everyone toward Down Town in search of food. He charges through the city and into a restaurant, where he eats several roasts, three roast turkeys, potato salad, biscuits, and begins eating a ham before the Down Officers capture him in a net. He is taken with the others to face Dad.

CHAPTER 9

The Hungry Tiger explains that he ate most of the food listed on the bill and says he is traveling with Betsy Bobbin and the others to the Emerald City. He refuses to settle Down Town, searches for an exit, finds the subway to Up Town, and later falls through the entrance to Cave Inn.

CHAPTER 10

The Hungry Tiger helps pull Betsy from the underground pool and carries Betsy and Prince Evered through the Lost Labyrinth while Carter pushes his wheel-barrow. He repeatedly slips in the labyrinth, becomes exhausted, and then eagerly eats the meal Kaliko provides.

CHAPTER 11

The Hungry Tiger carries Betsy Bobbin, Carter Green, and Prince Evered while fleeing the Gnome army. He leaps over the Gnome army, races through a tunnel, and accidentally carries the travelers over a cliff and down the fire-fall, surviving because of the Rash ruby.

CHAPTER 12

The Hungry Tiger fears ants and especially dislikes the idea of flying. When the Giants appear, he runs away but is caught by a great hand and carried toward Immense City after the little girl Giant calls him a kitten.

CHAPTER 13

The Hungry Tiger is missing after Reddy is carried over the wall. Carter and Betsy hope to find the Hungry Tiger and Reddy inside Immense City.

CHAPTER 16

The Hungry Tiger is being kept in the Giants' castle as Princess Elma's kitten. Elma forces him to drink cream, dresses him in a doll coat and cap, and takes him for a ride in a doll coach. Reddy finds him exhausted and humiliated, removes the doll clothing, and agrees to escape with him.

CHAPTER 17

The Hungry Tiger is dressed in a doll's night dress and treated like a kitten by Princess Elma. He helps Reddy escape, warns him about wearing the wig, and is carried beneath Reddy's arm while they leave the Giant Castle. He returns to Betsy Bobbin and Carter Green, then complains about his difficult day as a kitten.

CHAPTER 18

The Hungry Tiger remains concerned about food, the direction of travel, and crossing the Deadly Desert. He is relieved to meet Ozma but still wants to return to Oz, where meals are regular and a tiger is a tiger. He carries Ozma as the group begins traveling east.

CHAPTER 19

The Hungry Tiger initially urges the group to avoid Rash, but then charges into the city and helps defeat its defenders. He guards Irashi and Ippty, is delighted by the large meal prepared for him, and later threatens to eat the two prisoners.

CHAPTER 20

The Hungry Tiger celebrates that Atmos Fere will keep his secret by taking Ippty and Irashi away. He is relieved not to be a kitten, and his heart-ache from missing his friends in Rash cures him of wanting to eat a live man.
