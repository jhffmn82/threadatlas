Pioneer Guards in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Pioneer Guards was a military company and organization, described as the oldest in the state and the finest in the West. Formed for Washington's birthday celebrations, it received Minie rifles and used Armory hall from 1859 until war began. Its annual ball was a leading social event; it soon disbanded, with members joining Union and Confederate forces.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The People's theater, an old frame building on the corner of Fourth and St.

The Pioneer Guards used Armory hall from its construction in 1859 until the outbreak of the war. Their annual ball was a leading social event, and the organization disbanded shortly after the war began; many members became Union army officers, while two or three joined the Confederate forces.
