A dark, strong cave far under the earth, previously occupied by Jim and Zeb, becomes the wanderers’ prison when their route ends there. A small roof crack admits a ray of sunshine, but the passage to the surface has no outlet. Dorothy and the others finally escape using the Magic Belt.

14. Ozma Uses the Magic Belt

The travelers reach a small cave with no further outlet and discover sunlight far above through a crack in its roof. The cave is a strong underground prison, and the blocked passage leaves the group with no way to escape on foot. Dorothy and the others leave it through the Magic Belt.
