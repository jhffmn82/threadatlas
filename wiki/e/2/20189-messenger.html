Abstract written by a model

A Persian courier and survivor returns from Salamis to the Palace of Susa. After arriving with uncertain news, he reports Persia’s defeat: the fleet’s destruction, commanders’ deaths, slaughter on the islet, and the land force’s disastrous retreat. Xerxes survives but flees after seeing the ruin.
