Abstract written by a model

The golden key is a large, somewhat old but bright and perfectly shaped key, found buried in sand near the water's edge after Billina strikes it. Its purpose is initially unknown. Dorothy later finds it on the shore and uses it to unlock the rock door, gaining access to Tiktok.
