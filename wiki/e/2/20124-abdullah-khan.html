Abdullah Khan in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Abdullah Khan is a fierce Sikh trooper, an old fighting-man who fought at Chilian-wallah, and one of three Sikh conspirators. He threatens the narrator into joining a plot to seize Achmet and divide the rajah’s jewels, escorts Achmet, helps carry his body into Agra fort and conceal the treasure, swears unity with the four conspirators, and later negotiates with Major Sholto and Captain Morstan.
