Abstract written by a model

Argive shrines, a sacred precinct near Argos’s gates, are dedicated to gods as suppliant sites; Danaids sit there, and Pelasgus protects them, sending Danaus to city altars.

Enter the KING OF ARGOS.

The Argive shrines are where the Danaids sit as suppliants beneath olive branches. Pelasgus refuses to let them be torn from the statues and sends Danaus to place the signs of suppliance at the city’s sacred altars.
