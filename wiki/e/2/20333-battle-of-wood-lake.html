Abstract written by a model

Battle of Wood Lake, the Indian war's only real battle, occurred at Wood Lake on September 23. About 800 of Gen. Sibley's men fought roughly 800 Sioux; Col. Marshall's 400-man charge routed them, with four whites killed and forty or fifty wounded.
