Abstract written by a model

The hall of the house of representatives hosted Adalina Patti's 1857 concert, Washington's Birthday exercises, a mass meeting supporting the government after the attack on Sumter, and the first Republican state convention after Minnesota entered the Union. The hall was also the scene of speeches, resolutions, and political nominations.
