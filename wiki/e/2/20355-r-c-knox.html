FIREMEN AND FIRES OF PIONEER DAYS.

R.C. Knox made the first attempt to organize a St. Paul fire brigade in the fall of 1851. He raised money by subscription to purchase several ladders and was said to be able to tire out a dozen ordinary men at a fire.
