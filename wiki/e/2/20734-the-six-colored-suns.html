2. The Glass City

The six colored suns hang far below the falling travelers, with one large white sun surrounded by rose, violet, yellow, blue, and orange suns. Their rays illuminate the inner world and cast changing rainbow colors over the travelers and the Glass City. The suns produce bright light but no heat.
