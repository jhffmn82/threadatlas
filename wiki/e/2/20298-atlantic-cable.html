THE FIVE MILLION LOAN ELECTION.

The Atlantic cable was successfully laid in 1858, allowing messages to be exchanged between Queen Victoria and President Buchanan. It operated successfully for about four weeks before the last message was exchanged on the day of St. Paul's celebration.
