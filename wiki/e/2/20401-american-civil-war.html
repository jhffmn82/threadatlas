Civil War in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

The Civil War began in 1861 and became a desperate conflict lasting more than four years. It placed severe demands on the United States government, contributing in 1862 to delayed Sioux annuity payments and limited readiness for the Sioux outbreak. St. Paul mobilized through patriotic meetings, enlistment appeals, and support for soldiers’ dependents; the Pioneer Guards disbanded, with many members becoming Union officers and two or three joining Confederate forces. Many printers and editors enlisted or served as officers in infantry, cavalry, artillery, sharpshooter, and other units, straining the Press’s staff as it supported vigorous prosecution of the war and faced hostility to the draft. By Lincoln’s assassination, Richmond had fallen, Lee had surrendered, and Johnston’s probable surrender appeared likely to end the war.

American Civil War in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

The American Civil War was an American civil conflict and a great crisis and struggle in which Minnesota volunteers served. It included early Union success at the Battle of Mill Springs and the exceptionally severe Battle of Pittsburg Landing, with consequences for Union commanders and operations. On July 4, 1863, the battle of Gettysburg and the surrender of Vicksburg occurred. Later, fighting continued before Atlanta and in the march from Atlanta to the sea; enlistment also reduced volunteer fire departments, while daily newspapers expanded during the latter part of the war.

the Civil War in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

The Civil War, anticipated by the Halls amid heated Boston meetings and fears of friends lost in battle, depleted the U.S. Naval Observatory staff, brought battles and wounded soldiers to Washington, and exposed Hall’s family to disease, danger, and hardship. It ended with army reviews through the city.
