Agra in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Agra is a city and place of refuge for Europeans fleeing disturbances around Muttra, though it is filled with fanatics and fierce groups as British defenders concentrate in the old fort. It is also where Achmet is murdered, treasure is hidden and found by Major Sholto, and conspirators are tried.
