TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

Earle S. Goodrich was editor-in-chief of the Pioneer and was also a printer. When the old Press moved into a new building in 1869, he set the first type there, composing about half a column of editorial without copy; printers later found an error in its first line.
