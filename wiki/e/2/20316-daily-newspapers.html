Pioneer Press · 1862~

St. Paul daily newspapers began as small-staff operations using old hand presses, competing to print speeches, messages, telegraphic reports, and breaking news first. After the Winslow line reached St. Paul, they used telegraph news. They often paused around holidays before expanding late in the Civil War with the city’s population.
