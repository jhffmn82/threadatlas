the four treasure conspirators

group · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: all four, the four, the four conspirators, the four of us, the sign of the four, those who had most right to them

Abstract written by a model

The four treasure conspirators are Small, Mahomet Singh, Abdullah Khan, and Dost Akbar. They jointly possess and claim entitlement to the Agra treasure, represented by their shared sign. They murder and bury Achmet, conceal his jewels in the Agra fort, swear to act together, and are arrested and sentenced for murder. Later they offer the treasure’s location for freedom; their sign is left on Major Sholto and in the chemical laboratory to assert their claim.
