A threatened war between the Argive people and Aegyptus’ sons arises over the Danaids. Pelasgus fears both conflict if Argos protects them and divine pollution if it surrenders them. The Herald invokes Ares rather than testimony, and the approaching fleet threatens corpses and many deaths.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Enter the KING OF ARGOS.

Pelasgus fears that protecting the Danaids will force Argos into a mighty strife with the sons of Aegyptus. He also fears that surrendering the Danaids would bring divine anger and the pollution of their deaths upon the land.

Re-enter DANAUS.

The threatened war arises when the Herald declares that the dispute will be settled by Ares rather than testimony, with corpses and many deaths possible. The Chorus fears the bloodshed that may follow the pursuing fleet’s arrival.
