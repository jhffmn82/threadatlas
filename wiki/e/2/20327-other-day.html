THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

Other Day, an educated Dakota Indian who had adopted Christianity and civilized habits, guided sixty-two men, women, and children to safety through Kandiyohi, Hutchinson, Glencoe, and Carver. He later spoke in St. Paul and attributed their escape to the mercy of the Great Spirit.
