the unnamed narrator

Abstract written by a model

The unnamed narrator is a Worcestershire man, born near Pershore and born into the Smalls family. A youthful rover, he joins the 3rd Buffs after trouble over a girl, serves in India, and loses his right leg above the knee to a crocodile in the Ganges. Invalided out, he becomes Abel White’s plantation overseer, survives the Mutiny, and joins Agra’s volunteer corps. While commanding a postern gate, he agrees to rob and kill Achmet for treasure, but ultimately sends Achmet to the main guard.

“I am a Worcestershire man myself,—born near Pershore. I dare say you

The narrator says he was born near Pershore in Worcestershire and joined the 3rd Buffs after getting into trouble over a girl. A crocodile took his right leg in the Ganges, after which he became an indigo-plantation overseer, survived the Mutiny, and joined the volunteer corps at Agra. He initially agreed to help Mahomet Singh and Abdullah Khan rob and kill Achmet, but ultimately sent Achmet to the main guard.
