Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE SEVEN AGAINST THEBES

The Chorus of Cadmean maidens cries out in terror as the Argive army approaches and prays to the gods for protection. Eteocles condemns their panic, but they continue to describe the dangers of defeat, including slaughter, enslavement, and forced marriage.
