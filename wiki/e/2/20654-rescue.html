7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

Eureka's warning prompts Dorothy, the Wizard, and Zeb to prepare a rescue of Jim and the piglets. They reach the Black Pit and try to free the prisoners, but the rescuers are trapped inside and must escape through the mountain instead.
