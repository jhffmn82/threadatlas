the enchanted picture in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The enchanted picture hangs in Princess Ozma's room in the royal palace of Oz. When a user wishes or asks to see someone, it shows the exact desired scene, including Dorothy, trapped travelers, the Kansas farm, and Hugson's Ranch. Dorothy relies on it to help Ozma learn when aid is needed and later sees her relatives' return.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

14. Ozma Uses the Magic Belt

The enchanted picture hangs in Ozma's room and shows the exact scene where any of her friends are when Ozma asks about them. Dorothy depends on it to let Ozma see the trapped travelers and determine when help is needed.

20. Zeb Returns to the Ranch

The enchanted picture shows the scene that a person wishes to behold. Dorothy uses it to see that Uncle Henry and Aunt Em have returned to the farm in Kansas, and Zeb sees Hugson's Ranch.
