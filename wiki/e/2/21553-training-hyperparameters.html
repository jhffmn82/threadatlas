Training hyperparameters are table settings for DPR, LI, and LI-RAGE, tuned on validation performance. They cover learning rates, optimizers, batch sizes, training duration, warmup, decay, negatives, and GPU counts, with LI-RAGE values separated for NQ-TABLES and E2E-WTQ.

Dataset Statistics

The hyperparameters specify training settings for DPR, LI, and LI-RAGE, including learning rates, optimizers, batch sizes, gradient accumulation, training steps or epochs, warmup, decay, weight decay, label smoothing, negative samples, and GPU counts. LI-RAGE has separate values for NQ-TABLES and E2E-WTQ.
