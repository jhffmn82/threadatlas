Abstract written by a model

The rope enables Jonathan Small to climb and descend through a window, leaving blood marks after he slips and injures his hand. Later, wound around Tonga’s waist and secured for access through a laboratory roof, it helps the narrator climb, lowers the treasure-box, and is pulled up as Tonga escapes.
