Valley of Voe

place · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Voe, the Valley, the Valley of Voe, the magical Valley of Voe

Abstract written by a model

The Valley of Voe is a beautiful, well-watered valley below the first landing, shaped like the hollow of a great cup. It has lovely lawns and gardens, orchards bearing unknown fruits, crystal brooks, trees, cottages, a broad river, plentiful dama-fruit, and clear unseen light. At first it seems uninhabited, but it contains invisible people, bears, and birds, while fish are visible. Its greatest danger is the bears, which may eat the people. The travelers cross the valley toward Pyramid Mountain, climbing it as their only escape.

Valley of Voe is in one work on this site, Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

8. The Valley of Voices

The Valley of Voe is a beautiful, well-watered valley with lawns, gardens, orchards, brooks, trees, cottages, and unseen illumination. Its inhabitants and bears are invisible, and its main danger is that the bears may eat its people.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

The Valley of Voe contains unseen people, invisible bears and birds, visible fish, cottages, orchards, flowers, and streams. The travelers cross much of it while trying to reach Pyramid Mountain and avoid the bears.

10. The Braided Man of Pyramid Mountain

The Valley of Voe lies below the first landing, where its cottages look like toy houses from above. The travelers are climbing Pyramid Mountain as the only way to escape the valley.
