Nome King's palace in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Nome King's palace is a magnificent yet dreadful, creepy underground palace beneath the great mountain at the north end of Ev, reached through rock doors and a jewel-lit passage into a cavern. Though beautiful, it is dangerous: its rooms hold thousands of colored ornaments and bric-a-brac, including the transformed Queen of Ev, her children, and other royal family members. Visitors who fail to correctly guess an object's enchantment are themselves turned into ornaments. The palace is the destination of Ozma's dangerous expedition. Dorothy searches its rooms, restores Evring, and leaves through its massive rock doors. It then receives the Scarecrow and awaits Billina, while a later search for the transformed Tin Woodman among its many rooms and ornaments fails before the group departs.

the Nome King's underground palace in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The Nome King's underground palace is a magnificent cave palace, among fairyland's most splendid and beautiful places. Its numerous richly furnished rooms hold marble, carpets, draperies, carved furniture, the Nome King's throne, and ornaments of many kinds. Most importantly, purple, green, and gold ornaments conceal transformed captives, including Evoldo's wife and ten children and people of Oz. Ozma and the Tin Woodman enter through a cave-wall opening; later Billina restores the royal family of Ev, Ozma, and their followers as doors open and the Nome King's warriors gather inside.

12. The Eleven Guesses

The palace is a splendid underground structure filled with rooms, marble, carpets, draperies, carved furniture, and many ornaments. The transformed members of the royal family of Ev are hidden among its ornaments, and Ozma and then the Tin Woodman enter it through an opening in the cave wall.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The underground palace is described as one of the most splendid and beautiful places in fairyland, with rich velvet carpets and magnificent rooms. Its purple, green, and gold ornaments conceal many of the Nome King’s transformed captives.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The Nome King’s palace contains the enchanted ornaments and the rooms where Billina restores the royal family of Ev, Ozma, and their followers. Its doors open to reveal the restored people, while the Nome King’s warriors gather inside.

the Nome King's cavern in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

An underground cavern beneath earth in the Nome country, hidden from the Rainbow by concealed entrances. It contains the jeweled throne-room and Ruggedo's throne, where Tik-Tok challenges Ruggedo and later Betsy Bobbin, Hank, and General Guph arrive.

royal cavern (Nome King's cavern) in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Ruggedo's jeweled underground royal cavern in the Nome Kingdom contains his throne and serves as the setting for prisoners, executions, royal entertainment, confrontations, and magic against captives. He flees it from pursuing eggs, then later returns cautiously as the party reunites and receives Kaliko's refreshments.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seventeen

The cavern contains Ruggedo's throne, the prisoners, Tik-Tok, and the instruments prepared by the executioners. Ruggedo attempts to carry out his punishments there, while Polychrome repeatedly enters and leaves it.

Chapter Eighteen

Ruggedo's jeweled cavern is where he holds his royal entertainment, confronts Quox, and attempts to use magic against his captives. Ruggedo is forced to flee it because of the pursuing eggs.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The royal cavern is where the party reunites and receives refreshments from Kaliko. Ruggedo enters it cautiously and later joins the others there after Polychrome leaves.
