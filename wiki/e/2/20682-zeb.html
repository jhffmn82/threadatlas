Zeb in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

Zeb is practical, curious, and strong for his years because of farm work. Narrative, section by section

17 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Earthquake

Zebediah waits five hours at Hugson's Siding to take Dorothy to Hugson's Ranch. He drives the buggy and is frightened when the earthquake opens cracks beneath them; he clings to the buggy seat as they fall into a pit.

2. The Glass City

Zeb falls with Dorothy, Jim, and the buggy and remains frightened by the strange events. He cautiously learns to walk on the air and helps Dorothy and the animals reach the street. The man with the star questions him about the Rain of Stones.

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

Zeb drives the horse and buggy through the doorway of the glass palace and enters the hall with Dorothy. He follows Dorothy outside to watch the descending balloon and later into the hall to see Oz meet Gwig. He has lost his hat during the flight through the air.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

Zeb travels through the gardens with Dorothy and the others and asks questions about Mangaboo growth and eating. He becomes hungry and notices strawberries and melons that might provide food.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

Zeb explores the vegetable gardens with Dorothy and the Wizard and eats their fruits. He learns that the Wizard's piglets are real and can talk, then returns to the House of the Sorcerer and sleeps on a glass bench.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

Zeb fetches the Wizard's satchel, two lanterns, and a can of kerosene oil from the balloon's basket-car. He is spared from the Mangaboo destruction decree along with the Wizard and Dorothy.

7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

Zeb joins Dorothy and the Wizard in going to the rescue and carries the lanterns and oil can. After being wounded by the Mangaboos' thorns, he drives the buggy through the tunnel while the others hold lanterns.

8. The Valley of Voices

Zeb enters the valley with the other travelers and admires its surroundings. He helps question the invisible people after the piglets disappear and is startled when an unseen voice comes near him.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

Zeb says he might fight if necessary and carries a jointed sword and other useful things in his satchel. He applies the water-walking plant to his feet, Jim's hoofs, and the buggy's tires, then drives the buggy toward Pyramid Mountain.

10. The Braided Man of Pyramid Mountain

Zeb questions whether the stairs will become steeper and watches that nothing happens to the harness. He looks out at the Cloud Fairies and the enormous birds from the cavern.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

Zeb helps the Wizard and Jim move the buggy up the narrow stairs and unhooks Jim when the group reaches the wooden country. During the Gargoyles' second attack, he captures a crowned Gargoyle, binds its wings and arms with a strap, and puts it in the buggy.

12. A Wonderful Escape

Zeb uses the bound Gargoyle king as a club and knocks down dozens of Gargoyles before being restrained. He climbs down from the prison house, steals eight Gargoyle wings with Eureka's guidance, helps attach them to Jim and the buggy, and pilots the escape toward the rock.

13. The Den of the Dragonettes

Zeb helps the Wizard remove and burn the wooden wings and doors. He also helps take apart and move the buggy through the narrow passageways, pushes it over rough ground, and questions the dragonettes.

14. Ozma Uses the Magic Belt

Zeb escapes the moving rock with Dorothy, the Wizard, and the others, but the group later becomes trapped in a cave. He asks about Ozma's magic and is astonished by the promise of transportation to Oz. He arrives with the Wizard and Jim in the Emerald City.

15. Old Friends are Reunited

Zeb arrives at the Emerald City with Oz and is given a splendid room, fine clothing, and an invitation to dine with Princess Ozma and Dorothy. He is initially shy and overwhelmed by the palace, but he soon becomes comfortable with Dorothy's friends.

17. The Nine Tiny Piglets

Zeb drives Jim in the procession and later wrestles with a strong Munchkin, who defeats him three times. He organizes the race between Jim and the Sawhorse and warns Jim to control his temper after the kick.

20. Zeb Returns to the Ranch

Zeb wants to leave the Land of Oz because he and Jim do not seem to fit into a fairyland. Ozma sends him and Jim home, and Zeb arrives at Hugson's Ranch, where he explains that he has been in the world.
