THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

R.C. Munger was Dan Emmet's friend in St. Paul. Munger encouraged Emmet to publish "Dixie," financially assisted in placing it before the public, and received one of the first printed copies. The composition was first sung in the West in the music store of Munger Bros. in the old concert hall building on Third street.
