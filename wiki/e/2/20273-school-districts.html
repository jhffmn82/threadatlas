School districts generally offered reading, writing, and arithmetic as principal studies for up to six months yearly when able; older children missed summer school to cultivate soil, and four districts joined a grand spelling contest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SPELLING DOWN A SCHOOL.

The school districts generally offered reading, writing, and arithmetic, with school terms often lasting only six months a year. Older children could not attend during the summer because they were needed for cultivating the soil, and four districts participated in the grand spelling contest.
