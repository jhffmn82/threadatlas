blood vengeance

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: avenging my father, blood, blood and doom, murder and revenge, murder responsive to murder, requite evil for evil, the father's sufferings, the law, wage of wrong
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, murder and revenge govern the history of the family, with Orestes killing Clytemnestra in revenge for Agamemnon and the Chorus questioning the justice of the deed while recognizing the continuing chain of violence.
  • In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, blood vengeance drives Clytemnestra’s killing of Agamemnon and Aegisthus’s revenge against the house of Atreus, with each act of violence summoning another.
