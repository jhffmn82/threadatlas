protection

topic · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: aid, betrayed strangers, protection of the suppliants, refuge, relief, shelter of thy purity, succour, suppliance, suppliant branches, suppliant hands, suppliant protection, suppliants sitting at the shrines of the Gods, supplication and asylum

Abstract written by a model

In Four Plays of Aeschylus, protection is symbolized by the olive-shoot and sought from Zeus, the gods of Argos, and the rulers of the land. In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, protection shelters the children of Hercules at the altar and sanctuary while creating a moral and political obligation for Athens. In Hecuba and other plays, protection of the suppliants is provided by Demophoon, who defends the children of Hercules from Eurystheus.
