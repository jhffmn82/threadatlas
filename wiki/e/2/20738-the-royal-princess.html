Abstract written by a model

The Royal Princess is a beautiful vegetable lady who grows on the Royal Bush of the Mangaboos and is destined to succeed the Prince. Though initially called not fully ripe, she is picked by Dorothy and the Wizard, comes to life, receives the Star of Royalty, and becomes ruler and leader of Mangaboo. She walks in the air above her subjects, advises with her advisors, and first orders the visitors destroyed. After the Wizard’s fire test scorches her advisors, she recognizes his power and spares the humans.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The Royal Princess grows on the Royal Bush of the Mangaboos and is described as beautifully formed and dressed. She is destined to succeed the Prince, although he claims she is not quite ripe and need not yet be picked.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

The Royal Princess is picked by Dorothy and the Wizard and immediately comes to life. She accuses the Prince of leaving her unpicked to preserve his unlawful rule, receives the Star of Royalty, and is escorted to the glass city as the new ruler.

6. The Mangaboos Prove Dangerous

The Princess declares that the Wizard, Zeb, and Dorothy must be destroyed and that their animals must be sent to the Black Pit. After seeing the Wizard's fire test scorch her advisors, she recognizes him as greater than any Sorcerer the Mangaboos have known and spares the three humans.
