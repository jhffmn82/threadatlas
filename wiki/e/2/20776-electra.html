Electra of Euripides in The Electra of Euripides

Euripides · -0419/-0409

An ancient tragedy, dated 413 B.C., based on the legend of Orestes and Electra, examines the psychological and moral problems of their deliberate murder of their mother in a realistic, intellectual, rebellious form.

Introduction[1]

The Electra of Euripides is described as a close-knit, powerful, well-constructed, realistic, intellectual, and rebellious tragedy. It examines the psychological and moral problems involved in Orestes and Electra's deliberate murder of their mother.
