Abstract written by a model

In The Rhesus of Euripides, Electra is listed as a separate play translated into English rhyming verse with explanatory notes and offered in paper covers and crown octavo cloth. In The Electra of Euripides, Electra is an ancient tragedy dated 413 B.C. that examines the psychological and moral problems of Orestes and Electra’s deliberate murder of their mother in a realistic, intellectual, rebellious form.
