Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Suppliant Maidens

The flight from Egypt carries Danaus and his daughters across the sea to Argos. Danaus chose flight as the lesser of two griefs, and the daughters pray that the voyage will end in safety and freedom from forced embraces.

Enter the KING OF ARGOS.

The Danaids have fled across the sea from the sons of Aegyptus because they abhor the marriages being imposed upon them. They ask Argos to protect them rather than allow them to be dragged away.
