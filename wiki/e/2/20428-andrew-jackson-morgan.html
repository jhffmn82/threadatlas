Abstract written by a model

Andrew Jackson Morgan was a printer-politician brought to St. Paul by Col. D.A. Robertson, who made him foreman of the Democrat; he later edited it. He claimed to resemble Stephen A. Douglas and carried a silver dollar reportedly given by Andrew Jackson at his christening. He died in Ohio.

TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

Andrew Jackson Morgan was brought to St. Paul by Col. D.A. Robertson and made foreman of the Democrat. He was a printer-politician and editor who claimed a resemblance to Stephen A. Douglas, carried a silver dollar said to have been given to him by Andrew Jackson, and died in Ohio more than forty years before the account was written.
