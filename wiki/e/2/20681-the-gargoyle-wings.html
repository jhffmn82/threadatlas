the Gargoyle wings in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Wooden Gargoyle wings detach by unhooking hinges; in the underground country their magic provides flight. After Gargoyles leave them while sleeping, Zeb steals eight, and four are fastened to each side of the buggy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

12. A Wonderful Escape

The Gargoyles unhook their wings and place them in a corner while they sleep, and the wings themselves appear to contain the power of flight. Zeb steals eight wings, which the Wizard and the others fasten to Jim's harness and the buggy.
