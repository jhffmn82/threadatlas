  • In Ozma of Oz, children’s letters and requests inspire L. Frank Baum to write the book and ask for more about Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, and Ozma; they also suggest Dorothy return to Oz and meet Ozma.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, readers tell L. Frank Baum what to do with Dorothy, Aunt Em, and Uncle Henry, suggest future subjects, and are described as knowing what they want while he tries to please them.
  • In Sky Island, readers write to L. Frank Baum about how they like the book, after approving The Sea Fairies and saying they liked Trot almost as well as Dorothy.
  • In The Magic of Oz, readers send L. Frank Baum good letters; he says he loves them and hopes his books continue to give them pleasure.
