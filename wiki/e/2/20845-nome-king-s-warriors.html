Nome King's warriors in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Nome King’s warriors are his mail-clad palace army, armored in gem-inlaid polished steel and armed with bronze spears, swords, and poised battle-axes. They first stand in orderly, motionless ranks awaiting command, then enter the throne room to seize Dorothy. After resistance and terror at Billina’s eggs, they pursue the escaping party until Dorothy turns the foremost into eggs, and the rest flee.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The Nome King’s mail-clad warriors fill the domed cavern in orderly ranks. They stand motionless with battle-axes poised, awaiting the King’s command.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The Nome warriors enter the throne room after the Nome King summons them and prepare to seize Dorothy. They are briefly knocked down by Ozma’s private soldier, but flee in terror when Billina’s eggs strike the Nome King.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

A large number of the Nome King's warriors chase the escaping party with swords, spears, and axes. Dorothy uses the magic belt to turn the foremost warriors into eggs, causing the rest to flee.
