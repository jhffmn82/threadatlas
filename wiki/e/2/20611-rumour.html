Rumour swiftly traverses the city, roused by a joyful flame, repeatedly spreading false reports of Agamemnon's death before tales may prove true, deceive, or die.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And others, far beneath the Ilian wall,

Rumour has moved swiftly throughout the city, prompted by the joyful beacon-flame. Its reports may be true or may be deceptive, and lightly born tales may also quickly die.
