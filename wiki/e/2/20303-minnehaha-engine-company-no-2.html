Abstract written by a model

Minnehaha Engine Company No. 2, organized in winter 1857-1858, was a prosperous engine company with Grant and O'Connor among its officers. Its engine house near Third and Jackson streets served members as a lounging and reading room, and it participated in the 1859 parade.

Minnehaha Engine Company No. 2 was organized in the winter of 1857-1858 and received an engine house near Third and Jackson streets. It was described as prosperous, and its engine house served as a lounging and reading room; the company participated in the 1859 parade.
