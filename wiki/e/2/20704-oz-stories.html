Abstract written by a model

In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, Oz stories continue at children’s insistence, with Dorothy a beloved fixture; this installment includes Princess Ozma and revisits the Wizard and familiar characters. In The Emerald City of Oz, Oz stories are six books shaped partly by children’s ideas about Dorothy, Aunt Em, and Uncle Henry, offering useful, amusing lessons through quaint people and strange adventures, and ending with their story’s conclusion. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Oz stories are described as six books about the adventures of the people of Oz, loved by children, followed after two years of waiting by another Oz story.

Author's Note
