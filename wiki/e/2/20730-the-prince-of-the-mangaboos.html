the Prince of the Mangaboos

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Lord, Prince, Prince of Mangaboo, man, man with the star, my Prince, my son, the Prince, the man with the star, the one with the star

Abstract written by a model

The Prince of the Mangaboos is a long-lived, unlawful former ruler and person of authority in the Glass City, marked by a glistening Star of Royalty in his dark hair. He rules the Mangaboos while postponing the Royal Princess’s picking, selfishly delaying her succession because he does not want to resign. When Dorothy, Zeb, and Oz arrive after the Rain of Stones, he questions them, holds Dorothy responsible unless innocence is proved, and leads Dorothy to the House of the Sorcerer. He arranges a contest between Oz and Gwig for Chief Wizard, threatening Oz if he fails; after Oz wins, the Prince explains the Mangaboos’ vegetable nature and leads the travelers through the gardens. Although he proposes destroying the strangers for being inside the earth, his rule ends when Dorothy picks the Princess. Trembling, he surrenders the Star of Royalty to her and walks away alone, with his later fate unknown.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2. The Glass City

The man with the star appears to be an authority among the people of the Glass City. He questions Zeb and Dorothy about the Rain of Stones, declares that they will be held responsible unless they prove their innocence, and leads them toward the House of the Sorcerer.

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

The Prince questions Dorothy, Zeb, and Oz about their arrival and challenges Gwig's inaccurate predictions. He offers to make Oz the Chief Wizard if Oz proves superior to Gwig, while threatening to stop Oz from living if he fails. After Oz defeats Gwig, the Prince explains that the Mangaboos are vegetable beings and invites Oz to the public gardens.

4. The Vegetable Kingdom

The Prince leads the travelers through the gardens and explains the Mangaboos' vegetable way of growing and living. He says the Royal Princess will succeed him but delays picking her because he does not want to give up power, and he plans to destroy the strangers.

5. Dorothy Picks the Princess

The Prince returns with his people and trembles when he sees that the Royal Princess has been picked. He gives her the Star of Royalty, after which he walks away alone; his later fate is unknown.
