Roman Catholic Church in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

enfeebles Culdees [as its power advances] The Romish Church regarded the Culdees as heretical because they departed from its established rules in matters including marriage and poverty. As its power advanced, the Culdees were weakened and their communities were eventually suppressed.
