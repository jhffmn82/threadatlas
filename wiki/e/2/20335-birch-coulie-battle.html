THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

The Birch Coulie battle began around 4 a.m. when an estimated 500 or 600 armed and mostly mounted Sioux opened fire on Maj. Brown’s command. The defenders held out for two days, but about half of their force was killed or wounded before relief arrived.
