Prometheus's aid to mortals

event · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: all arts, for mortals’ use, Prometheus gave, boons, service wrought to them

Abstract written by a model

Prometheus aids mortals by teaching arts: understanding stars, numbers, writing, memory, song, animal harnessing, chariots, ships, medicine, prophecy, omens, sacrifice, construction, and metals. The aid proceeds through these gifts and ends with metals stored in the earth, presented as a complete body of arts for mortal use.

[_Exit OCEANUS._]

Prometheus aided mortals by teaching them how to understand the stars, numbers, writing, memory, song, animal harnessing, chariots, ships, medicine, prophecy, omens, sacrifice, and the metals stored in the earth. He presents these gifts as the complete body of arts given for mortal use.
