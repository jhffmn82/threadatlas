THE FIVE MILLION LOAN ELECTION.

The St. Paul procession honoring the Atlantic cable included cavalry, bands, military companies, a city battery, civic and state officials, religious and fraternal organizations, tradespeople, floats, and citizens. It marched for three and one-half hours along a route through many city streets before reaching its destination.
