St. Paul procession in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

The St. Paul procession honoring the Atlantic cable, nearly two miles long and led by marshal-in-chief A.C. Jones, marched for three and one-half hours with diverse civic, military, religious, trade, and citizen participants to its destination.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FIVE MILLION LOAN ELECTION.

The St. Paul procession honoring the Atlantic cable included cavalry, bands, military companies, a city battery, civic and state officials, religious and fraternal organizations, tradespeople, floats, and citizens. It marched for three and one-half hours along a route through many city streets before reaching its destination.
