the invisibility caused by dama-fruit

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: invis'ble, invisibility, invisible

Abstract written by a model

The invisibility caused by dama-fruit makes a person or animal unseen by human or animal eyes, though still audible and touchable. In the Valley of Voe, people use it to avoid bears that cannot see invisible prey. It also stops people from seeing one another, birds, and bears, so Dorothy and her companions refuse it to remain visible and return home.

8. The Valley of Voices

The invisibility caused by dama-fruit prevents a person or animal from being seen, although the invisible creature can still be heard and touched. The people of Voe use it to avoid being seen and eaten by the bears.

9. They Fight the Invisible Bears

Invisibility is valued by the people of Voe because it protects them from the bears, though it prevents them from seeing one another and the birds and bears. Dorothy and her companions resist becoming invisible because they want to remain visible and return home.
