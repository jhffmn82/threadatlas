the anonymous letter

Also called: Your unknown friend, a letter, letter, the letter, this letter

Abstract written by a model

The anonymous letter, signed without a name, summons Captain Morstan’s daughter Mary to the third pillar outside the Lyceum Theatre at seven o’clock. It promises justice for her as a wronged woman, forbids police, permits two friends, and is linked by Holmes to presents received after Major Sholto’s death.

Chapter II

The anonymous letter tells Mary to be at the third pillar outside the Lyceum Theatre at seven o’clock and promises justice to a wronged woman. It warns her not to bring the police and says that she may bring two friends if she is distrustful.

Chapter III

The letter sent to Captain Morstan’s daughter describes her as a wronged woman and promises justice. Holmes connects it with the presents she has received since shortly after Major Sholto’s death, although the reason for its timing remains uncertain.
