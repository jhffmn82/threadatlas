Abstract written by a model

Pondicherry Lodge is Major John Sholto’s large, square home in Upper Norwood, where he lived luxuriously with his sons and native servants and where Captain Morstan died. Enclosed by desolate grounds, a high glass-topped wall, and a single guarded entrance, it is gloomy, silent, and marked by treasure-seekers’ excavations. Its sealed garret above the highest room held hidden treasure. After Bartholomew discovers it, he dies in a locked room; police, investigators, Sherlock Holmes, and Dr. John Watson examine the house, garret, roof, and grounds, beginning a pursuit. It remains guarded afterward, while later news reports continued treasure searches and eventually its discovery.

Pondicherry Lodge is in one work on this site, The Sign of the Four. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

Pondicherry Lodge was Major John Sholto’s home in Upper Norwood, where he lived in luxury with his sons and native servants. Captain Morstan died there, and Bartholomew later discovered the hidden treasure in a sealed garret above the highest room.

Chapter V

Pondicherry Lodge stands within grounds surrounded by a high stone wall topped with broken glass, with a single guarded entrance. The house is huge, gloomy, and silent, and its grounds are covered with heaps and excavations made by treasure-seekers.

Chapter VI

The house contains the locked room where Bartholomew Sholto died, an inaccessible window, a garret, and a roof trap-door. Police and investigators enter it during the murder investigation.

Chapter VII

Pondicherry Lodge remains guarded by constables after the murder. Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson return there, examine the garret and roof, and begin the pursuit from the grounds.
