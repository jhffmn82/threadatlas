Io’s wandering

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Io's wandering, long wanderings, pilgrimage, scared circuitous wandering, wanderer through the whole earth, wandering, wanderings, weary wandering
  • In Four Plays of Aeschylus, Io’s wandering begins when Inachus expels her under the command of the oracle and Zeus’s threat, and Prometheus maps her future route through Scythia, the Caucasus, the lands of the Amazons, Asia, the Gorgon-haunted regions, and finally Egypt.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Io’s wandering, driven by Juno’s Fury and invisible stings through the whole earth, ends at the Nile in Egypt.
