Abstract written by a model

Queen of Ev is the queen mother of Evoldo's family and mother of the royal children of Ev. Sold by the King of Ev to the Nome King with her ten children, she is imprisoned underground as a royal prisoner and slave. The Nome King transforms her into an ornament or bric-a-brac, specifically a purple footstool. Billina restores her, and she gratefully embraces her nine recovered children while helping identify the grasshopper sought for Ozma. Welcomed back to the land of the living, she leaves the Nome King's palace with her children, rides the Tiger toward Ev, and worries for their safety near the giant man of iron. Back at the royal palace, she permits Princess Langwidere to retain her rooms and cabinet of heads for life and proclaims her eldest son King Evardo Fifteenth.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

The queen of Ev, wife of Evoldo, was sold to the Nome King along with her ten children. She is imprisoned underground and has been transformed into an ornament.

The Queen of Ev was sold by the King of Ev to the Nome King and is held as one of his slaves. The Nome King says that he transformed her into an article of ornament or bric-a-brac in his palace.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold
