Abstract written by a model

The Doloneia is an Epic rhapsody, now the Tenth Book of the Iliad, though it may once have circulated independently. It directly supplies the story and narrative framework of Rhesus: turned into drama, relocated to the Trojan camp, with a more tumultuous Greek camp and other variations.

INTRODUCTION

Doloneia is identified as the direct source of the story of Rhesus. The introduction describes it as an Epic rhapsody that now forms Book Ten of the Iliad and suggests that it may have circulated independently in the time of Euripides.

NOTES

The Doloneia, or Tenth Book of the Iliad, provides the immediate narrative framework for the Rhesus play. The play is described as the Doloneia turned into drama and relocated to the Trojan camp, though it includes a more tumultuous Greek camp and other variations.
