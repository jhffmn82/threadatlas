Rice house in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Rice house was a three-story hotel on Third street near Market, built by Henry M. Rice and commonly considered St. Paul’s first brick building. A principal hotel, it hosted Minnesota’s territorial legislature and the territory’s supreme court before burning to the ground in early winter 1856.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The Rice house stood on Third street near Market and was commonly considered the first brick building erected in St. Paul. Built by Henry M. Rice, the three-story hotel burned to the ground in early winter 1856 after having served as a principal hotel and a meeting place for the territorial legislature and supreme court.
