Thracian Charioteer

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: A THRACIAN, CHA., CHARIOTEER OF RHESUS, O charioteer of Thracia’s wretched king, Rhesus’s charioteer, THE CHARIOTEER OF RHESUS, THRACIAN, Thracian charioteer, charioteer, good charioteer, the charioteer of Rhesus, wounded man
  • In Hecuba and other plays, the charioteer of Rhesus wakes to the sound of dying men, sees Rhesus bleeding, is wounded while trying to find weapons, survives, accuses Hector of murdering the Thracians for their horses, and is taken to Hector’s house for treatment.
  • In The Rhesus of Euripides, the Thracian charioteer is deeply wounded in the attack on Rhesus and the sleeping Thracian army, reaches the Trojan guards, accuses Hector, and is ordered carried home for care.

ULYSSES, DIOMEDE.

Rhesus’s charioteer wakes to the sound of dying men, sees Rhesus bleeding, and is wounded while trying to find weapons. He survives, accuses Hector of murdering the Thracians for their horses, and is taken to Hector’s house for treatment.

_Enter_ PARIS.

The Thracian Charioteer reaches the Trojan guards wounded and reports that Rhesus and the sleeping Thracian army were attacked. He accuses Hector of arranging the killing, but Hector orders that he be carried to his house and cared for.
