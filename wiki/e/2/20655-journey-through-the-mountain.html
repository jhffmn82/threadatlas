7. Into the Black Pit and Out Again

The journey through the mountain begins when the travelers decide to follow the upward-slanting cavern. Jim pulls the buggy while Zeb drives and Dorothy and the Wizard provide lantern light; after a long, tiring climb, they emerge into a bright new country.
