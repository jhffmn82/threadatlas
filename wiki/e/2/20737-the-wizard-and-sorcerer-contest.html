1 section; each heading opens the text

3. The Arrival Of The Wizard

Oz and Gwig compete to prove whose powers are greater. Gwig creates the sound of unseen bells, while Oz produces a piglet from his hat, multiplies it into nine piglets, and makes them disappear. The Prince judges Oz superior, but Gwig attacks Oz with a spell that stops his breath, leading Oz to cut Gwig in two.
