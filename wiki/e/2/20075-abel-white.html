Abel White in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Abel White, a kind Worcestershire-born indigo-planter in India, owned a plantation at Muttra and employed the narrator as overseer; during the Mutiny, rebels attacked and burned his bungalow.

Abel White was an indigo-planter who employed the narrator as an overseer of his coolies. He remained at his bungalow during the early stages of the Mutiny, but rebels attacked and burned it while the narrator was away.
