Abstract written by a model

The Queen of Ev and her ten children are transformed by the Nome King after a wrong guess, instead of being enslaved for hard labor. Their transformed forms are scattered as ornaments and bric-a-brac throughout his palace. They are among the purple ornaments containing the royal family of Ev, while other unsuccessful guessers are likewise transformed into ornaments.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

11. The Nome King

The Nome King transformed the Queen of Ev and her ten children into articles of ornament and bric-a-brac rather than making them perform hard labor as slaves. He scattered their transformed forms throughout the rooms of his palace.

12. The Eleven Guesses

The royal family of Ev and unsuccessful guessers are transformed into ornaments. After Ozma's failure, the Nome King says that twelve persons are now transformed into ornaments.

13. The Nome King Laughs

Those who enter the Nome King’s palace and guess incorrectly are transformed into ornaments. The Tin Woodman, all twenty-six officers, and the private undergo this transformation in the unit.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The Nome King’s transformations include the purple ornaments containing the royal family of Ev, the green ornaments containing people of Oz, the gold ornaments containing the Scarecrow and Tiktok, and the emerald grasshopper containing Ozma. The Tin Woodman’s transformation remains unidentified.
