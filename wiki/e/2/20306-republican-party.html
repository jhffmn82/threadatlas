Republican Party

political party · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Republican, Republican party, Republicans

Abstract written by a model

The Republican Party held a separate constitutional convention in 1857, with proceedings assigned to the Minnesotian. In 1859 it campaigned for Alexander Ramsey and its legislative ticket, using speakers Galusha A. Grow and Schuyler Colfax. After winning a legislative majority, its newspapers, the Minnesotian and Times, disputed state printing patronage before leaders arranged a temporary consolidation. Its first state convention after Minnesota entered the Union nominated Col. Aldrich and William Windom for Congress; Republicans also backed Col. Marshall and attended Frederick Douglass's lecture.

Republicans held a separate constitutional convention in 1857 and their proceedings were assigned to the Minnesotian. In 1859 they worked to elect Alexander Ramsey and their legislative ticket, bringing Galusha A. Grow and Schuyler Colfax to Minnesota as speakers.
