James M. Goodhue

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Mr. Goodhue

Abstract written by a model

James M. Goodhue was a pioneer printer of Minnesota who arrived in St. Paul from Lancaster, Wis., in spring 1849 with a Washington hand press and fonts of type to start a paper in the new territory. He employed Capt. E.Y. Shelly at the Pioneer; they were working together when Goodhue died tragically. He later presented a retrospective account of St. Paul newspaper production, news gathering, conflicts, labor, politics, and wartime experiences.
