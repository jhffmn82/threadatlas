Caucasus

place · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Caucasian mountains, Caucasiis, Caucasus’ height, Mount Caucasus, mount Caucasus, peak, pinnacles, snowy Caucasus

Abstract written by a model

In Four Plays of Aeschylus, the Caucasus is a world-topping mountain peak that Io must reach after crossing the Stream of Violence, with heights that overtop the world and a river descending from them in spray. In The Argonautica, Caucasus is a place whose mountains rise beside the route to Aea and Phasis, where a serpent guards the fleece and Prometheus, bound on rocks, suffers an eagle feeding on his liver. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Caucasus is a mountain crossed by Alexander in sixteen days, near whose foot he founded Alexandria beside Prometheus's supposed cave and fetters.
