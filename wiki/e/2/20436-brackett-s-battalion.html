Brackett's battalion in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

Brackett's battalion was a military battalion of three Minnesota companies, mustered in September 1861. Initially assigned to Curtis horse and later the Fifth Iowa cavalry, it was the only Minnesota force engaged at Fort Donelson, then served in scouting, railroad protection, Tennessee campaigns, and Gen. Sully's Dakota campaign.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PRINTERS AND EDITORS OF TERRITORIAL DAYS.

Brackett's battalion consisted of three Minnesota companies and was mustered into service in September 1861. It was assigned to Curtis horse, later changed to the Fifth Iowa cavalry, and served at Fort Donelson, on scouting and railroad protection duty, in the Tennessee campaigns, and later in Gen. Sully's campaign across the Dakotas.
