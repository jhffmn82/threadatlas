Abstract written by a model

In Ozma of Oz, the Nomes are rock-colored sprites serving the Nome King who labor in underground furnaces and forges and produce metals, gems, and treasures. In The Emerald City of Oz, the Nomes are Roquat’s underground subjects, miners and metalworkers who serve as soldiers in the planned conquest of Oz. In The Emerald City of Oz, the Nomes are the Nome King’s fifty-thousand-strong army, following him and General Guph through a tunnel to invade Oz before marching home. In Tik-Tok of Oz, the nomes are subterranean workers and servants of Ruggedo, later swearing obedience to Kaliko and helping capture, guard, and search for prisoners and escapees. In Rinkitink in Oz, the nomes are mischievous underground inhabitants ruled by King Kaliko who work with gems, hide treasure, maintain an armed force, and fear eggs.

Nomes in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907 · the work’s page · read it where Nomes first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Nomes are powerful, queerly shaped, rock-colored sprites and rock-fairies who serve the Nome King and Roquat of the Rocks. They move over mountain rock and labor in underground furnaces and forges, producing metals, precious stones, and treasures hidden in rocks. They mock Ozma's party, vanish at the Nome King's growl, cannot bear Billina's egg, and are silent after their King's defeat.

Nomes in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910 · the work’s page · read it where Nomes first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Nomes are immortal underground beings and subjects of Roquat, the Nome King, living in his extensive caverns. They are miners and metal workers when not serving as soldiers, with great muscular strength gained through forging and mining, rock-colored uniforms, and skill in tunnel construction. Though good fighters, they are weak in magic and are judged not strong enough to conquer the Emerald City without help. Under Guph's command, they drill a long tunnel through solid rock beneath the Deadly Desert toward Oz, preparing an Invincible Army to march on the Emerald City for conquest, plunder, and slaves. They seek aid from the Whimsies and Growleywogs; the Growleywogs agree but secretly plan to conquer the Nomes afterward. The Nomes have previously been defeated by Oz people and remain vulnerable to hen's eggs, which are poisonous to underground beings. Guph therefore intends to keep them behind other forces, and they would flee from Billina's eggs. They remain allied with their underground monarch in the planned conquest and destruction of Oz.

the nomes in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where the nomes first appears

Abstract written by a model

The nomes are numerous subterranean people throughout the Nome Kingdom, laboring endlessly in caverns to hammer metals, melt ores, polish gems, and build the Metal Forest. They possess magic spears and serve under Ruggedo, General Guph, and later King Kaliko. They hate and fear Ruggedo, yet obey his commands: capturing travelers, imprisoning Queen Ann and her officers, binding and guarding prisoners, serving as executioners, searching for escapees, and pulling a chain to restrain Quox. Their greatest terror is eggs; contact with an egg's inside can destroy a nome unless a magical word is spoken quickly. Eggs drive them, along with Guph, Kaliko, Pang, and executioners, back into the caverns. After Ruggedo's rule, the chief nomes swear obedience to Kaliko, and the nomes are expected to welcome the change, help seek passages to the Metal Forest, and expel Ruggedo after he takes his permitted treasure. They are also mischievous, discouraging nearby settlement.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The nomes work in the caverns by hammering metals, melting ores, and polishing gems. They tremble when Ruggedo's gong sounds but continue working because none dares pause.

Chapter Fourteen

The nomes work industriously while Ruggedo is bored, although they hate him and can become sullen and refuse to work when he abuses them. They fear eggs because contact with the inside of one can make a nome wither up and blow away unless he quickly speaks a magical word.

Chapter Fifteen

The nomes gather around the Tube to capture the travelers and attack them with magic spears. Their weapons rebound harmlessly from Quox, and they retreat into the underground caverns when the Army of Oogaboo advances.

Chapter Sixteen

The nomes help Ruggedo imprison Queen Ann and her officers by operating the lever that replaces the pit's cover. They are astonished and frightened when Betsy asks for eggs.

Chapter Seventeen

Nomes bind Shaggy Man with ropes, serve as Ruggedo's executioners, and are sent to search for the escaped Queen Ann and Oogaboo people. They also follow Ruggedo's orders in guarding and handling the prisoners.

Chapter Eighteen

A thousand nomes pull the chain around Quox's neck, preventing the dragon from advancing. When they see the eggs, General Guph, Kaliko, Pang, and the executioners flee into the underground caverns and bar the door.

Chapter Nineteen

The nomes are expected to obey Kaliko and to welcome the change from Ruggedo's rule. Kaliko plans to assemble their chiefs and ask them to help search for the secret passages to the Metal Forest.

Chapter Twenty

The chief nomes swear obedience to Kaliko, and a band of nomes carries torches for the journey to the Metal Forest. Kaliko says the nomes will throw Ruggedo out after he takes whatever treasure he is permitted to carry.

the nomes in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916 · the work’s page · read it where the nomes first appears

Abstract written by a model

The nomes are numerous, mischievous underground people living in extensive caverns linked by arches and passages. Ruled by King Kaliko, they know where gold, silver, and precious stones are hidden, dig up and secretly rebury treasure, work with gems, maintain an armed force, and understand magic. They generally ignore Inga and Rinkitink in the caverns. Though not mortals, they are immortal unless touched by an egg or its shell or contents, which they fear intensely.

Chapter Seventeen

The nomes are numerous, mischievous people who live in extensive underground caverns connected by arches and passages. They know where the earth's gold, silver, and precious stones are hidden and secretly move and rebury treasures. At this time they are ruled by King Kaliko.

Chapter Eighteen

The nomes are numerous underground inhabitants who work with gold and precious gems and maintain a vast armed force. They understand magic, serve King Kaliko, and generally ignore Inga and Rinkitink as the visitors explore the caverns.

Chapter Twenty

The nomes are immortal unless they come into contact with an egg, including its shell or contents. They fear eggs so greatly that a nome will do anything to prevent one from touching it.
