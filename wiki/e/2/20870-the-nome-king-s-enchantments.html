the Nome King's enchantments in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The Nome King’s enchantments turn visitors who guess incorrectly into motionless objects and ornaments. Dorothy breaks one by guessing correctly, while the Scarecrow fails. Billina identifies many transformed living beings, repeatedly ringing the bell and restoring Ev’s royal family, Ozma, and their companions.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

The Nome King's enchantments transform visitors who guess incorrectly into objects. Dorothy breaks one enchantment by guessing correctly, while the Scarecrow fails to break any.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

The Nome King’s enchantment has changed people into ornaments and other objects, leaving them unable to move. Billina breaks the enchantment by correctly guessing which objects were once living beings.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The bell rings whenever an enchantment is broken. Billina breaks many of the Nome King’s enchantments, restoring the royal family of Ev, Ozma, and their companions to their natural forms.
