Captain Morstan's disappearance in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Captain Morstan disappeared in London on 3 December 1878 after leaving the Langham Hotel; inquiries and advertisements found nothing. Holmes linked the disappearance to Major Sholto, the only person Morstan could have visited, and suspected Sholto’s heir knew more. At Pondicherry Lodge, Morstan died after arguing with Major John Sholto over treasure, collapsing and striking his head on a chest. Sholto and Lal Chowdar concealed his body to avoid accusation and disclosure of the treasure.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

Captain Morstan disappeared on 3 December 1878 after arriving in London and going out from the Langham Hotel. Police inquiries and newspaper advertisements produced no result, and no word of him was heard afterward.

Chapter III

Captain Morstan’s disappearance is linked by Holmes to Major Sholto because Sholto was the only person in London whom Morstan could have visited. Holmes suspects that Sholto’s heir knows something about what happened and has tried to compensate Morstan’s daughter.

Chapter IV

Captain Morstan’s death occurred at Pondicherry Lodge after he and Major John Sholto argued over the division of the treasure. Morstan, who had a weak heart, suddenly collapsed and struck his head on the treasure-chest; Major Sholto and Lal Chowdar concealed the body rather than report the death.
