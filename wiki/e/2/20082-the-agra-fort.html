Abstract written by a model

Agra Fort, in Agra, has a central guard-house and a small isolated postern gate on its southwest side. It contains a modern occupied section and a larger deserted quarter of halls, passages, and corridors, with gates guarded by small detachments. Conspirators carry Achmet’s body to a crumbling hall and conceal a treasure chest beneath bricks in a best-preserved wall.
