Temple of Athena in The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies

Temple of Athena is a shrine on the Acropolis in Athens, containing Athena’s statue; Orestes seeks sanctuary there, and Athena’s arrival frames his dispute with the Furies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

_The scene changes to Athens. In the foreground, the Temple of Athena

The Temple of Athena on the Acropolis is where Orestes seeks sanctuary. Athena’s arrival there turns the shrine into the setting for the dispute between Orestes and the Furies.
