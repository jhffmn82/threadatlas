Agamemnon’s robe in The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies

Aeschylus · -0457

Agamemnon’s robe is a bloodstained device used to bind his hands and feet during his murder. Recalled and shown by Orestes, it serves as proof of Clytemnestra’s treacherous, dishonourable deed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

ORESTES (_approaching suddenly_)

The net and robe are recalled as the devices by which Agamemnon was trapped and murdered. Orestes and Electra invoke them at the tomb as evidence of the murder’s treachery and dishonour.

[_Enter Orestes and Pylades, in guise of travellers_.

Agamemnon’s robe is shown as the device that bound his hands and feet when he was killed. Orestes presents its bloodstained pattern as proof of Clytemnestra’s deed.
