The Minnesotian and Times was a consolidated newspaper, politically arranged from two rival Republican papers. Managed by Dr. Foster and Thomas M. Newson, it was marked by their uncordial relationship and ended when their partnership dissolved around the legislature’s adjournment.

The Minnesotian and Times was created through a politically arranged consolidation of the two rival Republican papers. The merged operation failed to produce a cordial relationship between Dr. Foster and Thomas M. Newson, and the partnership was dissolved around the time the legislature adjourned.
