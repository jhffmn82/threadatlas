the rajah's treasure

Abstract written by a model

The rajah’s treasure consists of precious stones and choice pearls placed in an iron box and sent to Agra for safekeeping. Abdullah Khan proposed dividing it among himself, Mahomet Singh, Dost Akbar, and the narrator. Years later it was found in the chemical laboratory at Pondicherry Lodge, then taken at night toward a ship; after Bartholomew Sholto’s death, it was missing and central to Jones’s accusation against Thaddeus Sholto.
