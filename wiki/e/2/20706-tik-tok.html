Tiktok in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Tik-tok is a new, queer and unusual copper mechanical man, manufactured by Smith & Tinker and identified in a patent as a machine. His jointed copper head and limbs contain separate clock-work mechanisms for thought, speech, and action; when wound by a key, he can think, speak, walk, act, and tell the time. He speaks in a monotonous voice with separated syllables, has plain thoughts, steel brains, no heart or conscience, and cannot feel sorrow or joy. Though very durable, he is complex and difficult to repair, does not sleep, and becomes helpless when his action or other machinery runs down. Formerly named Tik-tok by his master, he was imprisoned in a rocky chamber until Dorothy wound him. Because she rescued him, he becomes her obedient slave, machine man, counsellor, protector, and faithful follower. He explains the fate of Evoldo's family, the Wheelers, the Land of Ev, and the route to Evna; carries Dorothy's dinner-pail; drives away and captures Wheelers; and fights Princess Langwidere's soldiers until his action runs down. Rewound by Dorothy, he joins her, the Scarecrow, and the Tin Woodman on the journey to the Nome King's palace. He calmly passes beneath the giant's hammer, remains with Dorothy after others are transformed, and uses his final allowed guess before disappearing as a gold ornament. Restored afterward, he admits his thoughts can fail and attributes that to Smith & Tinker. He continues with Ozma's party to the Emerald City, belongs to Dorothy, is placed in Ozma's care, and receives two emerald bracelets.

Narrative, section by section

13 sections; each heading opens the text

front matter .. Author's Note

Tiktok is identified as one of the new and unusual characters in Ozma of Oz. A child who read the story before publication said that Tiktok was "REAL OZZY."

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

Tiktok is a copper mechanical man made by Smith & Tinker. After Dorothy winds his mechanisms, he thinks, speaks in a monotonous voice, walks stiffly, and tells how Evoldo locked him away after selling his family to the Nome King.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

Tiktok runs down while explaining that the Wheelers are harmless, but Dorothy rewinds him and restores his ability to speak and think. He explains the Wheelers, the royal family of Ev, his makers, and the Land of Oz, then devises a route to Evna and offers to protect Dorothy.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Tiktok uses the dinner-pail to frighten away the Wheelers and captures their leader, who tells him about the Land of Ev and Princess Langwidere. He fights Langwidere’s soldiers to protect Dorothy, but his action runs down, leaving him motionless in the drawing-room.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Tiktok is found in the palace and explains that he is a machine who can think, speak, and act when properly wound. His action has run down because Dorothy has the key, and he says he has Smith & Tinker's Improved Combination Steel Brains but no conscience or heart.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Dorothy winds Tiktok's action so that he can bow properly when introduced to the others. He is thanked by the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman for protecting Dorothy and later sits between them in the drawing-room.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Tiktok points out that the Nome King did not wrongfully obtain the royal family because Evoldo sold them to him. As Dorothy's slave, Tiktok says he will go wherever she goes and marches behind the Scarecrow.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

Tiktok explains that the iron giant was made for the Nome King by Smith & Tinker to prevent people from finding the underground palace. He says the giant cannot think or speak and that only the Nome King has the key to stop its machinery. Tiktok calmly walks beneath the raised hammer.

13. The Nome King Laughs

Tiktok remains with Dorothy and the Scarecrow after the others are transformed. He observes that the Tin Woodman was always an ornament to society and predicts that he and the Scarecrow will become absurd ornaments the next day.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

Tiktok stands motionless in the palace after using all but one of his allowed guesses. Dorothy winds him up, and he makes his final guess by touching a yellow vase and saying "Ev," but he disappears without being identified.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

Tiktok has been transformed into a gold ornament and is restored with the Scarecrow. He admits that he thought the Nome King was honest and says that Smith & Tinker are responsible when his thoughts do not work properly.

19. The King of Ev

Tiktok says that the Tin Woodman was a fine fellow, though tin was not very durable. Tiktok also wishes sometimes to be stuffed with straw instead of being made of copper, and later squeezes the Tin Woodman's hand so earnestly that some fingers are dented.

20. The Emerald City

Tiktok joins Ozma's party as Dorothy's faithful follower because he belongs to her. Dorothy winds up his machinery before the journey, and he later receives bracelets set with eight rows of sparkling emeralds.

Tik-Tok is a Smith & Tinker's Patent Double-Action, Extra-Responsive, Thought-Creating, Perfect-Talking Mechanical Man: a copper, clockwork man from the Land of Ev, moved by machinery and guaranteed to work for a thousand years. His thought, speech, and action mechanisms must be wound, and after water disables his joints he needs oil and drying. He cannot lie or think falsely because of his machinery, cannot fight, and can be injured only by a sledge hammer, though he carries a gun and a knapsack. Ozma sends him to guide and help Shaggy Man find his brother, but Ruggedo throws him into an old well. Shaggy Man and Betsy recover him and wind him up. He reluctantly becomes the Army of Oogaboo's only private soldier after Betsy promises to keep him wound, despite his inability to fight and concern that his works may run down. He is launched among rubber rocks, passes headfirst through the Hollow Tube, falls into a fountain, and is restored after Shaggy Man oils and dries him. His truthful account wins acceptance from the Private Citizen. On the way toward the nomes, he stubs his copper toe and needs help getting up. He reaches Ruggedo's throne-room, declares Ruggedo Queen Ann So-forth's prisoner, and raises his gun, but Kaliko knocks him down. Ruggedo and Guph weigh him down with a diamond and order him melted in a gold crucible. He later returns to Oz after faithfully serving Shaggy Man and the companions, praises Betsy, and supports admitting Shaggy Man's brother.
