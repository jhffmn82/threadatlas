George Allen & Company, Ltd. in The Rhesus of Euripides

Euripides · -0454/-0439

George Allen & Company, Ltd. is a publishing organization in London. It published The Rhesus of Euripides and a volume containing Euripides: Hippolytus; Bacchae; Aristophanes' 'Frogs,' then issued separate English rhyming-verse translations with explanatory notes of Hippolytus, Bacchae, The Trojan Women, Electra, Medea, Iphigenia in Tauris, The Frogs of Aristophanes, and Oedipus Tyrannus of Sophocles.

front matter

George Allen & Co., Ltd. published a volume containing Euripides: Hippolytus; Bacchae; Aristophanes' 'Frogs.' It also published separate editions of several plays translated into English rhyming verse with explanatory notes.
