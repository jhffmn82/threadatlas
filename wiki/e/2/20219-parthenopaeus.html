Parthenopaeus

person · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Part henopsus, the son of Zeus and of the mountain maid, this foe

Abstract written by a model

In Four Plays of Aeschylus, Parthenopaeus is assigned to the northern gate before the death-mound of Amphion, vows to storm the Cadmean stronghold, and bears a shield depicting the Sphinx carrying a Cadmean in its claws. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Parthenopaeus is the son of Atalanta and the daughter of Schoeneus, joins Adrastus's expedition against Thebes, and perishes in the war.
