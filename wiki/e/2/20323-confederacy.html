Abstract written by a model

In an untitled work of 1862~, the Confederate States are described as adopting “Dixie” as their national air after it became popular in New Orleans and throughout the South during the Civil War. (Novel-10146) In Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life, the Confederate States of America are described as providing no promised pay or assistance to General Price’s initial Missouri army, which nevertheless takes the field using weapons and supplies gathered from citizens and captured from Federal forces. In An Astronomer’s Wife, the Confederates are described as fighting Union troops during the Civil War, threatening Washington under Jubal A. Early in July 1864, causing alarm, and then being driven away after Union troops arrive. (An Astronomer's Wife)
