HOTEL FIRES.

The March 16, 1860 St. Paul fire began in a small wooden building on Third street near Jackson and became the city’s most destructive fire to that time. Thirty-four buildings burned, including the New England hotel and Hotel to the Wild Hunter, but firemen prevented the flames from crossing Robert street and destroying lower town.
