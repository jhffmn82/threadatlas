CHORUS

Athena’s covenant promises the Furies an eternal sanctuary, honor, worship, and influence over childbirth, marriage, fertility, and the prosperity of Athens. In return, the Furies accept the city, bless its people, and renounce the spreading of blight and civic vengeance.
