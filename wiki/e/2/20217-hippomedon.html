Hippomedon

person · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Hippomeilon

Abstract written by a model

In Four Plays of Aeschylus, Hippomedon is an Argive champion assigned to attack Pallas Onca’s portal, bearing Typhon on his smoke-emitting, snake-ringed shield; Eteocles counters him by assigning Hyperbius, whose shield bears Zeus. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Hippomedon joined the seven captains in the war against Thebes and perished in the war.
