the lost piglet (Princess Ozma's pet) in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

Princess Ozma’s white, pretty pet piglet wears an emerald collar meant for its fat neck. It disappears while asleep on a dressing-table, prompting an alleged-murder accusation and the mistaken belief that Eureka ate it. During Eureka’s attempt to attack it, the frightened piglet falls into a vase, where it is trapped at the bottom until the Tin Woodman breaks the vase and frees it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

18. The Trial of Eureka the Kitten

Ozma’s white piglet disappears from the dressing-table in her boudoir while it is asleep. Its disappearance is treated as an alleged murder, although the text does not establish what happened to it.

19. The Wizard Performs Another Trick

The lost piglet belongs to Princess Ozma and is mistakenly believed to have been eaten by Eureka. While Eureka attempts to attack it, the frightened piglet falls from a table into a vase and remains trapped there until the Tin Woodman breaks the vase and frees it.
