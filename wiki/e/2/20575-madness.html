madness

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Orestes’ madness and vision of the Furies, distraction, evil genius, heaven-inflicted calamity, madness and divine punishment, the vengeful wrath of heaven
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, madness afflicts Orestes after he kills Clytaemnestra, punishing him with pursuit by the Eumenides, terror, hallucinations, weakness, fasting, and repeated loss of reason.
  • In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, madness drives Orestes after the killings; he sees snake-haired Furies that the Chorus cannot see and flees toward Apollo’s shrine.
