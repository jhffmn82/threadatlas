madness

Also called: Orestes’ madness and vision of the Furies, distraction, evil genius, heaven-inflicted calamity, madness and divine punishment, the vengeful wrath of heaven

Abstract written by a model

In The House of Atreus; Being the Agamemnon, the Libation-Bearers, and the Furies, madness drives Orestes after the killings; he sees snake-haired Furies that the Chorus cannot see and flees toward Apollo’s shrine. In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, madness afflicts Orestes after he kills Clytaemnestra, punishing him with pursuit by the Eumenides, terror, hallucinations, weakness, fasting, and repeated loss of reason.
