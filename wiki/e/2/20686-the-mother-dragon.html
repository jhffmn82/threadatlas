the mother dragon in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908

The mother dragon is a two-thousand-year-old widowed female dragon who hunts elephants, rhinoceroses, people, buffaloes, or other food on weeks-long trips. Before leaving, she ties the dragonettes’ tails to rocks so they cannot wander, and is expected to return from the top of the earth.

13. The Den of the Dragonettes

The mother dragon is a widow about two thousand years old who hunts for elephants, rhinoceroses, people, buffaloes, or other food. She has tied all the dragonettes' tails to rocks while she is away and is expected to return from the top of the earth after several weeks.
