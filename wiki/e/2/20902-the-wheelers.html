the Wheelers in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The Wheelers are a strange group of humanlike wheeled creatures in a small part of the Land of Ev behind the woods. With wheels at the ends of their arms and legs, they roll swiftly on level ground, wear gorgeously colored embroidered garments, speak human speech and make wild outcries, but cannot cross the rough, jagged rocky hill. Mischievous and impertinent, they pretend to be fierce to frighten others, though they are physically unable to injure anyone. They chase, surround, and threaten Dorothy, Billina, and Tiktok, claiming the lunch-box and dinner-pail trees, until Tiktok frightens them with a dinner-pail. Their leader admits their helplessness and promises to stop frightening children; three remain guarding the hill, and some later promise obedience to King Evardo Fifteenth.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Letters in the Sand

The Wheelers are strange, humanlike creatures who move on four wheels instead of hands and feet. They chase Dorothy and Billina, surround the rocky hill, threaten to tear Dorothy into pieces, and claim ownership of the lunch-boxes and dinner-pails.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

The Wheelers are below the rocks and threaten to kill Dorothy and Tiktok. Tiktok says they are mischievous and harmless to anyone who dares to fight them, while Billina says they claim the lunch-box and dinner-pail trees for themselves.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

The Wheelers attack Dorothy, Tiktok, and Billina, but Tiktok frightens them with the dinner-pail. Their leader reveals that the Wheelers are physically helpless because their wheels prevent them from using fists, scratching, pulling hair, or kicking, and promises to stop frightening children.
