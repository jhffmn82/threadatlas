Captain Morstan in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Captain Morstan is an officer in an Indian regiment and its senior captain in 1878. He serves and commands native troops with Major Sholto and Lieutenant Bromley Brown, and is Sholto’s close friend. He possesses curiosities from the Andaman Islands and keeps a mysterious paper bearing a plan, a red cross, a distance note, and references to the sign of the four. In Small’s account, Morstan hears a proposed treasure bargain, goes to Agra, finds the treasure gone, and is said to have betrayed the conspirators with Sholto; he later leaves the army after an inheritance. In 1878 he returns to London on leave, stays at the Langham Hotel, sends for his daughter, and disappears, leaving luggage behind. He had gone to Pondicherry Lodge to claim treasure from Sholto, quarrels with him, suffers a fatal attack, falls, and dies.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

Captain Morstan was an officer in an Indian regiment and the senior captain of his regiment in 1878. He returned to England on leave, sent for his daughter, and disappeared after leaving the Langham Hotel.

Chapter III

Captain Morstan disappeared after coming to London, and Major Sholto was the only person there whom he could have visited. A curious paper found in his pocket-book contains a building plan, a red cross, a distance note, and references to the sign of the four.

Chapter IV

Captain Arthur Morstan had been Major John Sholto’s friend and came to Pondicherry Lodge to claim his share of a treasure acquired in India. During a quarrel, he suffered a fatal attack connected with his weak heart, fell backward, and struck his head against the treasure-chest.

He stopped, and held out his manacled hands for the whiskey-and-water

Captain Morstan is Major Sholto’s close friend and hears Small’s account before agreeing to the proposed treasure bargain. He later goes to Agra and discovers that the treasure has been taken; Small says that Morstan and Sholto betrayed the conspirators, while Morstan subsequently leaves the army after receiving an inheritance.
