Princess Langwidere's collection of heads in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Princess Langwidere’s collection is thirty interchangeable heads, stored in numbered, velvet-lined cupboards and varied in features, which she uses to change her appearance. It becomes central when she demands Dorothy’s head in exchange for an old cast-off head, but Dorothy refuses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Princess Langwidere’s collection contains thirty heads kept in separate velvet-lined cupboards numbered with gold. The heads vary in hair, eyes, noses, mouths, and other features, and she changes them to alter her appearance.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Princess Langwidere's heads collection is the reason she demands Dorothy's head in exchange for an old, cast-off head. Dorothy refuses because she does not want to exchange her own head for one she is not used to.
