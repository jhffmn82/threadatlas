the Nome King's army in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Nome King's army is a force of fifty thousand well-drilled Nome soldiers, armed with swords and pikes, wearing rock-colored uniforms, and belonging to and commanded by King Roquat the Red. General Blug first warns that it cannot cross the deadly desert and cannot withstand Princess Ozma's fairy powers. Roquat instead plans a tunnel route. General Guph leads the army for a surprise emergence in the Emerald City and a conquest of Oz alongside the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms, although the Phanfasms secretly intend to destroy it afterward.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

1. How the Nome King Became Angry

The Nome army consists of fifty thousand well-drilled Nome soldiers under General Blug. General Blug says the army cannot cross the deadly desert and would be helpless against Princess Ozma's fairy powers, though Roquat plans to send it through a tunnel.

4. How The Nome King Planned Revenge

The Nome army consists of fifty thousand Nomes armed with swords and pikes. It is well drilled and wears rock-colored uniforms, but Guph says it lacks the magical strength needed to defeat Oz.

6. How Guph Visited the Whimsies

The Nome King's army is led by General Guph in the planned invasion of Oz. Roquat intends for the army to march through the tunnel and emerge in the Emerald City without warning.

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

The Nome army is King Roquat the Red's great army, which General Guph commands. It is expected to join the Whimsies, the Growleywogs, and the Phanfasms in the conquest of Oz, though the Phanfasms secretly plan to destroy it afterward.
