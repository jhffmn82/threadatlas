Persian fleet in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Persian fleet first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Persian fleet, commanded by Megabates as admiral, suffered heavy storm and battle losses near Sepias and Artemisium. At Salamis it entered disorderly, lost its leading admiral, and fled after ships were sunk or captured. Later it withdrew from Samos to fortified Mycale, judged too weak to face the Greeks.

The Persian fleet suffered heavy losses in a storm near Sepias and later in battles near Artemisium. At Salamis, it entered the straits in disorder, lost its leading admiral, and was driven into flight after many ships were sunk or captured.

CHAP. IV.

The Persian fleet departed from Samos after hearing of the Greek expedition and withdrew to Mycale because it was considered too weak to face the Greeks. Its ships were hauled ashore and protected by a wall and a deep trench.

Persian fleet in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Persian fleet first appears

Abstract written by a model

Persian fleet, with Phoenician and Cilician seamen, had over ninety warships at Doryma. After Salamis, four hundred ships except Phoenicians wintered near Cuma, sailed to Samos to guard coasts and watch Ionian cities, then under Conon and Pharnabazus defeated Lacedaemonians at Cnidus, capturing fifty ships.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. HI.

After the battle at Salamis, the Persian fleet, except for the Phoenicians, wintered near Cuma and then sailed to Samos to guard the Ionian coasts. It consisted of four hundred ships and closely watched the Ionian cities because the Persians distrusted the Ionians.

CHAP. IX,

The Persian fleet commanded by Conon and Pharnabazus defeated the Lacedaemonian fleet at Cnidus. It pursued the enemy to shore and captured fifty of its ships.
