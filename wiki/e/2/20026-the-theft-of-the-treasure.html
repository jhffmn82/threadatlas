Abstract written by a model

The theft of the treasure is carried out by the narrator and Tonga, who enter the chemical laboratory through a roof trap-door, take the treasure-box, lower it by rope, and escape. The jewels are missing from the locked house; the sign of the four is left behind, and Holmes attributes the crime to the intruders.
