the Giant with the Hammer in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The Giant with the Hammer is a more than hundred-foot cast-iron man made by Smith & Tinker for the Nome King. Standing across a narrow road, it guards the Nome King’s palace by steadily pounding the earth with an iron mallet, preventing travelers from reaching the underground palace. It has no ability to think or speak and exists only to pound the road.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. The Giant with the Hammer

The Giant with the Hammer is a huge man made of cast-iron plates, standing across the narrow road and towering more than a hundred feet above it. Made for the Nome King by Smith & Tinker, it has no thinking or speaking attachment and is designed to pound the road to keep travelers from finding the underground palace.
