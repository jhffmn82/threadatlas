Abstract written by a model

The Winslow telegraph line, reaching St. Paul, was completed in summer 1860. It ended newspapers’ heavy reliance on steamboats and mail coaches for distant news. Before it, the narrator met arriving boats at the levee by their whistles; afterward, high rates limited use, though the Pioneer secretly obtained service.
