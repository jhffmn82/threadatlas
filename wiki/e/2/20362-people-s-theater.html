People's theater

Also called: People's Theater, The People's theater, this theater

Abstract written by a model

The People's theater was an old frame theatrical building on the corner of Fourth and St. Peter streets, described as the only real theatrical building in St. Paul. H. Van Liew leased and managed it with a stock company including Emily Dow, Harry Gossan, and Azelene Allen. In summer 1858, J.W. Wallack and Mrs. J.W. Wallack appeared for two weeks, performing works including Macbeth and The Man in the Iron Mask; Mrs. Wallack portrayed Lady Macbeth. Col. R.E.J. Miles was a star and first produced Mazeppa there. A.M. Carver often performed, including as Uncle Tom in Uncle Tom's Cabin. Its gallery charged the shaded gentry parquet prices, and Joe Rolette sometimes had epileptic fits during performances.

The People's theater, an old frame building on the corner of Fourth and St.

The People's theater was an old frame building at Fourth and St. Peter streets and the only real theatrical building in St. Paul. H. Van Liew managed it with a stock company that included Emily Dow, Harry Gossan, and Azelene Allen; the Wallacks performed Macbeth and The Man in the Iron Mask there in 1858, Col. R.E.J. Miles first produced Mazeppa there, and A.M. Carver acted there. Its gallery charged the shaded gentry the same admission as the parquet, and Joe Rolette's friends sometimes removed him after he had epileptic fits during performances.
