the magic belt in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The magic belt is a jeweled magical belt made in fairyland and belonging to the Nome King. Its powers work only in fairy countries. After it fails against the wooden Sawhorse, Dorothy takes it from the Nome King; it obeys her, opens a hidden passage, helps search unsuccessfully for the Tin Woodman, turns pursuing warriors into eggs, and stops the giant man of iron. Used by Ozma, it transports Dorothy to Uncle Henry. Dorothy then gives it to Ozma, who will use it to return her to the Land of Oz upon an agreed signal.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

The Nome King’s magic belt fails to stop the wooden Sawhorse because the creature is made of wood. Billina tells Dorothy that the jeweled belt unbuckles in the back and urges her to take it.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

The magic belt is taken from the Nome King by Dorothy and obeys her commands. It opens the hidden passage, is used in the unsuccessful search for the Tin Woodman, turns warriors into eggs, and stops the giant man of iron.

21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

The magic belt can transport Dorothy to Uncle Henry when Ozma uses it, but Dorothy gives it to Ozma before leaving the Land of Oz. Ozma will use it to return Dorothy to the Land of Oz if Dorothy makes the agreed signal.

magic belt in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Magic belt, belonging to Ozma, transforms anyone into any shape and mysteriously transports Jack Pumpkinhead and the forbidden flagon. Mogodore finds it in the palace, fastens it around his arm, transforms Scraps into a bird, then it carries Jack Pumpkinhead and the flagon to Mogodore's castle.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 17

The magic belt has the power to transform anyone into any shape. Mogodore finds it in the palace and fastens it around his arm because it is too small for his waist, then uses it to transform Scraps into a bird.

CHAPTER 18

The magic belt carries Jack Pumpkinhead and the forbidden flagon from the Red Jinn's palace to Mogodore's castle.
