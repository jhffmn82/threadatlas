Watson’s elder brother in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Watson’s elder brother, former owner of Watson’s watch, was careless and wasted good prospects, moving between poverty and brief prosperity, repeatedly pawning the watch before dying from drinking.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Sign of the Four / Chapter I

Watson’s elder brother was the former owner of Watson’s watch. Holmes infers that he was careless, had good prospects that he wasted, experienced poverty and occasional prosperity, pawned the watch repeatedly, and eventually died from drinking.
