Omby Amby in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

Omby Amby is a private soldier in Ozma's army. He guards a chariot, survives an encounter with the Giant with the Hammer by clinging to the giant's arm and falling onto the Scarecrow, then is summoned at a feast. Ozma promotes him to Captain General of all her armies and Commander of her Body Guard, with royal uniforms supplied.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

10. The Giant with the Hammer

The private initially remains on the far side to guard the chariot. After the Sawhorse is struck, he lands on the Giant with the Hammer's arm and clings there until he drops onto the Scarecrow's body, escaping without broken bones.

20. The Emerald City

Omby Amby is the private summoned from the barracks during the feast. Ozma promotes him to Captain General of all the armies of her kingdom and Commander of her Body Guard, and she promises to supply his uniforms from the royal treasury.

Omby Amby in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Omby Amby is a tall, slim, gentle traveler and Captain General of Ozma's army, marked only by a fierce mustache. After serving as a private in the war against the Nomes, he accompanies Dorothy's party and approves School Pills after arithmetic headaches.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

9. How the Wogglebug Taught Athletics

Omby Amby, the Captain General of Ozma's army, accompanies Dorothy's party. He is described as tall, slim, gentle, and dressed in a gay uniform. At the college, he approves of the School Pills because studying arithmetic once gave him headaches.
