  • In Ozma of Oz, the Magic Picture's changing scenes show any part of the world or living person when Ozma wishes, allowing Dorothy to see Kansas and Uncle Henry and Ozma to watch Dorothy for a return signal.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, the Magic Picture lets Ozma remotely view people and scenes anywhere, including Dorothy, Roquat the Red, and the Nome King's invasion preparations and retreat.
  • In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the Magic Picture constantly changes scenes to show important events throughout Oz, helping Ozma find lost children and see events in the forest.
  • In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Magic Picture shows scenes Ozma chooses with moving figures, and Ozma and the Wizard use it to watch adventurers until it shows their transport to Oz.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, the Magic Picture is a constantly changing moving picture showing worldwide events, adventures, and absent persons, including Button-Bright, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Scarecrow.
  • In Rinkitink in Oz, the Magic Picture shows scenes Dorothy wishes to see, including Inga's rescue mission, Nome Country, and the Nome King's caverns.
  • In The Lost Princess of Oz, the Magic Picture shows what anyone anywhere is doing, reveals those approaching Ozma’s palace to conquer it, and is used by Ugu to watch the travelers and conceal Ozma’s location.
  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, the Magic Picture shows anyone Dorothy wishes to see and reproduces their actions while she watches, including Button Bright, Aunt Em, their companions, and Mrs. Yoop's transformations.
  • In The Royal Book of Oz, the Magic Picture shows Ozma or Dorothy any friend and what that person is doing, and the Cowardly Lion suggests using it to find the Scarecrow.
  • In Kabumpo in Oz, the Magic Picture reveals Ruggedo carrying Ozma's palace and later shows the Runaway Country rushing toward the Nonestic Ocean.
  • In Grampa in Oz, Ozma's magic picture shows anyone a person wishes to see, and Dorothy plans to use it to reveal the lost Princess of Perhaps City's whereabouts.
  • In The Gnome King of Oz, the Magic Picture shows any subject's location and actions when Ozma commands it, and Scraps expects it to reveal her in Patch.
  • In The Giant Horse of Oz, the Magic Picture shows requested people where they are and displays Quiberon attacking the sapphire castle at the Wizard's command.
  • In The Yellow Knight of Oz, the Magic Picture normally shows a person's exact location when Ozma asks, but it is missing from the wall and hidden in her library when she seeks the Comfortable Camel.

the Magic Picture in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Magic Picture is Ozma’s royal treasure, a magic picture that shows what any person anywhere in the world is doing when someone wishes to see that person. It also reveals anyone approaching Ozma’s royal palace intending to conquer it. At the beginning, Dorothy, Betsy, and Trot find it missing from its usual place on the palace wall, at the same time Ozma is reported missing, so the people of the Emerald City cannot consult it to locate her. The travelers fear Ugu the Shoemaker has taken it with Ozma. Later it is found hanging in a room of Ugu’s castle, where Ugu uses it to watch the traveling party and learn of expeditions searching for him; it shows the travelers approaching in miniature. When consulted, it can show Ugu in dove form in the far Quadling Country with the golden jeweled dishpan beneath him. But when asked to show Ozma, it displays only a round black spot, revealing her concealment without showing her location.

The Magic Picture is a magical picture that shows a person’s location and actions. It first reveals Ruggedo carrying Ozma’s palace during the palace’s tilting, when it is knocked down and rehung. Later it shows the Runaway Country rushing toward the Nonestic Ocean, letting Ozma and others watch its plunge and learn Peg Amy and Pompa are visiting.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The Magic Picture can show where a person is and what that person is doing. When the Scarecrow asks where they are, it shows Ruggedo with Ozma's palace on his head; Ozma's later question confirms the same sight. The picture is knocked from the wall during the palace's tilting and is then rehung.

Chapter 22

The Magic Picture shows the Runaway Country rushing toward the Nonestic Ocean with Ruggedo on its highest hill. Ozma and the others use it to watch the country’s plunge and learn that Peg Amy and Pompa are coming to visit.
