Magic Belt in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Magic Belt is a magical belt and transport device once belonging to Roquat the Red, the Nome King. It formerly enabled many of his wicked plans and represented half his power. Dorothy captured it from him in a fair fight, after which it was possessed by Dorothy and worn by Princess Ozma; Guph calls it stolen by the Oz people. Ozma uses it to transport Uncle Henry and Aunt Em from Kansas to the Emerald City, while Roquat orders Guph's army to recover it. Its powers include granting wishes when Ozma wears it, transporting Kansas by a wish, and transforming the Whimsies by giving each a large, fine head, though it cannot increase the First and Foremost Phanfasm's sorcery. Invading forces hope to seize it during their conquest of Oz, and the Nome King expects it could send the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms home. Dorothy proposes using it to escape to Kansas, but Ozma refuses to abandon Oz. In the end, Ozma uses the belt to place dust in a tunnel, retrieves it from the palace, and wishes the Phanfasms, Whimsies, and Growleywogs safely back in their homes; the wish is immediately granted.

Ozma intends to use the Magic Belt to transport Uncle Henry and Aunt Em from Kansas to the Emerald City of Oz. She plans to obtain it after breakfast and use it to bring them to the palace, where Dorothy can explain their new surroundings.

The Magic Belt is described by Guph as belonging to the Nome King and having been stolen from him by the Oz people. Guph promises that Roquat will use its power to give the Whimsies large natural heads if they help capture it.

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

The Magic Belt was taken from the Nome King by Ozma, Dorothy, and their companions after they entered the Nome King's cavern and freed the slaves from Ev. The Growleywogs plan to seize the belt after helping conquer Oz.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The Magic Belt is to be used to give each Whimsie a large, fine head. General Guph says that it could not add to the First and Foremost Phanfasm's powers of sorcery.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

Dorothy suggests using the Magic Belt to send Ozma and the others to Kansas, where they could sell emeralds and pay off Uncle Henry's farm mortgage. Ozma rejects abandoning her people, though she offers to use the belt to send others away if they wish.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The Nome King plans to obtain the Magic Belt during the conquest of Oz. General Guph says the Belt will send the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms back to their own countries if the Nome King wishes it.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

Ozma uses the Magic Belt to place dust in the tunnel and later retrieves it from the palace. She wishes the Phanfasms, Whimsies, and Growleywogs safely back in their own homes, and the wish is immediately granted.

Magic Belt in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Magic Belt is a magic belt formerly owned by the Nome King and captured from him by Dorothy. Worn by Dorothy, it protects her so that nothing can hurt her. At first its other powers are unknown to her. She later secretly tests it, learning it grants one wish a day and performs transformations when its wearer uses its power. Dorothy uses it to restore an upside-down room, transform Ugu into a dove, and reduce the dove to canary size.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The Magic Belt is worn by Dorothy and protects her so that nothing can hurt her. Its other powers are unknown to Dorothy, and she does not use it to cross the mountains.

CHAPTER 22

Dorothy has secretly tested the Magic Belt and learns that it can perform transformations and grant one wish each day. By closing her right eye, wiggling her left toe, drawing a long breath, and wishing, she uses it to make the upside-down room revolve back to its normal position.

CHAPTER 23

The Magic Belt transforms Ugu into a dove and later reduces the dove's size to that of a canary bird. Dorothy explains that she had secretly practiced using its transformation power.

magic belt in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

The magic belt is Ozma’s protected magic treasure and Ruggedo’s most treasured possession. It can transform people into any shape or form and transport them wherever its controller desires. Peter hopes Ozma will use it to send him back to Philadelphia, while Ruggedo seeks it to make Kuma Party’s captured hand serve him and to use its awful power against the Emerald City. Ozma carries it when visiting Glinda; after an oztrich egg hatches, Ruggedo steals it and clasps it around his waist. He commands people to be transported until the Wizard removes the belt, leaving Ruggedo harmless.
