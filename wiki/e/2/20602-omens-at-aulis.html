omens at Aulis

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Calchas’ boding strain, the fateful eagles, the omen, the visioned eagles, the warning

Abstract written by a model

At Aulis, two eagles killed and ate a pregnant hare; Calchas read this as Troy’s delayed, painful fall, Artemis’s anger, adverse winds, and a sacrifice needed before the fleet could sail.

AGAMEMNON

At Aulis, Calchas interpreted two eagles tearing apart a pregnant hare as an omen of victory mixed with danger. The omen was linked to Artemis's anger, adverse winds, and the sacrifice required before the fleet could depart.
