TableQA in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

TableQA is a task of answering questions grounded in tables. It traditionally assumes gold tables, which are relatively sparse, and most questions focus on a particular cell. In open-domain TableQA, tables are selected from an entire candidate pool through a pipeline with a retriever and reader. Earlier work emphasized readers, including executable logical forms such as SQL queries; later systems use classification or autoregressive answer generation. For each retrieved table, TableQA generates an answer and returns the one with the highest probability or confidence. Joint training improves performance, while irrelevant tables hurt answer generation, motivating relevance tokens and a modified RAGE loss in LI-RAGE.

table-based question answering in TableRAG: A Retrieval Augmented Generation Framework for Heterogeneous Document Reasoning

Xiaohan Yu, Pu Jian, Chong Chen · 2025

Table reasoning is a system for answering user queries from tabular data. Its mainstream approaches are prompt engineering, including Tab-CoT and Chain-of-Table, and programs or agents, including Tabsqlify, DATER, BINDER, and InfiAgent-DABench. It is evaluated with WikiTableQuestion and other benchmarks for reasoning, comparison, aggregation, and multi-hop questions. TableRAG supports single-source and table-plus-text questions and incorporates SQL-based reasoning for efficient, accurate execution.
