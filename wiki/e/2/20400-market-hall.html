THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

Market hall was built in 1853 at Wabasha and Seventh streets and became one of St. Paul's principal places of amusement. It hosted the Hough Dramatic company and was used by the Wide Awakes as a drill hall in 1860.
