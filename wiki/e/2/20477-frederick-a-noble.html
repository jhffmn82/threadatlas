Abstract written by a model

Frederick A. Noble, pastor of House of Hope church, is portrayed as an eloquent, learned, fearless, forcible, and aggressive divine. During the Rebellion he supports the Union with stirring war addresses, admires Abraham Lincoln, and after Lincoln’s death delivers an emotional memorial sermon praising Lincoln and eulogizing Seward.

THE DEATH OF PRESIDENT LINCOLN.

Frederick A. Noble was the pastor of the House of Hope church and was described as an eloquent, learned, fearless, forcible, and aggressive divine. He delivered an emotional invocation and a memorial discourse praising Lincoln and also eulogizing Secretary of State Seward.
