THE DEATH OF PRESIDENT LINCOLN.

Frederick A. Noble was the pastor of the House of Hope church and was described as an eloquent, learned, fearless, forcible, and aggressive divine. He delivered an emotional invocation and a memorial discourse praising Lincoln and also eulogizing Secretary of State Seward.
