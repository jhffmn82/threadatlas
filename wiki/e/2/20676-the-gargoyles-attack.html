the Gargoyles' attack

Also called: the attack, the fight, the next time, their first attack

Abstract written by a model

The Gargoyles first swarm Jim but are scattered by his kicking; they return in greater numbers, attack the others, and withdraw after the Wizard fires and Zeb captures one.

11. They Meet the Wooden Gargoyles

The Gargoyles first swarm toward Jim, but his kicking scatters them. They later attack in greater numbers by flying over Jim toward the others; the Wizard's revolver shot knocks some down, and Zeb captures one before the rest withdraw.
