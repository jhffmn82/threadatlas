the purple ornaments in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

The purple ornaments are ten scattered transformations of the Queen of Ev and her nine children, in many shapes and sizes throughout the palace. Dorothy restores at least one; later Billina counts ten, identifies them, and restores all of them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

The purple ornaments are the transformed Queen of Ev and her children. They are scattered throughout the palace in many shapes and sizes, and Dorothy restores at least one of them.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

Billina counts ten purple ornaments scattered throughout the palace. She identifies them as transformations of the Queen of Ev and her nine children and restores all of them.
