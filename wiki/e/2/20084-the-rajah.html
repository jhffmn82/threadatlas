the rajah in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

The rajah of the northern provinces amassed inherited and hoarded treasure, dividing gold and silver in his palace from jewels secretly sent to Agra. After supporting the Sepoys, he was deposed and driven out of India; conspirators treated his property as loot.

The rajah of the northern provinces possessed substantial wealth and had divided it between gold and silver kept in his palace and jewels sent secretly to Agra. He supported the Sepoys after believing that the British were being defeated, causing his property to be treated by the conspirators as loot.
