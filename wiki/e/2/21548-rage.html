RAGE

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: RAGE loss, Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals

Abstract written by a model

RAGE is a loss for visual question answering that rewards a retriever with table-level signals from a reader during joint training. It is used in LI-RAGE and DPR-RAGE, with 5 offering the best performance-latency balance. Adapted for TableQA by modifying its loss, it is evaluated alongside relevance tokens, whose design performs best.

RAGE is in one work on this site, LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Introduction

RAGE loss, proposed by Lin and Byrne (2022) for visual question answering, rewards the retriever with table-level signals from the reader during joint training in the described setting.

Experimental Setup

RAGE supplies the loss used for joint training in LI-RAGE and DPR-RAGE. LI-RAGE also uses a binary relevance token in its output, and the experiments ablate both joint training and relevance tokens.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

RAGE is adapted for TableQA by modifying its loss. The relevance-token design performs better than selecting the answer from the highest-scoring table or combining retrieval scores with answer confidence.
