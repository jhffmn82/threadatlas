Dolon’s espionage mission

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Dolon our spy, who sallied forth t’ explore The navy of our foes, Dolon’s reconnaissance, emprise, enterprise, errand, journey, our wolf shall mount, spy, the man's return, the nocturnal spying mission, the spy, to go disguised to the Greek ships

Abstract written by a model

In The Rhesus of Euripides, Dolon’s espionage mission is to enter the Greek camp disguised as a wolf, discover whether the Greeks plan to retreat or prepare another operation, report to Hector before dawn, and possibly kill Odysseus or Diomedes as proof of loyalty. In Hecuba and other plays, the nocturnal spying mission is proposed by Aeneas and undertaken by Dolon to discover whether the Greeks intend to flee or ensnare the Trojans. In Hecuba and other plays, Dolon’s reconnaissance concerns the Greek fleet and keeps him away from the Trojan lines while Hector and the Chorus await his report.
