Second Minnesota Infantry Regiment

Abstract written by a model

The Second Minnesota Infantry Regiment was a Union infantry regiment from Minnesota, organized in 1861 under Colonel H.P. Van Cleve and headquartered at Fort Snelling. It moved toward Washington, was redirected to Louisville, reported to W.T. Sherman, and joined George H. Thomas's First Division. In the Mill Springs campaign, it camped at Logan's Cross Roads, fought in the center under heavy fire, used bayonets, lost twelve killed and thirty-five wounded, received praise, and was presented a silk flag.

THE DECISIVE BATTLE OF MILL SPRINGS.

The Second Minnesota Infantry Regiment was organized in 1861 under Colonel H.P. Van Cleve and assigned to George H. Thomas's First Division. At Mill Springs it fought in the center of the engagement, used the bayonet, and suffered twelve killed and thirty-five wounded; its conduct received enthusiastic praise.
