The Seven against Thebes

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Seven against Thebes, the enterprise, the war

Abstract written by a model

In Four Plays of Aeschylus, The Seven against Thebes depicts the defense of the Cadmea against Polynices and his Argive allies, ending with Eteocles and Polynices dead, the male line extinct, and Antigone protesting Polynices's dishonorable treatment. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Seven against Thebes is the expedition led to enforce Polynices's claim to the kingdom; the invading army ultimately fled and left its dead unburied.
