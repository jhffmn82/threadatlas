Norwood

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

In The Sign of the Four, Norwood is where Pondicherry Lodge is located, and the party travels there to meet Bartholomew Sholto and demand Miss Morstan’s share of the treasure. In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Norwood contains Deep Dene House, where Jonas Oldacre lived and where McFarlane meets Oldacre, examines documents, and investigates the murder scene with Holmes.
