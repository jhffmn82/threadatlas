Norwood in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Norwood is a location containing Deep Dene House, where Jonas Oldacre lived. McFarlane goes there to meet Oldacre and examine documents; Lestrade says the murder occurred there. Holmes investigates the house and fire scene, finds seemingly incriminating evidence, and ultimately exposes Oldacre's deception.
