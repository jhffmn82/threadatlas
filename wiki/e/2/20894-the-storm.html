Abstract written by a model

A weather event on the ocean, the storm endangers people and causes Dorothy and Billina's voyage. It darkens the day, tosses a ship, and confines passengers; later it blows their hen-coop away, leaving them wet and adrift as Billina nearly drowns. Sunshine later dries them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Girl in the Chicken Coop

The storm makes the day dark, tosses the ship dangerously, and forces the passengers to remain in the cabin. It eventually subsides, allowing Dorothy to float more gently and rest.

2. The Yellow Hen

The storm leaves Dorothy and Billina wet and adrift after the hen-coop is blown away from the ship. Billina nearly drowns but clings to the coop, and the sunshine later dries their clothing and feathers.
