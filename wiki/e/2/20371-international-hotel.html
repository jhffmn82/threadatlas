Abstract written by a model

The International hotel, formerly the Fuller house, was a five-story brick building at Seventh and Jackson streets, built by A.G. Fuller in 1856 for about $110,000. Renamed in 1861 and once regarded as the best hotel in the West, it burned in the February 3, 1869 fire.
