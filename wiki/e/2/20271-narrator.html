1 section; each heading opens the text

SPELLING DOWN A SCHOOL.

The narrator lived in a small village in northern Pennsylvania at about twelve years of age and was considered too young for joint spelling matches. The narrator secretly rode under a driver's seat to attend one contest, correctly spelled "phthisic," and spelled down four schools. Afterward, the narrator's reputation as a speller was established, and the narrator was no longer called last in spelling contests.
