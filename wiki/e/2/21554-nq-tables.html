NQ-TABLES in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

NQ-TABLES is a benchmark for open-domain TableQA, built from difficult NaturalQuestions answerable from tables rather than plain text. It contains 9,594 training, 1,068 development, and 966 test examples across 169,898 tables, evaluated with Token F1, exact match, and Recall@K. LI-RAGE attains state-of-the-art results: 54.17 Token F1, 46.15 EM, and 87.90 recall with five retrieved tables; joint training raises recall from 85.21 to 87.90.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

NQ-TABLES is one of the two benchmarks on which LI-RAGE achieves state-of-the-art results.

Experimental Setup

NQ-TABLES contains difficult questions from NaturalQuestions that can be answered from tables rather than plain text. The systems are evaluated on it using Token F1, exact match, and Recall@K; LI-RAGE obtains 54.17 Token F1, 46.15 EM, and 87.90@5 retrieval recall.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

NQ-TABLES shows more than three-point improvements in Token F1 and EM from LI-RAGE over previously reported results. LI-RAGE achieves 87.90% recall with five retrieved tables, while joint training improves recall from 85.21 to 87.90.

Dataset Statistics

NQ-TABLES contains 9,594 training examples, 1,068 development examples, 966 test examples, and 169,898 tables. LI-RAGE is trained on it for 20 epochs with a reader learning rate of 0.00002.
