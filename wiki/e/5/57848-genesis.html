Abstract written by a model

Genesis is a book of Holy Writ and Scripture. Its opening two chapters recount the world's formation: darkness lies over the deep, light is created, and man appears as the Creator's last work, receiving dominion over fish, birds, and other living things. It unfolds from darkness and creation to humanity's appointed rule.

Genesis is in one work on this site, The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Book of Genesis is presented as a source that sheds light on the mythological account of the world’s formation. Its first two chapters describe the creation of the world, including darkness spread over the deep.

notes .. notes

Genesis is cited as describing God’s creation of light and the granting of dominion to man over the fish, birds, and living things of the earth. The unit says that Ovid’s account presents information derived from Holy Writ in a disfigured form.
