House of Commons

Also called: British House of Commons, Commons, House of Commons at Westminster, Houses of Parliament, The House of Commons

Abstract written by a model

The House of Commons in England was an institution that controlled the purse and could pressure the Executive by threatening to withhold supplies. In 1822, it received a Union Bill concerning Upper and Lower Canada, which Papineau and John Neilson helped defeat. Later it appointed a royal commission to investigate grievances in Lower Canada, debated and overwhelmingly passed the Russell resolutions, and authorized the governor to pay expenses without Assembly authorization.

{21} CHAPTER IV THE RISE OF PAPINEAU In the year 1812 a young man took his seat in the House of Assembly for Lower Canada who was destined to play a conspicuous part in the history of the province during the next quarter of a century.

The House of Commons in England exercised control over the purse and could pressure the Executive by threatening to withhold supplies. A Union Bill concerning Upper and Lower Canada was introduced there in 1822, but Papineau and John Neilson helped secure its defeat.

{57} CHAPTER VII THE RUSSELL RESOLUTIONS When the legislature of Lower Canada met in the autumn of 1836, Lord Gosford earnestly called its attention to the estimates of the current year and the accounts showing the arrears unpaid.

The House of Commons debated and passed the Russell resolutions by overwhelming majorities. It authorized the governor to pay government expenses without authorization from the Assembly.
