Sardis in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Sardis is a city, identified as a Persian city; Xerxes marched there from Susa, sent heralds to Greece, and departed toward the Hellespont, while Tissaphernes fled there and Agesilaus's army devastated his garden.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

LAST TEN BOOKS / BOOK XI.

Xerxes marched from Susa to Sardis and sent heralds from there into Greece to demand earth and water. He later marched from Sardis toward the Hellespont.

CHAP. vm.

Sardis was the place to which Tissaphernes fled after the defeat near Sipylus. Agesilaus's army advanced as far as Sardis and destroyed a garden belonging to Tissaphernes.
