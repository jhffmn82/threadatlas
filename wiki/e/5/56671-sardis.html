Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Sardis was identified as a Persian city to which Xerxes marched from Susa, where he sent heralds to Greece, and to which Tissaphernes fled before Agesilaus's army devastated his garden. In Description of Greece, Sardis was the principal city of wealthy Lydia and the chief residence of the satrap by the sea; Agesilaus marched there after crossing to Asia Minor and later defeated Tissaphernes in the plain near the Hermus.

LAST TEN BOOKS / BOOK XI.

Xerxes marched from Susa to Sardis and sent heralds from there into Greece to demand earth and water. He later marched from Sardis toward the Hellespont.

CHAP. vm.

Sardis was the place to which Tissaphernes fled after the defeat near Sipylus. Agesilaus's army advanced as far as Sardis and destroyed a garden belonging to Tissaphernes.
