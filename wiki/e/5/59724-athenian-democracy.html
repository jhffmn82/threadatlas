Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Athenian democracy is described as a form of government restored to the Athenians, a promise used to secure peace or restore liberties, and a system whose supporters in Athens were restored after existing officials were removed. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, democracy in Samos is described as a democracy that the Athenians set aside after their misfortunes in Sicily and later restored after Theramenes helped bring about the change. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Athenian democracy is described as promoting democracy in allied cities, being supported by the people of Corcyra, and being abolished by a general vote before the government of the Thirty replaced it.

Athenian democracy in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Athenian democracy first appears

Abstract written by a model

Athenian democracy is a form of government restored to the Athenians after their exclusion from government. Democracy also appears as a promise used to secure peace or restore liberties, sometimes deceptively. In Athens, magistrates favoring it were restored after existing officials were removed, while Phocion and others were accused of seeking its abolition after the Lamian war.

Dionysius used the promise that he would restore democracy as a device to persuade the Syracusans to accept peace. His proposal was deceptive, and he attacked while the Syracusans were neglecting their guards.

Abstract written by a model

Democracy was promoted by Athens in its allied cities and supported by the people of Corcyra. The democratic faction at Corcyra expelled its oligarchic opponents, while Theramenes restored democratic government at Paros. The Athenian democracy was abolished by a general vote after Lysander threatened Theramenes and pressured the Athenians. Its replacement was the government of the Thirty.
