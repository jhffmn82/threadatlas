LUCIDITY.

Voltaire is identified as the principal agent of France's eighteenth-century travail for lucidity. Although Arnold says that Voltaire harmed France through his lack of seriousness and reverence, he credits Voltaire's lucidity with doing good and compares him to the Luther of the eighteenth-century cultivated classes.
