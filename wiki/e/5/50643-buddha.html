Buddha in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Buddha, presented as Gautama, is a prince, mortal sage, and son of a king who lived about one thousand years before Christ. After withdrawing from society, he practised six years of austerities and meditation, attained triple science and enlightenment, then began preaching and turning the wheel of the law at Benares. He taught Buddhism, converted followers including his father, had discourses recorded, and died at eighty.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BUDDHA

Buddha is presented as Gautama, also called Sakyasinha and Buddha, a prince and mortal sage who lived about one thousand years before Christ. He withdrew from society, practiced austerities for six years, attained liberating knowledge, preached at Benares, converted many followers including his father, and died at eighty.
