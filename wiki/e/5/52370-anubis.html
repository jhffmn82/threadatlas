Anubis in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Anubis, one of Osiris's sons, accompanies Osiris on an expedition as a valiant man, wearing dog-skin-covered mail; dogs and a dog's head represent him and his symbolic guarding of Osiris.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We shall first speak of the barbarians; not that we judge them

Anubis, one of Osiris's sons, accompanied him on his expedition and is described as a valiant man. He wore a coat of mail covered with a dog's skin, which is given as a reason Egyptians worshipped dogs.

Anubis in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Anubis, son of Osiris and Nephthys, assists Isis in searching for Osiris’s body and learning what happened to the coffin at Byblos.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MYTH OF OSIRIS AND ISIS

Anubis, described as the son of Osiris and Nephthys, helped Isis search for Osiris's body. He also aided her in learning what had happened to the coffin at Byblos.
