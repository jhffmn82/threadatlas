Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE BUILDING STONE SUPPLY.

Granite is particularly abundant in New England and occurs in lesser quantities elsewhere in the country. It was used for major buildings, monuments, paving, and breakwaters, and could be quarried into blocks of almost any size and shape.
