History of Ruggedo in Six Rocks in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The History of Ruggedo in Six Rocks is Ruggedo’s six-rock, carved life story, made over nearly two years to preserve his past against oblivion. It recounts his kingdom, transformations, conflicts with Oz, banishment, and attempted capture of Oz, while omitting or softening damaging facts.

Chapter 6

The History of Ruggedo in Six Rocks is Ruggedo’s account of his life, carved in stone over nearly two years. It records his former kingdom, transformations, conflicts with Oz, banishment, and latest attempt to capture Oz, although Ruggedo omits or softens facts that make him look bad. He creates it so that he can consult his past if he again falls into the Fountain of Oblivion.
