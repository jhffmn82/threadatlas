Abstract written by a model

A battle near Babylon between Cyrus's Greek-supported army and Artaxerxes's Persian forces. The Grecians routed their opponents and Clearchus's men inflicted most royal casualties, but Cyrus was killed after striking Artaxerxes. The royalists overcame Cyrus's main army, and the Grecians later defeated barbarians attacking after nightfall; Artaxerxes won.

CHAP. V-

The battle was fought between Cyrus's Greek-supported army and Artaxerxes's Persian forces near Babylon. The Grecians routed the forces opposing them, but Cyrus was killed after striking Artaxerxes, and the royalists eventually overcame Cyrus's main army. The Grecians then defeated the barbarians who attacked them after nightfall.

In the battle, Cyrus was killed and Artaxerxes's forces became victorious, while Clearchus's Lacedaemonians and mercenaries inflicted most of the king's casualties. More than fifteen thousand were killed on the king's side, about three thousand in Cyrus's army, and few Greeks were killed.
