Claros in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Claros, containing an oracle and an unfinished temple of Apollo, was reached by Cretans and captives; after conflict before Rhacius, it later received Smyrna’s envoys seeking the oracle.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Claros was reached by the Cretans under Rhacius and by the captives sent by Apollo to form a colony. The Cretans attacked the newcomers there and brought them before Rhacius.

CHAPTER V.

Claros was the place to which the people of Smyrna sent envoys to consult the oracle about their situation. It also contained a temple of Apollo in Colophonia that was not quite finished.
