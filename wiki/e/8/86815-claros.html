Claros

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Claros was the coastal place where Mindarus fled after being cut off from Cyzicum; Pharnabazus's army was encamped there and supported the Peloponnesians against the Athenians. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Claros contained an oracle and an unfinished temple of Apollo, was reached by Cretans and captives, and later received Smyrna’s envoys seeking the oracle after conflict before Rhacius.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Claros was reached by the Cretans under Rhacius and by the captives sent by Apollo to form a colony. The Cretans attacked the newcomers there and brought them before Rhacius.

CHAPTER V.

Claros was the place to which the people of Smyrna sent envoys to consult the oracle about their situation. It also contained a temple of Apollo in Colophonia that was not quite finished.
