the Council of State in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Council of State is Ozma's council of counsellors, summoned by the Scarecrow at Glinda's request in the palace throne room. Including the Tin Woodman, Scraps, the Shaggy Man, Tik-Tok, Jack Pumpkinhead, Cap'n Bill, Professor Wogglebug, the Frogman, Uncle Henry, the Wizard, the Scarecrow, and Glinda, it meets to rescue Ozma and Dorothy and decides to investigate the Skeezer country.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

The Council of State is summoned by the Scarecrow at Glinda's request. Ozma's counsellors gather in the palace throne room to decide how best to rescue Ozma and Dorothy.

Chapter Fourteen

Ozma's council includes the Tin Woodman, Scraps, the Shaggy Man, Tik-Tok, Jack Pumpkinhead, Cap'n Bill, Professor Wogglebug, the Frogman, Uncle Henry, the Wizard, the Scarecrow, and Glinda. It assembles in Ozma's throne room and decides to investigate the Skeezer country.
