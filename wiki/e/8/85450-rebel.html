Abstract written by a model

REBEL is a large-scale relation extraction dataset aligning Wikipedia abstracts with Wikidata knowledge triples, used to train DocuNet to infer text triples.

REBEL is in one work on this site, REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
