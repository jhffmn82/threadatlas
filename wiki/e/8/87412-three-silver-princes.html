Abstract written by a model

The three Silver Princes are the Scarecrow's haughty, overbearing royal sons and fathers of fifteen grandsons. They plot to kill the Scarecrow and succeed him, first considering throwing him into the silver river. They feign affection while making him feel old, seek the Grand Gheewizard's help to restore Chang Wang Woe's original form, and promote plans concerning succession and marriage. Resenting the Scarecrow's friends from Oz, they insist on continuing the Ceremony of Restoration. When the silver vase bursts over them, two become silver pigs and the eldest becomes a weasel, and they flee into the gardens.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

The three Princes greet the Scarecrow haughtily and discuss the possibility of succeeding him. They privately complain that he may live for many years, consider throwing him into the silver river, and begin discussing another plan to remove him.

CHAPTER 15

The three royal Princes pretend to show affection toward the Scarecrow while making him feel old and watching for an opportunity to carry out their plan. They arrange for the Grand Gheewizard to prepare a means of restoring him to his original form and announce plans connected with the Scarecrow's succession and marriage.

CHAPTER 20

The three Silver Princes resent the Scarecrow's friends from Oz and push for the Ceremony of Restoration to continue. The silver vase misses the Scarecrow and bursts over them, transforming two into silver pigs and the eldest into a weasel; they run squealing into the gardens.
