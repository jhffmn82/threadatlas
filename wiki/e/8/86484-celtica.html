Abstract written by a model

is_a country Celtica is presented as the realm where a famous ruler lived and where Hercules built Alesia. The inland peoples beyond Massilia, around the Alps, and on the northern side of the Pyrenees are called Celts, while the peoples farther south are called Gauls, though the Romans generally use the name Gauls for all of them.
