Chersonesus is a peninsula and region opposite Rhodes, named for its shape or an earlier petty prince; Curetes expelled Cares, divided it into five parts, and Cyrnus and Triopas later ruled.

Chersonesus was described as a region opposite Rhodes, named either for its shape or for an earlier petty prince. The Curetes expelled the Cares from it and divided it into five parts, while Cyrnus and Triopas later established power there.
