This work studies two dual problems in environmental analysis and proposes and assesses several methods within a retrieval-augmented generation framework powered by large language models. It is limited in its ability to identify evidence and detect targets, and it plans to investigate task interactions, claim detection, larger automatically labeled test sets, fine-tuning, and automatic correctness assessment.
