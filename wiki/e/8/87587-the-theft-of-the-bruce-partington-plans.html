Abstract written by a model

The theft of the Bruce-Partington plans begins after the originals are confirmed in the Woolwich Arsenal government office on Monday. Ten papers are removed from the locked office; seven are later recovered from Arthur Cadogan West, but three essential drawings remain missing and unsolved. Holmes observes that a clerk could have copied the plans, pointing toward outside involvement. Colonel Valentine Walter, seeking financial gain, takes an impression of his brother’s keys, removes the documents, and gives them to Hugo Oberstein for five thousand pounds; Oberstein keeps the three vital plans.

The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans

The theft occurred after the plans were confirmed to be in the Woolwich Arsenal office during working hours on Monday. Ten plans were taken, seven were recovered from Arthur Cadogan West, and three essential plans are still missing.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

The theft involves the removal of the original Bruce-Partington plans from a locked government office, with three vital drawings still missing. Holmes notes that taking originals was unnecessary for a clerk who could copy them, suggesting that an outsider may have been responsible.

Surely enough, a note awaited us at Baker Street. A government

The theft of the Bruce-Partington plans is committed by Colonel Valentine Walter, who takes an impression of his brother’s keys and removes the documents. He gives them to Hugo Oberstein in exchange for five thousand pounds, while Oberstein retains three essential plans.
