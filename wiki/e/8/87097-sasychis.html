Abstract written by a model

Sasychis, the second Egyptian law-maker, was a wise and prudent prince who added laws on honoring and worshiping the gods, discovered geometry, and taught astronomy.

• And now it is necessary for us to speak of the legislators of Egypt^

Sasychis is described as the second Egyptian lawmaker, a wise and prudent prince who added laws concerning the honor and worship of the gods. He is also credited with discovering geometry and teaching astronomy.
