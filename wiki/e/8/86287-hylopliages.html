Hylopliages

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Hylopliages are a nation of slender, light-bodied people who, with wives and children, climb and move among trees like birds, feeding on buds and tender branches. They wear no clothing, share wives and children, fight with clubs, and sometimes are furnished to death when blindness prevents food-gathering.

Hylopliages is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

The Hylopliages, with their wives and children, climb trees and feed on buds and tender branches. They are light and nimble, move from tree to tree like birds, go naked, share their wives and children communally, fight with clubs, and sometimes are furnished to death when blindness prevents them from obtaining food.
