the Oxshott murder

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: how they murdered him, the crime, the death of Garcia, the murder, the murder of Garcia, the tragedy

Abstract written by a model

The Oxshott murder is the killing of Mr. Garcia at Oxshott Common, a grave event connected with Garcia and Wisteria Lodge. Garcia’s servant and cook flee Wisteria Lodge that night, and police initially consider robbery and later arrest a man believed to be the cook. According to Miss Burnet’s testimony, Don Murillo planned the killing to eliminate a pursuer. After Murillo and Lopez force Miss Burnet to reveal an address linked to a planned attack, Murillo is believed to strike Garcia down among the gorse bushes while Lopez guards her. Miss Burnet then disappears, leaving her fate uncertain.
