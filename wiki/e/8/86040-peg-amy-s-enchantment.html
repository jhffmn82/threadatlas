Chapter 21

Glegg enchanted Peg Amy into a tree after Uncle Tozzyfog refused Glegg's demand to marry her. Glegg's scroll declared that she would remain a tree until two people called and believed her to be a Princess, and he repeatedly asked her to marry him while she remained enchanted.
