Knowledge Filtering module

module · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Knowledge Filtering, filtering, knowledge filter, knowledge filtering, knowledge filtering module, knowledge filtering techniques

Abstract written by a model

Knowledge Filtering is a module that uses the intrinsic capabilities of the Large Language Model as an effective knowledge filter. It addresses retrieved knowledge that may be irrelevant or harmful to answer generation, without requiring an extra language model, additional training data, auxiliary models, reflection outputs, or model-specific confidence measures. It independently filters knowledge retrieved for the original, externally augmented, and internally augmented queries, removing unrelated documents before the three filtered knowledge sets are combined. Experiments report improved precision and S-Precision, support for larger useful document sets, and stronger BlendFilter performance than methods incorporating more retrieval noise.

Abstract

Knowledge filtering uses the intrinsic capabilities of the LLM to effectively eliminate extraneous data.

1

The Knowledge Filtering module eliminates irrelevant retrieved knowledge. It operates without an extra language model by leveraging the LLM's innate filtering capability.

2

Knowledge filtering addresses the problem that retrieved knowledge can be irrelevant or harmful to answer generation. Proposed methods assess whether retrieval is needed or whether retrieved knowledge is relevant, but they may require additional training data, auxiliary models, reflection outputs, or model-specific confidence measures.

3 .. 3.4

Knowledge filtering uses the Large Language Model to remove irrelevant information from knowledge retrieved for the original, externally augmented, and internally augmented queries. The three knowledge sets are filtered independently before being combined.

4 .. 4.8

Knowledge filtering is presented as a mechanism for eliminating unrelated retrieved documents. The experiments report that it improves precision and S-Precision, supports larger useful document sets, and helps BlendFilter outperform methods that incorporate more retrieval noise.
