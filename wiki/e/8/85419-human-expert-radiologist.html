human expert radiologist in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

A board-certified radiologist, LA, with 9 years of experience in chest and oncologic imaging, answered radiology questions without online information, additional materials, or images. Evaluated with the same accuracy metrics as language models, the radiologist scored 63% (50/80; 95% CI 51%–72%) on RSNA-RadioQA and 62% (15/24; 95% CI 42%–83%) on ExtendedQA. On RSNA-RadioQA, RadioRAG-powered GPT-3.5-turbo, GPT-4, and Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 outperformed the radiologist, while the radiologist outperformed Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 and Llama3-8B; ExtendedQA differences were not significant.

Abstract

The human radiologist provided a performance comparison for the language models. RadioRAG matched or exceeded the human radiologist in question answering across radiologic subspecialties.

2. Materials and Methods / 2.1. RSNA Cases .. 2.6. Code and Data Availability

A board-certified radiologist, LA, with 9 years of experience and subspecialties in chest and oncologic imaging, answered the same questions as the language models without online information, additional materials, or images. STA and DT evaluated the radiologist's responses using the same accuracy metrics applied to the models.

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

The human expert radiologist achieved 63% (50/80) on RSNA-RadioQA and 62% (15/24) on ExtendedQA. On RSNA-RadioQA, the radiologist performed below RadioRAG-powered GPT-3.5-turbo, GPT-4, and Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1, while outperforming RadioRAG-powered Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 and Llama3-8B; differences on ExtendedQA were not statistically significant.
