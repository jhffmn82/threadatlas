Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

XVIII..

The storm is produced by violent winds that make the sea turbulent and prevent Leander from crossing. Leander appeals to Boreas to calm the waters, but the wind does not yield.

183 Am I, then, never to embrace you except when

Winter is associated with dangerous seas that have been assailed by storms. The speaker asks that winter persist once he has reached the addressee’s shore, where it will keep him from attempting to leave.

XIX

The storm delays Leander's journey and is said to fight against his coming. Hero argues that the previous night offered a gentler opportunity for crossing, but the storm-tossed sea quickly changed and now keeps him from her.
