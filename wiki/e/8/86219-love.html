Abstract written by a model

Love is the force joining Leander and Hero in spirit and driving their secret, mutual relationship. It gives Leander strength, hope, and guidance as he crosses cold, dangerous water to reach Hero, yet makes separation more painful and may lead to his death if he fails. Their love is constrained by shame, fear of men's speech, delay, and uncertainty about faithfulness. Hero fears Leander’s affection may wander and wants their shared flame to endure. The whole moves from love’s uniting and motivating power through perilous crossing and anxious separation toward a hoped-for reunion and freedom from secrecy.

love in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · about 24 to 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where love first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

XVIII..

Love gives Leander strength to swim through the cold and troubled waters and serves as his guiding light. It keeps hope alive for him, although it also makes the nearby separation from Hero more painful.

183 Am I, then, never to embrace you except when

The speaker’s love is not cautious and drives him onto the deep even when the sea is dangerous. His anxious love may end in death if the crossing fails.

XIX

Hero and Leander's love is presented as mutual but threatened by separation, delay, and Hero's fear that Leander's affection may wander like the wind. Hero wants their love to remain faithful and their flame not to become cold ashes, and she says that their true joy depends on their reunion.

165 Perhaps you fear the time may fail you for

The secret love is opposed by shame and fear of what people will say. The girl wishes either that the shame forcing them to love secretly would end or that their love would cease to fear public speech.
