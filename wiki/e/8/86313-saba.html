is_a capital city [of the Sabeans; situated on a high hill] Saba is the capital city of the Sabeans and stands on a high hill. It is the royal seat, where vessels, cups, beds, chairs, household goods, buildings, and temples are richly adorned with gold, silver, ivory, precious stones, and other costly materials.
