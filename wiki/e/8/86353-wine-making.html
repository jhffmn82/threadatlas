Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, wine-making is described through Dionysius’s discovery of the use of wine, teaching of vine-planting, and instruction in making Zythus. In The Library, wine-making is described as what Dionysus taught Icarius, who then gave the resulting drink to shepherds.
