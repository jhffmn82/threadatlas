Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" MY DISREPUTABLE FRIEND, MR.

The police pursue Rags after seeing him strike McGonegal and fall, blocking his route toward the river. They search Case's tenement and the roofs, question one another, and watch the surrounding area, but they fail to find him beneath the bedding.

The sun rose hissing out of the East River, a broad, red disc of heat.

The police are understood by Rags to be watching ferries and railway depots for departing criminals. He also knows of a police boat whose lights could expose someone crossing the river.
