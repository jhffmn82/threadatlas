Cuma

Abstract written by a model

Cuma was attacked and devastated by Alcibiades under the pretense of charges against its inhabitants. Its people defeated his force and afterward accused him at Athens of wasting a confederate city.

Cuma is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
