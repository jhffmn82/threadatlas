Abstract written by a model

The Heenan-Sayers fight is a heavily publicized contest between the Champion of the United States and the Would-be Champion, scheduled near Philadelphia in the Eagle Inn barn with $250 tickets. Editors await confirmation, initially doubting it occurred, until Gallegher brings the report. Correspondents urgently describe the fight and Hade’s final capture; afterward, the correspondents are arrested, and Captain Scott cites the fight as evidence that they broke the peace.
