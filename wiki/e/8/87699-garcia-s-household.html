Garcia's household in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Garcia's household consists of a faithful manservant and a skilled cook, both associated with Garcia's needs and travels. All were confederates in the same unknown crime. When Garcia did not return from his planned enterprise, the household disappeared from Wisteria Lodge, apparently following an arranged escape; Holmes believes one servant later returned for something precious left behind.

Garcia's household consists of a faithful foreign manservant who handles his needs and a skilled half-breed cook. Both disappear from Wisteria Lodge during the night along with Garcia.
