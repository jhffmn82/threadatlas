Abstract written by a model

High Gable is a large Jacobean grange near Oxshott and less than half a mile from a tragedy. Don Murillo took it as his English residence and later returned there; it is also home to Mr. Henderson’s unusual household. Holmes suspects Garcia’s note came from it. Its rooms include Miss Burnet’s confinement and a mysterious kitchen linked to the mulatto cook’s rituals.
