Martha in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Martha is the only remaining housekeeper at Von Bork’s house and secretly serves Holmes’s espionage operation. She keeps the house clear, signals when action is safe, collects addresses from posted letters, and is directed to report to Holmes in London.
