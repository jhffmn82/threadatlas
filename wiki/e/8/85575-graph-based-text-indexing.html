graph-based text indexing in LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zirui Guo, Lianghao Xia, Yanhua Yu, Tu Ao, Chao Huang · 2025

Graph-based text indexing is a method in LightRAG for representing complex interdependencies in text. It begins by segmenting documents into chunks, extracting entities and relationships, creating and deduplicating knowledge-graph elements, and generating indexed text key-value pairs. Combined with dual-level retrieval, it supports incremental knowledge-base updates without complete reprocessing, captures broader semantic context for queries, and improves retrieval efficiency and comprehension. Evaluation indicates it can provide sufficient answering context even when original text is removed from retrieval.

Graph-based text indexing is incorporated into LightRAG to represent complex interdependencies among entities. It is used together with dual-level retrieval to support comprehensive and efficient information access.

Graph-based text indexing segments documents into smaller chunks, extracts entities and relationships, generates indexed text key-value pairs, and deduplicates identical graph elements. It is used to support comprehensive information understanding and efficient retrieval.
