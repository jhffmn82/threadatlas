the engagement and breakup in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

An engagement lasting three months of continual companionship ends when Van Bibber tries to make it appear his fiancée chose the separation. Regretting the breakup, he seeks her in a secluded dell; they reconcile and are seen driving home together.

The engagement ends after three months of continual companionship, with Van Bibber trying to make it appear that his fiancée chose the separation. His regret leads him to seek her out, and the couple are later seen driving home together, indicating that the breakup has been reversed.
