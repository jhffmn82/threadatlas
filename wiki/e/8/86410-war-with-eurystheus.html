war with Eurystheus

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: fought Eurystheus, routed his army, the battle between the Heraclids and Eurystheus, this battle, war, πόλεμον
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is the battle in which the Heraclidae, assisted by the Athenians and commanded by Iolaus, Theseus, and Hyllus, fought Eurystheus and routed his army.
  • In The Library, it is the war with Eurystheus undertaken by the Athenians, who killed Eurystheus's sons.

the battle between the Heraclids and Eurystheus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Heraclidae, assisted by the Athenians and commanded by Iolaus, Theseus, and Hyllus, fought Eurystheus and routed his army. A great part of Eurystheus's forces was cut off, Eurystheus was killed by Hyllus, and all of Eurystheus's sons fell.
