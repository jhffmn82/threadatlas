Abstract written by a model

The Cornish Horror is a strange, mysterious reported case involving the Tregennis family at Tredannick Wartha: Brenda Tregennis dies, while Owen and George Tregennis lose their senses in extreme terror. The events stir Cornwall and western England, receive imperfect London coverage, and are investigated by Holmes and Watson, who discuss them with Sterndale.
