The Cornish Horror in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The Cornish Horror is a strange, mysterious reported case involving the Tregennis family at Tredannick Wartha: Brenda Tregennis dies, while Owen and George Tregennis lose their senses in extreme terror. The events stir Cornwall and western England, receive imperfect London coverage, and are investigated by Holmes and Watson, who discuss them with Sterndale.

The Adventure of the Devil’s Foot

The Cornish Horror is the name given to the events at Tredannick Wartha, where Brenda Tregennis dies and Owen and George Tregennis lose their senses while displaying extreme terror. The incident causes excitement throughout Cornwall and the west of England, though the London press receives only an imperfect account.
