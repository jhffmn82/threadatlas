Athenians' Sicilian expedition in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Athenians' Sicilian expedition was an Athenian siege of Syracuse by sea and land. The Athenians seized Epipolae, fortified Labdalon and Polichna, and built walls to block the city and port; Nicias commanded its continuation. Although reinforcements arrived, Gylippus and Syracusan aid, a failed assault, plague, and naval defeats reversed the siege. The remaining forces abandoned their fleet, retreated by land, and suffered eighteen thousand deaths and seven thousand captures.
