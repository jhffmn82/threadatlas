escape from Pokes in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

A successful escape rescues Dorothy and Sir Hokus from the Pokes after their singing prevents sleep; despite Dorothy's hokey pokey incapacitation, the Cowardly Lion ropes both through the gates to safety.

CHAPTER 6

The escape from Pokes depends on Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion singing loudly enough to keep from falling asleep. Dorothy is incapacitated by hokey pokey, but the Cowardly Lion pulls her and Sir Hokus through the gates with a rope and gets them safely outside the kingdom.
