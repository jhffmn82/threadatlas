Ervic in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Ervic is a young, brave, clever, honest Skeezer who leads the Skeezers and later serves as Prime Minister of Queen Aurex. Though compelled to serve Queen Coo-ee-oh to avoid her anger, he faithfully promises to help the three Adepts. After leading three other Skeezers from the iron boat and staying awake while they sleep, he carries the Adepts, then fishes, in his copper kettle to Reera the Red's cottage. He disappears during the night after accompanying Queen Coo-ce-oh and the Flatheads in the submarine. At Reera's cottage, he withstands hostile creatures, watches Reera's transformations, declines to use magic powder, and cleverly helps persuade Reera to change the fish into the three Adepts. He leaves with them, is credited with restoring and releasing them, and joins Glinda, the Wizard, and the Adepts in consultation about raising the island. Glinda says his actions may also have saved the Skeezers from permanent imprisonment.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Sixteen

Ervic leads the three other Skeezers after they are left floating in the iron boat. He stays awake while his companions sleep, agrees to help the three Adepts, follows their instructions, and carries them in a copper kettle to a purple cottage.

Chapter Seventeen

Ervic accompanied Queen Coo-ce-oh and the Flatheads in the submarine when they went to confront the Su-dic. He later mysteriously disappeared during the night, while the boat somehow floated to shore and stranded there.

Chapter Eighteen

Ervic enters Reera the Red's cottage carrying the copper kettle and calmly withstands attacks by the cottage's creatures. He refuses to leave, observes Reera's transformations, and cleverly suggests that the fishes be changed into three girls who are Adepts at Magic while insisting that the fishes remain as they are.

Chapter Nineteen

Ervic considers taking magic powder from Reera's cupboard but follows the fishes' advice and helps persuade Reera to transform them into girls. He praises Reera's red hair, eats supper with the others, thanks her, and leaves her cottage with the three Adepts.

Chapter Twenty-One

Ervic helped bring about the release of the three Adepts from their fish forms. Glinda declares that he will be properly rewarded because he helped the Adepts and may have helped save the Skeezers from permanent imprisonment.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Ervic enters the Dome with Glinda, the Wizard, and the three Adepts and joins the consultation about raising the island. Glinda tells the others that Ervic helped restore the three Adepts.
