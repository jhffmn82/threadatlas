Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XU.

Dionysius surrounded Rhegium and attacked it daily with very large engines. The Rhegians, commanded by Philo, made sorties, burned the engines, and killed many Sicilians, while Dionysius was seriously wounded but continued the siege.

CHAP. XIII.

The siege of Rhegium lasted eleven months under Dionysius. The blockade produced extreme famine and ended with the surrender of Rhegium and the capture or enslavement of many inhabitants.
