Abstract written by a model

The wedding of Miss Cambridge and young Standish, called Ted, is properly and publicly arranged by Cortlandt Van Bibber at Little Church Around the Corner. To give their elopement publicity and prevent scandal, Van Bibber stages an orthodox, fashionable ceremony with prominent club-men witnesses, invited women, flowers, organ music, bell, rice, and an old shoe.

" VAN BIBBER AS BEST MAN Young Van Bibber came up to town in June from Newport to see his lawyer about the preparation of some papers that needed his signature.

The wedding is organized by Van Bibber to give the elopement publicity and avoid talk or scandal. It includes invited women and club men, flowers, music, a church bell, rice, and an old shoe, and is intended to appear as an orthodox and fashionable wedding in the newspapers.
