The steel bridge connects Skeezer island to the mainland across the lake; after powder burns and “ee” is spoken, it extends joint by joint from the palace bank to the mainland shore.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The steel bridge is controlled by the magic word ee. After powder is burned and ee is spoken, a basement door opens and the bridge moves out, extends joint by joint, and rests on the mainland shore.
