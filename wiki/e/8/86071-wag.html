Wag in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Wag is a wandering rabbit of Oz, an expert digger, and at first Ruggedo’s only retainer. He helps build Ruggedo’s secret passage, gathers goods stolen from the Emerald City, and is paid with a monthly ruby; his purple wool socks are his most valued possessions. Though loyal in service, he mocks Ruggedo’s kingship, protects Peg Amy, fears Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic, and ultimately chooses Peg Amy over Ruggedo. Ruggedo imprisons him when he tries to leave, but Expanding Fluid enlarges Wag into a six-foot giant rabbit. Carrying Peg Amy on his back and shoulders, he crosses the Deadly Desert toward Ev and joins Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore. He faithfully helps the party: he fights the Twigs, suffers a torn ear, seeks cabbages, worries over dangers, and is revived after being thrown down during the Country’s flight. He angrily seeks to punish Ruggedo for the magical mischief, but is restrained because Ruggedo could endanger Ozma’s palace. Wag prefers plain folks to Princesses, yet insists Peg Amy is the proper Princess and helps free her by truly believing it. He rejoices when the Magic Mirror confirms her, celebrates her marriage to Pompadore, and travels with the group to the Emerald City and Pumperdink.

Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Wag is a wandering rabbit and expert digger who helps Ruggedo build the secret passage and serves him in exchange for a ruby each month and stolen goods. He mocks Ruggedo’s claim to be a king, protects Peg Amy from him, and collects the objects Ruggedo steals from the Emerald City. When Ruggedo discovers Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic, Wag recognizes its significance and reacts with alarm.

Chapter 9

Wag decides to leave Ruggedo and takes Peg Amy and his treasures with him. Ruggedo locks him in his room, where he waits anxiously and worries about starving. After being enlarged by the Expanding Fluid, Wag joins Peg Amy in chasing Ruggedo.

Chapter 10

Wag survives the explosion and discovers that he has become a giant rabbit, while his head has trouble keeping up with his growth and his words come out backward when he speaks quickly. He loses his stolen treasures in the destroyed cave but agrees that it is better not to keep them. He carries Peg Amy and sets off with her toward Ev across the Deadly Desert.

Chapter 13

Wag carries Peg Amy and becomes angry when Kabumpo laughs at her. He explains that Ruggedo used the Mixed Magic and vows to pound Ruggedo’s curly toes off. He agrees that Peg and he should travel with Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore.

Chapter 16

Wag returns from a Winkie farmhouse with two dozen cabbages and continues traveling toward Ev. He helps fight the Twigs by kicking them and suffers a badly torn ear that the travelers tie up afterward. He worries about crossing the Deadly Desert.

Chapter 17

Wag panics when the moving Country approaches and later protests that land is supposed to stand still. He demands lettuce, carrots, and cabbages before he will work, and he is carried into Ev with the others. The Country’s flight from the giant throws him through the air; he is knocked down and later revived after Kabumpo finds him.

Chapter 18

Wag backs away from Glegg’s Mixed Magic, accidentally strikes Ruggedo’s foot, recognizes him, and angrily tries to pound his toes with a stick. The others restrain him because Ruggedo’s movements could endanger Ozma’s palace, and he later complains that Ruggedo is getting off too easily.

Chapter 19

Wag reminds Prince Pompadore that he never asked the Question Box who the proper Princess was and insists that Ozma was not the Princess named in the scroll. He travels with Peg Amy, Pompadore, and Kabumpo toward the Winkie Country.

Chapter 20

Wag helps search for food, plays scop hotch with Pompa and Peg Amy, and becomes more cheerful after meeting the Hurrah Bird. He urges the others to approach the gold castle and celebrates when the Magic Mirror reveals that Peg Amy is the Proper Princess.

Chapter 21

Wag helps free Peg Amy by calling her a Princess and truly believing that she is one. He is warmly embraced by Peg Amy, celebrates her marriage to Pompadore, and travels with the group to the Emerald City and Pumperdink.
