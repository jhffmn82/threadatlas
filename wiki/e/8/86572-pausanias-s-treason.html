is_directed_against Greeks [in alliance with Persia] Pausanias's treason consisted of a secret agreement with Artabazus and Xerxes to betray Greece in exchange for wealth and marriage to the king's daughter. The conspiracy was revealed through intercepted letters and a staged conversation, leading to Pausanias's death and Sparta's loss of command at sea.
