2. The Tiger of San Pedro

The giant visitor appears at a window of Wisteria Lodge as a face with a strange clay-and-milk colouring, huge staring eyes, and large white teeth. A number twelve shoe print shows that the visitor had an exceptionally large foot, and the figure escaped through the shrubbery toward the road.
