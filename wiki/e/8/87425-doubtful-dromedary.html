Doubtful Dromedary in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Doubtful Dromedary is a habitually doubtful dromedary of Oz and friend of the Comfortable Camel, carrying goods, silk cushions, and a tent. It travels with the Camel and other travelers onto Wish Way, repeatedly doubting what it hears. It disappears into a big yellow butterfly, is restored by the Cowardly Lion’s wish, and doubts it wanted the change. Later it fears rejection in Oz and Sir Hokus’s loss before a cyclone carries it toward the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

The Doubtful Dromedary travels with the Comfortable Camel and repeatedly doubts what others say. It carries supplies including silk cushions and a tent, joins the travelers, and follows the Comfortable Camel onto Wish Way.

CHAPTER 17

The Doubtful Dromedary disappears and becomes a big yellow butterfly. It is restored when the Cowardly Lion wishes it to be itself again, then doubts that it would have wanted to become a butterfly.

CHAPTER 23

The Doubtful Dromedary worries that he and the Comfortable Camel will not be accepted in Oz, though Sir Hokus assures them that they are like family. He fears that Sir Hokus will never be seen again, then is carried toward the Emerald City by the cyclone.

Doubtful Dromedary in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

A dromedary and comparative newcomer to Emerald City, it repeatedly doubts courage’s value and rejects the Patchwork Girl’s proposed cure for the Cowardly Lion.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

The Doubtful Dromedary repeatedly doubts that courage is the finest thing in the world and doubts the Patchwork Girl's suggestion that eating a brave person would give the Cowardly Lion courage.
