Abstract written by a model

is_conducted_by Theramenes [with Athenian forces] The siege of Chalcedon was conducted by Theramenes with an Athenian fleet and army. The Athenians built a timber wall from sea to sea, defeated a sortie led by Hippocrates, and then reached an agreement under which Chalcedon paid its previous tribute.
