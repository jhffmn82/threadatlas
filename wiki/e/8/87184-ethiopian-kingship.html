DIODORUS SICULUS- / BOOK III.

Among the Ethiopians near Meroe, priests select a king from among their ranks according to which candidate is first seized by a carried deity, and the people then worship him as a god. Other Ethiopian peoples choose kings for beauty, wealth, courage, or industrious care for subjects.
