Peace of Callias in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Peace of Callias is a peace between the Persians and Athenians and their confederates. It frees Greek cities in Asia to govern by their own laws, keeps Persian commanders and warships from coastal areas, bars Athenian invasion of Artaxerxes’ provinces, and leads to withdrawal from Cyprus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

The peace required Persia to free the Greek cities in Asia and keep its commanders and warships away from specified areas near the Greek coast. The Athenians agreed not to invade any province of Artaxerxes, and the peace led them to withdraw from Cyprus.
