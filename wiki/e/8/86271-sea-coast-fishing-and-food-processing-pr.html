Abstract written by a model

use goat horns and rough stones [as fishing and cutting implements] The Ichthyophages use natural coastal passages and heaps of stones as fish traps, catching smaller fish by hand and larger creatures with goat horns or broken stones. They sun-fry their catch in stone pots, remove the flesh from the bones, boil it with Paliurus seed, and dry the resulting mass on tiles.
