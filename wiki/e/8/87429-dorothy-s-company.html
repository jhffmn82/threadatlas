Dorothy's company in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Dorothy's party is fastened to a parasol by ropes of varying lengths, flies upward through a passage, crashes and tumbles, returns to Oz, then is swallowed by earth after Dorothy's final wish.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 22

Dorothy's party flies upward through the passage, with each adventurer fastened to the parasol by a rope of different length. The members bump against the sides of the tube, and after the parasol crashes into something, they fall head over heels before rising again.
