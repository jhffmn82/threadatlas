Abstract written by a model

has_strength eighty-three thousand men [seventy thousand from Asia and thirteen thousand Greek mercenaries] Cyrus's army consisted of about seventy thousand men from Asia, thirteen thousand Greek mercenaries, and additional forces gathered during the march. It crossed the Euphrates and fought Artaxerxes's much larger army, but lost its leader and was ultimately defeated, although the Greek contingent remained victorious in its own engagements.
