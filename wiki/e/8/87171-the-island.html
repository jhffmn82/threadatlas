the island in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A round island, five thousand furlongs around, lies under the equator. It has equal days and nights, no noon shadow, a moderate climate, a rough tidal sea of fresh water, and many unusual plants, animals, and springs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The island was round and measured five thousand furlongs in circumference. It lay under the equator, had equal days and nights, no noon shadow, a rough surrounding sea with fresh water, and many unusual plants, animals, and springs.
