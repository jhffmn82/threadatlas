Abstract written by a model

Graph structures represent interdependencies and interrelationships among entities. Incorporated into LightRAG’s text indexing and retrieval with vector representations, they enable retrieval of related entities, relationships, and neighboring nodes in local subgraphs. From representing connections to supplying structural information, they improve retrieval comprehensiveness while preserving contextual relevance and supporting coherent, contextually rich responses.
