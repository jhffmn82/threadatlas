Captain Scott in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Captain Scott, a captain in charge of a raid, has newspaper correspondents arrested, declares they are under arrest, orders them taken to the station-house, and refuses release because they were under bonds to keep the peace and had fought again.

Captain Scott declares that the newspaper correspondents are under arrest and orders them taken to the station-house. He refuses to release them because they had recently been put under bonds to keep the peace and had been fighting again.
