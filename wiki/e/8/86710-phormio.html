Phormio

Abstract written by a model

Phormio succeeds Callias as Athenian general after Callias is killed at Potidea, renews its siege, and faces prolonged resistance. Made admiral, he commands twenty ships at Naupactus, occupies Criseus to block the Lacedaemonians, wins at Rhion, captures ships, and drives their fleet toward Corinth.

Phormio is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Phormio succeeded Callias as Athenian general after Callias was killed at Potidea. He renewed the siege and pressed the defenders with continual assaults, but the city's resistance prolonged the siege.

CHAP. Vlf. /

Phormio was made Athenian admiral and sailed with twenty ships to Naupactus, where he blocked the Lacedaemonian fleet. At Rhion he defeated a fleet of forty-seven ships, sank the admiral's vessel, captured twelve ships, and later fought the Lacedaemonians again until they withdrew toward Corinth.
