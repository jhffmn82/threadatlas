Beroe in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Beroe, Doryclus’s venerable aged wife, is ill and misses sacred rites; Iris assumes her appearance to deceive Trojan matrons.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thus far the sacred sports they celebrate:

Beroe is identified as Doryclus’s wife and an aged woman who is unable to attend the rites because of illness. Iris takes Beroe’s appearance to deceive the Trojan matrons.
