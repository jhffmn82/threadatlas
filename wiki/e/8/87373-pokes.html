Pokes in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Pokes are sleepy, slow-moving inhabitants of Pokes kingdom, ruled by the Chief Poker and unable to live outside it. They dislike noise and music, play croquet slowly, and arrest Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion. Sir Hokus's songs and later loud singing frighten them away; after a Poke puts Dorothy to sleep, the Cowardly Lion scatters them by dragging the travelers through the city gates.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

The Pokes are slow-moving, sleepy people who creep along, speak slowly, and dislike noise and music because it wakes them. They arrest Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion, but Sir Hokus's songs frighten them away from the hall.

CHAPTER 6

The Pokes flee when Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion sing loudly, but they follow the travelers when Dorothy stops singing. A Poke with cotton in his ears gives Dorothy hokey pokey, causing her to fall asleep, and the Chief Poker approaches with a rope. The Pokes are scattered when the Cowardly Lion drags Sir Hokus and Dorothy through the city gates.
