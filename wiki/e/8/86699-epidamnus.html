Epidamnus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A colony of Corcyra and Corinth, Epidamnus is disputed as Corinth’s metropolis; civil conflict brings expulsions, an Illyrian-backed siege, and appeals to Corcyra then Corinth.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Epidamnus suffered civil disturbances in which the stronger faction expelled many members of the opposing faction. The exiles allied with the Illyrians, seized the surrounding country, and besieged the city, leading the inhabitants to seek aid first from Corcyra and then from Corinth.
