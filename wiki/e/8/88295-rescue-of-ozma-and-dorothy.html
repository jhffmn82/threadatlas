rescue of Ozma and Dorothy in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The rescue of Ozma and Dorothy is an urgent effort agreed upon by the council. Glinda the Good, the Wizard, and companions travel to the Skeezer country, but cannot enter the sunken island; they study magic, seek another entrance, and encourage the girls in the Great Dome.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

The council agrees that Ozma and Dorothy must be rescued as soon as possible. Its members decide to travel to the Skeezer country, led by Glinda and the Wizard, to find a way to save them.

Chapter Twenty

The rescue of Ozma and Dorothy remains incomplete because Glinda and the Wizard cannot yet enter the sunken island. They continue studying magic, searching for another entrance, and signaling encouragement to the two girls, who remain safe inside the Great Dome.
