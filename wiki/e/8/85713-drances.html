Drances

Abstract written by a model

Drances is a rich, factious Latian lord and chief ambassador for Laurentum. Hating and politically opposing Turnus, he supports peace with Troy, praises Aeneas, blames Turnus for bloodshed, urges Lavinia’s marriage to Aeneas, and presses Turnus to fight or leave. Turnus denounces him as courageous only in council.

Drances is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK XI

Drances, who hates Turnus, praises Aeneas’s justice and strength and suggests that Turnus should leave the realm. He later aggravates the Latian accusations against Turnus by saying that Aeneas expects and dares him to fight.

Thus, full of anxious thought, he summons all

Drances speaks against Turnus, blaming him for the bloodshed and urging the king to give Lavinia to Aeneas as the basis for peace. Turnus answers by accusing Drances of being brave only in council and cowardly in the field.
