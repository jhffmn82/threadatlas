Kabumpo in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922 · the work’s page · read it where Kabumpo first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kabumpo is Pumperdink’s only elephant, its Elegant Elephant, and a favored member of the royal family. Splendidly dressed in robes, pearls, and jewels, he has royal quarters, an attendant named Spezzle, and deep devotion to Prince Pompadore, whom he loves, advises, guards, and carries. Mischievous as well as formidable, he steals feast food, blows out birthday candles and explodes the cake, frightens enemies with his trumpet, and can be impatient, proud, and easily angered by indignity. Kabumpo fiercely opposes Pompadore’s arranged marriage to Faleero, whom he considers unsuitable, and insists that Ozma is the Proper Princess. He helps Pompadore flee Pumperdink through a secret entrance, taking jewels, a magic mirror, and a gold door knob. Their journey leads through Rith Metic, where Kabumpo helps Pompadore escape and anxiously follows him; the Illumi Nation, where he damages his robe and tail but returns them safely to the surface; and the Soup Sea and Emerald City. Learning that Ozma has been taken to Ev, he urges her rescue and travels onward with Pompadore. Along the way Kabumpo meets Peg Amy and Wag. Though he first laughs at Peg Amy and quarrels with Wag, he becomes protective and caring, comforts Peg Amy, gives her a pearl necklace, promises to help her gain a Court position, and fights to protect the travelers from Twigs. Captured by the Runaway Country, he refuses to be used as a laborer and demands proper food. He helps seek Ozma and confront Ruggedo’s threat, while also trying to arrange a chance for Pompadore to ask Ozma to marry him. Ozma refuses Pompadore, disappointing Kabumpo, but he continues with his friends. He safeguards the magic mirror and gold door knob, refuses to proceed without Peg Amy, helps enter the gold castle, and rejoices when the mirror reveals Peg Amy’s identity. By the end, Peg Amy recognizes him as one of the friends who helped free her, and Kabumpo travels on with Pompadore.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

14 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Kabumpo is Pumperdink's only elephant and an elegant, favored member of the royal family. He mischievously steals food and lemonade during the feast, blows out the eighteen birthday candles, and thereby causes the royal birthday cake to explode.

Chapter 2

Kabumpo questions the plan to marry Pompadore to Faleero, calling her a thousand years old and ugly, and suggests that Pompadore run away. He also points out that the door knob and mirror may be connected with the scroll and learns from the mirror that he is an Elegant Elephant.

Chapter 3

Kabumpo dresses in his purple plush robe and pearls, gathers his jewels, and takes a gold door knob and a mirror for his disappearance. At night, he removes Pompadore’s crown, carries the sleeping Prince out of the palace, and escapes with him through a secret entrance.

Chapter 4

Kabumpo secretly carries Prince Pompadore away from Pumperdink to prevent him from being married to Faleero. He brings jewels, a magic mirror, and a door knob, then carries Pompadore toward the Emerald City while becoming increasingly irritated by the Curious Cottabus.

Chapter 5

Kabumpo enters Rith Metic with Pompadore and frightens the Figure Heads into silence with a tremendous trumpet. He carries out the addition by physically carrying the figures, helps Pompadore escape by urging him to somersault, and then searches anxiously when Pompadore disappears through the marble slab.

Chapter 11

Kabumpo knocks on the marble stone and falls into the Illumi Nation while trying to follow Pompadore. He accidentally blows out King Cheer and several Candlemen, loses the fringe from his robe and scorches his tail, but lifts his snuffer and leads Pompadore back to the surface.

Chapter 12

Kabumpo carries Prince Pompadore to the Soup Sea, helps him eat from it, and then carries him through the night to the Emerald City. After learning that Ozma has been taken to Ev, he urges Pompa to rescue her, takes a sword from beside the sleeping Soldier with the Green Whiskers, and sets out with him for Ev.

Chapter 13

Kabumpo catches up with Peg Amy and Wag while traveling with Prince Pompadore. He laughs at Peg Amy, quarrels with Wag, and later apologizes indirectly and offers to help defeat Ruggedo. He joins the others on their journey and carries Prince Pompadore.

Chapter 16

Kabumpo comforts Peg Amy about her strange memories and helps her clean and repair his plush robe. He gives her a pearl necklace and resolves to help her obtain a position at Court. During the Twig attack, he fights fiercely with his trunk, protects the travelers, and leads them to safety.

Chapter 17

Kabumpo is captured by the Runaway Country, which expects him to draw logs and help develop the land. He objects angrily to being treated like a slave and demands food, including a ton of hay a day. After entering Ev, he is thrown from the Country, sees the giant, helps move Wag away, and searches for the missing Magic Box.

Chapter 18

Kabumpo repeatedly stops Wag from disturbing Ruggedo and worries about saving Ozma before Glinda’s magic succeeds. He directs the use of the Question Box and tries to secure Pompadore an opportunity to ask Ozma to marry him.

Chapter 19

Kabumpo is disappointed when Ozma refuses to marry Prince Pompadore and briefly threatens to leave. He accompanies Pompadore, Peg Amy, and Wag on the planned journey to the Winkie Country.

Chapter 20

Kabumpo worries about the magic articles taken by Ozma and keeps the Magic Mirror and gold door knob safe. He refuses to travel without Peg Amy, uses the gold door knob to enter the gold castle, and is present when the Magic Mirror reveals Peg Amy’s identity.

Chapter 21

Kabumpo is recognized by Peg Amy as one of the friends responsible for her release. Peg Amy keeps the necklace and bracelet he gave her when he thought she was a wooden doll, and he celebrates her recovery before traveling with Pompadore.

Kabumpo in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925 · the work’s page · read it where Kabumpo first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kabumpo is the Elegant Elephant of Pumperdink’s royal family, a chief court ornament, faithful servant of Prince Pompadore, and one of Pompadore’s favorites. Gigantic, elegant, pompous, and magnificently dressed in pearls and velvet robes, he has rooms in the palace and later receives an emerald head-piece and silver robe. He helps rescue Peg Amy with Pompa and travels toward the Emerald City seeking excitement and trying to arrive before Mombi. After initially reacting angrily when Humpy strikes him and disapproving of shabby companions, he listens to Dorothy and joins her effort against Mombi and to find the lost King of Oz. He carries Dorothy, Humpy, Mombi, and the others, clears paths with trumpeting, pursues Mombi, rescues Humpy, and restrains and guards Mombi through the palace’s fall and restoration. At the end, he leads the grand procession with the King and Humpy on his back, then carries General Whiffenpuff, the Invisible Cook, and Snip toward Kimbaloo before returning to Pumperdink.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Kabumpo is an elegant elephant from the royal family of Pumperdink and a favorite of Pompadore. After Humpy strikes him, Kabumpo throws the dummy into the air but stops attacking when Dorothy explains the situation. He agrees to take Dorothy and Humpy toward the Emerald City on his back.

CHAPTER 15

Kabumpo returns after searching for lunch and initially objects to Dorothy associating with Snip and Tora because he considers them shabby. After Dorothy explains Mombi's threat, he becomes interested in helping find the lost King of Oz and carries the entire group toward the Emerald City. He travels rapidly, trumpeting to clear a path.

CHAPTER 16

Kabumpo stops abruptly before a steep rocky barrier, sending everyone over his head. He follows Mombi when she tries to take Humpy away, rescues Humpy from her, and carries her back to the others while holding her in his trunk. He agrees to continue toward the Emerald City while still holding Mombi.

CHAPTER 17

Kabumpo carries Mombi, Humpy, and the others to the Emerald City and is disappointed that no court is present to witness his grand entry. He restrains Mombi after Pajuka warns that she may steal Ozma's magic, and Mombi takes two buttons from his robe for her spell.

CHAPTER 19

Kabumpo survives the palace's fall, seizes Mombi when she tries to escape, and shakes her angrily. He guards Mombi while Dorothy makes her try to repair the damage, then is knocked over again when the castle rises.

CHAPTER 21

The Elegant Elephant leads the grand procession with the King and Humpy riding on his back. He later agrees to take General Whiffenpuff, the Invisible Cook, and Snip back to Kimbaloo, and leaves after lunch to return to Pumperdink.
