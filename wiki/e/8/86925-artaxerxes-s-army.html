Artaxerxes's army

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: his army, king's army, king's forces, king's side, the Persian army, whole army

Abstract written by a model

Artaxerxes's army, an army assembled from many regions, was estimated by Ephorus at at least four hundred thousand horse and foot. It first lost to the Grecians, then, after Cyrus's death, recovered, plundered his camp, defeated his main forces, attacked stragglers under Tissaphernes, and pursued the Greeks to the Carduchi without facing their whole army.

Artaxerxes's army is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. V-

Artaxerxes's army was assembled from many regions and was estimated by Ephorus at no fewer than four hundred thousand horse and foot. It initially suffered defeat against the Grecians, but after Cyrus was killed it recovered, plundered Cyrus's camp, and defeated his main forces.
