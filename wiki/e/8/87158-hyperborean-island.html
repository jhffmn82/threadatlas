Abstract written by a model

Hyperborean island is an island opposite Gaul under the Arctic pole, as large as Sicily, with rich soil, temperate climate, two annual crops, Apollo’s sacred grove, round temple, and consecrated city.

Hyperborean island is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Hyperborean island lay in the ocean opposite Gaul and was said to be as large as Sicily. It had rich soil, a temperate climate, a sacred grove, a round temple to Apollo, and a city consecrated to that god.
