Wish Way in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Wish Way is a narrow, tree-lined road marked by a sign. The travelers follow it rather than turn back and learn that every wish made there comes true. Dorothy wishes to be with the Scarecrow, causing her and the others to fall to Silver Island.
