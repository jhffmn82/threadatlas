ClimateGPT in SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs

Darío Garigliotti · 2024-08-16

ClimateGPT is a climate-focused family of language models, fine-tuned from corresponding Llama2 models on climate-change documents for climate-aware natural language processing. ClimateGPT-13b generates answers and, in SDG tasks, favors dense retrieval for evidence identification but performs best with lexical retrieval for target detection, dominating recall with SDG-explicit prompts.

ClimateGPT is a family of LLMs obtained by fine-tuning a corresponding Llama2 model on climate-change-domain documents. ClimateGPT-13b is used for answer generation.

ClimateGPT-13b often favors dense retrieval in SDG Evidence Identification but consistently performs best with lexical retrieval in SDG Target Detection. It dominates recall for SDG Target Detection with SDG-explicit prompts and improves relative to Llama2-13b when two examples are used in some configurations.
