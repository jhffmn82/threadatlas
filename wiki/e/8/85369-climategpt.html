Abstract written by a model

ClimateGPT is a climate-focused family of language models, fine-tuned from corresponding Llama2 models on climate-change documents for climate-aware natural language processing. ClimateGPT-13b generates answers and, in SDG tasks, favors dense retrieval for evidence identification but performs best with lexical retrieval for target detection, dominating recall with SDG-explicit prompts.

ClimateGPT is in one work on this site, SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

ClimateGPT-13b often favors dense retrieval in SDG Evidence Identification but consistently performs best with lexical retrieval in SDG Target Detection.
