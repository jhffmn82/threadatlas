Abstract written by a model

The journey to Ev is Prince Pompadore’s long expedition to save Pumperdink through his marriage. Peg Amy travels with Wag, who carries her, while Kabumpo carries Pompadore. Peg Amy and Wag set out first through the Winkies’ country; after joining Kabumpo and Pompadore, the four move through farmlands and rough hills toward Ev, but a Twig attack interrupts them. The Runaway Country then carries them across the Deadly Desert on its ten mountain feet and into Ev, where its fear of a giant makes it flee backward and scatter the travelers. Later, Ozma orders the Runaway Country to carry all four back across the Deadly Desert to the Winkie Country.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The journey to Ev is described as quite long and requiring travel across the Deadly Desert. Wag carries Peg Amy on his back and begins the trip by hopping west through the Winkies’ country, covering twelve feet with each hop.

Chapter 13

Peg Amy and Wag begin the journey to Ev ahead of Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore. After meeting them, the four travelers agree to continue together, with Wag carrying Peg Amy and Kabumpo carrying Prince Pompadore.

Chapter 16

Kabumpo, Peg Amy, Prince Pompadore, and Wag travel swiftly through the Winkie farmlands and then through rougher, hillier country. They expect to reach Ev by evening, but their progress is interrupted by the Twig attack.

Chapter 17

The Runaway Country carries Peg Amy, Pompa, Kabumpo, and Wag across the Deadly Desert while moving on its ten mountain feet. After Peg persuades it to continue toward supplies and the travelers near the border of Ev, it crosses into the kingdom and heads toward a tall purple mountain. Seeing the giant there, it abruptly runs backward and throws the travelers into the surrounding landscape.

Chapter 19

Ozma orders the Runaway Country to carry Prince Pompadore, Kabumpo, Peg Amy, and Wag across the Deadly Desert and set them down in the Winkie Country. In exchange, she promises to restore the Country's moving power and provide it with a little gnome for King.
