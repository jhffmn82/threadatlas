Abstract written by a model

Lady Frances Carfax’s old Spanish jewellery, made of silver and unusually cut diamonds, is valuable and cherished enough that she carries it rather than leaving it with her banker. It becomes the motive for her deception and captivity; its separate pendants pawned at Bovington’s link Henry Peters and Mrs. Peters to her disappearance.
