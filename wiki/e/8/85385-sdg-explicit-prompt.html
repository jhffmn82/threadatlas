Abstract written by a model

The SDG-explicit prompt frames queries or candidates as Sustainable Development Goal targets and requests relevant context or identifiers. Used in SDG Evidence Identification, its results vary with passage order and retrieval method; GPT-3.5 and ClimateGPT-13b lead different tasks.

SDG-explicit prompt is in one work on this site, SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
