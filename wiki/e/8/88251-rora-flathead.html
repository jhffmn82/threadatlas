Rora Flathead in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Rora Flathead, Su-dic's wife and a Flathead witch, had four cans of brains and magic once greater than Su-dic's. After the Skeezers barred Flathead fishing, she tried to poison their lake's fish. She also seized Adepts' magical tools to become a witch. Queen Coo-ee-oh transformed her into a Golden Pig and took her brains.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Rora Flathead was the Su-dic's wife and had four cans of brains, which made her a powerful witch. She tried to poison the fish in the Skeezers' lake after the Skeezers forbade Flathead fishing, but Queen Coo-ee-oh transformed her into a Golden Pig and took away her cans of brains.

Chapter Nine

Rora seized some of the Adepts' magical tools and used them to become a witch. She tried to poison all the fishes in the lake, and Coo-ee-oh transformed her into a Golden Pig.

Golden Pig in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Golden Pig is Rora Flathead, formerly the wicked wife of Su-dic, transformed by Queen Coo-ee-oh after she tried to poison all the fishes in the lake. Its solid-gold body has ruby eyes and polished ivory teeth, but it lacks brains and has lost and forgotten magic. It grunts that Flatheads should fight Skeezers; afterward, Su-dic says it cannot make more poison and it remains a Golden Pig.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Golden Pig is Rora Flathead after Queen Coo-ee-oh's transformation. It has a solid gold body, ruby eyes, and ivory teeth, and it grunts that the Flatheads should fight the Skeezers.

Chapter Nine

The Golden Pig is the form Coo-ee-oh gave Rora after Rora tried to poison all the fishes in the lake.

Chapter Eleven

Rora remains a Golden Pig after Queen Coo-ee-oh's earlier enchantment. Because she has become a Pig and forgotten her magic, the Su-dic says she cannot make more poison.
