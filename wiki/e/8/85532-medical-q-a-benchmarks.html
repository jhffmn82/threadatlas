Medical Q&A benchmarks were used to evaluate MedGraphRAG. The evaluation included 9 such benchmarks, on which MedGraphRAG consistently outperformed state-of-the-art models.

Introduction

Medical Q&A benchmarks were used to evaluate Medical GraphRAG across nine tasks. The reported results show that Medical GraphRAG materially outperformed classic retrieval-augmented generation and GraphRAG and surpassed many specifically trained language models on medical corpora.
