is_a wall [built by Demosthenes around naturally fortified Pylos] Demosthenes built a wall around Pylos in twenty days because the site was naturally strong and well supplied. The fortification survived repeated Lacedaemonian assaults and became the base from which the Athenians controlled the sea and threatened Laconia.
