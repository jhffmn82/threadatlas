Abstract written by a model

The RSNA Case Collection is a peer-reviewed, internationally sourced radiological case collection. It provided 80 cases across radiologic subspecialties; clinical histories and image characteristics from descriptions and captions were used to create RSNA-RadioQA questions, excluding images and provided differential diagnoses, to evaluate language-model performance.
