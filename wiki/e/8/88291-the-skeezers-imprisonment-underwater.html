the Skeezers' imprisonment underwater in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Skeezers’ imprisonment underwater is their entrapment, with Ozma, Dorothy, and Lady Aurex, beneath the lake in the Great Dome on the sunken Magic Isle. Coo-ee-oh’s unavailable magic leaves boats and doors unusable; opening the dome would flood it. Glinda rejects rescuing only a few, so all remain trapped.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twelve

The Skeezers, their island, Ozma, Dorothy, and Lady Aurex are trapped beneath the lake in the glass dome. Opening the dome would flood it, and the available boats and doors cannot be used because only Coo-ee-oh knew their magic.

Chapter Seventeen

The Skeezers are trapped on the submerged Magic Isle, while Ozma and Dorothy are also held beneath the Great Dome. Glinda rejects a plan that would rescue only a few prisoners because Ozma would not abandon the rest of her subjects or their lawful home.
