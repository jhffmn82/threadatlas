Hade in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Hade, Richard F. Burrbank’s secretary, is suspected of Burrbank’s murder and the safe burglary on circumstantial evidence. After Burrbank’s death, he disappears, hiding near Torresdale as Mr. Carleton and using the alias Arlie Lane. Missing the right trigger finger shot off in childhood, he attends a fight in disguise, fearing Keppler and the risk of staying away. During a police raid, Hefflefinger seizes him; Hade offers money and part of his fortune for release, but is identified as the murderer, arrested, and captured.

RAEGEN THE OTHER WOMAN THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Stephen S. Hade was Richard F. Burrbank's secretary and is regarded as the murderer based on circumstantial evidence. He disappeared after Burrbank's death and the theft from the safe, and Gallegher believes he has found Hade hiding near Torresdale under the name Mr. Carleton.

Hade attends the fight in disguise, anxious because he fears Keppler and the danger of remaining away. During the police raid, Hefflefinger seizes him and threatens to reveal his real identity, while Hade offers money and a share of his fortune in exchange for release. He is identified as the murderer and is taken under the supposed name of the burglar “Arlie” Lane, alias Carleton.
