Abe Hubbard in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Abe Hubbard, also known as Jimmie the Gent, is an escaped burglar traced to New York. A newspaper identifies him as the burglar whom Van Bibber helped after Van Bibber went to Jersey City to see a fight.
