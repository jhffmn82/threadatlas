Brenda Tregennis in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Brenda Tregennis, Mortimer Tregennis’s sister, loves Dr. Leon Sterndale. After playing cards with her brothers and Mortimer at Tredannick Wartha, she is found dead in her chair, horrified and uninjured, from the family’s mental affliction. Sterndale says Mortimer killed her with devil’s-foot poison, prompting his revenge.
