Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI

The infant survives the nurse's attempts to remove him from Canace's womb and is born after Canace's difficult labor. Aeolus discovers him, orders him exposed in a solitary place, and the child is destined to be torn apart by wild beasts.
