the King of the Middlings in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The King of the Middlings, monarch of the Middling Kingdom, wears a tall mud crown and stops the Scarecrow, takes an emerald toll, questions his work, then considers coating him in mud before Muddle releases him.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The King of the Middlings stops the Scarecrow in the passageway, accepts an emerald as his toll, and questions whether the Scarecrow can work. He considers giving the Scarecrow a coat of mud before Muddle releases him.
