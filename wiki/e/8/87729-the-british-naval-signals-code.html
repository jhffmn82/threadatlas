His Last Bow: The War Service of Sherlock Holmes

The British naval signals code is a major target of Von Bork’s espionage. The Admiralty has changed every code after apparently detecting the earlier theft, and Altamont claims to deliver a copy of the updated semaphore, lamp-code, and Marconi signals, though the parcel is actually a decoy.
