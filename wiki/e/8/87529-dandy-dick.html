Abstract written by a model

Dandy Dick is a sporting racehorse owned jointly by Sir Tristram Mardon and Georgiana Tidman. Wearing black-and-white colors with a pink cap and ridden by Tom Gallawood, he is entered in the Durnstone Handicap, also called Dandy Dick’s handicap. Sir Tristram considers him fit and properly weighted, while Georgiana, confident from knowing him thoroughly, buys a half share and bets on him. After a fire at the Swan Inn, where he had been stabled, he is rescued safe but cold and with a singed, later reported lost, tail. He is sheltered in the Deanery stables, feeds steadily, and is treated cautiously for a possible chill while remaining a favored contender, ahead of Bonny Betsy according to the Dean. His prospects are endangered when Blore secretly adds strychnine to his bolus; reports then reveal that he was being nobbled and had been dosed, though the chemist’s analysis is pending. Despite these dangers, Dandy Dick runs and wins the Races in a common canter, after the Dean has instructed Blore to back him for the Restoration Fund.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

Dandy Dick is Sir Tristram's racehorse, entered in the Durnstone Handicap with Tom Gallawood up. Sir Tristram says the horse is fit, shining, and not carrying too much or too little weight, while Georgiana declares that she knows the horse thoroughly and expects him to win. Georgiana reveals that she owns half of him and decides to bet on him.

THE SECOND ACT.

Dandy Dick is rescued from the burning Swan Inn safe and sound, but with part of his tail singed. He is being run up and down the lane to get warm after cold water was thrown over him, and Georgiana plans to put him in the Deanery stables.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

Dandy Dick is sheltered in the Deanery stables after the fire at the Swan and is reported to be feeding steadily. He has lost his tail and may have suffered a chill, but Georgiana and others expect him to compete in the handicap race. Unbeknownst to the Dean, Blore adds strychnine to the bolus prepared for him.
