burning of the Trojan ships

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Turnus bears the devouring torches, burn the sea, burning of the Trojan fleet, fires, flames were burning, hissing brands, set on fire, the Trojan ships, the fir’d pines, the flame, the ship

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, it is an attack by the Rutulians in which Turnus takes fire to the Trojan fleet and the Rutulians set the ships ablaze; the threatened destruction is prevented when the ships transform into sea nymphs and escape into the water. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Turnus carries torches against Aeneas’s pine-built ships, and the flames burn the pitch, wax, masts, sails, and benches; Cybele intervenes before the fleet is destroyed.
