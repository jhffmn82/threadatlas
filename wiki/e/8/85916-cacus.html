Cacus

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, Cacus is Vulcan’s fire-breathing robber and monster who inhabits a cave, steals Hercules’s cattle, hides their tracks, and is exposed, strangled, and displayed by Hercules. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Cacus is a huge giant who lives in a cave on Mount Aventine, plunders the surrounding country, steals some of Geryon's oxen by dragging them backward by their tails, and is discovered and slain by Hercules after the hidden cattle begin to low.
