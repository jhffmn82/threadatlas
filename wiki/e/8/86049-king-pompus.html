King Pompus

Abstract written by a model

King Pompus is the proud, fat, old King of Pumperdink, husband of Queen Pozy and father of Prince Pompadore. He rules through the dipping punishment, rewarding right conduct and punishing wrongdoing. After a birthday cake explodes, he orders footmen dipped and learns that Pompadore must marry the Proper Fairy Princess or Pumperdink will disappear. Fearing this fate, he chooses Princess Faleero as the Proper Princess, orders their wedding, and has the castle watched for disappearance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Pompus is the proud King of Pumperdink and the father of Prince Pompadore. After the royal birthday cake explodes, he orders Hashem, Eejabo, and the other footmen dipped, then learns from the scroll that Prince Pompadore must marry the Proper Fairy Princess or Pumperdink will disappear.

Chapter 2

King Pompus worries that he, his family, and Pumperdink may disappear and decides that Prince Pompadore must marry a Proper Princess immediately. He orders Pompadore to marry Princess Faleero, dismisses the mysteries of the gold door knob and mirror, and commands that the castle be guarded for signs of disappearance.
