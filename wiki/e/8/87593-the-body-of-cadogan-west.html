Abstract written by a model

Cadogan West’s body is found near Aldgate Station beside the Metropolitan train tracks, with a crushed bone, little external bleeding, and no ticket. It was killed inside Oberstein’s house, then placed on the roof of an Underground train. Disturbed soot and blood on the window and stair support Holmes’s conclusion that it fell from the carriage roof.
