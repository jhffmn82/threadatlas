Egyptian legal procedure uses detailed written pleadings, with plaintiff and defendant answering each point through reply and rejoinder before judges deliberate and the president indicates judgment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Egyptian legal procedure required the plaintiff to submit a detailed written complaint, after which the defendant answered each point in writing. The plaintiff replied and the defendant rejoined, and the judges then deliberated before the president indicated the judgment.
