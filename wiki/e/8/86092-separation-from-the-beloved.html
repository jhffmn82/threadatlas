separation from the beloved

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: cum domina, sine te
  • In Heroides and Amores: separates Ovid [from the beloved] Separation places the speaker away from the woman who causes his passion. It makes Sulmo and his father's country seem like Scythia, the fierce Cilicians, the green Britons, and rocks reddened by Promethean blood. The beloved had sworn to remain his companion, but the speaker says that girls' promises are often carried away by wind and water.
