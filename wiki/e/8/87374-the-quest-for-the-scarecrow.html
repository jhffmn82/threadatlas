the Quest for the Scarecrow in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Quest for the Scarecrow is undertaken and conducted by Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion. It begins when Sir Hokus joins Dorothy and the Lion in leaving Pokes; they sing to stay awake, travel toward the Emerald City, and seek information from King Fix It, who knows only a picture of the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 5

The Quest for the Scarecrow begins when Sir Hokus asks to accompany Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion. The travelers plan to leave Pokes and must sing to remain awake while pursuing the quest.

CHAPTER 12

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion continue searching for the Scarecrow while traveling toward the Emerald City. They ask King Fix It about him, but learn only that he has seen a Scarecrow in a picture.
