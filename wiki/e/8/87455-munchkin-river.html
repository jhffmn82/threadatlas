Munchkin River in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

A broad blue river in Munchkin Country lacks a ferry, difficult to cross for the Scarecrow until the A-B-Sea Serpent forms a bridge.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 2

The river is broad and blue and has no ferry. Because water does not agree with the Scarecrow, crossing it is difficult until the A-B-Sea Serpent stretches across it to form a bridge.
