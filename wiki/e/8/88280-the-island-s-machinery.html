the island's machinery in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

A system of cog-wheels, chains, and pulleys lacking visible motive power, it operates submarine rooms, their doors and pumps, and the bridge to the mainland, rather than controlling the island.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Two

The machinery consists of cog-wheels, chains, and pulleys with no visible engine or other motive power. The three Adepts determine that it operates the submarine rooms, their doors and pumps, and the bridge extending to the mainland, rather than controlling the island.
