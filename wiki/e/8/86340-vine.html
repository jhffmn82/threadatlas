vine in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Vine is a plant naturally produced by earth. It shoots branches and bears grapes, including wild grapes without cultivation; its growth, fruiting, destruction, pruning, and renewed flourishing were represented through Bacchus’s births, dismemberment, boiling, and revival.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Atlantides, and the greatest mountain in the world Atlas, after his

The vine was said to have been naturally produced by the earth and not originally planted or discovered by anyone, as shown by wild vines that bore grapes without cultivation. In mythological interpretations, its growth, fruiting, destruction, pruning, and renewed flourishing were represented through the births, dismemberment, boiling, and revival of Bacchus.
