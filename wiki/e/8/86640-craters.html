CHAP. XXIV.

The Craters were deep hollows in the ground at the temple of the Palici from which fire and water were said to burst upward with sulfurous fumes and terrifying noise. They were treated as a divine wonder, and the sacred site was used for oaths whose violation was believed to bring punishment.
