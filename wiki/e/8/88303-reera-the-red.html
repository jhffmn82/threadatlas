Reera the Red in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Reera the Red is a powerful, unsociable Yookoohoo who lives alone in her cottage, changes form with magic powder, and uses magic chiefly for selfish amusement rather than helping others. Her real form is initially unknown, though her natural form is a lovely young woman with bright auburn-red hair. She likes unpopular creatures such as toads, lizards, and rats, grants no favors, and is not known to harm anyone. Initially appearing as a gray ape, she changes forms, transforms creatures, and shares a meal with Ervic. Interested by his lack of fear, she ultimately turns three fish into young women and Adepts, then asks them and Ervic to save the Skeezer people without returning to or revealing her cottage.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

Reera the Red is a powerful Yookoohoo who uses magic for her own amusement and initially appears as a gray ape. She changes into a young woman, transforms several creatures, shares a meal with Ervic, and becomes interested in his unusual, unafraid behavior.

Chapter Nineteen

Reera feeds her pets, transforms a wolf into a singing bird, and later transforms the three fish into beautiful young women and Adepts at Magic. She then assumes her natural form, a lovely young woman with bright auburn-red hair, and asks the Adepts and Ervic to save the Skeezer people while never returning to or revealing her cottage.
