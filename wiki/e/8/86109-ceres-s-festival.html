Ceres’s festival in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

At Ceres’s annual festival, honoring Ceres and associated with Egyptian sistrum, the beloved must abstain and sleep separately; the speaker protests love, songs, and wine excluded.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX

Ceres’s annual festival requires the speaker’s beloved woman to remain alone in a separate bed. The speaker objects that a festival should welcome love, songs, and wine rather than impose separation.
