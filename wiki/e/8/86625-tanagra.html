Tanagra in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Tanagra is a place with temples to Dionysus, Themis, Aphrodite, Apollo, Artemis, Leto, and Hermes. Its people separate houses from temples, keep temples pure, greatly honor gods, and preserve traditions about Hermes, Corinna, and two kinds of cocks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXII.

Tanagra has temples to Dionysus, Themis, Aphrodite, Apollo, Artemis, Leto, and Hermes. Its people keep their houses and temples apart, place the temples in pure locations, honor their gods greatly, and preserve traditions about Hermes, Corinna, and two kinds of cocks.
