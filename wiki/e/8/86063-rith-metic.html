Rith Metic in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Rith Metic is a dry, choky kingdom ruled by the King of the Figure Heads, with arithmetic-book houses, sign-marked streets including Minus Alley, and inhabitants who require travelers to solve problems to pass.

Narrative, section by section

Chapter 5

Rith Metic is a dry, choky kingdom whose houses resemble enormous arithmetic books and whose streets bear plus, minus, and long division signs. Its inhabitants make travelers work through arithmetic problems before allowing them to pass.
