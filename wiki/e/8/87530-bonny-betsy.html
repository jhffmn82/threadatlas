Bonny Betsy in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

A horse handicap-race entrant loses the Races, finishing behind Dandy Dick with the policeman, after Blore plans to remove Dandy Dick but mistakenly wagers on her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

Bonny Betsy finished behind Dandy Dick and was described as walking in with the policeman. Blore had intended to remove Dandy Dick from the race so Bonny Betsy could win, and mistakenly placed the Dean's wager on her.
