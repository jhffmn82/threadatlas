Otus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Otus, son of Aloeus, joined Ephialtes to invade Strongyle, defeat and expel the Thracians, and rename it Dia; after they fought and died, Otus was adored as a demi-god.

Otus, sent by Aloeus to find Iphimedia and Pancratis, invaded Strongyle with Ephialtes. They defeated the Thracians, captured the city, later took possession of the island, expelled the Thracians, and renamed it Dia. Otus and Ephialtes subsequently fought one another and died in the battle.

Otus was fathered by Poseidon and Iphimedia and was called one of the Aloads. He wooed Artemis, helped threaten the gods, and was killed in Naxos when Artemis, disguised as a deer, caused him and Ephialtes to spear each other.
