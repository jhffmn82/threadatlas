Catana in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A Chalcidian city in Sicily, renamed Aetna by Hiero; its inhabitants defended lands, then defeat drove them to Etna as former inhabitants returned.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIX.

Catana's inhabitants defended lands assigned to them when Hiero established the colony. After being defeated by Deucetius and the Syracusans, they left Catana for Etna, while the former inhabitants returned.
