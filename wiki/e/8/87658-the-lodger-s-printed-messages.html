1 section; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

The lodger’s messages are short pencil-printed words such as “SOAP,” “MATCH,” and “DAILY GAZETTE.” Holmes concludes that printing conceals the writer’s handwriting and that the laconic wording may conceal a lack of English knowledge.
