An Ethiopian nation dwelling in large wooded forests, Elephantophages watch elephants from treetops, then hunt them by clinging to tails and cutting hind-leg sinews with sharp axes or by felling trees against which they sleep.

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

The Elephantophages live in large, wooded forests and watch elephants from the tops of trees. Some attack individual elephants by clinging to their tails and cutting the nerves and sinews of their hind legs, while others bring them down by sawing through trees against which the elephants sleep.
