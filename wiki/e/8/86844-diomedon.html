Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIV.

Diomedon was one of the condemned commanders and was described as an excellent soldier and a just man. Before his execution, he urged the Athenians to offer thanks to Jove, Apollo, and the glorious goddesses for the victory.
