capture of Oechalia

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Oechalia standing yet, Oechalia's fall, took the city
  • In Heroides and Amores, Oechalia's fall is treated as one of Hercules's recent military achievements, leading to Iole's captivity and Deianira's fear that Iole will become Hercules's wife.
  • In The Library, Hercules captures Oechalia with the aid of Arcadians, Melians from Trachis, and Epicnemidian Locrians, kills Eurytus and his sons, takes and pillages the city, and leads Iole away captive.
