the island societies in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The island societies are distinct kin-based groups of at most four hundred members, governed by the eldest under the island's king; members rotate occupations and public offices until old age.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The island societies were based on kinship and contained no more than four hundred members each. The eldest person governed each society, and members took turns in occupations and public offices, except when they were old.
