Motya in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Motya was an Agrigentine garrison captured by Deucetius after an Agrigentine relief force was defeated, and its castle was later retaken by Agrigentines. A Carthaginian colony on an island near Sicily, it was a strong castle and inlet with a narrow harbor. Attacked by Himiico's fleet, it then endured Dionysius's siege, breach, capture, sack, and a Sicilian garrison.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XXVIII.

Motya was an Agrigentine garrison that Deucetius besieged and captured after defeating an Agrigentine relief force. The Agrigentines later retook its castle, which contained a garrison belonging to Deucetius.

CHAP. VII.

Motya was a strong Carthaginian colony situated on an island near Sicily and served as an important castle and inlet. It resisted Dionysius while expecting aid from Carthage, and Dionysius returned with his army to besiege it.

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

Motya was attacked by Himiico's fleet and later subjected to a prolonged siege by Dionysius. Its walls were breached after repeated battering, and Dionysius's army captured and sacked the city before installing a garrison.
