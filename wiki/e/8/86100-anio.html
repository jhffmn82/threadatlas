Anio, a river god, sees grieving Ilia by his waters, rescues her from the river, offers refuge among his many nymphs, and marries her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI

Anio sees Ilia wandering barefoot and grieving beside his waters. He promises her refuge among his nymphs, rescues her when she throws herself into the river, and takes her as his bride.
