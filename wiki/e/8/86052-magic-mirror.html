Abstract written by a model

Magic Mirror is a truthful gold mirror found under a window. It displays descriptions of those who look into it, calling Kabumpo an Elegant Elephant, King Pompus a Fat Old King, Queen Pozy a Lovely Queen, and Prince Pompadore a Charming Prince. Later it identifies a woman on a golden bench as Glinda, the Good Sorceress of Oz, helping Prince Pompadore decide whether to ask her to marry. Finally, it reveals Peg Amy as Princess of Sun Top Mountain and the Proper Princess, followed by her transformation from a wooden doll into a living princess.

Magic Mirror is in one work on this site, Kabumpo in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

A gold mirror is found under a window and displays truthful descriptions of people who look into it. It identifies Kabumpo as an Elegant Elephant, King Pompus as a Fat Old King, Queen Pozy as a Lovely Queen, and Prince Pompadore as a Charming Prince.

Chapter 12

The magic mirror identifies the lovely woman on the golden bench as “Glinda, good Sorceress of Oz,” rather than Princess Ozma. Prince Pompadore uses it to determine whether she is the princess he should ask to marry.

Chapter 20

The Magic Mirror reveals that Peg Amy is Princess of Sun Top Mountain and then displays the words, "This is the Proper Princess." Its revelation is followed by Peg Amy’s transformation from a wooden doll into a living princess.
