Heliades in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Heliades are the seven sons of Sol and Rhode: Ochimus, Cercaphus, Macir, Actis, Tenages, Triopas, and Daedalus. They excel in learning, especially astrology, and first discover navigation and hours. After their brothers murder Tenages, the principal perpetrators and accomplices flee.

The Heliades were the seven sons of Sol and Rhode: Ochimus, Cercaphus, Macir, Actis, Tenages, Triopas, and Daedalus. They were credited with excellence in learning, astrology, navigation, and the division of the day into hours; after Tenages was murdered by his brothers, the principal perpetrators and their accomplices fled.
