Carbut's bakery store in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Carbut's bakery store is a bakery partly owned by apprentice Louis, who holds a third. Old Carbut offers the remainder for 20,000 francs so he can retire, but Louis's gambling jeopardizes the couple's purchase.
