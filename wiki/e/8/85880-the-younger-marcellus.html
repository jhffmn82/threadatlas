the younger Marcellus

Also called: godlike youth, youth

Abstract written by a model

The younger Marcellus, son of Marcellus, appears in shining armor yet gloomy and shadowed: a short-lived promise of glory. He briefly lives, dies young in the Martian field, and is mourned by the Tiber, admired after death as Trojan honour.

The younger Marcellus appears beside Marcellus in shining armor, but with gloomy eyes and a shadowed head. He will briefly appear on earth and then die young, causing great mourning and becoming a Roman and Trojan boast in death.
