System Ablation Study

Adaptive parameters are evaluated for their effect on performance, including their use in configurations with adaptive top-k selection.

Overall Results and Discussion

Adaptive parameters enhance the system's ability to provide accurate and relevant responses. Combining adaptive parameters with a reranker maximizes the proportion of correct responses.
