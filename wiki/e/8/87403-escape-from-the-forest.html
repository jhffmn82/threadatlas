CHAPTER 11

Bangladore snatches up Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and later the Cowardly Lion and drops them in a large field of daisies outside the forest. They identify the surrounding country as the Winkie Country, find a road, and continue hoping it will lead them toward information about the Scarecrow.
