Aldgate Station in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Aldgate Station is an Underground railway junction in London with a network of points. In The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans, it is near where Arthur Cadogan West’s body is found; Holmes, Watson, and Lestrade investigate a reported thud, the curve, and the points.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans

Aldgate Station is near the place where Arthur Cadogan West’s body was found on the left side of the eastbound track. Sherlock Holmes, Dr. Watson, and Lestrade plan to begin their investigation there.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

Aldgate Station is the location near which Cadogan West's body was discovered. A passenger reports hearing a heavy thud just before reaching the station, and the surrounding points and curve become central to Holmes's deduction.
