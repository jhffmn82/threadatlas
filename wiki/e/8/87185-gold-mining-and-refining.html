gold mining and refining in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Gold mining and refining in Egypt requires much labour: workers soften hard earth and rock with fire, break it with pickaxes and hammers, pound and grind it, then wash the powder with water to remove earthy and drossy matter. The gold is washed repeatedly, cleaned with sponges, weighed, and refined for five days and nights in clay-sealed earthen urns in a furnace with lead, salt, tin, and barley bran.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We sliall now speak a little of those inhabiting the southern parts>

Gold mining involves softening hard earth and rock with fire, breaking the rock with pickaxes and hammers, pounding and grinding it, and then washing the resulting powder. Refining includes repeated washing, removal of earthy matter with sponges, weighing, and heating the gold with lead, salt, tin, and barley bran in sealed pots.
