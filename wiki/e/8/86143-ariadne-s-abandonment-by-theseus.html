Ariadne's abandonment by Theseus

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Theseus's abandonment of Ariadne, abandoned, abandons her, barbarously deserts his companion, betrayed me, deserted, prodita sum, treason, without me

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Theseus abandons Ariadne on the shore while she sleeps, taking his ship without her; Ariadne searches, calls, and signals for him before facing isolation, exile, and the possibility of death. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Theseus carries Ariadne away after escaping the Labyrinth but barbarously deserts her on Dia, identified with Naxos.
