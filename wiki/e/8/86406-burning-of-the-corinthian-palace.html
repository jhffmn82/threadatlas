Abstract written by a model

is_caused_by Medea [by witchcraft] After being ordered to leave Corinth, Medea entered the palace at night and used a root associated with Circe that could not be extinguished once kindled. The palace burned, Jason escaped, and Glauce and Creon were consumed.
