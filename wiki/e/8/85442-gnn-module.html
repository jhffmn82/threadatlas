GNN module

software_component · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: GNN, GNN network, a GNN, graph neural network

Abstract written by a model

The GNN module in REANO performs multi-hop reasoning over knowledge graphs to identify triples useful for answering questions. It selects and ranks the top-K triples most relevant to a question, concatenating them as an additional passage for answer generation. Over L layers, it updates entity embeddings by aggregating neighboring-triple messages weighted by question-relation similarity, producing question-specific entity embeddings for triple scoring and entity relevance prediction. It is trained with Kullback–Leibler divergence between predicted and ground-truth relevant entity distributions.

GNN module is in one work on this site, REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The graph neural network in REANO identifies and selects the top-K knowledge triples most relevant to the question from the generated knowledge graph. The selected triples are ordered by relevance and concatenated as an additional passage for answer generation.

opening .. 3.3

The graph neural network updates entity embeddings by aggregating messages from neighboring triples, with weights based on the similarity between the question and each relation. It then supports triple relevance scoring and entity relevance prediction.

opening .. 5

The GNN module identifies triples that are useful for answering questions. In the example concerning Muzaffargarh Railway Station and Raisan Railway Station, it selects the two country triples stating that both stations are located in Pakistan.
