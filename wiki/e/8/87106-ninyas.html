Ninyas in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ninyas, son of Ninus and Semiramis, became ruler after Semiramis, who had first retained the kingdom as his regent. He reigned peaceably, unlike Semiramis avoiding valour and martial affairs, living secluded in his palace, and ruling through annually changed provincial armies and governors.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK IL

Ninyas was the son of Ninus and Semiramis. After Ninus died, Semiramis retained the kingdom as regent for him.

CHAP. IL

Ninyas, the son of Ninus and Semiramis, succeeded Semiramis and reigned peaceably. Unlike his mother, he avoided valour and martial affairs, remaining shut in his palace among his concubines and eunuchs and maintaining his rule through annually changing provincial armies and governors.
