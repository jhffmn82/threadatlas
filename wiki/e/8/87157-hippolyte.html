Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Hippolyte was an Amazon queen whom Hercules was ordered to fight; Hercules captured her and took her belt. In The Library, Hippolyte was the mother of Hippolytus; she armed Amazons at Phaedra’s wedding, threatened Theseus’s guests, and died in the ensuing battle under differing accounts. In The Library, Hippolyte was queen of the Amazons who held Ares’ belt as a token of supremacy, promised it to Hercules, and was killed by him after Hera’s incitement sparked suspicion. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Hippolyta was queen of the Amazons who agreed to give Hercules her girdle but was killed by him after he wrongly believed her treacherous.

Hippolyte was the mother of Hippolytus and was also called Glauce and Melanippe. During Phaedra’s wedding she appeared armed with Amazons and threatened to kill Theseus’s guests; in the ensuing battle she died, either accidentally at the hands of Penthesilia, by Theseus, or after Theseus’s men trapped and killed her.

Hippolyte is queen of the Amazons and possesses the belt of Ares as a symbol of her supremacy. She meets Hercules at Themiscyra, promises to give him the belt, but is killed by him when Hera incites the Amazons and Hercules suspects treachery.
