Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Lake Serbon lies between Coelo-Syria and Egypt and is narrow, extremely deep, and about two hundred furlongs long. Sand blown by southern winds makes its surface resemble dry land, causing people and even whole armies to sink into its mud and water.
