Cetivores

Abstract written by a model

The Cetivores are whale-eating people on the continental shore near the Chelonophages, living on cast-up whales and suffering near-starvation when none are available.

Cetivores is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
