Abstract written by a model

crosses Indus [built by Semiramis from boats] Semiramis constructed a broad bridge of boats over the Indus and crossed it with most of her forces, leaving sixty thousand soldiers to guard it. During the retreat she ordered its cords and tenons cut, causing many Indians on the boats to drown and securing her escape.
