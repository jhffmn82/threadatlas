Twig attack in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Twig attack is an assault by Twigs against travelers: they surround them, tear Wag’s ear, and scratch Prince Pompadore. Kabumpo, Wag, and Pompadore fight back, escape, and gain a mile’s distance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The Twig attack begins when the creatures surround the travelers, seize Wag by the ear, and claw at Pompadore. Kabumpo charges through them, Wag kicks them backward, and Pompadore cuts off their branch heads until the travelers break free and put a mile between themselves and the Twigs.
