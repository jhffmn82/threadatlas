Meleager's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: crime, death, funeral, hastened her son's death, her son's death, killed him, sacrifice

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Meleager was killed after Althea cursed him and the gods heard her prayer; another account says that his life depended on a firebrand preserved by Althea, which she threw into the fire after her brothers' deaths, thereby hastening his death. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Meleager's death results from Althæa's decision to burn the billet whose life was joined to his; he feels his entrails scorched by hidden fire, laments that he dies without shedding blood, and gradually expires as the fire and his spirit are extinguished together.
