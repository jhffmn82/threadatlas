Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

Tappy Oko, the Imperial Punster, accompanies the Scarecrow on a walk, sings songs about the Scarecrow's past, and tells him how he met and married Tsing Tsing. He helps the Scarecrow attend the kite-flying exhibition, fears the Scarecrow has been killed when he falls, and repairs him with straw and safety pins.

CHAPTER 9

Happy Toko is the only Silver Islander who follows the Scarecrow into danger. He carries a watering can, helps extinguish the Scarecrow when the fireworks set him smoking, and stays beside him during the celebrations and afterward.

CHAPTER 14

Happy Toko tries to cheer the Scarecrow, welcomes the Scarecrow's family, and helps manage the grandsons. He tells the Scarecrow that the grandsons' behavior is the fault of their upbringing, listens to stories about Oz, and sings him a humorous song. After overhearing the Princes whispering, Happy calls their conversation treason, but the Scarecrow draws him away.

CHAPTER 15

Happy Toko stays close to the Scarecrow, scowls at the Princes, and helps him observe their behavior. He tries to escape after the eldest Prince has him seized, but is bound around his waist and remains trapped with the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 18

Happy Toko is the Scarecrow's faithful servant and Imperial Punster on Silver Island. He lights the throne room, helps arrange cushions, warns that the Royal Princes and the Grand Gheewizard are approaching, and receives a language pill that enables him to speak and understand Ozish. He joins the others in climbing the bean pole.

CHAPTER 21

Happy Toko is chosen and installed as Emperor of the Silver Island. Though initially upset that the Scarecrow will leave, he accepts the throne, receives the allegiance of the Silver Islanders, and gives a speech recorded in the Royal Book of state.
