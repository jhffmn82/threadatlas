Laodamia

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: I, Laudamia, me, mistress, my, newly wedded bride, queen, Λαοδάμεια

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Laodamia writes from Thessaly after Protesilaus’s departure, grieves anxiously, prays and dreams of him, and urges his safe return. In The Library, Laodamia loves Protesilaus beyond his death, makes his likeness, rejoices when Hermes brings him from Hades, and kills herself after he is taken back.

XIII

Laodamia writes to Protesilaus from Thessaly after his departure. She grieves, neglects her appearance, prays at the altars, dreams of him, and repeatedly urges him to avoid danger and return safely.
