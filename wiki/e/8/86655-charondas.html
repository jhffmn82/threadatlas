Abstract written by a model

Charondas, a learned and accomplished citizen of Thurium, was chosen as legislator. He compiled laws from different countries and his own additions, addressing marriage, false accusations, harmful associations, education, orphans, military duty, and legal amendments. He died by his own sword after entering an assembly armed against his own law.

Charondas was chosen as Thurium’s legislator because he was regarded as learned and accomplished. He examined the laws of different countries, selected what he considered best, and added laws of his own concerning marriage, false accusations, harmful associations, education, orphans, military duty, and legal amendments. He died by deliberately running himself through with his sword after entering an assembly armed, contrary to a law he had made.
