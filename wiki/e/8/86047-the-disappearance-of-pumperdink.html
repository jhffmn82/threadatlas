the disappearance of Pumperdink in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The disappearance of Pumperdink is an imminent threatened event, conditioned on Prince Pompadore failing to meet the royal marriage condition in time. According to the scroll, Pumperdink would vanish forever from the Gilliken country of Oz, affecting everybody. The threat alarms King Pompus, who orders guards to watch for signs as small as a missing fence paling, while the palace repeatedly checks that it still exists. Kabumpo prepares to disappear elegantly but leaves with Pompadore, taking him to seek a Proper Princess. Though Pompadore wants to return and save his parents and kingdom, Kabumpo presses onward. The danger remains unresolved while Pompa seeks a princess whose marriage may save Pumperdink.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The disappearance of Pumperdink is presented in the scroll as the consequence of Prince Pompadore failing to meet the royal marriage condition. It would remove Pumperdink forever and even longer from the Gilliken country of Oz.

Chapter 2

The possible disappearance of Pumperdink alarms King Pompus and motivates his demand that Pompadore marry immediately. The King orders guards to watch for any sign of disappearance, including the loss of a fence paling.

Chapter 3

Disappearance is feared throughout Pumperdink, and the palace inhabitants repeatedly check whether they are still present. Kabumpo prepares to disappear elegantly and ultimately leaves Pumperdink with Pompadore.

Chapter 4

Pumperdink's threatened disappearance prompts Kabumpo to take Pompadore away and seek a Proper Princess. Pompadore initially wants to return to save his father, mother, and kingdom, but Kabumpo insists that they must go forward instead.

Chapter 12

Pumperdink’s disappearance threat remains active because Pompa has not yet married the Proper Princess. Pompa believes that rescuing Ozma and marrying her may save his parents and all of Pumperdink.

Chapter 20

Pumperdink’s disappearance is the reason Pompa believes he must marry the Princess of Sun Top Mountain and save his kingdom. Even after discovering Peg Amy’s identity, the need to save Pumperdink remains part of Pompa’s concern.
