Triple Graph Construction in Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Junde Wu, Jiayuan Zhu, Yunli Qi, Jingkun Chen, Min Xu, Filippo Menolascina, Vicente Grau · 2024-10-15

Triple Graph Construction is a more sophisticated graph construction technique introduced for the medical domain and developed for Medical GraphRAG. It creates a triple-linked structure that connects user documents and RAG data with credible medical papers, foundational medical dictionaries, and controlled vocabularies. It begins by linking entities from RAG documents to relevant entities in medical papers and books through cosine similarity between content embeddings, then similarly links those source entities to UMLS entities. This produces triples of [RAG data, source, definition], linking RAG entities to credible sources and professional definitions for traceable, explainable, evidence-based, context-aware medical responses. In ablation studies, it replaces GraphRAG's graph construction after Med-MetaGraph, produces significant gains, and raises accuracy to 91.1% on MedQA, 81.8% on PubMedQA, and 80.9% on MedMCQA.
