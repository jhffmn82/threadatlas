King Fix It in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

King Fix It is the king of Fix City, resembling its inhabitants except for his crown. He rules by handling one question at a time, hosts travelers, explains the people’s stillness preserves furniture, and knows Scarecrows only from a picture.

CHAPTER 12

King Fix It rules Fix City and looks like the other inhabitants except for his crown. He insists on handling one question at a time, gives the travelers dinner and beds, explains that the people stand still to preserve their furniture, and says he has seen a Scarecrow only in a picture.
