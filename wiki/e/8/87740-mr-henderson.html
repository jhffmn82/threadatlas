Mr. Henderson in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mr. Henderson, also Don Murillo and the Tiger of San Pedro, is an enormously rich, fierce, secretive former dictator who ruled San Pedro for ten or twelve years and is pursued for crimes and atrocities. Living in England as Henderson, he is master of High Gable, travels constantly with Lucas and his former secretary Lopez, employs Miss Burnet for his two daughters, and violently threatens others. After discovering Miss Burnet's betrayal, he and Lopez imprison and abuse her and arrange Garcia's killing. He escapes England with Lopez and is later believed murdered with him in Madrid.

He and Lopez discover Miss Burnet's betrayal, imprison and abuse her, and arrange for Garcia to be killed.
