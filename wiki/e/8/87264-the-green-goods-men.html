Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Green-goods men rent room No. 8, circulate counterfeit-money offers, then arrange meetings to watch and rob suspected customers; Alf Wolfe leads, with Snipes trailing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

The green-goods men send circulars offering counterfeit money and arrange meetings where suspected customers can be watched and robbed. Alf Wolfe leads the group connected with room No. 8, and Snipes works for them as a trailer.
