Merina, queen of the Amazons of Africa, commands a large army, conquers the Atlantides and Gorgons, overruns parts of Africa and Asia, builds cities including Merina after Cercene’s destruction, and consecrates Samothracia after a storm. She is killed battling Mompsus and Sipylus; remaining Amazons succeed her in defeat.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

Merina was queen of the Amazons and led an army of thirty thousand foot soldiers and two thousand horse. She conquered the Atlantides, fought the Gorgons, overran parts of Africa and Asia, founded cities, and consecrated Samothracia to the mother of the gods after surviving a storm. She was later killed in battle against Mompsus and Sipylus and was succeeded in defeat by the remaining Amazons.
