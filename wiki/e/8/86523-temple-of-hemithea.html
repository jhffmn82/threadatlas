Abstract written by a model

The temple of Hemithea at Castabus became famous, drawing inhabitants and strangers with sacrifices and gifts. Protected by religious devotion without guards or walls, it was said to escape Persians and thieves; its mysteries used water and honey offerings and excluded those touched by or eating swine.
