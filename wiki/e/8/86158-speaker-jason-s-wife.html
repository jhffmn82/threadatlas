speaker (Jason's wife) in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

A wife and mother of two who brought her husband a golden ram, Grecian youth, and her aid in saving and advancing him. She humbly appeals for restoration of their marriage, invoking her service and children, but ends by warning that her rising anger threatens grave consequences.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Quodsi forte preces praecordia ferrea tangunt,

The speaker humbles herself before her husband, pleads for the restoration of the marriage bed, and reminds him that she helped him and became the mother of his children. She warns that her anger is preparing great threats.
