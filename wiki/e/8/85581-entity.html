Abstract written by a model

In “A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models,” entities are graph elements that can be transformed into dense vectors and used as retrieval anchors, including as source, target, or seed entities during subgraph and path retrieval. In “LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” an entity is a node identified in text, with its name used as its index key and its profile containing a description and relevant text excerpts.
