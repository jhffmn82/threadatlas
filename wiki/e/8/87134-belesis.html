Abstract written by a model

CHAP. IL

Belesis was a Babylonian priest of the Chaldeans, reputed to practice astrology and divination. He persuaded Arbaces to revolt, encouraged the conspirators after their defeats by predicting success, and received the government of Babylon from Arbaces despite having secretly carried away much of Sardanapalus's gold and silver. Arbaces ultimately pardoned him, allowed him to retain Babylon, and gave him the treasure.
