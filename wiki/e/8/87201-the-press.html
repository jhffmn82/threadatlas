Abstract written by a model

The Press is a newspaper in Philadelphia. It employs Gallegher as an office-boy, but its managing editor discharges him for repeated truancy; Dwyer plans to seek his reinstatement after Gallegher discovers Hade. Late at night, its editors and compositors prepare the paper and await news of a fight and Hade’s arrest. The sporting editor sends a message, Dwyer acts as the Press’s representative, and payment depends on no other paper learning of the arrest. Gallegher races to deliver Dwyer’s notebook but is stopped seven blocks from the office.
