Abstract written by a model

Case's tenement is a dark, mostly empty, largely untenanted building with few chances to move between rooms. While fleeing, Rags enters and eventually hides in a nearly bare room, where he encounters Margaret. It contains room No. 8, rented by green-goods men who receive visiting countrymen.
