Case's tenement in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Case's tenement is a dark, mostly empty, largely untenanted building with few chances to move between rooms. While fleeing, Rags enters and eventually hides in a nearly bare room, where he encounters Margaret. It contains room No. 8, rented by green-goods men who receive visiting countrymen.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" MY DISREPUTABLE FRIEND, MR.

Case's tenement is mostly empty and offers few opportunities for Rags to dodge from room to room. Rags enters it while fleeing and eventually hides in one of its nearly bare rooms, where he encounters Margaret.

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Case's tenement contains room No. 8, which is rented by the green-goods men. Its residents have learned to leave visiting countrymen to the men operating from that room.
