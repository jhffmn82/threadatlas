DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIV.

The tyranny of the Thirty grew increasingly violent after its rulers secured a Lacedaemonian garrison. They executed prominent and wealthy citizens, confiscated their estates, and reduced Athens so severely that more than half of its inhabitants fled.
