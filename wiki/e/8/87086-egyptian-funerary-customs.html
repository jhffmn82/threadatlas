Egyptian funerary customs

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: burial, common burial, funerals, their rites and ceremonies in burying their dead

Abstract written by a model

Egyptian funerary customs honor the deceased through burial and praise. Friends and kindred mourn until interment by throwing dirt, running through the city, and abstaining; funerals range from magnificent to inexpensive, ending in burial in tombs, apartments, or houses.

CHAP, VII.

Egyptians mourn by throwing dirt on their heads, running through the city, and abstaining from baths, wine, pleasant foods, and rich clothing until burial. They have three kinds of funerals, magnificent, moderate, and inexpensive, and may bury the dead in tombs, in household apartments, or, in disfavored cases, within their own houses.
