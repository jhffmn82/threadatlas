Snipes

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Snipe, the boy, the little boy, the trailer

Abstract written by a model

Snipes is a very young, homeless, imperfectly trained but skilled trailer for the green-goods men, paid a quarter per shadowed victim. Unnoticeable in crowds, patient, and able to follow men through changing streets, he resists compulsory reform. He aids and sympathizes with an old farmer, protects Abraham, reaches Grand Central Depot, and declines farm life to remain in New York.

Snipes is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Snipes is a young homeless boy who works as a trailer for Alf Wolfe's green-goods men, earning a quarter for each victim he shadows. He sympathizes with the old farmer, tells him he will protect Abraham, helps him reach Grand Central Depot, and is invited to live on the farm, but chooses to remain in New York.
