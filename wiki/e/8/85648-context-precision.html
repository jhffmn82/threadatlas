context precision in CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Nengbo Wang, Xiaotian Han, Jagdip Singh, Jing Ma, Vipin Chaudhary · 2025

Context precision is a metric assessing how accurately retrieved content is correct, relevant, and causally related to a user query. It is used to compare retrieval approaches: regular RAG generally has lower precision, graph-based methods improve it via reranking and subgraph summaries, and CausalRAG leads the reported results with 92.86 versus 89.18 for GraphRAG-Local.

Context precision measures the proportion of retrieved context that is correct and causally related to the user's query. The reported experiments show that graph-based approaches markedly improve context precision through reranking and subgraph-summary steps, while regular RAG generally has lower precision.
