Nisus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Nisus is a Trojan warrior, Hyrtacus’s noble son, runner, and archer-spearman. His defining bond is friendship with Euryalus, sharing care and danger: he sacrifices his race by tripping Salius and receives a decorated shield. On night watch, he proposes reaching Aeneas through enemy lines, then accepts Euryalus as companion in seeking approval.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK V

Nisus leads the footrace until he slips on the blood-soaked ground near the finish. To aid Euryalus, he trips Salius, and Aeneas later gives Nisus a decorated shield in recognition of his claim.

BOOK IX

Nisus guards the Trojan camp with Euryalus and proposes secretly passing through the enemy lines to find Aeneas. He initially urges Euryalus to remain behind because of his youth, but accepts his companionship and joins him in seeking approval for the mission.

Nisus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Nisus is a warrior and Euryalus's friend who leads a nocturnal raid through a sleeping enemy camp, killing several foes. As dawn nears he tries to end it, returns to aid pursued Euryalus, kills more enemies including Volscens, and dies upon Euryalus's body; his head is displayed on a spear.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thus arm’d they went. The noble Trojans wait

Nisus leads the way through the sleeping enemy camp and kills Rhamnes, Remus, Lamus, Lamyrus, and Serranus. He urges Euryalus to end the raid when dawn approaches, but returns to find him pursued. From the forest he kills Sulmo and Tagus, then kills Volscens before dying upon Euryalus's body.

Nisus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Nisus, a Trojan warrior guarding the camp entrance and Euryalus's older friend, proposes a night crossing to seek Aeneas, rescues Euryalus, kills Volscens, and dies after Euryalus falls.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Nisus guards the entrance to the Trojan camp and proposes crossing the enemy camp to seek Aeneas. He joins Euryalus in the night raid, returns to rescue him when he is missing, kills Volscens after Euryalus is struck down, and is then killed by the Rutulians.
