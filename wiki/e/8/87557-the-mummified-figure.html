2. The Tiger of San Pedro

The mummified figure is a black, leathery, greatly shrunken object found on the kitchen dresser at Wisteria Lodge. It resembles either a dwarfish human figure or a twisted ancient monkey, and a double band of white shells is strung around its centre.
