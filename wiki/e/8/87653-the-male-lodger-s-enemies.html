The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

The male lodger’s enemies wait near Mrs. Warren’s house and abduct Mr. Warren by mistake, apparently believing him to be the lodger. Their actions indicate that they do not know the male lodger has been replaced by the female lodger.
