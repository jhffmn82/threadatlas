the taking of Troy in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The taking of Troy is attributed by some ancient poets to Hercules. After a battle between the Argonauts and the Trojans, Hercules suddenly assaults Troy, kills Laomedon, punishes the king's supporters, and installs Priam; another account has him take it with six ships without Argonauts.
