The Adventure of Wisteria Lodge / 1. The Singular Experience of Mr. John Scott Eccles

The telegram from John Scott Eccles tells Sherlock Holmes that Eccles has had an incredible and grotesque experience and asks whether he may consult him. Inspectors Gregson and Baynes use the telegram to trace Eccles from Charing Cross Post Office.
