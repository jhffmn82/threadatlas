Macareus

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Macareus, son of Crinacus and descendant of Jupiter, settled devastated Lesbos, gathered Ionians and others, ruled justly, and divided land among his followers. He extended his rule through colonies: a son led one to Chios, Neander ruled Cos, and with Leucippus he colonized Rhodes; colonies also went to Samos.

Macareus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
