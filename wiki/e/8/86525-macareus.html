He extended his rule through colonies: a son led one to Chios, Neander ruled Cos, and with Leucippus he colonized Rhodes; colonies also went to Samos.

Macareus, the son of Crinacus and descendant of Jupiter, settled Lesbos after the island had been laid waste. He gathered Ionians and people from other nations, ruled justly, divided lands among his followers, and sent colonies to Chios, Samos, Cos, and Rhodes.
