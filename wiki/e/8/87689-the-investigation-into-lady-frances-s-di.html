Abstract written by a model

The investigation into Lady Frances Carfax’s disappearance begins in London after nearly five weeks without her customary letter to Miss Dobney. Her last letter was from Lausanne, followed only by a payment to Marie Devine. After failed advertisements and clues, Holmes’s organisation and the official police pursue her pawned Spanish pendant, Philip Green’s surveillance, workhouse and doctor’s-office inquiries, and an attempted Peters-household search, ending in her rescue from suspected confinement.
