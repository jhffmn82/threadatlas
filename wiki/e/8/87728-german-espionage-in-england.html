German espionage in England in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

German espionage in England is a Von Bork-led secret network operating for the German government through paid agents such as James, Hollis, Steiner, and Altamont. It gathers British military, naval, and national information, but suffers arrests and losses, including Steiner’s imprisonment in Portsmouth. Holmes infiltrates the network, disrupts its plans, and seizes its documents.

The network gathers information on British military installations, naval matters, and other national affairs, but several agents have been arrested or lost, and Steiner has been taken to Portsmouth jail.
