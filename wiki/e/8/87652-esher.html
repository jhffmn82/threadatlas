Esher in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Esher is a pretty Surrey village. John Scott Eccles travels there to visit Wisteria Lodge, finds it deserted, and learns from Allan Brothers’ that its rent was paid in advance. Later Holmes, Watson, and Inspector Baynes arrive, and Holmes and Watson take rooms at the Bull before going to Wisteria Lodge.

The Adventure of Wisteria Lodge / 1. The Singular Experience of Mr. John Scott Eccles

John Scott Eccles travels to Esher to visit Wisteria Lodge. After finding the house deserted, he goes to Allan Brothers', the local house agents, who confirm that the rent for the property has been paid in advance.

We sat for some time in silence after our visitors had left. Holmes

Esher is the Surrey village where Holmes, Watson, and Inspector Baynes arrive nearly at six o'clock. Holmes and Watson take rooms at the Bull before setting out for Wisteria Lodge.
