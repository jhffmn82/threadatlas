Abstract written by a model

Esher is a pretty Surrey village. John Scott Eccles travels there to visit Wisteria Lodge, finds it deserted, and learns from Allan Brothers’ that its rent was paid in advance. Later Holmes, Watson, and Inspector Baynes arrive, and Holmes and Watson take rooms at the Bull before going to Wisteria Lodge.
