Nessus's love potion

object · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: his seed, love charm, love charm for Hercules, the poison of the arrow, the seed of Nessus, φίλτρον

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is a supposed love potion made from Nessus's blood, oil, and seed that Dejanira applies to Hercules's clothing, but it acts as a destructive poison. In The Library, it is a supposed love charm made from Nessus's blood and the seed he dropped, which Deianira applies to Hercules's tunic and which destroys his body.

Abstract written by a model

is_a love potion [made from Nessus's blood and seed] Nessus told Dejanira to mix his blood, oil, and some of his seed and use the mixture on Hercules's undergarment. Dejanira later applied the substance to Hercules's coat and shirt, believing it would preserve his love for her, but it acted as a destructive poison.
