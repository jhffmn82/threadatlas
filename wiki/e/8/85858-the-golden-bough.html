Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK VI

The golden bough grows on a hidden tree claimed by Proserpine. Two doves guide Aeneas to it, and he tears it away with ease before carrying it to the Sibyl’s palace as the required offering for entry into the underworld.

Ye realms, yet unreveal’d to human sight,

The golden bough is the shining, destined offering that the Sibyl reveals to Charon. Its sight appeases the gloomy god and later it is fixed above the entrance to Pluto’s palace.
