Indian laws in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Indian laws describe a quadrangular India and regulate war, social ranks, marriage, occupations, and strangers. They protect husbandmen during war, prohibit servitude while requiring equal respect for free people, yet restrict marriage and work to one’s tribe and inherited calling.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

India is of a quadrangular form, one side lying towards the east^

Indian laws include the protection of husbandmen during war, the division of society into ranks, restrictions on marriage and occupations, and provisions for strangers. One reported principle states that no one should be a servant and that all free people should receive equal respect.
