Cyrene in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Cyrene is a water-nymph, mother of Aristaeus, living with attendant nymphs in a river-bottom palace; she receives him and directs him to compel Proteus for answers about his destroyed bees.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVII

Cyrene was the water-nymph mother of Aristaeus and lived in a palace at the bottom of the river with attendant nymphs. She received Aristaeus, directed him to seize Proteus, and told him how to force the prophet to explain the cause of the bees' destruction and its remedy.
