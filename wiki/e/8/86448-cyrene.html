Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Cyrene is the daughter of Hypsaeus and the mother of Aristaeus by Apollo; Apollo carries her to Libya, where a city is later founded and named after her. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Cyrene is a water-nymph and the mother of Aristaeus who receives him and directs him to compel Proteus for answers about his destroyed bees.

Chapter XVII

Cyrene was the water-nymph mother of Aristaeus and lived in a palace at the bottom of the river with attendant nymphs. She received Aristaeus, directed him to seize Proteus, and told him how to force the prophet to explain the cause of the bees' destruction and its remedy.
