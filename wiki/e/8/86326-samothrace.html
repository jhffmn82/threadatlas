Samothracia in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Samothracia, a sacred island consecrated to the mother of the gods, was formerly called Samos, received its later name from colonies from Samos and Thrace, and preserves ancient speech, religious traditions, flood associations, Saon, and sacred mysteries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Samothracia was said to have been formerly called Samos and to have received its later name from colonies from Samos and Thrace. Its inhabitants preserve an ancient speech and religious traditions, and the island is associated with a great flood, Saon, and sacred mysteries.
