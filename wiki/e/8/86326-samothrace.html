Samothrace

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Samothracia is described as a sacred island consecrated to the mother of the gods, formerly called Samos and associated with ancient speech, religious traditions, flood traditions, Saon, and sacred mysteries. In The Library, Samothrace is the place Dardanus left after the death of his brother Iasion before going to the opposite mainland.

Abstract written by a model

Samothracia, a sacred island consecrated to the mother of the gods, was formerly called Samos, received its later name from colonies from Samos and Thrace, and preserves ancient speech, religious traditions, flood associations, Saon, and sacred mysteries.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Samothracia was said to have been formerly called Samos and to have received its later name from colonies from Samos and Thrace. Its inhabitants preserve an ancient speech and religious traditions, and the island is associated with a great flood, Saon, and sacred mysteries.
