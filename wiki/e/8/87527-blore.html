Also called: BLORE, Edward, Edward Blore, Mr. Blore, butler, old servant, veteran sportsman

Abstract written by a model

Blore is the venerable-looking butler and servant of Augustin, the Dean, working in the Deanery household. He is acquainted with Hannah Topping and gambles on horse races, both for himself and on commission for the kitchen. Hoping Bonny Betsy will win, he stakes his savings and then the Restoration Fund money on that horse. After taking the Dean’s fifty pounds to bet on Dandy Dick, he secretly puts strychnine in Dandy Dick’s bolus. He later visits Hannah at the police station, worried about the case’s effect on the Dean and Dandy Dick. Blore finally confesses his betting and poisoning; the Dean retains him in service in return for silence.

Blore is in one work on this site, Dandy Dick. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

Edward Blore admits that he has gambled at races on his own account and on commission for the kitchen, and he gives the Dean a tip that Bonny Betsy will win the handicap. After receiving the Dean's fifty pounds to bet on Dandy Dick, he secretly adds strychnine to the bolus intended for the horse.

THE THIRD ACT.

Edward Blore visits Hannah at the police station from the Deanery. He is troubled because the prisoner is connected with his master and worries about the effect of the case on the Dean and on Dandy Dick.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

Blore confesses that he added strychnine to the bolus because he wanted Bonny Betsy to win instead of Dandy Dick. He admits that he placed the Dean's fifty-pound offering on Bonny Betsy rather than Dandy Dick, and the Dean keeps him in service in exchange for silence.
