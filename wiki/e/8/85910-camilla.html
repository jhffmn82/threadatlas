Camilla in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Camilla is a Volscian warrior queen, Amazon, and leader of the Italian female warriors and Volscian troops. Daughter of Metabus and Casmilla, she is rescued by her father as an infant, raised in the wilderness, trained in arms, and devoted to Diana, chastity, and the Amazon way rather than spinning or weaving. Protected by Diana and bearing Diana’s arms, a bow, arrows, a myrtle javelin, and a Lycian quiver, she is famed for swift, astonishing movement across fields and sea. She leads the Volscians against the Tyrrhene troops, offers to fight while Turnus guards the town, and kills many enemies with spear, ax, and bow. After killing Aunus, she pursues the richly equipped Chloreus and is fatally struck in the breast by Aruns’s javelin.

And now the mighty labour is begun

Camilla comes from the Volscians and leads their troops as a warrior woman. Unskilled in spinning and weaving, she chooses warfare, outruns the winds, crosses fields without damaging the grain, skims over the sea, and astonishes everyone who sees her.

While they debate, nor these nor those will yield,

Camilla arrives with the Volscians and offers to meet the Tyrrhene troops, asking Turnus to remain behind and guard the town. Diana recounts that Camilla was rescued as an infant by her father Metabus, raised in the wilderness, trained in arms, and devoted to Diana and virginity.

Camilla rides fearlessly through the battle, killing numerous warriors with her spear, ax, and bow. She dismounts to confront Aunus, catches him as he flees, and kills him; later, while pursuing the richly equipped Chloreus, she is struck in the breast by Aruns’s javelin and dies.

Camilla in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Camilla is a huntress, virgin warrior, and Diana’s favored follower, raised by her father Metabus in woodland arts and devoted to rejecting marriage. She leads mounted followers, including women warriors, and allies with Turnus against the Trojans. Renowned for speed and weapon skill, she performs brave deeds against Trojans and Etruscans before Aruns fatally wounds her while she pursues a fleeing enemy; Diana then has Aruns killed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVI

Camilla, a huntress and warrior devoted to Diana, joins Turnus with mounted followers, including selected women warriors. She was raised in woodland arts by her father, Metabus, after he dedicated her to Diana as an infant, and she is renowned for extraordinary speed and skill with weapons.

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Camilla, a virgin warrior, performs many brave deeds and kills or strikes down numerous Trojans and Etruscans. She is fatally wounded by Aruns while pursuing a fleeing enemy, and Diana has Aruns killed in revenge.
