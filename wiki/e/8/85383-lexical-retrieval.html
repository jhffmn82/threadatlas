lexical retrieval

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: lexical, lexical matching, traditional lexical matching

Abstract written by a model

Lexical retrieval is one of two retrieval methods, alongside learned dense retrieval, for both tasks. It produces the strongest SDG Evidence Identification results for GPT-3.5 and Llama2-13b-chat and the best recall-oriented SDG Target Detection with ClimateGPT-13b, aided by distinctive matching words.

It also supports the best recall-oriented SDG Target Detection results with ClimateGPT-13b, possibly because distinctive words such as “overfishing,” “acidification,” “transport,” and “alien” improve matching.
