Abstract written by a model

Judge Burgoyne, identified as Henry Burgoyne, is a judge and lawyer and the former boyhood friend of Lewis Lockwood. They attended school, roomed at State college, practiced law, and shared shooting, but became estranged after both loved the same woman. Later, Lockwood visits Burgoyne to see whether their long separation can end, and they plan a shooting trip in Maryland.

Judge Burgoyne is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
