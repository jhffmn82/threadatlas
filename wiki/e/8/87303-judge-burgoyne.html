Judge Burgoyne is identified by Mr. Latimer as Henry Burgoyne, the former boyhood friend of Lewis Lockwood. He and Lockwood attended school together, roomed together at the State college, practised law together, and shared a love of shooting. He later became estranged from Lockwood after both men fell in love with the same woman.
