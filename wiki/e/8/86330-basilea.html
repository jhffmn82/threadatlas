Basilea in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Basilea, Uranus and Titea’s eldest and most prudent daughter, raised her brothers with maternal care and became the Great Mother. After her father’s death, the people and her brothers elected her queen while she was still a virgin; though initially unwilling, she married Hyperion to leave heirs.

Basilea was the eldest and most prudent daughter of Uranus and Titea. She raised her brothers with maternal care, became known as the Great Mother, and was elected queen after her father's death while still a virgin.
