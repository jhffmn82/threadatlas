Meantime, by Jove’s impulse, Mezentius arm’d,

Rhoebus is Mezentius’s well-trained horse and companion in war and peace. During the fight with Aeneas, Aeneas’s spear pierces both of Rhoebus’s ears; the wounded horse rears, throws Mezentius, and falls upon him.
