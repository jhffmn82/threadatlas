Abstract written by a model

Great Dome is a watertight glass dome, reinforced with stout steel frames, that covers the island and village of the Skeezers on Magic Isle. Built by the three Adepts in Sorcery so the island could disappear beneath Lake of the Skeezers, it encloses houses, streets, gardens, parks, and the Queen's palace at the village center. Its clear glass allows people inside to see outside, but it has no openable doors or windows and holds the Skeezers, Ozma, and Dorothy as prisoners at the lake bottom. The dome remains visible beneath the clear water, only a few feet below the surface, while Glinda and the Wizard circle it in a submarine and signal those inside. After an incantation raises it several feet above the lake, the Wizard removes a glass pane, secures a rope to a steel frame, enters through the opening, and lowers the others into the village.

Great Dome is in one work on this site, Glinda of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The glass dome covers the Skeezers' village and was built for them by the three Adepts in Sorcery. Lady Aurex says the Flatheads cannot break it, although Dorothy believes they may try to shoot arrows at it.

Chapter Ten

The Great Dome covers the Skeezers' village and is made of glass that does not leak when submerged. It descends beneath the lake's surface with the island, while its houses remain dimly visible through the water.

Chapter Twelve

The glass dome encloses the Skeezers and prevents them from escaping the bottom of the lake. Ozma, Dorothy, and Lady Aurex watch events outside through it, while Dorothy later observes the surrounding fish through its clear panes.

Chapter Thirteen

The great Dome of the Isle of the Skeezers is beneath the water of the lake. Ozma and Dorothy are imprisoned inside it.

Chapter Seventeen

The Great Dome is visible far from shore at the center of the lake, outlining the submerged island. Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers are still held beneath it.

Chapter Twenty

The Great Dome covers the Skeezer village and is visible beneath the clear water. Dorothy and Ozma are seen inside it, and Glinda and the Wizard use the submarine to circle it and exchange signals with them.

Chapter Twenty-One

The Great Dome covers the sunken Skeezer village and lies only a few feet below the lake's surface. If the lake is lowered, some of its glass could be removed so Glinda and the others could descend into the village by ropes.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Dome rises several feet above the lake when the incantation is completed. The Wizard removes one of its glass panes, ties a rope to a steel frame, and uses the opening to enter and lower the others into the village.
