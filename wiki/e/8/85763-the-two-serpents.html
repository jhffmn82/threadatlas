Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, the two serpents emerge from the sea as a terrifying portent, kill Laocoon and his children, then retreat to Minerva’s tower beneath her protection. In The Fall of Troy, the serpents are deadly dragons of Typhon’s brood from Calydna, summoned by Athena and sent toward Troy; they terrify island and sea, kill Laocoon’s sons, then disappear beneath earth into Apollo’s temple in Pergamus.
