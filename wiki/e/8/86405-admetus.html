Admetus

Also called: Admctus

Abstract written by a model

Thessalian son of Pheretes and king of the Molossians, Admetus courteously shelters, funds, and secretly helps Themistocles escape Lacedaemonian pressure.

Admetus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. VII.

Admetus, king of the Molossians, initially received the fugitive Themistocles courteously and promised to protect him. Under pressure from the Lacedaemonians, he gave Themistocles money and advised him to leave secretly.
