Eryx

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Eryx is said to be the son of Venus and a famous Sicilian prince, becomes king of part of Sicily, founds the city called Eryx on a high hill, and builds a temple to Venus there. In The Library, Eryx refuses Hercules the escaped bull, is defeated three times in wrestling, and is killed.
