  • In Heroides and Amores: The lover spends the night outside the closed door, repeatedly calling for the bar to be removed and listening for signs that the door has opened. He sheds tears, wears a garland that falls from his hair, and remains outside until the approach of dawn, when he leaves the garland on the threshold as evidence of his miserable vigil.
