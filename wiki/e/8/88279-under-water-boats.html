under-water boats in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Under-water boats are controlled and solely operated by Coo-ee-oh; though Skeezers possess others that might reach the surface, their use offers no known escape.

Chapter Twelve

The Skeezers possess other under-water boats that might reach the surface, but no one except Coo-ee-oh knows how to operate them. Their possible use therefore does not provide a known means of escape.
