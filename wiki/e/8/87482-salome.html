Salome

Abstract written by a model

Salome Jedd is Dr. Jedd’s about twenty-three-year-old daughter, sister to Sheba, niece to Georgiana Tidman, and the Dean’s child. An inexperienced young lady and a clergyman’s wife in training, she shares Sheba’s “wickedness and rebellion,” debts, and desire for independence. At first attracted to Gerald Tarver, she secretly plans a costly French Revolution costume and a visit to Durnstone’s masked ball despite the family’s need to retrench. With Sheba, she seeks Georgiana’s help to win money on Dandy Dick, joins plans to leave the Deanery, and assists the distressed Major Tarver. After the ball is cancelled, she returns angry over the wet journey and wasted expense, confronts Darbey and Tarver, then helps them escape. In the end, she accepts Major Tarver, is exposed as having attended the ball and incurred part of the £40 19s bill, confronts the Dean’s deception, and resolves with Sheba to begin life afresh after their marriage proposals.

DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

Salome Jedd plans to attend the Fancy Dress Masked Ball at Durnstone secretly with Gerald Tarver. She worries about the cost of her French Revolution costume, asks Dr. Jedd for money, and learns that the family must retrench.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

Salome welcomes Georgiana despite not understanding all of her racing language. She and Sheba reveal that they are in debt and ask Georgiana to help them win enough money to pay it. Salome agrees to put their money on Dandy Dick.

THE SECOND ACT.

Salome joins Sheba's plan to leave the Deanery secretly and discusses the successful dinner. She sits with the distressed Major Tarver, helps arrange his proposed singing, and later helps dismiss him and Mr. Darbey.

Salome returns from the cancelled ball in a rage, lighting the candles and complaining about the cold, wet journey and the wasted expense. She and Sheba confront Darbey and Tarver, then help them escape when Georgiana calls Sir Tristram to remove them.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

Salome accepts Major Tarver after Darbey presents his supposed medical need for marriage. She and Sheba are revealed to have attended the masked ball at Durnstone, won fifty pounds at the Races, and incurred part of the £40 19s costume bill. She later confronts the Dean about his deception and announces that she and Sheba intend to begin life afresh after receiving marriage proposals.
