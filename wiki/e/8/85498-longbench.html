LongBench is a real long-context evaluation benchmark containing 21 datasets with average contexts of 7k words. It supplies English query-based datasets, including MuSiQue, for analyses of model performance, RAG failure patterns, and comparisons among retrievers and models.

LongBench in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where LongBench first appears

Abstract written by a model

LongBench is a bilingual, multitask benchmark for long-context understanding, including multi-document question answering. It evaluates long-text and multi-document RAG: TurboRAG results report comparable accuracy to baseline or Naïve RAG while reducing average TTFT from 1165 ms to 134 ms, up to 9.4× improvement.

LongBench multi-document question answering benchmarks are used to evaluate TurboRAG. The reported results show up to a 9.4× TTFT improvement with comparable accuracy to the baseline.

LongBench is used to test long-text and multi-document RAG performance. TurboRAG-reordered achieves comparable accuracy to Naïve RAG and reduces average TTFT from 1165 ms to 134 ms on the reported multi-document QA tasks.
