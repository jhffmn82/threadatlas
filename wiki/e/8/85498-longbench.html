LongBench in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

LongBench is a bilingual, multitask benchmark for long-context understanding, including multi-document question answering. It evaluates long-text and multi-document RAG: TurboRAG results report comparable accuracy to baseline or Naïve RAG while reducing average TTFT from 1165 ms to 134 ms, up to 9.4× improvement.
