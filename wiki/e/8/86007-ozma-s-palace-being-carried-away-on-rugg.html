Ozma’s palace being carried away on Ruggedo’s head in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Ozma’s palace is impaled on Ruggedo’s giant head when he bursts through a cave ceiling, then carried across the country to Ev. After it lands there, its shaking, tilting occupants threaten Ruggedo with eggs to stop his movement.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

When Ruggedo bursts through the cave ceiling, Ozma’s palace is impaled on the spikes of his giant head. He carries it across the country to Ev, where it remains fixed to his head.

Chapter 14

The Magic Picture reveals that Ozma's palace has landed squarely on Ruggedo's head. Because Ruggedo's movements shake and tilt the palace, its occupants threaten him with eggs to keep him from moving.
