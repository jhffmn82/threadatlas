Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Protesilaus, Haemonian lord of Laodamia, leaves her to travel with the Danaans, joins the Greek forces at Troy, while she fears battle and urges his safe return. In The Library, Protesilaus first lands from his ship, kills many barbarians, is killed by Hector, then returns from Hades through Hermes and resurrects the dead. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Protesilaüs is the first Greek to land at Troy and the first to fall there, killed by Hector’s spear immediately upon landing, fulfilling fate’s decree.
