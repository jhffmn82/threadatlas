Hugo Oberstein in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Hugo Oberstein is a leading international agent living at 13 Caulfield Gardens with a valet. He buys stolen Bruce-Partington plans, helps conceal Cadogan West’s body, and flees to the Continent before Holmes lures him back to London, where he is arrested and imprisoned for fifteen years.

Hugo Oberstein lived at 13, Caulfield Gardens, whose back windows opened onto the Underground railway. He bought the stolen Bruce-Partington plans, helped conceal Cadogan West’s body, and fled to the Continent, but Holmes lured him back to London, where he was arrested and imprisoned for fifteen years.
