Fix City in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Fix City is a strange gated city inhabited by the Fixes, or Fix Its. It has one long street and circles for each resident or occupant. Its broad-faced, long-footed people stand motionless in chalked circles and use bells to summon moving furniture. From the entrance, King Fix sends Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion to buy a road at the street’s end.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Fix City is a strange city whose inhabitants stand motionless in chalked circles while tables, chairs, beds, clocks, and other furniture move around. Its people have broad, placid faces, no waists, and very long flat feet, and they use bells to summon things.

CHAPTER 13

Fix City consists of a long street lined with people and furniture that remain fixed in circles or move according to their nature. King Fix directs Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion to buy a road when they reach the end of the street.
