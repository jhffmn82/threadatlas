the Bruce-Partington submarine in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The Bruce-Partington submarine is a revolutionary invention of exceptional military importance, able to make naval warfare impossible within its operational range. The British government bought a monopoly and guarded its secret. Its construction requires the Bruce-Partington plans; three missing drawings were insufficient without the crucial double valves with automatic self-adjusting slots, which appear in the returned drawings.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans

The Bruce-Partington submarine is described as a revolutionary invention whose operation would make naval warfare impossible within its range. The British government spent a large sum to obtain a monopoly of the invention and has guarded it closely.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

The Bruce-Partington submarine could not be constructed from the three missing drawings alone unless the foreigners independently solved the problem of the double valves with automatic self-adjusting slots. The returned drawings include that crucial feature.
