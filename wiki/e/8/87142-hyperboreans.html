Hyperboreans

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: A Hyperborean, Hjrperboreans, happy race named the Hyperboreans, inhabitants, the Hyperboreans

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Hyperboreans inhabit an island in the ocean beyond Gaul and the north wind and are described as prosperous people with a temperate climate, a special friendship for the Greeks, and religious devotion to Apollo. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Hyperboreans are a happy race beyond the north wind, living in everlasting spring without disease, old age, toil, or warfare, in an inaccessible country beyond land or sea.
