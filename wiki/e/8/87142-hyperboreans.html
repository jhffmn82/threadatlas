  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Hyperboreans inhabit an island in the ocean beyond Gaul and the north wind and are described as prosperous people with a temperate climate, a special friendship for the Greeks, and religious devotion to Apollo.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Hyperboreans are a happy race beyond the north wind, living in everlasting spring without disease, old age, toil, or warfare, in an inaccessible country beyond land or sea.

Hyperboreans in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hyperboreans are a happy race beyond the north wind, living in everlasting spring without disease, old age, toil, or warfare; their inaccessible country remains beyond land or sea.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter I

The Hyperboreans were imagined as a happy people living beyond the north wind in an everlasting spring. They were exempt from disease, old age, toil, and warfare, and their country was inaccessible by land or sea.
