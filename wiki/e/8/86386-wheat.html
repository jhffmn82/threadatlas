Abstract written by a model

Wheat first grew spontaneously in Sicily, including near Leontium, among herbs and grass. Isis discovered its use in bread-making; it was carried and offered in her festivals. Ceres bestowed it on welcoming peoples, and Athenians spread its seed.

Wheat is said to have first grown spontaneously in Sicily, where wild wheat still grew in the territory of Leontium and elsewhere. Ceres later bestowed wheat on peoples who received her kindly, and the Athenians spread its seed to others.
