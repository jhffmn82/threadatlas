Abstract written by a model

Count It Up, a tall thin pencil-like person in Rith Metic, takes charge of Kabumpo and Pompadore, assigning arithmetic from addition to fractions and predicting Pompadore’s journey. When Pompadore somersaults, Count It Up is knocked down and his point breaks.
