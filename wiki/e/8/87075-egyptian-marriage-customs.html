Egyptian marriage customs in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Egyptian marriage customs permit priests one wife and other Egyptians multiple wives, require raising as many children as possible, and recognize bond-maid children as legitimate.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And since now we are come to mention the laws^ we conceive it

Egyptian priests marry only one wife, while all other Egyptians may have as many wives as they please. Egyptians are required to raise as many children as they can, and children of a bond-maid are not considered bastards.
