Hero in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Hero, a Thracian girl and Maid of Sestos, lives in a tower across the sea from Leander, whom she loves as her sole delight and who loves her in return. Exceptional in beauty, tender and vulnerable, she waits through the nights, using a beacon to guide him and a spindle to endure the hours. She has welcomed him from the sea before, caring for his wet clothes and hair. In her letter, separation becomes unbearable: she confides in her nurse, fears another woman, blames the storm, and begs Neptune to let Leander return.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XVIII..

Hero lives at Sestos and waits in the tower for Leander. She has previously welcomed him from the sea, embraced and kissed him, given him dry clothing, and dried his wet hair.

XIX

Hero writes to Leander that delay is unbearable and that, although they burn with equal love, she is weaker and more vulnerable. She spends the nights waiting for him, lighting a beacon, speaking of him with her nurse, watching for his footprints, and imagining his arrival. She fears that he may love another woman, but she also recognizes that the storm is preventing him from coming and begs Neptune to spare them.

Hero in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hero is a maiden and priestess of Venus in Sestos who loves Leander, guides his nightly swims with a tower torch, and after he drowns casts herself into the sea and dies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

Hero was a maiden and priestess of Venus who lived in Sestos. She guided Leander's nightly swims with a torch on her tower, and after his drowning she cast herself from the tower into the sea and died.
