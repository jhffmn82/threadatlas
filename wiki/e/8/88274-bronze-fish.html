bronze fish in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The bronze fish is one of three fishes into which Coo-ee-oh transformed the Adepts in Sorcery, tied to her continued magic. It waits for Queen Coo-ee-oh's downfall, helps pull a boat to shore, and enters a copper kettle. After her defeat, it expects restoration, consents to Reera's transformation, asks to be made an Adept at Magic, and becomes a young woman with dark brown hair and clear gray eyes.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The bronze fish is one of the three fishes into which Coo-ee-oh transformed the Adepts in Sorcery. Like the gold fish and silver fish, it is tied to Coo-ee-oh's continued possession of magic.

Chapter Sixteen

The bronzefish is one of the three Adepts and tells Ervic that the Adepts have waited for Queen Coo-ee-oh's downfall. It joins the other two fishes in pulling the boat toward shore and entering the copper kettle.

Chapter Nineteen

The bronzefish joins the others in consenting to Reera's transformation and asks to be made an Adept at Magic. It becomes a young woman with dark brown hair, clear gray eyes, and a complexion matching those features.
