six history rocks in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The six history rocks are Ruggedo’s carved life story. They trace his changing identities and kingdom under Ev’s mountains, the Queen of Ev and loss of his Magic Belt, failed efforts to conquer Oz and the Fountain of Oblivion, banishment by the Great Jinn Titihoochoo, and an alliance with Kiki Aru involving transformations before his return to normal form.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The six history rocks stand in a row along one side of Ruggedo’s cavern and contain his carved life story. The first gives the title and his changing identities; the second describes his kingdom under the mountains of Ev; the third covers the Queen of Ev, her children, and the loss of his Magic Belt; the fourth covers his failed conquest of Oz and the Fountain of Oblivion; the fifth describes his banishment by the Great Jinn Titihoochoo; and the sixth recounts his alliance with Kiki Aru and their transformations into Limoneags, a goose, and nuts before Ruggedo returned to his normal form.
