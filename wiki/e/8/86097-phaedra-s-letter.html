IV

Phaedra’s letter greets Hippolytus, asks him to read it, and carries her secrets across land and sea. It reveals her confession of love, praises Hippolytus, attacks Theseus, proposes that Phaedra and Hippolytus unite, and ends with tears and a plea for forgiveness.
