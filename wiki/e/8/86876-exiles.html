The exiles are recalled to Syracuse to help defend the country; Dionysius views them as changeable tools for his ambition, and they return after the decree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 3(111.

Dionysius persuaded the Syracusans to recall the exiles so they could help defend their country. He regarded them as politically changeable and useful to his ambition, and they returned after the decree was published.
