Abstract written by a model

The giant in Ev is a tremendous, terrifying three-legged giant who lives in Ev and sits on a mountain with his head and shoulders in the clouds. He carries Princess Ozma, everyone in the Royal Palace of Oz, and the palace itself from the Emerald City to Ev. His vast appearance terrifies the Runaway Country and travelers, making the Country flee. Others plan or seek ways to capture, pull down, or slay him, but no confrontation is shown.

Chapter 7

The three-legged giant is said by Tik Tok to live in Ev. Sir Hokus intends to travel to Ev and slay it, although the unit does not show him reaching or confronting the giant.

Chapter 8

Glinda concludes that the palace may have been taken by a giant from a strange country, since no giant in Oz is large enough to have done it. She studies an encyclopedia about giants while seeking a way to capture the marauding giant.

Chapter 12

The giant in Ev carried Ozma, everyone in the palace, and the castle itself away to Ev. Kabumpo believes he can pull the giant down with his trunk while Pompa dispatches him.

Chapter 17

The giant in Ev is so enormous that he sits on a mountain, with only his legs and feet visible while his head and shoulders disappear into the clouds. His appearance terrifies the Runaway Country and the travelers, causing the Country to flee.
