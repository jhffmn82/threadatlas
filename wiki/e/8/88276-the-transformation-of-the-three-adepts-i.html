Queen Coo-ee-oh invited the three Adepts to a palace banquet, stole their charms and magical instruments, transformed them into gold, silver, and bronze fishes, and cast them into a lake. The betrayal made the Flatheads turn against the Skeezers, causing misery. The Adepts sought Ervic's help and were later restored before meeting Glinda, without revealing their rescuer.

Chapter Nine

Coo-ee-oh invited the three Adepts in Sorcery to a banquet, stole their charms and magical instruments, and transformed them into a gold fish, a silver fish, and a bronze fish. The transformation caused the Flatheads to turn against the Skeezers and made both peoples miserable.

Chapter Sixteen

Queen Coo-ee-oh betrayed the three Adepts and wickedly transformed them into a goldfish, silverfish, and bronzefish. They remain in the lake and seek Ervic's help to regain their natural forms.

Chapter Twenty-One

Coo-ee-oh betrayed the three Adepts at a palace banquet, transformed them into fishes, and cast them into the lake. They have since been restored to their natural forms, though they do not reveal who released them.
