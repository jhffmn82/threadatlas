Miss Violet Westbury in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Miss Violet Westbury is Cadogan West’s fiancée and the last known person to see him. Living with his mother, she describes him as patriotic, honest, financially secure, and incapable of selling a State secret. Before his death, he grew worried about official matters, discussed spies, and abruptly left her in fog.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

Miss Violet Westbury, Cadogan West's fiancée and the last person known to have seen him, says that he was patriotic, honest, and financially secure. She reports that he had become worried about official matters, mentioned the value of secrets to foreign spies, and suddenly left her in the fog near the office.
