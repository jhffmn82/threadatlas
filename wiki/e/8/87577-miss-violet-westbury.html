Miss Violet Westbury

Abstract written by a model

Miss Violet Westbury is Cadogan West’s fiancée and the last known person to see him. Living with his mother, she describes him as patriotic, honest, financially secure, and incapable of selling a State secret. Before his death, he grew worried about official matters, discussed spies, and abruptly left her in fog.
