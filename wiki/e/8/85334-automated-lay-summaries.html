Abstract written by a model

Automated lay summaries make detailed biomedical research more comprehensible by reducing specialized jargon, though accuracy limits widespread use. A model initially generates a summary, uses it as a retrieval query during inference, then refines it through paraphrasing while aiming to include as many input keywords as possible.

Introduction

Automated lay summaries are being developed to make detailed biomedical research comprehensible and to remove complicated jargon. Their accuracy remains a concern that limits widespread use.

Methodology .. 2.3

The lay summary is initially generated by the model and then used as a query for retrieving relevant passages during inference. It is also refined through paraphrasing and is intended to incorporate as many input keywords as possible.
