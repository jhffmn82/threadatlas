the assault and abduction of Mr. Warren in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mr. Warren is abducted by two or more of the male lodger's enemies, who cover his head with a coat and drive him by cab for about an hour before releasing him on Hampstead Heath after realizing their mistake.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

Two or more men throw a coat over Mr. Warren’s head, force him into a cab, drive him for about an hour, and release him on Hampstead Heath. Holmes concludes that the men mistook him for the male lodger and released him after discovering the mistake.
