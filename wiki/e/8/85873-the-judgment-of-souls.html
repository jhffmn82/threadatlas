the judgment of souls in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The judgment of souls is conducted by Minos with assessors, who investigate the dead’s lives and crimes, compel guilty revelations, and determine each soul’s fate.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Ye realms, yet unreveal’d to human sight,

Minos acts as a strict inquisitor, hearing the lives and crimes of the dead with his assessors. Rhadamanthus also judges each crime, forcing the guilty to reveal their deeds before the Furies take them.
