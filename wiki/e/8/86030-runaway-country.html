Runaway Country in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The Runaway Country is a huge, jagged, living, rough and uncultivated country or plateau that moves on ten mountain feet, with two blue lake-eyes and a bubbling river-mouth. Tired of being a plateau, it wants development: settlers to farm its land, mine its gold, and build cities. It claims rescuers by discovery, makes Peg Amy its Princess and Kingdom, and carries Peg Amy, Prince Pompadore, Wag, and Kabumpo across the Deadly Desert. After seeing the giant in Ev, it flees backward in terror and throws off its travelers. Later it catches Ozma and the others. Ozma stops it with the Question Box and promises to restore its moving power, make it an island in the Nonestic Ocean, and provide a little gnome as King if it carries the travelers across the Desert. It does so, waits near Sun Top Mountain, then races onward with Ruggedo dancing on its highest hill. It plunges into the Nonestic Ocean, where Ozma stops it by magic, and it becomes Ruggedo’s Island.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

The Runaway Country is a huge, jagged living piece of land with ten mountain feet, lake-eyes, and a river-mouth. It captures the travelers because it wants settlers to cultivate its farms, mine its gold, and build its cities, and it names Peg Amy its Princess. It carries them across the Deadly Desert into Ev but runs backward in terror after seeing the giant, throwing the travelers off before disappearing.

Chapter 19

The Runaway Country catches Ozma and the others, carries them until Ozma stops it with the Question Box, and complains that it is tired of being a plateau. Ozma agrees to make it an island, restore its moving power, and give it a little gnome for King, provided it carries the travelers across the Deadly Desert.

Chapter 20

The Runaway Country carries the travelers across the Deadly Desert and stops in a field of daisies near Sun Top Mountain. It agrees to wait for the little old gnome and threatens to catch the travelers again if he does not appear.

Chapter 22

The Runaway Country rushes along faster than an express train while Ruggedo dances on its highest hill. It plunges into the Nonestic Ocean, where Ozma stops it by the magic spinning process.
