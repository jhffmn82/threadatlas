war between Carthage and Syracuse in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

War between Carthage and Syracuse began as Dionysius raised forces, built ships and weapons, sought allies, and demanded restoration of Greek cities. He marched on Eryx and Motya; Himiico tried to relieve Motya, but Dionysius captured and garrisoned it, then sent Leptinos to watch Carthage and raid Egesta and Entella.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VII.

Dionysius prepared for war against Carthage by raising troops, constructing ships and weapons, and seeking support from Sicilian cities and foreign soldiers. He declared war unless Carthage restored the Greek cities under its control, then marched toward Eryx and Motya.

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

The conflict continued through Himiico's attempt to relieve Motya and Dionysius's capture of the city. Afterward, Dionysius stationed a garrison at Motya and sent Leptinos with one hundred twenty ships to watch the Carthaginians and raid Egesta and Entella.
