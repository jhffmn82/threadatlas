Eryx’s gauntlets in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Eryx’s gauntlets are ancient fighting equipment of seven folds of bull hide reinforced with iron or heavy lead. Formerly used against the Herculean arm, they frighten Dares by their weight and are surrendered by Entellus after the fight.

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

Eryx's gauntlets are made of seven layers of tough bull hide and contain iron or heavy lead. They once belonged to Eryx and were used against the Herculean arm; their weight frightens Dares, and Entellus later gives them up after the fight.
