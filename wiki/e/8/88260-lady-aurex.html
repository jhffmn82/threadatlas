Lady Aurex in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Lady Aurex is a Skeezer who begins as Queen Coo-ee-oh’s fearful lady in waiting, living near the village’s edge and formerly a favorite of the Diamond Swan. After personally witnessing the three Adepts transformed into fishes, she aids Ozma and Dorothy: she brings them home, tells the history of the Skeezers and Flatheads, describes Coo-ee-oh’s cruel punishments, and warns of possible war. She shelters and feeds them, explains the submerged island, its defenses, submarines, and secrets known only to Coo-ee-oh, and advises Ozma. Trusted by Ozma, Aurex cares for Ozma and Dorothy while the Queen is absent. She is ultimately elected Queen of the Skeezers, rules under Ozma, installs herself in the palace, chooses Ervic as Prime Minister, and fears a threat from the Su-dic.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Lady Aurex brings Ozma and Dorothy to her house and, after Ozma protects the house from Coo-ee-oh's hearing, tells them the history of the Skeezers and Flatheads. She explains that Coo-ee-oh's punishments and cruelty have made the Skeezers fearful and unhappy, and warns that war may begin the next day.

Chapter Ten

Lady Aurex gives Dorothy and Ozma supper, provides them with a comfortable room, and explains the island's submergence and the Skeezers' defenses. She identifies the approaching underwater vessel as one of Queen Coo-ee-oh's submarines.

Chapter Twelve

Lady Aurex explains that only Coo-ee-oh knew how to raise the island, operate the under-water boats, and open the basement doors. She helps advise Ozma and is invited to stay at Coo-ee-oh's palace because of her knowledge of the Skeezers and their island.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Lady Aurex is elected Queen of the Skeezers and installed as mistress of the palace. She chooses Ervic as her Prime Minister and worries that the Su-dic may threaten her peaceful people.
