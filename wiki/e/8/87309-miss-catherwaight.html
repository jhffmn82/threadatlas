Miss Catherwaight in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Miss Catherwaight is a clever young woman, considered cynical by some friends for her collection of dishonored medals and her remarks about society. Once seen as cold and self-possessed, she travels widely with Mr. Catherwaight and regularly visits pawnshops and loan offices to buy decorations and uncover their histories. Her interest in a silver heart medal leads her to Lewis Lyman Lockwood. Ashamed of intruding into its story, she learns that Lockwood and Burgoyne were separated by a misunderstanding involving her mother. She urges Lockwood to keep the medal and reconcile with Burgoyne, hoping to repair harm her mother caused unwittingly.

THE CYNICAL MISS CATHERWAIGHT Miss Catherwaight's collection of orders and decorations and medals was her chief offence in the eyes of those of her dear friends who thought her clever but cynical.

Miss Catherwaight is a clever young woman whom some friends consider cynical because of her collection and her unpleasant remarks about society. She collects dishonored medals, visits pawnshops on the Bowery and Park Row, and seeks to learn the stories behind the objects she buys. After finding the silver heart medal connected with Lewis Lyman Lockwood, she visits his office, feels ashamed of her intrusion, and decides she cannot keep or tell its story.
