Lechaeum

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Lechmm, Lechæum

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Lechaeum is a castle at Corinth seized by exiles allied with the Lacedaemonians and later defended against Corinthian forces. In Description of Greece, Lechaeum is Corinth's seaport, containing a temple and a brazen statue of Poseidon.

BOOK II.--CORINTH.

Lechaeum was one of Corinth's seaports, said to have been named after Leches, the reputed son of Poseidon and Pirene. It contained a temple and brazen statue of Poseidon.
