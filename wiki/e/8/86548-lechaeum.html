Lechaeum in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Lechaeum, Corinth's seaport named after Leches, contains a temple and brazen statue of Poseidon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK II.--CORINTH.

Lechaeum was one of Corinth's seaports, said to have been named after Leches, the reputed son of Poseidon and Pirene. It contained a temple and brazen statue of Poseidon.
