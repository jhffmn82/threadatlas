Abstract written by a model

The Struthophages' bird is a large bird partly resembling a beast, with a long neck, round body, wings, small head, strong thighs and legs, and forked feathers. Flightless but swift-running, it uses wind to move and throws stones from its feet; low wind makes it easily caught.

The Struthophages' bird is a large creature with features of both a bird and a beast: it has a long neck, round body, wings, strong thighs and legs, and forked feathers. It cannot fly high but runs extremely swiftly, can use wind to move with its wings, and throws stones from its feet; when the wind is low, it is easily caught.
