Mortimer Tregennis in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mortimer Tregennis, a thin, dark, spectacled, stooping member of the Tregennis family, lodges at the vicarage while his siblings live at Tredannick Wartha. He spends an evening playing cards with Owen, George, and Brenda, leaves shortly after ten, and later discovers their condition with Dr. Richards and reports it to Sherlock Holmes. Though he recalls something outside the window, his footprints show that he walked swiftly to the vicarage shortly before the others were stricken. He is suspected of causing the first tragedy. Later, he dies in his vicarage room in terror and convulsions after a substance is burned in his lamp. Dr. Leon Sterndale identifies him as the victim of revenge, claiming Mortimer stole devil's-foot poison and used it against his family for money.

The Adventure of the Devil’s Foot

Mortimer Tregennis spends the evening with Owen, George, and Brenda at Tredannick Wartha and leaves them playing cards in good spirits shortly after ten o’clock. He discovers the family’s condition the next morning with Dr. Richards, reports the events to Sherlock Holmes, and recalls seeing or sensing something moving outside the sitting-room window.

It was not until long after we were back in Poldhu Cottage that Holmes

Mortimer Tregennis left the room shortly before the card-players were stricken. His footprints were traced through the wet, sandy path and indicated that he walked swiftly toward the vicarage.

I may have commented upon my friend’s power of mental detachment, but

Mortimer Tregennis dies in his room at the vicarage after displaying the same terror and convulsions seen in the earlier Tregennis tragedy. Holmes finds evidence that a substance was burned in his lamp and reasons that Mortimer may have caused the first tragedy. Holmes considers suicide possible but notes reasons against that conclusion.

Fortunately, there is one man in England who knows all about it, and I

Mortimer Tregennis is identified as the victim of Dr. Leon Sterndale's revenge. Sterndale claims that Mortimer stole devil's-foot root, used it against his family for money, and died within five minutes after Sterndale exposed him to the poison.
