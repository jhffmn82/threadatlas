war between the Agrigentines and Syracusans in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

War in Sicily between Agrigentines and Syracusans arose after Syracusans pardoned Deucetius; Siculi divided, armies fought at Himera, Syracusans won, and Agrigentines sought peace.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ne H*ar between the AgngetUines and Sgracu»ans in Sicily. Uie

The war arose in Sicily after the Syracusans pardoned Deucetius and sent him to Corinth without consulting the Agrigentines. The Siculi divided between the two sides, large armies were raised, and the Syracusans defeated the Agrigentines at the river Himera; the Agrigentines then sought peace.
