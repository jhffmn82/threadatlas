the speaker’s beloved woman

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: dominae, formosas, puella, puellis, te, the faithless girl, tibi, you

Abstract written by a model

The speaker’s beloved is a tall, handsome, fair woman with rosy cheeks, small feet, and star-like eyes. Though accused of false oaths, repeated deception, rejecting the speaker, and taking other lovers, she remains beautiful and apparently unpunished. Her beauty sustains the speaker’s love, drawing him back despite resentment from beginning to end.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

III

The faithless girl swore falsely by her own eyes and by the speaker's eyes, but her appearance remains unchanged and beautiful. She is presented as deceiving both the speaker and, apparently, the gods without suffering punishment.

IX

The speaker’s beloved woman repeatedly rejects or deceives him, receives other lovers, and gives him false assurances. He resents her conduct but remains attracted to her beauty, which repeatedly draws him back.
