Strongyle

Also called: Strong/le, Strongylei

Abstract written by a model

Strongyle, formerly called Dia, was Naxus: Thracian settlers, including Butes, practiced piracy and held it over two hundred years until drought and famine drove them away.

Strongyle is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
