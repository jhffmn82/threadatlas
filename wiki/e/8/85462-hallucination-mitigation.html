Juntong Song, Xingguang Wang, Juno Zhu, Yuanhao Wu, Xuxin Cheng, Randy Zhong, and Cheng Niu · 2024-11

Hallucination mitigation is a research domain focused on hallucination. It uses contrastive decoding and factuality-based training with external knowledge bases or large-model confidence scores. It includes contrastive decoding, which encourages models to adhere to provided context, and factuality-based training methods that use external knowledge bases or large-model confidence scores.

Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

Hallucination mitigation is assessed by comparing model hallucination rates before and after training. The reported results show substantial reductions on both RAGTruth and the WebGLM 1000-example set, while naive regeneration performs worse according to GPT-4 Turbo and doubles generation time.
