medical graph construction

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Medical Graph Construction, construct the knowledge graphs, graph construction

Abstract written by a model

Medical graph construction uses medical documents for knowledge graphs through semantic chunking, entity extraction, triple and relationship linking, producing tiered graphs connecting RAG documents, medical papers, books, and UMLS.
