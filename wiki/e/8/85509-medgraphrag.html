MedGraphRAG in Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Junde Wu, Jiayuan Zhu, Yunli Qi, Jingkun Chen, Min Xu, Filippo Menolascina, Vicente Grau · 2024-10-15

MedGraphRAG, also called Medical GraphRAG, is a graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework and method for the medical domain. It is designed to enhance Large Language Models by generating evidence-based medical responses, official medical terms with explanations, credible source documentation, and definitions, with the aim of improving safety, reliability, and accuracy when handling private medical data and complex medical reasoning. Building on GraphRAG, it begins by constructing knowledge graphs from documents through Triple Graph Construction, then organizes and summarizes those graphs in a hierarchical structure that clarifies relationships. It next uses U-Retrieval to retrieve source information and generate comprehensive responses; reported settings retrieve the top 60 entities and their 16-hop neighbors, using four levels on GPT-4 and five levels on other models, with OpenAI text-embedding-3-large for embeddings. Evaluations cover nine medical Q&A benchmarks, two health fact-checking benchmarks, and one long-form generation dataset, alongside human evaluation on MultiMedQA and DiverseHealth. The records report improvements over no-retrieval baselines, standard RAG, GraphRAG, and medical LLMs, including state-of-the-art results across all 11 datasets and the strongest human-evaluation ratings. Its hierarchical approach also efficiently incorporates additional data, with improvements over 6% using Med-Paper data and over 8% using Med-Dictionary data. A case example recommends bisoprolol for managing COPD and heart failure. Future work focuses on real-time data updates and validation on real-world clinical data.

Abstract

MedGraphRAG is a graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework designed for the medical domain. It uses Triple Graph Construction and U-Retrieval, and it reportedly outperformed state-of-the-art models across the evaluated benchmarks while providing credible source documentation and definitions.

Introduction

Medical GraphRAG is a graph-based retrieval-augmented generation method specialized for medicine. It is designed to produce evidence-based responses and official medical-term explanations, and evaluations report that it outperforms classic retrieval-augmented generation and GraphRAG across nine medical Q&A benchmarks.

Method / Medical Graph Construction / Step1: Semantic Document Chunking .. Step6: U-Retrieval

MedGraphRAG first constructs knowledge graphs from documents, then organizes and summarizes the graphs, and finally retrieves data to respond to user queries.

Experiment / Dataset / RAG data / Repository data / Test Data .. Case Study

MedGraphRAG uses U-Retrieval with four levels on GPT-4 and five levels on other models, retrieving the top 60 entities with their 16-hop neighbors. It improves accuracy over no retrieval and GraphRAG, performs especially well on smaller models, achieves new state-of-the-art results with larger models, and receives the strongest human-evaluation ratings.

Analysis / Ablation Study based on GraphRAG .. Which is important? Data or Method

MedGraphRAG is evaluated alongside RAG and GraphRAG using external RAG data. It efficiently handles additional data through its hierarchical structure, clarifies relationships, and shows strong improvements as more data is added. It achieves improvements of over 6% with Med-Paper data and over 8% with Med-Dictionary data.

Conclusion

MedGraphRAG improves the reliability of medical response generation through a graph-based RAG framework. It shows strong performance on benchmarks and human evaluations, particularly for accuracy in complex medical reasoning.
