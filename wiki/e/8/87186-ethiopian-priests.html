DIODORUS SICULUS- / BOOK III.

Ethiopian priests are devoted to purity and religious service, shave their heads, wear distinctive attire, and carry sceptres resembling ploughshares. At Meroe they possess authority to command kings to die, until Ergamenes rejected their commands and killed the priests at the golden temple.
