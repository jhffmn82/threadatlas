Abstract written by a model

Babylon’s wall surrounds the city, built of brick cemented with brimstone. It extends 360 furlongs, though others give 365, with 250 stately turrets, great height and breadth, space from houses, morass defenses, and room for six chariots abreast.

The city wall is described as extending 360 furlongs in circuit, or 365 furlongs according to other writers, and as being made of brick cemented with brimstone. It has numerous stately turrets, great height and breadth, and a two-hundred-foot space separates it from the houses; morasses provide natural defenses in some places.
