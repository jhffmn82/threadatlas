the copper kettle in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The copper kettle is one of two copper vessels brought to the lake by Flathead Su-dic. After he uses one vessel's contents against Queen Coo-ee-oh, he leaves the kettle on the sand, poisoned enough to kill every fish in the lake. Ervic finds and cleanses it, fills it with fresh water, and carries three fishes in it to Reera's cottage. He protects it from upsetting, and the fishes remain inside until Reera removes and transforms them into three young women.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

The Su-dic brings two copper vessels to the lake. He uses one to throw its contents into Queen Coo-ee-oh's face when she approaches him in a submarine.

Chapter Sixteen

Ervic finds the copper kettle on the sand where the Su-dic forgot it. He cleanses it of the poison it had contained, fills it with fresh water, and carries the three fishes in it toward the purple cottage.

Chapter Eighteen

Ervic carries the copper kettle into Reera's cottage and carefully prevents it from upsetting when the crocodile knocks him from the bench. He keeps it beside him and later draws it up to the table during the meal.

Chapter Nineteen

The three fish live in the copper kettle until Reera removes them and lays them on the floor for transformation. After she touches each fish, they become three young women.
