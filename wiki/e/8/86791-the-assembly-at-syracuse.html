Abstract written by a model

A deliberative civic body convenes after the Athenian defeat to decide the prisoners’ fate, weighing Diocles’ harsh decree against Hermocrates’ objection and Nicholaus’ plea for clemency.

The next day an assembly was called^ to consider what was to

The assembly is called to decide what should be done with the prisoners. Diocles presents a harsh decree, Hermocrates objects to it, and Nicholaus addresses the people in support of clemency.
