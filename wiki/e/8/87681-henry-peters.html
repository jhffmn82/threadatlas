Henry Peters

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Holy Peters, Mr. Peters, Peters, ex-missionary friends, our opponent, the man who lives here

Abstract written by a model

Henry Peters, an unscrupulous Australian criminal from Adelaide, formerly used the alias Rev. Dr. Shlessinger and exploited lonely women’s religious feelings. With Mrs. Peters, and identified alongside accomplice Fraser, he became involved in a plot against Lady Frances, pawning her jewellery at Bovington’s. He denied knowing her, claimed she had left after paying bills with jewellery, and tried to stop Holmes’s search, but the plan to bury her was thwarted.

The Disappearance of Lady Frances Carfax

Holy Peters is identified as the true identity of Dr. Shlessinger. He is described as an unscrupulous Australian criminal who beguiles lonely women by exploiting their religious feelings, with an Englishwoman named Fraser as his accomplice.

Henry Peters, formerly the Rev. Dr. Shlessinger, denies knowing Lady Frances and claims she left him and Mrs. Peters in London after giving them jewellery to pay her bills. He attempts to prevent Holmes from searching the house and calls the police, but his household’s plan to bury Lady Frances is thwarted.
