unified hallucination detection model in RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Juntong Song, Xingguang Wang, Juno Zhu, Yuanhao Wu, Xuxin Cheng, Randy Zhong, and Cheng Niu · 2024-11

The unified hallucination detection model is a Llama-3-8B Instruct-based model within the RAG-HAT pipeline. It takes a RAG output as input, identifies whether its sentences contain hallucinations, and provides human-readable descriptions of conflicts or baselessness; these descriptions guide GPT-4 Turbo in revising the output. It is trained exclusively for QA scenarios on multiple-domain QA datasets, using retrieved documents and artificially fabricated hallucinated answers that are critical but minimally different from gold answers. Selective sampling supports generated-data quality and correctness. Training proceeds in two stages, first label prediction and then interpretations, whose models operate in a cascade at inference. It does not encompass summarization or answers generated from structured data. On RAGTruth answer-level hallucination detection, it achieves 83.9% overall F1 and outperforms the Llama-2-13B base model. It was not evaluated in expert-knowledge domains such as finance and medicine because specialized annotators were unavailable.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The detection model receives a RAG output and identifies hallucinations in it. It provides human-readable descriptions explaining information conflicts or baselessness, which guide GPT-4 Turbo in revising the output.

Related Work / Hallucination Detection .. Hallucination Mitigation

Ravi et al. (2024) trained it on QA datasets from multiple domains using retrieved documents and artificially fabricated hallucinated answers that were critical but minimally different from gold answers. It is trained exclusively on QA scenarios and does not encompass summarization or generating answers based on structured data.

Methodology / Hallucination Detection Model Training / 4.1.1 .. Data Source Extension

The hallucination detection model identifies whether sentences contain hallucinations and provides descriptions of the identified occurrences. It is trained first for label prediction and then for interpretations, and the two models are used in a cascade during inference.

Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

The detector is a Llama-3-8B Instruct-based model fine-tuned for hallucination classification. It is used as one of the metrics for evaluating RAG-HAT, although it cannot fairly evaluate naive regeneration because the detector triggers that regeneration.

Limitions

The authors did not evaluate their model in domains requiring expert knowledge, including finance and medicine, due to a lack of specialized annotators.
