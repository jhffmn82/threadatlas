Dido and Aeneas's love affair in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Dido and Aeneas’s love affair begins in a rain-driven cave through Venus’s influence, then ends in Dido’s enduring love, shame, and accusations of Aeneas’s faithlessness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII

Dido recalls that the affair began when rain drove them into a cave, which she interprets as a sign of her doom. She continues to love Aeneas even while complaining of his faithlessness and feeling shame before Sychaeus.
