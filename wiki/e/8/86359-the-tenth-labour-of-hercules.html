the tenth labour of Hercules

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it was the tenth labour imposed upon Hercules, in which he drove away Gerion’s oxen from Iberia, defeated Chrysaor’s three sons, and took the herds. In The Library, it was the tenth labour of Hercules, requiring him to fetch the red kine of Geryon from Erythia.
