Pandarus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Pandarus, a Trojan warrior and guardian of the gate, is Bitias’s brother. After Bitias is killed, he opens and closes the gate, admits Turnus, confronts him, and is killed by Turnus’s sword.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

After Turnus kills his brother Bitias, Pandarus opens the heavy gate and admits some of his comrades while shutting others outside. He foolishly allows Turnus to enter, then confronts him and is killed when Turnus cleaves his head and face with a sword.
