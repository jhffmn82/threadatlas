Mr. Roundhay

Abstract written by a model

Mr. Roundhay is the vicar of Tredannick Wollas parish and an amateur archaeologist interested in local antiquities. Acquainted with Sherlock Holmes, he brings Mortimer Tregennis to Holmes and recounts the tragedy at Tredannick Wartha. Knowing of Dr. Leon Sterndale’s relationship with Brenda Tregennis, he telegraphs Sterndale in Plymouth after deaths and insanity strike the Tregennis family. Later, he urgently reports Mortimer’s death with the same symptoms and takes Holmes and Watson to the vicarage.

Mr. Roundhay is in one work on this site, His Last Bow. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
