Answer Predictor in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11/2024-08-16

Answer Predictor is a component of REANO that performs inference over unstructured retrieved passages and a generated structured knowledge graph. Conditioned on a question, it uses a graph neural network, including a GNN module and REM module, to identify, select, and refine relevant knowledge triples. Based on FiD, it then uses a T5-based model to generate answers from the relevant passages and generated knowledge graphs.
