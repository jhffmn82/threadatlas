Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

183 Am I, then, never to embrace you except when

The speaker longs to embrace the addressee and declares that he will attempt to cross the sea despite swollen waves. He hopes either to reach the addressee safely or to have death end his anxious love, and he promises to follow the letter himself with little delay.
