CHAP. V.

The Great Pyramid was attributed to Chemmis and had a square base measuring seven hundred feet on each side and a height of more than six hundred feet. It was built of solid marble, reportedly with three hundred and sixty thousand workers over nearly twenty years, and its laborers' herbs and onions were said to have cost more than sixteen hundred talents.
