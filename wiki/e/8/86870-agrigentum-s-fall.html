As wc were not willing to let these things pass altogether, so we shall

Agrigentum fell after its defenders and inhabitants were weakened by famine and the departure of their allied forces. Amilcar entered the abandoned city, killed almost everyone he found, and destroyed or removed its religious and civic wealth.

CHAP. 3(111.

Agrigentum's fall caused widespread panic and led refugees to seek safety in Syracuse or Italy. It also intensified accusations against Syracuse's commanders and enabled Dionysius to present himself as a necessary defender.
