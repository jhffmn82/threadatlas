Camilla’s death

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Camilla slain, dying lady, her death, the fate that dooms her to be slain

Abstract written by a model

Camilla’s death is a foretold battlefield killing in the approaching war. Diana foresees it and orders Opis to avenge her killer and preserve Camilla’s burial. While pursuing Chloreus’s golden equipment, Camilla is struck in the breast by Aruns’s concealed javelin, sends Acca to Turnus, dies, and reignites both armies’ fury.

Camilla’s death is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

While they debate, nor these nor those will yield,

Diana foresees that Camilla will die among her enemies. She commands Opis to avenge Camilla against whoever kills her, whether Trojan or Italian, and promises that Camilla’s body will be carried away and buried with her arms unspoiled.

She said. The faithful nymph descends from high

Camilla is fatally wounded in the breast by Aruns while she is intent on taking Chloreus’s golden equipment. She gives Acca a final message for Turnus, then dies on the plain, and her death renews the fury of both armies.
