Colonel Valentine Walter in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Colonel Valentine Walter, younger brother of Sir James Walter, lived at the Walter residence and incurred a Stock Exchange debt. Needing money, he became the insider in the theft of the Bruce-Partington plans and was guilty of treason. He confessed to helping Oberstein dispose of Cadogan West’s body, denied delivering the fatal blow, was imprisoned, and died near the end of his second year of sentence.

Colonel Valentine Walter, the younger brother of the late Sir James Walter, stole the Bruce-Partington plans because he needed money to pay a Stock Exchange debt.
