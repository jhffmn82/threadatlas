Reinforcement Learning for Readability Control (RLRC)

Abstract written by a model

Reinforcement Learning for Readability Control is a method and strategy used by RAG-RLRC-LaySum to fine-tune summary readability, making scientific content comprehensible to non-specialists. It takes a plain LED first-generation lay summary, uses ground truth as expected output, and applies PPO with a Gaussian-based reward to penalize large differences from the desired readability level.

Methodology .. 2.3

RLRC takes the first generation from the plain LED model and uses the ground truth as the expected output. It applies PPO to optimize a reward based on the difference between a generated summary's readability and a desired readability level.
