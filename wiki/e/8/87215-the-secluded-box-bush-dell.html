Abstract written by a model

A secluded, pretty Central Park spot, marked by a circle of box and a green bench beneath a box bush, is Van Bibber and his fiancée’s favorite meeting place. He arrives intending to bury the engagement ring, but finds her beneath the bush breaking off a sprig.
