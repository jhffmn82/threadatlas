Abstract written by a model

Acestes is an elderly king of Trojan lineage in Sicily, a friend, kinsman, and host to Aeneas. When the Trojan fleet arrives, he welcomes the Trojans with food and royal gifts for Anchises’s funeral games. Though an old companion of Entellus, he still joins the sports: he urges Entellus to face Dares, then wins honor in archery when his arrow catches fire, receiving Aeneas’s prize and laurel. Later, old and fearful Trojans are entrusted to him, and he rules the newly founded Acesta.

Acestes is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

BOOK V

Acestes sees the Trojan fleet approaching Sicily and hastens to welcome Aeneas. As a king of Trojan lineage, he provides the Trojans with food and contributes royal gifts to the games.

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

Acestes urges Entellus to defend the prize against Dares and later competes in the archery contest. His arrow catches fire as it flies, and Aeneas interprets the event as a divine miracle, awards him the prize, and crowns him with laurel.

Thus far the sacred sports they celebrate:

Acestes is identified as a friend and as being of Trojan kind. Nautes advises Aeneas to entrust the old and fearful to Acestes, who becomes ruler of the new settlement.
