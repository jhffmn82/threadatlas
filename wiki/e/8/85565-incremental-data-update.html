incremental data update in LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zirui Guo, Lianghao Xia, Yanhua Yu, Tu Ao, Chao Huang · 2025

Incremental data update lets LightRAG integrate newly extracted entities and relationships into its existing graph without reconstruction, keeping it current and responsive as new information arrives; GraphRAG must reconstruct community reports for comparably sized new data.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Evaluation / Experimental Settings / RAG Performance Comparison (RQ1) .. Case Study

Incremental data update is analyzed as part of the cost and adaptability evaluation. LightRAG integrates newly extracted entities and relationships into its existing graph, whereas GraphRAG must reconstruct its community reports when a comparably sized new dataset is introduced.

Conclusion .. Limitations

Incremental updating enables LightRAG to incorporate new information and remain current and responsive over time.
