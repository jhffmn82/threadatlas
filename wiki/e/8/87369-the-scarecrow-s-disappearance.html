the Scarecrow's disappearance in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Scarecrow's disappearance begins after the travelers leave the Emerald City, when he leaves without saying goodbye and does not return home. It causes Dorothy uneasiness; she and the Cowardly Lion search unsuccessfully, and Dorothy's account makes finding him Sir Hokus's new quest.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

The Scarecrow's disappearance causes Dorothy great uneasiness because he leaves without saying goodbye and has not returned to his home. Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion search for him but do not learn what happened to him in this unit.

CHAPTER 5

Dorothy tells Sir Hokus that the Scarecrow has disappeared after the travelers left the Emerald City. She and the Cowardly Lion are trying to find him, and this search becomes Sir Hokus's new quest.
