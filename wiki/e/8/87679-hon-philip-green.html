Abstract written by a model

Hon. Philip Green is a former wild youngster, son of Admiral Green, who later makes money in Barberton and remains deeply devoted to Lady Frances Carfax despite her earlier rejection of him for his conduct. In London, as Sherlock Holmes’s client, he seeks her after following her from Lausanne to Baden and Montpellier. He follows Mrs. Peters to Poultney Square, discovers a coffin being delivered, restrains himself on Holmes’s orders, and ultimately helps nurse the recovered Lady Frances.

Hon. Philip Green is in one work on this site, His Last Bow. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Disappearance of Lady Frances Carfax

Hon. Philip Green is the bearded Englishman who follows Lady Frances Carfax from Lausanne to Baden and Montpellier. He tells Sherlock Holmes that he loved Lady Frances, had once been rejected because of his conduct, and was trying to find her; he says he lost control when Dr. Watson accused him of hurting her.

Philip Green repeatedly seeks news of Lady Frances and follows Mrs. Peters from the pawnbroker’s to No. 36, Poultney Square. He reports seeing a coffin delivered to the house, obeys Holmes’s instruction not to use violence, and later arrives to help nurse Lady Frances.
