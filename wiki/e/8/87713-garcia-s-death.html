Garcia's death in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Garcia's death is a murder on Oxshott Common, occurring before rain began around one o'clock during or after a criminal enterprise for which he arranged an alibi. Attacked from behind, he is killed by heavy blows to the head and beaten long after death. Nothing is stolen and clues are unclear; Holmes suspects the enterprise's intended target caused it.
