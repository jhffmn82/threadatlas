Menes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Menes is an Egyptian legislator, heroic and famous for a commendable life, who first instituted written laws in Egypt, claiming Mercury gave laws meant to greatly benefit the public.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

• And now it is necessary for us to speak of the legislators of Egypt^

Menes is described as a heroic and famous man who was the first in Egypt to institute written laws. He claimed that Mercury had given him the laws and that they would greatly benefit the public.
