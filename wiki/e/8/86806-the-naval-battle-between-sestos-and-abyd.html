occurs_between Sestos [and Abydos] The naval battle between Sestos and Abydos was fought between the Athenian and Lacedaemonian fleets. The Athenians, aided by superior pilots and the arrival of twenty-five additional ships, defeated the Lacedaemonians, capturing many allied ships while losing five themselves.
