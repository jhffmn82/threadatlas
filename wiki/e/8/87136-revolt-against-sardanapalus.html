Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IL

The revolt was organized by Arbaces and Belesis with the Medes, Persians, Babylonians, and an Arabian king, and involved four hundred thousand soldiers from four provinces. After three initial defeats, the rebels were joined by the Bactrian army, defeated Sardanapalus's forces, and besieged Nineveh.
