Abstract written by a model

Dexippus, a Lacedaemonian living at Gela, aided Agrigentum by bringing five hundred soldiers and directing its defense. He later joined a war council, where he was accused of treachery and of accepting fifteen talents to urge Italian commanders to withdraw.

Dexippus, a Lacedaemonian living at Gela, came to the aid of the Agrigentines with five hundred soldiers and directed their defense. He later joined the council of war, was accused of treachery, and was reported to have accepted fifteen talents to advise the Italian commanders to withdraw from Agrigentum.
