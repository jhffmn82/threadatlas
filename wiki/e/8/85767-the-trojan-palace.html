Abstract written by a model

Priam’s household’s royal palace, initially enclosed by wood and sheltering Priam, is breached at night; Greeks enter its halls, kill defenders, and drive the royal household to seek shelter.

“’Twas in the dead of night, when sleep repairs

The palace is initially set apart from the streets by a surrounding wood, but its location does not protect it from the assault. The Greeks breach its doors, enter its halls and apartments, and kill its defenders while the royal household seeks shelter.
