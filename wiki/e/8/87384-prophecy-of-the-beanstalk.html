prophecy of the beanstalk in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The prophecy of the beanstalk foretells that Emperor Chang Wang Woe’s spirit will enter the first being to touch the magic beanstalk on the other side of the world and return fifty years later to save his people. When the Scarecrow arrives, the Grand Chew Chew declares it fulfilled and demands he face the danger to save the Silver Islanders.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The prophecy states that the spirit of Emperor Chang Wang Woe will enter the first being who touches the magic beanstalk on the other side of the world and will return fifty years later to save his people. The Grand Chew Chew declares that the Scarecrow's arrival fulfills the prophecy.

CHAPTER 8

The prophecy of the beanstalk promises that the Scarecrow will save the Silver Islanders. The Grand Chew Chew uses it to insist that the Scarecrow, as Emperor Chang Wang Woe, must face the danger.
