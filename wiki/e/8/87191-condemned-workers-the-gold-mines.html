Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We sliall now speak a little of those inhabiting the southern parts>

The condemned workers include criminals, war captives, falsely accused persons, and others who have angered the king, along with some of their relatives. They are bound in fetters, forced to work without rest, poorly clothed, and driven to labor despite sickness, injury, lameness, old age, or women's infirmities; many die from their misery.
