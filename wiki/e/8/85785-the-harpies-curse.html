the Harpies’ curse

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: dismal famine, famine, fierce famine, impending curse

Abstract written by a model

foretells famine [before the Trojans build their promised walls] The Harpies’ curse states that the Trojans will reach the Italian shores but will suffer fierce famine before building their promised walls. Celaeno says the famine is imposed because the Trojans attacked the Harpies and drove them from their home.
