Methodology / Hallucination Detection Model Training / 4.1.1 .. Data Source Extension

Selective sampling evaluates the binary sentence label produced by GPT-4 Turbo. When the label is incorrect, the data is regenerated until the label is correct or the specified attempt limit is reached.
