Abstract written by a model

The mistress’s guardian is a eunuch who strictly blocks Ovid’s access to the beloved mistress. Unable to share mutual sexual love, he is urged to accept gifts, gain honor and savings through silence, permit secret meetings, and avoid punishment for exposing an affair.

LIBER SECUNDUS

The mistress’s guardian, addressed as Bagoas, prevents Ovid from safely approaching the woman. Ovid urges him to stop earning hatred, accept gifts for keeping silent, and allow secret meetings, warning that a guardian who reveals an affair may be punished.
