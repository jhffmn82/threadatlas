Phaleron

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Pbaleron, Phalerum

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Phaleron served as the Athenians' port before the Piraeus harbor was developed. In Description of Greece, Phalerum was the Athenians' port before Themistocles established Piraeus because it was nearer to Athens.

Phalerum in Description of Greece

Pausanias · about AD 155 to 175 · the work’s page · read it where Phalerum first appears

Abstract written by a model

is_a port [before Themistocles made Piraeus the port] Phalerum was the Athenians’ port before Themistocles established Piraeus, because it was nearer to Athens. Menestheus was said to have sailed from there to Troy, and the area contained temples of Artemis, Demeter, Sciradian Athene, and Zeus, as well as several altars.
