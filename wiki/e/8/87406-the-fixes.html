Abstract written by a model

The Fixes are inhabitants of Fix City with large round heads and unusually long flat feet. Alike in simple gendered clothing, they stand in chalked circles while furniture moves, then sit to eat and stare at Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion as unusual runabouts.

CHAPTER 12
