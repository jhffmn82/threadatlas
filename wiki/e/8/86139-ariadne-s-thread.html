Ariadne's thread

object · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: a clue, clue of thread, fila, the clue, thread, thread gathered up again, thread given to Theseus, λίνον

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the thread given to Theseus guides his steps and shows him the way back through the Labyrinth. In The Library, the clue of thread is given to Theseus for entering and leaving the Labyrinth. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Ariadne's thread guides Theseus through the Labyrinth and back to the entrance.
