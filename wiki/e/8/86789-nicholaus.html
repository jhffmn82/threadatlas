Nicholaus

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: the old man

Abstract written by a model

Nicholaus is an aged Syracusan elder, supported by servants, who lost two sons in war. Despite this loss, he urges mercy for Athenian prisoners, arguing clemency would bring Syracuse honour, peace, and moderation.

Nicholaus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
