Nicholaus is an aged Syracusan elder, supported by servants, who lost two sons in war. Despite this loss, he urges mercy for Athenian prisoners, arguing clemency would bring Syracuse honour, peace, and moderation.

Nicholaus, an aged man who has lost two sons in the war, speaks in favour of mercy toward the Athenian prisoners. He argues that clemency would benefit Syracuse by bringing it honour, encouraging peace, and displaying moderation.
