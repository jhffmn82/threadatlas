Abstract written by a model

The siege of Mitylene followed a naval engagement: Callicratides, having taken the harbour outside the walls, landed troops and besieged the defended city on every side. Eteonicus maintained the siege while Callicratides sailed away, then withdrew the forces after learning of defeat.

CHAP. X.

After gaining control of the harbour outside the walls, Callicratides landed his men and besieged Mitylene on every side. The city had prepared defenses and was strengthened by Athenians and Mityleneans who entered it for protection.

CHAP. XIV.

The siege of Mitylene was being maintained by Etonicus while Callicratides sailed to meet the Athenians. After learning of the defeat, Etonicus withdrew his ships to Chios and marched his land army away to a neighboring city.
