Cydippe's rival suitor in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Cydippe's rival suitor, promised her by her father, remains healthy while pursuing an unjust claim that causes her illness and danger; Acontius urges her to reject and bar him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

nam quod habes et tu gemini verba altera pacti, 155

The rival suitor is presented as the cause of Cydippe’s illness and danger because he continues to pursue an unjust claim. Acontius urges Cydippe to bar him from her threshold and reject his suit.
