Minos's death

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Minos’s death, death, his dtrath, ἀνῃρέθη

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Minos's death is described as being caused by Cocalus, who suffocated him in a hot bath. In The Library, Minos's death is described as occurring at Cocalus's court, where Cocalus's daughters killed Minos by pouring boiling pitch over him while he bathed.
