Minos's death

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Minos’s death, death, his dtrath, ἀνῃρέθη
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Minos's death is described as being caused by Cocalus, who suffocated him in a hot bath.
  • In The Library, Minos's death is described as occurring at Cocalus's court, where Cocalus's daughters killed Minos by pouring boiling pitch over him while he bathed.

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

is_caused_by Cocalus [through suffocation in a hot bath] Minos's death occurred when Cocalus kept him in a hot bath until the steam and heat suffocated him. The Cretans buried him with great ceremony in a double sepulchre and established a temple to Venus near the visible upper part.
