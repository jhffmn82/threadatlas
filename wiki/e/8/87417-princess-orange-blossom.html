Abstract written by a model

Princess Orange Blossom, a richly clad, cross-looking elderly Princess and sister of the King of the Golden Islands, is proposed as bride for the eldest Prince. She arrives to marry the Emperor, persists after the ceremony is disrupted, swoons when the Cowardly Lion attacks her palanquin, and is carried away by order of the Scarecrow, whom she intends to marry.

CHAPTER 15

Princess Orange Blossom is presented as the proposed bride of the eldest Prince. She is described as a richly clad, cross-looking Princess of at least seventy-five and as a sister of the King of the Golden Islands.

CHAPTER 20

Princess Orange Blossom arrives in a palanquin to marry the Emperor and refuses to leave even after the ceremony is disrupted. She swoons when the Cowardly Lion leaps at her palanquin, and the Scarecrow orders her bearers to carry her away.
