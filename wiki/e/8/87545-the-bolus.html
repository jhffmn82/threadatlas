the Bolus in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

The Bolus is a simple veterinary remedy for chills, carried in a basin by Hatcham and administered by the Dean to Dandy. Presented as a remedy, it was intended by the Dean to affect the race outcome. Blore secretly introduced a foreign element, and analysis found sixteen grains of strychnine.
