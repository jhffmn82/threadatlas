Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VII.

Dionysius gathered craftsmen from his dominions, Italy, and Greece, organized the production of weapons and ships, constructed harbor facilities, and recruited soldiers from Sicily, Greece, and especially Lacedaemon. He ultimately assembled an army of about eighty thousand foot soldiers and more than three thousand horsemen for the campaign.
