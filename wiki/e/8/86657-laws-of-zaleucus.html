laws of Zaleucus

topic · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: laivs of Zaleucus, laws, precepts, the laws of Zaleuchus

Abstract written by a model

The laws of Zaleuchus begin by attributing good to the gods, requiring purity in Locris, and valuing righteous action over costly sacrifice. They demand prompt reconciliation after animosity and condemn implacability as fierce and inhuman. They regulate magistrates against pride, domination, and biased judgment. They also restrain women’s attendants, nighttime walks, and luxury, while barring men from gold rings or Milesian garments unless marked by dishonorable guilt.

But having spoken enough of Charondas, we shall now relate

The laws of Zaleuchus began with the worship of the gods and included rules on reconciliation, the conduct of magistrates, and the behavior and adornment of women and men. They restricted women's attendants, nighttime walks, and luxurious ornaments through dishonorable exceptions.
