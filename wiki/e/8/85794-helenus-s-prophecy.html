Helenus’s prophecy

Also called: prophetic Phoebus

Abstract written by a model

Helenus’s prophecy guides Aeneas’s Trojan voyage to Italy, warning of dangers and Greek-held shores, prescribing honor to Juno and consultation at Cumae, and culminating in the sow omen and establishment of the Trojan state.

“The prophet first with sacrifice adores

Helenus’s prophecy describes the distant route to Italy, the dangers of Sicily, the straits, the sow omen, the need to honor Juno, and the consultation with the Sibyl at Cumae. It also directs Aeneas to avoid certain Greek-held shores and predicts the establishment of the Trojan state.
