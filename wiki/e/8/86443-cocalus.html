Cocalus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cocalus, ruler of Sicily, courteously receives and befriends Daedalus, protects him from Minos, then causes Minos’s death in a hot bath and disguises it as an accident.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

Cocalus was the ruler of Sicily who received Daedalus courteously and made him his close friend. He protected Daedalus from Minos and caused Minos to be suffocated in a hot bath, afterward presenting his body to the Cretans as though he had accidentally fallen into the bath.
