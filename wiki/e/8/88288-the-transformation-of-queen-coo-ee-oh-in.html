Abstract written by a model

This event is the enchantment by Su-dic that transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan as punishment after she defies him. It occurs when she drops a rope and falls into water, after the Flatheads have gone away. Her new form is a large white swan with tiny diamond feathers, a polished-gold bill, amethyst eyes, and webbed feet. The change removes her sorcery, former knowledge, power, palace, and ability to rule or care for the Skeezers. Because she alone could command the island to rise and control its magical mechanisms, her transformation leaves the Skeezers unable to raise it.

Chapter Twelve

Coo-ee-oh is suddenly transformed into a Diamond Swan after the Flatheads have gone away. The transformation punishes her but also removes the only person who knew how to control the island's magical mechanisms.

Chapter Thirteen

Chapter Sixteen
