the transformation of Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

This event is the enchantment by Su-dic that transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan as punishment after she defies him. It occurs when she drops a rope and falls into water, after the Flatheads have gone away. Her new form is a large white swan with tiny diamond feathers, a polished-gold bill, amethyst eyes, and webbed feet. The change removes her sorcery, former knowledge, power, palace, and ability to rule or care for the Skeezers. Because she alone could command the island to rise and control its magical mechanisms, her transformation leaves the Skeezers unable to raise it.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

Queen Coo-ee-oh is transformed when she falls into the water after dropping the rope. The Diamond Swan is large and white, covered with tiny diamonds, and has a polished-gold bill and amethyst eyes; her webbed feet and loss of sorcery leave her powerless.

Chapter Twelve

Coo-ee-oh is suddenly transformed into a Diamond Swan after the Flatheads have gone away. The transformation punishes her but also removes the only person who knew how to control the island's magical mechanisms.

Chapter Thirteen

Queen Coo-ee-oh's transformation is carried out by the Flathead Su-dic, who changes her into a Diamond Swan. Since she alone could command the island to rise, her transformation leaves the Skeezers unable to raise it.

Chapter Sixteen

The Flathead Su-dic's magic transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan. Afterward, she abandons the Skeezers and shows no further care for her people.

Chapter Seventeen

The Su-dic transformed Queen Coo-ce-oh into a Diamond Swan after she defied him. The transformation removed her magic and former knowledge, although Glinda considers her loss of power, palace, and ability to rule the Skeezers a punishment.
