the massacre of the bridegrooms

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: caede, caedis, caesos, crime, death, deeds, killed, mass killing of the bridegrooms, murder, shedding of blood, slew their bridegrooms, the murder
  • In Heroides and Amores, it is the event in which the Danaids kill the sleeping sons of Aegyptus during the night, leaving Lynceus alone escaped by dawn as Hypermestra confirms the dying groans.
  • In The Library, it is the killing of the sons of Egyptus by the daughters of Danaus while the bridegrooms slept; Hypermnestra spares Lynceus, while the others bury the bridegrooms’ heads at Lerna and honor their bodies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV

During the night, the Danaids kill the sleeping bridegrooms, and Hypermestra hears and confirms the groans of the dying. At dawn Danaus counts the dead and finds that Lynceus alone has escaped.

is_a killing of the sons of Egyptus [by the daughters of Danaus while the bridegrooms slept] After Danaus agreed to the marriages, he gave his daughters daggers, and they killed the sons of Egyptus while the bridegrooms slept.
