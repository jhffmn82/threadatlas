the massacre of the bridegrooms

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: caede, caedis, caesos, crime, death, deeds, killed, mass killing of the bridegrooms, murder, shedding of blood, slew their bridegrooms, the murder

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, it is the event in which the Danaids kill the sleeping sons of Aegyptus during the night, leaving Lynceus alone escaped by dawn as Hypermestra confirms the dying groans. In The Library, it is the killing of the sons of Egyptus by the daughters of Danaus while the bridegrooms slept; Hypermnestra spares Lynceus, while the others bury the bridegrooms’ heads at Lerna and honor their bodies.
