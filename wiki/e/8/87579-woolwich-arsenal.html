  • In His Last Bow, Woolwich Arsenal employs clerk Arthur Cadogan West and houses a confidential office safe from which ten Bruce-Partington plans are removed, seven later being found on West.
  • In His Last Bow, Woolwich contains the government office from which the papers were taken, where Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson investigate and learn that West took the 8:15 train to London and that the office had been entered or observed through a window.
