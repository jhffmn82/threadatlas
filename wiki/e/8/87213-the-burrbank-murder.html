RAEGEN THE OTHER WOMAN THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

The Burrbank murder is one of the two major news stories occupying the newspapers. It concerns Richard F. Burrbank's killing, the opening of his safe, the theft of $200,000, and the disappearance of his secretary, Stephen S. Hade.
