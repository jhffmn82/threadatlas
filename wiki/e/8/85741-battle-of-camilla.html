Battle of Camilla in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Battle between the Trojan–Tuscan alliance and Latian forces, marked by repeated charges, retreats, deaths, and Camilla’s victories until her death collapses the Volscians and other Latin forces; it ends at night with armies entrenched before the town.

The battle involves the Trojan and Tuscan forces against the Latins and Camilla’s troops. It features repeated charges, shifting advances and retreats, many individual deaths, Camilla’s killing of numerous opponents, and her eventual death, after which the Volscians and other Latin forces collapse.
