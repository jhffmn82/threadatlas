The archery contest uses bows to shoot at a dove tied atop a mast: Hippocoon hits the mast, Mnestheus frees the dove, Eurytion kills it, and Acestes wins the chief prize with a fiery omen.

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

The archery contest uses a dove tied to the top of a mast as its target. Hippocoon strikes the mast, Mnestheus cuts the cord and frees the dove, Eurytion kills it in flight, and Acestes receives the chief prize after his arrow produces a fiery omen.
