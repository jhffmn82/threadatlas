Roman imperial rule

Also called: Rome’s dominion, awful empire, empire ocean, imperial arts, rule mankind, the Roman empire, the subject world

Abstract written by a model

Roman imperial rule is a foretold, boundless dominion over the subject world, without limit of years. Through Caesar of the Julian stock, it is said to extend to ocean and sky, ruling mankind by making the world obey, arranging peace and war, taming the proud, and freeing fettered slaves.

BOOK I

The Roman empire is foretold to rule the subject world without assigned bounds or a term of years. Jupiter says that Caesar from the Julian stock will rise, with an empire bounded only by the ocean and a fame bounded only by the skies.

Where long extended plains of pleasure lay:

Roman imperial rule is described as the art of ruling mankind, making the world obey, arranging peace and war, taming the proud, and freeing the fettered slave. These powers are presented as especially worthy of Rome.
