Megareans in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Megareans inhabit Megara. Fearing Corinthian strength, they allied with Athenians and, aided by Myronides, resisted Corinth. They later revolted from the Athenian people, allied with Sparta, sought aid against an embargo, and were defeated after Athenians invaded and plundered their country.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIX.

The Megareans, fearing their weakness against Corinth, formed a league with Athens. With Athenian assistance under Myronides, they resisted the Corinthian army.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

The Megareans revolted from the Athenians and made a league with Sparta through their ambassadors. The Athenians invaded their country, plundered it, and defeated the Megareans when they came out to defend it.
