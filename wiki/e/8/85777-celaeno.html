Celaeno in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Celaeno, a Harpy, messenger of Fate, and queen of the Furies, remains after the Harpies retreat, curses the Trojans, and foretells Italy, famine, and promised walls.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III

Celaeno, identified as the messenger of Fate and the queen of the Furies, remains after the Harpies retreat. She warns Aeneas that his people will reach Italy but will suffer severe famine before building their promised walls.
