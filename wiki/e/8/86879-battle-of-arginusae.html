A sea battle near Arginusae pitted 150 Athenian ships against 140 Lacedaemonian ships; Athens won despite losing twenty-five ships to seventy-seven Peloponnesian losses.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIV.

The battle was fought between an Athenian fleet of 150 ships and a Lacedaemonian fleet of 140 ships near the islands of Arginusae. The Athenians won, losing twenty-five ships, while the Peloponnesians lost seventy-seven.
