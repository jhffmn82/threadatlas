Sybaris in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Sybaris was a city in Italy between the Crathis and Sybaris rivers. Its fertile territory made it wealthy and populous, with three hundred thousand inhabitants. Under Telys it banished wealthy citizens; after defeat by the Crotonians it was sacked, rebuilt by Thessalians, then they were expelled.

Sybaris became wealthy and populous because of its fertile territory between the Crathis and Sybaris rivers, and it was said to have three hundred thousand inhabitants. Under the influence of Telys, it banished five hundred wealthy citizens and confiscated their estates. After the Crotonians defeated its army, the city was sacked and laid waste; although it was rebuilt by the Thessalians, they were later expelled by the Crotonians.
