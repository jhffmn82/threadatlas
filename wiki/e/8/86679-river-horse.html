Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The river horse is a large, four-footed, cloven-hoofed animal, five cubits long, living in the Nile by day and feeding on grass and corn at night until groups hunt, wound, and bleed it to death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

The river horse is described as a large, four-footed, cloven-hoofed animal that lives both on land and in water. It feeds on grass and corn at night and is hunted by groups who wound it with iron instruments and let it bleed to death.
