Egyptian colonization in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Egyptian colonization is the Egyptian claim that, from Osiris and Isis to Alexander the Great, colonies settled in Babylon, Argos, Colchos, among the Jews, and at Athens. Belus and Danaus supposedly led settlements, but the claims end judged unsupported by sufficient evidence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

From Osiris and Isis, to the reign of Alexander the Great, who

The Egyptians claim that colonies from Egypt settled in Babylon, Argos, Colchos, among the Jews, and at Athens. Belus is said to have led a colony to Babylon, Danaus to Argos, and the Athenians to have descended from the Saites; the text concludes that these claims are not supported by sufficient evidence.
