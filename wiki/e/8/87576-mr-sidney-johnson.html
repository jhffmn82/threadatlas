Abstract written by a model

Mr. Sidney Johnson is a senior clerk and draughtsman at Woolwich Arsenal's government office, married with five children. A habitual last leaver and one of two safe-key holders, he locked away the Bruce-Partington plans on Monday, then said he closed at five and denied stealing the three most important missing drawings.

Mr. Sidney Johnson, the senior clerk, says that he closed the government office at five on Monday and placed the plans in the safe. He explains the office's key arrangements and confirms that the three missing drawings were considered the most important, while denying any involvement in the theft.
