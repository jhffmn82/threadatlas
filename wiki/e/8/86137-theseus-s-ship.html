Theseus's ship

object · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: craft, keel, puppis, ratem, ship, ship of Theseus, the ship, τὴν ναῦν

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Theseus's ship carries Theseus away from Ariadne while she remains on the shore; Ariadne says that no other ship is available to carry her away and begs Theseus to turn it back. In The Library, Theseus's ship carries Theseus, Ariadne, and the rescued children from Crete toward Athens; Theseus forgets to change its black sail, leading Aegeus to believe that he has died.
