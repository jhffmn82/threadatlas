Bocchoris

Abstract written by a model

Bocchoris is the fourth Egyptian lawmaker, a wise and prudent king. He regulated royal matters, merchandise, and contracts through laws and rules whose excellent judgments remained remembered. Though physically very weak, he was extraordinarily covetous.

Bocchoris is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

• And now it is necessary for us to speak of the legislators of Egypt^

Bocchoris is described as the fourth Egyptian lawmaker, a wise and prudent king who regulated royal matters and established rules for contracts. His judgments were considered so excellent that many were still remembered, although he was said to have been physically weak and extraordinarily covetous.
