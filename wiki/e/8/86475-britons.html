Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

For over against the French shore^ opposite to the Hercynian

The Britons are the inhabitants of Britain and are said to have been compelled by Julius Caesar to pay tribute. They are described as using chariots in battle, living in simple cottages, storing ears of grain underground, and being governed by several kings and princes who were generally at peace with one another.
