the marriage of Deïanira and Hercules

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Hercules's marriage to Deianira, having given her hand to Hercules, married Deianira, she shall marry him, the recompense of the important services
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is described as Hercules’s marriage to Deianira at Calydon after Meleager’s death; Deianira was the daughter of Oeneus.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is presented as Deïanira’s reward to Hercules for the important services he rendered to her father, Œneus, after overcoming Acheloüs and proving himself the bravest suitor.
