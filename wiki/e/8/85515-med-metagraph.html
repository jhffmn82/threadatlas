Junde Wu, Jiayuan Zhu, Yunli Qi, Jingkun Chen, Min Xu, Filippo Menolascina, Vicente Grau · 2024-10-15

Med-MetaGraph is an implementation and ablated component added after the GraphRAG baseline. It incorporates entity structure and semantic document chunking incrementally, producing gradual gains: accuracy rises from 88.9% to 90.4% on MedQA, 77.9% to 78.5% on PubMedQA, and 77.2% to 78.1% on MedMCQA.

Experiment / Dataset / RAG data / Repository data / Test Data .. Case Study

Med-MetaGraph is an ablated component added after the GraphRAG baseline. Its addition raises accuracy from 88.9% to 90.4% on MedQA, from 77.9% to 78.5% on PubMedQA, and from 77.2% to 78.1% on MedMCQA.

Analysis / Ablation Study based on GraphRAG .. Which is important? Data or Method

Med-MetaGraph is incrementally incorporated into the GraphRAG baseline through features including entity structure and semantic document chunking. Its inclusion contributes to the gradual performance improvements observed in the ablation study.
