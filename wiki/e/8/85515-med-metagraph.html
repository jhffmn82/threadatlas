Med-MetaGraph

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Med-MetaGraph implementation

Abstract written by a model

Med-MetaGraph is an implementation and ablated component added after the GraphRAG baseline. It incorporates entity structure and semantic document chunking incrementally, producing gradual gains: accuracy rises from 88.9% to 90.4% on MedQA, 77.9% to 78.5% on PubMedQA, and 77.2% to 78.1% on MedMCQA.

Med-MetaGraph is in one work on this site, Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
