Grand Chew Chew

Abstract written by a model

Grand Chew Chew is a Silverman and the aged grand, chief court official who ruled the Silver Islands for fifty years before the Scarecrow’s return. In Chapter 7, he proclaims the Scarecrow to be Emperor Chang Wang Woe returned, explains the prophecy, seeks relief from state cares, supplies royal clothing, and accompanies the Scarecrow through the realm. He then serves as prime minister and minister, advises the Scarecrow on imperial etiquette, objects to informal conduct, and warns of the Golden Islands’ king and the beanstalk prophecy. After the victory, he organizes a celebration and becomes more agreeable under the Scarecrow’s reforms. In Chapter 15, he publicly announces Chang Wang Woe’s promised restoration and secretly confers with General Mugwump.

Grand Chew Chew is in one work on this site, The Royal Book of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The Grand Chew Chew, the ruler of the Silver Islands for fifty years, proclaims the Scarecrow to be Emperor Chang Wang Woe returned. He explains the prophecy, asks the Scarecrow to relieve him of the cares of state, provides him with royal clothing, and accompanies him to inspect the realm.

CHAPTER 8

The Grand Chew Chew serves as one of the Scarecrow's ministers and objects to the Scarecrow's informal behavior. He is left to take the Scarecrow's place during the walk with Tappy Oko, then warns that the King of the Golden Islands will burn the island and enslave its people, citing the prophecy of the beanstalk.

CHAPTER 15

The Grand Chew Chew announces that the Great and Imperial Chang Wang Woe will be restored to his rightful shape the next day through the Grand Gheewizard's magic formula. He joins General Mugwump in secretly conferring while the Scarecrow senses that something unusual is happening.
