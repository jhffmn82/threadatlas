Althæa in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

Althæa is the mother of Meleager and sister of Plexippus and Toxeus. She first rejoices in Meleager’s victory, but grief and vengeance follow when he kills her brothers. Torn between being mother and sister, she devotes Meleager to the Furies and burns the fatal billet preserved from his birth, killing him. Afterward, she takes her own life with a sword.

Althea in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Althea, queen of Calydon and Meleager's mother, preserves his life-controlling brand, burns it to avenge her brothers after he kills them, then takes her own life.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MEDEA AND AESON

Althea is Meleager's mother and preserves the fatal brand that controls his life. After Meleager kills her brothers, she burns the brand despite her conflict between maternal love and the desire to avenge them, and then takes her own life.
