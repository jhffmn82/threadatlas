the riding game in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The riding game, taught by Ascanius and brought to Alba, is received by Rome from the Latins; boys ride in three troops, maneuver, dart, pursue, and reunite.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

The riding game features Ascanius and two other youthful leaders guiding three troops of mounted boys. The riders form lines, divide and reunite, throw harmless darts, pursue and evade one another, and finally march together.
