Llama3-70B-instruct in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

Llama3-70B-instruct is model software evaluated on RSNA-RadioQA, where it had 66% ± 5 accuracy (53/80) without RadioRAG and recognized insufficient information when context was irrelevant. RadioRAG raised reported performance to 69% (55/80), not significantly, and was not significantly different from a human expert. It sometimes erred, including neurofibromatosis type 1; appendix results report 50% average accuracy and context-relevant incorrect-response rates of 14% and 18%.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

Llama3-70B increased from 66% (53/80) to 69% (55/80) on RSNA-RadioQA with RadioRAG, without a statistically significant difference (P=0.30). Its RadioRAG-powered performance was not significantly different from that of the human expert radiologist on RSNA-RadioQA (P=0.21).

4. Discussion

Llama3-70B-instruct was particularly adept at recognizing when it could not provide an informed answer, although it occasionally failed to do so. In the example involving scoliosis, epistaxis, and a pulmonary nodule, it incorrectly identified neurofibromatosis type 1 when powered by RadioRAG.

appendix

Llama3-70B achieved an average accuracy of 50% with RadioRAG and 50% without RAG on the RSNA-RadioQA dataset. Its context-relevant incorrect-response rate was 14% on RSNA-RadioQA and 18% on ExtendedQA.
