Van Bibber's fiancée in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Van Bibber's fiancée is a young, lovely, cultured woman, knowledgeable about the Louvre, painters, and cathedrals. After accepting a breakup and returning her engagement ring, she goes to their secluded box-bush dell, tearful, kisses a sprig of box, and is approached by Van Bibber.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" A WALK UP THE AVENUE He came down the steps slowly, and pulling mechanically at his gloves.

Van Bibber's fiancée has accepted the breakup and returned the engagement ring, but she goes to their secluded box-bush dell soon afterward. She breaks off a sprig of box, raises it to her lips, and has a tear on her cheek when Van Bibber approaches her.
