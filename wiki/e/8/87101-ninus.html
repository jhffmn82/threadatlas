Ninus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ninus, the first recorded king of Assyria, is warlike and ambitious for honour and glory. He allies with Arieus, conquers neighboring peoples and broad Asian territory, builds Nineveh, marries Semiramis during the Bactrian expedition, and fathers Ninyas. After his death, Semiramis becomes regent.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK IL

Ninus was the first Assyrian king recorded in history and was warlike, ambitious, and successful in conquest. He subdued many countries, built Nineveh, captured Bactria, fathered Ninyas by Semiramis, and died leaving Semiramis as regent.
