Chapter 6

The Emerald City underground cavern is a large, dark chamber directly beneath the royal palace and shaped like it. Ruggedo discovers it while digging a secret passage, takes possession of it, and fills it with stolen furnishings, six history rocks, tunnels, cushions, a lamp, a rocker, and Peg Amy. He has lived there secretly for almost two years, using it as his private kingdom and base for future schemes.
