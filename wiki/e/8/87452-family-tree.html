family tree in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

In the Royal Book of Oz, family trees become a concern when Professor Wogglebug questions what guests descended from. Scraps claims a clothes tree, while the Scarecrow is said to have a bean pole; Wogglebug's remarks distress him and spur his search for his own family tree.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Professor Wogglebug asks the guests where their family trees are and what they descended from. The Scarecrow is said to have only a bean pole for a family tree, while Scraps claims a clothes tree; the Scarecrow later sets out to find his own family tree.

CHAPTER 4

Professor Wogglebug's rude remarks about the Scarecrow's family tree leave the Scarecrow looking distressed. Dorothy thinks he may be worrying because Professor Wogglebug told him he had no family.
