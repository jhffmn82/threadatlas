Abstract written by a model

The three transformed fishes are a group of three Adepts in Sorcery: one goldfish, one silverfish, and one bronzefish. Formerly Adepts at Magic, they were transformed and remain together as companions in the lake. Their survival protects Queen Coo-ee-oh's magic and safety; if destroyed, she would be weakened. They also threaten Su-dic, whom Queen Coo-ee-oh says they will destroy in time. Dorothy searches for them so Ozma can restore them, while the Wizard believes their restoration may reveal how to raise the sunken island. Later, they swim quietly in Ervic's kettle, advise him not to take Reera's magic powder, and consent to be transformed into three beautiful girls and Adepts at Magic. They become three young women, revealed as Audah, Aurah, and Aujah, restored to their proper forms.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The three transformed fishes are the three Adepts in Sorcery in altered forms. Their survival protects Coo-ee-oh's magic, while their destruction would weaken her and their restoration could allow the Adepts to return and punish Rora and Su-dic.

Chapter Eleven

The three magic fishes remain a threat to the Su-dic after Queen Coo-ee-oh's transformation. Queen Coo-ee-oh says that they will destroy him in time.

Chapter Twelve

The three transformed fishes are gold, silver, and bronze, and Dorothy believes they remain together as companions. She searches among the many fishes in the lake for them so Ozma can restore them to their proper forms.

Chapter Seventeen

The three fishes were formerly Adepts at Magic from whom Queen Coo-ce-oh stole much of her knowledge. The Wizard believes that restoring them to their former shapes could reveal how to raise the sunken island, although Glinda does not know how to identify them among the lake's many fish.

Chapter Eighteen

The three fishes remain swimming quietly in Ervic's kettle throughout his visit. Reera wants to transform them, but Ervic objects and cleverly suggests transforming them into three girls who are Adepts at Magic.

Chapter Nineteen

The three fish advise Ervic not to take the magic powder because only Reera knows how to use it. They consent to becoming beautiful girls and Adepts at Magic, promise to leave whenever Reera commands, and are transformed into three young women.

Chapter Twenty-One

The three fishes were actually Audah, Aurah, and Aujah, the three Adepts transformed by Coo-ee-oh. The Adepts have been restored to their proper forms.
