Abstract written by a model

The first Tregennis tragedy is a nighttime calamity affecting the Tregennis siblings. After Mortimer Tregennis leaves them playing cards in good spirits, Brenda is found dead and Owen and George are found terrified and insane, without signs of intrusion or violence. Holmes investigates at the vicar’s and Mortimer’s request and concludes that Mortimer likely put a poisonous substance in the fire, causing madness or death.
