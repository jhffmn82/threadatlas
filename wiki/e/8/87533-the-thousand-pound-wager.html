the thousand-pound wager in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Georgiana Tidman proposes a thousand-sovereign wager tied to becoming richer by the next night and to her position in the Deanery. Alongside this, a race wager using the Dean’s fifty pounds was meant to back Dandy Dick for the Restoration Fund, but Blore placed it on Bonny Betsy.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

Georgiana says that if she is not a richer woman by a thousand pounds the following night, she will have had a bad day. She also jokingly offers to make a thousand-sovereign wager concerning her position in the Deanery.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

The race wager was made with the Dean's fifty pounds and was intended for Dandy Dick on behalf of the Restoration Fund. Blore mistakenly placed it on Bonny Betsy after altering the bolus.
