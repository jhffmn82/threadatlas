Abstract written by a model

foretell the flight from Troy [interpreted as divine approval] A lambent flame appears around Ascanius’s head without harming him. Soon afterward, thunder sounds and a streaming light crosses the sky toward Ida; Anchises interprets these signs as divine confirmation that the family should flee.
