the omens concerning Ascanius

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a lambent flame, the glad presage, the sacred fire, these omens
  • In The Aeneid: foretell the flight from Troy [interpreted as divine approval] A lambent flame appears around Ascanius’s head without harming him. Soon afterward, thunder sounds and a streaming light crosses the sky toward Ida; Anchises interprets these signs as divine confirmation that the family should flee.
