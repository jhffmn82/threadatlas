Messapus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Messapus, Neptune’s warlike son, is secure from steel and destined to survive fire. As the struggle begins, he leads Faliscans, other Italian peoples, Tyrrhene troops, and Latian horse to support Turnus and the Latins against Trojan and Tuscan forces.

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now the mighty labour is begun

Messapus, son of Neptune, appears secure against steel and fated to escape fire. He leads the Faliscans and other Italian peoples to Turnus, and his troops march in order while singing of their sea-born king.
