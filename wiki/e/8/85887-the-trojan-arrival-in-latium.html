Trojan arrival in Italy in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Trojan arrival in Italy occurs when Aeneas's fleet reaches the mouth of the Tiber and the Trojans unknowingly fulfill the Harpy's prophecy by eating their tables. Aeneas interprets Iulus's words as an omen, recognizes the land as their promised home, and sends envoys to seek friendship with Latinus.
