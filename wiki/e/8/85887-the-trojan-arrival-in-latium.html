the Trojan arrival in Latium

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, the Trojan arrival in Latium fulfills the prophecies concerning a foreign fleet and prince, as the Trojans enter the Tiber, settle near the destined place, and send an embassy to Latinus. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Trojan arrival in Italy occurs when Aeneas's fleet reaches the mouth of the Tiber, the Trojans unknowingly fulfill the Harpy's prophecy by eating their tables, and Aeneas recognizes the land as their promised home and sends envoys to Latinus.
