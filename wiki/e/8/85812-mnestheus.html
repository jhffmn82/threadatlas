Mnestheus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Mnestheus is a Trojan leader who commands the Dolphin in a naval race, advancing after Sergesthus’s accident to finish second to Cloanthus. Later, commanding the Trojans’ army, he rallies fleeing defenders and leads them to surround and overwhelm Turnus.

BOOK V

Mnestheus commands the Dolphin and advances from behind after Sergesthus’s accident. He overtakes Sergesthus and Gyas but finishes second to Cloanthus.

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

Mnestheus rallies the broken Trojan defenders after Turnus’s slaughter inside the camp. He rebukes the Trojans for fleeing from one warrior and leads them in surrounding and overwhelming Turnus.
