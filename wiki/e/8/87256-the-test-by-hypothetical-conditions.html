the test by hypothetical conditions

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a month to live, such a choice, test by hypothetical conditions, the conditions which you suggest, the thirty-day hypothetical, those thirty days, trick

Abstract written by a model

A test based on hypothetical conditions asks young Latimer to imagine thirty days before death with no moral or legal consequences, then choose between Ellen and another woman. The bishop treats Latimer’s refusal as an answer. Ellen used the trick to learn what he would say about his past love. Latimer condemns it as impossible, unjust, needless, and horrible, saying it confuses them by treating the unreal and impossible as significant.

" THE OTHER WOMAN Young Latimer stood on one of the lower steps of the hall stairs, leaning with one hand on the broad railing and smiling down at her.

The bishop asks young Latimer to imagine that he has only thirty days to live and that his conduct during those days would carry no moral or legal consequences. He asks whether Latimer would spend that time with Ellen or with another woman. Latimer rejects the question as impossible and unjust to answer, while the bishop treats the refusal as an answer.

The test by hypothetical conditions is the trick Ellen used to discover what Latimer would say about his past love. Latimer calls it a silly, needless, and horrible mistake that confuses and stultifies them by treating what is not real or possible as significant.
