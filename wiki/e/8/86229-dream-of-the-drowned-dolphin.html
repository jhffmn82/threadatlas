dream of the drowned dolphin

Abstract written by a model

A dream experienced before dawn depicts a dolphin swimming through wind-tossed waters, then cast onto thirsty sands where wave and life leave it. A girl fears its meaning, but a sacrifice wards against its threat and is considered to have cleared it.
