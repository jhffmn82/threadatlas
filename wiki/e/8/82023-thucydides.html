Thucydides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Thucydides, an Athenian historian, begins a history of the war between Athenians and Lacedaemonians, called the Peloponnesian war, and ends after recounting its first twenty-two years in eight books, or nine in some divisions.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Thucydides begins his history in this unit and proceeds to relate the war between the Athenians and the Lacedaemonians, called the Peloponnesian war. The text says that he wrote about the first twenty-two years of the twenty-seven-year conflict.

CHAP. 111.

Thucydides ended his history at this point, having covered twenty-two years of affairs in eight books, or nine according to some divisions. Xenophon and Theopompus began their histories where Thucydides ended.
