Abstract written by a model

The gold fish is a gold-colored transformed Adept, one of three fishes into which Coo-ee-oh transformed the Adepts. Its survival matters because destroying it would make Coo-ee-oh shrivelled and helpless and remove her stolen magic. Speaking softly and distinctly from the lake, it truthfully guides Ervic: tie Coo-ee-oh’s silver cord to the boat, carry the fishes in a copper kettle without spilling water, and observe Reera’s transformations. It urges letting Reera restore the fishes, promises not to harm or interfere, and becomes one of the three Adepts: a young woman with golden hair and blue eyes.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The gold fish is one of the three fishes into which Coo-ee-oh transformed the Adepts in Sorcery. Its destruction would cause Coo-ee-oh to become shrivelled and helpless and would remove her stolen magic.

Chapter Sixteen

The goldfish is one of the three Adepts and speaks to Ervic from the lake. It tells him to tie Coo-ee-oh's silver cord to the boat, later directs him to carry the fishes in the copper kettle, and warns him not to spill the water.

Chapter Eighteen

The goldfish gives Ervic instructions before he enters the cottage, telling him to remain there, observe Reera, and discover how she performs her transformations. It assures him that any enchantment placed on him can be easily broken.

Chapter Nineteen

The goldfish tells Ervic that the best plan is to let Reera transform the fish into their natural shapes. It agrees to become a beautiful girl and Adept at Magic, promises not to harm or interfere with Reera, and becomes a young woman with golden hair and blue eyes.
