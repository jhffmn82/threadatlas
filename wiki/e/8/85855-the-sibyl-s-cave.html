Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VI

The Sibyl’s cave is a large, artificially shaped cavern with a hundred doors and a hundred entrances. Its many openings echo the Sibyl’s words, and Apollo’s possession of her causes the cave and temple to shake.
