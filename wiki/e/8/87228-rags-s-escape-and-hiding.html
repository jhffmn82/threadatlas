Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" MY DISREPUTABLE FRIEND, MR.

Rags escapes from the rooftop by dropping back through the wooden trap and later hides beneath a pile of bedding in a dark room. He remains motionless while the police inspect the room, then stays concealed and spends the night caring for Margaret.

The sun rose hissing out of the East River, a broad, red disc of heat.

Rags considers escaping by having an old man row him across the East River to Astoria or by using a boat to reach the Jersey shore. He believes he could change his name, have money sent to him, and work until the matter was forgotten, but Margaret's condition keeps him from leaving.
