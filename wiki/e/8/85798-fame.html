Fame in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

A monstrous, winged, many-tongued phantom, Fame sleeplessly spreads and enlarges mixed truth and lies, carrying reports of Aeneas and Dido through Libyan cities to Hyarbas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

Fame spreads the report of Dido's relationship with Aeneas throughout the Libyan cities and eventually carries it to Hyarbas. She is portrayed as a vast, sleepless power who enlarges rumors, mixes truth with lies, and accuses Dido of abandoning honor and royal responsibility.
