  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books: includes chariots [use in warfare] The inhabitants of Britain are described as preserving their ancient customs, including the use of chariots in battle. They live in mean cottages covered mainly with reeds or sticks, store grain underground, and make bread from grain they bruise. They are characterized as sincere, honest, content with plain food, and strangers to excess and luxury.
