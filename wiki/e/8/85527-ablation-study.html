The ablation study evaluates the effects of Med-MetaGraph, Triple Graph Construction, and U-Retrieval by adding them incrementally to GraphRAG on the same RAG dataset. It finds that performance improves as the modules are added, with particularly significant gains from Triple Graph Construction and U-Retrieval.
