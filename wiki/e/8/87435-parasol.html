parasol in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The parasol, plucked from a beanstalk and fitted with a handle, enables flight when opened. It carries Dorothy's party upward, partly closes and drops them after a crash, then is reopened; later it slows descent and helps Dorothy and the Scarecrow pursue Sir Hokus.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 22

The parasol carries Dorothy's party upward through the passage. It crashes into something and partly closes, causing the party to fall, but Dorothy opens it wide again and it carries them upward faster than before.

CHAPTER 23

The parasol slows the party's movement when Dorothy partially lowers it and later carries them upward when she opens it. Dorothy and the Scarecrow use it to pursue Sir Hokus after the green branch takes him skyward.
