Grand Gheewizard in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Grand Gheewizard is a wizard of the Silver Island who lives in a dark cave in a silver mountain with a rheumatic dragon. He discovers a formula to break the Scarecrow's enchantment and prepares an elixir, consulting a yellow book and waiting for it to boil. Later he brings a sealed silver vase against the Scarecrow, but Dorothy's interference transforms the three Princes, and he is seized and taken to his cave.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 15

The Grand Gheewizard stirs a magic kettle in his cave and consults a large yellow book while preparing an elixir. He tells the youngest Prince that the elixir must boil for one more night and claims to have found the means to break the Scarecrow's enchantment.

CHAPTER 20

The Grand Gheewizard brings the sealed silver vase and prepares to use its magic on the Scarecrow. After Dorothy's interference causes the vase to transform the three Princes instead, the Scarecrow orders the Grand Gheewizard seized and taken to his cave.
