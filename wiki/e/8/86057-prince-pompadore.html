Prince Pompadore

person · 1 work · 56 quoted facts · 18 connections
Also called: Pompa, Pompadore, Prince, Prince Pompa, Prince of Pumperdink, Royal Prince of Pumperdink, a Prince, his Excellency, royalty, the Prince, the Prince of Pumperdink, the most charming Prince in the world, the once fastidious Prince of Pumperdink, young Prince
  • In Kabumpo in Oz: Prince Pompadore is the handsome Prince of Pumperdink, son of King Pompus and his queen, whose eighteenth birthday reveals a condition that he must marry a Proper Princess or Pumperdink and its people will disappear. He initially rejects marriage and is ordered to marry Princess Faleero, but, distressed by Queen Pozy's tears and his duty to save the kingdom, he agrees to seek a solution. Kabumpo secretly carries him from the palace, and the two travel toward the Emerald City, with Pompadore resolving to consider every princess they meet. Their journey brings trials and setbacks: Pompadore escapes Rith Metic by doing somersaults, falls into the Illumi Nation and loses his golden hair, and survives other dangers. In the Emerald City, he mistakes Glinda for Ozma, then believes rescuing Ozma from a giant in Ev may let him marry her and save Pumperdink. Joined by Peg Amy and Wag, he fights the Twigs, is injured and separated from supplies by the Runaway Country, and continues toward Ozma. When Ozma is found, Pompadore proposes, but she refuses. The Question Box reveals that he must marry the Princess of Sun Top Mountain. Though gloomy and reluctant to marry an unknown princess, he realizes at the gold castle that he would rather return to Pumperdink with Peg Amy. He then learns that Peg Amy is the Proper Princess, helps free her by recognizing and believing she is a princess, and marries her. Afterward, he travels with Peg Amy to the Emerald City and Pumperdink and is expected to visit Ozma and the others.

Prince Pompadore in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Prince Pompadore is the handsome Prince of Pumperdink, son of King Pompus and his queen, whose eighteenth birthday reveals a condition that he must marry a Proper Princess or Pumperdink and its people will disappear. He initially rejects marriage and is ordered to marry Princess Faleero, but, distressed by Queen Pozy's tears and his duty to save the kingdom, he agrees to seek a solution. Kabumpo secretly carries him from the palace, and the two travel toward the Emerald City, with Pompadore resolving to consider every princess they meet. Their journey brings trials and setbacks: Pompadore escapes Rith Metic by doing somersaults, falls into the Illumi Nation and loses his golden hair, and survives other dangers. In the Emerald City, he mistakes Glinda for Ozma, then believes rescuing Ozma from a giant in Ev may let him marry her and save Pumperdink. Joined by Peg Amy and Wag, he fights the Twigs, is injured and separated from supplies by the Runaway Country, and continues toward Ozma. When Ozma is found, Pompadore proposes, but she refuses. The Question Box reveals that he must marry the Princess of Sun Top Mountain. Though gloomy and reluctant to marry an unknown princess, he realizes at the gold castle that he would rather return to Pumperdink with Peg Amy. He then learns that Peg Amy is the Proper Princess, helps free her by recognizing and believing she is a princess, and marries her. Afterward, he travels with Peg Amy to the Emerald City and Pumperdink and is expected to visit Ozma and the others.

Narrative, section by section

15 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Prince Pompadore celebrates his eighteenth birthday at the royal feast and says he does not want to marry when the scroll reveals the royal marriage condition. The cake explosion knocks him beneath the table, but Kabumpo pulls him out and pours cream over his head.

Chapter 2

Prince Pompadore initially refuses to marry, but agrees after Queen Pozy weeps because he does not want Pumperdink to disappear. He is ordered to marry Faleero the next morning, is described by the gold mirror as a Charming Prince, and refuses Kabumpo’s suggestion that he run away.

Chapter 3

Pompadore sleeps uneasily on a couch without undressing. Kabumpo carries him from the palace and takes him away on his back, telling him that they are disappearing from Pumperdink.

Chapter 4

Prince Pompadore wakes in a dim forest with a rock under his head and discovers that Kabumpo carried him away during the night. He agrees to travel to the Emerald City, though he intends to consider every Princess they meet, and he tells the Curious Cottabus the full story of his birthday party.

Chapter 5

Pompadore travels with Kabumpo into Rith Metic and is forced to solve arithmetic problems. He follows the Curious Cottabus’s cryptic advice by turning repeated somersaults, which clears the streets and allows the travelers to escape, but he later vanishes after falling through a marble slab.

Chapter 11

Pompadore falls into the Illumi Nation with Kabumpo and is shocked when his golden hair is burned down to a short stubble. He is trapped under a brass snuffer, worries that he will never find a Proper Princess or save Pumperdink, and then escapes with Kabumpo through an uphill passage.

Chapter 12

Prince Pompadore catches and eats a carrot from the Soup Sea and then drinks several bowls of soup with buttered rolls. In the Emerald City he mistakes Glinda for Ozma until the magic mirror identifies her, then decides to rescue Ozma from the giant in Ev so he can become King of Oz and save Pumperdink.

Chapter 13

Prince Pompadore tells Peg Amy and Wag that a magic scroll requires him to marry a Proper Princess or his Kingdom and everyone in it will disappear. He believes Ozma may be the Proper Princess and is traveling to find her. He joins Peg Amy, Wag, and Kabumpo in going to Ev and plans to fight the giant who took the castle.

Chapter 16

Prince Pompadore travels with Kabumpo, Peg Amy, and Wag toward Ev while trying unsuccessfully to open Glegg’s Magic Box. He fights the Twigs with his sword and is left with seven scratches on his cheek and a badly torn velvet cloak, which Peg repairs.

Chapter 17

Pompa is captured and carried by the Runaway Country along with Peg Amy, Kabumpo, and Wag. He worries about reaching Ev and the giant who has taken Ozma’s palace, and Peg tells him to prepare his sword. He is thrown from the Country when it flees, suffers a black eye, loses the Magic Box, and is later helped to sit against a tree.

Chapter 18

Pompadore helps the others plan the rescue and asks the Question Box how to save Ozma. After Ozma arrives, he kneels and proposes marriage, but she refuses.

Chapter 19

Prince Pompadore explains his quest to Ozma and learns from the Question Box that he must marry the Princess of Sun Top Mountain. He reluctantly prepares to travel there with Kabumpo, Peg Amy, and Wag.

Chapter 20

Pompa remains gloomy about marrying the Princess of Sun Top Mountain and says he plans to leave after the wedding to spend his life traveling. On reaching the gold castle, he decides he would rather return to Pumperdink with Peg Amy than marry an unknown princess, but he learns that Peg Amy herself is the proper princess.

Chapter 21

Pompadore helps free Peg Amy by calling her a Princess and believing that she is one. He agrees to marry Peg Amy, is married to her at Sun Top Mountain, and travels with her to the Emerald City and Pumperdink.

Chapter 22

Pompa is expected to visit Ozma and the others with Peg Amy after the topsy-turvy palace is restored to order.
