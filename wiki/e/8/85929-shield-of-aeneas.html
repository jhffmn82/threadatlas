Abstract written by a model

A shield forged by Vulcan and the Cyclops from silver, brass, gold, and steel, it bears embossed Roman history: the rape of the Sabine women, Actium’s naval battle, and Caesar’s triumph. Aeneas carries it wonderingly, unaware of the scenes’ names.
