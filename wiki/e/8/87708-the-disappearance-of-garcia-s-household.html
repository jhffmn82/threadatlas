Abstract written by a model

The disappearance of Garcia's household is a major fact under investigation at Wisteria Lodge. By morning, John Scott Eccles finds Garcia, the foreign manservant, and the foreign cook gone; the house is deserted and Garcia's bed has not been slept in. Holmes first considers whether the servants fled because of a conspiracy, then concludes that Garcia's companions made a planned escape after he failed to return, avoiding investigation so they could renew the criminal attempt later.

The disappearance of Garcia's companions is one of the two major facts Holmes seeks to explain. He considers whether their flight resulted from their involvement in a conspiracy, while acknowledging that he does not yet have all the facts.
