the disappearance of Garcia's household in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The disappearance of Garcia's household is a major fact under investigation at Wisteria Lodge. By morning, John Scott Eccles finds Garcia, the foreign manservant, and the foreign cook gone; the house is deserted and Garcia's bed has not been slept in.
