Battle between the Athenians and the Thebans

Abstract written by a model

Battle between the Athenians and the Thebans ended in a decisive Athenian victory under Myronides over a larger Theban army, praised as equal in glory to Marathon and Plataea because Athens fought without confederates.

Battle between the Athenians and the Thebans is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. XXIV.

The battle ended in a decisive Athenian victory under Myronides. The text gives no detailed account of its order or manner, but considers it at least equal to Athens’s greatest earlier victories because the Athenians defeated the reputedly bravest Greeks without relying on confederates.
