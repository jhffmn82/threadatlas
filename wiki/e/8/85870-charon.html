Charon in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Charon, a grim, filthy god and boatman, rules the dreary coast and ferries ghosts in a leaky, canvas-sailed boat; initially refusing living Aeneas, he is appeased by the golden bough and carries Aeneas and the Sibyl across.

Narrative, section by section

Ye realms, yet unreveal’d to human sight,

Charon is the grim, filthy boatman who rules the coast of Acheron and ferries ghosts across the waters. He initially refuses Aeneas because he is living, but the golden bough appeases him and he carries Aeneas and the Sibyl over.

Charon in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Charon, called a ferryman in the Egyptian country language, pilots and governs a funerary ship carrying the dead across a lake in a ceremony Orpheus saw.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, VII.

Charon is the pilot who governs the ship prepared to carry the dead across the lake. The Egyptians call the pilot Charon, and the text says that Orpheus saw this ceremony in Egypt.

Charon in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Charon, an old ferryman of the dead, rows a boat on Acheron and unwillingly admits living Theseus and Pirithous.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Charon is represented as an old ferryman rowing a boat on Acheron. The Minyad says that he unwillingly admitted the living Theseus and Pirithous into his boat meant for the dead.

Charon in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Charon, an old, squalid yet strong ferryman, carries buried dead souls across Cocytus; he challenges living, armed Aeneas, then admits him and the Sibyl after seeing the golden branch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXV

Charon is an old, squalid, but strong ferryman who carries dead souls across the river Cocytus. He initially challenges Aeneas because Aeneas is living and armed, but the Sibyl displays the golden branch and Charon allows them aboard.
