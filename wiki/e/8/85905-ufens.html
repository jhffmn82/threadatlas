Abstract written by a model

Ufens, leader of Nursia’s mountain troops and ruler of the Equicolae, leads a hard people whose barren soil and harsh hearts sustain hunting, plundering, armed plowing, and readiness for battle.

And now the mighty labour is begun

Ufens leads the mountain troops sent by Nursia and rules the rude Equicolae. His people hunt and plunder, plow in arms, and remain prepared for battle because their soil is barren and their hearts are hard.
