Ligurians

Also called: Liguria, Ligutiao, the people here

Abstract written by a model

Ligurians are a group inhabiting Liguria, a rough, barren, mountainous, wooded land difficult to cultivate. Constant labour and scarce crops shape their hunting, gathering, tree-cutting, and rock-breaking. Men and women live in fields, hollow rocks, or caves; lean, strong, daring, lightly armed people also sail small vessels through dangerous seas.

The Ligurians lived in a rough, barren, mountainous, and heavily wooded country, where they worked constantly for little food. Men and women cut trees, broke rocks, hunted, and gathered wild plants; some lived in fields, hollow rocks, or caves. They were lean, sinewy, strong, daring, lightly armed, and willing to sail in small vessels through dangerous seas.
