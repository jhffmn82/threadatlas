Also called: Augmentation component, augmentation

Abstract written by a model

The augmentation component builds prompts supplying retrieved contexts or targets to an LLM and requesting concise answers with identifiers in square brackets. It varies SDG-explicit or SDG-implicit prompt type, few-shot example count, and passage order. Evaluation favors ranked passage order; a second example often helps Llama2-13b-chat but harms Llama2-13b.
