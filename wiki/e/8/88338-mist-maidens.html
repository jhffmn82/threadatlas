Abstract written by a model

Mist Maidens are fairies living in Mist Valley, with mist-colored hair and gray garments. Answering Ozma's call, they emerge from mist, carry Ozma and Dorothy over billows to a hillside, then return into mist.

Chapter Three

The Mist Maidens live in the Mist Valley and answer Ozma's call. They appear from the mist in gray, mist-like garments, carry Ozma and Dorothy over the billows to the opposite hillside, and then return into the mist.
