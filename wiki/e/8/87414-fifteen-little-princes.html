fifteen little Princes in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Fifteen little Princes, the Scarecrow’s solemn grandsons and sons of three Princes, formally greet him, deny Oz exists because it is absent from their map, and are returned by Happy Toko to the royal nursery.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

The fifteen grandsons greet the Scarecrow with formal bows and speak only in solemn, respectful phrases. They repeatedly say that Oz is not on their map and declare that they do not believe in Oz. Happy Toko takes them back to the royal nursery.
