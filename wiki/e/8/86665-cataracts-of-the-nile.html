Abstract written by a model

The cataracts are steep, rocky passages where the Nile plunges violently through narrow gulfs. Their whirlpools and rapids make navigation dangerous and prevent ships from sailing upstream, with the greatest cataract lying on the borders of Ethiopia and Egypt.
