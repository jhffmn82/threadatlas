Abstract written by a model

Grand Central station, also called Grand Central Depot, is a railway station where tickets are sold. Van Bibber brings a burglar there and buys him a ticket for Helena, Montana. It is also where Snipes takes an exhausted old farmer, who unexpectedly reunites with Abraham before they prepare to return home.

Grand Central station is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
