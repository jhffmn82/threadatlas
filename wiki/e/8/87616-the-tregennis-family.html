the Tregennis family

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Tregennis family, a family of tin-miners, the family, this family of Tregennis

Abstract written by a model

The Tregennis family was a family of tin-miners from Redruth that later sold its mining venture to a company. Disputes over dividing the proceeds caused a quarrel and estrangement involving Mortimer Tregennis, though the family reportedly reconciled. The family then suffered its first tragedy: terror, madness, and deaths associated with devil’s-foot poison in a room containing combustion. Holmes suspected, and Sterndale believed, that Mortimer attacked the others to become sole guardian of their joint property.
