invasion of Asia

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Agesilaus's campaign in Asia, Marching forward, Marching thence, drawing out his army into the plain of Caystrus, expedition, expedition to Asia
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is described as Agesilaus's campaign through Asia, involving plunder of Persian possessions, victories over Tissaphernes's forces, and a return to the sea.
  • In Description of Greece, it is described as the Lacedæmonians' invasion of Asia under Agesilaus, which achieved victories before the Corinthian War forced the army's return to Greece.

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

is_a military campaign [conducted in Asia] Agesilaus's campaign took him from Ephesus through the Caystrian plains, Mysia, the region near Sipylus, and toward Sardis. His army plundered Persian possessions, defeated Tissaphernes's forces, and later returned to the sea because the sacrifices did not indicate great success farther inland.

invasion of Asia in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Lacedæmonians resolved to invade Asia and attack Artaxerxes, sending Agesilaus as commander of the land forces. The expedition reached Asia Minor and achieved victories, but the Corinthian War forced Agesilaus to bring the army back to Greece.
