invasion of Asia

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Agesilaus's campaign in Asia, Marching forward, Marching thence, drawing out his army into the plain of Caystrus, expedition, expedition to Asia

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is described as Agesilaus's campaign through Asia, involving plunder of Persian possessions, victories over Tissaphernes's forces, and a return to the sea. In Description of Greece, it is described as the Lacedæmonians' invasion of Asia under Agesilaus, which achieved victories before the Corinthian War forced the army's return to Greece.
