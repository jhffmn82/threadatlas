Abstract written by a model

Tredannick Wartha house, the Tregennis house, is a large, bright dwelling with a considerable garden and the site of the first tragedy involving Mortimer Tregennis’s family. After Mortimer leaves the card-players, they are stricken; their cards remain on the table and chairs unmoved. Mrs. Porter says no one entered overnight. Holmes examines the house and reasons that a substance in the fire, with the closed window and chimney, controlled poisonous vapor.
