Abydos

place · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Abydeno, Abydoi, Abydon, the Asian side

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Abydos is Mindarus’s base, where he repaired his damaged ships before sailing toward Cyzicum. In Heroides and Amores, Abydos is Leander’s home and writing place opposite Sestos, whose people prevent his secret embarkation and whose travelers Hero seeks to question or ask to write to her husband. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Abydos is a town on the Asian side of the strait separating Asia and Europe and the home of Leander.

Abydos in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · about 24 to 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where Abydos first appears

Abstract written by a model

Abydos is Leander’s home and writing place, opposite Sestos across the Hellespont. Its people prevent his secret embarkation; Hero seeks travelers to ask after or write to her Abydos husband.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XVIII..

Abydos is Leander’s home and the place from which he writes. Its people watched the harbor, preventing Leander from secretly embarking with the bold sailor.

XIX

Abydos is Leander's place, and Hero repeatedly asks whether anyone has come from there or is going there so that she can inquire about him or write to him. She also refers to Leander as a husband from Abydos.
