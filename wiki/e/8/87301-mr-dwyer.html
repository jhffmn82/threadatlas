Abstract written by a model

Michael E. Dwyer is the sporting editor of the Press. After hearing Gallegher’s important report, he plans and joins a quiet trip to Torresdale with Gallegher and Detective Hefflefinger to secure Hade’s secret arrest, protect the story from competing papers, offer and share a $500 reward, and recommend Gallegher’s reinstatement.

Mr. Dwyer in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890 · the work’s page · read it where Mr. Dwyer first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mr. Dwyer is a Press representative and newspaper correspondent serving as sporting editor. During a raid, he writes in Dwyer’s notebook, identifies himself to Hefflefinger, and offers $500 for Hade’s secret arrest as the supposed burglar. He enters Keppler’s barn, remains near Hade during the fight, and signals or identifies him. After Captain Scott orders the correspondents arrested, Dwyer resists, entrusts Gallegher with his notebook and report, and is arrested; Gallegher delivers Dwyer’s prepared copy to the Press.
