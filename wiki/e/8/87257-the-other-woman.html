the other woman in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The other woman is alive and possibly married. Latimer loved her in the past, but they renounced their love when it became a crime or impossible, and she may have suffered silently. He suggests he might love her again if the obstacle between them ended.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

"Well, and suppose this other woman did exist, what then?

The other woman is described as someone Latimer loved in the past and might love again if the obstacle separating them ended. Latimer says she is not dead and suggests that she may be married.

The other woman is the person whom Latimer previously loved but renounced when their love became a crime or became impossible. Latimer says that both of them put their love away and that her silence may have involved suffering.
