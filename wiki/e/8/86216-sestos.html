Sestos

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Sesta, Sestds, Sestus, the European shore

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Sestos was the Athenian fleet’s anchorage when Dorieus approached the Hellespont and after the battle at Dardanium. In Heroides and Amores, Sestos was Hero’s land and the destination of Leander’s crossings. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Sestos was a town on the European side of the strait separating Asia and Europe where Hero lived as a priestess of Venus.
