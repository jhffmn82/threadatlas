Abstract

Diagnostic accuracy was investigated using 80 RSNA Case Collection questions and 24 expert-curated questions with reference standard answers. RadioRAG increased accuracy across most language models, with relative increases of up to 54%, although one evaluated model showed no improvement.

1. Introduction

Diagnostic accuracy is described as an area that artificial intelligence and large language models may improve. The study investigates whether RadioRAG improves the accuracy of language-model responses to detailed questions.
