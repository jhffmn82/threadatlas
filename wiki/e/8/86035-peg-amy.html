Princess of Sun Top Mountain in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Princess of Sun Top Mountain is a princess, Uncle Tozzyfog’s niece, and the proper bride the Question Box names for Prince Pompadore. Glegg’s enchantment turns her into a tree; Cap’n Bill carves the tree into the Wooden Doll Peg Amy, who becomes Trot’s friend. The Magic Mirror reveals Peg Amy as the lost princess, restoring her as a beautiful living princess. She reunites with Tozzyfog, helps bring happiness back to Sun Top Mountain, and agrees to marry Pompadore to save Pumperdink. Afterward she is Pompadore’s wife, Princess of Pumperdink, and possesses a necklace and bracelet from Kabumpo.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

The Question Box identifies the Princess of Sun Top Mountain as the Princess whom Prince Pompadore must marry. Pompadore is instructed to find her and bring her to the Emerald City after their marriage.

Chapter 20

The Princess of Sun Top Mountain is revealed to be Peg Amy when the Magic Mirror identifies her. She becomes a beautiful living princess while retaining the familiar twinkle in her eyes and pleasant curve of her mouth.

Chapter 21

Peg Amy is revealed to be the Princess of Sun Top Mountain and the niece of Uncle Tozzyfog. After her enchantment is broken, she reunites with Uncle Tozzyfog, helps restore happiness to Sun Top Mountain, and agrees to marry Pompadore so they can save Pumperdink.

Chapter 22

Peg Amy is the person Glegg had waited two years to regain control over. She and Pompa are expected to visit Ozma and the others after the topsy-turvy palace is set right.

Proper Princess in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The Proper Princess is the fairy princess Prince Pompadore must marry within the proper time to prevent Pumperdink and its people from disappearing. King Pompus identifies Princess Faleero because she is the only known fairy princess, while Kabumpo favors Ozma and Wag notes that the magic scroll names no one. Pompadore searches among princesses, increasingly worried his quest will fail after losing most of his hair.

Peg Amy in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Peg Amy begins as Trot’s favorite wooden doll, an old-fashioned, well-made figure carved by Captain Bill from yellow-tree wood. Stolen from the Emerald City palace garden by Ruggedo, she suffers his shaking, scolding, throwing, and other abuse, yet remains pleasant despite her damaged clothes and chipped nose. Ruggedo enlarges her from about ten inches to four feet and brings her to life with Re-animating Rays. Able to walk, skip, and run, she remembers his wickedness and plans against Ozma. Wag rescues and protects her, and she travels on his back while carrying Glegg’s magic box and seeking Ruggedo in Ev. As she journeys to help Ozma, Peg Amy discovers memories of an earlier life: a purple plush dress, the Winkie farmlands, and the country, sun, wind, and sky. Though made of wood, with stiff fingers and no need for food, she is clever, generous, and often the party’s most sensible member. She joins Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore, repairs Kabumpo’s robe, receives his pearl necklace, and offers herself to the Twigs rather than let Pompadore be struck. The Runaway Country declares her its Princess and future ruler, and she persuades it to stop for necessary supplies. When Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic opens, Peg Amy helps deal with Ruggedo, uses the Question Box, prepares the Trick Tea, and wishes Pompadore happiness. She asks Ozma to make the Runaway Country an island where she might settle, but Ozma instead promises her care in the Emerald City and sends her onward with Pompadore, Kabumpo, and Wag. Though Peg Amy fears that her doll form makes her unfit to accompany Pompa, the Magic Mirror reveals that she is the Princess of Sun Top Mountain and the Proper Princess. She then undergoes Peg Amy’s transformation and becomes a living princess.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Peg Amy is a wooden doll carved by Captain Bill for Trot and stolen by Ruggedo from the Emerald City. Ruggedo repeatedly shakes, scolds, throws, and abuses her, while Wag rescues and protects her. She remains pleasant-faced despite her chipped nose, missing pantalette, torn sleeves, and general wear.

Chapter 9

Peg Amy is enlarged from about ten inches to four feet by Ruggedo’s Expanding Fluid. The Re-animating Rays then bring her to life, allowing her to walk, skip, and run. She remembers Ruggedo’s plans against Ozma and tries to seize the magic box, but later stops Wag from attacking Ruggedo.

Chapter 10

Peg Amy emerges from the cave with Wag after Ruggedo’s final expansion and realizes that she has grown to about the height of the little girl who once played with her. She finds Glegg’s magic box, uses its Question Box to ask where Ruggedo is, and learns that he is in Ev. She travels on Wag’s back toward Ev, hoping to find Ruggedo and restore everything.

Chapter 13

Peg Amy travels with Wag toward Ev to help Ozma. She discovers that the Winkie countryside seems familiar and concludes that she has been alive before. She explains the effects of the Mixed Magic and agrees to travel with Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore.

Chapter 16

Peg Amy says she remembers being alive before and recalls a purple plush dress, the country, the sun, the wind, and the sky. She repairs Kabumpo’s robe, receives a pearl necklace from him, and hopes to help Prince Pompadore gain Ozma’s approval. During the Twig attack, she offers to be hit instead of Pompadore because she is made of wood.

Chapter 17

Peg Amy is scooped up with the others by the Runaway Country and is declared its Princess and future ruler. She calmly flatters the Country and persuades it to stop at another country for food, seeds, tools, and building supplies. After the Country flees from the giant and throws everyone into the air, Peg finds Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic and discovers that it is open.

Chapter 18

Peg Amy scatters the contents of Glegg’s Mixed Magic when Wag attacks Ruggedo and helps restrain him. She uses the Question Box, prepares the Trick Tea, and then runs away after wishing Pompadore happiness; the Scarecrow goes to find her.

Chapter 19

Peg Amy asks Ozma to let the Runaway Country become an island and offers to settle there. Ozma instead arranges for Peg Amy to travel with Prince Pompadore, Kabumpo, and Wag, and invites her to live in the Emerald City afterward.

Chapter 20

Peg Amy worries that her wooden-doll appearance will make her unsuitable company while Pompa meets the Princess, but Pompa and Kabumpo insist that she accompany them. When she looks into the Magic Mirror, it identifies her as Princess of Sun Top Mountain and the Proper Princess, after which she becomes a living princess.
