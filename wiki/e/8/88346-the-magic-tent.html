the magic tent in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

A handsome purple-and-white tent created instantly by Ozma's wand and mystic words appears on the plain, displaying Oz's royal banner and containing a furnished meal setting and beds, then disappears after the meal.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

The magic tent appears instantly on the plain after Ozma uses her wand. It is striped purple and white, bears the royal banner of Oz, and contains food, tableware, beds, chairs, and lamps before disappearing after the meal.
