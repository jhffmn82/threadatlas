Abstract written by a model

Tin is found in rocky ground in Belerium, mixed with veins of earth. Inhabitants melt and refine it, shape it into four-square pieces, carry it by carts at low tide to Ictis, and merchants transport it through France toward the mouth of the river Rhone.

For over against the French shore^ opposite to the Hercynian

Tin is dug from rocky ground in Belerium, where it is mixed with veins of earth. The inhabitants melt and refine it, beat it into four-square pieces, and carry it to Ictis; merchants then transport it through France toward the mouth of the river Rhone.
