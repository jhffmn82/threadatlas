Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, the sea nymphs are the transformed Trojan ships, swimming away from the shore at Cybele’s command. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the ships of Aeneas are transformed into sea nymphs by Cybele.
