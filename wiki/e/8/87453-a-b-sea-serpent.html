A-B-Sea Serpent

Also called: A-B-Sea, big serpent, huge serpent, larger creature, monster, the A-B-Sea Serpent

Abstract written by a model

A queer, huge serpent from Mer City is made entirely of alphabet blocks, with a serpent’s face and tape-measure tongue. It teaches Mer children their letters, has a large family but no memories, becomes a bridge across the Munchkin Country river for the Scarecrow, vacations yearly with Rattlesnake, can snap off its X block, and receives an emerald collar from Ozma.

A-B-Sea Serpent is in one work on this site, The Royal Book of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER 2

The A-B-Sea Serpent is a huge creature made of alphabet blocks, with a serpent's face and a tape-measure tongue. It teaches letters to Mer children, has an extensive family, and stretches across the river so the Scarecrow can walk over it.
