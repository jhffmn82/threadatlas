  • In The Aeneid, the Trojan camp is defended by Trojans behind ramparts and gates while Aeneas is absent, and its defenders strengthen its walls and towers and keep watch against Turnus and the Rutulians until Ascanius and the Trojan forces break out.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Trojan camp is attacked by Turnus while Aeneas is absent, but its defenders remain watchful and resist from within their intrenchments, while Nisus and Euryalus leave to carry a message to Aeneas.

Trojan camp in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The Trojan camp, defended by Trojans behind ramparts and gates, is left without Aeneas and besieged by Turnus and the Rutulians. Following their general’s orders, its defenders strengthen walls and towers and keep watch until Ascanius and the Trojan forces break out.

Narrative, section by section

BOOK IX

The camp is left without Aeneas and is attacked by Turnus and the Rutulians. The Trojans follow their general’s instructions by remaining behind the defenses, strengthening the walls and towers, and taking turns guarding them.

But, in another part, th’ Arcadian horse

The Trojan camp is described as having been besieged for a long time. Near the end of the fighting, Ascanius and the Trojan forces break out of it.
