Artabanus, Hyrcanian captain of Xerxes's guard, murders Xerxes, frames Darius, seeks the kingdom, and is killed by Artaxerxes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIII.

Artabanus, captain of Xerxes' guard, conspired with Mithridates to murder Xerxes and seize the kingdom. He manipulated Artaxerxes into killing Darius, then tried to kill Artaxerxes himself, but Artaxerxes killed him.
