the Red Circle in His Last Bow

The Red Circle is a secret Neapolitan society allied with the old Carbonari, using frightening oaths, blackmail, threats, and planned violence against Tito Castalotte after he refused payment.

Narrative, section by section

PART II

The Red Circle is a Neapolitan society allied with the old Carbonari and organized around frightening oaths and secrecy. Its members blackmail wealthy Italians, threaten those who resist, and planned violence against Tito Castalotte and his house.
