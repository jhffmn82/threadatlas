Abstract written by a model

McGovern's cab, kept at Bachman's livery stable and associated with Luke McGovern, carries the sporting editor and Gallegher through a cold, snowy journey to Torresdale. It waits near the lower gate with its lamps out for a rapid departure. Later Gallegher finds it at the furthermost gate, frees it, and drives toward Philadelphia, where cabmen stop it and question him.
