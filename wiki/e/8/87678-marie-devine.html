Marie Devine, formerly Lady Frances Carfax’s maid, lives in Montpellier after leaving service to marry Jules Vibart. Lady Frances gives her a fifty-pound wedding-present; Marie believes Lady Frances was afraid of a bearded man who followed her.

The Disappearance of Lady Frances Carfax

Marie Devine was Lady Frances Carfax's maid and received a fifty-pound check from her at Montpellier. She leaves Lady Frances because of her approaching marriage and believes that Lady Frances feared the bearded man who followed her.
