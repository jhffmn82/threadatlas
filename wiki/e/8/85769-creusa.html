Creusa

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: my faithful consort, my forsaken wife, wretched me

Abstract written by a model

Creusa, wife of Aeneas and mother of Ascanius, urges Aeneas to protect their family rather than return to battle. During Troy’s escape she is lost and dies, then appears as a ghost, revealing divine fate: she must remain in Troy while Aeneas journeys to Latium, kingdom, and a royal bride.

Creusa is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

“Then, not before, I felt my curdled blood

Creusa tries to stop Aeneas from returning to battle and urges him to protect his father, son, and wife. She is lost during the escape from Troy and later appears to Aeneas as a ghost. She tells him that the gods have decreed a long journey and that he will reach Latium, where he will find a kingdom and a royal bride.
