dual-level retrieval paradigm in LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zirui Guo, Lianghao Xia, Yanhua Yu, Tu Ao, Chao Huang · 2025

The dual-level retrieval paradigm in LightRAG is a two-level method, framework, and system for comprehensive information retrieval. It matters because it combines low-level discovery of specific entities and relationships with high-level discovery of broader topics, themes, and relationships, providing breadth and depth that either level alone can lose. It begins by generating detailed local query keywords and abstract global query keywords. It then uses graph and vector-based matching, including a vector database for keyword matching, to retrieve relevant information from both dimensions. The system extracts specific and abstract information, integrates entities with broader topics to improve diversity, and is described as significantly enhancing retrieval efficiency and response speed compared with brute-force searches for each generated community. It ends by supporting relevant, comprehensive responses to diverse user needs.
