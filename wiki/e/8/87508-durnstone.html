Also called: Durnstone Police Station

Abstract written by a model

Durnstone is a place containing the Athenaeum and the destination police station. It hosts a Fancy Dress Masked Ball, with Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey stationed there, and Georgiana Tidman stays at the Wheatsheaf. Later Noah plans to take a prisoner and the Dean there, where the Bench sits and the Dean’s case can be reported.

Durnstone is in one work on this site, Dandy Dick. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

Durnstone is the place where the Fancy Dress Masked Ball is being held and where Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey are stationed. Georgiana Tidman stayed there at the Wheatsheaf before coming to the Deanery.

THE THIRD ACT.

Durnstone is the destination to which Noah plans to take the prisoner after dinner. Hannah says its Superintendent, John Ruggles, and Inspectors Whitaker and Parker, together with their families, are chapel folk.
