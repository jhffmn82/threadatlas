the Minister Spire in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

The Minster Spire, somewhat out of repair, faces crumbling unless restoration begins immediately. The Dean, its principal creditor, offers a thousand-pound loan for preservation or repair, while Georgiana proposes using Dandy Dick’s race winnings to help fund the work.

THE SECOND ACT.

The Minster Spire is described as being somewhat out of repair and in need of restoration. The Dean's concern for it leads him to make his thousand-pound offer.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

The Spire is threatened with crumbling unless its restoration begins immediately, making it the Dean's principal creditor in his anxious reflections. Georgiana proposes using winnings from Dandy Dick's race to help pay for the Spire.
