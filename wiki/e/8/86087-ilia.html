Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI

Ilia wanders in distress because of her uncle's crime and Mars's wrongdoing, with her hair and cheeks marked by her nails. Fearing disgrace after being a Vestal, she throws herself into Anio's waters, which support her and make her his wife.
