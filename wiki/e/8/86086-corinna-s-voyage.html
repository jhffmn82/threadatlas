Corinna's voyage is a dangerous sea journey from home, exposed to adverse winds and storms; the speaker fears for her, urges her return, and hopes to welcome her safe escape.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

X

Corinna's voyage is described as a hazardous departure from her known home over treacherous waters. The speaker fears the winds and storms, urges a safe return, and imagines welcoming her ship, embracing her, and hearing how narrowly she escaped the sea.
