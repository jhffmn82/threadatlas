Abstract written by a model

The war between the Flatheads and the Skeezers is a conflict in Oz that threatens the Land of Oz with fighting and trouble. It is first revealed through Glinda’s Great Book of Records as a Skeezers’ declaration of war, though the cause is then unknown. Later, the Su-dic declares war on the Skeezers over Rora’s transformation and the Skeezers’ prevention of Flathead fishing in their lake. He plans to march all Flathead forces against them, while Ozma and Dorothy try to warn and aid the Skeezers. Ozma seeks peace, but both the Su-dic and Queen Coo-ee-oh refuse to yield. The Flatheads plan an attack, possibly first against the fishes, and Coo-ee-oh prepares for conflict. After Coo-ee-oh is transformed and the Skeezers are helpless, the Su-dic declares the Flatheads victorious; they return to their mountain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The war is revealed when Glinda's Great Book of Records reports that the Skeezers have declared war on the Flatheads. The cause of the conflict is unknown, and Ozma decides to intervene before fighting and further trouble occur.

Chapter Six

The Su-dic declares a war against the Skeezers in retaliation for Rora's transformation and because they prevent Flathead fishing. Ozma attempts to stop the war, but the Su-dic refuses her conditions and says he will fight even if many Ozmas oppose him.

Chapter Seven

The Su-dic is determined to begin a war against the Skeezers and plans to march all his forces against them. Ozma and Dorothy cannot stop the war immediately, so they set out to warn the Skeezers and help them as much as possible.

Chapter Eight

A war between the Skeezers and Flatheads is expected because the Flatheads plan to attack the Skeezers. Princess Ozma comes to settle the dispute and prevent fighting, but Queen Coo-ee-oh intends to fight rather than make peace.

Chapter Nine

A war between the Skeezers and the Flatheads is expected because the Flatheads want to destroy the fishes and possibly conquer the Skeezers. Lady Aurex says Coo-ee-oh has been preparing for the conflict, while Ozma suspects the Flatheads will first attack the fishes rather than the island.

Chapter Eleven

The Su-dic declares that the Flatheads have won the war after Queen Coo-ee-oh is transformed and the Skeezers are left helpless. The Flatheads then return to their mountain.
