Dragon

retriever · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: DRAGON, Dragon retriever

Abstract written by a model

In "Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?", Dragon is one of two retrievers studied, a generalizable dense retriever that retrieves 300-word chunks and performs highly in supervised and zero-shot settings without complex late interaction, with results consistent with Contriever for LC, RAG, and SELF-ROUTE. In "SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains", Dragon is a strong retriever for generated QA filtering, retrieving top-k documents for generated queries, retaining pairs whose answers appear there, and extending context length as an alternative retriever for SimRAG and baselines.
