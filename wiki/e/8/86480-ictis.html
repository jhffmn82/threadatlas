Ictis is a British isle near Belerium, connected by dry ground at low tide, when inhabitants carry large quantities of tin across in carts.

For over against the French shore^ opposite to the Hercynian

Ictis is described as a British island near Belerium. At low tide, the water between Belerium and Ictis becomes dry, allowing the inhabitants to carry large quantities of tin across in carts.
