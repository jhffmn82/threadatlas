Lewis Lyman Lockwood in Gallegher and Other Stories

Lewis Lyman Lockwood is an old, unmarried lawyer whose named medal is found and returned through Miss Catherwaight. The medal had been kept in his safe on Thirty-fifth Street before burglars robbed his home. He lives with and serves as guardian to Mr. Latimer, whom he adopts in a manner. THE CYNICAL MISS CATHERWAIGHT Miss Catherwaight's collection of orders and decorations and medals was her chief offence in the eyes of those of her dear friends who thought her clever but cynical.

Lewis Lyman Lockwood is a lawyer whose office Miss Catherwaight visits after finding a medal bearing his name. Mr. Latimer says the medal had been in Lockwood's safe when he lived on Thirty-fifth Street and was taken by burglars. Lockwood had been devoted friends with Henry Burgoyne, but they quarrelled over a woman and have remained apart.

"} They had been sitting there some time, he with his hands folded on the evenin

Lewis L. Lockwood visits Miss Catherwaight to thank her for returning his medal. He explains that he once misunderstood her mother's kindness and that pride prolonged the resulting separation from Burgoyne. After speaking with Burgoyne, he returns looking younger and happier and announces that they will go shooting in Maryland.
