Ozma and Dorothy's imprisonment in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Ozma and Dorothy are imprisoned in the great Dome of the Isle of the Skeezers beneath the lake when the Spider King’s spiders surround them with a purple web. He intends to force household work, but a green crab cuts the web and they escape as Glinda seeks rescue.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

The Spider King's imprisonment begins when his spiders surround Ozma and Dorothy with a purple web. He plans to force them to perform household work, but they escape after the green crab cuts the web.

Chapter Thirteen

Ozma and Dorothy's imprisonment takes place inside the great Dome of the Isle of the Skeezers beneath the lake. Glinda learns of it through her alarm bell and begins seeking a way to rescue them.
