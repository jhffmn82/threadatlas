3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

A passage from an EIA report can be evaluated as evidence for an SDG target or used as a query to detect addressed targets. Within RAG, passages are retrieved, included in prompts, and evaluated by an LLM.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.3

Passages were formed from sentences extracted from report pages, with each passage containing a randomly selected length of three to five sentences, subject to shorter trailing passages. The collection contains 16,474 passages, each assigned a unique random alphanumeric identifier.
