air apparitions in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Air apparitions are airy images in desert regions near the Syrtes, especially in calm weather, resembling large living creatures. They appear to stand, move, fly, or pursue people; their frightening effect on strangers can bring coldness and trembling.

Air apparitions appear in the deserts and near the Syrtes, especially during calm weather, in shapes resembling large living creatures. Some seem to stand, move, fly, or pursue people, frightening strangers and sometimes causing them to feel cold and tremble.
