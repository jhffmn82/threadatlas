Salmoneus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Salmoneus first dwells in Thessaly, then founds a city in Elis. Impiously claiming to be Zeus, he redirects sacrifices and imitates thunder and lightning with hides, kettles, and torches. Zeus destroys him by thunderbolt, along with his city and all its inhabitants.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX. Τῶν δὲ Αἰόλον παίδων ᾿Αθάμας, Βοιωτίας

Salmoneus dwells first in Thessaly and later founds a city in Elis. Claiming to be Zeus, he takes Zeus’s sacrifices for himself and imitates thunder and lightning. Zeus strikes him with a thunderbolt and destroys his city and all its inhabitants.
