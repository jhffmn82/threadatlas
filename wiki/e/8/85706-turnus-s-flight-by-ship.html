Turnus’s flight by ship

Also called: the delusion

Abstract written by a model

Juno’s phantom deceives Turnus into boarding a ship; Juno sends it away, leaving him disgraced and suicidal, before it carries him back to Laurentum and his father.

Turnus’s flight by ship is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

But, in another part, th’ Arcadian horse

Juno’s phantom of Aeneas tricks Turnus into pursuing it onto a ship. Juno cuts the ship loose, and it carries Turnus away while he laments his disgrace and considers suicide. The ship ultimately lands him on his native shores and restores him to his father.
