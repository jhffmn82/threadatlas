But, in another part, th’ Arcadian horse

Juno’s phantom of Aeneas tricks Turnus into pursuing it onto a ship. Juno cuts the ship loose, and it carries Turnus away while he laments his disgrace and considers suicide. The ship ultimately lands him on his native shores and restores him to his father.
