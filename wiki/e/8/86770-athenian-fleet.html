is_equipped_with 140 galleys [besides ships of burden and other ships] The Athenians equipped 140 galleys, in addition to ships for horses, provisions, and other necessities, for the expedition. The fleet sailed through the Ionian Sea and along the Italian coast to Sicily, later moved from Catana to the great port of Syracuse, and received further support from Athens.
