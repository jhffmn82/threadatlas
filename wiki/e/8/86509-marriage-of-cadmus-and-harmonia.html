marriage of Cadmus and Harmonia

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: first marriage, marriage, the marriage
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the marriage of Cadmus and Harmonia was described as the first marriage celebrated in the presence of the gods.
  • In The Library, the marriage of Cadmus and Harmonia was described as taking place after Cadmus completed his service to Ares, with Zeus giving Harmonia to Cadmus and the gods attending and celebrating the wedding with hymns.

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

is_a marriage [celebrated in the presence of the gods] The marriage of Cadmus and Harmonia was celebrated in the presence of the gods and was described as the first marriage so celebrated. Ceres, Mercury, Minerva, Electra, Apollo, and the other gods were said to have provided gifts, instruction, music, and joyful acclamations.
