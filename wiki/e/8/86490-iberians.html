Iberians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Iberians inhabit a mine-rich country, including Celtiberians and Lusitanians, and possess silver mines. Young, needy, courageous men, especially Lusitanians, formed mountain armed groups, raided for wealth, and sheltered in steep terrain from larger armies.

The Iberians are described as inhabiting a country rich in mines and including peoples such as the Celtiberians and Lusitanians. Young, needy, and courageous Iberians, especially Lusitanians, formed armed groups in the mountains and made raids for wealth, using steep terrain as shelter from larger armies.
