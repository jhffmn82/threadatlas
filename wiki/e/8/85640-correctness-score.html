Abstract written by a model

Correctness Score is a refined evaluation metric designed to capture partial correctness and minor variations beyond binary classification; binary evaluation uses a high threshold of 4 because inaccuracies are poorly tolerated. Across ablations, reranking generally improves correctness, peaking at 0.8402 with k, K, S, Q + reranker, though it can slightly reduce overall correctness; the knowledge graph preserves correctness.

Performance Evaluation / Dataset Generation .. Classifier Results

Correctness is evaluated with a refined metric intended to address the limitations of binary classification, including partial correctness and minor variations. A high threshold of 4 is used for binary evaluation because of low tolerance for inaccuracies.

System Ablation Study

Correctness Score increases from 0.8141 to 0.8178 with the k, Q + reranker configuration. The k, K, S, Q + reranker configuration achieves the highest Correctness Score at 0.8402, while the k, K, S configuration maintains 0.8141.

Overall Results and Discussion

Correctness is improved by reranking in the reported comparisons, although reranking can slightly reduce the overall correctness score. The knowledge graph maintains correctness while potentially reducing response quality.
