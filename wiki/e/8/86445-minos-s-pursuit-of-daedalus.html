Minos’s pursuit of Daedalus

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Minos's invasion of Sicily, arrived upon the coasts of Agrigentum, pursued Daedalus

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Minos’s invasion of Sicily begins after he learns that Daedalus has fled there; he sails to Agrigentum, demands Daedalus from Cocalus, and is killed in the ensuing encounter. In The Library, Minos pursues Daedalus through different countries, using a spiral-shell test that leads to Camicus, where Cocalus’s ruse kills him after he finds Daedalus’s hiding place.
