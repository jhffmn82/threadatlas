the devil's-foot poison in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The devil's-foot poison is a poison derived from Radix pedis diaboli. It can be burned in a fire or lamp; flaky ashes and brownish powder near a lamp’s talc shield indicate that its fumes produce toxic effects. It stimulates brain centres controlling fear, causing madness or death, and was allegedly used by Mortimer Tregennis and Dr. Leon Sterndale.
