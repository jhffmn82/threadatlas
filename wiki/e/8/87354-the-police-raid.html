the police raid in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The police raid targets a fight at the barn. It begins when barn doors are forced open, causing panic that scatters spectators; Hefflefinger seizes Hade and leaves. As the raid settles down, officers hold and arrest newspaper correspondents, amid disorder that lets Gallegher attempt to carry away the report.
