Von Bork in His Last Bow

His Last Bow: The War Service of Sherlock Holmes

Von Bork is a highly successful German secret-service agent who has posed as a sporting English country squire for four years while gathering intelligence. His safe contains papers on subjects including harbour defences, aeroplanes, Ireland, Egypt, Portsmouth forts, the Channel, and naval signals. He expects to leave England after receiving a copy of the British naval signals code, but the parcel contains a book on bee culture, and he is seized and chloroformed by the man posing as Altamont.
