Von Bork

person · 1 work · 21 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Mr. Von Bork, the German, the Prussian, the master spy, the prisoner, the sporting squire

Abstract written by a model

Von Bork is a senior German secret-service agent and spy in England, devoted to the Kaiser and German government. For four years he poses as a sporting English country squire, yachting, hunting, playing polo, driving a prize-winning four-in-hand, and boxing with officers while running German espionage from his country house. He holds intelligence papers and a locked safe covering English military and strategic subjects, and expects to leave after obtaining British naval signals. His family departs for Flushing, leaving Martha as housekeeper, while he pays and summons Altamont for a final delivery. Altamont brings a sealed parcel, but it contains a book on bee culture rather than the expected code. Altamont is Sherlock Holmes, who captures and chloroforms Von Bork with Watson’s help. Bound and taken toward Scotland Yard, Von Bork realizes his intelligence is unreliable.

His Last Bow: The War Service of Sherlock Holmes

Von Bork is a highly successful German secret-service agent who has posed as a sporting English country squire for four years while gathering intelligence. His safe contains papers on subjects including harbour defences, aeroplanes, Ireland, Egypt, Portsmouth forts, the Channel, and naval signals. He expects to leave England after receiving a copy of the British naval signals code, but the parcel contains a book on bee culture, and he is seized and chloroformed by the man posing as Altamont.
