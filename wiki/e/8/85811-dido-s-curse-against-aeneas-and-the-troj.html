Abstract written by a model

Dido’s curse invokes the Sun, Juno, Hecate, the Furies, and other powers to avenge her death: Aeneas is to suffer war, defeat, exile, separation, and an unburied death, while Tyrians and Trojans are bound to perpetual hatred and war.

The wretched queen, pursued by cruel fate,

Dido calls on the Sun, Juno, Hecate, the Furies, and other powers to avenge her death. She prays that Aeneas will suffer war, defeat, exile, separation from his subjects and son, and an unburied death, and she commands the Tyrians and their descendants to maintain perpetual hatred and war against the Trojan people and name.
