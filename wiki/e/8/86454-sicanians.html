Sicanians

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Sicaniaos, the Sicanians

Abstract written by a model

The Sicanians, described as Sicily’s first inhabitants, lived in villages and fortified hill towns under separate princes. Accounts dispute whether they came from Iberia or were indigenous; volcanic destruction drove them westward, where conflict with the Sicilians ended in established boundaries.

DIODORUS SICULUS. .. BOOK. V.

The Sicanians are presented as the first inhabitants of Sicily, although Philistus says they came from Iberia and Timaeus argues that they were indigenous. They originally lived in villages and fortified hill towns, later withdrew westward after volcanic destruction, and fought with the Sicilians until boundaries were established.
