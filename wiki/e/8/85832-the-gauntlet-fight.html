The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

The gauntlet fight is proposed as a contest in which the champion receives a bull and the loser receives a sword and helm. Dares first challenges the crowd, but Entellus fights him, defeats him, and takes the bull after Aeneas ends the combat.
