Abstract written by a model

Dwyer’s notebook contains his written account of the fight, including Gallegher’s work and Hade’s final capture. While the raid settles, Dwyer writes in it; Gallegher takes it from Dwyer’s coat, carries it toward the Press office, then hands over its damp, limp-covered copy.
