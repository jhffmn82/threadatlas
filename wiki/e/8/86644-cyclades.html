The Cyclades were formerly desolate and uninhabited until Minos sent Cretan colonies to many of them, divided their lands, and subdued much of the neighboring Asian coast. After the destruction of Troy, the Carians took control of some islands and shared others with the Cretan inhabitants, but the Greeks later expelled them from most of the Cyclades.
