Spermatophages

Abstract written by a model

Spermatophages are a nation that gathers and feeds on plentiful fallen fruit in summer, then eats a sweet, turnip-stalked herb growing in shady valleys during the rest of the year.

Spermatophages is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

The Spermatophages gather plentiful fallen fruit in summer and feed on it. During the rest of the year they eat a sweet herb that grows in shady valleys and has a solid stalk like a turnip.
