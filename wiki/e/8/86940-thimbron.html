Thimbron

Also called: Thiinhron

Abstract written by a model

Thimbron was a Lacedaemonian general appointed by the Lacedaemonians to wage war against the Persian king in Asia. With Spartan and allied troops, he reached Ephesus, took Magnesia, failed at Tralles, and withdrew before Tissaphernes. Later he occupied Jonda and Corossus, but Struthius surprised and killed him during a plundering expedition.

Thimbron is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. VI.

Thimbron was appointed by the Lacedaemonians to command the war against the Persian king with a thousand Spartans and authority to raise additional allied troops. He reached Ephesus with about seven thousand men, took Magnesia, failed to take Tralles, and withdrew to Ephesus when Tissaphernes approached.

CHAP. XL

Thimbron led the Lacedaemonian campaign in Asia with about eight thousand men and occupied the castle Jonda and the mountain Corossus. While he was away with a plundering party, Struthius attacked him by surprise and killed him, forcing the surviving soldiers to flee to Cnidinium.
