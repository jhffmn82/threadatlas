The Scythians began as a small and despised people near the river Araxes. Through valor they expanded their dominion across mountainous and plain regions, and their descendants conquered territories beyond the Tanais, as far as Egypt, the eastern ocean, the Caspian Sea, and the lake Maeotis. Their royal line eventually failed, and their nation was greatly weakened by the Sauromatians.
