Egyptian religious divisions

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: religious rites, religious rites and customs, their religion

Abstract written by a model

prevents Egyptians [from agreeing among themselves] One account says that a prudent ancient ruler divided Egypt into regions and assigned each region the worship of a particular beast or prohibited particular foods. This division allegedly prevented the Egyptians from uniting in rebellion, since each region honored its own creature and slighted the religious practices of others.
