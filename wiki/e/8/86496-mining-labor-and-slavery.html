Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Mining labor and slavery in Roman-managed Iberian mines involved Italian interests buying slaves after Roman control, forcing them under overseers to open shafts, dig galleries, and carry ore amid beatings, relentless labor, and deaths from overwork.

After Roman control began, Italian interests bought many slaves and placed them under mine masters and overseers. The slaves opened shafts, dug galleries, carried ore from deep underground, and worked without rest under compulsion and beatings; many died from overwork, while others regarded death as preferable to continued labor.
