Abstract written by a model

The submarine is a fully enclosed, blackened steel magic craft of the Magic Isle, usually kept in basement rooms beneath the Skeezers’ village. It has no oars, sails, or visible machinery; its collapsible roof makes it watertight and forms a water-proof chamber when submerged. Queen Coo-ee-oh uses mystic words or witchcraft to operate it, carrying armed Skeezers from the submerged dome to shore to fight the Flatheads. After her transformation, the Skeezers cannot control, submerge, protect, or return it, and it drifts or is pulled by three fishes via her silver rope to the sandy shore. Glinda then enchants the abandoned boat to obey her spoken commands. It carries Glinda and the Wizard across the lake, submerges and circles the Great Dome, but cannot enter the sunken island through its closed marble door before returning to shore.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

Queen Coo-ee-oh keeps several enclosed submarines in rooms beneath the Skeezers' village. One travels from the submerged dome to the shore, rises to the surface, and carries armed Skeezers and the Queen.

Chapter Eleven

The submarine is operated by mystic words spoken by Queen Coo-ee-oh rather than by machinery. After her transformation, the Skeezers cannot submerge it, restore its watertight shield, make it return to the castle, or put it back in its basement room.

Chapter Sixteen

The iron boat cannot be submerged or returned to the sunken island by the Skeezers because they have no knowledge of the witchcraft that moves it. Pulled by the three fishes using Coo-ee-oh's silver cord, it reaches the sandy shore.

Chapter Seventeen

The boat is a blackened steel submarine with a collapsible roof, but it has no oars, sails, or visible machinery. Glinda believes it can be made to obey her sorcery and could travel through the basement entrance of the submerged island to transport prisoners to shore.

Chapter Twenty

The abandoned submarine is enchanted by Glinda so that it obeys spoken commands. It carries Glinda and the Wizard across the lake, submerges, approaches the sunken island, circles the Great Dome, and returns to shore, but it cannot enter through the closed marble door.
