Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Tros was the son of Erichthonius and father of Ilus, Assaracus, and Ganymedes, and gave his name to the Trojans. In The Library, Tros succeeded to the kingdom, named the country Troy, and with Callirrhoe fathered Cleopatra, Ilus, Assaracus, and Ganymede.
