Abstract written by a model

The Silver Islanders, or Silvermen, are the people of the Silver Islands, described as an older race than their Chinese cousins and as people of the stars. They inhabit and live on Silver Island, eat its cuisine, and serve the Emperor through elaborate court ceremonies. They are portrayed as quarrelsome and too stupid to appreciate their country’s beauty. They watch a kite exhibition, panic at the approach of the King of the Golden Islands, and flee during the invasion, cheering faintly for Chang Wang Woe. After the Scarecrow defeats the invaders, they return, celebrate with speeches, a banquet, and fireworks, and carry him to the palace. They love him as their honorable little Father and resist his abdication, but ultimately pledge allegiance to Happy Toko as Emperor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The Silvermen are the people of the Silver Islands and are described by the Grand Chew Chew as an older race than their Chinese cousins. They consider themselves people of the stars and serve the Emperor with elaborate court ceremonies.

CHAPTER 8

The Silver Islanders are portrayed as quarrelsome and too stupid to appreciate the beauty of their country. They gather to watch the kite exhibition, and later panic when they learn that the King of the Golden Islands is approaching.

CHAPTER 9

The Silver Islanders flee when the invasion begins, though they cheer faintly for Chang Wang Woe. After the Scarecrow defeats the invaders, they return to the shore, celebrate the victory, carry him triumphantly to the palace, and attend the speeches, banquet, and fireworks.

CHAPTER 20

The Silver Islanders assemble for the Ceremony of Restoration and initially bow before the Scarecrow despite his edict. They reject his abdication and insist that he remain their Emperor, but after the Princes are transformed and the Grand Gheewizard is seized, they respectfully leave the throne room.

CHAPTER 21

The Silver Islanders assemble in the throne room and pledge allegiance to Happy Toko after the Scarecrow presents him as their new Emperor. They are startled by the Scarecrow's announcement and fall prostrate when he lifts the magic fan.
