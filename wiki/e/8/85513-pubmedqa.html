PQA-U in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16

PQA-U is the PubMedQA subset used by RAGBench, comprising 60k abstracts segmented into context chunks, embedded in a 200k-vector database, retrieved four at a time for newly generated LLM responses.

RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]

PQA-U is the PubMedQA subset used by RAGBench. Its segmented abstract context chunks are encoded into a vector database with OpenAI embeddings, four chunks are retrieved per question using FAISS with Euclidean distance, and the original responses and labels are ignored in favor of newly generated LLM responses.
