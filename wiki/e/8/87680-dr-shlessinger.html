Abstract written by a model

Dr. Shlessinger is a fraudulent missionary identity used by Henry Peters, a dangerous criminal. Claiming South American origins and recovery from illness, he travels from Baden toward London with Fraser presented as his wife and with Lady Frances Carfax. He exploits lonely women’s religious feelings, and Holmes ultimately identifies Shlessinger as Peters during their confrontation.

The Disappearance of Lady Frances Carfax

Dr. Shlessinger presents himself as a missionary from South America recovering from a disease and travels with his wife and Lady Frances Carfax from Baden toward London. Sherlock Holmes determines that he is actually Holy Peters, a dangerous criminal who exploits lonely women.
