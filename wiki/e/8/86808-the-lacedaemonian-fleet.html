Abstract written by a model

The Lacedaemonian fleet under Mindarus sailed to the Hellespont and fought the Athenians between Sestos and Abydos. Although it initially numbered eighty-eight ships and included Syracusan and other allied vessels, it was defeated and lost numerous ships.
