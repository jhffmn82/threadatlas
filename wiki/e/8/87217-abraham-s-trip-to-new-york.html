Van Bibber's fiancée has handed the engagement ring back to him after the breakup. He carries it to the secluded box-bush dell, intending to bury it beneath the bush as a symbol of his lost hopes.

Abraham travels to New York with the $200 he saved, but not to buy counterfeit money. After the Deacon permits him to marry Kate without requiring $2,000, he comes on the first train to buy a ring.
