Abraham's trip to New York in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Abraham travels to New York on the first train with the $200 he saved. After the Deacon permits him to marry Kate without requiring $2,000, he goes to buy a ring, not counterfeit money.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" A WALK UP THE AVENUE He came down the steps slowly, and pulling mechanically at his gloves.

Van Bibber's fiancée has handed the engagement ring back to him after the breakup. He carries it to the secluded box-bush dell, intending to bury it beneath the bush as a symbol of his lost hopes.

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Abraham travels to New York with the $200 he saved, but not to buy counterfeit money. After the Deacon permits him to marry Kate without requiring $2,000, he comes on the first train to buy a ring.
