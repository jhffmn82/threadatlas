Pharax, a Lacedaemonian councillor and admiral, commanded a fleet of one hundred and twenty ships, sailed from Rhodes to besiege Caunus, then withdrew to Rhodes before an approaching army.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. vm.

Pharax, the Lacedaemonian admiral, sailed from Rhodes with one hundred and twenty ships, reached Cassandra, and besieged Caunus. He raised the siege when Artaphemes and Pharnabazus approached with a large army and returned to Rhodes.
