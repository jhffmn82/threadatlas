The Ethiopian purification purges Ethiopia’s land and nation by sacrificing, crowning, and sending two stranger ritual purifiers south toward a fortunate island with provisions, under severe punishment for returning early.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The Ethiopian purification required two strangers to sail directly south toward a fortunate island. The Ethiopians sacrificed, crowned the strangers with garlands, and sent them away with six months' provisions; returning before reaching the island would bring severe punishment.
