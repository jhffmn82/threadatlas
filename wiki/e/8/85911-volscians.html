Volscians in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The Volscians are warlike troops led by Camilla who aid Turnus. They attack the Trojan defenses with siege equipment, using shields as a moving shelter, filling the ditch, damaging bulwarks, and raising ladders, but Trojan resistance breaks their assaults. They later wait outside the gate to face the Tyrrhene troops. After Camilla is killed, they retreat toward a Latin city, pursued by enemies, with many crushed or wounded at its gates.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

And now the mighty labour is begun

The Volscians send Camilla and their warlike troops to aid Turnus.

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

The Volscians advance against the Trojan defenses with shields held overhead in a moving shelter. They fill the ditch, pull down bulwarks, raise ladders, and attempt to scale the walls, but Trojan stones and weapons break up their assaults.

While they debate, nor these nor those will yield,

The Volscians wait outside the gate with Camilla as their leader. They are assigned to face the Tyrrhene troops.

She said. The faithful nymph descends from high

The Volscians fight under Camilla but lose their leader and withdraw from the field after her death. Their soldiers flee toward the city, pursued by their enemies, and many are crushed or wounded at the gates.
