the plain in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The plain is broad and delightful, covered with fragrant wild flowers, flowering bushes, fruit, and groups of stately trees. It has no dwellings or signs of life, is bordered by palm trees, and takes Dorothy and Ozma longer than expected to cross.

Chapter Four

The plain is broad, beautiful, and covered with fragrant wild flowers, flowering bushes, fruit, and groups of trees. It has no dwellings or signs of life, is bordered by palms on one side, and takes Dorothy and Ozma longer to cross than they expect.
