Abstract written by a model

Rags's fight with McGonegal was a street assault witnessed by police: Rags struck McGonegal, causing him to fall. It led to police pursuit and a criminal charge. Rags says he acted in self-defense, citing witnesses who saw McGonegal pull a pistol, and expects a short sentence.

Rags's fight with McGonegal is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
