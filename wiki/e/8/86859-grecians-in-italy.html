Abstract written by a model

Grecians in Italy were Greek forces from Italy in Dionysius's army. They fought near Gela, were overwhelmed with heavy losses, and left his camp for home. Separately, their confederate forces opposed Lucanians and Dionysius, but premature action and later defeat at Helorus ruined them.

CHAP. XVI.

The Italians attacked the Carthaginian camp near the shore during the battle before Gela. They were overpowered, lost more than a thousand men, and retreated into Gela. Afterward, all the Italians left Dionysius's camp and marched homeward.

CHAP. XU.

The Greeks of Italy had agreed to aid any city attacked by the Lucanians, but their forces were defeated after the Thurians advanced prematurely. They later raised a larger confederate army against Dionysius, placed Heloris in command, and were defeated at the river Helorus.
