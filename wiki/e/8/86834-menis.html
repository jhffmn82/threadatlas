DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK I.

Menis was said to be the first human king of Egypt after the gods. He taught the people to worship the gods and introduced richer coverings for beds and tables, as well as a delicate and sumptuous way of living; his reputation was later clouded by Gnephachthus's curse on luxury.
