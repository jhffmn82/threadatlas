Abstract written by a model

Menis was said to be Egypt’s first human king after the gods. He introduced worship of the gods and luxurious living, including rich bed and table coverings; later, Gnephachthus’s curse on luxury clouded his reputation.

Menis is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK I.

Menis was said to be the first human king of Egypt after the gods. He taught the people to worship the gods and introduced richer coverings for beds and tables, as well as a delicate and sumptuous way of living; his reputation was later clouded by Gnephachthus's curse on luxury.
