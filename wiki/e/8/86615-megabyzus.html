Megabyzus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Megabyzus was a commander of Persian land forces, leading three hundred thousand men in Cilicia. He commanded a renewed expedition against Egypt with Artabazus, helped trap the Athenian fleet, allowed the Athenians to leave Egypt unharmed, and later pursued peace with Athens.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIX.

Megabyzus commanded the renewed Persian expedition against Egypt alongside Artabazus. He participated in the strategy that trapped the Athenian fleet and agreed to let the Athenians leave Egypt without harm.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

Megabyzus commanded the Persian land forces and had an army of three hundred thousand men encamped in Cilicia. After the Persian defeats, he joined Artabazus in sending ambassadors to Athens to negotiate peace.
