SDG Target Detection

task · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: SDG target detection, SDG target detection task, TD, TD task, detect targets, target detection, target detection task

Abstract written by a model

SDG Target Detection is a task, and one of two tasks addressed in the work, for determining whether a passage or EIA-report excerpt addresses any targets in a supplied SDG target set. After passage candidates are selected, it retrieves targets for the excerpt and prompts a Large Language Model to generate the identifiers of all relevant targets. It is evaluated through test collections as part of assessing Large Language Models, alongside evidence identification. Results depend on retrieval method, prompt version, passage order, and number of examples. GPT-3.5 gives the best precision-oriented results over densely retrieved passages, while ClimateGPT-13b gives the strongest recall for SDG-explicit prompts over lexically retrieved passages. SDG 14 is the most frequent relevant target, followed by SDG 15; SDG 7, 9, 11, and 12 are less frequent. The work notes limitations and proposes using passages identified for a target to expand targets of interest in an environmental-impact report.

Abstract

SDG target detection is the second environment-analysis problem addressed in the paper. Its goal is to detect the Sustainable Developmental Goal target addressed in a textual passage, and its performance is evaluated as part of the assessment of Large Language Models.

1

SDG Target Detection is one of the two tasks addressed in the work. It concerns detecting the relevance of textual passages to SDG targets after passage candidates have been selected.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

SDG Target Detection determines whether any targets in a supplied set are addressed by a given passage from an EIA report. It requests a Large Language Model to generate the correct target identifiers provided in the prompt.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.3

The target detection task retrieves SDG targets for an EIA excerpt and asks an LLM to identify all targets relevant to the excerpt.

5 .. 5.4

SDG Target Detection achieves its best precision-oriented results with GPT-3.5, while ClimateGPT-13b generally provides the strongest recall for SDG-explicit prompts. The task is affected by retrieval method, prompt version, passage order, and the number of examples.

6

Target detection is studied alongside evidence identification and remains limited in the work's capabilities. A subsequent target-detection stage could use passages identified for an SDG target to expand the targets of interest for an environmental-impact report.
