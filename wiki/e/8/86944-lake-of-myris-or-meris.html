Abstract written by a model

Lake of Myris or Meris, built near Memphis, receives excess Nile water to prevent flooding and inadequate irrigation. Reported at three thousand six hundred furlongs around, it connects by an eighty-furlong trench, supports crocodile feeding and breeding, and yields fish revenue for the king’s wife’s clothing.

The lake of Myris or Meris was built near Memphis to receive excess water from the Nile and help prevent both destructive flooding and inadequate irrigation. It was described as three thousand six hundred furlongs in circumference, with a connecting trench eighty furlongs long, and its fish provided a daily talent of silver for the king’s wife’s clothing.
