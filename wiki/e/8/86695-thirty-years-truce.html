30-year peace between Sparta and Athens in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The 30-year peace was an agreement between Lacedæmonians and Athenians, made after Athens’ second reduction of Eubœa, excluding Argives but permitting private friendship, and recorded on a brazen pillar near Zeus’ statue.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXII.

The 30-year peace between the Lacedæmonians and Athenians was recorded on a brazen pillar near the statue of Zeus dedicated by the Greeks who fought at Platæa. It was made by the Athenians after their second reduction of Eubœa and excluded the Argives from sharing in the peace, while allowing private friendship between Athenians and Argives.
