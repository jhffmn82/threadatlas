Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

The Deanery of St. Marvells is the home of Dr. Jedd, Salome, and Sheba. Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey visit it to arrange the ball plans, and Georgiana Tidman is expected to move there.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

The Deanery receives Georgiana as Augustin's widowed sister and becomes the setting for her reunion with Augustin and Sir Tristram. Georgiana jokes that if she remains there until the autumn meeting, the Canons and the Bishop may be found lunching with her on the hill.

THE SECOND ACT.

The Deanery hosts the dinner and evening gathering, where the guests drink tea, listen to violin playing, and react to the Restoration Fund news. Georgiana proposes using its stables as shelter for Dandy Dick after the Swan Inn fire.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

The Deanery shelters Dandy Dick in its stables and Sir Tristram in the old nursery. Georgiana announces that smoking is permitted throughout the house, and the Dean fears that the horse, gambling, and Georgiana's behavior are contaminating its formerly respectable peace.

THE THIRD ACT.

The Deanery is the Dean's home and the place where Hannah formerly worked as cook for seven years. The Dean's household has sent Blore to inquire about the arrest, and Blore says he will report Noah's intended visit.
