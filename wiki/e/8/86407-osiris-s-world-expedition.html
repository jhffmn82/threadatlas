Osiris's world expedition

event · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Osiris's expedition, expedition, his expedition, marching out of Egypt

Abstract written by a model

Osiris's world expedition is an army-led journey through the inhabited world after he entrusted Egypt to Isis. Led to civilize people and teach agriculture, it passes through Ethiopia, Arabia, India, Asia, and Europe, crossing the Hellespont. With Anubis, Hercules, musicians, and satyrs, Osiris spreads vines, wheat, barley, foods, and drink, establishes cities, colonies, Nysa and Macedonia, and assigns Triptolemus Attica's culture and tillage.

Osiris's world expedition is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

We shall first speak of the barbarians; not that we judge them

After settling affairs in Egypt, Osiris entrusted the kingdom to Isis and set out with an army through the inhabited world. He intended to teach people to plant vines and sow wheat and barley, civilize them, and gain lasting honor by benefiting humankind.

CHAP. 11.

Osiris's expedition passes through Ethiopia, Arabia, India, Asia, and Europe. It includes musicians and satyrs, establishes cities and colonies, and spreads agriculture, useful foods, and drink.
