Lethe

place · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Lethe’s lake, the River Lethe, the flood, the river Lethe, the stream

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, Lethe is a silent river beside a grove where souls awaiting new bodies drink its waters, forget former lives and sufferings, and return to mortal flesh. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the River Lethe is a river flowing through Elysium’s tranquil valley whose waters souls awaiting new bodies drink to lose memories of former lives.
