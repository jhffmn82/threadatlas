RadioRAG-powered QA

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

RadioRAG-powered QA is question answering that proceeds through key-phrase extraction, Radiopaedia search, database creation and embedding, and LLM response generation. Using GPT-4, it takes about 115 seconds per question, roughly four times longer than conventional QA, with database creation and embedding taking the longest.

RadioRAG-powered QA is in one work on this site, RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Appendix S4

RadioRAG-powered QA involved approximately 10 seconds for key-phrase extraction, 30 seconds for searching Radiopaedia, 40 seconds for database creation and embedding, and 35 seconds for LLM response generation. The total was approximately 115 seconds per question using GPT-4, about four times longer than conventional QA.
