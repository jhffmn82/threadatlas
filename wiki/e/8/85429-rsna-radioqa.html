Abstract written by a model

RSNA-RadioQA is an open-source diagnostic radiology question-answering dataset, available in Appendix S1, created from peer-reviewed cases in the RSNA Case Collection for internal testing of RadioRAG. It contains 80 expert-curated questions and corresponding answers across 18 radiology subspecialties, generally with at least five cases per subspecialty. Cases provide clinical history and image characteristics but not images or differential diagnoses. Patients range from 2 days to 80 years old, with mean age 44 ± 21 years. The dataset supports measurement of specialty-level accuracy and hallucination rates across six language models, reporting accuracy as mean percentage accuracy. In reported evaluation, RadioRAG significantly improved GPT-3.5-turbo and Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 accuracy, and selected relevant articles for 58 of 80 questions. Metadata include identifiers, original-case DOIs, authors, titles, publication dates, and subspecialties. Its main limitation is publication bias and possible training-data bias because cases were previously published online.

RSNA-RadioQA is in one work on this site, RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

2. Materials and Methods / 2.1. RSNA Cases .. 2.6. Code and Data Availability

RSNA-RadioQA is a dataset of 80 questions based on peer-reviewed cases from the Radiological Society of North America Case Collection. It covers 18 radiology subspecialties and was curated by STA, ML, and DT; the dataset was made available as open-source in Appendix S1.

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

RSNA-RadioQA contained 80 questions and included patients with a mean age of 44 ± [SD] 21 years, ranging from 2 days to 80 years. RadioRAG improved the accuracy of GPT-3.5-turbo and Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 significantly on this dataset, and relevant articles were selected for 72% (58/80) of questions.

4. Discussion

RSNA-RadioQA is a diagnostic radiology question-answering dataset developed from the RSNA Case Collection for internal testing of RadioRAG. It contains 80 questions spanning multiple radiological subspecialties, although its use of previously published cases may introduce potential training-data bias.

appendix

The RSNA-RadioQA dataset contains 80 questions used to evaluate specialty-level accuracy and hallucination rates across six language models. The unit also presents answers from RadioRAG-powered GPT-3.5-turbo for the first 20 questions and conventional GPT-3.5-turbo for the first 30 questions.

Appendix S1

The dataset consists of 80 expert-curated questions and their corresponding answers. This unit provides metadata for numerous questions, including their identifiers, original-case DOIs, authors, titles, publication dates, and subspecialties.

Appendix S4

The time estimates were observed in experiments using the RSNA-RadioQA dataset.
