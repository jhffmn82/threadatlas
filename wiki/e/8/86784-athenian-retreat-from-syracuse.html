Athenian retreat from Syracuse in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Athenian retreat from Syracuse, led by Nicias alongside Demosthenes, follows a second naval defeat, a divided march toward Catana, harassment and blocked passes, and surrender when trapped at the Asinarus.

After the second naval defeat, the Athenians burned some ships and divided their army for a march toward Catana. The Syracusans blocked the passes, harried the rear for three days, and trapped the army at the river Asinarus, where the remaining Athenians surrendered.
