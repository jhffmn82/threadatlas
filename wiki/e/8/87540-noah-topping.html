Noah Topping in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Noah Topping is the rural, ill-fittingly dressed Head Constable of St. Marvells, married to Hannah and running the police station strictly and rigidly. Jealous of Hannah’s male friends, he threatens them and reacts strongly when he finds the Dean with her, locking the Dean in a cell and inventing charges including alienating Hannah’s affections and embezzling his meal. He holds an unknown man in custody, believes this lawful, and plans to take a prisoner to Durnstone after dinner, with a stop to see racing. He first suggests the missing Dean committed suicide, then produces and escorts the handcuffed Dean. Later, dishevelled, he says a lover escaped custody, wrongly identifies the Dean as that man, and is corrected before accepting an invitation to dinner with Hannah.

THE THIRD ACT.

Noah is the Head Constable at St. Marvells and plans to take the prisoner to Durnstone after dinner, stopping to glimpse the racing. He is jealous and threatens Hannah's male friends, returns unexpectedly to the station, discovers the Dean with Hannah, and locks the Dean back in the cell. He says he will add charges of alienating Hannah's affections and embezzling his midday meal.

Noah acts as the local constable at the police-station and initially proposes that the missing Dean committed suicide. He brings the Dean out of the cell in handcuffs, claims charges against him, and takes him toward Durnstone, apparently using Kitty in the cart instead of Old Nick.

Noah Topping rushes into the Deanery with Hannah, claiming that an unknown lover of his newly made wife was rescued from his lawful custody. He mistakenly identifies the Dean as the man he captured, but Hatcham reveals that the actual man was short and thin. The Dean invites Noah and Hannah to stay for a hot dinner with ale, and Noah urges Hannah to thank him.
