the nocturnal raid

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections

  • In The Aeneid, the nocturnal raid is conducted by Nisus with Euryalus against a sleeping enemy host; they silently kill many soldiers and depart through the slain as dawn appears.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Nisus and Euryalus's night raid targets the sleeping enemy camp as they seek Aeneas; they kill soldiers, are discovered over a glittering helmet, and die as Nisus tries to save Euryalus.

the nocturnal raid in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The nocturnal raid is conducted by Nisus with Euryalus against a sleeping enemy host; they silently kill many soldiers and depart through the slain as dawn appears.

Thus arm’d they went. The noble Trojans wait

The nocturnal raid is carried out by Nisus and Euryalus against the enemy camp. They move silently among the sleeping soldiers, kill many of them, and leave through a lane made among the slaughtered bodies when dawn begins to appear.

Nisus and Euryalus's night raid in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Nisus and Euryalus raid the sleeping enemy camp seeking Aeneas, kill soldiers, are discovered over a glittering helmet, and die as Nisus tries to save Euryalus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Nisus and Euryalus cross the sleeping enemy camp to seek Aeneas and kill several sleeping soldiers. They are discovered after Euryalus takes a glittering helmet; Nisus tries to save Euryalus, but both are killed.
