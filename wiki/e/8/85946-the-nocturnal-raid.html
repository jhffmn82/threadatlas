the nocturnal raid

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Nisus and Euryalus's night raid, a conquest gain’d without a fight, nocturnal thefts, such an undertaking, the attempt

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, the nocturnal raid is conducted by Nisus with Euryalus against a sleeping enemy host; they silently kill many soldiers and depart through the slain as dawn appears. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Nisus and Euryalus's night raid targets the sleeping enemy camp as they seek Aeneas; they kill soldiers, are discovered over a glittering helmet, and die as Nisus tries to save Euryalus.
