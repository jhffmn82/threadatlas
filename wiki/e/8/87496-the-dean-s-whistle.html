the Dean's whistle in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

The Dean’s whistle, possessed by Augustin Jedd, is Hannah’s whistle. In the third act, Hannah gives it to the Dean and instructs him to blow in Pear Tree Lane and after leaving the cart. Its double-whistle signal summons old Nick to help return him safely to the Deanery.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE THIRD ACT.

Hannah gives the Dean a whistle and tells him to blow once in Pear Tree Lane and again after jumping from the cart. She says old Nick will respond to the familiar double-whistle signal from his time in the Durnstone Fire Brigade.

[_HANNAH unlocks the door, and admits GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM, both dressed f

The Dean has Hannah's whistle. Hannah intends for the whistle, together with Nick the fire-brigade horse, to help bring him safely back to the Deanery.
