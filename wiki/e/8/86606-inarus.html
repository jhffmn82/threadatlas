Inarus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Inarus, chosen king by revolting Egyptians, led native and mercenary forces against Persia and sought Athenian aid.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIX.

Inarus was chosen king by the Egyptians during their revolt from Persia. He raised native and mercenary forces and sent ambassadors to Athens requesting aid.
