Canace in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Canace, Aeolus's daughter and Macareus's sister and lover, secretly bears their infant son. After Aeolus discovers the relationship, she loses the child and receives a sword as punishment. Preparing to die, she writes Macareus, recounts their love and resolves to follow their son in death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI

Canace writes to Macareus while preparing to die by the sword sent by Aeolus. She recounts her love, pregnancy, childbirth, the loss of her infant son, and her father's punishment, then declares that she will follow her child's shade in death.
