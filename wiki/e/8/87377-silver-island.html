Abstract written by a model

Silver Island, also called the Silver Islands, is a flowery, bewilderingly beautiful realm surrounded by silver water and filled with silver houses, towers, temples, bridges, waterfalls, gardens, lanterns, and a royal palace. Its Imperial Palace overlooks the harbor, and its court includes an imperial silver throne room with a bean pole at the center of the hall. The Silver Islanders live there under rulers whose palace and court are richly furnished with silver, precious stones, rugs, flowers, flags, and chimes. After fifty years under the Grand Chew Chew, the Scarecrow is proclaimed Emperor Chang Wang Woe. The island fears invasion by the King of the Golden Islands, who attacks, but the Scarecrow saves it with the little fan, bringing victory and a great celebration. His reforms make the island more prosperous, though he misses Oz. Later Dorothy and her companions find him there, where royal authorities threaten their imprisonment as they try to escape. At the Ceremony of Restoration, the Islanders expect him to resume rule, but he seeks to leave for Oz. After the Princes become pigs, Happy Toko becomes Emperor. The Scarecrow departs while promising to watch over the island with the magic fan, and the Magic Picture later shows Happy Toko serving as Emperor before the former court.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The Silver Islands are the realm where the Scarecrow is proclaimed Emperor Chang Wang Woe. Their palace and court are richly furnished with silver, precious stones, blue rugs, flowers, flags, and silver chimes.

CHAPTER 8

The Silver Islands are described as beautiful lands of silver waters, houses, towers, temples, bridges, waterfalls, gardens, and lanterns. Their people fear that the King of the Golden Islands will set fire to the islands and make them slaves.

CHAPTER 9

Silver Island is invaded by the King of the Golden Islands but is saved when the Scarecrow uses the little fan. Its people then hold a grand celebration with a feast, decorations, and fireworks.

CHAPTER 14

Silver Island becomes more prosperous after the Scarecrow institutes many reforms. The Scarecrow rules it as its Emperor while missing his former home in Oz.

CHAPTER 15

The Silver Islands are ruled by the Scarecrow as Emperor, and the eldest Prince says that he expects to become their Emperor after the Scarecrow's death.

CHAPTER 18

Silver Island is the place where Dorothy and her companions find the Scarecrow in a magnificent silver throne room. It is ruled by Chang Wang Woe, and its royal authorities threaten to imprison the escaping group.

CHAPTER 19

Silver Island is identified in the chapter title as the place from which the group is planning to fly, but the unit gives no further description of it.

CHAPTER 20

Silver Island is the Scarecrow's former realm, where the Ceremony of Restoration is held and the Silver Islanders expect him to resume his rule. The Scarecrow instead tries to leave Silver Island for Oz.

CHAPTER 21

The Silver Island receives Happy Toko as its new Emperor after the Princes are transformed into pigs. The Scarecrow says he will watch over the island with the magic fan, while the party leaves for Oz.

CHAPTER 24

Silver Island is identified as the kingdom formerly ruled by the Scarecrow under his name Chang Wang Woe. The Scarecrow's gifts include silver chains and silver slippers, and the Magic Picture shows Happy Toko serving as Emperor while the Scarecrow's former court looks on.
