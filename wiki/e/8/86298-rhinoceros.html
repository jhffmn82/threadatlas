Rhinoceros in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Rhinoceros is a beast bred in Ethiopia, nearly equal to elephants in courage and strength but shorter. With very hard, ox-colored skin and a flat iron-hard horn sharpened on stones, it fights elephants over pasture by goring beneath the belly.

The Rhinoceros is bred in Ethiopia and is described as nearly equal to the elephant in courage and strength but shorter. It has very hard, ox-colored skin and a flat iron-hard horn, which it sharpens against stones and uses to attack elephants beneath the belly.
