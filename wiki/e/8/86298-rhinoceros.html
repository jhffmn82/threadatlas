Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Rhinoceros is described as a beast bred in Ethiopia that fights elephants over pasture. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, rhinoceroses is a name applied to Ethiopian bulls with horns projecting from their noses.

Diodorus Siculus · 59 to 29 BC · the work’s page · read it where Rhinoceros first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Rhinoceros is a beast bred in Ethiopia, nearly equal to elephants in courage and strength but shorter. With very hard, ox-colored skin and a flat iron-hard horn sharpened on stones, it fights elephants over pasture by goring beneath the belly.
