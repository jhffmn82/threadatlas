CHAP. XII.

The Carthaginian army was recruited from Carthaginians, Africans, Spaniards, Balearic islanders, allied Mauretanians and Numidians, people near Cyrene, and Campanians hired from Italy. Timaeus estimated it at somewhat more than 120,000 men, while Ephorus gave a figure of 300,000.
