Tito Castalotte in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Tito Castalotte, a powerful New York fruit importer and senior partner of Castalotte and Zamba, employs and benefits Gennaro Lucca; after rejecting the Red Circle’s blackmail, he gives its notices to police and faces a plot against his house.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART II

Tito Castalotte was a powerful New York fruit importer who employed and befriended Gennaro Lucca after Gennaro saved him from ruffians. When Castalotte refused the Red Circle’s blackmail and gave its notices to the police, the society planned to destroy his house.
