the Sunken Island in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Sunken Island is a submerged island at the bottom of the lake, containing the Skeezer village and Great Dome. Princess Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers are confined there while Glinda and others seek entry or a way to raise it, investigating its basement without finding the method.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The sunken isle contains the Skeezer village and the Great Dome, with Princess Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers confined inside. Glinda and the others seek a way to raise it or enter it.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Sunken Island remains beneath the lake at the beginning of the unit. Glinda and the others investigate its basement to discover how it might be raised, but they do not yet learn the method.
