A fortification at Epipolae with six gates, towers, and a thirty-furlong wall, ordered by Dionysius and completed in twenty days by sixty thousand freemen and six thousand yoke of oxen.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Dionysius ordered a fortification at Epipolae with six gates, towers, and a wall thirty furlongs long. Sixty thousand freemen and six thousand yoke of oxen were employed, and the wall was completed in twenty days.
