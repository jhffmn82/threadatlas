Carthaginian fleet

Abstract written by a model

The Carthaginian fleet was a fleet commanded by Himiico, comprising more than two thousand warships, over three thousand transport ships, and long galleys used to carry Hannibal's army. It transported troops and supplies, helped capture Messana, defeated and captured Sicilian vessels near Catana, entered Syracuse's harbor, and supported its blockade. Storms destroyed transports carrying horses and chariots; ships were later attacked, broken or burned, though some were preserved overland. Gelon's horsemen burned ships in camp, and most twenty escaping ships were wrecked before a few men reached Carthage.

CHAP. U.

The Carthaginian fleet contained more than two thousand warships and over three thousand transport ships, although a storm destroyed the vessels carrying the horses and chariots. Gelon's disguised horsemen later burned the ships in the naval camp, and the twenty ships that escaped were eventually wrecked except for a few men who reached Carthage in a small boat.

The Carthaginian fleet at Motya was attacked unexpectedly by Himiico's own force before Dionysius could assist. Some of its ships were broken apart and others burned, while the remaining ships were moved overland out of the harbor and preserved.
