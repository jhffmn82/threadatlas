the Crotonians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Crotonians protected banished Sybarite citizens after Pythagoras’s advice, then under Milo defeated Sybaris with one hundred thousand men, gave no quarter, and destroyed it. Later they expelled Thessalians who rebuilt Sybaris and allied with Thurium’s inhabitants.

agreed on upon certain conditions. Whilst things were thus acting

The Crotonians protected the Sybarite suppliants after initially considering surrendering them. Under Milo’s command, they defeated the much larger Sybarite army, gave no quarter, and destroyed Sybaris. They later expelled the Thessalians who had rebuilt the city and formed an alliance with the inhabitants of Thurium.
