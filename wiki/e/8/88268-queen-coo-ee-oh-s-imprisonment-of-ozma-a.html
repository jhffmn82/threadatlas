Abstract written by a model

During the conflict with the Flatheads, Queen Coo-ee-oh orders Ozma and Dorothy imprisoned on Skeezer island in the submerged Great Dome. Lady Aurex provides food and lodging, and they may wander within the Dome, but cannot escape unless the island is raised. Glinda reports their danger to the council, whose counsellors must investigate Skeezer country before deciding how to save them.

Chapter Eight

Queen Coo-ee-oh orders Ozma and Dorothy kept as prisoners on Skeezer island until the battle with the Flatheads is over. She assigns Lady Aurex to provide them with food and lodging, while allowing them to wander anywhere under the Great Dome.

Chapter Thirteen

The danger threatening Ozma and Dorothy is that they are trapped as prisoners in the great Dome of the Isle of the Skeezers, which lies beneath the lake. They can live there for a long time, but they cannot escape unless the island is raised.

Chapter Fourteen

Ozma and Dorothy's plight is reported by Glinda to the assembled council. The details require the counsellors to investigate the Skeezer country before deciding how to save them.
