  • In Glinda of Oz: Magic Isle is a lovely, limited island in the center of the Lake of the Skeezers, in the Skeezer Country and part of the Land of Oz. It is the lawful home of the Skeezers and belongs to them, though Dorothy and Ozma claim it and Queen Coo-ee-oh disputes that claim. Magic protects the island and prevents outside knowledge of the Skeezers. Almost all of it is covered by a great round glass building, the Great Dome, with a high glass dome; polished white marble extends between the structure and the island's edge. Within are a marble village, gardens, parks, a central fountain, Queen Coo-ee-oh's palace, machinery for submerging the island, and rooms beneath the village containing a submarine. A retracting steel bridge can connect the palace to the shore. When attack by the Flatheads threatens, Queen Coo-ee-oh commands the island to sink. It trembles and creaks as it descends, stopping when the dome is underwater, then rests on the lake bottom on a solid-marble base, supported through a steel basin by a steel pillar. The sinking keeps the Flatheads from reaching or destroying the Skeezers, but traps the island's people, along with Ozma and Dorothy, beneath the water. Because the island obeys only particular witchcraft commands, Coo-ee-oh alone initially knows how to raise it. A council debates rescue; the three Adepts are expected to help, while Glinda and the Wizard seek entry. After the lake is lowered enough to expose the top of the Dome, the party enters through an opening and searches for restoration. Finally, after the word "oh" is spoken over burning powder, the island rises slowly and steadily above the lake, freeing those inside.

Magic Isle in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Magic Isle is a lovely, limited island in the center of the Lake of the Skeezers, in the Skeezer Country and part of the Land of Oz. It is the lawful home of the Skeezers and belongs to them, though Dorothy and Ozma claim it and Queen Coo-ee-oh disputes that claim. Magic protects the island and prevents outside knowledge of the Skeezers. Almost all of it is covered by a great round glass building, the Great Dome, with a high glass dome; polished white marble extends between the structure and the island's edge. Within are a marble village, gardens, parks, a central fountain, Queen Coo-ee-oh's palace, machinery for submerging the island, and rooms beneath the village containing a submarine. A retracting steel bridge can connect the palace to the shore. When attack by the Flatheads threatens, Queen Coo-ee-oh commands the island to sink. It trembles and creaks as it descends, stopping when the dome is underwater, then rests on the lake bottom on a solid-marble base, supported through a steel basin by a steel pillar. The sinking keeps the Flatheads from reaching or destroying the Skeezers, but traps the island's people, along with Ozma and Dorothy, beneath the water. Because the island obeys only particular witchcraft commands, Coo-ee-oh alone initially knows how to raise it. A council debates rescue; the three Adepts are expected to help, while Glinda and the Wizard seek entry. After the lake is lowered enough to expose the top of the Dome, the party enters through an opening and searches for restoration. Finally, after the word "oh" is spoken over burning powder, the island rises slowly and steadily above the lake, freeing those inside.

Narrative, section by section

13 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Magic Isle is an island in a great lake in the Skeezer Country. The Skeezers live there and use magic that prevents Glinda from learning much about them.

Chapter Seven

The Magic Isle is located in the center of the Lake of the Skeezers. It is almost entirely covered by a large round glass building with a high glass dome, surrounded by polished white marble.

Chapter Eight

Skeezer island is covered almost entirely by a great glass dome. It contains a marble village, gardens, parks, a central fountain, and Queen Coo-ee-oh's palace, and it can be reached from the shore by a steel bridge that extends from the palace and retracts with its passengers.

Chapter Nine

The Magic Isle is the island where the Skeezers live beneath the great glass dome. Lady Aurex expects that the Flatheads may attack the Skeezers there.

Chapter Ten

The island trembles, creaks, and sinks beneath the lake at Queen Coo-ee-oh's command. Once the top of the dome is underwater, the island stops sinking and remains stationary.

Chapter Twelve

The Skeezers' island lies at the bottom of the lake after Coo-ee-oh caused it to sink. Only Coo-ee-oh knew how to raise it, leaving the island and its inhabitants trapped underwater.

Chapter Thirteen

The Magic Isle of the Skeezers is submerged by Queen Coo-ee-oh and remains entirely under the lake's water. Ozma and Dorothy are trapped in its great Dome.

Chapter Fourteen

The sunken island of the Skeezers rests on the bottom of a lake. The council discusses raising it but cannot decide how until the group travels there to examine the situation.

Chapter Seventeen

The Magic Isle is the Skeezers' home and has been submerged by Queen Coo-ce-oh so the Flatheads could not reach or destroy it. Its magic causes it to obey particular commands of witchcraft, and it remains beneath the lake with its people imprisoned.

Chapter Nineteen

The Skeezer island is beneath the lake's surface, although Reera says it really belongs at the surface. The three Adepts are expected to use their abilities to raise it and save the Skeezer people.

Chapter Twenty

The sunken island lies beneath the lake and contains the Great Dome and a basement accessible through the marble door. Glinda and the Wizard seek a way to enter it and eventually raise it to free the people inside.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The submerged island becomes accessible when the lake is lowered and the top of its Dome rises above the water. The party enters it through an opening in the Dome and searches for the means to restore it to the surface.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The island rests on a base of solid marble and can be submerged on the bottom of the lake. After the magic word oh is spoken over burning powder, the island rises slowly and steadily above the lake.
