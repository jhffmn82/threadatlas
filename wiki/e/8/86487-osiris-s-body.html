Osiris's body in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Osiris's body is divided by Typhon into twenty-six pieces, then Isis finds all but the privy-members and reconstructs it into a man's shape.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

Typhon cuts Osiris's body into six-and-twenty pieces. Isis finds all the pieces except the privy-members, joins the recovered parts with wax and aromatic spices, and restores them to the shape of a man.
