Ye realms, yet unreveal’d to human sight,

Punishment in Tartarus includes rolling stones, being bound to burning wheels, enduring a vulture’s perpetual feeding, and suffering hunger while food is snatched away. Tyrants, traitors, the impious, the lustful, and those guilty of violence or betrayal remain there without hope of pardon.
