Durnstone Handicap in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

The Durnstone Handicap is a horse race scheduled for the following day. Dandy Dick, ridden by Tom Gallawood at nine stone two, and Bonny Betsy are contenders. Georgiana believes Dandy Dick has backed himself and worries cold water may have hurt his chances, though his singed tail reduces his carried weight. Despite ten-to-one odds against Dandy Dick, he is seen as strong, while Blore favors Bonny Betsy.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

The Durnstone Handicap is the race in which Dandy Dick is entered for the following day. Sir Tristram gives Dandy Dick's weight as nine stone two and names Tom Gallawood as his rider.

THE SECOND ACT.

Georgiana believes Dandy Dick has backed himself to win the handicap. She worries that the cold water thrown over him may have harmed his chances, but treats his singed tail as less weight for him to carry.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

The handicap race is scheduled for the following day. Dandy Dick is considered a strong prospect despite odds of ten to one against him, while Blore claims that Bonny Betsy is bound to win.
