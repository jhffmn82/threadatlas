Egyptian soldiers in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Egyptian soldiers held a major portion of the country for military service, with allotments meant to reward service and encourage families. They obeyed the king’s expeditions, faced disgrace for desertion, mutiny, or revealing secrets, and could regain trust through valour.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Egyptian soldiers received one of the three major portions of the country and were ready to obey the king's commands in every expedition. Their substantial allotment was intended to reward military service, encourage marriage and population growth, and give them valuable interests for which to fight.
