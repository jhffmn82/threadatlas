Egyptian royal monuments

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Colossuses and obelisks, Obelisks, Temples, monuments, public buildings, stately Sepulchres

Abstract written by a model

Egyptian royal monuments included temples, sepulchres, obelisks, colossal statues, and richly decorated public buildings. The monuments of Thebes were described as exceptionally ancient, costly, and magnificent, although many treasures were later taken by Cambyses and the Persians.
