CHAP. III.

Initiation into the sacred mysteries was originally restricted to those permitted to hear the rites, but Jupiter was said to have initiated strangers. Cadmus was initiated at Samothracia, and initiation was believed to bring divine assistance, piety, righteousness, and success in war.
