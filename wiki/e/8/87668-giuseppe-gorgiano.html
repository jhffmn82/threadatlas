Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART II

Giuseppe Gorgiano, a notorious member and organizer of the Red Circle, is found dead in an empty London flat with a knife driven into his throat. Emilia Lucca describes him as a violent giant who threatened Gennaro Lucca, assaulted her, and helped arrange the planned bombing of Tito Castalotte’s house.
