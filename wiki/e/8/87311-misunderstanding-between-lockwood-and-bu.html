misunderstanding between Lockwood and Burgoyne in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

A long estrangement between Lewis L. Lockwood and Henry Burgoyne began when Lockwood misread Miss Catherwaight's mother's kindness and endured because both men's pride prevented reconciliation.

The misunderstanding arose from Lockwood's misinterpretation of Miss Catherwaight's mother's kindness and was prolonged by the pride of both Lockwood and Burgoyne. It caused them to stop meeting and cease knowing each other.
