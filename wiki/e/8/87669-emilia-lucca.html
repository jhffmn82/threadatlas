Emilia Lucca, an Italian woman from New York and Gennaro Lucca’s wife, answers Sherlock Holmes’s summons, recounts Gennaro’s Red Circle conflict and Giuseppe Gorgiano’s killing, then goes with Gregson to the Yard.

PART II

Emilia Lucca arrives after Sherlock Holmes summons her by signal and identifies herself as Gennaro Lucca’s wife. She explains Gennaro’s connection to the Red Circle and his killing of Giuseppe Gorgiano, and Gregson takes her to the Yard while her account is investigated.
