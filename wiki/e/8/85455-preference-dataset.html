Methodology / Hallucination Detection Model Training / 4.1.1 .. Data Source Extension

The preference dataset is constructed automatically from original or regenerated answers, rewritten chosen responses, and rejected responses. It is expanded with XSum, Marco, and additional RAGTruth-generated answers to reflect the selected LLM's output distribution.
