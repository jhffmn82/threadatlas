the Trojan exile in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The Trojan exile comprises armed, treasure-bearing Trojan survivors of all ages, gathered at Ceres’s deserted seat and prepared to follow Aeneas through sea and land toward Latium after prolonged wandering.

“Then, not before, I felt my curdled blood

The Trojan exile consists of survivors gathered at Ceres’s deserted seat, including men, matrons, young people, and old people. They possess arms and treasure and are prepared to follow Aeneas through the dangers of sea and land.
