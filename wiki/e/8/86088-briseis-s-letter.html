III

Briseis's letter is written in Greek by her barbarian hand and is marked by tears that have blotted the writing. It complains of Achilles' delay, recalls her suffering, and repeatedly asks him to reclaim her. The letter ends by asking Achilles, whether he sails or remains, to command her to come as his captive or servant.
