KG Generator in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11/2024-08-16

KG Generator is a component and knowledge graph generation module of REANO. It infers a knowledge graph of knowledge triples from retrieved or relevant passages, capturing critical information contained in them. It proceeds through entity recognition and relation extraction, divided into intra-context and inter-context extraction. It produces intra-context triples with DocuNet, trained on the REBEL dataset through distant supervision, then combines them with inter-context triples. In REANO, the generated triples are used with a GNN to identify and select question-relevant information, improving retrieval-augmented readers.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The KG generator is a component of REANO that generates a knowledge graph consisting of knowledge triples based on the retrieved passages. Because it infers the graph from the passages, it can capture information contained in those passages.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.2 / 3.2.1

The KG Generator generates knowledge triples from relevant passages. It consists of entity recognition and relation extraction, with relation extraction further divided into intra-context and inter-context relation extraction.

opening .. 3.3

The KG generator produces intra-context relation triples with DocuNet and combines them with inter-context relation triples. Its DocuNet component is trained using the REBEL dataset through distant supervision.

opening .. 5

The KG generator generates knowledge triples from passages. In REANO, these generated triples are used with a GNN to identify and select information relevant to questions.

Limitations

The knowledge graph generation module is used by REANO to improve retrieval-augmented readers.
