Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Sipylus was a rising ground occupied by Agesilaus, where he defeated the forces of Tissaphernes. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Sipylus was the name of both a city and a mountain of Lydia, associated with the stone form of Niobe.
