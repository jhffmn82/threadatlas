Peg Amy's becoming alive

Abstract written by a model

Peg Amy’s becoming alive is her transformation from Trot’s small wooden doll into the Princess of Sun Top Mountain. After Ruggedo finds the box of Mixed Magic, she becomes alive by an unclear means. Her wooden body later melts away in the Magic Mirror, and her disenchantment is completed when Wag and Pompadore call and truly believe her a princess, as Glegg’s Question Box foretold.

Peg Amy's becoming alive is in one work on this site, Kabumpo in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Peg Amy was originally a small, unalive doll belonging to Trot. She became alive after Ruggedo found the box of Mixed Magic, though the exact way the magic caused this is not understood.

Chapter 20

When Peg Amy looks into the Magic Mirror, her wooden body melts away and a living princess takes its place. The transformed Peg Amy is the loveliest little princess in the world and declares that she is the happiest person in Oz.

Chapter 21

Peg Amy's disenchantment occurs when Wag and Pompadore call her a Princess and genuinely believe that she is one. Glegg says his power over her was lost when Cap'n Bill touched her with his knife, and his Question Box had foretold that Pompadore would bring about her disenchantment.
