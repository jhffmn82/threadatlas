BOOK V

Aeneas establishes funeral games in memory of Anchises, with sacrifices, public ceremonies, and prizes. The contests include a naval race and a footrace, and the games draw both Trojans and local Sicilians.

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

The funeral games include a gauntlet fight, an archery contest, and a mounted riding game. Aeneas directs the contests, distributes prizes, and brings the games to a ceremonial close with the display of the youths.
