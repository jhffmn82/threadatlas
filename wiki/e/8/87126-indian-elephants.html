Indian elephants are described as the largest, strongest, and most courageous elephants, numerous and heavily equipped for war. Captured, trained, and maintained by the king, they carry one calf after sixteen to eighteen months and suckle it for six years. In battle, they terrified enemies, destroyed mock elephants, trampled and tore apart soldiers, and helped defeat the Assyrian army.
