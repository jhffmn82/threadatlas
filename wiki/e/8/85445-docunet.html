DocuNet

software · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: DocuNet model

Abstract written by a model

DocuNet is a REBEL-trained relation extraction model in the KG generator that predicts relation triples between every entity pair in each passage in one forward propagation.

DocuNet is in one work on this site, REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
