the invisible stone wall in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The invisible stone wall, made by the Flatheads of cement-held solid stone, stands before the entrance to protect the mountain stairway from enemies. It blocks rushing attackers while allowing the Flatheads to pass through an opening between its hidden end and the mountain.

Chapter Five

The invisible stone wall is made of solid stone held together with cement and stands a short distance before the entrance. It is intended to keep enemies from rushing up the stairs, while allowing the Flatheads to pass around its hidden end.
