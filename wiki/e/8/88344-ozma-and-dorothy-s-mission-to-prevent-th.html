Abstract written by a model

Ozma and Princess Dorothy undertake a peaceful mission to the Skeezers’ Magic Isle and the Flatheads’ enchanted mountain. They plan to learn the cause of the dispute, persuade both peoples to stop fighting, and liberate them from wild, lawless, unjust, cruel leaders so they may have freedom and happiness.

Chapter One

Ozma and Dorothy plan to travel to the Magic Isle of the Skeezers and the enchanted mountain of the Flatheads. They intend to approach the two peoples peacefully, learn about their dispute, and persuade them to stop fighting.

Chapter Ten

Ozma identifies her mission as liberating the Skeezers and Flatheads from leaders who are wild, lawless, unjust, and cruel. She intends to secure freedom and happiness for them and believes she can accomplish this in time.
