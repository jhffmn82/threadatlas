Holmes's false illness

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: acting a part, malingering, that pretence, the reality of my condition

Abstract written by a model

Holmes creates the appearance of illness by fasting for three days, using vaseline, belladonna, rouge, and beeswax, and adopting delirious speech. He maintains the deception so Watson will convince Smith that his attack has succeeded.
