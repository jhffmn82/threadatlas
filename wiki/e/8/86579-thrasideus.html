Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Thrasideus, son of Theron, governs Himera harshly, succeeds Theron as ruler of Agrigentum, rules tyrannically, loses to the Syracusans, abdicates and flees, then kills himself after condemnation by the Megarensians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VII.

Thrasideus, son of Theron, governed Himera harshly and later ruled Agrigentum tyrannically after succeeding his father. He led an army against the Syracusans, was defeated, abdicated, fled to the Megarensians, and killed himself after being condemned to die.
