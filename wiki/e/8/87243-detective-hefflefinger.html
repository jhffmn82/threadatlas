Abstract written by a model

Detective Hefflefinger is a detective on Inspector Byrnes’s staff and in Byrnes’s detective force, carrying a badge and warrant. He comes to Philadelphia seeking a burglar, but the burglar has escaped. After Gallegher reports that Hade is near Torresdale, Hefflefinger agrees to arrest Hade quietly under a burglary pretext. He enters a barn, watches Hade during a fight, captures and handcuffs him in a police raid, rejects his bribe, and plans to take him to the station and then New York.

Detective Hefflefinger is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
