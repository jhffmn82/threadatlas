Detective Hefflefinger in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Detective Hefflefinger is a detective on Inspector Byrnes’s staff and in Byrnes’s detective force, carrying a badge and warrant. He comes to Philadelphia seeking a burglar, but the burglar has escaped. After Gallegher reports that Hade is near Torresdale, Hefflefinger agrees to arrest Hade quietly under a burglary pretext. He enters a barn, watches Hade during a fight, captures and handcuffs him in a police raid, rejects his bribe, and plans to take him to the station and then New York.

RAEGEN THE OTHER WOMAN THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Detective Hefflefinger, a member of Inspector Byrnes's staff, comes to Philadelphia seeking a burglar but finds that the burglar has escaped. Gallegher telegraphs him that Hade is near Torresdale and plans for him to make the arrest quietly using the warrant he already carries.

Hefflefinger agrees to arrest Hade secretly while pretending to arrest him for burglary. He enters the barn through a window, watches Hade throughout the fight, and captures him during the police raid. He refuses Hade’s bribe and says he will take him to the station and then to New York.
