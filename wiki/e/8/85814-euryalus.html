Euryalus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Euryalus is a beauteous young Trojan warrior, runner, and devoted friend of Nisus, with an aged mother of Priam’s royal race. In the footrace, he follows Nisus, then wins the foremost prize after Nisus falls and trips Salius. He joins Nisus’s nocturnal raid, refusing to let him go alone and accepting danger for fame and friendship; he asks that his mother be cared for if he dies. During the raid he kills Fadus, Hebesus, and Rhoetus and takes spoils, but his helmet exposes him to Volscens’s horsemen. Captured and killed, he is mourned as Turnus displays his severed head.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK V

Euryalus runs in the footrace and follows Nisus closely. After Nisus falls and trips Salius, Euryalus reaches the goal first and receives the race’s foremost prize.

BOOK IX

Euryalus refuses to let Nisus undertake the mission without him and declares that he is willing to risk his life for fame and friendship. He asks Ascanius and the council to care for his aged mother if he does not return.

Thus arm’d they went. The noble Trojans wait

Euryalus follows Nisus in the raid and kills Fadus, Hebesus, and Rhoetus. He takes Rhamnes's caparison and a gold-studded girdle, then wears Messapus's helmet. The helmet reveals him to Volscens's horsemen; Euryalus is captured and killed by Volscens.

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

Euryalus has been killed, and Turnus displays his severed head on a pointed spear. His death causes great grief among the Trojans, especially his mother.

Euryalus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Euryalus is a youthful, admired soldier and Nisus’s friend and companion in arms. He insists on joining a night raid, entrusts his aged mother to Iulus, is exposed by a glittering helmet, and receives a fatal sword wound from Volscens.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Euryalus is a young soldier distinguished for his appearance and qualities and is Nisus's friend and companion in arms. He insists on joining the night raid, asks Iulus to care for his mother, and is killed by Volscens after the glittering helmet he took reveals his position.
