Saturn

deity · 3 works · 17 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Chronos, Kronos, Satam, Satnrfi, Satom, Satorn, Saturnus, Satwn, Time, brother of Atlas
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Saturn is the eldest Titan, ruler of several kingdoms, and a participant in the Titans’ wars against Ammon and Dionysius.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Saturn is the father of Uranus, the ruler of Italy’s Golden Age, and the predecessor driven into Tartarus when Jupiter rules the world.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Saturn is an ancient Italian deity identified with Kronos, who is dethroned by Jupiter and reigns in Italy during the Golden Age.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Kronos is a Titan and son of Uranus who overthrows Uranus, is defeated by Jupiter, and is sometimes confused with Chronos, or Time.

Saturn in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Saturn, the eldest Titan and one of Coelus’s renowned children, was brother to Atlas and Rhea and married Rhea. After Hyperion’s death, he shared in the division of Coelus’s kingdom; accounts also say he ruled Egypt after Vulcan, or ruled Sicily, Africa, Italy, and the western world through garrisons and forts. He obtained a kingdom, brought subjects from barbarism toward a more civilized life, and was associated with justice, integrity, prosperity, and a blessed age. Yet he is also described as profane, covetous, cruel, and deceitful. With Rhea’s encouragement he joined the Titans against Ammon, defeated Ammon, then fought Dionysius, was wounded and defeated, and was pardoned. Some traditions say he killed his children after an oracle warned that Jupiter would overthrow him.

Saturn in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Saturn, father of Uranus, expels his father and seizes the throne. He reigns over Italy’s Golden Age, linked to perpetual spring but also wars and crimes. Before Jupiter rules the world, Saturn is driven into Tartarus.

Saturn in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Saturn is an ancient Italian deity identified with Kronos by Roman poets; dethroned by Jupiter, he flees to Italy and reigns during the Golden Age.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

Saturn was an ancient Italian deity whom Roman poets identified with the Grecian god Kronos. After Jupiter dethroned him, he was said to have fled to Italy, where he reigned during the Golden Age.
