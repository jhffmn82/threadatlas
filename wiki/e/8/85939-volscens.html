Abstract written by a model

Volscens commands and leads three hundred horse, pursues Nisus and Euryalus through the forest, kills Euryalus, and is killed by Nisus in revenge.

Thus arm’d they went. The noble Trojans wait

Volscens leads three hundred horse sent by the queen to King Turnus. He recognizes that Nisus and Euryalus are traveling through the darkness, pursues them into the forest, and kills Euryalus. Nisus then pierces Volscens's mouth and throat with his sword, killing him.
