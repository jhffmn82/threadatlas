the ship in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

A small seaworthy vessel, manageable by two men, carries Lambulus and his companion with six months’ provisions. Prepared by Ethiopians after their seven-year stay on an island, it is fitted out for their departure.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The Ethiopians prepared a small but seaworthy ship that two men could easily govern. They placed Lambulus and his companion aboard it with six months' provisions, and after the men's seven-year stay on the island another ship was fitted out for their departure.
