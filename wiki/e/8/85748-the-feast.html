the feast

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: feast, fervour of the feast, sumptuous feasts

Abstract written by a model

A feast brings together Trojan and Tyrian guests as Dido honors Aeneas and the Trojans with lavish food, wine, incense, furnishings, vessels, and entertainment. Aeneas sends Ascanius with gifts for the queen. After dining, Dido prays and pours wine, Bitias drinks, Iopas sings, and the guests celebrate.

To whom, with sorrow streaming from his eyes,

Dido prepares a feast for Aeneas and the Trojans, with incense, rich foods, wine, purple hangings, Tyrian carpets, and costly vessels. Aeneas sends Ascanius with precious gifts intended for the queen during the feast.

Cupid meantime assum’d his form and face,

The feast includes bread, water for washing, incense, dishes, wine, golden bowls, and entertainment. After the meal, Dido offers prayers and wine, Bitias drains a large goblet, and Iopas sings while the guests celebrate.
