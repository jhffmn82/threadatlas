Overly Cautious Penalization is a training penalization strategy preventing models from merely shortening responses: it randomly deletes one sentence from a chosen response, creating an additional rejected response to discourage shorter answers as a way to reduce hallucinations.

Methodology / Hallucination Detection Model Training / 4.1.1 .. Data Source Extension

Overly Cautious Penalization randomly deletes one sentence from a chosen response to create an additional rejected response. It is intended to discourage models from reducing hallucinations simply by producing shorter answers.
