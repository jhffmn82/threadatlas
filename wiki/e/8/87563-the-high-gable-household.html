the High Gable household in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The High Gable household is a secretive, violent household controlled by Don Murillo under the name Henderson. It includes Mr. Henderson, Mr. Lucas, the children, Miss Burnet, Lopez, José, a confidential manservant, a mulatto cook, and other servants, divided between separate wings. Miss Burnet serves as governess, but is imprisoned and drugged; the household also tries to conceal Garcia's murder before fleeing when police expose its identities.
