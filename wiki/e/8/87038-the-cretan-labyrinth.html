Abstract written by a model

The labyrinth was the place from which Theseus needed to escape after killing the Minotaur. Ariadne’s advice and assistance enabled Theseus to learn the passage out of it safely. The labyrinth was built by Daedalus to house and feed the Minotaur. It was full of winding passages and turnings and was impossible for strangers to find their way through; the seven boys and seven girls sent yearly from Athens were consumed there.
