Miss Burnet in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Miss Burnet, also Signora Victor Durando, is an English governess of about forty employed in Don Murillo’s household and caring for Mr. Henderson’s two daughters. After Murillo had her husband killed, she joined a revenge society, entered his household to watch him, and became Garcia’s confederate in a planned attack. Following the murder, she vanished from public view, was imprisoned and abused at High Gable for five days, then drugged and taken toward a train. With the discharged gardener’s help, she escaped and revealed her story.

Miss Burnet escapes from Don Murillo's custody after being drugged and taken toward a train, with help from the discharged gardener. She identifies herself as Signora Victor Durando and explains that Murillo had her husband killed, after which she joined the revenge society and became governess in Murillo's household to monitor him. She describes Garcia's planned attack, her imprisonment and abuse, and the attempt to force her to reveal Garcia's address.
