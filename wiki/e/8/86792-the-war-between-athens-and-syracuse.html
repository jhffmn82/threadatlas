The war begins when the Athenians attack Syracuse without provocation, breaking or threatening the peace. It brings calamity, including the loss of Nicholaus’s two sons, and ends in a reversal: the Athenians’ Sicilian expedition is completely destroyed and the former besiegers of Syracuse become prisoners.
