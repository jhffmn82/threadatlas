Abstract written by a model

defects_to the Lacedaemonians [under the protection of Brasidas] Scione defected from the Athenians to the Lacedaemonians and was placed under Brasidas's protection. It was besieged by Nicias and Nicostratus and later fiercely assaulted by Cleon, while its strong garrison defended it.
