Osiris and Isis reportedly ruled Egypt, benefiting humankind through agriculture, law, religion, and improved food; Osiris then left Egypt’s government to Isis during his expedition.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We shall first speak of the barbarians; not that we judge them

Osiris and Isis are said to have ruled Egypt and to have benefited humankind through agriculture, law, religious institutions, and the improvement of food. Osiris later entrusted the government of Egypt to Isis while he undertook his expedition.
