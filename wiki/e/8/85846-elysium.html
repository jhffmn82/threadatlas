the Elysian fields in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Elysian fields are a happy place on the western margin of the earth, by the River Ocean: groves with their own sun and stars. Mortals favored by the gods are transported there without dying for immortal bliss. After a dark middle region, happy heroes, priests, poets, and benefactors enjoy games, dancing, singing, and feasting.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter I

The Elysian Plain lay on the western margin of the earth by the River Ocean. Mortals favored by the gods were transported there without dying and enjoyed immortal bliss; it was also called the fortunate fields and the Isles of the Blessed.

Chapter XXV

The Elysian fields are the groves where the happy reside after passing through a dark middle region. Their inhabitants engage in games, dancing, singing, feasting, and other pleasant activities, and include heroes, holy priests, poets, and benefactors of mankind.
