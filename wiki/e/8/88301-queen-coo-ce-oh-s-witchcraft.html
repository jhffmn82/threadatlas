Queen Coo-ce-oh's witchcraft

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: all the witchcraft she ever knew, arts of witchcraft, her former knowledge, magic, the magic, witchcraft

Abstract written by a model

Queen Coo-ce-oh's witchcraft made the Magic Isle obey commands and enabled its submarine to become a boat, but she forgot this knowledge after becoming the Diamond Swan. She used witchcraft to sink the island, Great Dome, and all into the lake. Her specially prepared room and magical instruments require a spoken word, which Glinda and others investigate to overcome the witchcraft.

Queen Coo-ce-oh's witchcraft is in one work on this site, Glinda of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seventeen

Queen Coo-ce-oh's witchcraft made the Magic Isle obey particular commands and enabled the submarine to leave the island, rise to the lake's surface, and open as a boat. After her transformation into the Diamond Swan, she forgot all of this knowledge.

Chapter Twenty-One

Coo-ee-oh used witchcraft to sink the whole island, including the Great Dome, to the bottom of the lake. Glinda seeks a way to overcome this witchcraft and raise the isle.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Coo-ee-oh performed witchcraft in a special room containing her magical instruments. Ozma says Coo-ee-oh prepared it so that a spoken word was necessary, and Glinda and the others go to the room to investigate it.
