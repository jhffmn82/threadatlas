Abstract

Radiology is the domain in which RadioRAG and the evaluated language models were tested. The questions covered multiple radiologic subspecialties.

2. Materials and Methods / 2.1. RSNA Cases .. 2.6. Code and Data Availability

Radiology is the clinical domain addressed by the datasets, questions, retrieval sources, and model evaluation. The study covered 18 radiology subspecialties and focused on correct, concise diagnostic answers.

4. Discussion

Radiological diagnosis was the target task used to evaluate the framework. RadioRAG improved the accuracy of several models in diagnostic cases, including an example in which retrieved information corrected predictions of hepatoblastoma or other tumors to undifferentiated embryonal sarcoma of the liver.

references

Radiology is presented as a major area for large language model applications. The references cover structured reporting, chest-radiograph evaluation, lung-cancer information mining from CT reports, imaging-pattern differential diagnosis, speech-recognition error detection, and artificial intelligence for bedside chest-radiograph interpretation.
