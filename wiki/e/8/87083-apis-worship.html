Apis worship

Also called: the adoration of this ox

Abstract written by a model

is_based_on Osiris [according to Egyptian explanations] Apis worship involves treating the sacred ox as a god and maintaining a costly household for him. The Egyptians explain this worship by saying that the soul of Osiris enters one ox after another, while other accounts connect Apis with the members of Osiris.
