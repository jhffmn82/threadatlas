The Adventure of the Devil’s Foot

Mrs. Porter is the elderly cook and housekeeper at Tredannick Wartha. She says that she slept deeply and heard nothing during the night, discovers the family in the sitting-room in the morning, opens the window, sends for Dr. Richards, and decides to leave the house.
