old Nick in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Old Nick is a former Durnstone Fire Brigade horse. Noah plans to use him to take a cart quickly toward Durnstone, despite Hannah calling him vicious; his brigade service makes him answer a familiar double whistle. Later Hannah expects him to help bring the Dean back, but Noah leaves him at home.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE THIRD ACT.

Old Nick is the horse Noah plans to use for the journey to Durnstone. Hannah calls him vicious, while Noah says he can take the cart to the edge of the hill quickly; she explains that his former service in the Durnstone Fire Brigade makes him responsive to a double whistle.
