Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

The huntsmen, encouraged by Ptolemy the Second's generosity, undertook the dangerous task of bringing a giant serpent alive to Alexandria. After an initial failed attempt cost two men their lives, they used a bulrush net, stones, earth, horsemen, dogs, darts, slingers, and noise to drive the serpent into the trap.
