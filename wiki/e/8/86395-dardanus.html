Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Dardanus is described as the son of Jupiter and Electra who founded Dardanum, established Troy, succeeded Teucer as king, and gave his name to the Dardanians. In The Library, Dardanus is described as the son of Electra and Zeus and brother of Iasion who founded Dardanus and named the country Dardania.

Dardanus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Abstract written by a model

Dardanus, son of Jupiter and Electra and husband of Batia, left Samothracia for Asia, founded Dardanum, established Troy, succeeded Teucer as king, and gave his name to the Dardanians.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

Dardanus, a child of Jupiter and Electra, left Samothracia for Asia in a pinnace. He built the city of Dardanus, established the kingdom of Troy, gave his name to the Dardanians, and was said to have settled Dardanians above Thrace.

Dardanus in The Library

Apollodorus · AD 1 to 200 · the work’s page · read it where Dardanus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dardanus, son of Electra and Zeus and brother of Iasion, left Samothrace grieving Iasion, reached the mainland, married Batia, received land from Teucer, founded Dardanus, and named the country Dardania.

XII. Ἠλέκτρας δὲ τῆς "Ἄτλαντος καὶ Διὸς

Dardanus was the son of Electra and Zeus and the brother of Iasion. Grieving over Iasion's death, he left Samothrace, came to the opposite mainland, married Batia, founded a city called Dardanus, and later called the whole country Dardania.
