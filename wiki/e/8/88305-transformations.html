Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

Reera transforms from a gray ape into a young woman and changes a crocodile into a red wolf, lizards and horned toads into kittens, and rats into chipmunks. She says she sometimes changes the forms of her creatures a dozen times a day, and she offers to transform Ervic's fishes as well.
