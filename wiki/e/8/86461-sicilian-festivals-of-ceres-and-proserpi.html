Sicilian festivals of Ceres and Proserpine

Abstract written by a model

Sicilian religious festivals honor Ceres and Proserpine in gratitude for wheat: during harvest they commemorate Proserpine’s rape, then at sowing hold a ten-day Ceres festival with obscenity to cheer the grieving goddess.

DIODORUS SICULUS. .. BOOK. V.

The Sicilians held festivals honoring Ceres and Proserpine in gratitude for receiving wheat first. They celebrated the rape of Proserpine during the wheat harvest and held a ten-day festival for Ceres during wheat sowing, including ritual obscenity intended to make the grieving goddess smile.
