temple of Jupiter Olympus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The temple of Jupiter Olympus at Agrigentum, Sicily’s largest temple, was built with lofty outer pillars, foursquare inner pillars, walls, and carvings of the giants’ war and Troy. Near completion, war halted construction; after the city was sacked, it remained unfinished.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XII.

The temple of Jupiter Olympus at Agrigentum was near completion when the war stopped its construction, and it was never finished after the city was sacked. It was described as 340 feet long, 60 feet wide, and 120 feet high above its foundation, with walls and large exterior and interior pillars, and carvings depicting the giants’ war and the destruction of Troy.
