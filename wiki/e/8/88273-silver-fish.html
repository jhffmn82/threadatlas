Abstract written by a model

The silver fish is one of three transformed Adepts, changed by Coo-ee-oh or Queen Coo-ee-oh into fishes. The Flatheads seek to catch and destroy it and the other fishes. It helps pull an iron boat to shore and instructs Ervic to prepare a copper kettle with fresh lake water for them. Later, it and the three fishes wish to become girls, and it becomes a young woman with snow-white hair and brown eyes.

Chapter Nine

The silver fish is one of the three fishes into which Coo-ee-oh transformed the Adepts in Sorcery. The Flatheads sought to catch and destroy it, along with either of the other two fishes.

Chapter Sixteen

The silverfish is one of the three Adepts transformed by Queen Coo-ee-oh. It helps pull the iron boat to shore and instructs Ervic to clean the copper kettle, fill it with fresh lake water, and let the fishes swim into it.

Chapter Nineteen

The silverfish says that the three fish would like to become girls. It is transformed into a young woman with snow-white hair, deep brown eyes, pink cheeks, and ruby-red lips.
