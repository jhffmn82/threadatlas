Abstract written by a model

Turnus’s assault is an enemy siege of the Trojan camp begun after Iris reports Aeneas absent. Turnus’s forces approach, attack the fleet, walls, gates, and defenses with fire, ladders, missiles, and shields, while the Trojans resist. The Rutulians confine and weaken the defenders, but after Turnus is driven out, Aeneas’s returning fleet brings reinforcements.

BOOK IX

Turnus’s assault begins after Iris informs him that Aeneas is absent. His forces approach the camp, but the Trojans remain behind their walls while Turnus attacks the fleet and prepares to besiege the camp through the night.

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

The assault begins at dawn with trumpets, shouts, ladders, firebrands, and troops advancing under their shields. The attackers damage the defenses and breach the gate, but the Trojans resist with missiles and stones, eventually surrounding Turnus and driving him out.

BOOK X

The Rutulians keep the Trojan forces enclosed within their walls, attacking the gates, renewing fires, and inflicting heavy casualties. The defenders are weak and discouraged until Aeneas’s fleet returns with reinforcements.
