Abstract written by a model

Himiico, a Carthaginian general, sails from Carthage with one hundred galleys to surprise Dionysius’s fleet at Motya, destroys and burns ships, then returns to Africa after abandoning the attack against stronger defenses and forces.

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

Himiico, the Carthaginian general, sailed from Carthage with one hundred galleys to make a surprise attack on Dionysius's fleet at Motya. He destroyed some ships and burned others, but withdrew to Africa after failing to overcome the enemy's defenses and larger force.
