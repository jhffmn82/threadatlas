Abstract written by a model

Constable Walters is a police constable guarding the empty, police-controlled Wisteria Lodge. Around the murder, he sees a giant, unnatural-looking visitor with goggle eyes and white teeth at a window, pursues the suspected cook when he returns, and occupies the strange kitchen fetish while guarding the kitchen.

2. The Tiger of San Pedro

Constable Walters guards Wisteria Lodge and reports seeing a huge, unnatural-looking face at the window shortly before Holmes's arrival. He says the figure had enormous goggle eyes and large white teeth, and that it disappeared through the shrubbery. The newspaper later identifies him as the constable who pursued the suspected cook when the man revisited the lodge.
