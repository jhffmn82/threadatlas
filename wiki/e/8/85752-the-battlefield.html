Abstract written by a model

The battlefield is the plain holding the armies, marshes, pools, and the olive tree of Faunus. It begins as the scene of major combat, filled with slaughter, flight, dust, fire, weapons, and fallen warriors as Turnus and Aeneas devastate it. It later clears and bounds their circling flight while both armies watch.

When Turnus saw the Trojan quit the plain,

The battlefield is filled with slaughter, fleeing troops, dust, fire, weapons, and the bodies of fallen warriors. Turnus and Aeneas cause widespread destruction there while their forces fight across the plain.

Now Turnus rolls aloof o’er empty plains,

The battlefield becomes a cleared and bounded space where the armies halt their fighting to watch Turnus and Aeneas. It contains marshes, pools, and a sacred olive tree, and it is repeatedly circled during Turnus's flight.
