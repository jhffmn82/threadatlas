the old farmer in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

A strict-living father from a simple country family, he hosts city children at his farm, seeks Abraham in New York over counterfeit money, confronts Wolfe, reunites with Abraham, and invites Snipes home.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

The old farmer comes to New York looking for Abraham, whom he believes has been lured into buying counterfeit money by Perceval, Alf Wolfe's business name. He confronts Wolfe, is escorted out by Snipes, reunites with Abraham at Grand Central Depot, and invites Snipes to return with them to the farm.
