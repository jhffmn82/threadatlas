Having now said enough of these gallant men, we shall return

Themistocles sent his children's schoolmaster to tell Xerxes that the Grecians were sailing toward the bridge to destroy it. Xerxes believed the report, feared that his return to Asia would be blocked, and withdrew with his forces, leaving Mardonius behind.
