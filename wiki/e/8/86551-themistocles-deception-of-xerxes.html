Themistocles' deception of Xerxes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Themistocles' military stratagem deceives Xerxes into fearing the Grecians will destroy the bridge, causing him to withdraw toward Asia with his forces and leave Mardonius behind.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Having now said enough of these gallant men, we shall return

Themistocles sent his children's schoolmaster to tell Xerxes that the Grecians were sailing toward the bridge to destroy it. Xerxes believed the report, feared that his return to Asia would be blocked, and withdrew with his forces, leaving Mardonius behind.
