Abstract written by a model

The royal birthday cake is Hashem’s great, gorgeous pink masterpiece, made for Prince Pompadore. It disappears from the pantry table, reappears for his birthday feast, and is suspected of containing magic after a mysterious scroll and other objects appear. When Kabumpo blows out its candles, it explodes as the cake explosion.

Chapter 1

The royal birthday cake is Hashem's great pink masterpiece. It mysteriously disappears from the pantry table and then reappears, is carried to Prince Pompadore's birthday feast, and explodes when Kabumpo blows toward it.

Chapter 2

The birthday cake is blamed for the appearance of the scroll and other magical objects. Eejabo explains that it disappeared from the pantry table before the party and then returned, suggesting that magic was mixed into it.
