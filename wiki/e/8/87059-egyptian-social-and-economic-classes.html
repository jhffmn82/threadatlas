Egyptian social and economic classes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Egyptian society comprised priests, king, soldiers, shepherds, husbandmen, and artificers; soldiers formed a territorial portion, husbandmen rented land, shepherds cared for inherited flocks, and occupations generally remained inherited.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

Egyptian society included priests, the king, soldiers, shepherds, husbandmen, and artificers. Husbandmen rented cultivable land from the king, priests, and soldiers, while shepherds inherited the care of flocks and herds and each group generally maintained its inherited occupation.
