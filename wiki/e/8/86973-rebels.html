The Carthaginians being thus routed both by sea and knd, seat

The rebels in Africa gathered free men and slaves into an army reported to number two hundred thousand. They took Tunis, defeated the Carthaginians, and drove them within Carthage’s walls, but they lacked capable officers and provisions, quarreled over supreme command, and were weakened by bribery and desertion before dispersing.
