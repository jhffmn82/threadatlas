Lesbos in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Lesbos, once called Issa and Pelasgia, was seized by the Pelasgi, desolated after Deucalion's flood, settled by Macareus, and renamed when Lesbos arrived with colonists. It later revolted from Athens with the Lacedaemonians; after Mitylene surrendered, Athens demolished its walls and divided the island's lands, except Methymna's.
