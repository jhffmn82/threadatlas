Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Lesbos was described as an island once called Issa and Pelasgia, seized by the Pelasgi, desolated after Deucalion's flood, settled by Macareus, renamed when Lesbos arrived with colonists, and later revolting from Athens before its lands were divided. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Lesbos received Orpheus's head and lyre after they traveled from the river Hebrus, and the serpent attacking the head was turned into stone there.
