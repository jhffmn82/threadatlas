Abraham in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Abraham, the old farmer’s son, saves $200 for marriage, becomes engaged to Kate with the old Deacon’s consent, and goes to New York to buy a ring despite his father’s counterfeit-money suspicion.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Abraham is the old farmer's son, whom his father believes has gone to room No. 8 with $200 to buy counterfeit money. He is actually in New York to buy a ring after receiving permission to marry Kate without first saving $2,000.
