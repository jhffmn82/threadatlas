Abstract written by a model

Ozma's disappearance is the overnight removal of the Royal Palace of Oz, its occupants, and the palace itself from the Emerald City. A giant footprint and a palace-shaped hole remain in the gardens, while the palace is carried to Ev. The loss causes fear, leads Glinda to suspect magic and Ruggedo, and sends Peg Amy, Wag, Prince Pompadore, and Kabumpo toward Ev to help.

Chapter 8

The palace disappearance leaves Ozma, the Wizard, and other important people missing and causes widespread fear in the Emerald City. Glinda suspects that magic and possibly Ruggedo are connected with it.

Chapter 12

The Emerald City palace disappeared during the night before Glinda returned to the city. A great hole remains in the Royal Gardens, and Glinda has learned that a giant carried the palace and its occupants to Ev.

Chapter 13

Ozma’s disappearance is the reason Peg Amy and Wag are traveling to Ev to help her. Prince Pompadore and Kabumpo are also pursuing the matter while seeking the Proper Princess and the missing castle.
