Abstract written by a model

Raising the sunken island is the needed rescue that can free Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers without leaving the island's people homeless. At first, Glinda finds no known spell or ordinary sorcery able to do it: the Magic Isle answers only to Queen Coo-ce-oh's lost commands. Glinda and the Wizard seek its secret, considering the basement and hoping the three fishes can help. In the end, burning Gaulau powder in a steel basin and speaking “oh” raises the island slowly, lowers the water, and saves the cheering Skeezers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

Raising the sunken island is necessary to free Ozma and Dorothy from the submerged dome. Glinda finds no known spell for doing it and cannot accomplish it through her initial experiments.

Chapter Seventeen

Raising the sunken island is necessary to free the Skeezers and rescue Ozma and Dorothy without leaving the island's people homeless. Glinda says ordinary sorcery cannot accomplish it because the Magic Isle responds only to Queen Coo-ce-oh's lost commands, so she hopes the three fishes can provide the solution.

Chapter Twenty

Raising the island would allow the prisoners and the Skeezer people to be liberated, but Glinda and the Wizard do not yet know how Coo-ee-oh controlled the island's movement. Glinda believes entering the basement would be the easiest way to discover how to raise it.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The raising of the island is accomplished by burning Gaulau powder in the steel basin and speaking oh. The island rises slowly, the water level is lowered, and the Skeezers cheer because they are saved.
