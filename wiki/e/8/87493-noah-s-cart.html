Noah's cart

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: a flying dogcart, my cart, the cart, vehicle

Abstract written by a model

Noah's cart is a vehicle for taking the Dean, Augustin Jedd, to Durnstone, drawn by Old Nick on the journey. It contains Kitty and stands outside the police-station. Hannah's plan has the Dean jump clear and use a whistle while Old Nick runs on, but Noah leaves Old Nick at home.

Noah's cart is in one work on this site, Dandy Dick. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The cart is the vehicle in which Noah plans to take the Dean to Durnstone. Hannah's escape plan requires the Dean to jump from it, roll clear of the wheel, and use the whistle while old Nick runs on.
