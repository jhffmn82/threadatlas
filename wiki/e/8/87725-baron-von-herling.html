His Last Bow: The War Service of Sherlock Holmes

Baron Von Herling is the chief secretary of the German legation and a companion of Von Bork. He praises Von Bork’s espionage and discusses the possibility that England may remain neutral while Germany proceeds against France and Belgium. He leaves before the expected arrival of Altamont and does not witness Von Bork’s capture.
