Hyperion in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Hyperion, Basilea’s brother and beloved husband, fathered Helios and Selene. Said to have discovered the motions of the sun, moon, and stars, seasons, and divisions of time, he taught their nature and was called father of the planets. Envious brothers assassinated him.

And now, since we have made mention of the Atlantides, we judge

Hyperion was Basilea's brother and husband, whom she loved dearly. Their brothers conspired against him because of envy and fear that he would take the kingdom entirely, and they assassinated him.

CHAP. IV.

Hyperion was said to have first discovered the motions of the sun, moon, and other stars, together with the seasons and divisions of time. He was therefore called the father of the planets because he taught others their knowledge and nature.
