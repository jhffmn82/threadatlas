gold mines (Egypt, Arabia, and Ethiopia) in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Gold mines are a rich mining region in the confines of Egypt and neighbouring Arabia and Ethiopia, where vast labor digs gold from dark, winding workings through naturally black soil, white veins, gold-bearing rock, and other bright metals.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We sliall now speak a little of those inhabiting the southern parts>

The gold mines lie in the confines of Egypt and the neighbouring countries of Arabia and Ethiopia. They require the labor of a vast multitude of people and contain dark, winding workings with gold-bearing rock and other bright metals.
