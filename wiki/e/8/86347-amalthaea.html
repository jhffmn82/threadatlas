Amalthaea in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Amalthaea is a beautiful virgin encountered by Ammon in the Ceraunean mountains. She bears his son, Bacchus, becomes queen of the fertile Western Horn, and sees her child sent to Nysa because Ammon fears Rhea’s jealousy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The poet in his hymns confirms what we have before declared^

Amalthaea was a beautiful virgin whom Ammon met in the Ceraunean mountains and by whom he fathered a son who became famous for strength and beauty. She became queen of a neighboring fertile region called Amalthaea’s Horn, and her child was sent away to Nysa because Ammon feared Rhea’s jealousy.
