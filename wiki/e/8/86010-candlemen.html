Candlemen in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Candlemen are giant candle people of Illumi Nation, tall and tube-like with flaming heads; they supply candles and sing their national air for King Cheer, then try to light Pompadore and Kabumpo before several are blown out.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The Candlemen are tall, thin, tube-like beings whose heads are flickering flames. They sing for King Cheer, try to light Pompadore and Kabumpo as if they had wicks, and several are accidentally blown out by the travelers’ screams and Kabumpo’s actions.
