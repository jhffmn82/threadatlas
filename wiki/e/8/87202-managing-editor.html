The managing editor repeatedly tells the night editor that there is no news, then agrees to stop waiting and begin printing the paper. When Gallegher arrives, he takes Mr. Dwyer's copy, gives it to the foreman, and carries Gallegher in his arms while removing his wet shoes. He tells Gallegher, "Not this time," when Gallegher asks whether he will be dismissed for running away.
