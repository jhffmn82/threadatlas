Sappho's lyre in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Sappho's lyre, an Aeolian instrument, falls mute with its plectra silent in grief during Phaon's absence, turning away Lesbian women who come to hear it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Cur tamen Actiacas miseram me mittis ad oras, 185

Sappho's lyre is mute because of grief, and her plectra are silent. She says that the Lesbian women should not continue coming to hear her citharas while Phaon is absent.
