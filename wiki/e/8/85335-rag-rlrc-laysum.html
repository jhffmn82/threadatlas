  • In RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarization of Biomedical Texts: RAG-RLRC-LaySum is a framework for turning complex biomedical research into lay summaries. It combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Wikipedia knowledge retrieval, a neural re-ranker, external explanations for complex terms, readability-guided reinforcement learning, large-language-model prompt modification, and a Longformer Encoder-Decoder backbone. Its goal is to improve readability, factuality, relevance, and public engagement by democratizing scientific knowledge. It reportedly surpasses Plain Gemini on PLOS and eLife datasets and traditional models, with gains of 20% in readability, 15% in ROUGE-2 relevance, and 10% in factual accuracy; it ranks 11th on the BioLaySumm leaderboard. Limitations include computational complexity, reliance on Wikipedia and possible bias, readability metrics, generalizability, and automated evaluation.

RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarizati

RAG-RLRC-LaySum is a framework designed to make complex biomedical research understandable to laypeople. It combines a reranking-enhanced Retrieval Augmented Generation solution with Reinforcement Learning for Readability Control, and it reportedly outperforms Plain Gemini on the PLOS and eLife datasets.

Introduction

RAG-RLRC-LaySum is the proposed framework for generating simplified biomedical lay summaries. It combines Wikipedia knowledge retrieval, a neural re-ranker, readability-guided reward-based training, and the Longformer Encoder-Decoder backbone.

4

It effectively simplifies complex biomedical texts and is reported to enhance readability and factual accuracy for lay audiences. It surpasses traditional models, but it has limitations involving external-source bias, computational complexity, readability metrics, generalizability, and automated evaluation.
