Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XV

The speaker says that a journey with his beloved would be gentle even across the windy Alps, the Libyan Syrtes, and dangerous seas. With her, he would not fear the monsters beneath the maiden's groin, Malea, or Charybdis, and he would carry her if Neptune defeated the ship. The journey of the young man visiting Hero failed only because the way became blind.
