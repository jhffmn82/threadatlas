Little Church Around the Corner in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The Little Church Around the Corner is a church with an old rector and assistant organist. It hosts the marriage ceremony of young Standish and Miss Cambridge at three o’clock after Van Bibber secures the rector, opening, music, and bell arrangements.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" VAN BIBBER AS BEST MAN Young Van Bibber came up to town in June from Newport to see his lawyer about the preparation of some papers that needed his signature.

The Little Church Around the Corner is where Van Bibber arranges for young Standish and Miss Cambridge to be married. He secures the rector's cooperation, has the church opened at three o'clock, and arranges for music and the church bell.
