Agartharchides of Cnidus

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Agartharchides, Agatfaarchifdes of Cnidus, Agatharchidas, Agatharchidas of Cnidus, Agatharchides of Cnidus

Abstract written by a model

Agartharchides of Cnidus is an account writer whose second book on Asia treats Ethiopia and describes the pleasant hours before sunset. He is chiefly noted for explaining the Nile’s flooding as caused by continual summer rains in Ethiopia’s mountains, which fill the river until it overflows.

Agartharchides of Cnidus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Agartharchides of Cnidus comes nearest to the true explanation of the Nile’s flooding. He says that continual rains fall in the mountainous parts of Ethiopia from the summer solstice to the autumnal equinox, filling the river and causing it to overflow.
