Pasiphae in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Minos’s wife becomes enamored of a bull after his failed sacrifice, uses an artificial cow with Daedalus’s help, and bears the Minotaur.

Narrative, section by section

CHAP. V.

Pasiphae, the wife of Minos, became madly enamored of a bull after Minos failed to sacrifice the finest bull to Neptune. With Daedalus's help, she approached the bull by means of an artificial cow and gave birth to the Minotaur.
