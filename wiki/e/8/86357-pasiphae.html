Pasiphae

person · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Πασιφάης

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Pasiphae becomes enamored of a bull after Minos’s failed sacrifice, uses an artificial cow with Daedalus’s help, and bears the Minotaur. In The Library, Pasiphae bewitches Minos, falls in love with Poseidon’s bull, and receives Daedalus’s help through a wooden cow. In The Library, Pasiphae is the daughter of Perseis and the Sun and wife of Minos; she is made to desire a bull, uses a wooden cow with Daedalus’s help, and gives birth to the Minotaur.
