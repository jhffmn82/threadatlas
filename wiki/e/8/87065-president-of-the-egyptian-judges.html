Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

The president of the Egyptian judges was selected as the most eminent of the thirty judges, with another judge appointed in his place. He wore a golden chain bearing an image of truth, and when hearing a case he turned the image toward the party for whom judgment was given.
