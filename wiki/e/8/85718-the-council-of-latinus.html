Abstract written by a model

The council of the Latins hears Venulus’s report, debates responses to Diomede’s refusal and the war, then is dismissed by Latinus while negotiation, marriage, peace, and continued fighting are contested.

Thus, full of anxious thought, he summons all

The Latian council hears Venulus’s report and debates how to respond to Diomede’s refusal. The king advocates negotiation, Drances advocates peace through Lavinia’s marriage to Aeneas, and Turnus argues that the Latins should continue fighting unless a single combat is accepted.
