Dido’s magic rites

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the rites obscene, the solemn rites of sacrifice, these rites, this impious art

Abstract written by a model

Dido claims that a Massylian priestess can use magic to loosen or fix love, stop torrents, repel stars, summon ghosts, and shake the earth. The rites include altars, invocations of underworld powers, herbs gathered by moonlight, baleful juices, and the blood of a newborn foal, but Dido conceals that they are preparations for her suicide.
