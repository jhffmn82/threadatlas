CHAP. III.

The Panchean priests govern Panchsea, judge controversies, control public affairs, and distribute the common revenues and produce according to need and merit. They receive a double proportion, serve the gods, wear elaborate robes and ornaments, and are forbidden to leave the consecrated ground.
