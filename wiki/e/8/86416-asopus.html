Asopus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Asopus is a river god whose parentage is given variously as Ocean and Tethys, Poseidon and Pero, or Zeus and Eurynome. He marries Metope and fathers Ismenus, Pelagon or Pelasgus, and twenty daughters, including Aegina. After Zeus carries Aegina away, Asopus pursues him to Corinth, but Zeus drives him back to his own streams with thunderbolts.
