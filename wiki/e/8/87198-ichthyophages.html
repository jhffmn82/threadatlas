The Ichthyophages inhabit sea-coasts extending from Carmania and Gedrosia to the far end of the Red sea. They live mainly by catching and eating fish, shellfish, and other sea creatures, and some groups are described as indifferent to thirst, danger, injury, and emotional disturbance.

The Ichthyophages live dispersed along parts of the sea-shore bordering the Arabian gulf. They are said to preserve an ancient account that a former reflux left the gulf dry and green before the water returned to its channel.
