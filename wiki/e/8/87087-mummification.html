mummification in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Mummification preserves and scents a body: it is opened on the left side, intestines are removed while kidneys and heart remain, bowels are washed with wine and spices, then cedar oil and ointments treat it for forty days before myrrh and cinnamon rubbing and return to the family.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, VII.

The body is opened on the left side, the intestines are removed except for the kidneys and heart, and the bowels are cleaned and washed with wine and aromatic spices. The body is then treated with cedar oil and other ointments for forty days, rubbed with myrrh and cinnamon, and returned to the family with its features and limbs preserved.
