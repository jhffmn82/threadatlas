Abstract written by a model

Torresdale is a station outside Philadelphia on the Pennsylvania Railroad, near the old Eagle Inn. It matters as the site where suspected Hade gets off, Gallegher arranges Detective Hefflefinger’s meeting, and plans are made to take Hade away on the one o’clock train. A cab reaches Torresdale; nearby, at Keppler’s barn, Hade is arrested after a fight.

Torresdale is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
