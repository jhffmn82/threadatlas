Abstract written by a model

Sand Man is a little, old, gray-cloaked gentleman, surprisingly quick and nimble, able to fly and living near Ev with his wife. Carrying his sack, he springs cloud to cloud, mistakes Ozma's palace for an air castle, spills silver sand that sleeps nearly everyone, admires it, and hurries home to tell his wife.

Chapter 15

The Sand Man springs from cloud to cloud while carrying a huge sack and mistakes Ozma's palace for an air castle. He jumps toward the palace, spills his sack of silver sand, and causes nearly everyone inside to fall asleep. After staring at the palace in amazement, he takes his empty bag and hurries home to tell his wife.
