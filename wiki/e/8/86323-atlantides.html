Atlantides

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Atlantides

Abstract written by a model

The Atlantides are inhabitants of a rich country near Mount Atlas, bordering the ocean, noted for surpassing neighbors in receiving and entertaining strangers. Initially defeated by Merina and the Amazons, they submitted after Cercene’s destruction, surrendered their cities, formed a league with Merina, and honored her with rich gifts. They also preserve a genealogy of the gods, claiming the gods were born among them.

Atlantides is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. IV.

The Atlantides inhabited a rich country near Mount Atlas and were initially defeated by Merina and the Amazons. After the destruction of Cercene, they submitted, gave up their cities, and formed a league with Merina, who received them into her favor. They presented Merina with rich gifts and honored her.

And now, since we have made mention of the Atlantides, we judge

The Atlantides are said to inhabit a rich country bordering the ocean and to excel their neighbors in civilized reception and entertainment of strangers. They report an account of the genealogy of the gods, including the history of Uranus, Titea, Basilea, Hyperion, Cybele, and the other figures described here.
