Flathead Su-dic in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Flathead Su-dic is the cruel magician and Supreme Dictator of the Flatheads, ruling through threats and coercion. He transforms Queen Coo-ce-oh into a swan after she opposes him and prepares an attack on Ozma's party. The Flatheads rebel, capture and imprison him, take his cans of brains, and deprive him of power to cause further mischief.

Su-dic in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Su-dic is the elected ruler, leader, and Supreme Dictator of the Flatheads, ruling with his wife Rora and claiming that his word is law. He counts the votes himself to ensure yearly reelection and believes stolen canned brains make him a powerful sorcerer. His magic includes making people invisible or visible, though he forgets to use it when pursuing Ozma and Dorothy after their escape; the Golden Pig trips him and four soldiers. After the three Adepts in Sorcery are transformed, Su-dic and Rora become the Flatheads’ rulers. He rejects Ozma’s peace terms, orders her and Dorothy imprisoned, and prepares war against the Skeezers. Seeking revenge because Queen Coo-ee-oh transformed Rora into a Golden Pig, he leads armed Flatheads to the lake with copper vessels, including poison intended to kill every fish. He throws a vessel’s contents into Coo-ee-oh’s face and enchants her into a Diamond Swan. After accidentally spilling the poison, he orders the Swan shot, refuses the Skeezers protection, and returns to his mountain to feast.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Supreme Dictator, called the Su-dic, is the elected ruler of the Flatheads and claims that his word is law. He says he counts the votes himself so that he is always elected, and he believes his three cans of brains make him a powerful sorcerer. He rejects Ozma's peace terms and orders her and Dorothy cast into the bronze prison.

Chapter Seven

The Su-dic pursues Ozma and Dorothy after they escape, but the Golden Pig trips him and his four soldiers. He realizes that he could have used his own knowledge of magic to make the girls visible, but he remembered this too late. He then prepares to march all his forces against the Skeezers.

Chapter Nine

Su-dic and Rora became rulers of the Flatheads after the three Adepts in Sorcery were transformed. Su-dic stole canned brains from others to increase his power and came to the lake seeking to catch and destroy one of the three fishes.

Chapter Ten

The Su-dic leads armed Flatheads to the lake with two copper vessels, one of which contains enough poison to kill every fish in the lake. Seeking revenge because Coo-ee-oh transformed his wife into a Golden Pig, he attacks her by throwing a copper vessel's contents into her face.

Chapter Eleven

The Su-dic transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan and celebrates his revenge for Rora. He accidentally spills all of the poison he had to kill the fishes, orders his men to shoot the Swan, rejects the Skeezers' request for protection, and returns to his mountain to feast.
