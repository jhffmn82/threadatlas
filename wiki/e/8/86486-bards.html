Bards in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Bards are Gaulish poets who sing melodious praise and disparagement to harp-like instruments and are honored and obeyed in peace and war.

The Bards are poets who sing melodious songs to musical instruments like harps. They praise some people and disparage others, and they are honored and obeyed by the Gauls in peace and war.
