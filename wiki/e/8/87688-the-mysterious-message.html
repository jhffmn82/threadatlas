the mysterious message in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The mysterious message is a note in a woman's writing, signed “D.” It gives coded instructions using “our own colours,” green and white: green means open and white means shut. It directs its recipient to the main stair, first corridor, seventh right, and green baize. Holmes treats it as a signal for a dangerous assignation, ending with “Godspeed.”

The note's message reads: "Our own colours, green and white. Green open, white shut. Main stair, first corridor, seventh right, green baize. Godspeed. D." It appears to contain coded instructions, and the note is identified as being in a woman's writing.

The mysterious message contains the phrases 'Our own colours, green and white,' 'Green open, white shut,' and 'Main stair, first corridor, seventh right, green baize,' followed by 'Godspeed' and 'D.' Holmes interprets it as a signal and dangerous assignation written by an English person.
