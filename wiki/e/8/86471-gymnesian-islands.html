Gymnesian islands

Also called: Gymnesiui, these islands

Abstract written by a model

The Gymnesian islands lie opposite Spain and are so called because their inhabitants go naked in summer. The greater island is larger than all the islands mentioned except seven, while the lesser is noted for large cattle and mules; both are fertile and together have more than thirty thousand inhabitants.
