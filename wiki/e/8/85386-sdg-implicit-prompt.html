The SDG-implicit prompt masks the SDG framing by replacing references to SDG targets with environmental policies or policies. In the target detection task, target IDs are also replaced with random unique identifiers.

5 .. 5.4

With the SDG-implicit prompt, passage order more often favors ranked passages, especially for SDG Evidence Identification. GPT-3.5 generally leads precision, while SDG Target Detection recall is split between ClimateGPT-13b and Llama2-13b-chat across example settings.
