the Underground railway in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The Underground railway runs through London, including near Aldgate Station, and passes beneath or beside Caulfield Gardens. It carries the train connected to Arthur Cadogan West’s apparent fall or throwing, after which his body is found beside the track where the line emerges from the tunnel before Aldgate. Its curves and points suggest to Sherlock Holmes that the body fell from a carriage roof, having possibly been lowered onto it from Caulfield Gardens while trains were held there.
