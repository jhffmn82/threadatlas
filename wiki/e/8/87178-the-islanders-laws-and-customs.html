the islanders' laws and customs

Abstract written by a model

requires_execution_of the infirm [those who are lame or otherwise weak] The islanders killed people who were lame or otherwise physically infirm, and individuals who reached the legally appointed age ended their lives by lying on a special herb. They did not marry, shared women, raised children communally, wrote downward from the top of the page, followed prescribed diets, worshiped the heavens and celestial bodies, and buried their dead at the seashore.
