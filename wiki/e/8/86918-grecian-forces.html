Grecian forces in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V-

The Grecian forces included Lacedaemonians and other Greek mercenaries serving under Cyrus. They fought with greater skill than the lightly armed and inexperienced barbarians, routed the forces opposing them, and later repelled another attack after Cyrus's death.

Clearchus, after he had made a great slaughter among them, (it

The Greeks refused to surrender their arms after Cyrus's defeat and negotiated a temporary truce with Artaxerxes. After their commanders were massacred, they elected new officers under Chirosophus, fought their way through several hostile countries, reached the sea at the mountain Chenius, and eventually arrived at Chrysopolis.
