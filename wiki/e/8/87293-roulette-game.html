roulette game in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The roulette game limits each person to 6,000 francs and uses red as an outcome. It unfolds with successive Frenchman wins on red, ending when seven brings Cecil a larger combined win before another wager is stopped.

The roulette game produces successive wins for the Frenchman on red before the ball settles on seven, which is red, odd, and below eighteen. Cecil uses the result to secure a larger combined win and then prevents another wager.
