Sybarites in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The people of Sybaris banish five hundred wealthy citizens, then demand their surrender from Crotona and threaten war. With an army of three hundred thousand, they fight Crotona but are defeated as Sybaris is sacked. Survivors seek aid from the Lacedaemonians and Athenians to recover their country, rebuild Sybaris, and found a colony.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

agreed on upon certain conditions. Whilst things were thus acting

The Sybarites banished their wealthiest citizens and went to war against Crotona when Crotona protected the exiles. Their army was defeated, and most of it was destroyed while Sybaris was sacked. The surviving Sybarites later sought aid from the Lacedaemonians and Athenians to recover their country and establish a new colony.
