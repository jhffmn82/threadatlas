Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 is software evaluated with RadioRAG: on RSNA-RadioQA it stayed at 55% accuracy (44/80) without significant change and did not differ significantly from a human expert radiologist; on ExtendedQA, it rose from 46% conventionally to 71% with RadioRAG, up to 54% relative improvement.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

Mistral-7B-instruct-v0.2 showed no change on RSNA-RadioQA with RadioRAG, remaining at 55% (44/80; P=0.45). It showed a relative accuracy improvement of up to 54% on ExtendedQA, and its RadioRAG-powered performance did not differ significantly from that of the human expert radiologist on RSNA-RadioQA (P=0.22).

4. Discussion

Mistral-7B achieved 55% accuracy on RSNA-RadioQA both with and without RadioRAG. On ExtendedQA, its accuracy increased from 46% conventionally to 71% with RadioRAG.

Mistral-7B-instruct in SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Sihem Omri, Manel Abdelkader, and Mohamed Hamdi · 2025-02-17

Mistral-7B-instruct is a fine-tuned version of Mistral-7B and a baseline LLM model evaluated with SafetyRAG. Its developers described it as able to distinguish acceptable from toxic content, indicating some preexisting alignment, though it had lower internal alignment than GPT-3.5 and Llama-2. Without SafetyRAG, it blocked 56% of direct unsafe prompts and 24% of indirect prompt-injection prompts. With SafetyRAG, indirect interception rose to 59%, yet it could still generate harmful content.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract—Large Language Model (LLM) agents have

Mistral-7B-instruct was one of the LLM models used to evaluate SafetyRAG. The evaluation found improved ability to decline unsafe requests.

III. MATERIALS AND METHODS / A. Overall Framework .. 2) Baseline LLM models

Mistral-7b-instruct, a fine-tuned version of Mistral-7B, was used as a baseline LLM model. Its developers described it as able to differentiate between acceptable and toxic content, indicating preexisting alignment.

IV. RESULT AND DISCUSSION .. C. Discussion

Mistral-7b-instruct blocked 56% of direct unsafe prompts without SafetyRAG, and its performance improved after SafetyRAG was added. Under prompt injection, its interception rate was 24% without SafetyRAG and 59% with SafetyRAG, but it sometimes still generated harmful content and appended safety facts as a reminder.
