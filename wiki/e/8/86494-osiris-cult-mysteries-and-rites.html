Osiris cult mysteries and rites

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Egyptian rites and mysteries, Osiris worship, adoration, divine honours, mysteries and sacred rites, mystical ceremonies, rites and ceremonies, sacred mysteries, sacred rites, these rites, worship

Abstract written by a model

Osiris’s mysteries and rites honour him as a god through sacrifices, mystical ceremonies, priestly mourning, consecrated animals, and veneration of his represented phallus. Said to have been carried from Egypt into Greece by Orpheus, they were recast in Greek rites as those of Dionysius or Osiris, born from Semele by Jupiter.

CHAP. 11.

Osiris is worshipped as a god through sacrifices, mystical ceremonies, priestly mourning, and the veneration of consecrated animals. His privy-member is also represented by an image in temples and adored in rites, a practice the text connects with the Greek name Phallus.

From Osiris and Isis, to the reign of Alexander the Great, who

The mysteries and rites of Osiris are said to have been carried from Egypt into Greece by Orpheus. Greek rites declared that Dionysius or Osiris was born from Semele by Jupiter, and the text attributes this development partly to Greek ignorance and partly to the desire to regard the god as Greek.
