Cadogan West in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Cadogan West is a twenty-seven-year-old unmarried junior government clerk at Woolwich Arsenal, where he has served for ten years and personally handles the Bruce-Partington plans. Engaged to Violet Westbury and described by her as single-minded, chivalrous, and patriotic, he has ample salary, some savings, and a reputation for honesty, though Mycroft Holmes also calls him hot-headed and imperious. After a week of worry about official secrets and foreign spies, he suspects Colonel Valentine Walter of stealing the plans, follows him, and confronts Walter and Hugo Oberstein at Oberstein’s house. Oberstein kills him with a life-preserver; seven stolen plans are found on West’s body after it is placed on an Underground train, leading to an allegation of treason against him.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans

Arthur Cadogan West was a twenty-seven-year-old unmarried clerk at Woolwich Arsenal who had served for ten years and handled the Bruce-Partington plans in the course of his duties. He left Woolwich suddenly on Monday evening, disappeared from his fiancée in the fog, and was later found dead beside the Underground with seven stolen plans in his pocket.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

Cadogan West is believed to have taken the 8:15 train from Woolwich to London Bridge, appearing nervous and excited. His fiancée says he had recently worried about official secrets and foreign spies, while Holmes considers that West may have followed a foreign agent who stole the plans before his own death.

Surely enough, a note awaited us at Baker Street. A government

Cadogan West followed Colonel Valentine Walter after suspecting him of taking the plans and confronted him and Hugo Oberstein at Oberstein’s house. Oberstein struck him with a life-preserver, causing his death, and his body was later placed on the roof of an Underground train.
