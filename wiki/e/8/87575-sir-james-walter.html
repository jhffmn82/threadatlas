Sir James Walter in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Sir James Walter is a dead government official and scientist, official guardian of plans and one of two safe keyholders. He possessed office keys, was Colonel Valentine’s older brother, and died at his residence after the scandal of stolen plans; Holmes questions whether his death was natural or suicide.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

Sir James Walter, the dead scientist and senior official, dies on the morning Holmes and Watson visit his house. His brother says that the scandal concerning the stolen plans broke his heart, and Holmes wonders whether the death was natural or a suicide.
