Abstract written by a model

Glegg is a powerful, wicked old magician, judged lawless by Wag, who owns Glegg’s Magic Box and possesses Mixed Magic. Hidden near Sun Top Mountain, he enchants Peg Amy into a tree after she refuses to marry him, then uses Glegg’s Question Box to learn how she can be disenchanted. He buries his magic under Ozma’s palace and waits two years to seize Peg Amy again. Ozma transports him to the Emerald City, where Triple Trick Tea makes him explode into black soot.

Glegg is in one work on this site, Kabumpo in Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 21

Glegg was a powerful magician who changed Peg Amy into a tree after she refused to marry him. He later tries to claim her after her disenchantment, but Ozma transports him to the Emerald City by means of the Magic Belt.

Chapter 22

Glegg had buried his Mixed Magic under Ozma’s palace and waited two years for Peg to be disenchanted so he could regain control over her. He is transported to the Emerald City just as he is about to seize Peg Amy, and after being forced to drink Triple Trick Tea, he explodes and leaves black soot.
