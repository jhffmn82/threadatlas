debt and lending

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a debt, borrowed money, lent money, the debt, the debtor's goods, usury

Abstract written by a model

Debt and lending rules free a borrower who, under oath, denies an unwritten loan. Written loans may charge interest only up to double the principal. Collection reaches only the debtor’s goods, not the debtor’s body, which remains available to the city for public service.

When a borrower denies a loan made without a written record upon oath, the borrower is freed from the debt. Written loans may not bear interest exceeding double the principal, repayment is limited to the debtor's goods, and the debtor's body cannot be imprisoned for the debt because it belongs to the city for public service.
