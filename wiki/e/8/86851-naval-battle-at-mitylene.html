is_fought_at harbour at Mitylene [between Conon and Callicratides] Conon initially attacked part of Callicratides' larger fleet near Mitylene after drawing its leading ships away from the rest. Callicratides eventually brought the whole fleet together, forced Conon into the inner harbour, and gained control of the harbour outside the city walls.
