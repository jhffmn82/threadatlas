Athenian forts held money, naval preparations, and provisions; lightly guarded, they were seized by Syracusans during the sea-fight.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. II.

The Athenian forts contained money, naval preparations, and other provisions. They were lightly guarded and were seized by the Syracusans while the Athenians were drawn toward the sea-fight.
