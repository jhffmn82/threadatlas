Thurium was built in Italy near Thuria by an Athenian-led colony, after Apollo was declared founder following Delphi. Its compact plan had four lengthwise and three breadthwise streets. Thurium was built in Italy near a spring called Thuria, which the colonists believed fulfilled an oracle promising a city with little water and abundant bread. Its streets were laid out in a compact and beautiful plan, but conflict soon arose between the ancient Sybarite inhabitants and the new colonists. After the new colonists destroyed almost all the ancient inhabitants, Thurium became wealthy, allied with the Crotonians, and was governed as a democracy.
