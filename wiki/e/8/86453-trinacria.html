Trinacria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Trinacria, principal city of the Siculi, was wealthy, independent of Syracusan control, and noted for many valiant men and brave commanders; lacking assistance, it was defeated by Syracuse, its inhabitants enslaved, and the city razed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

Trinacria was the only Sicilian city not under Syracusan control. It was wealthy and known for its many valiant men and commanders, but after receiving no assistance it was defeated, its inhabitants were enslaved, and the city was razed.
