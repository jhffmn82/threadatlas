Lindus

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Lindus is a city built and named by Cercaphus’s son Lindus after he jointly ruled Rhodes and divided the country; it later contributed inhabitants to Rhodes’s incorporation into one city.

Lindus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
