Abstract written by a model

RepoGraph is a fixed repository graph for user RAG documents, providing established sources and controlled-vocabulary definitions. It unfolds through Semantic Document Chunking to U-Retrieval, with an upper layer from medical papers and books and a bottom layer from UMLS.
