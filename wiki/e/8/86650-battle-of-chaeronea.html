Abstract written by a model

The Battle of Chaeronea, following Tolmides’ capture of the city, saw Boeotians defeat Athenians, kill Tolmides, capture or kill Athenian forces, and restore Boeotian cities’ liberties.

Battle of Chaeronea is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

The Battle of Chaeronea followed Tolmides’ capture of the city. The Boeotians defeated the Athenians, killing or capturing the Athenian forces and forcing Athens to restore the Boeotian cities to their ancient liberties.
