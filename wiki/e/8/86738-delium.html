Delium

place · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Delium was occupied and fortified by Hippocrates and the Athenians, later assaulted by the Boeotians, who killed much of its garrison, captured 200 defenders, and forced the remaining Athenians to escape. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Delium is described as a place near the sea in Tanagra containing statues of Artemis and Leto.
