Wisteria Lodge in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Wisteria Lodge is a low, dark, run-down house between Esher and Oxshott, within a mile or two of Oxshott. It is home to Aloysius Garcia, his foreign household, servant, and cook, and its bell appears out of order. John Scott Eccles stays overnight, then finds the house deserted in the morning. After Garcia is killed and his servants flee, police examine the largely furnished lodge. They find a partly burned note and, in the kitchen, a mummified figure, a white cock, blood in a zinc pail, and charred lamb or kid bones. The tenants leave clothing, a pinfire revolver, a guitar, and Spanish novels behind; a giant visitor is reported at a window. Holmes, Watson, and Inspector Baynes later travel there.

Wisteria Lodge is a run-down house between Esher and Oxshott where Aloysius Garcia lives with his foreign servant and cook. John Scott Eccles stays there overnight, but finds it deserted in the morning; the police later examine the house and discover the partly burned note.

We sat for some time in silence after our visitors had left. Holmes

Wisteria Lodge is the house where Garcia's household, Scott Eccles, and the strange events were centered. Holmes infers that it lies within a mile or two of Oxshott and later travels there with Watson and Inspector Baynes.

2. The Tiger of San Pedro

Wisteria Lodge is a gloomy, largely furnished house where Mr. Garcia was killed and where his servants fled. Police find a strange mummified figure, the remains of a white cock, blood, and charred animal bones in its kitchen, while a giant visitor is reported at one of its windows.
