young Standish in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Young Standish, or Ted, is the younger son of the Standish family and brother of elder Standish. He elopes with Miss Cambridge and seeks a clergyman to marry them before returning to Boston. When his older brother arrives, Van Bibber deceives him to protect the couple and arranges their public marriage.

" VAN BIBBER AS BEST MAN Young Van Bibber came up to town in June from Newport to see his lawyer about the preparation of some papers that needed his signature.

Young Standish, also called Ted, has eloped with Miss Cambridge and is trying to find a clergyman to marry them before they return to Boston. When his older brother arrives, Van Bibber deceives the brother to protect the couple. Young Standish is married to Miss Cambridge in a public ceremony arranged by Van Bibber.
