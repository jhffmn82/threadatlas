Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the formation of the world begins with heaven and earth as one mass, from which matter separates and living creatures arise. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the creation of the world unfolds through the separation and ordering of matter, followed by the formation of man. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the creation of the universe is explained through mythological accounts involving Chaos, Earth, Heaven, the Titans, and Love.

Abstract written by a model

The formation of the world begins with heaven and earth as one mass. Fiery matter rises while earthy and watery matter sink; humid parts form the sea, and the sun hardens the earth. In Egypt, climate, heat, and fertile soil produce the first humans and other living creatures.

the creation of the world in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · about AD 8 · the work’s page · read it where the creation of the world first appears

Abstract written by a model

The creation of the world, explained by Moses in the first chapters of Genesis and contrasted with mythological accounts of Chaos, unfolds by separating and ordering matter, forming land, waters, climates, winds, stars, and creatures, then forming man last to rule the rest.
