the formation of the world in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The formation of the world begins with heaven and earth as one mass. Fiery matter rises while earthy and watery matter sink; humid parts form the sea, and the sun hardens the earth. In Egypt, climate, heat, and fertile soil produce the first humans and other living creatures.

the creation of the world in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The creation of the world, explained by Moses in the first chapters of Genesis and contrasted with mythological accounts of Chaos, unfolds by separating and ordering matter, forming land, waters, climates, winds, stars, and creatures, then forming man last to rule the rest.

creation of the universe in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

One account of the creation says that Chaos produced Earth and Heaven, from whom the Titans descended. Another says that Earth, Erebus, and Love were first, with Love issuing from the egg of Night that floated on Chaos and bringing life and joy through his arrows and torch.
