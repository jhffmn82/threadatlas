Alcmaeon in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alcmaeon was chosen by the Epigoni as their commander after the oracle promised victory if he led them. He was instructed to wage the war against Thebes and kill his mother, Eriphyle, and he led the forces that defeated the Thebans.

Alcmaeon in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Alcmaeon, son of Eriphyle, leads the Epigoni against Thebes after Apollo predicts victory and kills Laodamas. Obeying Apollo, he kills Eriphyle and is pursued by her Fury. Purified by Phegeus and Achelous, he marries Arsinoe, later Callirrhoe, colonizes Acarnania, and is killed by the sons of Phegeus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII. Κρέων δὲ τὴν Θηβαίων βασιλείαν παραλα-

Alcmaeon led the Epigoni after Apollo predicted victory under his leadership, although he initially wished to punish Eriphyle before leading the army. He killed Laodamas, later killed Eriphyle on Apollo's instruction, and was pursued by the Fury of his mother's murder. After purification by Phegeus and Achelous, he married Arsinoe and later Callirrhoe; he was eventually killed by the sons of Phegeus.
