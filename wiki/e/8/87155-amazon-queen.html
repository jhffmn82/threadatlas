Amazon queen in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

An Amazon queen governs and leads military campaigns, trains women, subdues neighbors, establishes martial laws, founds Themiscyra, and is called daughter of Mars after successes.

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Scytliians anti^ntly enjoyed but a small tract of ground

Amazon queens governed their nation and led its military expeditions. One especially renowned queen trained an army of women, subdued neighboring peoples, established laws assigning martial duties to women, and founded Themiscyra.
