single combat between Turnus and Aeneas in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

A solemnly sworn single combat between Turnus and Aeneas is meant to settle the quarrel, determine peace between Latins and Trojans, and decide Lavinia for the victor. Juturna’s provoked battle delays it and Aeneas is wounded. In a cleared field, the two fight with spears and swords; Turnus’s borrowed sword breaks, Aeneas pursues him, and wounds him with a spear.

Thus, full of anxious thought, he summons all

Turnus offers to answer Aeneas’s challenge in single combat and says that he alone will face him for the sake of the king, his people, and his own fame. The combat has not yet taken place in this unit.

BOOK XII

Turnus proposes that he and Aeneas decide the quarrel in single combat, with Lavinia going to the victor. The terms are accepted and formally sworn, but the fight does not begin because Juturna provokes a battle and Aeneas is wounded while attempting to stop it.

Now Turnus rolls aloof o’er empty plains,

Turnus and Aeneas meet in a formally cleared field while both armies stand aside. They fight first with spears and then with swords, but Turnus's borrowed sword breaks, Aeneas pursues him, and the duel ends when Aeneas's spear wounds him.

single combat between Aeneas and Turnus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Aeneas challenges Turnus to decide the war by single combat, but Turnus initially avoids the challenge. When the combat finally occurs, Aeneas wounds and kills Turnus after recognizing Pallas's belt.
