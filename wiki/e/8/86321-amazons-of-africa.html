Amazons of Africa in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Amazons of Africa were an ancient nation ruled by women, who held warfare and public authority while men managed domestic affairs and raised children. Armed with swords, darts, bows, and serpent-skin armor, they built Chersonesus, conquered the Atlantides, fought neighboring Gorgons, and expanded through Africa and Asia. After defeats by Mompsus and Sipylus, survivors withdrew into Libya; Hercules extirpated them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

The Amazons of Africa were an ancient nation governed by women who managed warfare, magistracies, and public offices, while men cared for household affairs. Under Merina, they conquered the Atlantides, fought the Gorgons, overran parts of Africa and Asia, built several cities, and possessed islands including Lesbos. After repeated defeats by Mompsus and Sipylus, the surviving Amazons withdrew into Libya, and Hercules later extirpated them.
