Abstract written by a model

In HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications, semantic chunking uses cosine similarity and a dissimilarity threshold to determine splits, with a buffer size controlling the minimum sentences before a split. In Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, semantic chunking is a baseline chunking method that enhances retrieval using relationships and embedding similarity between adjacent sentences, identifies semantic transitions by splitting at low-similarity points, and uses PIC-guided document summaries to maintain semantic coherence.
