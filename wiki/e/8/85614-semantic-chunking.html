Semantic chunking is a baseline chunking method that enhances retrieval by using relationships and embedding similarity between adjacent sentences. It identifies semantic transitions or coherence changes, splitting documents at low-similarity points, including below a 20th-percentile threshold. PIC guides this process with document summaries while maintaining semantic coherence.
