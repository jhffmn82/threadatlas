Abstract written by a model

Arginusae are islands between Mitylene and Cuma near Catanides, inhabited by Aeolians with a small town, where the Athenian fleet returned after a storm.

CHAP. XIV.

Arginusae consisted of islands lying between Mitylene and Cuma near the continent and the promontory Catanides. The islands were inhabited and contained a small town peopled by the Eolians, and the Athenian fleet returned there after the storm.
