Sabeans in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Sabeans are the most populous Arabian people, inhabiting wealthy Arabia the Happy. Exceptionally rich through high-priced trade, they remain unconquered; their hereditary kings cannot leave the palace, or an ancient oracle requires the people to stone them.

The Sabeans are described as the most populous of the Arabians and as possessing Arabia the Happy. They are exceptionally wealthy, trade valuable goods for high prices, and have remained unconquered; their kings inherit the crown but must not leave the palace, or the people stone them to death under an ancient oracle.
