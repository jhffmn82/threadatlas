Deception

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Deceit, the horse, the horse called Deception, the withers of the horse called Deception

Abstract written by a model

In Dandy Dick, Deception is described by the Dean as having frosted the genial summer of the Deanery with the winter of Deceit, and Georgiana says that once a person begins to use deception, it takes control and becomes difficult to stop. In Dandy Dick, the horse called Deception is compared by Georgiana to a horse that takes the bit between its teeth once driven with the whip, and she says that the Dean has been riding this horse hardest.
