Abstract written by a model

The Latin-Trojan peace proposal offers Trojans land by the Tiber and settlement rights, or ships to leave Italy, while dispatching one hundred senators with gifts, gold, and negotiating authority.

The Latin-Trojan peace proposal would give the Trojans land along the Tiber, allow them to build a settlement, and provide ships if they wished to leave Italy. The king also proposes sending one hundred senators with gifts, gold, and authority to negotiate.
