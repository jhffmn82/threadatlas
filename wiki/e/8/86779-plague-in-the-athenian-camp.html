Abstract written by a model

A plague caused by nearby marshy ground breaks out in the Athenian army, spreads, kills many Athenians, and makes them regret not departing earlier.

The plague broke out in the Athenian army because of a stinking marshy area nearby. It spread and killed a large part of the army, causing the Athenians to regret that they had not departed earlier.
