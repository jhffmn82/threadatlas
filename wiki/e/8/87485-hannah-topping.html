Hannah Topping in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Hannah Topping, also called Hannah Evans and Mrs. Topping, is a young, skilled cook who formerly served the Dean at the Deanery and is Noah’s wife. At St. Marvells police station, where she cooks, she finds her former master imprisoned. Using the Dean’s whistle and plans involving old Nick, a cart, handcuffs, and a duplicate key, she tries to free and feed him, but Noah discovers the effort and the Dean is returned to his cell. She later admits Georgiana and Sir Tristram, continues arranging escape, and seeks forgiveness and reconciliation with the Dean for Noah.

THE THIRD ACT.

Hannah cooks at the St. Marvells police station and learns that the prisoner is the Dean, her former master. She unlocks the prisoner's cell, feeds him, and gives him a plan involving old Nick, the cart, handcuffs, a duplicate key, and a whistle. Noah discovers them, and Hannah's attempt to help the Dean ends with him locked back in the cell.
