In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it denotes the warships Dionysius built, refitted, and repaired, most of which a Carthaginian night raid destroyed in Syracuse’s harbor. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is the fleet stationed in Motya’s harbor that Dionysius had dragged overland to sea, where it escaped, fought the pursuing Carthaginians, captured their admiral, destroyed twenty ships, and later aided his harbor attack.

Abstract written by a model

The Syracusan fleet, stationed in Motya’s harbor, was trapped when Himiico seized the passage. Dionysius had it dragged overland to sea, where it escaped, fought pursuing Carthaginians, captured their admiral, destroyed twenty ships, and later aided Dionysius’s harbor attack.
