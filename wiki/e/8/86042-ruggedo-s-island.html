Abstract written by a model

In Kabumpo in Oz, Ruggedo’s Island is the bad little island formed when Ozma stops the Runaway Country in the Nonestic Ocean. In The Gnome King of Oz, Ruggedo’s island is the lonely, rocky place where Peter searches for food and escape before leaving with Ruggedo.

Ruggedo’s island in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927 · the work’s page · read it where Ruggedo’s island first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ruggedo’s lonely island is a small, straggly, rocky, sparsely wooded place with about a dozen trees, no visible houses or people, and Ruggedo’s cavern. Peter reaches it after falling into the ocean, searches for food and escape, then leaves with Ruggedo by jumping across water when exposed sea bottom nearly touches it.

CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

The island is small, straggly, rocky, and only slightly larger than the public square near Peter's home. It has about a dozen trees, no visible houses or people, and contains Ruggedo's cave. Peter reaches it after falling into the ocean and begins searching for food and a way to leave.
