Memphis philosophers

group · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: philosophers

Abstract written by a model

propose three-zone theory [as an explanation of the Nile's summer flooding] The philosophers in Memphis argue that the Nile receives winter rains from the opposite zone, where it is then winter, and that its water is sweetened by passing through the torrid zone. Their theory is rejected as physically impossible and because boiled water would not support fish or other living creatures.
