From Osiris and Isis, to the reign of Alexander the Great, who

Orus is described as the son of Osiris and Isis and the last of the gods to reign in Egypt after Osiris's translation. Isis allegedly raised him from the dead after he was killed by the Titans and made him immortal; he is identified with Apollo and credited with benefiting humanity through medicine and divination.
