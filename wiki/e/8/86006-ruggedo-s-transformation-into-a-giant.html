Ruggedo’s transformation into a giant in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Ruggedo’s transformation begins when, fleeing Peg Amy and Wag, he pours Glegg’s Instantaneous Expanding Extract over his head. He grows sideways and upward, bursts through the cave ceiling, reaches three-quarters of a mile high, and becomes large enough for Ozma’s palace to be set on his head.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Ruggedo pours every drop of the Expanding Fluid over his head while fleeing Peg Amy and Wag. He grows sideways and then upward until he bursts through the cave ceiling and reaches a height of three-quarters of a mile.

Chapter 14

Ruggedo appears to have sprung into a giant, large enough to carry Ozma's palace on his head. The Magic Picture provides the evidence of this transformation, though it does not show his real size.
