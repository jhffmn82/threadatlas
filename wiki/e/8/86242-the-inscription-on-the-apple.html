the inscription on the apple

Also called: carmine, epistula, iurandi formula, iurantia verba, the written oath, verba, versiculis

Abstract written by a model

An inscription presents Cydippe a marriage oath; read before Diana, it makes her blush and binds her to Acontius, though she says it forced or deceived her.

XXI

The written oath presented Cydippe with a marriage formula and made her blush when she read it. Cydippe insists that Acontius's words forced or deceived her rather than obtaining her deliberate consent.
