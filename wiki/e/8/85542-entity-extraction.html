entity extraction

topic · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Entities Extraction, extracted entity
  • In Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, entity extraction identifies relevant entities in each document chunk and produces a structured name, type, and context description, with entity types selected from UMLS semantic types.
  • In HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases, entity extraction is refined through feedback when retrieved documents are incorrect, including correction of a missing entity and removal or substitution of incorrect entities.
