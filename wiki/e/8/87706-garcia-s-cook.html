Abstract written by a model

Garcia's cook, described as huge, hideous, and extremely strong, fled Wisteria Lodge after Mr. Garcia's death. Inspector Baynes's ambush caught a man believed to be him when he returned; he was arrested as a suspected assassin after resisting fiercely, biting Constable Downing, and speaking little intelligible English.

2. The Tiger of San Pedro

Garcia's cook fled Wisteria Lodge after Mr. Garcia's death and was described by witnesses as a huge and hideous mulatto with yellowish, strongly negroid features. Inspector Baynes's ambush led to the capture of a man believed to be the cook; the prisoner was described as extremely strong, savage, nearly unintelligible in English, and capable of badly biting Constable Downing.
