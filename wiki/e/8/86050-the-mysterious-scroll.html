the mysterious scroll

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: magic scroll, scroll, scrolls, that wretched scroll

Abstract written by a model

The mysterious scroll is found in the birthday cake after it explodes and contains seven-day magic. It warns that the Kingdom of Pumperdink will disappear forever unless Prince Pompadore marries the Proper Fairy Princess within the proper time. The warning makes the King wonder where she is and when Pompadore should marry her, alarms others, and remains puzzling because of unexplained initials and links to a gold door knob and gold mirror.

Chapter 2

The mysterious scroll causes the King to wonder where the Proper Princess can be found and when Pompadore should marry her. It is associated with the birthday cake and bears unexplained initials, while its connection to the gold door knob and gold mirror remains puzzling.

Chapter 3

The magic scroll is associated with the threatened disappearance that alarms Kabumpo, Spezzle, the Courtiers, and the servants. Its effects are discussed but not explained further in this unit.

Chapter 4

The mysterious scroll warns that Pumperdink will disappear forever unless its Prince marries the Proper Fairy Princess within the proper span of time. Kabumpo claims that the scroll’s magic will not take effect for seven days.
