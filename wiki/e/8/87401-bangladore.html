Bangladore in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Bangladore is a candy giant with taffy legs, a peppermint collar, sourball eyes, a chocolate coat, and huge marshmallow buttons. He bars smaller beings from his forest and sings of humans as food, threatens travelers, then falls under attack and meekly carries them out after promising silence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 11

Bangladore is a giant who threatens to eat Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion, but he falls after being attacked. The travelers discover that he is made of candy, and he meekly agrees not to sing his threatening song again in exchange for their silence before carrying them out of the forest.
