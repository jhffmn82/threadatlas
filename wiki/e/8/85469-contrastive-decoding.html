contrastive decoding

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Contrastive decoding

Abstract written by a model

Contrastive decoding is a method for context-based LLM responses that mitigates hallucinations by amplifying output-distribution differences with and without context, encouraging adherence to provided context.

contrastive decoding is in one work on this site, RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
