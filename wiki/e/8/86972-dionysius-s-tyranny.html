Dionysius's tyranny

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: his tyranny, slavery, the advancement of his tyranny, the sovereign power

Abstract written by a model

Dionysius's tyranny ruled Syracuse through mercenaries, slaves, and a fortified castle, stripping laws and liberty while imposing executions, banishments, confiscations, forced marriages, sacrilege, enslavement, and Sicilian cities' destruction or abandonment.

Dionysius's tyranny is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
