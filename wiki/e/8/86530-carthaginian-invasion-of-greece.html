Carthaginian invasion of Greece

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: invade the Greeks in Italy and Sicily

Abstract written by a model

The Carthaginians planned an invasion of Greeks in Italy and Sicily, preparing for three years, mustering three hundred thousand men and fitting out two hundred ships to coincide with Xerxes's expedition.
