the proposed marriage of Aeneas and Lavinia

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Lavinia’s bed, the dear alliance, the nuptial day
  • In The Aeneid: Latinus determines that Lavinia must marry a foreign prince rather than a native Ausonian. He identifies the foreign prince foretold by the oracles as the man destined to share his scepter and Lavinia’s bed, while Juno seeks to prevent or delay the marriage.
