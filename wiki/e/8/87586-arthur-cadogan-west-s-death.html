Arthur Cadogan West's death in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Arthur Cadogan West’s death is an unresolved incident in which his badly crushed body was found at six on Tuesday morning beside the eastbound Underground track near Aldgate Station, after being carried on the roof of a Metropolitan train. Seven Bruce-Partington plans were in his pocket. Investigators consider that he fell or was thrown from the train, while Holmes suspects he died elsewhere after confronting or pursuing the thief and was placed on the roof.

The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans

Arthur Cadogan West’s body was found at six on Tuesday morning beside the eastbound Underground track near Aldgate Station, with his head badly crushed. The circumstances suggest that he fell or was thrown from a train, but the exact cause of his death remains unresolved.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

Cadogan West's death remains unexplained after investigators find his body near Aldgate with the plans in his pocket. Holmes suspects that West died elsewhere and was placed on the roof of a train, possibly after confronting or pursuing the thief.
