2. Materials and Methods / 2.1. RSNA Cases .. 2.6. Code and Data Availability

ExtendedQA is a dataset of 24 typical radiology questions created by RS, a radiologist with 5 years of experience, and validated by DT, a board-certified radiologist with 14 years of experience. It was designed to address data-contamination concerns and was made available as open-access in Appendix S2.

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

ExtendedQA contained 24 questions. All evaluated LLMs improved with RadioRAG, with significant improvements reported for Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1, Llama3-8B, and Mistral-7B; relevant articles were selected for 83% (20/24) of questions.

4. Discussion

ExtendedQA is an external dataset containing 24 previously unseen questions that was created to assess whether findings from RSNA-RadioQA generalize beyond previously published cases. Its small size limits generalizability, despite the use of bootstrapping to mitigate this limitation.

appendix

The ExtendedQA dataset contains 24 questions used for hallucination-rate analysis. Retrieved context was relevant in 83% of cases, while context-relevant incorrect responses ranged from 8% to 21% across the models.

Appendix S2

The ExtendedQA dataset is described as a complete set of 24 expert-curated questions, each paired with a reference standard answer and subspecialty classification. Its questions address radiologic diagnosis across multiple clinical and imaging domains.
