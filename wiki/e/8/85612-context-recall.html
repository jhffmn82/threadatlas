Abstract written by a model

In HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications, it measures the proportion of ground-truth claims covered by retrieved context, with pattern-based chunking achieving 0.9046. In CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation, it measures how much relevant, grounded supporting information a RAG system retrieves and is reported at 49.46 for CausalRAG, ahead of GraphRAG-Global and GraphRAG-Local. In OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models, it measures the proportion of ground-truth-answer claims attributable to retrieved context, with OG-RAG improving correct-claim recall by 55% and matching RAPTOR for News.

Nengbo Wang, Xiaotian Han, Jagdip Singh, Jing Ma, Vipin Chaudhary · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where context recall first appears

Abstract written by a model

Context recall measures how much relevant, grounded supporting information a RAG system retrieves from external knowledge for a query. More retrieved documents generally improve it, while graph-based summarization can reduce it by pruning relevant passages. In the reported comparison, CausalRAG leads with 49.46, ahead of GraphRAG-Global at 47.22 and GraphRAG-Local at 41.54.
