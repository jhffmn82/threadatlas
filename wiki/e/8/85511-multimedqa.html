Abstract written by a model

In Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, MultiMedQA is a test-data collection contributing biomedical multiple-choice test splits from MedQA, MedMCQA, PubMedQA, and MMLU clinic topics. In Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review, MultiMedQA is a medical QA benchmark created to address limited breadth by combining seven datasets, from MedQA and MedMCQA through clinical topics and frequently searched medical questions.
