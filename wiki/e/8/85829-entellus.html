Abstract written by a model

Entellus, an aged, decrepit former champion and former pupil of Eryx, is reproached for yielding Dares the prize. He takes up Eryx’s gauntlets, defeats Dares despite a heavy fall, kills his bull prize as sacrifice to Eryx, and renounces fighting.

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

Entellus is reproached by Acestes for allowing Dares to claim the prize, then rises and offers Eryx's gauntlets for the fight. Though old and slow, he defeats Dares after falling heavily, wins the bull, kills it with one blow, offers it as a sacrifice to Eryx, and renounces the field.
