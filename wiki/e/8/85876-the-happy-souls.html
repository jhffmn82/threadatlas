Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Where long extended plains of pleasure lay:

The happy souls dwell in pleasant fields, exercising, singing, feasting, and practicing arts. They include patriots, priests, poets, inventors, and benefactors, and their heads are crowned with fillets and garlands.
