the list of names and addresses in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Holmes considers a list of six names and addresses—Lord Harringby, Sir George Ffolliott, Mr. Hynes Hynes, Mr. James Baker Williams, Mr. Henderson, and Rev. Joshua Stone—as a means of limiting the field of operations after visitors leave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We sat for some time in silence after our visitors had left. Holmes

The list of names and addresses includes Lord Harringby at The Dingle, Sir George Ffolliott at Oxshott Towers, Mr. Hynes Hynes at Purdley Place, Mr. James Baker Williams at Forton Old Hall, Mr. Henderson at High Gable, and Rev. Joshua Stone at Nether Walsling. Holmes regards it as a way of limiting the field of operations.
