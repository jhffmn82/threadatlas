Skeezer Country in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Skeezer Country is a far northwestern region of the Land of Oz bordering the north desert and containing Skeezers. Ozma and Dorothy travel there to learn of Skeezers and Flatheads, then rescuers plan to examine its sunken island.
