Indian campaign

Abstract written by a model

Indian campaign is an unprovoked undertaking against India. Semiramis gathered men, arms, and transportable boats in Bactria; after a naval victory including the Battle of the Indus, Indian elephants defeated the Assyrian army.

Indian campaign is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

For the Indian campaign, Semiramis ordered provinces to provide selected young men and new arms, assembled an enormous army in Bactria, and prepared boats that could be dismantled and carried overland. The campaign began with a naval victory but ended in defeat when the Indian elephants broke the Assyrian army.
