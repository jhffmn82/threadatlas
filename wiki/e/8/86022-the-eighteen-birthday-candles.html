Chapter 1

The eighteen birthday candles are placed on the royal birthday cake for Prince Pompadore's eighteenth birthday. They resist the attempts of Pompadore, Pompus, Pozy Pink, the Prime Pumper, and the others to blow them out, but Kabumpo extinguishes them with one breath.
