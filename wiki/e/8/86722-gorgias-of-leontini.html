Gorgias of Leontini in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Gorgias of Leontini, son of Carmantides, was a rhetorician said to be the first practitioner of rhetoric. Famous for eloquence at Olympia, he went with Tisias on an embassy to the Athenians, had a statue there placed by Eumolpus, and reportedly lived 105 years.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

Gorgias of Leontini had a statue at Olympia placed there by Eumolpus, his relative through marriage. He was the son of Carmantides and was said to have been the first to practise rhetoric, to have been famous for eloquence at the festival at Olympia, to have gone with Tisias on an embassy to the Athenians, and to have lived 105 years.
