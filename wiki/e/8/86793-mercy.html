mercy

Also called: clemency, compassion, favour, generosity, humanity, kindness, lenity

Abstract written by a model

Mercy is debated after victory over Athenian prisoners. It is praised as honourable, useful, and able to create friendship, peace, gratitude, and praise; it is also urged because of Athens, compelled allies, and Nicias’s kindness. Gylippus rejects it, citing aggression and atrocities.

Mercy is urged for the prisoners on the grounds of Athens's contribution to learning, the forced participation of some allies, and Nicias's former kindness toward Syracuse. Gylippus rejects mercy for the Athenians, arguing that their aggression and earlier atrocities have closed the way to compassion.
