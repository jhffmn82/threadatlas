Mercy is urged for the prisoners on the grounds of Athens's contribution to learning, the forced participation of some allies, and Nicias's former kindness toward Syracuse. Gylippus rejects mercy for the Athenians, arguing that their aggression and earlier atrocities have closed the way to compassion.
