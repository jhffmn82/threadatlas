Abstract written by a model

The Enchanted Mountain of the Flatheads is a round, oblong, saucer-shaped mountain just south of the Lake of the Skeezers. Towering above the plain, it has straight, steep sides and a flat, level hollow summit concealed from people below. The Flatheads live there among rock-built houses, paths, walls, and a central palace. Difficult to climb, it is entered by passing an invisible wall and using a rock-cut up-and-down stairway. There the Su-dic is overthrown, and Glinda transforms the Flatheads into Mountaineers.

Chapter Five

The flat mountain is round, steep-sided, and topped by a flat surface where the Flatheads live. Its sides are difficult to climb, and an entrance cut into the rock provides access to a stairway leading upward.

Chapter Six

Flathead Mountain has a saucer-shaped summit concealed from people below. Its rock-built houses, paths, walls, and central palace belong to the Flatheads.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Flatheads' Enchanted Mountain is reached by passing around an invisible wall and climbing an up-and-down stairway to the mountaintop. It is the place where the Su-dic is overthrown and where Glinda transforms the Flatheads into Mountaineers.
