Sun Top Mountain in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Sun Top Mountain is a kingdom in the very centre of North Winkie Country, crowned by a glittering gold castle and ruled by Princess Peg Amy. Near the travelers’ stopping field, it rises in a steep purple slope with gardens, golden arbors, and flower-bordered paths. After Peg Amy disappears it becomes gloomy, but her return brings bells, a feast, the Courtiers’ return, and her marriage to Pompadore.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

Sun Top Mountain is identified as the location of the Princess whom Prince Pompadore must marry. Its kingdom is said to be in the very Centre of the North Winkie Country.

Chapter 20

Sun Top Mountain is near the field where the travelers stop, although they do not initially realize how close they are. It has a steep purple slope, gardens, golden arbors, flower-bordered paths, and a glittering gold castle at its summit.

Chapter 21

Sun Top Mountain became gloomy after Peg Amy disappeared, with no singing, dancing, or happiness. It celebrates her return with bells, a feast, the return of the Courtiers, and the marriage of Peg Amy and Pompadore.
