Daphnaeus was appointed general of the Syracusan relief army, which included forces from Italy, Messana, Camarina, Gela, and elsewhere. He defeated the Carthaginian army after it crossed the Himera and then encamped in the enemy's former position, but restrained his troops from pursuing too far.
