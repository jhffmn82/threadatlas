Abstract written by a model

Theodorus, Rhoecus's son, made part of Apollo Pythius at Ephesus, governed Athens, held great noble authority, and urged removing Dionysius to restore laws and liberties.

Theodorus, a Syracusan nobleman of great authority who had served his country, urged the assembly to remove Dionysius and restore the laws and liberties. He accused Dionysius of tyranny, sacrilege, cruelty, military incompetence, and responsibility for the ruin of several Sicilian cities.
