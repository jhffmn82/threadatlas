Narrative, section by section

22 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

At Prince Pompadore's eighteenth birthday feast in Pumperdink, the royal birthday cake mysteriously disappears and then reappears before exploding when Kabumpo blows out its eighteen candles. King Pompus blames the cooks and footmen for the explosion and orders them dipped, while Queen Pozy Pink faints and the courtiers are knocked unconscious or thrown under the table. A scroll reveals that Pumperdink will disappear from the Gilliken country of Oz unless Prince Pompadore marries the Proper Fairy Princess within the required time.

Chapter 2

King Pompus and his household try to determine how to find the Proper Princess and prevent Pumperdink from disappearing. They blame the birthday cake for producing the mysterious scroll, a gold door knob, and a gold mirror, while the mirror reveals truthful descriptions of those who look into it. The King orders Prince Pompadore to marry Princess Faleero, an unattractive thousand-year-old fairy princess, and she accepts the proposal for the next morning. Kabumpo objects to the marriage and urges Pompadore to run away, but the Prince refuses because he does not want Pumperdink to disappear.

Chapter 3

Kabumpo prepares himself and his attendant Spezzle for the threatened disappearance caused by the magic scroll. He takes jewels, a gold door knob, and a mirror before returning to his room to wait for night. After the palace of Pumperdink is secured and everyone retires, Kabumpo steals Pompadore’s crown, carries the sleeping Prince outside, and escapes with him through a secret entrance. Kabumpo tells Pompadore that they are disappearing from Pumperdink.

Chapter 4

Prince Pompadore wakes in a forest and learns that Kabumpo secretly carried him away from Pumperdink to prevent his marriage to Faleero. They travel south toward the Emerald City, hoping that Ozma of Oz may be the Proper Princess whose marriage to Pompadore can save the Kingdom of Pumperdink from disappearing. Along the way they meet the inquisitive Curious Cottabus, who questions them repeatedly before they continue. Near the end of the journey’s first stretch, Pompadore spots a huge black flag covered with figures and signs.

Chapter 5

Kabumpo and Pompadore enter Rith Metic, a strange city inhabited by Figure Heads and ruled by a giant wooden ruler. The King of the Figure Heads orders Count It Up to force them through arithmetic problems, including long addition and fractions. The Curious Cottabus hints that somersaulting can provide a way out, and Pompadore’s somersaults frighten away the Figure Heads so the travelers can escape. Afterward, Pompadore vanishes when he falls through a marble slab marked, “Please Knock Before You Fall In.”

Chapter 6

Ruggedo, the former Gnome King, lives secretly in an underground cavern beneath the Emerald City, where he carves his life story into six rocks and plots to regain power. His rabbit servant Wag and the wooden doll Peg Amy are the cavern’s principal inhabitants and victims of Ruggedo’s temper. Ruggedo has stolen furnishings and household objects from the Emerald City for his cavern and gives many of them to Wag. At the end of the unit, Ruggedo discovers Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic and plans to use it against the Emerald City of Oz.

Chapter 7

Sir Hokus of Pokes decides to pursue a giant quest after hearing stories about giants, and he chooses to travel to Ev to confront a three-legged giant. Dorothy, the Patch Work Girl, the Scarecrow, Tik Tok, and other friends discuss the dangers of giants in the royal palace. Tik Tok explains that Ev lies beyond the Impassable Desert, and the Scarecrow supplies the missing end of his sentence when Tik Tok stops working. Before the group can retire, the royal palace is violently shaken and appears to fly into the air, leaving everyone confused and alarmed.

Chapter 8

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers discovers that Ozma’s Emerald City palace has vanished into a deep hole and alerts the city. Unk Nunkie directs the frightened inhabitants to seek Glinda, who consults the magic Book of Records and finds references to Glegg’s box of Mixed Magic and Ruggedo. A huge footprint suggests that a giant carried away the palace, while Ruggedo is missing. Glinda returns to her palace to study giants and magic in hopes of rescuing Ozma and the others.

Chapter 9

Ruggedo finally opens Glegg’s box of Mixed Magic after throwing it against a rock wall. He uses the magic on Peg Amy and Wag, enlarging them, and then uses the Re-animating Rays to bring Peg Amy to life. When Peg and Wag chase him, Ruggedo pours Expanding Fluid over himself and grows into a giant, carrying Ozma’s palace away on his spiked head. The resulting upheaval causes confusion in Oz and gives Ruggedo a severe headache.

Chapter 10

Peg Amy and Wag survive Ruggedo’s final expansion, which blows open the cave and leaves them greatly enlarged. They discover that Mixed Magic has made them much bigger while everything else, including the houses and garden plants, remains the same. Peg finds Glegg’s magic box, uses its Question Box to learn that Ruggedo is in Ev, and prepares to travel there with Wag. The pair leaves Oz heading west toward Ev to find Ruggedo and make everything right again.

Chapter 11

Pompadore and Kabumpo enter the Illumi Nation by knocking on a marble stone and falling down a hidden passage. They meet the Candlemen and King Cheer, accidentally extinguish the king and several Candlemen, and are trapped briefly under brass snuffers. Tommy Tallow leads them through an uphill passage to the surface, where they learn that Illumi candles shrink in the upper air. Still seeking a Proper Princess, they travel through the Gilliken country and follow the smell of cooking soup.

Chapter 12

Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore reach the Soup Sea, where they eat soup, carrots, and rolls before continuing to the Emerald City. They find Glinda instead of Princess Ozma and learn that Ozma, the Royal Palace, and everyone in it disappeared after a giant carried them to Ev. Glinda leaves to oppose the giant, while Kabumpo and Pompa decide to follow her and rescue Ozma before the seven-day deadline expires. They take a sword with an emerald handle from the sleeping Soldier with the Green Whiskers and set out for Ev.

Chapter 13

Peg Amy and Wag meet Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore while traveling to Ev to help Ozma. Peg discovers that she has seen the Winkie landscape before and says she has been alive before, while Pompadore explains that a magic scroll requires him to marry a Proper Princess or lose Pumperdink. The travelers learn that Ruggedo used Mixed Magic, causing Peg to become alive, Peg and Wag to grow, and Ozma’s castle to disappear. Peg, Wag, Kabumpo, and Prince Pompadore join forces and continue toward Ev, where they hope to make Ruggedo undo the magic.

Chapter 14

An earthquake-like upheaval leaves Ozma's palace in ruins and injures or incapacitates many of its occupants. The Magic Picture reveals that Ruggedo has become a giant and that Ozma's palace is sitting on his head. Ozma, Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Scraps, Sir Hokus of Pokes, Tik Tok, and the others plan to threaten Ruggedo with ten thousand eggs, which can destroy a gnome if they touch him. Their sign frightens Ruggedo into holding perfectly still, but the group then sees someone walking over the clouds who may be able to help.

Chapter 15

The Sand Man mistakes Ozma's palace for an air castle and jumps toward it, spilling his sack of silver sand. The sand puts Ozma, Dorothy, Trot, Betsy Bobbin, Sir Hokus, Ruggedo, and nearly everyone else in the palace into an overpowering sleep. Scraps and the Scarecrow remain awake and patrol the palace, arming themselves against possible attack. The Sand Man leaves to tell his wife about the real palace among the clouds.

Chapter 16

Kabumpo, Peg Amy, Prince Pompadore, and Wag travel through the Winkie farmlands toward Ev while discussing Peg’s memories and Glegg’s Magic Box. Kabumpo gives Peg a pearl necklace and helps repair her clothes, while Peg hopes to help Pompadore win Ozma’s approval. In a dead-looking forest, the travelers are attacked by the Twigs, but Kabumpo, Wag, and Pompadore drive them off and escape. They continue their journey to rescue Ozma, though Wag worries about crossing the Deadly Desert.

Chapter 17

Peg Amy, Pompa, Kabumpo, and Wag are captured by the Runaway Country, a living land that wants them to settle and develop it. Peg persuades the Runaway Country to stop for supplies, but it carries them across the Deadly Desert into Ev. When they encounter the enormous giant sitting on a mountain, the Runaway Country flees and throws the travelers into the air. After they recover, Peg finds Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic, which is open, while Pompa’s sword and the Magic Box are initially missing.

Chapter 18

Wag discovers that the giant is Ruggedo and nearly attacks him, risking the safety of Ozma’s palace on Ruggedo’s head. The Scarecrow explains that Ruggedo has been kept quiet while Glinda tries to reduce him, but the Question Box reveals that Trick Tea will make Ruggedo return to the Emerald City and shrink. Glinda brings Ozma down, and the tea sends Ruggedo marching away, while Pompadore proposes marriage to Ozma and is immediately refused. Peg Amy runs away before Pompadore can propose, and the Scarecrow goes after her.

Chapter 19

Ozma rejects Prince Pompadore's marriage proposal, uses the Question Box to learn that he must marry the Princess of Sun Top Mountain, and stops the Runaway Country. Glinda reports that Ruggedo has been reduced to his former size and locked up, so Ozma, Glinda, and the Scarecrow decide to banish him to the Runaway Country after making it an island. Ozma arranges for Prince Pompadore, Kabumpo, Peg Amy, and Wag to travel across the Deadly Desert to the Winkie Country, while she returns to the Emerald City.

Chapter 20

Pompa, Kabumpo, Wag, and Peg Amy travel with the Runaway Country into the Winkie Country while seeking the Princess of Sun Top Mountain and a way to save Pumperdink. They find a magnificent gold castle atop a purple mountain and enter its court room using the gold door knob. When Peg Amy looks into the Magic Mirror, it identifies her as Princess of Sun Top Mountain and the Proper Princess, transforming her from a wooden doll into a living princess.

Chapter 21

Uncle Tozzyfog explains that Glegg enchanted Peg Amy into a tree after she refused to marry him, and that she later became a wooden doll. Wag and Pompadore freed her by calling her a Princess and believing that she was one. Glegg tries to seize Peg Amy, but Ozma transports him away, and Pompadore and Peg Amy marry so they can help save Pumperdink. Peg Amy, Pompadore, Kabumpo, and Wag then travel to the Emerald City and return to Sun Top Mountain.

Chapter 22

Ruggedo confesses his actions to Ozma, who uses her Magic Belt to transport him and the Runaway Country into the Nonestic Ocean, where it becomes Ruggedo’s Island. Glegg is brought to the Emerald City just as he is about to seize Peg Amy, and Ozma makes him drink his own Triple Trick Tea, which causes him to explode. Ozma and the others put the topsy-turvy Ozma’s palace in order while preparing for Peg Amy and Pompa’s visit.
