Pokes in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921 · the work’s page · read it where Pokes first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pokes is the Great Grand and Mighty Slow Kingdom, a strange gray city in Oz with a dimly lighted main gate, gloomy garden, castle, and a speed limit of one-quarter mile an hour. Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion reach it through a forest and sleep at its gate. Its strict ban on quick movement and sound leads to their arrest, but singing keeps them awake and lets them escape with Sir Hokus beyond the gates.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

The Great Grand and Mighty Slow Kingdom of Pokes is a strange gray city reached by Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion after they follow signs through an unfamiliar forest. They arrive at its dimly lighted gate and fall asleep there.

CHAPTER 5

Pokes is a gray, sleepy kingdom with strict rules against running, singing, whistling, and speaking quickly. Its inhabitants arrest Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion for speeding and repeatedly return Sir Hokus whenever he falls asleep while trying to escape.

CHAPTER 6

The Pokes kingdom is a place where singing prevents people from falling asleep, and the travelers must sing to escape it. The Pokes cannot live outside the kingdom, so they do not pursue Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion beyond its gates.

Abstract written by a model

Pokes is a Kingdom called the stupidest in Oz, where a black Knight imprisoned Sir Hokus for five centuries; he escaped with Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion, and it remains tied to his former identity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

CHAPTER TWO

Sir Hokus was imprisoned in Pokes for five centuries after a black Knight transported him there. He escaped from Pokes with Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion, but remembers little of his earlier life.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Pokes is associated with Sir Hokus's former identity and is the place to which the Sultan sent him through forbidden magic.
