Abstract written by a model

Tyrrhenian society possessed fertile, well-tilled land and abundant harvests, supporting elaborate homes, servants, and luxury; after abandoning former sobriety, its people became idle, drunken, and debauched.

Tyrrhenian society was marked by fertile land, abundant harvests, elaborate homes, porticoes, fine carpets, silver cups, numerous servants, and richly furnished apartments for servants and freemen. The people were said to have abandoned earlier sobriety for idleness, luxury, drunkenness, and debauchery.
