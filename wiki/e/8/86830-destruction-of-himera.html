CHAP. VII.

Hannibal besieged Himera with Carthaginian and Sicilian forces, battering and undermining its walls. After the defenders were defeated and the city was entered, Hannibal ordered the inhabitants' slaughter to stop, but the city was plundered, its temples were burned, and Himera was razed.
