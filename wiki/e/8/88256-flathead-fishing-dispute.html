Flathead fishing dispute in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Flathead fishing dispute is a conflict between the Skeezers and Flatheads over Lake of the Skeezers fish. Flatheads fish because their mountain lacks fish; Rora's poisoning attempt transforms her and prompts the Su-dic to declare war.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Flatheads fish in the Lake of the Skeezers because they have no fish on their mountain, while the Skeezers forbid them to take fish from the lake. The dispute escalates after Rora attempts to poison the fish, leading to her transformation and the Su-dic's declaration of war.
