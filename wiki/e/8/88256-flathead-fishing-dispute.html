Chapter Six

The Flatheads fish in the Lake of the Skeezers because they have no fish on their mountain, while the Skeezers forbid them to take fish from the lake. The dispute escalates after Rora attempts to poison the fish, leading to her transformation and the Su-dic's declaration of war.
