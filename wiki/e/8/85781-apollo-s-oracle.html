Abstract written by a model

directs Aeneas [to seek the ancestors’ motherland] Apollo’s oracle tells the Trojan survivors to seek the mother earth from which their ancestors came and promises that the Aeneian house will rule widely. Anchises first interprets the answer as referring to Crete, but the household gods later reveal that it refers to Italy.
