Dionysius's seizure of power

Abstract written by a model

Dionysius seized power in Syracuse by accusing rivals, controlling soldiers, building a personal guard, recruiting supporters, removing opponents, and openly declaring himself king, establishing tyranny.

CHAP. 3(111.

Dionysius's seizure of power proceeded through accusations against rival commanders, control of soldiers, the creation of a personal guard, and the recruitment of mercenaries and exiles. He removed or sent away potential opponents and openly declared himself king after securing Syracuse with armed forces.
