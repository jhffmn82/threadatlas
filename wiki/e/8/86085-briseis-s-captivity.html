III

Briseis was captured when Achilles destroyed Lyrnesus and killed her husband and three brothers. She was then taken from Achilles and delivered to Agamemnon, and she fears being left behind or given to another man. She says that her captivity was once presented to her as a gain because it brought her to Achilles.
