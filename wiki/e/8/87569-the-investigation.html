the investigation in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The investigation, conducted by Sherlock Holmes with Dr. Watson, Inspector Lestrade, and Inspector Baynes, follows evidence from Aldgate to Woolwich and traces Henderson to Don Murillo. It links Miss Burnet to Garcia's death, uncovers an attempted concealment and the attempted murder of Miss Burnet, and pursues contradictions involving stolen plans, foreign spies, and West's final movements despite legal difficulties.

The investigation by Holmes and Inspector Baynes traces Henderson to Don Murillo, establishes Miss Burnet's connection to Garcia's death, and uncovers the attempted concealment of the crime. Although the police work advances, Holmes notes that legal difficulties remain because the evidence concerns a planned killing and the wider crimes occurred abroad.

An hour later Holmes, Lestrade and I stood upon the Underground

The investigation follows the evidence from Aldgate to Woolwich and examines the train, the office, the witnesses, and the stolen plans. Each inquiry produces further contradictions, so Holmes plans to investigate foreign spies and international agents while continuing to reconstruct West's final movements.
