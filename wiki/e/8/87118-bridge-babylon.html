bridge (Babylon) in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

A bridge over the Euphrates, built by Semiramis at its narrowest part, spans five furlongs and is thirty feet wide. Stone pillars, spaced twelve feet apart, support it; iron hooks, lead, pointed current defenses, and cedar, cypress, and palm flooring complete it.

Semiramis builds a bridge across the narrowest part of the Euphrates, five furlongs long and thirty feet broad. Its stone pillars are joined with iron hooks and lead, protected by pointed defenses against the current, and floored with cedar, cypress, and palm timber.
