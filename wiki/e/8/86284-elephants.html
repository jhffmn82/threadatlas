Abstract written by a model

Elephants are wild beasts among the southern parts of Troglodyta. When full after feeding, they sleep leaning against trees and cannot rise if they fall on their backs. Elephantophages hamstring or fell them by cutting trees, then kill and eat them; Ethiopians’ serpents also blind, coil around, and tear them apart. Ptolemy the Second collected and trained many for war.

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

Elephants are hunted by Elephantophages, who hamstring them with axes or cause them to fall when trees used as resting places are cut nearly through. An elephant sleeps leaning against a tree and cannot rise when it falls onto its back; the hunters then kill it and eat it.

Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

Ptolemy the Second collected and trained many elephants for war while encouraging their hunting. Ethiopians are said to have serpents that can coil around elephants, blind them with their eyes, and tear them apart after they fall.
