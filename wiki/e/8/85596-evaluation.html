Evaluation empirically assesses LightRAG on benchmark data, using LLM-based measures of comprehensiveness, diversity, empowerment, and overall performance to address RQ1–RQ3 on generation, retrieval, indexing, costs, and adaptability to data changes.

The evaluation uses benchmark data to study LightRAG's generation performance, retrieval and indexing mechanisms, costs, and adaptability to data changes. It employs an LLM-based assessment across Comprehensiveness, Diversity, Empowerment, and Overall dimensions.
