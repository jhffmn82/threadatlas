the magic parasol

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: little umbrella, magic parasol, parasol, the little parasol, the parasol

Abstract written by a model

The magic parasol is a magical object attached to Dorothy’s wrist that opens when she throws up her hands. Though broken after being snapped off the beanstalk, it carries Dorothy through a broken skylight, helps redirect a silver vase, lifts the party from the Silver Island, and lets Dorothy and the Scarecrow fly after Sir Hokus. Dorothy finally gives it to Ozma for storage with Oz’s magic treasures.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 20

The magic parasol is attached to Dorothy's wrist and opens when she throws up her hands. It carries her through the broken skylight, then brings her back down onto the silver vase and redirects it away from the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 21

The magic parasol carries the party upward when Dorothy opens it. After Dorothy previously closed it to descend, she opens it again with the others tied to its handle, allowing the group to escape the Silver Island.

CHAPTER 24

The magic parasol enables Dorothy and the Scarecrow to fly after Sir Hokus and then descend with him toward Ozma's palace. Dorothy gives the parasol to Ozma, who has it stored with the other magic treasures of Oz.
