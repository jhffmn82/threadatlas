the ward detectives in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Ward detectives pursue Ragsey Raegen on suspicion, investigate with McGonegal's friends Wade and Heffner, and watch a tenement from across the street, causing Rags to remain inside while they are nearby.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The sun rose hissing out of the East River, a broad, red disc of heat.

Ward detectives stand across the street from the tenement early in the morning and later speak with two of McGonegal's friends. Rags believes they are watching for him and avoids going outside while they remain nearby.
