Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Sphacteria is an island near Pylos at the harbor entrance, occupied by Spartans and allied elite soldiers until an Athenian fleet blockaded it and forced surrender. In Description of Greece, Sphacteria is an island before the harbour of Pylos, where Lacedæmonian reverses made it world-famous and Athenians captured shields and commemorated their success with a brazen Victory statue.

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Sphacteria lies before the harbour of Pylos, much as Rhenea lies in relation to the harbour of Delos. The reverses of the Lacedæmonians there made the island world-famous, and the Athenians erected a brazen statue of Victory in their Acropolis to commemorate their success.
