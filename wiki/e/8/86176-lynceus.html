Lynceus

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Lynceo, Lynceu, one brother left, unus, Λυγκεὺς

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Lynceus is the one son-in-law left alive after the massacre; Hypermestra wakes him, tells him to escape while night remains, and asks him either to rescue her or to kill and bury her if he cares for his pious sister. In The Library, Lynceus is the son of Egyptus who survives because Hypermnestra spares him, marries her, becomes king of Argos after Danaus, and fathers Abas.
