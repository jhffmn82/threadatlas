Pumperdink in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Pumperdink is a cozy, old-fashioned, ancient and honorable kingdom, also called a country, in the northern Gilliken Country of Oz. It has a palace and a courtyard well: inhabitants who do wrong are lowered into the stone well and dipped. It is ruled by Pompus and Pozy Pink, while Prince Pompadore is its prince and regards it as his homeland. Its central crisis is a magic scroll’s warning that Pumperdink and everyone in it will disappear unless Pompadore marries a Proper Princess, or Proper Fairy Princess, within the stated time, described as seven days. Fear of this danger darkens and secures the palace, and Pompadore and Kabumpo leave Pumperdink to seek help and continue a marriage quest. The Question Box later confirms that it has not disappeared, although it is subsequently described as having disappeared and still needing rescue. After Pompadore learns that Peg Amy is the proper princess, he considers returning with her. Their marriage saves Pumperdink; news is carried to the kingdom, and the couple visits its King and Queen.

Chapter 2

Pumperdink is threatened by a possible disappearance that could affect the kingdom and its people. King Pompus believes Pompadore’s marriage to the Proper Princess may prevent this, while Kabumpo and Pompadore remain concerned about the danger.

Chapter 3

Pumperdink is the place from which Kabumpo and Pompadore disappear. Its palace becomes dark and heavily secured as its people fear the threatened disappearance.

Chapter 4

The Kingdom of Pumperdink is an ancient and honorable kingdom in the Gilliken Country of Oz. Its continued existence depends on Prince Pompadore marrying a Proper Fairy Princess within the time stated by the scroll.

Chapter 11

Pompadore fears that Pumperdink will disappear forever if he cannot find a Proper Princess. He continues traveling south with Kabumpo despite this fear.

Chapter 12

Pumperdink is threatened with disappearance unless Prince Pompadore marries the Proper Princess within seven days. Pompa and Kabumpo travel from Pumperdink to the Emerald City seeking help for the threatened country.

Chapter 13

Pumperdink is Prince Pompadore’s Kingdom. According to the magic scroll, it and everyone in it will disappear unless Pompadore marries a Proper Princess in the proper time.

Chapter 18

Pumperdink is Pompadore’s homeland. The Question Box confirms that it has not disappeared.

Chapter 19

Pumperdink is threatened with disappearing unless Prince Pompadore follows the directions connected with the mysterious scroll. Prince Pompadore continues his marriage quest in an effort to save the kingdom.

Chapter 20

Pumperdink remains in danger because it has disappeared, and Pompa continues seeking a way to save the kingdom. Pompa considers returning there with Peg Amy after learning that she is the proper princess.

Chapter 21

Pumperdink is the kingdom Pompadore must save. After Pompadore and Peg Amy marry, a messenger carries the news to Pumperdink, and the couple visits its King and Queen.
