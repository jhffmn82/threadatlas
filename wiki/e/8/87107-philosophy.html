The Thebans claim to have been the first people to discover exact rules for improving philosophy.

CHAP. III.

The Chaldeans hold that the world is eternal, without a definite beginning or end, and that divine providence orders and sustains it. Their philosophical learning is transmitted from fathers to sons, while the text contrasts this continuity with the competing sects and changing opinions of the Greeks.
