Also called: British promontory of Belerium

Abstract written by a model

Belerium is a British promontory, four days' sail from the continent, whose inhabitants, shaped by merchant dealings, are courteous to strangers and mine and refine tin from rocky ground.

Belerium is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

For over against the French shore^ opposite to the Hercynian

Belerium is a British promontory described as four days' sail from the continent. Its inhabitants are considered more civilised and courteous to strangers because of their dealings with merchants, and they mine and refine tin there.
