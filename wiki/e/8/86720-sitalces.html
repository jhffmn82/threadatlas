Abstract written by a model

Sitalces, king of Thrace, is just and kind to his subjects, and enlarges his dominions through valor and prudence. With annual revenue above a thousand talents, he commands more than 120,000 foot and 50,000 horse. Allied with the Athenians, he invades Macedonia against Perdiccas to restore Amyntas, then makes peace with Perdiccas, confirms it by marriage, and withdraws into Thrace.

Sitalces is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sitalces, king of Thrace, greatly enlarged his dominions through valor and prudence and had an annual revenue of more than a thousand talents. He invaded Macedonia with more than 120,000 foot and 50,000 horse to assist the Athenians, oppose Perdiccas, and restore Amyntas, but later made peace with Perdiccas, confirmed it by marriage, and withdrew into Thrace.
