The Adventure of the Devil’s Foot

The sitting-room contains the table where the Tregennis siblings play cards, four burned-out candles, and the remains of a fire. Sherlock Holmes studies the chairs, window, garden view, floor, ceiling, and fireplace, but does not identify the cause of the tragedy.
