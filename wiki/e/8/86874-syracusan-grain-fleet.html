Abstract written by a model

carry grain [for the besieged at Agrigentum] The Syracusan grain fleet carried abundant corn intended for Agrigentum. It was attacked unexpectedly by Amilcar's forty galleys in winter; eight ships were sunk and the remainder were driven ashore and captured.
