Rhodope

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Mount Rhodope, Rhodopeia, the summit of a hill
  • In Heroides and Amores, Rhodope is the icy mountain region where Phyllis received Demophoon and ruled broad realms, extending toward Haemus.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Mount Rhodope is where Orpheus sings to his lyre while leading woods, rocks, and wild creatures with his music.
