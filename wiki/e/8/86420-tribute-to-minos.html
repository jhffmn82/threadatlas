tribute to Minos

tribute · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: bodies of a hundred bulls, conditions, fourteen boys, the Minotaur's tribute, tribute

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Minotaur's tribute consists of seven boys and seven girls sent by the Athenians to Minos for seven years, followed by fourteen children demanded and delivered after the seven years expired. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the tribute draws youths and virgins from the best Athenian families, with accounts varying between annual and nine-year dispatches and some specifying seven young men and seven virgins.

Abstract written by a model

The Athenians were required to send seven boys and seven girls to Minos for seven years so that the Minotaur could devour them. When the seven years had expired, Minos came again and demanded fourteen children, and the Athenians delivered them.
