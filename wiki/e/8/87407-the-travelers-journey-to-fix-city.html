Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion journey through deserted country, rest at a stream and pear orchard, then reach Fix City at night for food and transport by walking beds.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion travel along a wide, shady road through beautiful but deserted country. After stopping at a stream and a pear orchard, they reach Fix City at night, where they are given food and transported to sleeping quarters by walking beds.
