are_used_by Athenians [for naval combat and escape] War galleys were central to both naval engagements. The Athenians used eighty-six galleys in the first battle and 115 in the second, while the Syracusans used seventy-four in each; many were destroyed, disabled, or captured amid ramming, grappling, fires, and fighting aboard the ships.
