the note sent to Garcia in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

The note sent to Aloysius Garcia at Wisteria Lodge is a quarter-sheet cream-laid paper message, folded and sealed with purple wax and Lopez's sleeve-link. Recovered unburned from the back of the dog-grate after Garcia apparently tried to burn it, it is addressed in D.'s woman's handwriting. Holmes concludes that Miss Burnet wrote it in the High Gable household, while Lopez forced her to reveal Garcia's address and sent it. The coded message reveals Garcia's confederate and gives Miss Burnet's final instructions for a planned attack on Don Murillo: green or white window lights facing the drive, the San Pedro colours, would signal whether it was safe to proceed.

The note addressed to Aloysius Garcia is written on quarter-sheet cream-laid paper, folded and sealed with purple wax. Inspector Baynes recovers it unburned from the back of the dog-grate after Garcia apparently threw it into the fire.

The coded note contains Miss Burnet's final instructions to Garcia about the planned attack on Don Murillo. A green or white light in a window facing the drive was to signal whether it was safe to proceed, and the San Pedro colors were green and white. Lopez addresses and sends the note after forcing Miss Burnet to reveal the relevant address.
