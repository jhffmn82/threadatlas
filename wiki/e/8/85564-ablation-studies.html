ablation studies in LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zirui Guo, Lianghao Xia, Yanhua Yu, Tu Ao, Chao Huang · 2025

Ablation studies in LightRAG evaluate dual-level retrieval and graph-based text indexing by removing high-level retrieval, low-level retrieval, and original text; full dual-level retrieval is balanced, while graph indexing can preserve or improve results without original text.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Evaluation / Experimental Settings / RAG Performance Comparison (RQ1) .. Case Study

The ablation studies examine the effects of removing high-level retrieval, removing low-level retrieval, and removing original text from LightRAG. They find that the full dual-level configuration offers balanced performance, while graph-based indexing can preserve or sometimes improve results without the original text.
