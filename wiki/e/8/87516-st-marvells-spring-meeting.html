Abstract written by a model

The races at St. Marvells are scheduled for the following day and are a source of anxiety for Augustin because of Georgiana's racing interests. Georgiana and Sir Tristram discuss Dandy Dick's chances in connection with them. The Races are attended by Georgiana, Salome, and Sheba, with Georgiana winning fifteen hundred pounds over the Handicap and the girls winning fifty pounds. Dandy Dick wins, while Bonny Betsy loses.
