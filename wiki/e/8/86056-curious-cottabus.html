Curious Cottabus

Also called: Cottabus, a Cottabus, curious beast, queer creature, the Cottabus, the Curious Cottabus, the curious one, the foolish creature

Abstract written by a model

The Curious Cottabus is a large, luxurious, cat-shaped curious beast with bulging eyes and a fan-ended tail, as large as a pony. Living on other people’s affairs, it requires fifty questions daily and questions Pompadore and Kabumpo before helping. It follows them to Rith Metic, offers a cryptic chant, falls from a fence, then corrects “wintersault” to “summersault,” accidentally providing their escape clue.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The Curious Cottabus is a large, cat-shaped creature with bulging eyes and a fan-ended tail that lives on other people’s affairs. It questions Pompadore and Kabumpo about their identities, plans, and history, follows them after they leave, and eventually falls behind when Kabumpo runs away.

Chapter 5

The Curious Cottabus follows the travelers to Rith Metic and asks Pompadore a series of questions before offering a cryptic chant about turning a somersault. It falls from the fence, then reappears to correct “wintersault” to “summersault,” unintentionally giving Pompadore the clue that helps them escape.
