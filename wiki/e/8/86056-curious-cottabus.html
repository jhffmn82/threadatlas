Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The Curious Cottabus is a large, cat-shaped creature with bulging eyes and a fan-ended tail that lives on other people’s affairs. It questions Pompadore and Kabumpo about their identities, plans, and history, follows them after they leave, and eventually falls behind when Kabumpo runs away.

Chapter 5

The Curious Cottabus follows the travelers to Rith Metic and asks Pompadore a series of questions before offering a cryptic chant about turning a somersault. It falls from the fence, then reappears to correct “wintersault” to “summersault,” unintentionally giving Pompadore the clue that helps them escape.
