Philocles in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Philocles, an Athenian general and lord-chancellor, jointly commanded the army and fleet with Conon, led thirty ships at Aegos Potamos, was defeated by Lysander, captured, and executed at Lampsacus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XV.

Philocles was made general of the Athenian army and commanded the fleet jointly with Conon. At Aegos Potamos, he led thirty ships out of the harbour, was put to flight by Lysander, and was later captured and executed at Lampsacus.
