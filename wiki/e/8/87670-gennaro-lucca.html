Gennaro Lucca in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Gennaro Lucca, Emilia Lucca’s husband and a former Red Circle member, joined in Naples but sought escape after moving to New York. Ordered to bomb Tito Castalotte, he warned him instead. In London, he killed Giuseppe Gorgiano in self-defense and to protect Emilia.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART II

Gennaro Lucca joined the Red Circle in Naples but later tried to escape its control after moving to New York with Emilia. He was ordered to kill Tito Castalotte by bombing his house, warned Castalotte instead, and apparently killed Giuseppe Gorgiano when Gorgiano found him in London.
