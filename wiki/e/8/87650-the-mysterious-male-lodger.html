Abstract written by a model

A youngish, dark-bearded foreign lodger rents a room in Mrs. Warren’s house, pays for privacy, and keeps his identity concealed. Smartly dressed and carrying only a brown bag, he enters and leaves with a key. Unable to communicate directly with the concealed woman, he uses printed supply requests and newspaper signals, ultimately sending her an urgent warning.

The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

The mysterious male lodger is a youngish, dark, bearded, well-dressed foreigner who pays for a room and insists on complete seclusion. He leaves brief printed requests, apparently communicates through newspaper advertisements and signals, and sends an urgent warning to the woman concealed in the lodging-house.
