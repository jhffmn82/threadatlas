The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

The mysterious male lodger is a youngish, dark, bearded, well-dressed foreigner who pays for a room and insists on complete seclusion. He leaves brief printed requests, apparently communicates through newspaper advertisements and signals, and sends an urgent warning to the woman concealed in the lodging-house.
