Mrs. Hudson in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mrs. Hudson is Sherlock Holmes's long-suffering landlady, fond of and awed by him for his gentleness and courtesy. Alarmed by his apparent worsening illness, she conveys it to Dr. John Watson and waits trembling and weeping.

The Adventure of the Dying Detective

Mrs. Hudson is Sherlock Holmes's long-suffering landlady, deeply awed by him but also fond of him because of his gentleness and courtesy toward women. Alarmed by his worsening condition, she summons Dr. Watson despite Holmes's refusal to see a doctor and waits trembling and weeping when Watson leaves.
