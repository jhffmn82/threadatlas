Abstract written by a model

Lambulus, learned from youth, became a merchant after his father’s death. Captured in Arabia and used in the Ethiopian purification, he reached an island, lived there seven years with a fellow-captive, was expelled, reached Greece, and wrote of his adventures and India.

Lambulus is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

Lambulus was a learned man who became a merchant after his father's death. After being captured in Arabia and used in the Ethiopian purification, he reached the island with a fellow-captive, lived there for seven years, and was then expelled. He later reached Greece and wrote an account of his adventures and of India.
