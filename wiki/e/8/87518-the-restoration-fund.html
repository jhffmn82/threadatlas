Abstract written by a model

The Restoration Fund supports Minster Spire restoration. In the second act, it benefits from the Dean’s public thousand-pound offer, prompting seven wealthy donors to give matching sums; the Dean also directs a wager expected to add five hundred pounds toward the Spire.
