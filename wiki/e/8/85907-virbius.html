Virgil · -0028/-0018

Virbius, son of Hippolytus and Aricia, inherits his father’s skill with warrior steeds and rides with Turnus’s troops; his name also belongs to restored Hippolytus, hidden in the Egerian grove by Trivia.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now the mighty labour is begun

Virbius was the name given by Trivia to the hidden and restored Hippolytus in the Egerian grove. His son, also called Virbius, preserves his father’s art and drives warrior steeds; he rides with Turnus’s troops.
