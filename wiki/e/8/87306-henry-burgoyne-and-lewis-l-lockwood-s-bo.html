Abstract written by a model

Henry Burgoyne and Lewis L. Lockwood's boyhood friendship begins at school in Westchester County, deepens as they room together at State college, and continues when they practise law as a firm in Tuckahoe. They share leisure, hunting, shooting, and loyalty, and are described as closer than brothers. The old, deeply valued bond ends when both love the same woman and each believes the other disloyal. Despite a long separation, the friendship survives, and Miss Catherwaight's intervention moves them toward reconciliation.
