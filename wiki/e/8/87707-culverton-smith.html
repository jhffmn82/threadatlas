Culverton Smith in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Culverton Smith is a well-known Sumatra planter visiting London, an amateur and specialist in tropical and Eastern diseases. After an outbreak on his plantation, he studied the illness intensely, made it his dearest hobby, and maintained gelatine cultures of disease organisms. Vindictive and resentful of Sherlock Holmes for suspecting foul play in his nephew Victor Savage’s death, Smith claims to study microbes, admits causing Savage’s death, and admits sending Holmes an ivory box intended to kill him. Inspector Morton arrests him for Savage’s death.

The Adventure of the Dying Detective

Culverton Smith is a well-known resident of Sumatra who is visiting London and is said to know more about Holmes's disease than anyone else. Holmes explains that Smith studied the disease after an outbreak on his plantation, though Smith bears a grudge against Holmes because Holmes suspected foul play in the death of Smith's nephew.
