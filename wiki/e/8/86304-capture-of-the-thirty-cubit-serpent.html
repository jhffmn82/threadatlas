Abstract written by a model

The capture of a thirty-cubit serpent began after huntsmen found it in standing pools. Two men died in a direct attempt; hunters then blocked its den, used a bulrush net, and drove it in with men, horsemen, dogs, weapons, and trumpets to bring it to Alexandria.

The capture began when huntsmen found a thirty-cubit serpent lying in standing pools. After two men were killed during a direct attempt, the remaining hunters blocked its den, dug a second hole, placed a bulrush net over it, and drove the serpent into the net with armed men, horsemen, dogs, darts, slingers, and trumpets.
