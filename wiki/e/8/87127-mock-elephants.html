mock elephants in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Three hundred thousand mock elephants were made from black oxen skins and straw, each carrying men and a camel. They initially deceived Indian scouts and frightened horses from afar, but real Indian elephants exposed and destroyed them.

Semiramis had three hundred thousand black oxen slaughtered so that their skins could be stitched and stuffed to resemble elephants, with men and camels placed inside. The mock elephants initially deceived the Indian scouts and frightened the Indian horses at a distance, but they were exposed and destroyed by the real Indian elephants.
