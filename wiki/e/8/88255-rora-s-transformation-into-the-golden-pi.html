Abstract written by a model

Rora's transformation into the Golden Pig is Queen Coo-ee-oh's enchanted punishment after Rora, a witch, tries to poison the Lake of the Skeezers. Queen Coo-ee-oh changes her into a Golden Pig and removes her four cans of brains, leaving her unable to remember or use magic; Ozma offers partial restoration.

Rora's transformation into the Golden Pig is in one work on this site, Glinda of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Six

Rora's transformation occurred when she tried to dump magic poison into the Lake of the Skeezers. Queen Coo-ee-oh transformed her into a Golden Pig and removed her four cans of brains; Ozma offers to restore Rora's human form but not all four cans.

Chapter Eleven

Rora was transformed into a Golden Pig by Queen Coo-ee-oh before this unit. The Su-dic says that Rora's transformation avenged his wife but also left her unable to remember or perform her magic.
