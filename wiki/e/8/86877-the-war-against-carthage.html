the war against Carthage

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: the Carthaginian war, the common danger, the fight, the present war, the war

Abstract written by a model

The war against Carthage brought fear, accusations, and military preparations to Syracuse. Dionysius used the threat to recall exiles, raise soldiers’ pay, and gain sole command; despite calls to remove him, he attacked a plague-weakened Carthaginian army, took forts, and destroyed much of its fleet.
