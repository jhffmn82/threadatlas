Abstract written by a model

A small black-and-white ivory box with a sliding lid sits on Holmes's mantelpiece. Sent by Culverton Smith, it conceals a poisoned sharp spring that draws blood when opened. Holmes warns Watson not to touch it and uses sugar-tongs; Smith removes it as supposed evidence, but Inspector Morton recovers it.
