Abstract written by a model

Amphinome, Pelias’s daughter, Andrtemon’s wife, and Promachus’s mother, is sought for slaughter; she runs to the king’s palace, curses Pelias for impiety, then stabs herself to death.

Amphinome is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
