Hatcham in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Hatcham is a groom and stable man. He watches Dandy Dick overnight, carries Dandy’s dose, and reports that the villain who burned the Swan Inn was caught administering it and taken by constable to the lock-up.
