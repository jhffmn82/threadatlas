The Sicilians, soldiers in Dionysius's garrison and army, fiercely assaulted Motya, suffered heavy losses, then entered, killed inhabitants and plundered wealth despite Dionysius's order to spare prisoners.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

The Sicilians fought fiercely against the defenders of Motya and suffered heavy losses during the assaults. After entering the city, they killed inhabitants without regard to age or sex and plundered its wealth despite Dionysius's order to spare prisoners.
