Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VI

Misenus, the son of the God of Winds and a renowned warrior-trumpeter, is drowned after provoking the sea gods with his martial trumpet. His unburied body is found on the shore, and Aeneas orders funeral rites and a tomb for him.
