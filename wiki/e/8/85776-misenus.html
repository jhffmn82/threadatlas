Abstract written by a model

Misenus, a renowned warrior-trumpeter and son of the God of Winds, serves Hector, then Aeneas after Hector’s death. He provokes sea gods with his martial trumpet, is drowned by Triton, and receives funeral rites and a shore tomb ordered by Aeneas.

Misenus is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
