Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The courtiers attend Prince Pompadore's birthday feast and listen to fifteen speeches before celebrating with the cake. The cake explosion throws many of them under the table or knocks them unconscious, and they later crawl out and anxiously listen as the scroll is read.
