The building's dark, narrow interior has stairways, a roof ladder, and a small room with bedding, scant food, and whiskey. It offers little shelter: Rags runs upstairs, falls over a bucket, retreats from the roof, and hides from police in a room.

" MY DISREPUTABLE FRIEND, MR.

The building's interior is dark, narrow, and largely silent, with short turns, stairways, and rooms that provide little shelter. Rags runs up the stairs, falls over a bucket, retreats from the roof, and finally enters a room where he hides from the police.
