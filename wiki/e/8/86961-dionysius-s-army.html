Dionysius's army

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Dionysius's whole army, his army, the army

Abstract written by a model

A Syracusan army used six-floored siege towers and rams, advanced at night through Motya's breach and buildings, then captured the city amid killing and plunder.

Dionysius's army is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

Dionysius's army fought through Motya's breach and over the city's buildings after a nighttime advance secured a passage. It eventually broke into and captured the city, where many soldiers killed and plundered the inhabitants.
