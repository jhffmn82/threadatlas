Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Glegg’s Box of Mixed Magic is a small gold, gem-studded magic box, originally belonging to Glegg and hidden beneath a rock in a cavern floor. Recorded as discovered in the Book of Records, it is found by Ruggedo beneath Ozma’s palace after he notices a ring in a rock. He takes it intending to use its mixed magic against the Emerald City of Oz, and opens it by throwing it against a rock wall. The box holds magical devices and supplies: Flying Fluid, Vanishing Cream in a bottle, Glegg’s Instantaneous Expanding Extract, Spike’s Hair Strengthener, Triple Trick Tea with a tiny kettle and lamp, Re-animating Rays, and Glegg’s Question Box. The Question Box answers Ev’s question about Ruggedo’s location. Its unexplained magic brings Peg Amy to life, makes Peg Amy and Wag grow, and, according to Wag, makes Ozma’s castle disappear. After the cave is opened, Peg Amy finds the box, carries it in her pocket, and closes it with its magic catch, but cannot reopen it. She and Wag keep it from Kabumpo, hoping Ruggedo can open it and undo the damage. Pompa later loses it when he falls from the Runaway Country; Peg Amy finds it open. During Wag’s attack on Ruggedo, its contents are scattered and used in the attempt to rescue Ozma. Finally, Ozma takes possession of the box and plans to remove Glegg’s powers; the Expanding Extract is gone because Ruggedo used the whole bottle on himself.

Mixed Magic in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Mixed Magic is a box-contained collection of powerful substances and devices, including Expanding Fluid, Vanishing Cream, Re-animating Rays, and the Question Box. Glegg buried it beneath Ozma’s palace for two years while awaiting Peg’s disenchantment. It is suspected after the palace disappears; Glinda investigates it as a clue. Its effects make Peg Amy alive and enlarge Peg Amy and Wag, make Ruggedo a giant and cause his final expansion ending in an explosion, and apparently vanish the castle with its towers and turrets. The travelers cannot explain how these effects occurred. After Glegg’s punishment, it is stored in Ozma’s gold safe.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Magic is suspected in the palace’s disappearance, and Glinda considers Ruggedo and Glegg’s box of Mixed Magic possible clues. Glinda spends the night consulting books of magic while seeking a way to rescue the missing people.

Chapter 9

Mixed Magic includes several powerful substances and devices, including Expanding Fluid, Vanishing Cream, Re-animating Rays, and the Question Box. Its use enlarges Peg Amy, enlarges Wag, revives Peg Amy, and causes Ruggedo to grow into a giant.

Chapter 10

Mixed Magic causes Ruggedo’s final expansion and leaves Peg Amy and Wag much larger than before. It also appears to have made the castle vanish, so Peg Amy concludes that it must somehow be used to make things right again.

Chapter 13

Mixed Magic is responsible for Peg Amy becoming alive, Peg Amy and Wag growing large, and Ozma’s castle disappearing. The travelers do not understand exactly how the effects occurred.

Chapter 22

Glegg buried his Mixed Magic under Ozma’s palace and went without it for two years while waiting for Peg to be disenchanted. After Glegg’s punishment, the box of Mixed Magic is placed in Ozma’s gold safe.
