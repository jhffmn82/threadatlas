Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

Princess Faleero, a thousand-year-old, unattractive royal fairy princess, lives in a small Follensby forest hut near Pumperdink and gathers faggots. She gladly accepts Prince Pompadore and agrees to marry him the next morning.
