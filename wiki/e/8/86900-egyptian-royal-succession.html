Egyptian royal succession in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Egyptian royal succession is a priestly temple-register account listing successive kings, their qualities and deeds, totaling Egyptian, Ethiopian, Persian, and Macedonian rulers and continuing to Amasis.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK I.

Egyptian priests were said to preserve temple registers listing the kings successively across many generations, including their greatness, dispositions, and actions. The account gives totals of Egyptian, Ethiopian, Persian, and Macedonian rulers and continues the succession down to Amasis.
