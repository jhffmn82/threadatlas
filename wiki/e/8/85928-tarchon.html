Tarchon

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Tuscan chief, fiery Tarchon, strong Tarchon

Abstract written by a model

Tarchon is a Tuscan commander and leader. He allies his army with Aeneas and the Trojans, directs their landing but loses his own ship when it grounds and breaks apart. Sent by Jupiter to restore the abandoned battle line, he rallies retreating Tuscan troops, captures Venulus, and helps turn them against the Latins.

Tarchon is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Tarchon signs an agreement with Aeneas and joins the Trojan cause with the Tuscan army. During the landing, he selects a smooth part of the coast and orders the ships to run ashore, but his own ship is stranded, breaks apart, and throws his soldiers into the sea.
