The collection of dishonored honors is Miss Catherwaight's unusual hobby and consists of awards whose recipients have parted with them. She displays the objects to dinner guests and explains the circumstances of their sale or pawning, treating the contrast between public honor and private dishonor as the collection's chief interest. The silver heart medal prompts her to question whether every object should be treated as a collectible.
