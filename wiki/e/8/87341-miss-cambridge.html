Miss Cambridge in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Miss Cambridge is a very young, very pretty lady who elopes with young Standish. Frightened because they lack a clergyman, witnesses, and parish registry, she relies on Van Bibber to arrange a formal wedding. Though she weeps on the journey, she smiles happily as she walks up the aisle.

" VAN BIBBER AS BEST MAN Young Van Bibber came up to town in June from Newport to see his lawyer about the preparation of some papers that needed his signature.

Miss Cambridge has eloped with young Standish but is frightened because they lack a clergyman, witnesses, and a parish registry. She agrees to rely on Van Bibber, who arranges a formal wedding for them. She weeps during the journey to the church but is smiling and happy when she walks up the aisle.
