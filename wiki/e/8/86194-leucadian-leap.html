Leucadian leap

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Lover's-leap, cast himself down, desiluisse, her leap, leap
  • In Heroides and Amores, the Leucadian leap is presented as a possible cure for Sappho's unrequited love.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Lover's-leap is the name given to the leap from Leucadia, believed to cure love if survived.

Leucadian leap in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

is_a leap from the Leucadian cliff [a remedy for unrequited love] The Leucadian leap is presented as a possible cure for Sappho's unrequited love. Sappho resolves to make the leap, asks the breeze and Love to support her, and intends afterward to dedicate her lyre to Phoebus.
