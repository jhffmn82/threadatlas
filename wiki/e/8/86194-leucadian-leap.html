Leucadian leap

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Lover's-leap, cast himself down, desiluisse, her leap, leap

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the Leucadian leap is presented as a possible cure for Sappho's unrequited love. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Lover's-leap is the name given to the leap from Leucadia, believed to cure love if survived.
