Sheba

Abstract written by a model

Sheba Jedd is a seventeen-year-old young lady, daughter of Augustin Jedd, niece of Georgiana Tidman, sister of Salome Jedd, and a clergyman’s wife in training. She is engaged to Mr. Darbey and attracted to Nugent Darbey. With Salome, she has little money, owes forty pounds, nineteen shillings, and shares a spirit of wickedness and rebellion. Distressed by the cost of a flower-girl costume, she plans to attend a masked ball with Darbey, seeks money from Dr. Jedd, and initially resents Georgiana’s arrival before quickly liking her. The sisters accept Georgiana’s betting plan to win money through Dandy Dick, secretly leave the Deanery, and attend the Durnstone ball in scandalous costumes. Though the ball is cancelled, they win fifty pounds at the Races but face the costume bill. Sheba helps protect Darbey, accepts his proposal after hesitation, and finally confronts the Dean over his deception and intended marriage.

DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

Sheba Jedd plans to attend the Fancy Dress Masked Ball with Nugent Darbey and is distressed by the cost of her flower-girl costume. She joins Salome in asking Dr. Jedd for money and reacts angrily to the news that Georgiana Tidman will come to live with them.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

Sheba quickly takes a liking to Georgiana and describes herself and Salome as 'weary fragments' who are everything except chastened widows. She reveals that the girls owe forty pounds, nineteen shillings and asks Georgiana to help them win the money through Dandy Dick. She joins Salome in accepting Georgiana's betting plan.

THE SECOND ACT.

Sheba plans with Salome to leave the Deanery secretly through the front door and return through a window. She accompanies Mr. Darbey's violin playing, worries that the Dean is in the way, and helps send the evening's guests away.

Sheba is angry that the ball was cancelled after she and Salome had endured the inconvenience of being wicked and rebellious. She joins Salome in confronting Darbey and Tarver and clings to Darbey's legs to prevent Sir Tristram from harming them.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

Sheba accepts Mr. Darbey after briefly asking for time to consider his proposal. She and Salome are revealed to have attended the masked ball at Durnstone, won fifty pounds at the Races, and incurred the £40 19s costume bill. She later confronts the Dean about his deception and proposed marriage.
