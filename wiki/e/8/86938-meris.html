Meris

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Meris became king of Egypt after twelve generations. He built a magnificent northward portico at Memphis, a dyke and a lake bearing his name, with a sepulchre in its middle; its trench and sluices stored excess Nile water for fields. He gave its fishing revenue to his queen for dresses.
