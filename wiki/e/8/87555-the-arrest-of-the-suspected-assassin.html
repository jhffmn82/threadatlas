2. The Tiger of San Pedro

The arrest of the suspected assassin is reported in the newspaper as a supposed solution to the Oxshott murder. Inspector Baynes's men capture a huge, savage man after an ambush near Wisteria Lodge, although Holmes doubts that the arrest identifies the true culprit.
