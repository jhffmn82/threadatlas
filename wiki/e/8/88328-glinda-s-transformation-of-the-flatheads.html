Glinda's transformation of the Flatheads in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Glinda transforms each Flathead by placing that person's brain on the flat head and growing a round head over it, making them intelligent and good looking and ending the Flatheads as a separate people, who become Mountaineers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Four

Glinda transforms each Flathead by spreading that person's brain on the flat head and causing the head to grow over the brains in a round shape. The transformation makes the people intelligent and good looking, prevents anyone from being deprived of the brains belonging to him, and ends the existence of the Flatheads as a separate people.
