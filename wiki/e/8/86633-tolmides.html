Tolmides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Tolmides was an Athenian admiral and general. After persuading Athens to invade Laconia, he secretly enlarged his force, captured Methone, Gytheum, Zacynthus, and Naupactus, established a colony there, and continued in Boeotia and Euboea. He captured Chaeronea but was surprised by Boeotians and killed fighting.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XXIV.

Tolmides, an Athenian admiral, persuaded Athens to authorize an invasion of Laconia and secretly recruited more than the proposed thousand men. He sailed with fifty ships and four thousand soldiers, took Methone and Gytheum, destroyed Spartan shipping and naval stores, subdued Cephalenia, captured Naupactus, and later continued operations in Boeotia and Euboea.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

Tolmides, the Athenian general, captured Chaeronea. The Boeotians then surprised his forces, and Tolmides was killed while fighting bravely.
