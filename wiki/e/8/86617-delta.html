Abstract written by a model

The Delta, formed where the Nile divides into several channels in Egypt, resembles the Greek letter and has seven mouths opening to the sea. Enriched and watered by the Nile, its dikes, sluices, fertile soil, and crops make it Egypt’s most pleasant part.

Delta is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. III.

The Delta is formed where the Nile divides into several channels in Egypt and is named for its resemblance to the Greek letter. Its seven mouths empty into the ocean, and its dikes, sluices, fertile soil, and abundant crops make it the sweetest and most pleasant part of Egypt.
