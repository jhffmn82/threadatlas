Arabian Gulf in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Arabian gulf empties into the Southern ocean, bounded at its outlet by Arabia and Troglodyta; its route includes Serpent's Island, while its shores, islands, currents, harbors, shallows, and peoples are described.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

The Arabian gulf is said to empty into the Southern ocean and to be bounded by Arabia and Troglodyta. Its shores, islands, currents, harbors, dangerous shallows, and coastal peoples are described, including the Troglodytes and Ichthyophages.
