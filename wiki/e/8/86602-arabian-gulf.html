Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

The Arabian gulf is said to empty into the Southern ocean and to be bounded by Arabia and Troglodyta. Its shores, islands, currents, harbors, dangerous shallows, and coastal peoples are described, including the Troglodytes and Ichthyophages.
