Hippodamia

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Hippodamia was the daughter of Bystus and wife of Perithous, whose marriage feast became the occasion for the Centaurs’ attempted assault on the women present. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hippodamia was the bride of Pirithoüs targeted by Eurytus or Eurytion at her wedding, provoking the Centaur-Lapith conflict; one interpretation calls her a Centauress.
