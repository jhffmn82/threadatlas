EXPLANATION.

Hippodamia was the bride of Pirithoüs. The attempt of Eurytus, or Eurytion, on her at the wedding provoked the conflict between the Centaurs and the Lapithæ; one interpretation also proposes that she was a Centauress who married the prince of the Lapithæ.
