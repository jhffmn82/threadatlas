amber in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Amber is gathered on Basilea, cast up there by the sea, and transported by its inhabitants to Europe before reaching the regions described. Its account rejects the tale of Phaeton's sisters' tears and favors faithful historians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

For over against the French shore^ opposite to the Hercynian

Amber is described as being cast up by the working of the sea on the island of Basilea and as being transported by the inhabitants to the opposite continent before being brought to the regions of the text. The unit rejects the story that amber comes from the tears of Phaeton's sisters and instead gives precedence to the accounts of faithful historians.
