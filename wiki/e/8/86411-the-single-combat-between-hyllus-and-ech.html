Abstract written by a model

is_between Hyllus Hyllus challenged an enemy champion on the condition that victory would give the Heraclidae the kingdom of Eurystheus, while his own death would bar the Heraclidae from Peloponnesus for fifty years. Echemus, king of the Tegeans, accepted the challenge and killed Hyllus.
