India is of a quadrangular form, one side lying towards the east^

Indian philosophers are the least numerous but most esteemed social rank and are free from public offices. They perform sacrifices and funerals, advise the people through predictions about natural events, and lose the right to speak publicly if their predictions prove wrong.
