Monte Carlo

Abstract written by a model

Monte Carlo is a place containing a Casino and gambling at its tables. Harringford comes from Paris intending to die there, choosing it because it differs from home and because he recalls a young Russian who killed himself after losing at the tables; he waits outside the Casino late at night. During a marriage tour, a woman and Louis are there, though she says gambling tables are not for people like them and they must return to Paris next day. A Frenchman gambles there until Cecil urges him and his wife to leave that night, having seen arrivals rich depart paupers.

Monte Carlo is in one work on this site, Gallegher and Other Stories. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
