Abstract written by a model

Polyzelus, Hiero’s brother, refuses an army command he suspects was meant to kill him, flees to Theron, and is restored to Hiero’s favor after the Himeraean plot is exposed.

CHAP. VII.

Polyzelus suspected that Hiero had given him command of an army so that he would die in battle and therefore refused the command. He fled to Theron, and after the Himeraean plot was exposed Theron restored him to his brother's former love and favor.
