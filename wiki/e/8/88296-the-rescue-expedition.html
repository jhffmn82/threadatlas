the Rescue Expedition

event · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Rescue Expedition, expedition, expedition to the Skeezer country, go to the Skeezer country, leave the Emerald City, party, rescue expedition, rescue party

Abstract written by a model

The Rescue Expedition is an undertaking for Ozma, led by Glinda the Good with the Wizard of Oz and including the Cowardly Lion and assembled counsellors. It leaves the Emerald City at sunrise on foot, travels through the Winkie Country toward Oogaboo and into the Great Gillikin Forest, and is delayed when Button Bright disappears. After reaching the Lake of the Skeezers, it investigates the submerged island, restores the three Adepts with Ervic's help, and reaches the imprisoned Skeezers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

The expedition includes the assembled counsellors, with Glinda and the Wizard leading it. It will leave the Emerald City at sunrise, and because Glinda's stork chariot is being sent back to her castle, the group will travel together on foot.

Chapter Fifteen

The rescue expedition leaves the Emerald City amid the people's wishes for its success and safe return. It travels through the Winkie Country toward Oogaboo, then turns into the Great Gillikin Forest, where it is delayed by Button Bright's disappearance.

Chapter Seventeen

The Rescue Expedition reaches the shore of the Lake of the Skeezers after passing through the Great Forest. Its members investigate the submerged island and seek a way to rescue the prisoners.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Rescue Expedition is the undertaking whose events Glinda recounts to Ozma and the others. Its restoration of the three Adepts was accomplished with Ervic's help, and its members have now reached the imprisoned Skeezers.
