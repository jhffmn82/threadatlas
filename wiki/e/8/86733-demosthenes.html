Demosthenes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Demosthenes was an Athenian general and admiral who commanded forces and fleets. In Acarnania he defeated Ambracian troops, though the Acarnanians would not join an assault on Ambracia. He fortified and defended Pylos against the Lacedaemonians and joined Hippocrates in an unsuccessful effort at Megara. Expected with a fleet and reinforcements, he later arrived in Sicily with Eurymedon, urged attack at Epipolae, then advocated retreat from Syracuse. After a naval defeat he proposed breaking the harbor blockade, led a division in retreat, and was captured.

He later fortified Pylos and defended it against the Lacedaemonians, and he also participated with Hippocrates in an unsuccessful attempt to secure Megara through treachery.

Demosthenes was expected to arrive within a few days with a fleet and additional forces. The Athenians resolved to attempt nothing further until his forces arrived.

Demosthenes arrived in Sicily with Eurymedon and substantial reinforcements. He persuaded his colleagues to attack Epipolae, later urged an immediate return to Athens, and after the naval defeat proposed manning more ships to force the harbor blockade; he was captured during the retreat.
