The colony of the Thespiadae in Sardinia, established under Iolaus, defeated inhabitants and gained fertile Iolaion; it prospered through cultivation, mixed with barbarians, and eventually lived in inaccessible mountains.

The colony of the Thespiadae in Sardinia was established under Iolaus after the settlers defeated the inhabitants and received the most fertile part of the island by lot. The colony became prosperous through cultivation, later mixed with barbarians, and eventually lived in inaccessible mountain places.
