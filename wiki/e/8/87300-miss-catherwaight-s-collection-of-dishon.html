THE CYNICAL MISS CATHERWAIGHT Miss Catherwaight's collection of orders and decorations and medals was her chief offence in the eyes of those of her dear friends who thought her clever but cynical.

Miss Catherwaight's collection contains more than a hundred medals and decorations purchased during travels with her father. Each was awarded for public service, virtue, bravery, statesmanship, or artistic genius and was later pawned or sold by its recipient. She calls the collection dishonored honors, the Orders of the Knights of the Almighty Dollar, pledges to patriotism and the pawnshops, and honors at second-hand.
