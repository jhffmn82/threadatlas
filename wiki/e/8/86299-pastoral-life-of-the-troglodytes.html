pastoral life of the Troglodytes

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: lives of beasts under the shapes of men, pastoral lives

Abstract written by a model

The pastoral life of the Troglodytes consists of moving between pastures with their flocks and living chiefly on cattle products and meat. During rainy periods they consume boiled milk mixed with blood, and when pastures are burned by the sun they move to low marshes and sometimes fight for suitable grazing.
