Abstract written by a model

A funerary judgment before the coffin boards a ship permits accusations of evil life; proven charges deny ordinary burial, while false or malicious accusers face severe punishment.

CHAP, VII.

Before the coffin is put aboard the ship, anyone may accuse the deceased of having lived an evil life. A proven accusation prevents ordinary burial, while a false or malicious accusation brings severe punishment upon the informer.
