Tsing Tsing in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Tsing Tsing, Silver Fisher’s daughter, was Emperor Chang Wang Woe’s wife and Empress of the Silver Islands. When he became a fading crocus, her tears kept it fresh three days. She died, returning to silver ancestors; after Scarecrow rescued her, she married him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

Tsing Tsing was the beautiful wife of Chang Wang Woe and the Empress of the Silver Islands. After he was transformed into a fading crocus, she planted it in a silver bowl and kept it fresh for three days with her tears; she has since died and is said to have returned to her silver ancestors.
