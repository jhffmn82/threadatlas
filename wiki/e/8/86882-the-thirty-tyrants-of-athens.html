Abstract written by a model

The Thirty Tyrants were an oligarchic government, nominally magistrates but actually usurping tyrants, appointed after pressure to abolish democracy. They staffed offices with supporters, sought a Lacedaemonian garrison, banished and executed Athenians, and confiscated property, until Thrasybulus's forces expelled them and a decemvirate replaced them.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIV.

The Thirty Tyrants were appointed to govern Athens after Lysander pressured the Athenians to abolish the democracy. They filled offices with their supporters, summoned a Lacedaemonian garrison, executed wealthy citizens and other prominent men, and confiscated their property.

CHAP. VI.

The Thirty Tyrants continued banishing and executing Athenians and used foreign soldiers to control Athens. They were defeated by Thrasybulus's forces, expelled from the city, and replaced by a decemvirate.
