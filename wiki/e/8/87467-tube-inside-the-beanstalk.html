tube (inside the beanstalk) in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

A narrow, deep, black, chimney-like passage or tube links the Scarecrow’s fall downward with Dorothy’s party’s later upward flight. A board briefly stops the Scarecrow for questioning, then opens; later the party ascends for almost an hour, scraping its rough sides.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

The passageway is a narrow, dark route through which the Scarecrow falls. A board briefly blocks it so the King of the Middlings and Muddle can question him, and then it is opened so he can continue downward.

CHAPTER 22

The deep, black passage is the dark, chimney-like tube down which the Scarecrow had fallen. Dorothy's party flies upward through it for almost an hour, scraping against its rough sides.
