Abstract written by a model

A pretty, rather short, plainly dressed French bourgeois woman is newly married to Louis and has no friends in Monte Carlo. Though she has dowry money for part of Carbut's bakery store, Louis's roulette winnings are crucial to paying their debt and creating the independent little shop she wants. She begs the Goodwood Plunger, Cecil, to stop Louis from gambling further. After receiving the winnings, she and Louis go to Paris, own a shop, and she gratefully credits Cecil with helping secure their happier future.

As he turned, a woman came forward from out the lighted hall, hovered uncertainl

The woman is French, short, plainly dressed, and newly married to Louis. She explains that Louis has won a substantial amount at gambling but will not stop, and she begs the Goodwood Plunger to warn him because their future depends on the money.

" The woman ran ahead, with a murmur of gratitude, to the open door and pointed

The woman asks Cecil to stop her husband because he is risking money that could relieve their debt and support their home. She receives the winnings, travels with her husband to Paris, and thanks Cecil for helping them establish a happier life.
