RAG-HAT

object · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Hallucination Aware Fine-Tuning pipeline, RAG-HAT (full)

Abstract written by a model

RAG-HAT is a Hallucination Aware Fine-Tuning pipeline and hallucination mitigation method for Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems. It combines hallucination detection and mitigation without additional human annotations, using a detection model, GPT-4 Turbo, and Direct Preference Optimization. The pipeline unfolds through three parts: detection, rewriting, and mitigation. It is evaluated on RAGTruth and WebGLM, where it lowers average hallucination rates by 26.9% and 26.8%, respectively, while GPT-4 Turbo reports answer-quality win rates of 57.3% and 58.5%. It outperforms naive regeneration, reduces misinformation risk without compromising answer quality, and is integrated into NewsBreak's RAG experiments for more accurate and relevant local information.

RAG-HAT is in one work on this site, RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Introduction

RAG-HAT is a Hallucination Aware Fine-Tuning pipeline designed to combine hallucination detection and mitigation. It uses a detection model, GPT-4 Turbo, and Direct Preference Optimization to reduce hallucinations and improve response quality.

Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

RAG-HAT is evaluated for hallucination suppression and answer quality on RAGTruth and WebGLM. It lowers the average hallucination rate by 26.9% on RAGTruth and 26.8% on WebGLM, and achieves GPT-4 Turbo win rates of 57.3% on RAGTruth and 58.5% on WebGLM.

Analysis / Cautious Penalization (OCP) .. Industry Application

RAG-HAT is integrated into NewsBreak's RAG experiments to improve the accuracy and relevance of local information. Its detection, rewriting, and mitigation components reduce hallucinations and support higher-quality answers while mitigating legal and reputational risks.
