Ethiopian Hylogones in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ethiopian Hylogones are a few hunters who sleep in trees to avoid wild beasts, ambush animals at water pools, kill them with clubs, stones, and darts, and train boys in archery through withheld food.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

The Ethiopian Hylogones are few in number and live by hunting. They sleep in trees, ambush thirsty wild beasts at pools, kill them with clubs, stones, and darts, and teach boys archery by withholding food when they miss their targets.
