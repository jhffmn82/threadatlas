fleet transformed into sea nymphs

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Exalted each a nymph, Nereids, change their fading forms, chang’d the ships to daughters of the sea, fleet having received life in the form of sea Nymphs, like dolphins, ships transformed into nymphs, they plunge their prows, transformation of Aeneas's ships into sea nymphs, wood being changed into flesh
  • In The Aeneid, it is the event in which the Trojan ships, attacked with fire, break their hawsers and change into beautiful nymphs before diving into the sea and swimming away.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the event in which Cybele causes the fleet’s wood to change into flesh, with its parts becoming sea Nymphs that aid ships in danger unless they carry Greeks.
