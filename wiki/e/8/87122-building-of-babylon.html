The building of Babylon is a grand, costly construction project using architects, artists, materials, and two million workers from across the empire. It builds walls, a bridge, embankments, palaces, a river passage, and Jupiter Belus's temple, before much later decays.

The building of Babylon employs architects, artists, necessary materials, and two million men drawn from across the empire. The works include the city wall, bridge, embankments, palaces, a vaulted passage beneath the river, and a temple to Jupiter called Belus; the construction is presented as grand and costly, though much of it later falls into decay.
