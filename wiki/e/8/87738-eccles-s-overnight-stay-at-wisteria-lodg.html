Abstract written by a model

Eccles’s overnight stay at Wisteria Lodge is an invited visit planned by John Scott Eccles for the night of Garcia’s death. He arrives to a tense, strange evening with Aloysius Garcia, sleeps through the night, and wakes to find Garcia and the household gone. The unexplained experience could have provided an alibi and becomes a major mystery for Holmes.

John Scott Eccles arrives at Wisteria Lodge for an invited stay with Aloysius Garcia. The evening is tense and strange, and when Eccles wakes after sleeping through the night, Garcia and the household have disappeared.
