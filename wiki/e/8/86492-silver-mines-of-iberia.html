Abstract written by a model

Silver mines of Iberia contain very good, pure silver, with gold and brass, and are rich. Ore is extracted through deep shafts and galleries; diverted rivers and Egyptian pumps drain workings, generating wealth first for miners, later Italian managers and owners.

Having spoken of the Celts, we shall now give an account of tbck

The silver mines of Iberia were exceptionally rich, with ore found throughout the soil and extracted through deep shafts and underground galleries. Miners diverted underground rivers and used Egyptian pumps, attributed to Archimedes the Syracusan, to drain the workings. The mines generated great wealth first for ordinary miners and later for Italian managers and their owners.
