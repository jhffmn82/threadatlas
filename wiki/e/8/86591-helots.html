Helots

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the Helots
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Helots allied with Messenians against Sparta after an earthquake, raided Laconia from Messenia, and were later enslaved and subjected by Lacedaemonians.
  • In Description of Greece, the Messenian Helots revolted from the Lacedæmonians in the 79th Olympiad and went to Mount Ithome; the Messenians captured at Eira had been forcibly incorporated among the Helots.

Helots in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Helots allied with Messenians against Sparta after an earthquake, raided Laconia from Messenia, and were later enslaved and subjected by Lacedaemonians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIII.

The Helots joined the Messenians in a league against Sparta after the earthquake weakened the city. They later made daily incursions into Laconia from a fort in Messenia.
