Abstract written by a model

Leander's swimming voyage is a perilous attempt to cross the Hellespont to reach Hero. Stormy, dangerous seas hinder him, and Hero imagines his approach, considers meeting him in the water, and urges him to wait for calm conditions so he may arrive safely and embrace her.

XIX

Leander's swimming voyage is the means by which he reaches Hero, but the dangerous sea currently prevents it. Hero imagines him swimming toward her, considers meeting him midway in the water, and asks him to travel safely when the sea is calm.

165 Perhaps you fear the time may fail you for

Swimming across the sea is what Leander desires, but it is presented as a peril that sailors themselves fear. The girl urges him to avoid it when the waters are not calm, though she hopes he will reach her and embrace her with his weary arms.
