Mrs. Warren in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Mrs. Warren, a landlady who owns a lodging-house with her husband, rents its top room to a mysterious man who pays five pounds weekly for privacy. When her husband is abducted after ten days without seeing the lodger, she seeks Holmes’s help and helps watch the door.

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of the Red Circle / PART I

Mrs. Warren reports that her new lodger pays five pounds a week for privacy, leaves printed requests, and has not been seen for ten days. After her husband is abducted, she asks Holmes for help and arranges for him and Dr. Watson to watch the lodger’s door.
