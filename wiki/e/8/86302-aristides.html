Aristides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Aristides is a famous military commander and admiral called “the just” for justice, integrity, moderation, and fair administration. He supported Themistocles’s plan, commanded Athenians at Plataea and against Lesbos exiles, aided Megareans against Persian cavalry, gained Greek cities’ confidence, and established and fairly administered the common fund at Delos.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. HI.

Aristides commanded the Athenians at Plataea. He sent Athenian troops to aid the Megareans against the Persian cavalry, and the relief force killed the Persian cavalry commander and routed his men.

I.

Aristides joined Themistocles's plan as a trusted evaluator and judged it beneficial to the commonwealth. He later served as admiral, gained the confidence of the Greek cities through his justice and moderation, established the common fund at Delos, and administered its contributions fairly.
