Abstract written by a model

A girl from Surrey living near the Harringford family estate is an important object of Young Harringford’s affection. During her illness, he watches her lighted window and longs to meet her again. She openly asks after him despite social judgment, showing concern as he comes to Monte Carlo in a changed state of mind.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

"THERE WERE NINETY AND NINE" Young Harringford, or the "Goodwood Plunger," as he was perhaps better known at that time, had come to Monte Carlo in a very different spirit and in a very different state of mind from any in which he had ever visited the place before.

The girl from Surrey lives near Harringford’s family and has been an important object of his affection. Harringford watched her lighted window when she was ill, longs to meet her again, and learns from his father that she has asked about him without regard for what others might think.
