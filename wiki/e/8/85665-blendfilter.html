BlendFilter

framework · 1 work · 43 quoted facts · 17 connections
Also called: BlendFil- ter, BlendFilter framework, framework, proposed BlendFilter
  • In BlendFilter: Advancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models via Query Generation Blending and Knowledge Filtering: BlendFilter is a novel framework and retrieval-augmented generation approach for retrieval-augmented large language models. Its central contribution is combining query generation blending with knowledge filtering to improve retrieval quality and reduce noise in retrieved knowledge. It has three core components: a Query Generation Blending module, a self-reliant Knowledge Filtering module, and an Answer Generation module. The process begins with the original query and produces externally and internally knowledge-augmented queries. These augmented queries are blended with the original query to retrieve knowledge documents from a knowledge base. The framework independently filters the retrieved knowledge, using the LLM’s intrinsic capabilities and no extra language model to eliminate unrelated or extraneous content. It then takes the union of filtered knowledge and generates a final answer from that knowledge and the original query. Its best results occur when original, externally augmented, and internally augmented queries are all combined. Across HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and StrategyQA, BlendFilter significantly surpasses state-of-the-art baselines across GPT3.5-turbo-Instruct, Vicuna 1.5-13b, and Qwen-7b, and it generalizes to BM25 as a retriever. It outperforms ITER-RETGEN on retrieval performance with ColBERT v2 and BM25, while improving retrieval precision, recall, and S-Precision. Reported results include 0.508 Exact Match and 0.624 F1 on HotPotQA, plus 0.744 Accuracy on StrategyQA, with GPT3.5-turbo-Instruct and ColBERT v2. The framework also introduces hyper-parameter K, which controls the number of documents retrieved and is fixed at a value achieving good performance.

Abstract

BlendFilter is a novel approach that integrates query generation blending with knowledge filtering for retrieval-augmented LLMs. It combines external and internal knowledge augmentation with the original query and uses the LLM’s intrinsic capabilities to eliminate extraneous data. On three open-domain question-answering benchmarks, it significantly surpasses state-of-the-art baselines.

1

BlendFilter is a framework for retrieval-augmented LLMs that integrates query generation blending with knowledge filtering. It uses original and augmented queries, filters retrieved knowledge without an extra language model, and generates an answer from the filtered knowledge and original query; experiments show that it surpasses baselines across three backbones and three open-domain question answering benchmarks.

2

BlendFilter is presented as a framework that combines internal knowledge augmentation, external knowledge augmentation, knowledge filtering, and answer generation. Its process includes generating augmented queries, retrieving and filtering knowledge, taking the union of filtered knowledge, and producing a final answer.

3 .. 3.4

BlendFilter is a framework that improves retrieval quality by combining query generation blending with knowledge filtering. It uses external and internal knowledge augmentation, independently filters the resulting retrieved knowledge, and generates answers from their union.

4 .. 4.8

BlendFilter consistently outperforms the evaluated baselines across HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and StrategyQA with GPT3.5-turbo-Instruct, Vicuna 1.5-13b, and Qwen-7b. It also outperforms the baselines with BM25, improves retrieval precision, recall, and S-Precision through knowledge filtering and query blending, and performs best when its original, externally augmented, and internally augmented queries are combined.

5

BlendFilter is introduced as a comprehensive framework that combines query generation blending and knowledge filtering to improve retrieval-augmented generation. Experiments on three benchmarks show that it outperforms state-of-the-art baselines and generalizes to GPT3.5-turbo-Instruct, Vicuna 1.5-13b, and Qwen-7b.

Limitations

The BlendFilter framework introduces the hyper-parameter K to control how many documents need to be retrieved. The paper fixes K at a value that achieves good performance.
