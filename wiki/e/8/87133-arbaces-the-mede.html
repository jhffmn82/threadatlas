1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IL

Arbaces the Mede was a valiant and prudent general who was persuaded by Belesis to attempt the overthrow of the Assyrian empire. He organized the Medes, Persians, Babylonians, and an Arabian king against Sardanapalus, suffered several defeats and was wounded, but later defeated the Assyrian forces and was proclaimed king after Nineveh fell.
