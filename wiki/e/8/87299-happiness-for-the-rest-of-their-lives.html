happiness for the rest of their lives in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

A promise that a couple will be happy for the rest of their lives is voiced by the Frenchman, echoed by train wheels, linked by Cecil to his father’s letter, and repeated as he sleeps.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" The woman ran ahead, with a murmur of gratitude, to the open door and pointed

The Frenchman tells Cecil that he has made the couple happy for the rest of their lives, and the train's wheels echo those words after their departure. Cecil connects the phrase with his father's letter and later repeats it while falling asleep.
