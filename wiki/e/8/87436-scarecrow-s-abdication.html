Scarecrow's abdication

Abstract written by a model

Scarecrow's proposed abdication is his planned means of returning to Oz. He intends to give a farewell speech, abdicate in favor of his eldest son, keep his body, and leave with the others. Fearing Gheewizard's magic, the group prepares to flee; the Silver Islanders reject the abdication and demand he remain Emperor.

CHAPTER 19

The Scarecrow proposes abdication as a way to leave: he will give a farewell speech and return to Oz with the others. Happy Toko worries that the Gheewizard might use magic before the speech is finished, so the group prepares to make a dash for it if the abdication fails.

CHAPTER 20

The Scarecrow declares that he wants to abdicate in favor of his eldest son while keeping his current body and returning to Oz. The Silver Islanders refuse to accept his abdication and demand that he remain their Emperor.
