King of the Golden Islands in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

King of the Golden Islands rules the Golden Islands, commands an army, and has a sister, Princess Orange Blossom, proposed as a bride. He threatens to conquer, burn, and enslave the Silver Islands, sends a fleet, and leads an invasion, but the little fan drops him into the sea, forcing retreat.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The King of the Golden Islands sends a fleet toward the Silver Islands. The Grand Chew Chew says that the king will set fire to the island and make its people slaves.

CHAPTER 9

The King of the Golden Islands personally leads the invasion of Silver Island. The little fan lifts him into the air with his army, and when the Scarecrow closes it, he falls into the sea before retreating in his ships.
