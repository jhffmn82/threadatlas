Nysa in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Nysa is an island city surrounded by Triton, entered only through the Nysian gates. Fertile, beautiful, and healthful with meadows, gardens, streams, caves, flowers, trees, and birds, it is where Bacchus was raised and instructed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The poet in his hymns confirms what we have before declared^

Nysa is described as an island city surrounded by the river Triton and accessible through the Nysian gates. It is portrayed as a fertile, beautiful, and healthful place of meadows, gardens, orchards, streams, caves, flowers, trees, and birds, where Bacchus was raised and instructed.
