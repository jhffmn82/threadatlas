Abstract written by a model

The Adepts at Magic are three beautiful, stately young women, skilled in magic and originally wise, considerate rulers of the Flatheads. They use magic only for good, practice witchcraft to benefit their people, teach useful arts, and help the Skeezers. Queen Coo-ee-oh betrays them: after stealing their magical instruments, formulas, incantations, and arts, she transforms them into fishes and casts them into a lake. They remain there awaiting her deserved fate and enlist Ervic in a secret plan to regain their natural forms. Restored through Reera and Ervic, they promise secrecy about Reera's part and are expected to help raise the Skeezer island. They later seek Glinda with a young Skeezer, assist Glinda, the Wizard, and their companions in lowering the lake and entering the Great Dome, and explain matters concerning Coo-ee-oh's magic and the Dome's machinery. Finally, they return with Ozma's party to Enchanted Mountain, regain the Flatheads' allegiance, imprison the Su-dic, approve Glinda's plan, and rename their transformed people the Mountaineers, whom they rule by enforcing the laws of the Land of Oz.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The three Adepts in Sorcery were beautiful girls who ruled the Flatheads and used magic only for good. They taught the Flatheads useful arts and helped the Skeezers, but Coo-ee-oh stole their magical instruments and transformed them into three fishes.

Chapter Twelve

The Adept Witches originally practiced good witchcraft intended to help their people, but Coo-ee-oh stole some of their tools. The Adept Witches have been transformed into fishes, and Ozma considers restoring them if they can be captured.

Chapter Sixteen

The three Adepts were betrayed and transformed by Queen Coo-ee-oh into fishes. They have remained in the lake, waited for Coo-ee-oh to suffer the fate she deserved, and enlist Ervic to help them regain their natural forms.

Chapter Nineteen

The three newly transformed Adepts thank Reera and Ervic and explain how they became fishes and secretly planned to induce Reera to restore them. They promise not to return to Reera's cottage or tell anyone what she did, and they are expected to help raise the Skeezer island.

Chapter Twenty

Three visiting maidens of stately presence and remarkable grace approach the camp with a good-looking young Skeezer. The golden-haired maiden identifies the Wizard of Oz and explains that they are seeking Glinda, so the Wizard leads them to her tent.

Chapter Twenty-One

The three Adepts at Magic once ruled the Flatheads before Coo-ee-oh betrayed them, transformed them into fishes, and cast them into the lake. After their release, they explain that Coo-ee-oh used the formulas, incantations, and arts stolen from them, and they consider how to enter the Great Dome.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The three Adepts help Glinda, the Wizard, and the others lower the lake and enter the Dome. The Skeezers recognize and welcome them as friends. They explain that Coo-ee-oh taught them a method of magic involving spoken words and determine that the basement machinery controls the submarines and bridge rather than the island.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The three Adepts at Magic return with Ozma's party to the Flatheads' Enchanted Mountain after having formerly ruled the Flatheads wisely and considerately. They regain the people's allegiance, imprison the Su-dic, approve Glinda's plan, and decide to call their transformed people Mountaineers.
