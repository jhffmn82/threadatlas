retrospective study in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

A retrospective study approved by the Institutional Review Board of the Medical Faculty of RWTH Aachen University evaluated language models with and without RadioRAG on radiology question datasets, comparing their performance with a board-certified radiologist, in compliance with the Declaration of Helsinki and relevant guidelines and regulations.

2. Materials and Methods / 2.1. RSNA Cases .. 2.6. Code and Data Availability

The retrospective study was conducted in compliance with the Declaration of Helsinki and relevant guidelines and regulations, and its protocol was approved by the Institutional Review Board of the Medical Faculty of RWTH Aachen University (No. EK 028/19). It evaluated language models with and without RadioRAG on radiology question datasets and compared their performance with that of a board-certified radiologist.
