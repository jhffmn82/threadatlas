Ethiopian letters in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ethiopian letters are an image-based writing system used by Ethiopians, representing beasts, body parts, tools, and other objects; their symbolic meanings are learned through continual use and practice rather than syllabic combinations.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DIODORUS SICULUS- / BOOK III.

The Ethiopian letters represent animals, human body parts, tools, and other objects rather than expressing meaning through syllable combinations. Their meanings are learned through the symbolic significance of the images and through continual use and practice.
