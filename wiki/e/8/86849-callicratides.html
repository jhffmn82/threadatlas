Callicratides in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Callicratides is a Lacedaemonian admiral and general, esteemed among the Spartans for justice, integrity, and a plain, honest character. Appointed after Lysander and given a fleet of 150 ships, he took Methymna, besieged and surrounded Mitylene, and defeated Athenians in the harbour. Foreseeing death, he named Clearchus successor, fought at Arginusae, damaged enemy ships, and died when Athenians boarded his vessel.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

Callicratides was appointed admiral by the Lacedaemonians and received the fleet from Lysander. He took Methymna and besieged Mitylene, defeated the Athenians in the harbour, and then surrounded the city.

CHAP. XIV.

Callicratides commanded the Lacedaemonian fleet and accepted the reported prophecy that he would die, declaring that his death would not obscure the glory of Sparta. He fought fiercely, disabled or sank several enemy ships, and was killed after his vessel became fastened to Pericles the younger’s ship and was boarded by Athenians.
