Abstract written by a model

Euryalus’s mother, who weaves him a mantle, learns that Turnus has displayed his head. She runs lamenting through the fighting, grieving denied farewell and rites, until Actor and Idaeus carry her to her tent.

Now rose the ruddy morn from Tithon’s bed,

Euryalus’s mother learns that Turnus has displayed her son’s head and runs through the fighting, lamenting his death. She mourns that she could not kiss him farewell, close his eyes, wash his wounds, perform his funeral rites, or cover his body with the mantle she made. Actor and Idaeus carry her away to her tent.
