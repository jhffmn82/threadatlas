the royal marriage condition in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The royal marriage condition requires Prince Pompadore to wed a Proper Fairy Princess within a proper time or Pumperdink will disappear from the Gilliken country of Oz. The scroll starts his quest, and the Question Box redirects him from Ozma to the Princess of Sun Top Mountain.

The royal marriage condition requires Prince Pompadore to marry the Proper Fairy Princess within the proper span of time. If he does not, Pumperdink will disappear forever and even longer from the Gilliken country of Oz.
