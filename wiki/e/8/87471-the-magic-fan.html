the magic fan

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: The fan, fan, little fan, magic fan, small fan, the fan

Abstract written by a model

The magic fan is a small object first found by the Scarecrow in his pocket after a bullet tears his hat. When opened and waved, it blows the King of the Golden Islands and his army upward; when closed, they fall into the sea. The Scarecrow plans to use it to protect the Silver Island, whose people fear it. Later it escapes Dorothy’s pocket, sends three animals airborne, and is closed and returned by the Magic Belt. Dorothy gives it to Ozma for safekeeping.

the magic fan is in one work on this site, The Royal Book of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The Scarecrow finds the little fan in his pocket after a bullet tears through his hat. When he opens and waves it, the King of the Golden Islands and his army are blown upward; closing it causes them to fall into the sea.

CHAPTER 21

The Scarecrow says he will use the magic fan to watch over the Silver Island, vanquish its enemies, and punish offenders. When he lifts it before the Silver Islanders, they fall prostrate in fear or submission.

CHAPTER 24

The magic fan slips from Dorothy's pocket and causes the Cowardly Lion, the Comfortable Camel, and the Doubtful Dromedary to shoot through the air and remain suspended outside the palace. Dorothy uses the Magic Belt to wish the fan closed and returned, after which the three animals fall safely into rose bushes. She gives the fan to Ozma for safekeeping.
