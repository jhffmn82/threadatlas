the magic words in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The magic words are Coo-ee-oh’s three name syllables, known only to her and used to command machinery: Coo releases boats, oh raises the island, and ee extends the steel bridge; without them, Ozma and the others cannot operate the submarines, pumps, or bridge.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Two

The magic words are spoken commands needed to operate Coo-ee-oh's machinery and accomplish her designs. Ozma and the others do not know them, so they cannot use the machinery to operate the submarines, pumps, or bridge.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The magic words are the three syllables of Coo-ee-oh's name. Coo releases the boats, oh raises the island, and ee extends the steel bridge.
