the Girl He Knew in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The Girl He Knew is a young lady known to Van Bibber who wears a riding-habit. On a hot day in the Park, she meets him at the dock after his ride with three little girls, smiles kindly, and invites him to join her and her brother at the stables.
