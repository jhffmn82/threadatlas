Abstract written by a model

The Skeezers are a people of about one hundred who live on the Magic Isle, a glass-domed island in a great lake. Their village is orderly and prosperous, with marble houses, gardens, parks, and richly dressed inhabitants; they practice magic, protect the fish in their lake, and possess underwater defenses and a brass band. Though they initially conceal much from outsiders and are enemies of the Flatheads, they are fundamentally peaceful and unable or unwilling to fight. Their conflict with the Flatheads arises from Queen Coo-ee-oh's actions, including transforming Rora Flathead and the three Adepts in Sorcery. Coo-ee-oh rules the Skeezers through fear, cruelty, and punishments, leaving them unhappy and oppressed despite their comfortable lives. They rely on her magic, including the means to operate their submarine and survive beneath the water. After Coo-ee-oh is transformed and submerges their island, the Skeezers become helpless: most are imprisoned beneath the Great Dome, while four young men in an iron boat, led by Ervic, cannot operate the submarine. They accept Ozma's authority and await rescue. Three Adepts, aided by Ervic and the rescuers, raise the island from the lake. The Skeezers cheer when they realize they are saved, welcome the Adepts as friends, and are freed from both the water and Coo-ee-oh's rule. In the end, they accept Princess Ozma as their lawful Ruler and elect Lady Aurex as their Queen.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

19 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Skeezers are a previously unknown people in the Land of Oz who have declared war on the Flatheads. They number one hundred and live on a Magic Isle in a great lake, where they use magic to prevent outsiders from learning much about them.

Chapter Two

The Skeezers are associated with the Skeezer Country, and Ozma and Dorothy intend to learn about them during their journey. Ozma tells Dorothy to keep their plans concerning the Skeezers secret until they return.

Chapter Six

The Skeezers forbid the Flatheads from fishing in their lake and guard its shore. Queen Coo-ee-oh, their ruler, transformed Rora Flathead into a Golden Pig, causing the Su-dic to declare war on them.

Chapter Seven

The Skeezers are described by Ozma as possibly good people and worthy of friendship. They are threatened by the Su-dic, who plans to attack them, so Ozma and Dorothy intend to warn and help them.

Chapter Eight

The Skeezers live on a glass-domed island containing a well-kept village of marble houses, streets, gardens, and parks. They are richly dressed and have comfortable lives, but they appear solemn, nervous, or irritated rather than happy. They are ruled by Queen Coo-ee-oh and are preparing to fight the Flatheads.

Chapter Nine

The Skeezers are described as peaceful people who do not seek quarrels or fights, but they obey Coo-ee-oh because they fear her punishments. They became enemies of the Flatheads after Coo-ee-oh transformed the three Adepts in Sorcery into fishes.

Chapter Ten

The Skeezers live on the island beneath the Great Dome and are ruled by Queen Coo-ee-oh. They possess underwater defenses, including submarines, and are threatened by the approaching Flatheads.

Chapter Eleven

The Skeezers in the submarine are bewildered and unable to help Queen Coo-ee-oh after her transformation. They cannot operate the submarine or return to their village, and the Su-dic refuses to take them as prisoners or care for them.

Chapter Twelve

The Skeezers are left frightened and anxious after Coo-ee-oh's transformation, because they depended on her magic to defeat the Flatheads and raise their island. They accept Ozma as their Ruler and are told to remain patient and continue their daily tasks.

Chapter Thirteen

The Skeezers live on the Magic Isle of the Skeezers, which Queen Coo-ee-oh has submerged. Because Coo-ee-oh has forgotten her magic and has been transformed, none of the Skeezers knows how to raise the island.

Chapter Fourteen

The Skeezers are associated with the country containing the sunken island that the council plans to investigate. Their plight is connected to the need to raise the island.

Chapter Sixteen

The four Skeezers left in the iron boat are young men led by Ervic. They cannot operate the submarine, and after drifting on the lake, three sleep while Ervic watches; the Skeezers imprisoned in the Great Dome are unable to help them.

Chapter Seventeen

The Skeezers are the people whose home is the submerged Magic Isle. Three young Skeezers who escaped to the shore tell Glinda and the Wizard that their people remain under the Great Dome and need rescue.

Chapter Nineteen

The Skeezer people may be saved if the three Adepts raise their island to the surface of the lake.

Chapter Twenty

The Skeezers withdraw from the lake shore when Glinda begins her magic and later join the rescue party's camp. The Skeezers are among those who ask whether Glinda and the Wizard reached the island and saw Ozma and Dorothy.

Chapter Twenty-One

The Skeezers are confined in the sunken isle, along with Princess Ozma and Dorothy. Ervic's assistance to the Adepts may have saved them from being imprisoned forever.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Skeezers gather in the village to receive the rescuers and hold the rope steady as the Wizard descends. They welcome the three Adepts as friends and realize that an attempt is being made to rescue them after their imprisonment under water.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The Skeezers had previously been unable to move the great steel basin. They cheer lustily in the streets when the island rises and they realize they are saved.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Skeezers accept Princess Ozma as their lawful Ruler and elect Lady Aurex as their Queen. Their lake is refilled, and they celebrate being freed both from the water and from the cruelty of their former Queen.
