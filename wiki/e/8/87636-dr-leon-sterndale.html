Dr. Leon Sterndale

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

Dr. Leon Sterndale is a great, experienced African explorer and lion-hunter who lives secluded in a bungalow or cottage, smokes a perpetual cigar, and travels between England and Africa. Related to the Tregennis family through his Cornish mother, he has long loved, and been loved by, Brenda Tregennis. Returning from Plymouth after a telegram about the Tregennis tragedy, he questions Sherlock Holmes about the case. Sterndale possesses rare devil’s-foot poison from Africa and, acting as a law unto himself, kills Mortimer Tregennis after Mortimer causes Brenda’s death and two relatives’ insanity. He confesses to Holmes, who lets him go; Sterndale returns to his African work.

Dr. Leon Sterndale is in one work on this site, His Last Bow. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

I may have commented upon my friend’s power of mental detachment, but

Dr. Leon Sterndale visits Sherlock Holmes and asks whether he has explained the mysterious episode affecting the Tregennis family. He says that Mr. Roundhay telegraphed him about the tragedy, causing him to return from Plymouth instead of continuing toward Africa. Holmes verifies that Sterndale’s account of his hotel stay and baggage is true, but remains suspicious of his intense interest in the case.

Fortunately, there is one man in England who knows all about it, and I

Dr. Leon Sterndale admits that he killed Mortimer Tregennis by placing devil's-foot powder above a lamp and guarding the room with a revolver. He says he acted because Mortimer had caused Brenda Tregennis's death and the insanity of two other family members. Holmes allows him to leave, and Sterndale intends to return to his unfinished work in Africa.
