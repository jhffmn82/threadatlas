Question Box in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The Question Box is a box of Mixed Magic that answers any question when shaken three times. It provides crucial instructions throughout: it tells how to control Ruggedo with Trick Tea, assures Pompadore that Pumperdink remains, identifies the Princess of Sun Top Mountain as Pompadore’s intended bride, and explains how to stop the Runaway Country. It also directs Pompa and Kabumpo to trust the Magic Mirror and gold door knob, leading to Peg Amy’s identity being revealed. Earlier, it tells Glegg to bury the Mixed Magic and send the scroll to Pumperdink; later it tells Ozma to make Glegg drink Triple Trick Tea. It knows Peg Amy’s exact disenchantment day.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

The Question Box advises that Ruggedo can be controlled by pouring three drops of Trick Tea on his left foot and two on his right, causing him to return to the Emerald City and later shrink. It also answers Pompadore’s worried question by saying that Pumperdink has not disappeared.

Chapter 19

The Question Box answers Ozma's questions after being shaken three times and identifies the Princess of Sun Top Mountain as Pompadore's intended bride. It also tells Ozma how to stop the Runaway Country, and its directions cause the Country to halt.

Chapter 20

The Question Box had instructed the travelers to trust the Magic Mirror and the gold door knob. Its guidance leads them to use those objects at the castle, where Peg Amy’s identity is revealed.

Chapter 22

The Question Box had previously directed Glegg to bury his Mixed Magic under Ozma’s palace and send the scroll to Pumperdink after two years. When Ozma asks how to deal with Glegg, it directs her to make him drink a cup of his Triple Trick Tea.
