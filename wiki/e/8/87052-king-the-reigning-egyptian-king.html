king (the reigning Egyptian king) in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The reigning Egyptian king, attended day and night and bound by law to a daily regimen, reads letters, sacrifices, keeps appointed times, lives moderately, and rules justly with restrained appetites and punishments.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

The reigning Egyptian king read public letters in the morning, sacrificed to the gods, followed appointed times for his activities, and ate and drank in moderation. He was expected to rule justly, reward merit, punish less than the offenders deserved, and control his appetites and passions.
