Casino at Monte Carlo in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Casino at Monte Carlo is a place with green gambling tables and lights. Young Harringford, the “Goodwood Plunger,” stands by a pillar, watching the lights and waiting for closing. In a changed state of mind, he intends to die there after the lights are extinguished, considering the Casino a fitting setting.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

"THERE WERE NINETY AND NINE" Young Harringford, or the "Goodwood Plunger," as he was perhaps better known at that time, had come to Monte Carlo in a very different spirit and in a very different state of mind from any in which he had ever visited the place before.

Harringford stands beside a pillar of the Casino, watching the lights above the green tables and waiting for them to be extinguished. He plans to act after the Casino closes, regarding the place as a fitting setting for his death.
