The Royal Book of Oz

Abstract written by a model

The document is a fantasy adventure centered on the Scarecrow of Oz’s search for a family history after Professor Wogglebug proposes a Royal Book of Oz. At Ozma’s palace, Wogglebug’s claim that the Scarecrow has no ancestors hurts him, so he returns to his Munchkin farm, finds the original bean pole, and falls through it into the underground Silver Islands. There he is mistaken for the returned Emperor Chang Wang Woe, whose spirit was said to return by way of the magic beanstalk. Though made Emperor, given an imperial family, and credited with saving the islands from the King of the Golden Islands using a magic fan, the Scarecrow misses his friends and Oz. Dorothy, worried by his disappearance, sets out with the Cowardly Lion. Their search gathers Sir Hokus, a knight freed from the sleepy Kingdom of Pokes, and later the Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary. They survive encounters with Bangladore, Fix City, the Deadly Desert, and Wish Way, where wishes transform Sir Hokus and the Camel and finally send the whole company underground to Silver Island. Meanwhile, the Scarecrow’s supposed sons and the Grand Gheewizard plot to restore him to his former eighty-five-year-old imperial body, preventing his return to Oz. Dorothy’s party reaches him before the ceremony, and he chooses to remain the Scarecrow rather than rule as Chang Wang Woe. During the confrontation, Dorothy’s magic parasol and the Gheewizard’s elixir transform the three Princes into two silver pigs and a weasel. The Scarecrow dismisses his old court, rejects Princess Orange Blossom, and appoints his loyal punster, Happy Toko, Emperor of Silver Island. Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Sir Hokus, the Cowardly Lion, and the camels escape upward through the beanpole using the magic parasol. They return to the Munchkin Country after further mishaps involving a red bean, a growing branch, a cyclone, the magic fan, and Dorothy’s Magic Belt. The story ends with their safe arrival in the Emerald City, where the Scarecrow is welcomed as the former Emperor of Silver Island and all the travelers join the celebrations.

The Royal Book of Oz in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921 · the work’s page · read it where The Royal Book of Oz first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

24 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

Professor Wogglebug travels to the Emerald City to propose compiling a Royal Book of Oz containing the names and histories of Oz's people. At Ozma's palace party, he questions the guests about their ancestry and family trees, upsetting the Scarecrow by declaring him to have no ancestors or royalty. Dorothy, Scraps, Tik-Tok, and others give humorous answers, while Ozma permits the project to begin. The Scarecrow becomes determined to find his family tree and leaves to search for it.

CHAPTER 2

The Scarecrow leaves the Emerald City to return to the Munchkin farm and search for traces of his family. He meets the A-B-Sea Serpent and the Rattlesnake, who help him cross the river by forming a bridge. At the farm, he finds the original bean pole in the cornfield and begins digging around it. The ground gives way, and he falls down the bean pole into darkness.

CHAPTER 3

The Scarecrow of Oz slides down the bean pole and is stopped in an underground passageway by the King of the Middlings and Muddle, who demand a toll and question his ability to work. After paying with an emerald, he is released and continues falling through the Middlings' cavern. He eventually crashes into a magnificent silver palace, where its gray-skinned inhabitants mistake him for their returned Emperor and welcome him with great ceremony.

CHAPTER 4

Dorothy becomes uneasy when the Scarecrow is absent after Professor Wogglebug insults his family tree, so she searches for him and then travels with the Cowardly Lion to his home. Finding the Scarecrow missing, they plan to use Ozma's Magic Picture, but a storm forces them off course into an unfamiliar forest. Following signs, they reach the gates of the Great Grand and Mighty Slow Kingdom of Pokes, where both fall asleep from overwhelming drowsiness.

CHAPTER 5

Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion enter Pokes, where the sleepy inhabitants arrest them for exceeding the town's speed limit. They are brought before Sir Hokus, a long-imprisoned knight whose singing drives the Pokes away and awakens the travelers. Sir Hokus explains how he came to Pokes and joins Dorothy and the Cowardly Lion on the Quest for the Scarecrow. The three prepare to escape by singing so they will not fall asleep.

CHAPTER 6

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion escape from Pokes by singing loudly, since the Pokes become sleepy when they hear no singing. Dorothy is briefly overcome by hokey pokey, but the Cowardly Lion rescues her and Sir Hokus by pulling them through the city gates with a rope. Sir Hokus feels he has failed, though Dorothy helps restore his confidence, and the three begin traveling toward the Emerald City. Meanwhile, Glinda discovers in the Magic Record Book that the Emperor of the Silver Islands has returned to his people.

CHAPTER 7

The Scarecrow is proclaimed Emperor of the Silver Islands after the Grand Chew Chew identifies him as the returned spirit of Emperor Chang Wang Woe. The Grand Chew Chew explains that Chang Wang Woe was transformed into a crocus fifty years earlier and that a prophecy promised his spirit would return through the magic beanstalk. The Scarecrow learns that he supposedly has an imperial family, including the deceased Empress Tsing Tsing, sons, and grandsons, and that he is considered eighty-five years old. Dressed in royal robes, he begins to inspect the Silver Islands while appointing Happy Toko as his Imperial Punster.

CHAPTER 8

The Scarecrow struggles with the formal duties and customs of ruling the Silver Islands, then befriends Tappy Oko and learns about his former life as Chang Wang Woe. Disguised as a peasant, he attends a kite-flying exhibition, but a dragon kite catches him and carries him into the air before he falls onto the rocks and is repaired with straw and safety pins. From above, he sees the fleet of the King of the Golden Islands entering the harbor. The Grand Chew Chew warns that the king will burn the Silver Islands and enslave its people, while invoking a prophecy that the Scarecrow will save them.

CHAPTER 9

The Scarecrow leads the frightened Silver Islanders against the invading King of the Golden Islands, while the Imperial Army retreats to caves. When the Scarecrow opens the little fan, its magic lifts the king and his army into the air and then drops them into the sea, winning the Silver Island victory. The Silver Islanders celebrate with speeches, a banquet, and fireworks, but the fireworks set the Scarecrow's straw stuffing smoking until Happy Toko puts him out with a watering can. Despite the victory, the Scarecrow feels lonely and longs for his friends in Oz.

CHAPTER 10

Dorothy is away at the Scarecrow's, while Princess Ozma and Betsy Bobbin discuss her and the recent visit of the A-B-Sea Serpent and Rattlesnakes to the Emerald City. A messenger from Glinda, the Good Sorceress, reports that the Horners and Hoppers are at war again, so Princess Ozma, Betsy Bobbin, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Wizard of Oz travel to help settle the conflict. The A-B-Sea Serpent and Rattlesnakes return to the Munchkin River after receiving gifts from Princess Ozma. Professor Wogglebug works on the twelfth chapter of the Royal Book of Oz, while Princess Ozma does not use the Magic Picture to observe Dorothy and the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 11

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion become trapped in a dark forest and encounter the giant Bangladore. Sir Hokus attacks Bangladore, while the Cowardly Lion bites his leg, only to discover that the giant is made of candy. Dorothy negotiates their release in exchange for secrecy and Bangladore's marshmallow vest buttons. Bangladore carries them to a field outside the forest, where they continue toward the Winkie Country and hope to learn about the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 12

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion travel through a deserted countryside and arrive at Fix City. They encounter living bushes, flying lanterns, moving dinner tables, and inhabitants who stand still while their furniture moves. King Fix It provides them with dinner and beds but gives little useful information about the Scarecrow. The three travelers go to sleep in walking four-post beds.

CHAPTER 13

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion leave Fix City after buying a five-mile road that unrolls wildly and carries them through the air. The road ends above a stream in the Winkie Country, where Dorothy and Sir Hokus fall into the water. The Cowardly Lion rescues both of them, and Sir Hokus knights him for saving his life. Dorothy remains confident that they will eventually find the Emerald City and the Scarecrow.

CHAPTER 14

The Scarecrow, now ruling Silver Island, is prosperous but melancholy because he misses Oz. His three sons greet him coldly, while his fifteen grandsons behave solemnly and insist that Oz does not exist because it is not on their map. Happy Toko comforts the Scarecrow by listening to his stories about Oz and composing a song for him. The Scarecrow then overhears his sons discussing ways to get rid of him and learns that they may be plotting treason.

CHAPTER 15

The Scarecrow learns that the three royal Princes and the Grand Gheewizard plan to restore him to his original form, which would make him eighty-five years old and unable to return easily to Oz. The Princes also announce the proposed marriage of the eldest Prince to Princess Orange Blossom, whom the Scarecrow recognizes as an elderly, cross-looking Princess. That night, the eldest Prince has the Scarecrow and Happy Toko seized and tied to the magic beanpole until the transformation can occur. The Scarecrow begins thinking of a way to escape, while Happy Toko struggles against his bonds.

CHAPTER 16

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion spend three days wandering through a deserted part of the Winkie Country while trying to return to the Emerald City and find the Scarecrow. They nearly step onto the Deadly Desert, realizing they have traveled in the wrong direction. They meet the Comfortable Camel and the Doubtful Dromedary, share their food, and join the animals as a small caravan. The group then discovers a sign for Wish Way and follows the road into the forest.

CHAPTER 17

Dorothy discovers that the Doubtful Dromedary has disappeared, and the Comfortable Camel's tears transform Sir Hokus into a raven and the Camel into air. Dorothy wishes Sir Hokus back, while the Cowardly Lion realizes that every wish on the road marked "Wish Way" comes true. The Cowardly Lion restores the Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary, but Dorothy accidentally wishes that everyone would stop wishing, leaving the group unable to speak. Dorothy then makes an eight-word wish, causing the earth to open and swallow the entire company.

CHAPTER 18

Dorothy and her companions arrive on Silver Island after wishing to be with the Scarecrow and falling a great distance. They find the Scarecrow, who reveals that he is Chang Wang Woe, the Emperor of Silver Island, and that he is threatened with transformation into an old Emperor by the Gheewizard's elixir. The Scarecrow says he would rather remain himself and return to Oz than rule as Emperor. With the Royal Princes, the Grand Gheewizard, and the Scarecrow's bride approaching, the group decides to climb the Scarecrow's family tree, the bean pole, toward Oz.

CHAPTER 19

Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Cowardly Lion, Sir Hokus, Happy Toko, and the camels reunite at the foot of the beanstalk after realizing they cannot leave the Cowardly Lion behind. The Scarecrow plans to abdicate, give a farewell speech, and return with the others to Oz, escaping by force if necessary. Dorothy receives the magic fan and parasol from the Scarecrow and keeps them. At sunrise, a loud procession enters the hall where the group is waiting on or near the silver throne.

CHAPTER 20

The Scarecrow tries to cancel the Ceremony of Restoration, keep his current body, abdicate in favor of his eldest son, and return with Dorothy and the others to Oz, but the Silver Islanders refuse to let him go. Sir Hokus kills the Grand Gheewizard's rheumatic dragon, increasing the confusion while the Grand Gheewizard prepares to use a silver vase and magic elixir on the Scarecrow. Dorothy's magic parasol unexpectedly carries her through the broken skylight, and when she returns she knocks the vase onto the three Silver Princes, transforming two into silver pigs and the eldest into a weasel. The Scarecrow has the Grand Gheewizard seized, dismisses the Silver Islanders, and sends Princess Orange Blossom away after she refuses to abandon the proposed marriage.

CHAPTER 21

Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Sir Hokus, the Cowardly Lion, the Doubtful Dromedary, and the Comfortable Camel choose Happy Toko to rule the Silver Island after the Princes are transformed into pigs. The Scarecrow installs Happy Toko as Emperor and promises to watch over the island with the magic fan. Dorothy and the Scarecrow arrange their return to Oz by tying the party to the magic parasol. When Dorothy opens it, the entire party floats upward and escapes the Silver Island.

CHAPTER 22

Dorothy steers the parasol as she and her party fly through the deep, black passage where the Scarecrow had fallen. The travelers are tied to the parasol with ropes, but they bump against the sides of the tube during the dark ascent. After the parasol crashes into something and partly closes, Dorothy opens it again, and the party rises faster than before. The Scarecrow believes they are near the top, but says that someone has closed the opening.

CHAPTER 23

Dorothy, Sir Hokus, the Cowardly Lion, the Scarecrow, the Comfortable Camel, and the Doubtful Dromedary emerge through the beanstalk into the familiar Munchkin landscape of Oz. They cover the opening around the bean pole and plan to go to the Emerald City, but Sir Hokus eats a red bean and a green branch suddenly grows from his shoulders, carrying him upward. Dorothy and the Scarecrow follow him with the parasol, while the Cowardly Lion, the Comfortable Camel, and the Doubtful Dromedary remain below. A cyclone then lifts the three remaining companions and propels them toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 24

Dorothy and the Scarecrow use the magic parasol to catch up with Sir Hokus, who is growing into the clouds because he swallowed a red bean from the beanstalk. They bring Sir Hokus back to the Emerald City, where the magic beanstalk breaks away from him and disappears. Dorothy uses the Magic Belt to close the magic fan and return it to her, bringing the Cowardly Lion, the Comfortable Camel, and the Doubtful Dromedary safely down. The Scarecrow is welcomed as the former Emperor of Silver Island, while Sir Hokus and the animals join the celebrations in Oz.
