Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the second war against Thebes was undertaken by the Epigoni to avenge their fathers' deaths; they defeated the Thebans, who abandoned the city, after which it was entered and razed. In The Library, the war against Thebes involved the Epigoni ravaging the villages around Thebes and fighting the Thebans under Laodamas; after Laodamas was killed, the Thebans fled, and the Argives entered Thebes, took its booty, and destroyed its walls.
