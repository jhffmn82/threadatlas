blood of Nessus

object · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Nessus's blood, Nessus's poisoned blood, blood, love-spell, poison, poison from my cloak, poisoned blood of Nessus, poisonous gore, the blood that flowed from the wound inflicted by the barb, the spilt blood of Nessus, αἷμα, ῥυὲν αἷμα Νέσσου

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, it is Nessus's blood, reputed to have the power of love, that Deianira uses to treat the textile she sends to Hercules and thereby poisons the cloak. In The Library, it is presented by Nessus as a love charm that Deianira is told to preserve and later smear on Hercules's tunic, where it carries the Hydra's poison. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a supposed love charm kept by Dejanira and applied to Hercules's robe, where it poisons him when warmed on his body, causing agony and death.
