blood of Nessus

object · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Nessus's blood, Nessus's poisoned blood, blood, love-spell, poison, poison from my cloak, poisoned blood of Nessus, poisonous gore, the blood that flowed from the wound inflicted by the barb, the spilt blood of Nessus, αἷμα, ῥυὲν αἷμα Νέσσου
  • In Heroides and Amores, it is Nessus's blood, reputed to have the power of love, that Deianira uses to treat the textile she sends to Hercules and thereby poisons the cloak.
  • In The Library, it is presented by Nessus as a love charm that Deianira is told to preserve and later smear on Hercules's tunic, where it carries the Hydra's poison.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a supposed love charm kept by Dejanira and applied to Hercules's robe, where it poisons him when warmed on his body, causing agony and death.

blood of Nessus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Chapter XII

The blood of Nessus was kept by Dejanira as a supposed love charm. She soaked Hercules's robe in it, but when the garment warmed against his body, its poison caused him intense agony and led to his death.
