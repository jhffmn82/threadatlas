Abstract written by a model

The household stag, a tame beast raised by Tyrrheus’s family and especially Silvia, moves between home and woods. Ascanius wounds it while hunting; it returns home and dies groaning, prompting Silvia and countrymen to seek revenge and begin fighting.

Ascanius wounds it while hunting, and its return to the household, where it dies groaning, causes Silvia and the countrymen to call for revenge and begin the fighting.
