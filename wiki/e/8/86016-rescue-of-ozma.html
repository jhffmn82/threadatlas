rescue of Ozma in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The rescue of Ozma is the travelers’ main mission. After Glinda says Ozma is imprisoned in Ev, Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore leave the Emerald City with a sword to confront the giant, joined in the attempt by Peg Amy, Wag, and Scarecrow. As they continue, they seek a way to rescue her. It requires safely sending Ruggedo away; the Question Box provides the Trick Tea plan, which Glinda carries out after bringing Ozma down.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

The rescue of Ozma becomes Kabumpo and Prince Pompadore's new mission after Glinda tells them she is imprisoned in Ev. They leave the Emerald City with a sword to confront the giant and attempt the rescue.

Chapter 16

The travelers continue their journey with the goal of rescuing Ozma. Pompadore hopes the Magic Box might reveal how to rescue her, while Kabumpo says that rescuing Ozma is their main concern.

Chapter 18

The rescue of Ozma depends on keeping Ruggedo from disturbing the palace and finding a way to send him away safely. The Question Box supplies the Trick Tea plan, and Glinda carries it out after bringing Ozma down.
