Pompa and Kabumpo travel south from Pumperdink toward the Emerald City, beginning through a dim forest and planning a two-day trip. They encounter the Curious Cottabus and a huge black flag, later cross the Soup Sea by night and a sleeping Gilliken village, reach the city at dawn, seek Princess Ozma, and leave to rescue her in Ev.

Chapter 4

Pompadore's journey to the Emerald City begins when Kabumpo carries him south through a dim forest. They plan to reach the city in two days if nothing happens, encounter the Curious Cottabus on the way, and later see a huge black flag ahead.

Chapter 12

Pompa and Kabumpo travel from the Soup Sea through the night, passing through a sleeping Gilliken village before reaching the Emerald City at dawn. After discovering that Ozma has been taken to Ev, they leave the city together to pursue the rescue.
