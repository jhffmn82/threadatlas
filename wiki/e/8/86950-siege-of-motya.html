siege of Motya in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The siege of Motya was Dionysius’s campaign against the resisting Motyans after most nearby Sicilian cities had submitted. He filled the channel to approach the island, built a rampart, and used rams, engines, and six-floored towers. After several days of assault, his army entered through a passage seized at night.

CHAP. VII.

Dionysius besieged Motya after most of the surrounding Sicilian cities had submitted to him. He filled the channel between the island and the shore with stone and rubble so that his forces could bring their artillery engines closer to the town.

About this very time, Himiico, the Carthaginian general^ hearing

During the siege of Motya, Dionysius built a rampart and employed battering rams, siege engines, and six-floored towers. The assault lasted several days before a nighttime seizure of a passage allowed his army to enter the city.
