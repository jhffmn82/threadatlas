the healing animals

Also called: this little creature

Abstract written by a model

Small tortoise-like animals have four eyes and mouths but share one throat and digestive system; their blood instantly heals living wounds and, while fresh, restores severed nonvital limbs.

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The island had small animals with round, tortoise-like bodies, multiple eyes and mouths, many feet, and shared internal organs. Their blood instantly closed living wounds and could restore severed nonvital limbs if applied while the wound was fresh.
