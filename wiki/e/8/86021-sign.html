sign in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

A sign threatens Ruggedo with ten thousand eggs if he moves, turns, or shakes his head. The Scarecrow paints it, Ozma adds her name and the Royal seal, and Sir Hokus presents it; Ruggedo reads it and freezes in fright.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The sign states that if Ruggedo moves, turns, or shakes his head again, ten thousand eggs will be hurled from the palace windows. The Scarecrow paints it, Ozma adds her name and the Royal seal of Oz, and Sir Hokus lowers it in front of Ruggedo's nose. Ruggedo reads it, screams, and becomes rigid with fright.
