Abstract written by a model

A gentleman of the press experienced throughout New York at all hours, he regards city reporters’ work as commonplace and repetitive, with dramas rooted in crime, poverty, or drink rather than mystery. While accompanying Van Bibber to a Jersey City prizefight, he argues this view, recounts a Dickens story, and another reporter keeps time.

A reporter accompanying Van Bibber argues that city life offers no truly mysterious adventures and that its dramas usually arise from crime, poverty, or drink. He recounts a story Dickens told James Payn about a ragged man receiving a bouquet from a beautifully dressed woman at a ball. Another reporter holds the watch during the prizefight.
