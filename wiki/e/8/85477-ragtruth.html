Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

RAGTruth provides the test set used to assess DPO-based hallucination mitigation and detection performance. On its test set, RAG-HAT produces an average hallucination-rate reduction of 26.9% and receives answer-quality win rates of 57.3% according to GPT-4 Turbo and 40.8% according to human evaluation.
