Thebes's founding

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: built Thdbes, first founder

Abstract written by a model

Thebes’s founding is reported as Osiris’s building of a city named for his mother, later called Diospolis and Thebes, though Egyptian historians and priests dispute this and some credit a later king.

Thebes's founding is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
