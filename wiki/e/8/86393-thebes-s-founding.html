Thebes's founding in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Thebes’s founding is reported as Osiris’s building of a city named for his mother, later called Diospolis and Thebes, though Egyptian historians and priests dispute this and some credit a later king.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We shall first speak of the barbarians; not that we judge them

Thebes is reported to have been built by Osiris and named after his mother, later receiving the names Diospolis and Thebes. Egyptian historians and priests disagree, with some attributing its foundation to a later king.
