Abstract written by a model

In RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering, it translates text, images, or other data into numerical vectors so algorithms can compare semantic meaning through similar numerical patterns. In Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review, it was generated by OpenAI’s text-embedding-ada-002 in most reviewed studies, with other approaches including BGE, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, custom models, fine-tuning, and an embeddings-free approach. In SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation, it is an n-dimensional vector produced by an embedding model from textual input that represents the input’s semantic meaning.
