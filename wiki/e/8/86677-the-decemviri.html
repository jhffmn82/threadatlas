the Decemviri

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Decemviri

Abstract written by a model

The Decemviri are a body of ten law-makers who judge a young woman’s freedom claim. One corruptly makes her a bondslave; when soldiers revolt, they gather young men to defend him by force.

The Decemviri were responsible for judging the young woman's case, and one of them used his authority to obtain her as a bondslave through a corrupt judgment. When the soldiers revolted, the Decemviri gathered young men and prepared to defend their colleague by force.
