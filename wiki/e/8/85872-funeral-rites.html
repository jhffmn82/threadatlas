funeral rites in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Funeral rites are required for the buried dead before crossing the Styx and are owed after battle. They unfold through recovering and burying bodies, building pyres, circling, lamenting, burning weapons and spoils, sacrificing animals, and placing ashes and bones beneath turf mounds.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Ye realms, yet unreveal’d to human sight,

Funeral rites and burial are required before the dead may cross the Styx. Palinurus asks Aeneas to recover and bury his body so that he can escape the reproach of remaining unburied.

BOOK XI

Funeral rites include constructing pyres, circling them three times with horse and foot, lamenting, casting weapons and spoils into the flames, sacrificing animals, and burying the remaining ashes and bones beneath turf mounds.
