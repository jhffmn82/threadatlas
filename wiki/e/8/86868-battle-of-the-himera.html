Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Battle of the Himera was a battle near the Himera River in which Daphnaeus led the Syracusans against Carthaginian forces and allies. After a long engagement, the Syracusans won, routing the army, killing over six thousand soldiers, and pursuing survivors to camp.

At the Battle of the Himera, Daphnaeus led the Syracusan army against the Carthaginians and their allies. The Syracusans won after a long engagement, killed more than six thousand of the opposing soldiers, and pursued the survivors to their camp.
