Abstract written by a model

Umbro, priest and leader of the Marrubians, is sent by Archippus to aid Turnus. His wand, words, and herbs heal serpent wounds, but cannot cure a Dardan dart; he dies, mourned by the Angitian woods and Fucine floods.

And now the mighty labour is begun

Umbro, a priest, leads the Marrubians after being sent by King Archippus. His wand, holy words, and herbal powers can calm vipers and heal venomous wounds, but they cannot cure the wound caused by the Dardan dart; his death is mourned by the Angitian woods and the Fucine floods.
