CHAPTER 23

Dorothy says that she would not have had such lovely adventures or found Sir Hokus, the Comfortable Camel, and the Doubtful Dromedary if she had not left Oz. Sir Hokus believes their adventures are over just before the green branch begins carrying him upward.

CHAPTER 24

Dorothy and Sir Hokus rescue the Scarecrow during their adventures and return with him to Oz. The Scarecrow explains his royal identity, receives an emotional welcome from his friends, and promises never to leave them again. He is recorded as the former Emperor of Silver Island and celebrates his return with the people of Oz.
