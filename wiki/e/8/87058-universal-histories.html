Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE PREFACE.

Universal histories are described as providing wholesome instruction and political skill without exposing readers to the hazards of personal experience. They bring the actions of people throughout the world together as one account and allow readers to learn from the mistakes and successes of others.
