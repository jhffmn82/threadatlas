Queen Coo-ee-oh in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920 · the work’s page · read it where Queen Coo-ee-oh first appears

Abstract written by a model

Queen Coo-ee-oh is the young, haughty, wicked queen and sole Krumbic Witch of the Skeezers, a powerful sorceress whose rule rests on fear, stolen magic, and mechanical ingenuity. She claims supreme authority, punishes disobedience or unfavorable speech, and can hear what is said on the island unless magic prevents it. Suspicious of Ozma and Dorothy as possible Flathead spies, she imprisons them until the Flatheads are defeated. She transforms Rora, who tried to poison the lake, into a Golden Pig and removes Rora's cans of brains. Her greatest power comes from betraying the three Adepts at a banquet, stealing their charms, formulas, instruments, and arts, transforming them into fishes, and casting them into the lake. Her own magic and safety depend on those three fishes remaining alive. She controls the Skeezers' island, sinking it to defend against the Flatheads and being uniquely able to make it rise. She also operates submarines and other mechanisms through magic words, aided by a rare genius for mechanics and knowledge taught by the Adepts. When the Flatheads approach, she submerges the island and travels by submarine to confront the Su-dic, carrying a silver magic rope. Before she can use it, he throws material from a copper vessel into her face. She drops the rope, falls into the lake, and is transformed into a large Diamond Swan. Her witchcraft, former knowledge, palace, and power are lost or forgotten. Although she retains diamond-sprinkled wings and bright blue eyes and becomes absorbed in her beauty, she cannot or will not aid Ervic and the Skeezers stranded in an iron boat. She swims away unconcerned with her former people, while warning that the three magic fishes will eventually destroy the Su-dic.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

13 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Queen Coo-ee-oh is the young Skeezer Queen and a sorceress. She caught Rora Flathead attempting to poison the lake, transformed her into a Golden Pig, and removed Rora's four cans of brains.

Chapter Eight

Queen Coo-ee-oh is a proud and suspicious ruler who claims supreme authority over the Skeezers. She declares herself the only Krumbic Witch in the world and says her magic is greater than that of any fairy or Flathead. She refuses Ozma's claim to rule her and orders Ozma and Dorothy held as prisoners until the Flatheads are defeated.

Chapter Nine

Queen Coo-ee-oh rules the Skeezers through fear, punishes remarks she dislikes, and can hear everything spoken on the island unless prevented by Ozma's enchantment. She stole the charms and magical instruments of the three Adepts in Sorcery, transformed them into fishes, and depends on those fishes remaining alive to retain her magic.

Chapter Ten

Queen Coo-ee-oh orders the island submerged when the Flatheads approach and uses submarines to oppose them. She approaches the Su-dic in a submarine with a silver magic rope, but he throws the contents of a copper vessel into her face before she can throw it.

Chapter Eleven

Queen Coo-ee-oh drops the rope, falls into the lake, and is transformed into a large Diamond Swan. She loses her powers of sorcery and warns the Su-dic that the three magic fishes will eventually destroy him.

Chapter Twelve

After being transformed into a Diamond Swan, Coo-ee-oh is absorbed in admiring her beauty and refuses or is unable to help Ervic and the other Skeezers return to the island. She has forgotten the magic she knew as a girl and swims away, unconcerned about her people or their plight.

Chapter Thirteen

Queen Coo-ee-oh submerges the Magic Isle of the Skeezers and is the only one able to command it to rise. She is transformed by the Flathead Su-dic into a Diamond Swan.

Chapter Sixteen

Queen Coo-ee-oh leaves Ervic and the other Skeezers drifting in the iron boat after she is transformed into a Diamond Swan. She had taken the young men with her to assist in capturing the Flathead chief.

Chapter Seventeen

Queen Coo-ce-oh was a haughty and wicked witch who betrayed the three Adepts at Magic and ruled the Skeezers as slaves. After the Su-dic transformed her, she became the Diamond Swan, lost her magic, palace, and former knowledge, and now cares mainly about her beauty.

Chapter Twenty

Coo-ee-oh had used witchcraft to control the submarine and had made the marble door obey a single magic word. Glinda believes Coo-ee-oh probably invented that word, but Coo-ee-oh has forgotten it.

Chapter Twenty-One

Coo-ee-oh betrayed the three Adepts at a banquet, transformed them into fishes, and cast them into the lake. She used the Adepts' stolen magical formulas and arts and used witchcraft to sink the island with its Great Dome.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Coo-ee-oh used unusual magic to sink the island and apparently arranged for spoken words to activate her designs. She used the submarines to attack the Flatheads and controlled the submarine rooms, pumps, and bridge through commands whose magic words are unknown to the others.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Coo-ee-oh had a rare genius for mechanics and invented mechanical apparatus that ordinary witches, wizards, and sorcerers could not understand. Aujah the Adept believes she used her knowledge of expanding steel, Gaulau powder, and three syllabic magic words to control the boats, bridge, and island.

Diamond Swan in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920 · the work’s page · read it where Diamond Swan first appears

Abstract written by a model

Diamond Swan is an enchanted, diamond-covered swan transformed from Queen Coo-ee-oh, with gleaming amethyst eyes and glistening feathers. It abandons the Skeezers, ignores Ervic, crosses the lake, disappears into bushes, then swims near Glinda and the Rescue Expedition, having forgotten its former magic.

Chapter Sixteen

The Diamond Swan ignores Ervic's calls and pleadings and sails to the opposite shore of the lake. At evening, she walks out of the water, shakes her diamond-sprinkled feathers, and disappears among the bushes.

Chapter Seventeen

The Diamond Swan swims near Glinda and the Rescue Expedition, displaying diamond-sprinkled feathers and amethyst eyes. It is the transformed Queen Coo-ce-oh and has forgotten its former magic.
