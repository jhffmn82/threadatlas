Ephialtes

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Epiiialtes, ᾿Εφιάλτην, ᾿Εφιάλτου
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Ephialtes joined Otus in invading Strongyle, recovering Iphimedia and Pancratis, defeating and expelling the Thracians, renaming the island Dia, and dying while fighting Otus; afterward, the inhabitants worshiped them as demi-gods.
  • In The Library, Ephialtes was fathered by Poseidon and Iphimedia, was one of the Aloads, wooed Hera, helped threaten the gods, and was killed in Naxos when Artemis disguised as a deer caused him and Otus to spear each other.

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ephialtes, Aloeus’s Trachinian son, joined Otus to invade Strongyle, recover Iphimedia and Pancratis, defeat and expel the Thracians, rename the island Dia, and die fighting Otus; afterward inhabitants worshiped them as demi-gods.

Ephialtes joined Otus in invading Strongyle to recover Iphimedia and Pancratis. The brothers defeated and expelled the Thracians, renamed the island Dia, and later killed one another in battle; the inhabitants afterward worshiped them as demi-gods.
