CHAP. VII.

The Carthaginian army was described by Ephorus as numbering two hundred thousand foot and four thousand horse, while Timaeus gave a much smaller figure. It used siege towers, battering rams, archers, and fresh troops to overcome Selinus and Himera.

The Carthaginian army was weakened by a plague after the Carthaginians had damaged the suburbs and plundered the temples of Ceres and Proserpina. The disease killed many within five or six days, spread to those caring for the sick, and left the army unable to care for its afflicted members.
