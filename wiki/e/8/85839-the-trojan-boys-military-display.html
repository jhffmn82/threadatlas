the Trojan boys’ military display in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

A warlike equestrian performance brought to the Latins by Ascanius: Trojan boys, led in three troops, circle, divide, reunite, exchange harmless darts, pursue and evade, then join in friendship.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

The Trojan boys ride in three troops led by young Priam, Atys, and Ascanius. They circle the plain, divide and reunite in formations, throw harmless darts, pursue and evade one another, and end by joining in a friendly line.
