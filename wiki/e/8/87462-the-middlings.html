the Middlings in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Middlings are underground mud-made people with dried grass hair, changing faces, root-like hands and feet, gold teeth, glowing eyes, and dried-mud coats. They dig mud and clay, sing the Middling National Air, travel deep black passages in parties, and demand tolls from travelers.
