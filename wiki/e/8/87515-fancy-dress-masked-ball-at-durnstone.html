The Fancy Dress Masked Ball at Durnstone, promoted by the Officers of the Hussars, is planned for one night, with Salome and Sheba intending to attend secretly with Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey. DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

The Fancy Dress Masked Ball at Durnstone is promoted by the officers of the Hussars and is scheduled for that night. Salome and Sheba intend to attend secretly with Gerald Tarver and Nugent Darbey.

The ball is cancelled because the Military were summoned to St. Marvells. Salome and Sheba arrive at the Durnstone Athenaeum to find the cancellation notice and are jeered at by boys.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

The masked ball at Durnstone took place the night before the Dean returned home. Salome and Sheba attended it in costumes representing a lady of the French Revolution and a flower-girl of an unknown epoch.
