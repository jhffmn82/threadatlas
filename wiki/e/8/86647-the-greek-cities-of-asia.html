Abstract written by a model

The Greek cities of Asia were under Persian power before Xerxes’ invasion, then were to be free under their own laws by peace with the Athenians; fearing destruction after Tissaphernes’s arrival, they sought favor and Spartan protection.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

The Greek cities in Asia had been brought under Persian power before Xerxes’ invasion. Under the peace between Persia and the Athenians, they were to be free and governed by their own laws.

CHAP. VI.

The Greek cities of Asia feared destruction after Tissaphernes's arrival and sent ambassadors to Sparta for protection. They also sent messengers to Tissaphernes in an effort to secure his favor.
