DANDY DICK. / THE FIRST ACT.

The fancy dresses are being made in London from Salome and Sheba's brown paper patterns. Salome's costume is a French Revolution gown and Sheba's is a flower-girl skirt and bodice; the dresses arrive in a hamper labeled as cast-off clothing.
