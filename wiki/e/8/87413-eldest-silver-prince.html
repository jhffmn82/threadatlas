CHAPTER 15

The eldest Prince announces that the Scarecrow's restoration will be celebrated by his marriage to Orange Blossom. He later has the Scarecrow and Happy Toko seized and tied to the beanstalk, explaining that this is part of the Grand Gheewizard's formula, and says that he expects to become Emperor after the Scarecrow's death.
