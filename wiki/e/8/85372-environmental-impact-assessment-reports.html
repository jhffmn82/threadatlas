environmental impact assessment reports in SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs

Darío Garigliotti · 2024-08-16

Environmental impact assessment reports are specialized textual sources for identifying and detecting evidence relevant to SDG targets. Thirty-three reports, corresponding to projects developed in the country and nearby European countries, were selected from a public website, converted from PDF files into passages, and examined through excerpts for environmental expressions, claims, and SDG-target content.
