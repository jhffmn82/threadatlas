Marcellus in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Marcellus is a Roman hero, tireless and rich in regal spoils, who repeatedly defeats Punic bands, kills the Gaulish king in single combat, defends his country, and celebrates a Capitol triumph.

Where long extended plains of pleasure lay:

Marcellus is shown as a tireless hero rich in regal spoils. He will defend his country, frighten the Punic forces, kill the Gaulish king in single combat, and celebrate a triumph at the Capitol.
