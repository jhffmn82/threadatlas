retrieval component

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Retrieval component, retrieval, retrieval stage

Abstract written by a model

The retrieval component uses Pyserini for lexical matching and learned dense retrieval in evidence identification and target detection. It retrieves top three passages per target and top five targets per excerpt. Its effects vary by task and model: lexical retrieval is generally strongest for SDG Evidence Identification, while SDG Target Detection has differing precision and recall preferences.
