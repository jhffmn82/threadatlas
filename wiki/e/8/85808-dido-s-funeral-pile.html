The wretched queen, pursued by cruel fate,

Dido’s funeral pile is secretly erected in the court from cloven holms, pines, garlands, cypress, vervain, and yew. It holds Aeneas’s garments, weapons, gifts, image, and the bed associated with their love, and Dido climbs onto it before killing herself.
