Abstract

Long-context processing achieves higher average performance than RAG when sufficiently resourced. SELF-ROUTE maintains performance comparable to long-context processing while reducing computation cost.

opening

The unit finds that long-context LLMs generally achieve better performance than RAG when adequately resourced. SELF-ROUTE achieves performance comparable to long-context processing at a lower cost.

conclusion

The paper compares the performance of RAG and LC, finding that LC performs better in long-context understanding. The proposed dynamic routing method reaches performance comparable to LC.

notes

The text reports performance of 14.53 for GPT-3.5-Turbo and 23.58 for Gemini-1.5-Pro on MuSiQue using only internal knowledge.
