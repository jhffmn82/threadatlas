CHAP. XVI.

The peace between Carthage and Dionysius gave the Carthaginians power over the Sicanians, Selinuntines, Agrigentines, and Himeraeans. Gela and Camarina could remain inhabited without walls but had to pay tribute, while Leontium, Messania, and the other Sicilians retained their laws and liberties; the Syracusans remained subject to Dionysius.
