Parameter-Adaptive RAG

system · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: HyPA-RAG outputs, Hybrid Parameter-Adaptive RAG (HyPA-RAG), Our system, PA, PA models, PA-RAG, PA-RAG system, Parameter-Adaptive (PA), Parameter-Adaptive (PA) RAG

Abstract written by a model

Parameter-Adaptive RAG is a RAG system and variation that does not use knowledge graph retrieval. It integrates a fine-tuned DistilBERT query-complexity classifier to adapt retrieval parameters: it dynamically adjusts top-k retrieval and selects query rewrites according to complexity. In its 2-class configuration, simpler queries are labeled 0 and use 3, while more complex queries are labeled 1 and use 5. Its 2-class results include faithfulness of 0.9044, answer relevancy of 0.7910, absolute correctness of 4.2491, and correctness of 0.8104; the 3-class configuration reaches correctness of 0.8141. These configurations generally outperform fixed-k methods. Its adaptive strategies aim to improve computational efficiency, reduce energy consumption, and minimize carbon footprint. Outputs are for research only, not legal advice or decision-making.

System Design .. AI Legal and Policy Corpus

Parameter-Adaptive RAG is the variation of the system that does not use knowledge graph retrieval.

Performance Evaluation / Dataset Generation .. Classifier Results

Parameter-Adaptive RAG uses the query complexity classifier to adapt parameters. The two-class configuration achieves Faithfulness of 0.9044, Absolute Correctness of 4.2491, and thresholded Correctness of 0.8104, while the three-class configuration achieves thresholded Correctness of 0.8141.

RAG System Architecture / Parameter-Adaptive RAG (PA-RAG) .. RAG Results

Parameter-Adaptive RAG integrates a fine-tuned DistilBERT model to classify query complexity and dynamically select the number of query rewrites and the top-k retrieval value. Its 2-class and 3-class configurations generally outperform fixed-k methods, particularly on faithfulness, absolute correctness, and correctness.

Ethical and Societal implications

Hybrid Parameter-Adaptive RAG (HyPA-RAG) is deployed in AI legal and policy contexts, where its outputs raise concerns about accuracy, reliability, and potential misinterpretation. Its adaptive retrieval strategies are intended to improve computational efficiency, reduce energy consumption, and minimize carbon footprint. Its outputs are intended for research purposes only and should not be used for legal advice or decision-making.
