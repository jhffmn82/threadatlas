CHAP. IV.

Arabia the Happy is the fertile southern part of Arabia, known for its abundance of aromatic plants, gums, myrrh, frankincense, valuable stone, gold, cattle, fruits, and strange animals. Its rivers and summer rains create ponds and lakes, allowing some areas to produce two harvests.

Arabia the Happy is described as exceedingly rich, fertile, and especially suited to breeding cattle. It produces balsam, cassia, frankincense, myrrh, palm trees, calamus, cinnamon, and other fragrant plants, while also containing abundant gold and the wealthy capital city of Saba.
