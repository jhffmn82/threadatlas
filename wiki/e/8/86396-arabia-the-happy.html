Arabia the Happy

place · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Ambia the Happy, Arabia the happy, the Happy

Abstract written by a model

Arabia the Happy is the fertile southern part of Arabia, exceedingly rich in cattle, fruits, aromatic and fragrant plants, gums, gold, valuable stone, and strange animals. It produces balsam, cassia, calamus, cinnamon, mastick, myrrh, and frankincense, exported worldwide from its farthest parts. Its rivers and summer rains form ponds and lakes, enabling two harvests in some areas; its ocean-bordering region breeds elephants, and it contains the wealthy capital city of Saba.

Arabia the Happy is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
