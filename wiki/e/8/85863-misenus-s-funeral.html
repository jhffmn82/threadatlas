Misenus’s funeral

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: funeral state, fun’ral rites, his friend interr’d, his obsequies, such reverend rites, these rites

Abstract written by a model

Misenus’s funeral includes the building and burning of a funeral pyre, the washing and anointing of his body, the burning of oil, incense, and sacrificial fat, and the preservation of his remains in a bronze urn. Aeneas also orders a tomb marked by a trumpet, a soldier’s falchion, and a seaman’s oar.
