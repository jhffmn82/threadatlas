the islanders

Abstract written by a model

The islanders are unusually shaped people over four cubits tall, with flexible bones, limited hair, and generally healthy 150-year lives. They courteously provision Lambulus and his companion, then appear as scientifically and astrologically skilled tribes practicing subsistence work, worship, and communal child-rearing.

And now we purpose to say something briefly of a certain island*

The islanders received Lambulus and his companion courteously and supplied them with the provisions of the country. They were unusually shaped, long-lived, skilled in sciences and astrology, and organized into tribes and societies. They practiced fishing, fowling, manufacturing, agriculture-like gathering, religious worship, and communal child-rearing.
