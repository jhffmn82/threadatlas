Abstract written by a model

The Syracusans revolted against Thrasybulus and requested aid from Gela, Agrigentum, Selinus, Himera, and other Sicilian cities. With their allies, they defeated him by sea and land and forced him to leave Syracuse. The revolt began when Syracusan soldiers killed Doricus and called for liberty. The rebels joined with horsemen from Enna, sought naval aid from Messana and Rhegium, and offered rewards and freedom to those who would help kill the tyrant.
