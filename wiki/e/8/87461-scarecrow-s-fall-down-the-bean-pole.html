CHAPTER 2

While digging around the bean pole, the Scarecrow causes the bottom to give way and falls through. He catches and embraces a stout pole, then realizes he is sliding rapidly down the bean pole into darkness.

CHAPTER 3

The Scarecrow's fall carries him past the Middlings and through darkness for what seems like hours. He bumps against the passageway, eventually bursts through the roof of a silver palace, and lands in its great hall with a fan and a broken parasol.
