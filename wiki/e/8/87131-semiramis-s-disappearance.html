Semiramis's disappearance

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: disappeared, the end of Semiramis

Abstract written by a model

Semiramis's disappearance follows Ninyas's treacherous plot and a eunuch's assault. Remembering an oracle, she yields the crown and commands obedience to her son, then vanishes, interpreted as translation to the gods; some accounts say she became a dove.

Semiramis's disappearance is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

After an assault by a eunuch arranged through her son, Semiramis remembered the oracle that she would disappear when her son plotted against her. She surrendered the crown, ordered her subjects to obey him, and vanished; some accounts say she was transformed into a dove.
