Rutulians in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The Rutulians are a Latian people who cultivate Latium with the Auruncans and oppose the Trojan people in an unjust war in Italy. Led by Turnus, whom they support as champion, they claim land occupied by Evander’s colony and become a common enemy of Evander’s people. They march on, surround, attack, and contain the Trojan camp, fighting amid the wider battle. In the end, they first prepare for Turnus’s single combat, then reject peace and renew fighting after Juturna’s encouragement. Ordered to stand back, they witness Turnus flee and be defeated as their men and walls impede him.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK VIII

The Latian people prepare for war under Turnus and claim the land occupied by Evander’s colony. Their forces pursue the Trojans and are described as a common enemy of Evander’s people.

BOOK IX

The Rutulians march against the Trojan camp under Turnus’s leadership. They surround the camp with guards, fires, and horsemen, then prepare to renew the attack in the morning.

BOOK X

The Rutulians, led by Turnus, attack and contain the Trojan camp. Jupiter refuses to favor either the Rutulians or the Trojans and leaves their success to fate.

Meantime, by Jove’s impulse, Mezentius arm’d,

The Rutulians fight against the Trojans and include forces that attack Mezentius because of their hatred and desire for revenge. They join the surrounding battle and raise shouts when Mezentius and Aeneas engage.

BOOK XII

The Rutulians initially prepare for Turnus’s single combat but grow fearful when they compare him with Aeneas. Encouraged by Juturna’s disguised speech and the supposed omen, they reject the peace and join the renewed fighting.

Now Turnus rolls aloof o’er empty plains,

The Rutulians are ordered by Turnus to hold back while he alone undertakes the combat. They witness his flight and defeat, while their own men and walls obstruct his escape.

Rutulians in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Rutulians are a people and army ruled by Turnus, hostile to Aeneas, the Trojans, and long opposed by Evander. They wage war against the Trojans, unsuccessfully attack their camp, seek battle, surround Euryalus, and kill Nisus after Volscens is killed.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVI

The Rutulians are ruled by Turnus and are hostile to Aeneas and the Trojans. Evander has long been at strife with them, and Turnus leads them in the war against the Trojans.

NISUS AND EURYALUS

The Rutulians try to draw the Trojans into battle and later surround Euryalus during the night raid. They kill Nisus after Nisus kills their leader Volscens.
