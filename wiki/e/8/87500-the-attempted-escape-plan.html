the attempted escape plan in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Hannah proposes that the Dean and Noah ride in a cart, signal at Pear Tree Lane, escape, and free the arrested man with a duplicate key after Noah and old Nick depart. The Dean rejects it, and Noah’s return prevents any attempt.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE THIRD ACT.

Hannah's plan is for the Dean to travel with Noah in the cart, wait until Pear Tree Lane, blow the whistle, jump out, and use the handcuff key after Noah and old Nick continue toward Durnstone. The Dean rejects the plan, and Noah's return prevents it from being attempted.
