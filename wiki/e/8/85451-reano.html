REANO

system · 1 work · 47 quoted facts · 16 connections
Also called: REANO1, our REANO
  • In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation: REANO is a retrieval-augmented generative reader enhancement system and framework for open-domain question answering. It addresses incompleteness in existing KGs and dependencies among retrieved passages by generating a knowledge graph from relevant passages, then using both the passages and generated knowledge triples to produce an answer. It consists of a KG Generator and an Answer Predictor, with the latter based on FiD and augmented by a GNN. REANO maximizes the distribution of an answer given a question and relevant passages, performing joint inference over unstructured passages and the structured knowledge graph. The KG generator creates intra-context and inter-context triples; the GNN identifies and selects the triples most relevant to the question, with ten selected triples used as additional answer-generation context. The KG generator and answer predictor are trained separately because end-to-end optimization is non-differentiable. Evaluated as a FiD-based reader with a T5-base answer predictor, three GNN layers, AdamW, learning rate 1e-4, and batch size 64, REANO outperforms FiD on five datasets. It achieves Exact Match scores of 50.4% on Natural Questions, 69.1% on TriviaQA, 71.0% on EntityQuestions, 77.1% on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 31.8% on MuSiQue. Its improvement reaches 2.7% on EntityQuestions and averages 1.8% across the datasets. Ablations show that removing inter-context triples, intra-context triples, the REM module, or the GNN module significantly reduces performance on EQ, 2WQA, and MuSiQue. Generated and selected triples help answer questions and can reduce the number of passages needed while maintaining performance; without knowledge triples, REANO is equivalent to FiD. Its effectiveness has been verified with T5, while use with BART, decoder-only generative models, and joint optimization with the retriever remain unexplored.

REANO in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11/2024-08-16

REANO is a retrieval-augmented generative reader enhancement system and framework for open-domain question answering. It addresses incompleteness in existing KGs and dependencies among retrieved passages by generating a knowledge graph from relevant passages, then using both the passages and generated knowledge triples to produce an answer. It consists of a KG Generator and an Answer Predictor, with the latter based on FiD and augmented by a GNN. REANO maximizes the distribution of an answer given a question and relevant passages, performing joint inference over unstructured passages and the structured knowledge graph. The KG generator creates intra-context and inter-context triples; the GNN identifies and selects the triples most relevant to the question, with ten selected triples used as additional answer-generation context. The KG generator and answer predictor are trained separately because end-to-end optimization is non-differentiable. Evaluated as a FiD-based reader with a T5-base answer predictor, three GNN layers, AdamW, learning rate 1e-4, and batch size 64, REANO outperforms FiD on five datasets. It achieves Exact Match scores of 50.4% on Natural Questions, 69.1% on TriviaQA, 71.0% on EntityQuestions, 77.1% on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 31.8% on MuSiQue. Abstract

REANO enhances a retrieval-augmented reader model with a knowledge graph generation module. It consists of a knowledge graph generator and an answer predictor, and it improves exact match scores by up to 2.7% on the EntityQuestion dataset, with an average improvement of 1.8% across five datasets.

opening

REANO is a retrieval-augmented generative reader that generates a knowledge graph from retrieved passages and uses it with the passages to generate answers. It improves exact-match performance by up to 2.7% compared with state-of-the-art baselines across five open-domain question answering datasets.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.2 / 3.2.1

REANO maximizes the distribution of an answer given a question and relevant passages. It generates a knowledge graph from the passages and performs joint inference over the passages and knowledge graph to generate answers.

opening .. 3.3

REANO combines passages with generated knowledge graphs to predict answers. Its KG generator and answer predictor are trained separately because end-to-end optimization is non-differentiable.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.2

REANO is evaluated as a FiD-based reader for open-domain question answering using a T5-base answer predictor, three GNN layers, and ten selected triples. It achieves the best Exact Match performance on all five datasets: 50.4 on Natural Questions, 69.1 on TriviaQA, 71.0 on EntityQuestions, 77.1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 31.8 on MuSiQue.

opening

REANO is evaluated with different knowledge-triple settings, ablated components, passage counts, and values of the trade-off hyperparameter β. Removing inter-context triples, intra-context triples, the REM module, or the GNN module significantly reduces its performance on EQ, 2WQA, and MuSiQue. Its generated and selected triples help answer questions and reduce the number of passages needed while maintaining performance.

opening .. 5

REANO generates meaningful knowledge triples from passages and identifies triples that are useful for answering questions. It uses a KG generator to generate knowledge triples from passages and a GNN to identify and select the most relevant triples.

6

REANO is proposed to address the ODQA task. It contains a knowledge graph generator and an answer predictor based on FiD with an additional GNN model, and it improves EM score by up to 2.7% compared with state-of-the-art baselines.

Limitations

REANO is a knowledge graph generation module for improving the performance of retrieval-augmented readers. Its effectiveness was verified with an encoder-decoder T5 model, while its use with other generative readers and joint optimization with the retriever remain unexplored.
