Aeneas's flight and wanderings

Also called: Your flight, your wand’rings, your woes

Abstract written by a model

Aeneas’s flight and wanderings are a seven-year postwar course from the Trojan shore across many seas and coasts, marked by his men’s distress and a navy repeatedly tossed at sea.

Aeneas's flight and wanderings is in one work on this site, The Aeneid. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
