Aeneas's flight and wanderings in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Aeneas’s flight and wanderings are a seven-year postwar course from the Trojan shore across many seas and coasts, marked by his men’s distress and a navy repeatedly tossed at sea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Cupid meantime assum’d his form and face,

Dido asks Aeneas to describe his flight, wanderings, and woes after the war. She notes that his men have been distressed and his navy tossed on every sea and coast while seven years have passed.
