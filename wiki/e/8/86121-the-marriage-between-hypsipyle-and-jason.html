1 section; each heading opens the text

VI

The marriage between Hypsipyle and Jason was publicly sanctioned by Juno and Hymen, with wedding rites and a marriage torch. Hypsipyle presents it as chaste and legitimate, unlike Jason’s union with Medea, and asks to remain Jason’s wife after his return.
