Also called: char-woman, janitress, woman

Abstract written by a model

The char-woman, caretaker at the Twenty-first Precinct station-house, responds when an ill baby is brought downstairs. Taking the baby in her arms, she says heat and poor care caused its condition, orders milk, a surgeon, and ice, and insists it will not die.
