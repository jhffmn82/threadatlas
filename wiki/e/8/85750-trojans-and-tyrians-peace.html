Trojans and Tyrians' peace in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Trojans and Tyrians establish lasting concord as Dido invokes Jove, Bacchus, and Juno and asks Tyrian lords to vow ratification.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Cupid meantime assum’d his form and face,

Dido calls upon hospitable Jove, Bacchus, and Juno to bless the hour and establish lasting concord between the Trojan and Tyrian lines. She asks the lords of Tyre to make vows with her to ratify the peace.
