Marcus Furius in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Marcus Furius, made dictator during a Volscian attack, marches by night to destroy the Volsci from behind, then defeats the Aequi and Gauls.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XIII.

Marcus Furius was made dictator when the Volsci attacked the Roman camp. He marched out at night, attacked the Volsci from behind, helped destroy them between the Roman camp and his force, and later defeated the Aequi and the Gauls.
