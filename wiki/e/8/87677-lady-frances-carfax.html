Lady Frances Carfax

Abstract written by a model

Lady Frances Carfax is a noblewoman, the sole survivor of the late Earl of Rufton’s direct family, with limited means after the estates passed in the male line. She carries valuable Spanish jewellery and keeps a heavy trunk locked at the Hôtel National in Lausanne, where she had stayed for weeks while writing Miss Dobney every second week. She abruptly leaves without an address, travels to Baden with Dr. Shlessinger and his wife while pursued by Hon. Philip Green, and stops writing. Brought to London by Henry Peters and Mrs. Peters, she is held prisoner and targeted for murder because she could denounce them. After chloroform, she is placed alive in a specially measured coffin, but Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson rescue her before the planned funeral.

The Disappearance of Lady Frances Carfax

Lady Frances Carfax is a wealthy but solitary woman and the sole survivor of the direct family of the late Earl of Rufton. She leaves Lausanne suddenly, travels to Baden with Dr. Shlessinger and his wife, and then disappears after ceasing her regular letters to Miss Dobney.
