There are great controversies concerning the reasons of the over-

Democritus says that snow belongs to northern rather than southern regions and that vapors driven by the Etesian winds produce rain on the highest Ethiopian mountains. His explanation is criticized because the Nile swells before the Etesian winds begin and falls after they have passed.
