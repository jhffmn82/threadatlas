Abstract written by a model

The Flatheads are a previously unknown people of Oz, about one hundred men, women, and children, living in a basin-like hollow atop Flathead Mountain, south of the Lake of the Skeezers. They are isolated mountain people who practice magic, build chiefly with rock, and guard their summit with an invisible stone wall, a winding stairway, and a guarded route to the plain. They have bald, flat-topped heads, large outward-pointing ears, stubby noses, and bright violet eyes. Their metal costumes and weapons, bows, arrows, and steel axes, come from metals dug from their mountain. Their brains are carried in cans supplied by fairies, and their political system gives everyone an office, with the Supreme Dictator, the Su-dic, holding authority. The central matter in their story is their conflict with the Skeezers. Once friendly neighbors ruled by three benevolent Adepts in Sorcery, the Flatheads change after the Adepts are lost and Su-dic and Rora rule them. They fish in the Skeezers' lake against their wishes, prepare to destroy their island and one of the three fishes, and march to the lake under the Su-dic with armed followers. Though surprised by the Skeezers' submarine, they later return to their mountain when the Su-dic claims victory, while he and officers feast and others must serve. They subsequently poison the lake's fishes, and the Su-dic transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan. The Flatheads obey the Su-dic from fear rather than shared responsibility. When the three Adepts return, they cast down their arms and seek protection. Glinda places their brains inside growing, round heads, transforming them; they cease to be called Flatheads and become the Mountaineers.

Flatheads in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920 · the work’s page · read it where Flatheads first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Flatheads are a previously unknown people in the Land of Oz who are involved in a war with the Skeezers. They number about one hundred and live in a basin-like hollow atop a mountain south of the Lake of the Skeezers; they also work magic and generally keep themselves isolated.

Chapter Five

The Flatheads live on top of the flat mountain and use a guarded route to travel between the summit and the plain. They constructed an invisible stone wall and a winding stairway that warns them when intruders approach. They are described as bald people with flat-topped heads, large violet eyes, and metal clothing, and the men carry bows, arrows, and small steel axes.

Chapter Six

The Flatheads live on Flathead Mountain and use rock as their main building material. They carry canned brains supplied by fairies, and most serve as dictators or other office holders. They are in conflict with the Skeezers over fishing and prepare to fight them.

Chapter Seven

The Flatheads guard their mountain stronghold, but they fail to stop Ozma and Dorothy from escaping. The Su-dic intends to lead them in a war against the Skeezers. Ozma believes the Flathead people are not as responsible as the Su-dic and may become peaceful if he loses power.

Chapter Eight

The Flatheads are described as a neighboring people living on a barren mountain-top. Their Supreme Dictator is a Sorcerer, and they intend to attack the Skeezers.

Chapter Nine

The Flatheads were once friendly with the Skeezers and were ruled by three benevolent Adepts in Sorcery. After the Adepts were lost, Su-dic and Rora became rulers, and the Flatheads now threaten war and seek to destroy one of the three fishes.

Chapter Ten

The Flatheads approach the lake with armed followers under the Su-dic's leadership to attack the Skeezers. They do not know about the Skeezers' submarines and watch in surprise as one emerges from the submerged dome.

Chapter Eleven

The Flatheads march back to their mountain after the Su-dic declares that they have conquered the Skeezers. There, the Su-dic and some officers feast while the others are forced to serve them.

Chapter Thirteen

The Flatheads come to the lake to poison the fishes. Their Supreme Dictator, the Flathead Su-dic, transforms Queen Coo-ee-oh into a Diamond Swan.

Chapter Seventeen

The Flatheads are enemies of the Skeezers and came to the lake shore after the Magic Isle was submerged. Their approach led Queen Coo-ce-oh to use her submarine in an attempt to conquer the Su-dic.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Flatheads obey the Su-dic out of fear until the arrival of the three Adepts leads them to cast down their arms and seek protection. Glinda transforms them by placing their brains inside growing, round heads, after which they are no longer called Flatheads and become the Mountaineers.
