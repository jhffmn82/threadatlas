Greek mercenaries

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: mercenaries, the mercenaries, thirteen thousand mercenaries

Abstract written by a model

Greek mercenaries were about thirteen thousand experienced soldiers from the Peloponnesus and other parts of Greece. Commanded in separate contingents by Clearchus, Proxenus, Socrates, and Menon, they repeatedly routed less experienced barbarians. Under Clearchus and Aridaeus, they became the principal Greek force in the retreat, permitted peaceful passage through the king's territory if they committed no outrages.

CHAP. V-

The Greek mercenaries were drawn from the Peloponnesus and other parts of Greece and numbered about thirteen thousand. They were commanded in separate contingents by Clearchus, Proxenus, Socrates, and Menon, fought with skill gained from the Peloponnesian War, and repeatedly routed the less experienced barbarians.
