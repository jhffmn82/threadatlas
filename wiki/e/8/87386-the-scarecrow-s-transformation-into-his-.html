Gheewizard’s elixir transforms the lively Scarecrow into his old Imperial self, an old Emperor. Fearing loss of his Scarecrow identity and, at eighty-five, inability to return to Oz, he is bound by the eldest Prince so the change can proceed.

CHAPTER 15

The Scarecrow fears being transformed into his old Imperial self because he believes that, at eighty-five, he could not travel back to Oz. The eldest Prince has him bound so that the transformation can proceed.

CHAPTER 18

The Gheewizard's elixir is intended to transform the lively Scarecrow into an old Emperor. The Scarecrow fears this change because he does not want to be an old ruler or remain separated from his identity as the Scarecrow.
