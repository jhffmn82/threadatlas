Artabazus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Artabazus is a Persian commander and admiral of great account. He commanded a fleet of three hundred ships at Cyprus, led more than forty thousand Persian survivors from Plataea through Macedonia safely into Asia, later commanded a renewed expedition against Egypt with Megabyzus, helped raise the siege of the White Wall, and negotiated withdrawals and peace with Athens.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. HI.

Artabazus commanded the Persian survivors who fled from Plataea by a shorter route through Phocis. He led more than forty thousand men through Macedonia and brought them safely into Asia.

CHAP. XIX.

Artabazus was appointed by Artaxerxes to command the renewed Persian expedition against Egypt with Megabyzus. He helped raise the siege of the White Wall and negotiated the safe withdrawal of the Athenians.

DIODORUS SICULUS. / BOOK XIL

Artabazus commanded the Persian fleet, which numbered three hundred ships and was stationed at Cyprus. After the Persian defeats, he and Megabyzus sent ambassadors to Athens to negotiate peace.
