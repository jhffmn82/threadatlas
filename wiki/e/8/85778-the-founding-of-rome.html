Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, it is the founding of Rome traced through Aeneas, Ascanius, Alba Longa, Ilia, and Romulus, who founds the city and gives the Roman people their name. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the founding of Rome when Romulus establishes the city and becomes its first king.
