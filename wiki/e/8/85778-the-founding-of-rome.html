  • In The Aeneid, it is the founding of Rome traced through Aeneas, Ascanius, Alba Longa, Ilia, and Romulus, who founds the city and gives the Roman people their name.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, it is the founding of Rome when Romulus establishes the city and becomes its first king.

the founding of Rome in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

The founding of Rome is traced through Aeneas, Ascanius, Alba Longa, Ilia, and Romulus. Romulus is destined to gain his grandsire’s throne, found the city, and give the Roman people their name.
