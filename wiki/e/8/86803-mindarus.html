Mindarus

Abstract written by a model

Mindarus, a Lacedaemonian admiral, commanded Peloponnesian fleets from Abydos, fought Athenians near Sestos and Abydos, refitted ships and sought reinforcements, aided Dorieus, captured Cyzicum, and was killed defending ships at Claros, prompting Peloponnesian flight.

CHAP. 111.

Mindarus commanded the Lacedaemonian fleet in the naval battle between Sestos and Abydos. His fleet was defeated by the Athenians, and he later refitted the ships at Abydos and sent Epicles to gather additional ships from Euboea.

CHAP. V.

Mindarus commanded the Peloponnesian fleet and came from Abydos with ninety-four ships to aid Dorieus at Dardanium. He later besieged and captured Cyzicum, but at Claros he was killed while defending the ships, causing the Peloponnesians to flee.
