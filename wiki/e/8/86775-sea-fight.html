Abstract written by a model

The first sea-fight involved eighty well-manned Syracusan ships and sixty Athenian ships; the Athenians sank eleven Syracusan ships after turning back to fight. A later sea-fight in the Great Haven favored the Syracusans, who used their stronger, lower forecastles to board and defeat the Athenian galleys.
