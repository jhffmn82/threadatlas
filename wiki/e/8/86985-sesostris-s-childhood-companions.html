Seven descents after, (they say), Sesostris reigned, who excelled

Sesostris's childhood companions were born on the same day as he was and were raised with him under the same education, discipline, and exercises. They became his commanders and fellow soldiers, numbering more than seventeen hundred, and received large estates in the richest parts of Egypt in return for attending him in war.
