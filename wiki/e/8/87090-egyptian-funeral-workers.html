Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP, VII.

Egyptian funeral workers inherit and practice the arts of wrapping and burying the dead. The scribe marks the place for the incision, the Paraschistes makes the cut with an Ethiopian stone and is cursed and pelted for doing so, while the Taricheutae remove and cleanse the internal organs and are highly honored as priestly companions.
