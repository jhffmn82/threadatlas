Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

At royal sacrifices, the high priest publicly prayed for the king's health and prosperity and recited his virtues. After the king viewed the entrails and completed the sacrifice, priests read from sacred records the laws, edicts, and exemplary deeds of famous persons.
