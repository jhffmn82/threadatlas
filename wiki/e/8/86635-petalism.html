Petalism

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Petalism was Syracuse’s olive-leaf voting banishment law, modeled on Athenian ostracism: citizens expelled the most powerful man for five years without a crime. It ended when leading citizens withdrew from public affairs and disorder increased.

Petalism is in one work on this site, The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. XXIV.

Petalism was Syracuse’s version of ostracism, in which citizens wrote the name of a potentially dominant man on an olive leaf and banished the person receiving the most votes for five years. It was repealed after leading citizens withdrew from public affairs and disorder increased.
