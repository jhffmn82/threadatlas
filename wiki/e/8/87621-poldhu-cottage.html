Abstract written by a model

Poldhu Cottage, near Poldhu Bay on a grassy headland, is the place where Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson stay while Holmes is ordered to rest. Mr. Roundhay and Mortimer Tregennis bring the Tredannick Wartha tragedy to Holmes there. After returning, Holmes breaks his silence and walks the cliffs with Watson. Dr. Leon Sterndale visits, and Holmes’s lamp experiment drives Holmes and Watson from the poisoned room into the grounds.

Poldhu Cottage is in one work on this site, His Last Bow. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
