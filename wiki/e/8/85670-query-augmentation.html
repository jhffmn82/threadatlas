query augmentation

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: query augmentation methods

Abstract written by a model

Query augmentation is an input enrichment approach and LLM retrieval-process integration category for complex task inputs. It enriches original queries with information and keywords from internal LLM databases or external sources to facilitate retrieval. It can achieve state-of-the-art performance through collective use of retrieved knowledge during answer generation without training another rewriting model, but single-source augmentation may overlook the original query and limit performance.
