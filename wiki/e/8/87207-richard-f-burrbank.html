Richard F. Burrbank is a prominent, very wealthy New York railroad lawyer and railroad-stock owner, considered for many high offices, whose record ends with his being found dead from two pistol wounds above his heart.

1 section; each heading opens the text

RAEGEN THE OTHER WOMAN THE TRAILER FOR ROOM NO.

Richard F. Burrbank was a prominent New York railroad lawyer and wealthy railroad-stock owner. He was found dead at the foot of his hall stairs with two pistol wounds above his heart.
