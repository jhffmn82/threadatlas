Abstract written by a model

Radix pedis diaboli is a rare African poisonous root, called devil’s-foot root, presented by Dr. Leon Sterndale as reddish-brown powder. Used by medicine-men in certain West African districts as an ordeal poison, it stimulates brain centres controlling fear, causing madness or death, and is reportedly undetectable by toxicology.
