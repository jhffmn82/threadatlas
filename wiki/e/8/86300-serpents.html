Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

The inhabitants near the deserts report serpents of incredible size, although claims of serpents a hundred cubits long are treated as false. One serpent thirty cubits long was captured in standing pools and taken alive to Alexandria; Ethiopians also claim that giant serpents can overpower elephants.
