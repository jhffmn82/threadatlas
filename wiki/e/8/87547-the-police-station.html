Abstract written by a model

The police station is an auxiliary country station in St. Marvells, partly a kitchen and containing official documents, implements, and a prisoner’s cell. Hannah cooks and admits visitors, Noah works as constable, and Georgiana and Sir Tristram report the Dean’s disappearance. The Dean is held there before transfer to Durnstone.
