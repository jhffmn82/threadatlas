Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, Aeneas's sword is left behind in Carthage and Dido uses it to kill herself on the funeral pile. In Heroides and Amores, Aeneas's sword lies ready in Dido's lap as tears fall on its drawn steel, and she says it will soon be stained with blood instead of tears.
