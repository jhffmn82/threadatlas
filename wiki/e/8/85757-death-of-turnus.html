death of Turnus

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Turnus falls, Turnus’s defeat and death, death, his fate, my misfortunes, the coming blow, the death, the fatal weapon

Abstract written by a model

In The Aeneid, Turnus’s defeat and death ends the duel between Turnus and Aeneas: after his sword breaks, his escape is blocked, and a Fury destroys his courage and strength, Aeneas kills the wounded Turnus after recognizing Pallas’s golden belt. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Turnus falls after warfare between his forces and Aeneas’s side, before Ardea is consumed by fire.
