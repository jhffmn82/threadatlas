A caravan begins with Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary, loses its Karwan Bashi in a sandstorm, then travels with Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion.

CHAPTER 16

The Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary are described as a caravan before a sandstorm leaves them without their Karwan Bashi. After meeting Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion, they form a little caravan and travel together.
