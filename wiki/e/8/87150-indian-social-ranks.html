Abstract written by a model

has_number seven [covering almost all India] The people of India are divided into seven ranks: philosophers, husbandmen, herdsmen and shepherds, mechanics, the militia, ephori, and senators. Marriage and occupations are generally restricted to the rank into which a person was born.
