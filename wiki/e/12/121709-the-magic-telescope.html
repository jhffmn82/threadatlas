Abstract written by a model

A magic telescope in Chinda's tall, glass-enclosed tower provides divination about the missing package. Chinda uses it to learn that the Sultan's sought item will be found at midnight. Later, it shows Speedy the enchanted forest, focusing on a hollow tree where a magic date seed lies among a squirrel's nuts.

CHAPTER TWELVE

The magic telescope in Chinda's tall, glass-enclosed tower is used to investigate the missing package. Chinda gazes through its lens for ten minutes and receives a partial indication that what the Sultan seeks will be found in the middle of the night.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Chinda's magical telescope shows Speedy a section of the enchanted forest and narrows its view to the inside of a hollow tree. It reveals the gleaming magic date seed hidden among nuts gathered by a squirrel.
