Abstract written by a model

The Scottish women were politically organised through Women’s Suffrage societies and provided national service through voluntary work. During the war, they organised medical and practical service through the Scottish Women’s Hospitals, serving abroad as doctors, nurses, ambulance workers, and hospital staff.
