Niobe group

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the Niobe group

Abstract written by a model

The Niobe group is a grandly tragic sculpture of Niobe as Apollo and Diana's curse strikes her children. As they flee to her, she shields her youngest; despair and agony unfold without contortion. The original is lost, surviving in copied parts in the Uffizi Gallery at Florence.

Niobe group is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

In Athens, after Pheidias, the greatest sculptures were those

The Niobe group represents Niobe as Apollo and Diana's curse falls upon her children. The children flee to her for protection while she tries to shield her youngest, and the group presents despair and agony without contortion in a grandly tragic manner; the original is lost, but copies of parts are in the Uffizi Gallery.
