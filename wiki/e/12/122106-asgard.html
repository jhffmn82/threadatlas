Asgard in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Asgard, reached by crossing Bifrost, is the gods’ abode and home, containing golden and silver palaces including Valhalla; after Hermod speaks with Hela, the gods receive his account and hope Baldur returns.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

Asgard is the abode of the gods, reached by crossing Bifrost. It contains golden and silver palaces, including Valhalla, the residence of Odin.

Chapter XXXIII

Asgard is the home to which the gods hope Baldur will return. Hermod rides back to Asgard after speaking with Hela, and the gods there receive his account.
