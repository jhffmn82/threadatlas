Chapter XXXI

Asgard is the abode of the gods, reached by crossing Bifrost. It contains golden and silver palaces, including Valhalla, the residence of Odin.

Asgard is the home to which the gods hope Baldur will return. Hermod rides back to Asgard after speaking with Hela, and the gods there receive his account.
