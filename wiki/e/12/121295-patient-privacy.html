patient privacy in Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review

Lameck Mbangula Amugongo · 2025-06-11 · the work’s page · read it where patient privacy first appears

Abstract written by a model

Patient privacy is a safety concern for RAG-based LLM systems in healthcare, especially when external databases expose sensitive information such as prescription data. Most reviewed studies did not address privacy. Suggested protections include removing identifiable patient or doctor data from retrieval datasets, controlled deployment of open-source models, multilayered privacy and security measures, and prompting to detect and reduce retrieval-information leakage.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Ethical considerations

RAG-based healthcare LLM systems need to ensure and evaluate privacy and security. Most reviewed studies did not address privacy; reported measures included removing identifiable patient or doctor information from a retrieval dataset and applying a multilayered privacy and security approach with Azure OpenAI.

Data analysis

Privacy issues may arise when proprietary models are used with sensitive information such as patient data. Controlled deployment of open-source models is suggested as a possible solution.

Discussion

Patient privacy is threatened when RAG systems access external databases that may reveal sensitive information, such as prescription data. The unit suggests keeping personally identifiable patient information out of retrieval databases and exploring measures such as composite structured prompting to detect and reduce retrieval-information leakage.
