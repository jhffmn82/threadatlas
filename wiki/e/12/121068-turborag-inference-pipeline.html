TurboRAG inference pipeline in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

TurboRAG inference pipeline is a process that encodes documents offline, stores embeddings and KV caches in a vector index, retrieves relevant documents for a query through similarity search, concatenates their caches, performs online prefill, and generates an answer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3 / Methodology .. 3.4 / The TurboRAG Pipeline

The TurboRAG inference pipeline encodes documents offline, stores their embeddings and KV caches, encodes and retrieves documents for a query, concatenates the corresponding caches, performs online prefill, and generates a response. Its usage process remains fundamentally consistent with standard RAG.
