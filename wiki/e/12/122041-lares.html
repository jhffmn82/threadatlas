Lares in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Lares are household gods, regarded as deified spirits of mortals and souls of ancestors, who watch over and protect their descendants as ancestral household spirits.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

The Lares, or Lars, were household gods regarded as the deified spirits of mortals. The family Lars were believed to be the souls of ancestors who watched over and protected their descendants.
