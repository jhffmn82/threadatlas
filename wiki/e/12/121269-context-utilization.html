Context Utilization in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16

Context Utilization is a RAG evaluation and generator quality metric, introduced in RAGBench and TRACe to give a granular, actionable view of RAG performance. It measures, at the example level across context documents, the fraction of retrieved context the generator uses to produce a response. It is assessed by semantic comparison of context and response, including labels of utilized substrings. Low Utilization with low Relevance suggests a greedy retriever; low Utilization alone suggests a weak generator. On DelucionQA(40), GPT-4 span-level labels achieved 0.92 F1 and 0.94 accuracy against human judgements.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Context utilization is introduced as a new metric in RAGBench. It is intended to provide a more granular and actionable description of overall RAG system performance.

TRACe Evaluation Framework .. Adherence

Context Utilization measures the fraction of retrieved context used by the generator to produce the response. Low Utilization combined with low Relevance can indicate a greedy retriever, while low Utilization alone can indicate a weak generator.

RAGBench Statistics .. Alignment with Human Judgements

Utilization is labeled by asking the LLM annotator to identify utilized substrings in input documents. On DelucionQA(40), GPT-4's span-level Utilization labels have an F1 of 0.92 and accuracy of 0.94 against human judgements.

RAG Case Study .. Estimating Context Relevance is Difficult

Utilization measures the use of provided context in the response. It is generally easier to predict than Context Relevance because it can be assessed through a straightforward semantic comparison between context and response.
