Dr. Eleanor Soltau

Abstract written by a model

Dr. Eleanor Soltau was a doctor leading the Scottish Women’s Hospitals for Foreign Service’s first unit at Kragujevatz. She commanded the First Serbian Unit during the devastating typhus epidemic, until diphtheria made her ill in spring 1915.
