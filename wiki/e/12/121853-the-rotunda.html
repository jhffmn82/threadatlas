Abstract written by a model

The Rotunda in Dublin provides midwifery training through mixed classes, including clinical experience. It awards a Licentiate of Midwifery after six months and examination; Elsie takes three months’ training, praising its methods while noting women receive fewer clinical opportunities than male senior students.

The Rotunda in Dublin provides Elsie with three months of clinical training in midwifery. She praises its methods and the experience it gives her, but notes that male senior students receive opportunities to undertake much of the clinical work that women students are not offered.
