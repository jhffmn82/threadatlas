Abstract written by a model

In May 1810 Sir James Craig suppressed Le Canadien and arrested its printer and three proprietors: Taschereau, Blanchet, and Pierre Bedard. The stated pretext was the publication of notes about the conflict between the governor and the House of Assembly in Jamaica, but the deeper cause was Craig’s opposition to the newspaper’s policy.
