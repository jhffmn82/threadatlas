The RAG Case Study uses prompt templates to evaluate question answering with and without provided context. Its comprehensive results are reported in Table 6.

DeBERTa model training

The RAG case study reports that FAISS configurations generally have higher average relevance than corresponding TF-IDF configurations, and that k=2 generally outperforms k=4 on relevance. Among the listed configurations, FAISS with k=2, the long + CoT prompt, and GPT-3.5-turbo-0125 has 0.81 average relevance, 0.64 average utilization, 0.76 average completeness, and 0.07 percent hallucinated responses, while the corresponding GPT-4o configuration has 0.83, 0.69, 0.82, and 0.04.
