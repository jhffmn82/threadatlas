RAG Case Study

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: RAG case study, case study

Abstract written by a model

RAG Case Study is a case study that samples 100 realistic world knowledge queries from RAGTruth and 2,512 unique context document chunks. It evaluates 32 RAG systems varying in retriever, retrieved-document number, generation model, and prompt template, including question answering with and without provided context. Results in Table 6 show FAISS generally exceeds corresponding TF-IDF relevance and k=2 generally exceeds k=4; listed FAISS k=2 GPT-4o results are 0.83 relevance, 0.69 utilization, 0.82 completeness, and 0.04 hallucinated responses.

RAG Case Study is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

RAG Case Study

The RAG Case Study uses prompt templates to evaluate question answering with and without provided context. Its comprehensive results are reported in Table 6.

DeBERTa model training

The RAG case study reports that FAISS configurations generally have higher average relevance than corresponding TF-IDF configurations, and that k=2 generally outperforms k=4 on relevance. Among the listed configurations, FAISS with k=2, the long + CoT prompt, and GPT-3.5-turbo-0125 has 0.81 average relevance, 0.64 average utilization, 0.76 average completeness, and 0.07 percent hallucinated responses, while the corresponding GPT-4o configuration has 0.83, 0.69, 0.82, and 0.04.
