conquest of Canada in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The conquest of Canada by British arms during the Seven Years' War placed French inhabitants under British rule, creating possible prolonged conflict with British conquerors. Most French inhabitants stayed, however, and relations between French and English Canadians were generally peaceful.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{1} CHAPTER I CANADIANS, OLD AND NEW The conquest of Canada by British arms in the Seven Years' War gave rise to a situation in the colony which was fraught with tragic possibilities.

The conquest of Canada created the possibility of prolonged conflict between French inhabitants and the British conquerors. Instead, most French inhabitants stayed, and relations between French and English Canadians were generally peaceful.
