Abstract written by a model

Queen Marcia is the dark-skinned queen of Marshland, living in the Royal Hut with wild, uncombed hair, a swamp-grass skirt, marshmallows, attendants, and two contrasting eyes: one large, brown, lively, and turned inward, and one small, blue, and turned outward. Sister of Mira and formerly married to the previous Marshland king, she rules the Marshes and swears to marry the first stranger entering her kingdom, offering him the monarchy. When Sir Hokus arrives, she declares him her intended bridegroom, orders him treated as King and fed royal duck. After he refuses and will not salute her, she attacks him with tiger lilies and cat-tails; he escapes through a window.

CHAPTER TWO

Queen Marcia rules Marshland and has sworn to marry the first stranger who enters her kingdom. Getsom says that she has one wonderful eye, while Sir Hokus refuses the proposed marriage.

CHAPTER THREE

Queen Marcia sits in the Royal Hut eating marshmallows when Sir Hokus is brought before her. She declares that she has sworn to marry the first stranger who enters her Kingdom, orders him to be treated as King, and has him conducted to an apartment after ordering that he be served royal duck.

CHAPTER FOUR

Marcia enters with marsh maidens and a bouquet of tiger lilies and cat-tails, intending to have Sir Hokus salute her as Queen. Angered by his rejection, she attacks him with the bouquet, but he escapes through the window.
