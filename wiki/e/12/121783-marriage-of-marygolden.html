marriage of Marygolden in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

The marriage of Marygolden and Sir Hokus is planned to occur at once in Corabia. After Sir Hokus urges an immediate ceremony, it is solemnized at high noon with music, royal magnificence, and many guests; afterward, Speedy says goodbye and returns home.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

After the disenchantment, Sir Hokus asks Speedy to stay for the wedding, and the King encourages him to do so. Sir Hokus insists that the marriage take place at once, but the ceremony has not occurred by the end of the unit.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The marriage of Marygolden and Sir Hokus is solemnized at high noon with music, magnificence, and many royal guests. After the celebration, Speedy says goodbye and returns home.
