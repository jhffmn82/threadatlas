Hamadryads in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hamadryads are wood-nymphs bound to their abode trees, beginning and perishing with them, rewarding services and punishing injuries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XV

Hamadryads are another name for the wood-nymphs who come into existence with and perish with their trees. They can appreciate services as well as punish injuries, as shown by the stories of Erisichthon and Rhoecus.
