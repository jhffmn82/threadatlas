British constitution

Also called: Our constitution, constitution, constitution of 1791, constitution of Lower Canada, the constitution of 1791, the present constitution of Canada

Abstract written by a model

The British constitution is a model of law and custom studied by French-Canadian Assembly members after 1812. They valued its House of Commons power over the purse and believed Lower Canada lacked comparable powers. Papineau first admired it and British rule, but by 1832 regarded it as bad while increasingly opposing colonial government.

{21} CHAPTER IV THE RISE OF PAPINEAU In the year 1812 a young man took his seat in the House of Assembly for Lower Canada who was destined to play a conspicuous part in the history of the province during the next quarter of a century.

French-Canadian members of the Assembly studied the law and customs of the British constitution and concluded that they lacked the powers enjoyed by the House of Commons in England. Papineau initially expressed admiration for the constitution and the blessings of British rule, but later opposed the colonial government more strongly.
