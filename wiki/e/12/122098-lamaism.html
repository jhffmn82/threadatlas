Lamaism

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Lamaism is a form of Buddhism with convents for priests and nuns, processions, and elaborate worship; its resemblance to Roman Catholic institutions led missionaries to call it degenerated Christianity, possibly influenced by Nestorian Christians in Tartary.

BUDDHA

Lamaism is described as a form of Buddhism with convents for priests and nuns, processions, and elaborate forms of worship. Its similarities to Roman Catholic institutions led some Christian missionaries to regard it as a degenerated Christianity, though the text suggests that some practices may have come from Nestorian Christians.
