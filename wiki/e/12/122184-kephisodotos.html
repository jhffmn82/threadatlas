Kephisodotos was an Athenian sculptor between the earlier school and Scopas and Praxiteles, probably Praxiteles’ father. His famous Eirene and Ploutos preserved the Pheidian ideal while deepening feeling and spiritual life.

Kephisodotos was an Athenian sculptor who stood between the earlier school and Scopas and Praxiteles. His Eirene and Ploutos preserved much of the Pheidian ideal while expressing deeper feeling and more spiritual life, and he is presented as the probable father of Praxiteles.
