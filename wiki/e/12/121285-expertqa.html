ExpertQA

Also called: ExperQA, Expertqa

Abstract written by a model

ExpertQA is a general-knowledge collection of domain-expert-curated questions in science, arts, and law, with expert-curated relevant passages and original LLM-generated responses. Sourced through Google search and expert-composed questions, it is used directly by RAGBench; its 12% hallucination rate is among RAGBench’s highest.

ExpertQA is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
