RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]

ExpertQA contains curated questions from domain experts in science, arts, and law, along with expert-curated relevant passages and LLM-generated responses. RAGBench uses its existing LLM-generated responses directly rather than generating additional ones.
