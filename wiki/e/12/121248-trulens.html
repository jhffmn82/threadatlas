TruLens

system · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Trulens

Abstract written by a model

TruLens is an automated RAG evaluation system using zero-shot LLM prompts to predict curated metrics. It measures Context Relevance and answer faithfulness as Adherence; in the reported comparison, it does not evaluate Utilization.

TruLens is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
