Electra in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Electra, a Pleiad and mother of Dardanus, saves her brother Orestes by sending him to Strophius, urges vengeance for Agamemnon, then recognizes his disguised return amid a pathetic urn meeting.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXI

Electra saved Orestes from the conspirators by sending him secretly to Strophius and repeatedly reminded him of his duty to avenge Agamemnon. She recognized him when he returned in disguise, and Sophocles portrayed their meeting over the supposedly funerary urn as deeply pathetic.
