Abstract written by a model

The parashuter is Uncle Billy's strange lever-and-button-controlled invention, carrying Speedy and Princess Marygolden strapped to it. Speedy releases it from the Skyrocket and activates its handle rocket; it first misses a tunnel and tears through earth, rock, and sand, then carries them upward from underground, bursts through the crust, and lands on a rocky path.

CHAPTER EIGHT

Speedy releases the parashuter from the Skyrocket and points it upward, hoping it will carry him and Princess Marygolden through the shaft. It shoots through the blue stone sky but misses the tunnel and begins tearing through earth, rock, and sand.

CHAPTER THIRTEEN

The parashuter carries Speedy and Marygolden upward from underground after Speedy activates the rocket in its handle. It bursts through the earth's crust and lands them on a rocky path.
