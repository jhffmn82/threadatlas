RoPE

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: RoPE models, rotary phase term, rotary position embedding, rotary position embeddings, rotary position embeddings (RoPE)

Abstract written by a model

RoPE is a position embedding method that encodes relative pair-wise offsets, making absolute token indices irrelevant. It applies position IDs independently to queries and keys, so their interaction depends on positional difference. This supports rearranging IDs when chunk-level caches are stitched: RoPE is applied during key-value-cache precomputation in Composite Positions, then reapplied to cached or stitched key_states with new indices at inference time in Reordered Positions.

RoPE is in one work on this site, TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

Rotary position embeddings are described as depending on relative offsets rather than absolute token indices. This property supports rearranging position IDs when chunk-level caches are stitched.

3 / Methodology .. 3.4 / The TurboRAG Pipeline

RoPE applies position embeddings independently to queries and keys, and the resulting interaction depends on their positional difference. TurboRAG reapplies RoPE to cached keys with new position indices when forming a concatenated KV cache.

B / Positional-Encoding Options in

RoPE is applied during key-value-cache precomputation in Composite Positions and at inference time in Reordered Positions. In the Qwen-2 pseudocode, it is applied through apply_single_rotary_pos_emb on the stitched key_states.
