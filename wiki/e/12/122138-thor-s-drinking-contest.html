Thor's drinking contest in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Thor takes three determined draughts in Utgard-Loki's drinking challenge, fails to empty the horn, and later learns he lowered the sea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Thor competes against Utgard-Loki's drinking challenge and makes three determined draughts from the horn without emptying it. The contest is later explained as a feat against the sea, whose level Thor lowers.
