the memoir of Elsie Inglis in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

A memoir of Elsie Inglis, compiled from family, friends, patients, Units, letters, and stories, seeks a transparently honest, affectionate, faithful portrait, tracing her life from its opening footsteps through the sands of time.

Narrative, section by section

’ PREFACE The story of Elsie Inglis needs little introduction.

The memoir is written among Elsie Inglis’s family and kindred and draws on letters and stories from her sisters, friends, patients, and Units. It aims to follow her footprints faithfully and present a consistent portrait of her life.
