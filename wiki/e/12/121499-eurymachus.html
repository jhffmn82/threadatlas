Abstract written by a model

Eurymachus, Antenor’s brave Trojan warrior son, fights beside Aeneas, is encouraged by Apollo, helps rout the Greeks, then is driven back in their counterattack.

BOOK XI

Eurymachus fights shoulder to shoulder with Aeneas and is encouraged by Apollo in the shape of Polymestor. He helps rout the Greeks and pursues them with his spear until the Greek counterattack forces the Trojans back.
