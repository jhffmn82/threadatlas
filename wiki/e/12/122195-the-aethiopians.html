the Aethiopians in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

The Aethiopians, a countless swarthy host led by Memnon, arrive at Troy, fight as chief Trojan warriors, and kill many Danaans. After Memnon’s death, they mourn him until Eos transforms them into birds that wail around his barrow.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK II

The Aethiopians arrive at Troy with Memnon in a countless host. They fight as the chief warriors on the Trojan side and kill many Danaans.

Then to the side of Aeacus' mighty son

The Aethiopians fight as Memnon's comrades and mourn when their king is killed. Eos changes them into birds that fly around Memnon's barrow, wailing for him.

the Aethiopians in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The AEthiopians live near the River Ocean on the earth’s southern side, are happy and virtuous, favored by Olympian gods who share their sacrifices and banquets, and raise a tomb for Memnon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter I

The AEthiopians were said to live near the River Ocean on the southern side of the earth. They were happy and virtuous, and the gods sometimes left Olympus to share their sacrifices and banquets.
