CHAPTER 14

The Swingers are the aerial citizens of Swing City. They toss Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the others from swing to swing, stretch Belfaygor's beard into a tightrope, and are frightened into releasing their captives by the screams from Mogodore's sauce box.
