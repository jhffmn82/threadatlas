Abstract written by a model

Shrinking violets are food given to Snif by Peter. After Snif eats them, apparently unintentionally, they shrink him to no bigger than a squirrel, making him worry he cannot carry the others. Their effect eventually wears off, restoring his size and strength and breaking open the dungeon door.
