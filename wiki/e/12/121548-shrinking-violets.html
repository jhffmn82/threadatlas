shrinking violets in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Shrinking violets are food given to Snif by Peter. After Snif eats them, apparently unintentionally, they shrink him to no bigger than a squirrel, making him worry he cannot carry the others. Their effect eventually wears off, restoring his size and strength and breaking open the dungeon door.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The shrinking violets cause Snif to shrink after Peter gives them to him as food. Snif becomes no bigger than a squirrel and worries that he is no longer useful for carrying the others.

CHAPTER 8

The violets were eaten by Snif, apparently unintentionally, and caused him to shrink.

CHAPTER 12

The effect of the shrinking violets finally wears off, causing Snif to expand rapidly to his former size and strength. His expansion tears the dungeon door from its hinges and bends the bars outward.
