Abstract written by a model

The records concern Elsie Inglis’s family and kindred, the Inglis family of Kingsmills in Inverness-shire. Descended from George Inglis’s Inverness branch, the family included generations who travelled or served abroad, notably in Carolina and India; its letters show energy, exploration, religious faith, and theological reflection. After Mr. Inglis retired, the family left India, with Sona, a household member and nurse, and spent two years in Hobart, Tasmania, settling two sons on land. They then made Edinburgh home at 70 Bruntsfield Place, where Elsie and Eva attended school and Elsie’s education in Paris was supported.

The Inglis family originated at Kingsmills in Inverness-shire and included generations who travelled or served abroad, especially in Carolina and India. Family letters are described as energetic and exploratory, while also showing strong religious faith and frequent theological reflection.

CHAPTER III THE LADDER OF LEARNING 1876-1885 ‘Hast thou come with the heart of thy childhood back: The free, the pure, the kind?

The Inglis family left India after Mr. Inglis retired, spent two years in Hobart settling two sons on the land, and then made Edinburgh their home. The family lived at 70 Bruntsfield Place, sent Elsie and Eva to school, and supported Elsie’s education in Paris.
