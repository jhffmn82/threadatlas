Lord Durham's Report on the Affairs of British North America in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Durham's Report, issued by the Colonial Office in 1839 after rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada, attributes them chiefly to racial feeling and representative yet irresponsible government. It recommends legislative union and responsible government, though it was considered unfair to French Canadians.

{104} CHAPTER X THE LORD HIGH COMMISSIONER The rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada profoundly affected public opinion in the mother country.

Durham's Report, issued by the Colonial Office in 1839, attributed the rebellion mainly to racial feeling and the union of representative and irresponsible government. It recommended legislative union and responsible government, but was considered unfair to French Canadians.
