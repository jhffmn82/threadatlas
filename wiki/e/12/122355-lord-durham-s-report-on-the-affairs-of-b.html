Lord Durham's Report on the Affairs of British North America

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: _Report_, famous _Report on the Affairs of British North America_

Abstract written by a model

Durham's Report, issued by the Colonial Office in 1839 after rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada, attributes them chiefly to racial feeling and representative yet irresponsible government. It recommends legislative union and responsible government, though it was considered unfair to French Canadians.
