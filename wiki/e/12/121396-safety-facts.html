Abstract—Large Language Model (LLM) agents have

Safety facts about various unsafe prompts are provided to LLMs by SafetyRAG during generation. They are intended to enhance the models’ decision-making and safe behavior.

I. INTRODUCTION

A safety fact is explicit information provided to the LLM about a harmful behavior, such as stating that the behavior is illegal and conflicts with human values. SafetyRAG uses this information to strengthen the aligned model's awareness of human values.

IV. RESULT AND DISCUSSION .. C. Discussion

Safety facts were constructed from the selected unsafe prompts and retrieved from a knowledge base by SafetyRAG. The models used the retrieved facts to augment their responses and maintain safer behavior, although Mistral-7B sometimes placed the facts after an otherwise harmful answer.
