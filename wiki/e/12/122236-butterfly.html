butterfly in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

A butterfly symbolizes immortality of the soul, emerging on brilliant wings from a tomb after a dull caterpillar existence, presenting the soul’s transformation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The fable of Cupid and Psyche is usually considered allegorical.

The Greek name for a butterfly is Psyche, and the butterfly symbolizes the immortality of the soul. Its emergence on brilliant wings from a tomb after a dull caterpillar existence is presented as an image of the soul’s transformation.
