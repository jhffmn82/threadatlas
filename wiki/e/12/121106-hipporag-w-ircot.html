HippoRAG w/ IRCoT

method · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

HippoRAG w/ IRCoT is used as a multi-step retrieval comparison. On MuSiQue, GEAR shows a 21% relative improvement in R@15 and a 37% relative improvement in both EM and F1 compared with HippoRAG w/ IRCoT. HippoRAG w/ IRCoT generally requires at least two iterations to reach maximum performance and uses more LLM tokens than GEAR. GEAR has a higher mean recall on 2-hop questions and outperforms its median performance across both 3-hop subdivisions, while the two methods have similar interquartile ranges on 2-hop questions.
