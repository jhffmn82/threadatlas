  • In GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation: HippoRAG w/ IRCoT is used as a multi-step retrieval comparison. On MuSiQue, GEAR shows a 21% relative improvement in R@15 and a 37% relative improvement in both EM and F1 compared with HippoRAG w/ IRCoT.
