elective Legislative Council

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 5 connections
Also called: an agitation to make the Legislative Council elective, elective Legislative Council movement, elective principle, the application of the elective principle to the Legislative Council, the plan for an elective Legislative Council

Abstract written by a model

The elective Legislative Council movement was the Patriote party's chief policy under Papineau amid intensifying racial conflict in Lower Canada. It aimed to replace the nominated Legislative Council and remove its buffering role between the governor's Executive Council and the Assembly. After the Ninety-Two Resolutions and election, the British government rejected the demand; royal commissioners advised against it.

The elective Legislative Council movement became the chief policy of the Patriote party under Papineau. It sought to remove the existing Legislative Council's buffering role between the governor's Executive Council and the Assembly.

{44} CHAPTER VI THE ROYAL COMMISSION A general election followed soon after the passing of the Ninety-Two Resolutions and revealed the strength of Papineau's position in the country.

The demand for an elective Legislative Council was not accepted by the British government. The royal commissioners later advised against introducing one.
