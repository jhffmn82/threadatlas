Chapter XXXII

Thor keeps his mallet ready when Skrymir's snoring and the earthquake frighten the travelers. He uses it three times against Skrymir, whose blows fall on a mountain and make three deep glens, and later throws it at Hrungnir's stone club, breaks the club, and buries the mallet in Hrungnir's skull.
