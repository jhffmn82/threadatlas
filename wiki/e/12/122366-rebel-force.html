rebel force in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

A Lower Canada rebel force commanded by Robert Nelson grew to about 2,500 men at Napierville, mostly carrying pitchforks and pikes, with about 800 later armed with muskets; after defeats at Lacolle and Odelltown, it dispersed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

It grew to about twenty-five hundred men at Napierville, most armed only with pitchforks and pikes, though about eight hundred later had muskets. It suffered defeats at Lacolle and Odelltown before dispersing.
