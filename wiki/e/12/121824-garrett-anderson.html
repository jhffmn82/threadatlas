Abstract written by a model

Garrett Anderson was a leading woman physician and hospital practitioner. During 1892–1894 at London’s New Hospital for Women, she actively and influentially supported Elsie Inglis through advice on maternity work, social inclusion, surgical assistance, and encouragement of her professional development.
