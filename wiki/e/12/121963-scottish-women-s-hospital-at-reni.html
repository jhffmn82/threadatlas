Abstract written by a model

Scottish Women's Hospital at Reni is a hospital run by the unit and operated by Scottish Women's Hospitals. It treats Russian and Serbian soldiers, sailors, and other wounded patients, with icons for Russian religious use, tents for personnel, and care inspected for cleanliness, food, nursing, and medicine. Russian soldiers regard it as the best hospital on the front and defend its needs. It continues work through mud, weather, fuel shortages, and a spy investigation.

’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

The hospital at Reni treats soldiers and sailors from different parts of Russia and is inspected for cleanliness, food, nursing, and medical care. It receives religious blessings, gives Easter gifts to patients, and continues operating despite mud, weather, fuel shortages, and the spy investigation.

‘_On Active Service.

The Scottish Women’s Hospital at Reni treats Russian and Serbian patients, including wounded soldiers and a ten-year-old boy. Russian soldiers defend its reputation and insist that the Commandant provide whatever it needs.
