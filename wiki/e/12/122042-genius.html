Genius in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Genius is a spirit believed by Romans to give every man being, protect him throughout life, and receive offerings from men on birthdays.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

The Romans believed that every man had a Genius, a spirit who had given him being and protected him throughout life. Men made offerings to their Genius on their birthdays.
