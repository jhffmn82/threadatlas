the tunnel to Baffleburg in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

A narrow, dark, clammy, rough, unstable rocky tunnel descends unpredictably toward a precipice. After Peter and the others steady themselves by holding on and Peter grips an iron ring, it rights itself; Peter opens its wooden door to Baffleburg's cobblestone courtyard.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

The tunnel is dark, clammy, and rough underfoot, and it unexpectedly tips downward, throwing the adventurers toward the precipice. On a second attempt, Peter and the others hold onto one another and Peter grips the iron ring until the tunnel rights itself. Peter then opens the wooden door at its end, leading into a cobblestone courtyard.
