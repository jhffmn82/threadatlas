Also called: HeteQA benchmark, HeteQA dataset
  • In TableRAG: A Retrieval Augmented Generation Framework for Heterogeneous Document Reasoning: HeteQA is a new, curated benchmark for table-based and heterogeneous document question answering, designed to assess multi-hop reasoning over textual and tabular information. It has 304 high-quality examples across nine semantically diverse domains, spanning 136 tables and 5314 wiki knowledge entities. Each instance includes a query, corresponding answer, executable SQL sentence, and execution-derived answer; examples compose five principal tabular operations and can require arithmetic computation and nested logic. Its structural and semantic breadth makes it a resource for advancing question answering over heterogeneous documents. HeteQA is used to evaluate and analyze TableRAG, TableGPT2, and ReAct in single-source, multi-source, and overall settings. TableRAG outperforms existing baselines, and SQL execution is especially influential because HeteQA queries involve nested operations. The benchmark is restricted to English, with multilingual extension intended for future work.

Abstract

HeteQA is a novel benchmark developed to evaluate multi-hop heterogeneous reasoning capabilities. TableRAG outperforms existing baselines on HeteQA.

HeteQA is a benchmark introduced for evaluating multi-hop reasoning over heterogeneous documents. It contains 304 examples across nine diverse domains, with each example composing five distinct tabular operations.

HeteQA is presented as a benchmark for assessing multi-hop reasoning across heterogeneous documents. It contains 304 high-quality examples spanning 136 distinct tables and 5314 wiki knowledge entities.

5 / 5.1 / 5.1.1 .. 5.3

HeteQA is one of the benchmarks used to evaluate TableRAG, with results separated into single-source, multi-source, and overall settings. TableRAG obtains overall scores of 44.19 with Claude-3.5, 44.85 with DeepSeek-V3, and 38.82 with Qwen-2.5-72B. The ablation study finds SQL execution particularly influential on HeteQA because its queries involve nested operations.

HeteQA is the evaluation setting used for the error analysis of TableRAG, TableGPT2, and ReAct with DeepSeek-V3 and Qwen-2.5-72b backbones.

HeteQA is a benchmark introduced to rigorously assess systems handling heterogeneous textual and tabular data. Its evaluations show strong performance by TableRAG, but the benchmark is currently restricted to English.
