Sir George Prevost in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Sir George Prevost succeeds Sir James Craig as governor of Canada, reverses Craig’s harsh policy, and seeks to conciliate French Canadians. By restoring Pierre Bedard’s militia rank and appointing him judge, he helps reduce racial bitterness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{13} CHAPTER III 'THE REIGN OF TERROR' During the session of 1805 the Assembly was confronted with the apparently innocent problem of building prisons.

Sir George Prevost succeeded Sir James Craig and completely reversed Craig’s policy by seeking to conciliate the French Canadians. He restored Pierre Bedard to his militia rank and made him a judge, helping reduce racial bitterness.
