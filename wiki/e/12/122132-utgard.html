Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Utgard is a lofty city in the middle of a plain, populated by men of prodigious stature and ruled by Utgard-Loki. Thor, Thialfi, and Loki enter its palace, compete there, spend the night, and find the city gone when Thor tries to return and destroy it.
