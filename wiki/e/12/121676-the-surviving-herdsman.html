Abstract written by a model

The surviving herdsman is Laius’s serf, household thrall, and shepherd. Sole survivor of Laius’s death, he returns to Thebes and reports the murder. Sent to mountain pastures by Jocasta, he receives her infant Oedipus to be destroyed because of a prophecy, but pities and spares him, giving him to the Corinthian Messenger. Oedipus later orders him brought back for questioning.

CREON.

The surviving herdsman, described as a serf or bondsman, was the sole survivor who returned after Laius’s death and reported what happened. Jocasta had sent him away to the mountain pastures, and Oedipus orders that he be brought back for questioning.

[Enter JOCASTA.]

Laius’s Herdsman confirms that he tended sheep for Laius on Cithaeron. He admits that he received Laius’s child from within Laius’s household and gave the infant to the Corinthian Messenger. He says the child was intended to be destroyed because of the prophecy that he would kill his father, but that he spared him out of pity.
