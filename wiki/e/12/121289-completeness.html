Completeness in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16

Completeness is a generator quality metric introduced in TRACe. It measures how well a response incorporates relevant context, calculated as relevant substrings that are also utilized divided by all relevant substrings. Derived from span-level Relevance and Utilization annotations, it can rise with detailed chain-of-thought prompting and differs from Utilization because irrelevant use can still yield high Utilization.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

TRACe Evaluation Framework .. Adherence

Completeness measures how well the response incorporates relevant information from the context. It is calculated as the fraction of relevant substrings that are also utilized, and it differs from Utilization because a system can use irrelevant information while still having high Utilization.

RAGBench Statistics .. Alignment with Human Judgements

Completeness is derived from span-level Relevance and Utilization annotations rather than being explicitly requested from the LLM annotator. Detailed chain-of-thought prompting is associated with higher completeness rates in the reported RAG system example.
