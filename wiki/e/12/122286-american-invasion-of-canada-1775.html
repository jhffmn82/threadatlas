The American invasion of Canada in 1775, led by Benedict Arnold and Richard Montgomery, entered a colony shaped by British conquest; most habitants stayed at least passively loyal, few joined the invaders, and many enlisted under Carleton.

During the American invasion of Canada in 1775, Arnold and Montgomery invaded the colony. Most habitants remained at least passively loyal, while a few hundred may have joined the invaders and a much larger number enlisted under Carleton.
