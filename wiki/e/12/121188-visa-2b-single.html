VISA-2B-single

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

VISA-2B-single is a single oracle candidate fine-tuned on one oracle document image to generate answers and bounding boxes. It uses random cropping outside boxes and absolute coordinates, achieving Wiki-VISA box/answer scores of 23.8/65.3 and Paper-VISA scores of 63.0/38.3.

VISA-2B-single is in one work on this site, VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
