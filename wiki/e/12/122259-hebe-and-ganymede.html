Hebe and Ganymede in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hebe and Ganymede is Crawford's sculptural group in the Boston Athenaeum, presenting Hebe's fall and dismissal and Ganymede's ascent to succeed her as cupbearer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XII

Hebe and Ganymede are presented in an account adopted by the sculptor Crawford, in which Ganymede succeeds Hebe as cupbearer of the gods. Hebe was dismissed after a fall while serving the gods, and Ganymede was carried to heaven to take the vacant position.
