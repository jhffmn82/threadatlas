CHAPTER 8

The beard-crossing plan requires Belfaygor to walk three times around a tree so his beard will be held fast, while Snif flies the beard's end across the chasm and ties it to a tree. Peter and Jack will cross first, and Belfaygor will cut himself free and cross afterward.
