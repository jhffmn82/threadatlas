Abstract written by a model

Post-retrieval is a component of DioR that replaces single-batch document selection with dynamic, stepwise retrieval. It selects documents, extracts emerging keywords for additional retrieval, and segments selected documents into semantically coherent blocks. Removing it includes all documents in one prompt and yields declining reported scores.

post-retrieval is in one work on this site, DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Post-retrieval replaces single-batch document selection with a dynamic optimization strategy. It selects documents stepwise, extracts newly emerging keywords, performs additional retrieval, and segments selected documents into semantically coherent blocks.

Experiment / Experimental Setups .. Ablation Experiment

Post-retrieval contributes to the retrieval process by allowing more than a single-batch retrieval setup. Removing it leads to single-batch retrieval in which all documents are included in the prompt, and the reported scores decline.
