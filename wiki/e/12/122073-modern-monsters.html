Modern Monsters in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Modern Monsters are imaginary beings succeeding older superstition, chiefly described in old natural history books and travellers’ narrations, and continuing in popular belief after Christianity superseded Paganism.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXIX

Modern Monsters are presented as imaginary beings that succeeded the Gorgons, Hydras, and Chimeras of older superstition. Their accounts come chiefly from old natural history books and travelers’ narratives rather than ancient poetry.
