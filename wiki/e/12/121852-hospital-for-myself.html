‘If I don’t get into the Infirmary in Edinburgh, I mean to build a hospital for

The speaker intends to build this hospital if they do not get into the Infirmary in Edinburgh. It is to be built where the Cattle Market is, at the head of Lady Lawson Street, and is intended to be convenient for women in Fountainbridge, the Grassmarket, and Cowgate; initially, Eva’s hall is to be rented as a dispensary.
