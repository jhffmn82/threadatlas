Quick City, a city of the Quix visited before the forest, has moving buildings and inhabitants who run and age rapidly; Speedy is crowned, then escapes with Marygolden before the Quix regrow.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER THIRTEEN

Quick City contains buildings that roll about like taxis, while its inhabitants constantly run and rapidly grow older and younger. Speedy is crowned there, but he and Marygolden escape before the Quix fully grow up again.
