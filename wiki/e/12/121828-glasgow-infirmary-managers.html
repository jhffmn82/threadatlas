Glasgow Infirmary Managers in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Glasgow Infirmary Managers opposed women students attending mixed classes, even after full-course fees were paid. Their decision prompted organized resistance and was eventually abandoned.

’ In 1891 Elsie went to Glasgow to take the examination for the Triple Qualifica

The Glasgow Infirmary Managers repeatedly opposed women students attending mixed classes, including after the students had paid fees for the full medical course. Their decision led to organized resistance and was eventually abandoned.
