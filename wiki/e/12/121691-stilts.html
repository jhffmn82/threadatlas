stilts in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Stilts are used by Marshlanders, Mud Guards, and Stick-in-the-Muds to stay out of mud and travel Marshland; left by houses and brandished as threats, they are later knocked down and trampled into mire, preventing pursuit of Sir Hokus.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER THREE

The Marshlanders use stilts to keep out of the mud and to travel across Marshland. They leave them standing against their houses when they go inside, and the Mud Guards brandish them threateningly.

CHAPTER FOUR

Ploppa knocks down the stilts for miles around and tramps them into the mire. This leaves Marcia, her court, and the Stick-in-the-Muds unable to pursue Sir Hokus.
