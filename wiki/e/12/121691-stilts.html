CHAPTER THREE

The Marshlanders use stilts to keep out of the mud and to travel across Marshland. They leave them standing against their houses when they go inside, and the Mud Guards brandish them threateningly.

CHAPTER FOUR

Ploppa knocks down the stilts for miles around and tramps them into the mire. This leaves Marcia, her court, and the Stick-in-the-Muds unable to pursue Sir Hokus.
