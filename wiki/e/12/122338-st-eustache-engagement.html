St Eustache engagement in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The St Eustache engagement began on December 14 as Sir John Colborne's force approached the village. Rebels barricaded several buildings, but troops broke the defenses; about seventy rebels, including Dr J. O. Chenier, were reportedly killed, while troops suffered one death and nine wounded.

The St Eustache engagement began on December 14 when Sir John Colborne's force approached the village. Rebels barricaded the church, convent, presbytery, and Scott's house, but the troops broke their defenses; about seventy rebels were reportedly killed, including Chenier, while the troops lost one killed and nine wounded.
