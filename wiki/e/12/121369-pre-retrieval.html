Abstract written by a model

Pre-retrieval is a component of DioR’s contextual retrieval process. It analyzes candidate keywords with attention, TF-IDF, positional scores, and semantic similarity, ranks their importance, and uses the highest-ranked tokens with BM25 to retrieve candidate documents from an external knowledge base. Removing it instead retrieves tokens sequentially and lowers reported dataset scores.

pre-retrieval is in one work on this site, DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
