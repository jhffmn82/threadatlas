SKETCH OF THE HISTORY OF GREEK SCULPTURE

Polycleitos, a pupil of Ageladas, excelled in representations of human bodily beauty and sought perfection of form. His important works included the Doryphoros, the Diadumenos, the Amazon, and a chryselephantine Hera.
