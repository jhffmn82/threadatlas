Penthesileia's dream in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

A deceptive dream sent by Athena appears as Penthesileia’s father, urges her to fight Achilles, promises success, and leads her unknowingly toward destruction.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK I:

Athena sends Penthesileia a deceptive dream in which a figure resembling her father urges her to fight Achilles. Penthesileia believes the dream promises great success and goes to battle, unaware that it is leading her toward destruction.
