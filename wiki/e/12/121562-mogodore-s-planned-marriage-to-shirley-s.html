Mogodore's planned marriage to Shirley Sunshine in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Mogodore plans to force Princess Shirley Sunshine, despite her refusal and threats, to accompany him to the Emerald City, marry him instead of Belfaygor, and be crowned with Ozma's crown.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

Mogodore intends to force the Princess to marry him, although she refuses and threatens that her father and Belfaygor will destroy him. After Shirley prompts his ambition, he declares that she will accompany him to the Emerald City, where they will marry and she will be crowned with Ozma's crown.

CHAPTER 11

Mogodore declares that Princess Shirley Sunshine will marry him instead of Belfaygor. He plans to marry her in the capitol after reaching the Emerald City.
