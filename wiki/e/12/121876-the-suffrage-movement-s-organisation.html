Abstract written by a model

The Suffrage movement’s organisation was a federated political movement across social, professional, and industrial spheres, coordinated to pressure Parliament. It expanded through societies, workers, fundraising, meetings, debates, and a press campaign; Dr. Elsie Inglis’s administration and opportunism were important. Before the war it built efficient committees, organisers, speakers, and political networks, then redirected them from suffrage campaigning to national service and hospital units.
