Catholic clergy in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Catholic clergy in Canada remained loyal to British rule during the American invasion, the wars of 1775 and 1812, and under Mgr Briand, who was devoted to Great Britain. Though Haldimand later thought their views had changed, this was attributed to dissatisfaction with his rule. They later withdrew from Papineau over his republican and revolutionary ideas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{1} CHAPTER I CANADIANS, OLD AND NEW The conquest of Canada by British arms in the Seven Years' War gave rise to a situation in the colony which was fraught with tragic possibilities.

The clergy remained firmly loyal to Great Britain in 1775 and used their influence in its favor. Haldimand later claimed that they had changed their opinions, but the text attributes his impression to dissatisfaction with his high-handed rule and notes that Mgr Briand was devoted to Great Britain.
