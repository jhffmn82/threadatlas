VISA-7B-multi is fine-tuned with three document candidates to identify a relevant document, generate an answer, and predict its evidence bounding box. In the Oracle in Candidates setting, it achieves Wiki-VISA average bounding box and answer scores of 22.2 and 44.1, and Paper-VISA average scores of 59.9 and 39.2.
