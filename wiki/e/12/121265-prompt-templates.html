RAG Case Study

The prompt templates include NO CONTEXT, SHORT, LONG, and LONG + CoT. The LONG templates instruct the chatbot to use the documents, state when required information is missing, avoid unsupported quotations, and not make up answers; LONG + CoT additionally asks it to think step by step and explain its reasoning.
