The second rebellion in Lower Canada in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The second rebellion was a rebellion in Lower Canada that began in November 1838 after prolonged agitation along the American border, then quickly collapsed after defeats at Lacolle and Odelltown, lasting scarcely a week.

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

It arose from prolonged agitation along the American border rather than from Lord Durham's departure. Beginning in November 1838, it quickly collapsed after defeats at Lacolle and Odelltown and lasted scarcely a week.
