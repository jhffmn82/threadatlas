oracle concerning Lavinia's marriage in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Faunus’s dream oracle tells Latinus that Lavinia must marry a foreigner and bear a world-subduing race; Latinus identifies Aeneas as her destined husband despite her parents’ preference for Turnus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVI

The oracle warns Latinus that Lavinia's destined husband will come from a foreign land and that their union will produce a race destined to subdue the world. Latinus concludes that Aeneas is the promised husband, despite the preference of Lavinia's parents for Turnus.
