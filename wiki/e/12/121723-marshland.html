Marshland in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Marshland is a wild, desolate, treacherous, foggy swamp after the forest, ruled by Queen Marcia and inhabited by Stick-in-the-Muds and frog goblins. Its bog nearly swallows Sir Hokus; higher, dryer clearings hold tree dwellings and the Royal Hut. Sir Hokus then leaves, crossing a wide yellow plain toward a strange castle.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER TWO

Marshland is a wild, desolate swamp reached after the forest. Its treacherous bog nearly swallows Sir Hokus, and it is ruled by Queen Marcia.

CHAPTER THREE

Marshland is a muddy region inhabited by Stick-in-the-Muds and frog goblins. It contains higher, dryer patches of ground, tree-top dwellings, and Queen Marcia's Royal Hut.

CHAPTER FOUR

The Marshes contain Queen Marcia's hut, tree-houses, mud, fog, frog goblins, and tree-dwelling Stick-in-the-Muds. Sir Hokus leaves the Marshes and crosses a wide yellow plain toward a strange castle.
