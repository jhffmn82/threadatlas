Argive shield-wall in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Argive shield-wall is a locked, close-welded battle formation protecting Argives from missiles. At dawn, they raise shields rooflike overhead; it resists stones briefly before Aeneas' enormous stones shatter it and daze them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

When o'er the streams of Ocean Dawn drove up

The Argives lock their shields above their heads and stand shoulder to shoulder in a formation compared to a solid roof. The shield-wall withstands falling stones and missiles for a time, but Aeneas' enormous stones break it into fragments and daze the men behind it.
