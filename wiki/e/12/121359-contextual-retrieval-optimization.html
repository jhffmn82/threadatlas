Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Contextual Retrieval Optimization is a DioR component that retrieves documents step by step, prioritizes query keywords through contextual analysis, and uses newly retrieved concepts to refine later queries. It also uses sentence-level chunking to reduce the effects of long texts on model understanding and inference.

Related Work

Contextual retrieval optimization is identified as one of the two technical aspects of the DioR framework. The text does not provide further details about its operation.

Contextual retrieval optimization improves retrieval through pre-retrieval keyword selection and post-retrieval document optimization. It ranks candidate tokens, conducts stepwise retrieval, and segments retrieved documents into semantically coherent blocks.
