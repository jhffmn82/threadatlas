Abstract written by a model

Ymir, a Frost giant born from vapors when warm wind melted ice, was slain by Odin, Vili, and Ve; his body became the world.

Chapter XXXI

Ymir was the Frost giant who sprang from vapors formed when warm wind melted the ice. Odin, Vili, and Ve slew him and made the earth, seas, mountains, trees, heavens, and clouds from his body.
