A chariot race among Menelaus, Eurypylus, Eumelus, Thoas, and Polypoetes begins with Eumelus leading; Menelaus wins, while Thoas and Eurypylus fall and are wounded.

Then for the archery-test Oileus' son

Menelaus, Eurypylus, Eumelus, Thoas, and Polypoetes compete in the chariot race. Eumelus leads at first, but Menelaus wins through skill; Thoas and Eurypylus are wounded when they fall from their cars.
