prefill computation in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

Prefill computation computes a KV cache for an entire concatenated document set online and is a critical generation-pipeline bottleneck. As chunks are added, conventional prefill repeats work and incurs quadratic cost. TurboRAG shifts chunk-level cache computation offline, then loads and reuses precomputed passage caches online, reducing or eliminating retrieved-content prefill time.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

The conventional prefill paradigm computes one KV cache for a concatenated document set online, causing redundant computation and quadratic cost as more chunks are added. TurboRAG decomposes prefill into offline and online phases by precomputing chunk-level caches.

2 / Related Work

TurboRAG reduces prefill computation by loading precomputed passage caches from storage instead of computing long-context prefill online. Its approach targets elimination of online prefill computation for retrieved content, whereas speculative methods primarily reduce decoding FLOPs.

5 / Conclusion and Discussion .. Limitations

Prefill computation is identified as a critical bottleneck in the generation pipeline. TurboRAG reduces the time required for it by reusing pre-computed KV caches.
