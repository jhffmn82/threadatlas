Dr. MacEwan in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Dr. MacEwan was a first-rate surgeon and capital teacher at the Royal Infirmary of Glasgow. In 1891 he taught, supervised, questioned, and encouraged Elsie Inglis, letting her assist in surgery. He also advocated common medical education and women’s professional equality, supporting mixed classes and the same schools for women and men.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ In 1891 Elsie went to Glasgow to take the examination for the Triple Qualifica

Dr. MacEwan taught and supervised Elsie Inglis in the wards, questioned her closely, allowed her to assist with an operation, and encouraged her surgical training. He supported the women students during the dispute over mixed classes and believed women should ultimately attend the same schools as men.
