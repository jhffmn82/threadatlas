the tilting-tower spear defense in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

A city defense operated by Baffleburghers using machinery: four wall towers tilt and shower the courtyard with golden spears; adventurers crawl safely, until Belfaygor enters a tower window, stopping it and opening a beard route.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

Four towers on Baffleburg's walls tilt downward and fire showers of golden spears into the courtyard. The adventurers avoid injury by lying flat and crawling close to the walls, but the defense stops after Belfaygor jumps through a tower window. Its interruption leaves a possible route into the city through Belfaygor's beard.
