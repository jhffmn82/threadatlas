is_a agentic framework [for multi-turn interactions with the graph-enhanced retriever] The multi-step extension models an iterative information-seeking process in which the original question is decomposed into simpler queries. At each step, it retrieves passages, updates gist memory, assesses answerability, and either terminates or produces another query.
