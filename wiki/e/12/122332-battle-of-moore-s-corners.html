battle of Moore's Corners in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The battle of Moore's Corners was a night encounter in which about two hundred Patriote invaders met Captain Kemp's larger loyalist force. After a ten- or fifteen-minute fusillade, the rebels retreated, leaving casualties, two field-pieces, arms, ammunition, and standards, while loyalists suffered none and St Johns was spared a more serious encounter.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{89} But the end of rebellion was not yet in sight.

The battle of Moore's Corners began when about two hundred Patriote invaders encountered Captain Kemp's larger loyalist force at night. The rebels withdrew after a brief fusillade, leaving one dead, two wounded, two field-pieces, arms, ammunition, and standards; the encounter caused no loyalist casualties.
