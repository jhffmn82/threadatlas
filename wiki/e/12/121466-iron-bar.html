iron bar in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Iron bar, a massive athletic prize once owned by Antaeus, was taken by Hercules, given to Peleus, and passed to Achilles; so heavy that only Aias can hurl it, he throws it far in an archery-test.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then for the archery-test Oileus' son

The iron bar is so heavy that no Argive except Aias can hurl it, and two men can scarcely lift it. It once belonged to Antaeus, was taken by Hercules and given to Peleus, and then passed to Achilles before Aias throws it far.
