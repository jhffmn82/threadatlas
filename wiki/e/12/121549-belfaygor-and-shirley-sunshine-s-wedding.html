Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine's wedding in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine's wedding is satisfactorily arranged and prepared, but Mogodore abducts Shirley Sunshine. It is ultimately solemnized by Ozma's highest judge during a feast, followed by Ozade toasts and gifts of emeralds.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

The marriage of Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine has been satisfactorily arranged, and Belfaygor has prepared his palace and retainers for the wedding. The marriage is threatened when Mogodore abducts Shirley Sunshine.

CHAPTER 20

Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine's wedding is solemnized by the highest judge in Ozma's court during the feast. They are toasted in tall tumblers of Ozade and showered with emeralds and other gifts.
