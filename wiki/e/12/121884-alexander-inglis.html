Alexander Inglis

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Alexander, Alexander Inglis of Charleston

Abstract written by a model

Alexander Inglis was a merchant and property owner in Charleston. Around 1780 he emigrated to Carolina after marrying Mary Deas, leaving their four children to be educated in Scotland. He owned slaves and a pew in St. Michael’s Church, and supported the British Government and British constitution during the American conflict. His life ended in Charleston in 1791, when he was killed from wounds received in a duel.
