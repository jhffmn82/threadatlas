  • In Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study: RAGPR is a retrieval-augmented generation-based physician recommendation and triage model for web-based medical services. Developed and evaluated with a consultation records dataset, it aims to improve triage, personalize recommendations, and help patients identify suitable medical professionals by matching patient queries and symptom descriptions to physicians’ specialties. Its main components are document retrieval and ingestion and generation of user queries and responses: it embeds documents and queries using SBERT, stores document embeddings in Chroma, retrieves six relevant queries with physician information, and uses Mistral as its selected large language model to generate department and physician recommendations. In a case study, it accurately identified specialties, linking dermatological symptoms to dermatology and gastrointestinal symptoms to gastroenterology. It efficiently filters and selects physicians and may be integrated into healthcare website systems, providing a scalable approach to patient-physician matching. Implementation depends on IT infrastructure, training, privacy protections, interoperability, bias mitigation, and continuous monitoring; future work may add real-time support, specialty-specific applications, personalized medicine, and multimodal data integration.

Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical serv

The Retrieval-Augmented Generation-Based Physician Recommendation model was developed to improve triage performance and personalize physician recommendations. The study concluded that it can provide a scalable approach to improving patient-physician matching.

2 Methods .. 2.5 Baseline experiments

The Retrieval-Augmented Generation-Based Physician Recommendation (RAGPR) model comprises document retrieval and ingestion, along with user-query and response generation. It embeds documents and user queries, stores document embeddings in Chroma, retrieves six relevant queries with physician information, and uses a locally constructed large language model to generate department and physician recommendations.

3 Results / 3.1 Data set summary .. 3.4 Rationality evaluation of LLMs

RAGPR accurately identified medical specialties and recommended corresponding medical departments and physicians from symptom descriptions in a case study. It associated dermatological symptoms with dermatology and gastrointestinal symptoms with gastroenterology.

4 Discussion / 4.1 Principal findings .. 4.5 Limitations and future directions

The RAGPR model is a physician triage algorithm designed to improve web-based medical consultations by matching patients’ queries with physicians’ specialties. Its healthcare implementation depends on suitable IT infrastructure, professional training, privacy protections, interoperability, bias mitigation, and continuous monitoring. Future work may extend it to real-time decision support, specialty-specific applications, personalized medicine, and multimodal data integration.

5 Conclusion

The RAGPR model is designed to improve the performance of triage in web-based medical services. It efficiently filters and selects appropriate physicians.
