Vesta in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Vesta is a deity identified with Hestia who presides over public and private hearths. Her temple’s sacred fire, tended by six virgin priestesses, must not go out; neglect brings severe punishment, and an extinguished fire is rekindled from the sun’s rays.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

Vesta, identified with the Grecian Hestia, presided over the public and private hearth. A sacred fire burned in her temple under the care of six virgin priestesses called Vestals; if the fire went out through their neglect, they were severely punished, and it was rekindled from the rays of the sun.
