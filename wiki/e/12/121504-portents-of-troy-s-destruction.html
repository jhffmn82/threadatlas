portents of Troy's destruction

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: portents of calamity, signs, those portents dire

Abstract written by a model

The signs portending Troy's ruin are ominous sacrifices and worsening prodigies throughout Troy: failed rites, blood-red smoke, weeping statues, gore, cries, moving gates, desolation-crying birds, mist, withered bays, and invading wolves and jackals. The Trojans ignore them and do not fear.

But then a yet more hideous punishment

The sacrifices fail, flames die, smoke turns blood-red, statues weep, temple walls drip gore, and unseen groans and cries fill Troy. The gates move by themselves, birds of night cry 'Desolation,' a mist covers the city, the bays wither, and wolves and jackals enter the gates; these signs all foretell disaster, but the Trojans do not fear.
