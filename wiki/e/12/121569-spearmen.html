spearmen in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The spearmen are rough, boisterous warrior followers of Mogodore, first confined in a garden, then guarding prisoners, escorting Ozma, celebrating the intended marriage, and demanding renewed killing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 18

The spearmen guard the prisoners, escort Ozma into the palace, and celebrate Mogodore's intended marriage. They complain that the conflict has brought them no hangings, bonfires, or killings, and urge Mogodore to marry Ozma so they can resume killing.
