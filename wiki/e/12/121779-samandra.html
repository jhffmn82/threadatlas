Samandra in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Samandra is a very old kingdom and country in the northern Winkie Country of Oz, bordered by the Deadly Desert to the north and the Winkie River to the south, near the country of the Yellow Knight. Its ancient Samandran people live among golden deserts, fertile valleys, orange groves, date palms, and fig trees. Ruled by a Sultan for five hundred years, it sends a grand delegation to the Emerald City and contains the Sultan’s palace, royal camel quarters, golden dock, and Chinda’s telescope tower, whose Chief Seer uses a magic telescope. Its city has gold-domed buildings, a tall spired castle, steep streets, golden steps, and a magnificent throne room. Its defining restriction is that animals cannot talk, though Stampedro can still hear and understand them. Camy is returned there as a former beast of burden, later freed by Sir Hokus; Speedy escapes after stealing Confido. In the end, Ozma returns the Sultan, stripped of magic, to remain there for five hundred years and restore what he stole.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIVE

Samandra is a very old kingdom in the northern part of the Winkie Country, bordered by the Deadly Desert and the Winkie River. It contains golden deserts, fertile valleys, orange groves, date palms, and fig trees, and has been ruled by the Sultan for five hundred years.

CHAPTER SIX

Samandra is ruled by the Sultan and sends a grand delegation to the Emerald City. The Wizard later learns some information about Samandra, its ruler, and its customs from the encyclopedia of Oz.

CHAPTER TWELVE

Samandra is the Sultan's realm, where Camy formerly served as a beast of burden and where he is returned after his capture. The Sultan's palace, royal camel quarters, golden dock, and Chinda's telescope tower are located there.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Speedy realizes that the travellers are in Samandra because animals cannot talk there. Stampedro can still hear and understand them despite being unable to speak.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

The city is reached by Sir Hokus and his companions before sunset. Its gold-domed buildings, tall spired castle, steep streets, golden steps, and magnificent throne room are described, and the Sultan's guards pursue Speedy through the palace.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Samandra is the place from which Speedy escapes after stealing Confido, and where Sir Hokus finds and frees Camy. Camy describes it as a place where he hopes never to return.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Samandra is ruled by the Sultan, who is returned there after Ozma strips him of his magic. The Sultan must remain within Samandra for five hundred years and return his stolen possessions.
