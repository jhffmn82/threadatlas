Also called: Chryselephantine sculpture, chryselephantine work, inlaying of gold and ivory on wood (chryselephantine work)

Abstract written by a model

originates_in Crete Chryselephantine sculpture involved inlaying gold and ivory on wood. It began in Crete and was used for important divine images, including the Hera of Polycleitos and the great statues associated with Pheidias.
