lasts fourth year [because no provision was made for government upkeep] The supply crisis resulted from repeated failures to provide for the upkeep of government between 1833 and 1836. In 1836 the Assembly limited supply to six months and threatened to withhold further funds, while the Legislative Council rejected the supply bill, causing widespread distress among public officials.
