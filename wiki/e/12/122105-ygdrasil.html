Ygdrasil in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Ygdrasil is a mighty ash-tree supporting the whole universe, with three immense roots in Asgard, Jotunheim, and Niffleheim; the Norns tend its Asgard root.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

Ygdrasil is the mighty ash-tree believed to support the whole universe. Its three roots extend into Asgard, Jotunheim, and Niffleheim, while the Norns tend the root that reaches Asgard.
