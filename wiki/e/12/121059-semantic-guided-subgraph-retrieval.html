Abstract written by a model

Semantic Guided Subgraph Retrieval is a subgraph retrieval approach that uses semantic embeddings to reduce candidate search space before Graph Semantic Distance, then applies Top-k Retrieval Algorithm and optimized pruning for efficiency.
