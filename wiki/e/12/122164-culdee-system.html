Culdee system in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Culdee system was a monastery-led religious community combining preaching, education, and shared worship; unlike later monastic institutions, it required obedience but not poverty or celibacy, and members’ wives stayed at Eilen nam ban.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IONA

The Culdee system differed from later monastic institutions by combining religious community, preaching, education, and shared worship. Its members were not required to take vows of poverty or celibacy, although they were required to obey the rule.
