Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

The Yellow Knight of Oz

Stampedro is the horse of the Yellow Knight. He is named in the introductory poem as part of the adventures told in the book.

CHAPTER FOURTEEN

Stampedro emerges from a chestnut burr after Speedy knocks it down and identifies himself as the horse of the Yellow Knight. He refuses to go to America but agrees to carry Speedy and Marygolden while searching for his master, and he reveals that they are in the Magical Country of Oz.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Stampedro is a horse who cannot speak in Samandra, though he can understand speech and communicate by nodding, neighing, and other actions. Speedy explains that the magic stone changed him from a horse chestnut into a real chestnut horse and that he belongs to the Yellow Knight of Oz. Stampedro insists that Sir Hokus, Peter Pun, Marygolden, and Speedy all ride him toward the stolen Comfortable Camel.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Stampedro carries Sir Hokus and his companions into Samandra, leaps over the advancing spearmen, and carries Marygolden and Peter Pun away from the palace. He later brings the group through the orange groves and enchanted forest to the horse chestnut tree.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Stampedro worries about Speedy and nearly bites Confido when the little dog refuses to help. After the Corumbians are restored, he searches among the Knights for the Yellow Knight and urges that the Prince be brought back.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Stampedro rejoices when the restored Yellow Knight embraces him and carries him rapidly to the deserted Castle of Corabia. He tries to remind the Knight of his former companions and urges Speedy and the others to help him, promising to carry them back to America if necessary.
