Abstract written by a model

Trimurti is a triad uniting Brahma, Vishnu, and Siva as personified creator, preserver, and destroyer, described as a Brahmanical compromise to unite worshippers against Buddhists.

Trimurti is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXX

The Trimurti unites Brahma, Vishnu, and Siva as the three personified powers of creation, preservation, and destruction. The text describes it as a Brahmanical compromise intended to unite different classes of worshippers against the Buddhists.
