Abstract written by a model

A simple fountain in Mladenovatz, built by the Serbs in grateful memory of the Scottish Women’s Hospitals’ work. It commemorates that work and was dedicated to the memory of a woman called “the angel of their people.” At its opening, Dr. Elsie Inglis turned on the water after a Greek Church religious service, intended to flow in remembrance.
