Sir Hokus's quest in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Sir Hokus's quest is a perilous, planned adventure and the main adventure announced for The Yellow Knight of Oz. Planned to begin on Monday with Dorothy, it initially has no fixed destination: Sir Hokus seeks adventure, imagining a distressed maiden to assist, a monster or dragon to slay, or a monarch to serve. Traveling east, he also hopes to learn about his lost identity and early history. Guided by his belief that knights must reject comfort, seek danger, and fight when required, he leaves before the palace inhabitants realize he and the Comfortable Camel are gone. After finding an empty castle and freeing the jester, he continues with the jester and the Camel. The quest becomes a pursuit of the thief who took the Camel, tied to serving an enchanted King and rescuing their friend; Peter Pun and then Speedy join the search.

The Yellow Knight of Oz

Sir Hokus's quest is the main adventure announced for The Yellow Knight of Oz. It involves a strange Yellow Knight, Stampedro, a wicked old Shah, and a princess.

CHAPTER ONE

The quest is planned to begin on Monday, with Dorothy accompanying Sir Hokus. Its purpose and destination remain unspecified, though Sir Hokus imagines that it might involve rescuing a damsel, slaying a dragon, or challenging a giant.

CHAPTER TWO

Sir Hokus's quest is to find adventure, including a distressed maiden, a monarch in need, or a dangerous monster. He also hopes his journey east will reveal something about his lost identity and early history.

CHAPTER THREE

Sir Hokus says that a knight must avoid comfort, seek danger, and undertake curious quests in search of adventure. He explains that his quest requires him to fight, though he later says that fighting with ladies is not seemly for a knight.

CHAPTER SIX

Sir Hokus's quest has already begun when the others discover that he and the Comfortable Camel are missing. The palace inhabitants had been preparing to accompany him and therefore had not noticed his departure.

CHAPTER TEN

Sir Hokus is seeking a maiden to rescue, a monster to slay, or a monarch to serve. After finding an empty castle and freeing the jester, he continues the quest with the jester and the Comfortable Camel, hoping to find a dragon or a King.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Sir Hokus and Peter Pun are pursuing the thief who took the Comfortable Camel as part of their quest to serve an enchanted King and rescue their friend. Speedy joins the quest after deciding that the Camel and the magic dates are central to the mystery.
