Abstract written by a model

Dr. Jex Blake founded the Edinburgh School of Medicine for Women, where Elsie Inglis began medical study. Her management became contentious when she expelled two students for discipline failures; a lawsuit found their treatment unjustified. Despite profound disagreement over management, Inglis recognized Blake’s important pioneering work.

Dr. Jex Blake founded the Edinburgh School of Medicine for Women, where Elsie Inglis received her first medical teaching. She expelled two students for failing to observe the school’s discipline, but a lawsuit established that their treatment had been unjustified. Elsie Inglis continued to acknowledge Dr. Jex Blake’s important pioneering work despite their profound disagreement over the school’s management.
