Abstract written by a model

Russian soldiers, treated as patients at the Scottish Women’s Hospital at Reni, thank Elsie and her unit for their care and attention. They receive cigarettes and Easter eggs, attend religious services, and support the unit during a spy incident. They publicly defend the hospital’s supplies and tell the local Commandant it is the best hospital on the front.

’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

Russian patients are treated by Elsie's unit at Reni and are described as grateful, pleased by the unit's St. George Medals, and supportive during the spy incident. They received cigarettes and Easter eggs, and participated in religious services.

‘_On Active Service.

Russian soldiers tell the local Commandant that the Scottish hospital is the best hospital on the front and must receive whatever it wants. They are also described as friendly toward the hospital and as displaying a changed political and social attitude.
