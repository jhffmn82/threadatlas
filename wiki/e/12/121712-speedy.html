Abstract written by a model

Speedy is Bill, a ten-year-old American boy and orphan who has lived with his uncle, Uncle Billy, since he was two. Quick in sport, temper, fighting, and running, he holds high-jump and track records and helps Uncle Billy with inventions. After accidentally launching the Skyrocket and crashing into Subterranea, he is threatened with Lava Lake unless he mends the sky. He resists, discovers Princess Marygolden in a cave, rescues her from a groper, and escapes Subterranea with her. Declared King of the Quix, he and Marygolden flee Quick City, travel through Oz, and free Stampedro from enchantment. Although he wants to return to America, he joins a rescue quest to find and disenchant a King. He protects Confido, locates and retrieves a magic date seed, obtains six magic dates, and uses them to restore Corumbia, Corabia, their people, and the Castle of Corabia. He is honored and knighted by both Kings, is credited with restoring two kingdoms, a Prince, and discovering a Princess, then remains for Marygolden's wedding before returning to America to tell Uncle Billy of his adventures.

Speedy is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Speedy in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930 · the work’s page · read it where Speedy first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SEVEN

Speedy is a ten-year-old orphan named Bill who lives with Uncle Billy and is known for his quickness in sports. He accidentally launches the Skyrocket without Uncle Billy, steers it back toward Earth, and crashes into the Underworld. In Subterranea, he explains what happened but is ordered to mend the hole in the sky or be thrown to the fire fish in Lava Lake.

CHAPTER EIGHT

Speedy considers resisting the Subterraneans and escaping, but he is led to the Shah's hunting ground. He runs through the sun storm, crosses the underwood, discovers Princess Marygolden in a cave, and pulls her away from a groper. He returns with her to the square, escapes the angry Subterraneans in the Skyrocket, and uses the parashuter in an attempt to reach the surface.

CHAPTER THIRTEEN

Speedy escapes Subterranea with Marygolden in the parashuter and is declared King of the Quix. He experiences the Lively Hood's rapid aging effect, and Marygolden removes the hood when he reaches his proper age. He and Marygolden flee Quick City and rest in a forest.

CHAPTER FOURTEEN

Speedy rests with Marygolden, modifies her dress by cutting off its lace ruffles and ruff, and tries to help her return to America. He accidentally frees Stampedro from an enchantment by knocking a chestnut burr from a tree, rides with Marygolden through Oz, and calls across the river to a silver-armored knight for information.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Speedy meets Sir Hokus of Pokes and Peter Pun and tells them about his adventures, Marygolden, Stampedro, and the magic stone. He reasons that the magic dates connected to the Comfortable Camel may have caused the enchantment of Stampedro, Peter Pun, the King, and his subjects. He agrees to help find and disenchant the King before returning to America.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Speedy hides Confido inside his leather jacket, escapes the spearmen by hiding in an oak chest, and discovers the magic date seed's location through the magical telescope. He descends from the tower, runs to the river, reunites with Marygolden and Peter Pun, retrieves the seed from the horse chestnut tree, and proposes using Confido's knowledge to break the enchantment.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Speedy plants the magic date seed and is carried into the treetop when a palm rapidly grows beneath him. He returns with all six ripe dates, uses one seed to restore Corumbia, and is honored by the King and Queen before joining the feast.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Speedy witnesses Sir Hokus become the Yellow Knight and follows him with Marygolden and the Comfortable Camel. He uses the remaining magic dates to restore the Corabians and Castle of Corabia, and he agrees to help the enchanted people despite being offered half the Kingdom as a reward.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

Speedy explains Marygolden's situation to the King and watches the Yellow Knight win the contest. He is briefly distressed when Marygolden appears not to remember him, but she soon recognizes him, and he agrees to remain for her wedding.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Speedy is credited with restoring two kingdoms, restoring a Prince, and discovering a Princess. He is knighted by both Kings, but asks Ozma to return him to America after the wedding, where he tells Uncle Billy about his adventures.
