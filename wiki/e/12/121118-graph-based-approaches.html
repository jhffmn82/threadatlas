Introduction

Researchers use graph-based approaches to address the complex reasoning requirements of multi-hop QA. These approaches extract entities, atomic facts, or semantic triples to establish more direct pathways for multi-hop reasoning, although recursively prompting LLMs to traverse graphs can be computationally expensive.
