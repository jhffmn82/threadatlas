Chapter XVII

The bees of Aristaeus perished, leading him to seek help from Cyrene and Proteus. After Aristaeus followed Proteus's instructions and performed the required sacrifices and funeral honors, a swarm of bees occupied one of the cattle carcasses and worked there as in a hive.
