Abstract written by a model

Mogodore's castle is a grim red castle clinging to rocks halfway up Mogodore's mountain, with a tower, dungeon cells, great stone hall, and dark cellarway. Peter seeks Shirley Sunshine in its tallest tower, later escapes its dungeons with Snif and Belfaygor, searches for Jack's head and the pirate's sack, and finds a descent toward the lost labyrinth.
