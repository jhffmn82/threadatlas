Paper-VISA

dataset · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Paper, Paper-VISA dataset

Abstract written by a model

Paper-VISA is a synthetic training and evaluation dataset for visual source attribution in multi-modal, single-page scientific paper PDFs in the medical or biomedical domain. Derived from and augmenting PubLayNet, it uses screenshots of single-page PubMed PDF documents with a 3:2 aspect ratio, adding synthetic queries and answers generated from selected bounding-box regions. It contains 100k training examples and 2,160 test examples. Its evaluation separates passage from non-passage content and reports overall performance as macro-average category accuracy. In publication-domain attribution with one relevant document, fine-tuned Qwen2-VL-7B reaches 68.2% bounding-box accuracy and 43.8% answer accuracy, with visual fine-tuning outperforming the textual method. It also evaluates a modularized text-based RAG pipeline under a single-oracle candidate setting: Paper-VISA training yields 68.2% bounding-box accuracy on Paper-VISA but 0.2% on Wiki-VISA, while Wiki+Paper+FineWeb training reaches 67.6% on Paper-VISA.
