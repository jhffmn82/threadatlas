Quebec in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Quebec, a place in Canada, was besieged by Arnold in the 1775 American invasion, but Canadian officers helped Carleton repel Americans; during the rebellion it was quiet as the grave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{1} CHAPTER I CANADIANS, OLD AND NEW The conquest of Canada by British arms in the Seven Years' War gave rise to a situation in the colony which was fraught with tragic possibilities.

Quebec was besieged by Arnold during the American invasion of Canada in 1775. Canadian officers including Dupre, Dambourges, Dumas, and Marcoux helped Carleton drive back the invading Americans.
