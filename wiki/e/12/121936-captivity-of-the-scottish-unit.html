captivity of the Scottish unit in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Scottish unit spent about five months under foreign occupation caring for Serbian wounded; as fever subsided, it was sent north under Austrian guard through Vienna, ending in Zürich.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ As the fever died out, a worse enemy came in.

The Scottish unit spent about five months serving under foreign occupation and caring for Serbian wounded. On February 11, 1916, its members were sent north under Austrian guard with fixed bayonets, traveling through Vienna and eventually reaching Zürich.
