CovidQA-RAG in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16

CovidQA-RAG is a biomedical research dataset combining 2k expert-annotated COVID-QA questions with a vector database of 250,000 100-word passages. It embeds questions and passages, retrieves up to N passages through FAISS, then generates LLM RAG responses requiring multi-document retrieval and reasoning. Its 16% hallucination rate is among RAGBench’s highest.

RAGBench Construction / Component Datasets .. Data Splits

CovidQA contributes biomedical research papers and expert-composed questions. It is among the datasets requiring retrieval and reasoning over multiple context documents.

RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]

CovidQA-RAG combines 2k expert-annotated questions from COVID-QA with a vector database of 250,000 100-word passages. It embeds questions and passages with OpenAI embeddings, retrieves up to N passages using FAISS with Euclidean distance and max_distance=0.25, and generates LLM responses for the resulting RAG instances.
