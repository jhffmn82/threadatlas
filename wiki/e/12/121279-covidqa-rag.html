CovidQA-RAG

dataset · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: CovidQA

Abstract written by a model

CovidQA-RAG is a biomedical research dataset combining 2k expert-annotated COVID-QA questions with a vector database of 250,000 100-word passages. It embeds questions and passages, retrieves up to N passages through FAISS, then generates LLM RAG responses requiring multi-document retrieval and reasoning. Its 16% hallucination rate is among RAGBench’s highest.

CovidQA-RAG is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
