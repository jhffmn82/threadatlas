Quebec Act of 1774

law · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Quebec Act

Abstract written by a model

The Quebec Act of 1774 was a law passed after the conquest of Canada by British arms and before the American invasion of Canada in 1775. It granted concessions to French inhabitants of Canada, giving French civil law almost complete exclusion of English common law and legally enforcing Catholic tithes, thereby virtually establishing the Roman Catholic Church and helping produce French-Canadian loyalty to Great Britain.
