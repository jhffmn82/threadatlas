’ CHAPTER XI THE MOORINGS CUT ‘Not I, but my Unit.

The Serbian evacuation from Russia was the central task that Dr. Inglis completed before her death. She sent wires, planned the movement, organized the journey, and travelled with the Serbian soldiers and her unit toward England.
