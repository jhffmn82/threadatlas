Forbidden Flagon in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The Forbidden Flagon is a hidden, sealed magic flask: small and black with a red label and red liquid inside. It is forbidden and mysterious; even Mogodore does not know its contents or purpose. The Book of Baffleburg warns that breaking its seal or spilling a drop will bring dreadful disaster, woe to Baffleburg, and disaster to the responsible person. Successive Barons of Baffleburg, then Mogodore's guard, are charged with guarding it. Mogodore intends to open it after becoming king of Oz, but guards' conversation suggests it may be used against him. Hidden in a fountain of fire near an enchanted cavern beyond the lost labyrinth, it is retrieved by Peter, passed through Jack Pumpkinhead's hands, and carried to the palace. When Mogodore summons it, it arrives in Jack's hand. Jack shatters it against Mogodore's nose, releasing its liquid. The broken flagon shrinks Mogodore, his men, and Belfaygor to midgets, reducing Mogodore's army. The Red Jinn explains that Jack can avoid the foretold disaster by removing his head before throwing it.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The forbidden flagon is a sealed container whose contents and purpose are unknown to Mogodore. The Book of Baffleburg warns that disturbing its seal or spilling even one drop will cause a dreadful disaster, and Mogodore plans to open it after becoming king of Oz.

CHAPTER 12

The forbidden flagon is guarded in or near the enchanted cavern beyond the lost labyrinth. Jack learns from overheard guards that one tip of it may be important in opposing Mogodore, so Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor go to find it.

CHAPTER 13

The Forbidden Flagon is a small black flask hidden in the middle of a fountain of fire. Its label says that each successive Baron of Baffleburg must guard it and that breaking its seal or spilling its red contents will bring woe to Baffleburg and disaster to the person responsible. Peter retrieves it, carries it back to the palace, and keeps it in his pocket.

CHAPTER 15

Peter is about to give the flagon to Belfaygor when Snif warns that the pirate's sack is approaching. Jack snatches the flagon and dives from Snif, keeping hold of it during the fall. He later places it in his coat pocket.

CHAPTER 18

Mogodore commands the forbidden flagon and its guard to appear before him. The flagon arrives in Jack Pumpkinhead's hand and is shattered against Mogodore's nose, releasing a thin red liquid and revealing what happens when its seal is broken.

CHAPTER 19

The forbidden flagon shrinks Mogodore the Mighty and the men serving him. Jack Pumpkinhead identifies it as the cause of the invaders' sudden reduction.

CHAPTER 20

The forbidden flagon reduces Mogodore, his men, and Belfaygor to midgets after its seal is broken. The Red Jinn tells Jack that removing his head before throwing it will avoid the disaster promised by its label.
