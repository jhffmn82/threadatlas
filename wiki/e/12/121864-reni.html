Abstract written by a model

Reni is a small village beside the Danube, surrounded by Steppe, with shipping, a crowded wharf, and an important junction. By January 1917, the unit established a difficult-to-heat hospital there for badly wounded Russian Army and Navy patients connected with the evacuation station. Severe snow, mud, fuel shortages, and the frozen Danube affected its work. The Scottish Women’s Hospital base remained at Reni until it closed in August 1917.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

‘NEARING ODESSA, ‘_Sep.

Reni was a small village and an important junction with shipping and a crowded wharf. By January 1917 the unit had established a hospital there for badly wounded patients in connection with the evacuation station, though the unfinished building was difficult to heat.

’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

Reni is the location of the unit's hospital and several of Elsie's letters. The hospital experiences severe snow, mud, fuel shortages, and a frozen Danube, while continuing to care for Russian Army and Navy patients.

‘_On Active Service.

Reni is the site of the Scottish Women’s Hospitals’ hospital, which Inglis describes as being beside the Danube and surrounded by the Steppe. The hospital remains there until it closes in August 1917.
