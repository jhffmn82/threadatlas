St. Giles’ in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

St. Giles’ is a church central to Elsie Inglis’s healing mission, where she attended daily service and shared sympathy with friends after worship. It also became the site of Dr. Inglis’s final procession, funeral service, and burial, ending with the Hallelujah Chorus as the procession left.
