Abstract written by a model

The Subterraneans are undersized, thin, undernourished inhabitants of Subterranea, living in underground homes entered through trapdoors. They wear magnificent metal-cloth robes, headdresses, and jeweled gold shoes. Frightened by Speedy and deadly sun-storm rays, they hide, then gather around him before angrily attacking the Skyrocket with bars and clubs after Princess Marygolden appears.

CHAPTER SEVEN

The Subterraneans are undersized, thin, undernourished underground dwellers with square, granite-colored faces. They wear magnificent metal-cloth robes, stiff headdresses, and gold shoes decorated with precious stones, and they are frightened by Speedy's loud voice.

CHAPTER EIGHT

The Subterraneans live in underground homes entered through flagstone trapdoors and gather around Speedy as he travels. They hide during the sun storm, then later react to Princess Marygolden with angry shrieks and attack the Skyrocket with bars and clubs.
