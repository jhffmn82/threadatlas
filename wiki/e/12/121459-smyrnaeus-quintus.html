Smyrnaeus Quintus, also called Quintus of Smyrna, was a poet active in the fourth century A.D. who lived at Smyrna and knew western Asia Minor. He authored the Greek The Fall of Troy, drawing on Homeric language, versification, and spirit while creating an independent, practically original poem.

The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus, or Quintus of Smyrna, was a poet who lived at Smyrna approximately in the fourth century A.D. He wrote The Fall of Troy in Greek and drew on Homeric language, versification, and spirit, though the poem is described as independent and practically original.
