Abstract written by a model

Physicians are a diverse group of junior, intermediate, subsenior, and senior professionals spanning 44 departments. Their diversity can complicate recommendations, though senior physicians dominate consultations (62.65%), and 38.13% have response times under 90 minutes. De-identified codes, departments, and response times support recommendation generation. Physicians are matched to patient-query specialties, may use RAGPR-based decision support with training, and are ultimately filtered and selected for patients' healthcare needs.

2 Methods .. 2.5 Baseline experiments

The documents contain de-identified physician codes, physician departments, and response times. Physician information associated with retrieved queries is provided to the large language model for recommendation generation.

3 Results / 3.1 Data set summary .. 3.4 Rationality evaluation of LLMs

Physicians in the dataset include junior, intermediate, subsenior, and senior professionals across 44 departments. Senior physicians handled the majority of consultations, accounting for 62.65% of the records.

4 Discussion / 4.1 Principal findings .. 4.5 Limitations and future directions

Physicians are matched with patients according to the specialties indicated by patient queries. They may use RAGPR-based decision support during consultations, but require training to understand how recommendations are generated and how to interpret the results.

5 Conclusion

Physicians are filtered and selected by the RAGPR model to identify professionals appropriate for patients' specific healthcare needs.
