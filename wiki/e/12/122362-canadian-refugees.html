Canadian refugees in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Canadian refugees gathered in United States border towns from February 1838, received American sympathy and encouragement, planned an insurrection in Lower Canada, crossed the border with armed forces, and others later moved toward Napierville.
