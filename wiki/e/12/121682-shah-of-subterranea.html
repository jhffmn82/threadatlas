Shah of Subterranea in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

The Shah of Subterranea is an old, wicked monarch who rules the Subterraneans as Imperial Lowness in his tenth year of reign. Silent to save his voice, he communicates pleasure and displeasure through masks, wears gold, and rides a giant earthworm with attendants. After discovering the Skyrocket’s hole, he distrusts Speedy and demands repair or consequences. Later, Princess Marygolden’s appearance provokes increasingly ferocious disapproval.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SEVEN

The Shah is the ruler of Subterranea and travels on a giant blue earthworm with attendants. After seeing the hole made by the Skyrocket, he doubts Speedy's explanation and orders that the sky be repaired or that Speedy face consequences.

CHAPTER EIGHT

The Shah rides through the square on a giant undulating earthworm and repeatedly turns his frowning mask toward Speedy. When the Subterraneans see Princess Marygolden, he arrives with attendants and displays increasingly ferocious masks to show his displeasure.
