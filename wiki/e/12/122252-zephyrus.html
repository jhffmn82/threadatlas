Zephyrus

Abstract written by a model

Zephyrus, also called Favonius, is a gentle, obedient west wind and Flora’s lover. Jealous of Apollo because Hyacinthus prefers him, Zephyrus carries Psyche to a flowery valley and her sisters to a palace, but does not aid the sisters after they leap.

Zephyrus is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CUPID AND PSYCHE

Zephyr carries Psyche from the mountain to a flowery valley and later brings her sisters to the palace at her husband's command. He does not support either sister when she leaps from the mountain.

Chapter XV

Zephyrus, also called Favonius, is the west wind and is characterized by gentleness. He is the lover of Flora.
