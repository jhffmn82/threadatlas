enchanted cavern in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The enchanted cavern, reached through the lost labyrinth, contains magic-mirror walls and a smaller grotto. Its Forbidden Flagon is hidden in a fire fountain and guarded, with access requiring knowledge of labyrinth tricks. Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor enter, retrieve the flagon from the smaller cave, and leave through the labyrinth.

CHAPTER 12

The enchanted cavern is reached through the lost labyrinth and contains the forbidden flagon. Guards say that only someone who knows the tricks of the labyrinth can find it.

CHAPTER 13

The enchanted cavern contains walls made of magic mirrors and an opening into a smaller grotto. The smaller cave holds the Forbidden Flagon in a fire fountain, guarded by a sleeping guard. Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor retrieve the flagon and leave through the labyrinth.
