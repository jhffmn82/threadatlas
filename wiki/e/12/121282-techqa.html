Also called: The TechQA dataset

Abstract written by a model

TechQA is a collection of 1.4k real-world customer-support questions from IBMDeveloper and DeveloperWorks, paired with technical-support documents from technotes and tech forums. It includes unanswerable cases; RAGBench uses its splits, samples ten documents per question while retaining applicable answer documents, and generates LLM responses.

TechQA is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
