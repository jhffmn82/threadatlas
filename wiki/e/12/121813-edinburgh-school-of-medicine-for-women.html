Edinburgh School of Medicine for Women in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Edinburgh School of Medicine for Women was a school founded by Dr. Jex Blake. It gave Elsie Inglis her first medical teaching at the beginning of her studies. Its students encountered difficulties affecting women seeking medical education, including restricted clinical opportunities and professional opposition. The school was distinct from the Medical College for Women promoted by Inglis and other women students.
