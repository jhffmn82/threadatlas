Abstract written by a model

A British medical unit composed of British women, also described as Scottish Sisters in Russian service, traveled from Archangel through Moscow and Odessa to serve the Serbian 1st Division. Funded through the Russian Red Cross, it ran two field hospitals, at Medgidia and near the front at Bulbulmic, and later worked at Braila, Galatz, and Reni. During retreat it evacuated people and equipment, then treated Serbian, Rumanian, and Russian wounded amid cold, mud, shortages, overcrowding, suspicion, and language difficulties. Members received St. George Medals.

‘NEARING ODESSA, ‘_Sep.

The unit traveled from Archangel through Moscow and Odessa to the Serbian 1st Division. It established and operated hospitals at Medgidia and Bulbulmic, evacuated equipment and personnel during the retreat, and later helped treat wounded at Braila and Reni; Elsie considered its members energetic, cheerful, and exceptionally brave.

’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

The unit works in hospitals at Medgidia, Braila, Galatz, Reni, and elsewhere, caring for Serbian, Rumanian, and Russian patients. It faces severe cold, mud, fuel shortages, overcrowding, and suspicion of espionage, but is praised for its care and conduct.
