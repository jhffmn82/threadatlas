Memnon's slaughter of the Argives in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

During the battle for Troy, Memnon kills Antilochus, slaughters the fleeing Argives, drives them toward the Hellespont, and leaves the plain blood-stained and heaped with dead.

BOOK II

Memnon slaughters the Argives after killing Antilochus and drives them toward the Hellespont. He continues killing as the Danaans flee in panic, leaving the plain heaped with dead and stained with their blood.
