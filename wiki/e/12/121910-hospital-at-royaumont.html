Abstract written by a model

The hospital at Royaumont was established and operating under the French Red Cross by the end of 1914. Despite early shortages, it was equipped with beds, lighting, kitchen facilities, lavatories, and theatre sinks, and contained four wards named for Blanche of Castille, Queen Margaret of Scotland, Joan of Arc, and Millicent Fawcett.
