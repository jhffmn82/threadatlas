  • In Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz: Mogodore is the vain, cruel, wicked Baron of Baffleburg, a villainous bandit and robber baron who rules Baffleburg from a grim red castle halfway up his mountain. A reddy descended from Jair, he is credited by Wagarag with having slain an hundred bears and subdued an hundred barons. He has a formidable army and retainers, commands them in war, and is guarded by Biggen and Little. He possesses a sauce box, later the magic belt, and controls a planned invasion of Oz. Mogodore begins by kidnapping Shirley Sunshine from Belfaygor and carrying her unconscious to his castle. He intends to force her to marry him, but Shirley's praise of his strength feeds his ambition: he plans to conquer Oz, capture the Emerald City, be crowned King Mogodore the First of Oz, and make Shirley his queen. He studies a map, orders Belfaygor, Peter, and Jack punished, and marches on the capitol. Peter's party tries and fails to stop him, while fearing that he will enslave the city's inhabitants and seize its magic treasures. Mogodore captures the Emerald City with little opposition, binds Ozma and her companions, and claims the throne by seizure and conquest. Finding the magic belt, he turns Scraps into a bird, though she escapes with Ozma's crown. At a banquet, Mogodore changes his marriage plan: rather than Shirley, he says he will marry Ozma to become lawful King. Seeking the forbidden flagon's mystery, he commands the flagon to appear. Jack breaks it against Mogodore's nose, and its red liquid shrinks Mogodore and his retainers to midget size. He flees from Toto, becomes a guarded prisoner, and ends locked in the pantry with the tiny army.

Mogodore in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

A reddy descended from Jair, he is credited by Wagarag with having slain an hundred bears and subdued an hundred barons. Narrative, section by section

12 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Mogodore the Mighty has stolen Shirley Sunshine, the princess Belfaygor was to marry. No further details about Mogodore are given in this unit.

CHAPTER 7

Mogodore abducts Shirley Sunshine and carries her unconscious to his castle in Baffleburg. He is said to rule from a grim red castle on Mogodore's mountain and to have a formidable fighting force.

CHAPTER 8

Mogodore is associated with the mountain and the grim city on the rocks. Snif expects the Baffleburghers to take the travelers to Mogodore, where Peter can open the sack.

CHAPTER 9

Mogodore is a vain and cruel baron who has captured Shirley Sunshine and intends to force her to marry him. After Shirley flatters his strength, he decides to conquer Oz, become King Mogodore the First of Oz, and eventually open the forbidden flagon.

CHAPTER 11

Mogodore, Baron of Baffleburg, is found studying a map of Oz and planning an attack on its capitol. He recognizes Belfaygor, announces that he will marry Princess Shirley Sunshine, orders Peter, Belfaygor, and Jack punished, and begins a march toward the Emerald City. He intends to return as King of Oz.

CHAPTER 12

Mogodore has gone to capture the Emerald City and make himself a king. The characters believe that the forbidden flagon may contain something capable of destroying his power.

CHAPTER 14

Mogodore is the baron whom Peter and his companions are pursuing toward the Emerald City. His bodyguards, Biggen and Little, deliver dinner in the air, and he possesses the sauce box that falls from Peter's pocket during the struggle in Swing City.

CHAPTER 15

Mogodore rides out of a forest with his army, and Peter's group attempts to intercept him. He escapes when the pirate's sack misses him and falls onto the rescuers. Jack believes Mogodore will march to the capitol, enslave the inhabitants, and seize the magic treasures.

CHAPTER 17

Mogodore leads his army into the Emerald City and captures it with little opposition. He binds Ozma and her companions, claims he will be King of Oz, and announces that he will marry Ozma. After finding the magic belt, he transforms Scraps into a bird, but becomes confused when she escapes with Ozma's crown and retreats into the castle.

CHAPTER 18

Mogodore brings Ozma to the banquet and announces that he has chosen to marry her instead of Shirley Sunshine. He claims to be King of Oz by seizure and conquest, refuses to marry until he is ready, and commands the forbidden flagon to appear. Jack Pumpkinhead then breaks the flagon against Mogodore's nose, causing its red liquid to run down his face.

CHAPTER 19

Mogodore the Mighty is shrunk by the forbidden flagon along with his retainers. He tries to quiet his frightened followers, flees from Toto, and is guarded as a prisoner by the Soldier with the Green Whiskers.

CHAPTER 20

Mogodore and his men remain captured in the banquet hall after being reduced to midgets by the forbidden flagon. They are later locked in the pantry for safety along with the rest of the tiny army.
