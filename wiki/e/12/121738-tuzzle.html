Tuzzle in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Tuzzle is the Grand Vizier of Samandra, serving the Sultan and carrying out his orders. He prepares the Royal Sampan, demands elaborate clothing and servants, and travels with twenty slaves to Oz as the Sultan’s representative. At Ozma’s court, he brings embroidered shawls and asks for the Comfortable Camel’s return; when the Camel cannot be found, he leaves. Later, aboard the boat, Tuzzle commands his slaves to seize Camy and directs the voyage back to Samandra. Expecting reward for the capture, he is largely ignored by the Sultan, suffers Camy stepping on his toes, and complains that his success is unrecognized.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIVE

Tuzzle serves as Grand Vizier and carries out the Sultan's orders to prepare the Royal Sampan and travel to Oz. He demands elaborate clothing and servants, then departs with twenty slaves and reaches the outskirts of the Emerald City on the second evening.

CHAPTER SIX

Tuzzle arrives in the Emerald City as the Grand Vizier of Samandra, leading a procession of slaves bearing orange branches and embroidered shawls. He asks Ozma to return the Comfortable Camel to the Sultan of Samandra, but leaves when the camel cannot be found, giving Ozma the shawls as a gift.

CHAPTER ELEVEN

Tuzzle is identified as the huge fat person aboard the boat. He orders the twenty slaves to seize the Comfortable Camel and directs the rowing as the barge carries the Camel away.

CHAPTER TWELVE

Tuzzle captures Camy and tows him behind the Royal Sampan to Samandra, expecting rewards for his cleverness. The Sultan largely ignores him, and Camy steps heavily on his toes. Tuzzle follows the Sultan while complaining that his successful capture has gone unrecognized.
