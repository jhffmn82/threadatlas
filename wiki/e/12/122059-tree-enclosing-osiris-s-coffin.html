is_erected_as column [in the palace of the king of Phoenicia] The tree enclosing Osiris's coffin grew from reeds strengthened by the divine power in Osiris's body. It was felled and erected as a column in the palace of the king of Phoenicia, until Isis caused it to split open and release the coffin.
