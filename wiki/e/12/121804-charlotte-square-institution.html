The Charlotte Square Institution was the Edinburgh school for girls, where Elsie and her sister Eva attended under Mr. Oliphant. Elsie distinguished herself academically, edited the Edina, and persuaded the directors and neighbouring proprietors to allow the girls to play in Charlotte Square gardens.
