encounters professional opposition [during the opening of the profession to women] Women’s admission to the medical profession required them to overcome opposition, professional jealousy, restricted clinical access, and financial barriers. Women had staunch supporters among doctors, but the profession as a whole was described as insufficiently willing to share its knowledge with them.
