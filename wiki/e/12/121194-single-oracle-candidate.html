  • In VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution: The Single oracle candidate setting gives a model one ground-truth relevant document screenshot, ensuring the answer is present and removing the need to identify a document. 4 / 4.1 .. 4.2

The Single oracle candidate setting supplies one ground-truth relevant document screenshot, guaranteeing that the answer is present. It evaluates answer generation and visual attribution without requiring prediction of the relevant document identifier.

5

The single-candidate setting provides the model with only the relevant document. Fine-tuned models perform strongly in this setting, although their effectiveness varies with document characteristics such as passage location and whether the evidence is in passages, tables, or figures.
