Abstract written by a model

SafetyRAG’s external safety knowledge base is a collection of safety facts about unsafe, malicious, and adversarial prompts across domains. It is the primary source of domain-specific context for retrieval-augmented generation, helping LLM applications make safer decisions and generate safer responses without domain-specific retraining; its contents can be changed independently. Its coverage was analyzed with Top2Vec, reducing 51 initially identified topics to 20 representative clusters.

Abstract—Large Language Model (LLM) agents have

The external knowledge base stores safety facts about various unsafe prompts. SafetyRAG retrieves information from it to enhance an LLM’s decision-making and safe behavior.

I. INTRODUCTION

The knowledge base supplies domain-specific information for the RAG process. It is used to retrieve relevant information and provide context for generating responses, and its contents can be changed without retraining the model.

III. MATERIALS AND METHODS / A. Overall Framework .. 2) Baseline LLM models

The safety knowledge base is an external collection of safety facts covering diverse malicious and adversarial inputs, including topics beyond the medical domain. Its topics were analyzed with Top2Vec, which initially identified 51 topics that were reduced to 20 clusters for representation.
