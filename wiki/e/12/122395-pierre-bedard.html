Pierre Bedard in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Pierre Bedard was a House of Assembly member, prominent on Le Canadien’s staff, and an excellent mathematician. During the 1805 session, amid prison-building debates, Sir James Craig dismissed him from the militia and arrested him after Le Canadien was suppressed. Bedard refused release without trial, was ejected from prison, then restored to militia rank and appointed judge by Sir George Prevost.

{13} CHAPTER III 'THE REIGN OF TERROR' During the session of 1805 the Assembly was confronted with the apparently innocent problem of building prisons.

Pierre Bedard was a member of the House of Assembly and a prominent figure on the staff of Le Canadien. Sir James Craig dismissed him from the militia and later arrested him when Le Canadien was suppressed; Bedard refused release without a trial and was eventually ejected from prison at bayonet point. Sir George Prevost later restored him to militia rank and made him a judge.
