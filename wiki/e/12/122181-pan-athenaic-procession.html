SKETCH OF THE HISTORY OF GREEK SCULPTURE

The Pan-Athenaic procession was represented on the Parthenon frieze as a major religious ceremony. The scene includes virgins bearing incense, sacrificial animals, musicians, chariots, and riders, with deities surrounding Zeus as spectators.
