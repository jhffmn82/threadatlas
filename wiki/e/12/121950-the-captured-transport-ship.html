’ CHAPTER XI THE MOORINGS CUT ‘Not I, but my Unit.

The transport carried Dr. Inglis, her unit, and Serbian representatives toward England. During the voyage Dr. Inglis rested in a cabin, played patience, prescribed for an ill naval officer, and continued reviewing accounts and planning future work despite her illness.
