Abstract written by a model

The Restoration of Corumbia is a chapter event in which Speedy uses magic dates to free Corumbia from enchantment. Planting a seed restores forest trees and plants to the kingdom’s people, horses, and court; later, a date stone restores fishes and frogs, and the Corabians return to their castle, though the Yellow Knight remains unrestored.

The Restoration of Corumbia is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

The restoration of Corumbia occurs when Speedy plants the seed from a magic date and the enchanted forest's trees and plants become the kingdom's people, horses, and court. The King and Queen are reunited with their subjects, while the Yellow Knight remains to be restored.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Speedy eats a magic date and casts its stone into the river, causing the fishes' and frogs' heads to become human and the Corabians to emerge and return to their castle.
