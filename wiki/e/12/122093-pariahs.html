Pariahs in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Pariahs are a class below the Sudras, said to arise from unauthorized unions, assigned lowest services, denied civil rights and access to higher-caste spaces, and treated as unclean and polluting.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXX

The Pariahs are described as a class below the Sudras, arising from unauthorized unions between individuals of different castes. They were assigned the lowest services, denied civil rights, excluded from the temples, houses, markets, and wells of higher castes, and treated as polluting and unclean.
