Pandora's jar in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Pandora's jar, kept in Epimetheus's house, held unneeded noxious articles; when Pandora removed its cover, bodily and mental evils escaped and scattered, leaving hope inside.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II

Pandora's jar was kept in Epimetheus's house and contained noxious articles that had not been needed when man was fitted for his new abode. When Pandora removed its cover, bodily and mental evils escaped and scattered, leaving hope as the only remaining thing inside.
