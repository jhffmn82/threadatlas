Krushieevatz in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Krushieevatz, site of a Serbian military hospital, held 900 wounded in November 1915 and later 1,200 patients; during Austro-German occupation, the Scottish unit remained amid cold, hunger, refugees, prisoners, and military collapse.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ As the fever died out, a worse enemy came in.

Krushieevatz became the location where the Scottish unit remained during the Austro-German occupation. Its military hospital held 900 wounded in November 1915 and later as many as 1,200 patients, while the town and surrounding country suffered cold, hunger, refugees, prisoners, and military collapse.
