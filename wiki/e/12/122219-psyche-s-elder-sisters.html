Abstract written by a model

Psyche's two elder sisters, married to royal princes, envy her palace and incite her suspicion of her unseen husband. They urge her to use a lamp and knife; later each seeks Zephyr's aid and falls to her death.

CUPID AND PSYCHE

Psyche's elder sisters are married to royal princes but envy Psyche's splendid palace. They convince her that her unseen husband is a monster and advise her to use a lamp and knife against him; afterward, each tries to be carried to him by Zephyr and falls to her death.
