evaluates test-time-to-first-token latency [with batch sizes from 1 to 8] Batch scaling is evaluated with a fixed total recall text length of 8192 tokens, a query length of 128 tokens, and batch sizes from 1 to 8. TurboRAG retains a roughly fourfold TTFT advantage even with host-to-device KV-cache transfer, while its reported speedup without that transfer rises to 32.8x at batch size 8.
