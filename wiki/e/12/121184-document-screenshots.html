Abstract

Document screenshots serve as the visual source material in which VISA marks supporting evidence with bounding boxes.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

They represent retrieved documents in vision-based RAG pipelines. VISA draws bounding boxes around content in these screenshots that potentially supports generated answers.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

Document screenshots are the image inputs used for documents in the corpus of a vision-based RAG system. They are processed by an image encoder before their representations are passed to the language model.
