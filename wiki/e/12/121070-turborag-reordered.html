TurboRAG-reordered

model · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: TurboRAG -reordered

Abstract written by a model

TurboRAG-reordered is a TurboRAG model variant that uses reordered positions while preserving relative positional differences needed by RoPE. It remains robust without fine-tuning at RGB noise ratio 0.8, then after fine-tuning keeps its accuracy gap within 1% of Naïve RAG. It matches Naïve RAG on LongBench and generalizes across OpenCompass without significant degradation. On LLaMA-3.1-8B, it averages 95.0 in Chinese RGB and 97.4 in English RGB, while RGB Information Integration averages are 48.3 and 62.0. It also delivers an average 8.6x TTFT speedup on LongBench.

TurboRAG-reordered is in one work on this site, TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
