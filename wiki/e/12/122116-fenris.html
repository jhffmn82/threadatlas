Abstract written by a model

Fenris is a wolf, child of Loki and Angerbode, feared as a growing monster. He breaks strong fetters, is bound with Gleipnir after biting off Tyr's hand, then at Ragnarok kills Odin before Vidar slays him.

Fenris is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXXI

Fenris is Loki's wolf child and a growing monster feared by the gods. After breaking the strongest fetters, he is bound with Gleipnir, but bites off Tyr's hand when the gods refuse to release him.

Chapter XXXIII

Fenris is the wolf who is the child of Angerbode. At Ragnarok, he breaks his bonds, kills Odin, and is then slain by Vidar.
