Abstract written by a model

The magic dates are six ripe dates from a magic palm, each containing a magic stone or seed and valued by the Sultan as a precious package. They can cause the kingdom’s transformation through enchantment and provide the means to undo the Sultan’s remaining enchantments. Hidden in a secret compartment of Camy’s left-hand saddle sack, they are missing when the Sultan searches; later they are found in the Comfortable Camel’s saddle sack, stale and hard enough that Sir Hokus barely eats them. The Sultan accuses the visitors of stealing them and ruining his strongest enchantment. After Speedy finds a seed, a planted seed produces the palm, and Speedy collects all six dates. Their seeds are used successively: one restores the Corabians, another Castle Corabia, and the last, given to the Yellow Knight, restores Princess Marygolden when its seed is placed on the tower balcony ledge, ending the relevant enchantment.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER TWELVE

The magic dates are the valuable package the Sultan has been seeking and awaiting. They had been hidden in a secret compartment of Camy's left-hand saddle sack, but the compartment is open and the dates are gone when the Sultan searches it.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

The magic dates were in the Comfortable Camel's saddle sack and were so stale and hard that Sir Hokus barely managed to eat them. Speedy believes that whoever knew about the dates enchanted Stampedro, Peter Pun, the King, and the King's subjects.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

The Sultan accuses the visitors of stealing his magic dates and ruining his strongest enchantment. Speedy later finds one magic date seed, and Confido is persuaded to reveal the secret connected with the magic dates.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

The magic dates grow on the palm produced by the planted seed, and Speedy collects all six. Their seeds are used in successive enchantments, with dates remaining for the restoration of the Yellow Knight and for purposes not yet explained.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

The magic dates contain the means to undo the Sultan's remaining enchantments. Speedy uses one to restore the Corabians and another to restore Castle Corabia, while the remaining date is associated with restoring the Princess.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

The last magic date is given by the King of Corabia to the Yellow Knight. Eating it and placing its seed on the tower balcony restores Princess Marygolden and ends the relevant enchantment.
