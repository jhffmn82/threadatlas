It selects passages longer than 50 words, marks them with bounding boxes, and uses a VLM to produce supported queries and short answers. FineWeb-VISA is based on the FineWeb-edu corpus and uses screenshots of 60k sampled webpages captured at 980x3920 pixels. It selects passages longer than 50 words, highlights them with bounding boxes, and uses a VLM to generate supported queries and short answers; it is used only for training because its quality is not guaranteed to match human-annotated data.

6 / 6.1 .. 6.4

FineWeb-VISA is presented as a resource for improving zero-shot capabilities. Training on it alone achieves 37.6% bounding box accuracy on Wiki-VISA and 22.0% on Paper-VISA, and it performs especially well on Wiki-VISA cases requiring attribution to more than one passage.
