pathetic tendency in sculpture

Also called: extreme results of the pathetic tendency, pathetic tendency, the pathetic tendency

Abstract written by a model

The pathetic tendency sought to represent intense suffering, horror, suspense, and emotional action. In the Laocoon and Farnese Bull it reached a point where technical mastery and dramatic effect were judged to replace ethical meaning and a serious conception of life.
