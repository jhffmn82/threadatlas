Sultan's enchantment in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER TEN

The forest appears capable of enchanting living beings and plants into other forms. The jester says he had been a funnysuckle vine for so long that his head remains full of twits and twitters, and the characters fear that lingering in the forest could cause them to take root or turn into turnips.

CHAPTER FOURTEEN

An enchanted state has affected both Marygolden and Stampedro. Marygolden was found enchanted in a cave but is now restored, while Stampedro believes he had been confined inside a chestnut burr until Speedy broke the spell.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

The unit reveals that Stampedro and Peter Pun had been enchanted and that the King and his subjects may have been enchanted as well. Speedy connects these enchantments to the magic dates associated with the Comfortable Camel.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

The Sultan's enchantment has transformed the King of the Golden City and his subjects into forms within the enchanted forest. Speedy believes Confido knows how the spell may be broken, but the method is not yet disclosed.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

The enchantment of Corumbia was caused by the Sultan and lasted five hundred years. It transformed the King, Queen, subjects, horses, and court into trees, plants, and other forms until the magic date seed broke the spell.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

The Sultan's enchantment changed the people of Corabia into fishes and frogs and left their castle and Princess under magical effects. Speedy begins breaking the enchantment by using the magic dates.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

The Sultan's enchantment has kept Princess Marygolden from her parents and caused the Yellow Knight to forget his former identity and allegiances. The magic ends after the correct use of the magic date, allowing both Princess Marygolden and Sir Hokus to remember everything.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The Sultan's enchantments transformed the rulers and their children, sent Sir Hokus to Pokes, and changed Marygolden into a statue in Subterranea. Ozma renders all of the Sultan's spells and wizardry useless.
