Corabia in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Corabia is a kingdom with a castle, ruled by its King and conquered by the Sultan of Samandra five hundred years ago. The Sultan's enchantment turns its people into fishes and frogs and deserts the castle. Speedy restores them; Marygolden is restored, reunited with her parents, and marries Sir Hokus there.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Corabia has been affected by the Sultan's enchantment, which changed its people into fishes and frogs and left its castle deserted. Its people are restored by Speedy, but its Princess remains enchanted pending the grand test.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

Corabia is the kingdom whose King and Queen have been separated from Princess Marygolden by enchantment. Its people celebrate the Princess's restoration and the expected marriage to Sir Hokus.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Corabia is the kingdom where Ozma and Dorothy find the Comfortable Camel, Sir Hokus, and Princess Marygolden. Its King hosts the wedding of Marygolden and Sir Hokus.
