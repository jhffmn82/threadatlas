CHAPTER II ELSIE MAUD INGLIS 1864-1917 ‘Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is His reward.

The Inglis family’s childhood was marked by affection, religious practice, outdoor life, travel, and close companionship among the three sisters and their parents. The children shared walks, lessons, imaginative games with dolls, and memories of an equal and loving father.
