CHAPTER 8

The group cannot cross the chasm by ordinary means and postpones the attempt until morning because the climb to the city would be impossible at night. They intend to use Belfaygor's beard to cross.

CHAPTER 10

Belfaygor grows his beard long enough to stretch across the deep chasm and fastens its ends to trees on opposite sides. Peter crosses by swinging along it, Snif helps rescue Jack when Jack nearly loses his hold, and Belfaygor crosses after clipping his beard close to his chin.
