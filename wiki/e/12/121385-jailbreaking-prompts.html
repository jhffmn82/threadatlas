jailbreaking prompts

Abstract written by a model

In "RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models," jailbreaking prompts concatenate a harmful query with an adversarial suffix to maximize a target LLM's compliant responses and are optimized for retrieved-document conditions. In "RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models," jailbreaking is a vulnerability that red teaming attempts to expose, and adversarial prompts that succeed in non-RAG settings may fail in RAG settings. In "SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation," jailbreak prompts induce LLMs to bypass safety behavior and comply with harmful requests, including through automatically generated adversarial suffixes and manually crafted jailbreak inputs.

Abstract written by a model

Jailbreaking prompts concatenate a harmful query with an adversarial suffix to maximize a target LLM’s compliant responses. Optimized for RAG using retrieved documents, they generalize at test time; non-retrieval-trained prompts transfer poorly, while performance is best when training and testing document conditions match.
