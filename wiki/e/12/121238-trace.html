TRACe

framework · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: RAG evaluation framework, TRACe Evaluation Framework, TRACe evaluation framework

Abstract written by a model

TRACe is a RAG evaluation framework for RAG systems, formalized with explainable and actionable metrics applicable across RAG domains. It standardizes evaluation through a consistent, systematic approach to measuring performance across related dimensions. Its four metrics are Utilization, Relevance or Context Relevance, Adherence, and Completeness. It evaluates retrievers and response generators through retrieval efficiency, context use, incorporation of relevant information, and response grounding. TRACe is evaluated in a case study of different RAG configurations, where Relevance, Utilization, and Adherence are measured and metrics are predicted by LLM-based and fine-tuned judges. Post-processing for Adherence appears to improve relevance and utilization label quality and stability. It is proposed for adoption with RAGBench.

TRACe is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

TRACe is formalized as an evaluation framework comprising explainable and actionable RAG evaluation metrics applicable across RAG domains. The text proposes adopting TRACe with RAGBench to advance RAG evaluation systems.

TRACe Evaluation Framework .. Adherence

The TRACe Evaluation Framework measures Utilization, Relevance, Adherence, and Completeness in RAG systems. It evaluates the retriever and response generator by assessing retrieval efficiency, context use, incorporation of relevant information, and grounding of the response.

RAG Case Study .. Estimating Context Relevance is Difficult

TRACe is evaluated through a case study involving different RAG configurations and is used to measure Relevance, Utilization, and Adherence. Its metrics are also predicted by LLM-based and fine-tuned judges.

Conclusion

TRACe is proposed as a RAG evaluation framework with four metrics: utilization, relevance, adherence, and completeness. It standardizes evaluation and provides a systematic way to measure RAG system performance across multiple dimensions.

Annotation Post-Processing Steps

TRACe metrics are related, and the extra post-processing measures applied to Adherence qualitatively appear to improve the quality and stability of relevance and utilization labels.
