Hela in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hela is a goddess of death, child of Loki and Angerbode, and ruler of Hel, the dominions of those who die from sickness or old age. Odin casts her into Niffleheim, where she resides in Elvidnia. She later promises Baldur’s release if all things weep, but keeps him when Thaukt refuses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

Hela is Loki's child and the goddess of death. Odin casts her into Niffleheim and gives her authority over those who die of sickness or old age; her hall is Elvidnia, and half her body is flesh-colored while the other half is blue.

Chapter XXXIII

Hela is the daughter of Angerbode and rules the dominions of the dead. She agrees to release Baldur if every living and lifeless thing weeps for him, but keeps him in Hel after Thaukt refuses.
