Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CUPID AND PSYCHE

Psyche is the youngest daughter of a king and possesses beauty so extraordinary that people honor her as Venus. After Cupid becomes her husband, she disobeys his command not to look at him, loses him, completes Venus's difficult tasks, and is finally made immortal by Jupiter.

The fable of Cupid and Psyche is usually considered allegorical.

Psyche represents the human soul and is purified by sufferings and misfortunes before attaining true and pure happiness. She is depicted as a butterfly-winged maiden and, after wandering and learning to seek Love in the skies, becomes Love’s angel bride in heaven.
