Mogodore's invasion and capture of the Emerald City in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Mogodore's invasion and capture of the Emerald City is his planned attack to conquer Oz, depose Ozma, rule as King of Oz, and gain access to the forbidden flagon. He orders Wagarag to rouse the fighting men at daybreak and sends his forces across the Red Mountains toward the city. With no visible defenses, army, or guards, the forces capture the Emerald City without opposition, overpower Ozma's court, seize the palace, bind Ozma and the celebrities, imprison the servants, and leave soldiers controlling the city. Scraps reports the capture to Glinda.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

Mogodore decides to begin his conquest the following morning, ordering Wagarag to rouse his fighting men at daybreak. He plans to cross the Red Mountains, capture the Emerald City, depose Ozma, and use his new position to open the forbidden flagon.

CHAPTER 11

Mogodore's invasion begins when he sends his forces toward the Emerald City after declaring that he will conquer Oz. Peter believes the city may soon be taken because only one Knight and one soldier are in the palace, and the soldier cannot fight.

CHAPTER 12

Mogodore's attack on the Emerald City is imminent: he has gone to capture the city and make himself king. Peter and his companions consider flying there immediately, but delay in order to seek the forbidden flagon.

CHAPTER 17

Mogodore's invasion captures the Emerald City without opposition because the city has no visible defenses, army, or guards. His forces seize the palace, bind Ozma and the celebrities, imprison the servants, and leave soldiers to control the city.
