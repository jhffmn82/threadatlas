the retreat from Medgidia in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

A military retreat marked by panic and displacement: a field hospital, transport, and personnel withdrew as refugees and disorganized Russian, Serbian, and Rumanian soldiers crowded roads, abandoning equipment en route through Caramacat and Hershova to Braila.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

‘NEARING ODESSA, ‘_Sep.

The retreat began with the field hospital being moved back, followed by the transport and then the hospital personnel. Refugees and disorganized Russian, Serbian, and Rumanian soldiers crowded the roads, equipment and belongings were abandoned, and the unit moved through Caramacat and Hershova before reaching Braila.
