‘NEARING ODESSA, ‘_Sep.

The retreat began with the field hospital being moved back, followed by the transport and then the hospital personnel. Refugees and disorganized Russian, Serbian, and Rumanian soldiers crowded the roads, equipment and belongings were abandoned, and the unit moved through Caramacat and Hershova before reaching Braila.
