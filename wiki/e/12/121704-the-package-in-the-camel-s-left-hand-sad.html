Abstract written by a model

The package in the camel's left hand saddle sack is a valuable object containing magic, possibly concentrated in its seed. The Sultan of Samandra urgently wants the camel recovered because he says he is lost, ruined, and done for without the package, and he plans to hide it again. Later, Sir Hokus may have eaten its magic date, while its seed was thrown at a funnysuckle vine.

CHAPTER FIVE

The package in the camel's left hand saddle sack is the Sultan's real reason for wanting the camel recovered. He says that without it he is lost, ruined, and done for, and plans to hide it again once it is returned.

CHAPTER ELEVEN

The Comfortable Camel says that Sir Hokus has eaten a magic date and may turn into something else. Sir Hokus believes that the magic may have rested in the seed he threw at the funnysuckle vine, but the unit does not show him changing.
