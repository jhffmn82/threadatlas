Royal Sampan in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

The Royal Sampan is a yellow-sailed boat owned by a fat rogue and used for a camel-recovery mission from Samandra to the Emerald City. Loaded with supplies, gifts, servants, a golden halter, and equipment, it sails at dawn with Tuzzle and twenty slaves. Its crew captures the Comfortable Camel, then carries him away, prompting pursuit by Sir Hokus, Peter Pun, Speedy, and Stampedro.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIVE

The Royal Sampan is loaded with supplies, gifts, servants, a golden halter, and equipment for the journey. It sails at dawn down the Winkie River and carries Tuzzle and his twenty slaves to the outskirts of the Emerald City.

CHAPTER ELEVEN

The royal barge approaches along the river with twenty slaves rowing and yellow sails flapping. Tuzzle uses its crew to capture the Comfortable Camel, and the barge rapidly travels away until Sir Hokus and Peter lose sight of it.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

The fat owner of the yellow boat steals the Comfortable Camel. Sir Hokus, Peter Pun, Speedy, Peter Pun, and Stampedro pursue the thief.
