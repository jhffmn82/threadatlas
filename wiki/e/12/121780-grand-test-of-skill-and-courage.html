Abstract written by a model

A grand test of skill and courage is held to determine a husband for the enchanted Princess of Corabia and to release Princess Marygolden from disenchantment. On the day the Sultan's magic takes effect, the Yellow Knight and four others enter. Faced with three rings and supposed deadly dangers, the other suitors withdraw; the Yellow Knight succeeds and wins the Princess's hand.
