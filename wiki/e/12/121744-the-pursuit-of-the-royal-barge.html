the pursuit of the royal barge in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Sir Hokus, joined eagerly by Peter Pun, pursues the royal barge on foot along the riverbank. The barge outruns them and disappears, but they continue into open country, choose a branch by sword toss, and press toward the next bend.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER ELEVEN

Sir Hokus and Peter run along the riverbank after the royal barge, but the boat quickly outruns them. They continue after it into open country, choose the right river branch by tossing Sir Hokus's sword, and press on toward the next bend.
