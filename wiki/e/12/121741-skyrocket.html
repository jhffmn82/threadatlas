Skyrocket

Also called: the Skyrocket, the car, the ship, the strange craft, the torpedo, the torpedo ship

Abstract written by a model

Skyrocket is a flying torpedo for space travel, intended to take Speedy and Uncle Billy through the sky to Mars, with a tail rocket, oxygen sprays, and a parashuter. It launches before Uncle Billy reaches the driver’s seat. After Speedy turns it downward, it crashes into the flying field behind his Long Island home, breaks through the earth and Subterranea’s sky, cuts a great underground shaft, and stops in Subterranea’s public square. Used for Speedy’s adventures, it later carries Speedy and Princess Marygolden during an escape; Speedy releases the parashuter, which misses the shaft. Speedy later recounts the adventures.

Skyrocket is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER SEVEN

The Skyrocket is a flying torpedo designed to carry Uncle Billy and Speedy through the sky to Mars. It launches before Uncle Billy boards, is turned downward by Speedy, crashes through the earth, and eventually stops in the public square of Subterranea.

CHAPTER EIGHT

Speedy uses the Skyrocket to escape from the attacking Subterraneans with Princess Marygolden inside. He opens its roof and releases the parashuter, but the apparatus misses the shaft cut by the Skyrocket.
