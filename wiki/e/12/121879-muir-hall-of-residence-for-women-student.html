Muir Hall of Residence for Women Students in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Muir Hall of Residence for Women Students was founded in George Square to provide suitable accommodation for women students coming to the University from the country. Dr. Inglis served as honorary secretary until her death, helped students with illness and personal difficulties, and worked for their comfort.
