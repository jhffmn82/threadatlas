depicts Pan-Athenaic procession [including virgins, animals, musicians, chariots, and riders] The Parthenon frieze, executed under Pheidias’s supervision, depicts the Pan-Athenaic procession. It combines physical and intellectual beauty with studied contrasts between forceful action and gracious repose, and much of it has been preserved in the British Museum.
