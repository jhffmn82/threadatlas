Abstract written by a model

The King of Corumbia, sovereign and ruler of Corumbia, owns Stampedro according to Sir Hokus. Believed to be the King of the Golden Castle or Golden City, he and his subjects are sought after being enchanted and transformed in the enchanted forest. Restored from a great oak by Speedy’s magic date seed, he welcomes the liberators, orders his castle restored, rewards Confido, feasts, and prepares defenses. He later learns the Sultan transformed him and his family for five hundred years, helps sentence the Sultan, and knights Speedy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Sir Hokus says that Stampedro evidently belongs to the King of the Golden Castle. The group believes this King was enchanted along with his subjects and sets out to find and disenchant him.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

The King of the Golden City is sought by Sir Hokus and his companions. The Sultan is asked what he has done with the King and all his subjects, who are later understood to be among the people transformed in the enchanted forest.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

The King is restored from the form of a great oak when Speedy plants a magic date seed. He welcomes the liberators, orders the restoration of the castle, promises Confido a reward, and hosts a feast while preparing a guard against the Sultan.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The King of Corumbia learns that the Sultan treacherously transformed him and his family for five hundred years. He helps pronounce the Sultan's sentence and has Speedy knighted.
