TTFT in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

TTFT is a metric, measured in milliseconds, for time to first token in RAG comparisons. It matters because conventional RAG’s processing of many retrieved document chunks increases it, while TurboRAG substantially reduces it. Reported results begin with up to a 9.4× reduction and an 8.6× average reduction on LongBench; the advantage remains substantial or grows with larger batches and contexts. In Qwen-2 measurements, speedups range from 2.31× to 11.23×. One comparison lists TTFT falling from 87 for Naïve RAG to 36 for TurboRAG, and an on-device scenario keeps it below one second.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Time-to-first-token is increased by the online computation required to process numerous retrieved document chunks in conventional RAG systems. TurboRAG reduces it by up to 9.4x, with an average reduction of 8.6x.

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

TurboRAG reduces time to first token by up to 9.4×, with an average reduction of 8.6×, on the reported LongBench multi-document question answering benchmarks. The unit also states that TurboRAG can keep TTFT below one second in an on-device scenario with limited GPU availability.

4 / Experiments .. 4.5 / Batch Scaling

Test-time-to-first-token latency is measured on LongBench and under varying context lengths and batch sizes. TurboRAG-reordered reduces average LongBench TTFT by 8.6x, and TurboRAG's advantage increases or remains substantial as batch size and context length grow.

5 / Conclusion and Discussion .. Limitations

Time to first token is substantially reduced by TurboRAG compared with naïve processing. In the Qwen-2 measurements, speedups range from 2.31× for the 1.5B model at batch size 1 to 11.23× for the 72B model at batch size 2.

E / Supplementary Information for RGB

TTFT, measured in milliseconds, is one of the metrics reported in the comparison. The listed TTFT values are 87 for Naïve RAG and 36 for TurboRAG.
