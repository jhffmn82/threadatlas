Abstract written by a model

Erisichthon, a profane person who despises the gods, cuts down Ceres’s sacred oak and kills those resisting him. Famine’s visitation gives him unending hunger, consuming his wealth, leading him to sell his daughter and finally devour himself.

Chapter XV

Erisichthon despises the gods and cuts down a sacred oak in Ceres’s grove, killing the oak nymph and a servant who tries to stop him. Ceres punishes him by sending Famine into his body, giving him an unending hunger that consumes his wealth, forces him to sell his daughter, and finally drives him to devour himself.
