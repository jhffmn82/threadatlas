Abstract written by a model

HybridQA is a multi-hop table-based question-answering dataset combining tabular information and textual retrieval. Its evaluation keeps tables with over 100 cells; document retrieval is especially important, and TableRAG scores range from 47.87 to 48.52.

HybridQA is a multi-hop question-answering dataset involving tabular and textual information; the evaluation retains cases with tables containing more than 100 cells. TableRAG obtains a score of 47.87 with Claude-3.5 and DeepSeek-V3 and 48.52 with Qwen-2.5-72B. The ablation study identifies document retrieval as particularly important for HybridQA.
