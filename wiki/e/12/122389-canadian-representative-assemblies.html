Canadian representative Assemblies

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: ecclesiastical authorities

Abstract written by a model

Canadian representative Assemblies, created by the Act of 1791, allowed Catholics of Canada to sit as members, granting privileges denied to co-religionists in Great Britain. During the rebellion of 1837 in Lower Canada, ecclesiastical authorities maintained loyalty; their opposition helped prevent a more serious rebellion and contributed to its defeat.
