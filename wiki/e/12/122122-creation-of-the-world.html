Abstract written by a model

The world is formed after warm wind from the world of light melts the ice of the world of mist, producing Ymir and the cow Audhumbla. Odin, Vili, and Ve later make the earth, seas, mountains, trees, heavens, clouds, and Midgard from Ymir's body.
