Griffin in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

A lion-eagle monster with eagle head, wings, and feathered back, Griffin builds gold nests in India and guards buried treasure, protecting wealth from one-eyed Arimaspians and plunderers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The Griffin had the body of a lion, the head and wings of an eagle, and a feathered back. It was said to build nests of gold, guard buried treasure, and protect its wealth from the one-eyed Arimaspians.
