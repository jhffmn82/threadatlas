{13} CHAPTER III 'THE REIGN OF TERROR' During the session of 1805 the Assembly was confronted with the apparently innocent problem of building prisons.

The 'Reign of Terror' refers to the period after Sir James Craig’s arrival in Canada when his government acted against French-Canadian opposition and Le Canadien. It included the dismissal of militia officers, the suppression of the newspaper, and the arrest of its printer and proprietors.
