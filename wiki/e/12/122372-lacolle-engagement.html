Lacolle engagement in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The Lacolle engagement was a brisk clash in the renewed Lower Canada rebellion, when Cote's returning force met loyalist militia reinforced from Hemmingford; loyalist volunteers won, captured a six-pounder gun, and rebels lost eleven killed to two provincial troops killed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

The engagement occurred when Cote's returning force met loyalist militia reinforced from Hemmingford. The rebels were defeated after twenty to twenty-five minutes, with eleven rebels killed and two provincial troops killed.
