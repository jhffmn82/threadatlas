Abstract written by a model

The sauce box is Mogodore's talking magical box, found in his palace. When opened, it produces thunderous screams, screeches, and threatening warnings of being boiled, stewed, or fried. A hunting dog brings it to Peter, who silences it; later its noise frightens the Swingers, enabling an escape before it is lost.

CHAPTER 13

The sauce box suddenly speaks in a threatening voice, warning the group that they will be boiled, stewed, or fried. A hunting dog carries it to Peter, who closes its lid and stops the threats. Jack advises them to take it along because it may frighten others.

CHAPTER 14

The sauce box belongs to Mogodore and falls from Peter's pocket during the struggle in Swing City. When it opens, its screams, threats, and brazen screeches frighten the Swingers and enable Peter and his companions to escape, although the sauce box is lost afterward.
