R. W. Seton-Watson in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

R. W. Seton-Watson, a historian and political writer knowledgeable about Serbia, supported Scottish Women’s Hospitals through the Servian Unit and praised Elsie Inglis’s heroic work in Serbia and on the Eastern front as a model of sympathy between nations.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ Space utterly forbids our following the fortunes of the Scottish Women’s Hospi

R. W. Seton-Watson wrote that Elsie Inglis was a heroic figure whose work represented sympathy and comprehension between nations. He praised her work in Serbia and on the Eastern front and said that her memory would encourage supporters of the causes for which she gave her life.
