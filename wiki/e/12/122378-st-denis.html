St Denis

Abstract written by a model

St Denis is a village in Lower Canada. In autumn 1837, it was held and strongly defended by a Patriote force under Wolfred Nelson; Colonel Charles Gore's attack failed, making it the rebel forces' only victory. Lieutenant Weir's body was later found in the Richelieu near the village. Gore returned on December 1 to find St Denis quiet, and British soldiers burned much of it.

] {69} CHAPTER VIII THE DOGS OF WAR As the autumn of 1837 wore on, the situation in Lower Canada began to assume an aspect more and more threatening.

St Denis was held by a Patriote force under Wolfred Nelson and was strongly defended with a stockade, fortified brick house, and other positions. Colonel Charles Gore's attack failed, and the British later found Lieutenant Weir's body in the Richelieu near the village.

{82} CHAPTER IX _FORCE MAJEURE_ The check administered to Colonel Gore's column at St Denis, in the first engagement of the rebellion, was the only victory which fell to the rebel forces.

St Denis was the site of the only victory achieved by the rebel forces, when Colonel Gore's column was checked there. Colonel Gore re-entered the place on December 1 and found it quiet, after which British soldiers burned a large part of the village.
