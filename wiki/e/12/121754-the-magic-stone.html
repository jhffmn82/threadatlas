the magic stone

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: colored stone, magic stone, stone

Abstract written by a model

The magic stone is a colored stone Speedy finds in the forest and throws into a horse chestnut tree. Its impact knocks down a burr that becomes Stampedro, changing him from a horse chestnut into a real chestnut horse, and it may restore Peter Pun by breaking his enchantment. It cannot be found again.

CHAPTER FOURTEEN

The magic stone is a colored stone Speedy finds and throws into the branches of a horse chestnut tree. Speedy later suspects it had something magical about it because its impact knocked down the burr that became Stampedro, but the stone cannot be found again.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Speedy found the magic stone in the forest and used it by throwing it at a horse chestnut tree. Its effect changed Stampedro from a horse chestnut into a real chestnut horse, and Sir Hokus suspects it also restored Peter Pun.
