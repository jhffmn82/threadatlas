St Benoit in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

St Benoit, associated with Abbe Chartier and a cure who encouraged rebellion, saw about 250 inhabitants surrender unconditionally to Sir John Colborne under white flags; most of the village was then burned.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{89} But the end of rebellion was not yet in sight.

St Benoit was associated with Abbe Chartier, who supported the rebel movement and later fled. Although about two hundred and fifty inhabitants surrendered at St Benoit under white flags, most of the village was subsequently burned.
