Louis Hippolyte LaFontaine in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Louis Hippolyte LaFontaine was a politician who went abroad in 1837–38, was arrested on suspicion in 1838, later entered Canada’s parliament, formed an administration with Robert Baldwin, helped usher in responsible government, and was knighted by Queen Victoria.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{128} CHAPTER XII A POSTSCRIPT The rebellion of 1837 now belongs to the dead past.

Louis Hippolyte LaFontaine went abroad during the winter of 1837-38 and was arrested on suspicion in November 1838. He later entered the parliament of Canada, formed the LaFontaine-Baldwin administration with Robert Baldwin, helped usher in full responsible government, and was knighted by Queen Victoria.
