Ploppa in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Ploppa is a huge giant mud turtle, a hard-shelled turtle man who needs and loves marsh mud. Faithful and loyal to Sir Hokus, Ploppa rescues him from the marsh, carries him on his shell through the Marshes, and explains the Stick-in-the-Muds’ stilts. Ploppa brings him to Queen Marcia’s hut, answers his call for help by bending barred windows apart, and helps him escape by knocking down stilts. Though Ploppa asks Sir Hokus to stay, he carries him to the yellow plain’s edge and returns sadly to the mud.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER TWO

Ploppa is the giant mud turtle that rescues Sir Hokus from the marsh. Sir Hokus climbs onto Ploppa's shell and tells it to proceed toward the edge of the swamp.

CHAPTER THREE

Ploppa carries Sir Hokus through the marsh and explains that the Stick-in-the-Muds use stilts to keep out of the mud. He brings Sir Hokus to Queen Marcia's hut, later listens to his call for help, and bends apart the barred window with his claws.

CHAPTER FOUR

Ploppa arrives in time to help Sir Hokus escape and knocks down the stilts for miles around. He asks Sir Hokus to remain in the Marshes, but when Sir Hokus chooses to continue, Ploppa sadly carries him to the edge of a wide yellow plain and returns to the mud.
