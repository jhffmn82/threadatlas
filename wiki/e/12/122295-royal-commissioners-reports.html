Abstract written by a model

The royal commissioners' reports were six reports completed in autumn 1836 after an election showed Papineau's strength. They had little direct influence on events in Canada. The final report rejected responsible government and an elective Legislative Council, and considered whether a crisis might warrant total suspension of the colonies' constitution rather than partial interference.
