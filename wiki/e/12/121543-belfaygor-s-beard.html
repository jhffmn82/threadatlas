Belfaygor's beard in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Belfaygor's beard is an extraordinarily long, red, enchanted beard that grows steadily and continuously downward despite clipping and magical attempts to stop it. Its relentless growth first burdens Belfaygor, filling his palace and requiring Jack Pumpkinhead to bear or cut it. It then becomes useful: a swinging rope, bridge across a chasm, climbing line, and a lifesaving cable when it catches on a rock. Finally, it grows from a Baffleburg tower window so Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif can enter, while Belfaygor clips away unneeded portions.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Belfaygor's beard grows in a steady torrent from his chin, flowing down his breast and around his feet. He tries desperately to cut it, but throwing it over one shoulder is the first useful solution offered, after which Jack Pumpkinhead is appointed to bear it.

CHAPTER 7

The red beard grows continuously after the enchantment, filling Belfaygor's palace and later streaming behind him during flight. Belfaygor must repeatedly cut it with shears, while Jack Pumpkinhead cuts it after they stop outside Baffleburg.

CHAPTER 8

Belfaygor's beard is proposed as a bridge across the chasm. Snif will carry its end across and fasten it to a tree, after which Peter and Jack can cross and Belfaygor can cut the beard near his chin and follow.

CHAPTER 10

Belfaygor's beard grows long enough to form a swinging cable across the chasm and later serves as a rope for scaling the cliff. It catches on a rock and saves Belfaygor when the tunnel tips, and it then grows down from a Baffleburg tower window so Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif can enter. Belfaygor clips portions of it whenever it is no longer needed.
