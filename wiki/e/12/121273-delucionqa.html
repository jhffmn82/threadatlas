DelucionQA

dataset · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: DelucionQA [33], DelucionQA test split, DelucionQA(40), Delucionqa

Abstract written by a model

DelucionQA is a human-annotated, RAG-specific hallucination-detection benchmark in customer support. It is a curated collection of queries about operating the 2023 Gladiator, grounded in Jeep’s Gladiator User Manual. The dataset has 913 unique questions and 1,046 context passages; its questions are described as crowd-sourced and as initially generated by large language models. RAGBench builds a vector database from the passages, retrieves up to three documents per question with text-embedding-ada-002, and generates responses with various LLMs. Humans annotate contextual support and hallucination at example and span levels, with 421 annotated examples noted as remaining examples handled by an LLM annotator. The benchmark validates metric formulations and labeling, including comparison of GPT-4 and human annotations on its test split and granular Relevance and Utilization validation using the randomly sampled DelucionQA(40) subset.

DelucionQA is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

RAGBench Statistics .. Alignment with Human Judgements

DelucionQA is a curated benchmark of user queries about the operation of Jeep's 2023 Gladiator model, with contexts from the Jeep Gladiator User Manual and responses generated by various LLMs. Its test split is used to compare GPT-4 annotations with human annotations, and DelucionQA(40) is a randomly sampled subset used for granular Relevance and Utilization validation.

RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]

DelucionQA is a domain-specific RAG dataset based on the Jeep 2023 Gladiator manual, with automatically generated questions and answers and worker annotations of contextual support. RAGBench builds a vector database from its 1,046 passages, retrieves up to three context documents per question using text-embedding-ada-002, and generates LLM responses for 913 unique questions.
