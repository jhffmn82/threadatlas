Abstract written by a model

The Scares are frightful inhabitants and creatures of Scare City, subjects of Harum Scarum the Seventh. They capture Snif after he falls into their city, chain him up, keep him as a curiosity, and plan to make him devour travellers. They rush out to threaten Peter and Jack but are defeated when the pirate sack swallows them with their king. Later they escape, attack a banquet, and obey Ozma’s command to return to Scare City.

Scares is in one work on this site, Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

The Scares rush from the rock dwellings, order Peter and Jack to tremble before the King, and try to frighten and attack them. Peter throws food and dishes at them, after which the pirate sack swallows Harum Scarum and all the Scares.

CHAPTER 5

The Scares are defeated and swallowed by the pirate's sack during Peter and Jack's battle in Scare City. They had captured Snif, chained him to a pillar, and wanted him to devour travellers.

CHAPTER 20

The Scares spill from the pirate sack and attack the banquet hall, frightening and struggling with the Oz folk. Ozma commands them to return to Scare City, and they all obey.
