Mrs. Flinders Petrie in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Mrs. Flinders Petrie, Hon. Secretary, supports Scottish Women’s Hospitals through home fundraising and administration, encouraging subscriptions and receiving Inglis’s requests to hasten relief personnel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

‘_On Active Service.

Mrs. Flinders Petrie receives Inglis’s letters and is praised for encouraging subscriptions to the Scottish Women’s Hospitals. Inglis asks her to press for faster dispatch of relief personnel and sends regards to Professor Flinders Petrie.
