Abstract written by a model

Patients are a group with varying healthcare needs, making it difficult to identify suitable medical professionals. Across 646,383 consultation records, 67.05% are female, 32.95% male, and the largest age group is 20–39 years (54.6%). They may receive preliminary assessments and recommendations using symptoms, history, preferences, and demographics. Improved allocation could reduce waits and improve recommendations, while confidential data require protection. The RAGPR model assists them in finding professionals suited to their needs.
