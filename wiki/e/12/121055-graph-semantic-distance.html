Graph Semantic Distance in SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuzheng Cai, Zhenyue Guo, Yiwen Pei, Wanrui Bian, Weiguo Zheng · 2025

Graph Semantic Distance is a metric and distance measure in SimGRAG for quantifying alignment between a generated or desired pattern graph and candidate isomorphic subgraphs in the KG. Under an isomorphic mapping, it sums L2 distances between embeddings of corresponding nodes and relations, excluding terms marked unknown in generalized computation. Combined with graph isomorphism, it reduces noise and can incorporate somewhat distant entities or relations that provide valuable evidence, avoiding a need for oracle entities. SimGRAG and its retrieval algorithms use the metric to measure semantic difference, rank candidates, and retrieve or select the top-k subgraphs with the smallest distance.

Introduction

Graph Semantic Distance is introduced as a metric for measuring the alignment between a generated pattern graph and candidate isomorphic subgraphs. It sums the semantic distances between corresponding nodes and relations, and SimGRAG selects subgraphs with the smallest Graph Semantic Distance.

The SimGRAG Approach / Query-to-Pattern Alignment .. Verbalized Subgraph-Augmented

Graph Semantic Distance sums the L2 distances between embeddings of matched nodes and relations in an isomorphic pattern graph P and subgraph S. When entities or relations are marked unknown, their terms are excluded from the calculation.

Semantic Guided Subgraph Retrieval / Top-k Retrieval Algorithm .. Optimized Retrieval Algorithm

Graph Semantic Distance measures the semantic difference between the pattern graph P and a retrieved subgraph. The retrieval algorithms seek the top-k subgraphs with the smallest Graph Semantic Distance.

Conclusion

Graph Semantic Distance is introduced to quantify the alignment between a desired pattern and underlying subgraphs in the knowledge graph. It is used to retrieve top-k similar subgraphs with the smallest distance.
