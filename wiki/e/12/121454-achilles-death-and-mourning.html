So the kings drew him from that stricken field

Mourning for Achilles spreads through the Achaean host, the Myrmidons, Briseis and the captive women, Thetis and the Nereids, and the Muses. The lament lasts for many days and is compared to the cries of a city destroyed in battle and to the roar of sea-waves.

BOOK IV

The Argives mourn Achilles beside the ships, with no eye in the host tearless, while the Trojans rejoice at the sight of his funeral fire. Despite their grief, the Argives eat and sleep before preparing for battle and honoring him with games.
