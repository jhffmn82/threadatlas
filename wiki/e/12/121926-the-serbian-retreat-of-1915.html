Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

opening

The Serbian retreat of 1915 began after German forces appeared on the northern frontier, Bulgaria invaded from the east, and Austria advanced from the west. The Serbian army was caught in a trap, but 160,000 of its soldiers escaped.

CHAPTER X RUSSIA 1917 ‘Even so in our mortal journey, The bitter north winds blow, And thus upon life’s red river, Our hearts as oarsmen row.

The Serbian retreat produced large numbers of wounded whom the Scottish Women’s Hospitals treated. At Galatz, the unit worked continuously for fifty-eight of sixty-three hours while performing operations and dressings.
