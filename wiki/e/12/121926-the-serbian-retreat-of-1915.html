Abstract written by a model

The Serbian retreat of 1915 began when German forces appeared on the northern frontier, Bulgaria invaded from the east, and Austria advanced from the west, trapping the Serbian army. Though 160,000 soldiers escaped, the retreat produced many wounded, treated at Galatz by the Scottish Women’s Hospitals working continuously.

The Serbian retreat of 1915 began after German forces appeared on the northern frontier, Bulgaria invaded from the east, and Austria advanced from the west. The Serbian army was caught in a trap, but 160,000 of its soldiers escaped.
