Chapter XVIII

Simonides was a prolific Greek poet known especially for hymns, triumphal odes, and elegies, with a particular gift for pathetic poetry. At Scopas's court, he was summoned away by two young men before the banquet hall collapsed, and he concluded that they were Castor and Pollux.
