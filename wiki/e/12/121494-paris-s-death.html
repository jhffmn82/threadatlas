Paris's death

Abstract written by a model

Paris dies on Mount Ida after Philoctetes wounds him with poison; Oenone refuses healing, and Nymphs and neatherds mourn his body.

Paris's death is in one work on this site, The Fall of Troy. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
