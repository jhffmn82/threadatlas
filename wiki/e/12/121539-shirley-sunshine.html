Abstract written by a model

Shirley Sunshine is a princess, the loveliest in the valley, and Belfaygor's intended bride. Before their arranged marriage can be completed, Mogodore the Mighty abducts her while she is unconscious and confines her in a dismal tower in his castle at Baffleburg. She repeatedly refuses to marry Mogodore, though he intends to make her his bride and later his Lady in Waiting. Seeking delay and freedom, she flatters his ambition by urging him to conquer countries and become a king; this leads him to plan an invasion of Oz. Belfaygor and his companions seek to rescue her, with Belfaygor willing to sacrifice part of his beard. After Belfaygor escapes his adventures, Shirley arrives with him, is introduced to the company, and marries him at the wedding feast.

CHAPTER 8

Shirley Sunshine is locked in a dismal tower and is the reason the group is trying to reach Mogodore's city. Belfaygor is willing to sacrifice part of his beard to help rescue her.

CHAPTER 9

Shirley Sunshine is held captive in a tower by Mogodore and refuses to marry him. Hoping to delay the wedding and secure her freedom, she flatters his ambition by suggesting that he conquer other countries and become a king, but this causes him to plan an invasion of Oz.

Princess Shirley Sunshine is being held by Mogodore, who says she is to marry him rather than Belfaygor. Mogodore orders that she be readied while he marches toward the Emerald City.
