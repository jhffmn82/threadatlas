entering Baffleburg in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The approach to Baffleburg requires crossing a chasm and a tunnel into its courtyard. When gates remain shut after Peter rings, adventurers evade spear defense, enter via Belfaygor’s beard, and descend a spiral stairway.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

The adventurers pass through the tunnel into a courtyard outside Baffleburg's gates, but the gates do not open when Peter rings the bell. After the spear defense stops, Belfaygor enters a low tower window and lowers his beard so the others can climb inside. They then begin descending the tower's spiral stairway.
