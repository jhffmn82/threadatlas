Mladenovatz in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Mladenovatz is the place where the Serbian nation built a fountain near the camp hospital to commemorate Dr. Elsie Inglis and the Scottish Women’s Hospitals. It was formally opened on 7 October 1915 with a Greek Church religious service, and later recalled as a simple fountain dedicated two years earlier.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX SERBIA ‘Send thine hand from above; rid me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of strange children.

At Mladenovatz, the Serbian nation built a beautiful fountain close to the camp hospital to commemorate Dr. Elsie Inglis and the Scottish Women’s Hospitals. The fountain was formally opened on 7 October 1915 with a religious service according to the rites of the Greek Church.

notes

Mladenovatz was the location where the Serbians dedicated the simple fountain two years ago.
