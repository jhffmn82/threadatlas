Xueguang Ma, Shengyao Zhuang, Bevan Koopman, Guido Zuccon, Wenhu Chen, Jimmy Lin · 2025

Qwen2-VL-2B is a fine-tuned visual model evaluated on Wiki-VISA and Paper-VISA. It reaches 54.2% Wiki-VISA bounding-box accuracy, with performance falling from 70.0% for short passages to 18.7% for longer ones, and reports Paper-VISA results.

Fine-tuned Qwen2-VL-2B reaches 54.2% bounding-box accuracy and 65.2% answer accuracy on Wiki-VISA in the single-candidate setup. On Paper-VISA, its visual version achieves 63.0% average bounding-box accuracy and 38.3% answer accuracy.
