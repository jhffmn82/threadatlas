Chapter XXVIII

The Historical theory holds that mythological figures were real human beings whose stories were embellished later. It explains Aeolus as a just ruler who taught navigation and weather knowledge, and Cadmus as a Phoenician emigrant who introduced alphabetic learning to Greece.
