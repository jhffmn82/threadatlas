Penates in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ROMAN DIVINITIES

The Penates were household gods believed to attend to the welfare and prosperity of the family. Their name came from Penus, the pantry, which was sacred to them, and each master of a family served as priest to the Penates of his own house.
