Abstract written by a model

Virgil, also called Maro, was born in Mantua in 70 B.C. A leading poet of the Augustan age, he was praised for correctness and elegance. He wrote the epic Aeneid, depicting the infernal regions: Aeneas enters near Lake Avernus, guided by the Sibyl through the dead’s realms, including underground Elysium.

Virgil is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXV

Virgil places the entrance to the infernal regions near Lake Avernus and describes Aeneas's journey through the realms of the dead. His account assigns Elysium to an underground region and presents the Sibyl as Aeneas's guide.

Chapter XXVIII

Virgil, also called Maro, was born in Mantua in 70 B.C. He was one of the leading poets of the Augustan age, and although he was considered less original than Homer, he was praised for correctness and elegance.
