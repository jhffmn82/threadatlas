threatens French Canadians [by subordinating the French to the English element] The Union Bill of 1822 proposed to unite Upper and Lower Canada in terms that French Canadians believed would extinguish the Lower-Canadian Assembly and subordinate the French to the English element. Louis Joseph Papineau and John Neilson went to London to oppose it and succeeded in having it shelved.
