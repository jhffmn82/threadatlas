Dr. Inglis's funeral and burial in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Dr. Elsie Inglis’s funeral and burial took place in Edinburgh on November 29, 1917. Family, colleagues, societies, hospital officials, military personnel, Serbian representatives, and a massed crowd attended. Her Allied-flag-covered coffin travelled on a gun carriage through the city, with military honours, the Hallelujah Chorus, and Reveille, before Serbian representatives carried it to the grave.

Elsie Inglis’s funeral took place on November 29, 1917, in the presence of her family, colleagues, representatives of societies, hospital officials, military personnel, and Serbian representatives. Her coffin, covered by Allied flags, was placed on a gun carriage, followed through Edinburgh by a large crowd, and carried to the grave by representatives of the Serbian nation.
