consultation records dataset in Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study

Frontiers in Public Health · 2025-01-29

This dataset comprises 646,383 anonymized, structured web-based consultation records collected from 2016 to 2023 by the Internet Hospital of the First Affiliated Hospital of Xiamen University. It covers 193,675 patients, 858 physicians, and 44 departments. Most records involve patients aged 20–39 years, senior physicians, and responses within 90 minutes. The records were formatted for retrieval and analysis and used to evaluate embedding models and large language models for medical-condition label classification and clustering.

medical documents in Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study

Frontiers in Public Health · 2025-01-29

Medical documents are external data for retrieval-augmented generation, studied for integrating extensive records into personalized healthcare. They contain web-based patient-related queries, de-identified physician and patient codes, physician departments, response times, and test-set disease labels, and are used for training, retrieval, evaluation, and baseline experiments.

Medical documents are relevant external data sources that can be located by the retrieval component of Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The study asks how extensive medical documents can be integrated to provide personalized healthcare for patients.
