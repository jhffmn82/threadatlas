Abstract written by a model

The Wizard's searchlight is a nearly perfected new search device, fired like an ordinary shell from a cannon. It travels throughout Oz, finds missing objects or people, and flashes back exact locations. It discovers a magic picture in Ozma's library, then locates the Comfortable Camel and Sir Hokus in Corabia.

CHAPTER SIX

The Wizard's searchlight is a new invention that he is almost finished perfecting. Fired like a shell from a cannon, it is intended to travel throughout Oz, find whatever it is sent for, and return with its exact location.
