Midgard serpent in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Midgard serpent, child of Loki and Angerbode, dwells in the sea after Odin casts it into the ocean. It grows to encircle the earth, is disguised as a cat in Thor's lifting contest, and rises at Ragnarok, where Thor kills it but dies from its venom.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

The Midgard serpent is one of Loki's children. Odin casts it into the ocean surrounding the earth, where it grows so large that it encircles the whole earth with its tail in its mouth.

Chapter XXXII

The Midgard serpent is disguised as the cat in Thor's lifting contest. Thor stretches it so far that it is barely long enough to continue enclosing the earth between its head and tail.

Chapter XXXIII

The Midgard serpent is the child of Angerbode and rises from the sea at Ragnarok. Thor kills it but dies from the venom it vomits.
