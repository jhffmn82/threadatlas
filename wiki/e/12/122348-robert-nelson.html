Robert Nelson

Also called: Nelson, President of the _Provisional Government_

Abstract written by a model

Robert Nelson, brother of Wolfred Nelson, proclaimed Canada’s independence during a second rebellion, became president of a provisional government, and led Canadian refugees in an armed expedition from Napierville. After defeat at Odelltown, he fled to New York, where he practised surgery until his death.
