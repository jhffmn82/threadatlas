embedding models in Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study

Frontiers in Public Health · 2025-01-29

Embedding models encode semantic meaning as numerical vectors essential to retrieval systems and personalized recommendations. Evaluated for labeling medical conditions from disease descriptions, FastText, SBERT, and OpenAI showed that SBERT and OpenAI substantially outperformed FastText; SBERT was selected for implementation based on practical factors.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 Introduction

Embedding models provide linguistic representations that encode semantic meanings as numerical vectors. These representations are essential to retrieval systems, and the study examines their influence on the quality of personalized recommendations.

3 Results / 3.1 Data set summary .. 3.4 Rationality evaluation of LLMs

The embedding models evaluated were FastText, SBERT, and OpenAI for labeling medical conditions from disease-description texts. SBERT and OpenAI substantially outperformed FastText, while SBERT was selected for implementation after considering practical factors.
