Abstract

Heterogeneous documents contain both textual and tabular components. Existing approaches can mishandle them by flattening tables and using chunking strategies that disrupt tabular structure and undermine reasoning on multi-hop and global queries.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

Heterogeneous documents, including word, PDF, CSV, and Excel files, provide the source material for TableRAG's database construction. Structured components are extracted from them to yield a set of tables.
