Abstract written by a model

Heterogeneous documents combine textual and tabular components, including word, PDF, CSV, and Excel files. They supply source material for TableRAG database construction, where structured components yield tables. Existing RAG approaches may flatten or disrupt table structure, undermining reasoning over multi-hop and global queries.
