Psyche's beauty in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Psyche's beauty draws crowds to honor her as Venus, provoking Venus's persecution, yet despite praise and flattery it fails to attract Psyche a husband.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CUPID AND PSYCHE

Psyche's beauty is so great that crowds honor her as Venus, leaving Venus's altars deserted. Venus regards this beauty as an unlawful usurpation of her honors and responds by persecuting Psyche.
