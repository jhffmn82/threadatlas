Abstract written by a model

The royal commission, including Sir Charles Grey and Sir George Gipps, was sent after the Ninety-Two Resolutions and election to investigate and possibly settle Canadian disputes. Its incomplete arrears investigation preceded advice against responsible government and an elective Legislative Council, with possible constitutional suspension.

{44} CHAPTER VI THE ROYAL COMMISSION A general election followed soon after the passing of the Ninety-Two Resolutions and revealed the strength of Papineau's position in the country.

The royal commission was sent to investigate and, if possible, settle the questions in dispute in Canada. Its commissioners ultimately advised against responsible government and an elective Legislative Council and raised the possibility that suspending the constitution might be preferable to partial interference with it.
