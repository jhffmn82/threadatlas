Lord John Russell in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

held_office_in Melbourne administration [as chancellor of the Exchequer] Lord John Russell introduced ten resolutions in the House of Commons on March 6, 1837, dealing with the affairs of Canada. He proposed transferring certain Crown revenues to the Assembly in exchange for a permanent civil list while rejecting key Patriote constitutional demands.

{57} CHAPTER VII THE RUSSELL RESOLUTIONS When the legislature of Lower Canada met in the autumn of 1836, Lord Gosford earnestly called its attention to the estimates of the current year and the accounts showing the arrears unpaid.

Lord John Russell introduced ten resolutions in the House of Commons on March 6, 1837, dealing with the affairs of Canada. He proposed transferring certain Crown revenues to the Assembly in exchange for a permanent civil list while rejecting key Patriote constitutional demands.
