Apollo Belvedere in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Apollo Belvedere is a more-than-seven-foot marble statue in Rome, in the Belvedere apartment of the Pope's palace. An early Roman copy of a Greek original, it depicts Apollo immediately after shooting the arrow that destroys Python; its unknown artist, praised proportions, attitude, and triumphant expression define its significance.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXVIII

The Apollo Belvedere is a marble statue of Apollo, more than seven feet high, whose artist is unknown. It is thought to be Roman art from about the first century and represents Apollo immediately after shooting the Python; its attitude, proportions, and triumphant expression are highly praised.
