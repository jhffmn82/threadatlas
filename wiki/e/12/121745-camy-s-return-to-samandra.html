Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Camy’s return to Samandra ends his captivity by Tuzzle: brought by Royal Sampan to the Sultan’s city, watched along the bank, he cannot speak and is confined as a camel in the royal stable.

CHAPTER TWELVE

Camy is returned to Samandra after being captured by Tuzzle. He is unable to speak there, is treated as a camel rather than as the thinking companion he became in Oz, and is placed in the royal stable under the Sultan's close watch.
