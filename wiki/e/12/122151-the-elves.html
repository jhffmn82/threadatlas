the Elves in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Elves are powerful beings inferior to the gods, divided into fair, kindly Elves of Light and black or Night Elves, subterranean artificers who understand nature's powers and carve and explain runes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXIII

The Elves are beings inferior to the gods but possessing great power. The Elves of Light are fair and kindly, while the black or Night Elves are subterranean artificers who understand nature's powers and carve and explain runes.
