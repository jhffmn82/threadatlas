the royal edict

object · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: law, mandate, orders, ordinance, proclamation of today, royal edict, the edict
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone: imposes death penalty [for transgression] The royal edict orders that Polyneices receive no burial or mourning and that his corpse be left for dogs and birds. Anyone who violates the order is to be put to death.
