the royal edict

object · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: law, mandate, orders, ordinance, proclamation of today, royal edict, the edict

Abstract written by a model

imposes death penalty [for transgression] The royal edict orders that Polyneices receive no burial or mourning and that his corpse be left for dogs and birds. Anyone who violates the order is to be put to death.

the royal edict is in one work on this site, Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
