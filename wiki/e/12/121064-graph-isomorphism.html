Graph Isomorphism

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: graph isomorphism

Abstract written by a model

Graph Isomorphism maps pattern graph P bijectively to subgraph S, requiring corresponding edges in both graphs; it ignores edge direction because knowledge graphs may express the same relation in different directions.

Graph Isomorphism requires a bijective mapping between the nodes of the pattern graph P and subgraph S such that corresponding edges exist in both graphs. Edge direction is not considered during checking because knowledge graphs may express the same relation in different directions.
