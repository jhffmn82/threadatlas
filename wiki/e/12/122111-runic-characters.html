Runic characters in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Runic characters are straight-lined symbols, used first for divination and magic, then engraved in Denmark, Norway, and Sweden, mostly as epitaphs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXIII

Runic letters are chiefly made of straight lines resembling sticks and are found engraved on stones in Denmark, Norway, and Sweden. They were used for divination, magic, and later inscriptions, most of which are epitaphs.
