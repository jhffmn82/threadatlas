Gordius in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Gordius, father of Midas, is a poor countryman made king when an oracle identifies his wagon; he dedicates it and ties a fast knot.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

Gordius was a poor countryman who was made king after an oracle declared that the future king would arrive in a wagon. He dedicated his wagon to the deity of the oracle and tied it with a fast knot.
