Abstract written by a model

The pirate's sack is a famous, magical, trained sack associated with a pirate and later carried or possessed by Peter, then by Jack Pumpkinhead. It was one of two coarse sacks that once held gold from a pirate ship Peter discovered in Oz; after the gold was spent or exchanged, Peter finds a remaining gold piece in one. Though it can carry things, its chief power is swallowing enemies or other beings when opened, revealing fathomless depths. Peter first swings it open against Harum Scarum, the Scares, and the Fraid Cats; it flies out, swallows them, returns to him, and appears empty. It later swallows the whole company of horrors in Scare City, and Peter carries it onward. He plans to use it against Mogodore and his fighting men, but loses it temporarily when a spearman seizes him. Snif recovers it behind a screen, and Peter later finds it cannot swallow apparent Ugly Muglies because they are reflections. Peter again tries to open it over Mogodore's army, but wind tears it away and drops it onto Peter, Belfaygor, and Snif, leaving them and others inside with the Scares, a little boy, a baron, and a flying red monster. Jack takes the sack, seeks help from the Red Jinn, and finally turns it inside out and shakes it three times. This releases Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor, though the Scares initially come out as well.

pirate's sack in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Narrative, section by section written by a model

12 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The two coarse sacks once held gold from a pirate ship that Peter discovered in Oz. One sack's gold was spent on various improvements and purchases, while the other was exchanged for United States dollars and placed in a bank; Peter later finds a remaining gold piece in one of them.

CHAPTER 4

The pirate sack opens when Peter swings it and flies through the air, swallowing Harum Scarum, the Scares, and the Fraid Cats. It then returns to Peter and collapses at his feet, appearing empty; Peter ties it shut and decides to take it to the Emerald City to show the Wizard.

CHAPTER 5

The pirate's sack swallows the entire company of horrors during the battle for Scare City. Peter carries it when he and Jack leave the city, tightening its cord before they ride with Snif.

CHAPTER 7

The pirate's sack is a magical object carried by Peter. Peter plans to open it inside Mogodore's castle so it will swallow Mogodore and his fighting men.

CHAPTER 8

Peter is expected to open the sack once the group is brought before Mogodore. Snif believes the sack will swallow everyone, allowing the group to find Shirley Sunshine and return to the capitol on foot.

CHAPTER 11

Peter carries the pirate's sack into Mogodore's hall and prepares to open it when the discussion becomes dangerous. A spearman seizes Peter before he can use it, and Peter drops the sack during the struggle.

CHAPTER 12

Snif finds the pirate's sack behind a screen in Mogodore's castle. Peter takes it from Snif before they slide down the passage in search of Jack's head.

CHAPTER 13

Peter opens the pirate's sack when the group first sees the apparent Ugly Muglies, but it cannot swallow them because they are only reflections. He closes the sack and carries it with him when the group leaves the palace.

CHAPTER 14

The pirate's sack remains in Peter and his companions' possession throughout the events in Swing City. Peter still intends to open it when they reach Mogodore.

CHAPTER 15

Peter unties the pirate's sack and prepares to open it over Mogodore's army. A gust of wind tears it from his hands, sends it upward, and then brings it crashing down onto Peter, Belfaygor, and Snif. Jack retrieves the sack and intends to take it to the Wizard of Oz.

CHAPTER 16

The pirate's sack contains the little boy, the baron, and the flying red monster. Jack shows the sack to the Red Jinn and asks for help releasing its prisoners.

CHAPTER 20

The pirate sack swallows Peter, Snif, Belfaygor, and others during the adventure. Jack turns it inside out and shakes it three times, releasing Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor but initially unleashing the Scares as well.
