medical assistance application in SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Sihem Omri, Manel Abdelkader, and Mohamed Hamdi · 2025-02-17

The medical assistance application is an LLM-based medical question-answering system built with Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It follows a RAG workflow, retrieving relevant information from an external medical knowledge base to support responses to user queries, and uses aligned large language models. Its answers were first analyzed without SafetyRAG, then after a safety facts document was added to the knowledge base. SafetyRAG extends the application's resilience to malicious prompts while aiming to preserve its primary medical-assistant functionality and improve safe behavior.
