  • In Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz: Snif is a red, stone-like griffin or Iffin: a fabulous flying creature with a huge lion’s body, dragon claws, wide red wings, and a third hind claw. He eats flowers only, speaks gently in questions and in the Grif language, makes verses, and longs to become a true griffin again by recovering his growl. Captured and chained in Scare City for five years, he loses that growl until Peter frees him. Snif then faithfully serves Peter for seven years, carrying Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Belfaygor through the Land of the Barons and toward the Emerald City and Baffleburg. He is brave, cheerful, resourceful, and faithful: he proposes plans, flies riders and Belfaygor’s beard across a chasm, rescues Jack’s head, fights guards, and remains with Peter in captivity. After eating shrinking violets, he becomes squirrel-sized but continues thinking and making verses; when their effect ends, he regains full size and strength, breaks dungeon bars, and helps escape Mogodore’s castle. He faces reflected monsters, travels through Swing City and Mogodore, disappears into a pirate sack, then emerges with Peter and Belfaygor to fight the Scares and help rescue the Kingdom of Oz. At the celebration he entertains with Scraps through rhymes, receives geraniums and an Easter lily, and stays indefinitely in the royal stable.

Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 5

Snif is an Iffin who lost his growl after being captured and imprisoned in Scare City for five years. Peter frees him, and after briefly testing his wings, Snif agrees to carry Peter and Jack and serve them faithfully for seven years. He flies them over the wall into the Land of the Barons and lands in a peaceful valley.

CHAPTER 6

Snif carries Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead toward the Emerald City, stopping to eat geraniums and then waddling because he is too full to fly. He recognizes Belfaygor as one of the good barons and offers to help rescue Shirley Sunshine with his wings and a magic sack.

CHAPTER 7

Snif carries Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Belfaygor toward Baffleburg and helps them reach the ground outside the chasm. After eating the shrinking violets, Snif shrinks to a creature no bigger than a squirrel.

CHAPTER 8

Snif explains that he shrank after eating violets, but he remains able to think and make verses. He devises the plan to use Belfaygor's beard as a bridge and expects the Baffleburghers to seize the travelers and take them to Mogodore.

CHAPTER 10

Snif practices his growls and worries about being too small to fight, but he remains cheerful and helps the others. He flies Belfaygor's beard across the chasm, assists in scaling the cliff, rescues Jack's head during the tunnel accident, and later climbs into the Baffleburg tower by flying. Snif initially considers flying over the city walls to open the gates.

CHAPTER 11

Snif flies ahead, rests on Peter's shoulder, and pretends to threaten the mounted men with magic. He helps Peter during the capture attempt, bites and scratches the guards, and remains imprisoned with Peter because he cannot fit through the dungeon bars.

CHAPTER 12

Snif returns to his full size and strength after the shrinking violets wear off, breaking the dungeon bars and overpowering the guards. He helps Peter and Belfaygor escape Mogodore's castle and joins the search for the forbidden flagon.

CHAPTER 13

Snif panics at the apparent Ugly Muglies and charges into the magic mirrors, discovering that the monsters are only reflections. He helps Peter and Belfaygor return with the Forbidden Flagon, joins Jack and Peter for the departure, and nearly chokes after swallowing a geranium when the sauce box threatens them.

CHAPTER 14

Snif carries Peter and the others toward the capitol and provides them with food during the flight. In Swing City, he tries to rescue Peter and Belfaygor but is buffeted by the aerialists, and he later catches Jack's head as it falls. He helps the group escape the net and resumes the journey.

CHAPTER 15

Snif flies Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Belfaygor toward Mogodore and identifies Mogodore and his men below. He warns the others when the pirate's sack flies toward them. Snif disappears into the fathomless depths of the sack.

CHAPTER 20

Snif, an Iffin or Griffin, emerges from the pirate sack with Peter and Belfaygor and helps fight the Scares. He participates in the celebration, entertains the company with Scraps through rhymes, and is given eight geranium plants and an Easter lily.
