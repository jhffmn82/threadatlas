Oenone and Paris's cremation in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Oenone and Paris burn together on a pyre while her father and maidens sleep; after fire consumes them, wine quenches their embers, their bones enter a golden vase, and a mound with two pillars is raised.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then, while her father and her maidens slept,

Oenone and Paris burn together on the pyre, never to see the dayspring again. After the fire consumes them, their embers are quenched with wine, their bones are placed in a wide golden vase, and a mound and two pillars are raised over them.
