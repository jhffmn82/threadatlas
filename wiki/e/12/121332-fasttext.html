Abstract written by a model

FastText is a word-level embedding model using cc.zh.300. Evaluated on a 965-document test dataset, it showed moderate clustering of medical-condition labels but considerable cluster overlap, and achieved precision 0.52, recall 0.44, and F1-score 0.46.
