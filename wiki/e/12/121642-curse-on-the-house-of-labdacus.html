Abstract written by a model

The curse on the house of Labdacus, marked by blood-stains and hidden ills, afflicts Thebes and Oedipus’s sons as inherited suffering. After Jocasta’s death and Oedipus’s blinding, ruin deepens; the sons’ plan to leave the throne to Creon gives way to rivalry.
