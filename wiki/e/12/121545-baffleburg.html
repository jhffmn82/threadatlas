Baffleburg in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Baffleburg is a fortified mountain city and town built into a red mountain, surrounded by a chasm and guarded by warriors, forts, walls, and Mogodore's guards. Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 7

Baffleburg is a fortified city built into a red mountain, surrounded by a chasm and defended by warriors, forts, and city walls. No one is said to have entered the city or returned alive from Mogodore's mountain.

CHAPTER 9

Baffleburg is Mogodore's mountain domain, where he rules as baron. Wagarag argues that Mogodore should remain there, where he is known and powerful, rather than attempt to conquer Oz.

CHAPTER 10

Baffleburg is a fortified city with high gray walls, buttressed towers, wooden-backed gates, and a large bell over the entrance. Its defenses include four towers that tilt downward and shoot golden spears into the courtyard. The adventurers reach a tower inside the city walls but have not yet explored the city itself.

CHAPTER 11

Baffleburg is a steep mountain town with red stone cottages, a narrow cobbled street, and a castle reached through red gates. Its mounted men capture Peter's group and bring them to Mogodore.

CHAPTER 20

Baffleburg is part of the route in Jack Pumpkinhead's account of the journey from Scare City to Baffleburg and then to Swing City. The story also concerns Belfaygor's escape from Baffleburg.
