Abstract written by a model

Odelltown engagement was a battle in the second rebellion: rebel forces attacked about two hundred loyal militia under Lieutenant-Colonel Taylor at the Methodist church, but loyalist sallies and a flank attack defeated them after about two and a half hours, leaving fifty rebels dead.
