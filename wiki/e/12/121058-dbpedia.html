Abstract written by a model

DBpedia is a knowledge graph used with FactKG for fact verification. It contains Georgian-related entities, including Georgian architecture, architecture, buildingType, architecturalStyle, and architect, with stated keyword ranks. In a 10-million-scale retrieval experiment, optimized semantic guided subgraph retrieval verified statements in 0.74 seconds per query.

DBpedia is in one work on this site, SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
