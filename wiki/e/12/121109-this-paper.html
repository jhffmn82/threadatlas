Abstract written by a model

This paper scopes retrieval through a graph of triples bridging corresponding passages. It demonstrates the effectiveness of graph expansion and GEAR, using gist memory and a reasoner as agent components, while citing work on entity disambiguation, graph completion, inference, and validated practices. It ends by identifying components for future refinement.
