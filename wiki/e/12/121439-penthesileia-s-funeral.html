Then did the warrior sons of Argos strip

Penthesileia's funeral includes a pyre, armor, costly treasures, her war-horse, fragrant wine, ointment, and the fat of a heifer. Her bones are placed in a casket and buried beside Laomedon, while the Amazons are buried nearby.
