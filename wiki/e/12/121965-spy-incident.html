’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

During the spy incident, the unit is suspected because it is a foreign hospital near the front while spy fever and revolutionary unrest are widespread. General Kropensky helps resolve the matter, the unit is cleared and apologized to, and Elsie says its conduct preserved Russian confidence in England.
