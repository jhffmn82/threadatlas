Abstract written by a model

In "RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models," Claude-3.5-Sonnet was the most robust model evaluated, ranked safest in RAG, and achieved the highest reported RAG-task accuracy, though its accuracy dropped sharply with random documents. In "TableRAG: A Retrieval Augmented Generation Framework for Heterogeneous Document Reasoning," Claude-3.5-Sonnet served as the representative closed-source backbone for TableRAG and several baselines, achieving scores of 47.87 on HybridQA, 84.62 on WikiTQ, and 44.19 overall on HeteQA.
