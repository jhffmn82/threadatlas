Abstract written by a model

Cyclic Poets were bards who collectively narrated the Trojan War cycle, from before to after The Iliad; their works are lost, surviving only in fragments and summaries.

The Fall of Troy

The Cyclic Poets narrated events before and after those covered by The Iliad and together told the full cycle of the Trojan War. None of their works has survived, although fragments and summaries remain.
