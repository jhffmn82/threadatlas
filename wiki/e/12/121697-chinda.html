Abstract written by a model

Chinda is a seer, prophet, Chief Seer, Chief Prophet, and Grand Bozzywoz of Samandra. After searching for a lost camel for ten years, he uses a new lens in his magic telescope to locate it in the Emerald City, leading the Sultan to promote him to Magician Extraordinary and Grand Bozzywoz of the Realm. Later, he identifies Camy as the Sultan's Comfortable Camel and investigates a missing package, foretelling that part of it will be found in the middle of the night while continuing to seek its exact location.

Chinda is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER FIVE

Chinda, the Seer and Prophet of Samandra, explains that he has searched for the camel for ten years. Using a new lens in his magic telescope, he sees the camel in the Emerald City and is promoted by the Sultan to Magician Extraordinary and Grand Bozzywoz of the Realm.

CHAPTER TWELVE

Chinda announces that Camy is the Sultan's Comfortable Camel and later tells the Sultan to consult the magic telescope about the missing package. After looking through it for ten minutes, he divines that part of what the Sultan seeks will be found in the middle of the night. He then offers to continue working in silence to determine the package's exact location.
