legislature of Lower Canada in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The legislature of Lower Canada was a legislative body first called in 1792. Its first Parliament raised controversy over the status and use of the French language in the Assembly. When it met in autumn 1836, Lord Gosford urged attention to estimates and unpaid arrears, but it again refused necessary supplies until grievances were redressed. This continued resistance contributed to abandoning conciliation without concession.

{7} CHAPTER II THE RIGHTS OF THE DEFEATED The British did not treat the French inhabitants of Canada as a conquered people; not as other countries won by conquest have been treated by their victorious invaders.

The first Parliament of Lower Canada was called in 1792. Its opening raised the question of the status and use of the French language in the Assembly.

{57} CHAPTER VII THE RUSSELL RESOLUTIONS When the legislature of Lower Canada met in the autumn of 1836, Lord Gosford earnestly called its attention to the estimates of the current year and the accounts showing the arrears unpaid.

When it met in the autumn of 1836, the legislature of Lower Canada again refused to vote the necessary supplies because grievances had not been redressed. Its continuing resistance contributed to the abandonment of the policy of conciliation without concession.
