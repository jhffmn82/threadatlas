constitution of the colonies in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The constitution of the colonies was used by the royal commissioners as a reason for rejecting responsible government in its modern form. The commissioners also considered whether total suspension of the constitution might be less objectionable than altering particular clauses.
