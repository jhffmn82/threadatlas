Abstract written by a model

King of Corabia rules Corabia and is father to Princess Marygolden. After five hundred years immersed in the yellow river, he announces a contest to rescue his enchanted daughter, aids the Yellow Knight, offers Speedy half his kingdom, and supports punishing the Sultan.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

The King announces the conditions of the grand contest and explains that its winner must rescue his enchanted daughter. He gives the Yellow Knight the last magic date and later offers Speedy half his kingdom for helping restore the Princess.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The King of Corabia welcomes Ozma and hears the account of Sir Hokus and Speedy's adventures. He agrees that the Sultan should lose his magic, return what he stole, and remain in Samandra for five hundred years.
