CUAD

dataset · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Cuad

Abstract written by a model

CUAD is a collection of 510 commercial legal contracts from EDGAR, with 21k expert-annotated questions, responses, and relevant contract portions. It supplies legal-contract documents and expert-composed questions to RAGBench. Its up-to-11k-token, contract-specific contexts create a specialized long-context setting; RAGBench uses one contract, samples five questions, and generates LLM responses, with about a 1% hallucination rate.

CUAD is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CUAD contributes legal-contract documents and expert-composed questions. It has particularly long contexts, reaching up to 11k tokens per document, and is challenging because it represents a specialized domain and long-context RAG setting.

RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]
