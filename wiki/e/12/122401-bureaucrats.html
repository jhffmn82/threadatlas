Bureaucrats

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: 'Bureaucrats'

Abstract written by a model

Bureaucrats were the Chateau Clique, a government party and political faction: a local oligarchy, generally of English birth, that controlled Lower Canada through the governor, Executive Council, and Legislative Council. During Sir James Craig’s administration they surrounded him, could disregard the Assembly majority, and possibly sought racial conflict to suspend Lower Canada’s constitution.

Bureaucrats is in one work on this site, The 'Patriotes' of '37. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

{21} CHAPTER IV THE RISE OF PAPINEAU In the year 1812 a young man took his seat in the House of Assembly for Lower Canada who was destined to play a conspicuous part in the history of the province during the next quarter of a century.

The Bureaucrats were the local oligarchy that controlled the government of Lower Canada through the governor, Executive Council, and Legislative Council. They were generally of English birth and could often disregard the majority in the House of Assembly.
