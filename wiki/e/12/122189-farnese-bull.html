Farnese Bull

Also called: This group, the Farnese Bull

Abstract written by a model

The Farnese Bull, created by Apollonius with Tauriscos of Tralles, represents Dirke’s punishment by Antiope’s sons: one prepares to bind her to a bull as she pleads for mercy. Its technical skill is marvellous, but its complicated subject and subsidiary figures lessen its tragic grandeur.

In Athens, after Pheidias, the greatest sculptures were those

The Farnese Bull represents the punishment of Dirke by the sons of Antiope, with one son preparing to bind her to a bull while she pleads for mercy. Although technically remarkable, it is judged to lack tragic grandeur because of its complicated subject, subsidiary figures, and focus on momentary, apparently unmotivated suffering.
