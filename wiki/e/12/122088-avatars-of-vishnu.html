Avatars of Vishnu

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Avatar, Avatars, bodily forms, incarnations

Abstract written by a model

The Avatars of Vishnu are ten bodily forms through which Vishnu descends to protect the world in danger, beginning with Matsya, who preserved Manu in a deluge, including Krishna, and ending with Kalki at the present age’s end.

Avatars of Vishnu is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXX

The Avatars of Vishnu are bodily forms in which Vishnu descends to earth to protect the world during times of danger. The text identifies ten especially important Avatars, including Matsya, the Fish, who preserved Manu during a universal deluge, the Tortoise, and Krishna; Kalki is the tenth.
