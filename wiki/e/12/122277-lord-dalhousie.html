Lord Dalhousie in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Lord Dalhousie became governor of Lower Canada in 1819 and was described as liberal and friendly toward relations between the two races. After Papineau attacked him over financial irregularities and Caldwell's defalcations, he refused to recognize Papineau as speaker and dissolved the Assembly; a deputation later obtained his recall.

{21} CHAPTER IV THE RISE OF PAPINEAU In the year 1812 a young man took his seat in the House of Assembly for Lower Canada who was destined to play a conspicuous part in the history of the province during the next quarter of a century.

Lord Dalhousie became governor of Canada in 1819 and was described as liberal and friendly toward relations between the two races. Papineau attacked him over financial irregularities and Caldwell's defalcations, after which Dalhousie refused to recognize Papineau as speaker and dissolved the Assembly. A later deputation obtained Dalhousie's recall.
