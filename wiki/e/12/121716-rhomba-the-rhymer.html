Rhomba, the Rhymer in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Rhomba, a skilled rhymer and the Shah's Headman and Chief Counsellor, questions Speedy in verse, conveys the Shah's doubt, and demands that he mend the sky or face punishment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SEVEN

Rhomba is the Shah's Headman and speaks in rhymed verses. He questions Speedy, tells him that the Shah doubts his story, and explains that Speedy must mend the sky or be treated as a lawbreaker.
