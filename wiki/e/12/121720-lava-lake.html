Lava Lake in The Yellow Knight of Oz

CHAPTER SEVEN

Lava Lake contains fire fish. Rhomba says that lawbreakers are usually thrown to the fire fish there, and Speedy may suffer that fate for breaking the sky.

CHAPTER EIGHT

Lava Lake lies in the Shah's hunting ground beneath a lavafall. Fire fish about the size of sharks rise from it and snap their flaming teeth at Speedy, causing him to flee.
