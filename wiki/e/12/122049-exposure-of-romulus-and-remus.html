In infancy, Romulus and Remus are exposed on the Tiber after Rhea Silvia is punished, then rescued by a herdsman and raised among shepherds unaware of their parentage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Romulus and Remus are exposed on the Tiber after their mother, Rhea Silvia, is punished for breaking her vow as a Vestal Virgin. A herdsman rescues them, and they are raised among shepherds without knowing their parentage.
