query-to-pattern alignment

topic · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: query-to-pattern, query-to-pattern alignment stage

Abstract written by a model

Query-to-pattern alignment uses large language models to transform query text Q into a pattern graph P whose triples reflect query semantics, without requiring exact KG entities or relations. It begins by segmenting phrases for each triple, then applies explicit requirements and typically 12 manually constructed few-shot examples to guide prediction of nodes, edges, and structure. It can reach 98% accuracy on MetaQA and 93% on FactKG for up to 3 hops, but is difficult for industrial domain-specific knowledge graphs with divergent schemas.

The SimGRAG Approach / Query-to-Pattern Alignment .. Verbalized Subgraph-Augmented

Query-to-pattern alignment prompts a large language model to generate a pattern graph P whose triples reflect the semantics of a query. The process first asks for segmented phrases for each triple and uses explicit requirements and manually constructed few-shot examples to guide generation.

Limitations

Query-to-pattern alignment relies on the LLM to predict desired nodes, edges, and pattern structures. This process becomes difficult for industrial domain-specific knowledge graphs that diverge from commonly used schemas.
