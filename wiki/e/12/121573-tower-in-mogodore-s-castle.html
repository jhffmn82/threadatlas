Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The tower holds Snif and Peter in a dungeon and Belfaygor above; Snif regains full size, breaks the bars, and enables the prisoners' escape.

CHAPTER 12

The tower contains the dungeon where Snif and Peter are imprisoned and the higher cell where Belfaygor is held. Snif's return to full size breaks the dungeon bars, allowing the prisoners to escape.
