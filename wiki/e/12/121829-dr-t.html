Abstract written by a model

Dr. T. opposed women attending mixed classes at Glasgow Infirmary, conveyed managers’ ban in 1891, threatened the campaign with physical force, then, after resistance, conveyed that women could attend any classes.

’ In 1891 Elsie went to Glasgow to take the examination for the Triple Qualifica

Dr. T. conveyed the Glasgow Infirmary Managers’ decision that women students were not to attend mixed classes. After resistance from the students and their supporters, he eventually conveyed that they could attend any of the classes.
