CHAPTER SEVEN

The Underworld is divided into nine levels: Neath and Underneath, Low and Below, Down, Upsidedown, Farther Down, Allthewaydown, and Subterranea. Speedy travels through roots, rocks, lava, and underground seas before reaching Subterranea.
