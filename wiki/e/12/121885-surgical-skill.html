surgical skill in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Dr. Inglis developed marked surgical skill while working in the Scottish Women’s Hospitals, where the pressure of work often kept her operating for days. This experience indicated that she might have become a great surgeon if she had received equal opportunities during her peace practice.
