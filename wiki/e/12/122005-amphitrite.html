Amphitrite in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Amphitrite, daughter of Nereus and Doris and mother of Triton, becomes Neptune’s wife after he courts and wins her riding a dolphin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XV

Amphitrite is Neptune’s wife, the daughter of Nereus and Doris, and the mother of Triton. Neptune courts her while riding a dolphin, and after winning her he places the dolphin among the stars.
