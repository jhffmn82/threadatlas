Dr. Inglis's last message in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

A November 26, 1917 message, dictated through Evelyn Simson to the London Committee for S.W.H., apologizes for inability to go to London, asks hospitality for Serbian representatives, and urges support for Serbia through hospitals and transport, while thanking the committee.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

”’ As ‘We’ waited those anxious weeks for the news of the arrival of Dr.

In a message dated November 26, 1917, Elsie Inglis apologized for being unable to go to London and asked the Scottish Women’s Hospitals committee to meet Serbian representatives and show them hospitality. She specifically urged the committee to ensure that the Serbs had their hospitals and transport, thanked the committee for its kindness and support, and dictated the message to Miss Evelyn Simson.
