In a message dated November 26, 1917, Elsie Inglis apologized for being unable to go to London and asked the Scottish Women’s Hospitals committee to meet Serbian representatives and show them hospitality. She specifically urged the committee to ensure that the Serbs had their hospitals and transport, thanked the committee for its kindness and support, and dictated the message to Miss Evelyn Simson.
