Abstract written by a model

The bringing of Achilles' son from Scyros to the Achaeans begins when Odysseus and Diomede win him with honor, armor, gifts, and marriage prospects. Despite Deidameia's delay, he sails to camp, restores hope, arms, fights, and saves the Achaeans' day.

BOOK VII

Odysseus and Diomede bring Achilles' son from Scyros by offering him honor, Achilles' armor, gifts, and the possibility of marriage to Menelaus' daughter. His departure is delayed briefly by Deidameia's pleading, but he ultimately sails with them and twenty household attendants.

So came they to the heavy-plunging sea,

Neoptolemus's arrival at the Achaean camp restores hope to the hard-pressed Greeks. He immediately arms himself, joins the fighting, drives back Eurypylus's forces, and becomes the chief reason the Achaeans survive the day.
