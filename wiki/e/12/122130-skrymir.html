Skrymir in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Skrymir is a giant of great size whose glove and thumb are mistaken for shelter; traveling with Thor, he secures their food and, through Utgard-Loki’s illusion, endures Thor’s three mallet blows.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Skrymir is a giant whose glove is mistaken for a hall and whose thumb is mistaken for a chamber. He travels with Thor and his companions, ties their provisions so tightly that Thor cannot open the wallet, and withstands three blows from Thor's mallet by means of Utgard-Loki's illusion.
