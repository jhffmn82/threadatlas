[Enter TEIRESIAS and BOY]

Creon admits that he is responsible for Haemon’s and Eurydice’s deaths, calling himself their murderer. He asks to be led away and longs for death, while the Chorus says that he must endure the consequences and that wisdom comes through chastisement.
