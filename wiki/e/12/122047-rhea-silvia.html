Abstract written by a model

Rhea Silvia, Numitor's daughter, is made a Vestal Virgin by Amulius, bears Romulus and Remus by Mars after breaking celibacy, and is buried alive for breaking her vow.

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Rhea Silvia, the daughter of Numitor, is made a Vestal Virgin by Amulius. She breaks her vow of celibacy and gives birth to Romulus and Remus, after which she is buried alive.
