Dr. Inglis joined the Central Edinburgh Women’s Liberal Association in its early organised years and served as its Vice-President for sixteen years. She was one of its most active members, valued as a speaker and adviser, and respected for her enthusiasm for women’s welfare and her clear political judgment.
