Corumbia

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: Corumbians, two kingdoms

Abstract written by a model

Corumbia is a kingdom with the Golden Castle, Castle of Corumbia, ruled by its King. Conquered by the Sultan of Samandra five hundred years earlier, it is later released from his enchantment: its King, Queen, subjects, and castle are restored. Its Prince, enchanted as Sir Hokus of Pokes, returns as the Yellow Knight. The rulers celebrate, prepare for attack, join the Sultan's sentencing, and reward Speedy.

Corumbia is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Corumbia is released from the Sultan's five-hundred-year enchantment, and its King, Queen, subjects, and castle are restored. The kingdom celebrates its recovery while preparing for a possible attack by the Sultan.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Corumbia is the kingdom whose Prince was enchanted as Sir Hokus of Pokes and restored as the Yellow Knight. Its King and Queen celebrate the Prince's return.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

Corumbia is represented by its King and Queen, who arrive with Peter Pun after the enchantment is broken. They rejoice with the rulers of Corabia and offer Speedy a place and reward.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Corumbia is restored after the Sultan's transformations, and Sir Hokus is revealed to be its Prince. Its King participates in sentencing the Sultan and knights Speedy.
