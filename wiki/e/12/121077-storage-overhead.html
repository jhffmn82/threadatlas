5 / Conclusion and Discussion .. Limitations

Storage overhead is an intentional tradeoff in TurboRAG because pre-computed KV caches occupy substantial disk space. For Qwen2-7B, one million 512-token chunks require approximately 28 TB of FP16 storage, while practical deployments with fewer passages require less and can benefit from quantization.
