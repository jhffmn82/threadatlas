Sir Francis Bond Head in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Sir Francis Bond Head, newly appointed lieutenant-governor of Upper Canada, published Colonial Office instructions to its legislature; this exposed British opposition to real constitutional change and undermined Lord Gosford’s conciliatory policy in Lower Canada.

{44} CHAPTER VI THE ROYAL COMMISSION A general election followed soon after the passing of the Ninety-Two Resolutions and revealed the strength of Papineau's position in the country.

Sir Francis Bond Head, the lieutenant-governor of Upper Canada, disclosed his instructions from the Colonial Office to the legislature of Upper Canada. The disclosure revealed Britain's opposition to real constitutional changes and damaged Lord Gosford's conciliatory policy in Lower Canada.
