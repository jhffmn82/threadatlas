Subterranea in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Subterranea is an underground kingdom, city, and region at one of the lowest named levels of the Underworld, inhabited by the Subterraneans and ruled by the Shah. Its tiled passages, homes, square beneath a blue stone sky with radium stars, hunting grounds, Lava Lake, tunnels, and cavernous country define it. Its people fear sunlight during a sun storm. Princess Marygolden is sent there as a statue for the Shah; later, Speedy and Marygolden escape upward in the parashuter, leaving him behind.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SEVEN

Subterranea is an underground kingdom at one of the lowest levels of the Underworld. Its city has a public square, blue stone ceilings, radium stars, and tiled passageways decorated with precious stones.

CHAPTER EIGHT

Subterranea contains twisting tunnels, underground homes, a public square, the Shah's hunting ground, Lava Lake, and cavernous country beyond the underwood. Its inhabitants fear rays of sunlight, which fall during a sun storm.

CHAPTER THIRTEEN

Subterranea is the underground place from which Speedy and Marygolden escape. They rise out of it through the earth in the parashuter and leave the Shah behind.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Subterranea is the place to which the Sultan changed and sent Princess Marygolden, who was transformed into a statue and given to the Shah of Subterranea.
