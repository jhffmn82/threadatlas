retrieval triggers

mechanism · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: retrieval trigger, triggering RAG

Abstract written by a model

Retrieval triggers address ineffective control in existing dynamic RAG methods: an early confidence assessment activates retrieval when needed, and likely hallucinated generated tokens also activate it, introducing retrieved knowledge before or at the relevant generation point.
