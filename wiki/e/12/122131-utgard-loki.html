Utgard-Loki in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Utgard-Loki, king and ruler of Utgard, receives Thor, Thialfi, and Loki and uses illusions, including contests with a horn, to expose their apparent weaknesses. He later reveals the deceptions involving Skrymir, Logi, Hugi, the cat, and Elli, then disappears.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Utgard-Loki receives Thor, Thialfi, and Loki at his city and challenges them to contests designed to expose their apparent weaknesses. He later explains that he used illusions involving Skrymir, Logi, Hugi, the drinking horn, the cat, and Elli, then disappears before Thor can attack him or destroy the city.
