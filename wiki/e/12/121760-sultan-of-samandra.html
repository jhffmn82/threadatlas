Abstract written by a model

The Sultan of Samandra is Samandra’s sovereign, ruler, and monarch, said to have ruled for seven centuries. He possesses the Sultan’s magic, magic dates, a royal barge, a camel corps, and a royal camel herd; Confido, his Imperial Peke, is his confidant. Five hundred years earlier, he conquered Corabia and Corumbia by magic, stole the treasures of their kings, and cast enchantments against neighboring monarchs and their children. His spells transformed Corabia and Corumbia, including Princess Marygolden, whom he changed into a statue and kept separated from her parents and former identity for five centuries. In the later events, he has spent ten years seeking his lost favorite camel, Camy, though his real objective is a package in Camy’s left-hand saddle sack. Learning that Camy is in the Emerald City, he sends Tuzzle to Ozma with greetings and embroidered shawls to request the camel’s return. When Camy returns to Samandra, the Sultan has him taken to the Royal Camel Quarters, finds the package missing, angrily blames Camy, consults Chinda, and waits for a divination to reveal it. He urgently seeks magic dates and confronts Sir Hokus and companions, accusing them of stealing the dates and ruining his strongest enchantment; he orders their seizure and summons Samandran spearmen. Ultimately, while attacking the King of Corumbia, he admits his thefts and transformations. Ozma strips him of his magic powers and sends him back to Samandra, ordering him to return the stolen treasures and remain within his country for five hundred years.

Sultan of Samandra in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930 · the work’s page · read it where Sultan of Samandra first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIVE

The Sultan anxiously learns from Chinda that his lost camel is in the Emerald City and orders Tuzzle to bring it back. He reveals to Confido that his true concern is the package in the camel's left hand saddle sack, whose recovery he believes is essential to him.

CHAPTER SIX

The Sultan sends Tuzzle to Ozma with greetings and twenty embroidered shawls, asking for the return of a camel lost from his royal herd ten years earlier. The camel is described as his favorite mount and as a source of great comfort to him.

CHAPTER TWELVE

The Sultan welcomes Camy back to Samandra and personally has him taken to the Royal Camel Quarters. When he searches Camy's left-hand saddle sack, he discovers that the valuable package he wants is missing and angrily blames Camy. He consults Chinda and waits anxiously for nightfall after learning that part of the missing object will be found in the middle of the night.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

The Sultan confronts Sir Hokus and his companions while clutching Confido. He accuses them of stealing his magic dates and ruining his strongest enchantment, orders them seized and sliced in two, and summons the Samandran spearmen.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

The Sultan is identified as a possible pursuer and as the villain who cast the spell over Corumbia. He is not seen in this unit, but the King orders guards posted in case he attacks during the night.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

The Sultan's earlier enchantments have transformed the people and places of Corabia, although his magic is being undone through the remaining dates.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

The Sultan's spell is identified as the magic keeping Princess Marygolden from her parents and former identity. Speedy suspects the Sultan may be responsible when a sudden flash of magic strikes the company, but the text does not confirm this.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The Sultan is summoned while leading an attack on the King of Corumbia and admits that he stole treasures and transformed the rulers and their children. Ozma removes his magic powers, and he is sent back to Samandra to return what he stole and remain within his country for five hundred years.
