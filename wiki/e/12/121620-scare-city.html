Abstract written by a model

Scare City is a grim, red and sulphurous cliff city, home of the Scares, entered through a dark corridor and red spiked gates bearing scare heads. Its great court, crudely cut cliff dwellings and steps are lit by goblin lanterns, marked with frowning faces, and shrouded in bluish green smoke. Frightening noises, horrors, and prisoners frightened into statues or Fraid Cats make it far less beautiful than the Quadling Country. Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead enter when the gates open to a welcoming voice. When the Scares vanish, they are trapped in the silent city until their conquest; then they leave with Snif by flying over the wall. Later, Ozma sends the Scares back to Scare City after their attack on the banquet, and they return immediately.

Scare City is in one work on this site, Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3

Scare City is reached through a dark corridor filled with frightening goblin lanterns and noises. Its red, spiked gates bear scare heads and open while a voice welcomes Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead.

CHAPTER 4

Scare City is a grim cliff city with jagged rocks, carved dwellings and steps, frightening drawings, goblin lanterns, and bluish green smoke. After the Scares disappear, Peter and Jack cannot open the gate or find another way out, leaving them trapped in the silent city.

CHAPTER 5

Scare City is a red cliff city filled with horrors, goblin lanterns, sulphurous air, and prisoners who have been frightened into statues or Fraid Cats. After Peter and Jack's conquest, they leave the city with Snif by flying over its wall.

CHAPTER 6

Scare City is the place Peter and Jack Pumpkinhead have recently left. After experiencing it, they find the quiet valley and surrounding country especially beautiful.

CHAPTER 20

Scare City is the place to which Ozma sends the Scares after they attack the banquet. The Scares return there immediately on Ozma's command.
