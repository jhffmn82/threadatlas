Tithonus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Tithonus, son of Laomedon and Aurora’s favored mortal, fathers Memnon; granted immortality without youth, he ages helplessly in confinement and is eventually changed into a grasshopper.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XIX

Tithonus was the son of Laomedon and Aurora’s greatest mortal favorite. Aurora secured immortality for him, but not perpetual youth, so he grew old, lost the use of his limbs, and was shut in a chamber. He was eventually changed into a grasshopper.
