unsafe prompts in SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Sihem Omri, Manel Abdelkader, and Mohamed Hamdi · 2025-02-17 · the work’s page · read it where unsafe prompts first appears

Abstract written by a model

Unsafe prompts are harmful or offensive requests used to test and improve LLM safety. They are represented as safety facts in SafetyRAG’s knowledge base and include profanity, threats, discrimination, misinformation, cybercrime, and illegal or dangerous suggestions. The study selected and paraphrased 100 AdvBench prompts, encoded and matched them to safety-fact embeddings, and evaluated them directly and with adversarial prompt injection. SafetyRAG helps LLMs decline these requests and generally raises refusal rates, especially for prompt-injection cases.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract—Large Language Model (LLM) agents have

Unsafe prompts are the subject of safety facts stored in the knowledge base used by SafetyRAG. The framework helps LLMs decline requests involving them.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW / A. Threats of Unsafe Prompts for LLMs .. B. Unsafe Prompt Mitigation

Unsafe prompts can cause dialogue systems and LLMs to produce harmful or offensive content. The unit discusses detecting and mitigating them through benchmarks, guardrails, and the SafetyRAG framework.

III. MATERIALS AND METHODS / A. Overall Framework .. 2) Baseline LLM models

Unsafe prompts are encoded and matched against safety-fact embeddings through similarity search. The source prompts included content involving profanity, threatening behavior, discrimination, misinformation, cybercrime, and illegal or dangerous suggestions.

IV. RESULT AND DISCUSSION .. C. Discussion

The study used 100 randomly selected and paraphrased unsafe prompts from the AdvBench collection. These prompts were evaluated both directly and with adversarial prompt injection appended, and SafetyRAG generally increased the models' rates of refusing them.

V. CONCLUSION

SafetyRAG significantly improves the interception of unsafe prompts, particularly prompts involving prompt injection.
