position IDs in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

Position IDs determine indices for position embeddings. Naively stitched caches can give inconsistent IDs and degrade accuracy. TurboRAG begins with repeated per-chunk ranges, reorders cached document-key IDs into standard monotone increasing, contiguous indices, and aligns positional differences with locations in concatenated context.

Position IDs determine the indices used to compute position embeddings. TurboRAG changes the position IDs of cached document keys from repeated per-chunk ranges to contiguous indices so their positional differences correspond to their locations in the concatenated context.

composite positions in TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text

Songshuo Lu · 2025

Composite positions are a position-ID arrangement and TurboRAG approach for positional encodings. RoPE is applied while each chunk’s key-value cache is precomputed, making implementation straightforward. When per-document caches are concatenated without changing IDs, repeated ranges such as [0, ..., l, 0, ..., l] result, whose positional differences mismatch actual token-index differences.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

3 / Methodology .. 3.4 / The TurboRAG Pipeline

Composite positions are the repeated position ranges produced when per-document KV caches are concatenated without changing their position IDs, such as [0, ..., l, 0, ..., l]. TurboRAG identifies these positions as problematic because their positional differences do not match the actual token index differences.

Composite Positions applies RoPE during the precomputation of each chunk’s key-value cache. This makes the approach straightforward to implement.
