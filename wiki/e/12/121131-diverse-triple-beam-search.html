Diverse Triple Beam Search

algorithm · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: diverse triple beam search

Abstract written by a model

Diverse Triple Beam Search is a method for expanding initial triples into reasoning chains while retaining the top beam-size sequences. It scores triples with a dense embedding model, considers only neighbouring triples, avoids previously visited triples, and maps triples to source passages. Position-based diversity weighting promotes diversity across beams and aligns language modelling with information-retrieval objectives for multiple information needs in multi-hop queries. It consistently outperforms standard beam search across reported datasets and recall ranks; natural language inference is a possible alternative scoring function.

Diverse Triple Beam Search expands initial triples into reasoning chains while retaining the top beam-size sequences. It limits expansion to neighboring triples, avoids previously visited triples, and applies position-based weighting to promote diversity across beams.

Discussion / What makes GEAR work? .. Is GEAR efficient?

Diverse Triple Beam Search consistently outperforms standard beam search across all reported datasets and recall ranks. Its diversity weights align beam search with information-retrieval objectives involving multiple information needs in multi-hop queries.

Limitations

The diverse triple beam search uses a scoring function based on a dense embedding model, with natural language inference identified as a possible alternative.
