Acamas in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Acamas, Theseus’ warrior son and Aethra’s grandson, boxes Epeius, endures a knockdown, and wins a silver bowl. Later he helps Demophoon find Aethra, recognizes her as Theseus’ mother, and joyfully embraces her.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

Acamas challenges Epeius in boxing and uses fighting craft to evade and counter his blows. He is knocked down but rises and continues until the Argives halt the contest; he receives the other silver bowl.

Then also Menelaus in Helen's bower

Acamas helps Demophoon find Aethra and recognizes her as the mother of Theseus. He embraces and kisses her as she weeps with joy.
