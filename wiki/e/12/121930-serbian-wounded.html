Serbian wounded in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Serbian wounded, housed in great numbers at the Serbian military hospital at Krushieevatz, fill overcrowded hospitals during retreat and occupation; Scottish women tend them despite shortages.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ As the fever died out, a worse enemy came in.

Serbian wounded filled the hospitals during the retreat and occupation. The Scottish women refused to abandon them, caring for them in overcrowded buildings, corridors, outhouses, and makeshift wards despite shortages of beds, food, warmth, and medical resources.
