Corinthian Messenger in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

A messenger from Corinth who tends mountain flocks, finds the abandoned infant Oedipus in Cithaeron, and gives him to Polybus. Later he reports Polybus’s death and Corinth’s choice of Oedipus as king, revealing Oedipus’s adoption.

The Corinthian Messenger reports that Polybus is dead and that the people of Corinth have decided to make Oedipus king. He tells Oedipus that Polybus was not his biological father and that he received Oedipus as a child from Laius’s Herdsman after finding him on Cithaeron.
