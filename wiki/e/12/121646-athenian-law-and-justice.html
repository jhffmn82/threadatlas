Athenian law and justice

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Justice, right, sanction of your State or King, the law, the laws, the law’s eye, warranty of law

Abstract written by a model

Athenian law and justice deem Oedipus innocent, forbid unlawful abduction, and are invoked by Theseus to adjudicate and condemn Creon despite his exclusion claim.
