Athenian law and justice in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

Athenian law and justice deem Oedipus innocent, forbid unlawful abduction, and are invoked by Theseus to adjudicate and condemn Creon despite his exclusion claim.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Enter CREON with attendants]

Theseus declares that Athenian laws will adjudicate Creon's conduct and condemns him for acting like a freebooter in a State governed by law. Creon claims that he believed Justice in Athens would not permit a parricide and incestuous outcast to remain within its borders.
