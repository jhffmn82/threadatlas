Brahma in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Brahma is the supreme God and creator, personifying creation as one power of the supreme deity. As creator in the Trimurti, he is associated with the priestly class; after his creative work was regarded as finished, his worship disappeared, leaving one temple in India.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXX

Brahma is presented as the supreme God, creator of the universe, and source into which all individual deities and human souls will ultimately be absorbed. As the creator in the Trimurti, Brahma later became associated with the priestly class, but his worship eventually disappeared and he was left with only one temple in India.
