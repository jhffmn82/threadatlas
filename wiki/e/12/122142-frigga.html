Frigga in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Frigga, a goddess and Odin's wife living in Fensalir, protects Baldur through oaths but overlooks mistletoe; after his death, she seeks his return from Hel through ransom, love, and goodwill.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXIII

Frigga obtains oaths from fire, water, metals, stones, trees, diseases, animals, poisons, and creeping things that they will not harm Baldur, but overlooks mistletoe. After Baldur's death, she asks for someone to travel to Hel and offer a ransom for his return.
