Abbe Paquin in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Abbe Paquin, parish priest of St Eustache, repeatedly urged rebels to return home and held Chenier responsible for their misfortunes; when he tried to leave, Girod and Chenier effectively placed him under arrest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{89} But the end of rebellion was not yet in sight.

Abbe Paquin, the parish priest of St Eustache, repeatedly urged the insurgents to return home and told Chenier that he was responsible before God and man for their misfortunes. He sought to leave the parish but was effectively placed under arrest by Girod and Chenier.
