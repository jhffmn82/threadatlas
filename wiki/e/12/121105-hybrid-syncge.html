Hybrid + SyncGE in GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation

Zhili Shen · 2025

Hybrid + SyncGE is a single-step retrieval method whose Diverse Triple Beam Search improves recall across MuSiQue, 2Wiki, and HotpotQA. It achieves state-of-the-art retrieval on MuSiQue and HotpotQA, surpassing HippoRAG by up to 9.8% R@15 on MuSiQue, while using fewer tokens than HippoRAG w/ IRCoT; however, its cited single-iteration R@15 is substantially lower.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Results

Hybrid + SyncGE achieves state-of-the-art single-step retrieval performance on MuSiQue and HotpotQA. It surpasses HippoRAG by up to 9.8% at R@15 on MuSiQue and records end-to-end QA scores of 14.0 EM and 27.1 F1 on MuSiQue, 38.0 EM and 50.2 F1 on 2Wiki, and 45.0 EM and 63.4 F1 on HotpotQA.

Discussion / What makes GEAR work? .. Is GEAR efficient?

Hybrid + SyncGE benefits from Diverse Triple Beam Search, which consistently improves recall across MuSiQue, 2Wiki, and HotpotQA. As a single-step method, it uses fewer tokens than HippoRAG w/ IRCoT, although its R@15 is substantially lower in the cited single-iteration comparison.
