the flying field in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

A magically airborne field lifts Sir Hokus, Camy, their tent, tree, and rock at night, flies over Oz’s hills and forests, then descends at dawn, throws them off, straightens, and flies away.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER NINE

The field where Sir Hokus and Camy camp rises into the air during the night and carries the tent, tree, and rock through the sky. At dawn it descends, tilts sideways, and throws Sir Hokus and Camy off before straightening and flying away.
