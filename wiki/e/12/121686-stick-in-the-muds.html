Abstract written by a model

The Stick-in-the-Muds are Marshlanders ruled by Queen Marcia, living in tiny mud-thatched tree-houses and using long stilts to move through the Marshes. They attend Marcia and shout at Sir Hokus as he escapes, but cannot pursue him after their stilts are knocked into the mire.

CHAPTER THREE

The Marshlanders have bodies no larger than Sir Hokus's but use long, stick-like stilts to cross the marsh. They live in tiny mud-thatched houses built high in the trees, with each dwelling level with the occupants' feet.

CHAPTER FOUR

The Stick-in-the-Muds attend Marcia and shout and scold as Sir Hokus escapes. With their stilts knocked into the mire, they are unable to pursue or stop him.
