Dr Cyrile Cote in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Dr Cyrile Cote served in the Lower Canada Assembly, joined the provisional government during the second rebellion, commanded several hundred rebels toward Rouse's Point, fought at Lacolle, and fled across the border after defeat.

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

He was named as a member of the provisional government and later commanded several hundred rebels. He led a force toward Rouse's Point, fought at Lacolle, and fled across the border after being defeated.
