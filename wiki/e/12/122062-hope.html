hope in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Hope remains alone in Pandora's jar after evils escape, enduring as consolation that never entirely leaves mankind and prevents complete wretchedness.

CHAPTER II

Hope was the one thing left in Pandora's jar after the evils escaped. The narrative says that hope never entirely leaves mankind and can prevent people from becoming completely wretched.
