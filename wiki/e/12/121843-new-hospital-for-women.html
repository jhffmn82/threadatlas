New Hospital for Women

Abstract written by a model

The New Hospital for Women was a pioneering women’s hospital movement in Euston Road, London, founded for women patients and staffed by women doctors. By the time Elsie Inglis began working there, it had operated for nearly thirty years. It had four main wards, two small wards for serious cases, an extensive paying out-patient department with attractive waiting rooms, and women doctors including Garrett Anderson and Mrs. Scharlieb.

The New Hospital for Women in Euston Road had been established for nearly thirty years when Elsie joined it. It had four main wards, two smaller wards for serious cases, an extensive outpatient department, and a staff and student body largely organized around women doctors.
