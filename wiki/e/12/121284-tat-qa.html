TAT-QA

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

TAT-QA is a financial QA dataset built from 500 annual financial reports sourced from annualreports.com and expert-composed questions. It requires numerical reasoning across tables and text, contributes finance data to RAGBench, and has about a 1% hallucination rate there; RAGBench uses its full dataset to generate new LLM responses.

TAT-QA is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
