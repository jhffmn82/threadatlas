CHAPTER II

Epimetheus was charged with making animals and giving them faculties such as courage, strength, swiftness, sagacity, wings, claws, and shells. He used all his gifts before reaching man, accepted Pandora despite Prometheus's warning, and was connected with the jar kept in his house.
