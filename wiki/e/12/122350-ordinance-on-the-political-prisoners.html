{104} CHAPTER X THE LORD HIGH COMMISSIONER The rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada profoundly affected public opinion in the mother country.

Durham's ordinance granted a general amnesty while excepting twenty-four rebel leaders. It ordered eight prisoners transported to Bermuda and prohibited sixteen refugees from returning to Canada under penalty of death, but the British government later disallowed it.
