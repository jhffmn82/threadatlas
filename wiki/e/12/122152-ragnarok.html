Ragnarok in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Ragnarok is the foretold destruction of the visible creation, preceded by three winters without summer, war, earthquakes, and released enemies. Gods and enemies die in battle; the world burns and sinks, ending with a new heaven and earth created by Alfadur.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXIII

Ragnarok is the foretold destruction of the visible creation, preceded by severe winters, war, earthquakes, and the release of the gods' enemies. The gods and their enemies die in battle, the world burns and sinks, and Alfadur later brings forth a new heaven and earth.
