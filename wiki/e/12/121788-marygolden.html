Abstract written by a model

Marygolden is a little golden princess of royal blood, the only daughter of the King of Corabia, who has been enchanted by the Sultan and given to the Shah of Subterranea as a solid-gold statue. Speedy finds her in a cave and brings her to life when he takes her hands. She becomes a living princess, escapes a groper with him in the Skyrocket, and travels with him through many dangers. Old-fashioned but adaptable, she wears a crown until throwing it away because it hurts her head, learns to eat by copying Speedy, rides behind him on Stampedro, follows Speedy in everything, and plans to return to America with him as Mary. Marygolden helps Speedy escape Quick City by watching his changing age and removing the Lively Hood at the right moment. With Peter Pun, Sir Hokus, Confido, and the Comfortable Camel, she flees Samandra, comforts and persuades Confido to reveal the secret of the magic dates, and helps restore Corumbia. She owns Confido after rescuing him from the Sultan and stays near the Queen's chamber in the Golden Castle. She longs for the Yellow Knight's restoration, but is heartbroken when he does not remember her. The remaining spells are broken, and she is revealed as the enchanted Princess of Corabia. Restored fully, she remembers Speedy, recognizes the Comfortable Camel of Oz, returns to her real home in Corabia, and marries Sir Hokus of Pokes in a magnificent celebration.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

9 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER EIGHT

Princess Marygolden is found as a solid gold statue lying in a cave. When Speedy takes her hands, she comes to life, changing from a gold statue into a living princess with pink skin, silken clothing, and moving hair. She runs from a groper with Speedy and escapes with him in the Skyrocket.

CHAPTER THIRTEEN

Marygolden escapes Subterranea with Speedy and is taken to Quick City after the Quix recognize him as their king. She carefully watches Speedy's changing age, removes the Lively Hood at the right moment, and helps him escape. She tells Speedy that she intends to do everything he does.

CHAPTER FOURTEEN

Marygolden agrees to have her old-fashioned dress shortened and throws away her crown because it gives her a headache. She learns to eat by imitating Speedy, rides behind him on Stampedro, and helps identify their surroundings and discuss finding the Yellow Knight.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Marygolden travels with Speedy and meets Sir Hokus and Peter Pun. Sir Hokus offers to serve her, but Speedy says she will become plain Mary when they return to the United States. She joins the others in riding Stampedro toward the Comfortable Camel.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Marygolden escapes Samandra with Peter Pun on Stampedro and later helps Speedy by taking Confido in her arms. Her promise of cream and chicken hearts persuades Confido to tell her the secret of the magic dates.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Marygolden repeats Confido's instructions, helps protect and encourage Speedy, and promises Confido rewards for his assistance. She accompanies the restored Corumbians to the Golden Castle and is given a room beside the Queen's chamber.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Marygolden eagerly awaits the restoration of the Yellow Knight and is disappointed and heartbroken when the restored Knight does not remember her or agree to serve her. She follows him with Speedy and the Comfortable Camel, helps decide to break the Sultan's remaining spells, and watches the Knights prepare for the grand test.

CHAPTER NINETEEN

Princess Marygolden is revealed to be the enchanted Princess of Corabia. After Sir Hokus eats the magic date and places its seed on the tower balcony, she is restored, remembers everything, recognizes Speedy and the Comfortable Camel of Oz, and prepares to marry Sir Hokus.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Princess Marygolden is restored from her transformation into a statue and is revealed as the Princess of Corabia. She marries Sir Hokus at a magnificent celebration.
