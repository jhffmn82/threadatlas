Capitulation of Montreal

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

The Capitulation of Montreal in 1760 set terms for defeated French inhabitants of Canada, assuring property and civil rights and guaranteeing free exercise of religion; the British did not treat them as a conquered people.
