PubLayNet

dataset · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

PubLayNet is a dataset originally designed for document layout analysis of single-page PubMed PDF documents with a 3:2 aspect ratio. It supplies bounding box coordinates and class labels for paper screenshot elements, lacks queries and answers, and serves as Paper-VISA’s source dataset after training samples gain highlighted boxes and VLM-generated questions and short answers.

PubLayNet is in one work on this site, VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PubLayNet provides bounding box coordinates and class labels for elements in paper PDF screenshots, but it does not include associated queries or answers. Its training samples are augmented with highlighted bounding boxes and VLM-generated questions and short answers.
