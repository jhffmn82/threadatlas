Abstract written by a model

The lost labyrinth is a difficult network of branching tunnels below the castle, at the foot of the ladder, leading to the enchanted cavern. Jack gives the left-turn rule; Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor enter seeking the forbidden flagon. Belfaygor leads them through, then they return by turning right to the ladder and cellarway.

CHAPTER 12

The lost labyrinth lies below the castle and leads toward the enchanted cavern. Jack says that travelers must turn left at every opportunity, and Peter, Snif, and Belfaygor enter it to search for the forbidden flagon.

CHAPTER 13

The labyrinth lies at the foot of the ladder and branches repeatedly into multiple tunnels. Belfaygor leads the group into the enchanted cavern by always turning left, and they return by turning right until reaching the ladder and cellarway.
