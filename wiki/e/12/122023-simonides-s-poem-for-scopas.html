Simonides's poem for Scopas in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

A banquet poem commissioned to celebrate Scopas's exploits, it digresses to praise Castor and Pollux, prompting Scopas to pay only half the expected reward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVIII

Simonides's poem was commissioned to celebrate Scopas's exploits and was intended for recitation at a banquet. Simonides diversified it by praising Castor and Pollux, which led Scopas to pay only half the expected reward.
