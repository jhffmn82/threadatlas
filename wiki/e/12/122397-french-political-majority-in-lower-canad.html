French political majority in Lower Canada in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

French political majority in Lower Canada held thirty-four of the first Assembly’s members against sixteen English members. It elected Jean Antoine Panet speaker, secured French in Assembly proceedings, and proposed extra customs duties to raise money for prisons.

{7} CHAPTER II THE RIGHTS OF THE DEFEATED The British did not treat the French inhabitants of Canada as a conquered people; not as other countries won by conquest have been treated by their victorious invaders.

The French political majority in Lower Canada consisted of thirty-four members compared with sixteen English members in the first Assembly. It elected Jean Antoine Panet as speaker and secured the use of French in Assembly proceedings.
