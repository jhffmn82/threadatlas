Achilles' deification

Abstract written by a model

is_promised_by Poseidon [as consolation to Thetis] Achilles' deification is promised by Poseidon, who says that Achilles will not remain among the dead but will rise to the light of Zeus. He will dwell as a god on a holy island in the Euxine Sea and receive incense and sacrificial honors from the surrounding tribes.
