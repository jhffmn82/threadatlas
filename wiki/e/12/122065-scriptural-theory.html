Scriptural theory

Also called: The Scriptural theory

Abstract written by a model

Scriptural theory is a theory of mythology deriving legends from disguised, altered Scriptural narratives, identifying Deucalion, Hercules, and Arion with Noah, Samson, and Jonah, yet failing extravagantly to explain many myths.

Scriptural theory is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XXVIII

The Scriptural theory holds that mythological legends derive from Scriptural narratives whose facts were disguised and altered. It identifies Deucalion with Noah, Hercules with Samson, and Arion with Jonah, but the unit says the theory cannot account for a large proportion of the myths without extravagance.
