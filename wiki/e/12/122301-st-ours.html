St Ours in The 'Patriotes' of '37

St Ours hosted a large Patriote meeting on May 7, 1837, organized chiefly by Dr Wolfred Nelson and attended by Papineau. The meeting condemned the government and adopted a resolution to avoid imported English goods in favor of Canadian products.
