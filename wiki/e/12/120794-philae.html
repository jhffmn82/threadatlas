Philae in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Philae is a burying place for the nation, where Isis buried Osiris; it became a major pilgrimage site and gained a magnificent temple honoring him.

1 section; each heading opens the text

MYTH OF OSIRIS AND ISIS

Philae became the burial place of Osiris after Isis buried his body there. It was thereafter a major pilgrimage site, and a magnificent temple was erected there in his honor.
