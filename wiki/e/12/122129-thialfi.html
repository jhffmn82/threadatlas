Thialfi in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Thialfi, Thor’s swift servant, carries provisions, loses three races to Hugi at Utgard, then kills Hrungnir’s clay giant with his spade.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Thialfi accompanies Thor and Loki and carries Thor's provisions. He races Hugi three times at Utgard but is defeated because Hugi is really Thought. Later, Thialfi kills the clay giant accompanying Hrungnir with a spade.
