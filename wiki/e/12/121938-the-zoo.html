the Zoo in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Zoo was an overcrowded hospital magazine where wounded patients occupied three tiers of shelving; as fever died out, a worse enemy arrived, and patients later filled makeshift spaces amid dim light and beans.

’ As the fever died out, a worse enemy came in.

The Zoo was the Scottish Sisters' name for the hospital magazine, where wounded patients occupied three tiers of shelving and later overcrowded makeshift spaces. In dim light, patients received beans and moved among the shelves, creating a scene Dr. Inglis remembered as unforgettable.
