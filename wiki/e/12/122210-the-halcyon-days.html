1 section; each heading opens the text

CEYX AND HALCYONE: OR, THE HALCYON BIRDS

For seven placid days in winter, the sea remains safe while Halcyone broods over her floating nest. During this period, Aeolus restrains the winds and the sea is given to his grandchildren without disturbance.
