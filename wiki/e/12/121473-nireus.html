Nireus

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Nireus godlike-fair

Abstract written by a model

Nireus is a warrior renowned for great beauty but weak prowess; in the war, Eurypylus kills him with a spear, taunts him, and Argives fight to recover his body.

Nireus is in one work on this site, The Fall of Troy. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK VI

Nireus, famed for beauty, is killed in battle when Eurypylus's spear pierces him beneath the ribs. Eurypylus taunts him and tries to strip his arms, but the Argives later fight to recover his body.
