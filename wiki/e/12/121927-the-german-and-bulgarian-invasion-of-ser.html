Abstract written by a model

The German and Bulgarian invasion of Serbia was part of a wider autumn 1915 attack. Germans massed in the north, Bulgarians invaded from the east, and Austrian forces joined from the west, overwhelming Serbian defenses. The Austro-German invasion caused military collapse, refugees’ flight, soldiers’ capture, hospital abandonment or occupation, and evacuation or retreat decisions for Scottish units.
