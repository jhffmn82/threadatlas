Jugoslav Division

Abstract written by a model

The Jugoslav Division was a military division of volunteers, Serbs, Croats, and Slovenes from Austria-Hungary, who had been taken prisoner by Russia and volunteered to fight for the Entente and Serbia, seeking Southern Slav unity under the national dynasty of Kara George. In the 1916 Dobrudja campaign, its main body fell from 15,000 to 4,000 and needed medical and equipment support. Commanded by General Haditch, its Serbian troops fought General Mackensen at a front called a second Verdun and were praised as heroes. After the Reni hospital closed, it received Scottish Women's Hospitals support and later prepared another move at Hadji Abdul.

’ Space utterly forbids our following the fortunes of the Scottish Women’s Hospi

The Jugoslav Division, properly called the Jugoslav Division, consisted of Serbs, Croats, and Slovenes from Austria-Hungary who had been taken prisoner by Russia and volunteered to fight for the Entente and Serbia. Its main body was reduced from 15,000 to 4,000 during the Dobrudja campaign and later needed medical and equipment support.

‘NEARING ODESSA, ‘_Sep.

The 1st Division was commanded by General Haditch and was described as being at an important point in the war, called a second Verdun. Its Serbian soldiers fought against General Mackensen and were praised by Russian officers as heroes for their actions in the fighting in the middle of September.

‘_On Active Service.

The Serbian Division is expected to take the hospital unit back into its service after the Reni work ends. Inglis rejoins the Division in August and is later with it at Hadji Abdul while preparations are made for another move.
