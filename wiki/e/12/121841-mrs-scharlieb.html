Mrs. Scharlieb was a visiting doctor at the New Hospital for Women and worked with Elsie on ward care and operations. She was particular but kind, discussed the hospital’s future as a qualifying institution, advised Elsie about her work, and expected eventually to be consulted when Elsie became a surgeon.
