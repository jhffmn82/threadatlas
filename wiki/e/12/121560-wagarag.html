Wagarag in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Wagarag is Mogodore the Mighty’s thin, anxious, restless Baffleburgher chief steward and Major Domo, also serving as Captain of the Guard in improvised armor. He repeatedly warns Mogodore against the forbidden flagon and invading Oz. Mogodore instead makes him Royal Chancellor of Oz and takes him on the expedition.

Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER 9

Wagarag, Mogodore's chief steward and Major Domo, repeatedly urges him to stop worrying about the forbidden flagon and abandon his plan to invade Oz. Mogodore appoints him Royal Chancellor of Oz and orders him to accompany the expedition, despite his gloomy warnings.
