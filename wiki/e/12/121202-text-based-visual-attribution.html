Abstract written by a model

Text-based Visual Attribution is a modularized text-based RAG pipeline combining layout prediction, OCR, and text-based LLMs; assuming perfect layout detection, it extracts box text with pytesseract, achieving higher zero-shot bounding-box accuracy but lower answer accuracy from OCR errors.
