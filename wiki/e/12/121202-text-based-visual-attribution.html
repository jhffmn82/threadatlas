The modularized text-based RAG pipeline combines a layout prediction model, OCR, and text-based LLMs. In the comparison, it assumes a perfect layout detector, extracts text from each bounding box with pytesseract, and has higher zero-shot bounding box accuracy but lower answer accuracy because of OCR errors.
