Abstract written by a model

is_a village in the county of Two Mountains [about eighteen miles north-west of Montreal] St Eustache was a stronghold of the extreme Patriotes and became the center of an organized rebel rising under Amury Girod and Dr J. O. Chenier. The village was attacked and largely devastated by Sir John Colborne's forces.
