Ageladas

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Ageladas was an Argive sculptor and Argos school leader, distinguished for bronze Zeus and Heracles statues, whose greatest importance was teaching Myron, Polycleitos, and Pheidias.

Ageladas is in one work on this site, Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

SKETCH OF THE HISTORY OF GREEK SCULPTURE

Ageladas led a school of artists at Argos and was distinguished for bronze statues of Zeus and Heracles. His greater importance came from teaching Myron, Polycleitos, and Pheidias.
