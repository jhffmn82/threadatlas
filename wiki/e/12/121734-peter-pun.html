Peter Pun in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

When the vine unwinds, he becomes a person again, remembers the enchantment but not the golden castle, the King, or his former life, and joins Sir Hokus and the Comfortable Camel to make their journey merry with jokes, songs, riddles, and acrobatics. After the restoration, Peter embraces the King, names the rescuers as Corumbia’s liberators, and celebrates with them.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER TEN

The jester appears when the funnysuckle vine unwinds and changes into a person. He remembers being enchanted but cannot recall the golden castle, its King, or his former life. He joins Sir Hokus and the Comfortable Camel, using jokes, songs, and acrobatics to keep their journey merry.

CHAPTER ELEVEN

The jester reveals that his name is Peter Pun and celebrates it with a rhyme. He continues climbing, joking, riddling, and behaving playfully, while also helping Sir Hokus after the Camel is dragged into the river. Peter helps save Sir Hokus from sinking, joins the pursuit of the royal barge, and helps choose the river's right branch.

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Peter Pun recognizes Stampedro and recalls that the horse belonged to the King's son at whose court he was jester. He listens to Speedy's story, helps connect the horse with the enchanted kingdom, and joins the effort to find the Comfortable Camel. He rides behind Marygolden on Stampedro as the group departs.

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Peter Pun rides away from the palace with Marygolden and later waits with her for Speedy. He suggests that the magic seed might release enchanted people by touching the transformed trees, and he admires Speedy's plan to question Confido.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Peter Pun warns the others that they are being pursued and helps explain the events to Sir Hokus. After the restoration, he embraces the King, publicly identifies the liberators, and joins the celebration.
