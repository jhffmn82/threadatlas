Act of 1791

Also called: the Act of 1791

Abstract written by a model

The Act of 1791 divided Canada into Upper Canada and Lower Canada, granting both equal political rights. It treated French inhabitants not as a conquered people and created representative Assemblies in which Canadian Catholics could sit despite exclusion from the House of Commons at Westminster.
