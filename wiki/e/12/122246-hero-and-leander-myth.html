Hero and Leander concerns lovers who cross the Hellespont nightly by Hero’s torch until a storm extinguishes it, Leander drowns, and Hero throws herself into the sea.

Chapter VIII

The myth concerns Hero of Sestos and Leander of Abydos, who maintained a nightly love journey across the strait with the aid of Hero's torch. A storm extinguished the torch and caused Leander's drowning, after which Hero died by throwing herself into the sea.
