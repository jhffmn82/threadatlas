FinQA

Abstract written by a model

FinQA is a finance QA dataset built from financial report passages, earnings reports, and expert-composed questions. It contains 8,281 question-answer pairs requiring numerical reasoning across unstructured text and tables. RAGBench retains its train, validation, and test splits, generates two LLM responses per example, and reports about a 1% hallucination rate.

FinQA is in one work on this site, RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
