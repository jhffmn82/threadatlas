Abstract written by a model

Amury Girod arrived at St Eustache with apparent credentials from Papineau, organized and superintended the Patriote cause in the north, and helped command revolt. After posting rebels and fleeing the engagement, he falsely claimed victory, evaded capture for four days, then died by suicide.

{89} But the end of rebellion was not yet in sight.

Amury Girod arrived at St Eustache with apparent credentials from Papineau and organized the Patriote cause in the north. He helped command the rebels but fled during the engagement, falsely claimed victory while seeking reinforcements, and later killed himself when capture appeared imminent.
