Serbian nation in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Serbian nation suffered epidemic, famine, crowded hospitals, and devastation by typhus after the Austrians invaded. Scottish women, including Elsie Maud Inglis, went directly to aid it. In gratitude, Serbians called Inglis “the angel of their people,” dedicated a simple fountain in Mladenovatz to her memory, and sent the Serbian Legation to her funeral, where representatives carried her coffin and lowered it into the grave.
