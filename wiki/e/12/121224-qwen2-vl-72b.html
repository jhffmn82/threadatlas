Qwen2-VL-72B in VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution

Xueguang Ma, Shengyao Zhuang, Bevan Koopman, Guido Zuccon, Wenhu Chen, Jimmy Lin · 2025

Qwen2-VL-72B is a state-of-the-art instruction-tuned vision-language model evaluated with zero-shot prompting. It struggles with visual source attribution: on Wiki-VISA it reaches 60.4% answer accuracy but 1.5% bounding-box accuracy; on Paper-VISA, textual prompting yields 43.5% bounding-box and 16.7% answer accuracy, while visual prompting yields 1.5% and 43.1%.

Qwen2-VL-72B is evaluated with zero-shot prompting. It achieves 60.4% average answer accuracy on Wiki-VISA but only 1.5% bounding-box accuracy, and on Paper-VISA its visual prompting results are 1.5% for bounding-box accuracy and 43.1% for answer accuracy.
