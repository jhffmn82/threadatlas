Funeral games of Achilles in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Funeral games are athletic contests at Achilles' funeral-feast, held after Thetis arrives with prizes for Argive champions. Competitors race, wrestle, box, and contest archery for cattle, horses, handmaids, chariots, or silver bowls; Laertes cannot compete because of a wound.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

The funeral games are held after Thetis arrives with prizes for the Argive champions. They include a footrace, wrestling, boxing, and other athletic contests, with competitors receiving cattle, horses, handmaids, chariots, or silver bowls.

Then for the archery-test Oileus' son

The athletic contests take place at Achilles' funeral-feast. Competitors strive for the prizes that Thetis distributes, while Laertes is prevented from competing by a wound received in the fight around Achilles' dead body.
