model alignment

Abstract written by a model

Model alignment is the safety-oriented training and behavioral guidance of baseline LLMs, including moderation, fine-tuning, human rules, responsible behavior, and distinguishing acceptable from toxic content. It aims to limit harmful behavior but does not prevent all offensive outputs. Alignment supplies implicit knowledge about direct unsafe prompts and is crucial to SafetyRAG’s efficacy: combined with SafetyRAG’s explicit knowledge, it improves robustness. Its importance unfolds from incomplete protection against unsafe prompts, through its characterization across models, to evidence that weaker alignment in Mistral-7B reduced effectiveness and that future work should optimize SafetyRAG for less aligned models.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW / A. Threats of Unsafe Prompts for LLMs .. B. Unsafe Prompt Mitigation

Safety alignment is used to limit harmful LLM behavior, but it does not prevent all unsafe outputs. Even aligned LLMs can be induced to produce offensive content.

III. MATERIALS AND METHODS / A. Overall Framework .. 2) Baseline LLM models

Model alignment is described for all four baseline models through moderation-based training, fine-tuning, alignment with human rules, responsible-behavior training, or the ability to differentiate acceptable and toxic content.

IV. RESULT AND DISCUSSION .. C. Discussion

Model alignment provides implicit knowledge about direct unsafe prompts and plays a critical role in the overall efficacy of SafetyRAG. The discussion states that combining this alignment with SafetyRAG's explicit knowledge improves robustness, while weaker internal alignment in Mistral-7B reduced the method's effectiveness.

V. CONCLUSION

The underlying alignment of the base LLM plays a crucial role in determining SafetyRAG's efficacy. Future research should optimize SafetyRAG for less aligned models and investigate its integration with advanced alignment methods.
