rescue of Shirley Sunshine in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The rescue of Shirley Sunshine is a mission to save her from Mogodore. The group crosses a chasm, reaches Mogodore's city, and enters the tower where she is imprisoned; Belfaygor sacrifices his beard, while Peter, Snif, Belfaygor, and Jack's Pumpkinhead retrieve the Forbidden Flagon and reunite Jack's head and body before departing.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 8

The rescue of Shirley Sunshine requires the group to reach Mogodore's city and enter the tower where she is imprisoned. Belfaygor agrees to endure the loss of his beard to help accomplish the rescue.

CHAPTER 13

The rescue mission involves Peter, Snif, Belfaygor, and Jack's Pumpkinhead retrieving the Forbidden Flagon and reuniting Jack's head with his body. After completing these tasks, they prepare to leave Mogodore's palace and continue toward the Emerald City.
