opening

A bounding box identifies the relevant region within a retrieved evidence document. In VISA, the vision-language model returns the bounding box so users can visually locate and check the supporting evidence in its original context.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

They are used by VISA to locate supporting passages, tables, or images within document screenshots. VLMs can generate their top-left and bottom-right coordinates, and GUI agent systems use them to localize interface elements such as buttons.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

The bounding box is specified by the top-left and bottom-right xy-coordinates of supporting content within the relevant document screenshot. The generated coordinates are used to draw the box on the screenshot shown to the user.

6 / 6.1 .. 6.4

A bounding box identifies the document region used for visual attribution. The text-based comparison uses PubLayNet bounding boxes and OCR within each box, while VISA is evaluated on how accurately it predicts the supporting boxes.
