the Scottish Women’s Hospitals units in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The hospital units are intended as carefully selected, well-equipped teams staffed by women doctors, nurses, and ambulance workers. They are sent to Allied nations wherever wanted, and, as work expands, add motor ambulances and rest stations.
