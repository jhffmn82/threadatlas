Top-k Retrieval Algorithm

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: optimized algorithm, retrieval algorithm

Abstract written by a model

The Top-k Retrieval Algorithm is a practical subgraph isomorphism retrieval method optimized for efficient, scalable retrieval of similar subgraphs. It filters candidate nodes and relations, orders pattern triples or edges through DFS traversal, expands partial isomorphic mappings, and maintains the top-k completed subgraphs with the smallest Graph Semantic Distance in a priority queue.
