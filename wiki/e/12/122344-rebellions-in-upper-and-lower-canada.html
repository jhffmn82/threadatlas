rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada were armed revolts in an important British colony that profoundly shifted opinion in Britain, exposed the seriousness of Canadian Reformers’ grievances, and showed that alternating concession and repression had failed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{104} CHAPTER X THE LORD HIGH COMMISSIONER The rebellions in Upper and Lower Canada profoundly affected public opinion in the mother country.

The rebellions profoundly affected public opinion in Britain and forced the British government to recognize that Canadian Reformers' grievances were more serious than previously imagined. They demonstrated that the old system of alternating concession and repression had broken down.
