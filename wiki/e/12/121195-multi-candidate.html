Multi-candidate

setting · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Multi Candidates, multi-candidate, multi-candidate set, multi-candidate setting, multi-candidate settings, multi-candidate setup

Abstract written by a model

Multi-candidate is an evaluation setting for retrieval-augmented generation that supplies multiple retrieved document candidates, including relevant, irrelevant, and sometimes no relevant documents. It tests whether a model can identify relevant documents among hard negatives while generating an answer and bounding box with fine-grained attribution. Using three candidates limits training costs and OOM issues, and the setting is harder and more realistic than the single-candidate setting.

Multi-candidate is in one work on this site, VISA: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Visual Source Attribution. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Multi-candidate setting supplies multiple retrieved documents and tests whether the model can distinguish relevant documents from irrelevant ones while generating an answer and bounding box. The training setup uses three document candidates, including samples where no relevant document is present.

5

The multi-candidate setting uses multiple documents and more closely reflects real-world retrieval-augmented generation systems. It requires the model to identify the relevant document among hard negatives in addition to producing fine-grained attribution, making it more difficult than the single-candidate setting.
