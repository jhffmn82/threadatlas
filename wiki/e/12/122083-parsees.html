Abstract written by a model

Parsees are Persians who, after the Arab conquest, refused to abandon their ancestral religion, fled to Kerman and Hindustan, and became especially numerous in Bombay, practicing fire worship.

Chapter XXX

The Parsees are Persians who refused to abandon their ancestral religion and fled to Kerman and Hindustan after the Arab conquest. They remained especially numerous at Bombay, where the text describes them as active, intelligent, wealthy, honest, pure in life, and conciliatory in manner.
