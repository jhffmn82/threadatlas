Dawn in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Dawn is a golden-throned heavenly goddess, Dawn-queen and giver of light, whose daily course is appointed by Zeus and heralded by the Pleiads. As Memnon’s mother, she protects him from spears, then mourns him after Achilles kills him, covering herself in clouds and threatening darkness. Zeus and the Hours return her to heaven and her light-bearing course.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK II

Dawn is Memnon's mother and is described as the goddess who brings light to the world. She reluctantly climbs heaven's highway at morning, and as the Dawn-queen she turns aside the spears hurled at Memnon.

Then to the side of Aeacus' mighty son

Eos mourns Memnon after Achilles kills him and covers herself in clouds. She threatens to abandon the light of day and descend into darkness, but Zeus's thunder and the gentle pleadings of the Hours lead her back to her daily course.
