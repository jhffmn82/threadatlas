Abstract written by a model

Uncle Billy, Speedy’s uncle, is a great and famous scientist and inventor working in his workshop. He builds torpedo ships, invents the Skyrocket, misses its launch to Mars with Speedy, then hears Speedy’s adventures and proposes traveling to Oz.

Uncle Billy is in one work on this site, The Yellow Knight of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER SEVEN

Uncle Billy is a great scientist who invents the Skyrocket and intends to travel with Speedy to Mars. He fails to get into the craft before it launches and is left behind on Earth.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Uncle Billy finds Speedy back in his workshop and listens to the story of his adventures. He is building another torpedo ship and suggests that they may go to Oz rather than Mars.
