Experiments .. Overall Latency

FactKG contains colloquial and written claims that can be verified using DBpedia. SimGRAG achieves 86.8% accuracy, with reported accuracies of 89%, 88%, and 85% for two-hop conjunction, three-hop conjunction, and three-hop star queries, respectively.
