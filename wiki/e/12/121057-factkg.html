Abstract written by a model

FactKG is a fact-verification dataset of colloquial and written claims using DBpedia, requiring Supported or Refuted outputs; it evaluates multi-hop queries, where SimGRAG reports 86.8% accuracy.

FactKG is in one work on this site, SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

FactKG contains colloquial and written claims that can be verified using DBpedia. SimGRAG achieves 86.8% accuracy, with reported accuracies of 89%, 88%, and 85% for two-hop conjunction, three-hop conjunction, and three-hop star queries, respectively.
