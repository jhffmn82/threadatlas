Serbian military hospital at Krushieevatz in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Serbian military hospital at Krushieevatz, designed for 400 beds, held about 900 wounded on the Scottish unit’s arrival and later 1,200. As fever subsided, the unit took over sanitation and laundry and converted corridors, outhouses, a magazine, and other spaces for treatment.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

’ As the fever died out, a worse enemy came in.

The Serbian military hospital at Krushieevatz housed about 900 wounded when the Scottish unit arrived, with the number later rising to 1,200 despite accommodation designed for 400 beds. The unit also assumed responsibility for the hospital's sanitation and laundry and converted corridors, outhouses, a magazine, and other spaces into treatment areas.
