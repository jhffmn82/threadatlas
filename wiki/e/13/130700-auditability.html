auditability

Also called: Auditability, fully auditable

Abstract written by a model

Auditability requires logged decision steps from data input to final answer, recording inputs, sources, reasoning, and the entire chain for retrospective reconstruction. It lets clinicians examine recommendations and, through forensic audit logging of queries, patient-data access, sources, inference, outputs, and knowledge-base changes, reconstruct circumstances.
