Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 Introduction

Auditability requires logging the inputs, sources, and reasoning steps involved in producing an AI suggestion. It enables clinicians to examine how and why a recommendation was made.

3 Methods (framework design) .. 3.3.4 Step 4: Forensic audit logging

Auditability is supported by recording the query, patient-data access, retrieved sources, inference chain, generated output, and knowledge-base changes. These records are intended to allow auditors or institutions to reconstruct the circumstances surrounding a recommendation.
