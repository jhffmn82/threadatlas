Abstract written by a model

Domain ontologies are presented as an opportunity to ground and adapt the retrieval process without retraining. OG-RAG uses ontology-based structure for general-purpose domain-specific retrieval. Ontologies support domain adaptation and retrieval when they are well defined. Detailed ontologies are available in many legacy systems, but their availability can be limited in newer and fast-developing domains such as technology.
