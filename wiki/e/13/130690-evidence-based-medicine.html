Evidence-Based Medicine in An auditable and source-verified framework for clinical AI decision support: integrating retrieval-augmented generation with data provenance

Fidelis Fidelis Alu and Sunkanmi Oluwadare · 2026-02-04

Evidence-Based Medicine is a medical decision-making theory established in the 1990s. It requires therapeutic judgments to combine personal competence with the most reliable external evidence, making source verification foundational to clinical decision support and reflected in systems deriving recommendations from curated clinical guidelines.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 Introduction

Evidence-based medicine is described as requiring therapeutic judgments to combine personal competence with the most reliable external evidence. The proposed source-verification approach is aligned with this principle.

2 Background and related work

Evidence-Based Medicine established source verification as a foundational principle of clinical decision support in the 1990s. Its influence appears in systems that derive recommendations from curated clinical guidelines.
