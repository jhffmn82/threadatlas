Evaluation metrics and frameworks

Human evaluators assess the correctness of LLM outputs and characteristics such as grammar, understandability, meaning preservation, correctness of key information, and relevance of external information. Their evaluations may use Likert scales, but reliance on human evaluation limits scalability and comparability.
