MultiHop-RAG is an evaluation dataset and RAG-oriented multi-hop question answering benchmark requiring evidence aggregation from multiple sources. It provides question-answer pairs and gold evidence annotations for fine-grained retrieval and reasoning evaluation. Used to evaluate question decomposition, retrieval, latency, and answer accuracy, it reports gains over standard RAG baselines; QD+RR achieves the best reported retrieval scores, with 93.3% of generated subquery sets containing five subqueries.
