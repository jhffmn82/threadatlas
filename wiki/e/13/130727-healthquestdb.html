HealthQuestDB

Abstract written by a model

HealthQuestDB is a specialized public health benchmark dataset curated from PubMed and WHO IRIS. It comprises 890 independently sampled question-answer pairs spanning epidemiological research, prevention, treatment, and healthcare policy. Used exclusively to assess factual accuracy and reliability of AI-generated public health responses, it evaluates MEGA-RAG, which outperforms generic and domain-adapted baselines on the dataset.

HealthQuestDB is in one work on this site, MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
