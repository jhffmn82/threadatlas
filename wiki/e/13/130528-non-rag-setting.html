non-RAG setting in RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models

The non-RAG setting is the baseline framework in which LLMs answer from their own knowledge. It is the focus of most safety studies and is compared with RAG across eleven LLMs. Some models refuse nearly all harmful questions there, while unsafe behaviors can persist under RAG and models may become more vulnerable. Red-teaming methods, including GCG and AutoDAN, achieve high jailbreak success in non-RAG training, but prompts learned there mostly fail to transfer to RAG.
