1 section; each heading opens the text

3 Methods / 3.1 MEGA-RAG framework .. 3.6 Final answer fusion module

The Discrepancy-Identified Self-Clarification Module is activated when the relevant evaluation score exceeds the threshold of 0.5. It analyzes divergent answer content, generates targeted clarification questions, retrieves additional evidence, and refines answers using an accept-if-better rule and bounded iteration.
