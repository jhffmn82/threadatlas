The Discrepancy-Identified Self-Clarification Module activates when the evaluation score exceeds 0.5, detects divergent answers, asks targeted clarification questions, retrieves evidence, and iteratively refines answers using an accept-if-better rule.

The Discrepancy-Identified Self-Clarification Module is activated when the relevant evaluation score exceeds the threshold of 0.5.
