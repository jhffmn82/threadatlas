Abstract written by a model

Prompt-RAG is an embedding-free retrieval-augmented generation approach that uses an LLM and natural-language prompts to retrieve Korean medicine documents and generate QA chatbot responses without vector databases or indexing. It improves relevance and informativeness over ChatGPT and traditional models, but responds significantly more slowly.

Modular RAG

Prompt-RAG uses an LLM to perform document retrieval through natural-language prompts and generate responses without vector embeddings, a vector database, or indexing. In a QA chatbot using Korean medicine documents, it outperformed ChatGPT and traditional vector-embedding RAG models in relevance and informativeness, but had significantly slower response time.
