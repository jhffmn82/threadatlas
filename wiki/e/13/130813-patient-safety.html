Ethical considerations

Safety is an important concern for healthcare LLM applications because incorrect treatment or diagnostic suggestions could harm patients. Only three of the thirty-seven reviewed studies evaluated intentional or unintentional harms, using approaches including adversarial prompting, preoperative guidelines, and safety-flagging techniques.

Discussion

Patient safety is identified as essential because healthcare systems must avoid causing harm and comply with regulations. The unit reports that most reviewed studies did not address safety concerns and calls for safety to guide the development and adoption of RAG-based systems.
