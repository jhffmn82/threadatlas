The context window is the amount of information a model can process and generate in a session. Its token limit can omit needed information or, when filled with irrelevant material, impair recall. Longer context matters for longitudinal healthcare records, though its effect on hallucination remains unclear.

Discussion

The context window limits the amount of information a model can process and generate in a session. Insufficient context may omit necessary information, while excessive irrelevant context may impair recall of relevant information. Longer context is considered especially important for longitudinal clinical notes, laboratory values, imaging data, and multimodal healthcare information, although its effect on hallucination remains unclear.
