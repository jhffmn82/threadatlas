Advanced RAG

technology · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Advanced RAG systems, advanced RAG, advanced RAG methods

Abstract written by a model

Advanced RAG is a RAG technique used in healthcare that improves on Naive RAG through pre-retrieval, retrieval, and post-retrieval mechanisms. It optimizes indexing with chunking, metadata, mixed retrieval, knowledge graphs, thresholds, retrievers, ranking, context fusion, and reordering, then focuses crucial context to reduce noise and improve response generation and reasoning.

RAG overview .. Advanced RAG

Advanced RAG improves on Naive RAG by adding pre-retrieval, retrieval, and post-retrieval mechanisms. It uses strategies such as improved chunking, metadata, optimized indexing structures, mixed retrieval, similarity thresholds, multiple retrievers, ranking, context fusion, and reordering to improve relevance and reduce misleading or noisy answers.
