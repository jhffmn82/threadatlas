RAG overview .. Advanced RAG

Advanced RAG improves on Naive RAG by adding pre-retrieval, retrieval, and post-retrieval mechanisms. It uses strategies such as improved chunking, metadata, optimized indexing structures, mixed retrieval, similarity thresholds, multiple retrievers, ranking, context fusion, and reordering to improve relevance and reduce misleading or noisy answers.
