RQ2: What makes RAG-based LLMs .. Factor 3: An LLM’s Capability on RAG

Safety fine-tuning may discourage models from processing unsafe directions in non-RAG settings, but RAG instructions to synthesize relevant information can bypass or fail to activate that behavior. The unit identifies better safety training strategies as a topic for future work.

Discussions on Future Directions

Current safety fine-tuning methods are primarily designed for non-RAG settings. The text suggests tailoring safety fine-tuning specifically to RAG tasks when a model will be used in the RAG setting.
