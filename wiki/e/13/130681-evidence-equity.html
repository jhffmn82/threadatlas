4 Discussion .. 4.2 Bias mitigation and evidence equity

Evidence Equity describes an approach in which the system considers the relevance of evidence to a patient’s demographic context rather than providing only the most frequently cited answer. It is presented as a way to move beyond technical verification toward more representative clinical recommendations.
