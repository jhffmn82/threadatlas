Also called: KG, a KG, existing KGs, knowledge network

Abstract written by a model

The knowledge graph organizes fact-level relationships extracted from 66,581 HotpotQA documents. Manually prompted Llama-3 produced 211,356 triplets spanning 98,226 entities and 19,813 relations; each triplet links to its source chunk. It supports graph-guided expansion and context organization, with triplet counts showing a long-tail distribution across chunks and documents.
