semantic RAG

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Semantic RAG, semantic-based RAG approaches

Abstract written by a model

Semantic RAG is a retrieval-augmented generation method that retrieves query-similar documents or chunks, concatenates relevant semantic matches, and supplies them to an LLM. Its chunks can be homogeneous, redundant, isolated, and lack intrinsic relationships. It performs below KG2RAG in reported evaluations, though reranking improves results.
