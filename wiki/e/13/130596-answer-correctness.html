Experimental Setup

Answer Correctness combines Answer Similarity with factual similarity, defined as the F1-score between claims in the ground-truth answer and claims in the generated response.

Answer correctness measures how closely generated answers align with ground-truth answers when retrieved contexts are provided. OG-RAG improves answer correctness by 40% overall in the reported query-answering comparison.
