Abstract written by a model

A professional vector knowledge base for chronic low back pain, built from PubMed medical literature and guidelines from 2021–2025, including 4,612 relevant articles and the latest guidelines. It integrates research papers and guidelines, retrieves information relevant to extracted patient features, and combines retrieval-augmented generation with large language models to provide professional, comprehensive, authoritative, and timely support for clinical decision-making.
