Abstract written by a model

HotpotQA supplies annotated supporting facts for evaluating whether systems identify the evidence behind an answer. Supporting-fact EM, F1, precision, and recall are reported, along with joint metrics. Supporting-fact results show that QD+RR has the highest precision and recall, while RR has the highest EM and F1. QD+RR records 17.9 F1, 46.8 precision, and 11.2 recall in the reported table.
