Index graphs are a structured knowledge organization paradigm in GraphRAG systems. They organize raw text chunks or other retrievable items as nodes linked by query-oriented, semantic, keyword, or domain-specific relationships, localizing relevant knowledge and retrieving text for language-model-based answering.

II. OVERVIEW

Index graphs are described as one of two primary paradigms for structured knowledge organization in GraphRAG systems.

IV. KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION .. C. Hybrid GraphRAG

An index graph organizes text chunks or other retrievable items as nodes connected by query-oriented, semantic, keyword, or domain-specific relationships. It helps localize relevant knowledge and retrieve raw text chunks for language-model-based answering.
