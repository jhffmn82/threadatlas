C. Discussion on GraphRAG

Large-scale graphs create scalability challenges because their numbers of nodes and edges may exceed the input-length limits of large language models. Efficiently managing large-scale graph problems is identified as an important research area for applications including social network analysis, bioinformatics, and knowledge graph construction.
