Abstract written by a model

MEDRAG is a medical QA dataset of 7,663 questions, proposed early for medical retrieval-augmented generation. It improves question answering through external medical knowledge; MIRAGE evaluation found clear gains without prior training on two of five datasets, smaller elsewhere.
