transparency

Abstract written by a model

Transparency is a major adoption requirement and essential to trust in AI clinical decision support. It involves communicating how outputs are reached, system performance, capabilities, and limitations in forms suited to clinical users. It is supported by explanations, source links, dataset and algorithm documentation, explicit citations, and accessible evidence excerpts. The framework presents this information without default exposure to raw model outputs or complex probability scores, while preserving processing logs for deeper inspection. It unfolds from its role in trust and adoption, through transparency mechanisms, to evidence review and forensic audit logging.
