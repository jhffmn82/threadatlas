LLM in MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health

Shan Xu, Zhaokun Yan, Chengxiao Dai and Fan Wu · 2025-10-08

LLM is a standalone large language model using LLaMA3-70B, evaluated without external retrieval on medical queries; it achieves 0.6674 accuracy, 0.8696 recall, and 0.7643 F1-score.

4 Experiment / 4.1 Datasets .. 4.3 Experimental setting

The standalone LLM is evaluated without external retrieval to measure its intrinsic performance on medical queries. It achieves an accuracy of 0.6674, recall of 0.8696, and F1-score of 0.7643, and it is implemented using LLaMA3-70B.
