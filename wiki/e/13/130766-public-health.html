Abstract written by a model

Public health is an application domain encompassing disease management, efforts to improve health outcomes, and emergency response. It is the context for applying large language models and MEGA-RAG to health education, clinical communication, evidence-based policy development, and more robust clinical decision-making. Public health question answering draws on biomedical literature, official public health documents, and a biomedical knowledge graph to improve factual accuracy, interpretability, and evidence coverage. Its subject matter includes epidemiological research, disease prevention, treatment guidelines, healthcare policy, and geographically and demographically diverse queries. Evaluations of biomedical question-answering systems focus on reliability and transparency for policymaking, clinical decision support, and outbreak surveillance. Across epidemiological, preventive, treatment, and policy-related queries, MEGA-RAG mitigates hallucinations and improves accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score, with human-level recall on disease-related queries.
