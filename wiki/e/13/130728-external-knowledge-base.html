4 Experiment / 4.1 Datasets .. 4.3 Experimental setting

The external knowledge base is constructed from distinct IRIS passages and PubMed abstracts that are topically related to the evaluation queries but do not contain ground-truth answers. It is separated from the test question-answer pairs to prevent evaluation bias and simulate a realistic RAG scenario.
