Abstract written by a model

CLBP-GPT is a proposed generative model and clinical decision-supporting framework for chronic low back pain. It integrates GPT-4.0, retrieval-augmented generation, and LtM prompt engineering to improve the professionalism, interpretability, and clinical realism of personalized diagnosis and treatment recommendations. Its process begins by collecting patient questions and building a chronic low back pain knowledge base; GPT extracts features, retrieves knowledge, designs prompts, generates results, and provides personalized decisions. It was evaluated on 80 questions through an automated system based on the GPT-4.0 API, against GPT-4.0, ERNIE Bot, and DeepSeek. It achieved the highest reported scores: 4.38 accuracy, 4.39 relevance, 4.42 clarity, 4.38 benefit, 4.42 completeness, and 4.40 total score, and was particularly outstanding on complex tasks.

CLBP-GPT is the framework for chronic low back pain that integrates LLMs and RAG techniques. 4 Results

CLBP-GPT was evaluated against three other generative models on 80 questions. CLBP-GPT in Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology

Rong Chen, Siyun Zhang, Yiyi Zheng, Qiuhua Yu and Chuhuai Wang · 2025-05-14 · the work’s page · read it where CLBP-GPT first appears

Abstract written by a model

CLBP-GPT is a hybrid LLM-RAG clinical decision framework and decision-support model for chronic low back pain management. It combines seven modules, data collection, knowledge-base construction, feature extraction, knowledge retrieval, prompt engineering, result generation, and personalized decision-making, to turn hospital and online-platform data, including unstructured descriptions, into feasible treatment recommendations. GPT-4-based semantic analysis and a CLBP knowledge base support dynamic retrieval of clinical guidelines and evidence-based medicine. Its personalized decisions integrate biomedical, occupational, psychosocial, lifestyle, and workflow factors, while prompt engineering provides an auditable reasoning path. In reported evaluation, it scored higher than GPT-4.0, ERNIE Bot, and DeepSeek in accuracy, relevance, clarity, benefit, completeness, and total performance. Remaining limitations are limited long-term clinical validation, rapid knowledge-update needs, and limited cross-cultural adaptability.

The clinical decision framework for CLBP combines LLMs and RAG technology through seven modules: data collection, knowledge-base construction, feature extraction, knowledge retrieval, prompt engineering, result generation, and personalized decision-making. It is designed to produce clinically valuable and feasible treatment recommendations.

5 Discussion

CLBP-GPT is a decision-support model for chronic low back pain that combines GPT-4-based semantic analysis, dynamic knowledge retrieval, personalized decision-making, and clinical workflow constraints. It achieved higher mean scores than GPT-4.0, ERNIE Bot, and DeepSeek across accuracy, relevance, clarity, benefit, completeness, and total performance. Its remaining limitations include insufficient long-term clinical validation, knowledge-update challenges, and limited cross-cultural adaptability.
