Abstract written by a model

News is a publicly available Multi-hop RAG dataset used in retrieval evaluation. It generates a hard subset of RAGAS reasoning questions about complex, multifaceted news stories and contributes to more than 45K entities’ attributes and relationships with agriculture datasets. It is evaluated for retrieval, answer quality, and efficiency, where OG-RAG outperforms overall with lower preprocessing time.

News is in one work on this site, OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Experimental Setup

The news dataset is publicly available and comes from Multi-hop RAG. It is used to generate a hard subset of reasoning questions about complex and multifaceted news stories.

Experiments / Query answering .. Ontology sensitivity

News is evaluated for retrieval quality, answer quality, and efficiency. On News, OG-RAG matches RAPTOR on context recall while achieving better overall performance, and it has lower preprocessing time than RAPTOR and GraphRAG.
