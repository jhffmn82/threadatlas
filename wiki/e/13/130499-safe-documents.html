Safe documents can still be repurposed or interpreted to produce unsafe responses in RAG-based LLMs; Llama-3-8B shows higher unsafe-response rates with safe documents, and most of its unsafe outputs arise from them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

RQ2: What makes RAG-based LLMs .. Factor 3: An LLM’s Capability on RAG

Safe documents do not prevent unsafe responses. For example, Llama-3-8B produces unsafe responses with safe documents at a rate of 7.9%, compared with 0.3% in the non-RAG setting, and 81.8% of its unsafe responses arise from safe documents.
