3 Methods (framework design) .. 3.3.4 Step 4: Forensic audit logging

The knowledge base contains curated clinical guidelines, drug interaction databases, PubMed abstracts, peer-reviewed medical textbooks, and associated provenance and evidence-grading metadata. It is updated as new evidence emerges, with those updates recorded as audit-log transactions.

4 Discussion .. 4.2 Bias mitigation and evidence equity

The knowledge base is assumed to contain comprehensive, current medical evidence with provenance metadata. Its construction and maintenance would require licensing, continuous updates, resolution of conflicting guidelines, and mitigation of biases in source materials.
