AutoDAN

method · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

AutoDAN is a gradient-based method that optimizes adversarial suffixes for harmful queries, using controllable text generation and gradient-based token search to create human-readable jailbreaking prompts; it unfolds by generating and optimizing candidates, with slightly better transfer than GCG but mostly failed attempts.

AutoDAN is in one work on this site, RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

AutoDAN uses controllable text generation techniques to produce human-readable adversarial prompts while optimizing token candidates with gradient-based search. Its jailbreaks transfer slightly better than GCG from non-RAG to RAG settings, but most attempts still fail.
