PubMedGPT in MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health

Shan Xu, Zhaokun Yan, Chengxiao Dai and Fan Wu · 2025-10-08

PubMedGPT is a GPT-based biomedical language model fine-tuned on PubMed to generate responses for public health queries. It achieves 0.744 accuracy and 0.7781 F1-score, serving as the strongest domain-specific baseline for accuracy. Though generally accurate and guideline-aligned, it omits explicit references and is a black box, limiting interpretability; MEGA-RAG reaches 0.7913.

4 Experiment / 4.1 Datasets .. 4.3 Experimental setting

PubMedGPT is based on the GPT architecture and is fine-tuned on the PubMed dataset to generate responses for public health queries. It achieves an accuracy of 0.744 and an F1-score of 0.7781.

5 Results .. 5.3 Evaluating hallucinations in diﬀerent

PubMedGPT is evaluated as a domain-specific baseline and is described as the strongest baseline for accuracy at 0.744, while MEGA-RAG reaches 0.7913. It is generally accurate and aligned with known guidelines, but it omits explicit references and operates as a black box, limiting interpretability.
