Abstract written by a model

Annotation and validation of HealthQuestDB are performed by two domain experts, selecting question-answer pairs, assigning yes/no labels, resolving ambiguity with long-form answers, and independently annotating 20% with perfect agreement (Cohen's kappa 1.0).
