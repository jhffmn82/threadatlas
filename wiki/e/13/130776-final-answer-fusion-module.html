Final Answer Fusion module in MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health

Shan Xu, Zhaokun Yan, Chengxiao Dai and Fan Wu · 2025-10-08

Final Answer Fusion module operates when candidate responses have sufficient consensus, using majority voting and sentence-level embedding clustering to select representative answers and fuse them by concatenation or structured summary synthesis; no consensus invokes Discrepancy-Identified Self-Clarification Module.

1 section; each heading opens the text

3 Methods / 3.1 MEGA-RAG framework .. 3.6 Final answer fusion module

The Final Answer Fusion module operates when candidate responses show sufficient consensus. It applies majority voting to binary decisions, clusters responses using sentence-level embeddings, selects representative answers, and fuses them by concatenation or structured summary synthesis.
