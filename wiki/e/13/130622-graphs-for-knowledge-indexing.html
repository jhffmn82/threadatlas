IV. KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION .. C. Hybrid GraphRAG

Graphs for Knowledge Indexing symbolize text chunks as graph nodes and use edges to express query-oriented relationships. Multi-level versions support bottom-up knowledge summarization and top-down localization for coarse-to-fine retrieval.
