Abstract

Existing retrieval methods can be suboptimal for adapting large language models to specialized knowledge. OG-RAG anchors retrieval in domain-specific ontologies and is compared with baseline methods.

Related Work

Retrieval methods include traditional retrieval augmented generation and graph-based approaches. Recent methods use structured knowledge graphs, existing knowledge graphs, or domain experts-defined semantic schema to improve contextual and factual retrieval.

Limitations

Retrieval requires well-defined domain ontologies to work effectively in the described setting. The availability of these ontologies therefore limits retrieval in newer and fast-developing domains.
