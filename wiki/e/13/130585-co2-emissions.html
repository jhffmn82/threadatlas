Abstract written by a model

CO2 emissions are the target of a deductive reasoning experiment in which six agricultural facts and rules are used to derive emissions from herbicide, insecticide, and fungicide production. The stated emission factors are 18.22–26.63 kg CO2e per kg of herbicide, 14.79–18.91 kg CO2e per kg of insecticide, and 11.94–29.19 kg CO2e per kg of fungicide.
