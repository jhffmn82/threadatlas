Discussions on Future Directions

Red-teaming RAG-based models requires new methods. Possible approaches include adapting GCG and AutoDAN with re-retrieval after each optimization step, penalizing retrieval changes, or optimizing universal jailbreaking prompts over many retrieved documents.
