Abstract written by a model

LLM capability on RAG tasks comprises retrieval and document synthesis, shaping safety through extraction, summarization, and attention to retrieved documents; poor extraction may yield apparently safe refusals, while ignoring documents can produce unsafe internal-knowledge responses.
