KG-guided expansion

process · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Graph-guided Expansion, KG-guided chunk expansion, KG-guided chunk expansion process, KG-guided retrieval expansion, chunk expansion, expansion, graph-guided expansion

Abstract written by a model

KG-guided chunk expansion is a retrieval function and module in KG2RAG that enhances retrieval-augmented generation by delivering relevant and important knowledge. After seed chunks are retrieved, it extracts a relevant subgraph from the association KG and traverses graph relationships around the seed chunks’ triplets. Using breadth-first search over an m-hop neighborhood, it gathers and links chunks containing overlapping, connected, same, or related entities and triplets, including fact-level related chunks that semantic retrieval may miss. The expanded chunks become seed chunks for further retrieval. Although removing the process can raise retrieval precision, it lowers response quality and may omit necessary information; its effectiveness depends on collaboration with KG-based context organization.

Abstract

The KG-guided chunk expansion process is used by KG2RAG after seed chunks are retrieved. It uses knowledge graph guidance to expand the retrieved knowledge.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

Graph-guided expansion traverses the knowledge graph around the triplets associated with retrieved seed chunks. It gathers chunks containing the same or related entities or triplets, including chunks that may not be semantically similar to the query or located nearby in the documents.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3

KG-guided expansion uses graph relationships to identify and retrieve fact-level related chunks that semantic retrieval may miss. Removing it produces higher retrieval precision in the ablation but lowers response quality and can omit necessary information.

5

KG-guided chunk expansion is a module in KG2RAG that expands seed chunks using knowledge-graph guidance. The ablation study confirms that it contributes to KG2RAG's effectiveness in collaboration with KG-based context organization.

Limitations

KG-guided retrieval expansion is a retrieval function performed by KG2RAG to enhance retrieval-augmented generation.
