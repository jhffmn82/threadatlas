Related Work

Deductive reasoning is presented as an area where rule-based systems offer interpretability and control while LLMs offer flexibility and generalization. OG-RAG anchors unstructured text to domain-specific vocabulary so LLMs can apply domain-specific rules more effectively.

Experiments / Query answering .. Ontology sensitivity

Factual deduction evaluates whether LLMs can derive new conclusions from predefined agricultural facts and rules. OG-RAG generally improves correctness, similarity, and relevance for deductions involving CO2 emissions.
