Abstract written by a model

Clinicians are users of an AI framework who are wary of opaque algorithms and prioritize transparent, verifiable recommendations grounded in medical guidelines or high-quality studies. They seek concrete citations, control, reliability, usability, training, and workflow integration. The framework lets them enter patient-specific questions, receive concise actionable guidance such as fluid resuscitation for suspected sepsis with low blood pressure, inspect supporting evidence, and review forensic audit information in real time or retrospectively.
