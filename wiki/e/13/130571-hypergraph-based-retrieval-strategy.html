2 sections; each heading opens the text

OG-RAG .. Relevant Hyeredges as Context

Hypergraph retrieval identifies query-relevant hypernodes by comparing the query with hypernode keys and values. It then greedily selects hyperedges that cover the relevant hypernodes.

Limitations

The hypergraph-based retrieval strategy can benefit directly from future advances in automatically abstracting domain knowledge as ontologies. Its efficacy is established for accurately and succinctly answering domain-specific questions when an ontology is available.
