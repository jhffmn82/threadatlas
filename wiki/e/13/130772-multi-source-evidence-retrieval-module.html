Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval Module

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval, Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval (MSER) Module, evidence, evidence retrieval, multi-source evidence retrieval module, multi-stage evidence aggregation pipeline, structured multi-source evidence retrieval, structured, multi-source evidence retrieval

Abstract written by a model

The Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval Module is a central MEGA-RAG component that concurrently retrieves evidence from PubMed, the WHO IRIS database, and CPubMed-KG using FAISS, BM25, and graph-based retrieval. It then merges and reranks results with cross-encoder re-ranking and weighted entailment scoring to improve semantic and factual alignment.

The Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval Module concurrently retrieves evidence from PubMed, the WHO IRIS database, and the CPubMed-KG knowledge graph. It combines dense semantic retrieval, sparse lexical retrieval, and graph-based retrieval before merging and reranking the results.

5 Results .. 5.3 Evaluating hallucinations in diﬀerent
