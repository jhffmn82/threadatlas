The Diverse Prompted Answer Generation Module produces multiple evidence-grounded candidate answers from top-ranked passages and knowledge graph triples. It samples responses at temperatures between 0.6 and 1.0 and prompts the model to provide a short answer followed by a final yes/no decision.
