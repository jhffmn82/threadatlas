accuracy in Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology

Rong Chen, Siyun Zhang, Yiyi Zheng, Qiuhua Yu and Chuhuai Wang · 2025-05-14

Accuracy is an evaluation metric for generative models, measuring consistency with medical common sense on a five-point Likert scale from fundamental misunderstanding to highly accurate medical understanding. In results, CLBP-GPT scored 4.38, above GPT-4.0, ERNIE Bot, and DeepSeek.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

3 Experiment .. 3.1 Evaluation metrics

Accuracy measured whether an answer was consistent with medical common sense using a five-point Likert scale. Scores ranged from 1 for a fundamental misunderstanding of medical concepts to 5 for accurate information reflecting a high level of medical understanding.

4 Results

CLBP-GPT achieved an average accuracy score of 4.38, compared with 3.98 for GPT-4.0, 3.54 for ERNIE Bot, and 3.83 for DeepSeek.
