Abstract written by a model

Patient Decision Aid is an evidence-based tool that supports informed decisions by presenting clinical recommendations, treatment options, and outcomes. It improves knowledge, reduces decision conflict, and matches treatment to values and preferences; the introduction notes too few high-quality aids for chronic low back pain.

1 Introduction

A Patient Decision Aid supports informed patient decisions by presenting information about treatment options and related outcomes. High-quality Patient Decision Aids can improve knowledge, reduce decision conflict, and help match treatments to patients’ values and preferences, but the unit reports a lack of high-quality aids meeting international standards for chronic low back pain.
