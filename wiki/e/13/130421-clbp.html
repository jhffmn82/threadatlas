Abstract written by a model

Chronic low back pain is a global health and public health problem affecting approximately 70%–85% of the adult population and seriously reducing patients’ quality of life. It has a complex, heterogeneous presentation with non-specific symptoms, uncertain treatment responses, increased pain sensitivity, reduced lumbar mobility, weakened muscle strength, functional impairment, depression, anxiety, and psychological and social influences. Its causes include biomechanical, neurophysiological, and psychosocial factors, alongside biomedical, occupational, and lifestyle considerations. It is the clinical focus and target condition of the CLBP-GPT decision-support framework, whose dataset and knowledge base support diagnosis and personalized treatment recommendations across varied low back pain types.

1 Introduction

Chronic low back pain is described as a global public health problem affecting approximately 70%–85% of the adult population. It involves complex biomechanical, neurophysiological, and psychosocial factors and is associated with reduced lumbar mobility, weakened muscle strength, emotional disorders, increased pain sensitivity, and functional impairment.

2 Materials and methods / 2.1 Framework .. 2.4 LtM prompting

Chronic low back pain is the clinical focus of the framework, dataset, knowledge base, and decision recommendations. The dataset includes varied clinical types such as acute low back pain, chronic low back pain, and neuropathic low back pain.
