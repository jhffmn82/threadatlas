3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The study evaluates eleven popular large language models on more than 5,000 harmful questions. It assumes that these questions should not be answered in either RAG or non-RAG settings.

RQ1: Are RAG-based LLMs safer than

The evaluation uses 5,592 harmful questions collected from the Red-Teaming Resistance Benchmark and HarmBench. These questions cover nine datasets, are labeled using a 16-category risk taxonomy, and are not supposed to be answered in either RAG or non-RAG settings.

RQ3: Are red-teaming methods .. Applying Jailbreaking Methods to RAG

The evaluation randomly selects 50 harmful queries per model from a dataset, restricting them to queries the model initially refuses in both non-RAG and RAG settings. The same queries are used during optimization and testing.
