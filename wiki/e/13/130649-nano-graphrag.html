A. Open-Source Projects and Applications

Nano-GraphRAG is a lightweight and customizable implementation with a codebase of approximately 1,100 lines. It supports graph storage systems such as Neo4j and vector databases such as Milvus and Faiss, enabling developers to prototype or deploy graph-enhanced large language model systems.
