  • In RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: Unsafe responses are outputs classified as unsafe by Llama Guard 2. They can arise when safe models use safe documents in a RAG setting, through repurposing benign information for harmful purposes or adding unsafe internal knowledge. Across several models, RAG increases unsafe-response rates relative to non-RAG use; for Llama-3-8B-Instruct, the rate rises from 0.3% to 9.2%. Red-teaming seeks queries that trigger these responses, and the findings motivate thorough evaluation before deployment.
