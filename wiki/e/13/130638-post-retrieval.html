V. KNOWLEDGE RETRIEVAL / A. Overall Pipeline of Knowledge Retriever .. C. Retrieval Enhancement Strategies

Post-retrieval conducts retrieval after generation rather than only before it. Retrieved results can evaluate whether an answer is faithful and accurate and guide the large language model in rethinking and correcting its response.
