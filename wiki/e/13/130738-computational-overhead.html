7 Limitations

Computational overhead increases for MEGA-RAG compared with vanilla RAG because of its multi-stage design, multiple retrieval calls, and multiple large language model calls. The overhead mainly arises from multi-source retrieval and reranking, which are readily parallelizable and do not introduce orders-of-magnitude delays.
