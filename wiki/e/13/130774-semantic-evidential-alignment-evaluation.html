The Semantic-Evidential Alignment Evaluation Module assesses semantic disagreement among candidate answers and their coverage of retrieved evidence. It uses BERTScore and cosine similarity to calculate divergence and evidence alignment, which guide direct fusion or activation of self-clarification.
