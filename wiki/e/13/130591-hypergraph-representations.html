hypergraph representations

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: hypergraph, hypergraph H(I), hypergraph representation, ontology-grounded hypergraph

Abstract written by a model

A hypergraph representation represents domain documents. It consists of hypernodes and hyperedges, arbitrary sets of hypernodes that group related factual knowledge and capture multi-dimensional relationships between facts. Constructed from ontology-mapped information, it organizes factual-knowledge clusters for query-based retrieval, where a greedy algorithm selects a minimal set of relevant hyperedges as context.

Abstract

OG-RAG constructs a hypergraph representation of domain documents. This representation organizes clusters of factual knowledge grounded in a domain-specific ontology for query-based retrieval.

Introduction

Hypergraph representations are formed from domain documents in OG-RAG. Their hyperedges group related factual knowledge, and a greedy retrieval algorithm selects a minimal set of hyperedges for a query.

OG-RAG .. Relevant Hyeredges as Context

The hypergraph is constructed from ontology-mapped information and consists of hypernodes and hyperedges. Its hyperedges group multiple related hypernodes so that multi-dimensional relationships between facts can be represented.
