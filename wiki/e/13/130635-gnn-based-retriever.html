V. KNOWLEDGE RETRIEVAL / A. Overall Pipeline of Knowledge Retriever .. C. Retrieval Enhancement Strategies

The GNN-based retriever uses GNNs to encode nodes in graph knowledge bases. It locates knowledge through similarities between node representations that capture both semantic meaning and structural relationships, and it requires training an encoder with an appropriate loss function.
