Unlike taxonomy or classifications, it represents richer relationships through triples: subject-attribute-value structures in which attribute values are either entities or unspecified domain values denoted by ϕ. Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Ontologies conceptually organize domain knowledge by defining entities and their interrelationships. OG-RAG uses domain-specific ontologies to ground factual knowledge in documents.

Introduction

Ontologies define key entities and their relationships within a domain and provide structured representations for adapting to complex and evolving information landscapes. They can serve as templates for organizing and analyzing facts and are used by OG-RAG to ground retrieval.

Related Work

Ontologies traditionally provide a consistent and clear framework for organizing domain knowledge. With LLMs, automated ontology learning and extraction of domain-specific information have also been explored.

Background

An ontology is a formal representation of key entities and their relationships within a domain. It consists of triples connecting entities through attributes, with values that are either other entities or unspecified domain values denoted by ϕ.

OG-RAG .. Relevant Hyeredges as Context

The ontology provides formal representations of domain-specific concepts and their relationships. It is used to map document information into structured factual blocks suitable for retrieval.

Experiments / Query answering .. Ontology sensitivity

The ontology supplies domain-specific structure and ontology-mapped data for retrieval and deductive question generation. Dropping ontology attributes is used to test how dependent the results are on ontology quality.

Conclusion

Ontology is used to ground the retrieval-augmented generation framework and support controlled vocabulary and structured evidence retrieval. Future work may explore automated or semi-automated ontology construction.
