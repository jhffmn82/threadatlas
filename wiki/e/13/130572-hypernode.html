Abstract written by a model

A hypernode is a key-value pair formed from ontology entities and attributes; its concatenated nested key makes it relevant when query similarity matches key or value.

hypernode is in one work on this site, OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
