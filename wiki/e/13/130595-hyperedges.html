hyperedges

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: hyperedge

Abstract written by a model

A hyperedge encapsulates and groups related factual knowledge in clusters grounded using a domain-specific ontology. It is an arbitrary set of hypernodes representing a true fact. During retrieval, optimization selects hyperedges covering the greatest number of relevant hypernodes, producing retrieved context.
