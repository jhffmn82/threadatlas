Evaluation frameworks for medical RAG systems are reviewed through systematic analysis of RAG techniques. They assess retrieval and generation objectives and metrics, but metrics vary substantially across studies and no standardized framework exists. The review highlights RAGAs as separately evaluating retriever and generator, then recommends standardized, healthcare-contextualized metrics combining automated and human evaluation.

Abstract

The review finds a lack of standardized evaluation frameworks for RAG-based applications in healthcare.

Introduction

The review examines evaluation frameworks for Retrieval-Augmented Generation in medical settings.

Discussion

Evaluation frameworks assess RAG objectives and metrics, including retrieval and generation performance. The unit reports substantial variation in metrics across studies, making comparisons difficult, and recommends standardized, healthcare-contextualized metrics that combine automated evaluation with human evaluation. RAGAs is cited as a framework that evaluates the generator and retriever separately.

Conclusion

Evaluation frameworks for medical RAG systems are assessed in the paper. The findings indicate that no standardized framework currently exists for evaluating RAG pipelines in the medical field.
