Abstract written by a model

The evaluation process begins with standardized data preparation and model-answer collection, then has three experts score questions and answers, averages their scores, and compares models using evaluation metrics. CLBP-GPT is evaluated automatically; other models are manually questioned and recorded, yielding a reusable technical framework.

3 Experiment .. 3.1 Evaluation metrics

The evaluation process included standardized data preparation, collection of model answers, submission of questions and answers to three experts for scoring, averaging the expert scores, and comprehensive comparison across models. CLBP-GPT was evaluated automatically, whereas the other models were evaluated through manual questioning and recording.
