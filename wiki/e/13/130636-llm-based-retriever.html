V. KNOWLEDGE RETRIEVAL / A. Overall Pipeline of Knowledge Retriever .. C. Retrieval Enhancement Strategies

The LLM-based retriever uses large language models to interpret queries, expand or disambiguate them, identify key subgraphs, and synthesize candidate information. It can often be guided by prompts without retriever training, although training may be needed when retrieval results are used for model reflection or fine-tuning.
