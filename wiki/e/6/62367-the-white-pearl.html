the White Pearl

object · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: White Pearl, the Pearl, the Voice of the Pearl, the pearl, the third was pure white, the white pearl, white speaking magic pearl

Abstract written by a model

The White Pearl is a white, speaking magic pearl containing the magical Voice of the Pearl. Its small voice always gives wise and helpful words and guidance. At first, King Kitticut holds it to Inga’s ear, and it tells the Prince not to question truths he does not understand. Inga later consults it for the journey to liberate his parents: it directs him toward Regos and Coregos, foretells a storm-stranded boat, and explains the help of the Blue and Pink Pearls. It promises and provides the boat, then remains in Inga’s silken bag for further counsel. It teaches him to summon the boat from Coregos by clapping three times, guides him toward King Gos’s boat and the nomes’ caverns, and urges patience, bravery, and determination.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The white speaking magic pearl can speak, and its words are always wise and helpful. When King Kitticut holds it to Inga's ear, it tells the Prince not to question truths he does not understand.

Chapter Five

The White Pearl speaks when Inga holds it to his ear. It advises him to go to Regos and Coregos, predicts that a boat will strand on Pingaree after a storm, and tells him that the Blue Pearl will give him strength and the Pink Pearl will protect him.

Chapter Six

The White Pearl promises Inga a boat, and the promised boat appears on the shore of Pingaree. Inga believes its guidance has helped direct him toward Regos and Coregos.

Chapter Eight

Inga keeps the White Pearl in a silken bag and consults it after both magic shoes disappear. He asks what he should do, and it conveys advice from the Voice of the Pearl.

Chapter Fourteen

The White Pearl tells Inga that he can summon the boat by going to the south end of Coregos and clapping his hands three times. Inga follows its instructions and successfully brings the boat to shore.

Chapter Eighteen

The White Pearl guides Inga toward the boat of King Gos and advises him to follow the path to the caverns of the nomes. It directs Inga to summon Klik and later tells him to be patient, brave, and determined.
