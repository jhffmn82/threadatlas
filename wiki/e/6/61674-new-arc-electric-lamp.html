Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ON A NEW ARC ELECTRIC LAMP. .. _Electrical Arrangement_.

The new arc electric lamp has a fixed negative carbon and a movable positive carbon. Its regulation is separated from the lamp, allowing the lamp to be fixed in an inaccessible place.
