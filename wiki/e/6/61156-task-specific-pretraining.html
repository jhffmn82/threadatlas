Introduction

Task-specific pretraining refines the pretraining process to align more closely with a downstream task. The paper investigates it for distractor generation through Retrieval Augmented Pretraining.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

Task-specific pretraining integrates task-specific information into language model pretraining to improve performance in low-resource fine-tuning tasks. Studies report that aligning the pretraining task and dataset with the downstream task reduces overfitting and improves performance.

5

Task-specific pretraining is introduced as part of the paper's approach. Its integration with a knowledge base is associated with significant performance improvement.
