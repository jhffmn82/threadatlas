task-specific pretraining

Also called: Task-Specific Pretraining, Task-specific Pretraining, task- specific pretraining, task-pretraining, task-specific pretraining approach

Abstract written by a model

Task-specific pretraining refines language-model pretraining to align with downstream tasks by integrating task-specific priors and using similar datasets. It improves low-resource fine-tuning, reduces overfitting, and can outperform masked language modeling when alignment is closer. The paper investigates it for distractor generation through Retrieval Augmented Pretraining, where integration with a knowledge base is associated with significant improvement.
