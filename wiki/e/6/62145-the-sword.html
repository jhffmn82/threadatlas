the sword

Abstract written by a model

Electra’s sword, made keen for planned violence, rests across her knees as she waits for news and vows to strike if Orestes dies. It is a weapon used to kill Aegisthus: Orestes takes a heavier sword and strikes him, cleaving his brain and spine.

[ORESTES _and_ PYLADES _go in, following the_ SERVANTS.

Electra has a sword made keen for the planned violence. She keeps it across her knees while waiting for news and says that she will strike deeply if Orestes is killed.

CHORUS.

Orestes uses a Dorian blade to demonstrate his supposed Thessalian skill and then takes a heavier sword when Aegisthus asks him to cut the bull. With the heavier blade, Orestes strikes Aegisthus and cleaves the cord of his brain and spine.
