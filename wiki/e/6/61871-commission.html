A commission is urged to inspect solar heat apparatus at 30 Rue d'Assas, report promptly, and determine whether it is suitable for adoption.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

UTILIZATION OF SOLAR HEAT.

The writer calls for a competent commission to be sent immediately to 30 Rue d'Assas to examine and report on the apparatus. The commission would determine whether the apparatus was suitable for adoption.
