Abstract written by a model

The generator is a foundational element of CoV-RAG that produces answers and responses from questions, prompts, and retrieved references. In a RAG framework, it uses top satisfied documents, but may still generate hallucinations even when references are correct.

generator is in one work on this site, Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
