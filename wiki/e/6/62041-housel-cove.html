Abstract written by a model

Housel Cove is an Atlantic-coast cove and secluded bathing place with soft sand and a beautiful serpentine-rock dressing-room. On the sixth day, the narrator and companions spend a divine morning bathing there without organized amusement. On the eighth day, the women return through the fields and again find the bathing delightful.

DAY THE SIXTH And a day absolutely divine!

Housel Cove provides a secluded place for bathing, with soft sand and a beautiful dressing-room made of serpentine rock. The narrator and companions spend the whole morning there without band, promenade, or other organized amusement.

" DAY THE EIGHTH And seven days were all we could allow ourselves at the Lizard, if we meant to see the rest of Cornwall.

The women walk through the fields to Housel Cove for their bath. They find the bathing there as delightful as before.
