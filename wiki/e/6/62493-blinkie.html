Blinkie in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Blinkie is an old, one-eyed Wicked Witch of Jinxland, marked by a black patch over her blind eye. She lives in her own hut a few leagues from King Krewl's castle, leads Jinxland's witches, and serves Krewl through paid, evil witchcraft. Summoned to judge Cap'n Bill, she instead transforms the sleeping sailor into a small gray grasshopper with a wooden left leg. For payment, she agrees to freeze Princess Gloria's heart, performs the ceremony with three other witches, and rejoices at its success. After Trot and Pon witness the rite, Blinkie pursues them, attacks the Scarecrow, and continues the chase. Later she defies the Scarecrow until his magic dust shrinks her. She then restores Cap'n Bill and melts Gloria's heart. Antidote powder stops her shrinking but permanently removes her witchcraft, leaving her half her former size and an ordinary old woman. Grieving, she goes home and is advised to do good rather than evil.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

The Wicked Witch is summoned by King Krewl at Lord Googly-Goo's suggestion. She is expected to determine whether Cap'n Bill is a common man or a magician.

Chapter Twelve

Blinkie is an old, one-eyed Wicked Witch and the leader of the witches in Jinxland. She agrees to freeze Gloria's heart, then transforms the sleeping Cap'n Bill into a small gray grasshopper with a wooden left leg.

Chapter Thirteen

Blinkie is one of the Wicked Witches of Jinxland. She transforms a respectable old sailor into a grasshopper and plans to freeze Princess Gloria's heart.

Chapter Fourteen

Blinkie welcomes Gloria to her house and performs the heart-freezing ceremony after receiving payment from King Krewl. She summons three other witches, rejoices when the spell succeeds, and pursues Pon and Trot after they spy on the ceremony.

Chapter Fifteen

Blinkie traces Trot and Pon to a cornfield and chases them with a stick. She knocks the Scarecrow down, pulls the straw from his body, and then continues pursuing Trot and Pon without noticing that they have hidden from her.

Chapter Nineteen

Blinkie defies the Scarecrow but submits when his magic dust causes her to shrink. She restores Cap'n Bill, melts Gloria's frozen heart, and receives the antidote, which stops her shrinking but leaves her permanently smaller and powerless. She leaves for home grieving and is advised to do good instead of evil.
