THE FOOD AND ENERGY OF MAN.

Man is described as probably naturally frugivorous, like the monkeys, though he began eating animal food many ages ago. His digestive apparatus appears adapted to food capable of yielding nutriment, and his system can accommodate itself to changes in diet.
