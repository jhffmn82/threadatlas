The pool, a body of water inside the cavern under the sea, is fed by and leads into the ocean; it contains fish, yields a silver-scaled fish, and releases a strange winged creature.

Chapter Two

The pool is at the foot of the cavern's sandy beach and apparently leads into the ocean. Cap'n Bill fishes in it and eventually catches a silver-scaled fish. The strange winged creature later emerges from the pool.
