the river in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The river leads the group to the Great Waterfall, where it flows into a pool, rises again from a cave behind the waterfall as bubbling water, then finally dives underground and disappears before reappearing elsewhere.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The river is followed by the group until it reaches the Great Waterfall. It disappears over the waterfall into a pool, then rises again in a cavern before finally plunging underground and disappearing.
