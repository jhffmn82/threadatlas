Decision QA task .. Statistics of DQA

DQA contains 301 question-database pairs: 200 for Locating and 101 for Building. Each pair has relational and graph database versions, producing 602 databases in total. The average relational database has 2038.8 rows and 4.5 columns in Locating, and 579.0 rows and 4.5 columns in Building; the average graph database has 606.5 vertices and 1432.3 edges in Locating, and 204.3 vertices and 374.7 edges in Building.
