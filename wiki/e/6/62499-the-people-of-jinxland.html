Abstract written by a model

The people of Jinxland are mostly good but timid, fearing and secretly hating King Krewl. They gather for the Scarecrow’s threatened destruction, rejoice when the Orks rescue him, and hail him King. Later they choose Gloria, reject Krewl, and celebrate Gloria and Pon with a feast and dance.

Chapter Eighteen

The people of Jinxland fear King Krewl even though they hate him. They gather to watch the Scarecrow's destruction, then rejoice when the Orks rescue him and hail him as their King.

Chapter Twenty

The Jinxland people choose Gloria rather than the Scarecrow as their ruler, although some initially call for Pon. They cheer for Gloria and Pon, reject Krewl, and celebrate the new royal couple with a feast and dance.
