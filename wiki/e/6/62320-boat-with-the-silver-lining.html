Abstract written by a model

A black boat with a silver lining is a magic self-propelled craft, though Prince Inga also rows it. It comes from Pingaree when Inga claps three times, carries Inga, Rinkitink, Bilbil, and Zella along Regos, returns Zella, and pursues King Gos's boat for eight days without catching it.

Chapter Fourteen

The boat from Pingaree comes to Inga when he claps three times at the southern end of Coregos. Inga, Rinkitink, Bilbil, and Zella use it to travel along the coast of Regos, sleep in it overnight, and return to the City of Regos.

Chapter Sixteen

The silver-lined boat brings Zella back to Regos and later carries Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil away from the island. Inga rows it after King Gos's boat for eight days but does not overtake the barge.
