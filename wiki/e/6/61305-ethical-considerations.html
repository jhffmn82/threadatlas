ethical considerations in Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review

Lameck Mbangula Amugongo · 2025-06-11

Ethical considerations are challenges inherent to AI systems in clinical settings and discussed for retrieval-augmented generation in healthcare. They include correctness, privacy, data leakage, safety, robustness, bias, hallucination, trust through transparency or explainability, accountability, human oversight, and human autonomy. They matter because such systems may affect medical decisions and patient safety. Although mitigation includes oversight and autonomy, most reviewed studies do not assess or address these concerns.
