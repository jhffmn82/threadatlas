Abstract written by a model

Europa Universalis IV is a video game used to construct the Decision QA benchmark and simulator. It depicts international trade in Age of Discovery situations, with trade competition providing examples of Locating decisions.

Europa Universalis IV is in one work on this site, PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
