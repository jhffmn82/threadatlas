potash soap

object · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: potash soft soap, pure and neutral potash soap, pure neutral potash soap

Abstract written by a model

Potash soap is a soap described as superior to soda soap, especially for washing wool or woolen fabrics. Made with pure, neutral caustic potash, it suits wool washing, household use, and laundry. It may be made from tallow, grease, vegetable or animal oil, or cotton seed oil; the latter lathers very freely. It can be hardened by remelting with water and is made by fleece scouring.

potash soap is in one work on this site, Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

] SOAP AND ITS MANUFACTURE, FROM A CONSUMER'S POINT OF VIEW.

Potash soap is described as superior to soda soap, especially for washing wool or woolen fabrics. When made with pure caustic potash, it can be neutral and suitable for wool washing, household use, and laundry purposes.
