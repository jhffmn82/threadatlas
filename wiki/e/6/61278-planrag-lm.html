PlanRAG-LM

technology · 1 work · 22 quoted facts · 12 connections
Also called: PlanRAG

Abstract written by a model

PlanRAG-LM is a PlanRAG-based, RAG-based language-model decision maker implemented in a single-LM framework. It uses the Plan-Thought-Action-Observation-Re-plan prompt structure and conducts re-planning for questions; cases are categorized by step-count changes and plan-modification type, and a question can fall into several categories after more than two re-plans. With GPT-4 at zero temperature and LangChain, it achieved 64.3% accuracy in Locating and 45.0% in Building, outperforming IterRAG-LM by 15.8% and 7.4% and reducing missed-data analysis; its missed-data-analysis rates were 1.3% and 21.8%. In a prompt-structure comparison, it reached 57.5% in Locating and 50% in Building. Results also cover GPT-3.5, Llama 2, and Phi-2: GPT-3.5 performed poorly, while Llama 2 and Phi-2 could not solve DQA. Multiple-LM effectiveness remains unstudied.

PlanRAG-LM is in one work on this site, PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

PlanRAG-LM is the PlanRAG-based decision maker evaluated with GPT-4. It achieves 64.3% accuracy in Locating and 45.0% in Building, reduces candidate-selection and missed-data-analysis errors, and performs better than the other evaluated LMs on both relational and graph databases.

Limitations

The authors implemented PlanRAG-LM using a single LM. Its effectiveness in a multiple-LM framework is not a focus of the paper and remains for further study.

Building simulator algorithm / Business rules in DQA / Prompts for DQA / Previous RAG-based LMs / PlanRAG-based LM

PlanRAG-LM uses a Plan-Thought-Action-Observation-Re-plan prompt structure. In the reported comparison, it outperformed the other evaluated prompt structures, reaching 57.5% in Locating and 50% in Building.

Experimenting with other models

PlanRAG-LM is evaluated with GPT-3.5, Llama 2 (70B), Llama 2 (13B), and Phi-2. Its GPT-3.5 version scores 0% on Locating RDB, 4.0% on Locating GDB, and 1.0% on both Building RDB and Building GDB, while the Llama 2 and Phi-2 versions score 0% in all listed settings.

Re-planning cases

PlanRAG-LM conducts re-planning for questions and categorizes the resulting cases by changes in step count and by the type of plan modification. Some questions undergo re-planning more than twice and can therefore belong to several categories.
