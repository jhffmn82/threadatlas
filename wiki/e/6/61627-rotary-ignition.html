Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE "UNIVERSAL" GAS ENGINE.

The rotary ignition consists of a ratchet plate or flat disk with small radial slots that successively align with a slot at the end of the cylinder. When aligned, flame from the igniting gas jet passes through the openings and ignites the charge; the mechanism has a very small range of motion and is described as requiring no lubrication or attention.
