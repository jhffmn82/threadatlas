rotary ignition

Also called: ignition valve, ratchet plate, ratchet plate or flat disk, simple rotary ignition valve

Abstract written by a model

Rotary ignition is a gas engine design implemented as an ignition valve. A ratchet plate, or flat disk with radial slots, moves through a small range so its slots successively align with the cylinder-end slot, allowing flame from an igniting gas jet to ignite the charge without lubrication or attention.

THE "UNIVERSAL" GAS ENGINE.

The rotary ignition consists of a ratchet plate or flat disk with small radial slots that successively align with a slot at the end of the cylinder. When aligned, flame from the igniting gas jet passes through the openings and ignites the charge; the mechanism has a very small range of motion and is described as requiring no lubrication or attention.
