Charing Cross Hospital in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Charing Cross Hospital, located in London, employed Dr. James Mortimer as house-surgeon from 1882 to 1884; friends there gave him a stick on his marriage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1.

Charing Cross Hospital is identified as the institution represented by the initials “C.C.H.” on Mortimer’s stick. Mortimer served there as a house-surgeon from 1882 to 1884, and friends at the hospital presented him with the stick when he married.
