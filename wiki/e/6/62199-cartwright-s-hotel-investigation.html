Cartwright's hotel investigation in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Cartwright visits twenty-three nearby hotels, paying outside and hall porters while searching waste-paper for the cut-out center page of The Times; Holmes provides emergency money and requires a wire report to Baker Street before evening.

Cartwright's hotel investigation requires visits to twenty-three hotels, payments to the outside and hall porters, and a search for the cut-out center page of The Times. Holmes gives him additional money for emergencies and requests a wire report at Baker Street before evening.
