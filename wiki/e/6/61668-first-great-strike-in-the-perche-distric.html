] A CHARACTERISTIC MINING "RUSH.

The first great strike in the Perche district consisted of surface float found on a heavily timbered ridge by Jack Shedd. It included a 150-pound lump of ore worth an average of $11 per pound and an 87-pound block of horn silver containing specimens nearly 75 percent silver.
