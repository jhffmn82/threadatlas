defecator (apple jelly factory)

Also called: This defecator, the defecator

Abstract written by a model

The defecator is an eleven-foot by three-foot copper pan with steam-heated copper tubes. It clarifies cider as pomace and particles rise as scum removed by a floating rake, then partially evaporates the cider to about 20 degrees Baume before it enters the evaporator.

THE MANUFACTURE OF APPLE JELLY.

The defecator is an eleven-foot by three-foot copper pan containing steam-heated copper tubes. It clarifies the cider by causing pomace and other particles to expand and float as scum, which a floating rake removes, and it reduces the cider to a partial syrup of about 20 degrees Baume before the liquid enters the evaporator.
