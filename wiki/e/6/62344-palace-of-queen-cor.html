palace of Queen Cor in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Queen Cor’s palace is a great stone building in Coregos, belonging to Queen Cor. Reached after crossing the bridge of boats, it has open courtyard gates and a back door where Zella seeks admittance with honey. Its red-domed, dragon-painted throne room receives and frees Pingaree slaves, then shelters women and children for feast and lodging.

Queen Cor's palace contains the red-domed throne room, where the Pingaree slaves are brought and later freed. The women and children remain there for a feast and lodging while Inga and his companions plan their next actions.
