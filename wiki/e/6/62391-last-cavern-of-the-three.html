last cavern of the three

Abstract written by a model

The last cavern of three is a cavern whose flat rock floor becomes a monster furnace, entirely covered with glowing coals and flames. After its iron grating is removed, it contains the giant; Inga enters, defeats him, crosses toward the middle cavern, and later finds the previously empty cavern transformed.
