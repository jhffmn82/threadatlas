the search for Inga's parents and people in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

This is Inga's rescue mission to find and save his father, mother, and the people of Pingaree after Pingaree's destruction and their enslavement. He begins by voyaging to Regos and Coregos, relying on wisdom, caution, bravery, and the Magic Pearls. Although he plans to search immediately after taking Regos, the loss of the magic shoes forces him to postpone the effort. At the mines, King Kitticut has already been removed by King Gos. Inga learns his father was carried away by boat with King Gos and Queen Cor, and follows the rulers to Nome Country, where Dorothy goes to aid him.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Inga undertakes the voyage to Regos and Coregos so that he can rescue his parents and all his people from slavery. He believes the mission may succeed if he is wise, cautious, and brave.

Chapter Eight

Inga plans to begin searching for his father, mother, and the people of Pingaree immediately after taking Regos. After the loss of the magic shoes, he postpones the search because he cannot safely fight his way to the mines or attempt to conquer Coregos.

Chapter Fourteen

The planned rescue of King Kitticut does not succeed at the mines because King Gos removes him before Inga arrives. Inga learns that King Kitticut has been taken away in chains and later discovers that he has been carried off by boat with King Gos and Queen Cor.

Chapter Twenty

Inga's rescue mission is an attempt to save his captured parents and people after the destruction of Pingaree. He rows to Regos with the Magic Pearls and later follows the King of Regos and Queen of Coregos to the Nome King's country, prompting Dorothy to go to his aid.
