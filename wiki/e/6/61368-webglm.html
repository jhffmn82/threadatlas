Abstract written by a model

In RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, WebGLM is a web-enhanced question-answering dataset used for system evaluation and designed to resemble production scenarios. In Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation, WebGLM is the source dataset from which the CoV-RAG training dataset is derived.

Abstract written by a model

WebGLM is a web-enhanced question-answering dataset for system evaluation, designed to resemble production scenarios. It is trained with defensive advice as an ablation condition and achieves a 51.5% win rate on the full dataset; RAG-HAT reduces hallucination by 26.8% and wins 58.5% on answer quality.
