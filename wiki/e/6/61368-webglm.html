Dataset

WebGLM is a web-enhanced question-answering dataset used in the system evaluation process.

Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

WebGLM is used to provide evaluation data that more closely resembles the production web-enhanced question-answering scenario. On this data, RAG-HAT reduces the average hallucination rate by 26.8% and receives a 58.5% GPT-4 Turbo answer-quality win rate.
