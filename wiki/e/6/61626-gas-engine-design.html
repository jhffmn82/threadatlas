gas engine design

object · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: 2 horse power gas-engine, gas-engine, new and very simple form of gas engine, simple form of gas engine

Abstract written by a model

The design draws an explosive mixture of gas and air into the cylinder and ignites it once per revolution without compressing the charge. It uses valve and ignition mechanisms that can be duplicated at the front end to make the engine double-acting, while an eccentric keeps the exhaust valve open during the return stroke.
