the warning letter in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

The warning letter is a greyish envelope sent to Sir Henry Baskerville at the Northumberland Hotel, posted from Charing Cross. Made from pasted newspaper words and addressed in a disguised hand, it warns him of danger. Mrs. Stapleton created it by cutting out and assembling the message, and it reached Sir Henry as his initial warning.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4.

The warning letter is a greyish envelope addressed in rough printed characters to Sir Henry Baskerville at the Northumberland Hotel. It contains a folded half-sheet of foolscap with a message assembled from pasted printed words, and it was posted from Charing Cross the previous evening.

Chapter 15.

Mrs. Stapleton created the warning letter by cutting out words that formed a message and addressing it in a disguised hand. It reached Sir Henry and gave him an initial warning of danger.
