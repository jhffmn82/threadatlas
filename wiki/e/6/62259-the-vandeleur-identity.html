The Vandeleur identity names Mr. and Mrs. Stapleton as a married couple, documented by a photograph and school-era descriptions and established before British authorities in South America.

Chapter 13.

Holmes produces a photograph labeled 'Mr. and Mrs. Vandeleur' and written descriptions from the time they ran St. Oliver's private school. The evidence is used to establish that Mr. Stapleton and the woman presented as his sister were actually married under the Vandeleur identity.
