ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

Coal is a major operating cost in steam power, and its price and the engine's hourly consumption determine whether a more expensive engine is economical. In the example, coal consumption fell from five and one-half pounds to three pounds per hour per horsepower, reducing the stated 300-day cost from $11,088 to $6,134.
