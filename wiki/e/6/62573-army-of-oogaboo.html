Abstract written by a model

The Army of Oogaboo is Queen Ann Soforth's force, assembled from seventeen recruits from Oogaboo for invasion and conquest. Jo Candy refuses to join; the remaining men are organized as four Generals, four Colonels, four Majors, four Captains, and the sole private, Jo Files. Though equipped with impressive uniforms, swords, and other weapons, its sixteen officers seek plunder with as little fighting as possible. Under Ann, it becomes lost in barren country, hungry and discouraged, encounters a Rak, and camps by a brook. It later approaches Shaggy and companions intending conquest, but Hank routs the officers. After seeing the Love Magnet, they become Shaggy's friends and agree to help conquer Ruggedo. Files resigns, and Tik-Tok replaces him as private; the army is thereafter described as mainly composed of Tik-Tok. Traveling with Ann toward the Nome Kingdom, it plans to seize metals and jewels and divide the spoils. It follows Tik-Tok through the Tube, emerges in disorder, restrains the Peculiar Person, and follows Tubekins after invisible beings kick it. Finally, marching in single column toward the nomes, Tik-Tok falls, and the officers enter Ruggedo's Kingdom, fall into a hidden pit, and are imprisoned with Ann.

Army of Oogaboo in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where Army of Oogaboo first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Ann's Army is formed from the men of Oogaboo for her planned conquest. Four men become Generals, four Colonels, four Majors, and four Captains; Jo Files becomes the sole Private, while Jo Candy refuses to join.

Chapter Two

The Grand Army of Oogaboo consists of sixteen officers and the Private, Files. Its members wear impressive uniforms and carry weapons, but General Bunn says they seek plunder rather than trouble and prefer to avoid fighting.

Chapter Three

The Army of Oogaboo consists of sixteen officers and one private soldier under Ann Soforth. It becomes lost in an unknown, barren country, grows hungry and discouraged, encounters a Rak, and eventually camps beside a brook.

Chapter Eight

The Army of Oogaboo arrives intending to conquer Shaggy and his companions, but Hank routs its officers. After seeing the Love Magnet, its members become Shaggy's firm friends and agree to help conquer Ruggedo, with Tik-Tok replacing Private Files as the private soldier.

Chapter Nine

The Army of Oogaboo travels with its officers and Queen Ann toward the Nome Kingdom. According to the Long-Eared Hearer, it plans to capture the kingdom's metals and jewels and divide the spoils.

Chapter Ten

The Army of Oogaboo follows Tik-Tok into the Tube and slides down in a tangled crowd while its clanking swords strike and pummel the soldiers. The officers emerge in succession, help restrain the Peculiar Person, and later obey orders to follow Tubekins after invisible beings kick them.

Chapter Fifteen

The Army of Oogaboo advances toward the nomes in single column under Queen Ann's commands. It appears to be composed mainly of Tik-Tok, who falls after taking fewer than a dozen steps.

Chapter Sixteen

The Army of Oogaboo marches into Ruggedo's Kingdom under Queen Ann's command. Its officers fall into a hidden pit and are imprisoned with their Queen.
