obedience to the conductor in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Obedience to the conductor is the narrator’s golden rule of group travel: choose one competent decision-maker, require implicit obedience from the others, and let them coincide throughout the journey.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall / ] DAY THE FIRST I believe in holidays.

Obedience to the conductor is presented as the narrator's central rule for group travel. She tells her chickens that one person must decide while the others coincide, and that a competent conductor should be trusted absolutely.
