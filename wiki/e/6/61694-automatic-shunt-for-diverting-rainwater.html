Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

APPARATUS FOR OBTAINING PURE WATER FOR PHOTOGRAPHIC USE.

The automatic shunt prevents water from entering a cistern until the roof has been thoroughly washed. Its operation depends on a valve, lever, weight, and vessel with a small hole that restores the apparatus after rain ceases; careful adjustment of these parts is required.
