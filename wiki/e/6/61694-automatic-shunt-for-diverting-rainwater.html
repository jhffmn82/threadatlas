automatic shunt for diverting rainwater

object · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: forms of shunt, shunt, simple automatic shunt

Abstract written by a model

An automatic shunt for diverting rain-water prevents water from entering a cistern until roofs have been thoroughly washed, then provides clean, serviceable soft water. It uses valve V, lever L, weight W, and can C with a small hole; a movable strainer protects the cataract hole. Its operation requires adjustment of the weight and valve opening angle, and the can restores the apparatus after rain ceases.

APPARATUS FOR OBTAINING PURE WATER FOR PHOTOGRAPHIC USE.

The automatic shunt prevents water from entering a cistern until the roof has been thoroughly washed. Its operation depends on a valve, lever, weight, and vessel with a small hole that restores the apparatus after rain ceases; careful adjustment of these parts is required.
