Autoregressive Reasoning Chain Constructor in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Autoregressive Reasoning Chain Constructor first appears

Abstract written by a model

Autoregressive Reasoning Chain Constructor is a component of TRACE that builds novel, knowledge-grounded reasoning chains from a knowledge graph to support question answering. It unfolds autoregressively: at each step, it selects a logically connected knowledge triple based on the question and earlier triples, retrieves candidates with a bi-encoder triple ranker, and chooses a triple with an LLM-based selector. Beam search produces multiple chains, including top-5 chains, and chains may terminate when no further triples are needed. Demonstrations in the prompt guide construction; they are retrieved as the three most similar questions from manually labelled training questions. Its effectiveness is evaluated through TRACE-Doc and variants involving triple counts and demonstrations. A limitation is that the triple selector may be a black-box LLM.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The Autoregressive Reasoning Chain Constructor builds reasoning chains from the knowledge graph. These chains consist of logically connected knowledge triples that support question answering.

TRACE / Overall Framework .. Answer Generation

The reasoning chain constructor selects triples one at a time to build logically connected reasoning chains. It uses a bi-encoder triple ranker to retrieve candidate triples and an LLM-based triple selector to choose among them, applying beam search and an option to terminate a chain when no additional triples are needed.

Limitations

The reasoning chain constructor uses TRACE to construct reasoning chains. Its operation may be limited when the triple selector is a black-box LLM.

Baselines

The Reasoning Chain Constructor is evaluated using the top 10 or top 20 triples. Both variants perform below TRACE-Doc on the reported development-set results.

Additional Implementation Details

The reasoning chain constructor uses demonstrations retrieved from 20 manually labelled training questions from each dataset. E5-Mistral retrieves the top three most similar questions for this purpose.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

The reasoning chain constructor in TRACE uses demonstrations in the prompt to guide the construction of reasoning chains. Its variants are compared by removing demonstrations, fixing demonstrations across questions, and changing the number of demonstrations.
