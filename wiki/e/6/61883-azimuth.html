Also called: azimuth of Polaris, azimuth of the pole star

Abstract written by a model

Azimuth is an angle between an object's vertical plane and the meridian plane, measured on the horizon from south through west and north. For Polaris, it depends on latitude and changes slightly yearly as polar distance changes.

Azimuth is defined as the angle between an object's vertical plane and the plane of the meridian, measured from the south point through west, north, and back to south. The azimuth of Polaris depends on latitude and changes slightly by year as the star's polar distance changes.
