DAY THE SIXTH And a day absolutely divine!

DAY THE SIXTH is described as a day of perfect autumn weather, with brilliant green pasture, gleaming yellow cornfields, and clear skies. The narrator and companions spend it bathing, sketching, attending the rectory tea and evening church service, and walking beneath the moonlight.
