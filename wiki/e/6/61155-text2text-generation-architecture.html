Text2Text generation architecture

architecture · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Text2Text, Text2Text DG, Text2Text architecture, Text2Text model, Text2text, Text2text (RAP-BART), Text2text (RAP-T5), text2text DG model

Abstract written by a model

Text2Text generation architecture is a distractor generation architecture that frames distractor generation as a Text2Text task: it concatenates a question stem and answer and supplies them to a generative language model, including T5 or GPT. It is described as current state of the art. Evaluations with and without RAP and candidate augmentation show that RAP improves baselines on MCQ and SciQ and can outperform candidate augmentation alone.

Text2Text generation architecture is in one work on this site, Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Text2Text generation architecture formulates distractor generation as a Text2Text task by concatenating the question stem with the answer and providing them to a generative language model. Research based on this architecture represents the current state of the art in distractor generation.

Text2text models are evaluated with and without RAP and candidate augmentation. Text2text with RAP improves over the corresponding baseline on both MCQ and SciQ, while the results indicate that RAP can be more effective than candidate augmentation alone.
