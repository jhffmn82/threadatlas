Abstract written by a model

The Hank sizing and wringing machine, made by Heywood & Spencer, is suitable for fancy dyeing. It has two vats, gearing, and adjustable hooks. It alternately twists hanks, holds them for excess size to wipe off, reverses the twist, and removes them by hand or lifting lattice; capacity reaches 36 10 lb. bundles per hour.

HANK SIZING AND WRINGING MACHINE.

The hank sizing and wringing machine consists of two vats with driving gear between them and hooks whose spacing can be adjusted to the length or size of the hanks. It twists hanks, holds them briefly for excess size to be wiped off, reverses the twist, and removes them by hand or with a lifting lattice.
