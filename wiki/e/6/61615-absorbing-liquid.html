Abstract written by a model

An absorbing liquid for oxide of carbon, prepared from saturated copper chloride in hydrochloric acid with a small quantity of dissolved tin chloride and spirals of red copper. It is carefully kept from contact with air, used in gas analysis, and can be replaced or washed out before measurement.

BUNTE'S BURETTE FOR THE ANALYSIS OF FURNACE GASES. .. _Mode of Measuring the Gases and Absorption_.

The absorbing liquid for oxide of carbon is prepared from saturated copper chloride in hydrochloric acid with a small quantity of dissolved tin chloride and spirals of red copper. Caustic potassa or pyrogallate of potassa is used for other absorptions, and the liquids can be replaced or washed out before measurement.
