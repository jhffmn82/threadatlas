Abstract written by a model

The Long-Eared Hearer is a nome and Hearer with enormous, unusually long ears that can detect distant voices with exceptional precision. Serving Ruggedo, he reports adventurers, Shaggy Man, and Oogaboo's army, suffers Ruggedo's cruel ear-twisting, escapes, then later tracks returning invaders through the Hollow Tube. He cannot hear eggs, and Ruggedo's sceptre breaks the tip of one ear.

Long-Eared Hearer is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Nine

The Long-Eared Hearer listens for half an hour and reports that Shaggy Man seeks the Ugly One and that the Army of Oogaboo plans to raid the Nome Kingdom. Ruggedo cruelly pulls and twists his ears, but the Hearer escapes while Ruggedo chases Kaliko.

Chapter Fourteen

The Long-Eared Hearer reports that the invaders are returning through the Hollow Tube and estimates their distance by listening to their voices. He cannot hear eggs coming through the Tube. When Ruggedo throws his sceptre at Kaliko, it breaks off the tip of one of the Hearer's ears.
