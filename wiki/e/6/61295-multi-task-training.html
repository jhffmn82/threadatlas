Abstract written by a model

Multi-task training uses LoRA to train pre-trained LLMs, alternating Select and Copy, Correct and Complete, and Contextual Stimulation batches at 20%, 40%, and 40%, respectively.

Unsupervised Learning .. Training Strategy

Multi-task training mixes Select and Copy, Correct and Complete, and Contextual Stimulation and trains them alternately in batches. Select and Copy accounts for 20% of batches, while Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation each account for 40%.
