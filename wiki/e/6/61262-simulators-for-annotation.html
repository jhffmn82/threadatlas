2 sections; each heading opens the text

Ethical Considerations

The simulator was constructed from Europa Universalis IV and Victoria 3 after consideration of Paradox Interactive's end user license agreement. It was released under the MIT license.

Simulators for annotation

Simulators for annotation are developed for each scenario on DQA to calculate decision results deterministically. They take D with a decision as input, simulate the effects using business rules, and return an updated D.
