Abstract written by a model

Iodine solution, a favorite Texas remedy for rattlesnake bites, contains iodine in potassium iodide and, when tested on rattlesnake poison, immediately formed a dense light-brown noncrystalline precipitate.

RATTLESNAKE POISON.

Iodine solution was applied in reported cases of rattlesnake bites and was tested directly on rattlesnake poison. It immediately produced a dense light-brown precipitate with no crystalline structure visible under the microscope.
