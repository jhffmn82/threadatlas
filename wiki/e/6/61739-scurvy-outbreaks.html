THE FOOD AND ENERGY OF MAN.

Scurvy outbreaks had increased again, reaching ninety-nine in 1881, after a serious falling off since 1873. Mr. Thomas Gray attributed this possibly to neglect of varied food scales, and the lecture also suggests neglect of regulations concerning lime-juice.
