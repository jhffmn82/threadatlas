Orestes and Iphigenia recognize one another when Iphigenia dictates a message addressed to Orestes, and Pylades speaks his name. They confirm the recognition through memories of the Golden Lamb, Iphigenia's woven image of the sun, the water brought for Aulis, her hair, and the ancient lance hidden in her chamber.
