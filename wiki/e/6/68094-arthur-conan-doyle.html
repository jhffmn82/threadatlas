Arthur Conan Doyle in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10 · the work’s page · read it where Arthur Conan Doyle first appears

Abstract written by a model

Arthur Conan Doyle is the author of Sherlock Holmes. He first intended to end Holmes at the conclusion of The Memoirs, then revived him after public demand without regret. He says the lighter Holmes sketches coexisted with work in history, poetry, historical novels, psychic research, and drama.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

PREFACE

Arthur Conan Doyle explains that he created and wrote the Sherlock Holmes stories, initially intending to end Holmes after The Memoirs. He later responded to public demand by reviving Holmes and says that writing the lighter Holmes sketches did not prevent him from working in history, poetry, historical novels, psychic research, and drama.
