Orks in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The Orks are a race of flying creatures from Orkland, a flock usually staying near their own country, with strong claws and powerful revolving tails; they are kings of the air and rule all living things in Orkland. At least fifty arrive in Jinxland as the Scarecrow faces burning, scatter the bonfire, defeat King Krewl and his soldiers, and free the Scarecrow. They search Jinxland, find Blinkie beneath bushes, and bring her to him. They help conquer Jinxland, then mostly return home after Gloria's coronation, while four carry the travelers safely to the Land of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

The Orks arrive in a flock of at least half a hundred just as the Scarecrow is about to burn. Their revolving tails scatter the bonfire, knock down King Krewl and his soldiers, and rescue the Scarecrow. Their leader frees the Scarecrow, places Krewl's crown on his head, and declares that the Orks will see his orders obeyed.

Chapter Nineteen

The Orks accept the Scarecrow's request to find Blinkie and scatter across Jinxland searching for her. They discover her hiding beneath bushes, carry her through the air, and bring her before the Scarecrow.

Chapter Twenty

The Orks assist in the conquest of Jinxland, though most return to their own country after Gloria's coronation. Four Orks carry the Scarecrow, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright over the mountains and leave them safely in the Land of Oz.
