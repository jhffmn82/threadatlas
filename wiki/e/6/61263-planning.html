Abstract written by a model

Planning is PlanRAG’s central, traditionally human-handled step for determining the analyses needed for a decision. It takes a question, schema, and rules as input, generates an initial data-analysis plan, and guides retrieval. It unfolds by decomposing a decision question into steps such as identifying candidates, calculating potential profit changes, and selecting the best candidate; it is especially challenging for longer Building traversals.

Introduction

Step 1 planning consists of determining which kind of analysis is needed for a decision. The unit identifies it as the hardest step that humans have traditionally handled and as the central capability addressed by PlanRAG.

Methodology: PlanRAG

Planning takes the question, schema, and rules as input and generates an initial plan for data analysis. The plan describes the series of analyses needed for decision making and guides the retrieval step.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

Planning decomposes a decision question into steps such as identifying candidates, calculating potential profit changes, and selecting the best candidate. It helps PlanRAG-LM judge question difficulty and perform the required retrievals, although planning remains challenging for longer Building traversals.
