Abstract written by a model

The storm begins at night, obscuring the sea with torrential rain. On the fourth day, rain and wind turn sea and sky into indistinguishable blackness, hiding moon and stars, and continue until dawn. On the fifth day it gradually abates; by sunset wind and rain stop, though flooded lanes and an angry sea remain.

DAY THE FOURTH Sunday, September 4th--and we had started on September 1st; was it possible we had only been travelling four days?

The storm changes the sea and sky into an indistinguishable blackness and extinguishes the visible moon and stars. Its rain and wind continue until dawn, leaving the narrator convinced that the pleasant weather has ended.

DAY THE FIFTH "Hope for the best, and be prepared for the worst," had been the motto of our journey.

The storm brings intense rain and shifting winds, but gradually abates. By sunset the wind has stopped and the rain has ceased, though the lanes remain flooded and the sea still looks angry.
