Chapter Two

The boat carrying King Rinkitink approaches Pingaree under a canopy of purple silk embroidered with gold. It contains twenty rowers, Rinkitink, Bilbil in a cage, and boxes of sandalwood, and it has carried Rinkitink for four days.
