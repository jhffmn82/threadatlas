the murder plot against Sir Henry Baskerville in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

The murder plot against Sir Henry Baskerville is a refined, cold-blooded, deliberate scheme targeting him through the hound. Stapleton first considered killing Sir Henry in London, then waited in Devonshire; Holmes feared action before evidence was complete and used Sir Henry as bait to catch Stapleton.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12.

The murder plot is described by Sherlock Holmes as refined, cold-blooded, and deliberate. Holmes believes it is directed against Sir Henry Baskerville and fears that Jack Stapleton may act before the evidence is complete.

Chapter 15.

Stapleton first considered killing Sir Henry in London, but ultimately returned to Devonshire and waited for him. Holmes and his allies used Sir Henry as bait, allowing them to catch Stapleton in the course of carrying out the plot.
