Economical Steam Power is a paper presented by William Barnet Le Van, arguing that economical steam power depends on judiciously arranging equipment for each plant’s conditions, schedule, costs, and power needs.

ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

Economical steam power requires a judicious arrangement of engines, boilers, and transmission accessories. William Barnet Le Van emphasizes that the most economical choice depends on the plant's circumstances, operating schedule, fuel and water costs, and required power.
