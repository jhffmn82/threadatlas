Coombe Tracey in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Coombe Tracey is a destination and the home of Mrs. Laura Lyons. A letter signed L. L. is sent from there, and a hidden stranger may obtain supplies there. Dr. John Watson goes there to interview Lyons, whose centrally located, well-appointed rooms make her a possible source of assistance in the case; his trip is later noted in a written report.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10.

Coombe Tracey is the place from which the letter signed with the initials L. L. was sent. It is also where Laura Lyons lives and where the hidden stranger may obtain supplies.

Chapter 11.

Coombe Tracey is where Mrs. Laura Lyons lives and where the narrator goes to question her. The narrator finds her rooms centrally located and well appointed.

And that was a most vague direction. I realised it as I drove

Coombe Tracey is named in the written report found in the hut: “Dr. Watson has gone to Coombe Tracey.”

Chapter 12.

Coombe Tracey is where Dr. John Watson goes to interview Mrs. Laura Lyons. Sherlock Holmes says that she is the one person there who might assist them in the case.
