INTRODUCTORY NOTE

Pity is described as a rebel passion that opposes the strong, organized social force, conventional sanctions, and accepted gods. In the Troaedes, it appears as the crying of one of the world’s great wrongs made beautiful through music, and as a moving principle that can bring not peace but a sword.
