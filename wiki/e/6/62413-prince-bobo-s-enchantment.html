Prince Bobo's enchantment in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Prince Bobo's enchantment transforms Prince Bobo of Boboland into Bilbil the goat and leaves him quarrelsome and surly. Created by a clever, evil magician, it is among the most powerful spells; after the magician dies, its anti-charm is lost, though Glinda may break it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The enchantment on Bilbil is described as one of the most powerful ever accomplished. Its magician is dead and the secret of the anti-charm is lost, though the Wizard thinks Glinda may be able to break it.

Chapter Twenty Two

Prince Bobo's enchantment had changed him into Bilbil the goat and caused him to display a quarrelsome, surly nature. Glinda believes the spell was unusually difficult because it had been created by a clever and evil magician.
