IterRAG-LM in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

IterRAG-LM is an iterative RAG-based language-model decision maker based on Iterative RAG. It uses a ReAct-style Thought-Action-Observation prompt and is implemented with GPT-4 at zero temperature using LangChain; results are also reported for GPT-3.5, Llama 2 (70B and 13B), and Phi-2. It reaches 48.5% accuracy in Locating and 37.6% in Building, with missed-data-analysis rates of 3.3% and 33.2%, respectively, and higher such rates than PlanRAG-LM. Its reported prompt-structure accuracy is 37.5% for Locating and 30% for Building. GPT-3.5 outperforms PlanRAG-LM, while Llama 2 and Phi-2 cannot solve DQA and score 0% in listed settings.

IterRAG-LM is an iterative RAG-based decision maker whose prompt follows a ReAct-style Thought-Action-Observation process. Its reported prompt-structure accuracy was 37.5% for Locating and 30% for Building.

Experimenting with other models

IterRAG-LM is evaluated with GPT-3.5, Llama 2 (70B), Llama 2 (13B), and Phi-2. With GPT-3.5, it performs better than PlanRAG-LM, achieving 8.0% on Locating RDB, 2.5% on Locating GDB, 22.8% on Building RDB, and 3.96% on Building GDB; the Llama 2 and Phi-2 versions score 0% in all listed settings.
