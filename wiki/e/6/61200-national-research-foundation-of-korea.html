In "PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers," it supported the work through a Korea government (MSIT)-funded grant, numbered 2018R1A5A1060031 and RS-2023-00281635. In "RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," it supported the work in part through grants RS-2023-00280883 and RS-2023-00222663. In "Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation," it supported the research through grants NRF2023R1A2C3004176 and RS-2023-00262002. In "VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus," it received Korea government funding and supported the work through grant RS-2023-00256259.
