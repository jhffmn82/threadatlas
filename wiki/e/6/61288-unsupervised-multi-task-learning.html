2 sections; each heading opens the text

Unsupervised Learning .. Training Strategy

Unsupervised learning in INFO-RAG constructs training data from English Wikipedia without described manual labels. The data consists of sentence prefixes, target continuations, and retrieved-text contexts generated for three scenarios.

Experiments / Datasets and Evaluation Metrics .. Enhancing Previous SOTA in Open-Retrieval

Unsupervised INFO training is presented as improving the retrieval-augmented generation performance of LLaMA models across tasks. Its unsupervised and prefix language-modeling training paradigms are described as supporting generalization across natural-language and programming-language tasks.
