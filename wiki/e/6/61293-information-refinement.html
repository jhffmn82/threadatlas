Abstract written by a model

Information refinement is a RAG task trained through next-token prediction conditioned on a sentence prefix and retrieved texts. It aims to accurately extract correct information while avoiding interference from incorrect information. Its tasks select concise knowledge, correct or complete noisy knowledge, or use relevant context to stimulate generation; Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation help models learn this paradigm and improve correct-answer extraction.
