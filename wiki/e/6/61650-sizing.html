Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HANK SIZING AND WRINGING MACHINE.

Sizing uses a customary double operation consisting of running followed by finishing. The machine can perform running on one side while finishing occurs on the other, and it can also run on both sides if required.
