RATTLESNAKE POISON.

Henry H. Croft reports experiments and observations on rattlesnake poison in Texas. He tested poison from a rattlesnake with iodine solution, considered possible chemical explanations for remedies, and questioned the therapeutic value of potassium permanganate after absorption.
