the big pit in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The big pit is a covered trap and prison inside the rocky passage to the jeweled throne-room. It is opened to capture Queen Ann and her officers, then covered by the nomes; later, its supposed prisoners have vanished although the cover remains.

Chapter Sixteen

The pit lies just inside the rocky passage to the jeweled throne-room and is opened as a trap. Queen Ann and the officers fall into it, and the nomes replace its cover to hold them prisoner.

Chapter Seventeen

The pit is believed to hold Queen Ann and the Oogaboo people, but when Pang goes to fetch them, he finds the prisoners gone even though the cover remains in place.
