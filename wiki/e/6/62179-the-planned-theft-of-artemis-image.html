the planned theft of Artemis' image

Also called: bear both on the leaping seas, quest, seize, steal, the planned theft of the image, thieve that wondrous Image from its shrine

Abstract written by a model

The planned theft of Artemis' image is Apollo's prescribed means to release Orestes from suffering. Orestes and Pylades intend to wait for night and secretly take it, fearing death if seen, but are captured. Iphigenia instead plans and carries out its removal under cover of a purification rite.

Iphigenia in Tauris

Orestes and Pylades plan to wait until night and steal Artemis' image from the shrine. They consider entering secretly because being seen near the temple would mean death, but they are captured before carrying out the plan.

LEADER.

The planned theft of the image is Apollo's prescribed means of freeing Orestes. Iphigenia carries out the deception by removing the image herself, since no man may touch it, and presenting its removal as part of a sacred purification.
