Kaliko in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Kaliko is the Nome King who rules the nomes from an underground realm, commanding a large royal army, magic, an ivory throne, a crown, and a gold whistle. He holds King Kitticut and Queen Garee captive for King Gos, rejects Inga’s plea to free them, and deceitfully imprisons visitors while posing as a host. He tries many magical attacks on Rinkitink and Bilbil, but their protection defeats him, and he worries about Inga’s fate in the Three Trick Caverns. Though powerful, Kaliko is strongly frightened by eggs; Dorothy’s threat makes him release the captives. He remains behind when the others return to the Land of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

King Kaliko holds King Kitticut and Queen Garee captive and refuses Inga's request to release them because he has promised them to King Gos. He displays part of his large army, warns Inga that the nomes are powerful, and then secretly separates and imprisons the visitors while pretending to treat them as guests.

Chapter Nineteen

Kaliko repeatedly tests magical methods against Rinkitink and Bilbil, but their protection defeats every attempt. He admits that Rinkitink's magic can protect him from all of Kaliko's arts, then reveals his concern about whether Inga has survived the Three Trick Caverns.

Chapter Twenty-One

Kaliko refuses at first to release King Kitticut and Queen Garee because he promised King Gos to keep them captive. When Dorothy threatens him with eggs, he orders Klik to bring them out, and he later remains behind after the group returns to the Land of Oz.

Kaliko in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Kaliko is the good, metal Gnome King who rules all the gnomes, values jewels, and possesses magic exspectacles. Bored, he uses them to watch Betsy Bobbin and her companions, welcomes them to his underground castle, and hears of the Rash rubies. He secretly hides the second Rash ruby in his throne and delegates an attack on the travelers to Guph.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

Kaliko, King of all the gnomes, is bored until he uses the exspectacles to observe Betsy Bobbin and her companions in the Lost Labyrinth. He invites them to lunch, welcomes them to his underground castle, and listens to their account of their adventures. When Betsy describes the Rash rubies, he secretly hides a ring in a crevice of his throne, causing Carter Green to suspect that he may be planning something.

CHAPTER 11

Kaliko had purchased the second Rash ruby from an Ev fisherman, set it in a ring, and hidden it in the rock throne after Betsy mentioned the rubies. He delegates the attack on the travelers to Guph and is not present when the attack begins.
