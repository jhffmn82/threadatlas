Loe Pool in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Loe Pool is a small lake near Helstone, separated from the sea by a sandbar. Supposedly formed by Tregeagle, it is periodically ceremonially cut through by the Mayor of Helstone so fresh and salt waters meet; travelers discussed but did not visit it.

Loe Pool is described as a small lake near Helstone, separated from the sea by a sandbar. It is said to have been formed by the Cornwall man-demon Tregeagle, and the Mayor of Helstone ceremonially cuts through the bar so that the fresh and salt waters meet. The travelers did not visit it.
