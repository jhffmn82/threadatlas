Selden's prison escape in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Selden’s prison escape began at Princetown, leaving him at large for three days as warders watched roads and stations and farmers feared him. Seeking help from the Barrymores, he reached Baskerville Hall, then hid on the moor until the pursuit passed.
