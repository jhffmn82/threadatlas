Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MACHINE FOR COMPRESSING COAL REFUSE INTO FUEL.

The coal mixture becomes a plastic paste in a pug-mill when superheated steam is admitted. The paste is then led into an agitator to remove steam and distribute the material into the moulds of the compressing machine.
