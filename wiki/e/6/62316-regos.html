Regos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Regos is a city, capital of Regos, built on green meadows near the ocean and containing the palace of King Gos. Initially ruled and defended by Gos and his warriors behind heavily barred iron gates, it falls when Inga breaks the gates and the warriors flee. Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil conquer it; Inga rules its people kindly. After Queen Cor captures Inga, Gos returns with his warriors, restores the bridge of boats, and holds a carnival. Later, Inga meets Captain Buzzub there after liberating slaves and learns that Gos and Cor have abandoned the city with King Kitticut and the Queen of Pingaree.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The City of Regos is built on green meadows near the ocean and contains King Gos's palace. Its warriors defend it with weapons from the walls, but they flee when Inga breaks open its gates, leaving the city to the conquerors.

Chapter Eight

Regos is conquered by Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil after King Gos's warriors are defeated and the bridge of boats is destroyed. Inga establishes himself in the city and begins ruling its people kindly.

Chapter Ten

Regos is conquered by Prince Inga, causing King Gos and his warriors to flee. After Queen Cor captures Inga, Gos and his men restore the bridge of boats, return to Regos, and hold a carnival there.

Chapter Eleven

Regos is the city near Nikobob’s forest home and is ruled by King Gos. Gos and his warriors return to the city, but Zella avoids entering it because she fears being stopped by the warriors.

Chapter Fourteen

The City of Regos is where Inga meets Captain Buzzub after liberating the slaves. Buzzub tells him that King Gos and Queen Cor have abandoned the city and taken King Kitticut and the Queen of Pingaree with them.
