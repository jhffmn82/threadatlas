Noise Filtering in An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Kun Zhu, Xiaocheng Feng, Xiyuan Du, Yuxuan Gu, Weijiang Yu, Haotian Wang, Qianglong Chen, Zheng Chu, Jingchang Chen, Bing Qin · 2024

Noise Filtering is a filtering module for retrieval-augmented generation that compresses retrieved passages into a subset before generation, removing irrelevant, redundant, or misleading evidence. Its central aim is to preserve the precise intersection of retrieved passages and outputs while compressing irrelevant evidence to empty, balancing conciseness and correctness through an Information Bottleneck objective rather than log-likelihood-based filtering. The approach develops through SFT and DPO variants, trained with LLAMA2-7B using LORA, and experiments report fewer evidence words, reduced retrieval-induced mistakes, and comparable useful information. Remaining limitations include unsupported answering, inability to ensure generation-supporting results, and limited adaptation to compression extent.

document filtering in MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chia-Yuan Chang, Zhimeng Jiang, Vineeth Rakesh, Menghai Pan, Chin-Chia Michael Yeh, Guanchu Wang, Mingzhi Hu, Zhichao Xu, Yan Zheng, Mahashweta Das, Na Zou · 2025

Document filtering removes noisy retrieved documents after retrieval by ranking them for relevance and retaining those meeting an adaptive judge bar. In MAIN-RAG, multiple LLM agents perform it collaboratively; future improvements may use human feedback or tuning-based approaches.
