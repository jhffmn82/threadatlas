grapes in Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882

Various · 1881

are_grown_in outdoor grape cultivation [in limited varieties] The grapes discussed are difficult to ripen outdoors in the comparatively sunless and moist climate described. With careful training, stopping of laterals, mildew control, thinning, and exposure to sun and air, good edible grapes might often be produced. Glass-fronted bags are expected to improve their quality, maturity, and protection from rain and falling.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IMPROVED GRAPE BAGS.

The grapes discussed are difficult to ripen outdoors in the comparatively sunless and moist climate described. With careful training, stopping of laterals, mildew control, thinning, and exposure to sun and air, good edible grapes might often be produced. Glass-fronted bags are expected to improve their quality, maturity, and protection from rain and falling.
