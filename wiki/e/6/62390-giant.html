giant in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

An enormous unclothed, red-haired man with flaming eyes and crushing jaws sits in the third cave, guarding it against Inga's return. It challenges Inga to wrestle; Inga strikes its forehead with an iron post, and it falls and lies still.
