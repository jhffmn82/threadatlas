Jinxland

Abstract written by a model

Jinxland is a small, beautiful but hidden country in the Quadling Country, part of the Land of Oz and within Ozma's dominions. Forests, hills, fields, fountains, rivers, lakes, houses, castles, and palaces fill it, including the King's castle and the throne of Jinxland. Remote from the center of Oz, it is divided from the rest of the Quadling Country and the Land of Oz by high or steep mountains, a deep or bottomless gulf, and, by one account, a deadly desert. Although listed on the Map of Oz, it is cut off enough that Oz's laws are poorly obeyed. At first Jinxland is an unfortunate, fearful kingdom under King Krewl, a cruel, wicked, and illegitimate ruler who has usurped the throne. With his permission, several witches live there, and the people fear both him and the Wicked Witches. Pon claims the throne as son of the former King Phearse, while Princess Gloria is said to be the rightful Queen. Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright arrive as the first known strangers to enter Jinxland. They and their companions have adventures there, while Trot and Pon search its forests, fields, and villages for Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright. The Scarecrow comes from the Quadling Country to protect the strangers and, recognizing Krewl as a usurper, joins a plan to conquer the kingdom and restore Gloria. The Orks' intervention causes Krewl to lose control. The Scarecrow is briefly received as king, and the people learn of the wrongs of Blinkie, Krewl, and Googly-Goo. Finally Gloria becomes Queen, Pon becomes King and Royal Consort, and Jinxland celebrates with feasting, dancing, and the "Ork Trot."

Jinxland is in one work on this site, The Scarecrow of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

12 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Jinxland is a beautiful country with forests, hills, fields, fountains, rivers, lakes, houses, castles, and palaces. It lies in the Quadling Country of the Land of Oz but is separated from the rest of the Quadling Country by impassable mountains and is ruled by its own King.

Chapter Ten

Jinxland is ruled by King Krewl, although Pon claims he is the rightful prince because he is the son of the former King Phearse. Princess Gloria would be Queen of Jinxland if she had her rights, according to Trot.

Chapter Eleven

Jinxland is described as a hidden country reached by crossing a deadly desert. Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright arrive there as the first strangers known to have entered the country.

Chapter Twelve

Jinxland lies far from the center of Oz and is cut off by steep mountains and a bottomless gulf. Oz's laws are not well obeyed there, and several witches live in the country with King Krewl's permission.

Chapter Thirteen

Jinxland is a small region near the Quadling Country, separated from it by high mountains and a deep gulf. It is ruled by King Krewl, and its people live in fear of him and several Wicked Witches.

Chapter Fifteen

Jinxland is the country where Trot and Pon search through forests, fields, and villages for Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright. The Scarecrow comes there from the Quadling Country because the strangers are in danger from the cruel King and the Wicked Witch.

Chapter Sixteen

Jinxland is ruled by King Krewl, whom the Scarecrow identifies as a usurper. The group plans to conquer the Kingdom and place Princess Gloria on its throne.

Chapter Seventeen

Jinxland is the land where King Krewl rules and where Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, Gloria, and the others have been having their adventures.

Chapter Eighteen

Jinxland is identified as a part of Oz and therefore subject to the allegiance owed to Ozma of Oz. The Scarecrow declares that Princess Gloria should rule it, and the Orks' intervention causes King Krewl to lose control of the country.

Chapter Nineteen

Jinxland receives the Scarecrow as its new king after King Krewl's conquest. Its people become indignant when they learn about the wrongs committed by Blinkie, Krewl, and Googly-Goo.

Chapter Twenty

Jinxland receives Gloria as its new Queen and Pon as its King and Royal Consort. The kingdom celebrates with feasting and a dance featuring a piece called the "Ork Trot."

Chapter Twenty-One

Jinxland is an unfortunate country ruled by a wicked King and is part of Ozma's dominions. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright arrive there, and the Scarecrow enters it to protect them.
