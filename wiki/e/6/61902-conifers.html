THE MAIDENHAIR TREE.

Conifers include the Maidenhair tree, which is closely allied to the Yew despite being destitute of resin. Their affinities with higher Cryptogams are illustrated by features such as the corpuscles in their seeds and the Maidenhair tree's peculiar venation.
