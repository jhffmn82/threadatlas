Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

A rationale records the generator's evaluation of context passages and supports the final answer. RAG-Studio obtains ground-truth rationales using the gold answer, filters contradictory rationales with heuristic rules, and uses the resulting signals for generator and retriever training.
