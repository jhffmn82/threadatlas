2.3 / CoV-RAG Training

The verification criteria evaluate whether references relate to the question, whether the answer is correct, whether citations are accurate, and whether the answer is truthful, unbiased, and concise. They also determine a judgment and whether a question should be revised for easier retrieval and answering.
