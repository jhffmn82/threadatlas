retrieval information

Abstract written by a model

Retrieval information comes from retrievers, is produced by R2-Former from list-wise features, projected via an MLP into the LLM embedding space, and prepended to each document to guide attention.

R2AG / Problem Formulation and Overview .. Training Strategy

Retrieval information is produced by the R2-Former from the list-wise retrieval features and then projected into the LLM’s token-embedding space. The information is inserted before its corresponding document and is intended to guide generation without substantial information loss or added complexity.
