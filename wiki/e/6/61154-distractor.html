Abstract written by a model

Distractors are incorrect, non-answer options in multiple-choice or cloze questions. Designing them is described as the most challenging part of creating MCQs. They are generation targets in Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Augmented Generation, and are assessed for relevance, ability to divert from correct answers, and overall utility.
