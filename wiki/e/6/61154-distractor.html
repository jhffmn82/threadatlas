Introduction

Distractors are the incorrect options in multiple-choice questions, and designing appropriate distractors is described as the most challenging part of creating MCQs. The proposed methods aim to improve distractor generation.

A distractor is a non-answer option used as a generation target during Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Augmented Generation. Ground-truth distractors can also be used to retrieve text for pseudo-question construction and to label relevant knowledge triplets.
