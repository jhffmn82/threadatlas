Abstract written by a model

Scenario 1 is a setting where retrieved texts already contain all knowledge needed for a question and correct answers are selected using cover EM. It requires large language models to extract correct knowledge, remove irrelevant information, and produce direct, concise responses; INFO-RAG shows an advantage.

Scenario 1 is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
