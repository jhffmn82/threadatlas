King Krewl in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

King Krewl is the cruel, wicked, and unlawful king of Jinxland, a usurper who had formerly been Prime Minister under King Phearse. After trapping King Phearse beneath stones in a deep pond, he seized the royal succession and ruled from King Krewl's castle, wearing a jeweled crown and wielding a golden staff or sceptre. He loves wealth above everything, favors the witches of Jinxland despite the laws of Oz, and uses witchcraft for cruelty and revenge. His severe punishment of treason and fierce manner keep the people in constant fear; when away from his castle, he hunts with fierce courtiers and war captains. As Princess Gloria's cruel uncle and guardian, Krewl controls her life. He commands that she marry Googly-Goo, opposes her love for Pon, assaults Pon, confines Gloria, and has guards beat Pon. With Googly-Goo, he arranges for Blinkie to freeze Gloria's heart, paying the witch with money and jewels while threatening to burn her at the stake if she fails. He also distrusts Cap'n Bill, summons the Wicked Witch to examine him, and bars Trot and Button-Bright from the castle. When Pon calls on him to surrender to the Scarecrow, Krewl refuses and beats Pon. He later refuses the Scarecrow as well, strikes him, and orders him burned. The Orks scatter the bonfire, overthrow Krewl, and have soldiers bind and drag him before the Scarecrow. After his conquest, he is brought before the people in chains and hissed at. Gloria and Pon offer him work as a gardener's boy if he reforms and takes the name Grewl; he becomes gardener's boy under that name after promising to reform.

Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

King Krewl rules Jinxland and punishes his subjects severely for treason. The woman is afraid to discuss him and says that people are safer when they know less about his actions; he hunts with fierce courtiers and war captains when he is away from his castle.

Chapter Ten

King Krewl is Princess Gloria's guardian and has commanded her to marry Googly-Goo. He struck and kicked Pon, dragged Gloria into the castle, and later held her back while his guards beat Pon. Pon also says that Krewl trapped King Phearse beneath stones in a deep pond and took the throne of Jinxland.

Chapter Eleven

King Krewl receives Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright as strangers and becomes wary of Cap'n Bill's apparent magical powers. He discusses destroying Gloria's love for Pon, locks Gloria in a room after finding her with Pon, and summons the Wicked Witch to examine Cap'n Bill.

Chapter Twelve

King Krewl confines Gloria, opposes her love for Pon, and agrees with Googly-Goo to have Blinkie freeze her heart. He helps Blinkie reach the sleeping Cap'n Bill, then orders Trot and Button-Bright barred from his castle.

Chapter Thirteen

King Krewl rules Jinxland despite having no right to the title. He is described as wicked and fierce, and the people of Jinxland live in constant fear of him.

Chapter Fourteen

King Krewl escorts Princess Gloria to Blinkie's house and pays Blinkie with money and jewels to freeze Gloria's heart. He threatens to burn Blinkie at the stake if the witch fails, then leaves before the ceremony is completed.

Chapter Sixteen

King Krewl is described as a usurper who has no right to rule Jinxland. When Pon summons him to surrender to the Scarecrow, Krewl refuses and beats Pon with his golden staff.

Chapter Seventeen

King Krewl is described as a cruel ruler of Jinxland. The Ork wants to meet and severely punish him and his Wicked Witch.

Chapter Eighteen

King Krewl refuses to surrender when confronted by the Scarecrow and strikes him with his golden sceptre. He orders the Scarecrow burned, but the Orks scatter the bonfire and knock Krewl from his throne. The soldiers bind him and drag him before the Scarecrow's throne.

Chapter Twenty

King Krewl is brought before the people in chains after his conquest and is hissed at as he passes. Gloria and Pon offer him work as a gardener's boy if he reforms and changes his name to Grewl.
