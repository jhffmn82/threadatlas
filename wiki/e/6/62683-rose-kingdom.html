Abstract written by a model

Rose Kingdom is a flower-strewn kingdom devoted to cultivating the rarest and fairest Roses, admitting only Roses and barring strangers. Once ruled by Ozga, it expelled her and others; after its last ruler withered early, the Royal Gardener governs temporarily. Its rulers grow on Royal Bushes, and its subjects seek a male Rose King. A deep gulf and drawbridge partly separate it.

Rose Kingdom is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Five

The Rose Kingdom is devoted to cultivating the rarest and fairest Roses. Only Roses are allowed there, according to the Roses and the Royal Gardener.

Chapter Six

The Rose Kingdom is a flower-strewn territory surrounded in part by a deep gulf and governed temporarily by the Royal Gardener. Its rulers grow on Royal Bushes, and the kingdom's subjects want a male rose to become King. Shaggy Man, Betsy, Hank, and Princess Ozga are expelled from it.
