Abstract written by a model

The forest lies between Regos and the mountains, containing mines and Nikobob's cabin. Dark, tangled, and dangerous, it has hollow honey trees and is Choggenmugger's haunt. Zella and her mother search there for honey; later Zella clears its obstructing trees, vines, and logs with newfound strength.

Chapter Nine

The forest between the city and Nikobob's home is dark, tangled, and filled with dangerous creatures. It contains hollow trees where Zella and her mother search for honey and is the favorite haunt of Choggenmugger.

Chapter Eleven

The forest lies between the mountains and the City of Regos and contains Nikobob’s cabin. Its tangled trees, vines, and fallen logs obstruct Zella, but she clears them with her newfound strength.
