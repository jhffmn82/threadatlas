Blue Pearl in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The Blue Pearl is a blue magic pearl that grants extraordinary strength to its bearer or wearer. In the past, it enabled King Kitticut’s father and the people of Pingaree to drive away invaders during the Great Fight. Inga later places it in the pointed toe of his right shoe before rowing to Regos and Coregos. Its magic makes his oars light, lets him break the staples holding city-gate bars, and gives him marvelous strength to break free, wield a heavy iron post, and leap across a gulf.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The blue magic pearl gives its bearer strength so great that no power can resist him. It enabled King Kitticut's father and the people of Pingaree to drive away the invaders during the Great Fight.

Chapter Five

Inga places the Blue Pearl in the pointed toe of his right shoe and secures it there. It promises to give him the strength needed to row a boat to Regos and Coregos.

Chapter Six

The Blue Pearl gives Inga unusual strength, allowing him to row the large boat effortlessly. Inga identifies its magic as the reason the oars become light as feathers in his hands.

Chapter Seven

The Blue Pearl is said to give Inga the power to accomplish any feat of strength. He uses that strength to burst apart the heavy staples holding the bars of the city gates.
