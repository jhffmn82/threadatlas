Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE CHINESE SIGN MANUAL.

W. J. Herschel solicits information about skin-furrows on the hand. After twenty years in charge of registration offices in India, he employed finger marks as sign manuals to prevent personation and repudiation.
