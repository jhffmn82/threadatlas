Abstract written by a model

Goonhilly Down, an old Cornish-named hunting ground, is a broad, flat, bare, treeless district. Believed once to have been an enormous forest full of wild beasts, it contains serpentine blocks through the turf. Travelers cross it, gather Cornish heath, and encounter associations with St. Rumon and Goonhillies.

Goonhilly Down is a broad, flat, bare, treeless district that was believed once to have been a forest and hunting ground. The travelers wander there, gather Cornish heath, and learn about its older associations with St. Rumon and the breed of ponies called Goonhillies.
