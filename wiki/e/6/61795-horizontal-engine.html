Abstract written by a model

The horizontal engine is described as the best form for driving machinery, especially at large powers. Properly constructed, it is more durable and cheaper than a beam engine, needs less engine-house and foundation outlay, runs at higher speed, and is preferred overall to a vertical engine.

ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

The horizontal engine is described as the best form in most cases where machinery must be driven, particularly for large powers. It is said to be more durable and less expensive than a beam engine, capable of higher speed, and less demanding in engine-house and foundation costs.
