The Victory of Samothrace, a work attributed to an unknown artist probably following Scopas, stands winged on a ship’s prow; wind-blown draperies create vigorous, stately movement and conquering strength.

The Victory of Samothrace is attributed to an unknown artist, probably a follower of Scopas. The winged goddess stands on the prow of a ship, her draperies blown by the wind, and conveys vigorous, stately movement and conquering strength.
