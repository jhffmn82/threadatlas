Hugo Baskerville in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Hugo Baskerville, a direct-line ancestor who held Baskerville Hall during the Great Rebellion, was a wild, profane, godless, cruel man and father of Rodger, John, and Elizabeth. Family tradition says he began the Hound story after abducting a yeoman’s daughter, pursuing her, and dying beside her beneath a huge black hound.

Chapter 2.

Hugo Baskerville is described in the manuscript as a wild, profane, godless, cruel man who abducted a yeoman's daughter. He pursued her across the moor with hounds and was found dead beside her, with a huge black hound tearing at his throat.
