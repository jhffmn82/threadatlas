the trial case against Stapleton in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

The trial case against Stapleton is not yet established: Holmes says it rests only on surmise and conjecture, lacks motive, and has no direct evidence linking Stapleton to the hound or deaths; further investigation is needed.
