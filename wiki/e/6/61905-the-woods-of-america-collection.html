The Woods of America collection

collection · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: A collection of woods, THE WOODS OF AMERICA, the collection, the exhibition, the novel collection, the only complete one of its kind in the country, this collection of logs

Abstract written by a model

The Woods of America is a collection of American woods prepared by the American Museum of Natural History for exhibition. It contains 394 logs representing all but 26 varieties of trees indigenous to the United States, with accompanying foliage, fruits, flowers, commercial products, water-color drawings, scientific diagrams, and a herbarium. Prepared on the third floor of the Arsenal in Central Park, it is expected to be the country’s only complete collection of its kind, with no comparable royal-museum collection known in England, France, or Germany. It supports education, landscape gardening, and forest-tree conservation.

] THE WOODS OF AMERICA.

The collection is being prepared for exhibition and includes 394 logs representing all but 26 of the tree varieties indigenous to the United States. It will also include foliage, fruits, flowers, commercial products, water-color drawings, and scientific diagrams, and is described as the largest and only complete collection of its kind in the country.
