Abstract written by a model

Sennen is a pretty village with cottages, lodgings, a new schoolhouse, and the ancient parish church St. Sennen's. It is linked to a long-deceased curate whose son the traveling party meets at Land's End. After seeing lights on St. Sennen's in darkness, the party passes through the village in heavy rain; the narrator then encounters local residents and schoolchildren, highlighting schooling amid illiteracy.

Sennen is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sennen is associated with its church, St. Sennen's, and with a long-deceased curate known to the traveling party's acquaintances. The party meets the curate's son at Land's End, and later passes through Sennen village during a heavy downpour.

DAY THE TWELFTH Monday morning.

Sennen is described as a pretty village with an ancient church, a new schoolhouse, cottages, and lodgings to let. The narrator speaks with a coastguard and a fisher wife, intervenes in a quarrel between schoolchildren, and learns that the local children receive substantial schooling even though the fisher wife cannot read or write.
