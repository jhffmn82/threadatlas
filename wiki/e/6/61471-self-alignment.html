self-alignment

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Self-Alignment, Synthetic Data Curation with Self-Alignment, self-aligned training, synthetic data curation with self-alignment

Abstract written by a model

Self-alignment is a model-improvement approach that uses a model’s own generated outputs and feedback to improve performance and align responses with desired behaviors. It matters because it addresses data scarcity by eliminating human-labeled data and enabling autonomous synthesis of training ingredients. In RAG-Studio, the LLM generates raw samples, evaluates, refines, filters, and organizes them into contrastive training data and signals for unified retriever and LLM fine-tuning.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Self-alignment enables RAG-Studio to autonomously synthesize all training ingredients without external datasets or models. The LLM evaluates, refines, and filters its own outputs, while its feedback supplies training signals for the retriever.

Related Work / RAG Fine-tuning .. Self-Alignment

Self-alignment uses a model to improve its own performance and align its responses with desired behaviors. In this work, it supports fine-tuning a general retriever and LLM into a unified RAG system.

Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

Synthetic data curation with self-alignment is the approach RAG-Studio uses to create training data without human-labeled data. It generates, evaluates, filters, and organizes samples for generator and retriever fine-tuning.
