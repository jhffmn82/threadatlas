L. Frank Baum · 1915

Blinkie summons three witches, ugly hags who become beautiful maidens to help perform a ceremony, then resume ugly forms and fly after Pon and Trot.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

Three witches appear from flashes produced by Blinkie's kettle and temporarily transform themselves into beautiful maidens through witchcraft. They help perform the ceremony, then return to their ugly forms and pursue Pon and Trot by flying on canes or broomsticks.
