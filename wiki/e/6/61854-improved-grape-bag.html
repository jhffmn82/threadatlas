improved grape bag

object · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: IMPROVED GRAPE BAGS., bags, glass fronted bags, glass-fronted bags, grape bag, grape bags, new grape bag, these bags

Abstract written by a model

The improved grape bag, invented by M. Pelletier, is a grape bag fitted with a curved glass square on a light iron-wire support. Its glass-covered sun-facing portion increases light and warmth while an air layer prevents scorching, hastening grape maturity and providing up to 10 degrees more warmth than bags alone. It protects grapes from exposure and protects sulphur from driving rain, supporting mildew control. More effective than a plain grape bag, it suits wall-grown or screened grapes and is made in varied sizes for ripening pears and other choice fruits, while helping prevent ripe fruit from falling.

IMPROVED GRAPE BAGS.

The improved grape bag consists of a bag fitted with a curved glass square supported by light iron wire. The glass protects the fruit, increases light and warmth without scorching the grapes, helps prevent mildew from being washed away, and may keep ripe fruit from falling to the ground. It is suggested for grapes, pears, and other choice fruits.
