journey through the Tube in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The journey through the Tube lasts more than an hour, moving darkly and extremely fast through the earth. It begins as fearful and frightening, then becomes longer but more comfortable, letting travelers talk before they emerge into daylight at the mountain above the Nome King's caverns.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

The journey through the Tube is dark, extremely fast, and frightening, though the travelers gradually recover enough to converse. After more than an hour, they reach daylight on the opposite side of the world.

Chapter Fifteen

The journey through the Tube is longer than before but more comfortable, allowing the travelers to talk and become acquainted with Quox. They pass through the center of the earth and emerge into daylight at the mountain above the Nome King's caverns.
