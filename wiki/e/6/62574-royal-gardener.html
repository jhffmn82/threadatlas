Royal Gardener

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Gardener, Gardy, man

Abstract written by a model

Royal Gardener is a wonderful, royal man and gardener who tends the Roses of the Rose Kingdom while temporarily ruling and enforcing its Law until a royal bush produces a ripe ruler. He wears a rose-colored costume, carries a spading fork and watering pot, and has small twinkling eyes. In the greenhouse, Hank’s bray frightens him into dropping the watering pot over Hank’s head and hiding behind the Roses. He condemns the strangers, becomes affectionate toward Shaggy Man through the Love Magnet, explains the missing Ruler, then allows the group to cross the drawbridge and returns to the greenhouse.

Chapter Five

The Royal Gardener enters the greenhouse carrying a spading fork and a watering pot. Hank’s bray frightens him so badly that he throws the watering pot over Hank’s head, falls down, and hides behind the Roses before shouting that Betsy and Hank are breaking the law.

Chapter Six

The Royal Gardener declares that the strangers have broken the Law and must die, but the Love Magnet makes him affectionate toward Shaggy Man. He explains that the Rose Kingdom has no current Ruler because the previous one withered and no male rose is ripe enough to pick. After Betsy and Shaggy pick Princess Ozga, he tries to drive the group out with a garden fork and finally lets them cross the drawbridge before returning to the greenhouse.
