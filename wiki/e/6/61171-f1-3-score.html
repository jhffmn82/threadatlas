F1@3 score

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: F1 score, F1@3

Abstract written by a model

F1@3 score is an automatic metric for distractor generation, reported because test data contain three distractors. It is the harmonic mean of precision and recall at cutoff 3, where P@3 and R@3 are equal for label size three. Best-performing models reach 16.47 on MCQ and 16.50 on Sciq.

F1@3 score is in one work on this site, Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

The F1@3 score is used to report distractor generation performance. The best-performing model improves it from 14.80 to 16.47 on the MCQ dataset and from 15.92 to 16.50 on the Sciq dataset.
