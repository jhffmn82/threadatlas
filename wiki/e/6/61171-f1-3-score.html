Abstract

The F1@3 score is used to report distractor generation performance. The best-performing model improves it from 14.80 to 16.47 on the MCQ dataset and from 15.92 to 16.50 on the Sciq dataset.

F1 score is used as an automatic evaluation metric, with F1@3 reported because the test data contain three distractors. It is defined as the harmonic mean of precision and recall, and P@3 and R@3 are equal when the label size is three.
