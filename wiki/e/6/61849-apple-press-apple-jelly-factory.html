1 section; each heading opens the text

THE MANUFACTURE OF APPLE JELLY.

The apple press is made wholly of iron, and its motions, including the turning of the screws, are operated by water power. The apple cheese is formed in layers enclosed in strong cotton cloth, which replaces the straw formerly used and also strains the cider.
