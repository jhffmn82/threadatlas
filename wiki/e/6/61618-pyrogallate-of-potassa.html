BUNTE'S BURETTE FOR THE ANALYSIS OF FURNACE GASES. .. _Mode of Measuring the Gases and Absorption_.

Pyrogallate of potassa is used to absorb oxygen after carbonic acid has been removed with caustic potassa. It must be completely removed from the burette before the acid copper chloride solution is introduced.
