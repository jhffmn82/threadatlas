Coregos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Coregos is the smaller, fertile twin island north of Pingaree, close to Regos and ruled by Queen Cor. Its grain fields feed Regos's warriors, and its City of Coregos faces Regos. Its fierce people join Regos in invading Pingaree, carrying prisoners and plunder home so captives, including Inga's mother, can labor in the fields. Inga later sails toward Coregos and Regos, while King Gos and his fleeing warriors abandon Regos and settle on Coregos. Zella reaches it by a bridge of boats to deliver honey to Queen Cor. After Inga recovers his magic and frees the slaves, Queen Cor's rule is threatened with an end.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Coregos is one of the twin islands north of Pingaree and is inhabited by a fierce and warlike people. Its warriors once joined with those of Regos in invading Pingaree.

Chapter Three

Coregos is identified as one of the kingdoms from which the invading warriors come. The warriors carry Pingaree's prisoners and plunder back to Coregos, where the captives are to work in its grain fields.

Chapter Six

Coregos is paired with Regos as the destination of Inga's voyage. Its shores come into view with those of Regos on the morning of the fourth day.

Chapter Seven

The Island of Coregos lies very close to Regos and is smaller, rich, and covered with grain fields. Queen Cor rules it, and King Gos and his fleeing warriors reach it after abandoning Regos.

Chapter Eight

Coregos is the neighboring island where King Gos and his followers continue to live after fleeing Regos. Inga's mother is enslaved there, and Gos's followers fear Inga while plotting ways to overcome him.

Chapter Eleven

Coregos is reached by crossing the bridge of boats. Zella travels there to deliver honey to Queen Cor.

Chapter Thirteen

Coregos is the island ruled by Queen Cor. After Inga's recovery of his magic and the freeing of the slaves, Queen Cor's rule there is described as being in danger of ending.
