Abstract written by a model

An automatic cut-off apparatus for series-connected lamps bypasses a failed new arc electric lamp or broken carbon through equivalent resistance, keeping other lamps operating and allowing rapid relighting when contact returns.

ON A NEW ARC ELECTRIC LAMP. .. _Electrical Arrangement_.

The automatic cut-off is used when several lamps are connected in series. If a lamp fails or a carbon breaks, it connects the circuit through a resistance equivalent to the lamp, allowing the other lamps to continue operating and enabling rapid relighting when contact is restored.
