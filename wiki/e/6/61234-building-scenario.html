Building scenario

scenario · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: Building, scenario

Abstract written by a model

Building is a Decision QA benchmark scenario constructed from Europa Universalis IV and Victoria 3 as video-game scenarios. It uses relational and graph database representations and data on goods, buildings, supply, and demand. Each instance has a different GOODS value and asks which building ID to increase by five levels so that expanding factory production maximally decreases that good’s current market price. The scenario calculates current inputs, outputs, prices, and a TS value needed for the best decision; its goal is to minimize the current price of a given good. Problems with multiple answers are excluded. Building requires longer database traversals or more joins than Locating, making planning harder. In reported results, PlanRAG-LM achieved 45.0% accuracy and re-planned on 38% of questions, mainly by dividing into sub-steps; a prompt-structure comparison reported 50% accuracy. Table 11 reports GPT-3.5 results for IterRAG-LM and PlanRAG-LM on RDB and GDB. PlanRAG-based methods outperform the stated iterative RAG method by 7.4% in this scenario.

Building scenario is in one work on this site, PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

Building is one of the two scenarios in the Decision QA benchmark. The PlanRAG-based method outperforms the state-of-the-art iterative RAG method by 7.4% in this scenario.

Introduction

Building is one of the two DQA scenarios. It contains questions about how many resources, such as wood, should be supplied to a factory.

Decision QA task .. Statistics of DQA

The Building scenario uses data about goods, buildings, supply, and demand. Its goal is to minimize the current price of a given good by deciding which factory building to expand.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

The Building scenario requires longer database traversals or more joins than Locating and is therefore harder to plan. PlanRAG-LM reaches 45.0% accuracy, and it re-plans for 38% of questions, including substantially more cases with more than four re-plannings.

Appendix / Data collection

The Building scenario filters out problems that have multiple answers.

Data instances

In the Building scenario, every instance has a different GOODS value. The question asks which building ID should be increased by five levels to maximally decrease the market price of GOODS.

Building simulator algorithm / Business rules in DQA / Prompts for DQA / Previous RAG-based LMs / PlanRAG-based LM

The Building scenario uses goods, buildings, demand, and supply relationships to calculate current inputs, current outputs, and current prices. PlanRAG-LM achieved 50% accuracy in this scenario in the reported prompt-structure comparison.

Experimenting with other models

Building is one of the settings reported in Table 11. With GPT-3.5, IterRAG-LM scores 22.8% on RDB and 3.96% on GDB, while PlanRAG-LM scores 1.0% on both RDB and GDB.

Re-planning cases

The Building scenario primarily uses the Divide to sub-steps strategy during re-planning across most cases.
