DAY THE THIRTEENTH Into King Arthurs land--Tintagel his birth-place, and Camelford, where he fought his last battle--the legendary region of which one may believe as much or as little as one pleases--we were going to-day.

The sunset at Tintagel shows the black rock in the foreground, the sea covering vanished Lyonesse, and an amber sky marked by long bars. A low cloud shaped like a boat appears to carry three figures, which the travelers imagine as King Arthur and the three queens before joking that the dark shape at the bow is Arthur’s luggage.
