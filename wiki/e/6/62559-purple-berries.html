purple berries in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The purple berries are magic fruit with size-changing power and are the antidote to lavender berries. After lavender berries shrink people, purple berries restore their natural size: Pessim, the Ork, Cap'n Bill, and Trot use them this way. Cap'n Bill gathers six, protects some during flight, discards one badly jammed berry, and keeps three sound berries in a wooden box. In the end, he gives the three remaining berries to birds, which grow much larger.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

The deep purple berry is the antidote to the lavender berry. Pessim ate one after shrinking and returned to his former size, and the Ork also regained its natural size after eating one. Cap'n Bill gathers six deep purple berries to take along for restoring Trot and himself after their journey.

Chapter Six

The purple berries restore Cap'n Bill and Trot to their natural size when they eat them. Trot protects two berries during the flight, and Cap'n Bill places three sound berries in a wooden box after discarding one that was badly jammed.

Chapter Eight

Cap'n Bill still has three purple berries in his wooden box. He gives one to each of three birds, and the berries cause the birds to grow much larger.
