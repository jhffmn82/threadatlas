212 " SCHREIBER'S APPARATUS FOR REVIVIFYING BONE-BLACK. .. _The Animal Black Furnace_.

Schreiber's apparatus transfers used bone-black from wells to a rotary washer, then uses a steam filter, elevator, drier, furnace retorts, and cooling pipes to process it continuously. Its elevator and gearing operate automatically after the gearing lever is engaged.
