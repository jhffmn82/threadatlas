RDB in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

RDB is a database used for Decision QA in Building and Locating settings. It organizes scenario data into tables queried with SQL, supports substantially more rows per table than reviewed benchmarks, and frames the study's examination of relational-database Decision QA.
