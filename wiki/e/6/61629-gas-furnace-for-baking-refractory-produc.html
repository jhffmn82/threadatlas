gas furnace for baking refractory products

Also called: GAS FURNACE FOR BAKING REFRACTORY PRODUCTS, furnace, furnace of this kind, gas furnace

Abstract written by a model

The gas furnace for baking refractory products is set into the ground, with a one-and-a-half-meter-high baking chamber covered by removable refractory-clay slabs. After a compartment is emptied, a small car on a movable track transfers each slab to a newly filled compartment. Gas flows through channel G, pipe a, vertical conduits b, and tuyeres into the chamber.

The gas furnace for baking refractory products has a baking chamber only one and a half meters high and covered by removable refractory-clay slabs rather than a vault. A small car on a movable track removes each slab from an emptied compartment and places it over a newly filled compartment. Gas passes through channel G, pipe a, vertical conduits b, and tuyeres into the chamber.
