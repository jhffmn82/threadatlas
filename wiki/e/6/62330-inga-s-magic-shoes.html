Abstract written by a model

Inga's magic shoes are a pretty red-leather pair with pointed toes, high heels, and a distinctive Pingaree shape. Magic pearls concealed in their points give protection and strength while the shoes are worn or held in a coat. Rinkitink and an old woman discard the shoes separately, leaving Inga powerless. Nikobob finds them and gives them to Zella, who is protected from bees while wearing them. Zella later exchanges them with Inga, restoring his magic.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eight

Inga's magic shoes contain the pearls that provide his protection and strength. Rinkitink throws one away at a cat, and an old woman throws the other onto a dust-heap, leaving Inga without their magical powers.

Chapter Nine

Nikobob finds Inga's shoes separately near the palace and places them in his pocket, believing they are unwanted cast-offs. He gives them to Zella, who wears them while gathering honey and is protected from the bees.

Chapter Thirteen

The magical shoes belong to Inga and have a distinctive Pingaree shape, with high heels and pointed toes. Zella has been wearing them, but she exchanges them with Inga, allowing him to recover his magic.
