Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twelve

Bilbil charges King Gos at the top of the stairs and butts him down, causing the line of warriors behind him to fall in succession. He repeats the attack after King Gos returns, then lands on the heap of soldiers and kicks his way free.
