Abstract written by a model

Nikobob is a poor, humble, honest charcoal-burner who lives in a forest cabin with his wife and little daughter, Zella. He knows the forest well, carries torches and an ax, and does not hunt animals merely to hurt them. After finding magic shoes near the royal palace and bringing them home for Zella, he uses their hidden power to destroy Choggenmugger, believing the victory came from his own strength. He protects and guides Zella, though poverty leads him to permit her dangerous journey for Queen Cor’s payment. Offered rule of the Twin Islands and great wealth, he refuses both, fearing anxiety, violence, thieves, and marauders. Instead, he moves with his family to Pingaree, where he manages rebuilding and restores and directs the pearl fisheries. His judgment, industry, and honesty lead King Kitticut to make him Lord High Chamberlain, responsible for the fisheries and other business matters, while he lives in a cottage near the King’s palace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Nikobob, a poor charcoal-burner, finds a pair of shoes near the royal palace and brings them home for his daughter Zella. He destroys Choggenmugger with the help of the magic power in the shoes and believes his own strength accomplished the feat.

Chapter Eleven

Nikobob warns Zella to avoid the path used by King Gos’s warriors and says that wild beasts are more merciful than the warriors. He allows Zella to undertake the dangerous journey because the money from Queen Cor will help the poor family buy needed things.

Chapter Sixteen

Nikobob refuses Inga's offer to make him King of the Twin Islands and also rejects wealth because he fears attracting thieves, marauders, and deadly trouble. He later chooses to settle in Pingaree with his wife and Zella, and Inga appoints him general manager of its buildings and pearl fisheries.

Chapter Twenty Three

Nikobob directs the rebuilding of Pingaree, restores the pearl fisheries, and builds a cottage near the King’s palace for himself. Because of his good judgment, industry, and honesty, King Kitticut makes him Lord High Chamberlain and puts him in charge of the pearl fisheries and the island’s business matters.
