the Ork's escape from the whirlpool in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The Ork is pulled into a whirlpool, rescued by mermaids and sheltered overnight, then swims to the cavern where Trot and Cap'n Bill are staying.

Chapter Three

After the whirlpool pulls the Ork into the ocean, mermaids rescue him and drag him into a cavern. He remains there overnight, then dives into the water and swims upward and onward until he reaches the cavern where Trot and Cap'n Bill are staying.
