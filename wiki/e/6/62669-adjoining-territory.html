adjoining territory in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The adjoining territory is a very beautiful country, distinct from the Land of Oz and separated by an invisible barrier. The Army of Oogaboo enters unknowingly, believing it has reached Oz; at dawn Betsy sees flowered banks and leafy trees, but no people.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

The adjoining territory is distinct from the Land of Oz and separated from it by an invisible barrier. The Army of Oogaboo enters it without realizing at first that it has failed to reach Oz.

Chapter Four

At dawn, Betsy sees land ahead. It appears to be a very beautiful country with banks of flowers between leafy trees, but no people are visible.
