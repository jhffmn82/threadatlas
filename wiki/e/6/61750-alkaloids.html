Abstract written by a model

The poisonous principle in snakes was described by a French chemist as possibly belonging to the alkaloids. Croft compared the reaction of rattlesnake poison with iodine to the precipitates produced by most alkaloids and mentions a ferric chloride and potassium iodide mixture as a convenient alkaloid test.
