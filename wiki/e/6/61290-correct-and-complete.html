Correct and Complete

task · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Correct and Complete is an INFO-RAG training task, used as 40% of batches in mixed training. It identifies informative tokens through word-distribution instability across model layers, masks and replaces tokens to simulate incomplete, incorrect, and correct knowledge, and trains the model to extract, correct, and complete damaged sentences from processed retrieved sets. Separate training gains improve further with Contextual Stimulation.

Correct and Complete is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Unsupervised Learning .. Training Strategy

Correct and Complete identifies informative tokens using their instability across model layers, then applies masking and token replacement to simulate incomplete, incorrect, and correct knowledge. The model learns to extract, correct, and complete the damaged sentence from the processed retrieved set.

Analysis / Fine-grained Analysis for Three Scenarios .. Avoid Catastrophic Forgetting Experiment on

Correct and Complete is one of the three INFO-RAG training tasks and produces gains when trained separately. Its combination with Contextual Stimulation improves the result further.
