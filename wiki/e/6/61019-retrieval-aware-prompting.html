retrieval-aware prompting

method · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: retrieval-aware prompting strategy

Abstract written by a model

Retrieval-aware prompting is a method that injects retrieval information into LLM input embeddings and generation. It aligns retriever knowledge with LLMs without changing retrieved documents’ content or order, reducing information loss. It projects R2-Former information into token-embedding dimensions and prepends an anchor embedding to guide attention toward useful documents.

Introduction

Retrieval-aware prompting injects additional embeddings containing retrieval information into the LLM input. It aligns retriever knowledge with LLMs without changing the content or order of retrieved documents and is intended to reduce information loss.

R2AG / Problem Formulation and Overview .. Training Strategy

Retrieval-aware prompting projects the R2-Former’s retrieval information into the LLM token-embedding dimension and prepends the corresponding embedding to each document’s token embeddings. This embedding acts as an anchor that guides the LLM toward useful documents.
