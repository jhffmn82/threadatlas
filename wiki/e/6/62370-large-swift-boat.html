Chapter Fifteen

Queen Cor prepares a large and swift boat for the escape, loading it with bags of gold and jewels and selecting forty strong oarsmen from Regos. The boat carries Queen Cor, King Gos, King Kitticut, and Queen Garee across the Nonestic Ocean.
