HOW TO ESTABLISH A TRUE MERIDIAN. / INTRODUCTORY. .. ] APPROXIMATE EQUATION OF TIME.

The azimuth table lists the azimuth of Polaris for dates from 1882 through 1886 and latitudes from 26 to 50 degrees north. It shows that the azimuth increases with latitude and decreases slightly from year to year.
