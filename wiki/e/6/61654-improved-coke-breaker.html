improved coke breaker

object · 1 work · 24 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: breaker, coke breaker, machine, the machine, this coke breaker

Abstract written by a model

The improved coke breaker is a coke breaker used by the South Metropolitan Gas Company. It breaks coke with a single revolving drum, fitted with projecting cutting edges, acting against a fixed grid; it needs little power because it has no toothed gearing. The drum is cast in rings to ease repairs, and adding or removing rings varies capacity, while the grid’s regulated distance from the drum determines coke fineness. A full-size machine at Old Kent Road is driven by a one-horse-power Otto gas-engine and, at full designed power, handles 8 tons per hour or 80 tons per working day. It can also be made down to hand-power size. Two men feed large coke and shovel the broken product. Its stated costs total 10s. 6d. daily, or 9s. 1½d. per ton including dust and waste. By supplying broken coke, it opens a market for it.

] IMPROVED COKE BREAKER.

The improved coke breaker uses a single revolving drum with cutting edges to break coke against a fixed grid, without toothed gearing. Its capacity is adjusted by varying the number of cast rings, and the fineness of the product is controlled by changing the grid's distance from the drum. It can be made in different sizes, including hand-powered versions, and the full-size machine can break about 8 tons of coke per hour.
