Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MACHINE FOR COMPRESSING COAL REFUSE INTO FUEL.

The manufacture involves sifting and classifying the refuse, washing and partly drying the coal grains, mixing them with coal dust and pitch, crushing the mixture, converting it into a plastic paste with superheated steam, and distributing it into moulds for compression.
