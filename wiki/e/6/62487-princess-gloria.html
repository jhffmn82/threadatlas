Abstract written by a model

Princess Gloria is a princess of Jinxland, daughter of King Kynd and niece of King Krewl, who serves as her guardian. Described as the loveliest and sweetest maiden in Jinxland, she loves Pon and promises to marry only him, despite their unequal social positions. She repeatedly and permanently refuses Lord Googly-Goo, having rejected his proposal thirty-nine times. King Krewl finds Gloria with Pon, drags her back to his castle, and imprisons her in a locked lower-floor room. With Googly-Goo, he arranges for Blinkie and the witches to freeze Gloria's heart. Bound during the ceremony, Gloria loses all mortal love: she no longer loves Pon, Googly-Goo, Krewl, herself, or anyone else. Cold and reserved, she rejects them with dignity and shows little response even when Pon returns injured. While her heart remains frozen, the Scarecrow identifies Gloria as the rightful ruler entitled to Jinxland's throne and declares her Queen in place of Krewl. Blinkie's magic later melts the frozen heart, restoring Gloria's joy and affection. She immediately embraces Pon. Gloria then accepts the throne as Queen of Jinxland, names Pon her Royal Consort, forgives Krewl, and offers him work as a gardener's boy if he reforms.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

Princess Gloria is described as the niece of King Krewl and the loveliest and sweetest maiden in Jinxland. She loves Pon, has refused Googly-Goo thirty-nine times, and has told Pon that she will marry only him.

Chapter Eleven

Princess Gloria refuses to marry Lord Googly-Goo because she loves Pon. King Krewl finds her with Pon, drags her back to the castle, and locks her in a lower-floor room.

Chapter Twelve

Princess Gloria tells Trot that she loves Pon and will never love Googly-Goo. King Krewl and Googly-Goo plan to have Blinkie freeze her heart so that she can no longer love Pon.

Chapter Thirteen

Princess Gloria is a beautiful girl from Jinxland. Blinkie is planning to freeze her heart.

Chapter Fourteen

Princess Gloria is taken by King Krewl to Blinkie's house and bound while the witches perform their ceremony. After her heart is frozen, she can no longer love Pon, Googly-Goo, King Krewl, or even herself, and she rejects them all with cold dignity.

Chapter Fifteen

Princess Gloria encounters the Scarecrow's talking head and the grasshopper. She says that she does not love Pon or anyone else because the Wicked Witch has frozen her heart, and she declines to restuff the Scarecrow before Trot and Pon arrive.

Chapter Sixteen

Princess Gloria joins the council but remains cold and reserved. The Scarecrow declares that she is the rightful person to sit upon the throne of Jinxland, and he plans to make her Queen. She shows no apparent reaction when Pon returns injured after being beaten by King Krewl.

Chapter Seventeen

Gloria is introduced to the Ork at Pon's hut. The Ork learns that her heart is frozen.

Chapter Eighteen

Princess Gloria is declared by the Scarecrow to be the rightful Queen of Jinxland in place of King Krewl.

Chapter Nineteen

Gloria's frozen heart is melted by Blinkie's magic, and she becomes joyful and affectionate again. She immediately reunites with Pon and embraces him.

Chapter Twenty

Gloria is chosen as Queen of Jinxland and accepts the throne. She names Pon her Royal Consort, forgives Krewl, and offers him the position of gardener's boy if he reforms.
