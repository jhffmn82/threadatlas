Abstract written by a model

The stone huts are hundreds of abandoned structures on the hillside and moor. One has been used as a habitation, containing blankets, a fire, cooking utensils, water, food tins, spirits, and provisions. Believed to hold the lurking man, they become the narrator’s next focus after questions about Mrs. Laura Lyons remain unresolved.
