specialized domains in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024

Specialized domains, including medical, finance, law, private enterprise databases, biomedical information, and computing, challenge general RAG systems because of insufficient updated knowledge and deep understanding. RAG-Studio adapts such systems autonomously and showed superior performance across these domains in extensive experiments.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Specialized domains pose a challenge for off-the-shelf RAG systems. RAG-Studio is designed to autonomously adapt general RAG systems to these domains.

Introduction

Specialized domains include areas such as medical, finance, law, private enterprise databases, biomedical information, and computing. Existing systems using generally trained LLMs and retrievers often underperform there because they lack updated domain-specific knowledge and deep understanding.

Conclusion

Specialized domains were included in extensive experiments evaluating RAG-Studio. The framework consistently demonstrated superior performance across these domains.

Specialized domains, including science and medicine, are target environments for adapting large language models and general-purpose RAG systems. They pose distribution-shift, domain-information, privacy, and scarce-label challenges. SimRAG studies biomedical, natural and social sciences, and computer science question answering, then uses each domain’s unlabeled corpus to generate synthetic training data for domain-adaptive fine-tuning.
