THE BUILDING STONE SUPPLY.

The building stone supply developed slowly in the United States because early settlers relied mainly on wood and imported brick and stone. The unit traces the growth of domestic quarrying and describes the principal American sources and uses of granite, freestone, limestone, marble, slate, and other stones.
