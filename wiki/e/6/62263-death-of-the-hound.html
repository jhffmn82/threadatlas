Abstract written by a model

The death of the hound, caused by Holmes’s gunfire, shows that the apparently supernatural animal is mortal. Its glowing jaws and muzzle are traced to phosphorus. By the next morning, Holmes and the others search the mire and its hiding place, finding evidence of its confinement and use.
