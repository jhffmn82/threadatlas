ON A NEW ARC ELECTRIC LAMP. .. _Electrical Arrangement_.

The lamp balance consists of two solenoids: one carrying the main current and one carrying a shunt current. Changes in current alter their opposing magnetic effects, causing an iron rod to break or restore contact and thereby control the lamp's adjustment.
