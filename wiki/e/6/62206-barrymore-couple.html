Barrymore couple in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

The Barrymore couple are Baskerville Hall’s household servants, long connected to the Baskerville family and Sir Charles. After Sir Charles’s death, painful memories make them consider leaving; Holmes keeps them among the suspects until their innocence or guilt is determined.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6.

The Barrymore couple have served the Baskerville family for several generations and looked after Sir Charles Baskerville. They remain on Sherlock Holmes’s list of suspects because Holmes does not want to dismiss them without determining whether they are innocent or guilty. They tell Sir Henry that they may eventually leave Baskerville Hall because of their painful memories of Sir Charles’s death.
