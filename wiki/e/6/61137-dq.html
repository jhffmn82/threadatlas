Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Problem Formulation

The document set Dq is associated with a question and contains N documents, represented as {d1, d2, . . . dN}. The goal is to use the question and Dq to correctly generate the answer.

Limitations

The documents are retrieved from Wikipedia and contain entities used by the KG generator.

A

Documents Dq provide the set of documents used to construct R reasoning chains for answering question q. Their number is denoted by N in the complexity analysis.

Statistics and Analyses of the Generated

Dq contains the original documents from which reasoning chains identify a smaller subset of relevant documents. The original documents in Dq have substantially higher error rates than the documents selected from them.
