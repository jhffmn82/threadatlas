Zella's journey to Queen Cor in Rinkitink in Oz

Zella’s planned journey to Queen Cor in Coregos begins the next morning, carrying two pails of honey because Queen Cor likes it and the boy Prince may conquer Coregos. Chapter Nine

Zella is expected to travel to the City of Coregos to bring honey to Queen Cor. Because Queen Cor likes honey and the boy Prince may conquer Coregos, Zella's parents decide that she should begin the journey the next morning with two pails.

Chapter Eleven

Zella’s journey to Coregos is dangerous because she may encounter King Gos’s warriors or wild animals. She travels through the forest, crosses the bridge of boats safely, and reaches Queen Cor’s palace by noon.
