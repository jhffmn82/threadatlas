the Jinjin's judgment in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The Jinjin’s judgment clears the adventurers, blames Ruggedo alone, condemns him to leave the Nome Kingdom and wander permanently without home, country, companions, followers, or wealth beyond his pockets, and assigns Quox an unpleasant mission as punishment for disrespect.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

The Jinjin's judgment finds the strangers innocent and assigns blame to Ruggedo alone. It also makes Quox bear the unpleasant mission as punishment for his disrespect toward the Original Dragon.

Chapter Eighteen

The Jinjin's decree commands Ruggedo to leave the Nome Kingdom, go to the earth's surface, and wander without a home, country, friend, follower, or more riches than he can carry in his pockets.
