Locating scenario in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

Locating is a Decision QA benchmark scenario, constructed from Europa Universalis IV and Victoria 3 as video game scenarios and represented in relational and graph databases. It uses data on countries, trade nodes, trading powers, values, flows, merchants, and directional source connections. Each eligible country has a problem, except countries with low TPcountry. In each instance, a different COUNTRY_NODE and HOME_NODE pair defines the goal: decide where to place a merchant so as to maximize the change in profit for the target country, including calculation of TPR to support the decision. The scenario contains a nation intended to influence a region, although nation names were anonymized as three-letter codes because correct influence recommendations could be aggressive. Locating is easier than Building because Building requires a longer traversal. It primarily uses Add look-up for re-planning. Reported results include PlanRAG-LM accuracy of 64.3% versus 48.5% for IterRAG-LM, with re-planning on 6% of questions; another reported Locating accuracy for PlanRAG-LM is 57.5%.

Abstract

Locating is one of the two scenarios in the Decision QA benchmark. The PlanRAG-based method outperforms the state-of-the-art iterative RAG method by 15.8% in this scenario.

Introduction

Locating is one of the two DQA scenarios. It contains questions about which trade node a merchant should be placed on.

Decision QA task .. Statistics of DQA

The Locating scenario uses data about countries, trade nodes, trading powers, values, and flows. Its goal is to choose the trading node that maximizes the change in profit for a given target country.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

The Locating scenario asks where to locate a merchant to maximize profit. PlanRAG-LM reaches 64.3% accuracy compared with 48.5% for IterRAG-LM, and re-planning is used for 6% of its questions.

Ethical Considerations

In the Locating scenario, a correct answer about which nation should influence a particular region might be aggressive toward specific nations or regions. Nation names were therefore anonymized into three-letter codes.

Appendix / Data collection

The Locating scenario includes one problem for each country, except for countries with low TPcountry.

Data instances

In the Locating scenario, every instance has a different goal involving a different pair of COUNTRY_NODE and HOME_NODE. The question asks where to locate a merchant to maximize COUNTRY_NODE’s profit on HOME_NODE.
