Cartwright in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

We heard the steps of our visitors descend the stair and the bang

Cartwright, a fourteen-year-old district messenger with a bright, keen face, is assigned by Holmes to visit twenty-three nearby hotels. He is to search their waste-paper for a center page of The Times with holes cut in it and report by wire before evening.

And that was a most vague direction. I realised it as I drove

The boy repeatedly carries a bundle along the moor, apparently bringing food to the stranger. He is seen by Mr. Frankland through a telescope and may also be acting as the stranger's agent by reporting Watson's movements.
