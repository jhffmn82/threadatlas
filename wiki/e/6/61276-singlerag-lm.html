SingleRAG-LM in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

SingleRAG-LM is a single-turn RAG-based decision maker based on Single-turn RAG, implemented by GPT-4 with zero temperature and LangChain. It uses ReAct prompts for retrieval and answer generation, yet misses database results in over 60% Locating and 95% Building questions.
