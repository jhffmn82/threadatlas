SingleRAG-LM

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

SingleRAG-LM is a single-turn RAG-based decision maker based on Single-turn RAG, implemented by GPT-4 with zero temperature and LangChain. It uses ReAct prompts for retrieval and answer generation, yet misses database results in over 60% Locating and 95% Building questions.

SingleRAG-LM is in one work on this site, PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
