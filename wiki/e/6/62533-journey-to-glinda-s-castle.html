journey to Glinda's castle in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

A pleasure trip for Trot and Cap'n Bill toward Glinda's palace begins cheerfully after breakfast at the magic house. The Scarecrow leads the group astray to a river and the Great Waterfall, but, once restored, resumes leading them toward the palace.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Two

The journey to Glinda's castle begins cheerfully after the group leaves the magic house following a hearty breakfast. The travelers enjoy the scenery and magical provisions, but the Scarecrow takes a wrong path and leads them to the river and the Great Waterfall.

Chapter Twenty Three

The journey continues after the party restores the Scarecrow. He resumes leadership and guides Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright toward Glinda's palace.
