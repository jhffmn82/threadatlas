Abstract written by a model

Magic Pearls are three ancestral magical treasures of Prince Inga's family: blue, pink, and white. Hidden by the Kings of Pingaree in a secret cavity beneath the royal banquet-hall floor, they are reserved to protect Pingaree in danger. The blue pearl gives great strength, the pink protects its owner from danger, and the white speaks wise, helpful words. After the palace is destroyed, Inga secretly retrieves the pearls and conceals them in his shoes. During his voyage they give him confidence; he believes the white provides a boat, the blue strengthens his rowing, and the pink keeps him safe. Their powers help him and Nikobob resist enemies, but are lost with the shoes. When Inga regains them, he overcomes Queen Cor and frees slaves. He finally returns all three, in their silken bag, to the banquet-hall cavity for Pingaree's future protection.

Magic Pearls in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916 · the work’s page · read it where Magic Pearls first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The three Magic Talismans are magical pearls kept hidden by the Kings of Pingaree in a recess beneath a banquet-hall floor. The blue pearl grants great strength, the pink pearl protects its owner from danger, and the white pearl speaks wise and helpful words.

Chapter Five

The three magical pearls were hidden beneath a tiled floor in the royal banquet hall and remain there despite the destruction of the palace. Inga secretly retrieves them and keeps them concealed from King Rinkitink and Bilbil.

Chapter Six

The Magic Pearls give Inga confidence during the voyage. He believes the White Pearl provided the boat, the Blue Pearl provided his rowing strength, and the Pink Pearl will protect him from danger.

Chapter Eight

The magic pearls are concealed by Inga in his shoes and give him powers that protect him from danger and enable him to defeat King Gos's forces. Inga explains their secret to Rinkitink only after the shoes are lost.

Chapter Nine

The magic pearls are hidden in the points of Inga's shoes. They lend power to Nikobob's arm and prevent Choggenmugger's teeth from injuring him when he holds the shoes inside his coat.

Chapter Thirteen

The Magic Pearls' power returns to Inga when he regains possession of the magical shoes. Rinkitink realizes this when Inga is able to overcome Queen Cor and free the slaves.

Chapter Twenty Three

Inga places the three Magic Pearls in their silken bag and returns them to the secret cavity beneath the tiled floor of the banquet hall. They are kept there so they can be quickly secured if danger threatens Pingaree again.
