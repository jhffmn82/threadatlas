Abstract written by a model

Charles's horse is a handsome, well-rested animal accustomed to rough roads. Tethered in the ordinary Cornish fashion, it travels lively across the Downs to Mullion and Mullion Cove after resting. Before the return journey toward Falmouth, Charles plans to feed and groom it well.
