the horse in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Charles's horse is a handsome, well-rested animal accustomed to rough roads. Tethered in the ordinary Cornish fashion, it travels lively across the Downs to Mullion and Mullion Cove after resting. Before the return journey toward Falmouth, Charles plans to feed and groom it well.

Charles's horse has rested before setting out and travels lively across the Downs to Mullion and Mullion Cove. Charles says it is accustomed to rough roads and plans to feed and groom it well before driving it toward Falmouth.
