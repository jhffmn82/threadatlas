Scenario 3

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: S3, the third scenario

Abstract written by a model

Scenario 3 is a set of very difficult or long-tail questions whose retrieved texts contain no correct, usable answers, selected using cover EM. It is harder than Scenario 2 because retrieval models cannot find anything that solves the questions. Although the texts may be semantically related, large language models must use that context to stimulate knowledge in their parameters; INFO-RAG still shows an advantage.

Scenario 3 is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Scenario 3 concerns retrieved texts that do not contain an answer usable for solving the question. Because the texts may still be semantically related, LLMs should stimulate knowledge within their parameters based on that context to solve the question.
