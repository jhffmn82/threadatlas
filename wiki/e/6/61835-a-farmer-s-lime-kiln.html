Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

A FARMER'S LIME KILN.

A FARMER'S LIME KILN is described as a 13-foot-square structure 25 feet high, built from limestone quarried on the spot. It has iron rods and plates for reinforcement, an arched front with a plank floor, a fire-brick-lined well, movable iron bars for drawing the lime, and a railway track to facilitate filling. Its capacity is about 100 bushels in 24 hours.
