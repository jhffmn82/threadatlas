Information Refiner

role · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Information Re- finer, information refiner, “Information Re- finer”, “Information Refiner”

Abstract written by a model

Information Refiner is the proposed role of large language models in RAG and INFO-RAG. It combines knowledge from retrieved texts with knowledge in model parameters, even when retrieved texts are incorrect, incomplete, or unhelpful. Its central purpose is to produce positive information gain and improve RAG’s information bottleneck by generating text that is more concise, accurate, and complete than the input retrieved texts. From retrieval onward, the model robustly integrates the available texts and its parameter knowledge, then outputs a refined text rather than merely repeating the retrieved material.

Information Refiner is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

The Information Refiner is the proposed role of a large language model in RAG. In this role, the model integrates knowledge from retrieved texts and its parameters to generate text that is more concise, accurate, and complete than the retrieved texts.

Introduction

The unit proposes considering large language models as an “Information Refiner” in RAG. In this role, they generate text that is more concise, accurate, and complete than the input retrieved texts by combining retrieved knowledge with knowledge in their parameters.

Related Work / Retrieval Augmented Generation .. Our INFO-RAG

The “Information Refiner” is the role that INFO-RAG assigns to language models in RAG. In this role, a language model further improves the information bottleneck of RAG and is robust to retrieved texts.

Positive Information Gain in RAG

Information Refiner is the proposed role for LLMs in RAG. In this role, an LLM produces positive information gain in the information flow.

Conclusion

“Information Refiner” describes the proposed role of LLMs in RAG. In this role, LLMs integrate knowledge from model parameters and retrieved texts to produce more concise, accurate, and complete text.
