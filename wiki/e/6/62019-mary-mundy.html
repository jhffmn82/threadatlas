Mary Mundy

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Mary, Miss Mary Mundy

Abstract written by a model

Mary Mundy is the respected, hospitable innkeeper of the Old Inn at Mullion, a Lizard resident known for red cheeks, black eyes, and voluble, kind strong Cornish. She runs the inn, helps raise a young niece with her brother, and keeps a poem and portrait of the Scotch Professor. Away in Helstone when travelers arrive, she returns that evening, warmly feeds them, restores order, and remembers the Professor.

We had just time to dine, repose a little, and communicate our address to our af

Mary Mundy is away in Helstone when the travelers first reach the Old Inn, but she returns by the evening. She welcomes them warmly, provides a substantial tea, and is described as a capable, cheerful woman who puts everything and everybody to rights. She asks them to tell the Professor that she has not forgotten him.
