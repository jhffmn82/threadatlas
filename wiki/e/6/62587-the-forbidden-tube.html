the Forbidden Tube in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The Forbidden Tube, also called the Hollow Tube, is a steep, smooth metal passage with a hole through the earth, passing through its center and leading to the other side of the world. Built by Hiergargo the Magician for exclusive use, it is private property, forbidden to intruders, and marked at its mouth with warnings that outsiders stay out and those inside not come out. Entering or using it brings a punishment of nine days and ten nights of torture before being thrown back into it. Ruggedo has the Chief Magician redirect a path to its concealed opening and plans to drop the adventurers through it. It sends them at great speed for more than an hour and ejects them into a garden. Later it carries them back from Tititi-Hoochoo's land; Tititi-Hoochoo forbids rubbish from being thrown into it, and Quox finally shoots into open air above its entrance.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The Hollow Tube is a dangerous passage through which Ruggedo plans to drop the adventurers. At Ruggedo's order, the Chief Magician redirects the path toward its opening and makes the opening invisible.

Chapter Ten

The Hollow Tube is a steep, smooth passage leading through the earth to the opposite side of the world. It carries the travelers at great speed for more than an hour before ejecting them into a garden.

Chapter Eleven

The Forbidden Tube is a passage whose use is punishable by nine days and ten nights of torture followed by being thrown back into it. The party enters it unwillingly because Ruggedo makes their path lead to it and conceals its opening.

Chapter Thirteen

The Forbidden Tube carries the adventurers from Tititi-Hoochoo's land back toward their own side of the world. Its entrance bears warnings telling those outside to stay outside and those inside not to come out, and it was built by Hiergargo the Magician.

Chapter Fourteen

The Hollow Tube is the route through which Ruggedo threw the invaders and through which they are now returning. Ruggedo cannot turn it upside down, and Tititi-Hoochoo has forbidden the nomes to throw anything into it.

Chapter Fifteen

The Tube carries the travelers through the center of the earth. Quox shoots out of it into the open air more than a hundred feet above the entrance, surprising the nomes waiting below.
