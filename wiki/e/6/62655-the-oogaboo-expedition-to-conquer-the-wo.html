Abstract written by a model

The Oogaboo expedition to conquer the world is Ann Soforth's purpose for Ann's Army. Ann begins by planning to surprise and intimidate Princess Ozma's peaceful, unarmed forces, replace Ozma as ruler, and avoid bloodshed. The army sets out to conquer unknown territory, then plans to take Ruggedo's kingdom, rescue Shaggy's brother, and seize its gold and jewels. After severe hardships, Queen Ann abandons the conquest, and Ozma sends the expedition back to Oogaboo.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Ann plans the conquest of Oz because she believes Princess Ozma's peaceful, unarmed forces can be defeated through surprise and intimidation. She expects to set herself up as ruler in Ozma's place without shedding blood.

Chapter Three

Ann Soforth says that the army has started out to conquer the world and treats the unknown territory as part of that objective. Private Files refuses to remain with the Rak because he has an engagement to help Queen Ann achieve the conquest.

Chapter Eight

The proposed conquest of Ruggedo is meant to help Shaggy rescue his brother while allowing Queen Ann and her officers to claim the kingdom's gold, jewels, and other plunder. The plan begins when Tik-Tok accepts the role of private soldier and the flowers reveal the road to Ruggedo's kingdom.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Oogaboo expedition to conquer the world ends when Queen Ann changes her mind after suffering many hardships. Ozma sends the expedition back to Oogaboo.
