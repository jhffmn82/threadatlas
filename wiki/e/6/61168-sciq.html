SciQ

Also called: Sciq, Sciq dataset, Sciq-all, Sciq-train

Abstract written by a model

SciQ is a multi-domain multiple-choice question dataset of 13,000 science questions across physics, chemistry, biology, and other natural sciences. Each question has one answer and three incorrect options, with no blanks. Split 11,700/1,000/1,000 for train/dev/test, it supports distractor generation and cross-domain RAP pretraining; its best model raises F1@3 from 15.92 to 16.50.

SciQ is in one work on this site, Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
