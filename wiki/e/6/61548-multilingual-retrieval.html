1 section; each heading opens the text

H

Multilingual retrieval must support both monolingual retrieval, where query and datastore languages match, and cross-lingual retrieval, where they differ. The experiments retrieve from English Wikipedia, user-language Wikipedia, their concatenation, or Wikipedia in all considered languages.
