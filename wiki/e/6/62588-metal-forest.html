Metal Forest in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The Metal Forest is an artificial forest in the Great Domed Cavern, the largest cavern in the Nome Kingdom, within a vast dome beneath another mountain. Created by Ruggedo to keep precious metals from mortals, it has trees of gold and silver, solid-gold trunks, branches, and leaves, filigree-silver bushes and underbrush, jewel-strewn ground, and paths sometimes paved with diamonds. It also contains a green grove of natural trees and, beyond its metal growth, ordinary food-bearing trees. Ruggedo sends and holds the Ugly One there, protected by secret entrances that change weekly, though even Ruggedo cannot always find it. Later Queen Ann and the officers of Oogaboo enter through a tunnel and are trapped. After the Ugly One is rescued, the group crosses the forest; the officers collect jewels, and a jewel trail leads outward through the passage after Ruggedo drops them.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The Metal Forest was created by Ruggedo, who made every tree from gold and silver to keep precious metals away from mortals. The Ugly One has been sent there, but even Ruggedo says that he cannot always find it.

Chapter Nineteen

The Metal Forest lies in the Great Domed Cavern, the largest cavern in the Nome Kingdom. Its trees are made of gold and silver and its ground is covered with precious stones; the Ugly One is being held there, but its secret entrances change location weekly.

Chapter Twenty

The Metal Forest occupies a vast domed cavern beneath another mountain. Its trees are made of solid gold, its bushes and underbrush of pure filigree silver, and its paths are sometimes paved with diamonds; beyond it are ordinary trees bearing food.

Chapter Twenty-One

The Metal Forest is a huge dome containing glittering metal trees and masses of jewels. Queen Ann and the officers of Oogaboo enter it through a tunnel and remain trapped there until their situation changes.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The group walks through the Metal Forest after the Ugly One is rescued. Queen Ann and the Army officers gather jewels there, while a trail of jewels leads from the forest through the passage because Ruggedo dropped them.
