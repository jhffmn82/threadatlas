the guide in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

A solemn Cornish-born sailor-guide, formerly a man-of-war’s-man, leads visitors across fields to the Logan Rock and safely along Land’s End’s perilous rocks. He recounts shipwrecks, Royal Navy service aboard H.M.S. Agamemnon, and Balaklava, then gives the narrator a glittering stone.

A solemn young guide leads the party across the fields to the Logan Rock and helps the narrator manage the stiff climb. Later, a sailor-like guide carefully leads them along the perilous rocks at Land's End, shares stories of shipwrecks and his service aboard H.M.S. Agamemnon, and gives the narrator a glittering stone as a memento.
