Triple Ranker

Also called: Triple_Scorer, triple ranker, triple scorer

Abstract written by a model

Triple Ranker is a bi-encoder model and effectiveness component in TRACE-Doc. At each reasoning-chain step, it receives the current context and knowledge graph, selects top-K relevant candidate triples, and is evaluated against no ranking, DRAGON+, and E5; removing ranking lowers performance, while E5 outperforms DRAGON+.

Triple Ranker is in one work on this site, TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Baselines

The Triple Ranker is evaluated without ranking and with DRAGON+ or E5. Removing it lowers performance, while E5 performs better than DRAGON+ on the reported datasets.
