the winter hardship of the Lizard community in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

The winter hardship of the Cornish coast is seasonal unemployment: poor fishing and no market for serpentine can leave men without earnings for four months, endured through summer savings and stored hay to prevent starvation.

] DAY THE THIRD "And a beautiful day it is, ladies, though it won't do for Kynance.

The winter hardship of the Cornish coast includes months with little work, poor fishing, and no market for serpentine. A local man says that sometimes for four months a person does not earn a halfpenny and must save during summer to avoid starvation.
