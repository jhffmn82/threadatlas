escape from the island in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Escape from the island is Trot and Cap'n Bill's planned departure when no practical alternative or raft is available. They eat lavender berries to shrink, ride in Trot's sunbonnet tied around the Ork's neck, and the Ork carries them over the ocean to later regain size with deep purple berries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Ork cannot find another practical way to leave the island, and Cap'n Bill lacks tools to build a raft. They therefore plan for Trot and Cap'n Bill to eat lavender berries, ride in Trot's sunbonnet, and later eat deep purple berries to regain their size. The Ork carries them over the ocean after Pessim ties the sunbonnet around its neck.
