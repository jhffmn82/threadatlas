the golden crown in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The golden crown falls from King Krewl when Orks knock him from his throne, passes to the Scarecrow, then to Gloria as she becomes Queen, and belongs to her after coronation.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eighteen

The golden crown falls from King Krewl's head when the Orks knock him from his throne. The leader of the Orks places it on the Scarecrow's head.

Chapter Twenty

The glittering crown remains on the Scarecrow's head until he removes it and places it on Gloria's head. It then rests among her curls as she becomes Queen.
