flight of the rulers in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The flight is an escape by boat to Regos with Queen Garee, involving rulers of Pingaree as prisoners. It begins after Queen Cor flees her palace when Inga frees slaves and threatens imprisonment, then continues as Cor and King Gos leave Regos before Ingato arrives, heading toward Nome Kingdom with captives and bribes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

Queen Cor escapes from her palace after Inga frees the Pingaree slaves and threatens her with imprisonment. She runs toward Regos, collides with Bilbil, loses her crown in a ditch, and continues fleeing; the bridge of boats is later removed, suggesting that she has reached or returned toward Regos with Queen Garee.

Chapter Fifteen

Queen Cor and King Gos flee from Regos with the rulers of Pingaree before Prince Ingato arrives. They travel toward the Nome Kingdom, carrying gold and jewels to bribe the nomes and hide the captives.
