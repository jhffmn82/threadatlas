HOW TO ESTABLISH A TRUE MERIDIAN. / INTRODUCTORY. .. ] APPROXIMATE EQUATION OF TIME.

The equation of time is supplied in an approximate date-and-minute table for correcting observed solar time to true or mean solar time. The table indicates periods when the clock is faster or slower than the sun.
