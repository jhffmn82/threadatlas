domain adaptation in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024

Domain adaptation for RAG is implemented through RAG-Studio, which fine-tunes generator and retriever using synthetic data from a target-domain corpus. It begins with minimal manual intervention and ends as an autonomous in-domain framework requiring no external data or models.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Domain adaptation is addressed by fine-tuning both the generator and retriever with synthetic data derived from a target domain corpus. The approach is intended to improve RAG performance in specialized contexts with minimal manual intervention.

Conclusion

The paper applies domain adaptation to RAG systems through the RAG-Studio framework. This approach is based entirely on synthetic data and does not require external data or models.

Limitations

RAG-Studio is described as a strong autonomous framework for in-domain RAG adaptation.

domain adaptation in SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains

Ran Xu, Hui Liu, Sreyashi Nag, Zhenwei Dai, Yaochen Xie, Xianfeng Tang, Chen Luo, Yang Li, Joyce C. Ho, Carl Yang, Qi He · 2025

Domain adaptation is needed to address distribution shifts and limited access to domain-specific data when applying general-purpose retrieval-augmented generation systems to fields such as science and medicine. SimRAG performs this adaptation through self-generated, filtered training examples.

Abstract

Domain adaptation is needed to address distribution shifts and limited access to domain-specific data when applying general-purpose retrieval-augmented generation systems to fields such as science and medicine. SimRAG performs this adaptation through self-generated, filtered training examples.

domain adaptation in OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models

Kartik Sharma · 2025-11-04/2025-11-09

Domain adaptation is addressed by OG-RAG to make language systems adaptable and reliable across diverse domains. Earlier approaches train retrievers or generators, share backbones, self-improve, or specialize retrievers, but require retraining. OG-RAG’s applicability ultimately depends on well-defined domain ontologies.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Related Work

Domain adaptation for RAG has been pursued through retriever and generator training, backbone sharing, self-improvement, and domain-specialized retriever training. These approaches can enhance adaptation but involve retraining and limit adoption across arbitrary domains.

Conclusion

Domain adaptation is the application area addressed by OG-RAG. The method is presented as a way to make language systems more adaptable and reliable across diverse domains.

Limitations

Domain adaptation depends on the availability of well-defined domain ontologies. The text presents this dependency as a limitation in domains where such ontologies have not yet been developed.
