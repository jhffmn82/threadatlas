SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The blooming mill receives ingots removed from the soaking pits and swung to its live rollers by a crane. The machinery at Darlington was not adapted for rolling off in one heat, although it successfully bloomed the treated ingots and directly rolled some double-head rails.
