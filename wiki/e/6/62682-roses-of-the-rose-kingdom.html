Roses of the Rose Kingdom

Abstract written by a model

Roses of the Rose Kingdom are rare, fair, sweet-smelling but heartless beings, each with a lovely girl's face and thorns beneath green leaves. Awakened and frightened by Hank's bray, they demand that Betsy and Hank leave because only Roses belong there. Later they follow the Royal Gardener, oppose Princess Ozga as ruler, ignore the Love Magnet, and use thorns to drive the group out.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter Five

The Roses awaken when Hank brays and are frightened by his harsh voice. They tell Betsy that only Roses are allowed in the Rose Kingdom and urge her and Hank to leave before the Royal Gardener finds them.

Chapter Six

The Roses follow the Royal Gardener into the Royal Gardens and insist that the Rose Kingdom must have a male Ruler. They reject Princess Ozga because she is a girl and refuse to respond to the Love Magnet because, as Betsy observes, they have no hearts. Their hidden thorns help drive the group out of the kingdom.
