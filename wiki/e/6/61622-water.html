Abstract written by a model

Water fills Bunte's burette and funnel, drives gas into or out, and establishes measurement pressure by restoring the funnel level. It washes absorbing liquids, is retained in coal grains to equalize heat distribution, and in excess hinders perfect agglomeration.

BUNTE'S BURETTE FOR THE ANALYSIS OF FURNACE GASES. .. _Mode of Measuring the Gases and Absorption_.

Water fills the burette and funnel, drives gas into or out of the apparatus, and establishes the pressure needed for measurement. It is also used to wash absorbing liquids from the burette, except that dilute hydrochloric acid is used when an acid copper chloride solution is employed.
