Abstract written by a model

Reasoning chains are series of logically connected knowledge-graph triples constructed from documents Dq to answer a question q. Their central role is to identify and integrate supporting evidence: they can serve directly as context for generating an answer or identify relevant supporting documents while avoiding irrelevant or noisy ones. In TRACE, a constructor selects triples autoregressively according to the question and previously selected triples, preserving coherence and grounding the chains in retrieved documents. Construction proceeds iteratively, maintaining multiple candidate chains and retaining those with the highest cumulative scores. A chain can terminate once its selected triples are sufficient to answer the question; its required length may be determined adaptively. Multiple chains may contribute complementary information absent from an initial chain, creating enriched context and initially improving performance, although excessive length can introduce redundant or irrelevant information and degrade performance. Across datasets, chains average approximately three steps and identify about 2.6 relevant documents from Dq. Adaptive-length chains outperform chains fixed at length 4. Their grounding is intended to make reasoning more reliable and accurate than potentially inaccurate generated CoT sentences, and they generalise across reader models. Because annotated data is unavailable, the chains themselves are not directly and quantitatively evaluated; instead, their effectiveness is assessed through final QA performance, statistics, and qualitative results.

front matter .. TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Knowledge-grounded reasoning chains are the subject of construction in the paper’s title.

Abstract

Knowledge-grounded reasoning chains are series of logically connected knowledge triples used to identify and integrate supporting evidence for answering questions. Using them as context was often sufficient to answer questions correctly without using entire documents.

Introduction

Reasoning chains consist of several knowledge graph triples that logically connect supporting evidence to an answer. TRACE constructs them autoregressively, selecting each triple based on the question and previously selected triples so that the chain remains coherent. The chains are used either as direct context for answer generation or to identify useful documents.

TRACE / Overall Framework .. Answer Generation

A reasoning chain is a sequence of logically connected knowledge graph triples that supports answering a question. Its triples are selected autoregressively, and the chain can end when the previously selected triples are sufficient to answer the question.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

A reasoning chain is constructed from selected knowledge-graph triples or documents to provide supporting context to the reader. The chain constructor identifies evidence autoregressively, and longer chains improve performance initially before irrelevant or redundant information can cause degradation.

Related Work

TRACE constructs reasoning chains that are grounded in retrieved documents. This grounding is intended to make the reasoning process more reliable and accurate than reasoning based on potentially inaccurate generated CoT sentences.

Conclusion

TRACE builds reasoning chains from its knowledge graph using an autoregressive reasoning chain constructor. The chains can be used directly as context for generating answers or to identify relevant documents, and they generalize across various reader models.

Limitations

The reasoning chains are not directly and quantitatively evaluated because annotated data is unavailable. Their effectiveness is assessed through final QA performance and discussed through statistics and qualitative results.

A

The reasoning chains are constructed in a quantity of R from documents Dq to answer question q. Their construction has complexity O(N + LR) when adaptive chain termination is not used.

Prompts / Prompt for Generating KG Triples .. Prompt for Generating Answers / Demonstration Data / Experimental Details

Reasoning chains are constructed by selecting triples iteratively for up to a maximum length. Algorithm 1 maintains multiple chains and retains the candidates with the highest cumulative scores.

Baselines

A reasoning path is formed by selecting knowledge triples that extend an existing set of triples. Selection stops when the path is sufficient to answer the question.

Statistics and Analyses of the Generated

The reasoning chains average approximately three steps across datasets and identify about 2.6 relevant documents from Dq. They are used to select documents while avoiding irrelevant ones, but their effectiveness cannot be quantitatively evaluated because annotated data is unavailable.

Effects of Adaptive Chain Termination

TRACE uses reasoning chains whose lengths can be determined adaptively. Adaptive-length reasoning chains outperform chains fixed at length 4.

Case Study

Reasoning chains provide information used to answer questions and identify supporting documents. Generating multiple chains can add complementary facts that are missing from an initial chain, while using the chains can help avoid noisy documents.
