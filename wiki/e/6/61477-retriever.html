retriever in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024

The retriever is a dense retriever in a unified RAG system that selects top-K passages from a domain corpus for synthetic questions, excluding the ground passage. Initially limited by general-purpose training and lacking updated domain-specific knowledge and deep understanding in specialized contexts, it is jointly fine-tuned with synthetic data and the generator. It receives training and generation signals from LLM self-feedback elicited through Chain-of-Thought prompting, aligning retrieval with LLM preferences. As a result, it brings in more precise, helpful contextual information and reduces misleading contexts for a specific domain.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Retrievers in general-purpose RAG systems are trained on general-purpose datasets. RAG-Studio jointly fine-tunes them so they can bring in more precise information for a specific domain.

Introduction

The retriever gathers contextual passages relevant to synthetic questions and receives training signals from the LLM’s self-feedback about the helpfulness of retrieved contexts. It is fine-tuned to align more closely with the LLM’s preferences, provide helpful information, and reduce misleading contexts.

Related Work / RAG Fine-tuning .. Self-Alignment

The retriever can be optimized using generation signals from large language models so that it aligns with the LLM's preferences. The described approach fine-tunes it jointly with the generator in a unified RAG system.

Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

The retriever is a dense retriever that selects passages from the domain corpus for a question. It is fine-tuned to align its retrieved passages with the generator's preferences.
