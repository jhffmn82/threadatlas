Abstract written by a model

Selden dies after fleeing across the moor amid a terrible scream, apparently pursued by the hound. He falls over rocks, breaks his neck, and is found with a crushed skull and blood beneath him. Stapleton attributes the death to the hound, but no direct connection can be proved; Holmes urges Watson to preserve Stapleton’s account for Sir Henry.
