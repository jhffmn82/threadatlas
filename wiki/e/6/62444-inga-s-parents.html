Inga's parents in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Inga's parents are captured by King Gos with the people of Pingaree and carried away into slavery under the invaders. They are held in Regos and Coregos as slaves of King Gos and Queen Cor; Inga's mother is on Coregos, while his father's location is unspecified. Inga plans to find, rescue, and return them to Pingaree, refusing celebration while they remain enslaved.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

Prince Inga's parents are captured by the invaders and carried away with the people of Pingaree to become slaves.

Chapter Five

The White Pearl reveals that Inga's parents are held in slavery in Regos and Coregos. Inga intends to travel there and liberate them.

Chapter Eight

Inga continues planning to search for and rescue his father and mother. His mother is enslaved on Coregos, while the text does not specify the father's location.

Chapter Ten

Inga's parents are described as slaves of Queen Cor and King Gos. Inga refuses Queen Cor's invitation to feast because he cannot enjoy himself while his parents remain enslaved.
