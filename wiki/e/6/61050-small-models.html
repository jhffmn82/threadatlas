Small LLMs, including Llama3.2:3b and Phi3:3.8b, are evaluated for domain-specific responses. Although they generally perform below large models, the study notes that well-optimized small architectures can achieve strong results.

IV.

Small models, including Llama3.2:3b, Llama3.2:1b, Gemma2:2b, Phi3:3.8b, and Phi3.5:3.8b, generally struggle with complex queries and score lower across the evaluation metrics. They are computationally efficient and suitable for lightweight tasks, while Llama3.2:3b demonstrates that some small models can achieve much stronger results.
