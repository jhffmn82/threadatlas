Abstract written by a model

In "TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," reasoning chains are selected knowledge triples used to identify relevant documents and answer multi-hop questions, prepended to questions for readers, and used to support TRACE and TRACE-Doc retrieval. In "GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation," triple reasoning chains are iteratively selected by an LLM to ground answer generation or filter retrieved results, with the contributions of all participating triple elements considered. In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," reasoning chains simplify some retrieval problems by retrieving the path from a question to its target entity.

reasoning chains in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where reasoning chains first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reasoning chains are selected knowledge triples used to identify relevant documents and answer multi-hop questions. They are typically about 3.1 triples long, are prepended to questions for readers, and support TRACE and TRACE-Doc retrieval. Experiments use five chains with a maximum length of four; adding chains can supply missing information until performance stabilizes. TRACE-built chains generalize across readers rather than being tailored to one model.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Baselines

Reasoning chains are constructed from selected knowledge triples to identify relevant documents and answer questions. Their reported average lengths are approximately 3.1 triples across the datasets.

Additional Implementation Details

Reasoning chains can be prepended as context to questions before they are passed to the reader. Their maximum length is set to 4, and five reasoning chains are used throughout the experiments.

Additional Experimental Results and .. MuSiQue

The unit reports that reasoning chains constructed by TRACE generalize across different readers and are not tailored to a specific model. They are also used by TRACE-Doc to retrieve relevant documents from Dq.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

Reasoning chains provide information for TRACE's processing of multi-hop questions. Increasing their number can add information missing from earlier chains, but performance stabilizes once no further relevant information is added.
