Abstract written by a model

concerns Agamemnon [as the murdered king] The legend tells how Agamemnon's son and daughter eventually killed their guilty mother and her paramour in revenge, following Apollo's express command. Homer, Aeschylus, Euripides, and Sophocles treat the story in notably different moral and psychological styles.
