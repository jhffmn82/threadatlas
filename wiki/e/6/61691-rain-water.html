Various · 1881

Rain-water is water. Collected in a secluded district after previous rain, it has remarkable purity, contains traces of carbonic acid and other gases, and is purer than distilled water. Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

APPARATUS FOR OBTAINING PURE WATER FOR PHOTOGRAPHIC USE.

Rain-water collected in a secluded district after previous rain can be exceptionally pure, containing little more than traces of carbonic acid and other atmospheric gases. In towns, especially when it flows over dirty roofs, it can become contaminated, but clean rain-water collected after the roofs are washed is valuable for photographic work.
