Strong, fierce, good-natured, conceited, and fond of his own voice, he wears forty yards of enchanted ribbon and a great jeweled locket around his neck. After disrespecting the Original Dragon, he is made Tititi-Hoochoo’s messenger and Instrument of Vengeance. He serves Jinjin and is ordered to conquer Ruggedo. Quox announces Jinjin’s decree, uses eggs from his locket to drive Ruggedo away, breaks enchantments on the Rose Princess and Shaggy Man, breaks his own chains, and appoints Kaliko King of the Nomes, expecting wise rule. With his mission finished, he returns headfirst through the Tube toward his home on the other side of the world, planning to sleep until he arrives.

Quox in Tik-Tok of Oz

He has silver scales, fire inside him, brimstone breath, claws and teeth, flame-breathing power, and an electric light at the end of his tail. Strong, fierce, good-natured, conceited, and fond of his own voice, he wears forty yards of enchanted ribbon and a great jeweled locket around his neck. Carrying the travelers and invaders as a beast of burden through the Tube, he protects them from nomes’ spears with his tough silver-scaled body, then waits outside the caverns and cannot be awakened from his trance. In Ruggedo’s cavern, a thousand nomes chain him, but the enchanted ribbon removes Ruggedo’s magic. Quox announces Jinjin’s decree, uses eggs from his locket to drive Ruggedo away, breaks enchantments on the Rose Princess and Shaggy Man, breaks his own chains, and appoints Kaliko King of the Nomes, expecting wise rule. Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

Quox is a huge young blue dragon with silver scales, a jeweled locket, fire-breathing abilities, and an electric light on his tail. Because he disrespected the Original Dragon, Tititi-Hoochoo appoints him as the Instrument of Vengeance; Quox carries the adventurers through the Tube and says he will obey the Jinjin's orders.

Chapter Fourteen

The dragon carrying the invaders is described as a monster with an electric light on the end of its tail. Kaliko warns that it can use its claws, teeth, and flames, and that it is too tough for anyone to hurt easily.

Chapter Fifteen

Quox carries the travelers through the Tube and is described as good-natured, conceited, strong, and fierce. He shields the group from the nomes' spears with his silver-scaled body, then lands on a mountainside and agrees to wait while the others enter the caverns.

Chapter Seventeen

Quox sleeps in a trance on the rocks while the others enter the cavern. Polychrome strikes his eyelids and searches for a tender place to wake him, but he does not awaken.

Chapter Eighteen

Quox rushes into Ruggedo's cavern but is restrained by a chain pulled by a thousand nomes. He explains that the enchanted ribbon around his neck has taken away Ruggedo's magic, announces the Jinjin's decree, and uses eggs from the great jeweled locket to drive Ruggedo away.

Chapter Nineteen

Quox uses his pink ribbon to break the Nome King's enchantments on the Rose Princess and Shaggy Man. He breaks his chains, questions Kaliko about ruling the nomes, and appoints Kaliko as King of the Nomes.

Chapter Twenty

Quox tells the others that his mission is finished and that he must return to the other side of the world. He enters the Tube headfirst, intending to sleep through the journey and wake at home.
