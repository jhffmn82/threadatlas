bridge of boats in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The bridge of boats connects Regos and Coregos with planks across closely joined boats, leading to Coregos. King Gos and his warriors flee over it, then remove it in danger; Bilbil escapes across it, and later Zella crosses safely from the forest’s edge.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The bridge of boats connects Regos and Coregos, with planks laid across closely joined boats. King Gos and his warriors use it to flee to Coregos, then remove it and draw the boats to their side in hopes of stopping the invaders.

Chapter Eleven

The bridge of boats leads to Coregos from the forest’s edge. Zella crosses it safely without encountering any warriors.
