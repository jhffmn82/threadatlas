Abstract written by a model

The Lizard storm is an especially awful Cornwall storm of torrential rain and howling wind. Beginning on the fourth day after clear, silent skies, it brings an extraordinary deluge and shrieking wind, convincing the narrator that the weather has broken.

DAY THE FOURTH Sunday, September 4th--and we had started on September 1st; was it possible we had only been travelling four days?

The Lizard storm begins after a night of clear skies and silence, bringing an extraordinary deluge of rain and shrieking wind. The narrator compares it favorably in awfulness with Mediterranean and Highland storms and takes it as evidence that the weather has broken.
