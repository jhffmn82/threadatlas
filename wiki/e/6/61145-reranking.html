Reranking is a retriever enhancement strategy and module applied after initial retrieval. It filters irrelevant documents and reorders top-ranked documents with more precise relevance scores, improving query-document similarity, document repacking, and generated-response quality. It can use BERT, T5, LLaMA, or TILDE; deep language models can improve performance but may require slow inference, while TILDE precomputes query-term likelihoods. Its contribution is assessed by removal: performance dropped without it, and monoT5 outperformed monoBERT and RankLLaMA among tested DLM-based rerankers.

Retriever Enhancement Strategy

Reranking is necessary because retrieved chunks can be irrelevant and their number can be large. Deep language models such as BERT, T5, and LLaMA can provide better performance but require slow inference steps, while TILDE improves efficiency by precomputing and storing the likelihood of query terms and ranking documents by their sum.

Reranking Methods

Reranking is applied after initial retrieval to improve the similarity between the query and the top-ranked documents.

Document Repacking

Reranking supplies the relevancy scores used to order documents during repacking. The repacking module operates after reranking.

RR is a standard cross-encoder reranking approach and filtering module for expanded retrieval candidates in retrieval-augmented generation for complex and multi-hop question answering. After passages are retrieved for decomposed sub-questions and merged into a candidate pool, it scores documents against the original complex query. By jointly encoding each query-document pair, it captures fine-grained query-document token interactions, estimates relevance more accurately than dual-encoder architectures, and sorts, filters, and distills the pool into highly relevant passages. This reduces noise, improves evidence coverage and precision, and promotes the best passages before answer generation. Its main tradeoff is increased computational cost and latency because the cross-encoder scores and sorts candidates. With bge-reranker-large after initial retrieval, RR complements question decomposition's broader coverage, improves Hits@10, MAP@10, and MRR@10 on MultiHop-RAG, and achieves supporting-fact EM of 19.6 and F1 of 12.9 on HotpotQA.
