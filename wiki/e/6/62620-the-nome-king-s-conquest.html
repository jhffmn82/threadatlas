Abstract written by a model

The conquest is Queen Ann and the Army of Oogaboo's attempted invasion of the Nome King to defeat the Metal Monarch, seize his treasure, and drive him from his dominions. Ruggedo summons the nome army; Queen Ann and officers are trapped and Tik-Tok is knocked down, so it fails, though several invaders escape and weaken Ruggedo's control.

Chapter Fourteen

The invaders are arguing about who will conquer the Metal Monarch, seize his treasure, and drive him from his dominions. Their planned conquest causes Ruggedo to summon the nome army against them.

Chapter Sixteen

The intended conquest of the Nome King by Queen Ann and the Army of Oogaboo does not succeed in this unit. Queen Ann and her officers are trapped, while Tik-Tok is knocked down before he can carry out the conquest.

Chapter Seventeen

Queen Ann's army attempts the conquest of the Nome King, while Shaggy Man expects the Love Magnet to make the conquest easy. Ruggedo initially believes he has defeated the invaders, but several of them escape and his control of the situation weakens.
