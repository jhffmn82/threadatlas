Files in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Jo Files is a young, intelligent, ambitious man whose orchard produces steel files and story-books. He owns a gun, loaded with bullets from the Oogaboo bullet-tree, and intends to obtain a musket from a gun-tree. Though he prefers war and carnage, he is cheerful in hardship and keeps his engagement to help Ann Soforth conquer the world. He volunteers as the sole Private in Ann's Army, refusing officer rank and becoming a feared, trusted adviser to Queen Ann. During the march, he identifies a possible Rak, shoots it, and leads the Queen away when the officers flee. After planting Oogaboo's banner and declaring conquest, he refuses to bind harmless girls as slaves, resigning from the Army of Oogaboo rather than obey. He later guides adventurers, travels with Ozga and Polychrome, becomes friends with the Rose Princess, and is captured with her; Ruggedo handcuffs him to prevent an attack.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Jo Files is a young, intelligent, ambitious man whose orchards produce steel files and story-books. He refuses the officer ranks and volunteers to be the sole Private in Ann's Army, claiming that he will do the fighting; he also knows where to find a gun-tree and plans to pick a musket.

Chapter Two

Files is the Private in the army and carries a gun, which he has picked. He looks fierce enough to make his commanding officers secretly afraid of him, and he begins the march after receiving the order passed down through the ranks.

Chapter Three

Private Files remains cheerful during the army's hardships and becomes a trusted adviser to Queen Ann. He recognizes the approaching cloud as possibly the breath of a Rak, shoots the creature with his gun, and leads the Queen away after the officers flee.

Chapter Eight

Private Files plants the banner of Oogaboo and announces the conquest, but he refuses to bind the girls because it would be impolite. He resigns rather than obey Queen Ann's order and later proposes making Tik-Tok the Army's private soldier.

Chapter Seventeen

Files follows Shaggy Man into the cavern and is captured with the Rose Princess. Ruggedo places golden handcuffs on Files to prevent an attack.
