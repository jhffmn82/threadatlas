Chapter Four

The shipwreck occurs when the ship strikes a rock during the storm. The ship catches fire, explodes, and sinks, leaving Betsy and Hank on a floating hatch-cover.

Chapter Five

Betsy tells the Roses that she and Hank were on a big ship when a storm wrecked it. They survived by catching hold of a raft and floating ashore.
