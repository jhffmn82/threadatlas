Pon's beating in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Pon is beaten terribly by two of the King's guards in the grape arbor as Gloria watches, restrained by King Krewl; Pon says the beating helped break his heart.

Chapter Ten

Two of King Krewl's guards seize Pon in the grape arbor and beat him terribly while Princess Gloria watches. King Krewl holds Gloria back so that she cannot interfere, and Pon says the beating helped break his heart.
