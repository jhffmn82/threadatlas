Abstract written by a model

An unknown pursuer follows Baskerville through town in hansom cab No. 2704, apparently concealing himself with a likely false bushy black beard. After detecting Holmes and Watson, he escapes, ending their chance to observe him.

The unknown pursuer follows Baskerville in a hansom cab, apparently so that he can loiter behind or move ahead without being noticed. He has a bushy black beard that Holmes suspects was false and escapes after detecting Holmes and Watson.
