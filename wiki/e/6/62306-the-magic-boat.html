the magic boat

Abstract written by a model

The magic boat is a large, roomy boat owned by Prince Inga. After a storm, it strands on Pingaree in perfect condition, as the White Pearl predicted. Lustrous black outside and lined with polished silver, it carries fresh water, food, shining oars, a canopy, and comfortable furnishings. Though too heavy for King Rinkitink to row, it moves rapidly when Inga rows, carrying Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil toward the Islands of Regos and Coregos.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

The White Pearl predicts that a boat will strand on the shore of Pingaree after the storm. Inga plans to take it and row to Regos and Coregos, although the boat has not yet appeared by the end of the unit.

Chapter Six

The magic boat appears on the shore of Pingaree in perfect condition after the storm. It is black on the outside and lined with silver inside, and contains water, food, oars, a canopy, and comfortable furnishings. It is too heavy for Rinkitink to row but moves rapidly when Inga takes the oars.
