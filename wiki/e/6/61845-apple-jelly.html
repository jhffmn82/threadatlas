THE MANUFACTURE OF APPLE JELLY.

Apple jelly emerges from the evaporator at about 30 to 32 degrees Baume, the consistency found suitable after cooling. It is mixed to equalize variations in concentration and flavor, then drawn hot into wooden buckets and other packages for shipment. Each bushel of fruit produces about four to five pounds of jelly; crab apples produce the finest jelly, sour natural fruit makes the best-looking article, and mixed varieties provide satisfactory flavor and quality.
