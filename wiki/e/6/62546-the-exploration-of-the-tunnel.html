Chapter Three

The Ork first discovers the opening to the tunnel and encourages Trot and Cap'n Bill to investigate it. Later, after the group reaches a precipice, he flies down to find a black lake and a tunnel continuing on the left. He then walks ahead through the passage to check for danger while the others follow.

Chapter Four

The journey through the tunnel lasts through the night, and Cap'n Bill, Trot, and the Ork resume it after sleeping. They pass through narrow, sharply turning passages before reaching the end and finding the shaft.
