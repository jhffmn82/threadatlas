Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

212 " SCHREIBER'S APPARATUS FOR REVIVIFYING BONE-BLACK. .. _The Animal Black Furnace_.

Bone-black loses its decolorizing properties when its pores become filled with absorbed salts and insoluble compounds from sugar refining. In the apparatus, it is washed, elevated while wet, dried, revivified in heated retorts, cooled, and discharged ready to be bagged and reused.
