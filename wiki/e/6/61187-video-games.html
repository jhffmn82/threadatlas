Ethical Considerations

The video games used to construct DQA describe historical situations. Their content can include knowledge that contradicts contemporary common sense or might be aggressive toward certain groups.

Simulators for annotation

Games motivate the use of simulators because applying every decision to real games is impossible due to randomness and because games are not open-sourced.
