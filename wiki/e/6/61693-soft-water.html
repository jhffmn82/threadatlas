APPARATUS FOR OBTAINING PURE WATER FOR PHOTOGRAPHIC USE.

Clean soft water is useful for photographic processes because developers remain effective longer and carbon printing can be carried out more rapidly or at a lower temperature. Soft water collected from dirty roofs, however, may contain substantial carbonaceous and organic contamination.
