Glasgow in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Glasgow is a place with the large, wealthy Royal Infirmary and a hospital and medical school that offered Elsie Inglis favourable clinical teaching. It became the setting for her training, examination preparation, and fight over mixed classes. She completed clinical work there before passing the Triple Qualification in 1892, compared its practice with London hospitals, and attended medical and political meetings while writing about women’s rights, Home Rule, labour, and local discussion.

Glasgow offered Elsie Inglis more favourable facilities for clinical teaching than Edinburgh. Its hospital and medical school became the setting for her training, examination preparation, and participation in the fight over mixed classes.

Glasgow was where Elsie completed her clinical work before passing the Triple Qualification in 1892. She compared its practice with London hospitals, particularly regarding students’ administration of anaesthetics.

Glasgow was where Elsie Inglis studied and practised medicine, attended political and medical meetings, and wrote to her father about women’s rights, Home Rule, labour questions, and local political discussion.
