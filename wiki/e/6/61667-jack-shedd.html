Jack Shedd, who had discovered the Robinson mine in Colorado, was prospecting near the south branch of the north fork of the Perche River on August 15 when he made the district's first great strike. He found surface pieces and large blocks of exceptionally rich silver ore, and his location, the Solitaire, later attracted the interest of ex-Governor Tabor and Mr. Wurtzbach.
