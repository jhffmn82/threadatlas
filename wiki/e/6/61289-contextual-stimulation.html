Contextual Stimulation in Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024

Contextual Stimulation is a Scenario 3 and INFO-RAG training task. It removes the target sentence from retrieved texts but retains semantically relevant neighboring sentences, requiring the model to generate the target continuation from context and its own knowledge. It makes up 40% of mixed-training batches, gains when trained separately, and helps produce the strongest result with Correct and Complete and Select and Copy.
