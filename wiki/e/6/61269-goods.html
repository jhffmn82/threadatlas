GOODS is a set of goods in simulator notation. Each good has a name, code, base_price, current_price, and pop_demand; demand combines pop_demand with connected Demand max_demand values, while supply sums connected current_output values. Different Building scenarios use GOODS when asking about decreasing market price.

GOODS is in one work on this site, PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
