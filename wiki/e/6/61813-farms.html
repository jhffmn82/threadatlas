Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

AN INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION.

The number of farms has virtually doubled within twenty years. Growth occurred through the division of great estates, the creation of farms in newly settled regions, and increased demand from cities and towns.
