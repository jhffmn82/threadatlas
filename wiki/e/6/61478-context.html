Abstract written by a model

In RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment, context is classified as helpful, irrelevant, or misleading and comprises grounding plus retrieved passages, or retrieved passages alone, for evaluation. In HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation, context is the set of retrieved passages supplied to the large language model with the query, expandable through traversal to at most (nhop + 1) × topk vertices before pruning. In Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability, context is what generated text is assessed against by AlignScore, although important contextual information can sometimes be embedded in the query.
