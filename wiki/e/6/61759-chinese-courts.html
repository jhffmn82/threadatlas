Abstract written by a model

Chinese courts require thumb-print signatures on confessions, using women’s right thumbs and men’s left; without the prisoner’s print, a confession is not legally valid for execution.

Chinese courts is in one work on this site, Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE CHINESE SIGN MANUAL.

Chinese courts require criminals to sign confessions by impressing the whorls of their thumb-tips. Women use the right thumb and men the left, and a confession is not legally valid without the prisoner's thumb-print.
