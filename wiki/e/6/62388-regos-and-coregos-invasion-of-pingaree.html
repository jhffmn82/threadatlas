Abstract written by a model

The Regos and Coregos invasion of Pingaree began when ten boatloads of warriors landed in northern Pingaree to plunder and conquer it. Pingaree’s people drove them back to sea during the Great Fight, where a storm destroyed the warriors and their boats, after the island was devastated.

Chapter One

Many years before the story begins, ten boatloads of warriors from Regos and Coregos landed on northern Pingaree to plunder and conquer it. The people of Pingaree drove them back to the sea, where a great storm destroyed them and their boats.

Chapter Four

The destruction of Pingaree reduces its palace and houses to ruins, with buildings torn down or burned and the island swept bare of its valuables. Prince Inga is deeply grieved by the loss of his home and country.

Abstract written by a model

The men of Regos and Coregos invade Pingaree from the north, landing warriors near King Kitticut's palace, seizing the royal household, capturing inhabitants, and destroying homes. The island is stripped of people and valuables; Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil escape capture.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

The invasion of Pingaree begins when hundreds of boats filled with fierce warriors land near King Kitticut's palace. The invaders quickly seize the royal household and then spread across the island, capturing inhabitants and destroying their homes.

Chapter Four

The invasion leaves Pingaree without its people, valuables, houses, or boats. Prince Inga survives in hiding, while King Rinkitink and Bilbil also escape capture.
