IMPROVED GRAPE BAGS.

Outdoor grape cultivation is described as much less successful than cultivation under glass, with only about two varieties commonly ripening outdoors and even those not always succeeding. The text states that improved culture and glass-fronted bags might make outdoor cultivation possible in higher latitudes and allow continental varieties to ripen.
