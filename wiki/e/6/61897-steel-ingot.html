SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

A steel ingot contains apparent and latent heat in excess of that required for rolling. After being stripped, it is placed in a heated soaking pit, where its internal heat is redistributed and its surface becomes hot enough for rolling without a conventional reheating operation.
