Select and Copy

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Select and Copy is a training task for Scenario 1 that uses English Wikipedia sentences as retrieved texts. It supplies all sentences in a set and trains the model to identify the sentence matching a prefix and copy its target continuation, addressing extraction of correct knowledge from complex texts. It forms 20% of batches because it is simple for LLMs, but alone causes overfitting and harms performance; with Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation, it contributes to the best result.

Select and Copy is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
