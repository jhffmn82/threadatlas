Select and Copy in Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Association for Computational Linguistics · 2024

Select and Copy is a training task for Scenario 1 that uses English Wikipedia sentences as retrieved texts. It supplies all sentences in a set and trains the model to identify the sentence matching a prefix and copy its target continuation, addressing extraction of correct knowledge from complex texts. It forms 20% of batches because it is simple for LLMs, but alone causes overfitting and harms performance; with Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation, it contributes to the best result.

Unsupervised Learning .. Training Strategy

Select and Copy supplies all sentences in a sentence set as retrieved texts and trains the model to identify the sentence matching the prefix and copy its target continuation. It addresses extraction of correct knowledge from complex retrieved texts.

Analysis / Fine-grained Analysis for Three Scenarios .. Avoid Catastrophic Forgetting Experiment on

Select and Copy is the training task associated with Scenario 1. When used alone, it has negative effects, but combined with Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation it contributes to the best reported result.
