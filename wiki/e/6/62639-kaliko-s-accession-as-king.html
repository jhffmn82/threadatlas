Kaliko's accession as King in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Kaliko is approved by Tititi-Hoochoo, welcomed by delighted nomes, made Metal Monarch and King by Quox, takes the throne, and appoints Guph Royal Chamberlain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

Quox makes Kaliko the Metal Monarch and King of the Nomes after Kaliko says he could not be worse than Ruggedo and that the nomes would obey him. Kaliko then takes the throne and appoints Guph as Royal Chamberlain.
