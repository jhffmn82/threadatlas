Abstract written by a model

A planned but adaptable journey through Cornwall began at Exeter on 1 September 1881, guided by “Hope for the best, and be prepared for the worst.” Its first day brought rail travel, fellow-traveller conversations, Falmouth, and preparations for the Lizard; we three completed it in fifteen days.
