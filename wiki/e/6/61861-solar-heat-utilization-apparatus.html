Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

UTILIZATION OF SOLAR HEAT.

The apparatus concentrates solar heat in a reflector that follows the sun and heats a vertical boiler, producing steam power. A portable version could boil about two and one-half quarts an hour, could be carried on a man's back, and was demonstrated operating a printing machine, pump, and other equipment.
