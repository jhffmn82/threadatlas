E5-Mistral is a triple ranker with the e5-mistral-7b-instruct Hugging Face checkpoint. Additional Implementation Details

E5-Mistral retrieves the top three most similar labelled documents as demonstrations for generating the KG for a document. It similarly retrieves the top three most similar labelled questions as demonstrations for the reasoning chain constructor.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

E5-Mistral retrieves the top three most similar questions and their corresponding reasoning chains for use as demonstrations when constructing reasoning chains for a given question.
