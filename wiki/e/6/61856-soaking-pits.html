Abstract written by a model

Soaking pits are upright, slightly oversized pits sunk below floor level in brickwork, lined with six-inch firebrick and covered by separate brick lids. Each holds one upright steel ingot. After placement, ingot gas fills the pit and excludes air; hot brick walls retain and equalize heat, acting as a heat accumulator for twenty to thirty minutes.

SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

Soaking pits are upright, slightly oversized pits built in brickwork below floor level and lined with firebrick. Each pit is covered after an ingot is placed inside; the hot brickwork retains and equalizes heat, while gas released by the ingot fills the pit and excludes atmospheric air.
