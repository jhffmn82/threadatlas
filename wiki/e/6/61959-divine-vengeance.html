divine vengeance

topic · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: God curse, a deadly wrong, avenged, requital, revenge, smite them, vengeance
  • In The Trojan Women of Euripides, divine vengeance is the punishment Pallas Athena and Poseidon plan against the Greeks for Ajax's offense and the destruction of sacred places, bringing ruin to the Greek fleet and a deadly, unsuccessful return voyage.
  • In The Fall of Troy, divine vengeance follows Oileus' son's outrage against Cassandra and Athena's temple, and powers punish the Achaeans through storm, shipwrecks, Aias's death, Nauplius's deception, and the erasure of the Achaean walls.
