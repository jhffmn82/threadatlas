Positive Information Gain

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: information gain, positive information gain, “Positive Information Gain”

Abstract written by a model

Positive Information Gain is an information-refinement process in RAG where an LLM produces text more concise, accurate, and complete than retrieved input. It improves the information bottleneck by enabling correct extraction from complex texts, through concise extraction, verification and completion, or semantic context that stimulates relevant knowledge.

Introduction

Positive Information Gain refers to the process in which an LLM consistently produces more concise, accurate, and complete text than the retrieved input. The unit presents this as a way to improve the information bottleneck of RAG and approximate the combined knowledge of information retrieval and LLMs.

Positive Information Gain in RAG

Positive Information Gain means making retrieved information more useful for answering a question. Depending on the scenario, it involves concise extraction, verification and completion, or using semantic context to stimulate relevant knowledge.
