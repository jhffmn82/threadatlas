SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The heating furnace is the previously universal means of reheating steel ingots before rolling. The soaking pit process is presented as a way to dispense with such furnaces, the fuel they consume, their gas generators, their maintenance, and losses caused by oxidation or overheating.
