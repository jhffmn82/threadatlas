The Devil's Frying-pan near Cadgwith is a two-acre natural amphitheatre enclosed by a high semicircular cliff. It opens to the sea through a serpentine arch, where waves reach a shingly beach amid jagged rocks and flying seabirds.

The Devil's Frying-pan near Cadgwith is a natural amphitheatre about two acres in extent, enclosed by a high semicircular cliff. The sea enters its shingly beach through a serpentine arch, and the women travelers view its waves, jagged rocks, and flying seabirds from the grassy slope.
