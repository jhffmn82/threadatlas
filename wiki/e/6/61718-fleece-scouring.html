] SOAP AND ITS MANUFACTURE, FROM A CONSUMER'S POINT OF VIEW.

Fleece scouring involves washing wool while it is still growing on the sheep with potash soap made from waste tallow available on sheep farms. The article says it could benefit sheep owners by improving the condition of their wool and cleaning the sheep's skin.
