Farm owners or occupants, who own or rent and cultivate farms, increased by 205,000 in six states over a decade and stood differently from wage laborers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

AN INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION.

There were 205,000 more farm owners or managers in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Indiana, and Illinois than a decade earlier. Farm owners or occupants are described as standing on a different footing from wage laborers.
