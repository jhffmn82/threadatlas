PlanRAG in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where PlanRAG first appears

Abstract written by a model

PlanRAG is a proposed iterative plan-then-retrieval augmented generation methodology and RAG technique for Decision QA, designed for generative large language models as decision makers. Its central feature is that planning precedes retrieval: it examines the data schema and question to create a decision-making plan, then generates and poses queries to retrieve and analyze relevant data. A single language model carries out two types of reasoning: planning, and retrieving and answering. The process unfolds iteratively through planning, retrieving and answering, and re-planning. Retrieval continues until no further analysis is needed; if the plan, retrieved results, or initial plan are insufficient, PlanRAG re-plans and repeats the process before producing a decision answer. Experiments report that it outperforms compared RAG techniques and significantly outperforms SOTA iterative RAG on Decision QA, especially in the Locating scenario. It was implemented with a single LM, while effectiveness in a multiple-LM framework was not evaluated and is left for further study.

PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

PlanRAG is the subject of the work titled “PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers.”

Abstract

PlanRAG is a proposed iterative plan-then-retrieval augmented generation technique for addressing Decision QA. It first generates a decision-making plan and then uses retrieval to generate queries for data analysis.

Introduction

PlanRAG is an iterative plan-then-retrieval augmented generation technique proposed for Decision QA. It plans the required analysis from the schema and question, retrieves data through generated queries, re-plans when necessary, and then answers; the reported experiments find it more effective than iterative RAG.

Methodology: PlanRAG

PlanRAG is an iterative plan-then-retrieval augmented generation technique for Decision QA. It combines planning with retrieval-based answering and can re-plan when the current plan is insufficient.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

PlanRAG is evaluated as a decision-making approach that first creates a plan, retrieves and analyzes data according to that plan, and re-plans when the plan or results are insufficient. It outperforms the compared RAG techniques, especially in the Locating scenario.

Conclusions

PlanRAG is a proposed RAG technique that performs planning before retrieving and re-planning when the initial plan is not good enough. Experiments show that it significantly outperforms SOTA iterative RAG on the Decision QA task.

Limitations

The authors designed their PlanRAG methodology for Decision QA and implemented it using a single LM. Its effectiveness in a multiple-LM framework is left for further study.
