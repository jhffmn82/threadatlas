Egyptian mysteries in Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882

Various · 1881

Egyptian mysteries are initiation rites described by Abbe Terrason in Sethos, possibly involving a fire-traversal trial. The account considers crossing a burning chamber on a grate above a ditch fire, allowing passage without flames reaching the initiate.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

The unit considers whether initiation rites connected with the Egyptian mysteries may have involved crossing a burning chamber over an incandescent grate. It suggests that the account in the Abbe Terrason's "Sethos" may be better explained by a fire in a ditch beneath a grate, which would prevent flames from reaching the person crossing it.
