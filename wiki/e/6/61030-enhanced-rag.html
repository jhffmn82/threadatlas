Related Works / Retrieval Augmented Generation .. Enhanced RAG

Enhanced RAG includes methods that improve retrieval effectiveness through retriever supervision, document truncation, selection, evaluation, re-ranking, and compression. These methods can reduce noise and complexity, but may add inference costs or remove essential information.

Experiments / Datasets and Metrics .. Discussions

Enhanced RAG methods include CoT, RECOMP, CRAG, Self-RAG, LongLLMLingua, and RAFT. These methods are categorized as frozen or fine-tuned according to whether they require training the LLMs.
