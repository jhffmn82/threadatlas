retrieves_from Wikipedia [full Wikipedia at each reasoning step] In the open-retrieval setting, passages are retrieved from the full Wikipedia at each reasoning step. INFO-RAG is combined with SearChain in this setting and improves results on Multi-Hop Question Answering and Soft Filling.
