] A CHARACTERISTIC MINING "RUSH.

Ex-Governor Tabor was reported to have bought the Solitaire from Jack Shedd for $100,000 on August 25, but that report was described as untrue. His representative had examined the properties, and the Governor had apparently bonded the mine while viewing it favorably.
