Chapter Seventeen

The prisoners are King Kitticut and Queen Garee, whom King Gos delivers to Kaliko for safekeeping. Although Kaliko knows Gos's accusations are false, he confines them because Gos is stronger and offers valuable treasure. They are placed in a small cavern and remain captives after their chains are removed.
