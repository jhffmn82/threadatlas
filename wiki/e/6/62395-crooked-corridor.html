L. Frank Baum · 1916

A crooked corridor leading to the last cavern, with stepping-stones made from loosened wall rocks and many cracked projections; Inga traverses it after relief, unable to gain enough run to leap back over the second cave’s gulf.

Only pausing to draw one long breath of relief, Inga quickly traversed

The crooked corridor leads to the last cavern and contains loose, cracked rocks in its walls. Its shape prevents Inga from getting enough of a run to leap back over the gulf of the second cave.
