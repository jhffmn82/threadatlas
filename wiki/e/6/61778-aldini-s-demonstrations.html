Abstract written by a model

In Paris, before the Academic des Sciences, Aldini's demonstrations showed protective apparatus: a finger wrapped in asbestos and double wire gauze endured flame, while assistants handled hot iron, carried firebrands, and entered intense flames.

ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

Aldini's demonstrations began with holding a finger wrapped in asbestos and double wire gauze in a candle or alcohol flame. Assistants also handled red-hot iron, carried firebrands over burning fagots, and entered intense flames while wearing the protective apparatus.
