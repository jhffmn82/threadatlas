A multiple-choice question training instance contains a question stem, an answer option, and distractors. Its answer and text are used in Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Augmented Generation. For task-specific pretraining, a sentence containing an MCQ answer is retrieved, the answer option is replaced with [Mask], and the resulting pseudo question is paired with the original distractors.

Introduction

Pseudo questions are created by retrieving a sentence containing an MCQ answer and replacing that answer with the [Mask] token. The pseudo question is then paired with the original distractors for task-specific pretraining.
