Chapter Five

The shed is an open shelter made from a roof of boughs and branches fastened to the sides, facing the sea. Trot and Cap'n Bill take refuge there from the rain, and they later make beds of leaves inside it while Pessim sleeps in a hammock between two trees. The shed has no furniture except Pessim's stool, which he calls his throne.
