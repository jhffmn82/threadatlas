ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

A well-constructed condensing engine is generally economically preferable when sufficient condensing water is available at moderate cost. Its greater economy is said to justify the additional purchase cost except in very small-power cases or where exhaust steam from a non-condensing engine can be used for heating or drying.
