Kingdom of Rinkitink in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Kingdom near the Nonestic Ocean, separated from Land of Oz by the Nome King’s country and Sandy Desert, prospers through Gilgad’s trade with Pingaree, exchanging supplies and merchandise for pearls.

Chapter One

The Kingdom of Rinkitink lies near the Nonestic Ocean and gains wealth through coastal and island trade. Its city of Gilgad purchases Pingaree's pearls and sends the island food, supplies, and rich merchandise in return.
