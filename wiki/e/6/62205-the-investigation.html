Abstract written by a model

The investigation seeks proof linking a man to the hound threatening Sir Henry Baskerville. Holmes pursues hotel records, missing boots, Barrymore, the will, and a spy’s cabman, but loses several leads and awaits Watson in Devonshire. It seems to fail after a scream and an apparent killing, until the victim is identified as Selden and clothing links the hound to Sir Henry.

Chapter 5.

The investigation follows several leads: the hotel register, the missing boots, Barrymore's location, the financial interests in Sir Charles Baskerville's will, and the cabman who drove the spy. Holmes loses the leads concerning the hotel watchers, the newspaper clipping, and the cabman, leaving him to hope that the investigation will progress when Watson reaches Devonshire.

A terrible scream—a prolonged yell of horror and anguish—burst

The investigation appears to have failed when Holmes and Dr. John Watson initially believe that Sir Henry Baskerville has been killed. After identifying Selden, Holmes recognizes that the clothing provides a connection between the hound and Sir Henry, but he continues to insist that the case requires proof rather than conjecture.
