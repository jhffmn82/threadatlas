SOTA iterative RAG is an iterative retrieval/RAG technique for complex problems requiring further retrievals, mainly knowledge-based QA rather than decision-making QA. Retrieved results drive subsequent retrievals. Used as the basis for IterRAG-LM and as an experimental comparison method, it is surpassed by PlanRAG-LM on DQA, including Locating and Building.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Iterative RAG is an approach for complex problems in which retrieved results are used to perform further retrievals. The unit compares an iterative RAG-based language model with a PlanRAG-based language model on DQA.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

Iterative RAG is a previous retrieval approach used as the basis for IterRAG-LM. PlanRAG-LM improves over it by 15.8% in Locating and 7.4% in Building.

Conclusions

SOTA iterative RAG is used as a comparison method in the experiments. PlanRAG significantly outperforms it on the Decision QA task.
