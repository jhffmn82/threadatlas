computational complexity in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

Computational complexity for constructing reasoning chains is O(N + LR) without adaptive chain termination. It depends on document count N and chain length L with relevant documents R; as L and R grow, LR usually dominates N. Adaptive termination can improve actual efficiency.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

A

The computational complexity of constructing the reasoning chains is O(N + LR) without adaptive chain termination. The LR term tends to dominate N when the reasoning-chain length L and the number of relevant documents R are relatively large, while adaptive termination can further improve actual efficiency.
