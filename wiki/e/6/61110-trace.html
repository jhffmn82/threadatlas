TRACE-Triple in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

TRACE-Triple is a method for multi-hop question answering that enhances multi-hop reasoning for RAG models. It begins with question-associated retrieved documents, from which a knowledge graph generator creates a knowledge graph containing supporting evidence. An autoregressive reasoning chain constructor, using demonstrations by default, builds adaptive-length reasoning chains from that graph. These chains guide triple ranking and selection and identify a subset of relevant retrieved documents for answering. TRACE-Triple has a knowledge graph generator, reasoning chain constructor, triple ranker, and triple selector; it uses three reasoning chains by default. Across the HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue development sets, it outperforms IRCoT in reported EM and F1 results and achieves up to a 14.03% average maximum EM improvement over using all retrieved documents. Its performance initially improves as the number of reasoning chains increases, then stabilizes; removing demonstrations or using fixed demonstrations decreases performance. Adaptive-length chains significantly outperform fixed-length chains, while results also depend on the graph generator, constructor, ranker, selector, maximum chain length, and number of candidate triples.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

TRACE builds reasoning chains from question-associated documents to identify and integrate supporting evidence for multi-hop QA. Its TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc variants consistently outperform the reported baselines, while its performance depends on the knowledge-graph generator, chain constructor, triple ranker, triple selector, maximum chain length, and number of candidate triples.

Conclusion

TRACE enhances the multi-hop reasoning ability of RAG models by generating a knowledge graph from retrieved documents and constructing reasoning chains from it. It achieves an average performance improvement of up to 14.03% in EM on three multi-hop QA datasets compared with using all retrieved documents.

Additional Experimental Results and .. MuSiQue

With Mistral-7B-v0.1 as reader, TRACE-Triple achieves 50.68 EM and 65.49 F1 on HotPotQA, 46.30 EM and 55.49 F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 31.86 EM and 40.05 F1 on MuSiQue. It outperforms IRCoT on all three datasets under these reported metrics.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

TRACE-Triple uses a reasoning chain constructor with demonstrations by default and is evaluated using Gemma-7B as a reader. Its performance decreases when demonstrations are removed or generally when fixed demonstrations are used, and it initially improves as the number of reasoning chains increases before stabilizing.

Effects of Adaptive Chain Termination

TRACE-Triple is evaluated on three multi-hop QA datasets in the comparison between adaptive-length and fixed-length reasoning chains. Its adaptive-length version significantly outperforms its fixed-length counterpart.

TRACE-Doc in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

TRACE-Doc is a TRACE variant for multi-hop QA that uses adaptive-length reasoning chains to identify useful documents. It retrieves documents through triples in those chains and uses majority voting to order them. The identified documents contain significantly less noise than those for a vanilla RAG model, and TRACE-Doc achieves superior performance, including an average 13.46% Exact Match improvement over using all retrieved documents. On development sets, it reaches 69.20 EM/78.46 F1 on HotPotQA, 68.00/75.96 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 41.00/47.09 on MuSiQue. With a Mistral-7B-v0.1 reader, its reported results are lower across the three datasets. Ablations show that the KG generator, reasoning-chain constructor, triple ranker, and triple selector are important for retrieving relevant documents. As the number of reasoning chains increases, performance first improves and then stabilizes; adaptive chains substantially outperform fixed-length chains.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Baselines

TRACE-Doc is evaluated on HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue. Its development-set performance is 69.20/78.46 on HotPotQA, 68.00/75.96 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 41.00/47.09 on MuSiQue for EM/F1.

Statistics and Analyses of the Generated

TRACE-Doc uses documents identified through reasoning chains and achieves superior performance to the vanilla RAG model. The identified documents contain significantly less noise, which helps explain this performance.

Ablation studies find that the KG generator, reasoning chain constructor, triple ranker, and triple selector are important to TRACE-Doc's effectiveness in using reasoning chains to retrieve relevant documents. With Mistral-7B-v0.1 as reader, TRACE-Doc achieves 53.37 EM and 67.41 F1 on HotPotQA, 44.90 EM and 54.32 F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 30.66 EM and 38.22 F1 on MuSiQue.
