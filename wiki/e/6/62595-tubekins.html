Tubekins

Also called: Peculiar Person, man, the King of the Tube, their guide

Abstract written by a model

Tubekins is a beautiful but expressionless King of the Tube, with large blue eyes, bushy black hair, multicolored skin, and a scarlet dragon-embroidered robe. He directs invisible inhabitants and treats the Tube as private property. While hoeing pink violets, he is struck by Queen Ann’s landing and crown, then questions the travelers. Later he escorts the adventurers to the Forbidden Tube, orders them inside, waits for Quox, and remains behind as their journey begins.

Tubekins is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

A Peculiar Person is hoeing pink violets in a garden when the travelers emerge. Queen Ann lands on his head and smashes a jeweled crown over his eyes; after the officers restrain him, he questions the travelers and identifies the Tube as private property.

Chapter Thirteen

Tubekins escorts the adventurers to the mouth of the Forbidden Tube and orders them to enter. He waits for Quox, warns that the dragon is tiring company, and then remains behind as the journey begins.
