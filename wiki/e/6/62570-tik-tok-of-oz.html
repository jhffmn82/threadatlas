Tik-Tok of Oz

Abstract written by a model

The document is a fantasy adventure centered on two converging quests: Queen Ann Soforth of Oogaboo’s comic attempt to conquer the world and Shaggy Man’s effort to rescue his lost brother, the Ugly One, from Ruggedo, the Nome King. Ann raises Oogaboo’s eighteen men into an overranked army, with Jo Files as its sole Private, and marches toward Oz; Glinda secretly diverts them away from Oz. Meanwhile, Betsy Bobbin and her mule Hank survive a shipwreck, are expelled from the Rose Kingdom with Shaggy Man and Princess Ozga, and join Polychrome and Tik-Tok in searching for Shaggy’s imprisoned brother. The two parties meet when Ann tries to conquer the travelers. Hank scatters her soldiers, and Shaggy’s Love Magnet makes them friends. They unite against Ruggedo, who diverts them through the forbidden Hollow Tube. On the far side of the world, Tititi-Hoochoo judges them innocent, appoints the dragon Quox to punish Ruggedo, and sends the group back through the Tube. Ruggedo resists their return, captures or transforms several companions, turns Shaggy Man into a dove, and threatens Tik-Tok. Polychrome awakens Quox, whose enchanted ribbon strips Ruggedo of his magic. Quox drives Ruggedo from the Nome Kingdom and makes Kaliko the new Nome King. The companions then seek the Ugly One in the Metal Forest. Queen Ann and her officers reappear there, Ruggedo is captured, and Shaggy finds his brother, whose ugliness resulted from Ruggedo’s enchantment. Polychrome’s fairy kiss restores him. Ruggedo repents and is allowed to remain as a harmless common nome. Ann abandons conquest and is returned to Oogaboo with her people and the Rose Princess. Ozma has the Wizard bring Betsy, Hank, Shaggy Man, his brother, and Tik-Tok to Oz. The story ends with Hank discovering he can speak in Oz, Ozma reconciling animals arguing over the sweetest girl, and Betsy happily wishing all children could live in Oz.

Tik-Tok of Oz is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Tik-Tok of Oz in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where Tik-Tok of Oz first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

25 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Ann Soforth, Queen of Oogaboo, becomes dissatisfied with her small kingdom and decides to conquer the world, beginning with the Land of Oz. Salye's taunt inspires Ann to raise an army of Oogaboo's eighteen men, most of whom she assigns high military ranks. Jo Candy refuses to join, while Jo Files agrees to serve as the sole Private and promises to find a gun-tree. Ann's Army is assembled with four Generals, four Colonels, four Majors, four Captains, two prospective Lieutenants, and Jo Files as Private, though only sixteen men have joined.

Chapter Two

Three days after Queen Ann Soforth's decision to conquer the world, the Grand Army of Oogaboo assembles outside the royal palace. Ann orders the protesting women to be silent and leads her sixteen officers and the Private, Files, in the army's departure. The officers and Files pass orders down the ranks until Files begins marching, followed by the officers and then Queen Ann. The procession leaves Oogaboo through a narrow mountain pass toward Fairyland of Oz, seeking plunder and fame.

Chapter Three

Princess Ozma is unaware that Ann Soforth and the Army of Oogaboo intend to invade and conquer the Land of Oz. Glinda the Good learns of the plan through her Magic Record Book and uses magic to redirect the mountain pass, sending the army into an adjoining territory instead of Oz. Lost and hungry, the army encounters a Rak, which Private Files wounds with his gun before leading Queen Ann away from it. The army later reaches a valley, camps beside a brook, and continues to claim that it has begun the conquest of the world.

Chapter Four

Betsy Bobbin and Hank, a thin, sad-faced mule, are thrown into the sea when their ship strikes a rock during a storm. Betsy grabs Hank's tail, then helps him climb onto a floating hatch-cover that becomes their raft after the ship catches fire, explodes, and sinks. They endure the night on the raft, and Hank stays awake to guard Betsy while she sleeps. At dawn, they see a beautiful, apparently uninhabited land ahead.

Chapter Five

Betsy and Hank reach shore on a raft and discover a greenhouse in the Rose Kingdom. Inside, they find rosebushes bearing Roses with girls’ faces, who are frightened by Hank’s braying and demand that the refugees leave. The Roses explain that only Roses are allowed in the kingdom and that the Royal Gardener may cast strangers back into the sea. When the Royal Gardener arrives, he panics at Hank, invokes the Law of the Rose Kingdom, and is interrupted when a man crashes through the greenhouse roof.

Chapter Six

Shaggy Man falls into the Rose Kingdom while searching for his lost brother, and he, Betsy, and Hank are threatened with death for entering the kingdom. Shaggy uses the Love Magnet to win the Royal Gardener's affection, but it has no effect on the Roses because they have no hearts. Betsy and Shaggy pick the ripe Princess Ozga from the Royal Gardens, but the Roses reject her because they want a male Ruler. The Gardener and the Roses drive Shaggy, Betsy, Hank, and Ozga out of the Rose Kingdom, after which they agree to travel together in search of Shaggy Man's lost brother and the Nome King's cavern.

Chapter Seven

Polychrome becomes stranded on earth when the Rainbow rises and disappears before she can climb back onto it. She joins Betsy Bobbin, Hank, the Princess, and Shaggy Man as they search for Shaggy Man's brother, who is imprisoned by Ruggedo. At an old well, the travelers recover Tik-Tok, who explains that Ruggedo threw him there after he was sent from Oz to help the search. The reunited party prepares to choose a road toward Ruggedo's hidden cavern when an army approaches.

Chapter Eight

Queen Ann Soforth and the Army of Oogaboo arrive to conquer Shaggy Man and his companions, but Hank scatters the officers and Private Files refuses to bind the girls. Shaggy uses the Love Magnet to turn the Oogabooites into his friends and proposes that they help conquer Ruggedo and rescue Shaggy's brother. The officers make Tik-Tok their private soldier, and he accepts after being promised a share of the plunder. Princess Ozga asks the flowers for directions, and they indicate the road to Ruggedo's underground kingdom.

Chapter Nine

The adventurers approach Ruggedo's Nome Kingdom, crossing the Rubber Country and overcoming its strange rubber terrain and dry water. Ruggedo learns through Kaliko and the Long-Eared Hearer that Shaggy Man seeks the Ugly One and that the Army of Oogaboo plans to seize the kingdom's metals and jewels. Enraged, Ruggedo orders Kaliko and the Chief Magician to redirect the path toward the invisible Hollow Tube, despite warnings that this will anger Tititi-Hoochoo. Kaliko obeys but privately prepares letters of recommendation because he believes Ruggedo has ruined himself.

Chapter Ten

Polychrome, Queen Ann, the Army of Oogaboo, Betsy Bobbin, Hank, Shaggy Man, Tik-Tok, Ozga, and Files are drawn into the Hollow Tube while traveling through Ruggedo's cavern. They slide for more than an hour through darkness and emerge on the opposite side of the world, where Tik-Tok falls into a fountain and Queen Ann collides with a Peculiar Person. Tubekins, a strange multicolored inhabitant, orders them to the Residence after learning that they intended to conquer Ruggedo. Invisible inhabitants assist and threaten the travelers, who finally follow Tubekins toward the castle.

Chapter Eleven

Betsy, Polychrome, and the rest of the party are brought before the Private Citizen, ruler of a land where all Kings and Queens are equal. Tik-Tok explains that Ruggedo forced them through the Forbidden Tube while they were seeking to free Shaggy's lost brother. The Private Citizen decides that the party is not guilty and orders that Ruggedo alone be punished, though he has not yet determined how. The travelers are assigned as guests to various palaces until they return to the Residence the next day.

Chapter Twelve

Erma, the Queen of Light, welcomes Betsy and Polychrome to her crystal palace and introduces them to the six maidens who represent different kinds of light. She explains that the land is inhabited by important fairies and is ruled by Tititi-Hoochoo, a perfectly just Jinjin who has no heart but possesses exceptional Reason and Justice. Erma also tells them about the Original Dragon, the oldest and wisest living creature, whose emblem the inhabitants wear. After a lavish feast, Betsy is shown to a chamber by Electra and falls asleep.

Chapter Thirteen

Tititi-Hoochoo declares that the adventurers are innocent of entering the Forbidden Tube and orders them to return to their own side of the world. He selects Quox as his Instrument of Vengeance and sends him to punish Ruggedo by driving him from his caverns and taking away his magic powers. Tubekins escorts the adventurers to the Tube, where they mount Quox and begin a slower, more comfortable return journey. During the descent, Quox explains his age, dragon-fire, and mission while Queen Ann, Tik-Tok, Betsy, and the others discuss who will conquer Ruggedo.

Chapter Fourteen

Ruggedo learns from the Long-Eared Hearer that the invaders he threw down the Hollow Tube are returning to conquer the Metal Monarch and seize his treasure. Kaliko uses the Magic Spyglass and sees a dragon carrying the invaders through the Tube, but no eggs. Ruggedo rejects Kaliko's advice to release the Shaggy Man's brother and instead orders General Guph to prepare the nome army to seize the travelers. The Long-Eared Hearer loses the tip of one ear when Ruggedo's thrown sceptre passes close to him.

Chapter Fifteen

Quox carries Shaggy Man, Betsy, Tik-Tok, Queen Ann, and the others through the Tube, and they discuss the dragon and the effects of gravity during the journey. Quox emerges into the open air above the Nome King's underground caverns and lands on a mountain, avoiding the army of nomes gathered at the Tube's entrance. General Guph orders the nomes to attack, but Quox shields the travelers from their magic spears. The Army of Oogaboo advances, the nomes retreat into the caverns, and Queen Ann decides that the invaders must enter the Nome King's underground kingdom.

Chapter Sixteen

Queen Ann So-forth leads the Army of Oogaboo into Ruggedo's Kingdom to conquer the Nome King, but she and her officers fall into a concealed pit. Tik-Tok reaches the Nome King's cavern and challenges Ruggedo, but Kaliko knocks him down while Hank's kick sends General Guph into Ruggedo. Betsy Bobbin enters the cavern, receives food and shelter from Kaliko, and is hidden in his room. Queen Ann and the officers later discover a secret escape passage and crawl through it while Shaggy Man and the others wait outside.

Chapter Seventeen

Ruggedo learns that Shaggy Man intends to use the Love Magnet, so he orders nomes to bind Shaggy Man before he can display it. Polychrome cannot awaken Quox, but she returns to the cavern and repeatedly evades Ruggedo and Guph when they try to seize her. Ruggedo discovers that Queen Ann and the Oogaboo people, as well as Betsy Bobbin and Hank, have escaped, and he orders Tik-Tok to be melted in a gold crucible. He then transforms Shaggy Man into a dove, leaving the Rose Princess to protect it while Polychrome goes to seek Quox again.

Chapter Eighteen

Polychrome awakens Quox and tells him that Ruggedo has captured their friends and plans to destroy them. Quox enters Ruggedo's jeweled cavern, where a chain traps him, but the enchanted ribbon around his neck causes Ruggedo to lose all magical powers. Quox then announces Tititi-Hoochoo's decree that Ruggedo must leave the Nome Kingdom, and enchanted eggs drive Ruggedo from his cavern. Ruggedo becomes a penniless wanderer on the earth's surface, unable to regain his kingdom.

Chapter Nineteen

Quox restores the Rose Princess and Shaggy Man after Ruggedo flees, then frees himself from his chains and helps reunite the companions with Betsy Bobbin and Hank. Quox appoints Kaliko as the new King of the Nomes because Kaliko believes the nomes will obey him and he cannot rule worse than Ruggedo. Kaliko learns that Shaggy Man's brother, called the Ugly One, is confined in the Metal Forest, and the group plans to search for a secret passage to rescue him. Kaliko begins his reign by appointing Guph as Royal Chamberlain, while the mystery of Queen Ann and the Officers of Oogaboo's disappearance remains unexplained.

Chapter Twenty

Kaliko becomes King and directs the nomes to search for the hidden passages to the Metal Forest, while Quox returns through the Tube to his own country. Ruggedo, now a homeless wanderer, enlarges his robe with pockets and secretly discovers the passage to the Metal Forest. Polychrome observes Ruggedo and leads Kaliko, Shaggy, Betsy, and the nomes after him. They reach the marvelous Metal Forest, where they hear a scream and a struggle, suggesting they may have found Shaggy's brother, the Ugly One.

Chapter Twenty-One

Queen Ann and the officers of Oogaboo escape through a difficult rocky tunnel into the Metal Forest, where they capture Ruggedo. Kaliko explains that Ruggedo has been deposed and allows him to fill his pockets with jewels before leaving. Shaggy Man finds his long-lost brother, the Ugly One, hiding in a hut because Ruggedo's enchantment made him hideously ugly. The Ugly One comes out wearing a red polka-dotted handkerchief as a mask, but he remains afraid to return to the world.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Betsy, Shaggy Man, King Kaliko, Ruggedo, Polychrome, and their companions leave the Metal Forest after rescuing Shaggy Man's brother, the Ugly One. Ruggedo explains that one of three kinds of kisses might break the charm of ugliness, so Betsy, the Rose Princess, and Polychrome each kiss him in the dark passage. Only Polychrome's kiss, as a Fairy, restores Shaggy's brother's original appearance. The group then leaves the passage and prepares to return to the outside world.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Ruggedo follows the others back to the Nome King's royal cavern, where Betsy gives him food and asks Kaliko to let him stay. Ruggedo admits that he is sorry for his past behavior and promises to live harmlessly as a common nome, and Kaliko agrees to keep him if he behaves. Polychrome leaves the Nome Kingdom by riding a Rainbow back to her sisters in the sky. Betsy, Hank, Shaggy, and Shaggy's brother return sadly to the cavern after promising to remain together.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Queen Ann abandons her attempt to conquer the world and wishes to return to Oogaboo, so Ozma has the Wizard transport Ann and her people home, along with the Rose Princess. Shaggy Man remains with Betsy Bobbin, Hank, and his newly recovered brother after Tik-Tok is returned to the Land of Oz. Ozma, Dorothy, the Wizard of Oz, and Tik-Tok decide that Shaggy Man's group should be admitted to Oz, and the Wizard magically transports them there. Dorothy eagerly goes to welcome Betsy, while Shaggy Man weeps with joy at being brought to Oz.

Chapter Twenty-Five

Hank settles into Ozma's palace stable and discovers that he can speak in the Land of Oz. The Sawhorse, Cowardly Lion, and Hungry Tiger argue with Hank over which girl is sweetest, but Ozma teaches them that friendship matters more than rivalry and reconciles them. Dorothy discovers that Toto can speak words but prefers to communicate through barks and gestures. During a garden walk, Betsy Bobbin expresses her happiness in Oz and wishes that all children could live there.
