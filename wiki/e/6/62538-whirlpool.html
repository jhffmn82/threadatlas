whirlpool in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

A whirlpool in the sea begins when a whirl in the air creates a powerful swirl in the water. It slopes the ocean into a great basin with a black central hole, drawing a boat around its edge and inward. It captures the Ork and carries Trot and Cap'n Bill into a cavern.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Great Whirlpool forms when a whirl in the air creates a powerful swirl in the water. It makes the ocean slope downward into a basin with a large hole at the center, drawing the boat around its edge and toward the hole.

Chapter Three

The whirlpool captures the Ork when he flies too close and its air suction pulls him into the ocean depths. It also carries Trot and Cap'n Bill into the cavern where they meet the Ork.
