Abstract written by a model

The Orks' rescue and conquest of Jinxland is the overthrow of King Krewl and establishment of new rule. Planned by the Scarecrow to install Princess Gloria as Queen, it begins when he goes alone toward Krewl's castle. Fearing for him and seeking to punish Krewl, the Ork joins. The Orks rescue the Scarecrow from attempted burning, defeat Krewl, Googly-Goo, and the soldiers, and crown the Scarecrow. Gloria becomes Queen, Krewl's rule ends, and he is brought in chains before the new rulers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Sixteen

The Scarecrow announces that the group will conquer Jinxland and install Princess Gloria as Queen. After the failed summons and Pon's beating, the Scarecrow begins the conquest by going alone toward the King's castle.

Chapter Seventeen

The Scarecrow has gone alone to conquer King Krewl. After hearing this, the Ork decides to take part in the conquest because it fears for the Scarecrow and wants to punish King Krewl.

Chapter Eighteen

The Orks rescue the Scarecrow from the attempted burning and defeat King Krewl, Googly-Goo, and the soldiers. They place Krewl's crown on the Scarecrow, who takes the throne as the people and soldiers cheer.

Chapter Twenty

The conquest of King Krewl makes the Scarecrow popular with the people of Jinxland and ends Krewl's rule. Afterward, Krewl is brought in chains before the new Queen and King.
