Abstract written by a model

Mullion Cove, in Cornwall, is reached by a rough road and steep descent; it has a beach, grand serpentine rocks, offshore Mullion Island, and two sea-caves visited before travelers return to the Old Inn.

Mullion Cove is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
