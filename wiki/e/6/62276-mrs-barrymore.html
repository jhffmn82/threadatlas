Abstract written by a model

Mrs. Barrymore, also called Eliza Barrymore, is John Barrymore’s wife and a servant at Baskerville Hall. Though described as heavy, solid, puritanical, and respectable, she repeatedly shows deep sorrow through tears. The mystery of her distress ends when she reveals that Selden is her younger brother and that she asked John to help feed and protect him on the moor.

Mrs. Barrymore is in one work on this site, The Hound of the Baskervilles. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Mrs. Barrymore is described as heavy, solid, intensely respectable, and puritanical. Despite her unemotional appearance, she has repeatedly been seen with traces of tears, suggesting a deep sorrow or troubling secret.
