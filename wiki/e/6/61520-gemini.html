Gemini-1.5-Pro in Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?

Zhuowan Li · 2024-11-12/2024-11-16

Gemini-1.5-Pro is a recent long-context LLM from Google and one of the models evaluated against retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and SELF-ROUTE. It supports up to 1 million tokens. In reported comparisons, long-context processing has a substantial average advantage over RAG, including a 7.6% average difference; its long-context performance is 45.53 under a passage-only prompt. SELF-ROUTE performs slightly below long-context processing but well above RAG, cuts cost by 65%, uses about 38% of long-context tokens, and routes many queries to RAG. It scores 23.58 on MuSiQue using internal knowledge only.
