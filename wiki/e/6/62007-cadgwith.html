Cadgwith in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Cadgwith is a fishing village in a sheltered coastal bend, protected from the dense fogs of the Lizard. Described as pleasant in summer and winter, it has a small fishing station, village inn, mild climate suitable for invalids, and the nearby Devil's Frying-pan as its principal attraction.

Cadgwith lies in a sheltered bend of the coast and is described as one of the pleasantest nooks in summer and winter. It has a small fishing station, a comfortable village inn, a mild climate, and the nearby Devil's Frying-pan.
