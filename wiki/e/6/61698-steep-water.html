Abstract written by a model

Steep water is water in which malt has been steeped. It contains 1.08 percent total dry matter: 0.56 percent organic matter and 0.52 percent mineral matter, plus 0.033 percent nitrogen. Its mineral matter includes potash, phosphoric acid, lime, soda, magnesia, sulphuric acid, and traces of oxide of iron, with no chlorine or silica.

COMPOSITION OF STEEP WATER.

Steep water is water in which malt has been steeped. According to M. C. Leeuw, it contains 1.08 percent total dry matter, consisting of 0.56 percent organic matter and 0.52 percent mineral matter, and contains 0.033 percent nitrogen.
