Abstract written by a model

Shaggy Man searches for his lost brother after learning from Glinda’s magic Record Book that he disappeared. Believing him to be in the Nome King’s underground cavern, he travels with Betsy, Hank, and Princess Ozga. Their effort to free him brings the party against Ruggedo, who diverts them toward the Forbidden Tube. The brother is rescued from the Metal Forest, and Polychrome’s kiss removes his ugliness as the reunited group leaves for the outside world.

Chapter Six

Shaggy Man has been searching for his lost brother since learning from the magic Record Book of Glinda that the brother disappeared. He believes the brother may be in the Nome King's underground cavern and plans to keep traveling until someone can direct him there. Betsy, Hank, and Princess Ozga agree to help him.

Chapter Eleven

The party's quest to free Shaggy's lost brother leads it to march against Ruggedo, the Metal Monarch and King of the Nomes. Ruggedo prevents the party from reaching his cavern by directing it toward the Forbidden Tube.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The rescue brings Shaggy Man's long-separated brother out of the Metal Forest and into the company of Shaggy Man, Betsy, King Kaliko, and the others. The rescue is completed when Polychrome's kiss removes the charm of ugliness and the group leaves the passage for the outside world.
