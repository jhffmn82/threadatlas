Gloria's heart-freezing enchantment in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Blinkie plans a witchcraft enchantment to freeze Princess Gloria's heart so she can no longer love Pon. After King Krewl and Googly-Goo accept the plan, they arrange to take Gloria to Blinkie's house; Glinda learns of the threat, and the heart-freezing occurs.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twelve

Blinkie proposes freezing Gloria's heart so that Gloria can no longer love Pon. King Krewl and Googly-Goo accept the plan and arrange for Gloria to be taken to Blinkie's house the next day, but the enchantment has not yet been performed in this unit.

Chapter Thirteen

Blinkie plans to use witchcraft to freeze Princess Gloria's heart. Glinda learns of the threat through the Great Book of Records and regards the situation as dreadful.
