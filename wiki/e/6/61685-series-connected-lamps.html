Abstract written by a model

are_connected_in_series dynamo machine [in the illustrated electrical arrangement] Series-connected lamps receive current successively from the dynamo machine, with the current returning from each lamp to the balance and then proceeding to the next. The automatic cut-off allows a failed lamp to be bypassed by a resistance.
