Also called: each other lamp, several lamps in series
  • In Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882: are_connected_in_series dynamo machine [in the illustrated electrical arrangement] Series-connected lamps receive current successively from the dynamo machine, with the current returning from each lamp to the balance and then proceeding to the next.
