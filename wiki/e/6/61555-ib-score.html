IB score in An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation

A compression-evaluation metric ranging from 9α to 1; it combines reconstruction and ground-output probabilities, with lower scores indicating better filtration and guiding SFT selection and DPO preferences.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Noise Filtering .. Reinforcement Learning

The IB score combines the language model probability of reconstructing the retrieved passage from the compression with the probability of generating the ground output from the query and compression. Lower IB scores indicate better filtration, and the score is used to select SFT examples and label DPO preferences.
