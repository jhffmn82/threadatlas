Also called: Logan, Logan or rocking-stone, a big stone, that enormous mass of stone, the Logan

Abstract written by a model

The Logan Rock is a massive rocking stone near Treryn, regarded as England’s most remarkable specimen and celebrated since the narrator’s childhood. In 1824 Lieutenant Goldsmith displaced it and was forced to replace it. Though visitors later seek its motion with a guide, it no longer rocks properly.
