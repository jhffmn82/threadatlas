the proposed marriage of Gloria and Googly-Goo in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The proposed marriage is ordered by King Krewl and desired by the wealthy, older courtier Googly-Goo, who offers the King rich presents and jewels. Princess Gloria repeatedly refuses, thirty-nine times, while Krewl seeks to overcome her love for Pon.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

King Krewl wants Princess Gloria to marry the wealthy courtier Googly-Goo, who is old enough to be her father. Gloria has refused Googly-Goo thirty-nine times, but he continues to pursue her with rich presents for the King.

Chapter Eleven

Gloria has repeatedly refused to marry Googly-Goo. Googly-Goo offers precious jewels to King Krewl in connection with the marriage, while King Krewl seeks a way to overcome Gloria's love for Pon.
