is_a woman [heard sobbing in the house at night] The woman crying in Baskerville Hall is heard by Dr. Watson in the middle of the night. He identifies the sound as a woman’s sob, a muffled and strangling gasp caused by uncontrollable sorrow. He believes the sound is inside the house and not far away, but hears nothing further for half an hour.
