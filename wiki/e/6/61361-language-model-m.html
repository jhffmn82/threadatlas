Abstract written by a model

M is a language model trained with Multi Task Learning on combined data. It generates answers from questions, retrieval, and external knowledge; predicts reference and answer scores and judgments; revises questions; and uses updated references to generate new answers after an initial answer and verification.

2 / Methods .. Algorithm 1 CoV-RAG Inference

The language model M generates an initial answer from the question and retrieved references. It also predicts verification results and can generate a new answer from the initial question and updated references.

2.3 / CoV-RAG Training

Language model M is trained on the combined dataset D with Multi Task Learning. Its objectives are to generate an answer conditioned on a question and retrieval, and to generate reference scores, answer scores, a judgment, and a revised question.
