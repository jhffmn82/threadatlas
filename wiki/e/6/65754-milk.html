milk

Also called: Milk, bread and milk

Abstract written by a model

In Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes, milk is described as the best kind of food, supplying lime for bones and helping make muscle and fat. In Gallegher and Other Stories, Rags believes milk is the food babies need and plans to obtain some from a passing milk-cart, but gives Margaret moistened ham and bread instead because he cannot get outside safely.
