Scenario 2

Also called: S2, the second scenario

Abstract written by a model

Scenario 2 concerns retrieved texts containing usable but incomplete or incorrect knowledge, created by randomly replacing correct answers with phrases of the same properties. It requires large language models to verify content using their parameter knowledge, retain accurate information, rectify errors, and complete what is missing. INFO-RAG and Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation show gains in this scenario.

Scenario 2 is in one work on this site, Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Positive Information Gain in RAG

Scenario 2 concerns retrieved texts that contain some usable knowledge along with incomplete or incorrect knowledge. LLMs should use knowledge within their parameters to verify the retrieved content, use accurate knowledge, rectify incorrect knowledge, and complete missing knowledge.

Analysis / Fine-grained Analysis for Three Scenarios .. Avoid Catastrophic Forgetting Experiment on

Scenario 2 is constructed by randomly replacing correct answers in retrieved texts with another phrase having the same properties. INFO-RAG shows an advantage in this scenario, and Correct and Complete and Contextual Stimulation provide gains related to it.
