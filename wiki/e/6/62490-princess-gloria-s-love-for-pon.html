Princess Gloria's love for Pon in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Princess Gloria's love for Pon begins when she falls for him in the gardens, declares he has uniquely touched her heart, and vows to wed only him. It then leads her to reject Lord Googly-Goo's proposal, prompting King Krewl and Googly-Goo to consider destroying the love.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

Princess Gloria falls in love with Pon while he works in the gardens and tells him that he has touched her heart as no other young man has. She continues to meet him and assures him that she will wed only him.

Chapter Eleven

Gloria's love for Pon prevents her from accepting Lord Googly-Goo's marriage proposal. King Krewl and Googly-Goo discuss killing or destroying that love, but no method is carried out in this unit.
