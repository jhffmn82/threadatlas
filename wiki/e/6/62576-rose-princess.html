Abstract written by a model

Rose Princess, named Ozga, is a Royal Princess of the Rose Kingdom and a Princess of Roseland, described as a ripe princess growing on a bush in the Royal Gardens. Picked by Betsy and Shaggy to rule, she is rejected by the Roses because they want a King and is exiled by her subjects. She joins Shaggy Man, Betsy, Hank, Polychrome, and Private Files in seeking Shaggy Man's lost brother and Ruggedo's kingdom, using the roadside flowers' guidance. Suspected of being a fairy and formerly a Fairy as a Mortal Maid, she later enters a cavern, is captured with Files, and tenderly cares for Shaggy Man after he becomes a dove.

Rose Princess is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Princess Ozga is a ripe Royal Princess growing on a bush in the Royal Gardens and is a distant cousin of Ozma of Oz. Betsy and Shaggy pick her so that she can rule the Rose Kingdom, but the Roses reject her because she is a girl and they want a King. She is exiled with Shaggy Man, Betsy, and Hank and agrees to help search for Shaggy Man's lost brother.

Chapter Eight

Princess Ozga is introduced as an exiled princess. At Private Files's suggestion, she asks the roadside flowers which way leads to Ruggedo's kingdom, and they bend and nod toward the correct road.

Chapter Nine

The Rose Princess travels near the rear of the party with Files. Kaliko suspects that she may be a fairy.

Chapter Seventeen

The Rose Princess follows Shaggy Man into the cavern and is captured with Files. After Shaggy Man becomes a dove, she lets it alight on her shoulder and strokes it tenderly.
