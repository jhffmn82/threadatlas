Abstract written by a model

The throne of Jinxland belongs to Princess Gloria as its only entitled occupant. King Krewl uses it while awaiting the Scarecrow’s destruction; after Krewl’s defeat, the Scarecrow sits there and is hailed King.

Chapter Sixteen

The Scarecrow says that Princess Gloria alone is entitled to sit upon the throne of Jinxland. He plans to conquer King Krewl and place Gloria on it as Queen.

Chapter Eighteen

King Krewl sits on the throne to enjoy the planned destruction of the Scarecrow. After the Orks defeat Krewl, the Scarecrow sits on the throne, and the people and soldiers hail him as their King.
