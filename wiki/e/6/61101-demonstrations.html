demonstrations

object · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: Examples of the labelled data, demonstration data, examples of demonstrations

Abstract written by a model

Demonstrations contain examples of documents and corresponding KG triples. They guide the KG generator, triple selector, and reasoning-chain construction in TRACE. Retrieved from manually labelled training documents and questions, they are used for KG generation and chain construction. Removing them significantly degrades TRACE-Triple performance, while adding more does not necessarily help.

demonstrations is in one work on this site, TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Additional Implementation Details

Demonstrations for the KG generator are retrieved from 50 manually labelled training documents per dataset. Demonstrations for the reasoning chain constructor are retrieved from 20 manually labelled training questions per dataset.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

Demonstrations guide the construction of reasoning chains in TRACE. Removing them significantly degrades TRACE-Triple performance on all three datasets, while increasing their number does not necessarily improve performance.
