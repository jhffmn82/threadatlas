Stapleton’s luminous-hound deception in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Stapleton’s luminous-hound deception is a device involving a hound daubed with luminous paste, inspired by the family hell-hound legend. Intended to frighten Sir Charles to death and deter investigation, it frightens a convict and Sir Henry, driving them to run and scream.

Stapleton’s luminous-hound deception involved daubing the hound with a luminous paste inspired by the family hell-hound story. The device was intended to frighten victims to death or drive them into danger while discouraging witnesses from investigating closely.
