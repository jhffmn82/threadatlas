Chapter Nineteen

Kaliko's magic produces falling rocks, a golden thread that forms a net, an opening into the Bottomless Gulf, and knives that fly through the royal cavern. None of these arts can harm Rinkitink, and the knives cannot harm Bilbil while the Pink Pearl protects him.
