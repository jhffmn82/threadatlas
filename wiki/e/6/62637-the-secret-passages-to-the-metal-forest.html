Abstract written by a model

The secret passages to the Metal Forest are three weekly changing routes controlled by Ruggedo, linking the nomes’ caverns, the Metal Forest, and the outer world. After a three-day failed search, Polychrome sees Ruggedo open one and leads the others through; it later lies dark, jewel-strewn, then opens to daylight when the charm breaks.

Chapter Twenty

The nomes search for the hidden passages for three days without success. After Polychrome observes Ruggedo opening one, she guides the others through it toward the Metal Forest.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The passage leads from the Metal Forest to the outer world and is strewn with jewels that Ruggedo lost from his overloaded pockets. The passage is closed in darkness while the kisses are attempted, then opened to daylight after the charm is broken.
