Related Work

RAG libraries and studies provide or use varying selections of datasets. Differences in evaluation datasets contribute to fragmented experimental setups and prevent systematic comparisons.

6 .. Limitations

Datasets are used in BERGEN's experiments, but the study is restricted to a limited set because of computational demands. The authors plan to add more datasets and encourage evaluation on additional datasets, including in multilingual settings.
