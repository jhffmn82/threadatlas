island (the island previously visited) in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

An island, previously visited by the midgets, has forest, vines with melons, cherry trees, and wild plums. From the hill, ocean appears on three sides and beyond the forest; Trot fears imprisonment, while Cap'n Bill considers a raft or boat to leave.

Chapter Four

The land appears to be an island because ocean surrounds it on three sides from the hill and lies beyond the forest. Trot worries that they may be prisoners there, while Cap'n Bill considers building a raft or boat to leave if needed.
