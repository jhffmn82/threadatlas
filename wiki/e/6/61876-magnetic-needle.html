HOW TO ESTABLISH A TRUE MERIDIAN. / INTRODUCTORY. .. ] APPROXIMATE EQUATION OF TIME.

The magnetic needle is praised as useful for guiding mariners and explorers, but its use in determining permanent boundary lines is described as unsatisfactory. The text recommends substituting traversing from a true meridian for magnetic surveying where practicable.
