Chapter Ten

The travelers fall into the Tube without realizing that danger lies in their pathway. They are unable to stop themselves and slide down the passage in separate groups and positions.

Chapter Eleven

The party arrives in the land through the Forbidden Tube after Ruggedo causes them to fall into it. The Private Citizen concludes that their arrival was forced rather than a willing violation of the country's laws.
