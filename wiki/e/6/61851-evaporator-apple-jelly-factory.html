Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE MANUFACTURE OF APPLE JELLY.

The evaporator contains six twelve-foot copper tubes, each surrounded by an iron steam jacket. Sirup passes through the tubes for about three minutes, entering at 20 to 23 degrees Baume and emerging at 30 to 32 degrees Baume, the proper range for jelly after cooling. Steam pressure can be adjusted to control the reduction, and boiled cider can be drawn from an upper tube or produced by lowering the steam pressure.
