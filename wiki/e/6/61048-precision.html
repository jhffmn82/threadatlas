precision

metric · 8 works · 8 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Precision, joint precision, retrieval precision
  • In "Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain," retrieval precision improves through re-rankers or fine-tuned retrievers that raise gold documents’ ranks, likely improving performance.
  • In "Achieving Higher Factual Accuracy in Llama LLM with Weighted Distribution of Retrieval-Augmented Generation," precision measures the proportion of relevant retrieved instances and assesses accuracy; weighted RAG scores 89.3% versus 82.6% for baseline Llama Large.
  • In "SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs," precision evaluates the correctness of passage or target identifiers mentioned in generated answers against the relevant set.
  • In "CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation," retrieval precision is improved by CausalRAG through its use of causal graphs and causal relationships.
  • In "Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study," precision measures the accuracy of positive predictions as the ratio of correctly identified positive samples to all samples predicted as positive, and it is applied in baseline comparisons.
  • In "Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation," precision measures retrieval quality and the correctness of generated responses and retrieved chunks; KG2RAG reaches response precision of 0.690 and retrieval precision of 0.301 on Hotpot-Dist.
  • In "Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," QD+RR achieves the highest supporting-fact precision of 46.8 and joint precision of 23.1 on HotpotQA, suggesting that reranking can restore precision after broader, noisier retrieval.
  • In "MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health," precision assesses whether responses minimize unsupported claims, and MEGA-RAG significantly enhances it relative to standard approaches.
