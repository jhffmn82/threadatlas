the Wizard's magic

magic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: magic, my magic, the magic of the Wizard, wizardry, your magic
  • In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Wizard's magic transports people across great distances and enables Ozma to send Queen Ann's party home, using pink powder, an incantation, a silver plate, and perfumed smoke to bring Shaggy Man's group to Oz.
  • In The Magic of Oz, the magic of the Wizard is described as unlike the magic of the Magic Isle and as including powders, liquids, magic words, explosive sparks, a magic axe, and silver pincers that can grow or shrink; although he claims it can break enchantments known to ordinary witches and magicians, it does not free Trot and Cap'n Bill.
