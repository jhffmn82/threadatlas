Narrative, section by section

] SOAP AND ITS MANUFACTURE, FROM A CONSUMER'S POINT OF VIEW.

Cotton seed oil is recommended for manufacturers or for making laundry soap. The article says it produces a magnificent soap that lathers very freely.

COTTON SEED OIL.

Cotton seed oil can be used to make lead plaster of equally good quality as plaster made with olive oil when one and one-half pints of boiling water are added. It makes a better-consistency Linimentum Ammoniae, is superior to olive oil for dissolving camphor in Linimentum Camphorae, and performs well in Linimentum Chloroformi, but it is unsuitable for Linimentum Calcis because it does not readily saponify and separates quickly. When mixed with liquor plumbi subacetatis, it turns reddish after twelve to twenty-four hours, a change detectable in mixtures containing five percent or less cotton seed oil.
