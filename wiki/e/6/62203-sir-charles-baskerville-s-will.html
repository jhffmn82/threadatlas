Abstract written by a model

Sir Charles Baskerville's will gives five hundred pounds each to Barrymore and Mrs. Barrymore, one thousand to Dr. Mortimer, smaller sums to others, and its seven hundred and forty thousand pound residue to Sir Henry Baskerville, raising inheritance and motive questions if Sir Henry dies.

Chapter 5.

Sir Charles Baskerville's will provides five hundred pounds each to Barrymore and his wife, a thousand pounds to Dr. Mortimer, smaller sums to others, and the residue to Sir Henry Baskerville. It also raises questions about who would inherit if Sir Henry died and about whether the beneficiaries might have motives.
