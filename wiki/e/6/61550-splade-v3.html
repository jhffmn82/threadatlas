Abstract written by a model

SPLADE-v3 is a learned sparse retriever and new information retrieval baseline. In experiments, it generally outperforms BM25 and RetroMAE on reported KILT retrieval results, reaches 0.799 recall on KILT NQ at R@5, and, with DeBERTa-v3 reranking, achieves the best overall performance and supports NQ fine-tuning.

SPLADE-v3 is in one work on this site, BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
