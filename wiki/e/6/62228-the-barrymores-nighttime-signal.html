the Barrymores' nighttime signal in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

The Barrymores’ nighttime signal is a candle held by John Barrymore at the western lattice window of Baskerville Hall. He waits for Selden’s answering light on the moor; together the lights confirm Selden remains there and direct food to him.

The Barrymores’ nighttime signal consists of John Barrymore holding a candle at the window and waiting for a corresponding light from the moor. The two lights indicate that Selden is still present and show where food can be brought to him.
