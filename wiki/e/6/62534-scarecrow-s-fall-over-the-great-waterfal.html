Abstract written by a model

Scarecrow’s fall over the Great Waterfall is an accident in which he loses balance, falls headfirst into the river, and is swept over the edge into the basin and tiny lake below. He struggles in water inside the Ruby Cavern, is rescued, and his soaked, ruined straw requires the party to carry his head and clothing until fresh straw is found.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Scarecrow loses his balance while leaning over the Great Waterfall and falls headfirst into the river. He is swept over the edge and plunges into the basin below, then later appears struggling in the churning water inside the cave.

Chapter Twenty Three

The Scarecrow suddenly appears in the water and must be rescued from the Ruby Cavern. The accident ruins his stuffing, so the party carries his head and clothing until fresh straw can be found.
