RAG benchmarking is a benchmarking topic for large language models in retrieval-augmented generation. It faces inconsistent configurations that hinder comparison and component understanding, then systematically evaluates retrievers, rerankers, LLMs, metrics, and datasets.

Abstract

Benchmarking is challenged by inconsistent configurations across evaluation datasets, collections, metrics, retrievers, and LLMs. The unit presents a systematic study that benchmarks state-of-the-art retrievers, rerankers, and LLMs and analyzes existing metrics and datasets.
