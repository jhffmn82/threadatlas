the Great Book of Records in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Great Book of Records is Glinda the Good’s great, magical book, immense yet thin-paged and so heavy that three men could scarcely lift it. It truthfully and exactly records important events, adventures, and all that happens throughout the known world, including Oz, the outside world, and unknown places, at the moment they occur. Kept in Glinda’s drawing room on a big gold table, chained and locked with six golden padlocks whose keys Glinda carries, it is cut free and disappears while the castle sleeps. Its loss, along with Glinda’s magic instruments, prevents Glinda and the Wizard of Oz from learning what happened to Ozma. The travelers suspect Ugu the Shoemaker; the book identifies him in the northeast corner of Quadling Country. Ugu has stolen it and examines it at the Wicker Castle, using its record of the travelers’ actions to learn that expeditions seek him, though its knowledge is dangerous to his plans.

CHAPTER 19

The Great Book of Records tells Glinda the Good all that happens anywhere in the world and is dangerous to Ugu's plans. Ugu steals it from Glinda and later reads it at the Wicker Castle to learn that expeditions are searching for him.

Glinda's Great Book of Records in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Glinda's Great Book of Records is a magical treasure of Oz, chained to a marble table, that continually prints truthful brief accounts of events worldwide as they occur. Dorothy sees it report the Skeezers' war declaration. Later it informs Glinda of Ozma and Dorothy's journey, escape to the Skeezers' island, imprisonment beneath the lake, the island's submergence, and Queen Coo-ee-oh's transformation.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Glinda's Great Book of Records continually prints a truthful, brief record of every event occurring anywhere in the world at the moment it happens. While visiting Glinda, Dorothy sees it record that the Skeezers have declared war on the Flatheads.

Chapter Thirteen

The Great Book of Records tells Glinda that Ozma and Dorothy reach the Enchanted Mountain, escape to the Skeezers' island, and later become prisoners beneath the lake. It also records the submerging of the island and Queen Coo-ee-oh's transformation, though it initially gives few details.
