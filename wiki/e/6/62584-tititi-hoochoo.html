Abstract written by a model

Tititi-Hoochoo, also called the Great Jinjin and the Private Citizen, is a fairy and Jinjin who is the supreme ruler of a land on the other side of the world. Revered by Kings and Queens, he occupies the throne and rules with authority, superior powers, high Reason, and Justice. He has black eyes with red sparks, no heart, and a white robe bearing a dragon’s head; he is terrible to wrong-doers but never judges without reason. After warning Ruggedo against using the Hollow Tube again, he learns that Ruggedo forced travelers through the Forbidden Tube. He treats the travelers as innocent guests, declares Ruggedo responsible, and orders their return through the Tube. He selects Quox to punish Ruggedo, then uses an enchanted ribbon to strip Ruggedo of magic and decrees Ruggedo’s exile from the Nome Kingdom to wander the earth’s surface.

Tititi-Hoochoo is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Tititi-Hoochoo lives on the other side of the world and previously warned Ruggedo that he would take vengeance if Ruggedo again sent people through the Hollow Tube. Kaliko fears that Ruggedo's plan will make Tititi-Hoochoo angry.

Chapter Eleven

The Private Citizen is the supreme ruler of the land, where all Kings and Queens are equal before him. He learns from Tik-Tok that Ruggedo forced the party through the Forbidden Tube and decides that the travelers should be treated with justice rather than punished. He orders that the party be entertained as guests and says he will decide Ruggedo's fate before their return to the Residence.

Chapter Twelve

Tititi-Hoochoo is the Jinjin and ruler whom all the Kings and Queens of the fairyland revere. He has no heart but possesses great Reason and Justice, never punishing unjustly or without reason, though he is terrible to wrong-doers.

Chapter Thirteen

Tititi-Hoochoo declares that the adventurers were wickedly forced into the Forbidden Tube by Ruggedo and are innocent of wrongdoing. He orders them to return through the Tube and selects Quox to punish Ruggedo in his name.

Chapter Fourteen

Tititi-Hoochoo is a fairy who rules a kingdom on the other side of the world and possesses powers greater than those of Ruggedo and his nomes. He forbade the nomes to throw even rubbish into the Hollow Tube and would not allow the invaders to remain in his kingdom.

Chapter Eighteen

Tititi-Hoochoo, the Jinjin, enchanted the ribbon so that Ruggedo would lose all knowledge and power of magic when he looked at it. He decrees that Ruggedo must leave the Nome Kingdom and wander on the earth's surface.
