The Scarecrow of Oz

Abstract written by a model

The document is an adventure story following Trot and Cap’n Bill, longtime companions whose coastal cave exploration becomes an extraordinary journey through strange lands and into Oz. Their small boat is caught by a Great Whirlpool, which sweeps them into an underwater cavern. There they meet Flipper, an Ork from Orkland, who carries them through an underground gulf and tunnel to an island. After dealing with the island’s unhappy ruler Pessim and using size-changing lavender and purple berries, Trot and Cap’n Bill travel in the Ork’s sunbonnet across the ocean to the Land of Mo. They meet the Bumpy Man, find Button-Bright buried in warm popcorn snow, enlarge birds with berries, and fly across a desert into Jinxland. In Jinxland, they learn that King Krewl cruelly rules despite Princess Gloria’s rightful claim. Gloria loves Pon, a gardener’s boy who is actually the son of King Phearse, while Krewl seeks to force her to marry Googly-Goo. Krewl and the witch Blinkie imprison Gloria, turn Cap’n Bill into a gray grasshopper with a wooden leg, and freeze Gloria’s heart so she cannot love Pon. Trot, Pon, Button-Bright, and Cap’n Bill receive help when Glinda sends the Scarecrow from Oz. After Button-Bright is found by the returning Ork, the Scarecrow confronts Krewl. Krewl tries to have him burned, but Orks rescue him, defeat Krewl’s forces, and place the Scarecrow on the throne. The Scarecrow compels Blinkie to restore Cap’n Bill and Gloria’s heart, then removes her magic. Gloria becomes Queen, names Pon her Royal Consort, and Krewl is spared but made a gardener’s boy called Grewl. The travelers leave Jinxland with the Scarecrow and Orks, entering Oz. Ozma, Dorothy, and Betsy Bobbin have already watched their danger through the Magic Picture and arrange welcome and shelter. On the way to Glinda’s palace, the Scarecrow falls into a waterfall and is rescued from the Ruby Cavern, though he must be restuffed with fresh straw. Finally, Glinda welcomes the party, Ozma receives Trot and Cap’n Bill at the Emerald City with a banquet and reception, and Button-Bright wanders away again, though Ozma says he cannot go far astray in Oz.

The Scarecrow of Oz in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915 · the work’s page · read it where The Scarecrow of Oz first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

24 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Cap'n Bill and Trot, longtime companions, set out in a small boat to explore caves along the rocky coast. Cap'n Bill notices ominous stillness in the hot afternoon but agrees to continue. A Great Whirlpool draws their boat into its circular current, and despite Cap'n Bill's efforts, they are carried toward the dark hole at its center. Both remain brave as they face the danger.

Chapter Two

Trot and Cap'n Bill are swept from their boat into the ocean and carried underwater to a cavern beneath the sea. They explore the cavern, find a dark hole that may be their only escape, and struggle with the danger of starvation and thirst. Cap'n Bill catches and cooks a fish, but they know they cannot remain in the cavern indefinitely without drinking water. The next morning, a huge strange winged creature emerges from the pool and frightens them.

Chapter Three

Trot and Cap'n Bill meet an Ork named Flipper, who has escaped a whirlpool and a submerged cavern with the help of mermaids. The Ork explains that he comes from Orkland, can fly with small wings and a propeller-like tail, and carries Cap'n Bill and Trot across a vast underground gulf. The three enter a long tunnel, but their candles and food steadily run out as they travel for hours. They stop to sleep in the tunnel after the Ork's feet become painfully sore from walking.

Chapter Four

Cap'n Bill, Trot, and the Ork finish their journey through the tunnel and emerge into daylight at the bottom of a deep shaft. The Ork uses its mechanical propeller tail to fly all three of them out of the shaft and into a beautiful but apparently uninhabited island. They find abundant food and water, including melons, cherries, plums, and nuts, then explore the forest and confirm that the land is surrounded by ocean. At the forest's edge, Trot and Cap'n Bill discover a shed-like house and go to investigate it.

Chapter Five

Trot and Cap'n Bill meet Pessim, the quarrelsome Observer and self-proclaimed King of his own island, where they also reunite with the Ork after it has been shrunk by eating a lavender berry. Pessim explains that a deep purple berry reverses the lavender berry's effect, allowing the Ork to regain its natural size. After living unhappily on the island for several days, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Ork devise a plan to escape by shrinking Trot and Cap'n Bill and carrying them in Trot's sunbonnet. They set off over the ocean with Trot and Cap'n Bill in the sunbonnet tied around the Ork's neck.

Chapter Six

Cap'n Bill and Trot, reduced to midget size, travel for hours in a sunbonnet carried by the Ork over a large ocean. The Ork finds an unfamiliar land, lands on a mountain peak, and helps them leave the sunbonnet. Cap'n Bill and Trot eat purple berries and return to their natural size. Cap'n Bill stores three remaining berries in a wooden box before they begin examining the unnamed country.

Chapter Seven

Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Ork reach the top of a mountain in the Land of Mo and enter a stone house. They meet the Bumpy Man, who calls himself the Mountain Ear and says he listens to nature's sounds to keep the mountain from quaking or spouting. Their host serves them hot molasses candy and lemonade, explaining that Mo has no water and that it rains lemonade. The travelers sleep before the fire while the Mountain Ear remains alert and listens through the night.

Chapter Eight

Trot, Cap'n Bill, the Ork, and the Bumpy Man find Button-Bright buried safely in Mo snow, which is warm, buttered popcorn. Cap'n Bill captures three birds and uses purple berries to enlarge them so they can carry the travelers. He makes swings from strips of the Bumpy Man's old clothes, and the travelers begin following the Ork toward a sandy desert. The group plans to discover what lies beyond the desert by flying across it.

Chapter Nine

Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, the Ork, and three birds cross the great sandy desert and enter the beautiful country of Jinxland. The Ork leaves to search for Orkland, while the birds return to the Land of Mo, leaving the three travelers to continue on foot. A woman tells them that Jinxland is part of the Quadling Country in the Land of Oz but is ruled by its own King, Krewl, because the surrounding mountains cannot be crossed. After receiving food from the woman, the travelers head toward King Krewl's castle and hear someone sobbing in the nearby grove.

Chapter Ten

Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill meet Pon, the gardener's boy, who is grieving because Princess Gloria loves him but King Krewl opposes their relationship. Pon explains that Gloria is Krewl's niece and that Krewl is forcing her to marry the wealthy, elderly courtier Googly-Goo. Pon reveals that he is actually the son of King Phearse, whom Krewl trapped beneath stones in a deep pond, making Pon the rightful prince of Jinxland. After hearing Pon's story and learning that the King's guards beat him, Trot and Cap'n Bill promise to speak favorably of him if they get the chance to meet King Krewl.

Chapter Eleven

Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright enter King Krewl's castle in Jinxland and are treated as potentially magical strangers. They learn that Lord Googly-Goo wants to marry Princess Gloria, who loves Pon, and that King Krewl is considering how to destroy her love for Pon. King Krewl and Googly-Goo summon the Wicked Witch to test whether Cap'n Bill is a magician. After finding Gloria with Pon, King Krewl locks her in a room and prepares to meet the Wicked Witch.

Chapter Twelve

Trot frees Princess Gloria from her room and learns that Gloria loves Pon and refuses to love Googly-Goo. King Krewl and Googly-Goo enlist Blinkie to freeze Gloria's heart, while Blinkie transforms the sleeping Cap'n Bill into a grasshopper with a wooden leg. The transformed Cap'n Bill escapes through a window, and Trot and Button-Bright are expelled from King Krewl's castle. They find Pon, who shelters them for the night and suspects that Blinkie enchanted Cap'n Bill.

Chapter Thirteen

Glinda the Good learns from the Great Book of Records that Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill have arrived in Jinxland, where King Krewl and several Wicked Witches threaten them and Princess Gloria. She sends the Scarecrow to protect the three outsiders and gives him magical supplies, including a rope and pills for a spider. The Scarecrow crosses the mountains separating the Quadling Country from Jinxland, crosses the Great Gulf on a spider-silk bridge, and heads toward King Krewl's castle.

Chapter Fourteen

Button-Bright wanders away from Pon's house while eating blackberries and chasing a butterfly, becoming lost again. King Krewl brings Princess Gloria to Blinkie's house, where Blinkie and three summoned witches perform a ceremony that freezes Gloria's heart and removes her ability to love. Pon and Trot witness the ceremony, escape the pursuing witches, and find Gloria, but she rejects Pon and everyone else with cold indifference. Googly-Goo tries to force Gloria to marry him, but she strikes him into a muddy ditch and continues on alone.

Chapter Fifteen

Trot and Pon search Jinxland for Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright until they meet the Scarecrow, who comes from Oz to help them. Blinkie attacks the Scarecrow, pulls the straw from his body, and then resumes chasing Trot and Pon. The Scarecrow is restuffed, and Cap'n Bill reveals that Blinkie has transformed him into a gray grasshopper with a wooden leg. The Scarecrow explains that Glinda may be able to break the enchantment affecting Cap'n Bill and that she sent him to help the strangers in danger.

Chapter Sixteen

The Scarecrow convenes a council of war with Princess Gloria, Pon, Trot, and the grasshopper to plan the conquest of Jinxland and the removal of King Krewl. He declares that Gloria is the rightful heir to the throne and sends Pon to summon King Krewl to surrender. King Krewl refuses and beats Pon with his golden staff. The Scarecrow then sets out alone for the King's castle, intending to conquer Krewl with a borrowed riding-whip.

Chapter Seventeen

Button-Bright wanders lost through the countryside until he meets the Ork, who has returned from Orkland. The Ork carries Button-Bright through the air to Pon's hut, where he reunites with Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Gloria. After hearing about Gloria's frozen heart and the Scarecrow's attempt to conquer King Krewl, the Ork decides to help and flies home to prepare.

Chapter Eighteen

The Scarecrow of Oz confronts King Krewl and declares that Princess Gloria is the rightful Queen of Jinxland. King Krewl has the Scarecrow bound and orders him burned, but the Orks arrive and scatter the bonfire. The Orks rescue the Scarecrow, defeat King Krewl and his followers, and place the Scarecrow on the throne, where the people and soldiers hail him as their King.

Chapter Nineteen

After King Krewl's conquest, the Scarecrow becomes ruler of Jinxland and asks the Orks to find Blinkie the Witch. The Orks capture Blinkie, who restores Cap'n Bill from a grasshopper and melts Gloria's frozen heart under the Scarecrow's commands. The Scarecrow then uses a second box to stop Blinkie's shrinking, but the antidote also destroys her magic powers. Gloria and Pon are reunited, while Blinkie leaves powerless and sorrowful.

Chapter Twenty

The Scarecrow presides over the selection of Gloria as Queen of Jinxland, and Gloria names Pon her Royal Consort. Former King Krewl is spared revenge and made a gardener's boy under the name Grewl. After the celebrations, the Scarecrow, Trot, Cap'n Bill, Button-Bright, and four Orks leave Jinxland and fly over the mountains into the Land of Oz. They find an empty house with a magically prepared meal and beds, where Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright sleep.

Chapter Twenty-One

Ozma, Dorothy, and Betsy Bobbin watch the Magic Picture show Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill arriving in Jinxland aboard an Ork and three large birds. Ozma worries that the strangers may be mistreated because Jinxland is part of her dominions, but the Scarecrow arrives to protect them. After the adventures in Jinxland end, Ozma has the Wizard prepare a house for the travelers in the Quadling Country. Dorothy and Betsy then set out in the Sawhorse and Red Wagon to meet the strangers at Glinda's palace.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Scarecrow leads Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill toward Glinda's castle, but they mistakenly follow a river to the Great Waterfall. The Scarecrow falls into the river and is swept over the waterfall, causing Trot and Cap'n Bill to search for him. Button-Bright discovers a cave behind the waterfall, where the group finds ruby-lined walls and a cavern through which the river flows underground. They later see the Scarecrow rise briefly from the churning water inside the cavern.

Chapter Twenty Three

The Scarecrow is rescued from the water after falling into the Ruby Cavern, but his straw becomes soaked and ruined. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright empty his body, dry his clothing, and carry his head and clothes while he guides them toward Glinda's palace. Ozma arranges for fresh straw to be provided, allowing the Scarecrow to be restuffed and walk again. The party then continues its journey through the Land of Oz.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Dorothy and Betsy Bobbin travel in Ozma's Red Wagon to Glinda's palace to meet Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Scarecrow. Glinda welcomes the travelers, and the group then journeys to the Emerald City, where Ozma gives Trot and Cap'n Bill rooms in the Royal Palace. Ozma hosts a grand banquet and royal reception at which Trot and Cap'n Bill meet many people and animals of Oz. Button-Bright disappears during the reception, but Ozma assures Trot that no one can go far astray in Oz.
