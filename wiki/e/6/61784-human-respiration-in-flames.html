ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

The experiments are presented as evidence that a person can breathe in the midst of flames. This ability is attributed not only to cooling as air passes through wire gauze but also to the human organs' considerable resistance to heat.
