Asia Minor

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Asian

Abstract written by a model

In Description of Greece, Asia Minor is invaded by the Lacedæmonians under Agesilaus in an attempt to attack Persia, and Agesilaus wins a major victory over Tissaphernes there before the Corinthian War forces him to return to Greece. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Asia Minor is reached by people sent by Apollo, where the sons of Codrus and other Greek groups colonized maritime towns and established cities including Miletus, Ephesus, Priene, and Myus.

Abstract written by a model

Asia Minor is a place reached by people sent by Apollo, where the sons of Codrus and other Greek groups colonized maritime towns and established cities including Miletus, Ephesus, Priene, and Myus.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The sons of Codrus and their contingent crossed to Asia Minor, where different detachments settled different maritime towns. Miletus, Ephesus, Priene, and Myus are among the places associated with this settlement.

CHAPTER III.

Asia Minor was reached by the people whom Apollo sent to form a colony. Cretans, Ionians, and other Greek groups later settled or established cities there.
