the captives of Pingaree in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The captives of Pingaree, including King Kitticut, Queen Garee, the royal household, and island inhabitants, are conquered, bound, and taken away to become slaves, laboring in mines and fields and living confined underground.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

The captives of Pingaree include King Kitticut, Queen Garee, the royal household, and the island's inhabitants. They are bound, chained together in boats, taken away from Pingaree, and intended for labor in the mines of Regos and the grain fields of Coregos.
