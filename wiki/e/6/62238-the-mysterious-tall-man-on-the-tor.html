Abstract written by a model

The mysterious tall man on the tor is an unidentified tall, thin person, distinct from Selden. Watson sees him silhouetted against the moon on a granite tor, arms folded and head bowed; he vanishes when Watson turns to Sir Henry, who suggests he may be a warder.
