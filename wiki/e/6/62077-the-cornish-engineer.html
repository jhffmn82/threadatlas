the Cornish engineer

Abstract written by a model

The Cornish engineer is a clever, highly reliable mechanical engineer whose conscientiousness, probity, and fidelity are valued by employers and by the family he once served abroad. Young in Brazil, he worked for a gold-mining company, later superintended engineering projects, and built a lighthouse with sole charge. In later life he warmly welcomes the narrator’s party, displays inventions, preserves the Emperor’s candle as a relic, and offers hospitality.

" DAY THE NINTH And our last at the Lizard, which a week ago had been to us a mere word or dot in a map; now we carried away from it a living human interest in everything and everybody.

The Cornish engineer welcomes the narrator's party enthusiastically because of his enduring devotion to her family, for whom he had worked abroad. He describes his engineering work, shows them his inventions and the candle used by an emperor to light a lighthouse, and gives them tea, grapes, and apples.
