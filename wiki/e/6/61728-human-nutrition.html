human nutrition

Also called: nutrition, nutrition of man

Abstract written by a model

Human nutrition requires food, nitrogenous substances for tissue building, repair, and renewal, and irreplaceable salts. It is best maintained through a due admixture and variety of four alimentary classes, with water and salts; dietary monotony can seriously impair health, digestion, and assimilation.
