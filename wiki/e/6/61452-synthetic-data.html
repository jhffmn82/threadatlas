synthetic data

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Synthetic Raw Data Generation, raw data, self-produced data, synthetic raw samples, synthetic raw training samples, synthetic training data, training data

Abstract written by a model

Synthetic data is the sole training basis for RAG-Studio, produced from specialized domain-corpus passages without external datasets or models. It contains generated question-answer pairs, retrieved contextual passages, raw training samples, preference-training samples, and retriever-training signals. Generation prompts a model to create questions and answers, retrieves additional passages, and uses self-feedback to curate and refine samples for joint fine-tuning of the RAG system. Across datasets, 3,000 samples are generated per dataset. Filtering is crucial: filtered data improves RAG performance and substantially exceeds raw data, with average relative gains of 6.6% in ROUGE-L and 4.8% in accuracy, and it outperforms human-annotated data in reported fine-tuning experiments.

synthetic data in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

RAG-Studio synthesizes training data using useful domain knowledge identified from a specialized domain corpus. The synthetic data is used for joint fine-tuning of the RAG system.

Introduction

Synthetic data is used to train the adapted RAG system without external datasets or models. It includes question-answer pairs, retrieved contextual passages, and preference training samples produced and curated through the LLM’s self-feedback.

Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

Synthetic data are generated from domain-corpus passages by prompting the generator to produce questions and answers and by retrieving additional passages. The resulting samples are refined into preference data and retriever training signals.

Experiments / Setup / Datasets and evaluation metrics .. and Retriever Combinations

Synthetic training data is generated for each dataset using RAG-Studio's self-alignment strategy, with 3,000 samples per dataset. Filtered synthetic data performs substantially better than raw unfiltered data, producing average relative gains of 6.6% in ROUGE-L and 4.8% in accuracy across the domain-specific datasets.

Conclusion

Synthetic data is the sole data basis of RAG-Studio's training framework. Its use allows the method to operate without external data or models.
