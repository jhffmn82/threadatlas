Kennack Sands in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Kennack Sands is a solitary sandy bay on the Cornish coast, with smooth yellow sands, long Atlantic waves, rock shelters used for bathing, and water whose dangers women travelers observe while girls bathe and flowers are gathered.

] DAY THE THIRD "And a beautiful day it is, ladies, though it won't do for Kynance.

Kennack Sands has smooth yellow sands, long Atlantic waves, rock shelters, and a solitary bay. Girls bathe there in the sea, while the women travelers explore the shore, gather flowers, and observe the dangers of the water.
