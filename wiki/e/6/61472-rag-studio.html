Abstract written by a model

In “RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment,” RAG-Studio is a self-aligned training framework that generates and curates synthetic training data and fine-tunes the retriever and generator into a domain-specific system. In “RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment,” RAG-Studio is a self-aligned training framework and retrieval-augmented generation method for adapting general RAG systems to specialized domains.

RAG-Studio in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where RAG-Studio first appears

Abstract written by a model

RAG-Studio is a self-aligned training framework and retrieval-augmented generation method for autonomously adapting a general RAG system to a specialized domain. Starting with a target-domain corpus, a general retriever, and a general LLM generator, it identifies useful domain knowledge, samples passages, generates synthetic question-answer pairs, and self-curates preference-training samples. It requires no external datasets or models for training. The resulting synthetic data is used to fine-tune both the generator and retriever into a cohesive in-domain RAG system; reported setups use Llama-3-8B-Instruct, BGE, 3,000 synthetic samples per dataset, and LoRA for generator fine-tuning. Across four domain-specific datasets and TriviaQA, it generally outperforms baselines, including RAFT across five datasets and DSF on reported ROUGE-L and GPT-4 accuracy comparisons; it also exceeds human-annotated fine-tuning data by 5.1% on automated metrics and 3.2% on GPT-4 evaluation scores. Its current scope is single-hop question answering, one optimization round, and only retriever and generator optimization.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

RAG-Studio is a self-aligned training framework that adapts general RAG systems to specific domains. It identifies useful domain knowledge from a specialized domain corpus, synthesizes training data, and uses that data for joint fine-tuning of the RAG system.

Introduction

RAG-Studio is proposed as a self-aligned training approach for adapting RAG systems to specialized domains using synthetic data. It autonomously creates training ingredients, fine-tunes the generator and retriever, and outperforms baselines across five in-domain question-answering datasets.

Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

RAG-Studio fine-tunes a general LLM-based generator and dense retriever into in-domain RAG models using a domain corpus. It generates and curates synthetic training data autonomously, then fine-tunes the generator and retriever separately.

Experiments / Setup / Datasets and evaluation metrics .. and Retriever Combinations

RAG-Studio is evaluated on four domain-specific datasets and TriviaQA, where it outperforms all baselines except for TriviaQA EM, on which DSF ranks first. Its performance benefits from contrastive fine-tuning of both the generator and retriever, tailored chain-of-thought prompting, and filtered synthetic training data.

Limitations

RAG-Studio is presented as a promising autonomous framework for in-domain RAG adaptation, but its current work is limited to single-hop question answering. It performs one round of optimization and optimizes only the core retriever and generator components.
