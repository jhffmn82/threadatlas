Abstract written by a model

In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, knowledge triples encode relational information between entities and are generated, scored, and selected for answer generation. In TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, triples express factual units and are extracted or generated as candidate knowledge, ranked and selected to build reasoning chains. In GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation, semantic triples are extracted from passages and used to construct graphs, select reasoning paths, align passages, and expand reasoning chains. In GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation, a triple is defined as a subject, predicate, and object uniquely linked to one passage. In MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health, knowledge graph triples represent biomedical facts used to refine answers and clarify relationships.

triples in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where triples first appears

Abstract written by a model

Knowledge triples express single factual units as a head entity, relation, and tail entity. TRACE extracts or generates them from input and retrieved documents as concise candidate knowledge, reducing irrelevant information when finding supporting evidence. TRACE-Triple uses them to build reasoning chains: a triple ranker assesses relevance and a triple selector guides use and extends paths toward answers. Removing or replacing these components significantly harms performance. The triples are primarily formed between titles and entities in Wikipedia texts, and their quality is not directly or quantitatively evaluated because annotated data is unavailable. Adaptive chain termination determines how many triples a chain needs, avoiding unnecessary, redundant triples and improving performance.

Introduction

Knowledge triples express single pieces of factual knowledge in the form of a head entity, relation, and tail entity. TRACE extracts them from retrieved documents because their finer-grained and more concise form can reduce the impact of irrelevant information when identifying supporting evidence.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

Triples are the knowledge units used by TRACE-Triple to construct reasoning chains. Their relevance is assessed by a triple ranker and their use is guided by a triple selector; removing or replacing these components significantly harms performance.

Limitations

The KG triples are primarily formed between titles and entities within Wikipedia texts. Their quality is not directly and quantitatively evaluated because annotated data is unavailable.

Prompts / Prompt for Generating KG Triples .. Prompt for Generating Answers / Demonstration Data / Experimental Details

KG triples are generated from each input document. They are used as candidate knowledge for constructing reasoning chains.

Baselines

Knowledge triples are generated from documents in the form of a title, relation, and tail entity. They are selected to extend reasoning paths that can answer specified questions.

Effects of Adaptive Chain Termination

The adaptive chain termination strategy dynamically determines the required number of triples in a reasoning chain. This helps avoid introducing unnecessary and redundant triples, which improves performance.

semantic triples in GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation

Zhili Shen · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where semantic triples first appears

Abstract written by a model

Semantic triples are extracted from passages by GraphReader, TRACE, and HippoRAG-related methods. They are used to construct graphs, select reasoning paths, or align passages for retrieval. A triple represents a unit of graph knowledge and can be linked to a proximal triple or passage. Triples serve as initial nodes, are expanded into chains through neighboring triples, and are eventually aligned with source passages.
