Cornish hospitality appears in the fishermen's rescue and shelter of Brest survivors, the laundress's efforts to dry the travelers, the apple seller's generosity, and the Helstone couple's lavish tea and gifts of fruit. Those offering help often resist accepting payment or thanks.
