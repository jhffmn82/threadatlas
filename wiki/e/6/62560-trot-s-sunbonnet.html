Trot's sunbonnet in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Trot's sunbonnet becomes a hollow makeshift bag with knotted strings, holding tiny Trot, Cap'n Bill, and deep purple berries. Tied around the Ork's neck, it carries them during a long flight. Cap'n Bill cuts a slit for their exit, and Trot retrieves it after returning to natural size.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Five

Trot's sunbonnet serves as a makeshift bag because it is hollow in the middle and has two strings. It holds tiny Trot and Cap'n Bill, along with the deep purple berries, and is tied around the Ork's neck so the Ork can carry them.

Chapter Six

The sunbonnet carries Cap'n Bill and Trot during the Ork's long flight. Cap'n Bill cuts a slit in it so they can get out, and Trot picks it up after returning to her natural size.
