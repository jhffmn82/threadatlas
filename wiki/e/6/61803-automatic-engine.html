Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

An automatic engine costs more than a plain slide-valve engine but can reduce fuel, lubrication, repair, depreciation, and boiler-power costs. In the cited flouring mill example, one first-class automatic engine replaced two engines and reduced the stated annual coal and water costs from $11,503 to $6,560.
