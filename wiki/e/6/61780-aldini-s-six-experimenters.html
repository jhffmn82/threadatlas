ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

Six men wearing Aldini suits entered the passage between two blazing straw fences and walked slowly backward and forward while the fire was fed from outside. One of them carried an eight-year-old child who wore only an asbestos bonnet.
