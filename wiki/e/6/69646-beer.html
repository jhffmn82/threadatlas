beer in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Beer is a drink containing a little alcohol. It is made from barley after starch changes to sugar and yeast helps change the sugar into gas and alcohol; hops may add bitterness. It is not good food and does not provide real strength, though it may cause fleshiness. Drinking much of it can consume much alcohol and make drunkards.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

] CHAPTER V. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Beer is made from barley after its starch has been changed into sugar and yeast has helped change the sugar into gas and alcohol. Hops may be added to give beer a bitter taste, but beer is not good food because it contains alcohol.

] CHAPTER VI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Beer contains relatively little alcohol compared with distilled liquor. A beer-drinker may consume a great deal of alcohol by drinking much more of the weaker beverage and may become a drunkard.

CHAPTER VII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Beer contains a little alcohol.
