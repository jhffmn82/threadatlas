Abstract written by a model

In "Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation," RAG Models are evaluated as a baseline category that uses external knowledge, including ChatGPT, Perplexity.ai, WebGLM, and WebGLM variants trained on several model backbones. In "Ask in Any Modality," multimodal RAG models are trained through multiple stages to capture cross-modal interactions and use contrastive and other specialized losses to improve retrieval-augmented generation. In "MAIN-RAG: Multi-Agent Filtering Retrieval-Augmented Generation," training-free RAG baselines are outperformed by MAIN-RAG across various QA benchmarks.
