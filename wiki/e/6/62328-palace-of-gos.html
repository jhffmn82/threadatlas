palace of Gos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

An immense, richly furnished palace in a city, holding treasures King Gos stole from many nations. Inga takes it, shares a bedroom with Rinkitink, and rules from it. Bilbil is later confined upstairs; when the enemy retakes the palace, he escapes and flees.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eight

The palace of Regos is an immense, richly furnished building filled with valuable goods stolen by King Gos. Inga takes possession of it, shares a bedroom there with Rinkitink, and uses it as the center of his rule.

Chapter Twelve

The palace of King Gos is where Bilbil is confined in an upstairs room after his master settles there. When the enemy regains control of it, Bilbil breaks out and flees.
