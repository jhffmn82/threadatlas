Abstract written by a model

A group of girls related to and under the narrator’s care, practical in scrambling clothing and steady-headed at Kennack Sands. On the seventh day they accompany her to Kynance Cove, bathe and are repeatedly knocked down by waves, visit the Bellows, climb Asparagus Island, avoid more dangerous rocks, return safely, and reach Lizard Point along hedge tops.

DAY THE SEVENTH John Curgenven had said last night, with his air of tender patronising, half regal, half paternal, which we declared always reminded us of King Arthur--"Ladies, whenever you settle to go to Kynance, I'll take you.

The girls accompany the narrator to Kynance Cove, bathe in the waves, and enjoy themselves despite being repeatedly knocked down. They visit the Bellows and climb Asparagus Island by a difficult path, while promising not to attempt the more dangerous rocks. They return safely and later find a route along the tops of hedges to Lizard Point.
