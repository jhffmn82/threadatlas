Abstract written by a model

In An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall, the afterlife is considered a possible blessing beyond death, with faith that death may lead to the Father's care and that more and better things may follow. In Dr. Elsie Inglis, death and the afterlife is presented as a passage into fuller life, involving continued growth and learning through all eternity.
