] IMPROVED COKE BREAKER.

Coke is broken by the improved coke breaker and screened into larger coke, clean breeze, and dust. The dust is used at the works to mix with lime in the purifiers. Broken coke generally fetches 2s. to 2s. 6d. more per ton than large coke, while the machine produces only a small quantity of waste.
