mountains in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

A steep mountain range separating Jinxland from the Quadling Country and the Land of Oz. Its unclimbable sides isolate Jinxland from Ozma’s rule; though people cannot cross it, the Scarecrow later crosses using Glinda’s magic rope.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The row of high mountains separates Jinxland from the rest of the Quadling Country. Its sides are so steep that the woman says no one can cross it, leaving Jinxland isolated from Ozma's rule.

Chapter Thirteen

The mountains separate Jinxland from the Quadling Country and have sides too steep to climb. The Scarecrow crosses them with a magic rope supplied by Glinda.
