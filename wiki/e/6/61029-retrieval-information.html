retrieval information in R2AG: Incorporating Retrieval Information into Retrieval Augmented Generation

Fuda Ye, Shuangyin Li, Yongqi Zhang, Lei Chen · 2024

Retrieval information is the semantic representation supplied by retrievers in R2AG. It is transformed through an input-processing pipeline into retrieval-aware prompting, with R2-Former capturing nuanced retriever features. For each document, its unified features concatenate relevance, precedent similarity, and neighbor similarity scores, representing relationships between the query and documents and among documents. Integrated into LLM inputs, this information is intended to improve comprehension of key information and document relationships, fill a semantic gap, and aid generation. It serves as an anchor that guides the LLM toward useful documents: attention visualization shows retrieval information receiving higher attention in deeper layers, while the relevant document receives more attention in the first one to four layers.

front matter .. Abstract

Retrieval information is captured from nuanced retriever features by the R2-Former and integrated into large language models through retrieval-aware prompting. The analysis finds that it serves as an anchor that aids generation and helps fill the semantic gap.

Introduction

Retrieval information consists of semantic representations supplied by retrievers and is transformed into unified retrieval features in R2AG. Injecting it into LLM inputs is intended to improve the LLMs’ comprehension of relationships and key information among retrieved documents.

R2AG / Problem Formulation and Overview .. Training Strategy

Retrieval features are formed by concatenating each document’s relevance score, precedent similarity score, and neighbor similarity score. The resulting list-wise features represent relationships between the query and documents and among the documents themselves.

Experiments / Datasets and Metrics .. Discussions

Retrieval information acts as an anchor that guides the LLM toward useful documents. In the attention visualization, it receives higher attention scores in deeper layers, while the relevant document receives more attention in the first one to four layers.
