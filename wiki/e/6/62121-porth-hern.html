Porth Hern in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Porth Hern, the iron gate opposite Tintagel, receives a rocky stream from Trevena; across Arthurian days, the narrator and companions depart there by boat to visit sea-caves beyond Tintagel.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DAYS FOURTEENTH, FIFTEENTH, AND SIXTEENTH-- And all Arthurian days, so I will condense them into one chapter, and not spin out the hours that were flying so fast.

Porth Hern is the place from which the narrator and her companions take a boat to visit the sea-caves beyond Tintagel. It is identified as the 'iron gate' opposite Tintagel, where a rocky stream reaches the sea.
