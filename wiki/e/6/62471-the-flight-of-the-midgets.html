Abstract written by a model

The Flight of the Midgets is a journey by air chosen by the Ork as the route of travel. It begins with Cap'n Bill and Trot in the sunbonnet carried by the Ork over the ocean to an unfamiliar country. Later, following the Ork, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright cross mountains and the great sandy desert in swings borne by three enlarged birds, pass through pink-tinted mist, and end in Jinxland near the desert's edge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Flight of the Midgets is the journey of Cap'n Bill and Trot in the sunbonnet as the Ork carries them for hours over the ocean toward unknown land. It ends when the Ork lands on a mountain peak in an unfamiliar country.

Chapter Eight

The travelers begin a flight across the sandy desert after deciding to follow the Ork. The Ork leads while the three enlarged birds carry Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright.

Chapter Nine

Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright fly into Jinxland in swings carried by three birds, following the Ork over the great sandy desert and through a pink-tinted mist. They land near the desert's edge, while the Ork continues on alone and the three birds later return toward the Land of Mo.
