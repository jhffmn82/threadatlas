1 section; each heading opens the text

HOW TO ESTABLISH A TRUE MERIDIAN. / INTRODUCTORY. .. ] APPROXIMATE EQUATION OF TIME.

The true meridian is established by observing Polaris and applying the appropriate azimuth correction based on the observer's latitude and the date. Once established, it can be used to determine magnetic variation, local time, and differences of longitude.
