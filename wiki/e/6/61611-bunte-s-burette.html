Bunte's burette in Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882

Various · 1881

Bunte's burette, devised by Dr. Buente, is a burette for furnace-gas analysis. Simpler than the apparatus of Orsat, it uses a three-way cock and enables rapid oxide-of-carbon absorption, measuring gas composition through volume contractions after absorption or combustion.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BUNTE'S BURETTE FOR THE ANALYSIS OF FURNACE GASES. .. _Mode of Measuring the Gases and Absorption_.

Bunte's burette is used for the analysis of furnace gases and is described as simpler to use than the apparatus of Orsat. It permits rapid absorption of oxide of carbon and measures gas composition from volume contractions after absorption or combustion.
