compression

process · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: 2.5% compression rate, compressed retrieval evidence, noise compression

Abstract written by a model

Compression filters irrelevant retrieved-passage material while preserving target-output information, maximizing mutual information with ground output and minimizing it with passages. Candidate compressions receive IB scores; the best build supervised fine-tuning and preference datasets, achieving a 2.5% compression rate.

Abstract

Compression is optimized by maximizing the mutual information between compression and ground output while minimizing the mutual information between compression and retrieved passage. The approach achieves conciseness with a 2.5% compression rate.

Noise Filtering .. Reinforcement Learning

Compression removes irrelevant material from retrieved passages while attempting to preserve information needed to generate the target output. Candidate compressions are evaluated with the IB score, and the best candidates are used to construct supervised fine-tuning and preference datasets.
