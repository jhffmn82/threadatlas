Abstract written by a model

The regulator is a separate control apparatus in the Abdank lamp system, placed for easy inspection and manipulation; its balance and cut-off compensate carbon consumption, maintaining arc and electrical resistance through small successive steps.

ON A NEW ARC ELECTRIC LAMP. .. _Electrical Arrangement_.

The regulator is separated from the lamp and may be placed where it can be inspected and manipulated easily. It uses a balance and a cut-off to compensate for carbon consumption and maintain the required electrical resistance.
