Introduction

Retrieval collects relevant documents that are then ranked and provided to the generator. CoV-RAG performs additional retrieval after verification identifies problems with the external contextual knowledge.

2.3 / CoV-RAG Training

RAG Sampling collects diverse and balanced RAG samples before verification data is created. It uses the RAG Seed Model and negative RAG augmentation to update D2 into D2′.
