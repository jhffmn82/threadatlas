Chapter Eleven

The land contains hundreds of Kings and Queens who look like Tubekins, wear scarlet robes and jeweled crowns, and are equal under the Private Citizen. They bow before him and obey his order to receive the travelers as guests. Several remain visible to escort the party to their palaces.
