Pessim

Abstract written by a model

Pessim is a small, bearded man who exclusively owns and calls himself king and Observer of his island. Habitually quarrelsome and fault-finding, he complains about everything he sees; neighbors brought him there for those traits. After becoming tiny by eating a lavender berry, he returned to normal with a deep purple berry. He aids Trot and the Ork while predicting disaster.

Pessim is in one work on this site, The Scarecrow of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Five

Pessim calls himself the Observer and is the self-proclaimed King of the island, but he continually complains about everything, including the trees, rocks, sand, water, weather, and his visitors. He reveals that his neighbors brought him to the island because he was quarrelsome and fault-finding, and that he once became tiny after eating a lavender berry before returning to normal by eating a deep purple berry. He helps tie Trot's sunbonnet around the Ork's neck, although he predicts that the travelers will be drowned or crushed.
