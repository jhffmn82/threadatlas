Prince Bobo of Boboland in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Prince Bobo of Boboland is a prince who lived under a powerful enchantment as Bilbil, a talking goat. As Bilbil, he carried King Rinkitink, scolded people better than he talked, and resisted King Kaliko by throwing him into a wall after Kaliko kicked him. His true identity is revealed after it is learned that a cruel magician transformed him, and Glinda restores him through transformations. He becomes a tall, shapely young man, apologizes to Rinkitink for his former behavior, and is courteous, pleasant, reserved, and dignified. Greatly attached to Rinkitink, he returns to Pingaree with him and chooses to accompany his former master and present friend rather than desert him.

Chapter Twenty Three

Prince Bobo becomes greatly attached to Rinkitink and returns with him and the others to Pingaree. Rinkitink is especially fond of him.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Prince Bobo laughs when Rinkitink is found and says that Rinkitink has been caught and will be obliged to reign again. He decides to accompany Rinkitink rather than desert his former master and present friend.

Bilbil in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Bilbil is King Rinkitink’s old, skinny-looking talking goat, mount, friend, servant, and frequent critic. He speaks in a gruff, sulky, sometimes disrespectful voice; is habitually discontented, surly, clever, and wiser than many soldiers, yet remains loyal to Rinkitink and becomes Inga’s friend. He wears a red velvet saddle and regularly carries Rinkitink, complaining about rough treatment, hills, singing, and the King’s lack of brains. He can eat grass if food runs out, offers practical help with fish and marble blocks, and warns the others of danger. During the invasion, Bilbil avoids capture by staying silent and out of sight, helps Inga rescue Rinkitink from a well, and travels with them through the ruined island, storm, and boat journey. Protected by the Pink Pearl while touching Rinkitink, he charges enemy warriors, knocks several down, captures Captain Buzzub, and helps defeat the army. Though Rinkitink boasts that they are heroes, Bilbil points out Inga’s major part in the conquest. Confined alone in a palace room to keep him from quarreling with citizens, disliked by servants for scolding and disagreeable talk, and left without grass, Bilbil grows hungry, angry, and uncontrollable. He breaks down the door, attacks King Gos and his warriors, escapes to Queen Cor’s palace, and is later reunited with Inga and Rinkitink. He continues to support Inga’s rescue, carries Rinkitink on further journeys, and is protected from Kaliko’s flying knives by the Pink Pearl, even chewing Kaliko’s cloak embroidery. At the end, the Wizard identifies Bilbil as Prince Bobo of Boboland, enchanted into a talking goat. Ashamed of this transformation, Bilbil agrees to go with the others to the Land of Oz so Glinda may try to end the enchantment.

Narrative, section by section

12 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

Bilbil is King Rinkitink's talking goat, and he speaks in a gruff, sulky, and sometimes disrespectful manner. He complains about being handled roughly and about carrying Rinkitink up the hill, but eventually carries him to the palace.

Chapter Four

Bilbil escapes the invaders by keeping his mouth shut and staying away from them. He helps Inga pull King Rinkitink from the well and carries the King while they explore the ruined island.

Chapter Five

Bilbil says he can eat grass and repeatedly mocks Rinkitink for lacking brains, though he also helps Inga move the marble blocks and explains how to cook the fish. He becomes cross when Inga refuses to reveal the secret of the treasure. Bilbil later shelters from the storm with Inga and Rinkitink.

Chapter Six

Bilbil travels in the boat with Inga and Rinkitink after considerable difficulty getting aboard. He complains about Rinkitink's singing, trades insults and jokes with the King, and warns that the people of Regos and Coregos may kill or eat them.

Chapter Seven

Bilbil carries King Rinkitink while remaining in contact with Inga so the Pink Pearl protects them both. He charges the warriors, knocks several into a heap, catches Captain Buzzub, and helps defeat the army.

Chapter Eight

Bilbil enters Regos with King Rinkitink and Inga after the conquest. He reminds Rinkitink that Inga did much of the conquering, although Rinkitink says that Bilbil and he are the heroes.

Chapter Twelve

Bilbil becomes increasingly hungry, angry, and disagreeable after being confined alone without food. He breaks down his door, attacks King Gos and his warriors, escapes the palace, and runs into the palace of Queen Cor.

Chapter Thirteen

Bilbil arrives at the gates of Queen Cor's courtyard during his mad dash from Regos. He collides with Queen Cor and then with Rinkitink, but afterward is reunited with Inga and Rinkitink.

Chapter Fourteen

Bilbil travels with Inga, Rinkitink, and Zella to Regos and carries Rinkitink during part of the journey. He criticizes Rinkitink's singing and supports Inga's decision to continue the rescue.

Chapter Twenty-One

Bilbil is revealed by the Wizard to be an enchanted talking goat who is actually Prince Bobo of Boboland. He agrees with the others to return to the Land of Oz so Glinda can try to break the enchantment.
