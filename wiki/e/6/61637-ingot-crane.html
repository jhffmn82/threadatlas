Also called: crane, ingot cranes

Abstract written by a model

The ingot crane handles steel ingots between soaking pits and the blooming mill, placing stripped ingots into pits, later removing and swinging them to the mill, while commanding pit loading and unloading and the mill’s live rollers; it enables four men and a boy to pass the full make.

SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The ingot crane places stripped ingots into the soaking pits and later removes them and swings them to the blooming mill. The recommended arrangement uses a crane capable of commanding both the pits and the mill's live rollers; with such a crane, four men and a boy could pass the full make through the pits.
