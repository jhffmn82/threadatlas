Regos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Regos is a northern island, one of a twin pair with Coregos, lying north of Pingaree. Ten miles wide and forty miles long, it has fertile shores, rugged mountains, level green meadows by the ocean, dark tangled forest, and gold and silver mines worked by enslaved captives. Its City of Regos is ruled by King Gos, and its fierce, unruly warriors raid neighboring islands, including Pingaree, whose prisoners and plunder they carry to Regos for mine labor. Prince Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil journey toward Regos to free captives; its army flees and they conquer the island. King Gos has been defeated, but he and Queen Cor escape before the rescuers reach the city, later departing by boat with the rulers of Pingaree as captives. Queen Cor and Queen Garee also flee to Regos. After the rulers of Regos and Coregos run away, no ruler is appointed for Regos, and Nikobob ultimately leaves it for Pingaree.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Regos is one of the twin northern islands inhabited by a fierce and warlike people. Its warriors once joined with those of Coregos in attacking Pingaree.

Chapter Three

Regos is identified as one of the kingdoms from which the invading warriors come. The warriors carry Pingaree's prisoners and plunder back to Regos, where the captives are to work in its mines.

Chapter Six

Regos lies north of Pingaree and is one of the islands toward which Inga rows. Inga intends to reach it to free his parents and the people of Pingaree, although Rinkitink and Bilbil fear its inhabitants.

Chapter Seven

The Island of Regos is ten miles wide and forty miles long, with fertile shores, rugged mountains, and gold and silver mines worked by enslaved people. Its warriors raid neighboring islands, but Prince Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil conquer the island after its army flees.

Chapter Nine

Regos contains the city ruled by King Gos, dark forests, mines, and the feared Choggenmugger. Nikobob returns through its forest after King Gos has been conquered.

Chapter Thirteen

Regos is the island toward which Queen Cor flees. Zella's father found one of Inga's shoes by the palace wall and the other on an ash-heap there, and Bilbil arrives from Regos during the events.

Chapter Fourteen

Regos is the island containing the City of Regos and the mines where King Gos enslaves captured people. Inga and his companions travel there to rescue King Kitticut and the men from Pingaree, but King Gos and Queen Cor escape from the island before they arrive at the city.

Chapter Fifteen

Regos receives Queen Cor and Queen Garee after they flee from Pingaree. King Gos and Queen Cor depart from Regos by boat with the rulers of Pingaree as captives.

Chapter Sixteen

After the King and Queen of Regos and Coregos run away, Regos has no ruler appointed in this unit. Nikobob initially fears remaining there because of its fierce and unruly warriors, but later leaves for Pingaree.
