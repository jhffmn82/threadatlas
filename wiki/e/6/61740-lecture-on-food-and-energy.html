Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FOOD AND ENERGY OF MAN.

The lecture on food and energy explains the classification and uses of food, the relation between nutrition and bodily work, and the importance of variety in diet. It also considers alcohol and other restoratives, tobacco, scurvy among seamen, and the need for shipowners or the legislature to protect maritime health.
