Whitesand Bay in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Whitesand Bay is a wide, sunny sweep of fine sand with minute, varied shells, bright sea, and shallow-looking water. Revisited on a clear Monday for bazaar shells, it delights companions, though an old woman warns bathing may be unsafe from quicksands.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DAY THE TWELFTH Monday morning.

Whitesand Bay is revisited on a clear, sunny day so that one of the companions can collect shells for a bazaar. Its wide, fine sandy beach, bright sea, and shallow-looking water delight the party, although an old woman warns that bathing there is unsafe, possibly because of quicksands.
