Abstract written by a model

Lizard Town is a bright little town on the Lizard peninsula, with a post-office, serpentine shops, and two nearby hotels. Its commercial places provide food, drink, clothing, and ornaments. During the travelers’ excursion, they depart through it, later return toward it from Cadgwith, and on the fifth day wade in to seek a shop.

] DAY THE THIRD "And a beautiful day it is, ladies, though it won't do for Kynance.

The women travelers depart through Lizard Town at the beginning of their excursion and later return toward it from Cadgwith. They hear there that Cadgwith's parson and his wife kindly look after invalids.

DAY THE FIFTH "Hope for the best, and be prepared for the worst," had been the motto of our journey.

The travelers wade into Lizard Town in the afternoon to look for a shop. They find the post-office and serpentine shops, where they can buy food, drink, clothing, and various ornaments.
