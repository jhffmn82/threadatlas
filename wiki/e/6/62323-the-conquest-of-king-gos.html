the conquest of King Gos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The conquest of King Gos is Prince Inga’s campaign against King Gos and his warriors in Regos. Led by Inga with King Rinkitink and Bilbil, the group invades from the south, protected by magic against the army’s weapons. Inga uses the magic pearls’ powers to drive Gos and his men from the city, achieving the conquest and leaving Regos abandoned by them as they flee toward Coregos. The victory frightens Gos’s forces, though Queen Cor later captures Inga by deception.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

Prince Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil invade Regos from the south. Protected by magic, they withstand the army's weapons, enter the city, and cause King Gos and his warriors to flee to Coregos.

Chapter Eight

The conquest of Regos is achieved when Inga uses the magic pearls' powers to drive King Gos and his men from the city. Inga, King Rinkitink, and Bilbil then enter the city, while the defeated forces flee toward Coregos.

Chapter Nine

King Gos and his city have been conquered by a boy Prince from Pingaree. Nikobob reports this event to his wife and daughter after returning home.

Chapter Ten

Prince Inga's conquest drives King Gos and his warriors from Regos and frightens them into believing that they cannot oppose his power. Queen Cor later captures Inga through deception rather than defeating him in battle.
