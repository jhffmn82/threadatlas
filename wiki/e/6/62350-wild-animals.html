Wild animals dwell around Nikobob’s forest cabin, prowling and growling at night yet generally not molesting his family; during Zella’s journey, a brown bear watches her and a puma moves away.

Chapter Eleven

Wild animals surround Nikobob’s cabin and often prowl and growl there at night. They generally do not molest Nikobob’s family, and during Zella’s journey a brown bear watches her while a puma moves away from her path.
