R2AG

framework · 1 work · 41 quoted facts · 17 connections
Also called: R2AG framework, R2AG+RAFT, Retrieval Argumented Generation (R2AG), Retrieval Argumented Generation (named R2AG), enhanced RAG framework
  • In R2AG: Incorporating Retrieval Information into Retrieval Augmented Generation: R2AG is a novel, cost-effective enhanced RAG framework for retrieval-aware generation. It addresses the semantic gap between retrievers and LLMs by incorporating nuanced retrieval information into the LLM generation process without destructively altering the retriever or LLM. It begins with query and document representations from an encoder-based retriever, processes them into unified retrieval features, and uses a trainable R2-Former to capture retrieval information. Retrieval-aware prompting then supplies this information to the LLM, aiming to give it a more comprehensive understanding of retrieved documents. R2AG supports low-source settings: retrievers and LLMs can be frozen to save computational costs, with only R2-Former trained, though it also describes joint R2-Former and LLM alignment using query-document matching and language-modeling objectives. Across Natural Questions, HotpotQA, MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and DuReader, it generally improves foundation LLMs using standard RAG, outperforms most enhanced RAG methods, and outperforms ChatGPT on NQ and HotpotQA. It remains effective with long inputs, benefits from stronger retrievers, and achieves the best overall combined results with RAFT. Its limitations are dependence on encoder-based retriever representations, uncertain suitability for sparse and cross-encoder retrievers, possible incompatibility with more powerful closed-source LLMs, and difficulty when no relevant documents are available. Future work may add retrieval features or integrate self-RAG.

R2AG in R2AG: Incorporating Retrieval Information into Retrieval Augmented Generation

Fuda Ye, Shuangyin Li, Yongqi Zhang, Lei Chen · 2024

R2AG is a novel, cost-effective enhanced RAG framework for retrieval-aware generation. It addresses the semantic gap between retrievers and LLMs by incorporating nuanced retrieval information into the LLM generation process without destructively altering the retriever or LLM. It begins with query and document representations from an encoder-based retriever, processes them into unified retrieval features, and uses a trainable R2-Former to capture retrieval information. Retrieval-aware prompting then supplies this information to the LLM, aiming to give it a more comprehensive understanding of retrieved documents. R2AG supports low-source settings: retrievers and LLMs can be frozen to save computational costs, with only R2-Former trained, though it also describes joint R2-Former and LLM alignment using query-document matching and language-modeling objectives. Across Natural Questions, HotpotQA, MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and DuReader, it generally improves foundation LLMs using standard RAG, outperforms most enhanced RAG methods, and outperforms ChatGPT on NQ and HotpotQA. It remains effective with long inputs, benefits from stronger retrievers, and achieves the best overall combined results with RAFT. Its limitations are dependence on encoder-based retriever representations, uncertain suitability for sparse and cross-encoder retrievers, possible incompatibility with more powerful closed-source LLMs, and difficulty when no relevant documents are available. Future work may add retrieval features or integrate self-RAG.

front matter .. Abstract

R2AG is a framework that incorporates retrieval information into retrieval augmented generation to address the semantic gap between large language models and retrievers. It uses an R2-Former and retrieval-aware prompting, and experiments across five datasets validate its effectiveness, robustness, and efficiency.

Introduction

R2AG is a cost-effective enhanced RAG framework that incorporates retrieval information into generation. It uses an input processing pipeline, a trainable R2-Former, and retrieval-aware prompting, and it can operate with frozen retrievers and LLMs.

Related Works / Retrieval Augmented Generation .. Enhanced RAG

R2AG is described as a cost-effective and non-destructive approach for bridging the semantic gap between LLMs and retrievers. It is contrasted with joint modeling methods that require extra training to align semantic spaces and may hamper the generality of large language models.

R2AG / Problem Formulation and Overview .. Training Strategy

R2AG is a framework that processes query and document representations from a retriever into unified retrieval features, uses the R2-Former to extract retrieval information, and supplies that information to an LLM through retrieval-aware prompting. It jointly trains the R2-Former and LLM alignment with query-document matching and language-modeling objectives, while also supporting settings in which the LLM is frozen.

Experiments / Datasets and Metrics .. Discussions

R2AG is evaluated across five datasets and generally improves the performance of foundation LLMs using standard RAG. It outperforms most enhanced RAG methods in the reported results, remains effective with long inputs, and performs best overall when combined with RAFT.

Conclusion and Future Work

R2AG is a novel enhanced RAG framework that bridges the semantic gap between retrievers and LLMs. It incorporates retrieval information into the LLMs’ generation process and outperforms other competitors. Future work could apply more retrieval features to it.

Limitations

R2AG depends on semantic representations modeled by encoder-based retrievers. It may not be compatible with more powerful closed-source LLMs, may benefit from additional retrieval features, and requires further consideration for situations in which no relevant documents are available.
