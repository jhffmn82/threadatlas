Abstract written by a model

Penzance is a genteel, extremely civilised town in Cornwall, connected to Falmouth by a good but lonely road. Travelers pass through, preferring Marazion because Penzance seems too genteel despite lovely, richly vegetated surroundings and coastal views. They later return in the evening to arrange departure, discuss a Marazion house, and prepare Charles’s drive to Falmouth.

Penzance is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

] DAY THE TENTH I cannot advise Marazion as a bathing place.

The travelers pass through Penzance and decide they were right not to stay there because it seemed too genteel and civilized, although its surrounding countryside was lovely and richly vegetated. From the rising road near Penzance they see the coast from the Lizard Point to the Land's End.

DAY THE TWELFTH Monday morning.

The narrator and her companions return to Penzance as the lovely day turns into evening. They arrange their departure, consult their landlady about a possible house at Marazion for friends, and prepare for Charles's overnight drive to Falmouth.
