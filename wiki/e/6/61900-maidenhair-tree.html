Abstract written by a model

The Maidenhair tree is an ornamental deciduous tree with long-stalked, fan-shaped leaves that turn pale yellow before falling. Male catkins and female reproductive structures occur on separate plants, followed by drupaceous fruit. At Broadlands it reaches about 40 feet, with a 7-foot trunk girth and 45-foot spread.

THE MAIDENHAIR TREE.

The Maidenhair tree is an ornamental deciduous tree with long-stalked, fan-shaped leaves that turn pale yellow before falling. It bears male catkins and female reproductive structures on separate plants, produces a drupaceous fruit, and has several cultivated leaf varieties.
