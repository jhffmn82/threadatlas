Abstract written by a model

In BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RetroMAE is a dense retriever trained for MS MARCO passage ranking, and its reranked version generally improves zero-shot generation and retrieval with varying performance. In BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RetroMAE+RR is a retrieval system that adds DeBERTa-v3 reranking to RetroMAE, improving KILT QA retrieval R@5 and reaching 0.822 on KILT NQ.
