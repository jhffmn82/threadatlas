Abstract written by a model

In "Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain," retrieval recall should be prioritized when tuning retrieval systems because LLMs may exhibit pseudo-helpfulness when relevant gold documents are not retrieved. In "SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs," recall is used as a retrieval metric to evaluate how much of the known relevant passage or target set is identified by generated answers. In "Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study," recall measures the proportion of actual positive samples correctly identified and is applied in embedding-model and large-language-model baseline comparisons.
