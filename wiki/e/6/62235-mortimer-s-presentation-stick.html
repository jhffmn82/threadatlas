Chapter 1.

Mortimer’s presentation stick is a thick, bulbous-headed Penang lawyer with a silver band engraved “To James Mortimer, M.R.C.S., from his friends of the C.C.H.” and dated 1884. Its worn ferrule indicates extensive walking, while tooth marks show that a dog habitually carried it. Mortimer values the stick highly and returns to retrieve it.
