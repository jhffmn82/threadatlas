the Eddas in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

The Eddas are two Icelandic collections, one poetic and older and one modern prose, preserving Northern mythological records, stories, and beliefs alongside the Sagas.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXI

The Eddas are two collections containing Northern mythological records. The older Edda is in poetry and dates to 1056, while the more modern prose Edda dates to 1640.

Chapter XXXIII

The Eddas have come down from Iceland along with the Sagas. They preserve the northern stories and beliefs described in this unit.
