King Gos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

King Gos is the cruel, warlike, lawless king and ruler of Regos, later ruling both Regos and Coregos with Queen Cor. A great warrior but also a robber, outlaw, and, in Queen Cor’s judgment, a coward, he commands the warriors of Regos, enslaves people in his mines through cruel overseers, and owns storehouses holding goods taken from Pingaree. He captures King Kitticut and Queen Garee after defeating them, wages war and raiding against Pingaree and the rest of the world, and becomes an enemy of Prince Inga. When Gos orders an attack on Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil, the attack fails; the city gates are broken, and he panics and flees with his followers from Regos across boats to Coregos. Inga conquers him and drives him from Regos, while Gos remains on Coregos, sends spies, and plots to recover Regos. After Inga and Rinkitink are captured, Gos returns to Regos with his warriors, though Bilbil twice butts him down palace stairs. Expecting Inga at the mines, Gos orders guards to seize him, removes Kitticut, and escapes by boat with Cor and the Pingaree captives. Pursued after his slaves are freed and his palace searched, he takes Kitticut and Garee to King Kaliko, bribes Kaliko with gold and gems, and falsely portrays them as evil conquerors so they will be imprisoned. Gos and Cor then leave the caverns, but both are drowned.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

King Gos rules Regos and is described as cruel, warlike, and lawless. He orders his warriors to attack Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil, but after their weapons fail and the city gates are broken, he panics and flees across the bridge of boats to Coregos.

Chapter Eight

King Gos is driven from Regos by Inga and his magic, and Rinkitink fears that he may return to recapture the city. King Gos and his followers remain on Coregos, sending spies to learn whether Inga still occupies Regos and plotting against him.

Chapter Nine

King Gos has been conquered by a boy Prince from Pingaree before the events described at Nikobob's cabin.

Chapter Ten

King Gos says that he cannot fight Prince Inga's magical power and calls an attack against him folly. After Queen Cor captures Inga and Rinkitink, Gos and his warriors return to Regos and celebrate.

Chapter Twelve

King Gos comes up the palace stairs with his captains and warriors. Bilbil twice butts him down the stairs, causing him to fall into a heap of soldiers.

Chapter Fourteen

King Gos anticipates that Inga will come to the mines and orders the guards to capture him and keep him there. He removes King Kitticut from the mines before Inga arrives, then escapes with Queen Cor by boat, taking King Kitticut and the Queen of Pingaree.

Chapter Fifteen

King Gos initially laughs at Queen Cor's fear but agrees that her hostage plan may succeed. He goes to the mines, brings King Kitticut back to Regos, and then leaves by boat with the captives.

Chapter Sixteen

King Gos's former slaves are freed, and his storehouses and palace are searched for goods taken from Pingaree. His boat leaves before Inga and Rinkitink, and Inga pursues it.

Chapter Seventeen

King Gos takes King Kitticut and Queen Garee from the shore of the Wheelers to the Nome King, dragging them along while they carry bags of gold and jewels. He falsely tells King Kaliko that the prisoners are evil conquerors and asks that they be kept safely confined. After concluding the bargain, he warns Kaliko not to release them and leaves the caverns with Queen Cor.
