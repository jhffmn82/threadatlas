automatic drying system

Also called: automatic drier, automatic system of drying, system of drying

Abstract written by a model

The automatic drying system, located at the upper part of the animal black furnace, dries bone-black as it exits the washer. It consists of two undulating pipes with steam openings; waste heat and combustion products pass between them and leave through the chimney.

The automatic drying system is located at the upper part of the furnace and was devised to dry bone-black as it exits the washer. It consists of two undulating pipes with openings for steam to escape, while waste heat and combustion products pass through the space between them before leaving through the chimney.
