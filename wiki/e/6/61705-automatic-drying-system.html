212 " SCHREIBER'S APPARATUS FOR REVIVIFYING BONE-BLACK. .. _The Animal Black Furnace_.

The automatic drying system is located at the upper part of the furnace and was devised to dry bone-black as it exits the washer. It consists of two undulating pipes with openings for steam to escape, while waste heat and combustion products pass through the space between them before leaving through the chimney.
