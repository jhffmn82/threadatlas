LoRA

fine-tuning technique · 4 works · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: LoRA training, Low-rank Adaptation, Low-rank Adaptation (LoRA)

Abstract written by a model

In "RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LoRA training is used in the second stage of detection-model training, enabling interpretations based on prediction labels, including hallucination descriptions and defensive advice. In "Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LoRA is a plug-and-play training method that trains pretrained large language models on mixed data, reducing costs by loading for retrieval-augmented generation and unloading otherwise. In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LoRA is a fine-tuning method that updates model parameters using low-rank dimension 8, scaling factor 16, and dropout 0.05. In "GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Low-rank Adaptation is a fine-tuning method used on an LLM baseline, yielding large gains alone on ExplaGraphs but marginal gains when GRAG is used.
