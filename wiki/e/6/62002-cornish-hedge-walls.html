Cornish hedge walls in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Cornish hedge walls are stone-and-earth walls in the Lizard district, six to ten feet high and about two feet broad, dividing land instead of fences and forcing travelers along narrow, precarious tops.

"Shall we get a boat?

Cornish hedge walls divide land in the Lizard district instead of fences. They are six to ten feet high and about two feet wide, forcing travelers to walk along a narrow, precarious path on top of them.
