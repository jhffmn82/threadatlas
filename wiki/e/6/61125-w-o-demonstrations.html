Chain Constructor .. Chains R

w/o demonstrations is a TRACE-Triple variant that removes demonstrations and uses only the instruction to guide reasoning chain construction. Its performance is significantly lower than TRACE-Triple on HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue.
