Experimenting with other models

Experimenting with other models evaluates IterRAG-LM and PlanRAG-LM using four different models: GPT-3.5, Llama 2 (70B), Llama 2 (13B), and Phi-2. The experiments use vLLM for open-model inference and report the comparative results in Table 11.
