re-planning in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

Re-planning is an event in which an insufficient initial plan is assessed against retrieval results and revised to continue analysis or correct earlier direction. It uses the question, schema, previous plan, and query results. Revisions may increase, preserve, or decrease steps through re-ordering, replacement, target changes, added look-up or calculation actions, division, deletion, or merging. It may occur repeatedly and belong to multiple categories. Removing it lowers accuracy, especially in Locating; it occurs more often in Building.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology: PlanRAG

Re-planning occurs when the initial plan is not good enough to solve the decision question. The language model assesses the current plan using the results of retrieval steps and generates a new plan to continue analysis or correct the direction of earlier analysis.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

Re-planning revises an insufficient original plan using the question, schema, previous plan, and query results. Removing re-planning lowers accuracy by 10.8% in Locating and 0.9% in Building, while more frequent re-planning is associated with the harder Building scenario.

Re-planning cases

Re-planning changes an original plan by increasing, preserving, or decreasing its number of steps. It can involve re-ordering, replacing, changing targets, adding look-up or calculation actions, dividing steps into sub-steps, deleting steps, or merging steps.
