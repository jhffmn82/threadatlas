adaptive chain termination strategy

strategy · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: this strategy

Abstract written by a model

The adaptive chain termination strategy ends a reasoning chain when no additional triples are needed. It dynamically determines optimal chain lengths and required triples, avoiding unnecessary and redundant triples to improve QA performance. Though omitted from Algorithm 1 for clarity, it can be incorporated if needed. Experiments on MuSiQue show effectiveness consistent with HotPotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA.

Additional Experimental Results and .. MuSiQue

The adaptive chain termination strategy is examined through experiments varying the maximum reasoning chain length on MuSiQue. The results are consistent with those on HotPotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA and demonstrate the strategy's effectiveness.

Effects of Adaptive Chain Termination

The adaptive chain termination strategy allows the reasoning chain constructor to dynamically determine the required number of triples. It improves performance by avoiding unnecessary and redundant triples.
