Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Dolor Ugo is an inaccessible, unusable sea cave, solemn and colorful, with a floor entirely of clear, deep olive-green heaving water. Boats can enter only to the daylight’s reach; beyond that, its extent remains unknown because exploration would require dangerous swimming.

Dolor Ugo is a solemn, colorful cave whose floor is entirely heaving, clear, deep olive-green water. Boats can enter as far as the daylight reaches, but its inland extent is unknown because exploring it would require dangerous swimming.
