smoking furnace

object · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: S, small furnace, small smoking furnace

Abstract written by a model

The smoking furnace is a small gas-heated furnace for preliminary heating or smoking. It has a wide refractory-clay upper cylinder, a boiler-plate iron lower portion, and refractory-clay internal and perforated tubes. Air enters through cover apertures; gas combustion produces hot gases that pass through tube e and conduits into the smoking chamber. Adjustable registers vary their temperature with cold, dry air.

] GAS FURNACE FOR BAKING REFRACTORY PRODUCTS.

The smoking furnace, S, provides gas-heated preliminary heating or smoking. Air enters through apertures in its cover, combustion produces very hot gases, and these gases pass through tube e and vertical conduits b' into the smoking chamber. Adjustable registers admit cold, dry air to vary the temperature of the gases released through tube e.
