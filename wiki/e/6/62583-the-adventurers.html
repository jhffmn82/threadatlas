Abstract written by a model

The adventurers are an honest group of mortals and immortals led by a shared effort to free Shaggy Man’s lost brother. Their party includes Betsy Bobbin, Hank, the Princess, Shaggy Man, and later Polychrome and Tik-Tok; it also travels at times with Queen Ann, the Army of Oogaboo, the Rose Princess, and Files. They become lost while seeking Ruggedo’s cavern, recover Tik-Tok, confront an approaching army, and continue toward the Nome Kingdom through the Rubber Country despite Ruggedo’s hindrance. Forced by Ruggedo through the Forbidden Tube, they are judged innocent, hosted by Kings and Queens, and finally ordered to return through the Tube to their own side of the world.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

The travelers' party consists of Betsy Bobbin, Hank, the Princess, Shaggy Man, and later Polychrome and Tik-Tok. They search for Shaggy Man's brother, become lost while seeking Ruggedo's cavern, recover Tik-Tok, and face the approach of an army at a crossroads.

Chapter Nine

The adventurers travel toward the Nome Kingdom and pass through the Rubber Country despite Ruggedo's efforts to hinder them. They include Tik-Tok, Queen Ann, the Army of Oogaboo, the Shaggy Man, the Rose Princess, Polychrome, Betsy Bobbin, Hank, and Files.

Chapter Eleven

The party is brought before the Private Citizen after falling through the Forbidden Tube. Because Ruggedo forced them into it, the party is judged not to have willingly violated the country's laws. Its members are taken in as guests of various Kings and Queens until they return to the Residence the next day.

Chapter Thirteen

The adventurers are judged innocent and ordered to return through the Forbidden Tube. They travel on Quox's back, with Betsy and Hank in front, Shaggy and Polychrome behind them, Files and the Princess next, Queen Ann and Tik-Tok after them, and the officers in the rear.
