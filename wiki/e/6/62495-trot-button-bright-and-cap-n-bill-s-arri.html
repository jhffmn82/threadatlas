Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill's arrival in Jinxland in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The first arrival of strangers in Jinxland brings Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill from the Outside World after they cross the desert. King Krewl initially gives them rooms and a banquet, then suspects them, especially Cap'n Bill. The Great Book records their arrival; Trot and Button-Bright are expelled and sheltered by Pon amid danger from the king and witches.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

The arrival concerns Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright, who enter Jinxland after crossing the desert. King Krewl gives them rooms and a banquet but later views them, especially Cap'n Bill, with suspicion.

Chapter Thirteen

The Great Book of Records reports that three people from the Outside World have arrived in Jinxland. Trot and Button-Bright are expelled from King Krewl's castle and find shelter with Pon, while the three face danger from the king and the witches.
