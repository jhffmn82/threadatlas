is_a island Pessim's island has a grove, an ocean shore, trees bearing unusual fruits, and no other land visible nearby. Pessim considers it his exclusive property, but Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Ork remain there for three unhappy days because they cannot otherwise leave. The island contains both lavender berries that cause shrinking and deep purple berries that restore normal size.
