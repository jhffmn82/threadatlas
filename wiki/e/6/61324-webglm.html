WebGLM

method · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: WebGLM (GLM-10b), WebGLM (Llama2-13b), WebGLM-10b, WebGLM-13b

Abstract written by a model

WebGLM is a state-of-the-art retrieval-augmented generation method that jointly optimizes coarse-grained web search and fine-grained LLM-augmented retrieval. It fine-tunes its retriever and uses a GLM reward model to evaluate answers. It is evaluated with GLM-10b and versions trained on Vicuna, Llama2, and ChatGLM2. Used as a strong comparison method and CoV-RAG’s retrieval basis, it is consistently outperformed by CoV-RAG and struggles with questions unsuitable for retrieval.

WebGLM is in one work on this site, Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
