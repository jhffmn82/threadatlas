Delabole quarries in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Delabole quarries are a stark industrial world of enormous slate masses and debris heaps, worked since Queen Elizabeth’s time. In the region of Tintagel and Camelford, wagons, horses, and steam engines sustain wholesome labor, producing slate for nearby houses, walls, gates, and fences.

The Delabole quarries are a stark, industrial landscape of enormous slate masses and old heaps of debris. Slate is used for houses, walls, gates, and fences, while wagons, horses, and steam engines create an atmosphere of wholesome labor.
