Ygrayne

Abstract written by a model

Ygrayne, a hapless woman in the Arthurian legends, is first the duke of Cornwall’s wife. King Uther Pendragon falls in love with her; after her husband is slain, she marries Uther, bears Arthur, and later recognizes him when he is proclaimed king.

Ygrayne is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
