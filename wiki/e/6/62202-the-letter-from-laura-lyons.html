Abstract written by a model

Laura Lyons’s partially burned letter, sent from Coombe Tracey in a woman’s hand and signed L. L., asks Sir Charles Baskerville to burn it and meet her at the gate by ten o’clock. She wrote to seek his help with expenses for regaining freedom from her husband. Sir Charles burned it, though a fragment remained legible.

Chapter 10.

The letter from Laura Lyons is not identified by a full name in the surviving fragment, but it was sent from Coombe Tracey in a woman’s hand and signed with the initials L. L. It asked Sir Charles to burn the letter and meet the writer at the gate by ten o’clock.

Chapter 11.

Mrs. Laura Lyons's letter asked Sir Charles Baskerville to meet her at the gate by ten o’clock and urged him to burn it. She says that she wrote it to seek his help with expenses connected to her effort to regain her freedom from her husband. Sir Charles burned the letter, but part of it remained legible.
