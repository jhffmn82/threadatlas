Army departure from Oogaboo begins as officers relay “For-ward March!”; Files leads, officers follow, and Queen Ann comes last through a narrow mountain pass toward Fairyland of Oz.

Chapter Two

The army departs after the Generals, Colonels, Majors, and Captains pass the command "For-ward March!" down the ranks to Files. Files marches first, the officers follow, and Queen Ann comes last as the procession enters the mountain pass toward Fairyland of Oz.
