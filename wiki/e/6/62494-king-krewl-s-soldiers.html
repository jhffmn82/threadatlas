King Krewl's soldiers in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

King Krewl's soldiers serve him and guard Princess Gloria by force; they try to capture the Scarecrow, then after Krewl's defeat support the Scarecrow, bind Krewl and Googly-Goo, and bring Krewl before him.

Chapter Eighteen

The soldiers of Jinxland try to capture the Scarecrow, but their weapons only make holes in his clothes. After the Orks defeat King Krewl, the soldiers join the cheering, bind Krewl and Googly-Goo, and drag Krewl before the Scarecrow.
