Queen Guinevere in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Queen Guinevere, daughter of King Leodegrance and wife of King Arthur, loves Sir Launcelot to the detriment of her marriage. She forsakes Arthur for Launcelot; tradition says her body was found with Arthur’s at Winchester.

DAY THE THIRTEENTH Into King Arthurs land--Tintagel his birth-place, and Camelford, where he fought his last battle--the legendary region of which one may believe as much or as little as one pleases--we were going to-day.

Queen Guinevere is the daughter of King Leodegrance and becomes King Arthur’s wife. She forsakes Arthur for the love of Sir Launcelot, and medieval tradition is said to have found her body with Arthur’s at Winchester.
