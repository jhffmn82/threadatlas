Abstract written by a model

The officers of Oogaboo are sixteen immortal military officers, four Generals, four Colonels, four Majors, and four Captains, from the Army of Oogaboo. Traveling with Ann Soforth, they grow hungry, fretful, and disloyal, panic before the Rak, and run away. Marching behind Queen Ann, they fall into a concealed pit, escape through a secret passage, and later emerge from a rocky tunnel battered and in rags. In the Metal Forest, they capture Ruggedo, then release him after learning Kaliko is the new Nome King.

Chapter Three

The sixteen officers travel with Ann Soforth through the strange country and become hungry, fretful, and disloyal. When the Rak appears, they panic, fall to the ground, and later run away when it rolls off them.

Chapter Sixteen

The officers of Oogaboo march behind Queen Ann and fall into the concealed pit. They become frightened and unhappy in captivity, then follow Queen Ann through the secret escape passage.

Chapter Seventeen

The Oogaboo people are expected to be in the pit with Queen Ann, but Pang reports that they have escaped and are no longer there.

Chapter Twenty-One
