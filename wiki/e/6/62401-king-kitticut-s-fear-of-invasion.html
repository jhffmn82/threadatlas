King Kitticut’s fear of invasion in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

King Kitticut’s fear that Regos and Coregos will invade Pingaree again drives a constant beach watch for strange boats, though he keeps the threat from making his little-trained people unhappy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

King Kitticut fears that Regos and Coregos may send a larger army to conquer Pingaree and seek revenge for the earlier defeat. He maintains a constant lookout for strange boats because his people are little trained in fighting, although he does not allow this fear to make them unhappy.
