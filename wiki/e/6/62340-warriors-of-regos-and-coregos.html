warriors of Regos and Coregos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The warriors of Regos and Coregos are fierce invading warriors. First, ten boatloads land on Pingaree to plunder and conquer but are defeated by its people and destroyed with their boats by a storm. Later, warriors from the North return in hundreds of boats, seize inhabitants, plunder, destroy buildings, and carry captives away as slaves.

Chapter One

The warriors of Regos and Coregos are fierce and warlike. Ten boatloads once landed suddenly on Pingaree to plunder and conquer, but the island's people defeated them, and a storm destroyed the warriors and their boats.

Chapter Three

The Warriors from the North land on Pingaree in hundreds of boats, seize the inhabitants, plunder the island, destroy its buildings, and carry the captives away as slaves.
