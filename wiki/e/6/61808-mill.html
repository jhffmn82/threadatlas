1 section; each heading opens the text

ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

A mill's economical power arrangement depends on its required total power and the relationship among its engines, boilers, transmission equipment, and operating conditions. Dividing power among several engines can allow one department to stop while the others continue operating.
