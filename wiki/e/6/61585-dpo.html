DPO in RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Juntong Song, Xingguang Wang, Juno Zhu, Yuanhao Wu, Xuxin Cheng, Randy Zhong, and Cheng Niu · 2024-11

DPO is a training method for fine-tuning LLMs, including those used in RAG setups, to mitigate hallucinations while improving response and answer quality. It begins with a preference dataset of paired outputs: chosen or corrected responses that are free of hallucinations and rejected original or alternative responses. Such pairs may be automatically constructed through factuality evaluations, or built after supervised fine-tuning from sampled outputs ranked by prediction correctness; some rejected responses reflect overly cautious penalization. DPO then trains models to prefer responses with less hallucinatory content. Evaluations report markedly reduced hallucination rates and improved answer-quality win rates versus the original model, without sacrificing answer quality. However, one application using ranked sampled outputs remained suboptimal for hallucination-detection classification compared with training a model to output prediction labels directly.

Abstract

Direct Preference Optimization training uses a preference dataset containing original outputs and corrected outputs that are free of hallucinations. The resulting fine-tuning leads to large language models with a reduced hallucination rate and improved answer quality.

Introduction

Direct Preference Optimization is used with paired original and revised RAG outputs to train the LLM used in the RAG setup. This process is reported to reduce hallucination rates and improve response quality.

DPO is used to train LLMs to generate responses with reduced hallucination content. Its training data consists of chosen responses and rejected alternatives, including responses modified through overly cautious penalization.

Experiments / Implementation Details .. Answer Quality

DPO training is evaluated for its ability to mitigate hallucinations without sacrificing answer quality. The results indicate that DPO-trained RAG-HAT reduces hallucinations and improves answer-quality win rates compared with the original model.
