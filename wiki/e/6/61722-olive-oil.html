COTTON SEED OIL.

Olive oil was the oil traditionally used by S.S. Bradford for lead plaster and was compared with cotton seed oil in several liniments. When pure olive oil is mixed with liquor plumbi subacetatis, it does not undergo the reddish color change observed with cotton seed oil.
