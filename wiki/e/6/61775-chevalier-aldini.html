ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

Chevalier Aldini applied Davy's wire-gauze discovery to an envelope intended to allow a person to enter flames. He traveled through Europe giving free demonstrations of the apparatus, including demonstrations before a committee of the Academic des Sciences in Paris.
