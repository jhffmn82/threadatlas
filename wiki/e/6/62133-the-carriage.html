Abstract written by a model

The carriage is a comfortable vehicle used for the Lizard journey. Drawn by a horse, it carries the narrator and two young companions from Falmouth at a leisurely pace, stopping for blackberries and heaths. On the next day, Charles drives the women through Lizard Town and the countryside toward Kennack Sands, Poltesco, and Cadgwith, taking an inland route when the cliff route cannot be used.

DAY THE SECOND Is there anything more delightful than to start on a smiling morning in a comfortable carriage, with all one's _impedimenta_ (happily not much!

The comfortable carriage carries the narrator and her two young companions from Falmouth toward the Lizard. It stops while the young companions gather blackberries and later while Charles collects heaths, and it proceeds at a leisurely pace.

] DAY THE THIRD "And a beautiful day it is, ladies, though it won't do for Kynance.

Charles's carriage carries the women travelers through Lizard Town and across the countryside to Kennack Sands, Poltesco, and Cadgwith. It cannot use the cliff route directly from Poltesco to Cadgwith, so Charles drives one woman inland while the younger women walk with a guide.
