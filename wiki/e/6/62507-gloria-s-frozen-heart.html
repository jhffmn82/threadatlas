Gloria's frozen heart

Abstract written by a model

Gloria's heart, belonging to Princess Gloria, is frozen by the Wicked Witch: it turns from vivid red to gray and frosted white, leaving Gloria unable to feel mortal love and unfit to rule Jinxland. In Chapter Nineteen, Blinkie's spell melts it with purple magic, restoring warmth, normal beating, and Gloria's loving nature.

Gloria's frozen heart is in one work on this site, The Scarecrow of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Fourteen

Gloria's heart changes from vivid red to gray and then white as frost and icicles form around it. Afterward, Gloria is unable to feel mortal love and responds to Pon, Trot, and Googly-Goo with coldness or rejection.

Chapter Nineteen

Gloria's heart is initially frozen, making her unfit to rule Jinxland. Blinkie's spell causes the ice to disappear and the heart to become warm and beat normally, restoring Gloria's loving nature.
