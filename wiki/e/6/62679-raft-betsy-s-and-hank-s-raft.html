raft (Betsy's and Hank's raft)

Also called: hatch-cover, little raft, raft, rude raft, support

Abstract written by a model

The raft is a hatch-cover torn from the ship's deck. After the ship is wrecked, Betsy pulls onto it and helps Hank aboard. Floating well enough to prevent their drowning, it carries them at sea and gently reaches the sandy beach with them.

Chapter Four

The raft is a hatch-cover torn from the ship's deck. Betsy pulls herself onto it, and Hank swims to it and climbs aboard with her help. It keeps them from drowning and carries them toward land.

Chapter Five

The raft carrying Betsy and Hank gently reaches the sandy beach. Betsy and Hank had caught hold of it after their ship was wrecked and floated ashore.
