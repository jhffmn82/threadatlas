Merripit House

Abstract written by a model

Merripit House is Stapleton’s house near the moor, home to Jack and Beryl Stapleton and associated with Miss Stapleton and Mrs. Stapleton. It contains Stapleton’s museum room and is where Jack invites Dr. Watson to meet his sister. Later, Sir Henry and Watson plan to dine there after a quarrel ends. It becomes the scene of Holmes’s ambush and the hound’s attack; afterward, Mrs. Stapleton is found tied up, and Anthony’s role is suspected.

Merripit House is in one work on this site, The Hound of the Baskervilles. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7.

Merripit House is the home of Jack and Beryl Stapleton, situated near the moor. Jack invites Dr. Watson to visit it and meet his sister, and Beryl approaches Watson from its direction.

Chapter 9.

Merripit House is associated with Miss Stapleton and Stapleton. Sir Henry and Dr. Watson are to dine there the following Friday as a sign that the quarrel between Sir Henry and Stapleton has ended.

Chapter 14.

Merripit House is the site of Holmes’s ambush and the hound attack on Sir Henry. Holmes, Watson, and Lestrade later search the house, where they find Mrs. Stapleton tied to a post.

Chapter 15.

Merripit House was associated with Stapleton and Mrs. Stapleton. An old manservant named Anthony lived there and was likely the confidant who cared for the hound in Stapleton’s absence.
