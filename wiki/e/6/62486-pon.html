Pon in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Pon is a gardener's boy in humble life who serves the gardener, yet he is the son of King Phearse, former King of Jinxland, and has a rightful claim to its royal succession. He loves Princess Gloria devotedly, and she loves him, but King Krewl prevents their marriage. Pon tells Trot and Cap'n Bill that Krewl trapped his father beneath stones in a deep pond and that Krewl's guards beat him while Gloria watched. He shelters and feeds Trot and Button-Bright in his cottage, fears for Gloria's safety, and follows Krewl's soldiers. After Blinkie freezes Gloria's heart, Gloria rejects Pon despite their former love; he joins Trot in searching for missing friends and helps restuff the Scarecrow. Though powerless against Krewl's stronger soldiers, Pon serves as a messenger demanding Krewl's surrender and is beaten by Krewl with a golden staff. He later helps restore the Scarecrow to the throne. When Gloria's heart is restored, she returns to Pon's arms. After she becomes Queen, she names him her Royal Consort, and he changes from his old smock into princely clothing.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Ten

Pon, the gardener's boy, tells Trot and Cap'n Bill that Princess Gloria loves him and that King Krewl is preventing them from marrying. He reveals that he is the son of King Phearse, the former King of Jinxland, whom Krewl trapped beneath stones in a deep pond, and therefore considers himself the rightful prince. Pon also describes being beaten by two of Krewl's guards while Gloria watched.

Chapter Eleven

Pon is the gardener's boy whom Princess Gloria loves. He kneels before Gloria in the garden but escapes by climbing a ladder over the wall when King Krewl approaches.

Chapter Twelve

Pon remains loved by Princess Gloria despite opposition from King Krewl and Googly-Goo. He shelters Trot and Button-Bright in his cottage, feeds them, and suspects that Blinkie enchanted Cap'n Bill.

Chapter Fourteen

Pon follows King Krewl and the soldiers because he fears they intend to harm Gloria, then watches Blinkie freeze her heart. He tries to approach Gloria afterward, but she rejects him, so he joins Trot in searching for their friends.

Chapter Fifteen

Pon searches Jinxland with Trot and is pursued by Blinkie. He helps Trot restuff the Scarecrow and again appeals to Princess Gloria, who remains unable to love because the Wicked Witch has frozen her heart.

Chapter Sixteen

Pon participates in the council and is sent to King Krewl as a messenger to request his surrender. He enters the King's castle and delivers the summons to surrender to the Scarecrow, but Krewl beats him with a golden staff. Pon returns to the others sore, aching, and sobbing.

Chapter Nineteen

Pon helps replace the Scarecrow on the throne after the Orks blow him out of it. After Gloria's heart is restored, Pon timidly approaches her, and she joyfully returns to his arms.

Chapter Twenty

Pon is initially worried that Gloria will not love him after becoming Queen. Gloria names him her Royal Consort, and he retires to change into princely clothing while his old smock is given to Grewl.
