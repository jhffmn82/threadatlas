Hot-house experiments tested human resistance to extreme heat: Banks, Blagden, and Solander spent ten minutes at 211°F with near-normal body temperatures; Blagden later lasted eight minutes at 262°F, where eggs baked hard in twenty minutes.

ON SOME APPARATUS THAT PERMIT OF ENTERING FLAMES.

Joseph Banks, Charles Blagden, and Dr. Solander remained for ten minutes in a hot-house at 211 degrees Fahrenheit while their bodies stayed near normal temperature. Blagden later endured eight minutes in a hot-house reaching 262 degrees Fahrenheit, while eggs placed nearby were baked hard in twenty minutes.
