[ORESTES _and_ PYLADES _go in, following the_ SERVANTS.

Aegisthus is holding what the Old Man believes to be a feast of worship for the wild-wood nymphs. A wreathed ox has been prepared for the ceremony, and no Argive lord is present with him.
