the Ugly One in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

The Ugly One is Shaggy Man's lost brother, a miner who vanished in a Colorado gold mine about ten years earlier and was suspected by Shaggy Man of having been captured by the Nome King. He is held prisoner by Ruggedo in the Nome Kingdom, sent to the Metal Forest and unable to leave, though he is fed and rested. He does not work for the nomes because he can do nothing as well as they can. Once handsome, he is transformed by Ruggedo's enchantment into the homeliest man in the world. Ashamed and sensitive to ridicule, he hides in a hut and wears a polka-dot handkerchief as a mask. Shaggy Man and his companions seek the Metal Forest to free him. The Ugly One survives there on Three-Course Nuts and breakfast nuts. When Polychrome kisses him, the charm is broken: he is restored to his former appearance and recognized again as Shaggy Man's brother.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

Shaggy Man's lost brother was a miner in Colorado who entered a gold mine and never came out. He disappeared about ten years earlier, and Shaggy Man suspects that the Nome King captured him.

Chapter Seven

Shaggy Man's brother is being held prisoner in the Nome King's cavern. Tik-Tok confirms that he is there after seeing him through Ozma's magic picture.

Chapter Nine

The Ugly One is imprisoned by Ruggedo and has been sent to the Metal Forest. Ruggedo says Shaggy Man may have him, but believes the invaders will have difficulty finding the forest.

Chapter Eleven

Shaggy's lost brother is the person the party was seeking to free when it set out against Ruggedo. The Private Citizen hears that the party's journey was connected with this effort.

Chapter Nineteen

The Ugly One is identified as Shaggy Man's brother, who remains confined in the Metal Forest. Kaliko says he is well fed and rested but cannot leave when he pleases, and Shaggy Man asks to rescue him.

Chapter Twenty

The Ugly One has reportedly been a prisoner in the Metal Forest for two or three years. His whereabouts are being sought by Shaggy and the others, who believe he may be in the part of the cavern containing ordinary food-bearing trees.

Chapter Twenty-One

The Ugly One hides in a hut because Ruggedo's enchantment changed him from a handsome man into the homeliest man in the world. He eventually leaves the hut wearing a handkerchief mask, but he fears returning to the world unless he can remain masked.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Ugly One explains that he has survived in the natural forest by eating Three-Course Nuts and breakfast nuts. After Polychrome kisses him, his transformation is reversed and he becomes Shaggy Man's brother again.

the Shaggy Man's brother in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Shaggy Man's brother, a kindly, honest fellow, is held prisoner by Ruggedo after years imprisoned by the Nome King, then recovered and freed from ugliness; Ozma admits him to Oz with Shaggy Man and others.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Four

Shaggy Man's brother is recovered from his long imprisonment by the Nome King and is freed from the enchantment of ugliness. Although he has no independent claim to a home in Oz, Ozma admits him so that Shaggy Man and the others can come there.
