Mary Mundy's brother in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Mary Mundy’s brother is a man who helps manage the household but depends on Mary Mundy. At the Old Inn, he receives travelers during her marketing absence, regrets her absence, and struggles with household and tea arrangements while promising them tea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

We had just time to dine, repose a little, and communicate our address to our af

Mary's brother receives the travelers at the Old Inn while Mary is away marketing in Helstone. He is disappointed that she is absent and speaks of being unable to manage the full house and a party's requested tea without her, though he promises to provide tea for the travelers.
