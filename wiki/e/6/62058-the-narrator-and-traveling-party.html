Abstract written by a model

A traveling party on a Cornish tour, accompanied by Charles as carriage driver, moves through the Lizard, Marazion, Penzance, St. Ives, Land's End, and Whitesand Bay. Their chief goal is Land's End, a lifelong desire reached on a quiet Sunday after travel from Penzance and a visit to the Logan Rock. Before that, they spend their last Lizard day bathing, observing a solar rainbow, touring the Lizard Lights, and hearing wreck accounts; they also take an open-boat bath near Marazion and visit St. Michael's Mount. They admire scenery and associations but dislike Marazion's beach and St. Ives. At Land's End they explore cliffs with a sailor guide and hear local stories. Preferring terra firma to a mine, they return to Whitesand Bay for shell collecting, revisit Land's End, return to Penzance, and arrange departure.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

" DAY THE EIGHTH And seven days were all we could allow ourselves at the Lizard, if we meant to see the rest of Cornwall.

The traveling women spend their last day at the Lizard bathing, observing a solar rainbow, touring the Lizard Lights, and listening to accounts of wrecks. They decide to travel to Marazion and add a boat trip to sea-caves before leaving.

] DAY THE TENTH I cannot advise Marazion as a bathing place.

The travelers bathe from an open boat near Marazion, visit St. Michael’s Mount, and travel through Penzance and Castle-an-Dinas to St. Ives. They admire the scenery and historical associations but find Marazion’s beach and St. Ives town unattractive. They also speak with a Cornish milkmaid and a kindly elderly man in St. Ives.

DAY THE ELEVENTH The last thing before retiring, we had glanced out on a gloomy sea, a starless sky, pitch darkness, broken only by those moving lights on St.

The narrator and traveling party travel from Penzance toward Land's End, visit the Logan Rock, and reach Land's End after many years of anticipation. They explore the cliffs with a sailor guide, hear local stories, and return home through rain after briefly seeing Whitesand Bay.

DAY THE TWELFTH Monday morning.

The narrator and her companions choose to revisit Whitesand Bay rather than see Botallack Mine or the Gurnard's Head. They spend several lazy hours on the beach, visit Land's End again, return to Penzance, and arrange their departure for the following day.
