Abstract written by a model

The animal black furnace is Schreiber's apparatus for continuously revivifying bone-black. It has a masonry casing, coke-fired fire-place chamber, automatic upper drying system, and retorts in two rows. Bone-black moves from hopper through drier and retorts to cooling pipes and rotary cylinders, which regulate discharge into a rail car. It revivifies 120 hectoliters daily using 360–400 kilogrammes of coke.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

212 " SCHREIBER'S APPARATUS FOR REVIVIFYING BONE-BLACK. .. _The Animal Black Furnace_.

The animal black furnace has a masonry casing, a coke-fired chamber, and two rows of undulating cast-iron retorts enclosed in refractory clay blocks. Bone-black passes from the hopper through the drier, retorts, and cooling pipes; the cooling pipes end in rotary cylinders that regulate its discharge into a rail car. The furnace can revivify 120 hectoliters of bone-black in twenty-four hours with 360 to 400 kilogrammes of coke.
