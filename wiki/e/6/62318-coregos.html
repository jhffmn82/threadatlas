Chapter Nine

Coregos is the city where Queen Cor lives. Zella customarily carries honey there for the Queen, and her parents decide she should go there the next morning.

Chapter Ten

Coregos houses King Gos and his warriors after they flee from Regos, causing its people to complain about the crowding and loss of provisions. Queen Cor returns there with Inga and Rinkitink after capturing them.
