Abstract written by a model

A combustion tube is heated to redness by a Bunsen burner or alcohol lamp; gas mixed with atmospheric air passes through its palladium wire, causing combustion.

BUNTE'S BURETTE FOR THE ANALYSIS OF FURNACE GASES. .. _Mode of Measuring the Gases and Absorption_.

The combustion tube is heated to redness by a Bunsen burner or alcohol lamp. A mixture of gas and atmospheric air passes through it in the presence of a palladium wire, causing combustion.
