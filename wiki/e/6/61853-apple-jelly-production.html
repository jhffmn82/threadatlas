THE MANUFACTURE OF APPLE JELLY.

The Mexico factory's current production was 1,500 to 1,800 pounds of jelly per ten-hour day, and improvements were expected to increase output to more than one ton per day. Each bushel of apples yielded four to five pounds of jelly, and the finished product was packaged hot for shipment.
