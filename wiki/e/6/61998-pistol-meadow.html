Pistol Meadow in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Pistol Meadow is a green area on the cliff path containing foreign sailors' shipwreck burial place, feared after dark because of graves and stories that other things walk there.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

"Shall we get a boat?

Pistol Meadow is a green area where local people do not like to walk after dark. It is associated with the graves of foreign sailors and stories that other things walk there.
