Generating and Ranking (GR) framework in Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration

Han-Cheng Yu, Yu-An Shih, Kin-Man Law, Kai-Yu Hsieh, Yu-Chen Cheng, Hsin-Chih Ho, Zih-An Lin, Wen-Chuan Hsu, Yao-Chung Fan · 2024

Generating and Ranking (GR) is a distractor generation architecture and state-of-the-art Distractor Generation approach. It first generates a small, diverse candidate set using a knowledge base or language model, then ranks candidates with semantic rules and linguistic features, significantly improving distractor quality over traditional rule-based methods.

2 / 2.1 .. 2.3

The Generating and Ranking (GR) framework first creates a small candidate set of distractors and then ranks the candidates using semantic rules and linguistic features. Its candidate-generation approaches use either a knowledge base or a language model, improving distractor quality and diversity over traditional rule-based methods.
