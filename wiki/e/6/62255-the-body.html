Selden’s body, initially mistaken for Sir Henry Baskerville because of its ruddy tweed suit, is found face down on rocks below a cliff with a crushed skull; after a scream, it is covered on the moor until morning.

The body is first mistaken for Sir Henry Baskerville because it wears Sir Henry’s distinctive ruddy tweed suit. Holmes and Dr. John Watson discover that it is Selden, lying face down in a grotesque position with a crushed skull, and leave it covered on the moor until morning.
