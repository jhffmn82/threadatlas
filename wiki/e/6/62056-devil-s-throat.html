Devil's Throat

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Devil's Throat, also called Hell's Mouth, is a dangerous rock passage requiring a jump over boiling waves. John Curgenven identifies it as a place visitors should not attempt; despite an elderly clergyman's warning, two young people crawl toward its fissure and cave.

Devil's Throat is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
