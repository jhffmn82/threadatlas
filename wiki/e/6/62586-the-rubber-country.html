Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

The Rubber Country contains gray rubber rocks, trees, paths, and stepping-stones that cause people who touch them to bounce. Its stream is dry water, allowing the adventurers to wade across without getting wet.
