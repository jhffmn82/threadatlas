Building simulator algorithm

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Algorithm 2, Building simulator

Abstract written by a model

In PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers, the Building simulator algorithm provides the locating simulator mechanism, initializes total demand and supply, iteratively calculates demand input and supply output for up to 10 cycles, and computes current prices for goods. In PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers, Algorithm 2 initializes prices and TS(g), runs at most 10 cycles, calculates CI(g,b) and CO(g,b), and determines CP(g) from demand-supply differences to avoid local minimum prices.
