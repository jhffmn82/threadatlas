the Islands of Regos and Coregos in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The Islands of Regos and Coregos are a pair of islands ruled by King Gos and Queen Cor, whose people were conquered by them. The White Pearl sends Prince Inga there to free his enslaved parents; Gos claims Inga’s parents came to conquer, kill, and seize riches.

Chapter Five

The White Pearl advises Prince Inga to go to the Islands of Regos and Coregos, where he may liberate his parents from slavery.

Chapter Seventeen

Regos and Coregos are ruled by King Gos and Queen Cor. Gos claims that King Kitticut and Queen Garee came to these islands to conquer them, kill their people, and seize their riches.
