Chapter Seven

Molasses candy is served hot from a bubbling kettle in the Bumpy Man's house. Trot lets it cool, pulls it with her hands, and finds that it pulls beautifully; Cap'n Bill and the Ork eat some, while the Bumpy Man refuses to try it.
