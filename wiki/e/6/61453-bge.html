BGE

Also called: bge-en-large-v1.5, dense retriever BGE

Abstract written by a model

In "RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment," BGE is used as the primary retriever in the basic RAG system, paired with Llama-3-8B-Instruct in the main experiments and with alternative generators in combination experiments. In "Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," bge-en-large-v1.5 is used as both the embedding model for chunking and the retriever model for RAG. In "HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation," BGE is evaluated as a dense-retriever unstructured RAG baseline.
