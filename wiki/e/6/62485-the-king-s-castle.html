the King's castle in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

King Krewl's castle is a splendid royal castle in Jinxland, with towers above the trees, a garden before it, a Council Chamber, decorated winding passages, a central courtyard, and richly furnished rooms. It is King Krewl's residence and the seat of the new ruler, occupied by him, courtiers, and soldiers. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright approach it; Gloria lives there after Krewl drags her inside and locks her in a lower-floor room. The visitors are separated, Cap'n Bill escapes through a window, and later Pon is beaten there but escapes. By the end, the Ork can find its towers from the air, while Trot and Button-Bright have been turned out.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

King Krewl's castle is a splendid structure whose towers rise above the surrounding trees. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright walk toward it after learning that it belongs to King Krewl.

Chapter Ten

Princess Gloria lives in King Krewl's castle. After striking Pon, King Krewl dragged Gloria into the castle.

Chapter Eleven

King Krewl's castle is a large, richly furnished building with winding decorated passages, a central courtyard, and rooms that separate the three visitors. Gloria is locked in one of its lower-floor rooms.

Chapter Twelve

King Krewl's castle contains Gloria's locked room and the room where Cap'n Bill sleeps. Trot and Button-Bright are later forbidden to enter it, and Cap'n Bill leaves it only after being transformed and escaping through a window.

Chapter Sixteen

Pon enters the King's castle and passes into an enclosed court where King Krewl is seated with his courtiers. After Pon delivers the summons, Krewl has soldiers hold him while he beats him, and Pon then escapes from the castle.

Chapter Seventeen

The King's castle has towers that the Ork can locate from the air. Button-Bright has been turned out of the castle with Trot.
