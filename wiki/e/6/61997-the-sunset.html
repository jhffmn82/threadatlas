Abstract written by a model

The sunset occurs over the Atlantic beyond the Land's End, during a boat trip and as watched from nearby cliffs. The sun drops like a red round ball into the sea; after its last light disappears, the Lizard Lights burst out and an unusual green gleam appears, holding two young lovers' attention.
