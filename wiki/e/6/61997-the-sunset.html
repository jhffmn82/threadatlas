the sunset in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

The sunset occurs over the Atlantic beyond the Land's End, during a boat trip and as watched from nearby cliffs. The sun drops like a red round ball into the sea; after its last light disappears, the Lizard Lights burst out and an unusual green gleam appears, holding two young lovers' attention.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

"Shall we get a boat?

The sunset occurs during the boat trip, with the sun described as dropping like a red round ball into the sea. Immediately after its last light disappears, the Lizard Lights burst out.

DAY THE FOURTH Sunday, September 4th--and we had started on September 1st; was it possible we had only been travelling four days?

The sunset is watched by the narrator from the cliffs beyond the Land's End. After the sun disappears into the Atlantic, an unusual green gleam appears, and the narrator quietly leaves two young lovers who are absorbed in watching it.
