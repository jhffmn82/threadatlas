Abstract written by a model

In Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration, knowledge triplets are retrieved from a knowledge graph as auxiliary information for text-to-text distractor generation and guide the model after ranking by similarity or supervised classification, although irrelevant triplets can add noise and interfere with generation. In HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications, knowledge graph triplets are extracted from raw text using GPT-4o to provide added context for large language models, with each triplet consisting of a subject, predicate, and object. In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, knowledge triples are generated from passages by REANO to capture critical information, complement existing knowledge graphs, reduce the number of passages supplied to a decoder while preserving useful information, support GNN-based relevance selection, and provide context within and across passages. In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, relevant triples are selected from the knowledge graph by similarity and updated entity embeddings, then concatenated by descending relevance into an additional passage. In Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, triplets consist of a head entity, relation, tail entity, and the chunk from which they are derived; they form the knowledge graph and are concatenated into structured representations used to rank maximum spanning trees.

Han-Cheng Yu, Yu-An Shih, Kin-Man Law, Kai-Yu Hsieh, Yu-Chen Cheng, Hsin-Chih Ho, Zih-An Lin, Wen-Chuan Hsu, Yao-Chung Fan · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where knowledge triplets first appears

Abstract written by a model

Knowledge triplets are retrieved from a knowledge graph as auxiliary information for text-to-text distractor generation. Using question, answer, and candidate-distractor keywords, they are ranked by similarity or supervised classification, and top triplets guide the model. However, irrelevant triplets can add noise and interfere with generation, so relevance selection needs strengthening.

knowledge triples in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11 to 2024-08-16 · the work’s page · read it where knowledge triples first appears

Abstract written by a model

Knowledge triples are generated from passages by REANO to capture critical information and complement existing knowledge graphs. They help reduce the number of passages supplied to a decoder while preserving useful information. A GNN module and an additional GNN model select triples relevant to questions. They provide context within and across passages, exemplified by identifying two railway stations as located in Pakistan to answer whether they share a country.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Knowledge triples provide additional context that helps the reader understand passages and answer questions. Inter-context triples capture relations across passages, while intra-context triples capture relations among entities within the same passages. Triples selected from all 50 passages can preserve useful information even when only a smaller number of passages is supplied to the decoder.

opening .. 5

Knowledge triples generated from passages can represent information such as a railway station’s country. The unit identifies the triples stating that Muzaffargarh Railway Station and Raisan Railway Station are located in Pakistan as highly relevant to the question of whether they are in the same country.
