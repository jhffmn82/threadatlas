APPARATUS FOR OBTAINING PURE WATER FOR PHOTOGRAPHIC USE.

Naturally occurring water contains dissolved inorganic and organic impurities and gases. Impurities in water can shorten the effectiveness of iron and pyrogallic developers, produce calcium oxalate veils on negatives, and affect silver prints and carbon printing.
