How to be Good in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

How to be Good is a subject of Rinkitink's reading and the title of a manuscript. Its chapter teaches avoiding evil, speaking agreeably, defining a good man as never bad and good children as seldom punished, and pleasing others through goodness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

"How to be Good" teaches that a good man is one who is never bad and that people should avoid evil things. It also advises people to speak agreeably, says good children are seldom punished, and claims that being good may please other people more than being bad.
