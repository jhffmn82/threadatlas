Also called: Mistral Reader, TRACE reader, readers

Abstract written by a model

The reader is a component that generates answers from questions and prepended context, including selected documents or reasoning chains. Prompted for zero-shot in-context learning, it is instructed to output only an answer grounded in that context and uses greedy decoding. It is used with baseline-selected documents, then varied with Mistral-7B-v0.1 and Gemma-7B to test whether TRACE reasoning chains generalize across models, with similar results to LLaMA3.

The reader is prompted with in-context learning in a zero-shot setting to generate answers. It is instructed to output only the answer based on the given context, such as reasoning chains or documents.

Baselines

The reader generates answers from questions and selected documents or reasoning chains. It is also used with the documents selected by the baselines.

Additional Implementation Details

The reader receives questions with prepended context and generates answers. It uses greedy decoding.

Additional Experimental Results and .. MuSiQue

The reader is varied to test whether TRACE's reasoning chains generalize across models. Mistral-7B-v0.1 and Gemma-7B are used as readers, and their results are described as similar to those obtained with LLaMA3.
