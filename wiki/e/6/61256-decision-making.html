Decision making is a task that supports organizations by analyzing data to select the best alternative for a goal. It unfolds through planning analysis, retrieving data, and deciding for complex business questions.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Decision making is described as analyzing data and selecting the most suitable alternative to achieve a goal. It generally involves planning the needed analysis, retrieving data, and making a decision.

Conclusions

The paper addresses decision making through a task requiring the best decision for complex questions involving business rules and business situations represented in a large database.
