INFO-RAG

method · 1 work · 40 quoted facts · 15 connections
Also called: + INFO-RAG, Our INFO-RAG, information refinement training method, our INFO-RAG, our method

Abstract written by a model

INFO-RAG is an unsupervised, low-cost, general information-refinement training method for optimizing large language models for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Its central aim is to enable language models to refine retrieved information rather than simply use it as given. It classifies retrieved texts into three scenarios: texts that are complete, incomplete or incorrect, and relevant but answerless. For each scenario, it defines positive information gain, constructs sample pairs whose outputs improve on their inputs, and creates a corresponding unsupervised training task. Each sample is processed in only one scenario to avoid data leakage. The method unfolds through data collection, data construction, and unsupervised multi-task learning. It constructs Wikipedia-based training pairs, using prefixes and simulated retrieved texts so that large language models learn to condition target continuations on them. It then mixes the three information-refinement tasks during training. Across zero-shot evaluation on eleven datasets and seven tasks, INFO-RAG improves retrieval-augmented generation performance in short-answer, multi-hop, long-form, dialogue, language-modeling, and code-generation tasks. It improves LLaMA2 by an average of 9.39% relative points in the reported zero-shot prediction experiments, improves performance in all three scenarios, and improves SearChain in the open-retrieval setting. It also enhances in-context learning and can combine with the SOTA RAG framework for further gains. Its robustness results cover changes in the ratio and position of positive passages and in the number of retrieved passages, including incorrect, incomplete, and noisy retrieved texts. Finally, it enhances RAG while avoiding catastrophic forgetting: on MMLU without RAG, it performs close to the original LLaMA-2.

Abstract

INFO-RAG is an unsupervised information-refinement training method for optimizing large language models for RAG. It is described as low-cost and general across tasks, and it improves LLaMA2’s zero-shot performance by an average of 9.39% relative points across 11 datasets while also improving in-context learning and RAG robustness.

Introduction

INFO-RAG is an unsupervised training method that mixes three information-refinement tasks for retrieved texts that are complete, incomplete or incorrect, and relevant but answerless. It is reported to improve zero-shot RAG for LLaMA2 across question answering, slot-filling, language modeling, dialogue, and code generation, as well as in-context learning and RAG robustness.

Related Work / Retrieval Augmented Generation .. Our INFO-RAG

INFO-RAG is an unsupervised training method for enabling language models to perform information refinement in RAG. It summarizes retrieved texts into three scenarios, defines positive information gain for each, constructs sample pairs with information gain from input to output, and trains language models on three corresponding tasks.

Positive Information Gain in RAG

INFO-RAG is illustrated as using data collection, data construction, and unsupervised multi-task learning for the three scenarios. Each sample is processed for only one scenario to avoid data leakage.

Unsupervised Learning .. Training Strategy

INFO-RAG introduces an unsupervised learning method that constructs Wikipedia-based training pairs for information refinement. It trains large language models to condition target continuations on prefixes and simulated retrieved texts.

Experiments / Datasets and Evaluation Metrics .. Enhancing Previous SOTA in Open-Retrieval

INFO-RAG is evaluated as an unsupervised training method across eleven datasets and seven tasks. It improves retrieval-augmented generation performance across short-answer, multi-hop, long-form, dialogue, language-modeling, and code-generation tasks, and it further improves in-context learning and SearChain results.

Analysis / Fine-grained Analysis for Three Scenarios .. Avoid Catastrophic Forgetting Experiment on

INFO-RAG improves performance in all three RAG scenarios and is more robust to incorrect, incomplete, and noisy retrieved texts. It outperforms LLaMA-2 with RAG on the reported overall score, improves robustness to changes in retrieval results, and performs close to the original LLaMA-2 on MMLU without RAG.

Conclusion

INFO-RAG is an unsupervised, low-cost, and general information refinement training method. It improves LLM performance for RAG across 11 datasets and 7 tasks in a zero-shot setting, while also providing advantages in ICL and robustness and combining with the SOTA RAG framework for further gains.
