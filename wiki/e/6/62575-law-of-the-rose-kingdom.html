Law of the Rose Kingdom

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Law, Laws, the Law

Abstract written by a model

The Law of the Rose Kingdom governs its domains by forbidding strangers, including shipwrecked people, from entering. It requires the Ruler to condemn entering strangers to death and treats breaking greenhouse glass or intruding anywhere as violations. Though strangers enter, they leave before formal condemnation.

Chapter Five

The Law of the Rose Kingdom forbids strangers from entering its domains. The Royal Gardener says that the law makes no exception for shipwrecks.

Chapter Six

The Law of the Rose Kingdom states that strangers who enter the kingdom must be condemned by the Ruler and put to death. It also treats breaking the greenhouse glass and intruding into any part of the kingdom as violations, although the strangers leave before any Ruler formally condemns them.
