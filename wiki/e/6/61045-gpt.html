Generative Pre-trained Transformers in Improving Assessment of Tutoring Practices using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Zifei (FeiFei) Han · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Generative Pre-trained Transformers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Generative Pre-trained Transformers are large language models, including GPT-3.5 and GPT-4, investigated for automatically assessing human tutors’ social-emotional competencies. Using RAG-based prompts, they selectively retrieve database information and analyze tutoring transcripts according to specified principles and rubrics. They generate assessment scores, evidence, interpretations, and feedback, supporting evaluation of accuracy and financial feasibility for real-time, large-scale use. Across prompt strategies, GPT-4 generally yields more accurate evaluations than GPT-3.5, while RAG-based prompting performs consistently well across versions.

GPT LLMs in Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain

Abstract written by a model

GPT LLMs outperform smaller-scale open-source alternatives in experiments: they handle retrieval failures more gracefully, are less distracted by noisy documents when gold documents rank highly, show greater overall quality, and follow domain-specific instructions better. They are also more sensitive to prompting. The records progress from retrieval behavior to broader vendor-model comparisons.
