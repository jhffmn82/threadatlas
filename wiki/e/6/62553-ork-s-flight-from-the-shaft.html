Ork's flight from the shaft in The Scarecrow of Oz

Ork's flight from the shaft carries Trot on its shoulders and Cap'n Bill holding its rear legs as its propeller tail rises through the long shaft, bumps rough walls, and lands gently in sunshine.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

During the Ork's flight from the shaft, Trot sits across its shoulders while Cap'n Bill grips its rear legs. The Ork's propeller tail lifts them up the long shaft, though it bumps against the rough walls several times, and it eventually lands gently in the sunshine.
