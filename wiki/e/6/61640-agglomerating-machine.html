agglomerating machine

object · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: compressing machine, machine

Abstract written by a model

An agglomerating machine for compressing coal refuse into fuel. It must be very simple, operate in coal dust, and remain accessible for cleaning. Rapidly worn parts should be replaceable without interrupting operation. It progressively compresses the conglomerate over its surface, then easily removes the product from the mould without pistons or springs.

MACHINE FOR COMPRESSING COAL REFUSE INTO FUEL.

An agglomerating machine should be simple, accessible for cleaning, and designed to permit worn parts to be changed with minimal interruption. It should apply powerful, progressive compression over as much of the conglomerate's surface as possible and remove the product from the mould without relying on fouling pistons or springs.
