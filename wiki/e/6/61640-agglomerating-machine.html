Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MACHINE FOR COMPRESSING COAL REFUSE INTO FUEL.

An agglomerating machine should be simple, accessible for cleaning, and designed to permit worn parts to be changed with minimal interruption. It should apply powerful, progressive compression over as much of the conglomerate's surface as possible and remove the product from the mould without relying on fouling pistons or springs.
