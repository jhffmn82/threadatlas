Oogaboo in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Oogaboo is a small, poor but pretty valley with orchards in a remote mountainous corner of the Land of Oz. Its eighty-nine people include eighteen men, twenty-seven women, and forty-four children, and it keeps a royal family as a matter of pride while remaining under Princess Ozma's rule. Queen Ann's Grand Army leaves through a narrow pass to conquer Ruggedo's Kingdom, but an altered route prevents its return. Regretting the loss of their orchards, Queen Ann and her people ultimately wish to go home, and Ozma sends them back to Oogaboo.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Oogaboo is a small, poor valley in a remote mountainous corner of the Land of Oz. It has a royal family for pride, while its people remain under Princess Ozma's overall rule.

Chapter Two

Oogaboo's Grand Army assembles in the square before the royal palace. The army then marches out of Oogaboo through a narrow mountain pass toward Fairyland of Oz.

Chapter Three

Oogaboo is the army's homeland, a pretty valley with orchards. After the army passes through the altered mountain route, its members cannot find their way back there.

Chapter Sixteen

Oogaboo is the kingdom whose Queen and Army attempt to conquer Ruggedo's Kingdom. Its officers later regret leaving their orchards and wish they were back home.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Oogaboo is described as a corner of the Land of Oz and the home to which Queen Ann and her people wish to return. Ozma sends them back there.
