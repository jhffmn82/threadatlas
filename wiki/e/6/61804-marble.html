marble in Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882

Various · 1881

Marble is a stone closely related to limestone, abundant in the United States in many colors and varieties. Used for statuary, gravestones, ornament, and increasingly building exteriors, it came notably from Vermont and eastern and southern belts and was exported to Cuba.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE BUILDING STONE SUPPLY.

Marble was abundant in the United States and appeared in many colors and varieties. It was used for statuary, gravestones, ornamental features, and increasingly for building exteriors, with important sources in Vermont and a belt extending through the eastern and southern states.
