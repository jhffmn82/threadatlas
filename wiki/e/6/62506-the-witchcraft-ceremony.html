the witchcraft ceremony in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

A ceremony performed by Blinkie and three summoned witches uses substances, chanting, and dancing before Blinkie throws mixture onto Gloria's breast, freezing her heart.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

During the ceremony, Blinkie and three other witches add magical substances to a kettle, chant over it, and dance around Gloria. Blinkie finally throws a ladleful of the mixture onto Gloria's breast, successfully freezing her heart.
