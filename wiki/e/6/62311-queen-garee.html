Queen Garee in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Queen Garee is the queen of Pingaree and mother of Prince Inga. During an invasion, she is seized with the royal household, bound, carried by boat, and made a captive. Queen Cor enslaves her in the royal dairy to make butter, then removes her from Pingaree to Regos with King Kitticut. Later she and Kitticut are chained and taken to the Nome King's caverns, forced to carry treasure, and confined in a small cavern. Though first held under King Gos and Queen Cor, she is grateful for King Kaliko's more considerate care. Inga journeys through the Nome Country to free her. Dorothy ultimately secures the captives' release, and Garee joyfully reunites with Inga and blesses Dorothy. She returns to Pingaree with Kitticut, regains much of her spirits and composure, and directs her maids to embroider new throne draperies.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

Queen Garee is seized with the royal household during the invasion. She is bound, carried to a boat, and taken away with the other captives.

Chapter Thirteen

Queen Garee has been placed in Queen Cor's royal dairy to make butter. She is missing when Inga searches the dairy, although he finds one of her silk scarves. Inga and his companions suspect that Queen Cor has taken her to Regos.

Chapter Fifteen

Queen Garee is dragged from the royal dairy and taken from Pingaree to Regos by Queen Cor. She is carried away as a captive with King Kitticut.

Chapter Seventeen

Queen Garee is taken with King Kitticut as a chained prisoner to the Nome King's caverns and is forced to carry treasure during the journey. She is confined with Kitticut in a small cavern, where their chains are removed. She and Kitticut are grateful to be under Kaliko's more considerate care rather than Gos's control.

Chapter Eighteen

Queen Garee is held captive by King Kaliko after being taken by King Gos and Queen Cor. Inga continues his journey through the Nome Country in an effort to liberate her.

Chapter Twenty-One

Queen Garee is held captive with King Kitticut until Dorothy secures their release. She joyfully embraces Inga and kisses Dorothy's forehead while blessing her for restoring their freedom.

Chapter Twenty Three

Queen Garee returns to Pingaree with King Kitticut after recovering much of her good spirits and composure in Oz. In Pingaree she gathers her maids and has them embroider new draperies for the royal throne.
