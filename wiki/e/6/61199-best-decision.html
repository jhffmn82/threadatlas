Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology: PlanRAG

The decision is made after the language model uses retrieved data analysis results to determine whether additional analysis is needed. The process ends when the language model determines that no further analysis is required.

Simulators for annotation

A decision is supplied to the simulator with D as input, and its effects are calculated using business rules. All decision candidates are simulated to determine the best decision for a question.
