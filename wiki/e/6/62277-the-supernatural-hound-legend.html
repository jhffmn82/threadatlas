the supernatural hound legend

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: West-Country legend, legend, legend (West-Country legend), legends or rumours, the family legend, the stories that the peasants tell about a phantom hound

Abstract written by a model

The supernatural hound legend is a West-Country family legend told by peasants. It is believed fulfilled by Sir Charles’s death, apparently linking the last occupant of Baskerville Hall to a sinister influence. Later, Stapleton raises possible evidence of a phantom hound, but Holmes and Watson report only cries and do not prove it exists.

Chapter 10.

The family legend is cited as having been fulfilled by the death of the last occupant of Baskerville Hall. Its apparent connection with Sir Charles’s death contributes to Watson’s sense that a sinister influence surrounds the family.

A terrible scream—a prolonged yell of horror and anguish—burst

The supernatural hound legend is discussed when Stapleton asks whether Holmes and Dr. John Watson heard evidence of a phantom hound. Holmes and Watson deny hearing anything beyond the cries, while the hound’s actual existence remains unproven in their stated evidence.
