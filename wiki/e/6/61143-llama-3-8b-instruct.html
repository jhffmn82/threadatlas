Llama3-8B in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

Llama3-8B is a model/software evaluated with and without RadioRAG on RSNA-RadioQA and ExtendedQA. On RSNA-RadioQA, it rose from 58% (46/80) to 59% (47/80), a nonsignificant difference, with RadioRAG performance not significantly different from a human expert radiologist. On ExtendedQA, it increased from 50% to 67%; appendix results report 50% average accuracy in both RSNA-RadioQA conditions and context-relevant incorrect-response rates of 22% and 21%.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

Llama3-8B increased from 58% (46/80) to 59% (47/80) on RSNA-RadioQA with RadioRAG, without a statistically significant difference (P=0.39). It showed a relative accuracy improvement of up to 34% on ExtendedQA, and its RadioRAG-powered performance was not significantly different from that of the human expert radiologist on RSNA-RadioQA (P=0.31).

4. Discussion

Llama3-8B achieved 58% accuracy without RadioRAG and 59% with RadioRAG on RSNA-RadioQA. On ExtendedQA, its accuracy increased from 50% to 67% with RadioRAG.

appendix
