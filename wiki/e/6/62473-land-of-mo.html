Land of Mo in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

The Land of Mo is a magic country with mountains, valleys, hills, houses, people, animals, warm popcorn snow, talking educated birds, perfumed winds, and magic berries. It has no water but receives lemonade rain. Trot, Cap'n Bill, and the Ork arrive, then leave its mountain region with Button-Bright to seek another country.
