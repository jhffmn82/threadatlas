Limitations

The method enhances performance on the noise filtering task in retrieval-augmented generation. It introduces an additional predictive flag to assess whether current filtering outcomes should be discarded, reducing True-Flip-Rate while potentially decreasing False-Flip-Rate; training iterations mitigate this effect at increased training cost.
