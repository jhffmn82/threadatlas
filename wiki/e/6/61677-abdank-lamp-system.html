Abstract written by a model

The Abdank lamp system is a new arc electric lamp arrangement with an external balance-and-cut-off regulator, regulating the carbon gap in short steps and automatically cutting off accidents in series-connected lamps.

Abdank lamp system is in one work on this site, Scientific American Supplement No. 360, November 25, 1882. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

ON A NEW ARC ELECTRIC LAMP. .. _Electrical Arrangement_.

The Abdank lamp system uses an external regulator consisting of a balance and a cut-off. It regulates the carbon gap in short successive steps and includes an automatic cut-off for accidents when several lamps are connected in series.
