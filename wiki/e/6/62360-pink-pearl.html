Pink Pearl in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

The Pink Pearl is a magic pearl that protects its owner or bearer from danger, harm, injury, and shipwreck, and once protected King Kitticut's father during the Great Fight. Inga hides it in the pointed toe of his left shoe for the journey to Regos and Coregos. During an invasion, it shields Inga, Rinkitink, Bilbil, and anyone Inga touches, making weapons rebound or fall harmlessly. It is lost when Rinkitink throws Inga's shoe at a cat. Later Inga gives the pearl to Rinkitink, whose vest-pocket pearl protects him and Bilbil in the caverns of the nomes.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The pink magic pearl protects its owner from all dangers that may threaten him. It protected King Kitticut's father during the Great Fight.

Chapter Five

Inga places the Pink Pearl in the pointed toe of his left shoe and secures it there. It promises to protect him from harm during the journey to Regos and Coregos.

Chapter Seven

The Pink Pearl protects Inga and everyone he touches from harm. Its power causes arrows, spears, and other weapons to rebound or fall harmlessly, protecting Inga, Rinkitink, and Bilbil during the invasion.

Chapter Eight

The Pink Pearl is hidden inside Inga's left shoe. It is lost when Rinkitink throws that shoe at a cat, and the shoe is not recovered.

Chapter Eighteen

The Pink Pearl protects from harm the person who carries it. Inga gives it to King Rinkitink, who places it securely in his vest pocket before they enter the caverns of the nomes.

Chapter Nineteen

The Pink Pearl protects Rinkitink from Kaliko's falling rocks, golden net, Bottomless Gulf pit, and flying knives. It also protects Bilbil when Rinkitink's body is touching him.
