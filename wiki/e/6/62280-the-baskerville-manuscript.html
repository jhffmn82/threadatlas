Chapter 2.

The Baskerville manuscript is a family paper dated 1742 and entrusted to Dr. James Mortimer by Sir Charles Baskerville. It records the origin of the Hound of the Baskervilles in the story of Hugo Baskerville and warns the family to avoid the moor during the hours when evil powers are exalted.
