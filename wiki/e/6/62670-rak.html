Abstract written by a model

Rak is a terrible beast with a horrible appetite that flies, runs, and swims, breathes smoke from an internal furnace, and feeds on any living thing. Private Files shoots it, breaking its jaw, wing, and leg, but Rak cannot be killed by gunshot and says its injuries will heal.

Rak is in one work on this site, Tik-Tok of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter Three

The Rak is described as a huge flying, running, and swimming beast that breathes smoke, has a furnace-like body, and feeds on living things. Private Files's bullets break its jaw, left wing, and right leg, but it says that its injuries will heal and that it bears a charmed life.
