Nonestic Ocean

place · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: great body of water, great ocean, ocean, the Nonestic Ocean

Abstract written by a model

In Rinkitink in Oz, the Nonestic Ocean surrounds Pingaree and washes the shores of the Kingdom of Rinkitink, supporting Pingaree’s pearl fishing and trade. In Grampa in Oz, the Nonestic Ocean is a great body of water far to the Northwest; it contains the iceberg, receives Grampa and his companions after an eruption, and is crossed by the iceberg and adventurers toward Ev. In The Gnome King of Oz, the Nonestic Ocean surrounds an island at its center, lies between Peter and Oz, becomes violently turbulent as its waters boil, and carries Peter, Ruggedo, and a damaged ship toward Ev.

Nonestic Ocean in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924 · the work’s page · read it where Nonestic Ocean first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nonestic Ocean is a great body of water far to the Northwest, containing the iceberg. After an eruption casts Grampa and companions onto it, their lava island reaches a snowy shore. It later separates the iceberg from Isa Poso; the iceberg and adventurers cross it, followed by crows and Invisi-Bill flying toward Ev.

CHAPTER 11

The eruption throws Grampa and his companions into the Nonestic Ocean, a great body of water far to the Northwest. Their hardened lava island bobs across its waves until it reaches a snowy shore.

CHAPTER 13

The Nonestic Ocean separates the iceberg from Isa Poso after the connecting land is destroyed. The iceberg travels across its choppy waters, and the transformed adventurers begin flying over it toward Oz.

CHAPTER 15

The Nonestic Ocean is crossed by the three crows and Invisi-Bill during their flight toward Oz. They fly over it for about an hour before reaching the rocky shores of Ev.

Nonestic Ocean in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927 · the work’s page · read it where Nonestic Ocean first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Nonestic Ocean surrounds an island at its center and lies between Peter and Oz. Its strong tides become violent as its waters boil, churn into a huge green wall, and crash; after it sinks again, it covers the exposed sea bottom and carries Peter, Ruggedo, and a damaged ship toward Ev. It is also Ruggedo’s threatened destination for everyone he menaces.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4 Peter Flies With An Odd Bird

The Nonestic Ocean surrounds the island where Peter lands and is described by Ruggedo as lying between Peter and Oz. Its waters suddenly boil and churn, rising into a huge green wall that crashes down with a deafening roar.

CHAPTER 5 Sea Tips Upside Down In Quake

The Nonestic Ocean covers the place where the sea bottom had appeared after it sinks again. Its strong tides carry Peter, Ruggedo, and the damaged ship toward the shores of Ev.

CHAPTER 17 Mystery In The Emerald City

Ruggedo threatens to send everyone at the party to the bottom of the Nonestic Ocean at three o’clock.

CHAPTER 18 The Theft Of The Magic Belt

The Nonestic Ocean is the place where Ruggedo threatens to send everyone after taking the magic belt.
