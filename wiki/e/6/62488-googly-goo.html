Googly-Goo in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Googly-Goo is an old, wealthy, cruel courtier with fangs who wants to marry Princess Gloria regardless of her wishes. He offers King Krewl more than a bushel of precious jewels for permission to marry her, opposes her love for Pon, and pays Blinkie the Witch to freeze Gloria’s heart. Angry when he finds Gloria with Pon, he later pursues her and tries to restrain her, but she knocks him into a muddy ditch. He also helps bind the Scarecrow, proposes and lights a bonfire to burn him, and is driven into a tree by the Orks’ arrival. He falls and ends bound with ropes beside King Krewl.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

Lord Googly-Goo wants to marry Princess Gloria and offers King Krewl a bushel and a half of precious jewels if the marriage occurs. He suggests testing Cap'n Bill with the Wicked Witch and becomes furious when he finds Gloria with Pon.

Chapter Twelve

Googly-Goo helps King Krewl oppose Gloria's love for Pon and agrees to pay Blinkie for the heart-freezing enchantment. He does not accompany King Krewl and Blinkie to Cap'n Bill's room because he goes home to get the promised money and jewels.

Chapter Fourteen

Googly-Goo runs after Gloria and insists that she marry him because he paid for her heart to be frozen and paid King Krewl to permit their marriage. When Gloria refuses, he tries to restrain her, but she strikes him into a muddy ditch.

Chapter Eighteen

Googly-Goo helps bind the Scarecrow and suggests building a bonfire to burn him. He lights the fire, but the Orks' arrival sends him flying into a tree. Afterward, he falls from the tree and is seized and bound beside King Krewl.
