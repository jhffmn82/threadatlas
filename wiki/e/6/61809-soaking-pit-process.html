soaking pit process

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: new mode of treating ingots, new process, soaking pit system

Abstract written by a model

The soaking pit process is a method of treating steel ingots. Freshly stripped ingots are transferred to preheated, covered pits for about 20–30 minutes until their heat is nearly uniform, enabling rolling with initial heat, dispensing with a fuel-fired heating furnace, reducing oxidation, and improving steel yield.

SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The soaking pit process transfers freshly stripped steel ingots into preheated, covered pits, where they stand for about 20–30 minutes while their heat becomes nearly uniform. It is intended to permit rolling with the ingot's initial heat, eliminate the need for a fuel-fired heating furnace, reduce oxidation, and improve steel yield.
