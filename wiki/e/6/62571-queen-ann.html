Queen Ann in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Queen Ann Soforth is the eldest daughter of Oogaboo's royal family and acts as Queen of Oogaboo while her mother searches for King Jol Jemkiph Soforth. Ambitious and dissatisfied with her tiny, poor kingdom, she rules from the royal palace, wears a green, gold-braided uniform and soldier-cap with a purple plume, and organizes and commands the Army of Oogaboo, including its officers, Private Soldier, and Tik-Tok. She plans to conquer the world, beginning with the Land of Oz, and expects wealth, fame, plunder, and heroism; under the Army's arrangement, she is to receive half the plunder. She leads her army out of Oogaboo despite protests, resolves to conquer whatever territory they encounter, and claims land for Oogaboo. Her aggression falters when Hank defeats her attack and Private Files resigns, while the Love Magnet changes her anger into an embrace of Shaggy. She then joins a plan against Ruggedo. During the journey she faces the rubber terrain, crosses dry water, passes through the Tube, lands on a Peculiar Person, and regrets leaving Tititi-Hoochoo's rich country unconquered. Insisting that her Army, especially Tik-Tok, can conquer Ruggedo, she challenges General Guph, directs Tik-Tok's attack, and leads the party into underground caverns to conquer the Nome King and his people. The effort brings apparent imprisonment, escape, and hardship: she reaches the Metal Forest with her officers in torn clothes, a battered crown, and bruised limbs. Learning Ruggedo has been deposed, she asks about breaking the Ugly One's enchantment. Finally, she abandons conquest as too difficult, no longer wants to conquer the world, and earnestly wishes to return to Oogaboo. Ozma has the Wizard transport her and her people home.

Narrative, section by section

11 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Ann Soforth is the eldest daughter of Oogaboo's royal family and acts as Queen after her mother leaves in search of King Jol Jemkiph Soforth. Dissatisfied with ruling a tiny, poor kingdom, she decides to conquer the world and organizes an army of Oogaboo's men.

Chapter Two

Queen Ann Soforth appears in a green and gold uniform, commands the protesting women to keep silent, and orders her Army to form ranks. She leads the army out of Oogaboo, expecting it to return rich and famous through conquest, plunder, and heroism.

Chapter Three

Ann Soforth, Queen of Oogaboo, leads her army toward the Land of Oz intending to conquer it and the world. When the army reaches an unknown territory instead, she decides to conquer whatever land they find and later praises Private Files for his courage.

Chapter Eight

Queen Ann claims the territory for Oogaboo and orders her army to bind Shaggy's companions, but Hank defeats the attack and Private Files resigns. The Love Magnet makes her forget her anger and embrace Shaggy, after which she joins the plan to conquer Ruggedo.

Chapter Nine

Queen Ann becomes angry when the officers of the Army of Oogaboo are bounced around by the rubber terrain. She and her officers initially hesitate to cross the stream but later cross with the rest of the party by wading through the dry water.

Chapter Ten

Queen Ann disappears into the Tube after the officers and is carried through it in a sitting position. She emerges by sailing through the air, lands on a Peculiar Person's head, and struggles with him until the officers separate them.

Chapter Thirteen

Queen Ann is troubled that she must leave Tititi-Hoochoo's rich country unconquered. She claims that her invincible Army can conquer Ruggedo without Quox's help and identifies Tik-Tok as her great Army.

Chapter Fifteen

Queen Ann defies General Guph by challenging him to come up and capture the travelers. She directs Tik-Tok's attack, helps lead the advance, and decides that the group must enter the underground caverns to conquer the Nome King and his people.

Chapter Seventeen

Queen Ann and her army attempt to conquer the Nome King, but their apparent imprisonment in the pit is discovered to be an escape.

Chapter Twenty-One

Queen Ann escapes through the rocky tunnel into the Metal Forest with her officers, arriving with torn clothing, a battered crown, and bruised, sore limbs. She learns that Ruggedo has been deposed and asks whether his enchantment on the Ugly One can be broken.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Queen Ann admits that she no longer wants to conquer the world and earnestly wishes to return to Oogaboo. Ozma has the Wizard transport her and her people back to their home.

Queen Ann of Oogaboo in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Queen Ann of Oogaboo, also called Ann Soforth, rules Oogaboo and commands its Army. Seeking to conquer the peaceful Land of Oz and replace Princess Ozma, she claims leadership of a party but is dismissed by the Private Citizen and frightened by Tititi-Hoochoo. She leads her Army into Ruggedo's Kingdom, falls into a pit with her officers, and discovers and uses an escape passage.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Queen of Oogaboo is Ann Soforth, who rules eighteen men, twenty-seven women, and forty-four children. She decides that conquering the peaceful Land of Oz will allow her to replace Princess Ozma as ruler.

Chapter Eleven

Queen Ann claims leadership of the party, but the Private Citizen tells her to keep quiet and later recognizes Tik-Tok as the party's important private soldier instead. She becomes frightened and begins to tremble when she learns that the Private Citizen is Tititi-Hoochoo, the Great Jinjin.

Chapter Sixteen

Queen Ann leads her Army into Ruggedo's Kingdom and falls into the concealed pit with her officers. After discovering a secret opening in the pit wall, she leads the officers into a dark underground passage.
