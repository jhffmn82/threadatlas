slavery in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Slavery is the captivity imposed after invaders carry Prince Inga's parents and Pingaree's people away. The White Pearl locates his parents in Regos and Coregos, where Queen Cor and King Gos have enslaved the people; Inga goes against them to free all captives.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Four

The invaders carry Prince Inga's parents and the people of Pingaree away to be slaves. King Rinkitink remarks that there are worse things than slavery, though he is relieved not to have been captured.

Chapter Five

The White Pearl indicates that Inga's parents are being held in slavery in Regos and Coregos. Inga is advised to go there and free them.

Chapter Ten

Queen Cor and King Gos have made the people of Pingaree their slaves. Freeing them is one of Inga's stated purposes for coming against Gos and Cor.
