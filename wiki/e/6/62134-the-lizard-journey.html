the Lizard journey in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

The Lizard journey is a carriage trip beginning at Falmouth, taking the less regular route through Trelowarren and Goonhilly Down rather than Helstone, and ending at Lizard. Despite arriving late, the travelers enjoy scenery, flowers, geological features, and an unhurried ride.
