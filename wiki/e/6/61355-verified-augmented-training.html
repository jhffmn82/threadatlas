Verified Augmented Training

training process · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: augmented training, inference, retrieval augmented generation training, training

Abstract written by a model

In "Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation," retrieval augmented generation training incorporates a Chain-of-Verification into question-answering training and uses negative question-answering samples and verification explanations to improve single-iteration generation. In "Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation," Verified Augmented Training is the third training step, using Multi Task Learning on combined D1 and D2′ to train CoV-RAG with positive and negative verification data.
