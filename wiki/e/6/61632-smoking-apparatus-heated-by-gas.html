Abstract written by a model

A smoking apparatus heated by gas for baking refractory products avoids cinders and dust and facilitates gradual heating. It begins with cold air lowering smoke-gas temperature to 30 or 50 degrees, then gradually closes air entrances to raise temperature as desired.

] GAS FURNACE FOR BAKING REFRACTORY PRODUCTS.

The smoking apparatus heated by gas eliminates the cinders and dust associated with the ordinary system and makes gradual heating of objects to be baked easier. At the beginning, cold air lowers the smoke-gas temperature to 30 or 50 degrees; the air entrances are then gradually closed to increase the temperature as desired.
