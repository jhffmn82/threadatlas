Falmouth

Abstract written by a model

Falmouth is a place with a sheltered bay and glittering sea beyond. Reached after Plymouth, Saltash, Liskeard, Lostwithiel, and St. Austell, it provides a stay at the Green Bank hotel, arrangements for the Lizard, and the starting point for a carriage journey whose upland road looks back over the town and bay.

Falmouth is in one work on this site, An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall / ] DAY THE FIRST I believe in holidays.

Falmouth is reached after the party travels through Plymouth, Saltash, Liskeard, Lostwithiel, and St. Austell. The party stays at the old-fashioned Green Bank hotel rather than the fashionable Falmouth Hotel, and from Falmouth arranges lodgings and a carriage for the Lizard.

DAY THE SECOND Is there anything more delightful than to start on a smiling morning in a comfortable carriage, with all one's _impedimenta_ (happily not much!

Falmouth is the starting point of the carriage journey to the Lizard. From the upland road above it, the travelers see Falmouth, its sheltered bay, and the glittering sea beyond.
