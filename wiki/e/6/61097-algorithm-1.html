Algorithm 1

algorithm · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Knowledge Triple-Grounded Reasoning Chains Construction of TRACE, algorithm

Abstract written by a model

Algorithm 1 constructs knowledge triple-grounded reasoning chains for a question from retrieved documents. It receives a question, maximum chain length, candidate-triple number, and chain number; generates a knowledge graph for each document with a KG generator; scores candidate triples; selects triples with a triple selector; constructs chains in Part II; and outputs the highest-scoring reasoning chains. It omits adaptive chain termination, though it can be incorporated if needed.

Algorithm 1 is in one work on this site, TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
