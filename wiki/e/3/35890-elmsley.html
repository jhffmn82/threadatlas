Elmsley

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Elms., Elmsl.

Abstract written by a model

Elmsley is a commentator whose notes focus on grammar, vocabulary, and textual criticism. He begins by reading φυγη, then argues for alternatives such as και and μογερα, approves Porson’s reading despite printing another, and ends by preferring Kalends for the final item.

Elmsley is in one work on this site, The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

He also explains grammatical constructions, vocabulary, and textual alternatives throughout the commentary.
