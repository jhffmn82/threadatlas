James Ryder in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

James Ryder is the head attendant at the Hotel Cosmopolitan who also calls himself John Robinson. He takes the Countess of Morcar’s blue carbuncle with Catherine Cusack’s assistance, falsely accuses John Horner after claiming to find the jewel-case forced open, and hides the jewel in a goose. He mistakenly takes another barred-tail goose, confesses when Holmes confronts him, and is released after pleading for mercy.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

James Ryder, an upper-attendant at the Hotel Cosmopolitan, testifies that he showed John Horner to the Countess of Morcar's dressing-room to repair a grate. He says that after leaving Horner briefly, he returned to find the jewel-case forced open and the blue carbuncle missing.

“So much for Mr. Henry Baker,” said Holmes when he had closed the door

James Ryder, initially using the name John Robinson, is revealed to be the head attendant at the Hotel Cosmopolitan. He stole the Countess of Morcar’s blue carbuncle, placed it in a goose, mistakenly took another barred-tail goose, and confessed after Holmes confronted him. Holmes releases him after Ryder pleads for mercy.
