Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

Merman's controversy produces no income and causes his small reserve capital to be nearly exhausted. Julia reduces household wants and sells a valuable wedding jewel to pay the rent, while Merman's eventual low-level post becomes necessary because the household is conspicuously poor.
