pianoforte in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The pianoforte, a musical instrument, developed from the dulcimer and succeeded the clavicembalo. Invented at the beginning of the eighteenth century, it became highly popular and inspired composers through effects not properly rendered on other instruments.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The pianoforte developed from the dulcimer. Its predecessor, the clavicembalo, was a cembalo with a keyboard attached, and old examples preserve features associated with the earlier dulcimer.

CHAPTER X.

The pianoforte was invented at the beginning of the eighteenth century and became highly popular. Its characteristics inspired composers to create effects that cannot be properly rendered on other instruments.
