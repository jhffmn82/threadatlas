extractive summarization

method · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: extractive compression method, traditional extractive summarization
  • In Retrieval Augmented Generation and Representative Vector Summarization for Large Unstructured Textual Data in Medical Education, extractive summarization is applied to each representative chunk when the representative Document list may exceed the language model’s context window, consisting of keyword generation and mapping.
  • In An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation, extractive summarization uses ROUGE for comparison with human-annotated abstracts, provides paragraph- or sentence-level filtering candidates guided by query, supporting facts, and answer content, and enables studying compression and conciseness through the length of retained original text.
