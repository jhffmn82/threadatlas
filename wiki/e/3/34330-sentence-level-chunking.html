In "HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications," sentence-level chunking splits text at sentence boundaries while respecting token limits and overlap constraints, achieves the highest Context Precision and F1 scores, and is adopted for subsequent experiments. In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Sentence-level Chunking is the study’s selected chunking method, preserving text semantics while balancing simplicity and efficiency.
