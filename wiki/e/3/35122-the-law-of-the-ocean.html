the law of the ocean in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The law of the ocean grants lifelong ownership to anyone who saves a creature from violent death. King Anko invokes it over Trot, Cap’n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia, then orders the travelers home.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

The law of the ocean states that whoever saves a living creature from violent death owns that creature forever while life lasts. King Anko invokes this law to claim authority over Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia, but uses that authority to order the travelers' return home.
