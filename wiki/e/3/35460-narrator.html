narrator in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The narrator, Arthur’s parent, is in their room and determined to uphold the law. After losing honour, they call police to investigate missing stones, question and plead with Arthur, have him detained when he refuses, offer £1000, and seek help.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“By this time the whole house was astir, for I had raised my voice in

The narrator calls the police, places the investigation in their hands, and insists that the law should proceed. He questions and pleads with Arthur to reveal the location of the missing stones, then has him taken into custody when he refuses. The narrator offers a reward of £1000 and seeks help in solving the matter.
