Flutterbudget behavior in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Flutterbudget behavior consists of treating minor injuries as deadly, fearing events that have not happened, and imagining chains of unlikely disasters. The behavior is driven by foolish fears, worry, nervousness, and repeated “ifs,” even when there is no real danger.

23. How They Encountered the Flutterbudgets

Flutterbudget behavior consists of treating minor injuries as deadly, fearing events that have not happened, and imagining chains of unlikely disasters. The behavior is driven by foolish fears, worry, nervousness, and repeated “ifs,” even when there is no real danger.
