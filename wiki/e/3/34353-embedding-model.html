Abstract written by a model

In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Embedding Model supports semantic matching between queries and chunk blocks and enables selection of LLM-Embedder through FlagEmbedding evaluation for balanced performance and size. In "A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models," the embedding model serves as vector-database keys, represents text chunks semantically, and enables neighbor identification before BM25 re-ranking. In "SimGRAG: Leveraging Similar Subgraphs for Knowledge Graphs Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation," the embedding model transforms text into an n-dimensional vector capturing semantic meaning, but may struggle to link entities in domain-specific knowledge graphs without relevant training; future work may fine-tune it on domain-specific data or explore alternatives.

Preliminaries

An embedding model transforms a textual input into an n-dimensional embedding vector that captures its semantic meaning.

Limitations

An embedding model may struggle to link query entities to corresponding candidate entities in a domain-specific knowledge graph when it has not been trained on such data. Future work may fine-tune embedding models on domain-specific data or explore alternative entity linking approaches.
