the Trojan royal family

group · 3 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Priam’s sons, Trojan royal house, city, house of murderers, monarch of all the Trojans, my house, regal state, the Trojan royal house, the hundred wives

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the Trojan royal house is destroyed in the Trojan catastrophe, leaving Hecuba's husband and children gone and Hecuba enslaved. In Hecuba and other plays, the Trojan royal house is destroyed in the fall of Troy through the deaths of Priam, Hector, the royal sons, and the royal daughters, with Hecuba reduced to slavery and Astyanax killed. In The Aeneid, the Trojan royal family is trapped in the palace as Pyrrhus and the Greeks break through, with Polites killed before Priam, Priam slain at the altar, and the royal rooms, women, and treasures exposed.
