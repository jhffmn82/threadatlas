metrics in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024

RAGAS is a suite of fully self-contained, reference-free metrics for evaluating RAG architectures without ground-truth human annotations, supporting faster evaluation cycles. It measures three central quality aspects: faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance, by prompting an LLM. Its assessments are compared with human judgments, and current experiments show high correlation, though results partly depend on the evaluating LLM. The available metrics use OpenAI's API by default and can use other LLMs.

metrics in Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

The metrics evaluated faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance by selecting the answer or context preferred by the model and comparing that selection with human preferences. Accuracy was defined as the fraction of instances in which the model agreed with the annotators.
