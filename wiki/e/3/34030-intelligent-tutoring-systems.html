Intelligent Tutoring Systems

Also called: ITS, Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS)

Abstract written by a model

Intelligent Tutoring Systems enable independent, personalized education and provide a setting for integrating Large Language Models. They are hindered by information hallucination and weak conceptual coherence; the account introduces Knowledge Graph-enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation to improve their effectiveness and address these limitations.
