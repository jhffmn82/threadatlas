Abstract written by a model

MedCorp is a combined corpus that brings together PubMed, StatPearls, Textbooks, and Wikipedia for cross-source retrieval. It contains 30.4 million raw documents and 54.2 million snippets, described as 65.3 million items. Across MIRAGE tasks, GPT-3.5 with RRF-4 achieves 71.57% average accuracy; RRF-2 and RRF-4 are recommended.

MedCorp is in one work on this site, Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The MEDRAG Toolkit

MedCorp combines the PubMed, StatPearls, Textbooks, and Wikipedia corpora to facilitate cross-source retrieval. It contains 30.4 million raw documents and 54.2 million snippets, with an average snippet length of 221.

Results .. Practical Recommendations

MedCorp is a 65.3-million-item combination of the evaluated corpora and provides robust performance across MIRAGE tasks. With GPT-3.5 and RRF-4, it reaches a 71.57% average accuracy, and both RRF-2 and RRF-4 are recommended as reliable retrieval choices.
