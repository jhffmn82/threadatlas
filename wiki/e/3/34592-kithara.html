CHAPTER IV.

The kithara was a Greek lyre-like instrument with a square base, traditionally held against the breast, although the distinction from other lyres is not certain. It was used at an early period to accompany recitations and was employed for solo performances as early as B.C. 700. The Greeks also had a three-stringed kithara associated with the Lydians.
