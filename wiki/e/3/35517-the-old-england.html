Also called: that elder England

Abstract written by a model

Old England is a remembered rural society, romantically exciting to the narrator, with a living good old king. Before parliamentary reform, it has saleable or privately presented boroughs, waterless prisons mixing felons and maniacs, bloated charities, non-resident jovial clergy, militia-balloting, and ignorance of later judgment.

The old England of the narrator's memory had saleable or privately presented boroughs, poorly supplied prisons, bloated charities, non-resident clergy, militia-balloting, and widespread ignorance of how later generations would judge it. It also included a living good old king and a rural society that the narrator found more romantically exciting than his own adventures.
