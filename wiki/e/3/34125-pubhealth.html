Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17 · the work’s page · read it where PubHealth first appears

Abstract written by a model

PubHealth is a closed-set fact verification dataset about public health, evaluated by test-set accuracy. In experiments, SELF-RAG outperforms evaluated baselines; many retrieval-equipped baselines show little improvement over non-retrieval versions. Reducing SELF-RAG retrieval causes less deterioration than on PopQA.
