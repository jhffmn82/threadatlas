PubHealth in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17

PubHealth is a closed-set fact verification dataset about public health, evaluated by test-set accuracy. In experiments, SELF-RAG outperforms evaluated baselines; many retrieval-equipped baselines show little improvement over non-retrieval versions. Reducing SELF-RAG retrieval causes less deterioration than on PopQA.

RESULTS AND ANALYSIS / 5.1 / MAIN RESULTS .. 5.2 / ANALYSIS

On PubHealth, SELF-RAG outperforms the evaluated baselines, while many retrieval-equipped baselines do not improve notably over their non-retrieval counterparts. Performance deteriorates less when SELF-RAG retrieves less frequently than it does on PopQA.
