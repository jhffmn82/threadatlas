Abstract written by a model

King Dox, whose private name is Dox and who is also called King Dox, is a wise fox and king of Foxville. He rules as Foxville’s sovereign and, with his wife, receives Dorothy, Button-Bright, and the shaggy man in his royal palace. He welcomes them, explains Foxville’s location in relation to Oz and Kansas, recognizes Dorothy’s importance, changes Button-Bright’s head into a fox’s head, and entertains the travelers with dinner and a theater performance. He asks Dorothy for an invitation to Princess Ozma’s birthday celebration and hopes to cross the Dreadful Desert to the Emerald City if invited.

King Dox is in one work on this site, The Road to Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
