authors in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Authors are a miscellaneous class afflicted by physical and moral disorders, especially chronic ailments in those who produce little yet overvalue their work, and accept pleasing punishment for the One.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XV.

Authors are described as a miscellaneous class whose physical and moral diseases might include the whole procession of human disorders. The unit concentrates particularly on the chronic ailments associated with authors who produce little but attach excessive importance to their work.
