Saxe-Coburg Square in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Saxe-Coburg Square is a poky, shabby-genteel location containing Jabez Wilson’s corner pawnbroker’s shop; Holmes surveys it and nearby buildings, suspecting a serious crime connected with the square.

Saxe-Coburg Square is the shabby location of Mr. Jabez Wilson’s pawnbroking business. Sherlock Holmes studies the square and its surrounding buildings, including the nearby bank, restaurant, shops, and carriage-building depot, because he believes a serious crime may be connected with it.
