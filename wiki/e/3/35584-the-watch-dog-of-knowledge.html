VIII.

The Watch-Dog of Knowledge is the title of this unit, which considers the dangers of guarding inquiry through arrogant correction and cruel exclusion. The unit argues that the defense of truth should be restrained by fairness, kindness, and fellow-feeling toward contemporaries.
