Professor Wogglebug's Athletic College in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Professor Wogglebug's Athletic College is the Royal Athletic College of Oz, on a green plain and directed by Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E. It teaches youth athletics through many sports while School Pills provide academic knowledge without interrupting play.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

9. How the Wogglebug Taught Athletics

The Royal Athletic College of Oz stands on a green plain and is directed by Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E. Its students spend their class hours playing football, baseball, tennis, golf, swimming, rowing, basketball, cricket, boxing, and wrestling. The college uses School Pills so students can acquire academic knowledge without interrupting their athletic activities.
