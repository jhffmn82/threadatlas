retrospective optimists in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Retrospective optimists idealize the past, scorn contemporaries, and praise ancients; the narrator says they ignore vulgar, sordid elements in Greece and Italy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II.

Retrospective optimists treat distant ages, especially Greece and Italy, as more graceful and poetic than the present. The narrator argues that they overlook the vulgar and sordid elements that existed in those societies as well.
