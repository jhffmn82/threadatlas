Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, it is committed by Creon with attendants and by force. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, it is an unlawful seizure undertaken by Creon and opposed by Athens.
