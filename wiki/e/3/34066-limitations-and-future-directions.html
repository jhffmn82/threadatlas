Abstract written by a model

Limitations and Future Directions identifies the user study’s small, narrow sample and primarily qualitative evaluation as limiting generalizability. It calls for broader studies across institutions, disciplines, language proficiencies, cultural contexts, and learner populations; objective BLEU and ROUGE measures; automated knowledge graph construction; and enhanced adaptive scaffolding for deeper learning.
