Limitations and Future Directions include the small scale and scope of the user study and its primarily qualitative evaluation. The unit calls for studies involving multiple institutions, academic disciplines, language proficiencies, and cultural contexts, along with quantitative text-quality measures such as BLEU or ROUGE.
