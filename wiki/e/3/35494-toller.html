Abstract written by a model

Toller is a groom and one of two servants at the Copper Beeches, married to Mrs. Toller. Rough and frequently drunk, he alone can control Carlo and works in the deserted suite. While drunk, he apparently leaves its key in the door. He is found snoring on the kitchen rug, then sobers enough after Mr. Rucastle is attacked to help carry him inside and notify Mrs. Toller.

Toller is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
