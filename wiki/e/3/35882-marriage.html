  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I: connects Pylades [with Electra as intended spouses] Orestes says that he had betrothed Electra to Pylades because he revered their friendship, and he urges Pylades to marry elsewhere and become a father if Orestes dies. Pylades rejects this separation and says that he regards Electra as his wife because he has courted her. Electra later presents Hermione's possible marriage to Pylades as a reason for their hoped-for safety.
