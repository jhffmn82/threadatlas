Forbidden Fountain in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Forbidden Fountain is a golden basin in the palace grounds containing sparkling Water of Oblivion, marked to forbid everyone from drinking. Ozma plans to confront invading warriors there; when they arrive, the creatures drink, lose their memories, and the thrown-in Nome King becomes harmless and forgetful.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Forbidden Fountain stands in the palace grounds in a golden basin and bears a sign forbidding everyone to drink from it. Ozma plans to meet the invading warriors there when they break through the earth.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

The Forbidden Fountain is the site where the invading creatures drink and lose their memories. The Nome King is thrown into its basin, and the fountain's water makes him as harmless and forgetful as the other invaders.

the Fountain of the Water of Oblivion in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Fountain of the Water of Oblivion, on the palace grounds beside a marble bench, contains water causing forgetfulness; restored Kiki Aru and Ruggedo drink from it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

23. The Fountain of Oblivion

The Fountain of the Water of Oblivion is beside the marble bench where Dorothy, the Wizard, and Ozma discuss the two enchanted magicians. It provides the water that Kiki Aru and Ruggedo drink after being restored to their natural forms.
