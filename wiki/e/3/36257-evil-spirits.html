evil spirits in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Evil spirits are terrible, powerful creatures, including the Erbs, with magic sufficient to destroy and conquer the Land of Oz. Guph plans to gather and unite them, and the Nome King bargains for their aid. Unafraid of Billina's eggs, they are sent first through the tunnel to conquer and destroy.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4. How The Nome King Planned Revenge

Guph says that certain evil creatures possess enough magic to destroy and conquer the Land of Oz. He plans to obtain their assistance and band them together against Oz.

24. How the Tin Woodman Told the Sad News

The evil spirits are terrible, powerful creatures whom the Nome King has bargained with to assist his invasion. Unlike the Nomes, they are not afraid of eggs or anything else, and they are to enter the tunnel first to conquer and destroy.
