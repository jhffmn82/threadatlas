Abstract written by a model

Egyptian harps, called buni, varied in size and form: largest reached about six and a half feet; smaller ones could have stands. Without a front pillar, strings lacked modern-harp tension; a Louvre specimen has twenty-one strings.

CHAPTER II.

Egyptian harps varied in size and form, with the largest reaching about six and a half feet and smaller ones sometimes having stands for standing performers. They had no front pillar, so their strings could not have been under tension comparable to those of the modern harp.
