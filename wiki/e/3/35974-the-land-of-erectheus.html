Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE HERACLIDÆ.

The land of Athens receives Iolaus and the Heraclids as suppliants. Demophoon declares that it is not subject to Argos and that its inhabitants may decide how to protect those who have taken refuge there.

CHOR. O earth, and moon that shinest through the night, and most brilliant

The speaker acts on behalf of the country and refuses to surrender strangers at the bidding of Argos. The country is described as facing secret anger from Mycenae.
