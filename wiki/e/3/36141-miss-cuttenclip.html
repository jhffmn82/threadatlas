Abstract written by a model

Miss Cuttenclip is the flesh-and-blood Queen of Cuttenclip village, who received magic paper and happily uses scissors to create its living paper people, houses, trees, and flowers. Unchanging in age, she welcomes a party, shows the village, and repairs damage from the Shaggy Man’s sneeze.

10. How the Cuttenclips Lived

Miss Cuttenclip is the flesh-and-blood Queen of the Cuttenclip village and the maker of its paper people, houses, trees, and flowers. She received magic paper from Glinda, does not grow older, and remains happy making her village. She welcomes the party, shows them the village, and repairs the damage caused by the Shaggy Man’s sneeze.
