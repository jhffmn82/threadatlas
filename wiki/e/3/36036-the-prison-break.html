the prison break

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Bacchus's escape, Free in the woods, delivered myself, fled, left his fire, liberated, set me free

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it is Bacchus's escape from Pentheus's attempted imprisonment, freeing the stranger and returning him to the Chorus. In The Bacchae of Euripides, it is the divine prison break that frees Dionysus and the imprisoned Maids and enables Dionysus to escape.

Abstract written by a model

A divine prison break frees Dionysus and the imprisoned Maids: as guards return, the Maids’ restraints and bars open without mortal touch, and Dionysus escapes after Pentheus is deceived amid disturbances.
