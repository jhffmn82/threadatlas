Abstract written by a model

In James McCarthy's testimony, he said that Charles summoned him with “Cooee!”, that they argued, and that he left before hearing a hideous outcry. He returned to find Charles fatally injured, noticed a vague grey object near the scene, and could not explain the reference to a rat or why Charles had used the signal before seeing him.
