Middle Ages in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The Middle Ages are documented through manuscripts, sculptures, and paintings that preserve uncertain but valuable evidence of musical instruments. Wind instruments ranged from flutes and trumpets to early organs, alongside quaintly made instruments, organistrums, and percussion in medieval concerts.

CHAPTER VII.

Representations of musical instruments from the Middle Ages survive in manuscripts, sculptures, and paintings. These sources provide valuable evidence, but their dates and accuracy are sometimes uncertain.

[Illustration] [Illustration] CHAPTER VIII.

During the middle ages, wind instruments included flutes, syrinxes, trumpets, hunting horns, sackbuts, and early forms of the organ. Some instruments had quaint forms and rude construction, while medieval concerts also used the cumbrous organistrum and various percussion instruments.
