bell in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Bell is a musical instrument especially popular in England for centuries, suspended in towers, frames, gateways, and oak trees.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

The bell was especially popular in England, where bell-ringing had been a favored amusement for centuries. Bells were suspended in varied ways in different countries, including separate towers, timber frames, church gateways, and even oak trees.
