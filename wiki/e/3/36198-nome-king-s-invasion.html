Abstract written by a model

Nome King's invasion is a planned military invasion of the Land of Oz, centered on capturing and destroying the Emerald City and recovering the Magic Belt. Roquat orders General Blug to march the Nome army against Oz, but, unable to cross the deadly desert or defeat Ozma directly, relies on a secret tunnel beneath the desert. General Guph develops the wider campaign: a surprise attack intended to conquer Oz, destroy its people and Emerald City, seize treasure, and enslave inhabitants. He recruits the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms by promising rewards, and says their combined power makes victory assured. The allies, however, pursue their own gains: plunder, slaves, power, and, in the Phanfasms' case, eventual destruction of the other invaders as well as Oz. Roquat fears that these allies may become dangerous or demand more than promised. The plot calls for evil spirits to pass through the tunnel first, followed by the Nomes and their allies. After midnight, the Nome King and allied armies take up their weapons and enter the tunnel, intending to reach the Emerald City at daybreak, destroy it, and devastate Oz.

Nome King's invasion is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

9 sections; each heading opens the text

1. How the Nome King Became Angry

Roquat orders General Blug to march the Nome army to the Land of Oz, capture and destroy the Emerald City, and recover the Magic Belt. Because the army cannot cross the deadly desert or defeat Ozma directly, Roquat plans a secret tunnel under the desert.

4. How The Nome King Planned Revenge

The planned conquest involves marching the Nome army through a tunnel, conquering and destroying the Oz people and the Emerald City, taking its treasures, enslaving its people, and recovering the Magic Belt. Guph intends to use surprise and allied evil powers to make the conquest possible.

6. How Guph Visited the Whimsies

The Nome conquest of Oz is planned as a surprise invasion through a tunnel reaching the Emerald City. General Guph recruits the Whimsies as powerful allies by promising them a reward for fighting.

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

The proposed conquest is organized by the Nomes with help from the Growleywogs and a tunnel under the deadly desert. The Growleywogs expect to destroy Ozma's domain, enslave twenty thousand people of Oz, and take its treasure, while Guph hopes the victory will increase his own power.

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

The conquest of Oz is General Guph's proposed campaign to plunder the country and enslave its people. The Phanfasms agree to participate once the tunnel is completed, intending ultimately to destroy the other invading groups as well as Oz.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The conquest of Oz is planned by the Nomes with the aid of the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms. General Guph says the conquest is assured, although Roquat worries that the allies may demand more than promised or become dangerous.

24. How the Tin Woodman Told the Sad News

The Nome King's invasion plot begins with a tunnel beneath the deadly desert and relies on evil spirits to enter first, followed by the Nomes. Its purpose is to conquer and destroy Oz, then enslave its inhabitants and take the country's plunder.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Nome King's invasion includes the Nomes, Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms. They plan to enter Oz at daybreak, enslave its people, destroy the Emerald City, and devastate the Land of Oz.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The invasion begins after midnight when the Nome King and his allies seize their weapons and enter the tunnel. The invaders share the goal of destroying Oz, though each leader also plans to betray the others and claim its wealth or slaves.
