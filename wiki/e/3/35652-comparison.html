comparison in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Comparison is a guide to knowledge that teaches through likeness, leading people from familiar things to unfamiliar human forms and self-recognized corresponding tendencies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII.

Comparison is presented as the essential guide to knowledge: by likeness to what is already known, it helps a person investigate unfamiliar forms of human nature and detect corresponding tendencies in oneself. It can reveal similarities despite wide differences in temperament and circumstance.
