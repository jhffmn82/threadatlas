VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

Julia Stoner's death occurred two years before Helen consulted Holmes, shortly before Julia's wedding. She was found convulsed and unconscious after crying out, and medical aid failed; the coroner found no satisfactory cause, no poison, no violence, and no way for an intruder to have entered her locked room.

“And what do you think of it all, Watson?” asked Sherlock Holmes,

Helen Stoner’s sister’s death occurred in the middle bedroom after she was apparently alone in a room with sound walls and flooring and impassable doors, window, and chimney. Holmes investigates the room and its unusual fixtures because he believes the death had a tangible cause rather than being caused solely by fright.
