divine punishment in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

Divine punishment is inflicted by Bacchus on those who dishonor the gods. It punishes Pentheus for opposing Bacchus: the gods eventually pursue the impious, Pentheus is led to the Bacchae and dies by his mother’s hand, and his family suffers ruin, exile, and belated recognition of its error.
