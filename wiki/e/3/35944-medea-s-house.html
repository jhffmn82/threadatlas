Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Medea's house is ruled by a more powerful queen of the nuptial bed. In Medea, Medea's house is where she lies fasting and grieving after Jason's betrayal, where the Nurse fears she may die or use a blade against Jason, and from which she later comes out to address the Corinthian women.
