the divine curse on Oedipus

curse · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a flood of ills, curse of Oedipus's mother and father, the dogging curse of mother and sire, thy father's and thy mother's curse

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, the divine curse on Oedipus is associated with the revelation of Oedipus's crimes and coming ruin and drives him from the land. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the curse of Oedipus's mother and father drives Oedipus beyond the borders.
