musical instrument of green sonorous stone in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

A musical instrument made of green sonorous stone, preserved among Indian antiquities in Cuzco. About a foot long and an inch and a half wide, it is flat-sided, pointed, and arched, with a back hole for string suspension; when suspended and struck, it produces a singularly musical note.

G. T. Vigne saw this instrument among Indian antiquities preserved in Cuzco, Peru. It was a flat-sided, pointed, arched green stone about a foot long, with a hole for suspension; when struck, it produced a singularly musical note.
