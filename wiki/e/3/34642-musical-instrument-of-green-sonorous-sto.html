Also called: a musical instrument of green sonorous stone

Abstract written by a model

A musical instrument made of green sonorous stone, preserved among Indian antiquities in Cuzco. About a foot long and an inch and a half wide, it is flat-sided, pointed, and arched, with a back hole for string suspension; when suspended and struck, it produces a singularly musical note.
