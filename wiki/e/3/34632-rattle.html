Abstract written by a model

A rattle is an instrument of percussion, popular among Indians before the discovery of America. Usually made from an oval or round hollowed gourd with holes, a wooden handle, and little pebbles inside, it was also made of pottery; Mexicans called it ajacaxtli.

The rattle was popular among Indians before the discovery of America. The Mexicans called it ajacaxtli; it was usually made from an oval or round gourd pierced with holes, fitted with a wooden handle, and filled with small pebbles, though rattles were also made of pottery.
