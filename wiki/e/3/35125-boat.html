boat (Trot and Cap'n Bill's boat) in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Trot and Cap'n Bill's pretty, light, strong little boat is kept in a suspended shed in the bluff. It is lowered to the sea, rowed around caves into Giant's Cave, left floating there, then found again and rowed out of the cave.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap 2._

Trot's rowboat is lowered from its shelter on the cliff and pushed into the sea. Cap'n Bill rows it around the caves and into Giant's Cave, where it drifts while Trot and he admire the cavern.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._

Trot and Cap’n Bill find their little boat floating near the place where they left it in the Giant’s Cave. Cap’n Bill climbs aboard, brings Trot into it, and rows the boat toward and out of the cave.
