orchestras of the Middle Ages in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Medieval orchestras are represented by sculptors on monuments; although partly imaginative, the depicted instruments probably reflect contemporary orchestras and evidence medieval instrumental music.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

They are represented on medieval monuments, though their sculptors may have relied partly on imagination. The instruments shown were probably ones admitted into contemporary orchestras and provide evidence about medieval instrumental music.
