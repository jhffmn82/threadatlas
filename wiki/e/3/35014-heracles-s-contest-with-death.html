Abstract written by a model

Heracles’s contest with Death is a wrestling struggle to restore Alcestis to Admetus. After learning that she has died, Heracles vows to repay Admetus’s hospitality, planning to confront Death at the tomb or seek her return below. Among graves and the dead, he springs upon and grips Death as he flees, conquers him by force of combat, and brings Alcestis back from death.

INTRODUCTION

After learning that Alcestis has died, Heracles goes out and wrestles with Death, conquering him and bringing Alcestis back. The contest has a half-grotesque and half-ghastly quality, taking place amid graves with the cracking of ribs; Death is treated not as a great Olympian power but as a servant of Hades sent to fetch appointed victims.

After learning that Alcestis has died, Heracles vows to repay Admetus's hospitality by bringing her back. He intends to confront Death at the tomb or descend to the underworld and use strong prayers to obtain Alcestis's return.

Heracles says that he fought for life with Death among the dead, springing upon and gripping him as he fled. Through that struggle, he recovered Alcestis for Admetus.
