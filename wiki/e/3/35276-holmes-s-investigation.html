Holmes's investigation in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Holmes investigates warning letters, seaport postmarks, and attacks; identifies the Ku Klux Klan through the American Encyclopædia; tracks the Lone Star via Lloyd's registers and newspapers; names Captain James Calhoun as leader; and alerts police, but the ship is lost before arrests.

Holmes's investigation begins with the warning letters, their seaport postmarks, and the timing of the attacks. He consults Lloyd's registers and old newspapers, traces the perpetrators to the Lone Star, identifies Captain James Calhoun as their leader, and alerts the police, though the ship is lost before arrests can be made.
