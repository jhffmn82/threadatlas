Messenger

Also called: A MESSENGER, A SECOND MESSENGER, MESS., MESSENGER, the first Messenger

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Messenger reports that Haemon is dead by his own hand after attacking Creon over the murder of the maiden, describes the burial of Polynices, the discovery of the maiden hanging in the vault, and Haemon dying beside her. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the first Messenger reports the royal household’s disasters, witnesses Haemon’s death, and tells how Haemon dies by his own sword while clasping Antigone.
