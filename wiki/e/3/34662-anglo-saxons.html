use harp [to accompany vocal performances] The Anglo-Saxons accompanied vocal performances with a harp that was more or less triangular and represented a transition from the lyre to the harp. An Anglo-Saxon manuscript also depicts one gleeman playing the lyre and another playing a double-pipe.
