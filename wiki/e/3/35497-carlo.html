Carlo in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Carlo is Mr. Rucastle’s giant, tawny mastiff and savage household dog at the Copper Beeches. Carlo is Mr. Rucastle's mastiff, kept chained in an outhouse near the kitchen. He is fed sparingly, released every night by Toller, and is considered dangerous enough to threaten the life of anyone who crosses the grounds after dark.

The mastiff is described as savage and dangerous enough to be used as a threat against the young lady. Toller is the only person in the household said to have influence over it or to dare set it free.
