Abstract written by a model

Carlo is Mr. Rucastle’s giant, tawny mastiff and savage household dog at the Copper Beeches. Kept chained in an outhouse near the kitchen and fed sparingly, he is released nightly by Toller, the only person said to control him. Dangerous to trespassers after dark, Carlo is used to threaten the young lady.
