V. DISCUSSIONS

The chat history re-usage mechanism compares vector embeddings of new user queries with embeddings from previous queries stored in chat history using cosine similarity. When the similarity exceeds a proposed threshold such as 0.85, a relevant answer can be retrieved directly without reprocessing the query.
