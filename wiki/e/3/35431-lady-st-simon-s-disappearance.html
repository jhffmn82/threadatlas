Lady St. Simon’s disappearance in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

X. THE ADVENTURE OF THE NOBLE BACHELOR

Hatty Doran disappears during the wedding breakfast after briefly retiring to her room. She leaves the house wearing an ulster and bonnet, and the police begin energetic inquiries while newspapers report rumors of foul play.

Lady St. Simon’s disappearance results from her leaving the wedding breakfast and following Francis Hay Moulton. She is not dead or abducted; she has gone with her earlier husband to Gordon Square.
