Lady St. Simon’s disappearance

Abstract written by a model

Lady St. Simon’s disappearance occurs during the wedding breakfast, after Hatty Doran briefly retires to her room and leaves the house in an ulster and bonnet. Reported by Aloysius Doran and the bridegroom, it prompts police inquiries and rumors of foul play. She had instead followed her earlier husband, Francis Hay Moulton, to Gordon Square.
