the fuller’s-earth mining operation in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A secret fuller’s-earth mining operation near Reading extracts a small deposit and uses a hydraulic press to compress earth into bricks, aiming to earn enough to buy neighboring fields with larger deposits.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Stark claims that he and his associates are secretly working a small deposit of fuller’s-earth in order to earn enough money to purchase neighboring fields containing larger deposits. He says they erected a hydraulic press to assist their operations and compress the earth into bricks.
