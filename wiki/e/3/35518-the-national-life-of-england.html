the national life of England

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: English national life, Our national life, a national life in our veins, our beloved English, our national heritage, something specifically English

Abstract written by a model

England’s national life is a distinctive tradition-rooted continuity, likened to scenery: it avoids great convulsions yet responds delicately to political, social, and technological change. Though it retains a family likeness, rapid loss of traditions, customs, language, habits, and sentiments may weaken it; preservation requires moderating inevitable change.
