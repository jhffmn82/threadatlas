Semele's death by lightning

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Semele's burning, burned, burnt to ashes, the lightning flame, the lightning-bearing flame, the stroke of the thunder-bolt
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Semele's death by lightning is described as the result of Jove's thunderbolt striking her; Bacchus sees her tomb and the remains of her house and treats her death and the denial of his birth as insults he must avenge.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Semele's burning is described as being caused by Jupiter's divine lightning during his manifestation, burning her mortal body to ashes in his presence.
