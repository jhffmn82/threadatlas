Porson

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: PORSON, Pors.

Abstract written by a model

Porson is a textual critic whose record consists of proposed, adopted, and disputed readings. He suggests μακροπνουν, follows Wyttenbach on ταχ' αν πιθοιο, and conjectures σπευδε for an old man's answer. Across further passages, he supplies interpretations, punctuation, omissions, and judgments of corruption, sometimes agreeing or differing with Elmsley and Dindorf.

Porson is in one work on this site, The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
