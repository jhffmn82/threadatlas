the marriage of Irene Adler and Godfrey Norton in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Irene Adler and Godfrey Norton marry unexpectedly at the Church of St. Monica because of a license informality. The clergyman requires Dr. John Watson as witness, and Adler gives him a sovereign. By the next day, Irene is Irene Norton, née Adler, and says she and Norton love each other.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

II.

The marriage of Irene Adler and Godfrey Norton takes place unexpectedly at the Church of St. Monica. Sherlock Holmes is drawn into serving as their witness, and Adler gives him a sovereign afterward.

III.

Sherlock Holmes tells the King of Bohemia that Irene Adler married an English lawyer named Norton the previous day. Irene’s letter identifies her as Irene Norton, née Adler, and says that she loves and is loved by her husband.
