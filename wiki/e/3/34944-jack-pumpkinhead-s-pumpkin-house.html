In The Road to Oz, Jack Pumpkinhead's pumpkin house is a monstrous hollow pumpkin shell fitted with a door, windows, stovepipe, and six steps. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Jack Pumpkinhead's pumpkin house is a house made from an immense pumpkin shell that stands in a pumpkin-field and shelters travelers for one night.
