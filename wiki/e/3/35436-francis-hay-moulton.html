Abstract written by a model

Francis Hay Moulton, an American gentleman, secretly married the woman later known as Mrs. Moulton before leaving to seek his fortune. Reported killed but surviving an Apache attack and captivity, he returned to England, interrupted her second wedding, reunited with her, and lived with her in Gordon Square.
