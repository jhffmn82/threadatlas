Thebes royal palace in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

Thebes royal palace is the contested seat of rule between Eteocles and Polynices: Eteocles vows to occupy or yield it after their combat, and it ends before three fallen corpses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

MESSENGER, JOCASTA, CHORUS.

Eteocles says that if he kills Polynices he will dwell in the palace alone, while if he is defeated he will give it up to Polynices. The palace is therefore connected to the brothers' dispute over rule.
