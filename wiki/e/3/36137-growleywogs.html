Growleywogs in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Growleywogs are gigantic, dangerous, extremely strong creatures of bone, skin, and muscle, with powerful muscles beneath their skins. Surly, unsociable, disliked and avoided by everyone, they hate good and happy creatures. They are greedy allies of the Nome King and one of the invading forces assembled to conquer and destroy Oz. Led by their Grand Gallipoot and skilled generals, they promise assistance alongside the Whimsies in exchange for twenty thousand people of Oz as slaves, while secretly intending to conquer the Nomes afterward. General Guph also plans vengeance against them after using them, and the Phanfasms intend to destroy them. The Growleywogs march through the tunnel toward the Emerald City, hoping to pilfer and destroy Oz and claim Ozma as a slave. After emerging, however, they drink at the Forbidden Fountain, lose their hostility, become harmless, and are returned to their homes by Ozma's wish.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

The Growleywogs are gigantic, extremely strong creatures made of bone, skin, and muscle, and they are described as surly, unsociable, and hated by others. They agree to help the Nomes conquer the Land of Oz in return for twenty thousand people of Oz as slaves, while secretly intending to conquer the Nomes afterward.

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

General Guph vows to take vengeance on the Growleywogs after using them to help conquer Oz. They are identified as one of the groups supporting the planned invasion, but the Phanfasms secretly intend to destroy them afterward.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The Growleywogs promise to join the invasion and demand Oz people as slaves as their reward. General Guph does not tell Roquat the Red that they demanded twenty thousand slaves.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Growleywogs join the Nome King's planned invasion of Oz. Their Grand Gallipoot and skilled generals gather in the Nome King's cavern, and the Grand Gallipoot discusses marching to the Emerald City and claiming Ozma as a slave.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The gigantic Growleywogs march into the tunnel after the Phanfasms. They want to reach Oz so they can pilfer and destroy it.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

The Growleywogs drink from the Forbidden Fountain after emerging from the tunnel. They lose their former hostility and become harmless before Ozma's wish returns them to their own homes.
