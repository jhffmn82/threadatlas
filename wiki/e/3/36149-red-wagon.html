Red Wagon

object · 5 works · 12 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Ozma's Red Wagon, Sawhorse wagon, the Red Wagon, the red wagon, the wagon, their wagon, wagon
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, the red wagon carries the travelers toward Rigmarole Town and onward after they climb in, drawn and pulled by Sawhorse.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, the Red Wagon carries Dorothy and her companions to Glinda's palace and then onward to the Emerald City.
  • In The Lost Princess of Oz, the Red Wagon carries Dorothy, the Wizard, Trot, Button-Bright, and supplies through the Emerald City and Winkie Country before being left behind at the Merry-Go-Round Mountains crossing.
  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, the Red Wagon is ordered by Ozma for travel to meet the transformed adventurers, carrying Dorothy and Ozma with Toto while the Wooden Sawhorse pulls it.
  • In The Magic of Oz, the Red Wagon is the royal wagon Dorothy uses with the Wooden Sawhorse to travel to Glinda's Castle and back, though it cannot pass through the thick forests where the monkeys live.

the red wagon in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The red wagon, drawn and pulled by Sawhorse, carries the travelers after they climb in, its enchanted wheels rolling toward Rigmarole Town and onward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

22. How the Wizard Found Dorothy

The travelers climb into the red wagon after the Wizard turns the tents back into handkerchiefs. Its wheels are enchanted to roll in the right direction, carrying the group first to Rigmarole Town and then onward.
