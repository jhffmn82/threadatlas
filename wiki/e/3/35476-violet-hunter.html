Violet Hunter

person · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

Violet Hunter is an experienced governess who, after five years with Colonel Spence Munro, is unemployed and seeks a highly paid post at the Copper Beeches despite Sherlock Holmes’s reservations. Employed by Mr. Rucastle, she lives there caring for Edward Rucastle and complies with demands that she cut her luxuriant chestnut hair and wear an electric-blue dress. Her resemblance to Alice Rucastle makes her an unwilling impersonator. Alarmed by a duplicate lock of hair and a forbidden suite, she appeals to Holmes from the Black Swan Hotel in Winchester. Brave and sensible, she helps expose the empty chamber and escape route by trapping Mrs. Toller in the cellar. After Mr. Rucastle’s appearance, Holmes escorts her back to Winchester, and she later runs a private school at Walsall.

XII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE COPPER BEECHES

Violet Hunter, an unemployed governess, asks Sherlock Holmes whether she should accept a position with Jep hro Rucastle. Although the position requires unusual obedience, an electric-blue dress, and short hair, she accepts it because of the high salary. Two weeks later, she sends an urgent telegram asking Holmes to come to Winchester because she is at her wit's end.

Miss Hunter meets Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson at The Black Swan to seek advice about the Rucastle household. She describes the strange treatment she has received, the duplicate hair in her locked drawer, and her frightening entry into the forbidden suite.

Violet Hunter follows Holmes’s instructions by luring Mrs. Toller into the cellar and locking her there before Holmes and Watson arrive. She is present when the empty chamber and escape route are discovered and is frightened when Mr. Rucastle appears.
