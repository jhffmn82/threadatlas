the crossroads

place · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Branch roads from Delphi and from Daulis meet, Cross-roads, Cross-roads here, a cross-way, a spot where three roads meet, the cross-road, the crossroad where Laius was killed, the meeting of three roads, the parted ways, the place where the 3 roads meet, the scene of this unhappy blow, the triple-branching roads

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, it is the Phocian crossroad where roads from Dauha and Delphi meet and where Jocasta's description prompts Oedipus to recall killing an older man and his attendants. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, it is where three roads meet, where Oedipus is said to have murdered his father, and where the tombs of Laius and his attendant stand. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, it is where roads from Delphi and Daulis meet and where Laius was reportedly murdered; Oedipus recalls killing an older man and his attendants there.
