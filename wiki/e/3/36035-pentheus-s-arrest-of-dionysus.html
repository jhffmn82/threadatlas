Pentheus's arrest of Dionysus

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Caught, Pentheus's arrest of Bacchus, bind thee, bind you, bound, confine him, doom, hold thee prisoned, prisoned, put him in bonds, seize and bind, tie him
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, Pentheus's arrest of Dionysus is defeated by the prison break after Pentheus orders him bound and imprisoned.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Pentheus's arrest of Bacchus is carried out by the servant under Pentheus's orders, but does not succeed.
