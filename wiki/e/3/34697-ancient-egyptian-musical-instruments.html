Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Ancient Egyptian musical instruments included harps, the nofre, pipes, the sêbi, the double-pipe, trumpets, tambourines, drums, gongs, bells, cymbals, castanets, and the sistrum. Their construction and use are documented by monuments, tomb paintings, hieroglyphs, and surviving specimens.
