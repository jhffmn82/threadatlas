John Turner in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

John Turner is an elderly, wealthy landed proprietor, the largest in the district, who owns Turner Hall, the Turner estate, and Hatherley Farm. He made his money at the gold-mines in Victoria, Australia, returned to England, and became father to Alice after his wife's early death. He gave Hatherley Farm rent-free to McCarthy senior and supported McCarthy's son under coercion, but opposed James McCarthy's marriage to his daughter. His hidden past was as Black Jack of Ballarat, a former highway robber and member of the Ballarat Gang. McCarthy senior recognized him and blackmailed him for twenty years. With his health shattered by diabetes and failing health, Turner was near death when James McCarthy threatened Alice and sought to force her into marriage with McCarthy's son. Turner struck James down and confessed to killing him. Holmes identified him through the Ballarat reference, grey cloak, and traces at Boscombe Pool. Turner lived seven months after the interview before dying.
