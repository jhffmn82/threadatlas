TILDE is a reranking method that preprocesses candidate documents at indexing, independently predicts vocabulary-token probabilities, and stores query-term likelihoods. It rapidly ranks documents by summing pre-calculated log probabilities for each query token.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Reranking Methods

TILDE independently considers each query term and predicts token probabilities across the entire vocabulary. It enables rapid reranking by summing the pre-calculated log probabilities corresponding to query tokens for each document.
