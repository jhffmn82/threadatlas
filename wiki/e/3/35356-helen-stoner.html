Abstract written by a model

Helen Stoner, Sherlock Holmes’s client and Julia Stoner’s twin, lives at Stoke Moran with her violent stepfather, Dr. Grimesby Roylott. Engaged to Percy Armitage, she becomes terrified after moving into Julia’s former room and hearing the low whistle that preceded Julia’s death. She tells Holmes of Roylott’s violence, Julia’s death, and her own danger; Holmes finds marks on her wrist. At Stoke Moran, she confirms the room’s suspicious features and follows Holmes’s plan by signaling and sleeping elsewhere. Roylott’s snake-poison scheme targets her, but after his death Holmes and Watson take her to her aunt at Harrow.

VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

Helen Stoner comes to Sherlock Holmes in a state of terror because she has heard the same low whistle that preceded her sister Julia's death after moving into Julia's former room. She describes Dr. Grimesby Roylott's violence, Julia's death, and her own impending marriage, and Holmes finds five livid finger marks on her wrist.

“And what do you think of it all, Watson?” asked Sherlock Holmes,

Helen Stoner meets Holmes and Watson at Stoke Moran after Roylott has gone to town. She confirms details about the rooms, shutters, bell-rope, ventilator, and repairs, and agrees to follow Holmes’s instructions by signaling from her window and sleeping in another room. Holmes warns her that her life may depend on obeying him.

Miss Stoner is the intended victim of Dr. Grimesby Roylott’s snake-poison murder scheme. After Roylott’s death, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson convey her by morning train to the care of her good aunt at Harrow.
