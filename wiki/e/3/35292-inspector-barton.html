Abstract written by a model

Inspector Barton, investigating the St. Clair disappearance, meets Mrs. St. Clair in Fresno Street and returns with her and two constables to an opium den. Though initially doubtful, he treats the case seriously after finding a box of children’s bricks and investigates the rooms.
