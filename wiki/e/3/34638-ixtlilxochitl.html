Ixtlilxochitl in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Ixtlilxochitl, a Mexican of royal descent living in the early seventeenth century, wrote a historical essay describing sonorous metal and may have known his countrymen’s musical practices.

Ixtlilxochitl was a native of Mexico and of royal descent who lived in the beginning of the seventeenth century. He described the sonorous metal in a historical essay and may have been familiar with the musical practices of his countrymen.
