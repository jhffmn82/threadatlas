octopus (the bashful octopus) in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

octopus is a sea devil’s cousin with eight limbs used as arms and legs, dressed in a red coat, silk hat, and yellow embroidered vest. Earth people consider him wicked and deceitful, comparisons that offend and shame him. When the party repeats them, he weeps as they leave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

The octopus approaches the party wearing a red coat, brass buttons, a silk hat, and a yellow embroidered vest, while using some of his legs as arms. He is offended and deeply ashamed when Trot and Cap'n Bill say that people on earth compare octopuses to the Stannerd Oil Company and the Coal Trust, and he weeps as the party leaves.
