Honour

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Honour's meed, Tree of Honour, glory, greatness, holy Honour, honor, honour, she, undying honour
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, honour is lost by Aias after Achilles' armour goes to Odysseus and Aias's livestock is slaughtered, then restored through his honoured funeral.
  • In The Fall of Troy, Honour is a goal of rivalry for right-hearted men; Aias believes the brave no longer receive their due honor while baser men, especially Odysseus, are favored among the Greeks, while Odysseus says honor was the goal of their rivalry over Achilles' arms, and after Aias's death the Danaans honor him with offerings, cremation, a golden casket, and a great burial mound.

Honour in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

is_a goal of rivalry [for right-hearted men] Aias believes that the brave no longer receive their due honor while baser men, especially Odysseus, are favored among the Greeks. Odysseus says that honor was the goal of his rivalry with Aias over Achilles' arms, since it is the proper object of competition among right-hearted men. After Aias's death, the Danaans honor him with offerings, cremation, a golden casket, and a great burial mound.
