17. How They Came to Bunbury

C. Bunn, Esquire, identifies himself as a bread-and-butter man whose C stands for Cinnamon and says his family is the most aristocratic in Bunbury. He permits Dorothy to accept food that the inhabitants can spare, later shows her around town, and objects when Toto and Billina harm Bunbury inhabitants. He orders the travelers to leave and threatens to put them into the great ovens if they refuse.
