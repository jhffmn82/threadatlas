Contriever

model · 4 works · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Contriever architecture, Contriever model, a retriever

Abstract written by a model

In “Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine,” Contriever is a 110M-parameter general-domain semantic retriever in MEDRAG that uses inner product. In “Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs?”, Contriever is a contrastively trained dense retriever that outperforms BM25 on BEIR datasets and retrieves 300-word chunks. In “Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” Contriever is used for dense retrieval as an unsupervised contrastive encoder and serves as the second robust unsupervised retrieval baseline in the evaluation. In “Wiki-LLaVA: Hierarchical Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multimodal LLMs,” Contriever embeds passages and retrieves relevant ones in Wiki-LLaVA’s hierarchical scheme, supplying them after or alongside image-based entity retrieval to support answer generation.
