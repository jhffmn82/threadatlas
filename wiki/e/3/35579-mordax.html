Mordax

Abstract written by a model

Mordax is an admirable man: ardent in intellectual work, public-spirited, affectionate, and able to convey ingenious ideas and elevated feeling through well-chosen words. He is kindly, conscientious, generous, and tender toward a suffering friend outside special temptations. Yet he resists admitting error and welcomes neither novelty nor ideas he did not discover. When contradiction or correction threatens his fiery self-respect, arrogant egoism inflames him into self-justifying unscrupulousness, cruelty, and polemical rudeness.

VIII.

Mordax is ardent in intellectual work, public-spirited, affectionate, and skilled at expressing ingenious ideas and elevated feeling. He refuses to admit error or welcome ideas that are new to him, and when his ego is threatened by contradiction he can become unscrupulous, cruel, polemically rude, and self-justifying. His kindness toward a sick friend and his generosity in other relations are presented as compatible with this specific brutality, which arises when his arrogant egoism is inflamed.
