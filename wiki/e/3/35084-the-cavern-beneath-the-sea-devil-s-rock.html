the cavern beneath the sea-devil's rock in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

An enormous sea cavern beneath a sky-like dome contains the Palace of Zog, Zog's coral castle, artistic beds of sea flowers, and goldfish and silverfish. Its outside opening is guarded by the prince of the sea devils, and its brilliant light endures. Once associated with Zog's dangerous slaves, it becomes quiet, peaceful, and safe after Zog's destruction; the travelers reenter, then the four fugitives leave it.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

The sea cavern is enormous, with a dome resembling the sky viewed from earth. It contains Zog's coral castle, carefully arranged beds of sea flowers, and goldfish and silverfish moving among the foliage.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

The great dome surrounds the castle and has an opening that is guarded by the prince of the sea devils. Its brilliant light does not fade, even after several hours.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

The cavern is described as safe after Zog's destruction. The travelers reenter it through the opening in the dome and find the castle quiet and peaceful.
