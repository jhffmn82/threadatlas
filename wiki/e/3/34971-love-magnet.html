the Love Magnet in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

The Love Magnet is a small, dull brown horseshoe-shaped piece of metal, kept wrapped in paper and tied with cotton string. It is a charm that makes every living thing its bearer meets love the bearer, especially causing those near or encountering the Shaggy Man to love him. It makes Button-Bright, King Dox, the foxes, the Scoodlers, and the travelers love the Shaggy Man, and may help him gain King Kik-a-bray's affection after his transformation. The Shaggy Man uses it to summon Johnny Dooit, a powerful friend who loves him because he owns it. He later admits stealing it from a girl in Butterfield because he wanted love. Rather than give it to Dorothy, Button-Bright, or the Wizard, he agrees it should hang above the Emerald City gates, causing love for whoever enters or leaves.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Way to Butterfield

The Love Magnet is a small, dull, brown piece of metal shaped like a horseshoe. The Shaggy Man says it was given to him by an Eskimo in the Sandwich Islands and that every living thing he meets loves him while he carries it. He keeps it wrapped in paper and tied with a cotton string.

3. A Queer Village

The Love Magnet causes Button-Bright to love the Shaggy Man at once. It also makes the fox-captain, King Dox, and the other foxes love the Shaggy Man, although the Shaggy Man does not understand the strange effects connected with it.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

The Love Magnet may help the shaggy man win King Kik-a-bray's affection and continues to make the travelers love him after his transformation.

11. Johnny Dooit Does It

The Shaggy Man takes the Love Magnet from his pocket and calls Johnny Dooit by speaking to it. He says the charm enables him to call a powerful friend who loves him because he owns the magnet.

20. Princess Ozma Of Oz

The shaggy man admits that he stole the Love Magnet from a girl in Butterfield because he wanted to be loved. After considering giving it to Dorothy, Button-Bright, or the Wizard, he agrees that it should be hung over the gates of the Emerald City.

Love Magnet in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Love Magnet is a small silver horseshoe-shaped magic talisman whose charm makes those who see it love its owner, turning hatred into love. Shaggy Man uses it on the Royal Gardener, Hank, and the Army of Oogaboo, including Queen Ann, though the heartless Roses are unaffected. He hides it in his pocket; Ruggedo avoids seeing it, binds Shaggy’s arms, and transforms him into a dove.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Six

The Love Magnet is a small silver horseshoe-shaped enchantment that wins the heart of everyone who looks at it. Shaggy Man uses it to make the Royal Gardener and Hank affectionate toward him, but it has no effect on the Roses because they have no hearts.

Chapter Eight

The Love Magnet causes everyone in the Army of Oogaboo, including Queen Ann, to love Shaggy and abandon their hostility. Shaggy hides it in his pocket after Queen Ann embraces him.

Chapter Seventeen

The Love Magnet is in Shaggy Man's pocket and would affect Ruggedo if he looked at it. Ruggedo prevents Shaggy Man from displaying it by binding his arms and then transforms its owner into a dove.
