3 .. Faithfulness

Statements are extracted from the generated answer by creating one or more statements from each sentence. Their purpose is to decompose longer sentences into shorter and more focused assertions for faithfulness evaluation.

opening .. Context relevance

A statement is evaluated for whether the information in the context supports it. The verification prompt requires an explanation and a final Yes/No verdict for each statement.
