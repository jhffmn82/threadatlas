judgment of others by oneself in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Judgment of others by oneself is a method for knowing mankind, but it requires practice for wise judgment: it may reduce everyone to the judge’s low standard or too generously misread a lower nature.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII.

Judgment of others by oneself is, in its innocent sense, the briefest expression of the only method of knowing mankind. It can also become a vulgar mistake that reduces everyone to the judge’s own low standard or an amiable illusion that interprets a lower nature too generously.
