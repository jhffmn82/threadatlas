small authorship in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Small authorship causes a chronic moral ailment: magnified, disfiguring self-importance, leading sufferers to make an ordinary book or pamphlet central to identity and impose it on acquaintances.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XV.

Small authorship is presented as capable of producing a chronic moral ailment in which self-importance becomes magnified and disfiguring. Its sufferers may turn an otherwise ordinary book or pamphlet into the central feature of their social identity and continually impose it on acquaintances.
