Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

ANTIGONE

Creon’s proclamation forbids anyone to cover Polynices’ body, mourn him, or give him funeral rites. It declares that his corpse must remain exposed to carrion birds and dogs, and that anyone who disobeys will be killed.

CHORUS

Creon's decree leaves Polynices unburied and orders Antigone to be sealed in a tomb. He initially insists that neither divine intervention nor prophetic advice will change it, but finally resolves to undo the decree.

CHORUS.

Creon’s decree is treated as the cause of the disaster. Creon recognizes that his seemingly wise decision and his folly led to Haemon’s and Eurydicè’s deaths.
