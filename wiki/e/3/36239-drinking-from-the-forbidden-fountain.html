28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

Drinking from the Forbidden Fountain gives the invading creatures relief from their thirst but makes them forget who they are and why they came to Oz. The Nome King initially avoids drinking, but the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman throw him into the fountain.
