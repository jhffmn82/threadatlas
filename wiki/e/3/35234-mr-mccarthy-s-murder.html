Mr. McCarthy's murder in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mr. McCarthy's murder, a crime, occurred after he apparently arranged to meet someone at the pool; a heavy blow from behind shattered parts of his skull and caused his death.

Mr. McCarthy's murder was caused by a heavy blow that shattered the posterior third of the left parietal bone and the left half of the occipital bone. The injury indicates that the blow came from behind, and Mr. McCarthy had apparently arranged to meet someone at the pool.
