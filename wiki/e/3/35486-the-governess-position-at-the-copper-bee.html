XII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE COPPER BEECHES

The governess position at the Copper Beeches offers Violet Hunter £120 a year, far more than the usual salary she describes. Its duties include caring for one child and obeying Mrs. Rucastle's proper commands, including unusual instructions about dress, seating, and hair.
