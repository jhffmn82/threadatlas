Abstract written by a model

protects Medea [from expulsion or surrender] Aegeus swears by the earth, the sun, and all the Gods that he will not cast Medea out of his country or willingly surrender her if her enemies try to drag her away. Medea accepts the oath as sufficient security for her plans.
