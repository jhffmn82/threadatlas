Ozma's birthday in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where Ozma's birthday first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ozma's birthday celebration in the Emerald City, with ceremonies in the green fields, is prepared through arriving guests and their presents. It culminates in a large public procession and varied entertainment, music, magic, juggling, dancing, flying, and giant soap bubbles, attended by foreign guests and Oz citizens before the guests depart home.

Princess Ozma's birthday celebration in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where Princess Ozma's birthday celebration first appears

Abstract written by a model

Princess Ozma's birthday celebration is a planned royal function, scheduled for the twenty-first and intended as the grandest and most brilliant ceremony in Fairyland. It honors Ozma and is arranged on the broad green outside the Emerald City gates so all her people can attend without crowding. As preparations proceed, Dorothy and her companions travel to reach the party, while Nick Chopper is polished; King Dox and King Kik-a-bray seek invitations. Invited guests arrive the day before, including Santa Claus, Queen Zixi of Ix, the royal family of Ev, other rulers, and unusual visitors, bringing many presents. A grand evening banquet gathers the visitors, with the Wizard's magic exhibition planned afterward. The banquet is presented as only the introduction to the birthday celebration, which is scheduled for the following morning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

3. A Queer Village

Princess Ozma's birthday celebration is scheduled for the twenty-first of the month. King Dox describes it as the most brilliant royal ceremony ever held in any city in Fairyland and hopes Dorothy can obtain him an invitation.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

Ozma's birthday celebration is expected to be the grandest royal function in Fairyland. King Kik-a-bray wants an invitation, and Dorothy promises to request one for him if she reaches Ozma.

14. Tik-Tok and Billina

Ozma's birthday celebration is being prepared as a grand affair. Dorothy is glad that she and her friends have arrived in time to attend it, while Nick Chopper is being polished for the party.

17. The Royal Chariot Arrives

Ozma's party is the celebration toward which Dorothy and her companions are traveling. The Cowardly Lion says it will be a grand affair with many fat babies present, though the Hungry Tiger cannot eat them because of his conscience.

20. Princess Ozma Of Oz

Ozma's birthday celebration will take place on the broad green outside the gates of the Emerald City so that all her people can attend without being crowded. Invited guests will arrive the day before, and Ozma promises that interesting and curious people will be among them.

22. Important Arrivals

Ozma's birthday celebration brings many important guests to the Throne-Room, including Santa Claus, Queen Zixi of Ix, the royal family of Ev, and other rulers and unusual visitors. The guests bring numerous presents, and the remaining visitors prepare to attend the grand banquet that evening.

23. The Grand Banquet

The birthday celebration for Ozma is scheduled for the next morning. The royal banquet is described as merely the introduction to that celebration.
