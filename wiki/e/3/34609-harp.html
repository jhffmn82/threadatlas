THE PERSIANS AND ARABS.

The harp, called chang by the Persians and junk by the Arabs, was formerly a favorite Persian instrument but gradually fell into disuse. Persian harps appeared in sculptures and manuscripts, and an old Persian chang probably appears in a seventeenth-century representation of a Turkish woman playing the harp.
