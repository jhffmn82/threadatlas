Abstract written by a model

Generator model M is a large language model trained on outputs augmented with retrieved passages and reflection tokens. For each input and preceding generation, it predicts retrieval decisions and critique tokens; it either generates standard output segments or processes retrieved passages in parallel, with passages masked from training loss.

generator model M is in one work on this site, SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
