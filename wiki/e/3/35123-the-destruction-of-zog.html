the destruction of Zog in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The destruction of Zog ends the chief danger facing Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia in the cavern, ends Zog's centuries-old defiance of King Anko, frees Zog's slaves, and lets the travelers leave the enchanted castle safely for the open sea.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

Zog's destruction ends the chief danger facing Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia and removes King Anko's centuries-old enemy. The event also enables the travelers to leave the enchanted castle and return safely to the open sea.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

The destruction of Zog ends the danger in the cavern and frees his slaves from his rule. It also leaves the former slaves responsible for governing themselves and managing his possessions.
