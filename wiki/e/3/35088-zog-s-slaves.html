Abstract written by a model

Zog's slaves are hundreds of mortals rescued from drowning and brought to Zog's enchanted castle, where they are confined to the great cave and live underwater with gills. Men, women, and children wear gold ankle bands as badges of servitude, remain jolly and good-natured, yet tremble when Zog smiles. Born in the earth, they cannot return there without perishing. Some are goldsmiths taught to work metal underwater. After Zog's death, the former slaves inherit and occupy the castle, choose to remain, labor for their community and themselves, and accept Cap'n Joe as king.

Zog's slaves is in one work on this site, The Sea Fairies. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Zog's slaves include men, women, and children who wear gold bands around their ankles and have gills for breathing underwater. They appear jolly and serene rather than miserable, but they tremble whenever Zog smiles.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Some slaves in Zog's castle are goldsmiths who have been taught to forge and work metal under water. They live under Zog's control, and their work includes making gold ornaments and furniture.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Zog's hundreds of former slaves assemble in the golden hall after his death. They decide to remain in the castle, work for their own community, and accept Cap'n Joe as their king.
