I. A SCANDAL IN BOHEMIA

The photograph scandal concerns Irene Adler’s possession of evidence of her past relationship with the King of Bohemia. If she sends the photograph when the King’s betrothal is publicly proclaimed, it may end his planned marriage and compromise a reigning European family.
