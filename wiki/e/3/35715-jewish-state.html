A Jewish state is considered as a possible restoration on the old ground, serving as a center of national feeling, a source of dignifying protection, and a channel for distinctive energies. Its establishment would depend on sufficient Jewish communal feeling, widespread need, favorable political conditions, and leaders willing to guide the return.
