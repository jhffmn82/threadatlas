Jewish state in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

A Jewish state is envisioned as a restored center on historic ground: if Jewish communal feeling, widespread need, favorable political conditions, and willing leaders support return, it can protect the Jewish people with dignity, concentrate national feeling, and express national genius through special energies.

A Jewish state is considered as a possible restoration on the old ground, serving as a center of national feeling, a source of dignifying protection, and a channel for distinctive energies. Its establishment would depend on sufficient Jewish communal feeling, widespread need, favorable political conditions, and leaders willing to guide the return.
