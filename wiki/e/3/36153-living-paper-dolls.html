10. How the Cuttenclips Lived

The living paper dolls are cut from Glinda’s magic paper and can walk, talk, think, sing, dance, and perform ordinary village activities. They are nearly all the same height but have varied shapes and costumes, and they retreat into their houses when strangers approach.
