Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHORUS, PHÆDRA.

Phædra's letter is found sealed in her hand after her death. It accuses Hippolytus of forcing himself upon her and prompts Theseus to invoke Neptune's curse.

HIPPOLYTUS, THESEUS, CHORUS.

The letter left by the deceased woman accuses Hippolytus and is treated by Theseus as faithful proof that requires no interpretation by seers. Theseus relies on it instead of Hippolytus's oath and denial.
