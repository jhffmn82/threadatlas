[Illustration: _Chap. 6._

Queen Aquareine's palace contains couches, spacious rooms, a glass-roofed kitchen, hot springs, and flower gardens. Its curved kitchen roof concentrates sunlight strongly enough to cook food, while the hot springs provide heat on cloudy days. The palace is surrounded by dense shrubbery and has avenues leading from its sea garden into the ocean.
