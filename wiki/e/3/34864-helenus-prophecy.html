Helenus' prophecy

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Destiny divine, clearly told, divine prophecy, foretold, must be so, prophecies, prophecy, prophet, word of prophecy

Abstract written by a model

Helenus' prophecy, delivered by the Trojan captive and prophet Helenus, concerns Troy's capture. It declares that the Greeks cannot sack Troy until Philoctetes returns from Lemnos with his bow and is healed; then Troy will fall before harvest. Odysseus uses the prophecy to pursue Philoctetes, while it restrains him from attacking Troy before the destined time. Heracles confirms the foretold outcome as Zeus' intention.

Helenus' prophecy is in one work on this site, The Seven Plays in English Verse. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PHILOCTETES

Prophecy reveals that Philoctetes must return with the bow for Troy to be taken. Neoptolemus says that the prophecy prevents Philoctetes from bending his deadly shaft against Troy before the destined time.

_Enter_ PHILOCTETES.

Helenus, a princely seer and son of Priam, prophesied that the Greeks could not sack Troy until they brought Philoctetes from Lemnos. Odysseus captured Helenus and used this prophecy to initiate the pursuit of Philoctetes.

MONODY.

A prophecy attributed to Helenus states that Troy will fall before harvest if Philoctetes is brought to Troy and healed. Heracles confirms this outcome and presents it as part of Zeus' intention.
