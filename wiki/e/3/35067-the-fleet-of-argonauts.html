the fleet of argonauts in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The argonauts are shell-dwelling sea creatures that hide from wolf fishes, carry shells like humps, and use skinny sails underwater. A party encounters a school traveling upward to sail; at the surface, they float on air currents, inspiring men’s sailing boats.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

The party encounters a school of argonauts traveling toward the surface for a sail. Argonauts live in shells, hide in them from wolf fishes, carry the shells like humps, and spread skinny sails to travel through the water and later float on air currents at the surface.
