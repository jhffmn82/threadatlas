RAG practices study in Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xiaohua Wang, Zhenghua Wang, Xuan Gao, Feiran Zhang, Yixin Wu, Zhibo Xu, Tianyuan Shi, Zhengyuan Wang, Shizheng Li, Qi Qian, Ruicheng Yin, Changze Lv, Xiaoqing Zheng, Xuanjing Huang · 2024

This study identifies optimal practices for implementing retrieval-augmented generation to improve generated content. It introduces a comprehensive evaluation benchmark, compares representative methods for each RAG step while holding other modules constant, and assesses potential solutions for every module. It then explores promising combinations under different efficiency and performance priorities, recommends the most effective approach for each module, and contributes to deeper understanding and a foundation for future research.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

The study compares representative methods for each RAG step, evaluates individual methods while holding other modules constant, and then explores promising combinations for different efficiency and performance priorities. It aims to identify and recommend optimal RAG practices through extensive experimentation.

Conclusion

This study aims to identify optimal practices for implementing retrieval-augmented generation and systematically assesses potential solutions for each module within the RAG framework. It recommends the most effective approach for each module, introduces a comprehensive evaluation benchmark for RAG systems, and conducts extensive experiments to determine the best practices among various alternatives.
