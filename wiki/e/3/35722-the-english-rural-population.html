is mixed [in the present day] The English rural population of the narrator's father's time included miners, weavers, field-labourers, farmers, and people of other ranks whose motives and habits could be learned through daily fellowship. The narrator suggests that this population may have changed enough that his father would find the present rural population difficult to understand.
