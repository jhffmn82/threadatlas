Abstract written by a model

In The Road to Oz, the Rainbow is Polychrome’s home and father, from which she slides and which later descends to lift her skyward before fading as she merges with its colors. In Sky Island, the Rainbow appears through breaks in the clouds, descends to bear Polychrome and her sisters, and then fades, rises, dissolves, and disappears. In Tik-Tok of Oz, Rainbow is carried by the Daughters of the Rainbow as their bow, carries Polychrome toward her sky home, and later rescues her from the Nome Kingdom before melting into clouds. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Rainbow appears after rain, touches the ground, carries Polychrome and her sisters, and then lifts and slowly melts away as the sun emerges.

the Rainbow in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where the Rainbow first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Rainbow is Polychrome’s home and father, stretched across the country with one end on a lonely road. Polychrome slides down from it; it melts when she grasps it. Later it descends at a celebration, lifts her skyward, and fades after she merges with its colors.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

5. The Rainbow's Daughter

Polychrome's father stretched a rainbow across the country so that one end touched the lonely road. Polychrome slid from it to the ground, and the rainbow melted away when she tried to hold on.

24. The Birthday Celebration

A gorgeous rainbow suddenly descends onto the celebration platform after Polychrome says she may return home. Its end lifts Polychrome into the sky, then fades and disappears after she has merged with its colors.

the Rainbow in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912 · the work’s page · read it where the Rainbow first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Rainbow is immense, with vivid, blended colors. It appears through breaks in clouds near the Pink Country or over the Court of the Statues, descending to a field and bearing Polychrome and her sisters. After Polychrome departs or they are carried skyward, its colors fade and the arch rises, dissolves, and disappears.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

The Rainbow appears through a break in the clouds near the edge of the Pink Country, its end descending onto the pink field. Polychrome and the other Daughters of the Rainbow are seen upon it before Polychrome leaves, after which it rises, fades, and disappears.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Rainbow appears over the Court of the Statues and carries Polychrome and her dancing sisters away into the sky. Its colors then fade and the arch dissolves, while Polychrome says that behind the clouds is always the Rainbow.
