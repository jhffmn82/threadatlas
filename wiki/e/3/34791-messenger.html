Messenger

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: MESS., SECOND MESSENGER, Second Messenger, messenger

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Messenger reports Jocasta’s death and Oedipus’s blindness, then announces that Oedipus wants exile after the palace is opened. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Second Messenger reports Jocasta’s death, describing her hanging, Oedipus’s discovery and self-blinding, and his wish for exile.
