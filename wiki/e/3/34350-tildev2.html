TILDEv2

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

TILDEv2 is a reranking method recommended for Balanced Efficiency Practice. It improves efficiency relative to TILDE, greatly reduces index size by indexing only document-present tokens, uses NCE loss and document expansion, and is ideal for the quickest experience on a fixed collection.

TILDEv2 is in one work on this site, Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
