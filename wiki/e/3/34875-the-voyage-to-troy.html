Abstract written by a model

The voyage to Troy, ordained by heaven, is necessary for Philoctetes to be healed and join the Achaean host. Though he refuses because of suffering and abandonment, Odysseus presses, Neoptolemus hesitates, and Heracles commands; finally all prepare to leave Lemnos on a favorable wind.

CHORUS.

The voyage to Troy is presented as commanded by heaven and necessary for Philoctetes to join the Achaean host. Philoctetes refuses it because of his suffering and past abandonment, while Odysseus seeks to force it and Neoptolemus hesitates.

MONODY.

The voyage to Troy is proposed by Neoptolemus and rejected by Philoctetes until Heracles commands it. At the end of the unit, Philoctetes, Neoptolemus, and the Chorus prepare to depart from Lemnos with a favorable wind.
