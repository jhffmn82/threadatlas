Abstract written by a model

The Fuddles are peculiar people in the Land of Oz, made of many small pieces. Though jolly and pleasant when properly assembled, they scatter when strangers approach and never match themselves; after being put together, they welcome the travelers.

12. How they Matched the Fuddles

The Fuddles are peculiar people made of many small pieces that can be matched together. They are pleasant when assembled but scatter themselves when strangers approach, and they welcome the travelers after being put together.
