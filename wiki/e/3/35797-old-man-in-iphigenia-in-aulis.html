Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

IPHIGENIA IN AULIS.

The Old Man serves Agamemnon and is described as faithful to Agamemnon’s wife and household. He is instructed to carry Agamemnon’s sealed letter to Argos, but Menelaus intercepts him, seizes the letter, and threatens him.

[_Enter_ ACHILLES.]

The Old Man, a faithful former servant of Clytæmnestra's household, reveals that Agamemnon intends to sacrifice Iphigenia. He says that he had carried a letter warning Clytæmnestra not to bring her daughter, but Menelaus took the letter from him.
