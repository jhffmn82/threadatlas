Abstract written by a model

Eyford is a small Berkshire village or locality near Reading and the Oxfordshire border, served by a railway station. It matters as the rail-accessible centre for journeys to Colonel Lysander Stark’s remote country property and later for Inspector Bradstreet’s search. Hatherley is directed to meet Stark there, travels onward by carriage, escapes and returns to its vicinity for a morning train to Reading; afterward, the search party reaches Eyford and sees smoke from the nearby fire at Dr. Becher’s house.

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Eyford is a small place in Berkshire near the Oxfordshire border and within seven miles of Reading. Colonel Lysander Stark directs Hatherley to meet him there at about 11:15, after which they are to travel seven miles by carriage to the country property.

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

The narrator reaches Eyford by train and is told that the Stark residence lies roughly ten miles away. After escaping, he returns to the vicinity of Eyford and takes the morning train to Reading.
