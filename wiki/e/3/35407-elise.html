Elise

Abstract written by a model

Elise is a pretty, German-speaking woman of the Stark household who speaks broken English fearfully. She warns the narrator to leave the residence, then saves him from the hydraulic press and Colonel Lysander Stark by opening a concealed panel, leading him toward escape, and trying to stop Stark's attack. Afterward she is identified among fugitives from Dr. Becher's house, seen traveling toward Reading with people and bulky boxes, and is never heard of again.

Elise is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
