Elise in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Elise is a pretty, German-speaking woman of the Stark household who speaks broken English fearfully. She warns the narrator to leave the residence, then saves him from the hydraulic press and Colonel Lysander Stark by opening a concealed panel, leading him toward escape, and trying to stop Stark's attack. Afterward she is identified among fugitives from Dr. Becher's house, seen traveling toward Reading with people and bulky boxes, and is never heard of again.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

Elise warns the narrator to leave the Stark residence before it is too late. She opens a concealed panel to save him from the hydraulic press and leads him toward a bedroom and window, then tries to restrain Stark and prevent him from attacking the narrator.
