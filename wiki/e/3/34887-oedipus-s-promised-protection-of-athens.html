Oedipus's promised protection of Athens in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

Oedipus’s promised protection is a blessing granted to Athens and denied to Thebes. On a holy mission, Oedipus offers burial in Athenian land as a powerful defense against enemies; Theseus accepts it and protects him under Athenian authority.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

OEDIPUS AT COLONOS

Oedipus says that he has come on a holy mission bearing rich blessings for the people who shelter him. The introductory account states that this blessing will be secured to Athens and denied to Thebes.

_Enter_ THESEUS.

Oedipus promises that his burial in Athens will provide a powerful defense against its enemies. Theseus accepts this promised benefit and agrees to protect Oedipus, provided Oedipus remains under his authority and protection.
