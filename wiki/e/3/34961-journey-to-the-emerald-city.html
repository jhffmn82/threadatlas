Abstract written by a model

This is a journey toward the Emerald City and the Land of Oz, undertaken by Dorothy and companions while Dorothy intends to find Princess Ozma. After spending a night in Foxville, Dorothy, Button-Bright, and the Shaggy Man resume travel, promise to seek Princess Ozma, and are escorted toward an unknown village. Toto and Polychrome travel with Dorothy, Shaggy Man, and Button-Bright; Polychrome joins to keep warm and seek a way home. The road takes them through Dunkiton, where they stay overnight, then through green fields, groves, and unknown country. They encounter Allegro da Capo, leave the Truth Pond after Button-Bright and the Shaggy Man are restored, and seek directions. After meeting Tik-Tok and Billina, they continue toward the Emerald City, planning to stop at Nick Chopper's new castle before arriving the following day.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

4. King Dox

After spending the night in Foxville, Dorothy, Button-Bright, and the shaggy man resume their journey and promise to seek Princess Ozma. A fox-captain escorts them to an arch leading toward another village inhabited by large, supposedly stupid beasts. The travelers are uneasy because they do not know what these beasts are, but they continue along the road.

5. The Rainbow's Daughter

Dorothy, Toto, Shaggy Man, and Button-Bright are traveling toward the Emerald City. Polychrome joins them because travel will help keep her warm and because she hopes to find a way home.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

The travelers enter Dunkiton because the road passes through it, stay there overnight, and leave the next morning. They continue along the road through green fields and groves toward the unknown country and possibly the Land of Oz.

8. The Musicker

The travelers discover Allegro da Capo while going along the road toward the Land of Oz. They decline to take him along, hurry away to escape his music, and continue after reaching the other side of a hill.

13. The Truth Pond

After being restored, Button-Bright and the Shaggy Man join Dorothy, Polly, and Polychrome in leaving the Truth Pond. They continue searching for someone who can direct them on their way to the Emerald City.

14. Tik-Tok and Billina

The travelers continue along a road toward the Emerald City after meeting Tik-Tok and Billina. They plan to stop that night at Nick Chopper's castle and arrive in the Emerald City the following day.
