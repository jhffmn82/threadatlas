Nationalities in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Nationalities are valued for moderating inevitable racial fusion and opposing alien subjection when a distinct, coherent people forms a state; modern insistence condemns alien, antipathetic rule, but national claims require discrimination rather than arbitrary, inconsistent preference.

The modern insistence on Nationalities is said to have value because it condemns the subjection of a distinct and coherent people to an alien, antipathetic government. The text nevertheless warns that sympathy for national claims must be guided by discrimination rather than applied through arbitrary or inconsistent preferences.
