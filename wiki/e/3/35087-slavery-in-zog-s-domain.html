slavery in Zog's domain in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Slavery in Zog's domain is a system of servitude with Zog as sole master, encompassing dozens or hundreds of slaves, including Prince Sacho, who says they share a class and are commanded to hate Zog.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Zog's domain contains dozens or possibly hundreds of slaves, all serving Zog as their only master. Sacho says there is no jealousy among them because they belong to the same class, and he is commanded to hate Zog even though he does not personally hate him.
