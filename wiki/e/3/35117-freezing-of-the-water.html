freezing of the water in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Zog’s magic freezes all water in the room around the sleeping group, imprisoning Trot in ice until Queen Aquareine melts it and rescues them.

Zog's magic freezes all the water in the room while the group sleeps, leaving them trapped inside solid ice with only small air spaces. Queen Aquareine melts the ice and rescues them before they perish.
