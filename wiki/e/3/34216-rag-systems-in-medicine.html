RAG systems in medicine

Abstract written by a model

RAG systems in medicine are implemented for scientific and clinical question answering, literature information-seeking, and clinical decision-making. This first systematic evaluation studies how document collections, retrieval algorithms, and backbone Large Language Models affect performance. Using the MIRAGE benchmark, MEDRAG toolkit, and question-only retrieval in a more realistic medical QA evaluation, it produces deployment recommendations. It primarily evaluates vanilla RAG and identifies improvements through newer designs, additional corpora and FAQs, cross-encoder re-rankers, and evaluation beyond QA.
