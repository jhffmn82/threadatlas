Amazon-stone

Also called: This kind of stone

Abstract written by a model

Amazon-stone, named after the Amazon by early European visitors to America, makes a metallic sound when struck. Though not found only near the Amazon, American Indians formerly cut it into thin, centrally perforated plates and suspended them by strings.

Amazon-stone is in one work on this site, Musical Instruments. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Humboldt described Amazon-stone as producing a metallic sound when struck by a hard substance. American Indians formerly cut it into thin, centrally perforated plates and suspended them by strings; the stone was not found exclusively near the Amazon.
