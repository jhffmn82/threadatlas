VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

Helen says that after hearing the low whistle on the night of Julia's death, she heard a clanging or metallic sound like a mass of metal falling. Holmes questions whether she is certain of this sound, since the storm and the old house may have confused her.
