the mermaid palaces in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The mermaid palaces, the travelers’ destination, lie at the ocean’s deepest bottom and are inhabited only by mermaids, excluding sharks. Extensive buildings of white, pink, and yellow coral, they have archway entrances and glass roofs made through fairy powers. Electric lights and hundreds of electric jellyfish brightly illuminate rooms furnished with iridescent shells, coral, amber sponges, and polished tortoise-shell conveniences; glass ceilings let fishes and other sea creatures look inside.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

The mermaid palaces are the travelers' destination and are located at the very bottom of the ocean, in its deepest part. They are inhabited only by mermaids, so sharks cannot enter them.

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

The mermaids' palaces are extensive coral buildings made with white, pink, and yellow coral arranged in graceful designs. Their roofs are made of glass through Queen Aquareine's fairy powers, and archways serve as entrances.

[Illustration: _Chap. 8._

The mermaid palaces are brightly lit by electric jellyfish and contain rooms decorated with iridescent shells, coral furniture, amber sponges, and polished tortoise-shell conveniences. Their glass ceilings allow fishes and other sea creatures to look inside.
