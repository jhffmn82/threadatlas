Abstract written by a model

Violet Hunter's long, luxuriant, chestnut-colored hair is required to be cut short for employment. Though she first refuses, an increased offer leads her to sacrifice it. She keeps the cut coil in her trunk, then finds an identical coil in a locked drawer while hers remains there.

XII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE COPPER BEECHES

Violet Hunter's hair is long, luxuriant, and chestnut-colored, and she initially refuses to cut it for the position. After receiving Jep hro Rucastle's increased offer, she decides to sacrifice it and cut it before starting work.
