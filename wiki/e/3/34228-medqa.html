MedQA is a USMLE-style medical question answering dataset curated by medical examination experts from question banks. It provides 10,178 training, 1,272 validation, and 1,273 test multiple-choice question-answer pairs. It supports training filtering models and evaluating language models, RAG, retrieval, filtering, balanced retrieval, and rationale-query quality, with reported improvements from the authors’ methods.
