THE PERSIANS AND ARABS.

Khosroo Purviz was the Persian monarch during whose lifetime the sculptures at Tackt-i-Bostan are said to have been executed. The sculptures depict musicians, including harpers, trumpet players, drummers, a flutist, a player of a pandean pipe, and a bagpipe player.
