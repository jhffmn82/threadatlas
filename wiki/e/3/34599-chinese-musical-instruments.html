Chinese musical instruments

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: ancient Chinese musical instruments, instruments, musical instruments

Abstract written by a model

Chinese musical instruments included the king and pien-king made from sonorous stones, bells such as the tchung, wooden instruments such as the fang-hiang and ou, drums such as the po-fou and kin-kou, bamboo flutes and pipes, the clay hiuen, the cheng with tubes placed in a calabash, and the stringed kin and chê. They were used for accompanying songs of praise, religious observances, imperial and military purposes, marking time, producing rhythm, and performing melodies. Some instruments were arranged in sets according to prescribed intervals, while others followed the pentatonic scale.
