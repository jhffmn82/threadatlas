the cage formed by the sea devils in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The cage formed by the sea devils is an enclosure of interlaced scarlet arms around travelers, leaving no escape opening; as the sea devils swim, it carries the prisoners forward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

The cage formed by the sea devils is made by interlacing their long arms or feelers around the travelers. It has no opening large enough for escape and carries its prisoners forward as the sea devils swim.
