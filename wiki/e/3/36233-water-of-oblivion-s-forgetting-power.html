Water of Oblivion's forgetting power in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Water of Oblivion causes anyone who drinks it to immediately lose all prior knowledge and memories without madness. Its forgetting removes invaders’ identities, goals, alliances, and hostility toward Oz, leaving them ignorant, smiling, content, harmless, and childlike.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

Anyone who drinks the Water of Oblivion immediately forgets everything ever known and becomes as ignorant as a baby. The water does not cause madness, but it removes all knowledge and memories.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

The Water of Oblivion's memory loss removes the invaders' knowledge of their identities, goals, alliances, and hostility toward Oz. It leaves them smiling, content, and harmless, like children.
