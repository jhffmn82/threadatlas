Lord High Chigglewitz in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Lord High Chigglewitz, first named Larry, is Fuddlecumjig’s chief personage; travelers assemble him from his pieces, but part of his left knee is missing.

12. How they Matched the Fuddles

Lord High Chigglewitz is the chief personage of Fuddlecumjig, and his first name is Larry. The travelers assemble him from his bald head, blue coat, pink vest, and other pieces, though part of his left knee is missing.

Larry in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Larry, the bald-headed, fat Lord High Chigglewitz, wears a blue brass-buttoned coat, pink vest, and drab breeches, with part of his left knee missing; travelers match his scattered pieces, and he explains this is his peculiarity.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

12. How they Matched the Fuddles

Larry is the name of the Lord High Chigglewitz. After the travelers match him together, he congratulates them and explains that scattering himself is his personal peculiarity.
