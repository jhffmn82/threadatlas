Abstract written by a model

The manuscripts are left to judgment outside the speaker’s imagination; feared as flat and lacking subtilty, they unfold from reticent self-expression into paper garrulousness and may end as fuel for a domestic fire.

The speaker leaves the manuscripts to an external judgment but does not ask to hear that judgment during life. The speaker fears they may be judged flat and may be most usefully applied in lighting a domestic fire.
