Phanfasms

Abstract written by a model

The Phanfasms are dreaded, powerful, merciless Erbs and one of the world’s three most powerful bands of evil spirits. They live at the summit of the Mountain of Phantastico, can transform their appearances, and chiefly delight in destroying happiness. Seeking to destroy Oz people because they are good and happy, they ally with the Nome King for the invasion of Oz, though they secretly intend afterward to destroy the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Nome army and ravage the world. As the mightiest of the combined forces, they demand to lead the march through the tunnel; their First and Foremost boasts that Ozma will become his slave. They march first into Oz intending conquest and destruction, but many drink at the Forbidden Fountain, forget their hostility, identities, and purpose, and become harmless and childlike. Ozma then sends them safely home with the Magic Belt.

Phanfasms is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

The Phanfasms are powerful and dreaded Erbs who live on the Mountain of Phantastico and can transform their appearances. They agree to help the Nomes conquer Oz because they take pleasure in making happy people unhappy, while secretly planning to destroy the Whimsies, the Growleywogs, and the Nomes afterward.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The Phanfasms agree to assist the Nomes after the tunnel is completed. Roquat fears that they may turn against the Nomes and try to conquer them.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Phanfasms join the invasion as the mightiest of the combined forces. Their First and Foremost demands that they lead the march through the tunnel and boasts that Ozma will become his slave.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The Phanfasms march first through the tunnel under the command of the First and Foremost. They intend to conquer and destroy Oz.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

Many Phanfasms drink from the Forbidden Fountain to clear their dusty throats. They forget their hostility, identities, and purpose, becoming harmless and childlike before Ozma sends them safely home with the Magic Belt.
