Abstract written by a model

The study uses sentence-level chunking to balance simplicity and semantic preservation. It examines chunk size, chunking techniques, metadata addition, and embedding model, selecting LLM-Embedder for performance and size. Metadata addition is deferred to future work, while Appendix A.2 presents further discussion and comparative experiments.
