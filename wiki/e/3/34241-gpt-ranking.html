Also called: The second baseline

Abstract written by a model

In RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation, GPT Ranking is a baseline method that asks ChatGPT to select the preferred answer or context based on a definition of the relevant quality metric, and its agreement with human annotators is lower than that of RAGAS. In Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation, GPT Ranking is a baseline method that asks ChatGPT to select preferred answers or context, with agreement scores of 0.54 faithfulness, 0.40 answer relevance, and 0.52 context relevance.
