prompting in Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain

Yiyun Zhao, Hanoz Bhathena, Prateek Singh, Saket Sharma, Bernardo Ramos, Aviral Joshi, Swaroop Gadiyaram · 2024

Prompting is studied by varying instruction detail, direct quoting, metric introductions, citation requirements, response formats, and answer styles across nine prompts. The central finding is model-dependent robustness: OpenAI models show little quality change, with GPT-4 differing by under 4% and GPT-3.5-turbo by about 6%, while tested Llama-2 base models vary around 15%. Verbosity, quoting, and citation requirements show no salient effects.
