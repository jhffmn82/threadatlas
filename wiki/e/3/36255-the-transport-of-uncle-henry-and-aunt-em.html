Abstract written by a model

Ozma's transport of Uncle Henry and Aunt Em is a planned magical journey from Kansas to the Emerald City. To keep them from becoming nervous, Ozma gives no warning. The next morning she uses the Magic Belt to bring them suddenly to the Throne Room, welcomes them, and arranges for them to live permanently in Oz.

3. How Ozma Granted Dorothy's Request

Ozma plans to transport Uncle Henry and Aunt Em to the Emerald City without warning them first. She believes this will prevent them from becoming nervous if they are told they are making a magical journey, and she plans to carry it out the next morning.

5. How Dorothy Became a Princess

Princess Ozma uses the Magic Belt to transport Uncle Henry and Aunt Em from Kansas to the Throne Room in the Emerald City. They arrive suddenly and unprepared, but Ozma welcomes them and arranges for them to live permanently in Oz.
