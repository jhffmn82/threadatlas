Abstract written by a model

A violent quarrel beside Boscombe Pool, shortly before Charles McCarthy’s death, concerned Mr. McCarthy’s wish that James marry Miss Turner. James refused because he was secretly married, though Mr. McCarthy did not know this. James later withheld the subject and denied that it related to the subsequent tragedy.
