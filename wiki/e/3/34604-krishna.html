Krishna in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Krishna, in incarnation as Vishnu, is represented as a beautiful youth playing a flute, the distinctive favourite instrument constructed by Hindus.

Krishna, in his incarnation as Vishnu, is represented as a beautiful youth playing upon a flute. The Hindus construct a distinctive flute regarded as Krishna's favourite instrument.
