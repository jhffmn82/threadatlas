Krishna

deity · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: god Krishna, human form of Krishna

Abstract written by a model

In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Krishna is the human form in which Vishnu appeared as an invincible warrior who relieved the earth from tyrants and was praised in passages inserted into the Mahabharata. In Musical Instruments, Krishna is represented as a beautiful youth playing a flute.
