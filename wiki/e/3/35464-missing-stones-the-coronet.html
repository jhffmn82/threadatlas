missing stones (the coronet)

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: our stones, the beryls, the gems, the stolen gems, the stones, the three, the three missing stones, these gems, three gems

Abstract written by a model

The entity is three missing beryls from Mr. Holder’s coronet. After the coronet is damaged, the three stones disappear while thirty-six remain; searches fail and Arthur refuses to explain. Sir George keeps the gems after a struggle and sells them to a receiver for six hundred pounds each. Holmes recovers them by buying them back for one thousand pounds apiece and returns them to Mr. Holder.

Three stones are missing from the coronet. The narrator urges Arthur to disclose where the beryls are, but searches reveal nothing and Arthur refuses to speak about them.

“Do you receive much company?” he asked.

The gems are part of Mr. Holder’s coronet, and three of them disappear while thirty-six remain in the damaged coronet. Sherlock Holmes recovers the missing three and returns them to Mr. Holder.
