MedMCQA in Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Jiwoong Sohn · 2025

MedMCQA is a closed-book medical question-answering benchmark and exam-question dataset from AIIMS and NEET PG in India. It contains 182,822 training, 4,183 validation, and 6,150 test pairs, supports filtering-model training, and compares RAG methods; rationale-guided filtering improves reported model performance.

MedMCQA is one of the three closed-book medical question-answering benchmarks used to evaluate RAG2.

MedMCQA comprises examination questions from AIIMS and NEET PG in India. It contains 182,822 training, 4,183 validation, and 6,150 test question-answer pairs, and its training data is also used to train the filtering model for MMLU-Med experiments.
