starfishes in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Starfishes are numerous marine animals near the undiscovered island, lying on the sandy seafloor in varied colors. Usually motionless with five or six extended points, some wind around weeds or walk, and one claims age.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Hundreds of starfishes lie on the sandy ocean floor in various colors, usually motionless with their points extended. Some wind their arms around weeds or use their points as legs, and one claims to be over three months old.
