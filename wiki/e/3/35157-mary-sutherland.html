Mary Sutherland in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mary Sutherland is a young woman living at 31 Lyon Place, Camberwell, with her mother and stepfather, Mr. Windibank. She earns through typewriting and has £100 a year from £2500 in New Zealand stock, interest her stepfather uses for the household. Engaged to Mr. Hosmer Angel, she consults Sherlock Holmes after Angel disappears before their planned wedding and Windibank refuses police involvement. She provides four letters and an advertisement and remains devoted to Angel. Holmes concludes that Windibank, seeking to keep her income from being lost through marriage, impersonated Hosmer Angel and deceived her into believing her bridegroom vanished.

III. A CASE OF IDENTITY

Mary Sutherland consults Sherlock Holmes because Mr. Windibank refuses to involve the police or investigate Mr. Hosmer Angel's disappearance. She describes her engagement and planned wedding to Hosmer, gives Holmes four of his letters and an advertisement, and insists that she will remain true to him.

Miss Mary Sutherland is a warm-hearted young woman whose stepfather seeks to prevent her from marrying because her marriage would cost the household her income. She is deceived by Windibank's disguise as Hosmer Angel and is left believing that the bridegroom has mysteriously disappeared.
