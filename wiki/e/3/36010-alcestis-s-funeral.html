Admetus orders the corse to be carried out and commands mourning throughout Thessaly, including shorn locks, black clothing, and funeral honors. Pheres brings adornments, and Admetus's servants bear the body toward the tomb and pyre.

Alcestis's funeral procession proceeds toward a polished tomb beyond the suburbs on the road to Larissa. Admetus is occupied with carrying her body to burial when Hercules learns what has happened.
