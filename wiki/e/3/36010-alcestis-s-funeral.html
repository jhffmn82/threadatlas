Abstract written by a model

Alcestis's funeral is a twelve-moon mourning and burial observance. Admetus orders her body carried out, commands Thessaly to mourn with shorn locks, black clothing, and funeral honors, and receives adornments from Pheres. Servants bear the body in procession beyond the suburbs toward Alcestis's tomb and pyre on the road to Larissa, where burial is underway as Hercules learns of the calamity.
