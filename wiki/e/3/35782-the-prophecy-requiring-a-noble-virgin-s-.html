the prophecy requiring a noble virgin's sacrifice in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

A prophecy demands the voluntary sacrifice of a noble virgin, not an animal, to save the city and its suppliants. Demophon refuses to force any citizen's daughter. Macaria then offers herself, fulfilling the requirement; the account emphasizes her noble-minded willingness despite notes that the oracle's demand was omitted.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHOR. This land is ever willing to aid in a just cause those in difficulty;

The prophecy requires the sacrifice of a virgin of noble parentage, specifically not a bull or heifer, if the city and the suppliants are to survive. Demophon will not compel a citizen's daughter, and Macaria offers herself willingly to satisfy the requirement.

notes

The sacrifice involved Macaria's voluntary offering of herself in response to the oracle. The notes state that Euripides omitted the oracle's requirement in order to place Macaria's noble-mindedness in a stronger light.
