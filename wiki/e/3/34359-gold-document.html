Abstract written by a model

The gold document is a verified source or relevant document chunk containing the answer. It is used to test guaranteed retrieval by being added to retrieved lists first or last. Models generally perform better when it is retrieved, especially near the top; improving retrieval precision can raise its rank and reduce pseudo-helpfulness when relevant gold documents are absent.
