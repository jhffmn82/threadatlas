misjudges Lentulus [as an unopinionated, useful social listener] Society mistakes Lentulus for an agreeable, unopinionated man who can serve as an admiring listener or vote for others' friends. Lentulus believes that the confused condition of society reveals widespread errors in thinking, marriage, social arrangements, and moral schemes.
