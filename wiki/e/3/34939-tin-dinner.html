tin dinner in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

A plentiful dinner is served in the grand tin dining-hall at tin castle on polished tin dishes, accompanied by a Winkie orchestra. Toto eats beside Dorothy; Nick Chopper eats nothing; Polychrome tastes dewdrops, eats little, and dances afterward.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

15. The Emperor's Tin Castle

The guests eat a plentiful dinner from brightly polished tin dishes in the grand tin dining-hall, while a Winkie orchestra plays tin trumpets, fiddles, drums, cymbals, flutes, and horns. Toto eats from a tin platter while sitting in a tin high-chair beside Dorothy, and Nick Chopper eats nothing because he has no appetite inside him. Polychrome eats only a little and dances after tasting dewdrops.
