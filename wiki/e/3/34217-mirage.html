MIRAGE in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where MIRAGE first appears

Abstract written by a model

MIRAGE is a benchmark for systematically comparing zero-shot medical RAG systems using question-only retrieval. It contains 7,663 questions from five biomedical QA datasets. Across MMLU-Med, MedQA-US, MedMCQA, PubMedQA*, and BioASQ-Y/N, it reveals log-linear scaling with retrieved snippets and lost-in-the-middle effects, informing corpus, retriever, LLM, snippet-count, and context-arrangement choices.

Medical Information Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Medical Information Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Medical Information Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation, the expansion of MIRAGE, is a first-of-its-kind benchmark for evaluating components and configurations of medical Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems. It targets scalable evaluation reflecting real-world medical information needs through zero-shot, multi-choice, question-only retrieval evaluation. The benchmark contains 7,663 biomedical questions from five medical QA datasets, MMLU-Med, MedQA-US, MedMCQA, PubMedQA, and BioASQ-Y/N, and excludes ground-truth supporting contexts. It measures accuracy, standard deviation, and average accuracy across tasks. Used to evaluate medical question-answering systems, it supports comprehensive evaluations, observations, and practical recommendations; MEDRAG improves average benchmark accuracy over chain-of-thought without retrieval for all listed language models.

Introduction

Medical Information Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation is the expansion of MIRAGE. It names the benchmark designed to evaluate different components and configurations of medical Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems.

The MIRAGE Benchmark / Evaluation Settings .. Component Datasets

The MIRAGE Benchmark evaluates RAG systems for medical information needs using zero-shot learning, multi-choice evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, and question-only retrieval. It contains five datasets with 7,663 biomedical questions and measures accuracy, standard deviation, and average accuracy across the tasks.

The MEDRAG Toolkit

The MIRAGE benchmark is used to evaluate different retrieval-augmented generation systems for medical question answering. MEDRAG improves the average benchmark accuracy over chain-of-thought without retrieval for all listed language models.

Conclusion

The MIRAGE benchmark is introduced to evaluate RAG systems in medicine. It contributes to comprehensive evaluations that yield observations and practical recommendations.
