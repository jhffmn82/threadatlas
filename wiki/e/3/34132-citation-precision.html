Citation Precision

metric · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: CP, citation precision

Abstract written by a model

In "SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection," citation precision measures whether a model-generated claim is fully supported by cited evidence; SELF-RAG has higher citation precision than the evaluated models on ASQA except ChatGPT, and surpasses ChatGPT on this metric. In "Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation," citation precision is one of five human-evaluation dimensions for long-form responses; MedGraphRAG receives higher citation-precision ratings than Inline Search, ATTR-FIRST, and MIRAGE on both MultiMedQA and DiverseHealth. In "CLERC: A Dataset for U. S. Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Analysis Generation," Citation Precision measures relevant generated citations; it starts at 0%, improves with cited-case texts, but remains low for legal applications.
