Abstract written by a model

Sir Gavial Mantrap is judged a commercial swindler who devised companies such as the Eocene Mines, exploiting small investors and cheating widows, spinsters, and fathers of savings. After disgrace, he lives obscurely on his wife’s settlement, well fed and self-justifying, while Melissa calls him moral for family life and charity at Tiptop.

XVI.

Sir Gavial Mantrap devised companies, including the Eocene Mines, that punished the ignorance of people of small means and cheated widows, spinsters, and hardworking fathers of their savings. After his disgrace he lived in comparative obscurity on his wife's settlement, while remaining well fed and publicly self-justifying; Melissa nevertheless calls him moral because of his family life and charity.
