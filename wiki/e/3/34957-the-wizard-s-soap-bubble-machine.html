24. The Birthday Celebration

The Wizard's soap-bubble machine is hidden beneath the platform, with a tank of soapsuds and air-pumps beneath the flooring. It produces huge, durable bubbles by using glue in the soapsuds so the bubbles can float for hours without breaking.
