vihuela in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

A Spanish viol of the twelfth century, represented on the Portico della gloria in several forms: smaller three-string examples resemble rebec, while larger ones have broader bodies and different sound-holes; no bows appear.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

The Spanish viol of the twelfth century is represented in several forms on the Portico della gloria. Some resemble the rebec and have three strings, while larger examples have broader bodies, incurved sides, and different sound-holes; no bows are shown.
