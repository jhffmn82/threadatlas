Macaria’s sacrifice in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

is_a voluntary offering [made by Macaria] Macaria’s sacrifice is required by the oracles as the means of saving the city and the suppliants. She freely accepts this role, refuses to be chosen by lot, requests female attendants and proper rites, and departs to die for her brothers and Athens.

Macaria's sacrifice in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

Macaria's sacrifice is a proposed voluntary death to fulfill an oracle and save the children of Hercules, her brothers, the city, and the suppliant cause; she requests honorable rites and burial, but the text ends before it occurs.

CHOR. This land is ever willing to aid in a just cause those in difficulty;

Macaria's sacrifice is proposed as a voluntary death for her brothers, the city, and the suppliant cause. She asks that the rites be performed honorably and that her body be buried with fitting honor, but the text ends before the sacrifice occurs.
