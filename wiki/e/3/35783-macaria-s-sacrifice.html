Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Macaria's sacrifice is a proposed voluntary death to fulfill an oracle and save Hercules' children, the city, and the suppliant cause. In Hecuba and other plays, Macaria’s sacrifice is a voluntary offering required by the oracles to save the city and the suppliants, which she freely accepts before departing to die for her brothers and Athens.

is_a voluntary offering [made by Macaria] Macaria’s sacrifice is required by the oracles as the means of saving the city and the suppliants. She freely accepts this role, refuses to be chosen by lot, requests female attendants and proper rites, and departs to die for her brothers and Athens.
