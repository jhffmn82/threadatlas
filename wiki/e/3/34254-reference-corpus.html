Abstract written by a model

In "RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation," the reference corpus supplies retrievable passages and knowledge to large language models through RAG, supports up-to-date systems, reduces hallucinations, affects RAG performance, and may not predict downstream performance when evaluated with perplexity. In "Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation," the reference textual database supplies knowledge to large language models through RAG systems and serves as the textual source supporting the systems' natural language interaction with users.
