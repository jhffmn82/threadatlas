MedCPT in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where MedCPT first appears

Abstract written by a model

MedCPT is a 109M-parameter biomedical semantic, domain-specific retriever in MEDRAG that uses inner product. Trained on PubMed user logs, it performs especially well with PubMed and has superior, most reliable individual performance on MIRAGE, though not consistently outperforming BM25. Its results can improve further when combined with other retrievers, including BM25 in RRF-2.

MedCPT in Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Jiwoong Sohn · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where MedCPT first appears

Abstract written by a model

MedCPT is an off-the-shelf biomedical retriever and reranker tailored for the biomedical domain. It was developed to address limited medical query-article annotations using 255 million PubMed user click logs and is pretrained on PubMed search logs. As a reranker after balanced retrieval, it encodes a query with each document, scores relevance, and prioritizes closer matches. It may prefer PubMed documents over clinical guidelines and textbooks because of its training. In comparisons, it uses only the top-1 retrieved snippet and does not consistently improve average accuracy.
