Abstract written by a model

In "BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LlamaIndex is described as a RAG library and data framework for LLM applications, providing generic off-the-shelf modules for production-ready, high-level RAG development but offering limited retrievers, datasets, and metrics and insufficient flexibility for research environments. In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LlamaIndex is described as software that generates pseudo-queries to improve retrieval-document matching and optimizes chunking through Small2Big and sliding windows.
