the Rose Chamber in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The Rose Chamber is a room in Zog's castle, marked by pearl-sewn roses on seaweed hangings and containing a mermaid statue. Zog imprisons Aquareine, Clia, and Trot there; after returning from the goldsmiths' shop, they hear Sacho's warning, sleep there, and Aquareine charms the golden sword.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

The Rose Chamber is the room where Zog orders Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, and Trot to live until they die. Its entrance is marked by roses sewn in pearls on the seaweed hangings.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

The group returns to the Rose Chamber after visiting the goldsmiths' shop. Sacho warns them there about Zog's intentions, and they later sleep there while Aquareine charms the golden sword.
