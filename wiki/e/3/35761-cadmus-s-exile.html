Cadmus's exile

event · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Cadmus's and Harmonia's departure from Thebes, Cadmus's exile and wandering, a fugitive from my chamber, a long series of wandering, a miserable banishment, cast out from my country, exiled, go then, my child, quitted Thebes, Θήβας ἐκλιπὼν

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Bacchus decrees that Cadmus must leave his house and country as an aged exile, though he is foretold to lead an army among barbarians. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Cadmus is forced to leave Thebes and wanders with his exiled wife until they reach the coast of Illyria. In The Library, Cadmus and Harmonia leave Thebes for the Encheleans and lead the Encheleans against the Illyrians.
