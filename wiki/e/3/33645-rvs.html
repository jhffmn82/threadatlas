RVS

method · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: RAG-assisted Representative Vector Summarization, RAG-assisted Representative Vector Summarization (RVS), Representative Vector Summarization, representative vector summarization

Abstract written by a model

RVS is a combined extractive and abstractive summarization method for large unstructured textual data. It selects a predefined number of representative text chunks from a non-parametric knowledgebase, with the number determined by the maximum token limit, and uses representative vectors to generate a final summary. It also identifies chunk keywords and creates word-cloud and scatter-plot visualizations of overall content. Implemented in docGPT, RVS was tested against retrieve-stuff-summarize methods on Kumar and Clark Clinical Medicine 10th Edition and British National Formulary 82, including medical education use cases.

RVS is in one work on this site, Retrieval Augmented Generation and Representative Vector Summarization for Large Unstructured Textual Data in Medical Education. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
