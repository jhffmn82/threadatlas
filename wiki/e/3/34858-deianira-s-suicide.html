Deianira's suicide

event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: die, hanged herself, her own hand slaughtered her, herself she slew, mori, my death, new-given wound, suicide of Deianira, the fatal blow, the sudden end, this violence, ἀνήρτησεν

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Deianira's suicide occurs inside the household after she realizes that her gift has fatally harmed Heracles; she uses a blade against herself, and Hyllus recognizes too late that she acted from misguided love rather than deliberate malice. In Heroides and Amores, Deianira's suicide is anticipated as she repeatedly asks why she delays dying and declares that her death will be the pledge of her marriage to Hercules; the unit ends with her farewell without stating that she has yet killed herself. In The Library, Deianira's suicide is by hanging after she learns what has happened to Hercules when he wears the treated tunic.
