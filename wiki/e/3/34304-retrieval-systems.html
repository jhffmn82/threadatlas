Abstract written by a model

Retrieval systems are ranking systems used to select passages and provide context for evaluating LLMs. Experiments compare BM25, RetroMAE, and SPLADE-v3 by retrieval effectiveness and downstream generation: higher-quality retrieval generally improves RAG, especially on NQ, but does not outperform Oracle on WoW and performs poorly versus Closed Book on ELI5 and WoW.

Experiments .. 5.6

Retrievers are evaluated by their effect on downstream generation and, where available, retrieval effectiveness. The experiments compare BM25, RetroMAE, and SPLADE-v3, with higher-quality retrieval generally improving RAG performance.

A

The retrieval systems provide context for the evaluation of different LLMs.

B

Retrieval systems are compared using retrieval performance and generation quality. They perform poorly relative to the Closed Book setting on ELI5 and WoW, whereas stronger retrieval systems provide increasing benefits on NQ.
