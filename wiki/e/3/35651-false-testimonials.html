False testimonials are misleading self-descriptions believed by their originators, arising when people’s verbal accounts follow old principles despite contradictory habitual conduct.

XIII.

False testimonials arise when human beings believe their verbal accounts of their habitual conduct despite facts that contradict them. They may continue to describe themselves according to an old rule or principle long after their actual practice has departed from it.
