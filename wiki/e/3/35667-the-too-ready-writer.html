George Eliot · 1879

The too ready writer is a writer who exorbitantly occupies literary space through restless, ill-informed production, monopolizing attention, obstructing better work, and seeming authoritative through style rather than truth.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV.

The too ready writer monopolizes literary space as the excessive talker monopolizes conversation, presenting opinions on innumerable subjects whether or not he has adequate knowledge. His restless production can obstruct better work, spoil other people's knowledge in handling it, and give an impression of authority through style rather than truth.
