Abstract written by a model

Hatherley’s thumb is severed at the root when Stark’s cleaver strikes as he hangs from a bedroom windowsill. The attack causes heavy bleeding, dizziness, sickness, and a blood-soaked sleeve; he later awakens near the road. Watson cleans and dresses the wound with carbolised bandages. The severed thumb is later found on a second-floor window-sill at Dr. Becher’s house.

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Hatherley’s thumb has been hacked or torn out from the root by an instrument like a cleaver. Watson cleans and dresses the wound, which has bled heavily, and Hatherley travels to Holmes with four fingers remaining and a red, spongy surface where the thumb was.

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

As the narrator hangs from the bedroom windowsill, Stark’s cleaver cuts off his thumb. The blood loss causes dizziness and sickness, and the narrator later awakens near the road with his coat sleeve drenched in blood.
