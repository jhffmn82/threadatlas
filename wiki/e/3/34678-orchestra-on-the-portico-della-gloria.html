orchestra on the Portico della gloria in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The orchestra on the Portico della gloria is a group of twenty-four life-sized seated elders in high relief, dated 1188, each holding instruments including organistrum, harps, salterios, and vihuelas, shown tuning or preparing rather than playing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

It consists of twenty-four seated, life-sized figures in high relief, each holding an instrument. The group includes an organistrum, small and triangular harps, salterios, and several forms of vihuela; the performers are tuning or preparing rather than actively playing.
