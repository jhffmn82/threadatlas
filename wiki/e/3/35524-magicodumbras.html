Magicodumbras in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

III.

The Magicodumbras are symbolic monuments or related historical subjects whose connection across widely scattered races becomes central to Merman's theory. Merman claims that Grampus is wrong about them, while public discussion treats lax views of them as a danger to sound inquiry.

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

The Magicodumbras are a central subject of Merman's disputed theory and of the controversy with Grampus. They become widely repeated as part of Merman's supposed absurdity, are treated by Grampus's followers as subsidiary to Grampus's system, and contribute nothing to Merman's financial support.
