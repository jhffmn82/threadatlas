prediction-powered inference

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: PPI, prediction-powered inference (PPI)

Abstract written by a model

Prediction-powered inference is used by ARES to improve model-based evaluation accuracy and provide confidence intervals for RAG scores. It begins with a small human-annotated preference validation set, combines it with LLM-judge predictions on a much larger unlabeled set, and uses a rectifier function to estimate and mitigate judge errors. It produces tighter confidence intervals for RAG-system performance, improves ARES ranking accuracy, and mitigates accuracy losses under cross-domain shifts; its confidence bounds capture ground-truth average outcomes more than 95% of the time in the existing evaluation.

Abstract

Prediction-powered inference is used by ARES with a small set of human-annotated datapoints to mitigate potential prediction errors from its judges.

opening

Prediction-powered inference is used by ARES to improve model-based evaluation accuracy and provide statistical confidence intervals for RAG scores. It relies on a small set of human-annotated datapoints.

ARES .. 3.3

Prediction-powered inference combines human-labeled validation data with predictions from LLM judges on a much larger unlabeled dataset. It uses a rectifier function to estimate judge errors and produce tighter confidence intervals for RAG system performance.

Results & Analysis / 5.1 .. 5.4

PPI is used with ARES and the GPT-3.5 judge to rectify model predictions and produce confidence intervals. It improves ARES ranking accuracy, mitigates accuracy losses under cross-domain shifts, and provides confidence bounds that capture ground-truth average outcomes more than 95% of the time in the existing RAG-system evaluation.
