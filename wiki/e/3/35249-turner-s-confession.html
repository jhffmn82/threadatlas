Turner's confession states that he was formerly the highway robber Black Jack of Ballarat and that he killed McCarthy, who had blackmailed him for years and threatened his daughter Alice. Turner signs the statement, and Sherlock Holmes agrees to keep it secret unless it becomes necessary to save James McCarthy.
