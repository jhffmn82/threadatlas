Lernaean Hydra's poison

substance · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Hydra's poison, Lernæan poison, bane, burning poison, dark tinct of gall, gory venom, subtle poison, the hateful life, the poison, the poison of the hydra, the venomed shaft, venom of the Lernæan poison, venomed tooth

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Lernaean Hydra's poison from the shaft used by Nessus supplies poison to a robe, spreads blackly over Heracles, burns and consumes his body, and causes pain, convulsions, cries, and poisoning. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the Lernæan poison coats Hercules's arrow and mingles with Nessus's blood; when warmed in the tunic, it burns through his limbs and entrails, causing his death. In The Library, Hydra's poison is present in the material from Nessus's wound; when the treated tunic is warmed, it corrodes Hercules's skin and brings about his death.

Abstract written by a model

A deadly venom from the shaft used by Nessus supplies poison to a robe; it spreads blackly over Heracles, burns and consumes his body, and causes pain, convulsions, cries, and poisoning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

The poison comes from the gory venom of Nessus' wound and spreads blackly from the robe's effect. It burns or consumes Heracles' body, producing severe pain, convulsions, and agonized cries.

CHORUS.

The poison associated with the Lernaean Hydra is described as the venom on the shaft that supplied the deadly substance used by Nessus. It contributes to the robe's destructive power against Heracles.
