Admetus's bargain with Death in Alcestis

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ALCESTIS

Admetus's life was spared through a bargain in which another living soul had to die in his place. Alcestis becomes the person who fulfills that condition after Admetus's father and mother refuse to do so.
