honor and dishonor

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: a traitor's love, ancestral glory, disgrace, disgraceful, honor and disgrace, honour, my father's greatness, noble birth, nobleness, shame, the common honour of the dead
  • In The Electra of Euripides, it is a social and moral conflict shaping the treatment of Agamemnon's family, as Electra experiences dishonor while the Peasant's restraint and hospitality and true nobility in a poor man are presented as honorable.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, honor and disgrace guide the decisions of Iolaus and Demophoon, who refuse to abandon the children and reject violating the altar as disgraceful to Demophoon and the city.

is_a social and moral conflict [shaping the treatment of Agamemnon's family] Electra experiences dishonor through poverty, isolation, neglected mourning, and the treatment of Agamemnon's grave.
