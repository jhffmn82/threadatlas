note (from Dorothy Gale) in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

A note from Dorothy Gale, written on a broad white stork feather, says Oz is forever cut off and that Dorothy and Toto will always love its writer and loving children.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

30. How the Story of Oz Came to an End

The note tells the writer that he will never hear anything more about Oz because the country is cut off forever from the rest of the world. It also says that Dorothy Gale and Toto will always love him and the children who love them.
