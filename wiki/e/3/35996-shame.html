shame

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Shame, base things, disgrace, evil, sin

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, shame is associated with Phædra’s disordered speech and love for Hippolytus; she fears discovery would disgrace Theseus, her children, and herself, and prefers death to public knowledge of her wrongdoing. In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, shame is associated with Phaedra as both the deed and the suffering of her forbidden love, which she fears will be exposed and dishonour her husband, children, and household.
