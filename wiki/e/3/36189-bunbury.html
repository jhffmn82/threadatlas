17. How They Came to Bunbury

Bunbury is a town of houses made from crackers, with bread-crust walks and streets. Its inhabitants are made of buns, bread, and related baked foods, and its resources include butter mined from the flour-and-meal ground, doughleanders, doughderas, and dough-nuts.
