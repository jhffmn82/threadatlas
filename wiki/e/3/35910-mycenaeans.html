Mycenaeans

group · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Mycensans, Mycenæan people, Mycenæans, Myceusans, army of the Mycenæans, the Mycenaeans, the Mycenæan spear, the Mycenæans, Μυκηναίοις, Μυκηναίων

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the Mycenaeans are associated with Eurystheus and his authority over the exiled Heraclids, and Copreus claims that their opinion may oppose Athens's protection of the suppliants. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Mycenaeans manage the Nemean Games, refuse to submit to Argive government, support the Lacedaemonians at Thermopylae, and surrender to the Argives after famine forces them to do so. In The Library, the Mycenaeans receive an oracle, then send for Atreus and Thyestes to choose a Pelopid king for Mycenae.
