[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

The quarrels between the seals and turtles are frequent but usually temporary. In this incident, Chief Muffruff flips a turtle onto its back, the young seals push it into the water, and the turtles retaliate by driving the seals away and biting Muffruff. Queen Aquareine says the animals will probably be sleeping together again within an hour.
