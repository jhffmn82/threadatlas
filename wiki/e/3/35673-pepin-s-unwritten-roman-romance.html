1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV.

Pepin's unwritten Roman romance was planned as a grand, panoramic, philosophical depiction of the Roman Empire at the height of its visible grandeur and approaching spiritual decadence. It would treat statesmen, philosophers, poets, warriors, religion, gladiatorial spectacles, and Christianity in elevated language, while avoiding ordinary low-life speech. Because Pepin never wrote it, he has become more sarcastic about romances that were actually produced.
