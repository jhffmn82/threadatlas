Abstract written by a model

concerns Alcestis's death [as enduring bereavement] Admetus sees his life without Alcestis as worse than death and imagines enduring loneliness, reproach, and the empty household. His grief briefly deepens when the veiled woman resembles Alcestis, but it changes to astonishment and joy when she is revealed.
