Abstract written by a model

The undiscovered island is a small, barren rocky islet in an out-of-the-way ocean location, unnamed because humans have neither discovered nor inhabited it. It has no vegetation, high central cliffs where sea-gulls nest, seals in large groups, and turtles along the water’s edge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

The island has no name because it has never been discovered or inhabited by humans. It is a barren, rocky islet with no trees, bushes, or grass, and it is inhabited by seals and turtles, with sea-gulls nesting on its central cliffs.
