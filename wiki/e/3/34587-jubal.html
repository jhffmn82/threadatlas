Jubal in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Jubal is mentioned in Genesis as the father of those who handle harp and organ, and may be regarded as the first professional cultivator of instrumental music rather than inventor of every instrument.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Jubal is mentioned in Genesis as the father of those who handle the harp and organ, meaning performers on stringed and wind instruments. The text suggests that he may have been regarded as the first professional cultivator of instrumental music rather than the inventor of every instrument.
