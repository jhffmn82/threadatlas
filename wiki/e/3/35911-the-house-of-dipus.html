Abstract written by a model

The house of Œdipus is a family afflicted by curses, discord, exile, and earlier suffering. Its troubles are attributed to the sword, discord, Œdipus, or fate, and it ends ruined by Jocasta’s death and the murderous conflict and bloodshed between Eteocles and Polynices.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

POLYNICES, CHORUS.

The house of Œdipus is portrayed as suffering from curses, discord, exile, and the destruction caused by the conflict between the brothers. Jocasta attributes its troubles to the sword, discord, Œdipus, or fate.

ANTIGONE, CREON, CHORUS.

The house of Oedipus is destroyed by the bloodshed of Eteocles and Polynices, the death of Jocasta, and the earlier sufferings of Oedipus. Antigone mourns its three kindred dead and calls its ruin the result of dreadful and mournful blood.
