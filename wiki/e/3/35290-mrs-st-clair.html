Abstract written by a model

Mrs. St. Clair, daughter of a local brewer and wife of Neville St. Clair, lives at The Cedars villa and feels keen sympathy with her husband. In May 1884, while collecting a valuable parcel in the City, she saw Neville at a Swandam Lane window, then entered the opium den after he vanished. Forced out, she returned with police, fainted at blood on the window, and was escorted home. She insists Neville is alive, recognizes his handwriting, and says she would have sensed his death.

Mrs. St. Clair is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Mrs. St. Clair went into the City to collect a valuable parcel and passed through Swandam Lane at about 4:35. She saw Neville St. Clair at a second-floor window, rushed into the opium den when he disappeared, was forced back by the Lascar and a Dane, and later brought an inspector and constables to the premises.

“Mrs. St. Clair had fainted at the sight of the blood upon the window,

Mrs. St. Clair is escorted home by the police after fainting at the sight of blood upon the window. She tells Sherlock Holmes that she believes Neville is alive and identifies the handwriting in the letter as one of his forms of writing. She answers Holmes's questions about seeing Neville at the window in Swandam Lane and maintains that she would have sensed his death.
