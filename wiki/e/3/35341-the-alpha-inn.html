Also called: Alpha Inn, private bar, the Alpha

Abstract written by a model

The Alpha Inn is a small public-house near the Museum in Bloomsbury. Frequented by Henry Baker and others, it hosts a goose club instituted by its landlord, Windigate. Windigate buys and receives two dozen geese from Mr. Breckinridge, whom he identifies as the supplier.
