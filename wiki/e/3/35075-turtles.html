turtles in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Turtles, some wagon-wheel-sized and possibly hundreds of years old, have thick protective shells, are eaten by Queen Aquareine, battle seals, then rush them, bite Chief Muffruff, and take the island.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Many turtles rest at the edge of the water, including some as large as wagon wheels and possibly hundreds of years old. One turtle objects to Muffruff's treatment of it, and the turtles later rush the seals, bite Chief Muffruff, and take undisputed possession of the island.
