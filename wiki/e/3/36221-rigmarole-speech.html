Abstract written by a model

Rigmarole speech is lengthy, complicated talk using many words while saying little; compared to some college lecturers and ministers, it confuses travelers and prompts Dorothy to speak plainly.

Rigmarole speech is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
