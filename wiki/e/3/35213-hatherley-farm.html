Abstract written by a model

Hatherley Farm is a comfortable-looking, widespread two-storied farm held by John Turner and rented by Charles McCarthy, where McCarthy senior and junior lived or stayed. Beside Boscombe Pool, it frames events of June 3rd: Charles left toward the pool, James returned before arrest, and Holmes examined both men’s boots there.

IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

Hatherley Farm is a farm held by John Turner and rented by Charles McCarthy. Charles left it on June 3rd to walk toward Boscombe Pool, and James later returned there before being informed that he was under arrest.

There was no rain, as Holmes had foretold, and the morning broke bright

Hatherley Farm is where McCarthy senior and McCarthy junior lived or stayed, and it borders Boscombe Pool. Holmes begins his examination there by measuring the boots belonging to the father and son.
