temple of the unknown god in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

A large temple erected by Nezahualcoyotl and dedicated to the unknown god, “the cause of causes,” rises with a nine-story tower whose top-mounted musical instruments summon worshippers to prayer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] The Tezcucans (or Acolhuans) belonged to the same race as the Azt

The temple was erected by Nezahualcoyotl and dedicated to “The unknown god, the cause of causes.” It had a nine-story tower topped with various musical instruments used to summon worshippers to prayer.
