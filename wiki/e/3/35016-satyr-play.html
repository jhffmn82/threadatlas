Satyr-play is a play performed after three tragedies, using traditional saga plots, heroic characters, tragic diction, and a Chorus of Satyrs to create controlled burlesque; it joins revelry, joy, nature, Dionysus, earth’s renewal, and resurrection.

INTRODUCTION

The Satyr-play followed three tragedies and used a traditional saga plot, heroic characters, tragic diction, and a Chorus of Satyrs to create a controlled burlesque clash. Its subjects commonly included heroic figures congenial to revelry, and its tradition connects joy, nature, Dionysus, the renewal of the earth, and the resurrection of the dead.
