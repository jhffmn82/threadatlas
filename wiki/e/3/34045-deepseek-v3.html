DeepSeek-V3 in How to Build an Adaptive AI Tutor for Any Course Using Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (KG-RAG)

Chenxi Dong · 2023-11-29 · the work’s page · read it where DeepSeek-V3 first appears

Abstract written by a model

DeepSeek-V3 is an open-source, globally accessible Mixture-of-Experts LLM from Alibaba Cloud. It is selected as the primary reasoning and generation engine for a KG-RAG AI tutor because of strong reasoning, cost efficiency, and freedom from regional restrictions. It achieves 90.2% accuracy on the MATH benchmark and outperforms GPT-4 and Claude 3 Opus there. In the system, it first extracts entities and relationships from course-material chunks to construct knowledge graphs, then generates final answers from knowledge-guided retrieved context. Through the Alibaba Cloud API, it costs 2.18e-4 USD per question-and-answer exchange, about 7% of GPT-4’s expenses, supporting affordable personalized tutoring, reducing reliance on US-based models, and promoting more inclusive AI in education.
