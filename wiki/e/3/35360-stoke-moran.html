Stoke Moran is the Roylotts’ old ancestral home and estate on Surrey’s western border, containing Stoke Moran mansion. Dr. Grimesby Roylott lives there with Helen and Julia; after Julia dies there and Helen faces danger, Holmes and Watson travel to investigate.

Stoke Moran is the old ancestral home and estate of the Roylotts, located on the western border of Surrey. Helen and Julia live there with Dr. Grimesby Roylott, and Holmes and Watson plan to visit it.
