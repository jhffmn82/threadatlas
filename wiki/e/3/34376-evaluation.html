Also called: Comprehensive Evaluation, evaluating these abilities
  • In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Comprehensive Evaluation evaluates RAG systems across Commonsense Reasoning, Fact Checking, Open-Domain QA, MultiHop QA, and Medical QA, subsampling up to 500 examples from each dataset; it measures accuracy for Commonsense Reasoning, Fact Checking, and Medical QA, then token-level F1 and Exact Match for Open-Domain and MultiHop QA.
  • In "Fact, Fetch, and Reason: A Unified Evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation," evaluation is described as crucial as LLM-based systems see increasing real-world deployment; FRAMES is proposed to address gaps by evaluating factuality, retrieval, and reasoning together in end-to-end RAG scenarios.

Comprehensive Evaluation .. Results and Analysis

The Comprehensive Evaluation measured RAG capabilities on Commonsense Reasoning, Fact Checking, Open-Domain QA, MultiHop QA, and Medical QA. It used accuracy for Commonsense Reasoning, Fact Checking, and Medical QA, and token-level F1 and Exact Match for the two QA tasks.
