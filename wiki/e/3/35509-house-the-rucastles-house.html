Abstract written by a model

The Rucastle house is a large, damp-stained, whitewashed building near the Southampton high road, with grounds, woods, copper beeches, a drawing-room, an empty wing, and a concealed suite with a barricaded room. It frightens a young lady, who finds its inhabitants horrible, yet returns after sending a wire to resume looking after the child.
