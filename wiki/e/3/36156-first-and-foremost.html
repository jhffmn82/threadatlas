First and Foremost in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The First and Foremost is a powerful Phanfasm and the foremost leader of the Phanfasms, with sorcery that the Magic Belt cannot increase. It receives General Guph's proposal, displays its power by changing from a bear-headed hairy man to a beautiful woman and then a huge butterfly, and agrees to assist the conquest of Oz for the pleasure of destroying happiness. It promises the Phanfasms' aid without reward, leads them through the tunnel in battle array toward the Emerald City, and seeks Oz's treasures while intending to destroy Oz, kill or enslave its people, and later enslave the Nomes, Growleywogs, and Whimsies. In the dusty tunnel, it begins coughing and sneezing.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

The First and Foremost Phanfasm receives General Guph and listens to his proposal. It demonstrates the Phanfasms' power by transforming from a bear-headed hairy man into a beautiful woman and then into a huge butterfly, and it agrees to aid the conquest of Oz because of the opportunity to destroy happiness.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The First and Foremost Phanfasm promises to bring his people to assist the Nomes in destroying the Oz people. He demands no reward, since the pleasure of destroying the good and happy Oz people is enough for him.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The First and Foremost leads the Phanfasms through the tunnel toward the Emerald City. He intends to seize all the treasures of Oz, kill or enslave its people, destroy the country, and later conquer the Nomes, Growleywogs, and Whimsies. He begins coughing and sneezing as the tunnel fills with dust.
