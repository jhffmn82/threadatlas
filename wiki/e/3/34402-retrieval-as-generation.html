retrieval as generation in Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xiaohua Wang, Zhenghua Wang, Xuan Gao, Feiran Zhang, Yixin Wu, Zhibo Xu, Tianyuan Shi, Zhengyuan Wang, Shizheng Li, Qi Qian, Ruicheng Yin, Changze Lv, Xiaoqing Zheng, Xuanjing Huang · 2024

Retrieval as generation is a strategy for generating multimodal content faster. It retrieves verified multimodal materials, especially images whose stored textual descriptions align with a user query, rather than generating new images. This provides groundedness, supports visual question-answering, and greatly reduces time consumption while maintaining image quality. Its efficiency and maintainability improve as retrieval sources and search sources are expanded and improved.
