Chief of the Whimsies

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Chief, The Chief, Whimsie Chief, the Chief Whimsie, the Chief of the Whimsies

Abstract written by a model

The Chief of the Whimsies is their little-wiser-than-anyone leader, chosen because no Whimsie was better suited to rule, and lives in the Chief's house marked by a picture of his false head. After Guph's offer, he consults the Whimsies, who agree to help the Nomes. He leads false-headed forces into the tunnel, plotting to betray First and Foremost and Grand Gallipoot, seize Oz's slaves and treasure, and is left gasping for water amid dust.

Chief of the Whimsies is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

6. How Guph Visited the Whimsies

The Chief of the Whimsies is no wiser or more capable than the others and was chosen because no Whimsie was better suited to rule. After hearing Guph's offer, he consults his people, who agree to help the Nomes.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The Chief of the Whimsies leads his false-headed forces into the tunnel and plans to let the other armies conquer Oz before treacherously destroying them and taking Ozma's slaves and treasure. He asks for water when the dust makes him gasp and choke, but none of his followers carries any.
