VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

John Horner, a plumber, is accused of taking the blue carbuncle from the Countess of Morcar's jewel-case. He protests his innocence, struggles during his arrest, and is referred to the Assizes after evidence of a previous robbery conviction is presented.
