Aloysius Doran in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Aloysius Doran, a wealthy millionaire from California, lives at a furnished Lancaster Gate house with his only daughter, Hatty. When she disappears around her wedding to Lord St. Simon, he joins him in contacting the police.

X. THE ADVENTURE OF THE NOBLE BACHELOR

Aloysius Doran, a wealthy man from San Francisco, is the father of Miss Hatty Doran and has taken a furnished house at Lancaster Gate for the wedding party. After learning that his daughter has disappeared, he joins Lord St. Simon in contacting the police.
