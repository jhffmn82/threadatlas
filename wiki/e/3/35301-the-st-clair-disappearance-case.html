The St. Clair disappearance case concerns Neville St. Clair's unexplained vanishing after he is apparently seen at a Swandam Lane window, with blood and Hugh Boone raising key questions. Investigation initially leaves the case unsolved, but Holmes suspects Boone is St. Clair, removes his makeup, and St. Clair explains his double life.
