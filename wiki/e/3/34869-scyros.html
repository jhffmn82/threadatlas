Scyros

place · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Scyros' isle, rock-bound Scyros, sea-ringed Scyros, sea-washed Scyros, the Isle of Scyros, Σκῦρον
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Scyros is Neoptolemus' native, sea-girt home, to which he intends to return and be content with his life at home.
  • In The Library, Scyros is ruled by Lycomedes, where Odysseus and Phoenix persuaded Lycomedes to let Neoptolemus go to the Greek camp.
  • In The Fall of Troy, Scyros is a sea-ringed overseas island and the palace of Achilles' son, Deidameia, and Lycomedes; Odysseus and Diomede arrive to recruit Neoptolemus, who leaves for war.
