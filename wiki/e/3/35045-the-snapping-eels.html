The snapping eels wriggle and dart around Trot and Cap'n Bill, snapping near their fingers, noses, and faces. Merla orders them to stop and warns that Queen Aquareine can stiffen them for hurting earth people. The eels claim they will not harm the visitors, then swim away to frighten Mummercubble.
