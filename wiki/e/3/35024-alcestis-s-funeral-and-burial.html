Alcestis's funeral and burial in Alcestis

Euripides · -0437

Alcestis’s funeral and burial is an honorable, publicly mourned rite ordered by Admetus throughout Thessaly. After her death, mourners carry her body into the house; later, a procession bears it on a bier toward a grave-mound near the Larissa road and a burial urn. Citizens farewell her, and Pheres provides burial gifts.

ALCESTIS

After Alcestis dies, Admetus orders that she receive an honorable funeral and that the whole realm mourn her. Mourners carry her body into the house, and Admetus follows it while preparing further burial honors.

Alcestis's body is carried from the house in a funeral procession toward the grave-mound and urn. Admetus asks the citizens to bid her farewell, and Pheres arrives with burial gifts before the procession moves on.
