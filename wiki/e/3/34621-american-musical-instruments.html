American musical instruments in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Ancient American musical instruments, genuine contrivances of American Indians before European influence, reveal their makers’ artistic development, social condition, and musical systems through surviving pottery, bone, cane, stone, wood, and other instruments, including those used to summon worshippers to prayer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Ancient American musical instruments provide evidence about the artistic development, social condition, and musical systems of the Indians who made them. The surviving examples include instruments of pottery, bone, cane, stone, wood, and other materials.
