blinding of Polymestor

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: blind, deprived of the sight of mine eyes, made thine eyes sightless, my blindness, pierce and gore the wretched pupils of my eyes, these quenched orbs, they tore out my bleeding eyes

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the blinding of Polymestor is the event in which the Trojan women pierce and gore his eyes, leaving him sightless and seeking Agamemnon’s help. In Hecuba and other plays, the blinding of Polymestor is the event in which Hecuba and the Trojan women blind him after killing his sons, leaving him unable to see and appealing unsuccessfully for help.
