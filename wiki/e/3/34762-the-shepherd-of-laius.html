Abstract written by a model

The shepherd of Laius, a slave bred in Laius’s house, is the sole surviving witness of Laius’s death and reports the supposed robbers. After seeking exile in rural pastures, he is summoned to testify. He admits Jocasta gave him an infant to destroy; pity led him to give it to the Corinthian Shepherd, confirming it was Laius’s stock.

CHORUS.

The shepherd of Laius was the only survivor to return from the scene of Laius's murder. He later requested permission to live far from Thebes and its memories, and Oedipus orders that he be brought back to testify.

_Enter_ JOCASTA.

The Theban Shepherd, formerly a servant and shepherd of Laius, admits that he received the infant from Jocasta with orders to destroy him. Out of pity, he gave the child to the Corinthian Shepherd instead, and he confirms that the child was of Laius's stock.
