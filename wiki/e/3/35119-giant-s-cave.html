Giant's Cave in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Giant's Cave is a vast, low-roofed sea cave beneath the coast, hundreds of miles from mermaid palaces, with a rocky entrance near the sea bottom. Filled with deep, sapphire- or azure-blue water, it glows while its waves and spray shine like jewels. The journey begins there: travelers descend through its temporarily darkening water, leave for brighter sea, and later Trot and Cap’n Bill return to their boat and exit into sunlight.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap 2._

Giant's Cave is an extensive, low-roofed cave beneath the coast, filled with deep water. Its interior glows sapphire blue, and its waves and spray shine like jewels.

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

The cave is the place where the journey begins. It temporarily blocks the light as the travelers descend through the water, and the group leaves it before swimming onward through brighter parts of the sea.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._

The Giant’s Cave is reached through a rocky entrance near the bottom of the sea, and its inner water is azure-blue. Trot and Cap’n Bill return to their boat inside the cave and leave through its mouth into the sunlight.
