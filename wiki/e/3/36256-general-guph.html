Abstract written by a model

In The Emerald City of Oz, General Guph is the Nome King’s chief general who organizes an alliance against Oz, leads the allied armies and fifty thousand Nomes, and later drinks the Water of Oblivion and becomes happy and childlike. In Tik-Tok of Oz, General Guph is the Nome King’s famous general who summons and leads the Nome army, commands attacks on the travelers, warns Tik-Tok, tries to seize Betsy, and later helps pursue Polychrome. In The Hungry Tiger of Oz, Guph is Kaliko’s crooked Chief Chamberlain who proposes war, secretly plots the travelers’ destruction, seeks the second Rash ruby, attacks Prince Evered, and leads the Gnome army until Evered knocks him flat.

General Guph in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910 · the work’s page · read it where General Guph first appears

Abstract written by a model

General Guph is an old, long-whiskered Nome and the clever, evil-minded Lord High General and chief general of the Nome King’s army, serving King Roquat the Red. He hates good, happy, contented, and prosperous people, wants to spread unhappiness, and seeks the conquest and destruction of Oz. He is wise in dealing with the Nome King but fears eggs because he believes they may poison him. He also aspires to rule the Nomes after Oz is destroyed. He volunteers to be General and devises an alliance of evil magical powers, arguing that the Nomes cannot conquer Oz alone. Ordered to approach the Whimsies, he gains their support by promising reward. He then endures mistreatment while persuading the Grand Gallipoot and the Growleywogs to help, and tricks a scarlet alligator while traveling to the Mountain of Phantastico. There he persuades the First and Foremost Phanfasm and the Phanfasms by appealing to their wish to destroy happiness, while promising spoils except the Nome King’s Magic Belt. After reporting that the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms are ready, Guph proposes sending the allies first through the tunnel so Oz’s eggs will be exhausted before the Nomes arrive. He follows the allied armies with fifty thousand Nomes, then advises using the Magic Belt to return the allies to their own countries. Thirsty from dust, he reaches the Forbidden Fountain, knocks over the Nome King, and drinks the Water of Oblivion. He becomes happy and childlike, and the Wizard tells him to follow the Nome King home. He commands the Nome army until he becomes dog-feed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

4. How The Nome King Planned Revenge

Guph, an old Nome, volunteers to become General because he hates good, happy, contented, and prosperous people. He tells King Roquat that Oz cannot be conquered without additional magic power and proposes forming an alliance with evil creatures. He is ordered to visit the Chief of the Whimsies and arrange assistance.

6. How Guph Visited the Whimsies

General Guph becomes the new General of the Nome King's army and accepts the position because he wants to spread unhappiness and conquer Oz. He visits the Whimsies, promises them a reward for helping the Nomes, and successfully gains them as allies.

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

Guph crosses the changing hills and valleys of the Ripple Land and reaches the Growleywogs' domain. He persuades the Grand Gallipoot to promise assistance in conquering the Land of Oz, despite being tossed into the air, imprisoned, stuck with pins, and having his whiskers pulled out. He leaves delighted with the promise and begins seeking still more allies.

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

General Guph travels to the Mountain of Phantastico and crosses a lava-filled gully by tricking a scarlet alligator. He tells the First and Foremost Phanfasm about the planned conquest of Oz and persuades the Phanfasms to join by appealing to their desire to destroy happiness. He returns to report that the Phanfasms will aid the Nomes when the tunnel is finished.

13. How the General Talked to the King

General Guph reports to Roquat the Red that the Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms will assist in the conquest of Oz. He proposes sending the allies through the tunnel before the Nomes so that the eggs in Oz will be used up before the Nomes arrive.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

General Guph follows the allied armies into the tunnel at the head of fifty thousand Nomes. He advises the Nome King to use the Magic Belt to send the allied forces back to their own countries. The dust fills his throat, and he says he would give half the gold in Oz for water.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

General Guph arrives thirsty and drinks heartily from the Water of Oblivion after knocking the Nome King over. He becomes happy and childlike, and the Wizard tells him to follow the Nome King home.

General Guph in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914 · the work’s page · read it where General Guph first appears

Abstract written by a model

General Guph is the Nome King’s famous general and leader of Ruggedo’s forces. Ordered to summon, arm, and lead the nome army to the Hollow Tube, he commands an attack on the travelers, angrily orders them captured, and has spears thrown. He warns Tik-Tok, tries to seize Betsy, but Hank kicks him into the Nome King. Later he fetches the Long-Eared Hearer and unsuccessfully helps pursue Polychrome.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

General Guph is ordered by Ruggedo to summon and properly arm the army of nomes. He is to lead them in marching to the mouth of the Hollow Tube.

Chapter Fifteen

General Guph leads hundreds of nomes gathered at the Tube's entrance. He becomes angry when Quox and the travelers escape above them, orders the group to come down and be captured, and commands the nomes to throw their spears.

Chapter Sixteen

General Guph warns Tik-Tok that resistance is useless and then attempts to seize Betsy. Hank kicks him into the Nome King, leaving both of them dazed and crumpled on the floor.

Chapter Seventeen

Guph fetches the Long-Eared Hearer for Ruggedo and helps try to seize Polychrome. He cannot catch her because she repeatedly moves away from his grasp.

Guph in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926 · the work’s page · read it where Guph first appears

Abstract written by a model

Guph is Kaliko's crooked Chief Chamberlain, a wicked gnome who proposes war after seeing the travelers and seeks excitement and another ruby. He guides the travelers to Kaliko's castle, secretly plans their destruction, urges theft of the second Rash ruby, attacks Prince Evered, and leads the Gnome army until Evered knocks him flat.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 10

Guph is Kaliko's Chief Chamberlain and initially proposes that the gnomes start a war. After viewing the travelers through the exspectacles, he becomes eager for excitement, but he later guides them from the Lost Labyrinth to Kaliko's castle. Carter overhears Guph bitterly discussing how to obtain another ruby.

CHAPTER 11

Guph urges Kaliko to steal the second Rash ruby and destroy the travelers. He attacks Prince Evered with a pick-axe and later leads the Gnome army against the travelers, but their blows do no harm and Prince Evered knocks him flat with a sword.
