Old Man in The Electra of Euripides

Euripides · -0419/-0409

Old Man, Agamemnon's trusted former servant and Orestes' childhood rescuer and guardian, brings Electra gifts, recognizes Orestes by his brow scar, and confirms him as Agamemnon's son. Knowing Aegisthus' whereabouts, he counsels Orestes to find him and agrees to deceive Clytemnestra about Electra's son.

1 section; each heading opens the text

[ORESTES _and_ PYLADES _go in, following the_ SERVANTS.

The Old Man brings Electra a lamb, green crowns, curds, and stored wine. He recognizes Orestes by the scar he received while chasing a fawn with Electra and confirms that he is Agamemnon's son. He gives Orestes counsel about finding Aegisthus and agrees to tell Clytemnestra that Electra has borne a son.

Old Man in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

The Old Man is a former retainer and faithful servant of Agamemnon, loyal even after Agamemnon’s death. After Agamemnon’s murder, he secretly rescues Orestes, conveys him to Phocis, and raises him there. Sent from Phanoteus, he enters the palace disguised by age and long absence and reports the false news of Orestes’ death to Clytemnestra and Electra, describing an accident and cremation. When Orestes returns, the Old Man reveals his role in preserving him and urgently rebukes Orestes and Electra for hesitation.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

ELECTRA

The Old Man, formerly faithful to Agamemnon, rescued Orestes after Agamemnon's murder and raised him safely in Phocis. He is instructed to enter the palace, use his age and long absence as a disguise, and announce the false news of Orestes' death.

_Enter_ CLYTEMNESTRA.

The Old Man arrives from Phanoteus the Phocian and reports that Orestes has died. He gives a detailed account of Orestes' victories, the chariot accident, the burning of his body, and the preparation of his ashes for burial. Clytemnestra accepts his report and brings him into the palace.

_Enter_ ORESTES _and_ PYLADES, _with the urn_.

The Old Man rebukes Orestes and Electra for delaying and warns them that their opportunity will be lost if they hesitate. He is revealed as the person who once conveyed Orestes secretly to Phocis and helped preserve him.
