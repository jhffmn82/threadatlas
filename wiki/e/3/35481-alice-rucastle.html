Abstract written by a model

Alice Rucastle, Mr. Rucastle’s daughter, lives in Philadelphia and has inheritance rights. Unhappy under her father’s control, she refuses to surrender her money, suffers a six-week brain-fever, and has her hair cut off. Imprisoned and impersonated by Violet Hunter, she escapes through a skylight with her fiancé, Mr. Fowler, and marries him in Southampton.

Alice Rucastle was unhappy under her father’s control and had rights to property or money under a will. After she refused to sign away control of her money, Mr. Rucastle’s pressure caused her to suffer brain-fever; afterward he imprisoned her and used Violet Hunter to impersonate her. Alice escapes through the skylight with Mr. Fowler and marries him in Southampton.
