INTRODUCTION

A pro-satyric drama is a stage in the refinement of the complete Satyr-play, retaining a normal chorus while including one Satyric or revelling figure. Alcestis is identified as a clear example because it has tragic diction, a traditional saga plot, and Heracles as its central revelling figure.
