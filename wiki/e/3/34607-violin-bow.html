violin bow in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The violin bow is claimed by Hindus to have been invented in Hindustan, possibly for the ravanastron, with Sanskrit names dating 1500–2000 years ago; some historians instead claim England or Wales.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE HINDUS.

The Hindus claim to have invented the violin bow and associate its early use with the ravanastron. Sanskrit works dating from approximately 1500 to 2000 years ago are said to contain names for the bow, supporting the possibility that it originated in Hindustan.
