Discussion / Best Practices for Implementing RAG .. Multimodal Extension

The multimodal extension broadens RAG to multimodal applications by incorporating text2image and image2text retrieval with a collection of paired images and textual descriptions. The unit reports advantages in groundedness, efficiency, and maintainability.
