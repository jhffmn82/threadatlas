CHAPTER V.

Confucius, who lived about B.C. 500, is said to have become so enraptured on hearing Chinese music that he could not take food for three months. An expression attributed to him records his wish to be a “wooden-tongued bell of Heaven,” proclaiming divine purposes to the multitude.
