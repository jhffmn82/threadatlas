Confucius in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Confucius lived about 500 B.C.; hearing Chinese music so enraptured him that he could not eat for three months, and he wished to proclaim divine purposes to the multitude.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Confucius, who lived about B.C. 500, is said to have become so enraptured on hearing Chinese music that he could not take food for three months. An expression attributed to him records his wish to be a “wooden-tongued bell of Heaven,” proclaiming divine purposes to the multitude.
