the ritual offerings

event · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: atonement, libation, offering, prayer, rite, rites, ritual, sacrifice, service, the offering to the holy powers

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, the offering to the holy powers requires spring water, wool-wreathed bowls, three dawn libations, water and honey in the last vessel, olive twigs, and a silent prayer, and Ismene enters to perform it. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the ritual offerings require spring water drawn with undefiled hands, three wool-crowned bowls, honey, olive sprays, water and honey without wine in the final bowl, three libations toward dawn, thrice nine olive sprays, and a whispered prayer to the Gracious deities.

Abstract written by a model

An offering to the Gentle Powers requires spring water, wool-wreathed bowls, three dawn libations, water and honey in the last vessel, olive twigs, and a silent prayer; Ismene enters and goes to perform it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

_Enter_ ISMENE.

The offering requires clean water from a perennial spring, bowls wreathed with wool from a young ewe-lamb, and three libations at dawn. The last vessel is to contain water and honey, followed by eighteen olive twigs and a silent prayer to the Gentle Powers; Ismene goes to perform it.

the ritual offerings in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Abstract written by a model

The ritual offerings require spring water drawn with undefiled hands, three wool-crowned bowls, honey, and olive sprays. Water and honey without wine fill the final bowl; three libations are poured toward dawn, then thrice nine olive sprays are laid down and a whispered prayer is offered to the Gracious deities.

The offerings require water from a living spring, bowls with wool-crowned rims and handles, and three libations poured toward the dawn.
