Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chunking

LLM-Embedder is selected by the study as the embedding model for semantic matching between queries and chunk blocks. It is chosen for its balance of performance and size according to the evaluation module of FlagEmbedding.

Retrieval Methods

LLM-Embedder, recommended in Section 3.2, is used as the query and document encoder for evaluating the query transformation methods. Supervised methods outperform unsupervised methods, and LLM-Embedder achieves the highest scores when combined with HyDE and hybrid search.
