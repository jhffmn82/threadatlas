the review attack on Merman in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

The review attack on Merman appears in the leading Review three weeks after Grampus read the book. Written recognizably by Grampus, it pillories Merman’s alleged incompetence, portraying him as imprecise and unlearned and making him widely amusing and notorious.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

The review attack on Merman is written by Grampus and identifies Merman without requiring a signature through its distinctive knowledge and epithets. It portrays Merman as lacking the necessary precision and learning, uses an elaborate comparison to a walrus-like sciolist, and makes him a subject of widespread amusement and notoriety.
