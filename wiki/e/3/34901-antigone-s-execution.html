Antigone’s execution

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Antigone's burial, death, death-blow, die, make a grave for the unburied dead, ministered the last libations, she shall die, tending, Polynices, on thy corse
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Antigone's burial is ordered by Creon as confinement alive in a tomb; after Tiresias' warning, Creon agrees to release her from the vault.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Antigone’s execution is ordered by Creon despite her appeal to divine law, and Haemon declares that her death will be accompanied by another death.
