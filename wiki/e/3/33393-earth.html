Earth in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Earth is a good place to live upon, beneath Sky Island, with ocean and mainland. It is home to Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill when they are at home, and the intended destination of the strangers. Though the Boolooroo calls it an uninhabited, round, cold, barren ball of mud and water, the travelers contradict him. Unable to return without the Magic Umbrella, they eventually see Earth below, fly over its ocean, reach the mainland, and arrive at the high bluff near Trot's house.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

The travelers say that they live on the Earth when they are at home. The Boolooroo claims that the Earth is an uninhabited, round, cold, barren ball of mud and water, but the travelers contradict him.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

Earth is the place to which Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill hope to return. They believe they cannot get back to Earth without the Magic Umbrella.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Earth lies beneath Sky Island and is invisible from the Pink Country’s sky. Trot and Button-Bright want to return there, and Cap'n Bill says the strangers came from it.

THE JOURNEY HOME

The travelers eventually see Earth below them and fly over the ocean before reaching the mainland. The Magic Umbrella carries them to the high bluff near Trot's house.
