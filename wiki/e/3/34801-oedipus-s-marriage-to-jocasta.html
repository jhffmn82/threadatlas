Oedipus’s marriage to Jocasta in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

Oedipus’s marriage to Jocasta is an incestuous union: unknowingly, he marries the woman who bore him, becoming both her son and husband. Teiresias prophesies the revelation; Jocasta learns the truth first and leaves in anguish. The Chorus condemns the impious marriage, which produces two daughters who are also Oedipus’s sisters.
