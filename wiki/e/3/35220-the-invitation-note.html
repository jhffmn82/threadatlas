I. A SCANDAL IN BOHEMIA

The invitation note tells Holmes that a gentleman will visit him at a specified time to discuss a matter of the deepest importance. It warns that the visitor may wear a mask and is written on distinctive paper later linked by Holmes to Bohemia.
