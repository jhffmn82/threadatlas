Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XVII.

National consciousness is defined as a living force of shared sentiment arising from memories, common descent, historical struggle, and collective aspirations. It can inspire sacrifice, resistance, social discipline, poetry, thought, and action.
