Also called: THE BAGPIPE

Abstract written by a model

Bagpipe is a musical instrument, possibly denoted by the Hebrew sumphonia and perhaps related to the magrepha. A Tarsus terra-cotta, probably about 2000 years old, may represent an early form. Long favored by Celtic races and admired by Slavonic nations, it was of high antiquity in Ireland; in Poland and Ukraine it could be made from a whole goat skin.

CHAPTER III.

The Hebrew sumphonia is thought by Forkel and others to have denoted a bagpipe, while the magrepha may also have been a kind of bagpipe rather than an organ. A syrinx with bellows from Tarsus is offered as a possible early representation of such an instrument.

CHAPTER X.

The bagpipe was a long-established favorite among Celtic races and was also admired by Slavonic nations. In Poland and the Ukraine it could be made from the whole skin of a goat, while the instrument was of high antiquity in Ireland and was represented in Irish sources.
