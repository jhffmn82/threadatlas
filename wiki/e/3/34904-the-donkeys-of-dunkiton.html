the donkeys of Dunkiton in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

The donkeys of Dunkiton are a clever, adorned group with hoof-like limbs that use front legs as arms. Afraid of foxes, they make a racket by kicking suspended metal sheets. They mistake Button-Bright for a fox, but the shaggy man corrects them; they stop and agree to lead the travelers to King Kik-a-bray.

6. The City Of Beasts

The donkeys of Dunkiton pound their heels against suspended sheets of tin or iron to frighten away foxes. They initially mistake Button-Bright for a fox, but stop their noise when the shaggy man switches them and then agree to lead the travelers to King Kik-a-bray.
