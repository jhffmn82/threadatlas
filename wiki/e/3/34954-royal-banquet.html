23. The Grand Banquet

The royal banquet takes place in the palace's great dining-hall, where guests sit at decorated tables and are served an elaborate feast. Its entertainment includes the Woggle-Bug's ode, the Wizard's magical pie, Polychrome's Rainbow Dance, songs, drills, dances, and the Rubber Bear's bouncing performance. The banquet continues until late evening and serves as the introduction to the next day's birthday celebration.
