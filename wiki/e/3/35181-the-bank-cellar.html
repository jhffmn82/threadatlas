the bank cellar in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The bank cellar, part of City and Suburban Bank, is a heavily secured vault holding crates, massive boxes, and borrowed French gold. Protected by gates and guards, it becomes the hiding place where Holmes, Dr. Watson, Peter Jones, and Mr. Merryweather capture John Clay breaking through its floor.
