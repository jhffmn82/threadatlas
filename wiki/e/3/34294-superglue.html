SuperGLUE in ARES: An Automated Evaluation Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Jon Saad-Falcon · 2024

SuperGLUE is a widely used benchmark providing datasets for ARES. It supplies MultiRC and ReCoRD for retrieval-based open-domain experiments, then supports evaluation of ARES ranking and cross-domain performance, where fine-tuned LLM judges generalize strongly across reported domain shifts.

Experiments / 4.1 .. 4.3

SuperGLUE supplies the MultiRC and ReCoRD datasets used in the experiments. The authors create open-domain versions of these tasks by performing retrieval over their passages.

Results & Analysis / 5.1 .. 5.4

SuperGLUE datasets are included in the evaluation of ARES ranking and cross-domain performance. The fine-tuned LLM judges show strong generalization across the reported SuperGLUE domain shifts.
