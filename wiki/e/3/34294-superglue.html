SuperGLUE

Abstract written by a model

SuperGLUE is a widely used benchmark providing datasets for ARES. It supplies MultiRC and ReCoRD for retrieval-based open-domain experiments, then supports evaluation of ARES ranking and cross-domain performance, where fine-tuned LLM judges generalize strongly across reported domain shifts.
