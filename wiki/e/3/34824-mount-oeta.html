Mount Oeta

place · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Mount OEta, OEta, Oeta, Oeta's height, Oeta's topmost height, Oeta's vale, the crag of Zeus, Οἴτην

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Oeta is the mountain range overlooking a voyage route whose summit holds the pyre where Heracles orders Hyllus to burn his body. In The Library, Mount Oeta, in Trachinian territory, is where Hercules, afflicted by the tunic, orders and constructs a pyre before being carried up to heaven. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Mount Oeta is where Hercules built his funeral pile after being poisoned by Nessus's blood, lay upon it, and commanded Philoctetes to set it alight.
