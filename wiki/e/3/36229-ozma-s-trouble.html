Ozma's trouble in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Ozma's trouble is the threatened destruction of Oz by the Nome King's tunnel and alliance with evil creatures of the North. Her anxious friends travel to the Emerald City and resolve to stand by her, while Scarecrow advises enjoying remaining happy hours.

25. How the Scarecrow Displayed His Wisdom

Ozma's trouble is the threat posed by the Nome King's tunnel and his alliance with the evil creatures of the North. Her friends are saddened and anxious about her fate, travel to the Emerald City, and decide to stand by her even though she believes they cannot defeat the enemies.
