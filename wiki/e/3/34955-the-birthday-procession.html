the birthday procession in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

The birthday procession is a Birthday Celebration in Oz. Young girls carrying roses lead, followed by the four rulers, Emerald City Cornet Band, Royal Army, Princess Ozma, and notable companions including Santa Claus, Dorothy and the Scarecrow, Toto, and the Wizard. Foreign guests, familiar figures, servants, and Oz people join before it passes through the emerald gates.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

24. The Birthday Celebration

The birthday procession includes young girls carrying roses, the four rulers of Oz, the Emerald City Cornet Band, the Royal Army, Princess Ozma, the Blue Bear Rug, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, foreign guests, and many familiar figures from Oz. Palace servants and the people of Oz join at the end before the procession travels through the emerald gates.
