Retriever and Generator Fine-tuning

Surveys provide comprehensive overviews of existing RAG methodologies, including text generation, integration with LLMs, multimodal systems, and AI-generated content. They do not eliminate the challenge of selecting an appropriate algorithm for practical implementation.
