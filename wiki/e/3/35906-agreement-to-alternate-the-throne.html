agreement to alternate the throne in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

An arrangement between Eteocles and Polynices for yearly alternation of rule in Thebes. Polynices voluntarily goes into exile while Eteocles rules for one year, after which they are to exchange the sceptre. Eteocles, despite swearing to yield rule, later refuses to leave the throne.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE PHŒNICIAN VIRGINS.

Eteocles and Polynices agree that Polynices will first go into voluntary exile while Eteocles rules Thebes for a year, after which they will exchange the sceptre. Eteocles later refuses to leave the throne.

POLYNICES, CHORUS.

Polynices says that he voluntarily left Thebes so that Eteocles could rule for a year and then yield the government to him for an equal period. He claims that Eteocles swore to this agreement but has refused to fulfill it.
