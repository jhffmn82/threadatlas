Pondicherry in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Pondicherry is an Indian seaport identified by a letter postmark. In March 1883, Elias Openshaw receives the first warning letter from there, containing five orange pips and K. K. K.; Holmes then traces the Lone Star through shipping records.
