Eurydice's suicide

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: She with keen knife, This crime, Wounding herself to the heart, fell and closed her eyes in night, fresh inflicted blow, her self-destruction, is dead, and bleeding with a new-given wound, self-destruction, stabbed herself to the heart
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Eurydice's suicide occurs after she hears of Haemon's death; she wounds herself to the heart before the altar and curses Creon for the deaths of her children.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Eurydice's suicide occurs beside an altar with a sharp sword after she hears of Haemon's death; before dying, she mourns Megareus and Haemon and curses Creon for their deaths.
