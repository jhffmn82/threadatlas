King Kleaver

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: his Majesty King Kleaver, his Majesty the cleaver, the King, your Majesty

Abstract written by a model

King Kleaver is a cleaver and king who rules Utensia. Lazy and often smoking his long pipe or sleeping, he commands counselors, courtiers, and the Spoon Brigade, and appoints Judge Sifter and High Priest Colender. When prisoners are brought before him, he convenes officials, rebukes violent advisers, recognizes Dorothy’s innocence, and accepts her release.

King Kleaver is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
