King Kleaver in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

King Kleaver is a cleaver and king who rules Utensia. Lazy and often smoking his long pipe or sleeping, he commands counselors, courtiers, and the Spoon Brigade, and appoints Judge Sifter and High Priest Colender. When prisoners are brought before him, he convenes officials, rebukes violent advisers, recognizes Dorothy’s innocence, and accepts her release.

King Kleaver is the king of Utensia and commands the Spoon Brigade to bring all captured prisoners to him.

16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

King Kleaver, a cleaver who rules Utensia, convenes his officials to decide what to do with the prisoners. He rebukes several unruly or overly violent advisers, acknowledges that Dorothy has not been accused of anything, and accepts Judge Sifter’s order to release her.
