Tezcucans

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Acolhuans

Abstract written by a model

The Tezcucans, or Acolhuans, were of the same race as the Aztecs but were described as greatly surpassing them in knowledge, social refinement, and the arts and sciences. Their histories, poems, laws, and dialect were regarded as superior, and Tezcuco possessed the Council of music.
