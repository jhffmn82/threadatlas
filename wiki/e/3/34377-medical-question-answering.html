medical question answering in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024

Medical question answering is a large language model task with state-of-the-art performance but persistent hallucination and outdated-knowledge challenges. MIRAGE evaluates it zero-shot using biomedical multiple-choice examination and research questions. Results recommend retrieval-augmented generation with matched corpora: Textbooks and StatPearls for examination questions, PubMed for research-related questions.

Medical question answering is a retrieval-dataset category that provides short, concise answers and is used for medical dialogue and benchmark evaluation. It benefits from retrieval-augmented generation, including Naive RAG with multiple LLMs, corpora, and retrievers. Reviewed studies report improved accuracy from external context; performance can also improve through chunk selection, rank fusion, modular retrieval, query augmentation, LLM readers, knowledge graphs, and lightweight frameworks. It can address complex questions and use medical social-media data in low-resource settings. When answers are selected from given options, studies evaluate it with accuracy.
