the dispute between Oedipus and Creon

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a dim, unproved debate, dire calumny, discord, private broils, the fray, the quarrel, the quarrel between Oedipus and Creon, this embroilment of events, this wordy wrangle, wordy rancour, your feud
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, the dispute concerns Oedipus's accusation that Creon conspired with Tiresias to overthrow or murder him; Creon denies the charge, while Jocasta and the Chorus intervene until Oedipus agrees not to execute or exile him.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the dispute begins when Oedipus accuses Creon of arranging the seer's charge and plotting to take the crown; Jocasta and the Chorus intervene, after which Oedipus allows Creon to leave despite continuing to hate and suspect him.

the dispute between Oedipus and Creon in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

The dispute between Oedipus and Creon concerns Oedipus's accusation that Creon conspired with Tiresias to overthrow or murder him. Creon denies the charge and defends his lack of ambition, while Jocasta and the Chorus intervene until Oedipus agrees not to execute or exile him.
