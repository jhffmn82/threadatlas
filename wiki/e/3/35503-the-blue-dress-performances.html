the blue-dress performances in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The blue-dress performances are repeated drawing-room events at the Copper Beeches: Miss Hunter wears an electric-blue dress, sits with her back to the central front window, and listens as Mr. Rucastle tells stories or reads aloud, while the Rucastles show unusual eagerness.

The blue-dress performances involve Miss Hunter changing into a particular dress, sitting with her back to the central window, and listening to Mr. Rucastle tell amusing stories or reading aloud. The performances occur more than once, and the Rucastles appear unusually eager for her to take part.
