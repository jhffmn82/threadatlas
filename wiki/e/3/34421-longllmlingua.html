LongLLMLingua

method · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: LONGLLM-LINGUA, LONGLLMLINGUA, LongLLMlingua

Abstract written by a model

In "An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LONGLLMLINGUA is evaluated as a prompt-compression filtering baseline that partially restores EM, F1, and IB performance after retrieval but generally remains below the non-retrieval generator and the proposed information-bottleneck approach. In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," LongLLMLingua is described as an alternative to Recomp that improves LLMLingua by focusing on query-related information, but underperforms Recomp, has higher latency, and can distort semantics or produce incoherent rewritten content.

Summarization

LongLLMLingua improves LLMLingua by focusing on key information related to the query. It does not perform well on the evaluated datasets but demonstrates better generalization capabilities because it was not trained on them, so it is considered an alternative method.

Comprehensive Evaluation .. Results and Analysis

LongLLMLingua underperformed Recomp and sometimes distorted semantics or produced incoherent content because of its rewriting approach. Its tested configuration also had higher latency than Recomp.
