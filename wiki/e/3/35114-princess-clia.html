Princess Clia in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Princess Clia is a fairy mermaid princess of the mermaids' deep-sea kingdom, cousin to Merla, companion and palace attendant of Queen Aquareine, and a faithful friend of Trot. She possesses fairy powers jointly with Aquareine and can navigate the deceptive maze. Clia begins by inviting Trot and Cap'n Bill to the underwater kingdom, transforming them so they can live beneath the sea and escorting them through the ocean. She explains sea dangers and creatures, shortens their journey with fairy power, and leads them through the guarded gardens into Aquareine's palace. When dangers arise, Clia identifies and explains octopi, cuttlefish, sea devils, and the maze, stays close to protect Trot, and reassures the others despite their capture by Zog. Taken to the Rose Chamber with Aquareine and Trot, she helps seek routes out of Zog's castle, remains calm under his threats, and proposes that destroying Zog is necessary for freedom. She urges the secret making of a golden sword and, after being trapped in ice without suffering cold because she is a fairy, joins the escape. Afterward, Clia accompanies the group to King Anko's palace and participates in the allegiance ceremony. She then fulfills her promise to escort Trot and Cap'n Bill home, guiding them to the Giant's Cave, restoring their regular shapes, helping them return to their boat, and saying goodbye affectionately to Trot.

Narrative, section by section

13 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap 2._

Clia is a mermaid princess who invites Trot and Cap'n Bill to visit the mermaids' deep-sea kingdom. She offers Trot her hand and helps transform the visitors so they can live underwater.

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

Princess Clia helps Cap'n Bill enter the water and leads him and Trot through the ocean. She explains that mortals need protection from some sea creatures, describes the dangers of sharks and devilfish, and reveals that the sea serpents are harmless and kind. She uses fairy power to shorten the journey to the mermaid palaces.

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

Princess Clia leads Trot and Cap'n Bill through the sea gardens and into the mermaids' palace. She explains that the swordfishes guard the garden entrances and welcomes the visitors to the palace.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

Princess Clia travels with Queen Aquareine, Trot, and Cap'n Bill. She warns Trot that the creature approaching them is an octopus and explains that octopi are not very bad, though their cousins the sea devils are feared.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

Princess Clia explains how cuttlefish carry and release their ink, warns Trot not to look up, and stays close to protect her. She insists that the fairy circles have foiled their enemies before, but she is trapped with the others when the sea devils form their cage.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Princess Clia escapes with the group and reassures Cap'n Bill that Trot's end is not near. She says that she or Queen Aquareine could guide the others safely back through the maze. She leads Cap'n Bill by the hand as they follow Sacho.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Princess Clia asks why Zog seeks revenge against the prisoners even though they have never harmed him. She is taken with Queen Aquareine and Trot to the Rose Chamber.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Princess Clia notices a possible opening at the top of the castle's dome and helps the others consider escaping through it. She is unharmed by the Yell-Maker and by the collapse of the marble ceiling.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Clia remains calm after Zog's threat and asks Sacho whether he will help them escape. She also asks what causes the constant light in the castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Clia suggests that the group should destroy Zog so they can leave freely. She explains that King Anko could not destroy Zog because he could not reach him, while Aquareine can potentially do so. She also urges Agga-Groo to make the golden sword secretly.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Princess Clia is trapped in the ice but is not affected by the cold because she is a fairy. After Queen Aquareine frees her, she helps Trot warm Cap'n Bill and follows the group toward the opening in the dome.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

Clia accompanies Aquareine, Trot, and Cap'n Bill to King Anko's palace and joins in the ceremony of allegiance. She agrees to escort Trot and Cap'n Bill home and affectionately kisses Trot before their departure.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._

Princess Clia guides Trot and Cap’n Bill to the Giant’s Cave and explains that the entrance lies near the bottom of the sea. She changes them back to their regular shapes and helps them return to their boat before saying goodbye.
