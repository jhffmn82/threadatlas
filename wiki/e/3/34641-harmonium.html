harmonium in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The harmonium uses the free reed, a principle imported from China; suggested by the Chinese cheng, it was applied to organ stops and later became popular in western Europe, competing with pianoforte.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The harmonium uses the free reed, a principle imported from China. The Chinese cheng suggested to Kratzenstein the application of the free reed to organ stops, and instruments of the harmonium class later became widely popular in western Europe.
