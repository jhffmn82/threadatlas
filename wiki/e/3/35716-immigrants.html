Immigrants are depicted as coming from varied backgrounds, including Germanic, Semitic, Greek, Scottish, Irish, and Jewish communities. Although some fear that immigrants acquire wealth and influence or alter English speech and customs, the text argues that excluding them would not serve the long-term interests of the English people.
