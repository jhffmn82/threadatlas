trial of Orestes and Electra

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Orestes' trial, a public decision, the appointed day, the city of the Argives will pronounce their vote, the decree of the Argives, the vote

Abstract written by a model

The Argives are to decide whether Orestes should be stoned or killed with a sword. Orestes and Electra hope that Menelaus, as Agamemnon's brother, will help them before the decision is made. The trial concerns whether Orestes and Electra should live or die for the murder committed against Clytemnestra. The Argive assembly hears several speeches and finally condemns both of them.
