VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The adventure of the blue carbuncle begins as Sherlock Holmes investigates an apparently ordinary lost hat and goose. Peterson's discovery of the stolen gem transforms the matter into an investigation involving the Countess of Morcar, John Horner, Henry Baker, and the Hotel Cosmopolitan jewel robbery.

“So much for Mr. Henry Baker,” said Holmes when he had closed the door

The blue carbuncle investigation follows the stone through Henry Baker’s goose, the Alpha Inn, Mr. Breckinridge, and Mrs. Oakshott’s poultry business. Holmes solves the investigation by identifying Ryder and securing his confession.
