[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

Queen Aquareine's throne room is the most magnificent chamber in the ocean palaces, with coral walls inlaid with mother-of-pearl, polished shells, gem-patterned floors, and coral couches. Queen Aquareine reclines on a raised mother-of-pearl platform upon a couch covered with diamonds, rubies, emeralds, and pearls.
