the jellyfish in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Jellyfish are a rare variety of soft, transparent, tinted forms that float in clear ocean water in any direction. They resemble inverted flowerpots with fluted edges and flower-like centers, lack apparent heads, eyes, or ears, live long, but may be washed ashore near the surface.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 6._

The jellyfish are a rare variety that float in the clear water and resemble inverted flowerpots with fluted edges. They are soft, transparent, and tinted sapphire blue, rose pink, or topaz, with flower-like forms floating from their centers. They have no apparent heads, eyes, or ears, live for a long time, and may be washed ashore if they get too near the surface.
