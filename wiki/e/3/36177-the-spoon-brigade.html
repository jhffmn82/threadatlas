Abstract written by a model

The Spoon Brigade is a military brigade of armed spoons in Utensia's forces, commanded by Captain Dipp and serving King Kleaver. It surrounds Dorothy, Billina, and Toto, makes them prisoners, and escorts them toward the king in a hollow square, marching through the forest until halting at a clearing's edge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

The Spoon Brigade is a military group of armed spoons serving King Kleaver. It surrounds Dorothy, Billina, and Toto, declares them prisoners, and orders them to march to its king.

16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

The Spoon Brigade, consisting of six to eight dozen spoons, escorts Dorothy, Billina, and Toto through the forest in a hollow square. It marches after them until they reach the edge of the clearing, where it halts.
