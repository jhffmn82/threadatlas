Aias's corpse

Abstract written by a model

Aias’s corpse, a dead body found fallen on its sword in a lonely wooded spot, becomes the center of a burial dispute. Initially covered by Tecmessa and denied burial by Agamemnon, it is uncovered and defended by Teucer, who insists it be removed from the blade, receive lawful obsequies, and be prepared for burial.

Aias's corpse is found fallen upon his sword and is initially covered by Tecmessa. Teucer uncovers it, mourns its condition, and defends the right to remove it from the blade and give it a proper burial.

_Enter_ TEUCER.

Aias’s corpse is the subject of the dispute between Teucer and Agamemnon. Teucer refuses to let it remain without lawful obsequies, and the corpse is prepared for burial while its gashed veins are still darkly bleeding.
