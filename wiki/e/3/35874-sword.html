Abstract written by a model

The sword is a weapon central to Orestes' crisis: recalled as blood-stained after his mother's killing, considered for suicide or Electra's death, then concealed for the planned murder of Helen. Drawn in the attacks on Helen and Clytemnestra, it is finally held at Hermione's neck until Apollo's intervention makes Orestes release her.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

PYLADES, ORESTES, CHORUS.

The sword is one of the possible means by which Electra asks whether she and Orestes must die. The Chorus also recalls Orestes brandishing a blood-stained sword after killing his mother.

ELECTRA, ORESTES, PYLADES, CHORUS.

Orestes initially considers using the sword to kill himself, while Electra asks that Orestes kill her rather than let the Argives execute her. Orestes and Pylades conceal swords beneath their robes for the attack on Helen, and a sword is held to Hermione's neck.

PHRYGIAN, CHORUS.

Orestes and Pylades use drawn swords in the attack on Helen and Clytemnestra. Orestes later holds a sword over Hermione's neck, and Apollo's intervention causes him to release her.
