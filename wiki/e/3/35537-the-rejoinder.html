The rejoinder is a sixty-page work of concentrated argument in which Merman acknowledges his mistakes, restates his theory, and offers alternative interpretations of evidence cited by Grampus. It is eventually printed by a modest medium without requiring Merman to pay for insertion, but it receives no reply from Grampus and instead prompts further ridicule by Grampus's followers.
