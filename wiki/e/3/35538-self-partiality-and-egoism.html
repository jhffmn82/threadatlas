self-partiality and egoism in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Self-partiality and egoism distort judgment through self-satisfaction or discontent, foster spiritual pride through egoistic consolation, and are corrected by rejecting personal benefit as central to justice.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

I.

The narrator discovers that both self-satisfaction and self-discontent can distort judgment. He rejects consolations that turn personal disappointment into spiritual superiority and tries to correct the egoism that makes his own benefit seem central to the world's justice.
