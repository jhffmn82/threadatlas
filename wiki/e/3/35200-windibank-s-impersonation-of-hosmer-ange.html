Windibank's impersonation of Hosmer Angel

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Windibank's impersonation of Hosmer Angel is a deception to keep Mary Sutherland from other suitors and preserve her household income. Using a disguise, tinted glasses, altered voice, and the Testament to bind her fidelity, he courts and engages her, then disappears as the invented bridegroom.

Windibank's impersonation of Hosmer Angel was designed to keep Mary Sutherland from attracting another suitor and to preserve the income she contributed to the household. He disguised his appearance and voice, courted Mary, became engaged to her, and then vanished in a way intended to leave her faithful to his invented identity.
