Hippolytus's chariot

object · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: car, chariot, chariot side, flying chariot, my car, my chariot, one wheel-edge, the car drawn by the team, the chariot, the compact chariot, wheel-box
  • In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, the chariot left Trozên in exile, overturned when its wheel caught a rock, entangled and dragged Hippolytus, and was broken against rocks.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Hippolytus's chariot carried him from the shore after he was banished; a sea-born bull caused the horses to panic, and the chariot overturned, breaking its wheels and throwing Hippolytus into the reins.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Hippolytus's chariot was driven toward the lofty rocks when his horses panicked at the sea-monster; its wheel struck a stump and it broke apart on the long axle-tree, throwing Hippolytus out and leaving the reins entangled around his limbs.
