In Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation, human annotators judge faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance, agreeing in about 95% of faithfulness and context cases and 90% of answer cases, and resolving disagreements through discussion.

Abstract written by a model

Fluent-English human annotators assess WikiEval instances for faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. Their preferences provide reference judgments for model-agreement and accuracy comparisons. They agree on about 95% of faithfulness and context-relevance cases and 90% of answer-relevance cases, resolving disagreements through discussion.
