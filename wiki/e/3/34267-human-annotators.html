human annotators in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024

Fluent-English human annotators assess WikiEval instances for faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. Their preferences provide reference judgments for model-agreement and accuracy comparisons. They agree on about 95% of faithfulness and context-relevance cases and 90% of answer-relevance cases, resolving disagreements through discussion.

opening

Two fluent-English human annotators judged faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. They agreed in around 95% of cases for faithfulness and context relevance and around 90% of cases for answer relevance, resolving disagreements through discussion.

Human annotators provide the preferences used to assess model agreement in WikiEval. Their judgments serve as the reference for accuracy comparisons.
