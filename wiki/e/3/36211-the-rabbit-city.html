Abstract written by a model

The rabbit city is a city inside Bunnybury's marble wall, enclosed by a high marble wall. It has white marble streets, unusual marble houses, green clover lawns, and a central square with trees, Glinda the Good's bronze statue, and the Royal Palace's frosted-gold portals.

19. How Bunnybury Welcomed the Strangers

The rabbit city is enclosed by a high marble wall and contains unusual marble houses with slender spires and minarets, white marble streets, and green clover lawns. Its central square has pretty trees, a bronze statue of Glinda the Good, and the portals of a white marble Royal Palace covered with frosted gold.
