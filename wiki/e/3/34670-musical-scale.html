musical scale in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The musical scale was diatonic about a thousand years ago, except for a chromatic seventh interval because it included both b-flat and b-natural; period notation showed its compass and interval order.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] [Illustration] CHAPTER VIII.

About a thousand years ago, the musical scale was diatonic except for the seventh interval, which was chromatic because both b-flat and b-natural belonged to the scale. The notation of the period showed its compass and order of intervals.
