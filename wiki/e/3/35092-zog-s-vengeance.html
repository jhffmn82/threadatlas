Zog's vengeance in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Zog's vengeance is a wrong scheme against King Anko: unable to hurt Anko directly, Zog plans to make him suffer by destroying his friends, though Sacho hopes the prisoners escape it.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Zog's vengeance is directed at King Anko, whom he hates but cannot directly hurt. He intends to make Anko suffer by destroying Anko's friends.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Sacho says Zog has no time for vengeance, although he believes the magician may be plotting to kill the prisoners. He considers vengeance wrong and hopes the prisoners will escape it.
