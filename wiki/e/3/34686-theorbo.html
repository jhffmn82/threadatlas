theorbo in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The theorbo was a double-necked lute that appears to have come into use during the sixteenth century. It had additional bass strings beside the finger-board that could not be shortened by the fingers; its largest forms were the archlute and chitarrone.
