Abstract written by a model

The public scandal is the threatened disgrace arising from damage to the Beryl Coronet, a national possession. Alexander Holder fears harm to it will disgrace him, affect the noblest people, and threaten England. Holmes resolves the theft without prosecution, preventing exposure of Holder’s family and saving England from scandal.

XI. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BERYL CORONET

Alexander Holder believes that any harm to the Beryl Coronet would cause a great public scandal because it is regarded as a national possession. He fears that the damage will bring public disgrace upon him and affect the noblest people in the land.

“For Heaven’s sake, tell me, then, what is this extraordinary mystery!”

Holmes avoids a prosecution because exposing the theft would create a serious public scandal for Mr. Holder's family. Mr. Holder says that Holmes's actions have saved England from such a scandal.
