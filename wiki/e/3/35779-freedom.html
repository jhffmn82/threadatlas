freedom

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: free, free country, free land, freedom and self-rule, freedom of Athens, independent state, land of freedom, managing our city ourselves by ourselves
  • In Hecuba and other plays, it is defended by Demophoon against Argos, as Athens refuses to surrender suppliants or become an appendage of the Argive realm.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it characterizes Athens, as asserted by Demophoon, distinguishing it from a city subject to Argive commands.
