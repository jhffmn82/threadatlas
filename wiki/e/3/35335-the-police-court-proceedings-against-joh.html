the police-court proceedings against John Horner in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The police-court proceedings concern John Horner’s false accusation in the blue carbuncle theft. After his arrest and appearance in the dock, the magistrate refers him to the Assizes, but Holmes expects the case to collapse when Ryder will not testify.
