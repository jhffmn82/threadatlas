Abstract written by a model

The travelers are a traveling party in the Land of Oz, including Dorothy, the Wizard, Uncle Henry, the Shaggy Man, Omby Amby, and others. They expect hospitality, camp during their journey, and move through several communities. After crossing into the Country of the Winkies, they travel toward the Tin Castle. Along the way they enter the Cuttenclips’ village, where the Shaggy Man’s sneeze causes damage; help assemble the Fuddles; camp in a forest using tents made by the Wizard; and leave Flutterbudget Center after encountering fearful inhabitants. They continue through the Winkies’ country toward the Tin Castle.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

10. How the Cuttenclips Lived

The traveling party follows a sign to the Cuttenclips and enters the village on foot because the doorway is too small for the wagon. They are warned to move slowly, avoid coughing, and make no breeze, but they eventually leave after the Shaggy Man’s sneeze causes widespread damage.

12. How they Matched the Fuddles

The travelers include Dorothy, the Wizard, Aunt Em, Uncle Henry, the Shaggy Man, Omby Amby, the Yellow Hen, and the Captain General. They help assemble the Fuddles, are entertained by the experience, and then resume their journey.

14. How the Wizard Practiced Sorcery

The travelers leave Fuddlecumjig, follow a southwest route, and reach a forest near sundown. They camp there in tents made by the Wizard, eat a plentiful meal, and sleep until daylight is expected to return.

23. How They Encountered the Flutterbudgets

The travelers visit Flutterbudget Center and encounter several inhabitants who are terrified by harmless events or imaginary possibilities. They leave the village after the Wizard grows uneasy and continue through the Winkies’ country toward the Tin Castle.
