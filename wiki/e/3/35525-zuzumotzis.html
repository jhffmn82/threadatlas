III.

The Zuzumotzis are associated with the Magicodumbras in Merman's theory about ancient civilisations. Merman believes he has found the correct clue concerning them, but his views become objects of ridicule after Grampus's attack.

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

The Zuzumotzis are paired with the Magicodumbras as central elements in Merman's theory and in the ridicule directed at him. Their names become popularly associated with questionable or absurd ideas, while Merman's continuing fixation on them damages his professional reputation and income.
