Abstract written by a model

The excessive talker monopolizes company conversation, preventing others from taking turns and excluding their contributions; however varied or brilliant his talk, his assumed importance impoverishes discussion and makes him a nuisance.

XIV.

The excessive talker prevents the rest of the company from taking their turn and assumes that others would rather hear his opinions or experiences. Even when his talk is varied and brilliant, he is suspected of impoverishing conversation by excluding other minds and is regarded as a nuisance.
