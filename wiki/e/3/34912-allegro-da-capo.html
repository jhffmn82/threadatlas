Allegro da Capo in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Allegro da Capo is a little fat man and self-described famous musicker who lives alone beside the road. Bald, blue-eyed, and elaborately dressed, he carries a stout gold-headed cane. Reeds in his lungs make involuntary, error-free music whenever he breathes, so he calls himself a band. Musical since life began, he wants to accompany the travelers to Princess Ozma's birthday performance, but they leave him behind because his constant music troubles them.

8. The Musicker

Allegro da Capo is a little fat man who lives beside the road and calls himself a famous musicker. He says that reeds in his lungs make music whenever he breathes, and he asks to accompany the travelers to the Land of Oz so he can play at Ozma's birthday celebration. The travelers leave him behind because his constant music troubles them.
