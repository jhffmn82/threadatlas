Deianira's grief and jealousy

topic · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: another love, fear, jealousy and marital fidelity, longing, love passeth by, one man, pain, queror, shame, this heart-break, what I suffer

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Deianira fears Heracles will prefer the younger captive woman as a mate and attempts through the robe to preserve marital fidelity and Heracles' love, while acknowledging that a prudent wife should not openly resent such a rival. In Heroides and Amores, Deianira grieves over Hercules's absence, his new love, and Iole displayed as a captive, and her jealousy turns to horror and guilt when she learns that the gift she sent may be killing him.
