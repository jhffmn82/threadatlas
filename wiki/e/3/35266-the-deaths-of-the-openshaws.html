V. THE FIVE ORANGE PIPS

Elias Openshaw died in a shallow, green-scummed pool after receiving a warning, and his death was recorded as suicide. Joseph Openshaw died after falling into an unfenced chalk-pit after receiving a similar warning, and his death was recorded as accidental; John believes both deaths were connected to the warnings.
