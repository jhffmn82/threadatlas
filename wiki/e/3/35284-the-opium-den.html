Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The opium den is a smoky, dangerous murder-trap in Upper Swandam Lane, run and managed by a Lascar proprietor or manager. People lie in wooden berths and smoke opium. It has a furnished front room, a small bedroom overlooking a wharf, Hugh Boone’s lodgings, and a trap-door at the back near Paul’s Wharf. Mrs. St. Clair sees Neville St. Clair at an upstairs window and then sees blood there before he disappears; the Lascar manager resists a search.
