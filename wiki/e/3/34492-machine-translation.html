Abstract written by a model

Machine translation is a generation task evaluated by Selfmem. Retrieval techniques support localization by improving human translators’ productivity and consistency and have been used across translation and prompting settings. Selfmem evaluates four JRC-Acquis directions with BLEU, TER, and chrF++. It compares trainable small models, including Transformer, joint and dual architectures, with few-shot prompted LLMs; self-memory improves supervised results, while in-context demonstrations improve XGLM but remain below supervised baselines.

machine translation in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where machine translation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Cross-cultural translation of texts containing culturally nuanced entity names is a research topic in natural language processing. Although machine translation has advanced through multilingual language models, large multilingual datasets, and systems such as mBART-50, M2M-100, and NLLB-200, current systems still struggle with this setting, especially potentially for low-resource languages and dialects. XC-Translate is introduced as a benchmark for the task. Its English questions are translated into target languages by at least three human translators per question, then verified by at least three separate human annotators per translation. Evaluations compare machine translation systems and LLMs with KG-MT; KG-MT outperforms NLLB-200 and GPT-4 on XC-Translate while generally preserving or slightly improving quality on general-purpose WMT benchmarks.

Machine Translation in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Machine Translation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Machine Translation performs exceptionally well for high-resource languages and increasingly supports low-resource languages, but current systems and LLMs struggle with culturally variable entity names. KG-MT enhances it by retrieving relevant entities and using target-language names rather than memorizing transliterations or transcreations. XC-Translate serves the broader area as one of the largest MT benchmarks, focused on cross-cultural translation.
