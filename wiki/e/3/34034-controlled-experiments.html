IV. AI TUTOR EVALUATION

The controlled experiment randomly assigned 76 first-year university students with no prior finance coursework to standard RAG or KG-RAG tutoring. After self-study and a 45-minute interactive session, the KG-RAG group performed significantly better on the assessment, with t = -3.75, p = 0.00035, and Cohen's d = 0.86.
