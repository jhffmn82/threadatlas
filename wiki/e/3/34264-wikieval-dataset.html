WikiEval in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where WikiEval first appears

Abstract written by a model

WikiEval is a dataset of question-context-answer triples with human judgments of faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance, created from 50 Wikipedia pages about events since early 2022. It includes high- and low-quality examples, including questions about Oppenheimer and the PSLV-C56 mission. Its instances require comparison of two answers or context fragments, measuring agreement between model preferences and human annotators, and it was used to evaluate RAGAS.

WikiEval Dataset in Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2025-04-28 · the work’s page · read it where WikiEval Dataset first appears

Abstract written by a model

WikiEval Dataset is a dataset created to provide question-context-answer examples with human judgments of faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. Based on 50 Wikipedia pages covering events since early 2022, it includes comparisons of two answers or context fragments. Its assessments evaluate proposed metrics and baselines, with Ragas predictions closely matching human judgments, especially for faithfulness and answer relevance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening .. The WikiEval Dataset

The WikiEval Dataset was created to provide question-context-answer examples with human judgments for evaluating faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. It contains material based on 50 Wikipedia pages covering events since the start of 2022.

opening .. Experiments

The WikiEval dataset contains instances requiring comparisons between two answers or two context fragments. Its human assessments were used to evaluate agreement with the proposed metrics and baseline methods.

Conclusions

WikiEval is introduced as a dataset containing human judgements of faithfulness, answer relevance, and context relevance. Evaluation on WikiEval shows that predictions from Ragas closely align with human predictions, especially for faithfulness and answer relevance.
