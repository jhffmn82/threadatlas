CHORUS.

Deianira spreads the ointment over the robe with a tuft of wool, following the Centaur's instruction to keep it away from heat and sunlight. When the wool is exposed to Helios' beam, it crumbles away and leaves seething, foamy clots, revealing the ointment's destructive nature.
