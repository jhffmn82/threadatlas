SelfCheckGPT in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024

SelfCheckGPT is a hallucination detection method that samples multiple generated answers and assesses factuality by expecting factual answers to remain semantically stable across samples.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

2 / Estimating faithfulness using LLMs .. Automated evaluation of text generation systems

SelfCheckGPT samples multiple answers to assess factuality without relying on token probabilities. It is based on the expectation that factual answers are more semantically stable across samples than hallucinated answers.
