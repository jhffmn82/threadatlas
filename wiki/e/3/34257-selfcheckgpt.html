Abstract written by a model

In RAG-HAT: A Hallucination-Aware Tuning Pipeline for LLM in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, SelfCheckGPT detects hallucinations by sampling multiple LLM responses to the same prompt and examining consistency among generations. In RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation, SelfCheckGPT is a hallucination detection method that samples multiple generated answers and assesses factuality by expecting factual answers to remain semantically stable across samples.
