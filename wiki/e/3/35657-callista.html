Callista in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Callista presents herself as imaginative, claiming she would turn an old heap of stones into a castellated ruin. Her lax perception and memory instead lead her to confuse details, exaggerate events, and misrepresent facts. This habitual inaccuracy, if intensified or stimulated by circumstances, could make her a profuse writer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Callista claims to be imaginative and says she would transform an old heap of stones into a castellated ruin when describing it. She is shown to have lax perceptions and memories, confusing details and exaggerating events, so that her supposed imagination is really inaccurate observation and misrepresentation.
