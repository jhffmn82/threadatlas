the typewritten letters in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The typewritten letters, signed Hosmer Angel and attributed to him, offer little identifying evidence beyond a Balzac quotation; their matching typewriter defects reveal that James Windibank wrote them, making the signatures central to proving their true source.

Narrative, section by section

The typewritten letters attributed to Hosmer Angel contain no clear clue to his identity apart from a quotation from Balzac, but their typewritten signatures are significant. Their distinctive defects match the typewritten letter sent by James Windibank and help prove that Windibank produced them.
