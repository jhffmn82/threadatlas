King Kik-a-bray in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

King Kik-a-bray is the exalted ruler of the donkeys in Dunkiton, wearing a golden crown and wielding a jeweled magic staff. He greets travelers according to their stations, uses the staff to give the shaggy man a donkey’s head, directs him to the Truth Pond for restoration, supplies food and lodging, and asks Dorothy for an invitation to Ozma’s birthday celebration.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

6. The City Of Beasts

King Kik-a-bray is identified as the great, glorious, splendid, exalted, and intellectual ruler of the donkeys in Dunkiton. The donkeys say that he will greet the travelers according to their exalted stations.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

King Kik-a-bray rules Dunkiton and uses his magic staff to give the shaggy man a donkey's head. He tells the shaggy man that the Truth Pond can restore his original head, provides the travelers with their preferred foods and a place to sleep, and asks Dorothy to obtain an invitation to Ozma's birthday celebration.
