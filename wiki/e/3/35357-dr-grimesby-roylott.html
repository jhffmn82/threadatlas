Dr. Grimesby Roylott

Also called: Dr. Roylott, my stepfather, the Doctor, the dead man, the old man, their master, this brute, this man

Abstract written by a model

Dr. Grimesby Roylott is a doctor, the last survivor of the Roylotts of Stoke Moran, and stepfather of Helen and Julia Stoner. Immensely strong, violently temperamental, and feared locally, he keeps a cheetah and baboon at Stoke Moran and has assaulted a blacksmith. Financially motivated to prevent his stepdaughters’ marriages, he is associated with Julia’s unexplained death. After Helen seeks help, Roylott confronts and threatens Holmes, bending a steel poker. He uses a swamp adder, sent through a ventilator and down a dummy bell-rope, to attack a bedroom occupant. Holmes strikes the snake, which returns to Roylott and kills him by biting him.

Dr. Grimesby Roylott is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

Dr. Grimesby Roylott is Helen Stoner's stepfather and the last survivor of the Roylotts of Stoke Moran. He is described as immensely strong, violently temperamental, feared by the village, hostile to others, and associated with the unexplained death of Julia, though no cause was established.

“And what do you think of it all, Watson?” asked Sherlock Holmes,

Dr. Grimesby Roylott visits Holmes after tracing Helen Stoner to him and demands to know what she has said. He threatens Holmes, bends a steel poker with his hands, and leaves after warning Holmes not to meddle in his affairs. Holmes learns from the will that Roylott has a strong financial motive for preventing his stepdaughters’ marriages.

Sherlock Holmes and I had no difficulty in engaging a bedroom and

Dr. Grimesby Roylott uses a snake, the ventilator, and the dummy bell-rope to attack the occupant of the bedroom. After Sherlock Holmes strikes at the snake, it turns upon him, and Dr. Grimesby Roylott dies within ten seconds of being bitten.
