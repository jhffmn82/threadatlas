Abstract written by a model

Hindus are described through their music: they possess about fifty instruments, many long unchanged, including ancient vina, a Krishna-favoured flute, Arabic and Persian instruments, violin, and chorus. Their scale divides the octave into twenty-two sruti intervals; instruments span older traditions and comparatively recent introductions.

THE HINDUS.

The Hindus are described as possessing about fifty musical instruments, many of which have remained in popular use with little alteration. Their musical scale included twenty-two intervals called sruti, and they also used Arabic and Persian instruments introduced comparatively recently into Hindustan.
