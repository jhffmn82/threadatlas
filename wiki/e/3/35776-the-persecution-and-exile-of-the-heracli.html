the persecution and exile of the Heraclids

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the persecution and exile of the Heraclids are carried out under Eurystheus's authority, forcing them from Argos and other places where they attempt to settle; Iolaus remains with them because he would be ashamed to abandon the children after their father's death. In Hecuba and other plays, the exile of the children of Hercules drives them from their homeland and repeatedly expels them from places where they seek refuge; Iolaus leads them to the Athenian realm, where they seek protection from further exile.
