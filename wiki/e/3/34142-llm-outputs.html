Abstract written by a model

In SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection, LLM outputs can lack factuality and spread misinformation or dangerous advice, although the method improves factuality and some generated outputs remain unsupported by citations. In Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability, LLM outputs are often not declarative sentences, so they are converted into declarative forms before consistency evaluation. In SafetyRAG: Towards Safe Large Language Model-Based Application through Retrieval-Augmented Generation, LLM outputs become safer when LLMs use SafetyRAG to incorporate appropriate safety facts into their responses.
