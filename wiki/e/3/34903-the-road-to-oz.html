Narrative, section by section

24 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Way to Butterfield

Dorothy meets the Shaggy Man, who asks her for directions to Butterfield, though he admits he does not want to go there. While leading him across the countryside, Dorothy becomes lost when the crossroads inexplicably multiply into many unfamiliar roads. The Shaggy Man reveals that he carries a Love Magnet that makes every living thing he meets love him. He, Dorothy, and Toto then set off down the seventh road in search of a way forward.

2. Dorothy Meets Button-Bright

Dorothy, the Shaggy Man, and Toto travel along the seventh road and meet a little boy named Button-Bright. Button-Bright cannot say where he lives, where he came from, or what he plans to do, and he spends his time digging a hole. Dorothy questions him and becomes frustrated by his repeated answers of "Don't know," while the Shaggy Man suggests that Button-Bright come along with them. The travelers continue on, trusting that the road will eventually lead somewhere.

3. A Queer Village

Dorothy, Button-Bright, the Shaggy Man, and Toto enter Foxville after being stopped by fox-soldiers on the road. The fox-captain brings them to King Dox, who welcomes them and explains that Foxville is nearer to Oz than to Kansas. Dorothy learns that the Shaggy Man's Love Magnet makes the foxes love him, though he does not know why their journey occurred. King Dox tells Dorothy about Princess Ozma's upcoming birthday celebration and asks for help obtaining an invitation.

4. King Dox

King Renard the Fourth, also called King Dox, welcomes Dorothy, Button-Bright, and the shaggy man to Foxville. He transforms Button-Bright's human head into a fox's head, which distresses the boy but delights the fox-princesses. The travelers dine with the foxes, attend a theater performance, spend the night, and leave the next morning after King Dox asks Dorothy to secure him an invitation to Princess Ozma's birthday celebration. They continue toward another village inhabited by large beasts.

5. The Rainbow's Daughter

Dorothy and her companions travel along a lonely road and meet Polychrome, the Rainbow's Daughter, who has become lost on Earth. Polychrome explains that she slid from her father's rainbow when it touched the road, and that the rainbow disappeared before she could hold on. Dorothy invites her to join them on their journey to the Emerald City, where Princess Ozma may know how to send her home. Polychrome accepts and soon resumes dancing and laughing as they continue together.

6. The City Of Beasts

Dorothy, Button-Bright, Polychrome, and the shaggy man eat luncheon and continue along the road toward Oz. They reach Dunkiton, a walled city inhabited by donkeys, whose loud kicking against metal sheets is meant to frighten away foxes. The donkeys mistake Button-Bright, who is wearing a fox head, for a fox until the shaggy man explains the situation. The travelers are then taken to meet King Kik-a-bray, the donkeys' ruler.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

Dorothy and her companions enter Dunkiton, a scattered city inhabited by donkeys, and are brought before King Kik-a-bray. The King transforms the shaggy man's head into a donkey's head, telling him that only the Truth Pond can restore it. The travelers receive food and lodging, then leave Dunkiton and continue toward the Land of Oz. Dorothy promises to ask Princess Ozma to invite King Kik-a-bray to Ozma's birthday celebration.

8. The Musicker

Dorothy, Button-Bright, the shaggy man, Polychrome, and Toto encounter a little fat man named Allegro da Capo who makes constant music through his breathing. Allegro explains in song that his lungs contain reeds, so he cannot help producing the musical sounds. The travelers find the piping troublesome and hurry away, while Allegro asks to accompany them to the Land of Oz for Ozma's birthday. They leave him behind and continue their journey after the music fades beyond a hill.

9. Facing the Scoodlers

Dorothy, Button-Bright, Polychrome, the Shaggy Man, and Toto travel along a rough road through a rocky plain toward low mountains. They encounter the Scoodlers, two-sided black-and-white creatures who demand to use the travelers as soup. The Shaggy Man tries to protect the group with the Love Magnet and then attempts to lead them out, but the Scoodlers block the road and throw their detachable heads at him.

10. Escaping the Soup-Kettle

The shaggy man, Dorothy, Button-Bright, Polychrome, and Toto are captured by the Scoodlers and taken to their cave to be made into soup. The Queen of the Scoodlers orders the prisoners to be put through a meat chopper and cooked with vegetables. Polychrome distracts the guard, allowing the shaggy man to trap the guard's head and lead the others toward the exit. The shaggy man catches and throws every Scoodler head into the black gulf, defeating the Scoodlers and freeing the travelers.

11. Johnny Dooit Does It

Dorothy, Button-Bright, the Shaggy Man, Polychrome, and Toto reach a pleasant grove beside a deadly desert but cannot proceed toward the Land of Oz. The Shaggy Man summons his friend Johnny Dooit with the Love Magnet. Johnny Dooit quickly builds a sand-boat with runners, a mast, and a sail so the travelers can cross the desert without touching its sands.

12. The Deadly Desert Crossed

Dorothy, the shaggy man, Button-Bright, Polychrome, and Toto cross the Deadly Desert in a sand-boat made by Johnny Dooit. The boat speeds dangerously over the sand, and the shaggy man cannot slow it when they approach rocks at the desert's edge. The crash throws everyone onto a grassy tableland, but they are mostly unharmed and find the sand-boat destroyed. They believe they have reached the Land of Oz and decide to look for people who can guide them to the Emerald City.

13. The Truth Pond

Dorothy, the Shaggy Man, Button-Bright, and Polychrome discover the Truth Pond in the yellow Country of the Winkies. Button-Bright falls into the pond and is restored from a fox head to his original face, and the Shaggy Man then dives in and loses his donkey head. Dorothy explains that the Land of Oz has four colored countries, with the Emerald City at their center, and the travelers resume their journey there.

14. Tik-Tok and Billina

Dorothy and her companions meet Tik-Tok and Billina on a road through the Country of the Winkies. Billina tells Dorothy that she has hatched ten chicks, all named Dorothy, while Tik-Tok explains that Princess Ozma sent them to welcome the travelers and invite them to the Emerald City. The group learns that Ozma is preparing a grand birthday celebration and plans to spend the night at Nick Chopper's new castle before reaching the Emerald City the next day. From a hilltop, they see the castle's tin towers, steeples, domes, and gables shining in the setting sun.

15. The Emperor's Tin Castle

Nick Chopper welcomes Dorothy and her companions to his new tin castle, whose grounds contain tin statues of Dorothy, Toto, the Scarecrow, the Wizard, Ozma, Tik-tok, and others. He explains that the Land of Oz has no money and that people provide for one another through love, kindness, and the desire to please. The guests enjoy a tin dinner accompanied by a tin-instrument orchestra, while Polychrome dances after tasting dewdrops. The next morning, Nick Chopper joins the travelers on their journey to the Emerald City, and the Winkies cheerfully see their beloved Emperor depart.

16. Visiting the Pumpkin-Field

Dorothy, Button-Bright, Tik-tok, and the Tin Woodman travel through a large pumpkin-field in the yellow country of the Winkies. They visit Jack Pumpkinhead, who lives in a hollow pumpkin and explains that his previous pumpkin heads spoiled and were buried. Jack says he farms pumpkins so he will always have a replacement head and plans to attend Ozma's party in the Emerald City. The travelers leave Jack to continue their journey.

17. The Royal Chariot Arrives

The Shaggy Man, Tin Woodman, Dorothy, and Polychrome travel toward the Emerald City through the prosperous Winkie country. The Tin Woodman explains the history of the Powder of Life and how it brought Jack Pumpkinhead, the Saw-Horse, the Flying Gump, and a blue bear rug to life. At the Emerald City’s boundary, Polychrome encounters the Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger, whom Dorothy happily greets and introduces to her. The travelers are going to Ozma's party, which the Lion says will be a grand affair.

18. The Emerald City

The Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger bring Ozma's royal chariot to meet Dorothy and her companions, escorting Dorothy, Button-Bright, and Polychrome into the Emerald City. The travelers admire the City's jeweled buildings, happy inhabitants, and beautiful streets before arriving at the Royal Palace. Jellia Jamb welcomes them and tells Dorothy that Ozma will see her after she has rested and changed, and that Ozma's birthday is the day after tomorrow.

19. The Shaggy Man's Welcome

The shaggy man is respectfully escorted to luxurious apartments in Princess Ozma's palace and welcomed as an honored guest. He bathes, changes into an elegant shaggy outfit, selects jewelry from a box prepared for him, and waits to meet Princess Ozma. Meanwhile, Dorothy dresses Button-Bright and brings him and Toto to the drawing-room, where Princess Ozma waits eagerly to welcome Dorothy.

20. Princess Ozma Of Oz

Ozma welcomes Dorothy, Button-Bright, Polychrome, the Wizard of Oz, and the shaggy man to her palace before her birthday celebration. She reveals that she arranged the roads to guide Dorothy toward Oz and watched her journey in the Magic Picture. The shaggy man admits that he stole the Love Magnet and explains that he used it because he wanted to be loved. Ozma decides to hang the Love Magnet over the gates of the Emerald City, while the Wizard entertains the group with trained piglets.

21. Dorothy Receives the Guests

Dorothy welcomes Polly, the shaggy man, Button-Bright, and Toto to breakfast before receiving Ozma's invited guests in the royal palace. The Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, Glinda the Good, Billina, Tik-tok, and other familiar figures arrive for Ozma's birthday preparations. King John Dough, Chick the Cherub, and Para Bruin arrive from Hiland and Loland with a gingerbread crown for Ozma. Dorothy and the assembled company greet the foreign visitors and make them feel welcome.

22. Important Arrivals

Santa Claus arrives at Princess Ozma's birthday celebration with Ryls and Knooks from the Happy Valley and the Forest of Burzee, bringing Ozma presents. The Queen of Merryland, the Braided Man, the royal family of Ev, King Renard of Foxville, Queen Zixi of Ix, King Bud of Noland, and Princess Fluff also arrive with gifts and greetings. Dorothy reunites with many old acquaintances and prepares with Button-Bright for the evening banquet. Several guests remain in the Throne-Room while others withdraw to prepare for the celebration.

23. The Grand Banquet

Ozma hosts a magnificent royal banquet in Oz to honor her birthday, with Santa Claus, John Dough, Queen Zixi, King Bud, royal guests, animals, and other unusual companions in attendance. Santa Claus sits at the seat of honor and proposes a toast to Ozma's health and happiness. The guests enjoy a lavish feast and entertainments including poetry, magic, dancing, music, drills, and performances by the animals. The banquet lasts until late evening and serves as an introduction to the birthday celebration planned for the next morning.

24. The Birthday Celebration

Princess Ozma's birthday is celebrated in the Emerald City with a grand procession, a pavilion full of entertainments, and guests from many lands. The Wizard of Oz conducts performances including juggling, while the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Glinda, the Good Witch of the North, Johnny Dooit, and Para Bruin provide additional acts. The Wizard's giant soap bubbles carry several visitors home, including Button-Bright and Santa Claus, while Polychrome returns home by riding a rainbow. After the celebration, Dorothy, Toto, and the Shaggy Man remain in Oz for a time, although Dorothy and Toto are later sent by the Magic Belt to Kansas.
