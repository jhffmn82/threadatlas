Also called: The busy Adrastus

Abstract written by a model

Adrastus is a busy, prolific writer praised for unusual breadth. He publishes essays on tri-lingual inscriptions, the Idea of the Infinite among the prehistoric Lapps, the Colorado beetle, and grape disease in the south of France. His established name secures space denied to specialized researchers such as Theron.

XIV.

Adrastus publishes essays on subjects ranging from ancient inscriptions and prehistoric philosophy to agricultural pests and disease. His breadth and enormous productivity are generally praised, while his established name helps him obtain space that is denied to more specialized researchers such as Theron.
