Hugh Boone in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Hugh Boone is the fabricated public identity and disguise used by Neville St. Clair: a scarred, red-haired, limping professional beggar who appears to trade in wax vestas and receives charity in a greasy leather cap. For years he sits daily on Threadneedle Street with matches on his lap, while lodging on the second floor of an opium den. Though presented as crippled, he is apparently powerful. After Neville St. Clair disappears, Boone is found at the premises, denies that anyone else was in the front room or that he knows St. Clair, and is arrested as the supposed murderer. Police find no incriminating object; blood on his sleeve is attributed to a finger cut, and he claims the clothes in his room are mysterious. In a Bow Street cell, Holmes removes Boone's makeup and wig, revealing St. Clair.
