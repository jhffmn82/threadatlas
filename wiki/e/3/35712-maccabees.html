Maccabees in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

The Maccabees rescued Jewish religion, national identity, and independence from Syrian Greek rule through heroic resistance, inspiring later preservation and development of Jewish national life through legal administration and public teaching.

The Maccabees are credited with rescuing Jewish religion and national independence from the corrupting rule of the Syrian Greeks. Their heroic struggle stimulated later efforts to preserve and develop Jewish national life through legal administration and public teaching.
