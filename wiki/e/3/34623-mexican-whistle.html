Mexican whistle

Also called: small whistle formed of baked clay, whistle

Abstract written by a model

The Mexican whistle is a small baked-clay instrument, often shaped as a grotesque human figure, bird, beast, or flower. Some have a top finger-hole producing two tones; others contain a loose clay ball whose vibration creates a shrill, whirring sound when blown. It most likely served mainly for signaling.

CHAPTER VI.

The Mexican whistle was a small baked-clay instrument, often shaped as a grotesque human, animal, bird, or flower. Some had a finger-hole that produced two tones, while others contained a loose clay ball that made a shrill, whirring sound when the whistle was blown; it most likely served mainly for signaling.
