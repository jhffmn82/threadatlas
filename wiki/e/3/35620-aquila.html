Aquila is an agreeable, amusing, brilliant talker whose fluency and appearance make others credit him with knowledge. He habitually and innocently appropriates conversational ideas, clever statements, and allusions, building showy information despite probably not reading epoch-making modern books. He improves borrowed material and turns corrections into apparent continuations without admitting error.

XI.

Aquila is an agreeable and amusing conversationalist who habitually appropriates information and formulations offered by other people. Although he has not read many epoch-making modern books, he gathers clever statements and allusions through conversation and gradually improves them without admitting mistakes or corrections. His fluency and appearance lead others to credit him with knowledge, as shown when he turns a correction about the product of nine thirteens into an apparently intentional continuation of his argument.
