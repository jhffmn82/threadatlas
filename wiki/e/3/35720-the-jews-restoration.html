the Jews' restoration in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

The Jews' restoration is a possible national restoration, formerly interpreted in evangelical orthodoxy as fulfillment of prophecy. Its realization depends not on wealthy Jews leaving European homes, but on worthy leadership and a sufficiently strong communal desire to return.

The Jews' restoration was formerly associated in evangelical orthodoxy with the fulfillment of prophecy, though that interpretation is said to be less common. The text treats restoration as dependent not on wealthy Jews abandoning their European homes, but on the emergence of worthy leaders and a sufficiently strong communal desire to return.
