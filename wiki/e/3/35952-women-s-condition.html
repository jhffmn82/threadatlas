Women’s condition is wretched in marriage and domestic dependence: women must purchase husbands as masters and risk bad matches, while men can leave home and seek relief among friends.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MEDEA.

Medea argues that women are the most wretched of living beings because they must purchase a husband, accept him as their master, and risk receiving either a good or a bad man. She contrasts women's restricted choices with men's ability to leave home and seek relief among friends.
