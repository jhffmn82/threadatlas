XV.

Male vanity is described as human and native, like female vanity, but denser, less volatile, and more massive when encountered. It may appear in pamphlets presented as useful doctrine, in efforts to display literary talent, or in elaborate attention to clothing, posture, gait, and the admiration of beholders.
