Nightingale in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

The Nightingale is an admired, popular bird, possibly confused about its identity, that joins fluttering after a claim it made the honeycomb and does not positively deny it.

The Nightingale takes part in the fluttering that follows the suggestions that a popular bird made the honeycomb. The Nightingale does not give a positive denial, perhaps because of confusion about identity.
