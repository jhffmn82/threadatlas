Recomp

method · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Recomp extractive summarization method

Abstract written by a model

Recomp is a summarization method with extractive and abstractive compressors: one selects useful sentences, and the other synthesizes information from multiple documents. It preserves original-content integrity, addresses generator maximum-length constraints, and is recommended for outstanding performance, including on NQ, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA, in Best Performance and Balanced Efficiency practices.

Recomp is in one work on this site, Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Summarization

Recomp has extractive and abstractive compressors: the extractive compressor selects useful sentences, while the abstractive compressor synthesizes information from multiple documents. It shows outstanding performance on NQ, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA, and is recommended.

Comprehensive Evaluation .. Results and Analysis

Recomp is an extractive summarization method that outperformed LongLLMLingua in the evaluation. It preserves the integrity of the original content and was preferred for cases where the generator's maximum length constraint must be addressed.

Discussion / Best Practices for Implementing RAG .. Multimodal Extension

Recomp is the recommended summarization method in both the Best Performance Practice and the Balanced Efficiency Practice.
