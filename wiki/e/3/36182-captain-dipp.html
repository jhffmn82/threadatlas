16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

Captain Dipp commands the Spoon Brigade and brings Dorothy, Billina, and Toto to King Kleaver as prisoners. He admits that Dorothy has done nothing and later leads the Brigade after the released travelers until the forest’s edge.
