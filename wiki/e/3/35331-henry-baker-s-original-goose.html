Abstract written by a model

Henry Baker's original goose is a white, fat Christmas goose carried by Henry Baker and marked for Mrs. Henry Baker. After Baker abandons it, Peterson takes it to Sherlock Holmes, who recovers it. The blue carbuncle is found in its crop. Holmes and Watson then eat it, while its feathers, legs, crop, and other remains are retained.

Henry Baker's original goose is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The Christmas goose is carried by Henry Baker and marked with a card reading “For Mrs. Henry Baker.” Peterson takes it to Sherlock Holmes after Baker abandons it, and its crop is found to contain the blue carbuncle before the goose is eaten.

I had been delayed at a case, and it was a little after half-past six

Henry Baker's original goose was recovered by Sherlock Holmes but was eaten because it would otherwise have been of no use. Its feathers, legs, crop, and other remains were retained.
