Abstract written by a model

is_treated_as diffusive [by Euphorion] The unit treats intellectual property as a form of spiritual or mental production whose ownership is often obscured by the shared inheritance of human thought. It acknowledges that ideas develop through collective preparation and transmission, but rejects using this truth to excuse the non-payment of identifiable intellectual debts. Public recognition tends to favor prominent names, while less fashionable or obscure sources may be used privately without acknowledgment.
