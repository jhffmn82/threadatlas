Pop Over

Also called: Mr. Over, Pop

Abstract written by a model

Pop Over, a bun man, owns a waffle back fence, shortcake piano, and pumps. When Dorothy arrives, he offers the fence so she need not consume Bunbury people, provides milk, leads her to his house, and his wife brings a baked-dough pail.

17. How They Came to Bunbury

Pop Over offers Dorothy his waffle back fence so she can eat without consuming any of the Bunbury people. He also offers her a shortcake piano and provides milk from his milk pump. He leads Dorothy to his house and has his wife bring a baked-dough pail for the milk.
