CHAPTER VII.

An Anglo-Saxon fiddle, or fithele, from an eleventh-century manuscript has a pear-shaped body and four strings, although no bridge is shown. Fiddle-bow use is recorded in Germany in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, and Peterborough cathedral contains a possibly twelfth-century depiction whose instrument resembles the modern violin.
