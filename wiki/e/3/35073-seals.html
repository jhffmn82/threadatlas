seals in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Seals are a disorderly family on the undiscovered rocky island, led by Chief Muffruff. They sleep, sun themselves, crawl, bark, and play; use strong fins on land; eat sea-gulls and eggs; quarrel with turtles, which drive them into the water.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Many seals live on the rocky island, where they sleep, sun themselves, crawl about, and bark. The young seals are active and playful, and seals feed on sea-gulls and their eggs. They quarrel with the turtles and are driven into the water after the turtles attack them.
