reflection tokens in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17

Reflection tokens are special tokens added to the model vocabulary and used by SELF-RAG. Generated alongside task outputs, they create reflections on retrieved passages and the model’s own generations. They matter because they make the language model controllable during inference: they signal whether retrieval is needed, evaluate generation quality, and critique relevance, support, and utility. They are categorized as retrieval tokens or critique tokens and include Retrieve, ISREL, ISSUP, and ISUSE. During training they are inserted into outputs; during inference the model generates them, and their probabilities can guide customizable decoding and control critique effects on generation sampling. They help tailor behavior to diverse task requirements; predicted ISREL and ISSUP were mostly aligned with human assessments.

ABSTRACT

Reflection tokens are special tokens used by SELF-RAG to generate reflections on retrieved passages and the model's own generations. They make the language model controllable during inference, allowing it to tailor its behavior to diverse task requirements.

INTRODUCTION

Reflection tokens are special tokens added to the model vocabulary and generated alongside task outputs. They indicate retrieval needs and generation quality, and their probabilities can guide customizable decoding.

RELATED WORK

SELF-RAG uses reflection tokens to decide whether retrieval is needed and to self-evaluate generation quality. These tokens also enable controlled generation at inference and improve generation quality and attributions.
