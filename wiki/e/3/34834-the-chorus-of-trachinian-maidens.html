Abstract written by a model

The Chorus of Trachinian Maidens is a community of women from Trachis sympathetic to Deianira. It counsels and consoles her, urging hope, affirming her faithfulness, and recalling that joy and sorrow alternate. It celebrates Heracles’ expected return and reported victory, accepts Deianira’s plan despite unproven confidence, then recognizes Lichas’s concealment and the robe’s danger. It judges her error unwitting, mourns her death and Heracles’ suffering, and laments the household’s ruin by divine and fated forces.

THE TRACHINIAN MAIDENS

The Chorus comforts Dêanira by urging her not to abandon hope and by affirming that joy and sorrow alternate in mortal life. It celebrates the report of Heracles' victory, but later recognizes the danger in Lichas's concealment of the truth.

CHORUS.

The Chorus first celebrates Heracles' expected return and recalls that Deianira has long suffered in fear and separation. It accepts Deianira's plan to use the Centaur's gift, though it notes that her confidence lacks proof. After the robe's destructive effect is revealed, it acknowledges her fear but says that an unwitting error lessens the basis for wrath.

CHORUS.

The Chorus mourns Deianira's death, Heracles's suffering, and the ruin of his household. It fears the arrival of Heracles and laments the destruction brought about by divine and fated forces.
