Oedipus's abandonment

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: I found thee, Oedipus’s abandonment as an infant, a gift, babe, found in Cithaeron's hollowy wilderness, foundling, gave it thee, gave thee me, make away with it, the child, the infant
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, it is Oedipus’s abandonment as an infant on Cithaeron after his feet were pierced, ordered by Jocasta but thwarted by the Theban Shepherd’s pity.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, it is Oedipus’s abandonment as an infant, when Laius’s Herdsman spared him and passed him to the Corinthian Messenger, who gave him to Polybus and Merope.

Oedipus's abandonment in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

Oedipus was abandoned as an infant on Cithaeron after his feet were pierced. Jocasta ordered the Theban Shepherd to destroy him because of the prophecy about the child's future, but the shepherd pitied him and passed him to the Corinthian Shepherd.
