the wedding of Lord St. Simon and Hatty Doran in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The wedding of Lord St. Simon and Hatty Doran is a quiet London ceremony at St. George's, Hanover Square, attended by a small party including the Duchess of Balmoral and Mrs. Francis Hay Moulton. It is followed by a breakfast at Aloysius Doran's Lancaster Gate residence. Lord Robert St. Simon keeps it quiet for fear that Flora Millar will cause a scandal. Hatty is cheerful until she sees Francis Hay Moulton in the first pew; after dropping her bouquet, she receives his note, becomes agitated, abandons the wedding party, and follows him from the house.

The wedding takes place at St. George's, Hanover Square, with only a small group of relatives and friends present. The party then proceeds to Aloysius Doran's house at Lancaster Gate for breakfast, but the occasion is disrupted by Miss Flora Millar and Hatty Doran's subsequent disappearance.
