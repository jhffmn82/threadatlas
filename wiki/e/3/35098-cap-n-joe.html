Cap'n Joe

Abstract written by a model

Cap'n Joe Weedles, Cap'n Bill's long-lost brother and formerly a sailor on the brig Gladsome, is found after a shipwreck by Zog. Zog gives him gills, leaving him unable to breathe air, and keeps him as an underwater slave sewing buttons on Zog's clothes. Though Joe calls the work, rations, and later life easy and peaceful, he warns that no one escapes Zog. After Cap'n Joe's election, he becomes elected King of the Castle and rules the former slaves.

Cap'n Joe is in one work on this site, The Sea Fairies. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Cap'n Joe Weedles is Cap'n Bill's long-lost brother and a former sailor of the brig Gladsome. Zog gave him gills after finding him following a shipwreck, and Joe now lives underwater as a slave who sews buttons onto Zog's clothes. He says the work is easy and the rations are fair, but warns that Zog has never allowed anyone to escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Cap'n Joe agrees to rule the former slaves if they want him to and is elected King of the Castle. He tells Cap'n Bill that his new life is easy and peaceful.
