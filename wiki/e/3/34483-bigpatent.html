BigPatent in Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory

Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) · 2023-12-23 · the work’s page · read it where BigPatent first appears

Abstract written by a model

BigPatent is a large summarization evaluation dataset of 1.3 million U.S. patent records, pairing official patent documents with human-written abstractive summaries. Its documents have considerable similarity, making memory beneficial. It is used to evaluate text generation and summarization: BARTbase initializes a generator, while BARTjoint with self-memory/Selfmem reaches 62.9 ROUGE-1, alongside 48.1 ROUGE-2 and 59.6 ROUGE-L.
