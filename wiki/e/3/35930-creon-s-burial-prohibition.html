Creon's burial prohibition

law · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Creon’s interdict, death-warrant, determination of Eteocles, forbidden by the state, injunctions, laws, the established law, the interdict, the law, these human laws, these things shall be proclaimed, thy orders
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Creon's burial prohibition declares that anyone who crowns Polynices' corpse or covers it with earth will receive death, and forbids Antigone from bathing the body, bandaging its wounds, or otherwise showing it respect.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Creon's interdict forbids Antigone's burial of her brother, which she knowingly breaks, and Creon treats her disobedience as a crime deserving death.

Creon's burial prohibition declares that anyone who crowns Polynices' corpse or covers it with earth will receive death. Creon also forbids Antigone from bathing the body, bandaging its wounds, or otherwise showing it respect.
