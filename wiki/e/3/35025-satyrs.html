Abstract written by a model

Satyrs are attendant daemons of Dionysus, forming his revel rout. Combining human or divine and animal features, especially horses or wild goats, they embody joyous, instinctive nature, drunken absurdity, and mysterious wild joy; ancient religion connects them with resurrection of the dead.

INTRODUCTION

Satyrs are attendant daemons of Dionysus who form the revel rout and combine human or divine features with those of animals, especially horses or wild goats. They represent joyous, instinctive creatures unconstrained by law or self-control, expressing both the absurdity of half-drunken revellers and the mysterious joy of wild nature.
