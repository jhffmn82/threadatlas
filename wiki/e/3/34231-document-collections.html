2 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Medical corpora were evaluated in combination with retrievers and backbone LLMs. The results indicate that combining various medical corpora and retrievers achieves the best performance.

The MEDRAG Toolkit

The corpora component contains data from PubMed, StatPearls, Textbooks, and Wikipedia. Each corpus is divided into short snippets, and the sources are also combined into MedCorp for cross-source retrieval.
