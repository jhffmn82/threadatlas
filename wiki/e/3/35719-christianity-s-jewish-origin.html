Christianity's Jewish origin in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Christianity's Jewish origin is a substantial, genealogical reality, attested by Jesus Christ's concern for Israel and the Apostle of the Gentiles' kinship with Israel. It unfolds through references to the Jewish heart of Christ and the Apostle's attachment to Jewish kinsmen, making respect for the Jewish people central.

Christianity's Jewish origin is supported through references to the Jewish heart of Christ in Matthew and to the Apostle of the Gentiles' attachment to his Jewish kinsmen. The text presents this origin as central to understanding Christianity and as a reason to respect the Jewish people.
