wisdom and reverence toward the gods

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: It is not right for Gods to resemble mortals in anger, a wise understanding in respect to the Gods, extol the Gods, if there is any one who despises the Gods, if ye had known how to be wise, to be wise, and to reverence the Gods, to reverence things honorable, wisdom, wisdom and reverence for the Gods

Abstract written by a model

Wisdom and reverence toward the gods is a mortal virtue and the most honorable, wisest way of life. It requires respecting divine law and avoiding plans beyond mortal limits, bringing freedom from grief. The Chorus contrasts it with Pentheus's forceful resistance to a god; the Messenger and Bacchus conclude that Cadmus's family would have been happy through such wisdom and acceptance.
