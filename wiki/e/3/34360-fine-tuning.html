fine-tuning in LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering

Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, Jie Tang · 2024

Fine-tuning improves RAG components, scales LLM context lengths, and can markedly enhance LLM performance with limited high-quality data. It supports collaborative tuning of multiple components at minimal data expense, but generally has higher training and data costs than non-fine-tuned context scaling.

generator fine-tuning in RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment

Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024

Generator fine-tuning uses preference alignment with supervised fine-tuning on inputs, preferred responses, and non-preferred responses. It adds domain knowledge, improves context use, and teaches preferred rationale-and-answer style; in unified fine-tuning, it accompanies retriever improvement through generation signals.

Related Work / RAG Fine-tuning .. Self-Alignment

Fine-tuning has been used to enhance retrieval-augmented generation by improving retrievers with generation signals and improving generators' use of context. The described work applies unified fine-tuning to both the generator and retriever.

Generator fine-tuning uses preference data consisting of an input, a preferred response, and a non-preferred response. It aims to add domain knowledge, improve context utilization, and teach the preferred rationale-and-answer output style.

Generator Fine-tuning in Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xiaohua Wang, Zhenghua Wang, Xuan Gao, Feiran Zhang, Yixin Wu, Zhibo Xu, Tianyuan Shi, Zhengyuan Wang, Shizheng Li, Qi Qian, Ruicheng Yin, Changze Lv, Xiaoqing Zheng, Xuanjing Huang · 2024

Generator Fine-tuning is a method for LLM generators that studies how relevant and irrelevant contexts affect performance, leaving retriever fine-tuning for future exploration. It evaluates validation sets with Dg, Dr, Dgr, and D∅, trains on relevant, random, mixed, and duplicated contexts, and finds relevant plus a few random documents best.

Generator Fine-tuning

Generator Fine-tuning investigates how relevant and irrelevant contexts affect generator performance while retriever fine-tuning is left for future exploration. It trains models with different compositions of relevant, random, mixed, and duplicated contexts.

Limitations

The study evaluates the impact of various methods for fine-tuning LLM generators.
