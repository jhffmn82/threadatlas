the hall window of Holder's house in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The hall window of Holder's house is the entry point for the coronet theft, where Mary hands Sir George the coronet and Arthur passes during the pursuit; Holmes finds passage evidence there.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“For Heaven’s sake, tell me, then, what is this extraordinary mystery!”

The hall window is where Mary hands the coronet to Sir George and where Arthur later exits and re-enters during the pursuit. Holmes finds evidence on its sill and framework that someone passed through it.
