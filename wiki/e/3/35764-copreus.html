Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE CHILDREN OF HERCULES.

Copreus comes from Argos as Eurystheus’ herald to seize the children of Hercules and return them to Mycene. He argues that Argos may execute its own subjects and threatens Athens with war, but Demophoon rejects his demands. Copreus withdraws, declaring that he will return with an armed Argive force.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE HERACLIDÆ.

Copreus, an Argive herald sent by Eurystheus, attempts to remove the Heraclids from the altar by force. After Demophoon prevents him, he threatens to return with a large Argive army and Eurystheus.
