Flutterbudget Center is a large hilltop town and Defensive Settlement of Oz, housing people sent there after becoming Flutterbudgets; its inhabitants are excessively fearful and anxious.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

23. How They Encountered the Flutterbudgets

Flutterbudget Center is a large village built on top of a hill and is one of the Defensive Settlements of Oz. People who become Flutterbudgets are sent there to live. Its inhabitants are shown to be excessively fearful and anxious.
