Eurysakes in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

Eurysakes, child of Aias, is brought to his father’s body, protected by Teucer, and told to guard it during a burial dispute.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

_The scene changes to a lonely wooded spot._

Eurysakes is brought by Tecmessa to Aias's body. Teucer tells him to kneel, embrace his father, hold his hair against the corpse, and guard it until Teucer returns.
