Hecuba's dream

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Hecuba’s dream, black-winged phantom, dream, dreams, nightly vision, strange event, that dreadful apparition, the dream, the vision I abhor, vision, vision of mine eyes

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Hecuba's dream includes Achilles's apparition at Achilles's tomb and shows a dappled hind being torn from her bosom by a blood-stained wolf while Achilles's specter demands a Trojan woman. In Hecuba and other plays, Hecuba's dream shows a wolf tearing a dappled hind from her arms, warns her concerning Polydore and Polyxena, and includes Achilles' ghost demanding a Trojan captive.
