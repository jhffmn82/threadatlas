Hecuba's dream

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Hecuba’s dream, black-winged phantom, dream, dreams, nightly vision, strange event, that dreadful apparition, the dream, the vision I abhor, vision, vision of mine eyes
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Hecuba's dream shows a wolf tearing a dappled hind from her arms, warns her concerning Polydore and Polyxena, and includes Achilles' ghost demanding a Trojan captive.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Hecuba's dream includes Achilles's apparition at Achilles's tomb and shows a dappled hind being torn from her bosom by a blood-stained wolf while Achilles's specter demands a Trojan woman.

Hecuba’s dream in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Hecuba dreams of a wolf tearing a dappled hind from her arms, and she understands the vision as a warning concerning Polydore and Polyxena. She also sees Achilles’ ghost demanding a Trojan captive.
