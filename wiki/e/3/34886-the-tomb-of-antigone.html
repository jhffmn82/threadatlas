Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, the cave-like tomb is the strong, deep sepulchral prison-house where Creon confines Antigone alive to avert the city’s pollution and leave her fate to Death. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the cave tomb is the isolated rock-hewn place where Antigone is to be walled up and left alone, a dwelling where she may die or live in solitude. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the tomb of Antigone is Antigone’s rock-hewn grave, bridal bower, prison house, and everlasting home among the dead, where she is found dead and Haemon dies beside her.

the cave-like tomb in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · 449 to 429 BC · the work’s page · read it where the cave-like tomb first appears

Abstract written by a model

A strong, deep, cave-like sepulchral prison-house, it confines Antigone alive with provisions by Creon’s order, away from human footsteps. Intended to avert the city’s pollution while leaving her fate to Death, the entombment is later called a godless act provoking powers beneath.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

Creon orders Antigone to be confined alive in a cave-like tomb away from human footsteps. She is to receive enough provisions to prevent the city's pollution, and Creon says she may there discover whether Death will spare her.

CHORUS

Antigone's tomb is a strong, deep vault where Creon orders her to be enclosed alive, either to die or to continue living in a sepulchral cell. Tiresias says that her entombment is a godless act that provokes the powers beneath.

the tomb of Antigone in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912 · the work’s page · read it where the tomb of Antigone first appears

Abstract written by a model

Antigone’s tomb is a rock-hewn cavern and rocky cell, figured as her grave, bridal bower, prison house, and everlasting home among the dead. She expects reunion with her dead family there. Its course ends with Antigone found dead inside and Haemon dying beside her.
