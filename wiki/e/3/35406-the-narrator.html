the narrator in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A headstrong commissioned machine examiner, paid fifty guineas and knowledgeable about hydraulic machinery, travels from Reading to Eyford to inspect Colonel Lysander Stark’s press. Trapped inside it, he escapes with Elise’s help after losing his thumb, returns near Eyford station, and reports the case to Sherlock Holmes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

The narrator travels by train to Eyford and is taken by carriage to Colonel Lysander Stark’s residence. He examines Stark’s machine, is trapped inside the hydraulic press, escapes with Elise’s help, and returns to the vicinity of Eyford railway station after suffering a severe injury to his thumb.
