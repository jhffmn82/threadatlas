Abstract written by a model

Escape from Zog's castle is a difficult, planned attempt by Queen Aquareine and a little band of fugitives to leave the dangerous castle. Cap'n Joe cannot help because magic surrounds it and no slave has escaped, though Sacho may know more. The captives consider routes through a window, the dome opening, the maze, and the tunnel, but guards, sea devils, constant watch, and the lack of night make escape perilous. Aquareine decides they must act before Zog strengthens his defenses. Using the enchanted golden sword, she breaks a window and leads the group swimming through the vast dome toward its guarded opening and the ocean.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Cap'n Joe does not know how to help the prisoners escape because magic surrounds the castle and no slave has escaped Zog. He suggests that Sacho, who knows a great deal, may be able to help them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

The captives consider breaking a window and swimming upward to an opening in the dome, which they think would lead back to the ocean more quickly than the winding passages. Queen Aquareine advises waiting for a time when they are not closely watched.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Aquareine decides that the group must attempt to escape before Zog can devise stronger ways to destroy them. Their escape is difficult because the great dome's opening and every other entrance are guarded, and there is no night to conceal their movement.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

The group considers returning through the maze and tunnel to the open ocean, but the sea devils would likely force them back. Aquareine instead plans to use the charmed golden sword to overcome the guards and secure their escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Queen Aquareine leads the escape from Zog's castle after deciding that remaining there is too dangerous. She breaks a window with the golden sword, and the group swims into the vast dome toward its guarded opening.
