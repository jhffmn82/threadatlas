Abstract written by a model

Charles McCarthy's murder occurred at Boscombe Pool, in the adjoining wood. He suffered repeated blunt-force head injuries; James McCarthy's gun butt was considered a possible weapon after being found near the body. Holmes ultimately concluded that a left-handed man struck from behind with a stone.

IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

Charles McCarthy's murder was apparently committed with repeated blows from a heavy, blunt weapon. The injuries could have been inflicted by the butt-end of James McCarthy’s gun, which was found near the body.
