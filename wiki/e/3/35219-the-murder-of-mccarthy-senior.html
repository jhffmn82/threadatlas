IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

Charles McCarthy's murder was apparently committed with repeated blows from a heavy, blunt weapon. The injuries could have been inflicted by the butt-end of James McCarthy’s gun, which was found near the body.

There was no rain, as Holmes had foretold, and the morning broke bright

The murder occurred at Boscombe Pool and was caused by a blow from a stone. Holmes concludes that the attack was delivered from behind by a left-handed man.
