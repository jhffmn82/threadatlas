public spirit

Abstract written by a model

Public spirit is conduct governed by general welfare across every class, opposing indifference excused by private salvation or advantage, yet unfolding with a warning that political agitation must not displace competent bread-winning work.

XVI.

Public spirit should penetrate every class and influence every person's conduct, preventing private salvation or private advantage from becoming an excuse for indifference to general welfare. Public spirit that causes people to neglect their ordinary bread-winning work in order to pursue agitation would itself be harmful.
