Dundee in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Dundee is a seaport and the postmark source of warning letters. In January 1885, Joseph Openshaw receives a letter from Dundee containing five orange pips and instructions about papers on the sundial. Holmes later finds the Lone Star there, linking its crew to the persecution.
