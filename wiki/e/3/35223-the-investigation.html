The investigation concerns the murder of McCarthy senior. After Inspector Lestrade refers the case, Sherlock Holmes examines Hatherley Farm and Boscombe Pool, testing James McCarthy’s account against physical traces and final words, then identifies John Turner as the criminal and declares the mystery solved.

IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

The investigation is undertaken by Sherlock Holmes after Inspector Lestrade refers the case to him. Holmes intends to examine the scene and determine whether James McCarthy’s account can be supported.
