Mr. Hosmer Angel in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Hosmer Angel is the name used by James Windibank for a disguised, seemingly shy Leadenhall Street cashier. He courts and becomes engaged to Mary Sutherland while presenting weak eyes, a weak throat, tinted glasses, facial hair, and a hesitant voice. Before their wedding, he disappears from a four-wheeler outside the church and is advertised as missing. Holmes establishes that the disappearance was staged: Windibank adopted the identity and disguise to keep Mary attached to him and deter other suitors.

III. A CASE OF IDENTITY

Mr. Hosmer Angel is described as a shy, well-dressed cashier with weak eyes, tinted glasses, and a hesitant, whispering voice. He courted Mary Sutherland, became engaged to her, and disappeared from a four-wheeler outside the church on the day they were to marry.

Sherlock Holmes sat silent for a few minutes with his fingertips still

Hosmer Angel is presented as a missing bridegroom, but Holmes establishes that he was James Windibank in disguise. The disguise included tinted glasses, a moustache, bushy whiskers, and an altered voice, and the apparent disappearance was staged to keep Mary attached to him and discourage other suitors.
