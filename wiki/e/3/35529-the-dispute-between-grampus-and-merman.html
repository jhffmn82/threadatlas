the dispute between Grampus and Merman

Abstract written by a model

The dispute begins when Merman publishes a book correcting and attacking Grampus’s account of the Magicodumbras and Zuzumotzis. Though it gains attention in America, Germany, England, France, and Paris, Merman’s rejoinder does not provoke Grampus. Grampus’s followers spread ridicule through publications, lectures, journalism, popular literature, and sermons, damaging Merman’s legal and editorial career and contributing to his bitterness and decline.

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

The controversy begins with Merman's attempt to correct Grampus on matters involving the Magicodumbras and Zuzumotzis. Merman's rejoinder fails to provoke Grampus, but Grampus's followers expand the dispute into books, lectures, journalism, popular literature, and sermons, making Merman a byword for speculative error.
