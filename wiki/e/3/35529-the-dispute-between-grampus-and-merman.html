III.

The dispute begins when Merman publishes a book attacking Grampus's account of the Magicodumbras and Zuzumotzis. It attracts attention in America, Germany, England, and France, but public interest focuses mainly on the personal ridicule directed at Merman rather than on the historical issues.

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

The controversy begins with Merman's attempt to correct Grampus on matters involving the Magicodumbras and Zuzumotzis. Merman's rejoinder fails to provoke Grampus, but Grampus's followers expand the dispute into books, lectures, journalism, popular literature, and sermons, making Merman a byword for speculative error.
