II. THE RED-HEADED LEAGUE

Duncan Ross interviews Jabez Wilson, verifies that his hair is genuine, and awards him the Red-Headed League vacancy. He tells Wilson to copy the Encyclopædia Britannica from ten o'clock until two and initially supervises his work before appearing less frequently.
