VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The Hotel Cosmopolitan jewel robbery concerns the disappearance of the blue carbuncle from the Countess of Morcar's jewel-case on December 22nd. John Horner is arrested and accused, but the stone is not found on him or in his rooms before it later appears in the Christmas goose.
