1 section; each heading opens the text

JASON, MEDEA, CHORUS.

The Tutor reports that Medea's sons have been reprieved from banishment and that the royal bride accepted the gifts. He encourages Medea to bear her separation from the children, unaware at first that she intends to kill them.
