Apollo's oracle

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Apollo's express command, Apollo's sovereign will, The god gave him leave, a miscalled duty, an oracle, behest from heaven, command of Apollo, oracle concerning Orestes’ vengeance, the command to avenge Agamemnon, the duty and manliness of revenge, whether he should take vengeance on his father’s murderers, will of Heaven, word from Phoebus, εἰ τοὺς αὐτόχειρας τοῦ πατρὸς μετέλθοι

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Apollo's oracle commands Orestes to avenge Agamemnon through his own craft rather than with an openly armed force. In The Electra of Euripides, the command to avenge Agamemnon drives Orestes toward a miscalled duty and creates a conflict between religious duty and the moral horror of murdering his mother. In The Library, the oracle concerning Orestes’ vengeance authorizes Orestes to avenge Agamemnon’s murderers and directs him to retrieve the Taurian wooden image.
