Zog's magic

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Zog has his grip on you, his magic power, magic, magic powers, magician's body

Abstract written by a model

Zog's magic surrounds the castle, conceals the maze through deceptive barriers, and lets Sacho and Cap'n Joe live underwater, though Joe loses the ability to breathe air on land. Zog uses it to hold prisoners and prevent slaves leaving the great cave, claims supreme power within the enchanted castle, and plans to test and overcome the mermaids.

Zog's magic is in one work on this site, The Sea Fairies. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Zog's magic enables Sacho to live underwater like a fish and makes the maze's apparent barriers deceptive. Under its influence, a solid-looking marble wall melts away when the group swims toward it, revealing a splendid chamber.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Zog says he will use his magic to test and overcome the mermaids. He claims his power is supreme within the enchanted castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Zog's magic surrounds the castle and prevents the slaves from leaving the great cave. It also enabled Zog to give Cap'n Joe gills so he could live underwater, though Joe can no longer breathe air on land.
