Abstract written by a model

The battered black felt hat is Henry Baker’s three-year-old, once very high-quality hat, marked H. B. Its red silk lining is discoloured; it is cracked, dusty, tallow-stained, and missing its elastic securer. Lost when roughs assaulted Baker, it is examined by Sherlock Holmes, whose observations of its size, lining, hair, odors, dust, moisture marks, and stains lead him to infer Baker’s characteristics. Holmes retains it with the goose, then returns it to Baker.

VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The battered black felt hat bears the initials H. B. and is old, cracked, dusty, stained, and missing its elastic hat-securer. Sherlock Holmes uses its size, lining, hair-ends, odors, dust, moisture marks, and tallow stains to infer characteristics of its owner, Henry Baker.

Henry Baker's hat was retained with the goose and returned to him by Sherlock Holmes. Baker says that a Scotch bonnet is unsuitable for his age and gravity.
