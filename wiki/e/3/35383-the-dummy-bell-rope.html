the dummy bell-rope

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a cord, a thick bell-rope, the bell-pull, the bell-rope, the rope

Abstract written by a model

The dummy bell-rope hangs beside the bed in the middle bedroom, appearing to communicate with the housekeeper’s room. Holmes discovers it is not attached to a wire or bell, but is fastened by a hook above the ventilator opening. He concludes it serves as a bridge for the snake to pass from the ventilator to the bed.
