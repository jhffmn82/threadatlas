Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

The Centaur's gift is an ancient substance that Deianira kept hidden in a bronze urn and applied to Heracles' robe. Deianira believed it would prevent Heracles from receiving another love, but the substance instead destroys the wool exposed to sunlight and contributes to Heracles' suffering.
