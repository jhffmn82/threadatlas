the attempted killing of the narrator in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The attempted killing begins after Colonel Lysander Stark’s hydraulic press is identified for its true purpose. Stark locks the narrator inside and activates the descending ceiling to crush him; after escape, he pursues the narrator with a cleaver and strikes at him at a bedroom window.
