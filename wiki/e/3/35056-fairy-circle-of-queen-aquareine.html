Fairy Circle of Queen Aquareine

object · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Fairy Circle, Magic Circle, fairy circles, magic circles, the Magic Circle, the fairy circles, the protecting magic circles

Abstract written by a model

The Fairy Circle of Queen Aquareine is a protective fairy charm created when Queen Aquareine swims around Trot and Cap'n Bill in opposite-direction circles with her wand. It prevents enemies from touching those it protects and turns aside attacks during an underwater journey. It first protects Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, Trot, and Cap'n Bill from sawfishes and their saws, then keeps sea devils from physical contact despite their contempt for the circles. Finally, it shields the party from the Yell-Maker's attacks and falling marble.

Fairy Circle of Queen Aquareine is in one work on this site, The Sea Fairies. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

[Illustration: _Chap. 8._

The Magic Circle is a fairy charm that prevents enemies from touching the person it protects. Queen Aquareine creates it by swimming around Trot and Cap'n Bill in opposite-direction circles with her wand.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

The Magic Circle protects Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, Trot, and Cap'n Bill from the sawfishes. It is powerful enough to turn aside the fishes' ugly saws and prevent them from disturbing the party.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

The fairy circles protect Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia from physical contact with the sea devils. The sea devils claim to know about the circles and say they do not think much of them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

The Fairy Circle of Queen Aquareine keeps Queen Aquareine, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Princess Clia safe from the Yell-Maker's attacks. It also prevents the falling marble from touching them.
