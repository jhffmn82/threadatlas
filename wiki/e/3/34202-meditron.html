Abstract written by a model

MEDITRON is an open-source 70B biomedical language model with 4,096-token context; it outperforms Llama-2, and MEDRAG raises MIRAGE accuracy from 57.04% to 60.18%.

MEDITRON is in one work on this site, Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The MEDRAG Toolkit

MEDITRON is an open-source, biomedical-domain 70B language model with a 4,096-token context length. Its average MIRAGE accuracy increases from 57.04% with chain-of-thought alone to 60.18% with MEDRAG.
