Abstract written by a model

A twelve-performer orchestra bas-relief, formerly ornamenting the abbey at St. Georges de Boscherville, features mostly crowned musicians, varied instruments, and a head-standing tumbler; reportedly late eleventh century, it was removed to and is preserved in the museum of Rouen.

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

It is a twelve-performer group, reportedly dating from the end of the eleventh century and now preserved in the museum of Rouen. It includes performers on viols, an organistrum, a syrinx, several stringed instruments, harps, and a tintinnabulum, with a tumbler standing on his head.
