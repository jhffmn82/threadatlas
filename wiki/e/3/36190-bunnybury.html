Bunnybury

Abstract written by a model

Bunnybury is a fairy city and town made by Glinda the Good for pink-eyed white rabbits, under Glinda’s protection and subject to Ozma of Oz. A high solid-white-marble wall encloses it, and the Keeper’s Wicket admits visitors only with Ozma’s or Glinda’s authorization; Dorothy enters when the Keeper obeys Ozma’s command. The city has a luxurious palace, gardens, laws, rabbit subjects, nobles, attendants, soldiers, entertainers, a royal Bodyguard, and magic charms and enchantments that protect its inhabitants. Its King cannot resign under its laws, but Dorothy’s visit helps its dismal King recognize the pleasure of ruling this beautiful, secure city.

Bunnybury is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

19. How Bunnybury Welcomed the Strangers

Bunnybury is enclosed by a high wall of solid white marble and admits visitors only with an order or letter of introduction from Ozma of Oz or Glinda the Good. Its entrance is guarded, and Dorothy is allowed in only after the Keeper obeys Ozma's command.

20. How Dorothy Lunched With a King

Bunnybury is a walled city created by Glinda the Good for pink-eyed white rabbits. It contains a luxurious palace, beautiful gardens, royal attendants, soldiers, laws preventing a King from resigning, and a court of nobles.

21. How the King Changed His Mind

Bunnybury is presented as a beautiful, secure city with richly dressed rabbit nobility, skilled entertainers, a royal Bodyguard, and pleasant refreshments. Its Keeper of the Wicket says Dorothy has helped bring its dismal King to recognize the pleasure of ruling the city.
