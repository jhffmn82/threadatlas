the Copper Beeches in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The Copper Beeches is a large, square, whitewashed, weather-stained country house near Winchester in Hampshire, about a hundred yards from Southampton Road, marked by copper-beech trees and surrounded by grounds and woods. It holds the Rucastle household, a kennel, a dangerous mastiff, and a locked, deserted forbidden suite. Violet Hunter accepts an unusually well-paid governess post there, where she is made to impersonate the imprisoned Alice Rucastle. Holmes and Watson investigate, find that Alice has escaped, and see Mr. Rucastle attacked by the dog.
