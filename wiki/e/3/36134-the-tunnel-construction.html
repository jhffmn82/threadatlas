Abstract written by a model

The tunnel construction, conducted by the Nomes and workmen under Roquat, proceeds slowly but steadily beneath the Deadly Desert toward the Emerald City, producing a tunnel large enough for armies; Ozma watches it daily in the Magic Picture.
