the tunnel construction in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The tunnel construction, conducted by the Nomes and workmen under Roquat, proceeds slowly but steadily beneath the Deadly Desert toward the Emerald City, producing a tunnel large enough for armies; Ozma watches it daily in the Magic Picture.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

18. How Ozma Looked into the Magic Picture

The tunnel construction proceeds slowly but steadily through the rocks beneath the Deadly Desert. Princess Ozma watches the work in the Magic Picture every day because she feels it is her duty.
