faithfulness in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024

Faithfulness is a quality dimension and metric for whether claims in a generated answer are grounded in retrieved or given context. It measures information consistency, avoids hallucinations, and penalizes claims that cannot be deduced from the context. Its score F is computed as the number of supported statements divided by the total number of statements in the answer. Evaluation compares answers with and without additional context, using a question and corresponding Wikipedia page; correct identification of Christopher Nolan and Cillian Murphy illustrates high faithfulness, while incorrect names illustrate low faithfulness. Faithfulness is one metric in example evaluation, and RAGAS’s predictions for it closely align with human judgments.

Faithfulness in Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2025-04-28

Faithfulness is a quality aspect of retrieval augmented generation systems that measures whether a generated answer’s claims are grounded in and inferable from retrieved context. It is estimated by decomposing an answer into shorter statements, assessing each against the context, and dividing supported statements by total statements; unsupported claims are penalized. It is evaluated through human comparison of answers, including standard and no-context answers. Across experiments, Ragas agreed with human assessments on 0.95 of pairwise comparisons, and the conclusions emphasize its close alignment with human predictions.

2 .. Faithfulness

Faithfulness means that the claims in a generated answer can be inferred from the given context. It is estimated by using a large language model to decompose the answer into shorter statements and assessing those statements against the context.

opening .. The WikiEval Dataset

The faithfulness score is computed as the number of statements supported by the LLM divided by the total number of statements.

opening .. Experiments

Faithfulness was assessed by comparing a standard answer with an answer generated without context, given the question and corresponding Wikipedia page. Ragas agreed with human assessments on 0.95 of the pairwise comparisons.

Conclusions

Faithfulness concerns whether an answer is grounded in the retrieved context. It is one of the quality aspects assessed by the evaluation framework and Ragas, and Ragas predictions for it closely align with human predictions.
