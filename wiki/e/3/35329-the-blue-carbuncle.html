the blue carbuncle

Abstract written by a model

The blue carbuncle is an absolutely unique, brilliantly sparkling blue precious stone from the Amoy River, weighing forty grains and worth more than £20,000. Belonging to the Countess of Morcar, it has a sinister history of crimes committed for its sake. It is stolen from her jewel-case, hidden by James Ryder in a Christmas goose, recovered by Peterson from the goose’s crop, brought to Holmes, and kept in his strong-box after being shown to Ryder.

VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The blue carbuncle is a unique, brilliantly sparkling blue precious stone belonging to the Countess of Morcar. It was stolen from her jewel-case, passed into the Christmas goose, and is recovered by Peterson from the goose's crop; Holmes keeps it in his strong box.

“So much for Mr. Henry Baker,” said Holmes when he had closed the door

The blue carbuncle was stolen from the Countess of Morcar and hidden by James Ryder inside a goose. The goose carrying it reached Holmes, who keeps the stone in his strong-box and displays it to Ryder.
