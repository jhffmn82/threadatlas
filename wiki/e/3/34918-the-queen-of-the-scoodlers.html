10. Escaping the Soup-Kettle

The Queen of the Scoodlers has a fiery red side and a bright yellow side, two faces, and a dented silver crown. She orders the captives to be put through the meat chopper and commands that the soup contain salt, onions, garlic, and red pepper. Her head is thrown into the black gulf by the shaggy man.
