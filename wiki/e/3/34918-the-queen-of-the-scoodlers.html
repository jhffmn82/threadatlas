the Queen of the Scoodlers in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

The Queen of the Scoodlers rules the Scoodlers. Lean and bony, with fiery red and bright yellow sides, two faces, and a dented, twisted silver crown, she orders captives through a meat chopper and seasons soup before the shaggy man throws her head into the black gulf.

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. Escaping the Soup-Kettle

The Queen of the Scoodlers has a fiery red side and a bright yellow side, two faces, and a dented silver crown. She orders the captives to be put through the meat chopper and commands that the soup contain salt, onions, garlic, and red pepper. Her head is thrown into the black gulf by the shaggy man.
