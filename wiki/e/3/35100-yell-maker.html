Yell-Maker in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Yell-Maker is a dreadful black crab-like sea creature with six slender legs and immense lobster-like claws. It repeatedly tries to pinch four captives, but cannot touch them; Queen Aquareine's wand makes it melt away forever.

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

The Yell-Maker is a black, crab-like sea creature with six long hinged legs ending in powerful claws. It repeatedly tries to pinch the four captives but cannot touch them, and Queen Aquareine's wand turns it into a pulpy mass that melts away forever.
