the gardens surrounding the mermaids' palaces in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Dense groves of colorful sea shrubs with entwined branches, strange fruits, and avenues lead toward the palaces; swordfishes guard entrances and drive away uninvited creatures.

The gardens are dense groves of large, brightly colored sea shrubs with entwined branches, strange fruits, and avenues leading toward the palaces. Swordfishes guard their entrances and drive away creatures that try to enter uninvited.
