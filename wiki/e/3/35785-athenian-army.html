Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the Athenian army is stationed and marshaled under Hyllus, with the Athenian chiefs occupying the left wing as it prepares for battle. In Hecuba and other plays, the Athenian army assembles to resist and defeat the Argive forces, fights for its land and reputation, and contributes to Eurystheus’s capture.
