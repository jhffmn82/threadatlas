Euripides · -0429~

The Athenian army assembles to resist the Argive forces, with chiefs informed and its left wing formed after victims are removed. Ready for battle, it fights for its land and reputation, defeats the Argive army, and contributes to Eurystheus’s capture.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

DEMOPHOON, IOLAUS, CHORUS.

The Athenian army is assembled to resist the Argive forces. Hyllus’s attendant reports that its chiefs are informed, its left wing is formed, and the troops are ready for battle after the victims have been removed from the ranks.

SERVANT, ALCMENA, CHORUS.

The Athenian army fights in defense of its land and reputation. Its forces help defeat the Argive army and contribute to the capture of Eurystheus.
