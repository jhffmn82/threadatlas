pipes and flutes in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Pipes and flutes, often preserved because pottery and bone resist decay, included whistles, small pipes, flageolet-like instruments, bone flutes, and syrinxes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Pipes and flutes are among the most frequently preserved ancient instruments of the Aztecs and Peruvians because pottery and bone resist decay. They included whistles, small pipes, flageolet-like instruments, bone flutes, and syrinxes.
