Abstract written by a model

In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Miss Hatty Doran is the only daughter of millionaire Aloysius Doran, a Californian heiress who marries Lord Robert Walsingham de Vere St. Simon, leaves the wedding breakfast, and then departs with her previously presumed-dead first husband, Francis Hay Moulton. In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Hatty Doran is Mr. Doran’s wealthy, independent daughter who marries Lord Robert St. Simon, becomes agitated after dropping her bouquet, and later leaves in an ulster and bonnet and is seen walking in Hyde Park with Flora Millar.

Miss Hatty Doran in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25 · the work’s page · read it where Miss Hatty Doran first appears

Abstract written by a model

Hatty Doran is the only daughter of millionaire Aloysius Doran, a Californian heiress from San Francisco with a dowry over six figures. She marries Lord Robert Walsingham de Vere St. Simon, then leaves the wedding breakfast without explanation. Revealed as an American woman previously married to Francis Hay Moulton, she departs after seeing him alive and goes with him to Gordon Square.

Miss Hatty Doran, the only daughter of Aloysius Doran, marries Lord Robert Walsingham de Vere St. Simon in a quiet ceremony at St. George's, Hanover Square. At the wedding breakfast she complains of sudden indisposition, goes to her room, takes an ulster and bonnet, and leaves the house without explanation.
