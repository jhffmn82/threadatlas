Saraswati in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Saraswati is a goddess of music and speech and consort of Brahma, associated with Minerva. Credited with inventing the musical scale, she is represented playing a stringed instrument while seated on a peacock.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE HINDUS.

Saraswati is the consort of Brahma and the goddess of music and speech. She is credited with inventing the systematic arrangement of sounds into a musical scale and is represented playing a stringed instrument while seated on a peacock.
