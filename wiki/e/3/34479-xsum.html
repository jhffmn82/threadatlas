XSum in Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory

Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) · 2023-12-23

XSum is an extreme, highly abstractive summarization evaluation dataset containing single-document summaries from British Broadcasting Corporation articles. It is used to evaluate text generation and abstractive summarization with ROUGE. In the reported results, memory improves it less than BigPatent, while BRIOjoint with self-memory reaches 50.3 ROUGE-1, 26.7 ROUGE-2, and 41.6 ROUGE-L.

Juntong Song, Xingguang Wang, Juno Zhu, Yuanhao Wu, Xuxin Cheng, Randy Zhong, and Cheng Niu · 2024-11

XSum is the BBC News Summary dataset, associated with Narayan et al. It provides summarization samples for preference-data construction and extends hallucination-suppression training by adapting news articles. Its original answers are replaced by selected-LLM answers, which are processed to acquire chosen samples.
