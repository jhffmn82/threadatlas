Abstract written by a model

is_caused_by Nessus's robe [through its poisonous substance] Heracles suffers repeated convulsions and extreme pain as the robe eats through his flesh to the bone and damages his vital channels. He calls for death, asks Hyllus to kill him, and says that no previous labor or enemy caused comparable torment.
