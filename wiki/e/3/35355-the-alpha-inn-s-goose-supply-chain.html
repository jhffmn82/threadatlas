The Alpha Inn’s goose supply chain links Mrs. Oakshott, Mr. Breckinridge, and Mr. Windigate and the goose club. Holmes reconstructs its route from Brixton Road through Covent Garden Market to locate the goose carrying the blue carbuncle.
