the Alpha Inn’s goose supply chain in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The Alpha Inn’s goose supply chain links Mrs. Oakshott, Mr. Breckinridge, and Mr. Windigate and the goose club. Holmes reconstructs its route from Brixton Road through Covent Garden Market to locate the goose carrying the blue carbuncle.

The Alpha Inn’s goose supply chain runs from Mrs. Oakshott at Brixton Road to Mr. Breckinridge at Covent Garden Market, then to Mr. Windigate of the Alpha Inn and its goose club. Holmes reconstructs this chain to locate the goose that carried the blue carbuncle.
