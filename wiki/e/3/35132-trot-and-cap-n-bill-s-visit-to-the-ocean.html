Abstract written by a model

Trot and Cap'n Bill make a magical, perfectly safe visit to the mermaids' underwater kingdom. Trot accepts the invitation, enters the water unharmed with Clia, and urges Cap'n Bill to follow. Princess Clia and Merla guide them from the cave through mid-water, past harmless yellowtail fish and away from sharks and devilfish, while fairy power hastens their descent to the deepest ocean palaces. As Queen Aquareine's honored guests, they stay several days, see gardens, coral buildings, and the throne room, and learn how magic enables underwater travel. The mermaids return them to the Giant's Cave, restore their regular shapes, and receive their thanks and farewell.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap 2._

Trot's visit to the mermaids begins when she accepts their invitation to explore their underwater home. Clia takes her hand, Trot enters the water without being hurt, and she excitedly urges Cap'n Bill to come along.

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

The journey begins when Princess Clia and Merla guide Trot and Cap'n Bill away from the cave and through mid-water. The travelers encounter schools of harmless yellowtail fish and learn to avoid sharks and devilfish. Fairy power speeds their progress, and they eventually begin descending toward the palaces in the deepest part of the ocean.

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

Trot and Cap'n Bill are invited to stay in the ocean palaces for several days as honored guests. During their visit they see the gardens, coral buildings, and Queen Aquareine's throne room and learn about the magic that makes underwater travel possible.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._

Trot and Cap’n Bill’s visit ends safely after the mermaids transport them to the Giant’s Cave and restore their regular shapes. They thank and say goodbye to the mermaids, and Trot concludes that mortals know less than they think and are learning every day.
