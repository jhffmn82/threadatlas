Also called: Hermione's threatened sacrifice, I will slay thy daughter, a victim, murder on murder, the meshes of our toils, the trial
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I: Electra proposes that Orestes hold Hermione as a hostage after Helen's death. If Menelaus threatens Orestes, Electra, or Pylades, they are to threaten Hermione's life; if Menelaus refuses to spare them, Orestes is to plunge the sword into Hermione's neck. Orestes threatens to kill Hermione and holds a sword at her neck while demanding that Menelaus secure his acquittal or survival. Apollo stops the threat by declaring that Hermione is destined to marry Orestes.
