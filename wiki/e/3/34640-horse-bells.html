yotl-bells in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Yotl-bells are nearly round horse bells called yotl by the Mexicans. Depicted in Aztec picture-writings as tribute paid to sovereigns, they are represented by a cluster held in the British Museum’s Mexican antiquities collection.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] The Tezcucans (or Acolhuans) belonged to the same race as the Azt

The Mexicans called their horse bells yotl. Yotl-bells were depicted in Aztec picture-writings among the objects paid as tribute to sovereigns, and a cluster of them was held in the British museum's collection of Mexican antiquities.
