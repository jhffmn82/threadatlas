mirage in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

A mirage is a reflection of an earth scene produced by mermaid powers, used by Queen Aquareine to show visitors Trot’s sleeping mother; such scenes may appear above water for mortals to observe.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

A mirage allows Queen Aquareine to bring an earthly scene before the visitors. She uses it to show Trot and Cap'n Bill that Trot's mother is asleep in the cottage, and explains that such scenes may sometimes be reflected above the water for mortals to see.
