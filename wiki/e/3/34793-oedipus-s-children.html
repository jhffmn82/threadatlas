Oedipus's children

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: children, children’s children, inmates of his home, my children, my daughters, my poor maidens, my sons, sons, the children, the pair, these maidens

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Oedipus's daughters are brought to him, whom he embraces and mourns, and for whom he asks Creon to provide protection from poverty, abandonment, and reproaches attached to their parents. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Oedipus's children share his ills, face social disgrace from their parentage, and are the children for whom he asks Creon to care, especially Antigone and Ismene.
