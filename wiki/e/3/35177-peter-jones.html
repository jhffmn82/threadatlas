Peter Jones in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Peter Jones, a Scotland Yard police agent, joins Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson’s bank expedition, brings officers to guard the entrance, seizes John Clay’s accomplice during the arrest, and handcuffs Clay.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

It was a quarter-past nine when I started from home and made my way

Peter Jones joins Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson in the expedition and brings an inspector and two officers to guard the bank’s front door. During the arrest, he grabs at John Clay’s accomplice and later places handcuffs on Clay.
