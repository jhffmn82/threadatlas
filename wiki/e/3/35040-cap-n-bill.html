  • In The Sea Fairies, Cap'n Bill is Trot's protective sailor companion and an earth guest of Queen Aquareine who helps the travelers survive their underwater dangers.
  • In Sky Island, Cap'n Bill is Trot's protector, a palace slave and Royal Nectar Mixer, and later Trot's Commander in Chief, General, and Royal Manager.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, Cap'n Bill is Trot's faithful companion who helps her survive the undersea cavern, Jinxland, and the journey to Oz, including rescuing the Scarecrow.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, Cap'n Bill is a sailor transformed into a gray grasshopper by Blinkie, who escapes, helps restore the Scarecrow, and is eventually changed back.
  • In The Magic of Oz, Cap'n Bill is Trot's companion on the quest for the Magic Flower, helping her overcome the island's enchantments and carry the flower to Ozma's birthday feast.

Cap'n Bill in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Cap'n Bill is an old, experienced sailor, formerly captain and owner of the Anemone, who lives ashore with Trot's family after losing a leg and using a wooden one. Trot's longtime companion and dear friend, he tells her seafaring stories, sails or rows her boat, loves the ocean, and is deeply protective of her. Though tough, bald, and prone to talking or muttering to himself when upset, he is wary of danger and often skeptical. When Trot visits the mermaids, Cap'n Bill follows despite fear, chiefly to look after her. Underwater he is temporarily changed into a merman with a green-scaled tail, fin, and greater mobility than his wooden leg, while retaining his sailor's clothes, pipe, tobacco, and practical habits. As Queen Aquareine's earth guest, he explores the sea, asks persistent questions, smokes underwater, and comments on its creatures and customs. The journey grows dangerous when sea devils and Zog imprison the travelers. Cap'n Bill worries constantly for Trot, offers his own life for hers, survives Zog's threats, and discovers his long-lost brother, Cap'n Joe Weedles, in the Peony Room. He helps obtain a golden sword by trading his possessions, including his jackknife, endures freezing and danger, and is rescued with the others after King Anko arrives. After visiting Anko and saying farewell to Joe, he returns with Trot across the ocean, regains his regular form and wooden leg, rows their boat from Giant's Cave, and concludes that land is best for living despite being glad to have seen the mermaids.

Cap'n Bill is an old sailor, formerly the owner and commander of the schooner Anemone, who now lives ashore with Trot's family after losing a leg. He tells Trot that mermaids are beautiful fairies who lure people into the water, but he struggles to explain how anyone knows what they look like if those who see them do not survive.

[Illustration: _Chap 2._

Cap'n Bill rows Trot's boat into Giant's Cave and is frightened when the mermaids appear. Although he doubts their safety and worries about Trot, he agrees to follow her underwater so he can look after her.

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

Cap'n Bill enters the water with Trot and finds himself comfortable despite his fear of rheumatism. He discovers that he can swim, dive, and move more skillfully than ever, and he learns that his green-scaled tail is more useful than his wooden leg. He is guided by Merla and remains wary of sharks, devilfish, and other dangers.

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

Cap'n Bill descends into the ocean with Trot and observes that they can breathe and move without gills. He recalls that some swordfishes attack ships, is embarrassed by his former ideas about mermaids, and asks Queen Aquareine whether the dampness will worsen his rheumatics.

[Illustration: _Chap 5._

Cap'n Bill meets King Anko and exchanges questions about their ages and health. He admits that Anko is older than he is, says he would not want to live for so many years, and later observes that Anko's memory may be failing because he forgot to describe his third pain.

[Illustration: _Chap. 6._

Cap'n Bill rests in the palace, answers questions about life on earth, and eats everything offered at the queen's dinner. He successfully lights and smokes his pipe underwater because of the air space around him. During the afternoon swim, he avoids an eel's snap, objects when the eels call him a polliwog, and says that the eels are not respectful.

[Illustration: _Chap. 7._

Cap'n Bill explores the sea with Trot and Merla. He comments on codfish aristocracy, recognizes why people are called crabbed, and explains how balloonfish skins can be made into lanterns.

[Illustration: _Chap. 8._

Cap'n Bill is given a room near Trot's but refuses to change his clothes. He wonders why the deep water does not crush them and accepts Trot's explanation that magic keeps the water away from their bodies. After enjoying the banquet and entertainment, he receives the Magic Circle before leaving on the underwater journey.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

Cap'n Bill accompanies Queen Aquareine, Princess Clia, and Trot on their exploration. He agrees that scientists gave creatures difficult names, supports Trot's negative opinion of the octopus, asks about sea devils and Zog, and witnesses the failed sawfish attack.

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Cap'n Bill accompanies Trot, Queen Aquareine, and Clia through the ocean. He asks what island they are near and what the sea-gulls find to eat, and he is introduced to Chief Muffruff as an earth dweller and sailor.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

Cap'n Bill becomes disturbed by the cuttlefish ink and the sea devils, muttering and growling to himself. He travels with the others into the cage formed by the sea devils.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Cap'n Bill escapes the sea-devil cage with the others but remains deeply worried about Trot's safety. He doubts that Queen Aquareine can defeat Zog and finds the palace maze bewildering. He follows Clia through the maze while holding her hand.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Cap'n Bill insults Zog and offers to die in Trot's place if Zog will send her safely home, but Trot rejects the proposal. He is assigned to live in the Peony Room.

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Cap'n Bill explores the Peony Room and is startled when a supposed reflection turns out to be his long-lost brother, Cap'n Joe Weedles. He learns that Joe was transformed into a water-breathing servant of Zog and worries about the difficulty of escaping the castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Cap'n Bill eats with the others and discusses ways to escape from Zog's castle. The Yell-Maker cannot hurt him, and he remains unharmed when Zog causes marble blocks to fall from the ceiling.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Cap'n Bill's heart beats fast during Zog's marble-block threat, though he does not show his fear. He doubts their chances against Zog and hopes they will not have to fight the sea devils, but later eats dinner and sleeps in the Peony Room.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Cap'n Bill complains about the heat and says he does not want to be par-boiled. He suggests using his jackknife as a weapon, but Aquareine says she needs a golden sword. He persuades the goldsmith Agga-Groo to make the sword by trading his compass, scissors, and other small possessions, including his jackknife.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Cap'n Bill is found almost frozen stiff in his room after Zog freezes the water. Trot and Princess Clia rub him to revive him, and Queen Aquareine restores his warmth with her fairy wand; he then agrees to follow her escape plan.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._

Cap'n Bill draws Trot close and prepares to die with her when Zog surrounds the fugitives. After King Anko's arrival, Cap'n Bill is rescued along with the others and follows Anko toward the ocean above.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

Cap'n Bill comforts Trot and hopes to take her home, though he remains with the mermaids and King Anko out of politeness and gratitude. He visits Anko's palace, challenges the king's claim that Captain Kidd was called Captain Kid Glove, and approves of Anko's order to send them home. He is impressed by the enormous size of Anko's body.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Cap'n Bill agrees to visit the former slaves and says goodbye to his brother, Cap'n Joe. He is relieved that Cap'n Joe is well provided for as king of the castle.

Cap’n Bill travels with the mermaids across the ocean and returns to his regular form, including his wooden leg. He helps Trot into their boat and rows them out of the Giant’s Cave, agreeing that the land is best for living while being glad to have seen the mermaids.

Cap'n Bill in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Cap'n Bill is an old, retired, one-legged sailor from Earth who lives with Trot and her mother. Friendly, cheerful, honest, knowledgeable, and meek, he is Trot's close chum and devoted protector, caring for her exceptionally well. Though skeptical of Button-Bright's Magic Umbrella, he builds seats for it, joins Trot and Button-Bright on its flight, and becomes both frightened and resourceful when they reach Sky Island. Captured by the Boolooroo of the Blues, Cap'n Bill is made a palace slave and Royal Nectar Mixer. He explores the Blue City, escapes with Trot and Button-Bright, and endures the Fog Bank before the group reaches the Pink Country. There he favors the Pinkies over the Blues, supports Trot after she becomes Queen, and urges a campaign to recover the Magic Umbrella and confront the Boolooroo. As Commander in Chief of Trot's Army of Conquest, he leads the Pinkies through the Fog Bank and against the Blue City. During the fighting he directs the Pinkies but is captured after falling when his wooden leg enters a hole. The Boolooroo plans to patch him to Tiggle, but Trot repeatedly saves him from the Great Knife and frees him. He then helps bind the Boolooroo, supports Trot's rule, and becomes her General and Royal Manager of the Blue Country. After the victory, Cap'n Bill helps find the discarded umbrella, seats, and lunch basket. He travels back through the Fog Bank, prepares the equipment, pilots the return journey with Trot and Button-Bright, and regards getting back to Earth as the best part of the adventure.

Cap'n Bill in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Cap'n Bill is a person transformed by Blinkie the Witch into a little gray grasshopper with a wooden last joint on his left leg. Though able to speak in a tiny sharp voice, he finds the change inconvenient and humiliating rather than painful. He threatens Blinkie and King Krewl, escapes through a window with a remarkable jump, and reveals himself to Trot after the Scarecrow is restuffed. Riding on Trot's and then the Scarecrow's shoulders, he worries about eating grass and being stepped on. Finally, he jumps onto the Scarecrow's shoulder so Blinkie can change him back into Cap'n Bill.

Cap'n Bill in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Cap'n Bill is an old, fat sailor-man with one wooden leg and one flesh leg, strong hands, a knife, and a stout cord. He is Trot's oldest and most faithful comrade and friend. Seeking the Magic Flower as a birthday present for Ozma, he joins Trot and the Glass Cat on the journey to the Magic Isle. On the way, he makes Trot a small shelter, protects her from a Kalidah, knocks the attacker down with his wooden leg, and pins it with a stake. He builds a raft from logs and stakes and propels Trot and the Glass Cat across the river. On the island, his flesh foot grows roots into the ground, leaving only his wooden leg free; Trot is rooted too. He recognizes that the Glass Cat must seek the Wizard of Oz's help. While trapped, Cap'n Bill waits beside Trot, eats fruit produced by the Magic Flower, reflects on ordinary blessings such as walking and breathing, and helps question the Lonesome Duck. Though seated on a strong toadstool, he remains unable to leave. He calls their condition being anchored and fears cutting or pulling up their flesh roots would hurt terribly. As he and Trot shrink to about half their natural size, he believes escape is hopeless and asks that Ozma be told if they are destroyed. Cap'n Bill is transformed into a bumblebee, escapes the Magic Isle safely, and is restored to his usual form. After binding bark to his good foot, he returns, retrieves the golden flower-pot containing the Magic Flower, and carries it away from the enchanted Isle. He later helps with a board for the Cowardly Lion, repairs the monkeys' cage wires, secretly brings the flower-pot to Trot's room, and attends Ozma's birthday feast beside Trot, credited with carrying the flower from the Isle.
