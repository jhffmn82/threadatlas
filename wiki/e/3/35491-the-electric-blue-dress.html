Abstract written by a model

The electric-blue dress, formerly belonging to Alice Rucastle, is a well-made, previously worn garment said to be wanted for Violet Hunter to wear indoors in the morning. It fits Miss Hunter exactly, and Mr. and Mrs. Rucastle show exaggerated delight when she wears it by the window.
