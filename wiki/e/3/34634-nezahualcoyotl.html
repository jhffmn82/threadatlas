Nezahualcoyotl

Also called: the wise monarch Nezahualcoyotl

Abstract written by a model

Nezahualcoyotl is a wise monarch of the Tezcucans. He abhors human sacrifices, erects a large temple to “The unknown god, the cause of causes,” and founds the Council of music at Tezcuco, which advances music, other arts, and sciences.

Nezahualcoyotl is in one work on this site, Musical Instruments. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
