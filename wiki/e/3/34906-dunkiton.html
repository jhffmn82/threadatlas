Dunkiton in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Dunkiton is a large walled town inhabited by donkeys, with whitewashed houses scattered irregularly and a road passing through an open gate. Its donkeys call it the center of the world's highest civilization, though Dorothy considers them stupid beasts. Ruled by King Kik-a-bray, it uses metal sheets and loud kicking to drive away foxes; travelers stay one night before continuing.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

6. The City Of Beasts

Dunkiton is a large city surrounded by a high whitewashed wall and inhabited by donkeys. The road runs straight through the city, where the donkeys use metal sheets and loud kicking to scare away foxes.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

Dunkiton is a city of donkeys whose houses are scattered rather than arranged in regular streets. It is ruled by King Kik-a-bray, and the travelers spend one night there before continuing along the road.
