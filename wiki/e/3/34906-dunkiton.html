Abstract written by a model

Dunkiton is a large walled town inhabited by donkeys, with whitewashed houses scattered irregularly and a road passing through an open gate. Its donkeys call it the center of the world's highest civilization, though Dorothy considers them stupid beasts. Ruled by King Kik-a-bray, it uses metal sheets and loud kicking to drive away foxes; travelers stay one night before continuing.

Dunkiton is in one work on this site, The Road to Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
