Abstract written by a model

A retrieval-augmented generator is enhanced by self-memory candidates for dialogue generation. With fixed parameters, it distinguishes good from bad memory and is not Selfmem’s current bottleneck. Experiments show substantial dialogue benefits, tunable toward BLEU or diversity, and analysis finds superior self-selected memory and better long-tail handling.

Experimental Results / Machine Translation .. Dialogue Generation

The retrieval-augmented generator benefits substantially from self-memory in dialogue generation. Its performance can be directed toward higher BLEU or greater diversity by changing the metric used to optimize memory.

Further Analysis

A trained retrieval-augmented generator with fixed parameters can distinguish between good and bad memory. Its performance benefits from superior memory selected from its own unbounded output, and retrieval-augmented models handle long-tail inputs better than parametric models.
