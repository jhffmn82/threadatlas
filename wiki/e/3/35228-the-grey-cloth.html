IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

The grey cloth was a vague object James McCarthy recalled seeing on the ground about a dozen yards from the body and roughly the same distance from the wood. He said it had disappeared while he was kneeling beside Charles or before he went for help.
