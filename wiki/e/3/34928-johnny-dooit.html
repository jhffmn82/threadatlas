Johnny Dooit in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Johnny Dooit is a hard-working workman with grey hair and long grey whiskers, carrying tools in a copper chest. Called by the Shaggy Man using the Love Magnet, he appears, is said to be able to do anything, and quickly builds a twelve-foot sand-boat. After teaching the Shaggy Man to sail it, he disappears with his tools.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

11. Johnny Dooit Does It

Johnny Dooit appears when the Shaggy Man calls him with the Love Magnet and says that he can do anything. He quickly constructs a twelve-foot sand-boat from trees, rope, and canvas, then instructs the Shaggy Man to sail it like a ship before disappearing with his tools.
