Whimsies

group · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Chief of the Whimsies, These Whimsies, Whimsie, each Whimsie, false-headed forces, the Whimsies, the cunning of the Whimsies, wee-headed Whimsies

Abstract written by a model

The Whimsies are a people of strong, muscular, unkillable evil spirits with very small natural heads. They conceal these with large false pasteboard heads and are hated and feared because they are terrible fighters: strong, cunning, and unable to recognize defeat. Guph secures their aid for the Nome King’s conquest of Oz by promising that, if the Magic Belt is captured, they will receive large, fine natural heads. They become part of the false-headed invading forces allied with the Nome King, alongside other groups, although the Phanfasms intend to destroy them after the campaign. Summoned to the Nome King’s cavern, their Chief and generals join the gathered warriors. They then march through the tunnel under their Chief; one Whimsie had earlier nearly choked Guph in anger. At the Forbidden Fountain, they remove their false heads to drink, forget their hostile purpose, and harmlessly play with the other transformed invaders until Ozma sends them home.

Whimsies is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

6. How Guph Visited the Whimsies

The Whimsies are strong, muscular evil spirits with tiny natural heads covered by large pasteboard heads, and they cannot be killed. They agree to fight for the Nome King after Guph promises that their natural heads will be enlarged if the Nomes capture the Magic Belt.

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

The Whimsies are identified as allies whose assistance Guph has already secured before reaching the Growleywogs.

11. How the General Met the First and Foremost

The Whimsies are one of the groups General Guph says has promised to help the Nomes conquer Oz. The Phanfasms agree to work with them temporarily but plan to destroy them afterward.

13. How the General Talked to the King

The Whimsies agree to fight for the Nomes with their strength and cunning. They are promised large, fine heads in place of the small heads they currently wear.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Whimsies are summoned by the Nome King to join the invasion of Oz. Their Chief and their generals appear among the fierce forces gathered in the Nome King's cavern.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The Whimsies march into the tunnel under the Chief of the Whimsies. One of the wee-headed Whimsies earlier became angry at General Guph and nearly choked him to death.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

The Whimsies arrive at the fountain and cast off their false heads so they can drink. After forgetting their former purpose and hostility, they play harmlessly with the other transformed invaders until Ozma sends them home.
