Abstract written by a model

The truth-worship controversy is a publicly sanctioned conflict in which sarcasm and slanderous ridicule inflict cruel, lasting harm; Merman's treatment after challenging Grampus exemplifies its progression from challenge to maiming.

III.

The truth-worship controversy is described as a form of public conflict in which victims suffer bruising, laceration, and lifelong maiming through sarcasm and slanderous ridicule rather than physical torture. Merman's treatment after challenging Grampus is offered as a prominent example.
