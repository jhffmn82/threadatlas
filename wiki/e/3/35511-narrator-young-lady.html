narrator (young lady) in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A young lady, frightened of the Rucastles' house, servants, and child, seeks Mr. Holmes's advice and sends him a telegram. While caring for the child during the Rucastles' absence, she enters an empty wing, is disturbed by its lonely stillness, and is threatened on leaving, though curiosity rivals her fear.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“‘So,’ said he, smiling, ‘it was you, then. I thought that it must be

The young lady explains to Mr. Holmes that she entered the empty wing, became frightened by its loneliness and eerie stillness, and was threatened when she came out. She sent for Mr. Holmes because she feared the house and its occupants, but her curiosity remained almost as strong as her fear.
