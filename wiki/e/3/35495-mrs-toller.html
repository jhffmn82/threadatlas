Mrs. Toller in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mrs. Toller, a servant of the Rucastles and Alice Rucastle’s ally, initially alarms Violet Hunter and is locked in the wine-cellar. Released by Mr. Rucastle, she reveals her support for Alice and Mr. Fowler and explains their escape plan.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Holmes and I had listened spellbound to this extraordinary story. My

Mrs. Toller initially gives the alarm by her presence, so Violet Hunter locks her in the wine-cellar, but Mr. Rucastle later releases her. She reveals that she supported Alice and Mr. Fowler, accepted Mr. Fowler’s help, kept Toller supplied with drink, and assisted the escape plan. Her testimony explains Alice’s imprisonment, Violet’s impersonation, and the ladder at the skylight.
