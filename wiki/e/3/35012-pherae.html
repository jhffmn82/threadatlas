Pherae

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Phera!, Pherae's wallèd ring, Pherse, Pherseans, Pheræ, castle, city of Pheres, house, land of Pherae
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Pherae had a garrison of Cassander's soldiers and was ruled by Alexander, who imprisoned Pelopidas and Ismenias and later faced opposition from Epaminondas's forces.
  • In Alcestis, Pherae was a city in Thessaly ruled by Admetus, whose citizens and elders mourned Alcestis; Admetus ordered its people to wear mourning and forbade music and celebration for twelve moons.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Pherae was the city ruled by Admetus, whose people were ordered to share in mourning Alcestis and observe twelve months without the sound of pipes or the lyre.
