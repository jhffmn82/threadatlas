the fire diversion

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Fire, alarm of fire, false alarm, false fire alarm, fire, the alarm of fire, the cry of fire

Abstract written by a model

The fire diversion is a distraction in Holmes's operation at Briony Lodge, designed to create confusion and reveal the photograph's location. At Holmes's signal, Dr. John Watson throws an ordinary plumber's smoke-rocket through the open sitting-room window and cries fire. As others repeat the alarm, Irene Adler rushes toward the photograph's hiding place, allowing Holmes to see it before he declares the alarm false.

II.

The fire diversion is designed to draw attention and create confusion during Holmes's operation at Briony Lodge. At Holmes's signal, Watson is to throw an object into the sitting room and shout that there is a fire, prompting others to repeat the cry before Watson leaves the street.
