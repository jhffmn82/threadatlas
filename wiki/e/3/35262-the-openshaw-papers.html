the Openshaw papers in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The Openshaw papers were vital to the Ku Klux Klan and were kept in Elias Openshaw’s brass box. They included, or may have included, a register, diary, letters, memoranda, and receipts that could implicate prominent men in the South. After a warning from Pondicherry, Elias burned them; only a fragment survived, while later warnings demanded the papers be placed on the sundial.

V. THE FIVE ORANGE PIPS

The destroyed papers were kept in Elias Openshaw’s brass box and were burned after he received the warning from Pondicherry. A surviving fragment suggests they may have included letters, memoranda, receipts, and a register, while the later warnings instruct the recipients to put the papers on the sundial.
