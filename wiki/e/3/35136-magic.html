magic in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Magic, expressed as fairy power, is possessed by Queen Aquareine and her attendants. It protects mermaids and mortal companions, enables long ocean travel without tiring, and is tested against Zog's evil magic. Queen Aquareine uses it to protect captives and destroy the Yell-Maker, believing it may surpass Zog's power.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 3._

Fairy power protects the mermaids from injury and allows them to guard mortal companions in the ocean. Princess Clia uses some of this power to help the group travel hundreds of miles without tiring.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

The prisoners' fairy power is to be tested against Zog's magic. Zog plans to wear it down gradually.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Queen Aquareine believes her fairy powers may be stronger than the evil magic controlled by Zog. Her magic protects the captives, while her fairy wand destroys the Yell-Maker; Zog claims that he learned the same kind of destructive trick thousands of years earlier.
