Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Pentheus orders the assembly of shield-bearers, riders, and archers to attack the Bacchae, but abandons the plan when Bacchus offers to guide him secretly to observe them. In The Bacchae of Euripides, Pentheus orders the captain to gather men-at-arms at the Electran Gate and plans to march against the Bacchae, but Dionysus diverts him from the campaign.
