Pentheus's planned military campaign

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Pentheus's planned assault on the Bacchae, We march to war, hunt evils with evils, join battle with the Bacchæ, make an attack upon the Bacchæ, march and scatter them with serried lance
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, Pentheus orders the captain to gather men-at-arms at the Electran Gate and plans to march against the Bacchae, but Dionysus diverts him from the campaign.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Pentheus orders the assembly of shield-bearers, riders, and archers to attack the Bacchae, but abandons the plan when Bacchus offers to guide him secretly to observe them.

_Enter_ PENTHEUS _in fury_.

Pentheus orders the captain to gather men-at-arms at the Electran Gate, including cavalry, shield-bearers, and archers. He plans to march against the Bacchae because he cannot bear what he calls their deeds against men, but Dionysus diverts him from the campaign.
