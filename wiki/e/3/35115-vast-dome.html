vast dome in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

A vast dome encloses Zog's castle and gardens in clear water, its roof half a mile above the castle. A guarded round opening at the top becomes the fugitives' goal as they flee wolf-fish and Zog; King Anko descends through it and promises a way home after withdrawing his body.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

The vast dome surrounds Zog's castle and gardens with clear water. Its roof is about half a mile above the castle and contains an opening guarded by the prince of the sea devils.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._

The four fugitives try to reach the opening in the dome while fleeing the wolf-fish and Zog. King Anko descends through the opening and later says the way home will be open after he withdraws his body.
