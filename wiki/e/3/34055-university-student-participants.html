IV. AI TUTOR EVALUATION

The university student participants included 76 first-year students, 36 female and 40 male, who had no prior finance coursework. They completed standardized self-study, an interactive tutoring session, and a multiple-choice assessment; 76 participants also provided feedback using a 5-point Likert scale.

V. DISCUSSIONS

University students participated in the study, which involved a relatively small sample of 76 students. The unit indicates that broader groups of students across institutions and academic disciplines would provide stronger evidence of generalizability.
