Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Aias's madness results in the slaughter of horned and bleating animals, which he believes are the men who wronged him. In The Fall of Troy, Aias' madness causes him to attack and kill sheep while believing he is striking down the Danaans.
