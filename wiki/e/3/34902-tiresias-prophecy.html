foretells Creon's ruin [as punishment for his actions] Tiresias prophesies that Creon will soon give a corpse from his own bloodline in payment for Antigone's entombment and Polynices' lack of burial. He also foretells cries of mourning in Creon's palace and enmity from cities whose dead remain unburied.
