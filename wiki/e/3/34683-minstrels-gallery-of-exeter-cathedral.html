minstrels’ gallery of Exeter cathedral in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

A fourteenth-century gallery at Exeter cathedral, its front unfolds across twelve niches, each holding a winged figure or angel playing instruments from cittern and bagpipe to timbrel and cymbals.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

Its fourteenth-century front has twelve niches, each containing a winged figure or angel playing an instrument. The instruments identified include the cittern, bagpipe, clarion, rebec, psaltery, syrinx, sackbut, regals, gittern, shalm, timbrel, and cymbals.
