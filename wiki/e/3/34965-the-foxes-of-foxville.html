Abstract written by a model

The foxes of Foxville are civilized foxes living in a richly decorated city, wearing elaborate feather clothing and eating fowl. They come out to observe strangers, serve in the royal palace, and love the Shaggy Man because of the Love Magnet. They honor Button-Bright, host the travelers with a meal, play, rooms, and breakfast, then escort them out while remaining watchful of a neighboring village.

3. A Queer Village

The foxes of Foxville live in a richly decorated city and wear elaborate clothing, including feather gowns worn by the girl-foxes and women-foxes. They come out to observe the strangers, serve in the royal palace, and begin loving the Shaggy Man because of the Love Magnet.

4. King Dox

The foxes of Foxville dress in elaborate feather costumes and consider Button-Bright's fox head attractive and honorable. They prepare a lavish meal, perform a play, provide rooms and breakfast for the travelers, and escort them to the village's exit. They describe themselves as civilized, wise, gentle, and watchful against possible trouble from a neighboring village.
