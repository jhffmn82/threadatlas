Abstract written by a model

In The Emerald City of Oz, the writer receives a note from Dorothy Gale written on a broad, white feather from a stork's wing, feels disconcerted for a time, and reflects on the stories of Oz. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the Royal Historian of Oz is appointed by Dorothy Gale to chronicle Oz, builds a tower at Ozcot, learns wireless telegraphy, contacts Princess Dorothy, and uses the material to write six Oz books and stories for children of America.

The Royal Historian of Oz is an American writer appointed by Dorothy Gale to chronicle Oz. When Oz is to become invisible and communication end, he builds a tower at Ozcot, learns wireless telegraphy, contacts Princess Dorothy, and uses the material to write six Oz books and stories for children of America.

The Patchwork Girl of Oz / Prologue

The Royal Historian was an American writer appointed through Dorothy Gale's kindness to chronicle the Land of Oz. After learning that Oz would become invisible and communication with it would end, he built a wireless tower, learned wireless telegraphy, and contacted Dorothy for new material.
