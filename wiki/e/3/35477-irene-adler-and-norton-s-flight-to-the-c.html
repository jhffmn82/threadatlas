Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

Irene Adler and Norton leave England on the 5:15 train from Charing Cross for the Continent. In her letter, Irene says that they chose flight because they were being pursued by so formidable an antagonist as Sherlock Holmes.
