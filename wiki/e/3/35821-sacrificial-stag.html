A large, beautiful mountain-roaming stag provided by Diana appears as Iphigenia vanishes at Aulis, preferred to her as a sacrificial victim. Given to the Greeks in her place, its blood is used in the sacrifice before it is burned to ashes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[_Enter a_ MESSENGER.[102]]

A large and beautiful mountain-roaming stag appears when the girl vanishes at the moment of the blow. Diana is said to prefer it to the virgin, and its blood is used in the sacrifice before it is burned to ashes.

IPHIGENIA IN TAURIS.

Diana gives the Greeks a stag in place of Iphigenia when she is about to be killed on the pyre at Aulis.
