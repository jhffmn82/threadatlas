Abstract written by a model

This trip, arranged by Ozma, lets Dorothy take Uncle Henry and Aunt Em from their restless palace life. Dorothy, her relatives, and companions travel through the Land of Oz in a red wagon drawn by the Sawhorse. They visit the Royal Athletic College, ride from Cuttenclip toward Fuddlecumjig, visit the Fuddles, and continue. After reuniting, they plan further calls and resume through a forest into Rigmarole Town.

9. How the Wogglebug Taught Athletics

The journey through the Land of Oz is arranged by Ozma so Dorothy can take Uncle Henry and Aunt Em away from their restless palace life. Dorothy, her relatives, the Wizard, the Shaggy Man, Omby Amby, Toto, and Billina travel in a red wagon drawn by the Sawhorse. Their first described stop is the Royal Athletic College of Oz, after which they continue their journey.

12. How they Matched the Fuddles

The travelers ride away from the Cuttenclip village, follow the main road, and turn toward Fuddlecumjig. After visiting the Fuddles, they get back into the wagon and continue their journey.

22. How the Wizard Found Dorothy

After reuniting, the group plans to visit the Rigmaroles and Flutterbudgets and then call on the Tin Woodman, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Scarecrow. The journey resumes when the enchanted wagon carries them through the forest and into Rigmarole Town.
