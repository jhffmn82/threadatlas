double-pipe in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The mam, a popular Egyptian double-pipe, is repeatedly depicted in Egyptian performances and later appears in Assyrian processions; the Hebrew mishrokitha is considered a probable double-pipe despite imaginative organ illustrations.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The double-pipe, called mam, appears frequently in representations of Egyptian musical performances. Its repeated depiction suggests that it was a popular instrument.

CHAPTER III.

The double-pipe appears among the instruments played by Assyrian musicians in procession. The Hebrew mishrokitha is considered a probable double-pipe, although illustrations presenting it as a small keyed organ are judged imaginative.
