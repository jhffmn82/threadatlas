XV.

The Channel Islands, with Notes and an Appendix, is Vorticella's only book. Pumpiter newspapers and distant county journals praised it extravagantly, attributing to it historical, poetic, economic, humorous, fictional, eloquent, and religious merits, while one London notice called its style dull and pompous. The narrator later finds it an ordinary mixture of commonplace and ambition.
