Abstract written by a model

The barricaded central room in the forbidden suite is a locked prisoner’s chamber, secured with an iron bed bar, padlock, and cord. Miss Hunter sees light, a shadow, and moving footsteps within. Later Holmes, Watson, and Violet Hunter force it open to find sparse furnishings and the prisoner gone through the skylight.
