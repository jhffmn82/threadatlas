Reciprocal Rank Fusion in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where Reciprocal Rank Fusion first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reciprocal Rank Fusion is a fusion method used in MEDRAG to combine results from different retrievers, improving robustness and performance. RRF-2 fuses BM25 and MedCPT for PubMed, while RRF-4 fuses all four retrievers for MedCorp and reaches 71.57% average accuracy; poorly performing retrievers can hurt performance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The MEDRAG Toolkit

Reciprocal Rank Fusion is used in MEDRAG to combine results from different retrievers. The toolkit defines RRF-2 as the fusion of BM25 and MedCPT and RRF-4 as the fusion of all four retrievers.

Results .. Practical Recommendations

Reciprocal rank fusion combines retrieval results from multiple retrievers and generally improves robustness and performance. RRF-4 achieves 71.57% average accuracy with MedCorp, but adding a poorly performing retriever can hurt performance, so the choice of fused retrievers must be made cautiously.
