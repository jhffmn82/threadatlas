SERVANT. Pentheus, we are here; having caught this prey, for which you sent

The servant brings the captured stranger to Pentheus and reports that the stranger submitted calmly and even laughed while being bound. He also reports that the imprisoned Bacchae escaped when their fetters came loose and the prison doors opened without mortal hands.
