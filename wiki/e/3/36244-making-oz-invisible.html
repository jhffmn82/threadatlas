making Oz invisible

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: an edict, edict rendering Oz invisible, invisible, make Oz invis'ble, making our country invisible

Abstract written by a model

In The Emerald City of Oz, making Oz invisible prevents people outside Oz from seeing or finding the country, without affecting its inhabitants' ability to see one another or their land. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the edict rendering Oz invisible declares that Oz will thereafter be invisible to everyone living outside its borders.
