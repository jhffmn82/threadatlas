Eurydicè in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

Eurydicè, queen of Thebè and mother of Haemon, hears of Haemon’s death, leaves silently, then dies by suicide before the altar, lamenting Megareus and Haemon and cursing Creon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

Eurydicè hears the report of Haemon’s death and leaves without speaking. She later kills herself before the altar, lamenting the deaths of Megareus and Haemon and cursing Creon for the murder of his children.

Eurydice in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

Eurydice, Creon’s wife and Theban queen, mother of Haemon, hears of Haemon’s death, mourns Megareus and Haemon, then stabs herself beside an altar, cursing Creon.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Enter TEIRESIAS and BOY]

Eurydice hears the report of Haemon’s death and asks the first Messenger to tell her what happened. She later kills herself beside an altar after mourning Megareus and Haemon, and with her last breath curses Creon as the slayer of her child.
