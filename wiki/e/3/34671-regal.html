Abstract written by a model

The regal was a small, often embellished portable organ with smaller organ-like keys. It appears in sculptured representations at Beverley Minster and Melrose Abbey, and in a painting with piston-like keys. The name also denoted a percussion instrument of sonorous wooden slabs arranged like a harmonica.

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] [Illustration] CHAPTER VIII.

The regal was a small portable organ, often tastefully shaped and embellished, with keys generally like those of an organ but smaller. Sculptured representations survive in Beverley Minster and Melrose Abbey, and a painting by Melozzo da Forli shows a regal with keys resembling the pistons of certain brass instruments. The name regal was also applied to a percussion instrument made of sonorous wooden slabs arranged like a harmonica.
