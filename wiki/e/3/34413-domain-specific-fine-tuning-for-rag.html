Abstract written by a model

In LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering, Domain-Specific Fine-Tuning for RAG is a fine-tuning strategy for enhancing retrieval and generation components, including through collaborative fine-tuning. In RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment, domain-specific adaptations of RAG face data scarcity, which the described self-alignment approach addresses by fine-tuning a general retriever and LLM into a unified RAG system. In Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, retriever and generator fine-tuning optimizes RAG components separately or together, improving the retriever, generator, or both. In HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases, domain adaptation is an area where HYBGRAG offers no significant advantages, performing worse on STARK-Prime than on STARK-MAG.
