stag-horn pipe in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

A rudely made stag’s-horn pipe from the latest Stone Age was found in a stone-period burying-place near Poitiers, France; blown at the end, its three equidistant holes produced four tones.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The stag-horn pipe was found in a stone-period burying-place near Poitiers in France. It was blown at the end like a flûte à bec and had three equidistant finger-holes, allowing four distinct tones.
