Abstract written by a model

is_a lamentation for Agamemnon [unceasing and excessive] Electra mourns Agamemnon continuously by day and night and refuses to stop lamenting while she remains alive. She sees continued mourning as a duty to her murdered father and rejects comfort that would make her forget him.
