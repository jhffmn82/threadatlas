Retrieval in Retrieval Augmented Generation and Representative Vector Summarization for Large Unstructured Textual Data in Medical Education

Retrieval is a workflow for clinical medicine and general pharmacology: it extracts text from unstructured sources, applies OCR to images and scanned PDFs, chunks the text, and stores embeddings in a vector database. Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2.2

Retrieval extracts text from unstructured sources, uses OCR for images and scanned PDFs, splits the extracted text into chunks, and stores their embeddings in a vector database. At query time, it finds the most similar chunks and combines them with the original query.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.2

Retrieval responses for clinical medicine and general pharmacology were compared between a large language model without a non-parametric knowledge-base and a retrieval-augmented generation model using vector databases. docGPT produced more targeted and accurate answers, while ChatGPT answers were more generic.
