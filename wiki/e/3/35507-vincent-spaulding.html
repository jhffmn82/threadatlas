Vincent Spaulding in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Vincent Spaulding is Jabez Wilson’s assistant in his pawnbroker’s business, employed for about a month at half wages because he is handy and cheap. Small, stout-built, quick, clean-shaven, over thirty, with a white acid mark on his forehead and pierced ears, he persistently practises photography. He brings Wilson the Red-Headed League advertisement, urges him to apply, helps him through the Pope’s Court crowd, and offers to run the business during the League duties. Sherlock Holmes considers him remarkably smart and daring and examines his trouser knees.

II. THE RED-HEADED LEAGUE

Vincent Spaulding works as Jabez Wilson's assistant for half wages and brings the Red-Headed League advertisement to Wilson's attention. He encourages Wilson to apply, helps him get through the crowd at Pope's Court, and says he can look after the pawnbroker's business while Wilson performs the League's duties.
