Joseph Openshaw owned a Coventry factory, patented an unbreakable tire during bicycling’s invention, sold his successful business, and retired comfortably. After Elias Openshaw died, he inherited the estate and money. Warned by five orange pips from Dundee to place papers on the sundial, he ignored them and died in an apparent accidental chalk-pit fall.

V. THE FIVE ORANGE PIPS

Joseph Openshaw, John’s father, inherited Elias Openshaw’s estate and money. He received five orange pips from Dundee with a warning to put the papers on the sundial, ignored it, and died after falling into an unfenced chalk-pit; his death was judged accidental.
