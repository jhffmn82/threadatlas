machines in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XVII.

Machines are shown as increasingly delicate and powerful instruments that test, measure, register, and draw conclusions beyond ordinary human perception. Trost regards them as extensions and slaves of the human organism, while the narrator imagines them evolving into independent entities.
