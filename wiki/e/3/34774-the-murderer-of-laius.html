is_exiled_or_slain the land of Thebes [as required by Apollo's oracle] The murderer of Laius is publicly cursed and ordered to leave the land or be expelled. Tiresias declares that the murderer is Oedipus, a man thought to be a foreign sojourner who is actually a true-born Theban.
