Alice Turner in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Alice Turner, a lovely young woman and old Turner's daughter, seeks Sherlock Holmes's help because she is certain that James McCarthy is innocent. Though naturally reserved, she is excited as she explains that Mr. McCarthy wanted her to marry James, while James quarrelled with his father and her own father opposed the match. Her seriously ill father later says he killed McCarthy to protect her; Alice remains unaware of this secret and is expected to live happily with McCarthy's son.

Miss Turner asks Sherlock Holmes to investigate James McCarthy's case because she knows James is innocent. She explains that Mr. McCarthy wanted her to marry James, that James and his father had quarrelled about the matter, and that her father opposed the proposed marriage. She also tells Holmes that her father is seriously ill.
