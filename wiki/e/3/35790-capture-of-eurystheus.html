Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Iolaus seizes Eurystheus's four-horse chariot at the Scironian rocks, binds Eurystheus in fetters, and brings him alive before Alcmena. In Hecuba and other plays, after the Athenian victory, Iolaus pursues Eurystheus, seizes him in his chariot near the rocks of Scyron, binds him with chains, and brings him before Alcmene.
