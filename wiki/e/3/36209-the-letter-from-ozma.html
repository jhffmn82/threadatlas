the letter from Ozma in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

A royal missive signed by Ozma of Oz and sealed with the Great Seal of the Emerald City identifies Dorothy as a princess and bearer, directing Ozma’s subjects to welcome her courteously and allowing her to enter Bunnybury.

1 section; each heading opens the text

19. How Bunnybury Welcomed the Strangers

The letter from Ozma identifies Dorothy as a princess and directs Ozma's subjects to welcome her with courtesy and consideration. It bears Ozma's signature and the Great Seal of the Emerald City, enabling Dorothy to enter Bunnybury.
