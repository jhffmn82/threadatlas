Abstract written by a model

The Hebrews, known from biblical records to have possessed many musical instruments, including a favored lyre and single trumpet, lack trustworthy representations; their instruments are reconstructed from Egyptian and Assyrian examples.

CHAPTER III.

The Hebrews are known from biblical records to have possessed many musical instruments, but trustworthy representations are lacking. Their instruments are therefore reconstructed by comparison with ancient Egyptian and Assyrian examples.
