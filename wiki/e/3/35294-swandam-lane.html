Swandam Lane

Abstract written by a model

Swandam Lane is an unpleasant area branching from Fresno Street and containing an opium den. Walking there toward the station, Mrs. St. Clair sees Neville St. Clair at its window and faints at blood. Though he had not told her of the place, the den proves to be where a Lascar lodges him and helps him assume and remove his beggar disguise.

Swandam Lane is in one work on this site, The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
