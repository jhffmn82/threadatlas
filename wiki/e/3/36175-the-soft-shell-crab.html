the soft-shell crab in The Emerald City of Oz

The soft-shell crab, a crab in the zebra's pool, insists water exceeds land, is confirmed by the Wizard, quarrels with and pinches the zebra, then returns to the forest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

The soft-shell crab insists that there is more water than land in the world, and the Wizard confirms that he is correct. He laughs at and pinches the zebra during their quarrel before returning with him to the forest.
