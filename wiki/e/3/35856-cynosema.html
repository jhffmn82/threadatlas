Cynosema is a promontory of the Thracian Chersonese, Hecuba’s burial place, where Athenians won a naval victory over Peloponnesians and Syracusans.

notes

Cynosema is identified as the place of Hecuba’s burial, a promontory of the Thracian Chersonese. The notes also state that the Athenians won a naval victory there over the Peloponnesians and Syracusans during the Peloponnesian war.
