Orestes' chariot accident

Abstract written by a model

causes Orestes' reported death [according to the Old Man's account] During a chariot race, Orestes struck the edge of a stone after loosening the left-hand rein and shattered the axle. He fell, became caught in the reins, and was dragged by the horses until he died.
