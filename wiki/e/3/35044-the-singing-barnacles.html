The singing barnacles are singing creatures that cling in millions to the man-o'-war and steep rocks, producing a chorus all day. Their songs mention ships, anchors, and sea creatures, but usually mean nothing because they have few brains.

The singing barnacles cling to the steep surface of a large rock and produce a chorus of many small voices. Their songs concern ships, anchors, sea creatures, and other subjects but usually mean nothing because the barnacles have few brains. They sing all day and do not care whether their songs make sense.
