Oedipus's crimes

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: Oedipus's alleged crimes, deeds He did unknowingly, dire road, involuntary crimes, kindred crime, my murderer, sin, the deeds, the filcher of my crown, the handiwork, the horror, the horror of my birth, thine unwitting deed, wrong

Abstract written by a model

In *The Seven Plays in English Verse*, Oedipus’s crimes are identified as unknowingly killing his father and marrying his mother, making him both husband and son to Jocasta; he also condemns himself for having children through that marriage and argues that his life has endured more wrong than it has done. In *Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone*, Oedipus’s alleged crimes are killing Laius, defiling the bed of the man he killed, marrying his own mother, and killing his father; he fears that the first may have occurred and the rest may follow the prophecy.
