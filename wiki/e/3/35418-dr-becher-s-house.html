Dr. Becher's house in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Dr. Becher’s house, occupied by Dr. Becher, held the hydraulic engineer and housed a coining gang before a fire consumed it, leaving twisted cylinders, iron piping, nickel, and tin.

Dr. Becher's house is the location where the hydraulic engineer was held and where the coining gang operated. It burns almost completely, leaving only twisted cylinders, iron piping, and stored masses of nickel and tin.
