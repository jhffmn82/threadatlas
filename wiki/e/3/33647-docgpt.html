docGPT is a Python document intelligence program using langchain, with source available on GitHub. It extracts and processes varied document formats, retrieves relevant chunks, creates summaries, and compiles prompts for a large language model to generate answers. On clinical medicine and general pharmacology queries, its responses were described as more targeted and accurate than ChatGPT responses.
