the oracle’s command to seize the image

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: commanded by the oracles of Phœbus, oracles of Phœbus, sent me hither to seize the heaven-sent image

Abstract written by a model

Phoebus’s oracle commands Orestes to come to the barbarian land, seize the heaven-sent image of Diana, and place it in Athens. Orestes believes that obeying this command will end his madness and allow him to return to Mycenae.
