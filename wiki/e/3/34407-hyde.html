HyDE

method · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Hybrid with HyDE, generated pseudo-documents

Abstract written by a model

HyDE is a query-transformation method and implementation of pseudo-document generation. From an original query, it generates pseudo-documents to reduce semantic mismatches with relevant documents and enhance retrieval; combined with hybrid search, it achieved the highest reported scores, including a 0.58 RAG score, at 11.71 seconds per query.

HyDE is in one work on this site, Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
