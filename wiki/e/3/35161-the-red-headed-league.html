the Red-headed League

Abstract written by a model

The Red-Headed League is a purported organization, founded in name by Ezekiah Hopkins’s bequest, offering easy, well-paid work to eligible red-headed men. It hires Jabez Wilson to copy the Encyclopaedia for four pounds a week over eight weeks. The league is actually a criminal scheme designed to divert Wilson from his pawnbroker’s business while a tunnel and bank robbery are prepared. Once the tunnel is complete, its false offices close and the league abruptly disappears.

II. THE RED-HEADED LEAGUE

The Red-Headed League is presented as a fund established from the bequest of Ezekiah Hopkins to provide well-paid, nominal positions for eligible red-headed men. It employs Jabez Wilson for eight weeks before being abruptly dissolved.

The Red-headed League is the organization that gave Mr. Jabez Wilson work copying the Encyclopaedia and then vanished without explanation. Its supposed officials and addresses proved false, and Wilson lost a position that had paid him four pounds a week.
