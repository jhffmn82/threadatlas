Jewish national consciousness in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Jewish national consciousness is an organized memory and feeling of separateness, existing broadly and intensely among Jews worldwide. Formed through religious tradition, historical memory, resistance to foreign domination, and education in corporate existence, it preserved national identity and the national hearth of a religion that shaped Christianity. Its continuing strength is presented as essential to any restoration of Jewish national life.
