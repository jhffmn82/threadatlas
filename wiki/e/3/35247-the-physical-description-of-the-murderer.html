the physical description of the murderer in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The murderer is described as tall, left-handed, lame in the right leg, wearing thick-soled shooting-boots and a grey cloak, and using Indian cigars, a cigar-holder, and a blunt pen-knife.

Holmes describes the murderer as tall, left-handed, lame in the right leg, and wearing thick-soled shooting-boots and a grey cloak. He also says that the man smokes Indian cigars, uses a cigar-holder, and carries a blunt pen-knife.
