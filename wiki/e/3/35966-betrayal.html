Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, marital betrayal is committed by Jason through his new marriage. In Medea, Jason's betrayal and remarriage causes the exile of Medea and her children. In Medea, betrayal afflicts Medea through Jason's abandonment. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Jason's abandonment of Medea results in Medea being commanded to leave Corinth.
