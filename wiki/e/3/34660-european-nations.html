European nations in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

European instruments initially resembled Asiatic instruments, then showed little material construction improvement for nearly a thousand years, amid scarce evidence from the first five or six Christian-era centuries.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The earliest known European instruments were more similar in form and construction to Asiatic instruments than later European examples. Little material improvement in European instrument construction is perceptible over nearly a thousand years, although evidence from the first five or six centuries of the Christian era is scarce.
