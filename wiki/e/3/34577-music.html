Music in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08 · the work’s page · read it where Music first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

Music is universally appreciated as one of the Fine Arts, and vocal music may have originated alongside language. The music of each nation is said to possess distinctive characteristics, while the construction of its instruments can reveal its intervals, scales, modulations, and other features.

THE HINDUS.

Music is presented in Hindu tradition as being of heavenly origin, with some musical water-spirits possibly having originally been rain deities. The text also suggests that the rhythm of waves and the murmur of running water may have inspired traditions about music and instruments originating from water.
