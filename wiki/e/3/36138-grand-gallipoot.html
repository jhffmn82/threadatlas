Grand Gallipoot

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Grand Gallipoot of the Growleywogs, The Grand Gallipoot, the Gallipoot

Abstract written by a model

Grand Gallipoot is the chief and ruler of the Growleywogs, commanding and leading eighteen thousand powerful warriors. He agrees to aid the Nomes' conquest of Oz, but secretly intends to seize the Magic Belt, enslave the Nome King, destroy the First and Foremost, and take Oz's people and wealth. He marches with the invading Growleywogs, coughing through a dusty tunnel and longing for a drink.

Grand Gallipoot is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

8. How the Grand Gallipoot Joined The Nomes

The Grand Gallipoot questions Guph about the proposed conquest and agrees that the Growleywogs will assist the Nomes. He promises to march with eighteen thousand powerful warriors, while privately planning to take the Magic Belt, enslave the Nome King, and seize Oz's people and treasure.

27. How the Fierce Warriors Invaded Oz

The Grand Gallipoot leads the Growleywogs and secretly plans to murder or destroy the First and Foremost and take Oz's wealth for himself. He coughs in the dusty tunnel and longs to reach Oz for a drink.
