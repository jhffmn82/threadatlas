Abstract written by a model

Touchwood’s great book is an expected comprehensive work that would preserve his best self and benefit society. Though everyone believes him capable, his powers remain unproven; perhaps his love of disappointing expectations has kept the book unwritten.

VI.

Touchwood's great book is a comprehensive work that everyone believes he is capable of writing, although his great powers have so far been believed in rather than demonstrated. The text wishes that he would produce it and place his best self permanently on record, providing society with an unvarying service that would make his daily incalculableness easier to bear. It wonders whether his love of disappointing expectations has helped keep the book unwritten.
