Hippolytus's exile

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: another land, banished, banishment, cast him out, driven far, exile, the land, thy banishment, thy country’s land, thy exile

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Hippolytus's exile is imposed by Theseus without an examination of the oath, proof, or seers' sayings; Hippolytus leaves his country in grief, and the Chorus mourns his departure. In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, Theseus casts Hippolytus out of all his realms and commands him never to return to Athens or any land under Theseus's power; Hippolytus leaves reluctantly, asking his friends to give him god-speed and declaring that no man is more pure of spot.
