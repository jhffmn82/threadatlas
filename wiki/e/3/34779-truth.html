Abstract written by a model

Truth is portrayed as protected by its own power and compelled into speech by Oedipus's rage; Tiresias says it will expose Oedipus's birth, destruction, and guilt. Dê demands truth from Lichas, whose disclosure explains Heracles's conquest of Oechalia through a maiden's love.

[OEDIPUS _has entered during the Choral song_

Tiresias says that the might of truth protects him and that Oedipus's rage has forced the truth from his silence. He declares that truth will reveal Oedipus's birth, destruction, and hidden guilt.

DÊ. Nay, I entreat thee by His name, whose fire

DÊ insists that Lichas reveal the whole truth, saying that the name of liar is hateful to a free-born person. Lichas then tells her that the maiden's love caused Heracles's conquest of Oechalia.
