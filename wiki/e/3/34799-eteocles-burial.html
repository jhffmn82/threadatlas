Abstract written by a model

Eteocles receives lawful, fully honored burial rites: Antigone scatters dust over the body and pours three libations, but guards catch her after uncovering it.

CHORUS.

Antigone performed the burial by scattering dry dust over the body and pouring three libations from a brazen vessel. She was caught in the act after returning to the body when the guards had uncovered it.
