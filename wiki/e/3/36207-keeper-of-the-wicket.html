Keeper of the Wicket in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Keeper of the Wicket is a white rabbit and Bunnybury's gatekeeper, guarding the entrance and controlling the Keeper's Wicket. Distinguished by a monocle and ornate satin clothing, he questions Dorothy's entry, admits her after reading Ozma's letter, denies Billina and Toto, reduces Dorothy to rabbit size, and escorts her into Bunnybury.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

19. How Bunnybury Welcomed the Strangers

The Keeper of the Wicket guards Bunnybury's entrance and initially questions Dorothy's right to enter. After reading Ozma's letter, he admits Dorothy, refuses entry to Billina and Toto, reduces Dorothy to the rabbits' size, and escorts her into the city.
