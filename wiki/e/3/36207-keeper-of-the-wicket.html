Keeper of the Wicket

Abstract written by a model

The Keeper of the Wicket is a white rabbit and Bunnybury's gatekeeper, guarding the entrance and controlling the Keeper's Wicket. Distinguished by a monocle and ornate satin clothing, he questions Dorothy's entry, admits her after reading Ozma's letter, denies Billina and Toto, reduces Dorothy to rabbit size, and escorts her into Bunnybury.
