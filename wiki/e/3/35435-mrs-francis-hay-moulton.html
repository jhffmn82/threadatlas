Mrs. Francis Hay Moulton in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mrs. Francis Hay Moulton, publicly known as Lady St. Simon, had married Francis Hay Moulton, believed him dead after an Apache attack, and became engaged to Lord St. Simon. Seeing Francis alive at the St. Simon wedding, she left the ceremony, followed him, reunited with him in London, and went to Gordon Square.

Mrs. Francis Hay Moulton explains that she had married Francis years earlier and believed him dead after an Apache attack. At the St. Simon wedding she saw Francis alive, abandoned the ceremony, followed him from the house, and went with him to Gordon Square.
