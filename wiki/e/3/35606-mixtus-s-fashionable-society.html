Mixtus's fashionable society in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Mixtus’s fashionable society of half-aristocratic, professional, and artistic people enjoys his splendid entertainment and wealth but dismisses his opinions, artistic judgment, and earlier religious and intellectual life.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Mixtus’s fashionable society consists of half-aristocratic, professional, and artistic people whom he entertains with splendour. They value him as a wealthy, commercially successful, genial acquaintance, but generally disregard his opinions, doubt his artistic judgment, and know little of his earlier religious and intellectual life.
