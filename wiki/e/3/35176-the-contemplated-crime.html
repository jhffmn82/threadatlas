the contemplated crime in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A considerable crime is being planned in connection with Saxe-Coburg Square; Sherlock Holmes believes it can still be stopped and, despite Saturday complications, enlists Dr. John Watson for a nighttime armed intervention.

Sherlock Holmes and I surveyed this curt announcement and the rueful

Sherlock Holmes says that a considerable crime is being planned in connection with the case. He believes there is still time to stop it, but the fact that it is Saturday complicates matters, so he asks Dr. John Watson to assist him at night and bring his revolver.
