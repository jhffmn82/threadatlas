oracle of Phoebus

oracle · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: ancient oracle, ancient oracle of Apollo, it is fated, oracle, oracle of the God, oracular response, prophecy concerning Eurystheus's tomb

Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it is the topic of a prophecy foretelling Eurystheus's tomb near the temple of the divine virgin of Pallene and his protection of Athens from beneath the earth. In Hecuba and other plays, it is the oracle declaring that Eurystheus's corpse must be buried near the temple of the Pallenian maid and promising that he will protect Athens after death.
