Also called: the first method
  • In "RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation," GPT Score is a baseline method that asks ChatGPT to assign each candidate a score from 0 to 10 for faithfulness, answer relevance, or context relevance; ties are broken randomly, and its agreement with human annotators is lower than RAGAS’s.
  • In "Ragas: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation," GPT Score is a baseline method that asks ChatGPT to score three quality dimensions from 0 to 10, achieving agreement with human assessments of 0.72, 0.52, and 0.63.
