perforated bone whistle

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: perforated bone, reindeer-bone whistle, small bone, whistle

Abstract written by a model

A prehistoric musical relic made from a small ruminant first digital phalanx, less than two inches long. Its bone was drilled with a smooth cylindrical bore, reportedly using a flint knife; blown against the lower lip, it makes a shrill sound and was surmised to aid hunting animals.

CHAPTER II.

The perforated bone whistle is a first digital phalanx of a ruminant, drilled with a smooth cylindrical bore. When applied to the lower lip and blown, it produces a shrill sound; M. Lartet surmised that it was used for hunting animals.
