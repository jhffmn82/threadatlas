Abstract written by a model

Stoke Moran manor-house is an old ancestral, heavily mortgaged mansion of grey, lichen-blotched stone, partly ruined and divided into a central block and two wings. Only one wing, including three ground-floor bedrooms, is inhabited; Dr. Grimesby Roylott lives in its comparatively modern right-hand block. Scaffolding marks repairs as Holmes examines its exterior and internal features while investigating a death and Helen's threat.
