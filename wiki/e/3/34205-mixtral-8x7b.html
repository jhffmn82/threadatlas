Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 in RadioRAG: Online Retrieval-augmented Generation for Radiology Question Answering

Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh · 2024-07-22

Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 is a large language model evaluated with and without RadioRAG. Initially lagging GPT-4 in conventional question answering, it gained substantially with RadioRAG: RSNA-RadioQA accuracy rose from 65% (52/80) to 76% (61/80; P=0.02), and RadioRAG-powered performance exceeded human expert radiologists there (P=0.007). It reached 79% on ExtendedQA, the highest listed RadioRAG-powered result, with up to 47% relative improvement. It had among the fewest hallucinations, including 9% on RSNA-RadioQA and 8% on ExtendedQA.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 was evaluated with and without RadioRAG. It saw notable gains in diagnostic accuracy when using RadioRAG.

3. Results / 3.1. Dataset Characteristics .. 3.5. Comparison to Human Performance

Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 increased from 65% (52/80) to 76% (61/80) on RSNA-RadioQA with RadioRAG (P=0.02). It showed a relative accuracy improvement of up to 47% on ExtendedQA, had among the fewest hallucinations on both datasets, and its RadioRAG-powered performance was significantly higher than human expert radiologist performance on RSNA-RadioQA (P=0.007).

4. Discussion

Mixtral-8x7B-instruct-v0.1 initially lagged behind GPT-4 in conventional question answering but improved substantially with RadioRAG. Its RSNA-RadioQA accuracy increased from 65% to 76%, and its ExtendedQA accuracy reached 79%, the highest result among the listed RadioRAG-powered models on that dataset.

appendix

Mixtral-8x7B achieved an average accuracy of 67% with RadioRAG and 50% without RAG on the RSNA-RadioQA dataset. Its context-relevant incorrect-response rate was 9% on RSNA-RadioQA and 8% on ExtendedQA.
