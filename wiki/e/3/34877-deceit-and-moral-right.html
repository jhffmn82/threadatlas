CHORUS.

Deceit is used by Neoptolemus to obtain Philoctetes' bow under the appearance of friendship and protection. Philoctetes condemns this act as betrayal, and Neoptolemus recognizes it as a shameful deed learned from base men.

MONODY.

Neoptolemus recognizes that he acted wrongly by using deceit to take Philoctetes' bow and tries to restore what he took. The unit contrasts Odysseus' stratagem with Neoptolemus' attempt to act rightly, while also showing Philoctetes' struggle to trust an act of apparent friendship.
