Abstract written by a model

The synthetic dataset contains question–answer pairs from corpus passages, representing positive and negative query–passage–answer examples. ARES constructs it first by generating queries and answers with generative LLMs and in-domain examples, then removes low-quality queries that do not retrieve their original passage first.
