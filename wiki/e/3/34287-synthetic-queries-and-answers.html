synthetic queries and answers in ARES: An Automated Evaluation Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Jon Saad-Falcon · 2024

The synthetic dataset contains question–answer pairs from corpus passages, representing positive and negative query–passage–answer examples. ARES constructs it first by generating queries and answers with generative LLMs and in-domain examples, then removes low-quality queries that do not retrieve their original passage first.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The synthetic dataset consists of question–answer pairs generated from passages in the corpus by an LM. ARES constructs it in the first stage of its evaluation process.

ARES .. 3.3

Synthetic queries and answers are generated from passages in the target corpus using generative LLMs and few-shot in-domain examples. Low-quality queries are filtered by testing whether they retrieve their original passage as the top result.
