the illusion of an audience

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: My illusion, my approving choral paradise, the pleasing, inspiring illusion of being listened to, the vague, delightful illusion of an audience

Abstract written by a model

An illusion of a liberal, far-off, hazy assembly, numerous and idiom-near, is kept as manuscripts face judgment outside imagination, but is fatalized when a distinct auditor or foreground physiognomy emerges.
