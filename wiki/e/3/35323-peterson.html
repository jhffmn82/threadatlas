Peterson in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

Peterson, a commissionaire, finds the battered black felt hat and Christmas goose after Henry Baker flees on Tottenham Court Road. He brings them to Sherlock Holmes, later discovers the blue carbuncle in the goose's crop, and is sent to place an advertisement for Henry Baker in the evening papers.
