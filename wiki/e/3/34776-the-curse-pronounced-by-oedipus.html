Abstract written by a model

Oedipus pronounces a curse on Laius's murderer: exile from Thebes and human fellowship, denial of shelter, speech, and religious rites, and divine punishment for the killer or concealers. After recalling his actions at the crossroads, he realizes the curse may condemn himself.
