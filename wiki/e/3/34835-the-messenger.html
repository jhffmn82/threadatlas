1 section; each heading opens the text

THE TRACHINIAN MAIDENS

The Messenger first tells Dêanira that Heracles is alive, victorious, and returning. He then stops her from taking the captive women inside, exposes Lichas's misleading account, and states that Heracles attacked Oechalia because of his love for Iole. He identifies the captive woman as Iole, daughter of Eurytus.
