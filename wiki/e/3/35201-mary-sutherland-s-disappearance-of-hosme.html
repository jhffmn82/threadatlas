Mary Sutherland's disappearance of Hosmer Angel

Abstract written by a model

Mary Sutherland's disappearance of Hosmer Angel is revealed to be the staged abandonment of her supposed bridegroom at the church door. The apparent disappearance was engineered by James Windibank so that Mary would remain emotionally bound to Hosmer Angel and avoid marrying anyone else.
