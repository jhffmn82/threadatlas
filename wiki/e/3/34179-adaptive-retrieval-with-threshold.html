adaptive retrieval with threshold

method · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Adaptive retrieval with threshold, adaptive or no retrieval processes, adaptive retrieval, adaptive threshold, retrieval on demand

Abstract written by a model

Adaptive retrieval with threshold is a method that triggers retrieval when normalized Retrieve=Yes probability exceeds a threshold. It uses predicted retrieval-related reward-token probabilities to control frequency: raising the threshold reduces retrieval, with less performance deterioration on PubHealth than on PopQA.
