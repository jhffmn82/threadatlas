Reading

Abstract written by a model

In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Reading is the place toward which Sherlock Holmes, the hydraulic engineer, Inspector Bradstreet, and the others travel, and in whose direction the fugitives are last traced. In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Reading is associated with Mr. Sandeford and the final surviving bust, which Holmes determines contains the pearl.
