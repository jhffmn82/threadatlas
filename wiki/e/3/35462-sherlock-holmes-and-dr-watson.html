Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Holmes and I, Holmes and Watson, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson, both, my friend and colleague, our, us, we

Abstract written by a model

In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson discuss Violet Hunter's case at Baker Street, decide to travel together to Winchester, plan to take the 11:30 train, and meet Violet at the Black Swan Hotel. In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson share chambers in Baker Street, walk through London, return to find Trevelyan waiting, and accompany him to his residence to investigate Blessington's alarm over the alleged intrusion.
