Abstract written by a model

Aias's sword, a weapon received from the enemy Hector, is sharp, bloodied in livestock slaughter, and capable of cutting, piercing, hacking, and tearing. Aias says it has brought him no kindness among the Argives and calls it a weapon of hatred. Though he plans to bury it, he carries it away, sharpens and plants it in Trojan soil, then uses it to pierce his heart.

AIAS

Aias takes his sharp blade when he leaves his tent at night and uses it during the livestock slaughter. His weapon is described as newly bloodied and as cutting, piercing, hacking, and tearing the animals.

Of Aias, and his fortunes as they are. [AIAS _is discovered_

Aias says that Hector gave him the sword and that it has brought him no kindness among the Argives. He intends to bury it underground as a weapon of hatred, but instead carries it with him when he leaves for the countryside.

_The scene changes to a lonely wooded spot._

Aias sharpens and fixes Hector's sword in hostile Trojan soil before using it to pierce his heart. Teucer identifies the planted weapon as the instrument that gives verdict from Aias's breast.
