Zog's capture of the prisoners in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Zog's sea devils, warned by his spies after the prisoners left Queen Aquareine's palace, surround them from above, behind, and both sides. Protecting magic circles prevent contact, but the travelers are enclosed in a cage of interlaced feelers, carried away, and forced to the enchanted castle's entrance.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

The sea-devil capture begins when the monsters surround the group from above, behind, and both sides. Although the protecting magic circles keep the captors from touching them, the travelers are enclosed, carried away, and unable to escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Zog's sea devils captured the prisoners after his spies warned him that they had left Queen Aquareine's palace. They surrounded the group and forced them to the entrance of the enchanted castle.
