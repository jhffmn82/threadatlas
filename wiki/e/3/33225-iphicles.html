Iphicles in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Iphicles, brother of Hercules and father of Iolaus, fought beside Hercules against Augeas and the people of Elis, was wounded, taken to Pheneus, cared for by Buphagus and Promne, died, and was buried there; Pheneus honored him as a hero.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIV.

Iphicles, the brother of Hercules and father of Iolaus, was wounded while fighting with Hercules against Augeas and the people of Elis. His relations brought him to Pheneus, where Buphagus and Promne cared for him until he died of his wound and buried him. The people of Pheneus continued to honor him as a hero.
