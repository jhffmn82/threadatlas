III.

Merman's book attacks Grampus's interpretation and presents Merman's theory about the Magicodumbras, Zuzumotzis, and ancient social origins. Grampus eventually reads it and responds with a crushing review, after which Merman's mistakes and alleged lack of scholarly competence become widely discussed.
