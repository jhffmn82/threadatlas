Godfrey Norton in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Godfrey Norton is a dark, handsome, dashing Inner Temple lawyer who visits Irene Adler daily and hurriedly marries her at the Church of St. Monica, with Sherlock Holmes as witness.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II.

Godfrey Norton is a dark, handsome, dashing lawyer of the Inner Temple who visits Irene Adler once or twice a day. He hurries to the Church of St. Monica, where he marries Adler with Sherlock Holmes serving as the required witness.
