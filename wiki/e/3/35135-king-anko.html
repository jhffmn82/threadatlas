Later, he finds Zog's castle through a hole beneath the sea-devil prince's body. He enters through the dome opening, rescues the fugitives by seizing Zog in his coils, and destroys Zog. Finally, using his authority under the law of the ocean, Anko orders that Trot and Cap'n Bill return home and be restored to their natural forms, after his body clears the opening.

King Anko in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

King Anko is a powerful, ancient sea serpent and king of the ocean, ruling from his palace. He is an agreeable, talking monarch with a round head, tasselled pointed ears, mild blue eyes, gray hair and whiskers, a long brown serpentine body, and several back fins. His immense age and size are matters of pride: he has lived for several thousand years, is older than Cap'n Bill, and was last measured at 7,482 feet, five inches and a quarter. He has had only three pains, including a toothache, a tail kink caused by an octopus, and trouble from swallowing an anchor and chain. Anko is fond of Queen Aquareine and Trot, has a brother, Inko, and two other brothers in other oceans. His central conflict is with Zog, his enemy for centuries. In an earlier war he conquered Zog and drove him into a great cavern and enchanted castle; Zog fears Anko as the one being able to destroy him. When Anko first meets Mayre and Cap'n Bill in the throne room, he describes his history and invites the visitors to his home. Later, he finds Zog's castle through a hole beneath the sea-devil prince's body. He enters through the dome opening, rescues the fugitives by seizing Zog in his coils, and destroys Zog. Finally, using his authority under the law of the ocean, Anko orders that Trot and Cap'n Bill return home and be restored to their natural forms, after his body clears the opening.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap 5._

King Anko enters the throne room through an opening in the coral and meets Mayre and Cap'n Bill. He is a gentle, ancient sea serpent who remembers several thousand years, was once measured at 7,482 feet, five inches and a quarter, and has suffered three pains, including a toothache and a kink in his tail caused by an octopus. He says he is proud of his age, rule, and length, then leaves to go home for dinner and invites the visitors to see him.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

King Anko is the sea serpent who conquered Zog and drove him into his enchanted castle. He would kill Zog if he caught him outside the castle, and Zog knows this.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

King Anko is a sea serpent and Zog's great enemy. Zog says that Anko is the only being able to destroy him, but he has spent thousands of years trying to find a way to destroy Anko himself.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._

King Anko descends through the dome opening and rescues the fugitives by seizing Zog in his coils. He destroys Zog, then tells the group that they can return home through the dome once his body has cleared the opening.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

King Anko explains that he located Zog's castle through a hole beneath the body of the sea-devil prince and arrived in time to help destroy Zog. He tells Trot about his third pain, which came from swallowing an anchor and chain, and about the mustard poultice used to treat it. Claiming authority under the law of the ocean, he orders that Trot and Cap'n Bill be returned home and restored to their natural forms.
