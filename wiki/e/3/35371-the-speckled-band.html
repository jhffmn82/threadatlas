Abstract written by a model

The speckled band is Julia Stoner’s dying reference, initially uncertain and possibly thought to mean people or delirium. It is ultimately revealed as the yellow swamp adder that appears around Dr. Grimesby Roylott’s head after his death.

VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

The “speckled band” was Julia Stoner's final intelligible reference as she lay dying. Helen is uncertain whether it referred to a group of people, such as the gipsies, or was merely delirious speech.
