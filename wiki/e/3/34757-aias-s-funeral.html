Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Aias's burial is a contested funeral rite that Teucer defends and undertakes, with Odysseus supporting it under Heaven's laws. In The Fall of Troy, Aias's funeral burns his washed and shrouded body on a richly supplied pyre, then places his bones in a golden casket and buries them in a high mound near Rhoeteium.

Aias's burial in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · 449 to 429 BC · the work’s page · read it where Aias's burial first appears

Abstract written by a model

Aias’s burial is a contested funeral rite. Menelaus and Agamemnon oppose it, but Teucer defends and undertakes it as justice owed to the dead; Odysseus supports burial under Heaven’s laws. Teucer orders guards and prepares the grave, cleansing water, fire, arms, and attendants.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

_The scene changes to a lonely wooded spot._

Teucer insists that Aias receive immediate burial despite Menelaus's prohibition. He summons Eurysakes and Tecmessa to guard the body and declares that he will provide a grave even if the generals refuse consent.

Aias's funeral in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · about AD 200 to 299 · the work’s page · read it where Aias's funeral first appears

Abstract written by a model

Aias's funeral burns his washed and shrouded body on a richly supplied pyre, driven by a wind from Thetis. After the Danaans quench the fire with wine, they place his bones in a golden casket and bury it in a high mound near Rhoeteium.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Then Aias saw those sheep upon the earth

Aias's funeral includes washing and shrouding his body, placing it upon a large pyre, and surrounding it with wood, animals, clothing, gold, armor, silver, ivory, oil, and other offerings. A wind sent by Thetis drives the fire until his body is consumed. The Danaans quench the pyre with wine, place his bones in a golden casket, and bury them in a high mound near Rhoeteium.
