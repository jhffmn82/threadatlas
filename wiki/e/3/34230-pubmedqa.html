PubMedQA* in Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine

Guangzhi Xiong, Qiao Jin, Zhiyong Lu, Aidong Zhang · 2024 · the work’s page · read it where PubMedQA* first appears

Abstract written by a model

PubMedQA* is a biomedical research QA dataset in MIRAGE, derived from PubMedQA by removing contexts and retaining 500 questions with three options each. It benefits strongly from PubMed literature retrieval: MEDRAG performs well with one snippet, but irrelevant additions reduce accuracy; 79.6% of ground-truth snippets rank first.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The MIRAGE Benchmark / Evaluation Settings .. Component Datasets

PubMedQA* is a biomedical research QA dataset in MIRAGE with 500 questions and three options per question. MIRAGE removes the original PubMedQA contexts and uses only the questions.

Results .. Practical Recommendations

PubMedQA* is a research-related MIRAGE task that benefits strongly from PubMed because supporting literature can be retrieved there. MEDRAG can achieve high accuracy with one snippet, but performance decreases as more irrelevant snippets are added; 79.6% of ground-truth snippets are identified as the top-ranked context.

PubMedQA in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16 · the work’s page · read it where PubMedQA first appears

Abstract written by a model

PubMedQA is a biomedical research collection of PubMed research abstracts paired with automatically generated yes/no/maybe questions derived from article titles. It includes PQA-L, PQA-U, and PQA-A, and requires retrieval and reasoning across multiple context documents. In RAGBench, PQA-U is reframed with segmented chunks, retrieval from a 200k-entry vector database, and generated responses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

RAGBench Construction / Component Datasets .. Data Splits

PubMedQA contributes biomedical research data based on research abstracts. Its questions are automatically generated from research article titles, and its task requires retrieval and reasoning over multiple context documents.

RAGBench Dataset Details .. ExpertQA [25]

PubMedQA contains PubMed research abstracts paired with yes/no/maybe questions, with the questions derived from article titles using rule-based heuristics. For RAGBench, its PQA-U subset is reframed as a RAG task using segmented abstract chunks, a 200k-entry vector database, four retrieved chunks per question, and newly generated LLM responses.
