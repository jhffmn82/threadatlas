Morals in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Morals, an alias of Ethics in public discussion, are often narrowed to private sexual conduct and respectability, obscuring harmful public actions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XVI.

Morals are commonly used in a narrow sense to mean sexual conduct, family respectability, and related private decencies. This restricted use wrongly allows people whose public actions cause widespread injury to be praised as moral.
