Abstract written by a model

Zog is a wicked, powerful magician and sea monster: a dreadful composite being made part man, bird, fish, beast, serpent, and reptile. He has an eel-like body ending in a fish tail, cloven hoofs, feathery wings, and a robe that conceals his form. Twenty-seven thousand years old and long-lived for several thousand years, he is the feared, hated ruler of the hidden dominions and enchanted castle of Zog beneath the sea. He owns the palace, golden throne, slaves, and treasures plundered from sunken ships; sea devils serve him, and he controls magic, guards every entrance, prevents escape, and never sleeps. Zog hates everybody and the world, while the world hates him. His chief enemy is King Anko, who defeated him in the past, drove him into the invisible enchanted castle, and is the only being able to destroy him. Though Zog has supreme power within his retreat, fear of Anko limits his magic and keeps him from leaving by his own power or openly doing evil for many years. When he captures prisoners who are friends of Anko, Zog provides them rooms filled with stolen treasures but plans their destruction. He subjects them to the Yell-Maker, a collapsing marble ceiling, boiling water, and frozen water, while strengthening his guards, slaves, and sea devils. After the prisoners escape, he sends wolf-fish after them and pursues them in his concealed monstrous form, destroying Aquareine's golden sword and sending away her fairy wand. King Anko then captures Zog in his coils; upon release, Zog becomes a lifeless jelly-like pulp. His death ends the danger in the cavern, and his former slaves inherit his possessions and occupy his castle.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

12 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

Zog is described as the wicked genius of the sea and a powerful magician who is part fish, part man, part beast, and part serpent. King Anko defeated him and drove him into an invisible enchanted castle, where he has remained shut up and has not dared to do an evil deed for many years.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

Zog the Terrible is identified as the master whom the sea devils serve. Queen Aquareine believes the group may be near his hidden castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Zog the Forsaken is described as a fearful magician who hates all the world and is hated by all the world. He commands the group to appear before him and is said to become dangerous when he smiles. He rules a castle beneath the sea and keeps numerous slaves in his domain.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Zog is a wicked magician and sea monster who lives in an enchanted castle. He admits that he captured the prisoners to destroy friends of King Anko, and he claims that only Anko can destroy him.

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Zog provided handsome rooms for his prisoners and had furnished them with treasures taken from sunken ships. He transformed Cap'n Joe Weedles by giving him gills, keeps many slaves in his castle, and is described as cruel, grumbling, and nearly devilish.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Zog summons the captives to undergo a first trial of strength and orders the Yell-Maker to make them yell. He watches as Queen Aquareine destroys the creature, then causes the marble ceiling to collapse over the captives, but neither attack harms them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Zog stops the marble-block attack, threatens the prisoners, and sends them back to their rooms. He is said to be plotting to kill them, to guard every entrance, and to have kept the castle's light shining continuously; Sacho says Zog has not slept for hundreds of years.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Zog makes the water in the group's rooms boiling hot and continues plotting ways to destroy them. He is angry because they escaped the Yell-Maker, falling stones, and hot water. His power has strengthened the guards, slaves, and sea devils, but he fears King Anko and does not usually leave his retreat.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Zog uses magic to freeze all the water in the room while the others sleep, imprisoning and endangering them. His cruel designs have become so dangerous that Queen Aquareine decides they must leave his castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._

Zog sends wolf-fish after the escaping prisoners and then pursues them himself in his concealed monstrous form. He destroys Aquareine's golden sword and sends her fairy wand away, but King Anko captures him in his coils; when Anko releases him, Zog is a lifeless mass of jelly-like pulp.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

Zog is described as King Anko's enemy for centuries and as the evil genius whose powers threatened Trot, Cap'n Bill, Aquareine, and Clia. He is destroyed after Anko discovers the enchanted castle beneath the sea-devil prince.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Zog is dead, and his former slaves now inherit his possessions and occupy his castle. His destruction removes the danger from the cavern.
