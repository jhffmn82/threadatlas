Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the burial of Polynices is prohibited by Creon's burial prohibition on pain of death. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Antigone's burial of Polynices is an act of sisterly fidelity performed in defiance of Creon's edict.
