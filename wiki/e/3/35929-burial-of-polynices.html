Antigone's burial of Polynices in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

is_a act of sisterly fidelity [performed in defiance of Creon's edict] Antigone buried Polynices because she believed that denying him burial rites was intolerable. She acted despite her sister's warnings and Creon's death penalty, was caught, and was condemned to be buried alive.
