4. King Dox

Button-Bright's fox head replaces his curls, round face, and blue eyes with a hairy head having a sharp nose, pointed ears, and keen little eyes. Button-Bright dislikes it and cries, while the foxes, including the King's daughters, praise its beauty. Dorothy believes Princess Ozma can restore his original head.
