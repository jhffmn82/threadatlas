love potion in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

A proposed soothing philter to relieve Phædra’s illness without base deed or harm to her senses, requiring a word or relic from the beloved man to unite them in love.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PHÆDRA, NURSE, CHORUS.

The Nurse says that she has thought of soothing philters of love that might relieve Phædra’s illness without a base deed or harm to her senses. She says the remedy requires a word or relic from the person Phædra loves and joining the two into one love.
