Peruvian agricultural songs

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: hymns, particular agricultural songs, songs

Abstract written by a model

Peruvian agricultural songs are hymns sung while cultivating the lands of the Inca; they glorify the Inca and his dynasty, use rhythm to regulate laborers’ work, and often include hailli, meaning “Triumph.”
