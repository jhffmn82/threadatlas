Charles McCarthy in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Charles McCarthy, an ex-Australian tenant farmer and friend of John Turner, occupies Hatherley Farm rent free and employs household servants. He is James McCarthy’s father, described as a hard man, yet he alone supports James’s proposed marriage to Miss Turner. Having known Turner in Victoria, he goes to Boscombe Pool on June 3 for an appointment with an unidentified person. There he has a reported violent quarrel with James and is later found dead from repeated blows to the head. Before dying, he cries “Cooee!” and seemingly attempts to say “Ballarat,” which Holmes takes as a clue to his murderer.

IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

Charles McCarthy was an ex-Australian who rented Hatherley Farm from John Turner. He went to Boscombe Pool on June 3rd for an appointment, was later found dead with repeated blows to his head, and reportedly had a violent quarrel with his son, James.
