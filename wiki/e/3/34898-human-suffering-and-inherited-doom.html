Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, human suffering and inherited doom afflicts the stock of Cadmus across successive generations, as each generation receives fresh sorrow from those before it. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, the calamities of Cadmus's family, together with sorrow and numerous prodigies, drive Cadmus from Thebes, where he and Hermione recount their house's misfortunes.
