XV.

The critical opinions album is an elegantly bound volume containing newspaper reviews and paragraphs about Vorticella and her book. It lies prominently in her drawing-room beside the book itself, is offered to guests for perusal, and includes praise from Pumpiter papers and journals from distant counties, along with a hostile notice from the Candelabrum.
