sea horses in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Sea horses are small sea animals, about two or three inches high, with horse-shaped heads and tails that twine around seaweed stems for support. Living among ocean weeds, they swim slowly with side and back fins, face danger from fishes and other living things, and one survived over six weeks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

The small sea horses live among the ocean weeds and support themselves by twining their tails around the stems. Their side fins also serve as ears, they have small fins on their backs, and they swim less swiftly than mermaids and fishes. They face danger from fishes and other living things, though one has survived for over six weeks.
