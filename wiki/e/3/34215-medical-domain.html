The medical domain is a specialized evaluation domain covering five MIRAGE benchmark datasets plus LiveQA and MedicationQA. Stage-II pseudo-labeled samples use Textbooks, Wikipedia, and PubMed, while evaluation uses top-10 MIRAGE retrieval context. SimRAG improves over Llama and Gemma variants, averaging 66.04 with Llama3-8B-it and outperforming its baseline with DRAGON retrieval.
