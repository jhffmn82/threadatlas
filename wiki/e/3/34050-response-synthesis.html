III. AI TUTOR DESIGN: THE KG-RAG SOLUTION

Response synthesis uses the context produced by knowledge-guided retrieval and prompts DeepSeek-V3 to answer the student's question. It guides the LLM to produce relevant and complete responses based on the retrieved course material.
