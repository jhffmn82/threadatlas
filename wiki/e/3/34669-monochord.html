monochord in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Monochord is a stringed instrument with one string on fixed bridges over an oblong box; a turning screw adjusts tension and a movable bridge marks scale intervals, serving chiefly theorists rather than performance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] [Illustration] CHAPTER VIII.

The monochord had a single string stretched over two fixed bridges on an oblong box. Its string could be tightened or slackened with a turning screw, while a movable bridge regulated the marked intervals; it was chiefly used by theorists and was poorly suited to musical performance.
