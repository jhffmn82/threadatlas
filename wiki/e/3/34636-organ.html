[Illustration] [Illustration] CHAPTER VIII.

The organ is described as the most important wind instrument and the king of all musical instruments. Early organs had about a dozen pipes, and the largest made about nine hundred years ago had only three octaves without chromatic intervals; later examples show progress in construction. The pedal is generally attributed to Bernhard, who lived in Venice about 1470, though earlier indications of its invention exist.
