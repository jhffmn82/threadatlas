Abstract written by a model

The parishioners, including miners, weavers, field-labourers, and farmers, grumble while paying tithes and obligations to the narrator's father, yet welcome and love him; after the narrator's mother's death, they care for the nine-year-old narrator at their homesteads.

II.

The parishioners grumbled about their obligations to the narrator's father, especially the tithe, but they still welcomed him and loved him. After the narrator's mother's death, they cared for the nine-year-old narrator at their homesteads.
