the forest in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The forest is a beautiful natural place southwest across the meadow, with wide-spreading trees, flowering vines, soft moss, and blackberries. It is home to the zebra and leads through a clearing to Utensia. The travelers reach it near sundown, enter seeking a path but find none, and Dorothy becomes lost and meets the Spoon Brigade. After release, Dorothy, Billina, and Toto return to seek their camp and blackberries.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

14. How the Wizard Practiced Sorcery

The forest lies southwest across the meadow and takes the travelers nearly until sundown to reach. It is a beautiful place with wide-spreading trees, flowering vines, and soft moss beneath them.

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

The forest is where the zebra lives and where he carries the soft-shell crab back after their dispute. Dorothy, Billina, and Toto enter it looking for a path, but Dorothy becomes lost there and encounters the Spoon Brigade.

16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

The forest contains the clearing where Utensia is located. After their release, Dorothy, Billina, and Toto reenter it to search for their camp and for blackberries.
