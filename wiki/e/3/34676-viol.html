viol in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The viol is a bowed string instrument, most commonly with five strings, shown in sculptures held between the knees or almost like a violin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

It appears in the St. Georges de Boscherville group in two playing positions: one instrument is held between the knees like a violoncello, while another is held almost like a violin. The viols at Santiago da Compostella are also shown in related forms.
