Abstract written by a model

requires the sacrifice of Menoeceus [to preserve Thebes] The oracle of Tiresias states that Thebes will be preserved only if Menoeceus, a descendant of the dragon's teeth, is sacrificed. His blood must be offered in the dragon's cave so that Mars will become an ally of the Thebans and the land will be made favorable.
