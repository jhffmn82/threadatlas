Peru in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Peru organizes its army carefully, with division trumpeters and drummers, and sustains agricultural songs, minstrels, and religious and Christian musical performances among Indian Christians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

” As regards the ancient stringed instruments of the American Indians our inform

In Peru, each army division had trumpeters called cqueppacamayo and drummers called huancarcamayo. The country had agricultural songs, haravis, minstrels called haravecs, and religious and Christian musical performances that continued in some form among Indian Christians.
