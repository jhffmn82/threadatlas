Inspector Bradstreet in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Inspector Bradstreet is a Scotland Yard inspector in B division. He permits Holmes and Watson to visit Hugh Boone, accepts that Neville St. Clair need not face court if he stops posing as Boone, and later joins Holmes and the hydraulic engineer in investigating a suspected counterfeiting gang near Eyford, heading toward Dr. Becher's burning house.
