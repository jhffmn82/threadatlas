answer faithfulness in ARES: An Automated Evaluation Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Jon Saad-Falcon · 2024

Answer faithfulness is a RAG evaluation capability and criterion that measures whether a generated response is grounded in retrieved context or passage rather than hallucinated, extrapolated, contradictory, or non-attributed statements. It is a component-level dimension used by ARES to evaluate RAG systems. ARES uses a dedicated classification judge trained with positive and negative annotated examples, including answers from other passages and contradictory prompts, and reliably distinguishes faithful from hallucinated answers in AIS evaluation. It is not evaluated on KILT or SuperGLUE because they lack human-annotated hallucinated answers; ARES is instead tested on real attribution datasets.

Answer Faithfulness in Investigating Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Quranic Studies: A Study of 13 Open-Source Large Language Models

Zahra Khalila, Arbi Haza Nasution, Winda Monika, Aytug Onan, Yohei Murakami, Yasir Bin Ismail Radi, Noor Mohammad Osmani · 2025

Answer faithfulness is a human-evaluated evaluation metric for assessing whether generated responses accurately reflect retrieved information and datasets, without unsupported claims, inaccuracies, or hallucinations. Evaluators compare responses with retrieved content and score factual consistency. It is one of three human-evaluated metrics used to assess model performance. Large and medium models generally perform strongly; Llama3.2:3b has the highest reported score, 4.619, and Llama3.2:1b the lowest, 1.381.
