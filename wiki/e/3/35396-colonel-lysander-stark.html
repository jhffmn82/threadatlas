Colonel Lysander Stark

Abstract written by a model

Colonel Lysander Stark is a thin, apparently forty-year-old man with a German accent who owns a fuller’s-earth mining operation with associates. He hires Victor Hatherley for fifty guineas to inspect a hydraulic stamping machine at his secluded residence near Eyford, demanding secrecy and claiming it processes fuller’s-earth. When Hatherley doubts that explanation, Stark traps him in the hydraulic press, activates it, pursues him with a cleaver, and cuts off his thumb during his escape.

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Colonel Lysander Stark hires Victor Hatherley for fifty guineas to inspect a hydraulic stamping machine at a secluded property near Eyford. He questions Hatherley closely about secrecy and claims that the machine is connected with processing fuller’s-earth.

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

Colonel Lysander Stark meets the narrator at Eyford railway station and transports him to the Stark residence. He asks the narrator to inspect the hydraulic press, then locks him inside it and activates it after learning that the narrator suspects the fuller’s-earth explanation is false. Stark pursues the narrator with a cleaver and cuts off his thumb as he escapes through a window.
