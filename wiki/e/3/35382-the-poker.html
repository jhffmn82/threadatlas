the poker in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A steel fireplace tool is seized and bent into a curve by Dr. Grimesby Roylott, thrown into the fireplace, then straightened again by Sherlock Holmes.

Dr. Grimesby Roylott seizes the poker and bends it into a curve with his hands before throwing it into the fireplace. After Roylott leaves, Holmes straightens the poker again and remarks that his own grip is not much weaker.
