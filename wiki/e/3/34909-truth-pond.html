Truth Pond in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Truth Pond is a crystal, glass-still pond in a tree circle in the Country of the Winkies, somewhere in the Land of Oz. Its water restores altered appearances, giving Button-Bright his original face and the Shaggy Man his proper head after a donkey-head transformation. It also compels the Shaggy Man to tell the truth, leading him to admit he lied to Dorothy about the Love Magnet.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

The Truth Pond is somewhere in the Land of Oz. Its water can restore the shaggy man's original head after King Kik-a-bray's magic has changed it.

13. The Truth Pond

The Truth Pond is a crystal pool in the center of a circle of trees in the Country of the Winkies. Its water restores Button-Bright's original face and the Shaggy Man's proper head.

20. Princess Ozma Of Oz

The Truth Pond has caused the shaggy man to tell nothing but the truth. Because of it, he admits that he lied to Dorothy about the Eskimo giving him the Love Magnet.

the Truth Pond in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Truth Pond is an enchanted, beautiful tiled pond with a golden inscription; anyone who bathes in it must afterward tell the truth.
