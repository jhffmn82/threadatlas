Chunking Techniques in Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Xiaohua Wang, Zhenghua Wang, Xuan Gao, Feiran Zhang, Yixin Wu, Zhibo Xu, Tianyuan Shi, Zhengyuan Wang, Shizheng Li, Qi Qian, Ruicheng Yin, Changze Lv, Xiaoqing Zheng, Xuanjing Huang · 2024

Chunking Techniques are representative methods investigated and evaluated within the chunking module for effectiveness and influence. Advanced approaches include small-to-big and sliding window. They improve retrieval by organizing chunk-block relationships: queries match small blocks, then larger blocks containing the matches and contextual information are returned. Because vector-database construction and experiments are costly, evaluation is limited, with further exploration proposed for impacts on entire RAG systems.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chunking

Chunking Techniques include advanced methods such as small-to-big and sliding window approaches. These methods organize relationships among chunk blocks by matching queries with small blocks and returning larger blocks that include the matched blocks and contextual information.

Limitations

Because of the costs associated with constructing vector databases and conducting experiments, the study limits its evaluation to representative chunking techniques within the chunking module. It proposes further exploration of their impact on entire RAG systems.

The evaluated chunking strategies are post-retrieval chunking, post-retrieval chunking with reranking, and pre-retrieval chunking. Pre-retrieval chunking performs best on almost all sources, while first-token chunks are generally better than full text and reranking performs poorly.
