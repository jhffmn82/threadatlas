  • In How to Build an Adaptive AI Tutor for Any Course Using Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (KG-RAG): KG-RAG is a novel Knowledge Graph-enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework and unified AI Tutor framework for improving AI tutoring systems. It combines Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and structured knowledge graphs, using similarity-based context, expanded knowledge-graph context, and the query to generate responses. Its central innovation is knowledge-guided retrieval: connected concepts and their explicit relationships are incorporated into retrieved context, grounding responses in structured domain knowledge. This is intended to improve coherence, answer completeness, precision, and preservation of relationships; an example structures the relationship between Mortgage-Backed Securities and Collateralized Debt Obligations. The framework is presented with a comprehensive implementation approach and as a robust, economically viable blueprint for adaptable AI tutors and globally accessible customized learning solutions. In evaluation, the examples report improved response quality, and students in the KG-RAG Experimental Group achieved a mean assessment score of 6.37 out of 10, significantly above the RAG group. Powered by DeepSeek-V3, it is described as potentially viable for institutions globally, including underserved communities, while addressing regional restrictions associated with some prominent LLM services. The work concludes that KG-RAG substantially outperforms traditional RAG-based approaches and proposes future validation across domains and learner populations, automated knowledge-graph construction, and improved adaptive scaffolding.

KG-RAG in How to Build an Adaptive AI Tutor for Any Course Using Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (KG-RAG)

Chenxi Dong · 2023-11-29

KG-RAG is a novel Knowledge Graph-enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework and unified AI Tutor framework for improving AI tutoring systems. It combines Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and structured knowledge graphs, using similarity-based context, expanded knowledge-graph context, and the query to generate responses. Its central innovation is knowledge-guided retrieval: connected concepts and their explicit relationships are incorporated into retrieved context, grounding responses in structured domain knowledge. This is intended to improve coherence, answer completeness, precision, and preservation of relationships; an example structures the relationship between Mortgage-Backed Securities and Collateralized Debt Obligations. The framework is presented with a comprehensive implementation approach and as a robust, economically viable blueprint for adaptable AI tutors and globally accessible customized learning solutions. In evaluation, the examples report improved response quality, and students in the KG-RAG Experimental Group achieved a mean assessment score of 6.37 out of 10, significantly above the RAG group. Powered by DeepSeek-V3, it is described as potentially viable for institutions globally, including underserved communities, while addressing regional restrictions associated with some prominent LLM services. The work concludes that KG-RAG substantially outperforms traditional RAG-based approaches and proposes future validation across domains and learner populations, automated knowledge-graph construction, and improved adaptive scaffolding.

Abstract—Integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) in

Knowledge Graph-enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation integrates structured knowledge representation with context-aware retrieval for AI tutoring. The paper presents it as a framework that grounds AI responses in structured domain knowledge and reports empirical validation through controlled experiments.

I. INTRODUCTION

KG-RAG is introduced as a Knowledge Graph-enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework for improving Intelligent Tutoring Systems. It integrates structured knowledge graphs to enhance response coherence and answer completeness by organizing relationships between related concepts.

II. FOUNDATIONS OF KG-RAG

The KG-RAG framework is built on Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and Knowledge Graphs. It generates responses by combining similarity-based context, expanded knowledge-graph context, and the query.

III. AI TUTOR DESIGN: THE KG-RAG SOLUTION

KG-RAG combines LLMs, RAG, and a knowledge graph in a unified AI Tutor framework. Its core innovation, knowledge-guided retrieval, incorporates connected concepts into the retrieved context.

IV. AI TUTOR EVALUATION

Knowledge-Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation improves response completeness, precision, and relationship preservation in the examples presented. Students using KG-RAG formed the Experimental Group and achieved a mean assessment score of 6.37 out of 10, significantly exceeding the RAG group.

V. DISCUSSIONS

KG-RAG powered by DeepSeek-V3 is described as a potentially viable tutoring option for institutions globally, including underserved communities. Its implementation with DeepSeek-V3 is also presented as a way to address regional restrictions associated with some prominent LLM services.

VI. CONCLUSION

KG-RAG integrates structured knowledge representations with advanced language models to address fundamental challenges in AI tutoring. It substantially outperforms traditional RAG-based approaches in quantitative metrics and qualitative assessments. Future research will validate it across diverse academic domains and learner populations, automate knowledge graph construction, and enhance its adaptive scaffolding capabilities.
