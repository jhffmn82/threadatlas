Wasp in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Wasp, an insect producing honey and smeared with it, is hailed as a honey-producing genius; after Fox identifies and noses it, Wasp severely stings Fox, prompting a council to send a complimentary deputation.

The Wasp is identified by the Fox as the originator of the honey. After the Fox applies his nose to the Wasp, the Wasp stings him with severity, and the council resolves to send a complimentary deputation to the Wasp.
