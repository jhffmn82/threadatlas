the Tin Castle in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Tin Castle, Nick Chopper’s new residence in the Country of the Winkies, is a new tin castle full of lovely things, glittering in sunshine and kept rust-free by thousands of polishing Winkies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

23. How They Encountered the Flutterbudgets

The Tin Castle is Nick Chopper’s new residence in the Country of the Winkies. It is described as being full of lovely things and kept free of rust by thousands of Winkies. The travelers see it glittering in the sunshine and hurry toward it.

Tin Woodman's tin castle in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Tin Woodman's tin castle stands in southern Winkie Country, built entirely of brightly polished tin from ground to tallest turret. Its glittering turrets, walls, gates, grounds, and furnishings are tin; its grounds hold statues of Dorothy, Toto, the Scarecrow, the Wizard, the Shaggy Man, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Ozma.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Six

The Tin Woodman's tin castle stands in the southern part of the Winkie Country and has turrets that glitter in the sun. It is built entirely of brightly polished tin, with tin walls, gates, fountains, flowers, trees, bowers, furniture, and statues. The castle grounds contain statues of Dorothy, Toto, the Scarecrow, the Wizard, the Shaggy Man, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Ozma.
