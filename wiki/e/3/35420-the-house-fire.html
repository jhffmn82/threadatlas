the house fire in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A house fire at Dr. Becher's house begins during the night, apparently when the hydraulic engineer's crushed oil-lamp ignites wooden walls. It intensifies until the building is ablaze; firemen subdue it after the roof falls in, leaving the hydraulic machine destroyed.

The house fire at Dr. Becher's house begins during the night and intensifies until the entire building is ablaze. Firemen eventually subdue it, but the roof falls in and the machinery is destroyed. The fire is believed to have started when the hydraulic engineer's crushed oil-lamp ignited the wooden walls.
