VIII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE SPECKLED BAND

The Roylotts of Stoke Moran were once among the richest families in England, but four successive dissolute heirs ruined the family fortune. Their remaining estate consists of a few acres and the heavily mortgaged ancestral house.
