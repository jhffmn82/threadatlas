the purification ritual in Iphigenia in Tauris

Euripides · -0413/-0411

The purification ritual is Iphigenia's deception: she claims Orestes's bloodguilt has defiled Artemis's image and stages sea cleansing for Orestes, Pylades, and the image. She tells Thoas to keep the city still, makes ritual sounds, and uses the guards' distance to prepare escape.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

LEADER.

Iphigenia invents a purification ritual for Orestes, Pylades, and the image, claiming that their bloodguilt has defiled the goddess. She tells Thoas that the rite must occur at the sea and asks him to keep the city still and the shrine under cleansing fire.

CHORUS. [STROPHE.]

The cleansing ritual is a deception devised by Iphigenia. She makes ritual sounds and sings as though she is cleansing Orestes’ blood, while using the guards’ distance to prepare the escape.
