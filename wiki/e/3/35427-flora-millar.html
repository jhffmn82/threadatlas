Abstract written by a model

Flora Millar is a woman and former danseuse at the Allegro who had long known Lord Robert St. Simon and was devotedly attached to him. After his marriage, she threatens Hatty and creates a disturbance at the Doran residence while trying to enter after the wedding party, claiming a right to Lord Robert. Police remove and arrest her, and she is held in custody. Because she is later seen walking with Hatty in Hyde Park and a note’s initials seem suggestive, Lestrade suspects her of decoying Lady St. Simon away. The records state, however, that this suspicion is wrong and provide no evidence of her involvement in the disappearance.
