Flora Millar in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Flora Millar is a woman and former danseuse at the Allegro who had long known Lord Robert St. Simon and was devotedly attached to him. After his marriage, she threatens Hatty and creates a disturbance at the Doran residence while trying to enter after the wedding party, claiming a right to Lord Robert. Police remove and arrest her, and she is held in custody. Because she is later seen walking with Hatty in Hyde Park and a note’s initials seem suggestive, Lestrade suspects her of decoying Lady St. Simon away.
