notes

The unit is chiefly concerned with textual criticism, recording conjectures, manuscript readings, deletions, restorations, changes of speaker, and competing editorial judgments. Porson, Hermann, Markland, Dindorf, Elmsley, Musgrave, Reiske, Tyrwhitt, and the Cambridge editor are among those cited, and many passages are judged corrupt, interpolated, or spurious.
