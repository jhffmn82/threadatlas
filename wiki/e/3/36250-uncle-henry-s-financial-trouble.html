Uncle Henry's financial trouble in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Uncle Henry's financial trouble, caused by an unpaid mortgage, follows failed crops, rebuilding costs, poor health, and travel expenses. A banker demands payment within thirty days, threatening the farm and forcing Uncle Henry and Aunt Em to fear leaving and Dorothy working.

1 section; each heading opens the text

2. How Uncle Henry Got Into Trouble

Uncle Henry grows poorer because of failed crops, rebuilding costs, poor health, and the expense of a sea voyage to Australia with Dorothy. The banker demands payment within thirty days or the family must give up the farm. Uncle Henry and Aunt Em fear they will have to leave and that Dorothy may need to work.
