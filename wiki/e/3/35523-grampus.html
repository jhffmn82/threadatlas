Abstract written by a model

Grampus is an eminent, world-famous Cetacean scholar and author of an influential work on ancient civilisations. Challenged by Merman, he publishes a crushing review defending sound inquiry and exposing alleged ignorance and errors. His authority deters rebuttal; he later leaves Merman unanswered while followers continue his research, arguments, and attacks, amid Merman's claim that Grampus shifts ground and appropriates ideas.

III.

Grampus is the author of an influential work on ancient civilisations and the scholar whom Merman challenges. After learning of Merman's attack, he writes a crushing reply that exposes Merman's alleged ignorance and errors.

Meanwhile, how did he like it?

Grampus's learning and authority make Merman feel that Grampus has used scholarship to conceal sophistical evasions. Grampus does not answer Merman's rejoinder, while his followers attack Merman's theory; Merman later claims that a new work by Grampus shifts ground in a way suggesting that Grampus is appropriating his ideas.
