Abstract written by a model

The Welsh crwth is a fiddle resembling the rotta, distinguished by a central finger-board and few strings. It is shown in a modern form, then traced to the three-stringed Crwth Trithant, resembling a violin or rebeck.

CHAPTER VII.

The Welsh crwth is described as a species of fiddle resembling the rotta, but with a central finger-board and only a few strings. A modern crwth is illustrated, while an earlier three-stringed form called Crwth Trithant is described as resembling a violin or, more properly, a rebeck.
