Paper dolls’ fragility arises from paper construction: though strong and healthy, breezes and movements can blow, wrinkle, or scatter them.

10. How the Cuttenclips Lived

The paper dolls are strong and healthy but cannot withstand draughts or breezes. Laughter, wing movements, a swinging door, and especially the Shaggy Man’s sneeze can blow them over, wrinkle them, or scatter them.
