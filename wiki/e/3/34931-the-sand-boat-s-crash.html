L. Frank Baum · 1909

The sand-boat's crash occurs at rocks on the desert's edge when the shaggy man cannot lower the sail, throwing Dorothy, Button-Bright, Toto, Polychrome, and the shaggy man onto grass unharmed except for minor mishaps.

12. The Deadly Desert Crossed

The sand-boat's crash occurs when the boat reaches the rocks at the edge of the desert while the shaggy man cannot lower its sail. Dorothy, Button-Bright, Toto, Polychrome, and the shaggy man are thrown onto the grass, but they survive with minor troubles.
