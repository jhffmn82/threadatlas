BRIO

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: BRIOdual, BRIOjoint

Abstract written by a model

BRIO is a generator for XSum summarization. It uses candidate-level ranking loss, then cross-entropy in a second fine-tuning round. In reported XSum experiments, it uses retrieval or self-memory; BRIOjoint rises from 49.5 to 50.3 R-1 with self-memory.

BRIO is in one work on this site, Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
