Abstract written by a model

The golden sword is a graceful weapon forged by Agga-Groo from soft, pure gold. Though fragile and unsuitable for hard use, Queen Aquareine gives it a fairy charm, making it obey her commands, cut through enemies, and kill them instantly on touch. She first uses it to shatter a castle window and plans to use it against the prince of the sea devils.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

The golden sword is forged by Agga-Groo from soft, pure gold and has a graceful shape, a slender keen blade, and an ornamental handle. It would not withstand hard use because its edge would nick and curl, but Aquareine intends to give it fairy powers so it can obey her commands and fight their opponents.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

The golden sword receives a fairy charm from Queen Aquareine. She uses it to shatter the castle window and plans to use it against the prince of the sea devils.
