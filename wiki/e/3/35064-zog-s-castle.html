Abstract written by a model

Zog's castle is a strange, enchanted underwater coral castle, inlaid with silver and fitted with clear-glass windows. It stands at the center of a vast sea cavern, fifty fathoms beneath the sea, enclosed under a great dome with confined fresh water supplied by air fountains. Its earthly-castle-like interior has halls, stairs, balconies, rooms, corridors, guarded entrances and outlets, magical passages, a central chamber, throne, goldsmiths' shop, prisoners' rooms, the Rose Chamber, and the Peony Room. Many-eyed, quilled fish and powerful beings guard it; no one in the ocean can see it unless Zog wishes. Zog's power is supreme there: prisoners may move through parts of the castle under Sacho's supervision but cannot leave, while slaves, dozens or perhaps hundreds, work and are normally barred from leaving the great cave. The castle holds lavish gold, mirrors, unusual furniture, and goods plundered from sunken ships. Captives hope to escape through an opening in the dome, and later escape the interior through a shattered window into the dome. By the end, the castle is no longer enchanted: Zog's former slaves inherit it, remain and continue their work, and make Cap'n Joe their king.

Zog's castle is in one work on this site, The Sea Fairies. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

The Palace of Zog stands in the center of a vast sea cavern and is built of coral inlaid with silver, with clear glass windows. Its interior resembles an earthly castle, with halls, stairs, balconies, chairs, benches, curtained doorways, and magical passages. Strange many-eyed, quilled fish guard the entrance.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

The enchanted castle is Zog's underwater domain, where his power is supreme. Its passages are guarded, but Zog permits the prisoners to move throughout the castle while remaining unable to leave.

[Illustration: _Chap. 14._

Zog's enchanted castle contains lavish rooms decorated with gold, mirrors, unusual furniture, and objects plundered from sunken ships. It lies fifty fathoms beneath the sea and houses Zog's prisoners and many slaves, who are not normally allowed to leave the great cave.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Zog's castle is enclosed under a great dome and contains confined water kept fresh by air fountains. It has a large central chamber, guarded outlets, and a possible opening in the dome through which the captives hope to escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

The enchanted castle stands in a large cave beneath the ocean. No one in the ocean can see it unless Zog wishes, and its entrances are kept guarded.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Zog's castle contains rooms, corridors, a goldsmiths' shop, and entrances guarded by powerful beings. The group is allowed to move through parts of it under Sacho's supervision, while Zog repeatedly attempts to destroy them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Zog's castle contains the frozen rooms and gardens where Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia are imprisoned. The group escapes its interior through a shattered window into the surrounding dome.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Zog's silver castle is no longer enchanted and becomes the home of his former slaves. The slaves inherit it, continue their work there, and make Cap'n Joe their king.
