Abstract written by a model

In “SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection,” Llama2-chat13B is evaluated as a model trained using private data, and its retrieval-augmented version, Ret-Llama2-chat, is included among the baselines. In “DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models,” LLaMA-2-Chat models are selected to validate DRAGIN and other RAG baselines, using LLaMA-2-Chat-7B and LLaMA-2-Chat-13B. In “DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models,” LLaMA-2-Chat-13B is one of the selected LLMs used to evaluate DRAGIN and other RAG baselines. In “DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models,” Llama2-13b-chat is evaluated with and without retrieval augmentation and with multiple dynamic RAG methods; DRAGIN produces the highest listed results across all four datasets. In “Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain,” Llama-2-13b-chat is among the lower-performing models in the overall head-to-head tendency; the text does not report separate instruction-following details beyond broader findings for Llama-2 chat models. In “SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs,” Llama2-13b-chat is among the strongest models for SDG Evidence Identification, especially with two examples, and is competitive in recall in SDG-implicit SDG Target Detection settings, often benefiting from lexical retrieval. In “Unified Active Retrieval for Retrieval Augmented Generation,” Llama2-13B-chat’s self-aware classification accuracy rises from 73.48 using a value head to 76.28 by fine-tuning the whole model. In “Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” LLaMA-2-13B-chat is a pre-trained LLM fine-tuned with LoRA and the best-performing model in the analysis; INFO-RAG improves its zero-shot overall score from 43.23 to 46.55 and its NQ score from 50.36 to 54.04, and it serves as SearChain’s backbone.

and 1 more work

In “SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation,” LLaMA-2-chat with 7 billion parameters is used as the backbone LLM for SEAKR, with the same choices and constraints applied to baseline methods for fair comparison.
