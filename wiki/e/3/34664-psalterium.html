psalterium in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Psalterium denotes medieval triangular or square stringed instruments; a small sound-board form apparently prototyped the citole, while by the fourteenth century it became trapezium-shaped like a dulcimer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Medieval writers used psalterium for simple triangular and square stringed instruments, even though the forms differed. A small psalterium with strings over a sound-board was apparently the prototype of the citole, and the fourteenth-century psalterium had the trapezium shape of the dulcimer.
