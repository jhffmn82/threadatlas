Phrygian

Abstract written by a model

Phrygian is Helen’s Phrygian slave attendant and a survivor of palace conflict. He serves Helen, flees the palace during her murder, recounts Orestes and Pylades’ attack, fears death, and is pardoned after affirming that every person prefers light.

Phrygian is in one work on this site, The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

PHRYGIAN, CHORUS.

The Phrygian attendant escapes the palace and recounts how Orestes and Pylades attacked Helen and killed Clytemnestra. He fears death but is pardoned by Orestes after declaring that every person, even a slave, prefers to see the light.
