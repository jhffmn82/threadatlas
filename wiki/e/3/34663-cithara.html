Abstract written by a model

Cithara is a name for several stringed instruments varying greatly in form, sound power, and compass; a ninth-century St. Blasius example has a small top sound-board and appears to improve on the triangular psalterium.

CHAPTER VII.

Cithara was a name applied to several stringed instruments that varied greatly in form, sound power, and compass. A ninth-century cithara from the St. Blasius manuscript appears to improve on the triangular psalterium because it has a small sound-board at the top.
