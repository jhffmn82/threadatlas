Abstract written by a model

The Assyrian harp was a roughly four-foot, portable harp playable while dancing. It had no front pillar, hour-glass-shaped upper sound-holes, regularly arranged tuning-pegs below them, and possibly silk or catgut strings.

CHAPTER III.

The Assyrian harp was about four feet high but light enough to be carried and played while dancing. It had no front pillar, hour-glass-shaped sound-holes, tuning-pegs below them, and strings that may have been made of silk or catgut.
