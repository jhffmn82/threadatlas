1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X.

Peal-ringing was described as a pastime especially national to England. Peals commonly contained eight bells tuned to the diatonic scale, though sets of ten or twelve also occurred, and the bells were swung forcefully so that their mouths rose completely upward.
