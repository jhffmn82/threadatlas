riddle of the Sphinx in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The riddle asked what creature was two-footed, three-footed, and four-footed. Oedipus answered that it was a human being, who crawls on four limbs as an infant, walks on two feet as an adult, and uses a staff as a third foot in old age.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

KING OEDIPUS

Oedipus solved the Sphinx's riddle without assistance from the Thebans. His success ended the Sphinx's destruction of the city and led to his receiving the kingship and Jocasta's hand.

CHORUS.

The Sphinx's riddle is represented by the monstrous riddling maid whom Oedipus killed through surpassing skill. Solving it enabled him to rise to wealth and power and become a protector of Thebes.
