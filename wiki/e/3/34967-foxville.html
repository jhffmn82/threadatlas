Foxville in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Foxville is a fine, big, beautiful city in Fairyland, nearer to Oz than Kansas. It has marble houses decorated with birds and fox heads, a royal palace, and a fox theater. Ruled by King Dox and King Renard the Fourth, its civilized fox inhabitants welcome travelers, who stay overnight and leave through an arch.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

3. A Queer Village

Foxville is a beautiful city in Fairyland ruled by King Dox. Its marble houses are decorated with images of birds and fox heads, and it is nearer to Oz than to Kansas.

4. King Dox

Foxville is a large, beautiful city ruled by King Renard the Fourth. Its inhabitants are civilized foxes who wear clothing, raise fowl for food and feathers, attend a theater, and treat Dorothy, Button-Bright, and the shaggy man respectfully. The travelers stay there overnight before leaving through an arch on the opposite side of the village.
