Incest

Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, incest is part of Oedipus's predicted destiny; he later marries Jocasta, and they live together with their four children. In The Library, Incest is presented alongside parricide in the Oedipus legend and related tales, while the text rejects that such traditions necessarily preserve memories of an earlier widespread custom.
