La Bruyere in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

La Bruyere, a French writer, advocates discerning ridicule: identify genuine absurdity and express it gracefully to please and instruct, not indiscriminately burlesque serious subjects.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

X.

La Bruyere is quoted as saying that ridicule should be identified where it genuinely exists and drawn out gracefully in a way that pleases and instructs. His formulation is used to distinguish discriminating wit from the indiscriminate burlesquing of serious subjects.
