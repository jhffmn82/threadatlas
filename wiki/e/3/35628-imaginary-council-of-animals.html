imaginary council of animals in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

An imaginary council assembles to identify honeycomb’s maker, debates birds, the Musk-rat, and the Wasp, then accepts the Fox’s report that the Wasp produced the honey.

The imaginary council debates what sort of creature constructed the honeycomb. It hears competing claims about birds, the Musk-rat, and the Wasp, and ultimately accepts the Fox's report that the Wasp produced the honey.
