Aias' raid on the army's livestock

Abstract written by a model

Aias raids the army's flocks, herds, and herdmen alone at night, believing he is avenging himself on Greek leaders, and the raid is mistaken for an attack on the generals.

Aias conducts the raid alone at night after setting out with his weapon. He attacks the army's livestock and herdmen, believing that he is taking vengeance on the Greek leaders who denied him Achilles' armour.
