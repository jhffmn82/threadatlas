Supporting evidence is information in documents that directly helps answer questions. TRACE identifies it in retrieved documents by converting text into fine-grained knowledge triples and autoregressively selecting connected, grounded triples, reducing irrelevant context and improving answer accuracy.

Narrative, section by section

Introduction

Supporting evidence is information within documents that directly contributes to answering questions. TRACE identifies and integrates supporting evidence by converting documents into fine-grained knowledge triples and selecting logically connected triples.

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

Supporting evidence is the relevant information TRACE seeks within the retrieved documents. TRACE’s chain constructor identifies supporting evidence autoregressively, and its grounded triples help avoid irrelevant context and improve answer accuracy.
