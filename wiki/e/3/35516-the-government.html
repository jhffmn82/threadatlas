the Government in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Government, revered by the narrator’s father as the chief force for maintaining order, is opposed in the narrator’s learning to the morally charged idea of a rebel.

That part of my father's prime to which he oftenest referred had fallen on the d

Government was treated by the narrator's father as an object of awe and as the chief force required to maintain order. The narrator learned to place it in opposition to the morally charged idea of a rebel.
