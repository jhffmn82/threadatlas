Utensia in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Utensia is a kingdom ruled by King Kleaver, in a large forest clearing around stoves and kitchen equipment. Its inhabitants and military brigade are utensils, including spoons. Dorothy, unfamiliar with it, learns it has no food known to King Kleaver and is advised to seek blackberries.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

Utensia is a kingdom ruled by King Kleaver. Its military brigade is made up of spoons, and Dorothy has never heard of it before.

16. How Dorothy Visited Utensia

Utensia is a kingdom in a large forest clearing populated by animated kitchen utensils and equipment. It has no food that King Kleaver knows of, so Dorothy is advised to search the woods for blackberries.
