notes

The unit explains Greek usage through omitted prepositions, elliptical constructions, unusual appositions, alternative meanings, and words with both active and passive senses. It also records conjectural emendations, manuscript variants, punctuation disputes, proposed omissions, corrupt passages, and differing interpretations by commentators.

notes

The notes examine Greek constructions, supplied words, cognate accusatives, and alternative interpretations. They propose or adopt several emendations, question certain readings, identify awkward constructions, and report that Dindorf considered some lines spurious.
