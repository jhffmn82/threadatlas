Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the oracle expressly requires that one of Hercules’s descendants be devoted. In Hecuba and other plays, the oracles give differing instructions, but the relevant auspices agree that Ceres requires Macaria’s sacrifice.
