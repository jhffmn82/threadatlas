VII. THE ADVENTURE OF THE BLUE CARBUNCLE

The Countess of Morcar owns the blue carbuncle, which was stolen from her jewel-case at the Hotel Cosmopolitan. She has offered a reward of £1000 for its recovery and is said to have sentimental reasons for wanting it back.

“So much for Mr. Henry Baker,” said Holmes when he had closed the door
