Abstract written by a model

The Ku Klux Klan is a secret society founded by ex-Confederate soldiers after the Civil War, formerly active principally in the Southern states and using power for political purposes. It sent warning letters, marked “K. K. K.” and sometimes written in red ink, before committing outrages, including murder or driving targets away. It persecuted Elias, Joseph, and John Openshaw, then the movement suddenly collapsed in 1869.
