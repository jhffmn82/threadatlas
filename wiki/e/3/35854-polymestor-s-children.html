Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Polymestor's children accompany him into the tent and are stabbed and killed by the Trojan women. In Hecuba and other plays, the sons of Polymestor are separated from their father and killed by the Trojan women with concealed swords.
