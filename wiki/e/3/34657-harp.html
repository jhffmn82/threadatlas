CHAPTER I.

The harp can be traced in primitive forms among several barbarous tribes. The Damaras use a bowed, stringed instrument as a musical instrument, while neighboring African tribes add gourds, multiple strings, and wooden sound-boards; increasingly improved forms approach the construction of the harp.

THE HINDUS.

The Hindu harp, called chang, had become almost obsolete. Earlier drawings depict it with a triangular frame, making it closely resemble the Assyrian harp in construction, shape, and size.
