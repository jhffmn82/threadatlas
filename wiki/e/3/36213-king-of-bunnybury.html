King of Bunnybury

Abstract written by a model

The King of Bunnybury is a rabbit monarch elected by Bunnybury rabbits. Though he rules, wears a crown and ermine, owns an elaborate wardrobe, and enjoys luxurious furnishings, he is initially miserable because he was made king against his will and Bunnybury law prevents his resignation. He longs for his former forest life and asks Dorothy to seek Glinda’s help in replacing him. He entertains Princess Dorothy, presents Bunnybury’s entertainers and Bodyguard, and considers leaving with rabbits and possessions. After reconsidering Bunnybury’s pleasant features, he changes his mind, promises to remain merry and gay, and resolves to perform his duty by his subjects.

King of Bunnybury is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

20. How Dorothy Lunched With a King

The Rabbit King is miserable because he was elected against his will and cannot resign under Bunnybury's laws. He misses his former life as a wild rabbit in the forest, though he takes pride in his clothes, wardrobe, and luxurious furnishings. After Dorothy promises to ask Glinda the Good to replace him, he becomes joyful and arranges to entertain Dorothy and present her to the court.

21. How the King Changed His Mind

The King presents Princess Dorothy with Bunnybury’s entertainers and Bodyguard, explaining that they have comforted or protected him. He first asks Dorothy to request that Glinda let him take some rabbits and possessions away, but later decides to stay in Bunnybury and promises to stop complaining, remain merry, and do his duty by his subjects.
