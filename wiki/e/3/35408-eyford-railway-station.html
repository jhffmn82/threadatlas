Eyford railway station in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Eyford railway station is a little, dim-lit railway station where the narrator arrives after eleven o’clock as the train’s only departing passenger; its porter is on duty again in the morning, unaware of Colonel Lysander Stark and any waiting carriage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

The narrator arrives at the little, dim-lit Eyford railway station after eleven o’clock and is the only passenger to leave the train. The same porter is on duty when he returns in the morning, but the porter has not heard of Colonel Lysander Stark or seen a carriage waiting for the narrator.
