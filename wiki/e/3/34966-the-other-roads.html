the other roads in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Stationary roads lead somewhere and meet at the farm-house; at Butterfield, five crossroads become many landmarkless roads, leaving Dorothy and Toto unable to find home until the Shaggy Man proposes the seventh.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

1. The Way to Butterfield

The crossroads change from five roads into many roads running in every direction, with no familiar landmarks remaining. Dorothy and Toto cannot determine which road leads home, and the Shaggy Man suggests taking the seventh road because seven is lucky for girls named Dorothy.
