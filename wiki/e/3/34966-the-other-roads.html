1. The Way to Butterfield

The crossroads change from five roads into many roads running in every direction, with no familiar landmarks remaining. Dorothy and Toto cannot determine which road leads home, and the Shaggy Man suggests taking the seventh road because seven is lucky for girls named Dorothy.
