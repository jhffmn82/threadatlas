In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it is the event in which Hecuba and the Trojan women murder Polymestor's children after luring him into the tent. In Hecuba and other plays, it is the event in which Hecuba arranges the murder of Polymestor’s sons as revenge for Polydore’s murder.
