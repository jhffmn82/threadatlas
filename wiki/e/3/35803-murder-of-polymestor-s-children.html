murder of Polymestor’s sons in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Hecuba arranges the murder of Polymestor’s sons as revenge for Polydore’s murder. The Trojan women hold Polymestor and stab his sons with swords concealed beneath their garments.
