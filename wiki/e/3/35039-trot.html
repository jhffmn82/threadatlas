  • In The Magic of Oz, Trot is Ozma's guest and friend who helps seek the Magic Flower, becomes rooted beside Cap'n Bill, and returns safely to the Royal Palace.

  • In The Lost King of Oz, Trot is a United States girl who lives in Oz, attends Ozma's feast, is transported to Morrow, and investigates the golden feather and silver casket.

Trot in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Trot, whose real name is Mayre, is a young human girl from the earth and the daughter of Charlie Griffiths. She loves the ocean and is especially close to Cap'n Bill, her dear friend and traveling companion; she trusts his wisdom, enjoys his stories and toy-making jackknife, and is protected by him. Curious, outspoken, and adventurous, she wants to see mermaids and accepts their invitation despite danger and concern for her mother. With Cap'n Bill, Trot rows to Giant's Cave, meets the mermaids, and is changed so she can live beneath the water, with a pink, iridescent fish tail while her dress remains unaffected. As Queen Aquareine's guest, she marvels at sea gardens and palaces, enjoys sea fruit, gowns, pearls, and her room, asks many questions about ocean creatures, and reacts strongly to what she finds foolish, dangerous, or cruel. She is delighted by wonders but dislikes snapping eels, sea spiders, and octopuses. During journeys through the ocean, Trot shows calm courage amid sea devils and other dangers. Zog captures her, Cap'n Bill, and the mermaids, but she refuses to abandon Cap'n Bill, calls Zog wicked, helps consider escape, and remains confident in Aquareine's protection. Protected by the Magic Circle, she survives attacks, boiling water, a collapsing ceiling, and imprisonment in ice. After Aquareine frees her, she follows the queen in the escape, faces fear and pursuit, and says they have done their best and had a fine time. When the danger ends, Trot chooses to return home, says goodbye to the former slaves, receives Aquareine's pearl-set gold ring to summon the mermaids, regains her usual shape, reunites with Cap'n Bill in their boat, and leaves Giant's Cave.

Trot notices that the Magic Umbrella has passed the first island and helps determine that “Sky Island” may be a different destination. She becomes damp while passing through a rain cloud, imagines that an island might exist in the sky, and holds tight during the descent and landing.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

Trot helps free Cap'n Bill and speaks up against the Boolooroo's treatment of strangers. She shares apples with him and observes the blue garden, palace, and the bells he rings by touching parts of his body. When the travelers try to depart, blue cords catch her and pin her arms to her sides.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

Trot is claimed by the Six Snubnosed Princesses as their maid and slave. She protests that she will not be anyone's slave, but the princesses tie ribbons to her and drag her into the Blue Palace.

THE TRIBULATION OF TROT

Trot is brought to the luxurious apartments of the Six Snubnosed Princesses and refuses to accept their claim that she is a slave. After being shaken and slapped, she pretends to obey, feeds and befriends all six pets, and receives their protection when Princess Indigo attacks her. She stays in the apartments with the pets, ignores the Princesses' demands to come out, and goes to the servants' quarters to find dinner.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

Trot is reunited with Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill in the servants' hall and sits with the parrot, peacock, lamb, cat, dog, and blue rabbit around her. She says that the Snubnosed Princesses hate her and urges Button-Bright to recover the Magic Umbrella quickly. She waits with the others for Button-Bright to return so they can escape.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

Trot escapes the palace with Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill and holds hands with them so they will not become separated in the dark. The blue parrot later finds her and lands on her shoulder.

THROUGH THE FOG BANK

Trot becomes soaked while traveling through the Fog Bank in her thin blue silk costume. She helps hold up Cap'n Bill when he trips and later rides on the common frog's back with him and Button-Bright. After the frog's leap, she lands with the others on the soft grass of a meadow.

THE PINK COUNTRY

Trot emerges from the Fog Bank with Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, and the parrot, soaked and chilled but soon warmed by the pink sun. She explains to the Pinkies that the travelers are not Blueskins and agrees to go peacefully before Tourmaline.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

Trot is brought before Tourmaline with Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill after escaping from the Blueskins through the Fog Bank. She argues that a ruler deserves respect and asks whether the Pink Country has fairies who could help them return home.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

Trot stays with Button-Bright and Cap'n Bill at Coralie’s home and admires the splendor of the setting and rising sun. She asks to remain peacefully in the Pink Country until they can find a way back to the Earth, but learns that the Council may either accept or cast them away.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

Trot becomes worried when she realizes that she, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill may be thrown over the edge of the island. She suggests that Tourmaline should cast the deciding vote and later takes courage from Button-Bright when Rosalie votes to have them thrown into the sky.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

Trot is encouraged by the possibility that fairies will protect her and travels with Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright toward the edge of the Pink Country. Polychrome identifies her as lighter in color than Tourmaline, making her the rightful Queen under the Pink Country succession law. Trot receives Tourmaline's circlet and is accepted as the new ruler.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

Trot becomes the new Queen of the Pink Country and is dressed in one of its plain pink gowns. She greets the Pinkies, enjoys their enthusiastic homage, and initially feels proud of her new position, but she agrees that recovering the Magic Umbrella and returning home may be preferable. She approves the plan to prepare for an expedition against the Boolooroo.

THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

Trot worries over Cap'n Bill after his capture and asks Rosalie for a way to enter the Blue City. Wearing Rosalie's magic ring, she climbs the rope ladder, descends inside the City, and begins searching for Cap'n Bill while invisible.

TROT'S INVISIBLE ADVENTURE

Trot goes to the palace at night to find Cap'n Bill. She frees Tiggle by distracting the guard, then searches for Cap'n Bill and unknowingly helps Ghip-Ghisizzle escape when she enters his room.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

Trot enters the palace unseen, causes the Six Snubnosed Princesses to fight, and searches for Cap'n Bill and the Magic Umbrella. She frees Cap'n Bill and helps him reach the wall, but watches as the Blueskins recapture him. Trot stays behind in the palace to continue searching for the Magic Umbrella.

THE AMAZING CONQUEST OF THE BLUES

Trot remains invisible while following Cap'n Bill to the Room of the Great Knife and searches for a way to save him. She finds a rope, uses it to drag Cap'n Bill's frame away from the falling Great Knife twice, ties the billygoat to a pillar, and frees Cap'n Bill. She then helps him bind the Boolooroo beneath the knife and suggests that they can run the Blue Country themselves.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

Trot takes off Rosalie's ring and declares herself Queen of the Pinkies and Boss of the Blues. She makes the soldiers obey her, orders the Six Snubnosed Princesses locked in their rooms, and appoints Cap'n Bill as her General of the Army and Royal Manager of the Blue Country. She searches for the Magic Umbrella and orders the Blueskins to welcome the Pinkies with music, dancing, eating, and a grand celebration.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Trot is welcomed by the Blueskins and becomes Queen of Sky Island, including the Pink and Blue Countries. She appoints Ghip-Ghisizzle as Boolooroo of the Blues, refuses to permit further patching, banishes the former Boolooroo and the Six Snubnosed Princesses from the palace, and orders the Great Knife destroyed.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

Trot helps lead the search for the Magic Umbrella and questions the palace servants. After the umbrella is found, she refuses to leave immediately because she must fulfill her duties as Queen of Sky Island, including arranging for the Pinkies to return home safely.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

Trot enters the Fog Bank with Button-Bright, Rosalie the Witch, Cap'n Bill, and the Pinkies. She argues that the umbrella must accompany them and follows the transformed elephant through the fog to the Pink Country.

TROT REGULATES THE PINKIES

Trot returns to the Pink Country with her companions and becomes its Queen temporarily. She creates a new law giving the Queen equal conditions with the people, appoints Rosalie the Witch as Queen, orders a new palace and statues, and prepares to return to Earth.

THE JOURNEY HOME

Trot gives the parrot to Rosalie, says goodbye to the Pinkies, and travels home with Cap'n Bill and Button-Bright in the Magic Umbrella. She reaches Trot's house, greets her mother, and later receives Button-Bright's postal card.

Trot in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Trot is a real, mortal little girl, a traveler, and a friend of the Scarecrow, Benny, and Philador. Though she was formerly Queen of Sky Island, she values being a little girl in the Emerald City, where she lives with Ozma of Oz and remains ten as long as she wishes. After a shipwreck and Benny and the Scarecrow's collision with her, she joins their journey toward the Emerald City. Akbad carries her to the Ozure Isles, where she is chosen to save them but is ordered to serve Quiberon as his mortal maiden. With her companions, she flees through caves, reaches Cave City, and is captured there; King Silly the Second prepares to turn her into a shadow. She survives an explosion, escapes to Lake Orizon with Orpah's help, reunites with Benny and the Scarecrow, and travels toward Ozma to seek help for the Queen of the Ozure Isles. Along the way she endures the Round-abouties, rides High Boy, defends the Scarecrow, and reaches the Emerald City. She reports the group's experiences to Ozma, then travels to the Ozure Isles. After Benny chooses to remain a stone man, she celebrates him and organizes a party for him. Cheeriobed makes her Princess of the Ozure Isles, gives her a sapphire ring instead of a crown, and grants her the privilege of living in Sapphire City whenever she wishes; she then gallops away with the Prince of the Ozure Isles.

CHAPTER 13

Trot becomes giddy and breathless in the revolving door and struggles to stand or move inside the round house. The Round-abouties pull her through their circles and place her on a wooden zebra, but she escapes their ring leader by opening the tower door and climbing the spiral stairway. When her breath fails, Benny carries her to the top, and she later slides with him and the Scarecrow from the roof into the Munchkin Country.

CHAPTER 14

Trot tells Benny about conquering Sky Island and becoming its Queen, though she prefers being a little girl in the Emerald City. She recounts the group's experiences in Quiberon's cave, their meeting with Orpah, and their escape to the mainland, then rides High Boy toward the Emerald City to ask Ozma for help.

CHAPTER 15

Trot travels through Shutter Town with the others and objects when High Boy makes fun of the Scarecrow. She crosses the river on High Boy, is drenched when water pours from his umbrella tail, takes Herby's pills, and recognizes the green glow of the Emerald City's tower lights.

CHAPTER 16

Trot and her companions reach the Emerald City, according to Orpah's report to Cheeriobed. They have escaped injury, outwitted the Cave Men, trapped Quiberon in a passageway, and crossed the lake to the mainland.

CHAPTER 17

Trot returns to the Emerald City with the Scarecrow, High Boy, Prince Philador, Herby, and Benny. She tells Ozma and the others about Benny's coming to life, their fall to Oz, and their frightening experiences in Cave City, then travels with the group to the Ozure Isles.

CHAPTER 20

Trot welcomes Benny's decision to remain a stone man and praises his strength, appearance, and dancing. She is made a Princess of the Ozure Isles by Cheeriobed and receives a sapphire ring, with the privilege of living in Sapphire City whenever she wishes.
