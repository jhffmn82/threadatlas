John Dough in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

King John Dough, an edible gingerbread ruler of Hiland and Loland, arrives at Ozma's royal banquet worried visitors may eat him. Assured of safety by the Scarecrow, he gives Ozma his pearl-decorated gingerbread crown, a birthday present.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

21. Dorothy Receives the Guests

King John Dough arrives as the edible ruler of the two kingdoms of Hiland and Loland. He worries that the visitors may want to eat him, but the Scarecrow assures him that he is safe in Oz. He gives Ozma a lovely gingerbread crown decorated with pearls.
