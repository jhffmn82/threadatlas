moral currency in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Moral currency is a spiritual value system: inspiring facts and traditions foster generous motives and social life, but burlesque debases it by desecrating beauty, majesty, and pathos.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

X.

Moral currency names the value of inspiring facts and traditions in producing generous motives and elevating social life. It is debased when greedy buffoonery lowers the value of historic beauty, majesty, and pathos, leaving desecrated symbols unable to command ennobling emotions.
