the Lascar

Abstract written by a model

The Lascar manages an opium den in Swandam Lane, aided by a Dane, and has a reputation for vile antecedents and a grudge against Sherlock Holmes. He lodges Neville St. Clair, knows St. Clair’s connection to Hugh Boone, and keeps the rooms where St. Clair changes identities. St. Clair gives him a ring and a note for his wife; the Lascar probably passes the note to a sailor, delaying it. When Mrs. St. Clair seeks answers, he resists her and denies knowledge of Hugh Boone.
