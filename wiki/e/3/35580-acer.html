Acer in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Acer is a speaker and moral critic who questions asserted virtues, judging fairness and goodwill by tender use of public influence toward overlooked, disadvantaged people.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.

Acer questions the value of virtues that must continually be asserted against appearances. He argues that fairness and goodwill are tested by whether a person uses public influence tenderly toward obscure or disadvantaged people whose claims may otherwise be ignored.
