Also called: Apollo foretold, Apollo’s oracle, Apollo’s word, a heaven-sent oracle of dread import, god’s word, heaven-sent oracle, oracle of Apollo, oracles, prophetic, signs, the oracle
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, the oracle of Apollo foretells Oedipus’s death at Colonus and that he will murder his father Laius.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the oracle foretells Oedipus killing his father and marrying his mother, a prophecy later revealed to have been fulfilled.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Apollo’s oracle foretells Oedipus’s miseries, eventual respite, and benefits for the land receiving him, while warning his sons that destruction awaits them there.

OEDIPUS AT COLONUS

Apollo’s oracle foretold Oedipus’s miseries, a final respite after many years, and benefits for the land where he would dwell. It also promised signs such as an earthquake, thunderclap, or flash of lightning to mark the fulfillment of the prophecy.
