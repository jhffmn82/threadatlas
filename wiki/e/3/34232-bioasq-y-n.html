The MIRAGE Benchmark / Evaluation Settings .. Component Datasets

BioASQ-Y/N is a biomedical research QA dataset in MIRAGE with 618 questions, two options per question, and an average question length of 17 tokens.

Results .. Practical Recommendations

BioASQ-Y/N is a research-related MIRAGE task that benefits primarily from PubMed. Unlike PubMedQA*, its accuracy rises substantially as more snippets are supplied, and it can also benefit from reciprocal rank fusion when individual retrievers perform poorly.
