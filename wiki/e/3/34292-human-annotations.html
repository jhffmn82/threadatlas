Abstract

ARES uses a small set of human-annotated datapoints for prediction-powered inference to mitigate potential prediction errors. It accurately evaluates systems using only a few hundred human annotations during evaluation.

Results & Analysis / 5.1 .. 5.4

Human annotations are used for PPI validation and for comparison with sampled annotation methods. ARES achieves more accurate ranking than sampled annotations while using 78% fewer annotations, and experiments indicate that 150 annotations are the minimum number required in one setting.

Limitations

The annotations used by ARES may be easy to generate for general-domain applications but can require specialized expertise in domains such as law, medicine, and finance.
