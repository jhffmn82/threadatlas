Button-Bright's transformation in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Button-Bright's transformation begins when King Dox, King Renard the Fourth, waves his paw and replaces the boy's human head with a fox head. The King cannot undo it, worrying Dorothy and the shaggy man, though foxes consider it an honor. Later, Button-Bright falls into the Truth Pond, which reverses the change and restores his original appearance.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4. King Dox

King Renard transforms Button-Bright by waving his paw, causing the boy's human head to disappear and a fox's head to appear on his shoulders. The King says he cannot reverse the change, so Button-Bright must continue wearing the fox head for the time being. The transformation worries Dorothy and the shaggy man, though the foxes regard it as a great honor.

13. The Truth Pond

Button-Bright had been transformed by King Dox of Foxville so that he had a fox head. Falling into the Truth Pond reverses the transformation and restores his original appearance.
