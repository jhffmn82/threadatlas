Bunbury inhabitants in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Bunbury inhabitants, men, women, and children made of buns and bread, seclude themselves to avoid being eaten; frosted buns are especially important. They fear strangers, briefly offer spare food, then expel Dorothy, Billina, and Toto after Toto eats inhabitants and Billina is accused of harm.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

17. How They Came to Bunbury

The inhabitants initially fear Dorothy, Billina, and Toto because they are strangers and the inhabitants are edible to humans. Some inhabitants offer Dorothy spare food objects, but they become hostile after Toto eats Crumpets and a Salt-rising Biscuit and Billina is accused of harming a Raisin Bunn. They order the travelers to leave Bunbury.
