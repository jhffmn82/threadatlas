Philoctetes' pain

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: agony, anguish, direst woe, malignant pain, pain, poignant woe, this pain

Abstract written by a model

Philoctetes' pain is described as unutterable, soul-devouring, and capable of making him beg for death. During its recurrence he asks Neoptolemus to cut off his foot, burn him, or remain beside him until sleep relieves the attack.
