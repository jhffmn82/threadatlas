sea devils in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The sea devils are a race of fierce, wicked sea monsters: much larger than the bashful octopus, bright scarlet with black stripes, long snaky feelers, curling legs, and a prince who is their largest and fiercest member. Cousins of the bashful octopus, they live near the dismal caverns and are feared by sea people nearly as much as Zog. They are Zog the Terrible's only permitted sea-dwelling servants and guard his castle, its entrances, the great dome's opening, and routes toward the open ocean, though they dare not enter the enchanted castle. At Zog's command, they use cuttlefish to drive Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia toward the Devil Caves, then surround them and interlace their feelers into a scarlet-arm cage. They carry the prisoners to the castle entrance, but the group escapes through a silver passage, which the sea devils do not follow. Later, they remain numerous enemies guarding the passages and threatening to block escape again.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

The sea devils are described as much larger than an octopus, bright scarlet with black stripes, fierce, and terrible. They are the only ocean creatures the mermaids greatly fear, and they live near the dismal caverns.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

The sea devils use cuttlefish to drive the group toward the Devil Caves. They surround the travelers, laugh at the fairy circles, and interlace their long feelers to form a cage that carries the prisoners forward.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

The sea devils trap Trot, Cap'n Bill, Queen Aquareine, and Princess Clia in a cage of scarlet arms. They allow the cage to open but laugh harshly as the group escapes, warning that the escape has not yet fully succeeded. They do not follow into the silver passage.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

The sea devils are the only sea dwellers permitted to serve Zog. Acting on his command, they surrounded and tricked the prisoners into reaching the entrance of his enchanted castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

The sea devils guard the great dome's opening and are described as a race with curling legs. Their prince is the largest and fiercest of them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

The sea devils guard the route to the open ocean and previously drove Trot and the others back into the tunnel. Cap'n Bill expects them to block the group's escape again.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

The sea devils are enemies of the group. The passages out of the castle are guarded by scores of them, making the opening in the dome the preferred escape route.
