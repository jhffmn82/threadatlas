Five orange pips are a recognized Klan warning token, enclosed in warning letters. They appear first in Elias Openshaw’s letter from Pondicherry, then Joseph Openshaw’s from Dundee, and finally John Openshaw’s from London. Holmes later sends five orange pips to Captain James Calhoun, but the envelope never reaches him.
