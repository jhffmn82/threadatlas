the planned sacrifice of Orestes in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

The planned sacrifice of Orestes is to occur at Artemis's temple in barbarian land, where he is destined for ritual death by an Argive woman's hand and Iphigenia is to conduct the strangers' sacrifice. Iphigenia prevents it by pretending purification, leading the strangers to the shore, and helping them escape.

Orestes is destined for the gory streams and the sacrifice in the temple. He expects to die by an Argive woman's hand at the altar, while Iphigenia's role is to conduct the sacrifice of strangers.

Thoas expects Iphigenia to begin the sacrifice of the strangers in the temple. Iphigenia instead falsely presents the sacrifice as a purification and uses the occasion to lead the strangers to the shore and help them escape.
