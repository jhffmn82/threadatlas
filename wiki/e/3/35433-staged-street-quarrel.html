staged street quarrel in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A planned operation by Sherlock Holmes, the staged street quarrel uses hired accomplices near Briony Lodge. Posing as loafers, guardsmen, and bystanders, they create a convincing fight so Holmes can feign injury amid the disturbance.

The staged street quarrel is carried out by accomplices posing as loafers, guardsmen, and other bystanders near Briony Lodge. The fight gives Sherlock Holmes an opportunity to pretend to be struck and injured while the doctor and the other participants make the disturbance appear genuine.
