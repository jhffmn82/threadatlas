Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3. A Queer Village

The fox-captain and his soldiers surround Dorothy's party and initially declare them prisoners. After the Love Magnet affects him, he becomes friendly, escorts the party to King Dox, and instructs them in the proper way to enter the royal room.
