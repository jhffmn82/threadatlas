the planned murder of Helen

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Helen's murder, the death of Helen, the havoc, the murder, the murder of Helen, the murder that is perpetrated, the slaughtered Helen, the violated rites of Helen

Abstract written by a model

Orestes and Pylades plan to enter the house pretending to lament, conceal their swords, isolate Helen's attendants, and kill Helen. They justify the intended murder as vengeance for the Greeks who died and for the families left bereaved by the Trojan War. The attack begins, and Helen is heard crying that she has been slain. Orestes attempts to kill Helen, but she vanishes from the palace before the sword reaches her. Apollo states that Helen was not slain and that he carried her away to become immortal.
