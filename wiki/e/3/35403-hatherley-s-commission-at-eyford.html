IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Hatherley accepts Colonel Lysander Stark’s offer of fifty guineas for a nighttime professional opinion about a hydraulic stamping machine. Although he is uneasy about Stark’s manner, the secrecy, and the late hour, he travels to Eyford as agreed.
