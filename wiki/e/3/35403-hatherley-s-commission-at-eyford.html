Abstract written by a model

Hatherley’s Eyford commission is a secret nighttime job: he accepts Colonel Lysander Stark’s fifty-guinea offer to inspect an out-of-order hydraulic stamping machine, travels by train from Paddington, and arrives in Eyford uneasy about the secrecy, Stark, and late hour.

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Hatherley accepts Colonel Lysander Stark’s offer of fifty guineas for a nighttime professional opinion about a hydraulic stamping machine. Although he is uneasy about Stark’s manner, the secrecy, and the late hour, he travels to Eyford as agreed.
