In "Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering," accuracy, also called cover-EM, checks whether ground truth appears in generated answers and is reported for the main methods, while SearChain lacks F1 for long-form output. In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," ground-truth coverage is used as the evaluation metric because QA task answers are relatively short, measuring performance based on whether the gold response appears in the model's output.
