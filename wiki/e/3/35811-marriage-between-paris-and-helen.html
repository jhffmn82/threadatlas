Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it is the union of Paris and Helen blamed for bringing the Greeks against Troy and causing widespread suffering. In Hecuba and other plays, it is the marriage associated with Paris and Helen that is blamed for Troy’s suffering and called accursed. In Heroides and Amores, it is the marriage Paris seeks with Helen, promising that a future ceremony will correct her abduction.

marriage between Paris and Helen in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · about 24 to 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where marriage between Paris and Helen first appears

Abstract written by a model

Paris seeks marriage with Helen, whom Venus promised him and whose image sponsors his ship. Valuing her above kingdoms and rewards, he asks her to marry him, claiming a future ceremony will correct her abduction and promising oaths and his kingdom.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XVI

Venus has promised Helen to Paris for his marriage chamber, and a painted image of Venus accompanies Paris's ship as sponsor of the marriage. Paris places Helen above kingdoms and every other reward offered to him.

nun ego coniugium generosae degener opto,

Paris asks Helen to enter a marriage with him and argues that their present wrongdoing will be corrected by a future marriage ceremony. He promises to bind himself to her through oaths and to make her seek his kingdom.
