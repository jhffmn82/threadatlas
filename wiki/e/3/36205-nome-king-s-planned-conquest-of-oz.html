Abstract written by a model

The Nome King and the Nomes plan to conquer Oz by marching an army of evil spirits and Nomes through an underground tunnel to the Emerald City. Their intended attack threatens destruction, plunder, enslavement, and execution, turning Oz into a desert and placing its people under Nome control. Ozma discovers the tunnel's purpose and fears Oz cannot defeat the attackers, while the Tin Woodman resolves to stand with her as Oz's homes and institutions face ruin.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

18. How Ozma Looked into the Magic Picture

The Nomes' planned attack on Oz involves marching their army through the underground tunnel to the Emerald City. Princess Ozma realizes that the tunnel is being dug for this purpose.

24. How the Tin Woodman Told the Sad News

The Nome King's planned conquest of Oz is intended to destroy the country and bring its people under his control. Ozma believes that Oz's inhabitants lack the power to defeat the attacking evil spirits and Nomes, while the Tin Woodman prepares to stand beside her despite the danger.

25. How the Scarecrow Displayed His Wisdom

The planned conquest and destruction of Oz would leave the country a desert, make its people slaves, and destroy its handsome buildings. The travelers believe that Ozma's palace, the Scarecrow's mansion, the Tin Woodman's castle, and other homes and institutions would be lost.
