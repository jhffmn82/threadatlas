Abstract written by a model

Flutterbudgets are people marked by foolish fears and nervous worries about harmless or imagined dangers. Called the Defensive Settlements of Oz when sent to live at Flutterbudget Center, they repeatedly imagine catastrophes, from children being eaten to a finger prick causing death.

Flutterbudgets is in one work on this site, The Emerald City of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

23. How They Encountered the Flutterbudgets

Flutterbudgets are people who worry over harmless events and imagined dangers. In this unit, they fear that children might be crushed or eaten, that a small finger prick will cause death, and that closing their eyes will make them grow together. They are associated with foolish fears, nervousness, and repeated “ifs.”
