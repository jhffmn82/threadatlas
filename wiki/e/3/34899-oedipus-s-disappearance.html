Abstract written by a model

In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Oedipus's disappearance is witnessed only by Theseus after Oedipus dismisses his daughters and remains alone with him. In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Oedipus's disappearance is his vanishing from sight, witnessed only by Theseus after he sends his daughters away.

Oedipus’s disappearance is a vanishing from sight: after he sends his daughters away, witnesses find him gone; only Theseus sees what happened and prays to Earth and Heaven.
