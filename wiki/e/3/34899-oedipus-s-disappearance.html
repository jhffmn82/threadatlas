No lightning-flash or tempest causes it; the Messenger says that a heavenly messenger or the Earth opening beneath him may have carried him away, and that only Theseus witnessed what happened.

Oedipus's disappearance in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

Oedipus’s disappearance is a vanishing from sight: after he sends his daughters away, witnesses find him gone; only Theseus sees what happened and prays to Earth and Heaven.

After Oedipus sends his daughters away, the witnesses look back and find that he has vanished from sight. They see only Theseus, who raises his hand as though shielding his eyes from an unbearable sight and then prays to Earth and Heaven.
