Abstract written by a model

Colonel Openshaw, a former Confederate colonel and Florida planter, makes a considerable fortune in America and retires to a Sussex estate near Horsham. He lives fiercely apart from society, keeps a brass box and papers from America in a locked attic, and is believed to fear the Ku Klux Klan. After receiving five orange pips from Pondicherry marked “K. K. K.,” he burns papers, writes a diary page, and makes a will leaving his estate to Joseph Openshaw. Seven weeks later he dies in a pool, officially judged a suicide.

V. THE FIVE ORANGE PIPS

Elias Openshaw, John’s uncle, was a former Confederate colonel who lived near Horsham after making a fortune in America. He received five orange pips from Pondicherry with the mark K. K. K., burned papers from a brass box, made a will, and died seven weeks later in a pool; his death was judged a suicide.
