Abstract written by a model

In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Hippolytus's horses become terrified by the bull from the sea, ignore his control, and drag the chariot into the rocks. In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, the horses panic at the Sea-Bull, resist control, crash Hippolytus's chariot into rocks, and cause his fatal injuries.
