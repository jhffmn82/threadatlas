Abstract written by a model

The goose club, instituted by Windigate at the Alpha Inn, collected a few pence weekly from members and provided each a Christmas goose. It supplied Henry Baker with a goose, whose bird became the first link in Holmes’s investigation into the stolen blue carbuncle.
