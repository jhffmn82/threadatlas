the Stark residence in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

An isolated country house near Eyford, dark and silent, has barred shutters, neglected labyrinthine corridors, and concealed passages. A narrator is brought there without seeing its exterior; after escaping, he cannot see the house or garden from the road.

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

The residence is a dark, silent, isolated old house with barred shutters, neglected upper corridors, and concealed passages. The narrator is taken there without seeing its exterior, and after his escape he finds that neither the house nor its garden can be seen from where he lies near the road.
