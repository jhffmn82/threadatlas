the escape plan

Also called: all these matters, contrivance, device, journey homeward, our escape

Abstract written by a model

requires the ship [to carry Iphigenia, Orestes, Pylades, and the image away] Iphigenia will tell the king that Orestes and Pylades are polluted and cannot be sacrificed until they are purified at the sea. She will claim that the image was touched by them and must also be washed, then carry it to the ship herself while the others help preserve the secret.
