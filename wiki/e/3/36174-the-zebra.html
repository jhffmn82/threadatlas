the zebra in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The zebra, a white animal with dark brown bars living in the forest, drinks daily at the zebra's pool, disputes land versus water with a soft-shell crab, asks Dorothy and companions to decide, learns the crab is correct, and carries him toward the forest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

15. How Dorothy Happened to Get Lost

The zebra asks Dorothy and her companions to settle his long dispute with a soft-shell crab over whether there is more land or water in the world. After learning that the crab is correct, he becomes humbled, quarrels with the crab, and carries him back toward the forest.
