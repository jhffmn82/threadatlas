Abstract written by a model

A Peruvian flute found in an ancient tomb was made from a human bone, with four finger-holes on its upper surface and a blowing end at one extremity. Similar bone flutes from Truxillo in the British Museum differ in hole arrangements and blowing-end material.

CHAPTER VI.

A Peruvian bone flute found in an ancient tomb was made from a human bone and had four upper finger-holes, with the blowing end at one extremity. Two similar bone flutes from Truxillo are in the British Museum; one has five upper holes, and another has four upper holes and one underneath, with hardened paste or resin at the blowing end.
