the Wizard's School Pills in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Wizard’s sugar-coated educational pills replace lengthy study by providing learning and wisdom. Different pills teach algebra, geography, Latin, grammar, spelling, and elocution, letting students devote more time to athletics.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

9. How the Wogglebug Taught Athletics

The School Pills are invented by the Wizard and are used to teach academic subjects without lengthy study. Different pills cover algebra, geography, Latin, grammar, spelling, and elocution, with instructions for when to take them. The Wogglebug says they let students spend more time practicing athletics.
