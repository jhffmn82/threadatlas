Victor Hatherley in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Victor Hatherley is a young orphaned, unmarried hydraulic engineer who lives alone in London, with considerable experience from a seven-year apprenticeship to Venner & Matheson and chambers in Victoria Street. He accepts a confidential fifty-guinea night commission at Eyford from Colonel Lysander Stark. During the dangerous engagement, he is attacked and loses his thumb, receives brandy afterward, and is carried while unconscious. He identifies Dr. Becher’s burning house as the place of his confinement and escape through a window. He returns to London without the fee or thumb, though Holmes says he gained experience.

IX. THE ADVENTURE OF THE ENGINEER’S THUMB

Victor Hatherley is a young, orphaned, unmarried hydraulic engineer living alone in London. After accepting a highly paid secret commission from Colonel Lysander Stark, he travels to Eyford and returns with his thumb torn or hacked off.

We both sat in silence for some little time after listening to this

The hydraulic engineer confirms that he was driven for about an hour and remembers being lifted and conveyed while unconscious. He recognizes Dr. Becher's burning house as the place where he was held and where he jumped from a window. He returns to London having lost his thumb and a fifty-guinea fee, but Holmes says he has gained experience.
