sistrum in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The sistrum is a bronze or brass frame instrument with three or four loose metal bars that jingle when shaken. Also called seshesh and denoted by menaaneim, it resembles a bunibulum and was principally used by females in religious performances, sometimes decorated with animal forms or rings.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The sistrum was a bronze or brass frame with three or four loose metal bars that jingled when shaken. It could include snake-shaped bars, goose-head terminals, metal rings, or a cat figure, and it was used principally by females in religious performances.
