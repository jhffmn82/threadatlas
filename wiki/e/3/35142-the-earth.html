the earth

Also called: The earth, a country, earth, globe, land, native land, the globe, the ground, the land, the surface of the ocean, top the ground, your earth

Abstract written by a model

In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the globe is a heavy element that moves downward, can dissolve into water, and can form again from dense water as its surface changes. In The Sea Fairies, the earth is a dry, airy place where humans, horses, automobiles, and other creatures with legs live; Harry Hustle calls it stupid and nearly dies there, though he enjoys a circus, while Trot and Cap'n Bill prefer it over the ocean.

the earth in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911 · the work’s page · read it where the earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

The earth is a dry, airy place where humans, horses, automobiles, and other creatures with legs live. Harry Hustle calls it stupid and nearly dies there, though he enjoys a circus. It is Trot and Cap'n Bill's natural home, preferred over the ocean, where their sea forms are changed back on return.

The earth is the dry, airy place where Trot and Cap'n Bill came from and where humans, horses, automobiles, and other creatures with legs live. Harry Hustle says he nearly died there because it was stupid, dry, and airy, although he enjoyed seeing a circus.
