Water of Oblivion in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910 · the work’s page · read it where Water of Oblivion first appears

Abstract written by a model

Water of Oblivion is water in the Forbidden Fountain, placed there by Glinda, the Good Sorceress. When drunk, it causes forgetting and memory loss, erasing identities, purposes, and enmity. It first made a wicked King and his people innocent, then transformed invading warriors and the Nome King into harmless, childlike beings.

26. How Ozma Refused to Fight for Her Kingdom

The Forbidden Fountain contains the Water of Oblivion, which was placed there by Glinda, the Good Sorceress. Its water once caused a wicked King and his people to forget their wickedness and become innocent.

28. How They Drank at the Forbidden Fountain

The Water of Oblivion causes those who drink it to forget what they formerly knew, including their identities, purposes, and enmity toward Oz. It transforms the invading warriors and the Nome King into harmless, childlike beings.
