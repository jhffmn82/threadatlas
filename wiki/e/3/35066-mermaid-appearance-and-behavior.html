mermaid appearance and behavior in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Mermaids have a lovely lady’s form to the waist, with arms, pretty smiling faces, long silky floating hair, and fish lower halves with green, purple, and pink scales. Their sweet songs charm mortals; after smiling and winking, they cause them to plunge into the waves and drown.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: TROT AND CAP'N BILL

Cap'n Bill says mermaids have a lovely lady's form down to the waist and a fish's lower half covered with green, purple, and pink scales. He describes them as having arms, pretty faces, long silky hair that floats in the water, and sweet singing voices; he also says they charm people and cause them to plunge into the waves.
