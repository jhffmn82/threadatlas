Abstract written by a model

The prince of the sea devils is a sea devil, reputedly big, wise, and mighty, and the largest and fiercest of his race. He lies crouched day and night over the great dome’s opening, using curling legs to block passage, until Queen Aquareine plans to destroy him with the golden sword so the group can escape.
