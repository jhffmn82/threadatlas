the prince of the sea devils in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The prince of the sea devils is a sea devil, reputedly big, wise, and mighty, and the largest and fiercest of his race. He lies crouched day and night over the great dome’s opening, using curling legs to block passage, until Queen Aquareine plans to destroy him with the golden sword so the group can escape.

The prince of the sea devils lies crouched over the opening in the great dome day and night. He is the largest and fiercest of his race and prevents anyone from passing his network of curling legs.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

The prince of the sea devils guards the opening in the roof of the vast dome. Queen Aquareine plans to attack and destroy him with the golden sword so the group can escape into the ocean.
