Winchester in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Winchester is an old English capital and the nearest named destination to the Copper Beeches. After Violet Hunter’s telegram, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson travel toward Winchester to meet Miss Hunter, though Holmes notes that her countryside home, five miles away, causes his concern for her safety.
