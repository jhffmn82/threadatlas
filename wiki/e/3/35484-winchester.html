Abstract written by a model

In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Winchester is an old English capital and the nearest named destination to the Copper Beeches; Holmes and Dr. Watson travel toward it to meet Miss Hunter after her telegram. In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Winchester is the destination to which Holmes and Dr. Watson travel to see the race for the Wessex Cup. In The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes, Winchester is where Holmes, Dr. Watson, and Mr. Joyce Cummings spend the night while formalities are completed, and where Holmes interviews Miss Dunbar in her cell and develops the clue leading to the solution.
