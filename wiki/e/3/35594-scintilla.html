Scintilla in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Scintilla is a pretty, lively, musical, socially adept woman married to Mixtus for the advantages of being a rich man’s wife. She gathers fashionable society, associates with wealthy Greek merchant families and artists, and treats religious, social, and philosophical seriousness as absurd or dull, hoping to laugh Mixtus out of bachelor habits.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Scintilla is a pretty, lively, musical woman who associates with wealthy Greek merchant families and artists. She regards religious, social, and philosophical seriousness as ridiculous or dull, yet she is not described as wicked; she wants the advantages of being a rich man’s wife and believes Mixtus’s bachelor habits can be laughed out of him.
