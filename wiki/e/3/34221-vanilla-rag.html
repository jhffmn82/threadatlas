Vanilla RAG

method · 5 works · 11 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: typical RAG, vanilla RAG, vanilla RAG architecture, vanilla RAG model
  • In “LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering,” Vanilla RAG is a retrieval-augmented generation system that segments long contexts into smaller semantic units, which can disrupt structure and background information, retrieve low-quality chunks amid noise, and produce incomplete or inaccurate answers; it is evaluated with several LLMs and outperformed by LongRAG.
  • In “Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine,” Vanilla RAG is the most widely implemented RAG architecture and the study’s main architecture, directly prepending retrieved documents to the LLM context.
  • In “TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” Vanilla RAG uses both the questions and all the documents as inputs to the reader, corresponds to the baseline labeled “w. all documents,” and serves as a comparison point for TRACE-Doc, which performs better using less noisy documents identified through reasoning chains.
  • In “Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability,” Vanilla RAG is a retrieval-augmented generation method and accuracy-comparison baseline that retrieves the top 10 documents without additional modules, takes 0.32 seconds per task, and generally—and severely as adversarial sources increase—declines in accuracy under adversary-hammer and beta priors.
  • In “Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation,” Vanilla RAG is a RAG method relying on documents and paragraphs, which can add harmful noise and overlook entity relationships, limiting LLM-based recommender reasoning.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Vanilla RAG can disrupt contextual structure and background information through chunking, causing incomplete information and inaccurate answers. It can also retrieve low-quality chunks when long-context documents contain considerable noise and low evidence density.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

Vanilla RAG uses only a retriever and generator and is evaluated with several LLMs. Its segmentation of long contexts into smaller semantic units can disrupt coherent background information and original long-context structure, while LongRAG improves over it by up to 17.25%.
