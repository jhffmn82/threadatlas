Mr. Breckinridge in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mr. Breckinridge is a Covent Garden Market salesman who buys twenty-four geese from Mrs. Oakshott and sells them to Mr. Windigate for the Alpha Inn. He resists James Ryder’s inquiries, then reveals supplier and sales records after Holmes wins a wager.

Mr. Breckinridge sold two dozen geese from his Covent Garden stall to Mr. Windigate of the Alpha Inn. He resists James Ryder’s repeated inquiries about the birds but reveals his supplier and sales records after Holmes tricks him into a wager.
