the Rigmaroles in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

The Rigmaroles are inhabitants of Rigmarole Town who speak in long, deliberate speeches using many words while saying little, cannot or will not answer simply, and continue talking after travelers leave.

1 section; each heading opens the text

22. How the Wizard Found Dorothy

The Rigmaroles speak in long, deliberate speeches that use many words while saying little. They are unable or unwilling to answer simply, and one woman continues speaking even after the travelers have left.
