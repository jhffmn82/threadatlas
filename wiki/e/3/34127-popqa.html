PopQA in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17

PopQA is a short-form open-domain question answering task. Its long-tail subset contains 1,399 rare entity queries with fewer than 100 monthly Wikipedia page views; evaluation checks whether gold answers appear in generations. SELF-RAG performs strongly, improves with more training data, and suffers when always retrieving the top passage rather than retrieving adaptively.

PopQA is used for short-form open-domain question answering. The evaluation uses its long-tail subset of 1,399 rare entity queries, and systems are scored based on whether gold answers appear in their generations.

RESULTS AND ANALYSIS / 5.1 / MAIN RESULTS .. 5.2 / ANALYSIS

SELF-RAG achieves strong performance on PopQA and benefits substantially from increased training-data size. Always retrieving the top passage causes a large performance drop compared with adaptive retrieval.
