Abstract written by a model

In "Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Medicine," Textbooks supplies MEDRAG with domain-specific medical knowledge, comprising 18 documents and 125.8 thousand snippets for examination questions. In "Rationale-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation," medical textbooks are one of the smaller but crucial corpora included in RAG2's balanced retrieval strategy, although MedCPT may prefer PubMed documents over them. In "Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review," medical textbooks served as a knowledge source in Wang, Ma and Chen's system and outperformed Wikipedia for medical-domain tasks despite using a smaller dataset.
