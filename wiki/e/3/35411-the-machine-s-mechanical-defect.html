the machine's mechanical defect in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The machine's mechanical defect is water leakage through a side cylinder, caused when a shrunken india-rubber band around a driving-rod head no longer fills its socket, making the hydraulic press lose power and regurgitate water.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

The narrator finds that the hydraulic press has lost power because an india-rubber band around a driving-rod has shrunk and no longer fills its socket. This causes a slight leakage and regurgitation of water through one of the side cylinders.
