tin castle (Nick Chopper's castle)

Abstract written by a model

Nick Chopper's new tin castle was built because the old castle was damp and might rust his tin body. Shining from a hilltop, it has towers, steeples, domes, gables, flower-beds, fountains, and rooms whose furniture, floors, and walls are brightly polished tin. Its grounds display life-size tin statues of his friends, including Dorothy with Toto at her feet, and it contains a grand tin dining-hall for entertaining guests.

14. Tik-Tok and Billina

Nick Chopper built the new tin castle because his old castle was damp and might rust his tin body. The castle has many towers, steeples, domes, and gables, and the travelers see it shining from a hilltop.

15. The Emperor's Tin Castle

The tin castle has flower-beds, fountains, statues, and rooms furnished entirely with brightly polished tin, including its floors and walls. Its grounds contain life-size tin figures of Dorothy and Toto as well as figures of the Scarecrow, the Wizard, Ozma, Tik-tok, and others. The castle contains a grand tin dining-hall where Nick Chopper entertains his guests.
