Abstract written by a model

The harbor of Phorcys is a storm-sheltered haven between two points, containing an olive tree and the Cave of the Naiads; the Phaeacians end their voyage by landing sleeping Ulysses there.

BOOK XIII

The harbour of the old merman Phorcys lies between two points that shelter it from storms. It contains the olive tree and the Cave of the Naiads, and the Phaeacians land Ulysses there while he is asleep.
