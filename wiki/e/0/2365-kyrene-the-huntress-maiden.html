Abstract written by a model

Kyrene, a strong huntress-maiden and daughter of Hypseus, rejects weaving to defend her father’s herds from beasts; after Apollo finds her wrestling a lion, she becomes his bride and queen in Libya.

IX.

Kyrene was a strong huntress who disliked weaving and household feasts, fought wild beasts with spear and sword, and protected her father’s herds. Apollo found her wrestling a lion, and she became his bride and queen in Libya.
