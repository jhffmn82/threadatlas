Hyades in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

The Hyades are stars identified as transformed sisters of Hyas, named Phaesyle, Coronis, Cleeia, Phaco, and Eudora.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ASTRONOMY

The Hyades are identified as the transformed sisters of Hyas. Hesiod gives their names as Phaesyle, Coronis, Cleeia, Phaco, and Eudora, and says that people on earth call them Hyades.
