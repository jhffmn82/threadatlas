Ozma's transformation and accession

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Tip's transformation into Princess Ozma, became the rightful Ruler of this land, become a girl, restore you to your proper form, restored me to my proper person, the event, you are transformed into a girl

Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, it is the event in which Mombi reverses the enchantment that made Ozma appear to be a boy, transforming Tip into a young girl who retains Tip's memories and relationships. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, it is the event in which Ozma escapes Mombi with Jack Pumpkinhead and the Powder of Life, and Glinda recognizes and restores her, enabling her accession as Oz's rightful ruler.
