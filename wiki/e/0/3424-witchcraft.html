witchcraft

topic · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Witchcraft, good witchcraft, magic, magic art, magic charm, magical, magical arts, magical compounds, magical powers, magical things, sorceries, sorcery

Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, witchcraft is described as unnatural and feared, and Old Mombi uses it to create or seem to create sunflowers and alter the landscape through harmless deceptions. In The Scarecrow of Oz, witchcraft is forbidden in Oz but practiced in Jinxland, where King Krewl permits witches to use it and sometimes employs it for cruelty and revenge. In Glinda of Oz, witchcraft is described as unlawful magic unlike fairy magic; Coo-ee-oh's magic controlled the island's machinery, under-water boats, and basement doors until her transformation caused her to forget it.
