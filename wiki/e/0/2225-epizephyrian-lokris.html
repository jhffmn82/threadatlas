Epizephyrian Lokris in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Epizephyrian Lokris is a western Lokrian city where Truth dwells; its people honor Kalliope, mailed Ares, and Zeus’s thunderbolt, while Pindar celebrates its noble race.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI.

Epizephyrian Lokris is described as a western Lokrian city where Truth dwells. Its people honor Kalliope and mailed Ares, and Pindar celebrates the city's noble race.
