11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

Santa Claus's Christmas-night journey takes him swiftly from chimney to chimney in his new sledge. He uses ten reindeer and sings as they travel over the frozen snow. After discovering the children's stockings, he realizes that filling them saves time compared with finding each child and placing toys beside the bed.
