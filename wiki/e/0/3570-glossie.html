Glossie

animal · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Glossie is an intelligent, swift, strong reindeer of the Forest, guarded by Will Knook and the Knooks and protected by the Fairy Queen when away. Timid toward men outside the Forest, Glossie nevertheless joins Flossie to draw Claus’s sledge. Wearing Claus’s bells, Glossie helps the first journey by leaping roofs and racing home, then enjoys Christmas Eve’s night journey and carries Claus swiftly back before daybreak.

Glossie is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

Glossie visits Claus with Flossie and explains that the Frost King has hardened the snow. He agrees to pull Claus's sledge after receiving permission from Will Knook, helps leap from roof to roof, and races back toward the Forest before daybreak.

10. Christmas Eve

Glossie helps Claus on his night journey and returns one minute after daybreak, nearly receiving punishment from Will Knook. Glossie says the trip was enjoyable and later carries Claus swiftly home before daybreak on Christmas Eve.
