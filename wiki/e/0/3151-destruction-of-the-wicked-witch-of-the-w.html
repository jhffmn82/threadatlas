Chapter XI

The death of the Wicked Witch of the West is made the condition for every requested favor. Dorothy and her companions decide that they must destroy the Witch and bring Oz proof that she is dead.

Chapter XII

The Wicked Witch of the West melts after Dorothy throws a bucket of water over her. The Witch shrinks and falls apart into a brown, shapeless mass, and Dorothy sweeps away what remains before recovering the Silver Shoe.

Chapter XV

The Wicked Witch of the West is destroyed when Dorothy melts her with water. Oz is surprised and frightened by the news, but the travelers use the destruction as the basis for demanding that he honor his promises.
