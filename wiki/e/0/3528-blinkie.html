Abstract written by a model

Blinkie is a large, real cat with soft glossy fur who belongs to Claus after Peter the Knook gives her to him. She purrs continually and serves as Claus’s model while he carves and paints a wooden cat. Initially angry and later offended by the toy’s attention, she recognizes it is wood, resumes admiring Claus, hides from wind, and is terrified by Shiegra’s snarl.

Blinkie is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

2. How Claus Made the First Toy

Blinkie is a large cat given to Claus by Peter the Knook, and she sits beside the hearth while Claus stays indoors. She watches Claus carve a wooden cat, first reacting angrily to the image and then recognizing that it is made of wood. She resumes purring and gives Claus admiring glances.

3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

Blinkie is Claus's real cat. She hides under the table when the wind blows into the house, sits as a model while Claus paints the toy cat, appears offended by the attention given to it, and later becomes terrified when Shiegra snarls at her.
