Query reformulation research uses text generation for other retrieval tasks, while some methods need task-specific paraphrase data, external resources, or time-consuming reinforcement-learning training. These requirements make them less applicable or efficient for OpenQA.

Recent query reformulation studies use generation models for other retrieval tasks. The unit states that they may require external resources or time-consuming training such as reinforcement learning, making them less applicable or efficient for OpenQA.
