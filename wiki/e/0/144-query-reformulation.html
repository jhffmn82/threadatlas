In "Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering," query reformulation research uses text generation for other retrieval tasks, while some methods require task-specific paraphrase data, external resources, or time-consuming reinforcement-learning training, making them less applicable or efficient for OpenQA. In "Retrieval-augmented generation in multilingual settings," the query reformulation component was left out of the study's scope and was identified for inclusion in future work, especially for other tasks.
