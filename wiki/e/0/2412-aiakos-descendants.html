Aiakos' descendants in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Aiakos’ descendants are a heroic lineage, described as golden-charioted, far-shining, and enduring, with blood belonging to Zeus. Descended through Aiakos’ sons and grandsons, they include Peleus, Telamon, Achilles, and Aias. Their fame travels beyond Egypt and among the Hyperboreans; Achilles’ battle victories glorify Aigina and the root from which he sprang.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode III

Aiakos’ race is associated with a far-shining and enduring light. Its heroic members include Peleus, Telamon, and Achilles, and its blood is said to belong to Zeus.

Isthmian Ode V

The golden-charioted descendants of Aiakos are said to possess fame that travels along innumerable roads beyond Egypt and among the Hyperboreans. Their line includes Peleus, Telamon, and Aias, whose deeds are known even among peoples of unfamiliar speech.

Isthmian Ode VII

The Aiakid race descended from Aiakos and included Achilles. Achilles glorified Aigina and the root from which he sprang through his victories in battle.
