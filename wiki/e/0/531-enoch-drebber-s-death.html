Abstract written by a model

Enoch J. Drebber’s death is a poisoning discovered around two in the morning in an empty house after a constable sees a light. His unwounded body bears horror and hatred; Holmes identifies poison, and Jefferson Hope later says he caused Drebber’s end before pursuing Stangerson.

CHAPTER III.

Enoch J. Drebber’s death is discovered after a constable notices a light in the empty house at about two in the morning. His body shows an expression of horror and hatred, but no visible wound; Sherlock Holmes concludes that he died from poison.
