Righteousness

concept · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: right, righteousness, true justice, what is just

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Righteousness is Zeus-given law for mankind, unlike beasts’ rule; knowing and speaking it brings a nation blessing and prosperity, while violence, injustice, and false witness bring generational ruin. In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Righteousness is a daughter and Peace’s mother who orders the ode, standing beside the song of triumph.

HESIOD / HESIOD’S WORKS AND DAYS

Right is given to mankind by Zeus and is better than the law governing beasts, fishes, and birds. Those who know and speak right may receive prosperity, while false witnesses bring ruin upon their generations.

VIII.

Righteousness is presented as the mother of Peace and as a force associated with the order of the ode. She stands beside the song of triumph, and the poet prays for God's favour toward Xenarches and his family.
