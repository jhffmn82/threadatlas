Righteousness in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

As a daughter and Peace's mother, Righteousness orders the ode, standing beside the song of triumph.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.

Righteousness is presented as the mother of Peace and as a force associated with the order of the ode. She stands beside the song of triumph, and the poet prays for God's favour toward Xenarches and his family.

Righteousness in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Right is Zeus-given law for mankind, unlike beasts’ rule; knowing and speaking it brings a nation blessing and prosperity, while violence, injustice, and false witness bring generational ruin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HESIOD / HESIOD’S WORKS AND DAYS

Right is given to mankind by Zeus and is better than the law governing beasts, fishes, and birds. Those who know and speak right may receive prosperity, while false witnesses bring ruin upon their generations.
