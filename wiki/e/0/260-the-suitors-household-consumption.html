The suitors’ household consumption is their eating of Ulysses’s sheep, cattle, goats, pigs, heifer, wine, corn, and stores, culminating in its identification as wrongful seizure.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XX

The suitors consume the sheep, goats, pigs, heifer, wine, corn, and other provisions belonging to Ulysses's household. Philoetius and Telemachus both identify their consumption of Ulysses's cattle and stores as part of their wrongful seizure of his property.
