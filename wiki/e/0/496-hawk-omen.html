hawk omen

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: omen
  • In The Odyssey: signifies Telemachus's power [according to Theoclymenus] A hawk carrying a dove in its talons flies on Telemachus’s right, and feathers fall between him and the ship. Theoclymenus identifies it as a divine omen of Telemachus’s continuing power and royal status in Ithaca.
