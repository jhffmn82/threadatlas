Abstract written by a model

In “Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge,” FiD independently encodes retrieved passages, attends over all of them, and achieves state-of-the-art results on knowledge-intensive NLP tasks. In “RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” FiD is a retrieval-augmented generation question-answering system with strong open-domain QA performance. In “REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation,” FiDO is evaluated as a generative-reader baseline and achieves the reported Exact Match scores on five datasets. In “REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation,” Fusion-in-Decoder is the main framework and a baseline for retrieval-augmented generative reading.

Fusion-in-Decoder in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11 to 2024-08-16 · the work’s page · read it where Fusion-in-Decoder first appears

Abstract written by a model

Fusion-in-Decoder is a simple, effective retrieval-augmented generative reader model for open domain question answering, based on an encoder-decoder T5 model. It separately encodes each question-passage pair, concatenates token embeddings from all passages as decoder input, and generates an answer. Its independent passage encoding can overlook semantic relationships between passages. It is the main framework underlying REANO and a baseline, with reported Exact Match scores from 29.9 on MuSiQue to 74.1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA.
