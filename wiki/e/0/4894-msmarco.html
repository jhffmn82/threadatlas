MSMARCO in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023

MSMARCO is a well-known KIG dataset with a generative QA task, evaluated using 8.8M passages extracted from 3.6M web documents retrieved by Bing and ROUGE-L for end-to-end generation quality. Its NLGen split provides crowdworker-rewritten, well-formed answers for generative question answering. Because the challenge is retired and the full test set is unavailable, the original train split is divided into training and validation sets, with results reported on the original development split. It is used to evaluate open-domain and generative QA methods, including effectiveness and robustness. Reported best results include a 12.1% relative Recall@1 improvement and a 4.5% BLEU-4 improvement over baseline or RAG; a multi-task model reaches about 14.3% Recall@1 improvement over non-finetuned DPR and shows lower overfit than RAG.
