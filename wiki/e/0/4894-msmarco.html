MSMARCO

Also called: MSMARCO QA dataset, MSMARCO dataset

Abstract written by a model

MSMARCO is a well-known KIG dataset with a generative QA task, evaluated using 8.8M passages extracted from 3.6M web documents retrieved by Bing and ROUGE-L for end-to-end generation quality. Its NLGen split provides crowdworker-rewritten, well-formed answers for generative question answering. Because the challenge is retired and the full test set is unavailable, the original train split is divided into training and validation sets, with results reported on the original development split. It is used to evaluate open-domain and generative QA methods, including effectiveness and robustness. Reported best results include a 12.1% relative Recall@1 improvement and a 4.5% BLEU-4 improvement over baseline or RAG; a multi-task model reaches about 14.3% Recall@1 improvement over non-finetuned DPR and shows lower overfit than RAG.

MSMARCO is in one work on this site, Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Introduction

MSMARCO is used to evaluate the framework on generative question answering. The best model achieves a reported 12.1% relative improvement in Recall@1 and a 4.5% relative improvement in BLEU-4 over RAG.

Experimental Setup / Datasets .. Comparison Models

Results / RQ1: Effectiveness / Generative QA .. Case Studies

MSMARCO is used to evaluate Generative QA effectiveness and robustness. The multi-task model achieves about 14.3% relative improvement over non-finetuned DPR on Recall@1 and a 4.5% relative improvement on BLEU-4, while the proposed methods show lower overfit levels than RAG.

Conclusion

MSMARCO is one of the datasets used to evaluate the proposed methods. The best model achieves a 12.1% relative improvement on Recall@1, although the authors could not access its test set because the challenge is retired.
