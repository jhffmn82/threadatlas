Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TO PYTHIAN APOLLO—

Apollo kills the dragoness near the sweet-flowing spring with an arrow from his strong bow. He declares that she will no longer plague men and sheep, and her death gives the place the name Pytho.
