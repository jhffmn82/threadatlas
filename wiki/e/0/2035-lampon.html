Lampon in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Lampon, son of Kleonikos and father of successful athlete sons including Phylakidas, is praised for disciplined practice. He honors Hesiod, urges his sons to practice, kindly welcomes strangers, seeks the just mean, and speaks in accord with his thoughts, sharpening athletes like a Naxian whetstone.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode V

Lampon is the father of Phylakidas and is praised for practicing whatever he undertakes, honoring Hesiod’s teaching, encouraging his sons, welcoming strangers, and pursuing the just mean. His speech is said to agree with his thoughts, and among athletes he is compared to a Naxian whetstone that sharpens others.
