Phylakidas of Aigina

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: PHYLAKIDAS OF AIGINA, Phylakidas, Phylakidas', the winner's brother, youngest son

Abstract written by a model

Phylakidas of Aigina is Lampon’s youngest son and Pytheas’s brother, an athlete and pankration winner. He wins at the Isthmian Games, gaining double glory there and a pankratiast’s crown at Nemea. His victories prompt praise in Isthmian Odes IV and V and celebrate his family’s athletic achievements.

Phylakidas of Aigina is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Isthmian Ode IV

Phylakidas is celebrated as the winner of the pankration. He receives praise for his strength and victory, including a double glory at Isthmos and a pankratiast’s crown at Nemea.

Isthmian Ode V

Phylakidas, the youngest son of Lampon, won the pankration at the Isthmian Games. His victory is the occasion for the ode, which also celebrates his family’s athletic achievements.
