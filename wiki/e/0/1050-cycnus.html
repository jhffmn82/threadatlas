Cycnus

Abstract written by a model

Cycnus is a gallant, blameless son of Ares, also called son of Poseidon, married to Themistinoe and owner of fine bronze armour. He despoils rich offerings brought to Pytho. Near Apollo’s grove, he confronts Heracles and Iolaus to kill and strip them, refuses warning, attacks, and is killed by Heracles; his armour is stripped.

Cycnus is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE SHIELD OF HERACLES

Cycnus, the son of Ares, confronts Heracles and Iolaus near the grove of Apollo, intending to kill them and strip their armor. He refuses Heracles’ warning to yield, leaps from his chariot, and begins the battle against Heracles.

(ll. 402-412) As two lions 1806 on either side of a slain deer spring

Cycnus attacks Heracles with a brazen spear, but it does not break Heracles’ shield. Heracles kills him with a spear-thrust to the neck, and Cycnus’ armour is later stripped from his body.
