domain adaptation in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Domain adaptation in KGI consists of indexing a new corpus with a pretrained DPR and replacing the original Wikipedia index. This enables zero-shot slot filling for a new schema, while providing a few examples for each relation quickly improves performance in a few-shot setup.

Analysis .. Slot Filling Evaluation

Domain adaptation is studied by transferring KGI1 from Wikipedia-style data and Wikidata relations to TACRED passages and relations. The adapted system is evaluated in zero-shot and few-shot settings.

Conclusion

Domain adaptation is evaluated using the new zero/few-shot slot filling benchmark based on TACRED. The paper identifies more effective domain adaptation strategies, including combining zero-shot and few-shot learning with human-in-the-loop techniques, as a future research direction.
