Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

Koronis, daughter of Phlegyas, conceived a child by Apollo but took another spouse, Ischys, without her father's knowledge. Apollo became aware of this, and Artemis killed Koronis before she could give birth.
