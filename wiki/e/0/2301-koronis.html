Also called: fair-robed Koronis, the damsel

Abstract written by a model

Koronis, Phlegyas’s daughter, conceived Apollo’s child but secretly took Ischys; when Apollo learned of it, Artemis killed her, though her unborn Asklepios was rescued from her funeral pyre.

III.

Koronis, daughter of Phlegyas, conceived a child by Apollo but took another spouse, Ischys, without her father's knowledge. Apollo became aware of this, and Artemis killed Koronis before she could give birth.
