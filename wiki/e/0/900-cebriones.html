Abstract written by a model

Cebriones, Priam’s bastard son, Hector’s charioteer, brave Trojan fighter, and third in command with Hector and Polydamas, commands Trojans and allies until Patroclus kills him with a forehead stone; armies fight over, then Achaeans seize and strip his body.
