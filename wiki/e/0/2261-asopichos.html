Asopichos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Asopichos, a boy of Orchomenos, wins the Olympic boys' short foot-race in Pisa's valley, is garlanded, and brings honour to his father.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV.

Asopichos is celebrated as the winner of the boys' short foot-race for Orchomenos. In Pisa's valley, he crowned his boyish hair with garlands from the glorious games, bringing honour to his father.
