friendship and social conduct

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: brother, common feast, friend, neighbours

Abstract written by a model

Friendship and social conduct require treating friends justly, though not exactly as brothers, and repaying unreconciled wrongs doubly. The guidance then urges avoidance of shifting friendships, lavishness, churlishness, rogues, slander, boasting, taunting the poor, boorish feasting, and harmful speech.

A friend should not be treated exactly like a brother unless he is treated justly, and a wrong should be repaid double if the offender refuses reconciliation. Perses is told to avoid shifting friendships, lavishness, churlishness, rogues, slander, boasting, taunting the poor, boorishness at feasts, and harmful talk.
