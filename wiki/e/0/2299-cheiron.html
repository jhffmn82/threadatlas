Abstract written by a model

Cheiron is a wise, humane, bold centaur, son of Philyra, who once ruled Pelion’s glens. In his stone cavern and household, he raised Jason for twenty years, reared Asklepios and Achilles, and taught Asklepios healing drugs and arts that save lives; he also instructed Achilles to revere Zeus and honor a parent’s life. Summoned by Apollo, he witnessed Kyrene’s struggle with a lion, explained her lineage, and foretold her future in Libya. After Achilles left for Troy, Cheiron is dead in the speaker’s wished-for present, whose loss is felt as that of a healer’s source.

Cheiron is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Cheiron in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where Cheiron first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

III.

Cheiron, the son of Philyra, is described as a humane and wise beast who once ruled in Pelion's glens. He trained Asklepios to devise remedies that save lives, and the speaker wishes that he were still alive so that he might provide a healer for worthy men.

IV.

Cheiron raises Jason for twenty years in his cave, together with his daughters Chariklo and Philyra. Jason says that he learned wisdom from Cheiron and returns to Iolkos only after completing his upbringing.

IX.

Cheiron was summoned by Apollo to witness Kyrene’s struggle with the lion and to explain her lineage. He foretold that Apollo would take her to Libya, where she would become queen and bear a son.

Nemean Ode III

Cheiron reared Jason, Asklepios, and Achilles in his stone cavern. He taught Asklepios to prepare healing drugs and raised Achilles in a life of harmony until Achilles went to Troy.
