Apollo's pestilence

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a deadly dart, arrows, shafts, shot his arrows among the people, sorrow upon the Argives, the people died, the pestilence, this fearful pestilence

Abstract written by a model

Apollo sends a pestilence against the Achaeans after Agamemnon dishonors Apollo’s priest and Chryses prays. For nine days Apollo’s arrows cause widespread death. Calchas says it will continue until Chryseis is returned and a holy hecatomb is sent; it ends after Chryses receives her and sacrifices and prays.

Apollo's pestilence is in one work on this site, The Iliad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
