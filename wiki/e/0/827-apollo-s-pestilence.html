Apollo's pestilence in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Apollo sends a pestilence against the Achaeans after Agamemnon dishonors Apollo’s priest and Chryses prays. For nine days Apollo’s arrows cause widespread death. Calchas says it will continue until Chryseis is returned and a holy hecatomb is sent; it ends after Chryses receives her and sacrifices and prays.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK I.

Apollo’s pestilence is sent against the Achaeans because Agamemnon has dishonored Apollo’s priest. Apollo shoots his arrows among the host for nine days, causing widespread death. Calchas says that it will continue until Agamemnon returns Chryseis and sends a holy hecatomb to Chryse.
