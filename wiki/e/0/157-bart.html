BART-large in Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

Yuning Mao, Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Yelong Shen, Jianfeng Gao, Jiawei Han, Weizhu Chen · 2021

BART-large serves as GAR’s generative reader and query-context generator. It concatenates a question with top-retrieved passages within a 1,024-token limit to generate answers. GAR selects its checkpoint by best validation ROUGE-1 F1, while retrieval accuracy remains relatively stable across checkpoints.

BARTLARGE in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Michael Glass, Gaetano Rossiello, Md Faisal Mahbub Chowdhury, Alﬁo Gliozzo · 2021

BARTLARGE is a sequence-to-sequence generation component and implicit-knowledge baseline for KILT slot filling. It takes retrieved passages with a query, predicts tokens, and combines passage token distributions weighted by retrieval scores. It is the strongest implicit baseline, but scores about 40 accuracy points below KGI1 on T-REx.

BART receives each retrieved passage concatenated with the query and produces token prediction logits. Its token distributions are weighted by retrieval scores and combined across passages to obtain the next-token distribution.

BARTLARGE is described as the strongest implicit-knowledge baseline for KILT slot filling. It performs approximately 40 accuracy points below KGI1 on T-REx and is used to compare implicit and explicit knowledge.
