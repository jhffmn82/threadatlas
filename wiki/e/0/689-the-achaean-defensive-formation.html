Abstract written by a model

is_made_of spears and shields [densely serried] The Achaeans gather around the two Ajaxes in a tightly serried formation, with spear, shield, buckler, helmet, and man pressed closely together. This living fence prevents Hector from continuing easily through the tents and ships and drives him back for a time.
