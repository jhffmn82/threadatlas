abortion

Also called: attempted abortion, criminality, destroy the tender embryo, pierce, plucked forth, poisons, rid, same experiment, slain
  • In The Amores, or Amours, attempted abortion is portrayed as a criminal and unnatural act that destroys a tender embryo, may cause the woman to die, and deserves punishment if repeated.
  • In Heroides and Amores, abortion is condemned as self-destructive violence that wounds women, poisons unborn children, may cause death, and is urged to be allowed to reach natural maturity.

abortion in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Abortion is condemned as self-destructive violence: women wound themselves and poison unborn children to avoid bodily marks, may die after destroying pregnancy, and are urged to let pregnancy reach natural maturity.
