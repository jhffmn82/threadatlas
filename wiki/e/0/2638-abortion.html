Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, abortion is condemned as self-destructive violence that wounds women, poisons unborn children, may cause death, and is urged to be allowed to reach natural maturity. In The Amores, or Amours, attempted abortion is portrayed as a criminal and unnatural act that destroys a tender embryo, may cause the woman to die, and deserves punishment if repeated.
