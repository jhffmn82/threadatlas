Abstract written by a model

descend_from Perseus [through Herakles] The Aleuadai are associated with Hippokleas and with the request for a chorus celebrating him. They are said to be descended through Herakles from Perseus, and Pindar praises Thorax and his noble brothers for strengthening the Thessalian commonwealth.
