3. The Deputies of Santa Claus

The four little folk, Kilter, Wisk, Peter, and Nuter, begin riding in Santa Claus's sledge and entering houses through walls. Their work helps Santa Claus distribute gifts more quickly, and they continue accompanying him on his yearly ride.
