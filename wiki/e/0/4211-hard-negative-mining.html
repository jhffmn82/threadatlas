hard negative mining

method · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: hard negative mining strategy, hard negatives

Abstract written by a model

Hard negative mining is a DPR training method in KGI for improving the context encoder in end-to-end slot filling. It forms a query from the head entity and relation, retrieves BM25-ranked passages with Anserini, excludes ground-truth or answer-containing passages, and selects the highest remaining result.

hard negative mining is in one work on this site, Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Hard negative mining uses Anserini to retrieve top-ranked passages with BM25 from a query made from the head entity and relation. Passages overlapping the ground-truth paragraphs or containing a correct answer are excluded, and the remaining top-ranked result is used as a hard negative.
