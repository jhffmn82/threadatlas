Hazem Abdelazim · 2023 · the work’s page · read it where Sentence-BERT first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sentence-BERT is a modification of BERT that uses Siamese and triplet network structures to produce sentence embeddings capturing entire-sentence semantic meaning. Sentences are encoded separately, then compared with cosine similarity, reducing pairwise-processing cost. It is discussed amid the growing popularity and strong performance of sentence embeddings in monolingual sentence-level tasks.

SBERT in Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical services: model development study

Frontiers in Public Health · 2025-01-29 · the work’s page · read it where SBERT first appears

Abstract written by a model

SBERT is a pretrained sentence-level embedding model used to transform textual documents and user queries into numerical embeddings for semantic similarity, storage, and retrieval. In medical-condition classification and text-embedding tasks, it produced more distinct clusters of labels than FastText and achieved precision, recall, and F1-score of 0.95. It was selected as the optimal or preferred embedding model because it balances performance with affordability, cost effectiveness, data security, ease of migration, and ease of implementation. Its role unfolds from generating contextual sentence embeddings to supporting retrieval and classification, then being favored for its strong results and practical advantages.
