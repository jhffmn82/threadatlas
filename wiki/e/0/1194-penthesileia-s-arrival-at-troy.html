Penthesileia's arrival at Troy

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Then came the Amazon, Unto Troy-town Penthesileia came, comes to aid the Trojans, the arrival of Penthesileia
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Penthesileia arrives to aid the Trojans, demonstrates great prowess, and is killed by Achilles.
  • In The Fall of Troy, Penthesileia arrives from Thermodon with twelve Amazon princesses, seeking refuge and purification; Priam honors her, and her promised aid brings the Trojans hope and joy.

BOOK I:

Penthesileia arrives from Thermodon with twelve Amazon princesses, seeking refuge from reproach and purification from the accidental killing of Hippolyte. The Trojans marvel at her beauty and martial power, and Priam receives her with honor and hope.
