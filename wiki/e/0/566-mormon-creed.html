Mormon creed

Abstract written by a model

The Mormon creed is a sacred belief based on Egyptian-lettered writings on plates of beaten gold handed to Joseph Smith at Palmyra. Mormons demand that Ferrier and Lucy accept it to travel with them. Its requirements culminate in Brigham Young’s rule that each maiden marry one of the elect, which Ferrier has not arranged for Lucy.

PART II. / CHAPTER I.

The Mormon creed is presented as a sacred belief based on writings in Egyptian letters on plates of beaten gold that were handed to Joseph Smith at Palmyra. The Mormons insist that Ferrier and Lucy accept this creed if they are to travel with them.

CHAPTER III.

The Mormon faith requires, according to Brigham Young, that a maiden marry one of the elect rather than a Gentile. John Ferrier claims to have followed many of its public requirements but has not married or arranged Lucy's marriage to a Mormon.
