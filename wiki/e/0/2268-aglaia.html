Aglaia in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Aglaia, one of the Graces and daughter of the mightiest gods, brings about the Minyai’s Olympic victory through her gift and is asked to regard the triumphal company.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIV.

Aglaia is addressed as one of the Graces and as a daughter of the mightiest of the gods. The ode credits her with bringing about the Minyai's Olympic victory and asks her to look upon the triumphal company.
