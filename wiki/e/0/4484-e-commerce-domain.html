Abstract written by a model

In the e-commerce domain, bilingual product descriptions for products sold in both English and another marketplace are treated as parallel passage pairs. The text notes that the same products and their keyphrases can share semantic similarity across marketplaces and countries. It provides the setting from which EcommerceMKP is collected. Product text and purchase-producing search queries from a popular E-commerce shopping platform are used to construct the dataset.
