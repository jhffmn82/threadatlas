Melissos of Thebes

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Melissos, this man, this our man, this scion of Telesias' son

Abstract written by a model

Melissos of Thebes is a pankration victor trained by Orseas. Though small in stature, he is praised for lion-like courage, fox-like cunning, strength, and wrestling skill. After an earlier boys’ victory, he wins crowns at the Isthmian games and a Nemean chariot-race victory.

Melissos of Thebes is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Isthmian Ode III

Melissos wins crowns at the Isthmian games and in the chariot-race at Nemea, proclaiming Thebes as his home. Despite his small and unimpressive stature, he is formidable in strength, courage, and wrestling skill, and he wins a double victory at the yearly games after an earlier victory among the boys.
