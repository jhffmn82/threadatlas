Abstract written by a model

This is an armed battle between Trojans and Achaeans, arising from Alexandrus carrying Helen away from her husband and homeland. It is prepared through sacrifices, prayers, mustering, and the disposition of chiefs’ men, as Agamemnon urges an immediate attack. At first, both armies hope a single combat will settle the dispute and let them separate, but their ranks instead meet in sustained, bloody fighting of shields and spears. Warriors on both sides are killed as the struggle repeatedly shifts across the plain between Simois and Xanthus. Divine intervention is central: Apollo, Minerva, Mars, Juno, Venus, and others aid, restrain, rescue, or conceal fighters. Diomed’s attacks, including his wounding of Mars, temporarily check Trojan pressure; Apollo restores Aeneas, while Hector repeatedly rallies the Trojans. Agamemnon leads a devastating Achaean advance, but after he is wounded Hector helps reverse the course. The Trojans eventually confine the Achaeans at their ships. Patroclus attacks the Trojans but is killed, marking a major outcome of the fighting. Battle is also depicted on Vulcan’s shield through besieging armies, ambush, slain shepherds, armed hosts, Strife, Riot, and Fate.
