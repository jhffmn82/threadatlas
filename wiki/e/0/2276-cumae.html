Cumae in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Cumae is an Italian shore where Aeneas and the Trojans land. Its sacred hill holds Apollo’s temple and the Sibyl’s cave near Avernus; Helenus directs Aeneas there, where the Sibyl will reveal his wars, suffering, route, and passage toward the underworld.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

“The prophet first with sacrifice adores

At Cumae, Helenus says that Aeneas will find the Sibyl near the black waters of Avernus and the sounding wood. The Sibyl will explain his wars, future suffering, and route to the destined shores.

BOOK VI

Cumae is the Italian shore where Aeneas and the Trojans land. It contains the sacred hill, the temple of Apollo, the Sibyl’s cave, and the route toward the underworld.
