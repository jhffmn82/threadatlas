Cumae

Also called: Cumaean coast, Cumaean shore, the Cumaean coast

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Cumae is the place before which the Syracusans defeated the Etruscans in a sea-fight; its surrounding cliffs are also said to lie heavily on the imprisoned Typhon. In The Aeneid, Cumae is an Italian shore where Aeneas and the Trojans land; its sacred hill holds Apollo’s temple and the Sibyl’s cave near Avernus, where the Sibyl reveals Aeneas’s wars, suffering, route, and passage toward the underworld.
