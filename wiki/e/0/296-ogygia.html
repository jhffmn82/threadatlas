the Ogygian island in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The Ogygian island is a lonely, sea-girt, forest-covered island in the middle of the sea, far from Ulysses’s friends. It contains Calypso’s beautiful cave, vineyards, birds, streams, and flowering plants. Calypso takes in and keeps Ulysses there in sorrow; he eventually leaves after building a raft, only to be stranded there again by the gods.

Calypso's island in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Calypso's island is where Ulysses arrives after forming a raft from the keel and mast when his vessel is destroyed. He later departs from it on a raft supplied by Calypso, but a storm breaks the mast and threatens his passage.
