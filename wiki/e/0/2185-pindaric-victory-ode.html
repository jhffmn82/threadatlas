Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.

The ode sends the Muses' nectar to celebrate men who win at Pytho or Olympia. In this unit it accompanies Diagoras home, glorifies his boxing victory and lineage, and celebrates Rhodes and its ancestral myths.
