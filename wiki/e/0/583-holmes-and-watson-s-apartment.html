  • In A Study in Scarlet, Sherlock Holmes considers sharing a suite of rooms in Baker Street with Dr. Watson, and they agree to view it and determine the arrangements together.
  • In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Holmes and Watson's rooms are set on fire overnight without great harm, and Holmes believes the attackers thought he had returned.
  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Holmes and Watson's apartment has been kept largely unchanged under Mycroft Holmes's supervision and Mrs. Hudson's care, preserving its familiar objects.
  • In His Last Bow, Holmes's first-floor flat is Mrs. Hudson's residence and Holmes's lodging, characterized by untidiness, strange experiments, music at odd hours, revolver practice, and an atmosphere of danger; Watson visits the sick room there, is temporarily locked in, and is later sent to find Culverton Smith.
