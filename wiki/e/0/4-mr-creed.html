Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Mr. Creed is ordered £300 for cloth, reports Lord Sandwich’s recovery, then receives a £10,000 voyage imprest and helps seek ammunition for my Lord’s ships.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

JUNE, JULY & AUGUST 1661 June 1st.

Mr. Creed’s imprest of L10,000 was signed against the voyage. He dined with the diarist, accompanied him to the Tower to seek ammunition for my Lord’s ships, and walked with him around the Tower.
