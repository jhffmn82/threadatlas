Abstract written by a model

Puff-jaw, child of Mud-man and Waterlady, is king of the Frogs. He invites Crumb-snatcher aboard, abandons him to drown when a water-snake appears, urges war on Mice, and is struck by Bread-nibbler.

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

Puff-jaw is the king of the Frogs and invites Crumb-snatcher to ride on his back to his house. When a water-snake appears, he dives and leaves Crumb-snatcher to drown, then denies killing him and urges the Frogs to destroy the Mice. Bread-nibbler later strikes him on the toes.
