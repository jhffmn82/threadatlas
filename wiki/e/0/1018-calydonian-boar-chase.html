Calydonian boar chase in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV

Ovid · 0008~

The Calydonian boar chase is a legendary hunt, alluded to by Homer, in which Meleager gathers neighboring nobles and heroes, including Theseus and Atalanta, to destroy a boar sent by Diana after it ravages Œneus’s dominions and kills people. Hunters are killed or wounded before Atalanta and Meleager bring it down.
