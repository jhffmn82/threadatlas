Oenopion

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the king, Οἰνοπίωνα, Οἰνοπίωνος

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Oenopion, the father of Merope, blinded and expelled Orion after Orion outraged Merope while drunk, and later hid underground when Orion returned seeking punishment. In The Library, Oenopion made Orion drunk, blinded him while he slept, and threw him onto the beach after Orion wooed Oenopion's daughter Merope.
