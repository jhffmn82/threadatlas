house (Claus's house) in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Claus's house is a strong, roomy dwelling built overnight by the Knooks from his logs, with a furnished large room around a fireplace and a smaller room containing his bed and dried moss.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MANHOOD / 1. The Laughing Valley

Claus's house is built during the night by the Knooks from logs he has prepared. It has a large room with a fireplace, table, bench, and cupboard, along with a smaller room containing a bed, stool, stand, and dried moss.
