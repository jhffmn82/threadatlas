Abstract written by a model

Heterogeneous knowledge is a retrieval target for retrieval-augmented generation methods, combining structured, semi-structured, and unstructured sources with encyclopedic and commonsense scopes. Because a single source has limited coverage, proposed solutions retrieve and reason across sources jointly or iteratively to answer questions and improve knowledge coverage, supported by literature and experiments.
