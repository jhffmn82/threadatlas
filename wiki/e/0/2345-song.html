song in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

A song built in Delphi commemorates Xenokrates, the Emmenidai, and Akragas for a chariot-race victory, enduring beyond wind and winter storm.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI.

Song is portrayed as a treasure built for Xenokrates, the Emmenidai, and Akragas. Its clear light will announce the victory, and neither wind nor winter storm will carry it away into the sea.
