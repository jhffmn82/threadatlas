Delia in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · -0015

Delia, Tibullus's first love, mourns and kisses him at his funeral, then leaves recalling that he lived while she was his flame.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY IX.

Delia attends Tibullus's funeral and joins his relatives and Nemesis in mourning. As she leaves, she says that she was more fortunately beloved by Tibullus and that he lived while she was his flame.

Delia in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Delia, Tibullus’s first love and former beloved, mourns him at his funeral, kisses him with Nemesis and his family, and recalls being happily loved by him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX

Delia comes to Tibullus’s funeral and kisses him with Nemesis and his family. As she descends from the pyre, she says that she was more happily loved by Tibullus and that he lived while she was his beloved.
