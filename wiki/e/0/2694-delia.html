Delia

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Delia mourns Tibullus at his funeral, kisses him with Nemesis and his family, and recalls being happily loved by him. In The Amores, or Amours, Delia mourns and kisses Tibullus at his funeral, then leaves recalling that he lived while she was his flame.
