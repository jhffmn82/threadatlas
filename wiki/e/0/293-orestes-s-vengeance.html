Orestes’s vengeance

event · 5 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Aegisthus dead, Orestes' avenging of his father, avenged his father, avenged his father nobly, avenger, enterprise, he hath slain, he is dead, his debt is paid this day, killed both his mother and Aegisthus, killed false Aegisthus, onset, punisher, revenge against the murderers

Abstract written by a model

The Odyssey presents it as Orestes’s noble vengeance for Agamemnon, carried out by returning from Athens and killing Aegisthus. The Seven Plays in English Verse presents it as Orestes’s revenge against Clytemnestra and Aegisthus through the deception of the urn, the false death report, and their killings. The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I treats it as Orestes’s act of vengeance against his father’s killer and the reason the chorus would not call him cowardly. The Electra of Euripides presents it as Orestes’s killing of Aegisthus during a sacrifice after gaining his confidence by posing as a traveler. The Library presents it as Orestes’s secret return to Mycenae, killing Clytaemnestra and Aegisthus, followed by madness and pursuit by the Furies.
