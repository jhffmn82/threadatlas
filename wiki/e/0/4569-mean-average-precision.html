Also called: MAP, MAP@10, Mean Average Precision (MAP)

Abstract written by a model

Mean Average Precision was proposed for evaluating RAG systems' handling of language-specific nuances and complexities in Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation. MAP@10 was used as a mean average precision metric truncated at rank 10, with the document reporting that reranking improved it and that the combined approach reached 0.322 versus 0.274 for RAG + Reranker in Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
