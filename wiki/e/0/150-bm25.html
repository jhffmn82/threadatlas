BM25 in Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

Yuning Mao, Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Yelong Shen, Jianfeng Gao, Jiawei Han, Weizhu Chen · 2021 · the work’s page · read it where BM25 first appears

Abstract written by a model

BM25 is a classic sparse retrieval method for open-domain question answering. It retrieves passages, including Wikipedia passages, by using a question as a query, and serves as a sparse passage-retrieval baseline implemented with Anserini default parameters. In reader inference it uses 500 passages. It underperforms DPR on NQ retrieval when used alone. Across GAR experiments, BM25 first retrieves with original questions, then with generation-augmented queries; GAR substantially improves it, whereas RM3 yields only marginal or negligible gains. Fusing diverse generated contexts further improves retrieval accuracy, letting GAR with BM25 match or exceed some dense retrieval methods such as DPR.

BM25 is used with GAR as a sparse representation and achieves performance comparable to or better than state-of-the-art dense retrieval methods such as DPR. Fusing results from diverse generated contexts consistently improves retrieval accuracy.
