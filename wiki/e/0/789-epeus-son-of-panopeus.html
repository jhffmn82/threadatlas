Epeus son of Panopeus in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Epeus, son of Panopeus, is a brave, large, skilful champion boxer. He claims superiority, challenges the competitors, defeats Euryalus with a jaw blow, helps him up, and wins the she-mule.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thereon the son of Peleus, when he had listened to all the thanks

Epeus presents himself as the best boxer among the competitors and challenges anyone to fight him. He defeats Euryalus with a blow to the jaw, raises him after he falls, and wins the she-mule.
