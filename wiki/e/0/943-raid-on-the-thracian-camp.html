raid on the Thracian camp

event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: deed, entered Ilion, enterprise, exploit, foul deed, passed through our armed band, the Trojan night raid, the deeds of the heroes, this work, work
  • In Hecuba and other plays, the Trojan night raid is conducted by Ulysses with Diomede against Rhesus and the Thracians.
  • In The Rhesus of Euripides, the raid on the Thracian camp is a surprise night attack in which Odysseus and Diomede kill Rhesus and other Thracians, take the horses, and escape through the guards.
  • In The Iliad, the raid on the Thracian camp is carried out by Ulysses and Diomed during the night.
