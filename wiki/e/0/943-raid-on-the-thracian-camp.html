event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, the raid on the Thracian camp is carried out by Ulysses and Diomed during the night. In The Rhesus of Euripides, the raid on the Thracian camp is a surprise night attack in which Odysseus and Diomede kill Rhesus and other Thracians, take the horses, and escape through the guards. In Hecuba and other plays, the Trojan night raid is conducted by Ulysses with Diomede against Rhesus and the Thracians.
