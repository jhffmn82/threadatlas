is_to_share shared immortality of Kastor and Polydeukes [chosen in loyalty to Kastor] Polydeukes rejected the exclusive possibility of living in Olympos without death or old age. He chose instead to share equal time with Kastor, being alive beneath the earth for half the time and in the golden house of heaven for the other half.
