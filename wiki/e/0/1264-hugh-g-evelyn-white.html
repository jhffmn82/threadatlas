PREFACE

Hugh G. Evelyn-White prepared the volume's texts, largely forming his own text and arranging the Hesiodic material in a proposed original order. He stated that circumstances of the present time prevented him from giving the Introduction the full revision he would have desired, and he signed the preface from Rampton, NR. Cambridge, on September 9, 1914.
