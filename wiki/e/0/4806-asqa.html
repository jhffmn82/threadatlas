ASQA is an open-ended testbed for long-form QA and open-domain summarization, built around ambiguous questions with multiple interpretations. It requires answers covering every interpretation, making generation and evaluation difficult; performance is assessed with EM, Disambig-F1, ROUGE, and DR, with FLARE reporting the best listed results.

ALCE-ASQA in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17

ALCE-ASQA is a long-form question answering task with citations. It uses author-provided top five GTR-XXL documents and is evaluated for correctness, fluency, citation precision, and citation recall, with retrieval threshold zero. On ASQA, SELF-RAG improves citation precision and recall over most models, but forced top-passage use or removing ISSUP harms performance.

ALCE-ASQA is used as a long-form question answering task with citations. It is evaluated using correctness, fluency, citation precision, and citation recall, and the retrieval threshold is set to zero because of its citation requirements.

RESULTS AND ANALYSIS / 5.1 / MAIN RESULTS .. 5.2 / ANALYSIS

On ASQA, SELF-RAG shows substantially higher citation precision and recall than all models except ChatGPT and surpasses ChatGPT in citation precision. Its performance is harmed when the top retrieved passage is always used or when ISSUP is removed during critique-guided beam search.
