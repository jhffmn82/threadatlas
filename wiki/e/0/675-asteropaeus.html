Asteropaeus in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Asteropaeus, Pelegon’s son and descended from the river Axius, confronts Achilles with two spears, lightly wounds him, then is killed and stripped of armour.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXI.

Asteropaeus, son of Pelegon and descendant of the river Axius, confronts Achilles with two spears. He wounds Achilles lightly but is unable to defeat him; Achilles kills him with a sword and strips him of his armour.
