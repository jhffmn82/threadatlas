Catalogues in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Fragmentary Catalogues are placed after Theogony in the restored Hesiodic corpus and chiefly recovered through Hesiodic papyri, whose listed fragments are assigned to numbered fragments.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

PREFACE

The Catalogues are fragmentary, but Hugh G. Evelyn-White places them after the Theogony because he considers that their true position.

BIBLIOGRAPHY

The Catalogues are identified as the chief gain from the recovery of Hesiodic papyri. The unit lists papyrus fragments belonging to the lost Catalogues and assigns several of them to numbered fragments.
