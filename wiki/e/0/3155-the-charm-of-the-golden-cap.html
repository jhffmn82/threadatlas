the charm of the Golden Cap

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Golden Cap charm, a charm, the Golden Cap's charm, the charm, the charm of the Cap

Abstract written by a model

The charm of the Golden Cap is a set of words inside the Cap that its wearer recites while standing on the left foot, right foot, then both feet to summon and command the Winged Monkeys. It permits only three commands; after two earlier uses, the Wicked Witch’s final command captures Dorothy’s companions and brings Dorothy to her castle.

the charm of the Golden Cap is in one work on this site, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter XII

The Golden Cap charm permits its owner to command the Winged Monkeys three times, but no more. The Wicked Witch has already used it twice before this unit and uses the third and final command to capture Dorothy’s companions and bring Dorothy to her castle.

Chapter XIV

The charm of the Golden Cap consists of words written inside the Cap and requires Dorothy to stand on her left foot, right foot, and then both feet while speaking them. It summons the Winged Monkeys to obey the Cap's owner.
