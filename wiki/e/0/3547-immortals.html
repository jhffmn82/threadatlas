immortals in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Immortals are beings who live forever in the Forest of Burzee, supporting and protecting Claus. When evil Awgwas threaten him, they unite in the Laughing Valley, defeat evil, and return to their forest duties.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

7. The Great Battle Between Good and Evil

The immortals agree that the Awgwas must be destroyed because they devote themselves to evil and threaten Claus. They gather in the Laughing Valley and defeat the forces of evil before returning to their duties in the Forest.
