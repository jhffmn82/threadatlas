plague among the Achaeans in The Iliad

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK I.

A plague strikes the Achaeans after Apollo hears Chryses’s prayer. It lasts for nine days, killing people as well as mules and hounds, and fills the host with funeral pyres. The Achaeans purify themselves and offer sacrifices after learning that the plague will end only when Chryseis is returned and Apollo is appeased.
