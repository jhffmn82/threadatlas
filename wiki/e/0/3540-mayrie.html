Mayrie in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Mayrie is a poor small girl and child with a younger brother who loves a wooden rabbit and wants toy cats. She visits Claus, cries when her brother receives the only ready cat, and is frightened by Shiegra's image. Claus later makes her a cat; after she breaks it, he gives her Bessie, which delights her.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

4. How Little Mayrie Became Frightened

Mayrie visits Claus with her younger brother because she wants a toy cat. She cries when Claus gives the only ready cat to her brother, becomes terrified by Shiegra's image, and later happily watches Claus carve and paint a cat for her.

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

Mayrie comes to Claus crying because she has broken her toy cat by dropping it twice. Claus gives her the silk-robed Bessie image dolly, and Mayrie receives it with great joy.
