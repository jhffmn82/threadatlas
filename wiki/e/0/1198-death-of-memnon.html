death of Memnon

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: How Memnon, Son of the Dawn, for Troy's sake fell in the Battle, Memnon by Achilles, the death of Memnon, whereon Died Memnon

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the death of Memnon occurs when Achilles kills Memnon after Memnon kills Antilochus, after which Eos obtains immortality for Memnon from Zeus. In The Fall of Troy, the death of Memnon occurs when Achilles's sword pierces Memnon beneath the breast-bone; Memnon falls into a pool of blood, his comrades flee, the winds carry away his corpse, and Eos and others mourn him, followed by the transformation of the Aethiopians into birds.
