Venus’s wounding by Diomed in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Venus’s wounding is an event in which Diomed, permitted by Minerva to fight her, pursues Venus as she rescues Aeneas and pierces her hand near the wrist through her robe. Ichor flows, Venus drops Aeneas, and Dione cleans the wound, ending her pain.

BOOK V.

Diomed pursues Venus because Minerva has told him that he may fight her. He pierces the flesh of her hand through her ambrosial robe, causing ichor to pour from the wound and making her drop Aeneas.
