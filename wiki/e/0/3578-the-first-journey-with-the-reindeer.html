8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

The first journey with the reindeer takes Claus, Flossie, and Glossie through the night to a village and a large city. Claus distributes his toys through chimneys and returns to the Laughing Valley just before daybreak, while the deer hurry back to the Forest.
