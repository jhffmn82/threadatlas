Also called: helmsman, right-minded helmsman

Abstract written by a model

The right-minded helmsman recognizes a captive as possibly divine, urges release, receives Dionysus’s mercy and favor, and is made happy after Dionysus reveals himself.

VII. TO DIONYSUS

The helmsman recognizes that the captive may be a god and urges the crew to release him. Dionysus has mercy on him, holds him back from fleeing, makes him happy, and tells him who he is.
