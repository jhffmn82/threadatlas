arrows in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Arrows are deadly missiles kept in a quiver with the bow. Penelope brings them for a contest through twelve axes; Ulysses then shoots them one by one at the suitors until they run out.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXI

Penelope brings a quiver containing many deadly arrows with the bow. The contest requires one of these arrows to pass through each of the twelve axes.

BOOK XXII

Ulysses shoots the arrows one by one into the suitors, who fall thickly around one another. When the arrows run out, he puts the bow aside and continues the fight with spears.
