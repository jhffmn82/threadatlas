ship of Protesilaus in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

An Achaean ship that brought Protesilaus to Troy but did not return him becomes the focus of fierce fighting. Hector seizes its stern and orders fire; Trojans attack and ignite it. Patroclus kills Pyraechmes, drives them away, and quenches the flames, though the ship remains half-burnt.

The ship that brought Protesilaus to Troy but never returned him to his homeland becomes the center of a fierce close fight. Hector seizes its stern and orders the Trojans to bring fire.

BOOK XVI.

The ship of Protesilaus is attacked by the Trojans and set on fire. Patroclus kills Pyraechmes near its stern, drives the Trojans away, and helps extinguish the flames, leaving the ship half-burnt.
