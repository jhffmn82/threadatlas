Abstract written by a model

Iamos, son of Euadne and Apollo, was nursed at birth by the Fates and serpents, reared by Aipytos, received prophetic power from Apollo, and became ancestor of the renowned Iamidai.

VI.

Iamos was the son of Euadne and Apollo and was nourished by two bright-eyed serpents with honey. As a young man he received a twofold prophetic treasure from Apollo and became the ancestor of the Iamidai, who were destined to be renowned among the Hellenes.
