jealousy

topic · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: I am grieved, dolor, grief, jealousy and grief, my sorrow, torment, trouble
  • In The Amores, or Amours, jealousy and grief make the speaker wish to die when he remembers the mistress’s guilt, provoke anger and thoughts of attacking her, and continue because he fears that the rival received kisses of equal quality.
  • In Heroides and Amores, jealousy makes the speaker/lover angry when he sees the beloved girl kissing another man, torments him after she gives him better and seemingly newly learned kisses, and makes him fear that the rival tasted the same pleasures.
