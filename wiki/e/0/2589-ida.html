Ida

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: Cretan Ida, craggy Ida

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Ida was the Cretan mountain associated with the Idæi Dactyli and the upbringing of Jupiter; its cave was said to have concealed Jupiter, and the surrounding places were consecrated to him. In The Amores, or Amours, Ida was a Cretan place of groves where Ceres saw Iasius, and it grew white with corn during Ceres's stay there.
