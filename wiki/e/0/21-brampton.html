Brampton in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Brampton is the Pepys family estate, central to managing uncle Robert’s affairs after his death. Samuel Pepys travels there to join his father, remain through the burial and following week, sort papers, inspect lands, handle disputes and relatives, and arrange his aunt’s departure for £10. His father continues settling estate matters there before the mother arrives, while Samuel returns after visits to Yelling and Graveley and ultimately rides toward London.

4 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The narrator rode to Brampton after uncle Robert's death and found his father there. He remained there through the following week while the family arranged the burial, sorted papers, and dealt with disputes over the estate.

opening

Brampton is where Samuel Pepys and his father organize the uncle’s affairs, inspect and manage lands, negotiate with relatives, and settle an agreement concerning the aunt’s departure from the house. Samuel leaves Brampton for London on the 22nd, while his father remains there afterward.

Pepys finds his father well at Brampton and is glad that his aunt has left, although her departure costs the family £10. He returns there after visiting Yelling and Graveley, and later leaves from Brampton to ride toward London.
