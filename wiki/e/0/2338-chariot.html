Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V.

The chariot associated with Arkesilas's victory was apparently consecrated to Apollo and left at Delphi. Its harness and other workmanship were hung in Apollo's temple near a natural wooden statue in the Parnassian shrine.
