Praxidamas in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Praxidamas, an Aiakidai athlete descended from Sokleides, won at Olympia, five times at Isthmos, and three at Nemea; he honored Sokleides and preceded his son Alkimidas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode VI

Praxidamas is Alkimidas's father and predecessor, whose footsteps Alkimidas follows. He won an Olympic olive-wreath for the Aiakidai, was crowned five times at Isthmos and three times at Nemea, and brought honor to Sokleides.
