Timodemos

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: TIMODEMOS, a man of battles, son of Timonoös, this man, thou

Abstract written by a model

Timodemos is an Athenian pankration winner from the Timodemidai clan and Acharnai deme, son of Timonoös. He won the pankration at Nemea through stubborn prowess and was honored by Athens; guided by his father, he is expected to win the Isthmian and Pythian games.

Timodemos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode II

Timodemos of Athens won the pankration at Nemea. He is identified as the son of Timonoös and is praised for his stubborn prowess; the ode predicts that he will win again at the Isthmian and Pythian games.
