Neoptolemos

Also called: Achilles' son Neoptolemos

Abstract written by a model

Neoptolemos, son of Achilles, helped sack Priam’s city, then briefly ruled Molossia, whose kingdom his descendants held. Bringing Trojan spoils to Delphi, he was killed there in a quarrel over meat and lay beneath Pytho’s floor. After death he was honored at Delphi as an Aiakid lord and guardian of justice.

Neoptolemos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode VII

Neoptolemos, son of Achilles, helped sack Priam's city and later became king for a short time in Molossia, whose kingdom his descendants continued to hold. He went to Delphi with spoils from Troy and was killed there by a man in a quarrel over meat; afterward he remained at Delphi as a heroic guardian of justice. Pindar repeatedly insists that he has not dishonored Neoptolemos with irretrievable words.
