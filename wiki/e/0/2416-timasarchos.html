Abstract written by a model

Timasarchos of Aigina, of the Theandrid clan and son of Timokritos, is celebrated for winning the boys’ wrestling-match at Nemea. Previously crowned at Athens and Thebes, he now wins at Kleonai, adding to his clan’s praised, renowned victories.

Timasarchos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode IV

Timasarchos is celebrated as the winner of the boys' wrestling-match at Nemea. He has also won wreaths at the games of the Kleonaians, at Athens, and at seven-gated Thebes, and his clan's name is said to be preeminent in songs of victory.
