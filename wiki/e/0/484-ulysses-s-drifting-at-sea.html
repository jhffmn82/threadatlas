Ulysses’s drifting at sea

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Nine days did I drift

Abstract written by a model

Ulysses claims to drift for nine days after the shipwreck, until a great wave carries him to the Thesprotian coast on the tenth night. Pheidon's son finds him nearly dead from cold and fatigue and brings him to the king's house.
