the encounter of Aphrodite and Anchises

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: gone up upon the well-fitted bed, joined in love, mating with a mortal man

Abstract written by a model

Aphrodite goes to Mount Ida in the form of a mortal maiden and meets Anchises while he is alone and playing the lyre. Anchises believes her story that she is mortal, takes her to bed, and sleeps with her. Afterward Aphrodite reveals herself, and Anchises fears that lying with a deathless goddess will leave him weakened and miserable.
