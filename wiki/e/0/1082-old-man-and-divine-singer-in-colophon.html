old man and divine singer (in Colophon) in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

A blind, divine singer in Colophon, servant of the Muses and far-shooting Apollo, holds a lyre. Dwelling in rocky Chios, he is praised for supreme lays and named by Delian maidens as the sweetest singer at their gathering.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III. TO DELIAN APOLLO

The blind singer dwells in rocky Chios and is praised as having supreme lays. The maidens of Delos are instructed to identify him, in one voice, as the sweetest singer who comes to their gathering.
