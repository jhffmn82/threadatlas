Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

OLYMPIAN ODES. / I.

The ode glorifies the Olympian games, Hieron’s victory, and the founding story of Pelops. It praises Hieron in an equestrian song and declares that the speaker’s Muse may proclaim an even greater glory for him in the future.
