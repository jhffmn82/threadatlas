Abstract written by a model

is_a place in Locris [where Hesiod was entertained and murdered] Oenoe in Locris was the place where Hesiod retired after interpreting an oracle as warning him away from Nemea. It was also sacred to Nemean Zeus, and Hesiod was murdered there after his hosts suspected him of seducing their sister; tradition further associated Oenoe with his burial.
