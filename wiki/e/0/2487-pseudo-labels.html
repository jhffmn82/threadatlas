RL-REFLECT .. Stabilizing Training of RL

Pseudo-labels evaluate the lexical and rating similarity of an entire reference document and encourage selection of ref#3 in the example. They may underestimate the importance of ref#1 and ref#2 when those documents contribute complementary aspects, and they discourage retrieval of ref#2 because its rating is nearly opposite to the ground truth.
