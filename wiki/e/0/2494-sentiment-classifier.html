Also called: BiLSTM with an attention layer, rating classifier, standard sentiment classifier

Abstract written by a model

A pretrained BiLSTM with attention, built from a bidirectional LSTM and following attention pooling, is optimized with cross entropy on Amazon reviews; it predicts ratings for generated reviews, producing classification accuracy to measure rating consistency.
