Oxylus

person · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Oxylos, Oxylus, son of Andraemon, he, him, Ὄξυλος

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Oxylos, of Elean descent and living in Aitolia, leads the Dorian invasion and afterward becomes king of Elis. In The Library, Oxylus, son of Andraemon from Aetolia, returns from exile on a one-eyed horse, is chosen by oracle as guide of the Heraclids, and leads them to defeat the enemy. In Description of Greece, Oxylus, an Ætolian who had fled after accidentally killing his brother, leads the Dorians to the Peloponnese, receives the kingdom of Elis, defeats Elis’s champion in single combat, allows the Epeans to remain, introduces Ætolian colonists, consolidates Elis’s population, and invites Agorius and other Achæans to settle there.
