Sherlock Holmes's knowledge and abilities in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Sherlock Holmes has profound chemistry, practical geology and British law, extensive sensational literature, and strong poison recognition; anatomy is accurate but unsystematic. He plays violin and practices fighting skills, while literature, philosophy, and astronomy are nil, politics feeble, and botany variable.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Sherlock Holmes has profound knowledge of chemistry, accurate but unsystematic knowledge of anatomy, practical knowledge of geology and British law, extensive knowledge of sensational literature, and skill with the violin, singlestick, boxing, and swordsmanship. His knowledge of literature, philosophy, and astronomy is described as nil, his knowledge of politics as feeble, and his botany as variable, while his ability to distinguish poisons is strong.
