CHAPTER II.

Sherlock Holmes has profound knowledge of chemistry, accurate but unsystematic knowledge of anatomy, practical knowledge of geology and British law, extensive knowledge of sensational literature, and skill with the violin, singlestick, boxing, and swordsmanship. His knowledge of literature, philosophy, and astronomy is described as nil, his knowledge of politics as feeble, and his botany as variable, while his ability to distinguish poisons is strong.
