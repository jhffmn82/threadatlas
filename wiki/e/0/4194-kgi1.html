KGI1 in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Michael Glass, Gaetano Rossiello, Md Faisal Mahbub Chowdhury, Alﬁo Gliozzo · 2021

KGI1 is a model for slot filling that uses explicit knowledge and DPR-trained retrieval, including dynamic negative sampling, to generate restricted candidate fillers with justifying evidence. It improves slot-filling accuracy and combined KILT metrics over earlier systems, reaching the strongest reported development performance among listed KGI systems. On T-REx, it greatly outperforms BARTLARGE and performs best with gold passages, less well with retrieved passages, and worst with random passages, reflecting a tradeoff from implicit to explicit knowledge. It supports zero-shot TACRED and human-in-the-loop candidate filling, surpasses ranking baselines, and improves further with few-shot examples.

KILT Experiments .. Slot Filling Evaluation

KGI1 extends KGI0 by training DPR with dynamic negative sampling. It achieves the strongest reported development performance among the listed KGI systems and produces large gains over previous systems in slot-filling accuracy and combined KILT metrics.

Analysis .. Slot Filling Evaluation

KGI1 is the strongest system for generating slot fillers from relevant explicit knowledge and achieves the best results on the reported KILT and T-REx evaluations. It performs worst with random passages, indicating that it has traded some implicit-knowledge capability for better use of explicit knowledge. In TACRED, it outperforms the ranking baselines in zero-shot evaluation and improves with one or four examples per relation.
