Penthesileia in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Penthesileia is an Amazon warrior-queen, daughter of Ares and sister of Hippolyte. Haunted by Erinnyes after accidentally killing Hippolyte, she comes from Thermodon to Troy with twelve Amazon princesses to escape reproach and cleanse her pollution. Armed with gifts of Ares, she promises Priam to kill Achilles and rout the Argives. She leads Amazons and Trojans fiercely, kills several Greeks, and drives the Argives into panic, but her weapons fail against Achilles and Aias. Achilles kills Penthesileia and her horse with one spear, fulfilling the Fates' foretelling. Remembered for valour and beauty, she is mourned by the Trojans as a beloved daughter and honored as queen in a tomb beside Laomedon's bones, with her armour and war-horse buried with her.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

The Fall of Troy

Penthesileia is identified in the contents as the Queen of the Amazons. Book I concerns how she died for Troy.

BOOK I:

Penthesileia comes from Thermodon to Troy with twelve Amazon princesses, seeking to escape reproach for accidentally killing her sister Hippolyte and to cleanse herself of the resulting pollution. She promises Priam that she will kill Achilles and rout the Argives, then leads the Trojans and Amazons into battle, where she kills several warriors and drives the Argives back.

Penthesileia leads the Amazons in a fierce attack, killing many Greeks and driving them into panic. She challenges Achilles and Aias, but her weapons fail against them; Achilles pierces her and her horse with one spear, killing them both.

Penthesileia is presented as a strong and beautiful Amazon queen who was slain by Achilles. The Trojans mourn her as a beloved daughter and honor her as a queen beside Laomedon.
