the deadly poppy odor in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

A powerful odor from many poppies causes sleep when breathed; it makes Dorothy and Toto fall asleep, nearly overcomes the Cowardly Lion, and does not trouble the Scarecrow or Tin Woodman.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

The deadly poppy odor makes Dorothy's eyes grow heavy and causes her to fall asleep, while Toto also falls asleep. It nearly overcomes the Cowardly Lion, but it does not trouble the Scarecrow or the Tin Woodman because they are not made of flesh.
