Jerusalem in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Jerusalem, a revered Jewish city with a temple, was built by Moses; later Antiochus besieged it and demolished its walls after Jews surrendered hostages.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXXIV.

Jerusalem was besieged by Antiochus. After the Jews surrendered hostages, Antiochus demolished the city's walls.

BOOK XXXVIL

Moses built Jerusalem and the temple there after taking possession of Judea. The city and temple are described as highly revered among the Jews.

Jerusalem in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Jerusalem had a courtly musical tradition similar to that associated with Assyria and Babylon. In the time of David and Solomon it also contained the Temple, where many singers and instrumental performers were engaged in sacred music.
