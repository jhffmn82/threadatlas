Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Jerusalem is a revered Jewish city with a temple, built by Moses; later, Antiochus besieged it and demolished its walls after Jews surrendered hostages. In Musical Instruments, Jerusalem had a courtly musical tradition similar to that associated with Assyria and Babylon and, in the time of David and Solomon, contained the Temple, where many singers and instrumental performers were engaged in sacred music.
