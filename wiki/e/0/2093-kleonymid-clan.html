Kleonymid clan in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

The Kleonymid clan is a Theban clan, descended from Kleonymos and maternally related to the Labdakidai. Known from the beginning for chariot racing, it loses four members in one day of war, declines, then regains glory through Melissos's victories.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode III

The Kleonymid clan has ancient fame in chariot racing and is related on the mother's side to the Labdakidai. Four members of the house are lost in a single day of war, but the clan's glory blossoms again through Melissos's victories.
