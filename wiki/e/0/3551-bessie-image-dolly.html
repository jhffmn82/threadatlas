5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

Claus makes the Bessie image dolly from clay, bakes it over glowing coals, and dresses it in a lavender silk gown, lace collar, and pink silk shoes. He paints its face with Bessie’s brown eyes, golden hair, and rosy cheeks before giving it to Mayrie.
