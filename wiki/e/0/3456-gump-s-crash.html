Gump’s crash in The Marvelous Land of Oz

Gump misjudges a landing on a level rock, breaks both right wings, tumbles down a cliff, and is left suspended upside down after dumping travelers into the Jackdaw’s Nest.

1 section; each heading opens the text

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

While trying to land on a level rock, the Gump misjudges its speed, breaks both right wings, and tumbles down a cliff. It catches on a projecting rock bottom-side up, dumping the travelers into the Jackdaw’s nest.
