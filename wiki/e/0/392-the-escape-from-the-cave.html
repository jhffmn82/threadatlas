Abstract written by a model

Odysseus and the Achaeans escape Polyphemus’s cave by hiding beneath sheep; Polyphemus checks their backs but misses them, and they reach their ship with sheep and sail away.

Odysseus binds his companions beneath groups of sheep and hides beneath the belly of the largest ram. Polyphemus checks the animals’ backs but does not discover the men, allowing them to leave the cave, reach the ship with sheep, and sail away.
