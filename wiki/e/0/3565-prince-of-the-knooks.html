Abstract written by a model

Prince of the Knooks is the ruler and king of the Knooks in Burzee. On Christmas Eve, he permits Claus to use up to ten deer for one night each year, requires their return by daybreak, names them reindeer, and secures their protection through the Fairy Queen.

10. Christmas Eve

The Prince of the Knooks grants Claus permission to use up to ten deer once each year on Christmas Eve. He requires the deer to return to the Forest by daybreak and names those serving Claus reindeer, granting them additional protections and benefits.
