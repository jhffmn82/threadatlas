Abstract written by a model

In A Study in Scarlet, Baker Street contains 221B, the suite Holmes finds suitable for himself and Watson, where they discuss the Brixton Road murder and await a response to a ring advertisement. In The Sign of the Four, Baker Street is Holmes’s address, where Watson waits for news and which Holmes gives as a contact address in an advertisement; Holmes’s telegram directs Athelney Jones there. In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, Baker Street is Holmes’s residence and recurring base, where visitors seek help, Holmes keeps books and evidence, investigates the blue carbuncle, confronts James Windibank and James Ryder, and begins or discusses cases with Watson. In The Hound of the Baskervilles, Baker Street is Holmes’s residence, where Holmes and Watson return after meeting Sir Henry and John Clayton answers Holmes’s questions about the cab passenger who followed them. In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, Baker Street contains Holmes’s rooms, where Holmes and Watson use a wax bust as bait, receive Stanley Hopkins, examine evidence, prepare for Yoxley Old Place, and discuss a missing letter with Lord Bellinger and Trelawney Hope. In The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes, Baker Street is Holmes’s home, where he recovers, instructs Watson, receives visitors, develops and explains theories, and uses a bedroom exit, window, and wax dummy in his confrontation with Count Negretto Sylvius and Sam Merton.

Baker Street in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25 · the work’s page · read it where Baker Street first appears

Abstract written by a model

Baker Street is the location of Sherlock Holmes’s rooms and lodgings, including 221B, and a recurring base for his work with Dr. Watson. Visitors come there for help, including the King, Alexander Holder, Violet Hunter, Henry Baker, and James Windibank. Holmes keeps books and case evidence there, receives notes and telegrams, investigates the blue carbuncle, and discusses or explains cases after expeditions. It is also a place of confrontation: Holmes locks Windibank inside briefly, then confronts James Ryder and obtains his confession. From early visits and invitations through meetings before and after an arrest, it remains the place to which Holmes and Watson return, including for breakfast, and where new cases begin with Holder’s arrival and Hunter’s urgent telegram.

Baker Street in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10 · the work’s page · read it where Baker Street first appears

Abstract written by a model

Baker Street contains and is the home of Sherlock Holmes. There, Holmes recovers while feigning near death, gives Dr. John Watson instructions, and receives Watson after the operation at Baron Adelbert Gruner's house. It is also where Holmes develops and later explains his theory about Ferguson's wife. Holmes meets Dr. Watson, Trevor Bennett, and later Edith Presbury there. In its final recorded episode, its bedroom exit, window, and wax dummy aid Holmes's investigation and confrontation with Count Negretto Sylvius and Sam Merton.
