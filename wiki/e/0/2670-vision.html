vision

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Such a vision as this, imaginis, the dream vision, this vision, visa

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the dream vision shows a grove, a green meadow, a white heifer with her bull, and a crow that pecks the heifer before she joins other bulls; its interpretation presents the scene as a sign of the speaker's love, the girl's departure, and adultery. In The Amores, or Amours, the vision is a nighttime dream that terrifies the narrator: in a sunny grove, a crow pecks a cow, departs with her bull, and she enviously joins other herds.

ELEGY V.

The vision shows a grove beneath a sunny hill, a grassy meadow watered by a stream, a white cow, her bull companion, and a crow. The crow pecks the cow, then leaves with the bull; the cow responds with envy and joins other herds.
