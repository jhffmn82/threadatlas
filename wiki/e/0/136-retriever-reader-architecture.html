retriever-reader architecture

architecture · 4 works · 15 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Retrieve-then-Read, retrieve-then-read, retrieve-then-read paradigm, retriever-reader pipeline, retriever-reader pipelines
  • In “Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering,” the retriever-reader architecture is described as a two-component architecture that retrieves a document subset and reads it to extract or generate an answer.
  • In “LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering,” the retriever-reader pipeline is identified as an open-domain TableQA system and as a best-performing approach combining table-information retrieval with answer generation.
  • In “LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering,” retriever-reader pipelines are described as the typical implementation framework for recent open-domain TableQA models, consisting of retriever and reader components.
  • In “Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering,” retrieve-then-read is described as a paradigm for multi-hop question answering that decomposes questions, retrieves documents, and derives answers from them.
  • In “REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation,” the retriever-reader architecture is described as a system that retrieves relevant passages and processes them with a reader to generate an answer for ODQA.

retriever-reader architecture in Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

Yuning Mao, Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Yelong Shen, Jianfeng Gao, Jiawei Han, Weizhu Chen · 2021

The retriever-reader architecture consists of a retriever and a reader. It first uses a question to retrieve a small document subset from a larger collection, then reads those documents to extract or generate an answer, aiming to improve retriever effectiveness, efficiency, and reader performance.

retrieve-then-read in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024

Retrieve-then-read is a paradigm commonly used for multi-hop question answering. It breaks complex questions into simpler single questions, retrieves documents for each, then derives answers from them. Its effectiveness is constrained by retriever performance and noise or incompleteness in retrieved documents, and it overlooks world knowledge inherent in large language models.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The retrieve-then-read paradigm is limited by the quality of its retriever and the unavoidable noise in retrieved documents. GenGround is introduced as an alternative generate-then-ground framework.

Introduction

The retrieve-then-read paradigm first has large language models break a complex question into simpler questions, retrieves relevant documents for each step, and then derives answers from those documents. Its reliance on retrieved documents can cause performance declines when retrieval is incomplete or includes irrelevant statements.
