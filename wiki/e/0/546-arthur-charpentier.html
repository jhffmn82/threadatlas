Arthur Charpentier in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Arthur Charpentier, Madame Charpentier’s son and Alice Charpentier’s brother, is a naval sub-lieutenant who confronts Drebber over Alice, pursues him with a heavy stick, and is arrested in connection with Drebber’s death.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Arthur Charpentier, a sub-lieutenant in Her Majesty’s navy, intervenes when Enoch J. Drebber attempts to take Alice Charpentier away. He follows Drebber with a stick, is later arrested by Tobias Gregson, and acknowledges that he is being arrested for involvement in Drebber’s death.
