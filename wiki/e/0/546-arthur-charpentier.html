CHAPTER VI.

Arthur Charpentier, a sub-lieutenant in Her Majesty’s navy, intervenes when Enoch J. Drebber attempts to take Alice Charpentier away. He follows Drebber with a stick, is later arrested by Tobias Gregson, and acknowledges that he is being arrested for involvement in Drebber’s death.
