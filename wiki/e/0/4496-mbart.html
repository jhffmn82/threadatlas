mBART in Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training

Yifan Gao, Qingyu Yin, Zheng Li, Rui Meng, Tong Zhao, Bing Yin, Irwin King, Michael R. Lyu · 2022

mBART is a multilingual denoising pre-trained sequence-to-sequence language model used for multilingual keyphrase generation. It takes retrieved English keyphrases and a source passage separated by special tokens, then generates target-language keyphrases. Across monolingual, multilingual, English-joint-training, and English-pretraining evaluations, joint multilingual training generally outperforms separate monolingual training, indicating cross-language transfer of key-information locating and summarizing ability.

Model .. 4.3

mBART is used as the multilingual denoising sequence-to-sequence model for keyphrase generation. It receives retrieved English keyphrases and a source passage separated by special tokens, and it generates a target-language sequence of keyphrases.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It is evaluated in monolingual, multilingual, English-joint-training, and English-pretraining configurations for multilingual keyphrase generation. The multilingual configuration generally outperforms the monolingual configuration on the reported EcommerceMKP and AcademicMKP averages.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

mBART performs better when jointly trained on all languages than when separately trained on each language. This suggests that locating and summarizing key information can transfer across languages.
