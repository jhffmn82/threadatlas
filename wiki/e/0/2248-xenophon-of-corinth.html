Xenophon of Corinth in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Xenophon of Corinth, an Oligaithidai clan member, won the Stadion race and Pentathlon at Olympia in 464 B.C., as well as two victories each at the Isthmian games and Nemea. The account culminates in his crowned public return to Corinth.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII.

Xenophon won the Stadion race and the Pentathlon at Olympia in 464 B.C. He also had two victories at the Isthmian games and at Nemea, and the ode celebrates his return to Corinth with his Olympic crown.
