Hector's death in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Hector's death is a battle episode caused by Achilles. Achilles pursues Hector three times around the city; Zeus weighs their dooms, Hector's falls toward Hades, and Minerva tricks him into stopping. Achilles kills Hector with a spear through the throat, treats the victory as a triumph, and considers the next attack. Hector's loss leaves Troy and Ilius without their principal, bravest defender, prompting citywide lament and Priam's attempt to recover his body.

duel between Achilles and Hector in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Achilles pursues Hector around Troy's walls and finally confronts him when Hector stops fleeing. Achilles finds an opening at Hector's uncovered neck and kills him, refusing Hector's request for ransom or funeral consideration until later persuaded by Priam.
