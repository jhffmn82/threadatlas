Sir George Carteret in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Sir George Carteret is an office member who attends a meeting, helps approve a letter to the Duke of York on financial difficulties, and hosts invited officers for dinner at Deptford, without inviting Pepys.

opening

Sir George Carteret had invited some officers to dine at Deptford. Pepys goes out when he hears Carteret is coming to see whether he will invite him, but Carteret does not send for him, which troubles Pepys somewhat.
