Abstract written by a model

Theaios, son of Oulias, is a wrestler and victor at Argos. He first won at Pytho, then gained crowns at Isthmos and Nemea and twice at Athens; twice winning Hekatombaia at Argos brought relief from his labors.

Theaios is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode X

Theaios, son of Oulias, won the wrestling-match twice at the Hekatombaia and thereby gained relief from his labors. He was also first at Pytho, won crowns at the Isthmos and Nemea, prevailed three times at the Gates of the Sea and three times on the plains of Adrastos, and twice won at the festival of the Athenians.
