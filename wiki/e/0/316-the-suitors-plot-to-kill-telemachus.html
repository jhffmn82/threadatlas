the suitors' plot to kill Telemachus

Abstract written by a model

This is the suitors' unsuccessful plot to murder Telemachus before he returns home, driven by their fear of the trouble he may cause. Led by Antinous with twenty men, they sail to wait in the straits between Ithaca and Samos. Minerva doubts their success. Telemachus returns before they can capture him, and they consider attacking him at his farm or on the road. Penelope learns of the plan through Medon and rebukes Antinous. An eagle omen leads Amphinomus to predict failure and send them to dinner.

The suitors are preparing to murder Telemachus, but an eagle carrying a dove flies to their left. Amphinomus interprets the event as a sign that the plot will fail and persuades them to go to dinner instead.
