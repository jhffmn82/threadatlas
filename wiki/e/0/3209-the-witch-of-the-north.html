Abstract written by a model

The Witch of the North is a good, beloved witch who lives in the North, befriends the Munchkins, and has not seen Oz. Less powerful than the Wicked Witch of the East, she arrives after that witch’s death, explains the land, gives Dorothy silver shoes and protective kiss, directs her to the City of Emeralds, and disappears.

Chapter II

The Witch of the North comes to the Munchkins’ land after learning that the Wicked Witch of the East is dead. She identifies herself as a good witch, explains the divisions of the Land of Oz, gives Dorothy the silver shoes, and directs her to the City of Emeralds. She kisses Dorothy on the forehead for protection before disappearing.
