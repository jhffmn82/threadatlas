11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

The first stockings tradition begins when Margot, Dick, Ned, and Sara's wet stockings are hung by the chimney to dry. Santa Claus notices them, fills them with toys, and inspires the children to tell others about what happened. The custom spreads, helping Santa Claus find children and deliver their presents more quickly.
