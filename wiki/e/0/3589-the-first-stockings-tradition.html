the first stockings tradition in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

The first stockings tradition begins when Margot, Dick, Ned, and Sara hang wet stockings by the chimney to dry. Santa Claus fills them with toys. The children tell others, who adopt the custom; it helps Santa Claus find children and deliver presents more quickly.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

The first stockings tradition begins when Margot, Dick, Ned, and Sara's wet stockings are hung by the chimney to dry. Santa Claus notices them, fills them with toys, and inspires the children to tell others about what happened. The custom spreads, helping Santa Claus find children and deliver their presents more quickly.
