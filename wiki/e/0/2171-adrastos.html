Adrastos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Adrastos belongs to Argos as a returning exile or survivor and is in strife with the sons of Kadmos in an earlier legendary time. As king, he founds and ordains the Pythian games at Sikyon beside Asopos, honoring Phoibos with athletic and chariot contests.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode IX

Adrastos ordained the contest beside the stream of Asopos in honor of Phoibos. As king in that place, he founded a new festival and athletic and chariot contests that made his city known and glorious.
