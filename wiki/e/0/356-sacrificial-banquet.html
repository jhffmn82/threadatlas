sacrificial banquet in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Alcinous plans a magnificent banquet in honour of Ulysses; guests eat and drink, Demodocus sings as Ulysses hides tears, then athletic games follow.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VIII

Alcinous provides a magnificent banquet, having twelve sheep, eight full-grown pigs, and two oxen prepared for the assembled company. After the guests eat and drink, Demodocus sings, and Ulysses hides his tears beneath his mantle.
