Brigham Young in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Brigham Young is portrayed as a Mormon leader, Prophet, skilful administrator, and resolute chief. He visits John Ferrier’s farm to enforce Mormon marriage requirements, reproaching Ferrier over marriage and Lucy’s association with a Gentile. Young orders Lucy to marry a faithful Mormon, grants a month of grace, and, after Ferrier refuses, carries out his threat through a twenty-nine-day warning.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Brigham Young visits the Ferrier farm and reproaches John Ferrier for failing to marry and for allowing Lucy to be associated with a Gentile. He demands that Lucy marry a faithful Mormon, gives her a month to choose, and threatens Ferrier and Lucy if they resist.

CHAPTER IV.

Young had promised John Ferrier a month to amend his conduct. After Ferrier refuses the marriage proposals, the twenty-nine-day warning indicates that Young’s threat is being carried out.
