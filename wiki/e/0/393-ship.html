ship in The Odyssey

Homer · about 632 to 599 BC · the work’s page · read it where ship first appears

Abstract written by a model

The ship is a sailing vessel, with a rudder, used by Ulysses and his men. It sails on the sea carrying food and drink for the crew, though they have sorrow in their hearts. Nearly struck by a first falling rock, it is driven back toward the mainland; after a second rock falls, a wave drives it toward an island shore. It is run onto the sands, left when the men depart at dawn, and later brought into the island’s safe harbour. Circe orders it drawn onto land, with its gear and property hidden in a cave, then directs Ulysses to raise its mast and white sails for the North Wind to carry it across Oceanus. Though no ship can reach Hades, it is beached in Proserpine’s country before Ulysses proceeds there. After crossing Oceanus, it carries the men past the Sirens and through the straits to the sun-god’s island. When the cattle are slaughtered, Jove destroys it with white lightning, leaving only mast and keel with Ulysses.
