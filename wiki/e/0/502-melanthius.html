Melanthius

Abstract written by a model

Melanthius is a goatherd, son of Dolius, who serves and supports the suitors. He is hostile to Ulysses, abusing and kicking him while Ulysses is disguised, and threatens Eumaeus. He joins the suitors and secretly supplies them with shields, helmets, and spears. Eumaeus and Philoetius capture, bind, imprison, and suspend him in the store-room. He is later taken into the inner court, mutilated, and killed.

Melanthius is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

BOOK XVII

Melanthius meets Eumaeus and Ulysses on the road and verbally abuses Ulysses, predicting that stools will be thrown at him if he approaches Ulysses’s house. He kicks Ulysses and later joins the suitors inside.

BOOK XXII

Melanthius secretly enters the store-room and brings shields, helmets, and spears to the suitors. Eumaeus and Philoetius capture him, bind his hands and feet, lock him in the store-room, and suspend him from a pillar near the rafters.
