Minerva's aegis in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

A priceless, deathless shaggy-fringed aegis with a Gorgon head and hundred gold tassels; Jove gives it to Apollo, whose stillness allows strikes, but shaking it panics Achaeans.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XV.

The aegis is the shaggy-fringed weapon that Jove gives Apollo to carry. While Apollo holds it still, weapons strike on both sides, but when he shakes it at the Achaeans, they panic and lose their former courage.
