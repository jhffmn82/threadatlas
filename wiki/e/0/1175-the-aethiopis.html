Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Ionic School

The Aethiopis, attributed to Arctinus of Miletus, narrated events after the death of Hector. It included the arrival and deaths of Penthesilea and Memnon, the death of Achilles, and the dispute between Odysseus and Aias over Achilles’ arms.

THE AETHIOPIS

The Aethiopis is a five-book work by Arctinus of Miletus that follows the Iliad. It recounts the arrivals of Penthesileia and Memnon, the deaths of Antilochus, Memnon, and Achilles, the burial of Achilles, and the dispute over his arms.
