Abstract written by a model

The War of 1812–1814 was fought between the United States and Canada, repeatedly invaded by American armies. It revealed French-Canadian loyalty in Lower Canada: French-Canadian militiamen and officers fought alongside English-speaking Canadians, helped win the engagement at Chateauguay, and Lower Canada showed less disloyalty than Upper Canada after bitterness was reduced under Sir George Prevost’s conciliatory policy.

{1} CHAPTER I CANADIANS, OLD AND NEW The conquest of Canada by British arms in the Seven Years' War gave rise to a situation in the colony which was fraught with tragic possibilities.

During the War of 1812–1814, Canada was repeatedly invaded by American armies. French-Canadian militiamen under French-Canadian officers fought alongside English-speaking Canadians, and French-Canadian forces helped win the engagement at Chateauguay.
