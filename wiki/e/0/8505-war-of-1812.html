War of 1812 in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The War of 1812–1814 was fought between the United States and Canada, repeatedly invaded by American armies. It revealed French-Canadian loyalty in Lower Canada: French-Canadian militiamen and officers fought alongside English-speaking Canadians, helped win the engagement at Chateauguay, and Lower Canada showed less disloyalty than Upper Canada after bitterness was reduced under Sir George Prevost’s conciliatory policy.
