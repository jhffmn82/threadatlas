Triumph

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: This victory, Triumphe, the credit of the exploit, the triumph, triumph, triumphant laurels, victory
  • In The Amores, or Amours, Triumph is summoned by the speaker to commemorate his personal victory over obstacles to Corinna after gaining her without bloodshed.
  • In Heroides and Amores, the triumph is claimed by the speaker because Corinna is in his arms after being guarded by her husband, keeper, and door; it is described as a special victory won without bloodshed or military assistance.

ELEGY X.

The speaker calls for triumphant laurels and invites Triumph to come to him because he has obtained Corinna without bloodshed. His victory is presented as a personal achievement rather than one shared with soldiers.
