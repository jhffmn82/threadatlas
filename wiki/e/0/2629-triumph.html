Triumph

Also called: This victory, Triumphe, the credit of the exploit, the triumph, triumph, triumphant laurels, victory

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the triumph is claimed by the speaker because Corinna is in his arms after being guarded by her husband, keeper, and door; it is described as a special victory won without bloodshed or military assistance. In The Amores, or Amours, Triumph is summoned by the speaker to commemorate his personal victory over obstacles to Corinna after gaining her without bloodshed.
