the Wicked Witch of the East in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Wicked Witch of the East is one of the four witches of Oz. She ruled the Munchkins for many years, forcing them to work as slaves, and was proud of her silver shoes. Promised two sheep and a cow, she opposed the Tin Woodman’s marriage by enchanting his axe, leaving him without a heart. Dorothy’s house fell on her, killing and destroying her and freeing the Munchkins.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

The Wicked Witch of the East had ruled the Munchkins for many years and forced them to work as slaves. Dorothy’s house falls on her, killing her and freeing the Munchkins.

Chapter V

The Wicked Witch of the East was promised two sheep and a cow to prevent the Tin Woodman from marrying the Munchkin girl. She enchanted his axe so that it successively cut off his legs, arms, head, and body, leaving him without a heart.

Wicked Witch in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

The Wicked Witch is a powerful, cruel witch with magic arts, silver shoes, and control over Nimmie Amee as a slave. She opposes Nimmie Amee’s planned marriages, first to Nick Chopper and later to Captain Fyter. Angry at defiance and unwilling to allow the marriages, she enchants an axe to sever Nick Chopper’s limbs, body, and head, and enchants Captain Fyter’s sword to hack him apart. Though her magic kept her alive for a long time, Dorothy destroys her when her house falls on her.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Witch of the East is a powerful witch who wears silver shoes and keeps Nimmie Amee as her slave. Angry that Nimmie Amee plans to marry the woodchopper, she enchants his axe so that it cuts off his limbs.

Chapter Two

The Wicked Witch hates Nick Chopper for defying her and uses the enchanted axe to sever his limbs, body, and head. She hides his severed head and leaves Nimmie Amee enslaved to serve her.

Chapter Sixteen

The Wicked Witch opposed Nimmie Amee's marriage to Captain Fyter and enchanted his sword, causing it to hack him apart. The Scarecrow tells Captain Fyter that the Wicked Witch has been destroyed.
