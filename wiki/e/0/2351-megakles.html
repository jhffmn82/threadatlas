Megakles

Also called: Megakles of Athens, O Megakles

Abstract written by a model

Megakles, an Alkmaionidai member and probably nephew or possibly son of Kleisthenes, won the four-horse chariot race in 490 and had victories at Isthmos, Olympia, and Kirrha; despite these successes, he was ostracized twice.

Megakles is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

VII.

Megakles of Athens won the four-horse chariot-race in B.C. 490. He belonged to the Alkmaionidai and was probably the nephew, or possibly the son, of Kleisthenes. He and his father had won victories at Isthmos, Olympia, and Kirrha, and he was twice ostracized.
