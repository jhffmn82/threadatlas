Hellen in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Hellen, a mythical ancestor possibly born from Zeus, fathered Dorus, Xuthus, and Aeolus with Orseis, named the Greeks Hellenes, and divided the country among his sons.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Ἕλλην μὲν πρῶτος, ὃν ἐκ Atos γεγεννῆσθαι ϊ

Some say that Hellen was born from Zeus. By the nymph Orseis he had Dorus, Xuthus, and Aeolus, named the Greeks Hellenes after himself, and divided the country among his sons.
