Abstract written by a model

In RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation, Rouge-L evaluates the quality of generated commit messages. In Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory, ROUGE evaluates summarization with R-1, R-2, and R-L, and their combination defines the memory-selection metric; it evaluates summarization on XSum and BigPatent, where self-memory improves ROUGE for both BRIO and BART. In Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding, ROUGE-L evaluates end-to-end generation for MSMARCO and KILT-WoW and contributes to the KILT-RL combined metric; the multi-task method obtains 59.1 ROUGE-L on MSMARCO, while RAC obtains 16.81 ROUGE-L on KILT-WoW among the listed methods. In Crafting Personalized Agents through Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Editable Memory Graphs, ROUGE, including R-1, R-2, and R-L, evaluates generated answers for question answering; EMG-RAG with GPT-4 scores 93.46 for R-1, 83.55 for R-2, and 88.06 for R-L. In M-RAG: Reinforcing Large Language Model Performance through Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Multiple Partitions, ROUGE R-1, R-2, and R-L evaluate summarization; higher ROUGE values indicate better results, and M-RAG obtains the strongest reported scores in the main Mixtral 8×7B summarization comparisons. In R2AG: Incorporating Retrieval Information into Retrieval Augmented Generation, Rouge is used with F1 score to measure performance on DuReader, with R2AG obtaining 0.1663 and R2AG+RAFT obtaining 0.2734. In RAG-RLRC-LaySum at BioLaySumm: Integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Readability Control for Layman Summarization of Biomedical Texts, ROUGE, including ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L, measures relevance against reference summaries; Plain LED has average ROUGE-L of 42.70, while RAG-RLRC has the highest average ROUGE-L shown in the table at 43.38. In RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment, ROUGE-L is the official evaluation metric for the four domain-specific question answering datasets, with RAG-Studio achieving 37.5, 41.8, 40.0, and 47.1 ROUGE-L on Biomedical, Finance, Law, and Computing, respectively.

and 4 more works

In Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach: Document Question Answering using Large Language Model, ROUGE-L evaluates performance and text generation through candidate-reference overlap using the longest common subsequence, precision, recall, and F1 score, and shows the proposed QA system outperforming other QA applications on both datasets. In Mitigating the Privacy Issues in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) via Pure Synthetic Data, ROUGE-L is a performance indicator and primary similarity-based metric comparing generated answers with ground-truth answers; scores above 0.5 define Rouge Prompts and Rouge Contexts. In SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains, Rouge-L evaluates open-ended questions as the main metric alongside MAUVE and is also reported for the LiveQA and MedicationQA results in the medical table. In VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus, ROUGE-L measures the longest common subsequence between generated answers and ground truths and is one of the four metrics used to compare the systems.
