Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the Munchkins are the people of the eastern land formerly held in bondage by the Wicked Witch of the East; after her death, they are freed, thank Dorothy, explain the situation, and tell her that deserts block a direct return home. In The Giant Horse of Oz, the Munchkins are placed under the rule of Cheeriobed and Orin, while the Scarecrow identifies himself as a Munchkin and promises to be their loyal friend and subject.
