the Munchkins in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Munchkins are people living in the eastern land of the Munchkins, formerly ruled and holding them in bondage by the Wicked Witch of the East. After her death and destruction, they are freed, gratefully thank Dorothy, explain the situation, and tell her deserts block a direct return home.

The Munchkins are the people living in the eastern part of the Land of Oz, where the Wicked Witch of the East had ruled them. After the witch’s death, they thank Dorothy, explain what happened, and sadly tell her that deserts prevent her from returning directly home.
