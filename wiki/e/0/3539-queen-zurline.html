Queen Zurline in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Queen Zurline is a Fairy, Queen of the Wood-Nymphs, and lovely ruler of the Pool of Nares. She supports Necile's petition to keep an infant, tells Claus that all children deserve happiness and that he must make them glad, and guards children by advising him. When the Awgwas persecute Claus, she leads the immortals' protection by sealing his dwelling.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

The Queen of the Fairies tells Claus that rich and poor children alike naturally long for pretty playthings and can suffer loneliness or enjoy happiness. She declares that it is his duty to make all children glad.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

Queen Zurline learns of Claus's persecution and declares that the immortals must protect him. She places her Seal on every door and window of Claus's dwelling, adding it to the Seals of the Fairies, Ryls, and Knooks.

Fairy Queen in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

The Fairy Queen, queen of the fairies, promises on Christmas Eve to protect the deer while away from the Forest, then sends Fairies to recover toys stolen and hidden by the Awgwas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. Christmas Eve

The Fairy Queen promises that no harm will come to the deer while they are away from the Forest. At Ak's request, she also tries to find the toys stolen by the Awgwas and sends Fairies to search for them.
