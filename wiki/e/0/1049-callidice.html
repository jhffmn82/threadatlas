Callidice

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Calli- dice, ΚΚαλλιδίκη
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Callidice is queen of the Thesprotians who marries Odysseus; after her death, Polypoetes succeeds and Odysseus returns to Ithaca.
  • In The Library, Callidice is queen of the Thesprotians who asks Ulysses to remain, gives him the kingdom, bears him a son named Polypoetes, and after her death sees Ulysses give the kingdom to their son and return to Ithaca.
