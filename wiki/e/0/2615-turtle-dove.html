Also called: dove, the gentle dove, the turtle-dove, turtur, turtur amice

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the turtle-dove is a friendly, faithful bird that lives harmoniously as the parrot’s companion, remains loyal through their shared life’s end, and is urged to grieve first. In The Amores, or Amours, the turtle-dove is an affectionate bird that shares lifelong friendship with the parrot and is urged to mourn the parrot above other birds.
