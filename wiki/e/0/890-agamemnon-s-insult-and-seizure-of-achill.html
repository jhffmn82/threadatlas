Agamemnon's insult and seizure of Achilles' prize

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Agamemnon dishonours Achilles by taking the woman awarded to him, a seizure Achilles calls deception and a bitter wrong. Achilles withdraws, asks Thetis for divine compensation, and refuses to fight until his honour is restored; Jove agrees to aid the Trojans until the Achaeans restore it.
