quarrel between the sons of Atreus in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

A quarrel between Agamemnon and Menelaus over the Achaean homeward voyage: after Troy falls, Menelaus urges immediate sailing, Agamemnon demands sacrifices first, dividing the Achaeans.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III

After Troy falls, Menelaus urges the Achaeans to sail home, while Agamemnon wants them to wait and sacrifice to Minerva. Their quarrel divides the Achaeans, with some remaining under Agamemnon and others sailing away.
