ox-hide wind sack in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

An ox-hide sack tightly bound with silver thread contains roaring winds until Ulysses’s men open it, believing it holds gold and silver, and release them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK X

Aeolus makes an ox-hide sack to hold the roaring winds and binds its mouth tightly with a silver thread. Ulysses's men believe it contains gold and silver, open it while he sleeps, and release the winds.
