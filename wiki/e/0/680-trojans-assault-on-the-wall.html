Abstract written by a model

The Trojans assault the Achaean wall in companies, attacking its gates, breastworks, battlements, and buttresses. Sarpedon breaks a battlement and creates a breach; despite continued resistance, Hector orders an intensified attack, breaks open the gates, and the Trojans scale the wall and enter.

BOOK XII.

The Trojans assault the wall in companies, attacking its gates, breastworks, battlements, and buttresses. Their attack gains ground when Sarpedon breaks a battlement and makes a breach, but the Achaeans continue to resist.

Ajax and Teucer then both of them attacked him. Teucer hit him

Hector commands the Trojans to attack the wall, break it, and set fire to the ships. The Trojans rush forward, scale the wall, and enter through the gates after Hector breaks them open.
