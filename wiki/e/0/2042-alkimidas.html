Alkimidas

Also called: athlete boy, boy, thee

Abstract written by a model

Alkimidas is a boys’ wrestling winner at Nemea, trained by Melesias and belonging to the Bassid clan. Descended from Praxidamas, he returns victorious from Nemea; his win is his house’s five-and-twentieth athletic honour and evidence of excellence across generations, though Olympic wreaths were denied by lot.

Alkimidas is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode VI

Alkimidas wins the boys' wrestling-match at Nemea and returns from the pleasant meeting-places of Nemea. His victory is presented as proof that his family, like fruitful fields, produces excellence in successive generations. The ode calls his victory the five-and-twentieth honor shown forth by his house and says that two Olympic wreaths were denied to him and Polytimidas by the fall of the lot.
