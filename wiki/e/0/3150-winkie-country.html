Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the land of the Winkies is a rough, hilly country west of the Emerald City ruled by the Wicked Witch of the West, whose inhabitants are slaves. In The Road to Oz, Country of the Winkies is a yellow place ruled by the Tin Woodman, where Dorothy and her companions meet Tik-Tok and Billina en route. In The Emerald City of Oz, the Country of the Winkies is a yellow country of Oz ruled by Nick Chopper, the Tin Woodman, under Ozma's rule. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Winkie Country is Oz's yellow country, ruled by the Tin Woodman and notable for its yellow butterflies, tin, and skilled tinsmiths. In The Lost Princess of Oz, Winkie Country is a quarter of the Land of Oz that travelers cross during the search for news of Ozma. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Winkie Country is the western region of Oz governed by the Tin Woodman as Emperor and containing his glittering tin castle. In The Royal Book of Oz, Winkie Country is a yellow region bordered by the Deadly Desert that Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion cross. In Kabumpo in Oz, Winkie Country occupies the West of Oz, contains the Kingdom of Sun Top Mountain, and is where Ozma directs the Runaway Country to set down Pompadore's party.

In The Yellow Knight of Oz, Winkie Country is the yellow domain east of the Emerald City ruled by the Tin Woodman, where Sir Hokus investigates his former history.

Winkie Country in The Lost Princess of Oz

Abstract written by a model

Winkie Country is a country forming a quarter of the Land of Oz. It has settled, prosperous eastern and western regions, with rough, little-known territory and branches of the Winkie River between them. Its west reaches the Deadly Desert and includes tin mines and grain farms. During the search for news of Ozma, travelers cross the Winkie River near the vacant Scarecrow’s Tower, enter the Rolling Prairie, and later head east across part of the country toward the river. It also contains the City of Thi.
