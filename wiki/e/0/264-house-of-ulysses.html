the house of Ulysses in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The house of Ulysses is a palace with outer and inner courts, a banqueting-cloister, threshold, gatehouse, Penelope’s room, women’s apartments, and a store-room containing treasures, a bow, and a quiver. During Ulysses’s absence, the suitors occupy it as a household and banquet residence, consuming its livestock, grain, wine, and estate and dishonouring it. Ulysses, disguised as a beggar, moves through the house; its doorway and court host the beggars’ quarrel and fight. Penelope brings out the bow for a contest to determine who will marry her and leave with her. As conflict nears, doors and gates are secured. After Ulysses kills the suitors, their corpses are piled in the gatehouse, and fire and sulphur purify the house before he prepares to leave temporarily while Penelope remains inside.

house of Ulysses in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Ulysses’ house is a large, fortified, well-built household with successive buildings, battlemented walls, strong double doors, cloisters, stores, arms, servants, and property. During Ulysses’ absence, the suitors occupy it, pursue Penelope, feast and make music there, consume its provisions without payment, mistreat its household and guests, and disgrace and waste the estate. Telemachus repeatedly asserts that it is his own house rather than a public house, prepares to leave secretly, and warns the suitors of punishment. Minerva visits it and makes the suitors drowsy; Penelope prays there for Telemachus. As Ulysses returns disguised as a beggar, he sees its scale and its corruption. He and Telemachus plan to remove and hide its armour, confront the suitors there, and kill them. In the end, the suitors’ bodies remain in the house uncared for, while their friends still do not know what happened.

BOOK XXIV

The house of Ulysses is where the disguised Ulysses and Telemachus hide the armour and where the suitors are killed. Afterward, the suitors’ bodies remain there uncared for because their friends do not yet know what happened.
