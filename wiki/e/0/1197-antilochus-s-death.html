Antilochus's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Antilochus is slain by Memnon, his fall, that dear corpse, the dead, the death of Antilochus

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the death of Antilochus is the event in which Memnon kills Antilochus, after which the Achaeans bury him. In The Fall of Troy, Antilochus's death is the fatal battle in which Memnon pierces him above the breast and through the heart, causing grief for the Danaans and Nestor.
