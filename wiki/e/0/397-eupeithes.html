Eupeithes in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Eupeithes, father of Antinous, grieves his son's death, urges Ithacans to avenge the suitors, leads their armed attack on Ulysses, and is killed by Laertes in the Ithacan civil conflict.

Eupeithes, grieving over the death of his son Antinous, urges the Ithacans to avenge the suitors. He leads the armed opposition against Ulysses but is killed when Laertes's spear pierces his helmet.
