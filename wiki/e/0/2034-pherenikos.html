Pherenikos

Also called: the horse that won this race

Abstract written by a model

Pherenikos is Hieron of Syracuse’s winning horse. Running ungoaded beside the Alpheos, he wins the horse-race at Pisa and Olympia; he also wins the Pythian contest at Kirrha. His victories bring glory to Hieron and supply the splendour of his Pythian crown.

Pherenikos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

OLYMPIAN ODES. / I.

Pherenikos wins the horse-race at Pisa by running ungoaded along the banks of the Alpheos. His victory brings glory to Hieron, the Syracusans’ horse-loving king.

III.

Pherenikos is Hieron's horse, and he won the victory at Kirrha in the Pythian contest. His victory supplies the subject and splendour of Hieron's Pythian crown.
