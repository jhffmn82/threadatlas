Aristagoras of Tenedos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Aristagoras of Tenedos, son of Arkesilas and member of a famous clan, is newly elected Prytanis and presides over the Senate of Tenedos. Welcomed to Hestia's sanctuary, he is prayed to finish his twelve-month term with fair fame and an unvexed heart. Praised for a splendid body, inherited dauntless heart, and sixteen wrestling and pankration victories, he missed the Pythian and Olympic Games through parental caution despite expected honor.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode XI

Aristagoras is welcomed to Hestia's sanctuary on his election as Prytanis and is prayed to complete his twelve-month term with fair fame and an unvexed heart. He is praised for his splendid body, dauntless heart, noble ancestry, and sixteen victories in wrestling and the pankration among neighboring peoples. The ode says that excessive parental caution kept him from competing at the Pythian or Olympic contests, though the poet believes he would have won great honor there.
