Ergoteles in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Ergoteles, Philanor’s son, left Knosos because of civil dissension and became a naturalized citizen of Himera. A long foot-race winner, he won at Olympia, twice at Pytho, and at Isthmos, bringing glory to Himera.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII.

Ergoteles was a native of Knosos who was driven from his country by civil dissension and became a citizen of Himera. He won the long foot-race at Olympia, twice at Pytho, and at Isthmos, gaining glory for the land that had become his own.
