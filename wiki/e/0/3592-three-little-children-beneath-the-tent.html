12. The First Christmas Tree

The three little children live beneath a rude tent of skins on a broad treeless plain, and their parents sadly neglect them. Claus plants a pine tree near their tent, decorates it with candles, toys, and candies, and gives them their first great pleasure. They dance and laugh around the sparkling tree, while their parents begin to show them more respect and consideration.
