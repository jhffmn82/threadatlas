Aristokleides in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Aristokleides, son of Aristophanes of Aigina, wins the pankration at Nemea, bringing renown to Aigina through strength and fair deeds and earning poetic praise.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode III

Aristokleides, son of Aristophanes, won a pankration victory at Nemea. Through his strength and fair deeds, he brought new renown to Aigina and earned praise from the poet.
