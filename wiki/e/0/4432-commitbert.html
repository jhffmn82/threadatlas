CommitBERT in RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation

Ensheng Shi, Yanlin Wang, Wei Tao, Lun Du, Hongyu Zhang, Shi Han, Dongmei Zhang, and Hongbin Sun · 2022

CommitBERT is a pre-trained Seq2Seq model and hybrid baseline for commit message generation. It uses CodeBERT to learn semantic representations of code diffs and a Transformer-based decoder to generate messages. Fine-tuned on MCMD, it is evaluated as a pre-trained baseline and adapted to RACE, where it improves by 11% BLEU, 9% Meteor, 11% Rouge-L, and 12% Cider, though RACE scores higher on informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness.
