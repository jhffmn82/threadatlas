Mombi’s capture in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Mombi’s capture begins when she becomes a rose and the Tin Woodman unknowingly carries her from the Emerald City to Glinda’s camp. Glinda captures her in the desert with a golden magical thread, returns her by Gump, and questions her about Ozma.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Tin-Woodman Plucks a Rose

Mombi is not formally surrendered by Queen Jinjur, but her transformation into a rose causes her to be captured accidentally. The Tin Woodman carries the rose containing her out of the Emerald City and into Glinda’s camp.

Princess Ozma of Oz

Glinda captures Mombi in the desert with a golden magical thread around her neck. Mombi is taken safely back to camp in the Gump, where Glinda questions her about Ozma.
