ITER-RETGEN in Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy

Zhihong Shao, Yeyun Gong, Yelong Shen, Minlie Huang, Nan Duan, Weizhu Chen · 2023 · the work’s page · read it where ITER-RETGEN first appears

Abstract written by a model

ITER-RETGEN is a method and iterative retrieval-generation synergy for retrieval-augmented large language models. It addresses questions with complex information needs and supports multi-hop question answering, fact verification, and commonsense reasoning. Beginning with a question, it generates an output with an LLM; in later iterations, it uses the question and the complete previous output to retrieve five paragraphs of more relevant knowledge, then generates a new output using the retrieved knowledge as a whole. This retrieval-generation cycle repeats for T iterations, and the last output is the final response. It can produce chain-of-reasoning followed by a final answer under a few-shot setting, while avoiding retrieval interruptions during generation and preserving generation flexibility. Experiments use text-davinci-003 as the backend LLM. Two iterations provide the most performance gains, often requiring two API calls and ten retrieved paragraphs. ITER-RETGEN achieves up to 8.6% absolute gains over previous retrieval-augmented methods on four of six datasets, outperforms DSP and Self-Ask on question answering tasks, and is better than or competitive with retrieval-augmented baselines with fewer retrieval and generation overheads. Its main risk is that incorrect first-iteration reasoning can mislead later retrieval. It may be less suitable for long-form generation requiring finer-grained retrieval, but is presented as a simple, strong baseline for future research.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

ITER-RETGEN synergizes retrieval and generation iteratively: a model’s response indicates what may be needed to complete a task, guiding retrieval of additional knowledge for a better response in a later iteration. It processes all retrieved knowledge as a whole, preserves flexibility in generation, and performs better than or competitively with retrieval-augmented baselines with fewer retrieval and generation overheads.

1

ITER-RETGEN iterates retrieval-augmented generation and generation-augmented retrieval. It uses generated responses to retrieve more relevant knowledge, then uses the newly retrieved knowledge in another generation step; it achieves up to 8.6% absolute gains over previous retrieval-augmented methods on four of six datasets.

2

ITER-RETGEN iterates retrieval and generation, using the complete generation from the previous iteration as specific context for retrieving more relevant knowledge in the next iteration. It avoids interrupting generation with retrieval, processes retrieved knowledge as a whole during generation, and is described as conceptually simple and empirically strong for multi-hop question answering, fact verification, and commonsense reasoning.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.4

ITER-RETGEN repeats retrieval-generation for T iterations. It uses the previous iteration's generation and the question q to retrieve paragraphs, then produces the next output with an LLM.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.7

ITER-RETGEN repeatedly performs retrieval-augmented chain-of-thought generation, using the question and previous model output to guide retrieval in later iterations. Its second iteration gives the largest typical improvement, and it often surpasses retrieval-augmented baselines with only two API calls and ten retrieved paragraphs. Its errors commonly result from incorrect first-iteration reasoning that misleads subsequent retrieval.

6

ITER-RETGEN is effective for answering questions with complex information needs. Despite its simple workflow, it outperforms retrieval-augmented methods with more complex workflows and could serve as a strong baseline for future research. Generation-augmented retrieval adaptation can further improve its performance while reducing overheads.

Limitations

ITER-RETGEN enhances retrieval-augmented large language models by synergizing retrieval and generation iteratively. It demonstrates strong performance compared with more structured prompting techniques such as Self-Ask, but may be less suitable for long-form generation that benefits from more fine-grained retrieval.
