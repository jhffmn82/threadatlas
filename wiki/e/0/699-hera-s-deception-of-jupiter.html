Hera’s deception of Jupiter

Abstract written by a model

Juno deceives Jupiter by claiming that she is going to visit Oceanus and Tethys, obtaining Venus’s girdle, and arranging for Sleep to assist her. She then seduces Jupiter on Mount Ida, where Sleep causes him to fall into slumber.
