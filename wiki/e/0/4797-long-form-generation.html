long-form generation in Active Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zhengbao Jiang · 2023

Long-form generation handles complex information needs, including long-form question answering, open-domain summarization, and chain-of-thought reasoning. It requires gathering multiple pieces of knowledge and active information access. In active retrieval augmented generation, retrieval is triggered and interleaved with continued answer generation until the answer ends; FLARE focuses on this setting.

Introduction

Long-form generation involves complex information needs that are not always evident from the input alone. It may require gathering multiple pieces of knowledge throughout generation, as in long-form question answering, open-domain summarization, and chain-of-thought reasoning.

The generation process in active retrieval augmented generation interleaves retrieval with continued answer generation. Retrieval is triggered during generation until the answer reaches its end.
