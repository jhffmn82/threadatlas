Mean Reciprocal Rank

metric · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: MRR, MRR@10, Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
  • In Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation, Mean Reciprocal Rank is proposed as a language-sensitive metric for evaluating model capabilities with language-specific nuances and complexities.
  • In Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, MRR@10 is the mean reciprocal rank of the first correct passage, truncated at rank 10; the combined system reaches 0.635, compared with 0.586 reported for the strongest configurations in the original MultiHop-RAG paper.
