payment for sexual favors in The Amores, or Amours

Payment for sexual intercourse is sought only by women; Elegy X condemns it as prostitution, bribery, and bought testimony, ending a man's debt for acquiescence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY X.

The text condemns making sexual favors a matter of payment. It compares this practice with prostitution, bribery, and purchased testimony, and says that once a man has paid, he no longer remains a debtor for the woman's acquiescence.
