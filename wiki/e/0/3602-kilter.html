Kilter in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Kilter is a silent, swift man and one of Santa Claus’s four little helpers. Before Christmas he gathers children’s letters, passing through houses and walls, then joins Santa Claus’s journey to distribute toys.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3. The Deputies of Santa Claus

Kilter gathers the notes and letters that children write to Santa Claus. He explains that he can pass through walls, and he joins Santa Claus on the Christmas journey to help distribute toys.
