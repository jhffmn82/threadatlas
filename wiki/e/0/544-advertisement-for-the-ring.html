CHAPTER V.

The advertisement appears in the evening paper’s “Found” column under Dr. Watson’s name, announcing that a plain gold wedding ring was found in Brixton Road and could be claimed at 221B Baker Street. It attracts Mrs. Sawyer, whom Sherlock Holmes believes is an accomplice in disguise.
