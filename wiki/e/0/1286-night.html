Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE THEOGONY

Night arose from Chaos and gave birth to Aether and Day through union with Erebus. Without lying with anyone, she also bore Doom, Fate, Death, Sleep, Dreams, Blame, Woe, the Hesperides, the Destinies, Nemesis, Deceit, Friendship, Age, and Strife.
