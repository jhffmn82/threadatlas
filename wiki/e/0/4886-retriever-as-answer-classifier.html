Retriever as Answer Classifier

method · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: LRAC, RAC, RAC objective, Retriever as Answer Classifier (RAC), ours (RAC)

Abstract written by a model

Retriever as Answer Classifier is a proposed answer-encoding method and knowledge-constrained objective for retrieval-augmented generation. It trains a retriever to classify whether its top-n retrieved passages contain the knowledge in an answer, treating both example passages as positive. Training uses in-batch negative answers from the rest of the training set and binary cross-entropy loss. It is one of two knowledge-constrained objectives intended to guide retrieval toward knowledge-relevant passages. Evaluated against RAG and DPR, it reports 54.1 BLEU-1, 33.8 BLEU-4, and 59.0 ROUGE-L on MSMARCO; 11.2 R@1 on MSMARCO development; and 44.95 R-Precision on KILT-WoW development. In a Gouda cheese example, it retrieves the same Wikipedia page as DKS and gives a perfect knowledge match.

Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding / Abstract

Retriever as Answer Classifier is one of the proposed methods for incorporating rich answer encoding into retrieval-augmented generation.

Introduction

Retriever as Answer Classifier is one of the proposed objectives. It uses answer encoding to determine whether a retrieved passage contains the knowledge in the answer, making both example passages positive.

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

Retriever as Answer Classifier is an objective that uses rich answer encoding to train the retriever to determine whether the top-n retrieved passages contain the knowledge inside an answer. It uses in-batch negative answers and a binary cross-entropy training signal.

Experimental Setup / Datasets .. Comparison Models

It is one of the proposed-method variants evaluated against RAG and DPR. It obtains 54.1 BLEU-1, 33.8 BLEU-4, and 59.0 ROUGE-L on MSMARCO, and achieves the highest R-Precision, Recall@5, and end-to-end ROUGE-L among the listed KILT-WoW methods.

Results / RQ1: Effectiveness / Generative QA .. Case Studies

RAC is one of the proposed model variants. It improves over RAG in the reported evaluations and, in the Gouda cheese example, retrieves the same Wikipedia page as DKS and produces a response with a perfect knowledge match.

Conclusion

RAC is one of the two knowledge-constrained objectives included in the training framework to guide the retriever toward knowledge-relevant passages.
