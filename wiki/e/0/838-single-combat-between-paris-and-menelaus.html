single combat between Paris and Menelaus in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

A combat for Helen and her wealth is arranged, with peace promised to the loser; Menelaus gains advantage over Alexandrus until Venus rescues him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III.

The combat is arranged as a contest for Helen and her wealth, with the victor taking her and the defeated side accepting peace. Menelaus gains the advantage after Alexandrus's spear fails, piercing Alexandrus's defenses and dragging him away before Venus rescues him.
