Hymn to Pan in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

is_a song [a song seeking Pan’s favour] The Pan hymn praises Pan’s appearance, roaming, hunting, music, dancing, and parentage. It recounts his birth to Hermes and the daughter of Dryops, his reception among the immortals, and the reason for his name. It closes with a greeting to Pan and a promise to remember him in another song.
