Nausicaa's maids in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Nausicaa’s household servants accompany her to wash and fold clothes and play ball, flee Ulysses, then return at her command with aid.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VI

Nausicaa’s maids accompany her to the washing cisterns, help wash and fold the clothes, and play ball after dinner. They initially run from Ulysses, but return when Nausicaa tells them to assist him, bringing him clothes, oil, food, and drink.
