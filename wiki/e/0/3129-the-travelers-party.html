the travelers' party in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The travelers' party consists of Dorothy, Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, and Lion. It crosses the china wall, bogs, marshes, rivers, fields, and forests while traveling toward the Emerald City and later the Castle of Glinda. Carried downstream from the road of yellow brick, it reaches a bank with the Cowardly Lion's help, rescues Scarecrow, and enters a poppy field. Lost in the fields, it is carried to the Emerald City's gate by Winged Monkeys. It later meets animals, crosses the Hammer-Heads' hill, reaches the Quadlings, and continues to Glinda's castle.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

The travelers' party crosses the river, is carried downstream away from the road of yellow brick, and reaches the bank with the help of the Cowardly Lion. After the Scarecrow is rescued, the party follows the riverbank and enters the poppy field.

Chapter XIV

The four travelers try to walk east through the great fields toward the Emerald City but become lost. After Dorothy summons the Winged Monkeys, they are carried safely to the city's gate.

Chapter XXI

The travelers cross bogs, marshes, tall grass, underbrush, and a forest before stopping to sleep. They continue along a well-trodden path and meet hundreds of animals gathered in a forest opening.

Chapter XXII

The four travelers cross the forest, are carried over the Hammer-Heads’ hill by the Winged Monkeys, and arrive safely in the country of the Quadlings. They eat at a farmhouse and continue walking to the Castle of Glinda.
