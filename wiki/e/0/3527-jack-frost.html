Jack Frost in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Jack Frost is a jolly sprite, son and agent of the Frost King, who knows Claus from the Forest and nips noses, ears, and toes in winter. Visiting Claus at night, he boasts of nipping many people but promises to spare children if he can resist temptation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

2. How Claus Made the First Toy

Jack Frost visits Claus at night and boasts that he will nip many noses, ears, and toes. After Claus asks him to spare the children because they are tender and helpless, Jack promises not to pinch a child that night if he can resist the temptation.
