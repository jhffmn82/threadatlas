active retrieval

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: active retrieval strategy

Abstract written by a model

Active retrieval is a method that retrieves information when low-probability tokens signal a language model may lack knowledge, using a temporary next sentence as a query. It triggers retrieval through a confidence threshold, balancing help against noise from unnecessary retrieval. Across tasks, performance is generally good when retrieval is activated for roughly 40%–80% of sentences, with plateaus or declines beyond some rates.

Introduction

Active retrieval retrieves information only when a language model generates low-probability tokens, based on the hypothesis that low confidence often indicates a lack of knowledge. It also considers what the model intends to generate in the future by using a temporary next sentence as a retrieval query.

6 .. 6.2

Active retrieval selects when to retrieve using a confidence threshold. On 2WikiMultihopQA, performance plateaus when retrieval is used for more than 60% of steps, while on StrategyQA performance declines above 50%; using retrieval for roughly 40% to 80% of sentences usually performs well.
