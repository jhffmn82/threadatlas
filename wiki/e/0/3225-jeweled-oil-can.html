jeweled oil-can

object · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a silver oil-can, inlaid with gold and set with precious jewels, an oil-can, jeweled oil-can (Tin Woodman's oil-can), my oil-can, oil-can, the jeweled silver oil-can, the oil-can, the oil-can from Dorothy’s basket
  • In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, it is kept in the Tin Woodman's cottage and used to free his rusted joints, then placed in Dorothy's basket for future use.
  • In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, it is retrieved from a boat and used by the Scarecrow to oil each of the Tin Woodman's rusted joints.
  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, it is carried by the Tin Woodman and used to release the Tin Soldier from rust.
