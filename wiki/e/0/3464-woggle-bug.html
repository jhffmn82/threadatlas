Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, Woggle-Bug travels with the Scarecrow’s company, helps repair and animate their companions, and is later identified as a Public Educator in Princess Ozma’s government. In The Woggle-Bug Book, Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., pursues Grenadine through a series of comic mishaps and ultimately keeps a Wagnerian-plaid necktie as her token. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, the Woggle-Bug leads Royal College students in procession and serves as Public Accuser in Eureka’s trial. In The Emerald City of Oz, Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E., heads the Royal College and directs the Royal Athletic College, welcoming Dorothy and promoting School Pills. In The Magic of Oz, Professor H. M. Wogglebug, T.E., serves as Principal of the Royal Athletic College and invents the Tablets of Learning and Square-Meal Tablet. In Glinda of Oz, Professor Wogglebug heads the Royal Athletic College, proposes lifting an island, and agrees to travel to the Skeezer country. In The Royal Book of Oz, Professor Wogglebug proposes and begins writing the Royal Book of Oz by investigating the ancestry of Ozma’s guests.

Woggle-Bug in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06 · the work’s page · read it where Woggle-Bug first appears

Abstract written by a model

Woggle-Bug is a highly magnified, thoroughly educated insect, also called Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.; H. M. means Highly Magnified and T. E. Thoroughly Educated. He has a wide, round buglike body, slender limbs, and formal clothing including a dark-blue swallowtail coat, white vest, plush knickerbockers, and a tall silk hat. In his natural state he was an ordinary, unclothed insect that walked with his arms and legs and fed on smaller insects. He lived for about three years in a school-house hearth, learned by listening to Professor Nowitall, and gained marvelous knowledge. The Professor projected him onto a screen in a highly magnified state; he escaped the school-house while enlarged and received his stylish costume from a grateful tailor. Proudly presenting a card bearing his name and titles, he declares himself the only Highly Magnified and Thoroughly Educated Woggle-Bug in the world. He values education, culture, and puns, and plans to lecture in the Emerald City. He travels with the Scarecrow’s company, proposes a repair for the Saw-Horse, worries during talk of turning companions into food, and helps Nick Chopper give Jack Pumpkinhead a new leg. He carries a Gump’s head upstairs, urges that it receive magic powder, and admires the completed Thing. During the flight he boasts and puns, becomes airsick, finds a diamond necklace and two gold bracelets in a Jackdaws’ nest, and hides under straw because Jackdaws are enemies of his race. He helps count seventeen by twos, swallows a wishing pill that mends the Gump’s wings, and offers to swallow another to redirect it. At Glinda’s palace he questions the Wizard, withdraws when others defend him, and reacts strongly to the idea that Mombi concealed Ozma. He is later identified as a Public Educator of Princess Ozma’s government.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., approaches the travelers and presents a card bearing his name. He explains that H. M. means Highly Magnified and T. E. means Thoroughly Educated; he is a large, formally dressed insect who offers to relate his history.

A Highly Magnified History

The Woggle-Bug explains that he was once an ordinary insect but became thoroughly educated by listening to Professor Nowitall’s lessons for years. After being projected in a highly magnified state, he escaped the school house and later acquired clothing from a grateful tailor. He joins the company traveling toward the Emerald City and suggests how to repair the Saw-Horse.

The Scarecrow Takes Time to Think

The Woggle-Bug argues that the Scarecrow was King before Jinjur arrived and fears being turned into goulash or soup. He becomes distressed by the others’ discussion of eating the group’s members and helps Nick Chopper set Jack Pumpkinhead on his new leg.

The Astonishing Flight of the Gump

The Woggle-Bug brings a Gump’s head with antlers to the roof after carrying it up the stairs. He insists that the head must receive magic powder and admires the completed Thing.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The Woggle-Bug boasts of being Highly Magnified and Thoroughly Educated and makes several puns during the flight. He becomes airsick, finds a diamond necklace in the nest, and is hidden under straw because Jackdaws are said to be enemies of his race.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

The Woggle-Bug helps the group understand how to count seventeen by twos and swallows a wishing pill without pain. It wishes for the Gump’s broken wings to be mended, and the wish is immediately granted.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

The Woggle-Bug finds two gold bracelets in the Jackdaws' nest and is willing to swallow another Wishing Pill to redirect the Gump. At Glinda's palace, he questions whether the Wizard of Oz was merely a humbug but retreats when the others defend the Wizard. He reacts impressively to the suspicion that Mombi helped hide Ozma.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E. in The Woggle-Bug Book

L. Frank Baum · 1905 · the work’s page · read it where Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E. first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., is a highly magnified, thoroughly educated woggle-bug from the Land of Oz, about the size of a man, with four hands, concealed wings, brilliant multicolored clothing, and a proud strut. He claims descent from original protoplasm through his father. In America, he begins without money, works as a ditch-digger for four dollars a day, and gains eight silver dollars. His central pursuit is courtship of Grenadine: he mistakes a Wagnerian gown and its altered pieces, and repeatedly pursues their wearers. His efforts bring mishaps, including torn cloth, balloon and winged escapes, deception of Arabs, escape from King Weasel’s jungle, and rejection by a Swedish widow using insect Exterminator. He smashes a hat he considers unlucky, then returns to his quiet room convinced that a Wagnerian-plaid necktie is Grenadine, keeping it as a token and resolving to be content.

Professor Wogglebug is a bug, Prince of Bugs, educator, and self-appointed Great, Grand Genealogist of Oz.
