the dream of the eagle and geese

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: a dream that I have had, my dream

Abstract written by a model

foretells the death of the suitors [according to Ulysses’s interpretation] Penelope dreams that an eagle kills her twenty geese and later speaks to her, declaring that the geese are the suitors and that the eagle is Ulysses returning to destroy them. Ulysses gives this interpretation and says the suitors’ deaths are foretold, but Penelope considers dreams uncertain and doubts that hers came through the gate of horn.
