Abstract written by a model

Multilingual Semantic embedding models, released in recent years to address English-centered semantic models, handle multiple languages using language-specific information and a shared subword vocabulary. Ten models capable of embedding Arabic are examined and evaluated for Arabic retrieval on ARCD, compared with Recall@k across retrieval-hit levels; cross-lingual embeddings support knowledge transfer.
