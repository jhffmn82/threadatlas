GEN pretraining in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

GEN pretraining trains on top-K BLEU-1-selected references with cross-entropy against gold reviews; replacing them with REFLECT-retrieved references significantly decreases performance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

GEN .. Training GEN

GEN pretraining uses references selected by their top-K BLEU-1 scores as inputs and cross-entropy on gold reviews as the loss function. Using references retrieved by REFLECT instead leads to a significant performance decrease.
