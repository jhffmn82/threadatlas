Patroklos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Patroklos, son of Menoitios, shows courage beside Achilles when Telephos routs the Danaoi; afterward Achilles persuades him to remain near him in battle.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Patroklos went with the Atreidai to Teuthras and stood beside Achilles when Telephos routed the Danaoi. His courage was thereby shown, and Achilles later persuaded him not to stand far from his conquering spear in battle.
