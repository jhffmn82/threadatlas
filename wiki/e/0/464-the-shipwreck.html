the shipwreck

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: shipwreck, the men all fell into the sea, the ship went round and round, their destruction

Abstract written by a model

The shipwreck is the destruction of ships by Jove’s storms after departures: one vessel is snapped apart and burned after leaving the sun-god’s island, and another is struck by thunderbolts while bound for Libya. In both accounts, every crewman dies in the sea, while Ulysses alone survives by holding to the mast, or mast and keel.
