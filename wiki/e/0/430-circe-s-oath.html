Circe's oath

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Circe's oath is a solemn promise, required by Ulysses before he will go to bed with her, that she will plot no further harm; after she swears it, he trusts her, remains, and demands his men be restored.

Circe's oath is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
