Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, Dolon is a Trojan scout who volunteers to spy on the Achaean ships, reveals Trojan plans after being captured, and is killed by Diomed. In The Rhesus of Euripides, Dolon is a Trojan scout who volunteers to infiltrate the Greek camp in disguise, but is killed by Odysseus and Diomedes during the Argive night raid. In Hecuba and other plays, Dolon is a Trojan spy who volunteers for nocturnal reconnaissance of the Greek fleet in wolf disguise and later remains absent, feared to have fallen into an ambush.

Dolon in The Iliad

Homer · 749 to 724 BC · the work’s page · read it where Dolon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dolon is Eumedes’ only son and the brother of five sisters, an ill-favoured but swift Trojan scout rich in gold and bronze. Wearing a wolf skin, ferret-skin cap, bow, and carrying a pointed javelin, he volunteers to spy on the Achaean ships at night after Hector promises him horses and a chariot. Pursued and captured by Ulysses and Diomed, he reveals Trojan plans, camp locations, guards, and details of Rhesus and his horses and chariot. Though he asks to be spared or bound, Diomed kills him.

Dolon in The Rhesus of Euripides

Euripides · 454 to 439 BC · the work’s page · read it where Dolon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dolon is a Trojan scout whose name is associated with craft. He volunteers to infiltrate the Greek camp and report its plans to Hector, seeking Achilles' horses as reward. Disguised in a complete wolf-skin, he sets out before dawn toward the Greek ships, but during the Argive night raid Odysseus and Diomedes kill him and carry off his wolf-skin.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

RHESUS

Dolon volunteers to infiltrate the Greek camp and report its plans to Hector. He asks for Achilles' horses as his reward and prepares to approach the Greek ships disguised as a wolf, intending to return before dawn with proof of his success.

NOTES

Dolon volunteers to spy on the Greek camp and is associated with craft through his name. In the play he uses a complete wolf-skin disguise and is killed by Odysseus and Diomedes, who carry away his wolf-skin as a token.

Dolon in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · about 429 BC · the work’s page · read it where Dolon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Dolon is a Trojan spy serving Hector and Troy. He volunteers for a nocturnal reconnaissance of the Greek fleet, wearing a wolf disguise for covert movement and seeking Achilles’ steeds as reward. He boasts of returning before sunrise, perhaps with Ulysses’ or Diomede’s head, but later remains absent, prompting fear that he has fallen into an ambush.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

RHESUS.

Dolon volunteers to spy on the Greek fleet and agrees to undertake the mission in return for Achilles’ steeds. He boasts that he will return before sunrise, possibly after killing Ulysses or Diomede and bringing back one of their heads.

RHESUS, HECTOR, CHORUS.

Dolon has been sent by Hector to explore the Greek fleet. He has not yet returned as the Chorus expects, and his absence causes concern that he may have fallen into an ambush.
