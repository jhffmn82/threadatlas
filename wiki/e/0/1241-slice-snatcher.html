THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

Slice-snatcher is a powerful Mouse and the son of Gnawer, who is the son of Bread-stealer. He arms himself with split chestnut husks and threatens to destroy the Frogs, driving them toward the lake until Zeus intervenes.
