the affair between the speaker and Cypassis

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Cypassis and the speaker's affair, affair, coming together, concubitus, intrigue, our delightful intrigue, our stealthy amours, our stolen caresses, stolen delight, stolen love, the sin, your escapade

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the affair is a secret sexual relationship between Cypassis and the speaker; although he denies it to Corinna, he later refers openly to their previous encounters and uses knowledge of them to pressure Cypassis into another meeting. In The Amores, or Amours, the affair is a delightful and clandestine intrigue consisting of stolen, secret caresses between the speaker and Cypassis, which Corinna discovers; the speaker threatens to disclose where, how often, and in how many ways they have met.
