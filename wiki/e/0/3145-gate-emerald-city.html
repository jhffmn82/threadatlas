gate (Emerald City) in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Emerald City gate, at the end of the road of yellow brick, is studded with glittering emeralds. A bell opens it for Dorothy and her companions, then the Guardian opens another gate into the city streets.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The Emerald City gate stands at the end of the road of yellow brick and is studded with glittering emeralds. A bell opens it, allowing Dorothy and her companions to enter a high arched room, and the Guardian later opens another gate leading into the city streets.
