1 section; each heading opens the text

(ll. 383-404) When the Pleiades, daughters of Atlas, are rising 1310,

Winter brings frost, cold winds, snow, rain, and difficult conditions for sheep and men. People should prepare clothing, footwear, shelter, barns, and fodder beforehand, work before the dark clouds arrive, and give men more food while giving oxen half their usual food.
