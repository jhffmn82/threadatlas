consulting detective work

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Holmes’s consulting-detective profession, a trade of my own, detection, our profession, the detection of crime, the higher developments of his art, the scientific detective

Abstract written by a model

In A Study in Scarlet, consulting detective work is identified as Sherlock Holmes’s occupation, in which he studies evidence, applies his knowledge of crime, and directs detectives toward the correct solution. In The Sign of the Four, Holmes’s consulting-detective profession is presented as a specialized profession for examining difficult cases referred by official detectives and providing specialist opinions.
