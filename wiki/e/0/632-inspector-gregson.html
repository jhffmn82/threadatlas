Inspector Gregson in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Inspector Gregson is a Scotland Yard inspector and detective, described as quick, energetic, conventional, and among the smartest Yarders. He begins the Lauriston Gardens investigation by writing to Sherlock Holmes, preserving the room but allowing footprints outside to be disturbed, and reporting Drebber’s possessions and inquiries about Stangerson. Using Drebber’s hat, he traces him to Madame Charpentier’s establishment, arrests Arthur Charpentier, and wrongly concludes Arthur killed Drebber. After Lestrade’s report disproves that theory, Gregson asks Holmes to identify the murderer and helps subdue Jefferson Hope during his escape attempt. Although the newspaper credits Gregson and Lestrade with Hope’s capture, Holmes says this credit is incorrect.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Tobias Gregson writes to Sherlock Holmes about the body found at 3, Lauriston Gardens and meets Holmes and the Doctor at the house. He has preserved the interior but allowed the pathway to become crowded with footprints, and he reports the objects found in Enoch J. Drebber’s pockets and the inquiries sent about Joseph Stangerson.

CHAPTER VI.

Tobias Gregson traces Enoch J. Drebber through a hat made by John Underwood and Sons to Madame Charpentier’s boarding establishment. After questioning Madame Charpentier, he arrests her son Arthur Charpentier and theorizes that Arthur followed Drebber to Brixton Road and killed him with a heavy stick.

CHAPTER VII.

Inspector Gregson hears Lestrade’s report and acknowledges that his own earlier theory was wrong. He presses Sherlock Holmes to name the murderer and helps overpower Jefferson Hope when Hope attempts to escape.

CHAPTER VII.

Inspector Gregson failed to inquire in his telegram to Cleveland about Enoch Drebber’s former career. The Echo nevertheless credits Gregson and Inspector Lestrade entirely with Hope’s capture and suggests that they deserve a testimonial.

Inspector Gregson in His Last Bow

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1908-04-17

Inspector Gregson is a Scotland Yard inspector and detective. He investigates Garcia's murder with Inspector Baynes, follows John Scott Eccles and seeks his formal statement, then pursues Giuseppe Gorgiano with Mr. Leverton. Gregson leads Gorgiano's arrest attempt, discovers him dead, and takes Emilia Lucca to the Yard for questioning.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Adventure of Wisteria Lodge / 1. The Singular Experience of Mr. John Scott Eccles

Inspector Gregson comes to Sherlock Holmes's rooms with Inspector Baynes while following John Scott Eccles. He informs Eccles that Aloysius Garcia was murdered and requests a formal statement, warning that it may be used against him.

PART II

Gregson meets Sherlock Holmes outside the Howe Street flats while pursuing Giuseppe Gorgiano with Mr. Leverton. He takes responsibility for arresting Gorgiano, leads the entry into the flat, and later takes Emilia Lucca to the Yard for further questioning.
