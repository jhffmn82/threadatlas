L. Frank Baum · 1900-04 · the work’s page · read it where balloon first appears

Abstract written by a model

A more-than-twenty-feet-long green silk bag, coated inside with glue and filled with hot air, is tied to a clothes basket by many ropes. Oz and Dorothy make it, but its ropes break at launch, so it rises carrying Oz alone away from the Emerald City; Dorothy is glad she did not use it to return to Kansas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVII

Oz and Dorothy make a balloon from strips of light green, dark green, and emerald green silk coated with thin glue. A large clothes basket is fastened to it with ropes, but the ropes break during the launch and the balloon rises with Oz alone.

Chapter XVIII

The balloon goes up with Oz, taking him away from the Emerald City. Dorothy is glad she did not use it to try to return to Kansas.
