Abstract written by a model

E5-ML-Large is a top-performing Microsoft E5 model family model with 1024-dimensional embeddings. It consistently outperforms Multilingual Semantic embedding models, reaching 23% Recall@1 and 93% Recall@15, with the strongest retrieval performance across k values.
