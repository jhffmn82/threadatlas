Menoetius in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Menoetius, Patroclus's father and former adviser, brings him from Opoeis as a child and charges the older Patroclus to counsel and wisely guide Achilles, the better man.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thus then did they fight as it were a flaming fire. Meanwhile the

Menoetius once charged Patroclus to counsel and guide Achilles wisely, saying that Achilles was the better man but that Patroclus was older. Nestor reminds Patroclus that he has forgotten this charge.
