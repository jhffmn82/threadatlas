embassy to Achilles

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Embassy, chosen messengers, embassy, envoys, messengers, the embassy to Achilles, unsuccessful embassy
  • In The Iliad, it is a diplomatic mission consisting of Phoenix, Ajax, Ulysses, and the heralds Odius and Eurybates, sent to reconcile Achilles and present Agamemnon's appeal and proposed compensation.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is Nestor's proposed embassy sending Ulysses, Ajax, and Phoenix to Achilles with Agamemnon's apology, Briseis, and promised gifts, which Achilles rejects.
