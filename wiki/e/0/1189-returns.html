The Ionic School

The Returns, a five-book poem attributed to Agias or Hegias of Troezen, began after the Sack of Troy. It described the disputes and departures of the Greek heroes, the death of Agamemnon and vengeance of Orestes, and ended with Menelaus’s return home before the Odyssey.

THE RETURNS

The Returns, attributed to Agias of Troezen, follows the Sack of Ilium and recounts the Greeks’ journeys home. It includes the quarrel between Agamemnon and Menelaus, the storm at the Capherides, Neoptolemus’s overland journey, Agamemnon’s murder, Orestes and Pylades’ vengeance, and Menelaus’s return. It also contains accounts of Hades and its terrors, Medea’s rejuvenation of Aeson, Antiope’s betrayal of Themiscyra, and Tantalus’s punishment.
