  • In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the desert surrounds Oz, separates Dorothy from Kansas, and is proposed to be crossed by balloon.
  • In Ozma of Oz, the desert is a deadly barrier separating Ev from Oz, which Ozma's procession crosses on Glinda's magic carpet.
  • In The Road to Oz, the Deadly Desert is a vast, barren waste separating the travelers from the Land of Oz, which they cross safely in Johnny Dooit's sand-boat.
  • In The Road to Oz, the Deadly Sands turn living flesh that touches them into dust and prevent anyone from walking across the desert to Oz.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, the Deadly Desert surrounds Oz and prevents ordinary travel and invasion, so the Nomes dig a tunnel beneath it.

  • In The Hungry Tiger of Oz, the Deadly Desert is a wilderness of burning sand surrounding Oz and blocking the travelers, who finally cross it using Rash hurry-canes.
  • In The Gnome King of Oz, the Deadly Desert surrounds Oz and is dangerous enough to turn people into smoke, though Peter and Ruggedo cross it using magic-bulb elevator plants.

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

CHAPTER 7 Kaliko Falls As King of Gnomes

The Deadly Desert surrounds the whole country of Oz and is dangerous enough that a person can go up like a puff of smoke upon stepping onto its sand. Peter and Ruggedo cross it using the magic bulbs, which grow into elevator plants that carry them over the desert.
