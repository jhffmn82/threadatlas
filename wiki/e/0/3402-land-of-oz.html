L. Frank Baum · 1910 · the work’s page · read it where the Land of Oz first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Land of Oz is a mighty, beautiful, peaceful fairy country and fairyland beyond the Deadly Desert, ordinarily unreachable by crossing it. Ruled by Ozma, it has the Emerald City as its capital, a palace, happy and contented people, great riches, unusual people and places, and beautiful weather. Its country includes the Country of the Winkies, Utensia, Bunbury, Bunnybury, and Rigmarole Town. It has no recorded starvation; people may eat surplus eggs but do not eat chickens. Dorothy, Billina, and Toto are non-fairies in Oz, and Dorothy is especially beloved for the good she has done. Oz becomes the permanent, safe, and delightful home of Dorothy, Uncle Henry, and Aunt Em, who are welcomed with respect and told they belong there. The central development is a threat to this protected country. King Roquat, also called the Nome King, becomes angry and plans revenge: a secret tunnel beneath the Deadly Desert will enable an attack on Oz, whose magical defenses and surrounding desert had kept enemies away. Roquat and allies including the Nomes, Whimsies, Growleywogs, and Phanfasms intend to conquer, plunder, burn, devastate, and destroy Oz; destroy the Emerald City; enslave or seize its people and notable inhabitants; take its gold and jewels; and leave it a desert. While travelers journey through unfamiliar but beautiful parts of the country, Ozma learns through the Magic Picture that the tunnel is being dug toward her peaceful land. She seeks to save Oz without fighting. The records end as the fierce invading forces invade Oz with these destructive intentions.

Oz is a lovely, secluded fairyland and land ruled by Ozma, with Emerald City of Oz as its capital. Abstract written by a model

Oz is an enchanted kingdom, marvelous and divided into four smaller colored countries, with the Emerald City at its exact center. It includes Scare City and the royal stable, and the Quadling Country and Belfaygor's lands lie within it. Ozma is its rightful Queen and ruler, and the Emerald City holds its magic treasures. Peter returns to Oz through the yellow Winkie Country, recalling that he once saved the Emerald City from the Gnome King. The kingdom then faces Mogodore's intended conquest: he plans to capture the capital, rule Oz, and use the magic belt to destroy the country. Although he declares himself king by seizure, Wagarag says he cannot be lawful king unless he marries Ozma. Peter, Belfaygor, and Snif are captured or lost, leaving Jack Pumpkinhead to seek help for his endangered country. Oz is saved, celebrates the rescuers' return with a feast and Belfaygor and Shirley Sunshine's wedding, and ends with Ozma sending Mogodore and his followers to Baffleburg and Peter home to Philadelphia.

Abstract written by a model

Oz is a happy, informal, magical kingdom and continuing story-world, ruled by Ozma. It contains four great countries, the Emerald City as its capital, a distant glittering castle, hills and forests, and Samandra. Its inhabitants include wizards, witches, fabulous monsters, talking animals, knights, jesters, and celebrated subjects; everyone remains alive. Oz's encyclopedia and the Wizard's searchlight can provide information and locate missing people or objects anywhere in the land. The records follow travelers and events connected with Oz: the Royal Sampan heads for the Emerald City to recover a camel; Sir Hokus and Camy cross Oz while fearing they may leave it; and Speedy, Marygolden, and a talking horse arrive, with Speedy realizing the kingdom is real. Peter Pun and Sir Hokus describe its deathless magical life. Finally, Ozma and Dorothy return there from Corabia, while Speedy prefers Oz to Mars after his adventures.
