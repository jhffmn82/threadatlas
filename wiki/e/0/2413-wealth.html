wealth in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where wealth first appears

Abstract written by a model

Wealth is a human possession and source of power when made glorious by noble nature or pure virtue. Joined with good success and fair fame, it supports life’s bloom, brings honor, confidence, and crowns inherited worth. Heaven-sent prosperity shapes a race’s destiny, yet fortune may change. Arkesilas is praised for using great wealth righteously and wisely; gold is called mighty above all else.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

II.

The race's destiny is joined with heaven-sent prosperity, which brings wealth and honor and crowns inherited worth. Wealth made glorious by noble character gives a person power and inspires confidence, though fortune can later change for better or worse.

V.

Wealth is described as powerful when Fate gives it to a mortal together with pure virtue. Arkesilas possesses great wealth and is praised for using prosperity righteously and wisely.

Isthmian Ode IV

Wealth is described as containing two things that make life especially sweet: good success and the fair fame that follows it. Gold is praised as mighty above all else, and wealth is associated with the honor of Theia.

wealth and money in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879 · the work’s page · read it where wealth and money first appears

Abstract written by a model

Wealth and money enable social dominance in Mixtus's adopted world. As Mixtus grows richer, he sees money as the chief means to obtain the best things, yet still affirms mind and genius, leaving an unresolved alternation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Mixtus grows richer than he had imagined and comes to regard money as the chief means of obtaining what is best worth having. He also continues to affirm, when prompted, the supreme value of mind and genius, producing an unresolved alternation between material wealth and nonmaterial ideals.
