human mortality

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, human mortality is presented as an unavoidable condition of mortals, marked by illness, death, and changing fortunes, teaching mortals to recognize the limits of their allotted path rather than seek what belongs to the gods. In Alcestis, death and mortality is presented as an unavoidable human condition that every mortal must face, shaping Heracles's warning about death and the conflicting attitudes of Admetus and Pheres toward life.
