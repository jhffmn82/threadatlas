great Alkali Plain in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886 · the work’s page · read it where great Alkali Plain first appears

Abstract written by a model

Great Alkali Plain is a vast, barren, inhospitable desert between the Sierra Nevada and Nebraska, from the Yellowstone River to the Colorado. Snow-capped mountains and gloomy valleys lie within it, but it has no inhabitants. Saline dust, little water, bones, and a marked caravan route make it a deadly barrier to westward travel.

PART II. / CHAPTER I.

The great Alkali Plain is portrayed as a vast, barren desert between the Sierra Nevada and Nebraska, extending from the Yellowstone River to the Colorado. Its saline dust, lack of water, and scattered bones make it a deadly barrier to westward travel.
