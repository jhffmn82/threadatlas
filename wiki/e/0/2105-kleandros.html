Kleandros in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Kleandros is a pankration competitor and victor, son of Telesarchos. He wins the pankration at the Isthmian Games, shows strength at the Nemean Games, and is honored at the games of Alkathoos and Epidauros. His toils bring a triumph song and a tender myrtle wreath before Telesarchos's house.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode VII

Kleandros of Aigina won the pankration at the Isthmian Games and showed his strength at the games of Nemea. The ode calls on young men to sing before the house of Telesarchos and to crown Kleandros with tender myrtle for his victory. He had also been successful at the games associated with Alkathoos and at the games of Epidauros.
