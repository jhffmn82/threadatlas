retrieval-augmented models in REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024-08-11/2024-08-16

Retrieval-augmented models are an effective approach to open-domain question answering, using a retriever-reader pipeline: a retriever obtains relevant Wikipedia information and a reader extracts or generates answers. Their reader-focused performance improves through dense retrieval, hard negatives, knowledge distillation, reranking, large-language-model supervision, stronger generative readers, and joint optimization. REANO is evaluated to improve retrieval-augmented readers.
