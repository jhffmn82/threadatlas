Claus's departure ends his sweet, wild life in the Forest. Turning away instead of formally saying goodbye, he leaves the Forest people, passes through Burzee, and goes to the world of men for his mission.

1 section; each heading opens the text

7. Claus Leaves the Forest

Claus says farewell to the Forest people by turning away rather than speaking a formal goodbye, because his wild Forest life is over. He then passes through Burzee and goes out toward the world of men.
