axe in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The axe, enchanted by the Wicked Witch of the East, repeatedly cuts the Tin Woodman. After his rescue, he carries its gold-handled, polished blade and uses it to clear branches blocking the road.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

The axe once enchanted by the Wicked Witch of the East repeatedly cut off the Tin Woodman's body parts. After he is rescued, he carries it and uses it to clear branches blocking the road.

enchanted axe in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

An enchanted axe, controlled by the Wicked Witch and enchanted by the Witch of the East, is a powerful weapon. After the woodchopper refuses to stop visiting Nimmie Amee, it cuts off his legs, arm, remaining arms, body, and head, then chops his body into pieces.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Witch of the East enchanted the woodchopper's axe after he refused to stop visiting Nimmie Amee. The axe first cut off his right leg, then his other leg, and later twisted around and cut off one of his arms.

Chapter Two

The enchanted axe cuts off Nick Chopper's arms, body, and head at the Wicked Witch's command. The Witch then uses it to chop his body into several pieces.
