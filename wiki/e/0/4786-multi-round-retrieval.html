multi-round retrieval

retrieval method · 5 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Multi-round Retrieval, Multi-time retrieval, multi-round retrieval augmentation, multi-round retrieval-augmented LLM, multi-time retrieval, multi-time retrieval augmented approaches, multi-turn retrieval, multiple active retrieval calls

Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, multi-time retrieval generally outperforms single-time retrieval across previous-window, sentence, decomposition, and FLARE methods. In DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models, multi-round retrieval augmentation retrieves information during generation and is explored for complex tasks such as long-form question answering, multi-hop reasoning, and chain-of-thought reasoning. In Unified Active Retrieval for Retrieval Augmented Generation, multiple active retrieval calls occur within longer text responses, though implementation remains an area for future investigation. In A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models, multi-round retrieval incorporates contextual information and user feedback to refine retrieval dynamically and align the retriever with specific use cases and changing user intent. In DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation, multi-turn retrieval repeatedly retrieves information during generation rather than relying on a single retrieval step and helps Dynamic RAG handle complex problems.
