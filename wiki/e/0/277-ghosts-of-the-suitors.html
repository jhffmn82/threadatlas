Abstract written by a model

The souls of dead suitors, uncared-for in Ulysses' house, are led by Mercury to Hades, where Amphimedon tells Achilles and Agamemnon how Ulysses and Telemachus killed them.

BOOK XXIV

The ghosts of the suitors follow Mercury whining and gibbering. They meet the ghosts of Achilles and Agamemnon, and Amphimedon explains how they were killed by Ulysses and Telemachus.
