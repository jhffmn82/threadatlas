It repeatedly serves as the setting and destination for investigations: under a dense yellow fog, Arthur Cadogan West is last seen there with stolen plans; the theft, concealment of his body, and recovery effort unfold there, while Hugo Oberstein leaves and later returns. Emilia and Gennaro Lucca seek refuge there, but Giuseppe Gorgiano finds them and is killed in a watched flat. Lady Frances Carfax is believed to have been taken there, where its crowded population hinders the search until suspects are traced through a pawnbroker, an undertaker, and a Brixton house. Finally, Holmes plans to return with Watson and Von Bork, sending Von Bork to Scotland Yard.

Abstract written by a model

London is a city central to Elsie Inglis’s hospital work, activities, and wartime proposals. From 1892 to 1893, she worked at the New Hospital for Women, assisted Garrett Anderson in an operation, and visited churches, sermons, theatres, and dances. In September 1914, she presented proposals there to the National Union of Women’s Suffrage Societies and the War Office; the London society later helped fund hospital units.
