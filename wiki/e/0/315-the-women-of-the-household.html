the women of the household in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The women are female servants and household maids in Ulysses’ house. Some secretly sleep with the suitors and are said by Telemachus to be insolent to him and Penelope. They remove bodies and clean, are confined and hanged, while surviving women return with torches to embrace Ulysses.

Euryclea left the cloister to tell the women, and make them come to

The women remove the dead bodies, clean the tables and seats, and carry away the blood and dirt. They are confined between the wall of the domed room and the wall of the yard, then hanged one after another; afterward the surviving women come with torches to embrace Ulysses.
