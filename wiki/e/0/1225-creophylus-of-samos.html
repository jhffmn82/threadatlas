THE TAKING OF OECHALIA

Creophylus of Samos is said to have hosted Homer and to have received the attribution of The Taking of Oechalia as a reward. Another account says that Creophylus wrote the poem and Homer lent it his name. Creophylus also says that Eurytus had two sons and gives an account of Medea's actions.
