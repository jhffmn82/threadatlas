the war between Glinda and Jinjur in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

A secret conflict planned by General Jinjur’s Army of Revolt against Oz’s men and the Scarecrow’s rule begins when Queen Jinjur defies Glinda the Good and Princess Ozma. It ends when the Gump enables Jinjur’s capture, and her army submits.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The war is a secret conflict planned by General Jinjur’s army against the men of Oz and the rule of the Scarecrow. General Jinjur claims it will be pleasant because she believes no man will harm a girl.

The Riches of Content

The war begins when Queen Jinjur defies Glinda the Good and Princess Ozma. It ends when the Gump flies over the Emerald City's walls and the Captain and soldiers capture Jinjur, causing the Army of Revolt to submit.
