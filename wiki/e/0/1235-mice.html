Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

The Mice become enraged after Crumb-snatcher drowns and assemble for war against the Frogs. They arm themselves with bean-pod greaves, ferret-skin breastplates, lamp shields, bronze needles, and pea-nut helmets, but flee when the crabs attack.
