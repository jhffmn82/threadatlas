THE CYPRIA

The Cypria is an epic said to have been current in eleven books, continuing the account of the Trojan war. It recounts events from Zeus’s plan with Themis through the early Greek campaign against Troy, and fragments attribute various genealogical, mythological, and narrative details to its poet.
