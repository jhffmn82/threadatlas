Rhacius

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Rhacius, son of Lebes and Mycenaean by race, meets and marries Manto in accordance with Apollo's oracle. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Rhacius leads Cretans to Claros, understands Manto’s identity and mission, marries her, and makes her companions fellow-settlers.
