wealth

Also called: auro, census, gold, lucrum, money, munera, opes, riches, wealth and money

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, wealth and money control love, rank, public office, and social access; those who can pay command the narrator’s lady-love, and wealth gives weight to judges and status to knights. In The Amores, or Amours, wealth confers power, honours, and public authority; in the wealthy rival’s ill-gotten case, it lets him dominate the mistress while excluding the poor, prompting the speaker’s wish that it become dust.
