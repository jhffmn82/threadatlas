Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY VIII.

Wealth gives people power over women, social honors, and authority in the Senate-house, Forum, and field of Mars. The wealthy rival’s riches allow him to rule over the mistress like a slave, while the poor are excluded. The speaker-poet wishes that the rival’s ill-gotten wealth would be changed into dust.
