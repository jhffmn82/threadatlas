Scylla's rock

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Scylla rock, a rock, her rock, the Scylla side, the one

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, Scylla's rock rises sheer toward heaven, has a peak hidden in a dark cloud, and contains a high cavern where Scylla sits. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Scylla rock is what Scylla becomes before she can overwhelm the Trojan ships; it remains prominent with crags and is avoided by sailors.
