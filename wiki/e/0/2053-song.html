Nemean Ode VIII

Song preserves honor by raising a lofty column of praise for Deinis, Meges, and their house. It can make labor painless, celebrate worthy deeds, and give the dead an honorable name among their children and townsfolk.

Isthmian Ode IV

Song preserves the honor of great warriors and gives lasting glory through the lute, pipe, and the work of poets. The ode itself is a new winged hymn intended to celebrate Phylakidas’s victory.
