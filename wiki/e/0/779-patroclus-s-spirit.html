Patroclus's spirit in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Patroclus's spirit, a ghost, appears in Achilles' dream seeking swift burial and shared bones, warning that Achilles too will die beneath Troy's walls.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXIII.

Patroclus's spirit appears to Achilles in a dream, resembling Patroclus in stature, voice, eyes, and clothing. It asks Achilles to bury him quickly, to let their bones lie together in one golden urn, and warns that Achilles is also destined to die beneath Troy's walls.
