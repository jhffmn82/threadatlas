BOOK XXIII.

Patroclus's spirit appears to Achilles in a dream, resembling Patroclus in stature, voice, eyes, and clothing. It asks Achilles to bury him quickly, to let their bones lie together in one golden urn, and warns that Achilles is also destined to die beneath Troy's walls.
