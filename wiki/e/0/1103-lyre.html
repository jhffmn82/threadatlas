Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the lyre was made by Hermes, produced marvelous music, and was exchanged with Apollo for his whip and the office of cattle-keeping. In The Library, the lyre was made by Hermes from a tortoise shell and cords made from sacrificed cattle, and Apollo exchanged the cattle for it after hearing it.
