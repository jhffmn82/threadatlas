lyre

object · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the lyre, the lyre as token, this clear-voiced companion, this marvellous thing, λύραν
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the lyre was made by Hermes, produced marvelous music, and was exchanged with Apollo for his whip and the office of cattle-keeping.
  • In The Library, the lyre was made by Hermes from a tortoise shell and cords made from sacrificed cattle, and Apollo exchanged the cattle for it after hearing it.
