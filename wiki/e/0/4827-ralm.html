Also called: IC-RALM, retrieval-augmented language modeling (RALM)

Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, IC-RALM is identified as an existing method in the Previous-window category that retrieves every few tokens. In RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RALM is described as an in-context learning approach for large-language models that utilizes concatenated context.
