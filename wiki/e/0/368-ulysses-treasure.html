Ulysses’ treasure in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Ulysses’ treasure is a collection of Phaeacian gifts: bronze, gold, clothing, tripods, cauldrons, cloaks, shirts, a sword, and a golden goblet. Arete packs gifts in a chest, which Ulysses fastens. After waking, he counts everything, finds nothing missing, and stores it with Minerva in the Cave of the Naiads.

The gifts include cloaks, shirts, talents of fine gold, Euryalus's bronze sword with a silver hilt and ivory scabbard, and a golden goblet from Alcinous. Arete packs the gifts in a chest along with a cloak and shirt, and Ulysses fastens the chest with a bond taught by Circe.

BOOK XIII

Ulysses’ treasure consists of bronze, gold, clothing, tripods, cauldrons, and other gifts from the Phaeacians. He counts it after waking and finds nothing missing, then stores it with Minerva in the Cave of the Naiads.
