Chinese New Year, commonly known in Chinese as the Spring Festival, is a festival associated with myths and customs. It begins with house cleaning and red paper-cut and couplet decorations, includes family reunions and varying regional traditions, and has evolved from historical origins while affecting Chinese communities worldwide.
