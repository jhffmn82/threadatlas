Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XXVI. TO DIONYSUS

The Nymphs receive Dionysus from Zeus, foster and nurture him carefully in the dells of Nysa, and bring him up. Later they follow him as their leader through the wooded valleys, filling the forest with their outcry.
