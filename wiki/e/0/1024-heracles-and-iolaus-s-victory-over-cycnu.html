Heracles and Iolaus’s victory over Cycnus

Abstract written by a model

Heracles and Iolaus, aided by power Zeus gives Heracles, fight Cycnus, with Iolaus serving as charioteer and warrior. After Cycnus’ spear fails against Heracles’ shield, Heracles kills him with a thrust to the unguarded neck, and he and Iolaus receive Cycnus’ armour as spoils.
