Medea's prophecy

Also called: Medea's rhythmic utterance, prophecy of Medea, word of Medea

Abstract written by a model

Medea's prophecy foretells that Euphemos's descendants will found Kyrene and other Libyan cities, with Thera becoming their mother-city in the seventeenth generation.

Medea's prophecy is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

IV.

Medea prophesies that descendants of the Argonauts will establish cities in Libya and that Thera will become the mother-city of powerful communities. Her prophecy specifically links Euphemos's line with the later founding of Kyrene.
