Euryalus in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Euryalus, a Phaeacian athlete resembling Mars, excels at wrestling but rudely insults Ulysses; after Ulysses rebukes him, he apologizes and gives him a sword.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VIII

Euryalus is described as resembling Mars and as the best-looking Phaeacian after Laodamas. He excels at wrestling, but he insults Ulysses by suggesting that he is an unskilled trader, provoking Ulysses’ fierce rebuke.
