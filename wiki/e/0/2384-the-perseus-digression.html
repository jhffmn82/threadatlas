Also called: the digression about Perseus

Abstract written by a model

The Perseus digression occupies much of the ode and recounts Perseus's visit to the Hyperborean folk and his slaying of the Gorgon. The text says that its apparent reason is that Thorax, and perhaps Hippokleas, belonged to the Aleuadai, who were descended through Herakles from Perseus.

the Perseus digression is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
