kisses

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, kisses shared with the beloved girl are intimate; after another man apparently teaches her, her kisses to the speaker become heartfelt, powerful, and more skillful. In The Amores, or Amours, kisses are shared by the speaker with the mistress; she gives the rival passionate kisses and later gives the speaker exceptionally powerful, delightful, and more skillful kisses.
