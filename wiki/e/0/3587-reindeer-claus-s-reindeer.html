reindeer (Claus's reindeer) in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Claus's reindeer are a team of ten loyal, beautiful deer serving Santa Claus: Glossie, Flossie, Racer, Pacer, Reckless, Speckless, Fearless, Peerless, Ready, and Steady. Selected for likeness to lead deer Glossie and Flossie, they are strengthened, made fleet, and given long lives by plants. Wearing new leather harnesses, they pull Santa Claus's large sledge swiftly over land and water on yearly night trips around the world. Beginning with Glossie and Flossie wearing bells in the lead, the Gnome King gives each remaining reindeer a bell string over eight years, until all ten make merry music.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

Santa Claus's ten reindeer consist of Glossie, Flossie, Racer, Pacer, Reckless, Speckless, Fearless, Peerless, Ready, and Steady. They are selected for their likeness to Glossie and Flossie, strengthened with the casa plant, made fleet with the grawle plant, and given long lives through the marbon plant. They wear a new leather harness and pull Santa Claus's large sledge swiftly through the night.

12. The First Christmas Tree

Claus's ten reindeer carry him swiftly on his yearly trips around the world. Over eight years, each reindeer receives a string of bells from the Gnome King, so that all ten wear bells and make a merry tune as the sledge travels.
