Timodemidai

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 6 connections

Abstract written by a model

The Timodemidai are a clan of Salamis associated with Acharnai, pre-eminent in games. They win four Pythian victories beneath Parnassos, eight Isthmian crowns at Corinth, seven Nemean crowns, and innumerable victories at home in the games of Zeus.

Timodemidai is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode II

The Timodemidai are associated with Acharnai and rank there pre-eminent in games. They won four victories beneath Parnassos, eight crowns in the valleys of Pelops at Corinth, and seven at Nemea, besides many victories at home.
