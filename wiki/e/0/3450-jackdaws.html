Abstract written by a model

The Jackdaws are numerous, brave birds living as a colony in Jackdaw’s Nest, a hollow-ledge nest filled over years with stolen household articles and money. Fond of pumpkins, they return angrily when intruders occupy it, attack the Tin Woodman, Gump, Saw-Horse, and Scarecrow, strip and drop the Scarecrow’s straw into the gulf, then flee when the Gump breaks free into their nest.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The Jackdaws have built a nest in a hollow ledge and filled it with stolen objects gathered over many years. They return in a large, angry flock after hearing that intruders occupy their nest.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

The Jackdaws attack the Tin Woodman, the Gump, the Saw-Horse, and the Scarecrow’s straw. They tear away all of the Scarecrow’s straw and drop it into the gulf before fleeing when the Gump breaks free and flops into the nest.
