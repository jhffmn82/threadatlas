Chalcis

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Chaki- dians, Chal-* cis, Chalcidian, Chalcisj, Chalds, Chaleidiaos

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Chalcis in Euboea was the place where Homer and Hesiod were said to have met and competed at Amphidamas's funeral gathering, where its leading men and Paneides judged the contest and declared Hesiod the winner. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Chalcis was a city in Euboea that alone was garrisoned by the enemy; Cassander guarded it through Plistarchus until Ptolemy secured its surrender and removed the garrison.
