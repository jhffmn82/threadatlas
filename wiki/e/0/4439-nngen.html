NNGen in RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation

Ensheng Shi, Yanlin Wang, Wei Tao, Lun Du, Hongyu Zhang, Shi Han, Dongmei Zhang, and Hongbin Sun · 2022

NNGen is a simple retrieval-based, IR-based baseline model for code diffs. It represents diffs as bag-of-words vectors, uses cosine similarity and nearest neighbors to retrieve messages, and generally outperforms Lucene and end-to-end neural models, though retrieved messages can add incorrect information. RACE surpasses it in human-rated informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness.
