Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, Mombi is Tip’s guardian and a witch who transformed Ozma into Tip, animated Jack Pumpkinhead, aided Jinjur, and was captured by Glinda. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Mombi is the witch who stole and transformed baby Ozma and received the Powder of Life from Dr. Pipt to animate Jack Pumpkinhead. In The Lost King of Oz, Mombi is a wicked witch and cook who transformed Ozma, concealed Pastoria, and schemed to regain her magic before being washed away. In The Giant Horse of Oz, Mombi is a former wicked ruler who abducted queens and mothers, pursued Orin, and was conquered by Tattypoo.

Mombi in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06 · the work’s page · read it where Mombi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mombi is an old, magical witch or sorceress in the Gillikin country, feared and poorly regarded by her neighbors. She has a bad, revengeful disposition, practices witchcraft despite the law, and owns a four-horned cow, pigs, corn-fields, a chest of treasures, a black kettle, magical herbs and recipes, a magic mirror, and the Powder of Life. Tip’s guardian from childhood, she obtains new magic supplies through a trade with a crooked wizard and uses the Powder of Life to animate Jack Pumpkinhead. She then confines Jack and prepares to turn Tip into a marble statue, but Tip escapes with the Powder of Life after imitating her magic to bring the Saw-Horse to life. Furious, Mombi joins Queen Jinjur at the Emerald City, becomes Jinjur’s chief counsellor, and uses secret magic from the royal palace to hinder the Scarecrow’s party. Her witchcraft creates deceptive obstacles: a river, granite wall, whirling roads, and flame. She is less skilled than Glinda and is Glinda’s enemy. Glinda suspects Mombi helped hide Ozma, while Tip reports that Mombi is with Jinjur. When Glinda moves against her, Mombi repeatedly uses transformations to conceal herself and escape. She becomes a red rose in the palace garden and is carried unknowingly into Glinda’s camp by the Tin Woodman, then changes forms again until she is exhausted in Griffin form. Glinda captures her and restrains her with a magical thread. Mombi confesses that the Wonderful Wizard of Oz entrusted infant Ozma to her, and that she transformed Ozma into the boy Tip. She says she will restore Ozma to her proper form.

Abstract written by a model

Mombi is an ugly, ill-tempered, wicked old Gilliken witch who is also an excellent cook. Once powerful, she lost or forgot her witchcraft through Glinda's intervention after her wicked transformations, and works as King Kinda Jolly's cook while secretly seeking to recover power. She transformed Pajuka into a goose, changed Ozma into a boy called Tip, concealed the King of Oz in an unknown form, and caused King Kinda Jolly's disappearance. Although she claims not to remember what she did, she searches for the King because restoring him may restore her own power. Her journey moves from Kimbaloo through the Purple Forest and Catty Corners toward the Emerald City. She uses purple gelatin to cross the inland sea, escapes Catty Corners by making powerful pudding, and repeatedly schemes to steal Ozma's magic, take revenge, destroy Snip, and control the kingdom. Her cruelty is clearest when she throws Snip down a well and later tries to take Humpy, whom she recognizes as the transformed King. Captured by Kabumpo, she agrees to lead the party to the Emerald City, where she believes green magic can undo the enchantment. There, Mombi attempts the transformation formula after Humpy sits on the throne, but forgets the King's robe; the palace sinks instead. Under guard, she eventually admits that she turned Pastoria into a tailor and threw him down a well. After Pastoria is restored, Mombi is imprisoned. In the end, Ozma orders that water finish her, and Mombi is washed away, leaving only her buckled shoes.

Mombi in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928 · the work’s page · read it where Mombi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mombi is an old, ugly witch and former wicked ruler of the North, credited with the strength of ten men and with powerful magic tools and enchantments. She steals and abducts queens and mothers, including the young Queen of Cheeriobed and Philador's mother, and hides Philador's royal mother while causing his troubles. When Philador is two, she carries off the Queen of the Ozure Isles. After seeing Cheeriobed's intended wedding to Orin, Mombi falls in love with him, transforms herself into a young maiden, and seeks to marry him. Rejected, she abducts Orin and tries to turn her into a witch, but Orin drives her from the forest. Mombi also utterly destroys Cheeriobed's father, the good King of the Munchkins. Tattypoo captures and conquers her, taking her magic tools; later Ozma orders her put out. Her absence leaves some of her damage beyond remedy.
