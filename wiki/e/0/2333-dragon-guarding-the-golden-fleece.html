Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, it guards the Golden Fleece in a thick wood, and Jason kills or overcomes it through subtlety with Medea’s help before taking the fleece. In The Argonautica, it guards the Golden Fleece in the sacred grove of Ares until Medea charms it into sleep, allowing Jason to take the fleece. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, it guards the Golden Fleece until Medea’s herbs and words put it to sleep, allowing Jason to take it.

A huge, many-coiled monster serpent in Aea guards the Golden Fleece, spread on an oak in the sacred grove of Ares, unceasingly. Deathless and sleepless, it watches with eyes sleep never overcomes and terrifies the land with its hiss, making the fleece difficult to take.
