text chunk in A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models

Qinggang Zhang, Shengyuan Chen, Yuanchen Bei, Zheng Yuan, Huachi Zhou, Zijin Hong, Hao Chen, Yilin Xiao, Chuang Zhou, Junnan Dong, Yi Chang, Xiao Huang · 2025-09-29

A text chunk is a manageable segment or detailed source passage from a larger corpus used in traditional RAG knowledge organization. It is transformed into an embedding, stored for retrieval, and may carry metadata for filtering and re-ranking. In graph-based organization, it can be indexed as a node or linked to summarized knowledge, enabling retrieval of detailed context. Chunk size matters: overly fine chunks can lose semantic integrity, while overly coarse ones can add redundancy.
