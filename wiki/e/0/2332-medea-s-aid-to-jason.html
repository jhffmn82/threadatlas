Medea's aid to Jason

event · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: I saved thee, Medea's rescue of Jason, Medea's rescue of the Argonauts, accomplishment of the tasks, antidotes of cruel pain, assist them to the utmost of her power, conducted tht; Argonauts to the temple, favors, my deliverance, prayers and charms, preserved thee, services, the Quest I wrought, toils
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Medea rescues and assists the Argonauts by warning them of the danger from the local custom of murdering strangers, guiding them to the temple of Mars, helping them enter, poisoning the dragon guarding the fleece, and curing Jason and others of battle wounds.
  • In Medea, Medea says that she saved Jason by helping him yoke the fiery bulls, sow the field of the Lords of Death, kill the sleepless serpent guarding the Golden Fleece, and escape with the prize, and that she left her father and home and caused Pelias’s death for his sake.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, Medea says that she saved Jason by helping him overcome the fire-breathing bulls, defeat the dragon guarding the Golden Fleece, and escape danger, and that she betrayed her father and country and caused Pelias’s death for his sake.
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Medea aids Jason with drugs, prayers, and charms to protect him from Aietes’s bulls, complete his tasks, take the Golden Fleece, and escape.
