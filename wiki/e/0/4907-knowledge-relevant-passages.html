knowledge-relevant passages in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023

Knowledge-relevant passages are a subset of question-relevant passages containing the information needed to generate a label answer. Introduced as passages that may express answer knowledge despite little wording overlap, they are distinguished from label-relevant passages by a higher knowledge level. The framework treats them as positive examples, and its retrieval objectives aim to favor and retrieve them more effectively.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Knowledge-relevant passages contain knowledge expressed in the answer even when they do not closely match its wording. The proposed framework treats both passages in the tactful example as positive and aims to retrieve such passages more effectively.

Problem Statement

Knowledge-relevant passages are passages within the question-relevant passages that contain the knowledge needed to generate the label answer. They are defined at a higher knowledge level than label-relevant passages and can express the same meaning with little token overlap.

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

A knowledge-relevant passage contains information needed to answer the question. The proposed objectives are intended to make the retriever favor such passages.
