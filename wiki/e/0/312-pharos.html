Pharos

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: an island, the isle of Pharos, these inhospitable shores

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, Pharos is an island near Egypt with a good harbour where Menelaus is becalmed for twenty days until Idothea helps him reach the open sea. In Hecuba and other plays, Pharos is the island at the mouth of the Nile where Proteus lived and ruled, and where the scene is set at Proteus’ tomb near the entrance to Theoclymenus’ palace.
