single combat between Hector and Ajax in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

A single combat begins when Hector challenges the Achaeans and Ajax is chosen; they trade spears and stones until heralds end the unfinished fight at nightfall, after which reconciled Hector and Ajax exchange presents.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VII.

Hector challenges the Achaeans to single combat, offering the victor the opponent’s armour while requiring the defeated man’s body to be returned for burial. Ajax is chosen to face him; they exchange spear thrusts and stones until nightfall, when heralds stop them before either can finish the fight.
