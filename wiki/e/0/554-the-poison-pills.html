Abstract written by a model

Jefferson Hope makes two small pills for each intended victim, Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson: one with poison alkaloid and one harmless. He forces Drebber to choose while taking the other himself. After Stangerson’s death, pills found in his room are tested by Sherlock Holmes, who confirms one is deadly and one harmless.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

The pills are small, pearly grey, nearly transparent, and soluble in water. Sherlock Holmes tests them on a dying terrier and discovers that one is harmless while the other contains deadly poison, confirming their importance to the case.

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope makes two small pills for each intended victim: one containing poison alkaloid and one harmless. He forces Enoch Drebber to choose one pill and swallows the other, leaving chance to determine which of them will live.
