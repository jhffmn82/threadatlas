Abstract written by a model

In The Trojan Women of Euripides, war is shown to cost soldiers their lives, wives their husbands, children their fathers, and the dead their familiar burial places. In Heroides and Amores, war is treated as a more profitable pursuit than poetry and the liberal arts because it can bring military rank and wealth. In The Amores, or Amours, war is the subject of Macer's poem about Achilles, heroes, and their arms, though Love can also be sung amid warfare.
