war and its cost in The Trojan Women of Euripides

Euripides · -0414

The text emphasizes that war costs soldiers their lives, wives their husbands, children their fathers, and the dead their familiar burial places. Cassandra says Troy gained glory by dying for its people, while the Greeks gained grief, isolation, and a hollow victory.
