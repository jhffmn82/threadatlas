Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the Tin Woodman joins Dorothy’s party, seeks a heart from Great Oz, helps and protects his companions, receives a heart, and wishes to rule the Winkies. In The Marvelous Land of Oz, the Tin-Woodman appears in the chapter list as the figure whom the Scarecrow travels to meet and who plucks a rose. In The Marvelous Land of Oz, the Tin Woodman welcomes the Scarecrow and his companions, helps regain the throne, builds the Gump, and returns to the Winkies. In Ozma of Oz, the Tin Woodman commands Ozma’s army, leads the expedition to rescue the royal family of Ev, enters the Nome King’s palace, is transformed into an ornament, and is restored. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, the Tin Woodman welcomes Dorothy and Oz, leads the Royal Army, repairs the Sawhorse, and helps defend Eureka during her trial. In The Road to Oz, the Tin Woodman welcomes Dorothy and her companions to his castle, travels with them, shares Ozian knowledge, and stands beside the throne at a reception. In The Emerald City of Oz, the Tin Woodman receives Dorothy’s party, offers his army against the Nome King, remains with Ozma, and helps seize the Nome King at the Forbidden Fountain. In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the Tin Woodman lives in his castle, falls overboard and rusts, and is restored by Scarecrow and the Wizard.

and 8 more works

In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the Tin Woodman welcomes the visitors, helps Ojo by providing oil, refuses a butterfly wing, and accompanies the party to the Emerald City. In The Lost Princess of Oz, the Tin Woodman punishes a cruel ferryman, is assigned to Quadling Country, stays at Wogglebug’s college, and takes Patent Educational Pills. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, the Tin Woodman rules the Winkies, travels with Scarecrow and Woot to find Nimmie Amee, is transformed into a Tin Owl, and eventually accepts that Nimmie Amee is happy with Chopfyt. In The Tin Woodman of Oz, the Tin Owl is Nick Chopper after Mrs. Yoop transforms him, and it seeks to break the enchantment and regain its former form. In The Magic of Oz, the Tin Woodman prepares a birthday girdle for Ozma and attends her banquet, entertaining guests without eating. In Glinda of Oz, the Tin Woodman serves as Ozma’s adviser and Emperor of the Winkies and joins the Skeezer expedition. In The Royal Book of Oz, Nick Chopper identifies himself as a Tin Woodman and asks Professor Wogglebug to record him as Smith, rejecting his meat connections. In The Cowardly Lion of Oz, the Tin Woodman travels with Tik Tok and others to Mudge and reaches a lion enclosure.

Tin Woodman in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04 · the work’s page · read it where Tin Woodman first appears

Abstract written by a model

Tin Woodman is a man and woodman made entirely of tin, with a jointed tin head, arms, and legs, an axe, and an oil-can needed to prevent or repair rust. Once in love with and intending to marry a Munchkin girl, he lost his original body when it was replaced with tin and believes he lost his heart as well. Found immobilized by rust after more than a year in the forest, he is oiled by Dorothy and the Scarecrow, joins Dorothy’s party, and seeks a heart from Great Oz. Though he claims to lack a heart, he repeatedly protects and helps others: he kills threats, rescues the Field Mice’s queen, builds bridges, rafts, trucks, and ladders, and is careful not to harm living creatures after grieving over a beetle he accidentally kills. He is battered and bent after being dropped on rocks, then repaired by Winkie tinsmiths and given a gold-handled axe. Oz eventually places a silk, sawdust-stuffed heart in his breast, which Tin Woodman accepts joyfully. He continues south with Dorothy, receives a jeweled silver oil-can, and ultimately wishes to return to and rule the Winkies forever.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

16 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter V

The Tin Woodman is found rusted in place after standing in the forest for more than a year. Once Dorothy and the Scarecrow oil his joints, he can move again, joins their party, and explains that he wants the Great Oz to give him a heart.

Chapter VI

The Lion's claws cannot harm the Tin Woodman, though the attack knocks him down. He says he will ask Oz for a heart and explains that he believes he cannot have heart disease because he has no heart. After accidentally killing a beetle, he cries, rusts his jaws shut, and becomes more careful not to harm living creatures.

Chapter VII

The Tin Woodman chops wood for the campfire and later chops down a tree to make a bridge over the second gulf. During the Kalidah attack, he chops away the end of the tree bridge, causing the Kalidahs to fall into the gulf. He begins building a raft to cross the river.

Chapter VIII

The Tin Woodman helps build and steady the raft and uses a pole to push it through the river. He helps rescue the raft, worries about the Scarecrow, and later carries Dorothy with the Scarecrow through the poppies.

Chapter IX

The Tin Woodman kills the Wildcat with his axe and saves the Queen of all the Field Mice. He protects the mice from Toto, builds a truck from tree limbs and pieces of a tree trunk, and helps push the loaded truck out of the poppy bed.

Chapter X

The Tin Woodman travels with Dorothy toward the Emerald City and wants the Great Oz to give him a heart. He is fitted with locked green spectacles at the city gate.

Chapter XI

The Tin Woodman spends the night moving his joints to keep them in working order because he cannot sleep. Oz promises to give him the biggest, kindest, and most loving heart in the Land of Oz after the Wicked Witch of the West is killed. He agrees to go with Dorothy, saying that he has no heart to harm even a Witch.

Chapter XII

The Tin Woodman keeps watch and kills forty wolves with his axe. He protects Dorothy and the others from the bees because their stings break against his tin body. The Winged Monkeys carry him over sharp rocks and drop him, leaving him battered and dented so that he cannot move or groan.

Chapter XIII

The Tin Woodman is found battered and bent on a rocky plain, with his rusted axe nearby. Winkie tinsmiths repair him over three days and four nights, and a goldsmith gives his axe a solid-gold handle and polishes its blade. He rejoins Dorothy and the others and prepares to return to Oz to obtain his heart.

Chapter XIV

The Tin Woodman becomes discouraged during the long journey because he wants to reach Oz and receive his heart. He is carried safely by the Winged Monkeys to the Emerald City.

Chapter XV

The Tin Woodman demands the heart Oz promised him and becomes angry when Oz tries to delay fulfilling the promise. After discovering Oz is a humbug, he still agrees to return the next day to receive a heart.

Chapter XVI

The Tin Woodman visits Oz to receive a heart. Oz cuts a square hole in his breast, places a silk heart stuffed with sawdust inside, and solders the tin back together. The Tin Woodman is pleased and grateful despite the patch.

Chapter XVIII

The Tin Woodman mourns Oz and weeps while Dorothy wipes away his tears so he will not rust. He is pleased with his new heart and agrees to accompany Dorothy south, bringing his axe.

Chapter XIX

The Tin Woodman leaves the Emerald City with Dorothy and the others. He chops through a branch of a fighting tree, allowing the group to pass, frees Toto from another branch, and later decides to make a ladder to climb over the white china wall.

Chapter XX

The Tin Woodman makes a ladder from wood found in the forest so the group can climb the wall. He climbs over the wall last and helps the others cross the china country, warning that they may hurt its inhabitants.

Chapter XXIII

The Tin Woodman wishes to return to the Country of the West and rule the Winkies. Glinda promises that the Winged Monkeys will carry him safely to the land of the Winkies.

Tin Woodman in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06 · the work’s page · read it where Tin Woodman first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Tin Woodman, also called Nick Chopper, is a tin person and famous hero who rules the Winkies as their Emperor. Entirely composed of soldered and riveted tin, with brilliant nickel plating and an axe, he has a large, warm heart placed in his breast by the Wizard, which he regards as his best part. He is peaceful, kind, fond of flowers, and a faithful friend of Dorothy and the Scarecrow, though he can fight fiercely when needed. When the Scarecrow seeks his protection after the girls of Oz take the Emerald City, the Tin Woodman warmly receives him and his companions and leads them to help regain the throne. Along the journey he refuses to harm apparently living sunflowers, saves Jack Pumpkinhead’s pumpkin, repairs the Saw-Horse with Pumpkinhead’s leg, recognizes illusory fire, and calls on the Queen of the Field Mice, whose life he had previously saved. He frightens the city guards with his axe, loses it to Jinjur’s forces, and escapes after the mice cause panic. A skillful mechanic, he repairs Pumpkinhead again and builds the Gump from sofas, a Gump’s head, palm leaves, and a broom. He fights Jackdaws, protects and advises his friends, accompanies the Scarecrow to Glinda, and unknowingly carries Mombi out of the city when she is disguised as a rose in his buttonhole. Returning to the Winkies, he plans fresh nickel plating, invites Princess Ozma to visit, appoints the Scarecrow Royal Treasurer, and maintains that a good heart cannot be bought with money or created by brains.

Abstract written by a model

Tin Woodman, also called Nick Chopper, is a tin being, tin man, and warrior of Oz who was formerly a real man. Claiming his commander’s privilege, he enters the Nome King’s palace to seek Ozma’s rescue, makes an unsuccessful guess, and is transformed into a funny ornament. The ornament cannot be found, even by the Nome King, because he has been enchanted and concealed inside Evring’s tin whistle. Billina restores him by saying “Ev.” He rejoins his friends, is welcomed by the army and people, returns to the Emerald City, receives a diamond-studded axe, and remains a popular favorite.

Tin Woodman in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918 · the work’s page · read it where Tin Woodman first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Tin Woodman, also called Nick Chopper and the Tin Emperor of the Winkies, is a completely tin person who rules the Winkies from a splendid tin castle. Formerly a flesh-and-blood woodchopper in the Munchkin Country and Nimmie Amee's sweetheart, he was gradually remade in tin by Ku-Klip after an enchanted axe severed his limbs, body, and head. He is durable, highly polished when dry, resistant to injury, and carries a sharp axe, but must oil his joints and avoid dampness because he can rust. He has a kind heart but lacks a Loving Heart and cannot love as he once did. He leaves his castle with Scarecrow and Woot the Wanderer to find Nimmie Amee, intending at first to marry her and make her Empress. The journey tests his tin body: he is bound by the Loons, travels to Yoop Castle, and is transformed by Mrs. Yoop into a Tin Owl. As the owl, he escapes danger and considers abandoning marriage if he cannot regain his proper form. Ozma restores him to tin-man form, after which he continues his search. In the great Munchkin forest, he discovers another tin man, learns of Captain Fyter's love for Nimmie Amee, and agrees that she should choose between them. He revisits Ku-Klip's workshop, finds his former Head, and learns that parts from him and the Tin Soldier were used to make Chopfyt. After invisibility, a dent, shrinking, and restoration, he finally meets Nimmie Amee. He explains why he never married her, recognizes his arm on Chopfyt, but accepts that she is married to and happy with Chopfyt. He returns with his companions to the Emerald City and then to his tin castle, with Scarecrow accompanying him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

21 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

The Tin Woodman is the Emperor of the Winkies and lives in a splendid tin castle. He welcomes Woot, provides him with food, and begins recounting how he changed from a flesh-and-blood woodchopper into a tin man.

Chapter Two

Nick Chopper was gradually transformed from a meat-bodied woodcutter into the completely tin-bodied Tin Woodman after the Wicked Witch's enchanted axe severed his limbs, body, and head. He later became the Tin Emperor of the Winkies and decides to find Nimmie Amee and make her Empress.

Chapter Three

The Tin Woodman prepares to leave the Winkie Country with his axe and sets out to find Nimmie Amee. He says he intends to marry her because it is his duty, plans to make her an Empress, and wants to avoid the Emerald City until after their reunion.

Chapter Four

The Tin Woodman keeps watch overnight so the dampness will not rust his joints or dull his polish, then clears the forest path with his axe. The Loons bind him, but a thorn only blunts against his tin, and Woot later cuts his bonds with the axe.

Chapter Five

Nick Chopper travels with Woot the Wanderer and the Scarecrow to Yoop Castle. He carries Woot the Wanderer on his shoulders so Woot can unlatch the castle door, and he recalls Mr. Yoop. Mrs. Yoop plans to transform him into another form the next morning.

Chapter Six

Nick tells Mrs. Yoop about his search for Nimmie Amee and his intention to marry her despite having no Loving Heart. Mrs. Yoop transforms him into a Tin Owl, leaving him with tin feathers, beak, eyes, legs, and claws.

Chapter Eight

The Tin Owl escapes the Jaguar by flying to a high tree branch and later helps observe the beast. He learns that Glinda the Good Sorceress might be able to restore the group to their natural shapes.

Chapter Nine

The Tin Owl is found with the little Brown Bear and the Canary, conferring and wondering what became of Woot.

Chapter Eleven

Nick Chopper is in the form of a Tin Owl and is identified by the Scarecrow as the Tin Woodman and Emperor of the Winkies. He discusses giving up his hope of marrying Nimmie Amee if magic prevents him from regaining his proper form.

Chapter Twelve

The Tin Woodman leaves his tin castle with the Scarecrow and Woot the Wanderer and undertakes a journey. Mrs. Yoop transforms him, and Ozma and Dorothy watch his altered forms through the Magic Picture and seek to restore him.

Chapter Thirteen

The Tin Woodman had been transformed into a Tin Owl and is restored to his original tin-man form by Ozma. He oils his tin joints afterward because sorrow and weeping could cause his polished body to rust.

Chapter Fifteen

The Tin Woodman decides to continue searching for Nimmie Amee and says he will ask whether she still wishes to marry him. He travels with Scarecrow, Woot the Wanderer, and Polychrome through the great Munchkin forest, where he explains that he was born and once rusted while chopping a tree. He discovers an exact tin duplicate of himself, helps release it from rust, and identifies the duplicate as a tin man carrying a sword.

Chapter Sixteen

The Tin Woodman learns that Captain Fyter was also made by Ku-Klip and that both of them loved Nimmie Amee. He agrees to let Nimmie Amee choose between them and plans to accompany Captain Fyter to her house.

Chapter Seventeen

The Tin Woodman says that the forest has few inhabitants and that he cannot think of anyone nearby with whom Nimmie Amee might live. He recognizes Ku-Klip's workshop as the place where he was made and recalls Ku-Klip replacing his severed leg with a tin one. He tells the others to wait just as they are about to leave the workshop.

Chapter Eighteen

The Tin Woodman finds his former Head in one of Ku-Klip's cupboards and speaks with it about their identity and separation. He learns that Ku-Klip assembled Chopfyt from parts belonging to him and the Tin Soldier, and he joins the others in traveling toward Mount Munch to find Nimmie Amee.

Chapter Nineteen

Nick Chopper becomes invisible with the other travelers and collides with Captain Fyter, denting his side so that he leans to the left. Polychrome uses fairy magic to remove the dent and restore him to an erect posture.

Chapter Twenty

The Tin Woodman questions the Swynes about Professor Grunter Swyne and assures them that the travelers are not butchers. He spends the night beside Woot, polishes the Tin Soldier, and later has his own body polished.

Chapter Twenty-One

The Tin Woodman strikes the invisible wall and learns that it is a solid barrier. Although he worries that Nimmie Amee might not want a tiny husband, he decides to continue seeking her and is enlarged again by Polychrome.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Tin Woodman tells Nimmie Amee that he is her former sweetheart, Nick Chopper, and explains that he failed to marry her because he rusted. He recognizes his right arm on Chopfyt and offers to chop Chopfyt apart so that he and the Tin Soldier can reclaim their parts. He accepts that Nimmie Amee is married and happy.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The Tin Woodman fears that getting wet could cause him to rust again. He waits with the others in the dry tunnel and remains highly polished when the storm ends.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The Tin Woodman returns to the Emerald City with his companions and discusses Nimmie Amee's marriage to Chopfyt with Ozma and the others. He accepts that Nimmie Amee is satisfied with Chopfyt and returns to his tin castle with the Scarecrow accompanying him.
