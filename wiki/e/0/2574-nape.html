Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Nape is a personal attendant who arranges hair, gives signals, aids secret nighttime meetings, urges Corinna to visit Naso, and carries tablets to secure her response. In The Amores, or Amours, Nape is Corinna’s attendant who arranges hair and secret nighttime affairs, gives signals, aids Naso, and delivers tablets to Corinna to secure a reply.

Nape in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · about 24 to 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where Nape first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nape is a personal attendant, not a handmaid, skilled at arranging hair, giving signals, and aiding secret nighttime meetings. Faithful to Naso in trouble and touched by Cupid, she repeatedly urges Corinna to visit him. Naso ultimately entrusts her with tablets, asking her to secure Corinna’s response.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI

Nape is skilled at arranging scattered locks and is useful in secret nighttime activities and in giving signals. She has often urged Corinna to come to Naso and has proved faithful to Naso in difficulties. Naso asks her to deliver his inscribed tablets to Corinna, remove delays, observe Corinna’s reaction, and make sure she replies.

Nape in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where Nape first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nape, Corinna’s attendant, skillfully arranges hair and secret nighttime affairs, gives signals, and faithfully aids Naso, who asks her to deliver tablets to Corinna and secure a reply.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY XI.

Nape is praised for arranging hair neatly, managing secret nighttime arrangements, giving signals, and faithfully helping the speaker in his difficulties. The speaker asks her to deliver his tablets to Corinna and obtain a reply.
