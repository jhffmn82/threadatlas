MuSiQue is a multi-hop question answering dataset created through single-hop question composition. Its questions typically require two to four reasoning hops and are each associated with 20 Wikipedia documents. It is one of three multi-hop QA datasets used to evaluate TRACE; its original development set has 2,417 questions. Across reported evaluations, TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc outperform baselines on the test set, while ablations show reduced TRACE-Triple performance when demonstrations are removed or fixed. MuSiQue in GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation

Zhili Shen · 2025 · the work’s page · read it where MuSiQue first appears

Abstract written by a model

MuSiQue is a challenging open-domain multi-hop question answering dataset, with 3-hop questions and questions grouped by triple density in golden passages. It is used with IRCoT-provided data and full corpus. In evaluations, it is one of three benchmarks for retrieval and question answering, where GEAR reports gains exceeding 10% and Recall@5/10/15 of 58.4/67.6/71.5. For end-to-end QA, GEAR reaches 19.0 EM and 35.6 F1, above Hybrid + SyncGE’s 14.0 EM and 27.1 F1. GEAR is especially strong on 2-hop and 3-hop questions in a single iteration.
