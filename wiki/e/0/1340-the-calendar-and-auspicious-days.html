Abstract written by a model

The calendar and auspicious days comes from Zeus and marks days for observance. It unfolds by assigning monthly dates to work review, supplies, holy observances, travel, planting, births, marriage, animal work, ships, jars, and yoking; the fifth and some other days are unfavourable or changeable. The thirtieth is best for reviewing work and distributing supplies.

The thirtieth day is recommended for reviewing work and distributing supplies, while the first, fourth, seventh, eighth, ninth, eleventh, and twelfth have various favorable uses. Other days are assigned to planting, sowing, births, marriage, gelding animals, building ships, opening jars, yoking animals, and hauling ships, while fifth days and several others are declared unfavorable or changeable.
