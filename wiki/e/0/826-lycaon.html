Abstract written by a model

Lycaon, son of Priam and Laothoe, is captured and ransomed from Lemnos before fleeing Xanthus. He begs Achilles for mercy, recalling their past encounter, but Achilles kills him over Patroclus’s death and casts him into the river.

BOOK XXI.

Lycaon, a son of Priam, encounters Achilles while trying to escape from Xanthus. He begs for mercy and recalls that Achilles previously captured and sold him, but Achilles refuses to spare him because of Patroclus's death. Achilles kills Lycaon with his sword and throws his body into the river.
