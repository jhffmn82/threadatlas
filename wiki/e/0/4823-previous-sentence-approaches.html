Previous-sentence approaches

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Previous-sentence, Previous-sentence approaches, previous sentence, previous-sentence retrieval

Abstract written by a model

Previous-sentence approaches trigger retrieval at every sentence, using the preceding sentence as the query. They yield only a small improvement on 2WikiMultihopQA and perform worse than next-sentence retrieval on 2WikiMultihopQA and ASQA-hint because adjacent sentences may concern different entities or relations.
