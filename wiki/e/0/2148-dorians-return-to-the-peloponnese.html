Dorians' return to the Peloponnese

event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Dorian invasion of Peloponnesos, Dorian return to the Peloponnese, Doric return, Heraclid return, Heraclidæ returned, When the Dorians invaded Peloponnesos, expedition of the Dorians, return, return of the Heraclidæ, return to the Peloponnese, returned to the Peloponnese, the invasion, the return of the Dorians
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, the Dorian invasion of Peloponnesos was said to have been led in part by Oxylos.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, the Dorians returned to the Peloponnese by ship and landed near the Promontory of Rhium during the reign of Cypselus.
  • In Description of Greece, during the Dorians' planned return to the Peloponnese, an oracle instructed them to choose a three-eyed leader; they accepted Oxylus, followed his advice to travel by ship, and agreed to give him the kingdom of Elis.

CHAPTER V.

During the reign of Cypselus, the Dorians returned to the Peloponnese in ships and landed near the Promontory of Rhium. This differed from their earlier attempt, led by Hyllus, which approached by way of the Isthmus of Corinth.
