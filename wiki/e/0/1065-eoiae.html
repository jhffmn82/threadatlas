The Hesiodic Poems

The Eoiae was appended to the Catalogues of Women and consisted of heroines introduced by a recurring formula meaning “Or such as was.” A large fragment survives at the beginning of the Shield of Heracles, and the text suggests that some independent poems may have been expanded from episodes originally belonging to the Eoiae.
