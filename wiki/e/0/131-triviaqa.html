TriviaQA is an OpenQA dataset used in experiments and as an open-domain question-answering benchmark. It has 78,785 training, 8,837 validation, and 11,313 test samples; questions average 20.2 tokens, answers 5.5 tokens, and each question has 13.7 answers on average. GAR is evaluated on it, achieving state-of-the-art extractive performance, 62.7 extractive EM and 62.2 generative EM, with better retrieval than DPR when retrieving at least five passages.
