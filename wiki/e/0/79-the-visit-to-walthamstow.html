AUGUST 1661 August 1st.

The visit to Walthamstow took place on August 1, with Sir Williams both, the narrator, the narrator’s wife, and Mrs. Margarett Pen traveling by coach to see Mrs. Browne. The party ate a hot venison pasty, were merry, and returned home at night.
