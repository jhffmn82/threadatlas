the suitors' gifts in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The suitors’ gifts are beautiful presents sent to Penelope at her urging instead of merely consuming her household’s property: an embroidered dress, gold and amber chain, earrings, a necklace given by Pisander son of Polyctor, and other presents carried upstairs by maids.

At Penelope’s urging, the suitors send her presents instead of merely consuming her household’s property. The gifts include an embroidered dress with twelve gold brooches, a gold and amber chain, earrings with three pendants, a finely made necklace, and other beautiful presents, which her maids carry upstairs.
