Abstract written by a model

uses hard negatives [found by ANN search over the learned dense vector index] Dense Negative Sampling mines hard negatives by searching a learned dense vector index with ANN rather than locating negatives with BM25. DNS initially lowers T-REx retrieval performance relative to DPRBM25, but DNS followed by RAG training produces the best reported performance on T-REx and zsRE.
