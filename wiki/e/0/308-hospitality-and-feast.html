Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

Menelaus’s household welcomes the travelers by caring for their horses, bathing and clothing them, and serving them an abundant meal. The palace hosts a feast with relatives and neighbors, a bard, and tumblers in honor of two weddings.
