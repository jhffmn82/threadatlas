Lord Sandwich in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Lord Sandwich is a lord, the diarist’s patron or superior, and Master of Trinity House, with responsibility for Wardrobe business. Before leaving, he places great confidence in the diarist: Mr. Townsend is to explain the Wardrobe administration, and the diarist is to become Townsend’s deputy if Townsend dies. He also proposes that the diarist and Mr. Moore manage the business if necessary, promises the diarist first refusal of Mr. Young’s place if vacant, and entrusts him with a major preparation during his absence. Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

JUNE, JULY & AUGUST 1661 June 1st.

My Lord instructed the diarist about Wardrobe business during his absence and said that he intended to join the diarist and Mr. Moore in managing it if Mr. Townsend died. He expressed great confidence in the diarist and promised to give him the refusal of Mr. Young’s place in the Wardrobe if it became vacant. The diarist also obtained my Lord’s imprest of L1000 and sought ammunition for his ships.

opening

My Lord tells the diarist to go to Mr. Townsend, who is to disclose the whole mystery of the Wardrobe to him. He also says that the diarist will be made deputy to Mr. Townsend in case Townsend dies during my Lord's absence.

opening

The narrator's lord is to go to Algier to settle business and put the fleet in order there, then return to Lisbone with three ships. He entrusts the narrator with overseeing the great preparation during his absence.

opening

My Lord requires £300 worth of cloth to give as presents among the Turks in Barbary. He travels from Whitehall to Deptford, boards the Dutch yacht, becomes angry when W. Howe is late with his things, and then sails after taking leave of the King and his friends.

opening

My Lord is the intended recipient of the cloth. The diarist writes to him to report what has been arranged.

opening

Lord Sandwich is reported to be sick ashore at Alicante, causing Pepys serious concern about his health and his family’s condition. A letter from Mr. Creed says that Lord Sandwich has been relieved of his pain and fever and hopes to go aboard within a day or two.

opening

Lord Sandwich writes that he is well again after his fever but remains at Alicante. He is expected to proceed toward Portugal, and the diarist is concerned about how Edward Montagu will dispose of £5,000 intended for Lord Sandwich's departure.
