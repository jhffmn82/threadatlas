Penelope's prayer to Diana in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Penelope prays to Diana for death or removal, unable to bear grief and remarriage; recalling Pandareus’s daughters, she says she would go underground if she could still look toward Ulysses.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XX

Penelope asks Diana to kill her or carry her away because she cannot endure her grief and the prospect of marrying a worse man than Ulysses. She recalls the daughters of Pandareus and says that she would go beneath the earth if she could still look toward Ulysses.
