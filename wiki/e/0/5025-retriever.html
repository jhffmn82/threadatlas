Abstract written by a model

In "Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy," the retriever retrieves corpus paragraphs, addresses supporting-knowledge and distractor issues through generated phrases, and has its query encoder optimized by distillation from a re-ranker. In "DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models," the retriever is evaluated through BM25 and SGPT, with BM25 performing better across all three reported datasets. In "RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models," dense retrievers are not used, and exploring their impact is identified as a possible direction for future research.

Abstract written by a model

Retriever retrieves corpus paragraphs but has a representation bottleneck and semantic-gap difficulty. First, question-only retrieval misses supporting knowledge and is affected by distractive knowledge; then generated phrases retrieve relevant knowledge. Its query encoder can be optimized through distillation from a re-ranker.
