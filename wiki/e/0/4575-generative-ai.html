Abstract written by a model

In Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation, generative AI is described as being deployed in enterprises through a widely used architecture for domain-specific applications. In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, generative AI powers enterprise chatbots, enabling conversational responses beyond scripted intent recognition and aiming to enhance employee productivity while presenting engineering challenges. In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, generative AI solutions are described as requiring lengthy testing because of the need for human response validation. In Reducing hallucination in structured outputs via Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Generative AI lowers barriers to workflow building for novice users, but its propensity to hallucinate makes naive outputs untrustworthy, motivating a Retrieval-Augmented Generation system to improve structured-output quality. In Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology, Generative AI was not used in the creation of the manuscript. In MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health, Generative AI was not used in the creation of the manuscript. In An auditable and source-verified framework for clinical AI decision support: integrating retrieval-augmented generation with data provenance, Generative AI is described as an AI assistant technology that often provides outputs without evidentiary basis, aids manuscript figures and editing, and is piloted in healthcare for notes and clinicians’ questions.
