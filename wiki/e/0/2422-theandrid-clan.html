Theandrid clan in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

The Theandrid clan competes at Olympia, Isthmos, and Nemea, wins wreaths, and returns home with renown celebrated by a herald.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode IV

The speaker came as a ready herald of athletic games for the Theandrid clan. Members of the clan competed at Olympia, Isthmos, and Nemea and returned home with wreaths whose fruitage was renown.
