Abstract written by a model

Pall, the diarist’s maidservant, is associated with Kate Joyce at a child’s christening, causes the diarist great trouble, is reprimanded for her conduct and high spirit, and is sent to stay with the diarist’s parents in the country.

opening

Pall is reprimanded before the diarist's father for her conduct and high spirit. The diarist arranges for her to go into the country with his parents for a time, and she is left responsible for the household work until she departs.
