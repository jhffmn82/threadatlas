Lacedaemon

place · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Lacedaemon, a low-lying city containing Sparta, is the destination of Telemachus’s long sea voyage with Pisistratus. They arrive and stay at Menelaus’s palace while Telemachus asks about Ulysses. Minerva comes there to fetch him, and, urged to return home, he leaves with Pisistratus.

Lacedaemon is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
