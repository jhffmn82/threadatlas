begging and hunger

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: beg, begging, begging my way, cravings of a hungry belly, insatiable belly

Abstract written by a model

Ulysses proposes to begin begging in the town so that he will not trouble Eumaeus or his men. He plans to ask for drink and bread and to offer his services to the suitors. Telemachus sends Ulysses to the city to beg for drink and bread, since he cannot care for another burden. Ulysses says that hunger cannot be hidden and calls the cravings of the belly an enemy that troubles all people.
