afterlife judgment

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Rhadamanthos' just decree, penalty, sentence, souls pay penalty, the judgment

Abstract written by a model

Afterlife judgment judges the dead for sins in Zeus' realm: guilty souls are punished beneath the earth, while virtuous souls, especially those steadfast through repeated lives, receive an unlaborious, tearless life in sunlight at the tower of Kronos. It draws near sorrow or joy and may appear in dreams.

II.

The dead are judged for the sins committed in Zeus's realm, with the guilty souls paying penalties under the earth. The virtuous, especially those who have remained steadfast through repeated lives and avoided iniquity, travel to the tower of Kronos and enjoy a tearless life among the blessed.

FRAGMENTS OF DIRGES (thraenoi).

It draws near for sorrow or for joy and is sometimes revealed in dreams.
