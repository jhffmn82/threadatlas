Abstract written by a model

In “Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training,” Chinese is a language studied in multilingual keyphrase generation and is represented in AcademicMKP with reported RAMKG + RGIT precision, recall, and F1. In “Enhancing treatment decision-making for low back pain: a novel framework integrating large language models with retrieval-augmented generation technology,” Chinese language is used as the prompt language, with Chinese linguistic characteristics and contextual elements incorporated to improve the model’s understanding of patient complaints. In “TurboRAG: Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Precomputed KV Caches for Chunked Text,” Chinese language data is evaluated in the RGB Information Integration Task, with Naïve RAG averaging 51.7 and TurboRAG-reordered averaging 48.3; in the RGB task, the corresponding averages are 97.1 and 95.0.
