Abstract written by a model

Passage reading is evaluated with Exact Match using both extractive and generative methods. GAR is paired with corresponding readers, including an extractive DPR reader and a generative setup using the question and top-10 retrieved passages. Passage reading is evaluated as the final stage of GAR's OpenQA pipeline. Extractive GAR achieves strong performance on NQ and Trivia, while combining GAR with DPR improves both extractive and generative results.
