BOOK THE SECOND ELEGY I.

Naso attributes extraordinary power to verses: they can bring down the blood-stained Moon’s horns, recall the Sun’s snow-white steeds, make serpents burst, reverse flowing water, and open barred doors. Verses also win the beautiful fair being as a reward for the Poet.
