Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI. TO APHRODITE

The gold-filleted Hours welcome Aphrodite joyously in Cyprus. They clothe her with heavenly garments, crown her with gold, hang ornaments in her ears, and adorn her with golden necklaces and jewels. They wear these jewels themselves when they go to their father's house to join the lovely dances of the gods.
