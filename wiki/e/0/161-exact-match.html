metric · 10 works · 13 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: EM, EM score, EM scores, Exact Match (EM), F1, M, Match, Match metric, Total EM, exact match, exact match accuracy, exact match score, exactly matches, statistical evaluation
  • In "Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering," Exact Match is a reader evaluation metric measuring predicted answer spans that exactly match ground-truth answers after normalization, including article and punctuation removal.
  • In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Exact Match is applied more leniently during Generator Fine-tuning, with its score based on the gold response's presence in the model output.
  • In "BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Match is a non-commercial evaluation metric that measures exact label containment in generated answers and ranks among the most effective metrics tested.
  • In "TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Exact Match is one of the standard metrics used to evaluate QA performance; TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc achieve the best reported Exact Match results across the datasets, with TRACE-Triple obtaining the best result on 2WikiMultiHopQA and MuSiQue.
  • In "RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," exact match is the primary evaluation metric: normalized generated answers are correct only when they exactly match one of the dataset's correct answers.
  • In "Crafting Personalized Agents through Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Editable Memory Graphs," Exact Match accuracy is used to evaluate generated key entities for autofill forms and user services, with EMG-RAG achieving 92.86% for autofill forms, 96.43% for reminders, and 91.46% for travel with GPT-4.
  • In "REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation," Exact Match is an evaluation metric measured on EntityQuestion, and REANO improves its score by up to 2.7% over state-of-the-art baselines.
  • In "Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Exact Match is an evaluation metric for end-to-end question answering that measures the percentage of predicted answers exactly matching the ground truth.
  • In "SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation," exact match is one of the evaluation metrics used in the experiments alongside F1 measure, with SEAKR recording exact match scores of 30.2%, 27.9%, and 19.5% on the complex QA datasets and 25.6%, 54.4%, and 27.1% on the simple QA datasets.
  • In "Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," EM measures whether a predicted answer exactly matches the reference answer string, and analogous EM metrics are used for supporting facts and joint correctness; on HotpotQA, answer EM rises from 25.4 for Naive RAG to 28.1 for RAG + QD + Reranker.
