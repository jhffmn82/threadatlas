It expands from water rites to cleanliness, burial, childbirth, exposure, urination, plant-cutting, and divine punishment.

(ll. 618-640) But if desire for uncomfortable sea-faring seize you;

The speaker forbids making libations with unwashed hands, crossing rivers without washing, mocking sacrifices, polluting rivers and springs, and using uncharmed pots. He also gives rules about bodily cleanliness, burial, childbirth, exposure, urination, and cutting plants at a festival, warning that the gods punish ritual offenses.
