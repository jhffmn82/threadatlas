Abstract written by a model

Sir W. Batten is a Navy Office official, husband of Lady Batten, associated with a house and a baboon brought from Guiny by Captain Holmes. After travelling from Chatham, he socializes closely with Pepys at dinners, church, and his house, then joins Pepys and Sir W. Pen in reporting the Navy’s lack of money to the Duke of York.
