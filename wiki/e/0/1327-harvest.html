(ll. 383-404) When the Pleiades, daughters of Atlas, are rising 1310,

Harvest begins when the Pleiades rise, and the sickle should be sharpened at the proper seasonal time. Workers are told to rise early, bring the fruits home, and later cut grape-clusters, expose them to the sun, cover them, and draw off the wine.
