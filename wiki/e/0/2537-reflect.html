REFLECT in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

Jihyeok Kim · 2020-12-08/2020-12-13

REFLECT is a reference-selection module and component of AT2T. Given attributes, it retrieves candidate references and selects the most relevant K through Coarse-Grained REFLECT’s attribute-based matching followed by Fine-Grained REFLECT’s textual matching. Its relevance scores determine the final references, including reference reviews for GEN. Pseudo-labels discourage retrieval of references whose ratings nearly oppose the ground truth, though such references may guide generation. Retrieved references can mislead GEN when irrelevant, but rating-aware relevance estimation improves generated quality and rating consistency.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

REFLECT is the reference-selection component of AT2T. It selects the most relevant K references from the candidate set using Coarse-Grained REFLECT and Fine-Grained REFLECT.

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

REFLECT selects references by first applying coarse attribute-based matching and then applying fine-grained textual matching. Its relevance scores are used to select the final K references.

RL-REFLECT .. Stabilizing Training of RL

REFLECT is discouraged by pseudo-labels from retrieving a reference with a rating nearly opposite to the ground truth, even when other aspects of that reference can strongly guide generation.

GEN .. Training GEN

REFLECT provides retrieved references for GEN, but training GEN with those references caused a significant performance drop. The text attributes this drop to even a small amount of irrelevant references misleading GEN during training.

Experiments .. Analysis on Rating Consistency

REFLECT retrieves helpful reference reviews using coarse- and fine-grained models. Its references are used by GEN, and rating-aware relevance estimation improves the quality and rating consistency of the resulting generation.
