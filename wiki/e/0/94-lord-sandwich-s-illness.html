Lord Sandwich's illness

Abstract written by a model

Lord Sandwich’s illness was a serious fever at Alicante, with wind pain in the muscles of his right side, treated by twice being let blood. It caused Pepys to fear for his family’s future, but the pain and fever left him and he recovered, remaining at Alicante while possibly soon returning aboard.
