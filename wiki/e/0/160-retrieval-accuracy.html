Abstract written by a model

In Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering, top-k retrieval accuracy is a retriever evaluation metric measuring the proportion of questions whose top-k passages contain an answer span, setting an upper bound for extractive reader answers. In Document Segmentation Matters for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Hits@k measures whether a correct answer is among the top-k retrieved chunks and shows that PIC significantly improves retrieval accuracy across multiple QA benchmarks. In Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Hits@10 measures the percentage of questions for which at least one gold evidence passage appears among the top 10 retrieved passages.
