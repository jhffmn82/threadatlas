the Emmenidai

group · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Emmenidai, The Emmenidai, The clan of the Emmenidai, Theron's ancestors

Abstract written by a model

The Emmenidai, ancestors of Theron of Akragas, migrated from Rhodes, first colonized Gela and then Akragas. Especially devoted to and honoring the Dioskouroi with hospitality and pious rites, they receive glory through the Tyndaridai and a lasting treasure of song in Apollo’s valley.

III.

The Emmenidai are praised alongside Theron for receiving glory through the Tyndaridai of goodly steeds. They are especially devoted to the Dioskouroi and honor them with hospitality and pious rites.

VI.

The Emmenidai are among those for whom a lasting treasure of song is built in Apollo's valley.
