Abstract written by a model

Bread-nibbler, Crumb-snatcher's father, mourns sons lost to ferret, human trap, and Puff-jaw; enraged by Crumb-snatcher's death, he arms the Mice and strikes Puff-jaw.

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

Bread-nibbler is Crumb-snatcher's father and becomes enraged when his son dies. He tells the Mice that he has already lost three sons to the ferret, a human trap, and Puff-jaw, urges them to arm themselves, and later strikes Puff-jaw on the toes.
