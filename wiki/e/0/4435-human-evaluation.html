Abstract written by a model

Human evaluation, following Moreno et al. (2013), Panichella et al. (2016), and Shi et al. (2021b), randomly samples 50 testing-set code diffs and scores 250 code-diff/commit-message pairs from RACE and four baselines. Four volunteers rate semantic similarity through informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness; their average scores show high agreement and confirm RACE effectiveness.
