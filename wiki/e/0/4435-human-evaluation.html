human evaluation in RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation

Ensheng Shi, Yanlin Wang, Wei Tao, Lun Du, Hongyu Zhang, Shi Han, Dongmei Zhang, and Hongbin Sun · 2022

Human evaluation, following Moreno et al. (2013), Panichella et al. (2016), and Shi et al. (2021b), randomly samples 50 testing-set code diffs and scores 250 code-diff/commit-message pairs from RACE and four baselines. Four volunteers rate semantic similarity through informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness; their average scores show high agreement and confirm RACE effectiveness.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The human evaluation compares CommitBERT, NNGen, NMTGen, CoRec, and RACE on informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness. Standard deviations are reported in parentheses.

5.5

Human evaluation measures the semantic similarity of commit messages generated by RACE and four baselines: NNGen, NMTGen, CommitBERT, and CoRec. It uses ratings from four volunteers across Informativeness, Conciseness, and Expressiveness.
