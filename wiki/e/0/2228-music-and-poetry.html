music and poetry

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: banquet-songs, honey-voiced songs, song, song of triumph, songs, strains, the report that liveth after a man, the voice of the Pierides, themes of songs

Abstract written by a model

Music and poetry are divine, honey-voiced songs due to those who succeed through labor. They invite Muses, guide dancers and singers, soothe gods, celebrate triumph, and, through song and chronicle, preserve honor, glory, reputation, and the memory of both living people and the dead.

X.

The text says that honey-voiced songs are due when someone fares well through labor. Pindar promises sweet strains for Agesidamos and calls on the Muses to join in the song of triumph.

I.

Music and poetry are presented as divine gifts that soothe gods, guide dancers and singers, and preserve the memory of the living after death. The speaker says that singers and chroniclers reveal the lives of the dead through lasting report.
