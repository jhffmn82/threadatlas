Fragment #67—Scholiast on Euripides, Orestes 249: Steischorus says that

Zeus’s plan was to mingle storm and tempest over the earth, bring mortal life to an end, and destroy the children of gods and mortals. He intended thereby to prevent gods from mating with mortals and to restore their separation.
