hill of Kronos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Formerly nameless and snow-sprinkled when Oinomaos was king, the hill was named Hill of Kronos by Herakles at the Olympic sanctuary’s founding.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI.

Herakles named the previously nameless hill the Hill of Kronos. At the time when Oinomaos was king, it was described as being sprinkled with much snow.
