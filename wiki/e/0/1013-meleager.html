Meleager

person · 7 works · 39 quoted facts · 16 connections
Also called: Calydonian hero, MELEAGER, Meleager's, dear son of Oeneus and Althaea, golden-haired, son, strong Meleager, the conqueror, the hero, warlike, youth, Μελέαγρον, Μελέαγρος, Μελεάγρου

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Meleager slays the Calydonian boar, fights for Calydon, and is killed by Apollo. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Meleager joins the Calydonian boar hunt, first wounds the boar, gives its skin to Atalanta, kills the sons of Thestius while defending her, and dies before Hercules comes to Calydon. In The Library, Meleager hunts the Calydonian boar, gives its skin to Atalanta, kills the sons of Thestius, joins the Argo, dies, and is seen by Hercules in Hades. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Meleager leads the Calydonian boar hunt, kills the boar and awards its spoil to Atalanta, kills Plexippus and Toxeus, and dies through Althæa's burning of the fatal billet, a concealed burning, or poison. In The Library, Meleager leads the killing of Artemis's boar, gives its skin to Atalanta, kills Thestius's sons, and dies when Althaea burns the brand or while defending Calydon. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Meleager is depicted as a bearded figure above Ajax the son of Oileus and is the subject of differing accounts of the firebrand legend and his death. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Meleager leads and ends the Calydonian boar hunt, gives Atalanta its trophy, kills his uncles over it, and dies when Althea burns the fatal brand.

Abstract written by a model

Meleager, son of Œneus and Althæa, leads the Calydonian boar hunt, gathering neighboring nobles. After Atalanta wounds the boar, he kills it and awards her the spoil.
