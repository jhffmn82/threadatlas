The Alkmaionidai are a great house including Megakles, Kleisthenes, and Perikles; they rebuild Apollo’s temple, while Athens and their horses inspire song.

VII.

The Alkmaionidai were a great house to which Kleisthenes and Perikles belonged. Megakles was a member of the family, and the poem presents Athens and its fame as a foundation for songs about the Alkmaionidai and their horses.
