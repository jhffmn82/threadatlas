Abstract written by a model

In “Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge,” structured knowledge provides explicit grounding and relational information, and supports reasoning over query-document subgraphs to enrich document representations. In “A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models,” structured knowledge is examined as a central concern of GraphRAG, which addresses traditional RAG systems’ limitations in handling it. In “GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation,” structured knowledge is used to address multi-hop QA challenges with LLMs.
