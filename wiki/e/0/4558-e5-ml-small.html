E5-ML-Small

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: E5-ml-small, E5-small, e5s

Abstract written by a model

E5-ML-Small is a model with 384 embedding dimensions. It achieves 21% Recall@1 and 90% Recall@15, combining strong retrieval performance with relatively low dimensionality. Across the evaluation, it earns the highest overall model score and the best balance of accuracy and resource efficiency, leading to a strong recommendation for Arabic language semantic information retrieval.

E5-ML-Small is in one work on this site, Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
