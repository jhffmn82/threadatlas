Abstract written by a model

Dated probably to April 30, B.C. 463, the eclipse of the Sun causes the Sun to hide during daytime, making wisdom’s paths and human strength seem useless, and is treated as a sign of fortune, war, destruction, or disaster.

FRAGMENT OF A SONG WITH ACCOMPANIMENT OF DANCE

The eclipse of the Sun causes the Sun to hide during the daytime and makes the paths of wisdom and the wings of human strength seem useless. It is treated as a sign whose meaning may involve good fortune, war, destruction, or other disasters.
