Knowledge is information in knowledge-relevant passages enabling label-answer generation. It is represented as a dense bottleneck encoding from answers or question-passage pairs, guiding retrieval toward information relevant to answering questions.

Problem Statement

Knowledge is the information contained in knowledge-relevant passages that enables generation of the label answer.

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

Knowledge is represented as a dense bottleneck encoding extracted from an answer or from question-passage pairs. The objectives are designed to focus retrieval on knowledge relevant to answering the question rather than only on the answer label.
