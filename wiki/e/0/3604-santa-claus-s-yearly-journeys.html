Santa Claus's yearly journeys in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Santa Claus’s yearly Christmas Eve journey distributes gifts to children, expanding as they spread across Earth; reindeer pull his sledge over land and oceans, following the sun to encircle Earth in twenty-four hours.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

2. When the World Grew Old

Santa Claus’s yearly journeys extend farther as children spread over the earth. His reindeer travel over oceans as swiftly as over land, allowing his sledge to follow the sun and giving him twenty-four hours to encircle the earth each Christmas Eve.
