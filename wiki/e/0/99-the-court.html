the Court in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

The Court is openly criticized by Ned Pickering for lewdness and beggary and described as corrupt and disordered, with emulation, poverty, drinking, swearing, and loose amours; these conditions prompt fears of ruin and confusion.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Ned Pickering complains openly about the Court’s lewdness and beggary. Pepys fears that such conditions may bring everything to ruin again.

opening

The Court is described as being in very poor condition, marked by emulation, poverty, drinking, swearing, and loose amours. The diarist fears that these conditions will end in confusion.
