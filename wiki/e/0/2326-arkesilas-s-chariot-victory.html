Arkesilas's chariot victory

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: chariot-race victory, conquering car, glory in the chariot-race, this great deed, triumph, victory
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar: Arkesilas wins glory in the chariot-race at Pytho from the hands of the Amphiktyons. The ode is composed to celebrate this victory and to connect it with the divine and ancestral history of Kyrene. The victory was won by Arkesilas's team at the Pythian games, with Karrhotos driving. Among forty charioteers who fell, Karrhotos brought his chariot through unhurt and completed the twelve turns safely.
