the escape from the Emerald City in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The Scarecrow, Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Soldier escape on the Saw-Horse, break through palace and city gates, and end in a wide river.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Scarecrow Plans an escape

The Scarecrow, Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Soldier escape on the Saw-Horse after being tied together with a clothesline. They break through the palace gateway and the city gate, but their flight ends when the Saw-Horse leaps into a wide river.
