tables in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

This entity is a table used in open-domain TableQA. It can hold heterogeneous information across rows and columns, often has one gold table for a question, and may be large enough that fixed-size encoding loses useful detail. In the approach described, the table is linearized by a retriever into a structure-aware sequence containing its title, column and row information, and separation tokens such as <SOT> and <EOT>. Whole tables, rather than separate rows or columns, are compared with questions and retrieved from a precomputed index by score during LI-RAGE training. Joint training uses table-level relevance, inference can assess reliability, and retrieving too many tables may cause confusion despite richer representations helping long, diverse tables.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

They are compared with questions during retrieval and may contain heterogeneous row and column information. A table-level relevance signal is used during joint training, and table reliability can be assessed at inference time.

Introduction

A table can be very large and can contain heterogeneous information across rows and columns. Encoding it into a fixed-size vector risks information loss that can harm question-answering quality.

Related Work

Tables have been modeled with Transformers, structured parsing methods, and linearization schemes that use interleaving tokens to preserve tabular structure. In the described approach, whole tables are retrieved rather than decomposing them into rows and columns, so the reader receives all relevant information.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

Tables differ from open-domain QA passages because questions often have one gold table and focus on a particular cell. Retrieving more than five tables can increase confusion and mistakes, while long tables with diverse information benefit from richer table representations.

A / Table Linearization

The input table is linearized by the retriever component into a structure-aware sequence. The example includes a table title, column information, row information, and separation tokens such as <SOT>, <EOT>, <BOC>, <SOC>, <EOC>, <BOR>, <SOR>, and <EOR>.

Indexing and Dynamic Retrieval

Tables with the highest scores are retrieved during LI-RAGE training using the precomputed index.

tables in TableRAG: A Retrieval Augmented Generation Framework for Heterogeneous Document Reasoning

Xiaohan Yu, Pu Jian, Chong Chen · 2025

Tables are structured data objects defined by interdependent rows and columns. They are extracted from heterogeneous documents, selected from the Wikipedia dataset only when they have at least 20 rows and 7 columns, structurally deduplicated across similar schemas, and retained as 136 distinct benchmark tables. Each is described by a standardized schema with its name, columns, types, and examples, then rendered in Markdown, segmented into retrieval chunks, and ingested into a relational database for symbolic queries. Textual linearization and chunking can damage structure and hinder aggregation, computation, and multi-hop reasoning; TableRAG instead emphasizes precise tabular manipulation.

Tables are often linearized into textual representations and segmented into chunks for retrieval-augmented generation. This can compromise their structural integrity and prevent systems from performing global operations such as aggregation and mathematical computation.

Tables are curated from the Wikipedia dataset, with selection restricted to tables containing at least 20 rows and 7 columns. Structural deduplication removes redundancy across similar schemas, and the final benchmark spans 136 distinct tables.

Tabular data serves as the basis for table reasoning systems that answer user queries. The text describes methods that structure reasoning over tables, decompose tables into smaller sub-tables, generate intermediate SQL queries, combine Python and SQL code, or interact with a Python sandbox to obtain answers.

9

Tables can lose structural integrity when processed by existing RAG approaches, leading to information loss and degraded global, multi-hop reasoning performance. TableRAG addresses this issue through precise tabular manipulation.
