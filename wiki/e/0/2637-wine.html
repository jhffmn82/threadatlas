ELEGY IV.

The wine is mixed and served by the husband, while the mistress is instructed to ask a servant quietly for the wine she wants. The speaker plans to drink from the cup after the mistress has sipped from it. The mistress is also told to urge her husband to drink continually and to add wine to his drink by stealth until he falls asleep.
