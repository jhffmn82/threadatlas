the prophecy of Teiresias in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Teiresias’s prophecy concerns Ulysses’s return to Ithaca: Neptune will cause suffering, and failure to restrain his companions and spare the Sun’s flocks will cost ship and men. It then foretells the suitors’ slaughter, an inland oar-bearing journey and sacrifices to Neptune, ending in a peaceful old-age death from the sea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XI

Teiresias’s prophecy says that Ulysses will suffer on his return because of Neptune, must restrain his companions and spare the Sun’s flocks, and will otherwise lose his ship and men. It also foretells the suitors’ slaughter, a journey carrying an oar into an inland country, sacrifices to Neptune, and a gentle death in old age.
