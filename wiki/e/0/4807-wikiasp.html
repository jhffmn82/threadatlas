WikiAsp in Active Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zhengbao Jiang · 2023

WikiAsp is an open-ended generation task for evaluating aspect-based summaries of entities from 20 Wikipedia domains. It uses ROUGE, named entity-based F1, and UniEval for factual consistency. Examples include academic and historical aspects; FLARE leads compared methods on UniEval, entity-based F1, and ROUGE-L.

5 .. Open-domain summarization

WikiAsp is used to evaluate open-domain summarization by generating aspect-based summaries about entities from 20 Wikipedia domains. Its metrics include ROUGE, named entity-based F1, and UniEval, which measures factual consistency.

6 .. 6.2

On WikiAsp, FLARE achieves the best reported UniEval, entity-based F1, and ROUGE-L results among the compared methods. The unit characterizes WikiAsp as an open-ended task that increases the difficulty of generation and evaluation.
