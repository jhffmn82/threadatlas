Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, the throne is claimed by the Scarecrow, disputed by Glinda, and concluded to belong to Ozma. In The Lost King of Oz, the throne draws Humpy across the throne room, and Mombi uses his sitting upon it to cast her transformation spell.
