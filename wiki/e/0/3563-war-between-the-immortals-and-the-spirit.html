war between the immortals and the spirits of evil in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

War between good immortals and evil spirits begins when the Awgwas defy Ak and threaten Claus, ending with the immortals defeating them and their allies in the Laughing Valley.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

7. The Great Battle Between Good and Evil

The war begins after the Awgwas refuse to obey Ak and threaten to kill Claus. The immortals fight the Awgwas and their allies in the Laughing Valley and win the battle.
