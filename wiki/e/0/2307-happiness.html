happiness in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Happiness is unstable, short-lived among mortals, requires labour, and excessive happiness can yield anguish; Peleus and Kadmos suffer, while Hieron has a favorable lot.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

Happiness is portrayed as unstable and short-lived when it comes in excessive measure. Peleus and Kadmos experienced great happiness but also later suffered bitter anguish, while Hieron is nevertheless said to possess an especially favorable lot.
