THE GREAT EOIAE

The Great Eoiae is cited for genealogies and stories involving Argos, Epidaurus, Heracles, Telamon, Polycaon, Endymion, Melampus, Phineus, and the children of Phrixus. It tells how Hermes stole cattle and changed Battus into a rock after Battus revealed where the cattle were hidden.
