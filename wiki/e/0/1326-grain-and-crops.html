grain and crops

object · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: barley, beneficent gift of corn, corn, corn-ears, fruit, fruit that gives them life, grain, long ears of corn, seed, sheaves, σῖτον

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Demeter’s holy grain is sown in ready soil, guarded from birds, becomes full corn-ears with Zeus’s good result, then is winnowed, measured, jarred, and kept indoors. In The Library, corn is Demeter’s beneficent gift to mankind, spending six months in the earth and six above ground; Triptolemus reveals it to mankind.
