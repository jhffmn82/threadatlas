Demeter’s holy grain is sown in ready soil, guarded from birds, becomes full corn-ears with Zeus’s good result, then is winnowed, measured, jarred, and kept indoors.

(ll. 383-404) When the Pleiades, daughters of Atlas, are rising 1310,

Grain should be sown when the soil is ready and guarded from birds. It is to be winnowed on a smooth threshing-floor, measured, stored in jars, and kept safely indoors.
