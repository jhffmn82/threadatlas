Syra in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Syra is an island above Ortygia, divided into two communities ruled by Ctesius son of Ormenus; its fertile soil supports cattle, sheep, wine, and wheat, and its inhabitants suffer neither famine nor sickness.

Syra is described as an island lying above Ortygia. It has good soil, pasture for cattle and sheep, wine and wheat, two communities, and inhabitants who are said to suffer neither famine nor sickness.
