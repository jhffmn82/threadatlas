Abstract written by a model

Titanomachy, ascribed to Eumelus of Corinth and Arctinus of Miletus, begins with Theogony and recounts a ten-year, initially balanced war between the Titans and Cronos's gods, ending when Zeus, Cottus, Gyes, and Briareos defeat the Titans.

Titanomachy is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Titanomachy lasts ten full years as the Titans and the gods born from Cronos fight for victory. Zeus's thunderbolts and the assaults of Cottus, Gyes, and Briareos finally turn the battle against the Titans.
