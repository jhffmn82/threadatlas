In Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge, unstructured text documents are the type of material most existing retrieval-augmented generation work has focused on retrieving. In HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases, text documents serve as a reference source for answering questions, narrowing the search space, and providing later batches when an initial reference is not useful.
