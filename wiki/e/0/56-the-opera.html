the Opera in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

The Opera, associated with Sir William Davenant, presents the second part of The Siege of Rhodes and The Witts. It began in late June 1661; on July 2, a delayed performance featured magnificent scenery and generally good acting despite falling dust from a broken board.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

JULY 1661 July 1st.

Sir William Davenant's Opera had begun four days earlier when the diarist attended it on July 2nd. The performance was delayed while waiting for the King and the Queen of Bohemia, and a broken board caused dust to fall on the ladies and men. The scene was described as very fine and magnificent, and the acting was generally good.
