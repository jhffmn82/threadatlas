the land-purchase plan in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

A proposed land purchase: the diarist has his father offer £600 immediately and £150 annually until completion, aiming for £50 annual income while appearing able to save more than his actual £500.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The diarist plans to have his father present the purchase as an offer of £600 in ready money followed by £150 per year until the price is complete. He wants Robert to think he is a greater saver than he really is, since he has no more than £500 in ready money.
