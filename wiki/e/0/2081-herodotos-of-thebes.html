Abstract written by a model

Herodotos of Thebes, son of Asopodoros and linked with Orchomenos, wins a chariot race by driving his four-horsed car unaided; honored for this victory, he seeks further athletic wreaths, fame, and honor for Thebes.

Herodotos of Thebes is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Isthmian Ode I

Herodotos is honored for winning a chariot race with his four-horsed car while handling the reins himself, without assistance. He is praised as one who may yet win wreaths from Pytho, Alpheos, and the Olympian games and bring honor to Thebes.
