Arabic sentiment analysis has been studied on social media platforms such as Twitter and Facebook. Comparative experiments using GloVe, Word2Vec, FastText, and ARBERT with BiLSTM and CNN architectures found that contextualized transformer-based BERT embeddings achieved the highest performance in general.
