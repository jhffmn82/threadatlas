JUNE, JULY & AUGUST 1661 June 1st.

Sir W. Pen accompanied the diarist by water to Woolwich and Deptford on June 1 to inspect ships and give orders concerning their readiness to go forth. He later attended the Navy Office, went bowling with Sir R. Slingsby, and returned home with the diarist.

opening

Sir W. Pen accompanied the writer in his coach to Moorefields, where they walked and watched wrestling between north and west countrymen.

opening

Sir W. Pen joins Pepys and Sir W. Batten in reporting the Navy’s financial condition to the Duke of York. He later takes Pepys and Captain Ferrers from the Theatre, visits the Dolphin, and helps take Captain Ferrers home.
