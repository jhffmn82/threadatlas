Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the woman is criticized for charging for mutual sexual pleasure, hiring out her nights, and profiting from what both partners desire, though she may properly request gifts from a wealthy person and a poor lover should give whatever he has, including service, devotion, faithfulness, and poetry. In The Amores, or Amours, the woman is portrayed as selling sexual pleasure, gaining property through embraces, and prostituting beauty for profit; gifts from wealthy men are permitted, but bargaining payment for favors is condemned.
