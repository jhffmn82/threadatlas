Abstract written by a model

Rating semantics are a goal of the retrieval and generation process. Pseudo-labels can discourage retrieving references with ratings unlike the ground truth, although such references may still provide useful guidance for other aspects. Amazon Book review rating semantics are not fully captured by lexical similarity. The experiments show that explicitly incorporating rating accuracy helps generated reviews preserve the intended rating meaning.
