Mombi's transformations

transformation event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: Mombi's transformation spell, incantation, the art, the transformation, transformations, turned King Kinda to a collar button, turned Ozma to a boy

Abstract written by a model

The Marvelous Land of Oz describes Mombi's transformation spell changing infant Princess Ozma into the boy Tip and later reversing the change with a potion, burning herbs, magical powder, an incantation, and “Yeowa!” The Lost King of Oz describes Mombi's transformations changing Pajuka from an elegant man into a goose, Ozma into the boy Tip, and King Kinda Jolly into an unknown form.
