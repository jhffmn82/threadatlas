Mombi's transformation spell in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Mombi's transformation spell changed infant Princess Ozma into the boy Tip, who was raised as Tip; Mombi later reversed it with a potion, burning herbs, magical powder, an incantation, and “Yeowa!”

Princess Ozma of Oz

Mombi's transformation spell changed the infant Princess Ozma into a boy and caused her to be raised as Tip. Mombi later performs the spell's reversal by using a potion, burning herbs and magical powder, chanting an incantation, and crying “Yeowa!”

Mombi's transformations in The Lost King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1925

Mombi transformed Pajuka from an elegant man into a goose and transformed Ozma into the boy Tip. She also transformed King Kinda Jolly, although neither she nor Pajuka knows what form he now has.
