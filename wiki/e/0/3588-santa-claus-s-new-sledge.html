Abstract written by a model

Santa Claus's new sledge is a large, strong vehicle for swift travel and carrying toys on his yearly ride. Larger and better suited than his old sledge, it has curled steel runners with wheels inside, a high rounded dashboard and sides, and space for three sacks plus sledge-box toys. Though initially too heavy for two deer, it is pulled by ten reindeer over land and water.

11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

Santa Claus's new sledge is larger, stronger, and better suited for swift travel than his old one. It has curled steel runners from the Gnome King, a high rounded dashboard, high sides, and enough room for three full sacks of toys and additional toys in the sledge-box. The deer first consider it too large and heavy for two of them, so Santa Claus uses a team of ten.
