Laestrygonian attack

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: They threw vast rocks at us, death cries of my men, killing my men, shipwreck of Odysseus's fleet, speared them like fishes, τὰ μὲν σκάφη κατέαξαν

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, the Laestrygonian attack destroys all ships except Ulysses's ship. In The Library, the shipwreck of Odysseus's fleet is caused by the Laestrygones, who destroy the rest of the fleet.
