destroys ships [all ships except Ulysses's ship] The Laestrygonian attack begins when Antiphates kills one of Ulysses's men and summons the others. The Laestrygonians hurl rocks from the cliffs, crush the ships in the harbor, and kill the crews, while Ulysses cuts his cable and rows away with the surviving men.
