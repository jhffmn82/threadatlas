Continental Congress in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

sought_to_induce French inhabitants of Canada [to join the American revolutionary cause in 1775] The Continental Congress sought to induce the French Canadians to join hands with the American revolutionaries. Its emissaries Franklin, Chase, and Carroll made eloquent appeals, but the mass of the people resisted them.

{1} CHAPTER I CANADIANS, OLD AND NEW The conquest of Canada by British arms in the Seven Years' War gave rise to a situation in the colony which was fraught with tragic possibilities.

The Continental Congress sought to induce the French Canadians to join hands with the American revolutionaries. Its emissaries Franklin, Chase, and Carroll made eloquent appeals, but the mass of the people resisted them.
