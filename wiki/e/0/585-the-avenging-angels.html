Abstract written by a model

The Avenging Angels are a secret, sinister organization linked to Mormon authorities, feared for violence, murders, disappearances, and persecuting dissenters under religion. They pursue Jefferson Hope, John Ferrier, and Lucy, separating the fugitives; John dies, Lucy is forced back to Salt Lake City, and Hope follows their trail.

CHAPTER III.

The Danite Band, also called the Avenging Angels, is identified as a secret and sinister organization associated with murders, violence, disappearances, and the persecution of opponents of the Mormon authorities.

CHAPTER V.

The avenging angels are the pursuers whose track Jefferson Hope follows back toward Salt Lake City after discovering John Ferrier's grave and Lucy's capture. Their pursuit separates the fugitives and leads to John's death and Lucy's forced return.
