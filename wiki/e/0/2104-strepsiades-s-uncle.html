Strepsiades's uncle

Abstract written by a model

Strepsiades’s uncle, also named Strepsiades, is Diodotos’s warrior son. He fights at the front, is praised with Meleagros, Hektor, and Amphiaraos, and dies in battle when Ares gives him over to death; valor earns enduring renown.

Isthmian Ode VI

Strepsiades’s uncle, also named Strepsiades and identified as the son of Diodotos, dies after bronze-shielded Ares gives him over to death in battle. He is praised alongside Meleagros, Hektor, and Amphiaraos for fighting at the front, and his renown remains as a recompense for his valor.
