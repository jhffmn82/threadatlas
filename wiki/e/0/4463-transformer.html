  • In "Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training," Transformer is a neural-based supervised keyphrase generation approach that outperforms Copy-RNN by a large margin in multilingual results.
  • In "RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation," Transformer-based encoders capture long-range dependencies and contextual information in code diffs through multi-head self-attention, relative position embeddings, feed-forward networks, and add-and-norm operations.
  • In "Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory," Transformer is a building block for joint-encoder and dual-encoder generator architectures and a randomly initialized, trainable translation model; with self-memory, it improves both architectures over retrieval-memory versions across reported JRC-Acquis directions.
  • In "SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation," Transformer is described as a stack of decoder models in the mainstream LLM architecture, with each decoder processing hidden representations through attention and feed-forward sub-layers.
