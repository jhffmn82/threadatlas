Abstract written by a model

A yellow castle with no road to Emerald City, it first confines Dorothy, who is forced to work, and the Cowardly Lion, who is fenced in after their companions are captured. After the Witch melts away, it becomes a free, celebratory refuge where the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow are restored before the group leaves.

Chapter XII

The castle of the Wicked Witch is where Dorothy is taken after the Winged Monkeys capture her companions. Dorothy is made to clean the kitchen and perform other work there, while the Cowardly Lion is confined in a fenced courtyard. Dorothy becomes free to leave the castle after the Witch melts away.

Chapter XIII

The Yellow Castle becomes a place of freedom and celebration for the Winkies and a refuge for Dorothy and her friends. The Tin Woodman is repaired there, and the Scarecrow is restored there before the group leaves.
