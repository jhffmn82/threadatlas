Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

LUCIDITY.

The Salvation Army is presented as a movement displaying numbers, funds, energy, devotedness, excitement, and conversions, but a total absence of lucidity. Arnold says that a little lucidity would make the movement impossible because it assumes a basis that is no longer possible or receivable.
