Abstract written by a model

The immortals are supernatural rulers of the earth and servants of the world, subject to Ak. After Claus leaves Burzee, they direct him toward the Laughing Valley, where friendly beings support him; they favor his plan and befriend him for serving children. Though they fear and avoid the Awgwas, their friendship protects Claus through Fairy rescue and guarding Seals. In old age, they gather in Burzee, unanimously grant Santa Claus immortality, and bestow the Mantle upon him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

MANHOOD / 1. The Laughing Valley

The immortals appear to have directed Claus's steps toward the Laughing Valley after he left Burzee. The Knooks, Fairies, Ryls, and other friendly beings support him there.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The immortals fear and avoid the Awgwas, although the Awgwas are visible to them. The immortals' friendship protects Claus: the Fairies rescue him from the mountain cave, and their Seals guard his dwelling.

OLD AGE / 1. The Mantle of Immortality

The immortals gather in Burzee to consider Santa Claus's claim to immortality. Every immortal votes to give him the Mantle, and they carry it from its crypt and bestow it upon him.
