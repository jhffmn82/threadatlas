Madame Charpentier in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Madame Charpentier, a widowed boarding-house proprietor and mother of Arthur and Alice, reports Drebber’s offensive behavior toward Alice and Arthur’s pursuit of Drebber after confronting him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Madame Charpentier runs the boarding establishment where Enoch J. Drebber and Joseph Stangerson stayed. She tells Tobias Gregson that Drebber behaved offensively toward her daughter and that Arthur followed Drebber after a confrontation, but she cannot say when Arthur returned home.
