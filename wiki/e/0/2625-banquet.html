banquet

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: coena, epulas, feast, meal, same banquet, table, the husband-wife banquet

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the husband-wife banquet brings the speaker, Corinna, and Corinna’s husband together and provides the setting for the speaker to secretly direct Corinna through touches, looks, gestures, cups, and wine while forbidding affection for her husband. In The Amores, or Amours, the banquet is attended by the speaker, the mistress, and her husband and provides the setting for secret signs, shared wine, avoiding detection, and touching as they walk in the crowd.

P. OVIDI NASONIS AMORES / LIBER PRIMUS

The husband-wife banquet brings the speaker, the beloved woman, and her husband together. The speaker gives the woman covert instructions involving touches, looks, gestures, cups, wine, and the crowd, while warning her not to show affection to her husband.
