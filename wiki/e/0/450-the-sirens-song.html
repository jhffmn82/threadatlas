the Sirens' song

Abstract written by a model

The Sirens' song is exceptionally beautiful, sweet, and charming, promising knowledge of past and future events across the world. It makes listeners wise but causes death when heard too closely. Ulysses hears it only while bound to a mast as his crew stop their ears, row faster, and tighten his bonds until the ship passes beyond hearing.

BOOK XII

The Sirens' song is so sweet that it warbles those who hear it to death. Ulysses may listen if his crew stop their ears and bind him upright to the mast, tightening his bonds if he begs to be released.
