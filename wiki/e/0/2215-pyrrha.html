Pyrrha

person · 4 works · 15 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: daughter of Epimetheus, descended from Titan, she, sister, the woman, wife, woman surviving, Πύρρα, Πύρραν

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Pyrrha comes down from Parnassos with Deukalion, establishes a home at Protogeneia, and helps make a people from stones. In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Pyrrha survives the Deluge with Deucalion, throws stones behind her, and helps restore humankind by renewing the female race. In The Library, Pyrrha is Deucalion’s wife who survives the Flood and creates women from thrown stones. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Pyrrha is a faithful worshipper of the gods who survives the flood and casts stones that become women.

Pyrrha in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · about AD 8 · the work’s page · read it where Pyrrha first appears

Abstract written by a model

Pyrrha, a woman descended from the Titans, is Epimetheus’s daughter and Deucalion’s wife. More regardful of the deities than any other woman, she survives the Deluge with Deucalion and reaches Parnassus as its only saved woman. They pray, grieve as the apparent sole survivors, and receive a response. Pyrrha first refuses to cast what she believes are her mother’s bones, but, after Deucalion explains, she throws stones behind her. Her stones renew the female race, helping restore humankind.

opening

Pyrrha is the only woman saved from the deluge and reaches Parnassus with Deucalion. She is described as more regardful of the deities than any other woman. Deucalion addresses her as his sister and wife and grieves at the thought of their being the only survivors.

opening

Pyrrha prays with Deucalion and initially refuses to cast what she thinks are the bones of her mother. After Deucalion explains the response, she joins him in throwing stones, and the female race is renewed through her throwing.

notes .. notes

Pyrrha was the wife of Deucalion and the daughter of his uncle, Epimetheus. She was descended from the Titans because her grandfather, Iapetus, was the son of Titan and Terra.
