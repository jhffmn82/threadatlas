Abstract written by a model

In "Active Retrieval Augmented Generation," multihop question answering involves answering complex questions through information retrieval and reasoning, with an evaluation setting that generates both chain-of-thought reasoning and a final answer. In "DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models," multihop question answering is evaluated using 2WikiMultihopQA and HotpotQA, and DRAGIN shows more substantial performance improvements on these tasks than on StrategyQA. In "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," MultiHop QA is evaluated on two QA tasks simultaneously using GPT, with token-level F1 and Exact Match scores. In "Fact, Fetch, and Reason: A Unified Evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation," complex, multi-hop reasoning tasks are presented as challenging scenarios in FRAMES, which LLMs handle despite existing gaps in their ability; even advanced LLMs struggle significantly with them. In "SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation," complex QA requires multi-hop reasoning and multiple supporting knowledge pieces and is evaluated on 2WikiMultiHopQA, HotpotQA, and answerable IIRC.
