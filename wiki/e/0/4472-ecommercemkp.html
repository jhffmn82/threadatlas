EcommerceMKP in Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training

Yifan Gao, Qingyu Yin, Zheng Li, Rui Meng, Tong Zhao, Bing Yin, Irwin King, Michael R. Lyu · 2022

EcommerceMKP is a multilingual e-commerce keyphrase-generation dataset spanning German, Spanish, French, and Italian, with about 73,000 product examples. It uses product titles, descriptions, and bullet descriptions as source input; target keyphrases come from purchase-producing user search queries, are chunked with AutoPhrase, ranked by frequency, and threshold-filtered. It totals 62,368 training, 3,665 development, and 7,346 test examples. Passages average 161 in length and nine keyphrases, while 56.4% of keyphrases are absent. Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

EcommerceMKP is one of two new datasets contributed for multilingual keyphrase generation. It covers languages within the paper's six-language dataset collection.

Introduction

EcommerceMKP is a multilingual keyphrase dataset collected from a major real-world e-commerce website. It uses product descriptions as source texts and user search queries as target keyphrases, containing 73,000 examples in Spanish, German, Italian, and French.

Model .. 4.3

The EcommerceMKP Dataset contains German, Spanish, French, and Italian data, with 62,368 training examples, 3,665 development examples, and 7,346 test examples in total. Its passages have an average length of 161 and an average of nine keyphrases, while 56.4% of keyphrases are absent.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It contains 73k product examples in German, French, Spanish, and Italian. Product titles, descriptions, and bullet descriptions are used as source input, while keyphrases are selected from purchase-producing search queries, chunked with AutoPhrase, ranked by frequency, and filtered by a threshold.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

EcommerceMKP is used to report main results, retrieval performance, pseudo parallel passage mining, iterative-training effects, and a German product-description case study. On its DEV set, RAMKG + RGIT improves Recall@5 from 50.1% to 72.4%, and generation performance improves from 44.50 to 45.73.
