Roger Pepys in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Roger Pepys, a sober man who succeeded Talbot Pepys as Recorder of Cambridge in 1660, welcomes Pepys from Cambridge to Impington, discusses difficulties over an uncle's will, and fears Parliament's profane young men may obstruct serious proposals and cause renewed war.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Roger Pepys accompanies Pepys from Cambridge to Impington and gives him a respectful welcome. He explains the expected difficulties concerning the uncle's will and later discusses his serious concerns about Parliament, young men's opposition to serious business, profanity, and the possibility of renewed war.
