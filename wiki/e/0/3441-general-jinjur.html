Queen Jinjur in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06 · the work’s page · read it where Queen Jinjur first appears

Abstract written by a model

Queen Jinjur, formerly General Jinjur of the Army of Revolt, leads a revolt and becomes the usurping queen of the Emerald City. With Mombi as her chief counsellor, she takes the throne from the Scarecrow and her followers rob the city of its emeralds. Facing Glinda the Good’s army, Jinjur fears defeat, considers surrender, then follows Mombi’s deceptive plan and opens the city to Glinda’s search. She loses her throne and palace, refuses to submit to Glinda and Princess Ozma, is captured in the courtyard, and is released after promising good behavior.

General Jinjur in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06 · the work’s page · read it where General Jinjur first appears

Abstract written by a model

General Jinjur is a girl, general, and self-proclaimed Queen of the Emerald City. She commands the Army of Revolt to overthrow the Scarecrow, take the city’s gems and treasury, and rule it herself. Leading the army to the city, she takes the Guardian of the Gate’s keys and directs the city’s capture. She occupies the throne in Jinjur’s palace, wearing the Scarecrow’s second-best crown, holding the royal sceptre, and claiming the throne belongs to whoever can take it. When the Scarecrow escapes, she fears for her army and summons Mombi, offering rewards to help defeat him and the Tin Woodman. After capturing the Scarecrow’s party, she orders most prisoners destroyed and threatens others with becoming food. Mice frighten her from the palace, but after an unsuccessful effort against her, she remains Queen, guarded by girl soldiers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

General Jinjur commands the Army of Revolt and plans to overthrow the Scarecrow, seize the Emerald City’s gems and treasury, and govern the city herself. She leads the army to the city, takes the Guardian of the Gate’s keys, and directs the capture of the Emerald City.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.

General Jinjur, who commanded the Army of Revolt, becomes uneasy when the Scarecrow escapes from the Emerald City. She summons Mombi and promises her large rewards for helping the rebel army defeat the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman.

The Prisoners of the Queen

General Jinjur sits in the Scarecrow's throne wearing his second-best crown and holding the royal sceptre, claiming that the throne belongs to whoever can take it. After the Scarecrow's party is captured, she orders most of them destroyed, but mice frighten her into fleeing the palace and continuing to the city gates.

The Scarecrow Takes Time to Think

Jinjur continues to claim the throne and remains Queen after the group’s failed attempt to defeat her. She surrounds the palace with girl soldiers and has threatened to make tarts, soup, or goulash from members of the group.

Jinjur in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918 · the work’s page · read it where Jinjur first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jinjur, a generous, honest, sensible girl farmer, owns a home and treats, supports Woot sympathetically, then mistakes transformed travelers for animals, attacks them, apologizes on learning who they are, welcomes, cleans, and feeds Woot.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eleven

Jinjur returns home and attacks the transformed travelers with a broom because she believes they are wild animals. After learning their identities, she apologizes, welcomes them, and scrubs Woot after he becomes covered in soot. She later invites him to eat her special heliotrope-flavored caramels.
