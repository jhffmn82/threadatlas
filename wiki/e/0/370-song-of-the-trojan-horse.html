song of the Trojan horse

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Demodocus's song, his lays, his song, song, the story, the wooden horse

Abstract written by a model

A song by Demodocus describes the wooden horse and Troy’s sack. It tells of Achaeans sailing away or hiding inside the horse, Trojans bringing it into their fortress, and Achaeans emerging to destroy Troy, including Ulysses and Menelaus fighting at Deiphobus’s house; it makes the guest lament.
