Abstract written by a model

A journey toward the Emerald City by Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse follows a yellow-brick road. They begin with the city nine miles away and camp as darkness approaches. After crossing a broad river, the Saw-Horse’s speed separates Tip; Jack and the Saw-Horse reach the city and are escorted to the royal palace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Awakening of the Saw-horse

Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse begin traveling toward the Emerald City. Their path becomes a broad roadway paved with yellow brick, and a sign states that the Emerald City is nine miles away. Because darkness is approaching, Tip stops the journey and camps beside the road.

Jack Pumpkinhead’s Ride to the Emerald City

The journey continues over a road of yellow brick after Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse cross a broad river. The Saw-Horse’s great speed separates Tip from the others, but Jack and the Saw-Horse reach the Emerald City and are escorted to the royal palace.
