Abstract written by a model

The first toy is a wooden cat carved by Claus from a stick of firewood on a long winter evening, modeled after Blinkie with a head, body, haunches, tail, ears, eyes, and nose. It is painted black, with red lips and tongue, green eyes, yellow, and blue on its wooden ribbon. Claus is pleased, Blinkie reacts to it, Weekum likes its harmlessness, and a colored toy cat comforts a sick girl.

2. How Claus Made the First Toy

The first toy is a wooden cat that Claus carves from a stick of firewood during a long winter evening. He shapes it with a cat's head, body, haunches, tail, ears, eyes, and nose, modeling it after Blinkie. The completed toy pleases Claus and amuses him when Blinkie reacts to it.

3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

The toy cat is a wooden imitation of a real cat. Weekum likes it because it does not run away, scratch, or bite, and a colored toy cat later comforts a sick girl by easing her fever and pain.
