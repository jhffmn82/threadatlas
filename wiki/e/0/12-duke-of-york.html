Duke of York in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

The Duke of York receives reports that the office lacks money, supplies, and credit, and meets the speaker and Sir Williams at Whitehall to identify why ships were not sent out. Troubled by the Navy’s financial crisis, he orders a report on the ships and travels on the King’s yacht.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The office is to receive a letter informing him of its lack of money, its inability to obtain further supplies, and the low state of its credit.

opening

The Duke of York met the speaker and Sir Williams at Whitehall to receive an account of where the fault lay in the failure to send out the ships.
