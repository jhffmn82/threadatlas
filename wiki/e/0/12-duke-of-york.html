Duke of York

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Duke, the Duke, the Duke of York

Abstract written by a model

The Duke of York receives reports that the office lacks money, supplies, and credit, and meets the speaker and Sir Williams at Whitehall to identify why ships were not sent out. Troubled by the Navy’s financial crisis, he orders a report on the ships and travels on the King’s yacht.
