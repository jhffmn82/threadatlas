flood

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: all was sea, common danger, drown the world, flood of Zeus, mighty water
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, the flood is a mighty flood that swept over the dark earth until Zeus caused the waters to ebb, leaving survivors whose descendants became the ancestors of the people described in the ode.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the flood is a means of destroying the world, covering the earth and sweeping away people, animals, houses, temples, and crops, with only a few survivors and Parnassus above the waves.
