Abstract written by a model

Herodotos’ four-horsed car wins victory, is honored by Pindar, and is driven unaided by Herodotos; Poseidon grants its speed.

Isthmian Ode I

Herodotos’ four-horsed car is honored in song for its victory. Herodotos handles its reins with no helping hand, and Pindar attributes its speed to Poseidon.
