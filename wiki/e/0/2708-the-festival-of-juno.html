Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY XIII.

The festival is prepared by priestesses and includes distinguished games and a heifer from the country. Its procession features animal victims, youths and girls who sweep the roads, attendants bearing sacred vessels, and the silent participation of the people.
