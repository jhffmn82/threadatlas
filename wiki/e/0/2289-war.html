Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, war is figured as a sign and cloud of danger, bringing bloody conflict but also valor, defense, counsel, and renown. In The Aeneid, warfare encompasses siege fighting, naval movement, amphibious landings, spear attacks, and close combat between courageous armies.

war in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where war first appears

Abstract written by a model

War is a struggle of fighters, figured as a sign and a cloud of danger. It brings bloody conflict against enemy hosts, but valor in horse and foot battles, defense of country, and later counsel win boundless renown for Hieron and fellow citizens, even after death.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

II.

War and military valor are presented as grounds for Hieron's boundless renown. He is praised for victories in battles fought both with horsemen and on foot, while the counsels of his older years provide further cause for praise.

Isthmian Ode VI

War is portrayed as a cloud from which a warrior wards off a bloody shower while destroying the enemy host. A warrior who defends his country increases the renown of his fellow citizens even after death.
