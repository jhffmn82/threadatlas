The will dispute produces negotiations among Samuel Pepys, his father, the Trices, Mr. Greene, and others. They agree that Samuel’s aunt will leave the house in return for £10, while other matters will be left to the law; Samuel also seeks counsel about debts, surrenders, the heir at law, and the forthcoming assizes.
