Abstract written by a model

ASQA-hint is an open-domain summarization setting that gives language models a brief hint for answering ambiguous questions, using ASQA’s long-form QA metrics. FLARE achieves the best reported results; generic hints especially improve staying on topic when ambiguous aspects are hard to identify.

5 .. Open-domain summarization

ASQA-hint is a setting in which a brief hint is provided to guide language models when generating answers to ambiguous questions. It uses the same long-form QA metrics described for ASQA.

6 .. 6.2

FLARE achieves the best reported results on ASQA-hint, with 45.9 EM, 35.7 Disambig-F1, 37.5 ROUGE-L, and 36.6 DR. The improvement over ASQA is larger because a generic hint helps language models stay on topic when ambiguous aspects are difficult to identify.
