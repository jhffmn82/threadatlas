Lying Dream in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

A dream sent by Jove, appearing as Nestor, visits Agamemnon and falsely tells him to arm the Achaeans because Troy will fall, leaving him certain of victory that day.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK II.

The Lying Dream goes to Agamemnon's tent in the likeness of Nestor son of Neleus and tells him that Jove commands the Achaeans to arm themselves because Troy will fall. It leaves Agamemnon believing that he will take Priam's city that same day.
