Abstract written by a model

Retriever-generator iterative training is an iterative-training method for keyphrase generation that automatically mines pseudo training parallel passage pairs from unlabeled data to address scarce parallel passage-keyphrase data. It begins with a small set of initial parallel pairs, including robust results with 1.2k pairs, trains a retriever and a retrieval-augmented generator, and uses generation feedback to retain retrieved English passages whose keyphrases improve F1 beyond a threshold. It repeats retriever training, generator training, and mining, producing over 20,000 pseudo-parallel pairs in experiments. Stronger retrieval finds more relevant English keyphrases and improves retrieval and generation, though gains diminish as annotated parallel data increases. Adding RGIT to RAMKG improves reported EcommerceMKP and AcademicMKP scores.

Introduction

Retriever-generator iterative training automatically mines pseudo training parallel pairs from unlabeled data to address the scarcity of parallel passage-keyphrase pairs. It uses generation performance as feedback to retain useful pseudo-parallel data and repeatedly improve the retriever, mining more than 20,000 pseudo-parallel passage pairs in the reported experiments.

Model .. 4.3

Retriever-generator iterative training alternates between training a retriever, training a retrieval-augmented generator, and mining pseudo-parallel passage pairs. A retrieved English passage is retained as a pseudo-parallel example when its associated keyphrases improve keyphrase-generation F1 over a base generator by more than a threshold.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It denotes retriever-generator iterative training. Adding RGIT to RAMKG improves the reported average EcommerceMKP scores to 49.86 precision, 45.81 recall, and 45.73 F1, and the AcademicMKP scores to 32.31, 30.92, and 31.04.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

Retriever-generator iterative training alleviates data scarcity by using a stronger retriever to find more relevant English keyphrases. Its retrieval and generation benefits are robust with limited parallel data, although the benefit diminishes as more annotated parallel data becomes available.
