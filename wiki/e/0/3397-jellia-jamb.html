Jellia Jamb in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Jellia Jamb, born in the North Country, is a pretty, sweet, modest interpreter with green eyes, hair, and pea-pod-, clover-, and emerald-decorated clothing. Presented to His Majesty the Scarecrow to interpret Gillikin, she reveals the languages are the same but mischievously mistranslates between him and Jack Pumpkinhead. Later Mombi enchants her into Mombi’s appearance; Glinda exposes the trick, restores Jellia, and protects her.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

His Majesty the Scarecrow

Jellia Jamb is brought to His Majesty the Scarecrow as a supposed interpreter because she was born in the North Country and is believed to understand the language of the Gillikins. She reveals that the Gillikins' language and the language of the Emerald City are the same, while mischievously mistranslating remarks between the Scarecrow and Jack.

The Tin-Woodman Plucks a Rose

Jellia is enchanted to take on Mombi’s appearance and is delivered to Glinda as a supposed prisoner. Glinda exposes the deception and restores Jellia to her proper form, after which Jellia explains Mombi’s trick and asks for protection.
