Psaumis

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Psaumis'

Abstract written by a model

Psaumis, son of Akron, wins the mule-chariot race at Olympia and receives Pisan olive and an Olympian crown. His victory brings glory to Kamarina. Celebrated for his untiring car and zeal in rearing horses, he honors his father and city through sacrifices and contests. Hospitable, peace-loving, devoted to his city’s welfare, and guileless, he seeks good repute and sufficient wealth; a serene old age with his sons is prayed for.

Psaumis is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

IV.

Psaumis won the mule-chariot-race at Olympia in 452 and was crowned with Pisan olive. He is praised as zealous in rearing horses, hospitable, peace-loving, devoted to Kamarina's welfare, and guileless.

notes

Psaumis is described as probably having grey hair.

V.

Psaumis is celebrated as the winner of the mule-chariot race and as the owner of an untiring car. He honors Kamarina through sacrifices and contests, glorifies his father Akron and the city, and is prayed for a serene old age with his sons.
