Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXIII

The olive-tree bed was made by Ulysses from the stump of an olive tree that stood within the bedroom. He shaped the stump into the central bed-post, inlaid it with gold and silver, and covered the bed with crimson leather. Penelope uses its immovable construction to test whether the man before her is truly Ulysses.
