Lord Hinchingbroke in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Lord Hinchingbroke, Lady Sandwich’s son, becomes gravely ill and is feared to have smallpox, then steadily improves until sitting up and walking about his chamber.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Lord Hinchingbroke becomes very ill and is feared to have smallpox after Pepys gives fruit to his family. He improves over the following days, eventually sitting up and walking about his chamber.
