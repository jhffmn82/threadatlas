Abstract written by a model

Early Greek epic is poetry that slowly developed metre, diction, and structure from crude beginnings; its uncertain earliest phase gave way to maturity in the Iliad and Odyssey, then declined into Homeric romance or practical, didactic forms.

INTRODUCTION

Early Greek epic passed through phases of development, maturity, and decline. Its earliest period is known only through analogy and inference, while the mature period produced the Iliad and the Odyssey; afterward, epic became either a conventional continuation of Homeric romance or a practical and didactic form.
