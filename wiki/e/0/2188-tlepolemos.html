Tlepolemos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Tlepolemos, son of Herakles and a Herakleidai, accidentally killed Likymnios in anger, then, after an oracle, led Tirynthian colonization of Rhodes and was honored as its tutelary hero.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.

Tlepolemos, regarded as the founder of Rhodes and leader of the Tirynthians, accidentally slew Likymnios in Tiryns during a fit of anger. After consulting an oracle, he was associated with the colonization of Rhodes and received honor in games as the island's tutelary hero.
