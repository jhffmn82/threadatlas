overall model score in Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation

Hazem Abdelazim · 2023

Overall model score measures the accuracy-efficiency trade-off: average Recall@k × 1000 divided by embedding dimension. It combines retrieval accuracy and dimensions, favoring E5-ML-small because its strong accuracy is paired with a 384-dimensional embedding.

V. / A. ARCD Dataset .. C. Embedding Dimensions and Model Score

The model score is calculated as average Recall@k multiplied by 1000 and divided by embedding dimension. This score favors the E5-ML-small model because it combines strong retrieval accuracy with a 384-dimensional embedding.
