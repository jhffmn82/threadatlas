THE PRECEPTS OF CHIRON

The Precepts of Chiron contains precepts about sacrificing to the eternal gods upon returning home and hearing both sides before deciding a suit. It also includes a lifespan comparison involving crows, stags, ravens, phoenixes, and the rich-haired Nymphs. Earlier writers attributed it to Hesiod, but Aristophanes was the first to reject it as a work of Hesiod.
