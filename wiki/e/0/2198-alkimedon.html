Alkimedon in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Alkimedon, a beautiful boy of the Blepsiad clan, wins Olympic wrestling in 460 BC. Trained by Melesias, he proclaims Aigina, brings family glory, and renews his grandfather’s strength; Zeus guards his house. He is linked to Iphion, Kallimachos, and Timosthenes.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.

Alkimedon is presented as a beautiful boy who won the Olympic wrestling match without bringing shame upon his appearance. By winning, he proclaimed Aigina as his homeland, gained glory for his family, and renewed his grandfather’s strength in old age.
