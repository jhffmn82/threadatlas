1 section; each heading opens the text

THE THEBAID

The Thebaid was described as an epic of seven thousand verses, beginning with a song of parched Argos. It tells of Oedipus's curses against his sons, Adrastus's flight from Thebes, the burial of seven dead, and the battle in which Parthenopaeus was killed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IX.

The epic poem called the Thebais concerns the war of the Epigoni against Thebes. Callinus said that Homer wrote it, but the text considers it later than the Iliad and Odyssey.
