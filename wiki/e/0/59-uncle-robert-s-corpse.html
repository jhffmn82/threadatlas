Uncle Robert’s corpse, placed in a coffin on joint-stools in the hall at Brampton, begins to smell and is moved to the yard overnight under watch before burial in church. His will, papers, and estate become central business: money, debts, Sturtlow, and an uncertain inheritance for Samuel Pepys and others.
