Ios

Also called: Isle of Ios, the island Ios

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Ios is identified as Homer’s mother’s country and the place where he traveled, died, and was buried. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Ios is identified as the island that would receive Homer’s remains and is said to contain Homer’s tomb and the tomb of Clymene, Homer’s mother.
