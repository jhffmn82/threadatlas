Ios

place · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Isle of Ios, the island Ios
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Ios is identified as Homer’s mother’s country and the place where he traveled, died, and was buried.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Ios is identified as the island that would receive Homer’s remains and is said to contain Homer’s tomb and the tomb of Clymene, Homer’s mother.

Ios in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Ios was identified by an oracle as Homer’s mother’s country and as the place that would receive him dead. Homer eventually traveled there, stayed with Creophylus, died after falling in a clayey place, and was buried there.

Ios was identified by an oracle as Homer’s mother’s country and as the place that would receive him dead. Homer eventually traveled there, stayed with Creophylus, died after falling in a clayey place, and was buried there.

Ios in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ios is identified by the oracle concerning Homer as the island that would receive Homer’s remains. Its inhabitants show Homer’s tomb and, elsewhere on the island, the tomb of Clymene, whom they say was Homer’s mother.
