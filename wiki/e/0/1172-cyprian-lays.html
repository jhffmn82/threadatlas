Cyprian Lays

Also called: _Cyprian Lays_

Abstract written by a model

The Cyprian Lays, attributed to Stasinus of Cyprus or Hegesinus of Salamis, precede the Iliad, tracing Zeus’s purpose, the apple of discord, Helen’s rape, and the Trojan War’s opening through Achilles and Agamemnon’s quarrel.

The Ionic School

The Cyprian Lays, attributed to Stasinus of Cyprus or Hegesinus of Salamis, treated the causes and opening events of the Trojan War. They began with Zeus’s purpose to relieve the earth, the apple of discord, and the rape of Helen, and continued to the quarrel between Achilles and Agamemnon.
