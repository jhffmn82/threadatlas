Abstract written by a model

Will Knook, chief guardian of the deer, initially permits Flossie and Glossie to travel with Claus if they reach Burzee by daybreak. After they return late, he denounces their disobedience, seeks punishment, and objects to the deer drawing Claus’s sledge, but obeys the Prince’s order.

10. Christmas Eve

Will Knook complains that Glossie and Flossie disobeyed his orders by staying out past daybreak and threatens to set stinging gnats on them. He objects to deer drawing Claus's sledge, but the Prince of the Knooks orders him to obey the granted permission.
