RL in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

Jihyeok Kim · 2020-12-08/2020-12-13

RL is a learning scheme for training REFLECT at the retrieval stage. It retrieves references to maximize generated-review rating accuracy and lexical similarity, emphasizing references’ effects on generation rather than input-rating matching. From candidates filtered by Coarse-Grained REFLECT, it samples a reference set, generates a review using X∗ and GEN, calculates reward from Y and Y′, estimates partial relevance through references’ reflected contributions, and adapts sampling toward maximum reward. It maximizes R−˜R rather than R; likelihood-ratio adaptation, exploration prioritization, and a baseline stabilize discrete-sampling training. With multiple collaboratively contributing references, it outperforms SL and substantially improves rating accuracy.

opening .. Augmenting context using references

RL trains a retrieval model to maximize rewards that include the rating accuracy and lexical similarity of generated reviews. It retrieves references based on their effect on generated reviews rather than simply selecting references with the input rating.

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

Reinforcement learning is proposed as an alternative way to train REFLECT. It enables the model to effectively preserve rating semantics.

RL-REFLECT .. Stabilizing Training of RL

Reinforcement learning samples a reference set, generates a review, calculates a reward from the ground-truth and generated review, and adapts sampling to maximize the reward. The likelihood-ratio trick enables gradient-based adaptation despite discrete sampling, while exploration prioritization and a baseline reduce training variance.

Experiments .. Analysis on Rating Consistency

RL is used to train REFLECT with 50 samples per batch and to optimize rating-aware relevance. The RL-based GEN-C-F model substantially improves rating accuracy over the supervised-learning version.

Reinforcement Learning in Crafting Personalized Agents through Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Editable Memory Graphs

Zheng Wang, Zhongyang Li, Zeren Jiang, Dandan Tu, Wei Shi · 2024

Reinforcement Learning further optimizes EMG-RAG, addressing data collection, editability, and selectability. It models retrieval-augmented generation by optimizing graph memory selection end to end. Selected memories prompt a frozen large language model; improved answer quality supplies rewards. Training starts with supervised warm-starting, then uses REINFORCE policy gradients, with ROUGE and BLEU as example evaluation metrics.
