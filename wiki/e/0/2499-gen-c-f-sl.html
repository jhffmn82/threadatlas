Experiments .. Analysis on Rating Consistency

GEN-C-F (SL) uses references retrieved by Coarse- and Fine-Grained REFLECT trained with supervised learning. It obtains BLEU-1 31.82, BLEU-4 5.65, and rating accuracy 78.93; removing rating accuracy from relevance estimation lowers these to 31.43, 5.60, and 75.49.
