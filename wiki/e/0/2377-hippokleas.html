Hippokleas in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Hippokleas, son of Phrikias, is a boy celebrated for inborn valour and victory in Pytho’s two-stadion foot-race. Crowned by Pytho, he is praised around Parnassos as Pindar’s song seeks to increase his glory and admiration.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

X.

Hippokleas is celebrated as the winner of the two-stadion foot-race of boys at Pytho. His victory is praised among the people around Parnassos, and Pindar hopes to make him more glorious through song and cause him to be admired by young maidens and by his peers and elders.
