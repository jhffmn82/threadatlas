Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, Achilles throws it at Hector, Minerva retrieves and returns it, and Achilles uses it to strike Hector in the throat before drawing it from his body. In The Library, Achilles uses it to wound Telephus, and scrapes it to obtain rust to heal Telephus’s wound. In The Fall of Troy, it is carried by Neoptolemus to pierce Eurypylus’ throat and kill him.
