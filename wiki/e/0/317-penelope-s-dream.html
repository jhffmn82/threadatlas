Penelope’s dream

Abstract written by a model

Penelope’s dream is a vision sent by Minerva resembling Iphthime, comforting Penelope with Telemachus’s promised return but refusing to reveal whether Ulysses lives or dies.

Penelope’s dream is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Meanwhile the suitors were throwing discs or aiming with spears at a

In Penelope’s dream, a vision resembling her sister Iphthime tells her not to grieve because Telemachus has done the gods no wrong and will return. When Penelope asks about Ulysses, the vision declines to say whether he is alive or dead.
