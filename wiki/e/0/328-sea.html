Abstract written by a model

The sea causes Ulysses’s shipwreck and twenty-day wandering, carrying him from the Ogygian island toward the Phaeacian coast. It holds him beneath a heavy swell, then calms, but its violent surf, deep water, and rocky coast nearly destroy him. It also surrounds the Phaeacians’ land, harbours, and seafaring.

The sea holds Ulysses for two nights and two days under a heavy swell before becoming calm. It surrounds a rocky coast with violent surf and deep water, and it nearly carries Ulysses away or destroys him against the rocks.

BOOK VI

The sea surrounds the Phaeacians’ land and is associated with their harbours and seafaring. Ulysses has been tossed upon it for twenty days, and the waves have carried him from the Ogygian island to the Phaeacian coast.
