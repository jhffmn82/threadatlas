Guardian of the Gates in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Guardian of the Gates is an officer and guardian who lives by the Emerald City gate, dressed entirely in green. He keeps locked green spectacles in a green box and holds their only golden key. When Dorothy and companions arrive, he warns them about Oz, fits them with the spectacles, admits them, and leads them through the city toward the Palace of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The Guardian of the Gates meets Dorothy and her companions at the Emerald City gate. He warns them that Oz may destroy those who approach dishonestly or from curiosity, fits them with locked green spectacles, and leads them through another gate toward the Palace.

Guardian of the Gates of the Emerald City in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The Guardian of the Gates of the Emerald City is a faithful gate guardian, identified by green clothing, a peaked hat, spectacles, and keys on a golden chain. He guards the gateway until General Jinjur’s army surrounds him with knitting-needles, makes him surrender, and takes his keys.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The Guardian of the Gate initially treats General Jinjur’s army as harmless visitors and tells them to go home. The girls surround him with knitting-needles, causing him to surrender and allowing General Jinjur to take his keys.
