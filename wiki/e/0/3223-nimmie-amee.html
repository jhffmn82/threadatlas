Nimmie Amee in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Nimmie Amee is a beautiful, brave Munchkin girl whose life begins under the Wicked Witch's control: she lives with the Witch and is forced to labor from morning until night. She loves Nick Chopper, the woodchopper who becomes the Tin Woodman, faithfully helping gather his severed parts for the tinsmith and hoping to marry him. Yet after becoming entirely tin, Nick no longer loves her. Nimmie Amee later waits anxiously for him, and is described as sharp-tongued when angry. She also becomes interested in Captain Fyter as he is fitted with tin parts, agrees to marry him before he rusts, and is expected to choose between him and the Tin Woodman. When the Tin Soldier does not arrive to marry her, she weeps; the Witch beats her and plans to turn her into an old hag. After the Witch is destroyed, Nimmie Amee leaves the forest for Mount Munch, taking goods from the Witch's house. In time she lives in a house enclosed by a Wall of Solid Air that she creates with a magic formula, and she is happy as mistress of her own domain. By the time the Tin Woodman and Tin Soldier find her, she has forgotten them, is happily married to Chopfyt, and chooses her quiet life with him on Mount Munch over becoming Empress of the Winkies. She prefers to be left alone.

Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter One

Nimmie Amee is a beautiful Munchkin girl who lives with the Witch of the East and is forced to work as her slave. She falls in love with the Tin Woodman when he is still a flesh-and-blood woodchopper and continues to love him after he receives tin legs and a tin arm.

Chapter Two

Nimmie Amee remains faithful to Nick Chopper despite the Wicked Witch's actions, helps collect his severed parts, and brings them to the tinsmith. Although she still loves Nick and wishes to marry him, Nick discovers that he no longer loves her after becoming completely tin.

Chapter Three

Nimmie Amee is a Munchkin girl who has been waiting a long time for the Tin Woodman to return. The Tin Woodman believes she may be angry because he has taken so long, and he says she has a sharp tongue when angry.

Chapter Fifteen

Nimmie Amee is the Tin Woodman's former sweetheart, and the Tin Woodman believes she still loves him after all these years. She once lived with a Wicked Witch who made her a slave, but after the Witch was destroyed, Nimmie Amee was expected to live alone. The Tin Woodman and his companions travel through the forest to find her and ask whether she still wishes to marry him.

Chapter Sixteen

Nimmie Amee was first in love with Nick Chopper and later became interested in Captain Fyter as he was replaced with tin parts. She agreed to marry Captain Fyter before he rusted, and she will be allowed to choose between Captain Fyter and the Tin Woodman.

Chapter Seventeen

Nimmie Amee is not at her former cottage, which has been deserted for a long time. The travelers speculate that she left to live where other people are found, and they decide to ask Ku-Klip what became of her.

Chapter Eighteen

Nimmie Amee wept when the Tin Soldier failed to come and marry her, and the Wicked Witch beat her and planned to transform her into an old hag. After the Witch was destroyed, Nimmie Amee left the forest to live with acquaintances on Mount Munch, taking some goods from the Witch's house.

Chapter Twenty-One

Nimmie Amee lives in the house surrounded by the Wall of Solid Air and is said to be quite happy rather than weeping or wailing. She created the wall using a magic formula taken from an old Witch.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Nimmie Amee welcomes the visitors but does not recognize the Tin Woodman or Tin Soldier as her former sweethearts. She explains that she married Chopfyt after the Tin men failed to return, and says she is happy as the mistress of her own domain. She refuses the Tin Woodman's offer to become the Empress of the Winkies and asks to be left alone.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Nimmie Amee is described as happily married to Chopfyt. The others learn that she is content with him and that she has chosen to live with him on far-away Mount Munch rather than become Empress of the Winkies.
