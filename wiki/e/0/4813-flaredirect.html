FLAREdirect in Active Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zhengbao Jiang · 2023

FLAREdirect is a forward-looking active retrieval augmented generation method that uses temporary next sentences as queries, retrieving and regenerating when provisional tokens are uncertain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3 .. 3.3

FLAREdirect uses the language model’s generation as search queries and iteratively generates the next sentence to assess the future topic. If the provisional sentence contains uncertain tokens, it retrieves relevant documents and regenerates the sentence.
