Tlepolemus

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Tlepolemfta, Tlepolemus, the son of Hercules, Tlepotemus, son of Hercules

Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, Tlepolemus, son of Hercules, challenged Sarpedon, boasted of his father's destruction of Ilius, wounded Sarpedon in the thigh, and died after being struck through the throat. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Tlepolemus fled Argos after killing Licymnius, went to Rhodes, divided it into three parts, built Lyndus, Jalysus, and Camirus, became king of the Rhodians, and assisted Agamemnon at Troy.
