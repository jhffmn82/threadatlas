King Charles II in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

King Charles II travels by barge to adjourn Parliament amid pressure over Presbytery and dependence on the Lord Chancellor for policy and service; he later attends Sir William Davenant's Opera and excels at stag hunting.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

King Charles II is seen traveling by barge to adjourn Parliament. Samuel hears that the King may be forced to favor Presbytery or lose the City’s support, and that although the King does not love the Lord Chancellor as a companion, he needs him for policy and service.
