British Museum in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

British Museum is an organization containing ancient Greek sculpture, notably the Payne-Knight Apollo, remnants from Apollo’s temple at Phigales, much of the Parthenon frieze, and Parthenon gable fragments purchased by Lord Elgin.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SKETCH OF THE HISTORY OF GREEK SCULPTURE

The British Museum contains the Payne-Knight Apollo, remnants of the frieze from the Temple of Apollo at Phigales, and much of the Parthenon frieze. It also holds fragments of the Parthenon’s gable groups purchased by Lord Elgin.

British Museum in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

British museum contains Egyptian pipes, a fresco fragment, cymbals, bronze bells, and a fourteenth-century manuscript; one seven-holed pipe had two possible reed-like straws.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The British museum contains Egyptian pipes, a fragment of a fresco showing two female nofre performers, a pair of cymbals, and several small bronze bells. One Egyptian pipe has seven side holes, and two straws found with it may have served a purpose like a reed.
