Euryganea

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Euryganeia
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Euryganeia was the mother of Oedipus’s sons according to the Story of Oedipus.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Euryganea is said to be the mother of Oedipus’s four children and is depicted dejected by her sons’ quarrels.

CHAPTER V.

Euryganea, the daughter of Hyperphas, was said to be the mother of Oedipus’s four children rather than Jocasta. The painter Onatas depicted her dejected by the quarrels of her sons.
