Euryganea

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Euryganeia

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Euryganeia was the mother of Oedipus’s sons according to the Story of Oedipus. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Euryganea is said to be the mother of Oedipus’s four children and is depicted dejected by her sons’ quarrels.
