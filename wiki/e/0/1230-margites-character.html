Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE MARGITES

Margites knew many things but knew them all badly, and the gods had taught him neither to dig nor to plough nor any other skill. Though well grown up, he did not know whether his father or his mother had given him birth, and he would not lie with his wife because he feared she might report badly about him to her mother.
