label-relevant passages in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023

Label-relevant passages are a biased subset of question-relevant and knowledge-relevant passages, typically sharing high token overlap with the answer label. Retrievers trained solely with negative log-likelihood favor them because they support answer labels, even when not question-relevant. This preference can miss other knowledge-relevant passages and overfit training data, motivating proposed mitigating objectives.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Label-relevant passages have high token overlap with the reference answer. Conventional RAG training prefers these passages, which can cause it to miss other knowledge-relevant passages and overfit retrieval to the training data.

Problem Statement

Label-relevant passages are a biased subset of question-relevant passages that usually have high token overlap with the label answer. They are favored by the retriever during standard training and are included within the knowledge-relevant passages.

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

A label-relevant passage is a passage favored by a retriever trained solely with negative log-likelihood because it supports the answer label without necessarily being question-relevant. The proposed objectives are introduced to mitigate this preference.
