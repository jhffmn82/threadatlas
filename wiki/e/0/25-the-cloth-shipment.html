the cloth shipment in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

The cloth shipment is delayed when the purser does not come and the Duke of York has departed, then is sent by a Margate Hoy to the Downs.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The cloth shipment is delayed when the purser of the King's yacht does not come for it and the diarist learns that the Duke of York has already departed. After considering sending it by carrier or by a vessel hired for the purpose, he sends it by a Margate Hoy.
