Abstract written by a model

In Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding, retrieval augmentation is crucial for large language models, supports complex reasoning, faces knowledge-editing challenges, and may combine passages with databases and knowledge graphs. In Ask in Any Modality, augmentation refines retrieved data before generation, improving its structuring, integration, and multimodal interpretation. In GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation, retrieval-augmented models for QA are a research branch that augments language-model inputs with relevant passages and includes query expansion and iterative retrieval-and-prompting approaches.
