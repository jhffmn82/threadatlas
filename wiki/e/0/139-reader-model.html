Abstract written by a model

In Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering, it examines retrieved documents or passages to extract or generate an answer. In Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge, it uses retrieved information to produce answers, with its input augmented by retrieved text and its representations enriched by structured reasoning. In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, it interprets passages and, conditioned on questions and passages, extracts or generates answers. In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, it generates answers for open-domain question answering and is enhanced by a generated knowledge graph. In TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, it is the target of TRACE, which is designed to improve its multi-hop reasoning ability.
