Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the dawn ends the speaker’s night with the beloved woman and wakes people to their duties. In The Amores, or Amours, morning interrupts the speaker’s sleep and pleasure with his mistress and wakes people to their duties.

the dawn in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · about 24 to 15 BC · the work’s page · read it where the dawn first appears

Abstract written by a model

The dawn ends the speaker’s night with the beloved woman even though sleep is still deep, the air cool, and the birds singing. Its arrival wakes travelers, soldiers, farmers, boys, lawyers, and women who work wool.
