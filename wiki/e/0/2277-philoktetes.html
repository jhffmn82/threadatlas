Philoktetes, an archer son of Paian, suffers an ulcerous wound while going to war, is fetched from Lemnos, sacks Priam, and ends the Danaoi’s labours.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

I.

Philoktetes is used as an example for Hieron, who is said to have gone to war in a comparable way. Although Philoktetes suffered from an ulcerous wound, he was fetched from Lemnos, sacked Priam’s city, and ended the Danaoi’s labors.
