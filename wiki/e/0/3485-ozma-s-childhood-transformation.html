Ozma's childhood transformation

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Ozma's disappearance, ran away, stolen, transformed into a boy, what became of the child, Ozma

Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, it is Ozma's disappearance, explained by Mombi's revelation that she concealed Ozma by transforming her into the boy known as Tip. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, it is Ozma's transformation from a baby girl into a boy by Mombi, after which she serves Mombi and runs away.
