Fairies

group · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: A Fairy, band of Fairies, other Fairies, sweet Fairies, the Fairies

Abstract written by a model

Fairies are immortal, powerful guardians of mankind who protect humanity unseen, governed by laws forbidding familiarity with their human charges. Just and impartial, they nevertheless aid Claus when mystic words summon them, supplying warm clothing, blankets, household and work tools, and silks, laces, and golden threads for the Bessie image dolly. Later, when the Awgwas steal toys, the Fairies search the mountains, pass through rocks, find and open the cave, recover the toys, and carry them back to Claus.

Fairies is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

MANHOOD / 1. The Laughing Valley

The Fairies bring Claus skillets, pots, dishes, pans, cooking tools, wool clothing, and other comforts. They also provide warm blankets and clothing as his life in the Valley begins.

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

The Fairies help Claus make the Bessie image dolly by bringing colored silks, laces, and golden threads for its clothing.

10. Christmas Eve

The Fairies search the mountains for the toys stolen by the Awgwas. By joining hands and moving through the rocks from the peak downward, they discover the cave, open it, carry out the toys, and bring them to Claus.
