Abstract written by a model

The armor contest pits Ajax son of Telamon and Diomed in a shared-armor fight to first blood. Neither wins decisively, so Achilles divides the prizes: Diomed receives the Thracian sword and both contestants share the armor.

The armor contest requires two champions to fight with weapons until one wounds the other and draws blood, while the armor is shared. Ajax son of Telamon and Diomed compete, but neither decisively wins, so Achilles divides the prize by giving the sword to Diomed and the armor to both.
