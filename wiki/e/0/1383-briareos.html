Briareos

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Briareos, being goodly, Briareus, great-souled Obriareus, Βριάρεων
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Briareos is brought back from bondage beneath the earth, fights the Titans with Cottus and Gyes, dwells on Ocean's foundations, and becomes Poseidon's son-in-law when Poseidon gives him Cymopolea to wed.
  • In The Library, Briareus is described as a Hundred-handed Hecatoncheir born to Uranus and Earth, extraordinarily large and powerful, with one hundred hands and fifty heads.
