Abstract written by a model

The Soy bean is a vegetable production, singularly rich in fat and albuminoids and closer to animal food than any other known vegetable production. Largely used as food in China and Japan, it makes many foods and provides useful straw fodder. Efforts to acclimatize it in Europe achieved fair success in Italy and France.

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE SOY BEAN.

The Soy bean is described as being singularly rich in fat and albuminoids, and as approaching more nearly to animal food than any other known vegetable production. It is largely used as food in China and Japan; efforts to acclimatize it in Europe have achieved fair success in Italy and France. Many foods are made from it, and its straw is useful fodder.
