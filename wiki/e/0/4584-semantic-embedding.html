semantic embedding in Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation

Hazem Abdelazim · 2023

Semantic embedding, also called the vector embedding process, is a primary Information Retrieval process and numeric feature-vector conversion module at the heart of Retrieval Augmented Generation systems. It converts a user query, context, or corpus text chunk into an n-dimensional, fixed-size vector representing semantic features. In use, the model embeds both the query and each corpus chunk during the information-retrieval phase, before LLM comprehension and response. Its accuracy is paramount to RAG-system effectiveness. The study focuses on multilingual models that support Arabic text comprehension and generation, investigating ten models with Arabic-relevant features. It notes relatively limited research on Arabic semantic embeddings despite their uses in sentence-level tasks, retrieval, sentiment analysis, and question answering.

embedding space modeling in A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models

Qinggang Zhang, Shengyuan Chen, Yuanchen Bei, Zheng Yuan, Huachi Zhou, Zijin Hong, Hao Chen, Yilin Xiao, Chuang Zhou, Junnan Dong, Yi Chang, Xiao Huang · 2025-09-29

Embedding space modeling maps queries and graph entities into a continuous vector space for contextual and conceptual connections. Using TF-IDF, Word2Vec, and GloVe, it retrieves semantically relevant information without exact matches, but underutilizes graph structure.
