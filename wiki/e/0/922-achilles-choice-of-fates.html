Achilles' choice of fates

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: If I stay here and fight, if I go home my name will die, two ways in which I may meet my end

Abstract written by a model

Achilles says his mother Thetis told him that he has two possible fates: if he stays and fights, he will die but gain everlasting fame; if he returns home, his fame will die but he will live much longer. He emphasizes that life is worth more to him than all the wealth of Ilius.
