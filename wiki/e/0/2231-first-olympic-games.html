Abstract written by a model

The first Olympiad, a founding five-year Olympic festival associated with Herakles’ founding of the games, established victories in foot-racing, wrestling, boxing, chariot racing, javelin, and stone-throwing, won respectively by Oionos, Echemos, Doryklos, Samos of Mantinea, Phrastor, and Enikeus.

XI.

The first Olympiad is associated with the fifth-year feast and the victories established after Herakles founded the games. Its contests were won by Oionos in the foot-race, Echemos in wrestling, Doryklos in boxing, Samos in the four-horse chariot, Phrastor with the javelin, and Enikeus in stone-throwing.
