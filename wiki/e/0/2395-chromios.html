Chromios

Abstract written by a model

Chromios, son of Agesidamos and brother-in-law of Hieron, is a citizen of Aitna honored in Nemean Ode I. His chariot victories at Nemea and Sikyon bring honor and glory. Praised for wealth, foresight, generosity, courage, renown, and military ability, he serves in foot, horse, and naval fighting and helps ward off Enyalios’s destruction.

Chromios is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chromios in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode I

Chromios is praised as the victor whose car and victory at Nemea inspire the triumphal song. He is called the son of Agesidamos and is presented as possessing both practical ability and foresight, as well as generosity toward friends and strangers.

Nemean Ode IX

Chromios is praised as the winner of a chariot-race at Sikyon and as a fortunate man of wealth, renown, courage, and military ability. The song says that he served in foot, horse, and naval fighting and helped ward off the destruction of Enyalios.
