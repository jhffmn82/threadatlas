breastworks and battlements

object · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: battlement, battlements, the battlements, the breastworks, the buttresses

Abstract written by a model

The breastworks and battlements before the Achaean wall are fiercely contested by Lycians, Argives, and Danaans. During the assault, Sarpedon tears away a battlement to open a breach; Trojans and allies overthrow the defenses and pull up their buttresses, before Hector springs toward them and breaks through the gates.

BOOK XII.

The Trojans tear down the breastworks, overthrow the battlements, and pull up the buttresses placed before the Achaean wall. Sarpedon seizes a battlement and pulls it away, opening a breach through which many Trojans might pass.
