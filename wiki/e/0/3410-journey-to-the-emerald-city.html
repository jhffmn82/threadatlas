Journey to the Emerald City in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Journey to the Emerald City is a journey undertaken by Nick Chopper to restore the Scarecrow to his throne. Nick, the Scarecrow, Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse begin a return journey, with Nick leading and the others protecting Jack. They cross Mombi's altered landscape, become delayed when the Saw-Horse breaks a leg, repair it, and continue toward the Emerald City after Woggle-Bug joins them.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

A Nickel-Plated Emperor

Nick Chopper, the Scarecrow, Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse begin the return journey to the Emerald City. Nick leads with his gleaming axe, while Jack rides the Saw-Horse and Tip and the Scarecrow walk beside him to keep Jack from falling or being damaged.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.

During the Journey to the Emerald City, the travelers encounter Mombi's altered landscape, including a confusing field of sunflowers. They use the sun to keep their direction but are delayed when the Saw-Horse breaks a leg.

A Highly Magnified History

The Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Saw-Horse, Pumpkinhead, and Tip resume their journey toward the Emerald City after repairing the Saw-Horse. The Woggle-Bug accepts their invitation and travels with them.
