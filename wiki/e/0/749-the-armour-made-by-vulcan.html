the armour made by Vulcan in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Vulcan’s armour is a divine-made set for Achilles: a great, strong, richly worked shield; a breastplate brighter than fire; a close-fitting helmet with golden plume; greaves of beaten tin; and, when given, a sword. After Achilles loses his former armour when Patroclus falls, Thetis asks Vulcan to forge new armour. She promises its arrival, carries it to Achilles at daybreak, and gives it to him. The Myrmidons recoil in awe; Achilles puts it on, and it seems to buoy him up like wings.

The armour of Vulcan is given to Achilles by Thetis and causes the Myrmidons to recoil in awe. It includes greaves, a breastplate, a sword, a great shield, and a helmet with golden plumes, and it seems to buoy Achilles up like wings.
