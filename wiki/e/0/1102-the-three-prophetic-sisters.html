the three prophetic sisters

Also called: certain holy ones, sisters born—three virgins, teachers of divination, these

Abstract written by a model

The three prophetic sisters are winged virgins dwelling beneath a ridge of Parnassus. They teach divination apart from Apollo: honey inspires truthful speech, while deprivation brings falsehood. Apollo grants Hermes access to their instruction.

The three prophetic sisters are winged virgins who dwell under a ridge of Parnassus and teach divination apart from Apollo. They speak truth when inspired by honey, but speak falsely when deprived of the gods’ sweet food; Apollo gives Hermes access to their instruction.
