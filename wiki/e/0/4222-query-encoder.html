query encoder in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Michael Glass, Gaetano Rossiello, Md Faisal Mahbub Chowdhury, Alﬁo Gliozzo · 2021

The query encoder is a BERT model that independently encodes queries, comparing its outputs with passage representations through inner products. It is trained with the passage encoder for slot filling, then injected into RAG for Natural Questions, where retrieval-driven training updates it without disrupting generation.

The query encoder is trained for slot filling alongside the passage encoder and is later injected into the RAG model for Natural Questions. RAG training updates it through the effect of retrieval on generation without disrupting the quality of generation.
