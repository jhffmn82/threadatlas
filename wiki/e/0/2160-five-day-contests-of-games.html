five-day contests of games in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Five-day contests honor six twin altars with ox sacrifices, then horse, mule, chariot, and single-frontlet steed competitions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V.

Psaumis honors six twin altars in great feasts of the gods with sacrifices of oxen and five-day contests of games. These contests include competitions with horses, mules, and the steed of single frontlet.
