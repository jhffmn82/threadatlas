3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

Claus carves a wooden lioness in Shiegra's likeness, including her fierce teeth, frowning lines, crouching body, and long tail. He paints it with the Ryls' colors, and Shiegra considers it skillfully made and likely to please children.
