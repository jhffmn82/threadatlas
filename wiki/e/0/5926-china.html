China in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909 · the work’s page · read it where China first appears

Abstract written by a model

China is the distant destination of Rudolph Hackh’s appointment, imagined by him as mysterious, old, and difficult to understand. It first appears as a sullen, inhospitable coast approached by ship. It then unfolds through a desolate coast and muddy river, ruins, war-junks, boatmen, coolies, and a crowded native town near Stink-Chau, while European residents live separately at the settlement’s edge. An excursion reveals rice-wine fields, bamboos, river traffic, fishermen, and ruined forts, alongside hostility toward foreigners.
