dense retrieval methods in Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

Yuning Mao, Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Yelong Shen, Jianfeng Gao, Jiawei Han, Weizhu Chen · 2021

Dense retrieval methods embed queries and passages in a latent vector space to match semantic similarity beyond lexical overlap, retrieving lexically different relevant passages. They trade this capability for greater computational expense than sparse retrieval and information loss from fixed-size vectors.

Introduction

Dense retrieval methods embed queries and passages into a latent vector space, allowing semantic matching beyond lexical overlap. They can retrieve lexically different relevant passages but are more computationally expensive and may lose information by condensing text into fixed-size vectors.

fden in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024

fden is a dense retriever in a retrieval module, initialized with E5. It requires large amounts of annotated training data, is fine-tuned on limited claim-evidence pairs to rerank BM25’s subset, combines ranking and InfoNCE losses, and evaluates response relevance to claims and retrieved evidence.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

It is fine-tuned on limited claim-evidence pairs to rerank the subset produced by BM25. Its optimization combines a ranking loss with an InfoNCE-based contrastive term that separates annotated evidence from negative evidence.

Experiments / Experiment Design .. Response Generation Results

fden is the dense retriever in the retrieval module and is initialized with E5. It is also used to evaluate response relevance to the input claim and retrieved evidence.

dense retrieval in Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Paul J. L. Ammann · 2025-07-28/2025-07-29

Dense retrieval is a retrieval stage that compares a query representation with document embeddings by inner-product similarity, selects top-k semantically similar documents, and supplies them as grounding candidates for a language model response. QD+RR achieves highest reported MultiHop-RAG quality but increases latency.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Methodology .. Generation.

Dense retrieval compares the query representation with all document embeddings using inner-product similarity and selects the top-k documents. It identifies documents that are semantically similar to the query and provides candidates for grounding the response.

opening .. Efficiency

Retrieval quality is highest for QD+RR on MultiHop-RAG, with 0.763 Hits@4, 0.872 Hits@10, 0.322 MAP@10, and 0.635 MRR@10. The unit identifies a tradeoff between this quality and the system's increased latency.
