Abstract written by a model

The rival is a wealthy, recently enriched knight and soldier, marked by former fighting: helmet, sword, shield, scars, wounds, and blood on his right hand. He gained wealth through military wounds. He secretly exchanges signs and passionate kisses with the mistress, whom he is preferred by over the speaker-poet, causing the speaker fear and jealousy.

ELEGY V.

The rival is the person with whom the mistress exchanges secret signs and passionate kisses. The speaker fears that the rival received kisses equal to those the mistress gives him.

ELEGY VIII.

The wealthy rival Knight is a recently enriched soldier whose wealth was acquired through wounds. His head once wore a helmet, his side bore a sword, his left hand carried a shield, and his right hand has been stained with blood. The mistress prefers him to the speaker-poet.
