Thera is a holy island and mother-city of mighty commonwealths. It is destined to receive Euphemos’ descendants, who colonize it; after seventeen generations, Battos is instructed to leave Thera and found Kyrene in Libya.

IV.

Thera is the holy island from which Battos is instructed to depart in order to found Kyrene. Medea's prophecy declares Thera to be the mother-city of mighty communities.

[Footnote 1: Libya. Epaphos was son of Zeus by Io.]

Thera was the island where the clod dropped by Euphemos into the sea was expected to wash ashore. Euphemos’ descendants were to colonize Thera before proceeding, after seventeen generations, to found Kyrene in Libya.
