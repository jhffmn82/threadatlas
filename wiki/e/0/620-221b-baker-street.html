221B Baker Street in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

221B, Baker Street consists of two comfortable bedrooms and a large, airy sitting-room with two broad windows. It becomes the shared residence of Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson and serves as Holmes’s place of business, where clients and investigators come to consult him. 221B Baker Street is given in the advertisement as the place where the wedding ring may be claimed. Mrs. Sawyer visits the address, and Sherlock Holmes follows her after she leaves.

Baker Street in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Baker Street contains Holmes’s rooms, shared with Watson. Grant Munro first visits and impatiently waits there; later Trevelyan comes at about ten in the evening seeking advice and assistance. By the final record, the rooms have been set on fire during the night.
