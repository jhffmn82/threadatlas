Abstract written by a model

The reindeer are deer permitted to draw Claus's sledge on Christmas Eve, up to ten each year, if they return by daybreak. When assisting Claus, they eat casa plants; grawle gives fleetness, marbon long life, and the Pool of Nares gives sleek coats and beauty. Their fleet feet cross hard-frozen deep snow, pull Claus and his pack, leap rooftops, and race home before dawn.

8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

The reindeer can walk easily across deep snow when its surface is frozen hard, and their fleet feet allow them to travel much faster than Claus can walk. Flossie and Glossie pull Claus and his pack on a sledge, leap between rooftops, and race home before dawn.

10. Christmas Eve

The reindeer are deer permitted to draw Claus's sledge on Christmas Eve, with up to ten allowed each year. They receive access to nourishing and strengthening plants, the Pool of Nares, and the Fairy Queen's protection, provided they return by daybreak.
