Lotus-eaters

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the Lotus-eaters, the people of the place

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, the Lotus-eaters live on lotus and offer it to visitors, causing Ulysses' scouts to forget home and want to stay. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Lotus-eaters kindly entertain three of Ulysses's men and give them lotus-plant food, causing them to lose thoughts of home and wish to remain until Ulysses forcibly removes them.
