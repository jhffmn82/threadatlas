Lotus-eaters

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the Lotus-eaters, the people of the place
  • In The Odyssey, the Lotus-eaters live on lotus and offer it to visitors, causing Ulysses' scouts to forget home and want to stay.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Lotus-eaters kindly entertain three of Ulysses's men and give them lotus-plant food, causing them to lose thoughts of home and wish to remain until Ulysses forcibly removes them.

the Lotus-eaters in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The Lotus-eaters are people who live on lotus and offer it to visitors; it causes Ulysses' scouts to forget home and want to stay.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IX

The Lotus-eaters live on food that comes from a kind of flower. They do no harm to Ulysses' scouts but give them lotus, whose effect makes the men abandon their concern for home and want to remain with the Lotus-eaters.
