Introduction

English provides large-scale keyphrase resources that can be used as an external knowledge base for multilingual keyphrase generation. Related English passages and their keyphrases are retrieved to provide hints for generating keyphrases in lower-resource languages.

Model .. 4.3

English is described as having the most abundant text-keyphrase pairs in the e-commerce and academic paper domains. Its passages and associated keyphrases provide the external resource used for retrieval-augmented multilingual generation.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It is used to construct EcommerceMKP-EN and as an auxiliary training language. English passages are also paired with multilingual passages for training the cross-lingual dense passage retriever.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

English is used as the source of external large-scale data and as the language of retrieved keyphrases. Retrieved English keyphrases provide useful information but can also introduce noise.
