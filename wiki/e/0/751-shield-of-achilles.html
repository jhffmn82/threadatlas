Also called: Achilles' shield, Achilles’ shield, Achilles’ wondrous shield, great and terrible shield of Achilles, his round shield, its wondrous compass, shield, shield (Achilles' armor), that god-laboured work, that shield invincible, the fair strong corslet, the shield, the shield so great and strong, the strong shield
  • In The Iliad, Achilles’ shield is newly made for Achilles by Vulcan and depicts the world in its many aspects, shining brilliantly when Achilles carries it into battle.
  • In The Fall of Troy, the shield of Achilles is fashioned by Hephaestus for Aeacus’ son and displays lifelike scenes of the world, ending in an outer rim surrounded by Ocean.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the shield of Achilles is made of five metal plates, is pierced through two layers by Aeneas’s spear, and is later pierced by Achilles’s spear.

Achilles’ shield in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Newly made for Achilles, the shield is made by Vulcan five layers thick, with a gleaming threefold rim and silver baldric. It depicts earth, heaven, sea, constellations, two cities and their battle, fields, harvest, vineyard, cattle, pasture, dancers, musicians, and Oceanus around its outer rim. It ends as a huge, strong, brilliant shield whose firelike light shines into heaven when Achilles carries it into battle.

shield of Achilles in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

A shield fashioned by Hephaestus for Aeacus' son, it is a lifelike marvel depicting earth, heaven, sea, war, peace, agriculture, labor, festivals, gods, ships, and moving-seeming creatures, ending at an outer rim surrounded by Ocean.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK V

The shield of Achilles is shown as a marvel wrought by Hephaestus, depicting heaven, earth, sea, warfare, peace, agriculture, festivals, gods, ships, and the surrounding Ocean. Its scenes appear so lifelike that the portrayed creatures and people seem to move.

shield of Achilles in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Achilles's shield is made of five metal plates: two of brass, two of tin, and one of gold. Aeneas's spear pierces two layers but stops at the third, and Achilles's spear later pierces Aeneas's shield.
