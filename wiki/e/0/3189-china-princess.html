china Princess in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

A beautiful china princess, most beautifully dressed, lives contentedly in The Dainty China Country. She runs from Dorothy, fearing breakage; although mended, china people lose beauty, and those removed become stiff.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XX

The china Princess runs from Dorothy because she fears falling and breaking herself. She explains that china people can be mended but are never as pretty afterward, and that those taken away from their country become stiff and can only stand straight and look pretty.
