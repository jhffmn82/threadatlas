Teumessian vixen

animal · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Teumesian fox, The Cadmean fox, brute of a vixen, creature, fox, the Teumessian vixen, the vixen, vixen, ἀλώπεκος, ἀλώπηξ
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the Teumessian vixen is sent by the gods to punish Cadmus’s descendants and leads the Thebans to exclude Cadmus’s house from kingship.
  • In The Library, the Teumessian vixen ravages Cadmea, cannot be caught, and is turned to stone with Laelaps by Zeus.
