The underworld visit is a journey to the dead that takes Ulysses to the Cimmerians’ land beyond Oceanus and toward Hades. He summons and consults the dead, receives Teiresias’s prophecy, speaks with Anticlea, and sees ghosts before fear of their multitude drives him back to his ship.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XI

The underworld visit takes Ulysses to the Cimmerians’ land beyond Oceanus. During the visit he summons the dead, receives Teiresias’s prophecy, speaks with Anticlea, and sees the ghosts of famous women.

Here he ended, and the guests sat all of them enthralled and speechless

During his underworld journey, Ulysses consults the dead and hears their accounts of death, grief, punishment, and heroic fame. He ends the journey by returning to his ship on the river Oceanus after becoming frightened by the multitude of ghosts.
