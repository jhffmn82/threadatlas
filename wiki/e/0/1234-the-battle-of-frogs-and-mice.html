THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

The battle begins because Puff-jaw abandons Crumb-snatcher in the lake and the Mice seek revenge. The Mice and Frogs fight fiercely with improvised weapons, but the crabs eventually drive the Mice into flight, ending the one-day war at sunset.
