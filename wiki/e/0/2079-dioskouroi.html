Dioskouroi in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

The Dioskouroi are Kastor and Polydeukes, sons of Tyndareus, invoked at the ode’s beginning and honored by Theron and the Emmenidai through hospitality tables and rites.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

The Dioskouroi, identified as Kastor and Polydeukes and as the sons of Tyndareus, are invoked at the beginning of the ode. Theron and the Emmenidai honor them with tables of hospitality and observance of the rite of the blessed gods.
