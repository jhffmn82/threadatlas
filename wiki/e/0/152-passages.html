Abstract written by a model

In Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering, passages are retrieved from a document collection and supplied to a reader, with relevant passages serving as targets of augmented retrieval for OpenQA. In Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling, passages are indexed with Anserini and retrieved using BM25, with about 32 million KILT knowledge source passages later encoded as vectors for retrieval. In Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge, text passages are the commonly used unstructured knowledge source and can be combined with tables and knowledge-graph triples in a heterogeneous retrieval corpus. In SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection, retrieved passages are text chunks supplied when factual grounding is helpful and are evaluated for relevance and support before influencing generation and ranking. In An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation, a retrieved passage provides evidence for generating outputs and may contain redundant, noisy, irrelevant, harmful, or toxic information that a noise filter compresses. In SDG target detection in environmental reports using Retrieval-augmented Generation with LLMs, retrieved passages serve as language-model contexts and are usually presented in retrieval-ranked order before answer generation. In Wiki-LLaVA: Hierarchical Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multimodal LLMs, passages are retrieved from selected documents by similarity to a user’s question and inserted as context to support LLMs and augment dialogues. In HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation, passages are stored as vertices in a graph-structured index and linked by similarity, logical question triplets, and directed logical relations.

retrieved passages in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17 · the work’s page · read it where retrieved passages first appears

Abstract written by a model

Retrieved passages are text chunks supplied when SELF-RAG judges factual grounding helpful. SELF-RAG evaluates their relevance and whether they support generated segments before they affect candidate generation and ranking, while masking retrieved text from generator training loss. They can provide evidence for model-generated claims, but fixed-number, indiscriminate retrieval and incorporation can reduce versatility and produce unhelpful responses. Results show fine-grained selection and critique outperform always using the top passage, because relevance alone is insufficient.
