Chryses

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: his priest, priest, priest of Apollo, the old man

Abstract written by a model

Chryses is a priest of Apollo and father of Chryseis. He comes to the Achaean ships with Apollo’s sceptre and ransom to seek her release, but Agamemnon rejects and insults him. Chryses prays for vengeance; later Ulysses returns Chryseis to him, and he leads sacrifice and prayer that end the pestilence.

Chryses is in one work on this site, The Iliad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK I.

Chryses comes to the ships of the Achaeans carrying a ransom and the sceptre of Apollo, asking for the release of his daughter Chryseis. Agamemnon rejects and insults him, so Chryses prays to Apollo to avenge his tears. He is to receive Chryseis back when the Achaeans send her to Chryse.
