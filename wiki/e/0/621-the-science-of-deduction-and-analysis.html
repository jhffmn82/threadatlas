the Science of Deduction and Analysis

topic · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: THE SCIENCE OF DEDUCTION, The Science of Deduction, deductions, inferences, my methods, our little deductions, scientific deduction, the science of deduction, those rules of deduction
  • In A Study in Scarlet, it is described as a discipline based on long and patient study of observation and reasoning, which Sherlock Holmes applies professionally.
  • In The Sign of the Four, it is presented by Holmes as an exact, unemotional discipline that analyzes effects and traces them to causes.
  • In The Hound of the Baskervilles, it is demonstrated through Holmes’s examination of Mortimer’s stick, its inscription, its wear, and the marks left by a dog.

the science of deduction in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

The science of deduction is presented by Holmes as an exact, unemotional discipline based on analyzing effects and tracing them to causes. Holmes illustrates it by explaining how physical evidence from Watson’s clothing, desk, and watch supports broader conclusions.

scientific deduction in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Scientific deduction is demonstrated through the examination of Mortimer’s stick, its inscription, its wear, and the marks left by a dog. Holmes uses these clues to revise Watson’s mistaken assumptions and infer Mortimer’s profession, history, age, personality, and ownership of a curly-haired spaniel.
