the Science of Deduction and Analysis

topic · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: THE SCIENCE OF DEDUCTION, The Science of Deduction, deductions, inferences, my methods, our little deductions, scientific deduction, the science of deduction, those rules of deduction

Abstract written by a model

In A Study in Scarlet, it is described as a discipline based on long and patient study of observation and reasoning, which Sherlock Holmes applies professionally. In The Sign of the Four, it is presented by Holmes as an exact, unemotional discipline that analyzes effects and traces them to causes. In The Hound of the Baskervilles, it is demonstrated through Holmes’s examination of Mortimer’s stick, its inscription, its wear, and the marks left by a dog.
