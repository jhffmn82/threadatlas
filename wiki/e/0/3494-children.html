Abstract written by a model

In The Marvelous Land of Oz, children sent L. Frank Baum letters expressing pleasure in reading The Wonderful Wizard of Oz and asking for more about the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, children/readers loved Dorothy and demanded more Oz stories, sending thousands of letters and suggestions that shaped the book. In The Emerald City of Oz, children sent L. Frank Baum clever ideas in letters, helping create his stories and inspiring future fairy-tale authors. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, children who loved Oz stories sent letters seeking earlier adventures and suggesting that Dorothy communicate by wireless telegraph.
