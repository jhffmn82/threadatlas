Abstract

Rating is one of the attribute identifiers provided for review generation. It is also used to label texts from which references can be selected.

opening .. Augmenting context using references

Ratings are among the attributes aligned with Amazon Books product reviews. The unit explains that matching a reference's rating to the input rating does not necessarily ensure useful rating-related context or rating semantics in the generated review.

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

The rating is one of the review-specific attributes used as input for review generation. The text states that reinforcement learning can help REFLECT preserve rating semantics.

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

Rating information contributes to relevance supervision through an indicator that is one when a candidate and the input have the same rating and zero otherwise. Including rating accuracy helps guide generation toward a rating-consistent review.

Experiments .. Analysis on Rating Consistency

Ratings range from 1 to 5. Rating accuracy is measured by classifying generated reviews and is improved substantially by GEN-C-F (RL).
