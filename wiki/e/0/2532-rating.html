Abstract written by a model

Rating is a 1-to-5 attribute of Amazon Books product reviews, used with user and product identifiers in review generation and to label texts for reference selection. Matching a reference’s rating alone may not provide useful rating context or semantics. Rating also supplies relevance pseudo-supervision through same-rating indicators and rating accuracy. Across the approach, reinforcement learning helps preserve rating semantics, and GEN-C-F (RL) substantially improves rating accuracy, measured by classifying generated reviews.

opening .. Augmenting context using references

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

Rating information contributes to relevance supervision through an indicator that is one when a candidate and the input have the same rating and zero otherwise. Including rating accuracy helps guide generation toward a rating-consistent review.
