Chryseis' release and return to Chryses in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Chryseis’ release is Agamemnon’s response to Apollo’s plague: after Calchas explains the cause, Ulysses takes her and a holy hecatomb by ship to Chryses at Chryse.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK I.

Agamemnon agrees to release Chryseis after Calchas explains that Apollo’s plague is punishment for keeping her. He sends her aboard a ship commanded by Ulysses, together with a hecatomb for Apollo, so that she may be returned to Chryses at Chryse.
