Orchomenos

place · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Minyai's home, ORCHOMENOS, this city, town, town of Orchomenus

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Orchomenos is a city and land of goodly steeds, home of the ancient Minyai, whose people are declared winners through Asopichos’s Olympic victory; it becomes Asopodoros’s new country after his shipwreck and banishment, and its glory is celebrated. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, Orchomenos is held by the Spartans with a strong garrison until the Thebans attack and rout the Spartan force. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Orchomenus is the town’s name during Orchomenus’s reign; its inhabitants are called Orchomenians, and it receives Hyettus as a refugee and gives him the surrounding land.

Orchomenos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where Orchomenos first appears

Abstract written by a model

Orchomenos is a city and lusty land of goodly steeds drinking Kephisos’s waters, home of the ancient Minyai. Through Asopichos’s Olympic victory its people are declared winners. It later becomes Asopodoros’s new country, kindly sheltering him after shipwreck and banishment, and its glory is celebrated.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIV.

Orchomenos is the city of Asopichos and is described as the lusty land of goodly steeds that drink the waters of Kephisos. Its home, the Minyai's ancient race, is declared the winner through Asopichos's Olympic victory.

Isthmian Ode I

Orchomenos is described as Asopodoros’ new country. It sheltered him after he was driven ashore from a wrecked ship and is included among the places whose glory Pindar will celebrate.
