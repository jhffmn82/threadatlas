spectacles (Emerald City) in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Green glasses worn day and night by people of the Emerald City, locked on by Oz's order to prevent blindness from its brightness and glory; the Guardian fits and locks them on Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, the Cowardly Lion, and Toto.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The spectacles have green glasses and are required because the brightness and glory of the Emerald City could blind anyone without them. The Guardian of the Gates fits spectacles to Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, the Cowardly Lion, and Toto, then locks them on with golden bands and a key.
