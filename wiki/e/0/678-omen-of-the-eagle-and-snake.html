omen of the eagle and snake

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: a sign from heaven, an omen, the portent
  • In The Iliad: portends Trojans and their allies [in Polydamas's interpretation] Polydamas reads the omen as a warning that the Trojans may break through the Achaean wall but will not return safely, just as the eagle could not carry the snake home. Hector rejects this interpretation and refuses to let the omen stop the attack.
