the signs from heaven in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Signs from heaven begin when Ulysses seeks confirmation: thunder and a miller-woman’s prayer foretell his attack and vengeance on the suitors.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XX

Ulysses asks Jove for one sign from inside the house and another from outside. The thunder from heaven and the miller-woman's prayer provide the requested signs, which Ulysses understands as meaning that he will avenge himself on the suitors.
