Oz's deception in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Oz's deception uses a paper Head, disguises, skins, an oil-soaked cotton ball, and ventriloquism to create false Wizard manifestations and remain hidden, convincing his subjects that he is a powerful wizard.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XV

Oz's deception consists of using a paper Head suspended by wire, disguises, a structure of skins, a cotton ball soaked in oil, and ventriloquism to appear magical. He has kept himself hidden from his subjects so they would believe he was a powerful wizard.
