transformation of the hound and fox into stones

event · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: petrification of the vixen and dog, turned into stones, λίθους ἐποίησεν
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the hound overtakes the Teumesian fox near Teumessus, and both transform into stones.
  • In The Library, Laelaps pursues the Teumessian vixen until Zeus turns both the vixen and the dog into stones.
