Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

11. How the First Stockings Were Hung by the Chimneys

The children's stockings are hung in a row by the chimney so they can dry after Margot, Dick, Ned, and Sara play in the snow. Santa Claus sees them, stuffs toys into them, and leaves the children delighted the next morning. Their use spreads among other children as a convenient way for Santa Claus to deliver presents.
