Abstract written by a model

The Drebber–Stangerson murder investigation is Holmes’s logical reconstruction of clues, cab tracks, footprints, poison, a ring, cigar, nail marks, blood, and Cleveland information, to identify Jefferson Hope. Holmes focuses on a tall red-faced man seen by John Rance, plans to use the ring as bait, and solves the crimes despite public credit going to Gregson and Lestrade.

CHAPTER IV.

Holmes says the tall, red-faced man found by John Rance holds the clue to the mystery. He intends to capture him, using the ring as bait if necessary.

CHAPTER VII.

The investigation is presented by Holmes as a chain of logical sequences based on cab tracks, footprints, poison, a ring, a cigar, nail marks, blood, and information from Cleveland. Holmes traces the crimes to Jefferson Hope, while the public account wrongly gives the principal credit to Gregson and Lestrade.
