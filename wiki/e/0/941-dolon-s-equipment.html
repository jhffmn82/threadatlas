Dolon’s equipment

Abstract written by a model

Dolon’s equipment consists of a ferret-skin cap, wolf-skin, bow, and long spear. Ulysses takes these spoils, hangs them on a tamarisk tree in honour of Minerva, then hangs them at his ship’s stern as a sacred offering.
