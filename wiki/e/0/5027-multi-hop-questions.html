multi-hop questions in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024

A multi-hop question requires complex reasoning and may be hard for LLMs to answer directly. GenGround handles it step by step, breaking it into fine-grained single-hop questions. It alternates generation and grounding: formulates a simpler question, generates an answer, grounds the question-answer pair in retrieved documents, and repeats until deriving the final answer through a shorter reasoning trajectory.

multi-hop questions in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

Multi-hop questions are questions requiring multiple reasoning steps to obtain correct answers. They are central to this work and are represented by a question, answer, and retrieved documents. Because irrelevant retrieved information can degrade retrieval-augmented generation performance, TRACE constructs evidence chains across documents.

Narrative, section by section

Abstract

Multi-hop questions require multiple steps of reasoning, making them particularly vulnerable to performance degradation from irrelevant retrieved information.

Introduction

Multi-hop questions require multiple reasoning steps to obtain an answer. Irrelevant documents can significantly impair the ability of retrieval-augmented generation models to answer them, so TRACE constructs chains that connect evidence across documents.

Multi-hop questions are the focus of this work. Each multi-hop question is denoted as q and is associated with an answer a and a set of retrieved documents.
