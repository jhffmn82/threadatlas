Wikidata in Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs

Simone Conia · 2024

Wikidata is a large, diverse multilingual knowledge graph and knowledge source containing millions of elements. It provides entities and English and target-language names as translation hints, serving as the reference graph for KG-MT and selecting entities for XC-Translate. Its similar structure could support extension to other knowledge graphs. However, it is incomplete: selection can exclude entities lacking linked Wikipedia pages or English names, and it inherits biases and errors from Wikipedia and its editor demographics.

Related Work

Wikidata is identified as an example of a large knowledge source containing millions of elements. The intersection of retrieval-augmented generation and retrieval from sources such as Wikidata is described as understudied.

3 .. 3.3

Wikidata is used as the reference multilingual knowledge graph for KG-MT. The method is not limited to Wikidata and can be extended to other knowledge graphs with a similar structure.

Limitations

Wikidata is used to select entities for XC-Translate and is described as a large and diverse but incomplete knowledge graph. Its use can exclude entities without linked Wikipedia pages or English names, and its biases and errors may be inherited from Wikipedia.
