Golden Cap

object · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: This Golden Cap, the Cap, the Golden Cap

Abstract written by a model

The Golden Cap is a jeweled wedding present made for Quelala, marked with a circle of diamonds and rubies and containing written magic words. Its wearer can summon and command the Winged Monkeys three times. The Wicked Witch uses its commands to enslave the Winkies, drive the Great Oz from the western land, and attack Dorothy and her companions. The cap then fits Dorothy exactly; she uses it to call the monkeys but cannot reach Kansas. Dorothy gives it to Glinda, who uses its three commands to send Dorothy’s companions to their chosen destinations.

Golden Cap is in one work on this site, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Golden Cap in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04 · the work’s page · read it where Golden Cap first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XII

The Golden Cap has a circle of diamonds and rubies and allows its owner to summon the Winged Monkeys three times. The Wicked Witch used it first to enslave the Winkies and second to drive the Great Oz out of the western land. She uses its final summons to attack Dorothy and her companions.

Chapter XIV

The Golden Cap contains written instructions for a charm that summons the Winged Monkeys. Dorothy uses it to call them, and the Monkey King explains that its owner has three commands over the Winged Monkeys.

Chapter XVIII

Dorothy brings out the Golden Cap and uses its magic words to call the Winged Monkeys. She believes she has wasted its charm when they cannot help her reach Kansas.

Chapter XXIII

Dorothy gives the Golden Cap to Glinda because it is no longer useful to her. Glinda uses its three commands to send the Winged Monkeys to carry Dorothy’s companions to their chosen destinations.
