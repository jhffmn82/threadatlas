Peleus and Thetis marriage

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Peleus and Thetis' marriage, Thetis' bridals, Thetis' wedding, espoused one of the Nereids, marriage, marriage of Thetis

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Thetis’ wedding to Peleus is described as arranged after Themis’ oracle warned that her son by Zeus or one of his brothers would surpass his father, with Zeus and Poseidon attending. In The Library, Peleus and Thetis’ marriage is described as a silent, localized supernatural-bride tale, possibly including an immortality ritual, whose separation follows the bride’s return to her former home.

X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The Peleus and Thetis marriage is treated as a localized version of a popular tale type involving a supernatural bride. Its silence and separation are connected with the Cretan tale and with stories in which a supernatural spouse returns to its former home.
