CHAPTER VII.

The arrest of Jefferson Hope is carried out by Sherlock Holmes after Hope arrives disguised as a cabman. Holmes secretly fastens handcuffs on him, and Holmes, Dr. John Watson, Inspector Lestrade, and Inspector Gregson restrain him after he tries to flee.
