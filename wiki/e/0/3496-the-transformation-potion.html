The Marvelous Powder of Life

Mombi prepares the transformation potion in a small black kettle from equal parts milk and vinegar, along with herbs and powders measured according to a recipe. She tells Tip that, if properly made, it will transform him into a marble statue. The potion must cool overnight before Tip drinks it.
