NMTGen in RACE: Retrieval-Augmented Commit Message Generation

Ensheng Shi, Yanlin Wang, Wei Tao, Lun Du, Hongyu Zhang, Shi Han, Dongmei Zhang, and Hongbin Sun · 2022

NMTGen is a Seq2Seq-based, end-to-end neural network model used as a baseline. It is evaluated against RACE, which enhances it and outperforms it across all human-evaluation aspects. RACE raises NMTGen’s BLEU by over 20% for every programming language and improves average BLEU, Meteor, Rouge-L, and Cider.

Experimental Results / How does RACE perform compared with .. 5.4

NMTGen is an end-to-end neural baseline and one of the models enhanced by the RACE framework. RACE improves its BLEU performance by more than 20% on every programming language and boosts its average BLEU, Meteor, Rouge-L, and Cider results by 43%, 49%, 33%, and 61%, respectively.

5.5

NMTGen is evaluated as an end-to-end neural network-based baseline against RACE. RACE performs better than NMTGen across all three human-evaluation aspects.
