Generative GAR

system · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Generative Reader, generative reader

Abstract written by a model

Generative GAR is a generative reader for question answering: using BART-large, it concatenates a question with top-10 retrieved passages within 1,024 tokens to generate the desired answer.

Generative GAR is in one work on this site, Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
