Pastoria in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

Pastoria was the former King from whom the Wizard of Oz took the throne. Glinda identifies Pastoria's daughter, Ozma, as the rightful heir to the Emerald City after Pastoria's death.
