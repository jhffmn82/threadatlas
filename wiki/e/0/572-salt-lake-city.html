Salt Lake City in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Salt Lake City is a city in Utah and a Mormon city called the home of the saints. It develops as the centre of Mormon settlement, with streets, squares, farms, and a great temple, while John Ferrier becomes one of its richest men. Its society is dominated by fear of religious authorities. After Ferrier is killed and Lucy captured, pursuers return there; Lucy is forced to marry Drebber, and Jefferson Hope searches for Drebber and Stangerson before following Drebber abroad.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Salt Lake City develops as the centre of the Mormon settlement, with streets, squares, farms, and a great temple. John Ferrier becomes one of the city’s richest men.

CHAPTER III.

Salt Lake City is where Jefferson Hope and his comrades departed from three weeks before Brigham Young's visit. It is part of the Utah society described as dominated by fear of religious authorities.

CHAPTER V.

Salt Lake City is the destination from which the pursuers come and to which their tracks return after John Ferrier is killed and Lucy is captured. It is also where Lucy is forced to marry Drebber and where Jefferson Hope later searches for Drebber and Stangerson.

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope says he followed Enoch Drebber from Salt Lake City to St. Petersburg and across two continents. He also attributes the worsening of his aortic aneurism to exposure and under-feeding in the Salt Lake Mountains.
