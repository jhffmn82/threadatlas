sexual impotence in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Ovid experiences sexual impotence with a beautiful and willing girl, despite her embraces, kisses, words, and efforts to arouse him. He wonders whether magic, poison, or shame has caused the failure, and contrasts it with his recent success with Chlide, Pitho, Libas, and Corinna.
