Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, sexual impotence prevents Ovid from having sex with a beautiful and willing girl despite her efforts to arouse him. In The Amores, or Amours, impotence prevents the male speaker from enjoying the unnamed girl despite mutual willingness and her sustained efforts.
