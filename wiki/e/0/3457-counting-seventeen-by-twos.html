counting seventeen by twos in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Counting seventeen by twos starts at one-half, then proceeds through odd numbers to seventeen; the Saw-Horse discovers the method and Woggle-Bug confirms it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

Counting seventeen by twos can be done by starting with one-half and then saying one, three, five, and the successive odd numbers through seventeen. The Saw-Horse discovers this method, which the Woggle-Bug confirms.
