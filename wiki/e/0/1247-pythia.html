Pythia in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Pythia is Apollo's priestess at Delphi, who prepares by bathing and laurel crowning, inhales chasm vapor on a tripod, and speaks words interpreted by priests.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MYTH OF OSIRIS AND ISIS

The Pythia was the priestess appointed to serve Apollo's oracle at Delphi. After bathing at the fountain of Castalia and being crowned with laurel, she sat on a tripod over the chasm, inhaled its vapor, and spoke words that priests interpreted.
