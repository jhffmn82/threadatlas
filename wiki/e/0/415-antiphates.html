Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK X

Antiphates is a Laestrygonian whose daughter directs Ulysses's men to his house. He kills one of the men, begins eating him, and raises a hue-and-cry that brings the other Laestrygonians against the fleet.
