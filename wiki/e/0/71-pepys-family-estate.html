Pepys family estate in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Pepys family estate comprises land, crops, copyhold interests, London houses, money, debts, and surrenders. About £500 is found; a house carries a £400 fine and £46 rent. Pepys expects legal claims and debts to reduce £200-yearly land inheritance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The estate includes land, crops, copyhold interests, houses, money, debts, and surrenders. Pepys learns that about £500 has been found and that the estate may contain more, but he fears that debts and legal claims will substantially reduce the inheritance; he publicly says it amounts to £200 a year in land besides money.
