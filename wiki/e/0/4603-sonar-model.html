Also called: Meta SONAR, Meta SONAR:Language-Agnostic Representations, SONAR, SONAR’s, sonar

Abstract written by a model

Meta SONAR is a fixed-size sentence-embedding space with one text encoder for 200 languages and multiple modalities. It is presented as outperforming LASER3 and Google LaBSE in multilingual similarity search, though a study found that claim overstated for Arabic.

Sonar model is in one work on this site, Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
