Kyrene's founding and colonization in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Kyrene's founding is a prophesied and Apollo-guided colonization from Thera to Libya. Medea foretells it, the Delphian oracle chooses Battos, and descendants of Euphemos travel through Thera and Kalliste. Battos, also called Aristoteles, founds the city, establishes sacred works, and is later worshipped as a hero; the foundation fulfills Apollo's establishment of Kyrene as queen.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

IV.

Kyrene's founding is foretold by Medea and confirmed by the Delphian oracle's choice of Battos. The city is ultimately colonized in Libya by descendants of Euphemos who come through Thera and Kalliste.

V.

The foundation of Kyrene is attributed to Battos, also called Aristoteles, whom Apollo guided and whose settlers reached the city by sea. Battos planted groves for the gods, made a paved sacred road across the plain, and was worshipped as a hero after his death.

IX.

The founding of Kyrene is presented as the fulfillment of Apollo’s taking Kyrene from Pelion to Libya and establishing her as queen. The city became associated with her name, beauty, flocks, produce, and games.
