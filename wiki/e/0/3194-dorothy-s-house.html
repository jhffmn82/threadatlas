Dorothy's house in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Dorothy’s house is a small, dull-gray one-room house with basic furnishings and a cyclone cellar. In a cyclone it shakes, whirls, rises, and carries Dorothy and Toto miles away. It lands in the eastern Land of Oz on the Wicked Witch of the East, killing her and freeing the Munchkins from her rule.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter I

The house is a small one-room structure with four walls, a floor, a roof, beds, and basic furnishings. During the cyclone, it shakes, whirls around, rises into the air, and is carried miles away while Dorothy and Toto remain inside.

Chapter II

Dorothy’s house lands in the eastern part of the Land of Oz and falls on the Wicked Witch of the East. The house’s landing kills the witch and frees the Munchkins from her rule.
