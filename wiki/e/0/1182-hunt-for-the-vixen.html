hunt for the vixen

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: hunted the fox, hunting of the Teumesian fox, θήραν

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Cephalus hunts the Teumesian fox with his hound after purification for killing Procris; they overtake each other and become stones near Teumessus. In The Library, Amphitryon persuades Cephalus to join the hunt for the vixen with Laelaps, and Zeus turns the pursuing dog and vixen into stones.
