Sherlock Holmes’ test in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Sherlock Holmes’ test is a reagent-based medico-legal blood-stain test: hæmoglobin yields mahogany colour and brownish precipitate, reliably detecting old or new stains and, Holmes says, surpassing Guiacum and microscopy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

Sherlock Holmes’ test uses a reagent that produces a mahogany colour and brownish precipitate when hæmoglobin is present. Holmes claims that it will provide a reliable medico-legal test for blood stains and could have affected numerous past criminal cases.
