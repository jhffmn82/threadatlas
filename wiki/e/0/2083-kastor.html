Kastor

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Kastor of the brazen mail, Kastor of the golden car, son of Tyndareus

Abstract written by a model

Kastor, a heroic charioteer, is called son of Zeus and Leto and son of Tyndareus. Twin brother of Polydeukes, he guards Sparta and protects mariners and chariot-race victory. Idas kills him with a bronze spear; Polydeukes appeals to Zeus, and they share immortality between earth and heaven.

Kastor is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Nemean Ode X

Kastor was the brother of Polydeukes and was nominally a son of Tyndareus. Idas struck him with a bronze spear after Lynkeus discovered the brothers, and Kastor died with the death-gasp in his throat before Polydeukes appealed to Zeus.
