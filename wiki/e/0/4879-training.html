In Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding, training uses the negative log-likelihood loss to train the retriever and generator; standard training biases the retriever toward label-relevant passages, while the proposed approach focuses on knowledge-relevant passages. In Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, joint training of the retriever and generator includes both components; prior studies showed feasibility, while further exploration remains future work.
