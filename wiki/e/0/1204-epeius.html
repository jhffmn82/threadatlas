Epeius in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Epeius, son of Panopeus, is a boxer, Greek warrior, and foremost Argive woodwright. He fights Acamas with hide gauntlets until the Argives stop them and receives a silver bowl. Taught woodcraft by Athena, he builds the Wooden Horse in three days, secures its doors and ladders, enters last, and guards the bolt.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV

Epeius comes forward for the boxing contest and fights Acamas with bull-hide gauntlets. He and Acamas exchange severe blows until the Argives stop the fight, and Epeius receives one of the two silver bowls from Thetis.

BOOK XII

Epeius, taught woodcraft by Athena, builds the Wooden Horse in three days. He makes its opening and closing secure, enters last, and draws up the ladders before taking his place beside the bolt.
