chariot races in The Iliad

Chariot races are held as funeral contests for Patroclus, with five prizes for finishers. Diomed wins; Antilochus finishes ahead of Menelaus through a disputed maneuver; Meriones is fourth; and Eumelus, thrown from his chariot, finishes last.
