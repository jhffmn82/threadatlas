Abstract written by a model

General Jinjur plans to chop up the Saw-Horse and Jack Pumpkinhead's body for kindling, use the pumpkin to make tarts, burn the Scarecrow, cut the Tin Woodman into pieces for the goats, and make the Woggle-Bug into soup or goulash. Tip is excluded from this plan because he belongs to old Mombi and is to be returned to her.
