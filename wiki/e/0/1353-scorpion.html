Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ASTRONOMY

The scorpion was a very large creature sent up by Earth after Orion threatened to kill every beast on earth. It stung and killed Orion, and Zeus placed it among the stars as a memorial of what had occurred.
