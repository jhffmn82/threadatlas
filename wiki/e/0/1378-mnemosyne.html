Mnemosyne

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Memory, Mncmoajnc, Mnemosyne with the beautiful hair

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Mnemosyne is a Titaness and goddess of memory who reigns over Eleuther's hills and bears nine Muses after nine nights with Zeus. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, Mnemosyne is said to have discovered logic, assigned proper names to things, and assisted human memory.
