Mnemosyne in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Mnemosyne, Titaness and goddess of memory, reigns over Eleuther's hills; after nine nights with Zeus, she bears nine Muses whose songs ease sorrow and ills.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE THEOGONY

Mnemosyne, who reigns over the hills of Eleuther, bore nine daughters to Zeus after nine nights of union. These daughters are the Muses, whose songs bring forgetfulness of ills and rest from sorrow.
