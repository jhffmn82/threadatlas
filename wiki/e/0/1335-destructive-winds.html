destructive winds

Also called: blasts of the cruel winds, cruel uproar, fierce gales, gales, great havoc, harmful gales, mischief, raging blasts, storms, stormy gales, stormy winds, strong wind, winds, winds and storms

Abstract written by a model

Gales of many kinds rage when the Pleiades plunge into the sea or escape Orion’s strength. Notus accompanies Zeus’s heavy autumn rain, stirs and endangers the sea, though winds become steady and favorable fifty days after the solstice. Winds from Typhoeus bring havoc: they scatter ships, destroy sailors, and fill fields with dust and cruel uproar, spoiling them.
