Abstract written by a model

Death is the inescapable limit of human life, holding great power over the body. It claims Koronis and Asklepios, reaches both famous and obscure people, and remains a boundary even healing wisdom cannot cross, though divine favor and honorable stories may preserve fame afterward.

III.

Death claims Koronis before she can deliver her child and later takes Asklepios after Zeus strikes him down. The ode treats death as a boundary that even healing wisdom must not violate.

Nemean Ode VII

Death comes to the famous and the obscure alike. The text presents death as unavoidable, while noting that divine favor and honorable stories can preserve fame after death.
