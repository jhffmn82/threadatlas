Abstract written by a model

is_a friend of Ulysses [left in charge of Ulysses’s household] Mentor, described as a friend of Ulysses left in charge of his household and servants, condemns the people of Ithaca for allowing the suitors to abuse Ulysses's estate. He urges them to stop the wrongdoing, but Leiocritus rejects his appeal. Minerva later speaks to Telemachus in Mentor's likeness and voice.
