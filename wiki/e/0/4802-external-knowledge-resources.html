Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, external knowledge resources provide retrieved information to augment language models, offering a promising solution to hallucination and factual inaccuracy. In SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, external and unverified knowledge is incorporated into retrieval-augmented generation, increasing large language models' vulnerability because attackers can manipulate the knowledge.
