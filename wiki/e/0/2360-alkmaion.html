VIII.

Alkmaion is identified by Amphiaraos as carrying a speckled dragon on his shield and standing foremost at the Kadmean gates. The poet throws garlands and songs upon Alkmaion's grave, describing him as a neighbour and guardian of his possessions and as the source of a prophetic sign at the earth's centre-stone.
