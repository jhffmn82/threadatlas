Thrasyboulos

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Thrasyboulos, Xenokrates’s father, is praised in a poem and shares his kin’s victory glory. Following his uncle, he wisely handles wealth, avoids unrighteous pride, warmly welcomes guests, cultivates knowledge among the Pierides, and honors Poseidon with gladness.

Thrasyboulos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
