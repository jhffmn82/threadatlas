WebQuestions is used as a self-aware downstream task. UAR invokes retrieval for 25.0% of 7B samples and 31.2% of 13B samples, achieving 60.53% and 59.20% accuracy respectively.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.3

On WebQuestions, always-retrieval performs worse than never-retrieval, possibly because some documents disrupt correct knowledge within the model. UAR reduces retrieval frequency and outperforms the never-retrieval method.
