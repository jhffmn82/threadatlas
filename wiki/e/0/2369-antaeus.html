Antaeus

person · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: African giant Antaios, Antaios, house of Antaios, wrestler, ᾿Ανταῖος

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Antaios, the African giant in Libya, roofs Poseidon's temple with strangers' skulls, wrestles Herakles, and is defeated. In The Library, Antaeus, Poseidon's son and king of Libya, kills strangers by wrestling and gains strength from earth until Hercules lifts, breaks, and kills him. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Antaeus, Terra’s giant wrestler son, is invincible while touching Earth until Hercules lifts him away and strangles him in the air.
