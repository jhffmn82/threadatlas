Antaeus

person · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: African giant Antaios, Antaios, house of Antaios, wrestler, ᾿Ανταῖος
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Antaios, the African giant in Libya, roofs Poseidon's temple with strangers' skulls, wrestles Herakles, and is defeated.
  • In The Library, Antaeus, Poseidon's son and king of Libya, kills strangers by wrestling and gains strength from earth until Hercules lifts, breaks, and kills him.
