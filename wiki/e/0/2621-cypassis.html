Cypassis in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · -0015

Cypassis is Corinna’s swarthy, low-born handmaid, faithful and exceptionally skilled at arranging hair in many fashions. Though accused of supplanting Corinna with the speaker, she is first defended as undesired and untouched; later, she is revealed as experienced in an intrigue with him. The speaker seeks her company and threatens to expose their past meetings if she refuses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

ELEGY VI.

Cypassis is Corinna's handmaid, skilled at arranging hair and valuable for the work of her hands. Corinna accuses her of supplanting her with the speaker, but the speaker denies desiring or having sexual intercourse with her.

ELEGY VIII.

Cypassis is Corinna’s handmaid and is highly skilled at arranging hair in many fashions. The speaker describes her as experienced in their intrigue, asks her to reward him with her company, and threatens to reveal their past meetings if she refuses.

Cypassis in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Cypassis is Corinna’s skilled, faithful female servant and hairdresser. Accused of sharing Corinna’s couch with the speaker, she is first denied by him, then revealed as his secret partner; he threatens to expose their meetings if she refuses him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI

Cypassis is skilled at arranging Corinna’s hair and is described as a faithful servant. Corinna accuses her of sharing her mistress’s couch with the speaker, while the speaker first denies the charge and later addresses Cypassis as a partner in their secret affair. He threatens to tell Corinna where, how often, and in what ways they were together if Cypassis refuses him.
