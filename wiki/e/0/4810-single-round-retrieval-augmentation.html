single-round retrieval augmentation in DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models

Weihang Su, Yichen Tang, Qingyao Ai, Zhijing Wu, Yiqun Liu · 2024

Single-round retrieval augmentation uses the initial input to query an external corpus, then adds retrieved knowledge to model input; it can work well for simple needs but may lack sufficient knowledge for complex or lengthy tasks.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

2 / 2.1 .. 2.2

Single-round retrieval augmentation uses the initial user input as a query to retrieve information from an external corpus, then incorporates the retrieved knowledge into the model input. It can be relatively effective for simple tasks or clear-cut information needs, but may not provide all the knowledge required for complex or lengthy tasks.
