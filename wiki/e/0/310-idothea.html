Abstract written by a model

Idothea is a goddess and daughter of Proteus who pities stranded Menelaus at Pharos, prepares an ambush with seal skins and ambrosia, and enables him to capture Proteus.

When the child of morning, rosy-fingered Dawn appeared, Menelaus rose

Idothea, daughter of Proteus, takes pity on Menelaus when he is stranded at Pharos. She tells him how to ambush and hold Proteus, provides seal skins for the ambush, digs the pits, and places ambrosia under the men’s noses to counter the seals’ smell.
