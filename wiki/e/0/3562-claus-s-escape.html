Claus's escape in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Claus's escape is his rescue from the jungle by the Knooks and return from an Awgwa mountain-cave prison by Fairies, both transporting him home to the Laughing Valley.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The Knooks rescue Claus from the jungle after he uses their secret sign and signal, then transport him home to the Laughing Valley. When the Awgwas imprison him in a mountain cave, the Fairies answer his mystic words and carry him back home.
