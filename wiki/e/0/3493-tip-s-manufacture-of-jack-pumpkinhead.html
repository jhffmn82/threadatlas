Tip Manufactures a Pumpkinhead

Tip carves a large orange-red pumpkin into a face with round eyes, a three-cornered nose, and a broad smiling mouth. He cuts saplings for the limbs, fashions a bark cylinder for the body, adds a wooden neck and joints, and dresses the figure in purple trousers, a red shirt, a spotted pink vest, stockings, and shoes.
