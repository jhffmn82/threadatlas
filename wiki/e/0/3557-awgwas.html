Awgwas

Abstract written by a model

Awgwas are a gigantic, transient race of evil creatures, neither mortal nor immortal. Invisible to ordinary people but visible to immortals, they live in rocky mountains, travel swiftly through the air, hate mankind, and devote themselves to wickedness. Without conscience, they influence mortals and naughty children toward anger, quarrels, and bad acts; mortals cannot harm them, while immortals avoid them in fear. They choose King Awgwa by the most horrible proposed deed and obey him. They attack and persecute Claus, steal his toys, and hide them in a rock-covered mountain cave, though Fairies later open it. Refusing to stop and defying Ak, they summon allies for war; immortals destroy them, leaving earthen hillocks, and they perish from earth.

Awgwas is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The Awgwas are gigantic, evil creatures who are invisible to ordinary people but visible to immortals. They influence human minds, delight in wicked deeds, fight among themselves, and use their powers to attack Claus and prevent him from bringing happiness to children.

7. The Great Battle Between Good and Evil

The Awgwas refuse to stop persecuting Claus and defy Ak, claiming that their powers equal those of the immortals. They summon evil allies for war, but the immortals destroy them, turning them into earthen hillocks.

10. Christmas Eve

The Awgwas hid Claus's stolen toys in a deep cave in the mountains and covered the entrance with rocks. Their hiding place is eventually discovered and opened by the Fairies.
