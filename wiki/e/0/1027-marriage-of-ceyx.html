1 section; each heading opens the text

THE MARRIAGE OF CEYX

The Marriage of Ceyx describes Heracles landing from the Argo to look for water and being left behind in Magnesia near Aphetae. It also presents Heracles visiting Ceyx’s house at Trachis, refers to horse-driving Ceyx, calls tables tripods, and describes wood being brought from the forest to be burned.
