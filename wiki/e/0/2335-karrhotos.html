Abstract written by a model

Karrhotos, Alexibios’s son and brother of Arkesilas’s wife, drives Arkesilas’s Delphi-supplied chariot safely through twelve Pythian turns, preserves its harness, wins a crown, and is urged as Arkesilas’s chief friend.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

V.

Karrhotos, the brother of Arkesilas's wife, drove Arkesilas's chariot at the Pythian games. He handled the reins safely through twelve swift turns without breaking the strong harness and returned crowned with victory. Arkesilas is urged to honor him as his chief friend.
