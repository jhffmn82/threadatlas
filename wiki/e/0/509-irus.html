Abstract written by a model

Irus, born Arnaeus, is a notorious Ithacan beggar, glutton, drunkard, and errand-runner despite his hulking appearance and little strength. He insults and challenges Ulysses at a doorway, is struck and defeated, then dragged outside to guard the gate-house from dogs and pigs.

Irus is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK XVIII

Irus is a hulking, notorious beggar who insults Ulysses and tries to drive him from the doorway. He is forced into a fight, struck under the ear by Ulysses, and dragged outside to sit by the gate-house and keep dogs and pigs away.
