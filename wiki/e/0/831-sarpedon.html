Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, Sarpedon is a Lycian prince, king, captain, and Jove’s son who leads the Lycians, fights for Troy, and dies on the plain of Troy after confronting Patroclus. In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Sarpedon is the son of Europa and Zeus who rules Lycia with Zeus-given honor and long life, leads the Lycians to Troy, fights beside Hector, and attacks the Danaans' wall. In The Library, Sarpedon is Zeus's son, Lycian leader and king, who fights among Trojan allies and is reported killed by Hector or Patroclus. In The Library, Sarpedon is the son of Poseidon and brother of Poltys, born to Europa after Zeus came together with her in Crete; after quarrelling with Minos over Miletus, he flees, allies with Cilix, becomes king of Lycia, and receives three generations’ life from Zeus. In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Sarpedon is shown bearded beside Memnon in a painting, leaning his head on both hands while one of Memnon’s hands rests on his shoulder. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Sarpedon is a Trojan leader and Jupiter's son from Lycia who champions Troy, confronts Patroclus, dies by his spear, and is carried to Lycia for burial.

Sarpedon in The Iliad

Homer · 749 to 724 BC · the work’s page · read it where Sarpedon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sarpedon is a Lycian prince, king, captain, and Jove’s son, dearly loved by Jove and counted the best of the allied captains. He leads the Lycians from their distant land beside the Xanthus with Glaucus, his trusted comrade and brother, and insists that their honors require them to fight in the forefront. Having left wife, infant son, and wealth in Lycia, he rebukes Hector and fights for Troy. He kills Tlepolemus but is wounded in the left thigh, carried away, and revived. Later he leads the assault on the Achaean wall, kills Alcmaon, and tears down a battlement to make a breach. At the end, after reproving fleeing Lycians, he confronts Patroclus, is struck near the heart, and dies on the plain of Troy, calling on Glaucus to rally the Lycians and protect his armour.

Sarpedon was born to Europa after Zeus came together with her in Crete. Homer instead said that Sarpedon was born to Zeus and Laodameia, the daughter of Bellerophon.

Sarpedon quarrelled with Minos over their love for Miletus and fled after Minos gained the advantage in war. He allied himself with Cilix against the Lycians, received part of the country, and became king of Lycia; Zeus granted him life for three generations.
