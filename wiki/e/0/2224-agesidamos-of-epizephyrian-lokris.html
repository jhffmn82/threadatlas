Agesidamos of Epizephyrian Lokris

Abstract written by a model

Agesidamos, son of Archestratos and a man of Epizephyrian Lokris, won the boys’ Olympic boxing-match at Olympia. His victory brought him a crown of golden olive and a victory song: Pindar praises his beauty and natural gifts and promises that song and music will preserve his fame in his Italian home.

Agesidamos of Epizephyrian Lokris is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

X.

Agesidamos, son of Archestratos, won the boys' boxing-match. Pindar promises to place a glory of sweet strains upon his crown of golden olive and to write a longer ode for the celebration of his victory in his Italian home.

XI.

Agesidamos, son of Archestratos, won the boys' boxing-match at Olympia. Pindar praises his beauty, natural gifts, and victory, and says that song and music will preserve his fame.
