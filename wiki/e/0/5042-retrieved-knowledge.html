Abstract written by a model

In "Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy," non-parametric knowledge is processed as a whole under structural constraints to help a large language model generate better responses in later iterations. In "BlendFilter: Advancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models via Query Generation Blending and Knowledge Filtering," retrieved knowledge is obtained separately for an original query and two augmented forms, then filtered independently to assess necessity and avoid the harder, larger union set. In "Ask in Any Modality," retrieved data is refined or expanded before generation through additional text chunks, image tokens, structured data, entity relationships, semantic descriptions, and auxiliary multimodal context. In "SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation," retrieved knowledge snippets are re-ranked and dynamically integrated, preserving those that most reduce large language models' self-aware uncertainty.
