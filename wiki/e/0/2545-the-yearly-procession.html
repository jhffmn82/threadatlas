Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY XIII.

The yearly procession begins when a pipe gives its customary signal and moves along covered paths. Snow-white heifers, calves, a pig, and the leader of the flock accompany it, while the people observe religious silence and Juno follows her priestesses.
