Abstract written by a model

Arabic language is the research focus in work on Retrieval Augmented Generation, motivated by its significance and wide use despite relatively less emphasis in this field. The work aims to improve comprehension and generation of Arabic text, evaluates Arabic-capable embedding models with a publicly available Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset, and studies semantic retrieval and Retrieval Augmented Generation. It notes Arabic’s dialectical nature and prior work on classification, similarity, embeddings, sentiment analysis, and question answering. The study then reviews LLM and multilingual embedding support: several current models support Arabic, though not all do. It concludes that Meta SONAR’s claimed advantages over other models were overstated for Arabic.

The Arabic language is significant and widely used but has received relatively less research emphasis in Retrieval Augmented Generation. The study aims to improve models’ ability to comprehend and generate Arabic text effectively.

I.

Arabic language is the specific language focus of the research. The study evaluates embedding models capable of representing Arabic using a publicly available Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset.

Arabic language support is not available in all listed LLMs. The text identifies OpenAI GPT-turbo-3.5, Open AI GPT 4.0, Google Bard, Microsoft Bing Chat, Google PaLM2, and Jais as current Arabic LLM models.
