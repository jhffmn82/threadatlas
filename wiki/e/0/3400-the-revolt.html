Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The Revolt is General Jinjur’s uprising against male rule in Oz. Its stated aims are to conquer the Emerald City, dethrone the Scarecrow, obtain the city’s gems and treasury, and gain power for the girls.

A Nickel-Plated Emperor

The rebellion causes the girls of Oz to drive the Scarecrow from the Emerald City, seize the city, rob its treasury, and govern it themselves. Nick Chopper regards the rebellion as a calamity and agrees to oppose it.
