Mr. Moore

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Mr. Moore is a legal adviser and Privy Seal colleague of Samuel Pepys. He advises on the Pepys family will, explaining that its legatee-witnesses do not invalidate it, then reviews it further with Pepys. He also handles Seal-day business, reads bills for Lord Roberts, gives Pepys Hooker’s Ecclesiastical Polity, and socializes with him.

Mr. Moore is in one work on this site, The Diary of Samuel Pepys. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

opening

Mr. Moore advises Samuel Pepys that the will’s three witnesses being legatees does not harm the will. He later reads the will with Samuel, gives further opinions, accompanies him to an ordinary and the Theatre, and walks with him in London.

opening

Mr. Moore tells Pepys that he is needed for the next day's Seal day because it is Pepys's month to attend Lord Roberts. He works with Pepys at the Privy Seal and reads over the bills when Lord Roberts finally arrives.
