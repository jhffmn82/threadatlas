Mormon caravan in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

An enormous westbound caravan of about ten thousand Mormons crosses the desert with wagons, carts, armed horsemen, women, and children, rescuing John Ferrier and Lucy on the Sierra Blanco.

PART II. / CHAPTER I.

The Mormon caravan is an enormous westbound migration containing wagons, carts, armed horsemen, women, and children. It discovers John Ferrier and Lucy on the Sierra Blanco, rescues them, and provides them with food, drink, and a wagon.
