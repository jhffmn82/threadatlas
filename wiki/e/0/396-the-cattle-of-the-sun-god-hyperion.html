2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XII

The cattle of the sun-god are kept on the Thrinacian island in seven herds of fifty head each. They neither breed nor become fewer, and Circe warns Ulysses's crew to leave them unharmed.

“Here she ended, and dawn enthroned in gold began to show in heaven,

The cattle of the sun-god are described as fair and goodly and are guarded as the property of Hyperion. Ulysses's men slaughter the best of them after their provisions run out, and supernatural signs follow, including hides that crawl and meat that lows like cattle.
