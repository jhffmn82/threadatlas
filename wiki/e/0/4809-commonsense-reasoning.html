Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, commonsense reasoning was a task requiring world and commonsense knowledge and evaluated with StrategyQA using yes/no questions and exact-match scoring. In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, commonsense reasoning was an evaluation task for ITER-RETGEN, assessed on StrategyQA with accuracy and Acc† using few-shot prompting. In Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Commonsense Reasoning was one of the evaluated NLP tasks, measured with accuracy.
