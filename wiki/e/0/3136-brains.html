brains in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Brains are what the Scarecrow desires from Oz so he can be as much a man as anyone in Oz. Oz promises brains and wisdom for helping kill the Wicked Witch, then says experience has already given him knowledge but will still provide brains to learn to use.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XI

The Scarecrow asks Oz to replace the straw in his head with brains so that he can become as much a man as anyone in Oz. Oz promises him many brains and wisdom beyond that of anyone else in the Land of Oz if he helps kill the Wicked Witch of the West.

Chapter XV

Oz tells the Scarecrow that he does not truly need brains because he is gaining knowledge through experience. Nevertheless, Oz promises to stuff the Scarecrow's head with brains and says the Scarecrow must learn to use them himself.
