the bow-stringing contest in The Odyssey

Homer · about 632 to 599 BC · the work’s page · read it where the bow-stringing contest first appears

Abstract written by a model

The bow-stringing contest tests the suitors by requiring them to string Ulysses’s bow and shoot an arrow through twelve axes. Telemachus and the suitors attempt it, but fail; Antinous postpones it until after the feast of Apollo. Ulysses then strings the bow and completes the trial, surpassing all suitors.
