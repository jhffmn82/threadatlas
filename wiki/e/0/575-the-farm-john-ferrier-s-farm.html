John Ferrier’s farm grows from a log-house and tract of fertile land into a prosperous property with a roomy villa. Lucy Ferrier lives there and assists her adopted father in his work, and Jefferson Hope repeatedly visits the farm.

John Ferrier’s farm is watched on every side after he rejects the marriage proposals and defies the authorities. Ferrier, Lucy, and Jefferson Hope escape from it through a side window and cross the fields under cover of darkness.
