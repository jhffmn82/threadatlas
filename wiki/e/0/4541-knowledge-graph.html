I. INTRODUCTION

Knowledge graphs provide the structured integration used by KG-RAG. They explicitly organize relationships between concepts, enabling retrieval that supports more coherent and logically connected responses.

II. FOUNDATIONS OF KG-RAG

Knowledge Graphs structure domain knowledge as interconnected vertices and edges. In the framework, they enhance contextual understanding, support knowledge-guided retrieval, reduce context fragmentation, and improve the explainability of reasoning.

III. AI TUTOR DESIGN: THE KG-RAG SOLUTION

The knowledge graph represents course content through extracted entities and relationships stored as structured triples. It is traversed during knowledge-guided retrieval to incorporate related concepts into the context provided to the LLM.
