Abstract written by a model

The Sustainable Development Goals are a reference framework established by experts in the United Nations to guide human activities toward the common good. Environmental-assessment practitioners increasingly need to incorporate them. They structure tasks that identify textual evidence and detect correspondences between targets and environmental-impact assessment reports. Experiments use selected targets from SDGs 6, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15, covering a meaningful but incomplete part of the framework; SDG 14 targets occur most often, followed by SDG 15.
