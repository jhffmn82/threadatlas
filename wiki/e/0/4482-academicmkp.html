AcademicMKP in Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training

Yifan Gao, Qingyu Yin, Zheng Li, Rui Meng, Tong Zhao, Bing Yin, Irwin King, Michael R. Lyu · 2022

AcademicMKP is a multilingual academic-domain keyphrase-generation dataset drawn from Microsoft Academic Graph. It contains 2,693 manually checked, high-quality computer-science papers in Chinese and Korean: titles and abstracts are source texts, and author-provided keyphrases or keywords are targets. The data comprise 1,884 training, 268 development, and 541 test examples, with passages averaging 171 in length, four keyphrases, and 31.3% absent keyphrases. It is one of two new datasets for multilingual keyphrase generation and is part of a six-language collection. It is used to evaluate multilingual approaches; external English data does not improve results, attributed to a domain gap with English papers in KP20K.

Abstract

AcademicMKP is one of two new datasets contributed for multilingual keyphrase generation. It covers languages within the paper's six-language dataset collection.

Introduction

AcademicMKP is a multilingual keyphrase dataset in the academic domain. It contains 2,693 Chinese and Korean papers, using titles and abstracts as source texts and author-provided keyphrases as targets.

Model .. 4.3

The AcademicMKP Dataset contains Chinese and Korean data, with 1,884 training examples, 268 development examples, and 541 test examples in total. Its passages have an average length of 171 and an average of four keyphrases, with 31.3% absent keyphrases.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It contains 2,693 manually checked scientific-paper examples in Chinese and Korean. Paper titles and abstracts are used as source text, and author-provided keywords are used as target output.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

AcademicMKP is used to evaluate multilingual keyphrase-generation approaches. External English data has no positive effect on this dataset, which is attributed to a domain gap between multilingual papers and English papers in KP20K.
