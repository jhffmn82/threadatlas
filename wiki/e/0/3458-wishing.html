In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The wishing pills’ instructions promise that a wish will be immediately granted after the required counting. Jack Pumpkinhead wants to wish that his head will never spoil, but the travelers believe the pills are unusable.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

Wishing can alter events immediately, even producing a result that makes an earlier action seem not to have happened. Tip’s wish reverses his swallowing of a pill, while the Woggle-Bug’s wish repairs the Gump.
