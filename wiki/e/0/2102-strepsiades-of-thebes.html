Strepsiades of Thebes is a pankratiast who wins the pankration at Isthmos; renowned for strength, shape, and valour, he shares his crown with his uncle.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode VI

Strepsiades wins the pankration prize at Isthmos. He is described as remarkably strong, handsome, and courageous, and he gives his uncle of the same name a share in his crown.
