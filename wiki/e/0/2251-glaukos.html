Glaukos

Abstract written by a model

Glaukos, from Lydia, fought for the Trojan side in the Trojan War; feared by the Danaoi, he claimed Peirene’s ruler as his father.

Glaukos is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
