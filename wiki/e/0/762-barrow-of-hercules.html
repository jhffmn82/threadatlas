barrow of Hercules in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

The barrow of Hercules is a high masonry earth-barrow made by Trojans and Pallas Minerva, serving Hercules as refuge and sheltering gods watching battle.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XX.

The barrow of Hercules is a high earth-barrow made by the Trojans and Pallas Minerva. Neptune leads the gods supporting the Achaeans there, and they sit under a thick cloud of darkness while watching the battle.
