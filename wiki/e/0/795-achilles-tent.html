is_built_for Achilles [by the Myrmidons] Achilles' tent is a lofty dwelling built by the Myrmidons and enclosed by a fenced courtyard with a heavy barred gate. Mercury brings Priam and the treasure inside, where Priam finds Achilles seated with his men.
