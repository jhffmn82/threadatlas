forbidden desire in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

is_a desire for what is denied Forbidden desire makes people strive for what is denied and covet what is forbidden, like a sick person longing for prohibited water. The speaker uses the horse, Argus, Danae, and Penelope to show that restriction can intensify desire, while permission can weaken it.
