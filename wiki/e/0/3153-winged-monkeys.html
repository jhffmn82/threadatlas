Winged Monkeys in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Winged Monkeys are a band of flying creatures led by the King of the Monkeys. Once a free, mischievous people living in the great forest, they became bound by the Golden Cap charm to obey its owner three times; they belong to their country and cannot cross the desert. Under the Wicked Witch of the West's final command, they attack Dorothy's companions, carry Dorothy to the Witch's castle, and cannot harm Dorothy or Toto because of the Power of Good. When Dorothy gains the Cap, they carry her, her companions, and Toto to the Emerald City, though they cannot take her to Kansas. They are feared by Oz after his encounter with them in the Land of the West. On Dorothy's last summons, they carry the travelers over the hill to the Quadlings. Glinda then uses the Cap to send the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, and Lion to their destinations, exhausts its powers, and gives it to their King, freeing them forevermore.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XII

The Winged Monkeys are summoned for the third and final time by the Wicked Witch’s Golden Cap. They batter and disable the Tin Woodman, strip the straw from the Scarecrow, tie up the Cowardly Lion, and carry Dorothy to the Witch’s castle. They do not harm Dorothy or Toto because Dorothy is protected by the Power of Good, and their service to the Witch ends after this command.

Chapter XIV

The Winged Monkeys are summoned by Dorothy and carry her companions and Toto through the air to the Emerald City. Their King tells Dorothy that they were once a free, mischievous people and are now bound to obey the owner of the Golden Cap three times.

Chapter XV

The Winged Monkeys are mentioned as beings the Scarecrow threatens to call upon if Oz refuses to receive the travelers. Oz is frightened by this threat because he had previously encountered them in the Land of the West.

Chapter XVIII

The Winged Monkeys answer Dorothy's second call and enter the Throne Room. Their king tells her that they cannot leave their country or cross the desert to Kansas, so they fly away without helping her.

Chapter XXII

The Winged Monkeys answer Dorothy’s summons and carry the four travelers and Toto over the hill to the country of the Quadlings. Their leader tells Dorothy that this is the last time she can summon them before they depart.

Chapter XXIII

The Winged Monkeys are to carry the Scarecrow to the Emerald City, the Tin Woodman to the land of the Winkies, and the Lion to his forest. After Glinda uses the Golden Cap’s powers three times, she will give it to the King of the Monkeys so the Winged Monkeys can be free forevermore.
