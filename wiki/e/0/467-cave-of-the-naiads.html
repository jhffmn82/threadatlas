Abstract written by a model

The Cave of the Naiads is a sacred cavern near Phorcys’ harbour, with two entrances, water, vessels, and looms. Sacred to Naiads, it becomes Ulysses’ treasure store when Minerva helps hide and seal his gold, bronze, and clothing.

BOOK XIII

The Cave of the Naiads is a sacred cavern near the harbour of Phorcys, with stone mixing bowls, wine-jars, water, and looms on which the nymphs weave sea-purple robes. Minerva and Ulysses use it to hide his gold, bronze, and clothing, sealing the entrance with a stone.
