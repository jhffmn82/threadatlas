Knowledge-Intensive Generation

Abstract written by a model

Knowledge-Intensive Generation is a series of natural language generation tasks requiring large external knowledge sources, such as passage corpora or knowledge graphs. It matters because it combines access to question-relevant knowledge with natural-language response generation and is more challenging than fact checking and slot filling. It unfolds by retrieving question-relevant passages, then generating a response conditioned on the question and retrieved passages.

Introduction

Knowledge-Intensive Generation consists of tasks that require external knowledge sources, such as passage corpora or knowledge graphs, to generate natural language responses to questions.

Related Works

Knowledge-intensive generation consists of NLG tasks that require access to large external knowledge sources and the generation of natural-language responses. It is more challenging than knowledge-intensive tasks such as fact checking and slot filling.

Problem Statement

Knowledge-Intensive Generation is formulated as retrieving question-relevant passages from a knowledge source and generating a natural language response conditioned on the question and those passages.
