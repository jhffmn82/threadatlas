the tournament of axes

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a tournament of axes, trial of the axes, your tournament

Abstract written by a model

The tournament of axes is Penelope’s planned test for the suitors: they must string Ulysses’ bow and shoot an arrow through twelve axes. Telemachus prepares the trial by leveling a groove and setting the axes in a straight row. Penelope will follow the successful contestant, though Ulysses predicts none will succeed before his return.

Penelope plans to hold a tournament in which the suitors must string Ulysses’ bow and shoot an arrow through twelve axes. She will leave Ulysses’ house and follow the successful contestant, though Ulysses predicts that he will return before any suitor can complete the challenge.

BOOK XXI

Telemachus prepares the trial by placing the axes in a straight row in a groove he has dug and leveled. The proposed feat is to shoot an arrow through all twelve axes.
