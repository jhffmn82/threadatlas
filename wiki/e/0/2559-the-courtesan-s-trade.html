the courtesan's trade in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · -0015

The courtesan’s trade uses intermediaries, religious pretexts, calculated refusals, feigned affection, jealousy, deception, and perjury to attract, control, and profit from wealthy lovers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY VII.

The courtesan's trade is portrayed through Dipsas's instructions on attracting and managing wealthy lovers. It depends on beauty, calculated refusals, pretended affection, jealousy, deception, perjury, and the continual pursuit of material gain.
