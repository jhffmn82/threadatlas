ELI5 in BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

David Rau, Hervé Déjean, Nadezhda Chirkova, Thibault Formal, Shuai Wang, Stéphane Clinchant, Vassilina Nikoulina · 2024

ELI5 is a long-form question answering dataset with long reference labels. On its 1.5k-example dev split, multiple language models and retrieval systems yield zero Match values while LLMeval varies. Retrieval performance is below 0.3 R@5, generation deteriorates with retrieved context, and Closed Book outperforms Oracle retrieval. Partial annotation or missing relevant documents may explain these results, making ELI5 likely unsuitable for RAG evaluation at present.

Experiments .. 5.6

ELI5 contains long reference labels, making evaluation more difficult for surface-based metrics. Its performance deteriorates with retrieved context, and even oracle retrieval does not improve it in the reported experiment.

B

ELI5 is analyzed as a potential RAG evaluation dataset. Its retrievers achieve low retrieval performance, while the Closed Book setting outperforms Oracle retrieval, suggesting partial annotation and/or missing relevant documents.

E

KILT ELI5 is evaluated on a 1.5k-example development set with multiple language models and retrieval systems. Its Match values are reported as zero, while LLMeval varies across models and retrieval configurations.
