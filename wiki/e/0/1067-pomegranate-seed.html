Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the pomegranate seed binds Persephone to return to the underworld. In The Library, the pomegranate seed is given by Pluto to Persephone to eat, and Persephone eats it without foreseeing the consequence.
