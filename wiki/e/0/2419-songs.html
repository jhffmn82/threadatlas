Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode IV

Songs, described as wise daughters of the Muses, comfort people and are more enduring than deeds when shaped by the Graces. They celebrate Timasarchos's victories and make a person's rare deeds comparable to those of kings.
