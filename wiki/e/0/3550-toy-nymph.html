toy nymph in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

A clay toy nymph resembling Necile is formed, sun-baked, painted, and given by Claus to Bessie, who loves and calls it a dolly.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

Claus forms the toy nymph from moist clay, bakes it in the sun until it becomes hard, and paints it to resemble Necile. He gives it to Bessie, who loves it and calls it a dolly.
