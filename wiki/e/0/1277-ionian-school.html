Ionian School

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Epic School, Ionic School, Ionic School of Epic poetry

Abstract written by a model

The Ionian School was a third-period school of epic poetry in Ionia and the islands. Dominated by and following Homeric tradition, it treated romantic subjects in a stereotyped heroic style while avoiding Homer’s own subjects. Its originality lay in developing neglected legends of Troy, Thebes, the world’s origins, and the wars of heaven.

INTRODUCTION

The Ionian School followed the Homeric tradition by treating romantic subjects in a stereotyped heroic style. Its originality chiefly lay in selecting legends that earlier poets had neglected or treated only briefly and imperfectly.

The Ionic School

The Ionic School of epic poetry was dominated by the Homeric tradition but avoided directly cultivating the subjects treated by Homer. Its poets developed legends about Troy, Thebes, the origins of the world, and the wars of heaven.
