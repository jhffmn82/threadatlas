  • In The Marvelous Land of Oz, Glinda the Good helps identify Ozma as the rightful heir, captures Mombi, restores Ozma's form, and advises Princess Ozma.
  • In Ozma of Oz, Glinda the Good opposes an army in battle and advises Dorothy about the risks of the magic belt and giving it to Ozma.
  • In The Road to Oz, Glinda the Sorceress arrives as an important, kind, and powerful sorceress, greets Dorothy and her companions, and receives royal attendants.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, Glinda the Good creates and protects Bunnybury, safeguards Cuttenclip, and makes Oz invisible.
  • In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, Glinda the Good learns of a request to contact Dorothy and explains how to undo the Liquid of Petrifaction.
  • In Tik-Tok of Oz, Glinda the Good guards Oz's peace, reads her Magic Record Book, and blocks an invading army by altering a mountain pass.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, Glinda gives the Scarecrow a magic box and sends him to protect and rescue Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill.
  • In Rinkitink in Oz, Glinda learns of Prince Bobo's enchantment, questions Bilbil, and restores Bobo's human form.
  • In The Lost Princess of Oz, Glinda the Good protects Ozma and Oz's people while directing the search for her stolen magical possessions and the missing Ozma.
  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Glinda the Good is described as the world's most powerful sorceress and an authorized restorer of transformations.
  • In The Magic of Oz, Glinda discovers how to make emerald thread, advises Dorothy about Ozma's birthday surprise, and presents Ozma with a gown.
  • In Glinda of Oz, Glinda rules and resides in Quadling Country, practices sorcery, and leads the rescue of Ozma, Dorothy, and the Skeezers.
  • In The Royal Book of Oz, Glinda, the Good Sorceress of Oz, uses her Magic Record Book and sends Ozma news of renewed war.
  • In Kabumpo in Oz, Glinda investigates Ozma's disappearance, locates her in Ev, attempts to rescue her, and helps restore the palace.
  • In The Cowardly Lion of Oz, Glinda learns of the Cowardly Lion's danger, helps free him, and attempts to reverse his petrification.
  • In Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz, Glinda discovers Ozma's identity and forces Mombi to disenchant Tip.

Glinda the Good in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Glinda the Good is a gentle, powerful, and mighty sorceress, the delightful Queen of the South Country who rules the Quadlings and commands her own army. She is sought by the Scarecrow and his companions for help after Jinjur's takeover. Already aware of their escape and of the crisis in the Emerald City, she consults the Wizard's records book in her library and determines that Ozma is the rightful heir and that Mombi likely helped conceal her. Glinda marches against the Emerald City, demands Mombi, recognizes and frees the enchanted Jellia Jamb, and continues searching when Mombi escapes as a rose. She discovers the disguise, prevents Mombi's flight, pursues her, and destroys her final transformation magic with golden thread. After capturing Mombi, Glinda uses her truth-testing magic pearl and a threat of death to force the truth about Ozma, then has Mombi undo the spell that made Ozma into Tip. She later enters the city with the Gump, advises Princess Ozma on all occasions, and helps her rule wisely. She also owns the Golden Cap, which commands the Winged Monkeys, and rides in a palanquin borne by twelve servants.

Glinda in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Glinda is a good, powerful sorceress and witch who rules the Quadling country of Oz. Guided by the Magic Record Book, she learns that the Cowardly Lion is in grave danger and joins the Emerald City party, traveling to Mudge by magic words. There she works with the Wizard of Oz to control Crunch and free the Lion from burial, then helps attempt to reverse the Lion’s petrification, though their magic fails. She also assures Ozma about future rule of the skyle.
