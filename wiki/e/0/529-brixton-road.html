Brixton Road in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Brixton Road is a roadway containing the White Hart Tavern, Holland Grove, and an empty house. A gold wedding ring is supposedly found there. Late in a storm, Jefferson Hope brings Enoch Drebber to the house for a confrontation; Drebber’s body is discovered there, and Tobias Gregson suspects Arthur Charpentier.

Brixton Road is the location of the murder and the roadway where the gold wedding ring was supposedly found between the White Hart Tavern and Holland Grove.

Brixton Road is where Enoch J. Drebber’s body is discovered in an empty house. Tobias Gregson believes Arthur Charpentier followed Drebber there and killed him.

CHAPTER VI.

The Brixton Road contains the house that Jefferson Hope uses as a secluded place for his confrontation with Enoch Drebber. Hope drives Drebber there late at night during a storm.
