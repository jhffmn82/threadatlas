sentence bottleneck auto-encoder in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023

Sentence bottleneck auto-encoder extracts answer knowledge representations by using a frozen pre-trained encoder, aggregating its states with multihead attention into one bottleneck representation, then reconstructing the answer through that representation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

The sentence bottleneck auto-encoder is used to obtain answer knowledge representations. Its encoder produces states that a multihead attention bottleneck aggregates into a single representation, which is treated as the knowledge representation of the input answer.
