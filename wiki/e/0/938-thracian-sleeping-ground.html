Thracian sleeping-ground

place · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Thracian army camp, lines, our lines, sleeping-ground of the Thracians, the place, those dead men

Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, the Thracian sleeping-ground is the separated camp at the far end of the Trojan forces, where Ulysses and Diomed find the soldiers asleep in three orderly rows and attack them. In The Rhesus of Euripides, the Thracian army camp is inadequately guarded after the men lie down exhausted, allowing Odysseus and Diomede to enter, kill Rhesus and others, wound the Charioteer, and take the horses.
