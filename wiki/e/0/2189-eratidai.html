Abstract written by a model

The Eratidai, former royal dynasty of Ialysos, lost kingly power two hundred years earlier yet remained pre-eminent in Rhodes; Diagoras belonged to them, and the city shared their joy in his success.

VII.

The Eratidai were the former royal dynasty of Ialysos, whose kingly power had been extinct for two hundred years but whose family remained pre-eminent. Diagoras belonged to this family, and the city rejoices with the Eratidai in his success.
