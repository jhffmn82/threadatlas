Abstract written by a model

The china people are small, brittle figures of china, including milkmaids, shepherdesses, princesses, shepherds, princes, and clowns. They live contentedly and move about in The Dainty China Country, but away from it their joints stiffen. They fear breaking, though damage can be mended imperfectly.

Chapter XX

The china people include milkmaids, shepherdesses, princesses, shepherds, princes, and clowns, all made of china and small enough that the tallest is no higher than Dorothy's knee. They fear being broken, and damaged china people can be glued or mended but may no longer look as pretty.
