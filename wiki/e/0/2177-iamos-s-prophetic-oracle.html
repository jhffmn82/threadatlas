A prophetic oracle, received by Iamos from Apollo at Kronion, was established at Zeus’s altar after Herakles founded the Olympic festival and became hereditary in the Iamid clan.

Narrative, section by section

VI.

Iamos received prophecy from Apollo at the rock of Kronion. He was first to heed Apollo’s truthful voice, and after Herakles founded the Olympic festival and games, he was to establish another oracle at Zeus’s altar.
