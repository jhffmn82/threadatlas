Abstract written by a model

In A Study in Scarlet, Sherlock Holmes organizes an informal, mobile information-gathering corps that systematically searches London’s cab proprietors and finds Jefferson Hope among cabdrivers. In The Sign of the Four, the Baker Street Irregulars arrive at Holmes’s rooms, receive payment to search for the Aurora along both riverbanks and watch Mordecai Smith’s landing-stage, with a guinea promised for finding it.

Chapter VIII

The Baker Street Irregulars arrive at Holmes’s rooms after receiving his message. Holmes pays them to search both banks of the river, watch Mordecai Smith’s landing-stage, and report immediately if they find the Aurora, offering a guinea to the boy who locates it.
