Sherlock Holmes's Street Arab detective corps in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Sherlock Holmes organizes an informal, mobile information-gathering corps, which systematically searches London’s cab proprietors and finds Jefferson Hope among cabdrivers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Sherlock Holmes organizes a Street Arab detective corps and sends its members systematically to every cab proprietor in London. The corps successfully finds Jefferson Hope among the city’s cabdrivers.

the Baker Street Irregulars in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

The Baker Street Irregulars, dirty and ragged unofficial detectives, arrive at Holmes’s rooms, receive payment to search for the Aurora along both riverbanks and watch Mordecai Smith’s landing-stage, with a guinea promised for finding it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter VIII

The Baker Street Irregulars arrive at Holmes’s rooms after receiving his message. Holmes pays them to search both banks of the river, watch Mordecai Smith’s landing-stage, and report immediately if they find the Aurora, offering a guinea to the boy who locates it.
