journey through the world in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Claus, companion and pupil of Ak, is taken on a world journey from the Forest of Burzee to cities and dwellings of men. Though Necile grieves and Claus is honored, he observes humanity across regions and occupations, farmers, warriors, merchants, and children, then returns to Burzee transformed from an ever-joyous boy into a grave, thoughtful youth.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

5. The Master Woodsman

Ak plans for Claus to accompany him on a journey through the world after Ak has visited his many forests. Claus is pleased by the honor, while Necile is distressed by his departure.

6. Claus Discovers Humanity

Ak's journey around the world with Claus begins in a clearing in the Forest of Burzee and carries them through the air to cities and dwellings in many parts of the earth. They observe farmers, warriors, merchants, and children before returning to Burzee. The journey changes Claus from an ever-joyous boy into a grave and thoughtful youth.
