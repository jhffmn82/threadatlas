Ulysses's scar

Abstract written by a model

Ulysses’s leg scar, made above the knee by a wild boar during a hunt on Mount Parnassus, is touched by Euryclea while she washes his foot and identifies him to her.

Ulysses's scar is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The scar on Ulysses’ leg was caused by a wild boar that ripped him above the knee during a hunt with Autolycus and his sons on Mount Parnassus. Euryclea recognizes Ulysses by touching it while washing his foot.
