Abstract written by a model

In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, output is generated at each iteration, with the previous output helping retrieve paragraphs in later iterations, and the last output being produced as the final response. In An Information Bottleneck Perspective for Effective Noise Filtering on Retrieval-Augmented Generation, generated output arises from retrieved passages and may be influenced by harmful or toxic retrieved content. In Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review, generated output is evaluated for correctness, faithfulness to the provided context, relevance to the user’s question, fluency, readability, and other quality characteristics.
