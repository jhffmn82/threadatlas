the funeral of Achilles

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Achilles' funeral, death-pyre, death-rites of Achilles, death-rites of her son, fire, lay out the body of Achilles, pyre, woeful bale
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the funeral of Achilles involves the Achaeans laying out Achilles’s body while Thetis and the Muses bewail him; Thetis takes him from the pyre to the White Island, and the Achaeans pile a cairn and hold games in his honour.
  • In The Fall of Troy, death-rites of Achilles is held at the Hellespont and includes a vast pyre, battle gear, slain Trojan men, horses, cattle, sheep, captive women’s mantles, gold, amber, oils, honey, wine, and perfumes; Hermes summons winds to fan the fire, and the Achaeans gather the bones and build a barrow beside the Hellespont.
