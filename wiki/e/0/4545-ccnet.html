Abstract written by a model

CCNet is a web-scale retrieval corpus of 906 million documents; in reported DPR+FiD results, it underperforms Wikipedia retrieval on NaturalQuestions and HotpotQA.

CCNet is in one work on this site, Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
