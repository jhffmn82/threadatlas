warfare

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, warfare is what the disguised Ulysses claims to have favored over farming or domestic life, leading ambushes, killing enemies, and commanding campaigns before Troy. In The Fall of Troy, warfare and strategy contrasts direct warfare with cunning strategy, requiring courage and endurance for both open fighting and dangerous ambush.
