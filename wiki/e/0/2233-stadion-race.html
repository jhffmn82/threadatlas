Stadion race in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Stadion race is a short foot-race of about a furlong or 220 yards; Xenophon won at Olympia in 464 B.C., and Deinis won a race celebrated in a Nemean ode.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIII.

The Stadion race was the short foot-race of about a furlong or 220 yards. Xenophon won it at Olympia in 464 B.C.

Nemean Ode VIII

The short foot-race was won by Deinis. The ode celebrates the glory from Nemea associated with two races run by Deinis and Meges.
