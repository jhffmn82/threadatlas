Abstract written by a model

Frogs, a people living in and around the lake and ruled by Puff-jaw, gather against him, arm to fight the Mice, suffer heavy losses, and are rescued when Zeus sends crabs.

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

The Frogs gather in council, blame Puff-jaw for the quarrel, and arm themselves to fight the Mice. They suffer heavy losses during the battle and are rescued from near destruction when Zeus sends the crabs.
