Aitna city

Abstract written by a model

Aitna city is a new-made city near the mountain, founded by Hieron with god-built freedom and governed by Dorian laws of Aigimios in Hyllic pattern. Named for Aitna after Hieron’s chariot victory, it receives a triumph from Sikyon; the song seeks good laws, enduring prosperity, and shared civic glory.

Aitna city is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

I.

Aitna city was founded by Hieron near the mountain and was ennobled when its name was proclaimed after Hieron’s chariot victory. It was founded with freedom under laws of Dorian, Hyllic pattern.

Nemean Ode IX

Aitna is the new-made city to which the triumph is led from Sikyon. The song asks Zeus to grant its people good laws, long-lasting prosperity, and a share in the glories won by its citizens.
