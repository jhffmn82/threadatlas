Abstract written by a model

Laestrygonians are giant ogre-like inhabitants of Telepylus who attack Ulysses's fleet with vast rocks, kill and spear his men, then take them home to eat.

the Laestrygonians in The Odyssey

BOOK X

The Laestrygonians are described as giant, ogre-like people living at Telepylus. They attack Ulysses's fleet by throwing enormous rocks, crushing ships, spearing men, and taking them home to eat.
