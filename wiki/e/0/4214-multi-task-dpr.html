Related Work .. DPR for Slot Filling

Multi-task DPR trains the DPR passage and query encoders using multiple tasks from the KILT suite of benchmarks. Its top three retrieved passages are combined with the query and given to a BART model to produce the answer, resulting in large gains in retrieval performance.
