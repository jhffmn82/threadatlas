Nemesis

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, Nemesis mourns Tibullus at his funeral, kisses him, refuses to leave his pyre, and is imagined accompanying him in Elysium. In The Amores, or Amours, Nemesis attends Tibullus’s funeral, kisses him with Delia and his relatives, recalls his dying grasp, and refuses to leave the pyre alone.

IX
