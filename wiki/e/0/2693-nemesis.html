ELEGY IX.

Nemesis attends Tibullus's funeral with his relatives and Delia, sharing kisses and refusing to leave the pyre in solitude. She says that Tibullus grasped her with his failing hand as he died.

Nemesis in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Nemesis, Tibullus’s recent lover, mourns him at his funeral, kisses him, refuses to leave his pyre, and is imagined accompanying him in Elysium.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX

Nemesis mourns Tibullus at his funeral and kisses him before not abandoning the pyre. She claims that Tibullus clung to her with failing strength as he died, and she is imagined as accompanying him in Elysium.
