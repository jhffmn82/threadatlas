Abstract written by a model

Santa Claus's mission to bring happiness to children begins when Claus leaves the forest, resolves to care for mankind's children, and hopes their joy will preserve Necile's kindness in memory. On his first Christmas Eve, he delivers toys to sleeping children. For hundreds of years, he makes and distributes toys worldwide with the immortals' assistance and goodwill, until old age prevents him from making more toys.

7. Claus Leaves the Forest

Claus decides to care for the children of mankind and devote his life to their pleasure. He hopes that Necile's kindness in adopting and caring for him will be remembered through the happiness he brings to thousands of children.

10. Christmas Eve

Santa Claus's mission to bring happiness to children begins with his first Christmas Eve delivery of toys to sleeping children. The text says he has continued this mission for hundreds of years, bringing happiness to children's hearts.

OLD AGE / 1. The Mantle of Immortality

Santa Claus's Christmas work consists of making toys and distributing them to children throughout the world. He performs this work for many years with the assistance and goodwill of the immortals, until old age finally prevents him from making more toys.
