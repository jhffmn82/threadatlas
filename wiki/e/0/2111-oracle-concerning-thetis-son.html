oracle concerning Thetis' son

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a certain oracle, oracle, prediction, prophecy of Thetis's son
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, the oracle foretells that Thetis’ son will surpass his father and wield a fearsome bolt, leading Themis to direct Thetis’s marriage to Peleus.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, the prophecy states that Thetis’s son will surpass his father in gallant deeds and greatness, alarming Jupiter and causing him to yield Thetis to Peleus.

oracle concerning Thetis' son in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

The oracle foretells that Thetis’ son will surpass his father and wield a fearsome bolt if she marries Zeus or his brothers; Themis therefore directs her marriage to Peleus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode VII

The oracle concerning Thetis’ son foretold that Thetis would bear a royal offspring mightier than his father. The son would wield a bolt more terrible than lightning or Poseidon’s trident if Thetis married Zeus or one of Zeus’ brothers, so Themis advised that Thetis marry Peleus instead.
