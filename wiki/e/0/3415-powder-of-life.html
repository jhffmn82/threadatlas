Powder of Life in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The Powder of Life is a precious magical powder, kept by Mombi in an old pepper-box labeled by a wizard and obtained from a crooked sorcerer. When sprinkled on things and used with magical words or a charm, it brings them to life. It first animates Jack Pumpkinhead, then the Saw-Horse. Its limited supply dwindles to about one dose, proposed for an aerial machine. Tip finally sprinkles the remaining powder over the Thing’s wings, sofas, broom, and Gump’s head; with the magic charm, the Thing comes to life.

Powder of Life in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Powder of Life is a wonderful magic substance, a fine white powder made by Dr. Pipt from four boiling kettles. He alone knows its recipe, and it is said to be worth a kingdom. Stored in a small gold bottle with a pepper-box top in his locked cabinet, it brings to life anything upon which it is sprinkled. A previous supply given to Mombi awakened Jack Pumpkinhead; it also brought the Sawhorse to life, and Ozma's possession of it helped her escape Mombi and become ruler of Oz. Dr. Pipt prepares a batch for the Patchwork Girl, sprinkles it on her head and arms, and awakens her. Soon afterward, the remaining powder accidentally falls on a phonograph, bringing it to life and exhausting the supply. Dr. Pipt says another lot would take six years, but later Ozma says he will make no more because his kettles and recipe book have been destroyed.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

The Powder of Life is a magic substance that brings anything to life when sprinkled on it. Dr. Pipt previously gave a supply to Mombi the Witch in exchange for a Powder of Perpetual Youth that did not work, and Mombi used the Powder of Life to bring Jack Pumpkinhead to life.

Chapter Three

The Powder of Life is made from the contents of four kettles that boil away, leaving a small amount of fine white powder. Dr. Pipt says he alone knows how to make it and that it is worth the price of a kingdom. He places it in a small gold bottle and locks it away until it is cool and can be used the next morning.

Chapter Four

The Powder of Life is kept in a gold bottle inside Dr. Pipt's locked cabinet. He takes it out during the preparation to awaken the Patchwork Girl and sprinkles some of it directly onto her head and arms.

Chapter Five

The Powder of Life accidentally falls onto the phonograph and makes it alive. Dr. Pipt discovers that every remaining grain has been wasted on the phonograph, so he begins a process that will take six years to make another batch.

Chapter Seventeen

The Powder of Life is a powerful substance made by Dr. Pipt. It brought Jack Pumpkinhead and the Sawhorse to life, and Ozma's possession of it enabled her to escape from Mombi and helped lead to her becoming ruler of Oz.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

Ojo believes that he may have to wait six years for the Crooked Magician to make a new supply of the Powder of Life. Ozma says that Dr. Pipt will make no more, because his kettles and recipe book have been destroyed.
