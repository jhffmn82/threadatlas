Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

Abstract written by a model

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills are three silver wishing pills, made and created by Dr. Nikidik and kept in a pepper-box. Their label says to store them in a dry, dark place. A pill grants one wish after it is swallowed and seventeen is counted by twos. In the Jackdaws’ nest, travelers initially mistake seventeen’s oddness for making the pills unusable. Tip’s wish restores three pills after he swallows one; the Woggle-Bug successfully uses one, and the party uses one to escape the nest. The pepper-box then disappears, taking the remaining pills, but this no longer matters when the Gump reaches Glinda’s country.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

Dr. Nikidik’s wishing pills are described as granting a wish after one pill is swallowed and seventeen is counted by twos. Three pills are found in the pepper-box, but the travelers mistakenly conclude that they cannot use them because seventeen is an odd number.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

The pills grant wishes after the user swallows one and counts seventeen by twos. Three pills appear in the box after Tip wishes that he had never swallowed his pill, and the Woggle-Bug later swallows one to make a successful wish.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

The party uses one Wishing Pill to escape the Jackdaws' nest and intends to use another to correct the Gump's course. The remaining pills cannot be found after the pepper-box disappears, although the loss becomes unimportant when the Gump reaches Glinda's country.
