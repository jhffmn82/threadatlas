Mr. Chetwind in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Mr. Chetwind, formerly consumptive, becomes fat and sallow through habitual tobacco chewing and praises Hooker’s Ecclesiastical Polity as the best book and the only one that made him a Christian.
