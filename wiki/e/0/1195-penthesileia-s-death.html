Penthesileia's death

Also called: How died for Troy the Queen of the Amazons, Penthesileia, battle slain, death of Penthesileia, death of Penthesilia, is killed by Achilles, overthrown and slain, struck down in battle, the dread doom of his daughter, the killing of Penthesileia, the maiden's woeful end, was afterwards herself killed

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the killing of Penthesileia occurs when Achilles kills her after she comes to aid the Trojans; the Trojans bury her, and Thersites later criticizes Achilles over his supposed love for her. In The Library, the death of Penthesilia is caused by Achilles after she kills many in battle; Achilles falls in love with her after her death and kills Thersites for jeering at him. In The Fall of Troy, Penthesileia's death is a battle death caused by Achilles after she kills Podarces with her spear, prompting the Trojans to flee and mourn, the Greeks to marvel at her beauty, Achilles to regret and grieve, and Ares to mourn angrily until Zeus withdraws him.
