Bellerophon

person · 4 works · 14 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Aiolid king, his lord, Βελλεροφόντης, Βελλεροφόντον

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Bellerophon is a Corinthian hero who, with divine aid, obtains and masters Pegasos, campaigns against the Amazons, Solymoi, and Chimaira, and seeks heavenly habitations. In The Library, Bellerophon seeks purification after accidentally killing a relative, rejects Stheneboea, completes Iobates’s tasks by killing the Chimera and Lycians, marries Philonoe, and inherits Iobates’s kingdom. In Description of Greece, Bellerophon is honoured with a temple at Corinth and is aided by Athene, who breaks and bridles Pegasus and gives him the horse. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Bellerophon is a gallant young warrior who unknowingly bears an order for his death, defeats Chimaera with Pegasus, completes trials, marries Iobates's daughter, and dies miserably after being punished for trying to fly to heaven.

Bellerophon in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where Bellerophon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Bellerophon is a Corinthian hero celebrated in poetry who, with divine aid, obtains and masters Pegasos, campaigns against Amazons, Solymoi, and Chimaira, then seeks heavenly habitations; his later fate is not described.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII.

Bellerophon is presented as a Corinthian hero who obtained and mastered Pegasos with divine assistance. He used the winged horse against the Amazons, the Solymoi, and Chimaira, though the ode declines to describe his later fate.

Bellerophon came to Proetus for purification after accidentally killing a relative. He rejected Stheneboea, survived the tasks assigned by Iobates, killed the Chimera and the Lycians sent against him, and received Philonoe and the kingdom from Iobates.

Bellerophon in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855 · the work’s page · read it where Bellerophon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Bellerophon is a gallant young warrior deemed unconquerable, who unknowingly bears an order for his death, defeats Chimaera with Pegasus, completes trials and marries Iobates's daughter, then is punished for pride after trying to fly to heaven and dies miserably.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

Bellerophon unknowingly carried Proetus's letter ordering his own death, but Iobates sent him against the Chimaera instead. With Pegasus's aid he defeated the monster and completed further trials, yet his pride led him to attempt to fly to heaven, after which he was thrown, became lame and blind, and died miserably.
