Diagoras of Rhodes in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Diagoras of the Eratidai family of Ialysos, son of Demegetos, was a boxing champion celebrated in an ode. He won at Olympia, including twice overall, and accumulated victories at Pythian, Isthmian, Nemean, Athenian, Argive, Arcadian, Boeotian, Pellenian, Aeginetan, and Megarian games.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VII.

Diagoras, of the Eratidai family of Ialysos, won the boxing-match at Olympia and is celebrated in this ode. He also won twice at Olympia, four times at Isthmos, twice at Nemea, twice at Athens, and at several other games including six victories at Aigina.

Diagoras in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Diagoras was a Rhodian boxer and head of the Diagorid family. He won Olympic boxing among men, had a statue by Callicles at Olympia, and, after his sons Acusilaus and Damagetus won, was carried on their shoulders.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Diagoras of Rhodes won the Olympic boxing victory among men, and his statue was made by the Megarian Callicles. He came to Olympia with his sons Acusilaus and Damagetus and was carried on their shoulders after their victories; he was descended on his mother's side from Aristomenes.
