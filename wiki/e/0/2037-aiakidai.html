Aiakidai in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Aiakidai are a lineage of warrior heroes, sons of Endais, sprung from Kronos, Zeus, and golden nymphs. They honor and pray for Aigina beside Zeus Hellenios’s altar. Their family includes Phokos, and after a portentous unjust deed they are expelled from Oinone. Athletic victories, including Praxidamas’s Olympic wreath, and Achilles’s slaying of Memnon spread their glory widely.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode V

The Aiakidai are warrior heroes sprung from Kronos, Zeus, and the golden nymphs. Pytheas's victory honors them, and the ode recounts their ancestry and stories.

Nemean Ode VI

The Aiakidai received an Olympic olive-wreath through Praxidamas. Their mighty feats have given chroniclers wide avenues of glory, and their name has spread over land, beyond the sea, and even to the Ethiopians through the story of Achilles's slaying of Memnon.
