John Rance in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

John Rance is a police constable living at 46 Audley Court, Kennington Park Gate, who patrols Brixton Road. During a night beat, he finds a light and a body in an apparently empty Lauriston Gardens house and encounters a drunken man near its gate.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

John Rance tells Holmes and Watson that he found a light and a body inside the apparently empty house in Lauriston Gardens during his night beat. He also reports encountering a tall, red-faced, extremely drunken man in a brown overcoat near the gate, whom he and Murcher had to support.
