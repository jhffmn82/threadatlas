Claus's abduction in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Claus's abduction is a repeated forcible seizure by the Awgwas: they bind sleeping Claus and abandon him in an Ethop jungle, then capture him again and imprison him in a mountain cave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The Awgwas first bind the sleeping Claus and carry him to a remote jungle in Ethop, where they leave him among dangerous animals. Later, they seize him while he travels to a village and imprison him in a mountain cave behind huge rocks.
