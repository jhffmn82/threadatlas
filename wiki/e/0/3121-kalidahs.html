Kalidahs in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

Kalidahs are monstrous beasts with bear-like bodies, tiger-like heads, and long sharp claws; two attack travelers across a tree bridge, then fall into a gulf when its end is chopped away.

Chapter VII

The Kalidahs are monstrous beasts with bodies like bears, heads like tigers, and long, sharp claws. Two Kalidahs attack the travelers by crossing the tree bridge, but they fall into the gulf when the Tin Woodman chops away its end and are dashed to pieces on the rocks.

Kalidah in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Kalidah is a creature whose especial home is the forest containing the Isle of the Magic Flower and which attacks other beasts ferociously. It approaches Trot and Cap'n Bill and attacks them, but Cap'n Bill pins it with a stake. It frees itself by forcing the stake through its body, leaving two holes; its anger fades, and it seeks the Kalidah King for healing.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

9. The Isle of the Magic Flower

One Kalidah approaches Trot and Cap'n Bill and attacks them. Cap'n Bill knocks it over and pins it to the ground with a stake, leaving it unable to escape; the creature warns that other Kalidahs may come and says that Kalidahs can swim but do not go to the magical island.

10. Stuck Fast

The Kalidah, pinned by Cap'n Bill's stake, uses powerful breaths and its leg muscles to free itself. It pushes the stake through its body and removes it, leaving two holes. Since Trot and Cap'n Bill are trapped, its anger fades, and it goes to seek the Kalidah King, hoping he can heal it.

the Kalidahs in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Kalidahs are the most disagreeable, powerful, and ferocious creatures in the Land of Oz. Living in a forest, they swim like ducks, attack other animals, often fight among themselves, and avoid the Isle of the Magic Flower because they dislike magic.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

9. The Isle of the Magic Flower

Kalidahs are described as the most powerful and ferocious beasts in Oz, and the forest is their special home. They attack other animals and often fight among themselves, while avoiding the Isle of the Magic Flower because they dislike magic.
