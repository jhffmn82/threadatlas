Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, L. Frank Baum is the writer who created a modernized fairy tale for contemporary children. In The Marvelous Land of Oz, L. Frank Baum is the author of The Wonderful Wizard of Oz who, prompted by children’s letters, wrote and dedicated the book to David C. Montgomery and Frank A. Stone. In The Woggle-Bug Book, L. Frank Baum is identified as the author and held the 1905 copyright to the book. In Ozma of Oz, L. Frank Baum is the author of The Wizard of Oz, The Land of Oz, and Ozma of Oz, whose readers’ letters prompted him to continue writing. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, L. Frank Baum is a storyteller who writes Oz stories and says readers’ letters and suggestions shaped his continued tales. In The Emerald City of Oz, L. Frank Baum presents himself as an editor or private secretary for youngsters who uses children’s ideas and follows readers’ requests. In Sky Island, L. Frank Baum is the author who writes the introductory talk and invites children to write to him at Ozcot. In The Magic of Oz, L. Frank Baum is the Royal Historian of Oz, who hopes to answer readers’ letters and continue writing the Oz Books.
