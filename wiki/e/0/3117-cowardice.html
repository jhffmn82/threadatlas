cowardice

topic · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a coward, afraid, courage, coward, cowardly, desire to run away, the Cowardly Lion's cowardice
  • In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the Cowardly Lion’s cowardice makes him unhappy because his heart beats fast whenever there is danger, although other animals flee from his roar.
  • In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the Cowardly Lion identifies himself as cowardly, imagines attacking someone only to flee before punishment, and concludes after helping the baby’s mother that cowardice is preferable to doing wrong.
  • In The Cowardly Lion of Oz, the Cowardly Lion’s cowardice causes fear and trembling despite brave fighting; the Comfortable Camel considers it interesting, and it remains a burden as he wants freedom from his urge to run when danger threatens.

the Cowardly Lion's cowardice in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

The Cowardly Lion’s cowardice causes fear and trembling despite brave fighting; considered interesting by the Comfortable Camel, it remains a burden as he wants freedom from his urge to run when danger threatens.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Cowardice makes the Cowardly Lion tremble and feel frightened, even though he acts bravely in fights. The Comfortable Camel considers his cowardice part of what makes him interesting, while the Cowardly Lion wants to overcome it.

Chapter 20

The Cowardly Lion says that he fights when danger threatens but suffers from a strong desire to run away. He believes that he would be perfectly happy if he no longer felt that desire.
