watchfires in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Watchfires are fires lit by the Trojans at Hector's order, across the plain between the ships and the river Xanthus. Fed with gathered wood and tended by groups of about fifty men, they shine through the night, their light reaching toward heaven.

Hector orders the Trojans to gather much wood and burn watchfires whose light may reach heaven. The fires are kindled across the plain, with about fifty men sitting by each, and they shine between the ships and the river Xanthus throughout the night.
