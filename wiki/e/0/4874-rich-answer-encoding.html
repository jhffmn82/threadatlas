Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding / Abstract

Rich Answer Encoding is used to incorporate answer information into retrieval-augmented generation. The work implements it through Dense Knowledge Similarity and Retriever as Answer Classifier.

Introduction

Answer encoding represents the knowledge contained in an answer and supports both Retriever as Answer Classifier and Dense Knowledge Similarity. It shifts training from token-level matching toward sequence- or knowledge-level matching.
