the Soldier with the Green Whiskers in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers is an old, feeble soldier and the Royal Army of Oz, serving His Majesty the Scarecrow. He carries a long, unloaded green gun, fears women, escorts Jack Pumpkinhead, and brings Jellia Jamb as interpreter. When Gen. Jinjur’s Army attacks, he reveals his gun is unloaded and runs to the palace. After the conquest, he bars the palace, helps prepare an escape, plans to cut off his whiskers, and rides tied into the river.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers is the Royal Army of Oz and comes to defend the Emerald City. He reveals that his gun is not loaded, then runs through the gate toward the royal palace when the girls attack.

The Scarecrow Plans an escape

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers reaches the palace after the Emerald City is conquered and bars its doors and windows. He brings the Saw-Horse from the royal treasury and helps prepare the escape. He plans to disguise himself by cutting off his green whiskers and rides tied to the others into the river.

the Soldier with the Green Whiskers in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers is a tall soldier with a long green beard who faithfully serves Princess Ozma as her Body-Guard, the Royal Army of Oz, and the Emerald City Police Force. On Ozma’s orders, he identifies and arrests Ojo for allegedly wilfully breaking a Law of Oz, possibly by picking a six-leaved clover. He puts gold, jewel-set handcuffs on Ojo, leads him into the Emerald City, places him in prison clothing, delivers him to Tollydiggle with a receipt, reports his duty complete, and later escorts him from prison to the royal palace.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Fourteen

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers serves as Ozma's Body-Guard, the Royal Army of Oz, and the Police Force of the Emerald City. Acting on Ozma's orders, he identifies and arrests Ojo, placing gold handcuffs set with rubies and diamonds on him.

Chapter Fifteen

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers leads Ojo into the Emerald City, places him in a prisoner's robe and handcuffs, and takes him to Tollydiggle. He gives Tollydiggle a receipt for Ojo and reports that he has completed his duty to Ozma.

Chapter Sixteen

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers arrests Ojo because he is accused of wilfully breaking a Law of Oz. Dorothy learns that he arrested Ojo after the boy may have picked a six-leaved clover.

Soldier with the Green Whiskers in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

The Soldier with the Green Whiskers is Oz’s only soldier and entire army, a member of the Grand Army of Oz who reports to Ozma and guards Emerald City and the royal stable. He flees Wag and Peg, discovers the vanished palace, alerts the city, brings Glinda there, and watches the palace garden.
