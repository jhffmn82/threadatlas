Thorax

Also called: my host Thorax

Abstract written by a model

Thorax, of the Aleuadai, hosts and engages Pindar to write an ode for Hippokleas; his kindly courtesy and support, likened to yoking the Pierides’ four-horse car, advance Pindar’s fortune.

Thorax is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

X.

Thorax engaged Pindar to write the ode for Hippokleas and is described as Pindar's host. Pindar trusts in Thorax's kindly courtesy and compares him to a friend and willing guide who has yoked the four-horse car of the Pierides to advance Pindar's fortune.
