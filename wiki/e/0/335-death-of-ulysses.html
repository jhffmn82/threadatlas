death of Ulysses

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Ulysses died, dead, death, my own end, θνήσκει

Abstract written by a model

The Odyssey presents the death as something Teiresias says will come to Ulysses from the sea when he is full of years and peace of mind, with his life ebbing away gently and his people blessing him. The Library describes the death of Ulysses as Odysseus dying after Telegonus wounded him while he was defending cattle in Ithaca, with Telegonus later recognizing him and lamenting his death.
