Self-ask

topic · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Self-Ask, self-ask

Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, Self-ask is a question decomposition method that manually inserts follow-up sub-questions into downstream-task exemplars using Prompt D.2, triggering retrieval when the model generates a sub-question. In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, Self-Ask is a structured reasoning method that generates and answers retrieved follow-up questions before the original question, but ITER-RETGEN is generally more efficient, accurate, and robust.
