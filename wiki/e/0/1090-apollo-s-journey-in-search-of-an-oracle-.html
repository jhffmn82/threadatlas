TO PYTHIAN APOLLO—

Apollo travels from Pieria through many places, including Iolcus, Euboea, Thebe, Onchestus, Cephissus, Ocalea, Haliartus, and Telphusa, seeking a suitable site for an oracle. He finally selects Crisa beneath Parnassus.
