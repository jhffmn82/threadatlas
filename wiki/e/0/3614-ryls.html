Ryls in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Ryls are merry, light-hearted immortals, first cousins to the wood-nymphs, who watch over and tend flowers and plants as sacred work and are armed with sharp thorns. With Claus’s help, they promise him seasonal food, milk, bread, honey, apples, and grapes, so he need not plow and harm flowers or grass. Later, the Red, Green, Yellow, Black, and Blue Ryls bring paints and brushes, color a wooden cat, support colored toy-making, and add their seal to Claus’s protection.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

MANHOOD / 1. The Laughing Valley

The Ryls of the Field Flowers promise to supply Claus with food so that he will not need to plow the ground and destroy the flowers and grass. They subsequently provide milk, bread, honey, apples, grapes, and continuing food supplies.

3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

The Red, Green, Yellow, Black, and Blue Ryls visit Claus while he is carving a wooden cat. They bring paints and brushes made from the colors they use on flowers and plants, then help color the cat to resemble a real one.
