Dense Knowledge Similarity in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023

Dense Knowledge Similarity is a proposed answer-encoding method and knowledge-constrained objective for training a retriever to retrieve knowledge-relevant passages in retrieval-augmented generation. It changes the goal from generating a natural-language answer label to generating the knowledge needed to answer a question. It compares answer and passage encodings in a knowledge representation space, training a knowledge extractor to align question-passage representations with representations from gold answers through mean squared error loss, so relevant passages are closer to the answer than other passages. It uses continuous sequence-level features for conversational tasks. In experiments, it obtains 11.5 R@1 on MSMARCO and 47.02 R-Precision on KILT-WoW, the best R-Precision across models, with an 11.4% relative improvement over the baseline. It retrieves the same Wikipedia page as RAC and gives a perfect knowledge match in a sampled generation. It concludes as one of two objectives guiding retrievers toward knowledge-relevant passages.

Dense Knowledge Similarity is one of the proposed methods for incorporating rich answer encoding into retrieval-augmented generation.

Introduction

Dense Knowledge Similarity is one of the proposed objectives and compares answer and passage encodings in a knowledge representation space. It is designed to place knowledge-relevant passages closer to the answer than other passages.

Methodology / Retrieve-Generate Backbone .. Overall Architecture

Dense Knowledge Similarity is an objective that changes the target from generating an answer label to generating the knowledge required to answer a question. It trains a knowledge extractor to align question-passage knowledge representations with representations extracted from gold answers using mean squared error.

Experimental Setup / Datasets .. Comparison Models

It is one of the proposed-method variants. It obtains 54.9 BLEU-1, 34.1 BLEU-4, and 11.5 R@1 on MSMARCO, while on KILT-WoW it achieves the highest KILT-RL and KILT-F1 scores.

Results / RQ1: Effectiveness / Generative QA .. Case Studies

DKS is one of the proposed model variants and is reported as the best model under the development set for the KILT-WoW leaderboard submission. It achieves the best R-Precision across the models and produces a knowledge-matched Gouda cheese answer.

Conclusion

DKS is one of the two knowledge-constrained objectives included in the training framework to guide the retriever toward knowledge-relevant passages.
