Abstract written by a model

Aphrodite spreads love and sexual desire among gods, mortals, birds, beasts, and other creatures. Zeus places desire for Anchises in her heart, and she in turn puts desire in the hearts of the animals accompanying her. Desire leads Anchises to sleep with Aphrodite despite believing that she is mortal.
