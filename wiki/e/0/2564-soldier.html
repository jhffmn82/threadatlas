soldier in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

A warrior serves as a captain’s guard, keeping watch at the door and through the night. He endures long roads, harsh weather, and ground rest, scouts enemies, besieges cities, breaks gates, and embodies courage and endurance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX

The soldier serves in a camp, keeps watch through the night, rests on the ground, scouts dangerous enemies, besieges cities, breaks gates, and endures difficult journeys and weather. His courage and endurance are presented as qualities also sought by the beloved in a lover.
