Wicked Witch of the West in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Wicked Witch of the West is the only remaining wicked witch in Oz, a fierce ruler who lives in the West in her castle and rules the Winkies through long enslavement and cruelty. She has one telescope-powerful eye, possesses the Golden Cap, fears the Silver Shoes, and so fears water that she neither touches it nor permits it to touch her. Before Dorothy arrives, she uses the Cap and the Winged Monkeys to enslave the Winkies and drive Oz from the Land of the West. When Dorothy and her companions enter her country seeking to kill her, the Witch watches them, sends forces against them, and uses the Cap's third and final summons to call the Winged Monkeys. She captures and enslaves Dorothy in the castle kitchen and tries to take her Silver Shoes. Dorothy throws a bucket of water over her, causing her to shrink, melt, and be destroyed. Her destruction leaves her castle and cupboard, including the Golden Cap, to Dorothy and enables the travelers' return to the Emerald City.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XI

The Wicked Witch of the West is described by Oz as the only Wicked Witch left in the land. Oz orders Dorothy and her companions to kill her before he will grant their wishes, and they decide to travel to the land of the Winkies to seek her out.

Chapter XII

The Wicked Witch of the West watches Dorothy and her companions with one eye as powerful as a telescope and sends various forces to destroy or capture them. She uses the Golden Cap’s final summons to call the Winged Monkeys, then enslaves Dorothy and tries to steal both Silver Shoes. She is afraid of water, and Dorothy causes her to shrink and melt by throwing water over her.

Chapter XIII

The Wicked Witch has been melted by a bucket of water. Her castle and cupboard remain available to Dorothy and her friends, and Dorothy finds the Golden Cap in the cupboard.

Chapter XIV

The Wicked Witch of the West once possessed the Golden Cap and used the Winged Monkeys to enslave the Winkies and drive Oz out of the Land of the West.

Chapter XV

The Wicked Witch of the West is melted by Dorothy with a bucket of water. Her destruction allows the travelers to return to the Emerald City and claim the promises Oz made.
