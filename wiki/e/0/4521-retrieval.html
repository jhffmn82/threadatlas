retrieval

topic · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 4 connections
Also called: Multi-Hop Retrieval, knowledge retrieval, multi-hop retrieval, retrieval performance
  • In "Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge," retrieval is treated as a way to bridge the gap between model inputs and outputs by finding information relevant to both; the unit proposes unified retrieval, iterative retrieval across sources, and retrieval followed by structured-knowledge reasoning.
  • In "HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation," retrieval connects related passages through pseudo-queries, helping identify relevant passages within multi-hop neighborhoods of indirectly relevant passages; HopRAG achieved a 20.97% improvement in retrieval F1 over conventional information retrieval approaches.
  • In "Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation," multi-hop retrieval addresses queries spanning multiple evidence sources by decomposing questions into separate aspects; it improves retrieval quality, though gains diminish when decomposition or reranking fails.
