Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the tortoise was treated by Hermes as an omen of good luck, and its shell became the body of the first lyre. In The Library, the tortoise was found by Hermes browsing before the cave at Cyllene; its shell was used to make a lyre.
