IV. TO HERMES

The quarrel begins when Apollo accuses Hermes of stealing his cattle. Hermes denies seeing or taking them, claiming that he is only a newborn concerned with sleep, milk, and swaddling clothes. Apollo calls him a rogue and robber, and the two continue disputing until they are brought before Zeus and the immortals.
