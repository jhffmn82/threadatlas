Abstract written by a model

Nymphs are a forest people and immortal creatures: fair, youthful, innocent, pure, loving, dainty, and unchanging, with no need to regard time. They live happily in the Forest of Burzee, wear oak-leaf green, guard forests and trees, minister to young trees, and explain Burzee’s mysteries, habits, and living things. Sheltered in Burzee’s inner parts by its law, they dance at the Royal Circle each full moon and observe seasonal festivals and ceremonies. Queen Zurline is their queen, and they listen to Ak’s wisdom and serve Claus when called upon. They are dismayed by Necile and the infant at first, then support Ak’s permission for her to keep Claus, help find milk, and care for him. As Claus grows, they guide and protect him; when he leaves, they promise sympathy and help.

Nymphs is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

2. The Child of the Forest

The wood-nymphs guard forests, minister to young trees, and dress in oak-leaf green. They participate in festivals and dances, listen to Ak’s words of wisdom, and are generally sheltered within the inner parts of the Forest of Burzee by its law.

3. The Adoption

The nymphs are surprised and dismayed to see Necile standing with the infant. They hear Ak permit Necile to keep the child, and Queen Zurline encourages Ak to grant the petition.

4. Claus

The forest nymphs gather around Claus with curiosity and delight and praise Ak for allowing Necile to keep him. They search for bell-udders to provide him with milk and help care for him as he grows.

5. The Master Woodsman

The nymphs remain immortal and unchanging while Claus grows from a child into a youth. They follow Claus through the forest paths, explain Burzee's mysteries and living creatures to him, and protect and favor him along with Queen Zurline.

7. Claus Leaves the Forest

The nymphs listen as Claus explains why he must leave them and proudly promise to give him their sympathy and help. They remain among the Forest people whose aid Ak grants Claus.
