San Francisco in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

San Francisco is a place containing the Tarbox residence on California Street and the Neworths’ residence on Rincon Hill. It is to host Mr. Bilson and Frida’s marriage and is intended for Chris Calton’s specialist treatment. Edward Brice arrives there within twenty-four hours, is promoted to the company’s office, and Hiram Tarbox establishes his land and timber business while Flora stays with her mother. Eugenia lives there; Bray seeks and repeatedly visits her, eventually leaving his old partners.
