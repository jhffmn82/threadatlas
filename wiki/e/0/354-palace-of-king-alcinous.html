Abstract written by a model

King Alcinous’s palace is a richly adorned, guarded residence with bronze, gold, and silver features, servants, and a fertile garden. Ulysses enters under Minerva’s darkness and supplicates Arete and Alcinous.

BOOK VII

The palace has bronze walls, golden doors, silver pillars, golden and silver mastiffs made by Vulcan, richly covered seats, and golden figures holding torches. It contains many servants and is surrounded by a fertile garden with trees, vineyards, flowers, and two streams. Ulysses enters it under Minerva’s protective darkness and supplicates Arete and Alcinous.
