ELEGY XIV.

The sexual encounter is described as something that should occur in private, with secrecy maintained by closing out the public. It is portrayed through undressing, sexual touching, kissing, passionate speech, and the movement of the bed; afterward, the participants are expected to leave these signs and behaviors in the chamber.
