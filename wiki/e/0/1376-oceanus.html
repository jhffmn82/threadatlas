Oceanus

mythological entity · 4 works · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Ocean, Sire divine, Ocean’s stream, glorious Ocean, great Oceanus, namefellow, river Oceanus, sea-sire, the river Oceanus, the shore of Oceanus, the waters of Oceanus
  • In Four Plays of Aeschylus, Oceanus is a sea god and father of the Oceanides who visits Prometheus on a griffin, offers to seek Zeus’s relief, is warned of Zeus’s hatred, and leaves.
  • In The Odyssey, Oceanus is a deep river surrounding the Cimmerians and Proserpine’s fertile country, which Ulysses reaches by following its waters to summon the dead.
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Oceanus is a Titan born from Earth and Heaven who, with Tethys, fathers rivers and many daughters serving earth and deep waters.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Oceanus is one of the Titans who ruled over the sea with Tethys before Neptune and Amphitrite succeeded to dominion of the waters.

Oceanus in Four Plays of Aeschylus

Aeschylus · -0471

Oceanus is a sea god and father of the Oceanides. Moved by kinship with Prometheus, he visits on a griffin, offers to seek Zeus’s relief, is warned of Zeus’s hatred, and leaves.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PROMETHEUS BOUND

Oceanus visits Prometheus mounted on a griffin and offers to seek relief for his suffering from Zeus. Prometheus warns Oceanus that such an effort may bring Zeus’s hatred, and Oceanus finally leaves.

Oceanus in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Oceanus, a deep river surrounding Cimmerians and Proserpine's fertile country, is reached by Ulysses, who follows its waters to summon the dead.
