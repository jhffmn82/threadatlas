cloister in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The cloister, part of a house complex connected to the inner court, contains a wall trap door, tables, and seats, with Jove’s altar in the adjoining outer court. It is the setting for Ulysses’ struggle with the suitors; afterward survivors depart, the dead are removed, and it is cleaned and purified.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXII

The cloisters contain the main struggle between Ulysses and the suitors. After the suitors are killed, Phemius and Medon leave the cloisters and sit by the altar in the outer court while Ulysses searches the area for survivors.

The cloister is cleaned after the dead are removed, and Ulysses later purifies it with fire and sulphur.
