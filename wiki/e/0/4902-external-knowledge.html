external documents in Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Zhengliang Shi, Shuo Zhang, Weiwei Sun, Shen Gao, Pengjie Ren, Zhumin Chen, Zhaochun Ren · 2024

External documents are retrieved records used to ground question-answer pairs and revise initially non-factual model answers. They can contain noise, misinformation, or omit needed information, so direct use may yield plausible hallucinations. Instructional knowledge grounding incorporates them by directing the model to cite relevant evidence, mitigating these risks. In GenGround, they amend wrong predictions; despite possible misleading content, revision is reported to have a 5.6% error rate. Their limitations can still compromise the framework.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

6 / 6.1 .. 6.3

Retrieved documents are incorporated through instructional knowledge grounding, which directs the model to cite the most relevant evidence. This helps mitigate the negative effects of noisy documents, whereas direct generation from them can produce plausible-looking hallucinated statements.

7 / 7.1 .. 7.3

External documents are used by large language models to revise initially non-factual answers. Plausible-looking statements in the documents can mislead the models, although the reported error rate after revision is only 5.6%.

8

External documents are retrieved to ground question-answer pairs produced during GenGround's process. They help amend wrong predictions in the answer.

Limitations

External documents are used to correct initially non-factual statements generated by the model. If they lack necessary information or contain misinformation, they may compromise the framework's effectiveness.
