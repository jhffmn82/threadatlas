Peace

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Eirene, laws of peace, peace

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Eirene is characterized by harmless laws that the speaker wishes to follow, and the speaker asks Ares to grant the boldness to abide within those laws while avoiding strife, hatred, and violent fiends of death. In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Peace, the golden daughter of Themis and Righteousness, dwells in Corinth as a foundation of states, enriches men and makes cities great, holds keys to counsels and wars, gives and receives gentleness, and sinks Insolence in the sea in wrath.
