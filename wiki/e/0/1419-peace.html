Peace

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Eirene, laws of peace, peace
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Peace, the golden daughter of Themis and Righteousness, dwells in Corinth as a foundation of states, enriches men and makes cities great, holds keys to counsels and wars, gives and receives gentleness, and sinks Insolence in the sea in wrath.
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Eirene is characterized by harmless laws that the speaker wishes to follow, and the speaker asks Ares to grant the boldness to abide within those laws while avoiding strife, hatred, and violent fiends of death.

Peace in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Peace, the golden daughter of Themis and Righteousness, dwells in Corinth as a foundation of states, enriches men and makes cities great, holds keys to counsels and wars, gives and receives gentleness, then sinks Insolence in the sea in wrath.

Narrative, section by section

VIII.

Peace is called the daughter of Righteousness and the power that makes cities great. She holds the supreme keys of counsels and wars, gives and receives gentleness, and sinks Insolence in the sea when moved to wrath.
