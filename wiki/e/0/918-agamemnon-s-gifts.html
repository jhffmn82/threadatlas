Abstract written by a model

Agamemnon’s gifts are restitution and promised compensation offered to Achilles for the quarrel over Briseis. They include gold, seven unused tripods, twenty cauldrons, twelve prize-winning horses, seven skilled Lesbian workwomen, Briseis’s return, twenty Trojan women, a daughter in marriage with dowry, seven cities, and possible future spoils. Agamemnon presents the offer as atonement; Achilles rejects it, saying even greater gifts will not move him before full vengeance. Later Ulysses and other Achaeans bring the listed goods from Agamemnon’s tents and set them before the assembly.

BOOK IX.

Agamemnon offers Achilles seven tripods, ten talents of gold, twenty cauldrons, twelve prize-winning horses, seven workwomen, Briseis, twenty Trojan women, a chosen daughter in marriage, a large dowry, and seven cities. He presents these gifts as atonement for taking Briseis and promises additional spoils if Troy is sacked.
