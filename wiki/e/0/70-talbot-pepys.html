Talbot Pepys in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Talbot Pepys, the narrator’s old uncle and sixth son of John Pepys, was born in 1583, educated at Trinity Hall, became a barrister and Cambridge official, and died of plague aged eighty-three.

opening

The narrator visited his old uncle Talbot Pepys at Impington after traveling through Cambridge. The text identifies him as the sixth son of John Pepys of Impington, a former Cambridge-educated barrister and public official.
