In The Odyssey, Cyclops’ sheep belong to Cyclops, are brought ashore, and are divided equitably among the narrator’s comrades. In The Library, the sheep flock belongs to the island giant and provides the sailors with a means of escape as they tie themselves beneath the sheep and pass out of the cave.
