Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, the Achaean army gathers in confusion, is disciplined by Ulysses and reminded of its oath, labor, and promised victory; Agamemnon orders its preparation for battle, and its chiefs organize its vast, armoured ranks as Minerva strengthens its courage. In The Seven Plays in English Verse, the Achaean army is the Greek host besieging Troy, which Aias was expected to help; Menelaus claims Aias tried to attack and kill it by night, while Aias calls for vengeance on Achaean flesh and the camp.
