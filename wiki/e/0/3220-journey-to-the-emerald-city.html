journey to the Emerald City in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The journey to the Emerald City is a journey planned by Dorothy to claim Oz's promise. Dorothy begins with Toto after preparing food, water, clothing, and the silver shoes; after a night with the Munchkins, she follows the yellow brick road and rescues the Scarecrow. The party enters a dangerous forest, where the Cowardly Lion joins them. Dorothy, the Tin Woodman, the Lion, and the Scarecrow cross two gulfs, survive a Kalidah attack, and face a river. They later regain the yellow brick road, pass through the green country, and enter the city with the Guardian of the Gates' direction. After days at the Yellow Castle, they set out again, become lost in great fields, and are carried by Winged Monkeys to the city's gate.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

Dorothy begins the journey with Toto after preparing food, water, clothing, and the silver shoes. She spends one night with the Munchkins, continues along the yellow brick road, and adds the Scarecrow to her party after rescuing him from his pole.

Chapter VI

The journey leads Dorothy and her companions through a dangerous forest toward the Emerald City. The Cowardly Lion joins the journey, and the group continues peacefully after his arrival.

Chapter VII

Dorothy, the Tin Woodman, the Cowardly Lion, and the Scarecrow continue their journey through the forest toward the Emerald City. They overcome two gulfs, survive a Kalidah attack, and reach a river that they must cross.

Chapter X

Dorothy and her companions find the road of yellow brick again and continue toward the Emerald City. They pass through the green country, spend a night at a farmhouse, reach the city wall, and enter the city under the Guardian of the Gates’ direction.

Chapter XIII

After spending several days at the Yellow Castle, Dorothy and her friends begin their journey to the Emerald City. The Winkies say goodbye, give them gifts, and send them off with three cheers and good wishes.

Chapter XIV

The journey to the Emerald City becomes difficult when the travelers lose their direction in the great fields. The Winged Monkeys shorten the journey by carrying the travelers through the air and setting them down at the city's gate.
