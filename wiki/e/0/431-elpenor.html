Elpenor in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Elpenor is a young, unremarkable comrade of Ulysses. He dies drunkenly after falling from Circe’s house, leaving his body unburied. In Hades he appears first as a ghost and asks Ulysses to burn him, raise a sea-side barrow, and set his oar upon it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XI

Elpenor appears as the first ghost because his body was left unwaked and unburied at Circe’s house. He explains that he fell from the roof while drunk and asks Ulysses to burn him, build a barrow by the sea, and plant his rowing oar over the grave.
