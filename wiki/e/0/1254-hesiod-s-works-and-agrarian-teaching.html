Hesiod's works and agrarian teaching

Also called: peace and husbandry

Abstract written by a model

advocates peace and husbandry [in contrast with Homer’s war poetry] Hesiod recited verses describing the proper seasons for harvesting, ploughing, and sowing, and the need to work according to the seasons. Paneides preferred this teaching about peace and husbandry to Homer’s depiction of warfare.
