Hesiod's works and agrarian teaching

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: peace and husbandry
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica: advocates peace and husbandry [in contrast with Homer’s war poetry] Hesiod recited verses describing the proper seasons for harvesting, ploughing, and sowing, and the need to work according to the seasons. Paneides preferred this teaching about peace and husbandry to Homer’s depiction of warfare.
