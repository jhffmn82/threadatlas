Shiegra in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Shiegra is an old, formerly fierce lioness from the Forest of Burzee and Claus’s friend. She first crouches near an abandoned human infant intending to devour it, but Ak orders her to nurse and protect the child from every beast and reptile. She comes to love Claus from his babyhood and later visits him in the Laughing Valley despite the snow. She proposes a lioness toy, admires Claus’s wooden likeness of her, snarls at Blinkie, and returns toward her forest home. Though Claus trusts her, he recognizes that children may find her frightening.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

2. The Child of the Forest

Shiegra is a lioness who crouches near the Forest of Burzee, intending to devour the abandoned human infant. Ak commands her to lie beside the infant, feed it her milk, and ensure that no beast or reptile harms it.

3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

Shiegra is an old lioness who has loved Claus since his babyhood and visits him in the Laughing Valley despite her dislike of snow-drifts. She suggests that Claus make a lioness toy, admires the finished likeness, and returns toward her forest home after snarling at Blinkie.

4. How Little Mayrie Became Frightened

Shiegra is the lioness known to Claus, who is familiar with her and is not afraid of her. Claus realizes that children may view her as terrifying rather than friendly.
