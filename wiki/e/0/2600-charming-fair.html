Abstract written by a model

Charming fair are the women Naso addresses through his lines and loves as a poet. In the opening, he asks them to heed verses dictated by Cupid; later, he celebrates women of every temperament, appearance, age, manner, and accomplishment as inspirations for passion.

BOOK THE SECOND ELEGY I.

Naso calls on the charming fair to turn their eyes to his lines, which Cupid dictates to him.

ELEGY III.

Fair women attract the poet in every variety: modest or forward, prudish or learned, simple or accomplished, tall or short, adorned or unadorned, blonde or brunette, youthful or mature. He finds their different manners, voices, movements, talents, and appearances sufficient to inspire his passion.
