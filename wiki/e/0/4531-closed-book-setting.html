Closed Book setting

setting · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Close-Book, Closed Book, close-book setting, closed-book experiments, closed-book question answering

Abstract written by a model

In Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge, Close-Book setting fine-tunes large pre-trained language models solely on input-output pairs from target data. In BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Closed Book uses no retrieval or context, yet outperforms Oracle retrieval on ELI5 and retrieval systems on WoW.
