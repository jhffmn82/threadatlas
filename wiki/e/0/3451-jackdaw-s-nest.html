Abstract written by a model

Jackdaw’s Nest is an inaccessible nest on a hollow ledge beneath a mountain cliff, hundreds of feet above a great gulf, occupied by Jackdaws. It cushions the travelers’ fall after a crash and holds leaves, papers, valuables, stolen money, and stolen articles, including a diamond necklace. The party is attacked there; after the Scarecrow’s straw is thrown into the gulf, he is restuffed. The party searches for treasures, takes objects, and escapes by Gump at daylight as the Jackdaws return.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The Jackdaw’s nest lies on a hollow ledge beneath a mountain cliff and cushions the travelers’ fall after the crash. It contains leaves, papers, valuables, and other objects stolen by the birds, including a diamond necklace.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

The nest is attacked by Jackdaws and contains the party, the Scarecrow’s straw, and a large amount of stolen money. After the straw is thrown into the gulf, the nest becomes the place where the Scarecrow is restuffed.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

The party searches the Jackdaws' nest for treasures before leaving, and several companions take jewelry and other objects. They escape when the Gump flies away at daylight, while a flock of Jackdaws returns to reclaim the nest. The Scarecrow believes he left the pepper-box there.
