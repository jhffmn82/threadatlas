Abstract written by a model

Jinjur's takeover of the Emerald City is a bloodless usurpation carried out by General Jinjur's Army of Revolt, an invading regiment of girls of Oz. The army enters through the gateway, defeats the city's ineffective defenders, and captures the city as the Standing Army flees. The Scarecrow is driven from his throne and escapes because the conquerors plan to use his body for furnishings. Jinjur becomes a self-proclaimed queen; her followers enslave the men, remove the emerald jewels and treasury jewels, and govern as they choose. The takeover prompts plans to recapture the city and Glinda's march against it.

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The conquest of the Emerald City is achieved when General Jinjur’s army enters through the gateway after defeating the city’s ineffective defenders. The city is captured without a drop of blood being spilled.

The Scarecrow Plans an escape

The Emerald City is conquered by a regiment of girls, while the Standing Army runs because it cannot face the invaders’ weapons. The Scarecrow loses his throne but focuses on escaping because the conquerors intend to use his body for a rag carpet and sofa-cushions.

A Nickel-Plated Emperor

The girls of Oz capture the Emerald City after driving out the Scarecrow. They take all the jewels from its treasury and run the city to suit themselves, prompting Nick Chopper and the Scarecrow’s companions to plan its recapture.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

Jinjur's takeover placed the Emerald City under the rule of a self-proclaimed queen after the Scarecrow was displaced. Her followers enslaved the men and removed the city's emerald jewels, and Glinda decides to oppose the takeover by marching on the city.
