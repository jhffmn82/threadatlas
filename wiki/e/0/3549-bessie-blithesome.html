Bessie Blithesome in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Bessie Blithesome, a rich lord’s young daughter, visits Claus escorted by men-at-arms, saying she has no toy. He gives her a clay nymph doll, her first toy; she loves it as her baby and adopts the stockings custom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

Bessie Blithesome visits Claus with an escort of men-at-arms and asks for a toy, explaining that she has none despite being a rich lord’s daughter. Claus gives her a clay nymph doll, which she loves and calls her baby.
