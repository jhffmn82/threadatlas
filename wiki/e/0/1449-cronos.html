Cronos in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Cronos is a Titan, the youngest and most terrible child of Earth and Heaven, who hates Heaven. At Earth’s urging, he uses a jagged sickle against Heaven, leads the Titans’ revolt, and reigns over the universe after Heaven and Earth are separated. With Rhea, he becomes parent of Zeus and other children, but, fearing a child destined to take his kingship, swallows each at birth, including a stone he believes is Zeus. Rhea saves Zeus; when Zeus grows strong, he overcomes Cronos and forces him to regurgitate the children. Cronos also takes the shape of a horse and mates with Philyra, producing Cheiron.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

The Hesiodic Poems

Cronos leads the Titans’ revolt against Heaven and reigns over the universe after Heaven and Earth are separated. Knowing that one of his children is destined to overcome him, Cronos swallows each child at birth until Zeus is saved by Rhea; Zeus later defeats him, and Cronos is forced to disgorge the swallowed children.

THE THEOGONY

Cronos was the youngest and most terrible of Earth and Heaven's children, and he hated his father. At Earth's urging, he used a jagged sickle to cut off Heaven's members and later ruled until Zeus overcame him.

(ll. 453-491) But Rhea was subject in love to Cronos and bare splendid

Cronos swallows each child born to Rhea because he fears that one of his sons will take his kingship. Earth later deceives him, and he regurgitates his children after Zeus has grown strong.

FRAGMENTS OF THE EPIC CYCLE / THE WAR OF THE TITANS

Cronos is said by the author of the War of the Giants to have taken the shape of a horse and lain with Philyra, daughter of Ocean. Through this union Cheiron was born a centaur.

Cronus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Cronus, youngest of the Titans, overthrows Sky with an adamantine sickle and becomes ruler. Married to Rhea, he fathers Demeter, Hera, Pluto, Poseidon, and Zeus. Fearing a prophecy that a son will dethrone him, he imprisons the Cyclopes and swallows his children, but mistakes a wrapped stone for Zeus. Forced by Metis’s drug to disgorge the stone and children, he faces war from Zeus and them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

BOOK I

Cronus was the youngest of the Titans and used an adamantine sickle to cut off Sky's genitals and overthrow him. He became ruler, but again imprisoned the Cyclopes in Tartarus and swallowed his children because of the prophecy that his own son would dethrone him. When Rhea gave him a stone wrapped as a newborn child, he swallowed the stone instead of Zeus.

II. ᾿Ἐ ππειδὴ δὲ Ζεὺς ἐγενήθη τέλειος, λαμβάνει

Cronus swallows a drug given by Metis and is forced to disgorge first the stone and then the children he had swallowed. Zeus and those children subsequently wage war against him and the Titans.
