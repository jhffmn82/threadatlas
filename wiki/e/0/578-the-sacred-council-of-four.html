Abstract written by a model

The Sacred Council of Four, an authority whose commands Brigham Young conveys, tests Lucy Ferrier's faith by ordering her marriage to an eligible Mormon; after John Ferrier defies it, it controls guarded roads and travel orders.

CHAPTER III.

The Sacred Council of Four has decided that Lucy's faith will be tested through her marriage. Brigham Young presents its orders as commands that John Ferrier cannot safely resist.

CHAPTER IV.

The Council of Four is invoked by Stangerson as an authority Ferrier has defied. The roads around the settlement are watched and guarded, and passage requires an order from the Council.
