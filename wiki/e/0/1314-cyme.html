Abstract written by a model

Cyme is a high city, called a lovely maiden, near Sardene’s foothills and supplied by the divine Hermus. Its people are urged to reverence one needing a home and a stranger’s support; later, a speaker longs to leave its sacred streets for another country.

HOMER’S EPIGRAMS2601

Cyme is called a lovely maiden and a high city near the foothills of Sardene, whose inhabitants drink the water of the divine Hermus. Its people are urged to show reverence to a person who needs a home and a stranger’s support. In another epigram, the speaker says that his limbs do not wish to remain in Cyme’s sacred streets and that he wants to leave for another country.
