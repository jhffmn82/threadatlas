BIBLIOGRAPHY

The Works and Days manuscripts are designated S and A through Q and include witnesses from the fourth through fifteenth centuries, alongside the Oxyrhynchus Papyri witness. Rudolf Rzach groups them into Ωa, Ωb, Ψa, Ψb, Φa, and Φb.
