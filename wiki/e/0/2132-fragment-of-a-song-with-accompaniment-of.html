FRAGMENT OF A SONG WITH ACCOMPANIMENT OF DANCE

The fragment of a song with accompaniment of dance was written on the occasion of an eclipse of the Sun, probably that of April 30, B.C. 463. It presents a prayer concerning the eclipse and its possible consequences for Thebes.
