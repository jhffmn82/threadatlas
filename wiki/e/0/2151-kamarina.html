Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Kamarina is a new-peopled city destroyed by Gelo before its restoration, protected by its tutelary nymph, and glorified through Psaumis’s victory. In The Extant Odes of Pindar, the new city is identified with Kamarina, rebuilt after Gelo’s destruction and associated with bringing the commonwealth from perplexity to light.

Kamarina in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · 497 BC · the work’s page · read it where Kamarina first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kamarina is a new-peopled city, destroyed by Gelo before its restoration and protected by its tutelary nymph. Its citizens, sacred grove, lake, Hipparis-fed sacred channels, twin altars, and five-day games mark it. Psaumis seeks through victory to raise its glory, and Zeus is asked to glorify it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

IV.

Kamarina is the city whose glory Psaumis seeks to raise through his victory.

V.

Kamarina is called a new-peopled city and is praised as the city Psaumis helps make great. The ode asks Zeus to glorify it with the renown of brave men.
