Agesias of Syracuse in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Agesias, son of Sostratos, was an Iamid descendant, citizen of Syracuse and Stymphalos, joint founder of Syracuse, and treasurer to Zeus’s altar at Pisa; he won Olympia’s mule-chariot race and was praised in a triumphal ode.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI.

Agesias, the son of Sostratos, won the mule-chariot race at Olympia. He was a citizen of Syracuse and Stymphalos, descended from the Iamid clan, and is praised as the subject of the triumphal ode.
