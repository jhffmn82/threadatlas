the assembly

Also called: assembly, councils, full assembly, meeting of our councillors, place of assembly

Abstract written by a model

The assembly is a public council of Ithaca, convened after Ulysses’s departure and called by Telemachus. Minerva directs Telemachus to accuse the suitors before the gods; speakers debate the matter, but the gathering ends without restraining the suitors.

The assembly is where Minerva tells Telemachus to present his case against the suitors and call the gods to witness. Telemachus announces that he will meet the suitors there in the morning to give them formal notice to depart.

BOOK II

The assembly is called by Telemachus after Ulysses's long absence. It hears speeches from Aegyptius, Telemachus, Antinous, Halitherses, Eurymachus, Mentor, and Leiocritus, but it ends without restraining the suitors.
