BERT in Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation

Hazem Abdelazim · 2023

BERT is a pre-trained language model and transformer used for neural reading comprehension. It encodes sentences into fixed-length vectors; BERT-based embeddings led reviewed Arabic sentiment experiments, and its reader reached 61.3 F1 on ARCD.

III. .. Sentence-BERT: Sentence embeddings, which capture the

BERT-based contextualized embeddings achieved the highest performance in the reviewed Arabic sentiment-analysis experiments in general. A BERT-based reader was also used in SOQAL and achieved a 61.3 F1 score on the Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset.
