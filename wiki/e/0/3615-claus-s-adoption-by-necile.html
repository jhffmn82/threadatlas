Claus's adoption by Necile in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

Necile's adoption of Claus is permitted and sanctioned by Ak, while the forest nymphs' adoption with her draws interest from Ryls, Knooks, and Fairies unfamiliar with immortals adopting a human child.

4. Claus

The adoption of Claus by Necile and the forest nymphs is sanctioned by Ak. It attracts the interest of the Ryls, Knooks, and Fairies, whose customs or laws had not prepared them for an immortal community adopting a human child.
