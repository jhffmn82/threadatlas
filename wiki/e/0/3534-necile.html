  • In The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus: Necile is a beautiful, immortal wood- and forest-nymph of the Forest of Burzee, closely related to Queen Zurline. With chestnut-bur-lined hair, eyes blue in sunlight and purple in shade, and an oak-leaf-green costume, she is highly regarded for beauty and grace. She lives in her bower, guards and waters the trees, keeps hurtful weeds away, and frightens Gadgols. As the forest becomes established and her duties lessen, she broods over her life, grows discontented, and longs for adventure, though the Law of the Forest restrains her from leaving. Hearing of a forsaken human infant in danger, Necile feels tender pity and wilfully defies that Law. She rescues the child at the forest's edge, brings him into Burzee, and asks Ak to allow her to keep and care for him. Once granted permission, she rejoices, names him Claus rather than Neclaus, prepares a moss bed, and raises him in her bower. She teaches him to speak, walk, play, and understand the forest and its inhabitants, adapting her life to follow him through Burzee. Her love becomes that of a devoted foster-mother: she weeps when Ak takes him away, anxiously awaits him, and grieves at his increasing thoughtfulness. When Claus leaves the forest, he remembers her as his loving foster-mother and comrade, promising to love and cherish her; she embraces and kisses him tenderly. She later speaks with him about children and riches, and he makes the first toy nymph in her likeness. While Santa Claus grows old, Necile remains youthful, strong, and beautiful. Troubled by the fate threatening him, she seeks Ak's help and finally admires and kisses his immortal form.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

2. The Child of the Forest

Necile is a beautiful wood-nymph related to Queen Zurline who guards trees in the Forest of Burzee. As her duties become easier and less frequent, she grows discontented and longs for adventure. After hearing about a human infant in danger, she breaks the Law of the Forest, finds the child, and carries it into the forest.

3. The Adoption

Necile brings the sleeping infant into the Forest of Burzee and asks Ak to let her keep and care for it. After Ak grants her request, she hurries to her bower to rejoice over her new-found treasure.

4. Claus

Necile names the infant Claus, gathers soft moss for his bed, and cares for him in her bower. She teaches him to speak, walk, and play, and loves to call him Claus even after the Queen suggests the name Neclaus.

5. The Master Woodsman

Necile raises Claus and comes to love him dearly. She adapts her life to follow him through Burzee and teaches him about the forest and its inhabitants. She weeps when Ak takes Claus away for a time and clings to him as if she cannot bear to let him go.

6. Claus Discovers Humanity

Necile had rescued Claus when he was abandoned at the edge of the forest and had brought him to manhood under the protection of the immortals. She anxiously awaits his return and sighs when she sees how deeply thoughtful he has become.

7. Claus Leaves the Forest

Necile is remembered by Claus as his loving foster-mother and comrade. Claus kisses her and promises to love and cherish her while he lives, while she silently embraces and kisses him tenderly at his departure.

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

Necile listens to Claus’s concern about giving toys to rich children and says that riches are only like a gown that can be put on or removed, while children remain alike. Claus makes the first toy nymph in her likeness.

OLD AGE / 1. The Mantle of Immortality

Necile, who reared Santa Claus as his foster mother, remains youthful, strong, and beautiful while he grows old. Troubled by the fate threatening Claus, she seeks Ak's help and later kisses Claus while admiring his immortal form.
