Abstract written by a model

The party of prisoners is Scarecrow and Tin Woodman’s traveling party. It enters the Emerald City, reaches the royal palace, and is surrounded and captured by the Army of Revolt. Mice panic the rebels, allowing the party to escape, bar the palace entrances, and hold a council of war. Reassembling on the roof, it builds the flying Thing, rides in the Gump, and seeks Glinda the Good. Blown off course, it finds wishing pills, crashes safely in Jackdaw’s Nest, and protects vulnerable companions from returning birds.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

The Prisoners of the Queen

The Scarecrow's party enters the Emerald City and reaches the royal palace without resistance before being surrounded and captured by the Army of Revolt. It escapes when mice panic the rebels, then bars the palace entrances and gathers for a council of war.

The Astonishing Flight of the Gump

The adventurers reassemble on the roof with assorted materials for building a machine that can fly and carry them to safety. They help construct the Thing and struggle to avoid being blown from the roof when it begins moving.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The travelers fly with the Gump toward Glinda the Good, discover the wishing pills, and become lost beyond the Land of Oz. After the crash they land safely in the Jackdaw’s nest, where they face the returning birds and try to protect their more vulnerable companions.
