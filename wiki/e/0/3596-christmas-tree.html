Christmas tree in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

The first Christmas tree begins as a pine tree Claus plants for three little children, then is decorated with candles, toys, and candies. Pleased, Claus later carries and decorates trees for children in poor homes, with some fathers obtaining trees for him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

12. The First Christmas Tree

The first Christmas tree in this account is made when Claus plants a pine tree for the three little children and decorates it with candles, toys, and candies. Pleased with the idea, Claus later carries trees to poor homes and decorates them for the children, while some fathers obtain trees for him to prepare.
