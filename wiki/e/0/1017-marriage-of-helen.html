marriage of Helen

event · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Menelaus won her, marriage, marriage of Helen and Menelaus, marriage of the maid, suit for Helen, the hand of Helen
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, it follows Menelaus’s victory over the other suitors in the contest for Helen’s hand; Menelaus prevailed by offering the greatest gifts, and Helen later bore him Hermione and Nicostratus.
  • In The Library, it is the marriage for which Tyndareus chose Menelaus as Helen’s bridegroom after obtaining the oath of her suitors.
