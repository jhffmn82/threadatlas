Abstract written by a model

In The Amores, or Amours, poets are called hallowed and cared for by the Deities, and their inspired verses preserve lasting fame. In Impressions of Theophrastus Such, poets are admired by the narrator, though Lentulus regards them as merely versifiers and believes poetry should use true ideas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY IX.

Poets are called hallowed and are believed by some to possess divine inspiration. Their verses can preserve lasting fame, as shown by the enduring accounts of Trojan deeds and by Tibullus's surviving poetry.
