X.

The ode celebrates Hippokleas's victory and praises his family, Thorax, the Aleuadai, Thessaly, and Lakedaimon. It contains a long account of Perseus and the Hyperborean folk, and it is described as Pindar's earliest surviving complete poem, written when he was twenty.
