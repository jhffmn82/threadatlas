Abstract written by a model

Keyphrases are single- or multi-word lexical units that summarize text and support indexing, categorizing, and mining. In keyphrase generation, they are target outputs and associated passage labels. A multilingual method retrieves related English passages, uses their English keyphrase annotations as external knowledge or hints, and generates concatenated target-language keyphrases with separator tokens.

Abstract

Keyphrases are automatically predicted from long text in the keyphrase generation task. In the proposed method, English keyphrase annotations associated with retrieved English passages serve as external knowledge for multilingual generation.

Introduction

Keyphrases are single- or multi-word lexical units that summarize text and support indexing, categorizing, and mining. In the proposed multilingual method, English keyphrases retrieved from related passages serve as hints for generating keyphrases in another language.

Task Definition

A keyphrase is a target output corresponding to a given text. The task predicts a total of n target keyphrases in the same language as the text.

Model .. 4.3

A keyphrase is used as an associated label for a passage and as the output of the multilingual generation model. Retrieved English keyphrases provide external information, while target-language keyphrases are concatenated with separator tokens for training and generation.
