Oenomaus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Oenomaus, son of Mars and Harpina and king of Pisa, fathered Hippodamia; fearing an oracle about her marriage, he killed losing suitors in races until Pelops won, after which he killed himself.

Narrative, section by section

Oenomaus was the son of Mars and Harpina and the father of Hippodamia. Fearing an oracle that he would die when she married, he challenged her suitors to a horse race and killed those who lost; after Pelops won, Oenomaus killed himself.

Oenomaus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Oenomaus, king of Pisa and father of Hippodamia, possessed arms and horses given by Ares. He made her marriage a contest, pursued suitors in armor, killed those he overtook, and displayed their heads. He was eventually killed or died after losing the race to Pelops.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II. Ὅτι ὁ Τάνταλος ἐν “Atdov? κολάζεται,

Oenomaus was king of Pisa and the father of Hippodamia. He set the marriage contest for her, pursued her suitors in full armor, and killed those he overtook; he was eventually killed or died after losing the race to Pelops.
