Polyphemus's eye

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Cyclops’ eye, eye, eyesight, glaring eye, lids, orb of sight, the Cyclops’s eye, visual nerve

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, Polyphemus's eye is pierced by the heated olive-wood club, leaving him unable to see. In Hecuba and other plays, the Cyclops’s eye is pierced and burned by the heated brand, depriving Polyphemus of his eyesight.
