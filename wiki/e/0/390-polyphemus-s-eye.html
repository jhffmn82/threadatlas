Polyphemus's eye

Also called: Cyclops’ eye, eye, eyesight, glaring eye, lids, orb of sight, the Cyclops’s eye, visual nerve
  • In Hecuba and other plays, the Cyclops’s eye is pierced and burned by the heated brand, depriving Polyphemus of his eyesight.
  • In The Odyssey, Polyphemus's eye is pierced by the heated olive-wood club, leaving him unable to see.
