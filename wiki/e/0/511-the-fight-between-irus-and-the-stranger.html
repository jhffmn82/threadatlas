Abstract written by a model

The fight between Irus and the stranger is arranged by Antinous to entertain the suitors, with food for the victor. Irus strikes Ulysses’s shoulder, but Ulysses defeats him with a blow under the ear, breaks his skull bones, drags him outside, and leaves him unable to stand or return home.

BOOK XVIII

The fight is arranged by Antinous as entertainment for the suitors, with food promised to the victor. Irus strikes Ulysses's shoulder, but Ulysses hits him under the ear, breaks the bones of his skull, and knocks him down before dragging him outside.

Telemachus says that the fight did not turn out as the suitors intended because the stranger defeated Irus. Irus is left unable to stand or return home, and Ulysses refers to having beaten him when Eurymachus mocks him.
