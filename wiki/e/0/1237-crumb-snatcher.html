Crumb-snatcher

Also called: Mouse, dry-hobs, hapless Crumb-snatcher, wily Mice

Abstract written by a model

Crumb-snatcher is a Mouse, king and warrior, son of Bread-nibbler and Quern-licker. He prefers human food, avoids vegetables, and fears ferrets, hawks, and traps. Riding Puff-jaw, he is drowned when Puff-jaw dives from a water-snake. He later fights for dead Mice, kills Leeky and Cabbage-climber, and is killed by Croakperson.

Crumb-snatcher is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

Crumb-snatcher is a Mouse, the son of Bread-nibbler and Quern-licker, and he describes his preference for human foods and his fear of the ferret, hawk, and traps. After riding on Puff-jaw's back, he is abandoned in the lake when Puff-jaw dives from a water-snake and drowns. Later he fights to avenge the dead Mice, killing Leeky and Cabbage-climber before Croakperson kills him.
