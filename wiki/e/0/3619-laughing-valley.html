Laughing Valley

Abstract written by a model

The Laughing Valley of Hohaho is a happy, peaceful valley at the eastern edge of Burzee, between rolling green hills, with a brook wandering through it. Filled with grass, wildflowers, bees, butterflies, happiness, peace, and good-will, it has long awaited Claus as its tenant. Its cheerful natural life restores his merriment, and he chooses it as his home, dwelling, workshop, and the place where he makes and stores toys. Winter brings heavy snow that covers his cottage and hinders his visits to children, but after storms pass he travels from the Valley to help others. In warmer times its brook, grass, sun, and other features seem to laugh, inspiring Claus to sing, laugh, and whistle. Visitors, including Bessie’s cavalcade, come to his dwelling, while the Awgwas block children from reaching it. The Valley becomes a battlefield when Ak gathers the immortals there; they defeat the Awgwas and their allies. Claus also begins and ends his first reindeer journey there, and leaves and returns there on Christmas Eve after distributing toys. After many healthy years, when Santa Claus becomes old and weak, the once-merry Valley is hushed and mournful.

Laughing Valley is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

7. Claus Leaves the Forest

The Laughing Valley of Hohaho lies at the eastern edge of Burzee, between green hills with a brook running through it. Its cheerful natural life restores Claus's merriment, and he chooses the valley as his home.

MANHOOD / 1. The Laughing Valley

The Laughing Valley is a peaceful, flower-filled place inhabited by grass, a brook, wildflowers, bees, and butterflies. It claims Claus as its tenant, brings him happiness, and becomes his home.

2. How Claus Made the First Toy

The Laughing Valley is where Claus lives and where snowflakes fall heavily when winter arrives. Its winter weather covers Claus's dwelling in white and makes it difficult for him to visit the children.

3. How the Ryls Colored the Toys

The Laughing Valley is covered in snow during a fierce winter storm. Claus lives there, and after the wind moves north and the snow settles, he travels through the Valley to help others.

4. How Little Mayrie Became Frightened

After winter ends, the Laughing Valley becomes filled with joyous excitement, with the brook, grass, sun, and other natural features seeming lively and happy. Claus says that living in the Laughing Valley, where everything else laughs, makes him sing, laugh, and whistle.

5. How Bessie Blithesome Came to the Laughing Valley

The Laughing Valley is where Claus lives and where Bessie’s cavalcade arrives at his dwelling. After consulting Necile and the Queen of the Fairies, Claus returns to the Valley to make dolls.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The Laughing Valley is Claus's home and the place where he makes his toys. The Awgwas prevent children from reaching it after Claus gives up trying to travel to the villages.

7. The Great Battle Between Good and Evil

The Laughing Valley is where Claus makes his toys and where Ak orders the immortals to assemble for battle. The valley becomes the battlefield in which the immortals defeat the Awgwas and their allies.

8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

The Laughing Valley is Claus's home and workshop, where he stores and makes toys. Claus begins and ends his first journey with the reindeer there.

10. Christmas Eve

The Laughing Valley is where Claus returns to work after consulting Ak. He leaves it with Glossie and Flossie at moonrise for his Christmas Eve journey and returns there after distributing the toys.

OLD AGE / 1. The Mantle of Immortality

The Laughing Valley is described as a peaceful place where Santa Claus has lived and maintained perfect health for many years. When he grows old and weak, the Valley is hushed and mournful.
