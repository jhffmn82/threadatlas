Penelope's web

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Penelope's woven pall, a great tambour-frame, a pall, an enormous piece of fine needlework, her work, my great web, the funeral canopy of Laertes, the robe

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, Penelope's web is a burial pall for Laertes, woven by day and secretly unpicked by night for three years until maids reveal the deception and suitors force its completion. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Penelope's web is the robe she claimed to be preparing for the funeral canopy of Laertes, woven by day and unraveled at night as a proverb for work continually done but never finished.
