Wisk in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Wisk is a tiny, mischievous, lovable fairy and one of Santa Claus’s four little helpers. He suggests entering houses through walls when chimneys cannot be used, then tickles the reindeer and has his ears tweaked by Santa Claus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3. The Deputies of Santa Claus

Wisk suggests that the little folk enter houses through walls when chimneys cannot be used. During the journey, he mischievously tickles the reindeer with a feather, so Santa Claus watches him and tweaks his ears to make him behave.
