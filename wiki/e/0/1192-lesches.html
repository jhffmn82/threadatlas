Lesches

person · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Lescheos, Lescheos of Pyrrha, Lescheos the son of Aeschylinus of Pyrrha, Lesches of Mitylene, Lesches of Pyrrha or Mitylene, Lesches or Lescheos, the same poet
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, he is identified as the author of the four-book Little Iliad and is credited with accounts of the quarrel between Aias and Odysseus, Odysseus’s disguise, and details of the fall of Ilium.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, he gives accounts of the deaths of Eioneus, Admetus, Coroebus, Priam, Axion, and Agenor, saying that Priam was killed at the palace doors rather than at the altar of Household Zeus and that Coroebus was killed by Diomede.
