Abstract written by a model

The computer science domain is a specialized evaluation domain assessed with CS-Bench. It uses Wikipedia’s computer science subset and arXiv articles as source corpora for pseudo-labeled data, addressing limited computer-science coverage in the broader scientific domain. SimRAG scores 57.63 with an 8B backbone and 60.96 with 27B, improving on corresponding Llama and Gemma variants.

Experimental Setup / Tasks and Datasets .. Implementation Details

The computer science domain is evaluated with CS-Bench and is distinguished from the broader scientific domain because the latter has limited coverage of computer science topics. Wikipedia's computer science subset and arXiv articles are used as source corpora for pseudo-labeled data.

Experimental Results / Main Results .. Case Studies

In the computer science domain, SimRAG achieves overall scores of 57.63 with the 8B backbone and 60.96 with the 27B backbone. It improves over the corresponding Llama and Gemma variants and remains promising despite computer science being described as relatively new and less studied.
