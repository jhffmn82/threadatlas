Abstract written by a model

A marriage proposal is made by Mrs. Terry for Mrs. Whately’s young daughter to marry the narrator’s brother Tom. The narrator and his father discuss it with Mrs. Terry and the eager Mrs. Whately, who fears her daughter’s youth and small portion but offers £200; they await the father’s response from Hatfield.

opening

The proposal concerns Mrs. Whately’s daughter as a possible wife for the narrator’s brother Tom. The narrator and his father discuss it with Mrs. Terry and Mrs. Whately; Mrs. Whately is eager for it to proceed but worries that her daughter is too young and that her portion is insufficient, although she offers £200 down. The daughter is described as well-favoured, modest, and very young, and the narrator thinks she would suit his brother; the matter awaits word from her father, who is at Hatfield.
