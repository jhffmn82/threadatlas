Natural Questions in Generation-Augmented Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

Yuning Mao, Pengcheng He, Xiaodong Liu, Yelong Shen, Jianfeng Gao, Jiawei Han, Weizhu Chen · 2021

Natural Questions is an OpenQA dataset used as an open-domain question-answering benchmark and in GAR experiments. It has 79,168 training, 8,757 validation, and 3,610 test samples; questions average 12.5 tokens, answers 5.2 tokens, and each question has 1.2 answers on average. It is a main dataset for ablations and evaluation: GAR achieves state-of-the-art extractive performance, improves retrieval and end-to-end results, and gains further EM when combined with DPR, though dense retrieval is stronger at very small cutoffs without DPR.
