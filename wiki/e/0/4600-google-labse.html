In Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation, Google LaBSE is the research multilingual SBERT model producing 768-dimensional embeddings across 112 languages, including Arabic. In GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation, LaBSE is a BERT-based dual-encoder model for cross-lingual sentence retrieval.
