Lokrians

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Epizephyrian Lokrians, mother city of Lokrians, the maiden daughter of the Lokrian

Abstract written by a model

The Lokrians, linked to tree-filled Opous, honour Aias Oileus, excel in arts, and fight bravely with spears. Their ancestry runs through Lokros and Zeus; Epizephyrian Lokrians were shielded by Hieron from war and Anaxilaos’s attack, celebrated by a maiden’s song.

Lokrians is in one work on this site, The Extant Odes of Pindar. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

IX.

The Lokrians are associated with Opous, their mother city of beautiful trees. Their ancestral story traces them through Lokros, the daughter of Opöeis, and the child begotten by Zeus.

II.

The Epizephyrian Lokrians were protected by Hieron from the troubles of war and from Anaxilaos's threatened attack. Their maiden daughter is said to sing of her deliverance before the house-door.
