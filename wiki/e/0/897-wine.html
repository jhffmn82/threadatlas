Thus did they converse, and by sunset the work of the Achaeans

Wine came from Lemnos in many ships and was supplied by Eueneus the son of Jason. The Achaeans bought it with various goods and captives, but after Jove's thunder they poured it onto the ground and did not drink until they had made offerings.
