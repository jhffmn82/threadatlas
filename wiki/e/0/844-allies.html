allies in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

The allies, in the city with the Trojans, sleep instead of keeping watch, leaving the camp’s guard to the Trojans because their wives and children are absent.

Trojan allies in The Library

Allied forces from surrounding cities arrive after nine years, joining Trojans under numerous named leaders, from Aeneas through Sarpedon and Glaucus.

Trojan allies arrive from surrounding cities after nine years. They include forces led by Aeneas, Acamas, Theanus, Pyraechmes, Pylaemenes, Pandarus, Adrastus, Amphius, Asius, Hippothous, Chromius, Ennomus, Odius, Epistrophus, Phorcys, Ascanius, Mesthles, Antiphus, Nastes, Amphimachus, Sarpedon, and Glaucus.
