burial and burning of the dead in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

After the truce, Trojans and Achaeans gather, cleanse, mourn, and burn their dead on pyres; Trojans do so silently by Priam’s order, then Achaeans return to ships.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK VII.

After the armies agree to a truce, the Trojans and Achaeans gather their dead, wash away the blood, mourn them, and place them on pyres. The Trojans burn their dead silently by Priam’s order, and the Achaeans do likewise before returning to their ships.
