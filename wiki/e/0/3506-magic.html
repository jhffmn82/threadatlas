magic in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Magic begins when Tip uses the Powder of Life, a pepper-box, spoken words, and gestures to animate a wooden saw-horse. It later restrains and truth-tests Mombi and changes Ozma into Tip and back, while Glinda condemns transformations as dishonest witchcraft.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Tip Makes an Experiment in Magic

The magic in this unit involves the Powder of Life, the pepper-box, and the spoken words and hand gestures associated with Mombi. Tip uses this magic to bring the wooden saw-horse to life.

Princess Ozma of Oz

Magic is used to restrain Mombi, test whether she speaks truthfully, and transform Ozma into Tip and back again. Glinda says that transformations are dishonest magic used by unscrupulous witches and that she does not practice them.
