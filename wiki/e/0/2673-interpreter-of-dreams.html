interpreter of dreams

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: augur, interpres, interpreter, interpreter of the dreams of the night, the interpreter, the nocturnal dream augur

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the nocturnal dream augur interprets the speaker’s dream, identifying the white heifer as the beloved girl, the bull as the speaker, the crow as an old procuress, and the dark mark as evidence of adultery. In The Amores, or Amours, the interpreter of dreams weighs each detail of night visions, explaining symbols as the narrator’s mistress, narrator, Love, and a warning about a procuress.
