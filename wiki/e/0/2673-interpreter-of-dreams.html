interpreter of dreams

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: augur, interpres, interpreter, interpreter of the dreams of the night, the interpreter, the nocturnal dream augur
  • In The Amores, or Amours, the interpreter of dreams weighs each detail of night visions, explaining symbols as the narrator’s mistress, narrator, Love, and a warning about a procuress.
  • In Heroides and Amores, the nocturnal dream augur interprets the speaker’s dream, identifying the white heifer as the beloved girl, the bull as the speaker, the crow as an old procuress, and the dark mark as evidence of adultery.

ELEGY V.

The interpreter of dreams considers each detail of the vision and explains its meaning. The interpreter identifies the cow as the narrator's mistress, the bull as the narrator, the heat as Love, and the crow's attack as a warning about a procuress.
