StrategyQA

dataset · 6 works · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: SQA

Abstract written by a model

In Active Retrieval Augmented Generation, StrategyQA was a collection of crowdsourced yes/no questions used to evaluate commonsense reasoning, on which FLARE reached 77.3 EM. In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, StrategyQA was a commonsense reasoning dataset with 500 training questions used for evaluation, on which ITER-RETGEN reached 73.0 Acc† at two iterations. In BlendFilter: Advancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models via Query Generation Blending and Knowledge Filtering, StrategyQA was the public commonsense reasoning benchmark used for evaluation with the first 500 training questions. In DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models, StrategyQA was a benchmark dataset for commonsense reasoning on which DRAGIN was evaluated. In Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering, StrategyQA was one of four evaluated multi-hop question-answering benchmarks, on which GenGround achieved 77.12 Accuracy. In DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation, StrategyQA was a dataset used for question-answering evaluation, on which DioR achieved a BM25 Precision of 0.659.

StrategyQA in Active Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zhengbao Jiang · 2023 · the work’s page · read it where StrategyQA first appears

Abstract written by a model

StrategyQA is a collection of crowdsourced yes/no questions used to evaluate commonsense reasoning. It generates a chain of thought and final answer, scored by exact match against a gold answer. On it, FLARE reaches 77.3 EM, but retrieval beyond about half of sentences lowers performance through noise.
