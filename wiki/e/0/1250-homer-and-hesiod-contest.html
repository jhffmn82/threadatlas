OF THE ORIGIN OF HOMER AND HESIOD, AND OF THEIR CONTEST

The contest took place at Chalcis and involved questions, riddling verses, mathematical calculation, moral answers, and recitations from the poets’ own works. Homer was said to excel in the exchanges, but Hesiod won because Paneides judged that a poet who promoted peace and husbandry deserved the prize over one who depicted war and slaughter.
