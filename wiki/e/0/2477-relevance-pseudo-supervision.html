Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision generates a pseudo-label for each reference candidate by linearly combining lexical similarity to a ground-truth review with rating accuracy. It trains SL-REFLECT models using binary cross entropy, and the gold review is needed only during training.
