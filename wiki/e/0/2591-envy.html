Also called: Livor, envious, she, the other

Envy in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Envy is a personification of envy who lives in a concealed, sunless, cold, dark cave. She eats vipers, carries a thorn-wreathed staff and black poison, and suffers at others’ success. Commanded by Minerva, she poisons Aglauros by showing her Herse, Herse’s marriage, and Mercury, causing jealousy.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Envy's abode is hidden in the lowest recesses of a cave without sunlight or wind. It is dismal, filled with benumbing cold, always without fire, and always full of darkness.

opening

Envy is shown eating the flesh of vipers and suffering from jealousy of others’ successes. At Minerva’s command, she infects Aglauros with her poison and places Herse, Herse’s marriage, and Mercury before Aglauros’s eyes.
