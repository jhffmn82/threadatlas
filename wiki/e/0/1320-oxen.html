Abstract written by a model

Two nine-year-old oxen, best for agricultural labor and seasonal work, are chosen for ploughing; vulnerable to fighting in the furrow, they are fed, winter-rationed, and rested after work.

(ll. 383-404) When the Pleiades, daughters of Atlas, are rising 1310,

Two nine-year-old oxen are recommended for ploughing because their strength is unspent and they are in their prime. They should be fed in the byre before ploughing, given half their usual food in winter, and allowed to rest after work.
