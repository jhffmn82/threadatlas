Abstract written by a model

Discord, the only goddess among the combatants, is sent by Jove to the Achaean ships with war’s ensign; beside Ulysses’ hull, her cry gives the Achaeans courage to fight.

Discord is in one work on this site, The Iliad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
