Discord in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Discord, the only goddess among the combatants, is sent by Jove to the Achaean ships with war’s ensign; beside Ulysses’ hull, her cry gives the Achaeans courage to fight.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XI.

Jove sends Discord to the Achaean ships with the ensign of war. Standing beside Ulysses’ ship, she raises a loud cry that gives the Achaeans courage to remain and fight.
