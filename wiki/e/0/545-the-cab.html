the cab in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

The cab is a growler driven by Jefferson Hope in his pursuit and vengeance against Enoch Drebber. Hope uses it to follow Drebber and Joseph Stangerson around London, transport Drebber to the Brixton Road house, and return after losing Lucy Ferrier’s marriage ring. Its narrow wheel marks and the wandering horse help Holmes infer that the murderer drove it and entered the house.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope uses his cab to follow Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson around London and to transport Drebber to the house in the Brixton Road. After Drebber's death, Hope returns to the house in the cab's vicinity after realizing that he has lost Lucy Ferrier's marriage ring.

CHAPTER VII.

Holmes finds marks showing that a cab was at the house during the night and determines from the narrow wheel gauge that it was a cab rather than a private carriage. The horse’s wandering indicates that its driver was inside the house, leading Holmes to conclude that the murderer had used the cab to follow Drebber.
