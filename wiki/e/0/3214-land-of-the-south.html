Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the Land of the South is a country ruled by Glinda where the Quadlings live, depicted as a rich and happy land. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the South Country of the Quadlings is a wild, secluded southern country that travelers enter in search of a dark well and cross to reach a Quadling man's home. In The Lost Princess of Oz, Quadling Country contains a far-country tree where Ugu sat, and its northeast is where Ugu escapes while Scarecrow and Tin Woodman seek Ozma.

Abstract written by a model

The Land of the South is a country ruled by Glinda where the Quadlings live. Dorothy and her three companions choose to travel there despite dangers. It unfolds as a rich, happy land of grain fields, paved roads, bridged brooks, and red buildings, fences, bridges, and clothing.

The Land of the South is where Glinda rules the Quadlings. Dorothy and all three companions decide to travel there despite the dangers along the road.

The country of the Quadlings is rich and happy, with fields of grain, paved roads, brooks, and bridges. Its fences, houses, and bridges are red, and its short, fat inhabitants wear red clothing.
