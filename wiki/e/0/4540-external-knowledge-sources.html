  • In "Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge," external information sources provide knowledge, including texts, tables, and knowledge graphs, that RAG models can directly seek to help answer questions.
  • In "Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability," external databases provide information that supplements the limited internal knowledge of large language models and are typically composed of diverse sources.
  • In "DioR: Adaptive Cognitive Detection and Contextual Retrieval Optimization for Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation," external knowledge bases supply documents retrieved using prioritized keywords and BM25; selected documents are segmented and inserted into the LLM prompt as external knowledge to support continued generation.
  • In "LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation," the external knowledge database supplies the information that the retrieval module indexes and searches, with its indexed data structure expected to adapt as new information is added.
  • In "Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review," external knowledge datasets are information sources used to ground large language models for healthcare question-answering, dialogue, and retrieval.
