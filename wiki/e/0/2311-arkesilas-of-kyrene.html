Arkesilas of Kyrene in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Arkesilas is the king of Kyrene, of the seed of Battos in the eighth descent, and a victor in the chariot race at the Pythian games. He is celebrated by Ode V as wealthy, righteous, wise, eloquent, courageous, and favored and protected by the gods, with support linked to Kastor and friendship with Karrhotos. The records praise his lineage and victory, urge him to secure Kyrene’s destiny, and pray that divine favor and athletic renown continue through his descendants.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

IV.

Arkesilas is king of Kyrene and the victor in the chariot-race at Pytho. He is described as being in the eighth generation after Battos, and the ode praises his lineage while urging him to secure Kyrene's good destiny.

V.

Arkesilas is celebrated as the winner of the chariot-race at the Pythian games. He is praised as a divinely favored king of mighty cities, wealthy, righteous, wise beyond his years, eloquent, courageous, and gifted with the Muses. The ode prays that he will retain divine favor and that his descendants will win similar renown at Olympia.
