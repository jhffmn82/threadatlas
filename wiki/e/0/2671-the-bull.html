the bull

animal · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: A bull, The male, bull, cornigerum, her companion, her mate, taurum, taurus

Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the bull is the white heifer's happy mate, lying beside her while resting and chewing; after the heifer hesitates and leaves him, he is left on the abandoned bed. In The Amores, or Amours, the bull grazes and rests as the white cow's companion and mate; after the crow leaves them, the interpreter identifies him with the narrator and predicts he will be left behind.
