Also called: A bull, The male, bull, cornigerum, her companion, her mate, taurum, taurus
  • In The Amores, or Amours, the bull grazes and rests as the white cow's companion and mate; after the crow leaves them, the interpreter identifies him with the narrator and predicts he will be left behind.
  • In Heroides and Amores, the bull is the white heifer's happy mate, lying beside her while resting and chewing; after the heifer hesitates and leaves him, he is left on the abandoned bed.

ELEGY V.

The bull is the white cow's companion and mate, pressing and ruminating the tender grass before resting his horned head on the ground. After the crow leaves the cow and the bull, the interpreter identifies the bull with the narrator and predicts that the bull will be left behind.
