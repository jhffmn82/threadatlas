barley

Also called: Barley, the barley

Abstract written by a model

In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, barley is described as being used by Isis in the first making of bread, with Egyptians carrying it in honor of her discovery. In Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes, barley is described as a grain containing starch, used by the brewer to make beer and liquor, and ground into barley-cakes or pearl barley for food.
