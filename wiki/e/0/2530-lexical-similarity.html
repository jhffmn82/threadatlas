lexical similarity in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

Jihyeok Kim · 2020-12-08/2020-12-13

Lexical similarity is a retrieval and relevance feature based on word overlap, with TF-IDF used in T2T retrieval. In AT2T, it is insufficient alone because identifier inputs and Amazon Book reviews may not reveal rating semantics. It is later measured by the average of unigram, bigram, and four-gram BLEU scores and combined linearly with rating accuracy for reference relevance. SL uses it for reference selection, while RL-REFLECT uses BLEU-1 and prioritizes scores of at least 0.2. Analysis confirms its limits for rating semantics.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

opening .. Augmenting context using references

Lexical similarity assigns relevance based on word overlap, with TF-IDF given as an example. In AT2T, it is considered insufficient by itself because the input consists of identifiers and lexical similarity may not capture rating semantics.

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

Lexical similarity measures the similarity between a reference candidate and a ground-truth review. It is computed as the average of unigram, bigram, and four-gram BLEUs and is linearly combined with rating accuracy.

RL-REFLECT .. Stabilizing Training of RL

Lexical similarity is used by SL for efficient reference selection. RL-REFLECT uses BLEU-1 as a proxy for lexical-similarity-based look-ahead exploration and prioritizes references with BLEU-1 of at least 0.2.

Experiments .. Analysis on Rating Consistency

Lexical similarity is used alongside rating accuracy to estimate the relevance of references. The analysis finds that lexical similarity alone may fail to capture rating semantics.
