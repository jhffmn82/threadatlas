parallel passage mining

Also called: Parallel Passage Mining, parallel passage pairs, pseudo parallel passage pairs

Abstract written by a model

Parallel passage mining measures retrieved English passages by keyphrase generation, then iteratively selects those improving non-English predictions to produce pseudo parallel pairs for retrieval and generation.
