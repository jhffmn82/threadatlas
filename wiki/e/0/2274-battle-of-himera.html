Battle of Himera in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Battle of Himera ended in a decisive Sicilian victory achieved through Gelon's plan to kill Amilcar and burn the Carthaginian ships. About one hundred and fifty thousand Carthaginians were said to have been slain, while others surrendered because they lacked water.
