Battle of Himera

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Himera, battle, battle of Himera, by the fair-watered banks of Himëras, fight at Himera, the battle, the great battle of Himera

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, it is presented as a great battle against the Carthaginians in which the Syracusans under Hieron defeated them. In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, it is described as ending in a decisive Sicilian victory achieved through Gelon's plan to kill Amilcar and burn the Carthaginian ships.
