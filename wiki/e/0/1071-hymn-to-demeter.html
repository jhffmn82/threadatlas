Abstract written by a model

Hymn to Demeter concerns Persephone’s seizure by Hades and return from the lower world, unfolding into Demeter’s establishment of the Eleusinian mysteries; its text includes specified restorations used by Hugh G. Evelyn-White.

Hymn to Demeter is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
