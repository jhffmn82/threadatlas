Abstract written by a model

Hammer-Heads are armless creatures guarding a steep hill from behind rocks, refusing travelers passage and defending it by shooting and stretching heads that knock down the Scarecrow and Lion but not flying Winged Monkeys.

Chapter XXII

The Hammer-Heads guard the steep hill and refuse to let the travelers cross. They have no arms but can shoot and stretch their heads forward with enough force to knock down the Scarecrow and the Lion; they cannot reach the Winged Monkeys in flight.
