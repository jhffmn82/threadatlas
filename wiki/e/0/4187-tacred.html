TACRED

dataset · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: TACRED corpus, TACRED dataset, TACRED training set, zero-shot TACRED

Abstract written by a model

TACRED is a dataset and benchmark adapted from a supervised relation-extraction dataset into a new zero-shot and few-shot slot-filling variant for evaluating domain adaptation. It has the TACRED relation schema with 41 relation types and 2,673 test instances. Its corpus, relation labels, entity candidates, and annotated triples support evaluation of transfer to a different textual style and relation schema. No-relation instances are excluded from the ground-truth KG but retained in the corpus. Its supervised relation-extraction model was evaluated competitively against TAC-KBP 2015.

TACRED is in one work on this site, Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Abstract

TACRED is used in a new slot-filling variant to demonstrate the system's domain adaptation capability through zero-shot and few-shot learning.

opening

TACRED is used as the basis for a variant introduced to evaluate zero-shot and few-shot slot filling for domain adaptation. The unit describes this benchmark as a new dataset variant released with the paper.

Analysis .. Slot Filling Evaluation

TACRED is reorganized into a zero-shot and few-shot slot-filling benchmark with 2,673 test instances. Its corpus, relation labels, entity candidates, and annotated triples are used to evaluate KGI1's transfer to a different textual style and relation schema.

Conclusion

TACRED provides the basis for a new benchmark proposed and released for zero/few-shot slot filling. The benchmark is intended to evaluate domain adaptation.
