the battle among the gods in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

A conflict and quarrel among gods of divided counsels during the struggle around Xanthus. It begins when Mars attacks Minerva; Minerva defeats Mars and Venus, Juno defeats Diana, while Neptune and Apollo refrain. Xanthus attacks Achilles but submits after Vulcan’s fire prevails.

BOOK XXI.

The gods contend through their support for opposing sides in the struggle around Xanthus. Xanthus attacks Achilles to save the Trojans, while Juno commands Vulcan to burn the river; Vulcan's fire prevails, and Xanthus submits.
