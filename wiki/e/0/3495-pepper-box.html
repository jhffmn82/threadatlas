pepper-box in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

The pepper-box is a container with a faded label for Powder of Life and a lower bottom that unscrews to reveal a cavity. Tip uses its magical powder on the saw-horse. Later found on a sofa seemingly empty, it yields three silver wishing pills and a folded paper. During the party's flight it disappears, apparently left in the Jackdaws' nest.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Tip Makes an Experiment in Magic

The pepper-box contains the magical Powder of Life. Tip takes it from his pocket and uses it to sprinkle powder on the saw-horse.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The pepper-box is found on the sofa and initially appears empty. The Scarecrow and Tin Woodman discover that it has a false bottom, beneath which are three silver pills and a folded paper.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

The pepper-box contains the silver Wishing Pills carried by the Scarecrow. It disappears during the party's flight, and the Scarecrow believes he left it in the Jackdaws' nest.
