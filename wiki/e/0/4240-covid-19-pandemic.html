COVID-19 in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024

COVID-19 is a case study used to evaluate RARG. Reliable academic research articles related to it define the evidence domain. Across in-domain and cross-domain experiments, RARG outperforms baselines at generating high-quality counter-misinformation responses, while COVID-19 also serves as the reference domain for discussing substantially different test domains.
