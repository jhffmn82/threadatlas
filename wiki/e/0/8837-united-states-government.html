United States government in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

In 1862, during the Civil War, the United States government was heavily occupied and considered unprepared for the Sioux outbreak. It delayed Sioux annuity payments, though its officers promoted farming, supplied tools and seed, and built houses for some Sioux. After events at Sumter, St. Paul citizens pledged it earnest support, urging enlistment and aid for servicemen’s families.

THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

The United States government was heavily occupied with the Civil War and was considered unprepared for the Sioux outbreak. It had delayed annuity payments, while government officers had promoted farming, supplied tools and seed, and built houses for some Sioux.

The People's theater, an old frame building on the corner of Fourth and St.

At a mass meeting after the events at Sumter, St. Paul citizens pledged the American government their earnest support. They called on young men to enroll in military companies and promised financial aid for the dependent families of those who served.
