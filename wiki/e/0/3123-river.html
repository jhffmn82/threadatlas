river in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

River is a broad, swift river reached after the forest, separating the travelers from a beautiful country, the yellow brick road, meadows, flowers, and fruit trees. Its current carries their raft far downstream, away from the road, and leaves the Scarecrow stranded until the Stork rescues him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter VII

The travelers reach a broad river flowing swiftly after leaving the forest. A beautiful country, the yellow brick road, green meadows, flowers, and fruit trees can be seen on the opposite side.

Chapter VIII

The river has a swift current that carries the raft far downstream and away from the road of yellow brick. It leaves the Scarecrow stranded on a pole until the Stork rescues him.
