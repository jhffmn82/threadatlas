sacrifice to Minerva in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

A propitiatory offering to Minerva begins with Nestor's promise after her eagle appearance, then uses a gilded-horned heifer in prayers, offerings, burning, roasting, and eating.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

As he spoke the sun set and it came on dark, whereon Minerva said,

Nestor promises Minerva a gilded-horned heifer after she appears as an eagle. The sacrifice includes prayers, barley meal, burnt thigh bones wrapped in fat, wine offerings, and the roasting and eating of the remaining meat.
