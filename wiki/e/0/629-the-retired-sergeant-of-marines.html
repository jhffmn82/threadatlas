the retired sergeant of Marines in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

A retired Royal Marine Light Infantry sergeant, now a commissionaire, comes to 221B Baker Street with a large blue envelope for Sherlock Holmes. Holmes identifies his former service; he confirms it and explains his civilian employment before delivering the letter.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The retired sergeant of Marines approaches 221B, Baker Street carrying a large blue envelope and looking for the correct number. Sherlock Holmes identifies him as a retired sergeant of Marines, and the man confirms that he was a sergeant in the Royal Marine Light Infantry before explaining that he is currently a commissionaire.
