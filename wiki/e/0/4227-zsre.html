zsRE

dataset · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

zsRE is a zero-shot slot-filling dataset and one of two such tasks standardized by KILT, with 148K train-split instances. It serves evaluation of KGI, which reports a 21.25-point KILT-F1 improvement over previously submitted systems. For retrieval, DPRDNS+RAG achieves table-best 98.60 R-Prec and 99.70 R@5. DPR training takes about two hours on one NVIDIA V100; RAG training on 147,000 instances takes about 15 hours on one NVIDIA P100. KGI finishes top-1 on the KILT leaderboard.

zsRE is in one work on this site, Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

KILT Experiments .. Slot Filling Evaluation

zsRE is one of the two zero-shot slot filling datasets evaluated in KILT. DPR training on zsRE takes about two hours on one NVIDIA V100 GPU, while RAG training on 147,000 zsRE instances takes about 15 hours on one NVIDIA P100 GPU.

Conclusion

zsRE is one of the zero-shot slot filling datasets used to evaluate KGI. KGI ranked at the top-1 position on the KILT leaderboard for this evaluation, with a net KILT-F1 improvement of 21.25 percentage points.
