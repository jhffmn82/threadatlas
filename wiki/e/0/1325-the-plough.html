Hesiod · -0699~

The plough is prepared at home in two forms, one-piece and jointed, so one replaces the other if it breaks; its parts are made from suitable woods including holm-oak.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

(ll. 383-404) When the Pleiades, daughters of Atlas, are rising 1310,

Two ploughs should be prepared at home, one made all of a piece and one jointed, so that one can replace the other if it breaks. Their parts should be made from suitable woods, including laurel, elm, oak, and holm-oak.
