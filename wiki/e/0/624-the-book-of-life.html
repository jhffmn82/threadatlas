CHAPTER II.

The Book of Life is an article in a magazine that argues that an observant person can infer a man’s history and profession from details such as his fingernails, clothing, boots, callosities, expression, and shirt cuffs. Dr. John Watson considers its reasoning exaggerated and impractical, while Sherlock Holmes reveals that he wrote it and relies on its principles.
