land of the Cyclopes

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Cyclopes' island, country, island, the land of the Cyclopes, Κυκλώπων γῇ

Abstract written by a model

In The Odyssey, it is inhabited by lawless and inhuman Cyclopes who live in caves on high mountains, while the surrounding country has wild crops, sheep, goats, and stubble fires. In The Library, Odysseus reaches it with one ship after leaving the other ships at a neighboring island, enters Polyphemus's cave, and later escapes by sea. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is inhabited exclusively by the Cyclopes, who live in caves and keep flocks; Ulysses visits it for supplies and escapes after blinding Polyphemus.
