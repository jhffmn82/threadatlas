Eumelus of Corinth

person · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Eumelus

Abstract written by a model

Eumelus of Corinth is identified as a possible author of the War of the Titans. He says that Aegaeon was the son of Earth and Sea and an ally of the Titans, and he introduces Zeus dancing; the fragments also attribute to him a description of fish on a shield.

Eumelus of Corinth is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
