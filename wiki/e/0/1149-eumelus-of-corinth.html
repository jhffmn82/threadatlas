Eumelus of Corinth

person · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Eumelus
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica: Eumelus of Corinth is identified as a possible author of the War of the Titans. He says that Aegaeon was the son of Earth and Sea and an ally of the Titans, and he introduces Zeus dancing; the fragments also attribute to him a description of fish on a shield.
