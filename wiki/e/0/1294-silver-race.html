Silver Race

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: blessed spirits of the underworld, second generation, silver, silver age, silver race

Abstract written by a model

The Silver Race is a second mortal generation, less noble than the golden race. Some attribute it to Earth, and Hesiod’s Great Works makes it part of Earth’s family. It remains childish for a hundred years, becomes foolish and violent, fails to honor the gods, and Zeus destroys it.

Silver Race is in one work on this site, Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

HESIOD / HESIOD’S WORKS AND DAYS

The silver race is less noble than the golden race and remains childish for a hundred years. Its members become foolish and violent, fail to honor the gods, and are destroyed by Zeus.

THE GREAT WORKS

Some people attribute the Silver Race to the earth. This attribution is based on the claim that Hesiod makes silver part of the family of Earth in the Great Works.
