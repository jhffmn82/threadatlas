RAMKG in Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation with Retriever-Generator Iterative Training

Yifan Gao, Qingyu Yin, Zheng Li, Rui Meng, Tong Zhao, Bing Yin, Irwin King, Michael R. Lyu · 2022

RAMKG is a retrieval-augmented multilingual keyphrase generation framework and method designed to mitigate data shortages in non-English languages. Given a non-English source passage, it uses Cross-Lingual Dense Passage Retrieval to retrieve top relevant English passages and their English keyphrase annotations. It combines the source passage with retrieved English keyphrases as code-mixed input and uses mBART to generate keyphrases in the source language. Rather than only infusing external knowledge into model parameters, it supplies auxiliary knowledge directly to the generation module. RAMKG uses retriever-generator iterative training through RGIT. It outperforms EN Joint Train, EN Pretrain, and a wide range of baselines; on EcommerceMKP it reports average precision, recall, and F1 of 48.59, 44.61, and 44.50, and on AcademicMKP 31.31, 30.82, and 30.49.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The retrieval-augmented method for multilingual keyphrase generation is proposed to mitigate the data shortage problem in non-English languages. Given a non-English passage, it retrieves relevant English passages and uses their associated English keyphrases as external knowledge for generation in the current language.

Related Work

RAMKG is a Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation framework for multilingual keyphrase generation with code-mixed inputs. It retrieves top English passages and their associated keyphrases using cross-lingual retrieval, then supplies them to a generator.

Model .. 4.3

The Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generator combines cross-lingual passage retrieval with multilingual keyphrase generation. It takes a source passage and retrieved English keyphrases as code-mixed input and generates keyphrases in the source language.

Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It is the Retrieval-Augmented Multilingual Keyphrase Generation model. On EcommerceMKP it obtains average precision, recall, and F1 scores of 48.59, 44.61, and 44.50, and on AcademicMKP it obtains 31.31, 30.82, and 30.49.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

RAMKG outperforms the EN Joint Train and EN Pretrain variants when using external large-scale English data. Its retrieval-augmented approach supplies auxiliary knowledge as part of the generator input rather than only infusing knowledge into model parameters.

Conclusion

The framework uses retriever-generator iterative training. The proposed approach outperforms a wide range of baselines.
