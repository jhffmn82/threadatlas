prophecy of Troy’s fall

topic · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Calchas' prophecy, Calchas's prophecy, Troy was destined to be taken in a period of ten years, favourable signs, oracle, prediction, prophecy, prophecy of Troy's capture, spirit of prophecy, the oracles of heaven, the promise, the prophesyings of Calchas, δεκαετεῖ χρόνῳ δεῖν Τροίαν ἁλῶναι

Abstract written by a model

The prophecy of Troy’s fall foretells Troy's fall in the tenth year and is invoked by Ulysses to persuade the Achaeans to remain in The Iliad. The prophecy of Troy's capture foretells Troy's capture after ten years in The Library. Calchas's prophecy interprets the serpent devouring eight birds, foretells Greek conquest and Troy's fall, assigns the war nine years, and requires a virgin's sacrifice for success in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV. Calchas' prophecy foretells the fall of Troy in the tenth year and is invoked by Neleus' son to assure the Argives that they will receive glory and defeat Troy later in The Fall of Troy.
