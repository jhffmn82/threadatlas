Abstract written by a model

Tibullus's death causes grief for Venus, relatives, Nemesis, and Delia, and Elegy IX laments that ruthless Death takes good, pious, poetically excellent people.

ELEGY IX.

Tibullus's death causes profound grief among Venus, his relatives, Nemesis, and Delia. It also prompts the claim that ruthless Death takes both the good and the pious, regardless of worship or poetic excellence.
