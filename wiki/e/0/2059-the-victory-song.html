the victory song in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

A triumph song honors Chromios's chariot-race victory, performed with lyre and flute; it publicly records his good deed and renews the victory at a wine-filled banquet through praise and Charites' favor.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode IX

The victory song is presented as the proper public record of a good deed and as a means of renewing a victory beside the banquet bowl. It praises Chromios's achievement with lyre, flute, voice, wine, and the favor of the Charites.
