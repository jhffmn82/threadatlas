Aristomenes in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Aristomenes, a young wrestler of the Midylidai, follows Theognetos and Kleitomachos. He defeats four competitors to win the Pythian wrestling-match and also wins at Megara, Marathon, and Hera's games in Aigina, becoming his country's youngest glory.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.

Aristomenes wins the wrestling-match at the Pythian games. He has also won at Megara, Marathon, and the games of Hera in Aigina, and is praised as the youngest of his country's glories and as a member of the clan of the Midylidai.
