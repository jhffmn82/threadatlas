Lokros in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Zeus’s son by Protogeneia, Lokros rules Opous; childless, he receives Opöeis’s daughter after Zeus lies with her, and Zeus’s son becomes ruler.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Lokros received the daughter of Opöeis after Zeus had lain with her in Mainalos. Because Lokros was childless, Zeus's son was brought to him, and the child later became a ruler of a city and people.
