Mantle of Immortality in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

The Mantle of Immortality is the world’s only mantle, a unique robe existing since the Beginning and kept in a crypt of gold and platinum between earth and sky. It grants immortality to its recipient. After every immortal votes, Santa Claus wins it through loving deeds toward little children; it is placed upon him, restores his vigor, sinks into his body, and permanently becomes part of him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

OLD AGE / 1. The Mantle of Immortality

The Mantle of Immortality is a unique robe that has existed since the Beginning and is kept in a gleaming crypt between earth and sky. After every immortal votes for its use, it is placed upon Santa Claus, sinks into his body, and becomes a permanent part of his being.

2. When the World Grew Old

The Mantle of Immortality was won by Santa Claus through his loving deeds toward little children. It restores his vigor and means that he need never fear being parted from his dear ones.
