INTRODUCTION

Tradition says that Hesiod was murdered at Oenoe by Amphiphanes and Ganyetor, the sons of Phegeus, after they suspected him of seducing their sister. His body was cast into the sea and was said to have been brought ashore by dolphins, although the surrounding story contains miraculous elements and varies in detail among authorities.

OF THE ORIGIN OF HOMER AND HESIOD, AND OF THEIR CONTEST

Hesiod stayed at Oenoe with Amphiphanes and Ganyetor, whose sister he was suspected of seducing. The young men killed him and cast his body into the sea; dolphins later brought it ashore, and the brothers were eventually destroyed by Zeus according to one account.
