King Awgwa

Abstract written by a model

King Awgwa, leader and king of the Awgwas, is a wicked creature who plots to remove Claus from the children because Claus's toys make them less vulnerable. He orders failed attempts to abduct, imprison, rob, or destroy Claus, then leads an army against the immortals. After threatening Claus, he is cleaved in two by Ak's gleaming ax.

King Awgwa is in one work on this site, The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

6. The Wickedness of the Awgwas

The King Awgwa proposes destroying Claus because Claus's toys have made children less naughty and less vulnerable to the Awgwas. He orders attempts to abduct, imprison, and rob Claus, but each effort to destroy or permanently confine him fails.

7. The Great Battle Between Good and Evil

The King Awgwa rejects Ak's demand to leave Claus unharmed and threatens to kill Claus within three days. He leads a large army against the immortals, but Ak cleaves him in two with his ax.
