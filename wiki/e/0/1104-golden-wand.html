Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Hermes' golden staff was promised by Apollo to bring riches and wealth, protect Hermes, and help accomplish every task involving good words or deeds. In The Library, the golden wand was offered by Apollo to Hermes in exchange for Hermes's shepherd's pipe; Hermes also sought the art of divination.
