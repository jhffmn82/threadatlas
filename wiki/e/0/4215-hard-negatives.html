hard negatives

Also called: dense negative sampling, hard negative, hard negative passages, negatives

Abstract written by a model

Hard negatives are remaining top-ranked Anserini passages excluding overlaps or correct answers, paired with queries and positive passages for additional DPR training; in Dense Negative Sampling, ANN search over a learned dense vector index mines them instead of BM25.

opening

A hard negative is the remaining top-ranked Anserini result after passages overlapping the ground truth or containing a correct answer have been excluded. It is paired with a query and its positive passage in DPR training.

Hard negatives are passages used for additional DPR training. In the DNS setting, they are mined by ANN search over a learned dense vector index instead of by BM25.
