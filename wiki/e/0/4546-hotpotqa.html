HotPotQA in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

HotPotQA is a dataset for diverse, explainable multi-hop question answering, typically requiring two to four reasoning hops and associating each question with 10 Wikipedia documents. It is one of three datasets used to evaluate TRACE and baselines; its original development set has 7,405 questions. Analyses examine reasoning-chain length, candidate triples, generated knowledge graphs, and document selection: its test-set graphs average 83.21 entities, 79.06 triples, and 1.20% density, while chain-selected documents have a 21.06% error rate versus 79.66% for original documents. Performance initially improves as more reasoning chains are used. TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc achieve the highest reported test performance; TRACE-Doc reports 69.20 EM and 78.46 F1 on development, with further Mistral-reader results reported for both variants.
