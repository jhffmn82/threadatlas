Microsoft E5 model family

Abstract written by a model

Microsoft E5 is a free, open-source family of text embedding models trained with a contrastive approach on CCPairs. It supports retrieval, clustering, and classification, including retrieval requiring a single-vector representation. It surpasses BM25 in zero-shot BEIR retrieval and consistently outperforms other evaluated models across retrieval-hit levels, with superior Recall@k at all evaluated k values. E5-ML-Large is the top performer, while E5-small has the strongest combined model score. Its open-source nature broadens its appeal for applications.

Microsoft E5 model family is in one work on this site, Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
