BOOK XI

Anticlea, Ulysses’s mother, appears among the dead and speaks with him after drinking the sacrificial blood. She says that Penelope remains in Ulysses’s house, Telemachus holds the estate, and Ulysses’s father lives miserably in the country; she says that longing for Ulysses and affection for him caused her death.
