The Elders are a polygamous Mormon leadership group wielding religious and political authority over the settlement. Powerful and feared, they maintain harems and families, endorse Stangerson's and Drebber's sons for Lucy, and confront John Ferrier after he refuses the proposed marriages.

CHAPTER III.

The Elders are described as powerful Mormon leaders with harems and children to provide for. Brigham Young says that Stangerson's and Drebber's sons are suitable prospective husbands for Lucy.

CHAPTER IV.

The Elders are the authorities whose power John Ferrier has defied by refusing the proposed marriages. Their authority is feared throughout the settlement, and Ferrier believes that even wealth and position cannot protect him from them.
