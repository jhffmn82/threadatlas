Hymnoi (Hymns)

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Hymnoi, Hymns, choice hymns, hymns

Abstract written by a model

Hymnoi are choice hymns, songs sung by a choir to honour gods; they sound for bright-filleted Semele when spring flowers appear at Argive Nemea.
