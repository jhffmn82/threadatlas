Abstract written by a model

Gump’s flight unintentionally leaves Land of Oz, carrying travelers south in sofas; after rising slowly and speeding through the night, it passes South Country unseen.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The Gump flies south with the travelers in its sofa-like body, initially rising slowly and then traveling swiftly through the night. Because it is inexperienced and the travelers cannot see their surroundings, the flight carries them past the intended destination.
