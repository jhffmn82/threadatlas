Telphusa is a sweet-flowing sacred spring who deceives Apollo to preserve her renown; he discovers this, covers her streams with rocks, and establishes an altar nearby.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TO PYTHIAN APOLLO—

Telphusa persuades Apollo to build his temple at Crisa rather than beside her spring because she wants to retain renown for herself. When Apollo discovers her deception, he covers her streams with rocks and establishes an altar nearby.
