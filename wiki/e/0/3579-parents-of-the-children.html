parents (of the children) in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Parents are Santa Claus’s deputies: at first, some build houses that prevent Santa Claus from entering through fireplaces and cannot understand how he entered. Later they anxiously prepare Christmas trees or hang stockings for his visit; loving parents fill them after he leaves.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3. The Deputies of Santa Claus

At first, some parents build houses that prevent Santa Claus from entering through fireplaces. Later, parents become anxious for Santa Claus to visit and are asked to prepare Christmas trees or hang up stockings; loving parents may fill them after Santa Claus leaves.
