Scaean gates

city gate · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Dardanian gates, Scaean Gate, Scaean Gates, Scaean gate, the Scaean gates, the gates, the gates of Ilius, the lofty gates of Troy, the strong gates

Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, the Scaean gates shelter fleeing Trojans, are closed by Priam against Achilles, and remain where Hector faces Achilles and foretells Paris and Apollo will kill him. In The Fall of Troy, the Scaean Gates are the place before which Achilles is destined to die by Apollo's shafts, fulfilling the prophecy near Troy's walls.

Abstract written by a model

Scaean gates open to shelter fleeing Trojans, then Priam closes them against Achilles; Hector remains there before fleeing Achilles and foretells Paris and Apollo will kill Achilles there.

Xanthus was now beaten, so these two left off fighting, for Juno

The gates of Ilius are opened so the fleeing Trojans can enter and find refuge. Priam orders them closed once the people are inside because he fears Achilles may enter with them.

BOOK XXII.

Hector remains near the Scaean gates before fleeing Achilles. Hector predicts that Paris and Apollo will eventually kill Achilles at these gates.
