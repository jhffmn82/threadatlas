Syracuse's foundation in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Syracuse’s foundation unfolds as Archias plants a Corinthian colony, joined by first colonists and an ancestor of Agesias, linking Agesias to Syracuse.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI.

Syracuse is described as having been jointly founded by an ancestor of Agesias who came with Archias and the first colonists. This ancestral connection contributes to Agesias’s praise as a Syracusan.
