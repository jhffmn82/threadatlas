Post-Homeric and pre-academic epic poetry largely survives in this volume; after Homer it became conventional, imitating Homeric style for romantic legends or seeking new subjects despite inescapable Homeric influence.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

PREFACE

Practically all that remains of post-Homeric and pre-academic epic poetry is contained in this volume.

INTRODUCTION

Post-Homeric epic generally became conventional because Homer had exhausted the epic genre. Its poets either imitated Homeric style in treating romantic legends or sought new subjects, although complete freedom from Homeric influence was considered illusory.
