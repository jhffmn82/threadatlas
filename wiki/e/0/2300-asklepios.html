Asklepios, child of Apollo, was trained by Cheiron as a healer, curing bodily ailments with gentle remedies, spells, charms, and surgery until restoring a dead man brought Zeus's lightning.

III.

Asklepios was trained by Cheiron as a healer and cured many bodily ailments through spells, potions, charms, and surgery. He was tempted by a great reward to restore a dead man to life, after which Zeus struck him down with lightning.
