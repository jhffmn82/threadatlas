T5

language model · 5 works · 13 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: RAP-T5, T5 decoder, T5 encoder, T5 model, T5 off-shelf, Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer, Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer (T5)

Abstract written by a model

In “Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge,” T5 is described as a large pre-trained language model and encoder-decoder framework used for text generation; as a decoder in a reader model, it applies full attention after retrieved text is concatenated with the original input. In “Enhancing Distractor Generation for Multiple-Choice Questions with Retrieval Augmented Pretraining and Knowledge Graph Integration,” T5 is described as a pre-trained language model used for Text2Text distractor generation; evaluation finds that RAP improves its baseline, KAG is strong, but their combination inconsistently adds benefit. In “RE-RAG: Improving Open-Domain QA Performance and Interpretability with Relevance Estimator in Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” T5 is described as a generative model used by the relevance estimator and generator in open-domain QA research. In “REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation,” T5 is described as an encoder-decoder model that encodes questions with passages and selected relevant triples, initializes entity and relation embeddings, generates answers from concatenated passage and triple representations, provides Fusion-in-Decoder’s architecture, and is used to verify REANO’s effectiveness. In “Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach: Document Question Answering using Large Language Model,” Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer (T5) is described as an 11-billion-parameter model introduced by Colin Raffel that advanced question answering, reporting 63.3% exact match and 94.1% F1; later recall reached 34.6%.
