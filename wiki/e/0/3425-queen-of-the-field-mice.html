Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, the Queen of all the Field Mice rules thousands of field mice, orders them to rescue the Cowardly Lion after the Tin Woodman saves her from a Wildcat, directs Dorothy toward the Emerald City, explains the Golden Cap, and leaves with the mice to avoid the Winged Monkeys. In The Marvelous Land of Oz, the Queen of the Field Mice rules the Field Mice, provides Scarecrow with twelve subjects, guides travelers safely through Mombi's illusions toward the Emerald City, and departs at its gates.

Queen of all the Field Mice in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04 · the work’s page · read it where Queen of all the Field Mice first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Queen of all the Field Mice rules thousands of field mice. After the Tin Woodman saves her from a Wildcat, she orders her subjects to rescue the Cowardly Lion and offers future help. Later, answering Dorothy's whistle, she directs Dorothy toward the Emerald City, explains the Golden Cap, and leaves with the mice to avoid the Winged Monkeys.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter IX

The Queen of all the Field Mice is chased by a Wildcat until the Tin Woodman saves her. She orders thousands of field mice to help rescue the Cowardly Lion and promises to come to the group's assistance if they call for her again.

Chapter XIV

The Queen of the Mice responds to Dorothy's whistle and tells her that the Emerald City is behind them. She explains that the Golden Cap can summon the Winged Monkeys and leaves with the other mice because the Monkeys like to plague them.
