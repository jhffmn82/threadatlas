riches and wealth

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Riches, gain, profit, trade and profit, wealth

Abstract written by a model

Riches and wealth provide life to poor mortals and follow the blessed man. Hecate bestows them on those whose prayers she favorably receives. In sea-trading, greater cargo can increase wealth if winds prevent harmful gales, but carrying too much endangers it through disaster at sea; one should not risk all goods.

(ll. 618-640) But if desire for uncomfortable sea-faring seize you;

If Perses turns to trading to escape debt and hunger, the speaker advises placing freight in a large ship because greater lading can bring greater profit if the winds remain favorable. He also warns against risking all one's goods at sea.

(ll. 404-452) Again, Phoebe came to the desired embrace of Coeus.

Hecate bestows wealth upon those whose prayers she receives favourably. Her power is said to be responsible for this gift.
