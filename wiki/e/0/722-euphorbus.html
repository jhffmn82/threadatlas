Euphorbus

Abstract written by a model

Euphorbus is a Dardanian Trojan warrior, son of Panthous and famed as a spearman, horseman, and runner. He wounds Patroclus from behind, withdraws, then claims the first blow and challenges Menelaus. Menelaus kills him through the throat and strips his armour.

Euphorbus is in one work on this site, The Iliad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Meanwhile Patroclus, with many a shout to his horses and to

Euphorbus, a Dardanian and a highly skilled spearman, horseman, and runner, strikes the wounded Patroclus in the back. He withdraws after pulling his spear from the wound rather than facing Patroclus directly.

BOOK XVII.

Euphorbus claims the glory of having first struck Patroclus and challenges Menelaus. Menelaus kills him with a spear through the throat and strips him of his armour.
