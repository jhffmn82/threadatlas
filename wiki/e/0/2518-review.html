product reviews (Amazon Books) in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

Jihyeok Kim · 2020-12-08/2020-12-13

Amazon Books product reviews are token sequences and review-generation outputs that explicitly articulate users’ opinions. Each is aligned with user, product, and rating attributes. Reviews supply overall textual content, ground-truth text and lexical-similarity signals for training, and candidate references or inputs for reference encoding. Training uses gold reviews as GEN targets and cross-entropy pretraining; during inference, the gold review is not required.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Product reviews explicitly convey some users’ opinions and can serve as reference material for generating new reviews.

opening .. Augmenting context using references

Product reviews from Amazon Books are used to test AT2T, SL, and RL. They are aligned with corresponding user, product, and rating attributes.

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

A review is the output corresponding to the attributes and is represented as a sequence of tokens. Reviews from the training dataset are also used as candidate references.

Relevance Pseudo-Supervision .. Fine-Grained REFLECT

Reviews provide the ground-truth text used to compute lexical similarity during training and serve as textual inputs for fine-grained reference encoding. The gold review is not required during inference.

GEN .. Training GEN

Gold reviews are used as the training targets for GEN. GEN is pretrained with cross-entropy loss on gold reviews.
