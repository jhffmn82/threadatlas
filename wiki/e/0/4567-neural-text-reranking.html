Neural Text Reranking

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: re-ranking strategies, reranking

Abstract written by a model

In "Lift Yourself Up: Retrieval-augmented Text Generation with Self-Memory," Neural Text Reranking is described as a second-stage process that selects or improves generated text candidates using generative or discriminative approaches; the framework extends it by using selected candidates as memory for subsequent retrieval-augmented generation rather than as the final output. In "Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation," re-ranking strategies are described as contemporary RAG methodologies requiring exploration of linguistic characteristics from a language-specific perspective.
