transformation of the sailors

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: What miracle is this, a change, the transformation of the pirates into dolphins, the whole crew became dolphins, were changed into dolphins

Abstract written by a model

In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, the transformation of the sailors is the event in which pirates who leap overboard to escape Dionysus's lion and a miserable fate are changed into dolphins. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the transformation of the sailors is the event in which sailors who try to enslave Bacchus become dolphins while Acetes remains human.
