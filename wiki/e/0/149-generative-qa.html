Related Work

Generative QA generates answers through seq2seq learning instead of extracting answer spans, with recent studies focusing on improving the reading stage while reusing DPR as the retriever. GAR is orthogonal to generative QA because it generates pertinent contexts for retrieval rather than perfect answers, and it extracts factual knowledge from PLMs beyond answers alone.

Generative QA in Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding

Wenyu Huang, Mirella Lapata, Pavlos Vougiouklis, Nikos Papasarantopoulos, Jeff Z. Pan · 2023 · the work’s page · read it where Generative QA first appears

Abstract written by a model

Generative QA is a knowledge-intensive generation, or KIG, task requiring retrieval of appropriate external passages, with generation quality heavily dependent on that context. It is one of two KIG tasks used to evaluate proposed methods, on the NLGen split of MSMARCO QA with generation and retrieval metrics. Evaluation covers retrieval, end-to-end generation, robustness, and answer quality; better retrieval generally improves generation and reduces hallucination, while focus on label-relevant passages can overfit, mitigated by the training framework.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Retrieval Augmented Generation with Rich Answer Encoding / Abstract

Generative question answering is identified as a knowledge-intensive generation task that requires retrieving appropriate passages from external knowledge sources. Its generation quality relies heavily on the retrieved passages used as contextual information.

Experimental Setup / Datasets .. Comparison Models

It is one of the two KIG tasks used to evaluate the proposed methods. It is evaluated on the NLGen split of the MSMARCO QA dataset using generation and retrieval metrics.

Results / RQ1: Effectiveness / Generative QA .. Case Studies

Generative QA is evaluated for retrieval effectiveness, end-to-end generation, robustness, and answer quality in case studies. The results indicate that better retrieval quality generally benefits end-to-end generation and helps reduce hallucination.

Conclusion

Knowledge-intensive generation is the task context in which focusing on label-relevant passages can result in overfitting. The proposed training framework considerably mitigates this overfitting.
