Previous-window approaches in Active Retrieval Augmented Generation

Zhengbao Jiang · 2023

Previous-window approaches are a baseline category for retrieval during generation. They trigger retrieval every l tokens, querying with generated tokens from the previous window; here l=16, using a sentence’s first half to retrieve for its second half. They underperform FLARE and, on ASQA, single-time retrieval; queries over 32 past tokens hurt performance.

Previous-window approaches trigger retrieval every l tokens, using the generated tokens from the previous window as the query. The section follows Ram et al. (2023) in using a window size of l = 16.

6 .. 6.2

Previous-window retrieval uses prior context to retrieve information during generation. It performs below FLARE on the reported datasets, and using more than 32 past tokens as a query hurts performance; on ASQA, it also underperforms single-time retrieval.
