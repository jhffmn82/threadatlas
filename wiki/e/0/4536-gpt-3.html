GPT-3 in Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge

Wenhao Yu · 2022

GPT-3 is presented as an example of a large-scale pre-trained language model and as a counterpart to RAG models. Compared with GPT-3, RAG models are described as having explicit, scalable knowledge acquisition and as easing generation by relying on retrieved references rather than generating from scratch.
