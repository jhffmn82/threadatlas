the famine

Also called: cruel famine, most dreadful and cruel year
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica: threatens mankind [with destruction] The famine is caused by Demeter’s grief and anger over Persephone’s absence. It threatens to destroy mankind and to end the gifts and sacrifices owed to the gods, but it ends when Persephone’s return is arranged.
