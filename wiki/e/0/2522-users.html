Users are review-specific entities identified by User ID. They write product reviews expressing opinions about purchased products; opinions on products they have not bought are generally unavailable. Their reviews can serve as references for the same or other users reviewing the same product.

Abstract

User ID is one of the attribute identifiers provided for review generation. It is also used to label texts from which references can be selected.

Introduction

Users write product reviews that explicitly articulate some of their opinions, while their opinions about products they have not bought are generally unavailable.

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

The user is one of the review-specific attributes. References may include reviews written by the same user or by another user for the same product.
