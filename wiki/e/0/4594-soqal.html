SOQAL is an open-domain Arabic question-answering system built to address the scarcity of labeled Arabic QA datasets. It includes a hierarchical TF-IDF component and a neural reading-comprehension component based on the pre-trained BERT transformer, achieving a 27.6 F1 score in experiments on the Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset.
