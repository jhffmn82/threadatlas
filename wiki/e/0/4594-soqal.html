Abstract written by a model

SOQAL is an open-domain Arabic question-answering system addressing scarce labeled Arabic QA datasets. It combines a hierarchical TF-IDF component with a pre-trained BERT-based neural reading-comprehension component, achieving 27.6 F1 in experiments on the Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset.
