Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FRAGMENTS OF THE EPIC CYCLE / THE WAR OF THE TITANS

The War of the Titans contains traditions about Heaven, Earth, Aegaeon, the Titans, Zeus, Cronos, Cheiron, Heracles, and the apples of the Hesperides. It is attributed either to Eumelus of Corinth or to Arctinus, and it is identified as the first source for the story of Heracles sailing across the sea in a cauldron.
