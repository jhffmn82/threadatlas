Abstract written by a model

Ulysses’s twenty-day voyage from Ogygia begins when Calypso sends him away on a provisioned raft with clothing and a favorable wind. Near Scheria, after sighting the Phaeacian coast, Neptune raises a storm that destroys the raft. Ulysses swims ashore, reaches a river, and sleeps among leaves until Nausicaa finds him.

BOOK V

The voyage to Scheria begins after Calypso sends Ulysses away with provisions and a favorable wind. He sails for eighteen days and sees the Phaeacian mountains before Neptune’s storm disrupts the journey.

BOOK VII

Ulysses says that after seven years with Calypso on Ogygia, he was sent away on a raft with provisions, clothing, and a favorable wind. After sailing for seventeen days and sighting the Phaeacian coast, he was attacked by a storm raised by Neptune, lost the raft, swam to shore, and reached a river where he slept among leaves until Nausicaa found him.
