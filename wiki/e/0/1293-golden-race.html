Abstract written by a model

The golden race lives under Cronos without sorrow, toil, grief, or old age, receives effortless food, and after death becomes kindly guardians who give wealth.

The golden race lives during Cronos's reign like gods, free from sorrow, toil, grief, and old age. After death, its members become kindly spirits who guard mortal men and give wealth.
