Scotland Yard

organization · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Scotland Yard officials, the Police Station, the Scotland Yarders
  • In A Study in Scarlet, Scotland Yard is represented by Lestrade and Gregson, is publicly credited with Hope’s capture, and is mocked by Holmes for its failed pursuit, as he disputes the credit.
  • In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Scotland Yard receives an alarm, investigates a theft, searches Mrs. Tangey, employs Mr. Forbes, whom Holmes questions about an unresolved case, and believes three assailants sought revenge despite presumed loss at sea.

Scotland Yard in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Police organization represented by Lestrade and Gregson, publicly credited with Hope’s capture, though Holmes mocks their failed pursuit and disputes the credit.

CHAPTER VII.

Scotland Yard is represented in the newspaper report by Inspectors Lestrade and Gregson, who are credited with Hope’s capture. Holmes regards that credit as undeserved.
