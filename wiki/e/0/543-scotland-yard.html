Also called: Scotland Yard officials, the Police Station, the Scotland Yarders

Abstract written by a model

In A Study in Scarlet, Scotland Yard is represented by Lestrade and Gregson, is publicly credited with Hope’s capture, and is mocked by Holmes for its failed pursuit, as he disputes the credit. In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, Scotland Yard receives an alarm, investigates a theft, searches Mrs. Tangey, employs Mr. Forbes, whom Holmes questions about an unresolved case, and believes three assailants sought revenge despite presumed loss at sea.
