This edition orders complete and fragmentary poems by their probable original appearance, with study grounded in papyri, manuscripts, editions, fragments, translations, and literary-historical work.

PREFACE

The Hesiodic corpus is arranged in the edition according to the order in which its complete and fragmentary poems would probably have appeared if it had survived intact.

The Hesiodic Poems

The Hesiodic Poems fall into didactic and genealogical groups. Their surviving works include the Works and Days, the Theogony, the Catalogues of Women, the Eoiae, the Shield of Heracles, and several other fragmentary or disputed poems.

BIBLIOGRAPHY

The Hesiodic poems are discussed through their papyrus and medieval manuscript evidence, modern editions, fragments, translations, and literary-historical studies. Recent work on them is reviewed by Rudolf Rzach in Bursian’s Jahresberichte.
