Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, sexual pleasure is described as something that man and woman both enjoy and jointly produce, prompting the question of why one person should sell it and the other should have to buy it. In The Amores, or Amours, Delights of Love is described as a joy that delights both woman and man, provides vigour to the speaker's body, and prompts the question of why one should sell and the other buy a pleasure for which both combine with kindred impulse.
