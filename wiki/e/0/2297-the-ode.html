Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

III.

The ode is addressed to Hieron of Syracuse and celebrates Pherenikos's Pythian victory. It is concerned with Hieron's illness, the limits of mortal life, and the power of song to preserve virtue and fame.
