throne room in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

A glittering throne room holds General Jinjur on the Scarecrow's throne with crown and sceptre; rebels capture the party there, flee from mice, and the party later convenes a council of war.

The Prisoners of the Queen

The throne room contains General Jinjur seated on the Scarecrow's throne with his crown and the royal sceptre. The Army of Revolt captures the Scarecrow's party there, but the room is deserted when the mice send the rebels fleeing, and the party later uses it for a council of war.
