multi-hop reasoning

topic · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Multi-hop Complex Reasoning, latent multi-hop reasoning, multi-hop reasoning abilities, multi-hop relationships, multihop reasoning, multihop reasoning ability

Abstract written by a model

In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, multi-hop reasoning is presented as a task with complex information needs that one-time retrieval may not satisfy and that requires gathering and processing knowledge from multiple sources. In A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models, multi-hop reasoning is described as an LLM ability to follow connected relationships across multiple nodes and as a capability improved by graph-based retrieval and graph-enhanced Chain-of-Thought. In OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models, multi-hop reasoning is described as commonly required for specialized-domain tasks and as the focus of up to 100 generated questions in the query-answering evaluation. In SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation, multi-hop reasoning is described as required by complex question-answering questions that need multiple supporting knowledge sources, but not by simple questions.

multi-hop reasoning in A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models

Qinggang Zhang, Shengyuan Chen, Yuanchen Bei, Zheng Yuan, Huachi Zhou, Zijin Hong, Hao Chen, Yilin Xiao, Chuang Zhou, Junnan Dong, Yi Chang, Xiao Huang · 2025-09-29 · the work’s page · read it where multi-hop reasoning first appears

Abstract written by a model

Multi-hop reasoning is an ability in LLM performance that follows connected relationships across multiple nodes, requiring subgraphs, intermediate steps, relational information, and effective prompt structure. In GraphRAG, graph-based retrieval navigates entity-relation paths while preserving context, unlike traditional RAG’s locally similar chunks. Its performance on complex questions can be improved through multi-round integration and graph-enhanced Chain-of-Thought.

III. WHAT IS GRAPHRAG / A. Traditional RAG Pipeline .. D. Traditional RAG vs GraphRAG

Multi-hop reasoning follows several connected relationships to answer a query. GraphRAG supports it by navigating paths across entities and relations, whereas traditional RAG may retrieve locally similar chunks that lack sufficient connected context.

Multi-hop reasoning often requires intermediate reasoning steps and relational information. Multi-round integration and graph-enhanced Chain-of-Thought are used to improve performance on such complex questions.
