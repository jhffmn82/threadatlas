Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

X.

The Hyperborean folk are described as a blessed and hallowed people who live without pestilence, wasting old age, toil, or war. They feast and sing for Apollo, perform dances, play pipes, and crown themselves with golden bay leaves; Perseus once entered their homes while they sacrificed asses to Apollo.
