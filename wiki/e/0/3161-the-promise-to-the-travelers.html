Also called: Oz's promise, his promise, his promises, our promise, the gift Oz had promised to bestow

Abstract written by a model

The promise to the travelers is made by Oz: Dorothy is promised return to Kansas, the Scarecrow brains, the Tin Woodman a heart, and the Cowardly Lion courage. After destroying the Wicked Witch of the West, they return to claim it; Oz delays, then agrees to fulfill the requests and seek Dorothy’s return.

the promise to the travelers is in one work on this site, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter XIII

Dorothy and her friends decide to return to Oz and claim his promise. The Tin Woodman expects to receive a heart, the Scarecrow expects brains, the Cowardly Lion expects courage, and Dorothy hopes to return to Kansas.

Chapter XV

The travelers return to claim Oz's promises after destroying the Wicked Witch of the West. Oz initially tries to delay them, then agrees to provide brains, a heart, and courage and to seek a way to send Dorothy to Kansas.
