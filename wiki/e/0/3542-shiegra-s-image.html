Shiegra's image in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

Shiegra's image is a toy depicting Shiegra, with fierce teeth and glaring eyes. Claus offers it to Mayrie, frightens both children, then locks it away and rejects fierce toy models.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

4. How Little Mayrie Became Frightened

Claus takes the image of Shiegra from his cupboard and offers it to Mayrie as a toy. Its fierce teeth and glaring eyes frighten both children, so Claus locks it back in the cupboard and decides not to use fierce creatures as toy models.
