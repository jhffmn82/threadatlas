multi-task

Also called: multi-task model, multi-task setting model, ours (multi-task)

Abstract written by a model

This proposed multi-task method is evaluated as a variant on MSMARCO and KILT-WoW. It performs best overall on MSMARCO, with the highest retrieval recalls and 55.1 BLEU-1, 34.8 BLEU-4, 59.1 ROUGE-L, and 50.5 METEOR. It reports 12.0 Recall@1 on MSMARCO development and 19.04 end-to-end F1 on KILT-WoW, improves Recall@1 over non-finetuned DPR, but misses the gold passage in the Gouda cheese case.

Experimental Setup / Datasets .. Comparison Models

It is one of the proposed-method variants evaluated on both datasets. It performs best overall on MSMARCO, obtaining 55.1 BLEU-1, 34.8 BLEU-4, 59.1 ROUGE-L, 50.5 METEOR, and the highest retrieval recalls.

Results / RQ1: Effectiveness / Generative QA .. Case Studies

The multi-task model achieves the strongest reported retrieval improvement on MSMARCO, with about 14.3% relative improvement over non-finetuned DPR on Recall@1, and improves BLEU-4 by 4.5% relative to the baseline. In the Gouda cheese example, it fails to retrieve the gold passage but still gives an answer involving cow’s milk rather than the correct recorded-history fact.
