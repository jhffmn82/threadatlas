Demeter’s search for Persephone

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Demeter’s quest, seeking her child, wandered over the earth, went about seeking her
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Demeter searched for Persephone across the earth and sea for nine days, carrying flaming torches and asking gods and mortals for news; on the tenth day, Hecate directed her to Helios, who told her what happened.
  • In The Library, Demeter searched for Persephone throughout the earth with torches by night and day, and her search led her to Hermion and then to Eleusis.
