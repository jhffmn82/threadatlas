Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset

Abstract written by a model

Arabic Reading Comprehension Dataset (ARCD) is a publicly available dataset and evaluation benchmark for open-domain Arabic question answering and testing Arabic-capable or multilingual semantic embedding models. It uses Arabic Wikipedia as its knowledge source and was generated from crowd-worker questions on Wikipedia articles and machine translation of the Stanford Question Answering Dataset. ARCD contains 1,395 crowdsourced questions, each with a context paragraph and ground-truth answer, drawn from 465 paragraphs extracted from 155 randomly selected Arabic Wikipedia articles among the top 1,000 most viewed in 2018. These articles cover subjects including religious and historical figures, sports celebrities, countries, and companies. Evaluation compares models with average Recall@k; a BERT-based reader achieved 61.3 F1. Its knowledge corpus is formed by concatenating the question contexts.
