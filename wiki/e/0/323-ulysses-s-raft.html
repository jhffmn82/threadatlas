Ulysses's raft

Abstract written by a model

Ulysses builds a seaworthy timber raft with steering and sail, sent by Calypso; after departure, Neptune’s storm destroys it, leaving Ulysses briefly using a plank before swimming away.

Ulysses's raft is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

BOOK V

Ulysses builds the raft in four days from twenty trees, adding a deck, gunwale, mast, sail, rudder, and wicker protection. Neptune’s storm breaks it apart, and Ulysses uses one of its planks briefly before swimming away.
