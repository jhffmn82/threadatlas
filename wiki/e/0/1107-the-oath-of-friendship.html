the oath of friendship

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: promised, swore to be fellow and friend to Hermes, vowing that he would love no other

Abstract written by a model

The oath of friendship is sworn between Hermes and Apollo, confirmed by Zeus with an eagle. Hermes promises not to steal from or approach Apollo’s house; Apollo vows unmatched friendship, love, honor, gifts, and privileges for Hermes.

Hermes promises never to steal anything belonging to Apollo or approach his strong house. Apollo swears to be Hermes’ fellow and friend, to love no other among the immortals, gods, or men sprung from Zeus better than Hermes, and to honor him with gifts and privileges.
