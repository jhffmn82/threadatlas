Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, the chariot race begins when the praetor releases four-horse teams from equally placed starting barriers; the poet supports the woman’s favored charioteer, urges a restart when he loses ground, and sees him win after the second start, while his own desired prize remains to be won. In The Amores, or Amours, the chariot race begins with a procession at Circus Maximus, starts from a starting barrier, pauses for a restart, and ends in a final contest in which the mistress’s favored competitor wins the palm of victory.
