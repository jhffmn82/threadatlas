Abstract written by a model

In Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling, an entity is located in occurrences throughout a corpus, and information about its slot fillers is gathered from its context. In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation, entities are identified in passages using SpaCy for named entities and TAGME for Wikipedia entities, with embeddings initialized from contextualized mentions and updated by a graph neural network. In Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, entities are extracted from chunks to form subgraphs or connect chunks during graph-guided expansion, serving as graph nodes for context organization.
