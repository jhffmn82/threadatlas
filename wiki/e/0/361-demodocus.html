Abstract written by a model

Demodocus is a blind, Muse-beloved bard, inspired by heaven and favored and honored by others. At a banquet he is seated, fed, given wine and his lyre, and sings of Ulysses and Achilles’ quarrel. During athletic games he sings of Mars, Venus, and Vulcan. Later, after receiving roast pork from Ulysses, he accurately sings of the wooden horse, Troy’s sack, and Ulysses’s fighting. His song moves a guest to groan and lament, so he is asked to stop.

Demodocus is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
