Abstract written by a model

The suitors' feast is a meal in Ulysses' house, made from his household's oxen and other provisions. They sacrifice sheep, goats, pigs, and a heifer, prepare and distribute meat, bread, and wine, and eat and drink abundantly. Servants bring portions, and Ulysses receives bread, meat, and eventually a share equal to the others. A bard's music accompanies the banquet; after eating, the suitors demand singing and dancing. Insults and ominous signs attend the feast, with an expectation of a coming deadly meal. By evening, they return to their separate homes.
