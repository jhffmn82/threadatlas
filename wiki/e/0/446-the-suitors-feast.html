The suitors' feast is a meal in Ulysses' house, made from his household's oxen and other provisions. They sacrifice sheep, goats, pigs, and a heifer, prepare and distribute meat, bread, and wine, and eat and drink abundantly. During the banquet, the suitors eat the household's provisions while a bard sings. They give Ulysses bread and meat when he begs, and afterward they continue with singing and dancing as evening approaches.

The suitors sacrifice sheep, goats, pigs, and a heifer, distribute the cooked meat, mix wine, and eat abundantly. Ulysses receives a portion equal to the others, but the feast is accompanied by insults, ominous signs, and the expectation of a coming deadly meal.
