Euryalus in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Euryalus, Mecisteus's champion-boxer son, alone challenges Epeus, is knocked down, and is led away by comrades bleeding, disoriented, and unconscious.

Thereon the son of Peleus, when he had listened to all the thanks

Euryalus is the only man to rise against Epeus in the boxing contest. Epeus knocks him down, and his comrades lead him away unsteady and unconscious after he suffers bleeding and disorientation.
