Abstract written by a model

In Heroides and Amores, perjury occurs when the faithless girl breaks an oath sworn by her own eyes and by the speaker’s eyes; it remains unpunished while the speaker suffers instead. In The Amores, or Amours, perjury consists of the mistress breaking oaths sworn to the Gods and to the speaker; her perjuries go unpunished while others suffer for their offenses.
