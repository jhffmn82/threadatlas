Abstract written by a model

In The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, Oz is the supposed Great Wizard and ruler of Emerald City who is revealed as a powerless former balloonist and humbug. In The Marvelous Land of Oz, the Wonderful Wizard is the former ruler and builder of Emerald City who gave Scarecrow brains, displaced Pastoria, and hid Ozma. In Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, the Wizard is a former ruler of Oz and humbug who becomes a resourceful companion to Dorothy and the others before serving as Ozma's Official Wizard. In The Road to Oz, Wizard of Oz entertains Ozma's guests with trained piglets and serves as her Master of Ceremonies. In The Emerald City of Oz, Wizard is a former humbug and ruler who learns real sorcery from Glinda and uses it to aid Dorothy's party. In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the ferryman is the Wizard of Oz in disguise, pretending to carry travelers across the black-water lake by a spoken word. In Little Wizard Stories of Oz, the Wizard of Oz serves Ozma, rescues Jack Pumpkinhead and the children, restores the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman, and teaches Dorothy a lesson in disguise. In The Patchwork Girl of Oz, the Wizard of Oz assists Glinda, advises Ojo's search, removes Dr. Pipt's magical power, and helps restore Margolotte and Unc Nunkie.

In Tik-Tok of Oz, the Wizard of Oz invents a wireless telephone, observes adventurers with Ozma, and transports travelers and companions between places. In Rinkitink in Oz, Wizard of Oz travels with Dorothy, recognizes Bilbil's true identity, and helps Glinda restore him. In The Lost Princess of Oz, Wizard of Oz is a principal leader and problem-solver in the search for the missing Ozma, using magic and reasoning despite losing his tools. In The Magic of Oz, Wizard of Oz is Dorothy's magical companion who uses his tools and powers to rescue Trot and Cap'n Bill and defeat Kiki Aru and Ruggedo. In Glinda of Oz, the Wizard of Oz serves as Glinda's pupil and adviser and leads the expedition to rescue Ozma and investigate the Skeezers' dome. In The Royal Book of Oz, Wizard of Oz joins Ozma's journey to settle a war, helps investigate suspended animals, and makes Happy Toko's distant singing audible. In Kabumpo in Oz, the Wizard of Oz reassures frightened travelers during a disturbance and later disappears with Ozma and other important people. In The Cowardly Lion of Oz, Wizard of Oz joins Glinda in trying to free and restore the enchanted Cowardly Lion, ultimately reuniting him through Ozma's magic belt. In Grampa in Oz: Oz’s Wizard, one of only two permitted magic practitioners, lives in Princess Ozma’s Emerald City palace; Grampa and Prince Tatters seek his help recovering Fumbo’s head. In The Lost King of Oz, Wizard of Oz is Ozma's counselor who investigates the lost king and repeatedly tests magic to restore him and Pajuka. In The Gnome King of Oz, Wizard of Oz protects Ozma's magical treasures, defeats Ruggedo's threat, and secures dangerous magical objects. In The Giant Horse of Oz, the Wizard of Oz is the former Royal Wizard and ruler who uses magic to aid his companions, defeat Quiberon, and investigate Cheeriobed's fate. In The Yellow Knight of Oz, the little Wizard of Oz prepares magical appliances and develops a powerful searchlight to locate missing people and objects.

Oz in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04 · the work’s page · read it where Oz first appears

Abstract written by a model

Oz is the Great Wizard and ruler of the Emerald City, believed to be powerful, wise, good, and more powerful than the witches. Travelers seek him because Dorothy wants to return to Kansas, the Scarecrow wants brains, the Tin Woodman wants a heart, and the Lion wants courage. He receives them separately through frightening false forms, a Head, Lady, Beast, and Ball of Fire, and requires the death of the Wicked Witch of the West before helping them. After their return, he is revealed as a little old man, a former balloonist from Omaha, with no magical powers. A self-described humbug, he has ruled by hiding behind a screen, using ventriloquism, disguises, and the labor of the people who built the city and palace. Though he fears the Witch because she has real magic, he keeps his secret and tries to fulfill the companions' wishes through imagination and substitutes: stuffed brains for the Scarecrow, a silk-and-sawdust heart for the Tin Woodman, and a drink for the Lion. He cannot send Dorothy home, so he builds a balloon to cross the desert. It launches before Dorothy can board, carrying Oz away and leaving the others behind. Despite failing Dorothy, he is remembered as having done his best: a bad Wizard but a good man.

the Wizard in Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1908 · the work’s page · read it where the Wizard first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Wizard is a little, bald man and self-acknowledged humbug who calls himself a wizard. Born in Omaha as Oscar Zoroaster Phadrig Isaac Norman Henkle Emmannuel Ambroise Diggs, he shortened his initials to O. Z. He had a circus career, practiced ventriloquism and tricks, owned a balloon marked with his initials, and reached the Land of Oz by balloon. Mistaken there for a powerful wizard, he directed the building of the Emerald City and its palace and ruled Oz for many years, though he later escaped by balloon and returned to Omaha. He cannot perform magic without tools and machinery, but is skilled at amusing audiences and improvising with practical items. After returning to Oz, Princess Ozma appoints him Official Wizard and gives him a permanent home. Traveling with Dorothy, Zeb, Jim, and nine tiny piglets, he becomes a useful, resourceful companion despite his admitted fraudulence. He takes the House of the Sorcerer as lodging; uses lanterns and kerosene to save the group from the Mangaboos; carries supplies in his satchel; warns the others about dangerous fruit; kills an invisible bear; and helps navigate rivers, stairs, caverns, and an escape from Gargoyles. His revolvers and sword fail against the Gargoyles, but he recognizes how their wings work and helps make a flying buggy, later burning wooden wings and doors to delay pursuit. In the Emerald City he is warmly welcomed, performs piglet tricks for the people, and promises to remain to amuse them. His nine piglets are central to both his performances and his efforts to save Eureka: after one is lost, he secretly provides an identical piglet for the Tin Woodman to present as the missing pet. Across the records, he moves from a confessed humbug and former ruler to Ozma's faithful Official Wizard, valued for courage, tricks, tools, and loyalty to his companions.
