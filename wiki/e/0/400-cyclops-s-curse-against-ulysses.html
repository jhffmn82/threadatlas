In The Odyssey, it is the Cyclops’s prayer to Neptune, made while raising his hands to heaven, asking that Ulysses never reach home alive or return late and in poor condition after losing his men. In The Odyssey, it is the Cyclops’s curse praying that Ulysses never reach home alive, or return late and suffering after losing his men.
