Abstract written by a model

Fire, set and used by the Trojans to attack the Achaean ships, becomes their immediate objective. Hector repeatedly orders it brought, but Ajax resists. After Hector cuts Ajax’s spear, the Trojans ignite a ship’s stern; Patroclus and the Myrmidons drive them back and quench it.
