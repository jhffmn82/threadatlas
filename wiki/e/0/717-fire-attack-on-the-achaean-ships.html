fire attack on the Achaean ships in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Fire, set and used by the Trojans to attack the Achaean ships, becomes their immediate objective. Hector repeatedly orders it brought, but Ajax resists. After Hector cuts Ajax’s spear, the Trojans ignite a ship’s stern; Patroclus and the Myrmidons drive them back and quench it.

Fire is the Trojans’ immediate objective at the ships. Hector repeatedly orders his men to bring it, but Ajax kills or drives back those who approach with it.

BOOK XVI.

The Trojans throw fire onto the ships after Hector cuts Ajax’s spear. The fire spreads around the stern of the ship of Protesilaus until Patroclus and the Myrmidons drive the Trojans back and quench it.
