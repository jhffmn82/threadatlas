Achilles' armor

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: all the armour, armor, armour, celestial armor, his own armour, splendid armor, splendid suit of armor, suit of armor, the gleaming armour
  • In The Iliad, it is the armor requested for Achilles, consisting of a helmet, shield, greaves with ankle-clasps, and a breastplate, which Vulcan makes from copper, tin, silver, and gold.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a finely crafted suit Vulcan makes for Achilles at Thetis's request, and Greek chiefs award it to Ulysses after Achilles's death.

armour in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

The armor requested for Achilles consists of a helmet, shield, greaves fitted with ankle-clasps, and a breastplate. Vulcan makes the pieces from copper, tin, silver, and gold, and the finished work is made to amaze all who behold it.

Achilles' armor in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Vulcan makes Achilles a finely crafted suit in one night at Thetis's request, with shield, helmet, corslet, and greaves; after Achilles's death, Greek chiefs award it to Ulysses.
