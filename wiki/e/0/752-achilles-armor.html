Achilles' armor

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: all the armour, armor, armour, celestial armor, his own armour, splendid armor, splendid suit of armor, suit of armor, the gleaming armour

Abstract written by a model

In The Iliad, it is the armor requested for Achilles, consisting of a helmet, shield, greaves with ankle-clasps, and a breastplate, which Vulcan makes from copper, tin, silver, and gold. In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, it is a finely crafted suit Vulcan makes for Achilles at Thetis's request, and Greek chiefs award it to Ulysses after Achilles's death.
