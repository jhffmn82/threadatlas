sledge in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Sledge is a rude vehicle Claus constructs from wood-pile material, with two upward-curving runners, a short-board platform, and a stool. Before its first Christmas Eve journey, its harness is repaired and strengthened. Drawn by Flossie and Glossie, it carries Claus and toys swiftly over snow and rooftops south to children.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

Claus constructs a rude sledge from material in his wood-pile, using two upward-curving runners and short boards for a platform. He places a stool and his pack of toys on it, and Flossie and Glossie pull it swiftly over the snow and across rooftops.

10. Christmas Eve

Claus's sledge is drawn by Glossie and Flossie during his first Christmas Eve journey. Claus repairs its harness and strengthens it before loading it with toys and traveling south.
