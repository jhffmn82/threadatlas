Datasets .. Baselines and Ablations

It is evaluated using top-k recall for k values of 1, 2, 5, 10, and 20 on development sets. Retrieved English passages are intended to determine how much external knowledge can be used for keyphrase generation.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

Passage retrieval improves substantially with mined pseudo parallel passage pairs, with Recall@5 rising from 50.1% to 72.4% on the EcommerceMKP DEV set. Iterative-training retrieval performance also increases as more annotated parallel data is provided.
