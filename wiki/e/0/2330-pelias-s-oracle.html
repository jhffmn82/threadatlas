Pelias’s oracle in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

foretells Pelias’s death [at the hands of the one-sandaled man] Pelias’s oracle foretells that a hateful doom will come upon him through the man he sees arriving with one sandal. Jason fulfills the sign after crossing the Anaurus and leaving one sandal in the flood.

Pindar · -0497

foretells Jason [as the threat to Pelias] An oracle warns Pelias to guard against the man who comes wearing one sandal. Jason fulfills the sign when he arrives in Iolkos with a single sandal on his right foot, causing Pelias to recognize the danger.
