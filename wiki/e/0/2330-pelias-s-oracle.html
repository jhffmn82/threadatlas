Pelias’s oracle

oracle · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: man of one sandal, oracle, oracle of the one-sandaled man, plain sign of the single sandal, single sandal prophecy, that true report, the oracle, χρησμόν

Abstract written by a model

In The Extant Odes of Pindar, the single sandal prophecy foretells Jason as the threat to Pelias. In The Argonautica, Pelias’s oracle foretells Pelias’s death. In The Library, the oracle of the one-sandaled man warns Pelias to beware of the man with a single sandal.
