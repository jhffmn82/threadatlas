Jefferson Hope's pursuit and vengeance in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Jefferson Hope's lifelong pursuit and vengeance begins after Lucy Ferrier is forced to marry Enoch Drebber and John Ferrier is found dead. Seeking punishment for Drebber and accomplice Joseph Stangerson, Hope stalks them, attempts to kill them in the mountains, searches across the United States, follows them through Europe, and finally tracks them in London to impose his own judgment.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Jefferson Hope resolves to devote his life to revenge after finding John Ferrier dead and learning that Lucy has been taken. He stalks Drebber and Stangerson, attempts to kill them in the mountains, searches for them across the United States, and finally tracks them through Europe to London.

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope follows Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson across two continents, eventually locating them in London and tracking them through the city. He plans a personal judgment and execution because he believes the legal system can no longer convict them.
