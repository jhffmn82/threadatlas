Penelope's waiting women

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: die most miserably, execution of the women, her maidens, run them through with your swords, the waiting women

Abstract written by a model

Penelope’s waiting women, household maids, are summoned and confined between the domed room and yard, then Telemachus hangs them by their necks from a ship’s cable secured around the building.

Penelope's waiting women is in one work on this site, The Odyssey. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
