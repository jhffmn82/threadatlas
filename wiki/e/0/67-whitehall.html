Whitehall in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Whitehall is a place repeatedly visited by the diarist and speaker: they seek my Lord, meet Dean Fuller and the Duke of York, and see the Portuguese Embassador take leave of the King. Pepys waits for Lord Roberts at the Privy Seal, then goes with him to his chamber over the gate.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

The diarist goes to Whitehall several times, seeking my Lord and later meeting Dean Fuller there. He also returns to Whitehall after visiting Mr. Pierce and drinks with Mr. Symons at the Swan tavern.

opening

The speaker went to Whitehall with Sir Williams and met the Duke of York there. The Portuguese Embassador also came to White Hall to take leave of the King.

opening

Pepys goes to Whitehall early on the 8th and waits there for Lord Roberts at the Privy Seal. He returns there on the 9th, and after Lord Roberts arrives they go to his chamber over the gate.
