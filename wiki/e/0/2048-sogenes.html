Nemean Ode VII

Sogenes, son of Thearion, wins distinction in the boys' pentathlon and is celebrated as a victor from Aigina. Pindar praises his achievement, swears that he has not exceeded the proper bounds in praising him, and prays that Sogenes and his family may enjoy lasting strength, prosperity, and honor.
