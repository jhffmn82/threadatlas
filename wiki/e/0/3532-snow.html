Abstract written by a model

Snow is light snowfall, fresh, new-fallen, and white. It falls lightly and softly around Toto's cottage, layering the roof, pump, and windows, then covers the ground, heaps into drifts and shapes, and weighs down forest trees in feathery masses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Toto's Merry Winter / CHAPTER I.

Snow falls lightly and softly outside the cottage, covering the roof, the pump, and the pump's nose with white layers. The flakes press against the windows as if asking to be let inside. Toto has also built a snow fort and snowballed Bruin.

CHAPTER IV.

Fresh snow covers the ground and heaps into drifts and strange half-human shapes. Snow also weighs down the forest trees, falling in feathery masses as <DW53> and Toto enter the woods.

The Valley's snow lies many feet deep, but the Frost King freezes its surface so the deer can walk and the sledge can travel over it. The snow later supports the deer's rapid journey home, while Claus tumbles into a snowdrift when the sledge stops.
