Jefferson Hope in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

Jefferson Hope is a hunter, scout, trapper, silver explorer, ranchman, and later London cabman who becomes the confessed killer of Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson. Tall, powerfully built, sunburned, and marked by determination and sustained vindictiveness, he begins as a Washoe hunter from the Nevada Mountains. After rescuing Lucy Ferrier from a frightened horse, he falls in love with her and becomes engaged to her with John Ferrier’s consent. While working silver lodes, he receives Ferrier’s warning that Lucy and John are endangered in Salt Lake City. He returns secretly with horses, a mule, money, provisions, water, and firearms, and leads them toward Carson City. The escape fails when he returns from hunting to find John dead and Lucy carried off by pursuers. Lucy’s death and John’s fate turn Hope’s life into a pursuit of revenge. He returns to the Nevada mines to restore his health and gather money, then follows Drebber and Stangerson from Utah across the United States, Europe, and two continents. In London he works as a cabdriver, using his cab both for employment and for pursuing them. He kills Drebber by making him choose between poisoned pills, and later enters Stangerson’s hotel room through a window; he says he stabs Stangerson in self-defence after being attacked. He regards these killings as justice and revenge, including a debt owed to John Ferrier, rather than mere murder. Sherlock Holmes identifies Hope, lures the disguised cabman to his rooms, and captures him after he tries violently to escape through a window. Arrested at 221B Baker Street and held as a prisoner, Hope gives a confession to the investigators. He says his work is complete, but has a life-threatening aortic aneurism expected to burst soon. It does burst the night after his capture, and he dies in his cell before appearing before the magistrates.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Jefferson Hope is identified by Sherlock Holmes as the murderer of Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson. Disguised as a cabman, he is lured into Holmes’s room, handcuffed, and captured after violently trying to escape through a window.

CHAPTER II.

Jefferson Hope is a tall, capable hunter who has worked as a pioneer, scout, trapper, silver explorer, and ranchman. He rescues Lucy Ferrier from a frightened horse among a drove of cattle, falls deeply in love with her, and leaves to work silver lodes after securing her promise to marry him.

CHAPTER III.

Jefferson Hope has been away from Salt Lake City for three weeks with his comrades. John Ferrier plans to send him a message about the danger, believing he will return quickly and advise them.

CHAPTER IV.

Jefferson Hope returns from the Nevada Mountains after receiving Ferrier’s message and secretly reaches the watched farm by crawling across the ground. He brings horses, a mule, money, provisions, and water, then leads John Ferrier and Lucy through the fields and mountain pass toward Carson City.

CHAPTER V.

Jefferson Hope leads Lucy Ferrier and John Ferrier through the mountains toward safety, but returns from hunting to find John dead and Lucy taken by their pursuers. He spends years pursuing revenge against Drebber and Stangerson, following them from Utah through the United States and Europe until he reaches London.

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope confesses that he pursued and killed Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson in vengeance for the deaths of John Ferrier and Lucy Ferrier. He says that he followed them from Salt Lake City across two continents and that his work is complete, although he expects his aortic aneurism to kill him soon.

“That was how Enoch Drebber came to his end. All I had to do then was

Jefferson Hope tells how he tracked Stangerson to Halliday’s Private Hotel and entered his bedroom through a window. He says he killed Stangerson in self-defence after Stangerson attacked him, then continued cabbing until he was arrested.

CHAPTER VII.

Jefferson Hope is identified as the man who killed Enoch Drebber and Joseph Stangerson in revenge connected with an old romantic feud. He is captured in Sherlock Holmes’s rooms and dies in his cell when an aneurism bursts before he can appear before the magistrates.
