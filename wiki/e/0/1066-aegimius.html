Abstract written by a model

Aegimius is a poem, possibly attributed to Hesiod or Cercops of Miletus. It recounts Phrixus’s reception and purification of the fleece, Thetis testing her children’s mortality in a cauldron, a speaker’s refreshment place, and three Hellenic tribes settling in Crete.

AEGIMIUS

The author of the “Aegimius” says that Phrixus was received without intermediary because of the fleece and that, after the sacrifice, he purified the fleece before entering Aeetes’s halls. The second book says that Thetis threw her children by Peleus into a cauldron to discover where they were mortal. The work is also associated with an account of a speaker’s place of refreshment and with the settlement of three Hellenic tribes in Crete.
