the cloth

Abstract written by a model

The cloth, valued at £300 for presents to the Turks in Barbary, is bought for my Lord: two pieces each of scarlet, purple, and black cloth, plus £50 in linen. Mr. Shaw offers the money, my Lord orders Mr. Creed to advance it through Alderman Backwell, and the diarist struggles to send it before it is lately sent to sea.
