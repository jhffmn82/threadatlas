balloon launch in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

A balloon is heated by fire before the Palace, swells and lifts its basket, then rises when ropes crack, leaving Dorothy unable to board.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVII

During the launch, the Tin Woodman builds a fire and Oz holds the balloon over it until the balloon swells and lifts the basket. The ropes then crack, sending the balloon upward before Dorothy can board.
