Abstract written by a model

The entity consists of user ID, product ID, and rating attributes, also represented as user, product, and rating identifiers. Alone, these identifiers provide limited semantic or descriptive information, but they form the structured input for review generation and efficient coarse retrieval filtering. AT2T augments them with matching reference reviews, uses them to select references and generate text, and represents them as embeddings processed with attention and decoder initialization. Their biases can guide generated reviews; future work proposes keywords or topic distributions as alternative guidance.

Abstract

The attribute identifiers are user ID, product ID, and rating. They provide limited information for generation because they do not include specific descriptive content.

Introduction

Attributes include identifiers for users, products, and ratings in review generation. Their identifiers alone do not convey semantic information, so AT2T augments them with matching reference reviews.

opening .. Augmenting context using references

Attributes in AT2T are identifiers such as user ID, product ID, and rating. They provide the structured input used for review generation and for efficient coarse filtering during retrieval.

AT2T .. SL-REFLECT

The attributes are user, product, and rating identifiers, represented as A = {u, p, r}. They are provided as input to the review generation task and used to select references and generate text.

GEN .. Training GEN

Input attributes are represented by embedding vectors and processed with attention to produce an attribute representation for each decoding step. They are also used to initialize the decoder through a multilayer perceptron.

Conclusion

Their biases can guide generated reviews, and their keywords or topic distributions are proposed as alternative guidance methods for future work.
