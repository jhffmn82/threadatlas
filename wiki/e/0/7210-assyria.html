Assyria

Also called: ASSYRIA, Assyrian

Abstract written by a model

Assyria is a place said to be named after the deity Asshur, though scholars dispute the derivation; its name is interpreted through the Maya form Axul as “thy end.”

Assyria is in one work on this site, Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
