T-REx in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Michael Glass, Gaetano Rossiello, Md Faisal Mahbub Chowdhury, Alﬁo Gliozzo · 2021

T-REx is a slot-filling dataset and one of two zero-shot slot-filling tasks standardized by KILT. Its train split has 2.284 million instances, making it larger than zsRE, though experiments use only its first 500,000 training instances and the datasets have similar numbers of distinct relations. It is used to evaluate slot-filling accuracy, retrieval, and the effects of explicit knowledge across relations and entity-frequency groups. DPRDNS+RAG achieves the listed best retrieval results, 74.34 R-Prec and 82.89 R@5. KGI1 substantially outperforms BARTLARGE, while knowledge gains show no consistent entity-frequency pattern; KGI ranks top-1 with a 38.24-point KILT-F1 improvement.

T-REx is much larger than zsRE in number of instances, although their training sets contain similar numbers of distinct relations. The experiments use only the first 500,000 T-REx training instances, and KGI1 substantially improves performance on T-REx.

Analysis .. Slot Filling Evaluation

T-REx is used to evaluate slot-filling accuracy and to analyze how explicit knowledge affects different relations and entity-frequency groups. KGI1 substantially outperforms BARTLARGE on T-REx, while the gain from explicit knowledge shows no consistent dependence on entity frequency.

Conclusion

T-REx is one of the zero-shot slot filling datasets used to evaluate KGI. KGI ranked at the top-1 position on the KILT leaderboard for this evaluation, with a net KILT-F1 improvement of 38.24 percentage points.
