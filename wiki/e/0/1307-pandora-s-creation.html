Abstract written by a model

Pandora’s creation is a sheer, hopeless snare devised by Zeus after Prometheus steals fire. Zeus commands the Limping God to form a maiden from earth and water; Athene and other gods adorn her with clothing, beauty, speech, and deceptive qualities, making her irresistible guile.

Pandora's creation is ordered by Zeus after Prometheus steals fire. Hephaestus forms her from earth and water, while Athene, Aphrodite, Hermes, the Graces, Persuasion, and the Hours provide her clothing, beauty, speech, and deceptive qualities.
