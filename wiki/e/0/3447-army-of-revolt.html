Army of Revolt in The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1904-06

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt is a rebel army and regiment of about four hundred young women and girls assembled from all parts of the Land of Oz. Divided into four companies, they wear colorful uniforms and use knitting-needles as weapons. Opposing Scarecrow’s rule because they reject male rule, they intend to conquer Emerald City and obtain its royal treasury. They capture the city without bloodshed, overcome its defenders, drive out the Scarecrow, take his throne, rob the treasury of jewels, and control the city as an Army of Conquerors. They surround and guard the palace, though the Saw-Horse carries escaping prisoners past them. Later, worried that the Scarecrow may join the Tin Woodman, Jinjur seeks Mombi’s help. The army initially lets the Scarecrow’s party enter the city, then surrounds it in the throne room, but flees when the Scarecrow releases mice. Although it urges Jinjur to resist and makes fudge in the palace kitchens, it surrenders after Jinjur is captured, returns the stolen gems to Princess Ozma, and is disbanded; the girls go home to their mothers.

The Marvelous Land of Oz

Gen. Jinjur's Army of Revolt is named in the chapter list.

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

The Army of Revolt consists of about four hundred girls from all parts of the Land of Oz. Its members wear colorful uniforms and carry knitting-needles as weapons, then overcome the city’s defenders and become an Army of Conquerors.

The Scarecrow Plans an escape

The invading girls conquer the Emerald City and surround the palace. They guard the palace with knitting-needles, but the Saw-Horse knocks several over and carries the escaping prisoners past them.

A Nickel-Plated Emperor

The girls of Oz rise in revolt against the Scarecrow’s rule, saying that men have ruled the land long enough and that a poor rule does not work both ways. They drive the Scarecrow from the Emerald City, rob its treasury of jewels, and run the city themselves.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.

The Army of Revolt is endangered by the escape of the Scarecrow and the possibility that he will join forces with the Tin Woodman. General Jinjur seeks Mombi's assistance against them.

The Prisoners of the Queen

The Army of Revolt controls the Emerald City and initially allows the Scarecrow's party to advance without opposition. It surrounds the party in the throne room, but its members flee in terror when the Scarecrow releases mice.

The Riches of Content

The Army of Revolt advises Jinjur to resist and spends much of its time making fudge in the palace kitchens. It surrenders when Jinjur is captured, returns the stolen emeralds and other gems, and is disbanded, with the girls sent home to their mothers.
