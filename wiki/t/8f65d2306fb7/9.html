Ethical Considerations In developing FRAMES, we recognize the importance of addressing potential ethical considerations in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) research. Researchers using this benchmark should be mindful of possible biases in information sources, the need for safeguards against potential misuse of advanced RAG systems, and the environmental impact of computationally intensive approaches. We encourage exploring efﬁcient, accessible implementations and maintaining transparency in methodologies. By openly discussing these considerations, we aim to foster responsible advancement of RAG capabilities. We welcome ongoing dialogue within the research community to collectively navigate these important ethical aspects as the ﬁeld progresses.
