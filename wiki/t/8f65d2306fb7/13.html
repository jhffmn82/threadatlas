B Additional Results One of the issues we noticed quite commonly in multi-turn experiments was that models would get into loops of incorrect query generation and be unable to escape them. For the question "What is the middle name of the U.S. president who died on the same day of the year as Virginia Woolf?", the baseline multi-step search generated a sequence of queries that reﬂected incorrect initial reasoning: after identifying Virginia Woolf’s death date (March 28), it incorrectly ﬁxated on Franklin D. Roosevelt rather than Dwight Eisenhower. This is evident from its generated query sequence: 1. “Virginia Woolf death date” 2. “US presidents who died in March” 3. “US presidents who died on March 28” 4. “US presidents who had a middle name” 5. “Presidents who died in ofﬁce” 6. “Franklin D Roosevelt middle name” 7. “Franklin D Roosevelt death date” 8. “Franklin D Roosevelt biography” 9. “Franklin D Roosevelt middle name” 10. “Franklin Delano Roosevelt death” 11. “Franklin D Roosevelt death date” The model was unable to recover from this incorrect trajectory. However, when provided with in-context planning examples that encouraged exploration and diversity in search queries, the model maintained broader exploration and correctly identiﬁed Eisenhower as the relevant president. While this in-context approach solved similar trajectory problems in some samples, we observed that more aggressive methods are needed to fully address this issue in many other complicated cases where we didn’t see signiﬁcant improvements. 4756
