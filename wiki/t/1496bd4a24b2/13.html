B Evaluation Details In order to match the answers obtained by the model, we included the paradigm "So the answer is" in the exemplars to encourage the model to gen- erate in this format. Specifically, if "So the answer is" is absent from all of the model’s generations, during the evaluation phase, we append "So the an- swer is" to the end of the model’s output, prompting the model to generate again. Subsequently, we se- lect the words following "So the answer is" as the final answer.
