This section, in brief. This unit reports specialty-level accuracy for six language models on the RSNA-RadioQA dataset and hallucination rates on both the RSNA-RadioQA and ExtendedQA datasets. It compares RadioRAG and no-RAG performance and describes how relevant retrieval context relates to correct, incorrect, and hallucinated responses. It also lists RadioRAG-powered GPT-3.5-turbo answers for the first 20 RSNA-RadioQA questions with their sources, alongside conventional GPT-3.5-turbo answers for the first 30 questions.
