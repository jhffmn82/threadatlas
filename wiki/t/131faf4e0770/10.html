This section, in brief. This unit lists four RSNA RadioQA cases and their publication details. The cases are "Right vocal cord palsy," authored by R. Vyas, A. Vyas, R. Vyas, and A. Singh; "Putty Kidney," authored by O. Schoeck, M. Horrow, J. Ganeles, and J. Gubernick; "Duplicated Collecting System with Vesicoureteral Reflux in the Lower Pole Moiety," authored by P. Cormier, K. Ledbetter, J. Leschied, and G. Sabharwal; and "Schistosomiasis Bladder Wall Calcification," authored by A. Rand, D. Akselrod, and J. Tam. The cases were published between August 12, 2020, and September 29, 2021, and cover neuroradiology, head and neck, genitourinary, pediatric, and computed tomography subspecialties.
