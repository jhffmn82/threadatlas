This section, in brief. The unit introduces RadioRAG, an online retrieval-augmented generation system for radiology question answering. The work is authored by Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh and colleagues from institutions including University Hospital RWTH Aachen, Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Stanford University, and other centers in Germany and the United States. The document is identified as a preprint version of a paper published in Radiology: Artificial Intelligence in 2025. Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh is listed as the corresponding author.
