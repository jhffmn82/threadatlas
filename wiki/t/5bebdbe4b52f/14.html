SafeRAG is a dataset constructed to evaluate the security of retrieval-augmented generation under four adversarial attack tasks: silver noise, inter-context conflict, soft ad, and white DoS. The construction process generates attack contexts and combines them with golden contexts associated with questions. The resulting security evaluation datasets are DSN, DICC, DSA, and DWDoS, whose union defines the complete SafeRAG dataset.

SafeRAG Dataset Construction To evaluate the security of RAG under different adversarial scenarios, we design four attack tasks T = {SN, ICC, SA, WDoS}: silver noise SN, inter-context conflict ICC, soft ad SA, and white DoS WDoS. For each attack task t ∈T, mali- cious attack texts are generated and combined with golden contexts to construct the RAG security eval- uation dataset (i.e., SafeRAG dataset). The detailed 4619 process is as follows: 1) Silver Noise: 1. Decompose the golden contexts Cj g into mini- mal semantic units (i.e., propositions) P j g = {p1 g, p2 g, . . . }. 2. Select the proposition pi g most semantically relevant to question qj. 3. Use pi g as input to the DeepSeek to generate 10 diverse attack contexts. 4. Manually select 8 semantically consistent yet non-redundant attack contexts to form the sil- ver noise contexts: CSN,j a = {ck,SN a | k = 1, . . . , 8}. 5. Combine the silver noise contexts with the golden contexts to construct the silver noise security evaluation dataset: DSN =  (qj, i, Cj g |{z} CSN b ∪CSN,j a ) | j = 1, . . . , MSN . 2) Inter-Context Conflict: 1. Select a golden context ci g ∈Cj g. 2. Based on ci g, use strategies such as minimal perturbation to generate a text CICC,j a = {c1,ICC a } that clearly contradicts ci g. 3. Randomly select another golden context ce g ∈ Cj g \ {ci g}, where e̸ = i. 4. Combine the generated conflict context with the golden context ci g used to generate it and another golden context ce g to construct the con- flict security evaluation dataset: DICC =  (qj, i, {ci g, ce g} | {z } CICC b ∪CICC,j a ) | j = 1, . . . , MICC . 3) Soft Ad: 1. Select a golden context ci g ∈Cj g. 2. Manually read ci g and generate appropriate attack keywords a1,... (i.e., soft ad keywords). 3. Use strategies like direct insertion or in- direct insertion to embed attack keywords into ci g, generating the soft ad attack context CSA,j a = {c1,SA a }. 4. Randomly select two other golden contexts ce g and cl g from Cj g \ {ci g} for constructing the clean set, where i̸ = e, e̸ = l, l̸ = i. 5. Combine the soft ad attack context with the other two golden contexts ce g, cl g to construct the soft ad security evaluation dataset: DSA =  (qj, i, {ce g, cl g} | {z } CSA b ∪CSA,j a ) | j = 1, . . . , MSA . 4) White DoS: 1. Based on question qj, generate an attack con- text CWDoS,j a = {c1,WDoS a } containing white safety warnings. 2. Combine the generated white DoS context with the complete golden contexts Cj g to construct the White DoS security evaluation dataset: DWDoS =  (qj, i, Cj g |{z} CWDoS b ∪CWDoS,j a ) | j = 1, . . . , MWDoS . The complete SafeRAG dataset is defined as18: Dsfr = DSN ∪DICC ∪DSA ∪DWDoS. A.3.4
