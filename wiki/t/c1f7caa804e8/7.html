This section, in brief. The conclusion concerns MedGraphRAG, a graph-based retrieval-augmented generation framework for medical response generation. It uses Triple Graph Construction and U-Retrieval to improve evidence-based, context-aware responses and performs strongly on benchmarks and human evaluations. Future work will address real-time data updates and validation on real-world clinical data.
