This section, in brief. “Medical Graph RAG: Towards Safe Medical Large Language Model via Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation” is a work about Medical Graph RAG and Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for a safe medical large language model. It is authored by Junde Wu, Jiayuan Zhu, Yunli Qi, Jingkun Chen, Min Xu, Filippo Menolascina, and Vicente Grau. The authors are affiliated with the University of Oxford, Carnegie Mellon University, and The University of Edinburgh.
