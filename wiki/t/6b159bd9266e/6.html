This section, in brief. The unit evaluates PIC against sentence, paragraph, fixed-size, semantic, and proposition chunking methods on retrieval and end-to-end question-answering tasks across six datasets. PIC achieves the highest average retrieval performance and consistently produces the best QA results with both Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct. PIC performs less strongly on SQuAD, likely because its questions come from relatively few documents and resemble reading comprehension. QA accuracy is lower than retrieval accuracy overall, but PIC has the smallest performance drop, which the authors attribute to reduced irrelevant information in its chunks.

Similar phenomenon is also observed by Lee et al., 2019. 5.2 End-to-end QA Performance Similar conclusions can be drawn regarding QA performance. As shown in Table 2, experiments with two different LLMs demonstrate that PIC con- sistently achieves the best results. When using Qwen as the LLM, PIC shows an average improve- ment of 2 points across six datasets, with a remark- able 5.7-point increase on the EQ dataset. When using Llama-3, PIC also demonstrates an average improvement of nearly 1 point. It can be observed that the accuracy of the QA task is significantly lower than that of retrieval, indicating that sometimes, even when the correct relevant text is retrieved, the model still fails to provide the correct answer. This may be due to the retrieved text containing too much irrelevant information, making it difficult for the LLM to effectively capture the answer. However, among all methods, our approach exhibits the smallest performance drop. Taking the results on Qwen as an example, under the top-5 setting, our method decreases by 8.5 points (58.4 →49.9), while the proposition method decreases by 9.8 points (57.7 →47.9) and the Semantic method by 10.9 points (56.0 →45.1). We attribute this to the reduced irrelevant information in the chunks, making the LLM less prone to hallucinations.
