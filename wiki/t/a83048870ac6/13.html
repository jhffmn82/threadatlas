This section, in brief written by a model
The unit reports experimental results for LLaMA-3.1-8B, evaluating Naïve RAG and TurboRAG-reordered under different noise ratios. In RGB, Chinese results show average scores of 97.1 for Naïve RAG and 95.0 for TurboRAG-reordered, while English results show 98.4 and 97.4, respectively. In the RGB Information Integration Task, Chinese averages are 51.7 and 48.3, and English averages are 62.3 and 62.0, for Naïve RAG and TurboRAG-reordered, respectively. The results are presented as further validation of our method across RoPE-based models.
