This section, in brief. Table 3 reports human evaluation results for five models: CommitBERT, NNGen, NMTGen, CoRec, and RACE. The models are evaluated on informativeness, conciseness, and expressiveness, with standard deviations shown in parentheses. RACE receives the highest scores for informativeness (2.49) and conciseness (3.08), while CommitBERT receives the highest expressiveness score (2.46).
