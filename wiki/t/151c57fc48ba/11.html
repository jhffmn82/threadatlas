Dataset Details Table 8 shows the statistics of datasets used in our experiments. Both the datasets and knowledge sources are in English. For the MS- MARCO dataset, we split the original training set into our training set and validation set, and report all the experimental results on the original devel- opment set. This setting is for better comparison to the results reported in Lewis et al. (2020b). For both knowledge sources, we use the script provided by HuggingFace10 to build FAISS format corpus. Specially, for KILT-WoW knowledge source, we do an extra data cleaning process, including 1) re- moving paragraphs starting with Section:::: and BULLET::::; 2) removing all non-unicode charac- ters.
