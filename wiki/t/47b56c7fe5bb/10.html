7 Limitations While GRAG provides an effective framework for graph context-aware generation, the efficiency of GRAG’s textual subgraph retrieval depends on the quality of the initial node ranking and pruning mechanism. In cases where graph structure or node importance is difficult to estimate, this could lead to suboptimal retrieval performance.
