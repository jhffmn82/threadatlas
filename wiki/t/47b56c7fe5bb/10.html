This section, in brief. This unit discusses a limitation of GRAG, a framework for graph context-aware generation. Its textual subgraph retrieval depends on the quality of the initial node ranking and pruning mechanism. When graph structure or node importance is difficult to estimate, retrieval performance may be suboptimal. The work was supported by multiple National Science Foundation and NIH grants.

7 Limitations While GRAG provides an effective framework for graph context-aware generation, the efficiency of GRAG’s textual subgraph retrieval depends on the quality of the initial node ranking and pruning mechanism. In cases where graph structure or node importance is difficult to estimate, this could lead to suboptimal retrieval performance.
