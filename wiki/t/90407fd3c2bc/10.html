conclusion This paper presents a comprehensive comparison of RAG and LC, highlighting the trade-offs between performance and computational cost. While LC demonstrate superior performance in long-context understanding, RAG remains a viable option due to its lower cost and advantages when the input considerably exceeds the model’s context window size. Our proposed method, which dynamically routes queries based on model self-reﬂection, ef- fectively combines the strengths of both RAG and LC, achieving comparable performance to LC at a signiﬁcantly reduced cost. We believe our ﬁndings contribute valuable insights for the practical appli- cation of long-context LLMs and pave the way for future research in optimizing RAG techniques.
