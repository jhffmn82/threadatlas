This section, in brief written by a model
These notes provide textual, metrical, and interpretive commentary on Euripides’ Iphigenia in Aulis, frequently comparing readings with those of Markland, Hermann, Porson, Dindorf, and the Cambridge editor. They discuss Iphigenia’s sacrifice, her supposed marriage to Achilles, Agamemnon’s and Menelaus’ conduct, Achilles’ role, and references to Helen. Much attention is given to corrupt, incomplete, interpolated, or spurious passages, especially in the choruses and final scene.
