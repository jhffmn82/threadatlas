This section, in brief. The notes explain that the Dorian invasion of Peloponnesos was associated with Oxylos, who was said to be one of its leaders. Oxylos, of Elean descent but living in Aitolia, became king of Elis after the invasion. The judge at the Olympic games was thought to be descended from Oxylos or from an Aitolian who came with him. The Olympic games were held at the full moon in the middle of Hekatombaion, and Herakles was understood to have wanted to institute them at that favorable time.
