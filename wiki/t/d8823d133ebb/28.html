This section, in brief. Libya is connected with Epaphos, who was the son of Zeus and Io, and with a future colony founded by Battos. Euphemos was told that his descendants would first colonize Thera and, after seventeen generations, found Kyrene in Libya. Thera was identified as the island where a clod dropped by Euphemos would wash ashore. Kyrene is identified with the city whose fountain was called Kyra.
