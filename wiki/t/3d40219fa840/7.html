This section, in brief. The unit evaluates GAR for query-context generation, passage retrieval, passage reading, model generalizability, and computational efficiency in OpenQA, primarily on NQ and Trivia. GAR generates answer, sentence, and title contexts that increase lexical overlap with relevant passages, and combining these contexts improves retrieval because they are complementary. GAR substantially improves BM25 retrieval, approaches or exceeds DPR at larger retrieval cutoffs, and achieves strong end-to-end extractive and generative QA performance, especially when combined with DPR. GAR is also reported to require substantially less training, indexing, and retrieval computation than DPR.

That said, GAR +DPR appears to be less dependent on question memorization given its lower EM for this category.9 6.4 Efﬁciency of GAR GAR is efﬁcient and scalable since it uses sparse representations for retrieval and does not in- volve time-consuming training process such as RL (Nogueira and Cho, 2017; Liu et al., 2019). The only overhead of GAR is on the generation of query contexts and the retrieval with generation- augmented (thus longer) queries, whose computa- tional complexity is signiﬁcantly lower than other methods with comparable retrieval accuracy.
