Datasets Complexity of the data. While the number of documents in each dataset may appear modest, we emphasize that the knowledge base derived from these documents, via ontology grounding, is sig- nificantly richer and more complex than the raw document count suggests. The agricultural ontol- ogy (Figure 3) consists of 76 nodes (44 leaf nodes), while the news ontology (Figure 4) includes 40 nodes (19 leaf nodes). These ontologies are used to ground the document content, resulting in large and highly structured hypergraphs. Table 6 pro- vides detailed statistics illustrating the scale and complexity of these hypergraphs. Thus, even the filtered News dataset produces a hypergraph with 7,497 nodes and 4,573 hyperedges, with individual hyperedges connecting up to 22 nodes and node degrees reaching as high as 560. These properties indicate a highly interconnected structure, where retrieval is far from trivial. The resulting scale sim- ulates the kinds of complexity expected in larger real-world knowledge graphs. C
