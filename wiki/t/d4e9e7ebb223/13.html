This section, in brief. The unit concerns training hyperparameters for DPR and LI. Hyperparameters are listed in Tables 4, 5, and 7 and were tuned based on validation performance. DPR extracts table and question embeddings with dimension d = 768. LI extracts table embeddings of lt × 128 and fixed-length question embeddings of 32 × 128, padding questions shorter than 32 tokens.

Technical Details D.1 Hyperparameters The training hyperparameters are shown in Table 4, 5, and 7. The tuning of hyperparameters was per- formed on validation performance. DPR: The dimension of the extracted table/ques- tion embeddings is d = 768. LI: The dimension of the extracted table embed- dings is lt × d = lt × 128, where lt depends on the length of input tables. Following Santhanam et al. (2022b), the dimension of the extracted question embeddings is fixed to lq × d = 32 × 128. We pad the questions with less tokens than lq. D.2
