This section, in brief. This unit lists references concerning open-domain question answering, table retrieval, table parsing, and text-to-SQL generation. It includes work by Jonathan Herzig, Thomas Müller, Syrine Krichene, and Julian Eisenschlos on dense retrieval over tables, as well as work by Jonathan Herzig, Pawel Krzysztof Nowak, Thomas Müller, Francesco Piccinno, and Julian Eisenschlos on TaPas. Other references address dense passage retrieval, contextualized late interaction, retrieval-augmented generation, neural SQL execution, and table question answering. The unit also cites foundational work on attention, natural questions, semi-structured table parsing, Spider, and efficient similarity search.
