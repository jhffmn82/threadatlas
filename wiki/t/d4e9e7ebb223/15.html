This section, in brief. The unit reports the computational cost of LI, DPR, LI-RAGE, and DPR-RAGE. LI training time is not significantly higher than DPR training because both use contrastive learning, but LI index building takes around five minutes compared with 40 seconds for DPR. LI-RAGE has longer end-to-end training due to slower dynamic retrieval and refinement using larger multi-dimensional embeddings. Its inference speed increases only slightly, from 1.22 to 1.40 seconds per iteration, suggesting limited additional computation in real-world deployment.
