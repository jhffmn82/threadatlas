The unit concerns an ablation study of adaptive methods and configurations of adaptive k in a 3-class setting. It compares fixed and adaptive parameters, including query rewrites, reranking, and knowledge graph components, using faithfulness, answer relevancy, absolute correctness, and thresholded correctness. Adaptive methods generally improve faithfulness and answer quality, while the combination of adaptive parameters with a reranker achieves the highest thresholded correctness. The results also show trade-offs in which reranking or a knowledge graph can improve some measures while reducing overall response quality or other correctness scores.

Overall Results and Discussion Our analysis shows that adaptive methods generally outperform fixed baselines, particularly in improv- ing faithfulness and answer quality. Incorporating adaptive parameters such as query rewrites and reranking enhances the system’s ability to provide accurate and relevant responses. While adding a reranker improves correctness, it can slightly re- duce the overall correctness score, suggesting a trade-off between precision and answer quality. The introduction of a knowledge graph main- tains correctness but can add complexity, poten- tially lowering overall response quality. However, combining adaptive parameters with a reranker proves effective in maximizing the proportion of correct responses, even if it doesn’t lead to the highest scores in all metrics.
