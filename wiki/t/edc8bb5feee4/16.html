We compare RACG performance when providing top-1, 2, 5, and 10 documents. As shown by Figure 6, adding five documents yields the best results in most settings, except for StarCoder2 on RepoEval which best uses 8 docu- ments. Despite the drastic variance in length limits of StarCoder2 (16k) and DeepseekCoder (4k), the sweet spot is consistently 5 documents. While adding a few documents brings helpful contexts, adding more low-ranked documents may introduce noise and deteriorate generation due to the imper- fections of retrieval systems (Wang et al., 2023a).
