F Additional Future Directions High computational costs in video frame sampling and memory bottlenecks in processing multi-page documents with images remain key challenges in long-context processing. Fixed extraction rates strug- gle to capture relevant frames, requiring adaptive selection based on content complexity and move- ment (Kandhare and Gisselbrecht, 2024). Addi- tionally, retrieval speed-accuracy trade-offs in edge deployments and redundant computations in cross- modal fusion layers emphasize the need for efficient, scalable architectures. Personalization mechanisms, like user-specific retrieval (e.g., adapting to medical history), remain underexplored. As these systems evolve, ensuring privacy and preventing sensitive data leakage in multimodal outputs is critical.
