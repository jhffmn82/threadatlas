This section, in brief. “LongRAG: A Dual-Perspective Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigm for Long-Context Question Answering” is a work by Qingfei Zhao, Ruobing Wang, Yukuo Cen, Daren Zha, Shicheng Tan, Yuxiao Dong, and Jie Tang. The authors are affiliated with the Institute of Information Engineering, the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Tsinghua University, and Zhipu AI. The work concerns a dual-perspective retrieval-augmented generation paradigm for long-context question answering.
