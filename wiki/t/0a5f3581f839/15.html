D Prompts of LongRAG System We present all prompts in LongRAG’s components in Table 23. The "{content}" in different prompts represent different contextual information. To be specific, the "{content}" in the prompt of LLM- augmented information extraction represents all source long-context paragraphs p after the mapping strategy. In the prompt of the CoT guidance stage in the CoT-guided filter, it represents all retrieval chunks pc, while in the prompt of the filtering stage, it represents each pc.
