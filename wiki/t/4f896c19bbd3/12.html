The unit discusses limitations of the CoV-RAG framework and offers insights for future research. A small size LM distilled from GPT-4 generates training data for the generator to reduce time and financial costs, although using GPT-4 for all training data may improve performance against baselines. For efficiency, the retriever re-retrieves relevant references during verification before the LM predicts and outputs the final answer. Because a revised question may not yield the correct answer, multi-round validation may be needed and is left for future work.

Limitations There are also limitations in the CoV-RAG frame- work, we will discuss below to provide valuable insights for future research. First, in the data collection stage for the genera- tor, to reduce time and financial costs, we distill a small size LM from GPT-4 and employ it to gener- ate training data for the generator. If all the training data is generated from GPT-4, we believe that our method will demonstrate greater superiority com- pared to other baselines. Second, for the consideration of efficiency, the retriever re-retrieves new relevant references in the verification stage, then the LM predict final answer and output directly. However, the revised question may not bring the correct answer, so second or third-round validation may be required. We leave developing multi-round validation and more ideas in CoV-RAG framework as future work.
