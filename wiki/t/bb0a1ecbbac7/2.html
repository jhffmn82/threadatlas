1 Introduction In recent years, large pre-trained language models (PLMs), such as T5 (Raffel et al., 2020) and GPT- 3 (Brown et al., 2020), have revolutionized the field of natural language processing (NLP), achieving remarkable performance on various downstream tasks (Qiu et al., 2020). These PLMs have learned a substantial amount of in-depth knowledge from the pre-training corpus (Petroni et al., 2019), so they can predict the outputs on downstream tasks with- out access to any external memory or raw text, as a parameterized implicit knowledge base (Roberts et al., 2020). The way of fine-tuning PLMs using only input-output pairs of target data is often re- ferred to as close-book setting (Petroni et al., 2019).
