5 Conclusion This paper presents the RAGPR model, which is designed to improve the performance of triage in web-based medical services. The primary function of this model is to efficiently filter and select appropriate physicians, thereby assisting patients in identifying medical professionals best suited to address their specific healthcare needs. The implementation of this method has significant practical implications, suggesting its potential integration into various healthcare website systems to enhance the quality of physician recommendations.
