This section, in brief written by a model
The Yellow Knight of Oz is a 1930 book by Ruth Plumly Thompson, founded on and continuing the Oz stories by L. Frank Baum and illustrated by John R. Neill. Its introduction announces a quest by Sir Hokus of Pokes involving a strange Yellow Knight and his horse, Stampedro. The contents list adventures involving Queen Marcia, Ploppa, a camel, Tuzzle, a golden princess, and the restoration of Corumbia, culminating in the Yellow Knight's return and the marriage of Marygolden.
