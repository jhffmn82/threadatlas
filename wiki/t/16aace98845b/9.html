This section, in brief written by a model
This unit lists references concerning retrieval-augmented generation, large language models, question answering, hallucination, faithfulness, and attribution. It includes datasets and benchmarks such as TechQA, FinQA, PubMedQA, Natural Questions, KILT, FEVER, HotpotQA, and TAT-QA. It also cites evaluation frameworks and methods including RAGAs, ARES, AttributionBench, Prometheus 2, and automatic attribution evaluation. Several references address domain-specific or knowledge-grounded systems, including legal, biomedical, financial, conversational, and COVID-19 question answering.
