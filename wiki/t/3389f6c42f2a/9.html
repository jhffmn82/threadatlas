This section, in brief written by a model
This unit lists research on retrieval-augmented language models, dense and passage retrieval, knowledge graphs, and open-domain and multi-hop question answering. It includes work by Lewis et al. on retrieval-augmented generation, Karpukhin et al. on dense passage retrieval, and Izacard et al. on Atlas. It also covers knowledge-graph-enhanced methods such as KGPR, REANO, UniKGQA, KG-FiD, and subgraph retrieval, alongside datasets including HotpotQA and MuSiQue. Several works study chain-of-thought reasoning, interpretable or faithful large language model reasoning, temporal reasoning, and robustness to irrelevant context.
In this section: knowledge graph · retrieval-augmented generation · HotPotQA · MuSiQue · CoT · Dense passage retrieval · experimental datasets · large language models (LLMs) · Wikidata · large language model reasoning · dense retrieval · open-domain question answering · question answering · retrieval
