This unit reports additional experimental results and analyses for TRACE, including evaluations with Mistral-7B-v0.1 and Gemma-7B readers, ablations of TRACE-Doc, and studies of maximum chain length, demonstrations, chain count, adaptive termination, case studies, and efficiency. Using Mistral-7B-v0.1 as the reader, TRACE-Doc achieves the best reported performance among the listed systems on HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, while TRACE-Triple also outperforms IRCoT. The results indicate that TRACE's reasoning chains generalize across readers and that TRACE-Doc's components are important for retrieving relevant documents. On MuSiQue, the results further show that an appropriate maximum chain length and adaptive chain termination are important for performance.
