The unit identifies the paper “TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.” It was written by Jinyuan Fang, Zaiqiao Meng, and Craig Macdonald of the University of Glasgow. The paper appears in Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2024, pages 8472–8494, and concerns constructing knowledge-grounded reasoning chains for retrieval-augmented generation.
