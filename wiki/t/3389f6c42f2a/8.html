This section, in brief. The work identifies limitations concerning its focus on documents retrieved from Wikipedia, the evaluation of generated KG triples and reasoning chains, and the use of TRACE with a black-box LLM. The KG generator primarily creates triples between document titles and entities within the texts rather than between all possible entities. Because annotated data is unavailable, the work evaluates these components through final QA performance, while TRACE may produce only a single reasoning chain when it cannot access triple-selector logits.

Limitations We identify the following limitations of our work: (1) Since this work focuses on documents retrieved from Wikipedia, the KG generator primarily gener- ates KG triples between the title and entities within the texts, rather than between all possible entities. This approach simplifies the task, which would otherwise be more challenging, and we defer such exploration to future work; (2) Due to the lack of annotated data, we are unable to directly and quan- titatively evaluate the quality the generated KG triples and the constructed reasoning chains. In- stead, we assess their performance through the final QA performance. Moreover, we provide the statis- tics of the generated KGs and reasoning chains, as well as some qualitative results in Appendix C.3 to offer some insights into their structure and ef- fectiveness. We consider improving the evaluation methods for these two modules as one of our fu- ture work directions; (3) In the reasoning chain constructor, TRACE requires access to the logits of the triple selector to define a triple distribution. However, this may not be feasible when using a black-box LLM as the triple selector. In such cases, TRACE can use the option token output by the triple selector to select one triple at each step, lead- ing to a single reasoning chain. 8480
