The unit discusses limitations of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and the scope of KG2RAG. Limitations Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a sys- tematic engineering framework that can be refined from multiple perspectives, including query rewrit- ing (Xiao et al., 2023), retrieval optimization (Eibich et al., 2024), multi-turn dialogue (Yao et al., 2023) and so on (Gao et al., 2023). KG2RAG only focuses on the part of retrieval optimization and aims to perform KG-guided retrieval expansion and KG-based context organization to enhance RAG with the structured factual knowledge from KGs, without optimizing other modules. However, the proposed KG2RAG is orthogonal and compatible with the aforementioned modules. In the future, we will develop KG2RAG into a plug-and-play tool that can be easily integrated with other approaches, thereby better facilitating the research community.
