Case Study: Comparison between LightRAG and the Baseline NaiveRAG To further illustrate LightRAG’s superiority over baseline models in terms of comprehensiveness, empowerment, and diversity, we present a case study comparing LightRAG and NaiveRAG in Ta- ble 8. This study addresses a question regarding indigenous perspectives in the context of corpo- rate mergers. Notably, LightRAG offers a more in-depth exploration of key themes related to in- digenous perspectives, such as cultural significance, collaboration, and legal frameworks, supported by specific and illustrative examples. In contrast, while NaiveRAG provides informative responses, it lacks the depth needed to thoroughly examine the various dimensions of indigenous ownership and collaboration. The dual-level retrieval process employed by LightRAG enables a more compre- hensive investigation of specific entities and their interrelationships, facilitating extensive searches that effectively capture overarching themes and complexities within the topic. 9.6
