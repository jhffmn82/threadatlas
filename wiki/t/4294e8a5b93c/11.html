The narrator met uncle Wight at the Exchequer and went home with him to dinner. At dinner, the narrator and Mr. Norbury discussed Mr. Norbury's wife's house and land at Brampton, which the narrator found too much to buy. The narrator later went to the office and was pleased to see the house beginning to be clean. On the 22nd, the narrator dined with Lord Crew and was very much made of by Lord Crew and his lady.

Home, and so to the Exchequer, where I met with my uncle Wight, and home with him to dinner, where among others (my aunt being out of town), Mr. Norbury and I did discourse of his wife's house and land at Brampton, which I find too much for me to buy. Home, and in the afternoon to the office, and much pleased at night to see my house begin to be clean after all the dirt. 22nd. Abroad all the morning about several businesses. At noon went and dined with my Lord Crew, where very much made of by him and his lady.
