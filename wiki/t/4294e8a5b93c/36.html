This section, in brief written by a model
The writer considers subscribing L20 and reports that the Office is quiet because of a lack of money. The Office's bills have been offered for sale on the Exchange at a 10 per cent. loss. The Office may add Sir R. Ford's house, but difficulties over dividing it and personally guaranteeing the L200 annual rent may prevent this. The season is described as sickly everywhere because of strange and fatal fevers.

I think to subscribe L20. We are at our Office quiet, only for lack of money all things go to rack. Our very bills offered to be sold upon the Exchange at 10 per cent. loss. We are upon getting Sir R. Ford's house added to our Office. But I see so many difficulties will follow in pleasing of one another in the dividing of it, and in becoming bound personally to pay the rent of L200 per annum, that I do believe it will yet scarce come to pass. The season very sickly every where of strange and fatal fevers.
