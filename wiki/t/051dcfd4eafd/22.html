This section, in brief. This unit describes the prompts and workflow components of STARK, CRAG, and HYBGRAG for extracting entities and relations, selecting references, validating answers, and generating responses. STARK uses routers, a validator, and a commentor to decide between the knowledge graph and text documents and to correct extracted information. CRAG classifies each question by type, dynamism, domain, and reference source, then uses reflection, validation, commenting, and generation prompts. The unit also lists entity and relation types for finance, sports, music, movie, and encyclopedia domains, along with HYBGRAG's API-call and token counts.
