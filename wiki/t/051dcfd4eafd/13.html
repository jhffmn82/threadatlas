This section, in brief. This unit lists references concerning retrieval-augmented generation, large language models, graph-based retrieval, knowledge graphs, and self-reflection. It includes foundational work on retrieval-augmented generation, dense passage retrieval, language-model scaling, and chain-of-thought prompting. It also cites research on graph-based reasoning and retrieval, including GraphRAG, G-Retriever, QA-GNN, Think-on-Graph, and GraphReader. Several references study self-correction, critique, refinement, reflection, and agentic retrieval.
