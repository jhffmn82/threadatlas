This section, in brief. The unit presents the paper "HYBGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases." The paper concerns hybrid retrieval-augmented generation involving textual and relational knowledge bases. It was published in the Proceedings of the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics, Volume 1: Long Papers, on pages 879–893. The listed authors are Meng-Chieh Lee, Qi Zhu, Costas Mavromatis, Zhen Han, Soji Adeshina, Vassilis N. Ioannidis, Huzefa Rangwala, and Christos Faloutsos, affiliated with Carnegie Mellon University and Amazon.
