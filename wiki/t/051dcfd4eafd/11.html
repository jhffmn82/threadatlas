The unit presents HYBGRAG as a proposed approach for solving hybrid question answering (HQA). It uses a critic module for self-reflective question routing and a retriever bank for textual, relational, and hybrid questions. The approach is described as agentic, adaptive, interpretable, and effective, outperforming all baselines on HQA benchmarks. Applied on STARK, HYBGRAG achieves a 51% average relative improvement in Hit@1.
