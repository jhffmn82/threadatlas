Variants of Retrieval Targets There exists a misalignment between the retriever and the generator model. The retriever retrieves similar passages while the generator utilizes the associated keyphrases of these passages (not the retrieved passages) as external knowledge for gen- eration. Therefore, a good retriever does not neces- sarily guarantee the good quality and usefulness of these keyphrases. We tried two different retrieval targets which might close the misalignment.
