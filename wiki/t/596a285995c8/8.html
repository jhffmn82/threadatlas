Ethical Considerations While our work proposes an approach to reduce hallucination in structure output tasks, we do not claim that the risk of harm due to hallucination is eliminated. Our deployed system includes a layer of post-processing to clearly indicate to users the generated steps that do not exist and urge them to fix the output before continuing their work.
