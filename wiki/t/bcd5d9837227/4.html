The unit describes four datasets used for hallucination-related model training and evaluation. RAGTruth is a word-level hallucination evaluation resource designed for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and it is used for both training and system evaluation. Marco and XSum are used to expand the hallucination suppression training set, while WebGLM is used for system evaluation. Marco contains users’ queries and Bing web documents, WebGLM is a web-enhanced question-answering dataset, and XSum consists of BBC News summaries.

Dataset RAGTruth (Niu et al., 2024) dataset is a substan- tial, word-level hallucination evaluation resource specifically tailored for the RAG scenario, encom- passing several common tasks. We selected the RAGTruth dataset for our experiments because it is the largest available open-source dataset specif- ically designed for the RAG task. We adopt this dataset in both model training and system eval- uation processes. The detailed statistic of the RAGTruth dataset is demonstrated in Appendix, Table 7. Marco (Bajaj et al., 2018) is the Reading Compre- hension Dataset consisting of real users’ queries and web documents from Bing. We only used its question and web document pairs to enlarge our hallucination suppression training set. WebGLM (Liu et al., 2023): The Web-enhanced question-answering dataset, was used in our system evaluation process. XSum (Narayan et al., 2018): The BBC News Sum- mary dataset, was used to extend our hallucination suppression training set by adapting part of its news articles. 4
