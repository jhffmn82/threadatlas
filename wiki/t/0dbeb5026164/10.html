This section, in brief. The work studies review generation guided by reference documents. It finds that attribute-specific reference reviews provide useful inductive biases about given attributes, which can be delivered to generated reviews through a copying mechanism. As future work, it will investigate alternative guidance methods, including keywords or topic distributions of attributes.

Conclusion In this work, we study the problem of review generation guided by reference documents. Attribute- speciﬁc reference reviews provide useful inductive biases of given attributes, and such biases can be explicitly delivered to generated reviews using copying mechanism. Inspired by promising results, as future work, we will investigate alternative means of guidance, such as keywords or topic distribution of attributes.
