This section, in brief. GEN is an encoder-decoder model with a copying mechanism for generating reviews. It encodes reference texts with a bidirectional LSTM, contextualizes and aggregates them with attention, and also attends over input attributes. During decoding, GEN combines reference and attribute representations and can copy words from references. GEN is pretrained with top-K BLEU-1 references and cross-entropy on gold reviews, while training with REFLECT-retrieved references significantly reduces performance.
