This section, in brief. This unit evaluates IterRAG-LM and PlanRAG-LM with GPT-3.5, Llama 2 (70B), Llama 2 (13B), and Phi-2. The experiments use one machine with eight Nvidia A100 (80GB) GPUs, with vLLM used to accelerate inference for open models. Llama 2 and Phi-2 cannot solve any DQA problems, while GPT-3.5 performs better with IterRAG-LM than with PlanRAG-LM. The results are reported in Table 11 for Locating and Building across RDB and GDB settings.
