The front matter lists works by the same author and identifies their publishers, publication dates, formats, and prices. William Heinemann published History of Ancient Greek Literature, Carlyon Sahib, and Andromache. The Clarendon Press published several works on Greek literature and religion, including Euripidis Fabulae, The Rise of the Greek Epic, Four Stages of Greek Religion, and Nefrekepta. George Allen & Co., Ltd. published a volume containing works by Euripides and Aristophanes, while several Greek plays were also issued separately in English rhyming verse with explanatory notes.
