This section, in brief. Hero writes to Leander, urging him to come because every delay postpones their joy and she cannot patiently endure love. She describes her nights of waiting, watching the Hellespont, lighting a beacon, imagining his swim, and fearing that his love may wander or that another woman may keep him away. The stormy sea prevents Leander's swimming voyage, so Hero appeals to Neptune, recalls his past boldness, and proposes that they meet midway if necessary. Auspicious signs from the lamp, the nurse, and the weather give Hero hope that Leander will return.
