This section, in brief. Deianira writes to Hercules after hearing that he has conquered Oechalia but yielded to Iole, whom he may take as his wife. She contrasts Hercules's former labours and victories with his humiliation under Iole and recalls his association with Omphale. After Hercules is reportedly dying from the poisoned cloak she sent, Deianira blames herself, resolves to die, and bids farewell to her family and Hercules.
