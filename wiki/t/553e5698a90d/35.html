This section, in brief written by a model
Naso asks Nape to deliver wax tablets to Corinna and ensure that she reads and answers them promptly. Nape is described as skilled in arranging hair, serving during secret nighttime activities, and giving signals, and as someone who has often helped Naso. The message says that Naso lives in hope of a night with Corinna and asks her to come. Naso would dedicate the tablets to Venus if Corinna wrote only “Come!” in reply.
