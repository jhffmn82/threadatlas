This section, in brief. The unit concerns a lover who spends the night begging a janitor to open a barred door so that he may reach his mistress. Love gives the lover courage to move secretly at night, but the janitor repeatedly refuses, and the lover's pleas, threats, and appeals to past gratitude fail. The lover then blames anger and violence against the mistress, regretting that he struck and disfigured her before begging forgiveness. At dawn, he remains remorseful and asks that his own hands be punished.
