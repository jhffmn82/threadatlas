This section, in brief. Ariadne writes to Theseus from the shore where he has abandoned her while she slept. She describes waking alone, seeing Theseus’s ship sail away, and unsuccessfully signaling and calling for him. She recalls aiding Theseus with the thread in the Labyrinth and reproaches him for breaking his promise to remain hers. Alone on the island, Ariadne fears death, burial without rites, and the dangers of the sea, beasts, and strangers.
