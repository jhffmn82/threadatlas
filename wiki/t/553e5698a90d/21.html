This section, in brief. Helen replies to Paris's letter, condemning him for violating hospitality and attempting to win a lawful wife away from her husband. She defends her honor, contrasts her conduct with the assaults of Theseus, and questions whether Paris's love is genuine. Helen acknowledges Paris's beauty and flattering advances but insists that she will preserve her reputation and remain with Menelaus. She also challenges Paris's claims about his ancestry, the Judgment of Paris, and Venus's promise that she would become his bride.
