This section, in brief. Paris writes to Helen, declaring his love and asking her to receive him kindly. He recounts how Venus promised him Helen after he judged Venus, Juno, and Pallas, and how this promise led him to sail from Troy to Lacedaemon. Paris describes seeing Helen, finding her beauty greater than fame had proclaimed, and placing her above the rewards offered by Juno and Pallas. He promises that only death will end his love and asks Helen not to let his hopes fail.
