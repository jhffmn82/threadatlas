This section, in brief. Helen writes to Paris about her attraction to him and her struggle between desire and fear. She warns that Menelaus's absence does not make their affair safe, since his reputation, authority, and suspicions still constrain her. Helen fears public disgrace, retaliation from Sparta and Troy, and the dangers of following Paris to Troy, while Paris's past abandonment of Oenone makes her doubt his constancy. She postpones a secret meeting and ends the letter, leaving further discussion to Clymene and Aethra.
