This section, in brief written by a model
Medea recalls how she used her magic to help Jason win the golden fleece and escape Colchis, abandoning her father, homeland, and family for him. Jason later orders Medea to leave his palace because he is marrying Creusa, and Medea describes her grief at the wedding procession. She declares that Creusa will suffer through the poison and fire Medea has prepared, and that no enemy of Medea will remain unpunished.
