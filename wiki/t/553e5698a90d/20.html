This section, in brief. Paris urges Helen to accept him as her husband, claiming that his Trojan ancestry, wealth, beauty, and martial abilities make him a worthy match for her. He describes his intense love and jealousy, especially when he watches Helen with Menelaus, and asks her to meet him secretly. Paris promises Helen honor and luxury in Troy, argues that her abduction will not necessarily provoke war, and boasts that Troy and Asia can withstand the Greeks. He concludes that either Helen should leave with him peacefully or the Greek forces will yield to his strength.
