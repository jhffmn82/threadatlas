This section, in brief written by a model
Leander of Abydos sends Hero of Sestos a letter because violent winds and rough seas prevent him from swimming to her. He recalls his earlier crossings of the Hellespont, when Hero welcomed him, dried him, and shared the night with him. He compares his love for Hero to a guiding light and vows to attempt the crossing again, even if death ends his troubled love.
