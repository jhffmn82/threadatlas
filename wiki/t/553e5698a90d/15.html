This section, in brief written by a model
Laodamia writes to Protesilaus after his departure for Troy, describing her grief, anxiety, prayers, and longing for his return. She warns him to avoid danger in the Trojan War, especially the prophecy concerning the first Danaan to land at Troy, and urges him to fight cautiously and come home unwounded. She imagines the wives of Troy preparing their husbands for battle and keeps a wax image of Protesilaus as a substitute for him. She vows to follow Protesilaus wherever he is called, whether he survives or not, and closes by urging him to care for himself if he cares for her.
