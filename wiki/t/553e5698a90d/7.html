This section, in brief written by a model
Dido addresses Aeneas as he prepares to leave Carthage for Italy, accusing him of abandoning her and breaking his promises. She recalls how she received Aeneas, gave him power in Carthage, and entered into a love affair with him, but now fears that he will abandon her while she is pregnant. Dido urges him to remain, warning that the sea is dangerous and that faithlessness will bring punishment. She ultimately prepares for suicide, describing the sword in her lap and asking Anna to give her final funeral offerings.
