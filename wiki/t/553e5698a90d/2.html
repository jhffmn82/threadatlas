This section, in brief written by a model
Phyllis writes to Demophoon from Rhodope, complaining that he has not returned after promising to come within a stated time. She recalls how she welcomed him, aided him, gave him gifts and her virginity, and trusted his words, tears, oaths, and promises of marriage. She watches the sea for his ships, imagines excuses for his delay, and fears that he now has another wife. In despair, Phyllis threatens suicide and declares that Demophoon will be named on her tomb as the cause of her death.
