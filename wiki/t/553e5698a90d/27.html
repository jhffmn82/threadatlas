This section, in brief. Acontius writes to Cydippe, urging her to keep the oath she made to marry him after reading his apple-inscription in Diana's temple. He argues that his deception was motivated by love and that Diana witnessed and approved Cydippe's words. He warns that Diana causes Cydippe's recurring illness whenever she tries to break her oath, while also expressing jealousy of anyone who attends her during sickness. Acontius threatens further schemes or even force, but presents himself as willing to endure Cydippe's anger and devoted to winning her as his wife.
