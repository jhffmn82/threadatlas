This section, in brief. Penelope writes to Ulysses, urging him to return and lamenting that he has sent no reply. She recalls her fears during the Trojan War and rejoices that Troy has fallen and Ulysses survived, but his continued absence leaves her uncertain and distressed. Suitors from Dulichium, Samos, and Zacynthus occupy Ulysses's hall, consume his resources, and pressure Penelope to remarry. Penelope remains faithful to Ulysses while protecting Telemachus and caring for the aged Laertes.
