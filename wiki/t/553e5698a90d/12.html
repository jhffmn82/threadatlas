This section, in brief. Canace writes to her brother Macareus while holding a pen in one hand and a sword sent by Aeolus in the other. She recounts their incestuous love, her pregnancy and childbirth, the nurse's failed attempt to conceal the infant, and Aeolus's order that the child be exposed to wild beasts. Aeolus then sends Canace a sword as punishment, and she announces that she will kill herself and asks Macareus to bury her with their infant son.
