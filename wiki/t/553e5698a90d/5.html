This section, in brief. Oenone writes to Paris, recalling their former love and accusing him of abandoning her for Helen. She describes Paris’s departure by ship, his return with Helen, and the grief caused by seeing the new wife cling to him. Oenone argues that Helen’s former abduction and infidelity make her untrustworthy, while Oenone herself has remained faithful. She warns that Paris’s choice will bring war and offers her own healing knowledge, though she cannot cure her love for him.
