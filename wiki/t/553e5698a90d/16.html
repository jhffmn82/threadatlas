This section, in brief. Hypermestra writes to Lynceus from prison after refusing to kill him during the massacre in which her sisters killed their bridegrooms. She describes raising the sword three times, but fear and piety prevented her from striking, so she warned Lynceus to escape before dawn. Danaus discovered that Lynceus alone survived, had Hypermestra dragged away, and imprisoned her. Hypermestra asks Lynceus either to rescue her or to kill and bury her, affirming that she does not repent her piety.
