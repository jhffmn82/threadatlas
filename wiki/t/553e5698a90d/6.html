This section, in brief. Hypsipyle writes to Jason after hearing that he has returned safely from his voyage with the Golden Fleece and has taken Medea as a wife. She recalls their marriage on Lemnos, Jason’s promises, his departure, and the birth of their twin sons. She condemns Medea’s magic and betrayal, contrasts Medea with herself, and urges Jason to return to her and the children. She ends by wishing suffering on Jason and Medea for abandoning her.
