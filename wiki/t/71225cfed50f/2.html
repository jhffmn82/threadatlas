This section, in brief. The unit introduces the open domain question answering (ODQA) task, which answers questions using knowledge from an external corpus such as Wikipedia. It identifies retrieval-augmented models as an effective approach to ODQA. These models use a retriever-reader architecture consisting of a retriever and a reader, with the retriever selecting passages relevant to the questions before the reader processes them.
