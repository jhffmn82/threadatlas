This section, in brief written by a model
This unit introduces REANO, a framework that generates answers from a question, relevant passages, and a latent knowledge graph. REANO models answer generation with a KG Generator, which produces knowledge triples from passages, and an Answer Predictor, which uses the question, passages, and generated knowledge graph. The KG Generator performs entity recognition and relation extraction, with relation extraction divided into intra-context and inter-context components. Intra-context relation extraction uses DocuNet, while inter-context relation extraction uses the Wikidata API to retrieve relations among entities.

Since Gq is unknown, we treat it as a latent variable and rewrite pθ(a|q, Dq) as: pθ(a|q, Dq) = P Gq pθ(a|q, Dq, Gq)pϕ(Gq|Dq), (3) where the answer generation target pθ(a|q, Dq) is jointly modeled by a KG generator pϕ(Gq|Dq) and an answer predictor pθ(a|q, Dq, Gq). The KG gen- erator infers a KG conditioned on the retrieved passages Dq, while the answer predictor generates answers conditioned on the question q, the pas- sages Dq and the generated knowledge graph Gq. Next, we introduce the detailed parameterisation of the KG generator and the answer predictor. 3.2 Parameterisation 3.2.1 KG Generator For each question q, the KG generator pϕ(Gq|Dq) aims to deduce a set of knowledge triples in the form of ⟨head entity, relation, tail entity⟩based on the passages Dq. Following the established prac- tice of KG generation (Ji et al., 2022), we decom- pose this task into two components: entity recog- nition (ER) and relation extraction (RE). The ER focuses on identifying the entities within the pas- sages, while the RE aims to infer the relations among these entities. For the ER, we leverage SpaCy (Honnibal and Montani, 2017) to identify named entities and use TAGME (Ferragina and Scaiella, 2010), an entity linking system, to identify Wikipedia entities. We denote the entities identified within Dq as Eq. An entity e ∈Eq may have mul- tiple occurrences within Dq and we refer to each instance of this entity in the passages as a mention. Subsequently, we infer the relations among enti- ties. Given that current state-of-the-art RE models rely on a transformer backbone (Lu et al., 2022a,b), it is impractical to infer the relations among all the entities simultaneously. This is due to the con- strained maximum input length of these models, as processing all the passages at once would exceed this limit. Therefore, we further decompose the RE component into two sub-modules: intra-context RE and inter-context RE. Intra-Context RE. Intra-context RE focuses on extracting the relations among entities within a sin- gle passage, while inter-context RE aims to extract the relations among entities across passages. The intra-context RE model is defined as: pϕ(T I q |Dq, Eq) = Q di∈Dq pϕ(T I q,di|di, Eq,di), (4) where T I q,di denotes intra-context relation triples among entities Eq,di within a passage di. We in- stantiate the intra-context RE model pϕ with Do- cuNet (Zhang et al., 2021a), an effective RE model capable of predicting relations between every entity pair within a passage in a single forward propaga- tion.
