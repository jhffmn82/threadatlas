Limitation Our RAG2 framework has several limitations. First, our model has only been tested in the biomedical domain, leaving its applicability to general domains unexamined. We plan to explore its performance in various general domains in future research. Second, we used only one size of the Flan-T5 model as the filtering model; experimenting with different sizes or architectures may yield additional improvements. Third, an incorrect rationale from the model might prompt the retriever to gather distracting evidence. Although this risk is limited, as errors generally make up only a small portion of the rationale and our filtering model can discard distractors, a thor- ough analysis and evaluation of this issue remains essential for future work. Lastly, when creating perplexity-based labels, we evaluated each snippet individually, which may overlook the combined impact of multiple relevant snippets. Also, the Flan-T5 model can filter only one snippet at a time due to its limited context length. In future research, we plan to explore both a labeling approach that considers multiple related snippets and new encod- ing methods to overcome the structural limitations of the current filtering model. 12747
