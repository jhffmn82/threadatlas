This section, in brief written by a model
The unit recounts the destruction of Sybaris by the Crotonians and the subsequent founding of Thurium by Sybarite exiles, Athenians, and other Greek colonists. Thurium was initially troubled by conflict between the ancient Sybarite inhabitants and the new colonists, but the new colonists eventually gained control and established a democracy. Charondas was chosen as legislator and created laws concerning family life, false accusation, harmful associations, education, orphans, military service, and the amendment of laws. The unit also describes several successful challenges to Charondas’s laws and his remarkable death by deliberately enforcing his own prohibition against entering the assembly armed.
