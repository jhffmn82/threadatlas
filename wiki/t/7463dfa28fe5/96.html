This section, in brief. Egesta, fearing retaliation from the Syracusans and pressure from the Selinuntines, appealed to Carthage, leading Hannibal to prepare the Carthaginian war in Sicily. At Dardanium, the Athenian fleet defeated the Peloponnesian fleet after Alcibiades appeared with reinforcements, although a storm limited the pursuit. A violent sedition at Corcyra pitted oligarchic supporters of the Lacedaemonians against democrats supported by Athens, but a temporary settlement restored equal privileges. At Cyzicum and Claros, the Athenians defeated Mindarus and the Peloponnesians by coordinated naval and land operations, killing Mindarus and capturing ships and prisoners.
