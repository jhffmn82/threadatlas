This section, in brief. This unit recounts the legendary origins and descendants of the Aeolians, including Arne, Aeolus, Boeotus, Salmoneus, Tyro, Pelias, Neleus, and Nestor. It describes the Lapiths and Centaurs, the conflict between them, and the ancestry of Lapithus, Ixion, Perithous, and Hippodamia. It also follows Asclepius and his sons, the daughters of Asopus and the sons of Aeacus, including Peleus, Telamon, Achilles, and Ajax. Finally, it recounts the stories of Pelops, Tantalus, Oenomaus, Hippodamia, and the Trojan royal line from Ilus and Tros to Priam and Hector.
