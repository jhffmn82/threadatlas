Hercules completes his tenth labour by bringing Cerberus out of hell after being initiated at Eleusis, then undertakes the labour of obtaining the golden apples or sheep of the Hesperides. The account describes Orpheus, Atlas, the Hesperides, and the Amazons, including Theseus’s defeat of the Amazons in Attica. After completing his labours, Hercules establishes a colony in Sardinia through Iolaus and the Thespiadae, serves Omphale, attacks Troy, and conducts further wars against Augeas and the rulers of Sparta. The unit ends with Hercules’s relationship to Auge, the story of Telephus, and his marriage to Deianira after the death of Meleager.
