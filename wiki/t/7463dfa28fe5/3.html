This section, in brief. Diodorus first discusses the barbarians, especially the Egyptians, before turning to the history of the Grecians. The Egyptians are said to have originated the first humans and many living creatures because of Egypt's climate and the fertility of the Nile, and they identify the sun, moon, and five elemental qualities with various gods. The account then describes Osiris and Isis, their reign and civilizing works, the founding or disputed founding of Thebes, and the discoveries associated with agriculture, bread, wine, language, and the arts. Finally, Osiris begins a world expedition with Isis's advisers, Hercules, Apollo, Anubis, Pan, and others to teach cultivation and improve human life.
