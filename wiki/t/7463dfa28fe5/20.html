This section, in brief written by a model
Semiramis is described as undertaking extensive works in Babylon, Media, and throughout Asia before invading India. She prepared a huge army, transportable boats, and mock elephants for the Indian campaign, but Stabrobates and the Indian army defeated her forces after battles on and beyond the Indus. Semiramis escaped across a bridge of boats and returned to Bactria with less than a third of her army. Later, after an assault arranged by her son, she surrendered the crown and disappeared, with some accounts saying she was transformed into a dove.
