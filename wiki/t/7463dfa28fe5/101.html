This section, in brief written by a model
Hermocrates sent the bodies of Syracusans killed at Himera to Syracuse, causing the people to turn against Diocles, who was banished, though Hermocrates was not restored. Hermocrates later attempted to return to Syracuse with three thousand armed men, but the Syracusans killed him and many of his accomplices; Dionysius escaped after being reported dead. Callicratides, appointed admiral by the Lacedaemonians, defeated Conon’s smaller fleet near Mitylene and besieged the city after forcing the Athenians into its inner harbour.
