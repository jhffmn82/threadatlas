The unit describes the causes and opening campaigns of the Peloponnesian war between the Athenians and the Lacedaemonians. Pericles urged Athens to resist the Lacedaemonians, relying on Athenian wealth, allies, and naval strength, while the Lacedaemonians invaded Attica under Archidamus. Fighting occurred at Platea, Potidea, Methone, and Rhion, with Brasidas defending Methone and Phormio defeating the Lacedaemonian fleet. The unit also recounts the plague of Athens, the death and temporary political disgrace of Pericles, the surrender of Potidea, and the later naval victories of the Athenians.
