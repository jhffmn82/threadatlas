The unit describes Libya, its peoples, government, warfare, and contrasting landscapes. It identifies the Nasomones, Auchises, Marmarides, and Macs, including farmers, herdsmen, and independent raiders. Cyrene is fertile, while the southern regions are barren, sandy, and dangerous because of venomous serpents. The text also explains reports of enormous air apparitions in the deserts and near the Syrtes as condensed air shaped and moved by surrounding bodies.
