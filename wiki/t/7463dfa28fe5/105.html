This section, in brief. The Athenians defeated the Lacedaemonians in the great naval battle of Arginusae, though a storm prevented the recovery and burial of many dead. Callicratides was killed while fighting bravely, and the Peloponnesian fleet was routed. The Athenians later condemned eleven commanders to death for failing to bury the dead, including Diomedon, despite their victory. Sophocles died at about ninety years of age, after winning eighteen dramatic victories.
