This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the confederate war against the Lacedaemonians, including the battles at Aricas, the river Nemea, and Coronea, and the Persian fleet's victory over the Lacedaemonian fleet at Cnidus under Conon and Pharnabazus. It recounts the weakening of Lacedaemonian power, the rebuilding of Athens and the Piraeus walls, and the revolts of several cities against Lacedaemon. It also covers the Corinthian War and sedition, including fighting at Lechaeum and the actions of Iphicrates. The later section concerns Dionysius's conflicts with Rhegium and the Sicilians, Mago's campaign in Sicily, and the Carthaginian defeat at Abacene.
