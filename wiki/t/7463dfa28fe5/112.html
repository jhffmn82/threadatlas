Thrasybulus and the Athenian exiles overthrew the Thirty Tyrants, took the Piraeus, and helped restore Athens to its ancient laws, while opponents were allowed to withdraw to Eleusis. Sparta intervened through Pausanias, reconciling the exiles and citizens, while Lysander had been summoned by the new Athenian decemvirs. In Asia, Tissaphernes threatened the Greek cities, prompting Sparta to send Thimbron and later Dercyllidas against the Persian king; Pharnabazus and Tissaphernes meanwhile helped organize a Persian fleet under Conon. The unit also recounts Psammetichus's murder of Tamos, Xenophon's campaign against the Thracians, Socrates' trial and execution, and the fortification of the Chersonesus of Thrace.
