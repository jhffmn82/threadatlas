The unit reports what the Atlantides and Phrygians say about the genealogy of the gods and the origins of their worship. Uranus and Titea are presented as the parents of the Titans, including Basilea, Hyperion, and other divine ancestors. It recounts the deaths and deification of Helios and Selene, the history of Cybele and Atys, and the contest between Marsyas and Apollo. It also describes the Phrygian worship of Cybele and the division of the kingdom between Atlas and Saturn after Hyperion's death.
