This section, in brief. Polyxenus brought thirty allied warships to Syracuse, and the Syracusans won a naval victory over the Carthaginians while Dionysius was absent. Theodorus then urged the assembly to depose Dionysius and restore Syracusan laws and liberty, but Pharacides opposed the proposal and the mercenary soldiers rallied around Dionysius. A plague devastated the Carthaginian army, after which Dionysius launched a surprise attack that captured forts and destroyed many Carthaginian ships. The Syracusans celebrated the victory, while Dionysius encamped near the Carthaginians at the temple of Jupiter.
