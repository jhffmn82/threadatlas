This section, in brief written by a model
The unit examines the Nile, its headwaters, course, and annual overflowing, reviewing and criticizing explanations offered by ancient writers. Herodotus, Ephorus, Anaxagoras, Democritus, the philosophers of Memphis, and Agartharchides of Cnidus are among those whose theories are discussed. The account rejects explanations based on the Etesian winds, Ethiopian snow, Egypt’s underground waters, and an opposite inhabited zone. Agartharchides’ explanation, that continual summer rains fall in the mountainous parts of Ethiopia, is presented as coming nearest to the truth.
