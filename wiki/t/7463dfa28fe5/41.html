This section, in brief. The unit recounts the birth, upbringing, and early exploits of Hercules, son of Jupiter and Alcmena, including Juno's attempts to hinder him. Hercules frees Thebes from the tribute imposed by Erginus, marries Megara, and is later compelled by Eurystheus to undertake twelve labours. He kills the Nemean lion, destroys the Hydra of Lerna, captures the Erymanthian boar, defeats the centaurs, and completes several further tasks. He also assists the gods against the giants, frees Prometheus, and defeats the Amazons to obtain Hippolyta's belt.
