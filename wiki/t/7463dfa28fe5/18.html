This section, in brief written by a model
Book II begins its account of ancient Asia with Ninus, the first Assyrian king recorded in history. Ninus conquers Babylon, Armenia, Media, and many other Asian countries, builds Nineveh beside the Euphrates, and later invades Bactria. Semiramis, whose reported origins are connected with Derceto and pigeons, helps capture Bactria and becomes queen after her husband Menon dies and Ninus later dies. Ninus leaves Semiramis as regent for their son Ninyas.
