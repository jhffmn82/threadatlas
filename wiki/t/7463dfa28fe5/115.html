Dionysius retreated toward Syracuse as Himilco led a large Carthaginian force through Sicily, capturing and destroying Messana and recovering Motya. The Carthaginians defeated Dionysius's fleet in the naval battle near Catana, sinking or capturing more than one hundred Sicilian vessels and killing more than twenty thousand men. Himilco then brought his army and fleet to Syracuse, where he ravaged the surrounding country, built forts, and began a siege. Disease later spread among the Carthaginians, while Dionysius strengthened Syracuse and sought aid from the Greeks.
