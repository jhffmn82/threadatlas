This section, in brief. After Hercules's translation to the gods, the Heraclidae dwelt at Trachina with Ceyx until Eurystheus forced them to leave. The Athenians received them at Tricorythus, where they later defeated Eurystheus and his army, though Hyllus was subsequently killed in single combat with Echemus. The Heraclidae postponed their return to Peloponnesus for fifty years, while Licymnius, his children, and Tlepolemus settled in Argos. Tlepolemus later fled to Rhodes, divided the island into three parts, founded Lyndus, Jalysus, and Camirus, and assisted Agamemnon at Troy.
