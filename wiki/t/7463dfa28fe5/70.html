This section, in brief. After the Persian fleet withdrew from Salamis to Samos, the Athenians rejected Persian offers and reaffirmed their alliance with the Greeks. Mardonius invaded Attica, forcing the Athenians to evacuate to Salamis, and destroyed Athens and its remaining temples. The Greek confederacy assembled at Plataea under Pausanias and Aristides, defeated the larger Persian army, killed Mardonius, and drove the Persians from Greece. The Greeks buried their dead, distributed the spoils, honored Pausanias, and later punished the Theban leaders who had supported the Persians.
