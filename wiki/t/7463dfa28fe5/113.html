This section, in brief. Dionysius made peace with the Rhegians and Messanians, then prepared a large-scale war against the Carthaginians by building ships, weapons, armor, artillery engines, and military facilities. He secured support from many Sicilian cities, married Doris of Locris and Aristomache of Syracuse, and persuaded the Syracusans to approve war against Carthage. Dionysius declared that the Carthaginians should restore the Greek cities they had subdued and then marched against Motya, while most Sicilian cities submitted to him. The Carthaginians, weakened by a plague, sent a night raid that destroyed most of the ships in Syracuse's harbor, while Dionysius continued the siege of Motya.
