This section, in brief. Deucetius, general of the Siculi, captured Aetna and then invaded the country of the Agrigentines, taking Motya after defeating forces sent to relieve it. The Syracusans later defeated Deucetius in battle, while the Agrigentines retook Motya and joined the Syracusans. Deucetius fled to Syracuse and submitted himself and his country as a suppliant. The Syracusans spared him and sent him to Corinth, promising to provide for his needs.
