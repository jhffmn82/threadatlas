This section, in brief. A war arose in Sicily between the Syracusans and the Agrigentines after the Syracusans pardoned Deucetius and sent him to lead a private life at Corinth without consulting the Agrigentines. Deucetius later returned to Sicily to establish a colony, and some Siculi joined him. The Siculi divided between the two sides, and the Syracusans and Agrigentines raised large armies. The Syracusans defeated the Agrigentines at the river Himera, killing a thousand of them, after which the Agrigentines sent ambassadors to Syracuse to seek peace.
