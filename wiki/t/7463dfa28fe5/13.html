This section, in brief. The unit describes Egyptian laws and customs, including capital punishments, military discipline, commercial regulations, theft, marriage, child-rearing, education, astrology, and medicine. Egyptian laws punish perjury, murder, false accusation, rape, adultery, forgery, treason, and other offenses with penalties ranging from disgrace and scourging to death or mutilation. The text also explains that Egyptian priests teach sacred and ordinary subjects, observe the stars, and prescribe medical treatments, while the common people learn trades from their parents or kindred. It attributes some Egyptian practices and laws to influence on Solon and the Grecians.
