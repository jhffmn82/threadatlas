This section, in brief. After Pelias destroyed Jason’s whole family, Medea devised a stratagem that enabled Jason and the Argonauts to enter the palace and overthrow him. Disguised as an old woman and pretending to be an agent of Diana, Medea persuaded Pelias’s daughters that their father could be restored to youth, leading to Pelias’s death. Jason installed Acastus on the throne and later lived with Medea in Corinth, where he abandoned her to marry Glauce, prompting Medea to burn the palace and murder most of their children. The unit also recounts Hercules’s institution of the Olympic Games, the Argonauts’ dedication of the Argo to Neptune, and several traditions about their return after obtaining the golden fleece.
