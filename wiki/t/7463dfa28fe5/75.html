The unit describes Athenian wars against Aegina, Corinth, Epidaurus, and other opponents, along with the growth of Athenian imperial domination and resistance among its confederates. It recounts the Egyptian revolt from Persia, Athenian aid to Egypt, the Persian expedition, the siege of the White Wall, and the eventual withdrawal of the Athenians from Egypt. It also details civil conflict and political restoration in Sicily, including the struggles at Syracuse, Catana, and other cities. Finally, it covers wars involving Corinth, Megara, the Phocians, the Dorians, and the Lacedaemonians, culminating in the Battle of Tanagra.
