This section, in brief. After the Peloponnesian War, the Lacedaemonians imposed oligarchies and tribute on many Greek cities and restored Lysander as high admiral. Dionysius strengthened his tyranny at Syracuse, while Alcibiades was killed after Pharnabazus revealed his plans to Artaxerxes. Clearchus established a brutal tyranny in Byzantium, was defeated by the Lacedaemonians at Porus, and later became a general for Cyrus. The Thebans subdued the Oropians, the Lacedaemonians invaded the Elei under Pausanias, and Dionysius planned war against Carthage while fortifying Epipolae.
