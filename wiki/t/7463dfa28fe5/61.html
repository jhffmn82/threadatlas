This section, in brief. The unit recounts the legendary origins and later settlements of Rhodes, its inhabitants, and the neighboring Chersonesus. It describes the Telchines, Neptune, Rhode, Jupiter, Sol, and the Heliades, including floods, divine punishments, migrations, and the development of arts and astrology. It also explains the division of Rhodes among Lindus, Ialysus, and Camirus, and recounts the activities of Triopas in Caria and Chersonesus. The unit concludes with the story of Hemithea and the fame of her healing temple at Castabus.
