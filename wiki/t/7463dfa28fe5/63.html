This section, in brief. The unit describes traditions concerning the Cretan gods, heroes, rulers, and the origins of Crete and neighboring islands. Apollo, Hercules, Diana, Ceres, Jupiter, and Europa are associated with divine ancestry, inventions, travels, or worship. It recounts how Minos and Rhadamanthus ruled Crete and extended their influence across islands and Asian coasts. It also narrates the settlement of Lesbos, the activities of Macareus, the origins of the Cyclades, and the later expansion of the Greeks.
