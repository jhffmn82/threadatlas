This section, in brief written by a model
Cimon led the Athenians in campaigns against the Persians, capturing cities in Asia and winning victories at Cyprus and Eurymedon by sea and land. A devastating earthquake struck Sparta, after which the Helots and Messenians revolted and besieged Ithome, while the Argives destroyed Mycenae. In Sicily, Hiero died, Thrasybulus was overthrown by a Syracusan revolt, and democracy was restored in Syracuse. Artabanus arranged the murder of Xerxes, but Artaxerxes killed Artabanus and became king of Persia.
