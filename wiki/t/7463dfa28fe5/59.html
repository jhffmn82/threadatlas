The unit describes the islands in the Arabian ocean near Arabia, especially the Holy Island and Panchsea, including their products, government, religious institutions, economy, and legendary history. It then turns to the Aegean islands near Greece, beginning with Samothracia and its traditions concerning an ancient flood, Saon, and the island’s sacred mysteries. The account connects Samothracia with Jupiter, Dardanus, Jasion, Harmonia, Cadmus, Cybele, Ceres, and the gods associated with initiation. It also describes the marriage of Cadmus and Harmonia and the spread of religious rites from Samothracia to other places.
