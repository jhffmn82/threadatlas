This section, in brief. After Cyrus was killed and Artaxerxes's forces won the battle, Clearchus and the Greek army refused to surrender their arms and arranged a retreat toward the sea. Artaxerxes granted them a truce, but Tissaphernes later treacherously seized and killed their commanders, after which the Greeks elected Chirosophus as their general. The Greeks fought and suffered through the country of the Carduchi, Armenia, and other regions before reaching Trapezus and eventually Chrysopolis. The unit concludes with the hardships and progress of the Greek retreat, including the Armenian snowstorm and the honey poisoning.
