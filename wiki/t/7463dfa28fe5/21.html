This section, in brief written by a model
After Semiramis's death, Ninyas succeeded her, and the Assyrian kings thereafter lived in seclusion and luxury until the reign of Sardanapalus. Sardanapalus's slothful and corrupt life provoked Arbaces the Mede and Belesis to organize a revolt with the Medes, Persians, Babylonians, and an Arabian king. After several defeats, the rebels gained strength when the Bactrian army joined them, defeated Sardanapalus's forces, and besieged Nineveh. When the Euphrates destroyed part of Nineveh's wall, Sardanapalus burned himself with his treasure, eunuchs, and concubines, and Arbaces became king as the Assyrian empire was overthrown.
