This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes Celtica and Gaul, their peoples, geography, resources, customs, warfare, religion, and relations with neighboring nations and Rome. It recounts that Hercules visited Celtica, built Alesia, and fathered Galates, whose name was said to have given rise to Galatians and Gaul. The Gauls are portrayed as tall and warlike, skilled in combat, devoted to gold and imported wine, and guided in religious and political matters by Druids, Bards, and prophets. The unit also describes human sacrifice, including the killing of prisoners and captives as offerings to the gods.
