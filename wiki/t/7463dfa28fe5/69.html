This section, in brief written by a model
The Carthaginians invaded Sicily under Amilcar and besieged Himera, but Gelon of Syracuse defeated them through a stratagem that killed Amilcar and burned their fleet. The Carthaginian army suffered massive losses, while many survivors surrendered after being deprived of water. Gelon received widespread praise, treated the defeated Sicilians and Carthaginians generously, and made peace with Carthage in exchange for tribute and the construction of two temples. The victory at Himera was said to have occurred on the same day that Leonidas and the Greeks fought at Thermopylae during the Persian invasion of Greece.
