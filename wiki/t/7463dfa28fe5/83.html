This section, in brief written by a model
The Samians went to war with the Milesians over Priene and revolted from Athens, but Pericles captured Samos, imposed a fine, and took hostages. A later sedition in Samos enabled the aristocratic faction, aided by Pisuthnes and Persian soldiers, to seize the city and renew the war; after naval battles and a prolonged siege, Pericles retook Samos, punished the revolt's leaders, and restored democracy. In Sicily, the Syracusans attacked Trinacria, defeated and destroyed the city, enslaved its inhabitants, and sent the richest spoils to Delphi.
