Sitalces of Thrace invaded Macedonia with a large army to support the Athenians, restore Amyntas, and oppose Perdiccas, but he later made peace with Perdiccas and withdrew. The Athenians sent a major expedition to Sicily under Lachetes, Chabrias, Eurymedon, and Sophocles, but returned after the Leontines made peace with the Syracusans. Lesbos revolted from Athens, and the Athenians besieged and captured Mitylene before sparing its inhabitants and dividing most of Lesbos among themselves. The Lacedaemonians captured Plataea and killed its defenders, while plague and famine continued to afflict Athens.
