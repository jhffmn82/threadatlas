Diocles established laws for the Syracusans, and his strict Dioclean laws became influential throughout Sicily. After the Athenians suffered setbacks in Sicily, Athens replaced its democracy with the rule of the four hundred, but later restored democracy through the efforts of Theramenes. The Athenians defeated the Lacedaemonians in the naval battle between Sestos and Abydos, while Alcibiades helped prevent Persian support for the Lacedaemonians and secured his recall. The unit also describes the end of the Peloponnesian War, the point at which Thucydides ended his history, and subsequent events involving Athens, Sparta, and their allies.
