The Thebans, supported by the Lacedaemonians, sought to recover control of Boeotia, but Myronides led the Athenians to victory over them and then campaigned through Boeotia, Locris, Phocis, and Thessaly. Tolmides later invaded Laconia and attacked Spartan possessions, while Pericles conducted further Athenian campaigns in Peloponnesus and Acarnania. In Sicily, the Syracusans adopted Petalism, an olive-leaf form of ostracism, but soon abolished it after it encouraged disorder and drove useful citizens from public life. Deucetius united the Siculi, founded Palicon near the temple of the Palici, and the text describes the temple’s extraordinary Craters and sacred functions.
