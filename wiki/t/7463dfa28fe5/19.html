This section, in brief written by a model
Semiramis undertakes the construction and adornment of Babylon, assembling two million workers and completing its principal works with architects and artists. The city is built around the Euphrates and enclosed by a massive wall with turrets, while a bridge, embankments, palaces, a vaulted passage, and a temple are added. The unit also describes Babylon’s hanging garden, later attributed to Cyrus, and the commercial cities Semiramis established along the Euphrates and Tigris. Over time, the palaces and other structures decay, and much of Babylon becomes farmland and pasture.
