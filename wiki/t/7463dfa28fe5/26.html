This section, in brief. The unit recounts how Lambulus, a learned merchant, was captured and sent with another captive on a southward voyage to a fortunate island as part of an Ethiopian purification ritual. The island and six neighboring islands are described as fertile places inhabited by unusual, long-lived people with distinctive bodies, language, laws, social organization, and customs. After living there for seven years, Lambulus and his companion were expelled; the companion drowned near India, while Lambulus eventually reached Greece and wrote about his adventures.
