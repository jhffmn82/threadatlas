This section, in brief written by a model
The Carthaginians besieged Agrigentum in Sicily after the Agrigentines rejected offers of alliance or neutrality. Under Amilcar and Hannibal, they attacked the city, but Syracusan forces under Daphnaeus defeated the Carthaginian army at the Battle of the Himera and relieved the siege temporarily. A Carthaginian naval attack then destroyed the Syracusan grain fleet, after which the besieged Agrigentines suffered famine, evacuated, and abandoned many of their sick and elderly. Amilcar entered and sacked Agrigentum, killing many inhabitants and carrying its immense wealth and artworks to Carthage.
