This section, in brief. The unit concerns Orion, described as the biggest and strongest of the heroes and a great hunter who performed notable deeds. He is said to have made the harbour of Acte for Zanclus, from whom Zancle, later Messana and now Messina, took its ancient name. The text discusses traditions about Sicily becoming an island, including the formation of Rhegium, and reports that Orion raised the promontory Pelorus and built a temple to Neptune there. Orion later sailed to Euboea, and his fame was preserved by placing him among the stars.
