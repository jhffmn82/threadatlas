This section, in brief. The Lacedemonians invaded Attica under Agis and fortified Decelea, while the Athenians sent forces toward Laconia and Sicily. The Syracusans and Athenians fought a sea-fight in which the Athenians initially sank eleven Syracusan ships but also lost their forts on land. After Demosthenes was expected to arrive with a fleet, the Syracusans provoked another battle in the Great Haven and defeated the Athenians, sinking seven of their galleys and disabling many more. The Syracusans benefited from stronger, lower-built forecastles, while the Athenians were hindered by the narrow fighting space.
