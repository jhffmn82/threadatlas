This section, in brief. The unit recounts how Xerxes’ invasion of Greece threatened Greek freedom but ultimately brought Greece wealth, renown, and cultural flourishing. Cimon led an Athenian expedition against Cyprus, defeated Persian forces, and helped secure the Peace of Callias, under which the Greek cities in Asia were freed. After Cimon died in Cyprus, the Megareans revolted from Athens, and the Athenians later suffered defeat at Chaeronea, forcing them to restore Boeotian cities to their ancient liberties. Further revolts and Pericles’ campaign in Euboea led to a thirty-year peace.
