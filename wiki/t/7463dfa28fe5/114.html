This section, in brief. Himiico sailed from Carthage with one hundred galleys and attacked Dionysius's ships at Motya, destroying some and burning others, but he withdrew when he could not defeat the larger Syracusan force. Dionysius completed a rampart and used battering engines and tall siege towers to breach Motya's walls. After a prolonged assault, his army entered the city by night and overcame the Motyans. The Sicilians massacred many inhabitants and plundered the city, while Dionysius sold surviving citizens into slavery, except for certain prisoners whom he ordered to be executed.
