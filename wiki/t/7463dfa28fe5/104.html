This section, in brief written by a model
After Agrigentum fell to the Carthaginians, panic spread through Sicily and Syracuse. Dionysius, the son of Hermocrates, accused Syracuse's commanders of treachery, gained command, secured the recall of the exiles, and used the crisis to increase his influence. He obtained sole command, raised a personal guard and mercenary force, and eventually declared himself king of Syracuse. The Syracusans feared tyranny but were unable to resist him while the Carthaginians threatened the city.
