This section, in brief. The unit recounts Meleager's participation in the Calydonian boar hunt, his gift of the boar's skin to Atalanta, and his subsequent death after killing the sons of Thestius, which led Althea to curse him. It then describes Hercules's works in Calydonia, including diverting the river Achelous, fighting for the Calydonians, and later leaving the region after accidentally killing Eurynonius. Hercules's later conflicts, marriage-related affairs, and death from a poisoned garment are narrated, along with Dejanira's suicide and Hercules's translation to the gods. The unit ends by stating that Hercules went with the Argonauts and that their expedition will be discussed next.
