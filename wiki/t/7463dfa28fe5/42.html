This section, in brief. Eurystheus imposed upon Hercules the tenth labour of driving away the oxen of Gerion from Iberia. Hercules assembled a fleet and army, defeated the sons of Chrysaor, and took the herds. He then travelled through Libya, Iberia, Gaul, Italy, and Sicily, where he performed various feats, founded or improved places, and received honours and sacrifices.
