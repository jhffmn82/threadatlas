This section, in brief. Dionysius of Syracuse led expeditions into Italy, first suffering a naval defeat near Rhegium and later defeating the Greek forces at the battle at the river Helorus before besieging Rhegium. The Lucanians defeated the Thurians after the Thurians invaded Lucania without waiting for their confederates, but Leptines rescued about a thousand survivors and helped establish peace. Dionysius besieged Caulonia, destroyed the city, and transported its inhabitants to Syracuse. He later pursued the siege of Rhegium and sent lavish chariots, tents, and poems to the Olympic games, where his poetry was ridiculed.
