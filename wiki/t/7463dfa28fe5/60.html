This section, in brief. The unit recounts the early history of Naxus, formerly called Strongyle, beginning with the settlement of the Thracians under Butes. After the Thracians were expelled by Otus and Ephialtes, the island was called Dia until the Carians, led by King Naxus, renamed it after him. It also describes Theseus leaving Ariadne on Naxus after a vision of Bacchus, and the Naxians’ traditions about Bacchus’s upbringing and favor toward their island. The account concludes by describing the first inhabitants of Syme.
