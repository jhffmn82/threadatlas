This section, in brief. Jason, son of Æson and nephew of Pelias, leads an expedition of Argonauts aboard the Argo to Colchis to obtain the Golden Fleece. The Argonauts encounter Hesione, Phineus and his sons, and the hostile peoples of Pontus and Taurica before reaching Colchis. Medea, daughter of Æetes, helps Jason and the Argonauts escape, including by poisoning the dragon guarding the fleece, and Jason promises to marry her. After seizing the fleece, the Argonauts defeat pursuing Colchians and begin their return voyage, during which Glaucus foretells honors and future events.
