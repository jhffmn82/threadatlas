This section, in brief. Diodorus Siculus describes Egypt’s natural boundaries, population, cities, and resources. He explains the course of the Nile, its cataracts, islands, channels, mouths, and the formation of the Delta. The unit also discusses Egyptian plants, animals such as the crocodile and river horse, and the Nile’s annual inundation. It presents the inundation as the basis of Egypt’s fertile soil, abundant agriculture, and prosperity.
