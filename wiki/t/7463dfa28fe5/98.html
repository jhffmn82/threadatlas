This section, in brief written by a model
Hannibal led a large Carthaginian army from Spain and Africa to Sicily, captured Selinus after a prolonged siege, and destroyed the city. The Syracusans and other Sicilian allies prepared to aid Selinus but arrived too late, while Agrigentum received thousands of Selinuntine refugees. Hannibal then besieged and captured Himera, defeating its defenders and destroying the city after the Syracusan forces withdrew. He returned to Carthage with prisoners and abundant booty, and the people received him with great acclaim.
