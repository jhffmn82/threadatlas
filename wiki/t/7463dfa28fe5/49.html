This section, in brief. This unit recounts Theseus’s journey to Athens, his defeat of several dangerous men and creatures, and his killing of the Minotaur in Crete with Ariadne’s assistance. It explains how Minos demanded that the Athenians send boys and girls as tribute to be devoured by the Minotaur because of the murder of his son Androgeus. Theseus returned with black sails after leaving Ariadne, causing Aegeus to believe that he had died and to kill himself. The account also describes Theseus’s later rule, his conflict involving Phaedra and Hippolytus, his exile and death, and his participation with Perithous in the abduction of Helen and the attempt to seize Proserpina.
