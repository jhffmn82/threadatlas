This section, in brief written by a model
The unit recounts the causes and course of the war against Thebes, beginning with Laius's attempt to avert the oracle's prophecy and Oedipus's unwitting fulfillment of it. After Oedipus solves the Sphinx's riddle and becomes king, his sons Eteocles and Polynices quarrel over the succession, leading Polynices and Adrastus to gather the seven captains against Thebes. The expedition fails, with Eteocles, Polynices, Capaneus, Amphiaraus, Hippomedon, and Parthenopaeus among the dead, while Adrastus survives and the Athenian burial of the fallen is recorded. The Epigoni, led by Alcmaeon, later defeat Thebes, compel the Cadmeans to abandon the city, and destroy it; the unit also describes Tiresias's death and the subsequent movements of the Cadmeans and Dorians.
