This section, in brief. The unit concerns the conflict between the Athenians and the Lacedaemonians during the Peloponnesian War, especially the struggle over Scione and other cities in Thrace. Nicias and Nicostratus besiege Scione, while Cleon later campaigns against Torone and advances toward Amphipolis. At the battle near Amphipolis, both Cleon and Brasidas are killed, and the Lacedaemonians win. The Athenians and Lacedaemonians then make peace, ending the ten-year war.
