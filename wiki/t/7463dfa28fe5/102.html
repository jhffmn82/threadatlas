This section, in brief. The Carthaginians prepared a large invasion of Sicily after earlier successes, building Therma and assembling troops, warships, and transport vessels under Hannibal and Amilcar. A Syracusan naval force defeated part of the Carthaginian fleet at Eryx, but Hannibal then sailed toward Sicily to protect the army’s transport. The Syracusans sought aid from the Greeks in Italy and the Lacedaemonians, while the Agrigentines gathered their food and valuables inside Agrigentum. The unit describes Agrigentum’s wealth, temples, public works, lavish customs, and prominent citizens, especially Gellias, before noting the city’s vulnerability and the luxury of its inhabitants during the siege.
