This section, in brief written by a model
The Decemviri's abuse of power in Rome led to the young woman's death and provoked the soldiers at Algidum to seize the Aventine mount. The crisis was settled by creating ten tribunes of the people with broad authority, requiring that one consul come from the senators and the other from the common people, and allowing both consuls to be chosen from the common people if desired. Marcus Horatius and Lucius Valerius Potitus were then made consuls and completed the Roman laws by adding the final two of the twelve tables. The period ended with peace in Rome and throughout much of the known world.
