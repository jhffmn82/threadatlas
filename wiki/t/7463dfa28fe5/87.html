This section, in brief. The unit concerns the continuing war between the Athenians and the Lacedaemonians, including Demosthenes’s campaigns in western Greece and the fortification and siege of Pylos. The Spartans and their allies failed to take Pylos, and the Athenians captured the Spartan and allied soldiers trapped on the Sphacterian island. Nicias later led Athenian operations against Melos, Boeotia, Corinth, Cythera, and the Peloponnesian coast, while Brasidas expanded Lacedaemonian influence in Megara, Thrace, and Amphipolis. The Boeotians defeated the Athenians at the Battle of Delium and subsequently captured Delium.
