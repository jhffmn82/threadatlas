This section, in brief written by a model
The Lacedaemonians, exhausted by war, made peace with Artaxerxes through Antalcidas, on terms requiring the Greek cities in Asia to submit and the other Grecians to govern themselves. Artaxerxes then prepared a Cyprian War against Euagoras, while Dionysius captured Rhegium after a prolonged famine and cruelly punished Philo and his son. The Gauls invaded Italy, defeated the Romans at the river Allia, and sacked Rome, forcing the Romans to take refuge in the Capitol and Veii. Marcus Furius later defeated the Volsci and Gauls, while Marcus Manlius helped repel a Gallic attempt to enter the Capitol.
