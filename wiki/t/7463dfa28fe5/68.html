This section, in brief. Xerxes captured the passes after Thermopylae and sent the Persian fleet against the Grecians, but storms and fighting at Artemisium reduced Persian strength. Themistocles advised the Grecians to fight in confined waters, first at Artemisium and later at Salamis, where the Greek fleet defeated the Persian fleet. After Athens was burned and the Persian army devastated parts of Greece, Themistocles used false reports about the bridge to persuade Xerxes to withdraw to Asia, leaving Mardonius with a large army.
