This section, in brief. The unit describes coastal and inland regions of Arabia, their peoples, resources, hazards, ports, islands, and unusual natural phenomena. It recounts how the Garyndaneans took the palm country from the Mammecans and how the Nabathcans became pirates in the Red Sea before being defeated. Carmutha is presented as an exceptionally safe and commodious harbor, while Arabia the Happy is praised for its fertile land, abundant wealth, cattle, gold, and fragrant plants. The Sabeans, whose capital is Saba, are portrayed as prosperous and ruled by kings subject to strict ancient customs.
