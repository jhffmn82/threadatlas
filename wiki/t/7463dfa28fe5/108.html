This section, in brief. After the Peloponnesian War, Athens obtained peace with the Lacedaemonians on condition that it demolish its walls and govern according to its own laws. Under pressure from Lysander, the Athenians abolished their democracy and appointed the Thirty, who established an oligarchic tyranny, executed Theramenes, confiscated property, and drove many citizens into exile. The Lacedaemonians supported the Thirty and ordered other Greek cities to return Athenian fugitives. In Sicily, Dionysius fortified Syracuse, but the Syracusans revolted and besieged him until Campanian forces enabled him to defeat and disperse them.
