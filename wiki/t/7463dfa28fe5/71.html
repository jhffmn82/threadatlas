This section, in brief written by a model
Leotychides the Lacedaemonian and Xantippus the Athenian led the Greek fleet from Samos toward Mycale to help the Asian Greeks regain their liberty. The Persian fleet withdrew to shore, fortified its camp, and summoned a large land force, but the Persians were defeated after the Samians, Milesians, and other Asian peoples joined the battle. The victory at Mycale, occurring on the same day as the Greek victory at Plataea, resulted in the slaughter or dispersal of the Persian army. Afterward, the Ionians and Eolians were received as Greek confederates, but they ultimately remained in Asia, while the Athenians captured Sestos before returning to Athens.
