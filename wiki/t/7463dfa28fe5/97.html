This section, in brief written by a model
After learning of their rout at Cyzicum, the Spartans sent ambassadors to Athens to negotiate peace, with Endius as their chief representative. Endius proposed retaining the cities each side had gained, dismissing all garrisons, and exchanging prisoners one Athenian for one Lacedaemonian. He argued that the war harmed both sides but weakened the Athenians more, while also appealing to the Spartans' customary concern about bloodshed. Although moderate Athenians favored peace, Cleophon persuaded the assembly to reject it by magnifying the Athenians' recent victories, and the Athenians remained confident that they would soon recover their former reputation.
