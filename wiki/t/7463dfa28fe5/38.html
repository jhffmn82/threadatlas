It recounts the first Bacchus’s education, inventions, wars against Saturn and the Titans, worldwide benefactions, and eventual worship as a god. It also describes the roles of Rhea, Saturn, Jupiter, Aristaeus, Minerva, the Amazons, and others in these events. Finally, it identifies the second Bacchus as the son of Jupiter and Io and the third as the son of Jupiter and Semele.
