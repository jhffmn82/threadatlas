This section, in brief written by a model
Agesilaus led the Lacedaemonians into Asia, campaigning from Ephesus through the Persian territories and defeating Tissaphernes near Sipylus before returning to the coast. Egypt sent supplies rather than soldiers, while Pharax and the Lacedaemonian fleet failed to take Caunus and the Rhodians revolted and received Conon. Artaxerxes ordered Tithraustes to seize and execute Tissaphernes, after which Tithraustes made a six-month truce with Agesilaus. In Greece, the Phocians and Lacedaemonians fought the Boeotians and Athenians at Haliartus, where Lysander was killed; Conon then went to Babylon and secured Persian support to ruin the Lacedaemonian fleet.
