This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the adoration and worshipping of beasts among the Egyptians, including their feeding, care, burial, and protection of sacred animals. Egyptians devote land and great expense to animals such as cats, dogs, kites, ibises, crocodiles, wolves, and the sacred bulls Apis and Mnevis, and punish those who kill them. It recounts the worship of Apis at Memphis and explains several Egyptian accounts for animal worship, including usefulness, military standards, divine transformations, and associations with Osiris and Isis. The text also reports that a Roman was killed by a mob after accidentally killing a cat.
