This section, in brief. Amilcar the Carthaginian sacked Agrigentum, devastated the country around Gela and Camarina, and besieged Gela. Dionysius brought an army to relieve Gela, but his forces were defeated and he withdrew, later ordering the inhabitants of Camarina to remove to Syracuse. The resulting anger led the Italians to leave his camp and the Syracusans to revolt, though Dionysius quickly returned to Syracuse and suppressed the rebellion. Amilcar then made peace with Dionysius, leaving several Sicilian cities under Carthaginian power or tribute, and the first Carthaginian war against Dionysius ended.
