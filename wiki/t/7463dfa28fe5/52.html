This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns Daedalus, his inventions and works in Crete and Sicily, and his escape from Minos after helping Pasiphae approach the bull and building the labyrinth. It recounts Minos's invasion of Sicily and death at the hands of Cocalus, followed by the settlement of Cretans and the building of the temple of the Curetes or Corybantes. It also relates the genealogy and deeds of Aristaeus, the history of Eryx and Venus Erycina's temple, and the story of Daphnis among the Berei Mountains.
