Sesostris the Second succeeded his father but accomplished nothing notable in war and became blind, supposedly because of impiety toward the Nile; after ten years he was cured through an oracle's instructions and erected two obelisks. Actisanes, king of Ethiopia, conquered the tyrannical Ammosis and ruled Egypt moderately, while later Egyptian kings included Mendes, Proteus, Remphis, Chemmis, Cephres, Mycerinus, Bocchoris, and Sabacos. Chemmis built the Great Pyramid, Cephres built the second pyramid, and Mycerinus began the third pyramid. After Sabacos abandoned the throne, twelve Egyptian nobles ruled jointly until Psammetichus defeated the others with foreign troops; more than two hundred thousand Egyptian soldiers then migrated to Ethiopia after being dishonored.
