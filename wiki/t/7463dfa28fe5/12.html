This section, in brief. The unit describes the laws, customs, government, social classes, and judicial system of ancient Egypt, as reported from Egyptian priestly records. Egyptian kings were expected to obey laws in public and private life, follow a regulated daily schedule and diet, and govern with justice. The Egyptians divided land and society among priests, the king, soldiers, husbandmen, shepherds, and artificers, while assigning trades by inherited occupation. It also describes royal mourning and funerals, the organization of courts, and a written legal procedure intended to prevent rhetoric or influence from obscuring justice.
