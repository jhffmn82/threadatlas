This section, in brief written by a model
Osiris travels through Ethiopia, Arabia, India, Asia, and Europe, teaching agriculture, founding cities, and establishing governors and tribute collectors. After returning, he is murdered and dismembered by his brother Typhon. Isis and her son Orus defeat Typhon, recover most of Osiris's body, and arrange his burial and worship. Egyptian priests preserve his memory through rites, animal worship, and claims about his tombs at Memphis and near Philas.
