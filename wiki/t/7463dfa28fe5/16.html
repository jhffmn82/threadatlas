This section, in brief. The unit describes the Egyptian legislators and the laws they established, including laws concerning civic government, religion, military affairs, contracts, and provincial administration. It identifies Menes, Sasychis, Sesostris, Bocchoris, Amasis, and Darius as Egyptian lawgivers, while also discussing the later abrogation of some Egyptian laws by the Macedonians. It then reports Egyptian claims that many Greek religious practices, laws, sciences, literary ideas, and artistic techniques were derived from Egypt. The account concludes by describing Egyptian funerary customs, artistic methods, and the planned transition to the history of Assyria in Asia.
