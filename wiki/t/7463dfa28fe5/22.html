This section, in brief. The unit describes the Chaldeans, whom the Babylonians regard as their most ancient people, and their philosophy, astrology, divination, and ideas about the heavens. It explains the Chaldeans' account of the planets, zodiac, stars, eclipses, and the structure of the world. It compares Herodotus's and Ctesias's accounts of the Medes and their empire after the fall of the Assyrian empire, including conflicts with the Cadusians and other peoples. It concludes by stating that Cyrus conquered Astyages and transferred the empire from the Medes to the Persians before turning to India.
