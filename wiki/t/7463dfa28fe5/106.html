This section, in brief. Lysander assembled and supplied the Lacedaemonian fleet, captured Lampsacus, and defeated the Athenian fleet at Aegos Potamos. Conon escaped to Evagoras, while Philocles was captured and executed at Lampsacus. The Lacedaemonians and Peloponnesians then blockaded Athens by land and sea, forcing the Athenians to surrender and accept the demolition of their fortifications, a ten-ship limit, and subjection to the Lacedaemonians. Thus the Peloponnesian War ended after twenty-seven years.
