The unit describes renewed conflict during the Peloponnesian War after Athens and Sparta formed a private league, prompting Argos, Thebes, Corinth, and Elis to organize against them. It recounts the struggle between the Argives and Lacedaemonians, including the Battle of Mantinea, the Argive oligarchic coup, and the subsequent restoration of democracy. It also reports Roman war against the Fidenates, conflicts involving the Egestaeans and Selinuntines in Sicily, and Athenian operations under Nicias. The unit concludes with the Athenian decision to send an expedition to Sicily under Alcibiades, Nicias, and Lamachus against the Syracusans.
