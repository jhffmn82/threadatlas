This section, in brief written by a model
This unit describes the Multi-Source Benchmark prompts for generating factual contexts, misinformation, and answers. The factual data generation prompt asks for paraphrased contexts that contain enough information to answer a question. The misinformation generation process creates plausible but incorrect counterfactual answers and filters out cases where correct information is reintroduced. GPT-4o-mini is used to generate misinformation, while example outputs compare majority voting with RA-RAG and demonstrate answer generation from provided contexts.
In this section: Benchmark of Multi-source RAG · GPT-4o-mini · factual data generation · misinformation generation

H.2 Prompt for misinformation generation We create alternative responses that deviate from the correct answers, serving as potential misinfor- mation candidates. A secondary prompt is then designed to incorporate these incorrect alternatives, to elicit misleading or false information from the model. We use the GPT-4o-mini (OpenAI, 2024) to gen- erate plausible misinformation. However, GPT-4o- mini often shows resistance to producing misin- formation (Wallace et al., 2024), sometimes rein- serting correct answers even in contexts intended to contain falsehoods. To mitigate this, we care- fully craft prompts and manually post-process the model’s outputs to filter out incorrectly generated cases. Generate nine counterfactual answers, based on the question and its original answers. Ensure that each counterfactual answer is a plau- sible but incorrect response, clearly different from the original answers. Avoid repeating or paraphrasing the original an- swer or question. The counterfactual answers should be relevant to the context but should introduce a distinct and clearly incorrect or alternative response. You should write the answers in short closed form, limit to maximum 4 words length. The answers should not be sentence form, but rather a short phrase or word. Write in English. Figure A7: Prompt used for generating counterfac- tual answers. 34296 Answer the question based on the given context without using any internal knowledge. Provide only essential keywords without explanations or additional details. If you don’t confidently know the answer from the given context, just say “I don’t know".
