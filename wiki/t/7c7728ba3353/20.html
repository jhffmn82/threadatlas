This section, in brief. The unit evaluates computational efficiency using wall-clock time for inference and source reliability estimation on the NQ dataset with a Beta prior and a single RTX 6000 Ada GPU. The method maintains efficient inference as the number of sources increases, with times ranging from 1.21 seconds for 5 sources to 1.82 seconds for 1,000 sources. Source reliability estimation takes from 4.65 minutes for 5 sources to 14.81 hours for 1,000 sources and is described as a practical one-time preprocessing step. The results also report reliability estimation performance on real-world sources for Politician A, Politician B, and User A under augmented variation.
