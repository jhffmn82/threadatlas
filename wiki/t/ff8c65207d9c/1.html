This section, in brief. The unit concerns the Amours, described as a work that once consisted of five books and now consists of three. The author has preferred this work to the former version by removing two books. Although reading it may no longer give the reader pleasure, the reduced work requires less labour to read.

AN EPIGRAM ON THE AMOURS. |We who of late were five books [001] of Naso, are now but three: this work our author has preferred to the former one. Though it should [002] now be no pleasure to thee to read us; still, the labour will be less, the two being removed.
