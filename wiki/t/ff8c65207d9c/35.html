This section, in brief. The male speaker describes being unable to have sex with an unnamed girl despite her embraces, kisses, flattering words, and other efforts to arouse him. He lies inert and feels shame, while wondering whether magic, poison, or exhaustion from love has caused his impotence. He recalls that he had recently satisfied other women and had previously endured repeated demands from Corinna. After the failed encounter, the male sexual organ becomes rigid too late, and the unnamed girl leaves after disguising the incident from her attendants.
