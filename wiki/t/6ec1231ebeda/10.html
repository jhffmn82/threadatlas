This section, in brief. The unit presents GenGround, a generate-then-ground framework for multi-hop question answering. It combines the parametric knowledge of LLMs with external documents and alternates between simplifying a multi-hop question into a single-hop question and grounding the resulting question-answer pair in retrieved documents. The framework is extended to smaller models through instructional grounding distillation. Experiments on four datasets demonstrate the framework's superiority.
