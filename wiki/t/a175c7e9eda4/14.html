This section, in brief. The unit lists the official languages supported by Command-R and Mixtral. Command-R supports Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish, while Mixtral supports English, French, Italian, German, and Spanish. It also identifies problems in controlling prompt translation, including semantic errors, formal versus informal register, field-specific terminology, and the lack of a direct translation for “You are a helpful assistant” in some languages.
