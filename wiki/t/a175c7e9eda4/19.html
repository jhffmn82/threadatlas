This section, in brief written by a model
Tables 5 and 6 compare system prompts, retrieval settings, and generator models for multilingual response generation in Korean, French, and Russian. Explicitly instructing models to reply in the user language and translating the system prompt into that language most improves correct language rates, especially for SOLAR-10.7B and Command-R-35B. Table 6 shows that multilingual-by-design models, particularly Mixtral-8x7B and Command-R-35B, generally outperform English-centric models in generating non-English responses. Retrieval uses BGE-m3 and is evaluated with English Wikipedia, Wikipedia in user languages, or no retrieval.
