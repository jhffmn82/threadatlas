This section, in brief. This unit is an alphabetical index covering mythological figures, places, events, and objects from Tereis through Urania. It records genealogies, marriages, transformations, heroic deeds, wars, deaths, and journeys, especially those involving Theseus, Tiresias, Troy, the Trojan war, and Ulysses. It also summarizes the histories of Thebes, the Thebans, Hercules, Dionysus, Poseidon, Thetis, Achilles, and related figures.
