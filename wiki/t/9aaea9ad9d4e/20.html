This section, in brief. Theseus, son of Aegeus and Aethra, retrieves his sandals and sword from beneath a stone and travels on foot to Athens, clearing the road of criminals. He kills Periphetes and Sinis, taking Periphetes’ iron club for himself. Daedalus is tried in the Areopagus and, after being condemned, flees to Minos; he later helps Pasiphae approach Poseidon’s bull by making a wooden cow and constructs the labyrinth. The Athenians send seven youths and seven maidens each year to the labyrinth as food for the Minotaur.
