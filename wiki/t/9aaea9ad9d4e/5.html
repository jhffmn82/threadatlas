This section, in brief written by a model
The unit recounts the genealogy and conflicts surrounding Tyndareus, Hippocoon, Icarius, and their descendants. Hippocoon expels Tyndareus and Icarius from Lacedaemon, but Heracles later kills Hippocoon and his sons, allowing Tyndareus to regain the kingdom. It describes the marriages and children of Tyndareus, Leda, Icarius, Penelope, Helen, and Menelaus, including the accounts of Helen’s birth from Leda or Nemesis. It also recounts Theseus’s abduction of Helen, her recovery by Castor and Pollux, and the oath taken by Helen’s suitors before Menelaus became her husband.
