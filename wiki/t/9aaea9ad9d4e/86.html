This section, in brief. The unit is an index of mythological entries from Vine through Zeuxippe. It records Dionysus’s association with the vine, wine, Icarius, Oeneus, and Lycurgus. It gives an extensive account of Zeus, including his ancestry, offspring, punishments, transformations, and interventions in the stories of gods and heroes. It also records episodes involving Hercules, Hera, and Athena, along with entries for many other figures and objects.
