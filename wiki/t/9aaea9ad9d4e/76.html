This section, in brief. The unit records mythological figures and events from Erysichthon through Hephaestus. It describes the deeds, deaths, relationships, and genealogies of figures including Hercules, Eumolpus, Eurystheus, Hector, Helen, and Hephaestus. It also records episodes from the Theban conflict, the Trojan War, the Argonautic expedition, and the labours of Hercules.
