This section, in brief. Epeus constructs the Wooden Horse after Ulysses persuades the Greek leaders to hide inside it, while the other Greeks sail to Tenedos and leave Sinon to signal their return. The Trojans find the abandoned Greek camp, bring the horse into Troy, and debate whether to destroy it or preserve it as a sacred offering. Cassandra and Laocoon warn that armed men are inside, but the Trojans disregard them after serpents sent by Apollo devour Laocoon’s sons. That night the Greeks emerge, open the gates, and admit their returning forces, who sack and burn Troy.
