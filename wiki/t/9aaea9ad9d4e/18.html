This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the descendants of Pandion, including Erechtheus and his children, and recounts the stories of Procris, Cephalus, Orithyia, Boreas, and Eumolpus. Erechtheus inherited the kingdom, while his brother Butes received the priesthoods of Athena and Poseidon Erechtheus. Eumolpus supported the Eleusinians in their war against the Athenians, and an oracle required Erechtheus to sacrifice one of his daughters for victory. After the sacrifice and the ensuing battle, Erechtheus killed Eumolpus, but Poseidon killed Erechtheus and destroyed his household.
