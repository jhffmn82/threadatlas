This section, in brief. The unit concerns Hera, daughter of Cronus and Rhea, and records her conflicts, powers, cults, and actions involving gods and heroes. Hera persecutes Io, Hercules, Dionysus, Semele, Athamas, Ino, the Sphinx, Callisto, and others, while also aiding the Argonauts and being reconciled with Hercules. Zeus hangs Hera from Olympus, fears her, disputes with her before Tiresias, and makes Menelaus immortal. The Heraclids, the sons of Hercules, attack and conquer Peloponnese, later withdraw to Marathon, and seek to return.
