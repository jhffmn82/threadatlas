Alexander is wounded by Philoctetes, returns to Oenone for healing, and dies after she initially refuses to help him; Oenone then kills herself. Asopus searches for Aegina after Zeus carries her away, and Zeus fathers Aeacus with her on the island later called Aegina. Aeacus fathers Peleus, Telamon, and Phocus, but Peleus and Telamon kill Phocus and are driven into exile. Telamon receives the kingship of Salamis from Cychreus and later fathers Ajax, while Peleus goes to Phthia.
