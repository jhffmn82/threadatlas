This section, in brief written by a model
Agamemnon returns to Mycenae with Cassandra but is murdered by Aegisthus and Clytaemnestra, who then rule Mycenae. Electra saves Orestes and sends him to Strophius, and Orestes later kills his mother and Aegisthus after receiving divine permission to avenge his father. Orestes is pursued by the Furies, acquitted at the Areopagus, and sent to the Taurians to bring back their wooden image; he escapes with his sister, who serves as priestess there. Menelaus wanders for eight years before returning to Mycenae and then recovers his kingdom at Sparta with Helen.
