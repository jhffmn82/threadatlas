This section, in brief. Theseus killed the sow called Phaia at Crommyon; Phaia was named after the old woman who raised it, though some identified it as a child of Echidna and Typhon. Theseus then killed Sciron, described as a Corinthian and, according to differing accounts, either a son of Pelops or of Poseidon. Sciron forced travelers at the Scironian Rocks to wash his feet before throwing them into the depths as food for an enormous turtle, but Theseus seized him by the feet and threw him into the sea.
