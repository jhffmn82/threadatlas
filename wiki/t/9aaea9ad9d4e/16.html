This section, in brief. After Cecrops died, Cranaus became king of Athens, but Amphictyon expelled him and later Erichthonius expelled Amphictyon. Erichthonius was associated with Athena and Hephaestus, was raised by Athena, became king of Athens, established the Panathenaea, and married Praxithea, whose son was Pandion. During Pandion’s reign, Demeter and Dionysus came to Attica; Dionysus taught Icarius wine-making, but shepherds killed Icarius after drinking the undiluted wine. Icarius’s daughter Erigone found his body with the help of her dog Maera and hanged herself.
