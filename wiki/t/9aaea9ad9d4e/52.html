The unit recounts how Melampus obtained the kine of Phylacus so that his brother Bias could marry Pero, daughter of Neleus. Melampus was imprisoned at Othrys after attempting to take the cattle, but escaped when he learned from worms that the building was collapsing. He then cured Iphiclus's childlessness by discovering that a knife hidden in a wild pear-tree had caused it, and Iphiclus gave him the kine. The text compares differing ancient accounts of the cattle's ownership and discusses the story's use of sympathetic magic and its parallels in folktales.
