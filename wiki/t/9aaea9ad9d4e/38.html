This section, in brief. After Troy was laid waste, Calchas said that Athena was angry because of Ajax's impiety, but Ajax fled to an altar and was spared. Agamemnon and Menelaus quarrelled about whether to sail or remain and sacrifice to Athena; Menelaus later reached Egypt after a storm, while Diomedes and Nestor had a prosperous voyage. Calchas travelled with Amphilochus, Leonteus, Podalirius, and Polypoetes to Colophon, where Mopsus surpassed him in divination, and Calchas died and was buried at Notium. Athena sent a storm against the Greeks, Ajax perished after Poseidon split the rock where he had survived, and Nauplius used beacon fires to lure Greek ships onto the Capherian rocks after arranging punishments for those connected with Palamedes's death.
