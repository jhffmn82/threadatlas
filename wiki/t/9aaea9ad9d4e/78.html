This section, in brief. This unit is an index of mythological figures, places, objects, and events, with extensive entries on Hercules and his labours, adventures, death, and descendants. It also records the deeds and relationships of Hermes, including his assistance to Hercules and Perseus. Zeus appears as a god involved in Hercules’s immortality, the golden apples, and various other myths. The unit includes entries for Eurystheus, who commands Hercules’s labours and receives the objects he retrieves.
