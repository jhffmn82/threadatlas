This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns Achilles’s concealment at Lycomedes’s court and his eventual departure for Troy. Thetis disguises Achilles as a girl because Calchas has declared that Troy cannot be taken without him, while fearing that he will die if he campaigns. Achilles has a son, Pyrrhus, with Deidamia, and Odysseus discovers him by sounding a trumpet. Achilles travels to Troy with Phoenix and Patroclus, whose earlier history is also recounted.
