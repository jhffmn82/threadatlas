This section, in brief. Cecrops, an autochthonous king with the body of a man and a serpent, was the first king of Attica and renamed the country Cecropia. Poseidon and Athena contested possession of Attica, but the twelve gods awarded it to Athena after Cecrops testified that she had first planted the olive-tree. Athena named the city Athens, while Poseidon flooded the Thriasian plain in anger. The unit also traces Cinyras’s lineage and reports that Adonis was killed by a boar while hunting.
