This section, in brief. The unit recounts that Apollo loved Coronis, who chose to live with Ischys, and that Apollo killed her while rescuing their child Aesculapius from the pyre. Aesculapius learned healing and hunting from Chiron and became skilled enough to prevent deaths and raise the dead. Zeus struck Aesculapius with a thunderbolt, after which Apollo slew the Cyclopes who had made it. Zeus ordered Apollo to serve Admetus for a year, and Apollo tended Admetus’s herds at Pherae.
