This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns Smyrna, daughter of Thias, king of Assyria, and her illicit passion for her father. With her nurse's help, Smyrna shares her father's bed for twelve nights, is pursued when he discovers this, and is transformed by the gods into a myrrh tree. Adonis is born from the tree, and Aphrodite and Persephone dispute possession of him until Zeus divides the year among Adonis, Persephone, and Aphrodite. Adonis later dies after being struck by a boar while hunting.
