This section, in brief written by a model
The unit compares several Celtic, Basque, Rumanian, and Italian versions of the Ulysses and Polyphemus story. In the Islay tale, Conall Cra Bhuidhe blinds a one-eyed giant, escapes from his cave disguised in a buck’s skin, and defeats the giant with a talking ring and a severed finger. Related Basque and Rumanian stories likewise feature a one-eyed giant, escape through a sheep or ram disguise, a talking ring, and the severing of the hero’s finger. An Italian story includes the same general pattern, but its ring is enchanted rather than talking.
