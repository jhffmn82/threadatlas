This section, in brief. This unit is an index of mythological figures, places, objects, and events from the stories of the gods, heroes, and the Trojan War. It records accounts involving Zeus, Hera, Hercules, Dionysus, Medea, Theseus, Ulysses, Achilles, and Poseidon, along with events at Troy and Ilium and in Hades. It also lists genealogies, transformations, sacrifices, deaths, resurrections, journeys, and punishments.
