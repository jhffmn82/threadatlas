This section, in brief. The unit compares folk-tales in which the water of life or related precious waters lie between cliffs or mountains that open briefly and then clash together. Prince Ivan obtains healing and vivifying water between closing mountains, while Floria’s helper, a maimed stork, retrieves the water of life and water of death from between clashing mountains. Modern Greek stories, an Eskimo story about Giviok, and the Argonaut story feature similar dangers in which a person or animal barely escapes as rocks or icebergs close. The text concludes that the Clashing Rocks are more likely a folk-tale invention than a solar myth or a sailor’s memory of icebergs.
