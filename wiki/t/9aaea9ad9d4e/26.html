This section, in brief. Theseus and Pirithous agreed to marry daughters of Zeus. Theseus abducted Helen from Sparta, while Pirithous sought Persephone and descended with Theseus into Hades. The Dioscuri captured Athens, carried away Helen and Aethra, and brought Menestheus back to rule Athens. In Hades, Theseus and Pirithous were deceived and bound to the Chair of Forgetfulness; Heracles rescued Theseus, who was later driven from Athens by Menestheus.
