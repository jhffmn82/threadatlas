This section, in brief. Tantalus is punished in Hades with a stone hanging over him, inaccessible water, and fruit that is carried away by the wind; some say this is because he revealed the gods’ mysteries and tried to share ambrosia with his companions. Broteas failed to honor Artemis, claimed that even fire could not harm him, and threw himself into fire after becoming mad. Pelops was slaughtered and boiled at a banquet of the gods, restored to life more beautiful than before, and became Poseidon’s beloved, receiving a winged chariot. Pelops later sought Hippodamia in a contest arranged by her father Oenomaus, whose death resulted from the failure of his chariot during the race.
