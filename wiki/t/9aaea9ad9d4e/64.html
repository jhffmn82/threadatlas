This section, in brief written by a model
The Italian story concerns a man of Florence, a curate, and a workman who enter the house of a giant seeking employment. The giant kills the curate and workman, but the man of Florence blinds him with boiling oil, escapes after cutting off a finger trapped by a magical ring, and returns to Florence. The Serbian story concerns a priest and his scholar, who encounter a one-eyed giant in a cave. The giant roasts the priest, but the scholar blinds him, escapes disguised in a ram’s skin, and cuts off a finger trapped by a staff.
