This section, in brief. This unit is an alphabetical index of mythological figures, places, and objects from Meleager through Olympus. It records genealogies, marriages, births, heroic deeds, wars, deaths, and transformations. It includes accounts of Meleager and the Calydonian boar, Menelaus and Helen’s movements after Troy, Minos and the Minotaur in the Labyrinth, and numerous exploits of Hercules. It also mentions the gods Zeus, Apollo, and Poseidon in connection with births, punishments, prophecies, and divine actions.
