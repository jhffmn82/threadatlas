This section, in brief. The unit concerns stories in which Polyidus, Glaucus of Anthedon, and other heroes discover a magical herb that restores the dead to life. In the story of Tylon or Tylus, a serpent uses the “flower of Zeus” to revive a dead serpent, enabling Moire to restore her brother. Similar tales from Greece and other regions describe serpents, birds, weasels, mice, ravens, and other animals revealing healing plants or waters that reunite bodies and revive the dead. The unit also records a belief, especially among Armenians, that serpents know springs and plants that heal them, renew their youth, and can confer immortality.
