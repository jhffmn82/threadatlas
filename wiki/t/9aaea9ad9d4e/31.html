This section, in brief written by a model
The Greeks return to Aulis, where the fleet is wind-bound and Calchas declares that Iphigenia must be sacrificed to Artemis. Agamemnon deceives Clytaemnestra by claiming that Iphigenia will marry Achilles, but Artemis carries Iphigenia away and substitutes a deer at the altar. The Greeks then sail to Tenedos, where Achilles kills Tenes despite a warning from Thetis. During a sacrifice to Apollo, a water-snake bites Philoctetes, who is abandoned on Lemnos with Heracles’ bow.
