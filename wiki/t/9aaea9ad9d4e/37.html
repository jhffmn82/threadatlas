Ajax the Locrian violated Cassandra while she clung to Athena’s wooden image, which thereafter was said to look upward in horror. The Trojans were slain, Troy was burned, its spoil was divided, and Astyanax was thrown from the battlements while Polyxena was sacrificed on Achilles’ grave. Agamemnon received Cassandra, Neoptolemus received Andromache, and Ulysses received Hecuba, although another account gives Hecuba to Helenus and says she became a bitch. Laodice was swallowed by a chasm in the earth before everyone.
