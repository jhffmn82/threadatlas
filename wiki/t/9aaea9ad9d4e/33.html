This section, in brief. The unit concerns Achilles’ campaigns around Troy, the arrival of Trojan allies after nine years, and the major battles of the Trojan War. Alexander fights Menelaus but is rescued by Aphrodite, while Diomedes wounds Aphrodite and Ajax fights Hector. The Trojans drive the Greeks behind their wall and set fire to the ships, prompting Achilles to send Patroclus, whose death leads Achilles to return to battle. Achilles kills Hector, and Priam later ransoms Hector’s body and buries it.
