This section, in brief written by a model
Theseus clears the road to Athens, survives Medea’s attempt to poison him, and is recognized by Aegeus as his son. He joins the third tribute sent to the Minotaur, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne’s help, kills the Minotaur, and escapes with Ariadne and the rescued children. Dionysus carries Ariadne to Lemnos, while Theseus forgets to change the ship’s black sail, causing Aegeus to believe that Theseus has died and to kill himself. Theseus then takes control of Athens, and Minos imprisons Daedalus and Icarus in the Labyrinth until Daedalus makes wings for their escape.
