This section, in brief. The unit recounts the succession of Cecrops and Pandion, the division of power among Pandion’s sons, and Aegeus’s consultation of the oracle concerning children. It describes Aegeus’s encounter with Aethra at Troezen, the death of Androgeus, and Minos’s campaign against Athens and Megara. After famine and pestilence afflicted Athens, the Athenians agreed to send seven youths and seven maidens to the Minotaur. The unit also explains the Minotaur’s Labyrinth and Daedalus’s role in constructing it.
