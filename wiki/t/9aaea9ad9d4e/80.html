This section, in brief. This unit is an index of mythological figures, places, objects, and events, including Jason’s quest for the Golden Fleece, Medea’s aid and later actions, and the roles of the Argo and Pelias. It also records numerous deeds of Hercules, Ulysses, Theseus, and Achilles. The entries describe battles, marriages, punishments, voyages, transformations, and deaths associated with these figures.
