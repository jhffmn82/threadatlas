This section, in brief written by a model
After Cecrops dies, Cranaus becomes king and is described as autochthonous; during his reign, the flood of Deucalion is said to occur. Cranaus marries Pedias and has Cranaus, Cranaichme, and Atthis, who dies while still a virgin, after which he names the country Atthis. Amphictyon expels Cranaus and rules for twelve years before Erichthonius expels him. Erichthonius is said either to be the son of Hephaestus and Atthis or of Hephaestus and Athena, with the latter account involving Athena's visit to Hephaestus for weapons.
