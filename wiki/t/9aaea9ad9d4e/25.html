This section, in brief. The unit recounts Theseus’s conflict with the Amazons, his marriage to Phaedra, and the death of Hippolytus after Phaedra’s false accusation. It also describes Ixion’s punishment by Zeus and the birth of Centaurus from the cloud resembling Hera. Finally, it recounts the wedding conflict of Pirithous, Theseus’s battle with the centaurs, and Caeneus’s burial beneath fir trees.
