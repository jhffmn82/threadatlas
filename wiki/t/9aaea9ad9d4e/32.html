This section, in brief written by a model
The Greek forces sailed from Tenedos toward Troy and sent Ulysses and Menelaus to demand the restoration of Helen and the property, but the Trojans refused and threatened the envoys. The Greeks then attacked, and Protesilaus was the first to land, killing Trojans before Hector killed him. Achilles later landed with the Myrmidons, killed Cycnus with a stone, and helped drive the barbarians into Troy, which the Greeks besieged. Achilles also ambushed and killed Troilus in the sanctuary of Thymbraean Apollo, while Laodamia died after Protesilaus was returned to Hades.
