Ethics Statement We are totally aware that text generation technology has a potential to be used maliciously to generate fake, toxic, or offensive content. If the retrieved content includes harmful or toxic information, it will influence the output of the generated content. Our approach is proposed to mitigate the influence of noise from retrieval, which includes toxic con- tents. However, there is no assurance that our ap- proach will completely eliminate toxics.
