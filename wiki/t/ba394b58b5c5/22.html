This section, in brief. The unit preserves Hesiodic accounts of Helen’s suitors, Tyndareus’s oath, and the marriage of Helen and Menelaus, along with genealogies and episodes involving the gods and heroic families. Aphrodite punishes Tyndareus’s daughters for his neglect, while Zeus plans to destroy the demigods and separate gods from mortals. It also relates the Calydonian boar hunt, Meleager’s death at Apollo’s hands, and the descendants of Heracles, Amphiaraus, Electryon, Dardanus, and others. Later fragments concern Telephus, Dardanus, Ganymede, and Hyacinthus.
