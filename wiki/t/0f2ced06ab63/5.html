This section, in brief. The paper proposes R2AG, an enhanced retrieval-augmented generation framework designed to bridge the semantic gap between retrievers and LLMs. R2AG incorporates retrieval information from retrievers into the LLMs’ generation process to capture a comprehensive understanding of retrieved documents. Experimental results show that R2AG outperforms other competitors, and further analysis confirms its robustness and effectiveness. Future work may apply more retrieval features to the R2AG framework.
