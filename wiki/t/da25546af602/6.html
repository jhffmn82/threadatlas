The paper proposes K-RagRec, a framework that augments the recommendation capability of large language models by retrieving reliable and up-to-date knowledge from knowledge graphs. It uses a graph neural network and a pretrained language model for semantic indexing, supporting coarse- and fine-grained retrieval. A popularity selective retrieval policy improves efficiency by deciding whether an item should be retrieved based on its popularity. Experiments on three real-world datasets show that K-RagRec is effective, while its graph encoding helps large language models use structural information without requiring long context inputs.

5 Conclusion In this paper, we propose a novel framework K- RagRec to augment the recommendation capabil- ity of LLMs by retrieving reliable and up-to-date knowledge from KGs. Specifically, we first intro- duce a GNN and PLM to perform semantic index- ing of KGs, enabling both coarse and fine-grained 27159 retrieval for KGs. To further improve retrieval effi- ciency, we introduce a popularity selective retrieval policy that determines whether an item needs to be retrieved based on its popularity. Notably, K- RagRec performs more expressive graph encoding of the retrieved knowledge sub-graphs, facilitat- ing the LLM to effectively leverage the structure information and avoid long context input. Exten- sive experiments conducted on three real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our pro- posed framework.
