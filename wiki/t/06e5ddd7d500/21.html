Cell values in HippoRAG w/ IRCoT represent IRCoT’s thought process. demonstrate a consistent pattern across all methods: retrieval performance stabilises after approximately 4 iterations, with no substantial improvements or degradation in subsequent iterations. While some minor fluctuations occur beyond this point, they are negligible. This performance plateau can be attributed to two key factors. First, the query re-writing mech- anisms in all investigated approaches struggle to generate effective subsequent queries. Second, our analysis has identified several cases of unanswer- able queries within MuSiQue’s answerable subset.
