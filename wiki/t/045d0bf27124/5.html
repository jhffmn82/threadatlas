Retriever Enhancement Strategy Document chunking and embedding methods sig- nificantly impact retrieval performance. Common chunking strategies divide documents into chunks, but determining optimal chunk length can be chal- lenging. Small chunks may fragment sentences, while large chunks might include irrelevant con- text. LlamaIndex (Liu, 2022) optimizes the chunk- ing method like Small2Big and sliding window. Retrieved chunks can be irrelevant and numbers can be large, so reranking is necessary to filter irrelevant documents.
