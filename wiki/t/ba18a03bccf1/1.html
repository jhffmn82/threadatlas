This section, in brief. The unit presents the work titled "Unsupervised Information Refinement Training of Large Language Models for Retrieval-Augmented Generation." The work concerns unsupervised information refinement training for Large Language Models and its application to Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is authored by Shicheng Xu, Liang Pang, Mo Yu, Fandong Meng, Huawei Shen, Xueqi Cheng, and Jie Zhou, who are affiliated with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, and WeChat AI.
