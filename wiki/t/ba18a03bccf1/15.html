Performance on MMLU Experimental results on MMLU benchmark in the setting without RAG shown in Table 14 show that our INFO-RAG significantly improves the perfor- mance of LLMs in RAG, while still maintaining its versatility and avoiding catastrophic forgetting. MMLU is a benchmark that measures massive mul- titask language understanding ability of LLMs. It covers 57 subjects across STEM, the humanities, the social sciences, and more. It ranges in difficulty from an elementary level to an advanced profes- sional level, and it tests both world knowledge and problem-solving ability (Hendrycks et al., 2020).
