This section, in brief. This paper aims to enable large language models to perform information refinement in Retrieval-Augmented Generation through unsupervised training. The goal is to accurately extract correct information and avoid interference from incorrect information. Due to limited computing resources, the paper only evaluates models with 7B and 13B parameter sizes. It considers exploring larger models in the future with more computing resources.

Limitations This paper aims to enable LLMs to perform infor- mation refinement in RAG by unsupervised train- ing, so as to accurately extract correct information and avoid the interference of incorrect information. The main limitation of this paper is that due to the lack of computing resources, we only conduct experiments on models with 7B and 13B param- eter sizes. In the future, we consider using more computing resources to explore the performance of models with larger parameter sizes.
