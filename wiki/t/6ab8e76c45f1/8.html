Limitations Despite the benefits of HopRAG, the current eval- uation focuses on multi-hop or multi-document QA tasks. In order to mitigate the risk of per- formance fluctuations when applying HopRAG to other datasets, we should explore its generaliza- tion capabilities across a broader range of domains. Besides, more sophisticated query simulation and edge merging strategies may lead to further im- provements. Finally, though we were inspired by the theories of six degrees of separation and small- world networks, the degree distribution of our pas- sage graph vertices does not exhibit the power-law characteristic. On the one hand, these theories only serve as the motivation and intuitive analogy; on the other hand, exploring more appropriate degree dis- tribution strategies is an interesting research topic. We leave these research problems for future work.
