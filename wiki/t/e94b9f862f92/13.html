This section, in brief. The unit describes zero-shot evaluation of large language models using a shared prompt with retrieved passages or a closed-book variant, across ASQA, KILT QA tasks, POPQA, SCIQ, WIKIQA, and TruthfulQA. It compares retrieval systems including BM25, RetroMAE, SPLADE-v3, dense retrievers, and reranked variants, reporting Match, LLMeval, and R@5 results. On KILT retrieval tasks, SPLADE-v3+RR achieves the strongest listed average retrieval results among BM25, RetroMAE, and SPLADE-v3 variants. Additional experiments on KILT NQ with SOLAR-10.7B show that E5-large-v2 has the highest listed R@5 after reranking, while reranking can reduce E5 retrieval effectiveness.
