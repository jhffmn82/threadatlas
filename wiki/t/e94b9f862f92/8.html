This section, in brief. This unit is a references section covering information retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, question answering, language models, embeddings, and evaluation. It includes work on multilingual question answering, dense passage retrieval, hallucination detection, instruction tuning, and retrieval benchmarks. The references name datasets and systems such as XOR QA, MS MARCO, TyDi QA, TriviaQA, Natural Questions, ASQA, MKQA, KILT, and HotpotQA, alongside models and tools including Self-RAG, Atlas, FlashRAG, DSPy, Pyserini, LangChain, and LlamaIndex.
