This section, in brief. The unit presents “BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.” It appears in Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2024, on pages 7640–7663, published November 12–16, 2024. The authors are David Rau, Hervé Déjean, Nadezhda Chirkova, Thibault Formal, Shuai Wang, Stéphane Clinchant, and Vassilina Nikoulina, affiliated with the University of Amsterdam, Naver Labs Europe, and The University of Queensland.
