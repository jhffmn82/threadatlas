This section, in brief. Alexander and his army crossed a waterless desert in Susiana, suffering intensely from heat, thirst, and dehydration. Alexander refused water brought for his own use because it was intended for the soldiers’ children, and after reaching the river Oxus he ordered fires and water-carrying parties to help those left behind. Alexander later encountered the Branchidae, descendants of people removed from Miletus by Xerxes and accused of betraying Miletus, and allowed the Milesians to decide their fate. He then ordered the Branchidae city destroyed, its inhabitants killed, and its walls and sacred groves completely removed.
