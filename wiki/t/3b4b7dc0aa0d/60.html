This section, in brief. The unit describes the Aetolians' invasion of Thessaly, their withdrawal to defend Aetolia, and Polyperchon's defeat of their forces under Menon of Pharsalia. At Triparadisus, Antipater became protector with absolute authority and made a new division of the provinces, assigning commands throughout Asia. Antigonus then defeated Eumenes in Cappadocia, forcing him and about six hundred loyal followers to take refuge in the stronghold of Nora. Meanwhile, Ptolemy conquered Syria and Phoenicia from Egypt, while Antigonus's growing power threatened Antipater and the kings.
