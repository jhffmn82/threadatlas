This section, in brief. The unit recounts the Roman siege of Lilybaeum during the long war with Carthage, including naval battles, failed blockades, military losses, and the eventual peace after twenty-four years. It then describes Carthaginian expansion in Spain under Amilcar and Asdrubal, the rise of Hannibal, and the destruction of Saguntum that led to the Hannibalic War. It also includes observations about religious and moral life, military discipline, leadership, and the destructive effects of envy and luxury.
