Rhodian ambassadors came to Rome to seek pardon for appearing to favor Perseus during the war with him, and they were reproved and fined but relieved of their fears. Perseus, the last king of Macedonia, was captured by Aemilius, confined in the prison of Alba, and later died after being transferred to a less severe confinement. The unit traces the Cappadocian royal genealogy from Cyrus through Ariarathes and describes several alleged cases of people whose bodily characteristics were interpreted as those of both sexes. It also recounts the destruction of Carthage, the endurance and prosperity of Masinissa, and the violent seizure of the Bithynian kingdom by Nicomedes after killing Prusias.
