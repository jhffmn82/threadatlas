This section, in brief. Perdiccas and Philip defeated Ariarathes, gave Cappadocia to Eumenes, and then destroyed Larissa while the Isaurians chose death rather than surrender. Perdiccas married Nicaea and Cleopatra to advance his political aims, prompting him to move against Antigonos, who fled to join Antipater. Antipater and Craterus struggled against the Aetolians until Antigonos revealed Perdiccas's plans, leading them to make peace temporarily and prepare an expedition into Asia. Perdiccas sent Eumenes to secure the Hellespont and marched with his army from Pisidia toward Egypt to attack Ptolemy.
