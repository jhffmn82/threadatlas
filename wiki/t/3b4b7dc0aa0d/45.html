This section, in brief. The Lacedemonians were routed by Antipater in a great battle and were forced to send ambassadors to him. Antipater postponed their appeal until the general assembly of Greece met at Corinth. After disputes on both sides, the matter was left to Alexander's decision. Antipater received the chiefest of the nobility of Sparta as hostages, and the Lacedemonians sent ambassadors into Asia to beg pardon for their late revolt.
