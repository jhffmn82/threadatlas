This section, in brief. The unit describes the punishments and fortunes of several rulers, generals, and Roman statesmen. Caius Gracchus is killed by his servant, while Lucius Vitellius betrays him for a reward. Jugurtha defeats and kills his brother Adherbal despite Roman intervention. Publius Scipio Nasica is praised for his virtue and political judgment, including his argument that Carthage should be preserved; later, his son kills Tiberius Gracchus.
