This section, in brief. Alexander led the Macedonians through the snowy country of the Paropamisadae, crossed Caucasus, founded Alexandria and other cities, and then marched toward Bactria. Satibarzanes was killed in single combat by Erigyus, while Bessus was captured by his own commanders and delivered to Alexander for punishment. In India, Alexander massacred the Massaga mercenaries after they had surrendered their city and captured the supposedly impregnable rock of Aornos. After crossing the Indus, Alexander received Mophis, also called Taxiles, as a submissive ally and restored him to his kingdom.
