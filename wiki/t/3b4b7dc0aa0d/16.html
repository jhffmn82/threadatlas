This section, in brief. Dion landed in Sicily with a small force, gathered supporters, and entered Syracuse after the Syracusans chose him and Megacles as generals with absolute power. The Syracusans celebrated their liberation from Dionysius, but Dionysius soon returned, deceived them with negotiations, and attacked the wall from sea to sea. Dion defeated the Dionysian forces at the wall, after which Dionysius withdrew into the castle and continued seeking provisions and peace. In Greece, Alexander of Pherae was murdered by his wife and two brothers, while Philip subdued the Thessalian tyrants and restored the cities to liberty.
