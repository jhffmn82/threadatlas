This section, in brief. Agathocles defeated the Carthaginians in a difficult battle near a deep river, though Numidians raided his camp and carried away much of its baggage. He then persuaded Ophelas to bring an army from Cyrene to Africa, but killed Ophelas after accusing him of treachery and took his army into his own service. At Carthage, Bomilcar attempted a coup, but the citizens defeated his forces and executed him. Agathocles later sent spoils and captives toward Syracuse, while the Roman consuls fought in Italy.
