This section, in brief. Memnon, appointed by Darius to command the Persian forces, captured several cities and islands but died before invading Macedonia. Darius executed Charidemus the Athenian after rejecting his advice, then assembled a large army and marched toward Cilicia with his mother, wife, and children. Alexander recovered from a serious illness, seized Alexander of Lyncestes for investigation, and defeated Darius's army at Issus. Darius fled, while his mother, wife, son, and daughters were captured, and the Macedonians took the Persian camp and its treasures.
