This section, in brief written by a model
The Roman senate receives embassies concerning Ptolemy, Ariarathes, Demetrius, the Celtiberians, Carthage, and other foreign peoples, often imposing conditions or renewing alliances. The Carthaginians surrender hostages and arms but are later ordered to abandon Carthage and build elsewhere, prompting renewed resistance. The Aradians attempt to obtain Marathos, while the Marathonians appeal to them and are imprisoned. Scipio Africanus and other Roman ambassadors inspect Egypt and nearby regions, and later Bocchus secures Roman favor by helping Marius capture Jugurtha; Mark Antony and the Cretans also become involved in disputes over treaties and Roman commands.
