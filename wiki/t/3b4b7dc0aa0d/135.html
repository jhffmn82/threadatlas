This section, in brief. Alexander moved his army from Bactria toward India, leaving Amyntas with 3,500 horse and 10,000 foot in Bactria. He reorganized Alexandria in Paropamisus, appointed governors, received Taxiles and other princes, and divided his forces to advance toward the Indus. Hephsestion and Perdiccas besieged and captured the city of Astes after thirty days, while Alexander led another force through the territories of the Aspians, Phyrseans, and Arosacans. Alexander captured the first city he attacked after a brief assault, was slightly wounded, and ordered its prisoners put to the sword.
