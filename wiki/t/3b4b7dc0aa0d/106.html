This section, in brief. The unit recounts the rise and suppression of a major slave rebellion in Sicily, beginning with revolts against Licinius Nerva and expanding under Salvius, Athenion, and Tryphon. The fugitive slaves captured territory, defeated Roman forces, besieged Morgantium and Lilybaeum, and established Tricala as a royal seat. Lucius Licinius Lucullus and Caius Aquilius led Roman campaigns against the rebels, with Aquilius eventually killing Athenion and ending the resistance. The account states that the servants' war lasted almost four years.
