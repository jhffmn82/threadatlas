This section, in brief. Alexander subdued disturbances in Greece and neighboring regions before returning to Macedonia when Thebes and other Greek cities rejected his sovereignty. He besieged and captured Thebes after the Thebans resisted with outside aid, and the city was plundered, its people killed or enslaved, and its walls razed by decree of the Greek council. Alexander later pardoned the Athenian orators demanded from Athens and returned to Macedonia, where he began preparations for an expedition into Asia against the Persian monarchy.
