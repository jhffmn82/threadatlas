This section, in brief written by a model
Seleucus marched from Syria toward Babylon with only a small force, relying on the goodwill of the Babylonians and his previous conduct as governor. He recovered Babylon, defeated Nicanor through a surprise attack near the Tigris, and then brought Media, Susa, and neighboring provinces under his control. Ptolemy withdrew from Coelesyria into Egypt after Antigonus joined Demetrius and destroyed several cities in Syria and Phoenicia. Antigonus later sent Athenaeus against the Nabataeans, whose initial defeat was followed by the destruction of Athenaeus's army through an Arabian counterattack.
