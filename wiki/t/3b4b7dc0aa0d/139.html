This section, in brief written by a model
Book XVII concerns Alexander’s campaigns from Macedonia through Asia, Egypt, Persia, India, and Gedrosia, including the destruction of Thebes, the capture of Tyre and Persepolis, the battles of Issus and Arbela, and Alexander’s death at Babylon. Book XVIII describes the succession crisis after Alexander, the division of the provinces, the revolt of the Greeks, the Lamian War, and conflicts among Perdiccas, Ptolemy, Antigonus, Eumenes, and other commanders. Book XIX follows Antigonus’s struggle against Eumenes and the other successors, Cassander’s actions in Macedonia and Greece, Seleucus’s recovery of Babylon, and Demetrius’s campaigns.
