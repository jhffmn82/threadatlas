This section, in brief. Bessus, Nabarzanes, and Barzaentes flee to Bactria after Darius's death, and Bessus raises an uprising by persuading the Bactrians to claim their liberty and recognize him as king. Alexander rewards and dismisses the Greek auxiliaries, campaigns through Hyrcania and the Mardian country, recovers his war horse, and receives Thalestris, queen of the Amazons. Alexander adopts elements of Persian royal dress and courtly customs before dealing with rebellions and a plot involving Dimnus, Ceballinus, Philotas, and others. He then campaigns against the Arimaspi, appoints Teridates over the Arimaspi and Gedrosians, and subdues Arachosia.
