P. Scipio defeated the Carthaginians, compelled Hannibal to leave Italy, and finally overcame him and brought Carthage under Roman rule. The unit describes Perseus's failures, including his refusal to pay the Gauls, his loss of Macedonia, and his eventual captivity and death. It praises Tiberius Gracchus and Paulus Æmilius, contrasts Eumenes's generosity with Perseus's covetousness, and recounts Antiochus III's erratic conduct and Ptolemy's weakness. It also emphasizes Roman clemency toward defeated peoples, including the liberation of the Macedonian cities and favorable treatment of the Macedonians and Illyrians.
