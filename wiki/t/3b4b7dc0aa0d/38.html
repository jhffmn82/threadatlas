This section, in brief. After Darius and his army were routed, Darius fled toward the upper provinces, while Alexander pursued him for two hundred furlongs before returning to camp. When the women captives heard that Alexander had taken the spoils of Darius's tent, they mourned until Leonalus assured them that Darius was alive and that Alexander would respect their former dignity. The next morning Alexander visited the captives with Hephaestion and treated Sisygambis, Darius's wife, and Darius's little son with honor and kindness. Alexander restored the captives' royal privileges and promised to care for them, demonstrating the generosity and clemency for which the whole army praised him.
