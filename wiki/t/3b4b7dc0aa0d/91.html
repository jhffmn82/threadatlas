This section, in brief. Agathocles, unable to defeat the Carthaginians in Africa, suffered the desertion of many Africans and decided to flee with his younger son Heraclides. Archagathus exposed the plan, but Agathocles was later released and escaped by ship; the soldiers then killed his sons and made peace with the Carthaginians. The burning of the Carthaginian camp caused panic and heavy losses in both armies. After returning to Sicily, Agathocles ordered the massacre at Aegesta and, in response to his sons' deaths, Antander carried out a slaughter at Syracuse.
