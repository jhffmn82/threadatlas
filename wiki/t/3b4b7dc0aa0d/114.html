This unit describes Hannibal's military character, his conduct toward Roman captives, and his actions in Italy and Africa during the conflict with Rome. It recounts Scipio's humane treatment of Syphax and the Carthaginian ambassadors, as well as the fall of Syracuse and the punishment of Roman and Carthaginian offenders. It also reports the misconduct and misfortunes of Philip of Macedon and Antiochus, including their attacks on temples and defeats. The unit praises Roman fidelity to treaties and clemency while attributing the downfall of impious rulers to divine vengeance.
