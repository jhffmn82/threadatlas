This section, in brief. The unit describes the abundance of wild beasts and the hunting grounds in Bahastis, and then recounts Alexander's hunt in the country called Bazaria. Alexander ordered the game to be driven out, and when a huge lion attacked him, Lysimachus tried to intervene but Alexander killed the lion with one blow. Because of the danger, the Macedonians decreed that Alexander should not hunt on foot without a guard of captains and friends. The hunting expedition killed four thousand wild beasts while Alexander's army was in the forest.
