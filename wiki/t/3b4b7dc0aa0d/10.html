This section, in brief. Dionysius the tyrant sent Gauls and Spaniards to assist the Lacedemonians, while Pelopidas and Ismenias were imprisoned by Alexander, tyrant of Pherae, and later freed through Epaminondas’s actions. Epaminondas rescued the Boeotian army from attack by Alexander’s cavalry after being chosen general despite serving as a private soldier. The Lacedemonians defeated the Arcadians, and the Arcadians founded Megalopolis for security. In Sicily, Dionysius attacked the Carthaginians, died after excessive feasting and drinking, and was succeeded by Dionysius the younger; meanwhile, Alexander massacred the people of Scotussa, Coos was fortified and settled, and the Laconic and Boeotian War ended through the Persian king’s mediation.
