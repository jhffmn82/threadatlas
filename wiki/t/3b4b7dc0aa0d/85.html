This section, in brief. Predictions concerning the deaths of Eumelus and Satyrus were reported and credited by the inhabitants. Satyrus was warned to beware of Musculus, avoided people and animals bearing that name, and was eventually killed by a wound in the muscles of his arm. Eumelus was warned to beware of a moving house, and his death was attributed to the canopy placed on a chariot. In Italy, the Roman consuls defeated the Samnites at Talius, captured more than 2,200 prisoners, and brought several revolting cities under Roman control.
