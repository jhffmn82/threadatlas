This section, in brief written by a model
A massive slave mutiny in Sicily erupted after prolonged exploitation, cruelty, and unchecked violence by slaveholders, bringing many cities and the island itself near collapse. Similar unrest occurred in Asia, where slaves joined Aristonicus and helped fill towns and cities with bloodshed. Damophilus of Euna and his wife Megallis exemplified extreme cruelty toward slaves, while their daughter was spared because she had treated abused servants kindly. Eunus became king after the revolt, Cleon led another slave rebellion around Agrigentum, and Attalus's cruelty in Asia helped provoke opposition to his rule.
