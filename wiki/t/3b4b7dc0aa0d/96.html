This section, in brief. Agathocles attacked the peaceful inhabitants of the Lipari islands and extorted sacred treasure, but a storm destroyed many ships carrying the money; later tradition interpreted his misfortunes and death as divine punishment. Rome made peace with the Samnites, subdued the Aequi, and allied with the Marsi, Peligni, and Marrucini. Demetrius Poliorcetes captured Sicyon, Corinth, and other cities in Greece and Achaia while claiming to restore Greek liberty. In Italy, Cleonymus raised a large force for the Tarentines, seized Corcyra, and suffered reverses after returning to Italy. Cassander and Lysimachus allied with Ptolemy and Seleucus against Antigonus, whose forces contested Asia while Demetrius was summoned from Greece.
