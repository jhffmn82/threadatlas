This section, in brief. Athens, joined by Chios, Coos, Rhodes, and other Greeks, sent a fleet to aid Byzantium against Philip, who then raised his sieges and made peace. The Carthaginians invaded Sicily with a large army and fleet, but Timoleon defeated them in a battle aided by a sudden storm, killing or capturing many and taking their equipment. Timoleon then subdued tyrants in Sicily, freed the cities, resettled lands, revised Syracuse's laws, and established peace and prosperity. Syracuse and other Sicilian cities were enlarged by new colonists, while Argyra received inhabitants and public buildings.
