Ptolemy, king of Egypt, captured several cities in Cilicia and nearby regions before imprisoning and poisoning Ptolemy the captain, who had defected from Antigonus. Polyperchon restored Hercules, Alexander's son, but Cassander persuaded Polyperchon to murder him and then rewarded Polyperchon. In Sicily, the Syracusans defeated the Carthaginian army besieging Syracuse, captured Amilcar, and killed him after torturing him. In Africa, Agathocles survived a mutiny caused by his son Archagathus's killing of Lyciscus, regained the army's allegiance, and defeated the Carthaginians in a sudden attack.
