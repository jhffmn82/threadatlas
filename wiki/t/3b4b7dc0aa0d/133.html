This section, in brief. The king's expedition against the Nauticans, or Nomvans, marched from its winter-quarters toward Gabazat in the third month. On the third day, a violent storm brought lightning, thunder, and hail, scattering and terrifying the army. The cold caused many soldiers and camp attendants to die, while the king helped gather and shelter the survivors. The destruction carried away a thousand soldiers and other attendants, some of whom were found frozen standing beside trees.
