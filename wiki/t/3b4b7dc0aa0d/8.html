This section, in brief. During the rule of Lysislratus at Athens, a great sedition arose in Rome over whether to appoint consuls or military tribunes, followed by a period of anarchy. Six military tribunes were eventually chosen. In Thessaly, Polydorus the Pherean was poisoned by his brother Alexander, who succeeded him and ruled tyrannically. The Aleuadas of Larissa conspired against Alexander the Pheraean and sought aid from Alexander, king of Macedonia, who captured Larissa and Cranon but kept the Thessalian cities under his own control.
