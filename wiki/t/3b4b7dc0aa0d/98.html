This section, in brief written by a model
This unit introduces fragments from lost books of Diodorus Siculus, including material preserved by Photius, Eusebius, and other writers. One fragment reports Evemerus the Messenian’s sacred history, describing terrestrial gods, his voyage to Panch(a)ia, and inscriptions concerning Uranus, Saturn, and Jupiter. Another fragment recounts the rulers of the Corinthian kingdom from Alethes to Cypselus and describes the Bacchidae’s government at Corinth. A final fragment distinguishes Romulus from Aeneas, dates the founding of Rome, and relates traditions about Aeneas, Ascanius, and Alba.
