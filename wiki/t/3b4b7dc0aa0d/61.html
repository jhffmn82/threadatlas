This section, in brief. Antigonus defeated Alcetas in Pisidia, captured many of his commanders, and forced Alcetas to seek refuge in Termessus, where Alcetas was betrayed and killed himself. Antigonus then pursued wider control in Asia, confronting Aridaeus, seizing money intended for the kings, and attempting to remove provincial governors. After Antipater’s death, Polyperchon became protector of the kings in Macedonia, while Cassander plotted to displace him and Antigonus pursued sovereignty. Eumenes, previously defeated and besieged by Antigonus, was released after agreeing to cooperate with him and soon began rebuilding his forces.
