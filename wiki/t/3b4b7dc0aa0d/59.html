This section, in brief. After Antipater and Craterus transported their army out of Europe, Neoptolemus's treachery against Eumenes was discovered, and Eumenes defeated him, causing many Macedonians to join Eumenes's army. Antipater and Craterus divided their forces, but Eumenes defeated Craterus's army in Cappadocia, killing Craterus and Neoptolemus. Perdiccas then invaded Egypt, but his assault and attempted crossing of the Nile failed, leading his soldiers and commanders to turn against him and kill him. Ptolemy gained the Macedonians' favor, while Eumenes was condemned by them after news of his victory arrived.
