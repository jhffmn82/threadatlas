This section, in brief written by a model
Demetrius, acting on Antigonus's orders, liberated Athens by capturing the Piraeus and Munychia, and then restored the city's former privileges. He crossed to Cyprus, defeated Menelaus near Salamis, besieged the city with large engines including the Helepolis, and defeated Ptolemy in a major sea battle. Ptolemy withdrew to Egypt, while Demetrius took the cities of Cyprus and sent news of his victory to Antigonus. Antigonus, Demetrius, Ptolemy, and several other commanders then assumed the title of king.
