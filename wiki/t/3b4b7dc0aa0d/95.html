This section, in brief. The Rhodians ended a year-long war with Demetrius, rewarding loyal citizens and freeing and enrolling brave slaves, while repairing and beautifying their city. They honored Ptolemy, Cassander, Lysimachus, and others with statues, and dedicated a grove and gallery to Ptolemy after consulting the oracle of Ammon. Demetrius ratified peace with the Rhodians, went to Greece, freed the city of the Chalcidians from a Boeotian garrison, and prepared, with the Aetolians, to fight Polyperchon and Cassander. Eumelus, king of the Cimmerian Bosphorus, died after reigning six years, and his son Spartacus succeeded him.
