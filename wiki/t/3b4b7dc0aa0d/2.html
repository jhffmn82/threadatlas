This section, in brief written by a model
The Lacedaemonians captured Mantinea after diverting the river into the city and forced the Mantineans to return to their former villages. Dionysius expanded his influence around the Adriatic Sea, supported the Illyrians, founded settlements, raided Etruria, and prepared for war against the Carthaginians. The Carthaginians defeated Dionysius at Cronium after his earlier victory at Cabala, and peace was made on terms favorable to Carthage. In Greece, the Lacedaemonians seized the Cadmea at Thebes and continued their war against the Olynthians, suffering a defeat under Teleutias before renewing their preparations.
