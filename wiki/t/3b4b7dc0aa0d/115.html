This section, in brief. The unit concerns Hannibal's skill as a general and his command of a diverse army. Hannibal maintained his army's discipline without mutinies, despite differences in nationality, temperament, and language. He led large armies through Italy for seventeen years without lacking money or provisions, defeating powerful nations and winning every battle described. His campaigns destroyed cities and almost depopulated Italy, while his strategy overcame forces strengthened by foreign auxiliaries and allies.
