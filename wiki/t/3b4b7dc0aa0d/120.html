This section, in brief. The unit describes the cruelty of Ptolemy Physcon in Egypt, the virtues and death of Viriathus among the Lusitanians, and the atrocities of Diegylis in Thrace. It recounts the conduct of Viriathus at his marriage and the honors given at his burial. It also relates Pompey's capture of Lagnetia after the Lagnetians turned against their Numantian auxiliaries. The text further records the reactions of the Egyptians, the actions of Attalus, and the conduct of Ptolemy's general Hierax.
