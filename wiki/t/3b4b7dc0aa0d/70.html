This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes a battle between Antigonus and Eumenes, including the deployment of their cavalry, infantry, elephants, and commanders. Python's forces initially harassed Eumenes's right wing, but Eumenes's troops and elephants drove them toward the mountains, while Antigonus later routed Eudamus's forces and withdrew to Media. Both sides suffered casualties, and Antigonus secured the right to bury the dead, creating uncertainty about the battle's victor. The account then describes Ceteus's funeral, the Indian widow-burning law, and the self-immolation of one of his wives before Eumenes marched to Gabene.
