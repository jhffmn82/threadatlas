This section, in brief. The unit describes abuses by rulers and commanders, including Charopes, Orophernes, Prusias, the counterfeit Philip, and Demetrius of Syria. It contrasts these acts with the clemency and humanity shown by Philip son of Amyntas, Alexander son of Philip, the Romans, Publius Scipio Africanus, and Caius Julius Caesar. It also recounts Roman conquests and destructions involving Greece, Corinth, Carthage, Macedonia, and other regions. Several episodes present sacrilege, treachery, and broken faith as followed by punishment or failure.
