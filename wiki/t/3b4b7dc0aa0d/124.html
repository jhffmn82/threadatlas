This section, in brief. Pompoedius led armed Marsi and other men toward Rome to demand citizenship, but Caius Domitius persuaded him to approach the Senate peacefully, preventing violence. Lucius Sylla gained renown through military achievements, while Mithridates won support in Asia by treating Roman prisoners leniently. Gaius Marius’s greed helped provoke civil wars and severe calamities in Italy, including proscriptions and mass killings. Fimbria encouraged plunder and violence, whereas Cn. Pompeius distinguished himself through discipline, military training, and victory over Junius Brutus.
