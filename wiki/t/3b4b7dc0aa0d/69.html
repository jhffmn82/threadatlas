Eumenes learned that Antigonus was marching into Persia and moved to meet him, but illness delayed his march. The two armies approached one another without fighting, while Antigonus tried unsuccessfully to persuade Eumenes's governors and Macedonians to defect. Eumenes used a false report to make Antigonus halt his advance, allowing Eumenes's army to march toward Gabene, a fertile and defensible region. Antigonus eventually pursued, and both generals used stratagems before drawing up their armies for battle.
