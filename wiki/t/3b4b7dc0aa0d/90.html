Agathocles took the title of king, conquered Utica and the Horse-castle, and brought much of the African coast and interior under his power before sailing to Sicily. In Sicily, his captains defeated Xenodochus, and Agathocles captured several cities, but Dinocrates later gathered a large army of exiles and forced him into a running fight. In Africa, Archagathus divided his forces, but the Carthaginians defeated Eumachus and Eschrion, destroyed much of the Greek army, and blockaded Tunis. Agathocles escaped Syracuse with a fleet, defeated the Carthaginians in a naval battle, restored supplies to Syracuse, defeated the Agrigentines at Agrigentum, and massacred his suspected Syracusan opponents.
