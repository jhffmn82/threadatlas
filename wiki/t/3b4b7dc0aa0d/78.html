This section, in brief. The battle between Demetrius and Ptolemy, with Seleucian forces involved, ended in victory for Ptolemy and Seleucus after their troops repelled Demetrius’s elephants and routed much of his cavalry. Gaza fell when disorderly retreating soldiers and baggage blocked the gates, allowing Ptolemy’s troops to enter. Demetrius lost more than five thousand men, including prominent commanders, and over eight thousand were taken prisoner. Ptolemy later advanced through Phoenicia while Demetrius sought reinforcements and sent for help from his father, Antigonus.
