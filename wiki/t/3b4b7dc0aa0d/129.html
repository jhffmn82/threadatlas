This section, in brief written by a model
Alexander marched again to the river Oxus and then led part of his army against the Sogdians, who had revolted and occupied forts and castles. He divided his army, reduced much of Sogdiana, and gathered his forces at Maracanda before sending commanders against remaining opponents and the Scythians. The Sogdians later took refuge on a steep, snow-covered rock where Oxyartes had placed his wife and daughters. Alexander's Macedonians climbed the rock by night, causing the defenders to surrender, and captured many women and children, including Oxyartes's family.
