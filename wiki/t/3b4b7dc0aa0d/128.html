This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes Alexander’s suppression of a rebellion among the Sogdians and Bactrians, including the capture of several fortified cities and Cyropolis. His forces killed the male defenders, enslaved or carried away women and children, and subdued the remaining prisoners. The Asiatic Scythians then approached the river called Tanais in the text, identified in a note as the Iaxartes, and Alexander crossed it and defeated them. The unit also recounts Alexander’s decision to spare several Sogdian noblemen after being impressed by their courage and promises of loyalty.
