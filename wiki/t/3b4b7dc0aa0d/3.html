This section, in brief written by a model
Cleombrotus succeeded Agesipolis as king of Lacedaemon and reigned for nine years, while the Lacedaemonians sent Pelopidas against the Olynthians and brought them under Spartan rule. A plague and internal disorder weakened Carthage, though the Carthaginians later subdued the Africans and recovered Sardinia. The Boeotian War began when the Lacedaemonians detained the Cadmea and drove many Thebans into exile; aided by Athens, the exiles returned, and the Thebans and Athenians forced the Lacedaemonian garrison to surrender. Athens then helped organize a confederacy against the Lacedaemonians, with a general diet or senate meeting at Athens and one representative from each allied city.
