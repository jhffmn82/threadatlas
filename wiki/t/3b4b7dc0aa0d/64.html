Nicanor, holding Munychia for Cassander, deceives the Athenians, reinforces the fortress, and seizes the walls of the Piraeus and the harbor entrance. The Athenians appeal to Polyperchon and Olympias, but Alexander, the son of Polyperchon, retains Munychia and the Piraeus while secretly negotiating with Nicanor. Athens restores democratic magistrates, condemns Phocion and other supporters of the oligarchy, and sends them to be executed. Phocion and his associates are put to death by hemlock and their bodies are cast out of Attica unburied.
