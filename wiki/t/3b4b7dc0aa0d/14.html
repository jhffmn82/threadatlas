This section, in brief. This unit introduces Philip son of Amyntas, who became king of Macedon after Perdiccas was killed fighting the Illyrians. Philip strengthened the Macedonians, defeated an Athenian force supporting Argæus, made peace with Athens, subdued the Pæones, and defeated the Illyrians under Bardyllis. The Illyrians then surrendered the Macedonian cities they held and made peace, while Philip prepared Macedon for the later expedition against Persia that his death prevented him from undertaking.
