This section, in brief written by a model
Demetrius was initiated at Athens, moved his army into Thessaly, captured Larissa and other places, and restored several cities to their former laws and liberties. He made a conditional peace with Cassander so that he could obey Antigonus's order to transport his forces to Asia, where he recovered Ephesus and subdued cities near the Hellespont and Pontus. Cassander later recovered the coastal cities and sent Plistarchus to assist Lysimachus, but Plistarchus suffered a disastrous shipwreck. Ptolemy withdrew to Egypt after hearing a false report, while Seleucus entered Cappadocia and joined Lysimachus in preparing for war against Antigonus.
