This section, in brief written by a model
Books XIV–XVI recount wars, revolutions, tyrannies, and shifting alliances among the Greek cities, Persia, Sicily, and Italy. They describe the rule of the Thirty at Athens, the rise and actions of Dionysius I, the Persian expeditions involving Cyrus and Artaxerxes, and the wars of the Lacedaemonians, Thebans, Athenians, and others. The narrative includes the campaigns of Agesilaus, Epaminondas, Pelopidas, Timoleon, and Philip, as well as the Social War, the Phocian War, and the Battle of Chaeronea. It ends with Philip's preparations for war against Persia and his murder, while also recounting the careers and deaths of Dionysius the Younger, Dion, and Timoleon.
