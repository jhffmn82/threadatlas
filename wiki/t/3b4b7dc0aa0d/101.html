This section, in brief. The unit describes the early struggle between the Romans, Carthaginians, and their allies for control of Sicily, beginning with the siege of Messana and the Roman intervention. Hiero made peace with Rome for fourteen years, while the Romans and Carthaginians continued campaigns against cities including Agrigentum, Mytistratus, Camarina, Enna, and Panormus. The Carthaginians later defeated a Roman army in Africa under Attilius after hiring Xanthippus, and Roman fleets suffered major shipwrecks near Camarina and elsewhere. The unit ends with Cecilius defeating Asdrubal's undisciplined Carthaginian army at Panormus and capturing sixty elephants.
