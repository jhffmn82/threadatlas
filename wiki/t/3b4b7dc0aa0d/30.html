This section, in brief. Philip of Macedon captured Elatea and prepared to invade Attica, causing alarm in Athens. Demosthenes persuaded the Athenians to seek Boeotian assistance, and the Athenians and Boeotians united at Chaeronea against Philip. Philip and his son Alexander defeated them in the Battle of Chaeronea, after which Philip made peace with Athens and Boeotia. Lysicles was executed by the Athenians, while Philip was appointed general of all Greece and planned a war against Persia; Timoleon also died in Syracuse after governing there for eight years.
