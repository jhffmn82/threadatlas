This section, in brief. The unit describes a Persian revolt, Tachos's war against Persia, and the conflict between Tachos and his son Nectanabis for control of Egypt. Orontes and other Persian rebels betray their allies, while Datames defeats treacherous forces in Cappadocia before Artaxerxes arranges his death. Agesilaus helps restore Tachos after defeating the Egyptians, and later events include a civic conflict at Megalopolis, Alexander's attack on Peparethos, and Chares's abuses in Corcyra.
