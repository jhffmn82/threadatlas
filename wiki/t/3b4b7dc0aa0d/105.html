This section, in brief. The unit describes Antiochus's siege of Jerusalem, his eventual pardon of the Jews, and the demolition of Jerusalem's walls. It recounts the servile war in Sicily, where abused slaves under Eunus and Cleon seized cities, defeated Roman forces, and were eventually suppressed by Rupilius. It also describes the Roman difficulties caused by the Cimbrians and several smaller slave rebellions in Italy, including one led by Titus Minutius at Capua.
