This section, in brief. Darius assembled a vast army, equipped it with longer weapons and hooked chariots, and marched from Babylon toward Nineveh, encamping at Arbela. He made two peace offers to Alexander, but Alexander rejected them and demanded that Darius submit or fight for the empire. At the battle of Arbela, the Persian chariots initially terrified the Macedonians, but Alexander's countermeasure caused many to turn back or be destroyed. Alexander eventually routed Darius, although Mazaeus severely pressured the Macedonian wing before Parmenio rallied it; the Persians suffered over ninety thousand deaths, while the Macedonians lost about five hundred.
