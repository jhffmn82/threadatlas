This section, in brief. Antigonus marched from Susiana to Babylon, where Seleucus refused to account for the province's revenues and fled to Egypt, where Ptolemy received him. Ptolemy, Cassander, and Lysimachus formed a league against Antigonus after he rejected their demands for territorial concessions, while Antigonus sought allies and prepared fleets. Antigonus entered Phoenicia, built ships, conquered Gaza and Joppa, and besieged Tyre until it surrendered after thirteen months. The opposing commanders divided their forces among Cyprus, Caria, and Peloponnesus, while Antigonus sent fleets to the Peloponnesus and other islands.
