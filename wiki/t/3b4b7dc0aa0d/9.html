This section, in brief written by a model
The Lacedaemonians suffered defeats in Arcadia and Laconia, while the Arcadians, Argives, Elians, and Boeotians joined forces with Epaminondas and Pelopidas against Sparta. The Athenians aided the Lacedaemonians despite their former enmity, but Epaminondas’s army invaded Laconia, attacked Sparta, and devastated the country before withdrawing. Epaminondas then persuaded the allies to rebuild Messene and restore it to Messenian inhabitants. Later, Lycomedes captured Pellene, and Epaminondas broke through the wall and trench between Cenchreae and Lechaeum before attacking Corinth, where Chabrias and the Athenians repelled the Boeotians.
