This section, in brief. Alexander neglected the Macedonians' sacred day of Bacchus and sacrificed to Castor and Pollux instead. During a drunken dispute, Clitus criticized Alexander's imitation of barbarian customs and claimed credit for saving Alexander at the Granicus, after which Alexander killed him with a spear or sarissa. Alexander then deeply regretted the act, refused food and drink, and reportedly attempted to kill himself. Priests told him that his misfortune resulted from neglecting Bacchus, and he eventually sacrificed to the god.
