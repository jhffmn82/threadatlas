This section, in brief written by a model
Ambassadors from many regions came to Alexander to congratulate him, offer gifts, make alliances, settle accusations, or oppose the restoration of exiles. Alexander arranged an immense funeral for Hephaestion, costing more than twelve thousand talents, and ordered sacrifices to him as a demigod. Numerous prodigies were interpreted as signs of Alexander’s approaching death, and he died after a severe illness, having reigned twelve years and seven months. Some writers attributed his death to poisoning arranged by Antipater, while Darius’s mother, Sysigambis, died of grief and hunger five days later.
