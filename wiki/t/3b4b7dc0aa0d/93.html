This section, in brief. The Rhodians prepared to defend their city against Demetrius by mobilizing citizens, inhabitants, foreigners, freed servants, money, weapons, engines, and ships. Demetrius attacked the harbour and walls with floating engines, towers, ships, and soldiers, but the Rhodians repeatedly resisted, destroyed or damaged his machines and vessels, and captured or killed many of his men. A storm and a Rhodian sortie forced four hundred Demetrians to surrender, after which reinforcements arrived from Gnossians and Ptolemy. Elsewhere, Agathocles defeated Dinocrates and the exiles at Gorgius, then treacherously killed many of them after promising them safety; the Romans also defeated the Samnites and recovered allied cities.
