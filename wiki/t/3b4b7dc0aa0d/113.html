This section, in brief. The Carthaginians, after twenty-four years of war with the Romans for Sicily, were driven into severe distress when their mercenary soldiers revolted after being denied their pay. Spondius and the other rebel captains refused the Carthaginians permission to bury their dead and ordered cruel punishments for prisoners. Amilcar retaliated against the rebels by having captives tortured and trampled by elephants. The Hippacrineans and Uticans also revolted, while Amilcar later gained great honors and was briefly made general of all Libya after the war in Africa ended.
