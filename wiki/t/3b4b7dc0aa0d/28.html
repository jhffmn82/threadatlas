This section, in brief. Timoleon defeated or expelled tyrants in Sicily, liberated several cities, and gained the submission of many Greek and Sicilian communities. Carthage responded by raising troops, hiring foreign mercenaries, collecting money, and preparing a large fleet against him. Philip of Macedon conducted a prolonged and technically formidable siege of Perinthus, but Persian and Byzantine aid strengthened the defenders. Philip then divided his army, leaving part at Perinthus while marching with the rest to besiege Byzantium.
