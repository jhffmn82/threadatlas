This section, in brief written by a model
Olympias returned to Macedonia with Polyperchon and Alexander's son after Eurydice gathered an army, causing the Macedonians to abandon Eurydice and Philip III Arrhidaeus. Olympias imprisoned Eurydice and Philip, had Philip killed, and drove Eurydice to strangle herself; she then killed Nicanor and one hundred of Cassander's supporters, provoking hatred among many Macedonians. In Asia, Eumenes crossed the Tigris, reached Susa, received support from the eastern provincial governors, and organized their forces under a council meeting in the king's pavilion. Antigonus prepared an expedition against Eumenes, while several former commanders escaped from imprisonment but were eventually recaptured after a sixteen-month siege.
