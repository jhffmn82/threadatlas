This section, in brief. Philomelus raised Phocian forces, defeated the Locrians at the Rocks of Phaedra, and later used the sacred treasures to recruit mercenaries for the Phocian war. The Boeotians, Thessalians, Locrians, and other allies opposed the Phocians, while the Athenians and Lacedaemonians supported them. Philomelus was defeated by the Boeotians and killed himself by leaping from a precipice, after which Onomarchus became general. Onomarchus renewed the war, achieved several successes, and was ultimately defeated and killed by Philip of Macedon in Thessaly.
