This section, in brief. Alexander marched against Massaga, the greatest city of the country, after the barbarians dispersed into separate cities. The city was defended by seven thousand mercenary Indians, who fought fiercely against the Macedonians during a four-day siege. After the governor was killed and many defenders were lost or wounded, the mercenary Indians agreed to join Alexander but camped separately and planned to flee. Alexander surrounded their hill at night and put them all to the sword, then stormed and captured Massaga.
