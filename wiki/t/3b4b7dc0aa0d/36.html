This section, in brief. The Marmareofies, who inhabited a great fortified rock in the utmost borders of Lycia, attacked the rear of Alexander's army, killing many Macedonians and taking prisoners and carriage-horses. Alexander besieged the rock, but the Marmareofies resisted for two days and refused to make peace. Their leaders ultimately decided that the inhabitants should die and that the able young men should escape through the enemy's camp. The young men instead burned the houses and fled to the nearby mountains, making each house a sepulchre.
