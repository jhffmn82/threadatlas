Alexander continued his campaign in India, sending Hephastion to subdue a neighboring prince’s kingdom for Porus while conquering territories including those of the Audrastians and Cathari. He received the submission of Sopithes and Phigeus, learned from Phigeus and Porus about the Ganges and the powerful Gandarites, and considered invading them despite the exhaustion of his army. During the advance he witnessed Dioxippus defeat the Macedonian Coragus, though Dioxippus later killed himself after being falsely accused of theft. Alexander’s river voyage was endangered by whirlpools, and during the siege of the Oxydracee and Mallii capital he was severely wounded before being rescued by his guards.
