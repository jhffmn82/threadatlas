This section, in brief written by a model
The slave revolt and general lawlessness devastated Sicily, where rebellious slaves and impoverished free people committed widespread robbery, murder, and destruction in the absence of a Roman praetor. Saturninus was rebuked and removed from office by the Roman Senate for mismanaging the grain supply, but later became tribune of the people after reforming his way of life. The Roman people recalled Metellus from banishment after his son repeatedly pleaded for him, despite reluctance to establish a precedent for restoring exiles. The unit contrasts Roman luxury and moral corruption with the just provincial administrations of Quintus Mutius Scaevola, Lucius Asellius, and their associates, and concludes by introducing the young Marcus Livius Drusus.
