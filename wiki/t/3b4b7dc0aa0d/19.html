This section, in brief. The Sacred War began when Philomelus and the Phocians seized the temple at Delphos after the Amphictyons condemned the Phocians for cultivating land claimed by the oracle. Philomelus defeated the Locrians, fortified Delphos, destroyed records of the Amphictyonic decrees, and sought support from Athens, Lacedaemon, and other Greek cities. The Boeotians and Locrians opposed him in defense of the oracle. The unit also recounts the reported discovery of the Delphic oracle, the origin of the tripod, and Philomelus's coercion of Pythia into giving an ambiguous prophecy.
