This section, in brief written by a model
Cassander, excluded from the chief command of Macedonia, secretly went to Asia and sought Antigonus's support, claiming that Ptolemy would join them. Polyperchon, anticipating war with Cassander, consulted Macedonian leaders and resolved to restore the cities of Greece to their liberties and abolish oligarchies. A decree promised the Greeks peace, the governmental arrangements they had enjoyed under Philip and Alexander, the recall of many exiles, and the restoration of certain estates and franchises. Polyperchon also ordered political opponents in the cities punished, invited Olympias to return to Macedonia, and offered Eumenes support against Antigonus in defense of the kings.
