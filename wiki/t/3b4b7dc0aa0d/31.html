This section, in brief. Philip began a war against the Persians and sent Attalus and Parmenio into Asia to free the Greek cities there. He consulted the Delphic oracle, whose ambiguous answer he interpreted as predicting victory over the Persian king, and he celebrated the marriage of his daughter Cleopatra to Alexander, king of Epirus. At the festivities, Neoptolemus recited verses that further encouraged Philip in his Persian expedition. During a ceremony at the theatre at Aegae, Pausanias of Orestis stabbed Philip and was killed while attempting to flee.
