This section, in brief written by a model
While Alexander recovered from his wound, about three thousand Greek colonists in Bactria and Sogdiana rebelled, but all were killed after his death. Alexander then held a feast, where Dioxippus defeated Coragus in a duel, was later falsely accused of theft, and killed himself. Alexander continued down the Indus, receiving submissions and conquering territories, including those of the Brachmanes, before beginning the siege of Harmatelia. During fighting near Harmatelia, Ptolemy was poisoned but was healed after Alexander found an effective herb in a dream.
