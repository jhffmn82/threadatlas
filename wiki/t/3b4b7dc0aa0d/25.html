This section, in brief. The unit recounts the punishments that befell Philomelus, Onomarchus, Phayllus, and Phalaecus after the seizure and robbing of the oracle and temple at Delphi. Phalaecus and his mercenaries were driven from Lyctus in Crete, and Phalaecus later died while besieging Sidon, while the remaining mercenaries suffered defeat, enslavement, or execution. Archidamus assisted the Tarentines after helping the Lyctians, but he was killed in battle and was criticized for supporting the Phocians in the seizure of Delphi. The account concludes that divine vengeance punished the sacrilegious participants and their allies, while Philip prospered after defending the oracle, and then returns from the Sacred War to other matters.
