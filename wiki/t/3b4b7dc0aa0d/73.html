This section, in brief. Cassander captured Pydna after a prolonged siege that reduced Olympias and her defenders to extreme famine. Olympias surrendered, was condemned by the Macedonians, and was killed by the relatives of those she had executed. Cassander then consolidated his rule by marrying Thessalonice, founding Cassandria, imprisoning Roxana and her son, and preparing an expedition into Peloponnesus. He rebuilt Thebes before compelling several Peloponnesian communities to join him.
