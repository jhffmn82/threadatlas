This section, in brief. The Social War continued between the Athenians and the Chians, Rhodians, Coians, and Byzantines, with both sides preparing for a naval battle. Chares, Iphicrates, and Timotheus commanded the Athenian forces, but Iphicrates and Timotheus refused to fight during a storm and were fined and removed from command after Chares accused them of treason. Chares later joined Pharnabazus against the Persian king's forces and defeated them, but the Persian king's complaints and the rumored promise of Persian aid to Athens's enemies led the Athenians to make peace with the revolters, ending the war after four years. In Macedonia, Philip defeated the allied Thracians, Paeones, and Illyrians before they could assemble their forces and forced them under the Macedonian kingdom.
