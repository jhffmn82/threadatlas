The unit recounts Agathocles's successes in Africa and Amilcar's unsuccessful efforts to capture Syracuse, including the deception that followed a Carthaginian defeat and the relief of Syracuse by Agathocles's ships. Agathocles captured or received the submission of many African towns, while the Carthaginians repeatedly attacked Tunis and recovered some territory. In Macedonia and the Greek world, Cassander, Ptolemy, Antigonus, Polyperchon, and others maneuvered for power. In the Cimmerian Bosphorus, Eumelus, Satyrus, and Prytanis fought for the kingdom after Parisades's death, with Eumelus ultimately prevailing and later dying in a chariot accident.
