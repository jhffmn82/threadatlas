This section, in brief. The unit warns especially against covetousness and recounts how ambition, fraud, revenge, and cruelty brought disasters to rulers and cities. Antigonus, king of Asia, was defeated and killed after making war against Ptolemy Lagus, Seleucus, Lysimachus, and Cassander, while Agathocles conquered Crotona through deception and conducted campaigns in Italy. Agathocles was later poisoned and killed through the plotting of Archagathus and Menon of Aegista, after which the Syracusans restored their democracy. Foreign soldiers then massacred their hosts and seized Messana, naming it Mamertina.
