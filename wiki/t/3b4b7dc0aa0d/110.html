This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the ambitions, actions, and reputations of several Hellenistic rulers and historians, especially Agathocles, Lysimachus, Dromichaetes, Demetrius, and Apollodorus. Dromichaetes captures Lysimachus but treats him generously, while Seleucus rejects a proposal to kill the imprisoned Demetrius. The text condemns Timaeus and Callias for distorting their histories of Agathocles, in opposite directions. It also recounts the massacres and seizures carried out by the Mamertines and Campanians, the cruelty of Apollodorus, and Pyrrhus’s failure to punish Gauls who plundered royal tombs.
