This section, in brief. Runaway servants and others in Italy formed a powerful community called the Brutii after capturing several cities. In Sicily, Philistus unsuccessfully invaded the Leontines and later lost a sea fight against the Syracusans, while Dionysius's castle remained under his supporters. Factional conflict and unpaid soldiers weakened Syracuse, and Nypsius's surprise attack allowed Dionysius's forces to capture and sack the city. Dion then returned with his mercenaries, defeated the attackers, relieved Syracuse, and was granted supreme authority by the people.
