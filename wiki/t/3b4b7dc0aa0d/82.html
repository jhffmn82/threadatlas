This section, in brief. After being defeated by the Carthaginians at Himera, Agathocles returned to Syracuse and resolved to invade Africa with a select force. He left his brother Antander in command of Syracuse, seized money and property, and sailed with sixty ships when the Carthaginian fleet was drawn away. After surviving pursuit and an eclipse of the sun that alarmed his soldiers, Agathocles landed in Africa, burned his ships, and captured a wealthy city by storm.
