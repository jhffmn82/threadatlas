This section, in brief. The unit describes a dispute over the adoration of Alexander, initiated by Anaxarchus and opposed by Callisthenes. Callisthenes argues that Alexander deserves the highest human honors but not divine worship, while some Persians and others proceed to adore Alexander. The text then recounts the pages' conspiracy against Alexander, led by Hermolaus and Sostrates after Hermolaus was punished during a hunt. The conspirators are discovered, and Callisthenes is accused of encouraging them; the sources disagree about how Callisthenes died.
