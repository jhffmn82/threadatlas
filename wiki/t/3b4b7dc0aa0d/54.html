This section, in brief written by a model
After Alexander the Macedonian died without issue, a succession crisis arose, and Aridaeus, the son of Philip, was made king under the name Philip. Perdiccas received executive authority, while Alexander's commanders were assigned governorships across the empire. Perdiccas ordered the execution of Meleager and other turbulent soldiers, and the Macedonians rejected Alexander's costly plans recorded in his notebooks. The Greeks in the upper provinces revolted under Python's command against them, but the Macedonians ultimately broke their capitulation and killed them all.
