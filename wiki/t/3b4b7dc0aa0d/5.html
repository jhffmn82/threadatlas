This section, in brief written by a model
The Triballians invaded Thrace and suffered heavy losses after attacking the Abderites, but Chabrias later drove them out before being assassinated. Timotheus strengthened Athenian influence, allied with Acetas, and defeated the Spartans in a sea-fight at Leucades, while the Thebans routed a larger Spartan force at Orchomenos. Artaxerxes promoted a general peace in Greece so that he could recruit mercenaries against Egypt, but the Thebans rejected its terms. Epaminondas defended the Theban position, and renewed seditions and conflicts spread through Peloponnesus and other Greek cities.
