Ptolemy secured Egypt and formed a league with Antipater, while Perdiccas conquered Cappadocia and assigned it to Eumenes of Cardia. Leonatus was defeated and killed by the Greeks in Thessaly, but Craterus and Antipater later defeated the Greeks and compelled the Athenians and other cities to make peace. In the war in Cyrene, Thimbron initially prevailed but was eventually defeated and captured by Ophelas, after which Cyrene and neighboring cities became subject to Ptolemy. The unit also describes the settlement imposed on Athens and the destruction of the Cyrenian political order through internal conflict and Ptolemaic intervention.
