This section, in brief. Clodius took command of the Roman army besieging Lilybsum and criticized his predecessors, but acted rashly, punished soldiers severely, and was arrogant about his birth. Amilcar is praised as a courageous, prudent, industrious, and ambitious Carthaginian general. Hanno captured Hecatompylos, spared the city after taking three thousand hostages, and was rewarded with the inhabitants' hospitality. The wife of Attilius cruelly confined the captives, causing Bostar's death, until the magistrates intervened, punished the Attilii, and freed Amilcar.
