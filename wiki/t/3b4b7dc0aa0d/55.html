This section, in brief. After Alexander ordered the restoration of many Greek exiles, the Athenians and Aetolians opposed the measure and later declared war against Antipater, beginning the Lamian War. Athens organized a Greek alliance, raised forces under Leosthenes, and proclaimed that it would defend the common liberty of Greece and remove Macedonian garrisons. Leosthenes defeated the Boeotians and then forced Antipater to retreat into Lamia, where the Macedonians withstood a siege until Leosthenes was mortally wounded. After Leosthenes’s death, Antiphilus was appointed commander, while Craterus was summoned from Cilicia with Macedonian reinforcements.
