This section, in brief. Dionysius the younger neglected military affairs, made peace with Carthage, fought carelessly against the Lucanians, and ultimately lost his Sicilian dominion. Dion fled Sicily to Corinth, gathered mercenaries, and returned with the aim of restoring liberty to Syracuse and other Sicilians. Civil war in Euboea ended in an agreement, while the Athenians fought the three-year Social War against Chios, Rhodes, Cos, and Byzantium, with Chabrias dying in a naval battle. Philip captured Amphipolis and Pydna, allied with the Olynthians, developed Philippi and its gold mines, and strengthened Macedonia through wealth and military power.
