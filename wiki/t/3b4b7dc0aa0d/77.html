This section, in brief. Callantinians and Istrians rebel against Lysimachus, but he defeats their allies and besieges the Callantinian city before withdrawing to face forces sent by Antigonus. Antigonus campaigns to restore Greek city liberties, while Cassander contests him in Greece, Euboea, and Macedonia. Ptolemy suppresses revolts in Cyrene and Cyprus, campaigns in Cilicia, and then invades Coele-Syria with Seleucus as Demetrius prepares to oppose him. The unit ends with the armies of Ptolemy and Seleucus drawing up against Demetrius near Gaza.

In the front of this wing were placed light-armed soldiers, who were commanded to ply the elephants and their riders with darts and arrows as they came on.
