This section, in brief. Alexander marched into Egypt, where the cities submitted to him, and then undertook a journey to the temple of Ammon. During the journey, rain, stored water, and crows helped Alexander and his companions cross the desert and reach the grove of Ammon. At the temple, the oracle declared Alexander divinely favored, promised him victory, and confirmed that Philip’s murderers had been punished. Alexander then returned to Egypt, founded Alexandria, and arranged its streets, walls, palace, and defenses.
