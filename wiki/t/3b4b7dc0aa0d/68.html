This section, in brief. Antigonus marched from Mesopotamia through Babylon and across the Tigris region in pursuit of Eumenes, but Eumenes defeated his forces at the Coprates River and forced him to retreat toward Media. Antigonus took the difficult Cossean route into Media, suffering heavy losses before reaching its inhabited parts and gathering reinforcements. Eumenes withdrew from Pasitigris to Persepolis, where Peucestes held a magnificent feast for the army. Eumenes used a forged letter and legal proceedings against Siburtius to strengthen his own influence and weaken Peucestes's position, later receiving contributions from the commanders.
