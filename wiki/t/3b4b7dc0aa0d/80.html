This section, in brief. The Nabataeans recover their goods, evade Demetrius’s invasion, and negotiate an end to their conflict with Antigonus by offering gifts and hostages. Demetrius withdraws to Lake Asphaltites, whose bitumen resources Antigonus later tries and fails to exploit. Antigonus then sends Demetrius toward Babylon against Seleucus’s forces, while Roman-Samnite fighting continues in Italy. In Sicily, Agathocles consolidates power through executions and conflict with the Syracusan exiles and Carthaginians.
