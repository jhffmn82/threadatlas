The unit describes the Marsian war between the Romans and Italians, including the Italian attempt to establish a rival government called Italy and its eventual defeat. It then recounts seditions and civil wars in Rome involving Sylla, Marius, Caesar, Pompey, Brutus, Cassius, Antonius, and Augustus. It also explains the alleged Egyptian origin of the Jews, the foundation of Judea and Jerusalem under Moses, and the organization of Jewish laws and religious government. The unit concludes with an account of how fragments of Diodorus Siculus were collected and published.
