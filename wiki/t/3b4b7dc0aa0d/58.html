This section, in brief. Aridssus prepared an elaborate funeral chariot and conveyed Alexander's body from Babylon toward Egypt. Ptolemy received the body in Syria, brought it to Alexandria, and placed it in a temple he built for Alexander instead of sending it to the temple of Ammon. Perdiccas prepared an expedition into Egypt against Ptolemy and sent Eumenes with troops to the Hellespont to prevent Antipater and Craterus from entering Asia. Eumenes strengthened his forces there with cavalry raised from his own province.
