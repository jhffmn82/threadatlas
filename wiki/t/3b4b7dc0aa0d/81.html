This section, in brief. Cassander, Ptolemy, Lysimachus, and Antigonus made peace, but Cassander soon ordered the murder of Roxana and Alexander, the son of Roxana, causing the extinction of the royal line and leading the governors to take royal titles. The Carthaginians sent Amilcar with a large force against Agathocles in Sicily, but a storm destroyed much of their fleet before they landed. Agathocles murdered about four thousand Gelans, then fought Amilcar at the river Himera and was decisively defeated, losing about seven thousand Greeks. After the defeat, several Sicilian cities revolted from Agathocles or submitted to the Carthaginians, while Agathocles withdrew to Syracuse and prepared to carry the war into Africa.
