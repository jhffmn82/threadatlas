This section, in brief. The Arcadians divided between the Tegeans and Mantineans, leading the Tegeans to seek Boeotian aid and the Mantineans to seek Athenian and Lacedaemonian support. Epaminondas led the Boeotian alliance, attempted unsuccessfully to surprise Sparta, and then fought the allied forces at Mantinea. The Boeotians routed the Lacedaemonians after Epaminondas killed their general, but Epaminondas was mortally wounded and died after learning that his side had won. The exhausted Greeks then ended the war through a general offensive and defensive league, although the Lacedaemonians refused to join because of their hostility toward the Messenians.
