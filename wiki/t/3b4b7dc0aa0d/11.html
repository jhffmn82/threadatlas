This section, in brief. Arcadian exiles seized Lassia in Triphylia, leading to war between the Arcadians and Elians, in which the Elians were defeated near Lassia and the Arcadians captured several cities. The Pisates, aided by the Arcadians, took control of the 104th Olympiad and conducted the Olympic games, though the Elians rejected that Olympiad as unlawful. Epaminondas persuaded the Thebans to seek dominion of the sea, and Rhodes, Chios, and Byzantium were brought under Theban obedience, while the Thebans later destroyed Orchomenus after uncovering a conspiracy. The Thessalians sought Theban aid against Alexander of Pherae; Pelopidas won a victory but was killed, and the text praises his services to Thebes, including the recovery of Cadmea and victories over the Lacedaemonians.
