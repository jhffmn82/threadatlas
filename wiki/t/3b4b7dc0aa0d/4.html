This section, in brief. Acoris of Egypt raised a large foreign army against the Persian king and employed Chabrias, but Athens recalled Chabrias after Pharnabazus protested and requested Iphicrates. The Lacedaemonians and Athenians broke their earlier peace after Sphodrias attempted to seize the Piraeus, leading Athens to organize a confederacy and prepare for war. Agesilaus led the Spartan army into Boeotia, but Chabrias and the Thebans avoided a decisive engagement and preserved Thebes. Later, Chabrias defeated Pollis in the Battle of Naxos, giving Athens its first naval victory since the Peloponnesian War.
