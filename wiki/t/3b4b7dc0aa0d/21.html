This section, in brief. Phayllus raised mercenaries and secured support from several cities for the Phocians, but suffered defeats against the Boeotians before dying of a lingering illness. Phalaecus succeeded him as Phocian general, suffered defeats at Chaeronea, and later took and abandoned Chaeronea. The Lacedaemonians and Megalopolitans fought a war involving their allies, while the Thebans intervened on the Megalopolitans' side. The Thebans later sought and received three hundred talents from Artaxerxes to support their efforts in the Phocian war.
