Cassander seized the Piraeus with Nicanor’s support, while Polyperchon attempted to recover Attica and the Peloponnesus for the kings. The Megalopolitans resisted Polyperchon’s siege, defeating his assault and disabling his elephants with concealed spikes. Clitus first defeated the opposing fleet near Byzantium, but Antigonus then engineered a counterattack that destroyed Clitus’s fleet and led to Clitus’s death. Athens made peace with Cassander under terms that left Munychia in his hands, and Demetrius the Phalerian became protector of the city; the unit then introduces Agathocles’s rise to tyranny in Syracuse.
