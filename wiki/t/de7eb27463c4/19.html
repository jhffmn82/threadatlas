The Argonauts reach Salmydessus, where Phineus tells them how to escape the Harpies and pass the Clashing Rocks. They pass the rocks with the help of a dove and Hera, then travel to Colchis and ask Aeetes for the Golden Fleece. Medea promises Jason assistance after he swears to take her as his wife to Greece, and Jason uses her drug to yoke the brazen-footed bulls and defeat the armed men who rise from the dragon’s teeth.
