This section, in brief written by a model
After recovering from his illness following the sacrifice, Heracles sailed to Ilium with eighteen penteconters and an army of willing distinguished men. He left the ships under Oicles and advanced on the city with the other nobles, but Laomedon killed Oicles while fighting. Heracles's forces besieged Laomedon, and Telamon broke through the wall and entered first, followed by Heracles. Heracles captured the city, shot Laomedon and his sons except Podarces, and awarded Hesione, Laomedon's daughter, to Telamon.
