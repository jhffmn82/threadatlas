This section, in brief. Zethus married Thebe, while Amphion married Niobe, who bore seven sons and seven daughters according to this account, though other traditions gave different numbers. Niobe claimed to be more blessed with children than Latona, provoking Artemis and Apollo to kill her children, after which Niobe became a stone at Sipylus. After Amphion’s death, Laius ruled Thebes, but Oedipus unknowingly killed Laius, solved the Sphinx’s riddle, became king, and married Jocasta. When the truth was revealed, Jocasta hanged herself, Oedipus blinded himself, was expelled from Thebes, and later died at Colonus.
