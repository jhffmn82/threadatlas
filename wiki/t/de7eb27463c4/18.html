Pelias promised Alcestis to the man who could yoke a lion and a boar, and Apollo enabled Admetus to win her. After Admetus forgot Artemis at his marriage sacrifice, he was granted release from death if someone voluntarily died for him, and Alcestis did so; she was later restored according to one account by the Maiden and according to another by Hercules. Pelias later learned from an oracle to beware the one-sandaled man, and Jason became that man while crossing the Anaurus; Pelias therefore sent him to fetch the Golden Fleece from Colchis. Jason gathered the Argonauts aboard the Argo, whose voyage included Lemnos and Mysia, where Hercules and Polyphemus were left behind after Hylas disappeared.
