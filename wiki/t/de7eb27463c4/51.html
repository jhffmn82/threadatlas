This section, in brief written by a model
Tiresias tells the Thebans that they will win if Menoeceus, son of Creon, sacrifices himself to Ares, and Menoeceus kills himself before the gates. During the battle at Thebes, the Cadmeans are driven back, Zeus strikes Capaneus with a thunderbolt, and the Argives begin to flee. Eteocles and Polynices fight a single combat over the kingdom of Thebes and kill each other. Melanippus wounds Tydeus, Athena attempts to make Tydeus immortal, but withholds the gift after Tydeus consumes Melanippus's brains; Zeus later makes Amphiaraus immortal after the earth swallows him with his chariot and charioteer.
