This section, in brief written by a model
Medea came to Athens, married Aegeus, and bore a son named Medus. She later plotted against Theseus and was driven from Athens with Medus. Medus conquered many barbarians, named the territory under him Media, and died while campaigning against the Indians. Medea returned to Colchis, killed Perses after finding that he had deposed Aeetes, and restored the kingdom to Aeetes.
