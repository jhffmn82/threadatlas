This section, in brief. Apollo and Poseidon assumed human form and fortified Pergamum for Laomedon in return for wages, but Laomedon refused to pay them. Apollo sent a pestilence and Poseidon sent a sea monster that carried away people from the plain, and Laomedon exposed his daughter Hesione to it after oracles promised deliverance. Hercules offered to rescue Hesione in exchange for the horses Zeus had given to Tros, killed the sea monster, and saved her. When Laomedon again refused payment, Hercules threatened to wage war on Troy.
