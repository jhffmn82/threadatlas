This section, in brief written by a model
Sky first ruled the whole world and fathered the Hundred-handed, the Cyclopes, and the Titans with Earth. Sky cast the Cyclopes into Tartarus, while Earth persuaded the Titans to attack him, and Cronus overthrew him with an adamantine sickle. Cronus then ruled, but he swallowed his children because of a prophecy that he would be dethroned by his own son. Rhea secretly gave birth to Zeus in a cave of Dicte and arranged for him to be raised in Crete while Cronus swallowed a stone in his place.
