This section, in brief. Pluto, with Zeus’s help, secretly abducts Persephone. Demeter searches for Persephone throughout the earth and, after learning that Pluto took her, comes to Eleusis in the guise of a woman. Persephone eats a pomegranate seed given by Pluto and is required to spend one-third of each year with him and the rest with the gods.
