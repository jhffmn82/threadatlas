This section, in brief. Perseus beheaded Medusa with Athena’s guidance, rescued Andromeda from a sea monster, and turned Phineus and his fellow conspirators to stone with the Gorgon’s head. He returned to Seriphus, turned Polydectes and his gathered friends to stone, made Dictys king, and gave the Gorgon’s head to Athena. Perseus later caused the death of Acrisius by accidentally striking him with a quoit, exchanged Argos with Megapenthes, and ruled Tiryns while fortifying Midea and Mycenae. The unit also recounts the lineage of Perseus, including Electryon, Amphitryon, Alcmena, and the events leading to Electryon’s accidental death.
