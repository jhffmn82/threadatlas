This section, in brief. Hercules descends into Hades through Taenarum to fetch Cerberus, encounters Theseus and Pirithous, and brings Cerberus to Eurystheus before returning him to Hades. After his labours, Hercules gives Megara to Iolaus, seeks to marry Iole, and kills Iphitus after a fit of madness. To be cured, he is sold by Hermes to Omphale for three years as compensation for the murder. During his servitude, Hercules performs various deeds, including seizing the Cercopes, killing Syleus, burying Icarus, and participating in the voyage to Colchis and the Calydonian hunt.
