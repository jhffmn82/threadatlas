The unit traces the descendants of Porthaon and Thestius and describes the family of Oeneus, king of Calydon. Oeneus neglected Artemis in a sacrifice, prompting her to send a destructive boar and leading to the Calydonian boar hunt, in which Meleager killed the boar and awarded its skin to Atalanta. Meleager later died after Althaea burned the brand on which his life depended, though another account says he died fighting after defending Calydon. The narrative then follows Tydeus and Diomedes, including Oeneus’s overthrow, restoration, and burial.
