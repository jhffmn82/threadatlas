This section, in brief. Hercules undertakes his tenth labour by fetching the red kine of Geryon from Erythia. He crosses the ocean in a golden goblet given to him by the Sun, kills Geryon, Eurytion, and Orthus, and drives the kine through Europe and Italy. After recovering an escaped bull from Eryx and overcoming Hera's dispersal of the herd, Hercules brings the kine to Eurystheus. Eurystheus sacrifices the kine to Hera.
