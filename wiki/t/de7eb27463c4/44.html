This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concludes the account of the family of Inachus from Belus to the Heraclids and begins the account of the house of Agenor. Libya and Poseidon had Belus and Agenor; Agenor went to Phoenicia, married Telephassa, and had Europa, Cadmus, Phoenix, and Cilix. Zeus carried Europa to Crete in the form of a tame bull, where she bore Minos, Sarpedon, and Rhadamanthys. After Europa disappeared, Agenor sent his sons to search for her and ordered them not to return until they found her, while Telephassa accompanied them.
