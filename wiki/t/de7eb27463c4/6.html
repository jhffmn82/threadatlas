This section, in brief written by a model
Uranus was the first ruler of the entire universe. After marrying Earth, he fathered the Hecatoncheires: Briareus, Gyges, and Cottus. They were extraordinarily large and powerful, with one hundred hands and fifty heads each. The text also discusses the marriage of Sky and Earth as a widespread myth.
