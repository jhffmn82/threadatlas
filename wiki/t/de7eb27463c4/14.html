This section, in brief. Hellen, said by some to be the son of Zeus, had Dorus, Xuthus, and Aeolus by Orseis and divided the country among them. He named the Greeks Hellenes after himself, while his sons gave their names to the Dorians, Achaeans, Ionians, and Aeolians. Poseidon fathered Otus and Ephialtes, the Aloads, who tried to attack the gods but were killed by Artemis through deception. Endymion became ruler of Elis, and his son Aetolus later gave his name to Aetolia.
