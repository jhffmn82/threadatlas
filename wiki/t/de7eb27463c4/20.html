This section, in brief. Medea helps Jason obtain the fleece, escapes with him and Apsyrtus, and kills Apsyrtus when Aeetes pursues them. Zeus sends a storm because of Apsyrtus’s murder, and the Argonauts are purified by Circe before continuing their voyage through various dangers to Iolcus. Medea helps Jason bring about Pelias’s death, after which Jason and Medea are expelled from Iolcus and settle in Corinth. There, Jason marries Glauce, and Medea kills Glauce and her own children Mermerus and Pheres before fleeing to Athens.
