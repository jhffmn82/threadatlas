This section, in brief. Prometheus moulds human beings from water and earth and secretly gives them fire carried in a fennel stalk. Zeus punishes Prometheus by having him nailed to Mount Caucasus, where an eagle eats his liver daily, until Hercules later releases him. Prometheus's son Deucalion survives Zeus's Flood with Pyrrha in a chest, lands on Mount Parnassus, and restores humankind by throwing stones that become men and women.
