The unit concerns Zeus’s offspring and unions, including his children by Hera, Themis, Dione, Eurynome, Styx, and Mnemosyne. It recounts the stories of Orpheus and Eurydice, Hyacinth and Apollo, Thamyris and the Muses, and several children born from the Muses. It also describes Hera’s birth of Hephaestus and Zeus’s swallowing of Metis, after which Athena emerged from his head fully armed.
