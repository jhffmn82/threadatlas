This section, in brief written by a model
The Argives armed themselves and approached the walls of Thebes, with seven leaders stationed at the seven gates. Eteocles armed the Thebans, appointed matching leaders, arranged the battle, and sought through divination to learn how the Thebans could defeat the enemy. The Theban seer Tiresias was blind but possessed prophetic abilities, and different accounts explained his blindness and powers. One account says Athena blinded him after he saw her naked, while another says Hera blinded him after he judged that women experience greater pleasure in intercourse, and Zeus gave him prophetic power.
