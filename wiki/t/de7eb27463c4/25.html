This section, in brief. Acrisius learned from an oracle that Danae would bear a son who would kill him, so he confined Danae, but she gave birth to Perseus. Acrisius cast Danae and Perseus into the sea in a chest, and Dictys rescued and raised Perseus on Seriphus, where Polydectes later sent Perseus to obtain the Gorgon’s head. Guided by Hermes and Athena, Perseus obtained magical equipment from the nymphs, decapitated Medusa, and traveled to Ethiopia, where he found Andromeda exposed to a sea monster.
