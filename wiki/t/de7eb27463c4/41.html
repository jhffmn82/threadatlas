This section, in brief written by a model
Hercules travels with Deianira to Trachis, defeats the Dryopes and other opponents, and later captures Oechalia from Eurytus, taking Iole captive. Deianira, fearing that Hercules will love Iole more than her, treats a tunic with what she believes is a love charm made from Nessus's blood. The Hydra's poison in the tunic corrodes Hercules's body, leading Deianira to commit suicide and Hercules to order a pyre built on Mount Oeta. Hercules dies on the pyre and is taken to heaven, where he becomes immortal and reconciles with Hera.
