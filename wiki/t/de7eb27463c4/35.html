This section, in brief. The twelfth labor required Hercules to bring Cerberus from Hades. Cerberus had three dog heads, a serpent’s tail, and many snake heads along his back. Before going to him, Hercules went to Eumolpus at Eleusis to be initiated into the Eleusinian Mysteries, but first had to be purified because he had not been cleansed from the killing of the Centaurs. After Eumolpus purified him, he was initiated, and he later came to Tainaron in Laconia.
