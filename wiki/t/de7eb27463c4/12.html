This section, in brief. Earth, angered over the Titans, gives birth to the Giants, who attack the sky and are defeated by the gods with the aid of Heracles. Zeus learns that the Giants can be killed only with the help of a mortal, obtains a herb that would protect them, and summons Heracles through Athena. After the Giants are overcome, Earth unites with Tartarus and gives birth to Typhon, whom Zeus eventually defeats by casting Mount Etna upon him.
