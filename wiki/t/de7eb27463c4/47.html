This section, in brief. Cadmus and Harmonia leave Thebes and go to the Encheleans, who make them leaders against the Illyrians; Cadmus then reigns over the Illyrians, and later Zeus sends the pair to the Elysian Fields after transforming them into a serpent. Polydorus becomes king of Thebes, and after Labdacus dies, Lycus rules while Labdacus's son Laius is still a child. Zethus and Amphion, the sons of Antiope, kill Lycus and Dirce, fortify Thebes, and expel Laius. Laius later stays with Pelops and carries off Chrysippus while teaching him to drive a chariot.
