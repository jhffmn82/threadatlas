This section, in brief. When Zeus becomes full-grown, he takes Metis, daughter of Ocean, as his helper, and she gives Cronus a drug that makes him disgorge the stone and the children he had swallowed. With these children Zeus wages a ten-year war against Cronus and the Titans, following Earth’s prophecy that victory will come if he allies with those imprisoned in Tartarus. Zeus frees the Cyclopes, who provide divine weapons, and the gods defeat and imprison the Titans in Tartarus under the guard of the Hundred-handers. Zeus, Poseidon, and Pluto then divide sovereignty among the sky, sea, and Hades.
