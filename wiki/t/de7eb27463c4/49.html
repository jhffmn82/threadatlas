This section, in brief. Eteocles and Polynices agree that they will rule the kingdom of Thebes in alternate years, but Eteocles refuses to surrender the kingdom after his year, so Polynices goes to Argos. Adrastus reconciles Polynices and Tydeus, marries them to his daughters, and promises to restore them to their homelands; he then gathers the Seven Champions for the war against Thebes. Eriphyle accepts Polynices' necklace and persuades Amphiaraus to join the expedition, despite his foreknowledge that the champions will die. At Nemea, Hypsipyle leads the champions to water while leaving Opheltes behind, and a serpent kills the child; the champions kill the serpent, bury Opheltes, and establish the Nemean Games in his honor.
