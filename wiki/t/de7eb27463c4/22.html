This section, in brief. The unit traces the Inachean family from Inachus, son of Ocean and Tethys, through Phoroneus, Apis, Niobe, and their descendants. Phoroneus ruled the land later called the Peloponnese and fathered Apis and Niobe, while Apis was killed after ruling as a stern tyrant and was regarded as the god Sarapis. Niobe had Argos by Zeus, and Argos later ruled the kingdom, naming the Peloponnese after himself and fathering several children. The lineage also includes Argus the All-seeing, who possessed eyes throughout his body, performed heroic deeds, and avenged the killing of Apis.
