This section, in brief written by a model
Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus, secretly bore twin sons, Pelias and Neleus, after Poseidon approached her in the likeness of Enipeus. The twins were exposed, rescued and raised by a horse-keeper, later recognized Tyro, and killed their stepmother Sidero; Pelias ruled in Thessaly, while Neleus founded Pylus and fathered Nestor and other children. Neleus’s daughter Pero was won for Bias after Melampus obtained the kine of Phylacus, and Melampus later settled in Argos with Bias after healing the women driven mad by Dionysus. The genealogy continues through Bias’s son Adrastus, and through Cretheus’s son Pheres to Admetus, who reigned at Pherae while Apollo served him.
