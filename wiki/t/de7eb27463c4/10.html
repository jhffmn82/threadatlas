This section, in brief. Asteria fled Zeus by taking the form of a quail and throwing herself into the sea, after which the island was first called Asteria and later Delos. Hera pursued Leto across the earth until Leto reached Delos, where Artemis was born first and later helped deliver Apollo. Apollo took over the oracle at Delphi after killing Python, and he also killed Tityus and Marsyas. Artemis killed Orion, whose stories include his blinding by Oenopion, his restoration of sight, and different accounts of his death.
