This section, in brief. Arcas had two sons, Elatus and Aphidas, who divided the land, though Elatus held all the power. Elatus and Aphidas had descendants including Aleus, whose daughter Auge was seduced by Heracles and later married Teuthras, ruler of Mysia. Auge's exposed infant was named Telephus, was raised by herdsmen, and eventually became Teuthras's adopted son and successor. The unit also recounts the upbringing, contests, race, marriage, and later fate of Atalanta, including her pursuit by Melanion with golden apples from Aphrodite.
