This section, in brief written by a model
Proetus occupied Tiryns after his father-in-law restored him, while he and Acrisius divided the Argive territory, with Acrisius ruling Argos and Proetus ruling Tiryns. Proetus's daughters and the other Argive women were seized by madness, and Melampus cured the surviving daughters after Proetus agreed to his demands for land. Bellerophon came to Proetus for purification after accidentally killing a relative, but Stheneboea falsely accused him of making improper advances. Proetus sent Bellerophon to Iobates with a letter ordering his death, but Iobates instead assigned him dangerous campaigns, which Bellerophon completed with the aid of Pegasus and ultimately won Iobates's admiration and kingdom.
