This section, in brief. This unit concerns Io’s transformation into a cow, her wanderings under Hera’s persecution, and her return to human form in Egypt, where she gives birth to Epaphus. It traces the descendants of Epaphus through Libya, Agenor, and Belus to Egyptus and Danaus, whose descendants become involved in a forced marriage alliance. Danaus’s daughters kill the sons of Egyptus on their wedding night, except for Hypermnestra, who spares Lynceus. The account also describes Amymone’s encounter with Poseidon and the origins of the springs at Lerna.
