This section, in brief. The unit concerns Atreus and Thyestes, sons of Pelops, and their struggle over the kingdom of Mycenae. Aerope, the wife of Atreus, gives Thyestes the golden lamb after committing adultery with him, and Thyestes uses it to claim the kingdom until Zeus causes the sun to reverse its course and Atreus takes power. Atreus later murders Thyestes’ sons and serves them to Thyestes at the Thyestean banquet before banishing him. Thyestes then fathers Aegisthus with his daughter, and Aegisthus kills Atreus and restores the kingdom to Thyestes.
