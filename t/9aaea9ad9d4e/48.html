This section, in brief. The unit describes myths in which fire is stolen from Zeus, Hephaestus, a sky deity, or another supernatural owner and brought to men. Prometheus steals fire for men in Greek accounts, while birds, animals, heroes, and insects obtain it for humans in stories from Australia, Melanesia, Polynesia, and Asia. Several traditions explain the origin of fire-making by showing people how to rub sticks or wood together, sometimes after fire is hidden in trees. The Polynesian hero Māui obtains fire from the fire deity Mahuika, after which surviving fire takes refuge in trees.
