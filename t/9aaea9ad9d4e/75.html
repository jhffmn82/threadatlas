This section, in brief. Daedalus is an architect and inventor who murders Talos, flees to Minos, constructs the labyrinth, and later escapes from it with wings. Danae, daughter of Acrisius, conceives Perseus by Zeus, is cast into the sea with him, and returns to Argos. Danaus, son of Belus, flees to Argos with his fifty daughters, whose husbands they murder, and later gives them to the winners of an athletic contest. Dionysus, son of Zeus and Semele, discovers the vine, undergoes persecution and madness, spreads his rites, and is recognized as a god.
