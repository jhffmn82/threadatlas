This section, in brief. Neoptolemus remained in Tenedos for two days on the advice of Thetis, then traveled by land to Molossia with Helenus. After Phoenix died on the way, Neoptolemus buried him, defeated the Molossians, became their king, and fathered Molossus with Andromache. Helenus founded a city in Molossia and married Deidamia, while Neoptolemus later took Hermione from Orestes and was killed at Delphi. Another account says that Neoptolemus was killed by Machaereus after attacking Apollo’s sanctuary.
