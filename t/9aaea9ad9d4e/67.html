This section, in brief. A poor widow's boy and girl travel to the land of the Ogres to gather golden horns and become wealthy. An Ogre captures them, roasts and swallows the girl, and threatens to eat the boy, but the boy blinds him with a burning brand. The boy escapes the cave disguised among the sheep, and the Ogre later vomits up the girl alive. The brother and sister return to their mother with great riches.
