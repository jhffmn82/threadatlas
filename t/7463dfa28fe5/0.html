This section, in brief. The unit introduces the 1814 English edition of The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, translated by G. Booth. It explains that Diodorus Siculus wrote a forty-book general history from the beginning of the world to his own time, of which fifteen books survive. The edition includes the surviving books, fragments collected by Photius and others, excerpts published by H. Valesius, and a supplement from Quintus Curtius and Arrianus intended to fill a gap in the seventeenth book. The translator also explains why the first five books were newly translated despite an earlier English version.
