This section, in brief. Alcibiades returned to Athens and was restored to his property, absolved from his condemnation, and appointed commander with authority by sea and land. He defeated the Andrians but suffered a naval defeat at Notium when Antiochus disobeyed his orders and engaged Lysander. Agis attacked Athens but was repulsed, while Alcibiades later devastated Cuma and was accused by the Cumeans and others of misconduct. Athens then appointed Conon admiral, and Alcibiades surrendered the fleet and withdrew to Thrace.
