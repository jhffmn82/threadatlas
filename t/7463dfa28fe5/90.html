This section, in brief. The Athenians prepare a large expedition against Syracuse, sending a fleet, army, and the generals Alcibiades, Nicias, and Lamachus to Sicily. After Alcibiades is accused in Athens of conspiring against the democracy, he escapes from Sicily to Sparta and persuades the Lacedaemonians to assist the Syracusans. The Athenians seize Epipolae and besiege Syracuse, but Gylippus arrives with reinforcements, Lamachus is killed, and the Syracusans recover important positions. Athens sends further aid, while Lacedaemon breaks its league with Athens and renews the war.
