This section, in brief. The unit concerns Sesostris, who was trained with boys born on the same day and later became king of Egypt. He organized a large Egyptian army, conquered territories in Africa, Asia, and Europe, and sought to subdue the whole world before ending his expedition in Thrace. After returning to Egypt, he built temples, canals, dykes, fortifications, obelisks, statues, and other public works, while rewarding his soldiers and imposing tributes on conquered nations. He later became blind and voluntarily ended his life after reigning for thirty-three years.
