This section, in brief. The unit describes the ancient Amazons of Africa, who lived under female rule and became powerful warriors under their queen Merina. They conquered parts of Africa and Asia, including the Atlantides, but fought the Gorgons and were eventually extirpated along with them by Hercules. Perseus later conquered the Gorgons under Medusa, while an earthquake caused the Tritonis morass to disappear. The account also connects the Amazons with Atlas and the Atlantides and describes their eventual defeat by Thracian invaders.
