This section, in brief. Eurymedon and Demosthenes arrived in Sicily with a large Athenian force, but a night assault on Epipolae failed after the Syracusans rallied under Hermocrates. The plague and the arrival of further Syracusan aid weakened the Athenians, while a lunar eclipse delayed their planned departure. The Athenians then lost successive naval engagements in Syracuse harbor, abandoned their ships, and retreated by land, where the Syracusans defeated and captured them.
