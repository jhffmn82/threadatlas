This section, in brief. The unit concerns Crete, its earliest inhabitants, and the origins and deeds attributed to the Idæi Dactyli, Curetes, Titans, and numerous gods. It describes how these figures introduced useful arts, including fire-making, metalworking, agriculture, navigation, laws, justice, crafts, letters, and poetry. It recounts the upbringing and reign of Jupiter, his benefits to mankind, and his wars against the giants. It also explains the origins and functions attributed to Ceres, Proserpina, Pluto, Neptune, Minerva, the Muses, and other deities.
