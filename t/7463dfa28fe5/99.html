This section, in brief. Hermocrates the Syracusan, banished by the Syracusans, raised troops and attacked Carthaginian territories in Sicily, gaining high esteem among the Sicilians. The Syracusans regretted his banishment and began showing support for his recall. In Greece, Thrasybulus failed to take Ephesus, while the Athenians conducted operations around Chalcedon, Pylos, and Byzantium. Alcibiades and Theramenes ultimately captured Byzantium after a betrayal by some of its inhabitants.
