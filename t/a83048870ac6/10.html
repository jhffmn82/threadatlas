This section, in brief. The unit presents a data format for an AI assistant that answers questions by referencing external documents. It states that the documents may be unrelated to the question and provides them between document markers. The AI assistant should answer accurately when the documents contain the answer and refuse when they do not. The unit also specifies a required JSON output containing a summary and entity-specific cells.

C Data Format You are an accurate and reliable AI assistant capable of answering questions by referencing external documents. Please note that the external documents may not always be related to the question. The documents are as follows: <|doc_start|>{chunk_1}<|doc_end|> <|doc_start|>{chunk_2}<|doc_end|> <|doc_start|>{chunk_3}<|doc_end|> ... If the information in the documents contain the correct answer, you will provide an accurate response. If the documents do not contain the answer, you will refuse to answer. Question: {que}
