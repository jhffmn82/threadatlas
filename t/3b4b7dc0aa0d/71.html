This section, in brief. Cassander entered Macedonia, besieged Olympias at Pydna, and gained support after the Epirotes revolted against Aeacidas and allied with him. Olympias's position deteriorated when Macedonians joined Cassander and Callas bribed away much of Polyperchon's army in Perrhaebia. In Asia, Antigonus marched through the desert against Eumenes, but Eumenes delayed him with a fire-signal stratagem and gathered his scattered forces. Antigonus later defeated Eumenes's forces in battle, captured Eumenes through the treachery of the Macedonians, and eventually had him put to death.
