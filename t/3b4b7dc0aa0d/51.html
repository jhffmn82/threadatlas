This section, in brief. Alexander sailed into the main ocean, explored islands, returned up the river, and sent Nearchus and others to survey the sea-coast to the Euphrates. He marched through Gedrosia and neighboring regions, dividing his army into brigades, suffering severe losses during a desert crossing, and eventually reaching Carmania and then Susa. At Susa he married Statira, arranged other marriages, received thirty thousand trained Persians, and dealt with the misconduct of his governors and the mutiny of the Macedonians. The unit also recounts Calanus's self-immolation and Harpalus's corruption, flight, and death.
