This section, in brief. Eumenes, appointed by the kings as general with full authority over Asia, left Cappadocia and gathered Macedonian and other forces in Cilicia. He gained the support of the Argyraspides and used a throne bearing Alexander’s royal insignia to strengthen loyalty to the kings and avoid envy among the commanders. Ptolemy urged the Argyraspides and the governors of the garrison at Quinda not to support Eumenes, while Antigonus plotted to have him killed, but both efforts failed. Eumenes then marched toward Phoenicia to build a fleet and prepare to transport Polyperchon from Macedonia into Asia against Antigonus.
