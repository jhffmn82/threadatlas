This section, in brief. The unit describes Pyrrhus's intervention in Sicily, where he reconciled the factions at Syracuse, gained control of Agrigentum and many other cities, and conquered most Carthaginian possessions except Lilybaeum. The Carthaginians resisted his siege of Lilybaeum, forcing him to withdraw and plan to build a fleet for an invasion of Africa. It also recounts the rise and fall of Sicilian rulers, including Hicetas, Phintias, Thynion, Sosistratus, and Hiero, as well as the defeat and destruction of Brennus and the Gauls. Hiero later defeated the Mamertines near Messana, but Hannibal intervened and secured Messana from immediate surrender.
