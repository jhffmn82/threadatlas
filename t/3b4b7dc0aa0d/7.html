This section, in brief. Artaxerxes attempted to restore peace in Greece, but the Thebans refused to join the league because they kept Boeotia under their own jurisdiction, prompting the Lacedaemonians to prepare for war. Under Epaminondas, the Thebans defeated the Lacedaemonians at the battle of Leuctra, where Cleombrotus was killed and the Spartan army was routed. Jason of Pherae later persuaded the Thessalians to seek the sovereignty of Greece, while Argos was devastated by a violent civil sedition called Scytalism. Jason was eventually assassinated, and Polydorus succeeded him at Pherae.
