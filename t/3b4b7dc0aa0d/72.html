This section, in brief. A destructive flood near Rhodes killed more than five hundred people before a breach in the city wall drained the water into the sea. Antigonus uncovered Python’s conspiracy in Media, deceived Python into coming to him, had him executed, and defeated the conspirators. Antigonus then marched into Persia and established control over its provinces, while arranging for the gradual destruction of the troublesome silver shields. At Susa he seized the royal treasure and other valuables, accumulating twenty-five thousand talents in all.
