This section, in brief. The unit presents the speaker’s divided love for two beautiful women, his preference for a life consumed by erotic love, and his wish to die during its pleasures. It then describes his anxiety over Corinna’s intended sea voyage and his hopes for her safe return. The speaker celebrates gaining Corinna as a triumph achieved without military aid. Finally, he prays for Corinna’s survival during a dangerous pregnancy and condemns abortion as harmful to women and unborn children.
