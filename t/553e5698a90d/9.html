This section, in brief. Hermione writes to Orestes that Pyrrhus (Neoptolemus) holds her captive and has dragged her into his palace despite her resistance. She urges Orestes to reclaim her, arguing that he is both her husband and her kinsman, while Pyrrhus has no rightful claim superior to theirs. Hermione recalls her unhappy childhood, her mother's absence, and the destruction of Troy, which left her as Pyrrhus's captive. She declares that she will either die young or become Orestes' wife, and identifies the suffering of Tantalid women as an inherited family curse.
