This section, in brief. Cydippe writes to Acontius while suffering an unexplained illness that threatens her approaching marriage. She recalls how, during her voyage to Delos, Acontius deceived her into reading an oath written on an apple and thereby nearly swearing to marry him. She blames Acontius and Diana for her suffering, while Acontius and Cydippe's suitor remain in rivalry over her. Cydippe argues that Acontius should have sought her consent honestly rather than compelled her through deceit.
