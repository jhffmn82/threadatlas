This section, in brief. The poet encounters Elegy and Tragedy in a sacred grove, where they argue over the kind of poetry he should write. Tragedy urges him to abandon love poetry for heroic subjects, while Elegy defends her influence over the poet and over Corinna. The poet asks Tragedy to allow him time to continue writing about love, and Tragedy agrees. At a circus, the poet courts a woman who favors one of the charioteers, invokes Venus for success in love, and receives an encouraging smile and implied promise from the woman.
