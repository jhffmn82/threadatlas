This section, in brief. Briseis writes to Achilles, pleading with him to reclaim her after Agamemnon demanded her return. She accuses Achilles of delaying her restoration and refusing the gifts offered by Agamemnon, while recalling the destruction of her home and the deaths of her family and husband. She urges Achilles to end his anger, return to battle against Hector, and allow her to accompany him. Briseis declares that without him she would rather die, but asks him to preserve the life he once gave her and order her to come with him.
