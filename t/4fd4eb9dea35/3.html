This section, in brief. The dataset was manually curated with specialists in epidemiological studies who contributed typical questions from their work. It contains 306 question/SQL pairs using 13 tables and 44 columns, with substantial query complexity involving population selection, temporal relationships, aggregation, and mathematical operations. Each question-SQL pair was augmented with two paraphrased versions, although one version per question was used in subsequent evaluations due to budget limits. The dataset uses the OMOP-CDM to support applicability across real-world data databases with differing data models.

Cor- rectly answering questions often require multiple logical steps: selection of population(s) of interest, relationship between events within a specific time frame, aggregation statistics, and basic mathemat- ical operations (e.g., ratios). The dataset features questions in their natural, free-form language and it is augmented with two paraphrased versions per question-SQL pair, increasing volume while also offering validated labels for retrieval algorithms. To address the challenge of data retrieval variability across databases with differing data models, we lever- age the OMOP-CDM. This model, underpinned by standardized vocabularies (Reich et al., 2024), harmonizes observational healthcare data and it is widely recognized as the standard for RWD analy- sis, with data from over 2.1 billion patient records across 34 countries (Voss et al., 2023; Reich et al., 2024).
