  19. DeBERTa model training
This section, in brief. The unit describes DeBERTa model training on a Google Cloud Platform A-100 GPU instance for 3 epochs, using separate initial learning rates for the base model layers and heads, with warmup and linear decay. It also presents a RAG case study comparing TF-IDF and FAISS retrievers with k values of 2 and 4, several prompt versions, and GPT-3.5-turbo-0125 or GPT-4o as generation models. The table reports average relevance, utilization, completeness, and the percentage of hallucinated responses. FAISS generally produces higher relevance than TF-IDF, while k=2 generally performs better than k=4.
