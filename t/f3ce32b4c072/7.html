This section, in brief. Case law data is presented as a high-quality resource for building intelligence systems that assist legal professionals. Through an interdisciplinary effort with legal professionals, the authors build CLERC, a dataset for training and evaluating models on long-context case retrieval and analysis generation tasks. The work aims to encourage further discoveries in AI built with legal text data.
