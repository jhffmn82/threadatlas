This section, in brief. The unit consists of critical and explanatory notes on Euripides’ Iphigenia in Tauris, including the play’s plot, textual problems, mythological references, and proposed emendations. It discusses Iphigenia’s intended sacrifice at Aulis, her removal through the air by divine agency, and her service as a priestess in Tauris. It also treats Orestes’ wanderings, his encounter with Iphigenia, and the escape from Tauris, while repeatedly evaluating the Cambridge editor’s readings. The notes compare passages in the play with works by Aeschylus, Sophocles, Ovid, Virgil, and other authors.
