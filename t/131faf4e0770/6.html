This section, in brief. This unit lists references on large language models and their applications in radiology, medicine, clinical research, and scientific reasoning. It covers GPT-4 and ChatGPT uses including report structuring, chest-radiograph evaluation, data mining, differential diagnosis, error detection, and extraction of structured information from medical reports. It also addresses hallucination, ethics, data contamination, citation quality, evaluation frameworks, and retrieval-augmented generation. Several references examine medical benchmarks, clinical decision-making, and autonomous artificial intelligence agents.
