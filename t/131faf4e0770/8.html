This section, in brief. The Radiological Society of North America Case Collection adapted to RadioRAG pipeline question answering (RSNA-RadioQA) dataset is described as containing 80 expert-curated questions with corresponding answers. This unit lists metadata for RSNA-RadioQA questions, including identifiers, original-case DOIs, authors, titles, publication dates, and subspecialties. The cases cover breast, chest, musculoskeletal, gastrointestinal, cardiac, vascular, neurologic, oncologic, pediatric, and other radiological specialties. The material states that the original cases are copyrighted by the Radiological Society of North America, Inc. (RSNA).
