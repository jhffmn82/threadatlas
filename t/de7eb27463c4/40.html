This section, in brief. Hercules marched with the Calydonians against the Thesprotians, took Ephyra, and fathered Tlepolemus with Astyoche. While feasting with Oeneus, he accidentally killed Eunomus, then accepted exile and set out toward Trachis with Deianeira. At the river Evenus, Nessus attempted to assault Deianeira while ferrying her, so Heracles shot him in the heart. Dying, Nessus gave Deianeira instructions for making a love charm from his blood and the substance from his wound.

After these events, as he was feasting with Oeneus, he killed with a blow of his knuckles Eunomus, son of Architeles, when the lad was pouring water on his hands; now the lad was a kinsman of Oeneus.? The natives call them nouraghes. They are built in the form of truncated cones, and their material consists of squared or rough blocks of stone, sometimes of enormous size.
