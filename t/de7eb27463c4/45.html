This section, in brief. The unit recounts the descendants of Europa, including the careers of Minos, Sarpedon, Rhadamanthys, and their families. It describes Minos’s rise to power in Crete, the birth and confinement of the Minotaur, and the construction of the Labyrinth. It tells how Cadmus founded Thebes after killing a dragon and sowing its teeth, then married Harmonia. It also relates the death and resurrection of Glaucus and the death of Actaeon after he saw Artemis bathing.
