This section, in brief. The unit presents a work titled “Towards Cross-Cultural Machine Translation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs.” It concerns Cross-Cultural Machine Translation using Retrieval-Augmented Generation from Multilingual Knowledge Graphs. The work is authored by Simone Conia, Daniel Lee, Min Li, Umar Farooq Minhas, Saloni Potdar, and Yunyao Li, affiliated with Sapienza University of Rome, Adobe, and Apple.
