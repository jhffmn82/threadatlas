This section, in brief. The paper proposes REANO for the ODQA task. REANO generates knowledge graphs from passages and uses an answer predictor based on FiD to generate answers from both passages and the generated knowledge graphs. A GNN model helps identify and select knowledge triples relevant to questions. Experiments on five ODQA datasets show that REANO improves EM score by up to 2.7% compared with state-of-the-art baselines.

6 Conclusion This paper proposes REANO to address the ODQA task. REANO aims to capture dependencies among passages with a knowledge graph generation mod- ule. Specifically, it consists of a KG generator, which generates KGs based on the passages, and an answer predictor, which generates answers based on both the passages and the generated KGs. The answer predictor is based on the FiD model, with an additional GNN model to identify and select knowledge triples relevant to questions. Experi- mental results on five ODQA datasets indicate that REANO can improve the EM score by up to 2.7% compared with state-of-the-art baselines.
