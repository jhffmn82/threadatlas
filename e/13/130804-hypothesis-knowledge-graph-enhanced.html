Hypothesis Knowledge Graph Enhanced is a framework consisting of hypothesis output, Named Entity Recognition, knowledge graph retrieval, and noise knowledge filtering modules. In two medical question-answering tasks, it improved the baseline F1 score by 4.62% and addressed accuracy and interpretability challenges.
