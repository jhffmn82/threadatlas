Introduction

Corpus poisoning is described as a means of injecting harmful documents into a source corpus, enabling infusion attacks. The unit distinguishes this issue from unsafe behavior that can arise even with a safe corpus.

Related Work

Corpus poisoning is a threat to RAG LLMs in which injecting harmful documents leads to unsafe outputs.
