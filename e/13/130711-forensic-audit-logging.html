3 Methods (framework design) .. 3.3.4 Step 4: Forensic audit logging

Forensic audit logging records the original query, the retrieved passage from the Surviving Sepsis Campaign International Guidelines, and a cryptographic hash of the reasoning chain. In the clinical simulation, this information is committed to a permissioned distributed ledger to create an immutable, timestamped source-to-decision trail.
