GCG optimizes an adversarial suffix with gradient-based search to increase the likelihood that the target LLM generates a compliant response. It produces unreadable prompts, and its non-RAG jailbreaks transfer poorly to RAG; it also faces long-context validation costs that require tree-attention adaptations.
