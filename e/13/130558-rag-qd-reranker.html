RAG + QD + Reranker in Question Decomposition for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Paul J. L. Ammann · 2025-07-28/2025-07-29

RAG + QD + Reranker is a system that decomposes a query, retrieves passages for the resulting queries, merges candidates, and reranks them to select final top-k passages. It delivers the strongest overall MultiHop-RAG retrieval results, including 0.872 Hits@10 and 0.635 MRR@10, and the best reported HotpotQA answer metrics. It also achieves the highest HotpotQA supporting-fact precision, 46.8, and joint precision, 23.1. Its main trade-off is 18.9 seconds of end-to-end retrieval latency per query, although caching generated subqueries can skip decomposition.

RAG + QD + Reranker decomposes the query, retrieves passages for the resulting queries, merges the candidates, and reranks them to select the final top-k passages. It achieves the strongest overall MultiHop-RAG results and the best reported HotpotQA answer EM, F1, precision, and recall.

opening .. Efficiency

QD+RR achieves the highest supporting-fact precision (46.8) and joint precision (23.1) on HotpotQA, as well as the strongest answer metrics and retrieval results among the evaluated systems. It reaches 0.763 Hits@4, 0.872 Hits@10, 0.322 MAP@10, and 0.635 MRR@10 on MultiHop-RAG. Its retrieval latency is 18.9 seconds per query, but caching generated subqueries can skip the decomposition call.
