KG2RAG in Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Xiangrong Zhu · 2025-04-29/2025-05-04

KG2RAG is a retrieval-augmented generation method and retrieval workflow that enhances generation with the fact-level structure and factual knowledge of knowledge graphs. It begins with offline document processing: documents are chunked and chunks are linked to a specific KG. For retrieval, embedding and ranking methods obtain seed chunks, a relevant subgraph is extracted, and KG-guided expansion retrieves factually associated chunks, including across multiple documents. It then filters relevant information and uses the KG as a skeleton to organize retrieved chunks into coherent paragraphs before response generation. It is evaluated on HotpotQA and newly constructed variants under distractor and fullwiki settings, where it consistently outperforms baselines in response and retrieval quality. It also remains robust under variations in top-k, graph-expansion hops, and KG quality. Its source code is released on GitHub.

Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation in Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation

Xiangrong Zhu · 2025-04-29/2025-05-04

Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, also called KG2RAG, is a novel framework for retrieval optimization that enhances retrieval-augmented generation by integrating knowledge graphs and their structured factual knowledge. It links chunks to a specific knowledge graph to supply fact-level relationships between chunks, aiming to retrieve diverse, related information and organize it into coherent paragraphs for language-model response generation. Its process begins with semantic-based retrieval of seed chunks, then performs KG-guided chunk expansion and KG-based context organization. These steps improve the diversity and coherence of retrieved results. In extensive experiments on HotpotQA, KG2RAG has superior response quality and retrieval quality compared with existing RAG-based approaches. It focuses on retrieval optimization rather than other modules, while remaining orthogonal and compatible with query rewriting, retrieval optimization, and multi-turn dialogue. It is planned for development as a plug-and-play tool that can be easily integrated with other approaches and facilitate the research community.

Abstract

Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation, also called KG2RAG, uses knowledge graphs to improve the diversity and coherence of retrieved results. It performs semantic-based retrieval for seed chunks, followed by KG-guided chunk expansion and KG-based chunk organization.

1

Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation is introduced as a framework that combines chunking, knowledge-graph association, semantic retrieval, graph-guided expansion, and knowledge-graph-based context organization. It is designed to retrieve diverse, related information and present it in coherent paragraphs for language-model response generation.

5

KG2RAG is proposed as a framework that enhances retrieval-augmented generation by integrating knowledge graphs. It uses knowledge-graph-guided chunk expansion and knowledge-graph-based context organization, and it outperforms existing retrieval-augmented generation approaches in response quality and retrieval quality.

Limitations

KG2RAG focuses on retrieval optimization rather than other retrieval-augmented generation modules. It aims to enhance retrieval-augmented generation with structured factual knowledge from knowledge graphs and is described as orthogonal and compatible with other approaches.
