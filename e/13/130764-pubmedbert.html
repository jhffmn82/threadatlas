PubMedBERT in MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health

Shan Xu, Zhaokun Yan, Chengxiao Dai and Fan Wu · 2025-10-08

PubMedBERT is a domain-specific biomedical language model baseline using PubMed-specific pretrained BERT embeddings to answer public health-related queries. It achieves 0.558 accuracy and 0.5987 F1-score, scores lowest in most metrics, and in the silver ion water case severely hallucinates fabricated clinical evidence and unsupported treatment recommendations.
