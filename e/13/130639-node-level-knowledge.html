VI. KNOWLEDGE INTEGRATION .. C. Integration Enhancement Strategies.

Node-level knowledge focuses on individual graph nodes, which are often linked to documents such as abstracts in citation networks. Node and neighboring text can be supplied as context for prediction, and long texts can be distilled into embeddings.
