study in RAG LLMs are Not Safer: A Safety Analysis of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models

Bang An · 2025-04-29/2025-05-04

This study examines safety behaviors of general LLMs in retrieval-augmented generation. Using BM25 and benchmark-based unsafe-behavior definitions under a fixed user setting, it identifies three factors that may explain safety behavior. It assumes white-box red-teaming access and includes potentially harmful AI-generated language while analyzing jailbreaking vulnerabilities to support safer models and defenses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Limitations

The study focuses on general LLMs and their safety behaviors in retrieval-augmented generation settings. It uses BM25, assumes white-box access for red-teaming, and applies unsafe-behavior definitions from the Red-Teaming Resistance Benchmark and HarmBench.

Ethics Statement

The paper contains potentially harmful AI-generated language and discusses jailbreaking methods that may be used for malicious activities. Its goal is to uncover overlooked vulnerabilities of LLMs in the RAG setting, increase awareness of those risks, and accelerate the development of safer models and defense mechanisms.
