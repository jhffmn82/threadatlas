knowledge organization in A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models

Qinggang Zhang, Shengyuan Chen, Yuanchen Bei, Zheng Yuan, Huachi Zhou, Zijin Hong, Hao Chen, Yilin Xiao, Chuang Zhou, Junnan Dong, Yi Chang, Xiao Huang · 2025-09-29

Knowledge organization is a pre-retrieval, two-step process that prepares external knowledge bases for rapid, relevant retrieval. It is examined through knowledge carrier graphs and index graphs. In traditional RAG, it divides large corpora into manageable text chunks, transforms them into embeddings with an embedding model, and stores them in a vector database for distance-based semantic search; it also optimizes granularity through chunking and indexing through metadata and hierarchical indexing. In GraphRAG, it constructs more expressive graph structures, then retrieves and integrates query-relevant information, aiming to reduce irrelevant results, missing context, information overload, and knowledge conflicts.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

II. OVERVIEW

Knowledge organization is examined through two primary paradigms: knowledge carrier graphs and index graphs.

III. WHAT IS GRAPHRAG / A. Traditional RAG Pipeline .. D. Traditional RAG vs GraphRAG

Knowledge organization prepares external repositories for efficient and relevant retrieval. In traditional RAG, it commonly splits corpora into text chunks, creates embeddings, and stores them in a vector database; in GraphRAG, it can construct graphs as knowledge carriers or indexing mechanisms.

IV. KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION .. C. Hybrid GraphRAG

Knowledge organization is treated as a two-step process: constructing a graph structure and then retrieving and integrating query-relevant information. It is intended to reduce irrelevant results, missing context, information overload, and knowledge conflicts.

D. Traditional RAG Pipeline

Knowledge organization structures and prepares external repositories for rapid and relevant retrieval. It includes granularity optimization through chunking and indexing optimization through metadata addition and hierarchical indexing.
