Results / Included studies .. Datasets

MEDRAG is a medical QA dataset containing 7,663 questions and was proposed as an early dataset for medical retrieval-augmented generation. Evaluation with MIRAGE found clear improvements without prior training on two of five datasets, while gains on the remaining datasets were smaller.
