Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Red teaming is used as part of efforts to ensure LLM safety. Existing red-teaming methods are less effective in RAG settings than in non-RAG settings.

Introduction

Red teaming probes large language models with prompts to expose vulnerabilities, including jailbreaking attempts. The unit finds that methods designed for non-RAG settings may be ineffective for RAG-based models.

Related Work

Red-teaming identifies vulnerabilities in LLMs, commonly by jailbreaking them with human-designed tests or LLM-generated prompts. The unit notes that no red-teaming methods have been designed specifically for RAG LLMs.
