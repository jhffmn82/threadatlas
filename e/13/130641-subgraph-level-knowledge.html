VI. KNOWLEDGE INTEGRATION .. C. Integration Enhancement Strategies.

Subgraph-level knowledge represents larger, irregular structures containing multiple nodes and interconnections. It can be compressed with a graph encoder, transformed into sequences or executable graph queries, represented through adapted transformer architectures, or described directly in prompts.
