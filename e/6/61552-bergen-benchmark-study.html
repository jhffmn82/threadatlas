Introduction

The BERGEN benchmark study covers more than 500 experiments benchmarking state-of-the-art retrievers, rerankers, and LLMs in RAG. It concludes that semantic metrics, high-quality retrieval, suitable knowledge-intensive benchmarks, and retrieval for LLMs of any size are important considerations.
