Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

RATTLESNAKE POISON.

Rattlesnake poison was suggested to be an alkaloid-like substance. Poison taken from a fully charged rattlesnake gland produced a dense light-brown, apparently amorphous precipitate when iodine solution was added.
