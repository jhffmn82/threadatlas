Mattresses are river engineering structures that accumulate sand deposits from the river surface to nearly sixteen feet high; 125-foot-wide work was placed at Carroll's Island and begun at Beard's Island.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

RIVER IMPROVEMENTS NEAR ST.

Mattresses have accumulated sand deposits near the surface of the river to nearly sixteen feet in height. At Carroll's Island, mattresses 125 feet wide have been placed, and 4,000 feet have been begun at Beard's Island.
