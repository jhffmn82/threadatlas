Raven's Ugo in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Raven's Ugo is a sea cave, first examined by a party from a boat on their last day at the Lizard. Above the sea line and hung with sea-ferns, its entrance is occupied by a pair of ravens; formerly, smugglers hid kegs of spirits there.

" DAY THE NINTH And our last at the Lizard, which a week ago had been to us a mere word or dot in a map; now we carried away from it a living human interest in everything and everybody.

Raven's Ugo is the first cave the party examines from the boat. Its entrance lies above the sea line, is hung with sea-ferns, and has long been occupied by a pair of ravens; in smuggling days it was used to hide kegs of spirits.
