Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

Retriever fine-tuning uses passages labeled through the generator's rationale as positives or hard negatives. The original grounding passage remains positive, while helpful or misleading passages are assigned different roles depending on whether the generated answer is correct.

Experiments / Setup / Datasets and evaluation metrics .. and Retriever Combinations

Fine-tuning is applied to both the generator and retriever, with generator fine-tuning producing a larger ablation improvement than retriever fine-tuning. Retriever fine-tuning mainly improves NDCG@5 and the ranking of top retrieved passages, while generator fine-tuning improves final answer quality.
