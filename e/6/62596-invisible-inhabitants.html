Chapter Ten

Invisible inhabitants speak from the air, laugh at Tik-Tok's difficulty moving, lift him from the fountain, dry him with warm air, and provide an oil-can. They also shove Queen Ann and kick hesitant officers so that the group follows Tubekins.
