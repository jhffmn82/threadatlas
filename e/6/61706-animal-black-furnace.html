Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

212 " SCHREIBER'S APPARATUS FOR REVIVIFYING BONE-BLACK. .. _The Animal Black Furnace_.

The animal black furnace has a masonry casing, a coke-fired chamber, and two rows of undulating cast-iron retorts enclosed in refractory clay blocks. Bone-black passes from the hopper through the drier, retorts, and cooling pipes; the cooling pipes end in rotary cylinders that regulate its discharge into a rail car. The furnace can revivify 120 hectoliters of bone-black in twenty-four hours with 360 to 400 kilogrammes of coke.
