Sir Henry’s missing boot in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Sir Henry’s old black boot, marked “Meyers, Toronto,” is used by Stapleton to set a hound on Sir Henry’s track, then found by Holmes in mire after Stapleton throws it away while fleeing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now I come rapidly to the conclusion of this singular

Holmes finds Sir Henry’s missing boot projecting from a tuft of cotton grass in the mire. He concludes that Stapleton retained it after using it to set the hound on the track and threw it away while fleeing.
