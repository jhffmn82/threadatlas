ECONOMICAL STEAM POWER.

The flouring mill in Philadelphia operated two engines rated at seventy indicated horsepower each for an average of twenty-four hours per day on 300 days per year. After replacement by one automatic engine developing 142 horsepower, the stated coal and water costs fell from $11,503 to $6,560, producing a reported annual saving of $4,954.
