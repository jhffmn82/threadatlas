Dozmare Pool in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Dozmare Pool, near St. Neots, is proposed as the mere of King Arthur’s final departure after his death-wound. Though the travelers do not visit it, they imagine a melancholy, ghostly moorland lake, associated in Cornish ghost stories with Tregeagle, spectral hounds, a phantom coach, and unexplained lights.

Dozmare Pool is proposed as the mere where King Arthur was carried after receiving his death-wound, although the travelers do not visit it and instead imagine it. It is described as a melancholy, ghostly moorland lake associated with stories of Tregeagle, spectral hounds, a phantom coach, and unexplained lights.
