Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

TEMPERING BY COMPRESSION.

Compression is applied strongly to heated metal and maintained until the metal is completely cooled. Its effect is described as greater than that of hammering and hardening, especially when those operations are performed on nearly cold metal.
