Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FOOD AND ENERGY OF MAN.

Human nutrition is best maintained by a due admixture and variety of the four classes of aliment discussed in the lecture. Water and salts are also required, and monotony of diet can seriously affect health, digestion, and assimilation.
