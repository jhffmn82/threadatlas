RAFT

method · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: RAFT 27B, RAFT 8B
  • In “RAG-Studio: Towards In-Domain Adaptation of Retrieval Augmented Generation Through Self-Alignment,” RAFT is an in-domain RAG fine-tuning method and a main competing fine-tuning method, primarily fine-tuning the generator with raw LLM-generated pseudo questions, answers, and synthetic training data in noisy contexts; it uses gold passages in 60% of samples and only distractors in 40%, and generally performs below RAG-Studio.
  • In “R2AG: Incorporating Retrieval Information into Retrieval Augmented Generation,” RAFT significantly improves performance in the reported experiments, and combining RAFT with R2AG produces the best overall results on most listed datasets.
  • In “SimRAG: Self-Improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Adapting Large Language Models to Specialized Domains,” RAFT is evaluated as a domain-specific retrieval-augmented baseline and re-implemented with the same backbones as the proposed approach because its checkpoint was not released; RAFT 8B and RAFT 27B obtain the reported medical and scientific averages.

Related Work / RAG Fine-tuning .. Self-Alignment

RAFT is an in-domain RAG fine-tuning method that primarily fine-tunes the generator using raw LLM-generated pseudo questions and answers within noisy contexts. It differs from the described approach, which jointly fine-tunes the generator and retriever and uses self-curated contrastive training data.
