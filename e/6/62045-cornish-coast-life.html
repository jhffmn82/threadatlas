Cornish coast-life in An Unsentimental Journey through Cornwall

Dinah Maria Craik · 1884

Cornish coast-life in winter commonly brings rain, storms, and fogs, with little frost or snow; a storm during the journey shows the travelers what this life is like throughout the season.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

DAY THE FIFTH "Hope for the best, and be prepared for the worst," had been the motto of our journey.

A woman at the post-office says that Cornish coast-life in winter commonly brings rain, storms, and fogs, with little frost or snow. The storm helps the travelers understand what such life is like throughout the winter.
