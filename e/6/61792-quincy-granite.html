THE BUILDING STONE SUPPLY.

Quincy granite was used in the old Merchants' Exchange and Astor House in New York, the Custom House in New Orleans, and many other buildings along the Atlantic coast. It weighs about 165 pounds per cubic foot and was shipped to distant markets.
