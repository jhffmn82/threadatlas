Kelong Mao, Zheng Liu, Hongjin Qian, Fengran Mo, Chenlong Deng, Zhicheng Dou · 2024

A specialized domain corpus is a collection of passages that grounds RAG-Studio. It begins as input for identifying domain knowledge, yields passages for synthetic question-answer pairs and retrieved context, and ultimately supports training-data synthesis and adaptation of general RAG models to a specific domain.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Abstract

RAG-Studio accepts a specialized domain corpus as input. It uses the corpus to identify useful domain knowledge and synthesize training data.

Introduction

The target domain corpus provides the passages from which the LLM initially produces synthetic question-answer pairs. It is used alongside a general retriever and general LLM generator as the starting point for RAG-Studio.

Methodology / Preliminaries .. Fine-tuning RAG Models

The domain corpus contains multiple passages and supplies the grounding passage and additional retrieved context for synthetic training samples. RAG-Studio uses it to adapt general RAG models to a specific domain.
