Ruggedo's punishment in Tik-Tok of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1914

Ruggedo is punished for defying Tititi-Hoochoo and misusing the Forbidden Tube: he is driven from his underground caverns, stripped of magic powers, and made a homeless wanderer.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Thirteen

Ruggedo's punishment is to be driven from his underground caverns, stripped of his magic powers, and made a homeless wanderer on the face of the earth. Tititi-Hoochoo declares that this punishment is deserved because Ruggedo defied his orders and misused the Forbidden Tube.
