Zella in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Zella is Nikobob and Nikobob’s wife’s little daughter, a brave girl from Regos who later lives with them in a cottage in Pingaree. Her story begins when her father gives her Inga’s shoes, which fit perfectly. Wearing them, she gathers honey in the neighboring forest without bee stings, and her parents plan for her to carry two pails annually to Queen Cor. The next morning she sets out in a blue silk shawl and red shoes, carrying honey to sell. On the dangerous journey she discovers newfound strength, throws away a wild boar, crosses the bridge of boats, and reaches Queen Cor’s palace. There she exchanges the lost shoes for Inga’s newer magical shoes; Inga protects her, and Rinkitink pays her gold for the honey when she cannot return home. Zella asks to be taken home, helps plan the journey, cooks meals, and uses her knowledge of the mines of Regos to guide the group to the City of Regos. She returns in Prince Inga’s silver-lined boat, reunites joyfully with her parents, and her father agrees that they will move to Pingaree.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Zella receives Inga's shoes as a present from her father and finds that they fit her perfectly. While wearing them, she gathers honey without being stung by bees, and her parents plan for her to carry two pails of honey to Queen Cor.

Chapter Eleven

Zella sets out for Queen Cor with two pails of honey, wearing her best attire, a blue silk shawl, and red shoes. During the journey she discovers great strength, throws a wild boar away, crosses the bridge of boats, and seeks admittance at the back door of Queen Cor’s palace.

Chapter Thirteen

Zella comes to Queen Cor's palace to sell honey. She is wearing Inga's lost shoes, which her father found near the palace in Regos, and exchanges them for Inga's newer shoes. Inga protects her after the exchange, and Rinkitink gives her gold pieces for the honey when she is unable to return home.

Chapter Fourteen

Zella asks Inga to take her home, helps plan the journey, and guides the group toward the mines of Regos. She prepares their meals, identifies the path to the mines, and returns with the group to the City of Regos when it is considered unsafe for her to travel alone.

Chapter Sixteen

Zella returns to Regos in Prince Inga's silver-lined boat and is joyfully reunited with her father and mother. Her father later agrees to move with her to Pingaree.
