Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The Darlington Steel and Iron Company's Works began using the soaking pit process in early June. The works were blooming about 125 tons of steel per shift, or approximately 300 ingots every twelve hours, and had successfully rolled a considerable number of double-head rails directly from treated ingots.
