Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

LUCIDITY.

Lucidity is the perception that notions long current lack truth and validity, are no longer possible, and can no longer serve. Arnold presents it as inevitable and indispensable, and as the condition of all serious construction for the future.
