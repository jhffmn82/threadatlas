Chain-of-Verification in Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation

Bolei He, Nuo Chen, Xinran He, Lingyong Yan, Zhenkai Wei, Jinchang Luo, and Zhen-Hua Ling · 2024

Chain-of-Verification is a verification mechanism and method used by CoV-RAG to improve Retrieval Augmented Generation. It assesses retrieved references and generated answers through dimensional scores and judgment, then decides whether a question should be revised and retrieval updated for external knowledge errors. Its chain contains reference scores, answer scores, a judgment, a revised question, and rationale for a RAG tuple; CoV-RAG is trained to generate these with answers, using both positive and negative RAG data. From scoring to judgment to revision, it supports verification-aware question answering and improves accuracy, evaluation results, and multiple reported metrics over direct revision.

Retrieving, Rethinking and Revising: The Chain-of-Verification Can Improve Retrieval Augmented Generation

The Chain-of-Verification is presented as a method that can improve Retrieval Augmented Generation.

Introduction

The Chain-of-Verification assesses reference and answer quality through dimensional scores and judgment. It supports query revision and re-retrieval for external knowledge errors and improves single-iteration question answering through verification-aware training.

2 / Methods .. Algorithm 1 CoV-RAG Inference

Chain-of-Verification enables CoV-RAG to assess the retrieved references and generated answer. It evaluates answer quality and determines whether revision and updated retrieval are necessary.

opening

CoV is included with both positive RAG and negative RAG in the CoV-RAG training dataset.

2.3 / CoV-RAG Training

The chain of verification consists of generated reference scores, answer scores, a judgment, a revised question, and rationale for the quality of a RAG tuple. CoV-RAG is trained to generate this information together with answers.

4 / Results and Analysis / 4.1 / Main Results .. 4.4 / Further Analysis on Retriever

The Chain of Verification mechanism improves CoV-RAG’s accuracy and evaluation results. The score-to-judge-to-revise structure outperforms direct revision on judgement, revision, formatting, single-question-answering, multi-question-answering, and reference-delta metrics.
