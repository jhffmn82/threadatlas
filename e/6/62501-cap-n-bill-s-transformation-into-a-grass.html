Cap'n Bill's transformation into a grasshopper in The Scarecrow of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1915

Cap'n Bill's transformation into a little gray grasshopper is caused by Blinkie through an incantation while he sleeps. With a wooden left leg, he can still speak and jump, escapes Blinkie, and later fears being stepped on, dislikes grass, and mentions molasses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twelve

While Cap'n Bill sleeps, Blinkie mixes magical powders and transforms him into a little gray grasshopper with a wooden left leg. The transformation leaves him able to speak and jump, and he escapes before Blinkie can harm him.

Chapter Seventeen

Cap'n Bill is still transformed into a grasshopper when the Ork sees him. He dislikes the flavor of grass, fears being stepped on, and says he may give molasses only if he is squeezed.
