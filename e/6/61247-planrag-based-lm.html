PlanRAG-based LM is a language model that first generates a decision-making plan, then has a retriever generate data-analysis queries, outperforming iterative RAG by 15.8% in Locating and 7.4% in Building.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Abstract

The PlanRAG-based LM generates the decision-making plan as its first step and has the retriever generate data-analysis queries as its second step. It outperforms the state-of-the-art iterative RAG method by 15.8% in Locating and 7.4% in Building.
