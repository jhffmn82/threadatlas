Sir Henry's missing boots in The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1901/1902

Chapter 5.

One of Sir Henry Baskerville's new brown boots disappears, followed by one of his old black boots. The brown boot is later found under a cabinet, although no one explains how it got there, adding another unexplained incident to the investigation.
