CoV-RAG is designed to enhance external retrieval correctness. It corrects retrieval errors by using a revised query to retrieve new knowledge. CoV-RAG improves retrieval accuracy with both Bing and Chrome. Multi-iteration retrieval increases NQ accuracy from 65.0% to 66.8% with Bing and from 69.3% to 71.3% with Chrome, while on WebQ it increases Bing accuracy from 69.8% to 71.0% and leaves Chrome accuracy at 76.0%.
