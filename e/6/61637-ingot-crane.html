Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SOAKING PITS FOR STEEL INGOTS.

The ingot crane places stripped ingots into the soaking pits and later removes them and swings them to the blooming mill. The recommended arrangement uses a crane capable of commanding both the pits and the mill's live rollers; with such a crane, four men and a boy could pass the full make through the pits.
