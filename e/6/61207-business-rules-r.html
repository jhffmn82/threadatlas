Introduction

Business rules are one of the three inputs to Decision QA. They are provided alongside a database and a decision-making question so that the best decision can be generated.

Decision QA task .. Statistics of DQA

Business rules contain textual descriptions of formulas used to reason about the best decision. In the Locating scenario, the rules calculate trading power ratios, incoming value, and profit; in the Building scenario, they calculate total demand, total supply, and current price.

Data instances

The business rules R are shared by the instances in both scenarios.
