merchant in PlanRAG: A Plan-then-Retrieval Augmented Generation for Generative Large Language Models as Decision Makers

Myeonghwa Lee, Seonho An, Min-Soo Kim · 2024

A merchant is an entity placed on trade nodes to steer trade value and maximize profit. In the example, it is considered on Doab or Ganges; placing it in Doab is the decision that maximizes profit in Deccan. On a country’s trading node, it increases flow toward that country’s home node. It can be placed at girin when MCH has a presence there.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

A merchant is placed on a trade node to steer trade value. In the example, BAH should locate its merchant in Doab to maximize profit in Deccan.

Data instances

The merchant is the subject of the Locating scenario question, which asks where it should be located.

Simulators for annotation

The merchant in the example is located on either Doab or Ganges, and the simulator calculates the profit increment for each possible decision.
