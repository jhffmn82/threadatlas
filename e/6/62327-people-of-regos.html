people of Regos in Rinkitink in Oz

People of Regos serve King Gos, fear powerful Inga, and oppose Bilbil; after Gos’s defeat, they bow to Inga and Rinkitink, who treats them considerately.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eight

The people of Regos bow to Inga and Rinkitink as their new masters after King Gos is defeated. They fear Inga because of the strength he previously displayed, but Inga begins treating even the most humble with consideration.
