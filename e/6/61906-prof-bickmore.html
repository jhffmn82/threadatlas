Various · 1881

Prof. Bickmore, superintendent of the American Museum of Natural History, promotes neglected American woods’ commercial value through exhibitions and directs a log-seasoning method that prevents splitting by boring beside the heart.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] THE WOODS OF AMERICA.

Prof. Bickmore, superintendent of the museum, directs a method for preventing logs from splitting during seasoning. The method involves boring a deep hole into the base of a log alongside its heart, allowing inward pressure as the wood seasons.
