Zella's honey in Rinkitink in Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1916

Zella's honey is gathered from hollow trees in the forest, filling two pails. Protected by the shoes, Zella collects it with her mother, then carries it to Queen Cor, whose favor and payment will help Nikobob’s poor family buy needed things.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nine

Zella and her mother gather honey from hollow trees in the forest. With the protection of the shoes, Zella gathers enough to fill two pails and plans to take it to Queen Cor.

Chapter Eleven

Zella carries two pails of honey to Queen Cor. The money expected in exchange for the honey will help Nikobob’s poor family purchase needed things.
