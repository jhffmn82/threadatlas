This work presents a perspective that treats large language models in Retrieval-Augmented Generation as Information Refiners. It introduces INFO-RAG, an unsupervised training method intended to enable information refinement, and evaluates it zero-shot across 11 datasets and 7 tasks, reporting improved RAG performance. The experiments use only 7B and 13B models because of limited computing resources; larger models are left for future exploration. The paper states compliance with the ACL Ethics Policy and no additional ethical concerns, and acknowledges support from three Chinese funding organizations.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Conclusion

This paper proposes a novel perspective that treats LLMs in RAG as an “Information Refiner.” It introduces INFO-RAG and reports experiments across 11 datasets and 7 tasks showing improved RAG performance.

Ethics Statement

The paper is assessed by the authors as not introducing additional ethical concerns.

Acknowledgements
