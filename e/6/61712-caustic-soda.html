Narrative, section by section

] SOAP AND ITS MANUFACTURE, FROM A CONSUMER'S POINT OF VIEW.

Caustic soda is described as a different substance from caustic potash, although the two look alike. Products called crystal potash, ball potash, or rock potash are said to contain caustic soda, often adulterated, rather than potash.
