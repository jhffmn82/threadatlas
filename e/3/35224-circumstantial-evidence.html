IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

Circumstantial evidence appears to point strongly toward James McCarthy through his presence near the body, his bloodstained hand and sleeve, the quarrel, and the nearby gun. Holmes warns that such evidence may point differently when viewed from another perspective.
