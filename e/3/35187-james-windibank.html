III. A CASE OF IDENTITY

Mr. Windibank is Mary Sutherland's stepfather and a traveller for Westhouse & Marbank. He dislikes Mary attending social events and visitors, is away in France during her first meetings with Hosmer, and declines to go to the police after Hosmer disappears.
