Queen Aquareine in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Queen Aquareine is a fairy mermaid and good queen who rules the mermaids and their ocean dominions from her palace. Identified as the first mermaid ever created, she has fairy powers, a wand, a ring, and later a charmed golden sword. She is a powerful but caring ruler: she keeps track of events on earth, protects earth people, gives Trot and Cap'n Bill the ability to understand sea inhabitants, and uses magic to put Trot's mother into a pleasant enchanted sleep while safeguarding her home. She welcomes the visitors, provides food and lodging, and leads them through the ocean, explaining its creatures and dangers. Though she warns of Zog and initially fears sea devils, she relies on the Magic Circle and her fairy powers to defend Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Princess Clia. When the group is driven into Zog's hidden dominions, she chooses to confront him rather than retreat and becomes his prisoner. In the castle, she protects the captives from attacks, destroys the Yell-Maker, cools dangerously heated water, frees the group from ice, and plans their escape. She has a golden sword forged and charmed, leads the escape, and attacks the wolf-fish, but Zog crushes the sword and knocks away her wand. King Anko rescues the fugitives. Afterward, Aquareine escorts Trot and Cap'n Bill, offers friendship and aid to Sacho, returns the travelers toward home, and gives Trot a pearl-set gold ring so she can call for help. Before disappearing beneath the water, she asks Trot to speak well of mermaids.

Narrative, section by section

[Illustration: The PALACE OF QUEEN AQUARINE

Queen Aquareine welcomes Trot and Cap'n Bill and invites them to remain in the ocean palaces for several days. She uses magic to put Trot's mother into a pleasant sleep, protects the house, demonstrates a scene through a mirage, and explains how the visitors can breathe and remain dry underwater.

[Illustration: _Chap 5._

Queen Aquareine welcomes King Anko and explains to Trot that the sea serpent is friendly, rules the ocean, and does not rule the mermaids. She introduces the earth people to him and has given them the power to understand every inhabitant of the sea.

[Illustration: _Chap. 6._

Queen Aquareine welcomes Trot and Cap'n Bill, provides them with food and lodging, and asks them about life on earth. She invites them to her dinner and later sends them on an afternoon swim with Merla. Merla says that Queen Aquareine can stiffen the snapping eels when they misbehave.

[Illustration: _Chap. 8._

Queen Aquareine welcomes Trot and Cap'n Bill and orders a banquet and entertainment for them. She plans to take them to the largest and most remarkable sea creatures and to visit King Anko. She draws the Magic Circle around both guests and leaves Merla to rule the palaces while she travels with them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 9._

Queen Aquareine leads Princess Clia, Trot, and Cap'n Bill through the open sea to see unusual sea creatures and plants. She explains the nature of octopuses, sea devils, Zog, King Anko, sawfishes, and the Magic Circle, and urges the group to stay together.

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Queen Aquareine guides Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Clia upward through the ocean to an unnamed island. She explains the habits of starfishes, sea horses, turtles, seals, and sea-gulls, and recognizes Chief Muffruff. After the turtles defeat the seals, she leads the group back beneath the water.

[Illustration: _Chap. 11._

Queen Aquareine recognizes that the cuttlefish are driving the group toward the Devil Caves and tries to retreat, but the ink blocks their escape. She stops the group rather than letting it be driven farther and declares that the sea devils cannot harm them because of the protecting magic circles.

[Illustration: _Chap. 12._

Queen Aquareine leads the group out of the sea-devil enclosure and identifies their passage as possibly belonging to the hidden dominions of Zog. She chooses to face Zog rather than turn back and relies on her fairy powers to protect her companions. She recognizes the magical illusion concealing the chamber entrance and swims through it.

[Illustration: _Chap. 13._

Queen Aquareine confronts Zog and challenges his power to destroy the mermaids. Zog identifies her as the first mermaid ever created and says she is his prisoner in the castle.

[Illustration: _Chap. 15._

Queen Aquareine gives Tom Atto a ring that keeps prepared food from becoming wet and considers escaping through an opening in the castle's dome. Her magic protects the captives from the Yell-Maker and the falling marble, and she destroys the Yell-Maker with her fairy wand.

[Illustration: _Chap. 16._

Aquareine stays calm during Zog's attack and questions Sacho about guarded exits. She concludes that Zog will try more powerful attacks and plans for the group to escape, but learns that there is no night and that Zog never sleeps.

[Illustration: _Chap. 17._

Aquareine cools the water after Zog makes it dangerously hot and leads the discussion about escaping. She decides that a weapon is needed to overcome the guards and asks a goldsmith to forge a golden sword. After the sword is made, she plans to charm it with fairy powers and promises that the group will escape.

[Illustration: _Chap. 18._

Queen Aquareine melts the ice around herself, Trot, Princess Clia, and Cap'n Bill with her magic and frees them. She decides to escape Zog's castle, breaks its window with the golden sword, and leads the group toward the opening in the dome to confront the prince of the sea devils.

[Illustration: _Chap. 19._

Aquareine attacks the wolf-fish with her golden sword, killing them one by one. Zog takes and crushes her sword and then knocks her fairy wand away, leaving her unable to defend the fugitives until King Anko rescues them.

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

Aquareine accompanies Trot and Cap'n Bill to King Anko's palace and observes the formal ceremony required there. She explains that the king's dignity must be maintained and later agrees to escort the earth travelers home. She and Clia are pleased when Trot says she will always love the mermaids.

[Illustration: _Chap. 21._

Aquareine offers to take the travelers back to their mermaid home and leads them past the opening in the dome. She visits the castle to learn the fate of Zog's former slaves and offers Sacho her friendship and assistance.

[Illustration: _Chap. 22._

Queen Aquareine gives Trot a valuable pearl-set gold ring and promises to hear her call and assist her if she wears it. She asks Trot to speak well of mermaids when ignorant earth people condemn them, then says goodbye before disappearing beneath the water.
