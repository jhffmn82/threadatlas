The gold document is a relevant document chunk used to test the effect of guaranteed retrieval. In Retriever-W-GT conditions, it is added to the retrieved list and can be placed first or last.

The gold document is a source verified to contain the answer. Models generally perform better when it is retrieved, especially when it appears near the top of the retrieved list.

4

Gold documents are important to retrieve because LLMs may exhibit pseudo-helpfulness when relevant gold documents are absent from the retrieved results. Improving retrieval precision can improve their rank in the retrieved list.
