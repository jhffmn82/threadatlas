Jocasta's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Jocasta's royal head is sunk in death, death, end, her death, horror, the fatal cause
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Jocasta's death occurs by hanging in the bridal room after she recognizes the horror of her marriage and offspring, and the Messenger describes it as the source of the further calamity.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, Jocasta's death occurs by suicide when she hangs herself in the bridal chamber after realizing her incestuous marriage and its consequences; Oedipus finds her body.
