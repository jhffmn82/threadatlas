Cuttenclip village in The Emerald City of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1910

Cuttenclip village is a paper village ruled by Miss Cuttenclip as Queen, enclosed by a blue wall and linked to the main road. Its paper inhabitants and structures survive because Glinda arranged isolation and kept rainstorms away.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

10. How the Cuttenclips Lived

The Cuttenclip village is enclosed by a high blue wall with pink ornaments and contains paper trees, cardboard houses, paper flowers, and paper people. Glinda arranged the isolated location and kept rainstorms away so the village would not be blown apart or become wet.
