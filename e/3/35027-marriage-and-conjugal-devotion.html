Marriage and conjugal devotion is a rare lifelong unity, exemplified as Alcestis, Pelias’s daughter, dies for her lord, showing two hearts remain one-knit forever.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

Marriage is portrayed as a bond in which two hearts remain one-knit forever. The Daughter of Pelias exemplifies conjugal devotion by dying for her lord, and the elders call such a bride rare in the world.
