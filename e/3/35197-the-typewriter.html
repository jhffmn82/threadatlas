the typewriter in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The typewriter has individuality through identifying characteristics like handwriting: it produces slurred e characters, a defective r tail, and fourteen other features matching letters attributed to Hosmer Angel.

The typewriter is treated by Holmes as having identifying characteristics comparable to a person's handwriting. The machine used by Windibank's office produces a slurred 'e,' a defective tail on the 'r,' and fourteen other distinctive features that match the letters attributed to Hosmer Angel.
