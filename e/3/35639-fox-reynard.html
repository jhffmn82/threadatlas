Fox (Reynard) in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Fox is an animal of admitted acuteness. He enters with a dreadfully swollen snout, reports that Wasp produced the honey after finding it smeared with honey, and says Wasp stung him, with the swelling supporting his report.

The Fox enters with a dreadfully swollen snout and reports that the Wasp produced the honey. He says he found the Wasp smeared with undoubted honey after applying his nose to it, and that the Wasp stung him; the swelling is taken as evidence supporting his report.
