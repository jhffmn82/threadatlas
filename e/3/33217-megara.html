Megara in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

Megara is the daughter of Creon, the deceased king of Thebes, and the wife and consort of Hercules, with whom she has children. She is a refugee at Jupiter’s altar with Amphitryon and her children when Lycus threatens them with execution. Grieving for the absent Hercules and seeing little hope of escape or persuasion, she asks that her sons be dressed in funeral robes and leads them with Amphitryon toward death. She recalls Hercules’ plans for their inheritances and marriages, appeals to him for protection, and condemns their attackers as cowards. When Hercules returns, she tells him of Lycus’s killings and threats, then enters the palace with him and the children after his promise of safety. During a purification ceremony, Hercules kills Megara and a child in madness. He later mourns her as faithful to his bed and watchful over his house.

Narrative, section by section

6 sections; each heading opens the text

HERCULES DISTRACTED.

Megara, the daughter of Creon and wife of Hercules, remains with Amphitryon and her children at the altar. She grieves for Hercules, recognizes that escape and persuasion are unlikely, and asks Lycus to let her dress the children in funeral robes before their threatened execution.

CHORUS.

Hercules’ loved wife is seen in funereal vestments conducting Hercules’ sons together with his decrepit father. The Chorus mourns while looking upon them.

MEGARA, AMPHITRYON, CHORUS.

Megara mourns that she, her children, and the aged Amphitryon are being led together to death. She recalls the hopes Hercules had formed for his sons’ inheritances and marriages, calls on Hercules to protect them, and says that their murderers are cowards compared with him.

HERCULES, MEGARA, AMPHITRYON, CHORUS.

Megara recognizes Hercules when he returns and tells him that Lycus has killed her brothers and threatens her, her children, and Amphitryon. She enters the palace with Hercules and the children after he promises to protect them.

IRIS, A FIEND, CHORUS.

Megara is present with Hercules, Amphitryon, and the children during the purification ceremony. Hercules kills her and a child with a single shaft while in his madness.

THESEUS, AMPHITRYON, HERCULES, CHORUS.

Hercules’ wife is among those killed in the disaster. Hercules mourns her as the woman who preserved his bed inviolate and watched over his house.
