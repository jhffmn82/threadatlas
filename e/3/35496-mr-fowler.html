Mr. Fowler in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mr. Fowler is a small, bearded seaman in a grey suit, first seen standing by the railings in Southampton Road and looking toward Miss Hunter at the Copper Beeches. Though sent away and no longer seen there, he remains devoted to Alice Rucastle after her illness. He blockades the house, gains Mrs. Toller’s help, arranges a ladder, and helps Alice escape. He marries Alice in Southampton and takes a government appointment in Mauritius.

He blockaded the house, won Mrs. Toller’s assistance through kindness and payment, arranged the ladder, and helped Alice escape.
