the oracle concerning Laius's death in The Seven Plays in English Verse

Sophocles · -0449/-0429

Apollo's ministers issue an oracle that Laius's abandoned infant son will kill him; Jocasta later says the son supposedly died and robbers killed Laius, disproving it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHORUS.

The oracle concerning Laius's death said that Laius would be killed by his own son. Jocasta argues that it was disproved because Laius's infant son was abandoned and supposedly died, while Laius was said to have been killed by robbers.
