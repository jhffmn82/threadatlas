the robbery preparations in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The robbery preparations keep Jabez Wilson from his cellar while a tunnel is dug and bags and a chisel arranged; when the Red-headed League offices close, Holmes recognizes completion and arranges an ambush.

The robbery preparations include keeping Jabez Wilson away from his cellar, digging a tunnel, and arranging bags and a chisel for the theft. Once the Red-headed League offices close, Holmes realizes the preparations are complete and arranges the ambush.
