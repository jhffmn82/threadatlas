society in Impressions of Theophrastus Such

George Eliot · 1879

Society grows through fellowship, orderly self-interest, public conscience, relations between the sexes, sensitivity to others’ claims, and socially useful work; it is threatened by selfishness, oppression, dishonesty, malicious journalism, political partiality, and lax workmanship.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XVI.

Society depends on a sensibility to the claims of others and on work that genuinely contributes to effective social wealth. It is harmed by selfish and unfair action, dishonest products, malicious journalism, political partiality, and lax workmanship.
