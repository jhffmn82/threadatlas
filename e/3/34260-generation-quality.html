answer generation quality in Optimizing LLM Based Retrieval Augmented Generation Pipelines in the Financial Domain

Yiyun Zhao, Hanoz Bhathena, Prateek Singh, Saket Sharma, Bernardo Ramos, Aviral Joshi, Swaroop Gadiyaram · 2024

Answer generation quality is an outcome of the retrieval pipeline, prompts, and generation models, evaluated across 6 LLMs, 15 retrieval scenarios, 9 prompts, and 2 financial datasets. For extractive tasks it is measured by token F1, not Exact Match, reference-free metrics, or recall. Quality generally improves when a verified answer-containing document is retrieved, but varies with document number and order, model, prompt, retrieval recall and precision, and distractors.

Abstract

Answer generation quality is investigated as an outcome affected by the retrieval pipeline, prompts, and generation models. The study evaluates it across 6 LLMs, 15 retrieval scenarios, 9 prompts, and 2 real-world financial domain datasets.

2 / 2.1 .. Evaluation Metrics and Aspects

Answer quality is evaluated using token F1 scores because the tasks are extractive. Exact Match and reference-free metrics are not reported, and recall is avoided because it can be increased by copying many source sentences.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.4

Answer generation quality generally improves when a source verified to contain the answer is retrieved. It varies with the number and order of retrieved documents, the generation model, and the prompt.

4

Generation quality is influenced by several components of the RAG pipeline, especially retrieval recall, retrieval precision, and the presence of distractors.
