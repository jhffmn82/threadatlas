South Kensington collection in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

The South Kensington collection is a collection of foreign musical instruments, including Egyptian, western Asian, Chinese kin and chê, and a cither. Its chief importance is that some still resemble forms on ancient monuments, while old instruments illuminate historical literature and music.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

The South Kensington collection contains several foreign musical instruments that are of interest to musicians. It also includes instruments from Egypt and western Asia that retain the appearance of forms represented on monuments three thousand years old, as well as old instruments useful for understanding historical literature and music.
