healthcare in Retrieval augmented generation for large language models in healthcare: A systematic review

Lameck Mbangula Amugongo · 2025-06-11

Healthcare is a knowledge-intensive, highly regulated application domain for reviewed Large Language Model and Retrieval-Augmented Generation approaches. It requires accurate, current, domain-specific information and is guided by bioethics and data regulations. The review examines systems for medical questions, clinical dialogue, biomedical information, radiology, treatment-guideline recommendations, patient advice, diagnostic and treatment suggestions, disease phenotyping, trial matching, summarization, and clinical writing. These systems generally improve on foundational models or generic chatbots, but results depend on knowledge sources and query complexity; specialized sources, modular retrieval, knowledge graphs, and agentic reasoning are reported as beneficial. Because medical decisions are critical, privacy, safety, robustness, bias, trust, correctness, and hallucination are central concerns. The review identifies gaps in systematic understanding of datasets, RAG methods, evaluation frameworks, evaluation standardization, and ethical assessment, limiting responsible and effective adoption. Studies primarily used proprietary RAG models, particularly models from OpenAI.
