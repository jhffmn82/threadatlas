Kumar and Clark Clinical Medicine 10th Edition was used to build a vector database, evaluate retrieval responses, and test summarization methods. It contained 1,508 pages and 13,024 text chunks averaging 789 tokens, from which 19 representative chunks were selected under a 15,000-token limit.
