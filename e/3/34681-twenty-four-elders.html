twenty-four elders in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Twenty-four elders are seen by St. John in the Apocalypse, then depicted seated around Portico della gloria’s central arch, each with an instrument.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration] CHAPTER IX.

They are represented as seated figures around the central arch of the Portico della gloria, each with an instrument. Their instruments include organistrum, harps, salterios, and different forms of vihuela.
