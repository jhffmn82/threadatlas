CHAPTER VI.

Near the Orinoco, Gumilla found a wooden trumpet nearly seven feet long. He also observed the botuto among Indian tribes near the river; this baked-clay trumpet was commonly three or four feet long, though some were enormous, and its deep sound was used in mourning and funeral dances.
