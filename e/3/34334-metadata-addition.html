Metadata Addition

process · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Metadata Enrichment, metadata enrichment, metadata inclusion
  • In Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Metadata Addition enriches chunk blocks with titles, keywords, and hypothetical questions to improve retrieval, support post-processing, and help LLMs understand retrieved information; detailed study is deferred.
  • In FACTS About Building Retrieval Augmented Generation-based Chatbots, metadata enrichment is one of the RAG stages observed to have the greatest impact on chatbot response quality, strongly affecting retrieval relevancy and being optimized along with chunking, query rephrasal, and query reranking.
