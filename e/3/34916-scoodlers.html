Scoodlers in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Scoodlers are slender, hostile creatures living on rocks around a hollow in the hills and in the Scoodlers' cave, ruled by the Queen of the Scoodlers. Each has two fronts and no back, alternating black and white sides with different faces and yellow or purple hair, small sparkling eyes, T-shaped feet, and a removable head. They surround Dorothy and the travelers, demand them for soup, and attack by throwing their heads. After capturing the travelers and taking them to the Soup Kettle to chop them and make soup, they lose their heads to the Shaggy Man and become helpless.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

9. Facing the Scoodlers

The Scoodlers are slender, two-sided creatures whose black and white sides have different faces, hair, and coloring. They surround the travelers, identify themselves as Scoodlers, demand them for soup, and say they love the Shaggy Man in soup. When the Shaggy Man throws a stone, two Scoodlers remove their heads, hurl them at him, retrieve them, and replace them.

10. Escaping the Soup-Kettle

The Scoodlers attack the travelers by throwing their removable heads, capture them, and take them to their cave. They plan to run the prisoners through a meat chopper and make soup from them, but lose their heads to the shaggy man and are left helpless.
