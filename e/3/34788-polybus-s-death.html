Polybus's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Death confines him in a kingly grave, Polybus hath passed away, Polybus in his grave, Polybus is dead, dead, dead and in his grave, death of Polybus, he died, he is dead and gone
  • In The Seven Plays in English Verse, Polybus's death is reported by the Corinthian Shepherd as resulting from sickness and old age, initially seeming to silence the prophecy that Oedipus would kill his father but helping reveal that Polybus was not Oedipus's biological father.
  • In Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone, the death of Polybus is announced as a natural death after a full lifespan, initially reassuring Jocasta and Oedipus but later shown not to disprove the prophecy because Polybus was not Oedipus's biological father.
