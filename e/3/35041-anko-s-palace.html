Anko's palace in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

King Anko's home is a vast circular polished-alabaster palace with an ocean-style dome and round openings. Its halls show ocean scenes and sea-grass rugs, ending at a central chamber concealing thousands of Anko's coils.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 20._

King Anko's palace is a vast circular structure made entirely of polished alabaster, with a domed roof and round openings instead of doors and windows. Its halls contain engraved scenes of ocean life and woven sea-grass rugs, while the central chamber conceals thousands of coils of Anko's body beneath a purple-and-gold coverlet.
