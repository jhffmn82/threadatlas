Mrs. Rucastle in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

Mrs. Rucastle, Mr. Rucastle’s wife and Edward Rucastle’s mother figure, is pale, silent, and passionately devoted to her husband and young son. Often sorrowful, lost in thought, and seen in tears, she helps direct the blue-dress performances, watches Miss Hunter, and orders the blind drawn when a bearded man appears. Away when Holmes and Watson arrive, she returns with the country surgeon and nurses Mr. Rucastle after the dog’s attack.
