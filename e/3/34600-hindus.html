1 section; each heading opens the text

THE HINDUS.

The Hindus are described as possessing about fifty musical instruments, many of which have remained in popular use with little alteration. Their musical scale included twenty-two intervals called sruti, and they also used Arabic and Persian instruments introduced comparatively recently into Hindustan.
