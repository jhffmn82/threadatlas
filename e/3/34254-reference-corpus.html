reference corpus in RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation

Shahul Es, Jithin James, Luis Espinosa-Anke, Steven Schockaert · 2024

A reference corpus supplies retrievable passages and knowledge to large language models through RAG, supporting up-to-date systems and reducing hallucinations. It affects RAG performance, while perplexity-based evaluation on it may not predict downstream performance.

Abstract

A reference corpus supplies knowledge to large language models through RAG systems. The text presents this knowledge as a way to support up-to-date systems and reduce hallucinations.

opening

A corpus supplies passages that can be retrieved and given to a language model as part of Retrieval Augmented Generation. Perplexity-based evaluation may measure performance on a reference corpus, but this is not always predictive of downstream performance.
