IV. THE BOSCOMBE VALLEY MYSTERY

James McCarthy was arrested after returning to Hatherley Farm and being informed by the inspector of constabulary that he was a prisoner. He said he was not surprised and that the arrest was no more than his deserts, but he also protested his innocence.
