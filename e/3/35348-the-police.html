  • In The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, the police arrest Horner, hold Arthur, and search for the stolen coronet gems, but fail to find them or explain the case.
  • In The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes, the police plan to arrest Professor Moriarty and his principal gang members, examine the site of a falling brick, and take into custody Holmes’s attacker.
  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, the police constitute the regular official forces considered sufficient for suppressing crime, though Holmes says their involvement would expose private matters.
  • In The Return of Sherlock Holmes, the police search for Colonel Sebastian Moran, take him into custody, and investigate McFarlane’s case but initially miss Oldacre’s concealed room.
  • In The Valley of Fear, the police investigate Jack Douglas’s murder, search for a wet stranger, and find a bicycle near the Manor House.
  • In The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes, the police support Holmes’s cases, take suspects into custody, examine The Three Gables, recover evidence, pursue the suspected gang, and investigate Sir Robert’s concealment of a death.
  • In The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes, the police investigate the murders through Inspector MacKinnon, prepare to arrest Amberley, search his house, and take over the investigation after Holmes supplies his findings.

the police in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

The police arrest Horner for the jewel robbery and hold Arthur while investigating the stolen coronet gems and Arthur’s alleged theft. They search Arthur, his room, the house, and garden for missing gems, but find no trace and cannot explain the case. Holmes believes their investigation misses the goose-related line of inquiry, while Ryder fears a search will reveal the blue carbuncle.

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

The police are a group distinct from Scotland Yard that supports Holmes’s cases. They wait below his rooms, enter when summoned, handcuff Count Sylvius and Sam Merton, and take them away. After the burglary at The Three Gables, they examine the property, question Mrs. Maberley, take the recovered manuscript fragment, pursue the suspected gang, and investigate Sir Robert’s concealment of the death.
