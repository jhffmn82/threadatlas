Demophon in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

Demophon is king of Athens, ruling without absolute sovereignty. When Eurystheus and the Argive army threaten, he prepares Athens to defend Hercules' children. Bound by an oracle demanding a noble virgin, he refuses to sacrifice his own child or compel a citizen, seeking a way to save both suppliants and city.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHOR. This land is ever willing to aid in a just cause those in difficulty;

Demophon prepares the citizens, spies, sacrifices, and defenses of Athens against Eurystheus and the Argive army. He refuses to kill his own child or compel another citizen's child to die, and asks Iolaus to devise a way to save both the suppliants and the city.
