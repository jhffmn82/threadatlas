codfish in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Codfish are dark-backed, silver underneath, haughty fish who consider themselves the sea’s most aristocratic family and foolishly prize pedigree; offended by mermaids’ discussion and Trot’s insult, they leave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 7._

The codfish are described as dark-backed, silver underneath, and the most aristocratic family of fish in the sea. They are haughty and proud of their pedigree, take offense at being discussed by mermaids, and leave after Trot insults them.
