Al-Farabi in Musical Instruments

Carl Engel · 1875-08

Al-Farabi, an early tenth-century Arabian musical theorist, did not mention the fiddle-bow; his musical reputation was associated with Spain and Cordova.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE PERSIANS AND ARABS.

Al-Farabi was one of the earliest known Arabian musical theorists and lived at the beginning of the tenth century. He did not mention the fiddle-bow, and his musical reputation was associated with Spain and Cordova.
