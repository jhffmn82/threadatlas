the queen's dinner in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

The queen's dinner is a sea-food meal served at noon to Queen Aquareine's nobility, favorites, and guests. It progresses through varied dishes served backward and fussily by large lobsters, ending with flower-shaped ice cream made from northern iceberg ice.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 6._

The queen's dinner is served at noon to Queen Aquareine's nobility, favorites, and guests. It includes turtle soup, salmon steaks, fungus bread, oysters, clams, soft-shell crabs, seaweed salad, fruits, and flower-shaped ice creams. Large lobsters serve the courses backward and fussily, while the ice cream is made with ice brought from northern icebergs.
