Jabez Wilson in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

II. THE RED-HEADED LEAGUE

Jabez Wilson is a red-headed pawnbroker who is recruited into the Red-Headed League after applying for its advertised vacancy. He copies the Encyclopædia Britannica at Pope's Court for eight weeks, earning £4 per week, until he finds a notice announcing that the League has been dissolved.

Sherlock Holmes and I surveyed this curt announcement and the rueful

Mr. Jabez Wilson explains that the Red-headed League disappeared after he lost a position paying four pounds a week. He says that inquiries led him to discover that the supposed Duncan Ross was actually associated with a man named William Morris, whose stated new address was false. Wilson asks Sherlock Holmes to identify the League and determine why it played the prank on him.

It was a quarter-past nine when I started from home and made my way

Jabez Wilson is described as a not-over-bright pawnbroker whose presence at his shop is removed for several hours each day. The Red-headed League scheme lures him away so that John Clay’s accomplice can work in the cellar.
