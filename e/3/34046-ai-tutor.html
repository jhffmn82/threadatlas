AI Tutor in How to Build an Adaptive AI Tutor for Any Course Using Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (KG-RAG)

AI Tutor is an educational system built as a unified KG-RAG framework integrating DeepSeek-V3, retrieval-augmented generation, and a knowledge graph. Grounded in constructivist learning through concept relationships, it proceeds through knowledge graph construction, knowledge-guided retrieval, and response synthesis. 3 sections; each heading opens the text

II. FOUNDATIONS OF KG-RAG

The AI Tutor uses Knowledge Graphs to improve contextual understanding and relies on reasoning supported by DeepSeek-V3. Its educational application is associated with constructivist learning theory through the formation of relationships among concepts.

III. AI TUTOR DESIGN: THE KG-RAG SOLUTION

The AI Tutor integrates DeepSeek-V3, RAG, and a knowledge graph to deliver precise, context-aware educational responses. It uses knowledge graph construction, knowledge-guided retrieval, and response synthesis.

IV. AI TUTOR EVALUATION

The AI Tutor uses KG-RAG to provide more complete, precise, and relationally coherent explanations than the RAG responses shown in the comparison. Students rated it positively for relevance, accessibility, understanding improvement, and comparison with a human tutor.
