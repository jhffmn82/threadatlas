the fox soldiers in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

Fox soldiers are uniformed foxes who guard the road from Foxville to a neighboring beast village. Wearing colorful clothing and carrying toothed wooden swords, they surround Dorothy's party and escort it through Foxville to the royal palace.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3. A Queer Village

The fox-soldiers wear green jackets, yellow pantaloons, red caps and boots, and red bows on their tails. They surround Dorothy's party, carry wooden swords with rows of sharp teeth, and escort the group through Foxville to the royal palace.
