20. How Dorothy Lunched With a King

The palace contains a great hall, a throne room decorated with cloth-of-gold and gold furniture, and a reception hall furnished with jeweled pieces. Its gardens have flowers, shrubs, trees, and marble-paved walks. The Rabbit King hosts Dorothy there and prepares to present her to the nobility.
