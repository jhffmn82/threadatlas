Chief Muffruff in The Sea Fairies

L. Frank Baum · 1911

Chief Muffruff, chief of the island's disorderly seal family, leads the seals, recognizes Queen Aquareine and Clia, jokes about Cap'n Bill, then provokes turtles and escapes after they bite him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Illustration: _Chap. 10._

Chief Muffruff is the chief of the disorderly family of seals living on and around the island. He recognizes Queen Aquareine and Clia, jokes about Cap'n Bill being bald-headed, and starts a quarrel by flipping a turtle onto its back. The turtles bite his fins and tail before he escapes into the water.
