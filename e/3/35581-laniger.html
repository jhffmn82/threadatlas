Laniger has temper but no talent for repartee; after Mordax fiercely attacks him verbally, he is hurt and concludes Mordax is a wolf at heart while friends fail to grasp his pain.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII.

Laniger is fiercely attacked by Mordax and, lacking a talent for repartee, comes to believe that Mordax is a wolf at heart. Laniger suffers from the attack while his friends fail to form a lively conception of his pain.
