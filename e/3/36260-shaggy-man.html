Shaggy Man in The Road to Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1909

The Shaggy Man is a shaggy-clothed man, traveler, and Dorothy’s friend who calls himself good, despises money because it makes people proud and haughty, and wants love from everyone he meets. He carries the Love Magnet, which causes living things such as the foxes of Foxville to love him, though it does not protect him from the Scoodlers, who love him “in soup.” He begins as a stranger asking Dorothy about Butterfield while avoiding a man who owes him fifteen cents. He helps Dorothy when she is lost, proposes caring for the poor, lost Button-Bright, and joins Dorothy, Toto, Button-Bright, and later Polychrome on an uncertain journey, believing every road leads somewhere. He remains cheerful and practical, eating and sharing apples, refusing finer clothes because they would make him no longer the Shaggy Man, and protecting or aiding his companions. He is transformed by King Kik-a-bray into a man with a donkey head, dislikes it, and seeks the Truth Pond. Against the Scoodlers he leads the group, fights their guards, catches their thrown heads, and escapes with the others. When the party cannot cross the desert, he uses the Love Magnet to call Johnny Dooit and pilots the sand-boat across it. At the Truth Pond, his donkey head is removed and his proper shaggy human head restored. In the Land of Oz, he is impressed that money is not used and wishes to live there always. He is told he may stay if he proves himself honest, true, and worthy of friendship. At Ozma’s palace, unexpected welcome, fine shaggy clothes, jewels, and apartments astonish a man who has more often slept in hay-lofts and stables. Because bathing in the Truth Pond compels truth, he admits that he stole the Love Magnet from a girl in Butterfield after no one loved or cared for him. He agrees that it should hang over the Emerald City gates. After joining the reception and birthday procession, he remains in Oz while trying to prove himself honest and true.

Narrative, section by section

20 sections; each heading opens the text

1. The Way to Butterfield

The Shaggy Man asks Dorothy for directions to Butterfield but says he does not want to go there because a man there owes him fifteen cents. He takes Toto and several apples in his pockets, then helps Dorothy after she becomes lost. He says he owns a Love Magnet that makes every living thing he meets love him.

2. Dorothy Meets Button-Bright

The Shaggy Man travels with Dorothy and Toto along the seventh road, whistling cheerful tunes and eating apples. He says that Button-Bright should come along with them and explains that every road leads somewhere, even if they do not know where they are going.

3. A Queer Village

The Shaggy Man leads the party with Dorothy, Button-Bright, and Toto and is treated kindly by the foxes because of the Love Magnet. He tells King Dox that the party came to get away as soon as possible and denies being a magician or fairy responsible for their journey.

4. King Dox

The shaggy man is concerned about Button-Bright's transformation and about remaining in Foxville. He refuses the foxes' offer of finer clothing because he says he would no longer be the shaggy man without his usual clothes. He joins Dorothy and Button-Bright in leaving Foxville and continues toward the next village.

5. The Rainbow's Daughter

The Shaggy Man travels with Dorothy, Toto, and Button-Bright. He listens to Polychrome's story and cheerfully invites her to continue the journey with them.

6. The City Of Beasts

The shaggy man cuts and distributes the turkey at luncheon and carries the Love Magnet for protection. In Dunkiton, he uses a switch to stop the donkeys from kicking metal sheets and secures their respectful treatment by presenting the travelers as important and distinguished.

7. The Shaggy Man's Transformation

The shaggy man flatters King Kik-a-bray, who rewards him by transforming his human head into a donkey's head. He dislikes the change and must seek the Truth Pond to regain his own head, while the Love Magnet continues to make the others love him.

8. The Musicker

The shaggy man is irritated by Allegro da Capo's continual piping and repeatedly asks him to stop or quiet it. He suggests that the musicker may have swallowed an accordeon in his youth and urges the travelers to continue on their journey.

9. Facing the Scoodlers

The Shaggy Man divides two apples among the travelers and says that Dorothy's Magic Belt could help them, though she left it with Ozma. He tries to use the Love Magnet to stop the Scoodlers from harming him and his friends, but they say they love him in soup. He leads the group toward the road's opening, throws a stone to scare the Scoodlers away, and is knocked down when two of them hurl their detachable heads at him.

10. Escaping the Soup-Kettle

The shaggy man tries to lead the others away from the Scoodlers, but they are captured. He plans to fight rather than wait to be made into soup, catches the guard's head in a sack, and helps the others escape. At the cave entrance, he catches every head thrown at him and tosses the heads into the black gulf.

11. Johnny Dooit Does It

The Shaggy Man realizes that the group cannot cross the desert or safely return, but later thinks of summoning Johnny Dooit through the Love Magnet. He explains their predicament to Johnny and agrees to sail the sand-boat across the sands.

Shaggy Man is a polite, mortal human man, an American who formerly lived in Colorado before going to the Land of Oz. He knows Ozma of Oz and Princess Dorothy, whom he calls an old chum. A traveler and companion of Betsy Bobbin, Hank, Ozga, Polychrome, Tik-Tok, Queen Ann, and others, he carries the Love Magnet, a magic talisman kept in his pocket, and believes it can win allies and even conquer Ruggedo. What matters most to him is rescuing his long-lost brother, the Ugly One, who has been unjustly imprisoned by Ruggedo. He has sought him for a long time, commands or urges an expedition against Ruggedo, offers the Army of Oogaboo plunder for its help, and repeatedly presses onward through dangers toward the Nome King's caverns. He also shows care for companions: he explains the Rubber Country and gravitation to Betsy, assists Tik-Tok and Tubekins, comforts others, and earnestly promises never to desert Betsy. His journey begins when he falls into the Rose Kingdom while seeking the hidden passage to Ruggedo's cavern. After leaving with Betsy, Hank, and Ozga, he gathers companions, discovers Tik-Tok, and learns more of his brother's captivity. He plans to use the Love Magnet against Ruggedo, but nomes bind him before he can draw it, and Ruggedo transforms him into a dove. Restored by touching Quox's ribbon, he continues demanding his brother's release and leads the search into the Metal Forest. There he finds the Ugly One, welcomes and comforts him without needing to see his face, and gives him a handkerchief and knife to make a mask. After the enchantment is broken, Shaggy rejoices in his brother's restored face and freedom. He remains faithful to Betsy and his brother, chooses their company rather than returning alone, mourns Polychrome's departure, and finally reaches the Land of Oz with Betsy, Hank, and his brother, crying tears of joy.

Chapter Six

Shaggy Man falls through the glass roof of the Rose Kingdom's greenhouse after an apple-tree branch breaks. He explains that he is searching for his lost brother and is seeking the hidden passage to the Nome King's underground cavern. He helps Betsy pick Princess Ozga, is driven out of the Rose Kingdom with her, and continues traveling with Betsy, Hank, and Ozga.

Chapter Seven

Shaggy Man invites Polychrome to join the travelers and uses the Love Magnet to make her love him. He investigates the well, recognizes the recovered copper man as his old friend Tik-Tok, and learns that his brother is imprisoned in Ruggedo's cavern.

Chapter Eight

Shaggy introduces his companions to Queen Ann and tells her that Ruggedo should be conquered. He uses the Love Magnet to win the friendship of the Army of Oogaboo, then offers its members the plunder from Ruggedo's kingdom if they help rescue his brother.

Chapter Nine

Shaggy Man explains that the rocks, stepping-stones, and path in the Rubber Country are rubber and warns the party that they may get the bounce. The Long-Eared Hearer learns that Shaggy Man is coming to rescue his brother, the Ugly One.

Chapter Ten

Shaggy Man slides through the Hollow Tube with Polychrome and the others, briefly receiving her heel on his head and later her heel near his eye. He helps Tubekins remove the battered crown from his eyes and oils Tik-Tok after the copper man is lifted from the fountain.

Chapter Eleven

Shaggy Man is present with the party when it is brought before the Private Citizen. He is disturbed when he learns that the ruler is Tititi-Hoochoo, the Great Jinjin, and wipes perspiration from his brow.

Chapter Fifteen

Shaggy Man explains gravity to Betsy during the journey through the Tube and says that the earth's roundness and the travelers' speed carry them from one end of the hole to the other. He later helps raise Tik-Tok after Tik-Tok falls.

Chapter Sixteen

Shaggy Man remains confident that the Love Magnet can win the nomes to his side and intends to use it if Queen Ann and Quox fail. He waits outside Ruggedo's domains with Files, Polychrome, and the Rose Princess, wondering what has happened to Queen Ann and the others.

Chapter Seventeen

Shaggy Man plans to enter the caverns and use the Love Magnet to conquer Ruggedo. Nomes bind his arms before he can remove the talisman, and Ruggedo later transforms him into a dove.

Chapter Nineteen

Shaggy Man is restored from the form of a cooing dove when he touches Quox's ribbon. He helps Tik-Tok, asks Kaliko to release his brother, and urges the group to find a secret passage to the Metal Forest.

Chapter Twenty

Shaggy urges the others to follow Ruggedo into the Metal Forest so they can rescue his lost brother. At the Forest, he cries out anxiously for his brother.

Chapter Twenty-One

Shaggy Man anxiously searches for and finds his long-lost brother, the Ugly One. He gives him a handkerchief and a knife so he can make a mask, then welcomes and comforts him despite never seeing his face.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Shaggy Man rejoices at being reunited with his brother and anxiously asks whether the charm has been broken. After his brother is restored, he remarks that the man's recovered face is no more handsome than it ought to be.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Shaggy is pleased that Ruggedo has reformed but hopes he will keep his word. He notices the Rainbow approaching and mourns Polychrome's departure, then reassures Betsy that he will never desert her.

Chapter Twenty-Four

Shaggy Man finds his lost brother freed from Ruggedo's enchantment and remains with him, Betsy Bobbin, and Hank rather than returning alone to Oz. Ozma ultimately transports the group to the Land of Oz, where Shaggy Man cries tears of joy.
