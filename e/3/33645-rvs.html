RVS

method · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: RAG-assisted Representative Vector Summarization, RAG-assisted Representative Vector Summarization (RVS), Representative Vector Summarization, representative vector summarization
  • In Retrieval Augmented Generation and Representative Vector Summarization for Large Unstructured Textual Data in Medical Education: RVS is a combined extractive and abstractive summarization method for large unstructured textual data. It selects a predefined number of representative text chunks from a non-parametric knowledgebase, with the number determined by the maximum token limit, and uses representative vectors to generate a final summary. It also identifies chunk keywords and creates word-cloud and scatter-plot visualizations of overall content. Implemented in docGPT, RVS was tested against retrieve-stuff-summarize methods on Kumar and Clark Clinical Medicine 10th Edition and British National Formulary 82, including medical education use cases.

RAG-assisted Representative Vector Summarization selects a predefined number of representative text chunks from a non-parametric knowledgebase and uses a combined abstractive and extractive workflow to generate a final summary.
