the destruction of Pentheus's house

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Earthquake, a great flame, all is overthrown, all that high house, every thing was shivered in pieces, house shook, overthrown, racked in ruin, shaking the house, the earthquake, the house shook, threw down his house to the ground, wreck
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, it was an earthquake in which the Spirit of the Chained Earthquake shook the Castle and contributed to the overthrow of Pentheus's house.
  • In The Bacchae of Euripides, it destroyed the Castle after it was shaken and set against by fire, leaving it overthrown as a sign of Dionysus's bondage and Pentheus's opposition to his god.
  • In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, it was an earthquake summoned by Dionysus's voice that shook the pillars of Pentheus's Castle and accompanied the god's release and the house's overthrow.
  • In Hippolytus; The Bacchae, it was caused by Dionysus after earthquake and fire struck Pentheus's house, leaving it in wreck as punishment for opposing the god.
  • In The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I, it was caused by Bacchus, who shook Pentheus's house and brought it to the ground, shattering everything inside.
