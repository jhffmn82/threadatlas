experiments in SELF-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection

Akari Asai, Zeqiu Wu, Yizhong Wang, Avirup Sil, Hannaneh Hajishirzi · 2023-10-17

Experiments evaluate SELF-RAG against diverse baselines using zero-shot settings across closed-set, short-form, and long-form generation tasks. They measure correctness, factuality, fluency, citation precision and recall, including accuracy and answer inclusion, and use 150k Open-Instruct instruction-output training pairs.
