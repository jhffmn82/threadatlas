the journey from Eyford to the Stark residence in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1891-06-25

A journey from Eyford to Stark’s residence begins after changes at Reading and arrival at Eyford, then continues by one-horse carriage behind a fast chestnut horse for at least an hour over rough roads, ending at an unknown location.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“At Reading I had to change not only my carriage but my station.

After arriving at Eyford, the narrator is taken by a fast, fresh chestnut horse in a carriage with frosted windows. The journey lasts at least an hour over rough roads, although Stark had said the distance was only seven miles, and the narrator reaches the residence without learning its direction or exact location.
