Rose Fairfield in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Rose Fairfield, also called Rose Simon Fairfield, is Tom Fairfield and Susan Lasco’s daughter. During separation from her family, she cares for baby Suza in a cold cabin, is rescued with Roxie and Suza, and becomes Mrs. Evaline Estep’s foster daughter before Estep adopts her after her parents die. Identified through a breast-pin and marriage certificate, she inherits the Lasco estate, then dies childless and intestate of yellow fever returning from New Orleans.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Rose Fairfield cared for the baby, Suza, while she and her sisters were alone in the cold cabin. She was rescued with Roxie and Suza and was assigned to Mrs. Evaline Estep as a foster daughter.

SCENE FOURTH--ROXIE DAYMON AND ROSE SIMON.

Rose Simon Fairfield is identified as the daughter of Susan Lasco and Tom Fairfield through a breast-pin and a marriage certificate. She receives the Lasco inheritance after false testimony that she is the only surviving child, but dies of yellow fever on the way home from New Orleans, intestate and childless.
