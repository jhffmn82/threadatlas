alcoholic drinks in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Alcoholic drinks, substances specially referenced for effects on the human system, can become increasingly desirable with use; habitual takers may value them above everything else despite knowing the drinks are ruining them.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

front matter

Alcoholic drinks are among the substances whose effects upon the human system receive special reference in Child's Health Primer.

CHAPTER VII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Alcoholic drinks can become more desirable to a person as the person drinks more. People who habitually take them may care more for them than for anything else, even while knowing that the drinks are ruining them.
