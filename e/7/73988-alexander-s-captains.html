Alexander's captains in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander's captains were commanders and bodyguards who participated in settling the succession. Under the authority of the king and Perdiccas, they were assigned responsibility for governing provinces and regions, while former lieutenant-governors in Asia retained their governments. Their distribution begins with appointments across Egypt, Asia, and neighboring provinces, including military command for Seleucus, and extends through Persia, Media, Babylon, Mesopotamia, Thrace, Macedonia, and other regions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVIII.

Alexander's commanders and the king's bodyguards participated in the settlement of the succession and were made responsible for governing the provinces under the authority of the king and Perdiccas. They received assignments including Egypt, Syria, Cilicia, Media, Cappadocia, Phrygia, Lydia, Caria, Macedonia, and other regions.
