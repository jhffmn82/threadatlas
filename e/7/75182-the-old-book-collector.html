1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE EMPTY HOUSE

The old book collector appears to be a deformed, elderly man carrying valuable books, including The Origin of Tree Worship. He is later revealed to be Sherlock Holmes in disguise, using the appearance to avoid recognition while he is being hunted.
