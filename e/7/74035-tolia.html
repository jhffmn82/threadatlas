Aetolia in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Aetolia, a country with cities, is invaded and spoiled by Acarnanians while the Aetolians campaign in Thessaly; the returning Aetolians drive them off. Cassander then enters to aid the Acarnanians and leaves Lyciscus with forces to continue support.

While the Aetolians were campaigning in Thessaly, the Acarnanians invaded Aetolia, wasted and spoiled its country, and besieged its cities. The Aetolians returned with their own soldiers and so frightened the Acarnanians that they relieved the country.

And now, having related the things done throughout all Asia, we

Cassander entered Etolia to support the Acarnanians against the Aetolians. He later left Lyciscus in the region with a substantial force to continue assisting the Acarnanians.

Ætolia in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ætolia, inhabited by Ætolians, faced war with the Galati: after Callion’s destruction, its people returned from Thermopylæ to defend remaining towns. Later Augustus depopulated it to populate Nicopolis near Actium.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVIII.

Ætolia was depopulated by Augustus so that its people could be used to populate his city of Nicopolis near Actium. The people of Patræ had earlier joined the Ætolians in their war against the Galati.

CHAPTER XXII.

Ætolia was invaded by a selected Galati force under Orestorius and Combutis. After learning of Callion's destruction, the Ætolians returned there from Thermopylæ and fought to protect the remaining towns.
