In Europe, the Lacedtemoniaus being routed in a great battle by

The Lacedemonians' ambassadors were sent to Antipater after the Lacedemonians were routed. They were put off until the general assembly of Greece met at Corinth, and other ambassadors were later sent into Asia to beg pardon for the late revolt.
