brown mice in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Two brown mice live in the cottage cellar, troubling and damaging it; they enter at night, gobble squash pie, flee, and are to be sent to the forest.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

Two brown mice gnaw through the crack under the cupboard door, enter during the night, and gobble the piece of squash pie. They flee through the cellar-door after trying to climb Bruin’s back, and <DW53> decides to send them to the forest.
