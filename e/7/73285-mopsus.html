Mopsus in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Mopsus, son of Ampycus, is a seer and prophet among the Argonauts, skilled at interpreting signs. He reads a halcyon’s cry as favorable, wakes Jason, and urges propitiation of the Mother of the gods to end a storm. He also interprets the dove and hawk, advising the Argonauts to call on Cytherea, seek the maiden, and follow Argus’s counsel. Despite his prophetic power, fate brings his death from a serpent’s bite; the Argonauts bury and honor him.
