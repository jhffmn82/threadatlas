Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VL

The Argive sedition began when orators and popular leaders stirred the people against the great men of Argos. Suspected conspirators were condemned and killed without proper trial, and the violence eventually consumed the orators themselves before the people returned to peace.
