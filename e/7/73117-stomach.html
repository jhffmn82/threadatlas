stomach in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The stomach is an organ that takes care of food: a strong bag with muscular walls and a lining that prepares gastric juice. After swallowing, it works food over, squeezing, rolling, and mixing it with gastric juice until it becomes a thin, grayish fluid. It prepares this food for the blood to take and sends it toward the intestines. It needs rest and warmth; alcohol, or hurriedly swallowed food, can harm it or cause aching.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER X. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The stomach is a strong bag with muscular walls and a lining that prepares gastric juice. Its muscles squeeze, roll, and mix food until it becomes a thin, grayish fluid. The stomach needs rest and warmth to work well, and alcohol can injure it, cause sores, and sometimes lead to death.

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The stomach works swallowed food over and mixes it with gastric juice until it becomes a gray fluid. It then sends the prepared food toward the intestines; hurriedly swallowed food may give the stomach extra work and cause aching.

CHAPTER XVI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The stomach prepares food for the blood to take.
