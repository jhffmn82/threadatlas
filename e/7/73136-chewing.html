chewing in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Chewing mixes saliva with food, changes starch into sugar, should be slow and thorough, and precedes swallowing.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Chewing allows saliva to mix with food and change its starch into sugar. Food should be chewed slowly and well; otherwise, the stomach has extra work.
