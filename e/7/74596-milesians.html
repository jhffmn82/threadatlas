Milesians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Milesians, descended from Miletus, hated the Branchidae for ancestral betrayal; in Alexander’s army, Alexander left revenge or mercy to them, but they could not agree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. I.

The Milesians in Alexander’s army bore an inveterate hatred toward the Branchidae because their ancestors had been betrayed by them. Alexander called the Milesians together and left the question of revenge or mercy to them, though they could not agree.

Milesians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Asian people who joined Samians against Persians at Mycale, helping revive Greek courage and rout Persia, then fought Samians over Priene with Athenian support.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IV.

The Milesians joined the Samians in advancing against the Persians at Mycale. Their appearance helped revive the courage of the Greeks and contributed to the Persian rout.

CHAP. V.

The Milesians were involved in a war with the Samians concerning Priene. The Athenians favored them in that conflict.
