Alexander IV in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander IV is Alexander’s son and a young king, brought back into Macedonia with Olympias by Polyperchon and Aeacidas. Cassander later imprisoned him and his mother Roxana at Amphipolis, removed his companions, and ordered that he no longer be treated as a king but as a private person.
