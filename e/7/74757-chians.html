Chians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Chians joined Rhodians, Byzantines, Coians, and Mausolus against Athenians, defected, defended Chios during siege, helped raise Samos’s siege, prepared to relieve Byzantium, then made peace.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. 11.

The Chians defected from the Athenians and were supported by the Byzantines, Rhodians, Coians, and Mausolus. They defended Chios against the Athenian siege.

CHAP. V.

The Chians joined the Rhodians and Byzantines against the Athenians. They helped raise the siege of Samos, prepared to relieve Byzantium, and later agreed to peace with the Athenians.
