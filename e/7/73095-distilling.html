distilling in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Distilling is a tea-kettle process in the kitchen that boils liquid into vapor, then condenses it back into liquid, producing distilled water or stronger alcohol.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

] CHAPTER VI. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Distilling is the process of turning a liquid into vapor by boiling it and then turning the vapor back into liquid. Water can be distilled, and liquids containing alcohol can be distilled to make the alcohol stronger.
