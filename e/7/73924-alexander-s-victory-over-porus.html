Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Alexander’s victory causes Indian flight and heavy losses; he takes prisoners and elephants, sacrifices to the sun, and restores wounded Porus’s kingdom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. X.

Alexander's victory caused the remaining Indians to flee and resulted in a great slaughter. Alexander took prisoners and elephants, sacrificed to the sun for the victory, and restored the wounded Porus to his kingdom after he recovered.
