Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIV.

Seven thousand mercenary Indians entered Massaga to assist its defenders and fought fiercely against Alexander's army. After the governor was killed and many of their men were lost or wounded, they agreed to join Alexander, but planned to flee rather than fight other Indians. Alexander surrounded them at night and killed every one of them.
