the Hip-po-gy-raf in The Tin Woodman of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1918

Hip-po-gy-raf is a large leathery-skinned beast with a surprisingly long elastic neck, preferring straw to hay. In Chapter Nineteen, it smells and eats the Scarecrow's stuffing, then agrees to carry invisible travelers across a ditch it cannot jump.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

The Hip-po-gy-raf smells straw and wants to eat the Scarecrow's stuffing, but it cannot find him while the travelers are invisible. It agrees to carry the travelers across the ditch after eating the Scarecrow's straw, since its heavy body cannot jump over the ditch.
