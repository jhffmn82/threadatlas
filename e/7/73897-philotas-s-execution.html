Philotas's execution in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Philotas's execution was a Macedonian execution following judgment: after torture produced his confession, Philotas and other condemned conspirators, including Alexander Lyncestes, were put to death according to custom.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VIII.

The Macedonians examined the conspiracy and condemned Philotas and the other conspirators to death. Philotas confessed under torture, and he and the others were executed according to the custom of their country; Alexander Lyncestes was executed at the same time.
