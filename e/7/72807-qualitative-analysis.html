IV. RESULTS / A. Implementation .. C. Qualitative Analysis

The qualitative analysis compared baseline Mistral outputs with Mistral+MoE+RAG outputs on complex reasoning and information retrieval tasks. It found that the enhanced model generated responses that were more detailed, contextually rich, informative, and relevant.
