THE ADVENTURE OF THE DANCING MEN

The unknown dancing-men sender leaves messages secretly at night and is seen by Hilton Cubitt as a dark figure moving near the tool-house. The sender escapes before Cubitt can catch him, while Elsie Patrick appears to know who he is.
