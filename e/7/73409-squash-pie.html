squash pie in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

Squash pie is lovely and especially fine; after a remaining piece is left at dinner, two brown mice find it in the cupboard and eat it completely, leaving the plate white and empty.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER III.

Squeak and Scrabble find a large piece of squash pie in the cupboard and eat it completely. They leave the plate white and empty.

CHAPTER IV.

<DW53> describes the squash pie as especially fine and regrets that he did not eat the remaining piece at dinner. Two brown mice enter the cupboard and eat it while he is away.
