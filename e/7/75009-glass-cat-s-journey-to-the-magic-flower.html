Glass Cat's journey to the Magic Flower in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The Glass Cat's journey to the Magic Flower is a planned week-long trip with Trot and Cap'n Bill, guided by the Glass Cat to a hidden island by a river, reached by walking along its bottom because it cannot swim.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

5. A Happy Corner of Oz

The Glass Cat's journey to the Magic Flower is planned to begin with Trot and Cap'n Bill. The Glass Cat says the trip will take about a week and will lead to the hidden island by the river; it previously reached the island by walking along the river bottom because it cannot swim.
