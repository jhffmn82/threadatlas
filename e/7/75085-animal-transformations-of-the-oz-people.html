animal transformations of the Oz people in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Ruggedo plans, after conquering Oz, to transform all its people into various beasts and send them to forests and jungles. In the proposed exchange, beasts become men and women with comforts, while the transformed Oz people must hunt and fight for food.

3. Two Bad Ones

Ruggedo plans to transform all the people of Oz into various beasts after conquering the country. He intends to send them to live in forests and jungles.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

The proposed transformation would turn the beasts into men and women and the Oz people into beasts. The beasts would gain houses, food, clothing, and other comforts, while the Oz people would have to hunt and fight for food in the forest.
