Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian / BOOK XV.

Gaos, the Persian admiral's vice-admiral, helped quell a mutiny caused by shortages in the Persian camp. He later defeated Evagoras's fleet after a sharp engagement and then entered into a league with Acoris and sought support from the Lacedaemonians against Artaxerxes.
