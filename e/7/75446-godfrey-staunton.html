Godfrey Staunton in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Godfrey Staunton is an orphaned, healthy international rugby player: Cambridge’s leading three-quarter, also playing for Blackheath, with five Internationals. Nephew and heir of Lord Mount-James, he depends on concealing his secret marriage. Pale and troubled, he leaves Bentley’s private hotel with a bearded stranger, abandons his belongings, sends an urgent telegram to his intimate friend Dr. Leslie Armstrong, and disappears before the Cambridge–Oxford match. He is later found at a lonely cottage, kneeling beside his dead wife and overwhelmed by grief. His marriage to his former London landlady’s daughter had prevented him from withdrawing from the match without revealing it.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE MISSING THREE-QUARTER

Godfrey Staunton is Cambridge’s leading three-quarter, a player for Cambridge, Blackheath, and England. He appears pale and troubled before leaving Bentley’s private hotel with a bearded stranger, leaving his belongings behind and missing the next day’s match.

It was already dark when we reached the old university city.

Godfrey Staunton is an intimate friend of Dr. Leslie Armstrong who has disappeared after leaving his hotel. He paid Dr. Leslie Armstrong thirteen guineas the previous month and sent Armstrong an urgent telegram from London at six-fifteen the previous evening.

And yet the next day brought us no nearer to the solution of the

Staunton is found at the lonely cottage kneeling beside his dead wife and overwhelmed by grief. He had secretly married his former London landlady’s daughter and had been unable to withdraw from the Cambridge–Oxford match without exposing the marriage.
