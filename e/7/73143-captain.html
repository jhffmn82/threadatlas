captain in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

A captain observes rum weakening sailors, then leads a voyage without it, providing plentiful food and hot coffee during storms.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

A captain watched sailors drinking rum and saw that they became no stronger and later felt weaker. He then took a ship to sea without rum, provided plentiful food, and gave the sailors hot coffee during storms.
