Argonauts’ return voyage in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Argonauts’ return voyage is the perilous sea journey made by Jason and the heroes after escaping pursuit, carrying Jason from Aea toward Iolcus, Hellas, Achaea, and the land of Pelops with the golden fleece. Although the fleece quest began at Pelias’s command and was intended to prevent Jason’s return home, the return is sustained by the heroes’ hope, Jason’s prayer to Apollo, and Idmon’s prophecy that they will return with the fleece.
