six giant soldiers in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Six giant soldiers, enchanted former monkeys, are made fifty-foot-tall men by Kiki Aru. Trapped among forest trees, they cannot move, raise their arms, or draw their sharp swords. They wear caps with red and yellow plumes until the Wizard of Oz breaks the enchantment and restores their natural monkey forms.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

16. The Glass Cat Finds the Black Bag

The six soldiers are transformed from monkeys by Kiki Aru into fifty-foot-tall men. Their great size traps them among the trees, where they cannot move, raise their arms, or draw their swords; the Wizard of Oz later breaks the enchantment and restores them to their monkey forms.
