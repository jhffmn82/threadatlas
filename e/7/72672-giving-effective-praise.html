Giving Effective Praise is a Social Emotional Learning Principles topic that emphasizes praising students’ learning effort. It unfolds by promoting process-focused praise for perseverance and effort, while contrasting this with undesirable praise for being smart, correct answers, or good grades.

Giving Effective Praise emphasizes praising student learning effort rather than outcomes. The text presents praise focused on perseverance as desirable and praise focused on being smart or getting the answer correct as undesirable.

Appendix B. Social-Emotional Learning Principles

Giving Effective Praise involves praising students for putting forth effort through process-focused praise rather than praising them for getting an answer correct or receiving a good grade.
