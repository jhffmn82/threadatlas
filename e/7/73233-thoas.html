Thoas in The Argonautica

Apollonius Rhodius · -0299/-0200

Thoas, king of the Lemnian people before the massacre and father of Hypsipyle, is spared by her, escapes in a hollow chest to Oenoe, and fathers Sicinus with Oenoe.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Here the whole of the men of the people together had been ruthlessly

Thoas was king over the Lemnian people and was spared by Hypsipyle. She sent him away in a hollow chest, and fishermen brought him to Oenoe, an island later called Sicinus after Sicinus, whom Oenoe bore to Thoas.
