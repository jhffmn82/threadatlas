robbers in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

A heavily armed, forest-dwelling band of robbers stops a princess, questions her and her bag, mistakes her mangle remark for a countersign, takes her to its chief, and gives her ten thousand sapphires for secrecy.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER XI.

The robbers stop the princess in their forest and ask what she is doing there and what is in her bag. When she asks whether their grandmother has sold her mangle, they mistake the phrase for a countersign, lead her to their chief, and give her ten thousand sapphires so that she will not betray them.
