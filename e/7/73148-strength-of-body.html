strength of body in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

Strength of body is measured by raising a weight over a pulley and is gained through open-air exercise, pure air, and good, simple food.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

Strength of body can be measured by pulling a weight over a pulley and marking how high it is raised. It can be gained through exercise in the open air, pure air, and good, simple food.
