Corporal Waddle in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Corporal Waddle is a small brown stuffed bear and sentry guarding Bear Center during the Bear King’s absence. He carries a tin-barreled cork gun, captures Cayke and the Frogman for trespassing and slandering bears, and summons other bears by firing it.

Corporal Waddle is a small brown stuffed bear who guards the way to Bear Center. He captures Cayke and the Frogman, accuses them of trespassing and slandering the bears, and summons the other bears by firing his cork gun.
