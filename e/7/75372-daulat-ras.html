Daulat Ras in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Daulat Ras is an Indian student living on the second-floor college staircase near Hilton Soames. Quiet, inscrutable, steady, and methodical, he is strong academically but weak in Greek and prepares for an examination the next day. Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

Daulat Ras is an Indian student living on the same stair as Hilton Soames and had visited Soames to ask about the examination before the proofs were disturbed. He is described as quiet, inscrutable, steady, and methodical, with Greek as his weak subject. Holmes visits his room and sees him pacing restlessly behind his blind.

When we were out in the darkness of the quadrangle, we again

The Indian student is observed pacing his room while the others are invisible. Watson thinks his behavior and his queer look may be suspicious, but Holmes explains that he could have been trying to learn something by heart.
