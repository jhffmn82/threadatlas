Charlington Heath in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Charlington Heath is an open, gorse-covered heath beside the lonely road between Charlington Hall and Farnham Station. Violet Smith travels this road; Holmes directs Watson to hide among its gorse, where he watches the Hall gateway and observes her pursuer.

Charlington Hall in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Charlington Hall is an old, gloomy, decaying country house in its own grounds beside the road between Charlington Heath and Charlington Hall, bordered by woods, a yew hedge, magnificent trees, a lichen-covered gateway, and Tudor chimneys. Tenanted by Williamson, it has servants and regular week-end visitors, including Mr. Woodley; Holmes investigates its occupant after tracing a pursuer’s likely footpath toward it.
