UAR-Criteria in Unified Active Retrieval for Retrieval Augmented Generation

Qinyuan Cheng · 2024

UAR-Criteria is a unified active retrieval decision procedure and multifaceted decision tree for processing diverse retrieval scenarios uniformly. Its central feature is a priority-ordered sequence of criteria: it first checks intent-aware retrieval intent; absent explicit intent, it checks knowledge intensity, then time sensitivity, then whether the model knows the relevant information. It activates retrieval for explicitly retrieval-oriented instructions, time-sensitive knowledge-intensive tasks, or unfamiliar knowledge-intensive tasks, and cancels retrieval for non-knowledge-intensive or model-known instructions.
