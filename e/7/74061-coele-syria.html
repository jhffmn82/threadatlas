Coele-Syria in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Coele-Syria, a region near Egypt, was sought by Ptolemy with Phoenicia for its convenient position. Nicanor brought Syria and Phoenician cities under Ptolemy's power. After Ptolemy defeated Demetrius near Gaza, he remained there, then withdrew to Egypt as Antigonus approached.
