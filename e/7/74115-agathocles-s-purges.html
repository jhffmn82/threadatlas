Agathocles's purges in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Agathocles's purges were a campaign against Syracusan and neighboring opponents. He accused the wealthy Syracusan senate of plotting, seized leading men, and ordered killings and plunder. Using an attempted betrayal as pretext, he executed about 600 opponents from Taurominium and Messana and killed Centuripian leaders, leaving Messanians fearful and regretful.
