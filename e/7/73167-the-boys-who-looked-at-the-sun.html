A group of boys foolishly compete to look longest at the sun; all suffer brain and eye harm, and the boy who looks longest dies.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The boys foolishly tried to see which could look straight at the sun the longest. Their eyes and brains were greatly harmed, and the boy who looked longest died as a result.
