the patched-Dunlop bicycle in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

The patched-Dunlop bicycle, James Wilder’s bicycle, is left leaning against the Fighting Cock. Holmes finds its patched Dunlop tire, including a patch on the outer cover. Its back wheel leaves tracks emerging from Ragged Shaw and heading away from Priory School; Holmes considers a connection with the boy’s flight, but the tracks do not show the rider’s destination.
