the Athenian assembly

group · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: Athenian assembly, a common assembly, a general assembly, assembly, public assembly, the people
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, the Athenian assembly removed the existing magistrates, installed men who favored democracy, and condemned supporters of the oligarchy; it later condemned Phocion and the other accused men to death.
  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Athenian assembly condemned the Athenian commanders to death after allowing accusations over their failure to bury the dead to prevail.

the Athenian assembly in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

condemns Phocion [to death] The Athenian assembly met after Alexander’s actions and removed the existing magistrates, installed men who favored democracy, and condemned supporters of the oligarchy. It later condemned Phocion and the other accused men to death, although the crowd repeatedly prevented Phocion from presenting an effective defense.

CHAP. XIV.

The Athenian assembly initially applauded the commanders for the victory but became angry over the failure to bury the dead. It allowed the accusations to prevail, prevented the accused from being heard amid a tumult, and condemned the commanders to death.
