Li-Mon-Eags in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Li-Mon-Eags are Ruggedo and Kiki Aru’s invented mixed-beast identities: Sky Island magicians who claim friendship with beasts, then deceptively warn them about the people of Oz.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Li-Mon-Eags is a name Ruggedo invents for himself and Kiki Aru. They claim to be powerful magicians from Sky Island who are friendly to the beasts, although their warning about the people of Oz is presented deceptively.
