Strophades

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Floating Islands, those isles
  • In The Aeneid, Strophades are Ionian Sea islands where Aeneas lands, meals are ruined, and Celaeno foretells famine.
  • In The Argonautica, the Floating Islands are where Zetes and Calais nearly overtake the Harpies; after Iris’s intervention, the heroes turn back from them.

Strophades in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Ionian Sea islands, home of the Harpies, where Aeneas lands, meals are ruined, and Celaeno foretells famine.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK III

The Strophades are islands in the Ionian Sea and the home of the Harpies. Aeneas lands there, but the Harpies repeatedly ruin the Trojans’ meals and Celaeno delivers her prophecy of famine.
