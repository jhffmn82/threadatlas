Recall

metric · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Recall (ac), Recall (gc), recall
  • In "Achieving Higher Factual Accuracy in Llama LLM with Weighted Distribution of Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Recall measures retrieval completeness as the proportion of relevant instances successfully retrieved; weighted RAG scored 85.7% versus 78.9% for baseline Llama Large.
  • In "SafeRAG: Benchmarking Security in Retrieval-Augmented Generation," Recall measures retrieval of golden contexts and attack contexts through Recall (gc) and Recall (ac), and is used separately for each in the RA formula.
  • In "Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation," Recall measures retrieval quality between retrieved chunks and referenced facts; it is reported for both generated answers and retrieved chunks, with KG2RAG reaching response recall of 0.683 and retrieval recall of 0.908 on Hotpot-Dist.
  • In "MEGA-RAG: a retrieval-augmented generation framework with multi-evidence guided answer refinement for mitigating hallucinations of LLMs in public health," Recall is an evaluation metric used in the comparison; MEGA-RAG maintains a recall rate comparable to human annotators and generally outperforms other methods, especially in recall.
