Beppo in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Beppo is an Italian former sculptor, workman, and known criminal who worked for Gelder & Co. and formerly for Morse Hudson. Though considered a good worker and fluent in English, he earlier knifed another Italian at Gelder & Co., was arrested, and received a one-year sentence. Before that arrest, he hid the famous black pearl of the Borgias inside one of six wet plaster Napoleon busts. After imprisonment, he traces the dispersed busts across London and destroys them to recover the pearl. Holmes seeks him as the bust-breaker, and Beppo is captured at Laburnum Villa while smashing Josiah Brown’s bust, carrying a bloodstained long sheath knife.

Sherlock Holmes and I walked together to the High Street, where

Beppo worked for Morse Hudson and had also worked at Gelder & Co., where he was considered a good worker. More than a year earlier he knifed another Italian, received a one-year sentence, and was later sought by Holmes as the person destroying the busts. He is caught breaking Josiah Brown’s bust at Laburnum Villa and is arrested with a long sheath knife bearing recent bloodstains.
