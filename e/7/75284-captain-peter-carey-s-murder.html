Captain Peter Carey's murder in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Captain Peter Carey is murdered in his cabin about two o'clock, fully dressed and apparently expecting someone; a rack harpoon is driven through his chest into the wall, with no footmarks found.

Narrative, section by section

THE ADVENTURE OF BLACK PETER

Captain Peter Carey was murdered in his cabin at about two o'clock in the morning. He was fully dressed, apparently expecting someone, and was killed with a harpoon taken from a rack; no footprints were found inside or outside.

Peter Carey's killing in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Captain Peter Carey is killed in his hut, pinned to the wall with his own harpoon. Inspector Stanley Hopkins initially attributes the killing to John Hopley Neligan after a bloodstained notebook bearing Neligan’s initials is found. Holmes finds Neligan’s involvement impossible because of the strength required; Patrick Cairns admits killing Carey in self-defense after Carey attacked him.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The murder of Captain Peter Carey occurred in his hut, where he was pinned against the wall with one of his own harpoons. The unit does not establish who killed him, though John Hopley Neligan is arrested after a bloodstained notebook bearing his initials is found.

Peter Carey’s killing was initially attributed by Inspector Stanley Hopkins to young Neligan, but Holmes shows that this is intrinsically impossible because of the strength required to use the harpoon. Patrick Cairns admits killing Carey after Carey attacked him, describing the act as self-defense rather than murder.
