the key in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Bannister’s key, left in Hilton Soames’s outer door after he asked about tea, kept the unlocked door open, enabling someone to enter and leave.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

The key belonged to Bannister, who left it in Hilton Soames’s outer door after entering to ask whether tea was wanted. Because the outer door had no spring lock, the open door allowed someone to enter and leave the room.
