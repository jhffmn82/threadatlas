the Rolling Prairie in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Rolling Prairie is a sparsely inhabited landscape beyond the Winkie River, with uphill and downhill terrain, leading toward the Merry-Go-Round Mountains; the search party finds no news of Ozma and shelters with a lonely shepherd.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

The Rolling Prairie lies beyond the Winkie River and has few inhabitants. The search party finds no one there who has seen or heard of Ozma, and by nightfall it must seek shelter with a lonely shepherd.
