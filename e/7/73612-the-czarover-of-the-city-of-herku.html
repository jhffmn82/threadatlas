Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

Vig rules the City of Herku and is described as a thin but immensely strong monarch who can crumble solid marble and throw a giant through a window. He gives the Wizard zosozo, explains the condition of Herku, and warns the travelers that Ugu the Shoemaker may be dangerous.
