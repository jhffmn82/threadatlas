departure for the forest in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

In spring, the woodchuck, squirrel, wood-pigeon, raccoon, and Toto leave the cottage for the forest, looking back and waving; Bruin stays behind, intending to remain with Toto and the blind grandmother.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XV.

On a soft, bright April day, the woodchuck, squirrel, wood-pigeon, raccoon, and Toto prepare to leave the cottage for the forest. The animals depart with backward glances and waves, but Bruin does not go with them because he secretly intends to remain with Toto and the blind grandmother.
