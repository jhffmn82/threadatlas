Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XXII.

Phintias, tyrant of Agrigentum, defeated Hicetas's forces before later suffering hostility from the cities under his rule. He built Phintia, settled the displaced Gelans there, and was represented in a dream as being killed by a boar.
