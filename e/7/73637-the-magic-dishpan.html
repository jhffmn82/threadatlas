Cayke's dishpan in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

Cayke's magical dishpan, made of gold and covered with diamonds, is her lost treasure. Cayke and the Frogman travel from the Yip Country to recover it after Ugu steals it. The Scarecrow and Tin Woodman carry it to the Emerald City, where Ozma returns it to Cayke.

the Magic Dishpan in The Lost Princess of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1917

The Magic Dishpan is Cayke the Cookie Cook’s greatest treasure: a large, round beaten-gold dishpan, studded with three rows of diamonds. A family heirloom inherited through her mother and grandmothers since the beginning of time, it is cherished by Cayke and is believed to make whoever owns it a good cook. After it is stolen, Cayke’s cookies burn, become too tough, or are unusually poor. Mysteriously taken from Cayke’s house in the Yip Country on the same night Ozma disappears, the dishpan’s loss drives Cayke to leave the tableland and search the lower world with the Frogman. She suspects the Lavender Bear or bears of Bear Center, but the Bear King’s magic image and the Little Pink Bear reveal that Ugu the Shoemaker possesses it. The dishpan is also a magic instrument: it can grow large enough for a man to sit in, and someone grasping both handles can be transported anywhere within the borders of the Land of Oz. Ugu uses it to travel instantly to Quadling Country and to plunder Glinda the Good, the Wizard of Oz, and Ozma of Oz, carrying them or their possessions to the Wicker Castle. Cayke later finds it in Ugu’s golden cage but cannot retrieve it. It remains missing when the party reaches the Emerald City, where Ozma promises it will be restored; Cayke says she cannot be fully happy without it.

The diamond-studded gold dishpan is Cayke the Cookie Cook’s greatest treasure and a family heirloom from the Yip Country. It is believed to have magic powers that make its owner a good cook; after it was stolen, Cayke’s cookies burned, became too tough, or were unusually poor.

The diamond-studded dishpan was mysteriously stolen on the same night that Ozma disappeared from the Emerald City. The Frogman and Cayke continue traveling in search of it.

CHAPTER 14

The diamond-studded gold dishpan was mysteriously stolen from Cayke's house. Cayke believes that only a powerful fairy, magician, or similar person could have taken it from the Yip Country.

CHAPTER 15

Cayke’s jeweled dishpan is still missing in this unit. Cayke suspects that the Lavender Bear or one of the bears of Bear Center may have stolen it, but its location is not discovered.

CHAPTER 16

Cayke’s dishpan is a large, round beaten-gold pan decorated with three rows of diamonds. The Bear King’s magic creates an image of it, and the Little Pink Bear confirms that Ugu the Shoemaker has the actual dishpan.

CHAPTER 17

Cayke's dishpan has been stolen by Ugu the Shoemaker. It is made of gold, studded with diamonds, and has magic powers.

CHAPTER 19

The Magic Dishpan is a gold, diamond-studded dishpan that can grow large enough for a man to sit in and can transport him to any place within the Land of Oz when he grasps its handles. Ugu steals it from Cayke the Cookie Cook and uses it to plunder Glinda the Good, the Wizard of Oz, and Ozma of Oz and to carry them or their possessions to the Wicker Castle.

CHAPTER 22

Cayke's long-sought diamond-studded dishpan lies inside Ugu's golden cage. The cage keeps it from falling when Ugu turns the room upside down, and Cayke remains unable to retrieve it.

CHAPTER 23

Ugu uses the golden jeweled dishpan to transport himself and his dove form instantly to the northeast corner of Quadling Country. The Magic Picture later shows it lying beneath the tree where the dove is perched.

CHAPTER 25

Cayke’s jeweled dishpan is still missing when the party reaches the Emerald City. Ozma promises to see that it is restored, and Cayke repeatedly says that she cannot be fully happy without it.
