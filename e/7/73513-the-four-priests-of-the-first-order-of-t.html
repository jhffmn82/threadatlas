the four priests of the First Order of the Saki-Pan in Toto's Merry Winter

Laura E. Richards · 1887

The four priests of the First Order of the Saki-Pan guard the Golden Dragon, the Jewelled Shrine, and the Temple of the Saki-Pan. They drink wine offered by Chop-Chin and sleep while the idol is removed. Awakening to its disappearance, they fear death, then joyfully return the recovered Dragon to the Temple.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" And without further preface, she began as follows:-- CHAPTER II.

The priests of the Saki-Pan guard the Golden Dragon and do not allow ordinary people to look upon it. They drink the wine offered by Chop-Chin, fall asleep, and remain unconscious while he removes the idol.

Ten o'clock of a dark night.

The four priests awaken and discover that the Golden Dragon has disappeared from its altar. They fear death because they slept, but joyfully carry the Dragon back to the Temple of the Saki-Pan after it is found outside.
