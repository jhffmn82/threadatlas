Orophernes in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Orophernes, ruler of Cappadocia and brother of Ariarathes, deposes Ariarathes and rules through killings, payments, robbery, spoliation, and confiscation. He deposits four hundred talents with the Pirenians; as his affairs decline, he robs Jupiter’s temple to pay mercenary soldiers.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

77* After the Romans had conquered Perseus, (orratherCbaropea),

Orophernes deposed his brother Ariarathes and pursued money rather than governing prudently or winning the goodwill of his people. He put many to death, paid large sums to Timotheus and Demetrius, robbed and confiscated the goods of the Cappadocian nobility, and deposited four hundred talents with the Pirenians. When his affairs declined, he robbed Jupiter's temple to pay his mercenary soldiers.
