Theban exiles in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Theban exiles secretly return to Thebes at night with Athenian help, kill Lacedaemonian supporters, stir the people to defend liberty, and help begin the Cadmea's siege.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAR III.

The Theban exiles consulted together and, with Athenian help, returned secretly to Thebes at night. They killed supporters of the Lacedaemonians, stirred up the people to defend their liberty, and helped initiate the siege of the Cadmea.
