counter-misinformation response generation in Evidence-Driven Retrieval Augmented Response Generation for Online Misinformation

Zhenrui Yue, Huimin Zeng, Yimeng Lu, Lanyu Shang, Yang Zhang, and Dong Wang · 2024

Counter-misinformation response generation is a language generation method for early, scalable intervention against online misinformation. Given a misleading claim, it retrieves relevant scientific evidence and generates a polite, factual evidence-based response. RARG frames this as evidence retrieval plus retrieval-augmented response generation, while noting risks from domain shift, unreliable evidence sources, and missing human evaluation.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Introduction

Counter-response generation is proposed to facilitate early intervention against misinformation at scale. Existing generation methods are often trained end-to-end on specific domains, which can reduce quality after domain shifts and leave them dependent on retraining for updated knowledge.

Methodology / Preliminary .. Response Generation

It is formulated as a two-part task: retrieving relevant scientific evidence for a misleading claim and generating a response that demonstrates desirable properties such as politeness and factuality.

Limitations

The unit presents RARG as a method for evidence-based counter-response generation against online misinformation. It identifies domain shift, unreliable evidence sources, and the absence of human evaluation as limitations for this task.
