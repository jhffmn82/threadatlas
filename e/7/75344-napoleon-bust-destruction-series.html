Napoleon bust destruction series in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

A chain of incidents involving identical plaster busts of Napoleon begins with a bust destroyed at Morse Hudson's shop, continues through burglaries at Dr. Barnicot's residences, and reaches Horace Harker's house, where a bust's removal coincides with an unidentified man's murder.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SIX NAPOLEONS

The series begins with a bust destroyed at Morse Hudson's shop and continues with the two identical busts belonging to Dr. Barnicot. It later reaches Mr. Horace Harker's house, where the bust's removal coincides with the murder of an unidentified man.
