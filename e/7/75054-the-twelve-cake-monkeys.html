the twelve cake monkeys in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

The twelve cake monkeys are small, long-tailed monkeys kept and carried in a cage. After the Glass Cat teases them by pulling their tails, and the Hungry Tiger bends two bars, they escape briefly to surround her and smear her with blue mud before returning to the cage. At Ozma's birthday party, they leap from a Surprise Cake, bow, dance, perform acrobatics, and play golden instruments.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

20. The Monkeys Have Trouble

The monkeys are carried in their cage during the journey and are tormented by the Glass Cat pulling their tails. After the Hungry Tiger bends two cage bars, they use the opening to surround the Glass Cat, smear her with blue mud, and return to their cage.

22. Ozma's Birthday Party

The twelve cake monkeys leap from the Surprise Cake and bow to Ozma while congratulating her. They dance, perform acrobatic feats, and play a jolly tune on golden band instruments before the banquet ends.
