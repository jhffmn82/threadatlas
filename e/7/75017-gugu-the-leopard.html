Gugu the Leopard in The Magic of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1919

Gugu the Leopard is an enormous yellow leopard and King of Gugu Forest, also called the Forest of Gugu. Advised by three King's Counselors, he leads the Council of Beasts, maintains laws and order, punishes beasts that start fights with the Counselors, and rules wisely, justly, and well. When Ruggedo claims that the people of Oz mean to enslave the beasts, Gugu initially doubts him. After Kiki Aru transforms Loo into a man, Gugu recognizes the strangers' magic, summons the beasts, presents the claimed danger, and urges careful deliberation over whether to fight. He welcomes the Cowardly Lion and companions as guests, but when he attacks Kiki, Kiki transforms him into a fat Gillikin woman. Indignant at this imposed form, Gugu is to be taken to Glinda the Good for restoration.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

7. The Forest of Gugu

Gugu is an enormous yellow leopard who rules Gugu Forest as its King. He is advised by three King's Counselors and, with them, maintains the forest's laws and order.

8. The Li-Mon-Eags Make Trouble

Gugu presides over the Council of Beasts and initially doubts Ruggedo's claim that the people of Oz intend to enslave the beasts. After Kiki Aru transforms Loo into a man, Gugu accepts the strangers' magical power and orders the forest beasts to assemble at sunrise to decide whether to fight the people of Oz.

11. The Beasts of the Forest of Gugu

Gugu the Leopard, King of the Forest, presides over the gathering and introduces the strangers and the danger they claim to have discovered. He asks the beasts to decide whether to accept Ruggedo's plan, then advises them to think carefully before answering.

12. Kiki Uses His Magic

Gugu identifies himself as King of the forest and welcomes the Cowardly Lion and his companions as guests. When he tries to attack Kiki, Kiki transforms him into a fat Gillikin woman.

13. The Loss of the Black Bag

Gugu the King is transformed into a fat Gillikin woman and is indignant about his appearance. The group plans to take him to Glinda the Good so she can restore his natural shape.
