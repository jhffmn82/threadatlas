Tricala in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Tricala is Tryphon’s royal seat, a strongly fortified high rock with springs, vineyards, olive plantations, and rich land. Tryphon strengthened it, built institutions, and centered rebellion there; Roman forces besieged it but withdrew after losses.

Tricala was a strongly fortified place that Tryphon developed into the royal seat of the rebels. He strengthened its defenses, built a palace and marketplace, established a council and courts, and made it the center of the rebellion; Roman forces later besieged it but withdrew after suffering losses.
