Miles McLaren in The Return of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1903-09-26

Miles McLaren is a top-floor student, brilliant when he works but wayward and unprincipled. After a prior card scandal and poor examination preparation, he refuses Holmes entry and abusively appears suspicious to Soames.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE STUDENTS

Miles McLaren occupies the top student room. He is described as brilliant but wayward, dissipated, and unprincipled, with a prior card scandal and poor preparation for the examination. He refuses to open his door to Holmes and responds with abusive language, behavior that Soames considers suspicious.
