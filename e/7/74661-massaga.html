Massaga in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Massaga, the country’s greatest strong city, was defended by seven thousand mercenary Indians until its defenders were killed or withdrew; Alexander then captured it by storm and took the mother and daughter of Assacenus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

SUP. XIV.

Massaga was the greatest city of the country and was defended by mercenary Indians. After the defenders were killed or withdrawn, Alexander captured the city by storm and took the mother and daughter of Assacenus.
