the eyes in Child's Health Primer for Primary Classes

Jane Andrews · 1885

The eyes are organs of sight: with a pupil and nerve of sight, they take pictures all day, sending accounts to the brain. Their delicate nerves need care and protection from bright or insufficient light and careless actions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII. .. REVIEW QUESTIONS.

The eyes take pictures of things and send accounts of what is seen to the brain. They need protection from very bright light, insufficient light, and careless actions.
