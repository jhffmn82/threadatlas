CHAP. V-

Artaxerxes's army was assembled from many regions and was estimated by Ephorus at no fewer than four hundred thousand horse and foot. It initially suffered defeat against the Grecians, but after Cyrus was killed it recovered, plundered Cyrus's camp, and defeated his main forces.

The Persian army defeated Cyrus's forces, killing more than fifteen thousand on the king's side and pursuing the Greeks after their retreat. Under Tissaphernes, it attacked stragglers and pursued the Greeks as far as the country of the Carduchi, but did not dare confront their entire army.
