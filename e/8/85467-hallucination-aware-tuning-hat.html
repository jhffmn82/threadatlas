Hallucination Aware Tuning is a novel approach that detects hallucinations, uses descriptions and corrections to build a preference dataset, and trains with Direct Preference Optimization to reduce them.

1 section; each heading opens the text

Abstract

Hallucination Aware Tuning is introduced to reduce hallucinations in large language models. It uses hallucination detection results and corrected outputs to create a preference dataset for Direct Preference Optimization training.
