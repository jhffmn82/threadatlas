Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. V.

Daphnis was said to have been begotten by Mercury upon a nymph in Sicily's Berei Mountains. Raised by nymphs and owner of many herds, he became a skilled shepherd, composer, and inventor of bucolic songs; he also hunted with Diana. After lying with a king's daughter despite a nymph's warning, he lost his sight.
