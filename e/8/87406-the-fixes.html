the Fixes in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

The Fixes are inhabitants of Fix City with large round heads and unusually long flat feet. Alike in simple gendered clothing, they stand in chalked circles while furniture moves, then sit to eat and stare at Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion as unusual runabouts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 12

The Fixes stand in chalked circles and sit down to eat when their moving tables and chairs arrive. They resemble one another, wear simple clothing, and stare at Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion as unusual runabouts.
