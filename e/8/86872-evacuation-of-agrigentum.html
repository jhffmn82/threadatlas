evacuation of Agrigentum in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Evacuation of Agrigentum began when insufficient provisions prompted departure; guarded refugees reached Gela, then Leontium, while the sick and elderly were abandoned and some inhabitants killed themselves.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

As wc were not willing to let these things pass altogether, so we shall

The Agrigentines decided to leave after discovering that the city lacked sufficient provisions. Many men, women, and children reached Gela under military guard, while the sick, elderly, and others unable to leave were abandoned; some inhabitants killed themselves rather than depart.
