Readability control is addressed through the framework's reinforcement learning strategy, which improves the accessibility of biomedical scientific content for lay readers.

Methodology .. 2.3

Readability is optimized in the methodology using relevant background information and reinforcement learning. RLRC uses a Gaussian-based reward intended to control variations in the readability of generated summaries.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.5

Readability is evaluated with FKGL, DCRS, and LENS. RAG+ChatGPT and RAG+Gemini obtain relatively favorable readability results, including average FKGL values of 10.40 and 10.46 and average LENS values of 73.65 and 76.85.
