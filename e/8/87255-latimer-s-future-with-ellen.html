Latimer's future with Ellen in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Latimer envisions a long, quiet future with Ellen, including marriage and honorable ambition. He imagines using his position and resources to provide her comfort, pleasure, and charitable opportunities. The bishop agrees her material security would be assured, but worries that Latimer may not shield her from past consequences.

Young Latimer imagines a future in which he and Ellen are married and he uses his position and resources to give her comfort, pleasure, and opportunities for charity. The bishop says that Ellen's bodily comfort and material future would be secure with Latimer, but he remains concerned about whether Latimer can protect her from consequences of the past.
