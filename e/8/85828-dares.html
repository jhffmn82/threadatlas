Dares in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Dares is a gauntlet-fighting champion and Paris’s match in hand-to-hand combat. After fighting Butes at Hector’s funerals, he challenges a crowd and claims a bull prize, but Entellus defeats him, leaving him bleeding and carried away by friends.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The race thus ended, and rewards bestow’d,

Dares challenges the crowd to a gauntlet fight, boasts of having defeated Butes, and claims the bull before the contest. He refuses to use Eryx's heavy gauntlets, fights Entellus with equal arms, and is defeated; his mouth and nostrils bleed, his teeth are loosened, and his friends carry him away with the sword and casque.
