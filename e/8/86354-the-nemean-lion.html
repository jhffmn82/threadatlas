animal · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections

  • In The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books, the Nemean lion is a monstrous, invulnerable beast that Hercules strangles and whose skin he wears as protection.
  • In The Library, the Nemean lion is an invulnerable beast that Hercules hunts, chokes, and carries to Mycenae after Eurystheus orders him to bring its hide.

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

is_a monster [of monstrous size and invulnerable to weapons] The Nemean lion is a monstrous beast whose hide cannot be pierced or wounded by weapons or stones. Hercules traps it in its den, strangles it with his arms, and thereafter wears its skin as protection.

the Nemean lion in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

The Nemean lion is an invulnerable beast begotten by Typhon. After Eurystheus orders Hercules to bring its hide, Hercules hunts it, drives it into its two-mouthed cave, blocks one entrance, chokes it through the other, and carries its body to Mycenae.
