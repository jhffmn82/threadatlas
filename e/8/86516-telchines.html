Telchines in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Telchines were ancient inhabitants of Rhodes, said to descend from Thalassa. They invented useful arts and made early statues of gods, but were reputed magicians who raised storms and changed shape. Foreseeing an inundation, they forsook Rhodes and dispersed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nireus, the son of Charops and Aglaia, was, in after times^

The Telchines were said to have inhabited Rhodes, invented several useful arts, and made some of the first statues of the gods. They were also reputed to practice magic, raise storms, transform themselves, and envy those who learned their arts; foreseeing an inundation, they abandoned Rhodes and dispersed.
