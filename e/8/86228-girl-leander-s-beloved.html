Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

165 Perhaps you fear the time may fail you for

The girl fears for Leander whenever she looks toward the waves and is troubled by a dream of a drowned dolphin. She says that she will never be safe unless Leander is safe, and she sends him a letter while the sea is impassable.
