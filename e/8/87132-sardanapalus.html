1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IL

Sardanapalus was the thirtieth king from Ninus and the last king of the Assyrians. He exceeded his predecessors in sloth and luxury, lived in women's attire among his concubines, and practiced extreme sensuality. After Arbaces's revolt defeated his forces and besieged Nineveh, Sardanapalus burned himself, his treasure, eunuchs, and concubines in a palace fire rather than fall into enemy hands.
