Cadusians in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Cadusians, a nation famed for independence, were led by Parsodes in revolt against the Medes, defeated a Median army, made him king, and remained Median enemies until Cyrus transferred empire to the Persians.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III.

The Cadusians were persuaded by Parsodes, a Persian who had fled the Medes, to revolt and defend their liberties. They defeated a large Median army under Arsseus's successor, made Parsodes their king, and remained enemies of the Medes until Cyrus transferred the empire to the Persians.
