Siculi in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Siculi were a people whose cities, except kindred Trinacria, were controlled by the Syracusans while Deucetius sought power over them. They followed him against the Agrigentines, fought bravely near Nomae, were routed, and fled to forts and strongholds.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. XXVIII.

The Siculi followed Deucetius in his campaign against the Agrigentines. They fought bravely near Nomae but were eventually routed, and many were killed during the pursuit; others fled to forts and strongholds.

CHAP. V.

The Siculi were the people over whom Deucetius sought sovereign power after settling colonies in the country of the Callatines. The Syracusans controlled all their cities except Trinacria, whose inhabitants were related to them as kindred.
