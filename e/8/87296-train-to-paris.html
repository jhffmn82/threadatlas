train to Paris in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Train to Paris departs Monte Carlo at midnight; Cecil boards a couple, buys tickets, and warns they are alone in a locked compartment until home.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

" The woman ran ahead, with a murmur of gratitude, to the open door and pointed

Cecil hurries the couple onto the train to Paris and purchases their tickets. Before the guard locks the compartment, he tells them they are alone and cannot leave until they return home.
