Sphacteria in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Sphacteria is an island before the harbour of Pylos. Lacedæmonian reverses there made it world-famous; Athenians captured shields on it and commemorated their success with a brazen Victory statue.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Sphacteria lies before the harbour of Pylos, much as Rhenea lies in relation to the harbour of Delos. The reverses of the Lacedæmonians there made the island world-famous, and the Athenians erected a brazen statue of Victory in their Acropolis to commemorate their success.
