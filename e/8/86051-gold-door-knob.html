gold door knob in Kabumpo in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1922

A round, shiny gold door knob marked P. A. comes from the birthday cake, flies through the air to strike King Pompus, and remains mysteriously connected to the scroll. Kabumpo later carries it from Pumperdink, fits it to the gold castle door, and opens the glittering gold court room.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

A round, shiny gold door knob flies through the air and strikes King Pompus on the head. Unlike the palace’s ivory door knobs, it is gold and bears the initials P. A.; its connection with the scroll remains unexplained.

Chapter 20

Kabumpo carries the gold door knob from Pumperdink and fits it onto the small gold bar on the castle door. Turning it opens the way into the glittering gold court room.
