Scythia in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Scythia is an extremely cold region, opposite Basilea above Gaul across the ocean, where rivers freeze for armies and chariots, liquids congeal, body parts may fall off, and bronze statues split.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Next to these are those nations called Hylopliages and Spermato-

Scythia is described as an extremely cold region where rivers freeze strongly enough for armies and chariots to cross, liquids congeal, body parts can fall off from the cold, and even bronze statues may split. Its climate is contrasted with the intense heat of Egypt and the Troglodyte country.
