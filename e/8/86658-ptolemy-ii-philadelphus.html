Ptolemy the Second in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Ptolemy the Second marched into Ethiopia with a Greek army, avidly hunted and trained elephants for war, rewarded skilled hunters, and kept and fed a tamed thirty-cubit serpent brought to Alexandria.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sphinxes are bred near to the Troglodytes in Ethiopia, not nradi

Ptolemy the Second greatly enjoyed hunting elephants and offered rewards to skilled hunters of powerful wild beasts. He rewarded the huntsmen who brought him the thirty-cubit serpent, kept it in Alexandria, and fed it after it had been tamed.

Ptolemy II Philadelphus in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Ptolemy II Philadelphus is an Egyptian king represented by statues in the Athenian Odeum and among the Athenian Eponymi, near his sister Arsinoe.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Ptolemy II Philadelphus was one of the Egyptian kings whose statue stood in the Athenian Odeum, and he was also represented among the Athenian Eponymi. A statue of his sister Arsinoe stood near his.
