the Syracusan fleet in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The Syracusan fleet, stationed in Motya’s harbor, was trapped when Himiico seized the passage. Dionysius had it dragged overland to sea, where it escaped, fought pursuing Carthaginians, captured their admiral, destroyed twenty ships, and later aided Dionysius’s harbor attack.

The Syracusan fleet was initially trapped inside Motya's narrow harbor when Himiico seized the passage. Dionysius had the ships dragged overland into the sea at a greater distance from the harbor, allowing them to avoid the attack.
