the sack of Agrigentum in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

The sack of Agrigentum, carried out by Amilcar after the city was abandoned, saw remaining inhabitants killed, even in temples, and religious and secular places plundered; treasures and finest spoils were sent to Carthage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

As wc were not willing to let these things pass altogether, so we shall

During the sack of Agrigentum, Amilcar's forces killed inhabitants who remained, including people who sought refuge in temples. They plundered religious and secular places, seized paintings, statues, immense wealth, and the Phalarian bull, sending the finest spoils to Carthage.
