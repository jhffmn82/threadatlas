burglar in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

The burglar is a recently released prisoner who has served seven years in prison. Escaping with a revolver and bags of stolen goods, he is overpowered by Van Bibber after emerging beside a great house. He refuses to return to prison alive and says he stole money to reach his wife, living in the West under another name, and his daughter, who does not know him. Van Bibber sends him to Helena, Montana, where he seeks an honest living with them.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VAN BIBBER'S BURGLAR There had been a dance up town, but as Van Bibber could not find Her there, he accepted young Travers's suggestion to go over to Jersey City and see a "go" between "Dutchy" Mack and a <DW52> person professionally known as the Black Diamond.

The burglar emerges from the wall beside a great house carrying two bags of stolen property and a revolver. Van Bibber overpowers him, and the burglar says he has just escaped prison after serving seven years and refuses to return alive. He claims he stole to obtain money to join his wife and daughter in the West, and Van Bibber helps him travel to Helena, Montana.
