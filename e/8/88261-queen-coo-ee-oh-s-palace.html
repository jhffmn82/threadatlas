Queen Coo-ee-oh's palace in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Queen Coo-ee-oh's palace is on Skeezer island in the Dome's center. After the Skeezers accept Ozma as Ruler, she takes possession, discovers its private witchcraft room and tools, and later occupies it as the rope from the Dome descends before its entrance.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twelve

Ozma and Dorothy are escorted to Coo-ee-oh's palace after the Skeezers accept Ozma as their Ruler. Ozma takes possession of it and finds a private room devoted to witchcraft.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Queen's palace, now occupied by Ozma, stands directly in the center of the Dome. The rope lowered from the Dome comes down in front of its entrance.
