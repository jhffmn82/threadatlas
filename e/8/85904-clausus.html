Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

And now the mighty labour is begun

Clausus leads a numerous force drawn from the Sabine land and is described as an army in himself. He fathers the Claudian race, which is destined to share the greatness of imperial Rome, and commands troops from many towns and mountain regions.
