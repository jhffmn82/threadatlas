Sir Tristram in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

Sir Tristram is a bachelor, former Oxford associate and friend of Augustin Jedd, and a horseman/racing enthusiast. He stays at the Swan Inn with his horse, is a guest at the Deanery, habitually smokes a pipe, and co-owns the racehorse Dandy Dick, holding one half. Visiting Augustin, he eagerly discusses Dandy Dick's prospects in the Durnstone Handicap and learns that Georgiana, formerly George Tidd, owns the other half; they agree the horse is fit and likely to win. After Dandy Dick is rescued from the Swan Inn with a singed tail after cold water has been thrown over him, Sir Tristram and Georgiana arrange to move him to the Deanery stables. During a storm at the Deanery, Sir Tristram shares Georgiana's concern for the horse and hides with her to watch suspicious movement in the garden. He confronts Darbey and Tarver, questions Hatcham about the person who set fire to the inn, accompanies Georgiana to the police-station, dictates a description of the missing Dean, and helps plan and carry out the Dean's release and rescue. Learning that a bolus contained strychnine, he denounces the Dean. He proposes to Georgiana and is accepted. Finally, he helps show that the person secured in the stable is not the Dean and tells the Dean that there is no harm in laughter.

_GEORGIANA TIDMAN enters.

Sir Tristram visits Augustin and enthusiastically discusses the races, especially Dandy Dick's prospects in the Durnstone Handicap. He reveals that he is a half-owner of Dandy Dick and is delighted to discover that Georgiana, formerly George Tidd, owns the other half. He leaves after agreeing with Georgiana that Dandy Dick is fit and likely to win.

THE SECOND ACT.

Sir Tristram Mardon reports that Dandy Dick has been rescued safely from the Swan Inn, though the horse's tail is singed and two pails of cold water were thrown over him. He agrees with Georgiana that Dandy Dick should be brought into the Deanery stables and leaves with her to arrange it.

GEORGIANA and SIR TRISTRAM enter by the window.

Sir Tristram enters the Deanery with Georgiana and worries about Dandy Dick's tail. He is given accommodation in Sheba's old nursery and later meets Georgiana during the storm, when they hear people moving in the garden and hide to watch them.
