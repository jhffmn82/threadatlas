Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. IX,

The Corinthian sedition began when conspirators seeking power killed many people in the playhouse. With Argive assistance they killed one hundred and twenty citizens and banished five hundred, provoking Lacedaemonian intervention and further fighting.
