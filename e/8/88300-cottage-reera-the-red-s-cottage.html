cottage (Reera the Red's cottage) in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Reera the Red's purple, white-trimmed cottage is a lonely one-room home in an isolated place. Ervic reaches its gate near dawn, then encounters its threatening animal inhabitants, including Reera's gray-ape form. Reera keeps pets and performs transformations there, changing several creatures into less frightening forms. She hosts Ervic and the three Adepts for supper before they leave, promising never to return or reveal what happened.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Sixteen

The purple cottage has white trimmings and stands in a lonely, isolated place with no other buildings or cultivated ground nearby. Ervic reaches its gate near dawn after following the path and asks the fishes what to do next.

Chapter Eighteen

The cottage is a lonely one-room home filled with threatening creatures, including a crocodile, horned toads, spiders, a lizard, rats, and a huge gray ape. Reera changes many of these creatures into less frightening forms while Ervic remains inside.

Chapter Nineteen

Reera's cottage is where she keeps her pets, performs transformations, and hosts Ervic and the three Adepts for supper. The Adepts and Ervic leave it after promising never to return or tell anyone what happened there.
