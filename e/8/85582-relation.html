Also called: base graph’s edges, dependencies, edges, relation edge, relations, relationship, relationships, relevant nodes and edges
  • In “CausalRAG: Integrating Causal Graphs into Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” edges represent relationships inferred between nodes, guide expansion from initial nodes, and preserve causal and relational connections.
  • In “LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation,” a relationship connects entities in the knowledge graph, receives index keys derived from associated themes, and supports retrieval.
  • In “Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation,” relations are extracted from chunks, represented in knowledge-graph triplets, linked between head and tail entities, and retained as metadata on graph edges.
  • In “A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models,” a relation connects entities or concepts, expresses dependencies or associations, enables connected-knowledge discovery and multi-hop reasoning, exposes reasoning paths, and supports preprocessing and retrieval.
