Rhadamanthus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Rhadamanthus, son of Jupiter and Europa and brother of Minos, is renowned as the world’s most just man and an inexorable punisher of wickedness. He gains islands and Asian coasts, appoints rulers, and later judges hell with Minos.

Rhadamanthus, the son of Jupiter and Europa, was described as the most just man in the world and as inexorable in punishing thieves, robbers, and other wicked persons. He gained several islands and parts of the Asian coast, appointed rulers over territories, and was later regarded as a judge of hell with Minos.
