St. Marvells in Dandy Dick

Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

St. Marvells is a locality with little serious crime, a police station, and a small class of expected offenders; it returns a Conservative. Augustin Jedd is Dean, and Noah becomes Head Constable after arriving with Hannah. On the eve of races there, Georgiana arrives at the Deanery, troubling Augustin.

St. Marvells is the place where the races are about to take place. Augustin is concerned that Georgiana has arrived at the Deanery on the eve of the races there.

St. Marvells is where the police station is located and where Noah has recently become Head Constable. It is described as a place that returns a Conservative and as having a small class of expected offenders.
