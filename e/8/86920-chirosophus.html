Chirosophus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Chirosophus, a Lacedaemonian general and intimate friend of Anaxibius, commanded eight hundred soldiers sent to support Cyrus and delivered them at Issus. After Greek commanders were massacred, soldiers chose him as general; he sought ships at Byzantium but returned without them.

CHAP. V-

Chirosophus commanded the eight hundred Lacedaemonian soldiers sent to support Cyrus. He delivered the troops to Cyrus at Issus, where they were presented as having been sent privately by Cyrus's friends.
