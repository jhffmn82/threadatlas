the Flatheads' attack in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

The Flatheads plan to attack the Skeezers' island, but after it is submerged they arrive armed with poison; Queen Coo-ee-oh, expecting them, responds with a submarine and predicts they will not return.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Eight

Queen Coo-ee-oh says that the Flatheads intend to attack the Skeezers tomorrow. She claims that the Skeezers are prepared and that the Flatheads will not return to their barren mountain-top.

Chapter Ten

The Flatheads come to attack the Skeezers after the island has been submerged. Their leader brings armed followers and poison, while Coo-ee-oh responds with a submarine.
