the pregnancy and birth of twins in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Hypsipyle’s twin birth, caused by Jason: after he leaves knowing her pregnancy and hoping the child lives, Lucina aids delivery; Hypsipyle identifies the sons as both parents’ pledges.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

VI

Jason knew that Hypsipyle was pregnant when he left and hoped that the child would live so that they could share parenthood. Hypsipyle later gave birth to twins with the help of Lucina and identifies them as pledges belonging to both parents.
