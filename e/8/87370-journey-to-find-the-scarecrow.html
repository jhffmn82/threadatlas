journey to find the Scarecrow in The Royal Book of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1921

Dorothy conducts a search for the Scarecrow, first seeking clues through the palace, gardens, servants, and his empty home with the Cowardly Lion. The travelers return toward the Emerald City to use the Magic Picture, then journey with Sir Hokus through deserted Winkie Country, avoid the Deadly Desert, gain two helpers, and continue on Wish Way.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 4

Dorothy searches the palace, gardens, and servants for information about the Scarecrow before traveling to his home with the Cowardly Lion. When they find the home empty, they decide to return to the Emerald City and use the Magic Picture.

CHAPTER 16

The journey to find the Scarecrow leads Dorothy, Sir Hokus, and the Cowardly Lion through a deserted part of the Winkie Country and away from the Emerald City. After nearly entering the Deadly Desert, they gain the help of the Comfortable Camel and Doubtful Dromedary and continue along Wish Way.
