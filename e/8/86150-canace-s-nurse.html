Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI

Canace's nurse first recognizes that Canace is in love and helps conceal the pregnancy. She tries herbs and medicines to remove the growing child, later disguises him with sacrificial objects, and attempts to carry him past Aeolus.
