Relevance is an evaluation metric for generative models that measures whether answers directly address a question rather than go off-topic. Rated from 1 for complete irrelevance to 5 for accurate focus, it was used in experiments where CLBP-GPT scored highest at 4.39.

Relevance measured whether an answer directly addressed the question or was off-topic on a scale from 1 to 5. A score of 1 indicated complete irrelevance, while 5 indicated that the answer focused accurately on the question without deviation.
