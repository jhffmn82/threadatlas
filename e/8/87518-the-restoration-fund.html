Arthur W. Pinero · 1893

The Restoration Fund supports Minster Spire restoration. In the second act, it benefits from the Dean’s public thousand-pound offer, prompting seven wealthy donors to give matching sums; the Dean also directs a wager expected to add five hundred pounds toward the Spire.

THE SECOND ACT.

The Restoration Fund receives the benefit of the Dean's public thousand-pound offer. The offer prompts seven wealthy people, including three brewers, to come forward with a thousand pounds each.

_The second scene is the Morning Room at the Deanery again.

The Restoration Fund is the stated purpose for which the Dean instructed Blore to back Dandy Dick. The Dean expected the wager to provide five hundred pounds towards the Spire.
