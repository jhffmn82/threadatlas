THE SECOND ACT.

The Dean's offer of a thousand pounds to the Restoration Fund produces the desired result: seven wealthy people each offer a thousand pounds toward restoring the Minster Spire. The Dean is then obliged to pay his own thousand pounds and is distressed by the consequence.
