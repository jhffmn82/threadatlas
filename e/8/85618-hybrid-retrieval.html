hybrid retrieval strategy in HyPA-RAG: A Hybrid Parameter Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for AI Legal and Policy Applications

Rishi Kalra, Zekun Wu, Ayesha Gulley, Airlie Hilliard, Xin Guan, Adriano Koshiyama, Philip Treleaven · 2024-11-16

Hybrid retrieval is a method in HyPA-RAG that combines dense, sparse, and knowledge graph retrieval. It begins by pairing BM25 with semantic embeddings to balance keyword matching and contextual understanding, then uses this combination to enhance retrieval accuracy and improve document retrieval and RAG outcomes.
