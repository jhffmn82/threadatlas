Rhegians, related to Naxians and Cataneans, feared Dionysius after their enslavement, tried war with Syracusan exiles, withdrew after Messanian mutiny, then made peace.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VII.

The Rhegians feared Dionysius's growing power because he had enslaved the Naxians and Cataneans, who were related to them. They attempted to make war against him with Syracusan exiles, but withdrew when the Messanian troops mutinied, and later made peace with Dionysius.
