THE CYNICAL MISS CATHERWAIGHT Miss Catherwaight's collection of orders and decorations and medals was her chief offence in the eyes of those of her dear friends who thought her clever but cynical.

Henry Burgoyne and Lewis L. Lockwood were close friends from their school days in Westchester County. They roomed together at the State college, began practising law together, shared their spare time and hunting trips, and were described as closer than brothers. Their friendship ended after both fell in love with the same woman and each believed the other had acted disloyally.
