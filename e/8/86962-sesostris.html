Seven descents after, (they say), Sesostris reigned, who excelled

Sesostris was trained from childhood for command and later became king of Egypt. He conquered many territories, organized a vast empire, built extensive public works, and was celebrated for his military achievements and generosity. After reigning for thirty-three years, he became blind and voluntarily ended his life.

CHAP. V.

Sesostris the Second succeeded his father and took the same name, but did nothing remarkable by his arms. He became blind, was eventually cured after following an oracle's instructions, married a gardener's wife, punished adulteresses, and erected two large obelisks at the command of the oracle.

• And now it is necessary for us to speak of the legislators of Egypt^

Sesostris is identified as the third Egyptian lawmaker. In addition to his military achievements, he established laws for Egyptian military discipline and organized military affairs.
