Hippolyte in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Hippolyte, also called Glauce and Melanippe, was mother of Hippolytus. Armed with Amazons at Phaedra’s wedding, she threatened Theseus’s guests and died in the ensuing battle, under differing accounts.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

V. | "Ore Πενθεσίλεια, ᾿Οτρηρῆς καὶ ἔΑρεος,

Hippolyte was the mother of Hippolytus and was also called Glauce and Melanippe. During Phaedra’s wedding she appeared armed with Amazons and threatened to kill Theseus’s guests; in the ensuing battle she died, either accidentally at the hands of Penthesilia, by Theseus, or after Theseus’s men trapped and killed her.

Hippolyte in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Hippolyte, queen of the Amazons, holds Ares’ belt as supremacy’s token; at Themiscyra she promises it to Hercules, but Hera’s incitement sparks suspicion and Hercules kills her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, II. v. 4-5

Hippolyte is queen of the Amazons and possesses the belt of Ares as a symbol of her supremacy. She meets Hercules at Themiscyra, promises to give him the belt, but is killed by him when Hera incites the Amazons and Hercules suspects treachery.

Hippolyta in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Hippolyta, queen of the Amazons, kindly agrees to give Hercules her girdle but is killed by him after he wrongly believes her treacherous.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XII

Hippolyta was the queen of the Amazons. She received Hercules kindly and agreed to give him her girdle, but Hercules mistakenly believed she had acted treacherously and killed her during the Amazon attack.
