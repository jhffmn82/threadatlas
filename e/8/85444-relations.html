  • In REANO: Optimising Retrieval-Augmented Reader Models through Knowledge Graph Generation: Relations are inferred among entities in the passages. REANO extracts relations within individual passages and across passages. A relation's initial embedding is obtained by mean-pooling T5 token embeddings for its label. The relation embedding is refined by integrating the embeddings of the connected entities.
