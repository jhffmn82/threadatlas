Amilcar the Carthaginian in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Amilcar the Carthaginian is a Carthaginian general who commanded with Hannibal at Agrigentum and later commanded the Carthaginians after Hannibal’s death. During the siege he ordered assaults and siege works, sacrificed during plague, ambushed a Syracusan grain fleet, and plundered Agrigentum. He then devastated Gela and Camarina, marched against Gela, and made peace with Dionysius.

Amilcar commanded the Carthaginians with Hannibal during the siege of Agrigentum. He ordered assaults and the construction of siege works, sacrificed to the gods when plague struck his army, defeated the Syracusan grain fleet through a naval ambush, and entered and plundered Agrigentum after its evacuation.

CHAP. XVI.

Amilcar sacked Agrigentum, carried away valuable temple furnishings, devastated the territories of Gela and Camarina, and marched against Gela. After Dionysius withdrew and unrest broke out in Syracuse, Amilcar sent a herald to offer peace and concluded an agreement with Dionysius.
