the slaughter of the sleeping soldiers in The Aeneid

Virgil · -0028/-0018

Nisus, with Euryalus, conducts a slaughter of sleeping men and slaves; it ends with troops finding dead and dying amid crowds and reeking blood.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thus arm’d they went. The noble Trojans wait

The slaughter includes Rhamnes and his sleeping slaves, Remus and his retinue, Lamus, Lamyrus, Serranus, Fadus, Hebesus, and Rhoetus. The troops later find the dead and dying surrounded by crowds, with reeking blood overflowing the ground.
