  • In Heroides and Amores, Protesilaus, Haemonian lord of Laodamia, leaves her to travel with the Danaans, joins the Greek forces at Troy, while she fears battle and urges his safe return.
  • In The Library, Protesilaus first lands from his ship, kills many barbarians, is killed by Hector, then returns from Hades through Hermes and resurrects the dead.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Protesilaüs is the first Greek to land at Troy and the first to fall there, killed by Hector’s spear immediately upon landing, fulfilling fate’s decree.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIII

Protesilaus has left Laodamia and is traveling with the Danaans toward Troy. Laodamia fears for him in battle, warns him to avoid Hector and other Trojan warriors, and asks him to fight only to survive and return to her.
