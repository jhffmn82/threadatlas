royal daily regimen in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Royal daily regimen begins with morning correspondence, washing, dressing, and sacrifices, continues through public business, justice, air and bathing, and extends into prescribed nighttime duties.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAP. VI.

The royal daily regimen included reading public correspondence, washing, dressing in royal robes and insignia, sacrificing, conducting public business, administering justice, taking the air, bathing, and other prescribed activities. The king also had duties assigned during specified hours of the night.
