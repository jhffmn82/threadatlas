Hade's arrest in Gallegher and Other Stories

Richard Harding Davis · 1890

Hade's arrest is a secretly planned capture intended to avoid publicity from other newspapers. Gallegher and Michael E. Dwyer arrange for Detective Hefflefinger to seize the suspected Hade near the Eagle Inn after the fight and take him to New York on the one o'clock train. During a police raid, Hefflefinger uses his badge and a false burglary explanation to remove Hade from the barn. Editors waiting for confirmation are told by Gallegher that Hade was captured.
