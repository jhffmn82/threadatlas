Tyro in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus and Alcidice, is raised by Cretheus, loves Enipeus, and bears Pelias and Neleus after Poseidon approaches as Enipeus. She exposes them; grown, they recognize her and kill Sidero for ill-treating her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Now Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus and Alcidice,

Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus and Alcidice, was brought up by Cretheus and conceived a passion for the river Enipeus. Poseidon approached her in the likeness of Enipeus, and she secretly bore twin sons, Pelias and Neleus, whom she exposed. When they were grown, they recognized her and killed her stepmother Sidero, who had ill-treated her.
