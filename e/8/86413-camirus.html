Camirus in The Historical Library of Diodorus the Sicilian, in Fifteen Books

Diodorus Siculus · -0059/-0029

Camirus, a city built by one of Cercaphus's three sons, was named after its builder; it later received Althasmenes and his followers and contributed inhabitants to Rhodes during incorporation into one city.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nireus, the son of Charops and Aglaia, was, in after times^

Camirus was one of the three sons of Cercaphus who jointly ruled Rhodes. He shared in dividing the country and built a city that was called Camirus after him; later, Althasmenes and his followers settled in Camirus.
