Aujah in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Aujah, one of the three Adepts at Magic and formerly one of three transformed fishes, teaches Coo-ee-oh how to expand steel. He confirms the Adepts sought by Glinda and helps explain how Coo-ee-oh may have raised the island, endorsing a test of syllabic magic words.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

Aujah is one of the three Adepts at Magic and confirms that they are the three Adepts whom Glinda has been seeking.

Chapter Twenty-Three

Aujah explains that Coo-ee-oh may have expanded a steel pillar to raise the island. He identifies the powder as the likely substance used in the basin and concludes that Dorothy's idea about Coo-ee-oh's syllabic magic words is worth testing.
