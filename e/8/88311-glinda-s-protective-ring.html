Glinda's protective ring in Glinda of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1920

Glinda's protective ring, given to Dorothy, is turned right then left to summon Glinda and signal that Dorothy and Ozma face great danger.

Chapter Thirteen

Glinda's alarm bell rings when Dorothy turns the ring Glinda gave her once to the right and once to the left. Its ringing tells Glinda that Ozma and Dorothy are in great danger.
