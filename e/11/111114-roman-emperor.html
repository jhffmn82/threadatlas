Roman Emperor in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The elder Antonine, a Roman Emperor called Pius by the Romans, made Pallantium a tax-exempt free town, drove the Mauri from their territory, took Brigantes land, restored earthquake-struck Cos and Rhodes, aided needy peoples and public works, and made the Orontes navigable to Antioch by canal.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLIII.

The elder Antonine made Pallantium a town and granted it freedom and exemption from taxation. He drove the Mauri from their territory, took most of the Brigantes' territory after their attack on the Genunii, restored Cos and Rhodes after an earthquake, and supported needy Greeks and barbarians and public works.
