Amberley's wife in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Josiah Amberley’s younger, good-looking wife was made wretched by his niggardly ways and became involved with Dr. Ray Ernest. Amberley claimed she left with Ernest after taking his money and securities, but Holmes concluded Amberley murdered her in the strong-room.

THE ADVENTURE OF THE RETIRED COLOURMAN

Mrs. Amberley is Josiah Amberley's younger wife and is described as good-looking. Amberley claims that she had an intimacy with Dr. Ray Ernest, left with him, and took his deed-box containing money and securities.
