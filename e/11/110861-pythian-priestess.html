Pythian Priestess in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Pythian Priestess, located at Delphi, gives answers and commands: she directs Corœbus, Elis, and Thasos, warns Glaucus about intended perjury, and identifies forgotten Theagenes as causing Thasos's divine wrath and dearth.

CHAPTER XI.

The Pythian Priestess told the Thasians, "Your great Theagenes you have forgotten," after their first response to the oracle had not ended the dearth.
