Grumpy in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

Grumpy is a little small brown bear in Oz, also a person there, with bandy legs and a cross disposition though tired of being cross. Formerly the late Queen Cross Patch’s pet, he helped her grumble and growl; he is later Scraps’s pet and is also identified as the Queen of the Quilties’ pet. He speaks, likes verse, and chooses never to leave Oz because there he can be both bear and person. At the Quilty palace, he warns Scraps about the Scissor Bird and hides in a chest. Found there, he listens to Peter’s story, urges escape, and advises sleep when they do not know what to do. He helps the escape by washing dishes in a gingham apron and knocking a pursuing Quilty into a ditch. Traveling with Peter and Scraps toward the Emerald City, he guides them, escapes Soap Mountain, crosses the lake, and joins Ozwold and the oztrich. In Tune Town he proposes that the four present themselves as a quartet, dances, and escapes with them after a discordant scream. Hungry on the road, he eats an umbrella melon, falls into a ditch, and rides behind Peter while urging Ozwold to say “whum.”

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Grumpy is a small brown bear who had been the late Queen Cross Patch’s pet and helped her with grumbling and growling. He warns Scraps that the Scissor Bird would cut off her head if she tried to escape, and he hides in a chest when someone violently approaches the palace door.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Grumpy is found hiding in a chest and comments on the argument in the room. He listens eagerly to Peter’s story, urges Peter to tell Scraps about him and Scraps, and reminds them that they must first escape from the castle. He advises Peter to go to sleep when they do not know what to do, and Peter falls asleep resting on his shoulder.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

Grumpy offers to help Peter and Scraps and washes the dishes while wearing a gingham apron. During the escape, he sends one of the pursuing Quilties flying into a ditch and travels with Peter and Scraps toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 11 Scraps Meets Sultan Of Suds

Grumpy guides the travelers by climbing trees and insists that he will never leave Oz because he can be both a bear and a person there. After sliding down Soap Mountain, he is threatened with being made into a bath mat, but escapes with Peter and Scraps on the soap raft.

CHAPTER 12 Friend Oztrich Offers To Help

Grumpy crosses the lake with Peter and Scraps and becomes dizzy and confused in the Bewilderness. He helps identify Ozwold as a living creature and suggests that the Oztrich's egg could be endangered by the moving rocks. He rides on Ozwold's back to the Emerald City, bouncing and gasping during the journey.

CHAPTER 13 Tune Town Sings The Wanderers Onward

Grumpy enters Tune Town riding the oztrich and suggests that he and the others present themselves as a quartet. He dances and shuffles along with the music, cannot eat the leather music rolls at the Viol Inn, and joins the others in the discordant scream that leads to their escape.

CHAPTER 14 Ozwold And His Friends Rush On

Grumpy complains hungrily about the lack of biscuit bushes and carrot trees, then catches and eats an umbrella melon. He falls into a ditch when Ozwold stops suddenly and later rides behind Peter while urging Ozwold to say “whum” instead of “whoa.”
