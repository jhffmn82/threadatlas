1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XVII.

The sepulchre is a low mound of earth. The people of Tithorea sought to carry away earth from it when the Sun was in Taurus and place it on Antiope’s tomb, while the Thebans guarded it because they believed this would make Tithorea fertile and cause blight in Thebes.
