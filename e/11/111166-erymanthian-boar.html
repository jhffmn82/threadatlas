animal · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

  • In The Library, the Erymanthian boar ravages Psophis until Hercules pursues, nooses, and carries it alive to Mycenae.

Erymanthian boar in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The Erymanthian boar was said to surpass all boars in size and strength. Hercules hunted it on the Erymanthus; Cumæ’s people claimed teeth in Apollo’s temple were its, though the account doubts this.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIV.

The Erymanthian boar was said to exceed all other boars in size and strength. Hercules hunted it on the banks of the Erymanthus, while the people of Cumæ claimed that boar’s teeth kept in Apollo’s temple were its teeth, a claim the account considers improbable.
