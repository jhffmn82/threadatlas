Clymenus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Clymenus, son of Presbon and king of Orchomenus, fathered Erginus, Stratius, Arrho, Pyleus, and Azeus; Thebans killed him at Onchestian Poseidon's festival, and Erginus succeeded him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVII.

Clymenus was the son of Presbon and the father of Erginus, Stratius, Arrho, Pyleus, and Azeus. Some Thebans killed him at the festival of Onchestian Poseidon, after which Erginus succeeded him.
