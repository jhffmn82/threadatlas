Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VI.

Glaucus of Carystus was said to have come originally from Anthedon in Bœotia and to be descended from Glaucus the sea-god. Originally a husbandman, he used his hand to replace a ploughshare and later became a boxer; he won twice at the Pythian games and eight times at the Nemean and Isthmian games. His statue, made by Glaucias the Æginetan and erected by his son, shows him boxing, and the people of Carystus said he was buried on an island called Glaucus' island.
