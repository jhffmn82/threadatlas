Quilties in The Gnome King of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1927

The Quilties are magically constructed people of Patch, the Quilty Kingdom, in Oz. They gather cotton patches from gardens, stitch patch-work quilts, and perform mending and darning; Piecer and Scrapper are among their residents and ministers who govern Patch. Rather than dying, worn-out Quilties go to pieces, remain in a scrap-bag for about ten years, and return. They await Scrapper and Piecer, demand a ruler, then acclaim the angry Scraps, crown her Queen of Patch, and escort her to the palace. Later they imprison Peter and Scraps for household work, but are driven back while the captives escape.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The Quilties are the magically constructed people of Patch, who gather cotton patches, make quilts, and do mending and darning. They cannot die, but when they wear out they go to pieces, after which their scraps are stored in a scrap-bag for about ten years before they return.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

The Quilties await the return of Scrapper and Piecer and demand to see their ruler. They enthusiastically accept Scraps after seeing her anger, cheer for her, crown her Queen of Patch, and lead her into the palace.

CHAPTER 10 Escape From Patch At Last

The Quilties keep Peter and Scraps imprisoned and make them perform household work. Several Quilties chase the escaping group, but Kuma's hand, the Sandman's Nap Sack, and Grumpy drive them back.
