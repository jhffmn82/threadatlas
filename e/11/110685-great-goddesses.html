1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

The rites and mysteries of the Great Goddesses were introduced into Messenia by Caucon from Eleusis and later taught by Lycus to Aphareus, his family, and Arene. The mysteries were celebrated at Andania, and annual rites there continued in the time of Sybotas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXI.

The Great Goddesses are Demeter and Proserpine, the latter being called Preserver by the Arcadians. Their precincts contain statues, temples, and associated figures, and their worship is celebrated at Megalopolis.
