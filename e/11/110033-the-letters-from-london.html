the letters from London in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Letters from London, marked with a cross under the stamp and bearing the E.C. mark in illiterate handwriting, are reserved for Professor Presbury; his replies bypass Bennett and the household letter-basket.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE CREEPING MAN

Professor Presbury tells Trevor Bennett that letters from London marked with a cross under the stamp are to be reserved for him. Several arrive bearing the E.C. mark in illiterate handwriting, and any replies do not pass through Bennett or the household letter-basket.
