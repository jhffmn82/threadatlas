Phormio, son of Asopichus, was a blind fisherman from Erythræ who dreamed that women’s hair should be made into a rope to drag a raft ashore; afterward he regained sight for life.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Phormio was a blind fisherman from Erythræ who dreamed that the women should cut off their hair and that the men should make a rope from it to drag the raft ashore. After the raft was brought to land, he recovered his sight and remained able to see for the rest of his life.
