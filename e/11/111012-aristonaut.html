Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVI.

Aristonautæ was the seaport of Pellene. It was said to have received its name because the Argonauts put in at its harbor, and it lay 120 stades from Ægira by sea and half that distance from Pellene.
