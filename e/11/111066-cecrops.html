Cecrops in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Cecrops, son of Pandion and king of Athens, contemporary of Lycaon, first recognized Zeus as supreme and offered pelani instead of living sacrifices.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Cecrops, king of Athens, was thought to be a contemporary of Lycaon. He was the first to call Zeus supreme and offered national cakes called pelani instead of sacrificing anything living.
