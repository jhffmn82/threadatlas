Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Philetærus, a Paphlagonian eunuch associated with Docimus, revolted from Lysimachus and invited Seleucus I. Later, indignant at Agathocles’ death and suspicious of Arsinoe, he occupied Pergamum and offered himself and Lysimachus’s money to Seleucus.
