Irashi in The Hungry Tiger of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1926

Irashi is the self-appointed Pasha, sovereign, and ruler of Rash, a former ruler who seized its throne by treachery, stole its scepter and precious magic rubies, and hid Prince Evered. Feared and hated by the people of Rash, he is unjust, unwise, wicked, and bad-tempered. He rules through cruelty: he punishes Hasha over burnt pudding, approves bringing the Hungry Tiger from Oz to eat prisoners, appoints the tiger jailer, and orders prisoners—including the sad singer, Betsy, and Carter Green—sent to the courtyard. He mistakes events around the tiger, including believing Prince Evered has been eaten. Irashi scatters the three rubies by burying one, throwing one into a river, and tossing one into the air, losing their protection. When he attacks Reddy with his scimiter, the rubies make the blow harmless. Reddy takes Irashi's crown, defeats his army, and has him bound. Atmos Fere then carries Irashi into the sky as evidence that people live at the bottom of the air, intending to leave him on a lonely island after a lecture.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

The Pasha of Rash angrily punishes Hasha for serving burnt pudding and orders him sent to prison. He approves Ippty's plan to obtain the Hungry Tiger from Oz so that the animal can eat Rash prisoners, and he sends Ippty to fetch it.

CHAPTER 3

Irashi appoints the Hungry Tiger to serve as Rash's jailer and arranges to imprison him in the courtyard. He sends the sad singer to be eaten, enjoys the prospect of watching this happen, and mistakenly concludes that the Hungry Tiger has devoured him.

CHAPTER 5

Pasha Irashi becomes furious when Carter offers him a cabbage and orders the travelers to leave Rash. After learning that Carter is a Vegetable Man, he orders the Guards to throw Carter and Betsy to the tiger.

CHAPTER 6

Irashi the Rough rules Rash after stealing the royal scepter and hiding Prince Evered. He has prisoners thrown into the courtyard for the Hungry Tiger to eat, but later assumes that the tiger ate Evered after disappearing.

CHAPTER 8

Irashi is described by Prince Evered as the person who stole the precious Rash rubies and made himself Pasha of Rash.

CHAPTER 18

Irashi is described as having stolen the throne of Rash and thrown the three rubies into different places. One ruby was buried in a garden, another was hurled into a river, and the last was tossed into the air.

CHAPTER 19

Irashi attacks Reddy with a scimiter, but the blow is harmless because of the Rash rubies. Reddy takes his crown, defeats his army, and has him bound before Irashi is carried away by Atmos.

CHAPTER 20

Irashi is carried into the sky by Atmos Fere as a specimen to support his claim about people living at the bottom of the air. Atmos Fere plans to abandon Irashi on a lonely island after his lecture.
