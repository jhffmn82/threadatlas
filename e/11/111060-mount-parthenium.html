Mount Parthenium

Also called: mountain Parthenium
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II: contains enclosure sacred to Telephus Mount Parthenium lies on the road from Tegea to Argos. It contains an enclosure sacred to Telephus, who was said to have been exposed there as a boy and raised by a doe, and a nearby temple of Pan; it also has tortoises considered sacred to Pan and suitable for making lyres.
