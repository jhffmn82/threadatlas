Ruggedo escapes after Wumbo’s chant wears off, four hours later. Using his magic cloak, he flies over mountains, hills, and valleys toward the Emerald City. During the journey, Ozwold hears of the escape and worries Ruggedo may already have reached and captured the Emerald City.
