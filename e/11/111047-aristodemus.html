Aristodemus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Aristodemus, a Phigalian son of Artylas adopted by Tritæus, seized sole power in Megalopolis. Though termed a tyrant, he was called Good and True; during his rule Megalopolis defeated Lacedæmonians under Acrotatus. He offered Artemis the Huntress a temple and was buried in his tomb.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVII.

Aristodemus, a Phigalian and the son of Artylas, seized the chief power in Megalopolis after being adopted by Tritæus. Although he ruled alone, he was called Good man and True, and during his rule Megalopolis defeated the Lacedæmonians under Acrotatus.
