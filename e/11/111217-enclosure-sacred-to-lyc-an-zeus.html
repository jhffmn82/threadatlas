Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXVIII.

People may not enter the enclosure sacred to Lycæan Zeus, and anyone who violates the prohibition is said not to live more than a year. Men and beasts inside it are said to have no shadow at any time of year, so a hunter outside cannot see the shadow of a beast that enters it.
