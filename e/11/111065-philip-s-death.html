CHAPTER VII.

Philip’s death came when he was forty-six and fulfilled a Delphic oracle that had been given to him when he asked about the Persian war. The oracle’s reference to the crowned bull and the approaching sacrificer was shown to concern Philip himself rather than the Mede.
