Angelo Hall in An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall · 1908

Angelo Hall, son of Angeline Hall, was born on September 16, 1868, at 18 Gay Street, the third child. Described first as fat and rosy, then healthy, bright, handsome, and active, he helped with household and garden work before becoming a college student in 1887. In 1908, at Annapolis, he wrote An Astronomer’s Wife, a biography of his mother addressed to his daughter Peggy and the public.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

An Astronomer's Wife

Angelo Hall writes the biography of his mother, Angeline Hall, addressing it first to his daughter Peggy and also inviting the public to listen. He states that he is writing at Annapolis on November 1, 1908.

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XIV.

Angelo Hall was born at 18 Gay Street on September 16, 1868. His birth helped give the neighborhood its phrase, “Asaph, Sam, and Angelico.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER XVI.

Angelo Hall is described as a healthy, bright, handsome, and active child in the earlier letters, helping with household and garden tasks. Later he is at college with Percival, writes to Angeline, and is mentioned as possibly attending a boat-race.

front matter

Angelo Hall is identified as the author of _An Astronomer’s Wife_.
