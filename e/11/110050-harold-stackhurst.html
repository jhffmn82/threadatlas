Harold Stackhurst in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Harold Stackhurst is the headmaster of The Gables, a coaching establishment, and an excellent former rowing Blue. A neighbor and friend of Mr. Holmes, he employs Ian Murdoch as a mathematical coach. After Fitzroy McPherson collapses, Stackhurst aids Holmes, reports on The Gables, dismisses Murdoch after confronting him, investigates McPherson’s disappearance and death, helps destroy Cyanea capillata, and reconciles with Murdoch.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE LION'S MANE

Harold Stackhurst encounters Mr. Holmes while going for a swim and helps him respond when Fitzroy McPherson collapses. He remains with McPherson’s body, reports on the relationships at The Gables, dismisses Ian Murdoch after their confrontation, and assists Holmes in questioning the Bellamy family.

Stackhurst investigates the disappearance and death of Fitzroy McPherson and brings Ian Murdoch to Sherlock Holmes after finding him stricken on the beach. He helps Holmes crush Cyanea capillata beneath a boulder and later reconciles with Murdoch.
