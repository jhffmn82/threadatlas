CHAPTER XV.

The people of Elis perform sacrifices at the altars once every month. They burn frankincense and honey cakes, decorate the altars with olive branches, and pour out wine, except that wine is not offered to the Nymphs, the Mistresses, or the joint altar of all the gods.
