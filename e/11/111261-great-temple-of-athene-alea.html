Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLV.

The great temple of Athene Alea at Tegea surpassed all temples in the Peloponnese in beauty and size. Its first row of pillars was Doric, its second row Corinthian, and its outer pillars Ionic; its gables depicted the Calydonian boar hunt and the single combat between Telephus and Achilles.
