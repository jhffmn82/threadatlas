Adelbert Gruner's brown leather book in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

Adelbert Gruner's brown leather book is a locked, gold-armed volume kept, perhaps, in a pigeon-hole of his old bureau in the inner study. Written in Baron Adelbert Gruner's own hand, it is a compromising diary of his love or lust, containing snapshot photographs, names, and details of women he has ruined. During the evening operation Sherlock Holmes takes the incriminating book from Gruner's rooms. Sir James then carries it away to expose Gruner's moral character and break off his intended marriage to Violet de Merville.
