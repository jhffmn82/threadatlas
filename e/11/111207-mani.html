Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

Maniæ is a place about 7 stades from Megalopolis on the road to Messene, containing a temple of some goddesses. The name is thought to be a title of the Eumenides, and the Alpheus is about 15 stades from Maniæ.
