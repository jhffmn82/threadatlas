BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

The temple of Artemis Limnas stood on the border of Messenia and was shared by the Messenians and Lacedæmonians as the only Dorians with a share there. Conflicting traditions about an incident at the temple involving maidens, Teleclus, and Messenians became part of the causes alleged for the later war.
