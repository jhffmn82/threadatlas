Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The annual sacrifice of the most handsome maiden and lad was instituted after the oracle at Delphi attributed the country’s famine, sickness, and mortality to Comætho and Melanippus. The sacrifice continued until Eurypylus arrived with the chest containing the statue of Dionysus and was stopped.
