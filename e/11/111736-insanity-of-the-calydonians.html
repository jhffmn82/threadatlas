CHAPTER XXI.

The insanity of the Calydonians was caused by Dionysus after Coresus prayed to him. The Calydonians became insane as if drunk and died beside themselves, and the oracle at Dodona said the condition would not end until Callirhoe or another volunteer was sacrificed.
