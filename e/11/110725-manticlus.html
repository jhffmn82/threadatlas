Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

Manticlus fought among the first defenders of Eira and was appointed with Gorgus to lead the retreating Messenians. Later he argued that the exiles should sail to Sardinia, led the Messenian settlement in Sicily with Gorgus, and built a temple of Hercules for the new colony.
