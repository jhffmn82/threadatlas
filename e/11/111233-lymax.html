Lymax in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Lymax is a river flowing by Phigalia and winding into the Neda near hot baths; the Phigalians name it for Lymata, Rhea’s purifications.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XLI.

The Lymax flows by Phigalia and falls into the Neda near the hot baths. The Phigalians say it received its name from the purifications of Rhea, and its course is described as second only to the Maeander in winding.
