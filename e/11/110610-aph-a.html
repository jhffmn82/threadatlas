CHAPTER XXIX.

Aphæa is worshipped in Ægina, where she has a temple on the way to the mountain of Pan-Hellenian Zeus and where Pindar wrote an ode for the Æginetans. She is identified in the Cretan tradition with Britomartis, who is also called Dictynna in Crete.
