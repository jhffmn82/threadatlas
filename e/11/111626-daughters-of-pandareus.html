daughters of Pandareus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The daughters of Pandareus, Camiro and Clytie, were orphaned when their parents died through divine wrath. Aphrodite and other goddesses nurtured and gifted them, before Harpies carried them to the Furies; they are painted crowned with flowers, playing dice.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXX.

The daughters of Pandareus were Camiro and Clytie. Their parents died through the wrath of the gods while they were maidens; Aphrodite raised them, and other goddesses gave them gifts, but the Harpies carried them off and gave them to the Furies. Polygnotus painted them crowned with flowers and playing with dice.
