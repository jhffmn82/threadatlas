Gortys in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Gortys, formerly a town and now a village, contains a temple of Æsculapius with statues by Scopas, while the river Lusius flows by it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVIII.

Gortys is a village in the narrator's day but was formerly a town. It contains a temple of Æsculapius with statues of Æsculapius and Hygiea by Scopas, and the river Lusius flows by it.
