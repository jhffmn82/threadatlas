Alcamenes in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Alcamenes, son of Teleclus and king of the Lacedæmonians, made Hecate with three heads and bodies and led the night attack capturing Amphea.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK IV.--MESSENIA.

Alcamenes, the son of Teleclus, was king at Lacedæmon during a later generation of conflict. He was appointed general for the night attack that captured Amphea.
