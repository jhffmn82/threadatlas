Coresus, a Calydonian priest of Dionysus, grievously loves Callirhoe despite her dislike; when Dionysus afflicts the Calydonians, he sacrifices himself instead of her.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

Coresus was a Calydonian priest of Dionysus who loved Callirhoe, although she disliked him. After Dionysus afflicted the Calydonians, Coresus served as the sacrificial priest but killed himself instead of Callirhoe, yielding to love rather than anger.
