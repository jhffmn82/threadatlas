temple of Itonian Athene in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

The temple of Itonian Athene, named after Itonus, stands between Alalcomenæ and Coronea on the route to Coronea. It hosts the Bœotians’ general meeting and contains brazen statues of Itonian Athene and Zeus by Agoracritus, plus later statues of the Graces.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXXIV.

The temple of Itonian Athene stands between Alalcomenæ and Coronea and is the place where the Bœotians hold their general meeting. It contains brazen statues of Itonian Athene and Zeus designed by Agoracritus, as well as statues of the Graces erected during Pausanias’s time.
