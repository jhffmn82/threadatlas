Antiochus I in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Antiochus I, son of Seleucus I, was persuaded by Magas to plan invading Egypt but was stopped by Ptolemy’s raids and resistance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Antiochus I was persuaded by Magas to violate the agreement made by Seleucus I with Ptolemy I Soter and prepare an invasion of Egypt. Ptolemy sent raiders into his territory and checked stronger forces, preventing the invasion.
