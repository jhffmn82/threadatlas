Tattypoo in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

Tattypoo is the Good Witch of the North, an old, happy, good and powerful witch who rules the Gilliken Country wisely from her hut in the purple forest. She uses magic only for good, settles disputes, helps all who visit, defeated Mombi, and is the only witch of consequence in Oz. She owns a staff and believes magic for personal benefit would be wrong, yet cannot remember her youth or life before reaching the forest. After looking through her witch's window, she screams, leaps out, and disappears with her staff, cap, and spectacles. Her absence leaves the Gilliken Country without a ruler and vulnerable, prompting Ozma and others to seek her. Orin later reveals that Tattypoo was originally the Princess of the North, daughter of King Gil, wife of Cheeriobed, and mother of Philador. Mombi carried her from the Ozure Isles on a huge black eagle. Having lived as Tattypoo, she regains her true identity, returns to the Ozure Isles, and reunites with Cheeriobed and Philador.

CHAPTER 7

Tattypoo rules the Gillikens, settles disputes, and helps anyone who comes to her hut. She cannot remember her youth or life before coming to the purple forest and believes using magic for her own benefit would be wrong. After looking through the witch's window, she screams and jumps out, disappearing with her staff, cap, and spectacles.

CHAPTER 8

Tattypoo is gone when Philador awakens, and the two-tailed cat suggests that she may have fallen down the well. Philador later explains that Tattypoo had helped defeat Mombi before her disappearance.

CHAPTER 10

The Good Witch of the North is said to be absent from the Gilliken Country, leaving it without a ruler and open to war and invasion by hostile tribes. Philador promises to ask Ozma what became of her.

CHAPTER 17

Tattypoo is the good witch whose location is unknown after Philador reaches her hut. Ozma and the others plan to find her, and Ozma says there would be nothing more to worry about if they knew where she was.

CHAPTER 18

Orin tells how she was Princess of the North, married Cheeriobed, and was carried away from the Ozure Isles by Mombi on a huge black eagle. After living as the witch Tattypoo, she regained her true identity, returned to the Ozure Isles, and was reunited with Cheeriobed and Philador.

CHAPTER 20

Orin is declared King of the East by Ozma after the Wizard of Oz concludes that Cheeriobed's father has been utterly destroyed by Mombi. Orin and Cheeriobed are to rule the Munchkins, with Sapphire City as their capital.

Queen of the Ozure Isles in The Giant Horse of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1928

The Queen of the Ozure Isles, also Queen Orin and the Good Witch of the North, was abducted by Mombi when Prince Philador was two and remained missing for a long time. Her disappearance raised hopes that King Cheeriobed would seek her, while Trot and companions planned to ask Ozma for help. She returns crowned and young-looking, survives Quiberon's attack through Akbad's rescue, reunites with Philador, and reveals her identity.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 11

The Queen of the Ozure Isles was abducted by Mombi when the little Prince was two years old. Her disappearance led Orpah to hope that King Cheeriobed would search for her.

CHAPTER 12

The Queen of the Ozure Isles remains missing. Trot and her companions plan to ask Ozma to help find her.

CHAPTER 16

Queen Orin returns to the Ozure Isles in a fisherman's dory, appearing young and beautiful in her crown and blue dress. Quiberon attacks her boat, but Akbad rescues her and carries her to the sapphire castle.

CHAPTER 17

Queen Orin is reunited with Prince Philador and enters the Castle of Cheeriobed with the rescuers. She reveals that she is the Good Witch of the North, creating confusion because Philador had believed she was his mother, and agrees to tell her story.
