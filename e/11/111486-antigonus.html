Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER VII.

Antigonus was ruler of the Macedonians as regent for Philip, the son of Demetrius, and was Philip's uncle and stepfather. He made peace with Cleomenes and the Achæans, later defeated the Lacedæmonians with the Achæans at Sellasia, and surrendered rule to Philip when Philip came of age.
