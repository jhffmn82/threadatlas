Eretheum in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Erechtheum is a place containing altars to Supreme Zeus, Poseidon, Erechtheus, Butes, and Hephaestus, paintings of Butes’ family, and a sea-water well that roars in the south wind beside Poseidon’s trident mark.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXV.

The Erechtheum contained an altar of Supreme Zeus, altars to Poseidon, Erechtheus, Butes, and Hephaestus, and paintings of the family of Butes. It also had a well said to contain seawater, a roaring sound when the south wind blew, and a trident-shaped mark in the rock attributed to Poseidon.
