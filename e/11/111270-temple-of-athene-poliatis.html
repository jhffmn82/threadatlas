CHAPTER XLVII.

The temple of Athene Poliatis at Tegea is entered by its priest once each year and is called the temple of Protection. It is said to have been granted by Athene to Cepheus, son of Aleus, and to protect Tegea from capture through a lock of Medusa’s hair that Athene gave him.
