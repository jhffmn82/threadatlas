CHAPTER XXI.

Aristocrates betrayed Aristomenes by sending Anaxander a letter revealing the planned attack on Sparta. The letter was intercepted by Arcadians, who showed Anaxander’s reply to the people and thereby exposed Aristocrates as a traitor.
