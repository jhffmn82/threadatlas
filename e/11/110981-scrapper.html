Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1 Queen Cross Patch Flits

The Chief Scrapper reacts crossly to the Queen's going to pieces and says that finding a new ruler will interrupt the busiest season. He helps sweep up and store the Queen, carries the gold shears, cuts the golden spool from its tree, recites the succession command, and follows the spool toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 2 The New Queen Of the Quilties

Scrapper follows the Spool of Succession with Piecer into the Emerald City and recognizes the Patchwork Girl as a possible queen. He helps seize Scraps, carry her in the rag bag, direct the foot-path to Patch, and present her to the Quilties.

CHAPTER 3 Duties Of the Quilty Queen

Scrapper tells Scraps that the Queen must do all the work in the palace and explains that the royal Spool of Succession chose her. He encourages her anger, saying that Patch’s queens must be good scolders, and helps Piecer force her to work.

CHAPTER 9 Queen Scraps Meets Peter

Scrapper brings Peter and Ruggedo to the old Quilty woman so that Ruggedo’s cloak can be mended. He treats Ruggedo as an honored customer, ignores Scraps’s warnings, and accepts Peter as payment after Ruggedo calls him a slave. After Ruggedo vanishes, Scrapper becomes upset but still leaves Peter locked in the room.
