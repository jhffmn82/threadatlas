Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

The temple of Hera in Platæa is notable for its size and the beauty of its statues. It contains statues of Rhea and Hera, including a standing Hera called Full-Grown and a seated Hera called the Bride.
