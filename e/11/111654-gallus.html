Gallus in Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Gallus, a Roman Senator, is sent to Greece to arbitrate Lacedæmonian-Argive disputes. He treats Greeks, especially both parties, with extreme contempt and is empowered to release as many towns as he wishes from the Achæan League.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Gallus was sent to Greece to arbitrate the disputes between the Lacedæmonians and the Argives. He treated the Greeks, especially the Lacedæmonians and Argives, with great contempt, and was authorized to release towns from the Achæan League.
