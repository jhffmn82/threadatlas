BOOK X.--PHOCIS. / CHAPTER I.

The battle was commanded for the Phocians by Rhœus of Ambrosus and Daiphantes of Hyampolis, with Tellias of Elis as commander in chief. The Phocians fought in desperation after preparing for the possible destruction of their women, children, and possessions, and they won a famous victory.
