the Montreal riot of November 6 in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The Montreal riot of November 6 was a disturbance in Montreal that began with conflict between the Doric Club and the Fils de la Liberte; both sides were blamed, the Riot Act was read, and troops were called before it ended.
