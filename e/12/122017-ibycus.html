Ibycus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Ibycus is a pious poet gifted by Apollo with song and honeyed lips, killed by two robbers while traveling to Corinth; cranes he invokes lead his murderers to reveal themselves.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVIII

Ibycus was a pious poet traveling to the contests at the Isthmus of Corinth when two robbers attacked and killed him. Before dying, he called on the cranes overhead to take up his cause; later, his murderers revealed themselves when cranes flew over a theater.
