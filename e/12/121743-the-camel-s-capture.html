the camel's capture in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

The Comfortable Camel's capture is performed by Tuzzle and slaves for the Sultan. It begins when a thick rope is looped around Camy's neck; he is dragged into the water, towed behind the Royal Sampan to Samandra's golden dock, brought ashore, and forced toward the Royal Camel Quarters.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER ELEVEN

The Comfortable Camel's capture begins when slaves from Tuzzle's boat throw a thick rope around his long neck. He is dragged into the water and hauled aboard or away by the royal barge while Sir Hokus and Peter are left on the riverbank.

CHAPTER TWELVE

Tuzzle captures Camy and has him towed through the water behind the Royal Sampan. The slaves row the boat to the golden dock in Samandra, where Camy is brought ashore and forced toward the Royal Camel Quarters.
