Chapter XVIII in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Chapter XVIII presents legendary accounts of Arion, Ibycus, Simonides, and Sappho, tracing their lives, deaths or escapes, their influence on later poets, and the survival of only fragments of some works.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVIII

Chapter XVIII concerns Arion, Ibycus, Simonides, and Sappho, presenting legendary accounts of their lives and deaths or escapes. It also emphasizes the importance of their influence on later poets and notes that only fragments of some of their works remain.
