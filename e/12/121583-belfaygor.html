Belfaygor of Baffleburg in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Belfaygor of Baffleburg is a baron imprisoned in a pirate’s sack. Jack seeks the Red Jinn’s help to free him; after the forbidden flagon reduces him to a midget and his enchanted beard vanishes, he emerges, reunites with Shirley Sunshine, and marries her amid gifts and toasts.

Belfaygor in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Belfaygor of Bourne is a good Baron of Baffleburg, Lord of the Land, ruler of his palace and lands, and bridegroom engaged to Princess Shirley Sunshine. What most defines him is his famous enchanted red beard, which grows continuously and has become so enormous that his men abandon him, it trips him, and it prevents him from rescuing Shirley. He carries shears to cut it, though cutting it hurts, and at one point disguises himself with false whiskers. Introduced unable to act because of the beard, Belfaygor makes Jack Pumpkinhead its Royal Bearer and explains that the enchantment forced him to flee his palace. He joins Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif on the journey toward Baffleburg and Mogodore, though he loses confidence at Mogodore's mountain. His wish to save Shirley makes him repeatedly turn the beard from burden into aid: he lends it as a crossing line, lets Jack trim it at a cave, uses it to cross a chasm and climb a cliff, and anchors it at a tower window so the others can enter Baffleburg. Belfaygor confronts Mogodore and demands Shirley's release, but Mogodore recognizes him and has him locked in a high dungeon. Peter and Snif find and free him by following his enchanted beard. Belfaygor then helps navigate the lost labyrinth, captures a guard in his beard, gives Peter his shears to obtain the Forbidden Flagon, and cuts the beard shorter. He continues toward the Emerald City, hoping the Wizard of Oz can repair the beard and wanting to attack Mogodore before the pirate's sack opens. In Swing City his beard is used as a tightrope; after escape, he follows the others. While pursuing Mogodore, he is captured with Peter by the falling pirate's sack.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 6

Belfaygor of Bourne is introduced as the lord of the lands and a good baron. He has been abandoned by his men because of his enormous beard, and he cannot rescue the princess he was to marry because the beard trips him. He appoints Jack Pumpkinhead Royal Bearer of the beard after Jack suggests throwing it over one shoulder.

CHAPTER 7

Belfaygor explains that his beard-growing enchantment caused his red beard to grow uncontrollably, forcing him to escape his palace with shears. He joins Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif on their journey to Baffleburg, but loses confidence when they reach Mogodore's mountain.

CHAPTER 8

Belfaygor agrees to lend his beard for the crossing, even though cutting it will hurt, because he wants to save Shirley Sunshine. He sleeps with his head outside the cave while Jack trims the beard to keep it from blocking the entrance.

CHAPTER 10

Belfaygor uses his beard as a rope to cross the chasm and scale the cliff. He enters Baffleburg by plunging headfirst through a low tower window while the towers are firing spears, then anchors his beard to the window sill so the others can climb up. He follows Peter down the tower's spiral stairway.

CHAPTER 11

Belfaygor demands that Mogodore reveal what he has done with Princess Shirley Sunshine and declares that she should be released. Mogodore identifies him despite his false whiskers and says Belfaygor will not marry Shirley. Belfaygor is seized and locked in a dungeon higher in the tower.

CHAPTER 12

Belfaygor is freed from the tower after Peter and Snif reach his cell by following his enchanted beard. He accompanies them through Mogodore's castle and into the lost labyrinth, planning to use his beard to guide them back.

CHAPTER 13

Belfaygor leads Peter and Snif through the labyrinth by always turning left, then captures the enchanted cavern's guard by wrapping him in his red beard. He gives Peter his shears so a piece of the beard can pull the Forbidden Flagon from the fire fountain. Afterward, he cuts his beard shorter and travels with the others toward the Emerald City.

CHAPTER 14

Belfaygor wants to attack Mogodore before the pirate's sack is opened and hopes the Wizard of Oz can repair his growing beard. In Swing City, the Swingers stretch his beard between swings and use it as a tightrope, while he is held by the citizens. After escaping, he cuts a hole in the net and follows the others to the ground.

CHAPTER 15

Belfaygor travels with Peter, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Snif while pursuing Mogodore. He urges Peter to throw the flagon, but the falling pirate's sack captures him along with Peter.
