Siva in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Siva, the third person of the Hindu triad, personifies destruction but is particularly associated with regeneration in the Puranas. Having more worshippers than Brahma or Vishnu, his followers form one of two major Hindu sects.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXX

Siva is the third person of the Hindu triad and represents destruction, though in the Puranas he is more particularly associated with regeneration. He has more worshippers and a wider worship than Brahma, and his followers form one of the two major Hindu sects.
