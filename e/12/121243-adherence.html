Adherence in RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Robert Friel · 2025-01-16

Adherence is a generator quality metric that detects hallucinations in RAG responses by measuring whether a response is grounded in its provided context. Also called answer faithfullness, groundednes, or attribution in the literature, it requires every response part to be supported. At the example level, it is a boolean: all response sentences must be supported; annotations also exist at response, sentence, span, and token levels. Supported tokens enable granular evaluation, while disagreements between response- and sentence-level assessments flag examples for re-annotation. Extra annotation measures can improve relevance, utilization, quality, and stability. In reported evaluation on DelucionQA, GPT-4 labels achieved high example- and span-level F1 and accuracy. It can also be estimated on response tokens by a fine-tuned DeBERTa model.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

TRACe Evaluation Framework .. Adherence

Adherence detects hallucinations by measuring whether the response is grounded in the context. At the example level, it is defined as a boolean indicating whether all parts of the response are supported by the context; supported response tokens can also enable granular evaluation.

RAGBench Statistics .. Alignment with Human Judgements

Adherence is labeled by identifying which response sentences are supported by the provided context, with an example-level boolean label assigned when all response sentences are supported. GPT-4's Adherence labels achieve an F1 of 0.96 and accuracy of 0.93 at the example level, and an F1 of 0.97 and accuracy of 0.95 at the span level, on DelucionQA.

RAG Case Study .. Estimating Context Relevance is Difficult

Adherence is a TRACe metric estimated on response tokens by the fine-tuned DeBERTa model. GPT-3.5-judge, RAGAS, and TruLens also evaluate adherence or faithfulness-related response behavior.

Annotation Post-Processing Steps

Adherence is assessed at both the response and sentence levels. Conflicts between these levels are used to identify examples for re-annotation.
