Medical College for Women, Edinburgh in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Medical College for Women was a school of medicine for women in Edinburgh, promoted in 1891 by Elsie Inglis and other women students; Elsie was more closely associated with its fortunes than with the Edinburgh School of Medicine for Women.
