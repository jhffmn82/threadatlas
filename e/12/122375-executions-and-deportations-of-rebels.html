executions and deportations of rebels in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

During the second rebellion in Lower Canada, nearly one hundred lesser ringleaders were tried; twelve, including Hindenlang, were executed and most others sent to Australia, a response characterized by commendable moderation.
