Podaleirius in The Fall of Troy

Quintus Smyrnaeus · 0200~/0299~

Podaleirius is a physician, healer, and warrior, brother of Machaon and son of Asclepius. He tends wounded men and swiftly heals Philoctetes with salves. After learning Machaon has been struck down, he mourns, arms himself in anger, enters battle, and kills Cleitus and Lassus.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

By this to Podaleirius tidings came

Podaleirius receives news that his brother has been struck down while he is ministering to wounded men beside the ships. He arms himself in anger, enters the battle, and kills Cleitus and Lassus.

At last by Calchas' counsel Achaea's sons

Podaleirius, skilled in healing, treated Philoctetes when he reached the Achaean host. By spreading salves on the wound and invoking his physician-father, he made Philoctetes whole and sound.
