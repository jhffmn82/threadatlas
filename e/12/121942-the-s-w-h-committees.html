the S.W.H. Committees in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The S.W.H. Committees were strong, organized committees of women from Britain managing Scottish Women’s Hospitals. While Dr. Inglis served abroad, they carried the main burden of finance and administration: selecting personnel, supervising immense equipment and motor-transport shipments, dealing with the British Government, supporting Kossovo Day, and receiving reports from the Serbian mission.
