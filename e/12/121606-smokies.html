Narrative, section by section

CHAPTER 2

The Smokies emerge from the chimneys in Chimneyville as smoky figures with spark-like eyes. They surround Peter and Jack, threaten to throw them down the chimneys, and fill the square with a suffocating cloud, but they cannot overpower the travelers and are confused by Jack's lantern light.
