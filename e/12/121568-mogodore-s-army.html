Mogodore's army in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

Mogodore's army, one thousand spear-bearing men serving Mogodore, enters the Emerald City, divides to hold it and storm the palace, and captures, chains, and imprisons its people.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 17

Mogodore's army enters the Emerald City and drives its inhabitants into their homes. Five hundred soldiers remain to hold the city, while five hundred accompany Mogodore to attack the palace; they capture and chain the palace's inhabitants and imprison its servants.
