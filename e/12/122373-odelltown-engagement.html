Odelltown engagement in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Odelltown engagement was a battle in the second rebellion: rebel forces attacked about two hundred loyal militia under Lieutenant-Colonel Taylor at the Methodist church, but loyalist sallies and a flank attack defeated them after about two and a half hours, leaving fifty rebels dead.

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

The rebels attacked about two hundred loyal militia under Lieutenant-Colonel Taylor at the Methodist church. Loyalist sallies and a flank attack defeated them; the rebels left fifty dead, while the loyalists lost one officer and five men.
