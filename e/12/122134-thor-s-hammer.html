Thor's hammer in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Thor's weapon is kept ready against Skrymir, strikes a mountain to form three deep glens, then breaks Hrungnir's stone club and is buried in Hrungnir's skull.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XXXII

Thor keeps his mallet ready when Skrymir's snoring and the earthquake frighten the travelers. He uses it three times against Skrymir, whose blows fall on a mountain and make three deep glens, and later throws it at Hrungnir's stone club, breaks the club, and buries the mallet in Hrungnir's skull.
