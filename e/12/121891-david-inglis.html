David Inglis in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

David Inglis, Alexander’s son and Elsie Inglis’s grandfather, was raised in Scotland by his uncle William Inglis. He joined the East India Company as a writer in India in 1798, served in the Mahratta War in 1803, and administered the Peshwa’s territorial cessions under the Treaty of Bassein. He married Martha Money in 1806, later entered business in Bombay, and returned to England in 1812, living in Walthamstow. The records also identify him as a son of John Inglis and as a husband in John Inglis’s marriage counsel.
