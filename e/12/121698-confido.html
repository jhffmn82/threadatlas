Confido in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

Confido is the Sultan of Samandra’s Imperial Peke, a little dog also called the Imperial Houndling, later Princess Marygolden’s official pet. The Sultan believes he knows everything and tells nothing; he values chicken hearts, cream, chicken, care, freedom, and rewards. Speedy carries him from the palace. At first Confido refuses to reveal the Sultan’s magic-date enchantment, but after Marygolden promises food and freedom he speaks to her and directs the dates’ use. His instructions restore the Corumbians and castle, explain how to restore the Yellow Knight, and establish that the Princess of Corabia awaits a Knight’s grand test. He misses the Sultan, yet stays with Marygolden if carried and fed. After the Sultan is removed, he rests in her arms, dreaming of rewards.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER SIXTEEN

Confido is the Sultan's Imperial Peke, believed by the Sultan to know everything and tell nothing. Speedy carries him away from the palace, and although he first refuses to explain the enchantment, he agrees to whisper the secret of the magic dates to Marygolden after she promises him food and freedom from the Sultan.

CHAPTER SEVENTEEN

Confido directs the use of the magic dates but initially refuses to help unless promised rewards. He explains how to restore the Corumbians and castle and later tells the Queen how to restore the Yellow Knight; the King and Queen promise him whatever reward he desires.

CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

Confido says that he misses the Sultan but agrees to remain with Marygolden if she carries him and provides cream and chicken. He explains the effects of the remaining dates and tells the group that the Princess of Corabia cannot be restored until a Knight wins the grand test.

CHAPTER TWENTY

Confido identifies himself as important and is named the Imperial Peke of Samandra and the official pet of Princess Marygolden. He rests in Marygolden's arms after the Sultan is removed and dreams of the rewards he will demand.
