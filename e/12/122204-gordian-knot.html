Gordian Knot in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Gordian Knot is the fast knot binding Gordius's wagon; prophecy promised its untier lordship of Asia, until Alexander the Great cut it after failing to untie it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IV

The Gordian Knot was the fast knot with which Gordius tied his wagon. It was said that whoever untied it would become lord of all Asia, but Alexander the Great cut it with his sword after failing to untie it.
