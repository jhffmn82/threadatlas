Physician recommendations are a topic concerning how patients are directed to suitable departments or practitioners, often through manual scheduler triage. In web-based medical services, they face personalization challenges. The RAGPR study targets more accurate, reliable, personalized, and contextually relevant recommendations by generating them from retrieved queries and associated physician information. Integrating retrieval-augmented generation for enhanced personalized physician recommendations in web-based medical serv

Personalized physician recommendations were the main target of the RAGPR model. The study concluded that the model could improve their accuracy and personalization.

1 Introduction

Physician recommendations are often produced manually by schedulers who direct patients to suitable departments or practitioners. The unit proposes evaluating Retrieval-Augmented Generation as a way to make these recommendations more accurate, reliable, personalized, and contextually relevant.

2 Methods .. 2.5 Baseline experiments

Physician recommendation was generated from retrieved queries and associated physician information. Three staff members involved in triaging rated the match between the physician and the query on a 5-point Likert scale, and the Mann–Whitney U test was used to examine statistically significant differences.

3 Results / 3.1 Data set summary .. 3.4 Rationality evaluation of LLMs

Physician recommendations were evaluated using the precision, recall, and F1-scores of Mistral, GPT-4o-mini, and GPT-4o. GPT-4o achieved the highest F1-score at 0.95, while Mistral achieved 0.94 and GPT-4o-mini achieved 0.92.

5 Conclusion

Physician recommendations may be improved through the potential integration of the RAGPR method into healthcare website systems.
