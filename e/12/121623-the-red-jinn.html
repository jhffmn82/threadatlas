the Red Jinn in Jack Pumpkinhead of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1929

The Red Jinn is a sovereign with a vase-like body and red head who rules a red glass palace. He owns magical items and treats Jack Pumpkinhead as amusement until hearing his story. He repairs Jack, advises him, and considers using his looking-glass, umbrella, and Jinrickasha to aid a rescue and return him to Oz, but is frustrated when Jack vanishes by other magic.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 16

The Red Jinn rules the red glass palace and initially treats Jack Pumpkinhead as a source of amusement. After hearing Jack's story, he repairs Jack's broken leg and considers using his magic looking-glass, umbrella, and Jinrickasha to help rescue the prisoners and return Jack to Oz. He becomes frustrated when Jack vanishes through magic not caused by him.
