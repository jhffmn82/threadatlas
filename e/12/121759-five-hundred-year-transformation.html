five-hundred-year transformation in The Yellow Knight of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1930

A five-hundred-year transformation caused by magic dates reshapes an entire kingdom, including its King and subjects. Speedy and Sir Hokus trace the enchantment to the Comfortable Camel, seek to reverse it, and the Sultan is sentenced to restore stolen treasures and remain within Samandra for five hundred years.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER FIFTEEN

Speedy concludes that the magic dates may have been used to transform an entire kingdom, including its King and subjects. Sir Hokus agrees that the dates could explain the enchantments, and the group sets out to find the Comfortable Camel and reverse the harm.

CHAPTER TWENTY

The Sultan transformed the rulers and their children for five hundred years and stole their treasures. The rulers sentence him to return what he stole and remain within Samandra for five hundred years.
