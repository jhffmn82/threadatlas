At Epidaurus, celebrated oracle of Aesculapius, sick people seek answers and health by temple sleep, with treatment reported to resemble Animal Magnetism or Mesmerism.

MYTH OF OSIRIS AND ISIS

The oracle of Aesculapius was consulted by sick people who slept in the temple seeking both answers and recovery. The reported treatment was inferred to resemble Animal Magnetism or Mesmerism.
