Scottish Federation of Suffrage Societies in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

The Scottish Federation of Suffrage Societies was a federation carefully organised to coordinate Scottish suffrage societies and pressure Parliament. With Dr. Elsie Inglis as Honorary Secretary, it was strongly influenced by her policy from 1906 to 1914. In October 1914 it formally adopted the hospital scheme, chose the name Scottish Women’s Hospitals, and redirected suffrage organisations’ resources to wartime medical service as its final organisational scheme.
