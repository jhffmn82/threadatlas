Galatz in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Galatz is the fallback place for the hospitals. After equipment is recovered from a refugee train, it is brought safely there. The unit operates a hospital in a school, works through the night, later connects with Reni, and sends a messenger to fetch General Kropensky during a spy incident.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

’ ‘IN AN AMBULANCE TRAIN, ‘NEAR ODESSA, _Jan.

At Galatz, the unit operates a hospital in a school and later works in connection with Reni. Elsie recalls that the unit worked through the night there and that an armoured car section sent a messenger from Galatz to fetch General Kropensky during the spy incident.

’ CHAPTER XI THE MOORINGS CUT ‘Not I, but my Unit.

Galatz was the place to which the hospitals were ordered to fall back. The unit's equipment was brought safely there after Miss Borrowman and Miss Brown followed trucks that had been carried away with a refugee train.
