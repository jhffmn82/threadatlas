Sona in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Sona was an Indian ayah and long-serving household nurse to the Inglis family for more than thirty years. During the 1857 crisis, she accompanied some family members to England. She later became a Christian and was baptized in 1877. By the end of the account, she was regarded as one of the pillars of the Indian Christian Church in Lucknow.

Sona was an Indian ayah who served the Inglis family for more than thirty years and accompanied some of them to England during the 1857 crisis. She became a Christian, was baptized in 1877, and was later regarded as one of the pillars of the Indian Christian Church in Lucknow.
