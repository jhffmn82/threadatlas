[Enter SECOND MESSENGER.]

The house of Labdacus is said to bear blood-stains and hidden ills that cannot be washed away. After Jocasta’s death and Oedipus’s blinding, the house is overwhelmed by woe, ruin, death, disgrace, and every named evil.

[Enter ISMENE]

Oedipus's sons initially wished to leave the throne to Creon in order to remove the long-standing curse infecting their race. Their rivalry has instead intensified the danger associated with that curse.
