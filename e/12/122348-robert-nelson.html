Robert Nelson in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Robert Nelson, brother of Wolfred Nelson, proclaimed Canada’s independence during a second rebellion, became president of a provisional government, and led Canadian refugees in an armed expedition from Napierville. After defeat at Odelltown, he fled to New York, where he practised surgery until his death.

Narrative, section by section

' {117} CHAPTER XI THE SECOND REBELLION The frigate _Inconstant_, with Lord Durham on board, was not two days out from Quebec when rebellion broke out anew in Lower Canada.

He proclaimed the independence of Canada and became president of the provisional government. He led refugees into Lower Canada, directed the movement from Napierville, and then fled across the American border after the defeat at Odelltown.
