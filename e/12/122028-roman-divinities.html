ROMAN DIVINITIES

Roman divinities described in this unit include gods and goddesses associated with agriculture, war, boundaries, childbirth, hearths, gates, households, and personal protection. Some are identified as peculiar to Roman mythology, while others have names or counterparts connected with Grecian divinities.
