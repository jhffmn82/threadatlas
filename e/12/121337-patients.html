Patients are a group with varying healthcare needs, making it difficult to identify suitable medical professionals. Across 646,383 consultation records, 67.05% are female, 32.95% male, and the largest age group is 20–39 years (54.6%). 1 Introduction

Patients have varying needs that make it difficult to identify suitable medical professionals. They may receive preliminary assessments and provider recommendations from symptom checker tools or personalized systems using patient history, preferences, and demographic data.

3 Results / 3.1 Data set summary .. 3.4 Rationality evaluation of LLMs

Patients account for 646,383 consultation records, with 67.05% female and 32.95% male patients. The largest age group is 20–39 years, representing 54.6% of the records.

4 Discussion / 4.1 Principal findings .. 4.5 Limitations and future directions

Patients may benefit from improved consultation allocation, reduced wait times, and more accurate recommendations. Their histories, imaging data, and real-time physiological signals could be incorporated into future multimodal systems, while their confidential data must be protected.

Patients are assisted by the RAGPR model in identifying medical professionals best suited to address their specific healthcare needs.
