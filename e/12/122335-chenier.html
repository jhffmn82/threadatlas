Chenier in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

Chenier, a courageous young physician of St Eustache, supported Amury Girod and led the 1837 Lower Canada rebellion. Unlike other leaders, he fought to the last ditch, refused surrender, and was killed escaping the church during the engagement.

Dr J. O. Chenier was a courageous young physician who supported Amury Girod and helped lead the rebels at St Eustache. He refused to surrender and was killed while escaping from the church during the engagement.
