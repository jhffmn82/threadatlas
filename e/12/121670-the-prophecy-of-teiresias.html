Teiresias’s prophecy warns Creon he will pay with his child’s life for entombing Antigone and leaving Polyneices unburied; it unfolds through Haemon’s death, Eurydice’s suicide, and lamentation.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

[Enter TEIRESIAS and BOY]

Teiresias’s prophecy says that Creon will give the life of his own child in payment for entombing Antigone alive and leaving Polyneices unburied. It is fulfilled through Haemon’s death and is followed by further lamentation in Creon’s house.
