Remus in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Remus, Rhea Silvia’s twin son by Mars and Romulus’s brother, is exposed, rescued and educated, then helps avenge Amulius and found Rome.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NISUS AND EURYALUS

Remus is born to Rhea Silvia and is exposed on the Tiber with Romulus. A herdsman rescues and educates him, and he later helps take revenge on Amulius and founds Rome.
