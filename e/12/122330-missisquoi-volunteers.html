Missisquoi Volunteers in The 'Patriotes' of '37

Alfred D. Decelles · 1916

The Missisquoi Volunteers, a well-armed force of at least three hundred men commanded by Captain Kemp at Moore's Corners, escorted arms, armed local loyalists, and defeated invading Patriotes without casualties.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

{89} But the end of rebellion was not yet in sight.

The Missisquoi Volunteers, commanded at Moore's Corners by Captain Kemp, escorted a convoy of arms and ammunition. After local loyalists were armed, they defeated the invading Patriote force without suffering casualties.
