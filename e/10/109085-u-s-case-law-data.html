Introduction

U.S. case law data is used to build CLERC. The dataset is described as the largest Legal IR and RAG dataset using this type of data, with a pipeline for converting raw data into task formats.

6

Case law data is described as a high-quality resource for building intelligence systems to assist legal professionals.

Ethical Considerations

Case law data is used to build legal intelligence systems, creating a risk that historical biases and ideologies will be propagated.
