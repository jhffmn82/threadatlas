VisDoMRAG / 5.1 .. 5.2

Consistency constraint prompting directs the LLM to compare reasoning chains from the textual and visual pipelines. When inconsistencies occur, the LLM may reevaluate evidence or adjust reasoning steps to reconcile the differences.
