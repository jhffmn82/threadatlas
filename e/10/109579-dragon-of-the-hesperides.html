dragon of the Hesperides in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

An immortal, hundred-headed dragon, offspring of Typhon and Echidna, uses many varied voices to guard the golden apples; after eight labours had been performed, it is described in this role.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

When the labours had been performed in eight

The dragon of the Hesperides was an immortal offspring of Typhon and Echidna. It had one hundred heads and used many different and varied voices to guard the golden apples.
