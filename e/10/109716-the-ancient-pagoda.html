The ancient pagoda stands in a grove of camphor and Flame-of-the-Forest, its faded lilac shaft ruinously overgrown with trees and shrubs. It hosts the tiffin gathering, and its tower provides Rudolph and Mrs. Forrester with a high view of the surrounding countryside before Heywood interrupts them.
