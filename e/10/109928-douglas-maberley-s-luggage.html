THE ADVENTURE OF THE THREE GABLES

Douglas Maberley's trunks and cases, labeled 'Milano' and 'Lucerne,' arrived at The Three Gables the previous week and remain unpacked. Holmes identifies their recent arrival as a possible explanation for the sudden interest in the house and orders Mrs. Maberley to examine them.
