General de Merville in The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1921-10

General de Merville, a military general of Khyber fame and Violet’s father, changes from a strong soldier into a demoralized, broken man, unable to effectively oppose Gruner over her intended marriage.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE ILLUSTRIOUS CLIENT

General de Merville is Violet's father and a former strong soldier who has become demoralized and unable to oppose Gruner effectively. He is not the unnamed client, although his distress over Violet's proposed marriage is central to the situation.
