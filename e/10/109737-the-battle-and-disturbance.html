the battle and disturbance in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

A brief, violent attack by men from a neighboring hamlet begins when they hurl missiles at a duel. Europeans counterattack, disperse the rioters, and leave Nesbit and Rudolph wounded. The disturbance expands across the region, causing fires and attacks on houses and buildings. Amid crowds, weapons, shouts, smoke, gunfire, explosions, rockets, and alarms, Rudolph, Wutzler, Bertha, Chantel, and others struggle to escape or survive through fighting around several sites and a defended compound.

" CHAPTER IX PASSAGE AT ARMS "Boy.

The riot begins when men from the neighboring hamlet attack the duel with missiles. The Europeans counterattack and quickly disperse the rioters, though Nesbit and Rudolph are left wounded.

CHAPTER XVI THE GUNWALE Rudolph's mission began quietly, with a glimpse which he afterward recalled as incredibly peaceful.

The uprising spreads through the region with crowds, weapons, shouts, gunfire, smoke, and explosions. Houses and other buildings are attacked while Rudolph, Bertha, Chantel, Wutzler, and Rudolph's friends struggle to escape or survive.

CHAPTER XVII LAMP OF HEAVEN The white men peered from among the oil-jars, like two of the Forty Thieves.

The battle and disturbance fill the area with smoke, gunfire, rockets, gongs, horns, conches, and running combatants. Fighting occurs near the oil-jars, the clay-pit, the go-down, and the defended compound, while Rudolph and Wutzler attempt to cross the chaos.
