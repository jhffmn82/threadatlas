the spring in From Sand Hill to Pine

The spring is initially an unremarkable niche in the mountainside. Parkhurst finds a landslide, a crack, and gold-bearing decomposed quartz near it, after which the partners work and stake the surrounding ground.

That night Bray slept but little.

The spring is where Eugenia had a schoolboy leave her note for Bray. Bray went there for water, but failed to notice the note while searching around the mountain, and one of his partners found it instead.
