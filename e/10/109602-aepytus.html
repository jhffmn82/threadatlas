Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE LIBRARY, II. vir. 2

Aepytus is Merope's third son and is raised by his maternal grandfather. When he reaches manhood, he secretly returns, kills Polyphontes, and recovers his ancestral kingdom.
