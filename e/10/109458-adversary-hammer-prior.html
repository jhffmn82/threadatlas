adversary-hammer prior in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Estimation of Source Reliability

Jeongyeon Hwang, Junyoung Park, Hyejin Park, Dongwoo Kim, Sangdon Park, Jungseul Ok · 2025

The adversary-hammer prior is a discrete, extreme reliability prior for worst-case evaluation and reliability-estimation distortion. It assigns low reliability to adversaries and higher reliability to a hammer, with four adversaries and one hammer. Its results appear in Table A4 and, with Llama2-7B, assess accuracy as adversaries increase from one to seven across Natural Questions, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Experiments / Experimental setups .. Ablation studies and analysis

The adversary-hammer prior assigns source reliability to either 0.1 for adversaries or 0.9 for hammers. It creates an extreme reliability distribution for worst-case evaluation, and RA-RAG remains robust as the number of adversarial sources increases.

C

The adversary-hammer prior is used to illustrate distortion in reliability estimation. It contains four adversaries with ri = 0.1 and one hammer with ri = 0.6.

G

It is used to evaluate accuracy across one to seven adversaries for Natural Questions, TriviaQA, and HotpotQA.
