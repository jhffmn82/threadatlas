the quiet, observant passenger in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

A quiet, cynically observant passenger rides in a coach. When it jolts to a stop, he reassures a lady that there is no immediate danger, indirectly criticizes the expressman for leaving his post, initially refuses to go to the cabin, and later joins the road-clearing party.
