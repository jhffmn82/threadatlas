the stage robbery in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

The stage robbery is a robbery on a road targeting Yuba Bill’s stagecoach, its express box, and the greenbacks it carries. In one evening, two linked thefts occur: two supposed scouts secretly take the treasure from inside the box, then masked, armed men stop the coach, take the box, and let it continue. The later hold-up is believed connected to passengers and the gang. Afterward, local press gives extensive, praise-filled coverage to Bill, the expressman, passengers, and a lady passenger, while calling for a Vigilance Committee.

The stage robbery involves masked and armed men stopping Yuba Bill's coach, taking the express box, and allowing the coach to continue. The robbery is later revealed to have been preceded by the theft of the treasure from inside the box, possibly by passengers connected with the gang.

II The robbery furnished the usual amount of copy for the local press.

The robbery receives extensive praise-filled coverage in the local press, including commendations of Yuba Bill, the young expressman, the passengers, and a lady passenger. The article also calls for a Vigilance Committee, though local readers apparently think the wealthy express companies can protect their own property.
