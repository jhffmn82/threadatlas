CLERC in CLERC: A Dataset for U. S. Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Analysis Generation

Abe Bohan Hou · 2025

CLERC, the Case Law Evaluation and Retrieval Corpus, is a colossal dataset of historical U.S. federal case law, built with legal professionals for training and evaluating models. It supports two backbone tasks: information retrieval, including case and legal citation retrieval, and retrieval-augmented legal analysis generation. The dataset contains 25.5 million document and passage instances: 1.84 million documents, over 20.7 million citations, 23.7 million retrieval passages, and 6,000 generation passages. It provides long case documents, chunked passages for retrieval, and passages for legal-analysis generation, along with task formats and evaluations. Its retrieval collection combines documents and passages, while CLERC/generation includes a 1,000-instance test set. The records present a summary-judgment query with citation masking, including single-removed views that mask a central citation and salient sentence and all-removed views that mask all citations while retaining statutory references. Overall, CLERC unfolds from a large U.S. case-law corpus into structured retrieval and generation resources for long-context legal tasks.

Abstract

CLERC, the Case Law Evaluation and Retrieval Corpus, is a colossal dataset created with legal professionals. It supports training and evaluation for information retrieval and retrieval-augmented generation.

Introduction

CLERC is built as a dataset of U.S. case law to support information retrieval and generative language models for legal documents. It contains 25.5 million instances and provides task formats and evaluations for case retrieval and retrieval-augmented legal analysis generation.

Related Work .. 2.3

CLERC is presented through a query concerning summary judgment and citation masking. Its data views include single-removed, which masks the central citation and salient sentence, and all-removed, which masks all citations while retaining statutory references.

3 / 3.1 .. Input Preparation

CLERC contains long case documents, chunked passages for retrieval, and passages for generating legal analyses. Its final dataset includes 1.84 million documents, more than 20.7 million citations, 23.7 million retrieval passages, and 6,000 generation passages.

4 .. 4.2.2

CLERC is evaluated for legal citation retrieval and legal analysis generation. Its retrieval collection combines documents and passages, and its generation subset contains 1,000 test instances.

6

CLERC is built through an interdisciplinary effort with legal professionals. It is designed for training and evaluating models on long-context case retrieval and analysis generation tasks.

Ethical Considerations

The dataset is curated based on a corpus of historical U.S. federal case law.
