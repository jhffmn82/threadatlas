Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

THE ADVENTURE OF THE SUSSEX VAMPIRE

Holmes and Watson discuss traditional vampires as walking corpses held in their graves by stakes, as well as the belief that living people may drink blood. Holmes initially rejects the supernatural explanation but agrees to examine the reported case.

It was evening of a dull, foggy November day when, having left our bags

Vampirism is initially suspected because Ferguson saw his wife rise from beside the baby's cot with blood on her lips. Holmes rejects the supernatural explanation and shows that she was sucking poison from the child's wound.
