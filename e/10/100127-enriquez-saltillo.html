Enriquez Saltillo in From Sand Hill to Pine

Bret Harte · 1894-04-24

Enriquez Saltillo, a scion of an old Spanish-California family and Tiburcio’s cousin, knows the Spanish population and its lasso legend. After mysterious attacks, he tells Mr. Grey they may be linked to a condemned Spanish friar’s spirit returning to swing a lasso, without stating belief.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

He was still more concerned a week later, on returning unexpectedly one afternoo

Enriquez Saltillo tells Mr. Grey that the mysterious attacks may be connected to an old legend about a Spanish friar condemned to return as a spirit and swing a lasso. He says that the legend is known among his people but does not clearly state whether he believes it.
