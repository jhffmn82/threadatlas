the neighboring hamlet men in Dragon's Blood

Henry Milner Rideout · 1909

The neighboring hamlet men gather to watch foreigners fence, then riot by throwing cocoanuts, sticks, clay, and plaster before fleeing a European charge. Later, as rioters, they search Rudolph's go-down, seize and persecute a Christian captive for recantation, prepare the Lamp of Heaven, and flee after rifle fire kills or disables some.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

" CHAPTER IX PASSAGE AT ARMS "Boy.

The neighboring hamlet men gather at the breach in the compound wall to watch the foreigners fence. They begin a riot by throwing cocoanuts, sticks, clay, and plaster, but scatter and flee when the Europeans charge.

CHAPTER XVII LAMP OF HEAVEN The white men peered from among the oil-jars, like two of the Forty Thieves.

The rioters move through the smoke, search Rudolph's go-down, and capture the Christian captive. They threaten and beat him, prepare the Lamp of Heaven, and flee when rifle fire kills or disables some of their number.
