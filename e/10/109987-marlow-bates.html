THE PROBLEM OF THOR BRIDGE

Marlow Bates, manager of Gibson's estate, warns Holmes that Neil Gibson is a villain who treated his wife brutally. He says he has given Gibson notice and is leaving his employment, but states that he does not know how Gibson's wife died.
