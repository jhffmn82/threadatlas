SafeRAG Dataset Construction

Attack contexts are generated or modified texts used to test RAG security. They include silver noise contexts, contradictory inter-context conflict texts, soft ad texts containing attack keywords, and white DoS texts containing safety warnings.

Threat Framework: Attacks on the

Malicious contexts are selected from an attack source and injected into one or more stages of the RAG pipeline. They are intended to influence the system while preserving the appearance of normal operation.
