  • In The Marvelous Land of Oz, Glinda's palace is the travelers' intended destination, though they never reach it, and the Woggle-Bug compares their mountain nest with it as a worse prison.
  • In The Emerald City of Oz, Glinda's castle is a stately residence and the destination of Ozma and her companions, where Glinda greets them and explains how she will protect the Land of Oz.
  • In The Scarecrow of Oz, Glinda's palace is the travelers' destination, receives them overnight in its Azure Reception Room, and is protected by Glinda's magic.
  • In The Lost Princess of Oz, the castle of Glinda the Good stands in Quadling Country and contains her drawing room, Room of Magic, and guarded Great Book of Records until a thief removes the book, magical instruments, and chemical compounds.
  • In The Tin Woodman of Oz, Glinda's castle is Glinda's residence in the Quadling Country and the destination travelers intend to reach by traveling south through Munchkin Country.
