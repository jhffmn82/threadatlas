people of the Emerald City in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The people of the Emerald City have green skin, clothes, carts, and pennies, and are happy and prosperous. They watch Oz depart by balloon, accept Scarecrow as ruler, and lovingly remember and grieve for Oz.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XVII

The people come to watch Oz launch the balloon after hearing that he will visit a great brother Wizard in the clouds. They obey Oz's command to accept the Scarecrow as ruler, and they later remember Oz lovingly and grieve over his departure.
