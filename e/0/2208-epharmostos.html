Epharmostos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Epharmostos of Opous is a wrestler whose chief achievement is winning at Olympia. He accumulates victories at the Isthmus, Nemea, Argos, Athens, Marathon, Pellene, Eleusis, the assembly of Lykaian Zeus, and the tomb of Iolaos, ending by crowning the altar of Aias Oileus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

Epharmostos of Opous won the wrestling-match at Olympia. He also gained victories at the Isthmus, Nemea, Argos, Athens, Marathon, the assembly of Lykaian Zeus, Pellene, the tomb of Iolaos, and Eleusis, and at the end he crowned the altar of Aias Oileus.
