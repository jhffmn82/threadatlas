chariot in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

The chariot, provided by Nestor and drawn by fleet horses, carries Telemachus with Pisistratus driving from Pylos through Pherae toward Lacedaemon.

Nestor orders that Telemachus be sent to Lacedaemon in a chariot. Pisistratus drives the chariot, which carries Telemachus from Pylos to Pherae and onward through the open country.
