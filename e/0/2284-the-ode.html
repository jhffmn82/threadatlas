the ode in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

A Pythian ode sent from Thebes to Syracuse, celebrating Hieron’s chariot victory and protection of the Lokrians, then praising sincerity and condemning flattery, slander, envy, and deceit.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

II.

The ode is sent from Pindar's bright Thebes to Syracuse like merchandise across the sea. It celebrates Hieron's chariot victory and protection of the Lokrians, and later commends sincerity while condemning flattery, slander, envy, and deceit.
