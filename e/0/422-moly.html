Also called: Moly, herb moly, the herb, the herb that I shall give you, this herb, white flower
  • In The Odyssey, Moly is a herb Mercury gives Ulysses against Circe’s spells, with a black root and milk-white flower that mortals cannot uproot, though gods can.
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, moly is a white flower with a black root that the Cyllenian peace-bearer gives Ulysses to resist Circe’s treacherous potion.
