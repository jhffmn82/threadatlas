the forced marriage of Lucy Ferrier and Enoch Drebber in A Study in Scarlet

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1886

After John Ferrier is killed, a council gives Lucy Ferrier to Enoch Drebber rather than Stangerson. Despite her unwillingness and declining condition, she is married to Drebber in Salt Lake City; the forced marriage breaks her heart.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

After John Ferrier is killed and Lucy is carried back, a council gives her to Drebber rather than Stangerson. She is married to Drebber in Salt Lake City despite her unwillingness and visibly declining condition.

CHAPTER VI.

Jefferson Hope says that Lucy Ferrier was forced into marrying Enoch Drebber and that the marriage broke her heart. He regards Drebber's role in the marriage as part of the crime for which Drebber deserved punishment.
