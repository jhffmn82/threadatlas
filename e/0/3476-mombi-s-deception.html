The Tin-Woodman Plucks a Rose

Mombi deceives Glinda first by arranging for Jellia to impersonate her and then by transforming herself into a rose. The first deception is uncovered by Glinda, while the second succeeds until the Tin Woodman unknowingly removes her from the city.
