Mr. Townsend in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Mr. Townsend holds and administers the Wardrobe under my Lord, ordered to reveal the mystery only to the diarist; he makes the diarist deputy should he die during my Lord's absence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Mr. Townsend has been ordered by my Lord to reveal the whole mystery of the Wardrobe to the diarist and no one else. The diarist is to become his deputy because of the possibility of Townsend's death during my Lord's absence.
