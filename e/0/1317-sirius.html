Sirius in Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica

Hesiod · -0699~

Sirius is a star associated with oppressive summer heat: it briefly passes over men by day as autumn nears, then parches heads and knees and dries skin in intense heat.

Sirius passes over the heads of men for a short time by day and takes a greater share of night as autumn approaches. Later, Sirius parches the head and knees and makes the skin dry during the season of intense heat.
