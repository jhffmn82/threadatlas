Circe's prophecies in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Circe's prophecies warn Ulysses during the voyage home to avoid the Sirens, pass between Scylla and Charybdis, and avoid the sun-god's island, the crew's worst danger; Ulysses tells his men but conceals Scylla's threat.

Circe's prophecies warn Ulysses to avoid the Sirens, pass between Scylla and Charybdis, and keep away from the island of the sun-god. Ulysses shares these warnings with his men, though he withholds the danger posed by Scylla.
