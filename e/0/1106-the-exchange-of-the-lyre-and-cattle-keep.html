the exchange of the lyre and cattle-keeping

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: I will give you this lyre, exchange of the lyre for the cattle, gave the kine in exchange for it, ordained him keeper of herds, put his shining whip in Hermes’ hand, we shall settle our quarrel peacefully
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, it describes Hermes giving Apollo the lyre and Apollo giving Hermes a shining whip and appointing him cattle-keeper.
  • In The Library, it describes Apollo giving the cattle to Hermes in exchange for the lyre.
