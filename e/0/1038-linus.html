Linus

person · 2 works · 11 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Linus was the son of Urania by Amphiaraus, Linus, son of Urania and Amphiaraus, handsome Linus, verses of Linus
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Linus, the very lovely son of Urania, is skilled in all manner of wisdom and is invoked and bewailed by singers and harpers at the beginnings and endings of feasts and dances.
  • In Pausanias' Description of Greece, Volume II, Linus is a musician, son of Urania and Amphiaraus, represents Styx, has spurious verses, and receives funeral rites before the Muses' sacrifices.
