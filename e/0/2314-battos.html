Battos in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Battos is a person chosen by the Delphian oracle to establish Kyrene as a colony in fruitful Libya. He founds the city and becomes its king, later remembered as its founder and ancestral ruler. Apollo’s oracle is fulfilled when lions flee at his foreign-accented speech. Kyrene honors him as an enduring ancestral source of bliss, and his good fortune and legacy protect the city and continue with his race.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

IV.

Battos is chosen by the Delphian oracle to found a power in fruitful Libya and to establish the city of Kyrene. He is identified as the ancestor whose eighth-generation descendant is Arkesilas.

[Footnote 1: Libya. Epaphos was son of Zeus by Io.]

Battos was to lead the later foundation of Kyrene in Libya, after Euphemos’ descendants had colonized Thera and seventeen generations had passed.

V.

Battos is remembered as the founder and ancestral ruler of Kyrene. His ancient good fortune is said to continue with his race, and his city is protected by his legacy. Apollo caused lions to flee when Battos spoke to them in a foreign accent, fulfilling Apollo's oracle concerning Kyrene.
