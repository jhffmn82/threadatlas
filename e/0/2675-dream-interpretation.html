dream interpretation in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · -0015

Dream interpretation identifies the bull as the narrator and white cow as his mistress, then predicts a procuress's temptation, her inconstancy, and the narrator abandoned in a deserted couch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ELEGY V.

The dream interpretation states that an old hag of a procuress will tempt the narrator's mistress. It predicts that the mistress will leave the narrator to be chilled in a deserted couch and reads the cow's envy and black spots as signs of inconstancy.
