Lord Privy Seal in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Lord Roberts, the Lord Privy Seal, misses the 8th, delaying Privy Seal business and worrying Pepys over clerks; on the 9th he questions Pepys’s deputation, accepts it, and completes the business.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

Lord Roberts, the Lord Privy Seal, fails to attend during the morning and afternoon of the 8th, frustrating Pepys and causing concern about delays and the treatment of the Privy Seal clerks. On the 9th he asks Pepys what deputation he has from his lord, accepts Pepys's explanation, and completes the business.
