Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Hesiodic Poems

The Shield of Heracles is described as a work whose first 53 lines belong to the fourth book of the Catalogues of Women. Its later section, nominally about Heracles and Cycnus, is largely an inferior description of Heracles’ shield modeled on the Homeric shield of Achilles, and it is dated to the later half of the seventh century B.C.

THE SHIELD OF HERACLES

The Shield of Heracles is a vast, glittering shield made with enamel, ivory, electrum, gold, cyanus, and adamant. It depicts Fear, Strife, battle, monsters, animals, gods, Perseus, doomed warriors, festive cities, farming, contests, and Ocean with swans and fish around the rim.
