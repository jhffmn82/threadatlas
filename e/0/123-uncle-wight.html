uncle Wight in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Uncle Wight, the narrator's uncle, advises on a Brampton purchase and has daily long fits of stupefaction. After merriment at the Mitre and offense over Brampton, he dines with the narrator and learns of uncle Robert's death.

opening

The narrator visited uncle Wight after learning of uncle Robert's death and told him the news. Earlier, uncle Wight had been merry with the narrator at the Mitre, though he was offended that the narrator's father had gone to Brampton without informing him.
