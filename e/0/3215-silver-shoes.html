Silver Shoes in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The Silver Shoes are a pair of silver, pointed-toe shoes formerly owned by the Wicked Witch of the East. After the witch’s body disappears, the Witch of the North gives them to Dorothy. They fit Dorothy perfectly, tinkle as she walks, and she wears them on her journey and throughout her captivity. The Munchkins know they have a charm but not its nature; Oz identifies it as powerful, and the Wicked Witch fears it enough to steal one shoe, though Dorothy recovers it after the Witch melts. The shoes’ power is revealed at the end: activated by knocking their heels together three times with a spoken command, they can carry Dorothy anywhere in the world in three instant steps. They take her from Oz to Kansas, but fall off during the flight and are lost forever in the desert.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter II

The silver shoes belonged to the Wicked Witch of the East and are said to have a magical charm, although the Munchkins do not know what it is. The Witch of the North gives them to Dorothy after the witch’s body disappears.

Chapter III

The silver shoes belonged to the Witch of the East. Dorothy tries them on, finds that they fit perfectly, and wears them for the journey because she believes they cannot wear out; they tinkle as she walks.

Chapter XI

Dorothy tells Oz that she received the silver shoes from the Wicked Witch of the East when her house fell on and killed the Witch. Oz is very interested in the shoes and says that they bear a powerful charm.

Chapter XII

The Silver Shoes belong to Dorothy and possess a powerful charm. The Wicked Witch fears their power and steals one shoe by making Dorothy trip over an invisible iron bar. Dorothy retrieves the shoe after the Witch melts and puts it back on her foot.

Chapter XXIII

The Silver Shoes can carry Dorothy anywhere in the world in three steps, with each step taking place in the wink of an eye. Dorothy knocks their heels together three times and reaches Kansas, but the shoes fall off during the flight and are lost forever in the desert.
