multi-hop reasoning

topic · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Multi-hop Complex Reasoning, latent multi-hop reasoning, multi-hop reasoning abilities, multi-hop relationships, multihop reasoning, multihop reasoning ability
  • In Enhancing Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models with Iterative Retrieval-Generation Synergy, multi-hop reasoning is presented as a task with complex information needs that one-time retrieval may not satisfy and that requires gathering and processing knowledge from multiple sources.
  • In SEAKR: Self-aware Knowledge Retrieval for Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Generation, multi-hop reasoning is described as required by complex question-answering questions that need multiple supporting knowledge sources, but not by simple questions.
  • In A Survey of Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Customized Large Language Models, multi-hop reasoning is described as an LLM ability to follow connected relationships across multiple nodes and as a capability improved by graph-based retrieval and graph-enhanced Chain-of-Thought.
  • In OG-RAG: Ontology-grounded retrieval-augmented generation for large language models, multi-hop reasoning is described as commonly required for specialized-domain tasks and as the focus of up to 100 generated questions in the query-answering evaluation.

Multi-hop reasoning is an ability in LLM performance that follows connected relationships across multiple nodes, requiring subgraphs, intermediate steps, relational information, and effective prompt structure. In GraphRAG, graph-based retrieval navigates entity-relation paths while preserving context, unlike traditional RAG’s locally similar chunks. Its performance on complex questions can be improved through multi-round integration and graph-enhanced Chain-of-Thought.
