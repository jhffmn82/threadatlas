Hamburg in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Hamburg is a place with a mercantile connection to England that fostered exchanges beyond commerce. In 1756 or 1757, Johann Joachim Christoph Bode arrived there, becoming known for English language and literature. It became the center of his publishing activity, where periodicals first printed his German extracts from the Sentimental Journey. In 1775, it also issued several editions of the Yorick-Eliza letters and related collections, probably translated by Bode and favorably reviewed.
