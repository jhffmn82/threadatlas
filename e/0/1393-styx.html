THE THEOGONY

Styx, the chiefest of Ocean and Tethys's daughters, joined with Pallas and bore Zelus, Nike, Cratos, and Bia. She came first to Olympus with her children to support Zeus against the Titans, and Zeus made her the great oath of the gods.
