IRCoT in TRACE the Evidence: Constructing Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Jinyuan Fang · 2024

IRCoT is a chain-of-thought reasoning/model method used as a baseline for document retrieval and answer generation. It generates CoT sentences with LLaMA3-8B-Instruct, or thoughts/selects triples with LLaMA3-7B, and alternates thought generation with document retrieval. Its iterative autoregressive process requires multiple LLM forward propagations; reported HotPotQA development runtimes are 2.06e-3 seconds for indexing and 4.27 seconds for inference. In evaluations, it serves as a strong, best-performing baseline in the main comparison, but TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc outperform it in average EM and reported comparisons, partly because generated CoT sentences may be hallucinated or inaccurate. With a Mistral reader, it reports results on HotPotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Experiments / Experimental Setup .. Results and Analysis

IRCoT is the chain-of-thought baseline and the best-performing baseline in the main comparison. TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc significantly outperform IRCoT in average EM, which the text attributes partly to the risk of hallucinated or inaccurate chain-of-thought sentences.

Baselines

IRCoT is used solely to retrieve relevant documents from the document set in this evaluation. It alternates between generating CoT sentences and retrieving documents, using LLaMA3-8B-Instruct to generate the CoT sentences.

Additional Experimental Results and .. MuSiQue

IRCoT is a chain-of-thought model baseline compared with TRACE under different readers. With the Mistral reader, it reports 48.78 EM and 62.36 F1 on HotPotQA, 38.74 EM and 47.29 F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 22.84 EM and 29.69 F1 on MuSiQue.

Chain Constructor .. Chains R

IRCoT is a chain-of-thought model baseline whose context is used for comparison with TRACE. TRACE-Triple and TRACE-Doc achieve higher reported performance than IRCoT in Table 8.

Efficiency Analysis

IRCoT is used as the baseline for TRACE’s runtime comparison. It generates thoughts iteratively, requiring autoregressive generation and multiple forward propagations through LLaMA3-7B.

IRCoT in GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation

Zhili Shen · 2025

IRCoT is a multi-step retriever that interleaves retrieval and prompting so each step guides and refines the other. It proceeds through repeated retrieval and prompting steps, and is evaluated as a multi-step retrieval baseline using BM25 and ColBERTv2 on MuSiQue, 2Wiki-MultiHopQA, and HotpotQA.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Related Work / Retrieval-augmented Models for QA / Multi-hop QA with LLMs and Graphs

IRCoT interleaves retrieval and prompting steps so that each step can iteratively guide and refine the other. It is also used with HippoRAG for single- and multi-step retrieval.

Experimental Setup .. Baselines / Implementation Details

IRCoT is evaluated as a multi-step retrieval baseline using BM25 and ColBERTv2. Its R@15 scores are 57.9 on MuSiQue, 69.7 with ColBERTv2 on 2Wiki-MultiHopQA, and 92.8 with ColBERTv2 on HotpotQA.
