DistilBERT in Semantic Embeddings for Arabic Retrieval Augmented Generation

Hazem Abdelazim · 2023

DistilBERT is a language representation model derived from BERT through knowledge distillation during pre-training. It is smaller and more efficient, reducing model size by 40%, retaining 97% of BERT’s language understanding, and increasing speed by 60%; v1 and v2 are evaluated as hf1 and hf2.
