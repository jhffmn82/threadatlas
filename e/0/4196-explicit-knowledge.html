Analysis .. Slot Filling Evaluation

Explicit knowledge improves slot filling, especially for relations that are not central to an entity's meaning or predictable from surface features. Its benefit is greatest for relations such as performer, member of a sports team, author, place of birth, country of origin, cast member, and director.
