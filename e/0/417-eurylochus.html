Eurylochus in The Odyssey

Homer · -0632~/-0599~

Eurylochus is a captain and member of Ulysses's crew, and a near relation of Ulysses. He leads men sent to investigate Circe's house but stays outside, suspects treachery, and reports their disappearance. Later he insolently resists Ulysses's warning and counsels the men to kill the sun-god's cattle rather than starve.

Eurylochus leads the company chosen by lot to investigate Circe's house, but he remains outside because he suspects treachery. He returns to Ulysses and reports that the other men have disappeared, later warning the company not to approach Circe's house.

Eurylochus gives an insolent answer when Ulysses tells the men to avoid the island of the sun-god. He later gives the men the evil counsel to kill the sun-god's cattle, arguing that death by lightning would be preferable to starvation.
