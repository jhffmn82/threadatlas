Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XI

Ulysses digs a trench, pours offerings of honey and milk, wine, and water, scatters barley meal, and sacrifices two sheep. Their blood draws the ghosts near, but Ulysses prevents them from drinking until Teiresias has answered him.
