The Marvelous Powder of Life

Jack Pumpkinhead's animation occurs when Mombi sprinkles the Powder of Life over his pumpkin head and clothing and performs the required incantation. He immediately steps back, complains that Mombi is yelling, and demonstrates that he can speak and walk.
