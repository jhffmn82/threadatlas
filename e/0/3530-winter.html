2. How Claus Made the First Toy

Winter approaches after the flowers, beetles, butterflies, and brook have changed with the season. Snow covers the Laughing Valley, and the cold keeps Claus indoors for many days. During this period, Claus grows weary of having little to do and begins carving wood.
