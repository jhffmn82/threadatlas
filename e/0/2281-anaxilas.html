Anaxilas

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Anaxilaos
  • In The Extant Odes of Pindar, Anaxilaos was tyrant of Rhegium and Messana, was deterred by Hieron of Syracuse from attacking the Epizephyrian Lokrians, and died in 476 BC.
  • In Description of Greece, Anaxilas was king of Rhegium and fourth in descent from Alcidamidas, invited Messenian exiles to seize Zancle, aided its conquest by sea blockade, and urged killing suppliants and enslaving others.

Anaxilas in Description of Greece

Pausanias · 0155~/0175~

Anaxilas, king of Rhegium and fourth in descent from Alcidamidas, invited Messenian exiles to seize Zancle, aided its conquest by sea blockade, and urged killing suppliants and enslaving others.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXI.

Anaxilas, king at Rhegium, invited the Messenian exiles to Italy and promised them the fertile territory and city of Zancle in Sicily. He helped them conquer the people of Zancle by blockading them from the sea while the Messenians attacked by land, and urged the Messenians to kill the suppliants and enslave the rest.
