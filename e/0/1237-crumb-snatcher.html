Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE BATTLE OF FROGS AND MICE

Crumb-snatcher is a Mouse, the son of Bread-nibbler and Quern-licker, and he describes his preference for human foods and his fear of the ferret, hawk, and traps. After riding on Puff-jaw's back, he is abandoned in the lake when Puff-jaw dives from a water-snake and drowns. Later he fights to avenge the dead Mice, killing Leeky and Cabbage-climber before Croakperson kills him.
