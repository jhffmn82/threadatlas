golden wand

object · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Hermes' golden staff, a splendid staff of riches and wealth, golden rod, the golden wand, χρυσῆν ῥάβδον
  • In Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, Hermes' golden staff was promised by Apollo to bring riches and wealth, protect Hermes, and help accomplish every task involving good words or deeds.
  • In The Library, the golden wand was offered by Apollo to Hermes in exchange for Hermes's shepherd's pipe; Hermes also sought the art of divination.
