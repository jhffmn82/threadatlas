Ortygia in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Ortygia, Artemis’s dwelling and couch, Alpheos’s august resting-place, Syracuse’s scion and Delos’s sister, contains Arethusa; from it song goes forth before Chromios’s house.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode I

Ortygia is called the august resting-place of Alpheos, a scion of Syracuse, a couch of Artemis, and a sister of Delos. From Ortygia the song goes forth, apparently before the house of Chromios.
