Princess Ozma of Oz

Tip's transformation into Princess Ozma occurs when Mombi reverses the enchantment that made Ozma appear to be a boy. Tip sleeps through the process and rises from the couch as a young girl, while retaining Tip's memories and relationships.

Ozma's transformation and accession in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Ozma escapes Mombi with Jack Pumpkinhead and the Powder of Life; Glinda recognizes and restores her, enabling her accession as Oz's rightful ruler.

Chapter Seventeen

After Ozma escaped from Mombi with Jack Pumpkinhead and the Powder of Life, Glinda recognized who she was and restored her to her proper person. Ozma then became the rightful Ruler of the Land of Oz.
