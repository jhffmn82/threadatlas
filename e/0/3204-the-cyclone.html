the cyclone in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

The cyclone is a great whirlwind with a generally still center. It forms when north and south winds meet near Dorothy's house; pressure lifts the house as powerful winds howl around her. It carries Dorothy and her house many miles from Kansas to the Land of Oz, then gently sets the house down in a beautiful country.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter I

The cyclone forms as winds from the north and south meet near the house. Its pressure lifts the house high into the air and carries it away, while its powerful winds howl around Dorothy.

Chapter II

The cyclone carries Dorothy’s house far from Kansas and sets it down very gently in the midst of a beautiful country in the Land of Oz.

Chapter IV

The cyclone carried Dorothy from Kansas to the Land of Oz.
