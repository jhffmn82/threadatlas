A race at Pytho, instituted for rapid heavy-armed advance; Telesikrates won it in 478.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IX.

The foot-race in full armour had been instituted at Pytho for the kind of rapid advance required of a Hellenic heavy-armed soldier. Telesikrates won it in 478.
