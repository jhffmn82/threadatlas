Polydeukes in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Polydeukes, son of Zeus and Leto, is Kastor’s twin brother, co-protector of mariners, and a guardian of Sparta. After Kastor is struck, Polydeukes kills Lynkeus; Zeus kills Idas. Refusing permanent Olympus, Polydeukes chooses shared immortality with Kastor, alternating beneath earth and in heaven.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Nemean Ode X

Polydeukes fought Idas and Lynkeus after Kastor was struck, killing Lynkeus with his spear while Zeus killed Idas with a thunderbolt. When Kastor lay dying, Polydeukes begged Zeus to let him die with his brother and chose to share life and death with him rather than remain wholly immortal in Olympos.
