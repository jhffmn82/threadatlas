Patroclus's advance against the Trojans in The Iliad

Homer · -0749/-0724

Patroclus leads the Myrmidons against the Trojans near Protesilaus's ship, kills Pyraechmes, confuses the Paeonians, and helps drive the Trojans from the ships.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVI.

Patroclus advances with the Myrmidons and strikes into the densest part of the Trojan formation near the ship of Protesilaus. He kills Pyraechmes, throws the Paeonians into confusion, and leads the Achaeans in driving the Trojans away from the ships.
