Rhesus’s chariot

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: His chariot, Rhesus’s car, car, glittering car, the chariot, the king’s armour
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Rhesus’s car stands beside his sleeping place with the Thracian horses yoked to it, and Ulysses and Diomede seize it after killing Rhesus.
  • In The Iliad, Rhesus’s chariot is described as decorated with silver and gold, with his golden armour lying in it and his horses fastened to its topmost rim while he sleeps.
