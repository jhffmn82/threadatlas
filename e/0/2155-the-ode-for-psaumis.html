Pindar's ode, among his latest dated works, was written immediately after Psaumis's 452 victory and sung that night beneath the moon by Psaumis's friends returning to give thanks at Zeus's altar.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

IV.

The ode for Psaumis is dated to his victory in 452 and is among the latest dated works of Pindar possessed by the text. It seems to have been written immediately after the victory to be sung that same night beneath the moon by Psaumis's companions as they returned to give thanks at Zeus's altar.
