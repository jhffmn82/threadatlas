CopyRNN is a neural supervised keyphrase generation model proposed by Meng et al. (2017). It generates vocabulary words and points to source-text words, addressing absent keyphrases. It attains high precision among supervised approaches, though Transformer greatly outperforms it in multilingual use.

Related Work

CopyRNN, proposed by Meng et al. (2017), generates words from a vocabulary and points to words in the source text. It addresses the difficulty of predicting keyphrases that are absent from the source text.

Results and Analyses / Main Results .. Case Study

Copy-RNN receives high precision among the supervised approaches. In the multilingual scenario, Transformer outperforms Copy-RNN by a large margin.
