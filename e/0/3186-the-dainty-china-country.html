The Dainty China Country in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1900-04

A china country with a smooth white floor contains china houses, barns, fences, animals, and contented people, who become stiff when taken away.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter XX

The Dainty China Country has a smooth, shining white floor and small houses, barns, fences, animals, and people made entirely of china. Its inhabitants live contentedly there, but they become stiff when taken away from the country.
