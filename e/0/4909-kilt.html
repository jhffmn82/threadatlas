KILT in Robust Retrieval Augmented Generation for Zero-shot Slot Filling

Michael Glass, Gaetano Rossiello, Md Faisal Mahbub Chowdhury, Alﬁo Gliozzo · 2021

KILT is a benchmark and suite of benchmarks for zero-shot slot filling that standardizes zsRE and T-REx. It supplies an evaluation framework, slot-filling data, provenance annotations for positive passages, and a knowledge source of approximately 32 million passages for vector generation. KILT introduces baseline approaches, with RAG performing best among them for producing slot fillers. Its provenance ground truth trains DPR in Knowledge Graph Induction, while KILT-trained DPR supports retrieval evaluation and dense hard-negative mining. The evaluation culminates in KILT-Accuracy and KILT-F1, which credit slot fillers only when correct provenance is provided, enabling comparisons including KGI, DensePhrases, GENRE, Multi-DPR, RAG, and BARTLARGE.

KILT in BERGEN: A Benchmarking Library for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

David Rau, Hervé Déjean, Nadezhda Chirkova, Thibault Formal, Shuai Wang, Stéphane Clinchant, Vassilina Nikoulina · 2024

KILT is a benchmark for knowledge-intensive language tasks. It contains a fixed publicly available Wikipedia dump and passage- and document-level annotations of gold documents containing answers. Its datasets include ranking labels and are used to evaluate retrieval quality and effectiveness, compare retrieval with downstream generation, and support experiments on oracle passages and multilingual RAG.

Benchmarking Library BERGEN .. 4.7

KILT provides the publicly available Wikipedia dump used by BERGEN as a fixed retrieval collection. BERGEN processes this collection into non-overlapping 100-word passages, prepends article titles, and produces approximately 24.8 million passages in Hugging Face Arrow dataset format.
