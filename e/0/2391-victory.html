victory in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Victory is Midas' flute-playing match success, won in 494 or 450, producing a Pytho crown and showing achievement requires labor.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII.

The victory is Midas' success in the flute-playing match, commemorated by a crown that came from Pytho. The poem suggests that such human bliss and achievement are not made manifest without labor.
