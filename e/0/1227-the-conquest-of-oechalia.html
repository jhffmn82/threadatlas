THE TRACHINIAN MAIDENS

The conquest of Oechalia is described by Lichas as a victory for which Heracles is making sacrifices in Euboea. The Messenger gives a different account of its cause, saying that Heracles conquered the city because Eurytus would not yield Iole to him.

The conquest of Oechalia is attributed solely to the maiden's love for Heracles. Lichas says that Heracles did not ask him to conceal this cause, while DÊ accepts the event rather than adding to her misery by resisting Heaven.
