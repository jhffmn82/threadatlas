Tibullus’s funeral

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: at the funeral, funere, laying away, pyre, rogo, rogos, the funeral of Tibullus
  • In The Amores, or Amours, the funeral of Tibullus is the funeral pile where Tibullus’s body is burned, attended by his mother, sister Nemesis, and Delia.
  • In Heroides and Amores, Tibullus’s funeral is his burning on a pyre, attended by his mother, sister, Delia, and Nemesis.

the funeral of Tibullus in The Amores, or Amours

Ovid · -0015

includes the funeral pile [where Tibullus's body is burned] At the funeral of Tibullus, his body burns on an erected pile. His mother presses his tearful eyes, presents the final gifts to his ashes, and is joined in grief by his sister, Nemesis, and Delia.

Tibullus’s funeral in Heroides and Amores

Ovid · -0024~/-0015~

Tibullus’s funeral includes his burning on a pyre and the attendance of his mother, sister, Delia, and Nemesis. His mother closes his dying eyes, his sister shares the grief, and Delia and Nemesis offer kisses before the pyre is left.
