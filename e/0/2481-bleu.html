BLEU-1 in Retrieval-Augmented Controllable Review Generation

Jihyeok Kim · 2020-12-08/2020-12-13

BLEU-1, attributed to Papineni et al., measures content similarity between generated and ground-truth reviews. It serves as a proxy for look-ahead exploration, and references with BLEU-1 of at least 0.2 are prioritized to reduce variance without substantially compromising reference quality. It is reported alongside BLEU-4.
