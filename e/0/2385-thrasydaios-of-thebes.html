Thrasydaios of Thebes in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Thrasydaios of Thebes, a boy runner, won the short foot-race at Kirrha; competing naked in the stadion, he brought a third wreath and glory to his father’s house and shamed the Hellenes with his speed.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XI.

Thrasydaios won the boys' short foot-race at Kirrha in the rich cornlands of Pylades. His victory made his father's house glorious by adding a third wreath, and his speed put the host of the Hellenes to shame.
