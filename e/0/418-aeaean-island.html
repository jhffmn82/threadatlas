Aeaean island

place · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Aeaea, Aeaean isle, an island, famous harbour of Aeaea, land and palace, the Aeaean island
  • In The Argonautica, Aeaea is the land and palace of Circe, where the Argo anchors near the shore and Jason and Medea enter its halls as suppliants and receive purification there.
  • In The Odyssey, the Aeaean island is a low, sea-surrounded island where Circe lives, containing dense forest, a river, wild animals, and smoke from her house; sailors arrive after escaping death.
  • In The Library, the Aeaean isle is inhabited by Circe, and Odysseus reaches it with the one ship that survives the attack of the Laestrygones.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, the Aeaean isle is the home of Circe, where Ulysses and his surviving companions land after escaping the Laestrygonians and remain while Circe entertains them and helps prepare them for departure.
