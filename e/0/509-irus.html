Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK XVIII

Irus is a hulking, notorious beggar who insults Ulysses and tries to drive him from the doorway. He is forced into a fight, struck under the ear by Ulysses, and dragged outside to sit by the gate-house and keep dogs and pigs away.
