the blessed dead in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

The blessed dead are a group in their city, where some enjoy horses, bodily feats, dice, and harp-playing; they dwell beneath the shining sun amid fair-flowering bliss, incense, meadows, fruits, pleasures, and fragrance.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FRAGMENTS OF DIRGES (thraenoi).

They dwell where the sun shines below while it is night in the human world. Before their city are crimson-flowered meadows, frankincense-trees, golden fruits, pleasures, and continual fragrance.
