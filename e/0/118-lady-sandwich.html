Lady Sandwich in The Diary of Samuel Pepys

Samuel Pepys · 1660/1669

Lady Sandwich is a mother of Lord Hinchingbroke, Sidney, Oliver, John, and a newborn daughter, associated with the Wardrobe and Pepys’s home. Pepys first finds her household merry and well after returning to London. Later, she consults him over Lord Hinchingbroke’s illness and, fearing smallpox, sends her other three sons to his house before fetching them to dine with her. She is also mourning her brother, Mr. Saml. Crew, troubled by toothache, and managing poor housekeeping after my Lord’s departure.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

Pepys visits my Lady after returning to London and finds her household merry and well. He later takes two young gentlemen, two ladies, and Captain Ferrers to the Theatre and then brings them home for fruit and wine.

opening

Lady Sandwich consults with Samuel Pepys about Lord Hinchingbroke’s illness and sends her three other sons, Sidney, Oliver, and John, to Pepys’s house for fear of smallpox. She later has the children fetched to dine with her.
