BIBLIOGRAPHY

The Shield of Heracles manuscripts include papyrus witnesses and medieval manuscripts designated P, A, Q, and B through O. They belong chiefly to Ωa, Ωb, Ψa, and Ψb, with N and O additionally identified as mixed-family manuscripts.
