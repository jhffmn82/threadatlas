Asopodoros in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Asopodoros, Herodotos’ father, seemingly banished from Thebes, is shipwrecked before Orchomenos, driven ashore, then rises again to better fortune.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Isthmian Ode I

Asopodoros is identified as Herodotos’ father. Pindar promises to honor the story of his fate, including his being driven ashore by a wrecked ship and later rising again to enjoy better fortune.
