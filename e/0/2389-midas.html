Midas in The Extant Odes of Pindar

Pindar · -0497

Midas, a person, wins the flute-playing match, gains fair renown and a crown from Pytho, overcomes Hellas, and is welcomed to Akragas.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII.

Midas is celebrated as the winner of the flute-playing match and as having gained renown through a crown brought from Pytho. He is welcomed to Akragas after overcoming all Hellas in flute-playing.
