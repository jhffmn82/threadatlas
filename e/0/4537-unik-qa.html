Unik-QA in Retrieval-augmented Generation across Heterogeneous Knowledge

Wenhao Yu · 2022

UniK-QA is a prior open-domain question-answering model that retrieves from a merged corpus of knowledge-graph triples, tables, and text passages, and is evaluated on NaturalQuestions, TriviaQA, and WebQuestions.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

3 / 3.1 .. 3.3.3

UniK-QA is identified as one of the most relevant prior works. It retrieves information from a merged corpus of structured knowledge-graph triples, semi-structured tables, and unstructured text passages for open-domain question answering.
