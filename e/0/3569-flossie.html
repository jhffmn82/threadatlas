Flossie in The Life and Adventures of Santa Claus

L. Frank Baum · 1902

Flossie is an intelligent, swift, strong reindeer of the Forest, guarded by Knooks and Will Knook and protected by the Fairy Queen while away. Timid toward men outside the Forest, she nevertheless joins Glossie to draw Claus’s sledge. She secures permission for the first journey, wears Claus’s bells, pulls through the night, urges haste at dawn, and on Christmas Eve narrowly avoids punishment after returning late before racing home before dawn.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

8. The First Journey with the Reindeer

Flossie visits Claus with Glossie and explains that the snow is frozen hard enough for deer to walk on. She obtains permission from Will Knook to draw Claus on a journey, helps pull his sledge through the night, and urges Claus to hurry when dawn approaches.

10. Christmas Eve

Flossie helps Claus on his night journey and returns one minute after daybreak, nearly receiving punishment from Will Knook. Flossie explains that they tried to get home by daybreak and later races with Glossie to return before dawn.
