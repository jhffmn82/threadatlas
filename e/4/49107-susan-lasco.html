Susan Lasco in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Susan Lasco, daughter of an English gardener and sister of John A. Lasco, emigrated with her father and later married Tom Fairfield. Her inheritance was a cart-load of gourds, though she and Tom hoped important papers would recover a family fortune. She became mother to Rose Simon Fairfield and other daughters, inherited John’s bequest with her descendants, experienced the jerks at a New Lights meeting, and died beside Tom in their cabin after a cold winter evening.

” SCENE THIRD--THE SEPARATED SISTERS.

Tom Fairfield’s wife was the daughter of an English gardener, and her inheritance consisted of a cart-load of gourds. She shared Tom’s hopes of recovering a family fortune through important papers. After becoming dull and stupid one cold evening, she was found dead beside Tom the following morning.
