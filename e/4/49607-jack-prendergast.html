Jack Prendergast in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Jack Prendergast is a very tall convict aboard the Gloria Scott who has hidden money gained through fraud. Using it to recruit the crew and others, he leads a conspiracy to seize the ship. After the prisoners break free, he becomes the dominant mutineer in a bloody takeover, demanding that witnesses be killed. He allows Evans and seven others to leave by boat, kills the surgeon, and is aboard when the first mate causes the Gloria Scott to explode.

Jack Prendergast is a very tall convict and the leader of a plot to gain command of the Gloria Scott. He had obtained large sums of money through fraud, retained money that was not recovered, and used it to recruit the crew and others to the conspiracy.

“‘One evening, about the third week after our start, the doctor

Prendergast leads the mutiny after the prisoners break free, killing soldiers and insisting that the remaining warders, mates, and doctor must be silenced. He refuses to spare them, permits Evans and seven others to leave in a boat, and later kills the surgeon before the first mate causes the Gloria Scott to explode.
