VI. The Musgrave Ritual

Rachel Howells is the second housemaid at Hurlstone who had been engaged to Brunton before he abandoned her for Janet Tregellis. After Brunton’s disappearance she becomes ill and hysterical, then vanishes from her room; her footprints lead to the mere, but her body is not found.
