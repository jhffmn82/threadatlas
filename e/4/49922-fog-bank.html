Fog Bank in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Fog Bank is a thick, heavy, blue-grey fog bank across the center of the island, between the Blue Country and the Pink Country. It is an impassable-seeming barrier around the Blue Country: blinding, wet, clinging, sticky, and unhealthy for humans to remain in. Its dampness soaks clothes and hair, and those who enter are said to lose their six hundred allowed years and become liable to die at any time. Yet its pebbled ground can be seen, and it is the Paradise of Frogs, home to common frogs and giant frogs. It has no parsnips. It first separates the Blue Country from the Pink Country and from the travelers' approach. Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, Trot, and the blue parrot plunge into it to escape the Blueskins, who will not follow. A common frog guides them through its moist gray mist; near the Pink Country it turns soft rose, and they eventually emerge soaked, chilled, and with wrinkled, discolored clothing. Later, with a big frog, they escape through it again. Though a law sends Blueskins who break through it back with sharp sticks, it does not apply to the strangers. The Pinkies later cross it in about a dozen jumps with giant frogs and enter the Blue Country. Ghip-Ghisizzle fears Button-Bright may be lost in it. The fog remains the only part of Sky Island outside Trot's kingdom. It is crossed again with frogs and under the transformed elephant's guidance; its pink glow continues to signal the Pink Country. Trot and her companions plan to dive into it when leaving for the Land of Sunrise and Sunset.

Narrative, section by section

10 sections; each heading opens the text

THE BLUE CITY

The Great Fog Bank separates the Blue Country from the Pink Country. It is described as thick, blinding, damp, and dangerous, and those who enter it are said to forfeit their six hundred allotted years and become liable to die at any time.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Fog Bank hangs like a blue-grey cloud across the center of the island. Button-Bright, Cap'n Bill, Trot, and the blue parrot plunge into its thick mist to escape the Blueskins, who refuse to follow them.

THROUGH THE FOG BANK

The Fog Bank is moist, gray, and difficult to travel through, though its pebbled ground is visible. It is described as the Paradise of Frogs, and a common frog guides the travelers toward its border. The fog gains a soft rose tint near the Pink Country before the travelers pass out of it.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The travelers spend a long time passing through the Fog Bank before emerging in the Pink Country. The fog leaves them soaked, chilled, and with their clothing wrinkled and discolored.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Fog Bank lies between the Blue Country and the Pink Country and is described as wet, sticky, and unhealthy for humans to remain in. The travelers escape through it with help from a big frog and reach the Pink Country.

THE SUNRISE TRIBE AND THE SUNSET TRIBE

The Fog Bank separates the Blue and Pink sides of Sky Island. A law orders Blueskins who break through it to be driven back with sharp sticks, but Tourmaline says that law does not apply to the strangers.

THE WAR OF THE PINKS AND BLUES

The Fog Bank is crossed by the Pinkies with the help of the giant frogs. The journey is completed in about a dozen jumps, after which the army reaches the edge of the Blue Country.

GHIP-GHISIZZLE HAS A BAD TIME

The Pinkies break through the Fog Bank and enter the Blue Country. Ghip-Ghisizzle fears that Button-Bright, who escaped into it, may never be seen again.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

The Fog Bank remains outside Trot's kingdom. Trot and her companions plan to dive into it when they leave for the Land of Sunrise and Sunset.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The Fog Bank is crossed by the travelers with the frogs' assistance and later under the guidance of the transformed elephant. Its pink glow signals that the Pink Country is near.
