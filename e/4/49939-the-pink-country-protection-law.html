the Pink Country protection law in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

The Pink Country protection law grants the Ruler’s protection, a house, and a good living to everyone in the Pink Country except Blueskins, who must be driven back across the Fog Bank; it consequently protects Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap’n Bill.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

The Pink Country protection law states that everyone in the Pink Country is entitled to the Ruler's protection, a house, and a good living, except Blueskins, who must be driven back if they cross the Fog Bank. Polychrome points out that the law applies to Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill because they are in the Pink Country and are not Blueskins.
