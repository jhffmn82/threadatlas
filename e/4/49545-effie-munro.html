III. The Yellow Face

Effie Munro is Grant Munro's wife, formerly Mrs. Hebron, and a widow from Atlanta whose first husband and child died of yellow fever. She secretly visits the cottage, including at three in the morning, appears frightened and guilty, denies having been there, and begs Grant not to enter it.

Jack's wife clings to him and urgently begs him not to enter the cottage, promising to tell him everything someday. She says that the secret is being kept for his sake and warns that entering the cottage would end their relationship. She agrees to Jack's condition that there be no more secret visits or hidden activities.

“For two days after this I stayed at home, and my wife appeared

Effie secretly visits the cottage because she wants to see her daughter Lucy, whom she has kept hidden from Grant. She explains that fear of losing Grant caused her to conceal Lucy, and she asks what will become of herself and her child after the secret is exposed.
