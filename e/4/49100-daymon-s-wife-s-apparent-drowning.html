The apparent drowning was inferred from the discovery of Daymon's wife's shoe on the margin of Beargrass Creek and a woman's body in the creek. The body was badly damaged by fish and could not be recognized by its face, but witnesses identified its dress as one made for Daymon's wife; the conclusion was disproved when she appeared alive.
