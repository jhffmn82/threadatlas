murder of Hercules’ children and Megara in Hecuba and other plays

Euripides · -0429~

The murder of Hercules’ children and Megara is caused by Hercules’ madness during a purification ritual. In his frenzy, he kills Megara and his three children with arrows and a club until Athena stops him. The corpses leave him unable to perform burial rites and lead to his exile.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

IRIS, A FIEND, CHORUS.

The murder occurs after Hercules is seized by madness during a purification ritual. He kills his three children and Megara with his arrows and club before Athena interrupts his attack and he is bound while unconscious.

THESEUS, AMPHITRYON, HERCULES, CHORUS.

The murder occurs during Hercules’ frenzy and leaves the floor covered with corpses. Hercules regards it as the greatest of his sufferings and asks Amphitryon to bury the children because he cannot perform the rite himself.
