journey to obtain the dark-well water in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The journey to obtain dark-well water is a search for a gill of magic water. The Scarecrow seeks Jack Pumpkinhead’s advice, Dorothy brings a gold flask, and Ojo with companions travels through a great cave and slippery mine tunnel, obtains the water, and returns safely to Horner city.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Nineteen

The search for the magic water requires the adventurers to find a dark well and obtain a gill of its water. The Scarecrow asks Jack Pumpkinhead for advice, and Dorothy carries a gold flask that holds at least a pint.

Chapter Twenty-Four

The journey takes Ojo and his companions through the great cave and a steep, slippery mine tunnel to the dark well. They obtain the water and return safely to the Horner city.
