  • In Sky Island: Magic Umbrella is an old, large, strong but disreputable-looking family relic, handed down through Button-Bright's family. Its thick brown cloth has faded to dull drab, and its wooden handle is carved as an elephant's head with red-stone eyes and tiny ivory tusks. It has ropes, two wooden seats, and a wicker basket or lunch-basket attached, allowing it to carry as many as three passengers. Found by Button-Bright in his attic, it is magical through fairy enchantment rather than power of its own. When Button-Bright or another rightful family member holds it open and speaks a destination, it flies rapidly and safely there, obeying the destination but neither stopping nor changing course beforehand. It cannot grant wishes for ice-cream soda or money, and it refuses to return early when Button-Bright changes his mind. It carries Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill on journeys over land, ocean, clouds, and an arched path through the heavens, including travel to Sky Island and the Blue Country. On Sky Island, the Boolooroo repeatedly takes the umbrella to prevent the travelers' escape. Though it belongs to Button-Bright's family and will not work for the Boolooroo, he has it carried to the Royal Treasury, tests it, and throws it beneath a cabinet. It disappears from the Treasure Chamber and remains missing despite extensive searches. Eventually Tiggle recalls throwing it from beneath the cabinet onto a rubbish heap, where Cap'n Bill finds it and Button-Bright recovers it. When rejected by the Frog King, it transforms into a huge elephant, leads the travelers through the Fog Bank, and becomes an umbrella again at the Pink Country's edge. Finally, commanded to take the travelers to Trot's house, it carries Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright safely from Sky Island to Earth. Button-Bright later flies to Philadelphia with it, after which his father locks it in a strong chest in the attic.

Magic Umbrella in Sky Island

L. Frank Baum · 1912

Magic Umbrella is an old, large, strong but disreputable-looking family relic, handed down through Button-Bright's family. Its thick brown cloth has faded to dull drab, and its wooden handle is carved as an elephant's head with red-stone eyes and tiny ivory tusks. It has ropes, two wooden seats, and a wicker basket or lunch-basket attached, allowing it to carry as many as three passengers. Found by Button-Bright in his attic, it is magical through fairy enchantment rather than power of its own. When Button-Bright or another rightful family member holds it open and speaks a destination, it flies rapidly and safely there, obeying the destination but neither stopping nor changing course beforehand. It cannot grant wishes for ice-cream soda or money, and it refuses to return early when Button-Bright changes his mind. It carries Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill on journeys over land, ocean, clouds, and an arched path through the heavens, including travel to Sky Island and the Blue Country. On Sky Island, the Boolooroo repeatedly takes the umbrella to prevent the travelers' escape. Though it belongs to Button-Bright's family and will not work for the Boolooroo, he has it carried to the Royal Treasury, tests it, and throws it beneath a cabinet. It disappears from the Treasure Chamber and remains missing despite extensive searches. Eventually Tiggle recalls throwing it from beneath the cabinet onto a rubbish heap, where Cap'n Bill finds it and Button-Bright recovers it. When rejected by the Frog King, it transforms into a huge elephant, leads the travelers through the Fog Bank, and becomes an umbrella again at the Pink Country's edge. Finally, commanded to take the travelers to Trot's house, it carries Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright safely from Sky Island to Earth. Button-Bright later flies to Philadelphia with it, after which his father locks it in a strong chest in the attic.

Narrative, section by section

21 sections; each heading opens the text

Sky Island

The magic umbrella is named in the contents. Chapters include "The Magic Umbrella" and "The Fate of the Magic Umbrella."

A MYSTERIOUS ARRIVAL

Button-Bright carries an old, faded, disreputable umbrella with a wooden handle carved like an elephant's head. The elephant has red-stone eyes and tiny ivory tusks. Button-Bright says he used the umbrella to descend from the sky.

THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The Magic Umbrella is an old family relic that Button-Bright found in his attic. When he holds it open and tells it where to take him, it lifts him into the air and carries him rapidly to the named destination. It will not produce an ice-cream soda or money, and it will not return Button-Bright home after he changes his mind.

A WONDERFUL EXPERIENCE

The Magic Umbrella carries Trot and Button-Bright safely to town and returns them to Trot's house. It can lift three passengers in connected seats, fly over land and ocean, and follow a stated destination, but it cannot stop or change course before reaching that destination.

THE ISLAND IN THE SKY

The Magic Umbrella carries Cap'n Bill, Trot, and Button-Bright rapidly through the air. It passes the first island, travels through several clouds, follows an arched descending path, and finally drops toward an island in the sky without stopping or changing course.

THE BOOLOOROO OF THE BLUES

The Magic Umbrella carries the travelers to Sky Island and is held by Button-Bright after landing. The Boolooroo takes it to prevent the travelers from leaving, but Cap'n Bill forces him to drop it and Button-Bright recovers it. As the travelers prepare to depart, a blue line coils around the umbrella.

THE SIX SNUBNOSED PRINCESSES

The Magic Umbrella is captured by the Boolooroo and carried to his Royal Treasury by a Blueskin captain. Because the seats and lunch-basket are attached to it, they drag behind the captain as he carries it away.

THE BLUE CITY

The Magic Umbrella is in the Treasure Chamber. Button-Bright intends to enter the chamber and recover it.

THE KING'S TREASURE CHAMBER

The Magic Umbrella belongs rightfully to Button-Bright's family and obeys only a family member. The Boolooroo cannot make it work and throws it beneath a cabinet, while Button-Bright unknowingly passes near it before entering the Royal Treasure Chamber to search for it.

BUTTON-BRIGHT ENCOUNTERS THE BLUE WOLF

The Magic Umbrella is not in the Treasure Chamber. Button-Bright searches the room thoroughly but cannot find it.

THE PINK COUNTRY

The Magic Umbrella carried the travelers to the Blue Country. The Boolooroo stole it from them, leaving them to escape by passing through the Fog Bank.

TOURMALINE THE POVERTY QUEEN

The Magic Umbrella carried Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill to Sky Island. The Boolooroo stole it, preventing the travelers from returning home.

ROSALIE THE WITCH

The Magic Umbrella carries Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill through the air. Rosalie learns from her vision that the Boolooroo tested it and then threw it beneath a cabinet, and she concludes that it is enchanted by fairy power.

THE ARRIVAL OF POLYCHROME

The Magic Umbrella had carried Trot, Button-Bright, and Cap'n Bill during their adventures, but the Boolooroo stole it. The travelers escaped into the Pink Country because of this loss.

MAYRE, QUEEN OF THE PINK COUNTRY

The Magic Umbrella has been stolen by the Boolooroo. Button-Bright, Trot, and Cap'n Bill believe that recovering it is their means of flying safely home to Earth.

THE GIRL AND THE BOOLOOROO

The Magic Umbrella is no longer beneath the cabinet in the Boolooroo's apartments when Trot searches for it. Ghip-Ghisizzle promises to return it to Button-Bright and the others if they help him take the Blue City.

THE RULER OF SKY ISLAND

The Magic Umbrella remains missing. Trot and Cap'n Bill search every room of the palace, including the rooms of the Six Snubnosed Princesses, but do not find it.

TROT CELEBRATES THE VICTORY

Button-Bright's umbrella has not yet been found. Trot says that she and her companions will fly home as soon as they locate it.

THE FATE OF THE MAGIC UMBRELLA

The Magic Umbrella is searched for throughout the palace and surrounding areas. Tiggle remembers finding it beneath a cabinet and throwing it onto a rubbish heap, where Cap'n Bill uncovers it; Button-Bright takes hold of it. Trot says it can now take them home whenever they please.

THE ELEPHANT'S HEAD COMES TO LIFE

The Magic Umbrella is rejected by the Frog King as an insult to the foggy climate. It transforms into a huge elephant, leads the travelers through the Fog Bank, and returns to its umbrella form at the edge of the Pink Country.

THE JOURNEY HOME

The Magic Umbrella carries Trot, Cap'n Bill, and Button-Bright from Sky Island to Earth after being commanded to take them to Trot's house. Button-Bright later uses it to fly to Philadelphia, and his father locks it in a strong chest in the attic.
