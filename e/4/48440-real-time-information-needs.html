DRAGIN identifies LLM information needs across the full context by detecting tokens whose combined uncertainty, attention influence, and semantic score exceed a threshold, then uses their positions to trigger retrieval and truncate generated output.

Abstract

The information needs of LLMs may span the entire context rather than only the most recent sentence or last few tokens. DRAGIN uses these information needs to decide when and what to retrieve.

3 .. 3.3

An information need is detected when a token's combined uncertainty, attention influence, and semantic score exceeds the threshold. The detected position determines when retrieval occurs and where the generated output is truncated.
