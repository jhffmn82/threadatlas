SQuAD dev dataset in Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach: Document Question Answering using Large Language Model

Kurnia Muludi, Kaira Milani Fitria, Joko Triloka, Sutedi · 2024

SQuAD dev dataset is a test dataset for document-based question answering. It is used to test proposed document QA systems, including their ability to process documents, retrieve information from questions, and compare results with related ground truth. In evaluations, a proposed system was tested alongside another document QA application and reported 95.5% accuracy, with BERTScore precision, recall, and F1-Score of 82.8%, 87.0%, and 84.8%, plus BLEU precision of 17.7% and Jaccard similarity of 11.1%.
