raft construction in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Raft construction is performed by the Quadling man with travelers' help: he chops logs, Ojo fastens them, others carry materials, and the raft is completed by evening.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-Five

During raft construction, the Quadling man chops fallen trees into equal-length logs, while Ojo finds wood strips and nails them across the logs. Dorothy and the Scarecrow help roll and carry the materials, and the work uses the Quadling woman's clothesline, boards, firewood, and gold nails.
