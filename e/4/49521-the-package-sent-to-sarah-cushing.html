the package sent to Sarah Cushing in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The entity is a posted cardboard packet sent by parcel post from Belfast to Miss S. Cushing of Cross Street, Croydon. Wrapped in brown paper, smelling of coffee, and tied with tarred sailmakers’ string in an unusual sailor’s knot, it contains a little yellow box holding a female ear and a pierced male ear. Its remarkable contents are reported in a newspaper. The string, knot, port posting, and pierced male ear lead Sherlock Holmes to connect the crime with seafaring people.
