Nahualts are invading people from the west. They destroy Chichen-Itza, establish a capital at Uxmal, introduce cannibalism with the Aztecs, and alter Uxmal’s ornaments to display phallic worship and symbols of reciprocal natural forces.

The Nahualts are described as invaders from the west who introduced phallic worship and symbols of the reciprocal forces of nature. According to the narrator, they destroyed Chichen and established their capital at Uxmal, where they altered building ornaments to display their religious symbols.
