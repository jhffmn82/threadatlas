the black-water lake in Little Wizard Stories of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913

The black-water lake is a big, deep barrier without a bridge or boat; it blocks Dorothy and Toto until a tiny ferryman offers passage and the disguised Wizard carries them across in one giant step.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

LITTLE DOROTHY AND TOTO

The black-water lake is large and deep, blocking Dorothy and Toto's path because there is no bridge or boat. The tiny ferryman claims he can carry them across it, and the disguised Wizard crosses it in one giant step while holding them.
