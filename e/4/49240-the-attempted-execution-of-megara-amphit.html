The attempted execution of Megara, Amphitryon, and Hercules’ children is Lycus’ threatened collective killing of them. Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

HERCULES DISTRACTED.

Lycus threatens to kill Megara and the children of Hercules by burning them at the altar. He later allows them to enter Hercules’ house and put on funeral robes, after which he declares that he will send them to the Underworld.

MEGARA, AMPHITRYON, CHORUS.

The children’s sacrifice is imminent: Megara asks what priest or butcher will slay them or pierce her breast, and says that the victims are already prepared for descent to Pluto’s abode. She portrays the sacrifice as a forced, collective death involving the children, herself, and Amphitryon.

HERCULES, MEGARA, AMPHITRYON, CHORUS.

Lycus orders Megara, Amphitryon, and the children to appear so that they may die by their own hands. Their execution is prevented when Hercules returns, hides in the palace, and confronts Lycus.
