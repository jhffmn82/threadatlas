THE CAPTURE OF CAP'N BILL

Trot enters the Blue City at night by climbing Rosalie's rope ladder over the wall and descending on the inner side. Invisible because of Rosalie's magic ring, she passes a guard and hurries through the streets to search for Cap'n Bill.
