Maya calendar in Vestiges of the Mayas, or, Facts tending to prove that Communications and Intimate Relations must have existed, in very remote times, between the inhabitants of Mayab and those of Asia and Africa

Augustus Le Plongeon · 1881

Maya calendar distinguishes a civil year of eighteen twenty-day months plus five unlucky supplementary days from a sacred or sideral year of 365 days and 6 hours.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Circumcision was a rite in usage among the Egyptians since very remote times.

The Maya civil year is described as having eighteen months of twenty days, followed by five supplementary days considered unlucky. The Maya sacred or sideral year is said to have consisted of 365 days and 6 hours.
