Ojo's traveling party in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Ojo's traveling party includes Ojo, Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and Scraps, who join to help Ojo. Though planned with them, the Glass Cat and Woozy remain in the Emerald City by choice and Ozma's decision.

Chapter Eighteen

Ojo's traveling party is planned to include Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and Scraps. The Glass Cat chooses to remain in the Emerald City, and Ozma decides that the Woozy should remain there as well, though his hairs are needed for the charm.
