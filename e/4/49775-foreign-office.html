the Foreign Office in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

The Foreign Office is an organization located on Charles Street, with a servants’ side door. It employs Percy Phelps in a trusted role and keeps the Naval Treaty in a secure room, from which the original disappears while he investigates delayed coffee; examination seems to exclude ordinary entry or concealment.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XI. The Naval Treaty

The Foreign Office employs Percy Phelps in a position of trust and houses the secure room where he copies the Naval Treaty. The original treaty is stolen from a desk there while Phelps is away investigating a delay in receiving coffee.

“We examined the room also. There is no possibility of a secret

The Foreign Office is where Percy Phelps worked and where the important treaty was kept. The treaty disappeared from a room there under circumstances that appeared to rule out ordinary entrances and hiding places.

Foreign Office in Dr. Elsie Inglis

Lady Frances Balfour · 1918

Foreign Office was responsible for the safety of Scottish Women’s Hospitals’ British outpost hospitals. It cooperated with Dr. Elsie Inglis to equip and convey units overseas, agreed stipulations that did not hinder her work, and undertook extensive care and diplomatic work for the hospitals.

CHAPTER IX SERBIA ‘Send thine hand from above; rid me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of strange children.

The Foreign Office cooperated with Dr. Elsie Inglis and was responsible for the safety of the British outpost hospitals. It imposed stipulations that Dr. Inglis accepted when they did not hinder her work, and it undertook extensive care and diplomatic work on behalf of the hospitals.
