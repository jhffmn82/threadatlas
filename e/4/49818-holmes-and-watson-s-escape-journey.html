Holmes and Watson's escape journey in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Holmes and Watson evade Moriarty by leaving the express at Canterbury, replacing marked luggage with carpet-bags, traveling cross-country through Newhaven and Dieppe, then via Luxembourg and Basle, and ending in Switzerland.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

“Have you seen the morning paper, Watson?”

Holmes and Watson leave the express at Canterbury to avoid Moriarty, then plan to travel across country to Newhaven, cross to Dieppe, and continue at leisure through Luxembourg and Basle into Switzerland. They also plan to replace their marked luggage with a couple of carpet-bags.
