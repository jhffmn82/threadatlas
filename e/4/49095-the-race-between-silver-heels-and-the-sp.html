the race between Silver Heels and the Spotted Buck in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Near Helena, Arkansas, Silver Heels and the Spotted Buck contest a quarter-mile race for one hundred dollars. The Spotted Buck wins, though observers say jockeying decided it and Silver Heels was faster.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

The race was a quarter-mile contest near Helena, Arkansas, between ponies owned or ridden by old Billy Horner and Henry Mooney, with a stake of one hundred dollars. The Spotted Buck won, although some observers said it was a jockey race and that Silver Heels was the faster horse.
