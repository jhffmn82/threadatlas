  • In RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering: RetrievalQA is a dataset and benchmark for short-form open-domain question answering, created to assess adaptive retrieval-augmented generation and LLMs’ ability to decide when to retrieve external resources. It comprises 1,271 questions after filtering, spanning diverse topics in new-world and long-tail knowledge. The knowledge needed for these questions is absent from evaluated LLMs, so the set is extremely hard without external knowledge and makes retrieval necessary. It serves as a testbed for existing ARAG methods, including calibration-based and model-based approaches, evaluating retrieval decisions through retrieval accuracy and answer-match accuracy. To avoid bias toward methods that retrieve more often, its construction also included 1,514 discard-set questions answerable using GPT-2’s parametric knowledge. The dataset was curated from publicly available sources, examined for privacy concerns and violations, and is available at https://github.com/hyintell/RetrievalQA.

RetrievalQA in RetrievalQA: Assessing Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Short-form Open-Domain Question Answering

Zihan Zhang, Meng Fang, Ling Chen · 2024

RetrievalQA is a dataset and benchmark for short-form open-domain question answering, created to assess adaptive retrieval-augmented generation and LLMs’ ability to decide when to retrieve external resources. It comprises 1,271 questions after filtering, spanning diverse topics in new-world and long-tail knowledge. The knowledge needed for these questions is absent from evaluated LLMs, so the set is extremely hard without external knowledge and makes retrieval necessary. It serves as a testbed for existing ARAG methods, including calibration-based and model-based approaches, evaluating retrieval decisions through retrieval accuracy and answer-match accuracy. To avoid bias toward methods that retrieve more often, its construction also included 1,514 discard-set questions answerable using GPT-2’s parametric knowledge. The dataset was curated from publicly available sources, examined for privacy concerns and violations, and is available at https://github.com/hyintell/RetrievalQA.

Abstract

RetrievalQA is a benchmark comprising 1,271 short-form questions about new world and long-tail knowledge. The knowledge needed to answer its questions is absent from large language models, making external retrieval necessary and the benchmark suitable for evaluating adaptive retrieval-augmented generation methods.

notes

The dataset is available at https://github.com/hyintell/RetrievalQA.

opening

RetrievalQA is a short-form question-answering dataset created to evaluate adaptive retrieval. It covers new-world and long-tail knowledge across diverse topics and ensures that the knowledge needed to answer its questions is absent from the evaluated models, making retrieval necessary.

Dataset Construction

RetrievalQA is described as extremely hard for various sizes of LLMs when external knowledge is unavailable. The dataset includes additional questions answerable through GPT-2's parametric knowledge to avoid evaluation bias toward methods that retrieve more often.

RetrievalQA Challenges Adaptive RAG / 3.1 .. 3.4

RetrievalQA is used to evaluate adaptive retrieval approaches on 1,271 questions that require retrieval. Its experiments report retrieval accuracy and answer match accuracy.

Improving Adaptive RAG Prompting / Method .. 4.3 / Conclusion

RetrievalQA is a new dataset introduced to assess adaptive RAG for short-form open-domain question answering.

Ethical Statement

The RetrievalQA dataset was meticulously curated to evaluate LLMs’ self-awareness ability to decide when to retrieve external resources. It was constructed only using publicly available data sources, and its data was examined for privacy concerns and violations.
