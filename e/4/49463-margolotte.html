Margolotte in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Margolotte is Dr. Pipt’s wife, a chubby, pleasant-faced woman who lives high on a mountain at the north and is an excellent cook. She welcomes Ojo and Unc Nunkie, feeds them, and explains Dr. Pipt’s magic. Her chief aim is to create a living household servant: she makes the Patchwork Girl, later called Scraps, from an old patchwork quilt or bed-quilt and intends her to wash dishes, sweep, dust, and do other housework. Margolotte carefully chooses the servant’s brain qualities—Obedience first, with Amiability and Truth—because she believes a servant should not have too much intelligence, lest she become independent or high-minded. After helping Dr. Pipt finish the Powder of Life, Margolotte is accidentally struck by the Liquid of Petrifaction and becomes a motionless marble statue, along with Unc Nunkie. Ojo’s quest for a magic charm, including the Woozy’s three tail hairs, is meant in part to restore her. At the end, the Wonderful Wizard of Oz restores her from stone; she recognizes Dr. Pipt and runs into his outstretched arms.

Narrative, section by section

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

Dame Margolotte welcomes Ojo and Unc Nunkie, gives them a meal, and explains Dr. Pipt's magic. She wants to use the new Powder of Life to bring her Patchwork Girl to life as a housework servant.

Chapter Three

Margolotte constructs the Patchwork Girl and plans to give her only the qualities suitable for a servant, especially Obedience, Amiability, and Truth. She places the selected brain powders inside the girl's head and helps Dr. Pipt remove the finished Powder of Life from the kettles.

Chapter Four

Margolotte is Dr. Pipt's wife and an excellent cook who wants the Patchwork Girl to become her household servant. She prepared brains containing only the qualities she thinks a humble servant requires, and she intends for the Patchwork Girl to wash dishes, sweep, and dust the house.

Chapter Five

Margolotte is accidentally struck by the Liquid of Petrifaction and becomes a motionless marble statue. Dr. Pipt says that restoring her will also restore Unc Nunkie.

Chapter Nine

Margolotte is one of the people Ojo is trying to restore to life after an accident. The Crooked Magician requires the Woozy's three tail hairs as part of the magic charm intended to restore her.

Chapter Eleven

Margolotte was involved in the terrible accident at the Crooked Magician's house and was turned into a marble figure. Ojo's quest is also intended to restore her to life.

Chapter Sixteen

Margolotte makes Scraps from an old bed-quilt so that Scraps can serve as a slave and do housework. She is accidentally turned to marble with Unc Nunkie by the Liquid of Petrifaction, and Ojo seeks a magic charm to restore them.

Chapter Twenty-Eight

Margolotte is restored from her stone form by the Wonderful Wizard of Oz. She recognizes Dr. Pipt and runs to him, throwing herself into his outstretched arms.
