THE ROUGH DIAMOND in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

The Rough Diamond is a work representing America as a New World and its frontier, pioneer past; in its opening introduction, it preserves pride in log-cabin beginnings and the character formed before later magnificence.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Craig Kansas City, Missouri 1880 [Ill cover] [Ill 0020] SCENE FIRST--INTRODUCTION.

The Rough Diamond is presented as a work intended to preserve sentiments about America's earlier, rougher past and the character formed in it. The narrator compares the nation's log-cabin beginnings with later magnificence and hopes readers will retain pride in that past.
