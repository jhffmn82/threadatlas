Harold Latimer in The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1892~/1893~

Harold Latimer is a fashionably dressed young Englishman who, with Wilson Kemp, conspires against Sophy and Paul Kratides. He persuades Sophy to flee with him, holds Paul prisoner to force him to surrender property, and abducts and threatens Mr. Melas to interpret for Paul. After the conspiracy is exposed, Latimer flees England with Sophy and Kemp and is later reported to have died violently in Buda-Pesth.

X. The Greek Interpreter

Mr. Latimer, a fashionably dressed young man, takes Mr. Melas from his rooms under the pretext that an interpreter is needed for a Greek friend. He conceals the destination, carries a lead-loaded bludgeon, threatens Mr. Melas, and brings him to the house where Kratides is held.

Harold Latimer persuades Sophy Kratides to flee with him and later holds her brother Paul prisoner with Wilson Kemp. He attempts to force Paul to surrender his property and flees England with Sophy and Kemp when the conspiracy is exposed. A later newspaper report says that he died violently in Buda-Pesth.
