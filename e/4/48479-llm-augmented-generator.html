LLM-augmented Generator is a generator that combines retrieved chunks with extracted global information or filtered factual details to answer questions. Fed by the Extractor and CoT-guided Filter, it uses fewer tokens and produces better outcomes than longer, noisier-context strategies.

4 / 4.1 .. 4.5

The LLM-augmented Generator combines global information from the extractor with filtered factual details from the CoT-guided Filter. It uses these two perspectives to generate answers to questions.

5 / 5.1 .. 5.4

The generator receives retrieved chunks and, depending on the strategy, extracted global information or filtered details to produce the final response. Extractor & Filter feeds it fewer tokens while achieving better outcomes than strategies that provide longer, noisier contexts.
