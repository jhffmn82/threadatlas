journey to the Emerald City in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

A journey toward the Emerald City begins when Ojo, the Glass Cat, and Patchwork Girl leave the Munchkin house and take the widest, best path. Later, the party travels sadly from the Winkie Country to the Emerald City without important adventure.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Seven

After leaving the Munchkin house, Ojo, the Glass Cat, and Patchwork Girl continue their journey. Ojo chooses the widest and best path and plans to ask for directions to the Emerald City when they reach a house.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

The party journeys from the Winkie Country to the Emerald City after the Tin Woodman refuses to allow Ojo to obtain a butterfly's wing. The journey is sad for Ojo but occurs without any important adventure.
