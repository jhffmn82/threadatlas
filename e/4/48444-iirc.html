IIRC in DRAGIN: Dynamic Retrieval Augmented Generation based on the Information Needs of Large Language Models

Weihang Su, Yichen Tang, Qingyao Ai, Zhijing Wu, Yiqun Liu · 2024

IIRC is a reading-comprehension benchmark dataset for evaluating dynamic RAG frameworks. It generates final answers using the same metric as 2WikiMultihopQA. In overall and retrieval-timing evaluations, DRAGIN surpasses FLARE, FL-RAG, and FS-RAG, while BM25 surpasses SGPT.
