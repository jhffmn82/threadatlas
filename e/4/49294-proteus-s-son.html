Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

MENELAUS.

Proteus’s son rules Egypt after Proteus’s death and is hostile to the Greeks. His anger makes the palace dangerous for Menelaus and causes the female servant to fear that Menelaus will be killed.
