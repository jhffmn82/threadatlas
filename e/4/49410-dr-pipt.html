Crooked Magician in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Crooked Magician, also called Dr. Pipt, is a crooked magician who lives in Dr. Pipt's round blue house with his wife, Dame Margolotte. He owns a book of magic recipes and a gold bottle that once held Powder of Life. Over several years, he makes the Powder of Life by stirring four kettles at once with both hands and both feet, but the supply is exhausted after Margolotte and Unc Nunkie are turned to marble by the Liquid of Petrifaction. Horrified, he begins making more Powder and gives Ojo a recipe for a restoring compound. To restore the marble figures, he requires five ingredients: Woozy tail hairs, a six-leaved clover, a yellow butterfly's left wing, water from a dark well, and oil from a live man. He sends Ojo for Woozy hairs and may restore Unc Nunkie if Ozma permits it. He practices magic without a license, against the Law, and Scraps was made as his servant.

Narrative, section by section

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Two

Dr. Pipt is the Crooked Magician who lives in a round blue house and is making the Powder of Life. He is so crooked that he stirs four kettles at once with his hands and feet, and he recognizes Unc Nunkie as the Silent One.

Chapter Five

Dr. Pipt is horrified when the Liquid of Petrifaction turns Margolotte and Unc Nunkie to marble. He finds that the Powder of Life has been exhausted, begins preparing to make more, and gives Ojo the recipe for a different restoring compound.

Chapter Nine

The Crooked Magician has sent Ojo to obtain three hairs from the end of a Woozy's tail. Ojo believes the Crooked Magician may be able to remove the hairs if he brings the Woozy to the magician's house.

Chapter Eleven

The Crooked Magician requires five things for a charm that can restore the marble figures to life: three hairs from a Woozy's tail, a six-leaved clover, the left wing of a yellow butterfly, a gill of water from a dark well, and a drop of oil from a live man's body.

Chapter Fourteen

The Crooked Magician is the person for whom Scraps was made as a servant. Scraps does not want to return to his den and says that he may come to the Emerald City if he wants her.

Dr. Pipt in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Dr. Pipt is a crooked magician who lives high on a northern mountain with his wife, Margolotte. Proud of his crooked body and calling himself the only genuine Crooked Magician, he works magic chiefly for his own amusement and family despite being forbidden to practice it for others or as a profession. After nearly six years of preparation, he creates the Powder of Life, keeps it in a gold bottle and locked drawer, and uses it to animate creations including the Glass Cat, a phonograph, Scraps, and the Patchwork Girl. He also invents and unlawfully keeps the Liquid of Petrifaction, which turns objects to marble. Although he makes the Powder of Life without understanding its true value, he uses it in ways the Wizard calls foolish. His magic causes an accident that petrifies Margolotte and Unc Nunkie. Ozma allows him to make a restoring charm, then brings him to her palace, where his kettles are destroyed and recipe book burned so he can make no more Powder of Life. Finally, the Wonderful Wizard removes his magic, straightens his crooked limbs, makes him a simple Munchkin, and reunites him with Margolotte.

Narrative, section by section

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Three

Dr. Pipt, the Crooked Magician, completes his Powder of Life after nearly six years of preparation and stores it in a locked drawer until the next morning. He explains that he is proud of his crooked body and describes his Liquid of Petrifaction, which turns objects to marble. He also says that he is permitted to make a servant girl for his wife but is forbidden to work magic for others or as a profession.

Chapter Four

Dr. Pipt explains the Glass Cat's nature and says that his magic made it live, although he considers it useless and troublesome. He plans to bring the Patchwork Girl to life and later says he may replace the Glass Cat's brains with pebbles. After breakfast, he prepares the Powder of Life and sprinkles it over the Patchwork Girl.

Chapter Sixteen

Dr. Pipt, also called the Crooked Magician, makes the Glass Cat from glass and uses magic for his own family. He makes Scraps come to life with a Powder of Life, and Ozma of Oz had told him not to practice magic.

Chapter Seventeen

Dr. Pipt is described as the Crooked Magician who made the Powder of Life. The Wizard says that Dr. Pipt does not understand the powder's true value and uses it in foolish ways.

Chapter Eighteen

Dr. Pipt unlawfully kept the Liquid of Petrifaction and caused the accident that turned Margolotte and Unc Nunkie into marble statues. Ozma proposes allowing him to make the magic charm that will restore them, after which she will take away his magic powers.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

Dr. Pipt is brought to Ozma's palace. His four kettles are destroyed and his book of recipes is burned, and Ozma promises that he will make no more Powder of Life.

Chapter Twenty-Eight

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz removes Dr. Pipt's power to work magic and makes him a simple Munchkin. His crooked limbs straighten and become perfect, and he is reunited with Margolotte.
