Mount Pelion

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Pelion, Pelion’s cave, mountain-top, topmost heights
  • In The Argonautica, Mount Pelion overlooks the departure route, while its nymphs watch the heroes wielding the oars.
  • In Hecuba and other plays, Pelion is the cave to which Peleus says he will go after burying his grandson.
  • In The Library, Pelion hosted Acastus and Peleus’s hunt, where Peleus was abandoned and later rescued by Chiron.
