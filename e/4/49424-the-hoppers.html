the Hoppers in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

The Hoppers are a people and one of two nations on the mountain, living in their own country and marble city. Each has one leg, moves by hopping, and balances easily; their arms are longer than the Horners’ in combat. They fear the Horners’ sharp forehead horns. After Diksey’s joke angers them, they prepare for and declare war on the Horners. Dorothy persuades them to act as though they appreciate the joke, and they return laughing, tell Diksey it is good for a Horner, and ask him not to repeat it.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The Hoppers are a people who live on the mountain in their own country. Each Hopper has one leg and travels by hopping, which Hip Hopper considers more graceful than walking.

Chapter Twenty-Two

The Hoppers live in a pretty marble village and all have one leg, on which they hop and balance easily. They fear the Horners’ sharp forehead horns and are preparing to fight them after taking a joke as an insult.

Chapter Twenty-Three

The Hoppers become angry over Diksey's joke and declare war, but Dorothy persuades them to pretend to appreciate it. The Champion and the other Hoppers return laughing, tell Diksey the joke is good for a Horner, and ask him not to repeat it.
