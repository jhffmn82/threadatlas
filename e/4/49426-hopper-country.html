Hopper Country in The Patchwork Girl of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1913-07-01

Hopper Country, separated from Horner Country by a gated fence, lies on one side of the mountain and occupies half of the great cave. While its Hoppers war with the Horners, travelers are directed there; it contains Hopper village, about fifty artistic marble houses.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter Twenty-One

The Hopper Country lies on one side of the mountain and is separated from the Horner Country by a fence with a gate. The travelers are directed toward it by Hip Hopper while the Hoppers are at war with the Horners.

Chapter Twenty-Two

Hopper Country occupies one half of the great cave and is more grand in appearance than Horner Country. It contains a village of about fifty artistically designed marble houses.
