Don Carlo in Pen Pictures of Eventful Scenes and Struggles of Life

B. F. Craig · 1880

Don Carlo is Dan Carlo's brother and equal partner in the wood business, paying its expenses while Dan receives half the money. Associated with the Carlo wood yard, he supports the party seeking to improve society and is its silent spirit. After reading about Alonzo Phelps, he enters headquarters through a chimney, takes gambling-table money, defeats Brindle Bill, and becomes the Hero of Shirt-Tail Bend. Following the Red Stone explosion, papers from his pocket identify him as a recovered dead man, yet he survives after Suza Fairfield rescues him. He buys a small Kentucky farm, remains a bachelor, recognizes Suza at a camp meeting, courts her, and rides with her toward Port William.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

SCENE SECOND.

Don Carlo is Dan Carlo's equal partner in the wood business and pays all the expenses while Dan receives half the money. After reading about Alonzo Phelps, he goes to headquarters, enters through the chimney, takes the money from the gambling table, and fights Brindle Bill. He keeps the money after the fight and is later found among the dead or dying after the Red Stone explosion, becoming known as the Hero of Shirt-Tail Bend.

SCENE FIFTH.

Don Carlo survives the Red Stone explosion and later purchases a small farm in Kentucky. At a camp meeting he recognizes Suza Fairfield as the woman who rescued him and begins riding with her toward Port William.
