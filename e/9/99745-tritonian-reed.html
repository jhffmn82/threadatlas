Tritonian reed in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Tritonian reed is a flute associated with Hyagnis; Minerva discarded it when playing distended her cheeks, then Marsyas found or stole it and mastered it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

The flute was associated with Hyagnis, who was credited with inventing a peculiar kind of it and the Phrygian measure. Minerva discarded it because playing it distended her cheeks, and Marsyas found it and mastered it; Strabo says that Marsyas had stolen it from Minerva.
