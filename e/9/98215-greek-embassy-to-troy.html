Greek embassy to Troy: Ulysses and Menelaus demand Helen and property; Troy refuses and threatens them, but Antenor saves the envoys.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

ES 28 | ᾿Αναχθέντες δὲ ἀπὸ τῆς Τενέδου 1 προσέπλεον

The Greek embassy to Troy consisted of Ulysses and Menelaus, who demanded the restoration of Helen and the property. The Trojans refused the demand and threatened the envoys, who were saved by Antenor.
