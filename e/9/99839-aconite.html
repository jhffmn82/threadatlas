Aconite, or wolfsbane, was said to have grown abundantly near Acherusia after Cerberus was dragged through the cavern. Its name was derived from the Greek word for “a whetstone.” The explanation says that aconite and other poisonous plants were common in Thessaly and were used by women in pretended enchantments.
