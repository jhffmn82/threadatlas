Queen of the Tubbies in Grampa in Oz

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 14

The Queen of the Tubbies leads the wild wash women and angrily orders that Dorothy and Percy be taken to the wash tubs after Percy refuses to marry her daughter. She announces that her daughter is a Princess of the Tubbies.

CHAPTER 16

The Queen of the Tubbies keeps Dorothy, Toto, and Percy Vere prisoner and sentences Dorothy and Percy to work at the wash tubs. She calls from Monday Mountain that Percy should marry Pearl Borax and is expected to be furious when the prisoners escape.
