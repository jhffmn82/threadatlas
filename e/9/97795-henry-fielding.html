Henry Fielding in Laurence Sterne in Germany

Harvey Waterman Thayer · 1905

Henry Fielding is a literary influence: opposing sentimentalism while favoring realism and ordinary life, and shaping Christoph Martin Wieland and Tobias Knaut, whose bathing adventure is described as distinctly suggestive of him.
