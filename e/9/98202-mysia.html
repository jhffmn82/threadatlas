place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Μυσίαν, Μυσίας, Μυσίᾳ
  • In The Library, Mysia is ruled by Telephus, is mistaken for Troy by the Greeks, and is ravaged by them; Telephus defends Mysia and drives the Greeks back to their ships.
  • In The Library, Mysia is a land visited by the Argonauts after Lemnos and before the Bebrycian land; after Cyzicus was buried, they stopped there, while Hercules and Polyphemus remained searching for Hylas, and Polyphemus founded and ruled Cius. Teuthras ruled Mysia before Telephus succeeded him.

Πελίον θυγατέρα Αλκηστιν. ἐκείνου 1 δὲ δώσειν

Mysia was a place where the Argonauts stopped after burying Cyzicus. Hercules and Polyphemus were left there while searching for Hylas; Polyphemus founded Cius in Mysia and ruled it.

IX. ᾿Αρκάδος δὲ καὶ Λεανείρας τῆς ᾿Αμύκλου

Mysia was ruled by Teuthras. Telephus went there after receiving information from the god at Delphi and became Teuthras's adopted son and successor.
