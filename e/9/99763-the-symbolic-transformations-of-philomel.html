Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

opening

After Tereus pursues the sisters, Progne is transformed into a swallow and Philomela into a nightingale. Tereus is transformed into a crested bird with a long bill, the epops or lapwing.

notes .. notes

The transformations are presented as symbolic depictions of the characters’ different natures and actions. The principal account associates Tereus with the lapwing, Philomela with the nightingale, and Progne with the swallow, although Anacreon and Apollodorus reverse the transformations of the two women.
