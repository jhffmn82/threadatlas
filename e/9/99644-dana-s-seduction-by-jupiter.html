Danaë’s seduction by Jupiter in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Danaë’s seduction by Jupiter is a mythic event in which Jupiter reaches Danaë as a shower of gold; a rationalizing account attributes access to Prœtus bribing guards.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

The myth says that Jupiter seduced Danaë in the form of a shower of gold. One ancient rationalization instead identifies Prœtus, Danaë’s uncle, as the man who gained access to her tower by corrupting the guards with gold.
