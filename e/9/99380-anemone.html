  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, anemone springs from Adonis’s blood as a crimson wind-flower, opens when winds blow, and is short-lived because winds beat it down.

FABLE X. [X.708-739]

The flower that springs from Adonis’s blood is identified as the anemone, or wind-flower. It is crimson, short-lived, and easily beaten down by the winds.

Chapter V

The anemone grows when Venus sprinkles nectar on Adonis's blood. It is a flower of bloody hue that is short-lived, with its petals opened and blown away by the wind.
