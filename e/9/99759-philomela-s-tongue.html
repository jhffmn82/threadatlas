Philomela's tongue

object · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: speechless mouth, tongue
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII: Tereus cuts out Philomela's tongue while she struggles to speak and calls on her father. The severed tongue quivers on the black earth, and its loss prevents Philomela from verbally exposing Tereus's crime.
