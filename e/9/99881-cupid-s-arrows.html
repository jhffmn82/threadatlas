Cupid's arrows

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: arrow, arrows, darts, one, other, point of his arrow, the golden one, the leaden shaft, two arrows, two weapons
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII, Cupid's arrows are weapons that conquer all, including gods; directed by Erycina, Cupid chooses a particularly sharp, unerring arrow to use against Pluto, while the arrows' effects can repel or excite desire.
  • In Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable, Cupid's arrows wound Cupid himself when he touches Psyche after sprinkling her lips with bitter water, and later one of the arrows wakes Psyche from the Stygian sleep with a light touch.
