A baked replacement head for King Fumbo, made with melancholy prune eyes, red icing hair, and cinnamon whiskers, is glued to his shoulders and lets him walk calmly to his throne despite having no eyelids.

CHAPTER 2

The Baker makes a dough head resembling Fumbo’s own, with prune eyes, red icing hair, and cinnamon whiskers. Once glued onto Fumbo’s shoulders, it lets him walk calmly to his throne, although it has no eyelids.
