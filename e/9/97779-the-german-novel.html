Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

NEW YORK, May 1, 1905.

The German novel is portrayed as having moved beyond wild, extraordinary, and grotesque tales under the influence of classicism and growing interest in middle-class life. It remained burdened by pedantry, pedagogy, and theoretical speculation, often becoming a mirror of opinions about life rather than a consequential representation of events and character.
