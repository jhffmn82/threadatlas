the runaway road in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The runaway road is a sentient road with a gritty voice and stony eyes. It runs toward the pepper mine, carries Dorothy and Percy Vere to make them pepper diggers, and loses them when they catch a tree branch.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 9

The runaway road bursts from behind a rock, scoops up Dorothy and Percy Vere, and carries them toward a pepper mine to make them pepper diggers. It twists, bumps, and bounces violently, but Dorothy and Percy escape by catching a low tree branch as it passes beneath them.
