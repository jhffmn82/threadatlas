King Fumbo's loss of his head in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

King Fumbo loses his head in a storm but remains alive in Oz. After running headless, he is restrained while Prince Tatters, Grampa, and villagers search and provide temporary cabbage and then successful dough heads.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 1

King Fumbo loses his head in the storm but remains alive and lively, as people in Oz can come apart without dying. He runs around headless until Grampa restrains him.

CHAPTER 2

King Fumbo’s loss of his head causes confusion in the castle and prompts Prince Tatters and Grampa to begin a search. The villagers respond by making him a cabbage head temporarily and then a more successful dough head.
