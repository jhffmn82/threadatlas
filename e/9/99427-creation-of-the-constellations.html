creation of the constellations in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Jupiter rescues Callisto and Arcas, places them in the heavens as the Great and Little Bear, and, after Juno’s request, they circle without setting beneath Oceanus.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter III

Jupiter creates the constellations of the Great and Little Bear by rescuing Callisto and her son Arcas and placing them in the heavens. Because Juno persuades Tethys and Oceanus to forbid them from entering their waters, the two constellations circle the heavens without setting beneath the ocean.
