Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE ELEVENTH.

Orpheus's head is carried by the river Hebrus to Lesbos after his murder. A serpent attacks it on the shore, but Apollo drives the serpent away and hardens its open jaws into stone.
