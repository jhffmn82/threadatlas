HippoRAG in GEAR: Graph-enhanced Agent for Retrieval-augmented Generation

Zhili Shen · 2025

HippoRAG is a retrieval system that extracts semantic triples from passages offline, forms knowledge graphs, and aligns triples with passages. It uses Personalized PageRank, a PageRank variant, to retrieve passages while remaining agnostic to the triples’ semantic relationships. It operates as a single-step retriever or, with IRCoT, combines a multi-step agent with graph retrieval and improves multi-hop retrieval. Evaluations report R@15 and end-to-end QA results, while Hybrid + SyncGE and GEAR outperform it on reported measures.

Narrative, section by section

Related Work / Retrieval-augmented Models for QA / Multi-hop QA with LLMs and Graphs

HippoRAG aligns passages with extracted triples and uses Personalized PageRank to retrieve passages. It improves single- and multi-step retrieval when coupled with IRCoT but remains agnostic to the semantic relationships of extracted triples.

Experimental Setup .. Baselines / Implementation Details

HippoRAG is evaluated as both a single-step retriever and, with IRCoT, as a multi-step baseline. HippoRAG w/ IRCoT achieves R@15 scores of 58.9 on MuSiQue, 93.0 on 2Wiki-MultiHopQA, and 95.9 on HotpotQA.

Results

HippoRAG is outperformed by Hybrid + SyncGE by up to 9.8% at R@15 on MuSiQue. Its end-to-end QA scores are 8.2 EM and 18.2 F1 on MuSiQue, 39.8 EM and 51.8 F1 on 2Wiki, and 40.1 EM and 57.6 F1 on HotpotQA.
