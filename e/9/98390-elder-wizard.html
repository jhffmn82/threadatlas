VIII.—Tue ΠΟΘῈΝ or OEpIPuUs

The elder wizard predicts that the kid will be swallowed by a wolf and that the peasant’s unborn son will kill his father and marry his mother. He repeatedly dissuades the younger wizard from helping either the goat or the peasant’s wife.
