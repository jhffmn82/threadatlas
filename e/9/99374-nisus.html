Nisus in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Nisus, Pandion’s son and king of Megara, bore a fated purple hair whose removal meant death; when his daughter Scylla pulled it out in treachery, he died.

Nisus was one of Pandion’s sons and later ruled Megara. He had a purple hair in the middle of his head, and an oracle said that he would die if it were removed. His daughter Scylla pulled it out, and Nisus died through her treachery.
