sea monster in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The sea monster is a sea monster with a fish tail, sent by Neptune at the Nereids’ request against the Ethiopians and destined to devour the exposed Andromeda. It rises from the ocean and advances on her, but Perseus confronts it, wounds it repeatedly with his sword, and kills it by thrusting the sword through its entrails. Its skeleton was reportedly displayed by Joppa’s inhabitants.

opening

notes .. notes

The sea monster is sent by Neptune at the request of the Nereids against the Ethiopians. Jupiter Ammon’s oracle declares that Andromeda must be exposed to it, and Perseus confronts and rescues her from it.

notes .. notes

The monster was said to have been destined to devour Andromeda. Pomponius Mela and Pliny report that the inhabitants of Joppa displayed its bones, while Vossius suggests that the story may refer to the Syrian god Dagon, represented as a fish or sea-monster.
