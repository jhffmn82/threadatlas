2 sections; each heading opens the text

FABLES II. AND III. [XV.60-478]

Change and transformation affect time, the heavens, the seasons, human bodies, landscapes, waters, and all natural forms. Nothing wholly perishes; instead, things cease to be what they were and assume different appearances.

Transformation appears throughout the unit as the changing of bodies, animals, plants, waters, cities, nations, and souls into new forms. Examples include tadpoles developing legs, coral hardening in air, cities falling from greatness, and souls entering different bodies.
