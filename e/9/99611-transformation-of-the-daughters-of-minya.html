transformation of the daughters of Minyas into bats in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

The daughters of Minyas hide from fires and light, then membranes and wings transform them into squeaking bats that fly by night.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

opening

The transformation occurs as the daughters hide from fires and light. A membrane stretches over their small limbs, covers their arms with light wings, and leaves them with diminutive squeaking voices; they then fly by night and derive their name from the late evening.
