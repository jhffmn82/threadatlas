Evenus

place · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: river, river Evenus, stream, the Evenus, the river Evenus, Εἰ ὔ-, Εὔηνος, ποταμὸν Εὔηνον
  • In The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books VIII-XV, Evenus is a river swollen by winter rains, filled with whirlpools, and difficult to cross; Hercules swims across it while Nessus carries Deïanira to the opposite bank.
  • In The Library, Evenus is the river Hercules and Deianeira reached while traveling toward Trachis; Hercules crossed it himself, while Nessus ferried Deianeira.
