CHAPTER 10

Prince Forge John the First rules Fire Island and welcomes the unusual visitors. He offers Tatters a chance to marry a Fire Maiden and make a fortune in fireworks, but later sends the group toward Blazes, the keeper of the volcano, because he cannot otherwise save them.
