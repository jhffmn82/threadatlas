Aesacus, son of Priam and Arisbe, learned dream interpretation from Merops; summoned to interpret Hecuba’s dream, he warned that her child would ruin the country and advised exposing the infant.

Ἱστορία δὲ! ἡ περὶ τοῦ παλλαδίου τοιάδε

Aesacus was the son of Priam and Arisbe and had learned dream interpretation from his maternal grandfather, Merops. After Priam summoned him to interpret Hecuba’s dream, Aesacus declared that the child would bring ruin to the country and advised that the infant be exposed.
