Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

FABLE IV. [XIV.154-247]

Macareus, a companion of Ulysses, meets Achæmenides at Caieta and asks how he survived and where the Trojan ship is going. He recounts Ulysses’ encounter with Æolus, the release of the winds, the attack of the Læstrygons, and the danger posed by Circe.
