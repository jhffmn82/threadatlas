retrieved knowledge sub-graphs in Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation

Shijie Wang, Wenqi Fan, Yue Feng, Shanru Lin, Xinyu Ma, Shuaiqiang Wang, Dawei Yin · 2025

Knowledge sub-graphs are retrieved structures from knowledge graphs that represent an entity and its neighborhood at a specified hop level. They are indexed from item knowledge graphs at coarse and fine granularities and stored in a knowledge vector database. For recommendation, relevant sub-graphs are retrieved, reranked, graph-encoded, and converted or used to augment prompts, prioritizing useful structural information, reducing long context inputs, enhancing recommendations, and helping alleviate hallucinations in language models.

Narrative, section by section

4 sections; each heading opens the text

1

Knowledge sub-graphs are indexed from item knowledge graphs at coarse and fine granularities and stored in a knowledge vector database. K-RagRec retrieves and reranks relevant sub-graphs so that the most useful structured information is prioritized while avoiding unnecessarily long context inputs.

2

Retrieve-Rewrite-Answer retrieves relevant sub-graphs from a knowledge graph and converts them into text for generation. G-Retriever retrieves sub-graphs from different types of graphs to alleviate hallucinations in language models, and the proposed work retrieves knowledge sub-graphs to enhance recommendations.

3 / 3.1 .. 3.8

A knowledge sub-graph represents an entity and its neighborhood in the knowledge graph at a specified hop level. Its representation is stored in a knowledge vector database, retrieved for relevant items, re-ranked, and used to augment the recommendation prompt.

5

Retrieved knowledge sub-graphs receive expressive graph encoding in K-RagRec. This encoding facilitates the use of their structural information by large language models and helps avoid long context input.
