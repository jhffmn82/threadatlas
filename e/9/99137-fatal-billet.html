FABLE IV. [VIII.260-546]

The fatal billet is rescued from the fire at Meleager’s birth and hidden by Althæa, thereby preserving his life. When Althæa later throws it into the flames, it burns away and Meleager dies as it is consumed.

[Footnote 23: _Cocalus._--Ver. 261. He was the king of Sicily, who

The fatal billet was part of the story explaining Meleager’s death. The explanation suggests that the story may have been invented after Althæa devoted her son to the Furies, perhaps with the aid of magical incantations.
