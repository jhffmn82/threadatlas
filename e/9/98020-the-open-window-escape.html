Chapter III

The long, diamond-paned window was open to its full extent, and a smudge resembling a bloody boot sole marked the wooden sill. Barker and Sergeant Wilson inferred that the murderer left through the window and waded across the moat, although the timing made the escape difficult to explain.

It was nightfall before Inspector MacDonald and White Mason came back

MacDonald theorizes that Hargrave escaped after shooting Mr. Douglas, possibly through the moat and then by another route toward London or a safe hiding place. Holmes argues that Mrs. Douglas and Barker may have helped him escape or reached the room before he did so.
