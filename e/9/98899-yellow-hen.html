yellow hen in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Yellow hen is a brick layer given by Peer Haps to Tatters as a source of fortune. Though Bill calls it feathers, it flies up and lays a gold brick. It continues laying gold bricks, sold by twenty-four rustic laborers to fund Ragbad’s supplies, linens, and lawns.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 21

Peer Haps gives the yellow hen to Tatters as the source of a fortune. When Bill calls it only a bunch of feathers, the yellow hen flies up and lays a gold brick on the table.

CHAPTER 22

The yellow hen lays gold bricks, which the twenty-four rustic laborers sell. Its production helps provide Ragbad with money for supplies and care of the linens and lawns.
