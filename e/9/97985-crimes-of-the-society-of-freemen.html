The Society of Freemen's crimes included the planned shootings of Policemen Hunt and Evans, the shooting of Mrs. Larbey while she nursed her injured husband, the killings of the elder Jenkins and his brother, the mutilation of James Murdoch, the blowing up of the Staphouse family, and the murders of the Stendals.
