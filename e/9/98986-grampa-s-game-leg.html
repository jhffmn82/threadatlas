Grampa's game leg in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

Grampa's game leg, containing a blue pouch until the game begins, is taken by the Play Fellows while Grampa sleeps. Unable to walk normally, Grampa uses a croquet mallet as a crutch until Bill retrieves the leg and Grampa buckles it on.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 18

Grampa's game leg is taken by the Play Fellows while he sleeps under a checker bush. He cannot walk normally after discovering it is missing and escapes by using a croquet mallet as a crutch.

CHAPTER 21

Grampa's lost leg is taken from the playground by Bill, who snatches it away from King Capers and two Pierrettes. Grampa joyfully buckles it on when Bill returns it.
