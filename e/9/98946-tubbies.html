CHAPTER 14

The Tubbies are the tribe of wild wash women living on Monday Mountain. They become excited when Dorothy says the travelers are searching for a Princess, but turn hostile when Percy refuses to marry their Princess. They carry Dorothy and Percy away to the wash tubs.
