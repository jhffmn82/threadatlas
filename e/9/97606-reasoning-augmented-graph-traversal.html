reasoning-augmented graph traversal in HopRAG: Multi-Hop Reasoning for Logic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Hao Liu, Zhengren Wang, Xi Chen, Zhiyu Li, Feiyu Xiong, Qinhan Yu, Wentao Zhang · 2025

Reasoning-augmented graph traversal is a retrieval method and strategy that finds truly relevant passages in multi-hop neighborhoods of indirectly relevant passages. Guided by index structure and large language model reasoning, it proceeds through retrieval, selects promising neighboring vertices by reasoning over out-edge questions, expands beyond initially similar passages, and prunes.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

1 / Introduction .. Contributions

Reasoning-augmented graph traversal searches for truly relevant passages within the multi-hop neighborhoods of indirectly relevant passages. It follows a three-step paradigm of retrieval, reasoning, and pruning, guided by both the index structure and LLM reasoning.

3 / Method .. Algorithm

Reasoning-augmented graph traversal uses the large language model to select promising neighboring vertices by reasoning over questions on out edges. It expands retrieval beyond initially similar passages.
