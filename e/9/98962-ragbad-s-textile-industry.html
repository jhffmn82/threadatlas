CHAPTER 1

Ragbad was once famous for chintz and tapis trees, red ginghams, calico vines, cotton fields, linens, and lawns. After King Fumbo emptied the treasury, workers left and the textile plants grew wild, producing shreds, tatters, and rags instead of fine materials.
