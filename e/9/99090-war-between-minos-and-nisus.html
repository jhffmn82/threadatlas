Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE EIGHTH.

The war begins with Minos's attack on the coasts and city ruled by Nisus. Its outcome remains in suspense for a long time, until Scylla's removal of the purple lock enables Minos to prevail.
