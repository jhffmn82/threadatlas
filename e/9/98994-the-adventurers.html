the adventurers in Grampa in Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1924

The adventurers, including Prince Tatters and Grampa, are stranded on an iceberg, made warm and frost proof, transformed into crows by Yaga's tobacco, and fly east toward Oz.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER 13

The adventurers are stranded on a desolate iceberg after losing the connection to Isa Poso. The wizard's medicine makes them warm and frost proof, and the tobacco transforms them into birds or, in Bill's case, an unseen crowing sound. They then fly east across the Nonestic Ocean toward Oz.
