Acheloüs transforms from serpent to bull while fighting Hercules; weaker in strength, he escapes briefly, then is thrown down and loses a horn.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

BOOK THE NINTH.

When Acheloüs is inferior in strength, he first transforms into a long serpent and escapes Hercules's grasp. He then changes into a furious bull, but Hercules seizes his horn, throws him down, and breaks it off.
