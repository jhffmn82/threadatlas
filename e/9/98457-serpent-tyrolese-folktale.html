XII.—TuE Vow or [DOMENEUS

The serpent in the Tyrolese story demands that a merchant send it the first person he meets at home after he takes a singing and playing leaf. The merchant's youngest daughter goes to it, and it is revealed to be an enchanted nobleman who marries her after the spell is broken.
