Mudge in The Cowardly Lion of Oz

Ruth Plumly Thompson · 1923

Mudge is a hot, dry, blue desert country and kingdom in the southwestern part of the Munchkin country of Oz, also called a city and the land of the Mudgers. Surrounded by the royal lion reservation, it is subject to the lion tax, which its people resent. Mustafa rules Mudge; its borders are closed to outsiders, and a blue book dropped by Glinda forbids Mudgers to leave, under a law that says those who do lose their heads. Mudge protects a privately patented magic transformation formula and contains the blue-striped tent, the royal tent, and Mustafa’s tent. Notta first brings himself and Bobbie there, and later sends the Uns there with “Udge! Budge! Come to Mudge!” Mustafa cages twenty Uns and keeps 9,999 lions enclosed, intending to hold the Cowardly Lion once he enters. As travelers move toward Mudge, the Cowardly Lion faces grave danger and Crunch carries him there. In the end, Ozma orders petrified Crunch to stand in Mudge forever as a monument to Mustafa’s greediness and folly.

Narrative, section by section

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Mudge is a hot, dry, blue desert country in the southwestern part of the Munchkin country of Oz. Its people are forbidden to leave by a blue book dropped by Glinda, and its borders are also closed to outsiders.

Chapter 3

Mudge is the country to which Notta transported himself and Bobbie. It contains the blue-striped tent, the royal tent, the Mudgers, and Mustafa as its ruler.

Chapter 4

Mudge is a country ruled by Mustafa where the people have never seen anything like Notta's lion body and clown head. Its laws forbid any Mudger from leaving the country without losing his head.

Chapter 11

Mudge is the place to which Notta sends the Uns by using the magic verse. Each Un whom he touches while reciting the verse disappears and is said to go to Mudge.

Chapter 14

The travelers learn that moving straight ahead leads toward Mudge, while other directions make Bob and Notta turn blue. The Cowardly Lion considers going on to Mudge while the others seek help.

Chapter 15

Mudge is ruled by Mustafa, who is determined to keep the Cowardly Lion inside the kingdom once he enters it. Twenty Uns are confined in a cage in Mustafa's tent, and their escape has left twenty blue patches in the tent top.

Chapter 19

Mudge is the place where the Cowardly Lion is in grave danger. Notta uses the verse "Udge! Budge! Come to Mudge!" to transport every person he has touched from the Emerald City to Mudge.

Chapter 20

Mudge is the land of the Mudgers and the destination toward which Crunch carries the Cowardly Lion. Mustafa rules there and keeps 9,999 lions in an enclosure.

Chapter 21

Mudge is the place where Ozma orders the petrified Crunch to stand forever as a monument to Mustafa's greediness and folly.
