optimal textual subgraph ˆg in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025

The optimal textual subgraph ˆg is a query-tailored element of S(G) retrieved by GRAG to help the LLM generate answers aligned with expectations. It is formed by merging adaptively masked partial neighborhoods around selected key nodes, including important nodes and some neighbors, while pruning irrelevant content. Constructed with linear complexity, it uses learned relevance scaling factors and is represented as a soft prompt to reduce irrelevant-entity influence during encoding.

optimal subgraph structure in GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Yuntong Hu, Zhihan Lei, Zheng Zhang, Bo Pan, Chen Ling, Liang Zhao · 2025-07-12

The optimal textual subgraph is a query-tailored union of selected, adaptively masked node neighborhoods from graph G. Its information is concatenated with query q for LLMθ, aiming to maximize generation quality and produce answers aligned with expectations. Although directly finding the optimum is NP-hard, it is approximated through neighborhood selection and merging, and retrieved by divide-and-conquer with linear construction complexity.
