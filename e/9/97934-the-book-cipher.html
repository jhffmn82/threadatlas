Arthur Conan Doyle · 1914-09/1915-05

The book cipher, found in an anonymous warning letter, encodes messages by page, column, and word numbers; Holmes identifies its common double-column source as an old Whitaker’s Almanac.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

PART I / Chapter I

The book cipher uses a page, a column, and numbered words to convey a secret message. Sherlock Holmes concludes that the book must be common, standardized, large, and printed in double columns, ultimately identifying Whitaker’s Almanac.
