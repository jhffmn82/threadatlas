Arachne in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Arachne is a Lydian weaver and artist, daughter of Idmon and her mother, from humble Hypaepa and low extraction. Renowned throughout Lydia for exceptional skill in working wool, silk, and weaving, she is also said to have invented making linen cloths and nets. She challenges Minerva, or Pallas, to a weaving contest and creates a web representing transformations, deceptions, and numerous myths of the gods. After Minerva strikes her, Arachne hangs herself in despair. Minerva then takes pity on her and transforms her into a spider, completing the transformation associated with her challenge and web.

front matter

Arachne competes with Pallas in the art of weaving and represents various transformations on her web. She is changed into a spider.

BOOK THE SIXTH.

Arachne is the daughter of Idmon of Colophon and becomes renowned throughout Lydia for her exceptional skill in working wool. She challenges Minerva and produces a web depicting the transformations and deceptions of the gods. After Minerva strikes her, Arachne hangs herself, but Minerva takes pity on her and changes her form.

notes .. notes

Arachne is described as the daughter of Idmon, a Lydian of low extraction, and as the most skilful artist of her time at working in silk and wool. She challenged Minerva and represented numerous myths in her work; according to the explanation, she later hanged herself in despair and was associated with the story of transformation into a spider.

Arachne in Bulfinch's Mythology: The Age of Fable

Thomas Bulfinch · 1855

Arachne is a mortal maiden renowned for extraordinary weaving and embroidery. Denying Minerva as teacher, she challenges her, depicts gods’ failings, is defeated and humiliated, hangs herself, then becomes a spider.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter IX

Arachne is a mortal maiden whose weaving and embroidery are so skillful that the nymphs admire her. She challenges Minerva, creates a web depicting the failings of the gods, and is transformed into a spider after her defeat and humiliation.
