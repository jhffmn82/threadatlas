Danaë in The Metamorphoses of Ovid, Books I-VII

Ovid · 0008~

Danaë, daughter of Acrisius and mother of Perseus, is confined by Acrisius to avert a prophecy, yet conceives Perseus in a shower of gold, attributed to Jupiter or, in another account, to Prœtus through bribed guards.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

notes .. notes

Danaë was the daughter of Acrisius and the mother of Perseus. Acrisius confined her to prevent her from bearing the child foretold to kill him, but she was reached either by Jupiter in myth or, in one rationalizing account, by her uncle Prœtus through bribed guards.
