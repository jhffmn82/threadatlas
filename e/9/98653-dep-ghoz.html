Depé Ghoz in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Depé Ghoz is a semi-divine, human-formed one-eyed monster, protected from arrows and swords by his fairy mother’s ring. He devours Oghuz and exacts two men and five hundred sheep daily, captures Bissat, then is blinded, escaped, and beheaded by Bissat.

(33) A version of the tale which presents the main features

Depé Ghoz is a semi-divine, one-eyed monster who is invulnerable because of a ring given to him by his fairy mother. He captures Bissat, but Bissat blinds him with a red-hot knife, escapes in a ram’s skin, and finally cuts off his head.
