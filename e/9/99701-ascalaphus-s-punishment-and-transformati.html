opening

For revealing that Proserpine ate pomegranate seeds, Ascalaphus is punished by Proserpine. His head becomes a beak, feathers, and large eyes; his body is covered with tawny wings, and he becomes a sluggish owl.

notes .. notes

Ascalaphus's transformation is variously described as a change into an owl or into a lizard. The explanation connects the owl version with his death in Pluto's mines and the lizard version with the resemblance between his name and the Greek word for lizard.
