Earth people in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

Earth people are animals, birds, or fish who make war on Sky people, ascending by a chain of arrows; they win an initial battle, then are routed, killed, stranded in the sky, or transformed into stars.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

Il.—War or Eartn on HEAVEN

The Earth people are generally animals, birds, or fish who attack the Sky people. They ascend by a chain of arrows, win the first battle in one version, but are later routed, and some are killed, stranded in the sky, or transformed into stars.
