Homeric hymn-writer in The Library

Apollodorus · 0001/0200

An ancient writer recounts Celeus and Metanira's child Demophon, distinct from Triptolemus; after Metanira interrupts Demeter, Demeter angrily throws him down, leaving his survival or death unspecified.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

J.—PuttTinc CHILDREN ON THE FIRE

The Homeric hymn-writer names the parents as Celeus and Metanira and calls the child Demophon, distinguishing him from Triptolemus. The account says that Metanira interrupted Demeter, after which Demeter angrily threw the child down without stating whether he lived or died.
