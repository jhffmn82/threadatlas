X.—THE MARRIAGE OF PELEUS AND THETIS

The seal-skin or ham is the covering that allows seals or mermaids in the Faré Islands and Shetland to take human form. Men who hide it can marry the transformed female, but when she finds and resumes it, she returns to the sea.
