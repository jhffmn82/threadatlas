Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE FIVE MILLION LOAN ELECTION.

The Five Million Loan Election concerned a constitutional amendment authorizing state aid of $10,000 per mile to four railroad companies for each mile graded and ready to iron. Leading men of both parties supported it, and the amendment passed by an immense majority after the panic of 1857.
