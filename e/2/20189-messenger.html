Enter a MESSENGER.

The MESSENGER reports that the Persian host has been defeated at Salamis and describes the destruction of the fleet, the deaths of Persian commanders, the slaughter on the islet, and the disastrous retreat of the land force. He states that Xerxes survived but fled after witnessing the ruin.
