the Agra Fort in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Agra Fort, in Agra, has a central guard-house and a small isolated postern gate on its southwest side. It contains a modern occupied section and a larger deserted quarter of halls, passages, and corridors, with gates guarded by small detachments. Conspirators carry Achmet’s body to a crumbling hall and conceal a treasure chest beneath bricks in a best-preserved wall.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

“I am a Worcestershire man myself,—born near Pershore. I dare say you

The Agra Fort contained a modern occupied section and a much larger deserted quarter filled with halls, passages, and corridors. Its many gates were guarded by small detachments because the defenders lacked enough men for stronger posts.

He stopped, and held out his manacled hands for the whiskey-and-water

The Agra fort is where the conspirators carry Achmet’s body and hide it in a crumbling hall. They also conceal the treasure chest beneath bricks in the best-preserved wall, using plans to record its location.
