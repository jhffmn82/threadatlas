William H. Seward, Secretary of State and foremost statesman of his time, used diplomatic skill during the war to keep the United States free from foreign entanglements. Targeted in an assassination conspiracy and initially believed killed, he survived the dagger attack.

THE DEATH OF PRESIDENT LINCOLN.

William H. Seward, referred to in the text as Secretary of State Seward, was believed at first to have been killed by the conspirators’ dagger but survived. Noble eulogized him as the foremost statesman of his time and credited his diplomatic skill with keeping the country free from foreign entanglements.
