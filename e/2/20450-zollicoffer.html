Pioneer Press · 1862~

Zollicoffer, a Nashville Confederate commander who had opposed Tennessee's secession, followed the state after it seceded, commanded forces at the decisive battle of Mill Springs, and was killed there when Colonel Fry shot him.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE DECISIVE BATTLE OF MILL SPRINGS.

Zollicoffer commanded the Confederate forces at Mill Springs and was killed during the battle. Colonel Fry reported that he shot Zollicoffer in the breast after Zollicoffer's companion fired at him; Zollicoffer had previously opposed Tennessee's secession but followed the state after it seceded.
