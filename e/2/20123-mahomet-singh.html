Mahomet Singh in The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle · 1890-02

Mahomet Singh is a tall, great black-bearded Sikh trooper under the narrator’s command and an old fighting-man who fought at Chilian-wallah. One of three Sikh conspirators, he helps Abdullah Khan threaten the narrator and is assigned to watch for Dost Akbar and Achmet. He pursues a fat man through a passage with a flashing knife and stabs him after the man falls. Later he helps carry Achmet’s body into the fort, guards the door, and joins the conspirators in concealing treasure, swearing oaths, making charts, and negotiating with Major Sholto and Captain Morstan.
