Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE GREAT BATTLE OF PITTSBURG LANDING.

Henry W. Halleck suspended Grant from command shortly before the battle and later assumed personal command of the forces at Pittsburg Landing, placing Grant second in command. Halleck constructed extensive fortifications that were never occupied before he was ordered to Washington.
