HOTEL FIRES.

The International hotel, formerly the Fuller house, stood at Seventh and Jackson streets and was built by A.G. Fuller in 1856. The five-story brick building cost about $110,000 and was regarded for years as the best hotel in the West before it burned on February 3, 1869.
