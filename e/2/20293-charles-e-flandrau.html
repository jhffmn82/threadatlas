Pioneer Press · 1862~

Charles E. Flandrau dissented from the Minnesota Supreme Court bond opinion, organized volunteers at St. Peter during the 1862 Sioux outbreak, commanded New Ulm’s defense, and led its evacuation to Mankato when supplies ran short.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE GREAT SIOUX OUTBREAK IN 1862.

Charles E. Flandrau organized volunteers at St. Peter and marched to New Ulm. He commanded about 300 poorly armed and inexperienced men, helped defend the town, and led the evacuation to Mankato after provisions and ammunition became scarce.
