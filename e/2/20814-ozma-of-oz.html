Ozma of Oz in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

L. Frank Baum presents Ozma of Oz as a record of Dorothy Gale’s further adventures, bringing back Ozma, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, and the Cowardly Lion while introducing Billina, Tiktok, and the Hungry Tiger. Dorothy is swept from a ship bound for Australia when a storm carries away a chicken coop. Adrift at sea, she meets the talking yellow hen she names Billina. They reach an unknown shore, find a golden key, and flee the threatening Wheelers into a rocky hill, where they discover and wind up Tiktok, a mechanical man. Tiktok leads Dorothy and Billina into Ev, whose former king, Evoldo, sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King. At Ev’s palace, Princess Langwidere, owner of thirty interchangeable heads, imprisons Dorothy because she wants her head. Ozma arrives from Oz with Dorothy’s old friends, frees her, and organizes an expedition to rescue Ev’s royal family from Roquat of the Rocks, the Nome King. The party survives a dangerous route past a giant with an iron hammer and enters the Nome King’s palace. The King says he transformed the Queen of Ev and her children into ornaments and challenges the rescuers to identify them by guessing. Ozma, the Tin Woodman, the army, and others fail and are transformed into ornaments. Dorothy restores Prince Evring but cannot solve the enchantments. Billina, however, discovers that the Nome King fears eggs. Using this advantage and knowledge of the transformations, she restores the Queen, her children, Ozma, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, and the people of Oz. The Scarecrow’s use of Billina’s eggs drives off the Nome King’s warriors and leaves the King helpless. Dorothy takes the Nome King’s magic belt, helps the party escape, and later uses it against pursuing warriors and the iron giant. The Tin Woodman is finally restored when Evring’s tin whistle is used. Back in Ev, the eldest prince becomes King Evardo Fifteenth. The travelers then return to the Emerald City, where Dorothy is made a Princess of Oz. Finally, Dorothy sees through Ozma’s magic picture that Uncle Henry is ill and lonely in Australia. She gives Ozma the magic belt and uses its power to return to Uncle Henry, promising to care for him.

Narrative, section by section

front matter .. Author's Note

L. Frank Baum introduces Ozma of Oz as a record of Dorothy Gale's adventures with the Yellow Hen, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, Tiktok, the Cowardly Lion, and others. He explains that children asked for more about Dorothy, the Cowardly Lion, and what Ozma did after becoming ruler of Oz. Baum says the book includes old friends as well as new characters, including Billina, Tiktok, and the Hungry Tiger. He hopes his young readers will find the story "real Ozzy."

1. The Girl in the Chicken Coop

A fierce wind creates a terrible storm on the ocean while Dorothy Gale and her Uncle Henry travel by ship toward Australia. Dorothy leaves the cabin to search for Uncle Henry and is carried away when the wind lifts a chicken-coop from the deck. She survives on the coop as it floats across the waves, and after the storm subsides, she falls asleep beneath the moon and stars.

2. The Yellow Hen

Dorothy Gale awakens in a hen-coop drifting on the ocean and discovers that a yellow hen can talk. The hen, whom Dorothy renames Billina, explains how she survived the storm, and she and Dorothy drift toward an unknown land. After reaching the shore, Dorothy dries her shoes while Billina searches for food and argues that humans also eat animals. Billina finds a golden key buried in the sand, and Dorothy decides to explore the land for breakfast.

3. Letters in the Sand

Dorothy and Billina find the warning “BEWARE THE WHEELERS!” written in the beach sand and discover trees bearing lunch-boxes and dinner-pails. After Dorothy picks and eats a lunch-box and takes a dinner-pail, a Wheeler pursues her, followed by many others. Dorothy and Billina escape onto a rocky hill, where the Wheelers cannot follow because their wheels cannot roll over the jagged rocks. The Wheelers surround the hill and threaten Dorothy because she picked their food containers, invoking a law that demands death for anyone who picks a dinner-pail without permission.

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

Dorothy and Billina discover a hidden rock door on a hill and use a golden key to enter a small rocky chamber. Inside they find Tiktok, a copper mechanical man made by Smith & Tinker, and wind his clock-work mechanisms so that he can think, speak, and move. Tiktok tells them that the cruel King of Ev, Evoldo, sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King before locking Tiktok away and drowning himself. Tiktok then becomes Dorothy's obedient servant, provided she keeps him wound up.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

Dorothy rewinds Tiktok after he runs down and asks him about the Wheelers, who threaten to kill them below the rocks. While Tiktok thinks of a way to escape, Dorothy eats from her dinner pail and learns about the Land of Ev, its royal family, and Tiktok's makers. Tiktok explains that the Land of Oz is near the Land of Ev and tells Dorothy that Ozma of Oz now rules there after a revolution. Tiktok then leads Dorothy and Billina toward the town of Evna, promising to protect them from the Wheelers.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Dorothy, Tiktok, and Billina enter the Land of Ev after Tiktok defeats and questions the Wheelers. They reach the royal palace of Ev, where they meet Princess Langwidere, who has thirty interchangeable heads and demands Dorothy’s head for her collection. When Dorothy refuses, Langwidere orders her army to imprison Dorothy, while Tiktok runs down during the fight and Billina is sent to the chicken-house. The Nome King is identified as the captor of the royal family of Ev.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Dorothy Gale looks out from her tower prison in the Land of Ev and sees Ozma of Oz arriving across the desert with the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, the Cowardly Lion, Tiktok, and soldiers. Ozma confronts Princess Langwidere and agrees to help free the royal family of Ev from the Nome King, but first requires Langwidere to release Dorothy. Langwidere unlocks Dorothy, who returns to Ozma and her friends. While Ozma and Langwidere are away, the Scarecrow and Nick Chopper learn that Tiktok is a wind-up machine with mechanical brains but no conscience or heart.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Dorothy reunites with the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Tiktok, Cowardly Lion, and Billina after Ozma sets them free. She meets the Hungry Tiger, who explains that he refuses to eat living creatures despite his constant hunger. Billina is found fighting a speckled rooster and is brought back to the palace. Ozma announces a solemn conference to determine how to liberate the royal family of the Land of Ev from imprisonment.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Ozma of Oz organizes an expedition from the Land of Ev to the Kingdom of the Nomes to free the former royal family of Ev, who are imprisoned by Roquat of the Rocks. The group learns that Evoldo sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King in exchange for a long life, then drowned himself. Ozma, Dorothy, the Army of twenty-seven, and their companions prepare for the dangerous journey, while Princess Langwidere stays behind. At daybreak, the party leaves the palace in a procession headed toward the Nome King's palace.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

Dorothy, Ozma, Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, Tiktok, Sawhorse, Billina, and the army travel through a rocky valley toward the Land of Ev and the Nome King's underground palace. They cross a gulf on the magic carpet but are stopped by a giant iron man who pounds the narrow road with an immense iron mallet. The travelers pass beneath the hammer one by one, while the Cowardly Lion and the Tiger repeatedly carry the twenty-six officers to safety. Sawhorse is struck and damaged, the Scarecrow's foot is smashed, and the private is rescued after landing on the giant's arm.

11. The Nome King

Ozma of Oz, Dorothy, and their companions reach the mountain at the edge of the Kingdom of Ev and enter the Nome King's palace. The Nome King explains that the King of Ev exchanged the Queen of Ev and her ten children for a long life, and that he transformed them into bric-a-brac and ornaments. Ozma's attempt to negotiate their release fails until the Nome King offers a dangerous guessing challenge involving eleven objects in his palace. If Ozma identifies none of the royal transformations, she will herself become an ornament or article of bric-a-brac.

12. The Eleven Guesses

Ozma accepts the Nome King's challenge to identify members of the royal family of Ev among the ornaments in his palace, risking enslavement if she guesses incorrectly. She uses all eleven guesses without success, and her final blind guess transforms her into an emerald grasshopper ornament. The Nome King then invites another attempt, offering twelve guesses because twelve people are now transformed into ornaments. Dorothy volunteers, but the Tin Woodman claims the privilege of going next as commander of Ozma's army.

13. The Nome King Laughs

The Nome King laughs as the Tin Woodman and the twenty-six officers become ornaments after failing to guess correctly in his palace. Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, and a private remain, while the Cowardly Lion and Hungry Tiger sleep and Billina hides beneath the King’s throne. The King sends the survivors to sleeping rooms after the private is transformed, planning to continue the guessing challenge the next day. Dorothy later discovers that Billina is missing, but she cannot reopen the locked doorway to the throne room.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

The Nome King reveals that the Queen of Ev and her children have been transformed into royal-purple ornaments, while people from Oz have been changed into green ornaments. Dorothy enters the palace to wind Tiktok and make guesses, but Tiktok fails to identify the correct ornament. Dorothy succeeds in restoring Evring, a prince of Ev who had been changed into the image of a purple kitten, though her other guesses fail. She returns to the throne room with Evring.

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

Billina lays an egg under the Nome King's throne and uses the King's fear of eggs to secure permission to guess enchantments. Dorothy returns safely with Prince Evring after correctly guessing one enchantment, but the Scarecrow fails and is transformed into a solid gold card-receiver. Billina then enters the enchanted palace to guess, despite Dorothy's warning, while the Nome King worries about the talking hen.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

Billina uses her knowledge of the Nome King's transformations to restore the Queen of Ev, her nine children, the people of Oz, Ozma of Oz, the Scarecrow, and Tiktok. She recognizes the emerald grasshopper as Ozma and finds the Scarecrow and Tiktok among the gold ornaments, but no transformation that could be the Tin Woodman. The group returns toward the Nome King to demand information about the Tin Woodman, only to find the cavern filled with his motionless warriors and the enraged King waiting on his throne.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

Billina’s restoration mission succeeds, breaking the Nome King’s enchantments and restoring the royal family of Ev, Ozma, and their companions. The Nome King summons his warriors and attempts to seize Dorothy, but the Sawhorse protects her and cannot be stopped by the King’s magic. When the Scarecrow throws Billina’s eggs at the Nome King, the warriors flee in terror, leaving the King blinded and helpless. Billina then tells Dorothy to take the Nome King’s jeweled belt.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

Dorothy takes the Nome King's magic belt after Billina blinds him with broken eggs, and she uses it to open the passage out of his palace. Dorothy, Ozma, Billina, the Scarecrow, and the others search the palace for the transformed Tin Woodman but cannot find him. The Nome King claims he knows what ornament the Tin Woodman became, yet the ornament is gone when he searches for it. During the escape, Dorothy turns pursuing warriors into eggs and later stops the giant man of iron so the group can reach Ev safely.

19. The King of Ev

Ozma and the others return to Ev in her golden chariot after the defeat of the Nome King, while the royal children and Queen mother are welcomed home. Evring reveals that he picked up a tin whistle in the Nome King's palace, and Billina uses it to restore the enchanted Tin Woodman. At the palace of Ev, the Queen mother presents her eldest son as King Evardo Fifteenth, and the people approve his succession. The people celebrate the royal family's return, and Billina receives a necklace of pearls and sapphires.

20. The Emerald City

Dorothy accepts Ozma's invitation to return from Ev to the Land of Oz, traveling across the desert with Ozma, Tiktok, Billina, and the others on a magic carpet. They enter the Land of Oz, travel by yellow brick road to the Emerald City, and receive a joyful welcome. At a grand reception, the adventurers are honored with gifts, Dorothy is made a Princess of Oz, and Tiktok receives emerald bracelets. During the royal feast, Ozma promotes the private Omby Amby to Captain General and Commander of her Body Guard, while the Hungry Tiger consumes all the food and then loses his appetite.

21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

Dorothy uses Ozma's magic picture to see Aunt Em on the Kansas farm and Uncle Henry in Australia, where he appears ill and lonely. Glinda the Good explains that the magic belt can transport Dorothy out of a fairy country but would be lost when she arrived, so Dorothy gives it to Ozma and arranges a way to return. Dorothy says farewell to her friends in the Land of Oz and is transported to Uncle Henry, promising to care for him. Uncle Henry says he is already feeling better.
