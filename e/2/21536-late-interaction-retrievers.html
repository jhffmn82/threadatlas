Abstract

They are applied at retrieval time to enforce a finer-grained interaction between question and table embeddings.

Related Work

Late interaction retrievers are used to achieve finer-grained interaction between questions and tables than single-vector embedding retrievers. The work focuses mainly on applying them to table retrieval.
