INTRODUCTION

The Suppliant Maidens is probably Aeschylus's earliest extant play and is presented as possibly the earliest surviving poetic drama. It concerns the Danaids' flight from Egypt to Argos, their sanctuary there, and the rejection of the suitors' demand for their forced marriage.
