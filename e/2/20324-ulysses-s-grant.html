Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE GREAT BATTLE OF PITTSBURG LANDING.

Ulysses S. Grant commanded the Union forces at Pittsburg Landing and, despite an injured ankle, rode among the divisions throughout the first day directing their movements. He stopped a general flight toward the river, led the pursuit on the second day, and later argued that an immediate pursuit could have captured prisoners and guns.
