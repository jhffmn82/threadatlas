RAGE loss in LI-RAGE: Late Interaction Retrieval Augmented Generation with Explicit Signals for Open-Domain Table Question Answering

Weizhe Lin · 2023

RAGE loss is modified for tables by dropping its third term, which penalizes documents without gold answers that do not contribute to successful question-answering. Keeping that term penalizes K−1 documents in most cases and causes retriever collapse during joint training; removing it awards good tables and improves performance by around 1% EM on NQ-TABLES.

Results and Discussions / Main Results .. Remarks of Design Rationale

The RAGE loss is modified by dropping the term that penalizes documents lacking gold answers and not contributing to successful question answering. Keeping this term penalizes K−1 documents in most cases and causes retriever collapse during joint training, whereas removing it improves performance by around 1% EM on NQ-TABLES.
