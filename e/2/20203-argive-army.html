Argive army in The Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I

Euripides · -0437

The Argive army, from Argos, is assembled by Polynices with Adrastus’s support to attack Thebes. Numerous, bold, and supported by chariots, it marches and camps outside the city, with seven commanders assigned to besiege its seven gates. It surrounds Thebes but is held back by Theban resistance. After the brothers’ deaths, it is attacked before arming, routed, and suffers many dead.

Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

THE PHŒNICIAN VIRGINS.

The Argive army is assembled by Polynices with the support of Adrastus and marches against Thebes through seven gates. Antigone and the Tutor observe its leaders, including Hippomedon, Tydeus, Parthenopaeus, Amphiaraus, and Capaneus.

ETEOCLES, CHORUS, MESSENGER.

The Argive army prepares to surround Thebes with densely arranged forces and is said to be numerous, bold, and supported by chariots. It camps near the Theban walls and is commanded by seven leaders assigned to besiege the seven gates.

MESSENGER, JOCASTA, CHORUS.

The Argive army approaches the seven gates with its divisions and commanders, but Theban resistance prevents it from taking the city. After the brothers' deaths, it is attacked before it can put on its armor, routed, and left with many dead.

Argives in Four Plays of Aeschylus

Aeschylus · -0471

Argives are an invading army led by seven warriors, advancing with assault forces and equipment to surround, press, and besiege Cadmus’ city and its seven gates.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

THE SEVEN AGAINST THEBES

The Argives advance against Cadmus’ city with horsemen, shields, spears, battering equipment, and a force arranged for an assault on the seven gates. Their army is described as surrounding and pressing against the city’s walls.

Argives in The Library

Apollodorus · 0070~/0200~

Argives are an armed group led by seven leaders that approaches and fights at Thebes, threatening Tegea if Cepheus leaves. After Zeus strikes Capaneus and many fall, they suffer rout and flee.

Argive force in Plays of Sophocles: Oedipus the King; Oedipus at Colonus; Antigone

Sophocles · 1912

The Argive force is an invading army of seven forces under Polyneices and captains. It surrounds Thebes and attacks through its seven gates, but Zeus strikes it with lightning; it is driven back, and Eteocles and Polyneices die fighting each other.

Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

[Enter ANTIGONE and ISMENE with THESEUS]

The Argive host consists of seven forces led by Polyneices and captains including Amphiaraiis, Tydeus, Eteoclus, Hippomedon, Capaneus, and Parthenopaeus. It surrounds the plain around Thebes, and Polyneices says he cannot turn it back without shame.

ANTIGONE

The Argive force attacks Thebes with spearmen and seven captains at the city’s seven gates. It is driven back after Zeus strikes the invading host with lightning, and the two brothers Eteocles and Polyneices die fighting each other.
