King Evardo Fifteenth in Ozma of Oz

L. Frank Baum · 1907

King Evardo Fifteenth, the Queen mother's grave, quiet fifteen-year-old eldest son, returns to his country, inherits the throne of Ev, is proclaimed king, receives a ruby-set gold crown and ermine robe with fifteen silver buckles, then looks for cake in the royal pantry.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

19. The King of Ev

King Evardo Fifteenth is the Queen mother's eldest son and is fifteen years old. He has fifteen silver buckles, is proclaimed king by his mother, receives a gold crown and ermine robe, and then goes to look for cake in the royal pantry.
