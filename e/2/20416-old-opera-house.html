Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

The People's theater, an old frame building on the corner of Fourth and St.

The old opera house facing Wabasha Street was completed in the winter of 1866–67 and opened with an entertainment by local talent. Its boxes and some parquet seats were auctioned, and the first regular company was the Macfarland Dramatic Company, featuring Emily Melville. The theater seated about 1,000 people and hosted speakers including Wendell Phillips, Henry Ward Beecher, Theodore Tilton, and Frederick Douglass.
