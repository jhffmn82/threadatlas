Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HALF A CENTURY WITH THE PIONEER PRESS.

Frank Moore began working for the Minnesotian on April 17, 1858, at age fifteen, performing duties such as carrying water, retrieving newspapers from arriving steamers, and delivering papers. He joined the Pioneer Press when it absorbed the Minnesotian in 1861 and remained with the merged paper formed when the Press and Pioneer united in 1874. At age sixty-five, he was superintendent of the composing room, a position he had held for about forty years, and was recognized for his loyalty, devotion, and efficiency.
