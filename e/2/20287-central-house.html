Central house was a hotel at Bench and Minnesota streets, described as the first hotel of any pretension in St. Paul. Its dining room hosted the first territorial legislature session; later a boarding house, it burned in August 1873.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The Central house stood at Bench and Minnesota streets and was described as the first hotel of any pretension built in St. Paul. The first session of the territorial legislature met in its dining room, and after it became a boarding house it was burned in August 1873.
