THE FIRST AMUSEMENT HALLS IN ST.

Dan Emmet led a minstrel company at Irvine hall in 1857 and 1858, with performers including Frank Lombard, Max Irwin, and Johnny Ritter. He organized the first traveling minstrel troupe in the United States in 1843, later wrote popular melodies including "Dixie," and was the brother of Lafayette S. Emmett. He and his brother did not spell their surname alike.
