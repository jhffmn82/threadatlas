INTRODUCTION

The authorship of Rhesus is disputed despite strong external evidence connecting it with Euripides. One hypothesis attributes it to a young Euripides and later adaptation, while the more widely preferred view treats it as an anonymous fourth-century archaistic work. The introduction ultimately finds no adequate reason to reject the external evidence for Euripidean authorship, though it acknowledges that the alternative cannot be called improbable.
