As soon as Gen.

The Ladies Aid Society in St. Paul organized to sew for wounded soldiers and assist destitute refugees. Mrs. Stella Selby served as president, and Miss M.O. Holyoke served as secretary and devoted her time to collecting sanitary supplies.
