TERRITORIAL PRINTERS IN CIVIL LIFE.

Louis E. Fisher was foreman of the Commercial Advertiser and later an editor of the Pioneer and Pioneer Press. He was a staunch Democrat, ran for governor on the Democratic ticket, and was described as a potential model governor had he been elected.
