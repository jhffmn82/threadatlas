New England hotel in Novel-10146

Pioneer Press · 1862~

New England hotel was a two-story log house in St. Paul, among its first hotels. Gov. and Mrs. Ramsey lived there in 1849, giving it recognition as the first gubernatorial mansion, before the March 16, 1860 fire destroyed it.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

HOTEL FIRES.

The New England hotel was a two-story log house and one of the first hotels built in St. Paul. It burned in the March 16, 1860 fire; it had been known as the first gubernatorial mansion, where Gov. and Mrs. Ramsey began housekeeping in 1849.
