Pioneer Press · 1862~

During the steamboat era, Galena, Dunleith, Dubuque and Minnesota Packet company operated steamers on the upper Mississippi, linking St. Louis, Dubuque, Prairie du Chien and La Crosse; arrivals drew people to St. Paul's levee.

THE FIVE MILLION LOAN ELECTION.

The Galena, Dunleith, Dubuque and Minnesota Packet company operated splendid steamers serving points on the upper Mississippi. Its regular arrivals drew travelers, merchants, newspaper messengers, and town-lot dealers to St. Paul's levee.
