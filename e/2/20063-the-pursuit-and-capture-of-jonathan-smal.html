Narrative, section by section

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter X

The pursuit begins when the Aurora leaves Jacobson's Yard and races down the Thames. Holmes's launch follows through heavy river traffic, gradually gains on it, and finally catches it after the Aurora runs onto a muddy bank.

Chapter XI

Sherlock Holmes, Athelney Jones, and their companions pursue the Aurora down the Thames and capture Jonathan Small. Jones says that they barely overhauled the fast launch, while Small reveals that he had intended to reach the Esmeralda at Gravesend.

Chapter XII

The pursuit ends with Jonathan Small captured and taken to Baker Street, while the launch nearly catches him before he can dispose of the treasure. Small says that the approaching launch caused him to hide or discard the loot.
