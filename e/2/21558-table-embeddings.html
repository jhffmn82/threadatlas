Narrative, section by section

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Technical Details / Hyperparameters

DPR table embeddings have dimension 768. LI table embeddings have dimension lt × 128, with lt depending on the length of the input tables.

Indexing and Dynamic Retrieval

One-dimensional table embeddings are pre-extracted with DPR and indexed in the FAISS system. Their index enables fast nearest-neighbor search.
