female beauty and obesity in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Female obesity is a Moorish beauty standard: before marriage, betrothed girls are forcibly fed and kept from exercise to enlarge their lower bodies, with enormous women considered especially attractive.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER I.

Jewish women are described as often having fair complexions, delicate features, and expressive eyes, while Moorish standards value extreme size in the lower back, thighs, calves, and knees. Betrothed women may be deliberately fattened with paste and prevented from exercising, and enormous women are treated as especially attractive.
