Gomera in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

Gomera, or Badis, is a city on the mainland opposite Penon de Velez, identified with Parientina and possibly Acra of Ptolemy; reportedly once had 3,000 inhabitants, then was described as abandoned, inhabited only by serpents and hyenas.

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER IV.

Gomera, or Badis, lies on the mainland opposite Penon de Velez and is identified with several ancient names, including Parientina and possibly the Acra of Ptolemy. Although Count Graberg claimed that it contained 3,000 inhabitants, other and later writers described it as abandoned and inhabited only by serpents and hyenas.
