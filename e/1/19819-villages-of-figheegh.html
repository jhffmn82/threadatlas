villages of Figheegh in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

The twelve villages form a close cluster on the descent of Wal-el-Khalouf, connected and never more than a quarter of a league apart. Each has houses, a mosque, school, kady, and sheikh. Eternal strife among them, sometimes armed, has led to crenated walls and defensive towers; larger villages include Maiz, El-Wadghir, and Zenega.

The twelve villages are generally close together, no more than a quarter of a league apart, and lie on the descent of Wal-el-Khalouf. They have houses, mosques, schools, religious and civil leaders, crenated walls, and defensive towers; the larger villages include Maiz, El-Wadghir, and Zenega.
