El-Kesar in Travels in Morocco

James Richardson · 1860

El-Kesar is a fortified place in the Gharb, on the southern bank of the Luccos. Enlarged and beautified by Sultan Almansor around 1180 as a military magazine and rendezvous, it later declined, with narrow streets, ruined houses, inactive mosques, and four or five thousand souls. It hosted the Battle of the Three Kings in 1578.

Narrative, section by section

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

El-Kesar lies in the province of the Gharb on the southern bank of the Luccos. It was enlarged and beautified by Almansor around 1180 as a military magazine and rendezvous, but its narrow streets, ruined houses, inactive mosques, and small population reflect decline. The Battle of the Three Kings took place there in 1578.
