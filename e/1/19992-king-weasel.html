King Weasel in The Woggle-Bug Book

L. Frank Baum · 1905

King Weasel is the contempt-regarded king of the jungle, attended by prairie-dogs and a red squirrel, and father of a boy and girl. After the Woggle-Bug insults and slaps him, he accepts him as important, then orders gasoline to eradicate him; the Woggle-Bug escapes.

King Weasel rules the jungle but is treated with contempt and is served by prairie-dogs and a squirrel. At Miss Chim's suggestion, the Woggle-Bug insults and slaps him, after which the king accepts him as important. King Weasel ultimately orders gasoline brought to eradicate the Woggle-Bug, who escapes before this can happen.
